**Against Medical Advice Form - Help Guide**
=====================================

## Purpose
The Against Medical Advice Form is used to document a patient's decision to leave a medical facility against the advice of medical staff. This form helps to ensure that the patient is aware of the potential risks and alternatives involved in such a decision.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out the Against Medical Advice Form, follow these steps:

*   Start by providing patient information on page 1.
*   On page 1, enter the patient's full name as it appears on their medical records.
*   On page 2, enter the patient's date of birth.
*   On page 3, enter the hospital or clinic's medical record number, if available.
*   On page 4, enter the reason why the patient wishes to leave against medical advice.
*   On page 5, confirm whether the patient has been informed of the risks involved in leaving against medical advice.
*   On page 6, indicate whether alternatives were offered to the patient.
*   On page 7, enter the name of a staff member or family member who witnessed the patient's decision.
*   On page 8, enter the date the patient signed the form.
*   Finally, on page 9, enter your full name as a verification of the patient's signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient full name** (Page 2, Required): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their medical records.
*   **Date of birth** (Page 2, Required): Enter the patient's date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
*   **Reason for leaving against medical advice** (Page 4, Required): Explain in your own words why the patient wishes to leave against medical advice.
*   **Has the patient been informed of the risks** (Page 5, Required): Confirm whether the patient has been informed of the risks involved in leaving against medical advice.
*   **Were alternatives offered** (Page 6, Required): Indicate whether alternatives were discussed or offered to the patient.
*   **Witness name** (Page 7, Optional): Enter the name of a staff member or family member who witnessed the patient's decision.
*   **Date signed** (Page 8, Required): Enter the date and time the patient signed the form.
*   **Patient signature name** (Page 9, Required): Enter your full name as a verification of the patient's signature.

## Tips

*   Ensure that the patient's signature is properly dated and witnessed.
*   If the patient leaves against medical advice, ensure that the necessary documentation is completed and signed by the patient.
*   If the patient is unable to sign the form, consider having a witness sign on their behalf.
*   Always document the patient's understanding of the risks and alternatives involved.
