# Genetic Data Sharing Research Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Genetic Data Sharing Research Consent Form is designed to obtain consent from research participants to share their genetic data for specific research purposes. It is used to ensure that individuals understand the purpose and scope of the research, and that they provide informed consent before participating in the study.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer all fields that apply.
2. For fields that ask for "Yes" or "No" answers, select the correct option from the list.
3. For text fields, type your answer or statement in the provided space.
4. Make sure to sign and date the form to indicate your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Researcher Institution** (`researcher_institution`, select_one, required/optional): 
  This field asks you to confirm whether the research institution is involved in the study. Select "Yes" if the institution is participating, "No" otherwise.
* **Researcher Contact** (`researcher_contact`, text, required/optional): 
  This field is for you to provide contact information for the researcher or study team. Please type the name, email, and phone number of the researcher or team member responsible for the study.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, select_multiple, required/optional): 
  This field asks you to confirm whether you are a participant in the study. Select "Yes" if you are participating, "No" otherwise.
* **Genetic Data Sharing Purposes** (`genetic_data_sharing_purposes`, select_one, required/optional): 
  This field asks you to confirm the purpose of data sharing. Select "Yes" if the data is being shared for research purposes, "No" otherwise.
* **Participant Informed Consent** (`participant_informed_consent`, select_multiple, required/optional): 
  This field asks you to confirm whether you have received informed consent about the study. Select "Yes" if you have received consent, "No" otherwise.
* **Data Sharing Agreement** (`data_sharing_agreement`, text, required/optional): 
  This field is for you to type the agreement or terms of data sharing. Please be specific about the conditions and limitations of data sharing.
* **Participant Consent Signed** (`participant_consent_signed`, select_multiple, required/optional): 
  This field asks you to confirm whether you have signed the participant consent form. Select "Yes" if you have signed, "No" otherwise.
* **Research Purposes Statement** (`research_purposes_statement`, text, required/optional): 
  This field is for you to provide a detailed statement of the research purposes. Please explain the specific goals, methods, and expected outcomes of the study.
* **Data Sharing Statement** (`data_sharing_statement`, text, required/optional): 
  This field is for you to describe the terms and conditions of data sharing. Please be specific about the scope, duration, and any restrictions or limitations of data sharing.
