# Doctor's Notes Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Doctor's Notes Release Form is a form that allows patients to grant or deny authorization for their medical records to be released to other healthcare providers or organizations. This form is used to obtain a patient's consent for the release of their protected health information (PHI).

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and read each section thoroughly.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for each field as instructed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Authorization** (patient_authorization): Select "Yes" if you grant authorization for your medical records to be released.
* **Medical Records Release** (medical_records_release): This is a text field where you can provide any relevant details about the release of your medical records.
* **Authorized Provider** (authorized_provider): Select "Yes" if the medical records can be released to the healthcare provider you have authorized.
* **Release Date** (release_date): Enter the date you grant authorization for your medical records to be released.
* **Signature Date** (signature_date): Enter the date you signed this form.
* **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth): Enter your date of birth.
* **Patient Address** (patient_address): Provide your address.
* **Patient Phone** (patient_phone): Provide your contact phone number.
* **Authorization Date** (authorization_date): Enter the date you grant authorization for your medical records to be released.
* **Medical Group** (medical_group): If applicable, provide the name of your medical group or organization.
* **Authorized Provider ID** (authorized_provider_id): Provide your authorized provider's ID number (if applicable).
* **Patient Signature** (patient_signature): Sign your name, indicating your consent to the release of your medical records.
* **Doctor Signature** (doctor_signature): The authorized healthcare provider will sign this section, indicating their consent to the release of your medical records.
* **Medical Facility** (medical_facility): Provide the name of the medical facility where your records will be released.
* **Release By** (release_by): Provide the name of the person or entity releasing your medical records.
* **Authorized By** (authorized_by): Select "Yes" if you are authorizing someone else to access your medical records.
* **Medical Staff** (medical_staff): Select "Yes" if you are authorizing medical staff to access your medical records.
* **Release To** (release_to): If applicable, provide the name of the entity or organization you are authorizing to receive your medical records.
* **Medical License Number** (medical_license_number): If applicable, provide your medical license number.
* **Medical License State** (medical_license_state): If applicable, provide your state of medical licensure.
* **Medical License Type** (medical_license_type): If applicable, provide your type of medical licensure.
* **Medical License Expiration** (medical_license_expiration): If applicable, enter the date your medical license expires.
* **Medical License Issue** (medical_license_issue): If applicable, enter the date your medical license was issued.
* **Medical License Status** (medical_license_status): If applicable, provide your medical license status.
* **Authorized By Signature** (authorized_by_signature): The authorized healthcare provider will sign this section, indicating their consent to the release of your medical records.

This form is a critical document that ensures the confidentiality of your protected health information (PHI). Please complete it carefully and honestly to ensure that your medical records are handled correctly.
