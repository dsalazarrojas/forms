# supreme_nails_covid_19_liability_release_waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supreme Nails COVID-19 Liability Release Waiver form is used to gather important information from customers for liability release purposes. This form is intended to be used during a COVID-19 pandemic or similar public health emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Phone in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date Of Birth" field in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
6. Enter your Address in the "Address" field.
7. Enter your Zip Code in the "Zip Code" field.
8. Enter your Social Security Number (optional, but recommended for liability purposes) in the "Social Security Number" field.
9. If applicable, enter the Emergency Contact's Name in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
10. If applicable, enter the Emergency Contact's Phone in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.
11. Specify your relationship to the emergency contact (optional) in the "Relationship" field.
12. Enter the date you signed this waiver in the "Sign Date" field in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
13. Enter the time you signed this waiver in the "Sign Time" field.
14. Select your time zone in the "Time Zone" field.
15. Enter the location where you signed this waiver in the "Location" field.
16. Enter the IP address from which you accessed this form in the "IP" field (optional, but recommended for security purposes).
17. Enter the User Agent from which you accessed this form in the "User Agent" field (optional, but recommended for security purposes).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (id: 1, `text`, required: `false`): Your first name.
- **Last Name** (id: 2, `text`, required: `false`): Your last name.
- **Email** (id: 3, `email`, required: `false`): Your email address.
- **Phone** (id: 4, `text`, required: `false`): Your phone number.
- **Date Of Birth** (id: 5, `date`, required: `false`): Your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
- **Address** (id: 6, `text`, required: `false`): Your address.
- **Zip Code** (id: 7, `text`, required: `false`): Your zip code.
- **Social Security Number** (id: 8, `text`, required: `false`): Your social security number (optional, but recommended for liability purposes).
- **Emergency Contact Name** (id: 9, `text`, required: `false`): The name of your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Phone** (id: 10, `text`, required: `false`): The phone number of your emergency contact.
- **Relationship** (id: 11, `select_one`, required: `false`): Specify the relationship between you and your emergency contact.
- **Sign Date** (id: 12, `date`, required: `false`): The date you signed this waiver in MM/DD/YYYY format.
- **Sign Time** (id: 13, `time`, required: `false`): The time you signed this waiver.
- **Time Zone** (id: 14, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select your time zone.
- **Location** (id: 15, `text`, required: `false`): The location where you signed this waiver.
- **IP** (id: 16, `text`, required: `false`): The IP address from which you accessed this form (optional, but recommended for security purposes).
- **User Agent** (id: 17, `text`, required: `false`): The User Agent from which you accessed this form (optional, but recommended for security purposes).
