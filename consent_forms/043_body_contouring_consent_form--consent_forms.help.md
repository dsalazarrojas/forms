# Body Contouring Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Body Contouring Consent Form is a document that ensures the client understands and agrees to the terms and conditions of the body contouring procedure. It is a required form to be completed before the procedure can begin.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form completely and accurately to the best of your knowledge.
2.  Be honest and transparent about your personal and medical history.
3.  Review and understand the benefits, risks, and limitations of the procedure.
4.  Check the boxes to acknowledge your understanding and consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Client First Name** (`client_first_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
*  **Client Last Name** (`client_last_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
*  **Client Email** (`client_email`, `email`, required): Please enter your email address as it will be used for communication.
*  **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, `text`, required): Please enter your contact phone number.
*  **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*  **Procedure Name** (`procedure_name`, `text`, required): Please select the specific body contouring procedure you are having done.
*  **Treatment Areas** (`treatment_areas`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select all areas of the body to be treated (Abdomen, Flanks, Thighs, Buttocks, Arms, Back, Chin, Multiple Areas).
*  **Provider Name** (`provider_name`, `text`, required): Please enter the name of the practitioner performing the procedure.
*  **Procedure Explanation** (`procedure_explanation`, `select_one`, required): Please select how thoroughly the procedure has been explained to you (Yes, Thoroughly, Yes, Adequately, Somewhat, False).
*  **Benefits Understood** (`benefits_understood`, `select_one`, required): Please select if you understand the benefits of the procedure (True, False).
*  **Risks Understood** (`risks_understood`, `select_one`, required): Please select if you understand the risks of the procedure (True, False).
*  **Potential Risks** (`potential_risks`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select all potential risks you acknowledge (Pain or Discomfort, Swelling, Bruising, Redness, Numbness, Skin Irregularities, Infection, Scarring, Asymmetry, Unsatisfactory Results).
*  **Alternative Treatments** (`alternative_discussed`, `select_one`, required): Please select if alternative treatments have been discussed (True, False).
*  **Alternative Options** (`alternative_options`, `text`, optional): If applicable, please list any alternative treatments discussed.
*  **Medical History Reviewed** (`medical_history_reviewed`, `select_one`, required): Please select if your medical history has been reviewed (True, False).
*  **Questions Asked** (`questions_asked`, `select_one`, required): Please select if all your questions have been answered (Yes, All Answered, Some Answered, No Questions Asked).
*  **Realistic Expectations** (`realistic_expectations`, `select_one`, required): Please select if you have realistic expectations (True, False).
*  **No Guarantees Understood** (`no_guarantees`, `select_one`, required): Please select if you understand that results cannot be guaranteed (Yes, I Understand, False).
*  **Follow-Up Care Understood** (`follow_up_care`, `select_one`, required): Please select if you understand the post-procedure care requirements (True, False).
*  **Voluntary Consent** (`voluntary_consent`, `select_one`, required): Please select if you understand this is a voluntary procedure (Yes, I Understand, False).
*  **Right to Withdraw** (`right_to_withdraw`, `select_one`, required): Please select if you understand you have the right to withdraw from the procedure at any time (Yes, I Understand, False).
*  **Photo Consent** (`photo_consent`, `select_one`, required): Please select if you agree to have before and after photos taken (Yes, for Medical Records Only, Yes, for Marketing Too, False).
*  **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, `text`, required): Please type your full name as your signature.
*  **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, required): Please enter the date of your signature in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*  **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`, `text`, optional): If present, please sign as a witness.
*  **Witness Signature Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, optional): If present, please enter the date of your witness's signature in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
