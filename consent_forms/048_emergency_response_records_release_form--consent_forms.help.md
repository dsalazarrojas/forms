# Emergency Response Records Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients or their representatives for emergency response purposes. It is used to gather crucial details that may be needed in case of an emergency. Please note that this form is not a medical diagnosis or treatment form, but rather a tool to provide essential information to emergency responders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Patient Name** in the field provided. This is the name of the person who is or was in contact with an emergency response team.
2. Provide your **Contact Number** for the emergency response team to reach you with any questions or updates.
3. Answer the **Emergency Response** question by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' from the dropdown menu. 'Yes' indicates that the patient or patient has experienced an emergency, while 'No' suggests that there is no emergency situation.
4. Share any **Medical Information** related to the patient or patient's condition in the text field provided. This information will help the emergency response team better understand the situation and provide more effective assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (patient_name, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who is or was in contact with an emergency response team.
* **Contact Number** (contact_number, text, optional): Provide a valid phone number where the emergency response team can contact you for updates or questions.
* **Emergency Response** (emergency_response, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' if you or someone else is or was in emergency situation; select 'No' if not.
* **Medical Information** (medical_information, text, optional): Share any relevant medical information, including allergies, medical conditions, or recent medical procedures. This will help emergency responders better assist you.

## Tips
Remember to fill out this form completely and accurately to ensure the emergency response team receives all necessary information to provide the best possible assistance. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
