# Hydrodermabrasion Facial Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather necessary information from clients before undergoing hydrodermabrasion treatment. The form is divided into sections that cover client information, medical history, treatment acknowledgments, and consents. Completing this form ensures that clients understand the risks and procedures involved in the treatment and provides the aesthetician with essential information for their safety and medical record.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's information at the top of the form, including their name, photo ID number, and date and time of treatment.
2. Answer the questions in the "Client History" section, providing as much detail as possible about your medical history, allergies, and medications.
3. In the "Treatment Acknowledgments" section, select all the statements that you understand and agree to.
4. In the "Client Consent" and "Aesthetician Consent" sections, enter the required numbers to indicate your consent.
5. Sign and date the form in the "Aesthetician Signature" and "Client Signature" sections.
6. Finally, write any additional notes in the "Client Notes" and "Medical Notes" sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information**: Enter your name, photo ID number, and date and time of treatment.
* **Client History**: Please answer all the questions regarding your medical history, including your allergies, medications, and any medical conditions.
	+ This section is not required but it's a good practice to provide as much information as possible about your health status.
* **Treatment Acknowledgments**: Select all the statements that you understand and agree to.
	+ You must understand that the treatment can cause skin irritation, redness, and other side effects.
	+ You must understand that the treatment can cause skin conditions such as acne, rosacea, and eczema.
* **Client Consent**: Enter the required number to indicate your consent.
* **Aesthetician Signature**: Sign and date this section to confirm that you have read and understood the form.
* **Client Signature**: Sign and date this section to confirm that you have read and understood the form.
* **Date of Treatment**: Enter the date of the treatment.
* **Time of Treatment**: Enter the time of the treatment.
* **Service Provider**: Select the service provider (Spas, Estheticians, Skincare Clinics).
* **Treatment Plan**: Provide a brief description of the treatment you are undergoing.
* **Allergy List**: List any allergies you have.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions you have.
* **Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical History**: Provide a brief description of your medical history.
* **Medical Notes**: Provide any relevant medical information.
* **Client Notes**: Provide any notes or comments you think are relevant to your treatment.
* **Client Consent**: Enter the required number to indicate your consent.
* **Aesthetician Consent**: Enter the required number to indicate the aesthetician's consent.
* **Client Photo ID**: Enter your photo ID number.
* **Client Signature**: Sign and date this section to confirm that you have read and understood the form.
* **Client Notes**: Provide any additional notes or comments.

Remember to carefully read and understand each section before signing the form. If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your aesthetician for clarification.
