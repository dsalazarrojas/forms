# BIPOC Authors Support Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help the BIPOC authors support team understand your writing experiences, goals, and needs. The information collected will be used to provide tailored support, guidance, and resources to help you achieve your writing aspirations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in your **Author Name**, **Email Address**, and **Phone Number** (if provided).
2. Select your **Racial or Ethnic Identity** from the provided options.
3. Choose your **Primary Writing Genre** from the list of options.
4. If you have had previously published work, select **True**; otherwise, select **False** or **In Progress**.
5. Describe your **Current Project** in the provided text field.
6. Select the **Project Completion Stage** that best represents your current project status.
7. Choose the types of **Support** you require from the options provided.
8. Write a brief description of your **Writing Goals**.
9. If applicable, list your **Writing Background and Training**, such as degrees, workshops, or mentors.
10. Let us know if you are **Active in a Writing Community**.
11. If you have any additional **Goals or Objectives**, provide a brief description.
12. Select whether you are **Active in a Writing Community**.
13. Confirm that you consent to be contacted about the program.
14. Consent to the **Privacy Practices and Data Use**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Author Name (Author Name)**: Your legal or pen name.
* **Email Address (Email Address)**: For all communications related to the support process.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number)**: Provide your phone number for direct communication.
* **Racial or Ethnic Identity (Racial or Ethnic Identity)**: Select your racial or ethnic background.
* **Primary Writing Genre (Primary Writing Genre)**: Choose the genre that best represents your writing style, such as fiction, non-fiction, poetry, etc.
* **Years of Writing Experience (Years of Writing Experience)**: If you are unsure, simply leave this blank.
* **Have you had Work Previously Published? (Have you had Work Previously Published?)**: If you have had work published, select **True**; otherwise, select **False** or **In Progress**.
* **Tell Us About Your Current Project (Current Project)**: Describe your current project, including the title, genre, and brief description.
* **Project Completion Stage (Project Completion Stage)**: Choose the stage that best represents your current project status, such as Concept Stage, First Draft, etc.
* **What Support Do You Need? (Support Needs)**: Select all the types of support you require, such as mentorship, editing services, etc.
* **What Are Your Writing Goals? (Goals/Objectives)**: Describe your writing aspirations and objectives.
* **Writing Background and Training (Writing Background and Training)**: If applicable, list any degrees, workshops, or mentors you have had.
* **Are you active in a Writing Community? (Community Engagement)**: Select whether you are active in a writing community.
* **Consent to be contacted about this program (Consent to Contact)**: Confirm that you consent to be contacted about the program.
* **Consent to Privacy Practices and Data Use (Consent to Privacy Practices and Data Use)**: Confirm that you consent to the privacy practices and data use.
* **Additional Information or Comments (Additional Information or Comments)**: If you have any additional comments or concerns, provide them here.

Tips:
- Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete.
- If you have already submitted a similar form, please do not resubmit.
- This form is designed to help the support team provide tailored support, guidance, and resources to help you achieve your writing goals.
