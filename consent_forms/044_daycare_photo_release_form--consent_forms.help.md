## Daycare Photo Release Form - Help Guide

### Purpose
This form is intended to obtain consent and information from parents or guardians of children in daycare, allowing them to authorize the use of their child's photo in promotional materials and specify any copyright restrictions for the photo.

### How To Complete This Form

1. Please read the form carefully and make sure you have all required fields filled out before submission.
2. If you need help, you can always ask for assistance from the support team.
3. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information, and submit it for review.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Information** (`parent_info`, `text`, required/optional): This section is where you provide some general information about the parent or guardian, such as their name and contact details.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the parent or guardian who is submitting this form.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter a valid email address of the parent or guardian.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of birth of the child.
* **Photo Release** (`photo_release`, `select_one`, required/optional): Specify whether you consent to the use of your child's photo in promotional materials. Options are "Yes" or "No".
* **Photo Description** (`photo_description`, `text`, required/optional): If you consent to the photo being used, provide a brief description of the photo.
* **Photo Copyright** (`photo_copyright`, `text`, required/optional): If you hold any copyrights for the photo, specify them here.
* **Daycare Name** (`daycare_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the daycare center or organization that will be using your child's photo.

### Tips

* Make sure to submit the form with accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth processing experience.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact the support team.
