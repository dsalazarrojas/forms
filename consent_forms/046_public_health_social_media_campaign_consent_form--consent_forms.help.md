# Public Health Social Media Campaign Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to be used as part of a public health social media campaign to collect information about individuals who wish to participate in the campaign. By filling out this form, you will be sharing your name, date of birth, email, phone number, address, social media handles, and consent to participate in the campaign.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill out the form with the required information.
* Ensure that all required fields (First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, Social Media Platforms, and Social Media Handle) are filled out.
* You can leave the optional fields (Email, Phone, and Address) blank if you do not wish to provide them.
* Once you have completed the form, click the submit button to send your information for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., passport, driver's license).
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., passport, driver's license).
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you have an email address you would like to use to contact you, please enter it here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number you would like to use to contact you, please enter it here.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): If you have a physical address you would like to use to contact you, please enter it here.
* **Social Media Platforms** (`social_media_platforms`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the social media platforms where you would like to participate in the campaign (e.g., Instagram, Facebook, Twitter).
* **Social Media Handle** (`social_media_handle`, text, required): Please enter your social media handle for each platform you selected (e.g., @username).

## Tips
* Ensure that you are aware of the terms and conditions of the campaign before participating.
* Review this document for any further instructions or requirements specific to this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the campaign or this form, please contact the campaign administrators.

Note: Please be aware that this form is subject to change as the campaign evolves, and it's your responsibility to review and update your information accordingly.
