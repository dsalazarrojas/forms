# health_app_analytics_research_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect participant information for research studies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your participant details, including any relevant information about your participation in the study.
2. Provide consent details, if applicable.
3. Select the study institution that is conducting the research.
4. Enter the research goals of the study, if applicable.
5. Enter any additional participant info that is required or relevant.
6. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
7. Enter the date of your participation in the study.
8. Enter the start date of the study.
9. Enter the name of the research coordinator.
10. Enter the contact person for the study.
11. Enter the study duration.
12. Enter the date the study is expected to be completed.
13. Provide your informed consent for participating in the study.
14. Enter any additional research aims.
15. Enter the name of the study instrument being used.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required: false): 
Enter your name, address, phone number and other details that identify you as a participant.
* **Consent Details** (`consent_details`, text, required: false): 
This is where you can provide any additional details about your consent for participating in the study.
* **Study Institution** (`study_institution`, select_one, required: false): 
Select the institution conducting the study.
* **Research Goals** (`research_goals`, text, required: false): 
Enter any information about the research goals of the study.
* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, text, required: false): 
Enter any additional information about yourself as a participant.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): 
Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Date of Participation** (`date_of_participation`, date, required: false): 
Enter the date of your participation in the study.
* **Study Start Date** (`study_start_date`, date, required: false): 
Enter the start date of the study.
* **Research Coordinator** (`research_coordinator`, text, required: false): 
Enter the name of the research coordinator.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required: false): 
Enter the name of the contact person for the study.
* **Study Duration** (`study_duration`, text, required: false): 
Enter the expected duration of the study.
* **Study Completion Date** (`study_completion_date`, date, required: false): 
Enter the date the study is expected to be completed.
* **Participant Informed Consent** (`participant_informed_consent`, note, required: false): 
Please sign below to confirm you have read and understood this consent form.
* **Research Aims** (`research_aims`, text, required: false):
