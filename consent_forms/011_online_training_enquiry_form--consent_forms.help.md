# Online Training Enquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The online training enquiry form is designed to collect information from users interested in our online training programs. Please fill out this form to express your interest and we will get back to you with more details.

## How To Complete This Form
- Follow the instructions carefully.
- Fill out the form with accurate and true information.
- Ensure all fields with the 'Required' status are filled out.
- Click submit at the end of the form to send your details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. First Name (First Name, Text, Optional)
The first name field is where you can input your first name. Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.

### 2. Last Name (Last Name, Text, Optional)
The last name field is where you can input your last name. Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.

### 3. Email (Email, Email, Optional)
The email field is where you can input your email address that we can use to contact you.

### 4. Phone (Phone, Text, Optional)
The phone field is where you can input your phone number that we can use to contact you.

### 5. Date of Birth (Date of Birth, Date, Optional)
The date of birth field is where you can input your date of birth.

### 6. Health Conditions (Health Conditions, Text, Optional)
The health conditions field is for you to input any health conditions or disabilities that we need to be aware of for the training.

### 7. Expectations (Expectations, Text, Optional)
The expectations field is where you can input your expectations for the training, including any specific skills or knowledge you are looking for.

### 8. Emergency Contact Name (Emergency Contact Name, Text, Optional)
The emergency contact name field is where you can input the name of an emergency contact person.

### 9. Emergency Contact Relationship (Emergency Contact Relationship, Text, Optional)
The emergency contact relationship field is where you can input your relationship with the emergency contact person, e.g., parent, guardian, partner.

### 10. Emergency Contact Phone (Emergency Contact Phone, Text, Optional)
The emergency contact phone field is where you can input the contact number of your emergency contact person.

Please note: If any required fields are left empty, your submission will not be processed correctly.
