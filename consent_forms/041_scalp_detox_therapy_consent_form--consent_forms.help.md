# Scalp Detox Therapy Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to obtain consent from clients before undergoing scalp detoxy therapy. The form is designed to gather essential information about the client's health, consent status, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and fill in the required fields.
2. Select the correct options for each question or field.
3. Sign the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Client Consents

* **Client Consents** (`client_consent`, `select_multiple`, required): Please indicate your consent status for scalp detoxy therapy. Choose "Yes" if you agree to undergo the therapy, or "No" if you do not.
* **Health Details** (`health_details`, `text`, optional): Provide any relevant health information that may impact your scalp detoxy therapy.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, required): List any allergies or sensitivities you have that may be relevant to the therapy.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, optional): Share any existing medical conditions that may affect your scalp detoxy therapy.
* **Medication** (`medication`, `text`, optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Scalp Health** (`scalp_health`, `text`, required): Describe your current scalp health, including any symptoms or concerns.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `select_one`, required): Choose to sign as the client or as a parent/guardian.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, `date`, required): Enter the date you sign this form.
* **Salon Address** (`salon_address`, `text`, required): Provide the address of the salon where you will undergo the therapy.
* **Salons** (`salons`, `select_one`, required): Choose the salon where you will undergo the therapy.
* **Therapist** (`therapist`, `select_one`, required): Select the therapist who will provide the scalp detoxy therapy.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, optional): If you are a minor, provide the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, `email`, optional): If you are a minor, enter your parent or guardian's email address.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, `text`, optional): If you are a minor, enter your parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Minor** (`minor`, `text`, optional): If you are a minor, indicate your age.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, required): Select your consent status for the scalp detoxy therapy.
* **Signature** (`signature_text`, `text`, required): Sign this form to confirm your consent.

Please note that all fields marked as "required" must be filled in before submitting the form.
