# Eyelash Extension Consent & Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for recording client consent and appointment information for eyelash extension services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the consent questions (fields 2 and 3) to obtain the client's consent for the appointment.
2. Enter the appointment date and time (fields 4 and 5) for the scheduled appointment.
3. Enter the client's name (field 6) and contact information (field 7) for the appointment.
4. Add any additional notes (field 13) about the appointment if necessary.
5. Select the appointment status (field 12) to indicate whether the client has arrived, not arrived, cancelled, or rescheduled the appointment.
6. Have the client sign the agreement (field 8) and the staff member sign (field 9) to confirm the appointment.
7. Enter the date of signature (field 10) for both the client and staff member.
8. Enter any additional comments or notes (field 11) about the appointment if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consent** (2, text): This field is used to obtain the client's consent for the eyelash extension appointment. Please have the client read and sign this section to confirm their consent.
* **Consent Date** (3, date): This field is used to record the date of consent.
* **Appointment Date** (4, date): This field is used to enter the date of the scheduled appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (5, time): This field is used to enter the time of the scheduled appointment.
* **Client Name** (6, text): This field is used to enter the client's name.
* **Client Contact** (7, text): This field is used to enter the client's contact information (e.g., phone number, email).
* **Client Signature** (8, text): This field is used for the client to sign to confirm their consent.
* **Staff Signature** (9, text): This field is used for the staff member to sign to confirm the appointment.
* **Date of Signature** (10, date): This field is used to enter the date of signature for both the client and staff member.
* **Appointment Status** (12, select one): This field is used to select whether the client has arrived, not arrived, cancelled, or rescheduled the appointment.
* **Notes** (13, note): This field is used to enter any additional notes about the appointment.
* **Client Name** (14, text): This field is used to re-enter the client's name for confirmation.
* **Email** (15, email): This field is not currently used.

## Tips
* Make sure to obtain the client's consent before proceeding with the eyelash extension appointment.
* Verify the client's contact information to ensure proper communication.
* Enter all required fields to ensure accurate and complete records.
* Use this form for clients who have scheduled appointments for eyelash extension services only.
