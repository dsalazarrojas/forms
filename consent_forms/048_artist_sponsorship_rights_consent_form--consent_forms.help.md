# Artist Sponsorship Rights Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain the consent of an artist to a sponsorship agreement, outlining the terms and rights of the partnership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have all the necessary information and details about the sponsorship before proceeding.
2. Enter your artist information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
3. Provide the sponsor's information, including their name and the type of sponsorship they are offering.
4. Specify the scope of rights granted, including where and how the sponsor may use your name, likeness, and materials.
5. Choose the duration of the sponsorship and when it ends.
6. Confirm if this is an exclusive sponsorship and if there are any excluded uses.
7. Provide any additional compensation or royalties information, if applicable.
8. Sign and date the form, indicating that you consent to the terms and rights outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist or Performer Name (1)** (`artist_name`, text, required): Enter your professional name as it appears in public.
* **Email Address (2)** (`artist_email`, email, required): Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number (3)** (`artist_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Legal Representative or Manager (5)** (`legal_representative`, text, optional): If you have a representative, enter their name.
* **Sponsor Company or Organization Name (7)** (`sponsor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the sponsoring entity.
* **Type of Sponsorship (8)** (`sponsorship_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of sponsorship you are entering into (e.g., Performance, Tour, Album/Recording, etc.).
* **Sponsorship Amount or Value (9)** (`sponsorship_amount`, number, optional): Enter the value of the sponsorship.
* **Sponsor May Use Artist Name and Likeness (11)** (`name_likeness_rights`, select_one, required): Choose whether the sponsor may use your name and likeness in marketing.
* **Sponsor May Use Social Media Posts (12)** (`social_media_rights`, select_one, required): Choose whether the sponsor may share your social media content.
* **Sponsor May Use Performance Footage or Audio (13)** (`performance_rights`, select_one, required): Choose the rights to recordings of your performances.
* **Geographic Scope of Rights (15)** (`geographic_scope`, select_one, required): Specify where the sponsorship materials may be used (e.g., worldwide, specific country, etc.).
* **Duration of Sponsorship Rights (16)** (`duration_of_rights`, select_one, required): Specify how long the sponsorship rights are granted for.
* **Sponsorship End Date (17)** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the sponsorship period ends.
* **Is This an Exclusive Sponsorship? (19)** (`exclusive_sponsorship`, select_one, required): Choose whether this is an exclusive sponsorship.
* **Explicitly Excluded Uses (20)** (`excluded_uses`, text, optional): Specify any uses that are not permitted.
* **Additional Compensation for Rights (22)** (`additional_compensation`, select_one, optional): Choose whether there is any additional compensation or royalties.
* **Artist Consent (24)** (`artist_consent`, select_one, required): Confirm that you consent to the terms and rights outlined above.
* **Date of Consent (25)** (`signature_date`, date, required): Sign and date the form, indicating your consent.
