# Waxing Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Waxing Waiver Form is a document that confirms a client's consent to undergo waxing services. It is essential to complete this form accurately to ensure that the client's consent is obtained and that they are aware of the risks and benefits associated with waxing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary information ready, such as the client's name, email, and phone number.
2. Fill in the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
3. Enter the client's email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter the client's phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Review and submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Name (Required)
* **Client Name (Client Name, text, required):** Enter the client's full name as it appears on their identification documents.

### Email (Email, required)
* **Email (Email, required):** Enter the client's valid email address where they can be reached.

### Phone Number (Phone Number, required)
* **Phone Number (Phone Number, required):** Enter the client's phone number where they can be reached during the waxing process.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submission to ensure all required fields are filled in accurately.
* If the client has any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to them.
* The form must be completed by the client in their own handwriting or by typing on a computer. A digital signature is not accepted.
