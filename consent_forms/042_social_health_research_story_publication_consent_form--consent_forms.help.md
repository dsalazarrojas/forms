# Social Health Research Story Publication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain consent from participants for the publication of their research stories.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the participant's permission for publication (page 1).
	* Choose whether you want to be published anonymously or personally identified.
2. Specify the purpose of the publication (page 2).
	* Choose the purpose of the publication (research, education, or social media).
3. Select the medium of the publication (page 3).
	* Choose whether the story will be published in print, online, or in-person events.
4. Specify the participant's rights (page 4).
	* Choose whether you have exclusive, non-exclusive, or participant-only rights.
5. Select the timeframe for the publication (page 5).
	* Choose when you would like to publish the story (immediate, 1 year, or 5 years).
6. Provide your participant information (page 6-7).
	* Enter your name and email address.
	* (Optional) Enter your phone number.
7. Enter your research story (page 8).
8. Specify the purpose of your research story (page 9).
	* Choose why you are submitting your research story (academic, personal, or professional development).
9. Specify the anonymity of your research story (page 10).
	* Choose whether you want to be published anonymously or personally identified.
10. Specify the rights of your research story (page 11).
	* Choose whether you have exclusive, non-exclusive, or participant-only rights.
11. Select the timeframe for your research story (page 12).
	* Choose when you would like to publish your story (immediate, 1 year, or 5 years).
12. Provide your consent to publication (page 13).
	* Choose whether you consent to publication.
13. (Optional) Provide additional comments or notes (page 14-17).
	* Enter any additional notes or comments you would like to provide.
