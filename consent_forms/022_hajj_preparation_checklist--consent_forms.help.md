# Hajj Preparation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect necessary information from pilgrims and their group leaders to prepare for a safe and successful Hajj experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
- Review each field carefully and fill out any information that applies to your Hajj preparations.
- Ensure that all required fields are completed as indicated.
- Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Pilgrimage Dates** (`pilgrimage_dates`, date, optional): Enter the dates of your Hajj pilgrimage. If you're not sure of the exact dates, you can select "After Hajj starts" or "Before Hajj starts" accordingly.
- **Travel Plans** (`travel_plans`, text, optional): Describe your travel plans, including your mode of transportation, destination, and other essential details.
- **Accommodations** (`accommodations`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the type of accommodation you'll be using during your Hajj, such as Hotel, Hostel, Family, or Other.
- **Group Details** (`group_details`, text, optional): Provide details about your group, including its size and leadership structure.
- **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact person, someone who can be reached in case of an emergency.
- **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_one, optional): If you have any medical conditions that may affect your Hajj experience, please select "Yes" to indicate this.
- **Medical Certification** (`medical_certification`, note, optional): If you have any medical certification or documentation, you can attach it here.
- **Travel Documents** (`travel_documents`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the types of travel documents you'll need for your Hajj, such as Valid passport, Visa, ID card, or Other.
- **Flight Details** (`flight_details`, text, optional): Enter any relevant information about your flight, including flight number if necessary.
- **Travel Insurance** (`travel_insurance`, select_one, optional): If you have travel insurance, select "Yes" to indicate this.
- **Travel Documents Upload** (`travel_documents_upload`, text, optional): Upload scanned copies of your travel documents, such as your passport and visa.
- **Flight Number** (`flight_number`, text, optional): Enter your flight number.
- **Flight Arrival** (`flight_arrival`, time, optional): Enter your expected time of arrival.
- **Hajj Dates** (`hajj_dates`, select_multiple, optional): Select when you plan to arrive at Hajj, considering Before Hajj starts, After Hajj starts, or At Hajj.
- **Accommodation Cost** (`accommodation_cost`, number, optional): Enter the cost of your accommodation.
- **Group Size** (`group_size`, number, optional): Enter the number of people in your group.
- **Group Leadership Structure** (`group_leadership_structure`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the leadership structure of your group, such as Flat or Hierarchical.
- **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
- **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
- **Group Leader Name** (`group_leader_name`, text, optional): Enter your group leader's name.
