# Reading List Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reading List Release Form is designed to collect information from students about their reading lists, which is used to facilitate the process of obtaining necessary permissions and release information. This form is a crucial part of the educational workflow and helps to maintain transparency and accountability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "User Info" field with your name and other relevant details.
2. Provide your authorization in the "authorization" field, which is a written confirmation of your consent to share your reading list information.
3. Sign your name in the "signature" field, which serves as a physical confirmation of your authorization.
4. If required, upload any relevant files in the "file_upload" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info**: Enter your name and any other relevant details to identify yourself.
* **authorization**: Write a brief statement confirming that you have read and understand the terms of the form, and that you are willing to share your reading list information.
* **signature**: Sign your name to physically confirm your authorization.
* **file_upload**: If necessary, upload any supporting files, such as a scanned copy of your ID or a photograph of your book list. 

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any section, please ask your teacher or instructor for clarification.
* The information collected from this form will be kept confidential and used solely for educational purposes.
