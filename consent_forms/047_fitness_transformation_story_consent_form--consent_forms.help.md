# Fitness Transformation Story Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Transformation Story Consent Form is used to collect information about a client's personal details, transformation story, and consent to share this information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please read the form carefully and ensure you understand what information is being collected.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your consent to share your story and information with the assigned staff member.
3. Fill out your name, phone number (if applicable), and email address (if applicable) accurately and truthfully.
4. Write your transformation story in the "Client Story" field.
5. Select a "Client Success Story" date that reflects the date your transformation took place.
6. Indicate who the story will be assigned to.
7. Choose a "Assigned Date" for when the story will be assigned to the selected staff member.
8. Ensure you have filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Consent** (`client consent`, select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" to indicate you consent to share your transformation story and information with the assigned staff member. This will ensure that your story is reviewed and used as intended.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached for further questions or follow-up.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further questions or follow-up.
* **Client Story** (`client story`, note, required): Write a detailed account of your transformation story, including any challenges you faced and how you overcame them.
* **Client Success Story** (`client_story`, date, required): Select the date your transformation took place. If the date is not known, please select the current date.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required): Indicate who the story will be assigned to, i.e., which staff member will be responsible for your transformation story.
* **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`, time, required): Choose the date and time when the story will be assigned to the selected staff member.
