# Safety And Consent Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Safety And Consent Checklist form is used to gather necessary contact information and ensure the individual has given their consent for certain procedures or treatments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information in the fields on the first page, including email, phone number, name, address, city, state, and country.
2. Review and select the declaration of consent on the second page to confirm your agreement or disagreement.
3. Provide any additional comments you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
### 2. **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
### 3. **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address for communication.
### 4. **Address** (`address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your address for contact purposes.
### 5. **City** (`city`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the city where you reside or work.
### 6. **State** (`state`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the state where you reside or work.
### 7. **Country** (`country`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the country where you reside or work.
### 8. **Declaration of Consent** (`declaration_of_consent`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose how you intend to proceed with the consent.
### 9. **Consent** (`consent`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select how you intend to proceed with the consent.
### 10. **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or information about your consent.
### 11. **Another Declaration of Consent** (`consent`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Another opportunity to confirm your consent status. 

Note: The fields on this form are optional, but please provide accurate and relevant information to ensure proper communication and consent procedures are followed.
