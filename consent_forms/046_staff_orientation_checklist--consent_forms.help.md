# Staff Orientation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Staff Orientation Checklist is a comprehensive form designed to ensure that new staff members have a thorough understanding of their role and responsibilities, emergency contact information, and other essential details related to their employment. This guide will walk you through each section of the form, helping you to complete it accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by ensuring you have all the necessary information and documentation ready, including your staff ID, name, job title, department, and supervisor's name.
2. Fill in the staff policies section by selecting the relevant options that apply to your role and responsibilities. This section is required.
3. Complete the staff training section by providing any relevant details or certifications.
4. Enter your emergency contact information, including your emergency contact's name and contact details. This section is required.
5. Enter your work hours and shift details.
6. Update your staff status as per your current employment status (Active or Inactive).
7. Complete any additional notes or comments if necessary.
8. Review and update the review date and time if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Staff ID** (`staff_id`): Enter your unique staff ID number.
* **Staff Name** (`staff_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`): Enter your job title as per your role and responsibilities.
* **Department** (`department`): Enter the name of your department.
* **Supervisor** (`supervisor`): Enter the name of your direct supervisor.
* **Location** (`location`): Enter the location where you will be working.
* **Shift** (`shift`): Enter your shift details (e.g., morning, afternoon, night).
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`): Enter your regular working hours.
* **Staff Status** (`staff_status`): Select your current employment status (Active or Inactive).
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`): Enter the name and contact details of your emergency contact person.
* **Sign Date** (`sign_date`): Enter the date you completed the orientation.
* **Sign Time** (`sign_time`): Enter the time you completed the orientation. (Note: This field is not required.)
* **Signature** (`signature`): Sign and date the orientation document to confirm completion.
* **Staff Policies 1** (`staff_policies`): Select all the policies that apply to your role and responsibilities.
* **Staff Policies 2** (`staff_policies_2`): Select all the additional policies that apply to your role and responsibilities.
* **Staff Training** (`staff_training`): Enter any relevant staff training details or certifications.
* **Staff Training 2** (`staff_training_2`): Enter any additional staff training details or certifications.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`): Enter the date of the last review (if applicable).
* **Review Time** (`review_time`): Enter the time of the last review (if applicable).
* **Reviewer** (`reviewer`): Enter the name of the last reviewer (if applicable).
* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional notes or comments if necessary.
