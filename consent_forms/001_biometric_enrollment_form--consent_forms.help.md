# Biometric Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Biometric Enrollment Form is designed to collect personal and biometric data from individuals for specific purposes such as security, convenience, or identification. This data will be used for authentication, verification, or other authorized purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal information:
	* Enter your full name in the "Full name" field.
	* Enter your date of birth in the "Date of birth" field in YYYY-MM-DD format.
	* Enter your email address in the "Email address" field for notifications and enrollment confirmation.
	* If applicable, enter your organization identifier or employee id in the "Organization identifier or employee id" field.
2. Select your biometric data type:
	* Choose one of the available biometric modality options (fingerprint, facial recognition, iris scan, voiceprint, or palm vein).
3. Specify collection location and data retention period:
	* Enter the location where the biometric capture will take place in the "Collection location" field.
	* Choose the data retention period from the available options (6 months, 1 year, 3 years, or indefinite).
4. Provide consent and purpose:
	* Read and agree to the consent statement in the "Consent to collect biometric data" field.
	* Briefly describe the purpose for which the data will be used in the "Purpose of collection" field.
5. Review and acknowledge:
	* Read the privacy acknowledgment in the "Privacy and acknowledgment" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name**: Enter your full name as per your legal identification documents.
* **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Email address**: Enter your email address for notifications and enrollment confirmation.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number, including country code if outside local area. (Optional)
* **Organization identifier or employee id**: Enter your organization identifier or employee id if applicable.
* **Biometric data type**: Choose one of the available biometric modality options (fingerprint, facial recognition, iris scan, voiceprint, or palm vein).
* **Collection location**: Enter the location where the biometric capture will take place.
* **Data retention period**: Choose the data retention period from the available options (6 months, 1 year, 3 years, or indefinite).
* **Consent to collect biometric data**: Read and agree to the consent statement.
* **Purpose of collection**: Briefly describe the purpose for which the data will be used.
* **Privacy and Acknowledgment**: Read and acknowledge the privacy statement by signing. 

Please review the form carefully and ensure all fields are completed accurately to avoid any issues with enrollment.
