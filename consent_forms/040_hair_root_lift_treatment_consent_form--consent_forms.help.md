# Hair Root Lift Treatment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from clients who are interested in undergoing the Hair Root Lift treatment. It is intended to inform clients about the treatment and obtain their consent before proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your health details to ensure that the treatment is suitable for you.
2. Select your preferred appointment schedule for the treatment.
3. Confirm that you understand and consent to the Hair Root Lift treatment.
4. Specify the services you would like to receive during the treatment.
5. Sign below to confirm that you have read and understand the consent statement.
6. Enter your information and payment details, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Health Details** (client_health_details, text, optional): Please enter any health concerns or medical conditions that may impact the treatment.
* **Appointment Schedule** (appointment_schedule, text, optional): Choose a date and time for your appointment or enter a specific date and time that suits you.
* **Consent** (consent, text, optional): Check the box to indicate that you understand and consent to the Hair Root Lift treatment. 
* **Root Lift Services** (root_lift_services, text, optional): Select the services you would like to receive during the treatment.
* **Signed Consent** (signed_consent, text, optional): Please sign below to confirm that you have read and understand the consent statement.
* **Client Information** (client_information, text, optional): Enter your contact information for follow-up purposes.
* **Payment Details** (payment_details, text, optional): Provide your payment information if applicable. 

## Tips
Please ensure that you carefully read and understand each section of the form before submitting it. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us for clarification.
