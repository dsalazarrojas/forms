# medical_equipment_distribution_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Equipment Distribution Consent Form is a document used to obtain consent from patients for the distribution of medical equipment. This form is necessary for healthcare providers to ensure that patients understand and agree to the terms of equipment distribution. It is a legal requirement for medical professionals to obtain informed consent from patients before providing them with equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. **Enter the patient's name** (`patient_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the patient.
2. **Enter the provider's name** (`provider_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the provider (e.g., doctor, nurse, etc.).
3. **Describe the equipment** (`equipment_description`, text, required: false): Describe the medical equipment being distributed.
4. **Choose the distribution method** (`distribution_method`, select multiple, required: true): Select the method through which the equipment will be distributed (e.g., in-person, phone, email, mail).
5. **Review and agree to terms and conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, required: true): Review the terms and conditions of equipment distribution and agree to them.
6. **Sign and date the consent** (`consent_date`, date, required: true): Date and sign the consent form to indicate agreement.
7. **Choose the provider's signature type** (`provider_signature`, select one, required: true): Select the type of provider signing the form (e.g., physician, nurse practitioner, mid-level provider, etc.).
8. **Indicate patient's consent** (`patient_signature`, select one, required: true): Indicate whether the patient has consented to the equipment distribution.
9. **Upload signature image** (`signature_image`, select one, required: true): Indicate whether a signature image will be uploaded or not.
10. **Date the signed consent** (`date_signed`, date, required: true): Date the signed consent form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`): Enter the name of the patient receiving the medical equipment.
* **Provider Name** (`provider_name`): Enter the name of the healthcare professional providing the equipment.
* **Equipment Description** (`equipment_description`): Describe the equipment being distributed, including its type and any specific details.
* **Distribution Method** (`distribution_method`): Select the method through which the equipment will be distributed, such as in-person, phone, email, or mail.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`): Review and agree to the terms and conditions of equipment distribution.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`): Date and sign the consent form to indicate agreement.
* **Provider Signature** (`provider_signature`): Select the type of provider signing the form (e.g., physician, nurse practitioner, mid-level provider, etc.).
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`): Indicate whether the patient has consented to the equipment distribution.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`): Date the signed consent form.
* **Signature Image** (`signature_image`): Indicate whether a signature image will be uploaded or not.

## Tips
* Ensure that the patient's name is spelled correctly to avoid any miscommunication.
* Make sure to choose the correct distribution method for the equipment being provided.
* Review the terms and conditions carefully before signing the consent form.
* Date and sign the consent form accurately.
* Ensure that the provider's signature type is correct.
