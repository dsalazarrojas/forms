# Permission To Test Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain permission from participants to test a product or service. It's essential to provide clear and concise information to ensure that participants understand the purpose and scope of the testing process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Participant Details** section with the participant's name and any other relevant information.
2. Check the **Agreement** checkbox to confirm that you have read and understand the terms and conditions of the testing process.
3. Provide your **Test Availability** in the format MM/DD/YYYY, indicating when you are available to take part in the test.
4. Enter your **Participant Contact** information, including your phone number.
5. Add any additional **Additional Comments** or notes about your participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Details**: Enter the participant's name and any other relevant details about them.
  - **Agreement**: Check this box to confirm that you have read and understand the terms and conditions of the testing process.
  - **Test Availability**: Enter the date (MM/DD/YYYY) when you are available to participate in the test.
  - **Participant Contact**: Enter your phone number so that we can contact you for further information.
  - **Additional Comments**: Add any additional notes or comments about your participation.

## Tips
- Please make sure to complete this form accurately and thoroughly.
- Review the form before submitting it to ensure that all necessary information is provided.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us for further assistance.
