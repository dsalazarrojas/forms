# Survivor Story Sharing Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who are willing to share their personal stories related to their experience as a survivor of a traumatic event. The form is part of the organization's efforts to collect and share these stories in a safe and respectful manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand the purpose and the sharing preferences you have.
2. Fill in the general information section with your name, email address, and any other relevant details you'd like to provide.
3. Choose your sharing preferences from the options provided in the sharing preferences section.
4. If you have any additional details you'd like to share about your sharing preferences, please use the sharing details section.
5. Finally, type out your story in the story section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information (1)** (`general_info`, text, optional): Please enter your name and email address so we can contact you if necessary.
* **Sharing Preferences (2)** (`sharing_preferences`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to share your story, either with the organization, with others, or not at all.
* **Sharing Details (3)** (`sharing_details`, text, optional): If you chose to share your story, provide any additional details about how you'd like your story to be shared (e.g., anonymously, with attribution, etc.).
* **Your Story (4)** (`story`, note, optional): Share your personal story in the space provided. Be as detailed or brief as you like, but please respect the boundaries and guidelines provided.
