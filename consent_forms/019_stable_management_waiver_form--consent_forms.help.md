# Stable Management Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stable Management Waiver Form is a document that you are required to fill out before participating in certain activities, such as riding a horse. It's a crucial step to ensure your safety and the safety of others.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill in your personal information on pages 1 and 2.
- Select any medical conditions you have on page 3.
- Choose your emergency contact relationship on page 5.
- Fill in your medical declaration on page 9.
- Sign the form on page 11.
- Fill in your date of birth on page 12.
- Fill in your rider agreement on page 13.
- Fill in your emergency declaration on page 14.
- Sign the form on page 15.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Information** (Page 1, Required): Please fill in your name, address, and contact information.
- **Contact Information** (Page 2, Required): Fill in your contact information, including your phone number and email.
- **Emergency Contact** (Page 3, Required): Enter the name and relationship of your emergency contact.
- **Medical Conditions** (Page 4, Required): Select any medical conditions you have, such as allergies or asthma.
- **Rider Agreement** (Page 6, Optional): If you have any questions or concerns, please fill them in here.
- **Stable Agreement** (Page 7, Optional): If you have any questions or concerns, please fill them in here.
- **Medical Declaration** (Page 8, Required): Fill in your medical declaration, including any medical alerts or conditions.
- **Signature** (Page 10, Required): Sign your name to confirm that you have read and understood the form.
- **Date of Birth** (Page 11, Required): Fill in your date of birth.
- **Rider Agreement Text** (Page 12, Optional): If you have any questions or concerns, please fill them in here.
- **Emergency Declaration** (Page 13, Required): Fill in your emergency declaration, including any medical alerts or conditions.
- **Signature Date** (Page 14, Required): Fill in the date you signed the form.
- **Rider Agreement 2** (Page 15, Optional): If you have any questions or concerns, please fill them in here.

- **Rider Agreement 3** (Page 17, Optional): If you have any questions or concerns, please fill them in here.
- **Emergency Contact Information** (Page 18, Optional): Fill in your emergency contact's information.
- **Medical Info** (Page 19, Optional): Fill in any medical information you want to provide.
