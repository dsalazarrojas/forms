# Wisconsin COVID-19 Hardship Attestation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for individuals to provide a self-attestation that they are experiencing hardship due to the COVID-19 pandemic. It is used to demonstrate their understanding and agreement with specific conditions related to financial assistance programs.

## How To Complete This Form

Before starting the form, ensure you have all required information and documentation ready. This form is divided into 14 sections, each with a specific purpose:

1. **Agency Logo**: This section is informational and displays the logo of the agency providing the form. Please note that this section is not a required field.

2. **Attestation Form**: Here, you can enter a statement that describes your hardship situation due to COVID-19. Please provide as much detail as possible to help the agency understand your situation.

3. **Attestations Title**: Enter a concise title of your hardship situation, briefly describing the nature of your hardship.

4. **Attestations Date**: Enter the date you experienced the hardship, which should be in a standard date format (DD/MM/YYYY).

5. **Attestations Time**: If applicable, enter the exact time you experienced the hardship, using 24-hour format (HH:MM).

6. **Attestations Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID or other official documents.

7. **Attestations Address**: Enter your current address, which should match the address on your government-issued ID or other official documents.

8. **Attestations State**: Enter your state of residence.

9. **Attestations Zip**: Enter your zip code.

10. **Attestations City**: Enter your city of residence.

11. **Attestations Phone**: Enter your phone number.

12. **Attestations Email**: Enter your email address.

13. **Attestations Comments**: If you have any additional comments or information about your hardship situation, enter them here.

14. **Attestations Signature**: Select one of the two options provided:
   - If you have read, understand, and agree with the terms and conditions, select "I have read, understand, and agree."
   - If you do not agree with the terms and conditions, select "Other." You will be able to provide further explanation or justification for your selection in the comments field above.
   - Note: Selecting "Other" is not recommended as it may delay or affect the evaluation of your claim.

15. **Attestations Agreement**: Choose "True" if you agree with the terms and conditions of this form. This is a confirmation that you understand and accept the terms provided.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely to avoid delays or errors in processing your claim.
- Please review each field carefully before submitting the form to avoid misunderstandings.
- If you need assistance or clarification on any section, do not hesitate to contact the agency providing this form.
