# Therapy Data Retention Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain informed consent from clients for the retention of their therapy data. It is essential to complete this form carefully and accurately to ensure that clients understand how their data will be stored and used.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully to understand what information is being asked.
* Fill in the required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Form Title** (form_title, text, not required)
A title for the form that identifies it.

* A descriptive title for the form is helpful for organization and easy identification.
* You can leave this field blank if not necessary.

### **Client Name** (client_name, text, not required)
The client's name.

* Enter the client's full name.
* This field is not required, but filling it in is helpful for record-keeping purposes.

### **Client Phone** (client_phone, text, not required)
The client's phone number.

* Enter the client's phone number.
* This field is not required, but filling it in is helpful for contacting the client.

### **Client Email** (client_email, email, not required)
The client's email address.

* Enter the client's email address.
* This field is not required, but filling it in is helpful for communication purposes.

### **Client Address** (client_address, text, not required)
The client's address.

* Enter the client's address, including street, city, and state.
* This field is not required, but filling it in is helpful for record-keeping purposes.

### **Form Category** (form_category, select_one, not required)
The category of the form.

* Choose a category from the options provided (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* This field is not required, but selecting a category helps to organize the form.

### **Form Subcategory** (form_subcategory, text, not required)
A subcategory for the form.

* Enter a subcategory for the form, if desired.
* This field is not required, but filling it in is helpful for further organization.

### **Form Description** (form_description, text, not required)
A brief description of the form.

* Enter a short description of the form.
* This field is not required, but filling it in is helpful for clarity.

### **Therapist Name** (therapist_name, text, not required)
The name of the therapist.

* Enter the name of the therapist.
* This field is not required, but filling it in is helpful for record-keeping purposes.

### **Practice Name** (practice_name, text, not required)
The name of the practice.

* Enter the name of the practice.
* This field is not required, but filling it in is helpful for record-keeping purposes.

### **Form Date** (form_date, date, not required)
The date the form was completed.

* Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YY.
* This field is not required, but filling it in is helpful for record-keeping purposes.

### **Form Time** (form_time, time, not required)
The time the form was completed.

* Enter the time in the format HH:MM.
* This field is not required, but filling it in is helpful for record-keeping purposes.

### **Form Note** (form_note, note, not required)
A note about the form.

* Enter any additional information about the form.
* This field is not required, but filling it in is helpful for reference.

### **Form Email** (form_email, email, not required)
The email address for the form.

* Enter the email address for the form.
* This field is not required, but filling it in is helpful for communication purposes.

### **Form Phone** (form_phone, text, not required)
The phone number for the form.

* Enter the phone number for the form.
* This field is not required, but filling it in is helpful for contact purposes.

### **Form Address** (form_address, text, not required)
The address for the form.

* Enter the address for the form.
* This field is not required, but filling it in is helpful for record-keeping purposes.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields to ensure accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you need assistance or clarification, consult with the form administrator or responsible party.
* Remember that this form is for informed consent purposes only; it is not for any other purpose, such as treatment planning or therapy sessions.
