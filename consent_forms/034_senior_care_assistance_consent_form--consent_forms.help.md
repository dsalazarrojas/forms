# Senior Care Assistance Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to capture information for senior care assistance, including contact details, service dates and hours, and caregiver relationship, to ensure that the necessary support is provided efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the contact details:
    *   **First Name**: Enter the first name of the individual seeking senior care assistance.
    *   **Last Name**: Enter the last name of the individual seeking senior care assistance.
    *   **Email**: Enter the email address of the individual seeking senior care assistance.
    *   **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the individual seeking senior care assistance.
    *   **Street Address**: Enter the street address of the individual seeking senior care assistance.
    *   **Address 2**: Enter any additional address information.
    *   **City**: Enter the city where the individual seeking senior care assistance resides.
    *   **State**: Enter the state where the individual seeking senior care assistance resides.
    *   **Zip**: Enter the zip code of the individual seeking senior care assistance.
2.  Select the type of service required:
    *   **Type of Service**: Choose the type of senior care service required, such as **Senior Care Provider**, **Home Health Agency**, or **Other**.
3.  Provide service dates and hours:
    *   **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the senior care service.
    *   **End Date**: Enter the end date of the senior care service.
    *   **Hours of Service**: Enter the hours of the senior care service.
4.  Confirm the caregiver relationship:
    *   **Caregiver Relationship**: Choose the relationship of the caregiver, such as **Family Member**, **Family Friend**, or **Other**.
    *   **Caregiver Name**: Enter the name of the caregiver.
5.  Provide provider information:
    *   **Provider Name**: Enter the name of the senior care provider.
    *   **Provider Contact**: Enter the contact information of the senior care provider.
6.  Sign and acknowledge:
    *   **Date**: Enter the date of signature.
    *   **Time**: Enter the time of signature.
    *   **Signature**: Sign the form to confirm.
    *   **Acknowledgement**: Acknowledge that you have reviewed and agree to the terms.
7.  Finalize the approval:
    *   **Approval Status**: Choose the status of approval, such as **Not Approved**, or **Approved**.
    *   **Approval Date**: Enter the date of approval.
    *   **Approval Time**: Enter the time of approval.
    *   **Approval Note**: Enter any additional approval notes.
