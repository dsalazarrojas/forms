# Window Cleaning Ambassador Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Window Cleaning Ambassador Agreement is a document that outlines the terms and conditions for individuals who wish to become Window Cleaning Ambassadors. The form is used to gather essential details about the individual, including their contact information, role, and agreement terms. By completing this form, the individual is acknowledging that they understand and agree to the terms and conditions set forth in the agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Select your role from the "Role" field.
5. Provide a brief description of your responsibilities in the "Responsibilities" field.
6. Enter your onboarding date in the "Onboarding Date" field.
7. Enter your contract start date in the "Contract Start Date" field.
8. Enter your contract end date in the "Contract End Date" field.
9. Sign your name in the "Signature" field.
10. Read and agree to the "Agreement Terms" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Ambassador Details**: Enter a brief description of yourself as a Window Cleaning Ambassador.
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it should be displayed on the agreement document.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Role**: Select one of the available roles:
	+ Cleaning Ambassador
	+ Support Ambassador
	+ Sales Ambassador
* **Responsibilities**: Provide a brief description of your responsibilities as a Window Cleaning Ambassador.
* **Onboarding Date**: Enter the date you were onboarded as a Window Cleaning Ambassador.
* **Contract Start Date**: Enter the start date of your contract as a Window Cleaning Ambassador.
* **Contract End Date**: Enter the end date of your contract as a Window Cleaning Ambassador.
* **Signature**: Sign your name to acknowledge that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions of the agreement.
* **Agreement Terms**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the agreement by signing your name in this field.
