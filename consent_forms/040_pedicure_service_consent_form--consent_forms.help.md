# pedicure_service_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain informed consent from clients for pedicure services. It's essential to complete this form accurately and truthfully.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out each field completely and accurately.
2.  Review the form before submitting to ensure you've provided all necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Pedicure Service Consent Form Page 1 (1)** (`Pedicure Service Consent Form`, text, not required): This is the first page of the form where you can briefly introduce yourself or the pedicure service you're inquiring about.
*   **Pedicure Services Provided (2)** (`Services`, select_multiple, required): Select all pedicure services you're interested in or currently receiving. Choose from the following options: 
    \- service 1
    \- service 2
*   **Client Concerns and Questions (3)** (`Concerns and Questions`, text, not required): If you have any concerns or questions regarding the pedicure service, please describe them here.
*   **Client Agreement to Services (4)** (`Agree or Decline`, select_one, required): Select 'Agree' if you're comfortable with the selected pedicure services. Select 'Decline' if you're not.
*   **Contact Information (5)** (`Email`, email, not required): Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding the pedicure service.
