# Event Photography Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Photography Contract is a legal document that outlines the terms and conditions of a photography event. It ensures that both the client and photographer are on the same page regarding the event's scope, timeline, and image usage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information about the event.
2. Select the correct options for the photo release and photo deliverables.
3. Ensure that the event date and time are correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Photography Contract** (1, text, required): This is the title of the contract.
* **Photographer** (2, text, optional): Enter the name of the photographer who will be capturing the event.
* **Client** (3, text, optional): Enter the name of the client who will be receiving the photography services.
* **Contact** (4, text, optional): Enter the contact information of the client or photographer.
* **Email** (5, email, optional): Enter the email address of the client or photographer.
* **Photo Release** (6, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' to grant permission for the photographer to use the images taken during the event, or 'No' to restrict usage.
* **Photo Deliverables** (7, select_multiple, optional): Select the deliverables you require, such as edited and unedited photos, prints, or digital copies.
* **Event Details** (8, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the event, such as the event theme or description.
* **Event Date** (9, date, optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (10, time, optional): Enter the time of the event.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are accurately filled out to avoid errors.
* Review the form carefully before submitting.
* Store the completed contract as a reference for future use.
