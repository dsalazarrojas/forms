# MRI Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The MRI Screening Form is used to gather information from patients to ensure their safety during MRI screening procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. If you have any allergies, medical conditions, or other relevant medical history, list them in the provided fields.
3. Select the correct options for the "Do you have any allergies?" and "Medications/Conditions" questions.
4. Enter your date of birth, insurance provider, and medical devices used in the form provided.
5. Provide detailed information in the "Medical History" and "Medical Condition History" fields.
6. List any other medical devices, medications, or procedures you have had.
7. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Allergies List", "Medications List", "Other Allergies", and "Other Medications List" questions.
8. Enter the type and time of the MRI screening.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **MRI Screening Form**: This is the title of the form.
* **Patient Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Medical History**: Provide a detailed list of your previous medical history.
* **Do you have any allergies?**: Answer "Yes" if you have any allergies, "No" otherwise.
* **Medications/Conditions**: List any medical conditions you have, such as diabetes, high blood pressure, etc.
* **Insurance Provider**: Enter your insurance provider name and ID number.
* **Medical Condition History**: Provide a detailed list of your medical condition history.
* **Allergies**: Answer "Yes" or "No" if you have any allergies, such as peanuts, shellfish, etc.
* **Medications**: Answer "Yes" or "No" if you are currently taking any medications.
* **Medical Devices**: List any other medical devices you use, such as pacemakers, etc.
* **Medical Condition**: Enter any other medical conditions you have.
* **Surgical Procedures**: Enter any surgical procedures you have had.
* **Date of MRI Screening**: Enter the date of the MRI screening.
* **Type of MRI Screening**: Enter the type of MRI screening you are undergoing.
* **Time of MRI Screening**: Enter the time of the MRI screening.
* **Other Medical History**: Provide any other medical history not listed above.
* **Other Medical Devices**: List any other medical devices you use.
* **Other Medications**: List any other medications you take.
* **Other Allergies**: Answer "Yes" or "No" if you have any other allergies.
* **Other Medications List**: Answer "Yes" or "No" if you are taking any other medications.
* **Other Procedure**: Enter any other procedures you have had.
* **Other Med Device**: List any other medical devices you use.
* **Other Medications**: List any other medications you take.
* **Other Allergies**: Answer "Yes" or "No" if you have any other allergies.
* **Other Procedure**: Enter any other procedures you have had.

Note: Please be accurate and thorough in your answers. This information will help ensure your safety during the MRI screening process.
