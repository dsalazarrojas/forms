# Coaching Session Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form outlines the essential details of a coaching session, ensuring clarity and agreement between the coaching team and client. It covers coaching team information, client contact details, and session specifics, including type, date, time, duration, format, and fee.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Review the form carefully and ensure all fields are filled out accurately.
*   Enter the coach's and client's names, emails, and phone numbers.
*   Choose the session type, date, time, and duration.
*   Select the format for the session (in person, video call, phone call).
*   Choose the session fee range.
*   Select the payment due date.
*   Describe the goals for the session.
*   Choose the cancellation terms and required notice period.
*   Understand and agree to late cancellation fees.
*   Acknowledge the confidentiality and recording policies.
*   Agree to participate fully and honestly.
*   Acknowledge the medical disclaimer and liability waiver.
*   Finally, confirm agreement to all terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Coach Full Name** (`coach_name`, text, required): Enter the coach's legal name.
*   **Coach Email** (`coach_email`, email, required): Enter the coach's contact email address.
*   **Coach Phone** (`coach_phone`, text, required): Enter the coach's contact phone number.
*   **Client Full Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the client's legal name.
*   **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, required): Enter the client's contact email address.
*   **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, required): Enter the client's contact phone number.
*   **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, date, required): Enter the current date.
*   **Type of Session** (`session_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of coaching session (e.g., initial consultation, regular coaching session, follow-up session).
*   **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the session.
*   **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the session.
*   **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, select_one, required): Choose the session length (e.g., 30 minutes, 45 minutes, 60 minutes, 90 minutes, 120 minutes).
*   **Session Format** (`session_format`, select_one, required): Choose the format for the session (e.g., in person, video call, phone call).
*   **Session Fee** (`session_fee`, select_one, required): Choose the session fee range (e.g., $50-$100, $101-$200, $201-$300, $301-$500, over $500, included in package).
*   **Payment Due Date** (`payment_due`, select_one, required): Choose the payment due date (e.g., before session, day of session, after session, end of month).
*   **Goals for This Session** (`session_goals`, text, required): Describe the client's goals for the session.
*   **Cancellation Terms Understood** (`cancellation_terms`, select_one, required): Confirm understanding of the cancellation terms.
*   **Required Cancellation Notice** (`cancellation_notice`, select_one, required): Choose the required cancellation notice period (e.g., 12 hours, 24 hours, 48 hours, 72 hours).
*   **Late Cancellation Fee Applies** (`late_cancellation_fee`, select_one, required): Confirm understanding of late cancellation fees.
*   **Confidentiality Terms Accepted** (`confidentiality_terms`, select_one, required): Confirm acceptance of confidentiality terms.
*   **Recording Policy** (`recording_policy`, select_one, required): Choose the recording policy (e.g., not allowed, allowed with consent, coach records only, client may record).
*   **I Commit to Full Participation** (`participation_commitment`, select_one, required): Confirm commitment to full participation.
*   **I Commit to Honesty and Openness** (`honesty_commitment`, select_one, required): Confirm commitment to honesty and openness.
*   **Medical Disclaimer Acknowledged** (`medical_disclaimer`, select_one, required): Confirm understanding of the medical disclaimer.
*   **Liability Waiver Signed** (`liability_waiver`, select_one, required): Confirm signing of the liability waiver.
*   **Client Agrees to Session Terms** (`client_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm client agreement to session terms.
*   **Coach Agrees to Session Terms** (`coach_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm coach agreement to session terms.
*   **Date of Agreement** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the current date.
