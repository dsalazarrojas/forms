# Art Gallery Visit Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Gallery Visit Permission Form is designed to collect information necessary for a student's visit to the art gallery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form with the requested information, including the student's name, grade level, contact information, and details about the visit.
2. Select the mode of transportation for the student's visit.
3. Indicate the educational purpose of the visit.
4. Specify the time of departure from school and the expected return time.
5. Grant permission for your child to attend the visit and provide photo permission, as well as medical treatment permission.
6. Sign and date the form with your full name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Date**: Date of permission form (Date of the form, required).
* **Student Name**: Full name of student (Required).
* **Student Grade**: Grade level (Required).
* **Student ID**: Student identification number (Optional).
* **Parent Guardian Name**: Full name of parent or guardian (Required).
* **Parent Guardian Email**: Email address (Required).
* **Parent Guardian Phone**: Contact phone number (Required).
* **Home Phone**: Alternative contact number (Optional).
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Name of emergency contact (Required).
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Emergency contact phone (Required).
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Relationship to student (Required).
* **Gallery Name**: Name of gallery to visit (Required).
* **Gallery Address**: Address of gallery (Required).
* **Visit Date**: Date of planned visit (Required).
* **Visit Purpose**: Educational purpose of visit (Required).
* **Departure Time**: Time of departure from school (Required).
* **Return Time**: Expected return time (Required).
* **Transportation Method**: Mode of transportation (Required).
* **Teacher Name**: Name of supervising teacher (Required).
* **Teacher Phone**: Teacher contact number (Required).
* **Chaperone Names**: Names of accompanying chaperones (Optional).
* **Medical Conditions**: Any medical conditions to note (Optional).
* **Medications**: Any medications student is taking (Optional).
* **Allergies**: Any allergies (Optional).
* **Dietary Restrictions**: Any dietary restrictions (Optional).
* **Permission Granted**: Grant permission for your child to attend (Required).
* **Photo Permission**: Allow photos of your child for educational purposes (Required).
* **Medical Treatment Permission**: Authorize emergency medical treatment if needed (Required).
* **Parent Guardian Signature**: Type your full name as signature (Required).
* **Signature Date**: Date of signature (Required).
