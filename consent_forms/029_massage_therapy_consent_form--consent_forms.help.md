# Massage Therapy Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a consent form for massage therapy sessions, used to obtain informed consent from clients and therapists before, during, and after each session. It collects various information including client and therapist details, session notes, and consents.

## How To Complete This Form
Please complete this form carefully and thoroughly to ensure accurate information is recorded for your massage therapy session. Follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's details, including name, date of birth, and contact information.
2. Select the massage type, area of focus, and technique for the session.
3. Provide informed consent for the massage session.
4. List your emergency contacts, including at least two names and contact numbers.
5. Provide informed consent for the therapist and their role.
6. Add any additional comments or notes about the session.
7. Sign and date the form, both as the client and the therapist.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **client_info** (Client Name, Date of Birth, Contact Info): Enter the client's name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **massage_info** (Type, Area of focus, Technique): Select the type of massage, area of focus, and technique to be used in the session.
* **client_consent** (Informed Consent): Indicate whether you are providing informed consent for the session.
* **massage_consent** (Informed Consent): Indicate whether the therapist is providing informed consent for the session.
* **emergency_contact** (Emergency Contacts): List at least two emergency contacts with their names and contact numbers.
* **therapist_info** (Name, Role, Contact Info): Enter the therapist's name, role, and contact information.
* **therapist_consent** (Informed Consent): Indicate whether the therapist is providing informed consent for the session.
* **massage_session_info** (Session Notes): Add any additional comments or notes about the session.
* **client_signature** (Client Signature): Sign and date the form as the client.
* **therapist_signature** (Therapist Signature): Sign and date the form as the therapist.
* **massage_session_notes_for_therapist** (Session Notes): Add any additional comments or notes about the session for the therapist's reference.
* **massage_session_duration** (Session Duration): Enter the duration of the massage session.
* **massage_session_rate** (Session Rate): Enter the rate for the massage session.
* **payment_method** (Payment Method): Select the payment method (Cash, Card, Insurance).
* **payment_info** (Payment Information): Enter any payment information, e.g. amount paid, payment method.
* **client_review** (Client Review): Indicate whether you are satisfied with the session.
* **massage_session_feedback** (Session Feedback): Add any feedback about the session.
* **therapist_review** (Therapist Review): Indicate whether the session met your expectations.
* **client_signature_date_time** (Client Signature Date and Time): Sign and date the form as the client.
* **therapist_signature_date_time** (Therapist Signature Date and Time): Sign and date the form as the therapist.
* **massage_session_details** (Session Details): Enter any additional information about the session.
* **massage_session_notes** (Session Notes): Add any additional comments or notes about the session.
* **therapist_signature** (Therapist Signature): Sign and date the form as the therapist.
* **massage_session_rate** (Session Rate): Enter the rate for the massage session.
* **massage_session_duration** (Session Duration): Enter the duration of the massage session.
* **payment_method** (Payment Method): Select the payment method (Cash, Card, Insurance).
