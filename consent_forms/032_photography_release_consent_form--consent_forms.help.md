# Photography Release Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photography Release Consent Form is designed to obtain consent from clients or subjects before, during, or after a photography session. This form ensures that all parties involved in the photography session are aware of their roles, responsibilities, and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Photography Client** field with the client's name and contact information.
2. Enter the **Photographer** field with the name and contact information of the person capturing the images.
3. Provide the **Photography Location** where the photos will be taken.
4. Fill in the **Photography Date** and **Photography Time** fields with the dates and times of the photography session.
5. List the **Photography Subjects** in the relevant section, including any individuals or objects that will be photographed.
6. Select the desired **Photography Release** type from the options provided. The options are:
	* Client Consent: The client consents to the photography session without involving a third-party consent.
	* Client and Photographer Consent: Both the client and the photographer consent to the session.
	* No Release Given: No consent is provided, and the images may not be used for any purpose.
7. If applicable, fill in the **Consent Expiration Date** field with the date after which the consent is no longer valid.
8. Add any relevant **Photography Notes** in the text area.
9. Sign and date the **Client Signature** field with the client's signature of consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photography Client**: Enter the name and contact information of the person or entity being photographed.
* **Photographer**: Enter the name and contact information of the person capturing the images.
* **Photography Location**: Provide the location where the photos will be taken.
* **Photography Date**: Enter the date of the photography session.
* **Photography Time**: Enter the time of the photography session.
* **Photography Subjects**: List any individuals or objects being photographed.
* **Photography Release**: Select one of the three options to indicate the consent type.
* **Consent Expiration Date**: If applicable, fill in the date after which the consent is no longer valid.
* **Photography Notes**: Add any relevant notes or comments about the photography session.
* **Client Signature**: The client's signature of consent.
* **Client Signature**: This field is only a placeholder for the signature, please sign it in with a pen or digital tool.
