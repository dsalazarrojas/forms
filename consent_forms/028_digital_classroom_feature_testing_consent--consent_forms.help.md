# Digital Classroom Feature Testing Consent - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain consent from participants to test digital classroom features.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Enter your **Name** in the "Name" field. This is a required field, and your name will be used to identify you as a participant.
3. Select your role in the "Role" field. Please choose either "Yes" or "No" to indicate your role in the testing process.
4. Select your contact preferences in the "Contact" field. You can choose multiple options, such as "Yes" or "No", to indicate how you prefer to be contacted.
5. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field. This will be used to contact you, so please make sure it is accurate.
6. Enter your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field. This will help us contact you, so please make sure it is correct.
7. Select your consent status in the "Consent" field. Please choose either "Yes" or "No" to indicate your consent to participate.
8. If you have any additional comments or notes, you can enter them in the "Additional Notes" field. This is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (text, required)**: Enter your full name, which will be used to identify you as a participant.
* **Role (select_one, required)**: Indicate your role in the testing process by selecting either "Yes" or "No".
* **Contact (select_multiple, required)**: Choose how you would like to be contacted, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Email (email, required)**: Enter your valid email address to be contacted.
* **Phone (text, required)**: Enter your phone number to be contacted.
* **Consent (select_one, required)**: Indicate your consent status by selecting either "Yes" or "No".
* **Additional Notes (text, optional)**: Enter any additional comments or notes if you have any.
