# Student Technology Device Use Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Student Technology Device Use Agreement Form is a legal document that requires the consent and agreement of both the student and the institution for the use of technology devices. The form outlines the terms and conditions of technology device usage, including the purpose of the device, usage guidelines, and other essential details.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Institution Section**: Please enter your institution's name and relevant contact information in the Institution field.
2.  **Device Type**: Choose the type of device you're using (e.g., laptop, tablet, smartphone, etc.).
3.  **Purpose of Device**: Select the purpose for which you'll be using the device (e.g., academic purposes, personal use, etc.).
4.  **Usage Guidelines**: This section is not a field to fill out but rather a space for notes or additional information about the usage guidelines.
5.  **Student Agreement**: In this section, the student must agree to the terms and conditions outlined in the form, including the understanding of the institution's and device's capabilities and limitations.
6.  **Institution Agreement**: In this section, the institution must agree to the terms and conditions outlined in the form, including the understanding of the student's and device's capabilities and limitations.
7.  **Parent/Guardian Signature**: This section requires the signature of the parent or guardian, indicating their consent to the terms and conditions outlined in the form.
8.  **Date Signed**: Record the date the form was signed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Institution**: Please enter your institution's name and any relevant contact information.
    *   Type: `text`
    *   Required: `true`
*   **Device Type**: Choose the type of device you are using.
    *   Type: `select_one`

    *   Type: `select_multiple`

## Tips

- Make sure to carefully read and understand all the terms and conditions before signing the form.
- Ensure that both the student and institution agree to the terms and conditions.
- Keep a copy of the signed form for future reference.
