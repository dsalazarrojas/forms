# League Branding Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about the participant's name, image, and likeness and their guardian's details. It is a critical step in ensuring that we respect their rights and boundaries when using their name, image, or likeness for branding purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the participant's name as it appears on their official identification document (e.g., passport, driver's license).
2. Provide the guardian's name and relationship with the participant (e.g., Mom, Dad, Guardian).
3. Choose whether or not you consent to use your participant's name, image, and likeness for the league branding activities.
4. Enter your participant's date of birth.
5. Provide a valid phone number for you and your guardian.
6. Enter your participant's email address.
7. Enter your guardian's email address.
8. Make your consent choices for using your participant's name, image, and likeness in different media outlets (e.g., media, video, audio, social media).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, **required**): Enter the participant's name as it appears on their official identification document (e.g., passport, driver's license).
* **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, text, **required**): Enter the guardian's name.
* **Relationship** (`guardian_relationship`, select_one, **required**): Choose how you are related to the participant (e.g., Mom, Dad, Guardian).
* **Permission to Use Participant Name** (`permission_to_use_participant_name`, select_multiple, **required**): Choose whether or not you consent to use the participant's name for the league branding activities.
* **Permission to Use Participant Image** (`permission_to_use_participant_image`, select_multiple, **required**): Choose whether or not you consent to use the participant's image for the league branding activities.
* **Permission to Use Participant Likeness** (`permission_to_use_participant_likeness`, select_multiple, **required**): Choose whether or not you consent to use the participant's likeness for the league branding activities.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, note, **optional**): This is a signature field for the participant.
* **Guardian Signature** (`guardian_signature`, note, **optional**): This is a signature field for the guardian.
* **Participant Date of Birth** (`participant_date_of_birth`, date, **required**): Enter the participant's date of birth.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, **required**): Enter a valid phone number for the participant.
* **Guardian Phone** (`guardian_phone`, text, **required**): Enter a valid phone number for the guardian.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, **required**): Enter a valid email address for the participant.
* **Guardian Email** (`guardian_email`, email, **required**): Enter a valid email address for the guardian.
* **Participant Image Consent** (`participant_image_consent`, select_one, **optional**): Choose whether or not you consent to use the participant's image.
* **Participant Photo Consent** (`participant_photo_consent`, select_one, **optional**): Choose whether or not you consent to use the participant's photo.
* **Participant Video Consent** (`participant_video_consent`, select_one, **optional**): Choose whether or not you consent to use the participant's video.
* **Participant Audio Consent** (`participant_audio_consent`, select_one, **optional**): Choose whether or not you consent to use the participant's audio.
* **Participant Name In Media** (`participant_name_in_media`, select_one, **optional**): Choose whether or not you consent to use the participant's name in media.
* **Participant Name In Video** (`participant_name_in_video`, select_one, **optional**): Choose whether or not you consent to use the participant's name in video.
* **Participant Name In Photo** (`participant_name_in_photo`, select_one, **optional**): Choose whether or not you consent to use the participant's name in photo.
* **Participant Name In Poster** (`participant_name_in_poster`, select_one, **optional**): Choose whether or not you consent to use the participant's name in poster.
* **Participant Name In Program** (`participant_name_in_program`, select_one, **optional**): Choose whether or not you consent to use the participant's name in program.
* **Participant Name In Social Media** (`participant_name_in_social_media`, select_one, **optional**): Choose whether or not you consent to use the participant's name in social media.
* **Participant Name In Announcement** (`participant_name_in_announcement`, select_one, **optional**): Choose whether or not you consent to use the participant's name in announcement.
* **Participant Name In Other** (`participant_name_in_other`, select_one, **optional**): Choose whether or not you consent to use the participant's name in other.

## Tips
- Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information about the participant and their guardian.
- Review each question carefully and make your informed choices.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
