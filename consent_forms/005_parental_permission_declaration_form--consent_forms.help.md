# Parental Permission Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect parental permission for a child's activities. It's essential for parents to provide their child's information, including their name, activity name, activity date, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please ensure that you have all the necessary information required for this form before proceeding.
2. Enter your child's name in the "Parent Name" field.
3. Provide the name of the activity your child will be participating in in the "Activity Name" field.
4. Enter the date of the activity in the "Activity Date" field.
5. If applicable, enter the time of the activity in the "Activity Time" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Parent Phone" field, so we can contact you if needed.
7. Enter your email address in the "Parent Email" field, so we can keep you updated on your child's activity.
8. Enter the name of the school your child is attending in the "School Name" field.
9. Enter the address of the school in the "School Address" field.
10. Enter the contact person's name and number at the school in the "School Contact" field.
10. If your child is participating in an activity related to an organization, enter the name of the organization in the "Organization Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name (parent_name)**: Enter your name as a parent or guardian of the child.
* **Activity Name (activity_name)**: Enter the name of the activity your child will be participating in.
* **Activity Date (activity_date)**: Enter the date of the activity in the format of month/day/year (e.g., 02/12/2023).
* **Activity Time (activity_time)**: If applicable, enter the time of the activity in the 24-hour clock format (e.g., 14:30 for 2:30 PM).
* **Parent Phone (parent_phone)**: Enter your phone number, so we can contact you if needed.
* **Parent Email (parent_email)**: Enter your email address, so we can keep you updated on your child's activity.
* **School Name (school_name)**: Enter the name of the school your child is attending.
* **School Address (school_address)**: Enter the address of the school where your child will be participating in the activity.
* **School Contact (school_contact)**: Enter the name and contact number of the school representative or teacher who will be supervising your child during the activity.
* **Organization Name (organization_name)**: If your child is participating in an activity related to an organization, enter the name of the organization.
