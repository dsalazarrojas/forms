# Athlete Fan Interaction Media Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain athlete consent for media use during events and interactions with the organization, ensuring that athletes understand and agree to the terms of media use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with the requested information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Full Name** (`athlete_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as an athlete.
* **Name of Event** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event you will be participating in.
* **Date of Event** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the event, including the venue name and city.
* **Team or Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the team or organization you are representing.
* **I consent to being photographed during this event** (`photo_consent`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you consent to being photographed during the event (True/False).
* **I consent to video recording of my interactions** (`video_consent`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you consent to video recording of your interactions (True/False).
* **I consent to audio recording for interviews or clips** (`audio_consent`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you consent to audio recording for interviews or clips (True/False).
* **I consent to media use in official fan portals and apps** (`fan_portal_consent`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you consent to media use in official fan portals and apps (True/False).
* **I consent to media use for social media promotional posts** (`social_media_consent`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you consent to media use for social media promotional posts (True/False).
* **I consent to media use by approved commercial partners** (`commercial_partner_consent`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you consent to media use by approved commercial partners (True/False).
* **I agree that I will receive no financial remuneration for this media use** (`remuneration_agreement`, select_one, required): Indicate that you will receive no financial remuneration for this media use (I agree/Other).
* **I transfer all rights of the captured media to the Organization** (`rights_transfer`, select_one, required): Indicate that you transfer all rights of the captured media to the organization (I agree/Other).
* **I acknowledge the policy for withdrawing consent after publication** (`withdrawal_acknowledgement`, select_one, required): Indicate that you acknowledge the policy for withdrawing consent after publication (I acknowledge/Other).
* **Athlete Electronic Signature** (`athlete_signature`, text, required): Type your full name as an athlete.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature** (`guardian_signature`, text, optional): If you are under 18, enter your parent or guardian's signature.
* **Date of Signature** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your signature.
* **Email Address for Confirmation** (`confirmation_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for confirmation.

Note: This form is a legal document and should be reviewed carefully before completing it. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the organization.
