# Testosterone Treatment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Testosterone Treatment Consent Form is a crucial document that must be completed by patients before they begin testosterone treatment. It helps to ensure that patients are aware of the potential risks and benefits associated with the treatment and provides an opportunity for them to express their informed consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your patient information (page 1).
	* Enter your name, date of birth, and contact information in the Patient Information section.
2. Review your medical history (page 2).
	* Answer the questions honestly and accurately about your medical history, including any previous medical conditions or treatments you have received.
3. List any medications you are currently taking (page 3).
	* Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each medication you are taking to indicate whether you are currently taking it.
4. List any allergies you have (page 4).
	* Enter any allergies you have in the text box provided.
5. Describe your medical condition (page 5).
	* Enter a brief description of your medical condition in the text box provided.
6. Describe the test result (page 6).
	* Enter a brief description of your test result in the text box provided.
7. Provide consent (page 7).
	* Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your consent to undergo testosterone treatment.
8. Date and time of consent (page 8).
	* Enter the date and time you are providing your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`name`, `type`, required/optional): Enter your name, date of birth, and contact information in this section.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `type`, required/optional): Answer the questions honestly and accurately about your medical history, including any previous medical conditions or treatments you have received.
* **Medications** (`medications`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each medication you are taking to indicate whether you are currently taking it.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `type`, required/optional): Enter any allergies you have in the text box provided.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `type`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your medical condition in the text box provided.
* **Test Result** (`test_result`, `type`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your test result in the text box provided.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your consent to undergo testosterone treatment.
* **Date of Consent** (`date_of_consent`, `type`, required/optional): Enter the date you are providing your consent.
* **Time of Consent** (`time_of_consent`, `type`, required/optional): Enter the time you are providing your consent.
