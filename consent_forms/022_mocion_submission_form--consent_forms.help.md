# Mocion Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect data on meetings and their outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the meeting details, such as date, time, and location.
2. Select the status of the meeting (e.g., Draft, Approved, Rejected, etc.).
3. Choose the outcome of the meeting (e.g., Yes, No, etc.).
4. Provide any discussion points from the meeting.
5. Select the version of the motion submission form used.
6. Fill in the submitter's details, including name, email, phone, and role.
7. List the attendees of the meeting.
8. Optionally, fill in any additional meeting notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Details (Meeting Details)**: A text field for capturing the meeting's details.
	+ Required: No
	+ Hint: Meeting date, time, and location.
* **Motion Submission Date (motion_submission_date)**: A date field for capturing the date of the motion submission.
	+ Required: No
	+ Hint: The date the motion was submitted.
* **Motion Submission Time (motion_submission_time)**: A time field for capturing the time of the motion submission.
	+ Required: No
	+ Hint: The time the motion was submitted.
* **Motion Status (motion_status)**: A dropdown field for selecting the status of the motion.
	+ Required: Yes
	+ Hint: Draft, Approved, Rejected, etc.
* **Meeting Outcome (meeting_outcome)**: A dropdown field for selecting the outcome of the meeting.
	+ Required: No
	+ Hint: Yes, No, etc.
* **Discussion Points (discussion_points)**: A text field for capturing any discussion points from the meeting.
	+ Required: No
	+ Hint: Any important points discussed during the meeting.
* **Motion Submission Form (motion_submission_form)**: A text field for capturing the form used.
	+ Required: No
	+ Hint: The version of the motion submission form used.
* **Motion Submission Form Version (motion_submission_form_version)**: A dropdown field for selecting the version of the motion submission form used.
	+ Required: Yes

* **Submitter (submitter)**: A text field for capturing the submitter's name.
	+ Required: No
	+ Hint: The person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Email (submitter_email)**: An email field for capturing the submitter's email.
	+ Required: No

	+ Required: No

	+ Required: No
	+ Hint: The submitter's role in the meeting.
* **Submitter Department (submitter_department)**: A text field for capturing the submitter's department.
	+ Required: No
