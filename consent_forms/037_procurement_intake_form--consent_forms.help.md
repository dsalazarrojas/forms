# Procurement Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Procurement Intake Form is used to collect information about vendor details and procurement methods for new procurements. It's intended for one-time transactions and should not be filled out if the procurement is already in progress or has already been completed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out all required fields, marked as such in the form.
2. For vendor details, please fill out the vendor's name, email, phone number, and address.
3. Select the procurement type (Direct or Indirect) and method (Electronic or Physical Bid).
4. Choose the contract type (Fixed Price or CostPlus).
5. Select the procurement status (Open or Closed).
6. Choose the vendor representative (Internal or External).
7. Fill out the procurement date and time.
8. Fill out the vendor's contact information and method of contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Name** (vendor_name): The name of the vendor providing the goods or services.
* **Vendor Email** (vendor_email): The email address of the vendor.
* **Vendor Phone** (vendor_phone): The phone number of the vendor.
* **Vendor Address** (vendor_address): The address of the vendor.
* **Procurement Type** (procurement_type): The type of procurement (Direct or Indirect).
* **Procurement Method** (procurement_method): The method of procurement (Electronic or Physical Bid).
* **Contract Type** (contract_type): The type of contract (Fixed Price or CostPlus).
* **Procurement Status** (procurement_status): The status of the procurement (Open or Closed).
* **Vendor Representative** (vendor_rep): The vendor's representative (Internal or External).
* **Procurement Date** (procurement_date): The date of the procurement.
* **Procurement Time** (procurement_time): The time of the procurement.
* **Procurement Value** (procurement_value): The value of the procurement.
* **Purchase Order Number** (purchase_order_number): The purchase order number.
* **Contract Number** (contract_number): The contract number.
* **Contract Date** (contract_date): The date of the contract.
* **Contract Time** (contract_time): The time of the contract.
* **Contract Status** (contract_status): The status of the contract (Active, Cancelled, or Closed).
* **Notes** (notes): Any additional comments about the procurement.
* **Vendor** (vendor): The vendor providing the goods or services.
* **Contact Person** (contact_person): The contact person at the vendor's company.
* **Vendor Contact** (vendor_contact): The contact information of the vendor.
* **Contact Method** (contact_method): The method of contact (Email, Phone, or In person).
* **Contact Info** (contact_info): Any additional contact information.
