# Personal Care Assistant Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Personal Care Assistant Authorization Form is a document that requires you to provide information about the personal care assistant you are working with. This form helps us to understand your care needs and ensures that we have the necessary information to provide you with the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure that you have all the necessary information before submitting it.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Make sure to fill out all the required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
4. If you need help or have any questions, please contact our support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Family Name** (`family_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of your family.
* **Family Phone** (`family_phone`, text, optional): Please enter your family's phone number.
* **Care Type** (`care_type`, select_one, optional): Please select the type of care you need.
* **Care Start Date** (`care_start`, date, optional): Please enter the start date of your care.
* **Care End Date** (`care_end`, date, optional): Please enter the end date of your care.
* **Care Details** (`care_details`, text, optional): Please provide any additional information about your care needs.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Please enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, optional): Please enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Care Provider** (`care_provider`, text, optional): Please enter the name and contact information of your care provider.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, text, optional): Please enter any relevant medical information.
* **Care Agreement** (`care_agreement`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the agreements that apply to your care.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, optional): Please enter the date you consent to this form.
* **Caregiver Name** (`caregiver_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the caregiver.
* **Caregiver Phone** (`caregiver_phone`, text, optional): Please enter the phone number of the caregiver.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Please sign this form to confirm that you have read and understood the information provided.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, optional): Please enter the date you signed this form.

## Tips
* Make sure to review and update your information regularly to ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or need help, please contact our support team.
