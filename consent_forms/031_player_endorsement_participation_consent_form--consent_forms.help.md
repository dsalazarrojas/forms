# Player Endorsement Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for players who wish to participate in a game or event and need to provide their consent and, if applicable, their guardian's consent. It ensures that players and their guardians are aware of and agree to the rules and terms of the game or event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please enter your name in the field provided.
2. If you have a guardian, please enter their name in the field provided.
3. Choose your answer for the "Player Consent" field. If you are under 18, you must choose "Yes" to indicate that your guardian has given their consent.
4. If you have a guardian, choose their consent for each field in the "Guardian Consent" field.
5. Select the date of your birth in the "Date of Birth" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player Name** (`player_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your guardian's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Player Consent** (`player_consent`, select_one, required/optional): Choose "Yes" if your guardian has given their consent to your participation. If you are over 18, you must choose "Yes" to indicate your consent to participate.
* **Guardian Consent** (`guardian_consent`, select_multiple, required/optional): If you have a guardian, choose their consent for each field as applicable.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY or DD/MM/YY.
