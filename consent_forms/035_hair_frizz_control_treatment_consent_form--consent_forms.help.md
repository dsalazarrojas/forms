# Hair Frizz Control Treatment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hair Frizz Control Treatment Consent Form is designed for clients to provide their informed consent for a hair treatment that helps control frizz. The form is divided into several sections that gather essential information about the client's health, medical history, and consent for the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Client Details**: Fill in your basic information, including your date of birth, phone number, and email.
2. **Health Information**: Provide any relevant health information, including medical conditions, allergies, and medication you are currently taking.
3. **Consent**: Answer the consent questions truthfully and carefully. The questions are:
	* Are you aware of and understand the risks associated with the chemical frizz-control treatment?
	* Are you willing to participate in the chemical frizz-control treatment program?
4. **Additional Information** (optional): If you have any medical conditions or allergies that are not mentioned earlier, please provide that information here.
5. **Doctor's Information** (optional): If you are currently seeing a doctor, please provide their name and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details** (`client_details`, `text`, required): Please fill in your basic information, including your date of birth, phone number, and email.
* **Health Information** (`health_info`, `text`, required): Please provide any relevant health information, including medical conditions, allergies, and medication you are currently taking.
* **I am aware of and understand the risks associated with the chemical frizz-control treatment.** (`consent_1`, `select_one`, required): Please answer this question truthfully and carefully.
* **I am willing to participate in the chemical frizz-control treatment program.** (`consent_2`, `select_one`, required): Please answer this question truthfully and carefully.
* **Signed Consent** (`signed_consent`, `note`, required): This is a confirmation that you have read and understood the risks associated with the treatment and have provided your informed consent.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Please fill in your date of birth.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Please provide your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Please provide your email address.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, optional): If you have any medical conditions, please provide that information here.
* **Medical Allergies** (`medical_allergies`, `text`, optional): If you have any medical allergies, please provide that information here.
* **Medication** (`medication`, `text`, optional): If you are currently taking any medication, please provide that information here.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, `text`, optional): If you are currently seeing a doctor, please provide their name.
* **Other Doctor's Name** (`doctor_name_other_doctor`, `text`, optional): If you are seeing another doctor, please provide their name.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, `date`, optional): If you have an appointment scheduled, please provide the date.
* **Tips**: Please ensure that you have answered all the questions truthfully and carefully. If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask for clarification.
