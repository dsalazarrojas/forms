# Training Video Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The "Training Video Release Form" is used to capture participant information and release permissions for a training video. The form is divided into several pages, each containing relevant fields for participant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your participant information, including your name, email, and company name (if applicable).
2. Select the release permission and distribution methods that apply to you.
3. Provide your consent date and signature.
4. Add any additional details, such as your employee ID, department, job title, role, location, company address, contact number, and published information (if applicable).
5. Confirm your participant agreement.
6. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your professional email address.
* **Company Name**: Enter your company name (if applicable).
* **Video Title**: Enter the title of the training video being released.
* **Release Permission**: Select one or more options that apply to you, such as "Option 1", "Option 2", "Option 3".
* **Distribution Methods**: Select one or more options that apply to you, such as "Option 1", "Option 2", "Option 3".
* **Consent Date**: Enter the date you are providing your consent.
* **Participant Signature**: Sign your name to confirm your agreement.
* **Date Signed**: Enter the date you signed the form.
* **Time Signed**: Enter the time you signed the form.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID (if applicable).
* **Department**: Enter your department (if applicable).
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title (if applicable).
* **Role**: Enter your role (if applicable).
* **Location**: Enter your location (if applicable).
* **Company Address**: Enter your company address (if applicable).
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number (if applicable).
* **Participant Agreement**: Select "I agree" to confirm your consent.
* **Date Recorded**: Enter the date the video was recorded (if applicable).
* **Time Recorded**: Enter the time the video was recorded (if applicable).
* **Date Published**: Enter the date the video was published (if applicable).
* **Time Published**: Enter the time the video was published (if applicable).
* **Published By**: Enter the name of the person who published the video (if applicable).
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the video (if applicable).
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments about the video (if applicable).
