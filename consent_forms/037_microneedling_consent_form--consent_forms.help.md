# Microneedling Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather patient information for procedures related to microneedling, a cosmetic procedure that involves the use of micro-needling technology to improve skin texture and appearance. The purpose of this form is to inform patients of the procedure details, risks, and benefits, and to obtain their consent before starting the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the form completely and honestly.** Answer all questions and provide clear, concise responses.
2. **Read each field carefully** before proceeding to ensure you understand the purpose and requirements of each section.
3. **Save and print the form** for your records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Procedure** (`date_of_procedure`, date, required: false): The date you are scheduled to undergo the microneedling procedure.
* **Description of Procedure** (`description_of_procedure`, text, required: false): Briefly describe the microneedling procedure you are undergoing, including the area(s) being treated and the expected duration of the treatment.
* **Risks and Benefits** (`risks_and_benefits`, text, required: false): Please list any potential risks or benefits you have discussed with your healthcare provider related to the microneedling procedure.
* **Informed Consent** (`informed_consent`, select_multiple, required: false): Check all that apply:
	+ I have read, understand, and will follow all the instructions on this form.
	+ I have received a copy of this form, and I will keep it for my records.
	+ I have received a copy of this form, and I will show it to my family doctor.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required: false): This is not a signature field. It appears to be a placeholder for future use, but it is currently empty.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, note, required: false): This field is also not intended for signature. It is a placeholder, and you should not attempt to sign it.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): The date of signing this form, which should be the same as the date of procedure.
