# Student Project Promotion Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain consent from students and their parents/guardians for promoting their project, allowing us to collect and use their information for promotional activities such as social media, website, and print materials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's name and contact details (Student Name, Student Email, and Student Phone).
2. Fill in the parent/guardian's name and contact details (Parent Or Guardian Name, Parent Or Guardian Email, and Parent Or Guardian Phone).
3. Choose the educational institution your student is affiliated with (University of XYZ, ABC University, or Other).
4. Select the promotional materials you agree to use for your project (Social media, Website, Print Materials).
5. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.
6. Add any additional comments or declarations if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required): Enter the student's full name.
* **Parent Or Guardian Name** (`parent_or_guardian_name`, `text`, required): Enter the parent or guardian's full name.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, `email`, required): Enter the student's email address.
* **Parent Or Guardian Email** (`parent_or_guardian_email`, `email`, required): Enter the parent or guardian's email address.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Parent Or Guardian Phone** (`parent_or_guardian_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the parent or guardian's phone number.
* **I Agree To** (`agreement`, `select_one`, required): Check the box to agree to use your information for promotional activities.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required): Add any additional comments or information you'd like to include.
* **Educational Institution** (`educational_institution`, `select_one`, required): Select the educational institution your student is affiliated with.
* **Promotion Materials** (`promotion_materials`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the promotional materials you agree to use for your project.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are providing consent.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you are providing consent.
* **I Agree To Use The Above Information For The Following** (`agreement_details`, `select_multiple`, required): Check the box to agree to use the information for the selected promotional materials.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, required): Add any additional comments or information you'd like to include.
* **Declaration** (`student_declaration`, `note`, required): Read and agree to the declaration.
* **Declaration** (`institution_declaration`, `note`, required): Read and agree to the declaration.
* **Signature Of Student** (`student_signature`, `note`, required): Sign below to confirm your consent.
* **Signature Of Parent Or Guardian** (`parent_or_guardian_signature`, `note`, required): Sign below to confirm your consent.

Note: Please ensure you provide accurate and complete information to avoid any issues or delays in the promotional process.
