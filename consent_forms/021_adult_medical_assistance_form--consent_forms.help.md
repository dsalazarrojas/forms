# Adult Medical Assistance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adult Medical Assistance Form is used to gather information and obtain consent for the release of a patient's medical records. This form helps to ensure that the patient's privacy and confidentiality are respected while allowing authorized individuals or organizations to access their medical data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Patient Full Name** in the text field.
2. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
3. Select all categories of medical information you are willing to release to the **Recipient Organization or Individual**.
4. Choose whether you **Consent to the Release of Your Clinical Photographs** or not.
5. If you consent, specify the **Purpose of Photograph Release**.
6. Sign your name as the **Authorized Signature Name**.
7. Enter the **Date of Authorization** in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
8. Indicate whether the authorization was signed in the presence of a **Witness**.
9. (Optional) Enter the **Medical Coordinator Name**, if assigned.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 1990-01-01).
* **Information to be Released**: Select all categories of medical information you are willing to release, such as:
	+ Clinical Notes
	+ Lab Results
	+ Imaging Reports
	+ Prescription History
	+ All Medical Records
* **Recipient Organization or Individual**: Enter the name and details of who is authorized to receive this data.
* **Consent to Release Photographs**: Choose whether you consent to the release of your clinical photographs.
* **Photograph Release Consent**: Select your consent status.
* **Purpose of Photograph Release**: Specify the reason for releasing your clinical photographs (e.g., Teaching, Research, Consultation).
* **Authorized Signature Name**: Sign with your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Authorization**: Enter the date you signed this form in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Witness Verification**: Indicate whether the authorization was signed in the presence of a witness.
* **Medical Coordinator Name**: Enter the name of the staff member assigned to you, if applicable.
* **Witness Verification**: Indicate whether the authorization was signed in the presence of a witness.
* **Authorized Signature Name**: Type your name as a legal signature.
* **Date of Authorization**: Enter the date you signed this form in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
