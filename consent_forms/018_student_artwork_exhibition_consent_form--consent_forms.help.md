# Student Artwork Exhibition Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Artwork Exhibition Consent Form is designed to collect consent from students or their parents/guardians to display their artwork in an exhibition. This form helps to obtain necessary information and approvals before proceeding with the exhibition.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the **Parent Name** field with the name of the student's parent or guardian.
2. Provide your **Contact Information** in the **Contact Info** field, including your email address and phone number.
3. Answer the **Artwork Permission** question, choosing "Yes" or "No" if you agree or disagree to display the artwork.
4. Enter the **Submission** details, if applicable.
5. Select "Yes" or "No" for **Exhibition Permission**, indicating your consent to display the artwork in the exhibition.
6. Fill in the **Student Name** field with the student's name.
7. Enter the student's **Grade Level** in the **Student Grade** field.
8. Enter the student's email address in the **Email** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name**: Enter the name of the student's parent or guardian.
* **Contact Info**: Provide your contact information, including email and phone number.
* **Artwork Permission**: Answer "Yes" if you give permission to display the artwork, or "No" if you don't.
* **Submission**: Enter details about what is being submitted for the exhibition.
* **Exhibition Permission**: Answer "Yes" if you give permission to display the artwork in the exhibition, or "No" if you don't.
* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name.
* **Student Grade**: Enter the student's grade level.
* **Email**: Enter the student's email address.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your contact information and email address for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the exhibition organizers.
* This form is for internal use within the school or educational institution, so please ensure you are completing it for the correct purpose.
