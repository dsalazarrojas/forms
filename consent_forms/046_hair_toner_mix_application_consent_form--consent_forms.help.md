# Hair Toner Mix Application Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is a form for customers to provide their consent for a hair toner mix application. It's required for your appointment, and you should fill it out carefully and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this form for your actual appointment, and make sure it's accurate and up-to-date.
- If you've already filled out a similar form for the same appointment, review it to confirm the information is correct.
- If unsure, consult with your appointment scheduler or staff member to confirm the appointment status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **salon_info** (salon_info, text, optional): This field is for you to provide some basic information about the salon where you will be getting your hair toner mix application done. This can include details such as the name, address, and phone number of the salon.
* **client_info** (client_info, text, optional): In this field, please provide some details about yourself, including your name, contact number, and any other information the salon might need to get in touch with you.
* **appointment_info** (appointment_info, text, optional): Here, describe the details of your appointment, such as the date, time, and what type of service you're getting (Cut, Color, or Chemical treatment).
* **consent** (consent, select_one, optional): Please choose one of the two options: "I am 18 years old or older" or "I am under 18 years old" to confirm your age and consent to the service.
* **service_info** (service_info, select_multiple, optional): Check the services you want to get: Cut, Color, or Chemical treatment.
* **notes** (notes, text, optional): If you want to add any additional comments or details about your service or appointment, you can do so in this text field.
* **email** (email, email, optional): Enter your email address so the salon can get in touch with you after your appointment if needed.
