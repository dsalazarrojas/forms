# IRB Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your involvement in research studies, to ensure you are aware of the nature of the research, its goals, and potential risks and benefits.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2. Ensure you answer all required questions.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **IRB Consent Information** (`irb_consent_information`, text, optional): A brief statement of information about the research study, including its goals and potential risks and benefits.
* **Research Study Title** (`research_study_title`, text, optional): The title of the research study you are participating in.
* **IRB Informed Consent Form Date** (`irb_informed_consent_form_date`, date, optional): The date the informed consent form was completed.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, optional): Sign your name to confirm that you understand and agree to the terms of the research study.
* **Research Contact Information** (`research_contact_information`, email, optional): Contact information for the research team in case you have any questions or concerns.
* **Participant Phone Number** (`participant_phone_number`, text, optional): Your phone number for communication with the research team.
* **Participant Institutional Review Board IRB Approval** (`participant_institutional_review_board_irb_approval`, select_one, optional): Confirm if your Institutional Review Board IRB has approved the research study.
* **Participant Institutional Review Board IRB Approval Date** (`participant_institutional_review_board_irb_approval_date`, date, optional): The date your Institutional Review Board IRB approved the research study.
* **Participant Institutional Review Board IRB Expiration Date** (`participant_institutional_review_board_irb_expiration_date`, date, optional): The date your Institutional Review Board IRB approval for the research study expires.
* **Participant Institutional Review Board IRB Version** (`participant_institutional_review_board_irb_version`, text, optional): The version of your Institutional Review Board IRB approval for the research study.
* **Participant Institutional Review Board IRB Approval By** (`participant_institutional_review_board_irb_approval_by`, text, optional): The name or initials of the person who approved your Institutional Review Board IRB approval.
* **Participant Institutional Review Board IRB Number** (`participant_institutional_review_board_irb_number`, text, optional): The Institutional Review Board IRB number for your approval.
* **Participant Institutional Review Board IRB Expiration Version** (`participant_institutional_review_board_irb_expiration_version`, text, optional): The version of your Institutional Review Board IRB expiration date.
* **Participant Institutional Review Board IRB Status** (`participant_institutional_review_board_irb_status`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm the status of your Institutional Review Board IRB approval (Active or Inactive).
* **Participant Institutional Review Board IRB Version Approved** (`participant_institutional_review_board_irb_version_approved`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm if your Institutional Review Board IRB version is approved.
* **Participant Institutional Review Board IRB Approval Version** (`participant_institutional_review_board_irb_approval_version`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm the version of your Institutional Review Board IRB approval.
* **Participant Institutional Review Board IRB Status Approved** (`participant_institutional_review_board_irb_status_approved`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm the status of your Institutional Review Board IRB approval (Active or Inactive).
* **Participant Institutional Review Board IRB Status Version** (`participant_institutional_review_board_irb_status_version`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm the status version of your Institutional Review Board IRB approval (Active or Inactive).
* **Participant Institutional Review Board IRB Status Version Research** (`participant_institutional_review_board_irb_status_version_research`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm the status version of your Institutional Review Board IRB approval for research purposes (Active or Inactive).
