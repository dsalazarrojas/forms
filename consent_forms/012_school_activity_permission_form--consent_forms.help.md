# School Activity Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain permission from parents or guardians for their child to participate in school activities. It helps ensure that parents are informed and agree to their child's involvement in a particular school activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's information:
	* Student Name: Enter the student's name as it appears on their official records.
2. Confirm student status:
	* Student Grade: Select the student's grade level (if applicable)
3. Provide activity details:
	* Activity Name: Enter the name of the activity the student will be participating in.
	* Start Date and End Date: Enter the dates the activity will take place.
4. Add school information:
	* School Name: Enter the name of the school the student attends.
	* School Email: Enter the school's contact email address.
	* School Phone and School Fax: Enter the school's phone and fax numbers.
5. Provide parent contact information:
	* Parent Name: Enter the parent's name as it appears on their official records.
	* Parent Email: Enter the parent's email address.
6. Confirm permission:
	* Permission Granted: Select 'Yes' to confirm that the parent has granted permission for their child to participate.
7. Add any additional notes:
	* Notes: Enter any additional comments or instructions for the school staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's name as it appears on their official records.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, select_one, optional): Select the student's grade level (if applicable).
* **Activity Name** (`activity_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the activity the student will be participating in.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the activity will start.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the activity will end.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the school the student attends.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, email, optional): Enter the school's contact email address.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text, optional): Enter the school's phone number.
* **School Fax** (`school_fax`, text, optional): Enter the school's fax number.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, optional): Enter the school's address.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Enter the parent's name as it appears on their official records.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, optional): Enter the parent's email address.
* **Permission Granted** (`permission_granted`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' to confirm that the parent has granted permission for their child to participate.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or instructions for the school staff.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, optional): This is a note field for the school staff to sign and date.
