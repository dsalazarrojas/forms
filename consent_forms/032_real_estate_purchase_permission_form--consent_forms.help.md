# real_estate_purchase_permission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The real estate purchase permission form is used to record information about buyers and real estate agents involved in a purchase. It is a required form that must be completed to ensure accurate documentation and compliance with regulatory guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing the buyer's information, including their contact details in the **Buyer Information** field. This is a required field.
2. Next, provide the contact information of the real estate agent handling the sale in the **Contact Information** field. This is also a required field.
3. If applicable, provide the date of the purchase in the **Purchase Date** field.
4. Enter the real estate agent's contact information in the **Real Estate Agent Information** and **Real Estate Agent Phone** fields. While not required, this information can be helpful for future reference.
5. Finally, provide a note to sign in the **Buyer Signature** field. This is a required field for the buyer to provide their consent and signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Buyer Information**: Enter the buyer's name, address, and other relevant contact details.
* **Contact Information**: Enter the real estate agent's name, address, and other relevant contact details.
* **Purchase Date** (if applicable): Enter the date of the purchase.
* **Real Estate Agent Information**: Enter the real estate agent's name and other relevant contact details.
* **Real Estate Agent Phone**: Enter the real estate agent's phone number.
* **Real Estate Agent E-mail**: Enter the real estate agent's email address.
* **Buyer Signature**: A note for the buyer to sign and date, indicating their consent to the terms of the sale.
