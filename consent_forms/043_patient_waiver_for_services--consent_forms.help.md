# Patient Waiver For Services - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain a waiver for services provided by a healthcare facility to a patient. It is an agreement between the patient and the healthcare provider, outlining the terms and conditions of service.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Consent Statement**: Please read and sign the Consent Statement if you understand the information provided.
2. **Patient Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
3. **Healthcare Provider**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider who will be providing services.
4. **Healthcare Provider Title**: Enter the title of the healthcare provider, such as MD or RN.
5. **Healthcare Provider Contact**: Enter the contact information for the healthcare provider, including phone number and email.
6. **Facility**: Enter the name of the healthcare facility providing services.
7. **Date of Service**: Enter the date on which services will be provided.
8. **Time of Service**: Enter the time on which services will be provided.
9. **Service Benefits**: Describe the services that will be provided.
10. **Risks and Contraindications**: List any risks or contraindications associated with the services.
11. **Service Terms and Conditions**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions of service.
12. **Patient Signature**: Sign your name to acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms and conditions.
13. **Healthcare Provider Signature**: Sign your name to confirm that you have read and understood the terms and conditions.
14. **Date Signed**: Enter the date on which the form is signed.
15. **Time Signed**: Enter the time on which the form is signed.
16. **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the healthcare facility.
17. **Facility Address**: Enter the address of the healthcare facility.
18. **Facility City**: Enter the city of the healthcare facility.
19. **Facility State**: Enter the state of the healthcare facility.
20. **Facility Zip**: Enter the zip code of the healthcare facility.
21. **Facility Phone**: Enter the phone number of the healthcare facility.
22. **Facility Email**: Enter the email of the healthcare facility.
23. **Age**: Enter your age.
24. **Medical Record**: Enter any relevant medical information.
25. **Services Performed**: Describe the services performed.
