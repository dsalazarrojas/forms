# Technology Responsible Use Guideline - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Technology Responsible Use Guideline form is designed to capture and track information about the guidelines and policies related to the responsible use of technology within an organization. This form helps in maintaining a record of updates, revisions, and review history.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the appropriate category for the technology guideline from the options provided in the "technology_responsible_use_guideline_category" field.
2. Enter a brief description of the technology guideline in the "technology_responsible_use_guideline_description" field.
3. Specify the consequences of not following the technology guideline in the "technology_responsible_use_guideline_consequences" field.
4. Identify the potential impacts of the technology guideline on the organization and its stakeholders in the "technology_responsible_use_guideline_impacts" field.
5. Enter the guidelines and rules for the technology guideline in the "technology_responsible_use_guideline_guidelines" field.
6. Specify the training and education provided for the technology guideline in the "technology_responsible_use_guideline_training" field.
7. Review and update the technology guideline as needed, and enter the review date, time, and user information in the "technology_responsible_use_guideline_review", "technology_responsible_use_guideline_revision_date", "technology_responsible_use_guideline_revision_time", "technology_responsible_use_guideline_last_reviewed", "technology_responsible_use_guideline_last_reviewed_by", and "technology_responsible_use_guideline_last_reviewed_by_user" fields.
