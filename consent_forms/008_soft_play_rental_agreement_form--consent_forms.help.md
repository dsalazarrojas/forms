# Soft Play Rental Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to gather information from customers or clients who wish to rent a soft play area or equipment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the Business Name of the company or organization associated with the rental.
2. Provide the Contact Number of the person responsible for the rental.
3. Enter the Email Address of the person responsible for the rental.
4. Select the Rental Date for which you wish to rent the soft play area or equipment.
5. Choose the Rental Hours you wish to rent the soft play area or equipment.
6. Select the Rental Terms you are agreeing to, such as the Terms and Conditions and Cancellation Policy.
7. Choose the Payment Method you wish to use for the rental (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or PayPal).
8. You may also add any additional comments or details about your rental in the Rental Agreement field.
9. Lastly, provide any additional Payment Details if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name (Text)** (`Business Name`, required: false): Enter the name of the company or organization associated with the rental.
* **Contact Number (Text)** (`Contact Number`, required: false): Enter the contact number of the person responsible for the rental.
* **Email Address (Email)** (`Email Address`, required: false): Enter the email address of the person responsible for the rental.
* **Rental Date (Date)** (`Rental Date`, required: false): Select the date for which you wish to rent the soft play area or equipment.
* **Rental Hours (Number)** (`Rental Hours`, required: false): Choose the number of hours you wish to rent the soft play area or equipment.
* **Rental Terms (Select Multiple)** (`Rental Terms`, required: false): Select the rental terms you are agreeing to, such as Terms and Conditions and Cancellation Policy.
* **Payment Method (Select One)** (`Payment Method`, required: false): Choose the payment method you wish to use for the rental (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or PayPal).
* **Rental Agreement (Note)** (`Rental Agreement`, required: false): You may add any additional comments or details about your rental.
* **Payment Details (Text)** (`Payment Details`, required: false): Provide any additional details about your payment method if necessary.
