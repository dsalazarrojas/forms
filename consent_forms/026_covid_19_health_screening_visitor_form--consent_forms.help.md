# Covid 19 Health Screening Visitor Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is to be completed by visitors before entering a healthcare setting to assess potential risks of COVID-19 exposure.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the form:

1. Fill in your personal details such as name, contact phone, and contact email.
2. Answer the health status question to indicate if you have any symptoms or health concerns.
3. Provide information about your recent travel history, including dates and places visited.
4. Indicate if you have any medical conditions or health insurance.
5. Enter information about your emergency contact.
6. Specify your relationship with the patient you are visiting.
7. If you are visiting a patient, enter their name, contact information, and any other relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Visitor Name** (1): Enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Visitor Contact Phone** (2): Enter your phone number where you can be reached in case we need to contact you.
* **Visitor Contact Email** (3): Enter a valid email address where we can reach you.
* **Health Status** (4): Select one or more of the following options if you are experiencing any of the symptoms:
	+ Fever
	+ Cough
	+ Shortness of Breath
* **Symptoms** (5): Select any of the following symptoms you are experiencing:
	+ Fever
	+ Sore Throat
	+ Runny Nose or Stuffy Nose
* **Traveled Recently** (6): Indicate if you have traveled recently:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Don't Know
* **Last Travel Date** (7): If you answered "True" to the previous question, enter the date of your last travel.
* **Travel History** (8): Provide details about your recent travel history, including dates and places visited.
* **Medical Conditions** (9): If you have any pre-existing medical conditions, enter a brief description.
* **Health Insurance** (10): Indicate if you have health insurance:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Emergency Contact** (11): Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship with Patient** (12): Specify your relationship with the patient you are visiting:
	+ Family
	+ Friend
	+ Doctor
	+ Other
* **Patient Name** (13): If you are visiting a patient, enter their name.
* **Patient Contact** (14): Enter the patient's contact information.
* **Patient Phone** (15): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Patient Email** (16): Enter the patient's email address.

Please complete the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a safe and efficient experience for all.
