# Veterinary Telemedicine Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather consent and other relevant information from pet owners for telemedicine services, which involve online consultations between veterinarians and pet owners.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct options for each field based on your understanding of the questions.
2. Fill out all required fields (marked as required).
3. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Pet Owner Details
* **Pet Owner Details** (`pet_owners_details`, text, optional): This field is for providing information about the pet owner, including their name, address, and contact details.

### Telemedicine Consent
* **Telemedicine Consent** (`telemedicine_consent`, select_one, optional): Select one of the available consent options to confirm your understanding of the telemedicine terms and conditions.

### Authorization
* **Authorization** (`authorization`, select_one, optional): Select one of the available authorization options to confirm who will be authorized to access the telemedicine session.

### Payment Terms
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the available payment options to understand the payment terms and conditions for the telemedicine session.

### Telehealth Privacy
* **Telehealth Privacy** (`telehealth_privacy`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the available telehealth privacy options to understand how your data will be handled and protected.

### Medical Emergencies
* **Medical Emergencies** (`medical_emergencies`, time, optional): This field is likely intended for providing the time at which medical emergencies may occur or when to seek further assistance.

### Veterinary Clinics
* **Veterinary Clinics** (`veterinary_clinics`, note, optional): This field is for providing additional information about the veterinary clinic or facility where the pet is being treated.

### Telemedicine Session Length
* **Telemedicine Session Length** (`telemedicine_session_length`, number, optional): This field is likely intended for specifying the length of the telemedicine session.

### Telemedicine Session Type
* **Telemedicine Session Type** (`telemedicine_session_type`, select_one, optional): Select one of the available telemedicine session type options to understand the format of the virtual consultation.

## Tips
* Take your time to review each field carefully to ensure accurate and complete information.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the relevant personnel for clarification or guidance.
* Submit the form once you have filled out all required fields and reviewed the information carefully.
