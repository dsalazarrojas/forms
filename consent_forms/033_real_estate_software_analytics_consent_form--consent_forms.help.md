# Real Estate Software Analytics Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is meant to obtain client consent for the use of real estate software analytics. The goal is to inform clients about the collection and usage of their data and provide transparency into how their information will be handled.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply read each question carefully and provide your consent by typing your response into the corresponding text field. You may submit the form by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Consent**:
  - <label>Client Consent</label> (`client_consent`, text, required/false): This field asks you to indicate your consent for the use of real estate software analytics. Please type "yes" or "no" to answer this question.
- **Data Collection**:
  - <label>Data Collection</label> (`data_collection`, text, required/false): In this field, please explain what type of data is being collected and how it will be used. Keep your response brief, but clear.
- **Analytics Usage**:
  - <label>Analytics Usage</label> (`analytics_usage`, text, required/false): Here, describe how the collected data will be used for analytics purposes. Be concise and provide any relevant details.
- **Transparency**:
  - <label>Transparency</label> (`transparency`, note, required/false): Take a moment to read through this note to understand how your data will be handled. You'll find information on data storage, security, and access controls. Take note of any questions or concerns you may have, but do not type in the space provided for this field.
