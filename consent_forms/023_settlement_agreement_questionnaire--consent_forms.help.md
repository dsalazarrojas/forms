# Settlement Agreement Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for tracking and recording information related to settlement agreements within our organization. It helps in keeping accurate and up-to-date records of disputes, their resolution status, and relevant details about the parties involved.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the necessary fields as you see fit for your specific case.
2. Ensure you provide as much detail as possible.
3. Save the form once you're ready for final submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Settlement Agreement Questionnaire** (1): A text field for entering any additional comments or descriptions.
- **Party A** (2): Enter the name of Party A.
- **Party A Details** (3): Enter any details about Party A.
- **Party B** (4): Enter the name of Party B.
- **Party B Details** (5): Enter any details about Party B.
- **Dispute Details** (6): Enter a description of the issue at hand.
- **Resolution Status** (7): Select the current status of the resolution process (resolved, pending, or dismissed).
- **Dispute Resolution Date** (8): Enter the date of the resolution (if resolved).
- **Next Steps** (9): Enter any next steps or actions to be taken.
- **Resolution Comments** (10): Enter any comments about the resolution process.
- **Resolution Agreed** (11): Enter any agreement details (if resolved).
- **Resolution Disagreed** (12): Enter any reasons for disagreement (if not resolved).
- **Party A Comments** (13): Enter comments from Party A's side.
- **Party B Comments** (14): Enter comments from Party B's side.
- **Form Comments** (15): Enter any comments about the form.
- **Form Signature** (16): Enter the signature of the person filling out the form.
- **Form Date** (17): Enter the date the form was filled out.
- **Form Time** (18): Enter the time the form was filled out.
- **Party A Representative** (19): Enter the name of Party A's representative.
- **Party A Email** (20): Enter Party A's representative's email.
- **Party A Phone** (21): Enter Party A's representative's phone number.
- **Party A Address** (22): Enter Party A's representative's address.
- **Party B Representative** (23): Enter the name of Party B's representative.
- **Party B Email** (24): Enter Party B's representative's email.
- **Party B Phone** (25): Enter Party B's representative's phone number.

## Tips
- Make sure to save the form once you're done filling it out.
- Double-check for any missing information before final submission.
- If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to [your support contact].
