# Liability Waiver For Services - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Liability Waiver For Services form is a document that requires the service provider and emergency contact information, consent, and other details from the service provider and their emergency contact. This form is essential for ensuring that both the service provider and the emergency contact are aware of and agree to the terms and conditions of the services being provided.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Service Provider**: This is the name of the person or entity providing the service. Please enter the name of the service provider in this field.
2. **Contact Details**: This field is for the contact information of the service provider. Please enter the phone number, email, and address of the service provider.
3. **Emergency Contact**: This field is for the contact information of the emergency contact. Please enter the name and contact details of the emergency contact.
4. **Consent**: Please select all the options that apply to the consent, such as "Client" and "Parent/Guardian".
5. **Date**: This field is for the date when the service will be provided. Please select the date from the calendar.
6. **Time**: This field is for the time when the service will be provided. Please select the time from the calendar.
7. **Signatures**: This field is for the service provider and the emergency contact to sign above.
8. **Address**: This field is for the address of the service provider.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the service provider's name and contact information is accurate.
- Please make sure that the emergency contact's name and contact information is accurate.
- Please select all the options that apply to the consent.
- Please sign the signatures field above.
- Please ensure that the address field is accurate.

This help guide is intended to be a step-by-step reference for users to complete the Liability Waiver For Services form correctly and accurately.
