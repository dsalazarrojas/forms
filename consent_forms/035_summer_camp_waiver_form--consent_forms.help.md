# summer_camp_waiver_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Camp Waiver Form is a document that parents or guardians must sign before their child can participate in our summer camp programs. It's a standard form that provides necessary information about the parent and child and is used for administrative purposes. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Summer Camp Waiver Form, follow these steps:

1.  **Summer Camp Waiver Form Title**: Enter the title of this form.
2.  **Parent Name**: Please enter your name as the parent or guardian of your child.
3.  **Home Address**: Provide your full home address to ensure we can contact you in case of emergencies or important updates.
4.  **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can easily contact you.
5.  **Email**: Provide your email address to receive important updates and communications from us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Summer Camp Waiver Form Title** (`summer_camp_waiver_form_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title or name of this form.
* **Parent Name** (`name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as the parent or guardian of your child.
* **Home Address** (`address`, text, optional): Provide your full home address to ensure we can contact you in case of emergencies or important updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can easily contact you.
* **E-mail** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to receive important updates and communications from us.
