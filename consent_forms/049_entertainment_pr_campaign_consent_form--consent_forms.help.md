# Entertainment PR Campaign Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Entertainment PR Campaign Consent Form is designed to gather information for a promotional campaign in the entertainment industry. This form helps to ensure that all parties involved in the campaign have a clear understanding of the details and can work together effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

* Fill out each field with accurate and relevant information.
* Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Prophets and Actors** (`prophets_and_actors`, text, optional)
	+ This field is for listing the prophets and actors involved in the campaign.
	+ Provide the names of the individuals participating in the campaign.
* **Location name** (`location_name`, text, optional)
	+ This field is for entering the name of the location where the campaign will take place.
	+ Make sure to include the specific name of the venue or location.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional)
	+ This field is for selecting the date of the campaign.
	+ Use the date format `MM/DD/YYYY` (e.g., 02/12/2022).
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional)
	+ This field is for selecting the time of the campaign.
	+ Use the 24-hour format `HH:MM` (e.g., 14:30).
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional)
	+ This field is for entering the email address of the person or organization involved in the campaign.
	+ Use a valid email address that can be used for communication.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional)
	+ This field is for entering the phone number of the person or organization involved in the campaign.
	+ Use a valid phone number that can be reached for communication.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ This field is for indicating your consent to participate in the campaign.
	+ Select 'yes' if you are willing to participate, 'no' if you are not, or 'no preference' if you are unsure.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional)
	+ This field is for entering any additional comments or notes about the campaign.
	+ Use this field to provide any relevant information not covered in the previous fields.

Note: The form is optional, and you are not required to fill out every field.
