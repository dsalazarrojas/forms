# Homeless Outreach Data Protection Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from homeless individuals to provide them with support and services. It is essential to understand how we will use and protect their data.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully before filling it out.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
3. Make sure to select the options that best fit your preferences.
4. Read and understand the explanations provided for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`): Please enter your name and any other relevant personal information.
* **User Consent** (`consent`): This field is used to determine if you are willing to receive support and services from our organization. Please select "Yes" if you are interested in receiving support.
* **Preferred Contact Details** (`preferred_contact_details`): If you have any preferred contact method (e.g., email, phone, mail), please enter it here.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`): This field is used to determine the frequency and method of follow-up contact. Please select "Yes" if you would like to be contacted, and choose the frequency and method that best fit your needs.
* **E-mail** (`email`): If you have an email address that you would like to use for contact, please enter it here.
* **Phone** (`phone`): If you have a phone number that you would like to use for contact, please enter it here.
* **Follow-up Frequency** (`follow_up_frequency`): If you would like to be contacted regularly, please enter the frequency (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
* **Follow-up Method** (`follow_up_method`): If you have a preferred way of being contacted (e.g., email, phone, mail), please select it here.
* **Data Use** (`data_use`): This field is used to determine how we will use your information. Please select the options that best fit your preferences.

## Tips

* Please be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for clarification.
* We will respect your preferences and follow up with you according to your selection.
* This form is designed to ensure that your data is protected and used responsibly.
