# Community Service Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Service Verification Form is used to gather detailed information about an individual's community service experience, including their personal details, service provider information, and verification statement from their supervisor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal details, including your First Name, Last Name, and Email Address.
2. Provide your Phone Number and Date of Birth.
3. Enter your Street Address, City, State, and ZIP Code.
4. Enter your service information, including the Type of Service, Service Description, Service Period, Start Date, and End Date.
5. Enter your service provider's information, including their Name, Title, Email, and Phone Number (if applicable).
6. Enter your supervisor's name, title, and signature (if applicable).
7. Verify that you completed all the required fields and tasks.
8. Provide any Additional Comments, if applicable.
9. Enter your supervisor's name and signature for verification purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Information** (`volunteer_info`, `note`, Optional): This section is a note for you to enter any personal details that you feel are relevant to your service experience.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, Required): Enter your official email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, Required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, Required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Street Address** (`address`, `text`, Required): Enter your street address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, Required): Enter the city where you served.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, Required): Enter the state where you served.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, Required): Enter your ZIP code.
* **Service Information** (`service_info`, `note`, Optional): This section is a note for you to enter any details about your service experience.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, `text`, Required): Enter the name of the organization where you served.
* **Organization Address** (`organization_address`, `text`, Required): Enter the address of the organization where you served.
* **Organization Phone** (`organization_phone`, `text`, Optional): Enter the phone number of the organization where you served (if applicable).
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, `text`, Required): Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Supervisor Title** (`supervisor_title`, `text`, Optional): Enter your supervisor's title.
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, `email`, Required): Enter your supervisor's email address.
* **Type of Service** (`service_type`, `select_one`, Required): Select the type of service you provided from the list.
* **Service Description** (`service_description`, `text`, Required): Enter a description of your service experience.
* **Service Period** (`service_period`, `text`, Required): Enter the period of time you served.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, Required): Enter the start date of your service period.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, Required): Enter the end date of your service period.
* **Total Hours Completed** (`total_hours`, `number`, Required): Enter the total number of hours you served.
* **Schedule Details** (`schedule_details`, `text`, Optional): Enter any schedule details about your service experience.
* **Performance Evaluation** (`performance`, `note`, Optional): This section is a note for your supervisor to enter their evaluation of your performance.
* **Reliability Rating** (`reliability`, `select_one`, Optional): Select the reliability rating of your service experience.
* **Quality of Work Rating** (`quality_of_work`, `select_one`, Optional): Select the quality of work rating of your service experience.
* **Attitude Rating** (`attitude`, `select_one`, Optional): Select the attitude rating of your service experience.
* **Did Volunteer Complete All Requirements** (`completed_requirements`, `select_one`, Required): Select whether you completed all the required tasks.
* **Verification Statement** (`verification_statement`, `select_one`, Required): Verify that the information entered is accurate.
* **Supervisor Signature** (`supervisor_signature`, `text`, Required): Enter the supervisor's signature as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of Verification** (`signature_date`, `date`, Required): Enter the date of the verification.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, Optional): Enter any additional comments about your service experience.
