# COVID 19 Pandemic Hair Treatment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID 19 Pandemic Hair Treatment Consent Form is used to collect essential information about clients' COVID-19 status, health conditions, and allergies to ensure their safety and the safety of service providers during the pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the client information section with your full name, phone number, and email address.
2. Select your appointment date and time.
3. Choose the type of hair service you would like to receive.
4. Answer the health assessment questions about COVID-19 symptoms, exposure, and vaccination status.
5. Check if you have any pre-existing health conditions.
6. Check if you have any allergies or sensitivities to hair products or scalp conditions.
7. Confirm your understanding of COVID-19 transmission risks and safety precautions.
8. Give your consent to receive hair treatment services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached during your appointment.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for any last-minute updates or reminders.
* **Appointment Date**: Choose the date of your scheduled appointment.
* **Appointment Time**: Choose the time of your scheduled appointment.
* **Hair Service Type**: Select the type of hair service you would like to receive (e.g., Cut, Color, Styling, Wash, Conditioning Treatment, Highlight, Other).
* **COVID-19 symptoms in past 14 days**: Answer 'True' if you have experienced any COVID-19 symptoms in the past 14 days. If yes, describe the symptoms in the next field.
* **Describe symptoms if applicable**: Describe any COVID-19 symptoms you have experienced in the past 14 days.
* **Temperature above 100.4F today**: Answer 'True' if you have a temperature above 100.4F today.
* **Exposed to COVID-19 in past 14 days**: Answer 'True' if you have been exposed to COVID-19 in the past 14 days. If yes, describe the exposure in the next field.
* **Describe exposure if applicable**: Describe any COVID-19 exposure you have experienced in the past 14 days.
* **Tested positive for COVID-19 recently**: Answer 'True' if you have tested positive for COVID-19 recently.
* **Vaccination Status**: Select your vaccination status (Fully Vaccinated, Partially Vaccinated, Not Vaccinated).
* **Pre-existing health conditions**: Answer 'True' if you have any pre-existing health conditions.
* **Describe conditions if applicable**: Describe any pre-existing health conditions.
* **Allergies to hair products**: Answer 'True' if you have any allergies or sensitivities to hair products.
* **Describe allergies**: Describe any allergies or sensitivities to hair products.
* **Sensitive scalp or skin conditions**: Answer 'True' if you have any sensitive scalp or skin conditions.
* **Describe scalp conditions**: Describe any sensitive scalp or skin conditions.
* **I understand COVID-19 transmission risks**: Answer 'Yes, I Understand' to confirm you understand the risks associated with COVID-19 transmission.
* **I acknowledge safety precautions in place**: Answer 'True' to confirm you have understood and acknowledged the safety precautions in place.
* **I consent to hair treatment service**: Answer 'Yes, I Consent' to confirm you are willing to receive hair treatment services under the current circumstances.
