# Media Participation Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Media Participation Waiver Form is a document that requires consent from participants, parents, or guardians for media participation, such as photos, videos, or audio recordings. It ensures that participants understand and agree to the use of their media in specific contexts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your name and any other required fields as prompted.
2.  Answer the questions regarding your participation in media activities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Name** (Text, Optional)
Enter your name as it appears on your identification or as you would like it to appear on any media used for your participation.

### 2. **Name** (Text, Optional)
This field is for your name.

### 3. **Parent Guardian Consent** (Select One, Optional)
Indicate whether your parent or guardian has given consent to your participation in media activities. Select "True" if they have, and "False" if not.

### 4. **Participant Consent** (Select Multiple, Optional)
Select the types of media you are willing to participate in:
*   Photos
*   Videos
*   Audio

### 5. **Participant Name** (Text, Optional)
Enter your name as it appears on your identification or as you would like it to appear on any media used for your participation.

### 6. **School or Event Name** (Text, Optional)
Provide the name of the school or event you are participating in.

### 7. **Participant Date of Birth** (Date, Optional)
Enter your date of birth in the format "month/day/year", e.g., "June 20, 1995".

### 8. **Participant Email** (Email, Optional)
Enter your email address for contact.

### 9. **Participant Phone** (Text, Optional)
Enter your phone number for contact.

### 10. **Parent or Guardian Email** (Email, Optional)
Enter your parent or guardian's email address for contact.

### 11. **Parent or Guardian Phone** (Text, Optional)
Enter your parent or guardian's phone number for contact.

### 12. **Parent Guardian Address** (Text, Optional)
Enter your parent or guardian's address for contact.

Note: Some fields are optional, meaning you can leave them blank unless they are required for your specific context. Be sure to review each field carefully to understand its necessity.
