# Protein Research Participation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Protein Research Participation Survey aims to gather information from researchers regarding their background, interests, and contact details for potential collaborations. This survey will help identify potential collaborators for the protein research project and facilitate communication with interested researchers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each page carefully before proceeding.
2. Ensure you have all necessary information before submitting the form.
3. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to the project administrator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Researchers Background** (`researcher_background`): A text field for researchers to provide a brief description of their background and experience.
* **Collaboration Interest** (`collaboration_interest`): Select the option that best describes your collaboration interest (Collaboration, Not Interested, Depends on the project).
* **Lab Name** (`lab_name`): Enter your lab name or the name of the laboratory you are representing.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your contact email address to facilitate communication.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_phone`): Enter your contact phone number to facilitate communication.
* **Researcher Name** (`researcher_name`): Enter your name as it will be used for official communication and collaboration.
* **Research Area** (`research_area`): Enter the area of research you are interested in (e.g., genetics, biochemistry, etc.).
* **Background Information** (`background_info`): A free-text field for researchers to provide any additional background information they feel is relevant.
* **Lab Address** (`lab_address`): Enter your lab address to facilitate in-person collaborations and visits.
* **Lab Location** (`lab_location`): Select the location of your lab (North, East, West, South).
* **Collaboration Method** (`collaboration_method`): Select the collaboration methods you are interested in or willing to use (Collaboration 1, Collaboration 2, Collaboration 3).
* **Follow Up Contact** (`follow_up_contact`): Select the follow-up contact methods you are willing to use (Follow Up 1, Follow Up 2, Follow Up 3).
* **Collaboration Frequency** (`collaboration_frequency`): Select how often you are willing to collaborate (Once, Twice, Thrice).
* **Collaboration Duration** (`collaboration_duration`): Select the duration of your expected collaboration (Less than 1 year, More than 1 year, Not Applicable).
* **Collaboration Type** (`collaboration_type`): Enter a brief description of the type of collaboration you are interested in or willing to undertake.

**Tips**

* Please ensure you have the necessary permissions to provide the requested information.
* If you have any concerns or questions, do not hesitate to reach out to the project administrator.
* The information provided will be kept confidential and used for the sole purpose of facilitating collaborations.

By following this guide, you should be able to complete the form accurately and submit it successfully. If you encounter any issues or have further questions, don't hesitate to contact the project administrator.
