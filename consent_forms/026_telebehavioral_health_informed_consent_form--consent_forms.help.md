# Telebehavioral Health Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from patients for telebehavioral health services, including any potential risks and benefits. It is essential to review and understand the form carefully before proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have understood the purpose and the information provided.
2. Select the correct options for each field according to your situation.
3. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
4. Submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information**: This is a text field where you can provide any additional information about yourself as a patient. Please keep it concise and relevant.
* **Consent to treatment**: Select 'Yes' if you are willing to participate in telebehavioral health services, and 'No' if you are not. Please note that this consent is required for treatment to proceed.
* **Consent to data collection**: Select 'Yes' if you are willing to have your medical information collected and stored for future reference, and 'No' if you are not.
* **Contact Information**: Please select all the options that apply to you. This will help us contact you for any follow-up purposes.
* **Contact Email**: If you have an email address, please enter it here. This will help us contact you with any updates or information about your treatment.
* **Contact Phone**: If you have a phone number, please enter it here. This will help us contact you with any urgent matters or updates.
* **Medical Provider Information**: Please select all the options that apply to you. This will help us understand your medical provider's involvement in your treatment.
* **Medical Provider Name**: If you have a medical provider, please enter their name here.
* **Medical Provider NPI**: If you have a medical provider's NPI, please enter it here. (Note: NPI stands for National Provider Identifier)
* **Medical Provider NPI**: If you do not have a medical provider, please leave this field blank. 

## Tips

* Please review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure you have understood the information and provided accurate information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* Please note that this form is not a substitute for a comprehensive medical exam or treatment plan. If you have any medical concerns, please consult a healthcare professional.
