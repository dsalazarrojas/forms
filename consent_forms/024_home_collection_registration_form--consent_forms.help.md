# home_collection_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for users to register their interest in collecting information or resources about their home.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose your preferred contact method by selecting either "Phone", "Email", or "Text" from the "Contact Info" dropdown.
2. If you have a specific schedule request, select your desired date and time using the "Schedule Request" field.
3. For "Appointment Info", you can choose multiple options from the list (Date, Time, and Service) to indicate what you would like to schedule.
4. You can add any additional notes you would like to share about your request or appointment.
5. Confirm that your details are correct using the "Confirm" field.
6. Specify who you would like to schedule with or provide service for by selecting "John Doe", "Jane Doe", or "Both" from the "Service Requestor" dropdown.
7. Choose the specific service you would like to receive or schedule (Blood Test, Lab Test, or Other) from the "Service Details" field.
8. Select the date for your scheduled appointment using the "Schedule Date" field.
9. If applicable, choose the time for your scheduled appointment using the "Schedule Time" field.
10. Finally, add any additional comments or information about your request or appointment in the "Notes" field.

* **Contact Info** (Phone, Email, Text, required: false)
	+ Choose your preferred contact method for communication.
* **Schedule Request** (Date, required: false)
	+ If you have a specific schedule request, select your desired date here.
* **Appointment Info** (Date, Time, Service, multiple, required: false)
	+ Choose multiple options to indicate what you would like to schedule.
* **Notes** (Note, required: false)
	+ Add any additional comments or information about your request or appointment.
* **Confirm** (Number, required: false)
	+ Confirm that your details are correct.
* **Service Requestor** (John Doe, Jane Doe, Both, required: false)
	+ Specify who you would like to schedule with or provide service for.
* **Service Details** (Blood Test, Lab Test, Other, multiple, required: false)
	+ Choose the specific service you would like to receive or schedule.
* **Schedule Date** (Date, required: false)
	+ Select the date for your scheduled appointment.
* **Schedule Time** (Time, required: false)
	+ If applicable, choose the time for your scheduled appointment.
* **Notes** (Note, required: false)
	+ Add any additional comments or information about your request or appointment.
