# Handyman Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this Handyman Release Form is to document the details of the services provided by a handyman, including the date, time, location, and description of the service. This form helps to ensure that all parties involved in the transaction are aware of the terms and conditions of the release.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the correct options for each field:
	* Date Of Service: Enter the date on which the service was performed.
	* Time Of Service: Enter the time at which the service was performed.
	* Location Of Service: Enter the location where the service was performed.
	* Description Of Service: Describe the service that was performed.
	* Release Date: Enter the date on which the service is being released.
2. Fill in the required information for each field.
3. Review the form to ensure all fields are filled in correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Date Of Service** (`date_of_service`, date, required/optional): The date on which the service was performed.
* **Time Of Service** (`time_of_service`, time, required/optional): The time at which the service was performed.
* **Location Of Service** (`location_of_service`, text, required/optional): The location where the service was performed.
* **Description Of Service** (`description_of_service`, text, required/optional): A description of the service that was performed.
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, date, required/optional): The date on which the service is being released.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all fields accurately and truthfully.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the handyman or their representative for clarification.
