# Medical App Development Beta Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from participants in the medical app development beta testing program, ensuring their safety and security during the testing phase.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your participation in the medical app development beta testing program.
- Fill out your name, email address, date of birth, phone number, medical history, and other relevant fields as prompted.
- Select the correct medical app information that applies to you.
- Read and agree to the informed consent.
- Provide a signature in the space provided.
- Date and time of participation and medical app information fields should be filled out accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Beta Participation Consent** (`1`, select_multiple): 
  Indicates whether you are willing to participate in the beta testing of the medical app. Please select "Yes" to indicate your agreement.
* **Name** (`2`, text): 
  Your full name, as it appears on your ID or driver's license.
* **Email Address** (`3`, text): 
  Your email address where you can be reached for updates and information regarding the beta testing process.
* **Date of Birth** (`4`, date): 
  Your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Phone Number** (`5`, text): 
  Your phone number where you can be reached for any updates or concerns.
* **Medical History** (`6`, note): 
  Briefly describe any medical conditions, allergies, or sensitivities that may impact your participation in the beta testing process.
* **Medical App Information** (`7`, select_one): 
  Select the correct category for the medical app you are testing. Options are Medical App for Beta Testing, Medical App for Development, or Other.
* **Informed Consent** (`8`, text): 
  Read and agree to the terms of participation, indicating your understanding of the potential risks and benefits associated with testing the medical app.
* **Signature** (`9`, note): 
  Sign and date your signature to confirm your agreement.
* **Date** (`10`, date): 
  The date you filled out this form.
* **Time** (`11`, time): 
  The time you filled out this form.
* **Country** (`12`, select_one): 
  Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your nationality.
* **State** (`13`, select_one): 
  Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your state of residence.
* **Zip** (`14`, text): 
  Your zip code.
* **Medical Condition** (`15`, text): 
  Briefly describe any medical conditions you may have.
* **Allergies** (`16`, select_multiple): 
  Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate any allergies you may have.
* **Emergency Contact** (`17`, text): 
  Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Consent** (`18`, note): 
  Read and agree to the informed consent.
* **Date of Participation** (`19`, date): 
  The date you start participating in the beta testing process.
* **Participation Duration** (`20`, date): 
  The date you plan to participate in the beta testing process.
* **Medical App Information 1** (`21`, text): 
  Briefly describe the medical app you are testing.
* **Medical App Information 2** (`22`, text): 
  Further describe the medical app you are testing.
* **Medical App Information 3** (`23`, text): 
  More details about the medical app you are testing.
* **Medical App Information 4** (`24`, text): 
  More details about the medical app you are testing.
* **Medical App Information 5** (`25`, text): 
  More details about the medical app you are testing.

## Tips
- Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your emergency contact person.
- If you need to modify any of the information provided, please contact the testing team.
