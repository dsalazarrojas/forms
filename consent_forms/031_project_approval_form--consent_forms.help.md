# Project Approval Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Project Approval Form is used for project managers to collect information from clients or customers regarding project approval and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Project Fee field with the approved project fee amount.
2. Provide your contact information in the Contact Information field, including your name, email address, and phone number.
3. Confirm your consent to the Terms and Conditions of the project.
4. Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm your consent.
5. If you select "No", provide an explanation in the Terms And Conditions field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Project Fee
**Project Fee** (1, required, number): Enter the approved project fee amount.

### Contact Information
**Contact Information** (2, optional, note): This field is optional, but it's a good idea to provide your contact information so we can get in touch with you about the project.

### Terms And Conditions
**Terms And Conditions** (3, optional, select_multiple): Select "Yes" to confirm you have read and agree to the project terms and conditions.

### Consent
**Consent** (4, required, select_one): Select "Yes" to confirm your consent to the project terms and conditions. If you select "No", please provide an explanation in the Terms And Conditions field.

### Name
**Name** (5, required, text): Enter your name.

### Email
**Email** (6, required, email): Enter your email address.

### Phone
**Phone** (7, required, text): Enter your phone number.

### Address
**Address** (8, required, text): Enter your mailing address.

## Tips
Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to avoid any confusion or delays.
