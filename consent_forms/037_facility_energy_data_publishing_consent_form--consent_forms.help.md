# Facility Energy Data Publishing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facility Energy Data Publishing Consent Form is a document that allows facilities to provide consent for the publication of their energy data. This form is designed to obtain a formal agreement from facility representatives to share their energy data with third parties for specific purposes, such as research, marketing, or public reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide the facility name and energy data in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the scope of data publication from the options provided (Research, Marketing, or Public Reporting).
3. Choose the data format for publication (CSV, Excel, or API).
4. Specify the data retention period (1 year, 5 years, or Perpetual).
5. Indicate whether you want to share your data with third parties.
6. Select the purpose(s) for which you want to use the data (Analysis, Research, or Education).
7. Provide your contact information.
8. Add any additional comments if necessary.
9. Sign and confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is the purpose of this form?** (`facility_energy_data_consent_form_1`, text, required): This field is a placeholder to explain the purpose of the form. Please fill it out with a brief description of the form's purpose.
* **Facility Name** (`facility_energy_data_1`, text, required): Enter the name of the facility that owns the energy data.
* **Data Publication Scope** (`facility_energy_data_publication_scope_1`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable purposes for which you want to publish your energy data (Research, Marketing, or Public Reporting).
* **Data Format** (`facility_energy_data_format_1`, select_one, required): Choose the format in which you want to publish your energy data (CSV, Excel, or API).
* **Data Retention** (`facility_energy_data_retention_1`, select_one, required): Specify how long you want to retain your energy data (1 year, 5 years, or Perpetual).
* **Do you want to share your data with third parties?** (`facility_energy_data_sharing_1`, radio, required): Indicate whether you want to share your energy data with third parties (True or False).
* **Data Use** (`facility_energy_data_use_1`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable purposes for which you want to use your energy data (Analysis, Research, or Education).
* **Contact Information** (`facility_energy_data_contact_1`, text, required): Enter your contact information so that we can reach out to you if needed.
* **Additional Comments** (`facility_energy_data_comments_1`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes you'd like to share with us.
* **Signature** (`facility_energy_data_signature_1`, checkbox, required): Check the box to indicate that you agree with the terms of this consent form.
* **Review and Confirm** (`facility_energy_data_review_1`, radio, required): Confirm that you have reviewed and agreed to the terms of this consent form (True or False).
* **Date of Consent** (`facility_energy_data_date_1`, date, required): Enter the date on which you provide your consent.
