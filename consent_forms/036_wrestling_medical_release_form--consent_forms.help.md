# Wrestling Medical Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wrestling Medical Release Form is a necessary document that requires the wrestler to provide medical information to the team. This includes their name, contact information, and medical history. The form is also used to identify their emergency contact and provide their signature.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your personal information, including your first and last name, address, phone number, and date of birth.
2. Provide your medical insurance information, including the name of your insurance provider and your insurance card number.
3. List any medical conditions you may have, including allergies and medications.
4. Identify your emergency contact, including their name, phone number, and relationship to you.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm that you have provided accurate information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Home Address** (`3`, `text`, required): Enter your home address.
* **City** (`4`, `text`, required): Enter the city where you reside.
* **Phone Number** (`5`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`6`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Date of Birth** (`7`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Weight** (`8`, `number`, required): Enter your weight.
* **Height** (`9`, `number`, required): Enter your height.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (`10`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (`11`, `text`, required): Enter the last name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`12`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`13`, `select_one`, required): Select the relationship between you and your emergency contact (e.g. Mother, Father, Spouse).
* **Medical Insurance Provider** (`14`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your medical insurance provider.
* **Insurance Card Number** (`15`, `text`, required): Enter your insurance card number.
* **Medical History** (`16`, `text`, required): List any medical conditions you may have.
* **Allergy** (`17`, `text`, required): List any allergies you may have.
* **Medical Condition** (`18`, `text`, required): List any medical conditions you may have.
* **Medication** (`19`, `text`, required): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Physical Ability** (`20`, `text`, required): Describe your physical ability (e.g. able-bodied, mobility issues, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Signature** (`21`, `note`, required): Sign and date the form to confirm that you have provided accurate information.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`22`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`23`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`24`, `text`, required): Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`25`, `select_one`, required): Select the relationship between you and your emergency contact (e.g. Mother, Father, Spouse).

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any medical conditions or allergies that may affect your ability to participate in wrestling, list them in the Medical History field.
* If you have any medications that may interact with wrestling equipment or activities, list them in the Medication field.
* Make sure to include your emergency contact's phone number and email address in case we need to contact them for your safety and well-being.
