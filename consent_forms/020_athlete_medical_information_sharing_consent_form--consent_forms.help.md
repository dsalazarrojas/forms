## Purpose
The Athlete Medical Information Sharing Consent Form is designed to allow athletes to grant permission to share their medical information with authorized parties for specific purposes related to their athletic activities. This form ensures that you understand what information will be shared, with whom it will be shared, and for what reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Please fill in your full name, Date of Birth, and the name of your primary physician or clinic.
2.  Select the organizations and individuals authorized to receive your medical information, including the organization(s) and specific individuals such as coaches, athletic trainers, or team doctors.
3.  Specify the scope of information to be shared, which includes physical examination results, sports injury and treatment history, diagnostic imaging and test results, current medications and allergies, and return-to-play status and restrictions.
4.  Choose the purpose of sharing your medical information, which can be for coordination of medical treatment, clearance for sports participation, processing of insurance claims, academic support, or other reasons.
5.  If other reasons were selected, please describe the purpose of information sharing.
6.  Choose when this consent should expire, either at the end of the current athletic season, one year from the date of signature, or on a specific date.
7.  If a specific date was selected, please enter that date.
8.  Acknowledge your right to revoke this consent in writing at any time.
9.  Acknowledge the potential for re-disclosure of this information by the recipients.
10. Sign this form electronically with your full name to indicate your consent.
11. If applicable, sign on behalf of an athlete under 18, and provide your relationship to the athlete.
