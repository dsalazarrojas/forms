# County Permission Slip Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The County Permission Slip Form is used to collect permission from students and teachers for official school activities, events, or programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with the required information.
2. Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
3. Review the form before submission to ensure that everything is correct and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Student Name
**Student Name**: This is the student's name, as it appears on their record.

### 2. Parent Name
**Parent Name**: This is the parent or guardian's name, as it appears on their record.

### 3. Date of Birth
**Date of Birth**: This is the student's date of birth.

### 4. School Address
**School Address**: This is the address of the school where the student attends.

### 5. Student Grade
**Student Grade**: Select the grade level of the student (1-8).

### 6. Student Contact Information
**Student Contact Information**: Enter any additional contact information for the student, such as phone numbers or email addresses.

### 7. School Contact Information
**School Contact Information**: Enter any additional contact information for the school, such as phone numbers or email addresses.

### 8. School Permission
**School Permission**: This field is for any special permission that requires a school official's signature.

### 9. Teacher Signature
**Teacher Signature**: This field is for the teacher's digital signature, which is typically a timestamp or a digital representation of a physical signature.
