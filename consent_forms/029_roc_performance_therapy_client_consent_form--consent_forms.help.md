# ROC Performance Therapy Client Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ROC Performance Therapy Client Consent Form is used to collect information from clients, including their emergency contact details and medical history, and to obtain their consent for remote therapy services. It is crucial for legal and confidentiality reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form in its entirety, providing accurate and complete information about yourself and your emergency contact.
2. Review the consent section and select all the options that apply to your situation.
3. Make sure to ask for clarification if you are unsure about any of the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Client Information
* **Client Information** (`client_information`, text, required): Enter your name, address, and other relevant details about yourself.
### 2. Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
### 3. Emergency Contact Name
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
### 4. Emergency Contact Relationship
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Briefly describe your relationship with your emergency contact (e.g., spouse, parent, guardian).
### 5. Emergency Contact Phone
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
### 6. Emergency Contact Relationship Status
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Status** (`emergency_contact_relationship_status`, select_one, required): Select if your emergency contact will be notified in case of an emergency.
### 7. Medical History
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Briefly describe your medical history, including any relevant health conditions or allergies.
### 8. Medical History Details
* **Medical History Details** (`medical_history_details`, text, required): Provide more details about your medical history, including any medications or treatments you are currently receiving.
### 9. Consent
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_multiple, required): Select all the options below to grant consent for remote therapy services:
	+ Please consent to receive remote therapy services.
	+ Please consent to share my contact information with my emergency contact and the therapy team.
	+ Please consent to share my medical history with my emergency contact.
