# spa_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The spa consent form is a crucial document that helps establish a transparent and trusting relationship between you and your clients. This form collects essential information from clients, ensuring their needs are met and their rights are respected. It serves as a legal and ethical foundation for your spa practices.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please review the form to understand the purpose and the sections it contains.
2. Start by providing your **spa_consent_form** (Page 1).
3. Fill out the **consent_form** section (Page 2) by selecting one of the provided options for Client Name, Client Contact Information, Procedure Description, Procedure Risks, Consent Date, Consent Time, Date of Birth, Age, and Gender. You can select only one option from this section.
4. In the **procedure_description** field on Page 3, type a detailed description of the procedure you will be performing.
5. For the **procedure_risks** field, type any potential risks or complications that may arise during the procedure.
6. If required, enter a date for **consent_date** and select the time for **consent_time** on Page 4.
7. In the **date_of_birth** field, enter your client's date of birth.
8. Provide your client's **age**.
9. Select your client's **gender**.
10. Enter your client's **client_name** on Page 5.
11. Provide your client's contact information, including their **client_contact_information** and **client_contact_number** on Page 6.
12. Sign the **signature** page to confirm your client's consent.
13. If necessary, you can add any additional comments or information in the **spa_consent_form_3** field on Page 7.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **spa_consent_form (Page 1)**: This field is the initial page where you will enter your client's spa services consent.
* **consent_form (Page 2)**: Choose one of the provided options for your client's details.
* **procedure_description (Page 3)**: Provide a detailed description of the procedure you will be performing.
* **procedure_risks (Page 4)**: List any potential risks or complications that may arise during the procedure.
* **consent_date (Page 5)**: If required, enter a date for consent.
* **consent_time (Page 6)**: If required, select the time for consent.
* **date_of_birth (Page 7)**: Enter your client's date of birth.
* **age (Page 8)**: Provide your client's age.
* **gender (Page 9)**: Select your client's gender.
* **client_name (Page 10)**: Enter your client's name.
* **client_contact_information (Page 11)**: Provide your client's contact information.
* **client_contact_number (Page 12)**: Enter your client's contact number.
* **signature (Page 13)**: Sign to confirm your client's consent.
* **spa_consent_form_3 (Page 14)**: Add any additional comments or information if necessary.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you understand each field's purpose before completing the form.
- If you need help or clarification on any section, refer to your spa management system's guidelines and best practices.
- Remember to keep your client's information up-to-date and accurate to maintain a healthy and trustworthy relationship.
