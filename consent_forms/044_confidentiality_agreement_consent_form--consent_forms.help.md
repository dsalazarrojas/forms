# Confidentiality Agreement Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to establish a confidentiality agreement between parties regarding the sharing and protection of confidential information. This form ensures both parties understand their obligations, permissions, and responsibilities related to the handling and usage of confidential information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form efficiently and accurately, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form section by section, reading the hints for each field to understand the required information.
2. Ensure that you have the necessary information and documentation before proceeding.
3. If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to ask for clarification or guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Party Information

* **Full Legal Name** (`party_name`): Enter your complete name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address** (`address`): Enter your full mailing address.
* **City** (`city`): Enter the city where you reside or work.
* **State** (`state`): Enter the state where you reside or work.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`): Enter your postal or zip code.

### Page 2: Agreement Details

* **Agreement Type** (`agreement_type`): Select the type of NDA (Non-Disclosure Agreement) that applies to your situation (e.g., Mutual, One-Way, Employee, etc.).
* **Disclosing Party** (`disclosing_party`): Identify who is sharing the confidential information.
* **Receiving Party** (`receiving_party`): Identify who is receiving the confidential information.
* **Purpose of Agreement** (`purpose`): Explain the reason for the confidentiality agreement.
* **Effective Date** (`effective_date`): Specify the date when the agreement becomes effective.
* **Agreement Duration** (`duration`): Choose how long the agreement lasts (e.g., 1 year, 2 years, 3 years, etc.).
* **Exclusions from Confidentiality** (`exclusions`): If applicable, specify any information that is not considered confidential.
* **Definition of Confidential Information** (`confidential_info_definition`): Explain what constitutes confidential information.
* **Obligations** (`obligations`): Describe your responsibilities under the agreement.
* **Permitted Disclosures** (`permitted_disclosures`): Explain who you can share the information with.
* **Return of Materials** (`return_of_materials`): Specify when and how materials are returned.

### Page 3: Special Provisions and Certifications

* **Definition of Governing Law** (`governing_law`): Choose the jurisdiction under which this agreement falls (e.g., California, New York, etc.).
* **Remedies for Breach** (`remedies`): Explain any consequences of a breach of this agreement.
* **Have You Had Legal Review** (`legal_review`): Confirm if you or your representative has received legal review from a counsel.
* **Attorney Name** (`attorney_name`): If applicable, enter the name of the lawyer who reviewed the document.
* **Voluntary Agreement** (`voluntary_agreement`): Confirm if you are signing this agreement voluntarily.
* **Understanding Statement** (`understanding_statement`): Confirm that you understand the terms of this agreement.
* **Consent Statement** (`consent_statement`): Confirm that you consent to the terms of this agreement.
* **Date of Signature** (`signature_date`): Enter the date you signed this agreement.
* **Witness Name** (`witness_name`): If applicable, enter the witness's name who witnessed the signature.
* **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`): If applicable, enter the witness's signature.
