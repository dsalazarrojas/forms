# NGO Volunteer Photo Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from NGO volunteers for photo and video recordings made of them during events and activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as it should appear in photos and videos.
2. Enter the name of the event and location where you will be volunteering.
3. Choose the date of the event.
4. Select the type of content you wish to release (Photo, Video, or both).
5. Enter the name of your organization.
6. Enter your contact email address.
7. Enter your contact phone number.
8. Enter the contact information for your emergency contact.
9. Check the box indicating you have read and understood the terms and conditions.
10. Sign the acknowledgement section.
11. Sign the volunteer signature section with your name and date.
12. Sign the photo release section with your name and date.
13. Select the type of release you want to grant (Full, Limited, or No).
14. Enter the date when the release expires.
15. Enter any additional notes about the photo release.
16. Enter any terms and conditions for the photo release.
17. Enter a statement indicating you are releasing your image and likeness.
18. Upload a signature image of yourself.
19. Sign the signature section with your name and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Name** (1): Enter your name as it should appear in photos and videos.
* **Event Name** (2): Enter the name of the event and location where you will be volunteering.
* **Date of Event** (3): Enter the date of the event.
* **Location of Event** (4): Enter the location of the event.
* **Photo/Video** (5): Select the type of content you wish to release (Photo, Video, or both).
* **Release of Content** (6): Enter any conditions for the release.
* **Organization Name** (7): Enter your organization's name.
* **Contact Email** (8): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (9): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Emergency Contact** (10): Enter the contact information for your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (11): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Consent** (12): Check the box indicating you have read and understood the terms and conditions.
* **Terms and Conditions** (13): Read and understand the terms and conditions.
* **Acknowledgement** (14): Sign the acknowledgement section.
* **Volunteer Signature** (15): Sign the volunteer signature section with your name and date.
* **Photographer Signature** (16): Sign the photo release section with your name and date.
* **Witness Signature** (17): Sign the witness signature section with your name and date.
* **Photo Release Date** (18): Enter the date when the release expires.
* **Release Type** (19): Select the type of release you want to grant (Full, Limited, or No).
* **Release Expires** (20): Enter the date when the release expires.
* **Photo Release Notes** (21): Enter any additional notes about the photo release.
* **Photo Release Terms** (22): Enter any terms and conditions for the photo release.
* **Photo Release** (23): Enter a statement indicating you are releasing your image and likeness.
* **Signature Image** (24): Upload a signature image of yourself.
* **Signature** (25): Sign the signature section with your name and date.

Note: This form is mandatory for all volunteers, and it's essential to complete it carefully and accurately to ensure that your image and likeness are used respectfully and responsibly.
