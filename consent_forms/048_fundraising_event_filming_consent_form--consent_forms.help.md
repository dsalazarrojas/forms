# Fundraising Event Filming Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from participants in a fundraising event filming, ensuring they are aware and comfortable with being filmed or photographed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Participant Name.
2. Provide your valid email address.
3. Indicate your consent to be filmed or photographed by selecting 'True' for Yes, 'False' for No, 'Undecided' to indicate you are unsure, or 'I'm Not Sure' if you are not certain.
4. Enter your contact person's name.
5. Provide your contact information (name and phone number).
6. Enter the name of the event you are participating in.
7. Select the date of the event.
8. Choose the time of the event.
9. Enter the location of the event.
10. (Optional) Enter your team's name and team contact information (name and phone number) if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you with any event updates or information.
* **Photo/Video Consent**: Indicate your consent to be filmed or photographed during the event by selecting 'True' (yes), 'False' (no), or select 'Undecided' or 'I'm Not Sure' if you are unsure.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person we should contact in case of an emergency or question.
* **Contact Info**: Provide a valid phone number and any additional contact information we can use to reach out to you.
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event you are participating in.
* **Event Date**: Select the date of the event.
* **Event Time**: Select the time of the event.
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event where the filming will take place.
* **Team Name** (Optional): If you are part of a team, enter your team's name.
* **Team Contact** (Optional): If you are part of a team, enter your team contact information (name and phone number).
