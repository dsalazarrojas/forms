# Waxing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Waxing Consent Form is designed to gather necessary information from customers before providing a waxing service. This form ensures that the customer is informed and aware of the procedure's details, including any risks or contraindications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the customer's name in the **customer_name** field.
2. Enter the date of your visit in the **date_of_visit** field.
3. Provide a brief description of the procedure you are seeking in the **procedure_details** field.
4. Select your level of understanding of the information provided in the **consent** field.
5. If required, sign your name in the **signature** field.
6. If required, provide a signature for the customer in the **customer_signature** field.
7. If necessary, enter any relevant medical history or allergies in the **medical_history** and **allergies** fields respectively.
8. Enter contact information for emergency situations in the **emergency_contact** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer_name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be written in our records.
* **date_of_visit**: Enter the date you plan to visit us for the waxing service.
* **procedure_details**: Briefly describe the procedure you are seeking, including any specific areas of concern.
* **consent**: Select one of the options to indicate your level of understanding of the information provided:
	+ I have read and understand the information provided
	+ I have not read and do not understand the information provided
	+ I have partially read and do not understand the information provided
* **signature**: If required, please sign this field with your signature.
* **customer_signature**: If required, please sign this field with your signature, as we require a separate signature from you for your records.
* **medical_history**: If necessary, enter any relevant medical history or concerns.
* **allergies**: If necessary, enter any allergies or medical conditions that may impact your treatment.
* **emergency_contact**: Enter contact information for emergency situations, including name, phone number, and address.

## Tips
* Please take the time to carefully read and understand the information provided before selecting your level of understanding.
* If you are unsure about any information, please ask us and we will be happy to clarify.
* If you have any medical concerns or allergies, please inform us before proceeding with the procedure.
