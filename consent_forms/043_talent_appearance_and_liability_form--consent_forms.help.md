# Talent Appearance And Liability Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Talent Appearance and Liability Form is designed to collect information about your appearance and contact details for talent casting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please review each field carefully and answer accordingly.
2. For each field, provide accurate and truthful information.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to the contact person listed below for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Appearance Details** (appearance_details): This field is used to describe your overall appearance. Please provide a brief description of your height, weight, hair color, eye color, and any other distinguishing features.
* **Additional Appearance Information** (appearance_info): If there's more information you'd like to share about your appearance, such as tattoos, scars, or birthmarks, please describe them in this field.
* **Consent** (consent): We need your explicit consent to use your image or likeness. Please provide a "yes" or "no" answer to this field.
* **Contact Information 1** (contact_details): Please provide your full name, phone number, and email address so we can get in touch with you about this opportunity.
* **Contact Information 2** (contact_details_2): This field is for any additional contact information you'd like to share, such as a LinkedIn profile or agency contact.
* **Appearance Date** (appearance_date): If there's a specific date you'd like to be considered for, please enter it in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Appearance Time** (appearance_time): If you have a preferred time for your appearance, please enter it in the format HH:MM AM/PM.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to the contact person listed below.
* We appreciate your cooperation and understanding in this process.
