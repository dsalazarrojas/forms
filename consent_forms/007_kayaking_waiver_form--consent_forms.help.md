# Kayaking Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kayaking Waiver Form is designed to gather participant information, including contact details, emergency contacts, and medical information. This form is used to obtain informed consent from participants and their guardians (if applicable) before they start the kayaking activity.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Please fill in your participant information**:
	* Participant Name
	* Contact Information (email, phone number, etc.)
	* Participant Age
	* Participant Height
	* Participant Date of Birth
	* Participant Email
	* Participant Phone
	* Participant Address
2. **Provide guardian information (if applicable)**:
	* Guardian Name
	* Relationship
	* Guardian Phone
	* Guardian Email
	* Guardian Address
3. **Sign and date the waiver sections**:
	* I understand this information will be kept private and used solely for participant administration purposes.
	* I agree to participate in this kayaking activity.
	* I understand that there are risks involved in kayaking and I am aware of the inherent risks involved.
4. **Sign the participant and guardian sections**:
	* Participant Signature
	* Guardian Signature

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Information**: Provide your contact information, including your email address and phone number, so we can reach you in case of emergencies.
* **Participant Age**: Enter your age as of the current date.
* **Participant Height**: Enter your height in meters (m).
* **Participant Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Participant Address**: Enter your address, including your street address and postal code.
* **Guardian Name**: If you are under 18, enter the name of your legal guardian or parent.
* **Relationship**: Describe your relationship with your guardian (e.g., mother, father, guardian, etc.).
* **Guardian Phone**: Enter your guardian's phone number.
* **Guardian Email**: Enter your guardian's email address.
* **Guardian Address**: Enter your guardian's address.
* **Participant Signature**: Sign and date this section to confirm that you have read and understood this waiver form.
* **Guardian Signature**: Sign and date this section to confirm that you have read and understood this waiver form.
* **I understand this information will be kept private and used solely for participant administration purposes.**: Sign and date this section to indicate that you understand how your information will be used.
* **I agree to participate in this kayaking activity.**: Sign and date this section to indicate that you agree to participate in the kayaking activity.
* **I understand that there are risks involved in kayaking and I am aware of the inherent risks involved.**: Sign and date this section to indicate that you understand the risks involved in kayaking.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: If you are under 18, enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact.
* **Participant Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments for your participant record.
* **Guardian Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments for your guardian's record.
* **Participant Address**: Enter your address, including your street address and postal code.
* **Guardian Address**: Enter your guardian's address.
* **Participant Emergency Contact Name**: If you are under 18, enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact.

## Tips
- Ensure that you fill out the form accurately and honestly.
- If you are under 18, ensure that you have your guardian's signature and consent before participating in the activity.
- If you are a guardian, ensure that you have your child's signature and consent before participating in the activity.
