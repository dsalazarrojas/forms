# SUD Group Participation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant details, schedule information, support group details, recovery program information, and contact information for the SUD group.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information ready.
2. Enter your participant details in the first field.
3. Provide your schedule information in the second field.
4. Enter your support group information in the third field.
5. Provide your recovery program information in the fourth field.
6. Enter your contact information in the fifth field.
7. Finally, click the "Submit" button to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details (1)**: Please enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself as a participant.
* **Schedule Information (2)**: Please provide your schedule information, such as your availability for meetings or sessions.
* **Support Group Information (3)**: Please enter the details of your support group, including the group name, location, and leader's contact information.
* **Recovery Program Information (4)**: Please provide information about the recovery program you are participating in, including the program type and any specific dates or milestones.
* **Contact Information (5)**: Please enter your contact information, such as your email address, phone number, and any other relevant details.
* **Submit (6)**: Once you have completed all the fields, click the "Submit" button to submit your form.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have the necessary consent to participate in the SUD group before filling out this form.
* Please review your information carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you are experiencing any distress or concerns, please contact a mental health professional or call emergency services.
* This form is not a substitute for professional medical or psychological assessment.
* Please note that this form is a one-time data collection form and is not intended to be used as a regular assessment or check-in tool.
