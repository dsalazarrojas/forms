# Opt Out Fundraiser Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to capture information from individuals who wish to opt-out of fundraising efforts. It is used to gather private and secure information and may be shared with relevant parties for the purpose of respecting the donor's wish to opt-out.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Donor Name** in the corresponding text field.
2. Provide your **Home Address** in the text field below.
3. If you have a **Work Address**, please fill it in the next text field.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the text field.
5. In the text field, please provide a brief **Reason for Opting Out** to help us understand your decision.
6. If you have any additional **Note**, please enter it in the text field below.
7. If you have an **Email Address**, please enter it in the text field.
8. If you wish to **Donation**, please enter the amount in the number field.
9. If you wish to specify the **Amount**, please enter it in the number field below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Name** (`donor_name`, text, optional): Please fill in your name as it should be listed on any further correspondence from us.
* **Home Address** (`donor_address_1`, text, optional): Please enter your home address as you would like it to be shared with relevant parties.
* **Work Address** (`donor_address_2`, text, optional): If you have a work address, please enter it in the field below, so we can update your records accordingly.
* **Phone Number** (`donor_phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number in the format of 10 digits.
* **Reason for Opting Out** (`reason_for_opting_out`, text, optional): A brief explanation of your decision to opt-out will help us understand your choice.
* **Note** (`donor_note`, note, optional): Please use this field to add any additional comments or information you would like to share with us.
* **Email** (`donor_email`, email, optional): If you would like to be contacted via email, please enter your email address here.
* **Donation** (`donor_donation`, number, optional): If you have made a donation, please enter the amount in the field below.
* **Amount** (`donor_amount`, number, optional): If you wish to specify the amount, please enter it in the number field.

## Tips

* Please ensure all required fields are filled in accurately to ensure a smooth and efficient process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us via phone or email.
