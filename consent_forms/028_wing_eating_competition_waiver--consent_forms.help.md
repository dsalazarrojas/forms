# Wing Eating Competition Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants in the Wing Eating Competition, ensuring their safety and providing necessary medical clearance.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form accurately and honestly.
- Ensure that all fields are completed, especially those with a "required" flag.
- If you have any questions or need clarification on a particular field, please ask the competition organizers for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (id: 1)**: Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name (id: 2)**: Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email (id: 3)**: Enter your personal email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone (id: 4)**: Enter your personal phone number that we can use to contact you in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact First Name (id: 5)**: Enter the first name of the person you have authorized to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name (id: 6)**: Enter the last name of the person you have authorized to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (id: 7)**: Enter the contact phone number of the person you have authorized to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Guardian First Name (id: 8)**: (Optional) Enter the first name of the person responsible for your supervision during the competition.
* **Guardian Last Name (id: 9)**: (Optional) Enter the last name of the person responsible for your supervision during the competition.
* **Guardian Phone (id: 10)**: (Optional) Enter the contact phone number of the person responsible for your supervision during the competition.
* **Guardian Phone 2 (id: 11)**: (Optional) Enter an additional contact phone number of the person responsible for your supervision during the competition.
* **Emergency Contact Permission (id: 12)**: Check "True" if you authorize your emergency contact to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Permission** (id: 13): This field is duplicate of id: 12, and is provided for redundancy.
* **Participant Age (id: 14)**: Enter your age.
* **Participant Height (id: 15)**: Enter your height in centimeters.
* **Participant Weight (id: 16)**: Enter your weight in kilograms.
* **Participant Diet (id: 17)**: Describe any dietary restrictions or limitations you have.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship (id: 18)**: Describe your relationship with your emergency contact (e.g., parent, guardian, etc.).
* **Participant Medical Condition (id: 19)**: List any medical conditions that may impact your participation in the competition.
* **Participant Medication (id: 20)**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Participant Allergies (id: 21)**: Describe any allergies you have.
* **Participant Diet 1 (id: 22)**: Describe your first dietary restriction or limitation.
* **Participant Diet 2 (id: 23)**: Describe your second dietary restriction or limitation.
* **Participant Medication 1 (id: 24)**: List your first medication.
* **Participant Medication 2 (id: 25)**: List your second medication.

Please note that all fields with an "id" number are required fields. If you have any questions or need further clarification on a specific field, please reach out to the competition organizers.
