# legal_representation_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "legal_representation_consent_form" is used to obtain client consent for legal representation.

## How To Complete This Form
This form should be completed in the following steps:

1. Select the client's consent to representation.
2. Choose the type of representation the client is agreeing to.
3. Enter any additional details about the representation.
4. Fill in the client's contact information.
5. Enter any notes or comments about the representation.
6. Assign the representation to a user.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Consent** (`client_consent`, select_multiple, required): Select whether the client has given consent to representation.
* **Representation Type** (`representation_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of representation the client is agreeing to.
* **Representation Details** (`representation_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about the representation.
* **Legal Name** (`legal_name`, text, optional): Enter the client's legal name.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the client's address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the client's phone number.
* **E-mail** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the client's email address.
* **Representation Fee** (`representation_fees`, number, optional): Enter the representation fee.
* **Representation Duration** (`representation_duration`, date, optional): Enter the duration of the representation.
* **Representation Date** (`representation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the representation.
* **Time of Day** (`time_of_day`, time, optional): Enter the time of the representation.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, optional): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the representation.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, text, optional): Assign the representation to a user.
