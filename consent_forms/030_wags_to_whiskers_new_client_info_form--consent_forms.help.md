# Wags To Whiskers New Client Info Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from new clients who are interested in working with Wags To Whiskers. It is used for onboarding new clients and gathering their basic contact and demographic details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
- Fill in your first name.
- Enter a valid email address.
- Provide your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted.
- Enter your address (this will help us get you on the schedule).
- Enter your date of birth.
- Select your client type (individual or business).
- Enter your company name and job title, if applicable.
- Choose your company size (number of employees).
- Enter the date and time of your visit.
- Enter a client number (if provided by Wags To Whiskers).
- Add any additional notes or comments (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address to help us get you on the schedule.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Client type** (`client_type`, select_one, optional): Choose your client type - either individual or business.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter your company name, if applicable.
* **Job title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title, if applicable.
* **Company size** (`company_size`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the size of your company (1-10, 11-50, 51-100, 101-200, 200+).
* **Date of visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, optional): Enter the date of your visit (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Time of visit** (`time_of_visit`, time, optional): Enter the time of your visit (HH:MM).
* **Client number** (`client_number`, text, optional): Enter a client number provided by Wags To Whiskers.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your visit.
