# Antidepressant Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Antidepressant Informed Consent Form is a document that requires patients to provide informed consent for antidepressant medication treatment. This form helps clinicians and healthcare providers understand the patient's understanding of the risks and benefits of antidepressant therapy and their willingness to consider alternative treatments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out your personal details, including your full legal name and date of birth.
*   Specify the proposed medication name.
*   Read and acknowledge the potential risks and side effects of the medication.
*   Indicate if you have discussed alternative treatments with your healthcare provider.
*   Provide a name and phone number of an emergency contact person, if applicable.
*   Confirm your consent to start treatment by selecting "Yes I consent" and provide your healthcare provider's name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient name** (`Patient name`, `text`, required): Enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
    *   This field is required to ensure accurate identification and medical record keeping.
*   **Date of birth** (`Date of birth`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your birth in YYYY-MM-DD format for accurate medical records.
    *   This field helps healthcare providers track your medical history and potential medication interactions.
*   **Proposed medication name** (`Proposed medication name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the antidepressant medication prescribed for you.
    *   This field ensures you and your healthcare provider are on the same page with medication details.
*   **I understand the risks and potential side effects** (`I understand the risks and potential side effects`, `select_one`, required): Select "Acknowledged" if you understand the risks and potential side effects of the medication.
    *   This field is critical to ensure you are aware of potential side effects and can make informed decisions about your treatment.
*   **Alternatives to medication discussed** (`Alternatives to medication discussed`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all alternative treatments discussed with your healthcare provider, such as psychotherapy or lifestyle modifications.
    *   This field helps healthcare providers understand your treatment preferences and options.
*   **Consent to begin treatment** (`Consent to begin treatment`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes I consent" to start treatment with the prescribed medication.
    *   This field is essential for initiating medication treatment.
*   **Emergency contact name and phone** (`Emergency contact name and phone`, `text`, required): Provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact person, if applicable.
    *   This field is optional but recommended for your safety and well-being.
*   **Prescriber name** (`Prescriber name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your healthcare provider who is prescribing the medication.
    *   This field ensures accurate record keeping and communication between you and your healthcare provider.
*   **Consent to treatment** (`Consent to treatment`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your consent to start treatment with the prescribed medication.
    *   Select "Yes I consent" to start treatment.
