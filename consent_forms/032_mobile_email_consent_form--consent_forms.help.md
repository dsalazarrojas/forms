# Mobile Email Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mobile Email Consent Form is used to collect information about your email preferences and your consent to be contacted by the team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each field's purpose and requirements.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Choose how you would like to be contacted by the team in the "Email Preferences" field.
4. Provide any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field (optional).
5. Review and agree to the terms of communication in the "I Agree" field.
6. Enter any additional information as required in the "User Input", "Phone", "Date", "Time", "Team Name", and "Department" fields.
7. Submit the form to complete the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`User Input`, note, required: false): This field is for any additional information you would like to provide to us. It's a text area where you can type anything you think is relevant to your account.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address to which we will be contacting you.
* **I Agree** (`I Agree`, select_one, required: false): Please select "True" to agree to be contacted by the team, and "False" or "Prefer not to send" if you do not want to be contacted.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required: false): If you have a phone number, enter it here. This is optional but helps us reach you in case we need to contact you urgently.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, required: false): If you have a specific date in mind, enter it here. This is optional but helps us schedule appointments or events with you.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, required: false): If you have a preferred time, enter it here. This is optional but helps us schedule appointments or events with you.
* **Team Name** (`Team Name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your team or department if you want to receive emails from specific teams.
* **Department** (`Department`, text, required: false): Enter the department you belong to if you want to receive emails from specific departments.
* **Email Preferences** (`Email Preferences`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose how often you would like to receive emails from us. You can select "Send emails on weekdays", "Send emails on weekends", or "Send emails daily".
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, required: false): Provide any additional notes or comments about your email preferences or communication needs. This is optional but helps us understand your needs better.
