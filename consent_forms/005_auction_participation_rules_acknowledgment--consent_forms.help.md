# Auction Participation Rules Acknowledgment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from auction participants, ensuring they understand and agree to the rules, regulations, and terms of the auction. It covers essential details such as personal contact information, auction event specifics, and acknowledgments related to payment terms, item inspection, and liability release.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Participant Full Name**, which is your legal name for the auction.
2. Enter your **Email Address**, which will be used as the primary contact method.
3. Provide your **Phone Number**, which will be used for additional contact purposes.
4. Fill in your **Mailing Address**, which will be used for official correspondence.
5. If you have a **Bidder Registration Number**, please enter it. This is not required but will help with tracking your bid.
6. Enter the **Auction Event Name** to ensure you're participating in the correct event.
7. Select the **Auction Date** of the event you're participating in.
8. Choose your **Participation Type**, which determines how you'll participate in the auction (In-Person Bidding, Online Bidding, etc.).
9. Confirm you've **Read and Understand** the auction rules and regulations.
10. Acknowledge that all **Bids Are Binding** and legally enforceable.
11. Understand that a **Buyer Premium Percentage** will be added to winning bids.
12. Agree to **Pay Within Specified Terms** if you win.
13. Confirm you **Understand Items Are Sold As-Is** and you've had an opportunity to inspect them.
14. Agree to **Remove Purchased Items By Specified Deadline**.
15. Release the auction house from **Liability Related to Items Purchased**.
16. Consent to being **Photographed or Recorded During the Auction**.
17. Acknowledge your **Information Will Be Used Per the Privacy Policy**.
18. Type your **Full Name As Electronic Signature** for authentication.
19. Select the **Date of Acknowledgment** for this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name**: Your legal name for the auction.
* **Email Address**: Primary contact method for official correspondence.
* **Phone Number**: Additional contact method for communication.
* **Mailing Address**: Full address for official correspondence.
* **Bidder Registration Number**: Your assigned bidder ID, if already registered.
* **Auction Event Name**: Ensure you're participating in the correct event.
* **Auction Date**: Date of the auction event.
* **Participation Type**: How you'll participate in the auction (in-person, online, etc.).
* **Rules Read Acknowledgment**: Confirm you've read and understand the auction rules.
* **Binding Bid Acknowledgment**: Confirm bids are legally binding.
* **Buyer Premium Acknowledgment**: Understand a buyer premium will be added to winning bids.
* **Payment Terms Acknowledgment**: Agree to payment terms if you win.
* **Item Inspection Acknowledgment**: Confirm you understand items are sold as-is.
* **Removal Deadline Acknowledgment**: Agree to remove purchased items by specified deadline.
* **Liability Release**: Release the auction house from liability related to items purchased.
* **Photography Consent**: Consent to being photographed or recorded during the auction.
* **Data Privacy Acknowledgment**: Understand your information will be used per the privacy policy.
* **Electronic Signature**: Type your full name for authentication.
* **Signature Date**: Date of acknowledgment.

This comprehensive guide helps ensure that participants complete the form accurately, understand their roles and responsibilities, and are aware of the terms and conditions of the auction.
