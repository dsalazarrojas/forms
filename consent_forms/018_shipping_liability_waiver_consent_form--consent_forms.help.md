# Shipping Liability Waiver Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shipping Liability Waiver Consent Form is a document that provides a legal waiver of liability between the shipper and the consignee. This form is used to protect both parties from potential risks or liabilities associated with the shipment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **shipping_liability_waiver_consent**: Enter a brief description of the shipment, including the goods or services being transported.
2. **contact_information**: Provide your contact information, including your name and any relevant contact details.
3. **shipping_company**: Select the company type that best describes your role in the shipment (Shipper or Consignee).
4. **shipper_details**: Provide any additional details about your role in the shipment.
5. **terms_and_conditions**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the shipment.
6. **shipping_risks**: List any potential risks or liabilities associated with the shipment.
7. **risk_acknowledgement**: Acknowledge that you are aware of the risks involved and are willing to accept them.
8. **date_shipper_signed**: Enter the date you signed this form.
9. **date_shipper_signed_time**: Enter the time you signed this form.
10. **date_consignee_signed**: Enter the date the consignee signed this form.
11. **date_consignee_signed_time**: Enter the time the consignee signed this form.
12. **signed_by**: Select who signed this form (Shipper or Consignee).
13. **shipper_signature**: Sign your name as the shipper.
14. **consignee_signature**: Sign your name as the consignee.
15. **date_shipper_signed_date**: Enter the date you signed this form (same as date_shipper_signed).
16. **date_shipper_signed_time**: Enter the time you signed this form (same as date_shipper_signed_time).
17. **terms_and_conditions_version**: Select whether you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
18. **terms_and_conditions_url**: Enter the URL of the terms and conditions document.
19. **terms_and_conditions_link**: Enter the link to the terms and conditions document.

## Tips
* Make sure to read and understand the terms and conditions before signing this form.
* Enter accurate dates and times for each section.
* Sign this form with your actual signature.
* Make sure to keep a copy of this form for your records.
