# Internet Access Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Internet Access Consent Form is a document that aims to collect user information, internet access acceptance, and other relevant data from users. This form is essential for gathering informed consent and ensuring users understand their internet access rights and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user information in the "User Information" section.
2. Select the types of internet access you wish to access.
3. Choose your acceptance of internet access.
4. Read and understand the "Terms and Conditions" section.
5. Sign the form to confirm your understanding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, optional): Please enter your name and any other relevant information you would like to provide.
* **Internet Access** (`internet_access`, select multiple, optional): Select all the types of internet access you would like to have. You can choose from "Email", "Internet", and "Network".
* **Risk Acceptance** (`risk_acceptance`, select one, optional): Choose whether you accept or decline internet access.
* **Data Collection** (`data_collection`, note, optional): Please read the "Terms and Conditions" section carefully and understand how your data will be collected.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, text, optional): This section outlines the rules and guidelines for internet access. Please read and understand it carefully.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, optional): This section is to record the date you signed this form.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Please sign this form to confirm your understanding of the terms and conditions.
* **Decline** is the default choice when no other option is selected for **Risk Acceptance**.
* **Note:** Please ensure you understand that "Decline" means you are not accessing the internet.
