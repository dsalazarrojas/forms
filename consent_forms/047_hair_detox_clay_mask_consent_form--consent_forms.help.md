# Hair Detox Clay Mask Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from clients about their hair detox clay mask treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Client Details" page with your personal information, if applicable.
2. On the "Health Information" page, provide any relevant health details that may affect the treatment.
3. On the "Treatment Details" page, specify the details of the treatment you're about to receive.
4. Sign the "eSignature" field with your digital signature, if applicable.
5. Finally, fill out the "Date" field with the date of the treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details** (`client_details`, text, optional): This page is where you can enter your personal details. If you have a client ID or reference number, please enter it here.
* **Health Information** (`health_information`, text, optional): Please provide any relevant health information that may affect the treatment. This could include allergies, medical conditions, or previous treatments.
* **Treatment Details** (`treatment_details`, text, optional): Specify the details of the treatment you're about to receive, including the type of mask used and any other relevant information.
* **eSignature** (`e_signature`, text, optional): Please sign with your digital signature to confirm your consent for the treatment.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the treatment.
