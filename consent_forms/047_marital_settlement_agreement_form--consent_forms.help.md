# Marital Settlement Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document the terms and conditions of a marital settlement agreement between two parties involved in a divorce or separation. It helps to establish a formal understanding of the assets, property, and support arrangements to be considered during the separation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal information, including your first name, last name, date of marriage, and date of separation (if applicable).
2. Provide details about the properties owned by both parties, including addresses, ownership types, and approximate values.
3. List the assets you are willing to surrender or agree to maintain.
4. Specify any support arrangements for children, including custody, visitation, and financial provisions.
5. Sign and date the agreement in the designated spaces provided for each party and the mediator (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your full first name as it appears on your identification documents or driver's license.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/true): Enter your full last name as it appears on your identification documents or driver's license.
* **Date of Marriage** (`date_of_marriage`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you were married, if applicable.
* **Date of Separation** (`date_of_separation`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you and your spouse separated, if applicable.
* **Property Owned** (`property_owned`, `text`, required/true): List all properties owned by you, including addresses, types of property (e.g., house, car, land), and approximate values.
* **Assets List** (`assets_list`, `text`, required/optional): List any other assets you are willing to surrender or maintain, including personal property, investments, or other items of value.
* **Support Arrangements** (`support_arrangements`, `text`, required/optional): Specify any support arrangements for children, including custody, visitation, and financial provisions.
* **Signature of Party 1** (`signature_of_party1`, `text`, required/true): Sign and date the agreement in the space provided for Party 1.
* **Signature of Party 2** (`signature_of_party2`, `text`, required/true): Sign and date the agreement in the space provided for Party 2.
* **Signature of Mediator** (`signature_of_mediator`, `text`, required/optional): Sign and date the agreement in the space provided for the mediator, if applicable.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are accurately filled out and signed by both parties and, if applicable, the mediator.
* Review and edit the agreement carefully to ensure that all terms and conditions are understood and agreed upon.
* Consult with your attorney or seek legal counsel if you are unsure about any aspect of the form.
