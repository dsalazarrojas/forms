# Emergency Contact Directory Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from individuals to include their contact information in an emergency contact directory.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Click on the "First Page" section.
2. Select one of the consent options provided:
	* Emergency Contact Directory
	* Internal Directory
	* No Consent
3. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Page
* **First Page**: This is the starting section of the form, where you will select your consent option.

### Consent
* **Consent**: Select one of the three options provided:
	+ **Emergency Contact Directory**: You agree to include your contact information in the emergency contact directory.
	+ **Internal Directory**: You agree to include your contact information in the internal directory only.
	+ **No Consent**: You do not agree to include your contact information in either directory.
* Note: This field is optional, but please select one of the above options to provide your consent.

### Name
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it should appear in the directory.
* Note: This field is optional, but please enter your name to help us verify your identity.

### Email
* **Email**: Enter your email address as it should appear in the directory.
* Note: This field is optional, but please enter your email address to help us contact you.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure your contact information is included in the correct directory.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at <form@gic.mx>.
