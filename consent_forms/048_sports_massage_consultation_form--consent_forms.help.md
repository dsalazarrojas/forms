# Sports Massage Consultation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from clients regarding their consent to receive sports massage services. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and truthfully, as it helps us provide the best possible care and services. Please do not fill out this form if you have already completed a consultation with us in the past year.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Select the appropriate options for each field, and fill in any required fields with accurate and truthful information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Massage Type** (`massage_type`, select_one, required)
Select the type of massage you are interested in:
* Massage: A general massage, focusing on relaxation and muscle relief.
* Deep Tissue: A more intense massage, targeting deeper tissue and scar tissue.
* Swedish: A gentle, light massage, focusing on relaxation and promoting overall well-being.
* Myofacial: A specific technique targeting the muscles and connective tissue.

### 2. **Massage Duration** (`massage_duration`, select_multiple, required)
Choose the length of time you would like your massage to be:
* 30 minutes: A short, brief massage session.
* 60 minutes: A standard massage session, providing a good balance of relaxation and relief.
* 90 minutes: A longer massage session, providing more comprehensive relief and relaxation.

### 3. **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_multiple, required)
Please select any medical conditions you are aware of that may affect your massage experience:
* High Blood Pressure: If you have been diagnosed with high blood pressure, please let us know.
* Diabetes: If you have been diagnosed with diabetes, please let us know.
* Heart Condition: If you have a pre-existing heart condition, please let us know.

### 4. **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, required)
Please note any allergies or sensitivities:
* Yes: Do you have any allergies or sensitivities?
* No: No allergies or sensitivities.

### 5. **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required)
Please indicate your consent to receive sports massage services:
* I give permission to provide sports massage services: This confirms you are aware and consent to receive sports massage services from us.
* I do not give permission to provide sports massage services: This indicates you do not wish to receive sports massage services from us.

### 6. **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required)

Please fill in any medical history or information that may be relevant to your massage experience.
