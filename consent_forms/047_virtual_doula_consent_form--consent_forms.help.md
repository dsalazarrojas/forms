# Virtual Doula Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent and gather essential information from clients about their virtual doula experience. It is designed to ensure a smooth and safe experience for both the client and the virtual doula.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form in its entirety, making sure to answer all required fields.
2. For fields marked as "required," this means that an answer is necessary to complete the form.
3. If you are unsure about any field or have questions, please reach out to your virtual doula for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information** (`client_information`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to capture the client's name and other identifying information.
* **Birth Plan** (`birth_plan`, `text`, required/true): Please provide your birth plan, including any specific needs or requirements you may have during labor and delivery.
* **Virtual Doula Details** (`virtual_doula_details`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to capture any specific details about your virtual doula experience.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, required/true): Please select one of the options provided to indicate your consent to the virtual doula experience.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to capture the client's emergency contact information.
* **Insurance** (`insurance`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide your insurance information, including your insurance card or policy number.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required/true): Please provide your medical history, including any allergies, medical conditions, or other relevant health information.
* **Birth Location** (`birth_location`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to capture the location of your birth.
* **Medical Facility** (`medical_facility`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide the name of the medical facility where you plan to give birth.
* **Insurance Payer** (`insurance_payer`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your insurance payer information.
* **Insurance Card** (`insurance_card`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide a copy of your insurance card.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required/true): Please enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required/true): Please describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required/true): Please enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, `text`, required/true): Please enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Other Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact_other`, `text`, required/true): If applicable, please describe any other emergency contact.
* **Doulas Services** (`doulas_services`, `select_multiple`, required/false): Please select the services you would like to receive from your virtual doula.
* **Doulas Experience** (`doulas_experience`, `text`, required/false): This field is used to capture your doula experience.
* **Payment** (`payment`, `text`, required/true): Please enter the payment information for your virtual doula services.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `text`, required/true): Please select the payment method you would like to use.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, `text`, required/true): Please sign below to indicate that you have read and agreed to this consent form.
* **Doulas Signature** (`doulas_signature`, `text`, required/true): Please sign below to indicate that you have read and agreed to this consent form.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required/true): Please enter the date of your virtual doula appointment.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required/true): Please enter the time of your virtual doula appointment.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required/false): Please use this field to add any additional comments or information about your virtual doula experience.
