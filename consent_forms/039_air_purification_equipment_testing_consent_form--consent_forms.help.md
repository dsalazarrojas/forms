# Air Purification Equipment Testing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to obtain the participant's information and consent for the testing and evaluation of air purification equipment in a specific location. It ensures that the participant is aware of the testing process, the purpose of the test, and the potential risks involved.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Ensure you have read and understood the form and its purpose before completing it.
*   Fill out the form accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Information

*   **Participant Name** (Participant Name, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
*   **Test Location Address** (Test Location Address, text, required): Enter the address of the location where the equipment will be tested.
*   **Equipment Model** (Equipment Model, text, required): Enter the model or name of the unit being tested.
*   **Test Start Date** (Test Start Date, date, required): Enter the date when the test is scheduled to begin.
*   **Test End Date** (Test End Date, date, required): Enter the date when the test is scheduled to end.

### Test Details

*   **Purpose of Test** (Purpose of Test, select one, required): Select the primary goal of the test:
    *   Efficacy Testing: To test the equipment's effectiveness in removing pollutants.
    *   Noise Level Monitoring: To monitor and record noise levels during the test.
    *   Durability Check: To assess the equipment's durability and lifespan.
    *   User Experience Feedback: To gather feedback from users on the equipment's performance.

### Risks and Agreements

*   **Risk Acknowledgement** (Risk Acknowledgement, select one, required): Acknowledge that you understand potential risks involved with the test, such as noise, heat, or airflow.
*   **Data Collection Agreement** (Data Collection Agreement, select one, required): Consent to data logging from the device during the test.
*   **Withdrawal Rights** (Withdrawal Rights, select one, required): Understand that you can stop the test at any time.
*   **Signature** (Signature, text, required): Sign your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
*   **Date Signed** (Date Signed, date, required): Enter today's date when you sign the form.

## Tips

*   Ensure you complete the form accurately and truthfully.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask your supervisor or relevant authorities.
