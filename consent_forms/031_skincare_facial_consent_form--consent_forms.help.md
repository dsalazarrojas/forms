# Skincare Facial Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skincare Facial Consent Form is a document used to obtain client consent and gather necessary information for upcoming visits to a skincare service provider. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Fill out all required fields (indicated by *Required* in the form)
2.  Answer each question accurately and completely
3.  Select relevant options when prompted

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Upcoming Visit** (<name: upcoming_visit>, <type: text>, *Required: false*): 
This field is used to provide details about the upcoming visit, such as date and time.
* **Expectations** (<name: expectations, <type: text>, *Required: false*): 
This field is used to outline any specific expectations you may have for the service or visit.
* **Client Consent** (<name: consent, <type: select_one, *Required: false*): 
Choose 'True' if you consent to the services, or 'False' if you do not.
* **Service Details** (<name: service_details, <type: text, *Required: false*): 
This field is used to provide additional information about the service.
* **Allergies** (<name: allergy_declaration, <type: text, *Required: false*): 
List any allergies or sensitivities you have that may be relevant to the service.
* **Medical Conditions** (<name: medical_condition_declaration, <type: text, *Required: false*): 
List any medical conditions or disabilities that may impact the service.
* **Skincare Product Ingredients** (<name: skincare_product_ingredients, <type: text, *Required: false*): 
This field is used to list any specific skincare products you are allergic to or sensitive to.
* **Payment Method** (<name: payment_method, <type: select_one, *Required: false*): 
Choose your preferred payment method.
* **Payment Amount** (<name: payment_amount, <type: number, *Required: false*): 
Enter the amount to be paid for the service.
* **Special Instructions** (<name: special_instructions, <type: text, *Required: false*): 
This field is used to provide any special instructions or requests you may have.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- Provide accurate and clear information in each field.
- Review the form carefully before submitting.
