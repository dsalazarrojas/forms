# New Hire Worker Liability Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for new hires to complete in order to acknowledge their understanding of the terms and conditions of their employment. It ensures that the employee has been informed and agrees to the liability of their new role, including self-inflicted injuries and those inflicted by others.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required information in each field. Ensure that you have read and understood the terms and conditions before selecting the relevant options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Hire Details (page 1)**: Please enter your basic details as a new hire employee. This includes your name, job title, and department.
* **New Hire Agreement (page 1)**: Select one of the two options to indicate whether you have read and agree to the terms and conditions of your employment.
* **Employer Liability (page 1)**: Enter the amount of liability that you are willing to accept as a new hire employee.
* **Employee Liability (page 1)**: Select one or more of the options to indicate whether you are responsible for self-inflicted injuries or injuries inflicted by others.
* **Waiver (page 1)**: This is a free text field for any additional comments or waivers you would like to include.
* **Date (page 1)**: Enter the date on which you are completing this form.
* **Time (page 1)**: Enter the time on which you are completing this form.
* **Email (page 1)**: Enter your work email address.
* **Phone (page 1)**: Enter your work phone number.
* **Signature (page 1)**: Select one of the two options to indicate whether you have signed the form.
* **Acknowledgement (page 1)**: Select one or more of the options to indicate whether you understand the terms and conditions.
* **Employee Name (page 2)**: Enter your name as a new hire employee.
* **Employer Name (page 2)**: Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Job Title (page 2)**: Enter your job title.
* **Job Description (page 2)**: Enter a brief description of your job.
* **Department (page 2)**: Enter the department you will be working in.
* **Location (page 2)**: Enter the location of your workplace.
* **Supervisor Name (page 2)**: Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Job Status (page 2)**: Enter your job status.
* **Date Of Birth (page 2)**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Job Start Date (page 2)**: Enter the date on which you started your job.
* **Employee Signature (page 2)**: Please sign your name as a new hire employee.
* **Employer Signature (page 2)**: Please sign your supervisor's name as the employer.
* **Date Received (page 2)**: Enter the date on which the form was received.
* **Date Sent (page 2)**: Enter the date on which the form was sent.

Please note that some fields may not be required, but please ensure that you fill out as much information as possible to ensure a complete and accurate record of your employment.
