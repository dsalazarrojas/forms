# Spay/Neuter Procedure Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spay/Neuter Procedure Consent Form is used to obtain consent from a pet owner for spay/neuter procedures on their pet. It helps veterinary clinics and animal shelters to gather necessary information from the owner and to ensure that the pet receives proper care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the pet's details in the **Pet Details** section.
2.  Provide your own contact information and details in the **Owner Information** section.
3.  Describe the spay/neuter procedure you are requesting in the **Spay/Neuter Details** section.
4.  List any known medical history of the pet in the **Medical History** section.
5.  Specify any allergies the pet may have in the **Allergies** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Pet Details
* **Pet Details** (`pet_details`, text, optional): Enter the pet's name, age, species, and any other relevant details.

### Owner Information
* **Owner Information** (`owner_info`, text, optional): Please fill in your name, address, and contact number to ensure we can reach out to you if needed.

### Spay/Neuter Details
* **Spay/Neuter Details** (`spay_neuter_details`, text, optional): Specify what spay/neuter procedure you are requesting. Please include the type of procedure (e.g. spay, neuter, or both), the reason for the procedure (e.g. health reasons, population control, etc.), and any additional information the vet needs to know.

### Medical History
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, number, optional): List any known medical conditions the pet has. If none, please enter "None".

### Allergies
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): Specify any allergies the pet has. Please be as specific as possible (e.g. medications, food, etc.).
