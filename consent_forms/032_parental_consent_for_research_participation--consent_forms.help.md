# Parental Consent For Research Participation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a legal document that requires parents or guardians to provide consent for their child's participation in research activities. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and truthfully.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Parent Name** field with the full name of the parent or guardian.
2. Provide the **Parent Address** field with the parent or guardian's home address.
3. Enter the **Child Name** field with the child's name.
4. In the **Date of Birth** field, enter the child's date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
5. Enter the **Email** field with the email address of the parent or guardian.
6. Fill in the **Phone** field with the phone number of the parent or guardian.
7. Leave the **Signature** field for the parent or guardian's signature.
8. Enter the **Researcher Name** field with the name of the researcher conducting the study.
9. Fill in the **Researcher Title** field with the researcher's job title or position.
10. Provide the **Research Institution** field with the name of the institution conducting the research.
11. Enter the **Research Project** field with a brief description of the research project.
12. Fill in the **Research Location** field with the location where the research will take place.
13. In the **Research Start Date** field, enter the date when the research is scheduled to start.
14. In the **Research End Date** field, enter the date when the research is scheduled to end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name**: The full name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent Address**: The address of the parent or guardian.
* **Child Name**: The name of the child participating in the research.
* **Date of Birth**: The date of birth of the child participating in the research, in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Email**: The email address of the parent or guardian.
* **Phone**: The phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Signature**: A physical signature from the parent or guardian, indicating their consent.
* **Researcher Name**: The name of the researcher conducting the study.
* **Researcher Title**: The job title or position of the researcher.
* **Research Institution**: The name of the institution conducting the research.
* **Research Project**: A brief description of the research project.
* **Research Location**: The location where the research will take place.
* **Research Start Date**: The date when the research is scheduled to start.
* **Research End Date**: The date when the research is scheduled to end.
