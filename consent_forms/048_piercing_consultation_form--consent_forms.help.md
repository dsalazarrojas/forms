# piercing_consultation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The piercing_consultation_form is a tool used to collect information from clients about their body parts and any medical history they want to disclose before undergoing piercing or body modification procedures. It helps us understand their needs and provide the best possible experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and thoroughly.
3. Make sure to choose your relevant options for each field, and select only what applies to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Client Information** (client_info, text, optional)
Enter any personal details you want to share about yourself.

### 2. **Body Parts** (body_parts, select_multiple, optional)
Select all the body parts you want to get pierced or modified. Please choose from the following options:
- Ear
- Navel
- Nose
- Tongue
- Eyebrow
- Eyelid
- Back
- Surface
- Lip

### 3. **Consultation Details** (consultation_details, text, optional)
Please provide any additional information about your desired piercing or modification.

### 4. **Allergies** (allergies, select_multiple, optional)
Select any allergies you have, if any. Please choose from the following options:
- Penicillin
- Latex
- Metals
- Other

### 5. **Medical History** (medical_history, text, optional)
Please share any relevant medical history you want us to know about.

### 6. **Insurance** (insurance, select_one, optional)
Do you have insurance that covers piercing or body modification procedures?
- True
- False

### 7. **Contact Preferences** (contact_preferences, select_multiple, optional)
Select how you prefer to be contacted:
- Email
- Phone
- Text

### 8. **Consent** (consent, select_one, optional)
Have you read and understood the form instructions and the terms of this consultation?
- True
- False
