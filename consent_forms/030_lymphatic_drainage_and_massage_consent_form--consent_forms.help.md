# Lymphatic Drainage And Massage Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lymphatic Drainage And Massage Consent Form is a document that clients must complete to provide their consent for lymphatic drainage and massage procedures. This form ensures that clients are aware of the process, risks, and benefits of the treatments and are able to provide informed consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please read the form carefully and answer all the questions honestly.
2. Fill in the required information on each page, which is marked with an asterisk (*).
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Client Information (Client Info)
• **Client Information** (client_info, text, optional): This field is for you to provide your name, address, contact information, or any other details that you feel are relevant to your consent for lymphatic drainage and massage procedures.

### Lymphatic Drainage Information (Lymphatic Drainage)
• **Lymphatic Drainage** (lymphatic_drainage, text, optional): Please provide any relevant information about your lymphatic system, such as previous treatments or medical conditions that may affect your lymphatic system.

### Massage Information (Massage)
• **Massage** (massage, text, optional): Please describe the type of massage you are consenting to, such as frequency, duration, or any specific areas of the body to be massaged.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer all questions honestly and accurately.
- If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please inform your healthcare provider before proceeding with the procedures.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
