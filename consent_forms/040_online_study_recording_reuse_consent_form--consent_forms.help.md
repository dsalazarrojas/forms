# Online Study Recording Reuse Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect consent from participants to reuse their online study recordings. It is designed to be completed by participants after a recording has been made, and before their recording is used or shared with others.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as the participant who recorded the study.
2. Enter your email address if you want to be contacted about the consent.
3. Choose whether you give consent for the recording to be reused.
4. Provide a brief explanation of how you want the recording to be reused (optional).
5. Enter the date and time you are giving this consent.
6. Describe the details of the study recording you are consenting for (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as the participant who recorded the study.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address if you want to be contacted about the consent. This is an optional field, but it will allow the researchers to contact you with more information.
* **Do you give consent for the recording to be reused**: Choose 'True' if you agree to let the recording be reused, or 'False' if you do not want your recording to be reused. Please choose 'True' to allow your recording to be shared and used by the researchers.
* **Explain how the recording will be reused**: If you choose to give consent, provide a brief description on how you want your recording to be reused. This will help the researchers understand your wishes.
* **Date of Consent**: Enter the date you are giving this consent. This will help to keep track of when the consent was given.
* **Time of Consent**: Enter the time you are giving this consent. This will help to keep track of when the consent was given.
* **Study Recording Details**: If you want to provide more information about the recording you are consenting for, you can do so here. This is an optional field, but it will help the researchers to understand the context of the recording.
