# Parental Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Parental Authorization Form is a document used to obtain parental or legal guardian's consent for a minor to participate in various activities or programs offered by an organization. This form is typically used by schools, youth organizations, or other institutions to ensure that parents or guardians are aware of and agree to their child's participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate options for the guardian's response to questions about the minor's authorization.
2. Enter the guardian's name, email, and phone number.
3. Choose the type of relationship between the guardian and the minor.
4. Provide the minor's name, date of birth, and school information (if applicable).
5. Answer questions about the minor's activities and organization affiliation (if applicable).
6. Add any comments or notes about the minor's participation.
7. Sign and date the form to confirm the guardian's authorization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parental Authorization** (Yes/No): 
  This field asks the guardian to indicate whether they authorize the minor to participate in the activity or program.
* **Guardian Name**: 
  Enter the full name of the guardian or parent.
* **Guardian Email**: 
  Enter the email address of the guardian or parent.
* **Guardian Phone**: 
  Enter the phone number of the guardian or parent.
* **Guardian Relation**: 
  Choose the type of relationship between the guardian and the minor (Mother, Father, Guardian, Other).
* **Minor Name**: 
  Enter the full name of the minor.
* **Minor Birthdate**: 
  Enter the minor's date of birth.
* **Minor School**: 
  Choose the type of school the minor attends (Home Schooled, Public School, Private School, Other).
* **Minor Activity** (Yes/No): 
  This field asks the guardian to indicate whether the minor is authorized to participate in the activity or program.
* **Organization Name**: 
  Enter the name of the organization the minor is affiliated with.

  Enter the email address of the organization the minor is affiliated with.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure the form is complete.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask the organization for clarification.
* Make sure to keep a copy of the signed form for your records.
* This form is typically used for minors under 18 years old.
* If you are a guardian or parent, please note that you are responsible for the minor's participation.
