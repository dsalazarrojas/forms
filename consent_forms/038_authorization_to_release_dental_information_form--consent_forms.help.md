# Authorization To Release Dental Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request the release of dental information from your previous dentist or dental office. You will be asked to provide your personal information, the type of records you want to release, and the recipient's details. Completing this form will help ensure that your dental records are transferred smoothly and securely to your new provider or recipient.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details:
	* Form Date: Enter the date you are submitting this authorization.
	* Patient Full Name: Enter your full legal name.
	* Date of Birth: Enter your birth date.
	* Patient ID Number: If you have a dental record number, please enter it (optional).
	* Email Address: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
	* Phone Number: Enter your contact phone number.
2. Select your current dentist or practice:
	* Current Dental Provider Name: Enter the name of your current dentist or practice.
3. Enter your new recipient's details:
	* Practice Name: Enter the name of the recipient's practice or organization.
	* Practice Address: Enter the address where the records should be sent.
	* Practice Phone: Enter the contact phone number for the recipient.
	* Recipient Email: Enter the email address for electronic records transmission (optional).
4. Choose the type of records to release:
	* Select the types of records you want to transfer (e.g., Complete Dental Records, X-rays and Radiographs, etc.).
	* Records Date Range - From: Enter the earliest date of the records you want to release.
	* Records Date Range - To: Enter the latest date of the records you want to release (optional).
5. Specify the recipient's details:
	* Recipient Name: Enter the name of the recipient.
	* Recipient Type: Choose the type of recipient (e.g., Another Dentist, Dental Specialist, etc.).
6. Choose the release method:
	* Preferred Release Method: Select how you want the records to be sent (e.g., Fax, Email, Mail, etc.).
7. Specify the reason for release:
	* Reason for Release: Choose why you are releasing your dental records (e.g., Continuing Care with New Dentist, Specialist Consultation, etc.).
8. Confirm your authorization:
	* Authorization to Release Records: Choose whether you authorize the release of all or specific records.
	* One-Time or Standing Authorization: Choose whether this is a one-time release or a standing authorization with restrictions.
	* Any Restrictions on Records: Enter any specific restrictions on the records you want to release (optional).
