# Digital Lending Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Lending Consent Form is a document that requires borrowers or applicants to provide consent for digital lending services. This form is essential for lenders to understand the applicant's agreement, terms, and conditions of the loan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your personal details in the `applicant_details` field.
2.  Provide information about the loan you're applying for in the `loan_details` field.
3.  Enter your contact information in the `contact_information` field.
4.  If required, sign the `signature` field to verify your identity and agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **applicant_details**: Enter your name, email, phone number, and other personal details as requested. This is the first field in the form where you will provide basic information about yourself.
*   **loan_details**: Enter the details of the loan you are applying for, including the loan amount, interest rate, and repayment term. This is crucial information for the lender to process your loan request.
*   **contact_information**: Enter your current address, phone number, and any other relevant contact information. This ensures that the lender can reach out to you for any questions or further information about your loan.
*   **signature**: If required, sign this field to verify your identity and confirm your agreement to the loan terms. This is your opportunity to confirm that you are aware of and accept the loan terms and conditions.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that all fields are filled accurately and completely.
*   If any field is required, it is essential to fill it in correctly to avoid delays in the loan process.
*   Review the form carefully before submission to ensure that all information is correct.
*   Keep a record of your form submission for future reference.
