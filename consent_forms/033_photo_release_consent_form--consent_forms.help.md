# Photo Release Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from the photo owner for the use of their photograph or other visual content by an acquiring party. The form provides a clear understanding of the purpose and terms under which the photo will be used.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Review the form carefully to ensure that you understand the terms and conditions.
3. Sign and date the form to indicate your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Photo Owner (1)**: Enter the name of the person who owns the photo.
* **Acquiring Party (2)**: Enter the name of the party acquiring the photo (e.g. organization, company, or individual).
* **Purpose of Use (3)**: Select the intended purpose of use for the photo (e.g. None, Any other purpose which the acquiring party may desire). Please note that this field is optional.
* **Signature Date (4)**: Enter the date on which you sign this form.
* **Signature Time (5)**: Enter the time at which you sign this form.
* **Signature (6)**: Enter your signature to indicate your consent.
* **Photo Number (7)**: Enter the number of the photo (if applicable).
* **Photos Taken At (8)**: Indicate when the photos were taken (e.g. at the Photo Release Consent event, or at some other time).

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before signing to ensure that your consent is informed and voluntary.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to the acquiring party.
* Keep a copy of this form for your records.
