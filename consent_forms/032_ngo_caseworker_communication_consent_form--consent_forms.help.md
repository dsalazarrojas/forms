# NGO Caseworker Communication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for caseworkers to collect communication preferences and contact details from clients or individuals they work with. It helps to establish a clear and respectful communication channel and ensures that all parties are on the same page.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and the fields it contains.
2. Answer the questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Fill in the required fields (communication_preferences and contact_details).
4. Review and submit the form when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **communication_preferences** (text, required): 
  This field allows you to specify how you would like to be contacted (e.g., phone, email, SMS).
* **contact_details** (text, required): 
  Enter your full contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address, to ensure that the client or individual can get in touch with you.
* **consent_form_page** (text, optional): 
  This field is not required, but it is used to provide more information about your communication preferences if needed.
  Note: You can leave this field blank if you do not have any additional comments or information to add.

## Tips
- Make sure to review and understand the information gathered before proceeding with your work.
- Be clear and concise in your answers to ensure that the client or individual understands your communication preferences and contact details.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
