# Medical Research Data Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Research Data Declaration Form is used to collect and document information about medical research projects. This includes the project's name, location, team members, start and end dates, participant informed consent, and declaration. Completing this form helps ensure that all required information is accurately captured and avoids duplication of work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the project's name, location, team members, start and end dates, participant informed consent, and declaration.
* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Research Project** (`medical_research_project`): Enter the name of the medical research project.
* **Project Lead** (`project_lead`): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you are the project lead.
* **Medical Research Location** (`medical_research_location`): Enter the location where the medical research project will take place.
* **Research Team** (`research_team`): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you are part of the research team.
* **Medical Research Start Date** (`medical_research_start_date`): Select the start date of the medical research project.
* **Medical Research End Date** (`medical_research_end_date`): Select the end date of the medical research project.
* **Participant Informed Consent** (`participant_informed_consent`): Choose 'Option 1' or 'Option 2' to indicate your informed consent status.
* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`): Enter any relevant patient information.
* **Patient Contact Information** (`patient_contact_information`): Enter any relevant contact information for the patient.
* **Medical Research Declaration** (`medical_research_declaration`): Enter a brief declaration of the research project.
* **Medical Research Declaration Notes** (`medical_research_declaration_notes`): Enter any additional notes about the research project declaration.
* **Tips**:
* Make sure to answer all required fields accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant person or team.
* If you need to update the form, please use the most up-to-date information.
