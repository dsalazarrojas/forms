# Trust Amendment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Trust Amendment Form is used to create or modify trust arrangements by amending the terms or conditions of an existing trust.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the current trust document to understand the existing terms and conditions.
2. Identify the changes you wish to make to the trust arrangement.
3. Provide the necessary information for the amendments, such as grantor wishes, trust terms, and any other relevant details.
4. Sign the form, either as the grantor or as a notary public and trustee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Grantor Wishes
* **Grantor Wishes** (`grantor_wishes`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional wishes or instructions that the grantor would like to be included in the trust amendment.

### 2. Trust Terms
* **Trust Terms** (`trust_terms`, text, required/optional): Specify any changes to the original trust terms or conditions.

### 3. Amendments
* **Amendments** (`amendments`, text, required/optional): Describe the specific changes being made to the trust, including any deletions, additions, or modifications to the trust terms or conditions.

### 4. Changes in Circumstances
* **Changes in Circumstances** (`changes_in_circumstances`, text, required/optional): Explain any changes in the circumstances that have led to the amendment, such as changes in the law or new information that was not previously considered.

### 5. Legal Requirements
* **Legal Requirements** (`legal_requirements`, text, required/optional): Identify any new or modified legal requirements that apply to the amended trust.

### 6. Trust Management
* **Trust Management** (`trust_management`, text, required/optional): Outline any changes to the trust's management or administration.

### 7. Grantor Information
* **Grantor Information** (`grantor_info`, text, required/optional): Provide any information about the grantor, such as their contact information or instructions for communication.

### 8. Trustee Information
* **Trustee Information** (`trusteed_info`, text, required/optional): Provide any information about the trustee, such as their contact information or instructions for communication.

### 9. Notary Public
* **Notary Public** (`notary_public`, select_one, required): Select the notary public who will witness and sign the amendment.

### 10. Date of Signing
* **Date of Signing** (`date_of_signing`, date, required): Enter the date the amendment was signed.

### 11. Notary Public Fee
* **Notary Public Fee** (`notary_public_fee`, number, required/optional): Specify the fee paid to the notary public for their services.

### 12. Grantor Signature
* **Grantor Signature** (`grantor_signature`, note, required): A note to sign and date the grantor's acceptance of the amendment.

### 13. Notary Public Signature
* **Notary Public Signature** (`notary_public_signature`, note, required/optional): A note to sign and date the notary public's verification of the amendment.

### 14. Trustee Signature
* **Trustee Signature** (`trusteed_signature`, note, required): A note to sign and date the trustee's acceptance of the amendment.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and properly.
