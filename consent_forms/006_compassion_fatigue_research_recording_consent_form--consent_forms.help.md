# Compassion Fatigue Research Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect informed consent from participants in a compassion fatigue research study. It is a required step to ensure that researchers have the necessary permissions to collect and store audio and video recordings of participants.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and requirements.
2. Complete the form accurately and honestly.
3. Enter your participant name in the "participant name" field, or select the "anonymous" option if you prefer not to be identified.
4. Enter the internal reference number for the study in the "study id" field.
5. Indicate your consent to audio and video recordings by selecting the "True" option for both "consent to audio recording" and "consent to video recording" fields.
6. Provide a brief description of how the recordings will be used in the "permitted use of recordings" field.
7. Enter the duration of time that the recordings will be stored in the "data retention period" field.
8. Enter the email address of the researcher contact person in the "researcher contact email" field.
9. Sign your name in the "participant printed name" field, which serves as your consent.
10. Select the date of consent in the "date of consent" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Study Information --**: This field is a note field and does not require input.
* **participant name**: Enter your full name or select "anonymous" if you prefer not to be identified.
* **study id**: Enter the internal reference number for the study.
* **consent to audio recording**: Indicate your consent to audio recording by selecting "True".
* **consent to video recording**: Indicate your consent to video recording by selecting "True".
* **permitted use of recordings**: Provide a brief description of how the recordings will be used.
* **data retention period**: Enter the duration of time that the recordings will be stored.
* **researcher contact email**: Enter the email address of the researcher contact person.
* **participant printed name**: Sign your name, which serves as your consent.
* **date of consent**: Select the date of consent.

## Tips
* Ensure you have reviewed and understood the purpose and requirements of this form before completing it.
* Complete the form accurately and honestly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the researcher contact person listed in the "researcher contact email" field.
