# Faith Based Testimony Publication Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent for the publication of faith-based testimonies. It ensures that individuals providing testimonials have given their consent and gather information about their publication preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name
2. Provide your Email (if you want to be contacted about publication details)
3. Enter your Phone Number (if you want to be contacted about publication details)
4. Enter your Date of Birth (if applicable)
5. Select the Time of Service for your testimony
6. Choose whether or not to consent to publication of your testimony
7. Select the consent for publication of your testimony
8. Choose how you want your testimony to be published (Online or Offline)
9. Choose how frequently you want your testimony to be published (Once, Regularly, or Occasionally)
10. Add any additional notes or comments
11. If applicable, select the tool used for the testimony and enter its ID

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Please fill in your First Name
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Please provide your Email address if you want to be contacted about publication details
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Please enter your Phone number if you want to be contacted about publication details
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): If applicable, please enter your Date of Birth
* **Time of Service** (`time_of_service`, time, required: false): Please select the Time of Service for your testimony
* **Testimony Consent** (`testimony_consent`, select_one, required: false): This is a consent form to determine whether you give permission for your testimony to be published
* **Publication Consent** (`testimony_publication_consent`, select_one, required: false): This is a form to select whether or not to consent to the publication of your testimony
* **Publication** (`testimony_publication`, select_multiple, required: false): If you select to consent to publication, please select where you want your testimony to be published (Online or Offline)
* **Publication Frequency** (`testimony_publication_frequency`, select_multiple, required: false): If you select to consent to publication, please select how frequently you want your testimony to be published (Once, Regularly, or Occasionally)
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Please add any additional notes or comments
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required: false): If applicable, please select the tool used for your testimony
* **Assigned Tool ID** (`assigned_tool_id`, text, required: false): If applicable, please enter the ID of the tool used for your testimony
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, required: false): If applicable, please enter the output file

## Tips

* Please fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your testimony. We will handle it with care and respect.
