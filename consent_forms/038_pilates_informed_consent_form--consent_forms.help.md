# Pilates Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather participant information for Pilates sessions and ensure they understand and agree to the terms of their participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
2. Enter your email address where you can be contacted if needed.
3. Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
4. Enter the name of the activity (Pilates session) you are participating in.
5. If you are under 18, enter the name of your parent or guardian who is signing on your behalf.
6. Print and sign your name to confirm you understand and agree to the terms of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
- **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted if needed.
- **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
- **Activity Name** (`activity_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the Pilates session you are participating in.
- **Parent/Guardian Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, text, optional): If you are under 18, enter the name of your parent or guardian who is signing on your behalf.
- **Signature** (`signature`, note, required): Print and sign your name to confirm you understand and agree to the terms of the form.
