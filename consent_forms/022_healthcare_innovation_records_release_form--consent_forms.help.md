## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain release of information from a patient's healthcare innovation records, which includes a record of their treatment and care history. This form is required to be completed by the patient or their authorized representative to release this information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand what information is being requested.
2. Complete each field according to your understanding of the information required.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Identifier** (`patient_identifier`, text, optional): This field is used to identify the patient. Please enter your name and any other identifying information that you have for the patient.
* **Consent Type** (`consent_type`, select_one, optional): This field is used to indicate if you are granting consent for the release of the patient's information. Select 'Yes' to indicate that you are granting consent.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): This field is used to enter the patient's date of birth. Please enter the date in the MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g. 12/02/1999).
* **Date of Release** (`date_of_release`, date, optional): This field is used to select the date on which you are granting release of the patient's information. Please enter the date in the MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g. 12/02/2019).
* **Authorized Person** (`authorized_person`, select_multiple, optional): This field is used to indicate if you are an authorized person to release the patient's information. Select 'Yes' if you are authorized.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): This field is used to enter your contact information, such as your name and phone number.
* **Release Type** (`release_type`, select_one, optional): This field is used to select the type of release being requested. Select 'Yes' to indicate that you are requesting a specific type of release.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, optional): This field is used to sign the form. Please enter your signature in the space provided.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the patient's name. Please enter the patient's name as it appears on their record.

## Tips

* Please review the form carefully before completing it.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
* If you are unsure about any of the information requested, please ask for clarification before completing the form.
