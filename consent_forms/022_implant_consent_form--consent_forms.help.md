# Implant Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Implant Consent Form is a document designed to obtain consent from patients before undergoing an implant procedure. It serves as a legal agreement between the patient and the medical provider, outlining the terms and conditions of the implant, including the risks and benefits associated with the procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's name and contact information.
2. **Implant Information**: Provide details about the implant, including the type, size, and location.
3. **Signature Date**: Enter the date of the signature.
4. **Signature**: Sign and date the form to indicate consent.
5. Repeat steps 2-4 for up to 20 different signatures, as required for multiple signers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, required): This section is used to record the patient's name, contact information, and other relevant details.
* **Implant Information** (`implant_info`, text, required): This section is used to provide details about the implant, including its type, size, and location.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date the form is signed.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Sign and date the form to indicate consent.
* **Signature 2-20** (`signature_2`-`signature_20`, text, required): Repeat steps 2-3 for multiple signers as required.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form carefully and accurately.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed.
* Date the form when signing.
* Store the completed form securely and safely.
* Review and revise the form as needed to accommodate specific needs.
* Consider having a witness sign and date the form as an extra layer of verification.
