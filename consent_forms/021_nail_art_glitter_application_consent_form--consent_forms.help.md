# Nail Art Glitter Application Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from clients interested in nail art services offered by the salon. It aims to understand their details, preferences, and consent regarding the services they wish to receive.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your details, including your contact information.
2. Indicate any allergy information or concerns.
3. Select the services you are interested in receiving.
4. Sign your name to acknowledge you have read and understood the terms and conditions.
5. Provide your preferred appointment date and time.
6. Enter the technician's name or provide any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details (id: 1)** (`Client Details`, text, required: false): Please enter your name and any other identifying details you would like to share with us.
* **Allergy Information (id: 2)** (`Allergy Information`, text, required: false): Inform us of any allergies or concerns you have. This will help us ensure your safety and comfort during the appointment.
* **Services Selected (id: 3)** (`Services Selected`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the nail art services you wish to receive, such as French Tips, Glitter Finish, Gel Polish, or Nail Art.
* **Signature (id: 4)** (`Signature`, note, required: false): Sign your name to confirm you have read and understood the terms and conditions.
* **Email (id: 5)** (`Email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for us to contact you with appointment details and updates.
* **Phone (id: 6)** (`Phone`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number for us to contact you if needed.
* **Consent (id: 7)** (`Consent`, select_one, required: false): Please select "I have read and understand the terms and conditions" to acknowledge your consent.
* **Terms and Conditions (id: 8)** (`Terms and Conditions`, text, required: false): Briefly read and acknowledge the terms and conditions.
* **Date (id: 9)** (`Date`, date, required: false): Enter the preferred date for your appointment.
* **Time (id: 10)** (`Time`, time, required: false): Enter the preferred time for your appointment.
* **Technician Name (id: 11)** (`Technician Name`, text, required: false): Please enter the name of the technician you would like to work with, if applicable.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Use the same email and phone number throughout the form for consistency.
* If you have any special requests or concerns, please include them in the comments section.
* Review the terms and conditions before signing to ensure you understand the agreement.
* We will contact you within a timely manner with appointment details and updates.
