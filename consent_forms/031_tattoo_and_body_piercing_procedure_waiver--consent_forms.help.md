# Tattoo And Body Piercing Procedure Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from clients before proceeding with a tattoo or body piercing procedure, ensuring a safe and informed consent process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your client information, including name, address, and contact details.
2. Select the type of procedure you are undergoing (Tattoo or Piercing).
3. List any allergies or medical conditions that may affect the procedure.
4. Provide your medical history, including any relevant medical conditions, treatments, or medications.
5. Sign the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information** (ID: 1): This section is where you provide your personal details, including your name, address, and contact information.
* **Client Name** (ID: 2): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Tattoo Artist Information** (ID: 3): This is where you provide details about the tattoo artist you are working with.
* **Procedure** (ID: 4): Select the type of procedure you are undergoing, either a tattoo or a piercing.
* **Allergy or Medical Conditions** (ID: 5): If you have any allergies or medical conditions that may affect the procedure, please list them here.
* **Contact Information** (ID: 6): Provide the contact information for the person responsible for your care, including their name, email, and phone number.
* **Contact Name** (ID: 7): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Relationship** (ID: 8): Specify your relationship with the contact person (e.g., friend, family member, or healthcare provider).
* **Contact Email** (ID: 9): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (ID: 10): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Medical History** (ID: 11): Describe your medical history, including any relevant conditions, treatments, or medications.
* **Medical Condition** (ID: 12): If you have a specific medical condition, describe it here.
* **Medical Treatment** (ID: 13): If you are currently undergoing medical treatment, describe it here.
* **Medical Medications** (ID: 14): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies** (ID: 15): List any allergies you have.
* **Medical Notes** (ID: 16): Provide any additional medical notes or concerns you may have.
* **Client Signature** (ID: 17): Sign this form to confirm your consent.
* **Tattooer Signature** (ID: 18): The tattoo artist will sign this form to confirm their understanding of your situation.
* **Date Signed** (ID: 19): Enter the date you signed this form.
* **Time Signed** (ID: 20): Enter the time you signed this form.
* **Client Date Of Birth** (ID: 21): Enter your date of birth.
* **Gender** (ID: 22): Select your gender.
* **Age** (ID: 23): Enter your age.
* **Address** (ID: 24): Enter your address.
* **Occupation** (ID: 25): Enter your occupation.
* **Income** (ID: 26): Enter your income. 

Please note that some fields are not required, but it's essential to fill them out for a comprehensive understanding of your situation.
