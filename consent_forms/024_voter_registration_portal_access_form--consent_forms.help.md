# Voter Registration Portal Access Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Voter Registration Portal Access Form is designed to collect relevant information from voters to grant them access to the voter registration portal. This form is used to categorize and filter voters based on their registration status and assigned tools.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and tools to proceed.
2. Select the relevant categories for your voter registration portal access.
3. Provide a brief description of your registration status.
4. Select the assigned tool(s) for your voter registration portal access.
5. Attach any necessary files or documents.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select One: voter_registration_category** (`select_one`, required): Choose one of the available categories: 40064, 40065, 40066. This field helps to filter voters based on their registration status.
* **Select Multiple: voter_registration_portal_access_form_category** (`select_multiple`, required): Select one or more tools assigned to your voter registration portal access: 40065, 40068, 40067. This field is used to categorize voters with multiple assigned tools.
* **Note: voter_registration_portal_access_form_description** (`note`, required): Provide a brief description of your registration status. This field is used to provide additional context for your registration status.
* **Select One: voter_registration_portal_access_form_category_2** (`select_one`, required): Choose one of the available categories: 40064, 40066. This field is used to select an additional category for your voter registration portal access.
* **Text: voter_registration_portal_access_form_description_2** (`text`, required): Provide a short text description of your voter registration portal access. This field is used to provide a brief summary.
* **File: voter_registration_portal_access_form_output_file** (`text`, required): Attach any necessary files or documents related to your voter registration portal access. This field is used to upload additional supporting documents.
* **Select One: assigned_tool** (`select_one`, required): Choose the assigned tool for your voter registration portal access: chatjimmy, Other. This field is used to select the tool assigned to your voter registration portal access.

## Tips
* Ensure you have selected the correct categories and tool for your voter registration portal access.
* Double-check your selections before submitting the form.
* If you need assistance or have questions, contact your support team.
