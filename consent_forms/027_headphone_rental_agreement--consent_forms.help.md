# headphone_renal_agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect details for a headphone rental agreement. This form is used to gather essential information about the rental, including equipment type, rental duration, start and end dates and times, equipment location, responsible party, and a signature. This form ensures that all necessary details are collected for a smooth and organized rental process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the rental details in the **Rental Details** field.
2. Select the type of equipment being rented in the **Equipment Type** field.
3. Enter the rental duration in the **Rental Duration** field.
4. Enter the start date and time in the **Start Date** and **Start Time** fields, respectively.
5. Enter the end date and time in the **End Date** and **End Time** fields, respectively.
6. Specify the equipment location in the **Equipment Location** field.
7. Identify the responsible party for the rental in the **Responsible Party** field.
8. Sign the form in the **Signature** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Rental Details** (`Rental Details`, `text`, required): This field is where you can provide any additional details about the rental.
- **Equipment Type** (`Equipment Type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of equipment being rented, such as Headphone or Microphone.
- **Rental Duration** (`Rental Duration`, `number`, required): Enter the duration of the rental in a numerical value.
- **Start Date** (`Start Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the rental begins.
- **Start Time** (`Start Time`, `time`, required): Enter the start time of the rental.
- **End Date** (`End Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the rental ends.
- **End Time** (`End Time`, `time`, required): Enter the end time of the rental.
- **Equipment Location** (`Equipment Location`, `text`, required): Specify the location where the equipment will be used or stored.
- **Responsible Party** (`Responsible Party`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the party responsible for the rental, such as School, Studio, or Event Team.
- **Signature** (`Signature`, `text`, required): Sign the form to confirm the rental agreement.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure the form is complete and accurate.
- Double-check the equipment type and location to ensure they match the rental agreement.
- Review the rental duration, start and end dates, and times to ensure they are correct.
- Sign the form with your name and date to confirm your agreement.
