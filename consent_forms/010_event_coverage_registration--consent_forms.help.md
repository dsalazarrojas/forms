# Event Coverage Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Coverage Registration form is designed for event organizers and attendees to declare their interest in covering events. It's a crucial tool for event teams to track who's covering events, so they can plan accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Carefully review each field to ensure you understand what it's for.
* Fill out the form in its entirety, or save your progress and come back later.
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Team** (`event_team`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select whether you or your team will be covering the event. Options are "Yes" and "No".
* **Contact** (`contact`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select whether you will be providing contact information, and if so, whether it's "Yes" or "No". This field helps event teams know whether to expect your contact information.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): If you selected "Yes" in the **Contact** field, enter your email address here. This will allow event teams to contact you for coverage arrangements.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): If you selected "Yes" in the **Contact** field, enter your phone number here. This will allow event teams to contact you for coverage arrangements.

## Tips
* Please double-check your selections in the **Event Team** and **Contact** fields to ensure accuracy.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask your event team for clarification.
* If you're not planning to cover an event, you can leave the form blank or mark "No" in the **Event Team** field.
