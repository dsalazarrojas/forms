# Client Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Agreement Form is a document used to outline the terms and conditions of a development project between a client and an agency (developer). It serves as a mutual understanding of the project's scope, timeline, cost, and intellectual property ownership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your **Client Full Name/Company** and the **Developer/Agency Name** to ensure both parties are properly identified.
2. Select the **Type of Project** that best describes the project's scope and nature.
3. Provide a brief **Scope of Work** and **Brief Summary of Deliverables** to outline the project's objectives and expected outcomes.
4. Set the **Target Completion Date** for the project, which should be the estimated date of project completion.
5. Enter the **Agreed Total Project Cost** in dollars, which represents the total cost of the project.
6. Determine the **Upfront Deposit Amount** as a percentage of the project cost (typically 25-50%).
7. Acknowledge the **Terms and Conditions** by reading them carefully.
8. Choose your agreement on **Intellectual Property Transfer**, **Revisions Policy**, and **Ongoing Maintenance**.
9. Sign this form electronically by typing your **Electronic Signature** in the provided field.
10. Date and time the **Date of Agreement**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Full Name/Company (Required)**: A field to record the client's name or company.
* **Developer/Agency Name (Required)**: A field to record the name of the agency or developer handling the project.
* **Type of Project (Required)**: A field to select the nature of the project, such as a website or full-stack web app.
* **Scope of Work**: An introductory note to remind the client to provide a description of the project scope.
* **Brief Summary of Deliverables (Required)**: A field for the client to provide a brief description of the project deliverables.
* **Target Completion Date (Required)**: A field to set the estimated completion date for the project.
* **Agreed Total Project Cost ($)(Required)**: A field to enter the total cost of the project in dollars.
* **Upfront Deposit Amount (%) (Required)**: A field to determine the deposit amount as a percentage of the project cost.
* **Terms and Conditions**: A note to remind the client to read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **I agree that intellectual property transfer occurs upon full payment**: A field to acknowledge the terms of intellectual property ownership.
* **I acknowledge the policy of 2 rounds of minor revisions**: A field to acknowledge the revisions policy.
* **I understand that ongoing maintenance is a separate service**: A field to acknowledge the maintenance policy.
* **I agree to the mutual non-disclosure agreement (NDA)**: A field to acknowledge the agreement to maintain confidentiality.
* **Electronic Signature (Type Full Name)**: A field for the client to sign electronically.
* **Date of Agreement**: A field to date and time the agreement.
* **Thank you for your business**: A concluding note, reminding the client that a copy of this agreement will be sent to their email.
 
Please note that all fields marked as "Required" must be filled out by the client.
