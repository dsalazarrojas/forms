# Massage Consultation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Massage Consultation Form is designed to collect information from potential massage clients. This form will help us understand your needs and preferences for a tailored massage experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
3. Provide your Phone number in the Phone field.
4. Select your preferred contact method from the Best Contact Method dropdown.
5. Choose how you heard about us from the How Did You Hear field.
6. Indicate your interest in massage treatment in the Interested in Massage Treatment field.
7. If you have any medical conditions, please provide them in the Medical Conditions field.
8. Share your previous massage experience in the Previous Massage Experience field.
9. If you have any previous injuries, please provide them in the Previous Injury field.
10. Describe your current pain in the Current Pain field.
11. Specify where you feel pain in the Pain Locations field.
12. Indicate your pain levels using the Pain Levels field.
13. Describe your pain in the Pain Description field.
14. If applicable, provide your medical profession in the Medical Profession field.
15. Specify your insurance coverage in the Insurance Coverage field.
16. If applicable, provide the name of your medical provider in the Medical Provider field.
17. If applicable, provide your medical record in the Medical Record field.
18. If applicable, provide your medical history in the Medical History field.
19. If you have any allergies, please provide them in the Allergies field.
20. If applicable, list your medications in the Medications field.
21. If applicable, describe any previous treatment in the Previous Treatment field.
