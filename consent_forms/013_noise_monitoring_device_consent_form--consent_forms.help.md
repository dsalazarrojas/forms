# Noise Monitoring Device Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Noise Monitoring Device Consent Form is a document that allows users to provide their consent for the use of a noise monitoring device in their environment. This form is a necessary step to ensure that the device is only used with the explicit permission of the individuals affected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please review the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and implications of the noise monitoring device.
2. Enter your name and email address (or phone number) for contact purposes.
3. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the 'Noise Monitoring Device Consent' field to indicate your agreement or disagreement.
4. If you select 'Yes', choose the 'Contact Preference' option for how you would like to be contacted (True or False).
5. If you have any additional comments or concerns, you can include them in the 'Additional Comments' field.
6. Please review your selections carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_input`, `text`, required): Enter your name, as this will be used for contact purposes.
* **Noise Monitoring Device Consent** (`noise_monitoring_device_consent`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' to indicate that you agree to the monitoring of noise levels in your environment, and 'No' to decline.
* **Noise Monitoring Device Consent Agreed** (`noise_monitoring_device_consent_agreed`, `select_multiple`, required): Since this field is a select_multiple type, you should only select 'Yes' if you are certain you agree to the monitoring. If you are unsure, please select 'No' instead.
* **Contact Preference** (`contact_preference`, `select_one`, optional): Select 'True' if you would like to be contacted regarding the noise monitoring device, and 'False' if you do not want to be contacted.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person who should be contacted in case of any issues or concerns regarding the noise monitoring device.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the person who should be contacted in case of any issues or concerns regarding the noise monitoring device.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the person who should be contacted in case of any issues or concerns regarding the noise monitoring device.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or concerns you have regarding the noise monitoring device.

## Tips
* Please review each field carefully before submitting the form to ensure that your selections are accurate.
* If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please contact the contact person listed on the form.
* This form is only processed if you select 'Yes' for the 'Noise Monitoring Device Consent' field.
