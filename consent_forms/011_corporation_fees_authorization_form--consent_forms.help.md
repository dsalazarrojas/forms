# Corporation Fees Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The corporation fees authorization form is used to obtain authorization for corporation fees from the user.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields: vendor_name, and authorization_reason.
* Select the vendor type that best describes the vendor.
* Enter the fee amount and authorization amount, if applicable.
* Choose whether you will be doing a credit build out or not.
* Select the authorization date and time.
* Enter the reason for authorization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **vendor_type (Select One)**: Choose the type of vendor you are dealing with. This field has the following options:
	+ Virtual Office
	+ Small new vendor

* **fee_amount (Number)**: If you are authorizing fees, this is where you enter the amount.
* **credit_build_out (Select One)**: Choose whether you will be doing a credit build out or not.
	+ Yes
	+ No

* **authorization_amount (Number)**: If you are authorizing fees, this is where you enter the amount.
* **authorization_date (Date)**: Enter the date of the authorization.
* **authorization_time (Time)**: Enter the time of the authorization.
* **authorization_reason (Text)**: Enter a brief reason for the authorization.
* **vendor_name (Text)**: Enter the name of the vendor.

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill in all required fields.
* Enter the correct information for your vendor and authorization.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
