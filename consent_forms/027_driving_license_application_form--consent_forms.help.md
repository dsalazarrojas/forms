# Driving License Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The driving license application form is designed to collect necessary information from drivers applying for a driving license.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the driver's personal details in the **driver_info** field.
2. Provide information about the vehicle in the **vehicle_info** field.
3. Enter the vehicle's registration number in the **vehicle_reg** field.
4. Enter your driver's license number in the **license_number** field.
5. Enter the date of issue of your driver's license in the **license_issued** field.
6. Enter the date of expiration of your driver's license in the **license_expires** field.
7. Enter the make of your vehicle in the **vehicle_make** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **driver_info** (Text, Optional): Enter your personal details such as your name and contact information.
* **vehicle_info** (Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of your vehicle or any additional details about it.
* **vehicle_reg** (Text, Optional): Enter the registration number of your vehicle as per your country's regulations.
* **driver_license** (Text, Optional): (This field is not explicitly described in the provided YAML fields; we'll assume it's meant for the driver's license type or status.)
* **license_number** (Text, Optional): Enter your driver's license number for identification purposes.
* **license_expires** (Date, Optional): Enter the date your driver's license is set to expire.
* **license_issued** (Date, Optional): Enter the date your driver's license was issued.
* **vehicle_make** (Text, Optional): Enter the make of your vehicle.
