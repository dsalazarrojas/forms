# resident_biometric_access_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect biometric access consent from residents, allowing them to opt-in or opt-out of biometric authentication for sensitive information or resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Please review the information and select the type of biometric access you are granting or denying (Fingerprint, Face, or Voice).
2. Check the "Consent Given" field to indicate whether you consent to biometric access.
3. If you are granting biometric access, please add your signature in the "Signature" field.
4. Enter the date of when you are granting biometric access.
5. Enter the date of when you are submitting this form.
6. Once you have filled out all the necessary fields, please click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Resident Details** (`resident_details`, text, optional): Please enter your name, ID number, or any other identifying information that you are using to complete this form.
* **Biometric Access Type** (`biometric_access_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of biometric access you are granting or denying (Fingerprint, Face, or Voice).
* **Consent Given** (`consent_given`, select_one, required): Check the box if you grant consent to biometric access.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, optional): If you are granting biometric access, please add your signature here.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of when you are granting biometric access.
* **Resident Biometric Access Consent Form Date** (`resident_biometric_access_consent_form_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of when you are submitting this form.

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter accurate information in all fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please contact the authorized personnel.
* If you have already completed this form, do not fill it out again.
