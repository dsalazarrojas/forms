# Appraisal Timing Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a waiver for the timing requirements of an appraisal. The borrower is required to provide their information and reasons for requesting a waiver.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your information in the following fields:
	* Fill in your full name.
	* Enter your loan number (if applicable).
	* Enter your email address.
	* Enter your phone number.
2. Provide the details of your request:
	* Enter the address of the property for which you are requesting a waiver.
	* Choose the reason for your waiver request (Refinance Timing, Purchase Timing, Market Conditions, or Other).
	* Enter a detailed explanation of your reason for requesting a waiver.
3. Review and agree to the terms of the waiver:
	* Confirm that you agree to the terms of the waiver by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
4. Date and sign the form:
	* Enter the date you are signing the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Borrower Name** (`borrower_name`): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`email`): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`): Enter the address of the property for which you are requesting a waiver.
* **Desired Waiver Date** (`desired_waiver_date`): Enter the date you would like the waiver to be effective.
* **Reason for Waiver Request** (`waiver_reason`): Choose the reason for your waiver request from the available options (Refinance Timing, Purchase Timing, Market Conditions, or Other).
* **Detailed Explanation** (`explanation`): Enter a detailed explanation for why you are requesting a waiver.
* **Agree to Terms** (`agree_to_terms`): Confirm that you agree to the terms of the waiver by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Date of Signature** (`date_signed`): Enter the date you are signing this form.
