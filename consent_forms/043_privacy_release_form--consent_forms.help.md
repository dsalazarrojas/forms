# Privacy Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect privacy-related information from employees or employers, likely for official purposes such as HR or administrative tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the field marked "employee's name" on page 1.
2. Enter the date you signed this form in the field marked "date signed" on page 1.
3. Sign your employee ID card or a similar document to confirm your identity, and upload it or enter a copy of it in the field marked "employee_signature" on page 1.
4. Your employer or manager should sign this form to confirm their agreement, and sign in the field marked "employer_signature" on page 1.
5. Enter your address in the field marked "employee_address" on page 1.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee's Name** (employer_name, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it is written on your employee ID card or any other official document.
* **Date Signed** (date_signed, date, required/optional): Enter the date you signed this form.
* **Employee's Signature** (employee_signature, text, required/optional): Sign your employee ID card or a similar document to confirm your identity, and upload or enter a copy of it in this field.
* **Employer's Signature** (employer_signature, text, required/optional): Your employer or manager should sign this form to confirm their agreement.
* **Employee's Address** (employee_address, text, required/optional): Enter your address as it is written on your employee ID card or any other official document.
