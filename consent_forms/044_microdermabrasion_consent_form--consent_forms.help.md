# Microdermabrasion Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from patients for microdermabrasion treatment. It is essential to ensure that patients understand the treatment and its risks, and that they are willing to undergo the procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name in the **Patient Name** field.
2. Select the date of the treatment in the **Treatment Date** field.
3. Enter your address in the **Patient Address** field.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Patient Phone** field.
5. Enter your doctor's name in the **Doctor Name** field (if applicable).
6. Select your doctor's specialty in the **Doctor Speciality** field (if applicable).
7. Select whether you consent to the treatment in the **Consent Signed** field.
8. Enter the reason for your consent in the **Consent Reason** field (if you wish to provide additional information).
9. List any medical conditions you have in the **Medical Condition** field (if applicable).
10. List any allergies you have in the **Allergies** field (if applicable).
11. Provide your medical history in the **Medical History** field (if applicable).
12. Enter the date of your signature in the **Signature Date** field.
13. Sign your name in the **Signature** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
	+ Required: Yes
* **Treatment Date**: The date of the treatment you are consenting to.
	+ Required: Yes
* **Patient Address**: Enter your complete address, including street number and postal code.
	+ Required: Yes
* **Patient Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached during office hours.
	+ Required: Yes
* **Doctor Name**: Enter the name of your doctor if you are seeing a specific doctor for this procedure.
	+ Required: No
* **Doctor Speciality**: Select the specialty of your doctor (if applicable).
	+ Required: No
* **Consent Signed**: Confirm that you have read and understood the consent form, and are willing to undergo the treatment.
	+ Required: Yes
* **Consent Reason**: Provide a brief explanation of why you are consenting to the treatment (if applicable).
	+ Required: No
* **Medical Condition**: List any medical conditions you have that may affect your treatment.
	+ Required: No
* **Allergies**: List any allergies you have that may affect your treatment.
	+ Required: No
* **Medical History**: Provide a brief history of your medical conditions and treatments (if applicable).
	+ Required: No
* **Signature Date**: The date you sign this consent form.
	+ Required: Yes
* **Signature**: Sign your name to confirm your consent.
	+ Required: Yes

## Tips
* Make sure to carefully read and understand the form before signing.
* Ask a medical professional if you have any questions or concerns.
* Sign the form only if you are willing to undergo the treatment.
