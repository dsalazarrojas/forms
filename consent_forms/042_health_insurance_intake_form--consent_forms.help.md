# Health Insurance Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Insurance Intake Form is a critical document used by healthcare providers to gather information about a patient's medical history, insurance status, and other essential details. It is essential to complete this form accurately and completely.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by providing your personal details** (`Name`, text, **required**):
	* Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. **Provide your current address** (`Address`, text, **optional**):
	* Enter your current home or mailing address.
3. **Enter your phone number** (`Phone Number`, text, **required**):
	* Enter your primary phone number that you would like to be contacted at.
4. **Confirm your name** (`Name`, text, **required**):
	* Re-enter your name for verification purposes.
5. **Enter your phone number (again)** (`Phone Number`, text, **required**):
	* Re-enter your phone number for verification purposes.
6. **Describe your relationship with the patient** (`Relationship`, text, **required**):
	* Enter your relationship to the patient (e.g., parent, guardian, spouse).
7. **List any medical conditions you are currently experiencing** (`Medical Condition`, text, **required**):
	* Describe any medical conditions, illnesses, or disabilities you are currently experiencing.
8. **List any allergies you have** (`Allergies`, text, **optional**):
	* List any allergies or sensitivities you may have.
9. **List any medications you are currently taking** (`Meditations`, text, **optional**):
	* List any medications you are currently taking, including dosages and frequencies.
10. **Describe any surgery history** (`Surgery History`, text, **required**):
	* Describe any surgeries or medical procedures you have undergone.
11. **List any hospitalizations** (`Hospitalizations`, text, **required**):
	* List any hospitalizations or extended care stays.
12. **List any medical tests or procedures** (`Medical Tests`, text, **required**):
	* List any medical tests or procedures you have undergone.
13. **Sign and date the form** (`signature`, text, **required**):
	* Sign and date the form to confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields or questions, please ask for clarification or consult with a healthcare professional.
* Please do not leave any fields blank or incomplete, as this may affect your eligibility for health insurance coverage.
