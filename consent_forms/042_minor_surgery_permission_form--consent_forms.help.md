# Minor Surgery Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from a parent or guardian for minor surgery to be performed on a minor patient. It gathers essential information about the patient, the surgery, and the consent given.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Make sure to include the patient's name, date of birth, and address.
* Specify the procedure to be performed and the date and time of surgery.
* Indicate whether consent is given or not.
* Provide any additional comments or information as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Minor Surgery Permission** (`minor_surgery_permission_form`): This is the main section of the form where you can type in the details of the minor surgery.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`): Please enter the patient's name.
* **Patient Date of Birth** (`patient_date_of_birth`): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_or_guardian_name`): If the patient is a minor, please enter the name of the parent or guardian signing this form.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`): Enter the patient's address.
* **Procedure to be Performed** (`procedure_to_be_performed`): Describe the surgical procedure that will be performed.
* **Date of Surgery** (`date_of_surgery`): Enter the date on which the surgery will take place.
* **Time of Surgery** (`time_of_surgery`): Enter the time of the surgery.
* **Consent Given** (`consent_given`): Indicate whether consent is given for the surgery (Yes/No).
* **Comments** (`comments`): Add any additional comments or information as needed.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out completely and accurately.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to the medical team.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, please ask a medical professional for advice.
* This form is intended to be used for informational purposes only and should not be used as a legal document.
