# Security Audio Recording Consent - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Security Audio Recording Consent form is designed to obtain the consent of employees for audio recordings. This form is typically used to create a record of employee consent for audio recordings that may be used as evidence in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the instructions carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and requirements of the form.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Conduct Audio Recording" field to indicate your consent.
3. If you select "Yes", choose the relevant recording device(s) from the "Recording Device" field.
4. Enter the location where the recording will take place in the "Recording Location" field.
5. Select a date for the "Consent Date" field.
6. Choose a time for the "Consent Time" field.
7. Enter your name in the "Employee Name" field.
8. Enter your employee ID in the "Employee ID" field.
9. If applicable, enter the supervisor's name and department in the respective fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Conduct Audio Recording** (consent_to_audio_recording): This field asks you to confirm whether you agree to conduct an audio recording. Please select "Yes" if you are willing to be recorded, and "No" if you are not.
* **Recording Device** (recording_device): If you selected "Yes" for the previous field, you will be presented with a list of recording devices. Select the device(s) that will be used for the recording.
* **Recording Location** (recording_location): Enter the location where the recording will take place. This is important to ensure that the recording is accurate and relevant to the situation.
* **Consent Date** (consent_date): Select a date for the consent. This date will be used to track when the consent was given.
* **Consent Time** (consent_time): Choose a time for the consent. This time will be used to track when the consent was given.
* **Employee Name** (employee_name): Enter your name to confirm your identity and consent.
* **Employee ID** (employee_id): Enter your employee ID to confirm your identity and consent.
* **Supervisor Name** (supervisor_name): If applicable, enter the name of your supervisor who will be involved in the recording.
* **Department** (department): Enter the department you belong to for reference purposes.

## Tips
* Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that the consent is valid and enforceable.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, consult with your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
* Ensure that you understand the purpose and implications of this form before providing your consent.
