# Health Screening Parental Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Screening Parental Authorization Form is a form used to collect authorization and demographic information from parents or guardians of children. This form is used to inform and obtain agreement from parents/guardians about certain health screening activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately with your contact information and demographic details.
2. Ensure that you are completing the form for the correct child.
3. Double-check that all fields are filled out correctly before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consent Form** (Consent Form, note, not required): This section is a general note about the purpose of the form. Please read this section carefully and ask questions if you are unsure.
* **parent_name** (parent_name, text, not required): Please enter your name as a parent or guardian.
* **address_1** (address_1, text, not required): Enter your home address.
* **address_2** (address_2, text, not required): Enter your home address (second line).
* **city** (city, text, not required): Enter the name of the city where you reside.
* **state** (state, text, not required): Enter the name of the state where you reside.
* **zip_code** (zip_code, text, not required): Enter the zip code of your residence.
* **date_of_birth** (date_of_birth, date, not required): Enter the date of birth of the child.
* **phone_number** (phone_number, text, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **email** (email, email, not required): Enter your email address.

## Tips

* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authority for clarification.
* Please ensure that you are completing this form for the correct child.
* Please review this form carefully before submission to ensure that all fields are filled out correctly.
