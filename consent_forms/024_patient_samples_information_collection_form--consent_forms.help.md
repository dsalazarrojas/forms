# Patient Samples Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about patient samples, including their type, collection method, and storage conditions. This helps in maintaining accurate records and ensuring proper handling and storage of patient samples.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for each field, including:
	* Specimen Type (blood, tissue, or other)
	* Collection Method (blood collection, urine collection, fingerprint collection, or tissue sample collection)
	* Patient Name
	* Patient ID
	* Specimen Date Received (if applicable)
	* Consent Date (if applicable)
	* Signature Date (if applicable)
2. Choose the correct storage location and conditions for the sample
3. If necessary, provide additional information about the specimen condition or collection method

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Specimen Type**: Choose the type of specimen (blood, tissue, or other) from the provided options.
* **Specimen Id**: Enter a unique identifier for the specimen, if applicable.
* **Patient Name**: Enter the name of the patient who provided the sample.
* **Patient Id**: Enter the ID number or other identifier for the patient.
* **Specimen Date Received**: Enter the date the sample was received.
* **Collection Method**: Choose the method of collection (blood collection, urine collection, fingerprint collection, or tissue sample collection) from the provided options.
* **Collection Method Other**: Enter any additional details about the collection method, if necessary.
* **Location**: Enter the location where the sample was collected.
* **Storage Location**: Enter the location where the sample will be stored.
* **Storage Conditions**: Enter the conditions under which the sample will be stored.
* **Specimen Condition**: Enter any additional details about the specimen condition.
* **Specimen Condition Other**: Enter any additional details about the specimen condition, if necessary.
* **Consent Date**: Enter the date the patient's consent was obtained.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date the signature was obtained.
* **Signature**: Enter the patient's signature.
* **Blood Collection**: Enter any additional details about blood collection, if necessary.
* **Urine Collection**: Enter any additional details about urine collection, if necessary.
* **Fingerprint Collection**: Enter any additional details about fingerprint collection, if necessary.
* **Tissue Sample Collection**: Enter any additional details about tissue sample collection, if necessary.
* **Other**: Enter any additional details about the collection method, if necessary.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information before saving the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the responsible person for guidance.
