# Declaration Statement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect declarations from organizations, which may include descriptions of their activities, services, or products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the "Declaration" field with a brief description of the organization's activities, services, or products.
2. If the organization is part of an industry that requires it, list the relevant organizations in the "Organizations" field.
3. If you need to declare multiple things, you can do so in the "Declaration 1" field. This will allow you to add additional information without having to add a new form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Declaration** (`declaration_statement_form_declaration`, text, required: false): This field is where you will write a brief description of the organization's activities, services, or products.
* **Organizations** (`declaration_statement_form_organizations`, text, required: false): List any organizations that are relevant to the declaration, such as partners or affiliates.
* **Declaration 1** (`declaration_statement_form_declaration_1`, text, required: false): This field is an additional declaration space. If you need to declare multiple things, you can list them here.
