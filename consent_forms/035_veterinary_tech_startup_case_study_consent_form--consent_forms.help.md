# Veterinary Tech Startup Case Study Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to participate in a case study for a veterinary tech startup. It will help us understand your experiences and feedback to improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow the instructions below:
- Start by providing your User Information (Page 1).
- Fill in your Name, Email, and Phone (Pages 2-4).
- Choose your Role in the organization (Page 5).
- Select the department you're working in (Page 6).
- Choose your consent to participate in the case study (Page 7).
- Finally, add any Additional Comments (Page 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Information (Page 1)**: This page is for you to provide your basic contact information.
  - This information will help us identify you and get in touch with you for the case study.
  - Please fill it in with your real name and contact details.
- **Name (Page 2)**: This is your full name.
  - Please fill in your name as it appears on your official documents or ID.
- **Email (Page 3)**: This is your work or personal email address.
  - Make sure to use a valid email address that we can contact you on.
- **Phone (Page 4)**: This is your work or personal phone number.
  - Use your actual phone number to ensure we can reach you.
- **Role (Page 5)**: Select your role in the organization.
  - Choose from: Vets, Vet Techs, or Clinic Staff.
  - This will help us understand your position and perspective in the case study.
- **Department (Page 6)**: Select the department you're working in.
  - This will help us understand your work environment and tasks.
- **Consent (Page 7)**: Choose your consent to participate.
  - Select: I consent to participate in this study or I do not consent to participate in this study.
  - Please be honest about your decision.
- **Additional Comments (Page 8)**: This is an optional text area for any additional comments.
  - If you have any thoughts or suggestions, please share them here.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form carefully to ensure accuracy.
- If you have any issues or questions, don't hesitate to contact us.
- Remember to review and agree to the consent terms before submitting the form.
