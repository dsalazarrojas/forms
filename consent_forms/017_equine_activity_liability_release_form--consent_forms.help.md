# Equine Activity Liability Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equine Activity Liability Release Form is a document that requires participants to agree to the risks and responsibilities involved in participating in equine activities. This form is essential for ensuring that participants understand and accept the risks and liabilities associated with equine activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the participant's information, including their name, address, email, phone number, age, height, weight, medical condition, equine experience, and signature.
2. If the participant is under 18 years old, fill out the guardian's information, including their name, address, phone number, age, signature, and relationship with the participant.
3. If you are acting as a witness to the participant's agreement, fill out your name, address, phone number, and signature.
4. Select the participant's medical history and equine injury (if any) from the provided options.
5. Ensure the participant and guardian (if applicable) sign and date the participant agreement section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): This field is used to collect the participant's name.
* **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, text, required): If the participant is under 18 years old, this field is used to collect the guardian's name.
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, text, required): This field is used to collect the participant's address.
* **Email** (`participant_email`, text, required): This field is used to collect the participant's email address.
* **Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required): This field is used to collect the participant's phone number.
* **Equine Facility** (`equine_facility`, text, required): This field is used to collect the equine facility information.
* **Equine Activity** (`equine_activity`, text, required): This field is used to collect the equine activity information.
* **Age** (`participant_age`, number, required): This field is used to collect the participant's age.
* **Guardian Age** (`guardian_age`, number, required): If the participant is under 18 years old, this field is used to collect the guardian's age.
* **Height** (`participant_height`, number, required): This field is used to collect the participant's height.
* **Weight** (`participant_weight`, number, required): This field is used to collect the participant's weight.
* **Medical Condition** (`participant_medical_condition`, text, required): This field is used to collect the participant's medical condition.
* **Equine Experience** (`equine_experience`, select_one, required): This field is used to collect the participant's equine experience.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): This field is used to collect the participant's emergency contact information.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, required): This field is used to collect the participant's signature.
* **Guardian Signature** (`guardian_signature`, text, required): If the participant is under 18 years old, this field is used to collect the guardian's signature.
* **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`, text, required): This field is used to collect the witness's signature.
* **Witness Name** (`witness_name`, text, required): This field is used to collect the witness's name.
* **Witness Address** (`witness_address`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the witness's address (optional).
* **Witness Phone** (`witness_phone`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the witness's phone number (optional).
* **Witness Age** (`witness_age`, number, optional): This field is used to collect the witness's age (optional).
* **Witness Relationship** (`witness_relationship`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the witness's relationship with the participant (optional).
* **Medical History** (`participant_medical_history`, text, required): This field is used to collect the participant's medical history.
* **Equine Injury** (`equine_injury`, select_multiple, required): This field is used to collect any equine injuries.
* **Participant Agreement** (`participant_agreement`, text, required): This field is used to collect the participant's agreement.

Note: This guide assumes that the participant is an adult. If the participant is under 18 years old, please ensure that a guardian signs the form as well.
