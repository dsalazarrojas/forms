# Artwork Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Artwork Release Form is a document used to obtain consent from artists for the release of their artwork. This form ensures that artists understand the terms and conditions under which their work will be used, shared, or distributed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the artist's full name in the "Artist Full Name" field.
2. Select the date of agreement using the date picker.
3. Provide a brief description of the artwork being released, including the title and medium if known.
4. Choose the scope of rights granted, such as non-exclusive usage rights or exclusive usage rights.
5. Select all media platforms where the artwork will be used, such as print publications, digital content, or social media.
6. Choose the term of release, including perpetual, one year, or five years.
7. If applicable, enter the compensation or royalty agreement details.
8. Specify how the artist should be credited, if at all.
9. Enter the artist's email address.
10. Certify that you are the sole creator and owner of the artwork, or select "Other" if that's not the case.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist Full Name** (`artist_full_name`, text, required): Enter the artist's full name.
* **Date of Agreement** (`date_of_agreement`, date, required): Select the date of this agreement using the date picker.
* **Description of Artwork Released** (`artwork_description_release`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the artwork being released, including title and medium if known.
* **Scope of Rights Granted** (`rights_granted`, select_one, required): Choose the scope of rights granted, such as non-exclusive usage rights or exclusive usage rights.
* **Authorized Media Platforms** (`media_platforms`, select_multiple, required): Select all media platforms where the artwork will be used, such as print publications, digital content, or social media.
* **Term of Release** (`release_term`, select_one, required): Choose the term of release, including perpetual, one year, or five years.
* **Compensation or Royalty Agreement** (`compensation_details`, text, required): Enter the compensation or royalty agreement details, or enter "N/A" if unpaid/voluntary.
* **Artist Credit Requirement** (`credit_line`, text, optional): Specify how the artist should be credited, if at all.
* **Artist Email Address** (`contact_email_artist`, email, required): Enter the artist's email address.
* **I Certify** (`certification_statement`, select_one, required): Certify that you are the sole creator and owner of the artwork, or select "Other" if that's not the case.
