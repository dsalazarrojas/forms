# Wearable Health Device Tracking Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Wearable Health Device Tracking Consent Form is a document that requires participants to provide their consent for tracking their health data using wearable devices. This form is essential for ensuring that participants are aware of the data being collected and the purpose of its collection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Participant Details**: Enter your participant details, including your name, date of birth, and phone number.
2. **Device Info**: Provide information about the wearable device you are using, such as its make and model.
3. **E-Signature**: Select your E-Signature method, which is a unique identifier that confirms your identity.
4. **E-Signature Date**: Enter the date of your E-Signature.
5. **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth.
6. (Optional) **Date** and **Time**: Enter the date and time of the data collection (if applicable).
7. **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
8. **Date of Collection** and **Time of Collection**: Enter the date and time of data collection (if applicable).
9. **Location**: Enter the location where the data is collected (if applicable).
10. **Notes**: Provide any additional notes or comments about your participation in the study.
11. **Consent**: Confirm your consent for data tracking by selecting "I give permission".
12. **Signature Type**: Choose how you would like to sign this consent form, e.g., digital or physical.
13. **Terms and Conditions**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions of data collection.
14. **Participant Declaration**: Write a declaration stating your participation in the study.
15. **Participant Consent Form**: Select the consent form type (e.g., Consent Form 1 or 2).
16. **Participant Declaration Date**: Enter the date of your participant declaration.
17. **Participant Declaration Time**: Enter the time of your participant declaration.
18. **Participant Declaration Note**: Provide additional notes or comments about your participant declaration.
19. (Note: These last three fields are for internal tracking and do not require user input, they are only for internal reference)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required): Enter your participant details, including your name, date of birth, and phone number.
* **Device Info** (`device_info`, text, required): Provide information about the wearable device you are using, such as its make and model.
* **E-Signature** (`e_signature`, select_multiple, required): Select your E-Signature method, which is a unique identifier that confirms your identity. Options: E-Signature, E-Signature 2.
* **E-Signature Date** (`e_signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your E-Signature.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of data collection (if applicable).
* **Time** (time, optional): Enter the time of data collection (if applicable).
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Collection** (date, optional): Enter the date of data collection (if applicable).
* **Time of Collection** (time, optional): Enter the time of data collection (if applicable).
* **Location** (text, optional): Enter the location where the data is collected (if applicable).
* **Notes** (note, optional): Provide any additional notes or comments about your participation in the study.
* **Consent** (select_multiple, required): Confirm your consent for data tracking by selecting "I give permission".
* **Signature Type** (select_one, required): Choose how you would like to sign this consent form, e.g., digital or physical. Options: Signature 1, Signature 2, Signature 3.
* **Terms and Conditions** (note, required): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of data collection.
* **Participant Declaration** (text, required): Write a declaration stating your participation in the study.
* **Participant Consent Form** (select_one, required): Select the consent form type (e.g., Consent Form 1 or 2).
* **Participant Declaration Date** (date, required): Enter the date of your participant declaration.
* **Participant Declaration Time** (time, required): Enter the time of your participant declaration.
* **Participant Declaration Note** (note, required): Provide additional notes or comments about your participant declaration.
