# haunted_house_waiver_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a consent waiver for visitors of the haunted house. It collects essential contact and medical information for safety and liability reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in your full name in the <full_name> field.
2.  Enter the date of your visit to the haunted house in the <visit_date> field. Use the date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
3.  Fill in the time of your visit to the haunted house in the <visit_time> field. Please use the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
4.  Enter your email address in the <email> field.
5.  Fill in your phone number in the <phone_number> field.
6.  Type in your address in the <address> field.
7.  Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact person in the <emergency_contact_name> and <emergency_phone_number> fields, respectively.
8.  Describe any existing medical conditions in the <medical_condition> field. This is crucial for our staff's awareness of potential health risks.
9.  Read and agree to the terms of the waiver in the <waiver> field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **full_name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Fill in your full name, as it appears on your official documents.
* **visit_date** (`visit_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your planned visit to the haunted house in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **visit_time** (`visit_time`, `time`, required): Fill in the time of your visit in the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address that we can contact you with in case of emergencies.
* **phone_number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Fill in your phone number that we can contact you with in case of emergencies.
* **address** (`address`, `text`, required): Type in your full address, including street number and city.
* **emergency_contact_name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact person, who we will contact in case of emergencies.
* **emergency_phone_number** (`emergency_phone_number`, `text`, required): Fill in the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **medical_condition** (`medical_condition`, `text`, required): Describe any existing medical conditions that may affect your visit, so our staff can take necessary precautions.
* **waiver** (`waiver`, `note`, required): Carefully read and agree to the terms of the waiver.
