# Inheritance Rights Waiver Affidavit - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inheritance Rights Waiver Affidavit is a legal document used to waive the rights to an estate or property in favor of the beneficiary named in a will or trust.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, using the information provided below.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand what information is required and what format is expected.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Beneficiary Name** (`beneficiary_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the beneficiary who is waiving their rights.
* **Relationship to Deceased** (`relationship_to_deceased`, text, optional): Describe your relationship to the deceased, if any.
* **Address (Current Residence)** (`address_current_residence`, text, optional): Enter your current address, which is used as a contact address for communication purposes.
* **City** (`city_current_residence`, text, optional): Enter the city of your current residence.
* **State** (`state_current_residence`, text, optional): Enter the state of your current residence.
* **Zip** (`zip_current_residence`, text, optional): Enter the zip code of your current residence.
* **Address (Beneficiary Residence)** (`beneficiary_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the beneficiary's residence.
* **City Beneficiary Residence** (`city_beneficiary_residence`, text, optional): Enter the city of the beneficiary's residence.
* **State Beneficiary Residence** (`state_beneficiary_residence`, text, optional): Enter the state of the beneficiary's residence.
* **Zip Beneficiary Residence** (`zip_beneficiary_residence`, text, optional): Enter the zip code of the beneficiary's residence.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the beneficiary.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the beneficiary.
* **Date of Death** (`date_of_death`, date, optional): Enter the date of death of the deceased.
* **Time of Death** (`time_of_death`, time, optional): Enter the time of death of the deceased.
* **Date of Disposition** (`date_of_disposition`, date, optional): Enter the date the estate or property was disposed (distributed or settled).

## Tips

- Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form or the process, please contact the relevant authorities or an attorney.
- This form is not a substitute for legal advice, and you should consult an attorney if you are unsure about any of the information provided or have questions.
