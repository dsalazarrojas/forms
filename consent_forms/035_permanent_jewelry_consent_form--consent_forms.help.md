# Permanent Jewelry Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for clients to provide informed consent for permanent jewelry procedures. It collects information about the client's personal details, potential risks and concerns, and specific details about the permanent jewelry to be performed, such as design, size, type, and location of placement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Fill in your personal details in the "Client Information" section.
*   If you have any risks or concerns, please provide them in the "Risks and Concerns" section.
*   In the "Permanent Jewelry Details" section, choose the type of permanent jewelry you want to get (e.g., Pendent, Bar, Pendant, Ring, Nose Stud, Ladder, Chandelier, or Other).
*   Enter the size and type of permanent jewelry you want in the corresponding fields.
*   Select the location for the permanent jewelry.
*   Provide any additional comments or instructions about your permanent jewelry in the "Permanent Jewelry Comments" section.
*   Sign and date the form to indicate your consent.
*   Have a witness sign the "Permanent Jewelry Witness Signature" section to confirm they have witnessed your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Information** (`client_information`, text, required): This section is for you to enter your personal details such as name, phone number, and email.
*   **Risks and Concerns** (`risks_and_concerns`, text, required): Please list any risks or concerns you have regarding the permanent jewelry procedure.
*   **Consent and Agreement** (`consent_and_agreement`, text, required): This section is for you to confirm your consent and agreement to the permanent jewelry procedure.
*   **Permanent Jewelry Details** (`permanent_jewelry_details`, text, required): Here, you can enter any additional comments or instructions about your permanent jewelry.
*   **Permanent Jewelry Designs** (`permanent_jewelry_designs`, select multiple, required): Choose the type of permanent jewelry you want to get (e.g., Pendent, Bar, Pendant, Ring, Nose Stud, Ladder, Chandelier, or Other).
*   **Permanent Jewelry Size** (`permanent_jewelry_size`, number, required): Enter the size of your permanent jewelry.
*   **Permanent Jewelry Type** (`permanent_jewelry_type`, select one, required): Select the type of permanent jewelry you want (e.g., Implant, Jewelry, or Implant and Jewelry).
*   **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your consent.
*   **Consent Time** (`consent_time`, time, required): Enter the time of your consent.
*   **Permanent Jewelry Placement** (`permanent_jewelry_placement`, text, required): Specify the location for your permanent jewelry.
*   **Permanent Jewelry Material** (`permanent_jewelry_material`, text, required): Enter the material of your permanent jewelry.
*   **Permanent Jewelry Placement Location** (`permanent_jewelry_placement_location`, text, required): Specify the location where the permanent jewelry will be placed.
*   **Permanent Jewelry Diameter** (`permanent_jewelry_diameter`, number, required): Enter the diameter of your permanent jewelry.
*   **Permanent Jewelry Length** (`permanent_jewelry_length`, number, required): Enter the length of your permanent jewelry.
*   **Permanent Jewelry Gauge** (`permanent_jewelry_gauge`, number, required): Enter the gauge of your permanent jewelry.
*   **Permanent Jewelry Color** (`permanent_jewelry_color`, text, required): Select the color of your permanent jewelry.
*   **Permanent Jewelry Comments** (`permanent_jewelry_comments`, text, required): Enter any additional comments or instructions about your permanent jewelry.
*   **Permanent Jewelry Signature** (`permanent_jewelry_signature`, text, required): Sign the form to indicate your consent.
*   **Permanent Jewelry Witness Signature** (`permanent_jewelry_witness_signature`, text, required): Have a witness sign the form to confirm your consent.
*   **Permanent Jewelry Studio** (`permanent_jewelry_studio`, text, required): Enter the name of the studio where the permanent jewelry will be performed.
*   **Permanent Jewelry Designer** (`permanent_jewelry_designer`, text, required): Enter the name of the designer who will be performing your permanent jewelry.
*   **Permanent Jewelry Design Type** (`permanent_jewelry_design_type`, text, required): Select the type of design you want for your permanent jewelry.
*   **Permanent Jewelry Date** (`permanent_jewelry_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your permanent jewelry procedure.
*   **Permanent Jewelry Time** (`permanent_jewelry_time`, time, required): Enter the time of your permanent jewelry procedure.
*   **Permanent Jewelry Studio Signature** (`permanent_jewelry_studio_signature`, text, required): Enter the signature of the studio where the permanent jewelry will be performed.

Note: Some fields are marked as required, which means that they must be filled out for the form to be complete.
