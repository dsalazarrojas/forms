# Telehealth Counseling Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a consent form for telehealth counseling services. It collects essential information about the patient's contact details, medical history, and agreement to participate in telehealth counseling.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, date of birth, email address, and phone number in the "Patient Info" section.
2. Enter your name as it appears on your medical card or ID in the "Patient Name" field.
3. Provide your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Enter your email address where you can be reached for communication.
5. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
6. If applicable, enter your insurance information in the "Insurance Info" section.
7. If applicable, provide the name of your insurance provider.
8. If applicable, enter your policy number.
9. In the "Medical History" section, describe any medical conditions, medications, or surgeries you have had in the past 2 years.
10. In the "Emergency Contact" section, provide the name and contact information for someone you would like us to contact in case of an emergency.
11. In the "Emergency Contact Info" section, enter the relationship between you and the emergency contact person.
12. In the "Medical Authority" section, enter the name of the doctor or healthcare provider who referred you to our services.
13. In the "Electronic Signature" section, sign your name to confirm that you understand and agree to the terms of this consent form.
14. In the "Electronic Signature Date" and "Electronic Signature Time" sections, enter the date and time of your electronic signature.
15. In the "Electronic Signature IP" section, enter the IP address from which you are accessing this form.
16. In the "Electronic Signature Agreement" section, confirm that you have read and agree to the terms of this consent form.
17. In the "Electronic Signature Agreement Date" and "Electronic Signature Agreement Time" sections, enter the date and time of your agreement.
18. In the "Telehealth Agreement" section, confirm that you understand and agree to the telehealth agreement.
19. In the "Telehealth Agreement IP" section, enter the IP address from which you are accessing the telehealth platform.
20. In the "Telehealth Agreement Date" section, enter the date of your agreement.

* **Electronic Signature**: Sign your name to confirm that you understand and agree to the terms of this consent form.
* **Electronic Signature Date**: Enter the date of your electronic signature.
* **Electronic Signature Time**: Enter the time of your electronic signature.
* **Electronic Signature IP**: Enter the IP address from which you are accessing this form.
* **Electronic Signature Agreement**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms of this consent form.
