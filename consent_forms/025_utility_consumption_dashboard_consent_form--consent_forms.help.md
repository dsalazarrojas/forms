# utility_consumption_dashboard_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from residents for the utility consumption dashboard, allowing them to display and analyze their utility usage data.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully to ensure you understand the purpose and fields included.
* Answer all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the field label.
* Select your preferred options for each field, as described below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resident Permission**: Select your preferred level of access to the utility consumption dashboard.
	+ Utility Consumption Dashboard: Provides access to the dashboard.
	+ No Access: Denies access to the dashboard.
* **Display and Analyze Utility Usage Data**: Select whether you want to display and analyze your utility usage data.
	+ Yes: Allows display and analysis of your data.
	+ No: Denies display and analysis of your data.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address associated with your account.
* **Phone Number**: Enter a valid phone number associated with your account.
* **Date**: Enter a date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time**: Enter a time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **Energy Programs**: Select your preferred energy program (e.g. None, Comcast XOOM, ComEd).

## Tips

* Ensure you have entered accurate and valid information in all fields.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact your system administrator for assistance.
