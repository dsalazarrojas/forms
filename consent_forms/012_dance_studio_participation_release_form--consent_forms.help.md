# Dance Studio Participation Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dance Studio Participation Release Form is a document designed to collect vital information from dance studio participants. This form is essential to ensure a safe and responsible environment for everyone involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user information, including your name, address, and any other relevant details.
2. Provide contact information, such as your phone number and email address, so that the studio can reach you in case of an emergency.
3. List your emergency contacts, including their names, phone numbers, and their relationship with you.
4. Sign the consent section to confirm that you understand the studio's policies and procedures.
5. Provide any additional emergency contact details if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information (Page 1)**: This section is where you provide your personal details, such as your name, address, and any other relevant information.
* **Contact Information (Page 2)**: In this section, you'll provide the studio with your contact details, such as your phone number and email address, so that they can reach you if there's an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact (Page 3)**: List your emergency contacts, including their names, phone numbers, and relationship with you.
* **Consent (Page 4)**: Sign this section to confirm that you have read and understood the studio's policies and procedures.
* **Emergency Details (Page 5)**: If you have any additional emergency contact details or information, such as medical conditions or allergies, you can provide them here.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* If you're unsure about any information, ask the studio staff for clarification.
* Review the studio's policies and procedures before signing the consent section.
* Keep a copy of this form for your records.
