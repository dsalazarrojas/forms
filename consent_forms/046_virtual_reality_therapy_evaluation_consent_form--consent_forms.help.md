# Virtual Reality Therapy Evaluation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Reality Therapy Evaluation Consent Form is a document that clients must complete to provide informed consent before participating in our Virtual Reality Therapy (VRT) program. This form helps clients understand the benefits and risks of the therapy, as well as their rights and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Client Info section with your name, email, and phone number.
2. In the Explain VRT section, provide a brief explanation of your understanding of the VRT program.
3. In the Obtain Consent section, select 'True' if you understand and agree to the terms of the therapy.
4. In the Manage Data section, provide any other comments or information you would like to share with our team.
5. If applicable, fill out the Phone Number, Email, Date, and Time sections with the relevant information.
6. Finally, in the Other Info section, provide any other relevant information about your experience or expectations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Info** (`client_info`, text, required/optional): Please fill out this section with your name, email, and phone number. This information will be used to contact you with any updates or concerns.
* **Explain VRT** (`explain_vr_treatment`, text, required/optional): Please provide a brief explanation of your understanding of the Virtual Reality Therapy program. This will help us understand your expectations and goals.
* **Obtain Consent** (`obtain_consent`, select_one, required/optional): Please select 'True' if you understand and agree to the terms of the therapy. This is a required field, as it is essential to ensure that you are aware of the therapy's benefits and risks.
* **Manage Data** (`manage_data`, text, required/optional): If you have any other comments or information you would like to share with our team, please provide them in this section.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, number, required/optional): If you have a preferred phone number where we can contact you, please enter it here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): If you have a preferred email address where we can contact you, please enter it here.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): If you have a preferred date for the therapy sessions, please enter it here.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): If you have a preferred time for the therapy sessions, please enter it here.
* **Other Info** (`other_info`, text, required/optional): If you have any other relevant information about your experience or expectations, please provide it here.
