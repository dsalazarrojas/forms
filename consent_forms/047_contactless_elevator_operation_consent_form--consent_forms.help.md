# Contactless Elevator Operation Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather consent from residents or authorized users for contactless elevator operation. It ensures that everyone who uses the elevator has given their approval for the touchless system to be activated.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Type in your unit number in the "Unit Number" field.
4. Enter the name of the building in which you reside in the "Building Name" field.
5. Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
7. Select your occupancy type (Owner, Tenant, or Authorized Occupant) in the "Occupancy Type" field.
8. Choose whether you grant consent for contactless elevator operation in the "Consent Grant" field.
9. Select your preferred method of accessing the elevator (Mobile App, Key Fob, Access Card, Voice Control, or Motion Sensor) in the "Preferred Access Method" field.
10. If using a mobile app, select the type of mobile device you use (iOS - iPhone, Android, Not Using Mobile) in the "Mobile Device Type" field.
11. Enter the number of authorized users in the "Number of Authorized Users" field.
12. List all authorized users in the "Names of Authorized Users" field.
13. Indicate if you have any accessibility needs in the "Accessibility Needs" field.
14. Provide details about any accessibility needs you have in the "Accessibility Details" field.
15. Consent to data collection in the "Data Collection Consent" field.
16. Accept the terms and conditions in the "Terms and Conditions Acceptance" field.
17. Enter the name of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
18. Enter the phone number of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.
19. Sign the form digitally in the "Digital Signature" field.
20. Enter today's date in the "Date of Signature" field.
21. If applicable, provide the name of the witness who signed the form in the "Witness Name" field.
22. Enter the effective date when this consent starts in the "Effective Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`resident_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`resident_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Unit Number** (`unit_number`, text, required): Type in your apartment or unit number.
* **Building Name** (`building_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the building where you reside.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Occupancy Type** (`occupancy_type`, select_one, required): Select whether you are an Owner, Tenant, or Authorized Occupant.
* **Consent Grant** (`consent_grant`, select_one, required): Check if you grant consent for contactless elevator operation.
* **Preferred Access Method** (`access_method`, select_multiple, required): Select how you prefer to access the elevator.
* **Mobile Device Type** (`mobile_device`, select_one, not required): If using a mobile app, select the type of mobile device you use.
* **Number of Authorized Users** (`authorized_users`, number, required): Enter the number of people authorized to use the elevator.
* **Names of Authorized Users** (`user_names`, text, required): List all the names of authorized users.
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have any accessibility needs.
* **Accessibility Details** (`accessibility_details`, text, not required): Provide details about any accessibility needs you have.
* **Data Collection Consent** (`data_collection_consent`, select_one, required): Consent to data collection.
* **Terms and Conditions Acceptance** (`terms_acceptance`, select_one, required): Accept the terms and conditions.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Digital Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Sign the form digitally.
* **Date of Signature** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter today's date.
* **Witness Name** (`witness_name`, text, not required): If applicable, enter the name of the witness who signed the form.
* **Effective Date** (`effective_date`, date, required): Enter the effective date when this consent starts.
