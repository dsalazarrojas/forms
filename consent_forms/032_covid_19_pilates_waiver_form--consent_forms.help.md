# Covid 19 Pilates Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Covid 19 Pilates Waiver form is used to collect consent and medical history information from individuals taking part in Pilates classes during the Covid 19 pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First name and Last name in the appropriate fields.
2. Enter your Email address.
3. Enter your Phone number.
4. Enter your Date of birth.
5. Enter your Address.
6. Select any Medical conditions you have from the dropdown menu.
7. If you are taking any medications, enter the name in the text field.
8. If you have a doctor, enter their name and contact information in the text fields.
9. Select your release options for the waiver, each question is a release for specific information that the studio has the right to collect.
10. Sign and date the form.
