# Group Confidentiality Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from group participants to share their information and collaborate on a group project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your participant name in the "Participant Name" field.
2.  Enter your participant email in the "Participant Email" field.
3.  Write a brief agreement or statement in the "Agreement" field, indicating your understanding of the group's confidentiality guidelines.
4.  Enter the name of the group you are participating in in the "Group Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name (text, required):** Enter your name as the participant in the group.
* **Participant Email (email, required):** Enter your email address where you can be reached if necessary.
* **Agreement (note, required):** Write a brief statement indicating your understanding of the group's confidentiality guidelines.
* **Group Name (text, required):** Enter the name of the group you are participating in.
