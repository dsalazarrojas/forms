# Parent Guardian Attestation - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from a parent or guardian to confirm or attest to the health status of a student. It is typically used in situations where a student may have been exposed to COVID-19 or has shown symptoms of the disease.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the Parent Guardian's name in the "Parent Guardian Name" field.
2. Check any symptoms that you believe the student may be experiencing. You can choose one or more of the options from the dropdown menu. Note that if you select "Other (please describe)" you can provide a brief description of the symptom in the box below.
3. If you are the student, enter your own name and contact information in the "Attester's Name", "Attester's Phone", and "Attester's Email" fields.
4. If you are not the student, ensure that the student is aware that you are filling out this form on their behalf.
5. Submit the form once you have completed all the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Guardian Name** (parent_guardian_name): The name of the parent or guardian who is signing this form.
* **Have any of the following signs of COVID-19** (covid_symptoms): Check any symptoms that you believe the student is experiencing. These symptoms include:
	+ Fever
	+ Cough
	+ Shortness of Breath
	+ Loss of Smell
	+ Sore Throat
	+ Runny Nose
	+ Headache
	+ Fatigue
	+ Muscle Aches
	+ Nausea
	+ Diarrhea
	+ Vomiting
	+ Confusion
	+ Loss of Speech
	+ Loss of Appetite
	+ Loss of Taste
* **Attester's Name** (attester_name): This field is used to enter the name of the person filling out this form on behalf of the student.
* **Attester's Phone** (attester_phone): This field is used to enter the phone number of the person filling out this form.
* **Attester's Email** (attester_email): This field is used to enter the email address of the person filling out this form.
