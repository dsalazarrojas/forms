# Hypnotherapy Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hypnotherapy Informed Consent Form is used to record informed consent from a patient before proceeding with hypnotherapy services. This form ensures that the patient is aware of and agrees to the services provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the patient's contact number in the "Contact Number" field, if applicable.
3. Type in the patient's email address in the "Email Address" field, if applicable.
4. Fill in the patient's address in the "Address" field.
5. Select the date of service using the "Date of Service" field.
6. Fill in the time of service using the "Time of Service" field in 24-hour format.
7. Describe the hypnotherapy service in the "Service Description" field.
8. Enter your name as the therapist in the "Therapist Name" field.
9. Select one of the options in the "Consent" field to indicate agreement or disagreement with the hypnotherapy services.
10. Add a signature in the "Signature" field.
11. Date and sign the "Date Signed" field.
12. Add your signature as the therapist in the "Therapist Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the patient's phone number, if available.
* **Email Address**: Type in the patient's email address, if available.
* **Address**: Fill in the patient's address.
* **Date of Service**: Select the date of the hypnotherapy service.
* **Time of Service**: Enter the time of the service using 24-hour format.
* **Service Description**: Describe the hypnotherapy service being provided.
* **Therapist Name**: Enter your name as the hypnotherapy professional providing the service.
* **Consent**: Select the option "I consent to receiving hypnotherapy services" if you agree, otherwise select "I do not consent to receiving hypnotherapy services".
* **Signature**: Add your signature as the patient.
* **Date Signed**: Date and sign the field to indicate the date of signing.
* **Therapist Signature**: Add your signature as the hypnotherapy professional providing the service.

**Tips**: Make sure to review each field carefully to ensure accurate and complete information.
