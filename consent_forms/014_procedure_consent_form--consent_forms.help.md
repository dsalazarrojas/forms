# Procedure Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Procedure Consent Form is used to obtain consent from patients for a medical procedure. It is a crucial document that ensures the patient's rights and safety are protected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Enter your name in the "prophets_name" field.
2.  Describe the procedure you are consenting to in the "describe_the_procedure" field.
3.  If applicable, select any risks associated with the procedure in the "risk" field.
4.  If applicable, select the benefits of the procedure in the "benefit" field.
5.  Sign your name in the "prophets_signature" field.
6.  Enter the date the procedure was completed in the "prophets_date" field.
7.  Enter the name of the person who completed the form in the "prophets_completed_by" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **prophets_name** (text, Optional): Enter your name.
* **name** (text, Optional): Enter your name (this field seems to be a duplicate, please disregard or clarify the purpose of this field).
* **risk** (select_multiple, Optional): Select any risks associated with the procedure.
* **benefit** (select_one, Optional): Select if the procedure has benefits.
* **describe_the_procedure** (note, Optional): Describe the procedure in detail.
* **prophets_signature** (text, Optional): Sign your name to indicate your consent.
* **prophets_date** (date, Optional): Enter the date the procedure was completed.
* **prophets_completed_by** (text, Optional): Enter the name of the person who completed the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before signing.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask your healthcare provider.
* If you are unable to complete the form, please contact your healthcare provider for assistance.
