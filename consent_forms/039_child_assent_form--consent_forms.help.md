# Child Assent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Child Assent Form is a document used to ensure that children understand and agree to participate in research or procedures. This form aims to establish clear communication and obtain informed consent from child participants.

## How To Complete This Form

Before you begin, take a few minutes to review the form carefully. You will be asked to complete the form in sections, and each section has been designed to ensure you understand the study or procedure's purpose, risks, and benefits. Answer the questions truthfully, and remember that your participation is completely voluntary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Your name** (Your name, text, required): Enter your name to identify yourself as a participant.
- **Date of birth** (Date of birth, date, required): Provide your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
- **Age** (Age, number, required): Enter your age as a number.
- **Study or procedure title** (Study or procedure title, text, required): Enter the title of the study or procedure.
- **Investigator or researcher name** (Investigator or researcher name, text, required): Enter the name of the investigator or researcher.
- **Do you understand the purpose of this study?** (Do you understand the purpose of this study?, select_one, required): Choose one of the options to indicate your understanding of the study's purpose.
- **Do you understand what you will be asked to do?** (Do you understand what you will be asked to do?, select_one, required): Choose one of the options to indicate your understanding of the procedures involved.
- **Do you understand how much time is involved?** (Do you understand how much time is involved?, select_one, required): Choose one of the options to indicate your understanding of the time commitment required.
- **Do you understand the possible benefits?** (Do you understand the possible benefits?, select_one, required): Choose one of the options to indicate your understanding of the potential benefits.
- **Do you understand the possible risks or discomforts?** (Do you understand the possible risks or discomforts?, select_one, required): Choose one of the options to indicate your understanding of the potential risks or discomforts.
- **Do you understand that your information will be confidential?** (Do you understand that your information will be confidential?, select_one, required): Choose one of the options to indicate your understanding of the confidentiality of your information.
- **Do you understand you can stop at any time?** (Do you understand you can stop at any time?, select_one, required): Choose one of the options to indicate your understanding of the right to withdraw participation.
- **Have all your questions been answered?** (Have all your questions been answered?, select_one, required): Choose one of the options to indicate whether you have had all your questions addressed.
- **Do you have any remaining questions?** (Do you have any remaining questions?, text, optional): If you have any questions or concerns, enter them here.
- **I understand my participation is completely voluntary** (I understand my participation is completely voluntary, select_one, required): Choose one of the options to indicate your understanding of your voluntary participation.
- **Are you willing to participate in this study?** (Are you willing to participate in this study?, select_one, required): Choose one of the options to indicate your willingness to participate.
- **Does your parent or guardian also agree?** (Does your parent or guardian also agree?, select_one, required): Choose one of the options to indicate whether your parent or guardian has agreed to your participation.
- **Investigator confirms child understands the study** (Investigator confirms child understands the study, select_one, required): Choose one of the options to confirm the investigator's understanding of your comprehension.
- **I agree to participate in this study** (I agree to participate in this study, select_one, required): Check the box to indicate your agreement to participate.
