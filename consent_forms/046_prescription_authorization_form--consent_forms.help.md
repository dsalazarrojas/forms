# Prescription Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prescription Authorization Form is used in medical settings to collect information about a patient's medical history and obtain authorization from a prescriber to dispense prescription medications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Patient's Name** in the `patient_name` field.
2. Enter the **Patient's Date of Birth** in the `patient_dob` field in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
3. Enter the **Prescriber's Name** in the `prescriber_name` field.
4. Enter the **Prescriber's License Number** in the `prescriber_license` field.
5. Enter the **Prescriber's Phone Number** in the `prescriber_phone` field.
6. Enter the **Prescriber's Email Address** in the `prescriber_email` field.
7. Add the **Prescriber's Signature** in the `prescriber_signature` field, which serves as authorization for prescription dispensing.
8. If applicable, list the **Authorized Individual(s)** in the `authorized_individual` field, indicating who is authorized to dispense the prescription.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: The patient's name is required to be filled in for proper identification.
* **Patient Date of Birth**: The patient's date of birth is used to ensure that the patient's medical history is up-to-date.
* **Prescriber Name**: The prescriber's name is required to be filled in for proper authorization.
* **Prescriber License Number**: The prescriber's license number is used to verify their credentials.
* **Prescriber Phone Number**: The prescriber's phone number is used for contact purposes.
* **Prescriber Email Address**: The prescriber's email address is used for contact purposes.
* **Prescriber Signature**: The prescriber's signature serves as official authorization for prescription dispensing.
* **Authorized Individual**: If applicable, list the individual(s) authorized to dispense the prescription.
