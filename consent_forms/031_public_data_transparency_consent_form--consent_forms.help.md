# public_data_transparency_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about yourself for the purpose of public data transparency. This includes your personal details (name, date of birth, address, contact information) and consent to share and use your data. Please fill out the form carefully, as it is a legal requirement for our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out your personal details (first name, last name, birthdate, address, email, phone number) accurately.
2. Select the correct consent options for data sharing and usage.
3. Sign the form to confirm your agreement.
4. Choose the type of signature you're providing (e.g., handwritten, digital).
5. Add any additional notes or comments (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is spelled on your identification documents.
- **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is spelled on your identification documents.
- **Birth Date**: Enter your date of birth (DD/MM/YYYY).
- **Address**: Enter your residential or business address.
- **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
- **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
- **Consent to Share**: Select all the options where you consent to share your data (e.g., with third-party organizations, publicly).
- **Consent to Use**: Select all the options where you consent to use your data (e.g., for marketing purposes, research).
- **Signature**: Sign the form to confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions.
- **Date of Signature**: Enter the date you signed the form (DD/MM/YYYY).
- **Signature Confirmation**: This is a confirmation field to ensure your signature is genuine.
- **Signature Type**: Choose the type of signature you're providing (e.g., handwritten, digital).
- **Notes**: Add any additional comments or information you'd like to provide.
- **Organization**: If applicable, enter the name of your organization.
- **Title**: Enter your title or position.
- **Signature** (again): Sign the form to confirm your agreement.
- **Date of Signature** (again): Enter the date you signed the form (DD/MM/YYYY).
- **Signature Confirmation** (again): This is a confirmation field to ensure your signature is genuine.

## Tips
- Be accurate when filling out your personal details.
- Choose your consent options carefully, as they affect how your data is used.
- Use a reliable method to sign the form (e.g., print and sign with a pen, use an electronic signature tool).
- Ensure the date of signature is accurate. 

By following this guide, you should be able to complete the public data transparency consent form accurately and effectively. If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact our support team.
