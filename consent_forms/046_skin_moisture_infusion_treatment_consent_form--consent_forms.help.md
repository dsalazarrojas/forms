# skin_moisture_infusion_treatment_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a client-facing consent form for skin moisturization treatments, designed to ensure that clients are aware of and agree to the treatment procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client details on page 1.
2. Provide any relevant health information on page 3.
3. Select whether you consent to the treatment on page 4.
4. Sign and date the form on page 6.
5. Have the esthetician sign and enter their name on page 7.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **skin_moisture_infusion_treatment_consent_form_details** (`text`, required: false): This is the main form section where you can provide any details relevant to your skin moisturization treatment.
* **client_details** (`text`, required: false): Enter your contact information, such as your name, address, phone number, and email, so that we can communicate with you about your treatment.
* **health_information** (`text`, required: false): Provide any relevant health information that may affect the treatment, such as allergies, medical conditions, etc.
* **consent** (`select_one`, required: false): Please select "I consent" if you agree to the treatment procedures. If you do not agree, please contact us to discuss further.
* **signed_by** (`text`, required: false): You can sign your name on this field to confirm your consent.
* **signature** (`date`, required: false): Date and sign the form on this field.
* **esthetician_signature** (`text`, required: false): The esthetician will sign this field after performing the treatment.
* **esthetician_name** (`text`, required: false): Enter the name of the esthetician performing the treatment.

## Tips
* Please note that this form is a requirement for all skin moisturization treatments. 
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form or the treatment, please contact us prior to signing the form.
* If you are unable to sign the form due to circumstances, please discuss with the esthetician and come to a mutually agreed solution.
