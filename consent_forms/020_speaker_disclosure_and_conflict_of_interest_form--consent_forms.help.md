> ## Speaker Disclosure And Conflict Of Interest Form - Help Guide
> 
> ## Purpose
>
The Speaker Disclosure And Conflict Of Interest Form is used to collect information about speakers' affiliations, financial interests, and potential conflicts of interest related to the event they are attending. This form helps to ensure that speakers are aware of and comply with the event's policies and guidelines for maintaining impartiality and avoiding conflicts of interest.
>
> ## How To Complete This Form
>
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
> 1. Please review and provide the required fields marked as "Required" below.
> 2. Answer the questions truthfully and in full detail.
>
> ## Field-by-Field Explanation
>
* **Speaker Disclosure And Conflict Of Interest Form** (`speaker_disclosure_and_conflict_of_interest_form`, text, required: false): This is the title of the form, which cannot be completed.
* **Speaker Details** (`speaker_details`, text, required: false): Please provide your personal and professional information, such as your name, contact details, and title.
* **Speaker Affiliations** (`speaker_affiliations`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate if you have any affiliation with the event or organization hosting the event, such as employment or partnership. Select one or more of the options provided.
* **Speaker Financial Interests** (`speaker_financial_interests`, text, required: false): If you have any financial interests related to the event or organization hosting the event, describe them here.
* **Speaker Conflicts Of Interest** (`speaker_conflicts_of_interest`, text, required: false): If you have any conflicts of interest related to the event or organization hosting the event, describe them here.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required: false): Please sign this form to confirm that you have read and understood the event's policies and guidelines for maintaining impartiality and avoiding conflicts of interest.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required: false): Date and sign this form to confirm that you have read and understood the event's policies and guidelines for maintaining impartiality and avoiding conflicts of interest.
* **Speaker Financial Relationships** (`speaker_financial_relationships`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate if you have any financial relationships with the event or organization hosting the event, such as sponsorship or consulting. Select one or more of the options provided.
* **Conflict Of Interest Details** (`conflict_of_interest_details`, text, required: false): If you have any conflicts of interest related to the event or organization hosting the event, describe them here.

Note: All fields are not required for completion. Please only provide the necessary information for the fields marked as "Required".
