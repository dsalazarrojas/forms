# Mental Health Workshop Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Workshop Consent Form is used to gather information from participants for the mental health workshop. This includes details about the workshop, participant information, contact details, and emergency contacts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Complete all the required fields (marked as "required") accurately and thoroughly.
2. Review each field carefully, and make sure to provide the correct information.
3. If you have any doubts or concerns, feel free to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Mental Health Workshop (workshop_details)
* **Mental Health Workshop** (workshop_details, text, required/optional): This field is used to provide a brief description of the mental health workshop. Please enter a concise and accurate description of the workshop.

### 2. Participant Details (participant_details)
* **Participant Details** (participant_details, text, required/optional): This field is used to gather information about the participant. Please enter your details accurately and thoroughly.

### 3. Email (email)
* **Email** (email, email, required/optional): This field is used to collect your email address. Please enter your correct email address.

### 4. Phone (phone)
* **Phone** (phone, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect your phone number. Please enter your correct phone number.

### 5. Date of Workshop (date_workshop)
* **Date of Workshop** (date_workshop, date, required/optional): This field is used to gather the date of the workshop. Please enter the date of the workshop.

### 6. Time of Workshop (time_workshop)
* **Time of Workshop** (time_workshop, time, required/optional): This field is used to gather the time of the workshop. Please enter the time of the workshop.

### 7. Consent (consent)
* **Consent** (consent, select_one, required/optional): This field is used to confirm your consent to participate in the workshop. Please select "True" to confirm your consent.

### 8. Additional Notes (notes)
* **Additional Notes** (notes, note, required/optional): This field is used to gather any additional information you would like to share with the workshop organizers. Please enter any additional notes or comments.

### 9. Confirm Consent (consent_confirmation)
* **Confirm Consent** (consent_confirmation, select_one, required/optional): This field is used to confirm your consent to participate in the workshop. Please select "True" to confirm your consent.

### 10. Confirm Email (email_confirmation)
* **Confirm Email** (email_confirmation, select_one, required/optional): This field is used to confirm your email address. Please select "True" to confirm your email address.

### 11. Confirm Phone (phone_confirmation)
* **Confirm Phone** (phone_confirmation, select_one, required/optional): This field is used to confirm your phone number. Please select "True" to confirm your phone number.

### 12. Confirm Workshop Date (date_confirmation)
* **Confirm Workshop Date** (date_confirmation, select_one, required/optional): This field is used to confirm the workshop date. Please select "True" to confirm the workshop date.

### 13. Confirm Workshop Time (time_confirmation)
* **Confirm Workshop Time** (time_confirmation, select_one, required/optional): This field is used to confirm the workshop time. Please select "True" to confirm the workshop time.

### 14. Email Address (email_address)
* **Email Address** (email_address, text, required/optional): This field is used to gather your email address. Please enter your correct email address.

### 15. Phone Number (phone_number)
* **Phone Number** (phone_number, text, required/optional): This field is used to gather your phone number. Please enter your correct phone number.

### 16. Number of Workshop Attendees (workshop_attendees)
* **Number of Workshop Attendees** (workshop_attendees, number, required/optional): This field is used to gather the number of workshop attendees. Please enter the number of attendees.

### 17. Attendee Names (attendee_names)
* **Attendee Names** (attendee_names, text, required/optional): This field is used to gather the names of workshop attendees. Please enter the names of the attendees.

### 18. Workshop Location (workshop_location)
* **Workshop Location** (workshop_location, text, required/optional): This field is used to gather the workshop location. Please enter the location of the workshop.

### 19. Emergency Contact (emergency_contact)
* **Emergency Contact** (emergency_contact, text, required/optional): This field is used to gather emergency contact information. Please enter the name and relationship of the emergency contact.

### 20. Emergency Relationship (emergency_relationship)
* **Emergency Relationship** (emergency_relationship, text, required/optional): This field is used to gather the relationship of the emergency contact. Please enter the relationship of the emergency contact.

### 21. Emergency Number (emergency_number)
* **Emergency Number** (emergency_number, text, required/optional): This field is used to gather the emergency contact number. Please enter the correct emergency contact number.

### 22. Emergency Address (emergency_address)
* **Emergency Address** (emergency_address, text, required/optional): This field is used to gather the address of the emergency contact. Please enter the address of the emergency contact.

### 23. Medical Conditions (medical_conditions)
* **Medical Conditions** (medical_conditions, text, required/optional): This field is used to gather any medical conditions or concerns. Please enter any medical conditions or concerns.

### 24. Medications (medications)
* **Medications** (medications, text, required/optional): This field is used to gather information about current medications. Please enter any current medications.

### 25. Medical History (medical_history)
* **Medical History** (medical_history, text, required/optional): This field is used to gather medical history. Please enter any medical history.
