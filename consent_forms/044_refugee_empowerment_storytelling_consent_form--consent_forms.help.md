# Refugee Empowerment Storytelling Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Refugee Empowerment Storytelling Consent Form is designed to collect information about your consent for the use and sharing of your refugee empowerment storytelling experiences. This form is essential to ensure that you are aware of and agree to the use and sharing of your stories.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your User Details, including any additional information you'd like to share.
2.  Choose whether or not you consent to storytelling.
3.  Select the media types you are comfortable with (Video, Audio, Images).
4.  Choose the purpose of the storytelling (Publication, Social media, Presentation).
5.  Indicate whether you want to share your story (True or False).
6.  Select the methods you'd like to use for sharing (Email, Social Media, Print).
7.  Choose who you'd like to share your story with (Family Members, Friends, Community).
8.  Indicate how frequently you'd like to share your story (Occasionally, Frequently, Rarely).
9.  Choose where you'd like to share your story (Online, Offline).
10.  Provide your contact details and contact number.
11.  Enter any other information you'd like to share.
12.  Upload any media files you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details** (User Details, text, required): Enter your name, email, and any other details you'd like to share.
* **Consent To Storytelling** (Consent To Storytelling, select_one, required): Choose whether or not you consent to storytelling.
* **Media Usage** (Media Usage, select_multiple, required): Select the media types you are comfortable with (Video, Audio, Images).
* **Story Usage** (Story Usage, select_multiple, required): Choose the purpose of the storytelling (Publication, Social media, Presentation).
* **Sharing** (Sharing, select_one, required): Indicate whether you want to share your story (True or False).
* **Sharing Methods** (Sharing Methods, select_multiple, required): Select the methods you'd like to use for sharing (Email, Social Media, Print).
* **Sharing With** (Sharing With, select_multiple, required): Choose who you'd like to share your story with (Family Members, Friends, Community).
* **Sharing Frequency** (Sharing Frequency, select_multiple, required): Indicate how frequently you'd like to share your story (Occasionally, Frequently, Rarely).
* **Sharing Location** (Sharing Location, select_multiple, required): Choose where you'd like to share your story (Online, Offline).
* **Contact Details** (Contact Details, text, required): Provide your contact details.
* **Contact Number** (Contact Number, number, required): Enter your contact number.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email.
* **Other Info** (Other Info, text, optional): Enter any other information you'd like to share.
* **Media Files** (Media Files, text, optional): Upload any media files you'd like to share.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, optional): Enter any additional notes you'd like to share.
