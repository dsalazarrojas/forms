# performance_lighting_test_recording_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from participants and record their consent for a performance lighting test. The form is designed to gather demographic and test details from participants. Please ensure that you have not already completed this form for the same participant or test.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with the requested information.
2. Make sure to include all required fields (indicated as required).
3. Review and submit the form after completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Details
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, `text`, required: false): This field is a placeholder for any additional participant details.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the participant.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, `date`, required: false): This field is for the participant's signature, likely a date or timestamp.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Date of Test** (`date_of_test`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the performance lighting test.
* **Time of Test** (`time_of_test`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the performance lighting test.
* **Test Duration** (`test_duration`, `number`, required: false): Enter the duration of the test.
* **Recorded By** (`recorded_by`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person recording the participant's test.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
- Review the form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness. 

Please note that some fields are not required, so you can leave them blank if not applicable.
