# Independent Film Promotion Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect personal and talent details, review the terms and conditions of the promotion, and obtain the individual's consent to participate in the independent film promotion.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in your personal details in the **Personal Details** section. This includes any relevant information that we need to know about you.
2. In the **Talent Details** section, provide any relevant details about your talent or skills that will be used in the promotion.
3. In the **Materials** section, list any materials you will be contributing to the promotion (e.g., images, videos, etc.).
4. In the **Consent** section, select **I have read and understand the above terms.** to indicate your agreement to the promotion terms.
5. Finally, review the **Terms and Conditions** section to ensure you are aware of the terms and conditions of the promotion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Personal Details
* **Personal Details** (`personal_details`, `text`, required): Please fill in your name, contact information, and any other relevant personal details that we need to know about you.

### Talent Details
* **Talent Details** (`talent_details_page`, `text`, required): Please describe your talent or skills that will be used in the promotion. This could include your acting, music, or other creative skills.

### Materials
* **Materials** (`materials_page`, `text`, required): List any materials you will be contributing to the promotion, such as images, videos, or other assets.

### Consent
* **Consent** (`consent_page`, `select_one`, required): Please select **I have read and understand the above terms.** to indicate your agreement to the promotion terms.

### Terms and Conditions
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions_page`, `note`, required): Please review this section to ensure you are aware of the terms and conditions of the promotion.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have read and understood all the terms and conditions before completing this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* This form is a binding agreement between you and the promotion team.
