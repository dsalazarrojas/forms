# Back To School Health Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about a student's health status and any potential risks associated with returning to school during a pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate to the relevant page and fill out the fields with accurate and up-to-date information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Student name (**Student name**, Text, Required)
Fill in the student's full name as it appears on their identification.

### 2. Parent or guardian name (**Parent or guardian name**, Text, Required)
Enter the full name of the parent or guardian completing this form.

### 3. Date of submission (**Date of submission**, Date, Required)
Enter the date this form was completed.

### 4. Recent illnesses in last 14 days (**Recent illnesses in last 14 days**, Text, Required)
List any recent illnesses or medical conditions experienced by the student in the past 14 days. Be as detailed as possible.

### 5. Recent COVID exposure (**Recent COVID exposure**, Select_one, Required)
Choose whether the student has had recent contact with a confirmed case of COVID-19, or whether you are unsure.

### 6. Vaccination status (**Vaccination status**, Text, Not Required)
If you wish to provide additional information about the student's vaccination status, you can do so here.

### 7. Chronic conditions (**Chronic health conditions**, Text, Not Required)
If the student has any chronic health conditions, such as asthma, diabetes, or heart conditions, list them here. This information may be relevant to the school's medical team.

### 8. Current medications (**Current medications**, Text, Not Required)
List any medications the student is currently taking, including dosages.

### 9. Consent for care (**Consent for school to provide basic care**, Select_one, Required)
Choose whether the school is authorized to provide basic care to the student, such as administering first aid and notifying parents.

### 10. Name of person signing (**Name of person signing**, Text, Required)
Enter the typed full name of the person signing this form.
