# Background Check Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain the authorization of the applicant to perform a background check. The form is designed to collect necessary information to verify the identity of the applicant, including their name, date of birth, and last four digits of their Social Security Number (SSN). Additionally, it requires the applicant's consent to perform a background check and provides a space for the applicant to sign their name indicating their consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full legal name.
2. Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YY format.
3. If you have an SSN, enter the last four digits. Please note that this field is optional.
4. Choose the position you applied for from the list.
5. Select "True" to indicate your consent to perform a background check.
6. Type your full name in the signature field, indicating that you have read and understood the form.
7. Enter the date you signed the form.
8. If you have a contact email, you can enter it for any updates or questions regarding the background check.
9. You can optionally enter your contact phone number for any further communications.
10. Read and understand the privacy notice at the bottom of the page, which explains how your data will be used and stored.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant name**: Full legal name of the applicant.
* **Date of birth**: Date of birth in MM/DD/YY format for identity verification.
* **Last four digits of SSN**: Last four digits of Social Security Number for verification purposes. Please note that this field is optional.
* **Position applied for**: The role for which the applicant is applying. This information will be used to determine the level of background check required.
* **Consent to background check**: Select "True" to indicate your consent to perform a background check. This is a required field.
* **Signature name**: Type your full name here, indicating that you have read and understood the form.
* **Date of consent**: The date you signed the form.
* **Contact email**: Enter your contact email for any updates or questions regarding the background check. This field is optional.
* **Contact phone**: Optional contact phone number for any further communications.
* **Privacy notice**: Read and understand how your data will be used and stored. This field is informational only.
