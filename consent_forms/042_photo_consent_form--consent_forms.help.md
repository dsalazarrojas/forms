# Photo Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Photo Consent Form is a document used to obtain consent from individuals for photo usage. It's designed to be completed by users to provide their personal details, photographer's information, photo details, and date and time of photo shoot. This form is typically used for various purposes such as events, advertising, or editorial content.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Start by filling out the User Info page**: Provide your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information.
2. Navigate to the Photographer page: Enter the photographer's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
3. Select photo details: Choose the type of photo shoot from the available options (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
4. Enter date and time: Select the date and time of the photo shoot.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Info (1)
* **User Info** (`user_info`, Text, Optional): This page is where you, the user, provide your personal details such as your name and email address.

### Photographer (2)
* **Photographer** (`photographer_info`, Text, Optional): Enter the name and contact information of the photographer who took your photos.

### Photo Details (3)
* **Photo Details** (`photo_details`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the type of photo shoot you underwent, such as Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3. This helps the photographer understand the context and purpose of the photo shoot.

### Date and Time (4)
* **Date and Time** (`date_and_time`, Time, Optional): Select the date and time of the photo shoot. This helps the photographer understand when the photo shoot occurred.
