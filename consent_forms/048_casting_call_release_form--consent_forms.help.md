# Casting Call Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from performers regarding their release of rights for use in a production. It ensures that all necessary information is captured for the production team and the performer to understand their involvement and agreements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the production title, company, and date information.
2. Enter your name, date of birth, address, phone number, and email address.
3. Specify the role you played in the production and approximate filming dates.
4. Choose which distribution methods you permit for the production.
5. Confirm that you understand and agree to the terms of the release.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Release Date**: Enter the date you are releasing your rights for the production.
* **Production Title**: Enter the name of the film, show, or project you are working on.
* **Production Company or Studio Name**: Enter the name of the production company or studio responsible for the project.
* **Performer Name**: Enter your legal or professional name as it should be used in the production.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Address**: Enter your address to be used in production credits or other promotional materials.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for production contact.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address to be used for production contact.
* **Role Played**: Specify the character name or description you played in the production.
* **Filming Dates**: Enter the approximate or specific dates you filmed.
* **Filming Locations**: Enter the locations where filming took place.
* **Grant rights to use your likeness and image**: Choose "Yes, I grant these rights" to release your likeness and image for use in the production.
* **Grant rights to use your voice**: Choose "Yes, I grant these rights" to release your voice for use in the production.
* **Grant rights to use your performance**: Choose "Yes, I grant these rights" to release your performance for use in the production.
* **Permitted Distribution Methods**: Select all applicable methods of distribution for the production (e.g. Theatrical Release, Television Broadcast, etc.).
* **Compensation Received or Expected**: Enter any compensation you received or expect for your role in the production.
* **Screen Credit Requested**: Choose "Yes, requested" if you would like a screen credit in the production.
* **I confirm I understand and agree to this release**: Choose "Yes, I confirm" to confirm your understanding and agreement to the terms of the release.
