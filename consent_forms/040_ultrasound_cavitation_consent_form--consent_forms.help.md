# Ultrasound Cavitation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ultrasound Cavitation Consent Form is a medical form used to obtain consent from patients for ultrasound cavitation procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and fill out the required fields.
2. Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and honestly.
3. If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to ask for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Patient Info (Page 1)
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Medical Facility** (`medical_facility`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the medical facility where the procedure is being performed.
* **Medical Department** (`medical_department`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the medical department where the procedure is being performed.
* **Medical Provider** (`medical_provider`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the medical provider performing the procedure.

### Ultrasound Cavitation Info (Page 2)
* **Ultrasound Cavitation Info** (`ultrasound_cavitation_info`, number, required/optional): Enter the details of the ultrasound cavitation procedure, including the type and frequency.
* **Allergy Info** (`allergy_info`, select_multiple, required/optional): If the patient has any allergies, list them here. 
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief history of the patient's medical conditions and previous treatments.

### Consent (Page 3)
* **Consent** (`consent`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief statement of understanding and agreement to the procedure.
* **Form Completion Date** (`form_completion_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date on which the form is completed.
* **Signed** (`signed_at`, date, required/optional): Enter the date on which the form is signed by the patient or their representative.

### Signature Page
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required/optional): Sign and date the form to indicate understanding and agreement to the procedure.

### Additional Fields
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the doctor performing the procedure.
* **Doctor Email** (`doctor_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the doctor's email address.
* **Doctor Phone** (`doctor_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the doctor's phone number.

Please note that this form may require additional fields or pages based on the specific needs and regulations of your medical facility. Always consult with a medical professional before filling out this form.
