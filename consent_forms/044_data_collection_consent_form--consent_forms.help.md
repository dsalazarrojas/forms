# Data Collection Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Data Collection Consent Form is designed to collect sensitive information from users, including contact details, client information, and consent for data collection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
2. Provide client information, if applicable.
3. Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
4. Enter your date and time of birth, if applicable (HH:MM AM/PM).
5. Read and agree to the terms of consent, which outlines how your data will be collected, stored, and used.
6. Provide your contact address, if applicable.
7. Specify the purpose of data collection.
8. Select the method of data collection (phone, mail, or online).
9. Read and understand the data sharing, security, retention, deletion, modification, and use policies related to your data.
10. Review and agree to the terms of consent again, as outlined in the privacy policy link provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **contact_information** (`text`, required): Enter your name.
* **client_information** (`text`, required): Enter any relevant client information, such as company name or department.
* **contact_number** (`text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **email** (`email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **date_of_birth** (`date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **date_of_birth_hhmm** (`date`, optional): Enter your date and time of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **time_of_birth** (`time`, optional): Enter your time of birth in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **consent** (`text`, required): Read and agree to the terms of consent, which outlines how your data will be collected, stored, and used.
* **agreement** (`text`, required): Provide your agreement to the terms of consent.
* **contact_address** (`text`, optional): Enter your contact address, if applicable.
* **data_collection_purpose** (`text`, required): Specify the purpose of data collection.
* **data_collection_method** (`select_one`, required): Select the method of data collection (phone, mail, or online).
* **privacy_policy_link** (`text`, required): Read and understand the data sharing, security, retention, deletion, modification, and use policies related to your data.
* **data_sharing** (`text`, required): Understand how your data will be shared.
* **data_security** (`text`, required): Understand how your data will be secured.
* **data_retention** (`text`, required): Understand how your data will be retained.
* **data_deletion** (`text`, required): Understand how your data will be deleted.
* **data_modification** (`text`, required): Understand how your data will be modified.
* **data_use** (`text`, required): Understand how your data will be used.
* **contact_information_2** (`text`, optional): Enter any additional contact information, if required.
