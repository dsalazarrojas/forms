# Extracurricular Activities Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Extracurricular Activities Consent Form is used to obtain consent for students to participate in extracurricular activities. It is used to gather information about the student's activities, availability, and parent/guardian consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please fill in the form with the required information for the student participating in the extracurricular activities.
2. For each question, ensure that you answer truthfully and accurately.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to your school administrator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Extracurricular Activities Consent Form 1 (Page 1)**: This is the first page of the form where you can fill in the student's information.
* **Extracurricular Activities Consent Form 2 (Page 2)**: This page is for filling in the student's extracurricular activities details.
* **Parent Signature**: This is where the parent or guardian signs to provide their consent for the student to participate in extracurricular activities.
* **School Name**: Please fill in the school name where the student is enrolled.
* **Activities**: List the extracurricular activities the student is participating in.
* **Consent (Yes/No)**: This question is to confirm if the parent or guardian has given their consent for the student to participate in the activities.
* **Consent (Yes/No) 2**: This is another question to confirm if the parent or guardian has given their consent for the student to participate in the activities.
* **Time**: This field is not currently used, but it may be used in the future for scheduling purposes.
* **Date**: This field is not currently used, but it may be used in the future for record-keeping purposes.
* **Note**: This is a text field for any additional comments or notes about the student's participation.
* **Email**: This field is not currently used, but it may be used in the future for communication purposes.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have read and understood the purpose of this form before proceeding.
* If you are unsure about any information, please do not hesitate to reach out to your school administrator.
* This form is designed to be completed in conjunction with other relevant forms and documents.
