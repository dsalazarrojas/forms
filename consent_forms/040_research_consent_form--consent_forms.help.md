# Research Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain research consent from participants in a study. The form captures essential information about the participant, such as their name, email, and phone number. It serves as an essential part of the research process, ensuring that participants understand their rights and responsibilities before contributing to any research activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant name in the "participant_name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "participant_email" field.
3. If you have a phone number, please enter it in the "participant_phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Research Consent Form** (`research_consent_form`, text, **required**): This is the title of the form, which should not be filled in. It's a placeholder to identify the form.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, **optional**): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, **required**): This is the email address you use to communicate with researchers and receive updates about the study.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, **optional**): If you have a phone number, please enter it to help researchers contact you for any reason.
