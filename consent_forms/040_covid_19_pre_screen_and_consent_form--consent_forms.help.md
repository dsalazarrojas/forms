# COVID 19 Pre Screen and Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The COVID-19 Pre Screen and Consent Form is a required form for initial screening and assessment of individuals for COVID-19 risk factors. This form is used to gather accurate and truthful information from users to ensure effective risk assessment and provide necessary COVID-19 services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2. Ensure that you answer all questions truthfully.
3. If you have any pre-existing medical conditions, please describe them in the text box provided.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Full Name (1)
* Full Name is the primary identifier for your profile.
* Please enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Required**

### Date of Birth (3)
* Date of Birth is used to determine your age and calculate your risk level for COVID-19.
* Please enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

### Email Address (4)
* Email Address is used to contact you with screening results and further guidance.
* Please enter a valid email address that you have access to.

### Phone Number (5)
* Phone Number is used to contact you with screening results and further guidance.
* Please enter a valid phone number that you have access to.

### Address (6)
* Address is used for contact and follow-up purposes.
* This field is optional, but please ensure that you fill it out if you want to be contacted at your home or work address.

### Do you have fever or chills? (8)
* This question is asked to assess your current health status.
* Please choose 'True' if you have experienced fever or chills in the past 24 hours.

### Do you have a cough? (9)
* This question is asked to assess your respiratory health status.
* Please choose 'True' if you have experienced a cough in the past 24 hours.

### Do you have shortness of breath or difficulty breathing? (10)
* This question is asked to assess your respiratory health status.
* Please choose 'True' if you have experienced shortness of breath or difficulty breathing in the past 24 hours.

### Have you lost your sense of taste or smell? (11)
* This question is asked to assess your sense of taste and smell status.
* Please choose 'True' if you have experienced loss of taste or smell.

### Other COVID-19 symptoms (12)
* This question is asked to gather additional symptoms you may be experiencing.
* If you experience any other symptoms, please describe them here.

### Have you tested positive for COVID-19 in the past 14 days? (14)
* This question is asked to assess your COVID-19 infection status.
* Please choose 'True' if you have tested positive for COVID-19.

### Have you been in close contact with a confirmed COVID-19 case in the past 14 days? (15)
* This question is asked to assess your exposure risk.
* Please choose 'True' if you have been in close contact with a confirmed COVID-19 case.

### Have you traveled to a high-risk area recently? (16)
* This question is asked to assess your travel history.
* Please choose 'True' if you have traveled to a high-risk area.

### Do you have chronic health conditions? (18)
* This question is asked to assess your pre-existing health conditions.
* Please choose 'True' if you have chronic health conditions.

### If yes, describe your chronic health conditions (19)
* If you have chronic health conditions, please describe them here.

### Are you immunocompromised? (20)
* This question is asked to assess your immunocompromised status.
* Please choose 'True' if you are immunocompromised or unsure.

### I have answered all questions truthfully and accurately (22)
* This question is asked to ensure that you have completed the form truthfully.
* Please choose 'True' if you have completed the form accurately.

### I understand the screening and procedures (23)
* This question is asked to ensure that you understand the COVID-19 screening and procedures.
* Please choose 'True' if you understand the procedures.

### I consent to receive COVID-19 screening and services (24)
* This question is asked to obtain your consent.
* Please choose 'I consent' if you agree to receive COVID-19 screening and services.

### Date of Consent (25)
* Date of Consent is the date when you completed the form.
* Please enter the date you completed the form in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

Please ensure that you fill out the form accurately and truthfully. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask.
