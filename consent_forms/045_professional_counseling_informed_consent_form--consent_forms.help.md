# Professional Counseling Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from clients regarding their personal details, consent for counseling services, and other relevant particulars. This form ensures clients are aware of the scope of services, potential risks involved, and payment terms before starting therapy sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your personal details in the "Client Information" field.
2. Indicate your consent for counseling services by selecting one or more options in the "Consent" field.
3. If applicable, enter any risks or concerns you have regarding the counseling services in the "Risks Involved" field.
4. Specify any limitations you have in mind for the counseling services in the "Limitations" field.
5. Select one or more services you would like to receive from our counseling services in the "Services" field.
6. Choose one or more payment terms that suit your needs in the "Payment Terms" field.
7. If you have any concerns or questions, please enter them in the "Client Concerns" field.
8. If the therapist has any concerns, please enter them in the "Therapist Concerns" field.
9. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" field.
10. Finally, sign the "Client Signature" field to indicate your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information** (`client_info`, text, required: false): Enter your personal details such as name, address, and contact information in this field.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or more options to indicate your consent for counseling services. Possible options are: True, False.
* **Risks Involved** (`risks`, text, required: false): If you have any risks or concerns regarding the counseling services, enter them in this field.
* **Limitations** (`limitations`, text, required: false): Specify any limitations you have in mind for the counseling services.
* **Services** (`services`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or more services you would like to receive from our counseling services. Possible options are: Individual Counseling, Group Counseling, Other.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose one or more payment terms that suit your needs. Possible options are: Pre-Session Payment, Session-by-Session, Package Plan.
* **Client Concerns** (`client_concerns`, text, required: false): If you have any concerns or questions, enter them in this field.
* **Therapist Concerns** (`therapist_concerns`, text, required: false): If the therapist has any concerns, enter them in this field.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter your contact information such as email and phone number.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, note, required: false): Sign and date this field to indicate your consent.
* **Therapist Signature** (`therapist_signature`, note, required: false): Sign and date this field to confirm your understanding of the services and client's consent.
