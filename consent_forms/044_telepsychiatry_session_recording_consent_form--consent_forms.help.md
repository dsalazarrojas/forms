# Telepsychiatry Session Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain consent from clients for telepsychiatry sessions, ensuring they are aware of and agree to the recording of the session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the relevant options for each field. You can select multiple options for the "Consent Form" field. For other fields, select the corresponding option or enter the required information as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Consent Form (`id: 1, name: consent_form`)
* A multiple-choice field that asks the client to confirm they agree to be recorded during the session.
  - Select "Yes" to indicate agreement to being recorded.
  - Select "No" if you are not comfortable with recording.

### 2. Session Date (`id: 2, name: session_date`)
* Enter the date of the scheduled telepsychiatry session.

### 3. Session Time (`id: 3, name: session_time`)
* Enter the time of the scheduled telepsychiatry session.

### 4. Client Name (`id: 4, name: client_name`)
* Enter the client's name as it appears on their identification.

### 5. Client Email (`id: 5, name: client_email`)
* Enter the client's email address.

### 6. Phone Number (`id: 6, name: phone_number`)
* Enter the client's phone number.

### 7. Recording Agreement (`id: 7, name: recording_agreement`)
* This field is for additional notes or comments about the recording agreement.

### 8. Consent to Record (`id: 8, name: consent_to_record`)
* Select "Client agrees to be recorded" to indicate the client's consent to being recorded during the session.
  - Select "Client does not agree to be recorded" if the client has declined.

### 9. Consent to Share (`id: 9, name: consent_to_share`)
* Select "True" to indicate that the session will be shared with others.
  - Select "False" if the session is not to be shared.

### 10. Recording Location (`id: 10, name: recording_location`)
* Select "Remote" if the session will be conducted remotely.
  - Select "In-person" if the session is conducted in-person.

### 11. Session Length (`id: 11, name: session_length`)
* Select the length of the session: 15 minutes, 30 minutes, or 60 minutes.

### 12. Recording Device (`id: 12, name: recording_device`)
* Select "Webcam" if the recording will be done using a webcam.
  - Select "Phone" if the recording will be done using a phone.

### 13. Recording Quality (`id: 13, name: recording_quality`)
* Select "High" for high-quality recording.
  - Select "Medium" for medium-quality recording.
  - Select "Low" for low-quality recording.

### 14. Additional Comments (`id: 14, name: additional_comments`)
* Enter any additional comments or notes about the session.

### 15. Client Signature (`id: 15, name: client_signature`)
* Sign your name to confirm your agreement to the recording terms.

### 16. Client Name Signature (`id: 16, name: client_name_signature`)
* Enter your name as it appears on your identification.

### 17. Healthcare Provider Signature (`id: 17, name: healthcare_provider_signature`)
* Sign your name as the healthcare provider.

### 18. Healthcare Provider Name (`id: 18, name: healthcare_provider_name`)
* Enter your name as it appears on your identification.

### 19. Date Signed (`id: 19, name: date_signed`)
* Enter the date you signed the form.

### 20. Time Signed (`id: 20, name: time_signed`)
* Enter the time you signed the form.

### 21. Signature Date (`id: 21, name: signature_date`)
* Enter the date you signed as the healthcare provider.

### 22. Signature Time (`id: 22, name: signature_time`)
* Enter the time you signed as the healthcare provider.

### 23. Recorded By (`id: 23, name: recorded_by`)
* Enter the name of the person who recorded the session.

### 24. Recorded At (`id: 24, name: recorded_at`)
* Enter the date and time the session was recorded.

### 25. Recorded By 2 (`id: 25, name: recorded_by_2`)
* Enter the name of the person who recorded the session.

## Tips
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
* Ensure that you have signed the form before the session begins.
* Keep the form for your records and reference as needed.
