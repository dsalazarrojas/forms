# Diet And Nutrition Assessment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a crucial step in our diet and nutrition assessment process. It helps us understand your consent to proceed, any food allergies or restrictions you may have, and your nutritional needs to provide personalized guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read each field description to understand what information is requested.
2. Fill out all required fields (marked as required).
3. Select your responses from the provided options.
4. Be truthful and accurate in your responses.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Consent Form** (`consent_form`, `text`, required/optional: `true`): This field is where you give your consent to proceed with the diet and nutrition assessment. Please type yes if you agree to proceed with the assessment.
* **Client Consent** (`client_consent`, `text`, required/optional: `true`): This field is where you confirm your consent to participate in the diet and nutrition assessment.
* **Dietary Preferences** (`dietary_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required/optional: `false`): This field is where you can select your dietary preferences (yes/no) for any of the options listed.
* **Food Allergies** (`food_allergies`, `select_one`, required/optional: `true`): This field is where you select 'yes' if you have any food allergies.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, `text`, required/optional: `false`): This field is where you can provide any dietary restrictions you may have.
* **Nutritional Needs** (`nutritional_needs`, `text`, required/optional: `true`): This field is where you can describe your nutritional needs.
* **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`, `text`, required/optional: `false`): This field is where you can provide information about your healthcare provider.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, `note`, required/optional: `false`): This field is where you sign and date the form to confirm your agreement.

## Tips
* Please answer all required fields truthfully.
* Select all that apply for dietary preferences.
* If you have any dietary restrictions, please provide as much detail as possible.
* If you have any concerns or questions, do not hesitate to reach out to us.
