# travel_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The travel declaration form is used to collect information on an individual's travel history and potential health risks associated with their travel. This form helps assess and manage health risks related to travel.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Be honest about your travel history and symptoms.
3. Select the relevant options for each field based on your actual travel experience and health.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Page 1: Travel Declaration Form Page**
	+ **Travel Declaration Form Page**: This is the main page of the form.
* **Travel Declaration Form Questions**: Provide a brief description of your recent travel.
* **Travel Declaration Form Symptoms**: Select any symptoms you have experienced recently, such as Fever, Cough, Difficulty breathing, or Sore throat.
* **Travel Declaration Form Symptom Frequency**: Enter the frequency of your symptoms, e.g., how often you have experienced them.
* **Travel Declaration Form Temperature**: Enter your current temperature, if applicable.
* **Travel Declaration Form Travel Status**: Select your travel status:
	+ Arriving from a high-risk area: If you've traveled from an area with high health risks.
	+ Traveling to a high-risk area: If you're planning to travel to an area with high health risks.
	+ Neither of the above: If you don't fit into the above categories.
* **Travel Declaration Form Travel History**: Provide any relevant travel history, e.g., countries visited, dates of travel.
* **Travel Declaration Form Declaration**: Enter a brief statement of your travel declaration.
* **Travel Declaration Form Declaration Status**: Select your declaration status:
	+ Confirmed: If your declaration has been confirmed.
	+ Declined: If your declaration has been declined.
	+ Unknown: If your declaration status is unknown.
* **Travel Declaration Form Declaration Date**: Enter the date of your travel declaration.
* **Travel Declaration Form Declaration Time**: Enter the time of your travel declaration.
* **Travel Declaration Form Declaration Notes**: Add any additional notes about your travel declaration.
* **Travel Declaration Form Declaration Email**: Enter your contact email.
* **Travel Declaration Form Declaration Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask for help.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
