# Noah Bennett Photography Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Noah Bennett Photography Form is used to capture information related to photography sessions for both the client and the photography business.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user information, including your name and any other relevant details.
2. Read and agree to the terms of service to ensure you understand and accept the terms before proceeding with the session.
3. Enter the date and time of your session.
4. Specify the photographer and assistant names (if applicable).
5. Enter any relevant details about the location of the session.
6. Add any notes for the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_information`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other relevant details.
* **Client Agreement** (`client_agreement`, text, required): Read and agree to the terms of service to ensure you understand and accept the terms before proceeding with the session.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your session.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your session.
* **Photographer** (`photographer`, text, optional): Enter the name of the photographer.
* **Assistant** (`assistant`, text, optional): Enter the name of the assistant (if applicable).
* **Assistant Phone** (`assistant_phone`, text, optional): Enter the assistant's phone number (if applicable).
* **Assistant Email** (`assistant_email`, email, optional): Enter the assistant's email (if applicable).
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location of your session.
* **Photographer Notes** (`photographer_notes`, note, optional): Add any notes for the photographer.
* **Client Notes** (`client_notes`, note, optional): Add any notes for the client.
* **Assistant Notes** (`assistant_notes`, note, optional): Add any notes for the assistant.
* **Assistant Agreement** (`assistant_agreement`, text, optional): This field is not currently required; however, it is intended for assistants to agree on terms of service. 

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the photographer or assistant for clarification.
* This form is for internal use only, please do not share with others.
