# photography_portfolio_review_permission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "photography_portfolio_review_permission_form" is a form used to collect information from photographers to review their work and grant permission for it to be used in various contexts, such as exhibitions, publications, or events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your name and email address**: Fill in your name and email address in the respective fields.
2. Ensure that your email address is accurate and up-to-date, as it will be used for communication and updates.
3. Select the **Permission Type**: Choose the type of permission you wish to grant, such as "Review" or "Approve and Review".
4. **Add comments (optional)**: If you have any additional comments or notes, you can provide them in this field.
5. **Confirm your agreement**: Select the number of days you agree to be reviewed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Photographers Name**: Your name should be entered here, which will be used to address communication and updates to you.
* **Photographers Email**: Enter your email address here, which will be used for official communication.
* **Permission Type**: Choose the type of permission you wish to grant:
	+ **Review**: Grant permission for your work to be reviewed and potentially used in exhibitions, publications, or events.
	+ **Approve and Review**: Grant permission for your work to be reviewed and approved for use in exhibitions, publications, or events.
	+ **Approve**: Grant permission for your work to be used in exhibitions, publications, or events without review.
	+ **Not Approve**: Decline permission for your work to be used in exhibitions, publications, or events.
* **Comments**: If you have any comments or notes, you can provide them here. This is an optional field and does not affect the permission type.
* **I Agree to be reviewed**: Confirm that you agree to be reviewed for a specified number of days.
