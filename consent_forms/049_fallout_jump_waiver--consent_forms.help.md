# Fallout Jump Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fallout Jump Waiver form is a vital document used to gather essential information and ensure that jumpers, instructors, and parents/guardians are aware of their roles and responsibilities before participating in the jump activity.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Skydive Date**: Fill in the date when you plan to participate in the jump activity. Please use the date format as instructed.
2. **Jumper**: Enter your name as the person participating in the jump activity.
3. **Parent/Guardian Name**: If you are under 18, enter your parent/guardian's name.
4. **Emergency Contact**: Provide the name and contact information of a person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
5. **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
6. **Prophet's Name**: This field is not applicable for this guide.
7. **Jumper Signature**: Sign your name to confirm that you have read and understood the terms and conditions.
8. **Instructor Signature**: Sign your name as the instructor or person responsible for the jump activity.
9. **Jumpmaster Signature**: Sign your name as the jumpmaster or person in charge of the jump activity.
10. **Date Completed**: Fill in the date when you completed this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Skydive Date**: A date when you plan to participate in the jump activity.
* **Jumper**: Your name as the person participating in the jump activity.
* **Parent/Guardian Name**: Your parent/guardian's name (if you are under 18).
* **Emergency Contact**: A person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Phone Number**: The contact number of the emergency contact.
* **Prophet's Name**: Not applicable for this guide.
* **Jumper Signature**: Your signature confirming that you have read and understood the terms and conditions.
* **Instructor Signature**: The instructor's signature confirming their role and responsibilities.
* **Jumpmaster Signature**: The jumpmaster's signature confirming their role and responsibilities.
* **Date Completed**: The date when you completed this form.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have not completed this form previously.
* Read and understand the terms and conditions before signing the form.
* Fill in accurate and truthful information.
* Do not hesitate to contact the relevant authorities if you have any doubts or concerns.
