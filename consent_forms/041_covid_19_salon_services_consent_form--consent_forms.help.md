# Covid 19 Salon Services Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your personal and health details, as well as the purpose of your salon visit, in order to ensure a safe and healthy experience for both you and our employees. Please fill out this form carefully and honestly to help us provide the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the requested information. If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask a staff member for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, optional): If you would like to provide your date of birth for any medical or administrative purposes, please enter it here.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Please enter your contact phone number.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Confirm Email** (Confirm Email, email, required): Please re-enter your email address to help us verify your identity.
* **Consent** (Consent, note, optional): If you have any medical conditions or concerns that may affect your salon visit, please provide a note explaining them here.
* **Symptoms** (Symptoms, select multiple, required): If you are experiencing any of the following symptoms, please select them:
	+ Fever
	+ Cough
	+ Shortness of breath
	+ Sore Throat
	+ Runny nose/congestion
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Travel History** (Travel History, select one, required): Have you traveled recently or plan to travel soon?
	+ True
	+ False
* **Reason for Travel** (Reason for Travel, text, optional): If you answered "True" to the previous question, please provide the reason for your travel here.
* **Exposure** (Exposure, select multiple, required): If you have been exposed to anyone with COVID-19, please select the relevant circumstances:
	+ Close contact with someone with COVID-19
	+ Shared living space with someone with COVID-19
	+ Attended a crowded event
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Exposure Details** (Exposure Details, text, optional): If you selected "Close contact" or "Attended a crowded event", please provide more details about your exposure here.
* **Vaccination Status** (Vaccination Status, select one, required): Are you fully vaccinated, partially vaccinated, or not vaccinated?
	+ Fully Vaccinated
	+ Partially Vaccinated
	+ Not Vaccinated
* **Vaccination Details** (Vaccination Details, text, optional): If you answered "Partially vaccinated" or "Not vaccinated", please provide more information about your vaccination status here.
* **Health Status** (Health Status, select one, required): What is your current health status?
	+ Healthy
	+ Has symptoms of COVID-19
	+ Has COVID-19
	+ Has died of COVID-19
* **Health Details** (Health Details, text, optional): If you answered "Has symptoms of COVID-19" or "Has COVID-19", please provide more information about your health status here.
* **Salon Visit** (Salon Visit, select one, required): Do you have a scheduled salon visit?
	+ True
	+ False
* **Reason for Salon Visit** (Reason for Salon Visit, text, optional): If you answered "True", please provide the reason for your salon visit here.
* **Service Provided** (Service Provided, select multiple, required): Which services do you wish to receive?
	+ Haircut
	+ Color
	+ Styling
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Service Details** (Service Details, text, optional): If you selected "Other" as your service, please provide more information about the service here.
* **Appointment Time** (Appointment Time, time, optional): If you have a scheduled appointment time, please provide it here.
* **Appointment Date** (Appointment Date, date, optional): If you have a scheduled appointment date, please provide it here.
* **Employee Name** (Employee Name, text, optional): If you have been assigned a specific employee for your appointment, please provide their name here.
* **Employee Email** (Employee Email, email, required): Please enter your employee's email address.
* **Employee Phone** (Employee Phone, text, required): Please enter your employee's phone number.

Note: This form is used for internal purposes only. Please ensure that you fill out the form accurately and honestly to help us provide the best possible service for you.
