# Shelter Resident Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shelter Resident Participation Consent Form is a document that requires consent from shelter residents to participate in various activities, programs, and services within the shelter.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the resident details section with the resident's information.
2. List the names and contact information of the resident's emergency contacts.
3. Select the activities and programs the resident is interested in participating in.
4. Sign and date the consent section to confirm the resident's participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Resident Details**: This section requires you to fill out the resident's name, emergency contacts, and other relevant information.
* **Emergency Contact 1**: Provide the name and contact information of the resident's primary emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact 1 Relationship**: Specify the relationship between the resident and their emergency contact (e.g., parent, guardian, spouse).
* **Emergency Contact 1 Phone**: Enter the phone number of the resident's emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact 1 Email**: Enter the email address of the resident's emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact 2**: Provide the name and contact information of the resident's secondary emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact 2 Relationship**: Specify the relationship between the resident and their secondary emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact 2 Phone**: Enter the phone number of the resident's emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact 2 Email**: Enter the email address of the resident's emergency contact.
* **Activities and Programs**: Select the activities and programs the resident is interested in participating in.
* **Activities and Programs List**: This field will display the selected activities and programs.
* **Resident Signature**: This is where the resident signs the consent form.
* **Consent Date**: Enter the date the resident signed the consent form.
* **Shelter Resident Participation Consent Signed**: This is the section where the resident signs to confirm their participation.
* **Shelter Resident Participation Consent Form Signed Date**: Enter the date the resident signed the consent form.
* **Assigned Tool**: This field is reserved for future use and is not currently required.
