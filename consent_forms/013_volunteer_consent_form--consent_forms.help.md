# volunteer_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The volunteer consent form is used to gather and store information about volunteers, including their role details, work schedules, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Volunteer Information**: Enter your name and any additional information about yourself as a volunteer.
2. **Role and Responsibilities**: Select the roles and responsibilities you're currently involved in or have been involved in. You can select multiple options.
3. **Volunteer Status**: Choose your current volunteer status: Active or Inactive.
4. **Role ID**: Select whether you have a Role ID.
5. **Role Name**: Select whether you have a Role Name.
6. **Role Start Date**: Enter the date when your role started.
7. **Role End Date**: Enter the date when your role ended, if applicable.
8. **Shift Start Time**: Enter the time when your shifts typically start.
9. **Shift End Time**: Enter the time when your shifts typically end.
10. **Role Hours**: Select whether you have worked a certain number of hours.
11. **Work Location**: Select the location where you've worked.
12. **Volunteer Type**: Select the type of volunteer you are (e.g., volunteer, staff).
13. **Shift Frequency**: Select how often you work shifts (e.g., daily, weekly).
14. **Volunteer Status Notes**: Add any notes about your volunteer status.
15. **Role ID Notes**: Add any notes about your Role ID.
16. **Role Name Notes**: Add any notes about your Role Name.
17. **Role Start Date Notes**: Add any notes about your Role Start Date.
18. **Role End Date Notes**: Add any notes about your Role End Date.
19. **Shift Start Time Notes**: Add any notes about your Shift Start Time.
20. **Shift End Time Notes**: Add any notes about your Shift End Time.
21. **Role Hours Notes**: Add any notes about your Role Hours.
22. **Work Location Notes**: Add any notes about your Work Location.
23. **Volunteer Type Notes**: Add any notes about your Volunteer Type.
24. **Shift Frequency Notes**: Add any notes about your Shift Frequency.
