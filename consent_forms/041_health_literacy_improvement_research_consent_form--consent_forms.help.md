# Health Literacy Improvement Research Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Health Literacy Improvement Research Consent Form is designed to gather information from participants about their interest in improving their health literacy, and to obtain their consent for research participation. This form is used to assess participants' understanding of health-related terms and concepts, and to ensure that they are aware of the risks and benefits associated with the research.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each page carefully and understand the questions and instructions.
2. Answer all the questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Check the boxes next to the options that apply to you.
4. Sign and date the form to indicate your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Health Literacy Improvement Research**: Please choose one or both of the options that apply to your interest in improving your health literacy:
	+ 'Yes' - You are interested in improving your health literacy and are willing to participate in research related to this topic.
	+ 'No' - You are not interested in improving your health literacy at this time.
* **Participant Information Sheet**: Please read this page carefully and ensure you understand the research study and its objectives. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask.
* **Researcher Signature**: This is the space for the researcher to sign and date the form to confirm that they have informed you about the research and its associated risks and benefits.
* **Participant Signature**: This is the space for you to sign and date the form to indicate your consent to participate in the research.
* **Researcher Identification**: Please provide your name and contact information so that the researcher can contact you to discuss your participation in further detail.

## Tips
* Please take your time to read each page carefully and ask questions if you are unsure about anything.
* Make sure to sign and date the form to indicate your consent.
* If you have any concerns or questions, do not hesitate to ask your researcher.

Note: The form is divided into four pages: Health Literacy Improvement Research, Participant Information Sheet, Researcher Signature, and Participant Signature, and Researcher Identification.
