# Investment Statement Communication Consent - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from clients regarding their investment statement communication preferences. The purpose of this form is to obtain their consent for communication regarding their investment statements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Client Information (Page 1)**: Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant information.
2. **Client Signature (Page 2)**: Select the type of information you want to receive regarding your investment statements, which is represented by "Client Name" or "Signature".
3. **Advisor Information (Page 3)**: Enter the name and contact information of your advisor.
4. **Communication Preferences (Page 4)**: Select the methods you prefer to receive communication from your advisor, such as Email, Phone, or SMS.
5. **Frequency of Contact (Page 5)**: Select how frequently you want to receive communication from your advisor, which is Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
6. **Time of Day (Page 6)**: Select the time of day you prefer to receive communication from your advisor. (Note: This field is not required.)
7. **Note (Page 7)**: Enter any additional comments or instructions for your advisor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (Page 1)**:
	+ **Name**: Enter your name.
	+ **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
* **Client Signature (Page 2)**:
	+ **Client Name**: Select this option to receive information regarding your name.
	+ **Signature**: Select this option to receive information regarding your signature.
* **Advisor Information (Page 3)**:
	+ **Advisor Name**: Enter your advisor's name.
	+ **Advisor Email**: Enter your advisor's email address.
	+ **Advisor Phone**: Enter your advisor's phone number.
* **Communication Preferences (Page 4)**:
	+ **Email**: Select this option to receive communication via email.
	+ **Phone**: Select this option to receive communication via phone.
	+ **SMS**: Select this option to receive communication via SMS.
* **Frequency of Contact (Page 5)**:
	+ **Daily**: Select this option to receive communication daily.
	+ **Weekly**: Select this option to receive communication weekly.
	+ **Monthly**: Select this option to receive communication monthly.
* **Time of Day (Page 6)**: 
	+ Select the time of day you prefer to receive communication from your advisor. (Note: This field is not required.)
* **Note (Page 7)**:
	+ Enter any additional comments or instructions for your advisor.
