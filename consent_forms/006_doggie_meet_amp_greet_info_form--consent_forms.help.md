# Doggie Meet & Greet Info Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Doggie Meet & Greet Info Form is intended for internal use to collect information from users for a meet-and-greet event.
## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

* Read the form carefully and ensure you are providing accurate and complete information.
* Fill out the form with your contact information and pet details.
* Review your responses before submitting.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meet And Greet Info** (<type:text>):
	+ This is the first field of the form where you can enter any relevant meet-and-greet information.
* **Pet Name** (<type:text>):
	+ Enter your pet's name here.
* **Contact Info** (<type:text>):
	+ Provide your contact information, such as your name and address.
* **Phone** (<type:text>):
	+ Enter your phone number so we can reach you.
* **Email** (<type:email>):
	+ Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **Pet Species** (<type:select_one>):
	+ Select the species of your pet (Dog, Cat, or Other).
* **Pet Size** (<type:select_multiple>):
	+ Choose the size of your pet (Small, Medium, Large).
* **Meet Time** (<type:time>):
	+ Enter the time you will be available to meet.

### Tips
* Ensure you have filled out all the required fields correctly.
* Double-check your information for accuracy and completeness.
