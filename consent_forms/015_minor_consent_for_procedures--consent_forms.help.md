# minor_consent_for_procedures - Help Guide
## Purpose
The minor_consent_for_procedures form is designed to obtain consent from parents or guardians for medical or dental procedures for minors. It is used to collect necessary information from the parent or guardian, ensuring they are aware of and agree to the procedures that will be performed on their child.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the parent's name in the "Parent's Name" field.
2. Select the procedure type from the "Consent Details" field.
3. Select the school/camp/youth program the child is associated with from the "School/Camp/Youth Program" field (if applicable).
4. Enter the date the procedure is scheduled to take place in the "Date of Procedure" field.
5. Enter a brief description of the procedure in the "Procedure Description" field.
6. Sign and date the form as the parent/guardian in the "Parent Signature", "Guardian Signature", and "Date of Signing" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent's Name** (`id: 2`, `type: text`, required): Enter the name of the parent or guardian as it appears on their identification.
* **Consent Details** (`id: 4`, `type: select_one`, required): Select the type of procedure for which you are providing consent, such as "Dental", "Medical", or "Emergency".
* **School/Camp/Youth Program** (`id: 5`, `type: select_multiple`, required): Select the school, camp, or youth program the child is associated with (if applicable).
* **Date of Procedure** (`id: 25`, `type: date`, required): Enter the date the procedure is scheduled to take place.
* **Procedure Description** (`id: 7`, `type: text`, required): Enter a brief description of the procedure.
* **Parent Signature** (`id: 8`, `type: text`, required): Sign as the parent or guardian.
* **Guardian Signature** (`id: 9`, `type: text`, required): Sign as the guardian (if applicable).
* **Date of Signing** (`id: 10`, `type: date`, required): Enter the date you sign the form as the parent or guardian.
* **Name of Child** (`id: 11`, `type: text`, required): Enter the name of the child.
* **Birth Date** (`id: 12`, `type: date`, required): Enter the child's birth date.
* **Name of Guardian** (`id: 13`, `type: text`, required): Enter the name of the guardian (if applicable).
* **Relationship** (`id: 14`, `type: text`, required): Enter the relationship between the guardian and the child.
* **Guardian Phone** (`id: 15`, `type: text`, required): Enter the guardian's phone number (if applicable).
* **Guardian Email** (`id: 16`, `type: email`, required): Enter the guardian's email (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact Person** (`id: 17`, `type: text`, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`id: 18`, `type: select_one`, required): Select the relationship of the emergency contact person to the child.
* **Emergency Contact** (`id: 19`, `type: text`, required): Enter the contact information for the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`id: 20`, `type: text`, required): Enter the emergency contact person's phone number (if applicable).
