# Event Data Recorder Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Data Recorder Consent Form is a required form for event data recorders to provide their consent to record and use their data. This form ensures that the user has been properly vetted and onboarded to the system and understands the data usage and terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and title in the form title field.
2. Enter a brief description of the event data recording in the form description field.
3. Select the category of the event data recording from the options provided.
4. Enter multiple categories separated by commas in the categories field.
5. Enter your driver name and driver email.
6. Enter your owner name and phone number.
7. Describe the data usage in the data usage field.
8. Check the box to indicate that you have read and understand the terms and conditions.
9. Provide your signature.
10. Enter the date you signed the form in the date signed field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Data Recorder Consent Form**: Enter the title of the form in this field. This will be used as a reference for the form.
* **Description**: Enter a brief description of the event data recording in this field. This will help the user understand the purpose of the form.
* **Category**: Select the category of the event data recording from the options provided. This will help classify the form under the correct category.
* **Categories**: Enter multiple categories separated by commas. This will help the user understand the scope of the form.
* **Driver Name**: Enter your driver name in this field. This will help identify who is completing the form.
* **Driver Email**: Enter your driver email in this field. This will help the system contact you if needed.
* **Owner Name**: Enter your owner name in this field. This will help identify who is the owner of the form.
* **Owner Phone**: Enter your owner phone number in this field. This will help the system contact you if needed.
* **Data Usage**: Describe how your data will be used in this field. This will help the user understand how their data will be used.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Check the box to indicate that you have read and understand the terms and conditions. This will ensure that you are aware of your data usage.
* **Signature**: Provide your signature in this field. This will serve as a confirmation of your consent.
* **Date Signed**: Enter the date you signed the form in this field. This will help track when you signed the form.

Please note that all fields are optional, but it is recommended to complete all fields to ensure accurate data recording and usage.
