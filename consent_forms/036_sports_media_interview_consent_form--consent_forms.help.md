# sports_media_interview_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture information for sports media interviews, including details about the interview, contact information, and consent statements. It is a critical document that ensures the interviewee's rights are respected and their consent is obtained for the interview process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the Interview Details section with the relevant information about the interview, including the date, time, location, and media outlet involved.
2.  Provide your name and contact information in the Name, Contact Info, and Email Address fields.
3.  Specify the media outlets you are affiliated with, if any, in the Media Outlet field.
4.  Indicate your consent to the interview and recording process in the Consent Statement field.
5.  Provide any additional recording details, such as the recording format and location, in the Recording Details and Recording Location fields.
6.  Fill out the Recording Notes field with any further information about the recording process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Interview Details** (id: 1, Interview Details, text, optional): This field is used to capture the details about the interview, including the date, time, and location.
- **Name** (id: 2, Name, text, optional): This field is used to capture your name, which is used for identification purposes.
- **Contact Info** (id: 3, Contact Info, text, optional): This field is used to capture your contact information, including any relevant details that may be needed for follow-up.
- **Email Address** (id: 4, Email Address, email, optional): This field is used to capture your email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
- **Phone Number** (id: 5, Phone Number, text, optional): This field is used to capture your phone number, which may be needed for contact purposes.
- **Consent Statement** (id: 6, Consent Statement, text, optional): This field is used to obtain your consent to the interview and recording process.
- **Media Outlet** (id: 7, Media Outlet, select_multiple, optional): This field is used to specify the media outlets you are affiliated with, if any.
- **Recording Details** (id: 8, Recording Details, text, optional): This field is used to capture any additional details about the recording process, including the recording format and location.
- **Recording Format** (id: 9, Recording Format, select_one, optional): This field is used to specify the format of the recording, such as video, audio, or photo.
- **Interview Date** (id: 10, Interview Date, date, optional): This field is used to capture the date of the interview.
- **Interview Time** (id: 11, Interview Time, time, optional): This field is used to capture the time of the interview.
- **Location** (id: 12, Location, text, optional): This field is used to capture the location of the interview.
- **Recording Location** (id: 13, Recording Location, text, optional): This field is used to capture the location of the recording.
- **Recording Notes** (id: 14, Recording Notes, text, optional): This field is used to capture any additional notes about the recording process.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all fields that are marked as "required" to ensure the form is complete and accurate.
- Use the correct media outlet if you are affiliated with any.
- If you have any additional recording notes, use the Recording Notes field to provide further information.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all information is accurate and complete.
