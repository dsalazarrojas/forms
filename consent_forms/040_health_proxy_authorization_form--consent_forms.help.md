# Health Proxy Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to authorize a healthcare proxy to act on behalf of an individual who is unable to make medical decisions for themselves. It is a legal document that grants permission to a proxy to make medical decisions, access medical information, and sign documents on behalf of the individual.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Name of Authorized Person field with the name of the individual who is unable to make medical decisions for themselves.
2. Enter the Address of Authorized Person field with their address.
3. Select the relationship between you and the Authorized Person from the Relationship to You dropdown menu.
4. Select the relationship between the Proxy and the Authorized Person from the Relationship to Authorized Person dropdown menu.
5. Enter the Email of Authorized Person field with their email address.
6. Enter the Phone of Authorized Person field with their phone number.
7. If applicable, enter the Date of Death field if the Authorized Person is deceased.
8. Select the relationship between the Proxy and your family from the Relationship to Your Family dropdown menu.
9. Enter the Emergency Contact Name field with the name of an emergency contact.
10. Enter the Emergency Contact Phone field with their phone number.
11. Enter the Relationship to Emergency Contact field with their relationship to the Authorized Person.
12. Sign and date the form with the Signatory of Proxy field.
13. Sign and date the form with the Signatory of Authorizing Party field.
14. Sign and date the form with the Signatory of Other Proxy field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of Authorized Person**: Enter the name of the individual who is unable to make medical decisions for themselves.
* **Address of Authorized Person**: Enter the address of the individual who is unable to make medical decisions for themselves.
* **Name of Person Authorized**: Enter the name of the person authorized to make medical decisions on behalf of the individual.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the date of birth of the individual who is unable to make medical decisions for themselves.
* **Relationship to You**: Select a relationship between you and the Authorized Person, such as "Parent" or "Sibling".
* **Other Relationship**: If the relationship between you and the Authorized Person is not listed, enter the specific relationship in this field.
* **Name of Proxy**: Enter the name of the proxy authorized to make medical decisions on behalf of the individual.
* **Relationship to Proxy**: Select a relationship between the Proxy and the Authorized Person, such as "Parent" or "Spouse".
* **Email of Authorized Person**: Enter the email address of the individual who is unable to make medical decisions for themselves.
* **Phone of Authorized Person**: Enter the phone number of the individual who is unable to make medical decisions for themselves.
* **Address of Proxy**: Enter the address of the proxy authorized to make medical decisions on behalf of the individual.
* **Date of Death (if deceased)**: If the individual is deceased, enter the date of death.
* **Relationship to Your Family**: Select a relationship between the Proxy and your family, such as "Parent" or "Sibling".
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of an emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Relationship to Emergency Contact**: Enter the relationship between the emergency contact and the Authorized Person.
* **Signatory of Proxy**: Sign and date the form with the name of the proxy.
* **Date of Signature**: Enter the date of signature of the proxy.
* **Signatory of Authorizing Party**: Sign and date the form with the name of the individual authorizing the proxy.
* **Date of Signature of Authorizing Party**: Enter the date of signature of the authorizing party.
* **Date of Signature of Proxy**: Enter the date of signature of the proxy.
* **Signatory of Other Proxy**: If there is another proxy, sign and date the form with their name.
* **Date of Signature of Other Proxy**: Enter the date of signature of the other proxy.

Note: This form is a legal document and should be completed carefully and accurately.
