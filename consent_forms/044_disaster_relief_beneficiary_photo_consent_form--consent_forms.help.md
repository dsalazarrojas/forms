# Disaster Relief Beneficiary Photo Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Disaster Relief Beneficiary Photo Consent Form is a document designed to obtain consent from disaster relief beneficiaries for the use of their photo and other personal data. It is a crucial step in ensuring that their rights and privacy are respected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your beneficiary name, contact number, and contact address accurately.
2. Select the preferred date for the release of your information.
3. Choose how you want your photo to be shared (publicly or privately).
4. Indicate the type of consent you are giving (general or specific).
5. If you want to be contacted by the agency, provide your email address and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Beneficiary Name** (`beneficiary_name`, text, required): Enter your name as a disaster relief beneficiary.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number if you want the agency to reach you.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, optional): Enter your contact address if you want the agency to contact you there.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the date on which you are consenting to the release of your information.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select the time on which you are consenting to the release of your information.
* **Permission to Share** (`permission_to_share`, select_one, optional): Select 'True' if you are willing to share your photo and information with the agency, or 'False' if you are not.
* **Permission to Publish** (`permission_to_publish`, select_multiple, optional): Select the platforms on which you want your photo to be published (On Social Media, On Print Media, or On Digital Media).
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes you have regarding your consent.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you want the agency to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you want the agency to contact you.
* **Photo Permission** (`photo_permission`, select_one, optional): Select 'True' if you are willing to have your photo taken, or 'False' if you are not.
* **Photo Release** (`photo_release`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of photo release you want (Publicly or Privately).
* **Consent Type** (`consent_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of consent you are giving (General or Specific).
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, date, optional): Select the date on which you want your information to be released.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you have any questions or concerns regarding this form, please contact the agency.
- Review each field carefully before submitting the form to ensure that your rights and privacy are respected.
