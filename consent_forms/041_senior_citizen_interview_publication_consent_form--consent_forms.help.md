# Senior Citizen Interview Publication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture the consent of senior citizens for publishing their interview, including their contact details, age, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2. Ensure that you have permission from the senior citizen to fill out this form on their behalf.
3. Verify that you have not filled out this form for this senior citizen before.
4. If you are filling out this form for someone else, please make sure they are aware of and agree to the information being collected.
5. Enter the senior citizen's first name in the "First Name" field.
6. Enter the senior citizen's last name in the "Last Name" field.
7. Enter the senior citizen's age in the "Age" field.
8. Enter the senior citizen's contact details in the "Contact Details" field.
9. If applicable, enter the senior citizen's email address in the "Email" field.
10. If applicable, enter the senior citizen's phone number in the "Phone" field.
11. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Consent to Publish" to indicate whether the senior citizen consents to the publication of their interview.
12. Sign and date the "Signature" field to indicate that the form has been filled out honestly and correctly.
13. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Consent to Share" to indicate whether the senior citizen consents to sharing their information with third parties.
14. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Consent to Use" to indicate whether the senior citizen consents to the use of their interview.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: The first name of the senior citizen.
	+ Enter the senior citizen's first name in this field.
* **Last Name**: The last name of the senior citizen.
	+ Enter the senior citizen's last name in this field.
* **Age**: The age of the senior citizen.
	+ Enter the senior citizen's age in this field.
* **Contact Details**: The contact details of the senior citizen.
	+ Enter the senior citizen's contact details, including their address, phone number, and email address if applicable.
* **Email**: The email address of the senior citizen (if applicable).
	+ Enter the senior citizen's email address if you have their permission to do so.
* **Phone**: The phone number of the senior citizen (if applicable).
	+ Enter the senior citizen's phone number if you have their permission to do so.
* **Consent to Publish**: Indicate whether the senior citizen consents to the publication of their interview.
	+ Select "Yes" if the senior citizen agrees to the publication of their interview.
* **Signature**: Sign and date this field to indicate that the form has been filled out honestly and correctly.
	+ Sign and date this field to confirm that you have filled out the form correctly.
* **Consent to Share**: Indicate whether the senior citizen consents to sharing their information with third parties.
	+ Select "Yes" if the senior citizen agrees to share their information with third parties.
* **Consent to Use**: Indicate whether the senior citizen consents to the use of their interview.
	+ Select "Yes" if the senior citizen agrees to the use of their interview.
