# Cognitive Research EEG Study Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain informed consent from participants in a Cognitive Research EEG Study. The purpose of this form is to ensure that participants understand the study's procedures, risks, benefits, and confidentiality policies, and to obtain their voluntary consent to participate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the study title, researcher's name, and institution.
2. Provide your personal information, including your full name, date of birth, email address, and phone number.
3. Understand the study procedure, including the estimated duration and what will happen during the study.
4. Acknowledge that you understand the potential risks and benefits of the study.
5. Confirm that you understand how your EEG data will be used and stored.
6. Verify that you understand your participation is voluntary and that you can withdraw at any time without penalty.
7. Read and agree to the consent statement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Study Title**: The name of the research study. This will help you understand what the study is about.
* **Principal Researcher Name**: The name of the lead investigator conducting the study. This is who you will be working with.
* **Institution**: The university or research center where the study is being conducted.
* **IRB Approval Number**: A unique identifier for the study, indicating it has been approved by the Institutional Review Board.
* **Participant Full Name**: Your full name, which will be used to identify you in the study.
* **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth, which will help us understand your age and any relevant medical history.
* **Email Address**: Your email address, which we will use to communicate with you about the study.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number, which we may use to contact you with study updates.
* **I understand the study procedure**: Please select "Yes" to indicate that you have read and understand what will happen during the study.
* **I understand the EEG equipment and process**: Please select "Yes" to indicate that you have read and understand the EEG equipment and process.
* **Estimated Study Duration**: Select the estimated duration of the study, which will help us plan the study schedule.
* **I understand the potential risks**: Please select "Yes" to indicate that you have read and understand the potential risks of the study.
* **EEG is safe and non-invasive**: Please select "Yes" to indicate that you understand the EEG equipment is safe and non-invasive.
* **I have concerns about electrode placement**: Please select "Yes" if you have concerns about the EEG electrode placement.
* **I have a history of seizures**: Please select "Yes" if you have had a history of seizures.
* **I understand the potential benefits**: Please select "Yes" to indicate that you have read and understand the potential benefits of the study.
* **I understand compensation details**: Please select "Yes" to indicate that you have read and understand any compensation offered for your participation.
* **I understand how my identity will be protected**: Please select "Yes" to indicate that you have read and understand how your identity will be protected in the study.
* **I understand how my EEG data will be used**: Please select "Yes" to indicate that you have read and understand how your EEG data will be used.
* **I understand data retention policy**: Please select "Yes" to indicate that you have read and understand how long your data will be kept.
* **I understand participation is voluntary**: Please select "Yes" to indicate that you have read and understand that your participation is voluntary.
* **I can withdraw without penalty**: Please select "Yes" to indicate that you can withdraw from the study at any time without penalty.
* **I consent to participate**: Please select "I consent" to indicate that you agree to participate in the study.
* **Date of Consent**: Your date of consent, which will be recorded as proof of your agreement to participate.
