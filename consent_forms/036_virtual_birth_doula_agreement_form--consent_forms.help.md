# Virtual Birth Doula Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to establish a contract between a Virtual Birth Doula and a client. It outlines the terms and conditions of the services provided, including the doula's availability, contact information, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, address, and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your phone number and the doula's phone number.
3. Input the doula's name, email address, and agreement details in the corresponding fields.
4. Select the date and time you signed the agreement.
5. Sign both the doula's signature and your signature fields.
6. Choose the agreement status and select the corresponding options.
7. Enter the support level you need from the doula.
8. Choose the payment status and select the corresponding options.
9. Finally, review and confirm that all information is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): This is your name as the client.
* **Client Address** (`client_address`, text, optional): Enter your home or work address.
* **Doulas Name** (`doulas_name`, text, required): This is the name of the Virtual Birth Doula providing services.
* **Doulas Contact** (`doulas_contact`, text, required): Enter the doula's phone number and other contact information.
* **Doulas Phone** (`doulas_phone`, text, required): Enter the doula's phone number.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address, if you prefer to use email communication.
* **Doulas Email** (`doulas_email`, email, required): Enter the doula's email address.
* **Agreement Details** (`agreement_details`, text, required): Describe the terms and conditions of the services provided by the doula.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required): Enter the date you signed this agreement.
* **Time Signed** (`time_signed`, time, required): Enter the time you signed this agreement.
* **Doulas Signature** (`doulas_signature`, text, required): Sign this field to confirm the agreement.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, text, required): Sign this field to confirm the agreement.
* **Agreement Status** (`agreement_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of the agreement (Active or Inactive).
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, date, required): Enter the date this agreement was made.
* **Support Level** (`support_level`, select_multiple, required): Choose the level of support you need from the doula (None, Basic, Full).
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, required): Select the current payment status (Paid, Unpaid, Partially Paid).
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the services provided.
* **Doulas Address** (`doulas_address`, text, required): Enter the doula's address.
* **Doulas License** (`doulas_license`, text, optional): Enter the doula's license or certification number.
* **Doulas License Number** (`doulas_license_num`, text, required): Enter the doula's license or certification number.
* **Doulas License State** (`doulas_license_state`, text, required): Enter the state where the doula's license is issued.
* **Doulas License Expire** (`doulas_license_exp`, date, required): Enter the expiration date of the doula's license.
* **Doulas License Issue** (`doulas_license_issue`, date, required): Enter the issue date of the doula's license.
* **Doulas Agreement Type** (`doulas_agreement_type`, text, required): Describe the type of agreement or contract you have with the doula.

## Tips
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all information is accurate and complete.
* Make sure to sign both the doula's and client's signature fields to confirm the agreement.
* Keep a copy of the agreement for your records.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the agreement, contact the doula directly.
