# School Trip Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Trip Consent Form is designed for parents or guardians to provide consent for their child to participate in a school trip.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review the form and ensure you understand the information being requested.
2. Fill out your name as the Parent Name in the text field.
3. Fill out your child's name as the Student Name in the text field.
4. Provide any necessary comments or additional information in the note field.
5. Submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, Text, Required): Please enter your name as the parent or guardian of the student.
- **Student Name** (`student_name`, Text, Required): Please enter your child's name as the student participating in the trip.
- **Parent Consent** (`school_trip_consent_form`, Note, Required): This is a free-text field where you can provide any comments or additional information about your child's participation in the trip. You can also use this field to ask questions or raise concerns about the trip.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have carefully read and understood the information provided before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the school or the person responsible for organizing the trip.
- The school will review and consider your submission before making any decisions about your child's participation.
