# Horse Riding Indemnity Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a legal document that ensures riders acknowledge and agree to the risks associated with horse riding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your first name in the "first_name" field.
2.  Fill in your last name in the "last_name" field.
3.  Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4.  Mention your organization name in the "organization" field.
5.  Provide the name of the person you would like to contact in case of an emergency in the "contact_person" field.
6.  Enter your contact information (address, phone number) in the "phone" field.
7.  Provide the date of the event in the "date_of_event" field.
8.  Fill in the start time of the event in the "start_time" field.
9.  Fill in the end time of the event in the "end_time" field.
10. Provide information about the type of event in the "event_type" field.
11. Fill in your name as a rider in the "rider_name" field.
12. Enter your date of birth (d.o.b) in the "rider_dob" field.
13. Provide the name of the person who will be the emergency contact in case of an emergency in the "emergency_contact_person" field.
14. Enter the contact number of the emergency contact in the "emergency_contact_number" field.
15. Enter the email of the emergency contact in the "emergency_contact_email" field.
16. If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please mention them in the "medical_condition" field.
17. If you have any medical condition details, please provide them in the "medical_condition_details" field.
18. Sign the form with your signature in the "rider_signature" field.
19. Sign the form with the witness signature in the "witness_signature" field.
20. Fill in the name of the witness in the "witness_name" field.
21. Fill in the title of the witness in the "witness_title" field.
22. Provide the name of the witness organization in the "witness_organization" field.
23. Fill in the witness signature date in the "witness_signature_date" field.
24. Fill in the witness signature time in the "witness_signature_time" field.
25. Select one of the options from the dropdown menu to acknowledge the risks associated with horse riding in the "rider_waiver" field.
26. If you have any doubts or have not agreed to the terms, please select "False" in the "rider_agreed" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name** (Text, optional): Fill in your first name.

* **start_time** (Time, optional): Fill in the start time of the event.
* **end_time** (Time, optional): Fill in the end time of the event.
* **event_type** (Text, optional): Provide information about the type of event.
* **rider_name** (Text, optional): Fill in your name as a rider.
* **rider_dob** (Text, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **emergency_contact_person** (Text, optional): Provide the name of the emergency contact person.
* **emergency_contact_number** (Text, optional): Enter the contact number of the emergency contact person.
* **emergency_contact_email** (Email, optional): Enter the email of the emergency contact.
* **medical_condition** (Text, optional): If you have any medical conditions, mention them.
* **medical_condition_details** (Text, optional): If you have any medical condition details, provide them.
* **rider_signature** (Text, optional): Sign the form with your signature.
* **witness_signature** (Text, optional): Sign the form with the witness signature.
* **witness_name** (Text, optional): Fill in the witness name.
* **witness_title** (Text, optional): Fill in the title of the witness.
