# Personal Training Session Recording Consent - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain consent from clients for personal training session recordings, which ensures that clients are informed and agree to the recording and use of their training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this form accurately and truthfully.
- Make sure to include all required information, including session date, time, duration, recording method, client confirmation, first name, last name, email, phone number, and address.
- Review the recording method and client confirmation options carefully.
- Submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Session Date (id: 1)**: Please input the date of the training session.
- **Session Time (id: 2)**: Enter the time of the training session.
- **Session Duration (id: 3)**: Enter the duration of the training session.
- **Session Recording (id: 4)**: Select the method of recording you plan to use for the session (video, audio, both, or neither).
- **Recording Method (id: 5)**: Confirm the method of recording you selected for the session (video, audio, both, live, or none).
- **Client Confirmation (id: 6)**: Confirm whether the client has agreed to the recording and use of their session (True or False).
- **First Name (id: 7)**: Enter the client's first name.
- **Last Name (id: 8)**: Enter the client's last name.
- **Email (id: 9)**: Enter the client's email address.
- **Phone Number (id: 10)**: Enter the client's phone number.
- **Address (id: 11)**: Enter the client's address.
- **Notes (id: 12)**: Enter any additional notes about the session.
