# Share Your Story Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants about their willingness to share their story and the details of their sharing preferences. It is likely used for research or survey purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Participant Information" section with your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
2. In the "Contact Information" section, provide your contact details so that we can get in touch with you if needed.
3. In the "Participant Story" section, write your story in the space provided.
4. In the "Sharing Consent" section, select "True" if you are willing to share your story with others, and "False" if you are not.
5. In the "Sharing Participant with" section, choose the people you are willing to share your story with, such as Family, Friends, or Therapist.
6. In the "Sharing with Others" section, select "True" if you are willing to share your story with others beyond those you've already selected, and "False" if you are not.
7. In the "Sharing Other Info" sections, provide any additional details you feel comfortable sharing.
