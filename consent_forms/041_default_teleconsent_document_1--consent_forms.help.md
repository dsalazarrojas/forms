# Default Teleconsent Document (1) - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to create a default teleconsent document for a person. It is likely used for medical or healthcare-related purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the requested information.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the individual's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the individual's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the individual's birthdate in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Birth Country** (`birth_country`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the individual's country of birth.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the individual's current address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the individual's phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the individual's email address.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any relevant medical record number.
* **Date of Entry** (`date_of_entry`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of entry for the individual (not specified what this field is for, but it might be the date of a medical procedure or the date the form was created).
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any relevant medical history information.
* **Contact Person 1** (`contact_person_1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information for a contact person (if applicable).
