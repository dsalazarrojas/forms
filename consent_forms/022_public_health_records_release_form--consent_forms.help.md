# Public Health Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Health Records Release Form is used to obtain consent for accessing and sharing public health records. This form is essential in situations where a patient needs to share their health records with authorized entities, such as healthcare providers or insurance companies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Enter your date of birth.
4. Enter your phone number (if applicable).
5. Select the authorized provider(s) you trust with your health records (e.g., doctor, nurse, or social worker).
6. Select the role(s) the authorized provider(s) will take on (e.g., healthcare provider, researcher, or insurance company).
7. Select the facility where you would like to share your health records (e.g., hospital, clinic, or research institution).
8. Sign the form to confirm your consent.
9. Enter the date and time you signed the form.
10. If required, select who you are authorizing to share your health records with and when you would like them to be shared.
11. Add a witness signature and note (if applicable).
12. Enter the name of the witness and their title (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_title** (text, required): This is the title of the form, which should be completed with the date and your name.
* **name** (text, required): Enter your full name.
* **date_of_birth** (date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number (if you have one).
* **authorized_provider** (select one, optional): Select the authorized provider(s) you trust with your health records.
* **authorized_role** (select multiple, optional): Select the role(s) the authorized provider(s) will take on.
* **authorized_facility** (select multiple, optional): Select the facility where you would like to share your health records.
* **signature** (note, required): Sign the form to confirm your consent.
* **date_signed** (date, required): Enter the date you signed the form.
* **time_signed** (time, required): Enter the time you signed the form.
* **authorized_to_share** (select one, optional): Select whether you are authorizing others to share your health records.
* **authorized_to_share_with** (select multiple, optional): Select who you are authorizing to share your health records with.
* **authorized_to_share_when** (select multiple, optional): Select when you would like your health records to be shared.
* **patient_signature** (text, required): Add a note with your signature.
* **witness_signature** (text, optional): Add a note with your witness's signature.
* **witness_name** (text, optional): Enter the name of your witness.
* **witness_title** (text, optional): Enter your witness's title.
* **authorized_to_share_facility** (select multiple, optional): Select the facility where you would like to share your health records.
* **consent_date** (date, required): Enter the date you consented to share your health records.
* **consent_time** (time, required): Enter the time you consented to share your health records.
* **consent_facility** (text, required): Enter the facility where you consented to share your health records.
* **authorized_facility_list** (select multiple, optional): Select the facility where you would like to share your health records.
* **patient_signature_note** (note, required): Add a note with your signature.
* **witness_signature_note** (note, optional): Add a note with your witness's signature.
* **authorized_to_share_with_list** (select multiple, optional): Select who you are authorizing to share your health records with.
* **authorized_to_share_when** (select multiple, optional): Select when you would like your health records to be shared.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before signing it.
* Ensure that you understand who you are authorizing to share your health records with and when.
* Keep your health records private and confidential.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
