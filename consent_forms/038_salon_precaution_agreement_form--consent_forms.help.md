# Salon Precaution Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain the necessary consent and agreement from customers before they undergo services at the salon. It serves as a record of their acknowledgment and agreement to follow the salon's terms and conditions, including social distancing and other precautionary measures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and ensure you are agreeing or signing as requested.
2. If required fields are marked, please fill them out as per the instructions below.
3. Sign the form digitally or physically, and date it accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, text, optional): This is a signature field for the customer to sign their name. If you have no signature, you can leave this field blank.
* **Customer Consent** (`customer_consent`, text, optional): Please provide a brief statement agreeing to the salon's terms and conditions. This should include your understanding of and commitment to follow the salon's rules and guidelines.
* **Salon Terms and Conditions** (`salon_terms_and_conditions`, text, optional): This field is for you to agree to the terms and conditions of the salon. Please review the document and provide a signature if you agree.
* **Salon Social Distancing** (`salon_social_distancing`, select_one, optional): Please select "Yes" if you understand and agree to follow the salon's social distancing measures.
* **Customer Signature Date** (`customer_signature_date`, date, optional): This is the date on which you signed this agreement. Please ensure to fill in the correct date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

* **Salon Precaution Policy** (`salon_precaution_policy`, text, optional): Please describe in your own words your understanding of and agreement to the salon's precaution policy. This is a space to provide any specific questions or concerns you may have.

## Tips
* Please review each field carefully and ensure you are filling out the correct information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the salon staff.
* This form is a legally binding agreement.
