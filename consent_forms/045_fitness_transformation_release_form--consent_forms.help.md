# Fitness Transformation Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Transformation Release Form is designed to collect data from individuals who have undergone a fitness transformation program. This information will be used to evaluate and document the transformation, as well as potentially be used for future reference or follow-up communications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your User Info.
2.  Fill out your User Details.
3.  Indicate your Challenge Info (Yes/No).
4.  Choose your Challenge Type (Yes/No).
5.  Enter your Participant Details.
6.  Provide your Contact Info (email address).
7.  Confirm your Consent.
8.  Enter your Gym Details.
9.  Sign and date the document.
10.  Enter your Coach's Details (name, email, phone number, and address).
11.  Enter Program Coach Details.
12.  Enter Payment Terms.
13.  Sign the document with your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (Text, Optional): Enter your name, email, and any other relevant user information.
* **User Details** (Text, Optional): Provide any additional user details.
* **Challenge Info** (Yes/No, Optional): Indicate if you have participated in a challenge.
* **Challenge Type** (Yes/No, Optional): Specify the type of challenge.
* **Participant Details** (Text, Optional): Enter any relevant participant details.
* **Contact Info** (Text, Email, Optional): Enter your contact information.
* **Consent** (Yes/No, Optional): Confirm your consent for your information to be used.
* **Gym Details** (Text, Optional): Enter any relevant gym details.
* **Signed Consent** (Date, Optional): Date and sign the document.
* **Signed By** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the person signing.
* **Timestamp** (Time, Optional): Record the date and time of submission.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your or your coach's email.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your or your coach's phone number.
* **Address** (Text, Optional): Enter your or your coach's address.
* **Program Details** (Text, Optional): Enter any relevant program details.
* **Program Start Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the start date of the program.
* **Program End Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the end date of the program.
* **Program Duration** (Number, Optional): Enter the duration of the program.
* **Coach Details** (Text, Optional): Enter your coach's details.
* **Coach Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your coach's email.
* **Coach Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your coach's phone number.
* **Coach Address** (Text, Optional): Enter your coach's address.
* **Program Coach Details** (Text, Optional): Enter any relevant program coach details.
* **Payment Terms** (Text, Optional): Enter any payment terms or conditions.
* **Signature** (Text, Optional): Sign the document with your signature.

Please remember to complete each field accurately and truthfully. This information will be used for official documentation and future reference.
