# Social Media Post Sharing Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect user's consent to share content on various social media platforms. It is essential to ensure that users are aware of where their content will be shared and can choose their preferred platforms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the options for sharing on different social media platforms.
2. Select the platforms on which you want to share your content.
3. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.
