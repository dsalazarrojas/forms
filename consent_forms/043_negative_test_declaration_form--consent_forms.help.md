# Negative Test Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Negative Test Declaration Form is a form used to declare the results of a test related to a passenger, gathering information about the passenger, their travel history, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the passenger's information, including name, age, and contact details in the "Passenger Information" section.
2. Enter the travel date and flight number in the "Travel Information" section.
3. Select the test result in the "Test Result" section.
4. Enter any relevant medical information related to the test in the "Medical Information" section.
5. Enter any additional information that may be relevant to the test or passenger in the "Additional Information" section.
6. Enter the ship's name and count in the "Ship Information" section.
7. Enter the number of times the passenger has traveled in the "Travel Count" section.
8. Enter the test date in the "Test Date" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Passenger Information** (`passenger_info`, text, required: false): Enter the passenger's name, age, and contact information.
* **Travel Information** (`travel_info`, text, required: false): Enter the travel date and flight number.
* **Test Result** (`test_result`, text, required: false): Select the test result.
* **Contact Information 2** (`contact_info2`, text, required: false): Enter the contact information for the person submitting the form. (Note: This field is not clearly defined and may not be used in the form's purpose.)
* **Medical Information** (`medical_info`, text, required: false): Enter any medical information related to the test.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information that may be relevant to the test or passenger.
* **Ship Information** (`ship_info`, text, required: false): Enter the ship's name and count.
* **Travel Count** (`travel_count`, number, required: false): Enter the number of times the passenger has traveled.
* **Test Information** (`test_info`, text, required: false): Enter any test-related information. (Note: This field is not clearly defined and may not be used in the form's purpose.)
* **Test Date** (`test_date`, date, required: false): Enter the test date.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the form owner or submitter.
* This form is intended for passenger test declaration purposes only, and any errors or omissions may affect the passenger's travel or medical clearance.
* Please review and verify the form before submission to ensure accuracy.
