# cultural_diversity_celebration_photo_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The cultural diversity celebration photo consent form is designed to capture the information needed to take and share photos of cultural diversity celebrations. This form ensures that you have the necessary permissions and consents from the photo subjects and organizers involved.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information for each field.
2. Review and understand each field's explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consent Form** (`consent_form`, text, optional):
  This field is where you can provide a brief description of the consent form or agreement that the subjects and organizers have signed, confirming their permissions and consents.
* **Photo Description** (`photo_description`, text, optional):
  Enter a brief description of the photo, including the date, time, and location of the event.
* **Permission Level** (`permission_level`, select_multiple, optional):
  Select the levels of permission you are seeking from the subjects and organizers, such as "General permission", "Specific permission", or "No permission".
* **Permission Date** (`permission_date`, date, optional):
  Enter the date when the permission was granted or will be granted.
* **Photo Location** (`photo_location`, text, optional):
  Enter the location where the photo was taken.
* **Photo Subjects** (`photo_subjects`, select_multiple, optional):
  Select the subjects of the photo, such as "People", "Places", "Objects".
* **Photo Orientation** (`photo_orientation`, select_one, optional):
  Select the orientation of the photo, such as "Landscape", "Portrait", or "Other".
* **Photo Size** (`photo_size`, number, optional):
  Enter the size of the photo, in pixels or inches.
* **Photo File** (`photo_file`, select_multiple, optional):
  Select the file type of the photo, such as "JPEG", "PNG", or "TIFF".
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional):
  Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the photo event.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional):
  Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional):
  Enter the phone number of the contact person.

Note: Fields marked with an asterisk (\*) are optional.
