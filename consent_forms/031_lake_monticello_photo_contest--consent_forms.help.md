# lake_monticello_photo_contest - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for expressing interest in participating in the Lake Monticello Photo Contest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" to submit your photos.
2. Enter your name in the text field.
3. Enter your email address in the email field.
4. Enter your contact number in the text field.
5. Select "True" or "False" to indicate your consent to participate.
6. Upload a file (not specified, likely a photo).
7. Click "Submit" to submit your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **submit_your_photos** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose "Yes" if you want to participate in the photo contest and "No" if you do not.
* **participant_name** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be listed in the contest.
* **email** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your email address so that we can contact you about the contest.
* **contact_number** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your contact number so that we can get in touch with you about the contest.
* **consent** (`select_one`, required/optional): Choose "True" if you consent to participate and "False" if you do not.
* **file** (`text`, required/optional): Upload a photo or file to enter into the contest.
* **submit** (`text`, required/optional): Click "Submit" to submit your entry. 

## Tips
* Make sure to choose "Yes" to participate in the photo contest.
* Ensure that your email address and contact number are accurate so that we can contact you about the contest.
* Be sure to read and understand the rules and terms of the contest before participating.
