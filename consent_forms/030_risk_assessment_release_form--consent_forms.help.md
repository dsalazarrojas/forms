# Risk Assessment Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants involved in risk assessment activities. It is used to document the participant's details, the risk assessment activity, and any relevant notes or comments.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out each section carefully**: Ensure that you provide accurate and complete information for each field.
2. **Use the correct format for date and time fields**: When filling out the date and time fields, use the format specified in the field labels (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY for dates and HH:MM for times).
3. **Enter a brief description in the notes field**: Use this field to capture any relevant comments or observations about the risk assessment activity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Risk Assessment Release Form Participant**: This field is optional, and you can use it to enter any comments or descriptions about the participant.
* **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant involved in the risk assessment activity.
* **Participant E-mail**: Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Risk Assessment Release Form Activity**: Enter a brief description of the risk assessment activity, such as the type of activity or the specific task involved.
* **Risk Assessment Release Date**: Enter the date of the risk assessment activity in the format specified in the field label (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Risk Assessment Release Time**: Enter the time of the risk assessment activity in the format specified in the field label (e.g., HH:MM).
* **Risk Assessment Release Form Notes**: Enter any relevant comments or notes about the risk assessment activity.
* **Risk Assessment Release Form Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about the risk assessment activity.

## Tips

* Make sure to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that all necessary information is captured.
* If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please consult with the study coordinator or research coordinator.
* Remember to review and verify the information provided before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
