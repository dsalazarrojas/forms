# Clothing Rental Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from individuals who wish to rent clothing items for a specific period.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Please fill in your personal details**:
	* Renter Full Name: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
	* Email Address: Enter your personal email address.
	* Phone Number: Enter your phone number.
	* Current Address: Enter your full residential address.
	* ID Type - Number: Enter your ID number (e.g., driver's license, passport, etc.).
2. **Describe the item you wish to rent**:
	* Item Description: Briefly describe the clothing item you wish to rent.
	* Item Color: Enter the color of the clothing item (optional).
	* Item Size: Enter the size of the clothing item (optional).
3. **Rental Details**:
	* Rental Start Date: Enter the date you plan to pick up the clothing item.
	* Rental End Date: Enter the date you plan to return the clothing item.
	* Rental Duration - Days: Enter the total number of days you plan to rent the item.
4. **Rental Terms and Conditions**:
	* Daily Rental Rate - USD: Enter the cost per day for renting the clothing item.
	* Total Rental Cost - USD: Calculate the total cost based on the rental duration and daily rate.
	* Security Deposit - USD: Enter the amount to be held for any damages (optional).
	* Late Return Fee - USD per Day: Enter the cost for returning the clothing item after the end date (optional).
5. **Acknowledgments and Agreement**:
	* Renter acknowledges responsibility for damage: Select "Yes, I agree" to acknowledge your responsibility for any damage to the clothing item.
	* Item must be returned clean: Select "Yes, understood" to confirm you will return the item clean.
	* I have read and understand the rental agreement: Select "Yes, I agree" to confirm you have read and understood the terms.
	* I agree to the terms and accept responsibility: Select "Yes, I accept" to digitally sign the agreement.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any issues with the rental.
* Review the terms and conditions carefully before proceeding.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
* By digitally signing this agreement, you are confirming that you have read and understood the terms and conditions of the rental agreement.
