# Life Insurance Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Life Insurance Declaration Form is a form used to declare and manage life insurance policies. This form gathers essential information about the policyholder, including contact information, medical history, and policy details. The form is used to update and manage policyholder information for accurate and efficient processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the relevant contact information for the policyholder from the provided options.
2. Enter the policyholder's name and age.
3. Enter the policyholder's birth date.
4. Select the policyholder's occupation from the options provided.
5. Provide any necessary medical information.
6. Enter the policyholder's height and weight.
7. Enter the contact number of the policyholder.
8. Enter the policyholder's email address.
9. Confirm if emergency contact is required or not.
10. Sign the form to confirm the declaration.
11. Select the type of insurance policy.
12. Enter the name of the insurance provider.
13. Provide any necessary medical history.
14. Select the coverage type for the policy.
15. Enter the coverage amount for the policy.
16. Enter the policy effective date.
17. Enter the policy duration.
18. Enter the premium amount for the policy.
19. Select the policy status.
20. Enter the reason for policy termination.
21. Provide any additional policy notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, select_one, required=false): Select the relevant contact information for the policyholder from the provided options.
* **Name and Age** (`name_and_age`, text, required=true): Enter the policyholder's name and age.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, required=true): Enter the policyholder's birth date.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, select_multiple, required=false): Select the policyholder's occupation from the provided options.
* **Medical Information** (`medical_info`, text, required=false): Provide any necessary medical information.
* **Height and Weight** (`height_and_weight`, number, required=false): Enter the policyholder's height and weight.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required=true): Enter the policyholder's contact number.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required=true): Enter the policyholder's email address.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, select_one, required=false): Confirm if emergency contact is required or not.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required=true): Sign the form to confirm the declaration.
* **Insurance Type** (`insurance_type`, select_multiple, required=false): Select the type of insurance policy.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, required=true): Enter the name of the insurance provider.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required=true): Provide any necessary medical history.
* **Insurance Coverage** (`insurance_coverage`, select_one, required=false): Select the coverage type for the policy.
* **Coverage Amount** (`coverage_amount`, number, required=true): Enter the coverage amount for the policy.
* **Policy Effective Date** (`policy_effective_date`, date, required=true): Enter the policy effective date.
* **Policy Duration** (`policy_duration`, number, required=true): Enter the policy duration.
* **Premium** (`premium`, number, required=true): Enter the premium amount for the policy.
* **Policy Status** (`policy_status`, select_one, required=false): Select the policy status.
* **Termination Reason** (`termination_reason`, text, required=true): Enter the reason for policy termination.
* **Policy Notes** (`policy_notes`, text, required=false): Provide any additional policy notes.
