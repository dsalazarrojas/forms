# healthcare_eligibility_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help healthcare providers verify patient information, including contact details, insurance information, policy details, and signatures. The form is intended to be used in healthcare settings to ensure accurate and up-to-date patient information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the patient information section, which includes the patient's personal details.
2. Proceed to the contact information section, where you can provide the patient's contact details.
3. Next, fill out the insurance information section, which includes details about the patient's insurance coverage.
4. Move on to the policy information section, where you can specify the policy details.
5. In the policy details section, provide any additional information about the policy.
6. Sign the patient signature section, which is required to confirm the patient's consent.
7. Sign the healthcare provider signature section, which is required to verify the healthcare provider's identity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_info** (`patient_info`, required): This field is for the patient's personal information, such as name, date of birth, and contact details.
* **contact_info** (`contact_info`, required): Provide the patient's contact information, including phone number and email address.
* **insurance_info** (`insurance_info`, required): This field is for the patient's insurance information, including policy number, insurance provider, and coverage details.
* **policy_info** (`policy_info`, required): Specify the policy details, including policy type and effective date.
* **policy_details** (`policy_details`, required): Provide any additional information about the policy.
* **patient_signature** (`patient_signature`, required): Sign this field to confirm the patient's consent.
* **healthcare_provider_signature** (`healthcare_provider_signature`, required): Sign this field to verify the healthcare provider's identity.
* **healthcare_provider_info** (`healthcare_provider_info`, required): This field is for the healthcare provider's information, including name and contact details.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and up-to-date patient information.
* Verify the patient's identity and information before signing the form.
* Keep the form secure and confidential to protect patient privacy.
* Use this form in accordance with healthcare industry standards and regulations.
