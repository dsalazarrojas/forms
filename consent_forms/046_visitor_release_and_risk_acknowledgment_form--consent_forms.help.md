# Visitor Release And Risk Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from visitors, including their personal details, risk assessment, signature, risk acknowledgment, visitor agreement, contact information, and emergency contact information. The form is meant to be completed by visitors to acknowledge their release and risk understanding, and to provide a record of their visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in the required fields with your personal and contact information.
2. Provide your risk assessment and risk acknowledgment in the respective fields.
3. Sign the form digitally to confirm your understanding of the risks involved.
4. Complete the visitor agreement by acknowledging that you understand the risks and will follow the rules.
5. Enter your contact and emergency contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Visitor Details** (`visitor_details`, text, optional): Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Risk Assessment** (`risk_assessment`, text, optional): Describe the risks you're aware of and willing to take on during your visit.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Sign your name to confirm your understanding of the risks and terms of the visit.
* **Risk Acknowledgment** (`risk_acknowledgment`, text, optional): Confirm that you understand the risks involved and will follow the rules.
* **Visitor Agreement** (`visitor_agreement`, text, optional): Check the box to confirm that you understand and agree to the terms of the visit.
* **Date Visited** (`date_visited`, date, optional): Enter the date of your visit.
* **Time Visited** (`time_visited`, time, optional): Enter the time of your visit.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Enter your contact information, including phone number and address.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Visitor Information** (`visitor_information`, text, optional): Enter any additional visitor information.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill out all the required fields, and if you're unsure about any field, ask your host for clarification.
