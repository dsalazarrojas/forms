# Public Safety Program Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent and acknowledgment from participants and their guardians for the Public Safety Program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the participant's information, including their name and phone number.
2. Fill out the guardian's information, including their name and phone number.
3. Indicate whether the participant and guardian have signed and acknowledged the waiver terms.
4. Date and time stamp the acknowledgment and waiver.
5. Sign and date the participant's and guardian's signature fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the participant's name.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the guardian's name.
* **Guardian Phone** (`guardian_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the guardian's phone number.
* **Waiver Signed** (`waiver_signed`, select_one, required/optional): Check the box if the participant has signed the waiver.
* **Acknowledgment Date** (`acknowledgment_date`, date, required/optional): Date and time stamp the acknowledgment of the waiver terms.
* **Acknowledgment Time** (`acknowledgment_time`, time, required/optional): Time stamp the acknowledgment of the waiver terms.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, required/optional): Sign and date the participant's signature.
* **Guardian Signature** (`guardian_signature`, text, required/optional): Sign and date the guardian's signature.
* **Waiver Date** (`waiver_date`, date, required/optional): Date the waiver.
* **Waiver Time** (`waiver_time`, time, required/optional): Time stamp the waiver.
* **Waiver Expiration Date** (`waiver_expires`, date, required/optional): Date the waiver expiration.
* **Waiver Status** (`waiver_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select the current status of the waiver (Active or Inactive).
* **Participant Acknowledgment** (`participant_acknowledgment`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check the boxes for the participant's acknowledgment (Yes or No).
* **Guardian Acknowledgment** (`guardian_acknowledgment`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check the boxes for the guardian's acknowledgment (Yes or No).
* **Participant Waiver** (`participant_waiver`, select_one, required/optional): Check the box for the participant's waiver (Yes or No).
* **Guardian Waiver** (`guardian_waiver`, select_one, required/optional): Check the box for the guardian's waiver (Yes or No).
