# Trade For Print Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a standard Trade For Print Agreement used for capturing information about a photo shoot between a photographer and their model. It ensures that both parties have a clear understanding of the terms and conditions of the shoot.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the fields as follows:
	* Enter the photographer's name in the **Photographer Name** field.
	* Enter the model’s name in the **Model Name** field.
	* Enter the date of the shoot in the **Date of Shot** field.
	* Enter the photographer's email address in the **Photographer Email** field.
	* Enter the model’s email address in the **Model Email** field.
	* Add any additional details or notes in the **Agreement Details** field.
	* Specify the print details in the **Print Details** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Photographer Name**: The field for the photographer's name.
* **Model Name**: The field for the model's name.
* **Date of Shot**: The field for the date of the photo shoot.
* **Photographer Email**: The field for the photographer's email address.
* **Model Email**: The field for the model's email address.
* **Agreement Details**: A field for any additional details or notes about the agreement.
* **Print Details**: A field to specify any print details related to the photo shoot.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you understand the terms and conditions of the shoot before filling out this form.
* Double-check that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* Review and negotiate the agreement with your model before signing.
* Keep a copy of the completed form for your records.
