# Parent Permission Form For Field Trip - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from parents for field trips, ensuring their child's safety and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the "Field Trip Details" section with a brief description of the trip.
2. Enter your name in the "prophet's Parent Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your contact phone number in the format 123-456-7890 or 123-456-7890 ext. 123 in the "contact_number" field.
5. Select the schools your child is currently attending from the "school_permission" dropdown menu.
6. Sign and date the "Parent Signature" field with your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Field Trip Details**: Enter a brief description of the field trip, such as the destination, duration, and any other relevant details.
* **prophet's Parent Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **email**: Enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you.
* **contact_number**: Enter your phone number in the format 123-456-7890 or 123-456-7890 ext. 123.
* **contact_number**: This field will be used to contact you in case of an emergency. Please enter your phone number in the correct format.
* **school_permission**: Select all the schools your child is currently attending to ensure the trip is approved.
* **Parent Signature**: Sign and date your name in the provided space, indicating your permission for your child to participate in the field trip.
