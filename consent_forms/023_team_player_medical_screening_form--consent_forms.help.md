# Team Player Medical Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect medical history and emergency contact information from team players for the team's internal medical record.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your first and last name in the corresponding fields.
3. Enter your email address, if applicable.
4. Describe your relationship with the emergency contact.
5. Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
6. Describe any medical history, including any symptoms or treatments.
7. Check the boxes for any medical screenings you have undergone.
8. Add any additional medical history notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, *): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, *): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, email, *): Enter your email address, if you have one.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (Emergency Contact Relationship, text): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact (e.g. spouse, parent, friend).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Emergency Contact Name, text): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Emergency Contact Phone, text): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Medical History** (Medical History, note): Describe any medical history, including any symptoms or treatments.
* **Online Consent** (Online Consent, select_one): Select one of the options (Label 1, Label 2, Label 3) to indicate your consent.
* **Medical History Date** (Medical History Date, date): Enter the date of your medical history.
* **Medical History Time** (Medical History Time, time): Enter the time of your medical history.
* **Medical History Location** (Medical History Location, text): Describe the location of your medical history.
* **Medical History Symptoms** (Medical History Symptoms, text): Describe any symptoms or medical issues you have experienced.
* **Allergies** (Allergies, text): List any allergies you have.
* **Medical History Notes** (Medical History Notes, text): Add any additional notes about your medical history.
* **Medical Screening** (Medical Screening, select_multiple): Select any medical screenings you have undergone (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
