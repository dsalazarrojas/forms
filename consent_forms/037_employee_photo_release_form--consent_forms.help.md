# Employee Photo Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to request employee consent for their photo to be taken and released for various purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your consent is valid.
2.  Check that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
3.  If you are unsure about any field, seek clarification from your supervisor or HR representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Photo** (`employee_photo`, select_one, required): Choose whether or not to take your photo.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official government ID or employee record.
* **Date of Photo** (`date_of_photo`, date, required): Enter the date of the photo to be taken.
* **Photo Location** (`photo_location`, text, required): Enter the location where the photo will be taken.
* **Purpose of Photo** (`purpose_of_photo`, text, required): Enter the purpose of the photo (e.g., ID badge, company newsletter, etc.).
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter the name of the company taking the photo.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, text, required): Sign and date the form to show that you have read, understood, and agreed to the terms.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date the photo was taken.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, text, required): Sign and date the form to confirm that the employee's photo has been taken.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the manager who took the photo.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the company.
