# Art Contest Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Contest Voting Form is designed for evaluators to vote on art entries in a contest. It ensures the evaluators' votes are fair and impartial and maintains confidentiality if required.
## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the required fields marked with an *asterisk*.
2. Provide a quality rating and vote for each entry.
3. Leave comments about each entry if you wish.
4. Assess the overall quality of submissions and clarity of voting instructions.
5. Confirm that your vote is fair and impartial, and that you have no conflict of interest in the contest.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Voter Information
* **Voter Name** (`voter_name`, text, *required*): Enter your name for voting purposes.
* **Email Address** (`voter_email`, email, *required*): Enter your email address for correspondence.
* **Voter Affiliation or Category** (`voter_affiliation`, select_one, *required*): Choose your affiliation with the art contest (e.g., Professional judge, Artist, General public, etc.).
### Entry 1 Evaluation
* **Entry 1 ID Number** (`entry_1_id`, text, *required*): Enter the ID number of Entry 1.
* **Artwork Title** (`entry_1_title`, text, *required*): Enter the title of Entry 1.
* **Artist Name - If Identified** (`entry_1_artist`, text, *optional*): Enter the artist's name if known.
* **Quality Rating** (`entry_1_rating`, select_one, *required*): Rate the quality of Entry 1 (Excellent, Very good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Would You Vote for This Entry** (`entry_1_vote`, select_one, *required*): Indicate if you would vote for Entry 1 (Yes - strong vote, Yes - vote, Neutral, False, Strong no).
* **Comments About This Entry** (`entry_1_comments`, text, *optional*): Provide any comments about Entry 1.
### Entry 2 Evaluation
* **Entry 2 ID** (`entry_2_id`, text, *required*): Enter the ID number of Entry 2.
* **Entry 2 Title** (`entry_2_title`, text, *required*): Enter the title of Entry 2.
* **Entry 2 Rating** (`entry_2_rating`, select_one, *required*): Rate the quality of Entry 2 (Excellent, Very good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Entry 2 Vote** (`entry_2_vote`, select_one, *required*): Indicate if you would vote for Entry 2 (Yes - strong vote, Yes - vote, Neutral, False, Strong no).
* **Entry 2 Comments** (`entry_2_comments`, text, *optional*): Provide any comments about Entry 2.
### Overall Contest Feedback
* **Overall Quality of Submissions** (`overall_quality`, select_one, *required*): Assess the overall quality of all submissions (Excellent, Very good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Clarity of Voting Instructions** (`voting_clarity`, select_one, *required*): Rate the clarity of the voting instructions (Very clear, Clear, Somewhat clear, Unclear, Very unclear).
### Consent and Agreements
* **I certify my vote is fair and impartial** (`voting_integrity`, select_one, *required*): Confirm that your vote is fair and impartial (Yes, I certify, False).
* **I have no conflict of interest in this voting** (`conflict_of_interest`, select_one, *required*): Confirm that you have no conflict of interest in the contest (Yes, no conflict, Yes, but disclosed above, No, I have a conflict).
* **I agree to maintain confidentiality if required** (`confidentiality`, select_one, *required*): Confirm that you agree to maintain confidentiality if required (Yes, I agree, False).

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out all required fields.
* Be fair and impartial when voting.
* Provide clear comments and ratings for each entry.
* Assess the overall quality of submissions and clarity of voting instructions.
* Confirm that your vote is fair and impartial, and that you have no conflict of interest in the contest.
* If you have any issues with the form, contact form@gic.mx.
