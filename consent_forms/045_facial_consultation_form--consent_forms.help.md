# Facial Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facial Consultation Form is used to collect patient information, medical history, and consultation data for a facial consultation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the patient's details, including their name, date of birth, and any medical record number.
2. Enter the date and time of the consultation.
3. Provide your own details as the doctor or medical staff member performing the consultation, including your name, title, and specialty.
4. Fill in the contact information for the healthcare facility where the consultation took place.
5. Review the form carefully to ensure all required fields are filled in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facial Consultation Form**: This is the title of the form, which is for your reference only.
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their medical record.
* **Patient DOB (Date of Birth)**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Consent Date**: Enter the date the patient gave consent for the consultation.
* **Consent Time**: Enter the time the patient gave consent for the consultation.
* **Doctor Name**: Enter your own name as the doctor or medical staff member performing the consultation.
* **Doctor Title**: Enter your title (e.g., MD, DO, etc.).
* **Doctor Specialty**: Enter your medical specialty.
* **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the healthcare facility where the consultation took place.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number for the healthcare facility where the consultation took place.
* **Contact E-mail**: Enter the email address for the healthcare facility where the consultation took place.
* **Medical Record Number**: Enter the patient's medical record number, if applicable.

## Tips

* Please ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* Double-check the accuracy of all entered information.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the healthcare facility for guidance.
