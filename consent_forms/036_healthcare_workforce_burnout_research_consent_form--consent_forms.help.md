# Healthcare Workforce Burnout Research Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is intended for healthcare workers to provide demographic information and indicate their willingness to participate in research on burnout and job satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the answer "Yes" if you are a healthcare worker.
2. Enter the date of your birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
3. Enter the date and time you signed this form in the format MM/DD/YYYY HH:MM AM/PM.
4. Please enter your name as it appears on your identification document.
5. Enter your email address for contact and follow-up information.
6. Enter your phone number.
7. This is the space for your handwritten or digital signature of consent.
8. Enter the date of your consent in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
9. Select "Yes" if you are an informed participant and have all necessary information about this research project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Prophets**: Select "Yes" if you are a healthcare worker.
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification document.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact and follow-up information.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Consent Signature**: This is the space for your handwritten or digital signature of consent.
* **Consent Date**: Enter the date of your consent in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Informed Consent**: Select "Yes" if you are an informed participant and have all necessary information about this research project.
* **Participant Date of Birth**: Enter the date of your birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Healthcare Worker Name**: This field is not currently being used and may be removed in the future.
* **Healthcare Worker Title**: Select "Yes" if you are a healthcare worker, but this field is not currently active.
* **Time of Consent**: This field is not currently being used and may be removed in the future.
* **Participant Signature**: This field is not currently being used and may be removed in the future.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have all necessary information before completing this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to the research team.
* This form is intended for healthcare workers, so please only select "Yes" for "Prophets" if you are a healthcare worker.
