# Child Medical Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Child Medical Consent Form is a document used to obtain consent from a parent or guardian for a child's medical care. This form ensures that the parent or guardian is aware of and agrees to any medical services provided to their child.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, you will need to provide the following information:

* Select the medical services you consent to for your child (e.g., Routine Check-ups and Exams, Required Vaccinations, etc.)
* List any medications your child is allergic to
* Provide the name of your child
* Provide the date of birth of your child
* Enter the phone number of your primary contact person for medical updates
* Provide the name of your child's pediatrician (lead doctor)
* Enter the phone number of your child's primary clinic or doctor's office
* Sign the form with your full name, which serves as a legal acknowledgment

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <u>Child Full Name</u>
* (`Child Full Name`, text, required): Enter your child's full name as it appears on medical records.

### <u>Date of Birth</u>
* (`Date of Birth`, date, required): Select your child's birthday.

### <u>Parent or Guardian Name</u>
* (`Parent or Guardian Name`, text, required): Enter your full legal name.

### <u>Phone Number</u>
* (`Phone Number`, text, required): Enter the primary phone number for your contact information.

### <u>Primary Physician's Name</u>
* (`Primary Physician's Name`, text, required): Enter the name of your child's primary pediatrician.

### <u>Phone Number of Primary Physician's Office</u>
* (`Phone Number of Primary Physician's Office`, text, required): Enter the primary phone number for your child's clinic or doctor's office.

### <u>I Consent to the Following Services</u>
* (`I Consent to the Following Services`, select_multiple, required): Select all the medical services you consent to for your child.
	+ Select "Routine Check-ups and Exams" for regular check-ups and exams.
	+ Select "Required Vaccinations" for any required vaccinations.
	+ Select "Diagnostic Lab Tests" for any diagnostic tests.
	+ Select "Emergency Medical Care" for emergency situations.

### <u>List Any Medications Allergies</u>
* (`List Any Medications Allergies`, text, optional): List any medications your child is allergic to.

### <u>Electronic Signature - Type Full Name</u>
* (`Electronic Signature - Type Full Name`, text, required): Sign the form with your full name, which serves as a legal acknowledgment.

### <u>Date of Signature</u>
* (`Date of Signature`, date, required): Select the current date.

### <u>Additional Tips</u>
* Please ensure that all fields are filled accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any medical services or information, please reach out to the medical professional for guidance.
* Keep the signed form for your records, as it serves as a legal document.
