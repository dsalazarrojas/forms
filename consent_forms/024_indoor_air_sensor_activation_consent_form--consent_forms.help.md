# indoor_air_sensor_activation_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The indoor air sensor activation consent form is used to obtain consent from users for the activation of indoor air sensors. This form is necessary to ensure that users understand and agree to the activation of these sensors, which may collect personal data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out your First Name.
2. Fill out your Last Name.
3. Provide your Email Address (if you have one).
4. Enter your address.
5. Fill out your Date of Birth (if required).
6. Select whether you have indoor Air Sensors or not.
7. Select whether you would like to activate your indoor Air Sensors or not (this is a required field).
8. Select the date of consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Please fill out your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Please fill out your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, optional): If you have an email address, please enter it. This is not required.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Please enter your home address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Please enter your phone number (if you have one).
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): If required, please enter your date of birth.
* **Sensor Type** (`sensor_type`, `select_one`, optional): Please select whether you have indoor Air Sensors or not.
* **Sensor Location** (`sensor_location`, `select_one`, optional): Please select where your indoor Air Sensors are located.
* **Activation Agreement** (`activation_agreement`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select whether you agree to the activation of your indoor Air Sensors. 
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, `date`, optional): Please select the date of your consent.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have provided accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please reach out to your administrator.
* Please note that the activation of indoor Air Sensors is required for data collection and analysis.
* Ensure you have read and understood the terms of consent before completing this form.
