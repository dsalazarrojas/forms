# Probation Records Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Probation Records Release Form is designed to allow authorized individuals to release a record from the probation system. This form is typically used in situations where a record needs to be removed from the system for the purpose of correcting inaccuracies or providing access to information for the record owner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Ensure that you have the proper authorization to release the record.
3. Review each field carefully and answer truthfully.
4. Submit the form only when you are certain that the information is correct and you have the necessary authorization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Individual Authorizing** (ID 1, text, optional): Enter your name and title if you are the individual authorized to release the record.
* **Name** (ID 2, text, optional): Enter the full name of the record owner.
* **Date** (ID 3, date, optional): Enter the date you are releasing the record.
* **Time** (ID 4, time, optional): Enter the time you are releasing the record.
* **Record Type** (ID 5, select multiple, optional): Choose the type(s) of record being released.
* **Reason** (ID 6, text, optional): Enter a brief explanation for the record release.
* **Email** (ID 7, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (ID 8, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Record Release** (ID 9, select one, optional): Choose 'Yes' to release the record, or 'No' if you are not authorized or have not completed the form correctly.
* **Notes** (ID 10, note, optional): Enter any additional information or comments about the record release.
* **Submit** (ID 11, text, optional): Enter your name and title, as well as the date and time of the submission.
* **Cancel** (ID 12, text, optional): Do not use this field; it's only here for navigation purposes.
