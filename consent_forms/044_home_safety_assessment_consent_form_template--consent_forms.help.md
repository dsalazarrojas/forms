# Home Safety Assessment Consent Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from residents or occupants in a home or building for assessment and evaluation purposes. This information will be used to identify potential risks or hazards and ensure the safety and well-being of occupants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as the resident or occupant.
2. Enter the property address where you live or stay.
3. Provide any contact information you'd like to be reached at, such as your phone number or email.
4. Select your preferred contact method (e.g., email, phone call, text message).
5. Fill in the date you would like the safety assessment to take place.
6. If you would like to be contacted by a specific person, please specify their name.
7. If applicable, choose a contact relationship with the person you would like to be contacted by (e.g., owner, tenant, other).
8. Finally, please indicate your consent to participate in the safety assessment by selecting one or more of the options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Resident Name**: Enter your full name as the resident or occupant of the property.
* **Property Address**: Enter the address of the property you live or stay in.
* **Contact Information**: Provide any contact information you'd like to be reached at, such as your phone number or email.
* **Contact Method**: Select your preferred contact method. You can choose from:
	+ First Name
	+ Last Name
	+ Phone Number
	+ Email
	+ Address
* **Safety Check Date**: Enter the date you would like the safety assessment to take place. Please use the standard date format (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Contact Person**: If you would like to be contacted by a specific person, enter their name here.
* **Contact Relationship**: If you would like to be contacted by a specific person, choose their relationship with you (e.g., owner, tenant, other).
* **Consent Given**: Indicate your consent to participate in the safety assessment by selecting one or more of the options. You can choose:
	+ True
	+ False

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate contact information so that we can reach you effectively.
* If you have any special requests or concerns, please let us know.
* By filling out this form, you are giving your consent to participate in the safety assessment.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
