# Third Party Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Third Party Authorization Form is a document that grants permission for a third party to act on behalf of an individual or organization. This form is used to establish a clear understanding and agreement between the individual or organization and the third party.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate information.
2. Read and understand the terms and conditions of the form before signing.
3. Sign and date the form to indicate your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter the first name of the individual or organization granting permission.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter the last name of the individual or organization granting permission.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter the date of birth of the individual granting permission.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the individual or organization granting permission.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter the contact number of the individual or organization granting permission.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the email of the individual or organization granting permission.
* **Power of Attorney** (`power_of_attorney`, `select_one`, required): Select 'True' if you grant power of attorney to the third party, 'False' otherwise.
* **Entity Name** (`entity_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the entity (individual or organization) you are granting permission to.
* **Entity Type** (`entity_type`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Individual' or 'Organization' to indicate the type of entity.
* **Relationship** (`relationship`, `text`, required): Describe the relationship between you and the entity (individual or organization).
* **Account Number** (`account_number`, `text`, required): Enter the account number associated with the entity (individual or organization).
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date on which this form was signed.
* **Signature Time** (`signature_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time on which this form was signed.
* **Signed By** (`signed_by`, `select_multiple`, required): Select 'First Signer' or 'Second Signer' to indicate who signed the form.
* **Signed By Title** (`signed_by_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the individual or organization that signed the form.
* **Signers Title** (`signers_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the individual or organization granting permission.
* **Signers Address** (`signers_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the individual or organization granting permission.
* **Signers Contact Number** (`signers_contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter the contact number of the individual or organization granting permission.
* **Signers Email** (`signers_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email of the individual or organization granting permission.
* **Third Party Authorization Form** (`third_party_authorization_form`, `note`, required): This is a note field for the form, but it's not editable.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date on which this form was completed.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Enter the time on which this form was completed.
* **Signers Entity Name** (`signers_entity_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the entity (individual or organization) granting permission.
* **Signers Entity Type** (`signers_entity_type`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Individual' or 'Organization' to indicate the type of entity.
* **Signers Entity Address** (`signers_entity_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the entity (individual or organization) granting permission.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have filled out all required fields before signing the form.
* Read and understand the terms and conditions of the form before signing.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification before proceeding.
