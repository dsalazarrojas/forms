# Demographic Information & Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic information and consent from participants. It is a multi-page form that requires filling out demographic details and giving consent for participation. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by filling out the demographic information pages, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. On the next page, select your ethnicity from the dropdown menu.
3. Continue filling out your education level, country, language, and income if applicable.
4. If you have an occupation or other relevant details, please feel free to provide it.
5. On the next page, provide consent for the survey.
6. Fill out the consent details, including the date, time, and location.
7. Finally, sign and date the last page to confirm your participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Demographic Information
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Your name is the primary identifier for your demographic information. Please fill out your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
### Page 2: Demographic Information
* **Ethnicity** (`ethnicity`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your ethnicity from the options provided.
### Page 3: Demographic Information
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose your highest level of education completed.
* **Country** (`country`, `select_one`, required): Select your country of origin or current residence.
* **Language** (`language`, `select_one`, required): Choose your primary language.
### Page 4: Demographic Information
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Your phone number is your contact information. Please fill out a valid phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
### Page 5: Demographic Information
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Your time of participation is the time you complete this form.
### Page 6: Demographic Information
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, `text`, required): If you have an occupation, please fill it out. Otherwise, leave it blank.
* **Income** (`income`, `number`, required): Enter your annual income.
### Page 7: Consent
* **Consent** (`consent`, `text`, required): This is a note field for consent. Please type your consent statement.
### Page 8: Consent
* **Participant ID** (`participant_id`, `text`, required): This is a unique identifier for your participation. You will use this to access your participant portal.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required): This is a note field for any additional comments or details.
### Page 9: Consent
* **Survey Consent** (`survey_consent`, `text`, required): This is a text field for consent. Please type your consent statement.
* **Survey Consent Date** (`survey_consent_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are giving consent for the survey.
* **Survey Consent Time** (`survey_consent_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you are giving consent for the survey.
* **Survey Consent Location** (`survey_consent_location`, `text`, required): Fill out the location where you are giving consent for the survey.
* **Survey Consent Note** (`survey_consent_note`, `text`, required): This is a note field for any additional comments or details.
### Page 10: Consent
* **Other Consent** (`survey_consent_other_consent`, `text`, required): This is a text field for any other consent details.
* **Other Consent Note** (`survey_consent_other_consent_note`, `text`, required): This is a note field for any additional comments or details.
### Page 11: Signature
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, `text`, required): Sign your name to confirm your participation.
* **Participant Signature Date** (`participant_signature_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you sign this document.
* **Participant Signature Location** (`participant_signature_location`, `text`, required): Fill out the location where you sign this document.

## Tips
Please fill out this form accurately and truthfully. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
