# Body Piercing Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information and consent from clients before undergoing a body piercing procedure. It ensures that clients are aware of the risks, responsibilities, and expectations associated with body piercing.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2.  Review each field carefully and select the correct options.
3.  Ensure you answer each question truthfully.
4.  If you're unsure about any question or have questions, please ask your piercer for clarification.
5.  Once you've completed the form, review it with your piercer before the procedure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Date of Birth** (`client_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY or DD/MM/YY.
*   **Email Address** (`client_email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted for future communications.
*   **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, text, required): Enter a phone number where you can be reached during the piercing process and aftercare.
*   **Address** (`client_address`, text, required): Enter your address for contact and follow-up purposes.
*   **Piercer Name** (`piercer_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of your piercer.
*   **Location of Piercing** (`piercing_location`, select_one, required): Choose the location of the piercing from the options provided (Earlobe, Ear Cartilage, Nostril, Septum, Lip, Tongue, Nipple, Navel, Genital, Other).
*   **Type of Piercing** (`piercing_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of piercing from the options provided (Single Piercing, Multiple Piercings, Surface Piercing, Dermal Implant, Other).
*   **Type of Jewelry** (`jewelry_type`, text, required): Describe the type of jewelry you prefer, including the material and style.
*   **I understand the piercing procedure** (`procedure_understanding`, select_one, required): Select the option that reflects your understanding of the procedure (Yes, fully understand, Yes, mostly understand, Partially understand, Do not understand).
*   **I acknowledge the risks of piercing** (`risks_acknowledged`, select_one, required): Select the option that reflects your understanding of the potential risks (Yes, fully acknowledged, Yes, mostly acknowledged, Partially acknowledged, Not acknowledged).
*   **I understand infection risks** (`infection_risks_understood`, select_one, required): Select the option that reflects your understanding of infection risks (True, Mostly, Somewhat, False).
*   **I understand rejection risks** (`rejection_risks`, select_one, required): Select the option that reflects your understanding of rejection risks (True, Mostly, Somewhat, False).
*   **I understand scarring risks** (`scarring_risks`, select_one, required): Select the option that reflects your understanding of scarring risks (True, Mostly, Somewhat, False).
*   **I have disclosed relevant conditions** (`medical_conditions_disclosed`, select_one, required): Select the option that reflects your disclosure of any relevant medical conditions (Yes - Disclosed, No - No relevant conditions, Unsure).
*   **I have disclosed medications** (`medications_disclosed`, select_one, required): Select the option that reflects your disclosure of any medications (Yes - Disclosed, No - Not taking medications, Unsure).
*   **I have disclosed allergies** (`allergies_disclosed`, select_one, required): Select the option that reflects your disclosure of any allergies (Yes - Disclosed, No - No known allergies, Unsure).
*   **Pregnancy Status** (`pregnancy_status`, select_one, required): Select the option that reflects your pregnancy status (Correct - Not Pregnant, I am Pregnant, I am Nursing, Not Applicable).
*   **I understand aftercare instructions** (`aftercare_instructions_understood`, select_one, required): Select the option that reflects your understanding of aftercare instructions (Yes, fully understood, Yes, mostly understood, Partially understood, Not understood).
*   **I understand pain levels expected** (`pain_expectation`, select_one, required): Select the option that reflects your understanding of pain levels expected (True, Mostly, Somewhat, False).
*   **I understand healing timeline** (`healing_time_understood`, select_one, required): Select the option that reflects your understanding of healing timeline (True, Mostly, Somewhat, False).
*   **I commit to proper hygiene and aftercare** (`hygiene_commitment`, select_one, required): Select the option that reflects your commitment to proper hygiene and aftercare (Yes, I Commit, No, I Do Not Commit).
*   **I consent to this piercing** (`consent_to_piercing`, select_one, required): Select the option that reflects your consent for the piercing (I Consent, I Do Not Consent).
*   **I accept liability and waive claims** (`liability_waiver`, select_one, required): Select the option that reflects your understanding of liability and waiver (I Accept and Waive, I Do Not Accept).
