# Tattoo Parlor Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tattoo Parlor Waiver Form is used to collect information about you and your medical history to ensure a safe and successful tattooing process.
## How To Complete This Form
1. **First Name**: Please enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
2. **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
3. **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
4. **Phone**: Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
5. **Address**: Enter your current address for emergency contact purposes.
6. **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
7. **Age**: Enter your age in years.
8. **Tattoo Count**: Enter the number of tattoos you have received.
9. **Last Tattoo Date**: If you have had a previous tattoo, enter the date of your last tattoo.
10. **Tattoo Location**: Enter the location of your last tattoo.
11. **Medical Condition**: If you have any medical conditions that may impact the tattooing process, describe them here.
12. **Medical Condition Description**: Provide a brief description of your medical conditions.
13. **Allergy**: If you have any allergies, list them here.
14. **Emergency Contact**: If you have an emergency contact person, list their name and phone number.
15. **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
16. **Insurance Provider**: If you have medical insurance, list your provider here.
17. **Medical History**: If you have any medical history that may impact the tattooing process, describe it here.
18. **Medical History Description**: Provide a brief description of your medical history.
19. **Medication**: If you are currently taking any medication, list them here.
20. **Medication Name**: Enter the name of your medication.
21. **Tattoo Artist**: Enter the name of the tattoo artist who will be performing your tattoo.
22. **Tattoo Artist License Number**: Enter the license number of your tattoo artist.
23. **Tattooed By**: Enter the name of the person who performed your previous tattoo.
24. **Consent**: Select all of the following that apply:
	* I understand and agree
	* I do not understand and do not agree
	* I understand but do not agree
	* I do not understand but agree
25. **Assigned To**: Enter the name of the person assigned to you for your tattoo.
