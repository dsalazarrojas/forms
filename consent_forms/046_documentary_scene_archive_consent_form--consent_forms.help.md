# Documentary Scene Archive Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain consent from a subject for recording a documentary scene, typically in a legal or investigation context. This form ensures that the subject is aware of the recording and its purpose, and that the scene is witnessed and recorded accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter a unique ID for the form.
2. Fill in the subject's first name.
3. Fill in the subject's last name.
4. Enter the subject's email address.
5. Provide the subject's address (optional).
6. Select a date and time for the consent.
7. Enter the contact number of the person recording the scene.
8. Choose a witness (at least one) to verify the recording.
9. Add any additional notes or comments regarding the scene (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Id** (`form_id`, `number`, required): Enter a unique ID for this form.
* **Subject First Name** (`subject_first_name`, `text`, required): Enter the subject's first name.
* **Subject Last Name** (`subject_last_name`, `text`, required): Enter the subject's last name.
* **Subject Email** (`subject_email`, `email`, required): Enter the subject's email address.
* **Subject Address** (`subject_address`, `text`, optional): If applicable, provide the subject's address.
* **Date of Consent** (`date_of_consent`, `date`, required): Select a date for the consent to be recorded.
* **Time of Consent** (`time_of_consent`, `time`, required): Select a time for the consent to be recorded.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter the contact number of the person recording the scene.
* **Witness** (`witness`, `select_one`, required): Choose a witness to verify the recording (at least one).
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes regarding the scene (optional).
