# Family Member Terms Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Member Terms Agreement form is used to collect information and agreements from family members regarding their participation in various activities or programs. It is essential to complete the form accurately and truthfully to ensure that the individual understands their roles and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please review the relevant documents and policies related to the activity or program.
2. Answer the questions and provide the necessary information accurately and truthfully.
3. Select the correct family member(s) that will be signing the agreement.
4. Provide the date signed and date completed.
5. (Optional) Add any comments or comments related to the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Household Details** (`household_details`, text, required: false): Please provide a brief description of your household, including the number of family members and their relationships.
* **Family Member Details** (`family_member_details`, text, required: false): Please provide a detailed description of the family member(s) participating in the activity or program.
* **Expectations** (`expectations`, text, required: false): Please describe the expected behavior and responsibilities of the family member(s) participating in the activity or program.
* **Signatures** (`signatures`, select_one, required: false): Please select the family member(s) that will be signing the agreement. The options are Family Member 1, Family Member 2, Family Member 3, Family Member 4, and Family Member 5.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required: false): Please provide the date the agreement was signed.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, date, required: false): Please provide the date the agreement was completed or reviewed.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, text, required: false): Please provide the name of the assigned user who will be responsible for the family member's participation in the activity or program.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Please add any additional comments or notes related to the agreement.

Note: Please review the relevant documents and policies before completing this form to ensure accuracy and understanding.
