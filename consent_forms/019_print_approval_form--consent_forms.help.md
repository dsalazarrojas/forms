# Print Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Print Approval Form is a document used to obtain approval for printing a specific material or document. Authorized personnel review and approve this form to verify that the document or material meets the required standards and specifications before proceeding with printing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Ensure that you are submitting this form for a legitimate print approval request.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.
4. If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Name** (`form_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the document or material to be printed.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the customer or individual requesting the print approval.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the customer or individual requesting the print approval.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the customer or individual requesting the print approval.
* **Approval Note** (`approval_note`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the print approval.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the approval is being given.
* **Approval Time** (`approval_time`, time, optional): Enter the time on which the approval is being given.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the approval from the options: Approved, Not Approved, or Pending.
* **Approval Comments** (`approval_comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments about the approval decision.
