# Therapy Feedback Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Therapy Feedback Consent Form is a form used to collect feedback from patients about their therapy sessions. This form is used to gather information about the patient's experiences, thoughts, and feelings about their therapy sessions, which helps therapists to improve their practice and provide better services to their patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please enter your name as the patient.
2. Enter your contact number (mobile or phone number).
3. Enter your email address.
4. Select the date and time of your therapy session.
5. Enter the name of your therapist.
6. Enter the date of your therapy session.
7. Select the time of your therapy session.
8. Select your consent for the feedback. You can choose either "True", "False", or "Undecided".
9. If you wish, you can provide any additional comments about your therapy session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the patient.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, number, required): Enter your mobile or phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Date and Time** (`date_time`, date, required): Select the date and time of your therapy session.
* **Therapist Name** (`therapist_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your therapist.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your therapy session.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, required): Select the time of your therapy session.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_multiple, required): Select your consent for the feedback.
	+ "True": You agree to provide feedback.
	+ "False": You do not agree to provide feedback.
	+ "Undecided": You are unsure about providing feedback.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments about your therapy session.
