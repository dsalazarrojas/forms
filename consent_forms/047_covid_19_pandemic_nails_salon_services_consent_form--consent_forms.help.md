# COVID 19 Pandemic Nails Salon Services Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The COVID 19 Pandemic Nails Salon Services Consent Form is designed to collect necessary information from clients before providing them with nail salon services during the COVID-19 pandemic. This form will help the salon understand the client's health status, ensure their safety, and gather necessary consent for service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Enter your Full Name**: Type your name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
2. **Enter your Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for any last-minute updates or cancellations.
3. **Enter your Email Address**: Type in your email address where we can contact you regarding your appointment and any updates.
4. **Choose an Appointment Date**: Select a date and time that works best for you to schedule your appointment.
5. **Choose a Service**: Select all the nail services you want to receive during your visit.
6. **Indicate COVID-19 Status**: Answer the questions regarding your COVID-19 symptoms, exposure, and vaccination status.
7. **Acknowledge Pre-Existing Conditions**: Answer if you have any pre-existing health conditions.
8. **Describe Conditions (if Applicable)**: Provide details about any pre-existing conditions if you answered yes to the previous question.
9. **Indicate Allergies to Nail Products**: Answer if you have any allergies to nail products.
10. **Describe Allergies (if Applicable)**: Provide details about any allergies to nail products if you answered yes to the previous question.
11. **Understand COVID-19 Risks**: Confirm that you understand the risks associated with COVID-19 transmission.
12. **Acknowledge Salon Safety Measures**: Confirm that you have read and will follow the salon's safety measures to minimize the risk of COVID-19 transmission.
13. **Consent to Services**: Confirm that you consent to receive nail salon services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Client Information --**: This section is where you can enter your personal contact information, including your full name, phone number, and email address. This is important for us to contact you with any last-minute updates or cancellations.
* **Full Name**: Type your name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where we can reach you quickly in case of any changes or updates to your appointment.
* **Email Address**: Type in your email address where we can send you updates and reminders about your appointment.
* **Appointment Date**: Select a date and time that works best for your visit to our salon.
* **Appointment Time**: Choose a time that suits you best for your appointment.
* **Service Requested**: Select all the nail services you want to receive during your visit.
* **COVID-19 symptoms in past 14 days**: Answer if you have experienced any COVID-19 symptoms in the past 14 days.
* **Symptoms if Present**: If you answered yes to the previous question, describe any symptoms you have experienced.
* **Temperature above 100.4F today**: Answer if your temperature is above 100.4F today.
* **Exposed to COVID-19 in past 14 days**: Answer if you have been exposed to someone with COVID-19 in the past 14 days.
* **Tested positive for COVID-19 in past 10 days**: Answer if you have tested positive for COVID-19 in the past 10 days.
* **Vaccination Status**: Answer your current vaccination status.
* **Pre-existing health conditions**: Answer if you have any pre-existing health conditions.
* **Describe conditions if Applicable**: If you answered yes to the previous question, provide details about any pre-existing conditions.
* **Allergies to nail products**: Answer if you have any allergies to nail products.
* **Describe allergies (if Applicable)**: If you answered yes to the previous question, provide details about any allergies to nail products.
* **Understand COVID-19 risks**: Confirm that you understand the risks associated with COVID-19 transmission.
* **Acknowledge salon safety measures**: Confirm that you have read and will follow the salon's safety measures to minimize the risk of COVID-19 transmission.
* **I consent to receive nail salon services**: Confirm that you consent to receive nail salon services.
