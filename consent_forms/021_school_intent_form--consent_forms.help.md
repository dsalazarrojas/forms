# School Intent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about the school's intent, which is a crucial piece of information for various administrative and operational purposes within the institution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant information for each field as accurately as possible.
2. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required") as these are essential for processing your request.
3. Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Institution
• **Institution** (text, required): Enter the name of the institution (school) you are representing or affiliated with.

### Student Count
• **Student Count** (number, optional): If applicable, provide the estimated number of students affected by the request or action.

### Date
• **Date** (date, optional): If relevant, enter the date of the event or occurrence related to the school intent.

### Time
• **Time** (time, optional): If applicable, provide the time of the event or occurrence related to the school intent.

### Notes
• **Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional information or context that may be helpful for the processing of your request.

## Tips
- Be sure to fill in all required fields accurately to ensure your request is processed efficiently.
- If you're unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities or support personnel for assistance.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
