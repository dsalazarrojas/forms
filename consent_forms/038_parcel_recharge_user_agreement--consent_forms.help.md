# Parcel Recharge User Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parcel Recharge User Agreement is a form designed to collect user agreement and consent for parcel recharge services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details: First name*, Last name*, Address*, Email*, Phone*.
2. Schedule your appointment: Date*, Time*.
3. Confirm your details: Confirm Date*, Confirm Time*, Confirm Signature*.
4. Provide any additional information: Notes*.
5. Confirm your agreement: Confirm Agreement* and choose one of the options ("Yes" or "No").
6. Read and agree on terms: Terms of Agreement*.
7. Confirm Recharge: Agree on Recharge* and choose one of the options ("Yes" or "No").

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (First name, text, required*): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (Last name, text, required*): Enter your last name.
* **Address** (Address, text, required*): Enter your address.
* **Email** (Email, email, required*): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required*): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (Date, date, required*): Choose the date for your appointment.
* **Time** (Time, time, required*): Choose the time for your appointment.
* **Confirm Date** (Confirm Date, date, required*): Re-enter the date you chose for your appointment.
* **Confirm Time** (Confirm Time, time, required*): Re-enter the time you chose for your appointment.
* **Confirm Signature** (Confirm Signature, text, required*): Provide a physical or electronic signature.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, required*): Provide any additional information relevant to your appointment.
* **Confirm Notes** (Confirm Notes, note, required*): Re-enter your notes for your appointment.
* **Confirm Agreement** (Confirm Agreement, select_one, required*): Choose "Yes" if you agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Confirm Agreement Note** (Confirm Agreement Note, note, required*): Provide a reason for your agreement or disagreement.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or information.
* **Terms of Agreement** (Terms of Agreement, note, required*): Read and agree on the terms and conditions.
* **Agree on Recharge** (Agree on Recharge, select_one, required*): Choose "Yes" if you agree to the parcel recharge terms.
* **Agree on Recharge Note** (Agree on Recharge Note, note, required*): Provide a reason for your agreement or disagreement.
