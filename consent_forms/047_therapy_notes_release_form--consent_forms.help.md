# Therapy Notes Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information and obtain consent from clients for the release of their therapy notes. It is a crucial document for ensuring that clients are aware of and agree to the sharing of their personal data and therapy notes with third parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's information on page 1, including their name, date of birth, and consent type.
2. Select the authorization(s) for the release of their therapy notes on page 3.
3. Sign and date the form on pages 6 and 7.
4. Enter your signature and reason for signing on page 9.
5. Confirm that you have reviewed and understood the contents of this form on pages 10 and 11.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (page 1)**: Enter the client's name in this field. This field is required as it is a necessary identifier for the client.
* **Notes (page 2)**: In this field, enter any relevant notes or observations about the client's therapy sessions. This field is required to provide context for the release of the therapy notes.
* **Authorization (page 3)**: Select the authorization(s) for the release of the client's therapy notes. These options are:
	+ Client: Authorize the release of notes to the client themselves.
	+ Parent: Authorize the release of notes to the client's parent or legal guardian.
	+ Guardian: Authorize the release of notes to the client's guardian or power of attorney.
* **Date of Birth (page 4)**: Enter the client's date of birth in this field. This field is required to verify the client's identity.
* **Consent Type (page 5)**: Select the type of consent the client is giving for the release of their therapy notes. These options are:
	+ Informed: The client has read and understands the contents of this form.
	+ Implied: The client has not read the contents of this form, but agrees to the release of their therapy notes.
	+ Express: The client has explicitly agreed to the release of their therapy notes.
* **Client Signature (page 6)**: Sign and date this field to confirm that the client has acknowledged and agreed to the release of their therapy notes. This field is required.
* **Therapist Signature (page 7)**: Sign and date this field to confirm that you, as the therapist, have obtained the client's consent and understand the contents of this form. This field is required.
* **Date Signed (page 8)**: Enter the date you signed this form. This field is required.
* **Reason for Signature (page 9)**: Enter the reason why you signed this form. This field is required.
* **Client Acknowledgement (page 10)**: Confirm that you, as the client, have reviewed and understood the contents of this form. This field is required.
* **Therapist Acknowledgement (page 11)**: Confirm that you, as the therapist, have reviewed and understood the contents of this form. This field is required.

## Tips
Remember to carefully review and understand the contents of this form before completing it. Ensure that you, as the client, have read and understood the contents of this form before signing it.
