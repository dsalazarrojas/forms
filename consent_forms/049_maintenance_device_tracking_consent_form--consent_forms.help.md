# Maintenance Device Tracking Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Maintenance Device Tracking Consent Form is used to obtain consent from users for the tracking of maintenance devices. This form helps administrators or managers to record user consent for device tracking activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Review the form carefully**: Take a moment to read the form and understand the questions asked.
2. **Fill in your user information**: Fill in your name and any other relevant details in the first field "User Information".
3. **Select your consent option**: Choose whether you consent to tracking of devices by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Consent" field.
4. **List the devices you consent to**: If you select "Yes" for consent, list the devices you are willing to have tracked in the "Devices" field.
5. **Provide any additional tracking data**: If needed, provide any additional information about the tracking data you want to be collected in the "Tracking Data" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (Page 1, Text): This field is used to collect your name and any other relevant details. Please fill in this field accurately.
* **Consent** (Page 2, Select One):
	+ **Yes**: You are consenting to the tracking of devices.
	+ **No**: You are not consenting to the tracking of devices.
* **Devices** (Page 3, Select Multiple):
	+ List the devices you are willing to have tracked, separated by commas.
* **Tracking Data** (Page 4, Text): If you need to provide any additional information about the tracking data, fill this field accordingly.
