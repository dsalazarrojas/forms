# Student Success Story Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Success Story Release Form is designed to collect stories of students who have achieved outstanding academic or personal accomplishments. The form is used to gather details about the students' experiences and obtain their consent to share their story with the public.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your student ID, name, and school name in the respective fields.
2. Share your remarkable story in the "Story" field.
3. If you are comfortable with your story being published, select the publication options that suit your preferences.
4. If you are not comfortable with your story being published, select "No" for the publication permissions.
5. Choose the medium where you would like your story to be published (e.g., Radio, Newspaper, Social Media, Magazine, Book, or Other).
6. If you want to specify a particular date for publication, enter it in the "Publication Date" field. Otherwise, leave it blank.
7. Finally, sign the form to authenticate your identity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, required): Enter your student ID number.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your school.
* **Story** (`story`, text, required): Share your remarkable story, describing your accomplishments and experiences.
* **Publication Permissions** (`publication_permissions`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if you are comfortable with your story being published. Select "No" if you are not.
* **Publication Medium** (`publication_medium`, select_one, required): Choose the medium where you would like your story to be published (e.g., Radio, Newspaper, Social Media, Magazine, Book, or Other).
* **Publication Date** (`publication_date`, date, optional): If you want to specify a particular date for publication, enter it here.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required): Sign the form to authenticate your identity.
