# Field Trip Transportation Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Field Trip Transportation Authorization Form is a general authorization form used to collect necessary details about students and vehicle information for field trips.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with the required information about your student.
2. Provide your emergency contact information.
3. Answer questions about the vehicle transporting your child(ren), including vehicle type, capacity, and registration information.
4. Review and confirm the details of your student's participation in the field trip.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information** (`student_information`, text, required): Please enter your student's name and any other relevant student details.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter your name as a parent or guardian of your student.
* **Parent Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Provide your phone number for emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of an alternate emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone Number** (`emergency_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the alternate emergency contact.
* **School Permission** (`school_permission`, number, optional): Enter the number of students you are authorizing for the field trip.
* **Date of Trip** (`date_of_trip`, date, optional): Enter the date of the field trip.
* **Time of Trip** (`time_of_trip`, time, optional): Enter the time of the trip.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of vehicle transporting your child(ren), such as Bus, Car, or School Bus.
* **Vehicle Information** (`vehicle_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional vehicle details.
* **Driver Information** (`driver_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the vehicle's driver.
* **Student Count** (`student_count`, number, optional): Enter the number of students you are authorizing for the field trip.
* **School Permission 2** (`school_permission_2`, number, optional): Enter the school's permission for the field trip.
* **Student Information 2** (`student_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional student information.
* **Student List** (`student_list`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the students participating in the field trip.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional emergency contact information.
* **Vehicle License Number** (`vehicle_license_number`, text, optional): Enter the vehicle's license number.
* **Vehicle Registration Date** (`vehicle_registration_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the vehicle's registration.
* **Vehicle Registration Time** (`vehicle_registration_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the vehicle's registration.
* **Vehicle Capacity** (`vehicle_capacity`, number, optional): Enter the vehicle's capacity.
* **Vehicle Condition** (`vehicle_condition`, text, optional): Enter any additional vehicle condition information.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, optional): Enter the vehicle's make.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, optional): Enter the vehicle's model.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, optional): Enter the vehicle's year.
