# Notary Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from a notary about their acknowledgement of a document or signature.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form as instructed below.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Notary Acknowledgement Form** (`notary_acknowledgement_form`, text, optional): This field is where you should enter a brief description of the document or signature you are acknowledging.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, optional): Enter the date you signed the document or witnessed the signature.
* **Notary Signature** (`notary_signature`, select, optional): Select one of the options (1, 2, or 3) to indicate your level of acknowledgement (e.g., "1" for "I acknowledge").
* **Notary Stamp** (`notary_stamp`, select multiple, optional): Select all applicable options (1, 2, or 3) to indicate any stamps or certifications you have applied.
* **Notary Comment** (`notary_comment`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information related to the acknowledgement.
* **Notary Signature Verified** (`notary_signature_verified`, select multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if the signature has been verified, otherwise select "No".
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, optional): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`, time, optional): Enter the time the form was submitted.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, date, optional): Enter the date the form was completed.
* **Time Completed** (`time_completed`, time, optional): Enter the time the form was completed.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your selections in the select fields.
* If you have any issues, please contact your administrator for assistance.
