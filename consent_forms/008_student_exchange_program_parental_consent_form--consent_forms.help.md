# Student Exchange Program Parental Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for parents to provide their consent for their child to participate in the Student Exchange Program. It collects necessary information for emergency contacts, addresses, and signatures for both the student and the parent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your information, including your child's school name, student's name, and your name as a parent.
2. Provide emergency contact information for your child, including the name and relationship of the emergency contact person.
3. Add your address, city, state, zip code, phone number, and email (if applicable).
4. Select a date and time for your signature.
5. Sign this form by entering your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **school name** (school name, text, required): Enter your child's school name.
* **student name** (student name, text, required): Enter your child's name.
* **parent name** (parent name, text, required): Enter your name as a parent.
* **emergency contact name** (emergency contact name, text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **relationship** (relationship, text, required): Enter the relationship between the emergency contact person and your child (e.g., grandparent, aunt/uncle, etc.).
* **address 1** (address 1, text, optional): Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
* **city** (city, text, optional): Enter the city of the emergency contact person's address.
* **state** (state, text, optional): Enter the state of the emergency contact person's address.
* **zip code** (zip code, text, optional): Enter the zip code of the emergency contact person's address.
* **phone number** (phone number, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **email** (email, email, optional): Enter the email of the emergency contact person.
* **date** (date, date, optional): Enter the date for your signature.
* **time** (time, time, optional): Enter the time for your signature.
* **emergency contact name 2** (emergency contact name 2, text, optional): Enter the name of the second emergency contact person.
* **phone number 2** (phone number 2, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the second emergency contact person.
* **signature** (signature, text, required): Sign this form to provide your consent.
* **signature parent** (signature parent, text, required): Sign this form to confirm your signature.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (Emergency Contact Relationship, text, optional): Enter the relationship between the second emergency contact person and your child.
* **signature date** (signature date, date, required): Enter the date of your signature.
* **signature time** (signature time, time, required): Enter the time of your signature.
* **Parent Signature Date** (Parent Signature Date, date, required): Enter the date of your signature as a parent.
* **Parent Signature Time** (Parent Signature Time, time, required): Enter the time of your signature as a parent.
* **address** (address, text, optional): Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact City** (Emergency Contact City, text, optional): Enter the city of the emergency contact person's address.
* **Emergency Contact State** (Emergency Contact State, text, optional): Enter the state of the emergency contact person's address.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Please review all fields carefully to ensure accurate information is provided.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the form administrator.
