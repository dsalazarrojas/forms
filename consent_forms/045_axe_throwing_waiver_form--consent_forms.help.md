# Axe Throwing Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Axe Throwing Waiver Form is a document that ensures you, as an axe-throwing participant, understand and agree to the terms and conditions of taking part in axe-throwing activities. It's essential to complete this form carefully and honestly to ensure your safety and the safety of others.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form in its entirety, as indicated by the required fields.
2. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
3. Sign the form with your digital signature.
4. If you are under 18 years old, a parent or guardian must sign the form on your behalf.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`participant_full_name`): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`participant_dob`): Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address** (`participant_email`): Please enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`participant_phone`): Please enter your phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Name and Phone** (`emergency_contact_axe`): Please enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact in case of any incidents.
* **Emergency Contact Name and Phone is for** (`emergency_contact_axe`): If there is an emergency, we will contact this person first.
* **-- Waiver and Release of Liability --** (`section_waiver_terms`): Please read each statement carefully and understand the implications.
* **Risk Assumption** (`risk_assumption_axe`): Select "Yes, I assume all risks" to indicate that you understand and assume all risks associated with axe throwing, including physical injury or death.
* **Liability Release** (`liability_release_axe`): Select "Yes, I release liability" to indicate that you release the venue from all liability for any injury, loss, or damage sustained.
* **Safety Briefing** (`rules_briefing_axe`): Select "Yes, I understand" to indicate that you have received and understand the safety briefing.
* **Emergency Medical Consent** (`medical_consent_axe`): Select "Yes, I consent" to allow us to provide emergency medical treatment if required.
* **Under the Influence** (`influence_check_axe`): Select "I am sober" to ensure that you are not under the influence of drugs or alcohol.
* **Photo and Video Release** (`photo_release_axe`): Select "Yes, I give permission" to allow us to use your image for marketing purposes.
* **Participant Digital Signature** (`participant_signature_axe`): Please type your full name to certify that you have read and understood the form.
* **Date of Signature** (`signature_date_axe`): Enter the date you sign this form.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature** (`parent_guardian_signature_axe`): If you are under 18 years old, a parent or guardian must sign this form on your behalf.
* **Residential Address** (`address_participant`): Please enter your full residential address, including street, city, state, and zip code.
