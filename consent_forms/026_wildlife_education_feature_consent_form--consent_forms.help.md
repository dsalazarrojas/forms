# Wildlife Education Feature Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect user consent for participation in wildlife education features.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information (email and phone number) in the relevant fields.
2. Provide your date of birth.
3. Add any additional notes or comments about your participation.
4. Select one option for "Media consent" and one or more options for "Feature consent".
5. Review your submission and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Email address**: Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted for further information or to schedule appointments.
* **Phone number**: Enter a contact number where you can be reached.
* **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Note**: Add any additional comments or information about your participation.
* **Select one**: Choose one option for media consent.
	+ Option 1: I agree to be photographed or recorded.
	+ Option 2: I do not agree to be photographed or recorded.
* **Select multiple**: Choose one or more options for feature consent.
	+ Option 1: I agree to participate in all feature activities.
	+ Option 2: I agree to participate in some feature activities.
* **Date and time**: Enter the date and time of your availability.
* **Time**: Enter the time of your availability.
* **Decimal number**: Enter a decimal number (e.g., 3.5).
* **Integer number**: Enter an integer number (e.g., 5).
* **Media consent**: Select one option for media consent.
* **Feature consent**: Select one or more options for feature consent.
