# Home Renovation Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Home Renovation Waiver Form is a legal document that outlines the terms and conditions of a home renovation project. It is used to establish a clear understanding between the homeowner and the contractor regarding the project's scope, payment terms, and responsibilities. This form ensures that both parties are on the same page and aware of their obligations before starting the project.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Project Details section with the necessary information about the project, including the project's title, description, and any relevant dates.
2. In the Contract Terms section, specify the terms and conditions of the project, including any relevant laws, regulations, or industry standards that apply.
3. In the Payment Terms section, outline the payment schedule and any other financial details related to the project.
4. In the Responsibility section, clarify the roles and responsibilities of both the homeowner and the contractor.
5. Sign and date the form to complete it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, required/optional): This section is used to describe the project, including its title, description, and any relevant dates. Fill in this section with the details of the project, such as the project's scope, location, and expected completion date.
* **Contract Terms** (`contract_terms`, text, required/optional): This section is used to outline the terms and conditions of the project, including any relevant laws, regulations, or industry standards that apply. Be sure to include any specific details about the project's scope, duration, and payment terms.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, required/optional): This section is used to outline the payment schedule and any other financial details related to the project. Include details about the payment method, amount, and any deadlines or milestones.
* **Responsibility** (`responsibility`, text, required/optional): This section is used to clarify the roles and responsibilities of both the homeowner and the contractor. Be sure to specify who is responsible for what tasks, including any specific duties or obligations.
* **Homeowner Signature** (`homeowner_signature`, text, required/optional): This section is used for the homeowner to sign and date the form, indicating their agreement to the project terms.
* **Contractor Signature** (`contractor_signature`, text, required/optional): This section is used for the contractor to sign and date the form, indicating their agreement to the project terms.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): This section is used to record the date that the form is signed.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): This section is used to record the time that the form is signed.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): This section is used to record any additional comments or notes about the project.
