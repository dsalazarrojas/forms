# Facial Recognition Entry Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user consent for facial recognition, which can be used for various purposes such as security, convenience, or research. Please fill out the form accurately and truthfully to ensure that your consent and information are correctly recorded.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the form completely and truthfully. You can skip any non-mandatory fields if not applicable.
2.  For each field, ensure that you understand the purpose and options available before making your selection or entering your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Information** (`user_information`, `text`, not required): This field is for you to provide a brief description of yourself.
*   **Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, not required): Please select whether you grant or deny access to your facial recognition data. You may choose 'Yes' to grant access or 'No' to deny access.
*   **Facial Recognition Access** (`facial_recognition_access`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select the purpose of access you grant or deny. You may choose one or multiple options depending on your consent status.
*   **Purpose of Access** (`purpose_of_access`, `text`, not required): If you grant access, please describe the purpose of the access you are giving.
*   **How did you learn about this form?** (`how_did_you_learn`, `text`, not required): If you wish to provide additional information on how you learned about this form, please enter it here.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, not required): If you have any additional comments or questions, please enter them here.
*   **Submitter Information** (`submitter_info`, `text`, not required): This field is for the submitter to provide their information, such as name and contact details.
*   **E-mail** (`email`, `email`, not required): Please enter your email address.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, not required): Please enter your phone number.
*   **Name** (`submitter_name`, `text`, not required): Enter your name for contact purposes.

## Tips
- Always fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
- If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to contact the authorities for clarification.
- Ensure that you understand the purpose and options available before making any selection.
- If you have any additional comments or questions, please enter them in the Comments field.
