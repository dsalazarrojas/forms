# Senior Health Program Video Interview Consent - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Senior Health Program Video Interview Consent form is used to gather information from participants for video interviews conducted for the Senior Health Program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your First name and Last name in the First name and Last name fields, respectively.
2. Enter your email address in the Email field.
3. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
4. Select a date and time for the video interview in the Video Interview Date and Video Interview Time fields, respectively.
5. Check the Schedule Video Interview box to indicate whether you are available for a video interview.
6. Choose to give your consent for the video interview by selecting one or both of the Consent options.
7. Add any additional notes in the Notes field.
8. Click the Submit button to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`Last name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Video Interview Date** (`Video Interview Date`, `date`, required): Select a date for the video interview.
* **Video Interview Time** (`Video Interview Time`, `time`, required): Select a time for the video interview.
* **Schedule Video Interview** (`Schedule Video Interview`, `select_one`, required): Check to indicate whether you are available for a video interview.
* **Consent** (`Consent`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose one or both of the options to give your consent for the video interview.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, required): Add any additional notes about your video interview.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, `text`, required): Click to submit your form.

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter accurate information in all fields.
* Make sure to check the Schedule Video Interview box if you are available for a video interview.
* Ensure that you select one or both of the options in the Consent field to give your explicit consent.
* If you have any additional notes, use the Notes field to enter them.
