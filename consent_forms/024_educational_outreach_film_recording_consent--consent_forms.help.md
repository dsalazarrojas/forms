# educational_outreach_film_recording_consent - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Educational Outreach Film Recording Consent" form is designed to obtain permission from participants to capture educational footage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide the participant's information in the "Participant Information" section.
2. Answer the "Participant Permission" question by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
3. Indicate the type of filming you would like to conduct (Video, Audio, Still images, or All of the above) in the "Film Recording" section.
4. Enter the filming location in the "Filming Location" section.
5. Provide the filming date and time in the "Filming Date" and "Filming Time" sections, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`): 
    • First Name (`participant_info_part_1`): Enter the participant's first name.
    • Last Name (`participant_info_part_2`): Enter the participant's last name.
    • Email (`participant_info_part_4`): Enter the participant's email address.
    • Phone (`participant_info_part_5`): Enter the participant's phone number.
    • Address (`participant_info_part_6`): Enter the participant's address.
* **Participant Permission** (`participant_permission`): Select 'Yes' to give your consent for educational filming, or 'No' if you do not consent.
* **Film Recording** (`film_recording`): Indicate the type(s) of footage you would like to capture (Video, Audio, Still images, or All of the above).
* **Filming Location** (`filming_location`): Enter the location where the filming will take place.
* **Filming Date** (`filming_date`): Provide the date of the filming event.
* **Filming Time** (`filming_time`): Provide the time of the filming event.
