# Event Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Waiver Form is designed to ensure participants understand and agree to the terms and conditions of an event where they may be exposed to certain risks. This form is meant to be completed by participants before engaging with the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Read through each section carefully, ensuring you understand the questions and their relevance to the event.
2. Provide a brief description of the event in the "Brief Description of the Event" field.
3. In the "Select the Participant" field, choose the correct participant from the provided options.
4. In the "Describe the Potential Risks" field, provide a detailed description of the risks associated with the event.
5. In the "Select All Participants" field, choose all applicable participants from the provided options.
6. Answer "Yes" in the "Reviewed and Agreed" section if you have read and understood the waiver terms.
7. Answer "Yes" in the "Signed and Agreed" section if you are agreeing to the terms.
8. Answer "Yes" in the "Acknowledged and Agreed" section if you acknowledge and agree with the risks outlined.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Brief Description of the Event**: A short description of the event, its nature, and any relevant details participants should be aware of.
  * This field helps in setting the context for the event and ensuring participants understand what they are committing to.
- **Select the Participant**: Choose the participant you are completing this form for from the options provided.
  * Ensure you select the correct participant from the list.
- **Select All Participants**: Choose all applicable participants from the list.
  * This field allows you to select multiple participants if they are all applicable.
- **Describe the Potential Risks**: Provide a detailed description of the potential risks associated with the event.
  * This is an opportunity to describe any hazards or risks participants might face during or after the event.
- **Describe Additional Risks**: This field provides an additional space to mention any further risks that have not been previously described.
  * Use this to add any other risks that need to be acknowledged.
- **Reviewed and Agreed**: Answer "Yes" if you have read and understood the terms of the waiver.
  * This confirms your understanding of the event's conditions.
- **Signed and Agreed**: Answer "Yes" if you are agreeing to the terms of the waiver.
  * This indicates your commitment to the terms of the event.
- **Acknowledged and Agreed**: Answer "Yes" if you acknowledge and agree with the risks outlined.
  * This signifies your understanding and acceptance of the risks involved.
