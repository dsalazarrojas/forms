# Durable Power Of Attorney Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to grant power of attorney to a designated individual or entity to manage your financial and medical affairs. By filling out this form, you will be authorizing someone to make decisions on your behalf and act as your representative in certain situations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Provide your personal details:
	* First Name: Enter your first name.
	* Last Name: Enter your last name.
	* Address: Enter your current address.
	* City: Enter the city where you reside.
	* State: Enter the state where you reside.
	* Zip: Enter your zip code.
	* Date Of Birth: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
	* Relationship Status: Select your relationship status (Single, Married, Domestic Partnership, or Common Law Partnership).
2. Identify your beneficiary:
	* Name Of Beneficiary: Enter the name of the person or entity that will benefit from this power of attorney.
3. Select your attorney:
	* Name Of Attorney: Enter the name of the person or entity that you are granting power of attorney to.
4. Confirm your contact information:
	* Email: Enter your email address.
	* Phone: Enter your phone number.
5. Identify witnesses:
	* Name Of Witness 1: Enter the name of the first witness who will verify your identity.
	* Signature Of Witness 1: Have the witness sign their name in the provided space.
	* Name Of Witness 2: Enter the name of the second witness who will verify your identity.
	* Signature Of Witness 2: Have the witness sign their name in the provided space.
6. Confirm the document details:
	* Date: Enter the date you are signing this document.
	* Time: Enter the time you are signing this document.
	* Document Version: Enter the version number of this document.
	* Document Status: Select the status of this document (Draft, Published, Rejected, or Archived).
	* Document Number: Enter a unique number for this document.
	* Document Signed By: Enter the name of the person who signed this document.
	* Date Of Signature: Enter the date you signed this document.
	* Time Of Signature: Enter the time you signed this document.
7. Finalize the document:
	* Document Revision Date: Enter the date of any revisions made to this document.
	* Document Revision Number: Enter the number of revisions made to this document.
	* Document Revised By: Enter the name of the person who made any revisions.
	* Document Approved By: Enter the name of the person who approved this document.
	* Document Approved Date: Enter the date this document was approved.
	* Document Approved Status: Select the status of this document (Approved, Draft, Rejected, or Archived).
	* Document Version Number: Enter the version number of this document.
	* Document Signed Date: Enter the date this document was signed.
	* Document Signed By Name: Enter the name of the person who signed this document.
	* Document Signed By Title: Enter the title of the person who signed this document.
	* Document Revision Reason: Enter a reason for any revisions made to this document.
	* Document Approved By Title: Enter the title of the person who approved this document.
	* Document Signed By Name Title: Enter the title of the person who signed this document.
	* Date Of Signature Date: Enter the date you signed this document.
	* Time Of Signature Time: Enter the time you signed this document.
	* Document Signed By Issuer: Enter the name of the person who issued this document.
	* Document Signed Date Issuer: Enter the date this document was issued.
	* Document Signed By Issuer Title: Enter the title of the person who issued this document.
	* Document Revision Reason Issuer: Enter a reason for any revisions made to this document by the issuer.
	* Document Approved Status Issuer: Select the status of this document (Approved, Draft, Rejected, or Archived) by the issuer.
