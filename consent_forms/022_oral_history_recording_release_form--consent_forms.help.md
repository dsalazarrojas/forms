# oral_history_recording_release_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain the consent and agreement of participants to record their oral history for research, documentation, or other purposes. It captures essential details about the participant, the recording session, and the participant's consent, as well as space for any additional comments or requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant's details, including their name and any other relevant information.
2. Choose the recording date and time.
3. Select the location where the recording will take place.
4. Provide the interviewer's details, if applicable.
5. Sign the form to confirm your participation and consent.
6. Sign the form again to confirm your understanding and agreement with the terms.
7. Optionally, add any notes or comments in the participant notes section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details**: Enter the participant's name and any other relevant information.
* **Recording Date**: Choose the date the recording will take place.
* **Recording Time**: Select the time of the recording.
* **Recording Location**: Enter the location where the recording will take place.
* **Interviewer Details**: If applicable, provide the interviewer's name and contact information.
* **Participant Signature**: Sign your name to confirm your participation and consent.
* **Witnes Signature**: Sign below to witness your signature.
* **Participant Agreement**: Read and sign the participant agreement section to confirm your understanding and agreement with the terms.
* **Recording Consents**: Select all consents that apply to the recording.
* **Participant Notes**: Use this space to add any additional comments or requests from the participant.

## Tips
Please ensure you understand the purpose and terms of the recording before signing the form. If you have any questions or concerns, ask your interviewer or contact the form creators at form@gic.mx.
