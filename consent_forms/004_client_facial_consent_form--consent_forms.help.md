# Client Facial Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Facial Consent Form is a crucial document that helps us provide personalized and safe treatment for each client. It's essential for us to understand your medical history, preferences, and any sensitivities you may have. By filling out this form, you'll be able to give us the necessary information to tailor our services to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and take the time to understand each section and its purpose.
2. Fill out your name, date of birth, and the procedure you'd like to have performed.
3. List any known allergies or skin sensitivities you may have.
4. Provide details about any prior skin treatments you've had in the last 12 months.
5. Consent to clinical photography for treatment records.
6. Provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact person.
7. Confirm the name of the clinician performing the treatment.
8. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **client name** (`client_name`, text, required): Please enter your full legal name.
* **date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Provide your date of birth for identification and treatment planning purposes.
* **procedure to be performed** (`procedure`, select_one, required): Choose the treatment you'd like to have performed (Facial cleansing, Chemical peel, Microdermabrasion, Laser treatment, or Other).
* **allergies or skin sensitivities** (`allergies_or_sensitivities`, text, optional): List any known allergies or reactions you may have.
* **prior skin treatments** (`prior_skin_treatments`, text, optional): Include any prior skin treatments you've had in the last 12 months, such as injections, laser, or peels.
* **consent for clinical photography** (`photo_consent`, select_one, required): Check 'True' to consent to clinical photography for treatment records.
* **emergency contact name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Provide the name of someone we can contact in case of an emergency.
* **emergency contact phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **practitioner name** (`practitioner_name`, text, required): Confirm the name of the clinician performing the treatment.
* **date signed** (`date_signed`, date, required): Date and sign the form to confirm your consent.

## Tips
* Be accurate and honest when filling out this form to ensure your safety and the effectiveness of the treatment.
* If you're unsure about any section, don't hesitate to ask your clinician for clarification.
* Once completed, please return the form to us so we can review and prepare for your treatment.
