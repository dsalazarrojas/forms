# Elder Care Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Elder Care Authorization Form is used to obtain authorization from a patient's authorized caregiver for elder care services. This form is used to gather information about the patient and the authorized caregiver, including their names, contact details, and signatures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
* Select the relationship between the authorized caregiver and the patient from the "Relationship to Patient" dropdown menu.
* Answer "yes" or "no" to the question of whether you have legal guardian rights.
* Enter the authorized caregiver's name, phone number, address, city, state, and zip code in their respective fields.
* Enter the authorized caregiver's email address.
* Enter the patient's email address.
* Enter the patient's phone number.
* If necessary, add any additional comments or notes to the "Note to Authorized Caregiver" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Authorization Form Title** (`form_header`, Note, Required): This is a placeholder for the title of the form.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, Text, Required): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Relationship to Patient** (`relationship`, Select One, Required): Select the relationship between the authorized caregiver and the patient, such as "Child", "Parent", "Spouse", or "Other".
* **Do you have legal guardian rights?** (`legal_guardian`, Select One, Required): Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have legal guardian rights.
* **Authorized Caregiver Name** (`caregiver_name`, Text, Required): Enter the authorized caregiver's name.
* **Authorized Caregiver's Phone Number** (`caregiver_phone_number`, Text, Required): Enter the authorized caregiver's phone number.
* **Authorized Caregiver's Signature** (`signature`, Note, Required): Sign the form as the authorized caregiver.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, Date, Required): Enter the date on which the form was signed.
* **Time Signed** (`time_signed`, Time, Required): Enter the time on which the form was signed.
* **Authorized Caregiver's Address** (`authorized_caregiver_address`, Text, Required): Enter the authorized caregiver's address.
* **Authorized Caregiver's City** (`authorized_caregiver_city`, Text, Required): Enter the authorized caregiver's city.
* **Authorized Caregiver's State** (`authorized_caregiver_state`, Text, Required): Enter the authorized caregiver's state.
* **Authorized Caregiver's Zip Code** (`authorized_caregiver_zip`, Text, Required): Enter the authorized caregiver's zip code.
* **Authorized Caregiver's Email** (`authorized_caregiver_email`, Email, Required): Enter the authorized caregiver's email address.
* **Patient's Email** (`patient_email`, Email, Required): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Patient's Phone Number** (`patient_phone_number`, Text, Required): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Note to Authorized Caregiver** (`note`, Note, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes to the authorized caregiver.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, Text, Required): Enter a unique form ID.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date on which the form was submitted.
* **Submit Time** (`submit_time`, Time, Required): Enter the time on which the form was submitted.
* **Authorized Caregiver's Signature** (`authorized_caregiver_signature`, Note, Optional): Sign the form as the authorized caregiver.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, Note, Optional): Sign the form as the patient.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, Date, Required): Enter the date on which the form was completed.
* **Time Completed** (`time_completed`, Time, Required): Enter the time on which the form was completed.
* **Additional Comments** (`note`, Note, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes to the form.
* **Authorized Caregiver's Email** (`authorized_caregiver_email`, Email, Required): Enter the authorized caregiver's email address.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, Email, Required): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Patient Phone Number** (`patient_phone_number`, Text, Required): Enter the patient's phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check the information entered for accuracy.
* Ensure that the authorized caregiver's signature and the patient's signature (if applicable) are signed before submitting the form.
