# public_policy_research_records_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from researchers for the sharing of their data. It collects contact information and research project details, enabling the sharing of data while respecting individual preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the Researcher: Choose the researcher from the list.
2. Enter Contact Information: Provide your contact email, phone number, and address.
3. Enter Research Details: Enter the research institution, project name, start and end dates.
4. Choose Data Sharing Options: Select how you'd like your data to be shared.
5. Confirm Consent: Select your consent option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Researcher** (`Researcher`, select_one, required): Choose the researcher who will be sharing their data.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, email, optional): Enter the email address you'd like to use as contact information.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Address** (`Contact Address`, note, optional): Enter your address.
* **Research Institution** (`Research Institution`, text, optional): Enter the name of the institution where the research took place.
* **Research Project** (`Research Project`, text, optional): Enter the name of the research project.
* **Research Start Date** (`Research Start Date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the research project.
* **Research End Date** (`Research End Date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the research project.
* **Data Sharing** (`Data Sharing`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how you'd like your data to be shared. Options include "Option 1" and "Option 2".
* **Consent** (`Consent`, select_one, optional): Choose your consent option. Options include "Option 1" and "Option 2".

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Review your choices before submitting the form.
- Ensure you have permission to share your data according to your chosen options.
- This form is designed to ensure data can be shared safely and ethically.
