# Affidavit Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Affidavit Template is a document used to make sworn statements or declarations. It is intended for completion by an affiant (the person making the declaration) in the presence of a notary public.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and truthful information.
2. Verify that all required fields are completed before proceeding to the next section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Location where affidavit is made** (Venue) (`<name>: Location where affidavit is made.`):
  This field should be filled with the location where the affidavit is being made or witnessed.
* **State of** (State of): 
  This field should be filled in with the state where the affidavit is being made, e.g., California.
* **County of** (County of): 
  This field should be filled in with the county where the affidavit is being made, e.g., Los Angeles.
* **I, (Name of Affiant)** (Affiant Name): 
  This field should be filled with the full legal name of the affiant making the declaration.
* **Legal Age Confirmation** (Legal Age Confirmation):
  Select "Yes, I am over 18" to confirm that you are of legal age and competent to testify.
* **Numbered list of facts** (Statement of Facts):
  This section should be filled in with a numbered list of facts that the affiant wants to declare or attest to.
* **First fact** (1. Being duly sworn, deposes and says:): 
  This field should be filled in with the first fact that the affiant wants to declare or attest to.
* **Second fact** (2.0): 
  This field should be filled in with the second fact that the affiant wants to declare or attest to.
* **Third fact** (3.0): 
  This field should be filled in with the third fact that the affiant wants to declare or attest to.
* **Fourth fact** (4.0): 
  This field should be filled in with the fourth fact that the affiant wants to declare or attest to.
* **Further Affiant sayeth naught** (Closing Statement):
  This field should be filled in with any additional comments or explanations the affiant wants to make.
* **Affiant Signature** (Affiant Signature): 
  Select "Signed" to indicate that the affiant has signed the document.
* **Date Signed** (Date): 
  This field should be filled in with the date the affiant signed the document.
* **Subscribed and sworn to before me** (Notary Acknowledgment):
  This field will be completed by the notary public after verifying the authenticity of the document and the affiant's signature.
