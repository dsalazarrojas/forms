# Laser COVID 19 Liability Release Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain a client's consent and liability release for laser COVID-19 treatment. It confirms that the client has read, understood, and signed the waiver, which is essential for both the client's safety and the business providing the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the client's name.
2. Enter the client's email address.
3. Select the appointment date for the laser COVID-19 treatment.
4. Select the type of hair treatment the client is undergoing.
5. Enter the name of the laser technician performing the treatment.
6. Select the client's consent status:
	* "Client has read, understood, and signed this waiver."
	* "Client has not read, understood, and not signed this waiver."
7. Enter the date of the client's signature.
8. Enter the time of the client's signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client.
* **Client Email** (`email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the client.
* **Appointment Date** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the scheduled appointment.
* **Hair Type** (`text`, required/optional): Select the type of hair treatment the client is undergoing.
* **Laser Technician** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the laser technician performing the treatment.
* **Client Consent** (`select_one`, required): Select the client's consent status.
* **Signature Date** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the client's signature.
* **Signature Time** (`time`, required/optional): Enter the time of the client's signature.
