# Curriculum Content Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Curriculum Content Release Form is used to obtain the necessary permissions and approvals to release and share curriculum content with others.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information and permissions required.
2. Select the options for "User Input" that apply to you.
3. Provide your contact details, including email, phone, and address.
4. Specify the educational institution and department associated with the curriculum content.
5. Enter the course title and curriculum content.
6. Indicate your permission to use, share, and distribute the curriculum content.
7. Enter the date and time approved for the content.
8. Sign the form by selecting "Yes" in the "Signature" field.
9. Enter your name, title, and role.
10. Provide any additional notes or comments.
11. Submit the form to complete the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`select_one`, required/optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your agreement or disagreement with the terms of use.
* **Contact Details** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your email, phone number, and address to facilitate communication.
* **Educational Institution** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the educational institution associated with the curriculum content.
* **Department** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the department name associated with the curriculum content.
* **Course Title** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the title of the course or curriculum content.
* **Curriculum Content** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the curriculum content or materials to be released or shared.
* **Permission to Use** (`select_one`, required/optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your permission to use the curriculum content.
* **Permission to Share** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select "Yes" or "No" for each option to indicate your permission to share the curriculum content.
* **Permission to Distribute** (`select_one`, required/optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your permission to distribute the curriculum content.
* **Approved By** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the person or entity approving the content.
* **Signature** (`select_one`, required/optional): Select "Yes" to indicate your approval and agreement to the terms of use.
* **Date Approved** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the date the content was approved.
* **Time Approved** (`time`, required/optional): Enter the time the content was approved.
* **Approved By Time** (`time`, required/optional): Enter the time of approval.
* **Approved By Date** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the date of approval.
* **Email** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your email address to facilitate communication.
* **Phone** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number to facilitate communication.
* **Name** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your name to identify yourself.
* **Title** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your title to identify your role.
* **Role** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your role to identify your position.
* **Institution** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the institution where you work.
* **Address** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your address to facilitate communication.
* **Notes** (`note`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the content.
* **Submit** (`text`, required/optional): Click the submit button to complete the form.

## Tips

* Please review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed.
* Make sure to select "Yes" or "No" carefully for each option.
* Enter accurate and up-to-date information to avoid errors.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with the relevant authority.
* Submit the form only when you have all necessary permissions and approvals.

Note: Please ensure that all fields are reviewed carefully before submitting the form, as the permissions and approvals obtained through this form will be binding and legally enforceable.
