# Listing Withdrawal Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to withdraw a listing from your listing management system. Please fill out this form if you have any of the following reasons:

* You no longer have a listing active in our system that you would like to remove.
* You would like to withdraw an old listing due to a change in circumstances or errors with the listing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in all the required fields as best you can, using the options provided in each field.
2. Review your form for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Listing Withdrawal Form** (`listing_withdaral_form`, text, Optional)
Please provide a title for your form submission, this can be something like "Withdrawal Request for Listing XYZ".

### 2. **Listing** (`listing`, select_one, Optional)
Select one of the options for the listing you want to withdraw. This is usually the listing that corresponds to your current or past listing in our system.

### 3. **Real Estate Agent** (`real_estate_agent`, text, Optional)
Enter the name of the real estate agent or representative associated with your listing. This helps us keep our records up to date.

### 4. **Listing ID** (`listing_id`, number, Optional)
Enter the ID of the listing you wish to withdraw. This should match the ID of your listing in our system.

### 5. **Reason for Withdrawal** (`reason`, text, Optional)
Provide a brief explanation for the reason you are withdrawing the listing. This will help us to understand the circumstances surrounding the withdrawal.

### 6. **Comments** (`comments`, text, Optional)
Add any additional comments or information regarding the withdrawal of your listing.

### 7. **Real Estate Agency** (`real_estate_agency`, select_multiple, Optional)
Select all the agencies that were involved with the withdrawn listing.

### 8. **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, Optional)
Enter the name of the person or entity withdrawing the listing.

### 9. **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, Optional)
Enter the email of the person or entity withdrawing the listing.

### 10. **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, Optional)
Enter the phone number of the person or entity withdrawing the listing.

### 11. **Date and Time** (`date_and_time`, date, Optional)
Select the date and time when the listing was withdrawn.

### 12. **Signature** (`signature`, note, Optional)
Please provide a signature of acknowledgement of the withdrawal form submission, this could be your digital signature or a handwritten signature.

Please review your form carefully before submission and ensure all relevant fields are filled out accurately and completely. If you have any doubts or need assistance, please don't hesitate to contact us.
