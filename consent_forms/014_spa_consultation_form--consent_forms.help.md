# Spa Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spa Consultation Form is designed to collect necessary information from clients before their spa visit. It helps us understand their preferences, contact details, and schedule. Please fill out this form to ensure a smooth and personalized experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Client Information**: Provide your basic details, such as your name and any other contact information you'd like to share.
2. **Preferred Services**: Choose the spa services you'd like to receive during your visit. You can select multiple options.
3. **Contact Information**: Write down any important contact information you'd like us to have in case we need to reach you.
4. **Email**: Enter your email address so we can keep you updated on your appointment schedule and any important announcements.
5. **Phone Number**: Provide your phone number for additional contact and confirmation purposes.
6. **Date and Time**: Select your preferred date and time for your spa visit.
7. **Time**: Specify the exact time for your appointment.
8. **Comments**: Share any special requests, concerns, or additional information to help us tailor your experience.
9. **Signature**: Sign your name, so we can confirm your agreement to our terms and conditions.
10. **Terms and Conditions**: Read and agree to our terms and conditions, which outline our policies and procedures for your spa visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information** (`client_information`, text, required): Enter your name and any other contact information you'd like to share with us.
* **Preferred Services** (`preferred_services`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the spa services you'd like to receive during your visit, such as Massage, Facials, Haircuts, or Waxing.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, note, optional): Write down any important contact information we should have in case we need to reach you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can keep you updated on your appointment schedule and any important announcements.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Provide your phone number for additional contact and confirmation purposes.
* **Date and Time** (`date_and_time`, date, optional): Select your preferred date for your spa visit.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Specify the exact time for your appointment.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Share any special requests, concerns, or additional information to help us tailor your experience.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Sign your name, so we can confirm your agreement to our terms and conditions.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, text, required): Read and agree to our terms and conditions, which outline our policies and procedures for your spa visit.

## Tips
* Please ensure you fill out all required fields to ensure a smooth and personalized experience.
* You can select multiple services for your preferred spa experience.
* If you have any specific requests or concerns, use the comments field to share them with us.
* Review our terms and conditions carefully before signing to understand our policies and procedures.
