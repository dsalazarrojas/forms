# Winter Camp Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect essential information from parents or guardians of camp participants for emergency situations and to communicate the camp's policies and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read through the form carefully before starting. 
2. Answer all the required fields, which are marked as such. 
3. Fill in your contact information and emergency contact details accurately. 
4. Select the correct relationship between you and the camp participant. 
5. Confirm your consent for the participant to attend the camp. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parental Permissions** (`parental_permissions`, text, optional): A text field for parents or guardians to provide any necessary permissions or agreements for the participant's attendance at the camp.
* **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' if you want to be contacted in case of an emergency, 'No' if you do not.
* **Medical Details** (`medical_details`, text, optional): Provide any relevant medical information about the participant, such as allergies or medical conditions.
* **Contact Person** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship** (`contact_relationship`, select_one, required): Select how you are related to the participant, such as Parent, Guardian, or Emergency Contact.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): If the participant has any medical conditions, enter a brief description here.
* **Medication** (`medication`, text, optional): If the participant is taking any medication, enter a brief description here.
* **Allergies** (`medical_allergies`, text, optional): If the participant has any allergies, enter a brief description here.
* **Equipment** (`medical_equipment`, text, optional): If the participant has any medical equipment, enter a brief description here.
* **Emergency Contact Person Phone** (`emergency_contact_person_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Person Email** (`emergency_contact_person_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Person Relationship** (`emergency_contact_person_relationship`, select_one, required): Select how the emergency contact person is related to the participant.
* **Medical Information** (`medical_information`, note, optional): Add any additional medical information about the participant here.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required): Select 'True' to confirm your consent for the participant to attend the camp. If you select 'False', we will not be able to process the participant's registration.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the participant here.
* **Medical Insurance** (`medical_insurance`, text, optional): If the participant has medical insurance, enter the name of the insurance provider here.
* **Medical Note** (`medical_note`, text, optional): Add any additional medical notes about the participant here.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, optional): Enter the name of the insurance provider for the participant. 

Note: Please review this guide carefully to ensure you have completed the form accurately and comprehensively. If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to the camp staff for assistance.
