# Live Music Session Photography Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect consent from both parties for a live music session photography. It ensures a clear understanding of the terms and conditions of the session, including the use of images and their storage.
## How To Complete This Form

1. Carefully read the form and understand each field's requirement and purpose.
2. Fill in the required fields, including:
   - attendees_name
   - photo_release_consent
   - email
   - phone
   - attendees_email
   - attendees_phone
   - photo_shoot_date
   - photo_shoot_time
   - event_name
   - event_details
   - signature
3. If you are the event organizer, ensure you have permission to collect attendees' contact information and photo release consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Music Venue Information (music_venue_information)**: This field is for the event organizer or photographer to provide information about the music venue and session details.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
* **Attendees' Name (attendees_name)**: This field is for the attendee to provide their name. This is a required field.
  * Type: text
  * Required: true
* **Photo Release (photo_release)**: This field is not applicable in this form but has been included as it is part of the internal logic. Please ignore it.
* **Contact Details (contact_details)**: This field is not required and should not be filled in by the user. It is part of the internal logic.
* **Photo Shoot Dates (photo_shoot_dates)**: This field is not required and should not be filled in by the user. It is part of the internal logic.
* **Photo Release Consent (photo_release_consent)**: This field is required for the attendee to grant photo release consent. It is a multiple selection option of "Yes" and "No".
  * Type: select_multiple
