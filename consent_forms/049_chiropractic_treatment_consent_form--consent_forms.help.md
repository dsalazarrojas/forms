# Chiropractic Treatment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chiropractic Treatment Consent Form is a document used to collect essential information from patients before receiving chiropractic treatment. This form is a mandatory part of the consultation process and helps the chiropractor understand the patient's medical history and provide informed consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the required fields.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
3. Provide your signature in the designated area.
4. Ensure that you have read and understood the risks explained to you.
5. Sign and date the consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name (1)** (`Patient Name`, `text`, required): This is where you enter your full legal name.
* **Date of Birth (2)** (`Date of Birth`, `date`, optional): If you are a new patient, please enter your date of birth for medical record purposes.
* **Treatment Description (3)** (`Treatment Description`, `note`, optional): Briefly describe the proposed chiropractic treatment you are about to receive.
* **Risks Explained (4)** (`Risks Explained`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you have been informed of the risks associated with the treatment. Select 'True' to confirm that you have been explained the risks.
* **Consent Signature Name (5)** (`Consent Signature Name`, `text`, required): Sign your full name as a typed signature.
* **Date of Consent (6)** (`Date of Consent`, `date`, required): Date when you provided your consent.
* **Additional Notes (7)** (`Additional Notes`, `text`, optional): If you have any further questions or requests, please include them here.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any question, ask your chiropractor for clarification.
* Review the form carefully before signing.
* Keep the form for your records and bring it with you to future appointments.
