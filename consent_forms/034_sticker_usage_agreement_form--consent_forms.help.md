# Sticker Usage Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a user's sticker usage agreement, including whether they have read and agreed to the sticker placement terms, whether they have used the sticker in shared spaces, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your personal information, including your email address.
2. Select your response to each question, ensuring you have provided the correct information.
3. Review your answers and submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name and any other relevant personal information.
* **Sticker Placement** (`sticker_placement`, select_one, required/optional): Select whether you have placed the sticker in a shared space, such as a public area or on a private device.
* **Shared Spaces** (`shared_spaces`, text, required/optional): List any shared spaces where you have or plan to use the sticker.
* **Property Information** (`property`, select_one, required/optional): Select whether the sticker is for personal use or for use in a public or private property.
* **Sticker Type** (`sticker_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of sticker you have been given.
* **Shared Spaces List** (`shared_spaces_list`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check any shared spaces where you have or plan to place the sticker.
* **Property List** (`property_list`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check if you have or plan to place the sticker on a public or private property.
* **Sticker Placement Terms** (`sticker_placement_terms`, note, required/optional): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of sticker placement.
* **Acceptance** (`acceptance`, select_one, required/optional): Confirm that you have read and agreed to the sticker placement terms.
* **Signature** (`signature`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for verification purposes.
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you agree to the sticker usage terms.
