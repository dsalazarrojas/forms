# software_development_records_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The software_development_records_release_form is used to document and record the release of software development records. It is a crucial form for tracking and managing the release process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the release date of the software development project.
2. Provide the name of the developer or team responsible for the project.
3. Describe any sensitive records that are being released.
4. Add any additional details about the release.
5. Enter the contact email of the person or team responsible for the release.
6. Enter the name of the contact person or team.
7. Enter the phone number of the contact person or team.
8. Enter an alternative phone number of the contact person or team.
9. Enter the email of the contact person or team.
10. Select whether consents have been given and whether consents have been signed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Release Date** (release_date): Enter the date the software development project was released.
* **Developer Name** (developer_name): Enter the name of the developer or team responsible for the project.
* **Sensitive Records** (sensitive_records): Describe any sensitive records that are being released. This field is for records that may be considered confidential or proprietary.
* **Release Details** (release_details): Add any additional details about the release.
* **Contact Email** (contact_email): Enter the contact email of the person or team responsible for the release.
* **Contact Name** (release_contact_name): Enter the name of the contact person or team.
* **Phone Number** (release_contact_phone): Enter the phone number of the contact person or team.
* **Alternate Phone Number** (release_contact_phone_alt): Enter an alternative phone number of the contact person or team.
* **Alternate Contact Email** (release_contact_email_alt): Enter an alternative email of the contact person or team.
* **Consents Given** (consents_given): Select whether consents have been given for the release. Choose from the options "Yes" or "No".
* **Consents Signed** (consents_signed): Select whether consents have been signed. Choose from the options "Yes" or "No".

## Tips

* Be sure to complete all required fields to ensure accurate and complete records.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Only complete this form for software development projects that are complete and ready for release.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with your supervisor or team lead.
