# Skin Care Product Sensitivity Test Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for clients to provide consent for a skin care product sensitivity test. It gathers information about the client's skin history, the product being tested, and their personal details for the test. This form helps the skin care product manufacturer or researcher to understand any potential allergens or sensitivities associated with their product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your **Client Name**, which is your personal identifier for this consent.
2. Enter your **Contact Info**, including any relevant contact details such as phone number or email address.
3. Provide a brief description of your **Skin History**, including any pre-existing skin conditions or sensitivities you may have.
4. Specify the **Product Name** of the skin care product being tested.
5. Indicate the **Product Type** of the skin care product being tested (e.g., cream, serum, lotion, etc.).
6. Enter the date and time of the **Test Date** for which the product will be tested.
7. Optionally, enter the **Test Time** if applicable.
8. If applicable, select the **Test Duration** of the test, measured in minutes.
9. If applicable, select the **Test Location** where the test will take place.
10. Choose your **Test Result**, indicating whether you experienced any sensitivity or reaction to the product.
11. Finally, add any **Notes** you may have about the test or experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (Text, Required): Enter your full name as it should be recorded in our database.
* **Contact Info** (Text, Required): Enter any contact information you'd like us to use to follow up with you about this test.
* **Skin History** (Text, Optional): Describe any existing skin conditions or sensitivities you may have.
* **Product Name** (Text, Required): Clearly state the name of the product being tested.
* **Product Type** (Text, Required): Identify the type of product being tested (e.g., cream, serum, lotion, etc.).
* **Test Date** (Date, Required): Select the date on which the test will take place.
* **Test Time** (Time, Optional): If applicable, select the time of the test.
* **Test Duration** (Number, Optional): Measure the length of the test, in minutes.
* **Test Location** (Text, Optional): Specify the location where the test will occur.
* **Test Result** (Select Multiple, Required): Indicate whether you experienced any sensitivity or reaction to the product.
* **Notes** (Text, Optional): Add any additional comments or observations about the test or your experience.
