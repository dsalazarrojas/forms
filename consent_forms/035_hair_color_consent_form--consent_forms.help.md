# Hair Color Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hair Color Consent Form is a crucial document that helps us gather essential information from clients before proceeding with any hair color-related treatments or procedures. By filling out this form, clients confirm that they have read and understood the document's content, including potential risks, benefits, and side effects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Hair Color Consent Form, please follow these steps:

1. **Enter your First Name**: In this field, please provide your first name as it appears on your identification.
2. **Enter your Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
3. **Enter your Email**: Provide your valid email address.
4. **Enter your Phone**: Enter your phone number.
5. **Enter your Date of Birth**: Use the date format (mm/dd/yyyy) to fill in your date of birth.
6. **Select your Hair Color**: Choose the correct hair color from the options provided (Blonde, Brown, Gray, Red, Black).
7. **Provide Treatment Details**: Describe the treatment you are seeking.
8. **List any Allergies**: If you have any allergies, please list them here.
9. **List any Medical Conditions**: If you have any medical conditions, please list them here.
10. **Enter Emergency Contact Information**: Provide the name, phone number, and relation of your emergency contact.
11. **Confirm your Consent**: Sign below to confirm that you have read and understood this document.
12. **Signature**: Select "Yes" if you have read and understood the document.
13. **Date**: Please provide the date you signed this form (mm/dd/yyyy).
14. **Doctor Signature**: Select "Yes" if you have signed the form as a doctor.
15. **Doctor Date**: Please provide the date the doctor signed this form (mm/dd/yyyy).
16. **Doctor Time**: Enter the time the doctor signed this form (hh:mm am/pm).
17. **Clinic Information**: Provide the name, address, phone number, and email of the clinic.
18. **Clinic Address**: Enter the address of the clinic.
19. **Clinic Phone**: Enter the phone number of the clinic.
20. **Clinic Email**: Enter the email address of the clinic.
