# TV Series Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for obtaining consent from participants of a TV Series. It ensures that all involved individuals have given their consent to be part of the production.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand each field's purpose.
2. Fill out the form completely and truthfully, especially for fields with a red asterisk \*.
3. Ensure you only fill out this form once.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Info** (participant_info, text, optional):
	* This field is for providing general information about yourself.
- **Release Consent Participant** (release_consent_participant, select_one, optional):
	* Select "True" if you consent to being part of the TV Series.
	* Select "False" if you do not consent.
	* Select "Undetermined" if you are unsure.
- **Release Questions** (release_questions, text, optional):
	* This field is for any additional comments or questions you may have about the TV Series.
- **On Location Participation** (on_location_participation, select_multiple, optional):
	* Select all applicable locations where you will be participating.
	* Available options: Cast, Contributor, On-Location.
- **Media Usage** (media_usage, select_one, optional):
	* Select how the TV Series will be used (e.g., Film, TV, Online, Print).
- **Date of Consent** (date_of_consent, date, optional):
	* The date you give your consent.
- **Signature Date** (signature_date, date, optional):
	* The date you sign this form.
- **On Location Contact Info** (on_location_contact_info, text, optional):
	* Provide your contact information for on-location participation.

## Tips
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the production team.
- Ensure your contact information is up to date and accurate.
- Only submit one form per participant.
