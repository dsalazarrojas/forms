# Property Value Study Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect participant information and obtain consent for a property value study. It is a crucial step in the study process, ensuring that participants understand the purpose of the study and agree to participate.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the participant information fields: Participant Name, Participant Phone, Participant Email, and Participant Address.
2. Provide the study details in the Study Details field.
3. Enter the Researcher Name and Agency Name (if applicable).
4. Select the appropriate response for the Consent field.
5. Add any additional comments or information in the Additional Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, text, required): Enter your address for mail or courier services.
* **Study Details** (`study_details`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the study, including its purpose and any relevant details.
* **Researcher Name** (`researcher_name`, text, optional): Enter the name and title of the researcher conducting the study.
* **Agency Name** (`agency_name`, text, optional): Enter the name and title of the agency conducting the study.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required): Select "I give my consent" to participate in the study.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information about your participation in the study.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- Enter accurate and up-to-date information in all fields.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the study, do not hesitate to contact the researcher or agency conducting the study.
