# informed_consent_phone_text_and_video_form_ilp - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture informed consent from individuals for a phone and video conference. It is essential to complete this form accurately and truthfully.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter the date of the video conference in the "Date of Video Conference" field.
6. Enter the time of the video conference in the "Time of Video Conference" field.
7. Optionally, provide additional details about the video conference in the "Video Conference Details" field.
8. Select "True" in the "I Consent" field to confirm your informed consent for the video conference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): This field is used to capture your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): This field is used to capture your last name.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): This field is used to capture your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): This field is used to capture your email address.
* **Date of Video Conference** (`informed_consent_date`, `date`, required): This field is used to capture the date of the video conference.
* **Time of Video Conference** (`informed_consent_time`, `time`, required): This field is used to capture the time of the video conference.
* **Video Conference Details** (`video_conference_details`, `text`, optional): This field is used to provide additional details about the video conference.
* **I Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to confirm your informed consent for the video conference. Please select "True" to confirm your consent.
