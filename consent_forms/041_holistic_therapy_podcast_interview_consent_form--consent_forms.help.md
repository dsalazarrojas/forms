# Holistic Therapy Podcast Interview Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holistic Therapy Podcast Interview Consent Form is used to collect information from guests who participate in the podcast, including their personal details, contact information, medical history, and consent status. This form ensures that guests are aware of the recording process, their rights, and the use of their information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your guest information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Select your availability and preferred contact method for future communication.
3. Provide emergency contact information, including a phone number and the relationship between the person and you.
4. List any medical conditions or medications you are currently taking.
5. Describe your medical history.
6. Review and sign the consent form, indicating that you understand the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Intro**: This is a note field for introduction purposes, please do not fill this field.
* **Guest Information**: This field is where you should enter your name, which will be used to identify you as a guest.
* **Email**: This field is for your email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: This field is for your phone number, which will be used for communication purposes.
* **Availability**: This field is for selecting a time slot when you are available for an interview.
* **Best Time**: This field is for selecting a specific time when you are available for an interview.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: This field is for selecting how you would like to be contacted (email, phone, text message, social media, or other).
* **Emergency Contact Information**: This is for providing information about a person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Phone Number**: This field is for entering the phone number of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Relationship**: This field is for describing the relationship between the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Contact Method**: This field is for selecting how the person to contact in case of an emergency (email, phone, text message, social media, or other).
* **Emergency Message**: This field is for entering any additional information that needs to be passed on to the person in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Conditions**: This field is for listing any medical conditions you are currently taking.
* **Meditations**: This field is for listing any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical History**: This field is for describing your medical history.
* **List of Medical Conditions**: This field is for listing any additional medical conditions.
* **List of Medical History**: This field is for listing any additional medical history.
* **Medical History Description**: This field is for describing your medical history in more detail.
* **Consent Form**: This is a note field for the consent form, please do not fill this field.
* **Consent Form Text**: This field is for entering any additional information about your consent.
* **Signed**: This field is for indicating whether you have signed the consent form.
* **Signed Consent Text**: This field is for entering any additional information about your signed consent.
* **Signed Date**: This field is for entering the date when you signed the consent form.
* **Signed Time**: This field is for entering the time when you signed the consent form.

Note: The form is designed to be completed by the guest, and the fields marked as "required" should be filled in by the guest. The other fields are optional and can be filled in by the guest at their discretion.
