# Academy Salon Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for clients to consent to services provided by students at the academy salon, acknowledging the risks involved and releasing the academy and its staff from liability for any adverse reactions or dissatisfaction with services.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read and understand the contents of this form carefully before completing it.
- Select all services you wish to receive from the options listed.
- Answer "True" or "False" to questions about known allergies and skin conditions.
- Sign and date the form, acknowledging your understanding and agreement to the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (Text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last name** (Text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email address** (Email, required): Enter your valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone number** (Text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Date of birth** (Date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Date of service** (Date, required): Enter the date you will be receiving services.
* **Services requested** (Select multiple, required): Select all services you wish to receive from the options listed:
	+ Haircut and styling
	+ Hair coloring or highlights
	+ Chemical treatment (perm, relaxer)
	+ Facial or skincare
	+ Manicure or pedicure
	+ Waxing
	+ Makeup application
* **-- Health and Safety Information --** (Note, not required): If you have any known allergies or sensitivities, please list them here.
* **Do you have any known allergies to beauty or chemical products?** (Select one, required): Answer "True" if you have known allergies, or "False" if you do not.
* **If yes, please list your allergies** (Text, not required): If you answered "True", describe your allergies here.
* **Do you have any skin conditions or sensitivities?** (Select one, required): Answer "True" if you have known skin conditions or sensitivities, or "False" if you do not.
* **If yes, please describe** (Text, not required): If you answered "True", describe your skin conditions here.
* **I understand that services are performed by students under supervision and I accept the associated risks** (Select one, required): Answer "Yes - I understand and accept" to indicate your understanding and agreement.
* **I release the academy, its staff, and students from liability for any adverse reactions or dissatisfaction with services** (Select one, required): Answer "Yes - I agree" to indicate your agreement to the terms.
* **Do you consent to before and after photos being taken for educational purposes?** (Select one, required): Answer "True" if you give your consent.
* **Date of signature** (Date, required): Enter the date of your signature in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **I understand and agree** (Select one, not required): This section is not required, but indicates your understanding and agreement to the terms.
