# Self Permission Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Self Permission Acknowledgement Form is used to obtain self-acknowledgement from an individual for a particular action or purpose.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please provide your General Information on page 1.
2. On page 2, please indicate whether you agree or decline with the Self-Permission Acknowledgement.
3. Enter your Name on page 3.
4. Provide your Email address on page 4.
5. Enter your Phone Number on page 5.
6. Select the Date on page 6.
7. Choose the Time on page 7.
8. Add any additional comments or notes on page 8.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information**: This is the first page of the form where you will provide basic details about yourself.
* **Self-Permission Acknowledgement**: Please indicate whether you agree or decline with the self-permission acknowledgement by selecting "I Agree", "I do not agree", or "Decline".
* **Name**: This is the page to enter your full name.
* **Email**: Enter your Email address to allow us to contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number to ensure we can reach you.
* **Date**: Select the date on which you would like to participate or take the action.
* **Time**: Choose the time for the action or activity.
* **Note**: Add any additional comments or notes you would like to share with us.

Note: Please fill in all the required fields marked with an asterisk (*).
