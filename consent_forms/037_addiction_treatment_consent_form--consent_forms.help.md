# Addiction Treatment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Addiction Treatment Consent Form is a crucial document that outlines the terms and conditions of your addiction treatment plan. It is designed to inform you of the potential risks and benefits of the treatment and to obtain your consent for the services provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Carefully read and review the form, paying attention to the treatment options and modalities presented.
2. Select all the treatment components that have been discussed with you.
3. Acknowledge the potential risks and benefits of the treatment plan.
4. Confirm that you have received and reviewed the notice of privacy practices.
5. Consent to periodic drug and alcohol screening as part of your treatment.
6. Understand that you have the right to withdraw your consent at any time.
7. Agree to your financial responsibility for services rendered.
8. Provide emergency contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Full Legal Name (2)**: Please enter your full legal name.
* **Date of Birth (3)**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Proposed Treatment Components (5)**: Select all the treatment components that have been discussed with you. These may include:
	+ Medically Supervised Detoxification
	+ Residential / Inpatient Rehabilitation
	+ Intensive Outpatient Program (IOP)
	+ Individual Counseling / Psychotherapy
	+ Group Therapy Sessions
	+ Medication-Assisted Treatment (MAT)
* **Risks and Benefits (6)**: Please review the potential risks and benefits of the treatment plan.
* **I have been informed of the potential risks and benefits of the treatment plan (7)**: Acknowledge that you have been informed of the potential risks and benefits.
* **I have received and reviewed the Notice of Privacy Practices (8)**: Confirm that you have received and reviewed the notice.
* **I consent to periodic drug and alcohol screening as part of my treatment (10)**: Consent to periodic drug and alcohol screening.
* **I understand I have the right to withdraw my consent at any time (11)**: Acknowledge that you understand your right to withdraw your consent.
* **I understand that I am financially responsible for services rendered (12)**: Agree to your financial responsibility.
* **Emergency Contact Name (13)**: Provide the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (14)**: Provide the contact phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Date of Consent (15)**: Enter the date of your consent.
* **My selection here acts as my electronic signature for this consent form (16)**: Confirm that your selection acts as your electronic signature.

**Tips**

* Please take your time to carefully review the form and ensure that you understand each section.
* Ask a healthcare professional if you have any questions or concerns.
* Complete the form accurately and truthfully.
* Review and acknowledge each section before signing the form.

Note: The form is designed to be user-friendly and easy to navigate. Please take your time and complete the form carefully to ensure your consent is valid and complete.
