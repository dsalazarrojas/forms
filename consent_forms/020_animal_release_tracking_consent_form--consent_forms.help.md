# Animal Release Tracking Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animal Release Tracking Consent Form is used to track animals in a research or monitoring context. The form is designed to gather essential information about the tracking device, animal, and tracking conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the **Tracking Device ID or Serial Number** in the field labeled "Tracking Device ID or Serial Number" (Field 1). This is a unique identifier assigned to the tracking device.
2.  Enter the **Animal Name or Unique ID** in the field labeled "Animal Name or Unique ID" (Field 2). This could be the animal's common name or a unique identifier assigned to it.
3.  Select the **Species Classification** from the options provided in the field labeled "Species Classification" (Field 3). This is to ensure that the correct species is accurately represented.
4.  Enter a description of the **Release Location** in the field labeled "Release Location Description" (Field 4). This could be GPS coordinates or a description of the location where the animal was released.
5.  Enter the **Intended Duration of Tracking** in the field labeled "Intended Duration of Tracking" (Field 5). This specifies how long the tracking device will be active.
6.  Select the **Data Sharing Consent Level** from the options provided in the field labeled "Data Sharing Consent Level" (Field 6). This specifies the level of data sharing consent agreed upon for the tracking device.
7.  Choose the **Responsibility for Device Recovery** from the options provided in the field labeled "Responsibility for Device Recovery" (Field 7). This specifies who is responsible for recovering the tracking device.
8.  Select the **Liability Release for Tracking Activities** from the options provided in the field labeled "Liability Release for Tracking Activities" (Field 8). This releases the organization from liability related to the tracking device.
9.  Enter the **Emergency Contact for Tracking Alerts** in the field labeled "Emergency Contact for Tracking Alerts" (Field 9). This is the contact information for the person responsible for handling emergency situations related to the tracking device.
10.  Enter the **Signature of Authorized Representative** in the field labeled "Signature of Authorized Representative" (Field 10). This is the name of the person completing the form.
11.  Enter the **Date of Signature** in the field labeled "Date of Signature" (Field 11). This is the date the form is being completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tracking Device ID or Serial Number (Field 1)**: Enter the unique identifier assigned to the tracking device.
* **Animal Name or Unique ID (Field 2)**: Enter the animal's name or unique identifier.
* **Species Classification (Field 3)**: Select the correct species classification for the animal being tracked.
* **Release Location Description (Field 4)**: Describe the location where the animal was released.
* **Intended Duration of Tracking (Field 5)**: Specify the duration for which the tracking device will be active.
* **Data Sharing Consent Level (Field 6)**: Specify the level of data sharing consent agreed upon for the tracking device.
* **Responsibility for Device Recovery (Field 7)**: Choose who is responsible for recovering the tracking device.
* **Liability Release for Tracking Activities (Field 8)**: Select to release the organization from liability related to the tracking device.
* **Emergency Contact for Tracking Alerts (Field 9)**: Enter the contact information for emergency situations related to the tracking device.
* **Signature of Authorized Representative (Field 10)**: Enter the full name of the person completing the form.
* **Date of Signature (Field 11)**: Enter the date the form is being completed.

Note: It's crucial to review each field carefully to ensure that all required information is accurately recorded.
