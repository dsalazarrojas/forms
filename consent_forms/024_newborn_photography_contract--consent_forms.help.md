# Newborn Photography Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Newborn Photography Contract is a document that captures essential information from customers and photographers for a photography session. It ensures both parties have a clear understanding of the session details, terms, and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal contact information (session date, customer name, contact number, email, address, city, and province).
2. Enter your photographer's contact information (photographer name, contact number, and email).
3. Describe the session details in the "Session Details" field.
4. Select the consent agreement that applies to your session (e.g., "None", "Other").
5. Sign the "Signature" field to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the photography session.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the customer.
* **Customer Contact Number** (`customer_contact_number`, text, required): Enter your contact number.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, required): Enter your address.
* **Customer City** (`customer_city`, text, required): Enter your city.
* **Customer Province** (`customer_province`, text, required): Enter your province.
* **Photographer Name** (`photographer_name`, text, required): Enter your photographer's name.
* **Photographer Contact Number** (`photographer_contact_number`, text, required): Enter your photographer's contact number.
* **Photographer Email** (`photographer_email`, email, required): Enter your photographer's email address.
* **Session Details** (`session_details`, note, required): Describe the details of your photography session, including location, time, and any specific requirements or requests.
* **Consent Agreement** (`consent_agreement`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the following consent agreements that apply to your session:
	+ None
	+ Other (Please describe in the session details field)
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Sign your name to confirm your agreement with the photographer.

## Tips

* Please ensure you have a clear understanding with your photographer before completing this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to your photographer.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to your photographer.
* The consent agreement is not a substitute for legal documentation. If you have any concerns or questions, please discuss with your photographer.
