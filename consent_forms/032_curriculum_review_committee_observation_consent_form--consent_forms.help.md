# Curriculum Review Committee Observation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Curriculum Review Committee Observation Consent Form is used to collect information from observers on their impressions of a teacher's performance and teaching practices. It is essential to complete this form carefully and accurately to facilitate the evaluation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your teacher's name in the "Teacher Name" field (field 1).
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field (field 2), which will be used for communication purposes.
3. Select the date you observed the teacher (field 12) and the subject area (field 13) you observed.
4. Choose the grade level (field 14) of the class you observed.
5. Specify the size of the class (field 15) you observed.
6. Choose the observer's level of expertise (field 18).
7. If you have additional comments, please enter them in field 6.
8. Provide a signature in fields 21 and 8.
9. Fill in the school name (field 23) and school district (field 24).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher Name** (field 1, text, required): Enter your teacher's name.
* **Email** (field 2, email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Observation Date** (field 12, date, required): Select the date you observed the teacher.
* **Subject Area** (field 13, text, required): Choose the subject area you observed.
* **Grade Level** (field 14, select one, required): Choose the grade level of the class you observed.
* **Class Size** (field 15, select one, required): Choose the class size you observed.
* **Observer Info** (field 4, text, optional): Provide any relevant information about yourself as an observer.
* **Permission Granting** (field 5, select one, required): Choose the level of permission you are granting.
* **Comments** (field 6, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or concerns.
* **Additional Comments** (field 7, text, optional): Enter any further additional comments or concerns.
* **Signature** (field 8, text, required): Provide a digital signature of your agreement.
* **Observer Name** (field 9, text, optional): Provide your name as an observer.
* **Observer Position** (field 10, text, optional): Provide your position as an observer.
* **Observer School** (field 11, text, optional): Provide the name of the school you observed.
* **Date Observed** (field 12, date, required): Select the date you observed the teacher.
* **Grade Level** (field 14, select one, required): Choose the grade level of the class you observed.
* **Class Size** (field 15, select one, required): Choose the class size you observed.
* **Observer Expertise** (field 18, select one, required): Choose your level of expertise.
* **Comments** (field 19, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or concerns.
* **Date Review** (field 20, date, optional): Select the date you reviewed the form.
* **Signature 2** (field 21, text, required): Provide a digital signature of your agreement.
* **Teacher Email** (field 22, email, optional): Enter the teacher's email address.
* **School** (field 23, text, required): Provide the name of the school you observed.
* **School District** (field 24, text, required): Provide the name of the school district you observed.
