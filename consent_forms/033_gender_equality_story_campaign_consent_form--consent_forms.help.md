# Gender Equality Story Campaign Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals who wish to share their stories as part of the Gender Equality Story Campaign. It ensures that individuals provide their consent for their story to be shared, and that their information is shared with the correct individuals or groups.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form completely and accurately, including all required fields.
2.  If you are sharing your story, make sure you have the necessary permissions and consents from anyone mentioned in your story.
3.  Be aware that your story and contact information will be shared with the selected individuals or groups.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **consent_to_share** (`consent_to_share`, select_one, required): This field indicates your consent to share your story. Selecting 'Yes' means you agree to have your story shared publicly. Selecting 'No' means you do not consent to share your story.
*  **share_with** (`share_with`, select_multiple, required): This field allows you to select who you want to share your story with. You can choose 'Everyone', 'Colleagues', 'Friends', or 'Family' to share your story with.
*  **share_publicly** (`share_publicly`, select_one, required): This field determines whether you want to share your story publicly. Selecting 'Yes' means your story will be shared publicly, while selecting 'No' means it will not.
*  **share_preferences** (`share_preferences`, select_multiple, required): This field allows you to specify how you want your story to be shared. You can choose to 'Share anonymously', 'Share with name and contact info', or 'Share with name only'.
*  **share_with_email** (`share_with_email`, email, required): This field is for your email address, which will be used to share your story with the selected individuals or groups.
*  **contact_number** (`contact_number`, text, required): This field is for your phone number, which will be used to contact you regarding your story.
*  **story_details** (`story_details`, note, required): This field is for you to provide any additional details about your story.
