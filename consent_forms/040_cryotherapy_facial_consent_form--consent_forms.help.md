# Cryotherapy Facial Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cryotherapy Facial Consent Form is a document that collects essential information from patients or clients before undergoing a cryotherapy facial treatment. It is used to obtain consent for the treatment, gather medical history, and gather contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out all the required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
2. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
4. Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
5. Enter your Address in the "Address" field.
6. If applicable, provide your medical history in the "Medical History" field.
7. Check the box next to "Treatment Consent" to confirm that you understand and agree to the treatment.
8. Enter your Contact Phone number in the "Contact Phone" field (optional).
9. Enter your Contact Email address in the "Contact Email" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it is known to you.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it is known to you.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your current address as it is known to you.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please provide a brief description.
* **Treatment Consent** (`treatment_consent`, select_one, required): Check "True" to confirm that you understand and agree to the treatment.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number if you would like to be reached for any follow-up questions or concerns.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email address if you would like to be reached for any follow-up questions or concerns.

## Tips
* Please make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to ask your healthcare professional.
* This form is designed to be completed in a few minutes. If you need more time, please let your healthcare professional know.
