# Holistic Program Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holistic Program Agreement form is used to obtain consent and gather information from individuals who wish to participate in the holistic program. This form is used to ensure that participants are aware of the program's details and understand the terms of their involvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email address" field.
3. Enter your contact number in the "Contact number" field.
4. If you have any health conditions or allergies, please note them in the "Health information" field.
5. Select all the options that apply from the "Consent" field to indicate your agreement to participate in the holistic program.
6. Sign with your name in the "Signature" field to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (Name, text, required: false): This is the field where you enter your full name, as it appears on your official documents (e.g., passport, driver's license).
* **Email address** (Email address, email, required: false): This is the field where you enter your email address that we will use to communicate with you about the program.
* **Contact number** (Contact number, text, required: false): This is the field where you enter your contact number that we will use to reach you about the program.
* **Health information** (Health information, note, required: false): If you have any health conditions or allergies, please note them here to ensure we can provide you with the best possible care and accommodations.
* **Consent** (Consent, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the options that apply to indicate your agreement to participate in the holistic program.
	+ I am aware of the holistic program: This option indicates that you have read and understood the program's details.
	+ I have read and understand the program information: This option confirms that you have read and comprehended the program's information.
	+ I agree to participate in the holistic program: This option indicates your consent to participate in the program.
* **Signature** (Signature, text, required: false): Please sign with your name to confirm your consent and complete the form.
