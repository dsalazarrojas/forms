# Vaccination Record Sharing Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for obtaining authorization to share vaccination records with a specific institution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Institutional Type**: Select the type of institution you want to share your vaccination records with. You can choose from Medical Center, University, Insurance Company, or Government Agency.
2. Fill out the **Authorization Type** field to specify how you want to authorize the sharing of your records. You can choose to view, edit, or both.
3. Enter the **Start Date** when your records will be accessible to the institution.
4. Enter the **End Date** when your records will be no longer accessible to the institution.
5. Provide a **Reason** for why you want to share your records with the institution.
6. Enter the **Institution ID** if required by the institution.
7. **Provider Name**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider or institution that administered your vaccination.
8. **Record Status**: Select the current status of your vaccination records. This must be accurate to avoid errors.
9. **Additional Notes**: Provide any additional information you want to include about your records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Institutional Type (Medical Center, University, Insurance Company, Government Agency)**: Choose the type of institution you want to share your records with.
* **Authorization Type (View, Edit, Both, None)**: Specify how you want to authorize the sharing of your records.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date when your records will be accessible to the institution.
* **End Date**: Enter the date when your records will be no longer accessible to the institution.
* **Reason**: Provide a reason for why you want to share your records with the institution.
* **Institution ID**: Enter the institution's ID if required.
* **Provider Name**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider or institution that administered your vaccination.
* **Record Status (Active, Inactive, Pending, Cancelled)**: Select the current status of your vaccination records.
* **Additional Notes**: Provide any additional information about your records.

## Tips
* Be accurate when filling out the **Record Status** field to avoid errors.
* Ensure you have the necessary permissions to share your records with the selected institution.
* Double-check the **Institution ID** and **Provider Name** if required.
* Provide a clear **Reason** for sharing your records.
* Keep **Additional Notes** concise and relevant.
