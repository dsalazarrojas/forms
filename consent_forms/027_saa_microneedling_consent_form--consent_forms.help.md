# SAA Microneedling Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect a patient's information and consent for microneedling procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the patient's contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
3. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field (this field is optional and may not be relevant for all patients).
4. Specify the microneedling procedure in the "Procedure" field.
5. Provide any relevant medical history in the "Medical History" field.
6. List any allergies in the "Allergies" field.
7. List any current medications in the "Medication" field.
8. Provide emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contact" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the patient's contact number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the patient's date of birth (if applicable).
* **Procedure** (`procedure_name`, text, optional): Specify the microneedling procedure.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Provide any relevant medical history.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies the patient may have.
* **Medication** (`medication`, text, optional): List any current medications the patient is taking.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Provide the emergency contact information (name and contact number).
