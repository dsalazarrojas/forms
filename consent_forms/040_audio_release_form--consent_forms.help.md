# Audio Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Audio Release Form is designed to facilitate the transfer of ownership rights and payment details between audio creators and assignees. This form helps ensure a clear and concise agreement on the terms of the audio work release.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Provide the creator's and assignee's contact information in the "Creator and Assignee Details" section.
2. Enter the title and duration of the audio work in the "Audio Work Information" section.
3. Select the type of audio work and the type of rights transfer in the "Audio Work Information" and "Transfer of Rights" sections.
4. Specify the usage territory scope and payment status for the release in the "Transfer of Rights" section.
5. Provide the consideration paid for the release and the payment status in the "Transfer of Rights" section.
6. Sign the form digitally, providing your contact email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Creator Full Name**: Enter the full name of the creator, including their title (e.g., composer, songwriter, or performer).
* **Assignee Name (The Recipient)**: Enter the name of the production company or label receiving the release.
* **Title of the Audio Work**: Enter a brief description of the audio work, such as a song, album, or composition.
* **Duration/Length**: Enter the duration of the audio work in format e.g., 3m 45s.
* **Type of Audio Work**: Select the type of audio work from the options provided, such as "Musical Composition" or "Full Produced Song".
* **Type of Rights Transfer**:
	+ **Full Assignment (Ownership Transfer)**: Transfer full ownership of the rights to the assignee.
	+ **Exclusive License (Limited Term)**: Grant a limited-time license to the assignee.
	+ **Non-Exclusive License (Royalty Free)**: Grant a royalty-free license to the assignee.
	+ **Public Domain Dedication**: Release the work into the public domain.
* **Usage Territory Scope**:
	+ **Worldwide/Universe**: Grant release rights globally.
	+ **Single Country**: Grant release rights to a specific country.
	+ **Digital/Internet only**: Grant release rights to digital platforms only.
* **Consideration Paid (Amount)**: Enter the total fee paid for the release.
* **Payment Status**:
	+ **Paid in Full**: The full amount has been paid.
	+ **Pending (Pay on receipt)**: Payment is pending and will be paid upon receipt of the release.
	+ **No fee (Gratis)**: No fee was paid for the release.
* **I Warrant that this is an original work and I have full rights**: Select the "I Warrant" option to confirm that the work is original and you have full rights.
* **Creator Digital Signature**: Enter your digital signature, as a text confirmation of your identity.
* **Date of Release**: Enter the date of the release.
* **Creator Email Address**: Enter your email address, as your digital signature.
