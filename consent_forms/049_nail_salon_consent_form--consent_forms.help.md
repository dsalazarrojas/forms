# Nail Salon Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain the necessary information and consent from clients for salon services.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the form:

1. Enter your name in the `Client Name` field.
2. Provide the address of the salon where the services will be performed.
3. Enter the contact number of the salon.
4. (Optional) Enter your email address if you want to be contacted by the salon.
5. Describe the treatment you are booking (e.g., manicure, pedicure, etc.).
6. Select the date of your visit to the salon.
7. Choose the time of your visit to the salon.
8. Provide your signature to indicate your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **salon_address**: Enter the address of the salon where the services will be performed.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the phone number of the salon where you can be reached.
* **Email**: (Optional) Enter your email address if you want to be contacted by the salon.
* **Treatment**: Describe the services you are booking (e.g., manicure, pedicure, etc.).
* **Date Of Visit**: Select the date of your visit to the salon.
* **Time Of Visit**: Choose the time of your visit to the salon.
* **Client Signature**: Provide your signature to indicate your consent.
