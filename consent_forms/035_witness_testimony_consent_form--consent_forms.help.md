# Witness Testimony Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect witness contact information and witness name, email, and phone number. The purpose of this form is to gather essential details from the witness regarding their contact information and identify them for our records. Please note that this form is for administrative purposes and is not for testimonial or testimonial-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information in the first field "Contact Information". This is an optional field but will help us contact you if needed.
2. Enter the witness's name in the second field "witness_name".
3. Enter the witness's email address in the third field "witness_email". Please ensure this is a valid email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information (witness_info, text, optional)**: Enter your contact information in this field, if you want to be contacted about the form submission.
* **Witness's Name (witness_name, text, required)**: This field is used to capture the witness's name. Please enter their full name as it appears on their official documents.
* **Witness's Email (witness_email, email, required)**: Enter the witness's email address. This will be used to contact them, and to verify their email address. Please ensure this is a valid email address.
* **Witness's Phone (witness_phone, text, optional)**: This field is used to capture the witness's phone number. This is an optional field and is used for further contact purposes.
