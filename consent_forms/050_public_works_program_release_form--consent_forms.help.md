# Public Works Program Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential information from participants in public works programs, ensuring their consent and details about their involvement in these projects.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form completely and accurately.** Ensure all required fields are completed.
2. **Enter your name and email.** This information is crucial for future communications and updates.
3. **Provide your contact phone number (optional).** This helps us reach out to you with important project updates or changes.
4. **Enter your address (optional).** If you have a specific address associated with your project.
5. **Select the start and end dates for the program.** Choose the dates when the project begins and ends.
6. **Acknowledge the risks associated with your program.** This is an essential step to ensure you understand the potential hazards involved.
7. **Make a declaration about your participation.** Confirm your involvement in the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Public Works Program Release Form**: This is the title of the form, which you should read and understand before proceeding.
* **Participant Name**: Please enter your full name as the representative of your organization or individual participating in the project.
*   Type: text
*   **Required: yes**
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address for future communications, updates, and project notifications.
*   Type: email
*   **Required: yes**
* **Participant Phone (Optional)**: Provide your contact phone number, if you would like to be reached out with updates or changes.
*   Type: text
*   **Required: no**
* **Participant Address (Optional)**: Enter your address if you have a specific location associated with your project.
*   Type: text
*   **Required: no**
* **Program Start Date**: Select the date when your public works program begins.
*   Type: date
*   **Required: yes**
* **Program End Date**: Select the date when your public works program ends.
*   Type: date
*   **Required: yes**
* **Risk Acknowledgement**: Confirm that you understand the potential risks involved in your project, and acknowledge your acceptance of these risks.
*   Type: text
*   **Required: yes**
* **Participant Declaration**: Confirm your full participation and declaration about your involvement in the program.
*   Type: text
*   **Required: yes**

## Tips
* Double-check your input for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* Ensure you understand the risks associated with your program before submitting this form.
