# Telepsychiatry Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain patient consent for telepsychiatry sessions. It covers essential information, including patient details, medical history, contact information, and consent terms.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, fill in your name and contact information in the **Patient Information** section.
2.  In the **Medical History** section, describe any relevant medical conditions you have or have had in the past.
3.  If you have any allergies or special needs, please provide this information in the **Medical Conditions** section.
4.  In the **Contact Information** section, enter the contact details of the person to be reached in case of an emergency.
5.  In the **Emergency Contact Information** section, enter the address and phone number of the person to be reached.
6.  In the **Signature** section, please provide a handwritten signature as proof of consent.
7.  In the **Signature Image** section, upload a picture of your signature as an alternative to a handwritten signature.
8.  Please choose from the options in the **Submit** section to complete the form.

## Tips

1.  Ensure that the form is fully completed before submitting it.
2.  If there are any issues or doubts, please contact the service provider.
3.  This form is for patients to consent to telepsychiatry services. Please fill it out carefully and honestly.
4.  If you have any questions or concerns, please discuss with your healthcare provider.
