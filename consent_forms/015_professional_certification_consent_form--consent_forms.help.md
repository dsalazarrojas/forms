# professional_certification_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "professional_certification_consent_form" is designed to collect user information and certification-related data for a professional certification process. Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions and consent to complete this form.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Fill in the required and optional fields as per your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information**: (<id: 1>, <label: User Information>, <type: text, <required: false>): This is the primary section to provide your basic information.
* **Contact Number**: (<id: 2>, <label: Contact Number, <type: text, <required: false>): Enter your contact number to ensure we can get in touch with you regarding the certification process.
* **Email**: (<id: 3>, <label: Email, <type: email, <required: false>): Provide your email address for secure and official communication.
* **Date Of Birth**: (<id: 4>, <label: Date Of Birth, <type: date, <required: false>): Enter your date of birth in the standard date format (yyyy-mm-dd).
* **Certification Process**: (<id: 5>, <label: Certification Process, <type: text, <required: false>): Describe the certification process you are interested in or are currently pursuing.

## Tips
- Make sure all required fields are completed to ensure proper submission.
- If you're unsure about any field, please ask your administrator for clarification.
- This form will help us to keep you updated on the certification process.
