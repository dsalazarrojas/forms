# Hackathon Data Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hackathon Data Release Form is a document that collects information from participants in a hackathon event, including their personal details, project information, and consent for data sharing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your participant information on the first page.
2. Enter your email address in the email field.
3. Provide a title for your project.
4. Answer "Yes" or "No" to sharing your data, and select all the options that apply.
5. If you wish to share your data, enter any additional comments or terms related to data sharing.
6. Enter a submission ID (if required).
7. Confirm your consent by selecting "True".
8. Add any additional comments or feedback in the text area.
9. Sign and date your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other participant information.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Project Title** (`project_title`, text, optional): Enter a title for your project.
* **Share Your Data** (`data_sharing`, select_multiple, required): Answer "Yes" or "No" to sharing your data, and select all the options that apply.
	+ Select "Yes" to share your data with the hackathon organizers.
	+ Select "No" to not share your data.
* **Data Sharing Terms** (`data_sharing_terms`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or terms related to data sharing (if you select "Yes" to share your data).
* **Submission ID** (`submission_id`, text, optional): Enter a submission ID (if required).
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required): Confirm your consent by selecting "True".
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, optional): Sign and date your signature.

## Tips
* Double-check that you have completed all the required fields, including "Share Your Data" and "Consent".
* Ensure that you have provided accurate and complete information for all fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to contact the hackathon organizers for assistance.
