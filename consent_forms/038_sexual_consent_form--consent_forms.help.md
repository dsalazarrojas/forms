# Sexual Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will help you understand how to complete the Sexual Consent Form.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the relevant information in the corresponding fields. Ensure that all required fields are completed.
2. Fill in any other fields that are relevant to your situation.
3. Review your completed form for accuracy and completeness.
4. Save and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sexual Consent Form** (`consent_form`, text, not required): This is the title of the form that appears at the top.
* **Partner Information** (`partner_info`, text, not required): Provide any additional information about the partner.
* **Safe From Disease Status** (`safe_from_disease`, select multiple, not required): Check the options for any diseases or conditions that may apply.
* **Partner Permissions** (`partner_permissions`, select one, required): Select one option for the partner's permissions.
* **Agreement Duration** (`agreement_duration`, date, not required): Enter the date of the agreement.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of consent.
* **Safe From Disease Hint** (`safe_from_disease_hint`, text, not required): This field is intended for notes or comments about the partner's disease status.
* **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, text, not required): Enter the partner's name.
* **Partner Relationship** (`partner_relationship`, text, not required): Describe the nature of the relationship with the partner.
* **Consent Note** (`consent_note`, note, not required): Enter any additional comments or notes about consent.
* **Partner Email** (`partner_email`, email, not required): Enter the partner's email address.
* **Partner Phone** (`partner_phone`, text, not required): Enter the partner's phone number.
* **Partner Address** (`partner_address`, text, not required): Enter the partner's address.
* **Partner DOB** (`partner_dob`, date, not required): Enter the partner's date of birth.
* **Consent Form Note** (`consent_form_note`, note, not required): Enter any additional comments about the form.
* **Partner Relationship Status** (`partner_relationship_status`, text, not required): Describe the status of the relationship.
* **Consent Date End** (`consent_date_end`, date, not required): Enter the end date of the consent.
* **Consent Note 2** (`consent_note_2`, note, not required): Enter any additional comments or notes about consent.
* **Partner Name 2** (`partner_name_2`, text, not required): Enter the name of the second partner.
* **Partner Phone 2** (`partner_phone_2`, text, not required): Enter the phone number of the second partner.
* **Partner Address 2** (`partner_address_2`, text, not required): Enter the address of the second partner.
* **Partner DOB 2** (`partner_dob_2`, date, not required): Enter the date of birth of the second partner.
* **Partner Relationship Status 2** (`partner_relationship_status_2`, text, not required): Describe the status of the relationship with the second partner.
* **Consent Form Note 2** (`consent_form_note_2`, note, not required): Enter any additional comments about the form.
* **Consent Form Note 3** (`consent_form_note_3`, note, not required): Enter any additional comments or notes about consent.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are completed.
* Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submission.
* Use clear and concise language in the text fields.
* Be thorough in providing information for the partner's permissions and disease status.
* If unsure about any field, seek guidance from a relevant authority or expert.
