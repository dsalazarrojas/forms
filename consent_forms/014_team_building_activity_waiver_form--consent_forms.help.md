# Team Building Activity Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather essential information from team members participating in team building activities. This form helps the activity organizers to identify potential hazards and medical conditions, and ensures that participants understand and agree to the risks involved.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2. If you have any medical condition or allergy, please provide details in the "Participant Medical Condition" and "Participant Allergies" fields.
3. Select the type of activity you will be participating in from the "Activity Type" field.
4. Fill out the "Emergency Contact" fields with your emergency contact's information.
5. Sign and date the "Waiver Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Building Activity Waiver Form** (`team_building_activity_waiver_form`, note, required: false): This field is not a field to fill out, it is just a heading.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Please fill in your name as it appears on your government ID.
* **Participant Age** (`participant_age`, number, required: false): Please enter your age.
* **Activity Type** (`activity_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select the type of activity you will be participating in. Options: Hiking, Kayaking, Rock climbing, Other.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (`emergency_contact_first_name`, text, required: false): Please fill out the first name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (`emergency_contact_last_name`, text, required: false): Please fill out the last name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required: false): Please fill out the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Participant Medical Condition** (`participant_medical_condition`, text, required: false): If you have any medical condition, please describe it here.
* **Participant Medical Condition Details** (`participant_medical_condition_details`, text, required: false): If you have any medical condition, please provide more details about it.
* **Participant Allergies** (`participant_allergies`, text, required: false): If you have any allergies, please list them here.
* **Participant Allergies Details** (`participant_allergies_details`, text, required: false): If you have any allergies, please provide more details about them.
* **Waiver Signature** (`waiver_signature`, text, required: false): Please sign and date this field to confirm you have read and agree to the waiver terms.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form carefully and accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to ask the activity organizers.
* This form is only for team building activities, do not fill it out for any other purpose.
