# Script Reading Session Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Script Reading Session Recording Consent Form" is a document that aims to obtain the consent of participants to record their script reading sessions. This form is essential for ensuring that participants are aware of and agree to the recording of their sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your participant information (name, email, and phone number) on the first page.
2. On the second page, select your consent level:
	* Label 1
	* Label 2
	* Label 3
3. You may add any notes about your participation or session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`script_reading_session_recording_consent_form_1`, text, required: false): 
	+ This is the first section of the form where you will fill in your basic participant information (name, email, and phone number).
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): 
	+ Fill in your participant's name as it should appear on the consent form.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required: false): 
	+ Enter your participant's email address to ensure we can contact you if needed.
* **Participant Phone Number** (`participant_phone`, text, required: false): 
	+ Fill in your participant's phone number for further communication.
* **Script Reading Session Recording Consent** (`script_reading_session_recording_consent_form_2`, text, required: false): 
	+ This is a placeholder section for future expansion, and currently not used.
* **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`, select_multiple, required: false): 
	+ Select one or multiple options for giving your consent to record your session (Label 1, Label 2, Label 3). The Label options may have different levels of consent or other specific conditions for recording.
* **Notes** (`participant_notes`, note, required: false): 
	+ Add any personal thoughts, comments or any other information about your participation that you would like to share with us.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form carefully, and make sure to review it before submitting.
- Ensure that you select all the consent levels that apply to you.
- You can add any notes about your session, and we will take them into account during the recording.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, reach out to us for further guidance.
