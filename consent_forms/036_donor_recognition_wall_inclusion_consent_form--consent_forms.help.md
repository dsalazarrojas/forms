# Donor Recognition Wall Inclusion Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to obtain consent from donors and their spouses/partners for their information to be displayed on the Donor Recognition Wall. The Donor Recognition Wall is a public display of donors' names and contributions to our organization. By filling out this form, you are giving consent for your name and other identifying information to be included on the Donor Recognition Wall.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
2.  Select the option "Yes" under "Donor Recognition" if you wish to be included on the Donor Recognition Wall.
3.  Fill out your name, address, city, state, and zip as prompted.
4.  Provide your email and phone number (if desired).
5.  If you are a married couple or partner, provide your spouse/partner's name, address, city, state, and zip.
6.  Please review your responses and ensure they are accurate before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Donor Recognition** (`Donor Recognition`, `select_multiple`, required): Select "Yes" if you wish to be included on the Donor Recognition Wall. Select "No" if you do not want your information displayed.
*   **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as you would like it to appear on the Donor Recognition Wall.
*   **Name Of Organization** (`Name Of Organization`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your organization or employer (if applicable).
*   **Address** (`Address`, `text`, required): Enter your home or office address.
*   **City** (`City`, `text`, required): Enter the city where you live or work.
*   **State** (`State`, `text`, required): Enter your state of residence.
*   **Zip** (`Zip`, `text`, required): Enter your zip code.
*   **Consent** (`Consent`, `note`, required): This is an informational field explaining that by filling out this form, you are giving consent for your information to be displayed on the Donor Recognition Wall.
*   **Email** (`Email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted or if you would like to receive updates about the Donor Recognition Wall.
*   **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted or if you would like to receive updates about the Donor Recognition Wall.
*   **Name Of Spouse** (`Name Of Spouse`, `text`, optional): Enter your spouse's or partner's name.
*   **Name Of Spouse Organization** (`Name Of Spouse Organization`, `text`, optional): Enter your spouse's or partner's organization or employer (if applicable).
*   **Spouse Address** (`Spouse Address`, `text`, optional): Enter your spouse's or partner's address.
*   **Spouse City** (`Spouse City`, `text`, optional): Enter the city where your spouse or partner lives or works.
*   **Spouse State** (`Spouse State`, `text`, optional): Enter your spouse's or partner's state of residence.
*   **Spouse Zip** (`Spouse Zip`, `text`, optional): Enter your spouse's or partner's zip code.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.
*   Review your responses carefully to ensure they are accurate.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.

This form will help us gather the necessary information for the Donor Recognition Wall and ensure that we can display your contributions accurately and respectfully.
