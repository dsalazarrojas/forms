# podcast_guest_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect important information from podcast guests to ensure they are aware of the terms of publication for their appearance on the podcast and to obtain their consent to publish their content.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the `guest_name` field with the name of the guest.
2. Enter the `podcast_title` field with the title of the podcast episode.
3. Select the date of publication from the `publication_date` field.
4. Indicate whether the publication will be in audio format, text, or other medium in the `publication_medium` field.
5. Select whether the guest grants permission for the podcast episode to be published in its entirety (`Yes` or `No`).
6. Choose whether the guest grants permission for the podcast episode to be published in its entirety (`Yes` or `No`).
7. Indicate whether the guest grants permission for the content of the podcast episode to be used (`Yes` or `No`).
8. Upload an audio file if applicable.
9. Finally, select whether the guest grants permission for the content to be published (`Yes` or `No`).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **guest_name** (text, required): Enter the name of the guest who is releasing the content.
* **podcast_title** (text, required): Enter the title of the podcast episode featuring the guest.
* **publication_date** (date, required): Select the date the content will be published.
* **publication_medium** (text, required): Choose the medium in which the content will be published (e.g., audio, text, other).
* **publication_type** (select_multiple, required): Select whether the podcast episode can be published in its entirety and shared on social media.
* **publication_rights** (select_one, required): Select whether the guest grants permission for the podcast episode to be published.
* **content_type** (select_one, required): Select whether the guest grants permission for the content of the podcast episode to be used.
* **audio_file** (text, optional): If applicable, upload an audio file related to the content.
* **consent** (select_one, required): Select whether the guest grants final permission for the content to be published.
