# Youth Activity Transportation Release - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain youth activity transportation release from parents or guardians. The primary purpose is to ensure that the youth's safety and well-being are respected during activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the youth's ID number.
2. Provide the youth's name.
3. If an emergency contact is present, provide their contact information.
4. Select the emergency contacts from the provided options.
5. Sign the form to confirm that you grant permission for the youth's participation in activities.
6. Enter the date and time you sign the form.
7. Confirm that you grant permission for the youth's participation in activities.
8. Finally, have the youth sign to confirm their understanding and agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Youth ID (Text)**: Enter the youth's ID number. This is a unique identifier for the youth.
* **Youth Name (Text)**: Enter the youth's name.
* **Emergency Contact Information (Text)**: If an emergency contact is present, provide their contact information. This could be a name, phone number, or email address.
* **Emergency Contacts (Select)**: Choose one or more emergency contacts from the provided options:
	+ Parent/Guardian
	+ Other
* **Signature (Note)**: Sign the form to grant permission for the youth's participation in activities.
* **Signature Date (Date)**: Enter the date you sign the form.
* **Signature Time (Time)**: Enter the time you sign the form.
* **Parent Signature (Select)**: Choose one of the provided options:
	+ I agree and consent
	+ I do not agree
* **Youth Signature (Select)**: Choose one of the provided options:
	+ I agree and consent
	+ I do not agree

## Tips
- Ensure that you are the parent or guardian signing this form.
- Make sure to review the form carefully before signing.
- Keep a record of this form for future reference, if necessary.
