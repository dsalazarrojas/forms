# PAR Q Fitness Form GBPT - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your physical activity habits and health status for health and fitness purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions about your medical conditions that may impact your physical activity.
2. Enter your current age.
3. Enter your height in meters.
4. Enter your weight in kilograms.
5. Enter your blood pressure.
6. Enter your resting heart rate.
7. List any previous injuries or illnesses that may impact your exercise routine.
8. Choose your exercise frequency (days per week).
9. Choose how often you exercise.
10. Choose the duration of your exercise sessions.
11. Choose where you exercise (at home or in a gym).
12. Choose who you exercise with (if anyone).
13. Choose if you have any medical equipment for exercise.
14. Choose if you have plans for future exercise.
15. Choose how you usually exercise.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Do you have any medical conditions that may impact your physical activity?** (`medical_conditions`): Check any medical conditions that you have that may affect your physical activity. This could include anything from chronic diseases to injuries.
* **What is your current age?** (`age`): Enter your current age to help us understand your overall health status.
* **What is your height (in meters)?** (`height`): Enter your height in meters to help us understand your physical stature.
* **What is your weight (in kg)?** (`weight`): Enter your weight in kilograms to help us understand your body composition.
* **What is your blood pressure?** (`blood_pressure`): Enter your blood pressure to help us understand your cardiovascular health.
* **What is your resting heart rate?** (`resting_heart_rate`): Enter your resting heart rate to help us understand your cardiovascular health.
* **Have you ever had any previous injuries or illnesses that may impact your exercise routine?** (`previous_injuries`): Check any previous injuries or illnesses that may affect your exercise routine.
* **What is your exercise frequency (days per week)?** (`exercise_frequency`): Choose how many days per week you exercise.
* **How often do you exercise?** (`exercise_type`): Choose how often you exercise (e.g., daily, weekly, etc.).
* **How long do you exercise each session?** (`exercise_session_duration`): Choose the duration of your exercise sessions.
* **Do you exercise at home or in a gym?** (`exercise_location`): Choose where you usually exercise.
* **Who is your exercise buddy?** (`exercise_buddy`): Choose who you exercise with (if anyone).
* **Do you have any medical equipment for exercise?** (`medical_equipment`): Check if you have any medical equipment for exercise.
* **Do you have any plans for future exercise plans?** (`future_exercise_plans`): Check if you have any plans for future exercise.
* **How do you usually exercise?** (`exercise_type_2`): Choose how you usually exercise.

## Tips
- Please answer accurately and honestly to provide the best possible information.
- If you are unsure about any question, choose the option that best describes your situation.
- You can always reach out to the form administrators if you have any questions or concerns.
