## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from customers for the consolidation of their utility accounts. Utility account consolidation is a process where multiple accounts with the same customer are merged into a single account for easier management and billing. This form is used to verify that the customer is aware of and agrees to the consolidation of their accounts.
The form is designed to be completed by customers who have multiple utility accounts and wish to consolidate them into a single account. This form is typically completed by the customer in person or over the phone.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name and contact information (customer name, customer email, and customer phone number).
2. Select the type of utility service provider (e.g. Electricity, Gas, Water, or Other).
3. List the account numbers of the accounts you wish to merge.
4. Indicate whether you wish to merge your accounts ("Yes" or "No").
5. Read the consent statement and sign below if you agree to the consolidation of your accounts.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your utility account.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your valid email address.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Utility Service Provider**: Select the type of utility service provider you are working with (e.g. Electricity, Gas, Water, or Other).
* **Account Information**: This field is for any additional information you would like to provide about your account(s).
* **Account Numbers**: Select all the account numbers you wish to merge into your new consolidated account.
* **Merge Accounts**: Indicate whether you wish to merge your accounts ("Yes" or "No").
* **Consent Statement**: Please read the following statement and sign below if you agree to the consolidation of your accounts:
  "I, [Customer Name], hereby grant permission to the [Company Name] to consolidate my utility accounts under a single account, as specified above. I understand that this consolidation may result in changes to my billing and payment terms."
  Sign below with your signature.
* **Signature**: Sign your name in this field to indicate your agreement to the consolidation of your accounts.
## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Make sure to review your selections carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the consolidation of your accounts, contact our customer service team.
