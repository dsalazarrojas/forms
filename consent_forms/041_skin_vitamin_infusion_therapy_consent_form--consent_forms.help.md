# skin-vitamin-infusion-therapy-consent-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from patients for skin vitamin infusion therapy, ensuring they understand the treatment and consent to it.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the **Patient Details** field with your relevant information.
2.  Describe your **Medical History** in the text field provided.
3.  Select the **Treatment Consent** option that best describes your understanding of the treatment.
4.  Choose the **Skin Vitamin Treatments** you are willing to undergo from the dropdown menu.
5.  If necessary, provide additional **Notes** about your treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Patient Details (page 1)**: Please enter your personal details in this text field.
*   **Medical History (page 1)**: Provide a clear and concise description of your medical history.
*   **Treatment Consent (page 1)**: Choose one of the two options that best describe your understanding of the treatment:
    •   I have read and understand the information about the treatment
    •   I have not read and do not understand the information about the treatment
*   **Skin Vitamin Treatments (page 1)**: Select the treatments you are willing to undergo from the dropdown menu:
    •   Aminobutyric Acid (Aminobutyric Acid 0.05%)
    •   Biotin (Biotin 0.1mg/0.1ml)
    •   Glycoprotein (Glycoprotein 0.05%)
*   **Notes (page 1)**: Use this text field to provide any additional information that may be helpful for your treatment.
