# Cremation Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cremation Authorization Form is a document used to obtain authorization from authorized agents to cremate a deceased individual. It is used to provide the necessary information required for the cremation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand its purpose and the information required.
2. Fill out the required fields with the relevant information, including:
	* Authorized Agent's Response (Yes or No)
	* Authorized Agent's Email
	* Authorized Agent's Phone
	* Cremation Fund Amount (if applicable)
3. If "Authorized Agent" is Yes, select the authorized agents from the options provided.
4. Submit the completed form to the relevant authorities for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Cremation Authorization** (`cremation_authorization_form`, note, required: false): This is the main section of the form where the authorized agent's response is recorded. It is essential to answer this question truthfully to ensure the cremation process proceeds correctly.
* **Cremation Date** (`cremation_date`, date, required: false): This field is used to record the date of the cremation. Ensure you enter the correct date to avoid any discrepancies.
* **Cremation Time** (`cremation_time`, time, required: false): This field is used to record the time of the cremation. Enter the correct time to ensure accurate record-keeping.
* **Authorized Agent** (`authorized_agent`, select_multiple, required: false): If you are an authorized agent, select "Yes" from the options provided. This will allow you to proceed with the cremation process.
* **Authorized Agent Email** (`authorized_agent_email`, email, required: false): This field is used to record the authorized agent's email address. Enter the correct email address to ensure communication is maintained.
* **Authorized Agent Phone** (`authorized_agent_phone`, text, required: false): This field is used to record the authorized agent's phone number. Enter the correct phone number to ensure effective communication.
* **Cremation Fund** (`cremation_fund`, number, required: false): This field is used to record the amount of the cremation fund. If you are paying for the cremation, enter the correct amount here.
* **Cremation Fund Other** (`cremation_fund_other`, text, required: false): This field is used to provide additional information about the cremation fund. Enter any relevant details here.
