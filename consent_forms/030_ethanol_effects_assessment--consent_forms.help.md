This help guide is intended to provide users with a clear understanding of the Ethanol Effects Assessment form. It's designed to collect data on participants' experiences with alcohol and drug use, as well as their relationships with their parents, friends, and partners. This information will help researchers and clinicians better understand the impact of substance use on various aspects of a person's life.

Before starting the form, please ensure that you have not filled it out previously. If you've already completed this form, your responses will be overwritten.

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

## Purpose

The Ethanol Effects Assessment form is designed to collect information about your experiences with alcohol and drug use, including your age, gender, ethnicity, and substance use history. This data will help researchers and clinicians understand the effects of substance use on various aspects of your life.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Answer all questions honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.
2.  Fill in the form in sections, starting from the first page and moving to the last page.
3.  Use the options provided for each question to select the most accurate response.
4.  Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First name** (`participant_identifier`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Last name** (`participant_identifier`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Age** (`age`, `number`, required): Please enter your current age.
*   **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required): Please select your gender (M or F).
*   **Ethnicity** (`ethnicity`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select one or more of the following options: American Indian or Alaskan Native, Asian, Black or African American, White, Other.
*   **How much alcohol** (`alcohol_use`, `number`, required): Please select the amount of alcohol you have used in the past year. Note: Please answer this question only if you have used alcohol in the past year.
*   **How often** (`alcohol_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Please select how often you have used alcohol in the past year. Options are: Every day, Most days, Some days, Rarely.
*   **Have you ever drunk alcohol** (`drink_alcohol`, `select_one`, required): Please select whether you have ever used alcohol.
*   **Have you ever smoked tobacco** (`smoke_tobacco`, `select_one`, required): Please select whether you have ever smoked tobacco.
*   **How often smoke** (`smoke_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Please select how often you have smoked tobacco. Options are: Every day, Most days, Some days, Rarely. Note: Please answer this question only if you have smoked tobacco.
*   **Daily amount smoked** (`smoke_amount`, `number`, optional): Please enter the amount of tobacco you smoke per day. Note: Please answer this question only if you have smoked tobacco.
*   **Have you ever used drugs** (`drug_use`, `select_one`, required): Please select whether you have ever used drugs.
*   **How often use drugs** (`drug_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Please select how often you have used drugs. Options are: Every day, Most days, Some days, Rarely. Note: Please answer this question only if you have used drugs.
*   **Daily amount use** (`drug_amount`, `number`, optional): Please enter the amount of drugs you use per day. Note: Please answer this question only if you have used drugs.
*   **Have you been screened for alcohol or drug use** (`have_you_been_screened`, `select_one`, required): Please select whether you have ever been screened for alcohol or drug use.
*   **Date screened** (`screened_date`, `date`, optional): Please select the date of your last screening. Note: Please answer this question only if you have been screened for alcohol or drug use.
*   **Screened by** (`screened_by`, `text`, optional): Please enter the name of the person who screened you for alcohol or drug use. Note: Please answer this question only if you have been screened for alcohol or drug use.
*   **How often screened** (`screening_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Please select how often you have been screened for alcohol or drug use. Options are: Once, Multiple times. Note: Please answer this question only if you have been screened for alcohol or drug use.
*   **Have you ever told your parents about your drug or alcohol use** (`have_your_parents`, `select_one`, optional): Please select whether you have ever informed your parents about your drug or alcohol use.
*   **Do your parents know about your drug or alcohol use** (`parent_knowledge`, `select_one`, required): Please select whether your parents are informed about your drug or alcohol use.
*   **How often your parents know** (`parent_knowledge_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Please select how often your parents are informed about your drug or alcohol use. Options are: Every time, Rarely.
*   **Have you ever told your friends about your drug or alcohol use** (`have_your_friends`, `select_one`, optional): Please select whether you have ever informed your friends about your drug or alcohol use.
*   **Do your friends know about your drug or alcohol use** (`friend_knowledge`, `select_one`, required): Please select whether your friends are informed about your drug or alcohol use.
*   **How often do your friends know** (`friend_knowledge_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Please select how often your friends are informed about your drug or alcohol use. Options are: Every time, Rarely.
*   **Have you ever informed your partner about your drug or alcohol use** (`have_your_partners`, `select_one`, optional): Please select whether you have ever informed your partner about your drug or alcohol use.
*   **Do your partner know about your drug or alcohol use** (`partner_knowledge`, `select_one`, required): Please select whether your partner is informed about your drug or alcohol use.
*   **How often your partner know** (`partner_knowledge_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Please select how often your partner is informed about your drug or alcohol use. Options are: Every time, Rarely.

## Tips

*   Please answer all questions honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.
*   If you are unsure about any question, please consult with a healthcare professional or seek guidance from a trusted individual.
*   If you have any concerns or questions about the form, please contact the administrators or support team.
*   Please note that the information collected will be kept confidential and used for research purposes only.
