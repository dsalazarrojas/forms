# veterinary_care_agreement_extension_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information and obtain consent for the extension of veterinary care services. It is designed to collect essential details from the owner and their pet, as well as medical history, treatment, and consent dates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Pet Info section with your pet's details.
2. Provide your contact information as the owner in the Owner Contact section.
3. Enter the veterinary information and medical history in the Veterinary Info and Medical History sections.
4. Select the treatment options that apply to your pet's condition.
5. Sign the Owner Declaration to confirm that you have read and understood the information provided.
6. Provide the consent date and time in the Consent Date and Consent Time sections.
7. Choose the veterinary clinics where you would like to extend your care services.
8. Sign the Veterinary Signature to confirm that you have read and understood the information provided.
9. Add any additional notes or comments in the Notes section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Info** (Pet Info, text, required): Enter your pet's name, species, and age.
* **Owner Contact** (Owner Contact, text, required): Provide your name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Veterinary Info** (Veterinary Info, text, required): Enter the name and address of the veterinary clinic providing care.
* **Medical Info** (Medical Info, text, required): Enter any relevant medical information about your pet.
* **Treatment Info** (Treatment Info, text, required): Select the treatment options that apply to your pet's condition.
* **Owner Declaration** (Owner Declaration, text, required): Sign to confirm that you have read and understood the information provided.
* **Consent Date** (Consent Date, date, required): Enter the date you are consenting to the extension of care services.
* **Consent Time** (Consent Time, time, required): Enter the time you are consenting to the extension of care services.
* **Veterinary Care Agreement Extension** (Veterinary Care Agreement Extension, text, required): Enter any additional information or comments about the extension of care services.
* **Veterinary Clinics** (Veterinary Clinics, select_multiple, required): Choose the veterinary clinics where you would like to extend your care services.
* **Medical Conditions** (Medical Conditions, select_multiple, required): Select the medical conditions that apply to your pet's condition.
* **Medical History** (Medical History, text, required): Enter any relevant medical history about your pet.
* **Owner Signature** (Owner Signature, text, required): Sign as the pet owner to confirm that you have read and understood the information provided.
* **Veterinary Signature** (Veterinary Signature, text, required): Sign as the veterinarian to confirm that you have read and understood the information provided.
* **Notes** (Notes, text, required): Add any additional comments or notes about the extension of care services.

## Tips
Please take your time to fill out this form accurately and completely. Ensure that all fields are filled out with the most up-to-date information. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to your veterinarian for guidance.
