# patient_consent_validation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to validate a patient's consent for medical procedures or treatments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with the required information, or select an option from the provided choices.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out before proceeding.
3. Review the form carefully to ensure that all information is accurate and complete.
