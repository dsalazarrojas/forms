# Integrity Agreement For Coordinators - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about program coordinators, including their contact details, program information, and agreement dates. It is essential to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure the successful execution and management of programs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your personal and program information in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the program goals that apply to your program from the options provided.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm your understanding and agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Coordinator Information** (Text): Enter your personal details as a program coordinator.
* **Co-ordinator name** (Text): Enter your name as a program coordinator.
* **Co-ordinator email** (Email): Enter your email address as a program coordinator.
* **Co-ordinator phone** (Text): Enter your phone number as a program coordinator.
* **Program Information** (Text): Enter information about the program you are coordinating.
* **Program name** (Text): Enter the name of the program you are coordinating.
* **Program start date** (Date): Enter the start date of the program.
* **Program end date** (Date): Enter the end date of the program.
* **Program location** (Text): Enter the location of the program.
* **Program goals** (Select One): Select the goals that apply to your program from the options provided.
* **Coordinator agreement** (Note): Enter any additional information about your role and responsibilities as a program coordinator.
* **Co-ordinator agreement date** (Date): Enter the date you agreed to the program terms and conditions.
* **Program agreement** (Note): Enter any additional information about your program.
* **Program agreement date** (Date): Enter the date you agreed to the program terms and conditions.
* **Additional information** (Text): Enter any additional information about your program.
* **Coordinator Signature** (Text): Enter your signature as a program coordinator.
* **Date** (Date): Enter the current date.
* **Title** (Text): Enter your title as a program coordinator.
* **Name** (Text): Enter your name as a program coordinator.
* **Signature Date** (Date): Enter the date you signed the form.
* **Signature 2** (Text): Enter your signature as a program coordinator.
* **Role** (Text): Enter your role as a program coordinator.
* **Role other** (Text): Enter any other role you would like to specify.
* **Role other other** (Text): Enter any other role you would like to specify.
* **Signature 2** (Text): Enter your signature as a program coordinator.

Note: Some fields are not required, so fill them in accordingly based on your specific needs.
