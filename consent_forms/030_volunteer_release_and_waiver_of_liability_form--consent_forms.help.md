# volunteer_release_and_waiver_of_liability_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect volunteer information and obtain their waiver of liability agreements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer name**: Enter your name as a volunteer.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your contact information, including phone number and email address.
* **Address**: Enter your address (if applicable).
* **Volunteer Start Date**: Select the date you began volunteering.
* **Volunteer End Date**: Select the date you expect to end volunteering.
* **Organization's Awareness**: Choose whether your organization has made you aware of the risks and responsibilities.
* **Waiver of Liability**: Select whether you're waiving your liability.
* **Volunteer Acknowledgement**: Confirm that you're aware of the terms of your volunteering.
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide information about your emergency contact, including their phone number and relationship to you.
* **Volunteer Signature**: Enter your signature.
* **Organization's Signature**: Enter your organization's signature.
* **Date**: Enter the current date.
* **Time**: Enter the current time.
* **Volunteer's IP Address**: Optional: Enter your IP address (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact IP**: Optional: Enter your emergency contact's IP address (if applicable).
* **Volunteer Notes**: Optional: Enter any notes from you or your organization.
* **Organization Notes**: Optional: Enter any notes from your organization.
* **Emergency Contact Notes**: Optional: Enter any notes from your emergency contact.
* **Organization Comments**: Optional: Enter any comments from your organization.
