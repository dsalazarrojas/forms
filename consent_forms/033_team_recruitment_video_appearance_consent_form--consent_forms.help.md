# Team Recruitment Video Appearance Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain the consent of participants to be featured in a team recruitment video. It is designed to ensure that participants are aware of how their appearance will be used and to document their consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant information page with your details.
2. On the following page, choose the schools you have been to (e.g., School A, School B, School C).
3. If applicable, choose the HR teams you have worked with (e.g., HR A, HR B, HR C).
4. On the final page, select whether you are willing to provide your consent for the video appearance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`): This field is for you to provide your details. Please fill in your name, email, and phone number.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`): This field is for your name.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`): This field is for your email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`): This field is for your phone number. If you do not have a phone number, you can leave this field blank.
* **Participant Video Consent** (`participant_video_consent`): This field is to confirm whether you are willing to appear in the video. Please select "Yes" or "No".
* **School Video Consent** (`school_video_consent`): If you have been to any schools, please select the schools you have been to.
* **HR Video Consent** (`hr_video_consent`): If you have worked with any HR teams, please select the teams you have worked with.
* **Marketing Video Consent** (`marketing_video_consent`): If you are a marketing representative, please select the teams you have worked with.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all the required fields accurately.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the relevant personnel.
* Make sure to review your selections carefully before submitting the form.
