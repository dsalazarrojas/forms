# Field Trip Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Field Trip Consent Form is a necessary document that outlines the details of a field trip, including the students participating, trip dates and times, locations, and contact information for parents and school staff. This form helps ensure that everyone involved is informed and agrees to the trip's terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out your name and student ID (for students) or your name (for parents/teachers/staff) in the "Student Name" and "Student ID" sections.
2.  If you're a parent, fill out your name in the "Parent Name" section. If you're a student, leave this section blank.
3.  Provide your emergency contact's name and phone number in the "Emergency Contact Name" and "Emergency Contact Phone" sections, respectively.
4.  Enter the date and time of the field trip in the "Field Trip Date" and "Field Trip Time" sections.
5.  Enter the location of the field trip in the "Field Trip Location" section.
6.  Fill out the name of the chaperone assigned to you in the "Chaperone Name" section.
7.  Enter the chaperone's phone number in the "Chaperone Phone" section, if applicable.
8.  Sign and date the form with your signature and the date you signed it in the "Student Signature", "Chaperone Signature", "Parent Signature", "School Admin Signature", and "Teacher Signature" sections.
9.  Ensure that each person completes the form as required and signs it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Field Trip Consent**: This is the main section for the field trip details. It's a text field for capturing any specific information relevant to the trip.
* **Student Name**: Fill out your name if you're a student. This is a required field.
* **Student ID**: Enter your student ID if you're a student. This is a required field.
* **Parent Name**: Fill out your name if you're a parent. This is an optional field.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact person. This is an optional field.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person. This is an optional field.
* **Field Trip Date**: Enter the date of the field trip. This is a required field.
* **Field Trip Time**: Enter the time of the field trip. This is a required field.
* **Field Trip Location**: Enter the location of the field trip. This is a required field.
* **Chaperone Name**: Fill out the name of your assigned chaperone. This is a required field.
* **Chaperone Phone**: Enter the phone number of your chaperone, if applicable. This is an optional field.
* **Student Signature**: Sign and date this section if you're a student. This is a required field.
* **Chaperone Signature**: Sign and date this section if you're a chaperone. This is a required field.
* **Parent Signature**: Sign and date this section if you're a parent. This is a required field.
* **School Admin Signature**: Sign and date this section if you're a school administrator. This is a required field.
* **Teacher Signature**: Sign and date this section if you're a teacher. This is a required field.
* **Field Trip Conduct Supervisor**: Fill out the name of the field trip conduct supervisor. This is a required field.
* **Field Trip Coordinator**: Fill out the name of the field trip coordinator. This is a required field.
* **Teacher Name**: Fill out your name if you're a teacher. This is an optional field.
* **Field Trip Supervisor**: Fill out the name of the field trip supervisor. This is a required field.
* **Field Trip Chaperone**: Fill out the name of the field trip chaperone. This is a required field.
* **Date Signed**: Fill out the date you signed this form. This is a required field.
* **Time Signed**: Fill out the time you signed this form. This is a required field.
* **Signed By**: Fill out who signed this form. This is a required field.
