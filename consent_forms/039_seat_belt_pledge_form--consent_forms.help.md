# Seat Belt Pledge Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect self-reported information about seat belt usage from vehicle operators.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form for the correct vehicle you are operating.
2. For each field, provide accurate and honest information as requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Input** (`user_input`, note, required: false): This is a free-text note where you can provide any additional information or comments relevant to your seat belt usage.
- **Driver's License** (`driver_license`, text, required: false): Enter your driver's license number to identify you as the vehicle operator.
- **Seat Belt Status** (`seat_belt`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate whether you always wear your seat belt, sometimes wear it, or never wear it while driving.
- **Number of Passengers** (`passengers`, number, required: false): Report the number of passengers you are transporting.
- **Seat Belt Pledge** (`seat_belt_pledge`, select_one, required: false): Choose whether you will always wear your seat belt, never wear it, or are undecided about it.
- **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Record the date you completed this form.
- **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Record the time you completed this form.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
- Ensure you have completed this form for the correct vehicle.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or HR department.
