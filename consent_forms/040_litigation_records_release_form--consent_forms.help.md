# Litigation Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Litigation Records Release Form is a document used to request access to records related to a specific case from a litigious entity. This form is typically used to obtain necessary information or records for the purpose of litigation, settlement, or other legal proceedings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you are authorized to complete this form and that the case is currently active and not already released.
2. Enter the Case Number of the relevant case.
3. Enter the name of the Attorney of record.
4. Enter the Release Date, which should be the date the records are being released to the requesting party.
5. Provide the Reason for Release, which should be a brief explanation of why the records are being released.
6. Enter the Name on Record, which should be the name of the individual or entity whose records are being released.
7. Enter the Record Number, which should be the unique identifier for the record being requested.
8. If applicable, enter the Date of Death for the individual or entity whose records are being released.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Case Number** (`case_number`, number, required/optional): Enter the unique identifier assigned to the case by the court or legal entity.
* **Attorney Name** (`attorney_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the attorney representing the individual or entity related to the case.
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the records are being released to the requesting party.
* **Reason for Release** (`reason_for_release`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief explanation of why the records are being released.
* **Name on Record** (`name_on_record`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the individual or entity whose records are being released.
* **Record Number** (`record_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the unique identifier for the record being requested.
* **Date of Death** (`date_of_death`, date, required/optional): If applicable, enter the date of death for the individual or entity whose records are being released.

## Tips
* Ensure that the case is currently active and not already released before completing this form.
* Verify that you are authorized to complete this form and have the necessary permissions to access the requested records.
* If the records being released pertain to an individual or entity who is deceased, enter the Date of Death in the relevant field.
