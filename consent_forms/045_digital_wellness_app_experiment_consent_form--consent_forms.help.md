# Digital Wellness App Experiment Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Digital Wellness App Experiment Consent Form is a required form for participants to give their consent to participate in a digital wellness app experiment. This form must be completed by participants who have already been recruited and briefed on the study's objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully before filling it out.
2. Fill out the required fields (participant_name, consent_date, and consent_status).
3. Enter your participant information (participant_name, participant_email, participant_phone, and research_participant_id).
4. Enter study information (research_study_id, study_name, and study_start).
5. Sign the consent form by selecting 'Yes' for the consent_status field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): This is the name of the participant. Please enter your legal name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): This is the email address associated with your participant account.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): This is your contact phone number.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, required): This is the date on which you are giving your consent to participate in the study.
* **Consent Form** (`consent_form`, text, optional): This field is not used in this form, but it is kept for future reference.
* **Consent Status** (`consent_status`, select_one, optional): This is a confirmation that you are giving your consent to participate in the study. Please select 'Yes' to confirm your consent.
* **Research Participant ID** (`research_participant_id`, text, optional): This is a unique ID assigned to you by the research team. It will be provided to you if you are accepted into the study.
* **Research Study ID** (`research_study_id`, text, optional): This is the ID of the research study you are participating in. It will be provided to you if you are accepted into the study.
* **Study Name** (`study_name`, text, optional): This is the name of the research study you are participating in.
* **Study Start** (`study_start`, date, optional): This is the date on which the research study will start. It will be provided to you if you are accepted into the study.
