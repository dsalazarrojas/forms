# Nurse Practitioner Practice Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from nurse practitioners regarding their collaboration and practice details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the 'Signatures' field.
3. Enter your practice agreement information in the 'Practice Agreement' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Collaboration Details** (`collaboration_details`, text, optional): Please enter a brief description of your collaboration with the healthcare organization.
* **Practice Agreement** (`practice_agreement`, text, required): Enter the details of your practice agreement, including any relevant terms and conditions.
* **Signatures** (`signatures`, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' to indicate that you have received and reviewed a copy of the practice agreement, or 'No' if you have not.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have received and reviewed a copy of the practice agreement before completing this form.
* This form should only be completed once you have been accepted as a collaborator by the healthcare organization.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the relevant healthcare organization for clarification.
