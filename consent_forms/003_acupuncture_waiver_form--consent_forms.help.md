# Acupuncture Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Acupuncture Waiver Form is designed to inform and protect both the client and the practitioner by gathering essential information about the client, identifying potential risks and contraindications, and obtaining informed consent for treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Select the appropriate answers for each question.
3. Sign the form to confirm your understanding and agreement to the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Client Information/2): Please provide your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (Client Information/3): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contact Phone Number** (Client Information/4): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email Address** (Client Information/5): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Area(s) of treatment** (Client Information/6): Specify the area(s) of your body that will be treated.
* **Reason for seeking acupuncture treatment** (Client Information/7): Briefly describe why you are seeking acupuncture treatment.
* **Risk Acknowledgment and Consent** (Risk Acknowledgment and Consent/8): Please read and understand the risks associated with acupuncture treatment, including but not limited to bruising, soreness, infection, and pneumothorax.
* **I understand the risks** (Risk Acknowledgment and Consent/9): Select "I acknowledge" to confirm your understanding of the risks.
* **Informed Consent** (Informed Consent/10): Select "I agree" to confirm you have been fully informed of the treatment protocol and are ready to proceed.
* **Medical Conditions and Contraindications** (Medical Conditions and Contraindications/11): Read the note and acknowledge any medical conditions or contraindications that may affect treatment.
* **Bleeding disorder or blood thinning medication** (Medical Conditions and Contraindications/12): Select "True" if you have a bleeding disorder or take blood-thinning medication.
* **Cardiac Pacemaker** (Medical Conditions and Contraindications/13): Select "True" if you have a cardiac pacemaker or other electrical device implanted.
* **Pregnancy Status** (Medical Conditions and Contraindications/14): Select "True" if you are currently pregnant, "False" if you are not, or "Possibly" if you are unsure.
* **Active Infection or wound** (Medical Conditions and Contraindications/15): Select "True" if you have an active infection or open wound in the treatment area.
* **Recent Surgery** (Medical Conditions and Contraindications/16): Select "True" if you have had surgery in the treatment area within the past 6 months.
* **Medical Disclosure** (Medical Disclosure/17): Describe any other medical conditions or concerns that may affect treatment.
* **Release of Liability** (Release of Liability/18): Select "I agree" to release and hold harmless the practitioner and facility from claims or damages arising from treatment.
* **I release and hold harmless** (Release of Liability/19): Select "I agree" to confirm your release of liability.
* **Signature Acknowledgment** (Signature Acknowledgment/20): Select "I confirm" to affirm that all information provided is true and accurate.
* **Date of Signature** (Signature Acknowledgment/21): Enter the date of your signature in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
