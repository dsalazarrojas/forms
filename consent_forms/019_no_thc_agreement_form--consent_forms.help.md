# No THC Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The No THC Agreement Form is a document that aims to record and confirm whether employees or participants are aware of and agree to the use of THC in their work or participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please enter the name of the employee or participant who is submitting this form.
* Enter the name of the supervisor or manager who is acknowledging this agreement.
* Select a valid date for the acknowledgment.
* Choose the agreement status from the dropdown list (Accepted, Declined, or Pending).
* If the agreement status is Declined, please provide a reason for the decline.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee or Participant** (Employee or Participant, text, optional): Enter your name or the name of the person submitting this form.
* **Supervisor or Manager** (Supervisor or Manager, text, optional): Enter your name or the name of the person acknowledging this agreement.
* **Acknowledgment Date** (Acknowledgment Date, date, optional): Please enter a valid date for the acknowledgment.
* **Agreement Status** (Agreement Status, select_one, required): Select one of the options: Accepted, Declined, or Pending.
	+ *If you choose "Declined", please provide the reason below.*
* **Reason for Decline** (Reason for Decline, text, optional): Enter a brief explanation for declining the agreement.
* **Employee Name** (Employee Name, text, optional): Enter your name if you are the submitter.
* **Employee ID** (Employee ID, text, optional): Enter your employee ID if you are the submitter.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the employee or participant is aware of the form's purpose and requirements.
* If you choose "Declined" as the agreement status, it is helpful to provide a brief explanation for the reason.
* Review and verify the accuracy of the information before submitting the form.
