Emergency-Preparedness Documentary Consent Form - Help Guide
======================================================

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information and consent for documentary production and research purposes. It is intended for participants involved in emergency-preparedness documentaries, such as emergency responders, medical professionals, or individuals who have experienced emergency situations. The form aims to record participant roles, consent, and any additional information.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide your participant details, including your name and any relevant contact information.
2. Select your role(s) related to emergency preparedness (e.g., responder, medical professional, participant).
3. Choose the type(s) of consent you are giving for the documentary (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
4. If you wish, you can provide additional information about your experience or involvement in emergency situations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required: false)
Provide your name and any relevant contact information, such as your email address or phone number.

### **Participant Roles** (`participant_roles`, select_one, required: false)
Select the role(s) that best describe your involvement or experience in emergency preparedness, such as:

* Role 1
* Role 2
* Role 3

### **Consent** (`consent`, select_multiple, required: true)
Choose the type of consent you are giving for the documentary, such as:

* Option 1
* Option 2
* Option 3

### **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`, note, required: false)
You can use this field to provide any additional information about your consent or experience. However, this is optional.

### **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, required: false)
If you have any additional information you'd like to share about your experience or involvement in emergency preparedness, you can provide it here.

## Tips
Please ensure that you have read and understood the purpose and scope of this form before completing it. If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
