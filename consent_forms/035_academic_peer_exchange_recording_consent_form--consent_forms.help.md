# Academic Peer Exchange Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Peer Exchange Recording Consent Form is used to collect consent from participants in peer exchange recording sessions. This form ensures that participants are aware of and consent to the recording of their sessions, which may be published or used for evaluation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the participant's name in the "Participant name" field.
2. Enter the participant's email address in the "Participant email" field for recording confirmation purposes.
3. Select the title of the session being recorded in the "Session title" field.
4. Choose the type of recording being made (Audio, Video, or Both) in the "Recording type" field.
5. Describe how the recording will be used in the "Purpose of recording" field.
6. Indicate whether or not you consent to the recording being published in the "Consent to publish recording" field.
7. Specify the duration of retention of the recording in the "Data retention period" field (optional).
8. Type your name to serve as a signature in the "Participant signature" field.
9. Add any additional notes or instructions in the "Additional notes" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant name**: Full name of the participant who is being recorded.
* **Participant email**: Email address of the participant for recording confirmation purposes.
* **Session title**: Title of the peer exchange session being recorded.
* **Recording type**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Audio: Recording of audio only.
	+ Video: Recording of video only.
	+ Both: Recording of both audio and video.
* **Purpose of recording**: Describe how the recording will be used (e.g., for evaluation, publication, or other purposes).
* **Consent to publish recording**: Check the box to indicate that you consent to the recording being published.
* **Data retention period**: Specify the duration for which the recording will be stored (optional).
* **Participant signature**: Type your name to serve as a signature.
* **Additional notes**: Provide any additional notes or instructions (optional).
