# Online Game Demo Highlight Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from gamers for featuring their clips in an online game demo. It captures both game and gamer details to ensure that we have the necessary permissions to feature their content.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes, feature my clips" to give your consent for your clips to be featured in the online game demo.
2. Provide your gamer details, including your name, email, phone number, and tag.
3. If you are a parent or guardian of the gamer, provide your email address.
4. Upload any additional information or files as required.
5. Ensure that you have selected the correct options for game-specific information, such as game name, game tag, game platform, and game link.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Game Details**: Enter the game name, game tag, game platform, and game link.
* **Gamer Details**: Enter your gamer name, email, phone number, and tag.
* **Highlight Consent**: Select "Yes, feature my clips" to give your consent for your clips to be featured in the online game demo.
* **Upload Consent**: Select "Yes, feature my clips" to give your consent for your uploads to be featured in the online game demo.
* **Email Consent**: Select "Yes" to give your consent for us to contact you via email.
* **Contact Details**: Enter any additional contact details, such as your address or phone number.
* **Upload**: Enter any additional information or files as required.
* **Game Id**: Enter the game ID.
* **Upload Date**: Enter the date of the upload.
* **Upload Time**: Enter the time of the upload.
* **Game Name**: Enter the name of the game.
* **Game Tag**: Enter the tag of the game.
* **Game Platform**: Enter the platform of the game.
* **Game Link**: Enter the link to the game.
* **Team**: Enter the name of the team.
* **Gamer Tag**: Enter the tag of the gamer.
* **Parent/Guardian**: Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
* **Guardian Email**: Enter the email of the parent or guardian.
* **Game Platform Tag**: Enter the tag of the game platform.
* **Game Id Tag**: Enter the tag of the game ID.
* **Gamer Id Tag**: Enter the tag of the gamer ID.
