# movie_screening_rights_permission_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to capture information related to movie screenings, including contact details, screening details, and submission status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Screening Rights Form**: This is the main section of the form where you will enter the basic screening details.
	* Enter the Screening Date in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
2. **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person in charge of the screening.
3. **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
4. **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
5. **Date of Birth**: Enter the date of birth of the contact person.
6. **Time of Birth**: Enter the time of birth of the contact person. (Note: This field seems unusual; please double-check its purpose)
7. **Screening Date**: Enter the date of the screening event in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
8. **Screening Time**: Enter the time of the screening event in the format of HH:MM.
9. **Screening Location**: Enter the location where the screening will take place.
10. **Screening Duration**: Enter the duration of the screening event.
11. **Screening Venue**: Enter the venue where the screening will take place.
12. **Screening Equipment**: Enter any special equipment needed for the screening event.
13. **Screening Type**: Select the type of screening (Film, Video, Digital, Live, or Other).
14. **Screening Format**: Select all applicable formats (2D, 3D, HD, 4K, or Other).
15. **Screening Venue Occupancy**: Enter the occupancy of the screening venue.
16. **Screening Venue Capacity**: Enter the maximum capacity of the screening venue.
17. **Screening Venue Dimensions**: Enter the dimensions of the screening venue.
18. **Screening Venue Features**: Enter any notable features of the screening venue.
19. **Screening Venue Equipment**: Enter any special equipment available at the screening venue.
20. **Screening Rights Submitted**: Indicate if the screening rights have been submitted (True or False).
21. **Submitted Date**: Enter the date when this form was submitted.
22. **Submitted Time**: Enter the time when this form was submitted.
23. **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person submitting this form.
24. **Submitted Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
* Double-check the purpose and usage of each field to avoid any confusion.
* Review and verify information for accuracy before submitting the form.
