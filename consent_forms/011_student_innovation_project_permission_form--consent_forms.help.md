# Student Inovation Project Permission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from students and parents for a student's participation in an innovation project.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the "General Information" field with any necessary details.
2. Complete the "Student Details" field with the student's information.
3. Complete the "Parent Details" field with the parent/guardian's information.
4. Select "Consent" to indicate your agreement to the student's participation in the project.
5. Enter your contact information (email and phone number) in the "Email" and "Phone" fields.
6. Enter your parent/guardian's contact information (email and phone number) in the "Parent Email" and "Parent Phone" fields.
7. Sign and date the form with your signature and the date in the "Signature" and "Date Signed" fields.
8. Repeat steps 6 and 7 for the parent/guardian.
9. Ensure that both you and your parent/guardian have signed and dated the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information**: A free-form text field where you can enter any general information about the project or student.
* **Student Details**: A field for entering the student's personal details.
* **Parent Details**: A field for entering the parent/guardian's personal details.
* **Consent**: A select-one field that asks you to give your consent for the student's participation in the project.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Parent Email**: Enter your parent/guardian's email address.
* **Parent Phone**: Enter your parent/guardian's phone number.
* **Signature**: A field for signing the form.
* **Date Signed**: A field for entering the date you signed the form.
* **Student's Signature**: A field for the student to sign the form.
* **Date Signed (Student)**: A field for the student to enter the date they signed the form.
* **Date Signed (Parent)**: A field for the parent/guardian to enter the date they signed the form.
* **Parent's Signature**: A field for the parent/guardian to sign the form.

## Tips

* Ensure that both you and your parent/guardian have signed and dated the form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all information is accurate and complete.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to the project administrators.
