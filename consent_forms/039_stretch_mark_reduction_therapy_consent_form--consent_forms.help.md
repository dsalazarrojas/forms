# Stretch Mark Reduction Therapy Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a consent form for stretch mark reduction therapy. It collects client information, medical conditions, and consent details for treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the client's ID number in the "Client Id" field.
2. Enter the client's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format of MM/DD/YY.
3. Enter the client's email address in the "Email" field (optional).
4. Enter the client's phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).
5. Select the type of treatment the client will receive in the "Treatment Type" field:
	* Select "Stretch Mark Reduction" for standard stretch mark reduction therapy.
	* Select "Other (please specify)" for custom or other types of therapy.
6. If the client is receiving other types of therapy, enter the type in the "Other (please specify)" field.
7. Select any relevant medical conditions the client has in the "Medical Conditions" field:
	* Select "Hypertension" for clients with high blood pressure.
	* Select "Diabetes" for clients with diabetes.
	* Select "Pregnancy" for pregnant clients.
8. Enter the date and time of the consent form in the "Consent Form Date" and "Consent Form Time" fields, respectively.
9. Add a signature in the "Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Id** (1, number, required): Enter the client's unique identifier number.
* **Date of Birth** (2, date, required): Enter the client's date of birth in MM/DD/YY format.
* **Email** (3, email, optional): Enter the client's email address.
* **Phone** (4, text, optional): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Treatment Type** (5, select_one, required): Select the type of treatment the client will receive.
* **Other (please specify)** (6, text, optional): Enter the type of therapy if not selecting "Stretch Mark Reduction".
* **Medical Conditions** (7, select_multiple, optional): Select any relevant medical conditions the client has.
* **Consent Form Date** (8, date, required): Enter the date of the consent form.
* **Consent Form Time** (9, time, required): Enter the time of the consent form.
* **Signature** (10, note, optional): Add a signature to confirm consent.
