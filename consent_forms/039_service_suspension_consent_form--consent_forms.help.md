# Service Suspension Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Suspension Consent Form is used to obtain consent from a customer for the temporary suspension of a service. This form is used to ensure that customers understand and agree to the terms and conditions of the suspension, providing a clear record of their consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First name and Last name in the required text fields.
2. Enter your email address in the Email field.
3. Provide a phone number in the Phone field (if applicable).
4. Select the correct answer for Reason for suspension from the dropdown menu.
5. Select the correct answer for Customer consent from the dropdown menu, ensuring you have read, understand, and agree to the temporary service suspension.
6. Enter the date of the suspension in the Suspension date field.
7. Enter the time of the service suspension in the Service suspended field.
8. (Optional) Provide additional comments in the Additional comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
- **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide a phone number, if applicable.
- **Suspension date** (`suspension_date`, date, required): Select the date of the suspension.
- **Reason for suspension** (`reason_for_suspension`, text, required): Choose the reason for the suspension.
- **Service provider** (`service_provided`, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether the service provider is Yes or No.
- **Customer consent** (`customer_consent`, select_one, required): Select I have read, understand, and agree to the temporary service suspension or choose an alternative if applicable.
- **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or information about the suspension.
- **Service suspended** (`service_suspended`, time, required): Enter the time of the service suspension.
- **End date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the suspension, if applicable.
- **Reason end suspension** (`reason_end_suspension`, text, optional): Provide a reason for the end of the suspension.
- **Reason service suspended** (`reason_service_suspended`, text, optional): Choose a reason for the service suspension.
- **Reason suspended by** (`reason_suspended_by`, text, optional): Choose a reason for the suspension by the service provider.
- **Reason suspended by 1** (`reason_suspended_by_1`, text, optional): Choose an alternative reason for the suspension by 1.
- **Reason suspended by 2** (`reason_suspended_by_2`, text, optional): Choose an alternative reason for the suspension by 2.
- **Reason suspended by 3** (`reason_suspended_by_3`, text, optional): Choose an alternative reason for the suspension by 3.
- **Reason suspended by 4** (`reason_suspended_by_4`, text, optional): Choose an alternative reason for the suspension by 4.
- **Reason suspended by 5** (`reason_suspended_by_5`, text, optional): Choose an alternative reason for the suspension by 5.
- **Reason suspended by 6** (`reason_suspended_by_6`, text, optional): Choose an alternative reason for the suspension by 6.
- **Reason suspended by 7** (`reason_suspended_by_7`, text, optional): Choose an alternative reason for the suspension by 7.
- **Reason suspended by 8** (`reason_suspended_by_8`, text, optional): Choose an alternative reason for the suspension by 8.

Note: This form is designed to be filled out by the customer and submitted for review and approval. The fields may have additional validation and error handling in the actual application.
