# Parental Consent And Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Parental Consent And Waiver Form is a legal document that aims to safeguard the minor's well-being and rights while participating in activities offered by our organization/program. It is designed to obtain an agreement from the minor's parent/guardian that they understand and accept the terms and conditions of the activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have read and understand all the information provided.
2. Fill out the "Activity Participation" field with a brief description of the activity you are about to participate in or have your child participate in.
3. Indicate your agreement or waiver for your child's participation by providing a number value (e.g., 1 for agreement, 2 for waiver).
4. Sign the "Signature" field with your handwritten signature to confirm your understanding and agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Activity Participation**: (`activity_participation`, text, optional) Provide a brief description of the activity your child will participate in. This could be a sports program, a summer camp, or any other activity your child will engage in.
* **Waiver**: (`waiver`, number, optional) Indicate your level of agreement or waiver for your child's participation. If you choose 1, it indicates your agreement to the terms and conditions. If you choose 2, it indicates that you waive your right to any claims or liabilities. Please note that this option is not intended for emergency medical situations where choosing 1 might not be sufficient.
* **Signature**: (`signature`, note, optional) Sign this field with your handwritten signature to confirm your understanding and agreement. This is an essential step to validate your submission and demonstrate your commitment to your child's well-being and safety.
