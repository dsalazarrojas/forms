# Sample Telemedicine Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sample Telemedicine Consent Form is designed to collect information from patients for telemedicine consultations. This form helps healthcare providers gather essential details, including patient contact information, consent, and version control. Completing this form ensures that patient data is accurate and reliable.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the patient's information on page 1, including their name, email, and phone number.
2.  On page 2, select "Yes" or "No" for each of the two signature options to indicate the patient's consent.
3.  Enter the date and time of the patient's signature on the "Signature Date" and "Signature Time" fields.
4.  Enter the patient's email address in the "Email" field.
5.  Enter the patient's phone number in the "Phone" field.
6.  Enter the version of the consent form used (optional).
7.  Enter the name of the healthcare provider (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Patient Information**: This section collects the patient's basic details, including their name, email, and phone number. Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification documents. Enter a valid email address that can be used for communication. Enter the patient's phone number for further contact.
*   **Signature 1**: This field is a multiple-choice option to indicate the patient's consent for telemedicine services. Select "Yes" to indicate consent and "No" if the patient declines.
*   **Signature 2**: Similar to Signature 1, this is another multiple-choice option to confirm the patient's consent.
*   **Consent**: This field is a text input for the patient to provide additional comments or explanations regarding their consent.
*   **Signature Date**: Enter the date on which the patient signed the consent form.
*   **Signature Time**: Enter the time at which the patient signed the consent form.
*   **Email**: Enter the patient's email address for further communication.
*   **Phone**: Enter the patient's phone number for further contact.
*   **Version**: If required, enter the version of the consent form used. This helps track changes and updates to the form.
*   **Provider**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider (optional). This is helpful for billing or insurance purposes.
