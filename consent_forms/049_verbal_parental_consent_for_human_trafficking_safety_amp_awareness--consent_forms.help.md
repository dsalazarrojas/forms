# Verbal Parental Consent For HUMAN TRAFFICKING SAFETY & AWARENESS - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain verbal parental consent for your child's participation in programs or activities related to human trafficking safety and awareness. It is essential that parents or guardians understand the risks associated with human trafficking and are willing to give their consent for their child's involvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Ensure that you are the parent or guardian of the child for whom you are providing consent.
2. Read and understand the questions carefully, and answer them truthfully.
3. Select the relevant options for each question.
4. Sign the form to indicate your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Verbal Parental Consent Form** (`verbal_parental_consent_form`, note, required: false)
	+ This is the title of the form.
* **Human Trafficking Safety & Awareness** (`human_trafficking_safety_and_awareness`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is a text field where you can enter your thoughts or comments about human trafficking safety and awareness.
* **I am the parent or guardian** (`i_am_the_parent_or_guardian`, select_one, required: false)
	+ Select the correct option that applies to you: "I am the parent or guardian", "I am the step-parent", or "I am the legal guardian".
* **Have you signed a consent form before?** (`have_signed_a_consent_form_before`, select_one, required: false)
	+ Select the correct option: "True" or "False", indicating if you have signed a consent form before for this child.
* **Have you signed a consent form before for any other organizations?** (`have_signed_a_consent_form_before_other_organization`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Select the correct options: "Yes" or "No", indicating if you have signed a consent form for any other organizations.
* **Have you discussed Human Trafficking Safety & Awareness?** (`have_discussed_human_trafficking_safety_and_awareness`, select_one, required: false)
	+ Select the correct option: "True" or "False", indicating if you have discussed human trafficking safety and awareness with your child.
* **Will you sign the consent form?** (`will_sign_consent_form`, select_one, required: false)
	+ Select the correct option: "True" or "False", indicating if you are willing to sign the consent form.
* **Reason for not signing the consent form** (`reason_for_not_signing_consent_form`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter the reason why you are not willing to sign the consent form.
* **Have you signed consent form for any other organization?** (`have_signed_consent_form_for_any_other_organization`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Select the correct options: "Yes" or "No", indicating if you have signed a consent form for any other organization.
* **Will you sign consent form for any other organization?** (`will_sign_consent_form_for_any_other_organization`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Select the correct options: "Yes" or "No", indicating if you are willing to sign a consent form for any other organization.
