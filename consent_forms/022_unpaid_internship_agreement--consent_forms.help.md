# Unpaid Internship Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to create an official document that outlines the terms and conditions of an unpaid internship. It will help ensure both the intern and the organization hosting the internship are clear on their responsibilities and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all required and optional information.
2. Read each field carefully and make sure you understand its purpose before entering any information.
3. If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
4. Once completed, review and sign the form to confirm agreement.
