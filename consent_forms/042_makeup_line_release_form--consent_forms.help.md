# Makeup Line Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain the consent of participants to capture images or videos during a makeup event. It informs participants of how their captured media will be used and ensures they are aware of and agree to use for promotional purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Provide Participant Information**
	* Enter the participant's name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. **Confirm Event Details**
	* Enter the date of capture in the "Date of Capture" field.
	* Enter the location of capture in the "Location" field.
3. **Confirm Consent**
	* In the "Consent to Capture" field, enter "Yes" if you agree to capture images or videos.
	* In the "Consent to Promotion" field, enter "Yes" if you agree to use the captured images or videos for promotional purposes.
4. **Signature**
	* Select "True" in the "Participant Signature" field to confirm that you have read and understood the form.
	* Select "True" in the "Staff Signature" field to confirm that you are a staff member present at the event and have informed the participant of their rights.
5. **Media Release**
	* In the "Capture Details" field, describe how your images or videos will be captured (e.g. during a photoshoot or demonstration).
	* In the "Capture Image" field, indicate if you agree to capture images of yourself during the event.
	* In the "Use Image" field, indicate if you agree to use the captured images for promotional purposes.
	* In the "Participant Image Release" field, indicate if you release your images for use by the event organizers.
	* In the "Staff Image Release" field, indicate if you release your images for use by the event organizers.
6. **Promotional Use**
	* In the "Promotional Use" field, indicate if you agree to use captured media for promotional purposes.
7. **Video Release**
	* In the "Capture Video" field, indicate if you agree to capture video footage of yourself during the event.
	* In the "Participant Video Release" field, indicate if you release the video for use by the event organizers.
