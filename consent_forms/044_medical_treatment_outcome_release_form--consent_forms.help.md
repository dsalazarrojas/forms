# Medical Treatment Outcome Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to record and release medical treatment outcomes for patients. It is intended for patients to provide information about their treatment experiences and outcomes to authorized individuals or organizations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Patient Name** field with your name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. In the **Recipient Name** field, type the name of the person or organization you are authorizing to receive the medical treatment outcome information.
3. Enter your **Recipient Email** in the email field, ensuring it is a valid email address where the recipient can contact you.
4. If you have a **Date of Birth**, please provide it in the format "MM/DD/YYYY" (e.g., 02/12/1990).
5. If you have a **Signature Date**, enter the date you are signing the form. This should be in the format "MM/DD/YYYY" (e.g., 02/12/1990).
6. In the **Authorized By** field, type the name of the person or organization authorized to receive the medical treatment outcome information.
7. If you have an **Authorized On** date, enter it in the format "HH:MM AM/PM" (e.g., 10:00 AM).
8. In the **Medical Treatment Outcome** field, describe the outcome of your medical treatment in as much detail as possible.
9. Finally, add your **Signature** by typing your name as it appears on your identification documents.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: The patient's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Recipient Name**: The name of the person or organization authorized to receive the medical treatment outcome information.
* **Recipient Email**: A valid email address where the recipient can contact you.
* **Date of Birth**: The patient's date of birth in the format "MM/DD/YYYY" (e.g., 02/12/1990).
* **Signature Date**: The date you are signing the form in the format "MM/DD/YYYY" (e.g., 02/12/1990).
* **Authorized By**: The name of the person or organization authorized to receive the medical treatment outcome information.
* **Authorized On**: The date the authorization was given in the format "HH:MM AM/PM" (e.g., 10:00 AM).
* **Medical Treatment Outcome**: A detailed description of the outcome of your medical treatment.
* **Signature**: Your signature, typed as it appears on your identification documents.
