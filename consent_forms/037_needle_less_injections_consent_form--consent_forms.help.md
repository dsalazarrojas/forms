# Needle Less Injections Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from patients to obtain their consent for needle-less injections. The purpose of this form is to ensure that patients understand the risks and benefits of the procedure and provide their consent before undergoing the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter your patient's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
3. Provide your patient's address in the "Address" field.
4. Enter your patient's phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your patient's email address in the "Email" field.
6. If you have any medical history, please describe it in the "Medical History" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required/optional): Please fill in your patient's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Provide your patient's address, including street number, street name, city, state, and zip code.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your patient's phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your patient's email address.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required/optional): If you have any medical history, please describe it in detail, including any previous surgeries, allergies, or conditions that may be relevant to the treatment.
