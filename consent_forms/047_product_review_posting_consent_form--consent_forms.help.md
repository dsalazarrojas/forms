# Product Review Posting Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to capture information for product reviews that will be posted on our website. This information will help us understand customer feedback, improve our products, and provide valuable insights to fellow customers considering purchasing our products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your review details, including any specific product you are reviewing.
2. Share your review text, describing your experience with the product.
3. Choose your review rating to indicate your overall satisfaction with the product.
4. Decide whether to publish your review on our website (note that we will not publish reviews without your consent).
5. Enter your email address so we can contact you (if needed).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Review Details
* **Review Details** (`review_details`, text, required: false): Enter a brief title or description of the product you are reviewing.

### Review Text
* **Review Text** (`review_text`, text, required: false): Share your thoughts, experiences, and opinions about the product. Be as clear and concise as possible to help fellow customers make informed decisions.

### Review Rating
* **Review Rating** (`review_rating`, select_multiple, options: Good, Fair, required: false): Select one or more of the provided rating options to indicate your overall satisfaction with the product.

### Publish
* **Publish** (`review_publish`, select_one, options: Yes, Publish, No, Don't Publish, required: false): Check to allow us to publish your review on our website. If you choose not to publish, your review will not be visible to others.

### Email
* **Email** (`review_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you if we need further information from you.
