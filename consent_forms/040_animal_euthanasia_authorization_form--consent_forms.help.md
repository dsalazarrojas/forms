# Animal Euthanasia Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animal Euthanasia Authorization Form is a document that allows pet owners to provide informed consent for the humane euthanasia of their pets. This form is a critical part of the process, as it ensures that the owner understands the procedure and agrees to it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your full name and contact information (owner_name, owner_email, owner_phone).
2.  Provide your pet's information, including its name (pet_name), species (pet_species), age (pet_age), and medical condition (medical_condition).
3.  Specify the reason for the euthanasia (diagnosis) and prognosis (prognosis).
4.  Discuss your pet's medical condition with your veterinarian and indicate whether you have understood the explanation (vet_explanation).
5.  Indicate whether alternatives to euthanasia have been discussed (alternatives_discussed) and considered (alternatives_considered).
6.  Provide a statement of authorization for humane euthanasia (consent_euthanasia).
7.  Confirm that you understand the procedure is irreversible (understand_irreversible) and will be performed humanely (understand_humane).
8.  Select your preferred aftercare options (aftercare_choice).
9.  Specify any memorial items you would like to commemorate your pet (memorial_items).
10. Decide whether you want to be present during the procedure (present_during).
11. Confirm that you accept financial responsibility for the procedure (financial_responsibility).
12. Sign the form with your full name and date of signature (owner_signature, signature_date).
13. Have a clinic staff member sign as witness (witness_name).
14. The veterinarian responsible for the procedure will sign as well (veterinarian_signature).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Pet Owner Full Name** (owner_name)
*   This field requires your legal name.
### 2. **Email Address** (owner_email)
*   Please enter your contact email address.
### 3. **Phone Number** (owner_phone)
*   Enter your contact phone number.
### 4. **Address** (owner_address)
*   Provide your street address, city, state, and zip code.
### 5. **Authorization Form**
*   This is an introductory note that explains the purpose of the form.
### 6. **Date** (date)
*   Enter the current date.
### 7. **Pet Information** (pet_information)
*   This field is an optional note for you to provide any additional information about your pet.
### 8. **Pet's Name** (pet_name)
*   Enter your pet's name.
### 9. **Species** (pet_species)
*   Choose the species of your pet from the provided options.
### 10. **Age** (pet_age)
*   Enter your pet's age in years.
### 11. **Weight** (pet_weight)
*   Enter your pet's weight in pounds.
### 12. **Color and Markings** (pet_color)
*   Provide a description of your pet's color and any notable markings.
### 13. **Medical Condition or Reason** (medical_condition)
*   Explain the medical condition or reason for the euthanasia.
### 14. **Diagnosis** (diagnosis)
*   Enter the veterinary diagnosis.
### 15. **Prognosis** (prognosis)
*   Choose the expected outcome from the options provided.
### 16. **Veterinarian Discussion** (veterinarian_discussion)
*   This field is an optional note for you to describe your discussions with your veterinarian.
### 17. **Has the Veterinarian Explained the Procedure?** (vet_explanation)
*   Indicate whether the veterinarian fully or partially explained the procedure.
### 18. **Have Alternatives Been Discussed?** (alternatives_discussed)
*   Indicate whether alternatives to euthanasia have been discussed.
### 19. **Have Alternatives Been Considered?** (alternatives_considered)
*   Specify whether alternatives were considered.
### 20. **Authorization Statement** (authorization_statement)
*   Select "Yes, I authorize" to indicate your consent.
### 21. **I Understand This Procedure Is Irreversible?** (understand_irreversible)
*   Confirm that you understand the procedure is irreversible.
### 22. **I Understand the Procedure Will Be Performed Humanely?** (understand_humane)
*   Confirm that you understand the procedure will be performed humanely.
### 23. **Aftercare Options** (aftercare_options)
*   This field is an optional note for you to describe the aftercare options you prefer.
### 24. **Aftercare Preference** (aftercare_choice)
*   Select your preferred aftercare option.
### 25. **Memorial Items** (memorial_items)
*   Select any memorial items you would like to commemorate your pet.
### 26. **Do You Wish to Be Present During the Procedure?** (present_during)
*   Decide whether you want to be present during the procedure.
### 27. **Financial Responsibility** (financial_responsibility)
*   Confirm that you accept financial responsibility for the procedure.
### 28. **Estimated Cost** (estimated_cost)
*   Enter the estimated cost of the procedure.
### 29. **Do You Have Any Questions?** (questions)
*   You can ask any questions you have here.
### 30. **Owner Signature** (owner_signature)
*   Sign the form with your full legal name.
### 31. **Date of Signature** (signature_date)
*   Enter the date of your signature.
### 32. **Witness Name** (witness_name)
*   Enter the name of the witness.
### 33. **Veterinarian Signature** (veterinarian_signature)
*   Enter the signature of the attending veterinarian.

## Tips

*   Please fill out the form carefully and accurately.
*   Ensure that you have a clear understanding of the procedure and its implications.
*   Ask questions if you are unsure about any part of the form.
*   Review the form carefully before signing it.
