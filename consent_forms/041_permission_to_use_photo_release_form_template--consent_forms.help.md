# Permission To Use Photo Release Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form template is designed to be used when a photographer (Releasor) wants to use photos of a client (Releasee) for promotional purposes or other uses. The client must give their consent for the use of their photos.

## How To Complete This Form

To use this form, simply fill out the form with the required information and sign where indicated. You can complete this form on a computer or mobile device.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: The name of the person giving the consent (Releasee).
* **Photographer's Name**: The name of the person taking the photo (Releasor).
* **Company Name** (optional): The name of the company or organization the Releasor represents.
* **Address Line 1**: The first line of the Releasee's address.
* **City**: The city where the Releasee resides or is located.
* **State/Province**: The state or province where the Releasee resides or is located.
* **Zip**: The zip code of the Releasee's address.
* **Phone**: The phone number of the Releasee.
* **Release Date**: The date when the photo release will start. This is when the photo can be used.
* **Expires Date** (optional): The date when the photo release will expire. If no date is filled, the release will be perpetual.
* **Signature**: The Releasee must sign to confirm their consent.
* **Agree**: The Releasee must agree to the terms of the release. They can either agree or not agree.
* **I release**: The Releasee must choose to release or not release the photos. They can either choose to release or not release the photos.
