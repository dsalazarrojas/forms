# Student Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Consent Form is a document that students must complete to confirm their attendance and understanding of an event. It is a standard form used by institutions to gather necessary information and ensure student safety and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the required fields.
2. Enter your phone number and event title in the corresponding fields.
3. Provide a brief description of the event in the event description field.
4. Choose a date and time for the event in the event date and event time fields.
5. Enter the event location where the event will take place.
6. Sign and date your parent's signature (if applicable).
7. Sign and date your own signature.
8. Submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Consent Form**: This field is the main purpose of the form. Enter your name and any other required information as prompted.
* **Student Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Student Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Student Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Event Title**: Enter a brief title of the event.
* **Event Description**: Enter a brief description of the event, including any important details about the event.
* **Event Date**: Choose a date for the event by selecting a date from the calendar.
* **Event Time**: Choose a time for the event by selecting a time from the time options.
* **Event Location**: Enter the location where the event will take place.
* **Parent Signature**: If you are under 18 years old, your parent or guardian must sign this field.
* **Student Signature**: Sign your own signature to confirm your understanding and consent to the event.
* **Student Signature 1**: This field is not currently used. You can skip it.
* **Parent Signature 1**: This field is not currently used. You can skip it.

## Tips
* Make sure to review and understand the event details before signing the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask the event organizers.
* Ensure your parent or guardian signs the parent signature field if required.
* Keep a copy of this form for your records.
