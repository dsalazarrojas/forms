# Pet Surgery Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from pet owners for surgical procedures on their pets. It ensures that the pet owner is aware of the risks associated with the surgery and provides a record of their consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Owner Information** section with the pet owner's details.
2. Provide information about the **Surgical Risks** associated with the procedure.
3. Describe the **Procedure Details** in the required section.
4. Check the box next to **Owner Consent** to indicate that the pet owner has given their consent.
5. Date and sign the **Veterinarian Signature Date** field to confirm that the veterinarian has reviewed and agreed to the consent.
6. Date and sign the **Owner Signature Date** field to confirm that the pet owner has reviewed and agreed to the consent.
7. Date and sign the **Veterinarian Signature Date** field for the veterinarian who performed the procedure, which is usually different from the previous step.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Owner Information (text, required/optional):**
Enter the pet owner's name, contact information, and other relevant details.

### **Surgical Risks (text, required/optional):**
Describe the potential risks and complications associated with the surgical procedure. This is an important section to make the pet owner aware of what to expect.

### **Procedure Details (text, required/optional):**
Provide a detailed description of the surgical procedure, including the type of surgery, expected outcomes, and any necessary steps the pet owner should take after the surgery.

### **Owner Consent (select multiple, required/optional):**
Check the box next to "Yes" to indicate that the pet owner has given their consent for the surgical procedure. This confirms that the pet owner is aware of the risks and has agreed to the procedure.

### **Veterinarian Signature Date (date, required/optional):**
Date and sign this field to confirm that the veterinarian has reviewed and agreed to the consent.

### **Owner Signature Date (date, required/optional):**
Date and sign this field to confirm that the pet owner has reviewed and agreed to the consent.

### **Veterinarian Signature Date (date, required/optional):**
Date and sign this field to confirm that the veterinarian who performed the procedure has reviewed and agreed to the consent.
