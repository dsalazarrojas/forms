# webinar_organization_permission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The webinar_organization_permission_form is used to request permission from an organization to participate in a webinar. This form is typically used when an organization wants to access specific resources or services related to the webinar.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the name of the organization you are requesting permission for.
2. Select the role(s) within the organization that will participate in the webinar.
3. Provide the contact person's name within the organization.
4. Enter the contact person's email address.
5. Select the specific permission(s) the organization is granted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization**: Enter the name of the organization you are requesting permission for. This is a required field. Please make sure to enter the correct name to avoid any issues with communication.
* **Role**: Select the role(s) within the organization that will participate in the webinar. This field is used to specify who from the organization will be participating. You can select one or multiple roles.
* **Contact**: Enter the name of the contact person within the organization. This is the person who will be the main point of contact for the webinar organizers.
* **Email**: Enter the contact person's email address. This will help the webinar organizers communicate with the correct person within the organization.
* **Permission**: Select the specific permission(s) the organization is granted. This field allows you to choose which resources or services the organization has access to. You can select one or multiple options.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately to ensure proper communication and collaboration between the organization and the webinar hosts.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, refer back to the form's purpose or contact the webinar organizers for clarification.
* This form should be used once per organization, to avoid duplication of permissions.
