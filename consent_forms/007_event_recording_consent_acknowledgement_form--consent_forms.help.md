# Event Recording Consent Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Recording Consent Acknowledgement Form is a required document that participants must complete to confirm their consent for the recording of events. This form ensures that participants understand and agree to the recording of their participation and helps event organizers manage recordings according to the participant's wishes.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by carefully reading the form and understanding the instructions.
- Fill out the form completely and accurately with your participant information.
- Choose your consent preferences for audio and video recordings.
- Sign the form to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Organiser** (`event_organiser`, text, required): Enter the name of the event organizer who is collecting your consent.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it should be recorded for the event.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted for further information.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted for further information.
* **Audio Recording** (`consent_audio`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' to agree to audio recording or 'No' to decline.
* **Video Recording** (`consent_video`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' to agree to video recording or 'No' to decline.
* **Event Start Time** (`event_start_time`, date, required): Enter the start time of the event as it will be recorded.
* **Additional Notes** (`note`, note, optional): You can add any additional comments or information about your participation and consent.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, optional): Sign your name to confirm your consent.
* **Organiser Signature** (`organiser_signature`, text, required): Sign your name to confirm that you have read and understood the form and the event guidelines.

## Tips
- Please ensure that your contact information is up-to-date and correct.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please reach out to the event organizer.
- Make sure to sign both the participant and organizer signatures to confirm your consent.
- This form is a legal document and should be handled with care.
