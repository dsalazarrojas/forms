This guide explains the Payroll Record Review Consent Form, which is used to collect information about an employee's payroll records. The form aims to ensure that employees have reviewed and understood their own payroll records and are willing to share them with the relevant stakeholders, such as their manager and the HR department.

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name in the "What is your name?" field on Page 1.
2. On Page 2, select "True" if you have reviewed your payroll records, and "False" otherwise.
3. On Page 3, enter the number of times you review your payroll records in a month.
4. On Page 4, select your date of birth.
5. On Page 5, enter the time you work in the "What time do you work?" field.
6. On Page 6, enter your email address.
7. On Page 7, enter the total hours you worked last week.
8. On Page 8, select the department(s) corresponding to each hour you worked last week.
9. On Page 9, select whether you want to receive updates on your time-off balance.
10. On Page 10, enter your job title.
11. On Page 11, select your department.
12. On Page 12, select your reporting manager.
13. On Page 13, select whether you consent to share your pay stub information with your manager.
14. On Page 14, enter the date of your last payday.
15. On Page 15, select whether you consent to share your payroll records with the HR department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

• **What is your name?** (page 1, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
• **Have you reviewed your payroll records?** (page 2, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether you have reviewed your payroll records, or select "False" if not.
• **How often do you review your payroll records?** (page 3, number, required): Enter the number of times you review your payroll records in a month.
• **What is your date of birth?** (page 4, date, required): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
• **What time do you work?** (page 5, time, required): Enter your work hours in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00 or 12:00).
• **What is your email address?** (page 6, email, required): Enter your work email address.
• **Total hours worked last week?** (page 7, number, required): Enter the total hours you worked last week.
• **Hours worked last week by department?** (page 8, number, required): Select the department for each hour you worked last week.
• **Do you want to receive updates on your time-off balance?** (page 9, checkbox, optional): Select whether you want to receive updates on your time-off balance.
• **What is your job title?** (page 10, text, required): Enter your job title.
• **What is your department?** (page 11, text, required): Select your department.
• **What is your reporting manager?** (page 12, text, required): Select your reporting manager.
• **Do you consent to share your pay stub information with your manager?** (page 13, radio, required): Select whether you consent to share your pay stub information with your manager.
• **What date was the last payday?** (page 14, date, required): Enter the date of your last payday in YYYY-MM-DD format.
• **Do you consent to share your payroll records with the HR department?** (page 15, radio, required): Select whether you consent to share your payroll records with the HR department.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you fill in all the required fields accurately and completely.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your HR representative.
