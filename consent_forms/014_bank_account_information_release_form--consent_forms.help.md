# Bank Account Information Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide a bank account holder's information to a third party for a specific purpose, such as a loan application or legal proceedings. The form helps ensure that the account holder is aware of and consents to the disclosure of their account information.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your **Full Legal Name** in the field provided.
2.  Enter your **Social Security Number (Full or Last 4 Digits)**. This is required for account verification purposes.
3.  List all **Account Numbers** you wish to authorize for disclosure. This is crucial to ensure that the correct accounts are included in the scope of the disclosure.
4.  Enter the name of the **Organization** or **Recipient** (e.g., mortgage lender, accounting firm, or law office) you wish to receive your account information.
5.  Provide the **Contact Person Name** of the recipient organization.
6.  Enter the **Recipient's Email Address** where the information will be sent. This ensures that the recipient knows how to contact you with any questions or concerns.
7.  If desired, enter the **Recipient Phone Number** for additional contact purposes.
8.  Select the **Scope of Disclosure** you wish to release, which can include information such as current balance, average balance, or full transaction history.
9.  If you selected **Full Transaction History**, enter the **Disclosure Date Range** (start and end) for the range of dates you wish to include.
10.  Specify the **Purpose of Release** for the disclosure, e.g., loan application, audit, or legal proceedings.
11.  Choose the **Duration of Authorization** for the release, e.g., 30 days, 90 days, or continuous until revoked.
12.  Select **I Consent and Authorize** to confirm your authorization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Legal Name (account_holder_name)**: Your complete name as it appears on your account documents.
* **Social Security Number (ss_number)**: Your social security number or the last 4 digits if you do not wish to disclose your full number.
* **Account Number(s) to be Released (account_number_list)**: List all account numbers you wish to authorize for disclosure.
* **Organization Name (recipient_organization)**: The name of the organization or recipient authorized to receive your information.
* **Contact Person Name (recipient_contact_name)**: The name of the contact person at the recipient organization.
* **Recipient Email Address (recipient_email)**: The email address where your information will be sent.
* **Recipient Phone Number (recipient_phone)**: The phone number of the recipient organization for additional contact.
* **Information to be Shared (disclosure_scope)**: Select the level of information you wish to release, including current balance, average balance, or full transaction history.
*   **Disclosure Date Range - Start (date_range_start)**: If you selected **Full Transaction History**, enter the start date of the range you wish to disclose.
*   **Disclosure Date Range - End (date_range_end)**: If you selected **Full Transaction History**, enter the end date of the range you wish to disclose.
*   **Purpose of Release (release_purpose)**: Specify the purpose for the disclosure, e.g., loan application, audit, or legal proceedings.
*   **Duration of Authorization (expiration_duration)**: Select the duration for which you wish to authorize the release.
*   **I authorize my bank to release the information described above (consent_agreement)**: Confirm your consent and authorization for the disclosure.
*   **Date Signed (authorization_date)**: Enter the date you signed this form, indicating the effective date of your consent.
