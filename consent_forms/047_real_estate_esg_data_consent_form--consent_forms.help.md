# real_estate_esg_data_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect user consent for ESG data collection. It is used to ensure that users understand how their data will be used and to obtain their explicit consent before data collection.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the required fields (indicated by *) with your information.
2. Select the specific purpose for which you will use the ESG data from the dropdown menu.
3. Check the boxes to indicate what you agree to for data use and sharing.
4. Optionally, add any additional notes about your consent.
5. Submit the form to complete the consent process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name (real_estate_esg_data_consent_form_user_name)** (`user_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Company Name (real_estate_esg_data_consent_form_company_name)** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your company's name.
* **Company Address (real_estate_esg_data_consent_form_company_address)** (`company_address`, text, required): Enter your company's address.
* **Job Title (real_estate_esg_data_consent_form_job_title)** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Email (real_estate_esg_data_consent_form_email)** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone (real_estate_esg_data_consent_form_phone)** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **ESG Data Purpose (real_estate_esg_data_consent_form_esg_data_purpose)** (`esg_data_purpose`, select_one, required): Select the specific purpose for which you will use the ESG data:
	+ ESG reporting
	+ Analysis
	+ Communications
* **Data Use Agreement (real_estate_esg_data_consent_form_data_use_agreement)** (`data_use_agreement`, select_multiple, required): Check the boxes to indicate what you agree to for data use:
	+ I agree to use the collected data for ESG reporting.
	+ I agree to use the collected data for analysis.
	+ I agree to use the collected data for communications.
* **Data Retention (real_estate_esg_data_consent_form_data_retention)** (`data_retention`, date, optional): Enter the date when you would like the data to be retained.
* **Consent to Share (real_estate_esg_data_consent_form_consent_to_share)** (`consent_to_share`, select_one, required): Select whether you consent to share your data:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Consent to Share With (real_estate_esg_data_consent_form_consent_to_share_with)** (`consent_to_share_with`, select_multiple, required): Check the boxes to indicate with whom you consent to share your data:
	+ Real Estate ESG Data
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Notes (real_estate_esg_data_consent_form_notes)** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about your consent.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to complete the consent process.
* Be aware that your data will be used for the purpose(s) you select.
* If you select "Other (please specify)" for "Consent to Share With", please provide a brief description in the "Notes" field.
