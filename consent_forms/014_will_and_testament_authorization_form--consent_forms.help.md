# Will And Testament Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Will And Testament Authorization Form is a legal document that allows you to express your wishes for the distribution of your assets after death. This form is often used in conjunction with a will to ensure that your wishes are respected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer the questions accurately to reflect your true intentions.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for each field, taking into account your personal preferences.
3. Double-check your answers and selections before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **testament_info** (`testaments_info`, `text`, required/optional): This field is a text input where you can provide any additional information about your testament. You can write a brief description of your intentions, wishes, or any other relevant details.
* **beneficiaries** (`beneficiaries`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field asks if you have beneficiaries (people who will inherit your assets) Yes or No. If you select Yes, you will be able to list multiple beneficiaries.
* **testament_witnesses** (`testament_witnesses`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field asks if you have any testament witnesses, who will witness your will. If you select Yes, you will be able to select one or more witnesses.
* **testament_executor** (`testament_executor`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field asks if you have appointed an executor, who will manage your estate after your death. If you select Yes, you will be able to select one executor.
* **testament_guardian** (`testament_guardian`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field asks if you have appointed a guardian, who will care for any minor children or dependents. If you select Yes, you will be able to select one guardian.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer honestly and accurately to ensure your wishes are respected.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any question or option, ask for help or clarification.
* Remember to keep a copy of your completed form for your records.
