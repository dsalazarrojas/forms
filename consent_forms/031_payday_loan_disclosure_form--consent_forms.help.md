# Payday Loan Disclosure Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for borrowers to provide information about their payday loan, which will be used by the lender to understand and process the loan. The form requires borrowers to enter details about their loan amount, loan term, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure you are filling out this form as a borrower.
2. Select the loan amount you wish to borrow.
3. Choose the loan term you agree to (if applicable).
4. Enter your name.
5. Enter your email address.
6. Enter the loan rate (if applicable).
7. Select the date and time of the loan (if applicable).
8. Add any additional notes or comments about the loan.
9. Select any additional acknowledgement(s) you agree to (if applicable).
10. Select the loan agreement you have reviewed (if applicable).
11. Enter any other terms or conditions of the loan (if applicable).
12. Enter the total cost of the loan (if applicable).
