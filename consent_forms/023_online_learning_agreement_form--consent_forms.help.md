# Online Learning Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This online learning agreement form is designed to capture essential information and agreements from users, particularly relevant to online courses or training programs. It's meant to be a standard template for various types of online learning programs, such as courses, workshops, or conferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your information as requested.
2. Provide accurate and complete details for each field, and make sure to review your answers carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (id: 1, <type>: text, required/optional): Enter your personal details such as name, email, and title, to help us identify and contact you.
* **Course Info** (id: 2, <type>: text, required/optional): Provide the title of the online course, workshop, or conference you are participating in. This helps us to match you with the correct information.
* **Training Info** (id: 3, <type>: text, required/optional): Enter the training details such as date, duration, and location of the training or course.
* **Agreement Details** (id: 4, <type>: text, required/optional): This field is for any additional comments or agreements you have regarding the training or course, such as confidentiality, non-disclosure, or other terms and conditions.
* **User Declaration** (id: 5, <type>: text, required/optional): Confirm that you have read and understand the agreement and that you're entering this information voluntarily.
* **Agreement Confirmation** (id: 6, <type>: text, required/optional): Enter any questions or concerns you may have regarding the agreement.
* **Contact Info** (id: 7, <type>: text, required/optional): Enter any contact information, such as phone numbers or email addresses, where we can reach you if needed.
* **Review and Submit** (id: 8, <type>: text, required/optional): Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
