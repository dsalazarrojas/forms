# Thermal Scanning Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather consent and information from individuals who are participating in thermal scanning activities.

## How To Complete This Form

* Please fill out the form completely, ensuring you include all required fields. 
* Start by filling out the **Form Title** field (page 1), which is a free-text field where you can provide a brief description of your participation.
* In the **Description** field (page 2, page 2 and 3), describe your participation and any relevant details.
* In the **Participant Name** field (page 4), please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents or your preferred name for the purpose of this participation.
* In the **Email Address** field (page 5), enter your active email address where you can be reached for any further inquiries.
* In the **Phone Number** field (page 6), enter your active phone number where you can be reached for any further inquiries.
* In the **Date of Participation** field (page 7), select the date of your participation using the calendar icon.
* In the **Time of Participation** field (page 8), select the time of your participation using the clock icon.
* In the **Signature** field (page 9), sign your name as proof of your identity and participation. Please use the space provided to sign your name.
* In the **Signature Date** field (page 10), select the date on which you are signing the form using the calendar icon.
* In the **Consent** field (page 11), select 'True' if you have given your consent for temperature checks, 'False' if you have not, or 'Undecided' if you are unsure. This field is required to ensure that you have provided your consent for the thermal scanning process.
* In the **Consent Date** field (page 12), select the date on which you are providing your consent using the calendar icon.
* In the **Participant ID** field (page 13), enter a unique identifier assigned to you for participation tracking purposes. This field is optional and only required for tracking purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (page 1, text): A brief description of your participation.
* **Description** (page 2, text): A description of your participation and any relevant details.
* **Participant Name** (page 4, text): Your name as it appears on your identification documents or your preferred name for participation purposes.
* **Email Address** (page 5, email): An active email address where you can be reached for further inquiries.
* **Phone Number** (page 6, text): An active phone number where you can be reached for further inquiries.
* **Date of Participation** (page 7, date): The date of your participation, selected using the calendar icon.
* **Time of Participation** (page 8, time): The time of your participation, selected using the clock icon.
* **Signature** (page 9, note): A signature as proof of your identity and participation. Please sign your name using the provided space.
* **Signature Date** (page 10, date): The date on which you are signing the form, selected using the calendar icon.
* **Consent** (page 11, select_one): A field to confirm your consent for temperature checks. Select 'True' if you have given your consent, 'False' if you have not, or 'Undecided' if you are unsure.
* **Consent Date** (page 12, date): The date on which you are providing your consent, selected using the calendar icon.
* **Participant ID** (page 13, text): A unique identifier assigned to you for participation tracking purposes.
