# gym_marketing_material_usage_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to capture members' consent for marketing materials usage. It collects member information and preferences for marketing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the form by providing your First Name and Last Name.
2. Enter your email address, if you wish to be contacted.
3. Choose whether you consent to marketing materials usage by checking the "Marketing Consent" option(s) you are comfortable with.
4. Choose the type of marketing materials you would like to receive.
5. Click the "Submit" button to send your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Member Consent** (`Member Consent`, text, optional): A box where you can indicate your agreement or disagreement for different types of marketing materials. Please type "True" or "False" for your response.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): This field is for you to provide your email address where you would like to be contacted for marketing purposes, if you have any.
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Your First Name is a required field to help us identify you properly.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Your Last Name is a required field to help us identify you properly.
* **Marketing Consent** (`Marketing Materials`, select multiple, optional): Choose from the options below to indicate which marketing materials you consent to receive. Options are: Brochures, Flyers, Posters, Social Media.
* **Marketing Materials** (`Marketing Materials`, select one, optional): Select only the type of marketing materials you would like to receive.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, text, optional): Once you have filled out the form, click this button to send your completed form.
