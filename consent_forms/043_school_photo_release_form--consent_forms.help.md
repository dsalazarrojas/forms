# School Photo Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Photo Release Form is used to collect consent from students or their guardians to use their photos in various school activities. This form is a legal document that ensures that students' and guardians' permissions are obtained before their photos are used by the school.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's name in the `Student Name` field.
2. Enter the date the photo was taken in the `Photo Date` field, if applicable.
3. Provide the location where the photo was taken in the `Location of Photo` field.
4. Enter the name of your school in the `School Name` field.
5. Fill in the name of the guardian or parent providing consent in the `Guardian's Name` field.
6. Sign the `Signature` field with your signature.
7. Enter the contact email in the `Contact Email` field.
8. Fill in the phone number of the contact person in the `Phone Number` field.
9. Select the types of consents you grant for the use of your photo in the `Other Consents` field.
10. If you selected `Other (please specify)`, enter the specific consents you grant in the `Other Consents (if other)` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photo Release Consent** (`school_photo_release_form`, text, required): Provide a general consent statement for the use of your photo.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the student.
* **Photo Date** (`photo_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the photo was taken, if applicable.
* **Location of Photo** (`photo_location`, text, required): Provide the location where the photo was taken.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your school.
* **Guardian's Name** (`guardian_name`, text, required): Fill in the name of the guardian or parent providing consent.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Sign this field with your signature.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the contact email of the person who can be reached for further information.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Other Consents** (`other_consents`, select_one, required): Select the types of consents you grant for the use of your photo. Options include `School Events`, `Yearbook`, `Social Media`, `School Website`, and `Other (please specify)`.
* **Other Consents (if other)** (`other_consents_other`, text, optional): If you selected `Other (please specify)`, provide the specific consents you grant in this field.
