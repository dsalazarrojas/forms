# # informed-client-consent-form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain informed consent from clients for medical treatment. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1. Fill out the form on one or more pages.
2. Review each field for accuracy and completeness before submission.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
4. Once completed, submit the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Consent Form** (Page 1): This page is a general consent form that requires clients to acknowledge their understanding of and agreement with the medical treatment they are about to receive. Clients must sign this page to confirm their consent.
* **Informed Consent** (Page 2): This section provides clients with details about the medical treatment they will be receiving. It requires clients to indicate that they understand and consent to the treatment.
* **Client Practitioner Rights** (Page 3): This section explains the client's rights regarding their medical treatment and the rights of the healthcare provider. Clients must sign this section to confirm their understanding and agreement.
* **Client Privacy Notice** (Page 4): This page outlines the client's privacy rights and how their personal information will be used. Clients must sign this page to confirm their understanding.
* **Medical Conditions** (Page 5): This multiple-choice field allows clients to select any medical conditions that apply to them, such as allergies or medical history.
* **Medical Conditions 2** (Page 6): Similar to the previous page, this multiple-choice field collects additional medical conditions that may be relevant to the client's treatment.
* **Medical Treatment** (Page 7): This field is intended to capture any additional medical information relevant to the client's treatment.
* **Medical Condition Treatment** (Page 8): This multiple-choice field collects information about any medical conditions that may require special treatment, such as allergies or medical history.
* **Consent Date** (Page 9): This field captures the date on which the client provided their consent.
* **Consent Time** (Page 10): This field captures the time of day when the client provided their consent, using the format "HH:MM AM/PM".
* **Client Signature** (Page 11): This signature field requires the client to sign and date their agreement to the terms outlined in the form.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submission.
* Double-check the accuracy and completeness of all information before submission.
* If you need assistance with this form, contact the relevant authority or healthcare professional.
