# Real Estate Flood Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Real Estate Flood Disclosure Form is a document that real estate professionals use to gather information about a property's flood risk and provide a flood disclosure to the buyer or tenant. This form is essential for identifying potential flood hazards and ensuring compliance with regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Contact Information**: Enter the contact information of the person completing the form.
2. **Is this a sale or rental?**: Select the correct option for the transaction type (sale or rental).
3. **Property Identification**: Enter the property's address and other relevant details.
4. **Is the property in a flood hazard area?**: Select the correct option to indicate if the property is located in a flood hazard area.
5. **Please list the property features**: Describe the property's features, such as its location, elevation, and any flood-related characteristics.
6. **Are there any additional flood hazards?**: If yes, describe the additional hazards.
7. **Certification**: Select the type of certification (Option 7 or Option 8).
8. **Acknowledgement of Certification**: Select the acknowledgement of certification (Option 9 or Option 10).
9. **Flood Risk**: Select the type of flood risk (Option 11 or Option 12).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (Text)**: Enter the contact information of the person completing the form, including their name, phone number, and email address.
* **Is this a sale or rental? (Select One)**: Select the correct option to indicate if the property is being sold or rented.
	+ Option 1: Sale
	+ Option 2: Rental
	+ Option 3: Both
	+ Option 4: Other (specify)
* **Property Identification (Text)**: Enter the property's address, including the street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Is the property in a flood hazard area? (Select Multiple)**: Select all that apply for the following options:
	+ Option 5: In a flood hazard area
	+ Option 6: Not in a flood hazard area
* **Please list the property features (Text)**: Describe the property's features, such as its location, elevation, and any flood-related characteristics.
* **Are there any additional flood hazards? (Text)**: If yes, describe the additional hazards.
* **Certification (Select One)**: Select the type of certification:
	+ Option 7: Certification 1
	+ Option 8: Certification 2
* **Acknowledgement of Certification (Select One)**: Select the acknowledgement of certification:
	+ Option 9: Acknowledgement 1
	+ Option 10: Acknowledgement 2
* **Flood Risk (Select One)**: Select the type of flood risk:
	+ Option 11: Risk 1
	+ Option 12: Risk 2

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, consult with the real estate professional or contact the relevant authorities for guidance.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out.
* Keep a copy of the form for your records.
