# therapy_collaboration_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The therapy collaboration consent form is a mandatory form that ensures clients understand and agree to the sharing of their information with various parties involved in their therapy collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions honestly.
2. Select the correct options for each field, considering the sharing of information and confidentiality.
3. Ensure you have answered all questions before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <page 1: introduction>
* **introduction**: This is a general introduction to the form, explaining the purpose and importance of obtaining informed consent from clients. Please read it carefully before proceeding.

### <page 2: information_sharing>
* **information_sharing**: This question asks if you are willing to share information about your therapy collaboration with other parties (e.g., insurance providers, researchers). Please select 'Yes' if you are comfortable sharing, and 'No' otherwise.

### <page 3: sharing_scope>
* **sharing_scope**: This question asks about the scope of information to be shared with other parties. Please select 'Yes' if you are willing to share all relevant information, and 'No' if you are not. (Required)

### <page 4: sharing_frequency>
* **sharing_frequency**: This question asks about how often you are willing to share information about your therapy collaboration with other parties. Please select 'Yes' if you are willing to share information frequently, 'Yes, but only with [option 2]', 'Yes, but only with [option 3] and [option 4]' or 'No'.

### <page 5: client_consent>
* **client_consent**: This question asks for your consent to share information about your therapy collaboration with other parties. Please select 'Yes' if you are willing to share, and 'No' otherwise.

### <page 6: information_sharing_with>
* **information_sharing_with**: This question asks with whom you are willing to share information about your therapy collaboration. Please select 'Yes' if you are comfortable sharing with insurance providers, researchers, or other parties. Please select 'No' if you are not.

### <page 7: sharing_with_participants>
* **sharing_with_participants**: This question asks about sharing information with other participants in your therapy collaboration. Please select 'Yes' if you are willing to share, and 'No' otherwise.

### <page 8: client_information_protection>
* **client_information_protection**: This question asks about the protection of your client information. Please select 'Yes' if you are willing to protect your information, and 'No' otherwise.
