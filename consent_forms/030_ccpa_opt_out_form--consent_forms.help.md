# CCPA/CPRA Consumer Privacy Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form allows you to exercise your rights under California law regarding the collection, use, and sharing of your personal information. Please use this form to make a legitimate request for one or more of the following:
* Do Not Sell My Personal Information
* Do Not Share My Personal Information
* Request to Access (Right to Know)
* Request to Delete
* Request to Correct

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select all that apply**: Choose one or more of the options under "Type of Request" to specify what you'd like to do with your personal information.
2. **Enter your name**: Fill in your First Name and Last Name accurately.
3. **Provide your email address**: Enter the email address where you can be contacted for verification and communication.
4. **Verify your residency**: Confirm that you are a California resident. If you are not, please do not proceed with this form.
5. **Provide your street address**: Enter your current street address for verification purposes. This will help us confirm your residency.
6. **Specify your relationship to the business**: Indicate your current relationship with our business (e.g., current customer, former customer, etc.).
7. **Choose how to verify your identity**: Select how you'd like to verify your identity. We may require additional documentation in some cases.
8. **Declare under penalty of perjury**: Confirm that you are the consumer whose information is the subject of this request. If you're not, select "Other" and provide details.
9. **Date of Request**: Enter the date of your request.
10. **Optional: Opt out of feedback**: You may optionally opt out of feedback, as this form will process your request within 15 business days.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Type of Request**: This is the primary field to specify the request you'd like to make about your personal information (e.g., Do Not Sell, Do Not Share, Access, Delete, or Correct).
* **First Name**: This is your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: This is your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address**: This is the email address we'll use for verification and communication purposes.
* **Are you a California Resident?**: Confirm that you are a California resident.
* **Street Address**: Enter your current street address for verification purposes.
* **City**: Enter the city where you reside.
* **State**: Enter the state where you reside.
* **Your current relationship to our business**: Specify your current relationship with our business (e.g., current customer, former customer, etc.).
* **How would you like to verify your identity?**: Select how you'd like to verify your identity (e.g., Verification Link via Email, Security Question Confirmation, etc.).
* **Are you an authorized agent acting on behalf of a consumer?**: Confirm if you're an authorized agent or not.
* **Notice for Authorized Agents**: Please note that agents must provide written permission signed by the consumer.
* **I declare under penalty of perjury**: Confirm that you are the consumer whose information is the subject of this request. If not, select "Other" and provide details.
* **Date of Request**: Enter the date of your request.
* **Opt-out Confirmation**: This is an optional field to opt out of feedback after submitting your request.
