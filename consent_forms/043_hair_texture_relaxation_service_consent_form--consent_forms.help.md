# Hair Texture Relaxation Service Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is required to be completed by clients prior to undergoing the hair texture relaxation service. It helps us understand your medical conditions and gather necessary contact information to ensure a smooth and safe experience for you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete all fields marked as required.
2. For optional fields, provide the necessary information if you feel it's relevant.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Salon Information** (`salon_info`, note, required: false): This is a note field where you can provide any relevant information about the salon, but it's not required.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you for any updates or further instructions.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, required: false): List all your medical conditions that may affect the treatment, this will help us take necessary precautions to keep you safe.
* **Client Signature** (`signature`, text, required: false): Please sign this form to acknowledge you have read and understood the service details and any instructions provided.

## Tips
- Please be honest when listing your medical conditions to ensure your safety.
- Make sure to double-check your email and phone number for accuracy.
