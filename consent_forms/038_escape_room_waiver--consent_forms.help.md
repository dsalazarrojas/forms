# Escape Room Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Escape Room Waiver form is used to record a participant's waiver for an Escape Room activity. It collects the necessary information from both the participant and the staff involved in the activity.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Escape Room Waiver**: This is the title of the form.
2. Fill out the participant's information:
	* **Customer Name**: Enter the participant's name.
	* **Date Participating**: Enter the date you will be participating in the escape room.
	* **Time Participating**: Enter the time you will be participating in the escape room.
	* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
	* **Email**: Enter your email address.
3. Fill out the staff's information:
	* **Staff Name**: Enter the staff member's name.
	* **Staff ID**: Enter the staff member's ID.
	* **Staff Email**: Enter the staff member's email address.
	* **Staff Phone**: Enter the staff member's phone number.
4. Confirm the waiver:
	* **Participant Waiver Signed**: Select how the participant signed the waiver (e.g. Option 1, Option 2).
	* **Participant Waiver Signed Text**: Enter any additional text related to the participant's waiver.
	* **Staff Waiver Signed**: Select how the staff signed the waiver (e.g. Option 1, Option 2).
	* **Staff Waiver Signed Text**: Enter any additional text related to the staff's waiver.
5. Add any additional notes:
	* **Waiver Notes**: Enter any additional notes related to the waiver.
