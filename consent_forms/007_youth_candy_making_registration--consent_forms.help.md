# Youth Candy Making Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Candy Making Registration form is designed to collect essential information from parents or guardians regarding their child's participation in a youth candy making workshop. This form helps us understand the child's medical needs, allergies, and emergency contact information, ensuring a safe and enjoyable experience for all participants. Please take a few minutes to carefully fill out this form to help us better serve your child.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Fill out each field according to the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Parent Details
* **Parent Details**: This is a text field where you can provide any additional information about yourself and your relationship with the child.
* **Parent Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Parent Email**: Enter your contact email for any future communication.

### Child Information
* **Child Name**: Please enter your child's name.
* **Age Range**: Select the age range of your child (e.g., 5-12, 13-18, etc.).
* **Workshop Date**: Select the date you would like to attend the workshop (if not selecting, the default date will be used).
* **Start Time**: Select the start time you would like to attend the workshop (if not selecting, the default time will be used).
* **End Time**: Select the end time you would like to attend the workshop (if not selecting, the default time will be used).

### Contact Information
* **Emergency Contact**: If you are not able to attend the workshop, please provide a contact name and phone number.

### Medical Information
* **Medical Condition**: Check any medical conditions your child may have (e.g., allergies, asthma, etc.).
* **Allergies**: Check any food allergies your child may have.
* **Medical Equipment**: Check any medical equipment your child may have (e.g., EpiPen, oxygen tank, etc.).
* **Food Allergies**: Check any food allergies your child may have.
* **Medical Procedure**: Check any medical procedures your child is currently undergoing.
* **Consent Form**: Select which consent form your child will be using (if applicable).
* **Notes**: If you have any additional comments or requests, please provide them here.

### Optional Sections
* **Schedule**: Select which schedule type best suits your needs (e.g., morning session, afternoon session, etc.).
* **Medical Procedure**: Note: This field is not actually "Optional" due to its required property in the schema. Please select any medical procedures your child is currently undergoing.

### Additional Information
* **Notes**: Any additional comments or requests you would like to make.

Please review the information carefully before submitting. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
