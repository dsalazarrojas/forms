# Precautionary Liability Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This Precautionary Liability Release Form is a crucial document that requires customers to acknowledge and agree to the terms and conditions of the services provided by our spa or facility. It's essential to review and understand each field carefully to ensure accurate completion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the customer's personal details, including their name, email, and contact number.
2. Enter the service date and the services rendered.
3. Select the correct waiver status (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
4. Sign the form to indicate your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): This field is used to gather the customer's name. Please enter the customer's full name as it appears on their identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): This field is used to collect the customer's email address. Ensure that the email address is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's contact number, which will be used for communication purposes.
* **Service Date** (`service_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date on which the services were rendered or scheduled to be rendered.
* **Resources** (`resources`, `text`, required): Describe any resources used during the service, such as equipment, materials, or facilities.
* **Spa Name** (`spa_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the spa or facility where the services were rendered.
* **Services Rendered** (`services_rendered`, `text`, required): Describe the specific services rendered to the customer, including any details that may be relevant to the service.
* **Waiver Signed** (`waiver_signed`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one of the three options provided to indicate your agreement status. Please note that selecting "I do not understand and disagree" does not imply acceptance.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `note`, required): This is a signature field for the customer, indicating their agreement to the terms and conditions.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, `note`, required): This is a signature field for the customer, confirming their agreement to the terms and conditions.
* **Customer Name on Service Date** (`customer_name_on_service_date`, `date`, required): Enter the customer's name as it appears on their identification on the service date.
* **Customer Email on Service Date** (`customer_email_on_service_date`, `date`, required): Enter the customer's email address as it appears on their identification on the service date.
* **Customer Contact Number** (`customer_contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's contact number as it appears on their identification on the service date.
* **Spa Signature** (`spa_signature`, `note`, required): This is a signature field for the spa staff, confirming their agreement to the terms and conditions.
* **Waiver Status** (`waiver_status`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the three options provided to indicate the current status of the waiver.
