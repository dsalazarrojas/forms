# Pet Safety Awareness Event Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pet Safety Awareness Event Consent Form is a crucial tool for ensuring the pet's safety and well-being in case of an emergency. It gathers essential information about the pet's health, medical history, and contact details to provide the necessary care and attention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Review the form carefully and provide accurate information about your pet.
2. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.
3. Ensure that you provide all required information to ensure the pet's safety and well-being.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Safety Awareness Event Consent Form** (1):
    * A brief description of the form purpose and its relevance to the pet's safety and well-being.
* **Risk Assessment Questionnaire** (2):
    * Select the most accurate option that describes the pet's current health status.
* **Pet Information** (3):
    * Provide a brief description of the pet's species, age, weight, and any notable characteristics.
* **Emergency Contact Details** (4):
    * Enter the name, address, and contact information for the person or organization that will provide emergency contact and care for the pet.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (5):
    * Select all the people or organizations that the emergency contact person is related to the pet owner or caregiver.
* **Pet Medical History** (6):
    * Provide any relevant medical history or health issues that the pet has experienced.
* **Veterinary Contact** (7):
    * Select the veterinarian or veterinary clinic that the pet visits regularly.
* **Emergency Contact Authorization** (8):
    * Select the option that best describes the level of authority granted to the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (9):
    * Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (10):
    * Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Pet Medical Conditions** (11):
    * Select any medical conditions that the pet has or may have.
* **Medical Attention Details** (12):
    * Provide any additional information about the pet's medical conditions and needs.
* **Pet Training** (13):
    * Select any training that the pet has or is currently undergoing.
* **Pet Vaccinations** (14):
    * Select any vaccinations or treatments that the pet has received.
* **Medical Supplies** (15):
    * Select any medical supplies or equipment that the pet requires.

Please note that the form has 2 optional fields: **Pet Medical History** (6) and **Pet Vaccinations** (14) are not required fields. However, providing accurate information in these fields is crucial for the pet's safety and well-being.
