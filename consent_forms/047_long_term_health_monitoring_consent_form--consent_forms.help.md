# Long Term Health Monitoring Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain consent from participants for long term health monitoring. It is intended for patients to provide their consent and contact information, and to document the details of the monitoring process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your participant information (page 1).
2. Provide your contact information (page 2).
3. Choose your consent options (page 4, the 'Consent' field).
4. Provide a signature and the date and time of signing (pages 6 and 7).
5. Optionally, enter the details of the monitoring (pages 9 and 10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information (page 1)**: Please enter your name and any other relevant participant information.
* **Contact Information (page 2)**: Please enter your contact details, including your name, phone number, and address.
* **Health Monitoring (page 3)**: This field is for you to describe the health monitoring process. Please enter any relevant details.
* **Consent (page 4)**: Please choose whether you consent to the following monitoring options:
	+ None
	+ Other (please specify in the 'Health Monitoring' field above)
* **Signature (page 5)**: Please sign this form to confirm your consent.
* **Date (page 6)**: Please enter the date of signature.
* **Time (page 7)**: Please enter the time of signature.
* **Participant Signature (page 8)**: Please sign this form as the participant.
* **Monitor For (page 9)**: This field is not required, but if you would like to specify the condition you are being monitored for, please enter it here.
* **Monitor At (page 10)**: Similarly, this field is not required, but if you would like to specify the frequency or schedule of the monitoring, please enter it here.
* **Monitoring Frequency (page 11)**: If you would like to specify a frequency for the monitoring, please enter a number here.
