# AI Grading Pilot Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain the consent of participants to participate in an AI grading pilot program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as it appears on official documents.
2. Select your role in the pilot (Student, Instructor, Researcher, or Other).
3. Confirm your consent to participate by selecting "Agree".
4. Choose the types of data that you are willing to share (Assignment submissions, Metadata, Grades, or Feedback).
5. If applicable, provide your expected benefits from participating in the pilot.
6. Enter your email address where you can be reached for questions or inquiries about the pilot.
7. Select the date you are giving consent (YYYY-MM-DD format).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant name**: Enter your name as it appears on official documents.
* **Role in pilot**: Select your role in the pilot (Student, Instructor, Researcher, or Other). This will help us understand your perspective and involvement in the pilot.
* **Consent to participate**: Confirm your consent to participate by selecting "Agree". This is a crucial step in ensuring that you are aware of the pilot's terms and conditions.
* **Types of data shared**: Choose the types of data that you are willing to share with us (Assignment submissions, Metadata, Grades, or Feedback). Be aware that sharing data will help us improve the pilot and its outcomes.
* **Expected benefits**: If you have any expectations or benefits from participating in the pilot, feel free to share them with us. This will help us understand your motivations and gain a better understanding of the pilot's impact.
* **Contact email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for questions or inquiries about the pilot. This will ensure that we can contact you with any updates or follow-up information.
* **Date of consent**: Select the date you are giving your consent (YYYY-MM-DD format). This is essential for record-keeping and tracking purposes.

## Tips
* Please review your choices carefully before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to reach out to us for clarification.
* Once you have completed the form, you will receive a confirmation email with further instructions and information about the pilot.
