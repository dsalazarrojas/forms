# water_quality_monitoring_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The water quality monitoring consent form is a document that outlines the details of water quality testing, including the locations, frequency, and duration of testing, as well as the participant's contact information and testing schedule. This form ensures that all parties involved in the testing process are aware of the terms and conditions of the testing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page of the form carefully to ensure you have understood the instructions and requirements.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely, using the information provided.
3. For fields with options, select the correct response that applies to your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information (page 1)** (`participant_information`, note, optional):
	+ This section provides space for you to add any relevant information about yourself as a participant, such as your name, contact details, etc.
* **Monitoring Locations (page 2)** (`monitoring_locations`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ This field allows you to select multiple locations where water quality monitoring will take place. Choose "Yes" for each location that will be monitored.
* **Consent (page 2)** (`consent`, select_one, optional):
	+ Please select "I understand and agree to have my water tested" to confirm your consent for water quality monitoring.
* **Monitoring Frequency (page 3)** (`monitoring_frequency`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ This field allows you to select how often water quality monitoring will be performed. Choose "Yes" for each frequency that applies to your situation.
* **Monitoring Duration (page 4)** (`monitoring_duration`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ This field allows you to select how long water quality monitoring will be performed. Choose "Yes" for each duration that applies to your situation.
* **Test Frequency (page 5)** (`test_frequency`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ This field allows you to select how often water quality tests will be performed. Choose "Yes" for each frequency that applies to your situation.
* **Contact Method (page 6)** (`contact_method`, select_one, optional):
	+ Please select your preferred method of contact, such as mobile, email, or mail.
* **Test Frequency Unit (page 7)** (`test_frequency_unit`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the unit of time for the selected test frequency (e.g., day, week, month, etc.).
* **Contact Method Unit (page 7)** (`contact_method_unit`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the unit of time for the selected contact method (e.g., day, week, month, etc.).
* **Test Start Date (page 8)** (`test_start_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the start date for water quality testing.
* **Test Start Time (page 8)** (`test_start_time`, time, optional):
	+ Enter the start time for water quality testing.
* **Test End Date (page 9)** (`test_end_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the end date for water quality testing.
* **Test End Time (page 9)** (`test_end_time`, time, optional):
	+ Enter the end time for water quality testing.
* **Participant Notes (page 10)** (`participant_notes`, note, optional):
	+ This field is for you to add any additional notes or comments about your participation in the water quality monitoring process.
* **Tester Notes (page 11)** (`tester_notes`, note, optional):
	+ This field is for the tester to add any additional notes or comments about the testing process.
