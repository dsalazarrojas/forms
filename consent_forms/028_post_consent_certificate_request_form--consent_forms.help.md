# Post Consent Certificate Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a post-consent certificate from the project administrators. A post-consent certificate is typically used to confirm that a participant has given their consent to participate in a project or program. This form is used by project administrators to ensure that all necessary consents have been obtained before proceeding with the project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the participant's name in the **Participant Name** field.
2. Enter the participant's ID in the **Participant ID** field.
3. Select the date the request was made in the **Request Date** field.
4. Enter the name of the project or program for which consent was obtained in the **Project Name** field.
5. Select whether or not the participant has given their consent for the project or program in the **Program Type** field.
6. Select whether or not the certificate type is required in the **Certificate Type** field.
7. Enter the reason for requesting the certificate, if applicable, in the **Reason for Request** field.
8. Enter the type of certificate being requested in the **Certificate Request** field.
9. Enter the name of the person to contact in the **Contact Name** field (optional).
10. Enter the email address of the person to contact in the **Contact E-mail** field (optional).
11. Enter the phone number of the person to contact in the **Contact Phone** field (optional).
12. Enter the tool that was assigned to the participant in the **Assigned Tool** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (text, required): This field is used to enter the name of the participant who gave their consent.
* **Participant ID** (text, required): This field is used to enter the ID of the participant who gave their consent.
* **Request Date** (date, required): This field is used to select the date the request was made.
* **Project Name** (text, required): This field is used to enter the name of the project or program for which consent was obtained.
* **Program Type** (select_one, required): This field is used to select whether or not the participant has given their consent for the project or program.
* **Certificate Type** (select_one, required): This field is used to select whether or not the certificate type is required.
* **Reason for Request** (text, optional): This field is used to enter the reason for requesting the certificate, if applicable.
* **Certificate Request** (text, required): This field is used to enter the type of certificate being requested.
* **Contact Name** (text, optional): This field is used to enter the name of the person to contact in case of further questions or concerns.
* **Contact E-mail** (email, optional): This field is used to enter the email address of the person to contact in case of further questions or concerns.
* **Contact Phone** (text, optional): This field is used to enter the phone number of the person to contact in case of further questions or concerns.
* **Assigned Tool** (text, required): This field is used to enter the tool that was assigned to the participant.
* **Notes** (text, optional): This field is used to enter any additional notes or comments.
