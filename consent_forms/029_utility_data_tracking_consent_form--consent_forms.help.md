# Utility Data Tracking Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The "Utility Data Tracking Consent Form" is used to collect information from customers or clients regarding their consent to track their utility data. This form helps us understand their agreement to share their utility usage data.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your **Name**, **Email**, and **Date of Birth** fields as required.
2. Enter your **Phone** number (if you wish to be contacted for any updates or queries).
3. Enter your **Service Address** where your utility services are provided.
4. Select whether you **consent** to track your utility data (True or False).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (Text)**: Enter your personal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your contact email address where you can be reached for updates or queries.
* **Phone (Text)**: Enter your contact phone number if you wish to be contacted for any updates or queries.
* **Date of Birth (Date)**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 01/01/2000).
* **Utility Name (Text)**: Enter the name of the utility services you are applying for (e.g., electricity or gas).
* **Service Address (Text)**: Enter the complete address where your utility services are provided (e.g., apartment number, street, city, state).
* **Do you consent to (Select One)**: Choose "True" if you agree to track your utility data, or "False" if you do not. 

## Tips

* Please ensure you fill out all required fields correctly to avoid delays in processing your request.
* If you have any doubts or queries, feel free to contact us for assistance.
