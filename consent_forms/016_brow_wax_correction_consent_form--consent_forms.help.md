# Brow Wax Correction Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for clients to provide informed consent for a brow wax correction, a medical procedure that involves correcting imperfections in their brow shape. It helps the service provider understand the client's needs and requirements, ensuring a safe and effective treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the required fields accurately and thoroughly.
2. For the select_one and select_one options, choose the corresponding option that best describes your situation.
3. If you have any concerns or questions, please refer to the service provider.
4. Read and understand the risks and benefits explained.
5. Sign and date the consent form to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Full Name (Text, Required)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Date of Birth (Date, Required)**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Phone Number (Text, Required)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for any further communication.
* **Email Address (Email, Required)**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for any further communication.
* **Appointment Date for Correction (Date, Required)**: Enter the date of the appointment for the brow wax correction.
* **Date of Previous Waxing (Date, Optional)**: If you have previously waxed your brows, enter the date of the last waxing.
* **Reason for Correction (Text, Required)**: Describe the imperfections or unevenness you want to correct in your brow shape.
* **Skin Type (Select One, Required)**: Choose the option that best describes your skin type:
	+ Oily
	+ Dry
	+ Combination
	+ Sensitive
	+ Normal
* **Current skin conditions (Text, Optional)**: Describe any skin conditions you are currently experiencing, such as rashes, acne, sunburn, or wounds.
* **Known product or wax allergies (Text, Optional)**: If you have any allergies to specific products or waxes, list them here.
* **Sensitivity to waxing products (Text, Optional)**: Describe any previous reactions you have had to waxing products.
* **Any medications affecting skin (Text, Optional)**: List any medications that may affect your skin, such as blood thinners, antibiotics, or retinoids.
* **Recent facial treatments or procedures (Text, Optional)**: If you have recently undergone any facial treatments or procedures, describe them here.
* **Are you currently pregnant or nursing (Select One, Required)**: Choose one of the following options:
	+ Yes, pregnant
	+ Yes, nursing
	+ False
* **Your desired brow shape (Text, Optional)**: Describe the shape you want to achieve with your brow correction.
* **Pain Sensitivity Level (Select One, Required)**: Choose one of the following options:
	+ Low
	+ Normal
	+ High
* **Risks and benefits have been explained (Select One, Required)**: Choose one of the following options:
	+ Yes, I understand
	+ I have questions
* **I consent to the brow wax correction (Select One, Required)**: Choose one of the following options:
	+ Yes, I consent
	+ False
* **Date of Consent (Date, Required)**: Enter the date you are giving consent for the brow wax correction.
* **Note**: This form must be signed and dated by you to confirm your agreement to the brow wax correction.
