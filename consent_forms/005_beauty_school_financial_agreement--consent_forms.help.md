# Beauty School Financial Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from students enrolling in the beauty school's financial program. It ensures that the student understands and agrees to the school's policies and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details:
	* **Student Full Name**: Enter your full legal name as it appears on your ID.
	* **Student Email**: Enter your primary contact email address.
	* **Student Phone**: Enter your best contact number for the student.
	* **Student Address**: Enter your full street address.
	* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth.
2. Select your program:
	* **Program of Study**: Select the beauty program you are enrolling in (Cosmetology, Esthetics, etc.).
	* **Program Start Date**: Enter the date you will begin your program.
3. Provide guardian information (if under 18):
	* **Parent or Guardian Name**: Enter the name of the student's parent or guardian.
	* **Parent or Guardian Email**: Enter the contact email address for the parent or guardian.
	* **Parent or Guardian Phone**: Enter the contact phone number for the parent or guardian.
4. Enter payment information:
	* **Total Tuition Amount**: Enter the total program tuition as shown in the enrollment agreement.
	* **Registration Fee**: Enter the non-refundable registration fee.
	* **Student Kit Fee**: Enter the cost of the required student kit and supplies.
	* **Payment Method**: Select how you will be paying (Cash or Check, Credit Card, etc.).
	* **Payment Plan Option**: Select the payment plan if applicable (Monthly Installments, etc.).
5. Confirm your financial aid status:
	* **Have You Applied for Financial Aid**: Select whether you have submitted FAFSA or other aid applications.
6. Agree to the school's policies:
	* **Refund Policy Acknowledgment**: Select that you understand and agree to the refund policy.
	* **Payment Schedule Understanding**: Select that you understand the payment schedule and due dates.
	* **Late Fee Policy Agreement**: Select that you understand late payments incur additional fees.
	* **Withdrawal Policy Understanding**: Select that you understand the policy for withdrawing from the program.
	* **Collection Policy Agreement**: Select that you understand unpaid balances may go to collections.
	* **Additional Fees Acknowledgment**: Select that you understand there may be additional fees for licensing exams and materials.
7. Sign the agreement:
	* **Student Signature Agreement**: Select that you agree to all terms and conditions.
	* **Guardian Signature Agreement**: If the student is under 18, select that the parent or guardian agrees to the terms and conditions.
8. Date the agreement:
	* **Date of Agreement**: The date of the agreement will be automatically filled in.
