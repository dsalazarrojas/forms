# Influenza Declination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Influenza Declination Form is used to document the declination of services, including the date and reason for declination, facility information, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter the patient's information in the corresponding fields.
3. Enter the facility information in the corresponding fields.
4. Enter the contact information in the corresponding fields.
5. Enter the reason for declination in the corresponding fields.
6. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient ID** (1): Enter the patient's unique identifier.
* **Name** (2): Enter the patient's name.
* **Date of Service** (3): Enter the date of the service that was declined.
* **Time of Service** (4): Enter the time of the service that was declined.
* **Facility ID** (5): Enter the facility's unique identifier.
* **Physician ID** (6): Enter the physician's unique identifier.
* **Date Declined** (7): Enter the date of the declination.
* **Reason for Declination** (8): Enter the reason for the declination.
* **Signature** (9): Enter your signature.
* **Signature Date** (10): Enter the date of your signature.
* **Facility Name** (11): Enter the name of the facility.
* **Physician Name** (12): Enter the name of the physician.
* **Declination Date** (13): Enter the date of the declination.
* **Patient Age** (14): Enter the patient's age.
* **Reason for Declination** (15): Enter the reason for the declination.
* **Contact Person** (16): Enter the contact person's name.
* **Contact Number** (17): Enter the contact number.
* **Contact Email** (18): Enter the contact email.
* **Contact Title** (19): Enter the contact title.
* **Facility Address** (20): Enter the facility's address.
* **Facility City** (21): Enter the facility's city.
* **Facility State** (22): Enter the facility's state.
* **Facility Zip** (23): Enter the facility's zip code.
* **Facility Phone** (24): Enter the facility's phone number.
* **Facility Type** (25): Choose the type of facility from the dropdown menu.
