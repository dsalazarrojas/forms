# Power Of Attorney Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Power of Attorney Consent Form is used to establish a legal relationship between two parties, where one party (the grantor) grants authority to another party (the grantee) to act on their behalf. This form is a crucial document that confirms the grantor's consent and understanding of the power they are granting.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and make sure you understand the terms and conditions of the power of attorney being granted.
2. Complete the General Information page with your basic details.
3. Select the correct relationship between you and the grantee from the options provided.
4. Indicate the purpose for which you are granting power of attorney.
5. If applicable, specify the date when the power of attorney becomes effective.
6. If required, sign and date the form, and if needed, have it notarized by a notary public.
7. If necessary, draw a signature to confirm your identity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information**: This section is used to collect your basic information, such as name and contact details.
* **Grantor**: Please select how you know the grantee (e.g., Self, Spouse, Family Member, Friend, or Other).
* **Grantee**: Provide the name and contact details of the person receiving the power of attorney.
* **Relationship**: Specify the relationship between you and the grantee (e.g., Parent/Child, Spouse, Sibling, Family Friend, or Other).
* **Purpose**: Indicate the purpose for which you are granting power of attorney (e.g., Financial, Real Estate, Health Care, Business).
* **Date Effective**: If the power of attorney is not effective immediately, specify the date when it becomes effective.
* **Signed**: Confirm that you have signed the form by selecting True.
* **Signature**: This section is used to provide your signature, which is a confirmation of your identity.
* **Draw Signature**: If required, draw a signature to confirm your identity.
* **Notary Public**: Confirm whether the form has been notarized by a notary public.

## Tips

* Make sure to sign and date the form in the presence of a notary public if required.
* If you are unable to sign the form, explain why in the space provided.
* Review the form carefully to ensure you understand the scope and limitations of the power of attorney being granted.
