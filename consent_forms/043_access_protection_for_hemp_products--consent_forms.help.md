# Access Protection For Hemp Products - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to verify and acknowledge a customer's eligibility to access hemp products.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by reviewing the form and ensuring you have all required information available.
2. Fill out the customer's **Full Name** (section 2) as it appears on your ID.
3. Enter the customer's **Date of Birth** (section 3) in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Select **I Certify** to confirm you are at least 21 years old (section 5).
5. Confirm that you have provided a valid government-issued ID for **ID Upload Confirmation** (section 6).
6. Select **I Understand** to confirm you will comply with all local and state laws regarding hemp products (section 8).
7. Select **I Acknowledge** to confirm you are aware of the health warnings associated with hemp-derived products (section 9).
8. Select **I Pledge** to confirm you will not redistribute these products to minors (section 10).
9. Select **I Agree** to confirm you agree to the retailer's terms of service and privacy policy (section 12).
10. Select **I Release** to confirm you release the vendor from liability related to the misuse of these products (section 13).
11. Type your **Full Name as Signature** (section 15) to sign the form.
12. Select the current date for **Todays Date** (section 16).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name (2)**: Enter the customer's full name as it appears on their ID.
* **Date of Birth (3)**: Enter the customer's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **I Certify (5)**: Confirm you are at least 21 years old.
* **ID Upload Confirmation (6)**: Confirm you have provided a valid government-issued ID for verification.
* **I Understand (8)**: Confirm you will comply with all local and state laws regarding hemp products.
* **I Acknowledge (9)**: Confirm you are aware of the health warnings associated with hemp-derived products.
* **I Pledge (10)**: Confirm you will not redistribute these products to minors.
* **I Agree (12)**: Confirm you agree to the retailer's terms of service and privacy policy.
* **I Release (13)**: Confirm you release the vendor from liability related to the misuse of these products.
* **Full Name as Signature (15)**: Type your full name as a signature.
* **Todays Date (16)**: Select the current date.
