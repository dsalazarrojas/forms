# Single Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Single Release Form is used to collect information from contributors, including the release date, contributor's name, contact number, permission status, and a note for their signature.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. The form will display different sections based on the page id.
3. On the first page, select a date in the "Release Date" field.
4. On the second page, enter the contributor's name in the "Contributor Name" field.
5. On the second page, enter the contributor's contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
6. On the third page, select the contributor's permission status in the "Contributor Permission" field.
7. On the third page, add a note for the contributor's signature in the "Contributor Signature" field.
8. Click on the "Submit" button to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date on which the release is being made.
* **Contributor Name** (`contributor_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the contributor.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter the contact number of the contributor.
* **Contributor Permission** (`contributor_permission`, select_one, required: false): Select the contributor's permission status. Options are 'True', 'False', or 'Not Sure'.
* **Contributor Signature** (`contributor_signature`, note, required: false): Add a note for the contributor's signature. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately to ensure the form is complete and submitted correctly.
* Double-check the information entered before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the instructions above for clarification.
