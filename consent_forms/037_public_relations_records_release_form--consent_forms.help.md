# Public Relations Records Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about an individual's or organization's consent to share their public relations information. The form helps to ensure that the information is used correctly and that the person or organization is aware of the purpose and scope of use.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Select the correct options for "Consent Period" and "Use of Information" based on your needs.
3. Provide a brief description of the "Other Information Use" if you select "Other (please specify)".
4. Sign and date the form, indicating your agreement to the terms.
5. Submit the form to the relevant authorities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name (`organization_name`, text, optional):** Enter the name of the organization that you're representing.
* **Contact Person (`contact_person`, text, optional):** Enter the name of the person who can be contacted for further information.
* **Role (`role`, text, optional):** Enter your role within the organization.
* **Department (`department`, text, optional):** Enter the department within the organization that you're from.
* **Contact Email (`contact_email`, email, optional):** Enter the email address of the person or organization's contact email.
* **Phone Number (`phone_number`, text, optional):** Enter the phone number of the person or organization.
* **Consent Date (`consent_date`, date, optional):** Enter the date on which you're giving consent to share the information.
* **Consent Period (`consent_period`, select, optional):** Select the duration for which you're giving consent to share the information (6 months, 1 year, 2 years, 5 years).
* **Use of Information (`use_information`, select, optional):** Select the purpose for which you're sharing the information (Public relations activities, Marketing, Research, Other (please specify)).
* **Other Information Use (`other_information_use`, text, optional):** If you selected "Other (please specify)" above, provide a brief description of the other purpose.
* **Other (please specify) (`other`, text, optional):** If you need to specify something else, enter it here.
* **Signature Date (`signature_date`, time, optional):** Enter the date on which you signed the form.
* **Signature (`signature`, note, optional):** Sign the form, indicating your agreement to the terms.
* **Agreement (`agreement`, select, optional):** Confirm your agreement to the terms of the form by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Comments (`comments`, text, optional):** Add any comments or notes about the form.
* **Notes (`notes`, text, optional):** Add any additional information or notes.
* **Submitted by (`submitted_by`, select, optional):** Select who submitted the form (Client, Staff, Other).

Please review each field carefully and fill it out according to your needs. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
