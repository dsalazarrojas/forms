# Chiropractic Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help you provide accurate information for your initial consultation with our chiropractor. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form carefully and honestly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form in sections, from top to bottom.
2. Enter your personal details such as name, contact information, and medical history.
3. Describe your reason for visiting the chiropractor and any relevant medical conditions.
4. Answer questions about your lifestyle habits, including exercise, diet, and sleep patterns.
5. Confirm your medical treatment history and any other healthcare providers you're currently seeing.
6. Sign and date the form to acknowledge the accuracy of the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Patient Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Age**: Enter your age in years.
* **Gender**: Select your gender from the provided options.
* **Marital Status**: Select your current marital status from the provided options.
* **Current Occupation**: Enter your current occupation.
* **Employer Name**: Enter your current employer's name (if applicable).
* **Home Phone Number**: Enter your home phone number.
* **Mobile Phone Number**: Enter your mobile phone number.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
* **Home Address**: Enter your home address, including street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Emergency Contact Information**: Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: Select how you heard about our chiropractic services.
* **Referred By**: If you were referred by another person or doctor, enter their name.
* **Reason for Visit**: Describe your primary reason for visiting the chiropractor.
* **Primary Complaint**: Describe your main concern.
* **Pain Areas**: Select all areas of your body experiencing pain or discomfort.
* **Pain Intensity**: Rate your pain intensity on a scale of 1-10.
* **Pain Duration**: Select how long you've been experiencing your symptoms.
* **Pain Frequency**: Select how often you experience symptoms.
* **What Makes Worse**: Describe what makes your condition worse.
* **What Makes Better**: Describe what makes your condition better.
* **Medical History**: Provide any relevant medical history.
* **Current Medications**: List any medications you're currently taking, including dosages.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies you have, including medications, foods, or environmental factors.
* **Medical Conditions**: Select any medical conditions you have.
* **Previous Surgeries or Hospitalizations**: List any previous surgeries or hospitalizations, including dates.
* **Family Medical History**: Provide any relevant family medical history.
* **Lifestyle and Health Habits**: Describe your daily routines.
* **Exercise Routine**: Select your exercise frequency.
* **Diet Quality**: Select how you would describe your diet.
* **Water Intake**: Enter your average water intake in glasses per day.
* **Sleep Quality**: Select your sleep quality.
* **Sleep Hours**: Enter your average hours of sleep per night.
* **Tobacco Use**: Select whether you use tobacco products.
* **Alcohol Consumption**: Select your alcohol consumption frequency.
* **Caffeine Intake**: Enter your daily caffeine intake in cups.
* **Stress Level**: Select your current stress level.
* **Previous Chiropractic Care**: Describe any previous chiropractic care you've received.
* **Had Chiropractic Care Before**: Select whether you've had any chiropractic care before.
* **Previous Chiropractic Experience**: Select how you would describe your previous chiropractic experience.
* **Other Healthcare Providers**: List any other healthcare providers you're currently seeing or have seen in the past.
* **Currently Seeing Doctor**: Select whether you're currently seeing another healthcare provider.
* **Provider Name and Specialty**: Enter the name and specialty of your current healthcare provider (if applicable).
* **Current Treatment**: Select whether you're receiving treatment for this condition elsewhere.
* **Consent Agreement**: Confirm that you acknowledge the information provided is accurate.
* **I Certify Information Accurate**: Confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Consent to Treatment**: Confirm your consent to chiropractic examination and treatment.
* **Patient Signature**: Provide your digital signature to acknowledge the accuracy of the information provided.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date of your signature.

Note: Please fill out this form carefully and honestly to provide the best possible care for your initial consultation with our chiropractor.
