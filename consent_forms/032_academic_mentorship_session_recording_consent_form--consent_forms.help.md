# Academic Mentorship Session Recording Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to capture the necessary information and consent for recording and publishing academic mentorship sessions. It helps ensure that both the mentee and mentor are aware of and agree to the terms of the recording and its usage.

## How To Complete This Form

- Start by filling out the form with the necessary information for the mentee and mentor.
- Select the purpose of the recording from the options provided.
- Indicate your consent to record the session.
- Provide your digital signature to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mentee name** (`mentee_name`, `text`, required): The full name of the mentee participating in the session.
* **Mentor name** (`mentor_name`, `text`, required): The full name of the mentor participating in the session.
* **Session date** (`session_date`, `date`, required): The date of the mentorship session.
* **Purpose of recording** (`recording_purpose`, `select_one`, required): The reason why the session is being recorded. Choose from the options: Training, Internal notes, Archival, or Other.
* **Consent to record** (`consent_to_record`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you consent to record the session. Options: True or False.
* **Permission to publish excerpts** (`publish_permissions`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you consent to publish excerpts of the recording. Options: True or False.
* **Retention period for recordings** (`retention_period`, `text`, optional): The length of time the recording will be stored. 
* **Digital signature** (`signature`, `text`, required): Type your full name to confirm your consent.
* **Additional notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Any other instructions or restrictions for the recording.
