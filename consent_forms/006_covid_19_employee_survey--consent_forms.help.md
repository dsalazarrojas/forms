# COVID 19 Employee Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID 19 Employee Survey is designed to gather information from employees about their concerns and preferences regarding COVID-19 in the workplace. Please take a few minutes to answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out your employee details, including your full name, employee ID number, department, and position.
2. Next, select your primary concern about COVID-19 in the workplace from the provided list. Please describe your concern in detail in the box below.
3. Answer the questions about your perception of workplace safety measures and your preference for remote work.
4. In the "Consent and Agreement" section, read and agree or disagree with each statement.
5. Finally, provide any additional feedback or suggestions you may have for improving workplace safety.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (employee_name, text, required)
	+ Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Employee ID Number** (employee_id, text, required)
	+ Enter your employee ID number as assigned to you by the company.
* **Department** (department, text, required)
	+ Select your department from the list of options (if provided).
* **Job Position** (position, text, required)
	+ Enter your job title or position in the company.
* **Email Address** (email, email, required)
	+ Enter your official company email address.
* **Phone Number** (phone, text, optional)
	+ Enter your office phone number (if you have one).
* **What is your primary concern about COVID-19 in the workplace?** (primary_concern, select_one, required)
	+ Select one of the provided options: Safety of work environment, Adequate protective equipment, Health benefits coverage, Job security, Work schedule changes, or Other.
* **Please describe your concern in detail** (concern_description, text, optional)
	+ Provide a brief description of your primary concern.
* **How safe do you feel about current workplace safety measures?** (workplace_safety_measures, select_one, required)
	+ Select one of the provided options: Very safe, Somewhat safe, Neutral, Somewhat unsafe, or Very unsafe.
* **Would you prefer to work remotely?** (remote_work_preference, select_one, required)
	+ Select one of the provided options: Yes, full remote, Hybrid preferred, On-site preferred, or No preference.
* **I agree to follow all workplace safety protocols and procedures** (agree_safety_protocols, select_one, required)
	+ Select Agree or Disagree.
* **I consent to health screening procedures as implemented by the company** (agree_health_screening, select_one, required)
	+ Select Agree or Disagree.
* **I understand vaccination recommendations from public health authorities** (agree_vaccination, select_one, required)
	+ Select Agree or Disagree.
* **I acknowledge receiving and understanding COVID-19 workplace information** (acknowledge_information, select_one, required)
	+ Select True or False.
* **Any suggestions for improving workplace safety?** (suggestions, text, optional)
	+ Provide any suggestions you may have for improving workplace safety.
* **Survey Date** (survey_date, date, required)
	+ Date of completion of this survey.

Please take a moment to review your answers before submitting the form.
