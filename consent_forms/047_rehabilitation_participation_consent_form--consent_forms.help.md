# Rehabilitation Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rehabilitation Participation Consent Form is designed to collect consent from rehabilitation participants to participate in the program. This form is used to gather essential details from participants, ensuring they understand the program's terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant information on page 1.
2. Provide your contact details on page 2.
3. Select the relevant treatment procedures that apply to you on page 3.
4. Choose the treatment options you are interested in on page 4.
5. Confirm your informed consent on page 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_information (Page 1)** (`participant_information`, text, required/optional): This page is where you provide your name, address, and any other relevant participant information. Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately.
* **contact_details (Page 2)** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): This page is where you provide your contact information, including phone number and email. Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately.
* **treatment_procedures (Page 3)** (`treatment_procedures`, text, required/optional): This page is where you select the treatment procedures that apply to you. Please choose all procedures that apply.
* **treatment_options (Page 4)** (`treatment_options`, text, required/optional): This page is where you select the treatment options you are interested in. Please choose all options that apply.
* **informed_consent (Page 5)** (`informed_consent`, select_multiple, required): This page is where you confirm your informed consent to participate in the program. Please select "True" if you agree to the program's terms and conditions, and "False" if you do not.
