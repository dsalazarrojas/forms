# photography_session_contract_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the fields of the photography session contract form to help you complete it accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your User Information.
2. Select the Date and Time of the session.
3. Provide the Location of the session.
4. Enter the name of the Photographer and any Assistants.
5. Enter the Client's name.
6. Sign the contract to confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms.
7. Select the Type of Contract.
8. Enter the contract duration and price.
9. Specify the payment terms.
10. Confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms by selecting the relevant options.
11. Enter any contract notes (optional).
12. If you are the client, confirm that you have signed the contract.
13. Enter any other details as required (such as IP address, contract number, contract status).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (User Information, text, required: false)**: Enter your information to be used for correspondence and contract management.
* **Date Of Session (Date, required: true)**: Select the date of the photography session.
* **Time Of Session (Time, required: true)**: Select the time of the photography session.
* **Location (text, required: false)**: Enter the location of the session (e.g., studio, park, etc.).
* **Photographer (text, required: false)**: Enter the name of the photographer or assistant(s) involved in the session.
* **Assistant (text, required: false)**: Enter the name(s) of any assistants involved in the session.
* **Client (text, required: false)**: Enter your name or the name of the client.
* **Signature (text, required: true)**: Sign the contract to confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms.
* **Type Of Contract (select_one, required: true)**: Select the type of contract that applies to your session (e.g., wedding, portrait, commercial).
* **Contract Duration (number, required: true)**: Enter the duration of the contract in hours.
* **Contract Price (number, required: true)**: Enter the price of the contract.
* **Payment Terms (text, required: true)**: Specify any payment terms or conditions.
* **I Have Read And Agreed To The Terms (select_multiple, required: true)**: Confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms by selecting the relevant options.
* **Contract Notes (text, required: false)**: Enter any additional contract notes or comments.
* **Contract Signed (Time, required: false)**: Enter the time the contract was signed.
* **Signed By (text, required: false)**: Enter the name of the person who signed the contract.
* **Signed On (Date, required: false)**: Enter the date the contract was signed.
* **Signed At (Time, required: false)**: Enter the time the contract was signed.
* **Signed Via IP Address (text, required: false)**: Enter the IP address used to sign the contract.
* **IP Address (text, required: false)**: Enter the IP address associated with the contract.
* **Contract Version (text, required: false)**: Enter the version of the contract.
* **Contract Number (text, required: false)**: Enter the contract number.
* **Contract Status (text, required: false)**: Enter the status of the contract.
* **Signature Image (text, required: true)**: Upload a scanned or digital image of your signature to confirm that you have signed the contract.

Please fill out the form carefully and thoroughly to ensure a smooth and successful photography session. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the photographer or assistant.
