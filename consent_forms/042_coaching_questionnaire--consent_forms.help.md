# Coaching Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about you and your expectations from coaching services. Please answer the questions as accurately and truthfully as possible. This information will help your coach understand your needs and goals.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  The form is divided into several sections. Please fill each section out completely and accurately.
2.  You will be asked to provide some personal details such as name, email, and contact information.
3.  Please select the correct medical conditions and mental health history.
4.  Answer questions about your lifestyle, such as exercise habits and sleep patterns.
5.  Rate your stress levels and overall life satisfaction.
6.  If you have previous coaching experience, describe it briefly.
7.  Specify your expectations from coaching services.
8.  Finally, you will be asked to confirm that the information you have provided is accurate and that you consent to coaching services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Full Name**: Your legal full name.
* **2. Email Address**: Your primary contact email.
* **3. Phone Number**: Your contact phone number.
* **4. Date of Birth**: For identification purposes only.
* **5. Mailing Address**: Your full address.
* **6. Current Occupation**: Your current job or profession.
* **7. Employer Name** (optional): Name of your organization.
* **8. Marital Status**: Your relationship status.
* **9. Number of Dependents**: How many people depend on you.
* **10. Type of Coaching Seeking**: What type of coaching you want.
* **11. Primary Reason for Seeking Coaching**: What brings you to coaching.
* **12. Coaching Goals**: What you want to achieve through coaching.
* **13. Current Medical Conditions**: List any health conditions you have.
* **14. Chronic Illnesses or Diseases**: Any chronic conditions.
* **15. Current Medications**: List all medications you are taking.
* **16. Allergies**: Any known allergies.
* **17. Mental Health History**: Any mental health conditions.
* **18. Currently Seeing Therapist**: Are you in therapy now?
* **19. Therapist Name** (if applicable): Name of your therapist.
* **20. Sleep Patterns**: Describe your sleep habits.
* **21. Exercise Habits**: Describe your activity level.
* **22. Diet and Nutrition**: Describe your eating habits.
* **23. Alcohol or Substance Use**: Describe your consumption.
* **24. Smoking Status**: Do you smoke?
* **25. Current Stress Level**: Rate your stress level from 1 to 10.
* **26. Sources of Stress**: What causes you stress.
* **27. Support System**: Describe your support network.
* **28. Overall Life Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction from 1 to 5.
* **29. Previous Coaching Experience**: If you have had coaching before, describe it.
* **30. Expectations from Coaching**: What you expect from coaching.
* **31. I Consent to Coaching Services**: Confirm you agree to receive coaching.
* **32. I Confirm Information Is Accurate**: Confirm all information is true.
* **33. Questionnaire Date**: Today's date.

Note: Please ensure you fill out the form accurately and truthfully. If you have any doubts or questions, please contact your coach.
