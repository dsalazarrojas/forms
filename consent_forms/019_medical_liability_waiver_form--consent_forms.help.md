# Medical Liability Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a consent document that helps establish a clear understanding between a participant and an organization about the risks associated with a medical procedure or activity. It ensures that the participant acknowledges and accepts the risks, and provides necessary contact information for emergency purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in the participant's name and guardian's name (if applicable) in the respective fields.
2. Provide the name and contact information of the emergency contact person.
3. If the participant has a medical condition, please describe it in the medical condition field.
4. If a medical procedure is involved, please describe it in the medical procedure field.
5. Read and acknowledge the risk associated with the activity or procedure by typing 'yes' in the risk acknowledgement field.
6. Finally, sign and date the participant signature field to confirm that you have read and understood the risks and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Name** (Participant Name, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person participating in the medical procedure or activity.
  This field is required so that we can address the participant by their name throughout the consent process.
- **Guardian Name** (Guardian Name, text, required/optional): If the participant is a minor, enter their guardian's name.
  This field is optional as not all participants may have a guardian, but including it ensures that we can contact the guardian if needed.
- **Emergency Contact Name** (Emergency Contact Name, text, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information of the person to be notified in case of an emergency.
  This field ensures that we have a contact person who can be reached in case of a medical emergency.
- **Medical Condition** (Medical Condition, text, required/optional): Describe any medical condition that may affect the participant's ability to perform the activity or procedure.
  This field helps us understand any potential risks or limitations associated with the participant's medical condition.
- **Medical Procedure** (Medical Procedure, text, required/optional): Describe the medical procedure or activity that the participant will be participating in.
  This field helps us understand the specific risks associated with the procedure or activity.
- **Risk Acknowledgement** (Risk Acknowledgement, text, required/optional): Type 'yes' to indicate that you have read and understand the risks associated with the activity or procedure.
  This field is a critical component of the consent process, as it ensures that the participant is aware of the risks involved.
- **Participant Signature** (Participant Signature, text, required/optional): Sign and date this field to confirm that you have read and understood the risks and conditions outlined in this form.
  This field is required as it serves as proof that the participant has provided informed consent.
