# Medical Consent And Indemnity Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather necessary medical information, including treatment details, consent to treatment, and other relevant data. By completing this form, patients can provide their consent and medical history, allowing healthcare providers to better understand their needs and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form

* Ensure you have all necessary information and documents before completing this form.
* Review each field carefully and enter accurate details.
* Use the available options to select the correct answers.
* Sign the form with a signature (if applicable) and date it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, required: false): Enter your name, address, and any other relevant details as instructed by the healthcare provider.
* **Consent to Treatment** (`consent_to_treatment`, select one, required: false): Indicate your consent to treatment by selecting 'True'. If you do not consent, please select 'False'.
* **Treatment Details** (`treatment_details`, text, required: false): Describe the treatment you are receiving, including any medications or procedures.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required: false): Sign the form to confirm your understanding and consent to the treatment.
* **Medical Procedures** (`medical_procedures`, select multiple, required: false): List any medical procedures you have undergone or are undergoing, such as surgeries or tests.
* **Patient Contact Information** (`patient_contact_information`, text, required: false): Provide your contact information, including your phone number and any other relevant details.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, select multiple, required: false): Indicate if you have an emergency contact, and provide their name and contact information.
* **Witness Information** (`witness_information`, text, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of a witness, if applicable.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the treatment or procedure.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the treatment or procedure.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, select one, required: false): Indicate when you signed the form, using the 'today' option or selecting 'other' and entering the correct date.
* **Signature Time** (`signature_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time you signed the form.
* **Signature Notary** (`signature_notary`, select one, required: false): Indicate if the form has been notarized, selecting 'notarized' if true.
* **Notarized Date** (`notarized_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date the form was notarized.
