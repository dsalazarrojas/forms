# Theme Park Ride Filming Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from guests to film them while visiting the theme park. It is essential to have this consent to ensure that the guest is aware of the filming and gives their permission for it to take place.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the requested information, such as the filming location, date, time, and duration.
2. Indicate if you are filming with a team or not.
3. Specify any additional team members if you are filming with a team.
4. List any additional filming equipment you are using.
5. Add any additional filming notes.
6. Provide your name, email, and phone number.
7. Sign the form to confirm your consent.
8. If you are not filming with a team, provide any other team members you are with.
9. If you are not filming with standard equipment, specify the equipment you are using.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Theme Park Ride Filming Consent** (`theme_park_ride_filming_consent_form`, text, optional):
    * This field is where you can provide a brief description of the filming location and purpose.
* **Filming Location** (`filming_location`, text, optional):
    * Please specify where you are planning to film in the theme park.
* **Filming Date** (`filming_date`, date, optional):
    * Enter the date you plan to film.
* **Filming Time** (`filming_time`, time, optional):
    * Enter the time you plan to film.
* **Filming Duration** (`filming_duration`, number, optional):
    * Enter the length of time you plan to film for.
* **Filming Area** (`filming_area`, select_one, optional):
    * Check "Yes" if you plan to film in a specific area of the park, or "No" if you are not planning to film in a specific area.
* **Other (specify)** (`filming_area_other`, text, optional):
    * If you checked "Yes" for filming area, please specify which area you are planning to film.
* **Filming Team** (`filming_team`, select_multiple, optional):
    * Check "Yes" if you are filming with a team, or "No" if you are not.
* **Other Team Members** (`filming_team_other`, text, optional):
    * If you checked "Yes" for filming team, please list any other team members.
* **Filming Equipment** (`filming_equipment`, select_multiple, optional):
    * Check "Yes" if you are using standard park-approved equipment, or "No" if you are not.
* **Other Equipment** (`filming_equipment_other`, text, optional):
    * If you checked "No" for standard equipment, please specify the equipment you are using.
* **Filming Notes** (`filming_notes`, note, optional):
    * Add any additional notes about the filming.
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, optional):
    * Please provide your name as the guest being filmed.
* **Guest Email** (`guest_email`, email, optional):
    * Please provide your email address.
* **Guest Phone** (`guest_phone`, text, optional):
    * Please provide your phone number.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional):
    * Sign this form to confirm your consent for filming.
* **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`, text, optional):
    * A park representative will sign this field to confirm they have witnessed your consent.
* **Filming Agreement** (`filming_agreement`, select_one, optional):
    * Check "Yes" to confirm you have read and understood the filming agreement, or "No" otherwise.

# Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and honest information about the filming location, date, time, and duration.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask an authorized park representative for clarification.
* This form is for your consent, and you can withdraw your consent at any time.
