# Portrait Photography Session Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to book a portrait photography session with a professional photographer. To begin, ensure you have not already booked a session or have an existing session scheduled.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields marked as "required" in the form.
2. Ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date contact information, including your email address and phone number.
3. Choose the preferred location for the session (Studio, Home, or Online).
4. Select the type of session you would like to book (Family, Couple, or Individual).
5. Choose the duration of the session (30 minutes, 1 hour, or 2 hours).
6. Add any additional notes that you would like to share with the photographer or the client.
7. Review the form carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it would appear on your identification or documents.
* **Photographer Email** (`photographer_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the photographer you would like to book with.
* **Photographer Phone** (`photographer_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the photographer you would like to book with.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for communication.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Select the date of your desired photography session.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Select the time of your desired photography session.
* **Location** (`location`, `select_one`, required): Choose from the options: Studio, Home, or Online.
* **Session Type** (`session_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose from the options: Family, Couple, or Individual.
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose from the options: 30 minutes, 1 hour, or 2 hours.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required): Share any additional information with the photographer.
* **Client Notes** (`client_notes`, `note`, optional): Share any additional information with the client.
* **Photographer Notes** (`photographer_notes`, `note`, optional): Share any additional information with the photographer.
