# No Show Policy Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to document and acknowledge a patient's no-show, including the reason for the no-show and the method used to contact the patient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the service that the patient failed to show up for.
2. Provide a brief reason for the patient's no-show.
3. Enter the date of the scheduled appointment.
4. Enter the date the patient failed to show up.
5. Choose the method used to contact the patient (e.g., phone call, text message, email, or in-person).
6. Enter the contact number or email used to contact the patient.
7. Enter any additional service details.
8. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Service** (`Service`, select multiple): Select the type of service that the patient failed to show up for (e.g., Appointment, Consultation, Procedure).
- **Reason for Service** (`Reason for Service`, text): Provide a brief reason for the patient's no-show.
- **Appointment Date** (`Appointment Date`, date): Enter the date of the scheduled appointment.
- **No Show Date** (`No Show Date`, date): Enter the date the patient failed to show up.
- **Reason for No-Show** (`Reason for No-Show`, text): Provide a brief reason for the patient's no-show.
- **Contact Method** (`Contact Method`, select one): Choose the method used to contact the patient (e.g., phone call, text message, email, or in-person).
- **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, text): Enter the contact number or email used to contact the patient.
- **Email** (`Email`, email): Enter the email used to contact the patient.
- **Service Details** (`Service Details`, text): Enter any additional service details.
- **Signature** (`Signature`, text): Sign and date the form to acknowledge the patient's no-show.
- **No Show Policy** (`No Show Policy`, text): Enter any additional no-show policy information.
- **Date Signed** (`Date Signed`, date): Enter the date the form was signed.
- **Time Signed** (`Time Signed`, time): Enter the time the form was signed.
- **No Show Penalty** (`No Show Penalty`, number): Enter any applicable no-show penalty.
- **No Show Fees** (`No Show Fees`, text): Enter any additional no-show fees information.

Note: The fields marked as required are essential for completing the form. Please ensure that all required fields are filled in to maintain accurate records.
