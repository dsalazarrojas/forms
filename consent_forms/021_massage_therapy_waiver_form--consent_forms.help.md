# Massage Therapy Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Massage Therapy Waiver Form is used to collect essential information from clients before providing massage therapy services.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form correctly, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the title of the massage therapy form.
2.  Select the category of the form, which is usually "Consent Forms." 
3.  Provide a brief description of your massage therapy requirements, limitations, or any other relevant information.
4.  Enter your name and contact information for your client.
5.  Choose the date and time you wish to have the massage therapy session.
6.  List any risks involved in the massage therapy session.
7.  Confirm whether you are aware of the risks involved.
8.  Disclose any health information that could affect the treatment process.
9.  Provide emergency contact information.
10.  Enter your email address.
11.  Enter your phone number.
12.  Choose the massage therapist's name, email address, and phone number.
13.  Select the type(s) of massage you want to receive.
14.  Indicate the duration of the massage session.
15.  Choose the frequency of the massage sessions.
16.  Confirm whether you have given consent for the massage therapy session.
17.  Provide a note about any other relevant information.
18.  Sign the form to confirm your consent.
19.  Sign the form as the therapist to confirm your agreement to treat the client.
20.  Update the form submission status and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Massage Therapy Waiver Form** (`form_title`, text, not required): This is the title of the form that must be filled out by the client.
* **Category** (`form_category`, select_one, not required): This is the category of the form, which is usually "Consent Forms."
* **Description** (`form_description`, note, not required): This is a field where you can provide a brief description of your massage therapy requirements, limitations, or any other relevant information.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, not required): Enter your name as the client.
* **Date** (`form_date`, date, not required): Choose the date you wish to have the massage therapy session.
* **Time** (`form_time`, time, not required): Select the time you want to have the massage therapy session.
* **Risks Involved** (`form_risks`, text, not required): List any risks involved in the massage therapy session.
* **Are you aware of the risks involved?** (`risk_aware`, select_one, not required): Confirm whether you are aware of the risks involved.
* **Will you disclose any health information that could impact the treatment?** (`risk_disclose`, select_multiple, not required): Disclose any health information that could affect the treatment process.
* **Emergency Contact Information** (`emergency_contacts`, note, not required): Provide emergency contact information for the client or a family member.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, not required): Enter your email address.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **Therapist Name** (`therapist_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the massage therapist.
* **Therapist Email** (`therapist_email`, email, not required): Enter the massage therapist's email address.
* **Therapist Phone** (`therapist_phone`, text, not required): Enter the massage therapist's phone number.
* **Massage Type** (`massage_type`, select_multiple, not required): Choose the type(s) of massage you want to receive.
* **Massage Duration** (`massage_duration`, number, not required): Indicate the duration of the massage session.
* **Massage Frequency** (`massage_frequency`, select_multiple, not required): Choose the frequency of the massage sessions.
* **Frequency of Massages** (`massage_frequency_2`, select_multiple, not required): This field is redundant and has the same functionality as the previous field.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, not required): Confirm your consent for the massage therapy session.
* **Client Signature** (`form_signature`, select_one, not required): Sign the form to confirm your consent.
* **Therapist Signature** (`form_signature_2`, select_one, not required): Sign the form as the therapist to confirm your agreement to treat the client.
* **Submission Status** (`form_submission_status`, select_one, not required): Update the form submission status.
* **Submission Date** (`form_submission_date`, date, not required): Update the form submission date.
* **Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, text, not required): Enter who submitted the form.

Note: The fields that are marked as "not required" are not necessarily mandatory but are recommended for the smooth operation of the massage therapy service.
