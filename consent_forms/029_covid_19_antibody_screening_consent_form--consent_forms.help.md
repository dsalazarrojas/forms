# COVID 19 Antibody Screening Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from patients about their COVID-19 antibody screening test. The test is used to detect past infection with the SARS-CoV-2 virus, which causes COVID-19. Please note that this form is for screening purposes only and does not provide a definitive diagnosis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your patient's name, date of birth, and other demographic information in the corresponding fields.
2. Select your gender from the provided options.
3. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
4. Provide your address and zip code for billing and communication purposes.
5. Identify your emergency contact person.
6. Choose a test date and provider.
7. Read and acknowledge the test explanation and purpose.
8. Acknowledge that you understand the test's limitations and the meaning of the test results.
9. Provide your consent for blood sample collection and data sharing.
10. Choose how you would like to receive your test results.
11. Confirm that you understand the timing of your test results.
12. Provide your consent for your test results to be shared with health departments for surveillance purposes.
13. Choose whether you want to share your test results for research purposes.
14. If applicable, authorize your insurance company to bill your insurance.
15. If necessary, indicate if a follow-up discussion with your healthcare provider is needed.
16. Answer if your questions to the healthcare provider have been answered.
17. (Optional) If you have any remaining questions, enter them in this field.
18. Sign and date the form with your full name.
19. If applicable, sign and date the form with the witness's full name.
20. Date and sign the form with the healthcare provider's full name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (1, text, required): Fill in your patient's full legal name.
* **Date of Birth** (2, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the MM-DD-YYYY format.
* **Gender** (3, select_one, required): Select your gender from the provided options: Male, Female, Other, or Prefer Not to Say.
* **Email Address** (4, email, required): Enter your email address for test results and follow-up information.
* **Phone Number** (5, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Street Address** (6, text, required): Enter your street address for billing and communication purposes.
* **City** (7, text, required): Enter the city of your residence.
* **State** (8, text, required): Enter your state or province of residence.
* **ZIP Code** (9, text, required): Enter your postal code.
* **Emergency Contact** (10, text, required): Identify the person you want us to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Test Date** (11, date, required): Choose the date for your test.
* **Ordering Provider** (12, text, required): Choose the healthcare provider ordering the test for you.
* **Test Explanation** (13, note, optional): This field is for informational purposes only, providing detailed information about the antibody test.
* **I Understand Test Purpose** (14, select_one, required): Acknowledge that you understand the test's purpose, which is to detect past infection.
* **I Understand Test Limitations** (15, select_one, required): Acknowledge that you understand the test's limitations, which does not diagnose current infection.
* **I Understand Results Meaning** (16, select_one, required): Acknowledge that you understand the meaning of the test results, which may indicate past infection or not.
* **Voluntary Consent** (17, select_one, required): Provide your consent for the test, acknowledging that it's voluntary.
* **Blood Draw Consent** (18, select_one, required): Provide your consent for blood sample collection.
* **Risks Understood** (19, select_one, required): Acknowledge that you understand the test's risks, such as discomfort or bruising.
* **How to Receive Results** (20, select_one, required): Choose how you want to receive your test results: Patient Portal, Email, Phone Call, Mail, or Pick Up In Person.
* **Results Timing Acknowledgment** (21, select_one, required): Acknowledge that you understand the timing of your test results, which is typically 1-3 business days.
* **Data Sharing Consent** (22, select_one, required): Provide your consent for sharing your test results with health departments for surveillance purposes.
* **Research Use Consent** (23, select_one, optional): You can choose to share your test results for anonymous research purposes.
* **Insurance Billing Authorization** (24, select_one, required): Authorize your insurance company to bill for your test.
* **Follow-up Discussion Needed** (25, select_one, required): Indicate if a follow-up discussion with your healthcare provider is necessary.
* **Were Your Questions Answered** (26, select_one, required): Answer if your questions to the healthcare provider have been answered.
* **Patient Questions** (27, text, optional): If you have any remaining questions, enter them in this field.
* **Patient Signature** (28, text, required): Sign and date the form with your full name.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature** (29, text, optional): If the patient is a minor, sign and date the form with the parent or guardian's full name.
* **Witness Name** (30, text, optional): If a healthcare witness is present, enter their name.
* **Witness Signature** (31, text, optional): If a healthcare witness is present, sign and date the form with their full name.
* **Date Signed** (32, date, required): Date and sign the form with your full name.
* **Provider Signature** (33, text, optional): Sign and date the form with the healthcare provider's full name.
