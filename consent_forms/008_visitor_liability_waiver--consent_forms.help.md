# Visitor Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Visitor Liability Waiver form is a necessary document that visitors must complete before participating in an activity or entering the premises. It ensures that visitors are aware of and agree to be responsible for their actions during the event/activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Visitor Liability Waiver**: Please enter your name and date of visit in the designated fields.
2. **Visitor Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
3. **Date of Visit**: Enter the date of your visit in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. **Visitor Contact Information (Optional)**: If you have any contact information (e.g., phone number, email), please enter it for our records. This is not required but recommended in case we need to reach you.
5. **Visitor Signature**: Please select one of the two signature options:
	* If you are physically signing below, choose "Signature".
	* If you cannot physically sign (e.g., you are signing electronically), choose "Other".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Visitor Liability Waiver**: A text field where you can enter your name and date of visit.
* **Visitor Name**: A field to enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Date of Visit**: A date field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Visitor Contact Information (Optional)**: A field for providing your contact information (e.g., phone number, email) for our records.
* **Visitor Signature**: A field with two signature options:
	+ "Signature": Choose this option if you are physically signing below.
	+ "Other": Choose this option if you cannot physically sign (e.g., you are signing electronically).

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter accurate and complete information.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for assistance.
* If you cannot complete this form, please do not proceed with the activity/event.
