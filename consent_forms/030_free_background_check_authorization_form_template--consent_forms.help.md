# Free Background Check Authorization Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to capture the details of an applicant, including contact information, background check details, and consent. It's intended for use by HR or administrative teams to process background checks for employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your applicant details on page 1.
2. Select your preferred contact method (phone, email, or address) on page 2.
3. Provide a brief description of the background check on page 3.
4. Sign the consent form on page 4.
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Details (`applicant_details`, text, optional):** Enter your name, address, and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Contact Information (`contact_info`, select_one, optional):** Choose how you would like to be contacted: Phone, Email, or Address.
* **Background Check Authorization (`background_check`, text, optional):** Describe the background check you are requesting, including what type of check was performed and any results.
* **Consent (`consent`, note, optional):** Read and agree to the terms of the background check.
* **Signature (`signature`, time, optional):** Please sign and date this form to indicate your understanding and agreement.
