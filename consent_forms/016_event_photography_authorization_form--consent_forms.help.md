# Event Photography Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Event Photography Authorization Form is designed to capture the necessary information from event participants to manage event photography. This form is used to inform the event organizers about the participant's consent for the usage of their photo and other details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your **Name**: This is the name of the person filling out the form.
2. Enter your **Email**: This is the email address that the event organizers will use to contact you.
3. Type the **Event Title**: This is the title of the event you are participating in.
4. (Optional) If you want to specify your **Name** again, you can do so. This field is not required.
5. (Optional) If you want to specify your **Email** again, you can do so. This field is not required.
6. Select your **Participant Role**: Choose your role in the event from the options provided (Guest, Performer, Sponsor).
7. Provide your **Contact Information**: This is the address where the event organizers can contact you. This field is required.
8. Select how your **Photo Usage**: Choose which purpose your photo can be used for from the options provided (For marketing purposes, For event promotion, For social media).
