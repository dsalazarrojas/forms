# Covid 19 Questionnaire And Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Covid 19 Questionnaire And Release Form is designed to collect information from patients and healthcare professionals regarding the patient's status and medical history. The form is a crucial tool for medical personnel to gather data for monitoring and reporting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Patient Name field with the patient's name as it is written on their identification or hospital records.
2. Enter the patient's Phone number in the Phone field for contact purposes.
3. If the patient has an email address, enter it in the Email field. This is an optional field.
4. Describe the patient's medical history in the Medical History field, including any relevant medical conditions or allergies.
5. Indicate the patient's release status as True or False in the Release Status field, depending on whether they are being discharged or still in the hospital.
6. Select a date in the Release Date field, representing the date the patient is to be discharged or the date the patient's status changed.
7. If desired, add any additional comments or notes regarding the patient's release in the Release Note field. This is an optional field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (patient_name, text, required): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification or hospital records.
* **Phone** (patient_phone, text, required): Enter the patient's contact phone number for communication and follow-up purposes.
* **Email** (patient_email, text, optional): Enter the patient’s email address, if available, for further communication.
* **Medical History** (medical_history, text, required): Describe the patient's medical history, including any relevant medical conditions, allergies, or previous treatments.
* **Allergies** (allergies, text, required): List any allergies or medical conditions that the patient may have, which may be important for medical professionals.
* **Release Status** (release_status, select_multiple, required): Indicate the patient's release status as "True" for discharged or "False" for still in the hospital.
* **Release Date** (release_date, date, required): Select the date the patient is to be discharged or the date the patient's status changed.
* **Release Note** (release_note, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes regarding the patient’s release, if desired.
