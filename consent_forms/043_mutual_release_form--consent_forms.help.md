# Mutual Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a mutual release form, which is a legal document used in various industries such as finance, law, and real estate to release one party from a dispute or agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the second party involved in the release.
2. Enter the email address of the second party.
3. Provide a brief description of the dispute or issue to be released.
4. Enter the date of the agreement or release.
5. Enter any additional details about the dispute.
6. Sign and date the release (optional but recommended).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Field**: (`mutual_release_form_first_field`, text, optional) Enter a brief note if you need to make a note.
* **Second Party Name** (`mutual_release_form_second_party_name`, text, optional) Enter the name of the second party involved in the release.
* **Second Party Email** (`mutual_release_form_second_party_email`, text, optional) Enter the email address of the second party.
* **Dispute Description** (`mutual_release_form_dispute_description`, text, optional) Provide a brief description of the dispute or issue to be released.
* **Agreement Date** (`mutual_release_form_agreement_date`, date, optional) Enter the date of the agreement or release.
* **Dispute Details** (`mutual_release_form_dispute_details`, text, optional) Enter any additional details about the dispute.
* **Signature Date** (`mutual_release_form_signature_date`, date, optional) Enter the date of the signature (optional but recommended).
* **Signature** (`mutual_release_form_signature`, text, optional) Sign and date the release (optional but recommended).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure the information is valid and reliable.
* If you're unsure about any field, it's best to consult a legal expert or the relevant industry professional.
* Save the completed form as a PDF or print it out for safekeeping.
