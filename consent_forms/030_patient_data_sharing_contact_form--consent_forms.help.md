# Patient Data Sharing Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Patient Data Sharing Contact Form is used for patients to contact healthcare providers and share their personal data for care coordination, appointment scheduling, and other medical purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the contact information for you or your authorized representative. Please ensure that your contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
2. Provide your contact details, including your name, email, and phone number.
3. Select your consent status by choosing either 'Consent Given' or 'Consent Not Given' from the options provided.
4. Enter your date of birth.
5. Submit the form to share your data with healthcare providers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Data Sharing Form** (`patient_data_sharing_form`, text, required): This is the main contact form where you can share your medical information and preferences with healthcare providers.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required): Please enter your contact information such as your name, email, and phone number. This will help healthcare providers get in touch with you for appointments, lab results, and other medical matters.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required): Enter any additional details about how healthcare providers can contact you, such as your home address or work address.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether you give or do not give consent for healthcare providers to share your medical information with other parties. You can select either 'Consent Given' or 'Consent Not Given' from the options.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth. This will help healthcare providers to identify you correctly and access your medical records.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your contact information and consent status is accurate and up-to-date.
* Double-check your date of birth for correctness.
* If you are not comfortable sharing your medical information with healthcare providers, please contact your healthcare provider directly and express your concerns.
