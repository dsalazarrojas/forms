# Candidate Data Retention Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain a candidate's consent to retain their data for future job opportunities. It ensures that candidates understand how their data will be used and stored.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full legal name in the "Candidate Full Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field (optional).
4. Select the position you were a candidate for in the "Position Applied For" field.
5. Enter the approximate date of your last interaction with us in the "Date of Application" field.
6. In the "Data Retention Consent" field, select "I consent" to allow us to retain your data.
7. Choose how long you would like us to keep your data in the "Retention Period Preferred" field.
8. In the "Acknowledgment of Privacy Policy" field, select "I have read and agree" to acknowledge our full data protection terms.
9. If applicable, select "I consent" to allow us to perform third-party background checks.
10. Choose how you would like to be contacted for future opportunities in the "Consent to be Contacted" field.
11. Confirm your understanding of your right to withdraw consent in the "I understand my right to withdraw consent" field.
12. Finally, sign the "Digital Signature" field with your full name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Full Name**: This is your full legal name.
* **Email Address**: This is the email address we will use to communicate with you regarding your data.
* **Phone Number**: This is your phone number, if you wish to provide it.
* **Position Applied For**: This is the role you were recently a candidate for.
* **Date of Application**: This is the approximate date of your last interaction with us.
* **Data Retention Consent**: By selecting "I consent", you agree to allow us to retain your data for future job opportunities.
* **Retention Period Preferred**: This is how long you would like us to keep your data. You can choose from 6 months, 12 months, 24 months, or indefinitely until you withdraw your consent.
* **Acknowledgment of Privacy Policy**: By selecting "I have read and agree", you acknowledge our full data protection terms.
* **Consent for third-party background check**: If you select "I consent", we may perform a background check through a third-party service.
* **Consent to be Contacted**: Choose how you would like us to contact you for future opportunities. You can select to be contacted by email, phone, or both.
* **I understand my right to withdraw consent**: Confirm your understanding of your right to withdraw your consent at any time.
* **Digital Signature**: This is your full name for digital signature purposes.
