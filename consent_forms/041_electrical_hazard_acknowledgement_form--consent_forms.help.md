# Electrical Hazard Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Electrical Hazard Acknowledgement Form is used to record the presence and handling of electrical hazards in a work environment. It is a required form for all employees to acknowledge their understanding of electrical safety policies and guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, company, role, and location.
2. Enter the date you visited the area with electrical hazards.
3. Record the start time you began work.
4. Specify the duration of the work activity.
5. Sign the form as a supervisor and employee to confirm your presence and acknowledgement of electrical hazards.
6. Select "True" to confirm that you have read and understood the electrical safety policy and guidelines.
7. If you selected "False", provide a brief reason why in the "Reason why not" field.
8. Add any additional comments if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. Electrical Hazard Acknowledgement Form** (`title`, `text`, required): This is the title of the form that you are filling out.
* **2. Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or badge.
* **3. Company** (`company`, `text`, required): Type the name of your company or organization.
* **4. Role** (`role`, `text`, required): Enter your job title or position.
* **5. Location** (`location`, `text`, required): Specify the location where you worked with electrical hazards.
* **6. Date Visited** (`date_visited`, `date`, required): Enter the date you visited the area with electrical hazards.
* **7. Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, required): Record the start time of your work activity.
* **8. Duration** (`duration`, `text`, required): Specify the duration of your work activity.
* **9. Supervisor Signature** (`supervisor_signature`, `text`, required): Sign as a supervisor to confirm your presence and acknowledgement of electrical hazards.
* **10. Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, `text`, required): Sign as an employee to confirm your presence and acknowledgement of electrical hazards.
* **11. Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you finished filling out this form.
* **12. I acknowledge that I have read and understand the electrical safety policy and guidelines** (`electrical_hazard_acknowledgement`, `select_one`, required): Select "True" to confirm that you have read and understood the electrical safety policy and guidelines. If you select "False", fill in the reason why in the "Reason why not" field.
* **13. Reason why not** (`electrical_hazard_acknowledgement_reason`, `text`, optional): If you selected "False" in the previous field, provide a brief reason why you did not read and understand the electrical safety policy and guidelines.
* **14. Additional comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your work activity.

Remember to complete this form carefully and accurately to ensure your safety and the safety of those around you.
