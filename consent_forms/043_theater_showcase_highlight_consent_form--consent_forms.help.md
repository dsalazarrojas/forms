# theater_showcase_highlight_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The theater showcase highlight consent form is designed to obtain participant consent for various uses of their data in a theater showcase event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and school or group name.
2. Check your consent for recording, sharing, and editing your participant information.
3. Choose whether your data can be used for promotional, archival, and educational purposes.
4. If you have any additional contact information, please provide it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_name**:
	+ A field to input your name.
* **school_group_name**:
	+ A field to input your school or group name.
* **contact_information**:
	+ A field to input any additional contact information you have.
* **recording_consent**:
	+ Check if you consent to be recorded. If checked, your image, voice, and/or performance may be recorded.
* **sharing_consent**:
	+ Check if you consent to share your recorded data. If checked, your data may be shared with others.
* **editing_consent**:
	+ Check if you consent to have your data edited. If checked, your data may be modified or altered.
* **promotional_use**:
	+ Check if you consent to use your data for promotional purposes.
* **archival_use**:
	+ Check if you consent to store and maintain your data in an archive.
* **educational_use**:
	+ Check if you consent to use your data for educational purposes.
* **participant_information**:
	+ A field to input any additional information you have.
* **participant_information**:
	+ A field to input any additional information you have about the participant.
