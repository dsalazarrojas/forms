# Employee Internet Monitoring Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain consent from employees regarding internet monitoring, which is likely for monitoring employee activity on office and remote workspaces.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your details, including your preferences regarding internet monitoring.
2. Select the correct option for how you would like to be monitored (office, remote, hybrid, or no monitoring).
3. Confirm that you acknowledge the monitoring terms.
4. Fill out the date of consent.
5. Fill out your name, employee ID, department, email, and phone number.
6. Sign the form by filling out the signature date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Internet Monitoring** (`employee_internet_monitoring_form`, text, required/optional):
	+ A short answer to explain your preferences regarding internet monitoring.
* **Employee Internet Monitoring** (`employee_internet_monitoring_consent`, select_one, required/optional):
	+ Select one option for how you would like to be monitored:
		- Monitoring will be done for office usage
		- Monitoring will be done for remote usage
		- Monitoring will be done for hybrid usage
		- No monitoring will be done
* **I acknowledge that** (`acknowledgement`, text, required/optional):
	+ A short answer to confirm that you understand the monitoring terms.
* **Date** (`consent_date`, date, required/optional):
	+ The date of your consent.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required/optional):
	+ Your name.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required/optional):
	+ Your employee ID.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required/optional):
	+ Your department name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional):
	+ Your employee email.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional):
	+ Your phone number.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required/optional):
	+ The date of your signature.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
- This form is for one-time consent only, please do not submit it multiple times.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or HR department.
