# telemedicine_consent_form_new_york - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain informed consent from patients for telemedicine services. This form is designed to gather essential information about the patient's medical history, emergency contact details, and their consent for telemedicine services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question carefully and accurately.
2. If you're unsure about any question, please reach out to your healthcare provider for guidance.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`Patient Information`, `text`, required: false): 
	+ This field is for you to provide any relevant information about yourself that may affect your treatment or medical history.
* **Telemedicine Informed Consent** (`telemedicine_informed_consent`, `select_one`, required: true): 
	+ Select one of the options to indicate your interest in using telemedicine services.
	+ Option 1: I am interested in using telemedicine services
	+ Option 2: I am not interested in using telemedicine services
* **Telemedicine Agreement** (`telemedicine_agreement`, `note`, required: false): 
	+ Please note any specific agreements or requirements for your telemedicine services, if applicable.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false): 
	+ Describe any pre-existing medical conditions or allergies that may affect your treatment or medical history.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required: true): 
	+ Provide the name and contact information of a family member or friend who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`, `text`, required: true): 
	+ Enter the contact information for the emergency contact you provided above.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, `note`, required: false): 
	+ Please sign and date this form to confirm that you have read and understood the information provided.
* **Provider Signature** (`provider_signature`, `note`, required: false): 
	+ Your healthcare provider will sign this form to confirm that you have been informed and have provided your consent for telemedicine services.

## Tips
* Make sure to review and understand all fields before submitting the form.
* If you need help or have questions, please reach out to your healthcare provider.
* This form is for your safety and the success of your telemedicine treatment. Thank you for your cooperation.
