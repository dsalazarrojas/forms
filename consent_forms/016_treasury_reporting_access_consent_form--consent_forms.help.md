# Treasury Reporting Access Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Treasury Reporting Access Consent Form is a document that grants you access to confidential treasury reporting information. By submitting this form, you are providing consent to access this information and acknowledging that you have the necessary permissions and clearance to do so.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your consent to access treasury reporting information.
2.  Enter your name as it appears on your official ID or other identification documents.
3.  Select the role(s) that apply to your access request (Financial, Non-Financial, or Other).
4.  Enter your department as it appears on your official ID or other identification documents.
5.  Enter your email address for internal communication and contact.
6.  Enter your phone number for easy communication and verification.
7.  Provide any additional comments or notes about your access request, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. Treasury Reporting Access Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your willingness to access treasury reporting information.
* **2. Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your official ID or other identification documents.
* **3. Role** (`role`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose the role(s) that apply to your access request (Financial, Non-Financial, or Other).
* **4. Department** (`department`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your department as it appears on your official ID or other identification documents.
* **5. Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address for internal communication and contact.
* **6. Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number for easy communication and verification.
* **7. Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about your access request, if necessary.
