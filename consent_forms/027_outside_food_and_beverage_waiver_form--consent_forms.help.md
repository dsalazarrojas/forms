# Outside Food And Beverage Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from individuals for food and beverage consumption outside of the organization. It is intended to ensure that the individual is aware of and agrees to the terms and conditions of consuming food and beverage outside of the premises.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information as prompted. Please ensure that you have read and understood the form before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **event_details** (text, optional): A brief description of the event or occasion where the food and beverage will be consumed.
  - This field is for you to provide a brief description of the event or occasion where you plan to consume food and beverage.
- **acknowledgement** (text, optional): An acknowledgement that you have read and understood the form.
  - Please type 'yes' to confirm that you have read and understood this form.
- **waiver** (text, optional): A waiver of liability for the consumption of food and beverage.
  - Please type a short waiver of liability, indicating that you understand that consuming food and beverage outside of the premises is at your own risk.
- **waiver_acknowledgement** (text, optional): An acknowledgement of the waiver of liability.
  - Please type 'yes' to confirm that you have read and understood the waiver of liability.
- **signature_1** (select_one, optional): Your signature to indicate that you have read and understood this form.
  - Please select 'yes' or 'no' to indicate your response.
- **signature_2** (select_multiple, optional): A multiple-choice field to indicate that you have read and understood this form.
  - Please select 'yes' or 'no' to indicate your response. You can choose one or more of the options.
- **waiver_2** (text, optional): An additional waiver of liability.
  - Please type a short waiver of liability, indicating that you understand that consuming food and beverage outside of the premises is at your own risk.
- **waiver_acknowledgement_2** (text, optional): Another acknowledgement of the waiver of liability.
  - Please type 'yes' to confirm that you have read and understood the waiver of liability.
  Note: This field is not necessary to complete the form, but is an additional acknowledgement of the waiver of liability.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have read and understood the form before submitting it.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant personnel or manager.
- Please note that this form is for informational purposes only and is not a substitute for a formal written contract or agreement.
