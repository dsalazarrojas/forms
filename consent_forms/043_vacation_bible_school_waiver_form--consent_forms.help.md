# Vacation Bible School Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information from parents or guardians of students participating in Vacation Bible School activities. This information will help us ensure the child's safety and well-being during the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Read through each section carefully and fill out the required fields.
2. Answer each question truthfully and accurately.
3. If you are unsure about any section, please reach out to the VBS administration for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Information** (`parent_information`, text, optional): This section is where you will provide your name and contact information.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Please provide the name and contact information of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_multiple, optional): Specify the relationship between you and the emergency contact person (e.g., mother, father, guardian).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, select_multiple, optional): Check if you want to provide a phone number for the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, select_multiple, optional): Check if you want to provide an email address for the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone 2** (`emergency_contact_phone_2`, select_multiple, optional): Provide an additional phone number for the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship 2** (`emergency_contact_relationship_2`, select_multiple, optional): Specify the relationship between you and the second emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone 2** (`emergency_contact_phone_2`, select_multiple, optional): Check if you want to provide a phone number for the second emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email 2** (`emergency_contact_email_2`, select_multiple, optional): Check if you want to provide an email address for the second emergency contact person.
* **Student Information** (`student_information`, text, optional): Please provide your child's name.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your child's name.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your child's date of birth.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Provide your home address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Student Attendance** (`student_attendance`, select_multiple, optional): Specify if your child will be attending the VBS program.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Provide the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship 3** (`emergency_contact_relationship_3`, select_multiple, optional): Specify the relationship between you and the third emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone 3** (`emergency_contact_phone_3`, select_multiple, optional): Check if you want to provide a phone number for the third emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email 3** (`emergency_contact_email_3`, select_multiple, optional): Check if you want to provide an email address for the third emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone 4** (`emergency_contact_phone_4`, select_multiple, optional): Provide an additional phone number for the third emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship 4** (`emergency_contact_relationship_4`, select_multiple, optional): Specify the relationship between you and the fourth emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email 4** (`emergency_contact_email_4`, select_multiple, optional): Check if you want to provide an email address for the fourth emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone 5** (`emergency_contact_phone_5`, select_multiple, optional): Check if you want to provide a phone number for the fifth emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship 5** (`emergency_contact_relationship_5`, select_multiple, optional): Specify the relationship between you and the fifth emergency contact person.
