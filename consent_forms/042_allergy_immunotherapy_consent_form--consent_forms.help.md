# Allergy Immunotherapy Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a critical document that ensures patients understand the risks and benefits of allergy immunotherapy, a medical treatment that aims to reduce allergy symptoms. By signing this form, the patient acknowledges their consent to undergo the treatment, understanding its potential risks and benefits, and agreeing to follow the treatment schedule and reporting any health changes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the required information for each field. You will need to fill out the form with your personal details, including:
- Your first name
- Last name
- Date of birth
- Medical record number (Patient ID)
- The date you give consent to begin the treatment
- The name of your treating physician
- A description of the treatment procedure
- Understanding of the treatment's potential risks and benefits
- Agreement to stay for observation after injections
- Commitment to report any health changes
- Acknowledgement of potential systemic reactions
- Acknowledgement of local reactions
- Acknowledgement of beta-blockers and their potential risks
- Agreement to report pregnancy or any changes in your health status
- Commitment to regular appointments
- Understanding of the treatment schedule and activities to be avoided
- Understanding of emergency procedures
- Confirmation that alternative treatments have been discussed
- Confirmation that all questions have been answered
- Voluntary consent to begin the treatment
- Understanding that you can withdraw from the treatment anytime
- Signature of the patient and physician confirming the consent and authorization

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`patient_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your medical records.
* **Last Name** (`patient_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your medical records.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Patient ID** (`patient_id`, text, required): Enter your medical record number.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, required): The date on which you give your consent to begin the treatment.
* **Treating Physician** (`physician_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your treating physician.
* **Procedure Description Acknowledgment** (`procedure_description`, select_one, required): Choose the option "Yes, I understand allergy immunotherapy" to acknowledge that you understand the procedure's description.
* **Treatment Understanding** (`treatment_understanding`, select_one, required): Choose the option that best represents your understanding of the treatment, which can be: "I understand it involves gradual exposure to allergens," "I understand it helps build tolerance," "I understand it requires regular visits," or "I need more information."
* **Treatment Duration Understanding** (`treatment_duration`, select_one, required): Choose the option that best represents your understanding of the treatment duration, which can be: "Yes, I understand it takes 3-5 years," "Yes, but I need more details," or "No, I was not aware."
* **Benefits Understood** (`benefits_understood`, select_multiple, required): Select all the potential benefits of the treatment that you understand, which can be:
	+ Reduced allergy symptoms
	+ Decreased medication need
	+ Long-term relief
	+ Prevention of new allergies
	+ Improved quality of life
	+ May prevent asthma development
* **Risks Understood** (`risks_understood`, select_multiple, required): Select all the potential risks of the treatment that you understand, which can be:
	+ Local reactions at injection site
	+ Systemic allergic reactions
	+ Anaphylaxis (rare)
	+ Worsening of asthma
	+ Fatigue after injections
	+ Headache
* **Local Reactions Acknowledgment** (`local_reactions`, select_one, required): Choose the option "Yes, I understand redness and swelling may occur" to acknowledge that you understand local reactions.
* **Systemic Reactions Acknowledgment** (`systemic_reactions`, select_one, required): Choose the option "Yes, I understand rare systemic reactions may occur" to acknowledge that you understand systemic reactions.
* **Observation Period Agreement** (`observation_period`, select_one, required): Choose the option "Yes, I will remain 30 minutes after each injection" to agree to stay for observation after injections.
* **Health Status Reporting Agreement** (`health_status_reporting`, select_one, required): Choose the option "Yes, I will report any illness or symptoms" to agree to report any health changes.
* **Medication Disclosure Agreement** (`medication_disclosure`, select_one, required): Choose the option "Yes, I will disclose all medications" to agree to disclose all medications.
* **Beta-Blocker Acknowledgment** (`beta_blocker_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Choose the option "Yes, I understand beta-blockers may interfere" to acknowledge that you understand beta-blocker risks.
* **Pregnancy Acknowledgment** (`pregnancy_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Choose the option "Yes, I will inform immediately" to agree to inform your healthcare provider if you are pregnant or planning a pregnancy.
* **Appointment Compliance** (`appointment_compliance`, select_one, required): Choose the option "Yes, I can commit to regular appointments" to agree to commit to regular appointments.
* **Injection Schedule Understanding** (`injection_schedule`, select_one, required): Choose the option "Yes, I understand build-up and maintenance phases" to understand the treatment schedule.
* **Activity Restrictions Understanding** (`activity_restrictions`, select_one, required): Choose the option "Yes, I will avoid strenuous activity after injections" to understand activity restrictions.
* **Emergency Plan Understanding** (`emergency_plan`, select_one, required): Choose the option "Yes, I understand emergency procedures" to understand emergency procedures.
* **Alternative Treatments Discussed** (`alternative_treatments`, select_one, required): Choose the option that best represents the alternative treatments discussed, which can be: "Yes, medications discussed," "Yes, avoidance strategies discussed," or "Yes, both discussed."
* **Questions Answered** (`questions_answered`, select_one, required): Choose the option "Yes, all questions answered" to confirm that all questions have been answered.
* **Voluntary Consent** (`voluntary_consent`, select_one, required): Choose the option "Yes, I consent voluntarily" to indicate that your consent is voluntary.
* **Right to Withdraw Understanding** (`right_to_withdraw`, select_one, required): Choose the option "Yes, I understand I can withdraw anytime" to understand that you can withdraw from the treatment anytime.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, select_one, required): Choose the option "I consent to allergy immunotherapy" to confirm your consent to begin the treatment.
* **Patient Signature Date** (`patient_signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date on which you sign the consent form.
* **Witness Name** (`witness_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the witness who signs the form to confirm your consent.
* **Physician Signature** (`physician_signature`, select_one, required): Choose the option "I have explained the procedure and risks" to confirm that you have been fully explained the procedure and risks.
* **Physician Signature Date** (`physician_signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date on which the physician signs the consent form.

Please review each field carefully and ensure you understand the information before signing the form. If you have any doubts or questions, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
