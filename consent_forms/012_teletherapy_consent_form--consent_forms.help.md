# Teletherapy Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a crucial document for teletherapy sessions or consultations. It gathers essential information from patients, including general details, medical history, allergies, and emergency contacts. The purpose of this form is to ensure a smooth and effective teletherapy experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the General Information section with your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2. Provide emergency contact information, including names, relationships, and contact numbers.
3. Answer the question regarding teletherapy services with a brief description of the services you are interested in or have been recommended for.
4. Share your medical history, including any allergies or medical conditions that may affect your teletherapy experience.
5. Sign the E-Signature fields to confirm your consent to share information and agree to the terms of the teletherapy session.
6. Provide additional medical history and contact details as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information**: This section is for providing your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Emergency Contact**: Please fill in this section with the names, relationships, and contact numbers of individuals who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Teletherapy Services**: Briefly describe the teletherapy services you are interested in or have been recommended for.
* **Consent to Share**: Please sign this field to confirm your consent to share information with the teletherapy provider.
* **E-Signature**: Sign this field to agree to the terms of the teletherapy session.
* **Medical History**: Share your medical history, including any allergies or medical conditions that may affect your teletherapy experience.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies or medical conditions that may affect your teletherapy experience.
* **Contact Details**: Provide any additional contact details as needed.
* **Patient Agreement**: Please review and agree to the terms of the teletherapy session.
* **Emergency Contacts**: Please list any emergency contacts as needed.
* **Contact Relationship**: Describe the relationship between you and the emergency contacts.
* **Medical History 1-6**: Provide additional medical history as needed.
* **E-Signature 1-4**: Sign these fields to confirm your consent to share information and agree to the terms of the teletherapy session.
