# Hair Extension Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect client information, agree to payment terms, and obtain signatures for services or agreements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's name and information on page 1.
2. Select a date and time for the service or appointment on page 2.
3. Provide details about the service or agreement on page 3.
4. Read and agree to the payment terms on page 4.
5. Sign and date the agreement on page 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Please fill in the client's name.
* **Client Information** (`client_info`, text, optional): This is a text field for any additional information about the client.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select a date for the service or appointment.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select a time for the service or appointment.
* **Service Details** (`service_details`, text, optional): Describe the service or agreement details.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, optional): Read and agree to the payment terms.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, optional): This is a signature field for the provider.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, note, optional): This is a signature field for the client.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Read and understand the payment terms before signing.
- Sign and date both the provider and client signature fields.
- Keep a copy of the completed form for your records.
