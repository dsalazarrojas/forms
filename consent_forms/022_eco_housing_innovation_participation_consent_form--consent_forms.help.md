# eco_housing_innovation_participation_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect demographic and contact information from participants in the eco-housing innovation project, as well as their expertise and consent related to the project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant details, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Describe your expertise related to the project.
3. Provide your consent for participation in the project.
4. Confirm the project's consent for participation.
5. Enter your email and phone number for project communication.
6. Confirm your email and phone number for project communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, optional): Please fill in your name, title, and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Expertise** (`expertise`, text, optional): Describe your relevant expertise or experience related to the project.
* **Project Participant Consent** (`project_participant_consent`, text, optional): Please indicate your consent to participate in the project.
* **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`, text, optional): Please indicate your consent to participate in the project.
* **Project Consent** (`project_consent`, text, optional): Please indicate the project's consent to participate in the participant.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for project communication.
* **Project Email** (`project_email`, email, optional): Enter the project's email address for communication.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for project communication.
* **Project Phone** (`project_phone`, text, optional): Enter the project's phone number for communication.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the project lead or administrator.
* Please note that your participation in the project is subject to the terms and conditions of the project.
