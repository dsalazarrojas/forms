# Youth Resilience Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Resilience Program Application Form is designed to collect information from individuals who wish to participate in the Youth Resilience Program. The form aims to gather general information, contact details, and medical history to ensure that participants are eligible and prepared for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with accurate and truthful information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed.
3. Review each section carefully to ensure that you have provided the correct information.
4. Once you have filled out the form, click the "Submit" button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_info`, `text`, required: false): This section asks for general information about yourself, such as your name, date of birth, and any other details you feel necessary to share.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide your contact information, including your phone number and address, so that we can reach out to you in case of any further queries.
* **Parent Information** (`parent_info`, `text`, required: false): If you are a minor, please provide information about your parent or guardian, including their name, phone number, and address.
* **Student Information** (`student_info`, `text`, required: false): If you are applying as a student, please provide information about your school name, student program, and employment status.
* **Education** (`education`, `text`, required: false): Describe your educational background, including any relevant qualifications or training you have completed.
* **Employment** (`employment`, `text`, required: false): Provide information about your employment status, including your job title, employer, and employment duration.
* **Personal Details** (`personal_details`, `text`, required: false): Share any personal details that you think might be relevant to your application, such as hobbies or interests.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `select_multiple`, required: false): List any medical conditions you have, such as asthma or diabetes.
* **Medications** (`medications`, `text`, required: false): If you take any medications, please list them here.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `note`, required: false): Provide any relevant medical history, including past illnesses or injuries.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required: false): Provide information about an emergency contact person, including their name, phone number, and address.
* **Emergency Contact 2** (`emergency_contact2`, `text`, required: false): Provide information about a second emergency contact person.
* **Medical Info** (`medical_info`, `date`, required: false): Please provide your medical information, including any relevant dates and times.
* **Medical Info 2** (`medical_info2`, `date`, required: false): Provide additional medical information, including any relevant dates and times.
* **Personal Info** (`personal_info`, `time`, required: false): Share any personal information, including your availability for the program.
* **Medical Info 3** (`medical_info3`, `time`, required: false): Please provide any further medical information, including any relevant times and dates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Provide your phone number so that we can contact you.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Please provide your email address so that we can contact you.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Share your address so that we can mail you any relevant information.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `text`, required: false): Describe your employment status, including your job title and employer.
* **Student Status** (`student_status`, `text`, required: false): Provide information about your student status, including your student program.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, required: false): If you are a student, please provide your school name.
* **Student Program** (`student_program`, `text`, required: false): Provide information about your student program.
* **Program Details** (`program_details`, `text`, required: false): Describe any relevant details about the program you are applying for.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required: false): Once you have filled out the form, click the "Submit" button to complete your application.

Note: The form is not required to be completed in one sitting. You can save your progress and return to it later.
