# Nursing Home Admission Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nursing Home Admission Authorization Form is used to gather essential information about a patient's identity, medical history, and other relevant details for their admission to a nursing home.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's personal details, including their name and contact information.
2. Provide information about the patient's relationship with the emergency contact.
3. Enter the patient's address and phone number.
4. List the patient's emergency contact's name, relationship, address, and phone number.
5. Confirm the patient's medical history, including any medical conditions, allergies, and medication lists.
6. Confirm the patient's medication dosage and medical history.
7. Enter any additional medical conditions that may be relevant to the patient's care.
8. Select the nursing home where the patient will be admitted.
9. Enter the date and time of admission.
10. Sign the form to confirm that the information provided is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_name** (Text): Enter the patient's full name.
* **relationship** (Text): Describe the nature of your relationship with the patient (e.g., parent, guardian, spouse).
* **patient_address** (Text): Enter the patient's address.
* **patient_phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **emergency_contact_name** (Text): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **emergency_contact_relationship** (Text, Optional): Describe the nature of your relationship with the emergency contact (e.g., parent, sibling).
* **emergency_contact_address** (Text): Enter the emergency contact's address.
* **emergency_contact_phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **healthcare_provider** (Text): Enter the name of the patient's healthcare provider.
* **patient_medical_history** (Text): Provide a detailed description of the patient's medical history.
* **medication_list** (Select Multiple): Indicate whether the patient has a list of medications or not.
* **medication_dosage** (Select Multiple): Indicate whether the patient takes medications as directed or not.
* **medical_history** (Text): Provide any additional medical history that may be relevant.
* **allergies** (Text): List any allergies the patient may have.
* **medical_conditions** (Text): List any medical conditions the patient may have.
* **medical_conditions_2** (Text): List any additional medical conditions the patient may have.
* **medical_conditions_3** (Text): List any further medical conditions the patient may have.
* **nursing_home_name** (Text): Enter the name of the nursing home.
* **date_of_admission** (Date): Enter the date of admission.
* **time_of_admission** (Time): Enter the time of admission.
* **date_of_discharge** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of discharge.
* **time_of_discharge** (Time, Optional): Enter the time of discharge.
* **signature** (Note): Sign the form to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **medical_directive** (Text): Enter any special medical directives or instructions for the patient's care.
* **date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of completion of the form.

Note: Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and truthfully to ensure the patient's safety and well-being during their stay in the nursing home.
