# Virtual School Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual School Consent Form is a document that requires parental and student consent for participation in virtual school programs. It gathers essential information from parents and students, including their personal details, consent status, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you have read and understand the instructions and questions carefully.
3. Select "I consent" in the Consent field for parental or student consent.
4. Write your signature in the designated fields if required.
5. Review the contact information carefully and ensure it is up-to-date.
6. Submit the form to the virtual school administration or staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Information** (`parent_info`, `text`, required: false): This is the section where you can provide your personal details, such as your name, address, and other relevant information.
* **Student Information** (`student_info`, `text`, required: false): This section is for students to provide their personal details, such as their name and date of birth.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, required: true): Please select "I consent" if you give your consent for your child to participate in the virtual school program.
* **Contact Us** (`contact`, `note`, required: false): This is a field for providing contact information, which will help the virtual school administration or staff to get in touch with you.
* **Parent Signature** (`signature_1`, `text`, required: false): This field is for the parent's signature, which serves as an acknowledgment of the consent.
* **Virtual School Signature** (`signature_2`, `text`, required: false): This field is for the virtual school's staff or administration to sign and acknowledge the consent.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have read and understood the instructions and questions before filling out the form.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please contact the virtual school administration or staff.
* The form is designed to be completed by parents and students, so please be honest and accurate when filling it out.
* If you need help or have issues with the form, please reach out to the virtual school support team.
