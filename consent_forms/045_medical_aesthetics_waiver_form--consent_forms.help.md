2. **Share Medical History**: In the "Medical History" section, briefly describe any existing medical conditions, allergies, or previous surgeries that may impact their treatment.
3. **Read and Sign Informed Consent**: In the "Informed Consent" section, carefully read the document and sign it to indicate that you understand the risks and benefits associated with the treatment or service.
4. **Discuss Risk Information**: In the "Risk Information" section, acknowledge any potential risks or complications related to the treatment or service.
5. **Waive Medical Aesthetics Treatments**: In the "Medical Aesthetics Waiver" section, provide consent for the treatment or service and acknowledge any risks involved.
