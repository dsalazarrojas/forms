# Wax Temperature Tolerance Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Wax Temperature Tolerance Consent Form is used to record a client's consent and other relevant information for wax treatment procedures.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the client's consent by choosing "Yes" or "No" from the "Client Consent" dropdown menu.
2. Enter the client's wax temperature tolerance in the "Wax Temperature" field.
3. Indicate if the treatment area is affected by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the "Treatment Area" dropdown menu.
4. Record the date the form was completed in the "Date Completed" field.
5. Enter the name of the assigned user who completed the form in the "Assigned User" field.
6. If necessary, add any notes in the "Notes" field.
7. If the form is being submitted by someone other than the client, enter their name in the "Submitted By" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Consent
• **Client Consent** (Select One, required): This field asks the client to provide their consent to proceed with wax treatment procedures. Select "Yes" if the client has given their consent, "No" otherwise.
### Wax Temperature
• **Wax Temperature** (Number, required): Enter the client's wax temperature tolerance here.
### Treatment Area
• **Treatment Area** (Select One, required): Indicate if the treatment area is affected by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu.
### Consent Given
• **Consent Given** (Date, required): Record the date the client provided their consent. The date should be in the format MM/DD/YY.
### Signature
• **Signature** (Text, required): This field is intended for the client to sign their name, but its purpose is unclear. Please use with caution and follow your institution's guidelines.
### Client Signature
• **Client Signature** (Text, required): The client is expected to sign their name here.
### Date Completed
• **Date Completed** (Date, required): Record the date the form was completed.
### Assigned User
• **Assigned User** (Text, required): Enter the name of the user who completed the form.
### Notes
• **Notes** (Note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the client's treatment here.
### Submitted By
• **Submitted By** (Text, optional): If the form is being submitted by someone other than the client, enter their name here.
