# Loan Collateral Disclosure Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain the borrower's consent for disclosing information regarding the loan collateral. It's essential to complete this form accurately to maintain a smooth loan process and avoid any potential issues that may arise from unapproved disclosure.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Read the form carefully and understand what it's asking for.
2.  Select the correct answer for each field according to your understanding and knowledge.
3.  Ensure that you have read and understood the disclosures provided before submitting the form.
4.  Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
5.  Submit the form once you have reviewed and agreed to the disclosures.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **loan_collateral_disclosure_consent_form (Select Multiple):** Choose whether you have read and understand the loan collateral disclosure. Select "Yes" if you have read and understood, and "No" if you haven't.
*   **loan_collateral_disclosure_consent_form_1 (Select One):** Select "Yes" if you understand that the lender may disclose your loan collateral information to third parties, or "No" if you don't understand this information.
*   **loan_collateral_disclosure_consent_form_2 (Note):** Please note that by submitting this form, you are acknowledging that you have read and understood the above disclosures.
*   **loan_collateral_disclosure_consent_form_3 (Select Multiple):** Choose whether you consent to the lender disclosing your loan collateral information to others. Select "Yes" if you consent, and "No" if you don't.
*   **loan_collateral_disclosure_consent_form_4 (Select One):** Select "Yes" if you understand that you may be contacted by third parties regarding your loan collateral, or "No" if you don't understand this information.
*   **loan_collateral_disclosure_consent_form_5 (Select Multiple):** Choose whether you understand and consent to the disclosure of your loan collateral information by the lender. Select "Yes" if you understand and consent, and "No" if you don't.
*   **Note 1** (Note): This is a read-only field to acknowledge that you have read and understand the above disclosures.
