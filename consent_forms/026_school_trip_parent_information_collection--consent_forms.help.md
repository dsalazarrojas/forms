# School Trip Parent Information Collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Trip Parent Information Collection form is designed to collect necessary information from parents or guardians regarding their child's health, emergency contacts, and other relevant details related to school trips. This information is crucial for the school to ensure the child's safety and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
2. Enter the parent's name, as well as any other emergency contact's information, such as relationship and phone number.
3. Specify medical conditions, if applicable, and provide details of any other emergency contacts.
4. Confirm consent for medical treatment, including hospitalization, surgery, and other medical procedures.
5. Sign the form to indicate that you have reviewed and agreed to the provided information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Parent Name**
* Parent Name: Enter your child's name.

### **Parent Contact Relationship**
* Parent Contact Relationship: Select the relationship between the parent and child (e.g., Mother, Father, Guardian).

### **Phone Number**
* Phone Number: Enter the parent's or emergency contact's phone number.

### **Email Address**
* Email Address: Enter the parent's or emergency contact's email address.

### **Medical Conditions**
* Medical Conditions: If your child has any medical conditions, provide details in this field.

### **Emergency Contact Person**
* Emergency Contact Person: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.

### **Emergency Contact Relationship**
* Emergency Contact Relationship: Select the relationship between the emergency contact person and child (e.g., Mother, Father, Guardian, Friend).

### **Emergency Contact Phone Number**
* Emergency Contact Phone Number: Enter the emergency contact's phone number.

### **Emergency Contact Email Address**
* Emergency Contact Email Address: Enter the emergency contact's email address.

### **Other Emergency Contact**
* Other Emergency Contacts: If applicable, enter any other emergency contacts.

### **Consent for Medical Treatment**
* Consent for Medical Treatment: Confirm whether you consent for your child to receive medical treatment.

### **Consent for Medical Treatment and Hospitalization**
* Consent for Medical Treatment and Hospitalization: Confirm whether you consent for your child to receive medical treatment and be hospitalized.

### **Consent for Medical Treatment and Surgery**
* Consent for Medical Treatment and Surgery: Confirm whether you consent for your child to receive medical treatment and undergo surgery.

### **Consent for Medical Treatment and Anesthesia**
* Consent for Medical Treatment and Anesthesia: Confirm whether you consent for your child to receive medical treatment and anesthesia.

### **Consent for Medical Treatment Other**
* Consent for Medical Treatment Other: If you have any other medical treatment in mind, specify here.

### **Consent for Medical Treatment Details**
* Medical Treatment Details: Provide any additional details about medical treatment.

### **Signature**
* Signature: Sign the form to indicate that you have reviewed and agreed to the provided information.

### **School Trip Name**
* School Trip Name: Enter the name of the school trip.

### **School Trip Date**
* School Trip Date: Enter the date of the school trip.

### **School Trip Location**
* School Trip Location: Enter the location of the school trip.

### **School Trip Details**
* School Trip Details: Provide any additional details about the school trip.

### **School Trip Teacher**
* School Trip Teacher: Enter the name of the school trip teacher.

### **School Trip Coordinator**
* School Trip Coordinator: Enter the name of the school trip coordinator.

### **Other Emergency Contacts**
* Other Emergency Contacts: If applicable, enter any other emergency contacts.

### **Other Emergency Contacts Phone Numbers**
* Other Emergency Contacts Phone Numbers: Enter phone numbers for other emergency contacts.

Note: The form fields are optional unless marked as required. Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, consult with the school administration or the form's administrators.
