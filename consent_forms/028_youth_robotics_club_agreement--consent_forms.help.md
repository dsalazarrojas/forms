# Youth Robotics Club Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to gather parental consent and basic participant details for the Youth Robotics Club.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select one of the options on the `Parental Consent` page to indicate your agreement level with the terms and conditions.
2. On the `Participant Details` page, enter your name and any other relevant information as requested.
3. On the `Contact Info` page, enter your contact information (e.g., email, phone number, or address) if you have any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parental Consent** (Select One):
	+ Choose one of the three options: "I have read and agree to the terms and conditions," "I have read but do not agree to the terms and conditions," or "I have not read the terms and conditions."
* **Participant Details** (Text Field):
	+ Enter the participant's name and any other relevant information as requested.
* **Contact Info** (Text Field):
	+ Enter your contact information (e.g., email, phone number, or address) so we can keep in touch with you.

## Tips
- Be honest about your agreement level with the terms and conditions.
- Make sure to enter accurate contact information to ensure we can contact you effectively.
- If you have any questions or concerns about this form, feel free to ask the Youth Robotics Club staff.
