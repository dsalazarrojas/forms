# Microblading Consent & Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to obtain the customer's consent for microblading services and schedule an appointment. It ensures that the customer is aware of the procedures and has provided their consent before proceeding with the appointment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with the following information:
	* Enter your personal details in the **Customer Details** field.
	* Select your consent for microblading services in the **Microblading Consents** field. Please note that this is not a required field, but it's still important to provide your consent for us to proceed.
	* Choose an appointment time that works best for you in the **Appointment Time** field.
2. If you have any additional comments or notes, you can include them in the **Signature** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details**: This is where you will enter your personal information, such as your name, contact number, and email address.
* **Microblading Consents**: This field allows you to indicate your consent for microblading services. Please select **True** if you are consenting, and **False** otherwise.
* **Appointment Time**: Please select a specific time for your appointment that works best for you. You can choose a time that is not currently taken by selecting a different time slot.
* **Signature**: This is an optional field for any additional comments or notes you may want to include.
