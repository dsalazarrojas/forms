## Field-by-Field Explanation

### How To Complete This Form

This form is designed to be completed by the patient or their representative. Please ensure that you have all necessary information and support when filling out the form. If you need help or clarification on any field, do not hesitate to reach out to the medical team.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Full Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as per your identification documents.
* **Patient Date of Birth** (`patient_dob`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Medical Record Number (MRN)** (`medical_record_number`, `text`, required): Please enter your MRN as per your medical records.
* **Partner Full Name (if applicable)** (`partner_name`, `text`, optional): If you have a partner involved in the procedure, please enter their full name.
* **Agreed Number of Embryos to Transfer** (`number_of_embryos`, `select_one`, required): Select the number of embryos you have agreed to transfer (Single Embryo Transfer, 2, or Other as per clinical recommendation).
* **Embryo Source** (`embryo_source`, `select_one`, required): Choose the source of the embryos (Fresh Embryos, Frozen Embryos (FET), or Donor Embryos).
* **I acknowledge and understand the risks of multiple pregnancy** (`risk_acknowledgment`, `select_one`, required): Please confirm that you understand the risks of multiple pregnancy.
* **I understand the risks associated with the transfer procedure** (`procedure_risk_understanding`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you understand the risks associated with the transfer procedure.
* **Plan for non-transferred viable embryos** (`excess_embryo_plan`, `select_one`, required): Choose a plan for any non-transferred embryos (Cryopreservation (Freezing), Donation for research, Donation to another couple, or Disposal/Discard).
* **I hereby consent to the embryo transfer procedure** (`final_consent_to_transfer`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you consent to the embryo transfer procedure.
* **Patient Electronic Signature** (`patient_signature`, `text`, required): Please sign your electronic signature as a confirmation of your consent.
* **Date of Signature** (`consent_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your signature in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Physician/Witness Name** (`physician_witness_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the physician or witness who is confirming your consent.
