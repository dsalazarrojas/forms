# Veterinary Drop Off Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Drop Off Consent Form is designed to be completed by pet owners when dropping off a pet at our veterinary facility. This form serves as a way to gather necessary information from pet owners to ensure a smooth and efficient drop-off process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read and understand the form's purpose and instructions before proceeding.
2.  Fill in the required information, marked as "required" in the form.
3.  For fields marked as "optional," you may provide additional information if you feel it's necessary.
4.  Once you've completed the form, review it carefully for any mistakes or missing information before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Owner Info**: This field is used to gather information about the pet owner, such as name and contact details. Please provide your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Consent**: This field is used to confirm your consent to our veterinary facility's drop-off and treatment policies. Please indicate your agreement by selecting 'True' if you consent, or 'False' if you do not.
* **Pet Info**: This field is used to gather information about the pet being dropped off, such as breed, age, and any health conditions. Please provide as much detail as possible to ensure our staff can provide the best care for your pet.
