# renewable_energy_participation_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The renewable energy participation consent form is designed to collect participant information and consent for a renewable energy program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your participant details in the "Participant Details" section.
2. Select your consent status in the "Consent" section.
3. Sign and date the "Signature" section.
4. Provide any additional contact details in the "Contact Details" section (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`<Participant Details>`, `text`, required: false): This section is used to collect basic participant information. Please enter your details in the format specified.
* **Consent** (`<Consent>`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Here, you can select your consent status for the renewable energy program. Please choose either "I agree to participate in the clean energy program" or "I do not agree to participate in the clean energy program".
* **Signature** (`<Signature>`, `note`, required: true): Sign your name in the space provided to indicate your consent has been given.
* **Program Details** (`<Program Details>`, `text`, required: false): This section is optional and can be used to provide any additional details about the program.
* **Contact Details** (`<Contact Details>`, `text`, required: false): This section is also optional and can be used to provide any contact information, such as your email or phone number.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have read and understood the purpose of this form before completing it.
* Please provide accurate and truthful information in the "Consent" section.
* Sign and date your "Consent" section to confirm your participation.
