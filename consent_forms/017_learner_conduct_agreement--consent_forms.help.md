# Learner Conduct Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain an agreement from learners to uphold a code of conduct that promotes a safe and respectful learning environment. Learners must acknowledge their understanding of the conduct guidelines, agree to follow them, and confirm they will be monitored while participating in the program. This ensures a positive and productive learning experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Read the Learner Conduct Guidelines**: Understand the definition and expectations outlined in the guidelines.
2. Ensure you have read and understood the guidelines (acknowledge by selecting "True" in the `acknowledgement_1` field).
3. Confirm you are committed to following the guidelines (select "True" in the `committed_to_guidelines_1` field).
4. Identify the consequences of failing to follow the guidelines (enter the date in the `consequences_1` field).
5. Confirm you understand your participation will be monitored (select "True" in the `monitored_participation_1` field).
6. If you have any questions about the guidelines, provide them in the `questions_guidelines_1` field.
7. Confirm you understand your conduct will be reviewed and evaluated periodically (select "True" in the `reviewed_conduct_1` field).
8. Identify the consequences of not meeting expectations (select all that apply in the `meeting_expectations_1` field).
9. Confirm your commitment to promoting a safe and respectful learning environment (select "True" in the `positive_role_model_1` field).
10. Confirm your commitment to treating others with respect and kindness (select "True" in the `treat_others_with_respect_1` field).
11. Confirm you will not engage in bullying or harassing behavior (select "True" in the `no_bullying_1` field).
12. Confirm you understand the importance of being a positive role model (select "True" in the `positive_role_model_2` field).
13. **Provide Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement to the terms outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the definition of Learner Conduct?** (`learner_conduct_agreement_1`, text, required): This is the definition of Learner Conduct, which includes rules and guidelines for promoting a safe and respectful learning environment.
* **Have you read and understood the Learner Conduct Guidelines?** (`acknowledgement_1`, checkbox, required): Confirm you have read and understood the guidelines.
* **Are you committed to following the Learner Conduct Guidelines?** (`committed_to_guidelines_1`, select_one, required): Confirm your commitment to following the guidelines.
* **What are the consequences of failing to follow the Learner Conduct Guidelines?** (`consequences_1`, date, required): Enter the date of the consequences.
* **Do you understand that you will be monitored while participating in the program?** (`monitored_participation_1`, select_one, required): Confirm you understand monitoring.
* **Do you have any questions about the Learner Conduct Guidelines?** (`questions_guidelines_1`, text, optional): If you have any questions, provide them here.
* **Do you understand that your conduct will be reviewed and evaluated periodically?** (`reviewed_conduct_1`, select_one, required): Confirm you understand reviews.
* **Are you aware of the consequences of not meeting expectations?** (`meeting_expectations_1`, select_multiple, required): Identify all consequences of not meeting expectations.
* **Are you committed to promoting a safe and respectful learning environment?** (`positive_role_model_1`, select_multiple, required): Confirm your commitment to promoting a positive environment.
* **Are you committed to treating others with respect and kindness?** (`treat_others_with_respect_1`, select_multiple, required): Confirm your commitment to treating others with respect.
* **Do you understand that bullying or harassing behavior will not be tolerated?** (`no_bullying_1`, select_multiple, required): Confirm you understand bullying and harassment policies.
* **Do you understand the importance of being a positive role model?** (`positive_role_model_2`, select_multiple, required): Confirm you understand the importance of being a positive role model.
* **Signature** (`signature_1`, text, required): Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement to the terms outlined above.
