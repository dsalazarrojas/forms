# Elf On The Shelf Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Elf on the Shelf registration form is used to collect information from participants interested in the activity. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  In the "Holiday Signups" field, select "Yes" if you are interested in participating in the "Elf on the Shelf" activity, or "No" if you are not interested.
2.  If you selected "Yes" in the "Holiday Signups" field, fill out the "Participant Information" field with your relevant details.
3.  If you have an Elf, fill out the "Elf Details" field with information about your Elf.
4.  If you have a schedule, fill out the "Elf Schedule" field with information about when you will be available to participate in the "Elf on the Shelf" activity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Holiday Signups** (`Holiday Signups`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ This field asks if you want to participate in the "Elf on the Shelf" activity. Select "Yes" if you are interested, and "No" if you are not.
* **Participant Information** (`participant_details`, text, required: false):
	+ If you are interested in participating in the "Elf on the Shelf" activity, you will be asked to provide your details in this field. Please fill out this field with relevant information about yourself.
* **Elf Details** (`elf_details`, text, required: false):
	+ If you have an Elf, please provide its details in this field. This could include its name, age, and any other relevant information about your Elf.
* **Elf Schedule** (`elf_schedule`, text, required: false):
	+ If you have a schedule, please provide it in this field. This will help us know when you will be available to participate in the "Elf on the Shelf" activity.
