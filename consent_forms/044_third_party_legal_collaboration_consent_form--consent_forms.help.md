# Third Party Legal Collaboration Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Third Party Legal Collaboration Consent Form is a document that helps establish clear boundaries and expectations for information sharing between your organization and third parties. It ensures that all parties involved are aware of the purpose and scope of the collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Legal Collaboration Consent**: Select one of the options to indicate whether you are the client, attorney, or other.
2. **Information Sharing Purpose**: Choose the reason(s) why you are sharing information with this third party. You can select multiple options.
3. **Information Sharing Method**: Indicate how information will be shared with the third party.
4. **Information Sharing Frequency**: Select how often information will be shared.
5. **Information Sharing Method Details**: Enter any additional details about the method of sharing information.
6. **Information Sharing Frequency Details**: Enter any additional details about the frequency of sharing information.
7. **Third Party Details**: Enter any relevant information about the third party involved.
8. **Legal Collaboration Details**: Enter any additional details about the collaboration.
9. **Consent to Share Information**: Confirm whether you are willing to share information with the third party.
10. **Consent to Disclose Third Party**: Confirm whether you are willing to disclose the third party's information.
11. **Signature**: Enter the date you completed this form.
12. **Third Party Name**: Enter the name of the third party involved.
13. **Third Party Contact Info**: Enter the contact information of the third party.
14. **Third Party Email**: Enter the email address of the third party.
15. **Third Party Phone**: Enter the phone number of the third party.
16. **Third Party Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the third party.
17. **Consent Form Completion Date**: Enter the date this form was completed.
18. **Third Party Consent Form Completion Date**: Enter the date this form was completed by the third party.
19. **Third Party Consent Form Expiration Date**: Enter the date this form will expire.
20. **Third Party Consent Form Revision**: Enter the current revision number of this form.
21. **Third Party Consent Form Approval Date**: Enter the date this form was approved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Legal Collaboration Consent**:
	+ Choose "Client" if you are representing yourself in this collaboration.
	+ Choose "Attorney" if you are representing an attorney.
	+ Choose "Other" if you are representing a different party.
* **Information Sharing Purpose**:
	+ Select "To obtain legal opinion" to clarify legal matters.
	+ Select "To obtain legal advice" for guidance on legal matters.
	+ Select "To obtain information" for general information exchange.
	+ Choose "Other" for other reasons not listed.
* **Information Sharing Method**:
	+ Choose "Email" for electronic mail.
	+ Choose "Phone" for voice communication.
	+ Choose "Mail" for postal mail.
	+ Choose "Other" for other methods not listed.
* **Information Sharing Frequency**:
	+ Choose "Daily" for information shared daily.
	+ Choose "Weekly" for information shared weekly.
	+ Choose "Monthly" for information shared monthly.
	+ Choose "Other" for other frequencies not listed.
* **Information Sharing Method Details**:
	+ Enter any specific details about how information will be shared.
* **Information Sharing Frequency Details**:
	+ Enter any specific details about the frequency of sharing information.
* **Third Party Details**:
	+ Enter any relevant information about the third party involved.
* **Legal Collaboration Details**:
	+ Enter any additional details about the collaboration.
* **Consent to Share Information**:
	+ Choose "Yes" to confirm consent to share information.
	+ Choose "No" to decline consent to share information.
* **Consent to Disclose Third Party**:
	+ Choose "Yes" to confirm consent to disclose third party information.
	+ Choose "No" to decline consent to disclose third party information.
* **Signature**: Enter the date you completed this form.
* **Third Party Name**: Enter the name of the third party involved.
* **Third Party Contact Info**: Enter the contact information of the third party.
* **Third Party Email**: Enter the email address of the third party.
* **Third Party Phone**: Enter the phone number of the third party.
* **Third Party Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the third party.
* **Consent Form Completion Date**: Enter the date this form was completed.
* **Third Party Consent Form Completion Date**: Enter the date this form was completed by the third party.
* **Third Party Consent Form Expiration Date**: Enter the date this form will expire.
* **Third Party Consent Form Revision**: Enter the current revision number of this form.
* **Third Party Consent Form Approval Date**: Enter the date this form was approved.
