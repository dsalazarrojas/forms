# Research Data Usage Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is intended to collect information from participants regarding the usage of their research data. It ensures participants are aware of and agree to the terms of data usage.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete each page of the form carefully and accurately.
2. Fill in all required fields. The required fields are indicated by an asterisk (\*).
3. For select fields, choose the most suitable option that best describes your preference or choice.
4. For time fields, select the duration or frequency of data usage.
5. For date fields, select the date of participant consent.

* **Main Page** (`main_page`, text, optional): This is the first page of the form.
* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, text, optional): Please enter your name, email, or other contact information.
* **Research Project Information** (`research_project_information`, text, optional): Enter the title of the research project or study.
* **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`, text, optional): This field is for participant consent or agreement to the terms of data usage.

* **Data Usage Duration** (`data_usage_duration`, time, optional): Select the duration of data usage.
* **Data Usage Frequency** (`data_usage_frequency`, time, optional): Select the frequency of data usage.
* **Participant Communication Preference** (`participant_communication_preference`, select_one, optional): Select how you wish to be contacted:

	+ Label 3
* **Research Institution** (`research_institution`, text, optional): Enter the name of the research institution.
* **Research Team** (`research_team`, text, optional): Enter the name of the research team.
* **Data Usage Location** (`data_usage_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where data is to be used.
* **Research Study Title** (`research_study_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the research study.
* **Participant Informed Consent** (`participant_informed_consent`, text, optional): This field is for participant informed consent or agreement to the terms of data usage.
* **Participant Contact Info** (`participant_contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your participant contact information.
* **Research Project Description** (`research_project_description`, text, optional): Enter a description of the research project.
* **Data Usage Purpose** (`data_usage_purpose`, text, optional): Enter the purpose of data usage.
* **Data Usage Limitations** (`data_usage_limitations`, text, optional): Enter any limitations on data usage.
* **Participant Authorization** (`participant_authorization`, text, optional): This field is for participant authorization or agreement to the terms of data usage.
* **Research Institution Website** (`research_institution_website`, text, optional): Enter the website of the research institution.
* **Research Study URL** (`research_study_url`, text, optional): Enter the URL of the research study.
* **Research Project Privacy** (`research_project_privacy`, text, optional): Enter the project's privacy details.
* **Research Project Security** (`research_project_security`, text, optional): Enter the project's security details.
* **Participant Consent Date** (`participant_consent_date`, date, optional): Select the date of participant consent.

## Tips

* Please fill in the form carefully and accurately.
* Ensure you agree to the terms of data usage.
* Review and understand each field's purpose before filling it in.
* Ask for help if you need it.
