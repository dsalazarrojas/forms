# Open Resource Documentation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Open Resource Documentation Consent Form is used to document resources and collect metadata related to them. It's meant to be filled out by users who want to share their resources with the public, providing information about the resource, its permissions, and the terms of use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the User Info section with your name and any other relevant personal details.
* In the Resource Info section, provide a clear and concise description of the resource you are documenting.
* In the Permissions section, select the permissions you have given for your resource.
* In the Terms of Use section, describe the terms of use for your resource.
* Fill out the Email and Phone sections with your contact information.
* In the Resource License section, select the license under which your resource is being shared.
* In the Submission Terms section, describe any additional terms or conditions for the submission.
* In the Date and Time sections, provide the date and time of your submission.
* In the Resource Version section, provide information about the version of your resource.
* In the File Upload section, upload any supporting files or documents related to your resource.
* In the User Note section, provide any additional comments or notes about your resource.
* In the Resource Comment section, provide any comments or notes about your resource.
* In the Acknowledge Terms of Use section, indicate whether you acknowledge the terms of use.
* In the Agree section, indicate whether you agree with the terms of use.
* In the Resource Agreement section, indicate whether you agree with the resource agreement.
* In the Disagree section, indicate any disagreements with the terms of use or resource agreement.
* In the Submit Button section, click the submit button to complete and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Info (1)
* **User Info** (text): Enter your name and any other relevant personal details.

### Resource Info (2)
* **Resource Info** (text): Describe the resource you are documenting in a clear and concise manner.

### Permissions (3)
* **Permissions** (text): Select the permissions you have given for your resource. (Not currently visible in this template)

### Terms of Use (4)
* **Terms of Use** (text): Describe the terms of use for your resource.

### Email (6)
* **Email** (email): Enter your contact email.

### Phone (7)
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.

### Date (10)

### Resource Version (12)

* **Unsure** (select one): Unsure about the terms of use.

* **Agree** (select multiple): Indicate whether you agree with each term of use.
* **Disagree and Dismiss** (select multiple): Disagree with each term of use and dismiss it.
* **Disagree and Do Not Use** (select multiple): Disagree with each term of use and do not use it.

* **Disagree** (select multiple): Disagree with each resource agreement.
