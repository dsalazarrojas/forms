# Key Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Key Release Form is a document that outlines the details of accessing a rental property, including the renter's information, property specifics, and agreements between parties. This form helps ensure that all relevant parties are aware of the key or access details and their responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the correct "Renter," "Manager," or "Landlord" category for your entry.
2. Enter your contact information, including phone number and email, in the "Contact Info" field.
3. Select the "Reason for Entry" to indicate the purpose of your access.
4. Specify the "Access" details, choosing the areas of the property you have access to.
5. Add any additional "Notes" or comments about your entry.
6. Review and agree on the "Agreement" section, confirming your understanding of the key or access details.
7. Sign and date the form, using the "Date Signed" field for the renter and "Signature" fields for the manager and landlord.
