# Food Product Trial Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Product Trial Release Form is a critical document that ensures participants have provided necessary consents and details before participating in a food product trial. It helps prevent potential issues and ensures a smooth trial process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each section carefully and answer all required questions.
2. Fill out the Participant Identification section with your participant ID number.
3. Answer the Allergy Disclosure question truthfully.
4. Provide the Food Product Name and Trial Location where the trial will take place.
5. Give your consent for the trial by selecting "Yes" in the Participant Consent section.
6. Enter your email address and phone number for follow-up purposes.
7. Select "Yes" for the Participant Consent 2 and 3 sections, if applicable.
8. Provide any additional feedback or comments in the Feedback section.
9. Enter your notes about the trial in the Notes section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Identification** (`id`, `number`, required): Enter your participant ID number.
* **Allergy Disclosure** (`name`, `select_one`, required): Answer "Yes" if you have any known allergies that may be affected by the food product.
* **Food Product Name** (`id`, `text`, required): Provide the name of the food product being tested.
* **Participant Consent** (`id`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" to confirm your consent for the trial.
* **Participant Info** (`id`, `text`, required): Provide any relevant participant information that may impact the trial.
* **Trial Location** (`id`, `text`, required): Enter the location of the trial.
* **Assigned Tool** (`id`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the tool you will be using during the trial.
* **Output File** (`id`, `text`, required): Provide the location of the output file.
* **Data Collection** (`id`, `text`, optional): Enter any data collection procedures or methods you will use during the trial.
* **Follow-up Reminders** (`id`, `text`, optional): Enter any follow-up reminders or communications you need from the researcher.
* **Participant Email** (`id`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for follow-up purposes.
* **Participant Phone** (`id`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for follow-up purposes.
* **Assigned Researcher** (`id`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the researcher assigned to your trial.
* **Notes** (`id`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the trial.
* **Participant Consent 2** (`id`, `select_one`, optional): Select "Yes" to confirm your consent for the trial.
* **Participant Consent 3** (`id`, `select_one`, optional): Select "Yes" to confirm your consent for the trial.
* **Participant Consent 4** (`id`, `select_one`, optional): Select "Yes" to confirm your consent for the trial.
* **Follow-up Reminder 1** (`id`, `text`, optional): Enter any follow-up reminders or communications you need from the researcher.
* **Follow-up Reminder 2** (`id`, `text`, optional): Enter any follow-up reminders or communications you need from the researcher.
* **Follow-up Reminder 3** (`id`, `text`, optional): Enter any follow-up reminders or communications you need from the researcher.
* **Follow-up Reminder 4** (`id`, `text`, optional): Enter any follow-up reminders or communications you need from the researcher.
