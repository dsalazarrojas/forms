# Dermal Filler Treatment Medical Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain medical consent and collect information from patients before dermal filler treatment. It is a crucial part of the treatment process to ensure that the patient understands and agrees to the treatment, as well as provides necessary information about their medical history and treatment details.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in the patient's personal details, such as their name, contact information, and date of birth.
- Select all applicable medical conditions that apply to the patient.
- Provide the date of the treatment.
- Sign and date the form to confirm agreement and understanding of the treatment terms.
- Sign the form as a doctor to indicate that the medical treatment has been reviewed and approved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, `text`, Optional)
  - This field is intended to collect the patient's basic contact information and demographics.
  - Please fill in the patient's full name, address, and any other relevant details.
  
- **Medical Consent** (`medical_consent`, `select_multiple`, Optional)
  - This field allows the patient to select all applicable medical conditions that apply to them.
  - Select all conditions that you believe apply to you, or select "Other" if your condition is not listed.
  
- **Treatment Details** (`treatment_details`, `text`, Optional)
  - This field is meant for providing additional information about the treatment.
  - If necessary, provide details about the treatment, such as the type of dermal filler used, etc.
  
- **Signature** (`signature`, `note`, Optional)
  - This field is an area where the patient can sign to confirm their agreement to the treatment.
  - Please sign this field to acknowledge that you understand and agree to the terms of the treatment.
  
- **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, `note`, Optional)
  - This field is for the patient to sign their name, indicating their agreement.
  - Please sign your name to confirm that you have read and understood the treatment terms.
  
- **Doctor Signature** (`doctor_signature`, `note`, Optional)
  - This field is for the doctor to sign, indicating that they have reviewed and approved the treatment.
  - Please sign your name to confirm that you have reviewed and approved the treatment terms.
  
- **Date** (`date`, `date`, Optional)
  - This field is for the date of the treatment.
  - Please enter the date of the treatment.
  
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, Optional)
  - This field is meant to collect the patient's date of birth.
  - Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
  
- **Patient Contact Information** (`patient_contact_information`, `text`, Optional)
  - This field is for providing additional contact information for the patient.
  - If you have any other contact information, such as phone numbers or email addresses, please provide them here.
  
Note: This guide is based on the provided YAML fields and is intended to provide a concrete and practical explanation of the form. If any field is unclear or has a weak label, it has been explained best-effort based on the YAML fields provided.
