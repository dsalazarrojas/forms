# Palliative Care Storytelling Film Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Palliative Care Storytelling Film Consent Form is designed to gather informed consent from patients and their caregivers for the creation of a palliative care storytelling film. This form ensures that the patient's and their caregiver's wishes and boundaries are respected while allowing us to create a film that accurately reflects their care journey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the options that apply to you.
2. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
4. Enter your date of birth in the "date_of_birth" field.
5. Enter your contact address in the "contact_address" field.
6. Select the type of video you prefer by choosing from the options in the "video_type" field.
7. Specify the duration of the film in the "duration" field.
8. Enter the location where the film will be created in the "location" field.
9. Indicate your preferences for permissions and consent in the "permissions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consent Form** (`consent_form`): Please select whether you consent to create a palliative care storytelling film.
* **First Name** (`first_name`): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Please enter your date of birth.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`): Enter your contact address for communication and follow-up purposes.
* **Care Team** (`care_team`): Please select whether a care team will be involved in the creation of your film.
* **Video Type** (`video_type`): Choose the type of video you prefer, such as:
	+ Video of patient in hospital
	+ Video of patient at home
	+ Video of patient during a carer's visit
* **Duration** (`duration`): Specify the length of time you would like the film to be.
* **Location** (`location`): Enter the location where you would like the film to be created.
* **Permissions** (`permissions`): Please select whether you consent to the use of your film.
