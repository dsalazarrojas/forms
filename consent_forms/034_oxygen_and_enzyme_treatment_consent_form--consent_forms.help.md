# oxygen_and_enzyme_treatment_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect consent from patients or individuals for oxygen and enzyme treatment. It informs them about the potential benefits and risks involved in the treatment and ensures that they understand their role in the treatment process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please read the form carefully and answer all questions truthfully.
2. Select the correct answers based on your understanding of the treatment and its effects.
3. If you are unsure about any question, ask your treating physician or medical team for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Oxygen and Enzyme Treatment** (`oxygen_and_enzyme_treatment`, select_one): Choose "Oxygen and Enzyme Treatment" if this is the treatment you are undergoing. Choose "Other" if you are receiving a different treatment.
* **Consent Form** (`consent_form`, select_one): Check "Yes" if you have read and understand the treatment information, and are willing to proceed with the treatment. Check "No" if you are not willing to proceed.
* **Potential Benefits** (`potential_benefits`, text): Describe the potential benefits you have been told about by your treating physician or medical team.
* **Aftercare Instructions** (`aftercare_instruct`, text): Describe any aftercare instructions you have been given by your treating physician or medical team.
* **Potential Risks** (`potential_risks`, text): Describe any potential risks or side effects you have been told about by your treating physician or medical team.
* **Additional Information** (`oxygen_and_enzyme_treatment_consent_form`, text): If you have any additional information to share or comments about your treatment, please write them here.
