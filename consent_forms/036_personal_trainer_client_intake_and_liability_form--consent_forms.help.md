# Personal Trainer Client Intake And Liability Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for new clients to provide essential information and sign the necessary agreements to start a personal training program with our personal trainers. The form helps us understand the client's medical conditions, emergency contact information, and other relevant details to ensure their safety and provide the best possible experience. Please note that this form is mandatory for all new clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name, last name, email, and phone number in the respective fields.
2. Answer the medical conditions question with any pre-existing medical conditions that may affect your training sessions.
3. If you have an emergency contact, provide their name, phone, and relationship to you.
4. Confirm your relationship with the emergency contact by answering the "Relationship To Client" field.
5. Answer the "Medical Provider" field with your healthcare provider's information.
6. If you have any medical information, provide it in the "Medical Provider Info" field.
7. Answer the "Risk Factor" question with "Yes" if you have any risk factors that may affect your training sessions.
8. Answer the "Medical Clearance" question with "Yes" if you've received clearance from your healthcare provider.
9. Sign the "Client Signature" field to confirm that you've read and understood the agreements.
10. Finally, choose a date for the "Client Date" field, which will serve as a reference for all future appointments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any pre-existing medical conditions that may affect your training sessions.
* **Emergency Contact First Name**: Enter the first name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name**: Enter the last name of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship To Client**: Describe your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Medical Provider**: Provide your healthcare provider's information.
* **Medical Provider Info**: List any relevant medical information.
* **Risk Factor**: Answer "Yes" if you have any risk factors that may affect your training sessions.
* **Emergency Contact Info**: Provide your emergency contact's contact information.
* **Emergency Contact Signature**: Confirm your emergency contact's signature.
* **Client Statement**: Choose a date for your initial meeting with your personal trainer.
* **Medical Clearance**: Answer "Yes" if you've received clearance from your healthcare provider.
* **Signature Date**: Confirm the date of your client agreement.
* **Client Signature**: Confirm that you've read and understood the agreements.
* **Client Date**: Choose a date for your training sessions.
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide your emergency contact's information.
* **Medical Procedure**: List any specific medical procedures or surgeries that may affect your training sessions.
* **Medical Condition Note**: Provide any additional notes on your medical conditions.
* **Medical Procedure Note**: Provide any additional notes on your medical procedures.
* **Client Agreement**: Confirm that you've read and understood the agreements.
* **Disclaimer**: Confirm that you're aware of the risks and responsibilities involved in the personal training program.

Note: Please review each field carefully and answer accurately to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for both you and your personal trainer.
