# Game Highlights Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Game Highlights Release Form is a document that requires participants and teams to provide consent for the release of game highlights after an event. This form ensures that the participant or team has given their consent before sharing their highlights on social media.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **Participant Name** in the text field.
2. Enter your **Participant Email** in the email address format.
3. Select one of the options for **Team Name Check** to specify if you have a team name or not.
4. Enter the **Date of Event** when the game took place.
5. Enter the name of the **Event** where you played.
6. Select a date for the **Event Date** when the game took place.
7. Choose **Consent Given** to specify if you give consent for the release of your highlights.
8. Choose the social media channels where you want your highlights to be **Publish Highlights** (e.g., Facebook, Instagram, Twitter).
9. Provide a brief explanation for **Publish Highlights Why**.
10. Sign your **Participant Signature** to confirm your consent.
11. If you are not part of a team, enter your **Assignee Name**.
12. Enter the **Assignee Email** in the email address format.
13. Enter the **Assignee Phone** number.
14. Enter the **Assignee Address**.
15. Enter the **Assignee City** and **Assignee State**.
16. Enter the **Assignee Country**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official ID or driver's license.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address associated with your game profile.
* **Team Name**: If you have a team name, select it from the dropdown list. If not, choose "No Team".
* **Team Name Check**: Confirm your team name or confirm you don't have a team name.
* **Date of Event**: Enter the date of the event where you played.
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event where you played.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event where you played.
* **Consent Given**: Choose "Yes" if you give consent for the release of your highlights.
* **Publish Highlights**: Select the social media channels where you want your highlights to be published (e.g., Facebook, Instagram, Twitter).
* **Publish Highlights Why**: Provide a brief explanation for why you want your highlights to be published.
* **Participant Signature**: Sign your name to confirm your consent.
* **Assignee Name**: If you are not part of a team, enter your name.
* **Assignee Email**: Enter the email address associated with your game profile.
* **Assignee Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Assignee Address**: Enter your physical address.
* **Assignee City**: Enter your city.
* **Assignee State**: Enter your state.
* **Assignee Country**: Enter your country.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
