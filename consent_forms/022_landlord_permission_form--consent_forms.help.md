# Landlord Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Landlord Permission Form is used to collect information and approval from landlords for pet-related agreements or requests in a rental property or building.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
2. Enter the tenant's name.
3. Enter the pet's name.
4. Select the pet type (e.g., cat, dog, etc.).
5. Enter the pet's breed.
6. Enter the pet's size.
7. Enter the pet's diet.
8. Enter the pet's shed (if applicable).
9. Select the lease term for the pet (e.g., Small, Medium, Large).
10. Enter the pet's monthly fees (if applicable).
11. Enter the duration of the pet fees (if applicable).
12. Add any additional requests or concerns.
13. Enter the date of the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **tenant_name** (required): Enter the name of the tenant who owns the pet.
* **pet_name** (required): Enter the name of the pet.
* **pet_type** (optional): Select the type of pet (e.g., cat, dog, etc.).
* **pet_breed** (optional): Enter the breed of the pet.
* **pet_size** (optional): Enter the size of the pet.
* **pet_diet** (optional): Enter the diet of the pet (e.g., dry food, wet food, etc.).
* **pet_shed** (optional): Enter the pet's shed or coat length.
* **pet_lease** (required): Select the lease term for the pet (e.g., Small, Medium, Large).
* **pet_fees** (optional): Enter the monthly fees for the pet (if applicable).
* **pet_fees_duration** (optional): Enter the duration of the pet fees (if applicable).
* **additional_requests** (optional): Add any additional requests or concerns.
* **date_of_request** (required): Enter the date of the request for the pet.
* **landlord_permission** (required): Enter a brief statement of permission or refusal from the landlord.
* **tenant_signature** (required): Enter the tenant's signature.
* **landlord_signature** (required): Enter the landlord's signature.
