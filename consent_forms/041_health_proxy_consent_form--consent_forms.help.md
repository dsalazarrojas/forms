# health_proxy_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The health_proxy_consent_form is designed to obtain informed consent from a patient or their representative to grant access to their medical history.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read the form instructions and ensure you understand the purpose of this form.
2. Fill out the required fields:
	* First name
	* Last name
	* Address line 1
	* City
	* State (select "Yes" or "No")
	* Zip code
	* Phone number
	* Email address
	* Medical history
	* Healthcare facility
	* Name of healthcare facility
	* Contact person
	* Contact phone number
	* Contact email address
	* Name of healthcare provider
	* Name of healthcare provider title
	* Healthcare provider NPI
	* Healthcare provider license number
	* Healthcare provider facility
	* Healthcare provider name
	* Healthcare provider title
	* Healthcare provider address line 1
	* Healthcare provider city
	* Healthcare provider state (select "Yes" or "No")
3. If required fields are optional for you, fill them out as well for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Address line 1**: Enter the address where you can be reached.
* **City**: Enter the city where the address is located.
* **State**: Select "Yes" if you have any medical history or condition that may affect your health.
* **Zip code**: Enter the postal code of your address.
* **Phone number**: Enter a contact phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email address**: Enter a contact email address where you can be reached.
* **Medical history**: Provide any relevant medical information, if any.
* **Healthcare facility**: Enter the name of the healthcare facility you are associated with or have visited.
* **Name of healthcare facility**: Enter the full name of the healthcare facility.
* **Contact person**: Enter the name and contact information of the person who can be reached for more information.
* **Contact phone number**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact email address**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Name of healthcare provider**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider.
* **Name of healthcare provider title**: Enter the title of the healthcare provider.
* **Healthcare provider NPI**: Enter the unique identifier number for the healthcare provider.
* **Healthcare provider license number**: Enter the license number of the healthcare provider.
* **Healthcare provider facility**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider's facility.
* **Healthcare provider name**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider.
* **Healthcare provider title**: Enter the title of the healthcare provider.
* **Healthcare provider address line 1**: Enter the address line 1 of the healthcare provider's facility.
* **Healthcare provider city**: Enter the city where the healthcare provider's facility is located.
* **Healthcare provider state**: Select "Yes" if you have any medical history or condition that may affect your health.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
- If you are unsure about any field, ask for clarification before proceeding.
- Once completed, review the form for accuracy before submitting.
