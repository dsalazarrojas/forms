# Estate Planning Liability Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Estate Planning Liability Waiver Form is a document used to gather important information from clients regarding their estate planning needs and wishes. This form is intended to help our organization understand the client's preferences and boundaries when it comes to our services, ensuring we can provide personalized guidance and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each section and what information is requested.
* Answer each question truthfully and completely.
* For fields with options, select all that apply.
* Sign and date the form to confirm you have reviewed and agree with the information provided.
* Submit the completed form to our team for review and guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`1`): This section is a brief introduction to your estate planning needs and wishes. Please provide a short summary of your goals and objectives.
* **Client Information** (`2`): In this section, please provide your personal contact information, including your name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Liability Waiver** (`3`): Please provide a clear and concise statement of your understanding and agreement to our terms and conditions regarding our services. This is an important section that outlines your expectations and boundaries.
* **Signature** (`4`): Sign and date this section to confirm you have reviewed and agree with the information provided. This is your acknowledgment that you have read and understand the form and its contents.
* **Related Parties** (`5`): This section allows you to identify any other individuals or entities that may be involved in your estate planning process, such as beneficiaries, executors, or financial institutions. Select all that apply.
* **Date Signed** (`6`): Please provide the date on which you signed this form. This helps us keep track of your progress and ensures we can provide timely guidance and support.
* **Time Signed** (`7`): Enter the time at which you signed this form. This is optional but helps us understand your time zone and schedule.
* **Estate Information** (`8`): In this section, please provide any additional information about your estate, such as property values, financial assets, or other relevant details. This helps us understand your specific needs and provide tailored guidance.
