# Dental Records Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Dental Records Release Form is a document used to request and authorize the release of a patient's dental records. It is used by healthcare providers to request patient medical records for the purpose of continuing care or for other legitimate reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Name**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their medical record.
	* **Reason for Requesting Records**: Enter a brief explanation of why you are requesting the patient's dental records.
	* **Patient Signature**: Sign here to confirm that you are the authorized representative of the patient.
	* **Date of Request**: Enter the date you are requesting the patient's records.
	* **Address**: Enter the address where the patient's records will be sent.
	* **Phone**: Enter the contact phone number.
	* **Medical Group**: Confirm if you are a part of a medical group.
	* **Medical Record ID**: Enter the ID of the patient's medical record.
	* **Patient Sex**: Select the patient's sex.
	* **Patient Race**: Select the patient's race.
2. Fill in the optional fields:
	* **Healthcare Provider**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider.
	* **Doctor's Name**: Enter the name of the doctor providing care.
	* **Doctor's Specialty**: Enter the specialty of the doctor providing care.
	* **Patient Age**: Enter the age of the patient.
	* **Patient Medical Record**: Enter the medical record number.
	* **Doctor Medical Record**: Enter the medical record number of the doctor.
	* **Patient Medical Record 2**: Enter the medical record number.
	* **Doctor Specialty 2**: Enter the specialty of the doctor.
