# Remote Project Collaboration Disclosure Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for remote project collaboration. It's a mandatory declaration for individuals to agree to a set of conditions or guidelines when working together on a project remotely. This form helps to ensure that all collaborating parties are aware of their responsibilities and obligations, and it provides a clear declaration of understanding and agreement to the conditions of collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field. This is your full name, as per your official records.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field. This will be used for official communication and verification purposes.
3. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field. This is optional.
4. The "Date" field is not required, but if you need to specify a particular date for the collaboration, you can enter it here.
5. Similarly, the "Time" field is optional. If you need to specify a time for the collaboration, you can enter it here.
6. In the "Note" field, you can provide any additional comments or information relevant to the collaboration.
7. Finally, read and select your response to the "Consent" field. Choose "I understand and agree" if you agree to the conditions of collaboration, or "I do not understand and do not agree" if you have concerns or disagreements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (Required): Enter your full name as per your official records.
  - This field is required to ensure that your identity is verified.
- **Email** (Required): Enter your official email address for communication and verification purposes.
  - This field is required for official communication and verification.
- **Phone** (Optional): Enter your phone number if you have one.
  - This field is optional and only needed if you want to provide an alternative contact method.
- **Date** (Optional): If you need to specify a particular date for the collaboration, enter it here.
  - This field is not required but can be useful if you need to schedule specific dates.
- **Time** (Optional): If you need to specify a time for the collaboration, enter it here.
  - Similar to the "Date" field, this is not required but can be useful for scheduling specific times.
- **Note** (Optional): Add any additional comments or information relevant to the collaboration here.
  - This field is optional but can help clarify any additional context or details.
- **Consent** (Required): Select "I understand and agree" if you agree to the conditions of collaboration.
  - This field is required to ensure that you understand and agree to the conditions.
