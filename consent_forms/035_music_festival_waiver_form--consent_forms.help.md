# Music Festival Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is required for all festival participants, aged 18 or above, to provide necessary contact and health information, as well as grant permissions to use their name, photo, or likeness for festival promotional purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately to the best of your ability.
2. Ensure that you have permission from your emergency contact, if they are not with you.
3. Answer each field carefully, taking the time to understand the question or selection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`User Name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it is written on your official identification.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address where you can be reached during the festival.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached during the festival.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`Emergency Contact Name`, text, optional): If you are 18 or above, enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact, such as a parent or guardian.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`Emergency Contact Phone`, text, optional): If you provided an emergency contact name, enter their phone number here.
* **Relationship to Participant** (`Relationship to Participant`, select_one, required): Select the relationship between you and your emergency contact (e.g. Parent, Guardian, Other).
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Medical Conditions** (`Medical Conditions`, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions, please list them here. This information will be kept confidential.
* **Medication** (`Medication`, text, optional): If you take any medication, please list the name and dosage here. This information will be kept confidential.
* **Allergies** (`Allergies`, text, optional): If you have any allergies, please list them here. This information will be kept confidential.
* **Participant Consent** (`Participant Consent`, select_multiple, required): Check the boxes to grant permission for the festival to use your name, photo, or likeness for promotional purposes.
* **Emergency Contact Consent** (`Emergency Contact Consent`, select_multiple, required): Check the boxes to grant permission for the festival to use the name, photo, or likeness of your emergency contact for promotional purposes.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer all required fields accurately and truthfully.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please contact the festival organizers.
* The collected information will be kept confidential and used only for festival purposes.
