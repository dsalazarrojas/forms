# Medical Research Patient Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Research Patient Inquiry Form is a pre-screening form used to collect information from patients interested in participating in medical research studies.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow the instructions below:
1. Provide your First Name.
2. Provide your Last Name.
3. Enter your registered email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Enter your age.
6. Select your research interest (Cancer, Heart Health, Neurological Disorder, or Other).
7. Provide any pre-screening details in the notes section.
8. If applicable, choose a follow-up date and time for further research discussion.
9. If applicable, choose the type of consent you are willing to give for the research study (True or False).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `text`, required: false): Enter your registered email address. This will help us contact you for follow-up discussions.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, required: false): Enter your age to help us understand your demographic details.
* **Study Interest** (`study_interest`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your research interest (Cancer, Heart Health, Neurological Disorder, or Other).
* **Pre-Screening Details** (`pre_screening_details`, `note`, required: false): Provide any additional details that you would like to share about yourself or your research interest.
* **Follow-Up** (`follow_up`, `date`, required: false): Choose a follow-up date for further research discussion.
* **Follow-Up Time** (`follow_up_time`, `time`, required: false): Choose a follow-up time for further research discussion.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the type of consent you are willing to give for the research study (True or False).
