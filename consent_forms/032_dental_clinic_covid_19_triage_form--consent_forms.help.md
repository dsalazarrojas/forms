# Dental Clinic COVID 19 Triage Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dental Clinic COVID 19 Triage Form is used to collect relevant information from patients to assess and manage the risks associated with COVID-19. This form helps in the triage process to identify potential high-risk cases and provide necessary medical care. It is designed for both patients and healthcare professionals to fill out, either in person or remotely.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form completely to ensure the best possible care and risk assessment.
- If you have difficulty with English, please seek help from a healthcare professional or translator.
- For healthcare professionals, this form should be filled out for each patient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Consent Form (`consent_form`, `select_multiple`, required)
*   **Consent Form** (`yes`, `no`): Please select either "yes" or "no" based on your agreement or disagreement with the consent form.

### 2. Patient Info Form (`patient_info_form`, `number`, optional)
*   **Patient Info Form** (`number`): Enter your patient's ID number for easy identification and tracking.

### 3. Contact Form (`contact_form`, `text`, optional)
*   **Contact Form**: Write any questions or comments you have regarding your treatment or any concerns you may have.

### 4. Medical History Form (`medical_history_form`, `text`, optional)
*   **Medical History Form**: Provide any relevant medical history you feel might impact your treatment.

### 5. Medical History (`medical_history`, `select_one`, optional)
*   **Medical History**: Select any existing medical condition that may be relevant to your case from the provided options (asthma, cancer, heart disease).

### 6. Travel History (`travel_history_form`, `select_multiple`, optional)
*   **Travel History**: Select any countries or areas you have recently traveled to, especially those at high risk for COVID-19.

### 7. Exposure History (`exposure_form`, `select_multiple`, optional)
*   **Exposure History**: Select any high-risk contact you have had, especially those related to close contacts or travel.

### 8. Symptom Form (`symptoms_form`, `text`, required)
*   **Symptom Form**: Describe your symptoms and any issues you are experiencing.

### 9. Temperature Check (`temperature_form`, `number`, optional)
*   **Temperature Check**: Enter your current body temperature.

### 10. Medical Clinic Triage (`medical_clinic_triage_form`, `time`, optional)
*   **Medical Clinic Triage**: Select a time for a potential medical clinic visit based on your availability and needs.

### 11. Patient Signature (`patient_signature_form`, `select_one`, optional)
*   **Patient Signature**: Select either "I agree to the consent form" or "I decline the consent form."

### 12. Healthcare Professional Signature (`healthcare_professional_signature_form`, `select_one`, optional)
*   **Healthcare Professional Signature**: Select either "I agree to the consent form" or "I decline the consent form."
