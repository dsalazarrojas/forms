# Hot Tub Waiver Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hot Tub Waiver Template is a form used to collect necessary information from individuals who want to participate in hot tub activities. This form is typically required by hot tub facilities or service providers to ensure user safety and comply with regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal information in the "User Information" section.
2. If you have an emergency contact, please provide their details in the "Emergency Contact" section.
3. If you have any medical history, please list it in the "Medical History" section.
4. If you have any medical conditions, please list them in the "Medical Conditions" section.
5. If you have any emergency medical treatment plans, please provide this information in the "Emergency Medical Treatment" section.
6. If you are dealing with an emergency situation, please describe it in the "Emergency Medical Emergency" section.
7. In the "Waiver Info" section, provide any additional information that might be relevant to your safety or well-being.
8. Finally, sign and date the "Signature" field to confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information (1)**: Provide your name, contact information, and other personal details as requested.
* **Emergency Contact (2)**: Name and contact information of a person to be notified in case of an emergency.
* **Medical History (3)**: List any previous medical conditions, surgeries, or health concerns that might impact your hot tub experience.
* **Medical Conditions (4)**: List any current medical conditions you have, such as allergies or health issues, that might affect your hot tub use.
* **Emergency Medical Treatment (5)**: If you have any emergency medical treatment plans, such as an EpiPen or a rescue breathing device, please list them here.
* **Emergency Medical Emergency (6)**: Describe any emergency medical situations that you might encounter while using the hot tub.
* **Waiver Info (7)**: Provide any additional information that might help staff understand your needs or circumstances.
* **Signature (8)**: Sign and date this field to confirm your understanding of the hot tub rules and guidelines.

## Tips
* Please fill out this form accurately and honestly to ensure your safety and the safety of others.
* If you are unsure about any of the information, please ask a staff member for clarification.
* Keep a copy of this form for your records.

This guide is designed to be easy to understand and follow. If you have any further questions, please contact the hot tub staff or management.
