# Hair Color Correction Service Consent Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture client consent for a Hair Color Correction Service. It's a straightforward way to gather necessary information from clients before commencing any service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Client Information** page with your personal details as required.
2. In the **Consent** page, type a concise agreement or statement indicating your consent to the Hair Color Correction Service.
3. On the **Service Details** page, provide any additional information about the service you're interested in or have any specific requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information (page 1)**: Enter your personal details as required.
* **Consent (page 2)**: This is a text area where you can type a brief agreement or statement confirming your consent to the Hair Color Correction Service.
* **Service Details (page 3)**: Provide any additional information about the service or special requests here. 

## Tips
- Always ensure that the information you provide is accurate and clear.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask your service provider for clarification.
- Review the entire form carefully before submitting it, ensuring that all fields are filled out correctly.

Remember, this form is a template, and it is your responsibility to ensure that it accurately reflects your situation and needs. If you're unsure about any aspect of this form, consult your service provider for guidance.
