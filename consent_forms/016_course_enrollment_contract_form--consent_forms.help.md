> # Course Enrollment Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Course Enrollment Contract Form is a document used to collect personal and contact information from students who are enrolling in a course. It is intended for students who are new to a program or course, or who need to provide updated contact information. This form helps to establish a formal record of the student's enrollment and ensures they are aware of and agree to the contract terms and conditions.
## How to Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:
1. Start by entering your first name in the "first name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last name" field.
3. Provide your address in the "address" field (if applicable).
4. Enter your email address in the "email" field. Please ensure it is a valid email address that you check regularly.
5. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
6. Enter your date of birth in the "date_of_birth" field in the format of MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/1999).
7. Read and agree to the terms and conditions in the "contract_terms_and_conditions" field.
8. Finally, place your signature in the "student_signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **last name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **address** (text, optional): This field is used to collect your address for communication and administrative purposes.
* **email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address that you use regularly to receive important updates and notifications.
* **phone** (text, required): Enter your contact phone number, including area code and prefix (e.g., (123) 456-7890).
* **date_of_birth** (date, required): Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 01/01/1999).
* **contract_terms_and_conditions** (text, required): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the course.
* **student_signature** (text, required): Place your signature to acknowledge that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
