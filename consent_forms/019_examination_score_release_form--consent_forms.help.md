# Examination Score Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to release examination scores to authorized individuals or institutions. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all the required fields: 
	* User Name: Enter the name of the individual requesting the scores. 
	* School Name: Enter the name of the school where the scores were taken. 
2. Choose the correct Score Type: Select whether scores are being released with "Yes" or "No". 
3. Enter the Score Value: If scores are being released, enter the score value. 
	* Date: Enter the date the scores were taken. 
	* Time: Enter the time the scores were taken. 
4. Authorized Person Information: Fill in the information of the authorized person releasing the scores. 
	* Authorized Person: Enter the name of the person authorized to release the scores. 
	* Authorized Person Email: Enter the email of the authorized person. 
	* Authorized Person Phone Number: Enter the phone number of the authorized person. 
	* School Authorized Person: Enter the name of the school authorized person. 
	* School Authorized Person Email: Enter the email of the school authorized person. 
	* School Authorized Person Phone Number: Enter the phone number of the school authorized person. 
5. Add any additional Note: If you need to provide any further information, enter it in the Note field. 
6. Submit your request: Choose "Yes" to submit your request and "No" to cancel it.
