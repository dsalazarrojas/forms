# Beta Reader Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beta Reader Agreement Form is a crucial document for beta readers who wish to review and provide feedback on manuscripts. It helps ensure that all parties involved in the process are clear on their roles, responsibilities, and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your full name and email address to receive the manuscript and any communication about it.
2. Enter the title of the manuscript you are reviewing.
3. Confirm that you understand you have no ownership rights to the work.
4. Choose how you will provide your feedback (inline comments, separate document, or verbal discussion).
5. Select the areas of focus for your feedback (plot, character development, pacing, tone, etc.).
6. Agree to keep all content confidential and not share it with anyone else.
7. Confirm that you will not distribute or copy the manuscript.
8. Choose whether you would like to be mentioned in the acknowledgments section of the published work.
9. If you agree, enter your preferred name as it should appear in the acknowledgments.
10. Provide any additional notes or thoughts for the author.
11. Sign the agreement electronically by typing your name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reader Full Name**: Enter your full name as you wish to be addressed.
* **Reader Email**: Enter your email address to receive the manuscript and any updates.
* **Manuscript Title**: Enter the title of the manuscript you are reviewing.
* **Author Name**: Enter the author's full name.
* **Expected Feedback Date**: Choose the date when you plan to return your feedback.
* **NDA Agreement**: Confirm that you understand you have no ownership rights to the work and agree to keep it confidential.
* **I will not share the manuscript with anyone else**: Agree not to share the content with anyone.
* **I will not distribute or copy the manuscript**: Confirm that you will not distribute or copy the manuscript.
* **Primary Areas of Focus for Feedback**: Select the areas of focus for your feedback (e.g., plot, character development).
* **Preferred Feedback Format**: Choose how you would like to provide your feedback (inline comments, separate document, or verbal discussion).
* **Rights and Acknowledgments**: Confirm that you understand you have no ownership rights to the work.
* **I understand I have no ownership rights to the work**: Agree that all rights remain with the author.
* **Would you like to be mentioned in the acknowledgments**: Choose whether you would like to be mentioned in the acknowledgments section of the published work.
* **Name as it should appear in acknowledgments**: Enter your preferred name as it should appear in the acknowledgments.
* **Any additional notes for the author**: Enter any additional thoughts or comments for the author.
* **Electronic Signature**: Type your name to act as a legal signature.
* **Date of Agreement**: The date you are agreeing to the terms.
