# treatment_plan_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document a patient's consent for a treatment plan. It captures essential details such as the patient's name, contact information, medical history, treatment plan, and consent dates. The form is designed to be completed by healthcare providers to ensure that patients understand and agree to the treatment plan, and to provide a record of their consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's name in the `patient_name` field.
2. Enter the patient's contact number in the `contact_number` field.
3. Add the patient's email address in the `email_address` field.
4. Describe the patient's medical history in the `medical_history` field.
5. Outline the treatment plan in the detail in the `treatment_plan` field.
6. Choose the date of consent in the `consent_date` field.
7. Sign the form by selecting the date of signing in the `consent_sign_date` field.
8. Identify the healthcare provider in the `healthcare_provider` field.
9. Enter the facility name where the treatment will take place in the `facility_name` field.
10. Leave a note in the `patient_signature` field indicating that the patient has signed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_name** (text, required: false): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification document.
* **contact_number** (text, required: false): Enter the patient's contact number where they can be reached for follow-up.
* **email_address** (email, required: false): Enter the patient's email address for communication purposes.
* **medical_history** (text, required: false): Describe the patient's past medical history, including any allergies, medical conditions, or previous treatments.
* **treatment_plan** (text, required: false): Outline the details of the treatment plan, including the procedures and medications involved.
* **consent_date** (date, required: false): Enter the date on which the patient gave their consent.
* **consent_sign_date** (date, required: false): Enter the date on which the patient signed the form.
* **healthcare_provider** (text, required: false): Identify the healthcare provider who is administering the treatment.
* **facility_name** (text, required: false): Enter the name of the facility where the treatment will take place.
* **patient_signature** (note, required: false): Leave a note indicating that the patient has signed the form, as a confirmation of their consent.
