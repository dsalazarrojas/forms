# Drone Race Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain liability waivers from drone race participants, ensuring event organizers can manage participant data and maintain regulatory compliance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Provide your event name**: Enter your event name in the text field provided.
2. **Enter your participant name and date of birth**: Provide your name and date of birth in the format `yyyy-mm-dd`.
3. **Enter your emergency contact name (if applicable)**: If you have an emergency contact, enter their name in this text field.
4. **Enter your emergency contact relationship (if applicable)**: If you have an emergency contact, indicate your relationship with them in this text field.
5. **Enter your emergency contact phone number (if applicable)**: If you have an emergency contact, enter their phone number in this text field.
6. **Enter your emergency contact email (if applicable)**: If you have an emergency contact, enter their email in this email field.
7. **Sign the participant agreement**: Select the option "I have read and understand the waiver" to agree to the terms.
8. **Sign the participant waiver**: Sign with your name in the text field provided.
9. **Enter your participant notes (if applicable)**: Provide any additional notes or comments in this text field.
10. **Enter participant witnessed by (if applicable)**: If you are not signing this waiver in person, provide the name of the person who witnessed you sign it.
11. **Select the participant waiver version**: Choose the version of the waiver you are agreeing to, if applicable.
12. **Select the participant waiver status**: Select "Active" to indicate that you are agreeing to the waiver.

* **Event name (event_name)**: Enter your event name to allow event organizers to identify the event you are participating in.
* **Participant name (participant_name)**: Enter your name to help event organizers identify you and contact you in case of an emergency.
* **Participant date of birth (participant_date_of_birth)**: Provide your date of birth in the format `yyyy-mm-dd`.

* **Emergency contact relationship (emergency_contact_relationship)**: If you have an emergency contact, indicate your relationship with them to help event organizers understand their role.
* **Emergency contact phone number (emergency_contact_phone)**: If you have an emergency contact, enter their phone number to allow event organizers to contact them in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency contact email (emergency_contact_email)**: If you have an emergency contact, enter their email to allow event organizers to contact them in case of an emergency.
* **Participant signature (participant_signature)**: Sign with your name in the text field provided to indicate your agreement to the waiver.
* **Emergency contact signature (emergency_contact_signature)**: Sign with the emergency contact's name in the text field provided to confirm their agreement.
* **Event organizer (event_organizer)**: Enter the name of the event organizer to allow them to manage participant data.
* **Event organizer phone number (event_organizer_phone)**: Enter the event organizer's phone number to allow them to contact you in case of an emergency.
