# Unconditional Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to document the release of contracts or projects between parties. It ensures that all parties are in agreement with the terms of the release and records the progress and outcomes of this process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Contractor**: Enter the name of the general contractor.
* **Company**: Enter the name of the company.
* **Work Description**: Enter a brief description of the work being proposed.
* **Date**: Enter the date the work is to be completed.
* **Time**: Enter the time the work is to be completed.
* **Work Status**: Choose whether you accept or decline the work by selecting 'Accept' or 'Decline'.
* **Work Details**: Enter any additional comments or details about the work.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the progress of the work.
* **Subcontractor Name**: Enter the name of the subcontractor.
* **Subcontractor Email**: Enter the email of the subcontractor.
* **Subcontractor Phone**: Enter the phone number of the subcontractor.

## Tips

* Be accurate and up-to-date with all the information.
* Review the form carefully before submitting.
* This form is for internal documentation purposes only.
* Do not use this form for any other purpose than what it is intended for.
