# Trust Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trust Records Release Form is used to collect information from a legal professional or trustee to obtain consent for the release of trust-related information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the trust in the **Title** field. This is the name of the trust that the release is for.
2. Select the **Category** of the form from the dropdown menu. This will help us categorize the form for future reference.
3. Enter a **Description** of the reason you are requesting the release of trust-related information. This should include the name of the trust and a brief explanation of why you need the information.
4. Enter your **Name** in the **Name** field. This will be the name of the legal professional or trustee who is requesting the release.
5. Enter your **Email** address in the **Email** field. This will be used for communication and contact purposes.
6. Enter your **Phone** number in the **Phone** field. This will be used for communication and contact purposes.
7. Enter your **Address** in the **Address** field. This will be used for mailing purposes.
8. Enter the **City** where you are located in the **City** field. This will be used for mailing purposes.
9. Select your **State** in the **State** dropdown menu. This will be used for mailing purposes.
10. Enter your **Zip** code in the **Zip** field. This will be used for mailing purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title (text)**: Enter the title of the trust for which you are requesting the release of information.
* **Category (select_one)**: Select the category of the form from the dropdown menu. This will help us categorize the form for future reference.
* **Description (note)**: Enter a brief description of why you are requesting the release of trust-related information.
* **Name (text)**: Enter the name of the legal professional or trustee who is requesting the release.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address that will be used for communication and contact purposes.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number that will be used for communication and contact purposes.
* **Address (text)**: Enter your address that will be used for mailing purposes.
* **City (text)**: Enter the city where you are located that will be used for mailing purposes.
* **State (select_one)**: Select your state from the dropdown menu that will be used for mailing purposes.
* **Zip (text)**: Enter your zip code that will be used for mailing purposes.

## Tips
- Please make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
- Make sure to enter the correct category for the form.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for clarification.
