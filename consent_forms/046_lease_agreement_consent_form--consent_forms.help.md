# lease_agreement_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain tenant consent for a lease agreement. It captures the tenant's contact information, address, and signatures, providing a record of their agreement and key details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all necessary documents and information ready before filling out the form.
2. Fill out the form carefully, providing accurate and complete information in each field.
3. Review each field for completeness and accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant Name** (`tenant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Provide your contact number where you can be reached for communication.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your current address.
* **Landlord Name** (`landlord_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the landlord or property manager.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the property you are leasing.
* **Lease Start Date** (`lease_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the lease, if applicable.
* **Lease End Date** (`lease_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the lease, if applicable.
* **Tenant Signature** (`tenant_signature`, time, required): Sign the form to indicate your agreement.
* **Landlord Signature** (`landlord_signature`, time, required): Sign the form to confirm your agreement with the tenant.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, optional): Date and time of when you signed the form.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the agreement.

## Tips
* Ensure you review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* Keep the form accurate and up-to-date to avoid confusion or disputes.
* Store the form in a secure location to maintain confidentiality of the agreement.
