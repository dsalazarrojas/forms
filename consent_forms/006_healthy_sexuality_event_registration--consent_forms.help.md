# Healthy Sexuality Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about events related to healthy sexuality, such as workshops, conferences, or support groups. The goal is to register events and gather details about participants and event details for administrative or promotional purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your Event Name in the "Event Name" field.
2. Provide your Participant Name in the "Participant Name" field.
3. Select your Participant Gender Identity in the "Participant Gender Identity" field.
4. Choose your Participant Sexual Orientation in the "Participant Sexual Orientation" field.
5. Enter your event details in the "Event Details" field.
6. Select the date of the event in the "Event Date" field.
7. If you want to receive updates or notifications, enter your Participant Contact Email in the "Participant Contact Email" field.
8. Enter your Participant Phone number in the "Participant Phone" field.
9. Describe the location of the event in the "Event Location" field.
10. Select the start time of the event in the "Event Start Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name** (Text, required/optional): Enter the name of the event, such as "Healthy Sexuality Workshop" or "Sex Positivity Support Group".
* **Participant Name** (Text, required/optional): Type in the name of the participant registering for the event, or the name of the person organizing the event.
* **Participant Gender Identity** (Select One, required/optional): Select the participant's gender identity, such as "Male", "Female", "Non-Binary", etc. This helps us better understand the diversity of participants for event promotion and planning.
* **Participant Sexual Orientation** (Select Multiple, required/optional): Select the participant's sexual orientation, such as "Heterosexual", "Homosexual", "Bisexual", etc. This helps us cater to diverse participant needs and provide relevant support.
* **Event Details** (Text, required/optional): Describe the event in as much detail as possible, including its focus, activities, and expected outcomes.
* **Event Date** (Date, required/optional): Select the date of the event, including the year, month, and day.
* **Event Location** (Text, required/optional): Enter the location where the event will take place, including the address or name of the venue.
* **Event Start Time** (Time, required/optional): Select the start time of the event, including the hour and minute.
* **Participant Contact Email** (Email, required/optional): If you want to stay updated about the event, or receive notifications, enter your email address here.
* **Participant Phone** (Text, required/optional): Enter your phone number to receive phone-based updates or reminders.
* **Participant Gender Identity** and **Participant Sexual Orientation** fields are not strictly necessary but can help us better serve diverse participants and tailor our communication and support accordingly.
