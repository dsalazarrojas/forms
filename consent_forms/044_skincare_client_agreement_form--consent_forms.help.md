## Purpose
This form is a crucial document that outlines the terms and conditions of your services. It ensures that both you and your client understand each other's expectations, responsibilities, and boundaries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in the required information (indicated by a red asterisk (*) next to the field).
2. Review and select your preferred service options (e.g., Facials, Massage, Chemical Peel, Micro-Blading).
3. Enter your appointment date and time in the respective fields.
4. Choose your preferred payment method (Cash, Credit Card, Online).
5. Add any relevant notes or comments about your visit.
6. If you're assigned to a specific practitioner, select their name from the options list.
