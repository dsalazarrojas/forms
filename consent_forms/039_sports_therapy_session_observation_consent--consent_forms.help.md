# Sports Therapy Session Observation Consent - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from participants in a sports therapy session for observation. It ensures that the participant is aware of and agrees to the observation of their session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the participant's details on page 1.
2. Specify the purpose of the observation on page 2.
3. Select the type(s) of data that will be collected on page 4.
4. Enter the date and time of the observation and completion on pages 5-7.
5. Sign and date the form, and have the participant sign it as well on pages 8 and 9.
6. Add any additional comments on page 11 if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Participant Details** (`name`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the participant's name and any other relevant details that you need to include in the observation report.
- **Observation Purpose** (`name`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Specify the reason for the observation, such as to assess progress, evaluate technique, or provide feedback.
- **Signature** (`name`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This field is not actively used in the form and can be ignored.
- **Data Collection** (`name`, `type: select_multiple`, `required: false`): Select the type(s) of data that will be collected during the observation, such as movement analysis, muscle activity, or physiological data.
- **Date Observed** (`name`, `type: date`, `required: false`): Enter the date of the observation.
- **Time Observed** (`name`, `type: time`, `required: false`): Enter the time of the observation.
- **Date Completed** (`name`, `type: date`, `required: false`): Enter the date the observation was completed.
- **Date Signed** (`name`, `type: date`, `required: false`): Enter the date you signed the consent form.
- **Participant Signature** (`name`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Have the participant sign this field to indicate their consent.
- **Observer Signature** (`name`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Sign this field to indicate that you have observed the participant's session and obtained their consent.
- **Additional Comments** (`name`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Add any additional comments about the observation, such as weather conditions or equipment used.

Note: All fields are optional, but it's essential to complete all required fields to ensure accurate and comprehensive observation reports.
