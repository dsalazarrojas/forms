# Foster Care Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Foster Care Agreement Form is used to establish the terms and conditions for the care of a child. It collects information about the child's birth details, contact information, agency details, and dates of care.

## How To Complete This Form

* To complete this form, please follow the step-by-step process outlined below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Is the child in your care a child of yours?** (`foster_care_agreement`, select_multiple, required): Answer this question by selecting either "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the child is biologically yours or not.
* **Name of Child** (`name_of_child`, text, required): Please enter the full name of the child.
* **Birth Details (including birthdate)** (`birth_details`, text, required): Provide the child's birth details, including their birthdate.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_details`, text, required): Enter your contact information, including your name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Agency Name and Address** (`agency_details`, text, required): Enter the name and address of the agency providing foster care services.
* **Date of Agreement** (`agreement_date`, date, required): Select the date on which this foster care agreement was made.
* **Dates of Care (start and end)** (`care_duration`, select_multiple, required): Select the start and end dates of the foster care period.
* **Your Contact Information** (`caregiver_details`, text, required): Enter your contact information, including your name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or information to provide, please enter them here.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check the dates of care to ensure they are correct.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact the agency providing foster care services.
