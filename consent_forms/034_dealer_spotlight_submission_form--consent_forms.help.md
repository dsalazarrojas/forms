# Dealer Spotlight Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Dealer Spotlight Submission Form is a tool for dealers to submit their information for a "Dealer Spotlight" program. The form gathers details about the dealer's name, company, address, website, description, and agreement to submit their information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, fill in your dealer's name as it appears on any official documentation or marketing materials.
2. Enter your company name as it is registered and known to the public.
3. Provide your dealership's address for our records.
4. Enter your website's URL, if applicable.
5. Write a brief description of your dealership, highlighting your unique features and services.
6. If you have a logo, provide the URL where it can be found.
7. If you have a profile URL, enter it here.
8. You can optionally provide a media link to further showcase your dealership.
9. Please confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms of the submission form.
10. Confirm that you understand the language on the submission form is in English.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **dealer_spotlight_submission_form_input** (Required: False)
	+ This field is not required, but if filled in, it will be used to provide more details about your submission.
* **dealer_spotlight_name** (Required: True)
	+ Enter your dealer's name as it appears on any official documentation or marketing materials.
* **company_name** (Required: True)
	+ Enter your company's name as it is registered and known to the public.
* **dealership_address** (Required: True)
	+ Provide your dealership's address for our records.
* **website** (Required: True)
	+ Enter your website's URL, if applicable.
* **company_description** (Required: True)
	+ Write a brief description of your dealership, highlighting your unique features and services.
* **logo_url** (Required: False)
	+ If you have a logo, provide the URL where it can be found.
* **spotlight_image_url** (Required: False)
	+ If you have a profile URL, enter it here.
* **dealership_media** (Required: False)
	+ You can optionally provide a media link to further showcase your dealership.
* **dealer_spotlight_profile_url** (Required: False)
	+ Please enter your dealership's profile URL, if applicable.
* **dealer_spotlight_submission_form_agreement** (Required: True)
	+ Confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms of the submission form.
* **dealer_spotlight_submission_form_agreement_english** (Required: True)
	+ Confirm that you understand the language on the submission form is in English.
