# Waiver Of Notice Of Meeting Of The Board Of Directors - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to notify the board of directors about your absence or confirmation of attendance at a meeting, and to provide additional information as required.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the meeting date from the calendar to confirm your absence or presence.
2. Enter your full name as it appears on your identification or official documents.
3. Indicate whether you are attending the meeting by selecting 'True' or 'False' from the dropdown list.
4. If you are not attending, provide a brief reason for not attending.
5. You may provide any additional comments or information related to the meeting.
6. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
7. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
8. You may provide any other details about the meeting.
9. Enter your name and title to confirm your submission.
10. Enter your email address to confirm your submission.
11. Enter your phone number to confirm your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Date (1)** (`meeting_date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the meeting that you are either absent from or attending.
* **Full Name (2)** (`full_name`, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification or official documents.
* **Attendance (3)** (`attendance`, required/optional): Select 'True' if you are attending the meeting, and 'False' if you are not.
* **Reason For Not Attending (4)** (`reason_for_not_attending`, required/optional): If you are not attending, provide a brief reason for your absence.
* **Reason (5)** (`reason`, required/optional): You may provide any additional comments or information related to the meeting.
* **Comments (6)** (`comments`, required/optional): Enter any comments or information related to the meeting.
* **Email (7)** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone (8)** (`phone`, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Meeting Details (9)** (`meeting_details`, required/optional): You may provide any other details about the meeting.
* **Other Details (10)** (`other_details`, required/optional): Enter any other details about yourself or the meeting.
* **Submitter (11)** (`submitter`, required/optional): Enter your name and title to confirm your submission.
* **Submitter Email (12)** (`submitter_email`, required/optional): Enter your email address to confirm your submission.
* **Submitter Phone (13)** (`submitter_phone`, required/optional): Enter your phone number to confirm your submission.

## Tips
Please ensure that you fill out all the required fields accurately and completely to ensure that your absence or presence is recorded correctly. If you have any further questions or concerns, contact the relevant authorities or officials.
