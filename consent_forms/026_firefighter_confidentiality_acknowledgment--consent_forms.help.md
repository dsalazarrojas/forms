# Firefighter Confidentiality Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Firefighter Confidentiality Acknowledgment form is used by fire departments to track the acknowledgment of departmental confidentiality requirements from all their firefighters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:
1. Enter the title of the form, which is a brief description of what the form is for.
2. Select the category of the form from the provided options.
3. Enter a description of what the form is for and why it's essential to complete it.
4. Enter the title of the form again to confirm.
5. Enter a description of what documents firefighters have read, understood, and agreed to.
6. Select the category of the form from the provided options.
7. Enter the name of the output file, which is the file that will contain the completed form data.
8. Enter the name of the assigned tool, which is the tool that will process the form data.
9. Enter a unique number for the form ID, which is the unique identifier for the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Firefighter Confidentiality Acknowledgment** (`form_title`, required: false): A brief description of what the form is for.
* **Category** (`category`, required: false): Select the category of the form from the options provided, which is either "Consent Forms" or "Firefighter Forms".
* **Description** (`description`, required: false): A detailed description of the form and why it's essential to complete it. 
* **Firefighter Confidentiality Acknowledgment Form** (`form_title`, required: false): Confirm the title of the form.
* **Category** (`form_category`, required: false): Confirm the category of the form from the options provided, which is either "Consent Forms" or "Firefighter Forms".
* **Output File** (`output_file`, required: false): Enter the name of the output file that will contain the completed form data.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, required: false): Enter the name of the assigned tool that will process the form data.
* **Form IDs** (`form_ids`, required: false): Enter a unique number for the form ID.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all fields accurately to ensure that the form is correctly processed.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the description provided.
* Complete the form only if you have access to it.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact the form administrator.
* Ensure that you've selected the correct category to ensure that the form is correctly filed.
