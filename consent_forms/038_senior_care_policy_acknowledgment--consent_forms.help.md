# Senior Care Policy Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from care recipients, responsible parties, or facilities involved in senior care services. It helps track and document essential details for efficient and effective care.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the type of care recipient from the options provided.
2. Choose the responsible party from the options provided.
3. If the care recipient has a policy date, enter it.
4. Provide your signature and sign the form.
5. Enter the facility name (if applicable).
6. Enter the facility phone number (if applicable).
7. Enter the date you signed the form.
8. Enter the facility address (if applicable).
9. Enter the name of the care provider (if applicable).
10. Select the care providers (if applicable).
11. If there are other providers, enter their names.
12. Enter any other relevant details.
13. Finally, enter the date you signed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Care Recipient**: Select the type of care recipient from the dropdown menu. Options include "Caregiver," "Care Receiver," or "Other."
* **Responsible Party**: Choose the party responsible for the care from the dropdown menu. Options include "Family Member," "Friend," or "Facility."
* **Policy Date**: If the care recipient has a policy date, enter it here.
* **Signature**: Sign the form to confirm acknowledgment of the policy.
* **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the facility providing care services (if applicable).
* **Facility Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the facility (if applicable).
* **Date Signed**: Enter the date you signed the form.
* **Facility Address**: Enter the address of the facility (if applicable).
* **Care Provider**: Enter the name of the care provider (if applicable).
* **Care Providers**: Select the care providers involved from the dropdown menu (if applicable).
* **Other Providers**: If there are other care providers not listed, enter their names here.
* **Other Details**: Enter any additional information not covered in the previous fields.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date you signed the form.
