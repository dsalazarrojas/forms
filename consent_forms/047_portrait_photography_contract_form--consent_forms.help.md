# Portrait Photography Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Portrait Photography Contract Form is designed to collect contact and agreement information from a client or subject for a portrait photography session. This form helps ensure that all necessary details are covered before, during, and after the session.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form carefully, providing accurate information for each field.
2. Make sure to agree to the terms and conditions of the contract.
3. Sign the contract below to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Enter your contact information, including your name and any other relevant details.
* **Agree** (`terms_of_agreement`, select_multiple, required): Choose to agree to the terms and conditions of the contract. You can select "I agree" or "I do not agree".
* **Sign Below** (`sign_below`, note, required): This is where you will sign the contract.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required): Sign your name in the space provided.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of the session or the date you are completing this form.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, select_one, optional): If you wish to indicate your current status, you can select "Agreed" or "Disagreed".
* **Session Details** (`session_details`, text, optional): Provide any relevant details about the session, such as the location or any specific requirements.
* **Photo Details** (`photo_details`, text, optional): Enter any information about the photo shoot, such as the type of photos or the number of photos to be taken.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, optional): Describe any payment terms or arrangements made for the session.
* **Contract** (`contract_terms`, text, optional): Enter any additional terms or conditions of the contract.
* **Review** (`review`, text, required): Provide any feedback or review of the session.
* **Signature 2** (`signature_2`, text, required): Sign your name again below to confirm your agreement.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, optional): Check your contract status as "In Progress", "Completed", or "Signed".
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required): Add any additional notes or comments about the session.
* **Date 2** (`date_2`, date, optional): Enter any additional dates relevant to the session.
* **Info** (`info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information.
* **Info 2** (`info_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional information.
* **Info 3** (`info_3`, text, optional): Enter any additional information.
* **Notes 2** (`notes_2`, text, required): Add any additional notes or comments about the session.
* **Name 2** (`name_2`, text, optional): Enter your name again.
* **Email** (`email`, text, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Number** (`number`, number, optional): Enter any additional numbers relevant to the session.
* **Status 2** (`status_2`, select_one, optional): Check your payment status as "Paid" or "Unpaid".
* **Version** (`version`, number, optional): Enter the version of the contract.

Note: The required fields are marked with `**required**: true`. Please ensure that all required fields are filled out carefully before submitting the form.
