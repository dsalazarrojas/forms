# Warranty Claim Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Warranty Claim Consent Form is a document that customers must complete to provide their consent and details regarding their warranty claim. This form helps us to understand the claimant's identity, the product details, and their agreement to our terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the warranty claim details on page 1.
2. Provide the claimant's details on page 2.
3. Enter the product details on page 3.
4. Sign and date the form on page 4.
5. Enter your email address and contact number on page 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Warranty Claim Details** (`warranty_claim_consent_form`, text, required): Enter a clear and concise description of your warranty claim, including the reason for the claim and any supporting evidence.
* **Claimant Details** (`claimant_details`, text, optional): Provide the name and contact information of the claimant, as well as any other relevant details such as their relationship to the product.
* **Product Details** (`product_details`, text, optional): Enter the make, model, and serial number of the product associated with the warranty claim.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Sign and date this form to indicate your agreement to the terms and conditions of the warranty claim process.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted regarding your claim.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number where you can be reached regarding your claim.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and truthfully.
- If you have any issues or concerns while completing this form, do not hesitate to contact us.
- This form is only for customers with a valid warranty claim, do not submit it if you have already submitted a claim on this platform.
