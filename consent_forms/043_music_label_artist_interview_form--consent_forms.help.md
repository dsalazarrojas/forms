# Music Label Artist Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect and organize artist information for music labels to efficiently manage artist relations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with the requested information about the artist.
2. Make sure to complete all required fields, if applicable.
3. Provide accurate and up-to-date information about the artist.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist Name** (`artist_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the artist's name as it appears on their official documents or website.
* **Artist Bio** (`artist_bio`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the artist, including their background, experience, and notable works.
* **Artist Website** (`artist_website`, `text`, required: false): Enter the artist's official website or other relevant online presence.
* **Artist Social Media** (`artist_social_media`, `text`, required: false): List the artist's social media profiles (e.g. Instagram, Twitter, Facebook).
* **Artist Contact Email** (`artist_contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the artist's contact email address.
* **Artist Contact Phone** (`artist_contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the artist's contact phone number.
* **Label Contact Email** (`label_contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the label's contact email address (for follow-up communication).
* **Label Contact Phone** (`label_contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the label's contact phone number.
* **Interview Ready** (`interview_ready`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate whether the artist is ready for an interview (True or False).
* **Interview Questions** (`interview_questions`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional questions or information the artist has about the interview.
* **Artist Notes** (`artist_notes`, `text`, required: false): Capture any relevant notes about the artist for future reference.

## Tips

* Be sure to double-check the accuracy of the information you're providing.
* If the artist has changed their name or contact information, update the form accordingly.
* Use this form to help you stay organized and keep track of artist relations efficiently.
