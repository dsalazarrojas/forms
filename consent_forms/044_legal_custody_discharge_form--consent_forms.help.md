# Legal Custody Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the discharge of legal custody responsibilities, providing a clear and concise way to record the necessary information for the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form in its entirety, providing accurate and truthful information for each field.
2. Ensure that you are in a location where the child's information will remain private and secure.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it, ensuring that all fields are complete and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Discharge Date** (`discharge_date_label`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the custody discharge.
  * This is the date on which the custody will be discharged.
  * Format: MM/DD/YY (e.g., 01/01/2024)
- **Discharge Time** (`discharge_time_label`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the custody discharge.
  * This is the time at which the custody will be discharged.
  * Format: HH:MM AM/PM (e.g., 08:00 AM)
- **Discharge Location** (`discharge_location_label`, text, required/optional): Enter the location where the custody discharge will take place.
  * This is the physical location where the child will be discharged.
  * Example: Home, school, or a specific address.
- **Parent One Name** (`parent_one_name_label`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of Parent One.
  * This is the name of the parent or guardian relinquishing custody.
- **Parent Two Name** (`parent_two_label`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of Parent Two.
  * This is the name of the other parent or guardian (if applicable).
- **Parent One Signature** (`parent_one_signature_label`, note, required/optional): Enter the signature of Parent One.
  * This is a confirmation of the parent's agreement to the custody discharge.
  * Format: A typed signature with date.
- **Parent Two Signature** (`parent_two_signature_label`, note, required/optional): Enter the signature of Parent Two.
  * This is a confirmation of the other parent's agreement to the custody discharge.
  * Format: A typed signature with date.

## Tips
* Ensure that both parents/guardians sign the form if they are both present.
* If only one parent is present, ensure they sign as "Parent One".
* Keep a record of the form for future reference, if necessary.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of this form, consult with a legal professional.
