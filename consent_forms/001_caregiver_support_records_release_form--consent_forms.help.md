# Caregiver Support Records Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain authorization from a caregiver to release medical records of their patient. It ensures that the patient's confidentiality and rights are respected while enabling authorized personnel to access necessary medical information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out all required fields carefully and accurately**. Ensure you provide complete and up-to-date information about yourself and the patient.
2. **Review and select the correct authorization options** for the records to be released. This is crucial for the accuracy and relevance of the information shared.
3. **Specify the healthcare providers you wish to share the records with**. This helps ensure that the records are accessible to authorized individuals or institutions.
4. **Select the correct purpose of release**. This helps maintain transparency about how the records will be used.
5. **Choose the correct duration for the authorization**. This ensures the records are released only for a specific period.
6. **Confirm your authorization** by selecting the correct option to indicate your agreement to release the records.
7. **Sign and date the form** once you have completed all necessary sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Caregiver Full Name**: Enter your complete legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Phone Number**: Include your area code for accurate contact.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for secure communication.
* **Relationship to Patient**: Select the nature of your relationship to the patient (e.g., Parent, Spouse, Adult Child, Sibling, Extended Family, or Other).
* **Patient Full Name**: Enter the patient's complete legal name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Patient Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Medical Record Number**: If known, enter the patient's medical record number.
* **Records to Release**: Select all the medical records categories you wish to release, such as Medical Records, Mental Health Records, Prescription History, Lab Results, Imaging Reports, Consultation Notes, or Treatment Plans.
* **Healthcare Providers to Share With**: List the names and specialties of healthcare professionals or institutions you wish to authorize to access the released records.
* **Purpose of Release**: Indicate the reason for releasing the records to ensure transparency.
* **Release Records From Date**: Enter the start date from which the records should be released (inclusive).
* **Release Records To Date**: Enter the end date until which the records should be released (inclusive).
* **Authorization Validity**: Choose the duration for which the authorization remains valid (30 days, 60 days, 90 days, 6 months, 1 year, or until revoked by the caregiver).
* **Consent Confirmation**: Confirm that you are authorized to release the records by selecting the appropriate option.
* **Date of Consent**: Enter the date on which you signed the form in MM/DD/YYYY format.

By following these steps and guidelines, caregivers can ensure the accurate and secure release of medical records, maintaining patient confidentiality and rights while allowing authorized access to necessary information.
