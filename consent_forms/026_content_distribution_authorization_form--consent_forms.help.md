# Content Distribution Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain authorization for the distribution of content to various parties. It outlines the terms and conditions of the distribution, including the content description, distribution channels, geographic scope, and duration of the authorization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and honest information.
2. Make sure to review all fields carefully before submitting the form.
3. Double-check the fields for any errors or inconsistencies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Authorizing Party Name** (`authorizing_party_name`, text, required): Enter the full name or company name granting authorization.
* **Authorizing Party Email** (`authorizing_party_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the authorizing party.
* **Authorizing Party Phone** (`authorizing_party_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact number of the authorizing party.
* **Authorized Party Name** (`authorized_party_name`, text, required): Enter the full name or company receiving authorization.
* **Authorized Party Email** (`authorized_party_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the authorized party.
* **Authorization Date** (`authorization_date`, date, required): Enter the date of this authorization.
* **Authorization Type** (`authorization_type`, select one, required): Choose the type of authorization (Exclusive Distribution, Non-Exclusive Distribution, Limited Distribution, Digital Distribution Only, Print Distribution Only, Full Distribution Rights).
* **Content Description** (`content_description`, text, required): Describe the content being authorized.
* **Content Title** (`content_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the content.
* **Content Type** (`content_type`, select one, required): Choose the format of the content (Article, Video, Podcast, Image, Infographic, E-book, White Paper, Multiple Formats).
* **Authorized Distribution Channels** (`distribution_channels`, select multiple, required): Choose the distribution channels (Website, Social Media, Email Newsletter, Print Publication, Television, Radio, Streaming Platforms, Third-Party Websites).
* **Geographic Scope** (`geographic_scope`, select one, required): Choose the geographic scope (Local Only, Regional, National, International, Worldwide).
* **Authorization Duration** (`authorization_duration`, select one, required): Choose the duration of the authorization (3 Months, 6 Months, 1 Year, 3 Years, 5 Years, Perpetual).
* **Authorization Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date when authorization begins.
* **Authorization End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the date when authorization ends.
* **Modification Rights** (`modification_rights`, select one, required): Choose the modification rights (No Modifications Allowed, Minor Edits Allowed, Significant Edits Allowed, Full Modification Rights).
* **Attribution Required** (`attribution_required`, select one, required): Choose if attribution is required (Yes, Full Attribution, Yes, Partial Attribution, No Attribution Required).
* **Compensation Terms** (`compensation_terms`, select one, required): Choose the compensation terms (No Compensation, One-Time Fee, Royalty Based, Revenue Share, Trade Agreement).
* **Compensation Amount** (`compensation_amount`, number, optional): Enter the compensation amount if applicable.
* **Exclusivity Terms** (`exclusivity_terms`, select one, required): Choose if this authorization is exclusive (Exclusive, Non-Exclusive, Semi-Exclusive).
* **Termination Conditions** (`termination_conditions`, text, required): Enter any conditions under which this authorization can be terminated.
* **Additional Terms** (`additional_terms`, text, optional): Enter any other terms or conditions.
* **Authorization Confirmed** (`authorization_confirmed`, select one, required): Confirm if you authorize this distribution agreement (Yes, I Authorize, No, I Do Not Authorize).
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of authorization.

## Tips
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled accurately and honestly.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the relevant party or authority.
* This form is a critical document that protects the creators of the content and the authorized parties.
