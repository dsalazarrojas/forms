# Genetic Testing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect the patient's consent for genetic testing. The patient must agree or decline to proceed with the genetic testing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out all required fields, marked as "required".
2. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the patient's name and contact information.
* **Testing Details** (`testing_details`, text, required: false):
	+ Describe the type of genetic testing to be performed.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required: true):
	+ Select "I agree to proceed with genetic testing." or "I do not agree to proceed with genetic testing."
* **Genetic Testing Procedure** (`genetic_testing_procedure`, text, required: false):
	+ Describe the procedure of the genetic testing to be performed, if any.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you carefully read and understand the consent options before making a selection.
- Make sure to enter accurate and complete patient information to enable efficient communication with the patient.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your healthcare professional for assistance.
