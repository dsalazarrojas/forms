# Sugaring Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about the client's preferences and medical history before a sugaring consultation. This helps the practitioner understand the client's needs and ensure a safe and effective treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the fields as follows:
- Please answer all the questions honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
- You can add or remove pages as needed.
- If a field is marked as required, please ensure it is completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Preferences** (`Client Preferences`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to capture any personal preferences or allergies you may have, such as skin sensitivities or preferred treatment areas. Please provide any relevant information to help the practitioner tailor the treatment to your needs.
- **Medical History** (`Medical History`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to collect any relevant medical history that may impact the treatment, such as previous medical conditions or allergies. Please provide as much detail as possible to help the practitioner ensure your safety.
- **Medical Conditions** (`Medical Conditions`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to capture any existing medical conditions that may affect the treatment, such as diabetes, epilepsy, or skin conditions. Please be honest about any conditions that may impact the treatment.

## Tips
- Please be honest and thorough when filling in the form to ensure the practitioner has a complete picture of your needs and requirements.
- If you're unsure about any of the questions, please ask the practitioner for guidance.
- Remember to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure you've provided all the necessary information.
