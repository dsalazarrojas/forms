# Scuba Diving Medical Clearance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to ensure that the individual is healthy and fit for scuba diving activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in all the required fields with your relevant information. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction (Page 1)**: This is where you'll start your form. Please introduce yourself and briefly mention the purpose of the form.
* **Has Medical Condition (Page 2)**: Answer this question with either 'No' or 'Yes'. If you have a medical condition, you'll be asked to provide more details in the 'Medical Condition Details' field.
* **Medical Condition Details (Page 3)**: If you answered 'Yes' to the previous question, please provide a detailed description of your medical condition.
* **Meds (Page 4)**: Please indicate if you take any medications, including those that are 'As needed'. If you have any medications, you'll be asked to list them in the 'Meds List' field.
* **Allergies (Page 5)**: If you have any allergies, please describe them here.
* **Medical History (Page 6)**: If you have any medical history, please describe it here.
* **Height and Weight (Page 7)**: Please enter your height in centimeters and weight in kilograms. 
* **Height (in cm) (Page 8)**: Please enter your height in centimeters.
* **Weight (in kg) (Page 9)**: Please enter your weight in kilograms.
* **Blood Pressure (Page 10)**: Please enter your blood pressure reading. 
* **Pulse (Page 11)**: Please enter your pulse reading.
* **Blood Oxygen Saturation (Page 12)**: Please enter your blood oxygen saturation reading.
* **Heart Rate (Page 13)**: Please enter your heart rate reading.
* **Respiratory Rate (Page 14)**: Please enter your respiratory rate reading.
* **Blood Temperature (Page 15)**: Please enter your blood temperature reading.
* **Other Medical Conditions (Page 16)**: If you have any other medical conditions, please describe them here.
* **Height (in inches) (Page 17)**: Please enter your height in inches.
* **Weight (in Lbs) (Page 18)**: Please enter your weight in pounds.
* **Meds List (Page 19)**: If you take any medications, list them here.
* **Emergency Contact (Page 20)**: Please provide the name and contact details of an emergency contact person.
* **Doctor Name (Page 21)**: If you have seen a doctor for a medical checkup, please provide their name.
* **Doctor Phone (Page 22)**: If you have seen a doctor for a medical checkup, please provide their phone number.
* **Doctor Contact (Page 23)**: If you have seen a doctor for a medical checkup, please provide their contact details.
* **Last Dive Date (Page 24)**: Please enter the date of your last scuba diving activity. 

Please fill in all required fields to ensure that your medical clearance is valid.
