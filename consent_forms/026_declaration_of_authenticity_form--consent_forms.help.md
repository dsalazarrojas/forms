# declaration_of_authenticity_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to declare their authenticity and provide personal details for verification purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all necessary information before starting the form.
2. Fill in your first name, last name, and email address in their respective fields.
3. Enter your phone number.
4. Add your signature, declaration of authenticity, and date of birth (if applicable).
5. Select your nationality and country of residence from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name in this field. It will be used for verification purposes.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name in this field. It will be used for verification purposes.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address in this field. It will be used for contact and verification purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in this field. It will be used for communication and verification purposes.
* **Signature**: Sign your declaration of authenticity in this field. It should be your original handwritten or printed signature.
* **Declaration of Authenticity**: Describe your declaration of authenticity in this field. Please be clear and concise about your statement.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth (if applicable) in this field. It will be used for age verification purposes.
* **Nationality**: Select your nationality from the provided options. Please choose one that reflects your true identity.
*   *Nationality Options*: Select all nationalities that apply to you.
*   *Country of Residence*: Select your country of residence from the provided options. Please choose one that reflects your current location.
*   *Country of Residence Options*: Select all countries of residence that apply to you.
