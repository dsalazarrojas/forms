# Peptide Therapy Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain the consent of a patient for peptide therapy treatment. It ensures that the patient understands and accepts the treatment plan and any associated conditions or risks involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your answer to the question of whether you will undergo peptide therapy. Please select "Yes" if you are planning to undergo peptide therapy, or "No" if you are not.
2. Enter your patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
3. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
4. Select your medical condition from the list provided in the "Medical Condition" field.
5. Indicate whether you have a treatment plan in place for your condition. Please select "Yes" if you have a treatment plan, or "No" if you do not.
6. Indicate whether you have provided informed consent for your treatment. Please select "Yes" if you have provided informed consent, or "No" if you have not.
7. Provide your e-signature in the "e-Signature" field.
8. Enter the date and time of your treatment in the "Date" and "Time" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Peptide Therapy Form**: Select "Yes" if you are planning to undergo peptide therapy, or "No" if you are not.
* **Patient Name**: Enter the name of the patient undergoing treatment.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of the patient or their representative.
* **Medical Condition**: Select the medical condition you are treating or have concerns about.
* **Treatment Plan**: Select "Yes" if you have a treatment plan in place, or "No" if you do not.
* **Informed Consent**: Select "Yes" if you have provided informed consent, or "No" if you have not.
* **e-Signature**: Provide your electronic signature, which confirms that you have reviewed and understood the treatment plan.
* **Date**: Enter the date of your treatment.
* **Time**: Enter the time of your treatment.
