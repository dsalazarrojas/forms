# Performing Arts Terms Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to acknowledge that you have read and understand the terms and conditions for participating in performing arts activities. It is essential to complete this form to ensure that you are aware of your rights and responsibilities as a participant.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Read each field carefully and make sure you understand what is being asked.
2. Fill out the required fields marked as "required: true" (although most fields on this form are not required).
3. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose the correct answer from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Rehearsal Terms** (`rehearsal_terms`, text, not required): A space to enter any additional terms related to rehearsals.
- **Class Terms** (`class_terms`, text, not required): A space to enter any additional terms related to classes.
- **Production Terms** (`production_terms`, text, not required): A space to enter any additional terms related to productions.
- **Policy Acceptance** (`policy_acceptance`, note, not required): This field is not applicable as it is a type of "note" and does not have any fields to fill out.
- **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`, select_one, not required): A field to indicate whether you consent to participate in the performing arts activities. Choose "Yes" if you consent and "No" if you do not.
- **Participant Consent Rehearsal** (`participant_consent_rehearsal`, select_multiple, not required): A field to indicate whether you consent to participate in rehearsals. Choose all that apply.
- **Participant Consent Class** (`participant_consent_class`, select_multiple, not required): A field to indicate whether you consent to participate in classes. Choose all that apply.
- **Participant Consent Production** (`participant_consent_production`, select_multiple, not required): A field to indicate whether you consent to participate in productions. Choose all that apply.
- **Studios, Schools, and Programs** (`studioschoolsprograms`, select_one, not required): A field to select whether you are affiliated with studios, schools, or programs in the performing arts industry. Choose "Yes" if you are and "No" if you are not.
- **Participant Consent Organizations** (`participant_consent_organizations`, select_multiple, not required): A field to indicate whether you consent to participate in organizations in the performing arts industry. Choose all that apply.
- **Participant Consent Organizations 2** (`participant_consent_organizations_2`, select_one, not required): A field to select whether you are associated with organizations in the performing arts industry. Choose "Yes" if you are and "No" if you are not.
- **Participant Consent Organizations 3** (`participant_consent_organizations_3`, select_multiple, not required): A field to indicate whether you consent to participate in multiple organizations in the performing arts industry. Choose all that apply.
- **Participant Consent Organizations 4** (`participant_consent_organizations_4`, select_multiple, not required): A field to indicate whether you consent to participate in multiple organizations in the performing arts industry. Choose all that apply.
- **Participant Consent Organizations 5** (`participant_consent_organizations_5`, select_one, not required): A field to select whether you are associated with multiple organizations in the performing arts industry. Choose "Yes" if you are and "No" if you are not.
