# Pre Test Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Test Consent Form is used to obtain informed consent from participants before conducting a test. It ensures that participants understand the nature of the test, potential risks and benefits, and agree to participate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Participant Name**, **Participant Email**, and **Participant Phone** if applicable.
2. Provide a brief description of the **Test Description**.
3. Confirm that you have been **Given Informed Consent** by ticking the box.
4. Select the **Test Start Date** and **Test End Date** if the test has a specified timeframe.
5. Fill in the **Test Duration** if applicable.
6. Specify the **Test Location** where the test will be conducted.
7. Confirm that your **Test Supervisor** is aware of your participation and will be supervising the test.
8. Fill in your **Participant Declaration**, which is a statement of your understanding of the test.
9. Provide a **Participant Declaration Signature**, which is a signature confirming your understanding of the test.
10. Confirm that your **Participant Declaration** is signed by ticking the box.
11. If you are a **Test Supervisor**, you are required to fill in your **Test Supervisor Declaration**, confirm your signature, and provide any **Test Supervisor Notes**.
12. If you are a participant, confirm that your **Participant Declaration Supervisor** is aware of your participation and has declared their supervision.
