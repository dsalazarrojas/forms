# microgrant_applicant_data_usage_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect microgrant applicant data usage consent and agreement information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

* Complete the form in its entirety if you are consenting to the use of your data.
* If you do not want to provide your consent, please fill in the "I do not give my consent" option in the "Consent" field.
* If you need to provide contact information, please select the relevant option(s) in the "Contact Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction (Page 1)**
	+ This is an introductory page that provides a brief overview of the form and its purpose.
* **Applicant Information (Page 2)**
	+ This page asks for some information about the applicant. Please enter the necessary details in this field.
* **Consent (Page 3)**
	+ This field asks for your consent to use your data. Please select one of the two options provided:
		- I give my consent
		- I do not give my consent
* **Agreement (Page 4)**
	+ This field is for any additional agreements or conditions related to the use of your data.
* **Data Collected (Page 5)**
	+ This page provides a brief overview of the data that will be collected.
* **Contact Information (Page 6)**
	+ This field asks for your preferred contact method. Please select one or more of the following options:
		- Home
		- Work
		- Email
		- Phone
* **Data Usage (Page 7)**
	+ This field is for you to specify how you want your data to be used.
* **Submit (Page 8)**
	+ This is the submit button. Please review your answers before submitting the form.
* **Error (Page 9)**
	+ This field is not applicable, as it is a placeholder for potential errors.
* **Email (Page 10)**
	+ This field is for you to enter your email address.
* **Phone (Page 11)**
	+ This field is for you to enter your phone number.

Note: Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date contact information to ensure that we can get in touch with you regarding your microgrant application.
