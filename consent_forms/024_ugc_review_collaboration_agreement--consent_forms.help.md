# UGC Review Collaboration Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The UGC Review Collaboration Agreement form is a tool used to collect information from our users who are part of the review collaboration process. The form is essential for ensuring that our internal processes are followed correctly and for us to effectively collaborate with our users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "Brand" field. This is the name of the brand you are representing.
2. In the "Campaign Info" field, provide a brief description of the campaign you are working on.
3. Choose "Yes" or "No" in the "Creator" field to indicate whether you are the creator of the content or not.
4. In the "Agency" field, provide the name of your agency or the name of the company you are working with.
5. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
6. If applicable, enter the date you have agreed upon with us in the "Agreement Date" field.
7. Set the "Deadline Date" to the date you are expecting to complete your part of the review collaboration.
8. In the "Submitter" field, enter your name as it appears on the agreement you signed.
9. Enter your email address in the "Submitter Email" field.
10. Enter your phone number in the "Submitter Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Brand**: The name of the brand you are representing.
* **Campaign Info**: A brief description of the campaign you are working on.
* **Creator**: Indicate if you are the creator of the content ("Yes" or "No").
* **Agency**: The name of your agency or the name of the company you are working with.
* **Contact Info**: Your contact information, including your name, address, phone number, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Agreement Date**: The date you have agreed upon with us (if applicable).
* **Deadline Date**: The date you are expecting to complete your part of the review collaboration.
* **Submitter**: Your name as it appears on the agreement you signed.
* **Submitter Email**: Your email address.
* **Submitter Phone**: Your phone number.

Note: All fields marked as "required" must be filled out for the form to be submitted.
