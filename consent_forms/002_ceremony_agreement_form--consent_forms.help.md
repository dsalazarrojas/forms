# Ceremony Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ceremony Agreement Form is a document used to collect information and obtain agreements from clients for a wedding or other celebratory event. It ensures that all necessary details are captured for the event's organization and execution.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2.  Select the correct type of ceremony from the provided options.
3.  Enter your full name and email address as the main point of contact.
4.  Provide your phone number for communication purposes.
5.  Select the planned date and start time of the ceremony.
6.  Choose a venue and enter its full address.
7.  Estimate the expected guest count for the event.
8.  Review and approve the ceremony script, ensuring you're aware of the terms.
9.  Provide permission for photography and video recordings.
10.  Agree to the deposit and payment terms.
11.  Acknowledge the cancellation policy.
12.  Sign the agreement with the current date.
13.  Optionally, include any additional comments or requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Client Name** (text, required): Enter your full name as the main point of contact for the event.
* **Email Address** (email, required): Provide your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Type of Ceremony** (select one, required): Select the category of your event from the provided options.
* **Date of the Ceremony** (date, required): Enter the planned date of the ceremony.
* **Ceremony Start Time** (time, required): Specify the start time of the ceremony.
* **Venue Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the venue where the ceremony will be held.
* **Venue Address** (text, required): Provide the full address of the venue.
* **Estimated Guest Count** (number, required): Estimate the number of people attending the event.
* **Script Review and Approval** (select one, required): Review and approve the ceremony script at least 14 days prior to the event.
* **Photography and Video Permission** (select one, required): Decide whether to grant permission for photography and video recordings.
* **Deposit and Payment Agreement** (select one, required): Acknowledge and agree to the 50% non-refundable deposit for securing the date.
* **Cancellation Policy Acknowledgement** (select one, required): Read and agree to the cancellation policy in the ceremony packet.
* **Agreement Signature Date** (date, required): Sign the agreement with the current date.
* **Additional Comments or Requests** (text, optional): Include any special requests or information for the coordinators.
