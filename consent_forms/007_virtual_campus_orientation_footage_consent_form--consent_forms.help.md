# Virtual Campus Orientation Footage Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to gather participant information for the Virtual Campus Orientation Footage Consent Form. It is meant to be completed by participants to provide their consent for the use of their footage. Please note that this form is for informational purposes only and should not be used for commercial purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Start by selecting the "Participant Information" page, where you can choose to provide your:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Email
	* Email Confirmation
2. On the "Video Consent" page, indicate your consent status:
	* True: You grant permission for your footage to be used.
	* False: You decline permission for your footage to be used.
	* Declined: You choose not to answer.
3. On the "Date of Birth" page, enter your date of birth.
4. On the "Upload Video" page, select the upload time for your footage.
5. On the "Comments" page, provide any additional comments or notes you may want to share with us.
6. On the "Contact Us" page, provide your contact information for any further questions or concerns.
7. Finally, on the "Additional Comments" page, provide any additional comments or notes you may want to share with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information (First Name, select, optional)**: Select your first name from the list.
* **Participant Information (Last Name, select, optional)**: Select your last name from the list.
* **Participant Information (Email, select, optional)**: Select your email address from the list.
* **Participant Information (Email Confirmation, select, optional)**: Select your email confirmation status.
* **Video Consent (Video Consent, select multiple, optional)**: Choose whether you grant or decline permission for your footage to be used.
* **Date of Birth (Date of Birth, date, optional)**: Enter your date of birth in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Upload Video (Upload Video, time, optional)**: Select the upload time for your footage.
* **Comments (Comments, note, optional)**: Provide any additional comments or notes you may want to share with us.
* **Contact Us (Contact Us, text, optional)**: Provide your contact information for any further questions or concerns.
* **Additional Comments (Additional Comments, text, optional)**: Provide any additional comments or notes you may want to share with us.
