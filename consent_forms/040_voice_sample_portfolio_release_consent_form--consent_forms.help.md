# Voice Sample Portfolio Release Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Voice Sample Portfolio Release Consent Form is a legal document used to release voice samples or portfolios to clients, agents, or managers. This form ensures that artists agree to the terms of the release and provides the necessary contact information for future communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the release, a brief description of the portfolio, and the category of the release.
2. Select the date and date signed for the release.
3. Enter your name and signature, as well as your contact information (phone, address, company, title, and role).
4. Confirm that you have given your consent to the release terms by selecting "True" for the "Consent" field.
5. Confirm that you have signed the release by selecting "True" for the "Signed" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. Voice Sample Portfolio Release Consent Form** (`title`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief title for the release.
* **2. Category** (`category`, select_one, required/optional): Select the category of the release, such as "Category".
* **3. Description** (`description`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the portfolio.
* **4. Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the release.
* **5. Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you signed the release.
* **6. Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name.
* **7. Signed By** (`name_signed`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as it should appear on the release.
* **8. Signature** (`signature`, note, required/optional): This field is intended for your physical or digital signature. Please enter your signature as per your preference.
* **9. Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **10. Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **11. Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter your address.
* **12. Company** (`company`, text, required/optional): Enter your company name.
* **13. Title** (`title_2`, text, required/optional): Enter your job title.
* **14. Role** (`role`, text, required/optional): Enter your role or position.
* **15. Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **16. Consent** (`consent`, select_multiple, required): Confirm that you have given your consent to the release terms by selecting "True".
* **17. Signed** (`consent_signed`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have signed the release by selecting "True".
* **18. Date Signed Consent** (`date_signed_consent`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you signed the consent.
* **19. Name Signed Con** (`name_signed_con`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as it should appear on the signed release.
* **20. Signature Signed** (`signature_signed`, note, required/optional): This field is intended for your signature as per your preference.
* **21. Email Signed Con** (`email_signed_con`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address as it should appear on the signed release.
* **22. Contact Phone Signed** (`contact_phone_signed`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number as it should appear on the signed release.
* **23. Address Signed** (`address_signed`, text, required/optional): Enter your address as it should appear on the signed release.
* **24. Company Signed** (`company_signed`, text, required/optional): Enter your company name as it should appear on the signed release.
* **25. Title Signed** (`title_signed`, text, required/optional): Enter your job title as it should appear on the signed release.

## Tips
* Make sure to review each field carefully to ensure you have provided accurate information.
* Ensure that you have signed the release with your correct name and date.
* Review the terms of the release carefully before confirming your consent.
