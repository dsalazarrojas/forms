# Soccer Camp Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Soccer Camp Waiver Form is designed to collect information from participants to ensure their safety and well-being during the soccer camp. This form is used to gather essential details such as contact information, medical history, and consent for medical treatment in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your personal information, including your first name, last name, email address, and phone number.
2. If you have any emergency contact information, provide the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency and their relationship to you.
3. If you have any medical concerns or conditions, describe them in the "Health Concerns" field.
4. List any medications you are currently taking, if applicable.
5. Finally, select the appropriate options under "Consent" to confirm your understanding and agreement with the terms of the waiver.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name is the first name you are known by.
* **Last Name**: Your last name is the last name you are known by.
* **Email**: Enter your email address that we can use to contact you during the camp.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number where we can reach you in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Person**: If you have an emergency contact person, enter their name here. This is someone we can contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: If applicable, describe your relationship to the emergency contact person.
* **Health Concerns**: If you have any medical concerns or conditions that may affect your participation in the camp, describe them here.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions you may have, if applicable.
* **Allergies**: If you have any allergies, list them here.
* **Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking, if applicable.
* **Consent**: Select all of the following options to confirm your understanding and agreement with the terms of the waiver. 
  *   Soccer Camp Waiver: I understand and agree to the terms of the waiver, including the assumption of risk and the release of liability.
  *   Waiver of Liability: I understand and agree to release any liability for injuries or damages sustained during the soccer camp.
  *   Release of Medical Information: I understand and agree to allow the camp to release any medical information to emergency services or medical personnel in case of an emergency.
