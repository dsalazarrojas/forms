# Social Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Social Agreement Form is a document used to collect information from participants and event organizers to establish a mutual understanding of expectations and guidelines for events. This form is essential for ensuring that all parties involved are aware of their roles, responsibilities, and communication channels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Please fill out the form in English (default language).
*   Ensure you have a clear and accurate understanding of each field's purpose.
*   Read the field hints carefully to ensure you provide the correct information.
*   Complete all required fields (marked as `required: true`).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Participants** (< participants_details, type: note, required: false>): A section for you to provide any additional participant information. This is an optional field.
*   **Event** (<event_details, type: text, required: true>): Enter the name and date of the event. Please ensure you provide accurate information.
*   **Agreements** (<agreements, type: select_multiple, required: true>): List all agreements and expectations related to the event, such as rules, regulations, or guidelines.
*   **Expectations** (<expectations, type: select_multiple, required: true>): List all expectations you have for the event participants, such as behavior, attire, or equipment requirements.
*   **Communication Channels** (<communication_channels, type: select_one, required: true>): Choose your preferred communication method:
    *   SMS: Receive updates and notifications via SMS.
    *   Email: Receive updates and notifications via email.
    *   Phone: Be contacted directly via phone.
*   **Emergency Contact** (<emergency_contact, type: text, required: true>): Enter the name of a contact person in case of an emergency.
*   **Phone** (<emergency_phone, type: number, required: true>): Enter the contact person's phone number.
*   **Address** (<emergency_address, type: text, required: true>): Enter the address of the contact person.
*   **Participant Comments** (<participant_comments, type: note, required: false>): A section for you to provide any additional comments as a participant. This is an optional field.
*   **Event Comments** (<event_comments, type: note, required: false>): A section for the event organizer to provide any additional comments about the event. This is an optional field.
