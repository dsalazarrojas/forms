# smart_thermostat_program_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Smart Thermostat Program Consent Form is a document that requires the user to provide their name, contact information, and consent to participate in the smart thermostat program. The form also asks for details about their energy efficiency programs, thermostat installation, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*). These include:
	* Name
	* Phone number
	* Email address
	* Date
	* Time
	* Energy Efficiency Programs (select 'Yes' or 'No')
	* Location
	* Smart Thermostat Installation Date
	* Thermostat Model
	* Enrollment Process
	* Program Start Date
	* Program End Date
	* Assigned Tool
	* Notes
2. Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (\*)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Phone (\*)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Email (\*)**: Enter your email address.
* **Date (\*)**: Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time (\*)**: Enter the time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Energy Efficiency Programs (\*)**: Select 'Yes' if you are currently participating in any energy efficiency programs, or 'No' if you are not.
* **Location (\*)**: Enter the location where your smart thermostat is installed.
* **Smart Thermostat Installation Date (\*)**: Enter the date your smart thermostat was installed.
* **Thermostat Model (\*)**: Enter the model of your smart thermostat.
* **Enrollment Process (\*)**: Describe the process you used to enroll in the smart thermostat program.
* **Program Start Date (\*)**: Enter the date you started the program.
* **Program End Date (\*)**: Enter the date you plan to end the program.
* **Assigned Tool (\*)**: Enter the tool assigned to you for the smart thermostat program.
* **Notes (\*)**: Enter any additional information or comments about your participation in the smart thermostat program.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the program administrators for assistance.
* Please note that the form requires your consent to participate in the smart thermostat program. By completing this form, you are providing your consent to participate.
