# Financial Counseling Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Counseling Consent Form is a document that ensures both the client and the financial professional are aware of their roles and responsibilities in the financial counseling process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with accurate and truthful information.
2. Ensure you have the client's consent before proceeding with financial counseling services.
3. If you are a financial professional, sign and date the document to confirm your understanding of the client's consent.
