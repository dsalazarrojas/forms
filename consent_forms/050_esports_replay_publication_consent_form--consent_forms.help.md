# Esports Replay Publication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Esports Replay Publication Consent Form is a document that is used to obtain consent from participants to publish their replay footage. It is designed for use when capturing or publishing esports replays and helps to establish a clear record of the participant's consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the `Participant Details` page with the required information.
* On the `Replay Publication Consent` page, indicate your consent for the replay publication.
* On the `Digital Signature` page, add your digital signature.
* On the `Date & Time` page, select the date and time when you are completing the form.
* On the `Date Signed` page, mark the date you are signing.
* On the `Participant Signature` page, add your handwritten or digital signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details (Participant Details)** (`text`, optional):
	+ Fill out this section with your details as a participant in the event.
* **Replay Publication Consent (Replay Publication Consent)** (`text`, optional):
	+ Check the box to indicate your consent for the replay publication.
* **Digital Signature (Digital Signature)** (`date`, optional):
	+ Add your digital signature as a proof of identity verification.
* **Date & Time (Date & Time)** (`date`, optional):
	+ Select the date and time when you are completing the form.
* **Date Signed (Date Signed)** (`date`, optional):
	+ Mark the date you are signing the form.
* **Participant Signature (Participant Signature)** (`text`, optional):
	+ Add your handwritten or digital signature as a proof of identity verification.
