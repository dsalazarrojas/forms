# Authority For Salary Sacrifice Deduction Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain authorization from an employee for a salary sacrifice deduction. A salary sacrifice deduction is an arrangement where an employee agrees to sacrifice a portion of their salary to a third-party provider, such as a pension scheme or a savings account. This form ensures that the employee understands and consents to the terms of the arrangement, which includes the amount and frequency of the deduction, as well as any tax implications.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Form Date: The date the form is being completed.
	* Employee Full Name: The employee's full name.
	* Employee ID Number: The employee's identification number.
	* Employee Email: The employee's work email address.
	* Department Name: The department the employee belongs to.
	* Direct Manager Name: The employee's supervisor's name.
	* Salary Payment Frequency: The frequency at which the employee is paid (e.g., weekly, biweekly, monthly, annual).
2. Select the correct options:
	* Salary Sacrifice Scheme Type: The type of scheme being used (e.g., childcare vouchers, cycle scheme, car lease, etc.).
	* Specific Scheme Name: The name of the scheme being used.
	* Scheme Start Date: The date the scheme starts.
	* Scheme Duration: The length of time the scheme will be in effect (e.g., fixed for 12 months, ongoing until cancelled).
	* Scheme End Date (if fixed): If the scheme has a fixed end date, enter the date.
3. Confirm understanding:
	* Understand Tax Benefits: Confirm that the employee understands the tax implications of the arrangement.
	* National Insurance Impact Understood: Confirm that the employee understands the impact on NI contributions.
	* Impact on Pension Contributions: Confirm that the employee understands the impact on pension contributions.
	* Understand Termination Process: Confirm that the employee understands the process to cancel or terminate the arrangement.
	* Cancellation Notice Period Required: If applicable, select the notice period required to cancel the arrangement.
	* Alternative Arrangements Considered: Confirm if alternative options have been considered.
	* Financial Advice Received: Confirm if professional advice has been sought.
4. Authorize and declare:
	* Authorization and Declaration: Confirm that the employee authorizes and declares that the information provided is accurate.
