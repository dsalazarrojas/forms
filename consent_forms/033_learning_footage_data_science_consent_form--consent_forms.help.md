# Learning Footage Data Science Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information and gain consent for the use of footage data in research or training projects. This form is used by data science teams and individuals to obtain consent for the use of footage data in their projects. Please fill out this form to indicate your consent for the use of your footage data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Choose "Yes" or "No" for the education teams questions.
2. Enter your name and other contact information in the "Name," "Email," "Phone," and "Department" fields.
3. Choose "Yes" or "No" for the consent to use and record purpose questions.
4. Sign and date the form by entering your signature and consent date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Education Teams**: Choose "Yes" if you are a part of an education team.
	+ If you choose "Yes," please select all relevant teams from the provided list.
* **Researcher**: This field is required to be filled out by all team members.
* **Data Scientist**: This field is optional, but please fill it out if you are a data scientist.
* **Project Details**: This field is optional, but please describe the project you are working on.
* **Consent to Use**: Choose "Yes" to indicate your consent for the use of your footage data.
* **Training or Evaluation Project?**: This field is optional, but please select "Yes" if the project is for training or evaluation purposes.
* **Only for Analytics**: This field is optional, but please select "Yes" if the footage data is only for analytics purposes.
* **Record Purpose**: Choose "Yes" to indicate you are aware that the footage data will be recorded.
* **Other (please specify)**: If you chose "Yes" for "Record Purpose," please provide a brief explanation of the record purpose.
* **Record Retention**: Choose "Yes" to indicate you are aware of the record retention policy.
* **Record Deletion**: This field is optional, but please select "Yes" if you are aware of the record deletion policy.
* **Record Disposal**: This field is optional, but please select "Yes" if you are aware of the record disposal policy.
* **Data Retention**: This field is optional, but please select "Yes" if you are aware of the data retention policy.
* **Data Deletion**: This field is optional, but please select "Yes" if you are aware of the data deletion policy.
* **Consent Date**: This field is required and must be filled out with the current date.
* **Signature**: This field is required and must be signed by the team member or individual filling out the form.
* **Notes**: This field is optional, but please provide any additional notes about your project.
* **Email**: This field is optional, but please provide your email address.
* **Phone**: This field is optional, but please provide your phone number.
* **Name**: This field is required and must be filled out with the name of the team member or individual filling out the form.
* **Department**: This field is optional, but please provide your department information.
* **Job Title**: This field is optional, but please provide your job title.
* **Organization**: This field is optional, but please provide your organization information.
* **Contact Info**: This field is optional, but please provide your contact information.
* **Terms of Service**: This field is optional, but please acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms of service.

Please note that all fields marked as required are mandatory and must be filled out.
