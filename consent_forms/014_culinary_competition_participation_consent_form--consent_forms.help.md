# Culinary Competition Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants in a culinary competition. It is a required field to be filled out by all participants.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name in the "participant name" field.
2.  Enter your email address in the "email" field.
3.  Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
4.  Provide your participant details in the "participant details" field.
5.  Choose your consent to participate in the "consent to participate" field.
6.  If you have any dietary restrictions, enter them in the "participant dietary restrictions" field.
7.  If you have any special instructions, enter them in the "participant special instructions" field.
8.  Choose your preferred method of contact in the "contact preference" field.
9.  Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact person in the "emergency contact name", "emergency contact phone", "emergency contact relation", and "emergency contact address" fields.
10. Enter your participant address, zip code, state, city, and country in the "participant address", "participant zip", "participant state", "participant city", and "participant country" fields.
11. Enter your date of birth in the "participant date of birth" field.
12. Enter any essential information about yourself in the "participant essential info" field.
13. Enter the date of the event in the "event date" field.
14. Enter the start time of the event in the "event start time" field.
15. Enter the venue of the event in the "event venue" field.
16. Choose the type of culinary event in the "culinary event type" field.
17. If you have any additional comments, enter them in the "additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant name** (participant name, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or driver's license.
* **email** (email, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **phone** (phone, text, required): Enter your valid phone number.
* **participant details** (participant details, note, required): Provide any information that you think is relevant to your participation in the competition.
* **consent to participate** (consent to participate, select_one, required): Agree to participate in the competition.
* **participant dietary restrictions** (participant dietary restrictions, text, optional): If you have any dietary restrictions, enter them here.
* **however** (however, text, optional): If you have any additional information about your dietary restrictions, enter it here.
* **participant special instructions** (participant special instructions, text, optional): If you have any special instructions or requests, enter them here.
* **contact preference** (contact preference, select_multiple, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted: by Email, by Phone, or In person.
* **emergency contact name** (emergency contact name, text, required): Enter the name of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **emergency contact phone** (emergency contact phone, text, required): Enter the phone number of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **emergency contact relation** (emergency contact relation, text, required): Enter the relation of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **emergency contact address** (emergency contact address, text, required): Enter the address of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **participant address** (participant address, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **participant zip** (participant zip, text, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **participant state** (participant state, text, optional): Enter your state or province.
* **participant city** (participant city, text, optional): Enter your city or town.
* **participant country** (participant country, text, optional): Enter your country.
* **participant date of birth** (participant date of birth, date, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **participant essential info** (participant essential info, text, optional): Enter any essential information about yourself.
* **event date** (event date, date, optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **event start time** (event start time, time, optional): Enter the start time of the event.
* **event venue** (event venue, text, optional): Enter the venue of the event.
* **culinary event type** (culinary event type, select_one, required): Choose the type of culinary event: Cook off, Judging, Tasting, or Other.
* **additional comments** (additional comments, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your participation in the event.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* If you have any special dietary restrictions, be sure to include them.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizers.
