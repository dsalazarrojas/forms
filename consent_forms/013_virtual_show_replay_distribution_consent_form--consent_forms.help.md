# Virtual Show Replay Distribution Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from users for the distribution of virtual show replays. The purpose of this form is to gather information on whether or not the user consents to receiving emails and phone calls from the organization regarding the virtual show replays.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button after filling out all required fields.
2. Fill in the form fields as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Virtual Show Replay Distribution Form** (<font size="small">[Optional]</font>): This field is an optional text field where you can provide any additional comments or information.
* **Recording Consent** (<font size="small">[Select 'True' or 'False')</font>): Check if you consent to being recorded during the virtual show. 
* **Replay Consent** (<font size="small">[Select 'True' or 'False'</font>): Check if you consent to having your virtual show replay distributed.
* **Email Consent** (<font size="small">[Select 'True', 'False' for each item]</font>): Check which of the following email types you consent to:
	+ Email 1
	+ Email 2
	+ ...
* **Phone Consent** (<font size="small">[Select 'True', 'False' for each item]</font>): Check which of the following phone call types you consent to:
	+ Phone 1
	+ Phone 2
	+ ...
* **Recording Consent Note** (<font size="small">[Optional]</font>): This field is a note field where you can provide any additional comments or information regarding recording consent.
* **Replay Consent Note** (<font size="small">[Optional]</font>): This field is a note field where you can provide any additional comments or information regarding replay consent.
* **Email Address** (<font size="small">[Optional]</font>): Enter your email address if you consent to receive emails.
* **Phone Number** (<font size="small">[Optional]</font>): Enter your phone number if you consent to receive phone calls.

**Tips:**

* Only fill in fields that are relevant to your preferences.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the organization.
* Once submitted, you will not be able to modify your answers.
