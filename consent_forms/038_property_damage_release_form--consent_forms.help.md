# property_damage_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The property damage release form is used to document the release of a property or vehicle from a company's control. This form is a critical part of the process of transferring ownership or ending a lease agreement. It confirms that the property or vehicle has been returned to its rightful owner and that all damages or issues have been addressed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the relevant option for the property damage release from the options provided.
2.  Enter the name of the individual or organization releasing the property or vehicle.
3.  Provide the address of the property or vehicle being released.
4.  Enter any relevant contact information for the individual or organization releasing the property.
5.  Select the date of the release.
6.  Add any additional witness information, if necessary.
7.  Provide a brief description of the property or vehicle being released.
8.  Sign and date the form to confirm that the release has taken place.

* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address of the property or vehicle being released.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant contact information for the individual or organization releasing the property.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Select the date of the release.
* **Witness Information** (`witness_info`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional information from a witness, if necessary.
