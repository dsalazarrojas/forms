# data_transparency_in_medicine_research_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information and consent for medicine research studies. It is used to ensure that participants understand the purpose, risks, and benefits of the study, as well as their rights and responsibilities as a participant. This form is a critical document that must be filled out carefully and accurately to ensure that all parties involved are aware of their roles and obligations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Review the form carefully and ensure that you understand all the questions and sections.
*   Fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
*   If you are unsure about any question or section, ask your researcher or study staff for clarification.
*   Sign and date the form to confirm your participation and consent.
