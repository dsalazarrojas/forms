# Child Traveling With Grandparent Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for parents or legal guardians to grant permission for their child to travel with their grandparents.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the child's information, including their name and date of birth.
2. Enter the grandparent's name and contact details (address and phone number).
3. Specify the start and end dates of the travel period.
4. Mention the destinations or major cities the child will visit.
5. Read and accept the authorization statement.
6. Finally, sign the form with your electronic signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Child Full Name** (`child_name`): The child's full name who will be traveling with their grandparents.
* **Date of Birth** (`child_dob`): The child's date of birth, which should be within the last 18 years.
* **Grandparent Full Name or Names** (`grandparent_name`): The grandparent's full name, or names if there is more than one grandparent.
* **Grandparent Address and Phone** (`grandparent_contact`): The grandparent's address and phone number where they can be reached during travel.
* **Start Date of Travel** (`start_date`): The beginning date of the travel period.
* **End Date of Travel** (`end_date`): The end date of the travel period.
* **General Destinations** (`destination_text`): List the major cities or regions that the child will visit.
* **I authorize my child to travel with their grandparents** (`grandparent_travel_auth`): Select one of the options: Yes - Authorization Granted or No - Stay Home.
* **Electronic Signature - Type Full Name** (`signature_type`): Sign this form with your electronic signature, making sure to use your full name.
* **Date of Signing** (`signature_date`): The date you are signing this form.

## Tips
* Make sure you have the necessary permissions and authorization to sign this form.
* Ensure that the child has all necessary vaccinations and documents for travel.
* Be aware that the travel period and destinations should be accurately filled out.
