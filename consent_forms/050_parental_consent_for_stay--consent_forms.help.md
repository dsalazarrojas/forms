# Parental Consent For Stay - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain parental consent for a child to stay overnight or go on a trip. It's a simple, multiple-choice form that helps parents provide their consent for their child's activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form's purpose and ensure you are filling it out for the correct reason.
2. Select the relevant options for your response:
	* For an overnight stay, choose "Yes" if you consent to your child staying overnight.
	* For a trip, choose "Yes" if you consent to your child going on a trip.
3. If you're unsure about any of the options, please ask your child's guardian or supervisor for guidance.
4. Fill out the "Reason For Consent" field with a brief explanation of why you're providing consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Overnight Stay** (`stay_overnight`, Select Multiple, Required: false): Choose "Yes" if you consent to your child staying overnight.
	* This field is used to determine if you're providing consent for an overnight stay.
	* If you select "Yes," your child can stay overnight without your explicit permission on a subsequent form submission.
- **trip** (`trip`, Select One, Required: false): Choose "Yes" if you consent to your child going on a trip.
	* This field is used to determine if you're providing consent for your child to go on a trip.
	* If you select "Yes," your child can go on a trip without your explicit permission on a subsequent form submission.
- **duration** (`duration`, Text, Required: false): Enter the duration of the stay or trip.
	* This field is used to record the length of time your child will be staying or traveling.
	* Please enter a numerical value (e.g., 1 day, 3 days, etc.).
- **type** (`stay_type`, Select Multiple, Required: false): Choose the type of stay or trip.
	* This field is used to determine the type of stay or trip you're consenting to.
	* Select all that apply (e.g., "overnight," "trip," "camp," etc.).
- **Reason For Consent** (`reason_for_consent`, Text, Required: false): Explain why you're providing consent.
	* This field is for your personal explanation of why you're providing consent.
	* Please keep your response brief and clear.

## Tips
* Make sure to read and review the form carefully before proceeding.
* If you need to make multiple submissions, ensure you're not duplicating your response.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please ask your child's guardian or supervisor for guidance.
