# Documentary Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from participants to use their documentary images, voices, and statements for various purposes. The form captures information about the participants' images, voices, and statements, as well as their consent to use, hold, and date/time of consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your participant's information on the main page (Page 1).
2. Answer questions about your participant's images, voices, and statements on subsequent pages.
3. Indicate your consent to use, hold, and date/time of consent for each of these components.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of your participant.
* **Participant Image Information** (`participant_image_info`, `text`, required): Provide any relevant details about your participant's images.
* **Participant Voice Information** (`participant_voice_info`, `text`, required): Provide any relevant details about your participant's voices.
* **Participant Statement Information** (`participant_statement_info`, `text`, required): Provide any relevant details about your participant's statements.
* **Participant Image Consent** (`participant_image_consent`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate your consent to use your participant's images.
* **Participant Voice Consent** (`participant_voice_consent`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate your consent to use your participant's voices.
* **Participant Statement Consent** (`participant_statement_consent`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate your consent to use your participant's statements.
* **Participant Image Consent Holdings** (`participant_image_consent_holdings`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you have the consent to hold your participant's images.
* **Participant Voice Consent Holdings** (`participant_voice_consent_holdings`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you have the consent to hold your participant's voices.
* **Participant Statement Consent Holdings** (`participant_statement_consent_holdings`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you have the consent to hold your participant's statements.
* **Participant Image Consent Date** (`participant_image_consent_date`, `date`, optional): If you have given consent to use your participant's images, enter the date you gave consent.
* **Participant Voice Consent Date** (`participant_voice_consent_date`, `date`, optional): If you have given consent to use your participant's voices, enter the date you gave consent.
* **Participant Statement Consent Date** (`participant_statement_consent_date`, `date`, optional): If you have given consent to use your participant's statements, enter the date you gave consent.
* **Participant Image Consent Time** (`participant_image_consent_time`, `time`, optional): If you have given consent to use your participant's images, enter the time you gave consent.
* **Participant Voice Consent Time** (`participant_voice_consent_time`, `time`, optional): If you have given consent to use your participant's voices, enter the time you gave consent.
* **Participant Statement Consent Time** (`participant_statement_consent_time`, `time`, optional): If you have given consent to use your participant's statements, enter the time you gave consent.
* **Participant Image Consent Email** (`participant_image_consent_email`, `email`, optional): If you have given consent to use your participant's images, enter your email address.
* **Participant Voice Consent Email** (`participant_voice_consent_email`, `email`, optional): If you have given consent to use your participant's voices, enter your email address.
* **Participant Statement Consent Email** (`participant_statement_consent_email`, `email`, optional): If you have given consent to use your participant's statements, enter your email address.
* **Participant Image Consent Phone** (`participant_image_consent_phone`, `text`, optional): If you have given consent to use your participant's images, enter your phone number.
* **Participant Voice Consent Phone** (`participant_voice_consent_phone`, `text`, optional): If you have given consent to use your participant's voices, enter your phone number.
* **Participant Statement Consent Phone** (`participant_statement_consent_phone`, `text`, optional): If you have given consent to use your participant's statements, enter your phone number.
* **Participant Image Consent Note** (`participant_image_consent_note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes about your participant's images.
* **Participant Voice Consent Note** (`participant_voice_consent_note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes about your participant's voices.
* **Participant Statement Consent Note** (`participant_statement_consent_note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes about your participant's statements.

Note: Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions and follow all applicable laws and regulations when collecting, storing, and using participant consent.
