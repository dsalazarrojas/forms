# Animal Therapy Feedback Footage Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants after an animal therapy session. It gathers personal and session details, ensures informed consent for recording, and gathers information on your comfort and preferences related to recording and use of the footage.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all the required fields (marked as `required`).
2. Choose your preferred answers for the select options (e.g., animal type, session type, etc.).
3. Select all applicable areas for the `benefit_areas` field.
4. Sign your name in the `participant_signature` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name** (`participant_name`): Enter your legal name.
* **Email Address** (`participant_email`): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`participant_phone`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Street Address** (`participant_address`): Enter your street address (optional).
* **Therapy Program Name** (`therapy_program`): Enter the name of the program you are participating in.
* **Therapy Session Date** (`therapy_date`): Enter the date of your therapy session.
* **Therapy Animal Name** (`animal_partner_name`): Enter the name of the animal partner.
* **Animal Type** (`animal_type`): Select the type of animal that was part of your session (Dog, Cat, Horse, Rabbit, Bird, Other).
* **Type of Therapy Session** (`session_type`): Select the type of session you attended (Individual Session, Group Session, Family Session, Assessment Session, Follow-Up Session).
* **Therapist Name** (`therapist_name`): Enter the name of your licensed therapist.
* **Overall Session Rating** (`feedback_rating`): Rate your overall experience of the session (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Interaction with Animal** (`animal_interaction`): Rate your interaction with the animal (Very Positive, Positive, Neutral, Negative, Very Negative).
* **Therapeutic Benefit** (`therapeutic_benefit`): Rate the benefit you experienced from the session (Significant Benefit, Moderate Benefit, Some Benefit, Minimal Benefit, No Benefit).
* **Benefit Areas** (`benefit_areas`): Select all the areas where you felt a benefit from the session (e.g., Reduced Anxiety, Improved Mood, etc.).
* **Comfort Level** (`comfort_level`): Rate how comfortable you felt during the session (Very Comfortable, Comfortable, Neutral, Uncomfortable, Very Uncomfortable).
* **Consent for Recording** (`footage_consent`): Select whether you consent to recording (Yes I Consent to Recording, No I Do Not Consent).
* **Type of Recording** (`recording_type`): Select the type of recording you consent to (Video Recording, Audio Recording, Photography).
* **Purpose of Recording** (`footage_purpose`): Select the purpose for which the recording will be used (e.g., Therapist Training, Program Evaluation, Research Purposes, etc.).
* **How May the Footage Be Used** (`footage_usage`): Select all the areas where you are comfortable with the footage being used (e.g., Internal Review Only, Professional Training, etc.).
* **Identity Protection Preferences** (`identity_protection`): Select how you want your identity to be protected (May Use My Full Identity, Use First Name Only, etc.).
* **Footage Retention** (`footage_duration`): Select how long you want the footage to be retained (Until End of Current Program, 1 Year, etc.).
* **Understanding of Revocation Rights** (`revocation_rights`): Confirm you understand you can revoke consent at any time.
* **May Footage Be Shown to Third Parties** (`third_party_viewing`): Select who you want to share the footage with (e.g., Any Professional Use, With Restrictions, etc.).
* **Will You Receive Compensation for Footage Use** (`compensation`): Select whether you expect monetary or session credit compensation.
* **Do You Have Any Concerns or Questions** (`concerns_or_questions`): Select if you have concerns or questions.
* **Please Describe Your Concerns** (`concerns_details`): If you selected concerns or questions, explain them here.
* **Additional Feedback About Session** (`additional_feedback`): Enter any other comments about your session.
* **Electronic Signature** (`participant_signature`): Enter your full name for signature.
* **Date of Consent** (`consent_date`): Enter the date of your consent.
* **Witness Name** (`witness_name`): Enter the name of the witness if applicable.
* **Final Confirmation** (`final_confirmation`): Confirm you have read and understood this consent form.
