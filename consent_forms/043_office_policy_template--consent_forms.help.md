# Office Policy Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This template is designed to document and store office policies related to cancellations, financial responsibilities, offered services, payment terms, contact information, and phone numbers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the available fields and ensure that you are providing the necessary information for your office policies.
2. Fill out the form according to your office's specific needs and circumstances.
3. Save the completed form for future reference and updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Cancellation Policy** (`cancellation_policy`, text, required: false): Enter your office's cancellation policy. This includes details on when and how to cancel appointments, services, or other office activities.
* **Financial Responsibilities** (`financial_responsibilities`, text, required: false): Describe your office's financial responsibilities, including payment terms, refund policies, or any other relevant information.
* **Cancellation** (`cancellation`, select_one, required: false): Select whether your office has a cancellation policy in place. Options are 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Services** (`services`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate which services your office offers. Options are 'Yes' or 'No', and you can select multiple services.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, date, required: false): Enter your office's payment terms, including due dates, payment methods, or any other relevant details.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, email, required: false): Provide your office's contact information, including email addresses or other relevant contact details.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your office's phone number for easy contact.

## Tips
* Ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* Review and update the form regularly to reflect any changes in office policies.
* Use this form as a template to document and communicate office policies to employees or clients.
