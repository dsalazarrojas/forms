# Property Energy Audit Participation Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to capture basic information about the property and the individual submitting the form. The form is intended to gather details about the property and the user's contact information for follow-up communication.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the **Property Details** field with any relevant information about the property.
2. Provide your **Informed Consent** to participate in the energy audit or assessment.
3. Enter your **Contact** information so that we can reach out to you for further communication.
4. Enter your **Phone** number for contact purposes.
5. Enter your **Email** address for contact purposes.
6. Select a valid **Date** for the audit or assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Details**: This field is used to provide information about the property being audited or assessed. Please fill out any relevant details about the property, such as its address, location, or description.
* **Informed Consent**: This field is used to obtain your agreement to participate in the energy audit or assessment. Please provide your informed consent by entering your answer in this field.
* **Contact**: This field is used to gather your contact information. Please enter your name, address, or any other relevant contact details.
* **Phone**: This field is used to capture your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email**: This field is used to capture your email address for contact purposes.
* **Date**: This field is used to select a valid date for the audit or assessment. Please ensure you select a date that works best for you.
