# Water Project Beneficiary Interview Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from water project beneficiaries to ensure they understand the project's purpose, terms, and conditions. It's essential to complete this form accurately to avoid any misunderstandings or miscommunication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out each field accurately and thoroughly.
* Make sure to include your participant's correct contact information (First Name, Last Name, and Email).
* Provide your Participant ID for identification purposes.
* Specify the project name and its purpose.
* Ensure you are aware of the date of consent (when you're giving your consent).
* Sign the form with your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Participant ID** (`participant_id`, text, optional): Please enter your participant ID for reference.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number for us to reach you.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, optional): Please enter the project name for which you're giving your consent.
* **Purpose** (`purpose`, text, optional): Please briefly describe the purpose of the project.
* **Date of Consent** (`date_of_consent`, date, optional): Please enter the date on which you're giving your consent.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, optional): Please sign this form as a sign of your understanding and agreement.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required): Please choose "True" if you understand and agree with the project's terms and conditions, "False" otherwise. If unsure, select "Not Sure" for further clarification.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and honestly.
* Make sure to ask any questions or clarify doubts before signing the form.
* Keep a copy of the form for your records.
