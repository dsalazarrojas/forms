## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. **What is your name?**
	* Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
2. **Contact Information**
	* Provide your contact information, including your address and phone number.
3. **How did you become aware of this form?**
	* Briefly describe how you came across this form.
4. **Date of Last Training Session**
	* Enter the date of your last training session.
5. **Date to Return to Training**
	* Enter the date you are scheduled to return to training.
6. **Terms and Conditions**
	* Read and acknowledge that you understand the terms and conditions of your training.
7. **Safety Guidelines**
	* Read and acknowledge that you understand the safety guidelines of your training.
8. **First Time Returning to Training**
	* Select whether this is your first time returning to training or if you have previously returned.
9. **Previous Training Experience**
	* Briefly describe any previous training experiences.
10. **Medical Conditions**
	* List any medical conditions that may impact your ability to participate in the training.
11. **Medications**
	* List any medications you are currently taking.
12. **Physical Limitations**
	* Describe any physical limitations you have that may impact your ability to participate in the training.
13. **Support or Assistance**
	* Describe any support or assistance you may need during the training.
14. **Loud Noises or Vibrations**
	* Indicate whether you are sensitive to loud noises or vibrations.
15. **Physical Activity**
	* Describe any physical activity you are able to perform.
16. **Allergies or Sensitivities**
	* List any allergies or sensitivities you have.
17. **Mask Wearing**
	* Indicate whether you are able to wear a mask during the training.
18. **Physical Limitations with Mask**
	* Describe any physical limitations you have while wearing a mask.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask for clarification.
* If you have any medical conditions or physical limitations, please provide detailed descriptions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the training administrators.
* This form is designed to help ensure your safety and effectiveness during the training.
