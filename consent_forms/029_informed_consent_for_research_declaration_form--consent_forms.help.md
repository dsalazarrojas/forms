# Informed Consent For Research Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain informed consent from research participants, ensuring that they are aware of their rights and the terms of the research project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the participant identification section (page 1) with your name and any other relevant details.
2. Provide a clear and concise statement of the research objectives (page 2).
3. Sign the informed consent (page 3) to acknowledge that you understand the research project and its terms.
4. Review and understand your participant rights (page 4).
5. Choose your declaration status (page 5).
6. Provide your declaration agreement details (page 6-8).
7. Enter your contact information (page 9-10).
8. Sign and date the informed consent form (page 11).
9. Provide witness signature (page 12).
10. Complete your participant declaration (page 13-14).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Identification** (page 1, `participant_identification`):
	+ This section captures your identity details, including your name, which helps us verify your involvement in the research project.
* **Research Objectives** (page 2, `research_objectives`):
	+ Describe the research project, including its purpose, scope, and any risks or benefits associated with it.
* **Informed Consent** (page 3, `informed_consent`):
	+ Sign this section to confirm that you have read, understand, and agree to the terms of the research project.
* **Participant Rights** (page 4, `participant_rights`):
	+ This section outlines your rights as a participant, including your right to withdraw from the project at any time.
* **Research Declaration** (page 5, `research_declaration`):
	+ Select the option that best describes your declaration status regarding the research project.
* **Research Declaration Agreement Date** (page 6, `research_declaration_agreement_date`):
	+ Enter the date you agree to the declaration terms.
* **Research Declaration Agreement Time** (page 7, `research_declaration_agreement_time`):
	+ Enter the time you agree to the declaration terms.
* **Research Declaration Agreement Location** (page 8, `research_declaration_agreement_location`):
	+ Describe the location where you agree to the declaration terms.
* **Research Declaration Agreement Duration** (page 9, `research_declaration_agreement_duration`):
	+ Enter the duration for which you agree to the declaration terms.
* **Contact Information** (page 10, `contact_information`):
	+ Provide your contact information, including your email address and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Information Alternate** (page 11, `contact_information_alternate`):
	+ Provide an alternate contact information in case the first contact information is not reachable.
* **Informed Consent Form Date** (page 12, `informed_consent_form_date`):
	+ Sign and date the informed consent form to confirm your agreement.
* **Contact Person** (page 13, `contact_person`):
	+ Provide the name and contact information of the person who can be reached for further information.
* **Contact Phone** (page 14, `contact_phone`):
	+ Provide the phone number of the contact person.
* **Participant Signature** (page 15, `participant_signature`):
	+ Sign to confirm that you have read, understand, and agree to the terms of the research project.
* **Researcher Signature** (page 16, `researcher_signature`):
	+ Sign to confirm that you have obtained informed consent from the participant.
* **Witness Signature** (page 17, `witness_signature`):
	+ Sign to confirm that you have witnessed the informed consent process.
* **Participant Declaration** (page 18, `participant_declaration`):
	+ Sign and date the participant declaration to confirm your agreement.
* **Participant Declaration Time** (page 19, `participant_declaration_time`):
	+ Enter the time you agree to the participant declaration terms.
* **Participant Declaration Location** (page 20, `participant_declaration_location`):
	+ Describe the location where you agree to the participant declaration terms.
* **Participant Declaration Duration** (page 21, `participant_declaration_duration`):
	+ Enter the duration for which you agree to the participant declaration terms.
* **Participant Declaration Other** (page 22, `participant_declaration_other`):
	+ Provide any additional information that may be relevant to your participant declaration.
