# Customs Declaration Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from travelers for customs declaration purposes. It collects information about the type of trip, business or personal, category of goods, weight of goods, and a brief description of the goods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of trip you are taking:
	* Traveler Type: Choose between "Traveler type" or "Other" to indicate the purpose of your trip.
2. Indicate if you are on a business trip:
	* Business Type: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if your trip is for business or personal reasons.
3. Specify the category of goods you are traveling with:
	* Goods Category: Choose between "Goods category" or "Other" to indicate the type of goods you are traveling with.
4. Enter the weight of the goods (if applicable):
	* Goods Weight: Enter a number value to indicate the weight of the goods (this field is optional).
5. Provide a brief description of the goods:
	* Goods Description: Enter a short description of the goods you are traveling with.
6. Confirm the identity of the customs officer handling the declaration:
	* Customs Officer: Enter the name and title of the customs officer who will handle your declaration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Traveler Type** (`traveler_type`, select_one, required: false): Indicate the purpose of your trip by selecting "Traveler type" or "Other" from the dropdown menu.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if your trip is for business or personal reasons.
* **Goods Category** (`goods_category`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose between "Goods category" or "Other" to indicate the type of goods you are traveling with.
* **Goods Weight** (`goods_weight`, number, required: false): Enter a number value to indicate the weight of the goods (this field is optional).
* **Goods Description** (`goods_description`, text, required: false): Enter a short description of the goods you are traveling with.
* **Customs Officer** (`customs_officer`, note, required: false): Enter the name and title of the customs officer who will handle your declaration.
