# GPS Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The GPS Disclosure Form is used to collect information about a specific type of property, typically a GPS device, for record keeping and tracking purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the information about the GPS device or property you are disclosing information about.
2. Answer all the required fields (indicated by a asterisk *) as accurately as possible.
3. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **GPS Disclosure** (`gps_disclosure`, `note`, optional): This field is not visible in the form as it's a `note` type, but it's likely used for a description or comment about the GPS device or property.
* **Property Boundary** (`property_boundary`, `text`): Enter a brief description of the property's boundary, including any relevant details such as address, city, state, or zip code.
* **Easement Details** (`easement_details`, `text`): This field is likely used to describe any easement related to the property, such as access or utility rights.
* **Tracking Devices** (`tracking_devices`, `select_one`): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether there are any tracking devices installed on the property.
* **Property ID** (`property_id`, `number`, required): Enter a unique identifier for the property, which could be a number or any other unique identifier.
* **Date Recorded** (`date_recorded`, `date`, required): Enter the date the information was recorded.
* **Time Recorded** (`time_recorded`, `time`, optional): Enter the time the information was recorded, or leave blank if not applicable.
* **Recorded By** (`recorded_by`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person or organization that recorded this information.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the property.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required): Enter your signature, which is a mandatory field for this form.

## Tips
