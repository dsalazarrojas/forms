# UltherapyConsentForm - Help Guide

## Purpose
The UltherapyConsentForm is used to obtain informed consent from patients before undergoing Ultherapy treatments. This form gathers essential information about the patient's medical history, potential risks and consequences, and other relevant details to ensure their safety and well-being during the procedure.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, select the "Patient Information" page to input the patient's basic details.
2.  Next, click on "Procedure Information" to provide information about the specific Ultherapy procedure the patient is undergoing.
3.  After that, select "Risks and Consequences" to discuss potential risks and consequences of the procedure.
4.  On the "Consent" page, choose "True" to indicate that the patient has given their informed consent.
5.  In the "Signature" field, input your electronic signature as proof of your identity and agreement.
6.  Optionally, the patient can provide their signature in the "Patient Signature" field.
7.  In the "Healthcare Providers" field, select the healthcare provider(s) who will be performing the procedure.
8.  In "Medical Conditions", provide any pre-existing medical conditions that may affect the patient's suitability for the procedure.
9.  Provide details about the procedure in the "Procedure Details" field.
10.  Enter the date of consent in the "Consent Date" field.
11.  Optionally, input the date of the procedure in the "Procedure Date" field.
12.  In "Procedure Time", select the time of the procedure.
13.  In "Medical Conditions 2" and "Procedure Details 2", provide additional information about the patient's medical status and the procedure.
14.  In "Additional Comments", provide any other relevant comments or information about the patient's condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Information (1)** (Text, Required): Input the patient's name, date of birth, and contact details.
*   **Procedure Information (2)** (Text, Required): Provide details about the specific procedure the patient is undergoing, including the treatment area and expected outcomes.
*   **Risks and Consequences (3)** (Text, Required): List any potential risks and consequences of the procedure.
*   **Consent (4)** (Select One, Required): Indicate that the patient has given their informed consent. Choose "True" to confirm.
*   **Signature (5)** (Text, Optional): Input your electronic signature as proof of your identity and agreement.
*   **Patient Signature (6)** (Text, Optional): Optionally, input the patient's signature as proof of their consent.
*   **Healthcare Providers (7)** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the healthcare provider(s) who will be performing the procedure.
*   **Medical Conditions (8)** (Text, Optional): List any pre-existing medical conditions that may affect the patient's suitability for the procedure.
*   **Procedure Details (9)** (Text, Required): Provide details about the procedure, including the expected duration and any relevant information.
*   **Consent Date (10)** (Date, Required): Enter the date of consent.
*   **Signature Date (11)** (Date, Optional): Optionally, input the date of the procedure.
*   **Procedure Date (12)** (Date, Required): Enter the date of the procedure.
*   **Procedure Time (13)** (Time, Required): Select the time of the procedure.
*   **Medical Conditions 2 (14)** (Text, Optional): Provide additional information about the patient's medical status.
*   **Procedure Details 2 (15)** (Text, Optional): Provide additional information about the procedure.
*   **Additional Comments (16)** (Text, Optional): Provide any other relevant comments or information about the patient's condition.

## Tips

*   Be thorough and accurate when filling out this form to ensure informed consent from patients.
*   Review all fields carefully to avoid missing important information.
*   Make sure to input all required fields to ensure the form is complete and valid.
*   Use the correct dates and times for the procedure and consent.
*   Provide clear and concise information in all fields.
