## profile_information_sharing_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user information for profile sharing purposes. It is used to gather consent from users regarding the sharing of their personal information, such as email, phone number, and address. The form also requests permission to share the user's date of birth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Profile Information Sharing Consent**: Check the box next to "Yes" to indicate your consent to share your profile information.
2. **Email Sharing**: Select all the email addresses you are comfortable sharing your profile information with.
3. **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. **Email**: Enter your email address.
5. **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
6. **Address**: Enter your address.
7. **Name**: Enter your name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Profile Information Sharing Consent**:
	+ <label>Profile Information Sharing Consent</label> (1, text, optional): Check the box to indicate your consent to share your profile information.
* **Sharing Consent**:
	+ <label>Sharing Consent</label> (2, select_one, optional): Choose "Yes" to allow sharing of your profile information, or "No" to decline.
* **Email Sharing**:
	+ <label>Email Sharing</label> (3, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" to share your email address.
* **Date of Birth**:
	+ <label>Date of Birth</label> (4, date, optional): Enter the date of your birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Email**:
	+ <label>Email</label> (5, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone**:
	+ <label>Phone</label> (6, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address**:
	+ <label>Address</label> (7, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **Name**:
	+ <label>Name</label> (8, text, optional): Enter your name.

## Tips
* Make sure to carefully review your selections before submitting the form.
* You can select multiple email addresses to share your profile information with.
* The date of birth field should be entered in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* The email and phone fields should be formatted as per standard email and phone number formats.
