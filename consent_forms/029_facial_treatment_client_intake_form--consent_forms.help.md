# Facial Treatment Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facial Treatment Client Intake Form is used to gather information from clients who are interested in pursuing facial treatments at your establishment. This form helps the client provide details about their skin needs, medical history, and treatment preferences, ensuring that the treatment plan is tailored to their individual needs. Please fill out this form before scheduling a facial treatment appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by filling out the client_details page.
2. Answer the question about skin needs.
3. Provide any additional information for the treatment plan on the last page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Details
* **client_details** (`client_details`, `text`, Optional): Please provide your name, address, contact number, and any other relevant information about yourself.

### Skin Needs
* **skin_needs** (`skin_needs`, `select_one`, Required): This question is about your skin condition. Choose one of the options to indicate your skin needs. You can select either 'Yes' or 'No'.

### Treatment Plan
* **treatment_plan** (`treatment_plan`, `text`, Optional): Describe your treatment goals and any specific issues or areas of concern you have about your skin. Be as detailed as possible so we can create a personalized treatment plan for you.

## Tips
- Be honest when answering the question about skin needs, as it will help us understand your skin condition better.
- Please provide as much detail as possible for the treatment plan, so we can tailor the treatment to your individual needs.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
