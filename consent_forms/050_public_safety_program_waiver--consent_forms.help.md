# Public Safety Program Waiver - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Public Safety Program Waiver form is intended to collect information from participants in a public safety program. The form aims to gather essential details from the participant to ensure their safety and well-being while taking part in the program. Please review the following guidance on how to complete this form.

## How To Complete This Form
Before filling out the form, ensure you have all the necessary information ready. The form is divided into several sections, each requiring different types of input.

- **Program Information**: This section is a brief overview of the program you are enrolling in. It is not required but can provide context for your participation.
- **Participant Info**: Select one or more of the following options to provide your information:
  - First Name
  - Last Name
  - Email
  - Phone Number
- **Emergency Contact**: Choose the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
  - Mother
  - Father
  - Brother
  - Sister
  - Other
- **Participant Consent**: Indicate your legal status:
  - I am of legal age
  - I am not of legal age
  - I am of legal age (with parental consent)
- **Medical Details**, **Medical History**, **Medical Condition**, **Medication**, **Medical Emergency**: Provide any relevant medical details or information that might be necessary for your safety while taking part in the program.
- **Participant Statement**: Please use this section to add any additional information or notes about yourself that the program organizers might need to know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Program Information**: A brief description of the program you are enrolling in.
- **Participant Info**: This section helps the program organizers have a record of your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
- **Emergency Contact**: Choose the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency. This will help program organizers know who to reach out to in case you are unable to participate in the program.
- **Participant Consent**: This indicates your legal status and any necessary parental consent for participation.
- **Medical Details**: Share any medical conditions, allergies, or other health information that might impact your participation.
- **Medical History**: If you have a medical history, please describe it briefly.
- **Medical Condition**: If you have a known medical condition that might affect your safety, please state it.
- **Medication**: List any current medications or treatments you are taking.
- **Medical Emergency**: Describe any medical emergencies that you might face during the program.
- **Participant Statement**: This is a space for any additional information you think the program organizers might find helpful or need to know.
