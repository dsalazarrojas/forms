# Parent Acknowledgement Form For Covid 19 School Procedures - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to be completed by a parent or guardian to acknowledge and consent to the school's Covid-19 procedures and protocols. It is a crucial document that ensures the parent's understanding and agreement to the school's policies and procedures related to Covid-19.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the terms and conditions carefully.
2. Select the options that apply to your child's needs.
3. Enter the required information for your child and emergency contact.
4. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Acknowledgement Form**: Enter your name and date of completion.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Read and agree to the school's terms and conditions.
* **e-Signature**: Enter your electronic signature as proof of acknowledgment.
* **No Coding**: Select the option that best describes your child's access to coding during school hours.
	+ "no coding allowed"
	+ "only assistive technology is permitted"
* **Student's Name**: Enter your child's name.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number for school communication.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for school communication.
* **School Email**: Enter the school's email address for direct communication.
* **Date**: Enter the date of completion.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of your child's school.
* **Student ID**: Enter your child's student ID number.
* **Guardian Consent**: Select whether you, as the guardian, give consent to the school's Covid-19 procedures.
	+ "True"
	+ "False"
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Describe the relationship between you and the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Address**: Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Type**: Describe the type of relationship between you and the emergency contact person.
* **Parent Consent**: Select whether you, as the parent, give consent to the school's Covid-19 procedures.
	+ "True"
	+ "False"
* **Emergency Contact Consent**: Select whether the emergency contact gives consent to the school's Covid-19 procedures.
	+ "True"
	+ "False"

## Tips

- Please review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed.
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the school administration.
