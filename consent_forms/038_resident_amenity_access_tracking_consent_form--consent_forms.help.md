# Resident Amenity Access Tracking Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track resident amenity access and obtain consent from residents for accessing certain amenities in a housing facility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Please provide your resident information.
2. Select the amenities you would like to access.
3. Enter the date and time you would like to access the amenity.
4. Enter your email address.
5. Enter your phone number.
6. Sign the form to confirm your consent.
7. Select "Yes" to submit the form, or "No" if you do not want to access any amenities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resident Information (residend Information)** (<type:text, required:false>): Please enter your name and any other relevant information as requested.
* **Amenity Request (amenity Request)** (<type:select_multiple, required:false>): Please select the specific amenity you would like to access. You can select multiple options.
* **Date (date)** (<type:date, required:false>): Please enter the date you would like to access the amenity.
* **Time (time)** (<type:time, required:false>): Please enter the time you would like to access the amenity.
* **Email Address (email)** (<type:email, required:false>): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone Number (phone)** (<type:text, required:false>): Please enter your phone number.
* **Signature (Signature)** (<type:note, required:false>): Please sign and date this form to confirm your consent.
* **Submit (Submit)** (<type:select_one, required:false>): Please select "Yes" if you have read and agree to the terms, or "No" if you do not want to access any amenities.
