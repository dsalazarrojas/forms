# Heritage Site Management Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a heritage site's management, including its details, location, description, owner, responsibilities, declaration commitments, status, security, notes, contacts, references, and agreements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information about the heritage site.
2. Ensure that you have the authority to complete this form.
3. Review each field carefully and fill out only the required fields.
4. Select multiple options for fields that allow for multiple selections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Site Details
* **Site Details** (`Site Details`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the heritage site, including its name, address, and any other relevant details.

### Site Location
* **Site Location** (`site_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the heritage site, including the address, city, state, and country.

### Site Description
* **Site Description** (`site_description`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of the heritage site, including its history, cultural significance, and any other relevant information.

### Site Owner
* **Site Owner** (`site_owner`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of the site's owner or manager.

### Site Responsibilities
* **Site Responsibilities** (`site_responsibilities`, select multiple, optional): Select the responsibilities of the site's owner or manager, such as maintenance, preservation, and protection.

### Declaration Commitments
* **Declaration Commitments** (`declaration_commitments`, text, required): Enter a statement of commitment to manage the heritage site, including any promises or agreements made to the public or authorities.

### Site Status
* **Site Status** (`site_status`, select one, required): Select the current status of the heritage site, such as "active", "inactive", or "pending".

### Site Security
* **Site Security** (`site_security`, note, optional): Note any security concerns or measures taken to protect the heritage site.

### Site Notes
* **Site Notes** (`site_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the heritage site.

### Site Contacts
* **Site Contacts** (`site_contacts`, text, required): Enter the contact information of the site's owner or manager, including phone numbers, email addresses, and physical addresses.

### Site References
* **Site References** (`site_references`, text, optional): Enter any references or citations that support the heritage site's significance or management.

### Site Agreements
* **Site Agreements** (`site_agreements`, select multiple, optional): Select any agreements or contracts related to the heritage site, such as preservation agreements or management contracts.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and authority to complete this form.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please contact the form's administrators.
