# Hair Spa Protein Mask Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hair Spa Protein Mask Consent Form is a document that requires clients to confirm their understanding of the treatment, its risks, and benefits before proceeding with the hair spa service. This form is a part of our service protocol and ensures that clients are fully informed about the treatment they are about to receive.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please read each section carefully and fill out the information as instructed. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details** (Client Details, text, optional): This section is for collecting client contact information.
* **Pre-Service Screening** (Pre-Service Screening, text, optional): Please provide any relevant health information that could impact your treatment.
* **Consent** (Consent, select one, optional): Please select one of the options that best describe your understanding.
* **Client Understands Treatment** (Client Understands Treatment, select multiple, optional): Please select all of the options that you understand about the treatment.
* **Client Understands Risks** (Client Understands Risks, select multiple, optional): Please select all of the options that you understand about the risks.
* **Client Understands Benefits** (Client Understands Benefits, select multiple, optional): Please select all of the options that you understand about the benefits.
* **Client Name** (Client Name, text, optional): Please enter your name.
* **Client Email** (Client Email, email, optional): Please enter your email address.
* **Client Phone** (Client Phone, text, optional): Please enter your phone number.
* **Service Details** (Service Details, text, optional): Please describe the service you are having.
* **Treatment Details** (Treatment Details, text, optional): Please describe the treatment you are having.
* **Pre-Screening Questions** (Pre-Screening Questions, text, optional): Please answer any pre-screening questions.

Please fill out this form accurately and completely.
