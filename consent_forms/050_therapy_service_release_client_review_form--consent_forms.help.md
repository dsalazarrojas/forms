# Therapy Service Release & Client Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Therapy Service Release & Client Review Form is a document that clients must complete to provide consent and review information regarding the privacy and sharing terms of their therapy services. This form helps ensure that clients understand how their information will be shared or used, and it's essential to have a clear agreement in place before commencing therapy sessions or services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully, ensuring you understand all the questions and agreements.
2. Fill out all required fields with accurate information.
3. Make sure to sign and date the form once you've reviewed and agreed to all terms.
4. Submit the completed form to your therapist or service provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Full Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your current contact number for follow-up or emergency purposes.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for secure communication with your therapist.
* **Street Address**: Enter your current address where you can be reached.
* **State**: Please select 'Yes' or 'No' indicating whether you're aware of and agree to our state-specific regulations.
* **Zip Code**: Enter the zip code of your current address.
* **Therapist Name**: Enter the name of your assigned therapist.
* **Therapist Email**: Enter your therapist's email address for secure communication.
* **Therapist Contact Number**: Enter your therapist's contact number for scheduling or emergencies.
* **Licensing Number**: Enter your therapist's professional licensing number.
* **Address**: Enter your therapist's address for secure communication or meetings.
* **State**: Please select 'Yes' or 'No' indicating whether your therapist is aware of and agree to our state-specific regulations.
* **Zip Code**: Enter your therapist's zip code.
* **Consent to Share Testimonial**: Select 'Yes' if you agree to share your testimonial, 'No' otherwise.
* **Release of Privacy Information**: Select 'I have reviewed and agreed to the privacy information' if you've read and understood the terms, 'I have not yet reviewed the privacy information' if you need to review it first.
* **Signed Release Date**: Date and sign this form once you've reviewed and agreed to all terms.
