# Our Special Friendships Health Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Our Special Friendships Health Form" is a comprehensive health-related form that helps our organization better understand your health conditions, relationships with healthcare providers, and medical histories. This form will help us provide you with tailored support and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and answer every question to the best of your ability.
2. Fill in your medical conditions, allergies, and special needs in the text fields.
3. Select the relevant medical conditions from the options provided.
4. Check the checkboxes for your medical history and medical insurance information.
5. Choose your doctor from the dropdown list and select their specialty if necessary.
6. Enter your doctor visit frequency and time.
7. Provide any additional medical history and equipment information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions_page`): Enter any medical conditions you have, such as diabetes, high blood pressure, or allergies.
* **Allergies** (`allergies_page`): Check the box if you have any allergies. This will help us understand any potential risks or sensitivities.
* **Special Needs** (`special_needs_page`): Briefly describe any special needs or requirements you may have, such as mobility aids or communication needs.
* **Doctor** (`doctor_page`): Select your primary healthcare provider from the dropdown list.
* **Doctor's Specialty** (`doctor_specialties_page`): If your doctor has a specialty, please select it from the options provided.
* **Medical Equipment** (`medical_equipment_page`): Enter any medical equipment you use, such as a wheelchair or oxygen tank.
* **Medical Insurance** (`medical_insurance_page`): Enter your medical insurance provider and policy number.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history_page`): Enter any relevant medical history, such as previous surgeries or medical conditions.
* **Doctor Visit Frequency** (`doctor_visit_frequency_page`): Indicate how often you visit your doctor.
* **Doctor Relationship** (`doctor_relationship_page`): Choose how long you have been seeing your doctor.
* **Medical Record Provider** (`medical_record_provider_page`): Enter your medical record provider, if applicable.
* **Medical Equipment Page 2** (`medical_equipment_page_2`): Enter any additional medical equipment you use.
* **Medical Insurance Provider** (`medical_insurance_provider_page`): Enter your medical insurance provider name.
* **Medical History Page 2** (`medical_history_page_2`): Enter any further medical history, if applicable.
* **Doctor Visit Time** (`doctor_visit_time_page`): Enter your doctor visit time.
* **Doctor Known Time** (`doctor_known_time_page`): Enter the time you have known your doctor.
* **Medical Prescription Medication** (`medical_prescription_medication_page`): Check if you take any prescribed medication.

Note: Please be as thorough and accurate as possible when filling out this form. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us for assistance.
