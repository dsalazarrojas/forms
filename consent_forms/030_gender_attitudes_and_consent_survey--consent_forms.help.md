# Gender Attitudes And Consent Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gender Attitudes And Consent Survey is a form designed to collect data on respondents' attitudes towards gender and their consent to certain actions. The survey aims to gather information on gender identity, roles, and consent for research and analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction page for an overview of the survey.
2. Answer the questions about your gender identity, gender roles, and consent.
3. Provide your age in the required field.
4. Enter your email address (optional).
5. Enter your phone number (optional).
6. Write any additional comments or feedback you may have.
7. Review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is the first page of the form. It provides an overview of the survey and its purpose. Read it carefully to understand what the survey is asking you to do.
* **gender_identity**: This question asks you to indicate your gender identity. Choose "Yes" if you identify as male, female, or non-binary, and "No" if you do not identify with any of these options.
* **gender_roles**: This question asks you to select one or more gender roles that you identify with. Select "Yes" if you identify with a specific role, and "No" if you do not.
* **Consent**: This question asks you to indicate your consent to certain actions. Choose "Yes" if you consent, and "No" if you do not.
* **Age**: This is a required field where you must enter your age. Please enter a whole number (e.g., 25).
* **gender**: This question asks you to select your gender. Choose "Yes" if you identify as male, female, or non-binary, and "No" if you do not.
* **email**: This is an optional field where you can enter your email address.
* **phone**: This is an optional field where you can enter your phone number.
* **comments**: This is an optional field where you can write any additional comments or feedback you may have.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* Answer all questions to the best of your ability.
* Do not submit the form multiple times.
* Do not share your login credentials with anyone.
* Make sure to review your answers before submitting the form.
