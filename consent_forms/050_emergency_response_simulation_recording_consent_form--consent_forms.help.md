# Emergency Response Simulation Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Emergency Response Simulation Recording Consent Form" is used to gather information from participants in emergency response simulations. This form is essential to ensure that all participants are aware that the simulation will be recorded and that they understand the implications of this recording. It also helps to identify any medical conditions or concerns that may affect their participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your role in the simulation from the options provided (Simulation Participant, Simulation Observer, or Simulation Instructor).
2. If you are a member of an emergency response team, select your role within the team (Team Member, Team Leader, or Supervisor).
3. If applicable, enter your date of birth to help us understand any medical conditions that may affect your participation.
4. Enter the location of your role in the simulation.
5. Enter the date and time of the simulation.
6. Indicate if you are aware that the simulation will be recorded and if you agree to it.
7. If you have any medical conditions or concerns that may affect your participation, select the relevant options.
8. If you have an emergency contact, enter their contact number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your role in the simulation?** (`emergency_response_simulation_role`): Select your role in the simulation from the options provided.
* **What is your role in the emergency response team?** (`emergency_response_simulation_role_team`): If you are part of a team, select your role within the team.
* **What is your date of birth?** (`date_of_birth`): Enter your date of birth if you have any medical conditions that may affect your participation.
* **What is the location of your role in the simulation?** (`simulation_location`): Enter the location where you will be performing your role in the simulation.
* **What is the date of the simulation?** (`simulation_date`): Enter the date of the simulation.
* **What is the time of the simulation?** (`simulation_time`): Enter the time of the simulation.
* **Are you aware that the simulation will be recorded?** (`simulation_recording`): Indicate if you are aware that the simulation will be recorded.
* **Do you agree to the recording?** (`agree_to_recording`): Select "I agree" if you consent to the recording.
* **What is the expected duration of the simulation?** (`simulation_duration`): Enter the expected duration of the simulation.
* **What is the contact number of your emergency contact?** (`emergency_contact`): Enter the contact number of your emergency contact.
* **Do you have any medical conditions or concerns that may affect your participation?** (`medical_conditions`): Select all the options that apply to you.
* **What is the contact number of your supervisor/parent/primary caregiver?** (`supervisor_contact`): Enter the contact number of your supervisor, parent, or primary caregiver.

## Tips

* Be honest about your role in the simulation and your role within the team.
* If you have any medical conditions or concerns, select all the options that apply to you.
* If you are unsure about the date or time of the simulation, please check with your instructor or team leader.
* Make sure you are aware that the simulation will be recorded and select the correct option.
* If you have any medical conditions or concerns, please inform your instructor or team leader before participating in the simulation.
