# financial-agreement-confirmation-consent-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather financial information and confirm the terms of an agreement with a client. It's a critical document that requires careful completion.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Enter the client's details in the "Client Details" section.
3. Provide the agreement details in the "Agreement Details" section.
4. Confirm the signature in the "Signature Confirmation" section.
5. Select the date from the calendar in the "Date" section.
6. Select the agreement terms in the "Agreement Terms" section.
7. Enter the advisor's acceptance in the "Advisor Acceptance" section.
8. Review the form carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the client's name, email, and phone number.
* **Agreement Details** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the financial agreement, including the terms and conditions.
* **Signature Confirmation** (`3`, `text`, required/optional): Confirm the client's signature, ensuring it's authentic and not forged.
* **Date** (`4`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date of the agreement.
* **Agreement Terms** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Choose the terms of the agreement (Term 1, Term 2, Term 3).
* **Advisor Acceptance** (`6`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the advisor's acceptance, including their signature and date.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting.
* Ensure all required fields are completed.
* Use the provided options for the "Agreement Terms" section.
* Use the calendar for the "Date" section.
