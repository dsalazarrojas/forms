# Couples Program Development Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Couples Program Development Agreement form is a document that gathers information and signatures from program participants in couples. The form is essential for the program's administration and helps ensure that all participants are on the same page regarding program expectations and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2. Select the correct participant names from the options provided.
3. Review the program expectations and choose the ones that apply.
4. If you have any other expectations, provide them in the text field.
5. Sign the form to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`couples_program_development_agreement_form_input_1`): Enter the names of the couple participating in the program.
* **Participant Names** (`couple_participant_names`): Select the names of the participants from the provided options.
* **Participant Names Hints** (`couple_participant_names_hints`): If you need to provide additional information or clarification about the participant names, enter it here.
* **Program Expectations** (`program_expectations`): Select the expectations that apply to your participation in the program. You can also choose "I Understand" if all expectations are acceptable.
* **Other Program Expectations** (`program_expectations_other`): If there are any additional expectations not listed, provide them here.
* **Participant Signature** (`couples_program_development_agreement_form_signature`): Sign the form to confirm your agreement.
* **Counselor Signature** (`couples_program_development_agreement_form_counselor_signature`): A counselor's signature is not required but recommended for program administration purposes.
* **Date** (`couples_program_development_agreement_form_date`): Enter the date you are signing the form.

## Tips
- Please review the form carefully before submission.
- If you are unsure about any field, ask for assistance.
- Ensure that you have filled out the form accurately and honestly.
- Sign the form only if you are comfortable with the program's expectations and requirements.
