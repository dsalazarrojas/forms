# Hair Heat Styling Service Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect the necessary information from clients to provide a hair heat styling service. The information collected will help ensure that the service can be provided safely and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your client's details in the Client Details section.
2. Fill in your name in the Name field.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
5. If you have any medical conditions, please list them in the Medical Conditions field.
6. If you are currently taking any medications, list them in the Medications field.
7. Select your preferred Service Type from the list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details**: This section allows you to enter your client's details. Please make sure to enter the correct information, as this will be used to contact them for their appointment.
* **Name**: Please enter your name as it should be written on any communication related to your appointment.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for any updates or notifications.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted for any last-minute changes or reminders.
* **Medical Conditions**: If you have any medical conditions, please list them here. This will help the service provider take necessary precautions to ensure your safety during the service.
* **Medications**: If you are currently taking any medications, list them here. This will help the service provider be aware of any potential interactions during the service.
* **Service Type**: Select 'Yes' if you have any allergies or sensitivities that may affect the service, or 'No' if you do not have any. This will help the service provider choose the right service for you.

## Tips
- Please make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for further assistance.
- Once you have submitted the form, you will receive a confirmation email with further instructions.
