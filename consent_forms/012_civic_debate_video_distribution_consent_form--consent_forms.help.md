# Civic Debate Video Distribution Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to capture consent for the distribution of video recordings of debates or events. It aims to inform participants of their rights and obligations regarding video distribution and ensure that they understand the conditions under which their recordings may be shared.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form carefully, ensuring that you understand each section.
2. Answer all required fields (marked as "Required" in the form).
3. Review each section carefully before signing the form.
4. If you have any doubts or concerns, please reach out to the relevant authorities for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Full Name, Text, Required): Please enter your full name as it is to be displayed on the video recording.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, Email, Required): Please enter your email address to ensure we can contact you with any questions or concerns.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, Text, Required): Please enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you with any questions or concerns.
* **Age Confirmation** (I confirm I am 18 or older, Select One, Required): Please confirm that you are 18 years or older. If you are under 18, please ensure that a legal guardian has signed this form on your behalf.
* **Video Recording Consent** (Do you consent to being recorded during the debate, Select One, Required): Please indicate your consent to being recorded during the debate.
* **Purpose of Recording** (Text, Optional): Please describe the purpose of the recording, if applicable.
* **Video Distribution Consent** (Do you consent to distribution of the video, Select One, Required): Please indicate your consent to the distribution of the video recording.
* **Acceptable Distribution Platforms** (Select all that apply, Select Multiple, Optional): Please select the platforms where you consent to the distribution of your video recording.
* **Geographic Distribution Limitations** (Select One, Optional): Please select the geographic limitations for the distribution of your video recording.
* **Distribution Duration** (How long may the video be distributed, Select One, Optional): Please select the duration for which you consent to the distribution of your video recording.
* **I understand I can withdraw consent in writing at any time** (Select One, Required): Please indicate that you understand you can withdraw consent in writing at any time.
* **Do you wish to remain anonymous in the video** (Select One, Optional): Please indicate if you wish to remain anonymous in the video recording.
* **Consent to use your name for attribution if not anonymous** (Consent to use your name for attribution if not anonymous, Select One, Optional): Please indicate your consent to use your name for attribution.
* **I have read and understand all terms and consent to them** (Select One, Required): Please indicate that you have read and understand all the terms and conditions.
* **Date of Consent** (Date, Required): Please enter the date you are providing consent.
* **Is legal guardian consent required** (Select One, Optional): Please indicate if a legal guardian's consent is required (this section is only applicable if you answered "No" to the age confirmation question).

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have reviewed the form carefully before signing it.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please reach out to the relevant authorities for clarification.
* Please note that this form is a legal document, and any inaccuracies or misunderstandings may impact the validity of the consent provided.
