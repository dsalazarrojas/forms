# textbook_content_review_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information from individuals who review textbook content. It helps to evaluate and provide feedback on the content, ensuring it aligns with the institution's standards and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the provided textbook content carefully.
2. Evaluate the content using the options provided (Accept without modification, Accept with modification, or Reject).
3. Enter any comments or observations about the content.
4. Provide your name and contact information (email and phone number).
5. Enter the name, email, and phone number of the person who requested the review (Reviewee).
6. Enter any additional comments or observations.
7. Enter the name of the person submitting the form (Submitted By).
8. Enter the email and phone number of the person submitting the form.
9. Optionally, enter the school ID, school district name, and other relevant details if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Textbook Evaluation**: Evaluate the content using one of the provided options (Accept without modification, Accept with modification, or Reject).
* **Comments**: Provide any comments or observations about the content.
* **Submitted By**: Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Submitted Email**: Enter your email address for contact information.
* **Submitted Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact information.
* **Reviewee Name**: Enter the name of the Reviewee who requested the review.
* **Reviewee Email**: Enter the email address of the Reviewee.
* **Reviewee Phone**: Enter the phone number of the Reviewee.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or observations.
* **School ID**: Enter the school ID if applicable.
* **School District Name**: Enter the name of the school district if applicable.
* **Textbook Title**: Enter the title of the textbook.
* **Textbook Year**: Enter the year of the textbook.
* **Textbook Edition**: Enter the edition of the textbook.
* **User Name**: Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact information.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact information.
* **Publisher Name**: Enter the name of the publisher.
* **School District**: Enter the name of the school district.
* **Curriculum Team**: Enter the name of the curriculum team.
* **Date Completed**: Date and time of completion, in the format `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS`.
* **Time Completed**: Time of completion.
* **Date Submitted**: Date and time of submission, in the format `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS`.
* **Time Submitted**: Time of submission.
* **Submitted By**: Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Submitted Email**: Enter your email address for contact information.
* **Submitted Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact information.
* **School District Name**: Enter the name of the school district.
* **School ID**: Enter the school ID.

Note: Please make sure to review the fields carefully and fill them out accurately to ensure that the form is completed correctly.
