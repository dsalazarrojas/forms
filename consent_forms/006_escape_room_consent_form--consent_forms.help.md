# Escape Room Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Escape Room Consent Form is used to collect information from students, parents, and teachers for an escape room event. The form ensures that all necessary consents and agreements are in place before the event, promoting a safe and enjoyable experience for everyone involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. **Fill in your personal details**, such as student name, contact email, phone number, and date of birth.
2. **Choose your relationship**, as an emergency contact, parent, guardian, or teacher.
3. **Provide your teacher's name** and school name if you are a teacher.
4. **Check the correct date and time** for the event.
5. **Select the duration** of the event in minutes.
6. **Choose the event location** whether it's on-site or off-site.
7. **Select the type of event**, such as escape room challenge or team challenge.
8. **Make any comments** that you would like to share with the teachers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Name**: Enter your name, which is required for identification purposes.
- **Team Name**: Enter the name of your team, if you are participating with a team.
- **Teacher Name**: If you are a teacher, enter your name and your school's name.
- **Contact Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
- **Contact Phone**: Enter a valid phone number where you can be reached.
- **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth for age verification purposes.
- **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
- **Emergency Contact Info**: Enter any additional information about your emergency contact, such as their relationship with you.
- **Parent Consent**: Choose whether you are a parent or guardian giving consent for your child to participate in the event.
- **Student Consent**: Choose whether you give your consent for participating in the event.
- **Date of Participation**: Choose the date of the event.
- **Teacher Consent**: Choose whether your teacher gives consent for participating in the event.
- **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Choose your relationship with your emergency contact person.
- **Student Agreement**: Check the boxes to agree that you will not touch any objects or devices that are not allowed and that you will not push or pull any objects that are not allowed.
- **Emergency Contact Agreement**: Check the boxes to agree that you will not touch any objects or devices that are not allowed and that you will not push or pull any objects that are not allowed.
- **School Name**: Enter your school's name if you are a teacher.
- **School Grade**: Choose your grade level if you are a student.
- **Room Name**: Enter the name of the room where the event will take place.
- **Student Count**: Enter the number of students participating.
- **Event Date**: Choose the date of the event.
- **Event Time**: Choose the time of the event.
- **Event Time Duration**: Choose the duration of the event in minutes.
- **Event Location**: Choose whether the event will be held on-site or off-site.
- **Event Type**: Select the type of event you are participating in.
- **Teacher Comments**: Provide any comments that you would like to share with the teachers.
