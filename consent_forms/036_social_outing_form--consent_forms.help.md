# social_outing_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The social outing form is used to collect information about outgoings, allowing users to record details such as outing type, date, time, location, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter the requested details for the outing in the designated fields.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
* If a field is not required, you can leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Outing Request**: Enter a brief description of the outing.
* **Date**: Select the date of the outing.
* **Time**: Select the time of the outing.
* **Outing Type**: Select the type of outing (e.g., Social, Family, Medical).
* **Patient Name**: Enter the name of the patient or individual going on the outing.
* **Patient Contact**: Enter the patient's contact information.
* **Healthcare Provider**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider involved in the outing.
* **Location**: Enter the location of the outing.

## Tips
* Ensure that all relevant information is provided to ensure accurate and complete records.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
