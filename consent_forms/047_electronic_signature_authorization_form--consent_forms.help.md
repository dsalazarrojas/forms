# Electronic Signature Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to authorize electronic signatures on various documents within your organization. Please provide the necessary information to authenticate the electronic signature.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Organization**: Enter the name of your organization. This field is optional.
2. **Signature Date**: Provide the date on which you signed the document. This field is optional.
3. **Document ID**: Enter the ID number of the document being signed. This field is optional.
4. **Authorization Type**: Select the type of authorization that allows you to sign the document. Options include:
	* id prophets
	* id administrators
	* id other
5. **Authorized by**: Enter the name of the person authorizing the signature. This field is optional.
6. **Signature Level**: Select the level of signature authorization you are granting. Options include:
	* general
	* executive
	* other
7. **Document Type**: Select the type of document being signed. Options include:
	* contracts
	* policies
	* other
8. **Authorized Date**: Enter the date on which you were authorized to sign. This field is optional.
9. **Signature Location**: Enter the location where you signed the document. This field is optional.
10. **Document Location**: Enter the location where the document is stored or kept. This field is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Organization**: The name of your organization that is authorizing the electronic signature.
- **Signature Date**: The date on which you signed the document.
- **Document ID**: The ID number of the document being signed.
- **Authorization Type**: The type of authority that allows you to sign the document, such as a specific role or position within your organization.
- **Authorized by**: The name of the person or entity that is authorizing the signature.
- **Signature Level**: The level of signature authorization you are granting, such as general or executive.
- **Document Type**: The type of document being signed, such as a contract or policy.
- **Authorized Date**: The date on which you were authorized to sign the document.
- **Signature Location**: The location where you signed the document.
- **Document Location**: The location where the document is stored or kept.
