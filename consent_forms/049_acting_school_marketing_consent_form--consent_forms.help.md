# Acting School Marketing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for students and their guardians to grant consent for the marketing and promotional use of their image, voice, and performance recordings by the Acting School.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. If the student is under 18, enter the parent or guardian's name in the "Parent / Guardian Name" field.
3. Enter a valid email address in the "Contact Email" field.
4. Read and agree to the "Consent Terms" section by clicking on the "Media Consent" field. You can either grant or deny consent.
5. Select the platforms where you would like your media to be shared, if applicable.
6. Enter the date you are granting consent in the "Consent Start Date" field.
7. Confirm that you understand you can revoke this consent in writing at any time in the "Revocation Right" field.
8. Sign your full name in the "Digital Signature" field.
9. Enter the date of your signature in the "Date of Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name (Student Name, text, required)**: Please fill in the student's name as it should appear for marketing purposes.
* **Parent / Guardian Name (Parent / Guardian Name, text, required if student is under 18)**: If the student is under 18, please enter the name of the parent or guardian providing consent.
* **Contact Email (Contact Email, email, required)**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **-- Consent Terms -- (Consent Terms, note, not required)**: This section outlines the terms and conditions of consent, please read carefully and agree or disagree.
* **I hereby grant permission to the Acting School to use my image, voice, and performance recordings for marketing and promotional purposes. (Consent Text, note, not required)**: This is a statement explaining what you are granting consent for.
* **Media Consent (Media Consent, select_one, required)**: Select whether you grant or deny consent for the use of your media.
* **Select where your media can be shared (Allowed Platforms, select_multiple, not required)**: If you grant consent, select the platforms where you would like your media to be shared.
* **Consent Start Date (Consent Start Date, date, required)**: Enter the date you are granting consent.
* **I understand I can revoke this consent in writing at any time (Revocation Right, select_one, required)**: Confirm that you understand you can revoke consent in writing at any time.
* **Type full name (Digital Signature, text, required)**: Sign your full name as a digital signature.
* **Date of Signature (Date of Signature, date, required)**: Enter the date of your digital signature.

## Tips
- Make sure to review the Consent Terms section carefully before granting consent.
- If the student is under 18, please ensure the parent or guardian's consent is obtained.
- Enter a valid email address to allow communication.
- Select the platforms where you would like your media to be shared, if applicable.
- Confirm that you understand you can revoke consent in writing at any time.
- Ensure your digital signature is legible and accurate.
