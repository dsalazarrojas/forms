# health_ai_transparency_research_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect general information, participant details, AI study specifics, consent information, data capture details, AI study participants, participant privacy, and form submission details for a health-related AI transparency research project.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each section carefully and answer all the questions truthfully.
2. Enter your general information, participant details, and any other required information.
3. Provide details about the AI study, including its purpose and duration.
4. Review the consent information and confirm your understanding.
5. Capture any additional data capture requirements.
6. List the AI study participants and provide their contact information (if applicable).
7. Declare your understanding and preferences for participant privacy.
8. Review and submit the form to complete the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information**:
	+ (`title`, `type`: text): Please provide your general information, which will help us understand your perspective on AI transparency research.
* **Participant Information**:
	+ (`participant_info`, `type`: text): Please provide details about the participant(s) involved in the study.
* **AI Study Information**:
	+ (`ai_study_info`, `type`: text): Please describe the AI study, including its purpose and duration.
* **Consent Information**:
	+ (`consent_info`, `type`: text): Review and confirm your understanding of the consent information.
* **Data Capture**:
	+ (`data_capture`, `type`: text): Describe any additional data capture requirements for the study.
* **AI Study Participants**:
	+ (`ai_study_participants`, `type`: text): List the AI study participants and provide their contact information (if applicable).
* **Participant Privacy**:
	+ (`participant_privacy`, `type`: text): Declare your understanding and preferences for participant privacy.
* **Form Submission**:
	+ (`form_submission`, `type`: text): Review and submit the form to complete the process.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when providing information.
* Review each field carefully before proceeding.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
