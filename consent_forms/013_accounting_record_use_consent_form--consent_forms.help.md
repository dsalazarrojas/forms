# Accounting Record Use Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain consent from clients or entities for the use of their accounting records by an accounting firm. The form captures the necessary information to ensure that the firm is authorized to use the records for specific purposes, such as general ledger, tax returns, or audit reports.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the form accurately:

1.  Enter the **Client or Entity Name** in the field provided.
2.  Enter the **Authorized Representative** name and name of the person granting permission.
3.  Select all the **Records Authorized for Use** that apply to the services you want to perform.
4.  Specify the **Purpose of Record Use** for the authorized records.
5.  Enter the **Authorization Period Start** and **End** dates for the consent.
6.  Confirm that you agree to the **Confidentiality Agreement**.
7.  Enter your **Contact Email** for revocation requests.
8.  Type your **Digital Signature Authorization** with your full name.
9.  Enter the **Date Signed** for the consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client or Entity Name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of the client or entity for whom you are seeking consent.
- **Authorized Representative** (2, text, required): Enter the name of the person granting permission on behalf of the client or entity.
- **Records Authorized for Use** (3, select_multiple, required): Select all the records you are authorized to use, such as general ledger, tax returns, or audit reports.
- **Purpose of Record Use** (4, text, required): Describe the reason why you need to use the records, as specified above.
- **Authorization Period Start** (5, date, required): Enter the start date of the authorization period for which you are seeking consent.
- **Authorization Period End** (6, date, required): Enter the end date of the authorization period for which you are seeking consent.
- **Confidentiality Agreement** (7, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to the confidentiality terms.
- **Contact for Revocation** (8, email, required): Enter the email address where you can be contacted if you wish to revoke consent.
- **Digital Signature Authorization** (9, text, required): Type your full name as a digital signature.
- **Date Signed** (10, date, required): Enter the date you signed the consent.

## Tips

*   Be honest and accurate when completing the form.
*   Review the form carefully to ensure that you understand the purpose of each field.
*   If you are unsure about any part of the form, contact the accounting firm for clarification.
*   Keep the **Date Signed** up-to-date as consent is only valid for the authorized period.
