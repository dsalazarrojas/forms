# High Frequency Skin Treatment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The High Frequency Skin Treatment Consent Form is a document that clients must sign to acknowledge and agree to the terms and conditions of their high frequency skin treatment. This form is a legal and regulatory requirement and must be completed by the client before the treatment can begin.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your client details in the "Client Details" section.
2. Provide your health information in the "Health Information" section.
3. Check the box next to "Consent" to indicate that you have read and understand the terms and conditions of the treatment.
4. Enter your contact details in the "Contact Details" section.
5. List any medical history you have in the "Medical History" section.
6. Check any medical conditions that apply to you in the "Medical Conditions" section.
7. List any medical devices you use in the "Medical Devices" section.
8. Check any allergies you have in the "Allergies" section.
9. Sign and date the form in the "Signature" section.
10. If applicable, select one option from the "Select One" section.
11. If applicable, select multiple options from the "Select Multiple" section.
12. Enter any additional notes about your treatment in the "Note" section.
13. Enter your email address in the "Email" section.
14. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details**: Please enter your name, address, and contact information.
* **Health Information**: Please provide any relevant health information you think is necessary for your treatment.
* **Consent**: Please check this box to indicate that you have read and understand the terms and conditions of the treatment.
* **Contact Details**: Please enter your contact information so we can reach you before, during, and after the treatment.
* **Medical History**: Please list any medical history you have that may impact your treatment.
* **Medical Conditions**: Please check any medical conditions that apply to you.
* **Medical Devices**: Please list any medical devices you use.
* **Allergies**: Please check any allergies you have.
* **Signature**: Please sign and date this form to confirm that you have read and understand the terms and conditions of the treatment.
* **Select One**: Please select one option from the drop-down menu. 
* **Select Multiple**: Please select multiple options from the drop-down menu.
* **Note**: Please enter any additional notes about your treatment.
* **Email**: Please enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone**: Please enter your phone number so we can contact you.

Note: This form is only a sample and actual content and fields may be adjusted based on your actual requirements and business needs.
