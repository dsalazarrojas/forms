# Onboarding Financing Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Onboarding Financing Agreement form is used to document the terms of a financing agreement between a company and a customer. It ensures that both parties have a clear understanding of the agreement's terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form accurately and carefully.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Review the form for any errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer ID** (customer_id): Enter the customer's unique identifier.
* **Financing Requested Amount** (financing_requested_amount): Enter the amount of financing requested by the customer.
* **Financing Requested Term** (financing_requested_term): Select 'Yes' if the customer is requesting financing, 'No' otherwise.
* **Financing Requested Use of Funds** (financing_requested_use_of_funds): Enter the intended use of the financing funds.
* **Financing Terms and Conditions Accepted** (financing_terms_and_conditions_accepted): Select 'Yes' if the customer accepts the financing terms and conditions, 'No' otherwise.
* **Applicant Consent** (applicant_consent): Select 'Yes' if the customer consents to the agreement, 'No' otherwise.
* **Onboarding Financing Agreement Signed** (onboarding_financing_agreement_signed): Select 'Yes' if the agreement is signed, 'No' otherwise.
* **Date** (date): Enter the date of signing.
* **Time** (time): Enter the time of signing.
* **Email** (email): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone** (phone): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Financing Terms and Conditions** (financing_terms_and_conditions): Enter the terms and conditions of the financing agreement.
* **Onboarding Financing Agreement Form Version** (onboarding_financing_agreement_form_version): Enter the version of the agreement form used.
* **Onboarding Financing Agreement Form Version Comment** (onboarding_financing_agreement_form_version_comment): Enter any comments about the form version used.
* **Onboarding Financing Agreement Signed Version** (onboarding_financing_agreement_signed_version): Enter the version of the signed agreement.
* **Onboarding Financing Agreement Signed Version Comment** (onboarding_financing_agreement_signed_version_comment): Enter any comments about the signed agreement version.
* **Onboarding Financing Agreement Signed By** (onboarding_financing_agreement_signed_by): Enter the name of the person signing the agreement.
* **Onboarding Financing Agreement Signed Date** (onboarding_financing_agreement_signed_date): Enter the date of signing.
* **Onboarding Financing Agreement Signed Time** (onboarding_financing_agreement_signed_time): Enter the time of signing.
* **Onboarding Financing Agreement Signed IP** (onboarding_financing_agreement_signed_ip): Enter the IP address of the person signing the agreement.
* **Onboarding Financing Agreement Signed By Title** (onboarding_financing_agreement_signed_by_title): Enter the title of the person signing the agreement.
* **Onboarding Financing Agreement Signed By Name** (onboarding_financing_agreement_signed_by_name): Enter the name of the person signing the agreement.
* **Onboarding Financing Agreement Signed By Initials** (onboarding_financing_agreement_signed_by_initials): Enter the initials of the person signing the agreement.
* **Onboarding Financing Agreement Signed By Signature** (onboarding_financing_agreement_signed_by_signature): Enter the signature of the person signing the agreement.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
* Review the form carefully to avoid any errors or discrepancies.
* Keep the form up-to-date with the latest version.
* Store the signed agreement in a secure location.
* Make sure to include all required fields for signing.
