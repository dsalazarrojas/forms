# wellness_retreat_testimonial_release_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect testimonials from participants after a wellness retreat. This form is used to gather information about the participant's experience and to obtain their consent for publication of their testimonial. This form is typically used by organizations that host wellness retreats to collect feedback and evaluate the effectiveness of their program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by entering your name as a participant.
2. In the "Testimonial" field, provide a detailed and honest review of your experience at the retreat.
3. Select the publication options for your testimonial.
4. Enter your email address so we can contact you for further information.
5. If you'd like, you can also enter your phone number for easier contact.
6. Confirm your permission for us to share your testimonial.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Name (<participant_name>)
*   **Participant Name**: This is the name of the person who attended the wellness retreat.
    *   This field is optional, but it's helpful for us to know who provided the testimonial.
*   **Testimonial** (<testimonial>)
    *   Please write a detailed and honest review of your experience at the retreat.
    *   Share as much as you'd like about your experience, including what you enjoyed and what you didn't.
*   **Date of Testimonial (<date_of_testimonial>)**
    *   This is the date when you completed this testimonial form.
    *   Try to be as accurate as possible so we can keep track of your testimonial.
*   **Publication Permission** (<publication_permission>)
    *   Select one or more of the following options to indicate where you'd like your testimonial to be shared:
        + Published in print and digital media
        + Published on social media
        + Published on our website
*   **Email** (<email>)
    *   Enter your email address so we can contact you for further information.
    *   Make sure to enter a valid email address that we can use to reach out to you.
*   **Phone** (<phone>)
    *   If you'd like, enter your phone number for easier contact.
    *   This is optional, and only enter it if you're comfortable sharing your number.
*   **Permission** (<permission>)
    *   Confirm that you grant permission for us to share your testimonial.
    *   Select 'Yes' to confirm that you agree to share your testimonial.
