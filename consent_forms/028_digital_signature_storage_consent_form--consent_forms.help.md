# Digital Signature Storage Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user consent for the storage of their digital signatures. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question honestly and carefully.
2. Provide your digital signature as requested by the form (if applicable).
3. Review and agree to the terms of digital signature storage.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### User Consent
* **User Consent** (`user_consent`, text, optional): Please indicate your understanding and agreement to the storage of your digital signatures on behalf of <organization/entity name>. 

*   Provide a brief explanation of your understanding of your signature's storage.

### Signature Storage
* **Signature Storage** (`signature_storage`, text, optional): Please indicate where your digital signatures will be stored (e.g. internal servers, external servers, etc. 

*   This is the location where your digital signatures will be stored.

### Signature Retention
* **Signature Retention** (`signature_retention`, text, optional): Please indicate for how long your digital signatures will be stored (e.g. until revoked, 1 year, 5 years, etc. 

*   This is the period during which your digital signatures will be kept in storage.

### Consent to Store
* **Consent to Store** (`consent_to_store`, text, optional): Please confirm that you give your consent for your digital signatures to be stored as outlined below: 

*   This confirms your agreement to store your digital signatures as described in previous fields.

### Storage Location
* **Storage Location** (`storage_location`, text, optional): The location where your digital signatures will be stored (e.g. internal servers, external servers, etc. 

*   This is the specific location where your digital signatures will be kept.

## Tips

*   Review each field carefully and provide accurate information to ensure proper understanding and agreement on digital signature storage.
*   If you have any questions or doubts regarding digital signature storage, please contact <organization/entity name> for clarification.
