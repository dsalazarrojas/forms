# Covid 19 Liability Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Covid 19 Liability Acknowledgment Form is designed to document a participant's acknowledgment of the risks associated with Covid-19 and their understanding of the safety measures in place to mitigate these risks. This form ensures that participants are aware of and agree to adhere to the protocols and guidelines set forth by the organization or entity hosting the form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Participant Name** in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Enter your **Address** in the "Address" field.
3. Provide your **Phone Number** in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field (if available).
5. Select all applicable **Covid-19 Risk Protocols** that you are aware of or understand. These include:
	* Wearing masks/face shields
	* Practicing social distancing
	* Regularly cleaning/disinfecting
	* Providing hand sanitizer
	* Temperature checks
	* Limiting capacity
	* Staggered shifts
6. Select all applicable **Safety Protocols** that you are aware of or understand. These include:
	* Regularly disinfecting
	* Providing hand sanitizer
	* Temperature checks
	* Limiting capacity
	* Wearing masks/face shields
	* Providing PPE
	* Staggered shifts
7. Write a declaration in the **Participant Declaration** field, stating your understanding and agreement to adhere to the Covid-19 risk protocols and safety measures.
8. Finally, sign your name in the **Participant Signature** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**
	+ Label: Participant Name
	+ A text field to fill in your name.
* **Address**
	+ Label: Address
	+ A text field to enter your contact address.
* **Phone Number**
	+ Label: Phone Number
	+ A text field to input your contact phone number.
* **Email**
	+ Label: Email
	+ A text field to enter your email address (if available).
* **Covid-19 Risk Protocols**
	+ Label: Covid-19 Risk Protocols
	+ A multiple-select field with the following options:
		- Wearing masks/face shields
		- Practicing social distancing
		- Regularly cleaning/disinfecting
		- Providing hand sanitizer
		- Temperature checks
		- Limiting capacity
		- Staggered shifts
		- Other (please specify)
* **Safety Protocols**
	+ Label: Safety Protocols
	+ A multiple-select field with the following options:
		- Regularly disinfecting
		- Providing hand sanitizer
		- Temperature checks
		- Limiting capacity
		- Wearing masks/face shields
		- Providing PPE
		- Staggered shifts
	+ This field is required to be filled, as it documents your understanding of the safety measures in place.
* **Participant Declaration**
	+ Label: Participant Declaration
	+ A note field to write a declaration stating your understanding and agreement to adhere to the Covid-19 risk protocols and safety measures.
* **Participant Signature**
	+ Label: Participant Signature
	+ A signature field to sign your name, indicating your agreement.
* **Date Signed**
	+ Label: Date Signed
	+ A date field to input the date you signed the form.

## Tips
- Please fill in all required fields accurately and truthfully.
- Ensure you understand each field's purpose and requirements before proceeding.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to the form's purpose and content for clarification.
- Complete the form carefully, as it is a critical document for risk assessment and liability purposes.
