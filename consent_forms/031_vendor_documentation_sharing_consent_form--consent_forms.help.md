# vendor_documentation_sharing_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The vendor documentation sharing consent form is used to obtain the consent of a vendor to share their documentation with your organization. This form is typically used when you need to share sensitive or proprietary information with a vendor and want to ensure they understand and agree to the terms of sharing their documentation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the form carefully and ensure you understand the questions and options.
2. For the "vendor_documentation_sharing_agreement" field, select either "Yes" or "No" to indicate your preference.
3. If you select "Yes", provide the date you are agreeing to share the documentation in the "vendor_documentation_sharing_agreement_date" field.
4. Review your selections before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Documentation Sharing Consent** (vendor_documentation_sharing_consent, note, required: false): 
This field is a note where you can provide any additional comments or information related to the form.
* **Vendor Documentation Sharing Agreement** (vendor_documentation_sharing_agreement, select_multiple, required: false): 
Select "Yes" if you agree to share the vendor's documentation with the relevant stakeholders, and "No" if you do not.
* **Vendor Documentation Sharing Agreement Date** (vendor_documentation_sharing_agreement_date, date, required: false): 
If you select "Yes" for the vendor documentation sharing agreement field, enter the date you are agreeing to share the documentation.
