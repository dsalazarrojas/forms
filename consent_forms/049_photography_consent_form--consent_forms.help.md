# Photography Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for collecting consent from subjects (individuals or groups) being photographed. It captures essential information such as the subject's name, email, and phone number, as well as the photographer's name, email, and phone number. The form also captures the release date of the images, which is typically the date the photos were taken.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the photography session in the "Photography Consent Form" field.
2. Enter your name and email in the "Subject Name" and "Subject Email" fields, respectively.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Subject Phone" field.
4. Select the release date of the images (yyyy-MM-dd) in the "Release Date" field.
5. Enter the name of the photographer in the "Photographer Name" field.
6. Enter the email address of the photographer in the "Photographer Email" field.
7. Enter the phone number of the photographer in the "Photographer Phone" field.
8. Select the desired image release options for the subject in the "Image Release" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Photography Consent Form** (`photography_consent_form_title`, text, optional)
	+ This field is where you enter the title of the photography session.
* **Subject Name** (`subject_name`, text, optional)
	+ This field is where you enter your name as the subject of the photography session.
* **Subject Email** (`subject_email`, email, optional)
	+ This field is where you enter your email address as the subject of the photography session.
* **Subject Phone** (`subject_phone`, text, optional)
	+ This field is where you enter your phone number as the subject of the photography session.
* **Image Release** (`subject_image_release`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ This field allows you to select the desired options for image release. Please choose the options that apply to you.
* **Photographer Name** (`photographer_name`, text, optional)
	+ This field is where the photographer enters their name.
* **Photographer Email** (`photographer_email`, email, optional)
	+ This field is where the photographer enters their email address.
* **Photographer Phone** (`photographer_phone`, text, optional)
	+ This field is where the photographer enters their phone number.
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, date, optional)
	+ This field is where you enter the date the images were taken.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your consent is valid.
* Be sure to select all applicable image release options that apply to you.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the photographer or the project manager.
