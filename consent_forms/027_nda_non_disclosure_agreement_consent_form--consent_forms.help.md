# NDA (Non Disclosure Agreement) Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The NDA (Non Disclosure Agreement) Consent Form is a document used to obtain consent from individuals to keep confidential information related to a project, business, or other sensitive topic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your First name.
2. Enter your Last name.
3. Provide your Email address.
4. Select your Role (check one of the provided options).
5. Select one or more Departments you are involved in.
6. If required, enter the Date and Time (in your local time zone).
7. If not required, enter your Company name (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (First name, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Last name** (Last name, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Email** (Email, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address associated with your official identification documents.
* **Role** (Role, select_one, required): Select your job title or position from the provided options.
* **Department** (Department, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more departments you are involved with (if not required).
* **Date** (Date, date, required/optional): If required, enter the date in your local time zone.
* **Time** (Time, time, required/optional): If required, enter the time in your local time zone.
* **Company** (Company, text, required/optional): Enter your company name if you are representing a company (if not required).

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields marked as "required" are completed accurately to process your information correctly.
- You can select multiple Departments if your role involves multiple teams or divisions.
- If you have any issues or concerns, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
