# ferpa_release_form_for_parents - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "FERPA Release Form for Parents" is a form used to obtain parental consent and information for the release of a student's educational records and information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter the parent's name in the "parent_name" field.
3. Enter the student's name in the "student_name" field.
4. Enter the student's date of birth (in the format YYYY-MM-DD) in the "student_dob" field.
5. Enter the parent's contact phone number in the "parent_phone" field.
6. Enter the parent's email address in the "parent_email" field.
7. Sign and date the form with the "signature" field (if required).
8. Enter the date and time of submission in the "date" field.
9. Enter the time of submission in the "time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): This field is for the parent or guardian's name. Please enter your name as it appears on your ID.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): This field is for the student's name. Please enter the student's full name.
* **Student Date of Birth** (`student_dob`, text, required): This field is for the student's date of birth. Please enter the date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, required): This field is for the parent's or guardian's phone number. Please enter your phone number.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, required): This field is for the parent's or guardian's email address. Please enter your email address.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, optional): This field is for the parent's or guardian's electronic signature. If required, please print your name and date in this field.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): This field is for the date of submission. Please enter the date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): This field is for the time of submission. Please enter the time in the format HH:MM:SS.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Make sure to double-check your entries for correctness.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
* This form is a one-time submission form, not a recurring or subscription-based form.
