# Patient Signature Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for patients to sign and acknowledge their understanding of specific information. It is intended for patients who need to sign documents or confirm their understanding of certain conditions or procedures.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the corresponding fields, such as your name, email, phone number, address, and other required fields.
2. Enter your Date of Birth and Signature Date in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
3. Sign your name in the Signature field.
4. Confirm that you have read and understood the information provided by signing and dating the "Read and Understood" section.
5. Enter the required fields for the patient and staff, including their name, address, city, state, and zip code.
