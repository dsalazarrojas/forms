This form is for individuals to provide their consent for their environmental research drone footage to be collected and used for research purposes. The form is used to capture essential information, including first name, last name, date of birth, address, email, phone, drone model, drone type, research purpose, consent date, signature, and date of signature received. This information will be used to ensure the research project is conducted with the necessary consent and adherence to relevant laws and regulations.

To complete this form, start by providing your first name, followed by your last name, date of birth, address, email, and phone number. You will also need to specify the model and type of drone you are using, as well as the research purpose. Ensure that you select "Yes" for drone model and type if you will be using a drone, and "No" otherwise. You will then be asked to provide the consent date and sign the form. Finally, indicate any other research purposes that apply to you.

## Environmental Research Drone Footage Consent Form - Help Guide
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2.  Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3.  Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
4.  Enter your address in the "Address" field.
5.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7.  Enter the model of the drone you will be using in the "Drone Model" field.
8.  Select "Yes" if you will be using a drone, and "No" otherwise in the "Drone Type" field.
9.  Enter the purpose of the research in the "Research Purpose" field.
10. Enter the date you are providing your consent in the "Consent Date" field.
11.  Sign your name in the "Signature" field.
12. Enter the date you received the signed form in the "Date Signature Received" field.
13.  Select all relevant research purposes that apply to you in the "Research Purposes" field.
14. (Optional) If you have any other research purposes, enter them in the "Other (if applicable)" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   *First Name* (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name to identify yourself.
*   *Last Name* (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name to identify yourself.
*   *Date of Birth* (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth to validate your age.
*   *Address* (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your home or office address for contact purposes.
*   *Email* (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you for further information.
*   *Phone* (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   *Drone Model* (`drone_model`, `text`, required): Enter the model of the drone you will be using for the research.
*   *Drone Type* (`drone_type`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you will be using a drone by selecting "Yes" or "No".
*   *Research Purpose* (`research_purpose`, `text`, required): Enter the purpose of the research you are participating in.
*   *Consent Date* (`consent_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are providing your consent.
*   *Signature* (`signature`, `note`, required): Sign your name to confirm your consent.
*   *Date Signature Received* (`date_signature_received`, `date`, required): Enter the date you received the signed form.
*   *Research Purposes* (`research_purposes`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all research purposes that apply to you.
*   *Other (if applicable)* (`research_purposes_other`, `text`, optional): Enter any other research purposes that do not appear in the "Research Purposes" field.
