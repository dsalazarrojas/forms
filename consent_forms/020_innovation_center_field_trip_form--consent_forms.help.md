# Innovation Center Field Trip Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Innovation Center Field Trip Form is a collection of information about students and their accompanying emergency contact information. It is intended to provide necessary details for planning and executing field trips to the Innovation Center.

## How To Complete This Form
- Ensure you have the necessary information about the student, including their name, grade, and school affiliation.
- Carefully provide accurate emergency contact information, including name, phone number, and address, for a student's emergency contact person.
- Select the type of individual accompanying the student on the field trip.
- Add any relevant notes or comments as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **School Name (1)**: Enter the name of the student's school.
- **Student Name (2)**: Enter the student's name.
- **Grade (3)**: Enter the student's grade level (if not required, leave blank).
- **Student Email (4)**: Enter the student's email (if not required, leave blank).
- **Permission (5)**: Indicate whether you are a student, parent, teacher, or other.
- **Date of Birth (6)**: Enter the student's date of birth.
- **Emergency Contact Name (7)**: Enter the name of the student's emergency contact person.
- **Emergency Contact Phone (8)**: Enter the student's emergency contact phone number.
- **Emergency Contact Relationship (9)**: Indicate your relationship to the student (e.g. parent, guardian, etc.).
- **Emergency Contact Address (10)**: Enter the emergency contact person's address.
- **Emergency Contact Phone Alt (11)**: Enter an alternate phone number for the emergency contact person.
- **Emergency Contact Phone Ext (12)**: Enter the emergency contact person's phone extension (if applicable).
- **Emergency Contact Note (13)**: Add any additional emergency contact information or notes.
- **Innovation Center Agenda (14)**: Describe the student's schedule or activities for the field trip.
- **Innovation Center Agenda Time (15)**: Enter the time for the student's scheduled activity.
- **Innovation Center Agenda Location (16)**: Enter the location for the student's activity.
- **Innovation Center Agenda Note (17)**: Add any additional notes or comments.
- **Emergency Contact Note (18)**: Provide any additional information about the student's emergency contact person.
- **Student Conducted Work (19)**: Describe any work conducted by the student during or prior to the field trip.
- **Student Conducted Work Note (20)**: Add any additional notes or comments about the student's work.
- **Parent Conducted Work (21)**: Describe any work conducted by a parent during or prior to the field trip.
- **Parent Conducted Work Note (22)**: Add any additional notes or comments about the parent's work.
- **Student Conducated Work (23)**: ( Duplicate of Student Conducted Work. Duplicate removed for redundancy)
- **Student Conducated Work Note (24)**: Duplicate of Student Conducted Work Note. Removed for Redundancy)
- **Parent Conducated Work (25)**: Duplicate of Parent Conducted Work. Removed for Redundancy)

Note: Some fields were removed to avoid redundancy.
