# Counseling Confidentiality Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information related to patient confidentiality and consent in counseling settings.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your patient's information in the "Patient Information" section.
2. Enter your therapist's information in the "Therapist Information" section.
3. Review and agree to the "Confidentiality Agreement" to ensure you understand the importance of maintaining patient confidentiality.
4. Provide your consent in the "Patient Consent" section.
5. Enter emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contact" section.
6. Review and agree to the "Treatment Agreement".
7. Read and understand the "Confidentiality Statement".
8. Sign electronically with "Electronic Signature".
9. Acknowledge your understanding of the form with "Patient Acknowledgement".
10. Review and revise your entries as needed with "Review and Revision".
11. Confirm your submission with "Final Confirmation".
12. Save and exit the form with "Save and Exit".
13. Review and print the form with "Review and Print".
14. Save and print the form with "Save and Print".
15. Review and share the form with others with "Review and Share".
16. Share the form with your therapist with "Share with Therapist".
17. Review and print/share the form with "Review and Print/Share".
18. Save and print/share the form with "Save and Print/Share".
19. Review and share the form with your therapist with "Review and Share Therapist".
20. Save your therapist's entries with "Save Therapist".
21. Review your therapist's entries with "Review Therapist".
22. Print your therapist's entries with "Print Therapist".
23. Share your therapist's entries with "Share with Therapist".
