# Athlete Injury Prevention Study Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Athlete Injury Prevention Study Consent Form is a document that outlines the terms and conditions of participation in a research study on athlete injury prevention. This guide will walk you through each section of the form to ensure you understand the requirements and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out all required fields**: Each section marked as required (e.g., Study Title, Participant Full Name, Date of Birth, Email Address, Phone Number, etc.) must be filled out completely.
2. **Choose the correct option for each question**: When you see a dropdown menu or a checkbox, select the correct answer based on your understanding of the question. For example, in the "Competition Level" field, you must choose the level that accurately represents your current competitive standing.
3. **Sign the form electronically**: After filling out all required fields, sign the form electronically using your participant name. Ensure your name is typed correctly in the "Participant Electronic Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Study Title (ID 2)**: Enter the title of the study you are participating in.
- **Principal Investigator (ID 3)**: Enter the name of the person leading the research study.
- **Participant Full Name (ID 5)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., passport, driver's license).
- **Date of Birth (ID 6)**: Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
- **Email Address (ID 7)**: Enter your contact email address (e.g., example@domain.com).
- **Phone Number (ID 8)**: Enter your contact phone number.
- **Primary Sport (ID 9)**: Choose the sport you primarily participate in.
- **Current Competition Level (ID 10)**: Select the level that best describes your current competitive status (e.g., Professional, Semi-Professional, Collegiate, High School / Academy, Amateur / Club).
- **Informed Consent (ID 11)**: This section outlines the purpose and objectives of the research study. Please read each statement carefully and acknowledge your understanding.
- **Purpose Acknowledgment (ID 12)**: Indicate that you understand the purpose and objectives of the study by selecting "I acknowledge".
- **Procedures Acknowledgment (ID 13)**: Confirm that you understand the procedures and requirements for participation by selecting "I acknowledge".
- **Risk and Benefit Acknowledgment (ID 14)**: Acknowledge the potential risks and benefits of the study by selecting "I acknowledge".
- **Confidentiality (ID 15)**: Confirm that you understand your personal data will be kept confidential by selecting "I acknowledge".
- **Voluntary Participation (ID 16)**: Acknowledge that your participation is entirely voluntary by selecting "I acknowledge".
- **Withdrawal Rights (ID 17)**: Confirm that you understand you have the right to withdraw from the study at any time by selecting "I acknowledge".
- **Data Usage (ID 18)**: Consent to the anonymized use of your data for academic publication by selecting "Yes, I consent".
- **Participant Signature (ID 19)**: Type your full name to confirm your electronic signature.
- **Parent or Guardian Signature (ID 20)**: If applicable, enter the signature of your parent or guardian, especially for participants under the age of legal consent.
- **Date of Consent (ID 21)**: Enter the date you sign this consent form in YYYY-MM-DD format.
