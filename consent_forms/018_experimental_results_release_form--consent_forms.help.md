# Experimental Results Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Experimental Results Release Form is designed for researchers and investigators to collect and document information on experimental results release to collaborators, peers, or other stakeholders. The form helps ensure that experimental results are shared and handled properly, with clear conditions and consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your user input details in the "User Input" field.
2.  Provide recipient information in the "Recipient Information" field.
3.  Enter the recipient's email address in the "Recipient Email" field.
4.  Select the purpose of the release from the "Release Purpose" dropdown menu.
5.  Choose whether the release condition is clear or not in the "Release Conditions" dropdown menu.
6.  Provide details about the experiment in the "Experiment Details" field.
7.  Enter the release date in the "Release Date" field.
8.  List any pseudonyms used in the experiment in the "Pseudonyms" field.
9.  Add any release notes in the "Release Notes" field.
10. Confirm the recipient's consent in the "Recipient Consent" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`title`, `text`, required): Enter your name and any relevant contact information.
* **Recipient Information** (`title`, `text`, required): Provide the name and title of the person or organization receiving the results.
* **Recipient Email** (`title`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the recipient.
* **Release Purpose** (`name`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the purpose of releasing the experimental results (e.g., to share with a collaborator, to obtain feedback, or for further investigation and publication).
* **Release Conditions** (`name`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether the release condition is clear or not.
* **Experiment Details** (`name`, `text`, required): Provide a description of the experiment, including any relevant context or background information.
* **Release Date** (`name`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the results are to be released.
* **Pseudonyms** (`name`, `text`, required): List any pseudonyms used in the experiment, if applicable.
* **Release Notes** (`name`, `text`, required): Add any additional notes or context related to the release.
* **Recipient Consent** (`name`, `select_one`, required): Confirm the recipient's consent, which can be "Given", "Withdrawn", or "Unknown".
