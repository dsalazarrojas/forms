# Media Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Media Release Form is used to capture information about a media release, including the media outlet, story title, story type, and release date. It is designed to ensure that all necessary permissions are obtained and that the terms of the release are clearly understood.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the media outlet from the list of options.
2. Enter the story title and type (e.g. breaking news, feature article, opinion piece).
3. Enter the story date (if known).
4. Enter the photographer's name and contact information.
5. Select the photographer's agreement (yes or no).
6. Enter the date of the agreement (if applicable).
7. Enter any additional notes related to the agreement.
8. Select the release type (print, digital, or both).
9. Enter the release expiration date.
10. Enter the release expiration time.
11. Enter any additional notes related to the release.
12. Select the type of media releaser (public domain, private person, company, or organization).
13. Enter the media releaser's title.
14. Enter the date of birth of the media releaser.
15. Enter the media releaser's address.
16. Enter the media releaser's phone number.
17. Enter the media releaser's email address.
18. Enter any notes or additional information related to the media agreement.
19. Enter the version number of the media agreement.
20. Enter the date of the media agreement.
21. Enter the media agreement signature.
22. Enter any additional notes or information related to the media agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Media Outlet**: Select the media outlet where the story will be published (e.g. ABC News, CBS News, FOX News).
* **Story Title**: Enter the title of the story (e.g. "Media Release Form Help Guide").
* **Story Type**: Select the type of story (e.g. breaking news, feature article, opinion piece).
* **Media Releaser**: Select the type of media releaser (public domain, private person, company, or organization).
* **Media Releaser Title**: Enter the title of the media releaser (e.g. John Doe).
* **Date of Birth of Media Releaser**: Enter the date of birth of the media releaser (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Release Address**: Enter the address of the media releaser (e.g. 123 Main St, Anytown, USA).
* **Release Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the media releaser (e.g. 555-555-5555).
* **Release Email Address**: Enter the email address of the media releaser (e.g. johndoe@example.com).
* **Media Agreement**: Enter any notes or additional information related to the media agreement.
* **Media Agreement Version**: Enter the version number of the media agreement.
* **Date of Media Agreement**: Enter the date of the media agreement (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Media Agreement Signature**: Enter the media agreement signature.
* **Release Expiration Date**: Enter the release expiration date (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Release Expiration Time**: Enter the release expiration time (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Release Type**: Select the type of release (print, digital, or both).
* **Photographer's Credit**: Enter the photographer's name and contact information.
* **Photographer's Agreement**: Select the photographer's agreement (yes or no).
* **Date of Agreement**: Enter the date of the agreement (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Agreement Notes**: Enter any additional notes related to the agreement.
* **Media Release Notes**: Enter any additional notes related to the media release.
* **Media Releaser Agreement**: Select the media releaser's agreement (yes or no).
* **Media Releaser Agreement Date**: Enter the date of the media releaser's agreement (MM/DD/YYYY).

Note: Please fill out the form carefully and accurately, and make sure to review and understand the terms of the media release before submitting it. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
