# Employee Drug Testing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Drug Testing Consent Form is used to obtain the consent of employees for drug testing as a condition of employment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and requirements.
2. Fill in your name and reason for testing (if applicable) in the designated fields.
3. Select the type of drug test you are consenting to (e.g., Urine Test, Hair Test, etc.).
4. Choose the date you are consenting to undergo the test.
5. Sign the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee**: This field requires your name.
* **Reason For Testing**: Enter a brief explanation for the reason you are being tested (e.g., random testing, post-accident testing, etc.).
* **Drug Test Type**: Select the type of drug test you are consenting to. Please choose one of the following options:
	+ Urine Test
	+ Hair Test
	+ Blood Test
	+ Oral Test
	+ Breath Test
* **Consent Date**: Enter the date you are consenting to undergo the test.
* **Employee Signature**: Please sign the form to confirm your consent. This field is required for the form to be complete.

## Tips
* Ensure you understand the purpose and requirements of the form before completing it.
* Take a moment to review the form carefully and ask questions if you are unsure about any part of it.
* Complete the form accurately and honestly, as your signature serves as confirmation of your consent.
