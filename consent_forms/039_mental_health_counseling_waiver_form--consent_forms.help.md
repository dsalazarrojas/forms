# Mental Health Counseling Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is to be completed by clients before commencing mental health services, to obtain their written consent for the treatment or service they are seeking. It is a legal document that acknowledges the client's understanding of the risks involved and their agreement to the terms and conditions of the service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and date on the first page.
2. Select the type of mental health services you are seeking.
3. Read and acknowledge the risks involved with mental health services.
4. Agree to the terms and conditions of the service.
5. Provide information on your mental health counseling goals and treatment plan.
6. Provide emergency contact information for someone who can be reached in case of an emergency.
7. Sign the form to confirm your consent and understanding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Fill in your name as it is currently written on your identification card or passport.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required): Fill in the current date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Mental Health Counseling Services** (`mental_health_counseling_services`, select_multiple, required): Select all services you are seeking, such as:
	+ Cognitive Therapy
	+ Individual Counseling
	+ Group Counseling
	+ Family Therapy
* **Acknowledgment of Risks** (`acknowledgment_of_risks`, select_one, required): Select the option that best describes your understanding of the risks involved with mental health services:
	+ I have read and understand the risks involved with mental health services.
	+ I have not read and do not understand the risks involved with mental health services.
* **Client Agreement** (`client_agreement`, select_one, required): Select the option that best describes your agreement to the terms and conditions:
	+ I have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
	+ I have not read and do not agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Mental Health Counseling Goals** (`mental_health_counseling_goals`, text, required): Describe your mental health counseling goals, such as improving your mood or overcoming anxiety.
* **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, text, required): Outline your treatment plan, including any relevant medical history or previous therapy experiences.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Provide the name of a person who can be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Fill in the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship to Client** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Describe your relationship to the emergency contact person (e.g. family member, friend, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Fill in the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, required): Fill in the address of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, optional): Fill in the email of the emergency contact person (if available).
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required): Select the option that indicates your consent:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Signature** (`signature`, select_one, required): Select the option that indicates your signature:
	+ Signature 1
	+ Signature 2
	+ Signature 3

Please note that this form requires the client's signature to confirm their understanding and agreement to the terms and conditions.
