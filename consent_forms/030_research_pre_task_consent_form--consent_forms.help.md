# research_pre_task_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain participant consent for research activities prior to their start. It ensures participants are aware of the research activity and agree to participate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your participant information in the "Participant Information" page.
2. Select the consent options for the research activity on the "Consent" page.
3. If required, provide additional information about the research activity on the "Research Activity" page.
4. Sign the "Participant Agreement" section to confirm you understand and agree to the terms of the research.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, text, optional): Please provide your participant information, such as your name, email, and contact number. This will help us contact you with any updates or information related to the research activity.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more of the options below to indicate your consent for the research activity. Please note that you can select multiple options if applicable.
	+ Option 1: I agree to participate in the research activity.
	+ Option 2: I do not agree to participate.
	+ Option 3: I have questions or concerns about the research activity.
* **Research Activity** (`research_activity`, text, optional): If you selected option 1 or 3 for the consent field, please provide additional information about the research activity. This can include any questions or concerns you have.
* **Participant Agreement** (`participant_agreement`, note, optional): Please read and sign below to confirm you understand and agree to the terms of the research activity.
