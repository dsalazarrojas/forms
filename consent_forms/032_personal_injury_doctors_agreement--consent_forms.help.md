# Personal Injury Doctors Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for recording the agreement between a patient and their doctor after a personal injury. It gathers information on the patient's name, doctor's name, and other relevant details to ensure accurate documentation of the medical treatment provided.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
3. Enter your name and contact information in the "Doctor Name", "Provider Representative", and "Patient Representative" fields.
4. Select the relevant "Treatment Agreement" option.
5. Choose the correct "Fee Agreement" option(s).
6. Sign the form to confirm you have reviewed and agreed to the terms.
7. Enter the "Signed By" date to date and time of signing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their official documents.
* **Doctor Name**: Enter your full name as the treating doctor or healthcare provider.
* **Provider Name**: Enter the name of the provider or facility where the treatment was provided.
* **Treatment Agreement**: Select the treatment agreement that aligns with the terms of the patient's care, such as "Informed Consent" or "Emergency Treatment Only".
* **Fee Agreement**: Select the fee agreement that applies to the patient's treatment, such as "Standard Fee" or "Reduced Fee for Low-Income Patients".
* **Signed By**: Enter the date and time when you sign the agreement to confirm you have reviewed and agreed to the terms.
* **Signed**: Confirm that you have signed the agreement by selecting 'True'. If not, select 'False'.
* **Patient Representative**: Enter the contact information for the patient's representative, such as their name and email.
* **Insurance Provider**: Select the insurance provider that covers the patient's treatment.
* **Date of Injury**: Enter the date of the injury as documented in medical records.
* **Location of Injury**: Enter the location where the injury occurred.
* **Injury Description**: Provide a brief description of the injury.
* **Medical Record Number**: Enter a copy of the patient's medical record number.
* **Medical Record Number Copy**: Enter another copy of the patient's medical record number.
* **Insurance Provider**: Select the insurance provider that covers the patient's treatment.
* **Patient Representative**: Enter the contact information for the patient's representative.
