# Mentor Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mentor Agreement Form is a mandatory document that outlines the terms and conditions of the mentorship program. By completing this form, you, as a mentor, agree to the program's guidelines and rules. Please read each section carefully and select the correct options to ensure a smooth and productive mentoring experience.

## How To Complete This Form

### Completing this form will help you:

* Understand your role and responsibilities as a mentor
* Review and agree to the program's terms and conditions
* Provide necessary information to support your mentees and the program

### Please fill out the form with the following information:

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### General Information (Page 1)
* **General Information**: This field is where you can provide any relevant information about yourself as a mentor. Please fill in your name and any other details you think would be helpful for the program.

### Mentor Information (Page 1)
* **Name**: Please enter your full name as it should appear on any program materials and communications.
*   **Contact Information**: Please select the contact information that best represents you as a mentor. This will help the program team reach you and keep in touch.
*   **Terms of Agreement**: Select all the agreements that apply to you. These are terms that you, as a mentor, must agree to in order to participate in the program.

### Contact Information (Page 2)
*   **Digital Signature**: Please enter your digital signature as a representation of your agreement to the terms and conditions. This will be used for program materials and documentation.
*   **Contact Info**: Please select the contact information that best represents you as a mentor. This will help the program team reach you and keep in touch.
*   **Payment Term**: Select the payment term that applies to you as a mentor. This will determine your compensation for your role in the program.
*   **Payment Terms**: Select the payment terms that apply to you as a mentor. This will determine your payment schedule and frequency.
*   **Notes**: Please use this field to provide any additional information or notes that you think would be helpful for the program team.

### Additional Information (Page 3)
*   **Are you a first-time mentor for this program?**: Please select the option that applies to your situation. This will help the program team tailor their support and resources for you.
*   **Review Date and Time**: Enter the date and time you would like to review your mentee's progress.
*   **Payment Terms 2**: Select the payment terms that apply to you as a mentor. This will determine your payment schedule and frequency.
*   **Payment Terms 2**: Select the payment terms that apply to you as a mentor. This will determine your payment schedule and frequency.

## Tips
* Please answer all required fields carefully and accurately.
* Review each page before submitting to ensure you have completed all necessary fields.
* If you are unsure about any field or have questions, please reach out to the program team for assistance.
