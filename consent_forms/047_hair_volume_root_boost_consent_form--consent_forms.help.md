# Hair Volume Root Boost Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hair Volume Root Boost Consent Form is designed to gather essential information from clients or patients before undergoing a hair treatment or service that involves volume boosting of the roots. This form helps the practitioner or medical professional understand the client's medical history, contact details, and signature of consent for the procedure.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with the following fields:
	* **Client Details**: Enter your name, date of birth, and any other relevant details as requested.
	* **Contact Info**: Provide your contact information so we can get in touch with you regarding your appointment or any follow-up questions.
	* **Medical Info**: Share any medical conditions, allergies, or concerns you have that might affect the hair treatment.
	* **Signed Consent**: Type your signature to confirm that you understand and agree to the procedures.
	* **Client Signature**: Please sign this field with your actual signature to verify your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details** (`Client Details`, text, not required): This field is used to collect your name and any other relevant information.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, text, not required): We need your contact information to get in touch with you regarding your appointment or any follow-up questions.
* **Medical Info** (`Medical Info`, text, not required): If you have any medical conditions or allergies that might affect the hair treatment, please inform us here.
* **Signed Consent** (`Signed Consent`, text, not required): This field is not actually required, but please type your signature to confirm that you understand and agree to the procedures.
* **Client Signature** (`Client Signature`, note, required): Please sign this field with your actual signature to verify your consent.

## Tips
- Please be honest and thorough when filling out this form to ensure the best experience.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to ask your practitioner.
- The information collected in this form will be used to tailor the treatment and ensure your comfort and safety.
