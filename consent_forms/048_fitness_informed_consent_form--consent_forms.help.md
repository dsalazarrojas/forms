**Fitness Informed Consent Form - Help Guide**

**Purpose**
-----------

This form is intended to gather user information, fitness program details, and consent for participation in a fitness-related activity or program. The goal is to ensure that users are aware of the necessary information and risks involved and provide their informed consent for their participation.

**How To Complete This Form**
---------------------------

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the required fields with the requested information. You can skip fields that are marked as "optional".
2. Select the appropriate fitness level for yourself from the dropdown menu.
3. Provide any additional medical information that may be relevant to your participation in the fitness program.
4. Indicate whether you give your consent to participate in the program.
5. Enter the date and time of your participation in the program.
6. Add any additional notes about your participation or any concerns you may have.

**Field-by-Field Explanation**
---------------------------

### 1. **User Info** (text, optional)
---------------------------------

* **User Info**: Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.

### 2. **Program Details** (text, optional)
----------------------------------------

* **Program Details**: Provide a brief description of the fitness program you are interested in participating in.

### 3. **Fitness Level** (select_one, optional)
-------------------------------------------

* **Fitness Level**: Select the level of fitness you are currently at:
	+ Beginner
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced

### 4. **Medical Info** (text, optional)
-----------------------------------

* **Medical Info**: If you have any medical conditions or concerns that may impact your participation in the program, please describe them here.

### 5. **Consent** (select_multiple, optional)
------------------------------------------

* **Consent**: Indicate whether you grant your consent to participate in the program:
	+ 'True'
	+ 'False'

### 6. **Date** (date, optional)
------------------------------

* **Date**: Enter the date you plan to participate in the program.

### 7. **Time** (time, optional)
-------------------------------

* **Time**: Enter the time you plan to participate in the program.

### 8. **Notes** (note, optional)
------------------------------

* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or concerns you may have about your participation in the program.

**Tips**
---------

Please make sure to read the entire form carefully and answer all questions truthfully. If you have any doubts or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask for clarification.
