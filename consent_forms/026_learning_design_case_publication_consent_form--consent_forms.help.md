# Learning Design Case Publication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Learning Design Case Publication Consent Form is a required document that helps learners provide their consent for the publication of their work. This form ensures that learners are aware of and agree to the terms and conditions of publishing their Learning Design Cases.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Form Title** field with a brief and descriptive title for the form that will be published.
2. Select a category from the **Category** dropdown menu that best describes the form's purpose.
3. Provide a detailed description of the form in the **Description** field.
4. Check the **Anonymous Release** field if you want your name to be removed when the form is published. You can select both 'True' and 'False' options.
5. If you choose **Anonymous Release**, select **True** or **False** from the **Optional** field. This field will determine if your contact information is visible when the form is published.
6. Enter your email address in the **Email** field if you want your contact information to be visible in the published form.
7. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field if you want your phone number to be visible in the published form.
8. You can add any additional notes about your form in the **Notes** field.
9. If you want to attach a file to the form, enter the path to the file in the **File** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (text, optional): Enter a brief and descriptive title for the form that will be published.
* **Category** (select_one, optional): Select a category from the dropdown menu that best describes the form's purpose.
* **Description** (text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the form.
* **Anonymous Release** (select_multiple, optional): Check **True** to remove your name when the form is published.
* **Optional** (select_one, optional): If you choose **Anonymous Release**, select **True** or **False** to determine if your contact information is visible when the form is published.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address if you want your contact information to be visible in the published form.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number if you want your contact information to be visible in the published form.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional notes about your form.
* **File** (text, optional): If you want to attach a file to the form, enter the path to the file.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* If you choose **Anonymous Release**, your contact information will not be visible in the published form.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for correctness before submitting the form.
