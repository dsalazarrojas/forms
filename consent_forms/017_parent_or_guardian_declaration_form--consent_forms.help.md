# Parent Or Guardian Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Parent Or Guardian Declaration Form is a form used by schools or institutions to gather information about a student's parent or guardian. This form helps the institution understand the relationship between the student and their parent or guardian, and may be used to verify the student's identity or to gather demographic information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your contact phone number in the "contact_phone" field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the "date_of_birth" field in the format "mm/dd/yyyy".
6. Select your relationship to the child from the dropdown menu in the "relationship_to_child" field.
7. Enter the name of the child in the "child_name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format "mm/dd/yyyy".
* **Relationship to Child** (`relationship_to_child`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if you are the child's parent or guardian, "No" if you are not.
* **Child's Name** (`child_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the child.
