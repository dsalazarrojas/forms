# Video Recording Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to obtain video recording permission from individuals, ensuring that institutions or organizations have the necessary permissions to record video content featuring their students, clients, or staff members.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
2. Provide your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
3. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
4. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6. Indicate your consent to video recording by selecting "yes" or "no" in the "Video Recording Consent" field.
7. If you consent to being recorded, provide a reason for the recording in the "Reason for Recording" field.
8. Optionally, you can add notes about your consent in the "Notes" field.
9. Select the tool you are using for the recording from the "Assigned Tool" field.
10. Select the output file format from the "Output File" field.
11. Select the category for the recording from the "Category" field.
12. Enter a description of the recording in the "Description" field.
13. Enter a description of the category from the "Category Description" field.
14. If you are being assigned to a specific tool, select it from the "Assigned To" field.
15. If you are assigning the recording to someone, select who is doing the recording from the "Assigned By" field.
16. Enter the date and time when the recording was created in the "Created At" field.
17. Enter the date and time when the recording was updated in the "Updated At" field.
18. Select the category for the recording from the "Category 2" field.
19. Enter a description of the recording in the "Description 2" field.
20. Enter the ID of the recording in the "Form Id 2" field.
21. Select the category for the recording from the "Category 3" field.
22. Select the tool being used for the recording from the "Assigned Tool 2" field.
23. Select who is assigned to perform the recording from the "Assigned By 2" field.
