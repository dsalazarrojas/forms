# Community Theater Performance Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information and agreements from performers for a community theater production. It is a legally binding document that outlines the terms and conditions of participation, including performance rights, compensation, and professional conduct expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each section carefully and answer all questions truthfully.
2. Fill in your full name and email address on page 1.
3. Complete all questions and agreements on pages 2-4.
4. Sign the form electronically on page 5.
5. Return the completed form to the theater company by the designated deadline.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. Full Name** (`performer_full_name`, text, required): Fill in your full name as it appears on official documents.
*   **2. Email Address** (`performer_email`, email, required): Enter your primary contact email address.
*   **3. Phone Number** (`performer_phone`, text, required): Enter your best contact number.
*   **4. Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
*   **5. Parent/Guardian Name (if under 18)** (`parent_guardian_name`, text, optional): If you are under 18, fill in the name of your parent or guardian.
*   **6. Parent/Guardian Signature (if under 18)** (`parent_guardian_signature`, text, optional): If you are under 18, have your parent or guardian sign and date the form.
*   **7. Production Name** (`production_name`, text, required): Enter the title of the production you will be performing in.
*   **8. Performance Dates** (`performance_dates`, text, required): List the dates of your performances.
*   **9. Role/Character** (`role_character`, text, required): Enter the name of your role or character.
*   **10. Performance Release Agreement** (`section_release`, note, optional): This section outlines the terms of performance release. Read carefully before signing.
*   **11. Grant Performance Rights** (`grant_performance_rights`, select_one, required): Agree to grant the theater the right to use your performance, including live and recorded performances.
*   **12. Grant Recording Rights** (`grant_recording_rights`, select_one, required): Agree to grant the theater the right to record your performance for archival and promotional purposes.
*   **13. Grant Broadcast Rights** (`grant_broadcast_rights`, select_one, required): Agree to grant the theater the right to broadcast or stream your performance.
*   **14. Likeness and Image Release** (`section_likeness`, note, optional): This section outlines the terms of likeness and image release. Read carefully before signing.
*   **15. Release Photographs** (`release_photographs`, select_one, required): Release the theater from liability regarding use of your photograph.
*   **16. Release Video** (`release_video`, select_one, required): Release the theater from liability regarding use of your video likeness.
*   **17. Release Name/Biography** (`release_name_biography`, select_one, required): Release the theater from liability regarding use of your name and biography.
*   **18. Compensation Agreement** (`section_compensation`, note, optional): This section outlines the terms of compensation. Read carefully before signing.
*   **19. Understand No Compensation** (`understand_no_compensation`, select_one, required): Agree that this is a volunteer position with no monetary compensation unless otherwise specified.
*   **20. Understand Expense Responsibility** (`understand_expense_responsibility`, select_one, required): Agree that you are responsible for your own expenses unless pre-approved.
*   **21. Exclusivity and Commitment** (`section_exclusivity`, note, optional): This section outlines the terms of exclusivity and commitment. Read carefully before signing.
*   **22. Agree to Attend All Rehearsals and Performances** (`agree_attendance_policy`, select_one, required): Agree to attend all scheduled rehearsals and performances.
*   **23. Agree No Other Commitments** (`agree_no_other_commitments`, select_one, required): Agree not to schedule conflicting commitments during the production period.
*   **24. Understand Dismissal Policy** (`understand_dismissal_policy`, select_one, required): Understand that excessive absences may result in dismissal without refund or compensation.
*   **25. Professional Conduct** (`section_conduct`, note, optional): This section outlines the terms of professional conduct. Read carefully before signing.
*   **26. Agree Professional Behavior** (`agree_professional_behavior`, select_one, required): Agree to maintain professional behavior at all theater functions.
*   **27. Agree No Publicly Shared Script** (`agree_confidentiality`, select_one, required): Agree not to share script, music, or production details publicly before official release.
*   **28. General Provisions** (`section_general`, note, optional): This section outlines additional terms and conditions. Read carefully before signing.
*   **29. Understand Injury Risk** (`understand_injury_risk`, select_one, required): Agree that theater activities involve physical activity and risk of injury, and you accept responsibility for your own safety.
*   **30. Agree to Follow Directives** (`agree_follow_directives`, select_one, required): Agree to follow all director and stage manager directives during rehearsals and performances.
*   **31. Release Duration** (`release_duration`, select_one, required): Understand that this release is effective indefinitely unless otherwise specified.
*   **32. Additional Permissions or Restrictions** (`additional_permissions`, text, optional): If necessary, enter any additional permissions or restrictions.
*   **33. Date of Agreement** (`agreement_date`, date, required): Date and sign the form.
*   **34. Electronic Signature** (`electronic_signature`, text, required): Type your full name as an electronic signature.
*   **35. Witness Name (optional)** (`witness_name`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the witness.

## Tips
* Review each section carefully before signing.
* Ask questions if you are unsure about any of the terms.
* Ensure you understand all the agreements and conditions before completing the form.
* Return the completed form to the theater company by the designated deadline. 

Please note that this is a legally binding document and should be reviewed carefully before signing. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the theater company for clarification.
