# Spay And Neuter Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture essential information from pet owners regarding their pets' spay and neuter procedures. The form is intended to inform pet owners about the process and obtain their consent.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the "pet_information" field with your pet's relevant details, such as name, age, and weight.
2. In the "owner_contact" field, provide your contact information so that we can get in touch with you before, during, and after the procedure.
3. Choose the procedure type ("Spay", "Neuter", or "Both") in the "spay_and_neuter_procedure" field.
4. Select the specific "Surgical Procedure" options that apply to your pet in the "surgical_procedure" field.
5. In the "medical_history" field, provide any relevant medical history for your pet, including previous surgeries or medical conditions.
6. List any medications your pet is currently taking in the "medications" field.
7. Use the "medical_notes" field to provide any important medical notes about your pet.
8. In "contact_information", provide your contact information for follow-ups and any questions you may have.
9. Please sign the "owner_signature" field to indicate that you have read and understood the form.
10. Date and time of signing should be entered in the "date_signed" and "time_signed" fields.
11. The veterinarian will sign the "veterinary_signature" field after reviewing the form.
12. Date and time of signing should be entered in the "date_signed_veterinary" and "time_signed_veterinary" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **pet_information** (`pet_information`, text, required): This field is for providing your pet's details, such as name, age, and weight.
* **owner_contact** (`owner_contact`, text, required): Please provide your contact information so that we can get in touch with you before, during, and after the procedure.
* **spay_and_neuter_procedure** (`spay_and_neuter_procedure`, select_one, required): Choose the procedure type: "Spay", "Neuter", or "Both".
* **surgical_procedure** (`surgical_procedure`, select_multiple, required): Select the specific surgical procedures that apply to your pet.
* **medical_history** (`medical_history`, text, required): Please provide any relevant medical history for your pet, including previous surgeries or medical conditions.
* **medications** (`medications`, text, required): List any medications your pet is currently taking.
* **medical_notes** (`medical_notes`, note, required): Use this field to provide any important medical notes about your pet.
* **contact_information** (`contact_information`, text, required): Please provide your contact information for follow-ups and any questions you may have.
* **owner_signature** (`owner_signature`, text, required): Sign this field to indicate that you have read and understood the form.
* **date_signed** (`date_signed`, date, required): Date and time of signing should be entered in this field.
* **time_signed** (`time_signed`, time, required): Date and time of signing should be entered in this field.
* **veterinary_signature** (`veterinary_signature`, text, required): The veterinarian will sign this field after reviewing the form.
* **date_signed_veterinary** (`date_signed_veterinary`, date, required): Date and time of signing should be entered in this field.
* **time_signed_veterinary** (`time_signed_veterinary`, time, required): Date and time of signing should be entered in this field.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your pet receives the best care possible.
* If you are unsure about any information, please ask your veterinarian for guidance.
* Please sign and date the form in the presence of the veterinarian to ensure that you are aware of the procedure and its implications.
