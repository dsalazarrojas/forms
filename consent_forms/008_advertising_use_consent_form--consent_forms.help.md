# Advertising Use Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users on their personal details and consent for advertising usage. It is essential to read the form carefully and understand the implications of their consent before proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Full Name**, **Email Address**, and **Phone Number** in the respective fields.
2. Select the scope of usage for which you are consenting to, i.e., **Use of my name in marketing materials**, **Use of my photo/video in social media ads**, **Use of my testimonial on the website**, or **Use of my likeness in print advertising**.
3. Choose the **Duration of Consent**, i.e., **One-time use only**, **1 Year**, **3 Years**, or **Indefinitely (Perpetual)**.
4. Acknowledge that you understand the **Terms of Compensation** for any potential fee.
5. Select your choice on **Right to Revoke**, i.e., **I understand** or **Other**.
6. Sign the form by selecting **I Agree**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Required): This field requires your legal name as it will be used for advertising purposes.
* **Email Address** (Required): This is the address that we will use to contact you for marketing or promotional purposes.
* **Phone Number** (Required): This is your contact number that we may use for marketing or promotional purposes.
* **Scope of Usage**: Select all the ways in which you permit us to use your personal details, such as name, photo, or testimonial.
* **Duration of Consent**: Choose how long we can use the information you provide, i.e., one-time use, or for a specified period.
* **Terms of Compensation**: Check if you acknowledge the terms of compensation for any fee associated with your consent.
* **Right to Revoke**: Confirm that you understand you can revoke your consent at any time in writing.
* **Electronic Signature**: Sign this form by confirming that you agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Date Signed**: This field is automatically filled in upon submission and indicates the date you signed the form.
