# Safety Drill Participation Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from participants before they participate in safety drills. It ensures that all participants understand the purpose, risks, and benefits of the safety drill and are aware of their rights and responsibilities. The form helps to establish a clear understanding between the participant and the organization conducting the safety drill.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant contact details and medical information as required.
2. Choose the correct participant information and emergency contact details.
3. Confirm your consent to participate and consent to medical treatment and research.
4. Provide any necessary medical authority contact and details if required.
5. Specify any medical treatment or research details if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details** (select_one, required: false): Select the relevant contact details from the provided options.
* **Medical Information** (select_one, required: false): Choose the correct medical information from the provided options.
* **Participant Information** (select_one, required: false): Select the correct participant information from the provided options.
* **Emergency Contact** (select_one, required: false): Choose the correct emergency contact from the provided options.
* **Consent to Participate** (select_one, required: false): Confirm your consent to participate in the safety drill.
* **Medical Authority Contact** (select_one, required: false): Select the correct medical authority contact from the provided options.
* **Medical Authority Details** (select_one, required: false): Choose the correct medical authority details from the provided options.
* **Consent to Medical Treatment** (select_one, required: false): Confirm your consent to medical treatment in case of an emergency.
* **Consent to Medical Research** (select_one, required: false): Confirm your consent to medical research in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Treatment Details** (select_one, required: false): Specify the details of any medical treatment you may require in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Research Details** (select_one, required: false): Specify the details of any medical research you may be involved in during the safety drill.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any of the options, please select "Other" and provide a brief explanation.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the safety drill organizers.
