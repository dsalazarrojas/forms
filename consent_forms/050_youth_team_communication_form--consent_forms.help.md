# Youth Team Communication Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Youth Team Communication Form is used to collect information from parents or guardians for efficient communication.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form carefully, following the instructions for each field below.
2.  Make sure to select all applicable options for your preferred contact methods.
3.  If you have an emergency contact, please provide their details in the corresponding field.
4.  Indicate your consent preferences for receiving updates and urgent messages.
5.  Add any additional notes or information you'd like to share with the team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Team info** (`1`, `number`, required: false): This field is used to record the team ID number for reference purposes.
*   **Parent info** (`2`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the parent or guardian you are filling out this form for.
*   **Contact preferences** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the contact methods you'd like to use for communication (e.g., Email, Phone).
*   **Emergency contact** (`4`, `text`, required: false): If you have an emergency contact, please provide their name and number or email in this field.
*   **Consent** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate which types of messages you are willing to receive from the team (e.g., updates, urgent messages).
*   **Communication channels** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all the communication methods you'd like to use for receiving updates (e.g., Email, WhatsApp, Phone).
*   **Notes** (`7`, `note`, required: false): Write any additional information you'd like to share with the team.
*   **Urgent message** (`8`, `text`, required: false): In case of an urgent message, you can enter it here.

## Tips

*   Make sure to double-check your contact preferences to ensure you'll receive important updates.
*   Don't forget to add any urgent messages that require immediate attention.
*   Keep your emergency contact details up-to-date in case of an emergency.
