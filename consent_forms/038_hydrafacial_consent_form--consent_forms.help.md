# Hydrafacial Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hydrafacial Consent Form is a document used to obtain patient consent for a hydrafacial procedure. It is a crucial step in the patient's treatment process, ensuring that you understand the risks, benefits, and expectations of the procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your contact information: Provide your name, phone number, and email address.
2.  Enter your contact information: Enter the contact information of your medical practice, including their phone number and email address.
3.  Enter your medical procedure details: Describe the specific treatment or procedure you are undergoing.
4.  Sign and date the form: Sign your name and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter your contact information, including your relationship to the patient (e.g., "Parent" or "Guardian").
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the person or medical practice you wish to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address or the email address of the person or medical practice you wish to contact.
* **Medical Procedure** (`medical_procedure`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the treatment or procedure you are undergoing.
* **Medical Procedure Details** (`medical_procedure_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about the treatment or procedure.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required): Sign your name to confirm that you have read and understand the consent form.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required): Enter the date you signed the consent form.
* **Time Signed** (`time_signed`, time, required): Enter the time you signed the consent form.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth for verification purposes.
* **Age Verification** (`age_verification`, select_one, optional): Select your age range for verification purposes.
* **Consent Form Signature** (`consent_form_signature`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" to confirm your consent for the treatment.
* **Medical Practice** (`medical_practice`, text, optional): Enter the name and address of the medical practice providing treatment.
* **Doctor** (`doctor`, text, optional): Enter the name of the doctor performing the treatment.
* **Doctor Phone** (`doctor_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the doctor or medical practice.
* **Doctor Email** (`doctor_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the doctor or medical practice.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your home address for contact purposes.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city where you live.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the state where you live.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you provided consent for the treatment.
* **Consent Time** (`consent_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you provided consent for the treatment.
* **Doctor Notes** (`doctor_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes from the doctor.
* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the patient.
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
