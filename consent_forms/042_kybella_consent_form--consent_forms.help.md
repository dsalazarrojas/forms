## Purpose
The Kybella Consent Form is used to collect personal information and preferences from users. This form is likely used for onboarding new users or tracking contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your first name in the `first_name` field.
2.  Enter your last name in the `last_name` field.
3.  Enter your date of birth (in YYYY-MM-DD format) in the `date_of_birth` field.
4.  Select your preferred method of contact (e.g., email, phone, or text) in the `preferred_contact` field.
5.  Select one or more contact methods you'd like to use for communication (e.g., email, phone, text) in the `contact_method` field.
6.  Enter your phone number in the `phone` field.
7.  Enter your email address in the `email` field.
8.  Enter your address in the `address` field.
