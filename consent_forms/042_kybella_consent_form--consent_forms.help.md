# Kybella Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kybella Consent Form is used to collect personal information and preferences from users. This form is likely used for onboarding new users or tracking contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your first name in the `first_name` field.
2.  Enter your last name in the `last_name` field.
3.  Enter your date of birth (in YYYY-MM-DD format) in the `date_of_birth` field.
4.  Select your preferred method of contact (e.g., email, phone, or text) in the `preferred_contact` field.
5.  Select one or more contact methods you'd like to use for communication (e.g., email, phone, text) in the `contact_method` field.
6.  Enter your phone number in the `phone` field.
7.  Enter your email address in the `email` field.
8.  Enter your address in the `address` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name, as you would like it to appear on any correspondence or documents.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name, as you would like it to appear on any correspondence or documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 1990-02-12).
* **Preferred Contact** (`preferred_contact`, select_one, optional): Select how you would like to be contacted (e.g., email, phone, text).
*   *Option 1* (`option 1`, optional): Choose one option from the dropdown list.
*   *Option 2* (`option 2`, optional): Choose another option from the dropdown list.
*   *Option 3* (`option 3`, optional): Choose the last option from the dropdown list.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable methods you'd like to use for communication (e.g., email, phone, text).
*   *Option 1* (`option 1`, optional): Choose this contact method.
*   *Option 2* (`option 2`, optional): Choose another contact method.
*   *Option 3* (`option 3`, optional): Choose the last contact method.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your mailing address.
