# Spa Services Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Spa Services Waiver Form" is used for clients to provide information and acknowledgments before receiving services at a spa. This form helps to ensure clients understand and agree to the terms of their visit, including any risks involved with the services they'll be receiving.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by entering your name and date of visit, which helps the spa staff identify you and track your visit.
- Select the services you'll be receiving from the list provided. This helps the spa staff understand your needs and preferences.
- If you have any medical history or concerns, please provide a brief explanation in the "Medical History" field.
- Enter your contact information so the spa can reach you if necessary.
- Finally, sign and date the form to acknowledge that you've read and understand the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to visit the spa.
* **Services Performed** (`services_performed`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the services you'll be receiving. Please select all services that apply.
* **Risk Acknowledgement** (`risk_acknowledgement`, note, optional): This field is for you to acknowledge that you understand the risks involved with the services.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions or concerns, please provide a brief explanation.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact information so the spa can reach you if necessary.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Please sign and date this form to acknowledge that you've read and understand the terms.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, text, optional): This is for the client to sign and date their agreement.

## Tips
- Make sure to sign and date the "Client Signature" field to complete this form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask the spa staff for assistance.
- Review the form carefully before signing to ensure you understand the terms and conditions.
- This form is designed to be a simple and straightforward guide to help you understand what to do. If you're unsure about any field, please ask a spa staff member for guidance.
