## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Client Info (Page 1)
* **Client Info** (`client_info`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your name, phone number, and email address to contact you regarding your appointment and any changes or updates.
### Services (Page 1)
* **Services** (`services`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the lash lift and/or tint services you would like to book. You can select multiple services by holding down the Ctrl key (or Command key on Mac) while clicking on the options.
### Contact Info (Page 1)
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address where you can be reached for appointment reminders and updates.
### Services (Page 2)
* **Services** (`services`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Confirm or modify your service selection from the previous page.
### Appointment Time (Page 3)
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, `time`, required: false): Choose a time for your appointment, taking into account that the lash lift and tint process may take approximately <time> minutes.
### Appointment Date (Page 3)
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, `date`, required: false): Select a date for your appointment. Ensure that this date is accurate to avoid any conflicts with other clients or staff availability.
### Appointment Time (Page 3)
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, `time`, required: false): Confirm the time for your appointment as selected on the previous page.
### Appointment Duration (Page 3)
* **Appointment Duration** (`appointment_duration`, `number`, required: false): Please confirm the duration of your appointment, typically 30 minutes or more for lash lift and tint services.
### Payment Details (Page 4)
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, `text`, required: false): Provide any relevant payment information, such as payment method or additional payment requirements.
### Service Cost (Page 4)
* **Service Cost** (`service_cost`, `number`, required: false): Enter the estimated or actual cost for your selected services. This may vary based on your choices.
### Payment Method (Page 4)
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Specify your preferred payment method, such as cash, credit, or debit.
### Client Signature (Page 5)
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, `note`, required: false): Please sign this form to confirm that you have reviewed and agreed upon the details provided. This is an essential step to ensure your appointment is processed correctly.
### Staff Signature (Page 5)
* **Staff Signature** (`staff_signature`, `note`, required: false): Our staff member will sign this form to confirm they have received your agreement and understand their responsibilities for your appointment.

## Tips

* If you have any questions or concerns regarding the form, please don't hesitate to ask your staff member for assistance.
