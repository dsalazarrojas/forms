# Landlord And Tenant Disclosure Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from both landlords and tenants about their relationship, including their contact information, agreement terms, and concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please navigate through the following pages and complete each field as instructed:

### Page 1: Landlord Information

* **Landlord Information**: Please provide your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself as the landlord.

### Page 2: Tenant Information

* **Tenant Information**: Please provide your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself as the tenant.

### Page 3: Relationship Status

* **Relationship Status**: Please select your relationship status with the landlord (Tenant, Landlord, Both).

### Page 4: Tenant Contact Information

* **Tenant Contact Information**: Please select the contact information you would like to share with the landlord (First Name, Last Name, Email).

### Page 5: Tenant Phone Number

* **Tenant Phone Number**: Please provide your phone number for the landlord to contact you.

### Page 6: Landlord Contact Information

* **Landlord Contact Information**: Please select the contact information you would like to share with the tenant (First Name, Last Name, Email).

### Page 7: Relationship Start Date

* **Relationship Start Date**: Please enter the start date of your relationship with the landlord.

### Page 8: Relationship End Date

* **Relationship End Date**: Please enter the end date of your relationship with the landlord.

### Page 9: Agreement

* **Agreement**: Please provide a brief description of your agreement with the tenant.

### Page 10: Agreement Details

* **Agreement Details**: Please provide additional details about your agreement with the tenant.

### Page 11: Agreement Terms

* **Agreement Terms**: Please provide the terms of your agreement with the tenant.

### Page 12: Landlord Agreement

* **Landlord Agreement**: Please describe your agreement with the tenant.

### Page 13: Tenant Agreement

* **Tenant Agreement**: Please describe your agreement with the landlord.

### Page 14: Landlord Concerns

* **Landlord Concerns**: Please list any concerns you have as the landlord.

### Page 15: Tenant Concerns

* **Tenant Concerns**: Please list any concerns you have as the tenant.

### Page 16: Landlord Disclosure

* **Landlord Disclosure**: Please provide any disclosures you have as the landlord.

### Page 17: Tenant Disclosure

* **Tenant Disclosure**: Please provide any disclosures you have as the tenant.

### Page 18: Agreement Terms 2

* **Agreement Terms 2**: Please provide additional terms of your agreement with the tenant.

### Page 19: Landlord Agreement 2

* **Landlord Agreement 2**: Please describe your agreement with the tenant.

### Page 20: Tenant Agreement 2

* **Tenant Agreement 2**: Please describe your agreement with the landlord.

### Page 21: Landlord Concerns 2

* **Landlord Concerns 2**: Please list any concerns you have as the landlord.

### Page 22: Tenant Concerns 2

* **Tenant Concerns 2**: Please list any concerns you have as the tenant.

### Page 23: Landlord Disclosure 2

* **Landlord Disclosure 2**: Please provide any disclosures you have as the landlord.

### Page 24: Tenant Disclosure 2

* **Tenant Disclosure 2**: Please provide any disclosures you have as the tenant.

### Page 25: Agreement Terms 3

* **Agreement Terms 3**: Please provide additional terms of your agreement with the tenant.

Note to the user:
Please complete each page carefully and thoroughly before proceeding to the next one. If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask the form's administrator for clarification.
