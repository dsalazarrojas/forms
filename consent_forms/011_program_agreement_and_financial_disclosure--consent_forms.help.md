# Program Agreement And Financial Disclosure - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information about a participant's agreement to a program and their financial disclosures. It is likely used for general program participation purposes but could be adapted for other uses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information as follows:
- Start by reading and agreeing to the program terms.
- Then, fill in the participant's details: name, email, and phone number.
- Finally, review the financial disclosures if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Program Terms** (`program_terms`, note, required: false): Please review and agree to the terms of the program by reading this section.
- **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the participant.
- **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the participant.
- **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the participant.
- **Financial Disclosures** (`financial_disclosures`, note, required: false): Review the financial disclosures as required.

## Tips
- Please ensure you have reviewed and agreed to the program terms before submitting this form.
- Enter accurate information for participant details to ensure successful communication.
- Review the financial disclosures carefully to understand any requirements.
- If unsure about any field, consult the relevant authorities or contact support.
