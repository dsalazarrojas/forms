# entertainment_press_coverage_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from individuals for media coverage on various types of media outlets. It is recommended to fill out this form for all media requests to ensure that individuals are informed and in control of their personal data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select "Yes" or "No" for each field, depending on your consent preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consent Form** (`Consent Form`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose "Yes" or "No" to grant or deny consent for various types of media coverage.
* **name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as it should be displayed in the media coverage.
* **contact_number** (`contact_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact number that the media outlet will use to reach you.
* **email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address that the media outlet will use to reach you.
* **media_type** (`media_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of media coverage you are granting or denying consent for (Photo, Video, Audio, Interview, etc.).
* **media_outlet** (`media_outlet`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of media outlet where the media coverage will be published (Newspaper, Magazine, Online publication, Television, Radio, Social media, etc.).
* **publication_date** (`publication_date`, date, required/optional): Choose a date for the media coverage (if applicable).
* **publication_time** (`publication_time`, time, required/optional): Choose a time for the media coverage (if applicable).
* **publication_location** (`publication_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location where the media coverage will be published (if applicable).
* **media_contact** (`media_contact`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact person's name and information for the media outlet.
* **consent_type** (`consent_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of media coverage you are granting or denying consent for (Media coverage on social media, Media coverage in newspaper or magazine, Media interview on television, Media interview on radio, etc.).
* **consent_term** (`consent_term`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose how long you grant or deny consent for (Yes, No). 

Note: The form is not mandatory, but it's recommended to be filled out for all media requests.
