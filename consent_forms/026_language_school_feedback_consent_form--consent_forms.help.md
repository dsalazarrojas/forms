# Language School Feedback & Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and consent from students to improve the Language School's services and ensure that students are aware of and agree to the terms of use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Fill out the required fields with your first name, last name, contact information, and feedback.
2. Select the language you are comfortable with (French, Spanish, or English).
3. Indicate your language level (Beginner, Intermediate, or no selection).
4. Read and agree to the terms of use and privacy policy.
5. Select how you would like to be contacted (via email or phone).
6. Enter your email address where you can be reached.
7. Provide a date and time for your minimalist consent.
8. Provide a note for your minimalist consent.
9. Select whether the school can use your feedback, share it with others, and use it to improve services.
