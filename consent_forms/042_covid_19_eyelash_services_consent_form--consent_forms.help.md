# Covid 19 Eyelash Services Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to be a comprehensive consent document for customers seeking Covid-19 eyelash services. By filling out this form, customers will be able to express their understanding and agreement to the terms and conditions of the service, as well as their health status and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Read each section carefully and answer the questions accordingly.
2.  Ensure that you have understood each question and option before proceeding.
3.  Answer all questions truthfully to the best of your abilities.
