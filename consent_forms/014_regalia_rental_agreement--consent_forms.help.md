# Regalia Rental Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Regalia Rental Agreement is a form used to collect information and agreements from students and school administrators regarding regalia rental. This form ensures that both parties are aware of their responsibilities and expectations related to the rental period.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for your regalia rental, including your name, graduation date, rental start and end dates, and pickup location.
2. Select the items you would like to rent from the available options (e.g., Stole, Hood, Tassel, Sash).
3. Choose your preferred return method (e.g., In-person, Mail, Email, Pickup at school during office hours).
4. Enter your school's contact information, including name, email, phone number, and address.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement.
6. Ensure that the school administrator also signs and dates the form to confirm their agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, Text, Required): Enter your name.
* **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date you will be graduating.
* **Rental Start Date** (`rental_start_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date you would like to start renting the regalia items.
* **Rental End Date** (`rental_end_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date you would like to end renting the regalia items.
* **Pickup Location** (`pickup_location`, Select One, Optional): Select the location where you would like to pick up your regalia items (e.g., School A, School B).
* **Items** (`rental_items`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the regalia items you would like to rent (e.g., Stole, Hood, Tassel, Sash).
* **Return Preferences** (`return_preferences`, Select One, Optional): Choose how you would like to return the regalia items (e.g., In-person, Mail, Email, Pickup at school during office hours).
* **School Name** (`school_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your school's name.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, Email, Optional): Enter your school's email address.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your school's phone number.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, Text, Optional): Enter your school's address.
* **Rental Provider** (`rental_provider`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the rental provider.
* **Signature** (`signature`, Text, Optional): Sign the form to confirm your agreement.
* **Confirm Signature** (`confirm_signature`, Text, Optional): Confirm your signature.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, Date, Optional): Enter the date you signed the form.
* **Time Signed** (`time_signed`, Time, Optional): Enter the time you signed the form.
* **Confirm Date Signed** (`confirm_date_signed`, Date, Optional): Confirm the date you signed the form.
* **Confirm Time Signed** (`confirm_time_signed`, Time, Optional): Confirm the time you signed the form.
* **School Admin** (`school_admin`, Text, Optional): Enter your school administrator's name.
* **School Admin Phone** (`school_admin_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your school administrator's phone number.
* **School Admin Email** (`school_admin_email`, Email, Optional): Enter your school administrator's email address.
* **School Admin Signature** (`school_admin_signature`, Text, Optional): Enter your school administrator's signature.
* **Confirm School Admin Signature** (`school_admin_confirm_signature`, Text, Optional): Confirm your school administrator's signature.
* **School Admin Date Signed** (`school_admin_date_signed`, Date, Optional): Enter the date your school administrator signed the form.
* **School Admin Time Signed** (`school_admin_time_signed`, Time, Optional): Enter the time your school administrator signed the form.
* **Confirm School Admin Date Signed** (`confirm_school_admin_date_signed`, Date, Optional): Confirm the date your school administrator signed the form.
* **Confirm School Admin Time Signed** (`confirm_school_admin_time_signed`, Time, Optional): Confirm the time your school administrator signed the form.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to confirm that all information is correct.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your school administrator.
* This form is a binding agreement between you and your school, so please sign it carefully and intentionally.
