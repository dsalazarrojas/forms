# holiday_merchandise_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from individuals to purchase holiday merchandise. It's designed to capture the necessary information to accurately record and track consent, while ensuring that the individual is aware of their decision.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Name** in the space provided. This will help us identify you correctly.
2. Enter your **Email** address in the format of `username@example.com`. This will help us contact you for further information or to send updates.
3. Add your **Phone** number, if you wish to be contacted for further information or to discuss your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Name** (holiday_merchandise_consent_form_page_1, text, required: false)
This field is to record your name for identification purposes. Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.

### **Email** (name, email, required: false)
Enter your email address in the format of `username@example.com`. This will help us contact you for further information or to send updates.

### **Phone** (phone, text, required: false)
This field is optional, but if you wish to be contacted for further information or to discuss your consent, please enter your phone number.
