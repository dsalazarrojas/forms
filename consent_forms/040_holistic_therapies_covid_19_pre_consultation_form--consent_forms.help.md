# holistic_therapies_covid_19_pre_consultation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from patients before a consultation with a healthcare professional. The data collected will help the doctor assess the patient's medical history, potential risks, and other relevant factors to provide better care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's information (Page 1).
2. Choose your consent for the consultation (Page 2). Please select only one of the options.
3. Provide any additional contact information if needed (Page 3).
4. Enter your phone number (Page 4).
5. Enter your email address (Page 5).
6. Select the medical center where you are currently seeking treatment (Page 6). You can select multiple options if applicable.
7. Report your current vaccination status (Page 7). Please select only one option.
8. Provide any relevant medical history information (Page 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient info** (patient_info, text, optional): Enter your basic patient information, such as your name and any other relevant details.
* **consent** (consent, select_one, required): Choose your consent for the consultation. Please select only one option.
* **contact** (contact, note, optional): Provide any additional contact information if needed.
* **phone number** (phone_number, number, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **email** (email, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **medical center** (medical center, select_multiple, required): Select the medical center where you are currently seeking treatment. You can select multiple options if applicable.
* **vaccine status** (vaccine status, select_one, optional): Report your current vaccination status. Please select only one option.
* **medical history** (medical history, text, optional): Provide any relevant medical history information.
