# IV Therapy Injections Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IV Therapy Injections Consent Form is a document that patients must complete before receiving IV therapy injections. It is used to gather essential information about the patient's medical condition, allergies, medical history, emergency contacts, and consent status. This form ensures that the patient understands and agrees to the treatment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your medical condition in the Medical Condition field. This is a required field and must be filled out.
2. If you have any allergies, select all the applicable options from the Allergies field.
3. If you take any medications, enter them in the Medications field.
4. In the Medical History field, describe your medical history in as much detail as possible.
5. In the Emergency Contacts field, select all the emergency contacts that you have provided.
6. In the Consent field, select your consent status. You can select 'True' if you agree to the treatment, 'False' if you do not, 'Maybe' if you are unsure, 'Not Now' if you are not ready to provide consent, or 'Other' if you have other concerns.
7. Your signature is required to confirm your consent. Please enter your signature in the Signature field.
8. Finally, enter the date you signed the form in the Date Signed field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your medical condition that requires IV therapy injections.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, optional): If you have any allergies, select all the applicable options from this field.
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, optional): Enter any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, note, required): Describe your medical history in as much detail as possible.
* **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the emergency contacts you have provided.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, optional): Select your consent status. You can select 'True' if you agree to the treatment, 'False' if you do not, 'Maybe' if you are unsure, 'Not Now' if you are not ready to provide consent, or 'Other' if you have other concerns.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Enter your signature to confirm your consent.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required): Enter the date you signed the form.
