# Photo Release Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photo Release Permission Form is used to obtain consent for the use of an individual's photo. This form is used to ensure that the individual's privacy and rights are respected in any context where their photo is used, such as in media, advertising, or events. This form is a legal document that serves as proof of consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your details:
   - Provide your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the respective fields.
   - Enter your **Date of Birth**, **Contact Number**, and **Email** in their respective fields.
   - Enter the **Photo Location** where the photo was taken.
2. Provide details about the photo's purpose:
   - Describe the **Purpose of Use** of the photo (e.g., advertising, media, event).
3. Give consent:
   - Select **Consent** as 'True' to indicate that you give permission for your photo to be used.
4. Sign and date the form:
   - Sign your name in the **Signature** field.
   - Date and time of signing in the **Signed at** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name.
* **Last Name**: Your last name.
* **Date of Birth**: The date of birth of the individual.
* **Contact Number**: Your contact number for any further communication.
* **Email**: Your email address.
* **Photo Location**: The location where the photo was taken.
* **Purpose of Use**: A description of why the photo is being used (e.g., for advertising, media, event).
* **Consent**: A declaration of whether you grant permission for your photo to be used.
* **Signature**: Your handwritten or typed signature.
* **Signed at**: Date and time of signing.
* **Photo Release Permission Form ID**: A unique identifier for this form.
* **Photo Release Permission Form Date**: Date of form creation.
* **Photo Release Form Version**: Version of the form used.
* **Photo Release Permission Notes**: Optional notes for any additional information.
* **Assigned to**: Optional field for assignment of the form to a specific person.
* **Assigned by**: Optional field for assignment by a specific person.
* **Form ID**: Optional unique identifier for the form.
* **Created by**: Optional field for creation of the form by a specific person.
* **Created at**: Date of form creation.
* **Updated at**: Date of last update.
* **Updated by**: Optional field for last update by a specific person.
* **Updated At 2**: Duplicate of updated date field.
* **Assigned To 2**: Optional field for assignment to a specific person 2.
* **Assigned By 2**: Optional field for assignment by a specific person 2.
* **Notes**: Optional field for any additional comments.
