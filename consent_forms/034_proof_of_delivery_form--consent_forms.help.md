# Proof Of Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the delivery of goods or services to customers, ensuring accurate and timely confirmation of delivery details, customer information, and items received.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide information about the delivery details, including any relevant dates and times.
2. Enter customer information, including any necessary contact details.
3. Confirm that the delivery has been made and received by the customer.
4. Check the boxes to confirm that all items have been received by the customer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **delivery_details** (`1`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the delivery, including any relevant dates and times.
* **customer_info** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Enter the customer's name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **delivery_confirmation** (`3`, `text`, required: false): Check this box to confirm that the delivery has been made and received by the customer.
* **item_received** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Check this box to confirm that all items have been received by the customer.
