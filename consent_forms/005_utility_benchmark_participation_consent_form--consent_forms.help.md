# utility_benchmark_participation_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The utility benchmark participation consent form is a necessary step for participants to provide their details and give consent to the use and reporting of their data in the context of utility benchmarking studies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your participant details in the first field.
2. Provide your email address in the second field.
3. Enter your phone number in the third field.
4. Indicate your consent to the use of your data in the fourth field.
5. Indicate your consent to the reporting of your data in the fifth field.
6. If you are representing a company, enter its name in the sixth field.
7. If you are participating on behalf of a utility company, enter its name in the seventh field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details**: <i class="icon icon-text"> (text): Enter your name and any other relevant participant details.
* **Participant Email**: <i class="icon icon-text"> (email): Enter your email address so that we can contact you.
* **Participant Phone**: <i class="icon icon-text"> (text): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you.
* **Consent to Use Data**: <i class="icon icon-text"> (select one): Choose "True" to indicate your consent to the use of your data.
* **Consent to Reporting**: <i class="icon icon-text"> (select one): Choose "True" to indicate your consent to the reporting of your data.
* **Participant Company**: <i class="icon icon-text"> (text): Enter the name of your company if you are representing one.
* **Participant Utility**: <i class="icon icon-text"> (text): Enter the name of the utility you represent if you are participating on behalf of one. 

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all fields accurately and truthfully.
* Read through each field carefully before submitting the form. 

Note: The form fields are self-explanatory, the explanations are provided as a guide for the end user.
