# dance_choreography_recording_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dance_choreography_recording_consent_form is used to capture the consent for recording dance choreography sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Please fill out all required fields with accurate information.
2.  Make sure all required fields are filled before proceeding.
3.  Review and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Recording Permissions**: This field is required and should be filled out with the recording permissions agreed upon.
* **Contact Details**: Select the title that best describes the contact person.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Email**: Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Recording Types**: Select the type of recording that is taking place (Dance Class, Rehearsal, or Both).
* **Recording Frequency**: Select the frequency of recording (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
* **Consent**: This field is for the guardian or parent to acknowledge that they have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
* **Recording Consent**: Select whether or not to record the student.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the student's date of birth.
* **Age**: Enter the student's age.
* **Student ID**: Enter the student's ID number.
* **Guardian Name**: Enter the guardian's name.
* **Guardian Contact Number**: Enter the guardian's contact number.
* **Guardian Email**: Enter the guardian's email address.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Number**: Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the emergency contact's email address.
* **Relationship**: Select the relationship between the student and the emergency contact.
* **Permission**: Select whether or not you grant permission for the emergency contact to access your personal info.
* **Permission Type**: Select what type of information the emergency contact is allowed to access.
* **Permission Date**: Select the date of permission was granted.
* **Permission Time**: Select the time of permission was granted.
* **Permission Note**: Add any additional notes regarding permission.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions of recording.
* **Submit**: Select "Submit" to complete and submit the form.
