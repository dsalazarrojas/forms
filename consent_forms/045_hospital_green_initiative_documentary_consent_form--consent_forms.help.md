# Hospital Green Initiative Documentary Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Green Initiative Documentary Consent Form is a crucial document that requires patients, visitors, and medical staff to provide consent for the collection of their personal data for the purpose of promoting sustainability and patient safety in hospitals. This form is part of an initiative to ensure transparency and respect for individual privacy rights.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please review each field carefully and fill in the required information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out before proceeding.
3. If you are unsure about any field, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Details** (`patient_details`, text, optional): Please enter your name, address, and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Sign Here** (`signature`, text, optional): This field is for your signature, please print your name and date to confirm that you have read and understood the form.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth as per your birth certificate or passport.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Provide any contact information you have, such as your email address or phone number.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name, address, and contact information of an emergency contact.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text, optional): This field is for your signature, please print your name and date to confirm that you have read and understood the form.
* **Staff Details** (`staff_details`, text, optional): Please provide your name, role, and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Medical Staff Signature** (`medical_staff_signature`, text, optional): This field is for your signature, please print your name and date to confirm that you have read and understood the form.
* **Visitor Details** (`visitor_details`, text, optional): Enter your name, address, and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Visitor Signature** (`visitor_signature`, text, optional): This field is for your signature, please print your name and date to confirm that you have read and understood the form.
* **Medical Staff Contact** (`medical_staff_contact`, text, optional): Please enter the name and contact information of a medical staff member.
* **Patient Contact** (`patient_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the patient.
* **Staff Contact** (`staff_contact`, text, optional): Please enter the name and contact information of a staff member.
* **Visitor Contact** (`visitor_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the visitor.

## Tips
* If you are unable to fill out any required fields, please contact the hospital administration for assistance.
* Ensure that all signatures are notarized and dated accurately.
