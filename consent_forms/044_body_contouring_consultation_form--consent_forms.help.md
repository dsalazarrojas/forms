# Body Contouring Consultation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from clients about their body contouring goals, medical history, and consultation preferences. Completing this form will help the provider understand their needs and tailor a personalized consultation to address their concerns.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure the provider has a clear understanding of your needs.
2. If you have any previous consultations or medical history, please provide detailed information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Please enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Please enter your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required): Please select your gender from the options provided.
* **Height** (`height`, `text`, required): Please enter your height in feet and inches (e.g., 5'8").
* **Current Weight** (`current_weight`, `number`, required): Please enter your current weight in pounds.
* **Goal Weight** (`goal_weight`, `number`, required): Please enter your target weight.
* **Current BMI** (`bmi`, `number`, required): Please enter your current BMI (optional).
* **Primary Concern** (`primary_concern`, `text`, required): Please describe the area you're most concerned about.
* **Treatment Goals** (`treatment_goals`, `text`, required): Please describe what you hope to achieve through treatment.
* **Areas of Interest** (`areas_of_interest`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select all areas of your body you'd like to address.
* **Preferred Procedure** (`preferred_procedure`, `select_one`, required): Please select your preferred procedure (if you're unsure, select "Not Sure").
* **Previous Consultations** (`previous_consultations`, `select_one`, required): Please indicate if you've had previous consultations.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, required): Please list any relevant medical conditions.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, `text`, required): Please list all current medications.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, required): Please list any allergies.
* **Surgical History** (`surgical_history`, `text`, required): Please list relevant surgeries.
* **Lifestyle Factors** (`lifestyle_factors`, `text`, required): Please describe your diet and exercise routine.
* **Smoking Status** (`smoking_status`, `select_one`, required): Please select your smoking status.
* **Treatment Timeline** (`timeline`, `select_one`, required): Please select when you'd like to start treatment.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, `select_one`, required): Please select your estimated budget (if unsure, select "Flexible").
* **Special Events** (`special_events`, `text`, required): Please indicate if you're working toward a specific event.
* **Questions for Provider** (`questions_for_provider`, `text`, required): Please describe any questions you'd like to ask the provider.
* **Consultation Type** (`consultation_type`, `select_one`, required): Please select your preferred consultation type.
* **Preferred Consultation Date** (`preferred_date`, `date`, required): Please select a date for the consultation (if unsure, select "Anytime").
* **Referred By** (`referral_name`, `text`, required): Please enter the name of the person who referred you.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, required): Please add any additional information.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions accurately and honestly to ensure the best possible outcome.
* If you're unsure about any questions, please select "Not Sure" or "I don't know" and the provider will assist you further.
* You can always ask the provider if you have any questions or concerns during the consultation.
