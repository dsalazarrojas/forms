# General Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The General Informed Consent Form is a document used to obtain informed consent from clients or individuals for the collection and use of their personal data. This form ensures that we respect their rights and privacy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Read each page carefully and answer all the questions to the best of your ability.
2.  Answer the question "what_is_the_basis_of_your_request" on Page 1 with a brief description of the purpose or context of your request.
3.  On Page 2, select the relevant option for "informed_consent_status" that best describes your consent status.
4.  On Page 3, select the options that apply to your consent to collect and use your data.
5.  On Page 4, enter the date you are providing consent for.
6.  If required, sign your name and add any additional comments in the "signature" field.
7.  Enter your name and contact information, including email and phone number, in the "name_of_person", "contact_information", "email", and "phone" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*  **what_is_the_basis_of_your_request**: This field is a description of the purpose or context of your request. Please provide a brief explanation.
*  **informed_consent_status**: This field is used to indicate your informed consent status. Choose the option that best describes your consent status.
*  **consent_to_collect_and_use_your_data**: This field is used to select the options that apply to your consent for data collection and use.
*  **date_of_consent**: If you are providing consent, enter the date you are providing it for. This field is optional.
*  **signature**: This field is for your digital signature. If required, please sign your name and add any additional comments.
*  **name_of_person**: This field is for your name. Enter your name as you would like it to appear in our records.
*  **contact_information**: This field is for your contact information. Enter your contact information as it appears on your records.
*  **email**: This field is for your email address. Enter a valid email address.
*  **phone**: This field is for your phone number. Enter a valid phone number.
*  **address**: This field is for your address. Enter a valid address.
