# Lash Lift - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lash Lift form is used to collect information from clients regarding their Lash Lift appointment. This includes their first and last name, email, phone number, birth date, lash lift description, consent, and lash tint description.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First name in the **First name** field.
2. Enter your Last name in the **Last name** field.
3. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
5. Enter your birth date in the **Birth Date** field.
6. Provide a description of your Lash Lift in the **Lash Lift Description** field.
7. Select your consent option from the **Consent** field.
8. Provide a description of your lash tint in the **Lash Tint Description** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Birth Date** (date, optional): Enter your birth date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Lash Lift Description** (text, optional): Describe your Lash Lift appointment.
* **Consent** (select_one, optional): Choose one of the provided options to indicate your consent.
* **Lash Tint Description** (text, optional): Describe your lash tint appointment.
* **Consent** (select_one, optional) is used to confirm that the client has provided information for the purpose of the appointment.
