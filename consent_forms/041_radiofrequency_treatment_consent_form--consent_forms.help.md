# Radiofrequency Treatment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from patients before undergoing radiofrequency treatment. It ensures that the patient understands the treatment, its risks, and its benefits.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand the treatment and its implications.
2. Complete the form by providing your patient information, treatment details, and consent date.
3. If necessary, add additional notes about the treatment or patient.
4. Check the signature box to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information**: Enter your name, date of birth, and any other relevant details.
* **Treatment Information**: Describe the radiofrequency treatment you are undergoing, including its purpose, risks, and benefits.
* **Consent Information**: Confirm that you have read and understood the treatment details and are willing to undergo the treatment.
* **Consent Date**: Enter the date you are giving your consent.
* **Note**: Add any additional notes about the treatment or your medical history.
	+ **Note**: Select "Note" if you have additional comments about your medical history.
	+ **Other**: Select "Other" if you have any other relevant information to share.
* **Signature**: Sign your name to confirm your consent.

## Tips
- Make sure to review the form carefully before signing it.
- Ask a healthcare professional if you have any questions or concerns about the form or the treatment.
- Keep a copy of the form for your records.
