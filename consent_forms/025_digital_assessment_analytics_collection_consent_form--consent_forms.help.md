# Digital Assessment Analytics Collection Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect consent from users to access, process, and analyze their data for the purpose of improving services, products, or research.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in the form details as required or as necessary.
2.  Select your consent level (high, low, or medium) for data collection and analysis.
3.  Decide whether to grant consent by checking or unchecking the option.
4.  Provide your name and email address if you wish to be contacted or informed about any further actions or changes regarding your consent.
5.  If desired, enter your phone number for further contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Details** (`page_1`, text, required/optional): This is the first page of the form with no specific description provided.
* **What is your consent level?** (`consent_question`, select_one, required/optional): Select your level of consent for data collection and analysis. The options are high, low, and medium.
* **Do you grant consent?** (`consent`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check or uncheck to grant or deny consent to data collection and analysis.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, which will be used for identification or further communication regarding your consent.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address, which will be used for further contact or communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): If desired, enter your phone number for further contact or communication.
