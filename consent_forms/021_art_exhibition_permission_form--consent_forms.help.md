# Art Exhibition Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by artists to provide necessary information and grant permission for their artworks to be displayed, photographed, and potentially sold during an exhibition.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your artist details in the "Artist Details" section, including your full name, professional name (if applicable), email address, and phone number.
2. Provide information about the artwork in the "Artwork Information" section, including the title, medium, dimensions, and year of creation.
3. Specify your permissions and rights for the artwork, including display, photography, marketing and promotion, and sales.
4. Confirm your declaration and sign the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Professional Name (If different)**: If you have a professional name or artistic alias, enter it here.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Title of the Artwork**: Enter the title of the artwork you are submitting.
* **Medium (Materials used)**: Enter the medium or materials used to create the artwork (e.g., oil on canvas, digital print, bronze sculpture).
* **Dimensions (H x W x D)**: Enter the dimensions of the artwork, including height, width, and depth (in cm or inches).
* **Approximate Weight (for sculptures/installations)**: If the artwork is a sculpture or installation, enter the approximate weight.
* **Year of Creation**: Enter the year the artwork was created.
* **Display Permission**: Grant permission for the gallery to display your artwork during the exhibition.
* **Permission for visitors to photograph the artwork**: Choose whether visitors are allowed to photograph the artwork for personal use or any use.
* **Permission for the gallery to use images of the artwork for marketing and promotion**: Choose whether the gallery can use images of your artwork for marketing and promotion, including social media, catalogs, and website.
* **Is this artwork available for sale during the exhibition?**: Specify whether the artwork is available for sale or not.
* **Asking Price (Including gallery commission)**: If the artwork is for sale, enter the asking price, including the gallery commission.
* **Insurance Valuation**: Enter the insurance value of the artwork.
* **Artist Declaration**: Confirm that you are the sole creator and copyright owner of the artwork.
* **Date of Signature**: Sign the form with the current date.
* **Artist Digital Signature (Full Name)**: Enter your full name, as it appears on your identification.

Note: If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact the exhibition organizers for clarification.
