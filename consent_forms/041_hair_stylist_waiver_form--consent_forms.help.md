# Hair Stylist Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients for a hair stylist waiver. The purpose of this form is to gather contact and identification details from clients, which will be used by the hair stylist for communication and service purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your client's name in the "Client Name" field. This field is not required, but it's essential to provide accurate and clear identification.
2. Enter your client's contact number in the "Contact Number" field. This field is also not required but is crucial for communication and follow-up purposes.
3. Sign and date the "Signature" field. This field is optional, but it's recommended to include a signature for official acknowledgment and consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name (Text)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Contact Number (Text)**: Enter your phone number or other contact details where you can be reached for communication or follow-up purposes.
* **Signature (Note)**: Provide your signature and date to officially acknowledge and consent to the hair stylist's terms and conditions. This field is not required but highly recommended for official purposes.

## Tips

* Ensure to provide accurate and clear information in all fields.
* Double-check your entries for any typos or errors before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to the contact provided in the form's description.
