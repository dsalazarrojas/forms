# Direct Deposit Setup Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Direct Deposit Setup Consent Form is designed to collect employee consent and necessary information to set up direct deposit services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review this help guide to understand each field and its purpose.
2. Click on the first field to begin filling out the form.
3. Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Direct Deposit Setup Consent Form**
*   (1) **Direct Deposit Setup Consent Form**: This field is not a field to fill out, but rather the form title. Please do not fill it out.

*   **Employee Name** (2) **employee_name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID, payroll records, or other relevant documents.
    *   This field is for your reference and to ensure accurate processing of your information.
    *   Please type your name as it is written on your official ID, payroll records, or other relevant documents.

### **Direct Deposit Account Number** (3) **direct_deposit_account_number**: Enter the account number of your bank account where you want to receive direct deposits.
    *   This is the number associated with your bank account that you want to use for direct deposit services.
    *   Please double-check the accuracy of this number with your bank or financial institution.

### **Address** (4) **address**: Enter your address for direct deposit purposes.
    *   This is the address associated with your account that the bank will use for direct deposit.
    *   Please type in the exact address to which you want direct deposits to be sent.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
*   Double-check the account number with your bank or financial institution.
*   If you are unsure about any part of this form, please contact your HR department or financial advisor for guidance.
