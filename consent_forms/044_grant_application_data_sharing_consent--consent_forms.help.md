# Grant Application Data Sharing Consent - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Grant Application Data Sharing Consent form is used to obtain your consent for the sharing of data related to a grant application. This form is typically completed by applicants or their representatives to confirm their consent for the sharing of their personal and professional data with relevant authorities, partners, or third parties involved in the grant process. This data may be used for grant review, processing, and evaluation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand what data will be shared.
2. Enter your name in the "What is your name?" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "What is your email address?" field.
4. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "What is your phone number?" field.
5. Submit the form after entering all required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Grant Application Data Sharing Consent
*   **Grant Application Data Sharing Consent**: This is a brief note that explains the purpose and scope of the form. Review this section carefully before proceeding to understand what data will be shared.

### What is your name?
*   **What is your name?** (`grant_application_data_sharing_consent_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter your full name, as it will be used to identify you and for communication purposes.

### What is your email address?
*   **What is your email address?** (`grant_application_data_sharing_consent_3`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address where you can be contacted for grant-related communications.

### What is your phone number?
*   **What is your phone number?** (`grant_application_data_sharing_consent_4`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number, if applicable, to ensure we can contact you for further information or updates.
