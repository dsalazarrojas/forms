# mental_health_safety_plan - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an individual's mental health safety plan, including emergency contacts, mental health provider information, and follow-up details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your information in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*) next to the label.
2. Enter accurate and up-to-date information about your emergency contacts.
3. Choose the status of your mental health safety plan and mental health provider.
4. Select follow-up frequency and type.
5. Choose a contact method for follow-ups.
6. Add any additional follow-up notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mental Health Safety Plan** (`1`, text, required): This is the main section of the form where you can enter a brief description of your mental health safety plan.
* **Emergency Contact 1** (`2`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of your primary emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number 1** (`3`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your primary emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Alternate Phone Number 1** (`4`, text, required): Enter an alternate phone number of your primary emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact 2** (`5`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of your secondary emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number 2** (`6`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your secondary emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Alternate Phone Number 2** (`7`, text, required): Enter an alternate phone number of your secondary emergency contact.
* **Mental Health Crisis Plan** (`8`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of your mental health crisis plan.
* **Mental Health Provider** (`9`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of your mental health provider.
* **Mental Health Crisis History** (`10`, text, required): Enter any relevant mental health crisis history.
* **Mental Health Provider Phone Number** (`11`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your mental health provider.
* **Mental Health Crisis Plan Notes** (`12`, text, required): Enter any additional notes about your mental health crisis plan.
* **Follow-up Notes** (`13`, text, required): Enter any follow-up notes.
* **Follow-up Contact** (`14`, text, required): Enter the contact information for follow-ups.
* **Mental Health Safety Plan Completed** (`15`, date, required): Enter the date the mental health safety plan was completed.
* **Mental Health Safety Plan Expiration Date** (`16`, date, required): Enter the date the mental health safety plan will expire.
* **Mental Health Safety Plan Status** (`17`, select_one, required): Choose the status of your mental health safety plan (Active, Inactive, Expired).
* **Mental Health Provider Status** (`18`, select_multiple, required): Choose the status of your mental health provider (Active, Inactive, On Leave, Expired).
* **Follow-up Frequency** (`19`, select_one, required): Choose the frequency of follow-ups (Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **Follow-up Type** (`20`, select_one, required): Choose the type of follow-ups (Phone, Video, In-person).
* **Follow-up Method** (`21`, select_one, required): Choose the method of follow-ups (Phone call, Text message, Email).
* **Follow-up Contact Method** (`22`, select_one, required): Choose the method of follow-up contacts (Phone, Video call, In-person).
* **Follow-up Notes 2** (`23`, text, required): Enter any additional follow-up notes.
* **Relationship to Emergency Contact** (`24`, select_one, required): Choose the relationship to your emergency contact (Parent, Spouse, Partner, Child, Other).
* **Mental Health Provider Organization** (`25`, text, required): Enter the name of your mental health provider organization.
* **Mental Health Safety Plan Version** (`26`, number, required): Enter the version of your mental health safety plan.

## Tips
* Make sure to review and update your mental health safety plan regularly to ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you need help filling out this form, contact your mental health provider or emergency contact for guidance.
* Keep your mental health safety plan in a safe and secure location where it can be easily accessed in case of a crisis.
