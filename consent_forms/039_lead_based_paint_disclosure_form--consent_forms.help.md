# Lead Based Paint Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lead-Based Paint Disclosure Form is designed to collect information about the presence of lead-based paint on residential properties. This form helps to identify properties with lead-based paint, ensuring that homeowners and property managers take necessary precautions and safety measures to minimize exposure to lead.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the questions and fields presented on each page to understand what information is required.
2. Answer each question or provide the requested information accurately and completely.
3. Use the provided options or input fields as instructed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Lead-based Paint is Present

* **Lead-based Paint is Present** (`lead_based_paint_disclosure_form_page_1`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose from the options below to indicate the presence of lead-based paint on the property.
	+ Options: Lead-based Paint is Present, No Lead-based Paint is Present

### Page 2: Contact Information

* **Contact Information** (`contact_information_form_page`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the contact name and information of the property owner or manager.
	+ Please use a valid name and contact number for the property.

### Page 3: Lead Paint Homes

* **Lead Paint Homes** (`lead_paint_homes_page`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select all homes on the property that contain lead-based paint.
	+ Options: 1, 2, 3

### Page 4: Contact Number

* **Contact Number** (`contact_number_form_page`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the contact number for the property owner or manager.
	+ Please use a valid telephone number.

### Page 5: Date

* **Lead Paint Disclosure Date** (`lead_paint_disclosure_form_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date of the inspection or assessment.
	+ Please use the correct date format (mm/dd/yyyy).

### Page 6: Time

* **Lead Paint Disclosure Time** (`lead_paint_disclosure_form_time`, time, optional):
	+ Enter the time of the inspection or assessment.
	+ Please use the correct time format (hh:MM AM/PM).

### Page 7: Lead Paint Disclosure

* **Lead Paint Disclosure** (`lead_paint_disclosure_form`, note, optional):
	+ Enter any additional comments or notes about the lead-based paint on the property.
	+ Please provide any relevant information about the paint.

### Page 8: Lead Paint Disclosure Email

* **Lead Paint Disclosure Email** (`lead_paint_disclosure_email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter the email address of the property owner or manager.
	+ Please use a valid email address.

Note: The fields are marked as optional, but please ensure that you provide all required information to ensure accurate and complete responses. If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities for guidance.
