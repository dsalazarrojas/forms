# Exhibit F 1 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect demographic information from individuals who have visited or have been in contact with the organization. It includes fields for form number, name, date of birth, contact number, address, contact email, date of visit, time of visit, reason for visit, and consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your form number.
2. Provide your name.
3. Enter your date of birth.
4. Enter your contact number.
5. Enter your address.
6. Enter your contact email.
7. If your visit was on a specific date, enter the date of visit.
8. If applicable, enter the time of visit.
9. If applicable, enter the reason for visit.
10. Check your level of consent.
11. Enter any alternative contact number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form-number**: This is the unique identifier of the form submission. Enter the form number as provided.
* **name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **date-of-birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format `DD/MM/YYYY`.
* **contact-number**: Enter your primary contact number.
* **address**: Enter your current address.
* **contact-email**: Enter your official email address.
* **date-of-visit**: If you were visited on a specific date, enter the date.
* **time-of-visit**: If you were visited at a specific time, enter the time.
* **reason-for-visit**: If your visit was for a specific reason, enter it.
* **consent**: Select either "Yes" to indicate your consent or "No" if you do not consent.
* **contact-number-alt**: If you have an alternative contact number, enter it.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
- Ensure that you have filled all required fields.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact the organization's support team.
