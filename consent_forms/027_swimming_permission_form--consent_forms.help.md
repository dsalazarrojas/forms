# swimming_permission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The swimming permission form is used to collect information from parents or guardians of children participating in swimming lessons. This form helps us to tailor the lessons to each child's needs and abilities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your child's name and your contact information.
3. Indicate the child's swimming level and ability.
4. Choose your preferred contact method.
5. Provide emergency contact information.
6. Confirm your permission for your child to participate in swimming lessons.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent name (page 1)** (`parent_name`, required): Please enter your name as the parent or guardian of the child.
* **Child name (page 1)** (`child_name`, required): Please enter your child's name.
* **Swimmer level (page 1)** (`swimmer_level`, required): Choose the level of swimming ability your child has:
	+ Beginner: Starting to learn swim techniques.
	+ Intermediate: Has some experience swimming.
	+ Advanced: Skilled swimmer.
* **Swimmer ability (page 1)** (`swimmer_ability`, required): Choose your child's swimming ability:
	+ Beginner: Just starting out.
	+ Intermediate: Can swim some strokes.
	+ Advanced: Can perform most strokes.
* **Swimmer frequency (page 1)** (`swimmer_frequency`, required): How often does your child swim?
	+ Daily: Every day.
	+ 2-3 times a week: A couple of times a week.
	+ 1 time a week: Once a week.
	+ Less than once a week: Rarely.
* **Swimmer experience (page 1)** (`swimmer_experience`, required): Has your child had swimming experience?
	+ Yes: Has had some experience.
	+ No: Has no experience.
* **Instructor (page 1)** (`instructor`, required): Choose your preferred instructor:
	+ Jimmy: Our most experienced instructor.
	+ Jane: Our most patient instructor.
	+ Jack: Our most enthusiastic instructor.
* **Contact method (page 1)** (`contact_method`, required): How would you like to be contacted about your child?
	+ Email: Receive emails about your child's progress.
	+ Phone: Receive phone calls about your child's progress.
	+ Text: Receive text messages about your child's progress.
* **Emergency contact (page 2)** (`emergency_contact`, optional): Please enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency phone (page 2)** (`emergency_phone`, required): Please enter the phone number of an emergency contact person.
* **Notes (page 2)** (`notes`, optional): Any additional notes about your child's swimming abilities or requirements.
* **Email (page 2)** (`email`, required): Please enter a valid email address.
* **Parent permission for lessons (page 2)** (`parent_permission`, required): Do you grant permission for your child to participate in swimming lessons?
	+ Yes: Allow your child to participate.
	+ No: Do not allow your child to participate.
* **Medical conditions (page 2)** (`medical_conditions`, required): Please describe any medical conditions your child may have.
* **Instructor for lessons (page 2)** (`instructor`, required): Choose your preferred instructor for lessons:
	+ Jimmy: Our most experienced instructor.
	+ Jane: Our most patient instructor.
	+ Jack: Our most enthusiastic instructor.
