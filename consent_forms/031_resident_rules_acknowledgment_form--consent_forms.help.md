# Resident Rules Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to be completed by residents of a building or facility to acknowledge that they have read and understand the rules and regulations of the property.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form entirely or partially, as required. If a field is not marked as required, you may skip it.
- Please select the correct answer to the questions asked, as per the provided options.
- Make sure to review and understand the rules and regulations of the property before completing the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resident Rules Acknowledgment Form** (Resident Rules Acknowledgment Form, note, optional): This field is used to display the title of the form.
* **Resident Rules Acknowledgment Form 2** (Resident Rules Acknowledgment Form 2, select_one, optional): Choose "Yes" to indicate that you have read and understand the rules, or "No" if you have not.
* **Resident Rules Acknowledgment Form 3** (Resident Rules Acknowledgment Form 3, select_multiple, optional): Choose the options that apply to you, for example, "Yes" to indicate you have understood the rules, and "No" if you have not.
* **Resident Rules Acknowledgment Form 4** (Resident Rules Acknowledgment Form 4, date, optional): You may enter a date to indicate when you last reviewed the rules.
