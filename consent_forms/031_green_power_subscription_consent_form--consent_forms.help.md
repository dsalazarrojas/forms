# green_power_subscription_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain a customer's consent for the green power subscription.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read the terms and conditions carefully and agree to them by selecting the appropriate checkbox.
* Select the desired plan for the service from the provided options.
* Enter your name.
* Enter your phone number.
* Enter your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details**: This section is where you enter your information.
  * **Customer Name**: Enter your full name.
  * **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number.
  * **Customer Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Plan Selections**: Select the plan you are interested in.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Carefully read and agree to the terms and conditions of the service by selecting the checkbox.
* **Consent**: This is a note field to confirm your consent.
