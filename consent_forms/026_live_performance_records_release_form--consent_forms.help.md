# Live Performance Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Live Performance Records Release Form is used to obtain the consent of a live performance artist to release their recorded performance. This form helps ensure that the artist is aware of and consents to the release of their records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields, which include selecting the type of recording, granting or denying permission, and providing your name, email, phone number, and date of performance. You can also provide a manager or artist name if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Artist Information (<id: 1, name: artist_info>**) (Text): Enter your name as the artist performing.
* **Permission (<id: 2, name: permission>**) (Select One): Grant permission for the release of your live performance records:
	+ Recording: Grant permission for the release of video and audio recordings.
	+ Distribution: Grant permission for the release of your live performance records online or offline.
	+ Both: Grant permission for both video and audio recordings.
	+ Neither: Deny permission for the release of your live performance records.
* **Recording (<id: 3, name: recording>**) (Select Multiple): Select the type of recording you wish to release:
	+ Video: Grant permission for the release of video recordings.
	+ Audio: Grant permission for the release of audio recordings.
	+ Photographs: Grant permission for the release of photographs.
* **Distribution (<id: 4, name: distribution>**) (Select Multiple): Select where you wish to release your records:
	+ Online: Release your records online.
	+ Offline: Release your records offline.
	+ Live Stream: Release your records through live streaming.
* **Agreement (<id: 5, name: agreement>**) (Note): Please sign below to indicate your consent.
* **Signature (<id: 6, name: signature>**) (Date): Date and sign to confirm your agreement.
* **Email (<id: 7, name: email>**) (Email): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone (<id: 8, name: phone>**) (Text): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Name (<id: 9, name: name>**) (Text): Enter your name for contact purposes.
* **Date of Performance (<id: 10, name: date_of_performance>**) (Date): Enter the date of the performance.
* **Location (<id: 11, name: location>**) (Text): Enter the location of the performance.
* **Event Name (<id: 12, name: event_name>**) (Text): Enter the name of the event.
* **Artist (<id: 13, name: artist>**) (Text): Enter the name of the artist performing.
* **Manager (<id: 14, name: manager>**) (Text): Enter the name of your manager (if applicable).

## Tips
- Ensure that you have carefully reviewed and understood the purpose and fields of this form.
- Take your time to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
- Make sure to sign and date the agreement to confirm your consent.
