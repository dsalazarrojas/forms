# Skin Care Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skin Care Consent Form is a document used to obtain informed consent from clients before skin care procedures are performed. It is essential to understand that this form is intended for clients who are receiving skin care treatments or services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the procedure description (id: 3) to understand the skin care procedure you are about to receive. This section outlines the details of the treatment, including what the procedure entails and what you can expect.
2. Answer the questions on the client information page (id: 2) with your relevant details. This will help us understand who you are and your background.
3. Indicate your consent status (id: 4) by selecting "Yes" or "No". This is your acknowledgement that you have been informed and understand the procedure.
4. If necessary, sign the signature field (id: 5) with your name and date (id: 6 and id: 7) to confirm that you have read and understood the consent form.
5. Finally, enter your client name (id: 8) for our records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information (id: 2)**: This is where you will provide your personal details, such as your name, contact information, and any other relevant background information.
* **Procedure Description (id: 3)**: This section outlines the details of the skin care procedure you are about to receive, including what it entails and what you can expect.
* **Consent Status (id: 4)**: Select "Yes" if you understand and consent to the procedure, or "No" if you do not consent.
* **Signature (id: 5)**: This is where you sign your name to confirm that you have read and understood the consent form.
* **Date Signed (id: 6)**: Enter the date you signed the consent form.
* **Time Signed (id: 7)**: Enter the time you signed the consent form.
* **Client Name (id: 8)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.

## Tips
* Please review the procedure description carefully before indicating your consent.
* If you have any questions or concerns, ask your provider for clarification.
* Ensure that all fields are filled out completely and accurately.
* This form is a requirement for all skin care procedures. Please complete it before the procedure begins.
