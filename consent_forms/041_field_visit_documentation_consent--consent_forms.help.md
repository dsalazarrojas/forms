# Field Visit Documentation Consent - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Field Visit Documentation Consent form is used to gather important information about field visits, including client consent, photos, videos, location details, and more. This form ensures that all necessary information is collected and documented accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have all the necessary information about the field visit.
2. Enter the client consent (yes or no) in the "Client Consent" field.
3. Add any relevant photos or videos by selecting the appropriate options.
4. Enter the location details, including the latitude and longitude.
5. Provide a description of the field visit in the "Field Visit Notes" field.
6. Enter the date and time of the visit.
7. Indicate who you have agreed to share the visit with.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Field Visit Documentation Consent** (`field_visit_documentation_consent`, text, required): Enter your initial thoughts and observations about the field visit.
  This is an opportunity to provide a brief description of the visit, including any notable observations or findings.
- **Client Consent** (`field_visit_consent_2`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether the client gave their consent for the visit.
  This is a critical field that ensures compliance with regulations and maintains client trust.
- **Field Visit Notes** (`field_visit_notes`, text, required): Describe the field visit in detail.
  This field should include any significant observations, conversations, or findings from the visit.
- **Field Visit Photos** (`field_visit_photos`, select_multiple, required): Select any photos taken during the field visit.
  Choose one or multiple photos that are relevant to the visit.
- **Field Visit Videos** (`field_visit_videos`, select_multiple, required): Select any videos taken during the field visit.
  Choose one or multiple videos that are relevant to the visit.
- **Field Visit Audio** (`field_visit_audio`, note, optional): Provide additional audio notes about the field visit.
  This field is optional and can be used to provide additional context.
- **Field Visit Location** (`field_visit_location`, text, required): Enter the location details, including latitude and longitude.
  This field helps to identify the visit location and ensure accurate mapping.
- **Field Visit Date** (`field_visit_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the field visit.
  This field is essential for keeping accurate records and scheduling next visits.
- **Field Visit Time** (`field_visit_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the field visit.
  This field ensures that visit timings are accurately recorded.
- **Field Visit Latitude** (`field_visit_latitude`, number, required): Enter the latitude of the field visit location.
  This field is necessary for mapping and tracking visits.
- **Field Visit Longitude** (`field_visit_longitude`, number, required): Enter the longitude of the field visit location.
  This field is necessary for mapping and tracking visits.
- **Field Visit Additional Notes** (`field_visit_additional_notes`, text, required): Provide any additional information about the field visit.
  This field can include any extra details or comments.
- **Field Visit Agreed to Share** (`field_visit_agreed_to_share`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you have agreed to share the visit with others.
  This field is critical for maintaining transparency and collaboration.
- **Field Visit Agreed to Share With** (`field_visit_agreed_to_share_with`, text, required): Enter the name or contact information of the person you agreed to share with.
  This field should include the name or email of the person.
- **Field Visit Agreed to Share With Email** (`field_visit_agreed_to_share_with_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person you agreed to share with.
  This field should include the email address.
- **Field Visit Agreed to Share With Phone** (`field_visit_agreed_to_share_with_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the person you agreed to share with.
  This field should include the phone number.
