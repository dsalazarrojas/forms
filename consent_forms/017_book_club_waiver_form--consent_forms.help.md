# Book Club Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Book Club Waiver Form is a required document for new participants to join the book club. It collects personal and contact information, as well as consent and waiver agreements that outline the participant's understanding of the book club's expectations and rules.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, such as your name, email address, phone number, and date of birth.
2. Select the correct options for the Code of Conduct Agreement, Confidentiality Agreement, Attendance Expectation Understanding, Book Purchase Responsibility, Photo and Video Release, Liability Waiver, Medical Emergency Consent, Data Privacy Consent, and Book Purchase Responsibility sections.
3. Enter your address, city, and state.
4. Provide your ZIP code.
5. Fill in the name of the book club you are joining.
6. Select the start date for your membership.
7. Fill in the name, phone number, and relationship of your emergency contact.
8. If applicable, list any relevant medical conditions or allergies.
9. Finally, sign the form with your full name and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first and last name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Your phone number for emergency contact.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Your birth date.
* **Street Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Your home address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Your city.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Your state.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Your ZIP code.
* **Book Club Name** (`book_club_name`, `text`, required): Name of the book club you are joining.
* **Membership Start Date** (`membership_start_date`, `date`, required): When you are starting your membership.
* **Code of Conduct Agreement** (`code_of_conduct`, `select_one`, required): Check "Yes I agree" if you understand and accept the book club's code of conduct.
* **Confidentiality Agreement** (`confidentiality_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Check "Yes I agree" if you understand and accept to keep personal discussions confidential.
* **Attendance Expectation Understanding** (`attendance_expectation`, `select_one`, required): Check "Yes I understand" if you understand the importance of regular attendance in the book club.
* **Book Purchase Responsibility** (`book_purchase_responsibility`, `select_one`, required): Check "Yes I understand" if you are responsible for purchasing books for the book club.
* **Photo and Video Release** (`photo_release`, `select_one`, required): Check "Yes I grant permission" to use your photo for promotional purposes.
* **Liability Waiver** (`liability_waiver`, `select_one`, required): Check "Yes I waive liability" if you understand and accept the book club's liability waiver.
* **Medical Emergency Consent** (`medical_emergency_consent`, `select_one`, required): Check "Yes I consent" if you grant permission to seek medical help in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Full name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required): Relationship to you (e.g., family member, friend, or roommate).
* **Relevant Medical Conditions or Allergies** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, optional): List any medical conditions or allergies that may impact your participation in the book club.
* **Data Privacy Consent** (`data_privacy_consent`, `select_one`, required): Check "Yes I consent" if you understand and accept the book club's data privacy policy.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, `text`, required): Type your full name as a signature.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, required): Date of signing.
* **Witness Name** (`witness_name`, `text`, optional): Full name of witness if applicable.
* **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`, `text`, optional): Type witness full name as signature if applicable.
