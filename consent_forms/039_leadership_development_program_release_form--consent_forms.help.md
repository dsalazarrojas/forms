# leadership_development_program_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect consent from participants in the Leadership Development Program. It ensures that participants understand the program's expectations, risks, and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the correct participant type from the "Participant Details" field.
2. Check the "Program Expectations" field to confirm you understand the program's rules and regulations.
3. Select the appropriate answers for "Risks and Consequences" to demonstrate your understanding.
4. Sign and date the "Participant Signature" and "Supervisor/Manager Signature" fields.
5. Enter your name in the "Organization" field.
6. Sign and date the "Participant Signature Date" and "Supervisor Signature Date" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (Participant, Guest, required: false):
	+ Choose "Participant" if you are a member of the organization.
	+ Choose "Guest" if you are a visitor or not a member of the organization.
* **Program Expectations** (Select multiple options, required: false):
	+ Check the boxes indicating you understand and accept the expectations of the program.
* **Risks and Consequences** (Select multiple options, required: false):
	+ Check the boxes indicating you understand the potential risks associated with this program.
	+ Check the boxes indicating you understand the consequences of your actions while participating in this program.
* **Participant Signature** (Signature, required: false):
	+ Sign your name to confirm you have read and understood the form.
* **Supervisor/Manager Signature** (Signature, required: false):
	+ Sign your supervisor/manager's name to confirm they have discussed and agreed to the expectations.
* **Date** (Date, required: false):
	+ Enter the date you are signing the form.
* **Time** (Time, required: false):
	+ Enter the time you are signing the form.
* **Organization** (Text, required: false):
	+ Enter your name and title in the organization.
* **Participant Signature Date** (Date, required: false):
	+ Enter the date you sign the form.
* **Supervisor/Manager Signature Date** (Date, required: false):
	+ Enter the date your supervisor/manager signs the form.

## Tips
- Make sure to carefully read and understand each field before signing the form.
- Ensure you have discussed and agreed to the expectations with your supervisor/manager.
- Sign and date the form as instructed.
