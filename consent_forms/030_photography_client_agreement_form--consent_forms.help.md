# Photography Client Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect client information, contact details, and terms of service for a photography session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. Review the Terms and Conditions to understand the agreement.
3. Sign the agreement by typing your name in the Signature field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details** (`client_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, contact information (phone, email, address), and any other relevant details you want to share with the photographer.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact information, including phone number, email, and address.
* **Photography Session** (`photography_session`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the photography session you are planning.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, text, required/optional): Review and agree to the terms and conditions outlined in this section.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required/optional): Sign the agreement by typing your name in this field.
* **Session Details** (`session_details`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional details about the photography session, such as date, time, location.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, required/optional): Enter any payment information or instructions for the photography session.
* **Contact Us** (`contact_us`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact information or any questions you have for the photographer.

Note: All fields are optional and can be completed or left blank as per the client's preference.
