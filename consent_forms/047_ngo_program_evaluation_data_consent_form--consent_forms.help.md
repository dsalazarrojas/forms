# NGO Program Evaluation Data Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information and consent from participants in the NGO program evaluation process. It serves as a legal agreement between the participant and the evaluating organization, outlining the terms of data collection and usage.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please review the form carefully to ensure you understand the purpose and content before proceeding.
2. Fill out the required fields (marked as `required: true` in the form) accurately and honestly.
3. For fields with options (like `program_evaluation_participant_level` and `program_evaluation_program_phase`), select the most relevant option that applies to your situation.
4. For date fields (like `program_evaluation_evaluation_start`, `data_collection_data_collection_start`, `data_collection_data_collection_end`, and `consent_consent_date`), use the format `YYYY-MM-DD` to avoid any errors.
5. Sign the form by printing your name in the `signature` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`): Enter your basic contact information (name, email, phone number, and address).
* **Program Evaluation** (`program_evaluation`): Provide information about the program you are participating in (program name, evaluation start and end dates, participant level, and phase).
* **Data Collection** (`data_collection`): Specify the frequency and dates of data collection (start and end dates, data collection frequency).
* **Consent** (`consent`): Confirm your consent to participate in the program evaluation and specify the version of consent you are agreeing to.
* **Contact Us** (`contact_us`): Provide your contact information (email and phone number) for any follow-up questions or concerns.
* **Signature** (`signature`): Sign the form by printing your name to confirm your consent and participation.
* **Name** (`participant_information_name`): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email** (`participant_information_email`): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`participant_information_phone`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address** (`participant_information_address`): Enter your address for contact purposes.
* **Program Name** (`program_evaluation_program_name`): Enter the name of the program you are participating in.
* **Evaluation Start** (`program_evaluation_evaluation_start`): Enter the start date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation End** (`program_evaluation_evaluation_end`): Enter the end date of the evaluation.
* **Participant Level** (`program_evaluation_participant_level`): Select the level of participation (high school, college, university/college, or other).
* **Program Phase** (`program_evaluation_program_phase`): Select the phase of the program (phase 1 or phase 2).
* **Data Collection Start** (`data_collection_data_collection_start`): Enter the start date of data collection.
* **Data Collection End** (`data_collection_data_collection_end`): Enter the end date of data collection.
* **Data Collection Frequency** (`data_collection_frequency`): Select the frequency of data collection (monthly, quarterly, bi-annually).
* **Consent Given** (`consent_consent_given`): Confirm whether you have given consent to participate.
* **Version of Consent** (`consent_consent_version`): Specify the version of consent you are agreeing to.
* **Date of Consent** (`consent_consent_date`): Enter the date you gave consent.
* **Time of Consent** (`consent_consent_time`): Enter the time you gave consent (HH:MM format).
* **Version Number of Consent** (`consent_consent_version_number`): Specify the version number of consent you are agreeing to.
* **Email** (`contact_us_email`): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`contact_us_phone`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
