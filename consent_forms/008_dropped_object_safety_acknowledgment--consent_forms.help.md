# Dropped Object Safety Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dropped Object Safety Acknowledgment form is used to document and acknowledge the safety procedures and protocols related to a dropped object. This form helps ensure that all individuals involved in the incident are aware of the potential hazards and take necessary precautions to prevent accidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the worker's name and position in the corresponding fields.
2. Fill in the supervisor's name and position.
3. Describe the hazards associated with the dropped object.
4. Review and acknowledge the site procedures related to the incident.
5. Provide a brief statement indicating that you have read and understand the procedures.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **worker_name** (`worker_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the worker involved in the incident.
* **worker_position** (`worker_position`, text, required): Enter the position or role of the worker involved in the incident.
* **supervisor_name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the supervisor who reported the incident.
* **supervisor_position** (`supervisor_position`, text, required): Enter the position or role of the supervisor who reported the incident.
* **dropped_object_hazards** (`dropped_object_hazards`, text, required): Describe the potential hazards associated with the dropped object, such as sharp edges, heavy weight, or other dangers.
* **site_procedures** (`site_procedures`, text, required): Review and acknowledge the site procedures related to the incident.
* **worker_understanding** (`worker_understanding`, text, required): Provide a brief statement indicating that you have read and understand the procedures.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* If there are additional hazards or procedures related to the incident, include them in the corresponding fields.
* Review and submit the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
