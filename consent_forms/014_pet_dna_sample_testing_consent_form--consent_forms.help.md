# pet_dna_sample_testing_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect consent from pet owners for DNA sample testing. It is essential to have the owner's consent before proceeding with any testing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the pet owner's details in the "Pet Owner Details" field.
2. Select the owner's contact method (Email, Phone, or Address) in the "Owner Contact Info" field.
3. Provide the pet's details in the "Pet Details" field (if required).
4. Choose the testing preferences (Genetic Test, Non-Genetic Test, or Both Tests) in the "Testing Preferences" field.
5. Enter the testing results date in the "Testing Results" field.
6. Choose the pet owner's consent status (Consent, No Consent, or Pending Consent) in the "Pet Dna Sample Testing Consent" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Owner Details (`pet_owner_details`, text, required)**: Enter the pet owner's name and contact information.
* **Owner Contact Info (`owner_contact_info`, select_one, required)**: Choose how you would like to contact the pet owner (Email, Phone, or Address).
* **Pet Details (`pet_details`, text, optional)**: If you are providing pet details, please ensure they are accurate and up-to-date.
* **Testing Preferences (`testing_preferences`, select_multiple, required)**: Choose the type of testing you would like to perform (Genetic Test, Non-Genetic Test, or Both Tests).
* **Testing Instructions (`testing_instructions`, note, optional)**: Add any additional testing instructions or information.
* **Owner Notes (`owner_notes`, note, optional)**: Add any important notes or comments for the pet owner.
* **Testing Results (`testing_results`, date, required)**: Enter the date when the testing results will be available.
* **Pet Dna Sample Testing Consent (`pet_dna_sample_testing_consent`, select_one, required)**: Choose the pet owner's consent status (Consent, No Consent, or Pending Consent).
