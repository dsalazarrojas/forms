# Safety Training Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to acknowledge completion of safety training programs within the organization. It helps ensure that employees have received and understood the training they need to perform their job safely and effectively. This form is not mandatory, but it is recommended that employees complete it for their own records and to demonstrate their understanding of safety protocols.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the relevant fields with the required information. Please make sure to enter accurate and complete details for each field. If a field is marked as optional, you are not required to fill it in, but it is recommended that you do so if relevant to your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name (employee name, text, required/optional):** Enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID.
* **Training Program (training program, select_one, optional):** Select 'Yes' if you have completed the required safety training program. If you have not, select 'No'. This field is optional but helps management track training completion.
* **Training Date (training date, date, required/optional):** Enter the date you received the safety training. If you don't have a specific date, you can leave this field blank.
* **Training Location (training location, text, optional):** Enter the location where you received the training. This field is not mandatory, but it helps management track the training location.
* **Training Facilitator (training facilitator, text, optional):** Enter the name and title of the facilitator who conducted the training.
* **Employee ID (employee id, number, optional):** This field is not used in the current form version.
* **Signature Date (signature date, date, required/optional):** Enter the date you signed this acknowledgement form. This field is required for official records.
* **Employee Signature (employee signature, text, required/optional):** Sign this form to acknowledge that you have received and understood the training you need to perform your job safely and effectively.
