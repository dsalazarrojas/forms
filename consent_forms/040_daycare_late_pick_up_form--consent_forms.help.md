## Daycare Late Pick Up Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to inform the daycare staff of a late pick-up for a child. It helps ensure that all parties involved are aware of the situation and can make necessary arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the pickup time on the first page.
2. Enter the contact information of the late pick-up person on the second page.
3. Specify the relationship of the late pick-up person (e.g., Child, Parent, Guardian, Staff) on the third page.
4. Enter the phone number of the late pick-up person on the fourth page.
5. Enter the address where the late pick-up person will be waiting on the fifth page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pickup Time (pickup_time)**: Select the time you will arrive to pick up your child from daycare. This will help us ensure that we are aware of your expected arrival time.
* **Late Pick-up Contact Information (pickup_contact)**: Please enter the name, email, and phone number of the person picking up your child. This information will be used to contact you in case of any issues.
* **Late Pick-up Person's Name (late_pickers_name)**: Enter the name of the person picking up your child.
* **Late Pick-up Person's Relationship (late_pickers_relationship)**: Specify the relationship of the late pick-up person (e.g., Child, Parent, Guardian, Staff). This will help us understand who will be picking up your child.
* **Late Pick-up Person's Phone (late_pickers_phone)**: Enter the phone number of the late pick-up person in case we need to contact them.
* **Pickup Address (pickup_address)**: Please enter the address where the late pick-up person will be waiting. This will help us to direct our staff to the correct location.

## Tips

- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately to ensure a smooth late pick-up process.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us and we will be happy to assist you.
