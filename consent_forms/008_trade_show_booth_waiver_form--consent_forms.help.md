# Trade Show Booth Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees or visitors who plan to visit trade show booths at a trade show event. It's intended to capture personal details, such as name, address, and contact information, as well as event-specific information like booth number and show dates. The form also includes waivers, event schedules, and staffing details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To begin, please fill out the form with your personal details, such as your first and last name, address, city, state, and zip code.
2. Next, provide your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
3. If you're attending an event, select the corresponding options for event type, booth type, and event days.
4. Specify your booth information, including booth number, location, and hours of operation.
5. Select the event date and show time of the event you'll be visiting.
6. Identify the event name and location where the trade show will take place.
7. Indicate the number of attendees or staff members who will be working at your booth.
8. If you need to list a contact person in case of an emergency, provide their name and contact details.
9. Finally, review the waivers and terms of service, and select any applicable options.
10. Once you've completed all the fields, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
- **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address.
- **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city where you reside.
- **State** (`state`, select_one, optional): Select the state you reside in.
- **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, number, optional): Enter your zip code.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Terms** (`terms`, select_multiple, optional): Select any applicable terms and conditions.
- **Waiver** (`waiver`, date, optional): Leave this field blank unless you're required to do so.
- **Booth Number** (`booth_number`, number, optional): Enter the number of your trade show booth.
- **Booth Location** (`booth_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of your booth.
- **Show Date** (`show_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event.
- **Show Time** (`show_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the event.
- **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event.
- **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the event.
- **Attendee** (`attendee`, select_multiple, optional): Select the attendees or staff members who will be working at your booth.
- **Booth Hours** (`booth_hours`, number, optional): Enter the hours of operation for your booth.
- **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of event.
- **Booth Type** (`booth_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of booth you're representing.
- **Event Days** (`event_days`, select_one, optional): Select the days of the event you'll be attending.
- **Event Hours** (`event_hours`, number, optional): Enter the hours of operation for the event.
- **Staff Count** (`staff_count`, number, optional): Enter the number of staff members working at your booth.
- **Vendor License** (`vendor_license`, text, optional): Enter any necessary vendor license or permits.
- **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact details of an emergency contact person.
- **Terms** (`terms`, select_multiple, optional): Review and select any applicable terms of service.
- **Waiver** (`waiver`, date, optional): Leave this field blank unless you're required to do so.
