# Therapy Session Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Therapy Session Permission Form is a document that requires clients to provide their consent for a therapy session. It's essential to fill out this form to ensure a smooth and safe experience for both the client and the therapist.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the Session Date: Choose the date of the therapy session from the provided calendar.
2. Select the Session Time: Choose the time of the therapy session from the dropdown options.
3. Client Consent: Select "I have read, understand, and agree to the session" to indicate your consent.
4. Therapist Name: Enter the name of the therapist conducting the session.
5. Session Length: Enter the expected length of the session (e.g., 45 minutes).
6. Client Relationships: Select any relationships that apply to you, such as Family Members, Friends, or Others.
7. Contact Information:
	* Contact Phone: Enter your primary phone number.
	* Contact Email: Enter your email address.
8. Emergency Contact Information:
	* Emergency Contact: Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
	* Emergency Phone: Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
	* Emergency Email: Enter the emergency contact's email address.
9. Medical Information:
	* Medical Conditions: List any medical conditions you have or are concerned about.
	* Allergies: List any allergies you have.
	* Medication: List any medications you're currently taking.
10. Insurance Information:
	* Insurance Provider: Enter your insurance provider's name.
	* Insurance Payer: Enter your insurance payer's name.
11. Emergency Contact Instructions: Provide any additional instructions for the emergency contact.
