# Contingency Fee Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document a contingency fee agreement between a client and an attorney or law firm. It outlines the terms and conditions of the agreement, including the contingency fee percentage, responsibility for legal expenses, and the right to accept or settle a case.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Client Full Name and Attorney or Law Firm Name fields with the accurate and complete contact information.
2. Provide a brief Description of the Legal Matter to give context to the agreement.
3. Enter the Contingency Fee Percentage, which is the percentage of the gross recovery to be paid to the attorney or law firm.
4. Select the Responsibility for Legal Expenses, which determines how court costs and filing fees will be handled.
5. Choose the Right to Accept Settlement, which gives the client the authority to accept or reject a settlement offer.
6. Sign and date the agreement with your digital signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Full Name** (`client_full_name`, text, required): This field requires the client's full name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Attorney or Law Firm Name** (`attorney_firm_name`, text, required): This field requires the attorney or law firm's name and contact information.
* **Description of Legal Matter** (`case_description`, text, required): This field should provide a brief description of the case, such as "Personal Injury" or "Contract Dispute".
* **Contingency Fee Percentage** (`contingency_percentage`, number, required): This field requires the percentage of the gross recovery to be paid to the attorney or law firm.
* **Responsibility for Legal Expenses** (`expense_responsibility`, select_one, required): This field determines how court costs and filing fees will be handled. Options include:
	+ Client pays expenses upfront
	+ Attorney advances expenses - Client reimburses from recovery
	+ Attorney pays all expenses (Inclusive in fee)
* **Right to Accept Settlement** (`settlement_authorization`, select_one, required): This field gives the client the authority to accept or reject a settlement offer. Options include:
	+ Client has final authority to settle
	+ Client grants attorney power of attorney to settle (not recommended)
* **I understand the terms of termination for this agreement** (`termination_clause`, select_one, required): This field requires the client to acknowledge understanding of the termination terms. Options include:
	+ I Understand
	+ Other
* **Date of Agreement** (`agreement_date`, date, required): This field requires the date the agreement was signed.
* **Client Digital Signature** (`client_digital_signature`, text, required): This field requires the client's digital signature.
* **Attorney Digital Signature** (`attorney_digital_signature`, text, required): This field requires the attorney's digital signature.

Please note that this form is a general template and may need to be adapted to the specific needs and circumstances of your case. If you have any questions or concerns, please consult with a qualified attorney or legal professional.
