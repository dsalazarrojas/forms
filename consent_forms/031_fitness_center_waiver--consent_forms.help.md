# Fitness Center Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Center Waiver is a form used to collect information from individuals visiting or participating in fitness center activities. It is designed to ensure that each person understands and accepts the terms of the fitness center's policies and procedures. Please read the following help guide to understand how to complete this form correctly and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
- To begin, carefully read the instructions and questions on each page of the form.
- Fill out the form with the required information. Note that all fields marked as "required" must be filled out.
- Review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official government-issued ID or passport.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official government-issued ID or passport.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached by the fitness center staff.
- **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
- **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select the date you are participating in the fitness center activities.
- **Time** (`time`, time, required): Select the time you are participating in the fitness center activities.
- **Waiver Agreement** (`fitness_center_waiver`, text, required): Please read and agree to the waiver of the fitness center. This includes acknowledging that you understand and accept the terms of the fitness center's policies and procedures.

## Tips
- Please fill out all required fields carefully and accurately.
- If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask the fitness center staff for assistance.
- Submit the form before proceeding with your fitness center activities.
