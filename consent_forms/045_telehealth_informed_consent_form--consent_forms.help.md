# Telehealth Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Telehealth Informed Consent Form is a comprehensive document that collects essential patient information, medical history, and emergency contact details. This form is required to ensure the delivery of safe and effective telehealth services.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Ensure you have all necessary information and documentation before starting.
2.  Complete each field accurately and truthfully.
3.  Enter the patient's date and time of birth correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the patient's first name as it is written on their identification documents.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the patient's last name as it is written on their identification documents.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter the patient's email address used for telehealth services. This will be used for communication and updates.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter the patient's date of birth as it is recorded on their identification documents.
*   **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, `time`, optional): Enter the patient's time of birth as it is recorded on their identification documents.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the patient's primary phone number used for contact.
*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `note`, optional): Describe any existing medical conditions, allergies, or previous treatments that may impact the patient's health.
*   **Emergency Contact First Name** (`emergency_contact_first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the first name of the patient's emergency contact.
*   **Last Name** (`emergency_contact_last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the last name of the emergency contact.
*   **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `select_one`, optional):
    *   Choose the relationship between the patient and the emergency contact (e.g., Mother, Father, Spouse, Friend, Other).
*   **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the emergency contact.
*   **Second Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_phone_number_2`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the second emergency contact.
*   **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm that the patient understands the telehealth consent terms and agrees to participate. Choose 'True' to indicate the patient's agreement.
*   **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): Enter the current date.
*   **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Enter the current time.
*   **Doctor Signature** (`doctor_signature`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that the healthcare provider agrees to treat the patient. Choose 'True' to indicate the healthcare provider's agreement.
*   **Medical Provider** (`medical_provider`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the healthcare provider or organization offering telehealth services.

## Tips

*   Always complete this form accurately and truthfully.
*   If unsure about any information, seek clarification from the patient or medical team.
*   This form is not a substitute for in-person consultations or medical evaluations.
*   Ensure you have all necessary patient documentation and medical records before using this form.
