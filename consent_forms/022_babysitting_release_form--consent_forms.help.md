# Babysitting Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be completed by parents or guardians to authorize babysitting services for their child(ren). It's a legal agreement that releases the babysitter from liability for any injuries or accidents that may occur during the session, except in cases of gross negligence.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please read the form carefully and answer all required fields truthfully.
2. Fill in your child's name(s) in the "Child(ren) Name(s)" field.
3. Enter your full name and address in the "Parent or Guardian Full Name" field.
4. Provide the babysitter's name in the "Designated Babysitter Name" field.
5. Select "I agree to the release of liability" for the "Liability Release Clause" field.
6. Choose "I authorize treatment" for the "Emergency Medical Treatment" field.
7. If applicable, list the names and phone numbers of persons authorized to pick up your child in the "Persons Authorized to Pick Up Child" field.
8. Decide on whether or not you grant permission for the sitter to take photos of your child for your benefit.
9. If you have any specific prohibited activities you'd like the sitter to be aware of, please list them in the "Specific Prohibited Activities" field.
10. Provide your emergency contact number in the "Emergency Contact Number for this Period" field.
11. Enter the date of the agreement in the "Date of Agreement" field.
12. Finally, select "I acknowledge that this digital form serves as my signature" for the "Digital Acknowledgment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent or Guardian Full Name (Parent Guardian Full Name)** (`text`, required): Enter your full name and address to identify yourself as the parent or guardian.
* **Child(ren) Name(s) (Child Name(s))** (`text`, required): Fill in your child's name(s) to identify them.
* **Designated Babysitter Name (Designated Babysitter Name)** (`text`, required): Provide the name of the babysitter you've chosen.
* **Liability Release Clause (Liability Release Clause)** (`select_one`, required): Select "I agree to the release of liability" to release the sitter from liability for accidents or injuries.
* **Emergency Medical Treatment (Emergency Medical Treatment)** (`select_one`, required): Choose "I authorize treatment" to grant permission for emergency medical treatment.
* **Persons Authorized to Pick Up Child (Other than Parents) (Persons Authorized to Pick Up Child)** (`text`, optional): List the names and phone numbers of people authorized to pick up your child, if applicable.
* **Permission to take photos (Permission to take photos)** (`select_one`, required): Decide whether or not to grant permission for the sitter to take photos of your child.
* **Specific Prohibited Activities (Specific Prohibited Activities)** (`text`, optional): List any specific activities you'd like the sitter to avoid or prohibit.
* **Emergency Contact Number for this Period (Emergency Contact Number for this Period)** (`text`, required): Provide your emergency contact number.
* **Date of Agreement (Date of Agreement)** (`date`, required): Enter the date of the agreement.
* **Digital Acknowledgment (Digital Acknowledgment)** (`select_one`, required): Select "I acknowledge that this digital form serves as my signature." to confirm your acknowledgment.
