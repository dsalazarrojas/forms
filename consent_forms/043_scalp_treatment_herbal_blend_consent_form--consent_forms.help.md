# Scalp Treatment Herbal Blend Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scalp Treatment Herbal Blend Consent Form is a form used to obtain consent from clients for scalp treatment herbal blends. It's intended to gather essential information about the client's medical history, potential allergies, and consent for scalp services. This form should only be filled out by clients who have been recommended for scalp treatment herbal blends by a healthcare professional.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have been recommended for scalp treatment herbal blends by a healthcare professional.
2.  Start by filling out the Client History field, providing your medical history.
3.  If applicable, list any known allergies or conditions that may impact your treatment in the Allergies field.
4.  Choose whether you consent to scalp services by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the Scalp Services field.
5.  Sign your name in the Signature field, as your acknowledgment of the consent form.
6.  Date the form by filling in the Date field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Client History** (`client_history`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any relevant medical history that may impact your scalp treatment herbal blend.
    *   Enter any relevant medical history, such as pre-existing medical conditions, previous surgeries, or allergies.
*   **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, optional): This field is where you can list any known allergies or conditions that may impact your treatment.
    *   Select any known allergies or conditions that may impact your treatment by choosing "Yes" or "No" for each option.
*   **Scalp Services** (`scalp_services`, select_one, optional): This field is where you can indicate whether you consent to scalp services.
    *   Choose "Yes" if you consent to scalp services, or "No" if you do not.
*   **Signature** (`signature`, note, optional): This field is where you sign your name as acknowledgment of the consent form.
    *   Sign your name to confirm your consent.
*   **Date** (`date`, date, optional): This field is where you date the form.
    *   Enter the date you sign the form.

## Tips
*   Make sure to sign the form after completing all required fields.
*   Ensure you have been recommended by a healthcare professional before filling out this form.
*   Keep the Client History field clear and concise, avoiding unnecessary information.
