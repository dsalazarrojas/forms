# Animal Rescue Transport Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from volunteers who are willing to transport animals during rescue operations. The collected data will help us in coordinating transportations, ensuring animal safety, and providing necessary documentation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide accurate and truthful information for each field. If you're unsure about any field, do not hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or contact person for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Transporter Full Name (1)
*   **Transporter Full Name** (`transporter_name`, Text, Required): Your legal name as a volunteer transporter.

### Contact Email (2)
*   **Contact Email** (`transporter_email`, Email, Required): Your email address for contact purposes.

### Contact Phone (3)
*   **Contact Phone** (`transporter_phone`, Text, Required): Your mobile phone number for emergency contact.

### Date of Birth (4)
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, Required): Your date of birth to ensure you're 18 or older.

### Driver License Number (5)
*   **Driver License Number** (`driver_license_number`, Text, Required): Your valid driver's license number for transportations.

### License State (6)
*   **License State** (`license_state`, Text, Required): State where your driver's license was issued.

### Vehicle Type (7)
*   **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, Select One, Required): The type of vehicle you will be using for transportation (e.g., Car, SUV, Van, etc.).

### Animal Transport Capacity (8)
*   **Animal Transport Capacity** (`vehicle_capacity`, Select One, Required): How many animals you can transport at a time (e.g., 1-2 small animals, 3-5 small animals, etc.).

### Transport Date (9)
*   **Transport Date** (`transport_date`, Date, Required): The scheduled date for transport.

### Pickup Location (10)
*   **Pickup Location** (`pickup_location`, Text, Required): The address or facility where you will pick up the animals.

### Drop-off Location (11)
*   **Drop-off Location** (`dropoff_location`, Text, Required): The destination address or facility where you will drop off the animals.

### Estimated Distance (12)
*   **Estimated Distance** (`estimated_distance`, Number, Not Required): Approximate distance for transport.

### Animal Details (13)
*   **Animal Details** (`animal_details`, Text, Required): Species, breed, and names of the animals being transported.

### Special Needs or Requirements (14)
*   **Special Needs or Requirements** (`special_needs`, Text, Not Required): Any medical, behavioral, or other special needs of the animals.

### Emergency Contact Name (15)
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, Text, Required): The name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.

### Emergency Contact Phone (16)
*   **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, Text, Required): The contact phone number of the emergency contact.

### I Understand Transport Responsibilities (17)
*   **I Understand Transport Responsibilities** (`consent_statement`, Select One, Required): Acknowledge that you understand your transport duties and safety protocols.

### Safety Agreement (18)
*   **Safety Agreement** (`safety_agreement`, Select One, Required): Agree to follow all safety protocols.

### Vehicle Insurance Confirmation (19)
*   **Vehicle Insurance Confirmation** (`insurance_confirmation`, Select One, Required): Confirm that you have valid auto insurance.

### Background Check Status (20)
*   **Background Check Status** (`background_check`, Select One, Required): Status of your background check (Completed, In Progress, Not Yet Started).

### Previous Transport Experience (21)
*   **Previous Transport Experience** (`previous_experience`, Text, Not Required): Describe any previous transport experience.

### General Availability for Future Transports (22)
*   **General Availability for Future Transports** (`availability`, Select Multiple, Not Required): Select all available times for future transports.

### Will You Request Mileage Reimbursement (23)
*   **Will You Request Mileage Reimbursement** (`reimbursement_request`, Select One, Required): Whether you will request mileage reimbursement for your services.

### Electronic Signature (24)
*   **Electronic Signature** (`signature`, Text, Required): Type your full name for the electronic signature.

### Date of Consent (25)
*   **Date of Consent** (`consent_date`, Date, Required): The date you're giving consent to transport animals.

Note: This guide is designed to be a helpful tool for users to understand the form and its purpose.
