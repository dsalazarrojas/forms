# Social Assistance Program Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Assistance Program Consent Form is designed to collect demographic and information data from participants in a social assistance program. This form is meant to ensure that participants understand the program's terms and conditions, as well as their rights and responsibilities as part of the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your participant details in the "Participant Details" field.
2. Enter your participant information in the "Participants Info" field.
3. Sign the consent statement in the "Consent" field.
4. Confirm your recording consent in the "Recording Consent" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details**: Enter your name, email, and any other relevant details about yourself as a participant. This field is not required but is optional.
* **Participants Info**: Enter any information about the participants you are representing, such as their names, contact information, or any other relevant details. This field is also not required but is optional.
* **Consent**: Sign this field to indicate that you understand the program's terms and conditions, and that you grant permission for your participation. This field is required for participation.
* **Recording Consent**: Confirm that you grant permission for your recording (audio, video, or image) to be taken during the program or event. This field is required for participation. 

## Tips
* Please read and understand the terms and conditions of the social assistance program before filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the program administrators.
* Ensure you have the necessary permissions and consents from all participants before proceeding with recording or data collection.
