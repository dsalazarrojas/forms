# University Research Study Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Research Study Release Form is a document used to capture essential details about a research study, including participant information and researcher contact details. This form serves as a record of the study's progress and status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the participant information fields: First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, and Participant Agreement.
2. Next, provide the research study details: Research Study Title, Research Study Description, Research Study Start Date, Research Study End Date, and Research Study Funding Source.
3. Enter the researcher's contact information: University Researcher's Name, University Researcher's Email, University Department, and Researcher Institution.
4. Complete the participant's consent details: Participant Consent Status, Participant Consent Date.
5. Add any additional study information as needed, such as Research Study Completion Date and Institution.
6. Review the form carefully and ensure that all required fields (indicated by a red asterisk *) are completed accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First Name** (First Name, text, *): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (Last Name, text, *): Enter your last name.
- **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, *): Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
- **Research Study Title** (Research Study Title, text, *): Enter the title of the research study.
- **Research Study Description** (Research Study Description, text, *): Enter a brief description of the research study.
- **Research Study Start Date** (Research Study Start Date, date, *): Enter the start date of the research study in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
- **Research Study End Date** (Research Study End Date, date, *): Enter the end date of the research study in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
- **Participant Agreement** (Participant Agreement, text, *): Confirm that you have read and understand the research study's terms and conditions.
- **University Researcher's Name** (University Researcher's Name, text, *): Enter the name of the researcher associated with this study.
- **University Researcher's Email** (University Researcher's Email, email, *): Enter the email address of the researcher.
- **University Department** (University Department, text, *): Enter the department of the researcher's institution.
- **Contact Person's Name** (Contact Person's Name, text, *): Enter the name of the person you contact for further information.
- **Contact Person's Email** (Contact Person's Email, email, *): Enter the email address of the contact person.
- **University Research Study ID** (University Research Study ID, text, ): Enter the university's research study ID.
- **Research Study Funding Source** (Research Study Funding Source, text, ): Enter the funding source for the research study.
- **Participant Signature** (Participant Signature, note, ): Sign this form to indicate that you have reviewed and agree to the study's terms and conditions.
- **University Researcher's Signature** (University Researcher's Signature, note, ): Sign this form to confirm that you are the researcher associated with this study.
- **Research Study Completion Date** (Research Study Completion Date, date, ): Enter the date the research study was completed.
- **Participant Consent Status** (Participant Consent Status, select_one, *): Choose the status of your participant consent: Active, Inactive, or Pending.
- **Research Study Status** (Research Study Status, select_one, *): Choose the status of the research study: Active, Inactive, or Completed.
- **Participant Consent Date** (Participant Consent Date, date, ): Enter the date of your participant consent.
- **University Researcher's Institution** (University Researcher's Institution, text, ): Enter the name of the researcher's institution.
- **University Researcher's Title** (University Researcher's Title, text, ): Enter the title of the researcher.
- **Research Study Institution** (Research Study Institution, text, ): Enter the name of the institution associated with this research study.
- **Participant Institution** (Participant Institution, text, ): Enter the name of the institution associated with this participant.
- **Participant Consent Status** (Participant Consent Status, select_one, *): Choose the status of your participant consent: Active, Inactive, or Pending.
- **Research Study Completion Date** (Research Study Completion Date, date, ): Enter the date the research study was completed.
- **Participant Institution** (Participant Institution, text, ): Enter the name of the institution associated with this participant.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately to ensure the form is valid.
* Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
