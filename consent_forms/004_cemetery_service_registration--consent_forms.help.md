# Cemetery Service Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cemetery Service Registration form is a crucial document used to gather essential information from registrants to facilitate a smooth and respectful service for the deceased. This form ensures that all necessary details are collected to provide an appropriate and dignified service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name, email, and phone number to ensure the cemetery team can contact you for any updates or confirmation.
2. Select your relationship to the deceased (e.g., spouse, child, friend).
3. Enter the deceased's full name, date of birth, and date of death.
4. Choose the type of service you prefer (e.g., graveside committal, memorial service, celebration of life).
5. Select a preferred service date and time.
6. Indicate the approximate number of attendees you expect.
7. Identify any special requirements for the service (e.g., wheelchair accessibility, audio system).
8. Confirm your consent for the service and liability terms.
9. Select your preferred payment method.
10. Add any additional requests or comments for the service.
11. Sign the form with your full legal name and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required): Enter the date of service registration.
* **Registrant Name** (`registrant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as registered for the service.
* **Registrant Email** (`registrant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Registrant Phone** (`registrant_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Relationship to Deceased** (`relationship_to_deceased`, select_one, required): Select your relationship to the deceased (e.g., spouse, child).
* **Deceased Full Name** (`deceased_full_name`, text, required): Enter the deceased's full name.
* **Deceased Date of Birth** (`deceased_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the deceased's date of birth.
* **Deceased Date of Death** (`deceased_date_of_death`, date, required): Enter the deceased's date of death.
* **Burial Location** (`burial_location`, text, required): Enter the cemetery section and plot number.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of service you prefer (e.g., graveside committal, memorial service).
* **Preferred Service Date** (`preferred_service_date`, date, required): Enter your preferred service date.
* **Preferred Service Time** (`preferred_service_time`, time, required): Enter your preferred service time.
* **Alternative Date** (`alternative_date`, date, optional): Enter an alternative date if the first preferred date is unavailable.
* **Expected Attendees** (`expected_attendees`, number, required): Enter the approximate number of attendees you expect.
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the special requirements you need for the service (e.g., wheelchair accessibility, audio system).
* **Consent for Service** (`consent_for_service`, select_one, required): Confirm your consent for the service.
* **Liability Acknowledgment** (`liability_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Acknowledge the cemetery's liability terms.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select your preferred payment method.
* **Additional Requests or Comments** (`additional_requests`, text, optional): Enter any special instructions or additional information.
* **Signature Name** (`signature_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as signature.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of signing.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Double-check your relationship to the deceased and the deceased's details for accuracy.
* Select a preferred service date and time that suits you and the deceased's family.
* Be clear and specific when adding special requirements or comments.
* Sign the form with your full name and date to confirm your commitment.
