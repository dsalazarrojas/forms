# Ferpa Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Ferpa Release Form is a document used to formally release a Ferpa (File-Upload Protocol Agreement) file. This form is used to acknowledge that a user has reviewed and agreed to the terms and conditions of the Ferpa agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Date of Release" field with the date you are releasing the Ferpa file.
2. In the "Sign" field, select "Yes" if you agree to the terms and conditions of the Ferpa agreement, or select "No" if you do not agree. If you select "No", please provide an explanation in the "Comments" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. (Optional) Attach any additional files as needed using the "File Upload" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date of Release**: The date on which you are releasing the Ferpa file.
* **Sign**: Indicate your agreement to the terms and conditions of the Ferpa agreement.
	+ Yes: You agree to the terms and conditions of the Ferpa agreement.
	+ No: You do not agree to the terms and conditions of the Ferpa agreement. Please provide an explanation in the "Comments" field.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: (Not applicable for this form)
* **Record Number**: (Not applicable for this form)
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or explanations for your response in the "Sign" field.
* **File Upload**: (Not applicable for this form)

Note: The remaining "File Upload" fields are not applicable for this form and can be ignored.
