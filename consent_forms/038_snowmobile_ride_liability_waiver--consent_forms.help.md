# Snowmobile Ride Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Snowmobile Ride Liability Waiver is a crucial document that helps protect all parties involved in snowmobile ride activities. It aims to ensure that participants understand the risks associated with the activity and grant permission for their emergency contact to be notified in case of an incident.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2.  Provide accurate and truthful information for each field.
3.  Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the waiver.
4.  Sign and date the waiver to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Name\*** (2): Your full name, as it appears on your government-issued ID.
*   **Contact Details\*** (3): Share your current contact information so we can reach you.
*   **Emergency Contact Details (4)**: Provide the name, phone, and relation of your emergency contact person.
*   **Emergency Contact Consent\*** (7): Confirm if you want to grant permission for your emergency contact to be notified.
*   **Participant Signature\*** (8): Sign your name to confirm your agreement and consent.
*   **Guardian Signature (If Applicable)** (9): If you are a minor, your parent or guardian must sign this section.
*   **Guardian Consent (10)**: Confirm if you are the legal guardian.
*   **Waiver Terms and Conditions (11)**: Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the waiver.
*   **Participant Agreement (12)**: Acknowledge your understanding of the risks and terms of the activity.
*   **Signature Date (13)**: Date your signature to confirm the date you agreed to the waiver.
*   **Signature Time (14)**: Record the time of your signature.

Note: Fields marked with an asterisk (\*) are required fields.
