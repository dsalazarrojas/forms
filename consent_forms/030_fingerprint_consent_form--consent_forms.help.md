# Fingerprint Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fingerprint Consent Form is a document used to obtain consent from individuals to collect their fingerprint data. This form helps ensure that individuals understand and agree to the use of their fingerprint data for a specific purpose.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select one of the options in the "Are you willing to provide a fingerprint scan?" question.
2.  If you are not willing to provide a fingerprint scan, provide a reason for your refusal in the "Please provide reason for not providing fingerprint scan" field.
3.  Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birthday" field in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Are you willing to provide a fingerprint scan? (Select One)**: Choose "I am willing" to indicate your consent to provide a fingerprint scan or "I am not willing" to indicate your refusal. This question is not required to be answered, but it is recommended to provide clear consent.
* **Please provide reason for not providing fingerprint scan (Text)**: If you selected "I am not willing" in the previous question, provide a clear and concise reason for your refusal in this field.
* **Date of Birthday (Date)**: Enter your date of birth in the format "MM/DD/YYYY" to ensure accurate identification.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer truthfully and accurately.
* Provide a clear reason if you are not willing to provide a fingerprint scan.
* Ensure your date of birth is entered correctly to prevent errors.
