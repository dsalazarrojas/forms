# Hair Salon New Client Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from new clients to be treated in a hair salon. It is a necessary document that requires completion for all new clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Client Name** in the text field provided.
2. Choose your **Contact Method** from the dropdown menu. This will determine how you want to be contacted after your treatment.
3. Enter your **Address** in the text field if required by the business policy or regulation.
4. Enter your **Email** in the format of `example@example.com` if required by the business policy or regulation.
5. Enter your **Phone** number if required by the business policy or regulation.
6. If required, enter the **Emergency Contact Name** and **Emergency Contact Relationship** for the person to contact in case of an emergency.
7. Answer **Do you give consent to treatment?** with either **Yes** or **No**. This is a required field.
8. Answer **Do you give consent to photo/video recording?** with either **Yes** or **No**. This is a required field.
9. Answer **Do you give consent to contact?** with either **Yes** or **No**. This is a required field.
10. Answer **Do you give consent to share with others?** with either **Yes** or **No**. This is a required field.
11. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the format of `YYYY-MM-DD` if required by the business policy or regulation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Your name that the hair salon will use to address you.
* **Contact Method**: The way you want to be contacted after your treatment. You can choose between **Yes** or **No**. 
* **Address**: This is your address where you can be contacted or visited. If it's not required, you can leave it blank.
* **Email**: This is the email address where the salon can contact you. Use the format `example@example.com` if required.
* **Phone**: This is your phone number where the salon can contact you. Enter your 10-digit number.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: This is the name of the person you want the salon to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: This is the relationship of the emergency contact, e.g., parent, sibling, or friend.
* **Do you give consent to treatment?**: Answer with **Yes** if you agree to be treated. Answer with **No** if you don't.
* **Do you give consent to photo/video recording?**: Answer with **Yes** if you agree to be photographed or recorded. Answer with **No** if you don't.
* **Do you give consent to contact?**: Answer with **Yes** if you agree to be contacted by the salon. Answer with **No** if you don't.
* **Do you give consent to share with others?**: Answer with **Yes** if you agree to share your information with other parties. Answer with **No** if you don't.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`. This is a required field if needed by the business policy or regulation.
