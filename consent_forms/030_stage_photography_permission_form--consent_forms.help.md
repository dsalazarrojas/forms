# Stage Photography Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information and consent from participants and parents for the purpose of stage photography sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand what is required.
2. Complete the form with the required information.
3. Submit the completed form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Participant Information** (<participant_info>, text, required: false)
Please enter the participant's name, age, and any other relevant details.

### 2. **Contact Info** (<contact_info>, number, required: false)
Enter the contact information of the participant, such as a phone number or email address. This will help us get in touch with you if needed.

### 3. **Email Address** (<email_address>, email, required: false)
Please enter a valid email address for the participant or their parent/guardian.

### 4. **Phone Number** (<phone_number>, text, required: false)
Enter the phone number of the participant or their parent/guardian. This will help us get in touch with you if needed.

### 5. **Parent Signature** (<parent_signature>, note, required: false)
Check the box to confirm that the parent or guardian of the participant has given their consent for the photo session.

### 6. **Participant Signature** (<participant_signature>, note, required: false)
Check the box to confirm that the participant has given their consent for the photo session.

### 7. **Photographer's Name** (<photographer_name>, text, required: false)
Enter the name of the photographer who will be capturing the photos.

### 8. **Photo Description** (<photo_description>, text, required: false)
Provide a brief description of the photo shoot, including the event or activity being captured.

### 9. **Event Name** (<event_name>, text, required: false)
Enter the name of the event or activity being photographed.

### 10. **Date Taken** (<date_taken>, date, required: false)
Enter the date on which the photos were taken.

### 11. **Photo Usage** (<photo_usage>, select_multiple, required: false)
Select all the intended uses of the photos, such as School Use, Theater Use, or Studios Use.

### 12. **Parent Notes** (<parent_note>, text, required: false)
Enter any additional comments or notes from the parent or guardian.
