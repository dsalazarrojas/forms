# Diploma Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Diploma Verification Form is a document that allows institutions, organizations, or individuals to confirm the authenticity of a person's academic degree or qualification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the "User Details" section.
2. Enter the name of the institution that issued your degree or certification.
3. Provide the address of the institution.
4. Enter the title of your degree or certification.
5. Fill in the start and end dates of your degree or certification.
6. If applicable, upload a verification document, such as a transcript or diploma.
7. Write any additional comments or notes about your degree or certification.
8. Select the status of your degree or certification as "Verified", "Not Verified", or "Pending".
9. Choose the verification date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details** (`user_details`, `text`, not required): This is where you provide your personal details, such as your name and contact information.
* **Institution Name** (`institution_name`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the institution that issued your degree or certification.
* **Institution Address** (`institution_address`, `text`, not required): Enter the address of the institution that issued your degree or certification.
* **Degree Title** (`degree_title`, `text`, not required): Enter the title of your degree or certification, such as Bachelor of Science or Master of Arts.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the start date of your degree or certification.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the end date of your degree or certification.
* **Verified By** (`verified_by`, `text`, not required): If applicable, enter the name of the person who verified your degree or certification.
* **Verification Document** (`verification_document`, `text`, not required): If applicable, upload a verification document, such as a transcript or diploma.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, not required): Write any additional comments or notes about your degree or certification.
* **Verified** (`verified`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select the status of your degree or certification as "Verified", "Not Verified", or "Pending".
* **Verification Date** (`verification_date`, `date`, not required): Choose the date of verification.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate verification.
* If you are uploading a verification document, ensure it is in a format that can be easily read and verified.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
