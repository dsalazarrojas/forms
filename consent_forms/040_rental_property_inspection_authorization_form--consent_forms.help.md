# rental_property_inspection_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from renters (tenants) to authorize inspections of their rental properties. It helps property managers or owners to schedule inspections and communicate with tenants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the tenant's information in the "Tenant Information" field.
2.  Provide the inspector's information in the "Inspector Information" field.
3.  Select the date of the inspection in the "Inspection Date" field.
4.  Choose the time of the inspection in the "Inspection Time" field.
5.  Optionally, add any additional inspection details in the "Inspection Details" field.
6.  Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
7.  Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
8.  Select your preferred contact method from the "Preferred Contact Method" options.
9.  Choose the authorized access days of the week from the "Authorized Access Dates" options.
10.  Select the authorized access time slots from the "Authorized Access Times" options.
11.  Choose the authorized access frequency from the "Authorized Access Frequency" options.
12.  Mark whether an inspector is authorized to access the property as per the "Authorized Access Inspector" options.
12.  You can add any comments in the "Comments" field if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant Information** (`text`, required): Enter the name and contact information of the tenant.
* **Inspector Information** (`text`, required): Provide the name and contact information of the inspector.
* **Inspection Date** (`date`, required): Select the date of the scheduled inspection.
* **Inspection Time** (`time`, required): Choose the time slot for the inspection.
* **Inspection Details** (`text`, optional): Add any additional details about the inspection.
* **Contact Number** (`text`, required): Enter your contact number for communication.
* **Email Address** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`select_multiple`, required): Select the method you prefer to be contacted (Phone, Email, In-person).
* **Authorized Access Dates** (`select_multiple`, required): Choose the days of the week the property can be accessed.
* **Authorized Access Times** (`select_multiple`, required): Select the time slots when the property can be accessed.
* **Authorized Access Frequency** (`select_multiple`, required): Choose how often the property can be accessed (Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **Authorized Access Inspector** (`select_multiple`, required): Mark whether an inspector is authorized to access the property.
* **Comments** (`text`, optional): Add any additional comments or information.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
- Ensure you've selected the correct authorized access dates, times, and frequency.
