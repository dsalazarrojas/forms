# Psa Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Psa Form is a form designed to collect information about a child's parents, their contact details, and any additional parents or contact information. It is typically used for administrative or legal purposes, such as assessing the child's needs or gathering consent.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide the child's **Parents Name** in the text field.
2. Enter the **Child Name** in the text field.
3. Enter the child's **Date of Birth** in the date field.
4. Select the current **Assessment Level** of the child.
5. If applicable, list the child's **Medical Conditions** in the text field.
6. Check the box to confirm the **Terms and Conditions** (Yes/No).
7. Check the box to confirm the **Terms and Conditions** (Yes/No) for any additional parents.
8. Enter the **Parent Contact Info** for the child's parent(s) in the text field.
9. Specify the number of **Additional Parents** (0-10) for any additional parents.
10. Enter the **Additional Contact Info** for any additional parents in the text field.
11. Check the box to confirm the **Additional Terms and Conditions** (Yes/No) for any additional parents.
12. Check the box to confirm the **Additional Submit** (Yes/No) for any additional parents.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parents Name**: Enter the name of the child's parent(s) in this text field.
* **Child Name**: Enter the name of the child in this text field.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the child's date of birth in this date field (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Current Assessment Level**: Select the current assessment level of the child (e.g. 'Low', 'Medium', 'High').
* **Medical Conditions**: If the child has any medical conditions, list them in this text field.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Check the box to confirm you have read and agree to the terms and conditions (Yes/No).
* **Additional Parents**: Specify the number of additional parents involved (0-10).
* **Parent Contact Info**: Enter the contact information for the child's parent(s) in this text field.
* **Additional Contact Info**: Enter the contact information for any additional parents in this text field.
* **Media Declaration**: This is a field for any additional comments or information about the media used.
* **Parent Contact Info**: Enter the contact information for the additional parent(s) in this text field.
* **Additional Terms and Conditions**: Check the box to confirm you have read and agree to the terms and conditions for any additional parents (Yes/No).
* **Additional Submit**: Check the box to submit the information for any additional parents (Yes/No).
