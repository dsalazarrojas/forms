# Animal Acceptance Policy Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from pet owners to ensure the animal's acceptance and compliance with certain policies, including vaccination status, liability, and restraint. It's essential to complete this form to demonstrate your responsibility and commitment to following our animal acceptance guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out your pet's owner's full name in the **Pet Owner Full Name** field.
2. Enter your pet's name in the **Pet Name** field.
3. Select the type of animal you are bringing into the premises in the **Type of Animal** field.
4. Confirm that your pet is up to date on all core vaccinations in the **Animals must be up to date on all core vaccinations.** field.
5. Check that your pet is currently up to date on all vaccinations by selecting **Yes - Up to date** in the **Is your pet currently up to date on all vaccinations?** field.
6. Agree that you are financially responsible for any damage or injury caused by your pet in the **Do you agree to the liability terms?** field.
7. Confirm that you will keep your pet on a leash or in a carrier at all times in common areas by selecting **I Agree** in the **Do you acknowledge the restraint policy?** field.
8. Select **I Consent to all Terms** in the **Final Consent to Animal Acceptance Policy** field to confirm that you have read and understand the policy.
9. Enter the date of your consent in the **Date of Consent** field using the format **MM/DD/YYYY**.
10. Sign your full name in the **Digital Signature** field to finalize your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Owner Full Name**: Please enter your full name as the pet's owner.
* **Pet Name**: Enter your pet's name.
* **Type of Animal**: Select the type of animal you are bringing into the premises, such as Dog, Cat, Bird, etc.
* **Animals must be up to date on all core vaccinations.**: Confirm that your pet is up to date on all core vaccinations.
* **Is your pet currently up to date on all vaccinations?**: Select Yes - Up to date if your pet is up to date, or No - Needs update if your pet needs a vaccination.
* **Do you agree to the liability terms?**: Confirm that you are financially responsible for any damage or injury caused by your pet.
* **Do you acknowledge the restraint policy?**: Confirm that you will keep your pet on a leash or in a carrier at all times in common areas.
* **Final Consent to Animal Acceptance Policy**: Select I Consent to all Terms to confirm that you have read and understand the policy.
* **Date of Consent**: Enter the date of your consent in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Digital Signature**: Sign your full name to finalize your consent.
