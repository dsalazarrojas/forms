# Summer Camp Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for parents or guardians to provide their child's demographic information, contact details, and emergency contact information. It helps us gather crucial data to ensure your child's safe and enjoyable experience at the summer camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Fill in all required fields**: Make sure to complete all marked required fields. These are marked with an asterisk (\*) and are crucial for us to process your child's application.
2. **Provide accurate and up-to-date information**: Double-check the accuracy of your contact details and emergency information to avoid any issues during the camp.
3. **Choose 'yes' or 'no' for Parent Permission**: Decide whether you grant permission for your child to participate in the camp. If you're unable to grant permission, please explain the reasons why.
4. **Enter relevant medical conditions or medications**: If your child has any medical conditions or allergies, please list them in the provided fields.
5. **Sign and date the form**: Finally, sign and date the form to confirm your consent.
