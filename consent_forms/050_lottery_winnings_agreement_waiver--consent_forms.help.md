# Lottery Winnings Agreement Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lottery Winnings Agreement Waiver form is used to obtain a participant's agreement and consent on the terms of sharing lottery winnings. This form ensures that the participant is aware of the terms and conditions of the lottery winnings and has agreed to share their winnings accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the participant's information accurately, including their name, email, and contact number.
2. Enter the total winnings amount.
3. Select the percentage of winnings to be shared (50%, 75%, or 100%).
4. Sign and date the form.
5. Confirm the date and time of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lottery Winnings**: Enter the participant's lottery winnings amount.
* **Participant Name**: Enter the participant's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Participant Email**: Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Contact Number**: Enter the participant's contact number.
* **Share Winnings**: Choose the percentage of winnings to be shared (50%, 75%, or 100%).
* **Signature**: Sign the form to confirm agreement.
* **Date**: Enter the date of submission.
* **Time**: Enter the time of submission.
* **Confirm Date**: Enter the date and time of the submission confirmation.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the participant's information carefully before submitting the form.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
* Double-check the percentage of winnings selected to reflect the correct share of winnings.
* Sign and date the form before submitting it.
