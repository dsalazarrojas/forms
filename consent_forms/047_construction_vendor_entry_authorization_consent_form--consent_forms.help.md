# Construction Vendor Entry Authorization Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information from construction vendors before they enter a construction site. The form is used to obtain their authorization and ensure they understand the site's rules and regulations, liability terms, and confidentiality agreements. By completing this form, vendors agree to follow all site rules, take liability, and keep information confidential.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all required fields, marked with a red star (\*).
2. Select the correct options for each field.
3. Type your full name as the signature for the "Vendor Signature" and "Authorization Signature" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Authorization Date** (1): Date of this request.
	+ Enter the date you are requesting entry to the site.
* **Vendor Name** (2): Full name of visitor.
	+ Type the full name of the vendor or individual entering the site.
* **Vendor Company** (3): Company name.
	+ Type the name of the vendor's company.
* **Vendor Address** (4): Business address.
	+ Type the address of the vendor's business or location.
* **Vendor Email** (5): Email address.
	+ Type the email address of the vendor or individual.
* **Vendor Phone** (6): Contact phone number.
	+ Type the phone number of the vendor or individual.
* **Vendor License Number** (7): Contractor or business license number.
	+ Type the number of the vendor's license or certification.
* **Insurance Policy Number** (8): Liability insurance policy number.
	+ Type the number of the vendor's liability insurance policy.
* **Purpose of Visit** (9): Why are you visiting.
	+ Select the purpose of your visit from the options provided.
* **Purpose Details** (10): Describe in more detail.
	+ Type a brief description of the purpose of your visit.
* **Project Name** (11): Which project.
	+ Type the name of the project you are visiting.
* **Project Address** (12): Site to visit.
	+ Type the address of the project site.
* **Requested Entry Date** (13): When do you need access.
	+ Type the date you need to enter the site.
* **Requested Entry Time** (14): What time.
	+ Type the time you need to enter the site.
* **Expected Duration** (15): How long will you stay.
	+ Select the duration of your visit from the options provided.
* **Vehicle Information** (16): Make, model, color, plate.
	+ Type any vehicle information, such as make, model, and plate number.
* **Equipment Being Brought** (17): Tools or materials.
	+ Type any equipment or materials you will be bringing to the site.
* **Site Contact Name** (18): Who are you meeting.
	+ Type the name of the person you are meeting at the site.
* **Site Contact Phone** (19): Contact number.
	+ Type the phone number of the contact person.
* **Safety Orientation Completed** (20): Have you completed site orientation.
	+ Select whether you have completed site orientation.
* **PPE Requirements Understood** (21): Do you understand PPE rules.
	+ Select whether you understand the site's PPE rules.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (22): Person to contact.
	+ Type the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (23): Emergency number.
	+ Type the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Relevant Medical Conditions** (24): Any conditions we should know.
	+ Type any medical conditions that may affect your ability to work on the site.
* **Site Rules Acknowledged** (25): Do you agree to follow all rules.
	+ Select whether you agree to follow all site rules.
* **Liability Waiver** (26): Do you accept liability terms.
	+ Select whether you accept the site's liability terms.
* **Confidentiality Agreement** (27): Do you agree to keep information confidential.
	+ Select whether you agree to keep the site's information confidential.
* **Background Check Consent** (28): Do you authorize background verification.
	+ Select whether you authorize background verification.
* **Vendor Signature** (29): Type full name as signature.
	+ Type your full name as a signature.
* **Authorized By** (30): Site authorization name.
	+ Type the name of the person authorizing you to enter the site.
* **Authorization Signature** (31): Type full name as signature.
	+ Type the full name of the person authorizing you to enter the site as a signature.
