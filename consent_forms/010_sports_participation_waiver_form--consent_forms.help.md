## sports_participation_waiver_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sports participation waiver form is a document that requires participants to confirm their understanding and consent to engage in sports activities. It's a critical part of ensuring participant safety and liability protection.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions honestly and accurately. 
2. Select "I understand and consent" in the "Consent Form" field, indicating your understanding and acceptance of the risks and responsibilities associated with the activity.
3. If you are under 18 years old, please ensure a parent or guardian signs below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, select_multiple, required/optional): 
This field requires you to indicate whether you have any pre-existing medical conditions, injuries, or other factors that may impact your ability to participate in the activity.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required/optional): 
Please provide the name and contact information for your emergency contact, so we can reach out in case of an emergency.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required/optional): 
Describe any medical conditions, injuries, or health concerns that you have or have had in the past.
* **Consent Form** (`consent_form`, select_one, required): 
Select "I understand and consent" to confirm your understanding of the risks and responsibilities associated with the activity.
* **Participant Agreement** (`participant_agreement`, text, required/optional): 
This is a space for you to express any questions or concerns you have about participating in the activity.

## Tips
- Please ensure you answer all questions accurately and honestly.
- If you have any concerns or questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
- We're here to help and support you throughout your participation in the activity.
