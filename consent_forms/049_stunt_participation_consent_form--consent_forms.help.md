# Stunt Participation Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Stunt Participation Consent Form is a critical document that requires participants to provide necessary information about themselves and their stunt experience. This form ensures that participants are aware of and agree to the terms and conditions of the stunt participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your Participant Information (Page 1).
2. Share your Stunt Information (Page 2).
3. Select your Stunt Participant Information (Page 3).
4. Enter your Submission details (Page 4).
5. Read and agree to the terms and conditions (Page 5).
6. Add any Additional Information or comments about your participation (Page 6).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Participant Information
* **Participant Information** (`Participant Information`, `text`, required): Please fill in your name, email, and any other relevant contact information.

### Page 2: Stunt Information
* **Stunt Information** (`Stunt Information`, `text`, required): Share information about your stunt experience and any relevant details.

### Page 3: Stunt Participant Information
* **Stunt Participant Information** (`Participant Experience`, `Stunt Level`, `Interests`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply from the available options to describe your stunt experience, skill level, and interests.

### Page 4: Submission
* **Submission** (`Submission`, `number`, required): Enter the number of submissions you plan to make for this stunt.

### Page 5: Agreement
* **Agreement** (`Agreement`, `text`, required): Please read and agree to the terms and conditions by typing 'Yes' in the box.

### Page 6: Additional Information
* **Additional Information** (`Additional Information`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, please provide them here.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Review your submission details carefully before proceeding.
* If you have any doubts or questions, contact the stunt administrators for assistance.
* Ensure you understand and agree to the terms and conditions before proceeding.
