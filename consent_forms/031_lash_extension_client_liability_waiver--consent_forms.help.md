# Lash Extension Client Liability Waiver - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information and consent from clients for lash extension services. It includes fields for client contact and medical history, lash extension services, and consent to ensure a safe and successful experience for both the client and the technician.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form by providing the required information for each field.
2. Ensure you understand the purpose and implications of each field before proceeding.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please consult a relevant authority or a trusted resource for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Client Address** (`client_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your current address, including street number and city.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YY.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required: false): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Urgent Care** (`emergency_contact_urgent_care`, `select_one`, required: false): Select "Urgent Care" if you have an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Urgent Care Other** (`emergency_contact_urgent_care_other`, `text`, required: false): If you selected "Urgent Care" above, enter the name and contact information of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Urgent Care Other Description** (`emergency_contact_urgent_care_other_description`, `text`, required: false): Describe the emergency contact person's relationship with you.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `text`, required: false): List any medical conditions you have.
* **Medical Condition Description** (`medical_condition_description`, `text`, required: false): Describe your medical condition.
* **Medical Supervision** (`medical_supervision`, `select_one`, required: false): Select "True" if you require medical supervision during the service.
* **Medical Supervision Description** (`medical_supervision_description`, `text`, required: false): Describe the medical supervision required.
* **Lash Extension Service** (`lash_extension_service`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the lash extension services you are interested in.
* **Lash Extension Service Other** (`lash_extension_service_other`, `text`, required: false): If you have selected an "Other" lash extension service, enter the service name.
* **Lash Extension Service Other Description** (`lash_extension_service_other_description`, `text`, required: false): Describe the other lash extension service you have selected.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the statements that you understand and agree to.
* **Other** (`consent_other`, `text`, required: false): If you have selected an "Other" statement, enter the statement.
* **Consent Other Description** (`consent_other_description`, `text`, required: false): Describe the other statement you have selected.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `note`, required: false): Sign the form to confirm your understanding and consent.
* **Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of your signature.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, `note`, required: false): Sign the form to confirm your understanding and consent.
* **Client Date** (`client_signature_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of your signature.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, `text`, required: false): Enter the user ID or name that assigned this form.

Note: This form is for informational purposes only and should be reviewed and customized according to your organization's policies and procedures.
