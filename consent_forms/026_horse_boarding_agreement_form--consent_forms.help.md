# Horse Boarding Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Horse Boarding Agreement Form is designed for horse owners or boarders to agree on the terms of boarding their horse at a specific location.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the owner information section with your details.
2. Provide information about the horse, including its breed, age, size, and height.
3. Specify the boarding type, location, and frequency.
4. Choose the payment method and frequency.
5. Sign and date the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner Information**: This section is for you to provide your name, email, and phone number. Please fill in your details accurately.
* **Horse Information**: Enter your horse's breed, age, size, and height in this section.
* **Agreement Terms**: Describe any special requirements or conditions for boarding your horse in this section.
* **Boarding Start Date**: Select the start date for boarding your horse.
* **Boarding End Date**: Select the end date for boarding your horse.
* **Boarding Duration**: Enter the duration of boarding your horse.
* **Boarder Name**: Enter the name of the person or entity boarding your horse.
* **Boarder Email**: Enter the email of the person or entity boarding your horse.
* **Boarder Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person or entity boarding your horse.
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you will pay for boarding your horse (Cash, Credit Card, or Other).
* **Payment Frequency**: Select how often you will pay for boarding your horse (Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Payment Terms**: Describe any additional payment terms or conditions.
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional information about your horse or boarding requirements.
* **Signature**: Select which signature is required for this agreement (Signature 1, Signature 2, or Signature 3).
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date of your signature.
* **Signature Time**: Enter the time of your signature.
* **Boarding Type**: Choose the type of boarding (Full, Half, Pasture, or Boarding).
* **Boarding Location**: Select the location of boarding (Stable, Pasture, or Barn).
* **Boarding Frequency**: Choose how often you will be boarding your horse (Full Time, Part Time, or Occasional).
* **Horse Information 2**: Enter any additional information about your horse.
* **Horse Breed**: Enter your horse's breed.
* **Horse Gender**: Choose the gender of your horse (Male, Female, or Other).
* **Horse Age**: Select the age group of your horse (1-2, 3-5, 6-10, 11-15, 16-20, or 21+).
* **Horse Size**: Choose the size of your horse (Pony, Miniature, Small, Medium, Large, or XL).
* **Horse Height**: Enter your horse's height.
