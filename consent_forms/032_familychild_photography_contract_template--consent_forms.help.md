# family_child_photography_contract_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from clients for a family and child photography session. It covers session details, client contact information, and terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Session Details** field with the relevant information about the photography session.
2. Read and agree to the **Terms And Conditions** to ensure you understand the agreement.
3. Sign your name in the **Client Signature** field to confirm your understanding.
4. Fill in your **Name**, **Contact**, **Address**, **Email**, and **Phone** fields with your personal contact information.
5. Review the **Session Terms** field for any additional session-specific details.
6. Fill in the **Photographer Details** field with any relevant information about the photographer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Details** (`session_details`, text, required: false): Enter the details of the photography session, including date, time, location, and any specific requirements.
* **Terms And Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, text, required: false): Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the photography session.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, text, required: false): Sign your name to confirm your understanding of the terms and conditions.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your name.
* **Contact** (`contact`, text, required: false): Enter your contact information, including any relevant phone numbers or email addresses.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your address.
* **Email** (`email`, text, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Session Terms** (`session_terms`, text, required: false): Review any additional session-specific details.
* **Photographer Details** (`photographer_details`, text, required: false): Enter any relevant information about the photographer, including their name, contact information, and experience.
