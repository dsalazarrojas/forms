# Filming Interview Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Filming Interview Permission Form is used to obtain the filming interviewee's permission to record their interview for filming, video, or other purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Filming Interview Permission Form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill in each field accurately and completely.
3. Check the required fields and ensure they are filled before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Filming Interview Permission Form** (`filming_interview_permission_form`, `note`, required: false):
    This field is not required, but you may select it to specify the permission for filming. You can choose "Filming Interview Permission Form" if you want to film the interview for that specific purpose.
* **Filming Interview Permission** (`filming_interview_permission`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
    Select one or more options to specify the permission for filming. You can choose "Filming Interview Permission Form" or "Other" to indicate your intention. If you select "Other", you will have to provide more information in the next field.
* **Filming Interview Details** (`filming_interview_details`, `text`, required: false):
    This field is optional, but if you select "Other" in the previous field, you will be required to provide more details about the filming purpose in this field.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all the required fields accurately and completely.
- If you select "Other" in the Filming Interview Permission field, you will be required to provide more information in the Filming Interview Details field.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all fields are filled correctly.
