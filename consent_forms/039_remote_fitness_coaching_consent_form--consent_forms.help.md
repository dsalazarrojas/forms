# remote_fitness_coaching_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a critical part of the remote fitness coaching process, ensuring that clients understand and agree to the terms and conditions of the coaching program.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in the client's details, including their name, any relevant medical information, and any additional information that may be useful for the coaching process.
2. Answer the risk question with either "Yes" or "No".
3. Sign and date the form, if required.
4. Provide any additional information that may be relevant to the client's fitness goals and circumstances.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details** (`client_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the client's name, any relevant medical information, or other details that may be necessary for the coaching process.
* **Risks** (`risks`, `text`, required/optional): Identify any potential health risks or concerns that the client may have, such as pregnancy, diabetes, or other medical conditions.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, required/optional): Answer "Yes" if the client has understood and agreed to the terms and conditions of the coaching program. Answer "No" if the client has not provided consent.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `note`, required/optional): If required, sign and date the form to confirm that the client has read and understood the terms and conditions of the program.
* **Client Details Additional Info** (`client_details_additional_info`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any additional information that may be relevant to the client's fitness goals and circumstances.
