# Digital Lease Signature Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Digital Lease Signature Consent Form is a document that serves as a supplement to the lease agreement, ensuring that both landlords and tenants have read and understood the terms of the rental contract before finalizing it.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, you will need to provide the following information:

* Landlord's information (Section 1)
* Tenant's information (Section 2)
* Agreement Terms (Section 3)
* Lease Terms (Section 4)
* Electronic Signature (Section 5)
* Date of signing (Section 6)
* Time of signing (Section 7)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Landlord Name (text)**: Enter the name of the landlord or the property owner.
* **Tenant Name (text)**: Enter the name of the tenant or the renter.
* **Agreement Terms (text)**: Please read and understand the terms carefully. This section is for the landlord to describe the terms of the lease agreement.
* **Lease Terms (select_multiple)**: Select the lease term that applies to this agreement (6-month, 1-year, or 2-year lease).
* **Electronic Signature (text)**: Sign with your digital signature. This is your electronic representation of your signature.
* **Date (date)**: Enter the date of signing.
* **Time (time)**: Enter the time of signing.

## Tips

* Please make sure to read and understand the agreement terms carefully before signing.
* Ensure that the lease term selected correctly represents the duration of the lease.
* Enter accurate date and time of signing to avoid any disputes.
* Use a secure and reliable digital signature method to sign the form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all information is accurate and complete.
