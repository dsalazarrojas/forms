# Online Fundraising Livestream Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Online Fundraising Livestream Consent Form is designed to collect information from participants and their guardians for live events, such as charity fundraisers. This form helps ensure that all necessary consents and agreements are obtained before proceeding with the event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. **Participant Details**: Provide your name, email, and any other required information for your role as a participant or guardian.
2. **Media Release**: Read and agree to the media release section, which grants permission to be recorded or livestreamed during the event.
3. **Participant Signature**: Sign your name to signify your consent and agreement to the media release.
4. **Livestream Link** and **Livestream Platform**: Provide the link to the livestream and the platform it's on (e.g., YouTube, Facebook, Zoom).
5. **Livestream Type**: Select the type of livestream (public, private, recorded, paid, or free).
6. **Has Minor**: If you're participating with a minor, answer yes or no to this question.
7. **Has Minor Age**: If you answered yes to the previous question, provide the minor's age.
8. **Has Minor Parent Name**: If you're a parent or guardian of the minor, provide your name.
9. **Has Minor Email**: If you answered yes to the previous question, provide the minor's email address.
10. **Minor Signature**: If you're a minor participating with a guardian, sign your name to signify consent.
11. **Has Minor Parent Permission**: If you answered yes to the previous question, sign this field to confirm the parent's permission.
12. **Participant Agreement**: Review and agree to the participant agreement.
13. **Minor Agreement**: If you're a minor participating with a guardian, review and agree to the minor agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details (1)**: This field collects your name and contact information as a participant or guardian.
* **Media Release (3)**: This field requires you to agree to be recorded or livestreamed during the event. By signing this section, you grant permission to capture your likeness, voice, and/or performance for the purpose of the event.
* **Participant Signature (4)**: Sign this field to signify your consent and agreement to the media release.
* **Livestream Link (13)**: Provide the link to the livestream, so we can access it for your event.
* **Livestream Platform (14)**: Select the platform where the livestream is hosted (e.g., YouTube, Facebook, Zoom).
* **Livestream Type (20)**: Choose the type of livestream (public, private, recorded, paid, or free).
* **Has Minor (16)**: If you're participating with a minor, answer yes or no to this question.
* **Has Minor Age (17)**: If you answered yes to the previous question, provide the minor's age.
* **Has Minor Parent Name (18)**: If you're a parent or guardian of the minor, provide your name.
* **Has Minor Email (19)**: If you answered yes to the previous question, provide the minor's email address.
* **Minor Signature (22)**: If you're a minor participating with a guardian, sign your name to signify consent.
* **Has Minor Parent Permission (23)**: If you answered yes to the previous question, sign this field to confirm the parent's permission.
* **Participant Agreement (24)**: Review and agree to this section, which outlines the participant's terms of use.
* **Minor Agreement (25)**: If you're a minor participating with a guardian, review and agree to this section, which outlines the minor's terms of use.

## Tips

* Please ensure you answer all required fields to avoid issues with event registration.
* If you're participating with a minor, make sure to provide accurate information about the minor's age and contact details.
* Review each field carefully to ensure you understand the requirements and implications of your answers.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us!
