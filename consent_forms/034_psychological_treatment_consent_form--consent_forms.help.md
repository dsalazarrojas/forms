# psychological_treatment_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The psychological treatment consent form is used to gather essential information and consent from patients or individuals seeking psychological treatment. This form helps ensure that all parties involved are aware of their rights, responsibilities, and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Patient Information**: Provide basic details about yourself, including your contact information.
2. **Consent**: Choose whether you consent to participate in the treatment or decline.
3. **Emergency Contact**: Identify one or two emergency contacts in case you need help.
4. **Contact Information**: Fill out the contact information for your emergency contacts.
5. **Additional Details**: If required, provide extra information about yourself or your situation.
6. **Contact Person (if applicable)**: If you have a contact person, provide their details.
7. **Phone and Email**: Enter your phone number and email address.
8. **Confirm (if applicable)**: Repeat the consent or emergency contact information for confirmation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information**: This section is for your basic contact details, such as your name, address, and phone number.
* **Consent**: Please select "Consent to participate" to confirm your agreement to participate in the treatment.
* **Emergency Contact 1**: Name the person you want to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact 1 Phone**: Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact 1 Relationship**: Describe your relationship with the contact person.
* **Emergency Contact 1 Address**: Enter the contact person's address.
* **Emergency Contact 2 (if applicable)**: If you have another contact person, provide their details.
* **Emergency Contact 2 Phone (if applicable)**: Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact 2 Relationship (if applicable)**: Describe your relationship with the secondary contact person.
* **Emergency Contact 2 Address (if applicable)**: Enter the contact person's address.
* **Note**: If necessary, provide additional information or comments about yourself or your situation.
* **Contact Person (if applicable)**: Provide the contact person's details if you have one.
* **Contact Person Phone (if applicable)**: Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Person Relationship (if applicable)**: Describe your relationship with the contact person.
* **Contact Person Address (if applicable)**: Enter the contact person's address.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Email 2 (if applicable)**: If you have a secondary email, provide it.
* **Phone 2 (if applicable)**: If you have a secondary phone number, provide it.

Note: Please fill out all required fields and ensure the information is accurate and up-to-date. If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
