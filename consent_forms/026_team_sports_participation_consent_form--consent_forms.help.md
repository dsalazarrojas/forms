# Team Sports Participation Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Team Sports Participation Consent Form is designed to obtain consent for a child or minor to participate in sports activities. This form is used to gather essential information from parents or guardians to ensure the child's safety and well-being during the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields with accurate and valid information.
2.  For the "Parent Name" field, please enter the parent or guardian's name as it appears on their government-issued ID.
3.  In the "Emergency Contact" field, select "Yes" if you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency or "No" if not.
4.  Enter a valid phone number in the "Phone" field.
5.  Enter a valid email address in the "Email" field.
6.  For the "Participant Name" field, please enter the child's name as it appears on their government-issued ID.
7.  In the "Parent/Guardian Signature" field, please sign your name as a parent or guardian.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Parent Name** (Parent Name, text, required): Enter the parent or guardian's name as it appears on their government-issued ID.
*   **Emergency Contact** (Emergency Contact, select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" if you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency or "No" if not.
*   **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter a valid phone number.
*   **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
*   **Participant Name** (Participant Name, text, required): Enter the child's name as it appears on their government-issued ID.
*   **Parent/Guardian Signature** (Parent/Guardian Signature, note, required): Please sign your name as a parent or guardian.
*   **Participant's Date of Birth** (Participant's Date of Birth, date, required): Enter the child's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
*   **Relationship with Participant** (Relationship with Participant, select_multiple, optional): Select the relationship with the participant (e.g., Mother, Father, Other).
*   **Contact Address** (Contact Address, text, required): Enter a valid address.
*   **Participant Health Issues** (Participant Health Issues, text, optional): If applicable, list the participant's health issues.
*   **Emergency Contact Relationship** (Emergency Contact Relationship, select_multiple, required): Select the relationship of the emergency contact with the participant (e.g., Mother, Father, Other).

## Tips
Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date. This form is used to ensure the child's safety and well-being during the program. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
