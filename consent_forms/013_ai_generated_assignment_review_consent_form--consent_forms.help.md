# AI Generated Assignment Review Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AI Generated Assignment Review Consent Form is designed to gather information and consent from students and their guardians before submitting their work for AI-assisted review. This form helps to ensure that students and their guardians are aware of how their data will be used and stored.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your student's name in the **Student name** field, providing their full name as per your institution's naming conventions.
2. If you are a guardian or parent, enter your name in the **Guardian or parent name** field. This is required only if the student is a minor.
3. Select the **Course name or code** field to specify the course related to the assignment.
4. Choose 'True' or 'False' in the **Consent to AI assignment review** field to indicate whether you consent to AI reviewing your assignment.
5. In the **Data usage and retention** field, describe how the assignment data will be used and stored.
6. If you do not consent to AI review, provide instructions for opting out in the **Opt out instructions** field.
7. Enter the contact information of the person or department to contact for questions in the **Contact for questions** field.
8. Finally, enter the date of consent in the **Date of consent** field using the YYYY-MM-DD format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the student as per your institution's naming conventions.
* **Guardian or parent name** (`guardian_name`, text, optional): If the student is a minor, enter your name as guardian or parent.
* **Course name or code** (`course`, text, required): Select the course related to the assignment.
* **Consent to AI assignment review** (`consent_to_review`, select_one, required): Choose 'True' to consent to AI reviewing your assignment, or 'False' to decline.
* **Data usage and retention** (`data_usage`, text, required): Describe how the assignment data will be used and stored.
* **Opt out instructions** (`opt_out_instructions`, note, optional): If you decline AI review, provide instructions on how to opt out.
* **Contact for questions** (`contact_for_questions`, text, required): Enter the contact information of the person or department to contact for questions.
* **Date of consent** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of consent using the YYYY-MM-DD format.
