# Laser Tag Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Laser Tag Waiver Form is a crucial document that helps us ensure the participant's safety and well-being during activities involving laser tags. This form is designed to collect essential personal and medical information, and it's essential to fill it out accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To fill out this form, please follow the instructions on each page carefully.
2.  Fill in your personal details, such as your name, email, and date of birth.
3.  If you are a parent or guardian, please fill out the relevant sections for your minor child.
4.  Answer any medical questions truthfully, and provide details about any medical conditions, devices, or medications that you are taking.
5.  Finally, read and agree to the terms of the waiver by selecting "True" for "Parent/Guardian Consent" and "Minor's Consent".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required: `false`):
    *   Please enter your first name.
*   **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required: `false`):
    *   Please enter your last name.
*   **Email** (`3`, `email`, required: `false`):
    *   Please enter your email address.
*   **Date of Birth** (`4`, `date`, required: `false`):
    *   Please enter your date of birth in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (`5`, `text`, required: `false`):
    *   Please enter the name of your emergency contact person.
*   **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`6`, `text`, required: `false`):
    *   Please describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone** (`7`, `text`, required: `false`):
    *   Please enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
*   **Emergency Contact Email** (`8`, `email`, required: `false`):
    *   Please enter the email address of your emergency contact person.
*   **Emergency Contact Address** (`9`, `text`, required: `false`):
    *   Please enter the address of your emergency contact person.
*   **Relationship Type** (`10`, `text`, required: `false`):
    *   Please describe your relationship type with the emergency contact person.
*   **Medical Conditions** (`11`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`):
    *   Select any medical conditions you have, such as Allergies, High Blood Pressure, Epilepsy, Diabetes, or Other.
*   **Medical Condition Description** (`12`, `text`, required: `false`):
    *   Please provide a description of any medical conditions you have.
*   **Medical Device** (`13`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`):

*   **Minor's Emergency Contact Phone** (`25`, `text`, required: `false`):

## Tips

*   Please fill out this form accurately and truthfully.
*   If you are unsure about any questions, please consult with your emergency contact person or healthcare provider.
*   If you are a minor participant, please make sure that your parent or guardian has given their consent before completing the form.
