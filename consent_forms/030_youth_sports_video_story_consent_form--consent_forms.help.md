# youth_sports_video_story_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "youth_sports_video_story_consent_form" is a form used to obtain consent from parents or guardians for their child's participation in youth sports video stories. The form helps to ensure that parents or guardians are aware of and agree to the terms and conditions of video recording, sharing, and uploading of their child's sports-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select your response to the consent question**: Choose whether to give or decline consent for the video recording and sharing of your child's sports-related activities.
2. Fill out the sharing preferences and details sections, if applicable.
3. Enter your contact information for parental consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consent Form** (`consent_form`): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your agreement or disagreement to the video recording and sharing of your child's sports-related activities.
* **Parent Consent** (`parent_consent`): Select one or more options to indicate your consent for the sharing and use of your child's information and images. Choose 'Option 1', 'Option 2', or 'Option 3' for this field.
* **Sharing Preferences** (`sharing_preferences`): Provide any additional information or comments about your sharing preferences, if applicable. This field is optional.
* **Sharing Details** (`sharing_details`): Provide any additional information or comments about sharing, if applicable. This field is optional.
* **Video Capture** (`video_capture`): Enter the number of video captures you consent to (e.g., '1', '2', etc.). This field is optional.
* **Video Sharing** (`video_sharing`): Enter the number of times you consent to share the videos (e.g., '1', '2', etc.). This field is optional.
* **Recording** (`recording`): Enter the date of the recording or event (in format YYYY-MM-DD). This field is optional.
* **Sharing Duration** (`sharing_duration`): If you choose to share the video, provide the duration of the sharing (e.g., '1 day', '1 week', etc.). This field is optional.
* **Sharing Frequency** (`sharing_frequency`): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you consent to regular sharing of your child's sports-related activities.
* **Video Upload** (`video_upload`): This is a note field for any additional information or comments about video upload.
* **Sharing Method** (`sharing_method`): Select one or more options for sharing the video (e.g., 'Option 1', 'Option 2', etc.). This field is optional.
* **Sharing Scope** (`sharing_scope`): Provide any additional information or comments about sharing scope, if applicable. This field is optional.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`): Enter the date you grant consent for the video recording and sharing. This field is optional.
* **Sharing Status** (`sharing_status`): Provide any status or information about the sharing (e.g., 'Shared', 'Not Shared', etc.). This field is optional.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`): Enter your name as a parent or guardian.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`): This field is for your electronic signature, which indicates your agreement to the consent form.
