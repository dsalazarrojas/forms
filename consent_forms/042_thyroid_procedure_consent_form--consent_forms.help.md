# thyroid_procedure_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to be a template for obtaining consent from patients prior to undergoing a medical procedure, specifically related to thyroid procedures. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or derived from any form platform and is released under a Creative Commons license.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully and ensure you understand the questions being asked.
2. Fill in your procedure name and date of the procedure if required.
3. Enter your surgeon's name, patient's name, and procedure type.
4. Sign and date your signature if required.
5. Ensure all fields are accurately filled in before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Procedure Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the procedure you are consenting to.
- **Date of Procedure** (date, required): Enter the date of the procedure.
- **Surgeon's Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the surgeon performing the procedure.
- **Patient's Name** (text, required): Enter your name as the patient.
- **Procedure Type** (select_one, required): Select the type of procedure being performed, such as Left Laryngoscopy or Right Throidectomy.
- **Date of Signature** (date, required): Enter the date you are signing this form.
- **Signed By** (text, required): Enter your name as the patient.
- **Patient's Signature** (text, required): Sign your name to confirm your consent.
- **Date** (date, required): Enter the date you are signing this form.
- **Patient ID** (text, optional): Enter your patient ID if applicable.
