# Financial Services Liability Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to obtain the liability waiver and acknowledgement from clients for the financial services provided by the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the level of engagement you are agreeing to.
* Select the appropriate options for each field, taking into account your current engagement level with the organization.
* Fill out the form completely and accurately, including your signature and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Advisory Engagement** (`advisory_engagement`, select_one, required: false): Please select 'Yes' or 'No' indicating the level of advisory engagement you have with the organization.
* **Tax Engagement** (`tax_engagement`, select_one, required: false): Please select 'Yes' or 'No' indicating the level of tax engagement you have with the organization.
* **Planning Engagement** (`planning_engagement`, select_one, required: false): Please select 'Yes' or 'No' indicating the level of planning engagement you have with the organization.
* **Signed By** (`signed_by`, text, required: false): Please type your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required: false): Please enter the date you are signing this form.
* **Client Declaration** (`client_declaration`, text, required: false): Please provide a declaration of your understanding of the level of engagement you are committing to.
* **Financial Advisor Declaration** (`financial_adviser_declaration`, text, required: false): Please provide a declaration of your understanding of the level of financial advisor engagement you are committing to.
* **Financial Advisor Details** (`financial_adviser_details`, text, required: false): Please provide any additional details about your financial advisor engagement.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required: false): Please type your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Client Contact Details** (`client_contact_details`, text, required: false): Please provide your contact details for any further communication.
* **Financial Services** (`financial_services`, text, required: false): Please describe the financial services you are receiving from the organization.
* **Advisory Services** (`advisory_services`, text, required: false): Please describe the advisory services you are receiving from the organization.
* **Tax Services** (`tax_services`, text, required: false): Please describe the tax services you are receiving from the organization.
* **Planning Services** (`planning_services`, text, required: false): Please describe the planning services you are receiving from the organization.
* **Other Services** (`other_services`, text, required: false): Please describe any other services you are receiving from the organization.
* **Services Performed** (`services_performed`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select 'Yes' or 'No' indicating the services you have performed.
* **Signed** (`signed`, text, required: false): Please type your name as a confirmation of your understanding.
* **Acknowledgement** (`acknowledgement`, text, required: false): Please type your acknowledgement of this form.
* **Acknowledgement By** (`acknowledgement_by`, text, required: false): Please type your name as the acknowledgement.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Please enter the date this form was signed.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Please enter the time this form was signed.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, text, required: false): Please sign this form to confirm your understanding.

Note: Some fields are not required, please fill them out only if relevant to your situation.
