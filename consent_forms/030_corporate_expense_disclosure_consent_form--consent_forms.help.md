# Corporate Expense Disclosure Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Expense Disclosure Consent Form is designed to gather essential information from employees about their corporate expenses. This form helps ensure transparency and accountability within the company by collecting details about employee expenses, manager's name and email, and department ID.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
1. Enter your employee ID (ID #).
2. Provide your manager's name.
3. Enter your manager's email address.
4. Enter your department ID (optional).
5. Enter your department manager's name (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input**: Enter any additional information or comments about your expenses that you would like to disclose.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your company-issued employee ID number.
* **Manager Name**: Enter your direct manager's name, who will be responsible for reviewing and approving your expenses.
* **Manager Email**: Enter your direct manager's email address, who will be notified about your expense disclosure.
* **Department ID**: Enter your department ID number, which helps to track expenses by department (optional).
* **Department Manager**: Enter your department manager's name, who will be responsible for reviewing and approving your expenses (optional).

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have all the necessary information required to fill out this form accurately.
- If you're unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to contact your manager or HR department.
- Double-check your entered information before submitting the form.
