# Emergency Plumbing Service Waiver - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect necessary information and confirm consent from both the customer and the plumber for an emergency plumbing service provided. The information collected includes customer details, contact information, service area, and other relevant details. Completing this form ensures that both parties have agreed on the terms of the service and confirms their understanding of their roles and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the below steps:

1. Fill in all the required fields with accurate information.
2. Confirm your consent and understanding of the service terms.
3. Provide signature of both the customer and the plumber.
