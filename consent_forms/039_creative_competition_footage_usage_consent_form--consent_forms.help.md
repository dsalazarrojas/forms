# creative_competition_footage_usage_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from participants and submitters of creative competitions, allowing us to use their footage and images. It's essential to fill this form to ensure we have the necessary permissions to use their content.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the purpose of using the footage in the "Usage Purpose" field. Choose one or multiple options that apply to your project.
2. If you are the submitter of a creative competition, fill in the "Submitter Agreement" field. You will need to sign this agreement to confirm you understand and agree to our usage rules.
3. If you are a participant in the creative competition, fill in the "Participant Agreement" field. You will need to sign this agreement to confirm you understand and agree to our usage rules.
4. If you are a production company, fill in your name and website in the "Production Company" and "Production Company Website" fields, respectively.
5. Enter the contact person's name, email, and phone number in the "Contact Person", "Contact Person Email", and "Contact Person Phone" fields, respectively.
6. Finally, date the "Submitter Agreement Date" field with the date you are signing this agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Consent Form** (`consent_form`, `text`, optional): This field is used to record your consent to our usage of your footage and images.
- **Usage Purpose** (`usage_purpose`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or multiple options that describe the purpose of using your footage in our project.
  * Contests: For competitions, events, or festivals.
  * Festivals: For promotional purposes or exhibitions.
  * Campaigns: For marketing, advertising, or promotional campaigns.
- **Submitter Agreement** (`submitter_agreement`, `number`, optional): If you are a submitter, please fill in this field to confirm your understanding and agreement to our usage rules.
- **Submitter Agreement Signed** (`submitter_agreement_signed`, `text`, optional): If you are a submitter, please sign this agreement to confirm your understanding and agreement to our usage rules.
- **Participant Agreement** (`participant_agreement`, `number`, optional): If you are a participant, please fill in this field to confirm your understanding and agreement to our usage rules.
- **Participant Agreement Signed** (`participant_agreement_signed`, `text`, optional): If you are a participant, please sign this agreement to confirm your understanding and agreement to our usage rules.
- **Production Company** (`production_company`, `text`, optional): If you are a production company, please fill in your name.
- **Production Company Website** (`production_company_website`, `text`, optional): If you are a production company, please fill in your website URL.
- **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, optional): Enter the contact person's name.
- **Contact Person Email** (`contact_person_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the contact person's email address.
- **Contact Person Phone** (`contact_person_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the contact person's phone number.
- **Submitter Agreement Date** (`submitter_agreement_date`, `date`, optional): Date and sign this field with the date you are signing this agreement.
