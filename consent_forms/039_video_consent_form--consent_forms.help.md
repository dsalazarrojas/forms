# Video Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect video consent from individuals or entities that have a relationship with the content or subject of a video. It's intended to be used for both free and paid purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the Producer Information section with your name and contact details.
2. In the Owner Information section, enter the name and contact details of the owner or creator of the video.
3. In the Video Details section, provide a brief description of the video and its context.
4. In the Agreement section, select the type of permission you grant for the video's use.
5. In the Additional Comments section, add any further information or comments regarding the video's usage.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Producer Information**: This field is for you to provide your name and contact details. Please ensure that your information is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Owner Information**: This field is for the owner or creator of the video. Enter their name and contact details to allow us to reach them for any further questions or clarifications.
* **Video Details**: Provide a brief description of the video, including its title, date created, and any other relevant information.
* **Agreement**: Select the type of permission you grant for the video's use. Choose between 'I grant permission for the video to be used for free.' or 'I grant permission for the video to be used for paid purposes.'
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any further information or comments about the video's usage, please add them here.
* **Agreement (Select One)**: This field is a dropdown menu where you select one of the two options:
	+ I grant permission for the video to be used for free.
	+ I grant permission for the video to be used for paid purposes.
* **Additional Comments**: This field is a text box where you can add any further information or comments regarding the video's usage.

# Tips
Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth and efficient process. If you're unsure about any aspect of the video's usage, please consult a lawyer or relevant authorities.
