# Wildlife Rescue Public Awareness Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who want to participate in wildlife rescue public awareness campaigns. The collected information will be used to contact and follow up with participants, and to obtain their consent for their media to be used in promotional materials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the applicable options for each field and provide the necessary information. The form is divided into multiple pages, with each page asking for specific information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details** (user_details, Text, Required: false): This field is for your personal details such as name and contact information. Please provide your name, email address, and phone number so we can get in touch with you.
* **Do you allow media use** (media_use, Select Multiple, Required: false): Please select whether you allow the use of your media (photo, video, audio) in our promotional materials. You can choose 'Yes' or 'No' for each type of media.
* **Photo Use** (photo_use, Select Multiple, Required: false): If you selected 'Yes' in the previous field, you will be asked to indicate which types of photo use you consent to. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each type of photo use.
* **Video Use** (video_use, Select Multiple, Required: false): If you selected 'Yes' in the previous field, you will be asked to indicate which types of video use you consent to. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each type of video use.
* **Audio Use** (audio_use, Select Multiple, Required: false): If you selected 'Yes' in the previous field, you will be asked to indicate which types of audio use you consent to. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each type of audio use.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct options for each field to ensure that you are comfortable with how your media will be used.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us for more information.
* Thank you for participating in our wildlife rescue public awareness campaign! Your support is greatly appreciated.
