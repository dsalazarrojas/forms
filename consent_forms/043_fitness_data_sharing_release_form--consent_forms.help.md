# Fitness Data Sharing Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from individuals to share their fitness data with designated recipients, such as clients, doctors, or insurance companies. The form is used to gather information about the recipient, the purpose of sharing the data, and the time frame for sharing the data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the name and information of the recipient(s) you would like to share your fitness data with.
2. Specify the purpose of sharing your fitness data with the recipient(s).
3. Choose the time frame for sharing your data, which can be a specific date or a duration of time.
4. Select the designated recipient(s) you would like to share your data with from the list provided.
5. Sign the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Recipient (1)** (`Text`, required/optional): Enter the name(s) of the recipient(s) you would like to share your fitness data with.
* **Purpose (2)** (`Text`, required/optional): Describe the reason for sharing your fitness data with the recipient(s).
* **Time Frame (3)** (`Date`, required/optional): Choose the date or duration of time for sharing your data.
* **Designated Recipient (4)** (`Select One`, required/optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ Client
	+ Doctor
	+ Insurance Company
* **Signature (5)** (`Text`, required/optional): Sign your name to confirm your consent.
* **Date Signed (6)** (`Date`, required/optional): Enter the date you sign this form.
* **Date Received (7)** (`Date`, required/optional): This field will be filled in by the designated recipient when they receive your data.

Note: All fields with a checkbox of `required: false` are optional, but it is recommended to fill them in to provide a clear and complete consent.
