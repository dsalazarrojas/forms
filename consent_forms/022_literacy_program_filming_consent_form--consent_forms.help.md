# literacy_program_filming_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent for filming within a literacy program. It captures essential details such as filming location, date, and time, as well as contact information and relationship status of the person being filmed. The form also requires explicit consent from the individual before filming can take place.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the filming location where the filming will take place.
2. Enter the filming date when the filming will occur.
3. Enter the filming time.
4. Provide your name.
5. Enter your contact number for follow-up or any questions.
6. Indicate your relationship status with the individual being filmed (Yes or No).
7. Provide your explicit consent for filming by selecting "Yes" in the permission field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Filming Location (filming_location)**: Enter the location where filming will occur. This could be a physical address or a description of the location.
* **Filming Date (filming_date)**: Enter the date when filming will take place. This will help schedule the filming process and ensure everyone is aware of the dates.
* **Filming Time (filming_time)**: Enter the time when filming will occur. This will help in coordinating the filming schedule.
* **Name (name)**: Provide your name as the person or guardian/parent providing consent.
* **Contact Number (contact_number)**: Enter your contact number for any follow-up questions or concerns.
* **Relationship (relationship)**: Check "Yes" if you are a parent or guardian of the individual being filmed. If you are the individual being filmed, select "Yes" as well.
* **Permission (permission)**: Select "Yes" to explicitly give consent for filming to occur. This is a required field.
