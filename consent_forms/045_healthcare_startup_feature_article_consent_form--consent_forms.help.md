# Healthcare Startup Feature Article Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect necessary information for healthcare startups to provide a consent process for feature articles. It ensures that all required details are gathered in a single, easy-to-use form.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field and ensure you have the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Complete the form carefully, ensuring all fields are filled correctly.
3. If a field is not required, feel free to skip it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Featured In Article** (`featured_in_article`, select_multiple, required: false): 
  This field allows you to choose whether the article is featured or not. Select 'Yes' if the article is featured, and 'No' otherwise.
* **article_title** (article_title, text, required: false): 
  Enter the title of the feature article.
* **Article URL** (article_url, text, required: false): 
  Provide the URL of the article, so we can easily access it.
* **Email** (email, email, required: false): 
  Enter the email address of the person granting consent.
* **Phone** (phone, text, required: false): 
  Add your phone number for additional contact information.
* **Name** (name, text, required: false): 
  Enter your name for record-keeping purposes.
* **Date Of Consent** (date_of_consent, date, required: false): 
  Record the date of consent to track the timeline of approvals.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
- Double-check your answers before submitting to avoid errors.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask your healthcare startup representative.
