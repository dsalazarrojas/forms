# Release Of Medical Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain authorization from patients to release their medical information to various individuals or organizations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Release Of Medical Information** (`release_of_medical_information_form_1`): Select "Yes" if you want to release your medical information, and "No" if you do not. This is the main purpose of the form.
* **Owner** (`owner`): Enter your name as the owner of the medical information.
* **Authorization Type** (`authorization_type`): If you select "Yes" on the previous field, select "Yes" or "No" to specify if you want to grant authorization for the release of medical information.
* **Authorization Date** (`authorization_date`): If you select "Yes" on the previous field, enter the date you want the authorization to be effective.
* **Release Date** (`release_date`): Enter the date the medical information is to be released.
* **Medical Records Release** (`medical_records_release`): Select "Yes" if you want to release your medical records, and "No" if you do not.
* **Other Health Info Release** (`other_health_info_release`): Select "Yes" if you want to release other health information, and "No" if you do not.
* **Other Health Info Details** (`other_health_info_details`): If you select "Yes" on the previous field, provide more details about the health information you want to release.
* **Other Health Info Authorized Party** (`other_health_info_authorized_party`): If you select "Yes" on the previous field, provide the name of the authorized party.
* **Signature** (`signature`): Sign this section to indicate your agreement to release your medical information.
* **Owner Signature** (`owner_signature`): Sign this section with your name to confirm your agreement.
* **Authorized By** (`authorized_by`): If applicable, enter the name of the authorized party.
* **Authorization Date Signed** (`authorization_date_signed`): Enter the date you signed the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to carefully review the form before signing to ensure you understand who has access to your medical information.
* Take a copy of the completed form for your records.
* If you have any questions or concerns, ask your healthcare provider for assistance.
