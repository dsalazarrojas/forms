# corporate_matching_gift_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain employee consent for corporate matching gifts, which are funds matched by a company's matching gift program to their charitable donations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the information requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employer Name (employer_name)**: Please enter your employer's name as it appears on their official documents or website.
* **Company Address (company_address)**: Enter your company's address, including street address or PO box.
* **Employee Donor (employee_donor)**: Select the correct category to indicate your status as an employee of the company.
	+ Company Representative: You are a representative of the company who is submitting this form on behalf of another employee.
	+ Donor: You are the employee participating in the matching gift program.
	+ Other (please specify): If you are not an employee or a representative, please select this option and provide further explanation in the "Other" field.
* **Job Title (job_title)**: Enter your job title as it appears on your company's official documents or website.
* **Business Category (business_category)**: Select the correct category to indicate the type of business your company represents.
	+ Corporation: A traditional corporation.
	+ Foundation: A non-profit foundation.
	+ Other (please specify): If your company does not fit into one of the above categories, please select this option and provide further explanation in the "Other" field.
* **Gift Match Type (gift_match_type)**: Select the type of gift match that applies to your donation.
	+ Matching Gifts: The company matches your donation amount-for-amount.
	+ Non-Matching Gifts: The company provides a fixed amount for all or some of your donations.
	+ Other (please specify): If neither of the above applies, please select this option and provide further explanation in the "Other" field.
* **Gift Amount (gift_amount)**: Enter the total amount of your donation for which you are seeking a match.
* **Gift Fiscal Year (gift_fiscal_year)**: Enter the fiscal year (January to December) of your donation for which you are seeking a match.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your work email address.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter your work phone number.
* **Date of Birth (date_of_birth)**: Enter your date of birth (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Date of Gift (date_of_gift)**: Enter the date of your gift (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Signature (signature)**: Please sign and date your declaration to confirm your participation in the matching gift program.
* **Signature Date (signature_date)**: Enter the date you signed this declaration.
* **Employer Match Program (employer_match_program)**: Please note any special conditions or limitations of your company's match program, if applicable.
