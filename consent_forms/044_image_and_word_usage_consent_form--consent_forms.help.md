## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your user information in the "User Information" section.
2. Select the correct image rights from the "Image Rights" field. If the rights are not listed, choose "Other (Please specify below)" and add the rights in the "Other (optional)" field.
3. Similarly, select the correct word rights from the "Word Rights" field. If the rights are not listed, choose "Other (Please specify below)" and add the rights in the "Other (optional)" field.
4. Fill in the "Signed by" field with the name of the person signing the form.
5. Enter the date and time you signed the form in the "Date Signed" and "Time Signed" fields, respectively.
6. Fill in your organizational information, including job title, address, city, province, country, and postal code.
7. Add any other contact information if needed.
8. Finally, sign the "Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (1)** (`user_information`, text, optional): This field is for user to provide their basic information.
* **Image Rights (2)** (`image_rights`, select_one, optional): Choose how the image will be used (Print, Digital, or Other).
* **Other (optional) (3)** (`image_rights_other`, select_multiple, optional): If "Other" is chosen for image rights, specify how the image will be used in this field.
* **Word Rights (4)** (`word_rights`, select_one, optional): Choose how the words will be used (Print, Digital, or Other).
* **Other (optional) (5)** (`word_rights_other`, select_multiple, optional): If "Other" is chosen for word rights, specify how the words will be used in this field.
* **Signed by (6)** (`signed_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person signing the form.
* **Date Signed (7)** (`date_signed`, date, optional): Enter the date you signed the form.
* **Time Signed (8)** (`time_signed`, time, optional): Enter the time you signed the form.
* **Organization (9)** (`organization`, text, optional): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Job Title (10)** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Address (11)** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **City (12)** (`city`, text, optional): Enter your city.
* **Province (13)** (`province`, text, optional): Enter your province.
* **Country (14)** (`country`, text, optional): Enter your country.
* **Postal Code (15)** (`postal_code`, text, optional): Enter your postal code.
* **Phone (16)** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email (17)** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Fax (18)** (`fax`, text, optional): Enter your fax number.
* **Other Contact Information (19)** (`other_contact_info`, text, optional): Enter any other contact information.
* **Other (please specify) (20)** (`other_contact_info_other`, text, optional): If you have other contact information that doesn't fit in the above field, specify it here.
* **Signature (21)** (`signature`, text, optional): Sign your name to confirm the form.

Note: Some fields may be required or optional, depending on the purpose of the form. Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
