# music_therapy_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The music therapy intake form is designed to collect essential information from patients to help therapists provide effective and personalized music therapy services.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out each page sequentially, starting with page 1 (Patient Information) and moving to the last page (Therapist Information).
- Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and honestly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Information**: This is the initial contact information for the patient or their representative.
- **Contact Information**: This field is for providing contact information in case the therapist needs to reach out to you or your emergency contact.
- **Medical Conditions**: This is where you can select one or multiple medical conditions that affect you or your patient. Select all that apply.
  • Condition 1
  • Condition 2
  • Condition 3
- **Medications**: This is where you can indicate if you take any medications. Select all that apply.
  • 'Yes'
  • 'No'
- **Medical History**: This field is for providing any relevant medical history that may impact your therapy sessions.
- **Therapy Goals**: This field is for specifying your music therapy goals, such as improving mood or reducing anxiety.
- **Emergency Contact**: This is the contact information for an emergency contact person in case the therapist needs to reach out to them.
- **Emergency Contact Info**: This field is for providing the emergency contact's contact information.
- **Music Therapy Goals**: This field is for specifying your music therapy goals, such as improving memory or focus.
- **Medical Facility**: This field is for selecting the medical facility where you will be receiving music therapy.
  • Option 1
  • Option 2
  • Option 3
- **Medical Doctor**: This is for selecting the doctor that is responsible for your current medical care.
  • Option 1
  • Option 2
  • Option 3
- **Therapist Name**: This field is for providing the name of the music therapist who will be working with you.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and honestly.
- If you have any doubts or questions, please reach out to your music therapist for clarification.
- This form is designed to collect sensitive information, so please fill it out carefully and accurately.
