# Hydrogen Peroxide Treatment Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users regarding their medical history and consent for treatments involving hydrogen peroxide.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your name in the "User Name" field. This field is required.
2. If you need to contact us, please provide your contact number (optional) and email address (optional).
3. Provide a contact phone number (optional) if you want to be contacted by phone.
4. Please enter your medical history in the "Medical History" field. This field is required and allows you to provide detailed notes about your medical history.
5. Please select a signature from the "Signature" field (fields 17-22). Only "Signature 1" and "Signature 4" are required.
6. Enter the date you sign each of the signatures (fields 23-25). Only "Medical Signature Date 1" is required.
7. Review and submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name (1)**: Please enter your name.
* **Contact Number (2)**: Enter your contact number if you want to be contacted by phone.
* **Contact E-mail (4)**: Enter your email address if you want to be contacted by email.
* **Contact Number (5)**: Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted by phone.
* **Medical History (6)**: Provide detailed notes about your medical history.
* **Medical History 1-10 (7-16)**: Provide additional notes about your medical history (optional).
* **Signature 1 (17)**: Select a signature option.
* **Signature 2 (18)**: Select a signature option.
* **Signature 3 (19)**: Select a signature option.
* **Signature 4 (20)**: Select a signature option. This field is required.
* **Signature 5 (21)**: Select a signature option.
* **Signature 6 (22)**: Select a signature option.
* **Medical Signature Date 1 (23)**: Enter the date you sign this form.
* **Medical Signature Date 2 (24)**: Enter the date you sign this form (optional).
* **Medical Signature Date 3 (25)**: Enter the date you sign this form (optional).
