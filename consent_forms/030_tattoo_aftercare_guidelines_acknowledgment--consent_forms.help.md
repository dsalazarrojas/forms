# Tattoo Aftercare Guidelines Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to obtain acknowledgment from clients that they understand and agree to follow tattoo aftercare guidelines, which are essential for the proper healing and maintenance of their new tattoo.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the aftercare guidelines provided to you before answering this form.
2. Check the boxes to confirm that you understand and agree to follow the guidelines.
3. Select how you received the aftercare information.
4. Provide details about your new tattoo, including location, size, color, and style.
5. Check the boxes to confirm that you agree to be responsible for your tattoo care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Do you accept to follow tattoo aftercare guidelines?** (`accept_tattoo_aftercare_guidelines`, checkbox, required): Please check this box if you understand and agree to follow the aftercare guidelines.
* **Do you understand tattoo aftercare guidelines?** (`understand_aftercare_guidelines`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if you have read and understood the aftercare guidelines provided to you.
* **Do you agree to follow tattoo aftercare guidelines?** (`follow_aftercare_guidelines`, checkbox, required): Check this box to confirm that you agree to follow the aftercare guidelines.
* **Do you agree to be responsible for tattoo care?** (`responsible_for_tattoo_care`, checkbox, required): Check this box to confirm that you agree to take responsibility for your tattoo care.
* **How did you receive tattoo care information?** (`receive_tattoo_care_info`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options below to indicate how you received the aftercare information.
	+ From an artist
	+ Online
	+ From a parent
* **What type of tattoo is this?** (`tattoo_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply to indicate the type of tattoo you received.
	+ Hand-Drawn Tattoo
	+ Machine-Artwork
	+ Combination
* **Tattoo Location** (`tattoo_location`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply to indicate the location of your new tattoo.
	+ Head
	+ Neck
	+ Arms
	+ Legs
	+ Back
* **Tattoo Size** (`tattoo_size`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply to indicate the size of your new tattoo.
	+ Small
	+ Medium
	+ Large
* **Tattoo Orientation** (`tattoo_orientation`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply to indicate the orientation of your new tattoo.
	+ Horizontal
	+ Vertical
	+ Rotating
* **Tattoo Color** (`tattoo_color`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply to indicate the color of your new tattoo.
	+ Red
	+ Blue
	+ Green
* **Tattoo Style** (`tattoo_style`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply to indicate the style of your new tattoo.
	+ Line Art
	+ Dot work
	+ Color Fill
* **Session Notes** (`session_notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional information or notes about your tattoo session.
* **Tattoo Session Number** (`tattoo_session_number`, number, optional): Enter the number of your tattoo session.
* **Tattoo Session Date** (`tattoo_session_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your tattoo session.
* **Tattoo Session Time** (`tattoo_session_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your tattoo session.
* **Session Duration** (`tattoo_session_duration`, time, optional): Enter the duration of your tattoo session.
* **Aftercare Instructions** (`tattoo_aftercare_instructions`, note, optional): Provide any additional information or notes about your aftercare instructions.
