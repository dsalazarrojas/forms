# Government Assistance Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request access to employee information for government agencies or other official bodies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the organization's name to whom you are releasing the employee's records.
2. Enter the employee's name, which can be found on their ID card or other official documents.
3. Enter the employee's job title, if known.
4. Enter the employee's social security number.
5. Enter the employee's date of birth.
6. Enter the employee's address.
7. Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the government agency is requesting the employee's records.
8. Enter the date you would like to release the employee's records.
9. Enter any additional contact information for the employee.
10. Enter any additional notes about the employee, if necessary.
11. Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you are releasing the employee's records by yourself or by a third party.
12. Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you are releasing a range of dates for the employee's records.
13. Enter the reason for releasing the employee's records.
14. Enter the date you gave the employee's consent to release their records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, **text**, required): Enter the name of the organization requesting access to the employee's records.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, **text**, required): Enter the employee's full name, which can be found on their ID card or other official documents.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, **text**, optional): Enter the employee's job title if it's known.
* **Social Security Number** (`social_security_number`, **number**, required): Enter the employee's Social Security number for verification purposes.
* **Employee DOB** (`employee_dob`, **date**, optional): Enter the employee's date of birth if known.
* **Employee Address** (`employee_address`, **text**, optional): Enter the employee's address for contact purposes.
* **Government Agency** (`government_agency`, **select_one**, required): Select 'Yes' to indicate that you are from a government agency.
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, **date**, required): Enter the date you would like to release the employee's records.
* **Employee Contact** (`employee_contact`, **text**, optional): Enter any additional contact information for the employee.
* **Employee Notes** (`employee_notes`, **note**, optional): Enter any additional notes about the employee, if necessary.
* **Release By** (`release_by`, **select_multiple**, optional): Select 'Yes' to indicate that you are releasing the employee's records by yourself, or 'No' to indicate you are releasing by a third party.
* **Release Date Range** (`release_date_range`, **select_multiple**, optional): Select 'Yes' to indicate that you are releasing a range of dates for the employee's records.
* **Release Reason** (`release_reason`, **text**, required): Enter the reason for releasing the employee's records.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, **date**, required): Enter the date you gave the employee's consent to release their records.
