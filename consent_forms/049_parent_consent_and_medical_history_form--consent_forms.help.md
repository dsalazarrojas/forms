# Parent Consent And Medical History Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Parent Consent And Medical History Form" is a crucial document that captures critical information from a parent or guardian regarding their child's medical history, allergies, and emergency contact details. This form is used to provide essential information in case of an emergency or medical situations where the parent's consent is necessary for the child's well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form sections as instructed, making sure to enter accurate and relevant information for each field.
2. Please review each section carefully and ensure that all required fields are completed.
3. If a field is not required, you may leave it blank if not applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Consent** (`parent_consent`, text, optional):
	+ This section is intended for parents or guardians to provide their consent for their child to receive medical treatment.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional):
	+ Describe your child's medical history, including any previous medical conditions, allergies, or previous treatments.
* **Allergies** (`allergy`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select all the allergies your child has from the options provided.
* **Medications** (`medication`, text, optional):
	+ List any medications your child is currently taking, including dosages and frequencies.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional):
	+ Provide the name, phone number, and relationship of the emergency contact person.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional):
	+ Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person (e.g., mother, father, aunt, uncle, etc.).
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, text, optional):
	+ Please sign your name, date, and provide your contact information for verification.
* **Parent Signature**: Please note that this is the parent's signature section, and it is not intended for the child to sign.
