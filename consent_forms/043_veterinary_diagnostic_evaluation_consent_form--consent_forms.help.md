# Veterinary Diagnostic Evaluation Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Veterinary Diagnostic Evaluation Consent Form is designed to collect information from pet owners to aid in veterinary diagnostic evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to understand what information is being requested.
2. Select the applicable options for each field.
3. Answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Info** (`pet_info`, text, required): This field is for providing basic information about your pet, including its name, age, breed, and species.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, required): Describe any symptoms or health issues your pet is currently experiencing.
* **Consent** (`consent`, text, required): Check the box to indicate your consent for the veterinary diagnostic evaluation and treatment of your pet.
* **Diagnostic Procedures** (`diagnostic_procedures`, text, required): Provide information about any previous diagnostic procedures performed on your pet.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions accurately and truthfully.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your veterinarian for clarification.
- This form is for general use; however, it may be adapted for specific purposes, such as medical histories or treatment plans.
