## Personal Trainer Injury Waiver And Media Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is to be completed by clients or members who wish to engage in any type of physical activity or exercise under the supervision of our personal trainers. It is required to understand that this waiver and media consent form is a necessary step to ensure their safety and consent for any media release or photography. It is a one-time requirement that must be completed before starting any training sessions or activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the following steps:
- Carefully read and understand all the questions and sections.
- Fill in all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
- For fields with multiple options, select the most accurate and complete information.
- Review and sign the form after completion to confirm.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Waiver**: Please provide a written statement of your understanding that you are aware of the risks involved in physical activities, and that you are willing to participate with your consent.
* **Media Consent**: Please select whether you are willing to release your media (photography/video) for promotional use. This includes, but not limited to, social media posts and promotional materials for our business.
* **Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth**: Please enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Contact Number**: Please enter your contact number that we can reach you in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Please enter the name of the person who will be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Number**: Please enter the contact number of the person who will be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Please describe the relationship between you and the emergency contact person (e.g., parent, spouse, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Please enter the email of the person who will be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Medical History**: If you have any pre-existing medical conditions or concerns, please provide a detailed explanation.
* **Medication**: If you have any current or past medical conditions that may impact your training, please provide a detailed explanation.
* **Allergy**: If you have any allergies, please provide a detailed explanation.
* **Medical Conditions**: If you have any pre-existing medical conditions that may impact your training, please provide a detailed explanation.
* **Medical Procedure**: Please select whether you have undergone any medical procedures that may impact your training (e.g., surgery, etc.).
* **Media Release**: Please confirm that you are willing to have your photo and video taken during training sessions for promotional use.
* **Photo Release**: Please confirm that you are willing to have your photo taken during training sessions for promotional use.
* **Video Release**: Please confirm that you are willing to have your video taken during training sessions for promotional use.

Please take a few moments to carefully review this form and fill it out completely and accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to us. We appreciate your cooperation and understanding in this matter.
