# Real Estate COVID 19 Compliance Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about individuals accessing the property, ensuring a safe and healthy environment for everyone. It helps in identifying potential risks and takes necessary precautions to prevent the spread of COVID-19.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant properties you have access to (property_access).
2. Fill in your full name (full_name).
3. Provide your phone number (phone).
4. Enter your health information (health_info).
5. Share your travel history (travel_history).
6. Indicate if you're experiencing any symptoms (symptoms).
7. Specify if you've had any contact with someone with COVID-19 (contact_info).
8. Confirm your current travel status (travel_status).
9. Declare your exposure status (exposure_status).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Access** (`property_access`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select all properties you have accessed or plan to access.
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required: false): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Health Information** (`health_info`, number, required: false): Please enter 0 if you do not have any health issues or concerns.
* **Travel History** (`travel_history`, text, required: false): Share any recent travel history, including domestic or international travel.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, select_one, required: false): Check if you are experiencing any COVID-19 symptoms, such as fever, cough, or shortness of breath.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, select_one, required: false): Indicate if you have had any contact with someone with COVID-19 in the past 14 days.
* **Travel Status** (`travel_status`, select_one, required: false): Confirm your current travel status.
* **Exposure Status** (`exposure_status`, select_one, required: false): Declare if you've had any exposure to a confirmed COVID-19 case.
