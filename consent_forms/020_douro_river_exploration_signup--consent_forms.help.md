# Douro River Exploration Signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Douro River Exploration Signup form is used by tour operators to collect participant information and safety details for guided tours or exploration activities on the Douro River.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your participant details, including name, address, email, phone number, and participant date of birth.
2.  Confirm your safety acknowledgement and select "Yes" to indicate that you have read and understand the safety information provided.
3.  Provide details about your tour operator, including name, email, phone number, and emergency contact information.
4.  Confirm the emergency contact's details, including their name, phone number, and address.
5.  Sign and date the participant agreement section, indicating that you have read and understand the terms and conditions.
6.  Sign and date the tour operator agreement section, indicating that you have read and understand the terms and conditions.
7.  Fill in your emergency contact's details, including their name, phone number, and address.
8.  Sign and date the participant signature section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`): This field is used to collect general information about the participant.
* **Safety Acknowledgement** (`safety_acknowledgement`): This field is used to confirm that you have read and understand the safety information provided.
* **Tour Operator Details** (`tour_operator_details`): This field is used to store information about the tour operator's company.
* **Tour Operator Name** (`tour_operator_name`): This field is used to collect the name of the tour operator.
* **Tour Operator Email** (`tour_operator_email`): This field is used to collect the email address of the tour operator.
* **Tour Operator Phone** (`tour_operator_phone`): This field is used to collect the phone number of the tour operator.
* **Participant Safety Details** (`participant_safety_details`): This field is used to collect information about the participant's safety details.
* **Participant Agreement** (`participant_agreement`): This field is used to collect the participant's agreement to the terms and conditions.
* **Emergency Contact Details** (`emergency_contact_details`): This field is used to collect information about the participant's emergency contact.
* **Emergency Name** (`emergency_name`): This field is used to collect the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`): This field is used to collect the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`): This field is used to collect the participant's address.
* **Participant Postal Code** (`participant_postal_code`): This field is used to collect the participant's postal code.
* **Participant City** (`participant_city`): This field is used to collect the participant's city.
* **Participant Country** (`participant_country`): This field is used to collect the participant's country.
* **Tour Operator Agreement** (`tour_operator_agreement`): This field is used to collect the tour operator's agreement to the terms and conditions.
* **Tour Operator Termination Clause** (`tour_operator_termination_clause`): This field is used to collect information about the tour operator's termination clause.
* **Participant Agreement Text** (`participant_agreement_text`): This field is used to collect the participant's agreement text.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`): This field is used to collect the participant's signature.
* **Tour Operator Signature** (`tour_operator_signature`): This field is used to collect the tour operator's signature.
* **Participant Date** (`participant_date`): This field is used to collect the participant's date.
* **Participant Time** (`participant_time`): This field is used to collect the participant's time.
* **Participant Date of Birth** (`participant_dob`): This field is used to collect the participant's date of birth.
* **Participant Nationality** (`participant_nationality`): This field is used to collect the participant's nationality.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check the participant and emergency contact details for accuracy.
* Ensure that you have read and understand the safety information provided.
* Sign and date the participant and tour operator agreements sections.
* Keep the participant and emergency contact details up to date.
* Ensure that the participant and tour operator have the necessary permissions and agreements in place.
* Review the participant and tour operator details carefully before submitting the form.
