# Nail Tech Contractor Application And Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for nail technicians and contractors who want to work with our organization. It's used to collect contact information, services offered, and terms of agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the field labeled "First Name".
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the field labeled "Last Name".
3. Provide your **Email** address in the field labeled "Email".
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the field labeled "Phone".
5. Enter your **Address** in the field labeled "Address".
6. Select the **Services Offered** you are available to provide (e.g. Nail Tech, Nail Technician, Nail Art, etc.).
7. Read through and agree to the **Terms and Conditions**.
8. Sign the **Agreement** field to confirm your understanding of the terms.
9. Review the **Contract Terms** and ensure you understand the agreement.
10. Sign the **Contract Signature** field to confirm your agreement to the terms.
11. If you have any further questions or concerns, you can contact us through the "Contact Us" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter your address or physical location.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth (this will be used for age verification purposes).
* **Services Offered** (`services_offered`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the services you offer as a nail technician (e.g. Nail Tech, Nail Technician, Nail Art, etc.).
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, text, required/optional): Read through and agree to the terms and conditions of your contract.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required/optional): Sign your name to confirm your agreement to the terms.
* **Contract** (`contract`, note, required/optional): Review the contract terms before signing.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, required/optional): Review the terms and conditions before signing the agreement.
* **Contact Us** (`contact_us`, text, required/optional): If you have any questions or concerns, contact us through this field.
* **Contract Terms** (`contract_terms`, text, required/optional): Review the contract terms before signing.
* **Contract Signature** (`contract_signature`, text, required/optional): Sign your name to confirm your agreement to the terms.
* **Terms and Conditions Terms** (`terms_and_conditions_terms`, note, required/optional): Review the terms and conditions before signing.
