# Pet Adoption Contract And Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect and document the details of a pet adoption process, ensuring that both the pet and its new owner have a smooth transition.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the following fields:
	* **Pet Contract**: Enter a description of the terms and conditions of the adoption, including any specific care requirements for the pet.
	* **Pet Information**: Provide details about the pet, such as its name, breed, age, and health conditions.
	* **Owner Information**: Enter your contact information, including name, address, phone number, and email.
	* **Waiver**: Sign this section to indicate that you understand and agree to the terms of the adoption.
	* **Signature**: Sign this section to confirm that you are the new owner of the pet.
	* **Date**: Enter the date of the adoption.
	* **Time**: Enter the time of the adoption.
	* **Notes**: Leave any additional comments or instructions about the pet or adoption process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Contract** (`Pet Contract`, Text, Required): Describe the terms and conditions of the adoption, including any specific care requirements for the pet.
* **Pet Information** (`Pet Information`, Text, Optional): Provide details about the pet, such as its name, breed, age, and health conditions.
* **Owner Information** (`Owner Information`, Text, Required): Enter your contact information, including name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Waiver** (`Waiver`, Text, Required): Sign this section to indicate that you understand and agree to the terms of the adoption.
* **Signature** (`Signature`, Text, Required): Sign this section to confirm that you are the new owner of the pet.
* **Date** (`Date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date of the adoption.
* **Time** (`Time`, Time, Optional): Enter the time of the adoption.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, Text, Optional): Leave any additional comments or instructions about the pet or adoption process.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Review the terms of the adoption carefully before signing the waiver.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the pet adoption organization.
* Keep this form for your records and reference it in case of any issues with the pet's care.
