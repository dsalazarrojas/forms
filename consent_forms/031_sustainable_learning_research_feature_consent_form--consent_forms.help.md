Sustainable Learning Research Feature Consent Form - Help Guide
=============================================

## Purpose

This form is used to obtain user consent for participating in sustainable learning research features. It captures essential information for categorizing, tracking, and analyzing user data.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Form Title** field with a brief title for your research feature.
2. Provide a detailed description of your research feature in the **Form Description** field.
3. Select the relevant **Category** from the provided options (this field is required).
4. You may optionally select multiple **Assigned Tools** that will be used for your research feature.
5. If desired, specify a file name for the **Output File** field.
6. Enter the **Date** of your research feature (this field is required).
7. Optionally enter the time of your research feature in the **Time** field.
8. Add any relevant notes about your research feature in the **Note** field.
9. Enter your **Email** and **Phone** (if applicable) in their respective fields for contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (id: 1, text, optional): Enter a brief title for your research feature.
* **Form Description** (id: 2, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of your research feature.
* **Category** (id: 3, select_one, required): Select the category under which your research feature will be categorized.
* **Category** (id: 4, text, optional): This field appears to be a duplicate label. Please ignore it for now.
* **Description** (id: 5, text, optional): Enter a description for your research feature.
* **Form ID** (id: 6, number, optional): Enter a unique ID for your research feature.
* **Assigned Tool** (id: 7, select_multiple, optional): Select the tools assigned to your research feature.
* **Output File** (id: 8, text, optional): Specify a name for the output file related to your research feature.
* **Date** (id: 9, date, required): Enter the date for your research feature.
* **Time** (id: 10, time, optional): Enter the time of your research feature.
* **Note** (id: 11, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your research feature.
* **Email** (id: 12, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (id: 13, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Phone 2** (id: 14, text, optional): This field appears to be a duplicate label. Please ignore it for now.
