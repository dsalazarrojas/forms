## Nutrition Privacy Policy Acknowledgment - Help Guide

### Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain consent from users about the usage of their personal data in accordance with the nutrition company's privacy policy. This form is used to verify that users understand and agree to the terms of the privacy policy, which is essential for protecting their data and ensuring transparency.

### How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the provided instructions and ensure you understand the content.
2. Complete each field according to the guidelines below.

### Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nutrition Privacy Policy Acknowledgment** (`nutrition_privacy_policy_acknowledgment`, note, required): This is a note field where you can read and acknowledge the nutrition company's privacy policy. Please review the policy and acknowledge that you have read and understood it.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, email, required): Enter your contact information, including your email address. This will help the company contact you regarding any questions or concerns related to your data.
* **Email** (`email`, text, required): Enter your email address. This is a crucial piece of information that the company will use to communicate with you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number. This will help the company contact you regarding your data or any other matter.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address. This will help the company understand your location.
* **Privacy Policy Agreement** (`privacy_policy_agreement`, select_one, required): Select one of the two options: "I have read and agree to the Privacy Policy" or "I have not read and do not agree to the Privacy Policy". This is a critical field that verifies your understanding and agreement with the policy.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or concerns, you can enter them here. This is an optional field, and you can skip it if you don't have anything to add.

### Tips
* Make sure to carefully review the privacy policy before completing this form.
* Ensure that you understand the terms and conditions of the policy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the company's support team.
* Fill out the form accurately and completely to avoid any issues.
