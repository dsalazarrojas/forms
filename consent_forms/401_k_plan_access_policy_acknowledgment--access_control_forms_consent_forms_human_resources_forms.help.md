# 401(k) Plan Access Policy Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 401(k) Plan Access Policy Acknowledgment form is used to ensure that employees understand the 401(k) Plan Access Policy and acknowledge their rights and responsibilities related to it.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Employee Information**
	* `Employee ID`: Enter your unique employee identifier, usually a number or a combination of numbers and letters.
	* `Full name`: Enter your legal first and last name.
	* `Email address`: Provide your primary work email.
	* `Department`: Enter your department, e.g., Finance, Marketing, or IT.
	* `Job title`: Enter your job title, e.g., Senior Analyst, Project Manager.
2. **Employment Details**
	* `Job level`: Select the level that best describes your position: Entry-level, Mid-level, Senior, Manager, Director, or Executive.
	* `Hire date`: Enter the date you started employment.
	* `Years employed`: Enter the approximate number of years you have been employed.
3. **Plan Contribution Rate**
	* `401(k) Contribution Rate`: Enter your current contribution percentage (e.g., 5.5).
4. **Acknowledgment and Feedback**
	* `Policy acknowledgment`: Select `I acknowledge` to confirm you understand the 401(k) Plan Access Policy.
	* `Date of acknowledgment`: Enter the date you are signing this acknowledgment.
	* `Satisfaction`: Rate your satisfaction with the 401(k) Plan Access Policy: Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied.
	* `Communication rating`: Rate your communication from management regarding the plan: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
	* `Professional development`: Select your level of agreement with support for professional development: Strongly agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly disagree.
	* `Work-life balance`: Rate your work-life balance on a scale of 1-10.
	* `Improvement suggestions`: Provide any suggestions you may have for improvement.
	* `Additional comments`: Enter any additional comments or notes.
