# Parental Consent For Minors Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from a parent or legal guardian for a minor's participation in activities that involve some level of risk or require special handling.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with the parent or legal guardian's information.
2. Select "True" or "False" for the minor's participation in the activity.
3. Enter the parent or legal guardian's name and email address.
4. Enter the parent or legal guardian's phone number (if applicable).
5. Add any additional notes regarding the minor's participation (optional).
6. Sign and date the form to confirm consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Information** (`parent_information`, text, optional): Please fill out this field with the parent or legal guardian's name and any other relevant information.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, optional): Please enter the date on which the parent or legal guardian gave consent for the minor's participation.
* **Minor Participation** (`minor_participation`, select_multiple, optional): Select "True" if the minor will participate in the activity, and "False" otherwise.
* **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, text, optional): Please enter the parent or legal guardian's name.
* **Guardian Email** (`guardian_email`, email, optional): Please enter the parent or legal guardian's email address.
* **Guardian Phone** (`guardian_phone`, text, optional): If the parent or legal guardian has a phone number, please enter it here (if applicable).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Please add any additional notes regarding the minor's participation, if necessary.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, optional): Please enter the date of the parent or legal guardian's signature.
* **Signature** (`signature`, time, optional): Please sign to confirm that the parent or legal guardian is giving consent for the minor's participation.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Ensure that the parent or legal guardian's information is up-to-date and correct.
* Keep a record of the signed form for future reference.
