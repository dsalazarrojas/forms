# Mobile Health Study Data Use Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from participants to use their health data for research studies. It is essential to complete this form accurately and honestly to ensure that participants understand how their data will be used and to ensure the integrity of the research process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter participant information**: In this section, please provide your contact details (name, email, phone number, etc.) to enable us to communicate with you regarding the study.
2. **Choose Data Use Permissions**: Select all the options that apply to how you consent to use your health data. For example, you may choose to allow your data to be used for:
	* Option 1: General research purposes (e.g., improving health outcomes)
	* Option 2: Specific study objectives (e.g., studying a particular disease or condition)
	* Option 3: Other (please specify)
3. **Choose Study Involvement**: Select which study activities you are willing to participate in:
	* Option 1: Questionnaire or survey completion
	* Option 2: Clinical trial or experiment
	* Option 3: Other (please specify)
4. **Select Research Team Members**: Choose the research team members you are willing to work with:
	* Option 1: Primary investigator (PI)
	* Option 2: Co-investigator (co-PI)
	* Option 3: Other (please specify)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_info**:
	+ (text): Enter your participant information to enable us to contact you regarding the study.
* **contact_details**:
	+ (text): Enter your contact details (name, email, phone number, etc.) so we can communicate with you.
* **data_use_permissions**:
	+ (Select multiple): Choose all the options that apply to how you consent to use your health data.
* **study_involvement**:
	+ (Select one): Select which study activities you are willing to participate in.
* **research_team**:
	+ (Select multiple): Choose the research team members you are willing to work with.
* **goals**:
	+ (note): This is a place to provide additional information or context about your goals for participating in the study.
	(Note: This field is optional and intended for you to provide any additional context about your motivations for participating in the study.)
