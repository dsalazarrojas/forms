# DermaVirtualVisitsConsentForm - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather user information and consent for virtual visits. It is not a medical record and should not be used as such.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user information, including your name and contact details.
2. Provide your contact information, including your address and phone number.
3. Enter your patient information, including any relevant medical history or conditions.
4. Enter your emergency contact's name, relationship, and phone number.
5. Consent to the terms and conditions of virtual visits.
6. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`1`, `text`, required=false): This section collects your basic user information.
	+ Please fill in your name and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Information** (`2`, `text`, required=false): This section collects your contact details.
	+ Please fill in your address and phone number.
* **Patient Information** (`3`, `text`, required=false): This section collects patient information.
	+ Please provide any relevant medical history or conditions.
* **Emergency Contact** (`4`, `text`, required=false): This section collects emergency contact information.
	+ Please fill in your emergency contact's name, relationship, and phone number.
* **Consent Statement** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required=false): This section is for consent to the terms and conditions of virtual visits.
	+ Please select "agree" to confirm your consent.
* **Electronic Signature** (`6`, `select_one`, required=false): This section is for electronic signature.
	+ Please select "agree" to confirm your electronic signature.
* **Medical History** (`7`, `text`, required=false): This section is for any medical history, allergies, or previous surgeries.
	+ Please list any medical conditions, allergies, or previous surgeries.
* **Medical History Condition** (`8`, `text`, required=false): This section is for specific medical conditions.
	+ Please include the specific condition.
* **Medication** (`9`, `text`, required=false): This section is for any medication currently being taken or prescribed.
	+ Please list any medication currently being taken or prescribed.
* **Allergies** (`10`, `text`, required=false): This section is for any known allergies or sensitivities.
	+ Please list any known allergies or sensitivities.
* **Emergency Procedure** (`11`, `select_multiple`, required=false): This section is for emergency procedures.
	+ Please select any emergency procedures that may be necessary.
* **Medical Record Number** (`12`, `text`, required=false): This section is for your medical record number.
	+ Please enter the number of our system assigned to you for our records.
* **Insurance** (`13`, `text`, required=false): This section is for insurance information.
	+ Please fill in your insurance information.
* **Medical Record Number** (`14`, `text`, required=false): This section is for your medical record number.
	+ Please enter the number of our system assigned to you for our records.
* **Medical Provider** (`15`, `text`, required=false): This section is for your medical provider's name.
	+ Please enter your medical provider's name.
* **Medical Group** (`16`, `text`, required=false): This section is for your medical group's name.
	+ Please enter your medical group's name.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`17`, `text`, required=false): This section is for your emergency contact's name.
	+ Please fill in your emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`18`, `text`, required=false): This section is for your emergency contact relationship.
	+ Please enter the nature of your emergency contact relationship.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`19`, `text`, required=false): This section is for your emergency contact's phone number.
	+ Please fill in your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Consent 2** (`20`, `select_one`, required=false): This section is for consent to virtual visits.
	+ Please select "agree" to confirm your consent.
* **Consent 3** (`21`, `select_one`, required=false): This section is for consent to virtual visits.
	+ Please select "agree" to confirm your consent.
* **Patient Agreement** (`22`, `select_one`, required=false): This section is for patient agreement.
	+ Please select "agree" to confirm your patient agreement.
* **Signature Date** (`23`, `time`, required=false): This section is for signature date.
	+ This is not applicable as it's not provided in the form.
* **Digital Signature** (`24`, `text`, required=false): This section is for digital signature.
	+ Please sign with your digital signature.
* **Signature Time** (`25`, `time`, required=false): This section is for signature time.
	+ This is not applicable as it's not provided in the form.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are not duplicating an existing record.
* Please complete the form accurately and truthfully.
* Please select the correct options for each section.
* Please sign and date the form after completion.
