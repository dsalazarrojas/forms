# Photography Copyright Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from copyright owners to use, reproduce, or distribute their photographs. It captures the necessary information to establish the copyright owner's permission, including the photographer's name, address, the date of the release, authorized uses, and the copyright owner's signature.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the form, which is the title of the Photography Copyright Release Form.
2. Enter your name as the photographer or copyright owner.
3. Enter your address as the photographer or copyright owner.
4. Enter the date of the copyright release, using the date format (month/day/year).
5. Select the authorized uses for the photograph(s) from the provided options.
6. Sign the form to indicate your consent for the specified uses.
7. Date the form with the current date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Photography Copyright Release Form** (`photography_copyright_release_form_title`, text, required): This field is the title of the form, which is self-explanatory.
* **Photographer Name** (`photographer_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the photographer or copyright owner.
* **Copyright Owner** (`copyright_owner`, text, optional): This field is optional, but it's recommended to enter your name as the copyright owner.
* **Photographer Address** (`photographer_address`, text, optional): Enter your address as the photographer or copyright owner.
* **Date of Copyright Release** (`date_of_copyright_release`, date, required): Enter the date of the copyright release, using the date format (month/day/year).
* **Authorized Uses** (`authorized_uses`, select_multiple, optional): Select the authorized uses for the photograph(s) from the provided options. The options are:
	+ Print in newspapers and magazines
	+ Online publications
	+ Post on social media
	+ Reproduce or distribute copies
	+ Create derivative works
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required): Sign the form to indicate your consent for the specified uses.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Date the form with the current date.
