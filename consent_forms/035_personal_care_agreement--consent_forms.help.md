# Personal Care Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Care Agreement form is designed to collect essential information about a client's needs, medical requirements, and caregiver responsibilities to create a comprehensive care plan.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2. Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
3. Provide contact information for the client, including their email and phone number in the "Contact Information" field.
4. Check the "Responsible Caregiver" field to indicate if the client is responsible for their own care or if another person is responsible.
5. Enter the name of the person responsible for the client's care in the "Responsible Caregiver Name" field.
6. Select the specific needs that require attention, such as medical needs, caregiver needs, or other needs, from the "Client Needs", "Medical Needs", "Caregiver Needs", and "Other Needs" fields.
7. Provide additional details about each selected need in the "Needs Details" fields.
8. Review and agree to the "Agreement Terms" to ensure you understand the care plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Enter the client's name for reference purposes.
	+ Type: text
	+ Optional: no
* **Contact Information**: Enter the client's email and phone number for contact purposes.
	+ Type: text
	+ Optional: no
* **Responsible Caregiver**: Check yes or no to indicate if the client is responsible for their own care or if another person is responsible.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Optional: no
* **Responsible Caregiver Name**: Enter the name of the person responsible for the client's care.
	+ Type: text
	+ Optional: no
* **Email**: Enter the client's email for contact purposes.
	+ Type: email
	+ Optional: no
* **Phone**: Enter the client's phone number for contact purposes.
	+ Type: text
	+ Optional: no
* **Client Needs**: Select the specific needs that require attention.
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Optional: no
* **Medical Needs**: Select the specific medical needs that require attention.
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Optional: no
* **Caregiver Needs**: Select the specific needs that the caregiver requires.
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Optional: no
* **Other Needs**: Select any other needs that require attention.
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Optional: no
* **Needs Details**: Provide additional details about each selected need.
	+ Type: text
	+ Optional: no
* **Agreement Terms**: Review and agree to the care plan terms.
	+ Type: text
	+ Optional: no

## Tips

* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and approvals to collect this information from the client.
* Review each field carefully to ensure accurate and complete information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the care team for assistance.
