# Digital Health Education Film Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect participant information and consent for digital health education films. It is a required part of the filming process, ensuring that participants are aware of and agree to the terms of filming and data collection.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, using the following fields:
   * Participant Information: This field is used to gather basic participant information.
   * Contact Details: This field is used to collect participant contact information.
   * Consent: This field is used to indicate participant consent to filming, photo/video/audio, medical device usage, and participant permission. 
   * Participant Permission: This field is used to specify the type of participant permission (filming, photo/video/audio, medical device usage).
   * Additional Comments: This field is for any additional comments or questions about the participant.
   * Filming: This field is used to indicate if filming is allowed.
   * Filming End: This field is used to indicate the end time of filming.
   * Filming Start: This field is used to indicate the start time of filming.
   * Photo Video Audio: This field is used to specify if photo/video/audio is allowed.
   * Medical Device Usage: This field is used to specify if medical device usage is allowed.
   * Participant Signature: This field is used to collect participant signature.
   * Signature Date: This field is used to collect participant signature date.

* **Participant Information**: This field is used to gather basic participant information.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately before submitting.
* Review the fields carefully to ensure that you are indicating your consent and permission correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities or the person handling the filming session.
