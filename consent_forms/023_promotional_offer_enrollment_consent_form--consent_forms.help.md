# Promotional Offer Enrollment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to enroll users in promotional offers. It is meant for users who are new to the system and want to participate in offers. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure accurate and relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your First Name and Last Name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
2. Provide your Email address in the "Email" field to ensure we can contact you.
3. Enter a valid Phone number in the "Phone" field so we can contact you.
4. Select one or more offers you are interested in from the "Promotional Offer" field.
5. Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field. This will help us to ensure you meet the eligibility requirements for the offers.
6. If you would like to specify a Time of Enrollment, please enter it in the "Time of Enrollment" field. This is optional and will help us understand when you would like to participate in the offer.
7. Provide any additional Notes in the "Notes" field if you have any information you would like to include.
8. Choose the tool you would like to be assigned from the "Assigned Tool" dropdown.
9. If you want to, you can fill out the "Form ID" and "Category" fields for additional identification and organization.
10. Please enter a Description for your enrollment in the "Description" field.
11. Finally, enter the name of the output file in the "Output File" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Promotional Offer** (`promotional_offer`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more offers you are interested in.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth to ensure you meet the eligibility requirements.
* **Time of Enrollment** (`time_of_enrollment`, time, optional): If you would like to specify a time of enrollment, please enter it here.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional notes you would like to include.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required): Choose the tool you would like to be assigned.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, optional): Enter the form ID for additional identification.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Choose the category for your enrollment.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Enter a description for your enrollment.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, optional): Enter the output file name.

## Tips
Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date. This will help us to process your enrollment correctly. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
