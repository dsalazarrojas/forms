# Security Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Security Records Release Form is a critical document used to collect consent from individuals or organizations to release sensitive information related to security records. This form helps maintain transparency, trust, and legal compliance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields accurately and honestly.
2. Review the form for completeness before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Security Records Release Form**: This section is a placeholder for the form name and is not a field to fill out.
* **Organization**: Enter the name of the organization or entity that you are representing.
* **Individual Informed**: Select 'True' if the individual or organization being released has been informed about the release of their security records. If not, select 'False'.
* **Sensitive Information Available**: Select the types of sensitive information that are available for release, such as 'True', 'False', or 'unsure'. If unsure, please provide a brief explanation in the **Reason Release** field.
* **Release Information**: Provide a detailed explanation of the information being released, including any limitations or restrictions.
* **Reason Release**: Provide a clear and concise explanation of the reason for releasing the security records.
* **Date Release**: Enter the date when the security records are to be released.
* **Time Release**: Enter the time when the security records are to be released.
* **Contact Details**: Enter any contact details for follow-up or further information, such as a name, phone number, or email.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and honestly.
* Review the form for completeness before submission.
* Ensure that you have the necessary authority to sign on behalf of the individual or organization.
* If you are unsure about any field, please do not hesitate to contact the relevant authorities or seek clarification.
