# field_data_collection_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The field_data_collection_authorization_form is used to collect data from users for authorization purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by filling out the first page, which includes a single text field.
2. If necessary, proceed to the second page and select the desired options from the multiple-choice field.
3. On the third page, select the desired option from the single-choice field.
4. If you have any additional information to provide, you can do so on the fourth page in the form of a note.
5. Finally, on the last two pages, you can enter your email address and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **field_data_collection_authorization_form_first_page** (`field_data_collection_authorization_form_first_page`, text, optional):
	+ This is the first page of the form. Please enter any relevant information you have.
* **field_data_collection_authorization_form_second_page** (`field_data_collection_authorization_form_second_page`, select multiple, optional):
	+ This page allows you to select multiple options. Choose all that apply.
* **field_data_collection_authorization_form_third_page** (`field_data_collection_authorization_form_third_page`, select one, required):
	+ Select the option that best describes your status.
* **field_data_collection_authorization_form_date_of_birth** (`field_data_collection_authorization_form_date_of_birth`, date, optional):
	+ Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **field_data_collection_authorization_form_time_of_birth** (`field_data_collection_authorization_form_time_of_birth`, time, optional):
	+ Enter your time of birth in the format HH:MM am/pm.
* **field_data_collection_authorization_form_note** (`field_data_collection_authorization_form_note`, note, optional):
	+ Add any additional information you think would be helpful for us to know.
* **field_data_collection_authorization_form_email** (`field_data_collection_authorization_form_email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter your email address.
* **field_data_collection_authorization_form_phone** (`field_data_collection_authorization_form_phone`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your phone number.

## Tips
Please fill out the form as accurately and completely as possible. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
