# Smart Lighting Calibration Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Lighting Calibration Consent Form is a document that collects essential information and consent from relevant parties for Smart Lighting Calibration projects. This form ensures that all parties involved are aware of the project's scope, timeline, and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Category** field with the relevant category for this project (e.g., "Consents").
2. Provide a **Description** of the project in the corresponding field.
3. Enter the **Service Provider** responsible for the project.
4. Enter the **Property Owner** who will be impacted by the project.
5. Enter any **Approval Details** required for the project.
6. Add **Contact Details** for further communication and discussion.
7. Choose the **Assigned Tool** for this project (e.g., "chatjimmy").
8. Enter the **Date Signed** and **Time Signed** when the document was initially signed.
9. Enter the **Date Approved** and **Time Approved** when the document was approved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Smart Lighting Calibration Consent Form**: Enter the title of the form, which will be repeated at the top of each page.
* **Category**: Select the category for this form, typically "Consents".
* **Description**: Enter a brief description of the project, including its scope, timeline, and requirements.
* **Service Provider**: Enter the name of the service provider responsible for this project.
* **Property Owner**: Enter the name of the property owner who will be impacted by this project.
* **Approval Details**: Enter any additional approval details required for this project.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your contact information for further communication and discussion.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool assigned for this project (e.g., "chatjimmy" or "other").
* **Date Signed**: Enter the date when you initially signed this document.
* **Time Signed**: Enter the time when you initially signed this document.
* **Date Approved**: Enter the date when the document was approved.
* **Time Approved**: Enter the time when the document was approved.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about this project.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to ask for clarification or guidance.
* This form is designed to be used as a starting point, and you can always add or remove fields as needed.
* If you have any further questions or need assistance, contact: form@gic.mx.
