# Commencement Participation Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from students and graduates to confirm their participation in the commencement ceremony.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out each section accurately and thoroughly. The form is divided into several sections, each focusing on a different aspect of the information required. You can navigate through the form using the buttons at the top or bottom of each page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Graduate Full Name** (1, text, required): Enter your full legal name as it will appear on your diploma.
- **Student ID Number** (2, text, required): Enter your university student identification number.
- **Email Address** (3, email, required): Enter your primary contact email.
- **Phone Number** (4, text, required): Enter your contact phone number for ceremony updates.
- **Date of Birth** (5, date, required): Enter your date of birth for identification purposes.
- **Academic Information** (6, note, not required): This section provides a heading for the academic information below.
- **Degree Program** (7, text, required): Select your program of study.
- **Major** (8, text, required): Enter your primary field of study.
- **Minor** (9, text, not required): If applicable, enter your secondary field of study.
- **Graduation Term** (10, select_one, required): Select the term of your graduation.
- **Honors Designation** (11, select_one, required): Select any academic honors you have earned.
- **Ceremony Details** (12, note, not required): This section provides a heading for the ceremony details below.
- **Will You Attend Ceremony** (13, select_one, required): Confirm your attendance at the ceremony.
- **Ceremony Date** (14, date, required): Select the ceremony date.
- **Ceremony Location** (15, text, required): Enter the ceremony venue.
- **Cap and Gown Size** (16, select_one, required): Select the size of your cap and gown.
- **Guest Information** (17, note, not required): This section provides a heading for the guest information below.
- **Number of Guests** (18, number, required): Enter the number of guests attending.
- **Guest Tickets Needed** (19, number, required): Enter the number of tickets required.
- **Accessibility Needs for Guests** (20, text, not required): If needed, enter any accessibility accommodations for your guests.
- **Agreements and Consent** (21, note, not required): This section provides a heading for the agreements and consent below.
- **Participation Agreement** (22, select_one, required): Confirm your agreement to participate in the ceremony.
- **Conduct Agreement** (23, select_one, required): Confirm your agreement to follow ceremony conduct guidelines.
- **Photo and Video Release** (24, select_one, required): Confirm your agreement to allow photos and videos of the ceremony.
- **Name Pronunciation** (25, text, not required): If you prefer, provide guidance on how your name should be pronounced.
- **Emergency Contact** (26, note, not required): This section provides a heading for the emergency contact information below.
- **Emergency Contact Name** (27, text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Phone** (28, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
- **Relationship** (29, select_one, required): Select your relationship to the graduate.
- **Additional Information** (30, note, not required): This section provides a heading for any additional information.
- **Special Accommodations** (31, text, not required): If needed, enter any special requests or needs.
- **Additional Comments** (32, text, not required): Enter any other information you feel is relevant.

**Tips**

* Ensure you fill out each field accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any special needs or requests, please indicate them in the "Special Accommodations" field.
* If you need assistance with any field, do not hesitate to reach out to your university contact.
* Review each field for accuracy before submitting the form.
