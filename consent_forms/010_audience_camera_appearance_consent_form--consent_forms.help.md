# Audience Camera Appearance Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for audience participation in events where filming or recording is taking place. You will be asked to provide your personal details, event information, and consent to being recorded.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name exactly as it is written on your government-issued ID.
2. Provide the name of the event you are attending.
3. Fill in the date of the event.
4. Optionally, if you are not the event organizer, indicate the production company or organizer of the event.
5. Read and agree to the recording consent, indicating whether you consent to being filmed or recorded.
6. If you are a minor, provide your parent or guardian's name.
7. Enter your email address for a copy of this release.
8. Sign the form electronically using your full name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Attendee Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID. 
- **Event Information**: This section provides a note about the event. Please read and be aware of the event details.
- **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event you are attending.
- **Date of Attendance**: Fill in the date of the event.
- **Production Company / Organizer**: If you are not the event organizer, please fill in the name of the production company or event organizer.
- **Recording Consent**: Choose whether you consent to being filmed or recorded during the event.
  - If you choose "I do not consent", you will not be able to proceed with filming or recording.
- **Use of my Image, Likeness, and Voice**: Choose whether you authorize the use of your image, likeness, and voice for event recording.
  - If you choose "I do not authorize", this will be the default, and your image and likeness will not be used.
- **Is the participant a minor (under 18)?**: Indicate whether you are a minor or not.
  - If you are a minor, please provide the name of your parent or guardian.
- **Parent or Guardian Name**: If you are a minor, enter the name of your parent or guardian.
- **Email Address**: Enter your email address for a copy of this release.
- **Date of Agreement**: Fill in the date when you signed this form.
- **Electronic Signature (Full Name)**: Sign the form electronically using your full name.
