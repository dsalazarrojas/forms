# Waxing Appointment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect the necessary information for a waxing appointment from the client. Please complete this form carefully and thoroughly to ensure a smooth and successful appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Fill in your personal contact information, including your full name, service type you wish to receive, and medical information.
* If you have any allergies or medical conditions that may affect your appointment, please provide this information in the "Allergy Info" field.
* Enter your email address and emergency contact information to ensure we can reach you if needed.
* Confirm your risk information and sign the signature field to acknowledge that you have read and understood this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Your full name, as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Service Type**: Select the waxing service you wish to receive, such as Bikini Wax, Full Wax, or Trim.
* **Allergy Info**: If you have any allergies or medical conditions that may affect your appointment, such as a history of allergic reactions or medical procedures, please provide this information.
* **Medical Info**: Provide any relevant medical information that may be important for your appointment.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your contact information, including your address, phone number, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with appointment reminders and important updates.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Risk Info**: Provide any risk information that may be relevant to your appointment, such as a history of sensitive skin or medical conditions.
* **Signature**: Sign this field to acknowledge that you have read and understood this form and are aware of the risks associated with waxing.
