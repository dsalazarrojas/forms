# job_application_reference_check_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect a reference check consent from job applicants. It is used to gather necessary information and consent for a background check to be performed on the applicant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Review our <a href="https://www.example.com/privacy-policy">privacy policy</a> to understand what a reference check involves.
2. Answer the yes or no questions related to the reference check.
3. If you have an email address and/or phone number, please provide it for further contact.
4. Fill in the consent date and time.
5. Optionally, you can provide additional explanations for the reference check in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **job_application_reference_check_consent_form_page** (`text`, required: false): This is the label for the form, it is not a field that needs to be filled.
* **reference_check_consent** (`select_one`, required: true): Please answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate your consent for a background check.
* **email_address** (`email`, required: false): If you have an email address, please provide it for further contact.
* **phone_number** (`text`, required: false): If you have a phone number, please provide it for further contact.
* **consent_date** (`date`, required: true): Fill in the date you are giving consent for the background check.
* **consent_time** (`time`, required: false): Fill in the time you are giving consent for the background check.
* **reference_check_consent_explanation** (`text`, required: false): Please provide additional explanations for the reference check.
* **reference_check_consent_explanation_2** (`text`, required: false): This field can be used to provide additional information about your consent for the reference check.
* **reference_check_consent_explanation_3** (`text`, required: false): This field can be used to provide further details about your consent for the reference check.
* ... (all other fields are similar to the ones above)
