# hairVolumizingPowderApplicationConsentForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a consent form for a hair volumizing powder application treatment. It is designed to gather information from users about their consent and experience with the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Read the form carefully and make sure you have understood the information provided.
2. Fill out each field with the required information.
3. If you have any additional comments or concerns, use the "Additional Comments" field.

* **Consent** (required): Please type your understanding and agreement to the treatment process.
* **Salon Treatment** (required): Please provide details about your treatment experience, including dates.

* **Consent Date** (required): Type the date of your consent for the treatment.
