# Fat Cavitation Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fat Cavitation Client Intake Form is a comprehensive form used to collect information on a client's medical history, procedures, and consent for various medical practices in a healthcare setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's first and last name in the respective fields.
2. Provide the client's date of birth.
3. Enter the client's address.
4. Enter the client's phone number.
5. Enter the client's email address.
6. If applicable, enter any medical conditions the client is currently experiencing or has experienced in the past.
7. Enter the client's medical history.
8. List any medications the client is currently taking.
9. List any allergies the client has.
10. If applicable, provide information on the client's surgery history.
11. Enter any medical tests or exams performed on the client.
12. Enter the results of any medical tests or exams performed on the client.
13. Provide the date and time of any upcoming medical procedures.
14. Enter the name of the medical provider performing the procedure.
15. Provide detailed information on the medical procedure.
16. Enter any medical notes or comments.
17-24. Answer the consent questions accordingly, selecting "Yes" or "No" as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (first_name, text, required): Enter the client's first name.
* **Last Name** (last_name, text, required): Enter the client's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth, date, required): Enter the client's date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Address** (address, text, required): Enter the client's address.
* **Phone Number** (phone_number, text, required): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Email** (email, email, required): Enter the client's email address.
* **Medical Conditions** (medical_conditions, text, optional): If applicable, enter any medical conditions the client is currently experiencing or has experienced in the past.
* **Medical History** (medical_history, text, required): Enter the client's medical history.
* **Medications** (medications, text, required): List any medications the client is currently taking.
* **Allergies** (allergies, text, required): List any allergies the client has.
* **Surgery History** (surgery_history, text, optional): If applicable, provide information on the client's surgery history.
* **Medical Tests** (medical_tests, text, required): Enter any medical tests or exams performed on the client.
* **Medical Tests Results** (medical_tests_results, text, required): Enter the results of any medical tests or exams performed on the client.
* **Medical Procedure Date** (medical_procedure_date, date, required): Provide the date of any upcoming medical procedures.
* **Medical Procedure Time** (medical_procedure_time, time, required): Enter the time of any upcoming medical procedures.
* **Medical Provider** (medical_provider, text, required): Enter the name of the medical provider performing the procedure.
* **Medical Procedure Details** (medical_procedure_details, text, required): Provide detailed information on the medical procedure.
* **Medical Notes** (medical_notes, text, required): Enter any medical notes or comments.
* **Medical Consent 1** (medical_consent, select_multiple, required): Answer the first consent question accordingly.
* **Medical Consent 2** (medical_consent_2, select_multiple, required): Answer the second consent question accordingly.
* **Medical Consent 3** (medical_consent_3, select_multiple, required): Answer the third consent question accordingly.
* **Medical Consent 4** (medical_consent_4, select_multiple, required): Answer the fourth consent question accordingly.
* **Medical Consent 5** (medical_consent_5, select_multiple, required): Answer the fifth consent question accordingly.
* **Medical Consent 6** (medical_consent_6, select_multiple, required): Answer the sixth consent question accordingly.
* **Medical Consent 7** (medical_consent_7, select_multiple, required): Answer the seventh consent question accordingly.

## Tips

* Double-check the form for any incomplete or missing information before submitting.
* Ensure that you have reviewed and understood each field's purpose and relevance to the client's care.
* If you're unsure about any field or question, consult with a medical professional before proceeding.
