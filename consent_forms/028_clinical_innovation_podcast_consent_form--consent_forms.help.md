# Clinical Innovation Podcast Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Clinical Innovation Podcast Consent Form is a crucial part of the podcast production process. It ensures that participants understand and agree to the terms of their recording, including the use of their voice and image. This form is used to gather their consent and release any potential limitations or restrictions on the use of their recording.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name and professional title in the "Participant Full Name" and "Professional Title" fields, respectively.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Select the topic of the podcast episode in the "Podcast Episode Topic" field.
4. Choose a date for the recording in the "Scheduled Recording Date" field.
5. Review the consent statements and select "True" or "I consent" for each.
6. If you wish to limit the use of your recording, provide any specific topics you'd like to avoid.
7. Finally, select "True" or "I grant permission" for the use of your name and bio in promotional materials, and indicate whether you want to review the final edit before publishing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Full Name (required)
Your full name as it appears on your official documents.

### Professional Title (required)
Your official title or position.

### Email Address (required)
Your official email address.

### Organization (optional)
If you are representing an organization, provide its name.

### Podcast Episode Topic (required)
The topic of the podcast episode you will be participating in.

### Scheduled Recording Date (required)
Choose a date and time for the recording.

### Consent and Release --
This section includes the terms of your recording.

#### I consent to the recording of my voice and image for the podcast (required)
Check "True" if you give your consent.

### I agree that the production team owns all rights to the recording (required)
Check "True" to agree to this term.

### I understand that the recording may be edited for clarity and length (required)
Check "True" to indicate your understanding.

### I grant permission to use my name and bio in promotional materials (required)
Check "True" to grant permission.

### Limitations --
If you have specific topics you'd like to avoid, provide them here.

### Are there any specific topics you wish to avoid during the recording? (optional)
Provide any specific topics you'd like to avoid.

### Do you require a review of the final edit before publishing? (required)
Check "True" to request a review.

### Signature - Type Full Name (required)
Provide your full name as it appears on your official documents.

### Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Review the consent statements carefully before selecting "True" or "I consent".
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the production team.
* Keep a record of your completed form for your own reference.
