# School Trip Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Trip Permission Form is designed to obtain consent and necessary details from parents or guardians before their child participates in a school trip. This form helps schools ensure that all necessary information is collected and that students can attend trips safely and smoothly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Parent Details** fields with the parent or guardian's name and contact information.
2. Enter the **Student Details** fields with the student's name, student ID (if applicable), and any other relevant information.
3. Add the **Trip Details** fields with information about the trip, such as date, time, destination, and any other relevant details.
4. Sign and date the **Parent Signature** and **Student Signature** fields with your name to indicate your agreement to the trip details.
5. Check the **Consent** checkbox to confirm that you have read and understood the terms and conditions of the trip.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Details** (`parent_details`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the parent or guardian's name and contact information, such as phone number or email address.
* **Student Details** (`student_details`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the student's name, student ID (if applicable), and any other relevant information.
* **Trip Details** (`trip_details`, text, optional): This field is used to collect information about the trip, such as date, time, destination, and any other relevant details.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, text, optional): This field is used for the parent or guardian to sign and date their agreement to the trip details.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, text, optional): This field is used for the student to sign and date their agreement to the trip details.
* **Consent** (`consent`, text, optional): This checkbox is used for the parent or guardian to confirm that they have read and understood the terms and conditions of the trip.
