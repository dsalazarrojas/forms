# Academic Data Sharing Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather participant consent for sharing academic data, which includes their personal and research information. It's essential to fill out this form carefully to ensure that your data is handled according to your preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your full legal name in the `Participant name` field.
2.  Provide your email address in the `Contact email` field.
3.  Select all types of data that you're willing to have shared from the `Types of data to be shared` options.
4.  Specify the intended purpose and recipients of the data sharing in the `Purpose of data sharing` field.
5.  If you're willing, provide a data retention period in the `Data retention period` field.
6.  Choose whether you consent to anonymized analysis and reporting in the `Consent for analysis and reporting` field.
7.  If you wish to withdraw your consent, read and follow the instructions in the `Process to withdraw consent` field.
8.  Sign your name to confirm your consent in the `Signature confirmation` field.
9.  Enter the current date when you provided your consent in the `Date of consent` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Participant name** (`Participant name`, text, required): Enter your full legal name as it appears on your official documents.
*  **Contact email** (`Contact email`, email, required): Provide an email address that can be used to contact you about the data sharing.
*  **Types of data to be shared** (`Types of data to be shared`, select_multiple, required): Select all the types of data you're willing to have shared (Academic records, Research data, Demographic information, or Assessment outcomes).
*  **Purpose of data sharing** (`Purpose of data sharing`, text, required): Specify why you're consenting to data sharing (e.g., for research, publication, or collaboration).
*  **Data retention period** (`Data retention period`, text, optional): If you want to set a specific duration for which your data will be stored (e.g., until completion of the project, indefinitely).
*  **Consent for analysis and reporting** (`Consent for analysis and reporting`, select_one, required): Choose whether you consent to anonymized analysis and reporting (True or False).
*  **Process to withdraw consent** (`Process to withdraw consent`, note, optional): Read and follow the instructions for withdrawing your consent if you change your mind.
*  **Signature confirmation** (`Signature confirmation`, text, required): Sign your full name to confirm your consent.
*  **Date of consent** (`Date of consent`, date, required): Enter the date when you provided your consent.

## Tips
*  Review and understand what data will be shared before providing your consent.
*  Be precise in specifying the data retention period if you choose to set one.
*  Ensure your email address is up to date for any communication related to data sharing.
*  Read and understand the withdrawal process before signing the form.
