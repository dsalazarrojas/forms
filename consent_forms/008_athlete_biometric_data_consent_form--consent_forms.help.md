# Athlete Biometric Data Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Athlete Biometric Data Consent Form is used to gather biometric data from athletes, which will be used for performance optimization, injury prevention, and health monitoring. This form is a crucial document that ensures athletes understand how their data will be collected, stored, and used.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal information, including your name and date of birth.
2. Select the types of biometric data you are consenting to be collected.
3. Confirm the purpose of data collection.
4. Indicate your consent to the collection, storage, and sharing of your biometric data.
5. Confirm your understanding of the biometric data privacy policy.
6. Sign the form electronically, providing your full name and date of signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Full Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Team or Athletic Program**: Provide the name of your team or program.
* **Types of Data to be Collected**: Select all categories of biometric data you are consenting to be collected.
	+ Heart Rate and HRV (Heart Rate Variability)
	+ Sleep Patterns and Recovery
	+ GPS and Movement Tracking
	+ Body Composition (BMI, Body Fat)
	+ Blood Markers and Nutrition
	+ Biomechanical Mechanics
* **Purpose of Data Collection**: This is an optional field for you to describe the purpose of data collection.
* **I consent to the collection of the selected biometric data**: Select "True" to indicate your consent.
* **I consent to the secure storage of my biometric data**: Select "True" to indicate your consent to secure storage.
* **I consent to my performance data being shared with my coaches**: Select "True" to indicate your consent to sharing your performance data with coaches.
* **I consent to my health data being shared with team medical staff**: Select "True" to indicate your consent to sharing your health data with medical staff.
* **I consent to my anonymized data being used for internal research**: Select "True" to indicate your consent, and note that no personally identifying information will be included.
* **I acknowledge that data will be retained for the duration of my membership**: Select "I acknowledge" to confirm.
* **I have read and agree to the Biometric Data Privacy Policy**: Select "I agree" to confirm.

## Tips
Please note that you can select multiple categories for biometric data collection. Ensure you understand each category and its purpose before consenting. If you are under 18, please ensure a parent or guardian signs this form. If you are unsure about any of the fields or questions, please ask your coach or team staff for clarification.
