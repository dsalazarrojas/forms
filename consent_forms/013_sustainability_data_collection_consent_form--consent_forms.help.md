# Sustainability Data Collection Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for sustainability data collection purposes. It is intended to ensure that users have given explicit consent to collect their data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose one or more of the sustainability initiatives you're participating in (Climate change, Biodiversity, Water conservation, Energy efficiency, Waste reduction, Other (please specify)).
2. Select the data collection method you would like to be used (Online form, Mail-in form, Phone call, In-person survey, Other (please specify)).
3. Choose a method of contact (Email, Phone, Mail, Other (please specify)).
4. Confirm if you are willing to share your feedback form fields (True or False).
5. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sustainability Initiatives** (required: true, type: select_one): Select one or more of the sustainability initiatives you're participating in, such as Climate change, Biodiversity, Water conservation, Energy efficiency, or Waste reduction.
* **Data Collection Methods** (required: true, type: select_one): Choose a method of data collection that suits your needs, such as Online form, Mail-in form, or In-person survey.
* **Contact Info** (required: true, type: select_one): Please select a contact method for us to reach out to you, such as Email or Phone.
* **Feedback Form Fields** (required: true, type: select_one): Confirm if you're willing to share your feedback form fields, either True or False.
* **Review and Submit** (required: false, type: note): Double-check your selections before submitting the form. Ensure that all required fields are filled in.

## Tips
Please complete this form carefully and ensure that you're not collecting data from individuals or groups that have not given explicit consent. Use the "Other (please specify)" options if you need to provide additional information not listed in the standard options.
