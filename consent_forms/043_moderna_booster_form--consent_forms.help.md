# Moderna Booster Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Moderna Booster Form is used to collect personal details for the Moderna Booster program. This includes First Name, Email, Phone, and Date of Birth. Please note that this form is not intended for commercial purposes, but rather for the administration of the Moderna Booster program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the designated field. This field is optional but recommended for identification purposes.
2. Enter your **Email** address in the designated field. This field is optional but recommended for communication purposes.
3. Enter your **Phone** number in the designated field. This field is optional but recommended for scheduling purposes.
4. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the format of MM/DD/YYYY, using the calendar or keyboard input method provided. This field is optional but recommended for age verification purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your first name here. This will help us identify you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter your email address here. This will help us contact you for updates and scheduling.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your phone number here. This will help us contact you for scheduling.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional):
	+ Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY. This will help us verify your age.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* The information collected will be used solely for the administration of the Moderna Booster program.
* If you are eligible for a booster shot, we will contact you with scheduling options.
