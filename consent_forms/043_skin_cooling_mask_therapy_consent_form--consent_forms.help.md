# Skin Cooling Mask Therapy Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skin Cooling Mask Therapy Consent Form is designed to collect important information from clients before they undergo the skin cooling mask therapy treatment. This form is required for all clients to provide informed consent and to ensure their safety and well-being during the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Select the correct option for the "Consent" field.
3. Sign the form to acknowledge that you have read and understood the provided information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Health Details** (`client_health_details`, text, required): Please provide a brief description of your current health status, including any medical conditions you have or are experiencing.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required): Select one of the provided options to indicate your consent to undergo the skin cooling mask therapy treatment.
	+ Option 1: I understand and agree to the treatment.
	+ Option 2: I do not understand the treatment and need further explanation.
	+ Option 3: I decline the treatment.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, optional): If you wish to acknowledge that you have read and understood the provided information, please sign below.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please provide your email address so we can contact you regarding any updates or follow-ups.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please provide your phone number so we can contact you regarding any updates or follow-ups.
* **Client Information** (`client_info`, text, required): Please provide your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, required): Please list any medical conditions you have or are experiencing.
* **Medications** (`medication`, text, optional): Please list any medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): Please list any allergies you have.
* **Treatment Details** (`treatment_details`, text, required): Please provide a description of what to expect during the treatment and any questions or concerns you have.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Please provide a brief history of your medical history, including any previous illnesses or surgeries.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information in all fields.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask for further explanation.
* Please note that the treatment is only for clients in good health. If you have any medical concerns, please consult with your doctor before proceeding.
