# Hydromassage Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hydromassage Liability Waiver form is designed to inform and collect information from individuals who will be participating in a hydromassage session. This form helps ensure that participants are aware of the potential risks and liabilities involved in the treatment and are giving their informed consent to proceed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each section carefully and provide accurate information.
2. Answer all required questions to the best of your ability.
3. Fill out the form in a clear and legible manner.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General** (`general_informal`, text, optional): A brief statement explaining your general understanding and comfort level with the hydromassage session.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Please provide your full name as you would like it to be displayed on your waiver.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): This field is for providing your emergency contact information in case of any issues during or after the session.
* **Health Information** (`health_info`, text, optional): Please report any medical conditions, allergies, or concerns that we should be aware of.
* **Medical Provider Information** (`medical_provider_info`, text, optional): If you are under the care of a medical professional, please provide their contact information or a note from them.
* **Insurance Information** (`insurance_info`, text, optional): If you have insurance that covers the hydromassage session, please provide the relevant details.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately to ensure your safety and comfort during the session.
* If you are unsure about any question, don't hesitate to ask for clarification.
* If you have any medical conditions or concerns, please discuss them with your hydromassage provider before proceeding with the treatment.
