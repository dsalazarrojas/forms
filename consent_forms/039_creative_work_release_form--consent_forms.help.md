# creative_work_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for clients to provide information about a creative work, including its title, description, and creation date. It also captures the client's and artist's signatures, as well as their contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the "Work Title" field with the title of the creative work.
2. Provide a brief description of the work in the "Work Description" field.
3. Enter the "Date Of Creation" field with the date the work was created.
4. Enter the "Release Date" field with the date the work is being released.
5. Sign the "Client Signature" field with your name as the client.
6. Sign the "Artist Signature" field with your name as the artist.
7. Enter the "Date Signed" field with the date you signed the form.
8. If you have any contact information for the client, enter it in the "Client Address", "Client Phone", and "Client Email" fields.
9. You may provide an "Artist Statement" explaining the inspiration and creation process of the work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Work Title** (text): Enter the title of the creative work being released.
* **Work Description** (text): Provide a brief description of the creative work.
* **Client Name** (text): Enter your name as the client.
* **Date Of Creation** (date): Enter the date the work was created.
* **Release Date** (date): Enter the date the work is being released.
* **Client Signature** (text): Sign with your name as the client.
* **Artist Signature** (text): Sign with your name as the artist.
* **Date Signed** (date): Enter the date you signed the form.
* **Client Address** (text): Enter your contact address if you are the client.
* **Client Phone** (text): Enter your phone number if you are the client.
* **Client Email** (email): Enter your email address if you are the client.
* **Artist Statement** (text): Provide an explanation of the work's inspiration and creation process if desired.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) next to the field label.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, refer to the form's purpose and instructions above.
* This form is designed to be completed by the client, but the artist may also use it to provide information and sign their name.
* Make sure to sign both the client and artist signatures with your actual name and date the form accordingly.
