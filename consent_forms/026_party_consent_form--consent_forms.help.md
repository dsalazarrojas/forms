# Party Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Party Consent Form is used to collect consent and emergency contact information from parties involved in an event or activity. This information is essential for ensuring a safe and enjoyable experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Party Consent" section with a brief statement indicating that you understand and agree to the rules and procedures of the event or activity.
2. If applicable, provide the name and contact information for an emergency contact person.
3. If you have any special instructions or requirements, use the "Special Instructions" section to provide details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Party Consent** (party_consent, text, required): 
  Enter a statement indicating that you understand and agree to the rules and procedures of the event or activity.
* **Emergency Contact** (emergency_contact, text, optional): 
  Provide the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (emergency_contact_name, text, optional): 
  Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (emergency_contact_phone, text, optional): 
  Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (emergency_contact_email, email, optional): 
  Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Special Instructions** (special_instructions, text, optional): 
  Provide any special instructions or requirements that need to be considered.
* **Special Instructions Other** (special_instructions_other, text, optional): 
  If you have additional information that doesn't fit in the "Special Instructions" field, enter it here.
* **Attendee Consent** (attendee_consent, select_one, optional): 
  Select "Consent" if you agree to the rules and procedures, or "No Consent" if you don't. You can also select "Other (please specify)" and add a brief statement explaining your choice.
  *Please note that this field is not required, but it's essential to have a clear understanding of the attendee's consent.*

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields accurately and truthfully.
* If you're unsure about any part of the form, ask for clarification from the event organizer or activity leader.
* Keep the "Special Instructions" section concise and clear to ensure that your needs are communicated effectively.
* Remember that the information provided will be used for emergency contact and consent purposes only, so keep it private and secure.
