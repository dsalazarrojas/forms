# Brow Styling Gel Trial Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from participants in a brow styling gel trial to ensure their safety and understanding of the trial process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the following information for each field:

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`participant_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Phone Number** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for trial-related communication.
* **Email Address** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for trial-related communication.
* **Gel Trial Appointment Date** (`trial_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your scheduled gel trial.
* **Current Brow Type** (`brow_type`, select_one, required): Select your current brow type:
	+ Natural Brows
	+ Microbladed
	+ Tattooed
	+ Laminated
	+ Other
* **Have you used brow styling gel before** (`gel_experience`, select_one, required): Select how often you have used brow styling gel:
	+ Yes, regularly
	+ Yes, occasionally
	+ No, first time
* **Skin Type** (`skin_type`, select_one, required): Select your skin type:
	+ Oily
	+ Dry
	+ Combination
	+ Sensitive
	+ Normal
* **Known product allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): If you have any known allergies, please list them, including specific ingredients.
* **Skin sensitivities or conditions** (`sensitivities`, text, optional): If you have any skin sensitivities or conditions, please list them (e.g., rash, eczema, rosacea, etc.).
* **Current brow products used** (`current_products`, text, optional): If you use any brow products currently, please list them (e.g., brands or types).
* **Any medications affecting skin sensitivity** (`medications`, text, optional): If you are taking any medications that may affect your skin sensitivity, please list them.
* **Recent facial treatments** (`recent_treatments`, text, optional): If you have recently received any facial treatments, please list them (within the last 2 weeks).
* **Are you pregnant or nursing** (`pregnancy`, select_one, required): Select your current status:
	+ Yes, pregnant
	+ Yes, nursing
	+ False
* **Your expectations for this trial** (`expectations`, text, optional): Please describe your expectations for this trial.
* **Risks and benefits have been explained** (`risks_understood`, select_one, required): Select your understanding:
	+ Yes, I understand
	+ I have questions
* **I consent to participate in this gel trial** (`trial_consent`, select_one, required): Select your consent:
	+ Yes, I consent
	+ False
* **Date of Consent** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are signing this form.

## Tips
- Please answer each field carefully and honestly.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask the researcher or trial administrator for clarification.
- This form must be completed in its entirety before participating in the trial.
