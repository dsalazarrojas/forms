# Telehealth Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from patients for telehealth services.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out all required fields, including patient contact information and medical condition.
- Select the type of telehealth services you are consenting to receive (e.g. video, telephone, or email visit).
- Indicate your preferred method of signature (e.g. handwritten, E-Signature, or electronic signature).
- Enter your email address and phone number for communication purposes.
- If you are seeking services from a specific provider, enter their name and date of birth.
- If you have any concerns or questions, please do not hesitate to reach out to your healthcare provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, required/optional): This section collects your contact information, including name, address, city, state, zip code, and country.
  * Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
  * Enter your address, city, state, and zip code for billing and communication purposes.
  * Select your country from the list provided.

- **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, required/optional): This field captures the date on which you are consenting to receive telehealth services.
  * Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g. 01/01/2024).

- **Consent Time** (`consent_time`, time, required/optional): This field captures the time at which you are consenting to receive telehealth services.
  * Enter the time in the format HH:MM AM/PM (e.g. 02:30 PM).

- **Telehealth Services** (`telehealth_services`, select_one, required/optional): This section asks you to choose the type of telehealth services you are consenting to receive.
  * Select from the options "Video Visit", "Telephone Visit", or "Email Visit".

- **Consent Type** (`consent_type`, select_one, required/optional): This field indicates the type of consent you are giving.
  * Select either "General" or "HIPAA".

- **Signature Type** (`signature_type`, select_one, required/optional): This field determines the method of your signature.
  * Select from the options "Handwritten Signature", "E-Signature", or "Electronic Signature".

- **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): This field is used for communication purposes.
  * Enter your email address.

- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): This field is used for communication purposes.
  * Enter your phone number.

- **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required/optional): This field captures your medical condition.
  * Please enter your medical condition as it relates to the services you are consenting to receive.

- **Consent Statement** (`consent_statement`, note, required/optional): This field provides a statement to read and sign.
  * Please read and sign the consent statement provided.

- **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text, required/optional): This field is where you will sign your name as proof of consent.
  * Please sign your name.

- **Provider Signature** (`provider_signature`, text, required/optional): This field is for the healthcare provider to sign.
  * Please sign your name.

- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): This field captures your date of birth.
  * Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g. 01/01/1990).

- **Provider Name** (`provider_name`, text, required/optional): This field is for the healthcare provider to enter their name.
  * Please enter the name of your healthcare provider.

- **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to enter your name.
  * Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.

- **Medical Provider** (`medical_provider`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to enter the name of your medical provider.
  * Please enter the name of your healthcare provider.

- **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to enter your address.
  * Please enter your address for billing and communication purposes.

- **Patient City** (`patient_city`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to enter the city.
  * Please enter the city where you reside.

- **State** (`state`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to enter the state.
  * Please enter your state of residence.

- **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to enter the zip code.
  * Please enter your zip code for billing and communication purposes.

- **Patient Country** (`patient_country`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to enter the country.
  * Select your country from the list provided.

- **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to enter your phone number.
  * Please enter your phone number for communication purposes.

- **Patient Language** (`patient_language`, select_one, required/optional): This field is for you to select your language.
  * Select from the options "English" or "Spanish".

- **Telehealth Permission Date** (`telehealth_permission_date`, date, required/optional): This field captures the date of your consent.
  * Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g. 01/01/2024).

- **Telehealth Permission Time** (`telehealth_permission_time`, time, required/optional): This field captures the time of your consent.
  * Enter the time in the format HH:MM AM/PM (e.g. 02:30 PM).
