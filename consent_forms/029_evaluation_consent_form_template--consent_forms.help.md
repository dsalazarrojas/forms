# Evaluation Consent Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Evaluation Consent Form Template is a template used for obtaining participant consent for evaluations or assessments. This template is designed to gather necessary information from participants to conduct evaluations or assessments in a fair and transparent manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant's name, email, and phone number in the relevant fields.
2. Provide a clear and concise description of the risks and benefits associated with the evaluation or assessment.
3. Check the date and time when the participant signed the consent form.
4. Sign the consent form by providing your name and date.
5. Review and understand the information you are providing before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Evaluation Consent Form Template** (`evaluation_consent_form_template`): This field is for filling in a brief description of the evaluation or assessment being conducted.
* **Participant Label** (`evaluation_participant_label`): Enter a label or title for the participant, such as "Research Participant" or "Quality Control Subject".
* **Participant Role** (`evaluation_participant_role`): Provide a description of the participant's role or position, such as "Research Subject" or "Quality Control Specialist".
* **Evaluation Assessment Risks** (`evaluation_assessment_risks`): Describe the potential risks associated with the evaluation or assessment.
* **Evaluation Assessment Benefits** (`evaluation_assessment_benefits`): Describe the potential benefits or outcomes of the evaluation or assessment.
* **Consent** (`evaluation_consent`): Check the box to indicate that the participant has provided their consent for the evaluation or assessment.
* **Consent Date** (`evaluation_consent_date`): Fill in the date when the participant signed the consent form.
* **Consent Time** (`evaluation_consent_time`): Fill in the time when the participant signed the consent form.
* **Participant Name** (`evaluation_participant_name`): Enter the participant's name.
* **Participant Email** (`evaluation_participant_email`): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`evaluation_participant_phone`): Enter the participant's phone number.

## Tips
- Be honest and clear when describing the risks and benefits associated with the evaluation or assessment.
- Ensure that the participant understands the purpose and scope of the evaluation or assessment.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all necessary information is provided.
