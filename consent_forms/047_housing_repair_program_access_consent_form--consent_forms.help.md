# Housing Repair Program Access Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from a resident to access their housing repair program information. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and truthfully to ensure the resident's information is properly documented and to maintain the integrity of the housing repair program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Resident Name field with the resident's full name.
2. Enter the resident's contact number in the Contact Number field.
3. Provide the resident's email address in the Resident Email field.
4. Enter the resident's phone number in the Resident Phone field.
5. Enter the program manager's name in the Program Manager Name field.
6. Enter the program manager's phone number in the Program Manager Phone field.
7. Enter the program manager's email address in the Program Manager Email field.
8. Select 'yes' or 'no' from the Resident Consent field to indicate the resident's consent status.
9. In the Reason for Contact field, provide a brief explanation for the purpose of contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resident Name** (`resident_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the resident's full name as they appear on their identification documents.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the resident's contact number where they can be reached for further communication.
* **Resident Email** (`resident_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the resident's email address for electronic communication.
* **Resident Phone** (`resident_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the resident's phone number where they can be reached for further communication.
* **Program Manager Name** (`program_manager_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the program manager responsible for this resident's housing repair program.
* **Program Manager Phone** (`program_manager_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the program manager's phone number where they can be reached for further communication.
* **Program Manager Email** (`program_manager_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the program manager's email address for electronic communication.
* **Resident Consent** (`resident_consent`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select 'yes' to indicate the resident's consent to access their housing repair program information, 'no' if they decline.
* **Reason for Contact** (`reason_for_contact`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief explanation for the purpose of contact with the resident, such as for repairs, maintenance, or program updates.
