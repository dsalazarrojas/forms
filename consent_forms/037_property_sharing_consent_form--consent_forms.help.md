# property_sharing_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from property owners or administrators for the sharing of their property information, such as contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form's purpose and ensure that it's relevant to your current situation.
2. Select the correct answer to the "property_type" question, which will indicate whether the property is being shared or not.
3. Choose the individuals or parties you are sharing the property's details with by selecting all that apply from the "sharer" options.
4. Enter the name of the individual or party sharing the property's details in the "sharer_name" field.
5. Enter the phone number of the individual or party sharing the property's details in the "sharer_phone" field.
6. Enter the email address of the individual or party sharing the property's details in the "sharer_email" field.
7. Optionally, enter any additional information about the individual or party sharing the property's details in the "sharer_phone_number", "sharer_email_suffix", and "sharer_phone_extension" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **property_sharing_consent** (`property_sharing_consent`, text, optional):
	+ This field is where you enter your consent to share your property's details.
* **property_type** (`property_type`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select "Yes" if you are sharing the property's details, "No" otherwise.
* **sharer** (`sharer`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select all that apply, but only those who will be sharing the property's details.
* **sharer_name** (`sharer_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the individual or party sharing the property's details.
* **sharer_phone** (`sharer_phone`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the phone number of the individual or party sharing the property's details.
* **sharer_email** (`sharer_email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter the email address of the individual or party sharing the property's details.
* **sharer_phone_number** (`sharer_phone_number`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any additional information about the individual or party sharing the property's details.
* **sharer_email_suffix** (`sharer_email_suffix`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any additional information about the individual or party sharing the property's details.
* **sharer_phone_extension** (`sharer_phone_extension`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any additional information about the individual or party sharing the property's details.

## Tips

* Make sure to clearly understand who will be sharing your property's details before completing this form.
* Enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure seamless communication with the selected sharers.
