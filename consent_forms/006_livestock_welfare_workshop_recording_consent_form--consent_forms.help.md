# Livestock Welfare Workshop Recording Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed for workshop participants to provide their consent for their session to be recorded. It's necessary for maintaining accurate and informed consent records for the Livestock Welfare Workshops.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Start by filling out your participant name, then enter a valid contact phone number or email address that can be used to contact you if needed.
*   Check the appropriate option for recording consent, which allows our team to record your session.
*   Provide additional notes about your consent, if any further explanation is required.
*   Enter the date of the workshop you attended, if you're filling out this form at a later time.
*   If relevant, enter the time of the workshop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Name** (participant_name, text, required):
    Enter your name as it appears on your identity documents or as you prefer to be referred to during the workshop.
*   **Contact Phone/Email** (contact_phone, text, optional):
    A valid phone number or email address that can be used to contact you if needed. This can include your personal or work contact information.
*   **Recording Consent** (recording_consent, select_one, optional):
    This field allows you to choose whether you give your consent to be recorded. Please select "consent" if you agree to be recorded.
*   **Consent Note** (consent_note, note, optional):
    If there's anything you'd like to note about your consent, please enter it here. This can include any specific concerns or requests.
*   **Workshop Date** (workshop_date, date, optional):
    Enter the date of the workshop you attended. This is helpful for reference purposes.
*   **Workshop Time** (workshop_time, time, optional):
    Enter the time of the workshop you attended, if known.
