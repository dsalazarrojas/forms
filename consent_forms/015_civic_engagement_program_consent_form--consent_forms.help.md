## Civic Engagement Program Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information and obtain consent for the Civic Engagement Program. It is essential to complete this form accurately and truthfully.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the participant information section, including name, date of birth, and email address.
2. Answer the questions about your participation in the program, including the program duration and your understanding of the program description.
3. Read and understand the consent and acknowledgment sections, including the risks involved and your agreement to participate.
4. Fill out the emergency contact information section, including the emergency contact name and phone number.
5. Review the policy and conduct agreement section and indicate your agreement to follow the program rules.
6. Sign the final agreement, certifying that the information is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Please enter your date of birth in the standard format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Is participant under 18 years old** (`is_minor`, `select_one`, required): If you are 18 years old or older, select "No, 18 or older". If you are under 18, select "Yes, under 18". Please note that if you are under 18, you will need to fill out the parent or guardian section.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Please enter your email address as it appears on your identification.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Please enter your phone number as it appears on your identification.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, `text`, required): Please enter the name of the program you are participating in.
* **Program Duration** (`program_duration`, `select_one`, required): Please select the duration of the program you are participating in. Options include Single event, Weekend program, Week-long program, Monthly program, Full semester, and Full year.
* **Program Description Understood** (`program_description`, `select_one`, required): Please indicate whether you understand the goals and activities of the program.
* **Consent to Participation** (`participation_consent`, `select_one`, required): Please indicate your consent to participate in the program.
* **Risks Acknowledged** (`risk_acknowledgment`, `select_one`, required): Please indicate whether you understand and acknowledge any risks involved with the program.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Please enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Please enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship to Participant** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required): Please describe your relationship to the participant.
* **Agree to Rules and Conduct** (`rules_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Please indicate your agreement to follow the program rules.
* **Liability Waiver** (`liability_waiver`, `select_one`, required): Please indicate your agreement to release the organization from liability for injuries or damages.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, required if participant is under 18): Please enter the name of the parent or guardian if you are under 18 years old.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature Agreement** (`parent_signature_agreement`, `select_one`, required if participant is under 18): Please indicate your agreement to all terms on behalf of the minor participant.
* **Final Agreement** (`final_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Please certify that the information is accurate and complete.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you are under 18 years old, make sure to fill out the parent or guardian section accurately.
* If you are not sure about any of the questions, ask the program administrators for clarification.
* This form is designed to be completed in sections, with clear labels and instructions for each field. Take your time to review each section carefully before proceeding.
