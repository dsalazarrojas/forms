# Athletic League Safety Policy Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a mandatory acknowledgment of the safety policies and procedures of the athletic league. It is designed to ensure that participants are aware of and agree to follow the safety guidelines and protocols to prevent injuries and illnesses during athletic activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your full name and date of birth.
2. Select your role in the league (e.g., Athlete, Parent or Guardian, Coach, etc.).
3. Choose the primary sport you will be participating in.
4. Select the season and year you will be participating in.
5. Acknowledge that you have reviewed the safety policies and procedures, including concussion protocols, heat illness prevention, and emergency action plans.
6. Commit to prioritizing safety in all activities.
7. If you are a parent or guardian, provide your name and signature.
8. Sign the form to confirm that you have reviewed and agreed to the safety policies and procedures.
9. Date the form with the date of your signature.
10. If applicable, list any questions or concerns you have.
11. If you require additional safety training, choose the type of training you need.
