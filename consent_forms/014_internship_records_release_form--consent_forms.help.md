# Internship Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through completing the Internship Records Release Form, which is used to collect information from students regarding the release of their internship records.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by inputting the student's name in the "Student Name" field (id 1).
2. Enter the student's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field (id 2).
3. Provide the student's email address in the "Email" field (id 3).
4. Enter the student's ID number in the "Student ID" field (id 4).
5. Enter the name of the organization the student is working with in the "Organization" field (id 5).
6. Enter the department within the organization in the "Department" field (id 6).
7. Select the type of records being released in the "Consent Type" field (id 7) - choose between "Internship Records" or "Academic Records".
8. Input the date the records are to be released in the "Release Date" field (id 8).
9. Sign the form with the student's signature in the "Signature" field (id 9).
10. Add any additional comments or explanations in the "Notes" field (id 10).
11. Provide the student's contact number in the "Contact Number" field (id 11).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Student Name (id 1)
* Input the student's full name as it appears on their records.

### Date of Birth (id 2)
* Enter the student's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Email (id 3)
* Enter the student's valid email address.

### Student ID (id 4)
* Enter the student's unique ID number as assigned to them.

### Organization (id 5)
* Enter the name of the organization the student is working with.

### Department (id 6)
* Enter the department within the organization the student is working with.

### Consent Type (id 7)
* Select "Internship Records" to release records related to the internship or "Academic Records" to release academic records.

### Release Date (id 8)
* Input the date when the records are to be released.

### Signature (id 9)
* Sign the form with the student's signature.

### Notes (id 10)
* Add any additional comments or explanations about the records release process.

### Contact Number (id 11)
* Enter the student's contact number, which can be used for further communication.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are filled accurately and completely.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it.
- If any field is unclear, contact the form administrator for assistance.
