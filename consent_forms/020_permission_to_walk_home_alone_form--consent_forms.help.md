# Permission To Walk Home Alone Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain permission from parents/guardians for a student to walk home alone after school.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Ensure you are a parent/guardian with permission to complete this form for your child.
2. Select your child's school from the "School" field.
3. Enter your name and your child's name in the "Parent's Name" field.
4. Enter your child's school's address (if applicable) in the "School Address" field.
5. Enter your child's phone number and your school administrator's phone number (if applicable) in the respective fields.
6. Enter a brief reason for your child's ability to walk home alone in the "Reason for walking home" field (if applicable).
7. Review your completed form for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### School
* **School** (`school_name`, required): Enter the name of your child's school.
* **Parent's Name** (`prophets_name`, required): Enter your name and your child's name.
* **School Address** (`school_name`, optional): Enter your child's school's address (if applicable).
* **Phone Number** (`prophets_name`, optional): Enter your child's phone number.
* **Parent's Phone Number** (`prophets_name`, required): Enter your child's parent's phone number.
* **School Administrator** (`school_administrators`, optional): Enter your school administrator's name.
* **Reason for walking home** (`prophets_name`, optional): Enter a brief reason for your child's ability to walk home alone (if applicable).

## Tips
* Ensure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your school administrator.
* Once you have submitted the form, your child will be able to walk home alone after school.
