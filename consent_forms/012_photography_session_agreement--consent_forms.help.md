# Photography Session Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information and receive consent from clients for a photography session. It includes fields for session details, session location, and other essential information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your accurate and complete information.
2. Review the information to ensure accuracy before submitting.
3. Sign and submit the form to complete the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Coverage** (<code>session_coverage</code>, <type>text</type>, required:false): 
Describe the type of coverage you would like to have for your session (e.g., portrait, wedding, etc.)
* **Session Location** (<code>session_location</code>, <type>text</type>, required:false): 
Where would you like the session to take place (e.g., indoors, outdoors, studio, etc.)
* **Session Date** (<code>session_date</code>, <type>date</type>, required:false): 
Select a date for your photography session
* **Session Time** (<code>session_time</code>, <type>time</type>, required:false): 
Choose a time for your photography session (e.g., morning, afternoon, evening)
* **Session Cost** (<code>session_cost</code>, <type>number</type>, required:false): 
This is the estimated cost for your photography session
* **Coverage Terms** (<code>coverage_terms</code>, <type>text</type>, required:false): 
Describe any specific terms or requests you have for the coverage of your session
* **Terms And Conditions** (<code>terms_and_conditions</code>, <type>text</type>, required:false): 
Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the photography session
* **E Signature** (<code>e_signature</code>, <type>email</type>, required:false): 
Provide your email address for communication and confirmation purposes
