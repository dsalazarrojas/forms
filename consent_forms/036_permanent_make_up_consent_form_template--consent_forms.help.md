# Permanent Make Up Consent Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form template is intended to be used for obtaining permanent makeup (e.g. micropigmentation, tattooing) client consents. It is designed to ensure that clients understand and agree to the terms and conditions of the procedure, including the use of untested treatments and products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field (page 1).
2. Enter the client's phone number in the "Client Phone" field (page 2), if provided.
3. Enter the client's email address in the "Client Email" field (page 2), if provided.
4. Enter the client's address in the "Address" field (page 3).
5. Select all applicable statements that you understand and agree to from the "Consent" field (page 4).
6. Sign and date the "Signature" field (page 5).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Enter the client's full name as it appears on their identification.
* **Client Phone**: Enter the client's phone number, if available.
* **Client Email**: Enter the client's email address, if available.
* **Address**: Enter the client's full address.
* **Consent**: Select all applicable statements that you understand and agree to, such as "I understand that my signature indicates that I have read and will comply with all of the following" and "I acknowledge that all treatments and products used in my visit are not tested on animals." Make sure to read and agree to all statements before submitting the form.
* **Signature**: Sign and date the form to indicate your agreement to the terms and conditions. This is a required field, but please note that your signature is not the same as a legal signature; it's a consent confirmation. If you're unsure about any statement, please ask your technician for guidance.

## Tips
* Make sure to review all fields carefully before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any statement in the "Consent" field, please ask your technician for clarification.
* Keep in mind that your signature is a consent confirmation, not a legal signature.
