# Risk Mitigation Reporting Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for participants in risk mitigation reporting activities to provide their consent and details. It is used to gather essential information from participants, ensuring they understand the reporting process and agree to the terms. The form helps maintain transparency and accountability in risk mitigation efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Participant Name field with your full name.
2. Enter your Participant Email address.
3. Provide your Participant Organization, if applicable.
4. Select a Reporting Reason for submitting this form.
5. Confirm your consent by choosing "I understand and agree" from the Consent field.
6. Optionally, add Additional Comments if you have any further details to include.
7. Sign the form with your digital signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`risk_mitigation_reporting_consent_form_participant_name`): Your full name, as it appears on official documents.
* **Participant Email** (`risk_mitigation_reporting_consent_form_participant_email`): Enter your email address where you can be contacted for updates or further questions.
* **Participant Organization** (`risk_mitigation_reporting_consent_form_participant_organization`): If you are representing an organization, enter its name and contact information.
* **Reporting Reason** (`risk_mitigation_reporting_consent_form_reporting_reason`): Briefly describe the reason for submitting the risk mitigation report.
* **Consent** (`risk_mitigation_reporting_consent_form_consent`): Confirm that you understand and agree to the terms of the reporting process. Select "I understand and agree" to indicate your consent.
* **Additional Comments** (`risk_mitigation_reporting_consent_form_additional_comments`): If you have any further information or concerns, type them here.
* **Signature** (`risk_mitigation_reporting_consent_form_signature`): Provide a digital signature, indicating your agreement to the terms.

## Tips
* Please fill out this form thoroughly and accurately, as the information collected is crucial for effective risk mitigation.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant authorities or support personnel.
* Ensure your digital signature is clear and legible for verification purposes.
