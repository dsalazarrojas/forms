# Employee Photo Newsletter Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather consent from employees to use their photo in various types of publications. Please review the form carefully to ensure you are providing accurate information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Fill out your name and other contact information in the "Contact Preference" and "Email Address" fields.
2. Choose your consent preferences for each type of publication using the "Printed Publications", "Online Publications", and "Video and Audio" sections.
3. If you have specific photo usage details, such as project names or event dates, please enter them in the "Photo Usage Details" field.
4. If you hold the copyright to your photo, select "Self" in the "Copyright Holder" field. If you do not hold the copyright, select "Employer" and provide your employer's name in the "Photo Copyright" field.
5. Finally, review your responses and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Photo Newsletter Release Form** (Page 1):
	+ This is the title of the form.
* **I agree to use my photo** (Page 2):
	+ Check "True" to indicate that you agree to use your photo.
* **Printed Publications** (Page 3):
	+ Select "Newsletters" if you agree to use your photo in employee newsletters.
	+ Select "Employee Handbook" if you agree to use your photo in the employee handbook.
	+ Select "Other" if you agree to use your photo in other printed publications.
* **Online Publications** (Page 4):
	+ Select "Website" if you agree to use your photo on the company website.
	+ Select "Social Media" if you agree to use your photo on social media.
	+ Select "Email" if you agree to use your photo in email communications.
* **Video and Audio** (Page 5):
	+ Select "Video" if you agree to be recorded or appear on video.
	+ Select "Audio" if you agree to be recorded or have your voice used in audio recordings.
* **Photo Usage Details** (Page 6):
	+ If you have specific photo usage details, such as project names or event dates, enter them here.
* **Photo Copyright** (Page 7):
	+ If you hold the copyright to your photo, select "Self". If you do not, select "Employer" and provide your employer's name.
* **Copyright Holder** (Page 8):
	+ If you hold the copyright to your photo, select "Self".
* **Contact Preference** (Page 9):
	+ Select your preferred contact method for future communications.
* **Email Address** (Page 10):
	+ Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (Page 11):
	+ Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

Please note that this form is not intended for external use, and all responses will be reviewed internally. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to your HR department.
