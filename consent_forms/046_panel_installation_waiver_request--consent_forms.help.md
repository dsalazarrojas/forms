# Panel Installation Waiver Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a waiver for panel installation, which allows customers or employees to have panels installed on their premises or property. It is an important step in the panel installation process, ensuring that all necessary information is gathered before proceeding with the installation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your first name in the field labeled "first name".
2. Enter your last name in the field labeled "last name".
3. Enter your email address in the field labeled "email".
4. Enter your phone number in the field labeled "phone".
5. Choose whether you accept the panel installation waiver by selecting "True" or "False" in the field labeled "I accept the panel installation waiver".
6. Check the terms and conditions box by selecting "True" or "False" in the field labeled "terms and conditions".
7. If desired, provide additional emergency contact information in the fields labeled "emergency contact" and "emergency phone".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (first_name, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (last_name, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (phone, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **I Accept the Panel Installation Waiver** (waiver_acceptance, select_one, required): Choose "True" to accept the waiver or "False" to decline.
* **Terms and Conditions** (terms_and_conditions, select_multiple, required): Check the box to accept the terms and conditions.
* **Emergency Contact** (emergency_contact, text, optional): If you have an emergency contact person, enter their name or title.
* **Emergency Phone** (emergency_phone, text, optional): If you have an emergency contact person, enter their phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure the form is complete and can be processed.
* If you have an emergency contact person, make sure to fill out both the "emergency contact" and "emergency phone" fields.
* Review the terms and conditions before checking the box to accept them.
* If you need help or have questions, contact the panel installation team for assistance.
