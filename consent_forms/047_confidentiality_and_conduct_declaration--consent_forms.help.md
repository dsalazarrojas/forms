# Confidentiality And Conduct Declaration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your understanding and commitment to confidentiality, conduct, and data protection policies. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and truthfully to ensure a clear understanding of your role and responsibilities within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your full legal name, email address, and contact phone number in the corresponding fields.
2. Complete your address, city, state, and ZIP Code in the address fields.
3. Identify your organization or company, your department, and your job title.
4. Select the purpose of your declaration from the provided options.
5. Enter the date of your declaration.
6. Acknowledge your obligations under the confidentiality and conduct policies.
7. Identify the types of confidential information you will handle.
8. Describe the measures you will take to protect such information.
9. Select the conduct standards you agree to uphold.
10. Confirm that you have reviewed the code of conduct.
11. Indicate whether you have completed ethics training.
12. Select the purpose of your declaration.
13. Describe any potential conflicts of interest.
14. Confirm that you understand the rules on gifts and hospitality.
15. Confirm that you understand your GDPR privacy obligations.
16. Confirm that you have reviewed the social media policy.
17. Indicate that you know the process for reporting violations.
18. Confirm that you understand your whistleblower protection rights.
19. Confirm that you understand the disciplinary actions that may be taken.
20. Confirm that you have no questions or concerns.
21. Declare that you understand and affirm the confidentiality and conduct policies.
22. Commit to upholding the conduct standards.
23. Sign the declaration statement.
24. Confirm that your supervisor or manager has approved this declaration.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Legal Name** (`declarant_name`, Text, Required): Enter your complete name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Email Address** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter your work email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, Text, Required): Enter your work phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, Text, Required): Enter your complete address.
* **City** (`city`, Text, Required): Enter the city where you reside or work.
* **State** (`state`, Text, Required): Enter the state where you reside or work.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, Text, Required): Enter your postal code.
* **Organization** (`organization`, Text, Required): Enter your company or institution name.
* **Department** (`department`, Text, Optional): Enter your department name (if applicable).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, Text, Required): Enter your job title or position.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, Text, Optional): Enter your employee ID number (if applicable).
* **Purpose of Declaration** (`declaration_purpose`, Select One, Required): Select the purpose of your declaration from the provided options (e.g., New Employment, Project Access, Annual Renewal, etc.).
* **Declaration Date** (`declaration_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date of your declaration.
* **Confidentiality Acknowledgment** (`confidentiality_acknowledgment`, Select One, Required): Acknowledge your obligations under the confidentiality policy.
* **Types of Confidential Information** (`confidential_info_types`, Select Multiple, Required): Identify the types of confidential information you will handle (e.g., Trade Secrets, Customer Information, Financial Data, etc.).
* **Protection Measures** (`protection_measures`, Text, Required): Describe the measures you will take to protect confidential information.
* **Conduct Standards** (`conduct_standards`, Select Multiple, Required): Select the conduct standards you agree to uphold (e.g., Professional Behavior, Respectful Communication, No Harassment, etc.).
* **Code of Conduct Review** (`code_of_conduct`, Select One, Required): Confirm that you have reviewed the code of conduct.
* **Ethics Training** (`ethics_training`, Select One, Required): Indicate whether you have completed ethics training.
* **Training Completion Date** (`training_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date you completed ethics training (if applicable).
* **Conflict of Interest** (`conflict_of_interest`, Select One, Required): Identify whether you have any potential conflicts of interest.
* **Conflict Details** (`conflict_details`, Text, Optional): Describe any potential conflicts of interest (if applicable).
* **Outside Employment** (`outside_employment`, Text, Optional): Describe any other work activities you are engaged in (if applicable).
* **Gifts and Hospitality** (`gifts_hospitality`, Select One, Required): Confirm that you understand the gifts and hospitality policy.
* **Data Protection** (`data_protection`, Select One, Required): Confirm that you understand your GDPR privacy obligations.
* **Social Media Policy** (`social_media`, Select One, Required): Confirm that you have reviewed the social media policy.
* **Reporting Violations** (`reporting_violations`, Select One, Required): Indicate that you know the process for reporting violations.
* **Whistleblower Protection** (`whistleblower_protection`, Select One, Required): Confirm that you understand your whistleblower protection rights.
* **Disciplinary Action Understanding** (`disciplinary_action`, Select One, Required): Confirm that you understand the disciplinary actions that may be taken.
* **Any Questions** (`questions`, Text, Optional): If you have any questions or concerns, enter them here.
* **Declaration Statement** (`declaration_statement`, Select One, Required): Declare that you understand and affirm the confidentiality and conduct policies.
* **Commitment Statement** (`commitment_statement`, Select One, Required): Commit to upholding the conduct standards.
* **Date of Signature** (`signature_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date of your signature.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of your supervisor or manager.
* **Supervisor Approval** (`supervisor_approval`, Select One, Required): Confirm that your supervisor or manager has approved this declaration.

## Tips

* Please fill in the form accurately and truthfully.
* Review each field carefully to ensure you understand the requirements.
* If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to ask your supervisor or manager for clarification.
* Ensure that you have reviewed and understood all the policies and procedures mentioned in this form.
* This form is a legally binding agreement, and failure to complete it accurately and truthfully may result in disciplinary actions.
