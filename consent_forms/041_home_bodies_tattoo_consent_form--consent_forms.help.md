# Home Bodies Tattoo Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your medical history and tattoo preferences to ensure a safe and successful tattooing process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer all questions thoroughly, but accurately. 
- If a question does not apply to you, please select "None" for that field or indicate that it does not apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Tattoo and Covid Consent**: This is a free text field where you can provide any information about your consent for the tattoo procedure and any questions or concerns you have.
*  **Tattoo Details**: This field is for you to provide any details about your planned tattoo, such as size, color, and design.
*  **Tattoo Artist**: Please provide the name of your tattoo artist or the artist you plan to work with.
*  **Tattoo Size**: If you have a specific size in mind for your tattoo, please select the corresponding number from the options provided.
*  **Tattoo Color**: Choose one or more colors you are interested in for your tattoo from the options provided.
*  **Tattoo Location**: Select all locations on your body that you are interested in getting a tattoo from the provided options.
*  **Tattoo Design**: This is a free text field where you can provide any details about your tattoo design.
*  **Covid Vaccine Status**: Please select your current status regarding Covid vaccination.
*  **Emergency Contact**: Please provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact in case of an emergency during the tattooing process.
*  **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Please describe your relationship with the emergency contact.
*  **Medical Condition**: If you have any medical conditions, please select one or more from the provided options.
*  **Medical Condition Details**: Provide any additional information about your medical conditions.
*  **Contact Phone**: Please provide your contact phone number.
*  **Contact Email**: Please provide your email address.
*  **Medical Supplements**: This is a free text field where you can provide any information about your medical supplements.

Note: Please review this form carefully and answer all questions accurately to ensure your safety during the tattooing process. If you are unsure about any question, do not hesitate to ask your tattoo artist for clarification.
