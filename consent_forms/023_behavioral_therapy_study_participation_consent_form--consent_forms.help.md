# Behavioral Therapy Study Participation Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain participants' consent for a behavioral therapy study. It's essential to understand the study's purpose, procedures, risks, and benefits before participating. By completing this form, you will be providing informed consent to participate in the study.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant information, including your name, age, phone number, and email address.
2. Provide the study title, principal investigator's name, and investigator contact information.
3. Select the expected study duration.
4. Understand the study's purpose and procedures.
5. Select your time commitment per session.
6. Optionally, provide the number of sessions expected.
7. Acknowledge the potential risks and benefits of participation.
8. Confirm your understanding of the risks and benefits.
9. Select your agreement to participate voluntarily and withdraw at any time.
10. Confirm that you consent to participate in the study.
11. Confirm your agreement to confidentiality and data usage.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name (Participant Name)**: Enter your full name as you would like it to be recorded.
* **Age (Participant Age)**: Enter your age in numeric form.
* **Phone Number (Participant Phone Number)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email Address (Participant Email Address)**: Enter your email address for communication.
* **Study Title (Study Title)**: Enter the title of the study as described to you.
* **Principal Investigator Name (Principal Investigator Name)**: Enter the name of the principal investigator leading the study.
* **Investigator Contact Information (Investigator Contact Information)**: Enter the contact information of the principal investigator, including their phone number and email address.
* **Expected Study Duration (Study Duration)**: Select how long you expect the study to last from the provided options.
* **Purpose of the Study (Study Purpose)**: Briefly describe the purpose of the study as it was explained to you.
* **What Will Participation Involve (Participation Procedures)**: Describe how you will participate in the study, including any specific tasks or activities.
* **Time Commitment Per Session (Time Commitment)**: Select how many hours per session you expect to commit to the study.
* **Number of Sessions Expected (Number of Sessions)**: Optionally, provide the number of sessions you expect to participate in.
* **Risks and Benefits (Risks and Benefits)**: Read and understand the potential risks and benefits of participation.
* **I Understand Potential Risks (Risks Understand)**: Confirm that you understand the potential risks of participation.
* **Description of Potential Risks (Risks Description)**: If you have any questions or concerns about the potential risks, provide a description here.
* **I Understand Potential Benefits (Benefits Understand)**: Confirm that you understand the potential benefits of participation.
* **Voluntary Participation (Voluntary Participation)**: Select that you agree to participate voluntarily and that you can withdraw at any time without penalty.
* **I May Withdraw at Any Time (Withdrawal Right)**: Confirm that you understand you can withdraw from the study at any time without penalty.
* **Confidentiality (Confidentiality Consent)**: Select that you understand that your information will be kept confidential.
* **Data Usage (Data Usage Consent)**: Select that you agree to your data being used in research publications, with all identifying information removed.
* **Consent to Participate (Final Consent)**: Confirm your agreement to participate in the study.
* **Date of Consent (Consent Date)**: Enter the date on which you provided consent to participate.

Please note that you must agree to all the terms before proceeding.
