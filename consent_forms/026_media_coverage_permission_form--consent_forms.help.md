# Media Coverage Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register media representatives, such as journalists, photographers, and videographers, for various events, conferences, and activities managed by the organization. The form gathers essential information about the individual, including their name, contact details, role, location, and preferences for event coverage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email in the "Email" field, using the standard format (e.g., `john.doe@example.com`).
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field, without any non-numerical characters.
5. Select your Role from the "Role" dropdown menu (e.g., Journalist, Photographer, etc.).
6. Enter your Location in the "Location" field.
7. Choose your desired Permission Level from the "Permission Level" dropdown menu:
	* Public: Your name and contact information will be publicly accessible.
	* Private: Your name and contact information will not be shared publicly.
	* Restricted: Your name and contact information will be shared only with authorized personnel.
8. Provide a brief description of the event or activity you are covering in the "Event Description" field.
9. Select the Date of the event or activity you will be covering from the calendar.
10. Choose the Time of the event or activity you will be covering.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (1)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (2)**: Enter your last name.
* **Email (3)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (4)**: Enter your phone number without any non-numerical characters.
* **Role (5)**: Choose your role or position from the dropdown menu.
* **Location (6)**: Enter the location of the event or activity you will be covering.
* **Permission Level (7)**: Select your desired level of access and information sharing.
* **Event Description (8)**: Provide a brief description of the event or activity you will be covering.
* **Date (9)**: Select the date of the event or activity from the calendar.
* **Time (10)**: Choose the time of the event or activity you will be covering.

## Tips
- Ensure that the information you provide is accurate and complete.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the event organizer or manager for clarification.
- This form is for media representatives only; non-media attendees or individuals without a legitimate purpose for attending should not complete this form.
