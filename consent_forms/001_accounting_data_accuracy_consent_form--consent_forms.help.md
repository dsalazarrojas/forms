# Accounting Data Accuracy Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect accurate and up-to-date contact and consent information from clients, ensuring that financial data is processed correctly and reliably.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the **Full Name** field with your legal name as it appears on your official documents.
2. Enter your **Email Address** in the correct format (example@domain.com).
3. Provide your **Phone Number** including the area code.
4. Fill out the **Business Name** field with the name of the entity being represented.
5. Review the **Financial Data Accuracy Statement** carefully.
6. Select either **I confirm all data is accurate** or **I confirm data is accurate with minor discrepancies noted** from the **Do you confirm the accuracy of the provided data?** field.
7. If selecting **I confirm data is accurate with minor discrepancies noted**, describe the discrepancies in the **Description of Discrepancies** field.
8. Enter the current date in the **Date of Consent** field.
9. Type your full name in the **Digital Signature** field to sign the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your legal name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address in the correct format (example@domain.com).
* **Phone Number**: Include the area code in your phone number.
* **Business Name**: Enter the name of the entity being represented.
* **Financial Data Accuracy Statement**: Please read the statement carefully to understand your responsibilities.
* **Agreement Statement**: This statement explains your commitment to the accuracy of the financial data provided.
* **Do you confirm the accuracy of the provided data?**: Select either **I confirm all data is accurate** or **I confirm data is accurate with minor discrepancies noted**.
* **Description of Discrepancies**: If you select **I confirm data is accurate with minor discrepancies noted**, describe the discrepancies in this field.
* **Date of Consent**: Enter the current date in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Digital Signature**: Type your full name to sign the form.
