# Educational Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The educational records release form is used to collect information from students who are releasing their educational records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your first name in the first name field.
2. Enter your last name in the last name field.
3. Enter your birth date in the birth date field.
4. Select your current or previous institution from the institution field.
5. Select your department from the department field.
6. Enter the date of graduation from the date of graduation field.
7. Enter your email address in the email field.
8. Enter your phone number in the phone field.
9. Enter the name of your current or previous institution in the institution name field.
10. Enter the name of your department in the department name field.
11. Enter the date of reissue in the date of reissue field.
12. Select whether the institution has signed in the institution signature field.
13. Select whether the student has signed in the student signature field.
14. Enter the release date in the release date field.
15. Enter the date received in the date received field.
16. Enter the name of your current or previous institution in the institution name 2 field.
17. Enter the name of your department in the department name 2 field.
18. Enter the date of issue in the date of issue field.
19. Enter the date of reissue in the date of reissue field.
20. Select whether the institution has signed in the institution signature 2 field.
21. Select whether the student has signed in the student signature field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Birth Date**: Enter your birth date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Institution**: Select the institution where you attended. This could be your current or previous institution.
* **Department**: Select the department where you attended. This could be your current or previous department.
* **Date of Graduation**: Enter the date of graduation if you have already graduated.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Institution Name**: Enter the name of the institution where you attended.
* **Department Name**: Enter the name of the department where you attended.
* **Date of Reissue**: Enter the date of reissue if this is a reissue.
* **Institution Signature**: Select whether the institution has signed.
* **Student Signature**: Select whether the student has signed.
* **Release Date**: Enter the date of release.
* **Date Received**: Enter the date received.
* **Institution Name 2**: Enter the name of the institution where you attended.
* **Department Name 2**: Enter the name of the department where you attended.
* **Date of Issue**: Enter the date of issue.
* **Date of Reissue**: Enter the date of reissue.
* **Institution Signature 2**: Select whether the institution has signed.
* **Student Signature Date**: Enter the date of the student signature.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date of the signature.
* **Release By**: Select whether you are releasing your records.
* **Release Date 2**: Enter the release date.
* **Date Received**: Enter the date received.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your institution's registrar or contact person for clarification.
