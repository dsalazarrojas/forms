# Medical Record Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Medical Record Release Form is a document that allows patients to grant access to their medical records to external organizations. This form is designed to capture the necessary information from the patient, including their name, date of birth, address, medical history, and signatures from both the patient and their authorized representative.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Patient Name**: Enter the name of the patient as it appears on their medical records.
2. **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. **Patient Address**: Enter the patient's address, including their street address, city, state, and zip code.
4. **Medical History**: Describe the patient's medical history, including any relevant medical conditions or allergies.
5. **Signature**: Sign this field to confirm that the patient has given their consent for the external organization to access their medical records.
6. **Authorized Representative Name**: If the patient is not completing this form themselves, enter the name of the authorized representative who is signing on their behalf.
7. **Representative Signature**: Sign this field to confirm that the authorized representative has the patient's consent to access their medical records.
8. **Organization Name**: Enter the name of the external organization requesting access to the patient's medical records.
9. **Organization Phone**: Enter the phone number of the external organization.
10. **Date**: Enter the date that this form was completed.

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical records.
* **Date of Birth** (`birthdate`, `date`, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required): Describe the patient's medical history, including any relevant medical conditions or allergies.

* **Representative Signature** (`representative_signature`, `note`, required): Sign this field to confirm that the authorized representative has the patient's consent to access their medical records.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the external organization requesting access to the patient's medical records.
* **Organization Phone** (`organization_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the external organization.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date that this form was completed.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the patient understands the purpose and implications of this form before completing it.
* If the patient is completing this form themselves, ensure that they sign the **Signature** field to confirm their consent.
* If an authorized representative is completing this form on behalf of the patient, ensure that they sign the **Representative Signature** field and enter their name in the **Authorized Representative Name** field.
* This form should only be used in specific situations where the organization has a legitimate reason to access the patient's medical records. If you're unsure, please review the relevant policies and procedures before proceeding.
