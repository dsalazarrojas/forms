# Animal Welfare Event Coverage Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animal Welfare Event Coverage Consent Form is designed to gather essential information and consent from individuals participating in animal welfare events. It ensures that their media permissions, parental consent (if applicable), and personal details are accurately recorded, providing a comprehensive understanding of their willingness to be photographed, filmed, or interviewed during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
- Ensure you have read and understand the form's purpose and each field's significance.
- Fill out the form carefully, ensuring all required fields are completed.
- If you are under 18, provide your parent or guardian's contact information and consent.
- Select your consent for various media permissions.
- If applicable, specify any additional notes or restrictions on how your image can be used.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your complete name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Provide your primary email contact for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact phone number, if desired.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): If you are under 18, enter your date of birth for parental consent purposes.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, `text`, required): Specify the name of the animal welfare event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, `text`, optional): Provide the location of the event if applicable.
* **I consent to be photographed during the event** (`photography_consent`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you wish to be photographed.
* **I consent to be filmed or recorded during the event** (`video_consent`, `select_one`, required): Select your consent for video recordings.
* **I consent to audio recording of my voice or statements** (`audio_consent`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your consent for audio recording.
* **I grant permission to use my name and likeness in media** (`name_and_likeness`, `select_one`, required): Specify your willingness to be credited in published materials.
* **How may media of me be used** (`media_usage_scope`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the platforms and purposes for media use.
* **I consent to commercial use of my image or statements** (`commercial_use`, `select_one`, optional): Decide on commercial use of your image or statements.
* **Parent/Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your parent or legal guardian's full name if you are under 18.
* **Parent/Guardian Email Address** (`parent_email`, `email`, optional): Provide your parent or guardian's contact email.
* **Parent/Guardian consents to above media permissions** (`parent_consent`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm your parent or guardian's consent.
* **Additional Notes or Restrictions** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Specify any personal restrictions on how your image can be used.
* **I have read and agree to this consent form** (`signature_consent`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your agreement to the terms by clicking 'Yes, I agree'.

This form is intended to be completed carefully and accurately, ensuring that participants' privacy and consent are respected during the event.
