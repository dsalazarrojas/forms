# Declaration Of Suitability Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about the user's role, placement, activity, date, time, and any additional notes for a declaration of suitability purpose.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Select your role

*   **What role do you hold?** (`role`, `select_one`, required): Please choose the role you hold from the list of options.
*   **Where is this placement?** (`placement`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the locations where this placement will occur from the list of options. Select multiple locations if applicable.
*   **What activity will you be participating in?** (`activity`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the activities you will be participating in from the list of options. Select multiple activities if applicable.
*   **Date of Declaration** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date on which you are submitting this form.
*   **Time of Declaration** (`time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of day when you are submitting this form.

### Step 2: Add any additional notes or provide your contact information

*   **Any additional notes?** (`note`, `note`, optional): If you have any additional information or comments, please provide them here.
*   **Contact Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for follow-up purposes.
*   **Contact Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted for follow-up purposes.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your submission is accepted.
*   Be as accurate as possible with your responses to ensure the form is filled out correctly.
*   If you have any questions or need assistance, do not hesitate to contact us for support.
