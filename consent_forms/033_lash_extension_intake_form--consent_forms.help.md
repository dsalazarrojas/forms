# Lash Extension Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from clients for lash extension services, including contact details, appointment scheduling, and specific preferences for their lash extensions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your name and contact information so that we can get in touch with you.
2. Choose the contact method you prefer (phone, email, or in-person).
3. If you're unsure or have any questions, please provide additional contact details in the text field.
4. Indicate whether you've given your consent to proceed with the lash extension service.
5. Select the type of lash extension you're interested in (Classic, Hybrid, or Volume).
6. Choose the material you prefer for your lash extensions (Synthetic, Natural, or Combination).
7. Specify the desired length of your lash extensions.
8. Please let us know your preferred lash color.
9. If you don't have an appointment date and time in mind, please leave this field blank.
10. If necessary, provide additional notes for the technician.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lash Technician Info** (`lash_technician_info`, text, optional): This field is used to enter any additional information about the lash technician providing your services. If you'd like to specify any special requests or preferences, you can provide them here.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Please enter your full name.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select multiple, optional): Choose how you'd like to be contacted: by phone, email, or in-person.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): If you'd like to provide additional contact information, please enter it here. For example, if you'd rather not be contacted by phone or email, you can specify an alternative contact method in this field.
* **Consent Given** (`consent_given`, select one, optional): Indicate whether you consent to proceed with the lash extension service. Please respond with "True" or "False".
* **Lash Type** (`lash_type`, select multiple, optional): Select the type of lash extension you're interested in: Classic, Hybrid, or Volume.
* **Lash Material** (`lash_material`, select multiple, optional): Choose the material you prefer for your lash extensions: Synthetic, Natural, or Combination.
* **Lash Length** (`lash_length`, text, optional): Specify the desired length of your lash extensions.
* **Lash Color** (`lash_color`, text, optional): Let us know your preferred lash color.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, optional): If you have a specific appointment date in mind, please enter it here.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, optional): If you have a specific appointment time in mind, please enter it here.
* **Technician Notes** (`technician_notes`, note, optional): If necessary, provide additional notes for the technician to consider during your appointment.
* **Form Submission ID** (`form_submission_id`, text, optional): This field is automatically populated with a unique ID for your submission.
* **Technician Signature** (`technician_signature`, text, optional): This field is reserved for the technician to sign and date upon completion of the service.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, text, optional): Please sign and date this field to indicate your consent and completion of the service.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth experience for both you and the technician.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
* Please review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure everything is correct.
