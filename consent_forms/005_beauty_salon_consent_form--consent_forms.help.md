# Beauty Salon Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beauty Salon Consent Form is used to gather information about your preferences, medical history, and consent for services. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure a positive experience for both you and the salon.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by reviewing the form and ensuring you have all the necessary information to complete it.
2. Fill out each section carefully, making sure to select the correct options and provide accurate answers.
3. If you're unsure about any of the questions or options, don't hesitate to ask a salon staff member for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (Client Name, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required): Enter your email address for salon communication.
* **Date of Last Visit** (Date of Last Visit, date, optional): If you've visited the salon before, enter the date of your last visit.
* **Service Requested** (Service Requested, select multiple, required): Select the services you're interested in receiving.
* **Your Hair Type** (Your Hair Type, select one, optional): Select the type of hair you have.
* **Recent Color History** (Recent Color History, text, optional): Describe any recent color treatments you've had in the last 6 months.
* **Known Allergies** (Known Allergies, text, optional): List any known allergies, especially to hair dyes or chemical treatments.
* **Sensitive Scalp or Skin** (Sensitive Scalp or Skin, select one, required): Indicate if you have a sensitive scalp or skin.
* **Current Medications** (Current Medications, text, optional): List any current medications that may affect hair treatment results.
* **Consent Agreement** (Consent Agreement, select one, required): Select your agreement to the beauty services and understand the risks involved.
* **May we take photos** (May we take photos, select one, optional): Indicate if you agree to have photos taken of your hair for social media purposes.
* **Date** (Date, date, required): Date and sign the form to signify your consent.

Remember to review the form carefully before submitting it to a salon staff member. They'll be happy to answer any questions and guide you through the process.
