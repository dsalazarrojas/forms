# Site Orientation Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to confirm an employee's understanding of the site's orientation. It's essential to fill this out to ensure that you have a clear understanding of the site's rules, regulations, and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Site Orientation Acknowledgement" option and select either "Yes" or "No" to indicate your understanding of the site's orientation.
2. Enter the date on which you completed the orientation or training, and click on the correct date.
3. Set the time on which you completed the orientation or training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Acknowledgement** (site_orientation_acknowledgement_form, select_multiple, optional): Confirm your understanding of the site's orientation by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu.
- **Date** (site_orientation_form, date, optional): Enter the date on which you completed the site orientation or training.
- **Time** (site_orientation_time, time, optional): Set the time on which you completed the site orientation or training.
