# gdpr_consent_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The GDPR Consent Form Template is a standardized form used to collect personal data from users, ensuring compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) requirements. It is designed to gather essential information for data processing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your First name in the "First name" field.
* Enter your Last name in the "Last name" field.
* Provide your Email address in the "Email address" field.
* Select a date for "Date of birth" field, using the "dd/mm/yyyy" format.
* Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Consent" field, indicating your agreement or disagreement to data processing.
* (Optional) Enter your Phone number for contact purposes.
* (Optional) Add any Additional comments in the "Additional comments" field if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your given name, as it will be used to identify you in our records.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your family name as it will be used to identify you in our records.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your primary email address where we can contact you.
* **Date of birth** (`birth_date`, `date`, required): Select a date in the "dd/mm/yyyy" format, indicating your birth date.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, required): Choose "Yes" to indicate your agreement to data processing or "No" if you do not agree.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): If you wish, provide a phone number for contact purposes.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or information you feel is relevant to your data processing agreement.
