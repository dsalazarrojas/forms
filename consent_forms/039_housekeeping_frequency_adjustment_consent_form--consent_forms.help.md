# Housekeeping Frequency Adjustment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for residents to provide their consent and preferences for adjusting the frequency of housekeeping services in their housing community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Please ensure you are eligible to fill out this form**: Only residents who have been asked by staff to complete this form should fill it out.
2. Fill out the form in a quiet and comfortable setting, free from distractions and interruptions.
3. Answer the questions truthfully and carefully.
4. If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to reach out to your community staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resident Details**: <small>Please enter your name and contact information so we can keep in touch with you.</small>
* **Requested Changes**: Select all the services you would like to have adjusted in frequency. You can choose from the following options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Consent**: Indicate your consent for the changes you have requested. You can choose from the following options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Resident Contact Information**: Provide your contact information so we can keep in touch with you and update you on any changes to your housekeeping frequency. Please enter your name and any other relevant details.
* **Tips**: If you need help or clarification on any field, please do not hesitate to reach out to your community staff for assistance. We are here to support you!
