# plagiarism_free_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a submitter's declaration, including their ID, name, email, and agreement to terms and conditions. It is used to ensure that submitters are aware of and agree to the terms and conditions, and to record their declaration in a timestamped format.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your Submitter ID.
2. Enter your name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number (optional).
5. Select the original work you are submitting (optional).
6. Declare that the submitted work is original (required).
7. Declare that you are the owner of the original work (required).
8. Write your plagiarism declaration (required).
9. Write your declaration (required).
10. Select your agreement to terms and conditions (required).
11. Enter your declaration date.
12. Enter your declaration time (optional).
13. Add any additional notes (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submitter id** (1, number, required): Enter your unique identifier as a number.
* **Submitter name** (2, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your ID.
* **Submitter email** (3, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Submitter phone** (4, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you have one.
* **Original work** (5, text, optional): Select the original work you are submitting (e.g., essay, paper, article).
* **Original authorship** (6, text, required): Declare that the submitted work is original.
* **Plagiarism declaration** (7, text, required): Write your plagiarism declaration, stating that the submitted work is original.
* **Submitter declaration** (8, text, required): Write your declaration, stating that you are the owner of the original work.
* **Submitter agreement** (9, select_multiple, optional): Select your agreement to terms and conditions (I have read, understand, and agree or I have not read, do not understand, and do not agree).
* **Submitter declaration date** (10, date, required): Enter the date of your declaration.
* **Submitter declaration time** (11, time, optional): Enter the time of your declaration (if applicable).
* **Submitter declaration note** (12, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your declaration.
* **Submitter declaration email** (13, email, required): Enter your email address for declaration purposes.
* **Submitter declaration phone** (14, text, optional): Enter your phone number for declaration purposes.
* **Submitter declaration date** (15, date, optional): Enter the date of your declaration (if not already entered).
* **Submitter agreement date** (16, date, required): Enter the date of your agreement.
* **Submitter declaration agreement** (17, select_one, optional): Select your agreement to terms and conditions (I agree or I do not agree).
* **Submitter declaration note** (18, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your agreement.
* **Submitter declaration additional note** (19, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your declaration.
