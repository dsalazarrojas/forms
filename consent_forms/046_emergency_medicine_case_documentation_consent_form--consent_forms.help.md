# emergency_medicine_case_documentation_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The emergency medicine case documentation consent form is used to gather essential information about a patient's medical case, including their authorization, medical history, and contact details. This form is necessary for emergency medical cases to ensure that patients receive proper care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the patient's details, including their name, contact information, and medical history.
2. Select the applicable authorization options for the patient's emergency treatment.
3. Specify the patient's consent to emergency services and medical treatment.
4. Provide contact details for the patient's emergency contact.
5. Select the patient's status and case type.
6. Choose the case severity and location.
7. Select the medical provider and case status.
8. Enter the created date and time, as well as the user who created the form and the user who submitted it.
9. Sign and date the form to confirm completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Details** (Text, optional): This field is used to collect the patient's name, contact information, and medical history.
* **Authorization** (Select Multiple, required): Choose all applicable options for the patient's emergency treatment.
* **Case Info** (Text, optional): This field is used to provide additional information about the patient's case.
* **Consent** (Select One, required): Please select one option for the patient's consent to emergency services and medical treatment.
* **Contact Details** (Text, optional): Enter the patient's contact details, including phone number and address.
* **Emergency Info** (Text, optional): This field is used to provide additional emergency information about the patient.
* **Authorization Details** (Text, optional): Enter any additional authorization details.
* **Signature** (Note, required): Please sign and date the form to confirm completion.
* **Medical History** (Text, optional): Enter the patient's medical history.
* **Allergies** (Select Multiple, required): Choose all applicable options for the patient's allergies.
* **Emergency Services** (Select One, required): Does the patient have any emergency services?
* **Insurance** (Select One, required): Does the patient have any insurance coverage?
* **Emergency Contact** (Text, optional): Enter the patient's emergency contact information.
* **Patient Status** (Select Multiple, required): Choose all applicable options for the patient's status.
* **Case Type** (Select Multiple, required): Choose all applicable options for the case type.
* **Case Severity** (Select Multiple, required): Choose all applicable options for the case severity.
* **Case Location** (Select Multiple, required): Choose all applicable options for the case location.
* **Medical Provider** (Text, optional): Enter the medical provider's name and contact information.
* **Case Status** (Select One, required): What is the current status of the case?
* **Version** (Number, required): Enter the version number of the consent form.
* **Created By** (Text, required): Enter the name of the user who created the form.
* **Created At** (Date, required): Enter the date and time the form was created.
* **Updated At** (Date, required): Enter the date and time the form was last updated.
* **Assigned User** (Text, required): Enter the name of the user assigned to the case.
* **Submitted By** (Text, required): Enter the name of the user who submitted the form.
