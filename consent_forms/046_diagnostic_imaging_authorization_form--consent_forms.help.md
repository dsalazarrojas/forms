## Diagnostic Imaging Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Diagnostic Imaging Authorization Form is used to obtain consent from patients for diagnostic imaging procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Patient Information** (`patient_id`, text, optional): Enter the patient's ID number, if available.
2. **Authorization Request** (`authorization_request`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if you are requesting authorization for diagnostic imaging procedures.
3. **Consent for Procedure** (`consent_procedure`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you are seeking consent for a specific procedure.
4. **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): Enter the medical condition being treated or assessed during the imaging procedure.
5. **Medical Imaging Authorization** (`medical_imaging_authorization`, number, optional): Enter the number of imaging procedures authorized.
6. **Authorization Date** (`authorization_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the authorization was given.
7. **Expiration Date** (`expiration_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the authorization expires.
8. **Signature** (`signature`, note, optional): Please sign and date the form to confirm your authorization.
9. **Medical Provider Name** (`medical_provider_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the medical provider requesting authorization.
10. **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's name.
11. **Medical Organization** (`medical_organization`, text, optional): Enter the name of the medical organization requesting authorization.
12. **Medical Specialty** (`medical_specialty`, text, optional): Enter the medical specialty of the procedure.
13. **Procedure Code** (`procedure_code`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if the procedure is being performed.
14. **Authorization Status** (`authorization_status`, select_one, optional): Select "Active" to indicate the authorization is active.
15. **Note**: Please ensure all fields are filled out correctly before submitting the form.
