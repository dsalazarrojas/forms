# Ownership Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ownership Declaration Form is designed to collect information about your pet's ownership details, including its name, type, and owner's contact information. This form is used to create a record of pet ownership, which is crucial for various purposes such as pet insurance, veterinary care, and pet-related services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the form in a step-by-step manner, ensuring that you provide accurate and up-to-date information about your pet.
2. Enter your pet's name in the "Pet name" field.
3. Select the correct pet type from the "Pet type" dropdown menu.
4. Provide your own contact information in the "Owner name", "Owner email", and "Owner phone" fields.
5. Enter your pet's date of birth (if applicable) in the "Pet's DOB" field.
6. If your pet has a microchip, provide the microchip number in the "Pet microchip number" field.
7. If your pet has a license, provide the license number in the "Pet license" field.
8. If your pet has any medical conditions or vaccinations, provide the necessary information in the "Pet medical conditions" and "Pet vaccinations" fields.
9. Finally, sign and date the "Signature" field to confirm that the information provided is accurate and authentic.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of your pet.
* **Pet type** (Select, Required): Select the type of pet you own (Cat, Dog, Other).
* **Owner name** (Text, Required): Enter your name as the owner of the pet.
* **Owner email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Owner phone** (Text, Optional): Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Pet's DOB** (Date, Required): Enter your pet's date of birth.
* **Pet microchip number** (Text, Optional): If your pet has a microchip, enter the number here.
* **Pet license** (Text, Optional): If your pet has a license, enter the number here.
* **Pet vaccinations** (Text, Optional): If your pet has any medical vaccinations, enter the details here.
* **Pet medical conditions** (Text, Optional): If your pet has any medical conditions, enter the details here.
* **Signature** (Note, Required): Sign and date the form to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any information, please consult with your veterinarian or pet expert for guidance.
* Once the form is completed, you can print or save it for future reference.
