# Personal Injury Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from participants about a specific event, such as details about the event and the participant's involvement. The form is designed to be completed by participants to provide their consent and waiver for any potential risks or injuries that may occur during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Event Details" section with as much information as possible about the event you are participating in.
2. In the "Participant Details" section, provide your name and any other relevant information about yourself as the participant.
3. If you have any specific concerns or waivers to sign, you can list them in the "Participant Waiver" section. This is a free-text field where you can describe any activities you will be doing during the event and any risks or concerns you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Event Details** (event_details)
* This field is for providing event-specific information, such as the date, time, location, and a brief description of the event.

### **Participant Details** (participant_details)
* This field is for providing your name and any other relevant information about yourself as the participant.

### **Participant Waiver** (waiver)
* This field is for listing any specific activities you will be doing during the event and any risks or concerns you have. Please provide a detailed list of all the activities you will do.

## Tips
* Please make sure to provide as much information as possible in each field to ensure accurate and complete consent and waiver.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us for assistance.
* This form is not intended to be a contract, but rather a waiver of liability for any potential risks or injuries that may occur during the event.

Note: The form is designed to be completed by participants to provide their consent and waiver for any potential risks or injuries that may occur during the event. It is not a contract, but rather a way to collect information and ensure that participants are aware of the potential risks involved.
