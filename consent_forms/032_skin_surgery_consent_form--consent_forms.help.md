# skin_surgery_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from clients before undergoing skin surgery. It includes information about the client, their medical history, and the consent for the procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information about the client (client_info, procedure_details, risks_and_complications, consent_date, client_statement, consent_to_procedure, and client_signature).
2. If applicable, fill in the client's medical history (allergies, medical_history, medical_conditions, and medical_facility).
3. Provide information about emergency contacts (emergency_contact, emergency_phone, emergency_address).
4. Leave the other fields blank if not applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **client_info** (Text, required): This field is used to collect the client's name, address, and contact information.
* **procedure_details** (Text, required): Describe the skin surgery procedure the client is undergoing.
* **risks_and_complications** (Text, required): List any risks and complications associated with the procedure.
* **consent_date** (Date, optional): Date and time the client provided consent for the procedure.
* **signature** (Text, required): Client's signature, confirming they have read and understand the consent form.
* **client_signature** (Text, optional): Second signature by the client, confirming their consent.
* **doctor_signature** (Text, optional): Signature by the doctor, confirming they have explained the procedure to the client.
* **consent_to_procedure** (Select One, required): Client's consent to undergo the procedure.
* **medical_record** (Text, optional): Additional medical information about the client.
* **other_medical_info** (Text, optional): Any other medical information not listed.
* **allergies** (Select Multiple, optional): List any allergies the client may have.
* **medical_history** (Text, optional): Client's medical history.
* **medical_conditions** (Text, optional): List any medical conditions the client may have.
* **emergency_contact** (Text, optional): Name and contact information of the client's emergency contact.
* **emergency_phone** (Text, optional): Phone number of the client's emergency contact.
* **emergency_address** (Text, optional): Address of the client's emergency contact.
* **medical_facility** (Text, optional): Medical facility where the client will undergo the procedure.
* **medical_insurance** (Text, optional): Client's medical insurance information.
* **procedure_cost** (Text, optional): Cost of the procedure.
* **client_statement** (Text, required): Client's statement confirming they have read and understand the consent form.
* **procedure_date** (Date, optional): Date and time the procedure is scheduled.
* **follow_up** (Text, optional): Follow-up information after the procedure.
* **contact** (Text, optional): Additional contact information.
* **consent_note** (Text, optional): Any additional consent notes.
* **other_info** (Text, optional): Any other information not listed.

## Tips
- Always ensure that the client has read and understand the consent form before signing.
- Make sure to provide a clear and concise explanation of the procedure to the client.
- Keep the client's medical history up-to-date.
- Ensure that the client's emergency contact information is accurate.
- Keep the consent form on file for future reference.
