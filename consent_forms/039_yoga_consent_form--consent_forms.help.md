# yoga_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The yoga_consent_form is a crucial document that requires students to provide general information about themselves, their medical conditions, and emergency contact details. This form helps instructors and medical staff ensure students' safety during and after yoga practices.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form accurately and honestly.
- Provide detailed information for each field as required.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all necessary information is provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Information (1)**: This field is for providing basic information about yourself.
  - Type in your name, age, and any other relevant details you feel are necessary.
  - Please note that this field is not required, but it's helpful for our records.

- **Student Details (2)**: Please provide any additional details about yourself that we should know.
  - This could include any previous medical conditions, allergies, or other relevant information.
  - This field is not required but is helpful for us to provide better services.

- **Injury Declaration (3)**:
  - Select any injuries you have, such as minor or major injuries. If you have a specific description of an injury, we recommend providing more information in the injury description field below.
  - This field is required to ensure we can provide a safe environment for you during practices.

- **Medical Conditions (4)**:
  - Select any medical conditions you have, such as allergies or conditions we should be aware of.
  - This field is required to ensure we can provide the necessary assistance during practices.

- **Emergency Contact (5)**: Provide the emergency contact person's name and details.
  - Please note the relationship between you and the emergency contact.
  - This field is required.

- **Emergency Contact Relationship (6)**:
  - Please provide details on your relationship with the emergency contact, such as mother, father, partner, or friend.
  - This field is not required, but it's useful for us to know.

- **Emergency Contact Phone (7)**: Provide the emergency contact phone number.
  - Please note that this field is not required, but it's helpful in case we need to contact them.

- **Emergency Contact Address (8)**: Provide the emergency contact's address.
  - This field is required for us to be able to contact them quickly in case of an emergency.

- **Student Signature (9)**: Sign this form to confirm that you have read and understood the information provided.
  - Please sign this field as it is required and confirms your understanding of the risks and benefits of practicing yoga.

## Tips
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please do not hesitate to ask your instructor for help.
- Please review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all necessary information is provided.
- If you need any clarification on any of the fields, please ask your instructor or contact us.
- Your honesty and accuracy in filling out this form are crucial for your safety and well-being during our practices.
