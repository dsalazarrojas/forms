# Brow Threading Service Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to gather information from clients to understand their preferences and medical history before undergoing the brow threading service. It is essential to fill out this form thoroughly and accurately to ensure that you receive the best possible care and service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the required fields with your personal information.
2. Select the correct options for your skin type, pain sensitivity level, and medical history.
3. Answer the questions regarding your medical history, including any medications or allergies.
4. Sign the consent and waiver sections, indicating that you understand the risks and procedures involved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Full Name**: (`client_name`, text, required): Please fill in your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth**: (`client_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Phone Number**: (`client_phone`, text, required): Provide your phone number for contact and follow-up purposes.
* **Email Address**: (`client_email`, email, required): Enter your email address to receive appointment reminders and follow-up communications.
* **Brow Threading Appointment Date**: (`appointment_date`, date, required): Select the date you would like to schedule your appointment.
* **Appointment Time**: (`appointment_time`, time, required): Select the time you would like to schedule your appointment.
* **Have you had threading before**: (`threading_experience`, select_one, required): Choose whether you have had threading before or if it is your first time.
* **Skin Type**: (`skin_type`, select_one, required): Select your skin type from the options provided.
* **Current skin conditions**: (`skin_conditions`, text, optional): If you have any skin conditions, such as rashes or acne, please describe them here.
* **Known product or chemical allergies**: (`product_allergies`, text, optional): If you have any known allergies to certain products or chemicals, please list them here.
* **Pain Sensitivity Level**: (`pain_sensitivity`, select_one, required): Choose your level of pain sensitivity.
* **Any medications affecting skin**: (`medications`, text, optional): If you are taking any medications that affect your skin, such as blood thinners or antibiotics, please list them here.
* **Recent facial treatments or procedures**: (`recent_treatments`, text, optional): If you have had any recent facial treatments or procedures, please describe them here.
* **Are you currently pregnant or nursing**: (`pregnancy`, select_one, required): Choose whether you are pregnant or nursing.
* **Your desired brow shape**: (`desired_shape`, text, optional): Describe your desired brow shape or preference.
* **I consent to the brow threading service**: (`consent`, select_one, required): Select the option to indicate that you consent to the service.
* **I understand and accept all risks**: (`waiver`, select_one, required): Select the option to indicate that you understand and accept all risks involved.
* **Date**: (`signature_date`, date, required): Date and sign this form to confirm that you have read and understood the information provided.
