# Sports Facility Marketing Event Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect necessary information and consent from participants and emergency contacts for sports facility marketing events. It is essential to fill this form accurately and honestly to ensure the event organizers can contact and communicate with you and your emergency contacts during the event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your details as a participant or emergency contact.
2. Provide any necessary contact information for the event organizers to reach you or your emergency contacts during the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Details

* **Participant Details**: 
	+ **Type**: Text field
	+ **Required**: Optional
	+ **Purpose**: Enter your name as a participant in this event. This will help us identify you as a participant in the event.
* **Emergency Contacts**: 
	+ **Type**: Text field
	+ **Required**: Optional
	+ **Purpose**: Enter any emergency contact information, such as a name and phone number or email, in case we need to contact you during the event.
* **Marketing Permissions**: 
	+ **Type**: Text field
	+ **Required**: Optional
	+ **Purpose**: This field is for you to indicate your consent to be contacted by us during and after the event. Please select "Yes" if you agree to be contacted, and "No" if you do not.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and honestly.
* If you are a minor, please have a parent or guardian sign this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact [contact email].
