# Electronic Medical Record Access Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from a patient and a healthcare provider to access the patient's electronic medical record.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Enter the patient's name in the "Consent Form Patient Name" field.
2.  Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Consent Form Patient Date of Birth" field.
3.  Select the healthcare provider's name from the "Provider" field.
4.  Choose the desired "Access Level" from the available options.
5.  Enter the date in the "Date" field.
6.  Enter the time in the "Time" field.
7.  Enter the IP address of the system used to access the record in the "IP Address" field.
8.  Enter the name and version of the browser used to access the record in the "Browser" field.
9.  Enter the location where the access will be made in the "Location" field.
10. Enter any additional "Notes" regarding the consent.
11. Enter the patient's email address in the "Email" field.
12. Enter the patient's phone number in the "Phone" field.
13. Enter the number of faxes received in the "Faxes" field.
14. Enter the patient's medical record number in the "Medical Record Number" field.
15. Enter the healthcare provider's email address in the "Provider Email" field.
16. Enter the healthcare provider's phone number in the "Provider Phone" field.
17. Enter the number of faxes sent by the healthcare provider in the "Provider Faxes" field.
18. Obtain the patient's signature in the "Patient Signature" field.
19. Obtain the healthcare provider's signature in the "Provider Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Consent Form Patient Name** (`consent_form_patient_name`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the patient's name.
* **Consent Form Patient Date of Birth** (`consent_form_patient_date_of_birth`, `date`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Provider** (`consent_form_provider_name`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to select the healthcare provider's name.
* **Access Level** (`consent_form_access_level`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field allows you to choose the desired level of access to the patient's medical record.
* **Date** (`consent_form_date`, `date`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the date of the consent.
* **Time** (`consent_form_time`, `time`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the time of the consent.
* **IP Address** (`consent_form_ip_address`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the IP address of the system used to access the patient's medical record.
* **Browser** (`consent_form_browser`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the name and version of the browser used to access the record.
* **Location** (`consent_form_location`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the location where the access will be made.
* **Notes** (`consent_form_notes`, `note`, required/optional): This field is used to enter any additional notes regarding the consent.
* **Email** (`consent_form_email`, `email`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the patient's email address.
* **Phone** (`consent_form_phone`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the patient's phone number.
* **Faxes** (`consent_form_faxes`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the number of faxes received.
* **Medical Record Number** (`consent_form_medical_record_number`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the patient's medical record number.
* **Provider Email** (`consent_form_provider_email`, `email`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the healthcare provider's email address.
* **Provider Phone** (`consent_form_provider_phone`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the healthcare provider's phone number.
* **Provider Faxes** (`consent_form_provider_faxes`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the number of faxes sent by the healthcare provider.
* **Patient Signature** (`consent_form_patient_signature`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to obtain the patient's signature.
* **Provider Signature** (`consent_form_provider_signature`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to obtain the healthcare provider's signature.
