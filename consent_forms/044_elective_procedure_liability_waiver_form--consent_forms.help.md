# Elective Procedure Liability Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture informed consent from patients for elective procedures. It helps to ensure that patients understand the risks and consequences associated with the procedure and provides a clear way of obtaining their liability waiver.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Elective Procedure Liability Waiver Form Data in the first field. This field is required and should include information about the procedure and its associated risks.
2. Fill in the Patient First Name and Patient Last Name fields with the relevant information. These fields are not required but are essential for patient identification and contact.
3. Enter the Procedure Name field with the specific name of the procedure the patient is undergoing.
4. Choose a Consent Date, the date when the patient is expected to undergo the procedure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Elective Procedure Liability Waiver Form Data**: A text field where you need to enter information about the elective procedure, including associated risks and responsibilities.
  * Type: Text Field
  * Required: Yes
* **Patient First Name**: A text field to enter the first name of the patient. 
  * Type: Text Field
  * Required: No
* **Patient Last Name**: A text field to enter the last name of the patient. 
  * Type: Text Field
  * Required: No
* **Procedure Name**: A text field to enter the name of the procedure the patient is undergoing. 
  * Type: Text Field
  * Required: No
* **Consent Date**: A date field to select the date when the patient is expected to undergo the procedure. 
  * Type: Date Field
  * Required: No

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* Review the form carefully to ensure that all information is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities or medical professionals for guidance.
