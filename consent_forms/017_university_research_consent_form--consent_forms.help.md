# University Research Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for university research studies. It's designed to gather information from research participants about their involvement in a study.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
2. For each section, read the label carefully and provide the requested information.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Research Participant Information** (`research_participant_information`, text, required): Enter your name, address, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Study Purpose** (`study_purpose`, text, optional): If you'd like to provide additional context about the study, you can describe its purpose here.
* **Informed Consent** (`informed_consent`, text, optional): Please indicate if you have provided informed consent for this study. If yes, indicate the date.
* **Research Participant Rights** (`research_participant_rights`, text, optional): Describe any rights you have as a research participant.
* **Study Conduct** (`study_conduct`, text, optional): Explain how the study will be conducted.
* **Study Duration** (`study_duration`, number, optional): If applicable, specify the length of time the study will last.
* **Study Location** (`study_location`, text, optional): Provide the location where the study will take place.
* **Informed Consent Document** (`informed_consent_document`, select_multiple, optional): If you have provided informed consent, select 'Yes'. If not, select 'No'.
* **Informed Consent Date** (`informed_consent_date`, date, optional): If you have provided informed consent, indicate the date it was given.
* **Research Participant Information Verification** (`research_participant_information_verification`, select_one, optional): If you have confirmed your participant information, select 'Yes'. If not, select 'No'.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, required): Please sign your name as the research participant.
* **Investigator Signature** (`investigator_signature`, text, optional): If you're an investigator, please sign your name here.
