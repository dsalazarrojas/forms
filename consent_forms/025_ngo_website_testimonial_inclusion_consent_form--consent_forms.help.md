# ngo_website_testimonial_inclusion_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect consents for the inclusion of testimonies, photos, and audio on the NGO's website. It is intended to ensure that individuals understand how their information will be used and to obtain their explicit consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and understand the purpose and implications of sharing your testimonial, photo, and audio on the NGO's website.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for each question regarding consent.
3. Fill in your Name and Email (if applicable).
4. Provide your testimonial text.
5. If applicable, provide a URL for your photo or audio.
6. If you have any other information you'd like to share, type it in the "Other (specify)" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **I give permission** (`testimonial_inclusion`, select_one, required: false): This field asks whether you give permission to include your testimonial on the NGO's website.
  - Select "Yes" if you want to share your testimonial.
  - Select "No" if you do not want to share your testimonial.
- **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): This field is for your name.
  - Type your name as you want it to appear on the NGO's website.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): This field is for your contact information (e.g., email, phone number, etc.).
  - Type your contact information if you want to be contacted for further information or questions.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): This field is for your email address.
  - Type your email address if you want to be contacted for further information or questions.
- **Testimonial** (`testimonial_text`, text, required: false): This field is for your testimonial.
  - Type your testimonial text as you want it to appear on the NGO's website.
- **Photo Consent** (`photo_consent`, select_one, required: false): This field asks whether you consent to sharing your photo on the NGO's website.
  - Select "Yes" if you want to share your photo.
  - Select "No" if you do not want to share your photo.
- **Photo URL** (`photo_url`, text, required: false): This field is for your photo URL (e.g., Instagram, Facebook, etc.).
  - If you want to share your photo, type your photo URL here.
- **Audio Consent** (`testimonial_audio_consent`, select_one, required: false): This field asks whether you consent to sharing your audio on the NGO's website.
  - Select "Yes" if you want to share your audio.
  - Select "No" if you do not want to share your audio.
- **Audio URL** (`audio_url`, text, required: false): This field is for your audio URL (e.g., SoundCloud, etc.).
  - If you want to share your audio, type your audio URL here.
- **Other (specify)** (`other`, text, required: false): This field is for any other information you'd like to share.
  - Type any other relevant information you'd like to share with the NGO.
