# Quality Assurance Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quality Assurance Records Release Form is used to release quality assurance records after they have been thoroughly reviewed and deemed suitable for release. This form is essential for ensuring compliance with regulations and internal policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Organization Name, which is the name of the organization responsible for the quality assurance records.
2. Provide the Contact Person's name and contact information, including their Phone and Email.
3. Enter the Consent Date, which is the date the records were reviewed and deemed suitable for release.
4. Select the Record Type, indicating whether the records are being released or not.
5. Choose the Record Location, specifying where the records will be stored after release.
6. Provide a brief Reason for Release, explaining why the records are being released.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the organization responsible for the quality assurance records.
- **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the person to contact with regard to the records.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
- **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, required): Enter the date the records were reviewed and deemed suitable for release.
- **Record Type** (`record_type`, select_one, required): Choose 'Yes' if the records are being released, 'No' otherwise.
- **Record Location** (`record_location`, select_multiple, required): Choose 'Yes' if the records will be stored in multiple locations, 'No' otherwise.
- **Reason for Release** (`reason_for_release`, text, required): Provide a brief explanation for releasing the records.

## Tips
- Ensure the information entered is accurate and up-to-date.
- Verify that the records are suitable for release before filling out this form.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it for approval.
