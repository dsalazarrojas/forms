# Wedding Photography Contract And Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to outline the terms and conditions of the wedding photography service, including the details of the wedding, the services to be provided, and the expectations of both parties.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Bride Information (1)**: Enter the bride's name and any other relevant information.
2. **Groom Information (2)**: Enter the groom's name and any other relevant information.
3. **Ceremony Details (3)**: Describe the ceremony location, date, and time.
4. **Contract Terms (4)**: Provide any special requirements or restrictions for the photography services.
5. **Bride Signature (5)**: Confirm that the bride has reviewed and agreed to the terms.
6. **Groom Signature (6)**: Confirm that the groom has reviewed and agreed to the terms.
7. **Contract Acknowledgement (7)**: Acknowledge that both parties have reviewed and agreed to the terms.
8. **Contract Date (8)**: Enter the date of the contract.
9. **Photographer Name (9)**: Enter your name as the photographer.
10. **Photographer Address (10)**: Enter your address as the photographer.
11. **Photographer Contact (11)**: Enter your contact information as the photographer.
12. **Client Name (12)**: Enter the client's name.
13. **Client Address (13)**: Enter the client's address.
14. **Client Contact (14)**: Enter the client's contact information.
15. **Wedding Date (15)**: Enter the date of the wedding.
16. **Ceremony Place (16)**: Enter the location of the ceremony.
17. **Reception Place (17)**: Enter the location of the reception (if different from the ceremony).
18. **Photographer Terms (18)**: Describe your photography services and any terms or conditions.
19. **Client Terms (19)**: Describe the client's expectations and any terms or conditions.
20. **Photographer License (20)**: Enter your professional license or certification as a photographer.
21. **Photographer Experience (21)**: Describe your experience as a photographer.
22. **Photographer Equipment (22)**: List any equipment you will use for the photography services.
23. **Contract Total (23)**: Enter the total cost of the photography services.
24. **Payment Schedule (24)**: Describe the payment schedule and any payment terms.
25. **Contract Acknowledgement Date (25)**: Enter the date of contract acknowledgement.
