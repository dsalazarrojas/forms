# health_safety_self_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect health and safety information from employees, including their contact details, medical conditions, and emergency contacts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee name, email address, and phone number in the respective fields.
2. Describe your current symptoms, if any.
3. List any medical conditions you have or have had in the past.
4. Mention any medications you are currently taking.
5. Provide details about your medical history.
6. Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
7. Specify your relationship with your emergency contact (e.g., family member, spouse, etc.).
8. Answer the question about whether you consent to share your information with others.
9. If you consent, choose from the options (e.g., with HR, with management, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Symptoms**: Describe any current symptoms or health issues you are experiencing.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions you have or have had in the past.
* **Medications**: Mention any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical History**: Provide details about your medical history, including any relevant dates and events.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person you want to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Specify your relationship with your emergency contact (e.g., family member, spouse, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Consent to Share Info**: Answer whether you consent to share your information with others. Select 'Yes' if you are okay with sharing your information.
* **Consent to Share Info With Who**: If you answered 'Yes' above, choose who you want to share your information with (e.g., HR, management, etc.).
