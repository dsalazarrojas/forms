# Hospital Discharge Records Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain the patient's consent for the release of their medical records to a third party (recipient). It is essential for hospitals, medical facilities, and other healthcare providers to obtain this consent to ensure that sensitive medical information is shared with the right person.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's details:
   - Enter the patient's name and date of birth.
2. Select the authorization method for releasing the records:
   - Choose the method by which the patient is authorizing the release of their records.
3. Enter the recipient's details:
   - Fill in the recipient's name and address.
4. Fill in the date of release and the reason for releasing the records (optional).
5. Sign the form:
   - The patient must sign the form to confirm their consent.
   - The authorized individual must sign the form to confirm that they are the authorized person releasing the records.
   - A witness must sign the form to confirm that they witnessed the signing of the authorized individual.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Patient Name** (Patient Name, text, required: false)
Enter the full name of the patient whose medical records you are seeking to release.
### 2. **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required: true)
Enter the patient's date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
### 3. **Patient Address** (Patient Address, text, required: false)
Enter the patient's address for contact and record-keeping purposes.
### 4. **Authorization Method** (Authorization Method, select_one, required: true)
Choose the method by which the patient is authorizing the release of their records:
   - **Signature**: The patient signs the form to authorize the release of their records.
   - **Notarized Letter**: The patient provides a notarized letter to authorize the release of their records.
   - **Power of Attorney**: The patient gives power of attorney to an individual to release their records.
   - **Both**: The patient signs and provides a notarized letter to authorize the release of their records.
### 5. **Recipient Name** (Recipient Name, text, required: true)
Enter the name of the person or organization receiving the patient's medical records.
### 6. **Recipient Address** (Recipient Address, text, required: true)
Enter the address of the person or organization receiving the patient's medical records.
### 7. **Date of Release** (Date of Release, date, required: false)
Enter the date on which the records will be released (optional).
### 8. **Reason for Release** (Reason for Release, text, required: false)
Enter the reason for releasing the records (optional).
### 9. **Patient Signature** (Patient Signature, text, required: true)
The patient must sign this section to confirm their consent to the release of their records.
### 10. **Authorized Signature** (Authorized Signature, text, required: true)
The authorized individual must sign this section to confirm that they are releasing the patient's records.
### 11. **Witness Signature** (Witness Signature, text, required: true)
A witness must sign this section to confirm that they witnessed the signing of the authorized individual.

# Tips
- Ensure that you have obtained the patient's informed consent before filling out this form.
- Verify that the patient's signature is notarized if required.
- Ensure that the witness signature is from a person who is not a part of the patient's family.
- Review the completed form carefully before distributing the records.
