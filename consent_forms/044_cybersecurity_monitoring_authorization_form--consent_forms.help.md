# Cybersecurity Monitoring Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Cybersecurity Monitoring Authorization Form is used for internal purposes within an organization to request monitoring of security systems. It helps to gather necessary information for internal security teams to ensure effective monitoring and protection of organizational assets.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you are authorized to submit this form on behalf of your organization.
2. Enter your Organization ID, Name, Email, and Phone number.
3. Provide your Date of Birth (if required).
4. Enter the Employee Count and Employee Type relevant to the monitoring request.
5. Specify the Department and Role associated with the monitoring request.
6. State the Purpose of Monitoring and Scope of Monitoring.
7. Select the Consent option to authorize monitoring.
8. Review and agree to the Terms and Conditions.
9. Optionally, provide additional Monitoring Purpose Details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization ID** (`organization_id`): Enter your organization's unique identifier for internal purposes.
* **Name** (`name`): Enter your organization's name for identification.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your organization's email address for communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your organization's phone number for contact.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): If required, enter your Date of Birth for identification purposes.
* **Employee Count** (`employee_count`): Enter the number of employees associated with the monitoring request.
* **Employee Type** (`employee_type`): Select the type of employee related to the monitoring (Senior Management, Middle Management, Junior Staff, Other).
* **Department** (`department`): Specify the department associated with the monitoring request.
* **Role** (`role`): Enter the role associated with the monitoring request.
* **Purpose of Monitoring** (`purpose_of_monitoring`): Describe the reason for monitoring.
* **Scope of Monitoring** (`scope_of_monitoring`): Describe the scope of monitoring (e.g., network, system, employee, etc.).
* **Start Date** (`start_date`): Enter the start date of monitoring (if applicable).
* **End Date** (`end_date`): Enter the end date of monitoring (if applicable).
* **Consent** (`consent`): Select 'Yes' to authorize monitoring.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`): Review and agree to the terms and conditions of monitoring.
* **Monitoring Purpose Details** (`monitoring_purpose_details`): Provide additional details about the monitoring purpose (if required).

## Tips

- Ensure all required fields are completed before submission.
- Review the form carefully to avoid errors.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact your internal security team for clarification.
