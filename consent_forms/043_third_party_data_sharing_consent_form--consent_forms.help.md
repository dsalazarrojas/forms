# Third Party Data Sharing Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Third Party Data Sharing Consent Form is designed to collect your consent for sharing your data with third-party entities. This form is crucial for ensuring that you understand and agree to the sharing of your data with parties outside our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Read through each field carefully to understand the questions and ensure you're answering honestly.
2.  If you're unsure about any field, refer to the explanations below for clarification.
3.  Fill out the form accurately and completely, providing the requested information.
4.  Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **I understand that my data will be shared with third parties**: This is a text field where you should express your understanding and consent to share your data with external parties.
* **Do you agree to share your data with third parties**: This is a select-one field with options like "Third Party A", "Third Party B", or "Third Party C". Choose the party or parties you're willing to share your data with.
* **Data will be shared with**: This is a select-multiple field where you can list all the third parties to be shared with, such as "Third Party A" and "Third Party C".
* **What data will be shared**: This field requires you to specify the categories of data that will be shared, such as personal details, medical records, or financial information.
* **What will be shared with third parties**: This field is an exact duplicate of the previous one, "What data will be shared", and is likely redundant. Please skip it.
* **Where will data be shared**: This field should be used to detail where your data will be stored or accessed, like "cloud servers" or "data centers".
* **With whom will data be shared**: This is another field with an unclear purpose, potentially overlapping with "Data will be shared with". It seems intended to detail specific individuals or entities, but its relation to "third parties" is ambiguous.
* **Why will data be shared with third parties**: This field should be used to explain the reasoning behind sharing your data, such as for research, marketing, or collaboration purposes.
* **Any other data to be shared**: This field is for any additional data categories you wish to share with third parties, beyond what's already mentioned.
* **Contact Information**: This field is for providing your contact details, which might be used for further communication or clarification.
* **Name**: This is a text field for your name.
* **Email**: This is a field for your email address.
* **Phone**: This is a text field for your phone number.
* **Address**: This is a text field for your address.
* **City**: This is a text field for your city of residence.
* **Postal Code**: This is a text field for your postal code.
* **State**: This is a text field for your state of residence.
* **Country**: This is a text field for your country of origin.
* **Other**: This is a text field for any other information you'd like to provide.
* **Date of Birth**: This field is for your date of birth.
* **Signature**: This is a text field for your physical signature, indicating your consent.
* **Date of Signature**: This is a date field for the date when you sign the form.
* **What else will be shared with third parties**: This field seems to be another near-duplicate of "What data will be shared", and is likely redundant.
* **What else will be shared with third parties**: This field has an unclear purpose, potentially overlapping with "What data will be shared", and its relation to "third parties" is ambiguous.
* **Submit**: This is a text field where you'll submit your form.
