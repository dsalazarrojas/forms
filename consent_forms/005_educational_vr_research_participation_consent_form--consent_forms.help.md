# Educational VR Research Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from participants for educational VR research studies. It includes fields for participant details, consent, demographics, equipment, research study, survey, emergency contact, participant declaration, consent date, consent time, signature date, and signature image.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Participant Information**: Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant details about yourself as a participant.
2. **Consent**: Provide your informed consent for participating in the research study.
3. **Intake Details**: Describe your current health and medical conditions, medications, or any other relevant details for the research study.
4. **Demographics**: Enter your age, gender, and other demographics relevant to the research study.
5. **Equipment**: List any equipment or devices you will be using during the research study.
6. **Research Study**: Provide details about the research study, including its purpose, methods, and any other relevant information.
7. **Survey**: Describe the survey you will be participating in, including its purpose and any other relevant details.
8. **Emergency Contact**: Provide contact information for an emergency contact person.
9. **Participant Declaration**: Declare your understanding of the research study and your role as a participant.
10. **Consent Date**: Enter the date you are providing your consent.
11. **Consent Time**: Enter the time you are providing your consent.
12. **Signature Date**: Enter the date you sign this consent form.
13. **Signature Image**: Capture a clear image of your signature.
