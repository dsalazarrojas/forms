# Facial Consent For COVID 19 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facial Consent For COVID 19 form is used to obtain informed consent from clients before providing aesthetic or spa services. This form ensures that clients understand the treatments they are receiving and provides an informed consent to proceed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and fill out all required fields.
2. Answer all questions honestly and accurately.
3. Sign and date all signature fields where required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facial Consent Form** (`consent_form`, note, required: false): This is a note section for clients to review and understand the consent form.
* **Client Consent** (`client_consent`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' to confirm you understand the consent form and agree to the treatments.
* **Service Treated** (`service_treatments`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the services that will be provided, if applicable.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter your contact information.
* **Treatment Date** (`treatment_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the treatment.
* **Treatment Time** (`treatment_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the treatment.
* **Service Provider** (`service_provider`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the service provider.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Enter the location where the treatment will take place.
* **Consent Signature** (`consent_signature`, text, required: false): Sign and date this section to confirm your consent.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the consent.
* **Consent Time** (`consent_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the consent.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, text, required: false): Sign your name to confirm your consent.
* **Client Signature Date** (`client_signature_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your signature.
* **Client Signature Time** (`client_signature_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of your signature.
* **Spa Name** (`spa_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the spa.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required: false): Enter the contact name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the treatment.
* **Client Notes** (`client_notes`, text, required: false): Enter any notes about the client.
* **Spa Notes** (`spa_notes`, text, required: false): Enter any notes about the spa.
* **Service Notes** (`service_notes`, text, required: false): Enter any notes about the service.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, required: false): Enter any payment terms or conditions.
* **Client Signature 2** (`client_signature_2`, text, required: false): Sign and date this section to confirm your consent.

## Tips
- Ensure that you understand the consent form before signing.
- Review each field carefully and answer accurately.
- Sign and date all signature fields where required.
