# Tattoo Aftercare Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about the client's understanding of tattoo aftercare instructions and the responsibilities of the studio and artist.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and answer each question honestly.
2. If you have any questions or concerns, ask your artist for clarification.
3. Complete all required fields (marked as "required") to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Client understands aftercare instructions
* **Client understands aftercare instructions** (`client_understands_aftercare`, number, required: false): This field is used to track whether the client understands the aftercare instructions provided by the studio.

* **Note**: This field is not required but is essential for ensuring that clients have a clear understanding of their post-tattoo care responsibilities.

### 2. Studio responsible
* **Studio responsible for aftercare instructions** (`studio_responsible`, select_one, required: false): This field is used to indicate whether the studio is responsible for providing aftercare instructions.

*   *Note: If 'No' is selected, the artist will be responsible for aftercare instructions.*

### 3. Artist responsible
* **Artist responsible for aftercare instructions** (`artist_responsible`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is used to indicate which artists or artists are responsible for providing aftercare instructions.

*   *Note: Select all artists who will be providing aftercare instructions to the client.*

### 4. Client acknowledgment of aftercare instructions
* **Client acknowledgment of aftercare instructions** (`client_acknowledgment`, time, required: false): This field is used to record the time and date when the client acknowledged the aftercare instructions.

*   *Note: This field is not required but is useful for tracking client acknowledgment.*

### 5. Artist Name
* **Artist Name** (`artist_name`, text, required: false): This field is used to record the name of the artist who provided the tattoo service.

*   *Note: This field is not required but is helpful for tracking artist involvement.*

### 6. Client Name
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required: false): This field is used to record the client's name.

*   *Note: This field is not required but is helpful for tracking client information.*

### 7. Client Phone
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, required: false): This field is used to record the client's phone number.

*   *Note: This field is not required but is useful for contacting the client.*

### 8. Client Email
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, required: false): This field is used to record the client's email address.

*   *Note: This field is not required but is helpful for contacting the client.*

### 9. Date of service
* **Date of service** (`date_of_service`, date, required: false): This field is used to record the date of the tattoo service.

*   *Note: This field is not required but is helpful for tracking service dates.*

## Tips
- Be honest when answering the questions.
- Ensure that you have understood the aftercare instructions provided by the studio.
- If you have any questions or concerns, ask your artist for clarification.
