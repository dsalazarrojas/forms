## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to capture an employee's consent for telecommuting, allowing them to work from home on specific days and hours, as well as their manager's approval and note.

The form is designed for employees to submit their requests to work from home and for managers to review and approve or decline these requests.

Before proceeding, ensure you understand your company's telecommuting policy and that this form is only for employees who are eligible to work from home. This form was generated using AI, but it's been designed with human understanding in mind, so don't hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or need further clarification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** accurately.
2. Provide a valid **Email** address that can be contacted by your manager or HR representative.
3. Identify your **Department** (if applicable).
4. Enter your **Job Title** as it appears on your company's official records.
5. Select the days you wish to work from home using the provided options (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday).
6. Specify the hours you will work from home using the 24-hour clock format (e.g., 09:00 - 17:00).
7. Choose your preferred **Remote Work Arrangement** (Hybrid, Full Remote, Part-Remote, Flexible).
8. Indicate if your **Manager Approval** is required for your work-from-home arrangement. If it is not applicable, select "Not Applicable".
9. If your manager approval is required, they will see a **Manager Note** field where they can leave additional comments.

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official company records.
