# Donor Quote Publication Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from a donor to publish a quote.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you agree to the publication of your quote.
2. Sign your name to confirm your consent.
3. Enter your first name.
4. Enter your last name.
5. Enter your email address.
6. Enter your phone number.
7. Select the date you are submitting this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Quote Publication Consent** (`donor_quote_publication_consent_form`, select_multiple, required: false): This field asks you to indicate whether you agree to the publication of your quote. Select "Yes" to agree, or "No" to decline.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required: false): This is where you sign your name to confirm your consent. Please print your name clearly.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address here. This will help us contact you with any questions or concerns.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number here. This will help us contact you with any questions or concerns.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Select the date you are submitting this form.

## Tips
* Make sure to select "Yes" to agree to the publication of your quote.
* Enter your name and email address clearly and accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [insert contact information].
