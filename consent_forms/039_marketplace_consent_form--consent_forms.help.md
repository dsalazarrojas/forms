# Marketplace Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketplace Consent Form is designed to collect essential information about your relationship with the marketplace. It's a series of questions that will help us understand how you plan to use our service and ensure a smooth experience for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and ensure you understand the questions and options presented.
2. Choose the correct answers for each question, and select the options that apply to your situation.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask, and we'll be happy to help.
4. Once you've completed the form, click submit to send your information to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: What is your relationship with the marketplace?
* **What is your relationship with the marketplace?** (`marketplace_consent_form_first_page`, select_one, required/optional): This question helps us understand your connection to the marketplace. Please select 'Yes' if you have a business or organization account with us, and 'No' if you're new to the service.

### Page 2: How do you plan to use this marketplace?
* **How do you plan to use this marketplace?** (`marketplace_consent_form_second_page`, select_multiple, required/optional): This question helps us understand how you intend to use our service. Select the options that apply to your situation, such as 'Yes' if you plan to list your products or services, and 'No' if you don't plan to use it for business purposes.

### Page 3: How will you be accessing the marketplace?
* **How will you be accessing the marketplace?** (`marketplace_consent_form_third_page`, select_one, required/optional): This question helps us understand how you'll be interacting with our service. Choose 'Yes' if you'll be accessing through an organization account, and 'No' if you're a personal user.

### Page 4: Do you have any other businesses or organizations that you represent?
* **Do you have any other businesses or organizations that you represent?** (`marketplace_consent_form_fourth_page`, select_multiple, required/optional): This question helps us understand if you're representing multiple entities. Select 'Yes' if you have other businesses or organizations you're representing, and 'No' if it's a single business or organization.

### Page 5: Have you ever had a business or organization account with us before?
* **Have you ever had a business or organization account with us before?** (`marketplace_consent_form_fifth_page`, select_one, required/optional): This question helps us determine if you're a returning user. Choose 'Yes' if you've had an account with us before, and 'No' if it's your first time using our service.

### Page 6: What is your role within your current or past business/organization?
* **What is your role within your current or past business/organization?** (`marketplace_consent_form_sixth_page`, select_multiple, required/optional): This question helps us understand your position within the organization. Select the roles that apply to your situation, such as 'Yes' if you're a CEO or owner, and 'No' if you're an employee.

### Page 7: Have you read and agree to the terms of service?
* **Have you read and agree to the terms of service?** (`marketplace_consent_form_seventh_page`, select_one, required/optional): This question confirms your understanding and agreement to our terms and conditions. Choose 'Yes' to confirm your understanding and agreement, and 'No' if you haven't read or don't agree to the terms.

### Page 8: Is this your first time using our service?
* **Is this your first time using our service?** (`marketplace_consent_form_eighth_page`, select_one, required/optional): This question helps us understand if you're new to our service. Choose 'Yes' if it's your first time using our service, and 'No' if you've used it before.

### Page 9: Is this your first time using a service like this?
* **Is this your first time using a service like this?** (`marketplace_consent_form_ninth_page`, select_one, required/optional): This question helps us understand if you're new to services like ours. Choose 'Yes' if it's your first time using a service like this, and 'No' if you've used similar services before.

### Page 10: Do you have any questions or concerns?
* **Do you have any questions or concerns?** (`marketplace_consent_form_tenth_page`, text, required/optional): This question allows you to ask any questions or express concerns you might have. Please feel free to use this space to provide any additional information or clarify any doubts you may have.
