# Pedagogical Reflection Collection Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pedagogical Reflection Collection Consent Form is a tool to collect information about users who submit their pedagogical reflections, such as their name, contact email, and consent to use their data. The form is designed to be a consent-based data collection mechanism to ensure that users understand and agree to the use of their data.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Form Name** (Field 1) - enter a name to identify the form you are submitting.
2. Provide your **User Name** (Field 2) - enter your name as it appears on your university or institution's records.
3. Enter your **Email** (Field 3) - enter the email address associated with your university or institution's account.
4. Enter your **Contact Email** (Field 4) - this is the email address where you would like to receive updates or notifications.
5. Select one or more of the options in the **Consent Given** (Field 5) - check all of the options that apply to you.
   - **Consent to use the form data**: indicates that you consent to the collection and use of the form data.
   - **Consent to use data for research**: indicates that you consent to the use of the data for research purposes.
   - **Consent to use data for other purposes**: indicates that you consent to the use of the data for other purposes.
6. Select a date and time in the **Consent Date** and **Consent Time** (Fields 6 and 7) - these fields are not required but can be used to track when you gave your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Name** (`form_name`, text, required: false): Enter a name to identify the form you are submitting.
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required: false): Provide your name as it appears on your university or institution's records.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address associated with your university or institution's account.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address where you would like to receive updates or notifications.
* **Consent Given** (`consent_given`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or more of the options below to indicate your consent. 
  - **Consent to use the form data**: indicates that you consent to the collection and use of the form data.
  - **Consent to use data for research**: indicates that you consent to the use of the data for research purposes.
  - **Consent to use data for other purposes**: indicates that you consent to the use of the data for other purposes.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, required: false): Select a date to track when you gave your consent.
* **Consent Time** (`consent_time`, time, required: false): Select a time to track when you gave your consent.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, feel free to reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
