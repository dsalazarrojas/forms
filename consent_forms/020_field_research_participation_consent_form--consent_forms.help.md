# Field Research Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Field Research Participation Consent Form is a mandatory document that captures the necessary information from potential participants to ensure their involvement and consent in field research studies. It's essential to provide accurate and up-to-date information to facilitate smooth data collection and analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Fill in your personal details in the form fields.
2.  Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
3.  Review each field carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the research project or study you are participating in.
	+ This field is meant to provide a brief and descriptive title of the research project or study.
* **Researcher's Name** (`researcher_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the researcher or person conducting the study.
	+ This field captures the name of the researcher or person conducting the study.
* **Research Title** (`research_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the research project or study.
	+ This field is meant to provide a brief and descriptive title of the research project or study.
* **Research Description** (`research_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the research project or study.
	+ This field helps to provide a better understanding of the research project or study.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as a participant.
	+ This field captures your name as a participant in the study.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
	+ This field is meant to provide a contact point for communication and updates about the study.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
	+ This field is meant to provide an additional contact point for communication and updates about the study.

## Tips
* Be accurate and honest in your responses.
* Review each field carefully before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the researcher or study administrator.
