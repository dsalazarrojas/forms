# return_to_activity_clearance_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for students to clear their activities after participating in a school program, ensuring proper documentation and consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Activity** name, which is the title of the activity you participated in.
2. Fill in the **Student's Name**, which is your name as recorded in the system.
3. Enter the **Parent's Name**, which is your parent's name as recorded in the system.
4. Select the **Date Signed**, which is the date you're signing this form.
5. If applicable, select the **Time Signed**, which is the time you're signing this form.
6. Choose who **Signed By**, which can be one of the options or Other (if not listed).
7. Add any **Comments** about your experience or activity.
8. Enter your **Email**, which is your contact information.
9. Enter your **Phone**, which is your contact information.
10. Select your **Student's Signature**, which confirms your agreement to the form contents.
11. Select your **Parent's Signature**, which confirms your parent's agreement to the form contents.
12. Choose who **Coacher's Signature**, which confirms their agreement to the form contents.
13. Select who **Staff's Signature**, which confirms their agreement to the form contents.
14. Enter the **Activity Date**, which is the date the activity took place.
15. Select the **Activity Time**, which is the time the activity took place.
16. Enter the **Activity Location**, which is where the activity occurred.
17. Choose the **Activity Type**, which categorizes the type of activity you participated in.
18. Enter **Activity Details**, which describes the activity you participated in.
19. Enter your **Student's Age**, which is your age at the time of the activity.
20. Enter your **Student's Grade**, which is your grade level at the time of the activity.
21. Select your **Student's Gender**, which is your gender.
22. Enter **Staff 1**, which is additional information about the staff involved.
23. Enter **Staff 2**, which is additional information about the staff involved.
24. Enter **Staff 3**, which is additional information about the staff involved.
25. Enter **Staff 4**, which is additional information about the staff involved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Activity**: Enter the name of the activity you participated in.
* **Student's Name**: Enter your name as recorded in the system.
* **Parent's Name**: Enter your parent's name as recorded in the system.
* **Date Signed**: Enter the date you're signing this form.
* **Time Signed**: Enter the time you're signing this form (if applicable).
* **Signed By**: Choose who signed this form (one of the options or Other).
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments about your experience or activity.
* **Email**: Enter your contact information.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact information.
* **Student's Signature**: Confirm your agreement to the form contents.
* **Parent's Signature**: Confirm your parent's agreement to the form contents.
* **Coacher's Signature**: Confirm the coach's agreement to the form contents.
* **Staff's Signature**: Confirm the staff's agreement to the form contents.
* **Activity Date**: Enter the date the activity took place.
* **Activity Time**: Enter the time the activity took place (if applicable).
* **Activity Location**: Enter the location where the activity occurred.
* **Activity Type**: Choose the type of activity you participated in (one of the options).
* **Activity Details**: Describe the activity you participated in.
* **Student's Age**: Enter your age at the time of the activity.
* **Student's Grade**: Enter your grade level at the time of the activity.
* **Student's Gender**: Choose your gender (Male or Female).
* **Staff 1**: Additional information about the staff involved.
* **Staff 2**: Additional information about the staff involved.
* **Staff 3**: Additional information about the staff involved.
* **Staff 4**: Additional information about the staff involved.

Note: Some fields are not required and can be left blank if not applicable.
