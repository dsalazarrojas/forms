# Hunting Expedition Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants and their legal guardians before embarking on a hunting expedition. The information collected will help us ensure the participant's safety and well-being during the expedition.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields to ensure that we have accurate and complete information about the participant.
2. For select multiple fields, select all applicable options.
3. Ensure that all dates and times are entered in the correct format (dd/mm/yyyy and 12:00 AM/PM).
4. Enter all contact information and emergency contact details accurately.
5. Sign and date all required signature fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information**: This section is where you will enter the participant's basic information, including their age, email, phone number, and address.
* **Legal Guardian**: This is where the legal guardian's information is entered, including their phone number and emergency contact.
* **Participant Age**: Please enter the participant's age in numbers.
* **Participant Signature**: This is where the participant will sign to confirm they have read and understood the consent form.
* **Legal Guardian Signature**: This is where the legal guardian will sign to confirm they have read and understood the consent form.
* **Participant Date of Birth**: Enter the participant's date of birth in the correct format (dd/mm/yyyy).
* **Participant Time of Day**: Enter the participant's time of day in the correct format (12:00 AM/PM).
* **Participant Emergency Contact**: Enter the participant's emergency contact information.
* **Legal Guardian Emergency Contact**: Enter the legal guardian's emergency contact information.
* **Participant Email**: Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Vehicles**: This is where you can enter any vehicles the participant has access to.
* **Participant Medical Conditions**: If the participant has any medical conditions, please enter them here.
* **Participant Medication**: If the participant takes any medication, please enter it here.
* **Participant Allergies**: If the participant has any allergies, please enter them here.
* **Participant Injury History**: If the participant has any injury history, please enter it here.
* **Participant Emergency Services**: If the participant has any emergency services, please enter them here.
* **Participant Vehicles Insurance**: If the participant has any vehicles insurance, please enter it here.
* **Participant Medical Expenses**: If the participant has any medical expenses, please enter them here.
* **Participant Medical Expenses Covered**: If the participant's medical expenses are covered, please select 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Legal Guardian Medical Expenses**: If the legal guardian has any medical expenses, please enter them here.
* **Participant Medical Expenses Covered By**: If the participant's medical expenses are covered, please select 'Yes' or 'No'.
