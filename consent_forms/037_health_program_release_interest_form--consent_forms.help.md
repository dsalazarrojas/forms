# Health Program Release Interest Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Health Program Release Interest Form is designed to gather information from participants about their interest in health programs. This form helps to understand the type of programs participants are interested in, how they prefer to be contacted for follow-up questions or surveys, and their consent to be contacted.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form in its entirety, or you can choose not to fill out a field if it's not relevant to you.
* Enter your participant name in the "participant_name" field.
* Enter your email address in the "email" field.
* Select the type of health program you are interested in from the "program_type" dropdown menu.
* Confirm your consent to be contacted for follow-up questions or surveys in the "participant_consent" field.
* Choose how you would like to be contacted for follow-up questions or surveys in the "follow_up" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be referenced in the program.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Program Type** (`program_type`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following health program types:
	+ Wellness
	+ Fitness
	+ Health
* **Participation Consent** (`participant_consent`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm your consent to be contacted:
	+ False
	+ True
* **Follow-up Contact** (`follow_up`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted:
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ Mail

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields, even if they are marked as optional.
* Be accurate and honest when filling out your participant name and email.
* If you are unsure about any of the options, select "Unknown" or "Do not provide" for that field.
* You can update any field after submitting the form, but keep in mind that your previous answers will be lost.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
