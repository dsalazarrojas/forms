# Pledge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect pledges from users for donations or financial commitments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field.
4. Select the **Amount** of your pledge from the dropdown list.
5. Choose the **Currency** of your pledge from the dropdown list.
6. Select the **Frequency** of your pledge: choose between **Monthly**, **Once**, or **Yearly**.
7. Determine if the pledge frequency is **Yes** or **No**.
8. If the frequency is **Yes**, choose the **Frequency Interval** (e.g., 1, 2, 3, etc.) to specify how often the pledge is made.
9. Choose whether to include the **Frequency Unit** (e.g., weeks, months, etc.).
10. Select the **Frequency Start** date to specify when the pledge starts.
11. Optionally enter any additional **Notes** about your pledge.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address here.
* **Amount** (`Amount`, number, required/optional): Select the amount of your pledge from the dropdown list.
* **Amount Currency** (`Amount Currency`, select_one, required/optional): Select the currency of your pledge from the dropdown list (e.g., USD, EUR, JPY).
* **Frequency** (`Frequency`, select_one, required/optional): Choose between **Monthly**, **Once**, or **Yearly** for the frequency of your pledge.
* **Frequency Period** (`Frequency Period`, select_one, required/optional): If the frequency is **Yes**, choose the interval for the pledge (e.g., 1, 2, 3, etc.).
* **Frequency Unit** (`Frequency Unit`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether to include the frequency unit with your pledge.
* **Frequency Start** (`Frequency Start`, date, required/optional): Select the start date for the pledge frequency.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about your pledge.
