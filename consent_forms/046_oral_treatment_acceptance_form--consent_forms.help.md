# oral_treatment_acceptance_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The oral treatment acceptance form is used to gather information from patients regarding their acceptance of oral treatment. It is used to record the patient's acceptance or decline of a treatment, along with other relevant details such as appointment price and time. This form is essential for maintaining accurate records and ensuring that patients are informed about the treatment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Patient Info section with the patient's details.
2. In the Treatment Terms section, describe the treatment the patient will be receiving.
3. In the Patient Acceptance section, select either "Acceptance" or "Decline" to indicate the patient's response.
4. If applicable, enter the Appointment Price and select the appointment time.
5. If necessary, add any Additional Info regarding the patient or treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Info** (1, text, optional): Enter the patient's name, address, phone number, or any other relevant contact information.
- **Treatments Terms** (2, text, optional): Describe the oral treatment being offered, including any necessary details or explanations.
- **Patient Acceptance** (3, select_one, optional): Select "Acceptance" to indicate that the patient has agreed to receive the treatment, or "Decline" to indicate otherwise.
- **Appointment Price** (4, number, optional): Enter the price of the appointment if applicable.
- **Appointment Time** (5, time, optional): Select the scheduled appointment time if applicable.
- **Additional Info** (6, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the patient or treatment.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter the patient's correct information to ensure accurate records.
- Clearly and concisely describe the treatment terms to avoid any confusion.
- Select "Acceptance" or "Decline" accurately based on the patient's response.
- Only enter appointment price and time if the appointment is confirmed.
- Use the "Additional Info" field for any important details that may not fit in other fields.
