# Family Room Guidelines Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a crucial document for family members or legal representatives of care facility residents, which requires their consent and information for visiting a resident.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please read each field explanation carefully and fill in the required information.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled correctly, and if you are unsure about any field, please contact the care facility staff for guidance.
3. Once you have filled out the form, review it carefully before submitting it to the care facility.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`user_input_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Date of Birth** (`user_input_birthdate`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Relationship to Resident** (`user_input_relationship`, select, required/optional): Choose how you are related to the resident, either "Yes" for "Yes" or "No".
* **Acknowledgement** (`user_input_acknowledgement`, text, required/optional): Please acknowledge that you have read and understood the family room guidelines.
* **Email** (`user_input_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`user_input_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number for communication purposes.
* **Comments/Concerns** (`user_input_visitor_comments`, text, required/optional): Add any comments or concerns you may have regarding your visit.
