# medical_device_testing_participation_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information and consent for medical device testing. It's meant to be used for testing purposes, and users should understand that their data will be used for this specific purpose.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the participant details section with your personal information.
2. Choose your informed consent option from the provided list.
3. Provide details about the medical device testing you're participating in.
4. Select your participant status (Active, Inactive, or Not Applicable) and any additional participant notes.
5. Optionally, you can add any medical device testing participant notes.
6. Sign and date the form to confirm your participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`): Please fill out your personal details such as name, contact information, etc.
* **Participant Informed Consent** (`participant_informed_consent`): Select one of the provided options to indicate your informed consent for medical device testing.
* **Medical Device Testing Details** (`medical_device_testing_details`): Provide any additional details about the medical device testing you're participating in.
* **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`): Select all applicable options to indicate your consent for medical device testing.
* **Medical Device Testing Participants** (`medical_device_testing_participants`): Add any additional participant information if needed.
* **Medical Device Testing Results** (`medical_device_testing_results`): Record the date and time of the testing results.
* **Medical Device Testing Participant Status** (`medical_device_testing_participant_status`): Select your participant status (Active, Inactive, or Not Applicable) and any other relevant information.
* **Medical Device Testing Participant Notes** (`medical_device_testing_participant_notes`): Add any additional participant notes or comments.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`): Please sign and date the form to confirm your participation.
* **Medical Device Testing Participant Date** (`medical_device_testing_participant_date`): Record the date of your participation.

## Tips
- Make sure to review and understand the form's purpose and usage before filling it out.
- Fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your supervisor or the form administrators for assistance.
