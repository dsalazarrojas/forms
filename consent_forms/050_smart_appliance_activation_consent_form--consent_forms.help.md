# Smart Appliance Activation Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect details from customers for smart appliance activation. It is not a mandatory form and customers can choose to submit it or not. The form is designed to be used by utility companies, retailers, or installers to understand customers' preferences and needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer details on the first page.
2. Select your authorization type from the options provided.
3. Enter your activation details on the second page.
4. Choose a preferred date and time for activation from the options provided on the third page.
5. Add any additional notes on the fourth page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details (id: 1)** (`<type:text>`, required: false): Enter your name, email, and phone number to help us contact you for further assistance.
* **Authorization (id: 2)** (`<type:select_multiple>`, required: false): Select the entity authorizing your smart appliance activation. You can choose one or more of the following options: Utility Company, Retailer, or Installer.
* **Activation Details (id: 3)** (`<type:text>`, required: false): Enter your smart appliance's serial number, make, and model to facilitate activation.
* **Scheduling Preferences (id: 4)** (`<type:select_one>`, required: false): Choose a preferred date and time for activation. You can select one of the following options: 1st Available, 2nd Available, 3rd Available, Custom, or Not Available.
* **Notes (id: 5)** (`<type:note>`, required: false): Add any additional notes about your smart appliance activation. This is an optional field for providing more details.

## Tips

* Please provide accurate and detailed information to help us process your activation request smoothly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* This form is not a submission-based form, and you can choose to provide as much or as little information as you want.
