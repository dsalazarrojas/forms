# Policyholder Story Sharing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Policyholder Story Sharing Consent Form is designed to gather information from policyholders, including their story, consent for various aspects of data sharing, and personal details such as name, email, and phone number. This form is likely used in an insurance context for research or promotional purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand its purpose and the required information.
2. Fill in all the required fields accurately and completely.
3. Make sure to submit the form only once for each set of data collection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Policyholder Story Sharing Consent Form** (`policyholder_story_sharing_consent_form_page_1`, note): A brief overview of this form.
* **Policyholder Testimonial** (`policyholder_testimonial`, text): Share your story or experience related to your policy with us. This is your opportunity to tell us about any challenges or successes you've encountered.
* **Policyholder Permissions** (`policyholder_permissions`, select_multiple): Specify which aspects of your data can be shared with other parties. You can select one or more of the following:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Follow ups** (`follow_up`, time): Indicate the frequency and duration of follow-up contacts you'd like to have with us. Please use the format "X days" or "X months" where X is a number.
* **Data Collection** (`data_collection`, select_one): Choose how we can collect your data:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Approved Story Sharing** (`approved_story_sharing`, select_one): Specify if you approve of your story being shared with other parties:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Insurance Company** (`insurance_company`, text): If applicable, please enter the name of your insurance company.
* **Policyholder Name** (`policyholder_name`, text): Enter your full name.
* **Policyholder Email** (`policyholder_email`, email): Enter your email address.
* **Policyholder Phone** (`policyholder_phone`, text): Enter your phone number.
