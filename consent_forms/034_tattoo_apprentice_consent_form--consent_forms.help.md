# Tattoo Apprentice Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to capture the necessary information for a tattoo apprentice to provide consent for a tattooing session.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each field honestly and thoroughly.
2. Ensure that you understand each field's purpose and requirements before proceeding.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your mentor or studio staff for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Apprentice Information**: Please provide your name, address, and contact information.
* **Mentor Information**: Please provide your mentor's name, contact information, and a brief description of your current tattooing progress.
* **Studio Information**: Please provide the name and address of the studio where the tattooing session will take place.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Please read and agree to the terms and conditions of the tattooing process, including the risks and responsibilities associated with tattooing.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you understand each field's purpose and requirements before proceeding.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your mentor or studio staff for clarification.
* This form is a necessary step in the tattooing process, and your consent is required before proceeding.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, please do not hesitate to ask for help.
