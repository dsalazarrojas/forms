# Building Occupancy Tracking Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent for building occupancy tracking. It is intended for employees and occupants of a building to provide information about their occupancy data and how it will be used.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide your employee or occupant ID.
3. Select the department or area you are associated with.
4. Indicate the purpose and scope of tracking for which you are consenting.
5. Choose how your data will be used (e.g., for space planning, building optimization, security, etc.).
6. Select the methods and frequency of data collection (e.g., continuous real-time, daily, weekly, etc.).
7. Choose the level of detail collected (e.g., individual level, department level, etc.).
8. Select who will have access to your occupancy data (e.g., facility management, building management, etc.).
9. Determine whether data will be shared with third parties and indicate the retention period for the data.
10. Confirm that you understand the data anonymization and protection methods used.
11. Indicate your understanding of the security measures in place.
12. Confirm your rights and options regarding access to your own data.
13. Confirm you can withdraw your consent.
14. Confirm your understanding of the conditions for consent and withdrawal.
15. Select your preferred method of notification for updates on tracking or data use.
16. Provide contact information for a privacy officer or contact person.
17. Provide any questions or concerns about tracking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email address**: Enter your email address as it is registered in the organization's system.
* **Phone number**: Enter your contact phone number in case of any urgent matters.
* **Employee or occupant ID**: Enter your ID number as assigned by the organization.
* **Department or area**: Choose the department or area you are associated with for the purpose of occupancy tracking.
* **Tracking purpose and scope**: This field indicates the purpose and scope of tracking for which you are consenting. Please choose all that apply.
* **Tracking type**: This field indicates the type of occupancy tracking you are consenting to. Please choose all that apply.
* **Purpose of tracking**: This field explains why your occupancy data is collected. Please choose the relevant options.
* **Intended use of occupancy data**: This field indicates how your occupancy data will be used. Please choose all that apply.
* **Data collection methods**: This field indicates the methods used to collect your occupancy data. Please choose all that apply.
* **Collection technology**: This field indicates the technology used for tracking. Please choose all that apply.
* **Frequency of data collection**: This field indicates how often data is collected. Please choose one option.
* **Level of detail collected**: This field indicates the level of detail collected for occupancy tracking. Please choose one option.
* **Who has access to occupancy data**: This field indicates who has access to your occupancy data. Please choose all that apply.
* **Data sharing with third parties**: This field indicates whether data will be shared with third parties. Please choose one option.
* **Data retention period**: This field indicates for how long data is kept. Please choose one option.
* **Data anonymization and protection**: This field indicates how data is anonymized and protected. Please choose one option.
* **Security measures in place**: This field indicates security measures that are taken. Please choose all that apply.
* **Can you access your own data**: This field allows you to confirm whether you can access your own data.
* **Can you opt out of tracking**: This field allows you to confirm whether you can opt out of tracking.
* **Can you request data deletion**: This field allows you to confirm whether you can request data deletion.
* **Do you consent to building occupancy tracking**: This field confirms your consent to occupancy tracking.
* **Conditions or restrictions on consent**: This field allows you to indicate any conditions or restrictions on your consent.
* **Can you withdraw consent**: This field allows you to confirm whether you can withdraw your consent.
* **Do you understand your rights**: This field allows you to confirm whether you understand your rights.
* **Preferred notification method**: This field allows you to choose your preferred method of notification.
* **Privacy officer or contact person**: This field is for contact information for a privacy officer or contact person.
