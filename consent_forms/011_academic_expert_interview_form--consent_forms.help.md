# Academic Expert Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Expert Interview Form is a tool used to collect information from academic experts to aid in the creation of knowledge and research materials. The form gathers essential details such as the expert's name, affiliation, contact email, and consent to record and publish the interview. This information is crucial for scheduling and conducting the interview and ensuring that all parties involved are aware of and consent to the terms of the recording and publication.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your full name as the expert in the "Expert name" field.
2. Enter your institution or organization in the "Affiliation" field.
3. Type in your email address in the "Contact email" field to enable easy communication and follow-up.
4. Choose a proposed interview date in the "Proposed interview date" field.
5. Indicate your consent to record the interview by selecting either 'True' or 'False' in the "Consent to record" field.
6. Similarly, select whether you consent to publish the interview materials or not in the "Consent to publish" field.
7. Optionally, provide key themes or questions to be discussed during the interview in the "Topics to discuss" field.
8. Enter your name as the person conducting the interview in the "Interviewer name" field.
9. Provide any additional notes or instructions in the "Additional notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Expert name** (`expert_name`, required): Enter your full name as the expert being interviewed.
* **Affiliation** (`affiliation`, optional): Provide the institution or organization you are associated with.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, required): Enter your email address for scheduling and follow-up purposes.
* **Proposed interview date** (`interview_date`, optional): Select a preferred or scheduled date for the interview.
* **Consent to record** (`consent_to_record`, required): Indicate whether you consent to have the interview recorded.
* **Consent to publish** (`consent_to_publish`, required): Choose whether you consent to the publication of the interview materials.
* **Topics to discuss** (`topics_to_discuss`, optional): Provide key themes or questions for the interview.
* **Interviewer name** (`interviewer_name`, required): Enter your name as the person conducting the interview.
* **Additional notes** (`additional_notes`, optional): Add any special instructions or considerations for the interview.
