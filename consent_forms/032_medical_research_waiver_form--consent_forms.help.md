# Medical Research Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Medical Research Waiver Form is a document used to obtain consent from participants in medical research studies. The form is used to gather information about the participant's involvement in the study and to obtain their consent for the research.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction carefully and review the waiver section to understand the terms of the study.
2. If you are a participant, indicate whether you have been informed about the risks and benefits of the study. (Field 3: informed_consent)
3. If you have any health risks associated with the study, select "Yes" in Field 4: risks_involledged.
4. Provide any additional contact information in Field 5: contact_info, if necessary.
5. If you are a participant, sign your name in Field 6: signature.
6. Record the date and time of completion in Field 7: date and Field 8: time.
7. If you are a participant, select "Yes" in Field 9: waiver, indicating that you understand and agree to the terms of the waiver.
8. If you are a participant, sign your name in Field 10: participant_signature.
9. If you are a participant, provide any additional contact information in Field 11: participant_contact.
10. Enter the name of the research institution conducting the study in Field 12: research_institution.
11. Enter the name of the principal investigator of the study in Field 13: principal_investigator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (Field 1): A brief description of the study and its purpose.
* **Informed Consent** (Field 2): A statement indicating that you have been informed about the risks and benefits of the study. Please select "Yes" if you have been informed.
* **Risks Involledged** (Field 3): If you have any health risks associated with the study, select "Yes" in this field.
* **Participant Info** (Field 4): Select "Yes" if you are a participant in the study.
* **Contact Info** (Field 5): Provide any additional contact information you would like to share with the researchers.
* **Signature** (Field 6): Sign your name as a participant, indicating your understanding and agreement to the terms of the waiver.
* **Date** (Field 7): Record the date of completion.
* **Time** (Field 8): Record the time of completion.
* **Waiver** (Field 9): As a participant, select "Yes" indicating that you understand and agree to the terms of the waiver.
* **Participant Signature** (Field 10): Sign your name as a participant, indicating your understanding and agreement to the terms of the waiver.
* **Participant Contact** (Field 11): Provide any additional contact information you would like to share with the researchers.
* **Research Institution** (Field 12): Enter the name of the research institution conducting the study.
* **Principal Investigator** (Field 13): Enter the name of the principal investigator of the study.
