# Magazine Page License Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for individuals or companies who want to request permission to reuse content from magazine pages. The form helps content owners or publishers understand how the content will be used, ensuring that the reuse is done in accordance with their policies and guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the `First Name` field with your full name as it appears on your official documents.
2. Fill out the `Last Name` field with your full last name as it appears on your official documents.
3. Enter the name of the company you represent in the `Company` field, if applicable.
4. Describe how you intend to use the content in the `Intended Usage` field.
5. If necessary, attach supporting documents related to your request in the `Upload Supporting Documents` field.
6. Click the `Submit` button to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reuse Permission Request** (`Reuse Permission Request`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter a brief description of why you want to reuse the content.
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your full first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Enter your full last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Company** (`Company`, text, optional): If you represent a company, enter the name of your organization.
* **Intended Usage** (`Intended Usage`, text, optional): Describe how you plan to use the content, e.g., for educational purposes, research, or commercial use.
* **Upload Supporting Documents** (`Upload Supporting Documents`, text, optional): If you have any supporting documents related to your request, attach them here.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, text, optional): Click this button to send your request to the content owner or publisher.
