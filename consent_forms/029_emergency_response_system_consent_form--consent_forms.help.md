# emergency_response_system_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The emergency response system consent form is used to collect information from participants in emergency situations, helping the response team to provide timely and effective assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand the sections and fields that apply to your situation.
2. Complete each section according to your knowledge and circumstances.
3. Submit the form once you have filled in all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_details** (`participant_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as the participant in the emergency situation.
* **emergency_response_details** (`emergency_response_details`, text, required/optional): Describe the emergency situation and how you were affected by it.
* **contact_information** (`contact_information`, text, required/optional): Provide any relevant contact information, such as your address or email.
* **emergency_contact_phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **participant_phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for further communication.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to aid the emergency response team in assisting you.
* If you are unable to complete the form, please contact the emergency response team for support.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
