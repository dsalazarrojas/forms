# Music Remix Inclusion Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect user consent for the inclusion of their music remixes in various projects. Users must provide their contact information, project preferences, and agreement to terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each section carefully and fill out the required information accurately.
- Use the provided options for project and agreement selections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (User Information, number, optional): This section is used to collect user details. If you have any information to provide, please fill out the corresponding fields.
* **User Email** (User Email, email, optional): Enter your email address to enable us to contact you regarding your submitted music remix.
* **User Phone** (User Phone, text, optional): Provide your phone number for additional contact purposes.
* **Project Information** (Project Information, select one, optional): Choose the project you'd like your music remix included in from the available options.
* **User Terms** (User Terms, select multiple, optional): Select all terms that apply to your music remix.
* **User Agreement** (User Agreement, text, optional): This section is used to provide any additional agreements or comments you'd like to include with your music remix.
* **Music Remix Agreement** (Music Remix Agreement, select one, optional): Choose an agreement option for your music remix.
* **Submit** (Submit, select one, optional): Confirm your submission by selecting 'Yes' to submit your music remix.
* **Terms and Conditions** (Terms and Conditions, select multiple, optional): Accept the terms and conditions of your chosen project.
* **Privacy Policy** (Privacy Policy, select multiple, optional): Confirm your understanding of the project's privacy policy. 

Note: All sections are optional unless marked as 'required'.
