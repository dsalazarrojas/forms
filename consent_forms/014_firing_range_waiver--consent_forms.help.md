# Firing Range Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain a waiver from individuals who will be entering a firing range. It gathers information about their consent, potential dangers, and emergency contacts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and title in the "Firing Range Waiver" field.
2. In the "Legal Restrictions" field, list any medical conditions that may affect your ability to safely participate in the range.
3. In the "Potential Dangers" field, acknowledge that you understand the risks associated with the range.
4. In the "Range Rules" field, list any rules or guidelines you agree to follow while on the range.
5. In the "Emergency Contacts" field, provide the names and contact information of your emergency contacts.
6. In the "Medical Condition" field, list any medical conditions that may require special attention while on the range.
7. In the "Medical Supervision" field, specify the medical supervision required for your participation.
8. In the "Witness Signature" field, have a witness sign the form to confirm your presence and consent.
9. In the "Witness Name" field, fill in the name of the witness.
10. In the "Witness Title" field, fill in the title of the witness.
11. In the "Witness Relationship" field, specify the relationship between you and the witness.
