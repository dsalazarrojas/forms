# Facility Virtual Inspection Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facility Virtual Inspection Consent Form is used to obtain consent from facility staff for virtual inspections. This form ensures that the facility staff is aware of and agrees to the terms and conditions of the virtual inspection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the facility address in the "Facility Address" field.
2. Select the facility inspector from the "Facility Inspector" dropdown list.
3. Choose a preferred inspection time from the available options.
4. Enter the contact person's name in the "Contact Person" field.
5. Enter the contact person's phone number in the "Contact Number" field.
6. Enter the contact person's email in the "Contact Email" field.
7. Read and agree to the terms and conditions in the "Signed Consent" field.
8. Enter the facility manager's name in the "Facility Manager Name" field.
9. Enter the facility manager's email in the "Facility Manager Email" field.
10. Enter the facility manager's phone number in the "Facility Manager Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facility Address**: The address of the facility that will be inspected virtually. Please enter the full address to ensure accurate navigation.
* **Facility Inspector**: Select the individual who will perform the virtual inspection. This is typically the person responsible for overseeing the facility's compliance and safety.
* **Preferred Inspection Time**: The preferred time for the virtual inspection. This can be any 24-hour period of the day, seven days a week.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person who will be available during the inspection. This person will be responsible for answering any questions or providing additional information.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the contact person's phone number. This will be the number that the inspector can contact if needed.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the contact person's email address. This will be the email that the inspector can use for questions or concerns.
* **Signed Consent**: By checking this box, the facility staff is providing their consent for the virtual inspection. This ensures they are aware of and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Facility Manager Name**: Enter the name of the facility's manager or supervisor.
* **Facility Manager Email**: Enter the facility manager's email address.
* **Facility Manager Phone**: Enter the facility manager's phone number.
