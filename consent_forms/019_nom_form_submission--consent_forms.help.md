# NOM Form Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "NOM Form Submission" form is used to collect basic contact information and demographic details from users. It is a straightforward form designed to gather essential data that can be helpful in specific contexts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have not already submitted this form in the past to avoid duplication of effort.
2. Fill out the fields as accurately and completely as possible.
3. If you have any issues or questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to the relevant department for further assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name, as it is typically known to you.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name, as it is typically known to you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address. Please note that this will be used as a contact point.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, including country code if applicable.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): If you are affiliated with an organization, enter the name of your organization.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your contact information is up-to-date and accurate to allow for efficient communication.
* If you have any issues or questions while filling out this form, please don't hesitate to contact the relevant department for further assistance.
