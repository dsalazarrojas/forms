# Workplace Safety Training Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a mandatory waiver form that confirms an employee has completed and understood the relevant safety training program. It is essential to fill out this form accurately to demonstrate compliance with company safety policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your first name and last name.
2. Enter your date of birth.
3. Provide your address.
4. Enter your email (if applicable).
5. Choose the relevant training program from the dropdown menu.
6. Enter the date of the training program.
7. Describe the potential safety risks associated with your job or work area.
8. Acknowledge the safety training waiver and the legal protection provided.
9. Confirm your awareness of the safety award.
10. Confirm your awareness of safety awareness.
11. Sign the waiver form to signify that you have understood and completed the relevant safety training program.
12. Enter the date of the waiver.
13. (Optional) Provide your employer's name, address, supervisor's name, and title, and employee ID.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your current mailing address.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your work email address.
* **Training Program** (`training_program`, `select_one`, required): Select the relevant training program from the dropdown menu.
* **Training date** (`training_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when you completed the training program.
* **Safety Risks** (`safety_risks`, `text`, required): Describe the potential safety risks associated with your job or work area.
* **Acknowledgments** (`acknowledgments`, `text`, required): Acknowledge that you have read and understood the safety training waiver.
* **Legal Protection** (`legal_protection`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm that you are aware of and have read the safety training waiver and that you do not have any legal protection.
* **Safety Award** (`safety_award`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm that you are aware of the safety award.
* **Safety Awareness** (`safety-awareness`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm that you are aware of the safety awareness.
* **Waiver Form Signature** (`waiver_form_signature`, `time`, required): Sign the waiver form to confirm that you have understood and completed the relevant safety training program.
* **Date of Waiver** (`date_of_waiver`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the waiver.
* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your employer's name.
* **Employer Address** (`employer_address`, `text`, optional): Enter your employer's address.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `number`, required): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, optional): Enter your department.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Supervisor Title** (`supervisor_title`, `text`, optional): Enter your supervisor's title.
* **Safety Officer Name** (`safety_officer_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your safety officer's name.
* **Safety Officer Title** (`safety_officer_title`, `text`, optional): Enter your safety officer's title.
* **Date Created** (`date_created`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when the form was created.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.

## Tips

* Ensure that each form is submitted only once for each training program attended.
* Double-check the training program selected to ensure it matches the one you actually attended.
* Fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Sign the waiver form with a pen and date it.
* Keep a copy of the form for your records.
