# Myeloid Gene Panels Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Myeloid Gene Panels Submission Form is designed to collect information related to myeloid gene panels submissions. This form is intended for clinicians or other authorized personnel to collect details about the testing type, date, and results, as well as the clinician's information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the **first_name** field.
2. Fill in your last name in the **last_name** field.
3. Enter your date of birth in the **date_of_birth** field in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
4. Select the type of testing you are submitting (**testing_type**) from the options provided.
5. Enter the date of the testing (**testing_date**) in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
6. Enter the testing results in the **testing_result** field.
7. Provide submission information in the **submission_info** field.
8. Enter your name as the clinician submitting this form (**clinician_name**).
9. Enter your email address (**clinician_email**).
10. Enter your phone number (**clinician_phone**).
11. Enter the institution where you are submitting from (**clinician_institution**).
12. Enter your department (**clinician_department**).
13. Enter your title (**clinician_title**).
14. Enter the name of the laboratory where the testing was done (**lab_name**).
15. Enter the department of the laboratory (**lab_department**).
16. Enter the date of consent (**consent_date**) in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
17. Enter the consent version (**consent_version**).
18. Select the type of consent (**consent_type**) from the options provided.
19. Enter the name of the person signing the consent (**consent_signed_by**).
20. Enter the date the consent was signed (**consent_signed_date**) in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
21. Enter the title of the person signing the consent (**consent_signed_title**).
22. Enter any additional notes (**notes**, if applicable).
22. Review your submission carefully before clicking the **Submit** button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name** (text, required): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **last_name** (text, required): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **date_of_birth** (date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **testing_type** (select_one, required): Select the type of testing you are submitting:
	+ Blood test
	+ Saliva test
	+ Tissue test
* **testing_date** (date, required): Enter the date of the testing in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **testing_result** (text, required): Enter the results of the testing.
* **submission_info** (text, required): Enter any additional submission information.
* **clinician_name** (text, required): Enter your name as the clinician submitting this form.
* **clinician_email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **clinician_phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **clinician_institution** (text, required): Enter the institution where you are submitting from.
* **clinician_department** (text, required): Enter your department.
* **clinician_title** (text, required): Enter your title.
* **lab_name** (text, required): Enter the name of the laboratory where the testing was done.
* **lab_department** (text, required): Enter the department of the laboratory.
* **consent_date** (date, required): Enter the date of consent in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **consent_version** (text, required): Enter the consent version.
* **consent_type** (select_one, required): Select the type of consent:
	+ Patient
	+ Parent/Guardian
	+ Clinician
* **consent_signed_by** (text, required): Enter the name of the person signing the consent.
* **consent_signed_date** (date, required): Enter the date the consent was signed in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **consent_signed_title** (text, required): Enter the title of the person signing the consent.
* **notes** (text, optional): Enter any additional notes (if applicable).
* **Submit** (text, required): Review your submission carefully and click the **Submit** button to complete the submission process.
