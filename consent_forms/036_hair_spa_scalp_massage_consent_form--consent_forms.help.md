# Hair Spa Scalp Massage Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hair Spa Scalp Massage Consent Form is a crucial document that helps us understand your health and appointment details to provide the best possible service during your scalp massage. This form ensures your safety and well-being throughout the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Client Health Details section with your personal health information.
2. Select your preferred appointment schedule from the options provided.
3. List any allergies or sensitivities you have, if applicable.
4. Indicate whether you have given signed consent for the treatment.
5. Provide any relevant medical conditions that may affect your treatment.
6. Enter your contact information for us to get in touch with you.
7. If applicable, provide the date and time you signed this form.
8. Optionally, provide a note with any additional information about your appointment or concerns.
9. If you have any medical history or health concerns, provide a brief description.
10. If you have health insurance, provide your insurance details.
11. If you have an emergency contact person, please provide their name and contact information.
12. If you have any specific treatment requirements, please provide a brief description.
13. If you have any payment information, please provide it.
14. If you have any special instructions, please provide a brief description.
15. Finally, sign and date this form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Health Details** (`client_health_details`, note, required): This section is where you will provide your personal health details, such as any medical conditions, medications, or health concerns.
* **Appointment Info** (`appointment_info`, select_one, required): Please select your preferred appointment schedule, Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
* **Scalp Massage Details** (`scalp_massage_details`, date, required): This is where you will provide the date of your appointment.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, required): If you have any allergies or sensitivities, please list them here.
* **Signed Consent** (`signed_consent`, select_one, required): Please indicate whether you have given signed consent for the treatment.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, required): If you have any medical conditions that may affect your treatment, please describe them briefly.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, email, required): Please provide your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, required): This is where you will provide the date you signed this form.
* **Consent Time** (`consent_time`, time, required): This is where you will provide the time you signed this form.
* **Note** (`note`, text, required): If you have any additional information about your appointment or concerns, please provide it here.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): If you have a medical history, please provide a brief description.
* **Health Insurance** (`health_insurance`, text, required): If you have health insurance, please provide your insurance details.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): If you have an emergency contact person, please provide their name and contact information.
* **Treatment Info** (`treatment_info`, text, required): If you have any specific treatment requirements, please provide a brief description.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, required): If you have any payment information, please provide it.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, required): If you have any special instructions, please provide a brief description.
* **Signed By** (`signed_by`, text, required): This is where the client signs to confirm their consent.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required): This is where you will provide the date you signed this form.
* **Time Signed** (`time_signed`, time, required): This is where you will provide the time you signed this form.
* **Staff Signature** (`staff_signature`, text, required): This is where the staff member signs to confirm they have read and understood the form.
* **Staff Name** (`staff_name`, text, required): This is the name of the staff member who signs this form.
* **Staff Title** (`staff_title`, text, required): This is the title of the staff member who signs this form.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, text, required): This is where the client confirms their consent by signing this form.
