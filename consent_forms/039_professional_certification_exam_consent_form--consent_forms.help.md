## Professional Certification Exam Consent Form - Help Guide

### Purpose
The Professional Certification Exam Consent Form is a document that requires candidates to confirm their understanding and agreement to the exam terms and conditions before taking the professional certification exam. It ensures that candidates are aware of and agree to abide by the rules and regulations of the exam, which is crucial for maintaining the integrity and fairness of the examination process.

### How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the exam terms and conditions.
2.  Check the box next to "Contact Details" if you agree to provide your contact information.
3.  Select "Yes" or "No" next to "Contact Details" if you have any contact information (e.g., phone number, email) you would like to provide.
4.  Enter your educational institution's name, address, and other relevant information if applicable.
5.  Provide your exam scheduling information if applicable.
6.  Review and sign the form to confirm your understanding and agreement to the exam terms and conditions.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Candidate Consent**: This field requires your agreement and understanding of the exam terms and conditions. Please ensure you have reviewed and agreed to the terms before completing this field.
*  **Exam Terms and Conditions**: This field is a text-based explanation of the exam rules and regulations. Please carefully read and review this section to understand your responsibilities during the exam.
*  **Contact Details**: Please check this box if you would like to provide contact information (e.g., phone number, email) for communication purposes. If checked, please provide your contact details in the text field below.
*   **Contact Details**: This field is only required if you checked the "Contact Details" box. Please enter your contact information (e.g., phone number, email) if applicable.
*  **Educational Institution**: If you have an educational background, please enter your institution's name, address, and other relevant information.
*  **Exam Scheduling**: This field is only required if you have an upcoming exam scheduled. Please provide your exam scheduling information.

### Tips
*  Please ensure that you have reviewed and agreed to the exam terms and conditions before completing this form.
*  If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
*  The completion of this form is mandatory for all candidates participating in the professional certification exam.
