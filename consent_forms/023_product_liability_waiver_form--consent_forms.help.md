# Product Liability Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain product liability waiver from users before they use a product. It is an essential part of the product's usage agreement. Completing this form ensures users understand and acknowledge the risks associated with using the product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. Review each field carefully to ensure accurate and truthful information is provided.
3. Sign the waiver text field after completing the form.
4. Submit the completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Liability Waiver Form** (`form_title`, text, not required): Enter a title for the waiver form.
* **Description** (`form_description`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of the product and its usage.
* **Category** (`form_category`, select_one, not required): Select the category of the product liability waiver (Consent Forms, Waiver Forms, Liability Forms).
* **Subcategory** (`form_subcategory`, text, not required): Enter a subcategory for the product liability waiver.
* **Product Name** (`form_product_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the product.
* **Product Description** (`form_product_description`, text, not required): Enter a description of the product.
* **Product Image** (`form_product_image`, text, not required): Enter an image or reference to the product.
* **Waiver Text** (`form_waiver_text`, text, not required): Enter the waiver text to be signed by the user.
* **Waiver Signature** (`form_waiver_signature`, text, not required): Enter the signature of the user (recommended to be a digital signature).
* **Waiver Date** (`form_waiver_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the waiver.
* **Waiver Time** (`form_waiver_time`, time, not required): Enter the time of the waiver.
* **Waiver IP** (`form_waiver_ip`, text, not required): Enter the IP address where the user accessed the product.
* **Waiver YN** (`form_waiver_yn`, select_one, not required): Select "Yes" if the user understands and agrees to the product liability waiver.
* **Waiver Textarea** (`form_waiver_textarea`, note, not required): Enter any additional comments or notes for the waiver.
* **Waiver File** (`form_waiver_file`, text, not required): Upload any supporting files for the product.
* **Waiver Email** (`form_waiver_email`, email, not required): Enter the email address of the user.
* **Waiver Phone** (`form_waiver_phone`, text, not required): Enter the phone number of the user.
* **Waiver Note** (`form_waiver_note`, note, not required): Enter any additional comments or notes for the waiver.
* **Waiver Checkbox** (`form_waiver_checkbox`, select_multiple, not required): Select multiple options for the waiver (e.g., "Yes" for each option).
* **Waiver Radio** (`form_waiver_radio`, select_one, not required): Select a single option for the waiver (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Waiver Select** (`form_waiver_select`, text, not required): Enter a selection or choice for the waiver.
* **Waiver Multi Select** (`form_waiver_multi_select`, select_multiple, not required): Select multiple options for the waiver.
* **Waiver Upload** (`form_waiver_upload`, text, not required): Upload files for the waiver.
* **Waiver Email Upload** (`form_waiver_email_upload`, text, not required): Upload email attachments for the waiver.
* **Waiver Submit** (`form_waiver_submit`, text, not required): Click to submit the completed form.

## Tips
* Review each field carefully to ensure accurate and truthful information is provided.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the administrator for guidance.
* This form is for your protection and understanding of the product's usage.
