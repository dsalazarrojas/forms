# Oral Surgery Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Oral Surgery Consent Form is designed to collect information about the patient's consent for an oral surgery procedure. It helps ensure the patient and the surgeon are on the same page about the surgery, its risks, and benefits. This form should be filled out by the patient prior to the procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information, including your name, contact info, and address.
2. Enter your date of birth and the date of the procedure.
3. Provide the name of the procedure you're undergoing.
4. Enter your surgeon's name and any relevant experience.
5. Sign the form, choosing "Doctor's Signature" or "Patient's Signature".
6. Add any additional comments or notes as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info** (`Patient Info`, text, required/optional): Please fill in your personal contact information.
* **Patient Name** (`Patient Name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Patient Age** (`Patient Age`, number, required/optional): Enter your age at the time of the procedure.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, text, required/optional): Enter your personal contact information, including phone number and email.
* **Consent Date** (`Consent Date`, date, required/optional): Date of the consent form.
* **Procedure Info** (`Procedure Info`, text, required/optional): Describe the procedure you're undergoing.
* **Procedure Name** (`Procedure Name`, text, required/optional): Enter the specific name of the procedure.
* **Surgeon Info** (`Surgeon Info`, text, required/optional): Enter the name and relevant details of your surgeon.
* **Surgeon Name** (`Surgeon Name`, text, required/optional): Enter your surgeon's name.
* **Surgeon Experience** (`Surgeon Experience`, number, required/optional): Enter your surgeon's years of experience.
* **Patient Signature** (`Patient Signature`, text, required/optional): Sign this form to indicate your consent to the procedure.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, required/optional): Date of the procedure.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, required/optional): Time of the procedure.
* **Surgeon Signature** (`Surgeon Signature`, text, required/optional): Sign this form to indicate your surgeon's approval of the procedure.
* **Signature Type** (`Signature Type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose between "Doctor's Signature" or "Patient's Signature".
* **Signature Image** (`Signature Image`, text, required/optional): If applicable, upload an image of your signature.
* **Signature Text** (`Signature Text`, text, required/optional): Enter your signature text.
* **Signature Location** (`Signature Location`, text, required/optional): Enter where you signed the form.
* **Note** (`Note`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your procedure.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`Address`, text, required/optional): Enter your mailing address.
* **City** (`City`, text, required/optional): Enter the city of your residence.
* **State** (`State`, text, required/optional): Enter your state of residence.
* **Zip Code** (`Zip Code`, text, required/optional): Enter your zip code.

## Tips
- Ensure you sign the form before proceeding with the procedure.
- Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
- Make sure to provide your surgeon's contact information.
- Review and understand all the procedures and risks involved before signing.
