# Fundraising Guideline Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fundraising Guideline Acknowledgement Form is a mandatory document that ensures all parties involved in the fundraising process are aware of and agree to follow the guidelines set forth in the fundraising guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the guidelines carefully before starting the form.
- Select the correct acknowledgement option on the first page.
- If you have any comments or questions, please provide them in the Comments field.
- Enter your name and contact information, including your email and phone number.
- Select a start date and time for when you plan to begin using the guidelines.
- Select a finish date and time for when you plan to complete the fundraising activities.
- If you agree to follow the guidelines, select the "I agree" option.
- If you do not agree to follow the guidelines, select the "I do not agree" option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Fundraising Guidelines** (`fundraising_guideline_acknowledgement_form_page_1`): Please read and acknowledge that you understand the guidelines.
* **Acknowledgement** (`acknowledgement_form_acknowledgement`): Choose whether you agree or disagree with the guidelines.
* **Agreed** (`acknowledgement_form_agreed`): Select whether you agree or disagree with the guidelines.
* **Comments** (`acknowledgement_form_comments`): Enter any comments or questions you may have about the guidelines.
* **Name** (`acknowledgement_form_name`): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`acknowledgement_form_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`acknowledgement_form_phone_number`): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`acknowledgement_form_date`): Select the date you plan to start using the guidelines.
* **Time** (`acknowledgement_form_time`): Select the time you plan to start using the guidelines.
* **Comments** (`acknowledgement_form_comments`): Enter any additional comments or questions you may have.

## Tips
- Please note that the form must be completed once for each set of guidelines you are agreeing to follow.
- If you are unsure about any of the guidelines, please do not hesitate to contact the fundraising team for clarification.
- This form is not a substitute for actual compliance with the guidelines, but it is a necessary step to ensure that all parties are aware of and agree to follow the guidelines.
