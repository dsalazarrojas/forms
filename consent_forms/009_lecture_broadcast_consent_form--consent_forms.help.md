# Lecture Broadcast Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to capture the consent of participants to broadcast their lecture or presentation. It ensures that participants are aware of and agree to the terms of the broadcast, including what type of audio and video recording will be made, how it will be shared, and what the participant's role will be in the broadcast.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to answer all required fields.
3. If you have any additional comments or concerns, use the "Additional Comments" field to note them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the lecture or presentation.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as the participant.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Recording Consent** (`recording_consent`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if you agree to being recorded, "No" otherwise.
* **Sharing Consent** (`sharing_consent`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if you agree to share your recording, "No" otherwise.
* **Audio and Video Consent** (`audio_consent`, select_multiple, required): Select the types of recordings you agree to, including Video, Audio, or both.
* **Other Media** (`other_media`, text, optional): If you have other media you'd like to share, enter it here.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or concerns.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, required): Sign your name to confirm your consent.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of the lecture or presentation.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time of the lecture or presentation.
* **IP Address** (`ip_address`, text, required): Enter the IP address of your device.
* **Browser** (`browser`, text, required): Enter the name and version of your browser.
* **Operating System** (`os`, text, required): Enter the name and version of your operating system.
* **User Agent** (`user_agent`, text, required): Enter the user agent string of your browser.
* **IP Location** (`ip_location`, text, required): Enter your IP location.
* **Participant IP** (`participant_ip`, text, required): Enter the IP address of your device during the lecture.
* **Participant Browser** (`participant_browser`, text, required): Enter the name and version of your browser during the lecture.

Please note that you must have a valid reason for not completing all fields. For example, if you are presenting from a different network, you can explain this in the Additional Comments field. If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to us and we will be happy to assist you.
