# Community Health Initiative Filming Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to obtain informed consent from participants or their guardians for the filming and recording of their image, voice, and actions for educational and promotional purposes. The recordings may be used on social media, websites, and print materials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your participant's or guardian's name in the corresponding field.
2. Select your consent for each of the three recording types: video, audio, and still photography.
3. Type your full name to confirm your electronic signature.
4. Enter the date you signed this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Full Name**: Enter your full name, as displayed on your identification.
- **Legal Guardian Name**: Enter the name of your legal guardian, if you are under 18 years old. This field is not required if you are 18 or older.
- **Date of Recording**: This is the date on which the recording was made.
- **Consent for Video Recording**: Select "I Consent" if you agree to being recorded on video.
- **Consent for Audio Recording**: Select "I Consent" if you agree to having your voice recorded.
- **Consent for Still Photography**: Select "I Consent" if you agree to being photographed digitally or on film.
- **Use Case Agreement**: Read and agree that the media may be used for educational or promotional purposes, or select "Other" if you have specific requirements.
- **Withdrawal Policy**: Acknowledge that you can withdraw your consent before publication of the recording.
- **Electronic Signature Confirmation**: Type your full name as a confirmation of your electronic signature.
- **Date Signed**: Enter the date you signed this form.

## Tips
- If you are a minor, please make sure to have a legal guardian sign this form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the Community Health Initiative team.
- This form must be signed and submitted before any recording can take place.
