# Tenant Data Consent Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Data Consent Awareness Survey is designed to gather information from tenants about their consent and awareness on various aspects of data handling. This form aims to ensure that tenants are informed about how their data will be collected, stored, and used.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to understand the questions and their options.
2. For the "Tenant Consent" page, select "Yes" if you consent to having your data collected and stored.
3. On the "Data Collection" page, provide a brief description of how you think your data will be collected.
4. On the "Tenant Feedback" page, select "Yes" if you agree to receive emails from us about your data.
5. On the "Personal Data Use" page, select all the methods you are comfortable with your data being used for.
6. On the "Data Retention" page, select "Yes" if you agree with our data retention policy.
7. On the "Tenant Contact Info" page, provide a brief description of how you would like to be contacted.
8. On the "Data Sharing" page, select all the methods you are comfortable with your data being shared for.
9. On the "Contact Preferences" page, select "Yes" if you agree to receive contact from us.
10. On the "Survey Completed" page, select "Yes" if you have completed this survey.
11. On the "Consent Date" and "Consent Time" page, these fields are used to record the date and time of your consent.
12. On the "Tenant Survey Completed" page, select "Yes" if you have completed this survey.
13. On the "Tenant Survey Frequency" page, select "Yes" if you agree to be surveyed more frequently.
14. On the "Data Collection Method" page, select all the methods you are comfortable with your data being collected.
15. On the "Tenant Survey Method" page, select all the methods you are comfortable with your survey being conducted.

* **Consent Time** (id: 11): The time of your consent.
