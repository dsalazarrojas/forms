# Chaplaincy Support Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chaplaincy Support Request Form is a tool for individuals to seek chaplaincy care. It's essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the chaplains receive the necessary information to provide the best possible support.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling in the "Request Date" field with the date the support is requested.
2.  Enter your details in the "Requestor Name," "Requestor Email," and "Requestor Phone" fields. These are mandatory fields.
3.  Provide information about the person requiring support in the "Name of Person Needing Support," "Relationship to Recipient," and "Recipient Contact Information" fields. These are also mandatory.
4.  Choose the type of support needed in the "Type of Support Requested" dropdown menu. Select one of the available options, such as "Spiritual Counseling," "Crisis Intervention," or "Grief Support."
5.  Indicate the urgency of the support needed in the "Urgency of Support Needed" dropdown menu. Choose from "Emergency - Immediate," "Urgent," "Soon," or "Not Urgent."
6.  In the "Situation Description," provide a clear and concise explanation of why support is needed.
7.  If applicable, fill in the "Current Location of Recipient" field with the current location of the person requiring support.
8.  If necessary, provide medical information about the recipient in the "Relevant Medical Information" field.
9.  Indicate whether family members have been notified in the "Has Family Been Notified" dropdown menu.
10. If you have a preferred chaplain, enter their name in the "Preferred Chaplain" field.
11. Choose your preferred method of follow-up contact in the "Follow-Up Contact Method" dropdown menu.
12. Finally, confirm that you authorize chaplaincy contact by selecting "Yes, Authorized" from the "I Authorize Chaplaincy Contact" dropdown menu.

## Tips

*   Double-check the information provided to ensure accuracy and completeness.
*   Be clear and concise in your responses to provide the best possible support.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the chaplaincy team.
*   This form is for the chaplaincy care team to provide support.
