# Off Label Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Off Label Consent Form is used to collect consent from patients for the off-label use of medication. This form ensures that patients understand the risks and benefits associated with off-label use and provides their consent for it.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Complete each page thoroughly**: Each page of the form is designed to gather specific information related to off-label use. Please make sure to fill out each page carefully to ensure accurate completion.
2. **Provide patient information**: On the first page, please enter the patient's information, including any relevant medical history.
3. **Provide medication information**: On the second page, please enter the details of the medication being used off-label, including the name and dosage.
4. **Provide consent date**: On the third page, please select the date you are providing consent for off-label use.
5. **Sign as a physician**: On the fourth page, a physician or healthcare professional should sign to verify the consent is valid.
6. **Sign as a patient**: On the fifth page, the patient should sign to confirm their consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's name, medical record number, and any other relevant details.
* **Medication Information**: Enter the name of the medication, dosage, and any other relevant details related to its off-label use.
* **Consent Date**: Select the date you are providing consent for off-label use.
* **Physician Signature**: A healthcare professional should sign to verify the consent is valid.
* **Patient Signature**: The patient should sign to confirm their consent.

## Tips

* Make sure to review each page carefully before submitting the form.
* Ensure that the patient understands the risks and benefits associated with off-label use before asking for consent.
* Keep a record of the patient's consent and the off-label use case for future reference.
