# Healthcare Design Lab Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect participant information for a healthcare design lab study. Please read the form carefully and answer accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read the form and understand each field's purpose.
- Fill out all required fields, marked as "required".
- You can skip optional fields, but it's best to provide as much information as possible for a more accurate study.
- Once you've filled out the form, review your answers for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, optional): This field is to provide additional details about yourself as a participant. Feel free to include any relevant information that might be helpful for the study.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, optional): Please select "True" to indicate your consent for participating in the study.
* **Participant ID** (`participant_id`, number, optional): This is your unique identifier for the study. If you have a participant ID, please enter it here.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the best way for the researchers to contact you (Phone, Email, Mail, In-person).
* **Study Details** (`study_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional study-specific information.
* **Research Goals** (`research_goals`, text, optional): Describe the research goals of the study.
* **Study Duration** (`study_duration`, number, optional): If known, please indicate the expected duration of the study.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide any contact information that might be helpful for the researchers to reach you.
* **Study Name** (`study_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the study.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, note, optional): You can add any additional comments or notes about your participation in the study.

## Tips
- Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact the researchers.
- Thank you for participating in the study!
