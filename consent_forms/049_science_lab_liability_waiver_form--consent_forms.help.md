# Science Lab Liability Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Science Lab Liability Waiver Form is designed to collect essential information and permissions from participants and their guardians to ensure a safe and responsible experience for all parties involved in science lab activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your participant info, including any relevant contact details.
2. Provide your waiver, which includes your acknowledgment of the risks associated with participating in science lab activities.
3. Read and acknowledge the safety guidelines to ensure you understand the lab environment and equipment.
4. Sign the electronic signature to confirm you have read and understood the above sections.
5. If applicable, sign as a parent or guardian.
6. Provide school contact information, including school address, phone number, and email.
7. Fill in program contact information, including program name, director's name, and contact phone number.
8. Identify your emergency contact person and provide their name, phone number, and email.
9. List any medical conditions, medications, or allergies you have.
10. Confirm your emergency contact's relationship with you.
11. Sign the form to complete it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant info (1)**: Please provide your name, any other names you go by, and your date of birth.
* **Waiver (2)**: Please sign and date this section to acknowledge the risks associated with participating in science lab activities. These include physical and emotional risks, as well as potential exposure to hazardous materials and equipment.
* **Safety acknowledgment (3)**: Read and understand the lab environment and equipment. Acknowledge that you have received and understood the safety guidelines.
* **Electronic signature (4)**: This is your digital signature, confirming you have read and understood the above sections.
* **Participant signature (5)**: If you are 18 years or older, sign your name to confirm you are the participant.
* **Parent signature (6)** (if applicable): If you are under 18 years old, please have a parent or guardian sign and date this section.
* **School name (7)**: Please provide your school name.
* **School address (8)**: Provide the address of your school.
* **School phone number (9)**: Enter the phone number of your school.
* **School email (10)**: Enter the email address of your school.
* **Program name (11)**: Please enter the name of the program you are participating in.
* **Program director (12)**: Enter the name of the program director.
* **Program phone number (13)**: Enter the phone number of the program director.
* **Emergency contact (14)**: Provide the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Medical conditions (15)**: List any medical conditions you have, including any allergies or sensitivities.
* **Medications (16)**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies (17)**: List any allergies you have.
* **Emergency contact name (18)**: Provide the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency contact phone (19)**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency contact relationship (20)**: Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency contact info (21)** (optional): You may provide any additional information about your emergency contact.
* **Emergency contact email (22)**: Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Terms of service (23)**: Please read and agree to the terms of service, which outline your rights and responsibilities as a participant in the science lab activities.
