## mental_health_access_records_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mental health access records release form is used to obtain patient consent for accessing their mental health access records. This form is a critical part of the process for handling and storing sensitive medical information.
## How To Complete This Form
Before filling out the form, please make sure you have the patient's consent for accessing their mental health access records. This form is usually filled out by patients or their authorized representatives. Follow the instructions below to complete the form accurately.

### Step 1: Patient Name
* **Patient Name** (Patient Name, text, required): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification documents.

### Step 2: Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).

### Step 3: Address
* **Address** (Address, text, optional): Enter the patient's current address. This field is not required but recommended for proper identification.

### Step 4: Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required): Enter the patient's phone number. This field is required for contact purposes.

### Step 5: Email
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter the patient's email address. This field is not required but recommended for contact purposes.

### Step 6: Healthcare Provider Name
* **Healthcare Provider Name** (Healthcare Provider Name, text, required): Enter the name of the healthcare provider who will be accessing the patient's records.

### Step 7: Date Released
* **Date Released** (Date Released, date, required): Enter the date when the patient's mental health access records were released.

### Step 8: Authorization Type
* **Authorization Type** (Authorization Type, select one, required): Select the type of authorization for accessing the patient's records. Options are Medical Professional, Healthcare Organization, Both, or Other.

### Step 9: Release Date
* **Release Date** (Release Date, date, required): Enter the date when the patient's mental health access records were released.

### Step 10: Authorized Name
* **Authorized Name** (Authorized Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or entity authorized to access the patient's records. This field is not required but recommended for identification purposes.

### Step 11: Authorization Number
* **Authorization Number** (Authorization Number, text, required): Enter the authorization number assigned to the patient's mental health access records.

Please fill out the form accurately and completely. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the healthcare provider or relevant authorities.
