# Workplace Safety Training Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about employees' safety training history and consent for upcoming training programs. It helps to ensure that employees have received necessary training and are aware of their responsibilities for workplace safety.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully and check the boxes for the training programs you have completed.
2. Enter your employee ID, date, and time as required.
3. If you have any additional notes or comments, you can include them in the "Note" field.
4. Check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
5. Enter your employer's name, address, job title, and supervisor's contact information as required.
6. Select your department and location.
7. If you have completed any additional training programs, choose them from the list.
8. Check the boxes to confirm that your supervisor has approved your training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Is this form a safety training consent** (`safety_training_consent`): Please check yes or no if this form is a safety training consent.
* **Training Programs** (`training_programs`): Select all the training programs you have completed. You can choose from:
	+ OSHA 10-Hour
	+ OSHA 30-Hour
	+ Fire Safety
	+ Hazardous Materials Training
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`): Enter your employee ID number as required.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date you completed the training as required.
* **Time** (`time`): Enter the time you completed the training as required.
* **Note** (`note`): If you have any additional comments or notes, you can enter them here.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address as required.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number as required.
* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`): Enter your employer's name as required.
* **Employer Address** (`employer_address`): Enter your employer's address as required.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`): Enter your job title as required.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`): Enter your supervisor's name as required.
* **Supervisor Phone** (`supervisor_phone`): Enter your supervisor's phone number as required.
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`): Enter your supervisor's email address as required.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`): Enter your name as required.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`): Enter your phone number as required.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`): Enter your email address as required.
* **Department** (`department`): Enter your department as required.
* **Job Code** (`job_code`): Enter your job code as required.
* **Location** (`location`): Enter your location as required.
* **Shift** (`shift`): Enter your shift as required.
* **Training Programs Attended** (`training_programs_attended`): Select all the training programs you have attended. You can choose from:
	+ OSHA 10-Hour
	+ OSHA 30-Hour
	+ Fire Safety
* **Training Programs Completed** (`training_programs_completed`): Select all the training programs you have completed. You can choose from:
	+ OSHA 10-Hour
	+ OSHA 30-Hour
	+ Fire Safety
* **Supervisor Approval** (`supervisor_approval`): Check the box if your supervisor has approved your training.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`): Enter the date you completed the training as required.
