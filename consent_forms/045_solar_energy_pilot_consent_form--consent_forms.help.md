# Solar Energy Pilot Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide is meant to assist users in completing the Solar Energy Pilot Consent Form, which is a crucial document required for participation in the Solar Energy Pilot program. This form is used to capture essential information from participants, including their contact details and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (marked as "required") with your personal and relevant information.
2. Enter your name in the `participant_name` field.
3. Provide your email address in the `participant_email` field.
4. Enter your address in the `participant_address` field.
5. If you wish to add a contact method, select it from the `contact_details` field. You can choose from the options provided, such as "Participant Contact Details" or "participant contact method".
6. If you have any specific details about the solar pilot, you can enter them in the `solar_pilot_details` field, if required.
7. Sign and date the form (though it's not required, it's a good practice to have a signed and dated form for record-keeping).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name (`participant_name`, text, required)**: Enter your name as it appears on your official government-issued ID or passport.
* **Participant Email (`participant_email`, email, required)**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted for updates and information.
* **Participant Address (`participant_address`, text, required)**: Enter your physical address where you can be reached.
* **Contact Details (`contact_details`, select_multiple, optional)**: Choose one or more of the provided options to indicate your preferred method of contact. You can select from "Participant Contact Details" or "participant contact method".
* **Solar Pilot Details (`solar_pilot_details`, text, optional)**: Enter any additional details about the solar pilot, if required.
* **Participant Signature (`participant_signature`, text, optional)**: This field is not required, but it's a good practice to have a signed and dated form for record-keeping.
