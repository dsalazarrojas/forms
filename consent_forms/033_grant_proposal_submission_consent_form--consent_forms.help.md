# grant_proposal_submission_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The grant_proposal_submission_consent_form is used to gather information for grant proposals submitted to the organization. It is completed by the grant applicant or their representative to provide context and requirements for the grant proposal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read the form carefully and ensure you are submitting the correct form for the intended purpose.
* Enter the grant amount and start and end dates of the grant proposal.
* Provide the name and address of the organization submitting the grant proposal.
* Enter the contact person's name, email, and phone number.
* Select the type of grant proposal (yes/no).
* Enter the purpose of the grant proposal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Grant Amount** (`grant_amount`, Number, Optional)
Enter the amount of the grant being requested.
### **Grant Start Date** (`grant_start_date`, Date, Optional)
Enter the start date of the grant.
### **Grant End Date** (`grant_end_date`, Date, Optional)
Enter the end date of the grant.
### **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, Text, Optional)
Enter the name of the organization submitting the grant proposal.
### **Organization Address** (`organization_address`, Text, Optional)
Enter the address of the organization submitting the grant proposal.
### **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, Text, Optional)
Enter the name of the person representing the organization.
### **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, Email, Optional)
Enter the email address of the contact person.
### **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, Text, Optional)
Enter the phone number of the contact person.
### **Grant Type** (`grant_type`, Select Multiple, Optional)
Select one or multiple types of grant proposals.
### **Grant Purpose** (`grant_purpose`, Text, Optional)
Enter a brief description of the grant proposal's purpose.
 
## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the organization's contact person.
