# Science Lab Project Consent Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from students, parents, and teachers for a science lab project. The form gathers contact information, student and parent details, project description, and consent statements from all parties involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information, such as student and parent names, dates, and times.
2. Select the correct student age group from the drop-down menu.
3. Describe the project in detail.
4. Sign and date the form to indicate your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the student's name.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the parent's name.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date in MM/DD/YY format.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00 AM).
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the contact phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the contact email address.
* **Student Age Group** (`student_age_group`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the student's age group (e.g., 1st Grade, 2nd Grade, etc.).
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the project in detail.
* **Parent Name (Student's Last Name)** (`student_name_parent`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the parent's last name.
* **Relationship** (`student_name_parent_relationship`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the relationship between the student and parent.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, `text`, required/optional): Sign the form as the student.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, `text`, required/optional): Sign the form as the parent.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date the form was signed in MM/DD/YY format.
* **Time Signed** (`time_signed`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time the form was signed in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00 AM).
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the teacher's name.
* **Teacher Signature** (`teacher_signature`, `text`, required/optional): Sign the form as the teacher.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the school name.
* **Project Title** (`project_title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the project title.
* **Student Consent** (`student_consent`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the consent statement (e.g., I have read and understood this consent form, etc.).
* **Parent Consent** (`parent_consent`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the consent statement (e.g., I have read and understood this consent form, etc.).
* **Teacher Consent** (`teacher_consent`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the consent statement (e.g., I have read and understood this consent form, etc.).
