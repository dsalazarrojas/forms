# Gymnastics Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gymnastics Waiver Form is a document that helps protect the rights and safety of participants in a gymnastics program by obtaining informed consent and agreement from them and their parents/guardians about the associated risks.

## How To Complete This Form
Please complete the form by filling out all the required fields. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `text`, required: false): This is an optional introduction where you can explain the purpose and context of the waiver form.
* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, `text`, required: false): This section is where you can provide information about the participant's contact information.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required: false): In this section, please provide the name and contact information of the person to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Info** (`medical_info`, `text`, required: false): This is where you should provide any relevant medical information about the participant.
* **Waiver** (`waiver`, `text`, required: true): This is the main part of the form where you need to sign and agree to the terms of the waiver. Please make sure to read and understand the waiver before signing.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, `text`, required: true): This is where the parent/guardian signs to acknowledge that they have read, understand, and agree to the waiver terms.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, `text`, required: true): This is where the participant signs to acknowledge that they have read, understand, and agree to the waiver terms.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Please review and read the waiver terms carefully before signing.
- This form is for one-time use, not for recurring registration.
