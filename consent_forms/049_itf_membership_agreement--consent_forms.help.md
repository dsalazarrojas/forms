# itf_membership_agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information and agreements from members of an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Form Title** in the text field provided. This will be the title of the membership agreement document.
2. Select the **Category** of membership from the text field.
3. Provide a **Description** of the membership agreement in the text field.
4. Enter your **Member Name** in the text field.
5. Enter your **Contact Information** in the **Contact Information** field, which is a note field for additional details.
6. Enter your **Email** address in the email field.
7. Enter your **Phone** number in the text field.
8. Select the relevant **Member Terms and Conditions** from the dropdown menu. Please choose one of the provided options to indicate your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a title for the membership agreement document.
* **Category** (`form_category`, text, optional): Select the category of membership.
* **Description** (`form_description`, text, optional): Provide a description of the membership agreement.
* **Member Name** (`member_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as a member.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, note, optional): Enter any additional contact information you'd like to provide.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Member Terms and Conditions** (`member_terms`, select_one, optional): Select one of the three options to indicate your agreement to the membership terms and conditions.
