# Game Studio Public Relations Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from a game studio for public relations purposes, including marketing, media outreach, or other public communication activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information:
	* Game Studio Public Relations Consent Form: This is the title of the form.
	* Company: Enter the name of the game studio you are interacting with.
	* Email: Enter the email address of the contact person.
	* Name: Enter the name of the contact person.
	* Phone: Enter the contact phone number.
	* Address: Enter the physical address of the game studio (if applicable).
	* Contact Person: Enter the name of the person to contact.
	* Message: Enter a brief message or comment.
	* Company Consent: Select 'Yes' if the company consents to public relations engagement.
	* Terms Use: Select 'Yes' if you agree to the terms of use for public relations.
	* Date: Optional field for recording the date of the consent.
	* Time: Optional field for recording the time of the consent.
	* Note: Optional field for additional comments or notes.
