# COVID 19 Salon Company Consent Form Premiere - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect critical information from guests visiting the Premiere Salon Company during the COVID-19 pandemic, ensuring their safety and well-being while at the salon.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your **Guest Full Name** (Field 1) with your full name as it appears on your official identification.
2. Enter your **Phone Number** (Field 2) accurately, as this will be used for contact and contact tracing purposes.
3. If you have a preferred stylist, please type their name in the **Preferred Stylist** (Field 3) field.
4. In **Premiere Membership ID** (Field 4), please enter your membership ID number, if applicable.
5. Answer the question in **I acknowledge that I am free of COVID-19 symptoms** (Field 5) by selecting "I acknowledge" if you are not experiencing any COVID-19 symptoms.
6. For **I acknowledge that I have not traveled internationally in the last 14 days** (Field 6), select "I acknowledge" if you have not traveled internationally in the last 14 days.
7. In **I agree to follow the Premiere Salon PPE and mask policy** (Field 7), select "I agree" if you are willing to follow the salon's PPE and mask policy.
8. For **I agree to sanitize my hands upon entering the salon** (Field 8), select "I agree" to indicate your understanding and agreement to this policy.
9. Select "I agree and release" in **I release Premiere Salon and Spa from liability for COVID-19 exposure** (Field 9) to release the salon from liability for any COVID-19 exposure.
10. For **I understand the COVID-19 cancellation and safety surcharge policies** (Field 10), select "I understand" to acknowledge your understanding of these policies.
11. In **Appointment Time and Date** (Field 11), please enter your appointment time and date for the purpose of contact and contact tracing.
12. Finally, certify the truth of the above information in **I certify the above information is true** (Field 12) by selecting "True".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Full Name (Field 1)**: A text field to collect the full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Phone Number (Field 2)**: A text field for your contact and contact tracing information.
* **Preferred Stylist (Field 3)**: A text field to specify your preferred stylist.
* **Premiere Membership ID (Field 4)**: A text field for your membership ID number, if applicable.
* **I acknowledge that I am free of COVID-19 symptoms (Field 5)**: A select-one field to indicate that you do not have any COVID-19 symptoms.
* **I acknowledge that I have not traveled internationally in the last 14 days (Field 6)**: A select-one field to confirm your recent travel history.
* **I agree to follow the Premiere Salon PPE and mask policy (Field 7)**: A select-one field to indicate your agreement to the salon's PPE and mask policy.
* **I agree to sanitize my hands upon entering the salon (Field 8)**: A select-one field to confirm your understanding and agreement to sanitizing your hands upon entry.
* **I release Premiere Salon and Spa from liability for COVID-19 exposure (Field 9)**: A select-one field to release the salon from liability for COVID-19 exposure.
* **I understand the COVID-19 cancellation and safety surcharge policies (Field 10)**: A select-one field to indicate your understanding of these policies.
* **Appointment Time and Date (Field 11)**: A text field to record your appointment time and date.
* **I certify the above information is true (Field 12)**: A select-one field to certify the truth of the above information.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all information is accurate and truthful.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, please ask your stylist or our staff for assistance.
- This form is a prerequisite for a safe and enjoyable experience at the Premiere Salon Company.
