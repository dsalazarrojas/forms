# Client Satisfaction Survey Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather client feedback and satisfaction information after a service has been provided. It is intended to improve our service and help us understand your experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please enter your participant name and email address in the first two fields.
2. Select the date of service or purchase.
3. Review the consent and privacy information and select your agreement.
4. Choose your preference for contact and marketing updates.
5. Finally, date and confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (1): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (2): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your responses or follow-up questions.
* **Date of Service or Purchase** (4): Select the date you received the service or made the purchase.
* **-- Consent and Privacy Information --** (5): Please review the following information carefully before proceeding.
* **I understand this survey is to gather feedback on my experience** (6): Select "I agree" to confirm your understanding.
* **I understand my participation is voluntary** (7): Select "I agree" to confirm your consent.
* **I understand my data will be kept confidential** (8): Select "I agree" to confirm your consent.
* **I consent to my data being used for service improvement** (9): Select "I consent" to confirm your permission.
* **Would you prefer your response to be anonymous** (10): Select "True" if you wish to remain anonymous.
* **May we contact you for follow-up questions?** (11): Select "True" if you are okay with being contacted.
* **May we contact you with relevant updates or offers?** (12): Select "True" if you are interested in receiving updates.
* **I have read and agree to the privacy policy** (13): Select "I agree" to confirm your consent.
* **I have read and agree to the terms and conditions** (14): Select "I agree" to confirm your consent.
* **I provide full consent to participate in this survey** (15): Select "I consent" to confirm your consent.
* **Date of Consent** (16): Select the date you are giving your consent.
