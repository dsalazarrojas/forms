# Building Automation System Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain consent from the property owner for the installation, configuration, and monitoring of a building automation system. It covers essential details about the system type, scope of consent, and other related matters to ensure a smooth and secure implementation.
## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your property owner's name and title if applicable.
2.  Enter your company's name if it is a commercial property.
3.  Provide your contact email address for communication.
4.  Enter your contact phone number for immediate communication.
5.  Select the date you grant consent for the system installation.
6.  Enter the name of your property for the system's installation.
7.  Provide the full address of your property.
8.  Select the type of your property (residential, commercial, etc.).
9.  Enter the size of your building in square footage.
10.  Select the company providing the automation system.
11.  Choose the type of automation system you are installing.
12.  Select the scope of consent for the system's operation.
13.  Enter the date the system installation is expected to start.
14.  Select the duration of consent for the system's operation.
15.  Choose whether to collect building data.
16.  Select the types of data to be collected.
17.  Choose whether to allow remote system access.
18.  Select whether to share data with third-party partners.
19.  Choose the frequency of monitoring.
20.  Select your preferred notification method.
21.  Choose whether to grant emergency override authorization.
22.  Select how long consent is valid.
23.  Acknowledge your understanding of consent revocation terms.
24.  Finally, select "Yes - I Accept" to agree to all terms and conditions.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Property Owner Name** (`property_owner`, text, required): Your full name as the property owner.
- **Owner Title** (`owner_title`, text, optional): Your title if you have one (e.g., Mr., Mrs., Dr., etc.).
- **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): If you are a commercial property, enter your company's name.
- **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Your email address for communication purposes.
- **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Your contact number for immediate communication.
- **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, required): The date you grant consent for the system's installation.
- **Property Name** (`property_name`, text, required): The name of the property for the system's installation.
- **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required): The full address of your property.
- **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, required): The type of your property (residential, commercial, etc.).
- **Building Size** (`building_size`, number, optional): The size of your building in square footage.
- **Automation Provider** (`automation_provider`, text, required): The company providing the automation system.
- **System Type** (`system_type`, select_one, required): The type of automation system you are installing.
- **Scope of Consent** (`consent_scope`, select_multiple, required): The scope of consent for the system's operation, which includes system installation, configuration, remote monitoring, data collection, system maintenance, software updates, third-party access, and cloud connectivity.
- **Installation Start Date** (`installation_start`, date, optional): The date when system installation is expected to start.
- **Expected Duration** (`installation_duration`, text, optional): The estimated duration of the system installation.
- **Data Collection Consent** (`data_collection_consent`, select_one, required): Whether you consent to collect building data.
- **Data Types Collected** (`data_types`, select_multiple, optional): The types of data to be collected (e.g., energy usage, temperature, occupancy patterns, etc.).
- **Remote Access Authorization** (`remote_access`, select_one, required): Whether you grant remote system access.
- **Third Party Data Sharing** (`third_party_sharing`, select_one, required): Whether you allow data sharing with third-party partners.
- **Monitoring Frequency** (`monitoring_frequency`, select_one, required): The frequency of system monitoring (e.g., continuous, hourly, daily, etc.).
- **Notification Preferences** (`notification_preferences`, select_multiple, required): Your preferred notification method (email, SMS, phone call, etc.).
- **Emergency Override Authorization** (`emergency_override`, select_one, required): Whether you grant emergency override authorization.
- **Consent Duration** (`consent_duration`, select_one, required): The length of time consent is valid for the system's operation.
- **Revocation Terms** (`revocation_terms`, select_one, required): Acknowledgment of understanding of consent revocation terms.
- **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_accepted`, select_one, required): Selection of "Yes - I Accept" to agree to all terms and conditions.
- **Signature Acknowledgment** (`signature_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Acknowledgment of authorizing consent and terms.
