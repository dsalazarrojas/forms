# Vendor Content Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Vendor Content Release Form is a document used to obtain permission from vendors for the use of their content, materials, or other intellectual property. This form is essential for ensuring that the use of such assets is approved and compliant with applicable laws and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Select the correct options for the `content_type`, `partner_materials`, and `content_agreement`, `terms_agreement` fields.
3. Ensure that the dates and phone numbers are in the correct format.
4. Sign and date the form, indicating that the release agreement has been signed.
5. Review and submit the form for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendors Name**: Enter the name of the vendor providing the content.
* **Vendors Email**: Enter the email address of the vendor.
* **Content Type**: Select the type of content that the vendor is providing (e.g., text, image, video, etc.).
* **Date of Permission**: Enter the date on which the permission was granted.
* **Release Agreement Signed**: Indicate that the release agreement has been signed.
* **Agreement Expires**: Enter the date on which the agreement expires.
* **Partner Materials**: Select whether the partner materials are included (yes or no).
* **Vendors Signature**: Enter the signature of the vendor, confirming that they have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company associated with the vendor.
* **Company Title**: Enter the title of the company associated with the vendor.
* **Address Line 1**: Enter the first line of the company's address.
* **Address Line 2**: Enter the second line of the company's address.
* **City**: Enter the city of the company's location.
* **State**: Select the state in which the company is located (yes or no).
* **Zip Code**: Enter the zip code of the company's location.
* **Country**: Enter the country of the company's location.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the company.
* **Fax**: Enter the fax number of the company.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the company.
* **Website**: Enter the website URL of the company.
* **Content Agreement**: Select whether the vendor agrees to the content agreement (yes or no).
* **Terms Agreement**: Select whether the vendor agrees to the terms and conditions (yes or no).
* **Agreement Version**: Select the version of the agreement that has been signed.
