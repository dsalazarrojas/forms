# Meal Break Waiver Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to record an employee's consent for meal breaks. Completing this form helps you to keep track of meal breaks and ensures that your consent is valid.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your information on each field as prompted.
2. Ensure you fill in all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (first name, text, required/optional): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (last_name, text, required): Enter your last name here.
* **Employee Number** (employee number, number, required): Enter your employee number here.
* **Date Signed** (date_signed, date, optional): If you have a date you'd like to use for your signature, enter it here.
* **Time Signed** (time_signed, time, required): Enter the time you signed this form.
* **Signature** (signature, note, required): Sign this form to confirm your consent for meal breaks.
* **Employee Name** (employee_name, text, optional): Enter your name here if you'd like to include it.
* **Department** (department, text, optional): If you'd like to include your department, enter it here.
* **Shift Number** (shift_number, number, optional): If you'd like to include your shift number, enter it here.
* **Date Waived** (date_waived, date, optional): Enter the date you're waiving meal breaks for this period.
## Tips

- Be sure to read the form carefully before submitting.
- Fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
