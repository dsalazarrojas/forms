# Documentary Project Distribution Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information and consent from individuals involved in documentary projects. It's used to gather data on users' personal details, project specifics, and consent for project-related activities. The form ensures that users understand the rights and permissions required for project distribution and that they have given their consent in writing.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete the form, follow these steps:

    1.  Ensure you have all required information and permissions before filling out the form.
    2.  Start by filling out the "User Information" page, providing your name, contact details, and any other requested information.
    3.  Proceed to the "Project Information" page, where you will enter details about the documentary project, such as its title, location, and duration.
    4.  On the "Distribution Rights" page, explain the rights you are granting or requesting for the documentary project.
    5.  Sign and date the "Signature" page, confirming your consent to the project's distribution.
    6.  Optionally, enter the "Date" and "Time" of the project's events or activities.
    7.  If applicable, provide the "Location" where the project took place or will take place.
    8.  Enter your "Phone Number" and "Email" for contact purposes.
    9.  Add any "Notes" about the project or your involvement in it.
    10. Finally, enter the "Distribution Details" to specify how you want the project to be distributed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Information**
    **Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents. This will help us identify you as an individual involved in the documentary project.
*   **Project Information**
    **project_details** (`project_details`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the documentary project, including its title, location, and duration. This will help us understand the context and scope of the project.
*   **Distribution Rights**
    **Distribution Rights** (`distribution_rights`, text, required/optional): Explain the rights you are granting or requesting for the documentary project. This could include permission to use your likeness, images, or voice in the project, as well as any other specific rights you are granting or requesting.
*   **Signature**
    **Signature** (`signature`, text, required/true): Sign and date the form to confirm your consent to the project's distribution.
*   **Date**
    **Date** (`date`, date, required/false): Enter the date of the project's events or activities. This will help us track the timeline of the project.
*   **Time**
    **Time** (`time`, time, required/false): Enter the time of the project's events or activities. This will help us schedule and coordinate the project's timeline.
*   **Location**
    **Location** (`location`, text, required/false): Provide the location where the project took place or will take place. This will help us understand the project's context and setting.
*   **Phone Number**
    **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes. This will help us reach you with any questions or updates about the project.
*   **Email**
    **Email** (`email`, email, required/false): Enter your email address for contact purposes. This will help us reach you with any questions or updates about the project.
*   **Notes**
    **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/false): Add any additional comments or information about the project or your involvement in it. This will help us better understand your perspective and contribution to the project.
*   **Distribution Details**
    **Distribution Details** (`distribution_details`, text, required/false): Enter the details of how you want the project to be distributed, such as through social media, exhibitions, or other channels. This will help us understand your preferences and goals for the project's dissemination.
