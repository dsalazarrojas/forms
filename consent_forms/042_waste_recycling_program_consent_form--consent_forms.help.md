# waste_recycling_program_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The waste recycling program consent form is used to collect participant information and consent for the waste recycling program. It's a crucial document that ensures participants are properly enrolled in the program and aware of their obligations and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the participant's information: Enter the participant's name, organization, and contact details in the respective fields.
2. Enter the consent status: Select the participant's consent status from the available options.
3. Enter any additional comments: If you have any comments or notes about the participant, you can enter them in the comments field.
4. Sign and date the form: Finally, sign and date the form to confirm that you've reviewed and agreed to the participant's information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the participant's name.
* **Organization** (`organization_name`, `text`, required: true): Enter the participant's organization or affiliation.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the participant's address if applicable.
* **Contact Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the participant's contact number.
* **Contact Email** (`email_address`, `email`, required: true): Enter the participant's email address. Please note that this field is a required field.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, `date`, required: true): Enter the date of the participant's consent.
* **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select the participant's consent status from the available options.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes about the participant.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required: true): Sign the form to confirm that you've reviewed and agreed to the participant's information.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, required: true): Enter the date of your signature.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the assigned user.
* **Date Submitted** (`submitted_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the form was submitted.
