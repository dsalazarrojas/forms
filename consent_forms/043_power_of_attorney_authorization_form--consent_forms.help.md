# Power Of Attorney Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to grant legal authorization to an individual to act on your behalf in various matters, typically in situations where you may not be able to make decisions for yourself.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide general information about yourself, including your name, address, and contact details.
2. If applicable, provide information about the attorney or individual you are authorizing to act on your behalf.
3. Specify the type of decision-making authority you are granting, such as financial, health, or other personal matters.
4. Sign and date the form to verify the grant of authority.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information
* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, optional): Please provide your name, address, and contact details to help identify you and facilitate communication.

### Attorney Information
* **Attorney Information** (`attorney_info`, text, optional): If you are authorizing someone else to act on your behalf, please provide their name, address, and contact details.

### Decision Making Information
* **Decision Making Information** (`decision_making_info`, text, optional): Describe the type of decision-making authority you are granting, such as financial, health, or other personal matters.

### Power Of Attorney
* **Power Of Attorney** (`power_of_attorney`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable options from the list:
	+ I am authorizing the above named individual to make decisions on my behalf.
	+ I am authorizing the above named individual to manage my bank accounts.
	+ I am authorizing the above named individual to make health care decisions on my behalf.

### Trusted Person Information
* **Trusted Person Information** (`trusted_person_info`, text, optional): If you feel it is necessary, provide additional information about the trusted person or individual you are authorizing to act on your behalf.

### Signature
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, optional): Please sign and date this form to verify the grant of authority.

### Tips
* Ensure that you have carefully read and understood the purpose and implications of this form before signing.
* Make sure to keep a copy of this form for your own records.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of this form, please consult a legal professional before signing.
