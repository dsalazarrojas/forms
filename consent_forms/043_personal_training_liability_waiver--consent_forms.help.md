# Personal Training Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a liability waiver for personal training purposes, designed to be completed by clients or individuals who are participating in personal training activities. Its main goal is to gather information and obtain consent from clients to conduct personal training sessions, ensuring both parties understand the terms and conditions of the training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Personal Training Liability Waiver**: This is the main section of the form where you will enter the details of the personal training session.
2. **Date**: Enter the date of the training session. Please ensure it is in the correct format (DD/MM/YY or MM/YY/YY).
3. **Client Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your ID or document.
4. **Client Email**: Enter your valid email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Training Liability Waiver** (`personal_training_liability_waiver`, text, optional): A brief description of the training session, including details about the activities, location, and any potential risks involved. Please provide a clear and concise summary of what to expect during the session.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the training session in the format of your choice (DD/MM/YY or MM/YY/YY). This field is used to track the date of the training session.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or document, such as a driver's license or passport.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address that you use to communicate with us. This will help us contact you with any important updates or changes to the training schedule.
