# Telemental Health Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telemental Health Consent Form is used to obtain consent from patients for medical treatment or services. It collects patient information, consent date, and healthcare provider details to ensure that patients understand their rights and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Date Of Birth" field.
3. Enter the patient's social security number, medical record number, and address in their respective fields.
4. Enter the patient's email and phone number in their respective fields.
5. Record the consent date in the "Consent Date" field.
6. Sign the form in the "Signature" field to indicate that the patient has read, understands, and agrees to the terms.
7. The patient's signature should be dated and timed.
8. Enter the healthcare provider's name, title, and fax number in their respective fields.
9. Enter the facility's name, address, email, and phone number in their respective fields.
10. Enter the facility's license number and NPI (National Provider Identifier) in their respective fields.
11. Finally, the healthcare provider should sign the form in the "Healthcare Provider Signature" field.
