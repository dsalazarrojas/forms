# mental_health_counseling_renewal_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mental health counseling renewal form is designed to collect information from clients seeking mental health counseling services for renewal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your client's information in the **Client Information** section, including any relevant details that may be helpful for their counseling sessions.
2. Enter your contact information in the **Contact Information** section, so we can reach you to schedule appointments or ask questions.
3. If applicable, fill in your insurance information for billing and payment purposes in the **Insurance Information** section.
4. Briefly describe the services you are seeking in the **Service Information** section.
5. Complete the **Billing Information** section with your relevant details.
6. Enter your payment information in the **Payment Information** section to ensure smooth processing of your payments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Information (Required)**: A text field for the client to enter their personal details.
- **Contact Information**: A text field for the client to enter their contact information, such as phone number and email.
- **Insurance Information**: A text field for clients with insurance coverage to provide their insurance details.
- **Service Information**: A text field for the client to describe the counseling services they are seeking.
- **Billing Information**: A text field for clients to provide their billing information.
- **Payment Information**: A text field for clients to enter their payment details.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
