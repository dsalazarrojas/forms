# Parent Volunteer Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parent Volunteer Consent Form is a document used to obtain consent from parents/guardians for their child to participate in various activities, events, or programs. This form helps ensure that parents are informed about their child's involvement and provide their consent for their child's participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Fill in the required fields**: The form is not too long, and most fields are not required, so you can fill it out quickly.
2. **Be specific with your contact information**: Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete contact information so that we can reach you if needed.
3. **Check your activity name**: Double-check the activity name to ensure it's correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): This is the parent's name.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required): Please provide your contact information, including your name, address, phone number, and any other relevant details.
* **Volunteer Name** (`volunteer_name`, text, required): This is the name of the volunteer who will be working with your child.
* **Activity Name** (`activity_name`, text, required): This is the name of the activity or program your child will be participating in.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date your child will start participating in the activity.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time your child will start participating in the activity in 24h format. For example, if your child starts at 08:00, you would enter 08:00.

## Tips
* Please make sure to review all the fields carefully to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
* You can save your progress on the form and come back later to complete it or modify it as needed.
