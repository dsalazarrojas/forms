# disclaim_inheritance_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "disclaim_inheritance_form" is a form designed to collect information about a beneficiary's disclaim statement. This form is used to gather details about the beneficiary's name, relationship, address, reason for disclaim, signature date, and signature. The form is intended to be completed by the person providing the disclaim statement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the beneficiary's name in the "Name" field.
2. Describe the relationship between you and the beneficiary in the "Relationship" field.
3. Provide the beneficiary's address in the "Address" field.
4. Enter the reason for the disclaim statement in the "Reason for Disclaim" field.
5. Enter the date of the disclaim signature in the "Disclaim Signature Date" field.
6. Sign the "Disclaim Signature" field with your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Name (`beneficiary_name`, text, required: false)
* Enter the beneficiary's full name, including their first and last name.

### Relationship (`beneficiary_relationship`, text, required: false)
* Describe your relationship with the beneficiary, such as spouse, heir, or beneficiary of the estate.

### Address (`beneficiary_address`, text, required: false)
* Enter the beneficiary's physical address.

### Reason for Disclaim (`reason_for_disclaim`, text, required: false)
* Explain the reason for the disclaim statement.

### Disclaim Signature Date (`disclaim_signature_date`, date, required: false)
* Enter the date of the disclaim signature.

### Disclaim Signature (`disclaim_signature`, note, required: false)
* Sign your name to confirm the disclaim statement.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to avoid any issues with the disclaim process.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all fields are filled out correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact form@gic.mx for assistance.
