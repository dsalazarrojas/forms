# Shelter Volunteer Health Risk Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shelter Volunteer Health Risk Consent Form is used to collect information from volunteers about their general information, emergency contacts, consent status, health risks, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your general information.
2. Indicate your emergency contacts.
3. Select your consent status.
4. Report any health risks you may be aware of.
5. Provide your signature.
6. Enter the date you signed the form.
7. Provide your volunteer information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information**: Enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Emergency Contacts**: Select 'Yes' if you have emergency contacts that need to be notified in case of an incident. Provide their names and contact information.
* **Consent Date**: Enter the date you are giving your consent to volunteer.
* **Health Risks**: Select 'Yes' if you are aware of any health risks associated with volunteering, such as allergies or medical conditions.
* **Signature**: Enter your signature to confirm you have read and understood the information provided.
* **Signed By**: Enter your name as it appears on your ID.
* **Date Signed**: Enter the date you signed the form.
* **Volunteer Information**: Enter any additional information about your volunteer experience or skills.
