# Academic Innovation Archive Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect consent from contributors to archive their work in the Academic Innovation Archive. The form ensures that contributors understand their rights and any restrictions related to the archive, and that their work will be treated and accessed appropriately.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Fill in your name and email address** in the "Contributor name" and "Contact email" fields, respectively.
2. **Provide a brief description of the work** to be archived in the "Work description" field (optional).
3. **Select "Consent to archive work"** by choosing "True" from the dropdown menu. This indicates your permission for your work to be included in the archive.
4. **Specify any access restrictions** you'd like to apply to your archived work in the "Access restrictions" field (optional).
5. **Choose a retention period** for your archived work in the "Retention period" field (optional).
6. **Confirm your signature** by typing your full name in the "Signature confirmation" field.
7. **Enter the date** you're providing consent in the "Date of consent" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contributor name**: Enter your full legal name as it appears on your ID.
* **Contact email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for archive notifications.
* **Title of work to archive**: Enter the title of the innovation output you're submitting for archiving.
* **Work description**: Provide a brief summary of the archived material (optional).
* **Consent to archive work**: Select "True" to indicate your permission for your work to be included in the archive.
* **Access restrictions**: Specify any restrictions on who may view the archived work (e.g., "public", "institutional", "private").
* **Retention period**: Choose a time period for how long the archived work will be retained (e.g., "public", "5 years", "10 years").
* **Signature confirmation**: Type your full name to confirm your consent.
* **Date of consent**: Enter the date you're providing consent.
