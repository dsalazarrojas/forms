# Performance Lighting Design Documentation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Lighting Design Documentation Consent Form is a tool used to capture information from contributors regarding their consent for their work to be used in various contexts such as events, productions, or educational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your name**: In the "Contributor Name" field, please type your name as it appears on your official identification.
2. **Select the relevant event date**: Choose the date of the event, production, or educational activity you are participating in.
3. **Choose the type of production**: Select the type of production you are participating in from the options provided (Event, Production, Education, or Promotion).
4. **Specify your permission to use**: Choose whether you grant permission to use your work in the selected context. Options are:
	* True: You grant permission for your work to be used.
	* False: You do not grant permission for your work to be used.
	* Maybe: You are unsure or do not want to grant permission.
5. **Provide contact information**: Enter your contact email address and/or phone number so that we can reach you if needed.
6. **Add production rights notes**: If necessary, provide additional information about your production rights.
7. **Sign the contributor agreement**: Choose whether you agree or disagree with the contributor agreement.
8. **Specify production details**: Choose whether you grant or do not grant permission for your work to be used in production contexts.
9. **Specify educational purpose**: Choose the educational purpose for which your work will be used.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contributor Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event, production, or educational activity you are participating in.
* **Production Type**: Select the type of production you are participating in from the options provided.
* **Permission to Use**: Choose whether you grant permission to use your work.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your contact email address so that we can reach you if needed.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number if you wish to be contacted.
* **Production Rights Notes**: Provide additional information about your production rights if necessary.
* **Contributor Agreement**: Choose whether you agree or disagree with the contributor agreement.
* **Production Details**: Choose whether you grant or do not grant permission for your work to be used in production contexts.
* **Educational Purpose**: Choose the educational purpose for which your work will be used.
