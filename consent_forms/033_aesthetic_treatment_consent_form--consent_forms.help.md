# Aesthetic Treatment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture the patient's consent to undergo aesthetic treatments, such as Botox, dermal fillers, chemical peels, laser hair removal, and other procedures. By completing this form, the patient confirms their understanding of the risks and potential complications associated with these treatments and agrees to follow post-treatment instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form in its entirety, making sure to answer all required fields.
2. Select the treatment type from the options listed.
3. Confirm that you understand the risks and complications associated with the treatment.
4. Check that you have no guarantees of results.
5. Confirm that you are not pregnant or breastfeeding (if applicable).
6. Decide whether you consent to before/after photos.
7. Sign the form, indicating your consent to the treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name.
* **Date of Birth** (`dob`, `date`, required): Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Treatment Type** (`treatment_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of treatment you are consenting to:
	+ Botox / Dysport
	+ Dermal Fillers
	+ Chemical Peel
	+ Laser Hair Removal
	+ IPL Photofacial
	+ Microneedling
* **Understanding of Risks** (`risks_acknowledgment_header`, `note`, required): Please read carefully the potential risks and complications associated with the treatment, including swelling, bruising, infection, etc.
* **Understanding of No Guarantee of Results** (`no_guarantee`, `select_one`, required): I understand that results vary and no specific outcome is guaranteed.
* **Pregnancy/Nursing Confirmation** (`pregnancy_check`, `select_one`, required): Please confirm that you are not pregnant or breastfeeding (if applicable).
* **Photography Consent** (`photo_consent`, `select_one`, required): Decide whether you consent to before/after photos for your medical record:
	+ Yes, for medical record only
	+ Yes, for medical record and marketing
	+ No photos
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, `select_one`, required): Please sign your name, indicating your consent to the treatment.
* **Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, required): Please enter the date you sign this form.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions carefully, as they are an important part of the consent process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your provider.
* Please note that this form should be completed before undergoing any aesthetic treatment.
