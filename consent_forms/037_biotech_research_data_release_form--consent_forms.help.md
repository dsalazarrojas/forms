Biotech Research Data Release Form - Help Guide
================

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from requestors for biotech research data. The purpose is to collect details on the requestor's identity, data requirements, and intended use of the data.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Enter your details**:
    *   Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Professional Title**.
    *   Provide your **Email Address** and **Phone Number**.
    *   Enter your **Organization Name** and **Organization Type** (e.g., Academic Institution, Research Institute, etc.).
    *   Enter your **Organization Address**.
2.  **Specify your data requirements**:
    *   Choose the **Project Name** and **Project ID** for the research project.
    *   Select all types of data you need (e.g., Clinical Trial Data, Preclinical Study Data, etc.).
    *   Provide a detailed description of the specific data you need.
    *   Specify the **Date Range** of data you need.
3.  **Explain your purpose and intended use**:
    *   Choose the **Purpose of Request** for your research (e.g., Academic Research, Commercial Development, etc.).
    *   Enter a detailed description of your **Purpose Description**.
    *   Specify how you **Intended Use** the data (e.g., for publication, for internal use only, etc.).
4.  **Confirm data security and compliance**:
    *   Confirm that you understand **Data Security Plan** and **Regulatory Compliance**.
    *   Specify how you will store and maintain **Data Retention Plan**.
    *   Confirm that you will maintain **Confidentiality Agreement**.
5.  **Finalize your submission**:
    *   Choose the **Delivery Method** for the data (e.g., secure email, secure file transfer, etc.).
    *   Select the **Data Format Preference** (e.g., CSV, Excel, PDF, etc.).
    *   Specify the **Timeline** for the data delivery.
    *   Provide your electronic **Signature** and confirm that you agree to all terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **First Name**: Enter your first name.
*   **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
*   **Professional Title**: Enter your professional title.
*   **Email Address**: Provide your professional email.
*   **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Organization Name**: Enter your organization's name.
*   **Organization Type**: Choose your organization's type (e.g., Academic Institution, Research Institute, etc.).
*   **Organization Address**: Enter your organization's complete address.
*   **Project Name**: Enter the name of your research project.
*   **Project ID**: Enter the project identification number.
*   **Data Type Requested**: Select all types of data you need (e.g., Clinical Trial Data, Preclinical Study Data, etc.).
*   **Data Description**: Provide a detailed description of the specific data you need.
*   **Date Range**: Specify the time period of data you need.
*   **Purpose of Request**: Choose the purpose of your request (e.g., Academic Research, Commercial Development, etc.).
*   **Purpose Description**: Enter a detailed description of your purpose.
*   **Intended Use**: Specify how you will use the data (e.g., for publication, for internal use only, etc.).
*   **Data Format Preference**: Select your preferred data format (e.g., CSV, Excel, PDF, etc.).
*   **Delivery Method**: Choose the method of data delivery (e.g., secure email, secure file transfer, etc.).
*   **Timeline**: Specify the required timeline for data delivery.
*   **Data Security Plan**: Confirm that you understand the plan for data security and maintenance.
*   **Data Retention Plan**: Specify how you will store and maintain the data.
*   **Confidentiality Agreement**: Confirm that you will maintain confidentiality.
*   **Redistribution Prohibition**: Confirm that you will not redistribute the data.
*   **Acknowledge Requirement**: Confirm that you will acknowledge the data source.
*   **Liability Understanding**: Confirm that you understand the liability limitations.
*   **Authorized Signatory**: Enter your name and title to sign the agreement.
*   **Signature**: Enter your electronic signature.
*   **Date of Signature**: Enter the date of signature.
