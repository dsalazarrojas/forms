# Financial Department Data Usage Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by the financial department to collect sensitive information from individuals for internal purposes. It is essential to understand that this form is for legitimate requests only and should not be submitted as a test or duplicate.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with your personal details as requested.
2. Answer the affiliation questions truthfully.
3. Sign the form to indicate your consent to the usage of your financial department data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your name?** (`name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed on our records.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number for us to reach you if needed.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address for us to contact you.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in DD/MM/YYYY format.
* **Reason for Request** (`reason`, text, optional): Please specify a clear reason for requesting your financial data.
* **Purpose** (`purpose`, text, optional): Specify the purpose of accessing your financial data.
* **Are you affiliated with any financial institution?** (`affiliation_1`, select_one, required): If you are affiliated with a financial institution, select 'True'. If not, select 'False'.
* **Do you have any other financial institution affiliations?** (`affiliation_2`, select_multiple, required): If you have multiple affiliations, list them here. If not, select 'No, I have no affiliations'.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required): Please sign below to indicate your consent to the usage of your financial department data.

## Tips
* Ensure you fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the financial department.
* This form is for legitimate purposes only; if you're unsure, please contact us before submitting.
