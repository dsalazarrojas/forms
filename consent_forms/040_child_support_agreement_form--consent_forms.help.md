# Child Support Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to establish a child support agreement between two parents or guardians, ensuring both parties are in agreement on the terms of financial support, custody, and visitation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Parent A Full Name** (`Parent A Name`, text, required): Enter the full legal name of the first parent.
- **Parent B Full Name** (`Parent B Name`, text, required): Enter the full legal name of the second parent.
- **Names and Birthdates of children** (`children_names_birthdates`, text, required): List all minor children covered by this agreement, including their names and birthdates.
- **Monthly Support Amount** (`monthly_support_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of monthly financial support to be provided.
- **Payment Commencement Date** (`payment_commencement_date`, date, required): Specify the effective date when support payments will begin.
- **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`, select_one, required): Select how often payments will occur (Monthly, Bi-weekly, or Weekly).
- **Shared Expenses and Responsibilities** (`shared_expenses`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional expenses that will be covered, such as health and dental insurance, school tuition and fees, or childcare and daycare.
- **Primary Custody Arrangement** (`custody_arrangement`, select_one, required): Choose the primary custody arrangement (Sole Custody Parent A, Sole Custody Parent B, or Joint Physical Custody).
- **Description of Visitation Schedule** (`visitation_schedule_desc`, text, required): Describe the schedule for visitation, including specifics on weekdays, weekends, and holidays.
- **We both agree to the terms listed above** (`terms_acceptance`, select_one, required): Acknowledge mutual agreement to the terms of the agreement.
- **Date of Agreement Signing** (`agreement_signing_date`, date, required): Enter the date on which both parties sign this agreement.

Note: This form is designed to be filled out by both parents or guardians to establish a mutual understanding of child support responsibilities. It is essential to review and understand each section before completing the form.
