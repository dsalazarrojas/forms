# Investment Performance Disclosure Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain your consent to share your investment data with our firm. It is used to ensure that you are aware of and agree to the sharing of your investment performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information in the "Client Information" section, including your name and any other relevant details.
2. Choose how you would like to be contacted by selecting one or multiple options in the "Contact Preference" section.
3. If you have a preferred language, select it in the "Preferred Language" section.
4. If you have any other languages that you would like to be contacted in, please enter them in the "Other Language" section.
5. In the "Investment Account" section, please enter your investment account number or details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name and any other relevant contact details.
* **Contact Information** (`email`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your email address and any other contact details.
* **Consent to Share Investment Data** (`select_multiple`, `yes/no`, required/optional): Select "Yes" if you agree to share your investment data with our firm.
* **Contact Preference** (`select_one`, `email/phone/both`, required/optional): Select how you would like to be contacted - "Email", "Phone", or "Both".
* **Best Time to Contact** (`time`, `no specific time`, required/optional): Enter the time of day that is best for us to contact you.
* **Preferred Language** (`select_one`, `english/french/spanish`, required/optional): Select your preferred language of communication.
* **Other Language** (`text`, `other language(s)`, required/optional): Enter any other language(s) you would like to be contacted in.
* **Investment Account** (`text`, `investment account number`, required/optional): Enter your investment account number or details.
