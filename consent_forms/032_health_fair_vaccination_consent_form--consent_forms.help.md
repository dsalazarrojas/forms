# AI-Generated Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The AI-Generated Form is a customizable, web-based form designed to collect participant information and consent for research, studies, or other data collection purposes. This form is created using AI algorithms and public metadata from online surveys and can be freely modified, distributed, or even sold, provided credit is given.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form completely**: Provide accurate and honest answers to all questions.
2. **Review and understand each field**: Make sure you understand the purpose of each field before filling it out.
3. **Submit the form**: Click the "Submit" button to send the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect basic participant information, such as their name and contact details.
* **Vaccination Info** (`vaccination_info`, text, required/optional): Provide any relevant vaccination details, if applicable.
* **Consent Details** (`consent_details`, select_multiple, required): Please select "Yes" to indicate your consent or "No" if you decline. If you select "No", please specify the reason in the "Other Reason" field.
* **Other Reason** (`other_reason`, text, required/optional): If you select "No" in the "Consent Details" field, please provide a brief explanation for your reason.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, note, required): Please sign your name as a participant to confirm your consent.
* **Organization Signature** (`organization_signature`, note, required): If you are acting on behalf of an organization, please sign with your organization's name and title.
* **Date** (`date_signed`, date, required/optional): If required, please provide the date you completed the form.
* **Timestamp** (`timestamp`, time, required/optional): If required, please provide the time you completed the form.
