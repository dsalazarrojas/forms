# professional disclosure statement and informed consent - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be a template for a professional disclosure statement and informed consent in a healthcare setting. It aims to provide a clear and transparent understanding of the patient-provider relationship, ensuring that both parties are aware of their roles and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's personal details:
	* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their official identification documents.
	* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YY).
	* **Date of Service**: Enter the date of the consultation or appointment.
2. Fill in the healthcare provider's details:
	* **Healthcare Provider Name**: Enter the healthcare provider's name as it appears on their official identification documents.
	* **Healthcare Provider Title**: Enter the healthcare provider's title or profession.
3. Sign and verify:
	* **Patient Signature**: Sign the form to acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms of the consent.
	* **Healthcare Provider Signature**: Sign the form to confirm that you have read and understood the terms of the consent.
4. Confirm the information:
	* **Patient Name Check**: Verify the patient's name for accuracy.
	* **Healthcare Provider Title Check**: Verify the healthcare provider's title for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their official identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YY).
* **Date of Service** (`date_of_service`, date, required): Enter the date of the consultation or appointment.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text, required): Sign the form to acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms of the consent.
* **Healthcare Provider Name** (`healthcare_provider_name`, text, required): Enter the healthcare provider's name as it appears on their official identification documents.
* **Healthcare Provider Title** (`healthcare_provider_title`, text, required): Enter the healthcare provider's title or profession.
* **Healthcare Provider Signature** (`healthcare_provider_title`, text, required): Sign the form to confirm that you have read and understood the terms of the consent.
* **Patient Name Check** (`patient_name_check`, text, required): Verify the patient's name for accuracy.
* **Healthcare Provider Title Check** (`healthcare_provider_title_check`, text, required): Verify the healthcare provider's title for accuracy.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the current date in the correct format (DD/MM/YY).

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* Review the form carefully before signing to ensure you understand the terms of the consent.
* Keep a copy of the signed form for your records.
