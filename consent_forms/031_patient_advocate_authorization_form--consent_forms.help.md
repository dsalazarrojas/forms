# Patient Advocate Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Advocate Authorization Form is designed to collect information about a patient's advocate, which is an individual or organization that will act on behalf of the patient in medical or personal matters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the patient's name and relationship to the patient.
2.  Provide your contact information so that the patient can reach out to you.
3.  If you have a relationship with the organizer, specify it.
4.  Enter your contact information for the advocate.
5.  If necessary, provide the advocate's phone number and emergency contact information.
6.  Enter your email address to enable communication.
7.  Optionally, you can provide the advocate's address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`Patient Name`, text, required): This is the patient's name, who is the subject of the authorization.
* **Relationship To Patient** (`Relationship To Patient`, text, required): Specify the relationship you have with the patient (e.g., family member, caregiver, friend).
* **Name Of Advocate** (`Name Of Advocate`, text, required): Enter your name or the name of the organization representing you as the patient's advocate.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, text, required): Provide your contact information (e.g., phone number, email) so that the patient can reach out to you.
* **Advocate Phone** (`Advocate Phone`, text, optional): If you want to provide a phone number for the advocate, you can do so.
* **Relationship To Organizer** (`Relationship To Organizer`, text, required): If you have a relationship with the person or organization that is responsible for the patient's care, specify it.
* **Contact Info 2** (`Contact Info 2`, text, optional): Provide an additional contact method for the advocate, such as an email or a phone number.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address so that the patient or their representative can contact you.
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, text, optional): If you want to provide an emergency contact person or phone number, you can do so.
* **Advocate Address** (`Advocate Address`, text, optional): If you want to provide the advocate's address, you can do so.

## Tips

*   Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to maintain the patient's trust and confidentiality.
*   If you are completing the form on behalf of the patient, ensure you have the patient's permission to do so.
*   Please review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed.
