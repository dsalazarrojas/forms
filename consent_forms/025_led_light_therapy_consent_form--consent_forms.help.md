# LED Light Therapy Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is a consent form for LED light therapy, a medical treatment that uses light to stimulate cellular processes and promote healing. The form is designed to gather information necessary for safe and effective treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction carefully and ensure you understand the purpose of the form.
2. Answer each question to the best of your knowledge and provide as much detail as possible.
3. Sign and date the consent section to indicate your agreement to receive LED light therapy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Medical History**: Please provide your medical history, including any pre-existing conditions or allergies.
- **Allergies**: List any allergies you have, such as medications or substances that cause adverse reactions.
- **Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking.
- **Client Information**: Please select your gender (Male/Female).
- **Medical History Details**: Provide any additional information about your medical history.
- **Treatment**: Select the type of light treatment you wish to receive (LED Light Therapy, Other).
- **Signature**: This is a note field for your signature.
- **Date of Birth**: Please enter your date of birth.
- **Date of Last Treatment**: If you have had previous treatment, please enter the date.
- **Consent**: Please indicate your agreement to receive LED light therapy (True/False).
- **Signature Date**: This is a note field for the date of your signature.
- **Note**: This is a free-text field for any additional comments or concerns.
- **Contact Info**: Please provide your contact information for follow-up purposes.
- **Email**: Please enter your email address.
- **Phone**: Please enter your phone number.
- **Address**: Please enter your address.
- **Medical Practitioner**: Please enter the name of your medical practitioner.
- **Medical Facility**: Please enter the name of the medical facility.
- **Emergency Contact**: Please provide the name of your emergency contact.
- **Relationship**: Please specify your relationship to the emergency contact.
- **Phone**: Please enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
- **Signature Signature**: This is a note field for your signature.
- **Consent Signature**: This is a note field for your consent signature.
- **Note**: This is a free-text field for any additional comments or concerns.

## Tips
- Please answer each question carefully and accurately.
- If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
- Ensure you sign and date the consent section to confirm your agreement to receive LED light therapy.
