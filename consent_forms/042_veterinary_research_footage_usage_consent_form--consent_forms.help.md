# Veterinary Research Footage Usage Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Research Footage Usage Consent Form is a vital document that helps researchers understand how pet owners feel about the use of their pet's footage in research projects. It also helps pet owners understand how their pet's footage will be used and stored.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "Owner Name" field.
2. Enter your title in the "Owner Title" field.
3. Provide a brief description of the research project in the "Research Description" field.
4. Select the type of animal being recorded (e.g., dog, cat, other).
5. Enter the start and end dates of the recording session.
6. Enter the location where the recording took place.
7. Choose the method of recording (e.g., camera, sensor, etc.).
8. Describe how you want your pet's footage to be used.
9. Select whether you consent to share your pet's footage publicly or with research team members.
10. Provide your signature and the date of consent.
11. Enter your contact information (name, email, phone number) in case of any questions or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Research Footage** (1): Enter a brief title for the research project.
* **Owner Name** (2): Enter your name.
* **Owner Title** (3): Enter your title.
* **Research Institution** (4): Enter the name of the institution where the research is taking place (optional).
* **Research Description** (5): Enter a brief description of the research project.
* **Animal Type** (6): Select the type of animal being recorded (e.g., dog, cat, other).
* **Recording Start Date** (7): Enter the start date of the recording session.
* **Recording End Date** (8): Enter the end date of the recording session.
* **Recording Location** (9): Enter the location where the recording took place (optional).
* **Recording Method** (10): Choose the method of recording (e.g., camera, sensor, etc.).
* **Usage Description** (11): Describe how you want your pet's footage to be used.
* **Usage Purpose** (12): Enter the purpose of using your pet's footage.
* **Data Sharing** (13): Select whether you consent to share your pet's footage publicly or with research team members.
* **Data Sharing Reason** (14): If you chose to share your footage, provide a brief reason for your choice (optional).
* **Owner Signature** (15): Provide your signature.
* **Date of Consent** (16): Enter the date of consent.
* **Researcher Signature** (17): The researcher conducting the study should sign below.
* **Research Notes** (18): Enter any notes about the research project (optional).
* **Consent to Share Anonymously** (19): Select whether you consent to share your pet's data anonymously.
* **Consent to Share Anonymously Reason** (20): If you chose to share anonymously, provide a brief reason for your choice (optional).
* **Consent to Share Anonymously with** (21): If you chose to share anonymously, select with whom you want to share (e.g., public, research team).
* **Research Contact Information** (22): Enter your contact information (name, email, phone number) in case of any questions or concerns.
* **Research Contact Name** (23): Enter the name of the person conducting the research.
* **Research Contact Email** (24): Enter the email of the person conducting the research.
* **Research Contact Phone** (25): Enter the phone number of the person conducting the research.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and complete information.
- Review the form carefully before signing.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the research team.
