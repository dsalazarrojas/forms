# Marine Biology Workshop Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marine Biology Workshop Admission Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in participating in a Marine Biology Workshop. This form is used to efficiently collect and process applications for the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Application Details page.
2. Enter your Name, Email, and Phone Number.
3. Select the Course(s) you are interested in (e.g., Biology, Marine Science, Conservation Biology).
4. Provide any relevant Biological Details.
5. Choose your preferred Workshop Location.
6. Select the Submission Date for your application.
7. Read and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Application Details
* **Application Details** (`workshop_application`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your application and why you are interested in the Marine Biology Workshop.

### Name
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address that you check regularly.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

### Course Interested
* **Course Interested** (`course`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or multiple courses you are interested in (Biology, Marine Science, Conservation Biology).

### Biological Details
* **Biological Details** (`bio_details`, text, required): Provide any relevant biological details that may be useful for the workshop organizers.

### Workshop Location
* **Workshop Location** (`workshop_location`, select_one, required): Choose the workshop location that suits you best (Location 1, Location 2, Location 3).

### Submission Date
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Select the date you would like to submit your application.

### Agreement
* **Agreement** (, note, required): Read and agree to the terms and conditions.

### Workshop Agreement
* **Workshop Agreement** (, note, required): Read and agree to the workshop terms and conditions.

## Tips
* Please make sure to submit the form only once.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the workshop organizers.
