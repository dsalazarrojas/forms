# Academic Honesty Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for academic institutions to collect information about student behavior, including incidents of academic dishonesty, and have students acknowledge and sign off on the institution's academic honesty policy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and provide the required information as prompted.
2. Enter your student name and student identification number in the relevant fields.
3. Describe the incident or situation in the space provided.
4. Provide any corrective actions you plan to take to rectify the issue (if applicable).
5. If applicable, indicate if someone else witnessed or reported the incident and their name.
6. Confirm your awareness of the institution's academic honesty policy.
7. Sign your full name as requested.
8. Date the signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Honesty Statement --** (`section_statement`, `note`, not required): This is a placeholder section for any additional information or statement regarding the incident.
* **Student name** (`student_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as a student.
* **Student identification number** (`student_id`, `text`, required): Enter your university or registration ID number.
* **Course code or title** (`course_code`, `text`, required): Specify the course affected by this incident.
* **Incident description** (`incident_description`, `text`, required): Describe the incident or situation in detail.
* **Corrective actions** (`corrective_actions`, `text`, not required): If applicable, provide steps you plan to take to rectify the issue.
* **Witness or reporter name** (`witness_or_reporter`, `text`, not required): If someone else witnessed or reported the incident, enter their name.
* **Acknowledgement of policy** (`acknowledgement`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your awareness of the institution's academic honesty policy by selecting either "I acknowledge and accept the policy" or "I do not acknowledge".
* **Signature name** (`signature_name`, `text`, required): Type your full name to sign the form.
* **Signature date** (`signature_date`, `date`, required): Date the signature to indicate the date of acknowledgement.
