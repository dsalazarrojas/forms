# Eyebrow Lamination Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Eyebrow Lamination Consent Form is used to collect necessary information from clients and their consent to proceed with the service. This form helps to ensure that you have the required details and consent to provide the service.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please complete each field with accurate and relevant information.
- Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details (text)** (`client_details`, not applicable, required: false): Enter a brief description of the client's visit.
* **Client Info (text)** (`client_details_label`, not applicable, required: false): Enter a label or note related to the client's information (e.g., "Client Name: John Doe").
* **Name (text)** (`name`, not applicable, required: false): Enter the client's name.
* **Contact Number (text)** (`contact_number`, not applicable, required: false): Enter the client's contact number.
* **Email (email)** (`email`, not applicable, required: false): Enter the client's email address.
* **Date Of Visit (date)** (`date_of_visit`, not applicable, required: false): Enter the date of the visit.
* **Time Of Visit (time)** (`time_of_visit`, not applicable, required: false): Enter the time of the visit.
* **Consent (text)** (`consent`, not applicable, required: false): This field is not applicable as it is a separate section for consent.
* **Consent (select_one)** (`consent_label`, yes, required: false): Select one of the options:
	+ I have read and understand all terms and conditions.
	+ I have NOT read and understand all terms and conditions.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
- If you are unsure about any field, please consult with your supervisor or the relevant department.
- Review the terms and conditions carefully before making a selection.
