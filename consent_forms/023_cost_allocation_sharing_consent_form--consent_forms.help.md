# Cost Allocation Sharing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Cost Allocation Sharing Consent Form" is a document used to obtain consent for sharing cost allocation data within the organization. This form is a crucial step in the process of ensuring that all stakeholders are aware and in agreement to share sensitive information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Review the "Agreement Details" section and make sure you understand the scope of data sharing.
2. Select "Agree" from the "Consent" dropdown menu if you are willing to share your cost allocation data.
3. Fill out the "Submission Date" and "Form Version" fields accurately.
4. Enter your "Submitter Name", "Submitter Title", and "Submitter Email" details.
5. You can add additional comments if needed in the "Submission Notes" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Agreement Details (cost_allocation_sharing_consent_form_1)**: A brief description of the data sharing agreement.
* **Consent (cost_allocation_sharing_consent_form_2)**: Choose "Agree" to indicate your consent for data sharing.
* **Consent Form Details (cost_allocation_sharing_consent_form_3)**: Optional notes about your consent.
* **Consent Date (cost_allocation_sharing_consent_form_4)**: The date you are providing consent for data sharing. 
* **Notes (cost_allocation_sharing_consent_form_5)**: Additional notes about your consent.
* **Form Version (cost_allocation_sharing_consent_form_6)**: The version of the form you are submitting.
* **Submission Date (cost_allocation_sharing_consent_form_7)**: The date you are submitting this form.
* **Form Version Number (cost_allocation_sharing_consent_form_8)**: The version number of the form you are submitting.
* **Form ID (cost_allocation_sharing_consent_form_9)**: A unique identifier for the form.
* **Submitter Name (cost_allocation_sharing_consent_form_10)**: Your name and title.
* **Submitter Email (cost_allocation_sharing_consent_form_12)**: Your email address.
* **Submitter Phone (cost_allocation_sharing_consent_form_13)**: Your phone number.
* **Submission Notes (cost_allocation_sharing_consent_form_14)**: Additional comments about your submission.
* **Approval Notes (cost_allocation_sharing_consent_form_15)**: Comments for approval purposes.
* **Submitter Comments 1-10 (cost_allocation_sharing_consent_form_16-25)**: Additional comments from the submitter.

Note: The fields marked as "required" are mandatory fields and must be filled out accurately. The other fields are optional and can be used for additional information.
