# Case Report Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Case Report Consent Form is designed to obtain informed consent from participants or patients for a research case report. This form guides you through the various aspects of the consent process, including your rights, risks, and benefits, as well as your agreement to the storage and use of your data.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Case Report Consent Form**: This is the title of the form and a brief introduction.
2. **Consent Date**: Enter the date you're providing consent for the case report.
3. **Study Information**: Enter a brief description of the research study or project.
4. **Study Title**: Enter the title of the study or research project.
5. **Principal Investigator Name**: Enter the name of the lead researcher or investigator.
6. **Institution**: Enter the name of the institution or organization conducting the research.
7. **IRB Approval Number**: Enter the IRB (Institutional Review Board) approval number for the study.
8. **Participant Information**: Enter any additional information about the participant, such as medical history or relevant medical conditions.
9. **Participant Full Name**: Enter your full legal name.
10. **Participant ID or Medical Record Number**: Enter your participant ID or medical record number.
11. **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth.
12. **Gender**: Select your gender identity.
13. **Purpose of Consent**: Enter the purpose for which you're providing consent (e.g., case report publication, medical research, educational purposes).
14. **Scope of Information**: Select the types of information you're consenting to be used (e.g., medical history, treatment records, laboratory results).
15. **Identifiable Information**: Indicate if you have any identifiable information that may be used.
16. **How to Protect Your Identity**: Select how your identity will be protected (e.g., fully de-identified, coded, limited identifiers).
17. **Identifiers Used**: Select which identifiers may be used (e.g., age, gender, medical record number).
18. **Risks and Benefits**: Understand the potential risks and benefits of participating in the research.
19. **I Understand the Potential Risks**: Acknowledge that you understand the potential risks.
20. **I Understand the Potential Benefits**: Acknowledge that you understand the potential benefits.
21. **Voluntary Participation**: Confirm that you understand that your participation is voluntary.
22. **I Understand My Participation Is Voluntary**: Confirm that you understand that your participation is voluntary.
23. **I Understand I Can Withdraw at Any Time**: Confirm that you understand you can withdraw from the study at any time.
24. **Data Storage and Access**: Understand how your data will be stored and accessed.
25. **How Long Will My Data Be Kept**: Select how long your data will be kept (e.g., until study completion, 5 years after study).
26. **Who Will Have Access to My Data**: Select who will have access to your data (e.g., research team, IRB members, regulatory agencies).
27. **Future Use of Data**: Understand that your data may be used for future research.
28. **Future Research**: Select if your data may be used for future research.
29. **Contact Information**: Enter any contact information you'd like to have for questions or concerns.
30. **I Have Contact Information for Questions**: Confirm if you have contact information.
31. **Have All Your Questions Been Answered**: Confirm if all your questions have been answered.
32. **Consent Decision**: Finalize your consent decision.
33. **Do You Consent to Participate**: Confirm that you consent to participate in the study.
34. **Participant Signature Date**: Enter the date you sign this consent form.
35. **Witness Name**: Enter the name of the witness or person witnessing your signature.
36. **Witness Signature Date**: Enter the date the witness signs this consent form.
37. **Researcher Signature Date**: Enter the date the researcher obtains your signature.
38. **Guardian Consent**: If you're a minor, indicate if a guardian's consent is required.
39. **Is Guardian Consent Required**: Confirm if guardian consent is required.
40. **Guardian Name**: Enter the name of the guardian (if applicable).
41. **Guardian Relationship**: Enter the relationship between the guardian and the participant.
42. **Guardian Signature Date**: Enter the date the guardian signs this consent form.

## Tips

* Read each section carefully to understand your rights, risks, and benefits.
* Ask questions and seek clarification if you're unsure about any information.
* Ensure you understand and agree to the terms before signing.
* Keep a copy of this consent form for your records.
* If you're a minor, ensure a guardian signs this form.
* If you're unsure about any aspect of the form, consult with the researcher or study administrator.
* Review and understand all the sections before signing.
* Sign the form carefully and date it.
