# Employee Bio Release Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect employee bio release consent from employees. It's a simple form used to obtain agreement from employees to share their biographical information with others. Please fill it out to provide necessary consent before sharing your bio.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
	* Enter your employee bio details in the "employee_bios" field.
	* Sign your name in the "signature" field.
	* If applicable, sign on behalf of your organization by filling out "organization_signature" field.
	* Finally, enter the date you signed this form in the "date_signed" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_bios (<type:text>)**: Enter your employee bio information such as name, job title, department, location, etc.
* **signature (<type:text>)**: Sign your name to confirm that you consent to share your bio information.
* **date_signed (<type:date>)**: Enter the date when you signed this form, which is the date from which your consent starts.
* **organization_signature (<type:text>)**: If you are signing on behalf of your organization, enter your name and title within your organization. 

## Tips

* Please ensure to fill out this form carefully as it represents your consent to share your employee bio.
* If you are filling this form out on behalf of your organization, please make sure you have the authority to sign on behalf of the organization.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or HR department for guidance.
