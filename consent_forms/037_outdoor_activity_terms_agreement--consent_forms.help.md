# Outdoor Activity Terms Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential data for outdoor activities. Please review each field carefully to ensure you provide accurate and relevant information for the activity you are participating in.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully and fill in the required information accurately.
2. For participant consent, select "Option 1" or "Other" to express your consent.
3. For emergency contact, provide the name and contact information of the person to be contacted in case of an emergency.
4. For terms and conditions, review and agree to the conditions outlined.
5. For emergency contact relationship, select the relationship between the emergency contact and the participant.
6. For participant age, enter the participant's age accurately.
7. For participant date of birth, enter the participant's date of birth in the correct format (yyyy-mm-dd).
8. For participant gender, select the participant's gender from the options provided.
9. For guardian name, enter the name of the participant's guardian or parent.
10. For guardian contact information, provide the contact details of the guardian or parent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`, `select_one`, required/optional)
	+ Select "Option 1" if you have consented to participate in the activity.
	+ Select "Other" if you wish to express other types of consent (e.g., "Parental consent").
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required/optional)
	+ Enter the name of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
	+ Provide their contact information (e.g., phone number, email).
* **Parent Participant Consent** (`parent_consent`, `select_multiple`, required)
	+ Select all that apply regarding the participant's consent.
* **Terms And Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, `note`, required/optional)
	+ Review and agree to the terms and conditions outlined.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `select_one`, required/optional)
	+ Select the relationship between the emergency contact and the participant (e.g., parent, guardian).
* **Participant Age** (`participant_age`, `number`, required/optional)
	+ Enter the participant's age accurately.
* **Participant Date Of Birth** (`participant_date_of_birth`, `date`, required/optional)
	+ Enter the participant's date of birth in the correct format (yyyy-mm-dd).
* **Participant Gender** (`participant_gender`, `select_one`, required/optional)
	+ Select the participant's gender from the options provided.
* **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, `text`, required/optional)
	+ Enter the name of the participant's guardian or parent.
* **Guardian Contact Information** (`guardian_contact_information`, `text`, required/optional)
	+ Provide the contact details of the guardian or parent.
