# <string> - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users for the purpose of creating a testimonial. It is used to gather details such as their name, email address, a testimonial text about their experience, and their consent to use their information for marketing, social media, and press releases. The form also allows users to specify if they have any other areas they would like to consent to, and if so, to provide a brief description of those areas.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Enter your User Name**: Please fill in your full name as it appears on your official identification documents.
2. **Enter your Email Address**: Provide your email address to enable us to contact you in case we need more information or have any questions about your testimonial.
3. **Choose Testimonial Prophet Name**: Select the name of a person who you would like to credit as the testimonial provider.
4. **Write your Testimonial Text**: Please provide a brief description of your experience with us in the text box provided.
5. **Select areas of Consent**: Indicate which areas you agree to by selecting them from the list provided.
6. **Provide additional Consent Details (if applicable)**: If you have any other areas that you would like to consent to, please provide a brief description in the text box below.
7. **Choose an Agreement Date**: Select a date for your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, Text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, Email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Testimonial Prophet Name** (`test_prophet_name`, Select One, required): This field is not required if you are providing a testimonial for a specific person. 
* **Testimonial Text** (`testimonial_text`, Note, required): Please provide a brief description of your experience with us.
* **Consent** (`consent`, Select Multiple, required): Please indicate which areas you agree to by selecting them.
* **Other (if applicable)** (`other_consent_area`, Text, optional): If you have any other areas that you would like to consent to, please provide a brief description.
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, Date, required): Please select a date for your agreement.
