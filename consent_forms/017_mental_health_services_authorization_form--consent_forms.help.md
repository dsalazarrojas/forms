# Mental Health Services Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from patients about their mental health services needs. It is meant to be filled out by patients themselves, and the information collected will be used to provide them with relevant mental health services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Review the questions carefully and answer them as accurately as possible.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact your healthcare provider or mental health professional.
*   Ensure that you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Patient Information** (`Patient Information`, text, required/optional): This section is where you'll provide basic information about yourself, such as name, date of birth, and contact details.
*   **Contact Preferences** (`Contact Preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted by your healthcare provider or mental health professional.
    *   Contact me by phone
    *   Contact me by email
    *   Contact me in person
*   **Mental Health Services** (`Mental Health Services`, select_one, optional): Select the type of mental health services you are seeking.
    *   Individual counseling
    *   Group counseling
    *   Medication management
*   **Medical History** (`Medical History`, text, optional): Please provide any relevant medical history that may be useful for your mental health services.
*   **Medical Conditions** (`Medical Conditions`, select_multiple, optional): If you have any medical conditions, such as diabetes, hypertension, or high cholesterol, please list them here.
    *   Diabetes
    *   Hypertension
    *   High Cholesterol
*   **Medications** (`Medications`, text, optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
*   **Medication Side Effects** (`Medication Side Effects`, select_multiple, optional): If you have experienced any side effects from your medications, please list them here.
    *   Headaches
    *   Dizziness
    *   Nausea
*   **Authorization** (`Authorization`, text, optional): This section is for you to authorize the mental health services provider to access your medical information and provide treatment. 

## Tips
*   Please answer all questions accurately and truthfully.
*   If you're unsure about any questions, ask your healthcare provider or mental health professional for guidance.
*   This form should only be filled out by the patient themselves. 

Note: This guide is intended to be a reference for patients to understand the purpose and usage of this form. Please ensure that it is reviewed and updated accordingly to reflect any changes to the form or its usage.
