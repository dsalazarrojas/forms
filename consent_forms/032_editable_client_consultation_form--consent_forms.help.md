# Editable Client Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Editable Client Consultation Form is a tool used to collect relevant information from clients during a consultation. The form helps the healthcare provider to understand the client's medical history, current medications, allergies, and other important details that may impact their treatment. The information gathered through this form will aid the provider in making informed decisions and providing tailored care to the client.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:
1. Fill in the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Enter the client's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
3. Fill in the client's contact phone number and email address in the "Contact Phone" and "Contact Email" fields, respectively.
4. If applicable, describe the client's medical conditions in the "Medical Conditions" field.
5. Indicate whether the client takes any medication(s) in the "Medication List" field.
6. Specify the dosage for each medication taken in the "Medication Dosage" field.
7. Check "Yes" if the client uses medical supplements, and "No" if not, in the "Medical Supplement" field.
8. List the client's allergies in the "Allergies" field.
9. Check "Yes" for each medical condition that the client has in the "Medical History" field.
10. Indicate whether the client has undergone any medical procedures in the "Medical Procedure" field.
11. Check "Yes" or "No" for each medical specialty that the client has been diagnosed with in the "Medical Specialty" field.
12. Fill in any additional medical notes in the "Medical Notes" field.
13. Check "Yes" or "No" for each address component that the client has, in the "Client Address" field.
14. Check "Yes" or "No" for each medical record that the client has, in the "Medical Record" field.
15. Fill in the name and contact information for the client's emergency contact, in the "Emergency Contact" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (Text, Required): Enter the client's name.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Required): Enter the client's date of birth.
* **Contact Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the client's contact phone number.
* **Contact Email** (Email, Optional): Enter the client's email address.
* **Medical Conditions** (Note, Optional): If applicable, describe the client's medical conditions.
* **Medication List** (Select One, Optional): Check "Yes" if the client takes any medication.
* **Medication Dosage** (Select Multiple, Optional): Specify the dosage for each medication taken.
* **Medical Supplement** (Select One, Optional): Check "Yes" if the client uses medical supplements.
* **Allergies** (Select Multiple, Optional): List the client's allergies.
* **Medical History** (Select Multiple, Optional): Check "Yes" for each medical condition that the client has.
* **Medical Procedure** (Select One, Optional): Check "Yes" if the client has undergone any medical procedures.
* **Medical Specialty** (Select Multiple, Optional): Check "Yes" for each medical specialty that the client has been diagnosed with.
* **Medical Notes** (Note, Optional): Fill in any additional medical notes.
* **Client Address** (Select Multiple, Optional): Check "Yes" or "No" for each address component that the client has.
* **Medical Record** (Select Multiple, Optional): Check "Yes" or "No" for each medical record that the client has.
* **Emergency Contact** (Optional): Fill in the name and contact information for the client's emergency contact.
