# digitalHealthPolicyResearchInterviewConsent - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather participant information and consent for a research interview.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read and understand the purpose and instructions before proceeding.
2. Fill out the Participant Information field with your basic information.
3. Select the research team conducting the interview from the options provided.
4. Choose your agreement to participate in the research interview.
5. If you are comfortable, record your response to the Recording Preference field.
6. Enter your text-based consent for participating in the research in the Participant Consent field.
7. Select whether you agree or disagree with the Participant Agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`participantinformation`, text, required): This field asks for basic information about the participant.
* **Research Team** (`researchteam`, select_one, required): Select the research team conducting the interview.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_multiple, required): Agree or disagree to participate in the research interview.
* **Consent Date** (`consentdate`, date, optional): Record the date of your consent, if desired.
* **Recording Preference** (`recordingpreference`, select_one, required): Choose whether you want to be recorded during the interview.
* **Participant Consent** (`participantconsent`, text, required): Enter your text-based consent for participating in the research.
* **Participant Agreement** (`participantagreement`, select_multiple, required): Select whether you agree or disagree to participate in the research.
