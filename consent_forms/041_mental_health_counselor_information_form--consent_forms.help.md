# Mental Health Counselor Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect accurate and up-to-date information from mental health counselors. This information will be used to create or update their profiles in the system, which is crucial for administrative and operational reasons.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out each field carefully and accurately.
2.  Ensure that you do not duplicate any information for the same mental health counselor.
3.  If you are unsure about any field, it is recommended that you ask the mental health counselor to clarify any information that you are not sure about.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Form ID (1)**: This is a unique identifier for the mental health counselor information form. It is used to link the counselor's profile to their information. 
    *   **Type: Number**: This is a required field.
*   **Assigned Tool (2)**: Please enter the tool that the mental health counselor is assigned to. This could be a specific tool used in their practice.
    *   **Type: Text**: This is a required field.
*   **Client Name (3)**: Enter the name of the mental health counselor's client. Please use the exact name as it appears on their documents.
    *   **Type: Text**: This is a required field.
*   **Address (4)**: Enter the address of the mental health counselor's office or practice. This will help us in sending information and notifications to the correct location.
    *   **Type: Text**: This field is optional.
*   **Email (5)**: Enter the email address of the mental health counselor. This is used for communication and sending information.
    *   **Type: Email**: This field is optional.
*   **Phone (6)**: Enter the phone number of the mental health counselor. This is used for communication and contact.
    *   **Type: Text**: This field is optional.
*   **Medical History (7)**: Enter the mental health counselor's medical history, if any.
    *   **Type: Text**: This field is optional.
*   **Counselor Notes (8)**: Enter any notes about the mental health counselor. This could be their strengths, areas for improvement, or any other relevant information.
    *   **Type: Note**: This field is optional.
*   **Intake Date (9)**: Enter the date the mental health counselor was seen or admitted. This is used to track their intake information.
    *   **Type: Date**: This is a required field.
*   **Mental Health Counselor Name (10)**: Enter the name of the mental health counselor. Please use their full name as it appears on their documents.
    *   **Type: Text**: This is a required field.
*   **Mental Health Counselor Role (11)**: Select the role of the mental health counselor. Examples include 'therapist', 'counselor', 'psychologist', etc.
    *   **Type: Text**: This is a required field.
*   **Mental Health Counselor Specialty (12)**: Enter the mental health counselor's specialization. For example, if they specialize in hypnosis, please enter 'hypnosis'.
    *   **Type: Text**: This field is optional.
*   **Mental Health Counselor Education (13)**: Enter the level of education the mental health counselor has. For example, 'Bachelor's', 'Master's', 'Ph.D.' etc.
    *   **Type: Text**: This is a required field.
*   **Mental Health Counselor License (14)**: Enter the mental health counselor's license number. Please ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
    *   **Type: Text**: This is a required field.
*   **Mental Health Counselor Certification (15)**: Enter the mental health counselor's certification. For example, 'Certified Clinical Hypnotherapist' etc.
    *   **Type: Text**: This field is optional.
*   **Mental Health Counselor Languages Spoken (16)**: List the languages spoken by the mental health counselor. Please separate each language with a comma.
    *   **Type: Text**: This field is optional.
*   **Mental Health Counselor Experience (17)**: Enter the experience of the mental health counselor. For example, 'number of years practicing' etc.
    *   **Type: Text**: This field is optional.
*   **Mental Health Counselor Education Level (18)**: Select the level of education of the mental health counselor. For example, 'Bachelor's', 'Master's', 'Ph.D.' etc.
    *   **Type: Text**: This is a required field.
*   **Mental Health Counselor Licensed In (19)**: Enter the location where the mental health counselor is licensed. For example, 'State A', 'State B', 'Country X' etc.
    *   **Type: Text**: This is a required field.
*   **Mental Health Counselor License Number (20)**: Enter the number of the mental health counselor's license.
    *   **Type: Text**: This is a required field.
*   **Mental Health Counselor Licenses (21)**: Select the licenses held by the mental health counselor. Please see the dropdown list for options.
    *   **Type: Select One**: This is a required field.
*   **Mental Health Counselor Insurance (22)**: Check if the mental health counselor has insurance coverage. Please see the dropdown list.
    *   **Type: Select Multiple**: This is a required field.
*   **Mental Health Counselor Therapist License (23)**: Check if the mental health counselor has a therapist license. Please see the dropdown list.
    *   **Type: Select Multiple**: This is a required field.
*   **Mental Health Counselor Board Certification (24)**: Enter any board certification the mental health counselor may have.
    *   **Type: Text**: This field is optional.
