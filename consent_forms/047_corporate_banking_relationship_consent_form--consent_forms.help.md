# Corporate Banking Relationship Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from a client for a corporate banking relationship. It captures information about the client's account, company, and bank representative, and serves as a record of the client's consent to the terms and conditions of the relationship.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by reviewing the form header and ensuring that you have the necessary permissions to fill out this form.
2. Fill out the required fields, which include:
	* Account Number (account_number)
	* Company Name (company_name)
	* Client Type (client_type)
	* Consent Given (consent_given)
3. Fill out any additional fields as applicable:
	* Client ID (client_id)
	* Date of Birth (date_of_birth)
	* Date Established (date_established)
	* Bank Branch (bank_branch)
	* Bank Officer (bank_officer)
	* Bank Phone (bank_phone)
	* Email (email)
	* Address (address)
	* Notes (notes)
	* Bank Email (bank_email)
4. Choose the correct client type from the options provided:
	* Individual
	* Business
	* Government Institution
5. Choose whether consent is given or not:
	* Given
	* Not Given
6. Fill out the date and time of consent:
	* Consent Date (consent_date)
	* Consent Time (consent_time)
7. Add any relevant signature(s) from the client:
	* Client Signature (client_signature)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Header (form_header)**: This is the title of the form, indicating that it is a corporate banking relationship consent form.
* **Account Number (account_number)**: This is the account number associated with the client's account.
* **Company Name (company_name)**: This is the name of the client's company.
* **Client ID (client_id)**: This is the ID number associated with the client.
* **Client Type (client_type)**: This is the type of client (Individual, Business, or Government Institution).
* **Date of Birth (date_of_birth)**: This is the date of birth of the client (only applicable for Individual clients).
* **Date Established (date_established)**: This is the date the client's company was established.
* **Bank Branch (bank_branch)**: This is the branch of the bank associated with the client's account.
* **Bank Officer (bank_officer)**: This is the name of the bank officer associated with the client's account.
* **Bank Phone (bank_phone)**: This is the phone number of the bank associated with the client's account.
* **Email (email)**: This is the email address of the client or the company.
* **Address (address)**: This is the address of the client or the company.
* **Notes (notes)**: This is a field for any additional comments or notes about the client.
* **Bank Email (bank_email)**: This is the email address of the bank associated with the client's account.
* **Consent Given (consent_given)**: This is a checkbox to indicate whether consent has been given or not.
* **Consent Date (consent_date)**: This is the date the consent was given.
* **Consent Time (consent_time)**: This is the time the consent was given.
* **Client Signature (client_signature)**: This is a field for the client to sign their signature, which can be done multiple times.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authority for clarification.
* This form should only be filled out by authorized personnel.
* Save a copy of the form for your records.
