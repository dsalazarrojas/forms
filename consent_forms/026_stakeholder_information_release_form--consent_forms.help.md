# Stakeholder Information Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stakeholder Information Release Form is a tool for capturing and documenting information about stakeholders involved in projects or initiatives. This form helps to centralize and organize stakeholder data for project updates, reporting, and auditing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: "Stakeholder Title", "Stakeholder Name", "Stakeholder Contact Information", and "Stakeholder Email" are not explicitly marked as required but are likely to be necessary fields to complete accurately.
2. Choose the correct authorization status: Select "Authorized" or "Not Authorized" for the "Stakeholder Is Authorized" field, depending on the stakeholder's level of access or clearance.
3. Confirm stakeholder status: Choose "True" or "False" for the "Stakeholder Is Active" field, indicating whether the stakeholder is currently involved or active in the project.
4. Add any additional notes: The "Stakeholder Notes" field is optional but can be used to capture any additional information about the stakeholder.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Stakeholder Title** (`stakeholder_title`, `text`, not required): Enter the title or position held by the stakeholder in the project or organization.
* **Stakeholder Name** (`stakeholder_name`, `text`, not required): Enter the full name of the stakeholder.
* **Stakeholder Contact Information** (`stakeholder_contact_information`, `text`, not required): Enter the stakeholder's contact information, such as phone number or other communication details.
* **Stakeholder Role** (`stakeholder_role`, `text`, not required): Enter the role or function of the stakeholder within the project or organization.
* **Stakeholder Department** (`stakeholder_department`, `text`, not required): Enter the department or team the stakeholder belongs to.
* **Stakeholder Email** (`stakeholder_email`, `email`, not required): Enter the stakeholder's email address.
* **Stakeholder Phone** (`stakeholder_phone`, `text`, not required): Enter the stakeholder's phone number.
* **Stakeholder Notes** (`stakeholder_notes`, `note`, not required): Enter any additional notes or comments about the stakeholder.
* **Stakeholder Is Authorized** (`stakeholder_is_authorized`, `select_one`, not required): Select "Authorized" or "Not Authorized" to indicate the stakeholder's level of access or clearance.
* **Stakeholder Is Active** (`stakeholder_is_active`, `select_one`, not required): Select "True" or "False" to indicate whether the stakeholder is currently involved or active in the project.
* **Stakeholder Is Authorized** and **Stakeholder Is Active** fields are optional but crucial for accurately capturing stakeholder information. However, they might be required fields depending on the specific project or organizational context. It is essential to review and understand the project's or organization's specific guidelines before filling out these fields.
