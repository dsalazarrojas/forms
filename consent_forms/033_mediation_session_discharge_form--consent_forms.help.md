# Mediation Session Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mediation Session Discharge Form is used to document the outcomes and feedback from a mediation session. It helps to provide a clear and concise record of the session, allowing for better communication and follow-up with participants. This form is usually completed by the participant after a mediation session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form as soon as possible after the mediation session while the details are fresh in your mind.
* Be honest and thorough in your responses, as this form will be used to improve our services and ensure that we provide the best possible experience for all participants.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Outcomes** (`session_outcomes`, text, optional): Please describe the outcomes of the mediation session, including any agreements or actions that were reached. Be as detailed as possible so that we can better understand the effectiveness of our mediation process.
* **Participant Feedback** (`participant_feedback`, text, optional): Share your thoughts and feelings about the mediation session. This will help us identify areas of improvement and provide better support for future participants.
* **Sign-Off** (`sign_off`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you are satisfied with the outcome of the mediation session. Please choose 'Yes' if you are satisfied, 'No' if you are not satisfied, or indicate that you need more time to decide.
* **Mediator Comments** (`mediator_comments`, text, optional): If you have any comments or suggestions for the mediator, please share them here. This will help us improve our services and support.
* **Participant Comments** (`participant_comments`, text, optional): If you have any comments or suggestions for us, please share them here. This will help us identify areas of improvement and provide better support for all participants.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents, so that we can verify your identity and ensure accurate record-keeping.
