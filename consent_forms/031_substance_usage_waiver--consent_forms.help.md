# Substance Usage Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Substance Usage Waiver" form is used to obtain informed consent and release of liability from participants regarding their substance usage or medical treatments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all applicable substances or medical treatments from the "Consent Form" dropdown menu.
2. If you are a participant, confirm your answer to the "Release of Liability" question.
3. If you are a medical provider, sign and date the "Emergency Medical Treatment" section.
4. Sign and date the "Participant Signature" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Consent Form** (`consent_form`, select_multiple, required): Select all the substances or medical treatments you are using or have used. This will help us understand your medical history and ensure your safety during the research or clinical process.
* **Release of Liability** (`release_of_liability`, text, optional): Please answer "yes" or "no" to confirm your understanding of the release of liability.
* **Emergency Medical Treatment** (`emergency_medical_treatment`, text, optional): If you are a medical provider, please sign and date this section to acknowledge your understanding of the emergency medical treatment procedures.
* **Medical Provider Signature** (`medical_provider_signature`, text, optional): If you are a medical provider, please sign and date this section to acknowledge your understanding of the participant's medical history and treatment.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, required): Please sign and date this section to confirm your understanding of the form's purpose and your agreement to the waiver.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any substance or medical treatment, please consult with a medical professional before answering.
* If you are a medical provider, please ensure that you have reviewed the participant's medical history and treatment plan before signing the "Emergency Medical Treatment" and "Medical Provider Signature" sections.
* If you are a participant, please ensure that you have understood the waiver and its implications before signing the "Participant Signature" section.
