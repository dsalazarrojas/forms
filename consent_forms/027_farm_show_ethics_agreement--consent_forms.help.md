# Farm Show Ethics Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Farm Show Ethics Agreement is a form that collects information from exhibitors about their agreement to follow event rules, uphold animal welfare standards, and adhere to conduct expectations. This form helps the event organizers ensure that all exhibitors are aware of and agree to the rules and standards that govern their participation in the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your Exhibitor Information, including your name and contact details.
2. Confirm your agreement to the Event Rules and Animal Welfare Standards.
3. Indicate your adherence to Conduct Expectations.
4. If necessary, provide a Signed Acknowledgment and your Date and Time of completion.
5. Enter your Contact Information, including your contact number and email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exhibitor Information (1)**: This is the contact information of the person completing this form on behalf of the exhibitor. This includes name and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Number (2)**: This is the phone number of the person completing this form. This can be any relevant contact number for the exhibitor.
* **Event Rules Agreed (3)**: This question asks whether the exhibitor agrees to the event rules. Please select 'Agreed' if you agree to the rules.
* **Animal Welfare Standards (4)**: This question asks whether the exhibitor agrees to adhere to animal welfare standards. Please select 'Yes' if you agree to these standards.
* **Conduct Expectations (5)**: This question asks whether the exhibitor agrees to adhere to conduct expectations. Please select 'Yes' if you agree to these expectations.
* **Signed Acknowledgment (6)**: This is a field for the person completing this form to sign their name and acknowledge that they are completing this form on behalf of the exhibitor.
* **Date (7)**: This field allows you to enter the date on which this form is completed.
* **Time (8)**: This field allows you to enter the time on which this form is completed.
* **Email (9)**: This is the email address of the person completing this form. This can be any relevant contact email for the exhibitor.
* **Contact Number (10)**: This is an additional contact number for the person completing this form.
* **Contact Email (11)**: This is an additional contact email for the person completing this form.

Note: Please ensure that all fields are accurate and complete before submitting the form.
