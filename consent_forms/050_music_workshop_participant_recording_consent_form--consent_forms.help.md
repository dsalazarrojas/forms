# Music Workshop Participant Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music Workshop Participant Recording Consent Form is a crucial document that ensures participants' rights and privacy are respected while being recorded during the workshop. By completing this form, participants will be able to provide their consent for recording and understand the purpose of the recording.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Participant Name** in the first field. This is the name that will be used to refer to you during the workshop.
2. Provide your **Email** address, which will be used to contact you for any future recordings or follow-up information.
3. Select your preferred **Contact Method**. You can choose to be contacted through **Mobile**, **Email**, or **None**. This will determine how we will reach out to you for any further communication.
4. Decide on your **Consent to Record**. Please select **True** if you consent to being recorded during the workshop, and **False** if you do not.
5. If you consent to being recorded, select the **Recording Purpose** for which you are granting your consent. You can choose **Audio Recording**, **Video Recording**, or **Both**.
6. Choose your **Consent Date**, which is the date on which you grant your consent.
7. Sign your name in the **Consent Signature** field, which will confirm your consent.
8. In the **Contact Information** field, you can provide any additional contact information you'd like us to have.
9. In the **Participant Information** field, please provide a brief description of yourself, including any information that will help us to identify you accurately.
10. In the **Workshop Details** field, you can provide any additional information about the workshop you're participating in.
11. If you'd like to include, you can provide the **Recording Location** and **Recording Equipment** details in the respective fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Name** (Required): The name of the participant, as it will be referred to during the workshop.
  - Type: text
  - Purpose: Identify the participant
  - Example: John Doe
- **Email** (Required): The email address of the participant to contact for further information.
  - Type: email
  - Purpose: Provide a means of communication for further information or follow-up.
  - Example: johndoe@example.com
- **Contact Method** (Required): The means by which you would like to be contacted.
  - Type: select_multiple
  - Purpose: Determine the method of communication for further information.
  - Example: Mobile, Email, None
- **Consent to Record** (Required): Consent to record during the workshop.
  - Type: select_one
  - Purpose: Grant or deny consent for recording purposes.
  - Example: True, False
- **Recording Purpose** (Optional): The purpose of the recording (Audio, Video, Both).
  - Type: select_multiple
  - Purpose: Determine the type of recording.
  - Example: Audio Recording, Video Recording, Both
- **Consent Date** (Required): The date on which consent is given.
  - Type: date
  - Purpose: Record the date of consent.
  - Example: 2024-02-20
- **Consent Signature** (Required): A signature confirming the participant's consent.
  - Type: note
  - Purpose: Verify the participant's consent.
  - Example: John Doe's signature (handwritten)
- **Contact Information** (Optional): Any additional contact information you'd like to share.
  - Type: text
  - Purpose: Provide additional contact information.
  - Example: 123-456-7890, johndoe@email.com
- **Participant Information** (Required): A brief description of the participant.
  - Type: text
  - Purpose: Provide information about the participant.
  - Example: John Doe, participant of the music workshop
- **Workshop Details** (Optional): Any additional information about the workshop.
  - Type: text
  - Purpose: Provide information about the workshop.
  - Example: The music workshop on composition techniques for beginners
- **Recording Location** (Optional): The location of the recording.
  - Type: text
  - Purpose: Provide the location of the recording.
  - Example: Music room, University Hall
- **Recording Equipment** (Optional): The equipment used for recording.
  - Type: text
  - Purpose: Provide the equipment used for recording.
  - Example: Microphone, camera

Please note that this form is for your protection, and your consent is required to ensure a smooth and respectful recording experience.
