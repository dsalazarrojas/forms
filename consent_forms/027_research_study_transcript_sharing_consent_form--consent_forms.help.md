# Research Study Transcript Sharing Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to obtain agreement from participants to share their research study transcripts or data with third parties.

## How To Complete This Form

* To complete this form, fill in your name, contact details (email, phone number, and address), and date of birth.
* Please provide a valid email address in the format 'username@example.com'.
* Enter your address as per your country's postal code.
* Select your date of birth in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
* If you are a participant in a research study, provide the study title and description.
* Sign your name in the space provided for participant signature.
* If you are the director of a research study, sign your name in the space provided for research study director signature.
* Select 'True' if you consent to sharing your transcript or research study data with third parties and 'False' if you do not.
* If you select 'True', provide the reasons for your consent in the text box provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as it should be displayed on your transcript or research study data.
* **Research Study Identifier**: Enter the identifier or code assigned to your research study.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your contact email address, phone number, and address.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number in the format '###-#####'.
* **Email**: Enter your email address in the format 'username@example.com'.
* **Address**: Enter your address as per your country's postal code.
* **Date of Birth**: Select your date of birth in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
* **Research Study Title**: Enter the title of the research study you are participating in or conducting.
* **Research Study Description**: Provide a brief description of the research study.
* **Participant Signature**: Sign your name in the space provided.
* **Research Study Director Signature**: If you are the director of a research study, sign your name in the space provided.
* **Date**: Enter the date in the format 'YYYY-MM'.
* **Time**: Enter the time in the format 'HH:MM'.
* **Research Study Transcript Sharing Consent**: Select 'True' to agree to sharing your transcript or research study data with third parties, and 'False' to decline.
* **Research Study Transcript Sharing Consent Reasons**: If you select 'True', provide the reasons for your consent in the text box provided.
