# Payroll Statement Delivery Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Payroll Statement Delivery Consent Form is a document that ensures employees provide their consent for the delivery of payroll statements. This form is used to collect information about how employees want to receive their payroll statements, including the type of statements and the method of delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Answer each question carefully, taking into consideration the options available.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your HR department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Is this correct** (`employee_consent`)

* This field allows you to confirm that the information provided is correct. Please select "Yes" if everything is accurate, and "No" if you need to make any corrections.

### **Consent to receive electronic delivery** (`consent_to_send`)

* This field allows you to choose how you want to receive your payroll statements. Please select one or more of the following options:
	+ Payroll statements to home
	+ Payroll statements to email
	+ Both

### **Delivery start date** (`consent_date`)

* This field is used to specify the start date for the delivery of your payroll statements. Please select a date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### **Type of statements to be delivered** (`consent_to_statement_type`)

* This field allows you to select the type of statements you want to receive. Please select all that apply:
	+ Pay stubs
	+ Payroll summary
	+ Benefits details

### **Email address** (`email_address`)

* This field is used to provide your email address for electronic delivery. Please enter your email address in the format `name@domain.com`.

### **Phone number** (`phone_number`)

* This field is used to provide your phone number for receiving statements. Please enter your phone number in the format `XXX-XXX-XXXX`.

### **Comments** (`comments`)

* This field is used to provide any additional comments or information about your payroll statement delivery preferences. Please use this field to provide any relevant details.

### **Submit** (`submit`)

* This field is used to submit your completed form. Please fill out all the fields accurately before submitting the form.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form carefully and accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your HR department.
* This form is used solely for the purpose of payroll statement delivery, and the information collected will only be used for this purpose.
