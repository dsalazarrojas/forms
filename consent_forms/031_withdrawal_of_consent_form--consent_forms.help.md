# Withdrawal Of Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Withdrawal of Consent Form is used to document the withdrawal of consent from an individual. This form is completed by the individual who has granted consent and wishes to revoke or withdraw it. It ensures that the reason for withdrawal and the signature of the individual are recorded accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand the purpose and the sections required.
2. Answer all the required fields accurately and truthfully.
3. Choose the correct options for the reason for withdrawal and witness signature.
4. Sign the form with your signature, and include the date of signature.
5. Ensure that a witness verifies your signature and date of signature.
6. Have the verification done by a witness who can confirm your identity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Withdrawal Of Consent Form** (`withdrawal_of_consent_form`, note, optional): This section is the introduction and title of the form. You can write or enter any additional information about the reason for this particular form, if needed.
* **Reason for Withdrawal** (`reason_for_withdrawal`, text, optional): Please describe the reason for withdrawal of consent. Be as detailed as possible to ensure clarity. This section is not required but highly recommended for a clear understanding of the reason for withdrawal.
* **Date Of Signature** (`date_of_signature`, date, optional): Enter the date you are signing this form. This is a required field to ensure the form is dated accurately.
* **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`, select one, optional): Choose the name of the witness who will verify your signature and date of signature. You can choose from the options provided.
* **Withdraw Consent For** (`withdraw_consent_for`, select multiple, optional): Select all the consents you are withdrawing. You can select one or more consents from the provided options.
* **Signature of Withdrawal** (`signature_of_withdrawal`, select one, optional): Choose the name of the individual who is signing this form to withdraw consent.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are signing this form to withdraw consent. This is a required field to ensure accuracy.
* **Date Of Signature Verification** (`date_of_signature_verification`, date, optional): Enter the date the witness verified your signature. This is a required field to confirm the signature is valid.
* **Verification of Withdrawal** (`verification_of_withdrawal`, select one, optional): Choose the reason for verification from the provided options. 

## Tips
- Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure the withdrawal of consent is properly documented.
- Ensure that a witness verifies your signature and date of signature.
- Keep a copy of the form for your records.
- If you need assistance with filling out this form, please ask a trusted person or authority figure.
