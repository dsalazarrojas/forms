# Practitioner Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture the essential information required for a formal agreement between a practitioner and a client.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Introduction**: Please read this section carefully to understand the purpose and importance of this form.
2. Fill in the **Practitioner Name** section with your name as the practitioner.
3. In the **Practitioner Contact Information** section, provide your contact details such as your address and phone number.
4. In the **Client Name** section, fill in the name of the client.
5. In the **Client Contact Information** section, provide the client's contact details such as their address and phone number.
6. In the **Agreement Date** section, select the date of the agreement using the calendar.
7. In the **Agreement Time** section, select the time of the agreement using the time picker.
8. In the **Practitioner Responsibilities** section, describe the responsibilities of the practitioner.
9. In the **Client Responsibilities** section, describe the responsibilities of the client.
10. In the **Termination** section, you may optionally provide details about how the agreement will be terminated.
11. In the **Practitioner Signature** section, select your signature from the drop-down list.
12. In the **Client Signature** section, select the client's signature from the drop-down list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Practitioner Name** (`practitioner_name`, text, required): This field asks you to fill in your name as the practitioner.
* **Practitioner Contact Information** (`practitioner_contact_info`, text, required): This field is for you to provide your contact information such as address and phone number.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): This field is for you to fill in the name of the client.
* **Client Contact Information** (`client_contact_info`, text, required): This field is for you to provide the client's contact information such as address and phone number.
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, date, required): This field is for you to select the date of the agreement using the calendar.
* **Agreement Time** (`agreement_time`, time, required): This field is for you to select the time of the agreement using the time picker.
* **Practitioner Responsibilities** (`practitioner_responsibilities`, text, required): This field is for you to describe your responsibilities as the practitioner.
* **Client Responsibilities** (`client_responsibilities`, text, required): This field is for you to describe the client's responsibilities.
* **Termination** (`termination`, text, optional): This field is for you to optionally provide details about how the agreement will be terminated.
* **Practitioner Signature** (`practitioner_signature`, select_one, required): This field is for you to select your signature from the drop-down list.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, select_one, required): This field is for you to select the client's signature from the drop-down list.
