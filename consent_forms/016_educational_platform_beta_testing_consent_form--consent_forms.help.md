# educational_platform_beta_testing_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect participant details, obtain their consent, and outline their expectations for the educational platform beta testing process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Participant Details section, providing your information as required.
2. Select the appropriate consent option(s) that best align with your understanding of the beta testing process.
3. In the Expectations section, type in any specific expectations or concerns you have for the beta testing process.
4. If you have a Participant ID, please enter it in the designated field.
5. Enter your Participant Email address for contact purposes.
6. Provide your Participant Phone number for any further communication.
7. If you know the Expected Launch Date and Time of the platform, please enter it in the designated fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required: false): Fill in your name, contact information, and any additional details as required.
- **Consent** (`consent`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select all that apply from the provided options to indicate your understanding and agreement to the beta testing process.
  - Option 1: I understand the platform is for testing purposes and may not be stable or fully functional.
  - Option 2: I acknowledge that my participation in the beta testing process may involve some risks or inconveniences.
- **Expectations** (`expectations`, text, required: false): Type in any specific expectations or concerns you have for the beta testing process.
- **Participant ID** (`participant_id`, number, required: false): Enter your Participant ID if you have one.
- **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
- **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number for any further communication.
- **Expected Launch Date** (`expected_launch_date`, date, required: false): If you know the expected launch date of the platform, please enter it here.
- **Expected Launch Time** (`expected_launch_time`, time, required: false): If you know the expected launch time of the platform, please enter it here.
