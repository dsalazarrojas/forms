# Patient Communication Data Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient communication data for the purpose of tailoring communication strategies to individual patient needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the preferred channels used for communication (e.g., Email, Phone, Other).
2. Choose the frequency of communication (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
3. Enter the contact number for the patient (e.g., phone number).
4. Enter the patient's email address.
5. Provide the date of the last contact with the patient.
6. Specify the preferred time of contact (e.g., morning, afternoon).
7. Add any additional comments from the patient.
8. Describe any follow-up actions taken or planned.
9. Enter the date of the next follow-up contact.
10. Select the preferred channels for follow-up (e.g., Email, Phone, Other).
11. Add any additional comments on follow-up actions.
12. Specify the status of follow-up actions (e.g., Not Started, In Progress, Completed).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Channels Used** (`communication_channels`): Select the preferred methods of communication for the patient (e.g., Email, Phone, Other).
* **How Often** (`communication_frequency`): Choose the frequency of communication (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): Enter the contact number for the patient (e.g., phone number).
* **Email** (`email`): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Date of Last Contact** (`date_of_last_contact`): Provide the date of the last time the patient was contacted.
* **Time of Contact** (`preferred_time_of_day`): Specify the preferred time of contact (e.g., morning, afternoon).
* **Comments** (`comments`): Add any additional comments from the patient.
* **Follow-up Actions** (`follow_up_actions`): Describe any follow-up actions taken or planned.
* **Next Follow-up Date** (`next_follow_up_date`): Enter the date of the next follow-up contact.
* **Preferred Channels** (`communication_preferences`): Select the preferred communication channels for follow-up (e.g., Email, Phone, Other).
* **Follow-up Comments** (`follow_up_comments`): Add any additional comments on follow-up actions.
* **Follow-up Status** (`follow_up_status`): Specify the status of follow-up actions (e.g., Not Started, In Progress, Completed).
