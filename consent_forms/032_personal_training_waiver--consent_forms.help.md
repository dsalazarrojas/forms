# Personal Training Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Training Waiver form is designed to gather essential information for personal training. This form is intended for individuals seeking personal training services, and it's a requirement for trainers to have this information before commencing training sessions. It's crucial to provide accurate and truthful information to ensure a safe and effective training experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your contact information (page 1) including your Name, Birthdate, and any other required fields.
2.  Provide emergency contact information (page 1), including the name and relationship of the emergency contact person, their phone number, and address.
3.  Indicate if the emergency contact is available at the training location (page 1).
4.  Confirm your medical status regarding physical and medical conditions (pages 1-2).
5.  Declare your medical insurance status (page 2).
6.  Specify your medical insurance provider and policy number (page 2) if applicable.
7.  Confirm your medical conditions that may affect training (page 2).
8.  Select a time and date for the training session (page 2).
9.  Provide your email address (page 2).
10.  Indicate your training location preferences (page 2).
11.  Sign the form to confirm your agreement and understanding (page 3).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contact Information** (page 1):
    *   *Name* (`<name>`, `text`, Required): Please enter your full name.
    *   *Birthdate* (`<birthdate>`, `date`, Required): Enter your birthdate in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
    *   *Emergency Contact* (`<emergency_contact>`, `text`, Required): Provide the name of the emergency contact person.
    *   *Relationship* (`<emergency_relationship>`, `select_multiple`, Required): Indicate the relationship of the emergency contact person (e.g., parent, spouse, friend).
    *   *Phone* (`<emergency_phone>`, `text`, Required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
    *   *Address* (`<emergency_address>`, `select_multiple`, Required): Indicate if the emergency contact is available at the training location.
*   **Emergency Contact Information** (page 1):
    *   *Emergency Contact* (`<emergency_contact>`, `text`, Required): Provide the name of the emergency contact person.
    *   *Phone* (`<emergency_phone>`, `text`, Required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
    *   *Address* (`<emergency_address>`, `select_multiple`, Required): Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
*   **Medical Information** (page 2):
    *   *Medical Condition* (`<medical_condition>`, `select_multiple`, Required): Confirm any medical conditions that may affect your training experience.
    *   *Medical Insurance* (`<medical_insurance>`, `select_multiple`, Required): Declare your medical insurance status.
    *   *Insurance Provider* (`<insurance_provider>`, `text`, Optional): Enter your medical insurance provider name.
    *   *Policy Number* (`<policy_number>`, `text`, Optional): Enter your medical insurance policy number.
*   **Training Schedule** (page 2):
    *   *Date* (`<date>`, `date`, Required): Select the date for your training session.
    *   *Time* (`<time>`, `time`, Required): Select the time for your training session.
*   **Training Location** (page 2):
    *   *Training Location* (`<training_location>`, `select_multiple`, Required): Indicate your preferred training location.
*   **Medical Insurance** (page 2):
    *   *Medical Insurance* (`<medical_insurance>`, `select_multiple`, Required): Declare your medical insurance status.
*   **Signature** (page 3):
    *   *Signature* (`<signature>`, `note`, Required): Sign the form to confirm your agreement and understanding.

**Tips**:

*   Please ensure you answer all required fields.
*   If you are unsure about any medical conditions, consult your doctor or medical professional before training.
*   Keep your contact information up-to-date to ensure the trainer can contact you in case of an emergency.
*   If you have any other medical conditions not listed, please include them in the "Medical Condition" field.
*   Please note that medical insurance information is not required but is recommended for your convenience.
