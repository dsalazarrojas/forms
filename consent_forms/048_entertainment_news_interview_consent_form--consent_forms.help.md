# Entertainment News Interview Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Entertainment News Interview Consent Form is a document that collects consent from both media professionals and interviewees for the release of a potential news story. This form is a crucial step in the process of publishing an interview, ensuring that all parties involved are aware of the terms of the release.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the Media Professional's name in the "Media Professional" field.
2. Enter the Interviewee's name in the "Interviewee" field.
3. Select the relevant "Interview Details" options that apply to the interview (Interviewer, Interview Time, or Location).
4. Choose the type of consent given by selecting "Exclusive" or "Non-Exclusive".
5. Enter the date and time for the release of the interview in the "Release Date" and "Release Time" fields.
6. Select the release period for the interview from the options provided.
7. Indicate if consent was given by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Consent Given" field.
8. Add any additional comments in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Media Professional**: Enter the name of the media professional conducting the interview.
* **Interviewee**: Enter the name of the person being interviewed.
* **Interview Details**: Select the details of the interview that apply. This can be the interviewer, the interview time, or the location.
* **Consent Type**: Choose the type of consent given. Exclusive means the interview will be exclusively published with the media professional's organization, while non-exclusive means it can be published with other organizations.
* **Release Date**: Enter the date of release for the interview.
* **Release Time**: Enter the time of release for the interview.
* **Release Period**: Select the period of release for the interview, which can be one day, three days, or one week.
* **Consent Given**: Indicate if consent was given for the release of the interview. Select "Yes" if consent was given, and "No" if not.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the interview.
