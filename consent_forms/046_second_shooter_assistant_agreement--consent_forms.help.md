# Second Shooter Assistant Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to capture information from clients and photographers regarding a "Second Shooter" agreement, which is likely a contract for a photography service where one photographer works alongside another to capture a specific event or shoot. The form is designed to collect contact and other basic information from both the client and the photographer.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing the form's purpose and fields.
2. Complete the "Client Information" section, providing the client's details as required.
3. Then, move on to the "Photographer Information" section, entering the photographer's details.
4. Finally, provide a brief description of the shoot details in the "Shoot Details" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information** (`client_info`, text, optional): This section is for entering the client's basic contact information, such as name, phone number, and email. Enter the client's details accurately to ensure they can be reached for any further communication or updates.
* **Photographer Information** (`photographer_info`, text, optional): This section is for entering the photographer's details, including their name, contact information, and other relevant details. The photographer may need to be contacted directly for any further questions or shoot updates.
* **Shoot Details** (`shoot_details`, text, optional): This field is for providing a brief description of the shoot details, such as the event or subject being photographed, and any relevant dates or locations. This information will help both the client and photographer understand the scope and requirements of the shoot.
