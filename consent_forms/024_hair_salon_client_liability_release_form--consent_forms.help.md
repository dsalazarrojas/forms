# Hair Salon Client Liability Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hair Salon Client Liability Release Form is a document that acknowledges the client's understanding and acceptance of the risks associated with receiving services from the hair salon. This form is essential for clients to acknowledge their responsibility and liability for any potential harm or injury that may arise during the salon's treatment or services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each field carefully and answer all the required questions.
2. Provide your signature and the date you sign this form to confirm your understanding.
3. Answer all questions accurately and truthfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Agreement** (`customer_agreement`, text, required): This is the main section where you will declare your understanding of the risks associated with receiving services from the hair salon. Please type your agreement in this field.
* **Accept Risk** (`accept_risk`, text, required): Please type your acceptance of the risks associated with receiving services from the hair salon.
* **Risk Explanation** (`risk_explanation`, text, optional): If you have any questions or concerns about the risks associated with receiving services from the hair salon, please provide your explanation here.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, optional): This field is for the date you sign this form. Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Salutation** (`salutation`, select_one, required): Please select one of the options provided: 'Yes' or 'No'. This is to confirm whether you are aware of the risks associated with receiving services from the hair salon.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): This is your first name. Please enter your first name accurately.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): This is your last name. Please enter your last name accurately.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): This is your address. Please enter your address accurately.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This is your phone number. Please enter your phone number accurately.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This is your email address. Please enter your email address accurately.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): This is the space for you to sign your name.
* **Date Signed 2** (`date_signed_2`, date, optional): This field is for the date you sign this form. Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Tips
- Make sure to sign and date this form accurately.
- If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please ask your hair stylist or management.
- Complete this form before receiving any services from the hair salon.
