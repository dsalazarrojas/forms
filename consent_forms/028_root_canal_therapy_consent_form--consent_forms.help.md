# Root Canal Therapy Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to capture a patient's consent and personal information for root canal therapy. It includes fields for patient demographic information, medical history, and provider details. Completing this form will help the dentist or clinic understand the patient's needs and ensure a smooth and effective treatment experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the patient's **First Name**, **Date of Birth**, and **Phone Number**. These fields are mandatory.
2. Provide your **Email** address so the clinic can contact you if needed.
3. Disclose any **Medical History** you have, if any.
4. List any **Allergies** you have (e.g., Penicillin, Latex).
5. Indicate if you have any **Medical Conditions** (e.g., Diabetes, Hypertension, Asthma).
6. Select your **Consent** to undergo the root canal therapy treatment (choose "Yes" or "No").
7. Sign your name to confirm your consent.
8. Enter the **Date** of your signature.
9. Select your **Provider** (dentist/doctor), **Clinic**, and enter their **Name**, **Address**, and **Phone**.
10. If applicable, enter your **Title**, **Specialty**, **Medical School**, and **Graduation Date**.
11. Provide your **License Number** and **Expiration Date** of your provider's medical license.
12. Enter your **Clinic Phone** and **Clinic Email**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name. This will help the clinic staff address you correctly.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format of month/day/year (e.g., 02/12/1990).
* **Address**: This field is optional and not required. If you have a specific address, enter it here.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number so the clinic can contact you if needed.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so the clinic can contact you if needed.
* **Medical History**: Describe any medical conditions or surgeries you have had previously.
* **Allergy**: Select any allergies you have (e.g., Penicillin, Latex).
* **Medical Condition**: Select any medical conditions you have (e.g., Diabetes, Hypertension, Asthma).
* **Consent**: Select "Yes" to consent to the root canal therapy treatment. If you have any concerns, contact the clinic before signing.
* **Signature**: Sign your name to confirm your consent.
* **Date**: Enter the date of your signature.
* **Provider**: Select the provider (dentist/doctor) who will be performing the treatment.
* **Clinic**: Select the clinic where you will be receiving the treatment.
* **Name**: Enter the name of your provider (dentist/doctor).
* **Address**: Enter the address of your provider (dentist/doctor).
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of your provider (dentist/doctor).
* **Title**: This field is optional and not required. Enter your title (e.g., Dr., Dr.).
* **Specialty**: This field is optional and not required. Enter your provider's specialty (e.g., General Dentist, Specialist).
* **Medical School**: This field is optional and not required. Enter the name of your provider's medical school.
* **Graduation Date**: This field is optional and not required. Enter the date of your provider's graduation.
* **License Number**: Enter the number of your provider's medical license.
* **Expiration Date**: Enter the expiration date of your provider's medical license.
* **Clinic Phone**: Enter the phone number of the clinic.
* **Clinic Email**: Enter the email address of the clinic.
* **Other Info**: This field is optional and not required. Enter any additional information that may be relevant to your treatment.

Remember to review all the fields carefully before submitting the form. If you are unsure about any field, ask the clinic staff for clarification.
