# Pedagogical Innovation Showcase Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a consent form for a pedagogical innovation showcase. It is designed to collect user information, details about their teaching projects, and their sharing preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Complete the User Information section with your name and contact information.
2.  Answer the question "Are you part of a teaching project?" by selecting either "True", "False", "Not Sure" or "Other".
3.  If you selected "True" or "Not Sure", use the field "Project Prophets and Suggestion" to provide any prophetic ideas or suggestions related to your project.
4.  If you selected "True", use the field "Teaching Project Details" to provide additional project details.
5.  If you're a student, use the field "Student Works" to provide any relevant student work.
6.  Select how you prefer to share your work or information, by choosing "Public", "Private", or "Other" in the "Sharing Preferences" field.
7.  Provide your consent for your parent/guardian to share your information, by selecting either "I consent" or "Not consent" in the "Parent/Guardian Consent" field.
8.  Use the "Other Details" field to capture any additional information not covered in other fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **User Information** (`User Information`, `text`, required): Complete this field with your name and contact information.
*   **Teaching Project** (`Teaching Project`, `select_multiple`, required): Answer whether you are part of a teaching project or not.
    +   If "True", provide additional details about your project in the "Project Prophets and Suggestion" field.
    +   If "False", skip to the next field.
    +   If "Not Sure", provide additional project-related information in the "Project Prophets and Suggestion" field.
*   **Prophets and Suggestion** (`Prophets and Suggestion`, `note`, required): Provide any prophetic ideas or suggestions related to your project. This field is only visible if you selected "True" or "Not Sure" in the "Teaching Project" field.
*   **Project Prophets and Suggestion** (`Project Prophets and Suggestion`, `note`, required): Provide additional project-related details. This field is only visible if you selected "True" in the "Teaching Project" field.
*   **Student Works** (`Student Works`, `note`, required): Provide any relevant student work related to your project. This field is only visible if you are a student.
*   **Sharing Preferences** (`Sharing Preferences`, `select_one`, required): Select how you prefer to share your work or information, whether "Public", "Private", or "Other".
*   **Parent/Guardian Consent** (`Parent/Guardian Consent`, `select_multiple`, required): Provide your consent for your parent/guardian to share your information, by selecting either "I consent" or "Not consent".
*   **Other Details** (`Other Details`, `note`, required): Use this field to capture any additional information not covered in other fields.
