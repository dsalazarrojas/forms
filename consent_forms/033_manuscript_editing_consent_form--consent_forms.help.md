# Manuscript Editing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manuscript Editing Consent Form is used to collect consent from authors for the editing of their manuscripts. This form helps ensure that authors understand and agree to the terms and conditions of the editing process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Author Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Author Email" field. This will help us contact you if we need any additional information.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Author Phone" field (optional).
4. Select the relevant project details in the "Project Details" field (optional).
5. Choose the type of manuscript you are submitting in the "Manuscript Type" field (optional).
6. Enter the title of your manuscript in the "Manuscript Title" field (optional).
7. Select the date you submitted your manuscript in the "Manuscript Date" field (optional).
8. Choose the status of your manuscript in the "Manuscript Status" field (optional).
9. Add any comments regarding your manuscript in the "Manuscript Comments" field (optional).
10. Select the consent option that best describes your agreement to the editing process in the "Author Consent" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Author Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your passport or ID.
  - This field is used to identify you as the author of the manuscript.
- **Author Email** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you.
  - This field is used to communicate with you regarding your manuscript.
- **Author Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for additional contact (optional).
  - This field is used to contact you for further information or clarification.
- **Project Details** (select_one, required/optional): Select the relevant details about your project.
  - This field helps us understand the context of your manuscript.
- **Manuscript Type** (select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the type of manuscript you are submitting.
  - This field helps us understand the nature of your manuscript.
- **Manuscript Title** (text, required/optional): Enter the title of your manuscript.
  - This field is used to identify your manuscript.
- **Manuscript Date** (date, required/optional): Select the date you submitted your manuscript.
  - This field helps us track the submission date of your manuscript.
- **Manuscript Status** (select_one, required/optional): Choose the status of your manuscript.
  - This field helps us understand the current state of your manuscript.
- **Manuscript Comments** (note, required/optional): Add any comments or remarks regarding your manuscript.
  - This field is used to provide additional information about your manuscript.
- **Author Consent** (select_one, required/optional): Select the option that best describes your agreement to the editing process.
  - This field ensures you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions of the editing process.

## Tips
* Please fill out this form carefully and accurately.
* Ensure you have read and understood the terms and conditions before selecting your consent option.
* Contact us if you have any questions or concerns regarding the form or the editing process.
