# team_transportation_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The team transportation consent form is used by parents or guardians to provide essential details about a child's transportation and emergency contact information, ensuring that schools or organizations can keep their students safe and well-informed during school-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each field in the form according to your personal information and needs.
2. Fill out the form carefully, as it is crucial for the school or organization to have up-to-date and accurate information about your child.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed, if applicable, to prevent any delays or issues.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Details** (`parent_details`, text, required: false): Enter your name and contact information as the parent or guardian of the child.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Provide your contact details, such as your name, email, and phone number, so that schools or organizations can stay in touch with you.
* **Travel Details** (`travel_details`, text, required: false): Describe any travel arrangements or plans your child may have, including dates, times, and modes of transportation.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false): List the contact information for an emergency contact person, such as a family member or trusted adult.
* **Student Details** (`student_details`, text, required: false): Add any relevant details about your child, such as medical conditions or allergies, that may impact their transportation or well-being.
* **Transportation Details** (`transportation_details`, text, required: false): Specify any special transportation requirements or arrangements for your child, such as wheelchairs or specific vehicle needs.
* **Consent** (`consent`, text, required: false): Provide your consent for your child to participate in school-related activities, understanding that you will receive updates and notifications from the school or organization.

## Tips
- Double-check the form for any typos or errors before submitting.
- Keep the information up-to-date and accurate at all times.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact the school or organization for clarification.
- Ensure that you have provided all necessary contact information for emergency situations.
