# Tenant Experience Program Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Experience Program Consent Form is designed to collect information about a tenant's consent to participate in the Tenant Experience Program. This form is used to gather their preferences and management details about property teams.
 
## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete the form, navigate through the pages below.
2. Review each section carefully and enter your information accurately.
 
## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Tenant Experience Program Consent Form
* **Tenant Experience Program Consent Form**: This is the main section where you can input any comments or information about your consent to participate in the Tenant Experience Program. You can enter your response in the space provided.
 
### Page 2: Sharing Consent Forms
* **Sharing Consent Forms**: In this section, please indicate whether you would like to share your consent form with other property teams. You can select either "Yes" or "No" to indicate your preference.
 
### Page 3: Property Teams
* **Property Teams**: This section is for you to input any information about the property teams you wish to share your consent form with. You can enter your response in the space provided.
 
### Page 4: Tenant Consent Management
* **Tenant Consent Management**: In this section, please input any information about how you would like to manage your consent. You can enter your response in the space provided.
 
## Tips
- Please review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
- This form is required for participation in the Tenant Experience Program.
