# Tattoo Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tattoo Release Form is a document that outlines the terms and conditions of the release of a tattoo design. It ensures that both the tattoo artist and the client are aware of their rights and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand the terms and conditions of the release.
2. Select the correct options for your situation.
3. Provide accurate and truthful information in all fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Submission** (`1`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' if the client has submitted the form correctly.
* **Client Details** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required): Select 'Yes' if the client's details are correct.
* **Tattoo Details** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required): Select 'Yes' if the tattoo details are accurate.
* **Client Statement** (`4`, `text`, required): Provide a clear and concise statement of the client's thoughts and feelings about the tattoo.
* **Release Agreement** (`5`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' if the client agrees to the release terms.
* **Consent** (`6`, `date`, required): Enter the date the client agrees to the release.
* **Signature** (`7`, `note`, required): Sign the form to confirm your acceptance of the release terms.
* **Email** (`8`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to ensure you can be contacted.
* **Phone** (`9`, `text`, required): Enter a contact phone number.
* **Client Name** (`10`, `text`, required): Enter the client's name.
* **Client Address** (`11`, `text`, required): Enter the client's address.
* **Artist Statement** (`12`, `text`, required): Provide a statement of your thoughts and feelings about the tattoo.
* **Tattoo Artist** (`13`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the tattoo artist.
* **Studio Address** (`14`, `text`, required): Enter the studio's address.
* **Contact Number** (`15`, `text`, required): Enter the contact number.
* **Contact Email** (`16`, `email`, required): Enter the contact email.
* **Release Date** (`17`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the release.
* **Signature Date** (`18`, `date`, required): Enter the date you sign the release form.
* **Artist Signature** (`19`, `note`, required): Sign the form as the artist.
* **Client Signature** (`20`, `note`, required): Sign the form as the client.
* **Client Phone** (`21`, `text`, required): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Artist Name** (`22`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the artist.
* **Artist Email** (`23`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Studio Email** (`24`, `email`, required): Enter the studio's email address.
* **Studio Name** (`25`, `text`, required): Enter the studio's name.
* **Release Number** (`26`, `text`, required): Enter a unique release number.
* **Client Statement 2** (`27`, `text`, required): Provide a statement of the client's thoughts and feelings about the tattoo (if applicable).
* **Tattoo Artist Statement** (`28`, `text`, required): Provide a statement of your thoughts and feelings about the tattoo (if applicable).
* **Release Agreement 2** (`29`, `select_one`, optional): Select 'Yes' if the client agrees to the release terms (if applicable).
