# education_platform_testimonial_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The education platform testimonial release form is a tool used to collect student testimonials for the education platform. This form is used to collect valuable feedback from students about their experience on the platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the student's testimonial details in the "Student Testimonial Details" field.
2. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
3. Enter the date of graduation in the "Date of Graduation" field in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
4. Enter the student's email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter the student's phone number (optional) in the "Phone" field.
6. Sign the form with your signature in the "Signature" field.
7. Upload any supporting media (e.g., photos, videos) in the "Media Upload" field (optional).
8. Write your testimonial in the "Testimonial" field.
9. Enter the release date in the "Release Date" field in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
10. Check the box for "Signature of Consent" to confirm that you have read and understood the terms of service.
11. Select "Have you consented" to confirm that you have read and understood the terms of service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Testimonial Details**: Enter a brief description of your experience on the education platform.
* **Student Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Graduation**: Enter the date you graduated from the education platform in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Signature**: Sign your name to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Media Upload**: Upload any supporting media (e.g., photos, videos) that demonstrate your experience on the education platform (optional).
* **Testimonial**: Write a brief testimonial about your experience on the education platform.
* **Release Date**: Enter the date you are releasing your testimonial in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Signature of Consent**: Check the box to confirm that you have read and understood the terms of service.
* **Have you consented**: Select the option that best describes your level of consent.
