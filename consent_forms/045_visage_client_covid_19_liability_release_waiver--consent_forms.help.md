# visage_client_covid_19_liability_release_waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from clients who are participating in Visage's COVID-19 liability release and waiver program. The purpose of this form is to gather basic contact information and emergency contact details of the client. This information will be used for the administration and management of the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the field labeled "First Name".
2. Enter your last name in the field labeled "Last Name".
3. Enter your street address in the field labeled "street".
4. Enter your city in the field labeled "city".
5. Enter your state in the field labeled "state".
6. Enter your zip code in the field labeled "zip_code".
7. If you need to provide emergency contact information, enter the emergency contact's first name in the field labeled "emergency contact First Name".
8. If you need to provide emergency contact information, enter the emergency contact's last name in the field labeled "emergency contact Last Name".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (First Name, text, Optional)**: Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name (Last Name, text, Optional)**: Enter your last name here.
* **street (street, text, Optional)**: Enter your street address here.
* **city (city, text, Optional)**: Enter your city here.
* **state (state, text, Optional)**: Enter your state here.
* **zip_code (zip_code, number, Optional)**: Enter your zip code here.
* **emergency contact First Name (emergency_contact_first_name, text, Optional)**: If you need to provide emergency contact information, enter the emergency contact's first name here.
* **emergency contact Last Name (emergency_contact_last_name, text, Optional)**: If you need to provide emergency contact information, enter the emergency contact's last name here.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure proper communication and emergency response.
