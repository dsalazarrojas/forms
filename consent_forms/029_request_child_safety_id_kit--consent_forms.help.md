# Request Child Safety ID Kit - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Request Child Safety ID Kit form is a critical tool used in schools to identify and verify the safety of children on school premises. This form is completed by school staff to document information about students, including their personal details, school affiliation, and safety status. The purpose of this form is to gather accurate and comprehensive information to ensure the safety and well-being of students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1.  Enter your information as requested in the form.
2.  Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
3.  Review your information carefully before submitting the form.
4.  Submit the form once you have verified the accuracy of your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (Page 1)** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter the student's first name as it appears on their identification card or school records.
* **Last Name (Page 1)** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter the student's last name as it appears on their identification card or school records.
* **School Name (Page 1)** (`school_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the student's school or institution where they are enrolled.
* **Address 1 (Page 1)** (`address1`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the student's residence or the school address where the student attends.
* **City (Page 1)** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city where the student resides or the city of the school's location.
* **State (Page 1)** (`state`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' if the student is currently residing in this state or 'No' if they are not.
* **Zip (Page 1)** (`zip`, `number`, required): Enter the zip code of the student's residence or the school's address.
* **School Grade (Page 1)** (`school_grade`, `select_multiple`, required): Select 'Yes' if the student is enrolled in this school grade level or 'No' if they are not.
* **School Administrator Name (Page 2)** (`school_admin_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the school administrator or staff member responsible for the student's records.
* **School Administrator Title (Page 2)** (`school_admin_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title or position of the school administrator or staff member.
* **Contact Email (Page 2)** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): Enter a valid email address for the school administrator or staff member.
* **Contact Phone (Page 2)** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter a valid phone number for the school administrator or staff member.
* **Additional Notes (Page 2)** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional information or comments about the student's safety status.
* **School Program (Page 2)** (`school_program`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select 'Yes' if the student is enrolled in this school program or 'No' if they are not.
* **Program Type (Page 2)** (`program_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select 'Yes' if the student is enrolled in this program type or 'No' if they are not.
* **Staff Name (Page 2)** (`staff_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the staff member or administrator responsible for the student's information.
* **Staff Title (Page 2)** (`staff_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title or position of the staff member or administrator.
* **Student Name (Page 3)** (`student_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the student as it appears on their identification card or school records.
* **Student Grade (Page 3)** (`student_grade`, `select_multiple`, required): Select 'Yes' if the student is currently in this grade level or 'No' if they are not.
* **Student DOB (Page 3)** (`student_dob`, `date`, required): Enter the date of birth of the student.
* **Consent (Page 3)** (`consent`, `note`, required): This is a note field for the parent or guardian to sign and date, confirming their consent for the student's safety information.
* **Time Completed (Page 3)** (`time_completed`, `time`, required): Enter the time the form was completed.
* **Date Completed (Page 3)** (`date_completed`, `date`, required): Enter the date the form was completed.
* **Staff Email (Page 3)** (`staff_email`, `email`, optional): Enter a valid email address for the staff member or administrator.
* **Staff Phone (Page 3)** (`staff_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter a valid phone number for the staff member or administrator.
