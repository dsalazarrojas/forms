# Eco Documentary Interview Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Eco Documentary Interview Consent Form is a collection of fields designed to gather information and obtain consent from individuals participating in an eco-documentary project. The form includes demographic and contact information, project details, and consent sections to ensure a smooth and compliant project execution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the demographic information, including project name, location, and date.
2. Provide your contact information, including email and phone number.
3. Select your age group for the consent section.
4. Choose the applicable project terms and conditions.
5. Sign the contact consent section.
6. Review and sign the project terms and conditions.
7. Complete the additional contact and consent sections if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the project you are participating in.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the project, if applicable.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact information, including address if desired.
* **Interview Details** (`interview_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional interview details or notes.
* **Interview Location** (`interview_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the interview, if applicable.
* **Interview Date** (`interview_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the interview.
* **Interview Time** (`interview_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the interview.
* **Interview Duration** (`interview_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the interview.
* **Contact Consent** (`contact_consent`, select_one, optional): Select your age group for contact purposes.
* **Project Terms** (`project_terms`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the applicable project terms and conditions.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Project Terms 2** (`project_terms_2`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the applicable project terms and conditions.
* **Model Release** (`model_release`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the applicable model release forms.
* **Consent 2** (`consent_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select your age group for consent purposes.
* **Contact Consent 2** (`contact_consent_2`, select_one, optional): Select your age group for contact purposes.
* **Contact Terms** (`contact_terms`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the applicable contact terms and conditions.
* **Contact Email 2** (`contact_email_2`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone 2** (`contact_phone_2`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, optional): Select your age group for consent purposes.
* **Contact Terms 2** (`contact_terms_2`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the applicable contact terms and conditions.
* **Project Terms 3** (`project_terms_3`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the applicable project terms and conditions.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are completed accurately and honestly.
* Double-check your selections for consistency and accuracy.
* Review the project terms and conditions carefully before signing.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the project team.
* This form is for your protection and ensures a smooth project execution.
