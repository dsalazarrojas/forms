# high_intensity_fitness_activity_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The high_intensity_fitness_activity_consent_form is a mandatory document that requires participants to provide personal and medical information, as well as consent from a fitness professional, before engaging in high-intensity fitness activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide information about your emergency contact, including their name and relationship to you.
3. Disclose any medical conditions, medications, or allergies.
4. Confirm your medical clearance status.
5. Choose your fitness level.
6. Provide your signature, as well as your fitness professional's signature, and emergency contact's signature (if applicable).
7. Finally, confirm the date of birth and age of the participant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Please enter your name to confirm your identity.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you in case of an emergency.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the relationship between you and your emergency contact (Parent, Spouse or Partner, Other).
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Other** (`emergency_contact_relationship_other`, text, optional): If your emergency contact relationship is "Other", please specify it here.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions, please list them here.
* **Medications** (`medication`, text, optional): If you are taking any medications, please list them here.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, optional): Choose any allergies you may have (Yes, No).
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your consent for high-intensity fitness activities.
* **Fitness Professional Name** (`fitness_professional_name`, text, required): Please enter your fitness professional's name.
* **High-Intensity Risks** (`high_intensity_risks`, text, required): List any high-intensity workout risks or concerns you have.
* **Fitness Level** (`fitness_level`, select_one, required): Choose your fitness level (beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_multiple, optional): Repeat the relationship between you and your emergency contact (Parent, Spouse or Partner, Other).
* **Medical Clearance** (`medical_clearance`, select_one, required): Confirm your medical clearance status (True, False, Doctor's note required).
* **Medical Clearance Note** (`medical_clearance_note`, text, optional): If a doctor's note is required, please provide it here.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Fitness Professional Signature** (`fitness_professional_signature`, text, required): Please sign this form to confirm your consent.
* **Emergency Contact Signature** (`emergency_contact_signature`, text, optional): If an emergency contact is listed, please sign this form to confirm their consent.
* **Medical Provider Signature** (`medical_provider_signature`, text, optional): If a doctor's note is required, please sign this form to confirm their clearance.
* **Participant Date of Birth** (`participant_date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Participant Age** (`participant_age`, number, optional): Enter your age.
* **Participant Height** (`participant_height`, number, optional): Enter your height in inches.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, required): Please sign this form to confirm your consent.
* **Participant Signature 2** (`participant_signature`, text, optional): A redundant field, please disregard.
* **Emergency Contact Phone 2** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): A redundant field, please disregard.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship 2** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_multiple, optional): A redundant field, please disregard.
* **Medical Clearance 2** (`medical_clearance`, select_one, optional): A redundant field, please disregard.
