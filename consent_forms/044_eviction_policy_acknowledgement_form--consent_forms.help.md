# Eviction Policy Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Eviction Policy Acknowledgement Form is used to inform tenants of the policies and terms of their lease agreement. It is essential for tenants to understand these policies to ensure a smooth and trouble-free tenancy.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read each question and its corresponding options.
2. Answer each question based on your understanding of the lease terms and policies.
3. Make sure to answer all questions truthfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant Acknowledgment** (`tenant_acknowledgment`):
	+ This question asks you to confirm whether you have received and acknowledged the tenant policies.
* **Policy Acknowledged** (`policy_acknowledged`):
	+ This question asks you to select whether you have understood and accepted the policies of your lease agreement.
* **Lease Terms** (`lease_terms`):
	+ Select all lease terms that apply to you. This includes any restrictions, conditions, or requirements of your lease.
* **Understand Lease Terms** (`understand_lease_terms`):
	+ Indicate whether you have read, understood, and are willing to comply with the lease terms.
* **Acknowledge Eviction Policy** (`acknowledge_eviction_policy`):
	+ Select whether you have understood and accepted the eviction policy of your lease agreement.
* **Lease Agreement Terms** (`lease_agreement_terms`):
	+ This is a note field for you to add any additional terms or conditions of your lease agreement.
* **Lease Agreement Terms Understood** (`lease_agreement_terms_understood`):
	+ Indicate whether you have read, understood, and are willing to comply with the additional lease terms.

## Tips
* Answer each question truthfully based on your understanding of your lease agreement.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to ask your landlord or property manager.
* Make sure to sign and date the form once you have completed it to show that you have acknowledged the policies and terms of your lease agreement.
