# Personal Information Sharing Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a crucial document that helps us understand how to handle and share personal information, ensuring that individuals' rights are respected and their data is handled with care. The form is designed to be simple and easy to understand, allowing you to provide consent for various aspects of personal information sharing and data collection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill out the "Personal Information Sharing Consent" section to indicate whether you are willing to share your personal information.
* Select your preferred contact method (Email, Phone, or Address) in the "Contact Information" section.
* Choose whether you would like to share your data or not in the "Data Collection" section.
* If you have any additional comments, please provide them in the "Additional Comments" section.
* Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" section.
* If you wish, enter your Time of Birth in the "Time of Birth" section (this is optional).
* Sign the "Signature" section to confirm your agreement.
* Confirm that you have read and understood the consent in the "Confirm Consent" section.
* Confirm that your signature is accurate in the "Confirm Signature" section.
* Click the "Submit" button to finish the form.
* Double-check that the submitted information is accurate in the "Confirm Submit" section.
* In case you need to cancel, click the "Cancel" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Information Sharing Consent** (Page 1): Please select "Yes" to indicate your willingness to share your personal information.
* **Contact Information** (Page 1): Choose your preferred method to contact you (Email, Phone, or Address).
* **Data Collection** (Page 1): Select "Data Sharing" to allow data collection or "No Sharing" to decline.
* **Additional Comments** (Page 1): If you have any further comments or requests, please provide them here.
* **Date of Birth** (Page 1): Enter your date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Birth** (Page 2): If you wish to provide your time of birth, enter it here (optional).
* **Signature** (Page 2): Sign with your name, please.
* **Confirm Consent** (Page 2): Confirm that you have read and understood the consent.
* **Confirm Signature** (Page 2): Confirm that your signature is accurate.
* **Submit** (Page 2): Double-check your information and click to submit.
* **Confirm Submit** (Page 3): Make sure your submission is accurate and complete.
* **Cancel** (Page 3): If you need to cancel, click this button.

## Tips
Please make sure to carefully review your submission before clicking "Submit". If you have any questions or need further guidance, feel free to ask.
