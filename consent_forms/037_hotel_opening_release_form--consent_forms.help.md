# Hotel Opening Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the opening of a hotel, including details about the hotel such as its name, location, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Enter the title of the form in the field "Hotel Opening Release Form", which is automatically pre-filled.
* Select the category for this form, which should be either "Consent Forms" or "Release Forms".
* Provide a brief description of the hotel opening in the text field "Description".
* Enter the name of the hotel in the text field "Hotel Name".
* Select the owner/manager of the hotel from the dropdown menu "Hotel Owner/Manager".
* Enter the date of opening in the field "Date of Opening".
* Enter the time of opening in the field "Time of Opening".
* Enter the location of the hotel in the text field "Location".
* Enter the contact number of the hotel in the text field "Contact Number".
* Enter the email of the hotel in the text field "Email".
* Add any additional notes about the hotel opening in the text field "Notes".
* Enter the form ID, which is automatically generated.
* Enter the form version, which is automatically generated.
* Select the status of the form from the dropdown menu "Form Status".
* Enter the date and time when the form was updated in the fields "Updated At".
* Enter the date and time when the form was created in the fields "Created At".
* Enter the user who updated the form in the text field "Updated By".
* Select whether the form was submitted or not from the dropdown menu "Form Submitted".
* Select the status of the hotel opening from the dropdown menu "Hotel Opening Release Form Status".
* Enter the reason for the form status in the text field "Form Status Reason".
* Add any comments about the hotel opening in the text field "Comments".
* Enter the date and time when the form was submitted in the field "Submitted At".
* Enter the user ID in the text field "User ID".
* Enter the date and time when the form was created in the field "Form Created At".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hotel Opening Release Form (title)**: 
	+ This field is the title of the form, which is automatically pre-filled and cannot be changed.
* **Category (Category)**: 
	+ This field allows you to categorize the form as either "Consent Forms" or "Release Forms".
* **Description (description)**: 
	+ This field is a brief description of the hotel opening.
* **Hotel Name (hotel_name)**: 
	+ This field is where you enter the name of the hotel.
* **Hotel Owner/Manager (hotel_owner_manager)**: 
	+ This field is a dropdown menu where you select the owner/manager of the hotel.
* **Date of Opening (date_of_opening)**: 
	+ This field is where you enter the date of the hotel opening.
* **Time of Opening (time_of_opening)**: 
	+ This field is where you enter the time of the hotel opening.
* **Location (location)**: 
	+ This field is where you enter the location of the hotel.
* **Contact Number (contact_number)**: 
	+ This field is where you enter the contact number of the hotel.
* **Email (email)**: 
	+ This field is where you enter the email of the hotel.
* **Notes (notes)**: 
	+ This field is for any additional notes about the hotel opening.
* **Form ID (form_id)**: 
	+ This field is automatically generated and cannot be changed.
* **Form Version (form_version)**: 
	+ This field is automatically generated and cannot be changed.
* **Form Status (form_status)**: 
	+ This field is where you select the status of the form.
* **Updated At (updated_at)**: 
	+ This field is where you enter the date and time when the form was updated.
* **Created At (created_at)**: 
	+ This field is where you enter the date and time when the form was created.
* **Updated By (updated_by)**: 
	+ This field is where you enter the user who updated the form.
* **Submitted By (submitted_by)**: 
	+ This field is where you enter the user who submitted the form.
* **Form Submitted (form_submitted)**: 
	+ This field is a dropdown menu where you select whether the form was submitted or not.
* **Hotel Opening Release Form Status (hotel_opening_release_form_status)**: 
	+ This field is a dropdown menu where you select the status of the hotel opening.
* **Form Status Reason (form_status_reason)**: 
	+ This field is where you enter the reason for the form status.
* **Comments (comments)**: 
	+ This field is where you add any comments about the hotel opening.
* **Submitted At (submitted_at)**: 
	+ This field is where you enter the date and time when the form was submitted.
* **User ID (user_id)**: 
	+ This field is automatically generated and cannot be changed.
* **Form Created At (form_created_at)**: 
	+ This field is where you enter the date and time when the form was created.

## Tips
Please fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly. Ensure that you understand the purpose and use of each field before submitting the form. If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or manager for guidance.
