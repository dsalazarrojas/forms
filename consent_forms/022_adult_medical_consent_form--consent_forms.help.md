# Adult Medical Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Adult Medical Consent Form is a document that allows an adult to provide informed consent for medical treatment or procedures. It is used to ensure that the individual is fully aware of the risks and benefits associated with the treatment or procedure and has the opportunity to make informed decisions about their care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your full name in the **Full Name** field.
2. Enter your date of birth in the **Date of Birth** field in the format **MM/DD/YYYY**.
3. Provide your contact number in the **Contact Number** field.
4. In the **Description of Medical Treatment or Procedure** field, please briefly describe the treatment or procedure that you are consenting to.
5. Review and acknowledge the **Risks** section by checking the boxes for each risk that applies to you.
6. Select **Informed Consent Agreement** to confirm that you have read and understand the risks and benefits associated with the treatment or procedure.
7. Sign your name in the **Patient Electronic Signature** field. Please type your full legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
8. Enter the date of the signature in the **Signature Date** field in the format **MM/DD/YYYY**.
9. Enter the name of the healthcare provider performing the procedure in the **Healthcare Provider Name** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (required): This field is used to collect your full name as it appears on identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (required): Enter your date of birth in the format **MM/DD/YYYY** (e.g., 02/12/1990).
* **Contact Number** (required): This field is used to collect your contact number for follow-up purposes.
* **Description of Medical Treatment or Procedure** (required): This field is used to describe the medical treatment or procedure that you are consenting to. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Risks** (optional): Check the boxes for each risk that applies to you. This section is used to acknowledge that you have been informed of the potential risks associated with the treatment or procedure.
	+ **I understand the risks and benefits explained to me.** (optional)
	+ **I have had the opportunity to ask questions.** (optional)
	+ **I understand that no results are guaranteed.** (optional)
* **Informed Consent Agreement** (required): Select **I give my full informed consent** to confirm that you have read and understand the risks and benefits associated with the treatment or procedure.
* **Patient Electronic Signature** (required): Please type your full legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Signature Date** (required): Enter the date of the signature in the format **MM/DD/YYYY** (e.g., 02/12/2023).
* **Healthcare Provider Name** (required): Enter the name of the healthcare provider performing the procedure.

Note: Please fill out this form carefully and accurately to ensure that your healthcare provider has the necessary information to provide you with the best possible care. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask your healthcare provider.
