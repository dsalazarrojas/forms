# Smart Waste Bin Monitoring Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from participants in the Smart Waste Bin Monitoring project. It is a consent form that requires participants to provide their contact information, address, and any additional notes for the researcher or participant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Participant Information** section with your details.
2. In the **Address** section, provide your address as it appears on your mail or utility bills.
3. In the **Consent** section, indicate your agreement to participate in the Smart Waste Bin Monitoring project.
4. In the **Participant Details**, **Contact Information**, and **Participant Notes** sections, provide any additional information you feel is relevant or necessary.
5. In the **Researcher Notes** and **Contact Notes** sections, the researcher or project team will use these fields for their own notes, so you can skip these unless you have specific comments or requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information**: Please provide your name, email, and any other identification details as required.
* **Address**: Enter your address as it appears on your mail or utility bills for identification purposes.
* **Consent**: Select "Yes, I agree to participate" to indicate your consent.
* **Participant Details**: Provide any additional information about yourself that you feel is relevant to the project.
* **Contact Information**: Include your contact details such as phone number, social media handles, or any other contact methods you prefer.
* **Participant Notes**: Add any additional comments or requests you have about the project.
* **Researcher Notes**: This is for the researcher or project team to take notes, you can skip this if you have no comments.
* **Contact Notes**: Similarly, this is for the project team to take notes, you can skip this if you have no comments.
