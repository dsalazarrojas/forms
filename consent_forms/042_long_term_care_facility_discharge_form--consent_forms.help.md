# Long Term Care Facility Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Long Term Care Facility Discharge Form is a document used to record the details of a resident's discharge from a long term care facility. It captures essential information about the resident's status and facilitates the transition process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the required details for the resident's discharge.
2. Select the discharge team involved in the process.
3. Provide contact information for the facility and the resident.
4. Add any additional instructions for the next steps.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Discharge Info** (`facility_discharge_info`): A brief description of the discharge, including any relevant details.
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`): The name of the long term care facility where the resident is discharging from.
* **Resident Name** (`resident_name`): The name of the resident being discharged.
* **Discharge Date** (`discharge_date`): The date on which the resident will be discharged.
* **Discharge Time** (`discharge_time`): The time at which the resident will be discharged.
* **Reason for Discharge** (`reason_for_discharge`): A description of the reason for discharge, including any relevant medical information.
* **Next Step Instructions** (`next_step_instructions`): Additional instructions for the next steps following discharge.
* **Discharge Team** (`discharge_team`): Select all applicable teams involved in the discharge process.
* **Facility Phone** (`facility_phone`): The contact phone number for the facility.
* **Facility Email** (`facility_email`): The email address for the facility.
* **Resident Phone** (`resident_phone`): The phone number for the resident.
* **Resident Email** (`resident_email`): The email address for the resident.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled accurately and completely.
* If unsure, consult with the facility administration or the discharge team.
* Review the form before submission to prevent errors.
