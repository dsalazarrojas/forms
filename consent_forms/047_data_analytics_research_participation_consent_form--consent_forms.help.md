# Data Analytics Research Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather participant information and consent for data analytics research participation. It is essential to ensure that all participants understand the purpose and scope of the research, and that they provide informed consent before participating.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and truthful information.
2. Ensure that all fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask the research team for clarification.
4. If you have already provided consent in a previous form, please do not complete this form again.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Participant Name
* Participant Name: The name of the participant, as recorded in your participant database or identification documents.
* Type: Text
* Required: No

### 2. Email
* Email: The email address of the participant, where they can be contacted for research updates.
* Type: Email
* Required: No

### 3. Contact Number
* Contact Number: A telephone number where the participant can be contacted for research purposes.
* Type: Text
* Required: No

### 4. Date of Birth
* Date of Birth: The participant's date of birth, as recorded in their identification documents.
* Type: Date
* Required: No

### 5. Time Zone
* Time Zone: The participant's time zone, where they are located.
* Type: Time
* Required: No

### 6. Consent
* Consent: Select one of the following options:
	+ Option 1: I give my consent to participate in this research.
	+ Option 2: I do not give my consent to participate in this research.
	+ Option 3: I wish to be contacted for more information before making a decision.
* Type: Select One
* Required: No

### 7. Note
* Note: Any additional comments or information about the participant.
* Type: Note
* Required: No

### 8. Assigned to
* Assigned to: The research team or contact person assigned to this participant.
* Type: Text
* Required: No

### 9. Participant Details
* Participant Details: Additional information about the participant, as needed.
* Type: Text
* Required: No

### 10. Research Details
* Research Details: Information about the research project, as needed.
* Type: Text
* Required: No

### 11. Participant Signature
* Participant Signature: A confirmation of the participant's consent.
* Type: Select One
* Required: No

### 12. Research Signature
* Research Signature: A confirmation from the research team.
* Type: Select One
* Required: No

### 13. Research Title
* Research Title: The title of the research project.
* Type: Text
* Required: No

### 14. Researcher's Signature
* Researcher's Signature: A confirmation from the research team.
* Type: Select One
* Required: No

### 15. Research Date
* Research Date: The date of the research project.
* Type: Date
* Required: No

### 16. Participant Signature Date
* Participant Signature Date: The date of the participant's signature.
* Type: Date
* Required: No

### 17. Researcher's Signature Date
* Researcher's Signature Date: The date of the research team's signature.
* Type: Date
* Required: No

### 18. Participant Signature Time
* Participant Signature Time: The time of the participant's signature.
* Type: Time
* Required: No

### 19. Researcher's Signature Time
* Researcher's Signature Time: The time of the research team's signature.
* Type: Time
* Required: No

### 20. Consent Version
* Consent Version: The version of the consent form.
* Type: Text
* Required: No

### 21. Participant Version
* Participant Version: The version of the participant's consent.
* Type: Select One
* Required: No

### 22. Research Version
* Research Version: The version of the research project.
* Type: Select One
* Required: No

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask the research team for clarification.
* Please do not complete this form if you have already provided consent in a previous form.
