# Sports Team Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Team Participation Consent Form is used to obtain consent from a parent/guardian for a child's participation in a sports team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Parental Consent** field with a brief statement of consent for your child's participation in the sports team.
2. Provide your child's **Participant Information**, including their name, in the **Participant Information** field.
3. Enter your contact details in the **Email** and **Phone** fields, so that the coach can reach out to you with any questions or updates.
4. Select your assigned coach from the **Assigned Coach** dropdown menu.
5. If you have any additional information to provide, you can add it in the **Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parental Consent**: A text field where you can provide a brief statement of consent for your child's participation in the sports team.
* **Participant Information**: A field where you can enter your child's name and other relevant information.
* **Coach Information**: A field where the coach can enter their name and contact details.
* **Consent**: A dropdown menu where you can select "Consent" to indicate your agreement to your child's participation in the sports team.
* **Date**: A date field where you can select the date for your child's participation in the sports team.
* **Time**: A time field where you can select the time for your child's participation in the sports team.
* **Notes**: A field where you can add any additional information about your child's participation in the sports team.
* **Email**: A field where you can enter your email address so that the coach can contact you.
* **Phone**: A field where you can enter your phone number so that the coach can contact you.
* **Assigned Coach**: A dropdown menu where you can select your assigned coach.
* **Athlete Information**: A field where you can enter any additional information about your child's participation in the sports team.
