# recycling_program_release_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain participant release of consent for a recycling program.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Participant Information" section with your name, address, and other required information.
2. Select "Yes" for "Release" to indicate that you give your consent to participate in the recycling program.
3. Add any additional comments or information you would like to share about your participation in the program.
4. Enter your email address so we can contact you about program updates and notifications.
5. Enter your phone number so we can contact you about program updates and notifications.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (participant_information):
	+ This section is used to capture participant's basic information.
* **Release** (release_consent):
	+ Select "Yes" to give your consent to participate in the program.
* **Initiatives** (recycling_initiatives):
	+ This section is not mandatory but can be used to provide additional information.
* **Additional Comments** (additional_comments):
	+ This field is for any additional comments or information you would like to share.
* **Email** (participant_email):
	+ Enter your email address so we can contact you about program updates and notifications.
* **Phone** (participant_phone):
	+ Enter your phone number so we can contact you about program updates and notifications.
