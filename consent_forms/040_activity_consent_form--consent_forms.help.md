# Activity Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information and consent from participants before engaging in activities that may involve certain risks or require medical considerations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your participant details, including your name and date of birth.
2. If you have an emergency contact, enter their name, relationship, and phone number.
3. Enter the name of the activity you will be participating in, along with the date it will take place.
4. Answer the questions regarding risks and medical conditions.
5. Indicate your consent to being photographed or recorded during the activity.
6. Sign the liability waiver.
7. Date and sign your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Participant Date of Birth** (`participant_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format of month/day/year (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person you would like us to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Enter the relationship of the emergency contact to you (e.g., parent, guardian, spouse).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number for the emergency contact.
* **Name of Activity** (`activity_name`, text, required): Enter the official name of the activity you will be participating in.
* **Activity Date** (`activity_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the activity will take place, in the format of month/day/year (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Acknowledgment of Risks** (`activity_risks_acknowledged`, select_one, required): Select "Yes, I acknowledge and accept the risks" if you understand and acknowledge the risks associated with this activity.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if you have any medical conditions that might affect your participation.
* **Details of Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions_details`, text, optional): If you selected "Yes" above, please provide details of your medical conditions.
* **Photo and Video Consent** (`photo_video_consent`, select_one, required): Select "Yes, I consent" if you agree to being photographed or recorded during the activity.
* **Liability Waiver** (`liability_waiver`, select_one, required): Select "Yes, I waive liability" if you understand and agree to waive your right to hold the organizers liable for injuries.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your consent and signature.
