# Emergency Medication Client Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Medication Client Agreement form is a mandatory document that outlines the client's understanding and agreement to receive emergency medication. It is essential to complete this form accurately and truthfully to ensure that the client's rights and safety are protected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please enter your first name and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. If applicable, indicate your emergency contact relationship (e.g., spouse, parent, etc.) and enter their name and contact information (phone and email).
4. Select the correct options for medication administration location and reason for administration.
5. Enter the name of the medication, dosage instructions, administration frequency, administration route, and any known allergies or medical history.
6. If necessary, provide information about potential side effects of the medication and emergency medication policy.
7. Confirm your emergency contact permission by entering a brief statement.
8. Finally, date and time-stamp the form with the current date and time, and sign it with your printed name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
  *Example: John
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
  *Example: Doe
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
  *Example: 01/01/1990
- **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, required): Check the box next to 'Yes' if you have an emergency contact, and enter their name and contact information below.
- **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
  *Example: Jane Doe
- **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
  *Example: 123-456-7890
- **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, optional): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
  *Example: jane.doe@example.com
- **Medication Administration Location** (`medication_administration_location`, required): Select the location where you will be receiving the medication.
  *Options: 'Yes', 'No'
- **Reason for Administration** (`reason_for_administration`, required): Explain the reason for medication administration.
  *Example: To manage pain for a medical condition
- **Medication Name** (`medication_name`, required): Enter the name of the medication.
  *Example: Acetaminophen
- **Dosage Instructions** (`dosage_instructions`, required): Enter the dosage instructions for the medication.
  *Example: 500mg, twice a day
- **Administration Frequency** (`administration_frequency`, required): Enter the administration frequency for the medication.
  *Example: every 8 hours
- **Administration Route** (`administration_route`, required): Enter the administration route for the medication.
  *Example: orally
- **Allergies** (`allergies`, optional): If you have allergies, enter them here.
- **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, optional): If you have a medical condition, enter it here.
- **Medical History** (`medical_history`, optional): If you have medical history, enter it here.
- **Medication Side Effects** (`medication_side_effects`, optional): If you experience side effects, enter them here.
- **Emergency Medication Policy** (`emergency_medication_policy`, optional): If you have a policy for emergency medication, enter it here.
- **Emergency Contact Permission** (`emergency_contact_permission`, required): Confirm your emergency contact permission by entering a brief statement.
  *Example: I grant permission for [Name] to contact me in case of an emergency.
- **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, required): Date and time-stamp the form with the current date.
  *Example: 01/01/2023 12:00 PM
- **Signature Time** (`signature_time`, required): Date and time-stamp the form with the current time.
  *Example: 12:00 PM
- **Signature Name** (`signature_name`, required): Sign the form with your printed name.
  *Example: John Doe
- **Printed Name** (`printed_name`, required): Enter your printed name as it appears on your identification.
  *Example: John Doe
- **Printed Date** (`printed_date`, required): Date the form with the current date.
  *Example: 01/01/2023
- **Printed Time** (`printed_time`, required): Time-stamp the form with the current time.
  *Example: 12:00 PM
