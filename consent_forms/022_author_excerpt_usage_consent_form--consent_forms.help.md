# Author Excerpt Usage Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain written consent from authors for the use of their excerpts in various projects or publications. This ensures that authors are informed about the intended use of their work and provides a clear understanding of the terms and conditions of the use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2.  Ensure that the consent date is accurate and up-to-date.
3.  Provide a detailed description of the intended use and how it will be distributed.
4.  Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the use.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consent Date**: The date on which this consent is granted.
* **Author Name**: The full name of the copyright holder.
* **Author Email**: The email contact of the copyright holder.
* **Author Phone**: Optional contact phone number of the copyright holder.
* **Original Work Title**: The title of the book, article, or other original work being referenced.
* **Original Publication Date**: The date the original work was published.
* **Type of Original Work**: The type of original work (e.g., book, article, blog post, research paper).
* **Excerpt Details to Be Used**: A description of the portions of the original work to be used.
* **Approximate Length of Excerpts**: The length of the excerpts to be used.
* **Requestor Name**: The name of the person or entity requesting permission to use the excerpt.
* **Requestor Email**: The email contact of the person or entity requesting permission.
* **Requestor Organization**: The organization or company requesting permission.
* **Intended Use of Excerpt**: The specific use of the excerpt (e.g., educational material, marketing/promotional material).
* **Detailed Description of Use**: A detailed description of how the excerpt will be used.
* **How Will It Be Distributed**: The method of distribution (e.g., print, digital, social media).
* **Geographic Territory**: The geographic area where the excerpt will be distributed.
* **Duration of Use Permission**: The length of time for which permission is granted.
* **Credit/Attribution Required**: Whether attribution or credit is required.
* **Exclusive vs Non-Exclusive Use**: Whether this permission is exclusive or non-exclusive.
* **Compensation or Royalties**: Whether payment is involved.
* **Agreed Compensation Amount**: The amount of compensation agreed upon (if applicable).
* **Author Approval**: The author's approval status for the use.
* **Conditions or Requirements**: Any special conditions or requirements for the use.
* **Legal Authority to Consent**: The authority to grant permission (e.g., owner of copyright, licensed to grant permission).

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Review and agree to the terms and conditions before submitting the form.
* If unsure about any of the fields or terms, please contact the relevant contact person or entity.
* This form is a crucial document that ensures the use of your excerpt is lawful and respectful.
