# Telehealth Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient information and obtain consent for telehealth services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have filled in all required fields.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for each question that applies to you.
3. Provide your signature in the designated area.
4. Complete all fields marked as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient information (1)**: Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your current status.
	+ If "Yes", please enter your name.
* **consent date (2)**: Enter the date you are granting consent for telehealth services.
* **patient name (3)**: Enter your full name.
* **doctor name (4)**: Enter your doctor's name.
* **signature date (5)**: Enter the date of your signature.
* **electronic signature (6)**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you are granting consent electronically.
* **signed (7)**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you are signing this form electronically.
* **consented (8)**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you are granting consent for telehealth services.
* **patient consent (9)**: Enter a brief statement indicating your understanding of telehealth services.
* **consented by (10)**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate who consented to telehealth services.
* **consented date (11)**: Enter the date you consented to telehealth services.
* **consented time (12)**: Enter the time you consented to telehealth services.
* **signature type (13)**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you signed this form electronically.
* **ipa (14)**: Enter your IPA (Indian Physician's Association) number if applicable.
* **electronic signature image (15)**: Enter the URL or file location of your electronic signature.
* **signed at (16)**: Enter the date and time you signed this form.
* **signed by (17)**: Enter the name of the person who signed this form.
* **ipa signature (18)**: Enter your IPA signature.
* **signature image (19)**: Enter the URL or file location of your signature image.
* **consented time (20)**: Enter the time you consented to telehealth services.
* **consented by time (21)**: Enter the time you consented to telehealth services.
* **ipa signature image (22)**: Enter the URL or file location of your IPA signature image.
* **electronic ipa (23)**: Enter your electronic IPA number if applicable.
* **electronic ipa signature (24)**: Enter your electronic IPA signature.
* **signed time (25)**: Enter the time you signed this form.

Please note that some fields may not be required, and it is essential to review the form carefully before completing it.
