# digital_identification_card_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is to help users understand how to fill out the digital identification card consent form accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field. This field requires your full name as it will be used to identify you.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This is your contact information.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. You may be asked to provide up to 3 photos for verification purposes. These are optional fields, "Photo 1," "Photo 2," and "Photo 3."
5. Select your consent option in the "Consent" field. You have two options: "I give my consent" or "Do not give my consent."
6. Provide any additional details you feel are necessary in the "Details" field. This is optional.
7. Review and acknowledge that the submission will be sent to an authority in the "Submission" field.
8. Finally, sign and date your consent in the "Signature" field.
9. Confirm the timestamp of submission in the "Timestamp" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Photo 1**: Provide an optional photo for identification.
* **Photo 2**: Provide an optional photo for identification.
* **Photo 3**: Provide an optional photo for identification.
* **Consent**: Select "I give my consent" or "Do not give my consent."
* **Details**: Provide any additional details you feel are necessary.
* **Submission**: Review and acknowledge that the submission will be sent to an authority.
* **Signature**: Sign and date your consent.
* **Timestamp**: Provide the date and time of your submission.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately.
- Be honest in your consent and provide clear details if necessary.
- Review and confirm your submission before signing and submitting. 

Please note that the form fields and labels might vary based on the specific requirements and regulations of the authority you are submitting to. Be sure to review and understand the requirements before filling out this form.
