# Camp Leader Policy Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camp Leader Policy Acknowledgement Form is a crucial document that ensures all staff members understand and agree to the essential policies and procedures that govern their role as camp leaders. This form serves as a confirmation that staff members are aware of their responsibilities and obligations, and that they are committed to upholding the safety, confidentiality, and well-being of campers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and provide accurate and truthful information.
2. Make sure to understand each policy and procedure before agreeing to it.
3. Complete all required fields, including your full name and the date of signature.
4. Get your supervisor to review and witness your completed form.
5. Sign the "Final Acknowledgment" section to confirm your understanding and agreement to all the policies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Camp Leader Policy Acknowledgement --**: This section serves as an introduction to the form and explains its purpose.
* **Staff Member Full Name**: Please provide your full name as a staff member.
* **Position - Role at Camp**: Indicate your position or role at the camp.
* **Assigned Camp Session(s)**: Select the sessions you are assigned to.
* **-- Policy Sections and Agreements --**: This section serves as a header for the policy sections that follow.
* **Child Safety and Protection Policy**: Please select "I Have Read and Agree" to confirm your understanding and commitment to upholding all safety protocols and reporting requirements.
* **Staff Code of Conduct**: Select "I Have Read and Agree" to confirm your commitment to maintaining professional standards and behavior.
* **Social Media and Communication Policy**: Select "I Have Read and Agree" to understand the restrictions on communicating with campers online.
* **Workplace Health and Safety Policy**: Select "I Have Read and Agree" to follow all safety guidelines for activities and facilities.
* **Emergency Response Procedures**: Select "I Have Read and Agree" to review the emergency manual and protocols.
* **Confidentiality and Privacy Policy**: Select "I Have Read and Agree" to protect the private information of campers and families.
* **Staff Electronic Signature**: Please type your full name as a staff member.
* **Date of Signature**: Please enter the date of signature.
* **Supervisor - Witness Name**: Provide the name of the person who reviewed this form with you.
* **Final Acknowledgment**: Select "I Understand and Acknowledge" to confirm your understanding and agreement to all the policies and procedures.

## Tips

* Please complete this form thoroughly and accurately to avoid any misinterpretation or oversights.
* Make sure to review each section carefully before agreeing to it.
* Get your supervisor to review and witness your completed form before signing.
* This form is a crucial document that ensures your commitment to upholding the policies and procedures that govern your role as a camp leader.
