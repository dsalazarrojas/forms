# Lifemark Informed Consent Telehealth Treatment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lifemark Informed Consent Telehealth Treatment form is used to gather essential patient information and ensure informed consent for telehealth treatment. This form is designed to be completed by patients during their telehealth sessions, and it helps to document their identity, contact details, and consent to treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and date of birth to identify yourself as the patient.
2. Provide your address to ensure we can contact you if needed.
3. Select your emergency contact, which can be a family member, friend, or other.
4. Enter your contact information if you wish to be contacted by us.
5. Specify your healthcare provider and any relevant medical information.
6. Upload your patient photo ID (e.g., driver's license or passport) to confirm your identity.
7. Sign to confirm you understand the terms of your treatment.
8. Sign again to confirm the witness has witnessed your signature.
9. Date and time of signing should match your actions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address where you can be contacted, including house number and street name.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, select_one, required): Select one of the options:
	+ Family Member
	+ Friend
	+ Other (Please specify)
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact information if you wish to be contacted by us. This can be a phone number, email, or mailing address.
* **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`, text, required): Enter the name and title of your healthcare provider.
* **Patient Photo ID** (`patient_photo_id`, text, required): Upload a clear scan or photo of your identification document (e.g., driver's license or passport) to confirm your identity.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, select_multiple, required): Sign to confirm you understand the terms of your treatment.
* **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`, select_multiple, required): Sign to confirm you have witnessed the patient's signature.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required): Enter the date you signed this form.
* **Time Signed** (`time_signed`, time, required): Enter the time you signed this form.
