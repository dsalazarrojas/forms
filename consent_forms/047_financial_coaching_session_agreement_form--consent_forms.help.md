# financial_coaching_session_agreement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients participating in a financial coaching session. It helps ensure that both the client and the coach are on the same page and understand the terms of the agreement before starting the session.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the **Client Details** section with your basic information, including name, contact details, and other relevant data.
2.  Move on to the **Session Details** section and provide information about the session, such as date, time, and any additional details.
3.  In the **Financial Coaching Session Agreement** section, read through the terms and agree to them.
4.  In the **Client Confirmation** section, confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms.
5.  Enter the date you agree to the terms in the **Client Agreement Date** field.
6.  If applicable, provide any additional coaching terms in the **Coaching Terms** text area.
7.  If applicable, enter the goals of the session in the **Session Goals** text field.
8.  If applicable, document any session notes in the **Session Notes** text field.
9.  If applicable, document the outcomes of the session in the **Session Outcomes** text field.
10. If applicable, evaluate the session in the **Session Evaluation** text field.
11. Finally, provide any next session details in the **Next Session Details** text field, if applicable.
