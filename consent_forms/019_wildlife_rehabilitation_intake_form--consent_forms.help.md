# Wildlife Rehabilitation Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wildlife Rehabilitation Intake Form is used to collect information about a patient being brought to the wildlife rehabilitation center for treatment. This form is essential for providing appropriate care and making informed decisions about the patient's treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you provide the correct information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (page 1)** (`patient_information`, `text`, optional): This field is used to provide any relevant background information about the patient, such as its species, size, and age.
* **Species (page 1)** (`species`, `select_one`, optional): Please select the correct species of the patient from the options provided:
	+ Mammal
	+ Bird
	+ Reptile
* **Injury Description (page 1)** (`injury_description`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe the injury or condition of the patient.
* **Treatment Goal (page 1)** (`treatment_goal`, `select_one`, optional): Please select the primary treatment goal for the patient from the options provided:
	+ Rehabilitation
	+ Release
	+ Other (please describe in the space provided)
* **Release Plan (page 1)** (`release_plan`, `text`, optional): If applicable, provide a brief description of any release plan or next steps for the patient after treatment.
* **Veterinary Services (page 1)** (`veterinary_services`, `text`, optional): If applicable, provide any relevant veterinary services or treatments the patient has received or requires.
* **Medical History (page 1)** (`medical_history`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please indicate the patient's medical history:
	+ True (has a known medical history)
	+ False (no known medical history)
	+ Unknown (unknown medical history)
* **Contact Information (page 1)** (`contact_information`, `text`, optional): Provide any relevant contact information for the patient's care, such as a contact phone number or address.
* **Contact Person (page 1)** (`contact_person`, `text`, optional): If applicable, provide the name and contact information for the person bringing the patient to the center.
* **Contact EMail (page 1)** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): If you have an email address, provide it for further communication about the patient's care.

## Tips
- Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form to ensure the best possible care for the patient.
- If unsure about any field, contact the wildlife rehabilitation center for guidance.
- Please note that some fields may not be required, but providing as much information as possible will help the center provide the best care for the patient.
