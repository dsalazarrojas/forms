# Online Medical Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Online Medical Consent Form is designed to gather essential information from patients to ensure they receive the best possible care from healthcare providers. This form will be used to collect demographic details, medical data, and consent for treatment, which are all vital components for the healthcare provider to provide quality care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, paying attention to the fields marked as "required."
2. Enter your information in the fields provided, using the format specified for each field (e.g., date format for dates).
3. Review the fields carefully and ensure you understand what is being asked.
4. Sign the form digitally using the E-Signature field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (Page 1)**:
  * **Patient Information (`patient_information`, text, required/optional)**: Enter your demographic details such as name, address, phone number, and date of birth.
* **Parent/Guardian Contact (Page 1)**:
  * **Parent/Guardian Contact (`parent_guardian_contact`, text, required/optional)**: Enter the name and contact information of your parent or guardian.
* **Medical Data (Page 1)**:
  * **Medical Data (`medical_data`, text, required/optional)**: Describe your medical history, including any current or previous medical conditions, allergies, or medications.
* **Consent Waiver (Page 1)**:
  * **Consent Waiver (`consent_waiver`, select_multiple, required/optional)**: Choose one of the following:
    + Yes, I grant consent to the terms and conditions outlined in this form.
    + No, I do not grant consent.
* **E-Signature (Page 1)**:
  * **E-Signature (`e_signature`, text, required/optional)**: Sign the form digitally using the provided field.
* **Patient Signature (Page 1)**:
  * **Patient Signature (`patient_signature`, text, required/optional)**: Sign the form digitally using the provided field.
* **Emergency Contact (Page 1)**:
  * **Emergency Contact (`emergency_contact`, text, required/optional)**: Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Name (Page 1)**:
  * **Emergency Contact Name (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required/optional)**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (Page 1)**:
  * **Emergency Contact Phone (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required/optional)**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have completed all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* Make sure to sign the form digitally to authenticate your identity.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the healthcare provider for assistance.
