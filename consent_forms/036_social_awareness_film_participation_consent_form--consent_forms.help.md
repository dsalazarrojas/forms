# Social Awareness Film Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants and filmmakers in Social Awareness Films, ensuring they understand how their data will be used. The form requires consent to release information for public use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant information, including name, email, and location.
2. Select the purpose of your participation (Education, Advocacy, Non-Profit, or Film).
3. Provide your email address and phone number (work or personal).
4. Enter the date and time of your participation.
5. Add any additional notes.
6. Choose whether you consent to release your information for public use (Yes or No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information (participant_info)** (participant_info, text, required/optional): Provide your name, as the participant.
* **Social Awareness Film (social_awareness_film)** (social_awareness_film, text, required/optional): Briefly describe the Social Awareness Film in which you are participating.
* **Filmmaker (filmmaker_info)** (filmmaker_info, text, required/optional): Provide information about the filmmaker(s) involved in the Social Awareness Film.
* **Purpose (purpose)** (Purpose, select_one, required): Select the reason why you are participating in the Social Awareness Film. This includes:
	+ Education
	+ Advocacy
	+ Non-Profit
	+ Film
* **Filmmaker (filmmaker_credit)** (filmmaker_credit, text, required/optional): If you are a filmmaker, provide a brief description of your role in the Social Awareness Film.
* **Filmmaker Info 2 (filmmaker_info_2)** (filmaker_info_2, text, required/optional): If you are a filmmaker, provide more information about your role in the Social Awareness Film.
* **Email (email)** (email, email, required/optional): Enter your email address (work or personal).
* **Phone Number (phone)** (phone, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number (work or personal).
* **Location (location)** (location, text, required/optional): Enter your location (City, State, Country).
* **Date of Participation (date_of_participation)** (date_of_participation, date, required/optional): Enter the date of your participation in the Social Awareness Film.
* **Time of Participation (time_of_participation)** (time_of_participation, time, required/optional): Enter the time of your participation in the Social Awareness Film.
* **Notes (notes)** (notes, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Consent to Release (consent_to_release)** (consent_to_release, select_one, required): Choose whether you consent to release your information for public use. This includes:
	+ Yes
	+ No

## Tips

* Please ensure that you are providing accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you are a filmmaker, please provide accurate contact information for yourself.
* If you are not a filmmaker, please provide accurate contact information for the filmmaker(s).
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the form administrators (form@gic.mx).
