# Zipline Activity Liability Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Zipline Activity Liability Waiver Form is a crucial document that aims to ensure participants are aware of and understand the potential risks and liabilities associated with specific activities. The form helps to inform participants about the activity rules and guidelines and obtain their consent before participating.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your personal details in the Participant Details section.
2. Enter your name, email, and phone number accurately.
3. Select the type of activity you will be participating in (Activity or Tour).
4. Check if you have read and accepted the waiver terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Details
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, not required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, not required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, not required): Enter your phone number.

### Activity Information
* **Activity Type** (`activity_type`, `select_one`, not required): Select the type of activity you will be participating in (Activity or Tour).
* **Waiver Version** (`waiver_version`, `select_one`, not required): Select the version of the waiver you are accepting (V1 or V2).

### Waiver Acceptance
* **Waiver Acceptance** (`waiver_acceptance`, `select_one`, not required): Check if you have read and accepted the waiver terms. If you decline, it may lead to disqualification from the activity.

### Tips
* Double-check your details before submitting.
* Make sure to select the correct activity and waiver version.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, ask your activity organizer or instructor for guidance.
* Please review the waiver carefully before accepting or declining it.
