# Veterinary Product Evaluation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about the owner and product being evaluated in a veterinary setting. The form gathers data to facilitate evaluation and potential improvement of the veterinary product.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form in its entirety if possible.
- You can skip fields if you don't have the required information.
- Review your responses carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Owner Information
* **Owner Information** (`owner_info`, text, optional): Please provide your name and contact information so we can reach out to you for any follow-up questions or to confirm your consent.

### Product Information
* **Product Information** (`product_info`, text, optional): Describe the veterinary product you are evaluating (e.g., food, toy, bedding, etc.).

### Consent
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate your level of consent to the evaluation process by selecting one or more of the following options:
	+ Yes: You agree to participate in the evaluation.
	+ No: You decline to participate in the evaluation.
	+ Maybe: You are unsure or need more information about the evaluation process.

### Additional Information
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Provide any extra details or comments about the product or your experience with it.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer honestly and accurately.
- If you're unsure about any field, leave it blank or reach out for clarification.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
