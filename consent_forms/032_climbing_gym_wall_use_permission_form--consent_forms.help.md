# Climbing Gym Wall Use Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to obtain written consent and gather necessary information from participants prior to their climbing activity at the gym. It is essential that participants provide accurate and truthful answers to ensure their safety and well-being while participating in climbing activities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the participant information section with your details.
2. Provide accurate contact information for your emergency contact.
3. Answer questions related to your health and medical conditions.
4. Answer questions related to recent injuries or ongoing treatments.
5. Acknowledge the risks involved in climbing activities and agree to follow safety rules.
6. Confirm that you have inspected and will properly use all safety equipment.
7. Release the facility from liability for injuries sustained.
8. If you are a minor, provide written consent from a parent or guardian.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (2, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Age** (3, `number`, required): Enter your age.
* **Date of Birth** (4, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Email Address** (5, `email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (6, `text`, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (8, `text`, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (9, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship to Participant** (10, `text`, required): Describe your relationship to the participant.
* **Any medical conditions or allergies?** (12, `text`, required): List any medical conditions or allergies you have.
* **Currently taking medications?** (13, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' if you are currently taking any medications.
* If you selected 'Yes', enter a description of the medications you are taking: **If yes, please describe** (14, `text`, required): Provide a description of your medications.
* **Any restrictions on physical activity?** (15, `text`, required): List any restrictions on your physical activity.
* **Any recent injuries or ongoing treatments?** (17, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' if you have recent injuries or ongoing treatments.
* If you selected 'Yes', describe the injury: **If yes, describe the injury** (18, `text`, required): Provide a description of your injury.
* **I understand the risks involved in climbing activities** (20, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' to acknowledge the risks involved.
* **I will follow all facility safety rules and instructions** (21, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' to confirm you will follow safety rules.
* **I will inspect and properly use all safety equipment** (22, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' to confirm you will properly use safety equipment.
* **I release the facility from liability for injuries sustained** (23, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' to release the facility from liability.
* **Parent/Guardian consent (for minors)** (24, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' if you are a minor and a parent or guardian has provided written consent, or 'Not applicable' if you are an adult.
