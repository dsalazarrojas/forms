# MVR Release Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from employees about their consent for the release of their Motor Vehicle Report (MVR) data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the purpose and instructions on this page carefully.
2. Answer the questions as accurately and truthfully as possible.
3. Select the correct response for each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, text, required: false): Please enter the name of your current or former employer.
* **Job Title** (`employer_title`, text, required: false): Please enter your job title or position at the time of your employment with the above employer.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required: false): Please enter your employee ID or social security number.
* **MVR Consent** (`mvr_consent`, select_one, required: false): Please select your response to the release of your MVR data.
	+ Select "Yes" if you consent to the release of your MVR data.
	+ Select "No" if you do not consent to the release of your MVR data.
* **MVR Consent Date** (`mvr_consent_date`, date, required: false): Please enter the date you granted or denied consent for the release of your MVR data.
* **Job Requirements** (`job_requirements`, text, required: false): Please describe your job requirements or duties at the time of your employment with the above employer.
* **MVR Release Consent** (`mvr_release_consent`, text, required: false): This field is not actually part of the form.
