# historical_photo_archive_submission - Help Guide

## Purpose
The historical_photo_archive_submission form is used to collect and store historical photos and their related metadata. This form helps to create a comprehensive and organized archive of historical photos, including details about the photo, contributor, and submission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the historical_photo_archive_submission form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields: **Submission Details**, **Submission Type**, and **Photo License**.
2. Provide additional details about the photo, such as **Photo Year** and **Photo Description**.
3. Choose the **Submission Date** and **Submission Date Time**.
4. Add any relevant **Photo Tags** and **Photo Tags 2**.
5. Upload any relevant files, such as **File Upload 1**, **File Upload 2**, **File Upload 3**, and **File Upload 4**.
6. Enter the contributor's details, including **Contributor Address**, **Contributor Email**, and **Contributor Name**.
7. Optionally, add a **File URL** and **File URL 2**.
