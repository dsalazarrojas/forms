## Surgical Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to capture patient information and their consent for a surgical procedure.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  The patient's full name and date of birth should be provided in the respective fields.
2.  The contact number and emergency contact name should be filled in if applicable.
3.  Select the relation to the emergency contact, if applicable.
4.  Provide any existing medical conditions and the date they were diagnosed.
5.  Enter the name of the procedure to be performed.
6.  Enter the date of the procedure.
7.  Enter the date of signing the form.
8.  Enter the name of the surgeon performing the procedure.
9.  Sign the form with the patient's signature.
10.  Sign the form with the guardian's signature, if applicable.
11.  Sign the form with the surgeon's signature.
12.  Acknowledge that you have read and understood the information provided.
13.  Select whether you are authorizing the procedure.
14.  Date and sign the acknowledgment of your authorization.
15.  If you have any additional medical conditions, provide a brief description.
16.  Date and sign the waiver, if applicable.
17.  Provide additional details about the medical condition in the "other_medical_conditions" field, if applicable.
18.  Date any additional details about the medical condition in the "other_medical_conditions_date" field.
19.  Enter any additional informed consent text.
20.  Date and sign the informed consent.
21.  If any of the provided options in the "other_medical_conditions" field do not apply, provide an alternative medical condition and date.
22.  Enter any additional details about the alternative medical condition in the "other_medical_conditions" field.
23.  Date any additional details about the alternative medical condition in the "other_medical_conditions_date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   *full_name* (full_name, text, optional): Enter your full name.
*   *date_of_birth* (date_of_birth, date, optional): Enter your date of birth.
*   *contact_number* (contact_number, text, optional): Enter your contact number.
*   *emergency_contact_name* (emergency_contact_name, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact name.
*   *relation* (relation, select_one, optional): Select your relation to the emergency contact (Parent, Spouse, Sibling, Friend, Relative, Doctor, Other).
*   *medical_history* (medical_history, note, optional): Enter any medical history you have.
*   *medical_conditions* (medical_conditions, select_multiple, optional): Select any medical conditions (Diabetes, Hypertension, Asthma, Pregnancy, Other).
*   *procedure* (procedure, text, optional): Enter the name of the procedure.
*   *procedure_date* (procedure_date, date, optional): Enter the date of the procedure.
*   *signature_date* (signature_date, date, optional): Enter the date of signing the form.
*   *surgeon_name* (surgeon_name, text, optional): Enter the name of the surgeon performing the procedure.
*   *patient_signature* (patient_signature, text, optional): Sign the form with the patient's signature.
*   *guardian_signature* (guardian_signature, text, optional): Sign the form with the guardian's signature, if applicable.
*   *surgeon_signature* (surgeon_signature, text, optional): Sign the form with the surgeon's signature.
*   *patient_acknowledge* (patient_acknowledge, text, optional): Acknowledge that you have read and understood the information provided.
*   *patient_authorize* (patient_authorize, select_one, optional): Select whether you are authorizing the procedure (Yes, No).
*   *acknowledge_date* (acknowledge_date, date, optional): Date and sign the acknowledgment of your authorization.
*   *patient_waiver* (patient_waiver, text, optional): Enter a waiver, if applicable.
*   *waiver_date* (waiver_date, date, optional): Date the waiver.
*   *waiver_text* (waiver_text, text, optional): Enter any text about the waiver.
*   *other_medical_conditions* (other_medical_conditions, text, optional): Enter any additional medical condition you have.
*   *other_medical_conditions_date* (other_medical_conditions_date, date, optional): Date any additional medical condition.
*   *medical_condition_other_details* (medical_condition_other_details, text, optional): Enter any additional details about the medical condition.
*   *medical_condition_other_details_date* (medical_condition_other_details_date, date, optional): Date any additional details about the medical condition.
*   *patient_informed_consent* (patient_informed_consent, text, optional): Enter any informed consent text.
