# Veterinary Practice Audit Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for veterinary practices to participate in an audit, which is an evaluation process aimed at improving the standards and services offered by veterinary practices. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review each section carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
2. Fill out each section accurately and completely.
3. Make sure to sign and date the form.

* **Section 1**: Please indicate your practice's name, address, and contact information to be contacted if further details are needed.
	+ Required: Yes
	+ Type: text
* **Section 2**: Provide a brief introduction to your practice, including its type of practice and specialties offered.
	+ Required: No
	+ Type: text
* **Section 3**: Outline the services your practice provides, including veterinary care and other services.
	+ Required: No
	+ Type: text

	+ Type: text
