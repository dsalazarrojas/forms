# Pet Ownership Guidelines Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about pet ownership guidelines, including pet type, care expectations, and owner contact details. It's intended to ensure that pet owners understand their responsibilities and obligations regarding the care and well-being of their pets.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and pet name in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the species of your pet from the options provided.
3. Provide your contact information, including email and phone number.
4. Sign and date the form to acknowledge that you have read and understood the pet ownership guidelines.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Ownership Guidelines Agreement** (`pet_ownership_guidelines_agreement`, text, required): This is a brief description of the pet ownership guidelines that you agree to follow.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Please enter your name as the owner of the pet.
* **Name of Pet** (`name_of_pets`, text, required): Enter the name of your pet.
* **Species of Pet** (`species_of_pets`, select_one, required): Select the species of your pet from the options provided (Cat, Dog, Other).
* **Age of Pet** (`age_of_pets`, text, optional): If you wish to provide additional information about your pet's age.
* **Responsible Care Expectations** (`responsible_care_expectations`, text, optional): If you have specific care requirements for your pet, please provide details here.
* **Signed By** (`signed_by`, text, optional): Enter your name as the owner of the pet.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, optional): Date and sign this form to acknowledge that you have read and understood the pet ownership guidelines.
* **Time Signed** (`time_signed`, time, optional): Enter the time of day you signed the form.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or information about your pet.
* **Acknowledgment** (`acknowledgment`, text, optional): Enter your acknowledgment of the pet ownership guidelines.
* **Certification** (`certification`, text, optional): If you are certifying an organization or service, enter their name and details.
* **Certifying Organization** (`certifying_organization`, text, optional): Enter the name of the certifying organization or service.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person for the certifying organization or service.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Certifying Date** (`certifying_date`, date, optional): Date of certification or agreement.
* **Certifying Time** (`certifying_time`, time, optional): Time of certification or agreement.
* **Certifying Organization Signature** (`certifying_organization_signature`, text, optional): Enter the signature of the certifying organization or service.
* **Certifying Date Signature** (`certifying_date_signature`, text, optional): Date of signature of the certifying organization or service.
* **Certifying Organization Holding** (`certifying_organization_holding`, text, optional): Enter the certifying organization or service holding information.
* **Certifying Time Signature** (`certifying_time_signature`, text, optional): Time of signature of the certifying organization or service.
* **Certifying Phone** (`certifying_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the certifying organization or service.

Please note that while this form has several fields, many of them appear to be optional, and you should review the specific requirements of your use case to ensure that you're capturing the necessary information.
