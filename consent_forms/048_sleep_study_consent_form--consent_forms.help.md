# Sleep Study Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect consent from patients participating in a sleep study. It is essential to understand the study's nature, risks, and benefits before completing this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each section carefully and answer all questions accurately.
2. Provide clear and concise information in each field.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Patient Information (Patient Information)
* **Patient Information** (`Patient Information`, text, required/optional): This section collects basic information about the patient, including their name and contact details. Please provide your name and any other relevant contact information.

### 2. Sleep Study Details (Sleep Study Details)
* **Sleep Study Details** (`Sleep Study Details`, text, required/optional): This section collects information about the sleep study, including its nature, duration, and any other relevant details. Please provide a clear and concise description of the study.

### 3. Contact Information (Contact Information)
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, text, required/optional): This section collects information about the contact person or organization responsible for the study. Please provide the name and contact details of the person or organization.

### 4. Consent Information (Consent Information)
* **Consent Information** (`Consent Information`, text, required/optional): This section provides information about the consent process and the patient's rights and obligations. Please read this section carefully and ask questions if you have any doubts.

## Tips
