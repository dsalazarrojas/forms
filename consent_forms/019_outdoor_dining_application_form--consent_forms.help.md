# Outdoor Dining Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals to submit an application for outdoor dining requests, providing necessary information about themselves and their dining preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your basic information by filling in the **User Information** section.
2. Share your dining preferences and requests in the **Dining Information** section.
3. Include any relevant details about the host or location for outdoor dining, such as their name and contact information in the **Host Information** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **User Information (Text, Optional)** 
* This section is for you to provide your basic details. Please fill in your name and any other relevant personal information you wish to share.

### **Dining Information (Text, Optional)**
* In this section, please describe your outdoor dining preferences, such as the type of food, location, or activities you would like to enjoy while dining outdoors.

### **Host Information (Text, Optional)**
* If you are inviting others to dine with you, provide the host's name and contact information so that they can be reached for coordination purposes.
