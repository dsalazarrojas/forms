# Household Camera Usage Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information about individuals within a household who wish to use a camera or camera system for various purposes. This includes details about their name, contact information, and preferences for camera usage. The form is intended to help us understand and manage camera usage within the household for administrative and regulatory purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please review the purpose of this form and ensure you understand what information is being collected.
2. Complete each field with accurate and complete information.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please do not hesitate to contact our support team for assistance.

* **Household Camera Usage Consent Form Page 1** (id: 1, type: number, required: false): This field is intended for you to enter a page number or some other unique identifier for your entry. Please enter your page number or a relevant number if applicable.

* **Shared Spaces** (id: 3, type: select_multiple, required: false): This field asks you to select multiple shared spaces where cameras are installed. Please choose all the relevant shared spaces where cameras are used.
