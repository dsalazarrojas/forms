# Guest Speaker Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guest Speaker Intake Form is used to gather information from potential guest speakers for events. This includes details such as contract sign date, speaker's contact information, and any other relevant notes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in all required fields**: Each page has a "required" status marked next to the field label. Please ensure you fill in all required fields.
2. **Provide accurate and complete information**: Double-check your input for accuracy and completeness.
3. **Submit the form**: Once you have completed all the required fields, click the submit button to finalize your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Guest Speaker Information (Page 1)
* **Guest Speaker Information**: This field is used to collect basic information about the guest speaker. 
*   **Label:** Guest Speaker Information
*   **Type:** text
*   **Required:** false
*   Enter the speaker's name and any additional details that may be relevant.

### Contract Sign Date (Page 2)
*   **Contract Sign Date**: This field is used to note the date the speaker agrees to the contract terms. 
*   **Type:** date
*   **Required:** false

### Contact Information (Page 3)
*   **Contact Information**: This field is used to gather speaker's contact details.
*   **Label:** Contact Information
*   **Type:** text
*   **Required:** false

### Contract Signature (Page 4)
*   **Contract Signature**: This field is a space for speaker to sign the contract.
*   **Label:** Contract Signature
*   **Type:** note
*   **Required:** false

## Tips

*   Please ensure you fill out the form accurately and completely to avoid any confusion or misunderstandings.
*   If you are unsure about any field or have additional information to provide, do not hesitate to ask event staff for clarification.
*   This form will be used to facilitate communication and coordination during the event. Please provide all necessary information to ensure a smooth experience.
