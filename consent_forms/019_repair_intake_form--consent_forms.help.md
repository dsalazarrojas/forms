# Repair Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect essential information for repairing and servicing customer equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the following steps:

1. Review the form to understand the required fields.
2. Select the correct options for the "Repair Consoles" section.
3. Fill out the required fields, which are marked as "required".
4. Enter the date and time of the repair in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
5. Enter any additional comments or notes about the repair in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Repair Consoles** (`repair_consoles_input`, select_multiple, required: false): This field allows you to select whether the equipment has a repair console. Select all that apply.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person submitting the repair form.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the person submitting the repair form.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the repair form.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes about the repair.
* **Repair Details** (`repair_consoles`, text, required: false): Enter any other details about the repair.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the repair.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the repair.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required: false): Enter the tool assigned for the repair.
* **Category** (`category`, text, required: false): Enter the category of the equipment being repaired.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the problem or issue with the equipment.
* **Title** (`title`, text, required: false): Enter a title for the repair.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the customer.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the customer.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the customer.
* **Customer Email Verified** (`customer_email_verified`, text, required: false): Confirm whether the customer's email is verified.
* **Customer Phone Verified** (`customer_phone_verified`, text, required: false): Confirm whether the customer's phone number is verified.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, required: false): Enter the customer's address.
* **Customer Address Line 1** (`customer_address_line_1`, text, required: false): Enter the customer's address line 1.
* **Customer Address Line 2** (`customer_address_line_2`, text, required: false): Enter the customer's address line 2.
* **Customer City** (`customer_city`, text, required: false): Enter the customer's city.
* **Customer State** (`customer_state`, text, required: false): Enter the customer's state.
* **Customer Zip** (`customer_zip`, text, required: false): Enter the customer's zip code.
* **Customer Comments** (`customer_comments`, text, required: false): Enter any comments or notes about the customer.

## Tips
* Ensure you have permission to access and complete this form.
* Review existing records before filling out this form.
* Enter accurate and complete information to ensure the repair process proceeds smoothly.
