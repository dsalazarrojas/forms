# talent_photo_shoot_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a photo shoot, including details about the shoot, the talent involved, and the photographer and agency responsible.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the details of the photo shoot, including the date and time of the shoot.
2. Select the talent involved in the photo shoot.
3. Select the photographer responsible for the shoot.
4. Choose the campaign(s) this shoot is for.
5. If applicable, provide agency details and location of the shoot.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photo Shoot Details (1)**: Please provide a brief description of the photo shoot, including the date and time of the shoot.
* **Photo Shoot Date (2)**: Enter the date of the photo shoot in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Photo Shoot Time (3)**: Enter the start and end time of the photo shoot. If not required, leave blank.
* **Talent Details (4)**: Provide a brief description of the talent involved in the shoot, including their name and any relevant contact information.
* **Guardian Consent (5)**: If the talent is a minor, please have a guardian sign below to confirm their consent for the photo shoot.
* **Photo Shoot Location (6)**: Provide the location of the photo shoot, if applicable.
* **Agency Details (7)**: If applicable, provide agency details, including the agency name, contact name, and contact information.
* **Photographer Details (8)**: Select the photographer responsible for the shoot from the options provided.
* **Campaign Details (9)**: Select the campaign(s) this shoot is for from the options provided.
