# Auto Repair Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from the legal owner of a vehicle for repair or service. It ensures that the owner acknowledges and understands the terms and conditions of the repair, including authorization for used or aftermarket parts, test drives, liability waivers, and storage fees.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please enter your full name, which is the legal owner of the vehicle.
2. Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) in the format of 17 digits.
3. Describe the vehicle in detail, including year, make, and model.
4. Review and acknowledge the Release of Liability section, which includes authorization for used or aftermarket parts, test drives, and liability waivers.
5. Confirm that you have authorized the use of recycled or aftermarket parts as discussed.
6. Agree or disagree with the authorization for technicians to operate the vehicle for test purposes.
7. Choose whether or not to accept liability for loss or damage to the vehicle or its contents.
8. Acknowledge a mechanic's lien on the vehicle for repairs.
9. Agree or disagree with the storage fees if the vehicle is not picked up within 48 hours.
10. Sign this form to confirm your understanding and agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner Full Name** (`owner_full_name`, `text`, required): This is the legal owner of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle VIN** (`vehicle_vin`, `text`, required): Enter the 17-digit Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Description** (`vehicle_desc`, `text`, required): Describe the vehicle in detail, including year, make, and model.
* **Authorize Used or Aftermarket Parts** (`accept_used_parts`, `select_one`, required): This is an authorization for the use of recycled or aftermarket parts as discussed.
* **Authorize Test Drive** (`authorize_test_drive`, `select_one`, required): This is an authorization for technicians to operate the vehicle for test purposes.
* **Liability Waiver** (`liability_waiver`, `select_one`, required): This is a waiver of liability for loss or damage to the vehicle or its contents.
* **Acknowledge Mechanic's Lien** (`acknowledge_lien`, `select_one`, required): Acknowledge a mechanic's lien on the vehicle for repairs.
* **Agree to Storage Fees** (`storage_fees`, `select_one`, required): This is an agreement to pay storage fees if the vehicle is not picked up within 48 hours.
* **Signature (Printed Name)** (`signature_name`, `text`, required): Sign this form to confirm your understanding and agreement.
* **Signing Date** (`signing_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date this form is signed.

Note: Please review and read each field carefully to ensure that you understand its purpose and implications. This form is a crucial part of the repair process, and accurate completion is essential.
