# Health Screening Permission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from patients for health screenings, which can include routine, special, or emergency screenings. It helps ensure that patients understand the purpose and scope of the screening, and that they are aware of their rights and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
4. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
5. Select the Medical Group you are associated with from the "Medical Group" dropdown menu.
6. Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm you have given consent, which will update the "Consent Given" field.
7. Provide a brief reason for the screening in the "Reason for Screening" field.
8. Choose the type of screening you are undergoing, such as "Routine Screening", "Special Screening", or "Emergency Screening".
9. Select how often you want to be screened, such as "One Time", "Regularly", or "As Needed".
10. Enter your Patient Identifier, such as a patient ID number or a unique identifier.
11. Select the date you gave your consent.
12. Choose the status of your consent, such as "Active" or "Inactive".
13. Add any additional notes about your screening in the "Notes" field.
14. Select the person assigned to your screening from the "Assigned To" dropdown menu.
15. Enter the date when you were assigned to the screening.
16. Enter the date when the screening was completed.
17. If you completed the screening, select "Yes" from the "Completed By" dropdown menu.
18. If you reviewed the screening, select "Yes" from the "Reviewed By" dropdown menu.
19. Enter the date when you reviewed the screening.
20. Enter the next review date, if applicable.
21. Enter the date when you were reminded about the screening.
22. If you were reminded, select "Yes" from the "Reminder Sent" dropdown menu.
23. If you were reminded, select the person who sent the reminder from the "Reminder Sent By" dropdown menu.
24. Select "Yes" from the "Submitted" dropdown menu if you have completed and submitted the screening.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, Optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, Optional): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YY format.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, Required): Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, Optional): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted.
* **Medical Group** (`medical_group`, `select_multiple`, Required): Select the medical group you are associated with.
* **Consent Given** (`consent_given`, `select_one`, Required): Confirm you have given consent for the screening.
* **Reason for Screening** (`reason_for_screening`, `text`, Required): Provide a brief explanation for the screening.
* **Screening Type** (`screening_type`, `select_one`, Required): Choose the type of screening you are undergoing.
* **Screening Frequency** (`screening_frequency`, `select_one`, Required): Choose how often you want to be screened.
* **Patient Identifier** (`patient_identifier`, `text`, Required): Enter your patient identifier, such as a patient ID number.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, `date`, Required): Enter the date you gave your consent.
* **Consent Status** (`consent_status`, `select_one`, Required): Choose the status of your consent, such as "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, Optional): Add any additional notes about your screening.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `select_one`, Required): Select the person assigned to your screening.
* **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`, `date`, Required): Enter the date when you were assigned to the screening.
* **Completed Date** (`completed_date`, `date`, Optional): Enter the date when the screening was completed.
* **Completed By** (`completed_by`, `select_one`, Optional): Select the person who completed the screening.
* **Reviewed By** (`reviewed_by`, `select_one`, Optional): Select the person who reviewed the screening.
* **Reviewed Date** (`reviewed_date`, `date`, Optional): Enter the date when you reviewed the screening.
* **Next Review Date** (`next_review_date`, `date`, Optional): Enter the date of the next review, if applicable.
* **Reminder Date** (`reminder_date`, `date`, Optional): Enter the date when you were reminded about the screening.
* **Reminder Sent** (`reminder_sent`, `select_one`, Optional): Select "Yes" if you were reminded about the screening.
* **Reminder Sent By** (`reminder_sent_by`, `select_one`, Optional): Select the person who sent the reminder.
* **Submitted** (`submitted`, `select_one`, Optional): Select "Yes" if you have completed and submitted the screening.
