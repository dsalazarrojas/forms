# Healthcare Design Thinking Lab Filming Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from participants prior to filming in the Healthcare Design Thinking Lab. The form is used to document the participant's understanding and agreement to the terms of use of their recording, including its purpose, duration, and potential use. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Participant Details section with your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2. In the Filming Details section, describe the filming process, including the date, time, and location of the filming, as well as any specific details that are relevant to the filming.
3. In the Use of Recording section, specify how you intend to use the recording, and whether it will be used for educational, research, or promotional purposes.
4. In the Additional Comments section, provide any additional information that you would like to share with the filmers.
5. In the Contact Details section, provide your contact information, including name, number, email, and address.
6. Finally, sign and date the participant signature section to confirm that you have read and understand the terms of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details**: Please enter your name, address, and contact information in this section.
* **Filming Details**: Describe the filming process, including date, time, and location. You can also provide any other relevant details about the filming.
* **Use of Recording**: Specify how you intend to use the recording. Choose one or more of the options: Educational Use, Research Use, or Promotional Use.
* **Informed Consent**: Please provide a clear and concise statement of your understanding and agreement to the terms of use of your recording.
* **Educational Use**: If you selected Educational Use, specify how you plan to use the recording in your educational activities.
* **Research Use**: If you selected Research Use, specify how you plan to use the recording in your research activities.
* **Promotional Use**: If you selected Promotional Use, specify how you plan to use the recording in your promotional activities.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional information that you would like to share with the filmers.
* **Contact Details**: Please enter your name, number, email, and address in this section.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number here.
* **Contact E-mail**: Enter your contact email here.
* **Contact Address**: Enter your contact address here.
* **Participant Signature**: Please sign and date this section to confirm that you have read and understand the terms of the form. 

## Tips
* Please make sure to read and understand the terms of use before signing the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the terms, please do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
* Please sign and date the form to confirm that you have read and understand the terms of use.
* Keep a copy of the signed form for your records.
* If you need to make any changes to the form, please contact the filmers in advance.
