# field_trip_check_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The field_trip_check_out_form is a tool for collecting and verifying information related to field trips. This form aims to ensure that all necessary data is gathered for the success and safety of the field trip.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and required fields.
2.  Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
3.  Verify the information you've entered is correct before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Input
* **User Input** (`<text>`, optional): This field allows you to enter any additional information relevant to the field trip. Be concise and clear in your entry.

### Date Of Birth
* **Date Of Birth** (`<date>`, optional): Enter the student's date of birth. This will help in verifying the student's age and eligibility for the field trip.

### Student ID
* **Student ID** (`<text>`, optional): Enter the student's ID number. This helps identify the student and ensures they are accounted for during the trip.

### Guardian Name
* **Guardian Name** (`<text>`, optional): Enter the name of the student's guardian or parent. This ensures that the guardian is aware of the student's whereabouts during the trip.

### Guardian Phone
* **Guardian Phone** (`<text>`, optional): Enter the phone number of the student's guardian or parent. This allows for easy contact in case of emergencies.

### Guardian Email
* **Guardian Email** (`<email>`, optional): Enter the email of the student's guardian or parent. This allows for secure and efficient communication.

### School Admin Name
* **School Admin Name** (`<text>`, optional): Enter the name of the school administrator responsible for the field trip. This ensures that the school's staff is aware of the trip's organization and logistics.

### School Admin Phone
* **School Admin Phone** (`<text>`, optional): Enter the phone number of the school administrator. This allows for easy contact with the school staff during the trip.

### School Admin Email
* **School Admin Email** (`<email>`, optional): Enter the email of the school administrator. This allows for secure and efficient communication with the school staff.

### Date Of Departure
* **Date Of Departure** (`<date>`, optional): Enter the date the field trip will depart. This helps in planning and organizing the trip.

### Date Of Return
* **Date Of Return** (`<date>`, optional): Enter the date the field trip will return. This helps in scheduling the return transport and reintegration of students.

### Time Of Departure
* **Time Of Departure** (`<time>`, optional): Enter the time the field trip will depart. This helps in scheduling the departure and ensuring punctuality.

### Time Of Return
* **Time Of Return** (`<time>`, optional): Enter the time the field trip will return. This helps in scheduling the return transport and reintegration of students.

### Mode Of Transportation
* **Mode Of Transportation** (`<select_one>`, optional): Choose one of the following options: Car, Bus, Train, Airplane, or Other. This determines how students will travel to and from the field trip destination.

### Other
* **Other** (`<text>`, optional): If the mode of transportation is not listed above, enter the specific mode of transportation used.

### School Address
* **School Address** (`<text>`, optional): Enter the school's address. This helps in mapping the trip's location and ensures the school's presence is accounted for.

### Guardian Signature
* **Guardian Signature** (`<note>`, optional): This field is not applicable for signature purposes as it's currently set to "note" instead of "signature". However, it's likely intended for the guardian's confirmation or acknowledgment of the field trip details.

### School Signature
* **School Signature** (`<note>`, optional): This field is not applicable for signature purposes as it's currently set to "note" instead of "signature". However, it's likely intended for the school's confirmation or acknowledgment of the field trip details.

### School Signature Confirmation
* **School Signature Confirmation** (`<select_one>`, optional): Choose whether the school has signed the field trip agreement. This ensures the school's commitment to the trip's terms and conditions.

### School Admin Confirmation
* **School Admin Confirmation** (`<select_one>`, optional): Choose whether the school administrator has confirmed the field trip details. This ensures the school's staff is aware of the trip's organization and logistics.

### School Admin Confirmation Text
* **School Admin Confirmation Text** (`<text>`, optional): Enter additional text related to the school administrator's confirmation. This provides further insight into the school's stance on the field trip.

### Student Signature
* **Student Signature** (`<note>`, optional): This field is not applicable for signature purposes as it's currently set to "note" instead of "signature". However, it's likely intended for the student's confirmation or acknowledgment of the field trip details.

### Student Signature Confirmation
* **Student Signature Confirmation** (`<select_one>`, optional): Choose whether the student has confirmed the field trip details. This ensures the student's understanding and commitment to the trip.

### Student Signature Confirmation Text
* **Student Signature Confirmation Text** (`<text>`, optional): Enter additional text related to the student's confirmation. This provides further insight into the student's understanding of the field trip.

### Date Of Submission
* **Date Of Submission** (`<date>`, optional): Enter the date the form is submitted. This helps in tracking the trip's organization and progress.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* If a field is not applicable, leave it blank or indicate as such.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors or omissions.
* If you're unsure about any field or requirement, contact your school's field trip coordinator or administrator.
