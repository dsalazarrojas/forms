# PR Campaign Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PR Campaign Permission Form is a mandatory document that enables participants to provide their consent for a PR campaign. It's crucial to fill out this form accurately and honestly, as it will be used to track and record participant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Participant Name** (`participant_name`) accurately and truthfully.
2. Enter your **Email** (`email`) address to ensure we can contact you for any updates or questions.
3. Enter your **Phone** (`phone`) number so we can reach you if needed.
4. Select your **Role** (`role`) in the campaign (e.g., PR Representative, Media Professional, Influencer).
5. Enter your **Company** (`company`) name to associate with your participation in the campaign.
6. Fill in your **Location** (`location`) where you'll be working for the campaign.
7. Enter the **Start Date** (`start_date`) and **End Date** (`end_date`) of the campaign period.
8. Provide a brief **Description** (`description`) of your participation in the campaign.
9. Enter the **Campaign Name** (`campaign_name`) to associate with your participation.
10. Enter the **Participant Count** (`participant_count`) to help us track the number of participants.
11. Optionally, you can add a **Participant Count Note** (`participant_count_note`) to provide additional context.
12. If applicable, select the **Category** (`category`) of the campaign (e.g., Consent Forms, Campaign Forms, Other Forms).
13. If needed, select the **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`) used by the participant.
14. Finally, review your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name (Participant Name)**: This field is required and should be filled in accurately and truthfully.
* **Email (Email)**: This is a required field to ensure we can contact you for any updates or questions.
* **Phone (Phone)**: This is a required field to reach you if needed.
* **Role (Role)**: Select your role in the campaign from the provided options.
* **Company (Company)**: Enter your company name to associate with your participation in the campaign.
* **Location (Location)**: This field should be filled in accurately to track your location.
* **Start Date (Start Date)**: Enter the start date of the campaign period.
* **End Date (End Date)**: Enter the end date of the campaign period.
* **Description (Description)**: Provide a brief description of your participation in the campaign.
* **Campaign Name (Campaign Name)**: Enter the campaign name to associate with your participation.
* **Participant Count (Participant Count)**: Enter the number of participants in the campaign.
* **Participant Count Note (Participant Count Note)**: This is an optional field for providing additional context.
* **Assigned Tool (Assigned Tool)**: This field is optional, and you should select the tool used by the participant if applicable.
* **Category (Category)**: Select the category of the campaign from the provided options.
* **Category (Category)**: This is a required field to ensure we can categorize the campaign properly.
