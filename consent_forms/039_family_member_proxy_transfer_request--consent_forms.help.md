# family-member-proxy-transfer-request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a proxy transfer for a family member to access to your health information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start the proxy transfer process, select the family member you wish to grant access to your health information from the "family-members" dropdown menu. 
2. If necessary, provide more information about yourself in the "transfer-requester-info" field.
3. Attach any relevant documentation that supports your request, such as identification or proof of relationship, to the "authorization-docs" field.
4. Upload any supporting documents that may be required for the proxy transfer, such as a letter or medical record, to the "supporting-docs" field.
5. Specify the consent status you are willing to grant to the family member by selecting from the options in the "consent-status" dropdown menu.
6. Finally, click the "submit-button" to submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Transfer-Requester-Info (transfer_requester_info)**: Enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details that may aid in the proxy transfer process.
* **Family Members (family_members)**: Select the family member you wish to grant access to your health information. This can be an adult child, spouse, or other designated proxy.
* **Authorization Docs (authorization_docs)**: Attach any identification or proof of relationship documents that support your request.
* **Supporting Docs (supporting_docs)**: Upload any supporting documents that may be required for the proxy transfer process. This can include medical records or letters.
* **Consent Status (consent_status)**: Specify the level of access you are willing to grant to the family member. Options will vary depending on the specific requirements of the proxy transfer process.
* **Submit-Button (submit_button)**: Click this button to submit your completed form for review and processing.
