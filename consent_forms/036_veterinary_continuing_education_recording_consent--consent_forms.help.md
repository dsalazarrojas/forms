# veterinary_continuing_education_recording_consent - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain consent for recording veterinary continuing education events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your general information.
2. Provide your contact information.
3. Check the boxes next to your preferred options for "Recording Permissions".
4. Enter any special requests or comments in the "Additional Comments" section.
5. Sign your name in the "Signature" field.
6. Finally, confirm your consent by typing your name in the "Confirm" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_information`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, required: false): Provide your contact information, such as your phone number and email address.
* **Do You Consent** (`consent`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check the boxes next to the options that apply to you. This field is not required, but it's essential to check the boxes if you consent to the recording of your veterinary continuing education events.

* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, `text`, required: false): Enter any special requests or comments you have.
