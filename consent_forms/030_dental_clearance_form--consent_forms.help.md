# Dental Clearance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dental Clearance Form is a tool used by dental professionals to gather information about patients' medical histories, allergies, and other relevant medical conditions before performing dental procedures. This helps ensure that patients receive proper care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To begin, fill in the patient's name, date of birth, and contact information in the "Patient Information" section.
2. In the "Medical History" section, provide any relevant medical history, including illnesses, surgeries, or other significant medical events.
3. List any allergies the patient has in the "Allergies" section, selecting all that apply from the provided options.
4. In the "Medications" section, list any medications the patient is currently taking.
5. Select the appropriate dental procedure from the options provided in the "Dental Procedures" section.
6. Provide any additional medical procedure details in the "Medical Procedure Details" section.
7. Finally, describe the patient's current medical condition in the "Medical Condition" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`Patient Information`, text, required): Fill in the patient's name, date of birth, and contact information. This section is crucial for identifying the patient and communicating with them.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, text, required): Describe any significant medical events, such as illnesses, surgeries, or injuries, that may impact the patient's treatment.
* **Allergies** (`Allergies`, select multiple, required): Select all allergens that apply to the patient, such as Peanut, Pollen, or Latex.
* **Medications** (`Medications`, text, optional): List any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Dental Procedures** (`Dental Procedures`, select one, required): Choose the dental procedure to be performed, such as Prophylaxis, Periodontal Evaluation and X-ray, or Biopsy.
* **Medical Procedure Details** (`Medical Procedure Details`, text, optional): Provide any additional details about the medical procedure, including the location and any other relevant information.
* **Medical Condition** (`Medical Condition`, text, optional): Describe the patient's current medical condition, if applicable.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form to ensure the patient receives proper care and treatment.
* If you are unsure about any section, ask the patient or their medical professional for clarification.
* This form should be filled out only once per patient visit.
