# Data Privacy Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information for data privacy declaration and contact details of users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please start by reading and understanding the data privacy policies.
2. Fill out the "User Agreement" field, which outlines your agreement to the terms and conditions of data usage.
3. Provide information on how your data will be used in the "Data Use" field.
4. Fill out your contact details, including name, address, phone number, email, and other relevant information.
5. Choose how you wish to be contacted in the "Contact Us" field (optional).
6. Fill out the "Data Privacy Declaration" field with your declaration regarding data privacy.
7. Fill out the "Declaration Date" and "Declaration Time" fields with the current date and time.
8. Provide a complete address in the "Contact Address" field.
9. Enter your city and state in the "City" and "State" fields.
10. Enter your zip code in the "Zip Code" field.
11. Select your country of origin in the "Country" field.
12. Fill out the "Contact Name" field with the name of the contact person.
13. Enter the contact person's email address in the "Contact Email" field.
14. Enter the contact person's phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
15. Fill out the "Contact Address" field with the contact person's address.
16. Enter the city and state of the contact person in the "Contact City" and "Contact State" fields.
17. Enter the zip code of the contact person in the "Zip Code" field.
18. Select the country of the contact person in the "Contact Country" field.
