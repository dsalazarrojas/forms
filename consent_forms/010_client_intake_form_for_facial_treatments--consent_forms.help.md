# Client Intake Form For Facial Treatments - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients prior to facial treatments. It helps our team understand your skin health, medical history, and preferences, allowing us to provide you with the best possible experience and treatment outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure our team has all the necessary information to provide you with optimal care.
2. Be honest and thorough when answering the questions, as your responses will help us tailor your treatment plan.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Age** (`age`, number, optional): If you're comfortable sharing your age, please provide it to help us better understand your skin concerns.
* **Skin Assessment** (`section_assessment`, note, optional): This is a brief description of your skin concerns. You can type up to 255 characters.
* **What are your primary skin concerns?** (`skin_concerns`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply from the list:
	+ Acne
	+ Fine Lines
	+ Dark Spots
	+ Redness
	+ Dryness
	+ Large Pores
* **List the skincare products you currently use at home** (`current_skincare`, text, optional): Please describe the skincare products you use daily.
* **Do you wear sunscreen daily?** (`sunscreen_use`, select_one, required): 
	+ True
	+ False
* **Average daily water intake** (`water_intake`, select_one, optional): Select your average daily water consumption:
	+ 0-2 glasses
	+ 3-5 glasses
	+ 6 or more glasses
* **Medical History** (`section_medical`, note, optional): This is a brief description of your medical history, up to 255 characters.
* **Do you have a history of cold sores?** (`cold_sores`, select_one, optional): 
	+ True
	+ False
* **Have you ever had a reaction to a skincare product or treatment?** (`previous_reactions`, text, optional): If yes, please provide a brief description.
* **Are you currently pregnant or trying to conceive?** (`pregnancy_status`, select_one, optional): 
	+ True
	+ False
* **Treatment Consent** (`section_consent`, note, optional): This is a brief description of your consent for treatment, up to 255 characters.
* **I understand that minor discomfort may occur during manual extractions** (`extraction_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): 
	+ True
	+ I understand
* **Signature - Type Full Name** (`signature`, text, required): Please sign your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.

## Tips
- Be thorough and honest when answering the questions to ensure the best possible experience.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask.
- Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
