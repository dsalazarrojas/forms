# LLC Consent Letter Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for obtaining member consent for a company action. It is meant to provide a formal record of member consent, including their name, address, and contact information, as well as details about the company action.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the Member Name field.
2. Provide your address in the Member Address field.
3. Select your title (Member, Officer, or Partner) in the Member Title field.
4. Add a brief description of the company action in the Action Description field.
5. Confirm your consent by selecting "True" in the Consent Given field.
6. If necessary, select one or more options in the Company Action field.
7. Add any additional comments about the member's consent in the Comments field.
8. Enter your email address in the Email field.
9. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **consent_form**: A text field where you can enter your member name.
* **Member Name (Member Name)**: Enter your name as a member of the LLC.
* **Member Address (Member Address)**: Enter your address as a member of the LLC.
* **Consent Date (Consent Date)**: This is a date field that will be automatically filled in by the system.
* **Member Title (Member Title)**: Select your title as a member of the LLC (Member, Officer, or Partner).
* **Action Description (Action Description)**: A text field where you can add a brief description of the company action.
* **Consent Given (Consent Given)**: Confirm your consent by selecting "True".
* **Company Action (Company Action)**: Select one or more options that describe the company action.
* **Comments (Comments)**: A text field where you can add any additional comments about the member's consent.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (Phone)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Created At (Created At)**: This is a date field that will be automatically filled in by the system.
* **Updated At (Updated At)**: This is a date field that will be automatically filled in by the system.
