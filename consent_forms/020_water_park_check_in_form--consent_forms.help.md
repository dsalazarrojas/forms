# Water Park Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from visitors to the water park, including personal details, visit specifics, and other relevant data to enhance their experience and maintain park records.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Guest Information** in the first field. This is a general field where you can provide any relevant details about yourself, such as your name and contact information.
2. Select the **Visit Date** for the duration of your visit. This will help the park staff plan and manage their resources effectively.
3. Choose the **Visit Time** for when you plan to arrive and depart from the park. This will help staff coordinate their schedules and allocate resources accordingly.
4. If you're visiting with a group, provide details in the **Group Information** field. This can include group names, sizes, and any special requests or requirements.
5. Indicate the number of **Adults** and **Children** in your group. This helps the park staff prepare for adequate facilities and services.
6. Add any **Notes** you'd like to share with park staff, such as special requests or allergies.
7. Enter your **Email** and **Phone** for contact purposes, in case the park staff need to reach out to you during your visit.
8. If assigned, provide the **Assigned Tool** to the park staff for efficient allocation and maintenance.
9. Select the **Category** of your visit, which can help the park categorize and analyze visitor data.
10. Add a **Description** of your visit, which can include any additional context or details about your group's plans.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Information**: A general field for providing personal details about yourself.
* **Visit Date**: The date of your planned visit to the water park.
* **Visit Time**: The time of your planned visit, including arrival and departure times.
* **Group Information**: A field for describing your group, including size and any special requests.
* **Adults**: Number of adults in your group.
* **Children**: Number of children in your group.
* **Notes**: Any additional comments or requests you'd like to share with park staff.
* **Email**: Your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Your phone number for park staff to reach out during your visit.
* **Assigned Tool**: If you've been assigned a tool or equipment for your visit, provide its details here.
* **Category**: The category of your visit, which helps the park with visitor data analysis.
* **Description**: A description of your visit, including any context or details about your group.
