# Advance Directive Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to capture an individual's preferences and instructions for medical treatment in the event they become unable to communicate their wishes themselves. This form is used to ensure that their healthcare providers respect their decisions and act in accordance with their instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name and date of birth.
2. Provide your contact information and a contact number for a person authorized to make decisions on your behalf (proxy).
3. Indicate your relationship to the proxy.
4. Answer questions about your treatment preferences, including life-sustaining treatments, artificial nutrition and hydration, and specific instructions for pain management.
5. Decide on your organ donation preference.
6. Sign the form to confirm your preferences and instructions.
7. Provide the names and signatures of two witnesses who are not the designated proxy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Please fill in your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Please enter your date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Please enter your contact number for the proxy or healthcare provider to reach you.
* **Proxy Name** (`proxy_name`, `text`, required): Please enter the name of the person authorized to make decisions on your behalf.
* **Proxy Relationship to Principal** (`proxy_relationship_to_principal`, `text`, required): Please indicate your relationship to the proxy (e.g., spouse, child, friend).
* **Proxy Contact Phone** (`proxy_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Please enter the contact phone number for the proxy.
* **Life Sustaining Treatment Choice** (`life_sustaining_treatment_choice`, `select_one`, required): Please select one of the options for life-sustaining treatments.
* **Artificial Nutrition and Hydration** (`artificial_nutrition_and_hydration`, `select_one`, required): Please select one of the options for artificial nutrition and hydration.
* **Specific Instructions for Pain Management** (`specific_instructions_for_pain_management`, `text`, optional): Please provide any additional instructions for pain management.
* **Organ Donation Preference** (`organ_donation_preference`, `select_one`, required): Please select one of the options for organ donation.
* **Witness 1 Name** (`witness_1_name`, `text`, required): Please enter the name of the first witness.
* **Witness 2 Name** (`witness_2_name`, `text`, required): Please enter the name of the second witness.
* **Principal Signature** (`principal_signature`, `text`, required): Please sign your name to confirm your preferences and instructions.
* **Date of Execution** (`date_of_execution`, `date`, required): Please enter the date the form is being executed.
