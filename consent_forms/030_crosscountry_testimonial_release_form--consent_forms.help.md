# Crosscountry Testimonial Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather testimonials from users to understand their experiences with our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  **Testimonial Release Form** (`testimonial_release_form`, text, required): This is the main section where you'll write your testimonial. Please provide a brief but genuine review of your experience with our services.
2.  **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required): In this field, please provide your contact information so we can get in touch with you to follow up on your testimonial.
3.  **Your Testimonial** (`testimonial_text`, text, required): This is where you'll write the details of your testimonial. Be as descriptive as possible, but make sure it's concise.
4.  **Date** (`date`, date, required): Please enter the date of your visit or interaction with our services. This will help us understand when you experienced our services.
5.  **Realtor Information** (`realtor_info`, text, optional): If you had any interaction with our realtor, please provide their name and any additional details about your interaction with them.

## Tips
- Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, feel free to contact us for help.
- We appreciate your feedback and look forward to hearing about your experience with our services.
