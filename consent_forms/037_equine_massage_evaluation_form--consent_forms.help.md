# Equine Massage Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and details about equine massage sessions. It helps massage practitioners evaluate their sessions and owners provide valuable insights into the effectiveness of the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please select the relevant information and input it accurately. Each section should be filled out based on the specific details of the session being evaluated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equine Massage Session Notes**: Please describe the session's details, including any specific areas of focus, techniques used, and notable outcomes.
* **Owner Consent**: Select the owner's consent for the massage session from the provided options.
* **Evaluation Outcomes**: Choose the evaluation outcomes from the provided options that best match the session's results.
* **Follow-up**: If applicable, provide a follow-up date for the next session.
* **Massage Type**: Select the type of massage performed during the session.
* **Massage Area**: Indicate the areas of the equine's body that were massaged.
* **Massage Duration**: Enter the duration of the massage session.
* **Follow-up Contact**: Provide any contact information for the owner or equine's owner.
* **Follow-up Time**: If applicable, provide the follow-up time for the next session.
* **Owner Name**: Enter the name of the equine's owner.
* **Owner Phone**: Enter the owner's phone number.
* **Owner Email**: Enter the owner's email address.
* **Owner Notes**: Add any additional notes from the owner.
* **Equine Massage Session Notes 2**: Add any additional session notes (this field can be repeated up to 13 times).
* ... (additional fields with similar labels)

Note: The fields are designed to be repeated up to 13 times for session notes, allowing for multiple sessions to be documented with their respective notes.
