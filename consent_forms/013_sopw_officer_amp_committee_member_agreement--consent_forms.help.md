# SOPW Officer & Committee Member Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for officers and committee members to document their understanding and agreements regarding confidential conversations and mutual obligations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form's purpose and ensure you understand the context and requirements.
2. Read each field explanation carefully to ensure you understand its relevance and expected input.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Officer & Committee Member Agreement** (`officer_and_committee_member_agreement_form`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter your agreement or understanding with the other committee members.
  - Please write down any promises, commitments, or expectations you have with fellow committee members.
  - You may include specific details about how you will communicate, share information, or collaborate.
  - Remember to keep this field concise and focused on the main points of your agreement.
- **Agreement** (`agreement`, text, optional): This field is for any additional agreements or comments that don't fit in the previous field.
  - You can use this space to elaborate on any specific points or details you've agreed upon.
  - Keep your response clear and concise, as it will be read by others.
- **Confidential Conversations** (`confidential_conversations`, text, optional): This field is for discussing sensitive or confidential topics that need special consideration.
  - If you have conversations or topics that should remain confidential, write them down here.
  - Specify the reasons why they should be kept private and what measures you'll take to ensure confidentiality.
- **Help Others** (`help_others`, text, optional): This field is for offering any help, support, or services you can provide to fellow members.
  - List any specific ways you're committed to assisting others in your role or on the project.
  - Specify any limitations or constraints that you're aware of regarding your ability to help others.
  - This will help others understand your willingness to collaborate and support each other.

## Tips
- Be clear and concise in your responses.
- Proofread carefully before submitting the form.
- This form is meant to be a collaborative document; it's okay to discuss and revise it with your peers.
- If you're unsure about any aspect, consult with your team or the form creator.
