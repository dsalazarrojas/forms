# COVID 19 Vaccine Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID-19 Vaccine Consent Form is a critical document that ensures patients are well-informed and willing participants in the vaccination process. It outlines the necessary information and risks associated with the vaccine, as well as the patient's understanding and consent for administration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your patient's full name in the "Patient Full Name" field.
2. Enter your patient's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
3. Enter your patient's email address in the "Email Address" field.
4. Enter the name of the healthcare provider or facility in the "Healthcare Provider or Facility Name" field.
5. Enter the name of the administering physician or nurse in the "Administering Physician or Nurse Name" field.
6. Read and agree to the "Vaccine Information and Understanding" section.
7. Confirm that you understand the benefits of the COVID-19 vaccine in the "I understand the benefits of COVID-19 vaccination" field.
8. Confirm that you understand the risks and potential side effects of the vaccine in the "I understand the risks and potential side effects" field.
9. Read and agree to the "Alternatives and Options" section.
10. Confirm that you understand alternative options and consequences of not vaccinating in the "I understand alternative options and consequences of not vaccinating" field.
11. Confirm that all your questions have been answered in the "All my questions have been answered to my satisfaction" field.
12. Confirm that you have disclosed all chronic health conditions in the "I have disclosed all chronic health conditions" field.
13. Confirm that you have disclosed all medications you are taking in the "I have disclosed all medications I am taking" field.
14. Confirm that you have disclosed all known allergies in the "I have disclosed all known allergies" field.
15. Confirm your pregnancy status in the "I have accurately reported pregnancy status" field.
16. Read and agree to the "Consent Statement" section.
17. Confirm that you voluntarily consent to receive the COVID-19 vaccine in the "I voluntarily consent to receive the COVID-19 vaccine" field.
18. Confirm that you acknowledge you have been informed of potential risks in the "I acknowledge I have been informed of potential risks" field.
19. Confirm that you understand you can refuse or withdraw consent at any time in the "I understand I can refuse or withdraw consent at any time" field.
20. Confirm that you authorize the healthcare provider to administer the vaccine in the "I authorize the healthcare provider to administer the vaccine" field.
21. Release the provider and facility from liability related to vaccination in the "I release the provider and facility from liability related to vaccination" field.
22. Enter your emergency contact's name in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
23. Enter your emergency contact's phone number in the "Emergency Contact Phone Number" field.
24. Enter your digital signature or consent in the "Digital Signature or Consent" field.
25. Enter the date of consent in the "Date of Consent" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Full Name** (1, required): Enter your patient's full name, including first and last name.
* **Date of Birth** (2, required): Enter your patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address** (3, required): Enter your patient's email address.
* **Healthcare Provider or Facility Name** (4, required): Enter the name of the healthcare provider or facility administering the vaccine.
* **Administering Physician or Nurse Name** (5, required): Enter the name of the administering physician or nurse.
* **Vaccine Information and Understanding** (6, not required): Read and understand the vaccine information sheet. This section provides detailed information about the vaccine, including its ingredients, side effects, and benefits.
* **I have received and understood the vaccine information sheet** (7, required): Confirm that you understand the vaccine information sheet.
* **I understand the benefits of COVID-19 vaccination** (8, required): Confirm that you understand the benefits of the COVID-19 vaccine.
* **I understand the risks and potential side effects** (9, required): Confirm that you understand the risks and potential side effects of the vaccine.
* **Alternatives and Options** (10, not required): Read and understand the alternatives and options available for vaccination.
* **I understand alternative options and consequences of not vaccinating** (11, required): Confirm that you understand alternative options and consequences of not vaccinating.
* **All my questions have been answered to my satisfaction** (12, required): Confirm that all your questions have been answered to your satisfaction.
* **I have disclosed all chronic health conditions** (13, required): Confirm that you have disclosed all chronic health conditions.
* **I have disclosed all medications I am taking** (14, required): Confirm that you have disclosed all medications you are taking.
* **I have disclosed all known allergies** (15, required): Confirm that you have disclosed all known allergies.
* **I have accurately reported pregnancy status** (16, required): Confirm your pregnancy status.
* **Consent Statement** (17, not required): Read and agree to the consent statement.
* **I voluntarily consent to receive the COVID-19 vaccine** (18, required): Confirm that you voluntarily consent to receive the COVID-19 vaccine.
* **I acknowledge I have been informed of potential risks** (19, required): Confirm that you acknowledge you have been informed of potential risks.
* **I understand I can refuse or withdraw consent at any time** (20, required): Confirm that you understand you can refuse or withdraw consent at any time.
* **I authorize the healthcare provider to administer the vaccine** (21, required): Confirm that you authorize the healthcare provider to administer the vaccine.
* **I release the provider and facility from liability related to vaccination** (22, not required): Release the provider and facility from liability related to vaccination.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (23, required): Enter your emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (24, required): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Digital Signature or Consent** (25, required): Enter your digital signature or consent.
* **Date of Consent** (26, required): Enter the date of consent.
