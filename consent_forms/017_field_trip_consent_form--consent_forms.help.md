# Field Trip Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Field Trip Consent Form is designed to gather consent from students and their parents/guardians for a field trip. The form ensures that both the student and parent/guardian are aware of the trip details and have agreed to its terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Field Trip Consent Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's name, parent's name, and parent's email address.
2. Provide the student's phone number and address.
3. Briefly describe the trip, including the date and time.
4. Choose your agreement for taking the trip (student and parent).
5. Add any additional trip details (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name as it appears on their records.
* **Parent Name**: Enter the parent/guardian's name associated with the student.
* **Parent Email**: Enter the parent/guardian's email address for communication purposes.
* **Student Phone**: Enter the student's phone number for contact during the trip.
* **Student Address**: Enter the student's home address for contact purposes.
* **Trip Description**: Briefly describe the trip, including any relevant details.
* **Trip Date**: Enter the date of the trip (YYYY-MM-DD format).
* **Trip Time**: Enter the time of the trip (HH:MM format).
* **Student Agreement**: Select the option that best reflects the student's agreement to take the trip.
* **Parent Agreement**: Select the option that best reflects the parent's/guardian's agreement to the trip's terms.
* **Trip Details**: Add any additional details about the trip, such as meeting points, transportation, or other logistical information.
