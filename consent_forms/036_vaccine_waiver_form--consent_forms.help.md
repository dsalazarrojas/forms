# Vaccine Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vaccine Waiver Form is used to record a patient's reason for not receiving a vaccine. This form helps healthcare providers understand patients' vaccination status and can aid in patient education and care planning.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure you have a valid reason for not receiving a vaccine.
2. Select your answer from the dropdown menus as accurately as possible.
3. Complete all required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your full legal name as it appears on your medical record.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select all that apply from the provided options.
* **Reason Not Vaccinated** (`reason_not_vaccinated`, text, optional):
	+ Provide a brief explanation for why you are not receiving the vaccine.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date you visited the healthcare provider or the date you received this form.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional):
	+ Enter the time you visited the healthcare provider or the time you received this form.
* **Physician Signature** (`physician_signature`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select your physician's name from the provided options.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select your signature type from the provided options.

**Tips:**
- Ensure accurate and clear responses for better care planning.
- Review your answers before submitting the form.
- If you need assistance, ask your healthcare provider for help.
