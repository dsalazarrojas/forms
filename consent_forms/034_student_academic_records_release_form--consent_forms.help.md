# Student Academic Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain student consent for the release of their academic records. Students can choose who to release their records to, including academic departments or programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the release option for the intended recipient from the dropdown menu. You can choose one or multiple options.
2. Enter your student ID in the format required by your student ID system.
3. Provide your name as it appears on your academic records.
4. If required, enter a name for the contact person or organization.
5. If required, enter a valid email address for contact.
6. Enter a valid phone number for contact.
7. Select a date for the release of records, which will indicate when the release is valid from and to.
8. Select a date to indicate when the release of records will expire.
9. Select who you are authorizing to release your records to (e.g. Student, Academic Advisor, etc.).
10. Provide a reason for the authorized release.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID (Student ID, number, required)**: Enter your unique student identification number as it appears in your student system.
* **Release To (Release To, select one or multiple, required)**: Choose who you would like to release your records to, such as an academic department or program.
* **Name (Name, text, required)**: Enter your name as it appears on your official records.
* **Contact Name (Contact Name, text, required)**: Enter the name of the person or organization that will be receiving your released records.
* **Email (Email, email, required)**: Enter a valid email address for contact.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number, text, required)**: Enter a valid phone number for contact.
* **Release Date (Release Date, date, required)**: Select the date when the release of records will be valid from.
* **Release Expires (Release Expires, date, required)**: Select the date when the release of records will expire.
* **Authorized Person (Authorized Person, select multiple, required)**: Choose who you are authorizing to release your records to (e.g. Student, Academic Advisor, etc.).
* **Authorized Reason (Authorized Reason, text, required)**: Provide a reason for the authorized release.

# Tips
Please ensure that you are releasing your records to the correct party and for the correct reasons. If you are unsure, contact your academic department or student affairs office for guidance.
