# Tinker Tree Group Family Daycare Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tinker Tree Group Family Daycare Agreement form is a legal document that outlines the terms and conditions of a child's care at the daycare center. It serves as a formal agreement between the parent or guardian and the daycare center, ensuring that both parties understand their responsibilities and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the User Information page.
2. Provide your Parent/Guardian Name and other relevant details.
3. Enter your Email and Phone number for contact.
4. Specify your Address for communication and record-keeping purposes.
5. Define your relationship with the child (e.g., parent, guardian, or emergency contact).
6. Date of consent (the date you agree to the terms).
7. Sign and date the Parent Signature and Staff Signature fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information**: A general information page for you to provide your details about yourself.
* **Parent/Guardian Name**: Your name and surname.
* **Email**: A valid email address for communication and record-keeping purposes.
* **Parent/Guardian Phone**: A phone number for emergency contact and communication.
* **Parent/Guardian Address**: A physical address for the daycare to keep records and send important notices.
* **Parent/Guardian Relationship**: Specify how you are related to the child (e.g., mother, father, brother, or emergency contact).
* **Consent Date**: The date you agree to the daycare's terms and conditions.
* **Parent Signature**: A signature confirming your agreement to the terms and conditions of the daycare.
* **Staff Signature**: A staff signature from the daycare, indicating that you've been informed of and agreed to the terms.
**Tips**

* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the daycare center staff.
* Date and sign the form when you're ready to confirm your agreement.
