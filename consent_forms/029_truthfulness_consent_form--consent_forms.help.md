# truthfulness_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for the truthfulness consent process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the User Name field with your name as it appears on official documents.
2. Enter your Contact Number, which should be a valid phone number that can be used to contact you.
3. Select one or more of the options in the Truthfulness Declaration field, indicating your understanding and agreement to the terms of the truthfulness process.
4. If you have a valid Email address, enter it in the Email field. This will help us keep you informed about updates and notifications.
5. If you are affiliated with an organization, enter the name of your organization in the Organization field.
6. Enter the Date and Time of the truthfulness process in the Date and Time fields.
7. Use the Notes field to provide any additional information that is relevant to the truthfulness process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on official documents.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required/optional): Enter a valid phone number that can be used to contact you.
* **Truthfulness Declaration** (`truthfulness_declaration`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more of the options to indicate your understanding and agreement to the terms of the truthfulness process.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address to receive updates and notifications.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your affiliated organization, if applicable.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the truthfulness process.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the truthfulness process.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional information relevant to the truthfulness process.
