# Hospital Staff Wellbeing Research Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is to obtain your consent for participation in hospital staff wellbeing research. It's a necessary step to ensure that you are aware of and agree to participate in the research.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Read through each section carefully and answer all the questions that apply to you.
2. Select the relevant options for each field, as instructed below.
3. Enter your contact details and email address.
4. Confirm your email address.
5. Choose your contact preferences and data sharing options.
6. Select your research participant group.
7. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Research Participation** (Select One):
	+ Select the option that best describes your interest in participating in the research: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
* **Wellbeing Topics** (Select Multiple):
	+ Choose the wellbeing topics that are most relevant to you: Option 4, Option 5, and/or Option 6.
* **Informed Consent** (Note):
	+ This is a space for you to provide your informed consent for the research. Please type out your informed consent, and make sure to understand the terms and conditions of the research.
* **Contact Details** (Text):
	+ Enter your name, address, and any other contact information you wish to provide.
* **Date of Birth** (Date):
	+ Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Phone Number** (Text):
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Email Address** (Email):
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Confirm Email** (Email):
	+ Re-enter your email address to confirm.
* **Contact Preferences** (Select One):
	+ Choose how you would like to be contacted: Option 7, Option 8.
* **Data Sharing** (Select Multiple):
	+ Choose the options for which you are willing to share your data: Option 9, Option 10, and/or Option 11.
* **Research Participant Group** (Select One):
	+ Select your research participant group: Option 12, Option 13.
* **Wellbeing Questionnaire** (Note):
	+ This is a space for you to provide any additional information or comments about your wellbeing.

Note: The order of the fields in the form may change in future updates, so it's essential to review each field carefully and ensure you understand its purpose before providing your answers.
