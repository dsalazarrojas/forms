# Smart Access Card Setup Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture the setup information for a Smart Access Card, including employee details, access levels, and signature confirmation. It is designed to ensure that all necessary information is collected and signed off by the employee before issuing the Smart Access Card.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and documents (e.g., employee ID, signature, etc.)
2. Fill in the form fields as follows:

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `number`, required: false): Enter the employee's ID number.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the employee's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the employee's last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the employee's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the employee's phone number.
* **Reason for Access** (`reason_for_access`, `text`, required: false): Briefly explain the reason for accessing the Smart Access Card.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `note`, required: false): Sign your signature to confirm you have read and understood the information.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you signed the form.
* **Time Signed** (`time_signed`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you signed the form.
* **Employee/Contractor/Visitor** (`employee_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether you are an Employee, Contractor, or Visitor.
* **Access Level** (`access_level`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the access level (High, Medium, Low).
* **Has Access Card** (`has_access_card`, `select_one`, required: false): Confirm if you have an Access Card.
* **Signature Confirmation** (`signature_confirmation`, `select_one`, required: false): Confirm if you have signed the form.
* **Other Reason for Access** (`reason_for_access_other`, `text`, required: false): Enter an alternative reason for accessing the Smart Access Card.
* **Other Reason for Access Description** (`reason_for_access_other_description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description for the other reason.
* **Other Reason for Access Note** (`reason_for_access_other_note`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional notes for the other reason.
* **Other Has Access Card** (`has_access_card_other`, `select_one`, required: false): Confirm if you have an Access Card for the other reason.

## Tips
- Double-check all fields to ensure you have completed them correctly.
- If you need more information or have questions, don't hesitate to ask your supervisor or HR representative.
