# pet_microchip_study_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Microchip Study Consent Form is designed to gather information from pet owners for research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the pet owner's details, including their name and contact information.
2. Select the relevant details about your pet, including its microchip details, breed, color, size, and weight.
3. Select the correct option for the pet owner's consent to the study.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Owners Details** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): This section is where you can enter your name and contact information.
* **Pet Microchip Details** (`2`, `number`, required/optional): Please enter your pet's microchip details, if applicable.
* **Pet Owner Consent** (`3`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select one of the three options for pet owner consent.
* **Pet Breed** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose the breed(s) of your pet.
* **Pet Color** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose the color(s) of your pet.
* **Pet Size** (`6`, `number`, required/optional): Please enter your pet's size.
* **Pet Weight** (`7`, `number`, required/optional): Please enter your pet's weight.
* **Owner Signature** (`8`, `note`, required/optional): Please sign your name to confirm your participation.
* **Date of Signature** (`9`, `date`, required/optional): Please enter the date you signed the form.
* **Time of Signature** (`10`, `time`, required/optional): Please enter the time you signed the form.
* **Email Address** (`11`, `email`, required/optional): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`12`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your phone number.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the relevant authorities for guidance.
* The information collected will be used for research purposes only and will not be shared with any third parties.
