# Adventure Activity Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather participant information, medical conditions, and emergency contact details for adventure activities. It helps organizers and staff assess potential risks and ensure participant safety.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Enter Participant Full Name**: Enter the full name of the participant, as it appears on their official identification documents.
2.  **Enter Participant Age**: Enter the participant's age to ensure they are eligible for the activity.
3.  **Enter Date of Birth**: Enter the participant's date of birth to help assess their medical history.
4.  **Enter Parent/Guardian Name**: If the participant is under 18, enter the name of their parent or guardian.
5.  **Enter Parent/Guardian Contact**: Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
6.  **Enter Activity Name**: Enter the name of the adventure activity the participant will be participating in.
7.  **Enter Activity Date**: Enter the date of the activity.
8.  **Enter Activity Location**: Enter the location where the activity will take place.
9.  **Acknowledge Activity Risks**: Select "Yes" to acknowledge the inherent risks of this activity.
10. **List Medical Conditions**: Select any applicable medical conditions that may affect participation.
11.  **List Medications Currently Taken**: List any medications the participant is currently taking.
12.  **List Allergies**: List any allergies the participant has.
13.  **Enter Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact.
14.  **Enter Emergency Contact Relationship**: Enter the relationship to the participant (e.g., parent, guardian, friend, etc.).
15.  **Enter Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
16.  **Give Permission and Consent**: Select "Yes" to give permission and consent for the participant to engage in this activity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Full Name**: The full name of the participant, as it appears on their official identification documents.
*   **Participant Age**: The age of the participant to ensure they are eligible for the activity.
*   **Date of Birth**: The participant's date of birth to help assess their medical history.
*   **Parent/Guardian Name**: The full name of the parent or guardian if the participant is under 18.
*   **Parent/Guardian Contact**: The phone number of the parent or guardian.
*   **Activity Name**: The name of the adventure activity the participant will be participating in.
*   **Activity Date**: The date of the activity.
*   **Activity Location**: The location where the activity will take place.
*   **Acknowledge Activity Risks**: A confirmation that the participant acknowledges the inherent risks of this activity.
*   **Medical Conditions**: A list of medical conditions that may affect participation.
*   **Medications Currently Taken**: A list of any medications the participant is currently taking.
*   **Allergies**: A list of any allergies the participant has.
*   **Emergency Contact Name**: The name of the emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Relationship**: The relationship to the participant.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone**: The phone number of the emergency contact.
*   **Permission and Consent**: A confirmation that the participant gives permission and consent to engage in this activity.
