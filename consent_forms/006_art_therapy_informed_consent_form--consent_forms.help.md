# Art Therapy Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Therapy Informed Consent Form is a document that ensures clients provide their informed consent for the treatment and procedures during their art therapy sessions. It's a legal and ethical requirement that protects both the client and the therapist.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your full name clearly and accurately.
2. Enter your date of birth.
3. Write down the name of the therapist assigned to you.
4. Briefly describe your understanding of the art therapy process.
5. Indicate whether you understand the confidentiality policy (yes or no).
6. Choose whether you consent to storing your artwork at the clinic (yes or no).
7. Select whether you agree to permit photography of your artwork for clinical records (yes or no).
8. Provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact person.
9. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Art Therapy Clinic Informed Consent --**: This is a note field where you can provide any additional information about your understanding of the art therapy process.
* **Client Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Assigned Therapist Name**: Type in the name of the therapist assigned to you.
* **Understanding of Art Therapy**: Briefly describe your understanding of the art therapy process and how you see it helping you.
* **I understand the confidentiality policy**: Indicate whether you agree to keep all information shared during therapy sessions confidential, except where required by law.
* **Consent to Store Artwork**: Choose whether you consent to storing your artwork at the clinic for the duration of therapy.
* **Permission to photograph artwork for clinical records**: Select whether you permit the photography of your artwork for clinical records, without any personal identifiers.
* **Emergency Contact Name and Phone**: Provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact person who can be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Signature Date**: Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Tips
* Please take the time to carefully read and understand each field before completing the form.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, ask your therapist for clarification.
* This form is a requirement for art therapy sessions, and it's essential to complete it before starting treatment.
