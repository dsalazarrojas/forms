This form is used for temporary custody arrangements between two parents when one parent takes temporary physical and legal custody of their child. To be eligible for this form, both parents are usually required to be living in the same household or nearby. Before completing the form, ensure that you have discussed and agreed upon the temporary custody arrangement with all parties involved. This form is not for permanent custody arrangements or for situations where one parent is seeking legal separation or divorce custody.

# Temporary Custody Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document temporary custody arrangements between two parents for the benefit of all parties involved, including the child and any other caregivers. It is essential to review and agree upon this arrangement with your child's other parent before submitting this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide Parent 1 Information**: Enter the name, contact information, and address of the parent taking temporary custody (you).
2. **Provide Parent 2 Information**: Enter the name, contact information, and address of the other parent.
3. **Specify Child Custody Arrangement**: Briefly describe the arrangement for temporary custody, including any specific details about the child's care and supervision during this time.
4. **Set Custody Dates**: Enter the start and end dates of the temporary custody period.
5. **Specify Temporary Custody Period**: Choose the duration of the temporary custody period in days.
6. **Sign and Date the Form**: Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent 1 Information**: 
  • **Parent 1 Information (Parent 1's name, contact information, and address)**: This field is for the parent taking temporary custody. Enter your name, contact information (phone number and address), and any other relevant details.
* **Parent 2 Information**: 
  • **Parent 2 Information (Parent 2's name, contact information, and address)**: This field is for the other parent. Enter their name, contact information (phone number and address), and any other relevant details.
* **Child Custody Arrangement**: 
  • **Child Custody Arrangement**: Describe the agreement you have made with the other parent for temporary custody, including any specific rules and expectations for the child's care and supervision during this time.
* **Custody Start Date**: 
  • **Start Date of Temporary Custody**: Enter the date when temporary custody will begin. Use the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Custody End Date**: 
  • **End Date of Temporary Custody**: Enter the date when temporary custody will end. Use the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Temporary Custody Period**: 
  • **Duration of Temporary Custody**: Choose the number of days you are requesting for temporary custody.
* **Parent 1 Signature**: 
  • **Agreement from Parent 1**: Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement with the other parent for temporary custody.
* **Parent 2 Signature**: 
  • **Agreement from Parent 2**: Sign and date the form to confirm their agreement with you for temporary custody.

## Tips

* Always review and agree upon this arrangement with the child's other parent before submitting this form.
* This form is not for permanent custody arrangements or situations where one parent is seeking legal separation or divorce custody.
* Keep a copy of this form for your records and for the child's records.
