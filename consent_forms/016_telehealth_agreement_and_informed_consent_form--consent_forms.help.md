# telehealth_agreement_and_informed_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The telehealth agreement and informed consent form is a patient-facing form designed to collect patient information, agreement, and signature for telehealth services. It is essential for patients to understand and agree to the terms of telehealth services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field accurately and thoroughly. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
2. Review the telehealth agreement terms to understand the terms and conditions of service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, date of birth, and address.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required/optional): Provide your contact information, including your phone number and email address.
* **Telehealth Agreement** (`telehealth_agreement`, text, required/optional): Read and agree to the terms of telehealth services.
* **Informed Consent** (`informed_consent`, text, required/optional): Understand and agree to the informed consent terms.
* **Patient Understanding** (`patient_understanding`, text, required/optional): Confirm your understanding of telehealth services.
* **Electronic Signature** (`electronic_signature`, text, required/optional): Sign electronically to confirm your consent.
* **Date of Service** (`date_of_service`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you will receive telehealth services.
* **Time of Service** (`time_of_service`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you will receive telehealth services.
* **Service Location** (`service_location`, text, required/optional): Specify the location where you will receive telehealth services.
* **Telehealth Provider** (`telehealth_provider`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your telehealth provider.
* **Telehealth Agreement Terms** (`telehealth_agreement_terms`, text, required/optional): Review the telehealth agreement terms.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required/optional): Provide an emergency contact's name and phone number.
* **Contact Information Agreement** (`contact_information_agreement`, text, required/optional): Agree to allow contact from your telehealth provider.
* **Consent Signature Date** (`consent_signature_date`, date, required/optional): Sign the consent date.
* **Electronic Signature** (`electronic_signature`, text, required/optional): Sign electronically.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the current date.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter the current time.
* **Telehealth Provider Signature** (`telehealth_provider_signature`, text, required/optional): Sign as your telehealth provider.

## Tips
* Ensure you have read and understood all the terms and conditions before completing the form.
* Review each field carefully to avoid errors.
* Ask your telehealth provider if you have any questions or concerns about the form.
