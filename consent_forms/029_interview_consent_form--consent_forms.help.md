# Interview Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Interview Consent Form is used to collect data for consent purposes in an interview setting. It captures essential information from the interviewee, which can be used to verify their consent and ensure transparency throughout the interview process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the interviewee's name.
* Enter their email address.
* Provide their phone number.
* Choose a date for the consent.
* Select the start and end time for the interview.
* Specify the purpose of consent.
* Enter the name of the interviewer.
* Add any additional notes about the interview.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consent Form**: This is the main section of the form that captures the interviewee's consent.
*   **Interviewee Name**: Enter the name of the person giving consent.
*   **Interviewee Email**: Enter the email address of the person giving consent.
*   **Interviewee Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person giving consent.
*   **Date of Consent**: Select the date on which the consent is given.
*   **Start Time**: Select the start time of the interview.
*   **End Time**: Select the end time of the interview.
*   **Purpose of Consent**: Specify the purpose or reason for the consent.
*   **Interviewer**: Enter the name of the person conducting the interview.
*   **Notes**: Add any additional notes about the interview. 

## Tips

* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting.
* Double-check the entered information for accuracy.
* Ensure the form is completed for the correct interview and purpose.
* Store the form securely and confidentially to protect the interviewee's data.
