# virtual_meeting_recording_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect consent from participants for virtual meeting recordings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the meeting name in the "meeting_name" field.
2. Provide the meeting link in the "meeting_link" field.
3. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your consent to be recorded in the "recording_type" field.
4. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your consent to your recording being shared in the "recording_purpose" field.
5. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your consent to participate in the meeting in the "participant_permission" field.
6. Enter your name in the "name" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
8. Enter your contact number in the "contact_number" field.
9. Enter your company in the "company" field.
10. Enter your job title in the "job_title" field.
11. Enter your department in the "department" field.
12. Enter your team in the "team" field.
13. Enter the meeting ID in the "meeting_id" field.
14. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have given consent in the "consent_type" field.
15. Sign your name in the "signature" field.
16. Enter the date and time of your signature in the "signature_date" and "signature_time" fields.
17. Enter the label for the submit button in the "submit_button_label" field.
18. Enter the text to be displayed on the submit button in the "submit_button_text" field.
19. Enter the form submission in the "form_submission" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **meeting_name** (2, text, required): Enter the name of the meeting.
* **meeting_link** (3, text, optional): Enter the link to the meeting.
* **recording_type** (4, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to indicate you consent to be recorded.
* **recording_purpose** (5, select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" to indicate you consent to your recording being shared.
* **participant_permission** (6, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to indicate your consent to participate in the meeting.
* **name** (8, text, required): Enter your name.
* **email** (9, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **contact_number** (10, text, required): Enter your contact number.
* **company** (11, text, required): Enter your company name.
* **job_title** (12, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **department** (13, text, required): Enter your department.
* **team** (14, text, required): Enter your team name.
* **meeting_id** (15, text, required): Enter the meeting ID.
* **consent_type** (20, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to indicate you have given consent.
* **signature** (21, text, required): Sign your name.
* **signature_date** (22, date, required): Enter the date of your signature.
* **signature_time** (23, time, required): Enter the time of your signature.
* **submit_button_label** (24, text, required): Enter the label for the submit button.
* **submit_button_text** (25, text, required): Enter the text to be displayed on the submit button.
* **form_submission** (26, text, required): Enter the form submission.
