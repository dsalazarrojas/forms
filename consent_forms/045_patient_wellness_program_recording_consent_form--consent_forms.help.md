# Patient Wellness Program Recording Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Patient Wellness Program Recording Consent Form is a tool for healthcare professionals to record patient information and obtain their consent for the patient wellness program.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the **Patient Name** field.
2. Provide the patient's email address in the **Patient Email** field.
3. Enter the patient's phone number in the **Patient Phone** field.
4. Select the date of the patient's session in the **Session Date** field using the date picker.
5. Select the time of the patient's session in the **Session Time** field using the time picker.
6. Choose from the list whether you have the patient's **Recording Permission**. Select "Yes" if you have their permission, and "No" otherwise.
7. Enter the location where the patient's recording will be taken in the **Recording Location** field.
8. Select the type of recording you will be doing (e.g. video, voice, or both) from the **Recording Mode** dropdown menu.
9. If you have obtained the patient's consent, enter their consent statement in the **Recording Consent** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Patient Email**: Enter the patient's email address where they can be reached for further information or communication.
* **Patient Phone**: Enter the patient's phone number where they can be contacted.
* **Session Date**: Select the date of the patient's session using the date picker. This is the date when the recording will be taken.
* **Session Time**: Select the time of the patient's session using the time picker. This is the time when the recording will be taken.
* **Recording Permission**: This field is used to indicate whether you have obtained the patient's permission for the recording. Select "Yes" if you have their permission, and "No" otherwise.
* **Recording Location**: Enter the location where the recording will be taken, such as a specific room or area.
* **Recording Mode**: This field is used to select the type of recording you will be doing. You can select from video, voice, or both.
* **Recording Consent**: If you have obtained the patient's consent, enter their consent statement in this field. This is an optional field and is used to document their agreement to be recorded.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have obtained the patient's consent before filling out this form.
* Review internal policies and regulations regarding patient data collection and storage.
* Use clear and concise language when interacting with patients.
* Keep the patient's information confidential and secure.
