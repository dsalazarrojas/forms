# educational_data_ethics_research_application_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from researchers applying for educational data ethics research approval. It helps the review committee to assess the research proposal, ensuring that it meets the necessary standards and guidelines for ethical data use.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form completely and accurately, following the instructions for each field.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Provide detailed descriptions for each field where necessary.
4. Double-check your responses before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details**: Please enter your name and contact information.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your contact information, including name, email, and institution.
* **Study Details**: Describe your research study in one or more sentences.
* **What is your research question?**: Describe your research question in one or more sentences.
* **Research Description**: Provide a detailed description of your research.
* **Which institution(s) is/are you affiliated with?**: Choose all the institutions involved in your research.
* **Who is on your research team?**: List team members involved in your research.
* **What is your research methodology?**: Describe your research methodology in detail.
* **What data will you be using?**: Choose the data set(s) you will be using or specify other (please describe).
* **What will you do with the data collected?**: Select how you will use the data (e.g., analyze and publish, share with partners, etc.).
* **What will you do with the data collected? (Cont.)**: Describe how you will keep the data secure.
* **How will you manage institutional review?**: Describe the process for managing institutional review.
* **How will you obtain ethics committee review?**: Describe the process for obtaining ethics committee review.
* **What is your expected timeline?**: Enter your expected completion date.
* **By when do you plan to submit this application?**: Enter the date you plan to submit your application.
* **How will you manage the review process?**: Describe your plan for managing the review process.
* **What are the decision criteria?**: Describe the criteria for making decisions.
* **Has your research been approved?**: Select yes or no.
* **If approved, what was the approval date?**: Enter the approval date.
* **By whom was it approved?**: Select the person who approved your research.
* **Any approval notes?**: Enter any notes from the reviewer.
* **Any other notes for the reviewer?**: Enter any additional notes.
* **Any additional comments?**: Enter any additional comments from reviewers.

By following this guide, you should be able to complete the form accurately and efficiently. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
