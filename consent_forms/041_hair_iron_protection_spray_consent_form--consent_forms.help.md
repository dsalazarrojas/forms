# Hair Iron Protection Spray Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect client information and consent for hair iron protection spray treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully to ensure you have filled out all required information.
2. Fill out the "Client Authorization" field with your name and contact information.
3. Choose the correct "Contact Details" field that best describes how you can be reached.
4. Select "I agree" in the "Signature" field to indicate your consent for the treatment.
5. Fill out the "Date of Service" field with the date of your appointment.
6. Fill out the "Time of Service" field with the time of your appointment.
7. The "Stylist Signature" field is for the stylist to sign and date.
8. The "Salon Signature" field is for the salon representative to sign and date.
9. Fill out the "Date of Birth" field with your date of birth.
10. Fill out the "Client Address" field with your address.
11. Fill out the "Client Notes" field with any additional information you'd like to provide.
12. Fill out the "Stylist Notes" field with any additional information for the stylist.
13. Fill out the "Signature Date" field with the date of your signature.
14. Fill out the "Signature Time" field with the time of your signature.
15. Select the correct "Date of Service" field from the options (Today or Yesterday).
16. Select "I agree" in the "Client Signature" field to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Authorization** (`client_authorization`, `text`, required): This field is for you to enter your name and contact information.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose how you can be contacted (e.g. phone, email, in-person).
* **Signature** (`signature`, `select_one`, optional): Select "I agree" to indicate your consent for the treatment.
* **Date of Service** (`date_of_service`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your appointment.
* **Time of Service** (`time_of_service`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of your appointment.
* **Stylist Signature** (`stylist_signature`, `note`, optional): This field is for the stylist to sign and date.
* **Salon Signature** (`salon_signature`, `select_one`, optional): This field is for the salon representative to sign and date.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Client Address** (`client_address`, `text`, optional): Enter your address.
* **Client Notes** (`client_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information you'd like to provide.
* **Stylist Notes** (`stylist_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information for the stylist.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your signature.
* **Signature Time** (`signature_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of your signature.
* **Date of Service** (`date_of_service`, `select_one`, optional): Select the date of your service (Today or Yesterday).
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, `select_one`, optional): Select "I agree" to confirm your consent.
