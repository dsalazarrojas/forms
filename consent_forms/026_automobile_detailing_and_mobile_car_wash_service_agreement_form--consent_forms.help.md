# Automobile Detailing And Mobile Car Wash Service Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Automobile Detailing And Mobile Car Wash Service Agreement Form is designed to collect information from clients for an automobile detailing and mobile car wash service. This form is used to ensure that the client's vehicle is properly prepared and serviced according to their preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your client name, email address, and phone number in the respective fields.
2. Provide your vehicle's year, make, model, color, and license plate number.
3. Select the type of vehicle you are booking the service for (Sedan, SUV, Truck, Van, Motorcycle, or Other).
4. Choose the type of service you prefer (Basic Wash, Premium Detailing, Full Interior Detail, Full Exterior Detail, Complete Detail Package, Custom Service).
5. Select a date and time for the service.
6. Choose an estimated duration for the service (1 hour, 2 hours, 3 hours, 4 hours, 5+ hours).
7. If applicable, select any additional services you would like to add (Interior Vacuum, Carpet Shampoo, Upholstery Cleaning, Window Tinting, Ceramic Coating, Paint Protection, Odor Removal).
8. Rate the current exterior and interior condition of your vehicle (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor).
9. Finally, confirm that you agree to the service terms and pricing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Service Address** (`service_address`, required): Enter the location where the service will be performed (e.g., home, office, etc.).
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, required): Enter the year of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, required): Enter the make of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, required): Enter the model of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Color** (`vehicle_color`, optional): Enter the color of your vehicle.
* **License Plate Number** (`license_plate`, optional): Enter the license plate number of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, required): Select the type of vehicle you are booking the service for (Sedan, SUV, Truck, Van, Motorcycle, or Other).
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, required): Choose the type of service you prefer (Basic Wash, Premium Detailing, Full Interior Detail, Full Exterior Detail, Complete Detail Package, Custom Service).
* **Preferred Service Date** (`service_date`, required): Select the date you would like to schedule the service.
* **Preferred Service Time** (`service_time`, optional): Select a time for the service (if applicable).
* **Estimated Service Duration** (`service_duration`, required): Choose the estimated duration for the service (1 hour, 2 hours, 3 hours, 4 hours, 5+ hours).
* **Additional Services** (`add_on_services`, optional): Select any additional services you would like to add (Interior Vacuum, Carpet Shampoo, Upholstery Cleaning, Window Tinting, Ceramic Coating, Paint Protection, Odor Removal).
* **Current Exterior Condition** (`exterior_condition`, required): Rate the current exterior condition of your vehicle (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor).
* **Current Interior Condition** (`interior_condition`, required): Rate the current interior condition of your vehicle (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor).
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, required): This field will be auto-filled based on your service selections.
* **I Agree to the Service Terms and Pricing** (`payment_agreement`, required): Confirm that you agree to the service terms and pricing.
