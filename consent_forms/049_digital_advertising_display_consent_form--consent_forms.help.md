# Digital Advertising Display Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent for the display of digital advertisements. It is a mandatory form that must be completed by all users to ensure that they are aware of and agree to the terms of digital advertising display. The form is used to collect information such as the user's category, description, date of birth, time of birth, email, phone number, and consent to the display of digital advertisements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant category from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter a brief description of yourself.
3. Enter your date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy` (e.g., 12/25/1999).
4. Enter your time of birth (HH:MM 24-hour format, e.g., 14:30).
5. Enter your email address.
6. Enter your phone number.
7. Select all the consent options that apply to you.
8. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **category**: A dropdown menu that asks you to select the category you fall under. This is a required field.
  Please select the correct category that best describes you. This will help us understand your preferences and tailor the advertising experience to your needs.
* **description**: A text field that asks for a brief description of yourself.
  Enter a short description of yourself, such as a few sentences that describe your interests or hobbies.
* **date_of_birth**: A date field that asks for your date of birth.
  Enter your date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy` (e.g., 12/25/1999).
* **time_of_birth**: A time field that asks for your time of birth.
  Enter your time of birth in 24-hour format (HH:MM), such as 14:30.
* **email**: An email field that asks for your email address.
  Enter your email address that you use to receive emails and notifications.
* **phone**: A text field that asks for your phone number.
  Enter your phone number that you use to receive calls and text messages.
* **consent**: A multi-select field that asks for your consent to the display of digital advertisements.
  Select all the options that apply to you:
  + Option 1: You are comfortable with being displayed as an example in our ads.
  + Option 2: You are interested in participating in our focus groups and surveys.
  + Option 3: You are willing to receive promotional emails from us.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us.
