# Animation Workshop Publicity Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent for the use of participant's images, videos, and names for various purposes such as organization website, social media posts, email newsletters, printed promotional materials, and more. The form helps to ensure that participants understand how their content will be used and shared.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide your workshop information, including the workshop name, date, and session you are attending.
3. Answer the questions regarding photography and video recording permissions.
4. Specify how you would like your images and videos to be used and shared.
5. Choose your social media sharing preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: Your full name as a participant of the workshop.
* **Email Address**: Your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Workshop Name**: The name of the workshop you are attending.
* **Workshop Date**: The date of the workshop you are attending.
* **Workshop Session or Section**: The session or section of the workshop you are attending.
* **Instructor Name**: The name of the instructor leading the workshop.
* **Photography Permissions**: Choose if you consent to being photographed during the workshop.
* **Video Recording Permissions**: Choose if you consent to being filmed or recorded during the workshop.
* **Social Media Sharing**: Choose if you consent to having your content shared on social media.
* **Tagged Permission**: Choose if you consent to being tagged or mentioned in posts.
* **Credit Permission**: Choose if you consent to having your name used for credit.
* **Commercial Use**: Choose if you consent to having your content used for commercial purposes.
* **Future Use**: Choose if you consent to having your content used beyond this workshop.
* **Parental Consent (if under 18)**: If you are under 18, provide the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Name**: Full name of the parent or guardian.
