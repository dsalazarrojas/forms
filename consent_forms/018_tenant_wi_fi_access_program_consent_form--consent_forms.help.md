# Tenant Wi-Fi Access Program Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for tenants to provide information and consent for access to the Tenant Wi-Fi Access Program. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your Property Name in the **Property Name** field.
2. Enter your Property Address in the **Property Address** field.
3. Enter your Tenant Name in the **Tenant Name** field.
4. Enter your Tenant Email in the **Tenant Email** field.
5. Enter your Date of Birth in the **Date of Birth** field (in the format MM/DD/YYYY).
6. Enter your Phone number in the **Phone** field.
7. Enter your Network Name in the **Network Name** field.
8. Enter your Router Password in the **Router Password** field.
9. Enter the date you would like to connect to the program in the **Connection Date** field (in the format MM/DD/YYYY).
10. Sign and date your **Tenant Signature** to confirm you have read and agree to the terms of the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the property where you reside.
*   **Property Address** (text, required): Enter the address of the property where you reside.
*   **Tenant Name** (text, required): Enter your name as it is listed on your lease agreement.
*   **Tenant Email** (email, required): Enter your email address as it is listed on your lease agreement.
*   **Date of Birth** (date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number as it is listed on your lease agreement.
*   **Network Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the network you wish to connect to (e.g. Network 1, Network 2, etc.).
*   **Router Password** (text, required): Enter the password for the router on your network.
*   **Connection Date** (date, required): Enter the date you would like to connect to the program in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Tenant Signature** (select one, required): This field is for the tenant to sign and date confirming they have read and agree to the terms of the program.
    *   Option 1: I have read and agree to the terms of the program.
    *   Option 2: I have not read the terms of the program and do not agree to participate.
 
## Tips
*   Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
*   If you are having trouble with the form, please contact your property manager or landlord for assistance.
*   This form is required for all tenants participating in the program.
*   Please note that the connection date you enter will be used to determine when you will start receiving service.
