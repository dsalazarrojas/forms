# Music Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music Release Form is a tool for artists to provide information about their music releases. Fill out this form to share your music with the world.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "artist_name" field with your name as the artist.
2. Enter the "song_title" field with the title of your song.
3. In the "song_description" field, provide a brief description of your song.
4. In the "release_date" field, select the date you plan to release your song.
5. If applicable, enter any credits (e.g. producer, mixer, etc.) in the "artist_credit" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Artist Name**: <artist_name> (text, optional): Enter your name as the artist.
  - **Song Title**: <song_title> (text, optional): Enter the title of your song.
  - **Song Description**: <song_description> (text, optional): Briefly describe your song.
  - **Release Date**: <release_date> (date, optional): Select the date you plan to release your song.
  - **Artist Credit**: <artist_credit> (text, optional): If applicable, list any credits associated with your release (e.g. producer, mixer, etc.).

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check the information before submitting the form.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, feel free to contact form@gic.mx for assistance.
