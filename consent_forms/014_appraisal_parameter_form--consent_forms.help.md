# Appraisal Parameter Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Appraisal Parameter Form is designed to gather detailed information about a property's characteristics and condition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information about the property and the appraiser.
2.  Fill in the Appraiser Information section with your name and contact details.
3.  Enter the Appraiser's name in the Appraiser Name field.
4.  Enter the Appraiser's License Number in the Appraiser License Number field.
5.  Move on to the Property Parameters section.
6.  Enter the Subject Property Address in the Property Address field.
7.  Enter the Lot Size in Square Feet in the Lot Size field (if applicable).
8.  Enter the Building Size in Square Feet in the Building Size field (if applicable).
9.  Enter the Year Built in the Year Built field (if applicable).
10. Enter the Number of Bedrooms and Number of Bathrooms in their respective fields (if applicable).
11.  Select the Property Condition from the dropdown list.
12. Enter the Zoning Classification in the Zoning field (if applicable).
13. Select any available Utilities from the dropdown list in the Utilities field (if applicable).
14. Finally, enter any Special Features or Improvements in the Special Features field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Appraiser Information** (note): This section is where you enter your name and contact details as the person completing the appraisal.
* **Appraiser Name** (`text`, required): Enter the name of the person completing the appraisal.
* **Appraiser License Number** (`text`, required): Enter your professional license number as an appraiser.
* **Property Parameters** (note): This section contains information about the property's characteristics and condition.
* **Subject Property Address** (`text`, required): Enter the address of the property being appraised.
* **Lot Size** (`number`, optional): Enter the size of the property lot in square feet (if applicable).
* **Building Size** (`number`, optional): Enter the size of the property building in square feet (if applicable).
* **Year Built** (`number`, optional): Enter the year the property was built (if applicable).
* **Number of Bedrooms** (`number`, optional): Enter the number of bedrooms in the property (if applicable).
* **Number of Bathrooms** (`number`, optional): Enter the number of bathrooms in the property (if applicable).
* **Property Condition** (`select_one`, required): Select the condition of the property from the dropdown list (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Zoning Classification** (`text`, optional): Enter the zoning classification of the property (if applicable).
* **Utilities Available** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select any available utilities from the dropdown list (Electric, Gas, Water, Sewer, Internet).
* **Special Features or Improvements** (`text`, optional): Enter any special features or improvements of the property (if applicable).

## Tips
* Be sure to enter accurate and relevant information about the property and the appraiser.
* If a field is not applicable, you can leave it blank or note the reason in the field.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
