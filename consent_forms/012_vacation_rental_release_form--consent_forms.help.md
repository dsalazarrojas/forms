# Vacation Rental Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a general information gathering form for guests or renters providing their contact and emergency contact information, and agreement to the rental terms.

## How To Complete This Form
Please fill out this form with accurate and clear information. The form is divided into several sections for your convenience. Start by filling out the Guest Information section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Information**: Provide your personal details, such as your full name, contact number, and email address.
* **Agreement**: Select one of the three provided options to agree with the rental terms. Please note that this field might not be required but is crucial for us to know your stance on the rental terms.
* **Rental Terms**: This is the place where you can provide any additional information about the rental terms you are willing to agree with. If you are unsure, please refer to the main rental agreement document provided to you by the rental agency.
* **Rental Process**: Describe how you would like the rental process to take place, such as payment terms, check-out time, and any other specific requests.
* **Contact Information**: Provide contact details of the person responsible for handling emergencies or important messages regarding the rental.
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Relationship**: Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person, such as family member, neighbor, or colleague.
