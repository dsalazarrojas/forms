# Automotive Model Release Test Drive Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Automotive Model Release Test Drive Form is used to gather information from potential test drivers who wish to participate in test drives of new vehicles. This form helps the dealership understand the participant's contact information, vehicle preferences, and test drive details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant's information: Enter your full name, email address, phone number, and date of birth.
2. Provide address information: Enter your current street address, city, state, and zip code.
3. Provide driver's license information: Enter your driver's license number, state, and expiration date.
4. Select a vehicle model you are interested in driving: Choose from the available options (Sedan, SUV, Truck, Crossover, Sports Car, or Other).
5. Select a preferred test drive date and time: Choose from the available options (Morning, Afternoon, Late Afternoon, or Flexible).
6. Confirm your insurance status and consent to the test drive: Answer whether you have valid auto insurance and agree to follow all dealership and vehicle rules.
7. Sign the consent form: This is a required step to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Information
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it should be on your driver's license.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.

### Address Information
* **Street Address**: Enter your current street address.
* **City**: Enter the city where you reside.
* **State**: Enter the state where you reside.
* **Zip Code**: Enter your zip code.

### Driver's License Information
* **Driver License Number**: Enter the last 4 digits of your driver's license number for security purposes.
* **Driver License State**: Enter the state where you obtained your driver's license.
* **Driver License Expiration Date**: Enter the date your driver's license expires.

### Vehicle Information
* **Model Interested in Driving**: Choose a vehicle model you are interested in driving (Sedan, SUV, Truck, Crossover, Sports Car, or Other).
* **Specific Model Year**: If desired, enter the specific model year of the vehicle you are interested in driving.
* **Preferred Test Drive Date**: Choose a date you are available to test drive the vehicle.
* **Preferred Time**: Choose a preferred time for the test drive (Morning, Afternoon, Late Afternoon, or Flexible).

### Consenting and Release Information
* **I have valid auto insurance**: Answer whether you have valid auto insurance.
* **I agree to follow all dealership and vehicle rules**: Agree to follow all rules set by the dealership and vehicle manufacturer.
* **I release the dealership from liability during test drive**: Accept or decline liability during the test drive.
* **I consent to photos or video during test drive for promotional use**: Accept or decline to be photographed or recorded during the test drive.

### Final Step
* **Date of Consent**: Sign this form to confirm your agreement.
