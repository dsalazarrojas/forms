# medical_and_media_release_form_mission_trip_participant_manual - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for mission trip participants to provide medical and media release information. It is used to gather the necessary permissions and agreements before allowing participants to engage in media and medical activities related to the mission trip.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each field explanation to understand what information is required.
2. Fill in the necessary information for each field according to the explanations provided.
3. Submit the completed form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical and Media Release** (`1`, `text`, required: `false`): This is where you can enter any medical and media release information, such as consent to participate in medical procedures or media releases for any photo or video recordings.
* **Behavior Parameters** (`2`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is for any specific behavior parameters or guidelines that participants should adhere to during the mission trip.
* **Consent** (`3`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Choose one of the three options to indicate your consent to participate in certain activities, such as media and medical releases.
	+ Option 1: I consent to participate in media and medical releases.
	+ Option 2: I do not consent to participate in media and medical releases.
	+ Option 3: I consent to participate in certain activities, but only if accompanied by a chaperone.
* **Off-Campus Event Consent** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Check all that apply for any off-campus events you wish to participate in, such as events with media presence or medical activities.
