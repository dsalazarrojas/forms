# Subcontractor Lien Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Subcontractor Lien Release Form is used to record the release of liens on a construction project. A lien release is a document that confirms the release of a contractor's or subcontractor's lien on a property or asset.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required information for each field. The form has six sections: General Information, Lien Details, Release Information, Signature Block, Notes, and Contact Information. Complete each section as follows:
1. General Information: Enter the name of the subcontractor releasing the lien.
2. Lien Details: Enter the details of the lien being released.
3. Release Information: Enter the date and conditions of the release.
4. Signature Block: Sign and date the document.
5. Notes: Enter any additional comments or notes about the release.
6. Contact Information: Enter the contact information of the individual releasing the lien.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`name`: General Information, `type`: text, required): Enter the name of the subcontractor releasing the lien. This is typically the name of the company or individual releasing the lien.
* **Lien Details** (`name`: Lien Details, `type`: text, required): Enter the details of the lien being released, such as the lien amount or the property address associated with the lien.
* **Release Information** (`name`: Release Information, `type`: text, required): Enter the date and conditions of the release, including the date the lien was released and any conditions that must be met for the release to be valid.
* **Signature Block** (`name`: Signature, `type`: text, required): Sign and date the document with your name and title/position.
* **Notes** (`name`: Notes, `type`: text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the release.
* **Contact Information** (`name`: Contact Information, `type`: text, optional): Enter the contact information of the individual releasing the lien, including their name, title, and contact details.
