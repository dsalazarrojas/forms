# Residency Application Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is part of the residency application process. It's designed to collect your consent for data sharing and collection. Please fill it out accurately to ensure a smooth application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

* Start by carefully reading the form and its instructions to understand what each question means.
* Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Residency Application Consent Form (1)**: This is the main section of the form where you will be asked to give your consent for data sharing and collection. Please enter 'True' if you consent, and 'False' if you do not.
* **Share Your Contact Info (2)**: Please provide your contact information so that we can get in touch with you regarding your application.
* **Consent To Data Collection (3)**: This question is asking for your consent to collect data about you. Please choose 'True' if you consent to data collection and 'False' if you do not.
* **Consent To Data Storage (4)**: This question is asking where you want your data to be stored. Choose 'In the cloud' or 'Locally only'.
* **Share With Other Departments (5)**: This question is asking if you want to share your application with other departments. Please choose 'True' or 'False' according to your preference. 

## Tips
* Please fill out the form carefully and accurately.
* If you're unsure about any question, don't hesitate to reach out to the relevant authorities.
* This form is not a duplication of any other form, and it's a unique document designed specifically for this purpose.
* Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
