# Devotional Reminder Signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect your personal details for sending devotional reminders. We will use this information to send personalized reminders to you via your preferred communication channel (email or phone).

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the text field labeled "First Name".
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the text field labeled "Last Name".
3. Provide your **Email** address in the format of `yourname@example.com`.
4. If you have a phone number, enter it in the text field labeled "Phone".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents. This will help us identify you accurately.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter a valid email address where you can be reached for reminders. We will use this email to send you personalized reminders.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it here to receive reminders via a phone call or SMS.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your email address for accuracy to avoid missed reminders.
* If you have any issues with your submitted information, do not hesitate to contact us.
* If you're unsure about your first and last name, please ask your local authority for verification.
