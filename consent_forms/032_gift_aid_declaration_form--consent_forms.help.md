# Gift Aid Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to declare gifts to a charity for tax relief in the UK. It collects information from donors, including their personal details, donation details, and declaration dates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your name, address, postcode, and contact information (email and phone number).
2. Provide your charity's name and number (if applicable).
3. Enter the date you declared your gift (Gift Aid Declaration Date).
4. Enter the start and end dates of your gift.
5. Enter the amount of your donation.
6. Confirm your pledge status (yes or no).
7. Sign the form to confirm your declaration.
