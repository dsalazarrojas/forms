# Co-Living Shared Resource Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather consent from residents of a co-living shared resource space. It ensures that all residents understand their responsibilities, liabilities, and expectations regarding shared facilities, utilities, and maintenance. Completing this form helps to maintain a harmonious and responsible community living environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, following the instructions for each field.
2. Make sure to provide your digital signature at the end of the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resident Full Name** (`resident_name`, Text, Required): Enter your full name as it is written on your identification.
* **Unit or Room Number** (`unit_number`, Text, Required): Enter the number of your unit or room assigned to you.
* **-- Shared Resource Agreement --** (`section_resource_agreement`, Note, Optional): This section explains the shared resource agreement, which outlines the rules and guidelines for using shared resources such as the kitchen, common spaces, and amenities.
* **I agree to follow the communal kitchen usage and cleaning guidelines** (`kitchen_guideline_ack`, Select One, Required): Check "I agree" if you agree to follow the kitchen usage and cleaning guidelines.
* **I consent to the fair-use policy for shared laundry facilities** (`laundry_policy_ack`, Select One, Required): Check "I agree" if you understand and agree to the fair-use policy for shared laundry facilities.
* **I agree to respect the designated quiet hours - 10 PM to 7 AM** (`quiet_hours_ack`, Select One, Required): Check "I agree" if you understand and will respect the quiet hours.
* **-- Utilities and Shared Costs --** (`section_costs`, Note, Optional): This section explains the billing process and payment terms for shared utilities.
* **I understand the shared utility billing process and payment terms** (`utility_billing_ack`, Select One, Required): Check "I understand" if you understand and agree with the billing process.
* **I agree to contribute to the monthly communal cleaning supplies fund** (`cleaning_supplies_fund_ack`, Select One, Required): Check "I agree" if you understand and agree to contribute to the fund.
* **-- Liability and Maintenance --** (`section_liability`, Note, Optional): This section explains the liability and maintenance responsibilities for shared spaces.
* **I understand I am responsible for any damage caused by me or my guests** (`damage_responsibility_ack`, Select One, Required): Check "I understand" if you agree with this responsibility.
* **I will report any maintenance issues in shared areas immediately** (`maintenance_reporting_ack`, Select One, Required): Check "I will" if you understand and agree to report maintenance issues.
* **Resident Digital Signature** (`resident_signature`, Text, Required): Enter your digital signature, as it is required to confirm your consent.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, Date, Required): Enter the date you signed this form.
