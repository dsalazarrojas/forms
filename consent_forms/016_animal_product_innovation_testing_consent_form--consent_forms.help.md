# Animal Product Innovation Testing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a consent form for animal product innovation testing. It ensures that participants and their owners understand the purpose, process, and confidentiality aspects of testing new animal products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Full Name** (`participant_full_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name.
* **Participant Email Address** (`participant_email`, email, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Product Category** (`product_category`, select_one, required): Please select the category that best describes the product being tested:
	+ Animal Feed or Nutrition
	+ Health Supplement
	+ Grooming and Hygiene
	+ Housing and Bedding
	+ Toys and Enrichment
* **Description of Innovation Being Tested** (`innovation_description`, text, required): Summarize the product being evaluated in a few sentences.
* **Expected Duration of Participation** (`participation_duration`, text, required): Enter the expected duration of participation, e.g. 2 weeks, 3 months.
* **Acknowledgment of Confidentiality** (`confidentiality_agreement`, select_one, required): Please select the option that best describes your agreement to maintain confidentiality:
	+ I agree to maintain confidentiality
	+ Other
* **Informed Consent for Testing** (`informed_consent_statement`, select_one, required): Please select the option that best describes your agreement to participate:
	+ I voluntarily agree to participate
	+ Other
* **Acknowledgment of Right to Withdraw** (`withdrawal_rights`, select_one, required): Please select the option that best describes your understanding of your right to withdraw:
	+ I understand I can withdraw at any time
	+ Other
* **Emergency Contact for Animal** (`animal_emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the name and phone number of the emergency contact person for the animal.
* **Date of Consent** (`consent_date`, date, required): Please select the date on which you are giving your consent.
