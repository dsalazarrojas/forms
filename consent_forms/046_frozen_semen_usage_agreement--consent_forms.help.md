# Frozen Sement Usage Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to establish a formal agreement between the owner and the recipient of Frozen Sement regarding its usage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the owner's name.
2. Enter the breeding permission date.
3. Provide the usage terms.
4. Fill in the recipient's name.
5. Specify who has breeding permission, who has usage permission, and if either is revoked.
6. Specify the status of breeding permission.
7. Add any notes about breeding permission.
8. Specify the status of usage permission.
9. Add any notes about usage permission.
10. Sign and date the agreement.
11. Fill in the recipient's informed consent version.
12. Enter the date of the recipient's informed consent.
13. Sign the recipient's informed consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text, required): Fill in the name of the owner of Frozen Sement.
* **Breeding Permission Date** (`breeding_permission_date`, date, required): Enter the date when breeding permission was granted.
* **Usage Terms** (`usage_terms`, text, required): Provide the terms of usage for Frozen Sement.
* **Recipient Name** (`recipient_name`, text, required): Fill in the name of the recipient of Frozen Sement.
* **Agreement Accepted** (`agreement_accepted`, select_one, required): Check the box to indicate that the agreement has been accepted.
* **Breeding Permission Given** (`breeding_permission_given`, select_multiple, required): Specify who has breeding permission (Owner, Recipient, Both).
* **Usage Permission Given** (`usage_permission_given`, select_multiple, required): Specify who has usage permission (Owner, Recipient, Both).
* **Breeding Permission Revocation** (`breeding_permission_revocation`, select_one, required): Check the box if breeding permission is revoked.
* **Breeding Permission Relevant Date** (`breeding_permission_relevant_date`, date, required): Enter the date when breeding permission was granted or relevant.
* **Recipient Informed Consent** (`recipient_informed_consent`, select_one, required): Check the box to indicate that the recipient has been informed.
* **Usage Terms Version** (`usage_terms_version`, select_one, required): Specify the version of usage terms.
* **Usage Terms Revision Date** (`usage_terms_revision_date`, date, required): Enter the date when usage terms were last revised.
* **Breeding Permission Status** (`breeding_permission_status`, text, required): Specify the status of breeding permission.
* **Breeding Permission Notes** (`breeding_permission_notes`, note, required): Add any notes about breeding permission.
* **Usage Permission Status** (`usage_permission_status`, text, required): Specify the status of usage permission.
* **Usage Permission Notes** (`usage_permission_notes`, note, required): Add any notes about usage permission.
* **Agreement Signed Date** (`agreement_signed_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the agreement was signed.
* **Agreement Signed By** (`agreement_signed_by`, text, required): Fill in the name of who signed the agreement.
* **Recipient Informed Consent Version** (`recipient_informed_consent_version`, select_one, required): Specify the version of informed consent.
* **Recipient Informed Consent Date** (`recipient_informed_consent_date`, date, required): Enter the date of informed consent.
* **Recipient Informed Consent Signature** (`recipient_informed_consent_signature`, text, required): Sign the informed consent.
