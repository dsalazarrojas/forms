# Fire Safety Drill Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Safety Drill Waiver Form is used to collect demographic information and obtain consent from parents or guardians of participants in a fire safety drill. This form is used to identify the participant, their emergency contact information, and the relationship between the participant and their emergency contact. This form also requires a signature from the parent or guardian, as well as the date of the signature.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant's information, including their name, emergency contact information, and relationship to their emergency contact.
2. Fill in the emergency contact's information, including their name, phone number, email, and relationship to the participant.
3. If applicable, fill in the parent or guardian's information, including their name, phone number, email, and relationship to the participant.
4. Sign and date the form, indicating that you have reviewed and agree to the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant in the fire safety drill.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Relationship to Participant**: Describe your relationship to the participant (e.g. mother, father, guardian, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the emergency contact.
* **Parent/Guardian Name (if applicable)**: Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
* **Relationship to Participant (if applicable)**: Describe your relationship to the participant (e.g. mother, father, guardian, etc.).
* **Parent/Guardian Phone (if applicable)**: Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent/Guardian Email (if applicable)**: Enter the email address of the parent or guardian.
* **Signature (Parent/Guardian)**: Sign and date the form, indicating that you have reviewed and agree to the information provided.
* **Date of Signature**: Enter the date you signed the form.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the participant's date of birth.
