# Academic Peer Review Publication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect consent from peer reviewers for the inclusion of their reviews or excerpts in academic publications.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete the form in its entirety to ensure that we have your consent to publish your review or excerpt.
2. Enter your full name in the "Reviewer name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Contact email" field.
4. If you consent to publish your review, select "Yes" in the "Consent to publish review" field.
5. If you prefer to anonymize your review, select "Yes" in the "Request to anonymize review" field.
6. If there are any restrictions on the use of your review, enter them in the "Restrictions on use" field.
7. Sign your full name in the "Signature confirmation" field to confirm your agreement.
8. Enter the date in the "Date of consent" field to mark the date when consent was given.
9. Enter any additional notes or details in the "Additional notes" field if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reviewer name** (Reviewer name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your passport or official ID.
* **Contact email** (Contact email, email, required): Enter your email address where we can contact you regarding publication permissions.
* **Consent to publish review** (Consent to publish review, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if you consent to publish your review or excerpt.
* **Request to anonymize review** (Request to anonymize review, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you prefer to anonymize your review or excerpt.
* **Restrictions on use** (Restrictions on use, text, optional): Indicate any limitations on the use of your review or excerpt.
* **Signature confirmation** (Signature confirmation, text, required): Sign your full name to confirm your agreement.
* **Date of consent** (Date of consent, date, required): Enter the date when consent was given.
* **Additional notes** (Additional notes, text, optional): Enter any other clarifying details or comments about your review or excerpt.
