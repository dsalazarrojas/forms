# Nutrition Science Awareness Campaign Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect information from participants in the Nutrition Science Awareness Campaign. It captures personal details such as name, email, phone number, and demographic information such as date, time, and note. The form also includes an informed consent field to ensure that participants are aware of the campaign's terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select a date for the "Date" field.
5. Enter a time for the "Time" field.
6. Write a note in the "Note" field (this is optional).
7. Select your preferred informed consent option from the "Informed Consent" dropdown menu.
8. If you have any additional information to share, enter it in the "Additional Information (optional)" field.
9. You may also enter a category in the "Category" field.
10. Write a brief description of your experience in the "Description" field (this is optional).
11. Finally, enter any other relevant information in the "Form" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: (`form_1`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name.
* **Email**: (`form_2`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: (`form_3`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date**: (`form_4`, `date`, required): Select a date for the campaign.
* **Time**: (`form_5`, `time`, required): Enter the time for the campaign.
* **Note**: (`form_6`, `note`, optional): Write a brief note about your experience with the campaign.
* **Informed Consent**: (`form_7`, `select_one`, optional): Select one of the informed consent options.
* **Additional Information (optional)**: (`form_8`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional information to share, enter it here.
* **Category**: (`form_9`, `text`, optional): You may enter a category for your experience.
* **Description**: (`form_10`, `text`, optional): Write a brief description of your experience.
* **Form**: (`form_11`, `text`, optional): Enter any other relevant information.
