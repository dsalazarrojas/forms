# tenant_background_check_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **tenant_background_check_consent_form** is used to gather the necessary consents and information from the tenant to determine whether to include a background check as part of the tenant application process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, start by filling out the form in the browser or on a desktop device.
2. Select one of the options from the **category** field to indicate your choice.
3. In the **background_check_description** field, provide a clear and concise description of your background check.
4. Once you have filled out the entire form, click the submit button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **background_check**: This field is a text input where you can give your consent for the background check.
* **category**: This field is used to select whether to include a background check as part of the tenant application process. Please choose 'Yes' or 'No' from the provided options.
* **background_check_description**: This field is a text input where you can provide a clear and concise description of your background check. This will help to clarify any details about your background check.
