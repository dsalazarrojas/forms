# Youth Literacy Program Guardian Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used by the guardian or parent of a child to provide consent for their child to participate in the Youth Literacy Program. It asks for their name, contact information, and an emergency contact's information. The form is used to identify the child's emergency contact in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  The guardian or parent must complete the form in its entirety.
2.  Ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately.
3.  If a field is not required but is relevant, it may be filled in at the guardian's or parent's discretion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, `text`, required: false): Please fill in the name of the guardian or parent.
*   **Guardian Phone** (`guardian_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact phone number of the guardian or parent.
*   **Guardian Email** (`guardian_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the guardian or parent.
*   **Relationship to Child** (`guardian_relationship`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the relationship between the guardian or parent and the child. Choose from: Parent or Guardian, Grandparent, Other.
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person who will serve as the emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact phone number of the emergency contact.
*   **Relationship to Child** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the relationship between the emergency contact and the child. Choose from: Parent or Guardian, Grandparent, Other.
*   **Emergency Contact Preferred Method** (`emergency_contact_preferred_method`, `text`, required: false): Describe how the emergency contact would like to be contacted (e.g., phone, text, email).
*   **Guardian Signature** (`guardian_signature`, `note`, required: false): This is where the guardian or parent will sign to confirm their consent.
*   **Emergency Contact Signature** (`emergency_contact_signature`, `note`, required: false): This is where the emergency contact will sign to confirm their role.

## Tips

*   Be sure to fill in all required fields accurately.
*   If a field is not required but is relevant, it may be filled in at the guardian's or parent's discretion.
*   Ensure that all signatures are authentic and in their original form.

This guide is meant to be used by the end-user of the form, the guardian or parent of the child participating in the Youth Literacy Program. It is not a technical guide and is intended to be used as a resource for completion of the form. 

If you have any questions or concerns about this guide or the form, please contact the Youth Literacy Program administrators.
