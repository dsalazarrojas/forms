# Group Therapy Confidentiality Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Group Therapy Confidentiality Form is designed to ensure that each group member understands and agrees to maintain confidentiality during the therapy sessions. This form requires users to select a category, provide a description, and sign off on the terms and conditions of the group therapy session. The form is divided into the following sections:
## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Select the correct category from the options provided.
- Provide a description of your understanding of the group therapy session.
- Sign off on the terms and conditions of the group therapy session.
- Date and time should be filled out correctly.
- If you are attending the session in person, select "In Person". If you are attending by phone or video, select "By Phone/Video".
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Group Therapy Confidentiality Form Title** (`group_therapy_confidentiality_form_title`, text, optional): A brief title for the form. You can enter a descriptive title for the group therapy session.
* **Category** (`group_therapy_confidentiality_form_category`, select_one, optional): Select the category of the group therapy session (e.g., Consent Forms, Other).
* **Description** (`group_therapy_confidentiality_form_description`, note, required): A detailed description of your understanding of the group therapy session.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`group_therapy_confidentiality_form_terms`, text, required): Read and sign off on the terms and conditions of the group therapy session. This is a mandatory field.
* **Signature** (`group_therapy_confidentiality_form_signature`, select_multiple, optional): Check the options to confirm you have read and understood the terms and conditions. You can choose one or both options.
* **Date** (`group_therapy_confidentiality_form_date`, date, required): Fill out the date you completed the form.
* **Time** (`group_therapy_confidentiality_form_time`, time, required): Fill out the time you completed the form.
* **In Person or By Phone/Video?** (`group_therapy_confidentiality_form_in_person`, select_one, optional): Select whether you are attending the session in person, by phone, or by video.
* **Comments** (`group_therapy_confidentiality_form_comments`, text, optional): Any additional comments you would like to add.
* **Agreed to Terms and Conditions** (`group_therapy_confidentiality_form_agreed`, select_one, optional): Confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
* **Date Agreed** (`group_therapy_confidentiality_form_agreed_date`, date, required): Fill out the date you agreed to the terms and conditions.
This form aims to maintain confidentiality and ensure a positive and productive group therapy experience. Please fill it out carefully and honestly.
