# Athletic Training Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is a waiver form for participants enrolling in the athletic training program. It contains information about the participant, their medical history, and their agreement to participate in the program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the participant's full name and date of birth in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the participant's gender.
3. Provide your home address, city, state, and zip code.
4. Enter your contact phone number and email address.
5. Enter the name, phone number, and relationship of an emergency contact person.
6. Select which athletic training program you are enrolling in.
7. Enter the length of your participation in the training program.
8. If you have any medical conditions or allergies, list them in the text field.
9. If you take any medications, list them in the text field.
10. If you have any previous injuries, list them in the text field.
11. If you have any current injuries or limitations, list them in the text field.
12. Enter the name and phone number of your primary physician.
13. Enter the date of your last physical examination.
14. Select whether you have been cleared for training.
15. If you have not been cleared, select the reason for not being cleared.
16. Select whether you understand the inherent risks of athletic training.
17. Select whether you understand that injuries may occur.
18. Select whether you understand that equipment malfunctions may cause injury.
19. Select whether you are participating voluntarily.
20. Select whether you waive claims against the organization for ordinary negligence.
21. Select whether you assume all risks associated with athletic training.
22. Select whether you agree to indemnify the organization from claims.
23. Select whether you grant permission to seek emergency medical treatment.
24. Select whether you understand you are responsible for your own medical insurance.
25. Select whether you agree to follow all rules and instructions.
26. Select whether you understand that participation may be terminated at any time.
27. Select whether you are allowed to take photos and videos during training.
28. If you are under 18, enter the name of your parent or guardian.
29. Sign your name as the participant.
30. If you are under 18, sign your parent or guardian's name.
31. If applicable, enter the name of the witness.
32. Sign the date of your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as the participant.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): Select your gender from the options provided.
* **Street Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your home address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city of your residence.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state or province of your residence.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter your ZIP or postal code.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Enter your relationship to the emergency contact person.
* **Training Program** (`training_program`, select_one, required): Select which athletic training program you are enrolling in.
* **Program Duration** (`program_duration`, select_one, required): Select the length of your participation in the program.
* **Medical Conditions or Allergies** (`medical_conditions`, text, required): If you have any medical conditions or allergies, list them in this field.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, text, optional): If you take any medications, list them in this field.
* **Previous Injuries** (`previous_injuries`, text, required): If you have any previous injuries, list them in this field.
* **Current Injuries or Limitations** (`current_injuries`, text, required): If you have any current injuries or limitations, list them in this field.
* **Primary Physician Name** (`physician_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your primary physician.
* **Primary Physician Phone** (`physician_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your primary physician.
* **Physical Examination Date** (`physical_exam_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your last physical examination.
* **Medical Clearance Status** (`medical_clearance`, select_one, required): Select whether you have been cleared for training.
* **Risk Acknowledgment** (`risk_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Select whether you understand the inherent risks of athletic training.
* **Injury Risks Acknowledgment** (`injury_risks`, select_one, required): Select whether you understand that injuries may occur.
* **Equipment Risks Acknowledgment** (`equipment_risks`, select_one, required): Select whether you understand that equipment malfunctions may cause injury.
* **Voluntary Participation** (`voluntary_participation`, select_one, required): Select whether you are participating voluntarily.
* **Liability Waiver** (`liability_waiver`, select_one, required): Select whether you waive claims against the organization for ordinary negligence.
* **Assumption of Risk** (`assumption_of_risk`, select_one, required): Select whether you assume all risks associated with athletic training.
* **Indemnification Agreement** (`indemnification`, select_one, required): Select whether you agree to indemnify the organization from claims.
* **Medical Treatment Permission** (`medical_treatment_permission`, select_one, required): Select whether you grant permission to seek emergency medical treatment.
* **Insurance Responsibility** (`insurance_responsibility`, select_one, required): Select whether you understand that you are responsible for your own medical insurance.
* **Rule Compliance Agreement** (`rule_compliance`, select_one, required): Select whether you agree to follow all rules and instructions.
* **Termination Clause** (`termination_clause`, select_one, required): Select whether you understand that participation may be terminated at any time.
* **Photo and Video Release** (`photo_release`, select_one, required): Select whether you are allowed to take photos and videos during training.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, text, optional): If you are under 18, enter the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, required): Sign your name as the participant.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature** (`parent_guardian_signature`, text, optional): If you are under 18, sign your parent or guardian's name.
* **Witness Name** (`witness_name`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the witness.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Sign the date of your signature.

## Tips

* Ensure that you fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are under 18, ensure that your parent or guardian signs the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
* Please review all the fields carefully to ensure that you understand the implications of your answers.
