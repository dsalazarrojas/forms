# Veterinary Case Conference Filming Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Case Conference Filming Consent Form is a document that obtains consent from participants in veterinary case conferences for recording and filming purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you are not duplicating the form for the same participant.
2. Fill out the form with the required information:
	* Participant Details: Provide basic information about the participant, including their name and contact details.
	* Filming Consent: Choose whether the participant consents to filming.
	* Recording Purpose: Select the purpose of the recording (e.g., case conferences, teaching rounds, or professional events).
	* Participant Name: Enter the participant's name.
	* Email Address: Enter the participant's email address.
	* Phone Number: Enter the participant's phone number.
	* Participant Date of Birth: Enter the participant's date of birth.
	* Participant Date of Record: Enter the participant's date of record.
	* Filming Location: Enter the location where the filming will take place.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, `text`, required: false): This field is for providing basic information about the participant, which may include their name and contact details.
* **Filming Consent** (`filming_consent`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose whether the participant consents to filming.
* **Recording Purpose** (`recording_purpose`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the purpose of the recording (e.g., case conferences, teaching rounds, or professional events).
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the participant's name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required: false): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Date of Birth** (`participant_date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Enter the participant's date of birth.
* **Participant Date of Record** (`participant_date_of_record`, `date`, required: false): Enter the participant's date of record.
* **Filming Location** (`filming_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location where the filming will take place.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when providing the participant's contact information.
* Ensure that the participant's consent status is up-to-date.
* If the participant's contact information changes, please update the form accordingly.
* If you have any doubts or questions about this form, please contact your veterinary clinic administrator.
