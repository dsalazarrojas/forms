# Newsletter Subscription Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for newsletter subscription consent. Users will be asked to provide their name, email address, and other details to confirm their subscription preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the first field. This is a text field where you can type in your name as you'd like to be addressed.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the second field. Similar to the first field, this is where you can type in your last name as you'd like.
3. In the third field, enter your **Email**. This is where you'll enter the email address where you'd like to receive newsletters.
4. In the **Type of Email** field, select your email type from the provided options: **Company**, **Personal**, or **Other**. This will help us understand your email association.
5. In the **Type of Newsletter** field, choose how often you'd like to receive our newsletter: **Daily**, **Weekly**, or **Monthly**.
6. For the **Do you consent to receive our newsletter?** field, select **True** if you're willing to receive our newsletters, or **False** if you'd rather not.
7. If you'd like, enter your **Date of Birth** in the corresponding field. This is an optional field and is likely used to verify your age.
8. Finally, review your selections and submit the form to confirm your newsletter subscription preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as you'd like to be addressed.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as you'd like to be addressed.
* **Email**: Enter the email address where you'd like to receive newsletters.
* **Type of Email**: Select your email type from the options provided: **Company**, **Personal**, or **Other**.
* **Type of Newsletter**: Choose how often you'd like to receive our newsletter: **Daily**, **Weekly**, or **Monthly**.
* **Do you consent to receive our newsletter?**: Select **True** to consent or **False** to opt-out.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth (optional, likely for age verification).
