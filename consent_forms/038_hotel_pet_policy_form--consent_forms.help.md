# Hotel Pet Policy Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Pet Policy Form is designed to collect essential information about your pet. This form is meant to ensure that we can accommodate your pet's needs while maintaining a safe and comfortable environment for all hotel guests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the relevant answers to each field. If you're unsure about a particular field, you can leave it blank or contact our hotel staff for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Hotel Pet Policy** (`pet_policy_title`, text, optional): Please enter a brief title describing your pet's policy.
* **Pet Name** (`pet_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your pet.
* **Pet Type** (`pet_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all applicable pet types (Cat, Dog, Bird, Fish, Other).
* **Pet Breed** (`pet_breed`, text, optional): Enter your pet's breed (if applicable).
* **Pet Size** (`pet_size`, select_one, optional): Select the size of your pet (Small, Medium, Large, XL).
* **Pet Weight** (`pet_weight`, number, optional): Enter your pet's weight (if applicable).
* **Pet Diet** (`pet_diet`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your pet's diet (Carnivore, Herbivore, Omnivore).
* **Pet Housing** (`pet_housing`, select_multiple, optional): Select how your pet will be housed during your stay (Crate, Kennel, Cage).
* **Pet Feeding** (`pet_feeding`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how you usually feed your pet (Alimentum, Kibble, Raw).
* **Pet Medication** (`pet_medication`, text, optional): Enter any medication your pet may be taking.
* **Pet Training** (`pet_training`, select_multiple, optional): Select the level of training your pet has (Basic, Advanced, None).
* **Pet Licence** (`pet_licence`, select_multiple, optional): Check if your pet has a licence.
* **Pet Vaccination** (`pet_vaccination`, select_multiple, optional): Check if your pet is vaccinated.
* **Pet Medical Condition** (`pet_medical_condition`, text, optional): Enter any medical conditions your pet may have.
* **Pet Allergies** (`pet_allergies`, text, optional): Enter any allergies your pet may have.
* **Pet Health Issues** (`pet_health_issues`, text, optional): Enter any health issues your pet may have.
* **Pet Special Needs** (`pet_special_needs`, text, optional): Enter any special needs your pet may have.
* **Pet Equipment** (`pet_equipment`, text, optional): Enter any equipment your pet may need (e.g., wheelchairs, etc.).
