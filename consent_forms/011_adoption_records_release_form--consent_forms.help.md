# Adoption Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adoption Records Release Form is a crucial document used to verify the identity and authority of a requester when accessing adoption records. It helps maintain the confidentiality and integrity of the records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your full name as per your government-issued ID.
2.  Enter your valid email address.
3.  Enter your phone number.
4.  Indicate your relationship to the adoptee.
5.  Provide the adoptee's full name.
6.  Specify the adoptee's date of birth.
7.  Select the adoptee's age status.
8.  If applicable, provide the adoption agency name, adoption date, and case number.
9.  Indicate what records you are requesting to be released (e.g., birth certificate, adoption decree, medical records, etc.).
10.  Check the box to confirm that you understand some records may be withheld for the privacy of others.
11.  Confirm that you consent to the release of the requested records by selecting the "I consent" option.
12.  Finally, ensure that you understand that you are confirming your consent and authorization to release your records by selecting the "I acknowledge" option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (`requester_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as per your government-issued ID.
*   **Email Address** (`requester_email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address for contact and communication purposes.
*   **Phone Number** (`requester_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact and communication purposes.
*   **Relationship to Adoptee** (`requester_relationship`, select_one, required): Indicate your relationship to the adoptee.
*   **Adoptee Full Name** (`adoptee_name`, text, required): Provide the full name of the adoptee.
*   **Adoptee Date of Birth** (`adoptee_date_of_birth`, date, required): Specify the date of birth of the adoptee.
*   **Adoptee Age Status** (`adoptee_age_status`, select_one, required): Select the current age status of the adoptee.
*   **Adoption Agency Name** (`adoption_agency`, text, optional): If known, provide the name of the adoption agency.
*   **Approximate Adoption Date** (`adoption_date`, date, optional): Provide the approximate date of adoption if known.
*   **Case or Docket Number** (`case_number`, text, optional): If known, provide the case or docket number.
*   **Records Requested** (`records_requested`, text, optional): Indicate what records you are requesting to be released.
*   **Original birth certificate** (`birth_certificate`, select_one, optional): Check if you are requesting the original birth certificate.
*   **Adoption Decree and court papers** (`adoption_decree`, select_one, optional): Check if you are requesting the adoption decree and court papers.
*   **Medical and health records** (`medical_records`, select_one, optional): Check if you are requesting medical and health records.
*   **Social history and background information** (`social_history`, select_one, optional): Check if you are requesting social history and background information.
*   **Other records requested** (`other_records`, text, optional): Provide any other records you are requesting to be released.
*   **Purpose of Records Request** (`purpose_of_request`, text, required): Describe the purpose of requesting these records.
*   **Consent and Authorization** (`consent_to_release`, select_one, required): Confirm that you consent to the release of the requested records by selecting the "I consent" option.
*   **Confidentiality Agreement** (`confidentiality_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand some records may be withheld to protect the privacy of other parties by selecting the "I acknowledge" option.
