# Lien Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lien Release Form is used to formally release a lien on a property. This form is typically used by the lien holder to indicate their intention to release the lien and provide details about the lien and its resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand the purpose and the required information.
2. Confirm that you are the lien holder and have the authority to release the lien.
3. Complete each field as instructed below:

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Lien Holder Name** (`lien_holder_name`, text, **required**): Enter your name as it appears on the lien documentation or as registered with the relevant authorities.
- **Date Paid** (`date_paid`, date, **not required**): If you paid off the lien, enter the date of the payment. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
- **Lien Amount** (`lien_amount`, number, **not required**): Enter the amount of the lien, if applicable.
- **Lien Description** (`lien_description`, text, **not required**): Provide a brief description of the lien, if applicable.
- **Borrower Signature** (`borrower_signature`, text, **not required**): This field is intended for the borrower to sign, indicating their agreement to release the lien.
- **Lien Holder Signature** (`lienholder_signature`, text, **not required**): This field is for the lien holder to sign, confirming their intention to release the lien.
- **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, **not required**): Enter the date you signed this form.
- **Title Issuer** (`title_issuer`, text, **not required**): If applicable, enter the name of the authority that issued the lien title.

## Tips
* Make sure to sign the form with your authenticating signature.
* Keep a copy of the completed form for your records.
* If you are unsure about any part of the process, consult a relevant authority or seek guidance from a qualified professional.
