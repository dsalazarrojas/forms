# Competition Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from participants in a competition, including competition details, participant information, and sensitive topics such as medical conditions, emergency contact details, and consent for medical treatment, photography, and data processing.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Competition Name**: Enter the name of the competition.
2. **Competition Date**: Select the date of the competition.
3. **Competition Location**: Enter the location of the competition.
4. **Participant Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
5. **Participant Email**: Enter your contact email address.
6. **Participant Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
7. **Participant Address**: Enter your mailing address.
8. **Date of Birth**: Select your date of birth.
9. **Age Category**: Select the age category you belong to.
10. **Gender**: Select your gender.
11. **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact.
12. **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of your emergency contact.
13. **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Select your relationship to the participant.
14. **Medical Conditions**: If you have any medical conditions, enter them here.
15. **Meditations**: If you take any regular medications, list them here.
16. **Allergies**: If you have any food, drug, or environmental allergies, list them here.
17. **Health Insurance Provider**: If you have health insurance, enter your provider's name.
18. **Insurance Policy**: If you have an insurance policy, enter your policy number.
19. **Liability Waiver Agreement**: Select whether you agree to assume all risks and waive liability.
20. **Medical Treatment Consent**: Select whether you consent to emergency medical treatment.
21. **Photo and Video Consent**: Select whether you consent to being photographed or filmed.
22. **Data Privacy Agreement**: Select whether you agree to data processing terms.
23. **Rules and Regulations Agreement**: Select whether you agree to follow all competition rules.
24. **Code of Conduct Agreement**: Select whether you agree to uphold the code of conduct.
25. **Travel Arrangements**: If you need help with travel arrangements, select the relevant option.
26. **Accommodation Needed**: If you require any special accommodations, enter them here.
27. **Participant Signature**: Sign your name as the participant.
28. **Signature Date**: Select the date of signing.
29. **Guardian Signature**: If you are under 18, your guardian must sign here.
30. **Guardian Relationship**: If you are under 18, enter your relationship to the participant here.

*   Make sure to review the form carefully and answer all required fields.
