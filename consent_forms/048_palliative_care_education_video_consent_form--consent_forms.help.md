# Palliative Care Education Video Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Palliative Care Education Video Consent Form is used to obtain informed consent from participants who will be viewing educational videos on palliative care. This form is used to ensure that participants understand the purpose and scope of the video, and to obtain their consent to participate in the video program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Participant Name" field. This is required to identify you as a participant in the video program.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, if you wish to be contacted about future video programs.
3. If you wish to be contacted for future video programs, please enter your email address in the "Email" field. If you are not interested in being contacted, simply leave this field blank.
4. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Video Release" field to indicate whether you have consented to be recorded while viewing the video.
5. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Consent Date" field to confirm the date of your consent.
6. In the "Hospice or Hospital" field, select "Yes" if you are a healthcare professional working in a hospice or hospital setting, or "No" otherwise.
7. In the "Other Education" field, select "Yes" if you have previously participated in educational programs outside of the palliative care program, or "No" otherwise.
8. Enter any additional education programs you have participated in, if applicable, in the "Other Education Programs" field.
9. Enter your phone number in the "Participant Phone" field, if you wish to be contacted for future video programs.
10. In the "Confirm Email" field, select "Yes" if the email address you entered earlier is correct, or "No" otherwise.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for future video programs.
*   **Consent Date**: The date you are signing this consent form.
*   **Video Release**: Indicate whether you have consented to be recorded while viewing the video. Select "Yes" to indicate your consent.
*   **Hospice or Hospital**: Indicate whether you are a healthcare professional working in a hospice or hospital setting.
*   **Other Education**: Indicate whether you have previously participated in educational programs outside of the palliative care program.
*   **Other Education Programs**: Enter any additional education programs you have participated in, if applicable.
*   **Participant Signature**: Please sign your name to confirm that you have read and understood this consent form.
*   **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for future video programs.
*   **Confirm Email**: Confirm that the email address you entered earlier is correct.

Note: Please review each field carefully and ensure that your information is accurate and complete. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
