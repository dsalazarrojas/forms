# Signature Collection Challenge - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Signature Collection Challenge form is designed for collecting signatures from various stakeholders. It's a structured form for ensuring that all necessary information and agreements are captured in a clear and concise manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** (required) in the first field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** (required) in the second field.
3. Provide your **Email** (required) in the third field. Please ensure that it's a valid email address.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** (required) in the fourth field.
5. Fill in the **Sign At** (required) field with the date on which you intend to sign the agreement.
6. Fill in the **Sign By** (required) field with the date and time on which you sign the agreement.
7. Sign your name in the **Signature** (required) field, which is a note field for handwritten signatures.
8. Select one of the options in the **Agree** (required) field to indicate your agreement.
9. Optionally, you can add any **Comments** (not required) in the text field.
10. In the **Terms and Conditions** field, select **Accept** to agree to the terms and conditions of the agreement.
11. Fill in the **Agree Date** (required) field with the date on which you agree to the terms and conditions.
12. Fill in the **Sign Date** (required) field with the date on which you sign the agreement.
13. Select the **Signature Status** (required) field to indicate the status of your signature.
14. Select the **Signature Type** (required) field to indicate the type of signature you provided.
15. Fill in the **Agree Time** (required) field with the time on which you agree to the terms and conditions.
16. Optionally, you can add any additional **Comment** (not required) in the text field.
17. In the **Comment By** (not required) field, select the user who made the comment.
18. Select the **Comment Status** (not required) field to indicate the status of your comment.
19. Fill in the **Comment Date** (not required) field with the date on which the comment was made.
20. Enter the **Signature Count** (required) field with the number of signatures collected.
21. Enter the **Agree Count** (required) field with the number of agreements made.
22. Enter the **Comment Count** (required) field with the number of comments made.
23. Select the **Status** (required) field to indicate the status of your form submission.
24. Select the **Signature Type Status** (required) field to indicate the status of your signature type.

* **Signature Status** (select_one, required): Select the status of your signature.
* **Signature Type** (select_one, required): Select the type of signature you provided.
* **Agree Time** (time, required): Fill in the time on which you agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Comment** (text, not required): Optionally, add any comments in this field.
* **Comment By** (select_one, not required): Select the user who made the comment.
* **Comment Status** (select_one, not required): Select the status of your comment.
* **Comment Date** (date, not required): Fill in the date on which the comment was made.
* **Signature Count** (number, required): Enter the number of signatures collected.
