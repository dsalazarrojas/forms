# Animal Training Session Filming Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain consent from pet owners for filming and sharing their animals during training sessions. It outlines what can be done with the footage, under what circumstances, and ensures that both parties understand their roles and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your pet's information:
   * Enter your pet's name.
   * Choose the species of your pet.
   * If applicable, enter your pet's breed.

2. Enter your contact information:
   * Enter your full name.
   * Enter your email address.
   * Enter your phone number.

3. Confirm the training session details:
   * Enter the date of the training session.
   * Enter the location of the training session.

4. Choose how you want to be involved in filming and sharing:
   * Choose whether you consent to filming.
   * Choose how you want your pet to be identified (e.g., use its name, description only, or no identification).
   * Choose how you want the footage to be used (e.g., internal use, private client portal, public website, social media, YouTube, print materials, presentations, or broadcast media).
   * Choose whether you want to be notified before use or have approval rights before publication.

5. Review and agree to terms and conditions:
   * Confirm that you understand your consent can be revoked at any time.
   * Confirm that you release the trainer from liability.
   * Confirm that you have no concerns or questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Owner Full Name** (`1`): Enter your full name as the pet owner.
* **Email Address** (`2`): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`3`): Enter your phone number.
* **Street Address** (`4`): This field is optional and not required for the form submission, but it helps us get in touch with you.
* **Animal Name** (`5`): Enter your pet's name.
* **Species** (`6`): Choose your pet's species from the dropdown menu.
* **Breed** (`7`): Enter your pet's breed if applicable.
* **Trainer Name** (`8`): Enter the name of the trainer conducting the session.
* **Training Organization** (`9`): Enter the name of the organization or facility conducting the training session.
* **Training Session Date** (`10`): Enter the date of the training session.
* **Session Location** (`11`): Enter the location where the training session will take place.
* **Consent to Film Training Session** (`12`): Choose whether you consent to filming and sharing your pet during the session.
* **Type of Recording** (`13`): Choose all types of recording you consent to (video, audio, photography, live streaming).
* **Purpose of Filming** (`14`): Choose all reasons for filming (trainer education, owner review, marketing and promotion, etc.).
* **Where May Footage Be Used** (`15`): Choose where the footage may be used (internal use only, private client portal, public website, etc.).
* **Identity Disclosure Preferences** (`16`): Choose how you want your identity and your pet's identity to be handled.
* **Animal Identification** (`17`): Choose how you want your pet to be identified in the footage.
* **How Long May Footage Be Retained** (`18`): Choose how long you want the footage to be retained.
* **May Footage Be Shared with Third Parties** (`19`): Choose whether you want the footage to be shared with other trainers or partners.
* **May Footage Be Used for Commercial Purposes** (`20`): Choose whether you consent to commercial use of the footage.
* **Will You Receive Compensation** (`21`): Choose whether you expect compensation for your pet's participation.
* **Do You Want Approval Rights Before Use** (`22`): Choose whether you want approval rights before use of the footage.
* **Understanding of Revocation Rights** (`23`): Confirm that you understand that you can revoke your consent at any time.
* **Liability Release** (`24`): Confirm that you release the trainer from liability related to filming.
* **Do You Have Any Concerns or Questions** (`25`): Choose whether you have any concerns or questions about the filming process.
* **Please Describe Your Concerns** (`26`): If you selected yes, describe your concerns in this field.
* **Additional Permissions or Restrictions** (`27`): Enter any special conditions or permissions you want to add.
* **Owner Electronic Signature** (`28`): Enter your full name as the owner.
* **Date of Consent** (`29`): Enter the date you are providing consent.
* **Trainer Electronic Signature** (`30`): Enter the full name of the trainer.
* **Witness Name** (`31`): Enter the name of the witness if you want one.
* **I Have Read and Understand This Consent Form** (`32`): Confirm that you have read and understand the consent form.

## Tips

* Please make sure to read and understand each field before filling it in.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please express them in the "Do You Have Any Concerns or Questions" field.
* You can revoke your consent at any time by contacting us.
* This form is a crucial part of the filming process, and it's essential to understand it well to avoid any potential issues.
