# orthotic_device_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The orthotic_device_consent_form is a consent form used to obtain agreement from patients for the fitting of an orthotic device. This form helps ensure that both the patient and healthcare provider understand the terms and conditions of the device fitting process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the form with the requested patient information.
2. Provide contact information for the patient.
3. Enter the patient's date of birth (D.O.B).
4. Enter the device fitter's name and other device details.
5. Document the date and time of the device fitting.
6. Include a signature from both the patient and the healthcare provider.
7. Fill in any additional provider comments (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their ID.
* **Patient Contact Info** (`patient_contact_info`, text, required): Enter the patient's contact information, including phone number and address.
* **Patient D.O.B** (`patient_dob`, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Device Fitter** (`device_fitter`, text, required): Enter the name of the healthcare provider who fitted the device.
* **Device Details** (`device_details`, text, required): Enter any relevant details about the device, such as model number and manufacturer.
* **Device Fitting Date** (`device_fitting_date`, date, required): Enter the date the device was fitted.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text, required): Sign and date the patient's signature here.
* **Provider Name** (`provider_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the healthcare provider who fitted the device.
* **Provider Contact Info** (`provider_contact_info`, text, required): Enter the contact information for the healthcare provider.
* **Provider Signature** (`provider_signature`, text, required): Sign and date the healthcare provider's signature here.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date the form was completed.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time the form was completed.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the device was fitted (e.g., clinic, home, etc.).
* **Device Manufacturer** (`device_manufacturer`, text, required): Enter the name of the device manufacturer.
* **Device Model Number** (`device_model_number`, text, required): Enter the model number of the device.
* **Device Serial Number** (`device_serial_number`, text, required): Enter the serial number of the device.
* **Device Version Number** (`device_version_number`, number, optional): Enter the version number of the device (if applicable).
* **Patient Medical Condition** (`patient_medical_condition`, text, required): Enter any relevant medical conditions that led to the device fitting.
* **Provider Licensing Information** (`provider_licensing_info`, text, optional): Enter any relevant licensing information for the healthcare provider.
* **Device Fitting Technician** (`device_fitting_technician`, text, required): Enter the name of the healthcare provider who fitted the device.
* **Device Fitting Notes** (`device_fitting_notes`, text, required): Enter any additional notes about the device fitting process.
* **Device Return Policy** (`device_return_policy`, text, optional): Enter the return policy for the device (if applicable).
* **Patient Informed Consent** (`patient_informed_consent`, text, required): Obtain the patient's informed consent for the device fitting.
* **Patient Understanding** (`patient_understanding`, text, required): Ensure the patient understands the terms and conditions of the device.
* **Provider Comments** (`provider_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments from the healthcare provider.
