# Air Filtration Device Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain consent from participants who will be exposed to air filtration devices within their work environment. This form ensures that all participants are aware of the potential risks and benefits associated with the devices and their usage.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Participant Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your personnel documents.
2.  **Date**: Enter the date you are completing this form.
3.  **Location**: Enter the room or office number where the air filtration device is located.
4.  **Device Type**: Select the type of air filtration device being used (e.g., HEPA Air Purifier, UV-C Sanitizer, etc.).
5.  **Purpose of Use**: Select the reason for deploying the device (e.g., Air Quality Study, Infection Control, Allergen Reduction, etc.).
6.  **Risks Explained**: Acknowledge that you understand potential noise or airflow changes associated with the device.
7.  **Benefits Explained**: Acknowledge that you understand why the device is being used.
8.  **Consent Statement**: Select 'I Consent' if you agree to the operation of the device in your space.
9.  **Signature**: Enter your full name as a signature of consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Name**: This field is used to record your name.
*   **Date**: This field is used to record the date you are completing this form.
*   **Location**: This field is used to record the room or office number where the device is located.
*   **Device Type**: This field is used to select the type of air filtration device being used. Please note that the options are:
    *   HEPA Air Purifier
    *   UV-C Sanitizer
    *   Carbon Filter Unit
    *   Industrial Air Scrubber
*   **Purpose of Use**: This field is used to select the reason for deploying the device. Please note that the options are:
    *   Air Quality Study
    *   Infection Control
    *   Allergen Reduction
    *   Odor Control
*   **Risks Explained**: This field asks you to acknowledge that you understand potential noise or airflow changes associated with the device.
*   **Benefits Explained**: This field asks you to acknowledge that you understand why the device is being used.
*   **Consent Statement**: This field is used to select whether you consent to the operation of the device in your space.
*   **Signature**: This field is used to record your full name as a signature of consent.

## Tips

*   Please answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
*   Make sure to understand the potential risks and benefits associated with the air filtration device you are being exposed to.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or facility management.
