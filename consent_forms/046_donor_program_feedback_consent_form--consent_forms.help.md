# Donor Program Feedback Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Donor Program Feedback Consent Form is designed to collect feedback from donors on their experience with the program. This feedback will help us understand your needs and improve the program to better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all the questions to the best of your ability.
2. You can skip questions if they don't apply to you.
3. Provide your consent to be contacted via your preferred method.
4. Choose how often you wish to be contacted.
5. Enter your email address (if you choose to be contacted via email).
6. Enter your phone number (if you choose to be contacted via phone).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How would you describe your feedback experience with our Donor Program** (<name>: `donor_feedback`, <type>: `text`, required: false): Please provide a brief description of your experience with our Donor Program. This will help us understand what we are doing well and what we can improve.
* **We would like to obtain your consent to contact you via** (<name>: `donor_program_consent`, <type>: `select_one`, required: false): Please select the method(s) you would like to be contacted through (e.g., Email, Phone, or Mail). This will help us tailor our communication with you.
* **How often do you wish to be contacted** (<name>: `consent_frequency`, <type>: `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select how often you would like to be contacted (e.g., Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly). This will help us plan our communication with you.
* **Preferred contact method** (<name>: `contact_preference`, <type>: `select_one`, required: false): Please select your preferred contact method (e.g., Email, Phone, or Mail). This will be used to contact you in the future.
* **Email** (<name>: `email`, <type>: `email`, required: false): If you chose to be contacted via email, please enter your email address below. We will use this to contact you with relevant information.
* **Phone** (<name>: `phone`, <type>: `text`, required: false): If you chose to be contacted via phone, please enter your phone number below. We will use this to contact you with relevant information.
