# Medical Research Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect consent and information from participants in medical research studies. It is essential to understand the purpose of the form before proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and the information required.
2. Fill in your participant information, including your contact details.
3. Provide information about your medical history and any medication allergies.
4. Sign the consent statement to confirm your understanding and agreement to participate.
5. Date the consent statement if required.
6. Sign your name as a signature to confirm your identity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_information** (Text, Optional)
	+ Enter your personal details, such as name, address, and contact information.
* **researcher_information** (Text, Optional)
	+ Enter the name and contact information of the researcher who is conducting the study.
* **study_details** (Text, Optional)
	+ Provide a brief description of the study, including its purpose and expected duration.
* **medical_history** (Select Multiple, Optional)
	+ Check the boxes if you have any medical history or conditions.
* **medication_allergies** (Select Multiple, Optional)
	+ Check the boxes if you have any medication allergies.
* **consent_statement** (Text, Required)
	+ Write your understanding and agreement to participate in the study. Please provide a detailed statement.
* **date_of_consent** (Date, Optional)
	+ If required, enter the date you provided consent.
* **signature** (Note, Required)
	+ Sign your name as a participant to confirm your identity and confirm your participation.
* **participant_statement** (Text, Optional)
	+ Enter any additional information you would like to provide about your participation in the study.

# Tips

* Please ensure that you understand the terms and conditions of the study before providing consent.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask the researcher.
* Keep a record of your completed form for your personal records.
Note: The form fields are not necessarily presented in order of the page numbers in the YAML schema. This is because the fields are grouped by category for better understanding.
