# International Travel Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a student's international travel plans, including their destination, travel dates, and necessary documents required for travel.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure that you have all necessary information available.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Select the correct options for each field as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Country of Destination** (`country_of_destination`, select_one, required/optional): Select the country you are traveling to.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Parent Contact Info** (`parent_contact_info`, text, required/optional): Provide your parent or guardian's contact information, including their name, phone number, and email.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Student Grade Level** (`student_grade_level`, select_one, required/optional): Select your current grade level (Elementary School, High School, Postgraduate, University, or Other).
* **Other (please specify)** (`student_grade_level_other`, text, required/optional): Enter your grade level if it's not listed above.
* **Adult Travelers** (`adult_travelers`, text, required/optional): Provide the names of adult travelers accompanying you, including their contact information.
* **Child Travelers** (`child_travelers`, text, required/optional): List the names and contact information for any children traveling with you.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional contact information, such as the school or travel agency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, text, required/optional): Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship To Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, required/optional): Select the relationship between the emergency contact person and you.
* **Flight Details** (`flight_details`, text, required/optional): Enter the flight details, including flight numbers and departure/arrival times.
* **Travel Dates** (`travel_dates`, date, required/optional): Enter the travel dates in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Return Date** (`return_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the return date in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Travel Documents** (`travel_document`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the necessary travel documents required for your travel, such as a passport, visa, or ID.
* **Student Travel History** (`student_travel_history`, text, required/optional): Provide any relevant student travel history, such as previous international travel experiences or any issues during previous trips.

Please note that all fields are optional unless marked as required. If you're unsure about any field, please contact the travel agency or school for clarification.
