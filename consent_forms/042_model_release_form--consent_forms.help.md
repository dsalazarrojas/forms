# Model Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Model Release Form is used to obtain consent from subjects for the commercial use of their images or videos.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the subject's name in the **Subject Name** field.
2. Provide the subject's contact information, including address, in the **Contact Information** field.
3. Enter the subject's date of birth in the **Date of Birth** field.
4. In the **Permission** field, type 'I' to indicate your permission for your image to be used for commercial purposes.
5. Select one of the options in the **Model Release** field, which determines the scope of your consent.
6. For multiple contact persons, select all relevant options in the **Contact Person** field.
7. Fill out the **Contact Phone** and **Contact Email** fields with the contact information of the person handling the model release.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Subject Name** (1, text, required: false): Enter the subject's full name.
* **Contact Information** (2, text, required: false): Provide the subject's address, ensuring they can be contacted regarding the use of their image or video.
* **Address** (3, text, required: false): Enter the subject's address for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (4, date, required: false): Enter the subject's date of birth, used for identification purposes.
* **Permission** (5, note, required: false): Type 'I' to indicate your permission for your image or video to be used for commercial purposes.
* **Model Release** (6, select_one, required: false): Select one of the options to determine the scope of your consent.
	+ Option 1: Permission granted for commercial use.
	+ Option 2: Permission granted for non-commercial use.
	+ Option 3: Permission not granted (subject does not consent).
* **Contact Person** (7, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or multiple options for the contact person handling the model release.
* **Contact Phone** (8, text, required: false): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Email** (9, email, required: false): Enter the contact person's email address.
