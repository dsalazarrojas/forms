# marketing_release_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather marketing release authorization and agency information for various marketing campaigns.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each field carefully, ensuring you understand the purpose and requirement for each one.
2. Select the relevant options for each field as instructed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Consent** (`user_consent`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable options (Yes) to indicate your consent for marketing release authorization.
* **Marketing Release** (`marketing_release`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the marketing release.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, optional): This is an explanation of the terms and conditions for marketing release. Read and understand before proceeding.
* **Signature** (`signature`, select_one, optional): Select your name (John Doe, Jane Doe, Other) to indicate your signature.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the contact number for marketing.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address for marketing purposes.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, optional): Enter the date you submitted this form.
* **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`, time, optional): Enter the time you submitted this form.
* **Marketing Agency** (`marketing_agency`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable marketing agencies (Agency A, Agency B, Agency C) involved in the marketing campaign.
* **Marketing Medium** (`marketing_medium`, select_one, optional): Select the marketing medium (Social Media, Email, Phone) used for the campaign.
* **Marketing Channels** (`marketing_channels`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable marketing channels (Social Media, Email, Phone) for the campaign.
* **Contact Number Agency** (`contact_number_agency`, text, optional): Enter the contact number for the agency.
* **Email Address Agency** (`email_address_agency`, email, optional): Enter the email address for the agency.
* **Marketing Medium Agency** (`marketing_medium_agency`, select_one, optional): Select the marketing medium for the agency (Social Media, Email, Phone).
* **Marketing Channels Agency** (`marketing_channels_agency`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable marketing channels for the agency (Social Media, Email, Phone).
* **User Consent Agency** (`user_consent_agency`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable options (Yes) for user consent for the agency.
* **Terms And Conditions Agency** (`terms_and_conditions_agency`, note, optional): This is an explanation of the terms and conditions for marketing release authorization. Read and understand before proceeding.
* **Contact Number Agency 2** (`contact_number_agency_2`, text, optional): Enter the contact number for the agency (if applicable).
* **Email Address Agency 2** (`email_address_agency_2`, email, optional): Enter the email address for the agency (if applicable).
* **Marketing Agency Agency** (`marketing_agency_agency`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable marketing agencies (Agency A, Agency B, Agency C) involved in the marketing campaign.
* **Marketing Medium Agency Agency** (`marketing_medium_agency_agency`, select_one, optional): Select the marketing medium for the agency (if applicable) (Social Media, Email, Phone).
* **Marketing Channels Agency Agency** (`marketing_channels_agency_agency`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable marketing channels for the agency (if applicable) (Social Media, Email, Phone).
* **User Consent Agency Agency** (`user_consent_agency_agency`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable options (Yes) for user consent for the agency.
* **Terms And Conditions Agency Agency** (`terms_and_conditions_agency_agency`, note, optional): This is an explanation of the terms and conditions for marketing release authorization. Read and understand before proceeding.

Please note that some fields are marked as required or optional, depending on the specific use case. Ensure you review each field carefully before proceeding with the form submission.
