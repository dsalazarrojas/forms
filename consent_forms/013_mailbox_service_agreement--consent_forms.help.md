# Mailbox Service Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mailbox Service Agreement form is used to collect customer information and consent for the Mailbox Service. This form is not a contract or a legally binding agreement, but rather a collection of fields that the customer fills out to provide their input and consent for the service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Navigate through the pages and fill out the fields as prompted.
2. Fill out the customer details page (page 1) with your information.
3. Move on to the service preferences page (page 2) and fill out your service preferences.
4. If you are agreeing to the E-Signature, select "Yes" in the E-Signature field.
5. If you are providing a date and time for the E-Signature, fill out the E-Signature Date/Time fields.
6. Choose a tool for the Assigned Tool field, if applicable.
7. Fill out any additional notes or comments in the Notes field.
8. Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details** (Page 1): This is the first page of the form where you will fill out your personal details. Enter your information accurately and completely.
  * **Name**: Fill out your full name.
  * **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
  * **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
  * **Company Name**: If applicable, enter your company name.

* **Service Preferences** (Page 2): This page is where you will select your service preferences.
  * **Service Preferences**: This is a text field where you can enter any special requirements or instructions for your service.
  
* **E-Signature** (Page 3): This is where you will agree or refuse to the E-Signature.
  * **E-Signature**: Select "Yes" to agree to the E-Signature.
  * **E-Signature Date**: If you are providing a date for the E-Signature, fill out the date accurately.
  * **E-Signature Time**: If you are providing a time for the E-Signature, fill out the time accurately.
  * **E-Signature Date/Time**: If you are providing both a date and time for the E-Signature, fill out both accurately.

* **Assigned Tool** (Page 3): This is where you will select a tool from the available options.
  * **Assigned Tool**: Select a tool that is relevant to your service needs.
  
* **Output File** (Page 3): This is where you can enter any additional file information.
  * **Output File**: If you have any additional files to upload, enter the information here.
  
* **Notes** (Page 3): This is where you can enter any additional comments or notes.
  * **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your service.
  
* **Submit** (Page 3): Click "Submit" to complete the form.
  * **Submit**: Click "Submit" to complete the form and submit your information.
