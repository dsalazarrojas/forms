# VISAGE COVID 19 Liability Release Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a liability release waiver for individuals who are participating in a study or program related to COVID-19. It is used to gather information from individuals who have been exposed to or have tested positive for COVID-19.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer all required questions truthfully.
2. Answer questions 2-3 with either "True" or "False" based on your current status.
3. If you have any of the symptoms listed in question 4, select all that apply.
4. Enter the date you were exposed to COVID-19 in question 5.
5. If you have a medical condition that could impact your participation in the study, select the condition in question 7 and provide additional details in question 8.
6. Enter your contact information in question 9.
7. Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact in question 10.
8. Answer questions 11-12 with either "True" or "False" based on your consent to participate and share information with VISAGE.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Informations** (`general_informations`, `text`, optional): This field is a free-text field where you can enter any information you feel is relevant to your participation in the study or program.
* **I have been exposed to COVID-19** (`consent_1`, `select_one`, optional): Answer this question with either "True" or "False" based on your current status.
* **I am currently positive for COVID-19** (`consent_2`, `select_one`, optional): Answer this question with either "True" or "False" based on your current status.
* **Do you have any of these symptoms?** (`symptoms`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the symptoms that you currently experience.
	+ **Fever**
	+ **Cough**
	+ **Fatigue**
	+ **Headache**
* **Exposure Date** (`exposure_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you were exposed to COVID-19.
* **Contact Tracing** (`contact_trace`, `text`, optional): Enter any contact tracing information here.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `select_one`, optional): Select any pre-existing medical conditions that could impact your participation in the study or program.
	+ **None**
	+ **Asthma**
	+ **Heart Disease**
* **Detail** (`medical_condition_detail`, `text`, optional): If you selected a medical condition in the previous field, provide additional details about your condition here.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact information here (e.g., phone number, email).
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, optional): Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **I am consenting to participate in this study** (`consent_3`, `select_one`, optional): Answer this question with either "True" or "False" based on your consent to participate.
* **I am consenting to share information with VISAGE** (`consent_4`, `select_one`, optional): Answer this question with either "True" or "False" based on your consent to share information with VISAGE.

## Tips
* Answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any question, contact the study or program administrators for clarification.
* Do not answer questions that you believe are confidential or sensitive in nature.
