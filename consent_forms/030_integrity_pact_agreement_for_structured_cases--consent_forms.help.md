# integrity-pact-agreement-for-structured-cases - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain the integrity-pact-agreement-for-structured-cases from individuals who will be accessing structured cases.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the case name.
2. Fill in the project name.
3. Fill in the case date.
4. Select the current status of the case.
5. Select the type of agreement.
6. Fill in any comments (if necessary).
7. Fill in the submitter's email address.
8. Fill in the submitter's phone number.
9. Fill in the submitter's name.
10. Fill in the submitter's title.
11. Fill in the submitter's role.
12. Fill in the submitter's department.
13. Fill in the submitter's organization.
14. Fill in the submitter's location.
15. Fill in the submitter's job title.
16. Fill in the submitter's employee ID.
17. Select the agreement signatory.
18. Fill in the signatory's name.
19. Fill in the signatory's title.
20. Fill in the signatory's department.
21. Fill in the signatory's organization.
22. Fill in the signatory's location.
23. Fill in the signatory's job title.
24. Fill in the signatory's employee ID.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **case_name** (`case_name`, `text`, required): The name of the structured case.
* **project_name** (`project_name`, `text`, required): The name of the project related to the case.
* **case_date** (`case_date`, `date`, required): The date the case was last updated.
* **project_date** (`project_date`, `date`, required): The date the project was last updated.
* **case_status** (`case_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the current status of the case.
* **agreement_type** (`agreement_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of agreement.
* **comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments (if necessary).
* **submitter_email** (`submitter_email`, `email`, optional): The submitter's email address.
* **submitter_phone** (`submitter_phone`, `text`, optional): The submitter's phone number.
* **submitter_name** (`submitter_name`, `text`, optional): The submitter's name.
* **submitter_title** (`submitter_title`, `text`, optional): The submitter's title.
* **submitter_role** (`submitter_role`, `text`, optional): The submitter's role.
* **submitter_department** (`submitter_department`, `text`, optional): The submitter's department.
* **submitter_organization** (`submitter_organization`, `text`, optional): The submitter's organization.
* **submitter_location** (`submitter_location`, `text`, optional): The submitter's location.
* **submitter_job_title** (`submitter_job_title`, `text`, optional): The submitter's job title.
* **submitter_employee_id** (`submitter_employee_id`, `text`, optional): The submitter's employee ID.
* **agreement_signatory** (`agreement_signatory`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the agreement signatory.
* **signatory_name** (`signatory_name`, `text`, optional): The signatory's name.
* **signatory_title** (`signatory_title`, `text`, optional): The signatory's title.
* **signatory_department** (`signatory_department`, `text`, optional): The signatory's department.
* **signatory_organization** (`signatory_organization`, `text`, optional): The signatory's organization.
* **signatory_location** (`signatory_location`, `text`, optional): The signatory's location.
* **signatory_job_title** (`signatory_job_title`, `text`, optional): The signatory's job title.
* **signatory_employee_id** (`signatory_employee_id`, `text`, optional): The signatory's employee ID.
