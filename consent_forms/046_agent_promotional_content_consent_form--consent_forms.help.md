# Agent Promotional Content Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain written consent from individuals for the use of their images, testimonials, and other content for promotional and marketing purposes by the Agency. This form ensures that the individual's rights are respected and that they understand how their content will be used.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your name as the participant.
2. Enter your contact email address so we can reach you in case of questions or clarification.
3. If you have a phone number, please provide it for additional contact.
4. Describe the specific project or event (if applicable).
5. Select the types of content you are authorizing for use (e.g., photographs, video footage, etc.).
6. Choose the duration of consent (1 year, 3 years, or indefinite).
7. Acknowledge that you grant the Agency the right to use the selected content for promotional and marketing purposes.
8. Choose whether you require prior approval for final content pieces.
9. Sign the form with the date of consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`): Please enter your name as the participant providing the consent.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address so we can contact you in case of questions or clarification.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): If you have a phone number, please provide it for additional contact. (Optional)
* **-- Promotional Content Consent --** (`title`): This is the header for the form.
* **-- Content Usage --** (`content_header`): This is the header for the content types section.
* **Types of content authorized** (`content_types`): Select all the types of content you are authorizing for use (e.g., Photographs, Video Footage, etc.).
* **Description of specific project or event** (`project_description`): If applicable, describe the project or event for which the content will be used.
* **Duration of Consent** (`consent_duration`): Choose how long you grant permission for us to use the content (1 year, 3 years, or indefinite).
* **-- Rights and Permissions --** (`rights_header`): This is the header for the rights and permissions section.
* **Grant of Rights** (`grant_statement`): Acknowledge that you grant the Agency the right to use the selected content for promotional and marketing purposes.
* **Prior Approval Requirement** (`approval_requirement`): Choose whether you require prior approval for final content pieces.
* **Date of Consent** (`consent_date`): Sign the form with the date of consent.
* **Revocation Notice** (`revocation_notice`): This is a reminder that you can revoke your consent at any time by providing written notice to the Agency.

## Tips

* Make sure to carefully review and understand the types of content you are authorizing for use.
* Choose the duration of consent that best suits your needs.
* If you require prior approval for final content pieces, please ensure that the Agency understands this.
* Sign the form with the date of consent once you have reviewed and agreed to the terms.
