# Experiment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Experiment Consent Form is designed to obtain informed consent from participants in research studies. It serves as a legal record of the participant's agreement to participate and outlines the essential details of the study, including the purpose, risks, and benefits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Provide your participant ID in the "Participant ID" field.
3. Write a brief description of the experiment in the "Experiment Title" field.
4. List the members of the research team in the "Research Team" field.
5. Enter the name of the research institution in the "Research Institution" field.
6. Describe the purpose of the experiment in the "Purpose of Experiment" field.
7. List any potential risks and discomforts associated with the experiment in the "Risks and Discomforts" field.
8. Explain the benefits of the experiment in the "Benefits of Experiment" field.
9. Check the box to acknowledge your informed consent in the "Informed Consent" field.
10. Sign and date the form with your participant signature and date in the "Participant Signature" and "Date Signed" fields.
11. The research investigator must also sign and date the form in the "Investigator Signature" and "Date Signed" fields.
12. Enter your email address in the "Participant Email" field.
13. Enter the contact information for the research team in the "Contact Info" field.
14. Provide the name and contact information of the contact person in the "Contact Person" field.
15. Enter the contact email and phone number in the "Contact Email" and "Contact Phone" fields, respectively.
16. Enter the address of the research institution in the "Address" field.
17. Add any additional notes about the experiment in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as a participant in the experiment.
* **Participant ID**: Enter your participant ID, which is usually a unique identifier assigned to you by the research team.
* **Experiment Title**: Briefly describe the experiment, including its purpose and scope.
* **Research Team**: List the names and positions of all team members involved in the experiment.
* **Research Institution**: Enter the name of the institution where the research will be conducted.
* **Purpose of Experiment**: Describe the main objective and goals of the experiment.
* **Risks and Discomforts**: List any potential risks or discomforts associated with the experiment.
* **Benefits of Experiment**: Explain the potential benefits and advantages of participating in the experiment.
* **Informed Consent**: Check the box to acknowledge that you understand the experiment's purpose, risks, and benefits.
* **Participant Signature**: Sign your name to confirm your agreement to participate.
* **Date Signed**: Date and time you signed the form.
* **Investigator Signature**: The research investigator must sign the form to confirm their role and responsibility.
* **Date Signed**: Date and time the research investigator signed the form.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address for contact and communication purposes.
* **Research Institution**: Enter the name of the institution conducting the research.
* **Contact Person**: Provide the name and contact information of the person responsible for the experiment.
* **Contact Info**: Enter the contact information for the research team, including name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the research institution.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the experiment.
