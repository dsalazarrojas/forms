# Research Data Anonymization Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit requests for anonymization of research data. Please note that anonymization may be subject to certain restrictions or requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all required and optional fields as necessary.
2. For fields marked as `optional`, feel free to provide information if it is relevant to your request.
3. Select the correct anonymization method from the options provided.
4. If you have any additional comments or requests, please include them in the `Notes` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name (1)**: Your name as listed on your personnel file or as used in your official correspondence.
* **Contact Email (2)**: Your email address where we can reach you with further questions or instructions.
* **Contact Phone (3)**: A contact phone number where we can reach you with further questions or instructions.
* **Research Institution (4)**: The name of the institution where your research is conducted.
* **Department (5)**: The department or unit within the institution where your research is conducted.
* **Research Project (6)**: A brief title of the research project.
* **Project Description (7)**: A brief description of the research project.
* **Data Description (8)**: A description of the data that needs anonymization.
* **Request Date (9)**: The date you are requesting anonymization for.
* **Anonymization Method (10)**: Select the method you desire for anonymization. Please see our anonymization options for details.
* **Notes (11)**: Additional comments or instructions that may be helpful for us to process your request.
* **Research Area (12)**: Select all relevant research areas that your project falls under. Please see below for more information.
