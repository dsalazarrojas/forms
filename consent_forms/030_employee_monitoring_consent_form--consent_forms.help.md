# Employee Monitoring Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to document employee monitoring consent, including the activities, frequency, and methods of monitoring. It is essential to ensure that employees understand their rights and responsibilities regarding employee monitoring.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your employee name in the "employee name" field.
3. Select your role from the "role" field (e.g., Employee, Manager, Supervisor).
4. Choose the activities that you are comfortable with being monitored from the "monitoring activities" field (e.g., performance, behavior, location).
5. Select the frequency of monitoring that you are comfortable with from the "monitoring frequency" field (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
6. Choose the method of monitoring that you are comfortable with from the "monitoring method" field (e.g., Video camera, Microphone, GPS).
7. Indicate whether you consent to monitoring from the "consent given" field (e.g., "True", "False").
8. If you are consenting to monitoring, enter the date and time you gave your consent.
9. Enter your employee ID if you have one.
10. Add any relevant notes about monitoring in the "monitoring notes" field.
11. Add any additional notes about your consent in the "consent note" field.
12. Choose whether you have agreed to monitoring from the "employee agreement" field (e.g., Agreed, Not agreed).
13. If you have agreed to monitoring, sign your name in the "signature" field.
14. Enter the date and time you signed.
15. Enter your IP address if you have one.
16. Enter your MAC address if you have one.
17. Enter your device model if you have one.
18. Enter your browser type if you have one.
19. Enter your OS version if you have one.
20. Enter your screen resolution if you have one.
21. If you have a different browser type, enter it in the "other browser types" field.
22. If you have a different OS version, enter it in the "other OS versions" field.
23. Add any additional information about your monitoring conduct in the "monitoring conduct" field.
24. Add any additional notes about you as an employee in the "employee notes" field.

* **employee name**: Your name as an employee.
* **role**: Your role in the organization (e.g., Employee, Manager, Supervisor).

* **monitoring method**: The method of monitoring that you are comfortable with (e.g., Video camera, Microphone, GPS).
* **consent given**: Whether you consent to monitoring ("True" or "False").
* **date given**: The date you gave your consent.
* **time given**: The time you gave your consent.
