# Website Publishing Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Website Publishing Permission Form is designed to obtain consent from website owners or users to publish their website's information on our platform.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with your accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Provide your website name and URL.
3. Confirm your consent to publish your website's information.
4. Optional: provide additional contact information such as email address and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (Text, required: false)
	+ This field is for you to input any additional information about yourself.
* **User email** (Email, required: false)
	+ Please enter your email address so we can contact you with updates and information about your website.
* **Permission Date** (Date, required: false)
	+ This date field is for you to specify the date when you would like to publish your website's information.
* **Website Name** (Text, required: false)
	+ Enter the name of your website.
* **User Consent** (Select multiple, required: true)
	+ Choose "Yes" to confirm that you consent to publish your website's information.
* **Comments** (Text, required: false)
	+ Add any additional comments or notes you would like to provide about your website.
* **Website URL** (Text, required: false)
	+ Enter the URL of your website so we can access it for publishing.
* **Contact Info** (Text, required: false)
	+ Provide any additional contact information you would like to share with us.
* **Contact email** (Email, required: false)
	+ Enter your contact email address for us to reach out to you with questions or updates.
* **Contact phone** (Text, required: false)
	+ Enter your contact phone number for us to reach out to you with questions or updates.

Note: If you're unsure about any of the fields, please ask us for clarification or guidance.
