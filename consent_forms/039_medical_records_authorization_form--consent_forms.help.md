# Medical Records Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect the necessary information for a patient to authorize the use of their medical records for healthcare services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the **Patient Name** field.
2. Enter the provider's name in the **Provider Name** field.
3. Enter the date of service in the **Date of Service** field. This is the date the patient received the healthcare service that requires access to their medical records.
4. Enter the patient's date of birth in the **Date of Birth** field.
5. Enter the patient's medical record number in the **Medical Record Number** field. This is a unique identifier for the patient's medical record.
6. Select the authorization type in the **Authorization Type** field. Options include **HIPAA**, **State**, and **Other**. Choose the type of authorization that best fits the purpose of the request.
7. Enter the start date of the authorization in the **Authorization Start Date** field. This is the date the authorization begins.
8. Enter the contact number in the **Contact Number** field. This is the phone number of the contact person or organization.
9. Enter the email address in the **Email** field. This is the contact person or organization's email address.
10. Enter the address in the **Address** field. This is the contact person or organization's address.
11. Enter the emergency contact name in the **Emergency Contact Name** field. This is the name of the person who can be contacted in case of an emergency.
12. Enter the emergency contact phone number in the **Emergency Contact Phone** field. This is the phone number of the emergency contact.
13. Enter the emergency contact relation in the **Emergency Contact Relation** field. This is the relation of the emergency contact to the patient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (text, required): Enter the patient's full name, including their first name and last name.
* **Provider Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the healthcare provider who is requesting access to the patient's medical records.
* **Date of Service** (date, required): Enter the date the patient received the healthcare service that requires access to their medical records.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Medical Record Number** (text, required): Enter a unique identifier for the patient's medical record.
* **Authorization Type** (select_one, required): Choose the type of authorization that best fits the purpose of the request. Options include:
	+ **HIPAA**: Authorization for healthcare services under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act.
	+ **State**: Authorization for healthcare services under state law.
	+ **Other**: Choose this option if the authorization is for a different purpose.
* **Authorization Start Date** (date, required): Enter the date the authorization begins.
* **Contact Number** (text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person or organization.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person or organization.
* **Address** (text, required): Enter the contact person or organization's address.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the person who can be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relation** (text, required): Enter the relation of the emergency contact to the patient.
