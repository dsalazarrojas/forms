# research_publication_appearance_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect consent for the appearance of a research publication. It is used to document the details of a research publication and the consent given by the relevant parties. This information is necessary for maintaining accurate records and ensuring compliance with research publication guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the type of publication (journal, conference, poster, video, or other) for which you are seeking consent.
2. Enter the name of the publication in the "publication_name" field.
3. Provide the URL of the publication (if applicable).
4. Choose how the publication will be made public (face, voice, name, or other).
5. Select the date when consent was given.
6. Choose who gave consent (researcher or participant).
7. Choose the status of the consent (pending, active, or inactive).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **research_publication_appearance_consent_form_page_1**: This field is not applicable as it's not a field on the form.
* **research_publication_form_consent_given_by**: This field allows you to select who gave consent for the publication. Please choose between "researcher" or "participant".
* **publication_type**: Select the type of publication for which consent is being sought. Options include "journal", "conference", "poster", "video", or "other".
* **publication_name**: Enter the name of the publication for which consent is being sought.
* **publication_url**: If the publication is available online, provide the URL where it can be accessed.
* **appearance_on_publication**: Choose how the publication will be made public. Options include "face", "voice", "name", or "other".
* **consent_date**: Enter the date when consent was given for the publication.
* **consent_given_by**: Select who gave consent for the publication. Options are "researcher" or "participant".
* **consent_status**: Choose the status of the consent. Options are "pending", "active", or "inactive". 

## Tips
- Ensure that you are seeking consent for the correct type of publication.
- Double-check the publication details before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact your supervisor or colleague for guidance.
