# Smartwatch Usage Acknowledgement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and manage smartwatch usage within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the user information fields with the relevant details about the user who is using the smartwatch.
2. Select the user's role within the organization.
3. Choose the department where the user belongs.
4. Select the type of device being used (smartwatch or smartband).
5. Enter the start and end dates for the usage period.
6. Provide a reason for the return of the device.
7. Enter any comments or additional information.
8. Specify the tool assigned to the user.
9. Select who submitted the form (likely an administrator or manager).
10. Enter the title of the person who submitted the form.
11. Select the date the form was submitted.
12. Specify the person or department assigned to the user.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`user_information`): Enter the name and other relevant details about the user who is using the smartwatch.
* **Role** (`user_role`): Select the role of the user within the organization (Administrator, Developer, or User).
* **Department** (`department`): Choose the department where the user belongs (Sales, Marketing, or Support).
* **Device Type** (`device_type`): Select the type of device being used (Smartwatch or Smartband).
* **Start Date** (`start_date`): Enter the start date of the usage period.
* **End Date** (`end_date`): Enter the end date of the usage period.
* **Reason for Return** (`reason_for_return`): Provide a reason for returning the device.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Enter any additional comments or information.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): Specify the tool assigned to the user.
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`): Select who submitted the form (e.g., Jimmy or Jane).
* **Form Submitted By Title** (`form_submitted_by_title`): Enter the title of the person who submitted the form.
* **Form Submitted On** (`submitted_on`): Select the date the form was submitted.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`): Specify the person or department assigned to the user.
* **Department** (`department`): Choose the department where the user belongs (Sales, Marketing, or Support).
* **User Information 2** (`user_information_2`): Enter the name and other relevant details about the secondary user (if applicable).
* **Role 2** (`user_role_2`): Select the role of the secondary user (Administrator, Developer, or User).
* **Assigned Tool 2** (`assigned_tool_2`): Specify the tool assigned to the secondary user.
* **Form Submitted On 2** (`submitted_on_2`): Select the date the form was submitted for the secondary user.
* **Form Submitted By Title 2** (`submitted_by_title_2`): Enter the title of the person who submitted the form for the secondary user.
