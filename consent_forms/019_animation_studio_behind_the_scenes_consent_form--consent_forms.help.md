# Animation Studio Behind The Scenes Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is required for all visitors to the animation studio who will be participating in behind-the-scenes tours or visits. It is a legal agreement that outlines the terms under which the visitor's image, audio, and video recordings will be used by the studio for promotional and commercial purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your **Full Name**, **Email Address**, and **Phone Number**.
2. If you are under 18, please enter your **Date of Birth**.
3. Select the type of **Tour or Visit** you will be experiencing.
4. Indicate your **Group Affiliation**, if applicable.
5. Choose how you would like to be **Identified as a Visitor**.
6. Provide your permission for the studio to **Photograph**, **Record**, and **Film or Video Record** you.
7. Indicate your consent for your **Name and Likeness Usage**.
8. Decide on the **Distribution and Usage** of your recorded material, including **Social Media Sharing** and **Promotional Materials**.
9. Specify your consent for **Commercial Use**.
10. Confirm your consent for **Derivatives Permission**.
11. If you are under 18, provide your **Parent or Guardian's Name** and consent for the above permissions.
12. Confirm your parent or guardian's consent for all terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your complete name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter the email address associated with your visit.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth if you are under 18.
* **Tour or Visit** (`tour_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of tour or visit you will be experiencing.
* **Group or Organization** (`group_affiliation`, text, optional): If you are visiting with a group, enter their name.
* **Visitor Type** (`visitor_type`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to be identified as a visitor.
* **Permission to photograph you during the visit** (`photo_permission`, select_one, required): Choose your consent for being photographed.
* **Permission to film or video record you** (`video_permission`, select_one, required): Choose your consent for being filmed or video recorded.
* **Permission to record your voice or statements** (`audio_permission`, select_one, required): Choose your consent for recording your voice or statements.
* **I grant permission to use my name in credits** (`credit_name`, select_one, required): Choose your consent for having your name used in credits.
* **I grant permission to use my image and likeness** (`likeness_usage`, select_one, required): Choose your consent for using your image and likeness.
* **Permission for social media sharing** (`social_media_use`, select_one, required): Choose your consent for sharing on social media.
* **Permission for promotional materials** (`promotional_use`, select_one, required): Choose your consent for promotional use.
* **Permission for commercial use** (`commercial_use`, select_one, required): Choose your consent for commercial use.
* **Permission to edit or modify content** (`derivatives_permission`, select_one, required): Choose your consent for editing or modifying your recorded material.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Enter your parent or guardian's name if you are under 18.
* **Parent or guardian consents to above permissions** (`parent_consent_agreement`, select_one, optional): Confirm your parent or guardian's consent for all terms.

## Tips

* Please review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask a member of the studio staff for clarification.
* This form is required for all behind-the-scenes visits, tours, and events.
* By signing this form, you are granting the animation studio permission to use your image, audio, and video recordings for promotional and commercial purposes.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the studio administration.
