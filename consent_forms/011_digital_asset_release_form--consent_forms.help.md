# Digital Asset Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Digital Asset Release Form is a document that grants permission for creators to use their digital assets in various contexts, including commercial and non-commercial purposes. It's an essential tool for ensuring that creators understand how their contributions will be used and that the organization is compliant with relevant laws and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with a asterisk (\*).
2. Choose the correct asset type from the select options (Asset Type).
3. Specify the use permission (Commercial Use, Non-Commercial Use, or Both).
4. Enter the intended use of the asset (if applicable).
5. Provide your contact information and signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Creator Name** (`creator_name`, text, not required): Enter your name as the creator of the digital asset.
* **Asset Type** (`asset_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of digital asset you are releasing (Image, Video, or Audio).
* **Asset Date Of Release** (`asset_date_of_release`, date, not required): Enter the date the digital asset was released or created (if applicable).
* **Use Permission** (`use_permission`, select_one, required): Select the intended use of the digital asset (Commercial Use, Non-Commercial Use, or Both).
* **Intended Use** (`intended_use`, text, not required): Describe the specific purpose of using the digital asset (if applicable).
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, not required): Enter your contact information, including your name and email address or phone number.
* **Creator Signature** (`creator_signature`, text, not required): Enter your signature, which confirms that you have reviewed and agreed to the terms of use.
* **Date Of Signature** (`date_of_signature`, date, not required): Enter the date you signed this document.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, not required): Enter the tool or software used to create the digital asset (if applicable).
