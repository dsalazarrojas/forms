# Facility Rules And Regulations Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Facility Rules And Regulations Acknowledgment" form is designed to ensure users are aware of and commit to following the rules and regulations of the facility. This form collects personal and contact information, as well as specific details about the user's role, allergies, and awareness of emergency procedures and equipment usage.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by reading through the form carefully to ensure you understand each field's purpose.
2. Provide your name as it appears on your ID in the "What is your name?" field.
3. Enter your contact information in the "What is your contact information?" field.
4. Answer the "Do you have any questions or concerns about the facility's rules and regulations?" field with any relevant thoughts or concerns.
5. Confirm that you have read and understood the facility's rules and regulations in the "Have you read and understood the facility's rules and regulations?" field.
6. Indicate your role at the facility in the "What is your role at the facility?" field.
7. If applicable, describe any dietary restrictions or allergies you have in the "Do you have any dietary restrictions or allergies that the facility should be aware of?" field.
8. Confirm that you are aware of the emergency exits and procedures in the "Are you aware of the emergency exits and emergency procedures in the facility?" field.
9. Explain how you will be using the facility's resources and facilities in the "How will you be using the facility's resources and facilities?" field.
10. Confirm that you have received training on the use of the facility's equipment and resources in the "Have you received training on the use of the facility's equipment and resources?" field.
11. Confirm that you understand the consequences of not following the facility's rules and regulations in the "Do you understand the consequences of not following the facility's rules and regulations?" field.
12. Indicate that you are aware of the facility's policies on reporting incidents or near misses in the "Are you aware of the facility's policies on reporting incidents or near misses?" field.
13. Finally, confirm that you agree to abide by the facility's rules and regulations by checking the "Do you agree to abide by the facility's rules and regulations?" checkbox.
