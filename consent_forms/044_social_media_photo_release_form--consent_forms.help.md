# Social Media Photo Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect consent from individuals for the release and usage of their photos on various social media platforms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the General Information section with your name and contact information.
2. In the Photo Details section, provide a brief description of the photos you are releasing, including their location and date taken.
3. In the Photo Usage section, specify how you would like your photos to be used, such as for promotional purposes or for a specific event.
4. In the Release Information section, provide details about any restrictions or limitations on the usage of your photos.
5. In the Release Agreement section, sign and date the form to indicate your consent.
6. Finally, click the Submit button to submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information
* **General Information** (`name`, `email`, `phone`, `address`, `city`, `state`, `zip`, `country`, `phone_number`, `mobile_number`, required/optional): 
  This field is used to collect your contact information, including your name, email, phone number, and address. Please fill in this section with accurate and up-to-date information.

### Photo Details
* **Photo Details** (`description`, `location`, `date_taken`, required/optional): 
  In this field, please provide a brief description of the photos you are releasing, including where they were taken and the date they were taken.

### Photo Usage
* **Photo Usage** (`usage`, required/optional): 
  This field is used to specify how you would like your photos to be used. Please select all applicable options from the list provided, such as "for promotional purposes" or "for a specific event".

### Release Information
* **Release Information** (`restrictions`, `limitations`, required/optional): 
  In this field, please provide any restrictions or limitations on the usage of your photos. For example, if you do not want your photos used for commercial purposes, please specify this here.

### Release Agreement
* **Release Agreement** (`agreement`, required/optional): 
  This is where you will sign and date the form to indicate your consent to the usage of your photos.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and honestly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* Make sure to keep a copy of this form for your records.
