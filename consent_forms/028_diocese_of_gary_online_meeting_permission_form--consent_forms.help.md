# Diocese Of Gary Online Meeting Permission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Diocese Of Gary Online Meeting Permission Form is a document that captures the details of an online meeting, including meeting name, date, time, purpose, location, and attendees. This form is designed to ensure that parish leaders have the necessary permission to gather online, allowing them to effectively communicate with their community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the meeting name in the "Meeting Name" field.
2. Select the meeting date and time using the "Meeting Date" and "Start Time" fields.
3. Enter the purpose of the meeting in the "Purpose of Meeting" field.
4. Enter the location of the meeting in the "Location" field.
5. Select the attendees who will be participating in the meeting from the "Attendees" options.
6. Enter your contact information in the "Email" and "Phone" fields.
7. Provide a brief statement in the "Permission to Gather" field, explaining why you need to gather online.
8. Sign and date the form using your "Parish Leader Signature" field.
9. Enter the date you signed the form in the "Date Signed" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Name (text)**: Enter the name of the meeting, e.g., "Monthly Parish Council Meeting".
* **Meeting Date (date)**: Select the date of the meeting, e.g., "2022-02-15".
* **Start Time (time)**: Enter the start time of the meeting, e.g., "14:00".
* **End Time (time)**: Enter the end time of the meeting, e.g., "15:00".
* **Purpose of Meeting (text)**: Briefly describe the purpose of the meeting, e.g., "To discuss parish business and events".
* **Location (text)**: Enter the location of the meeting, e.g., "Online via Zoom".
* **Attendees (select_multiple)**: Select the attendees who will be participating in the meeting, e.g., "Parish Leader", "Parishioner".
* **Email (email)**: Enter your work email address.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your work phone number.
* **Permission to Gather (text)**: Provide a brief statement, e.g., "I need to gather online to discuss important parish matters."
* **Parish Leader Signature (text)**: Sign and date the form using your parish leader's signature.
* **Date Signed (date)**: Enter the date you signed the form, e.g., "2022-02-16".
