# Tutoring Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for students to agree to the terms of a tutoring contract.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in its entirety. Each field is required unless otherwise noted.
2. Enter your name in `form_2` as it will be used as a reference point for further communication.
3. Enter the date you wish to start the tutoring sessions in `form_4`.
4. Enter your email address in `form_6` so the tutor can contact you.
5. Select 'Yes' for `form_8` if you agree to the terms of the contract, otherwise 'No'.
6. If applicable, select multiple options for `form_11` that apply to your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form_1**: This is where you will enter the name of the form to be used for reference.
* **form_2** (Text): Enter your name as it will be used as a reference point for further communication.
* **form_3** (Number): Enter the number of hours you wish to spend in tutoring sessions.
* **form_4** (Date): Enter the date you wish to start the tutoring sessions.
* **form_7** (Text): Enter a description of your understanding of your role and responsibilities as a student in this tutoring relationship.
* **form_8** (Select One): Select 'Yes' if you agree to the terms of the contract, otherwise 'No'.
* **form_10** (Select Multiple): This field is not currently being used in this form.
* **form_11** (Select Multiple): Select multiple options that apply to your situation as a student. This could be things like agreeing to meet with the tutor, to receive emails from the tutor, etc.
* **form_9** (Note): This field is a space to enter any additional comments or notes about the tutoring contract.
