# Facility Photography Publication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture the consent of a facility manager or administrator regarding the use and publication of photographs taken at their facility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary permissions and approvals from your facility's management before submitting this form.
2. Fill in the required fields:
	* Facility ID: Enter the unique identifier of your facility.
	* Photo taken by: Enter the name of the photographer who took the photo.
	* Photo date: Enter the date the photo was taken.
	* Photo location: Enter the location where the photo was taken.
	* Photo purpose: Enter the intended use or purpose of the photo.
3. Select your response to the following questions:
	* Photo release: Decide whether you consent to the photo being published (Yes/No).
	* Photo sharing: Decide whether you consent to the photo being shared on our media channels (Yes/No).
	* Facility sharing: Decide whether you consent to the facility being shared on our media channels (Yes/No).
