# Educational Institution Partnership Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a partnership consent form for educational institutions to partner with our organization. It collects essential information about the partner institution, including contact details and a signature page confirming their consent to the partnership terms.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and email address in the **User Name** and **Email** fields, which are required fields to identify you as the partner representative.
2. Enter the name of your educational institution in the **Institution Name** field.
3. Provide your contact information in the **Contact Number** field.
4. Select the **Role** you hold at the institution.
5. If you want to add an **Address**, you can do so in the **Address** field, but it's not required.
6. Enter the date of the signature in the **Date** field, if you're signing on behalf of the institution.
7. In the **Terms and Conditions** field, please select **I have read and understand the terms and conditions** to confirm your understanding of the partnership agreement.
8. Sign the **Signature** field on the last page to confirm your consent to the partnership.
9. If you're signing on behalf of someone else, you can add their name in the **Representative Name** field.
10. Provide your institutional email address in the **Institution Email** field, if applicable.
11. Finally, sign the **Submitter Signature** field to confirm your consent to the partnership terms.
