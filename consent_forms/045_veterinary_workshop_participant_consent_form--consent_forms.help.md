# Veterinary Workshop Participant Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Workshop Participant Consent Form is a crucial document that ensures you're aware of and agree to the workshop's terms and conditions, as well as your contact information and emergency contact details. By filling out this form, you'll be able to participate in the workshop and be informed about important updates and communications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Participant Name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Provide your Email address in the "Email" field.
3. If applicable, enter your Contact Name in the "Contact Name" field.
4. Check the "Relationship" field to indicate whether you have a pre-existing relationship with a participant, such as "Yes" or "No".
5. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field, if applicable.
6. Check your Workshop Participation preference in the "Workshop Participation" field, whether you'd like to participate in the workshop or not.
7. Choose how you'd like to be contacted for workshop communications in the "Communications" field.
8. Check the "Signed Consent" field to indicate you've read and agreed to the workshop's terms and conditions.
9. If applicable, enter your Emergency Contact's Name in the "Emergency Contact" field.
10. Check the "Relationship" field for your Emergency Contact, whether they're a family member, friend, or another relation.
11. Enter your Emergency Contact's Phone number in the "Phone" field.
12. Finally, check the "Signed Consent" field to confirm you've read and agreed to the workshop's terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as a workshop participant.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for workshop communications.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter a contact name for someone you'd like to be contacted in case of emergencies.
* **Relationship** (`contact_relation`, select_one, optional): Check "Yes" if you have a pre-existing relationship with a participant, "No" otherwise.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for workshop communications.
* **Workshop Participation** (`workshop_participation`, select_multiple, optional): Check "Yes" to participate in the workshop, "No" otherwise.
* **Communications** (`communications`, select_one, optional): Choose how you'd like to be contacted for workshop updates and communications: via email, workshop updates and reminders, or never.
* **Signed Consent** (`consent_signed`, note, required): Check to confirm you've read and agreed to the workshop's terms and conditions.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_relationship`, select_one, optional): Check "Yes" if your emergency contact is a family member, "No" otherwise.
* **Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Signed Consent** (`workshop_participation_signed`, note, required): Check to confirm you've read and agreed to the workshop's terms and conditions.
