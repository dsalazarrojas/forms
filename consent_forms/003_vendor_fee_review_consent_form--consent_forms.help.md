# vendor_fee_review_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vendor_fee_review_consent_form is used to review and consent to vendor fees. This form is designed to collect information about vendor fees and to ensure that the vendor is aware of the terms and conditions of the review process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the vendor fee review form carefully and ensure that you are completing it for the correct purpose.
2. Enter your consent to the vendor fee review in the vendor_fee_review_consent_form_page_1 field.
3. Select one or more options from the vendor_fee_review_consent field.
4. Enter your contact details in the contact_details field.
5. Enter the vendor's name and email address in the vendor_name and email fields, respectively.
6. Enter the vendor's phone number in the phone field.
7. Add any comments or notes in the comments field if necessary.
8. Select the person who reviewed the vendor's fee in the reviewed_by field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Fee Review Consent Form** (`vendor_fee_review_consent_form`, text, optional): This is the first field of the form where you can enter your consent to the vendor fee review.
* **Vendor Fee Review Consent** (`vendor_fee_review_consent`, select multiple): Select one or more options from this field to consent to the vendor fee review.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter your contact details in this field, including your name and any other relevant information.
* **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, text, optional): Enter the vendor's name in this field.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the vendor's email address in this field.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the vendor's phone number in this field.
* **Signed** (`signed`, note, optional): Add any notes or comments in this field, if necessary.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes in this field, if necessary.
* **Reviewed By** (`reviewed_by`, select one): Select the person who reviewed the vendor's fee in this field.

## Tips
* Ensure that you complete the form correctly and accurately.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant person or team for assistance.
