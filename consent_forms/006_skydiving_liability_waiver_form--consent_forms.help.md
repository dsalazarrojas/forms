# Skydiving Liability Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skydiving Liability Waiver Form is a document that outlines the risks associated with skydiving and requires the participant to acknowledge and accept those risks. It's essential to read and understand the form carefully before proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your name, address, email, and phone number in the respective fields.
2.  Acknowledge that you understand the risks associated with skydiving and accept them.
3.  Describe any medical conditions that may impact your ability to participate in skydiving.
4.  Provide information about your emergency contact, including their name, phone number, and relationship to you.
5.  Sign the form to confirm that you have read and understood the risks and the form's requirements.
6.  Sign the emergency contact acknowledgement to confirm that you have informed them of the risks and the form's requirements.
7.  Sign the form with your signature and date.
8.  Provide your staff's information and sign the form to confirm that you have informed them of the risks and the form's requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Address**: Enter your home address where you can be contacted in case of an emergency.
*   **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted in case of an emergency.
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted in case of an emergency.
*   **Risk Acknowledge**: This section explains the risks associated with skydiving. Read this section carefully and acknowledge that you understand these risks.
*   **Safety Procedure**: Describe any safety procedures that you have learned or have been advised to take during the skydiving experience.
*   **Medical Condition**: If you have any medical conditions that may affect your ability to participate in skydiving, describe them in this section.
*   **Name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact.
*   **Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
*   **Relationship**: Describe your relationship with your emergency contact (e.g., parent, guardian, spouse).
*   **Safety and Risk**: This section explains the safety and risk procedures that will be followed during the skydiving experience.
*   **Signature Date**: Sign and date the form to confirm that you have read and understood the form.
*   **Signature**: Sign your name to confirm that you have read and understood the form.
*   **Emergency Contact Signature**: Sign your emergency contact's signature to confirm that they have read and understood the form.
*   **Participant Signature**: Sign the form to confirm that you have read and understood the form.
*   **Staff Signature**: Sign the form to confirm that you have informed them of the risks and the form's requirements.
*   **Safety Officer Signature**: Sign the form to confirm that you have been informed of the risks and the form's requirements.
*   **Acknowledge**: Sign this section to confirm that you have informed your emergency contact of the risks and the form's requirements.
*   **Emergency Contact Info**: Provide any additional information about your emergency contact that we should know in case of an emergency.
*   **Staff Info**: Provide any additional information about your staff contact that we should know in case of an emergency.
*   **Emergency Contact Staff**: Sign the form to confirm that you have informed them of the risks and the form's requirements.
*   **Participant Info**: Provide any additional information about yourself that we should know in case of an emergency.
*   **Risk Information**: Provide any additional information about the risks associated with skydiving that we should know.

## Tips

*   Make sure to read and understand the form carefully before signing it.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, ask your staff or safety officer for clarification.
*   Sign the form with your signature and date to confirm that you have read and understood the form.
*   Make sure your emergency contact signs the form to confirm that they have read and understood the form.
*   Ensure that your staff and safety officer sign the form to confirm that they have informed you of the risks and the form's requirements.
