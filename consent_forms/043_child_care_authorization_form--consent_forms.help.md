# Child Care Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for authorizing child care providers to care for your child. It collects necessary information about the child, the care provider, and the care arrangement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and address as a parent or guardian.
2. Select your relationship to the child (Parent, Legal guardian, Custodian, or Other).
3. Enter your phone number and email address for contact purposes.
4. Provide your child's name, date of birth, and age.
5. Select the type of care (Full-time, Part-time, After-school, Weekend, Emergency only, or Other).
6. Choose how you would like to authorize emergency contact information.
7. Decide on medical authorization (Authorized, Not authorized, Limited authorization).
8. Specify approved disciplinary methods.
9. List any special needs, allergies, or restrictions.
10. Authorize or deny regular activities and outings.
11. Choose whether you grant permission for photos or videos.
12. Agree to payment terms and fees.
13. Finally, confirm your authorization to care for your child.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (Parent/guardian name, Text, Required): Enter your name as a parent or guardian.
* **Relationship to Child** (Relationship to child, Select one, Required): Choose how you are related to the child (Parent, Legal guardian, Custodian, or Other).
* **Parent Address** (Parent address, Text, Required): Enter your address as a parent or guardian.
* **Phone Number** (Phone number, Text, Required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email Address** (Email address, Email, Required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Child's Name** (Child's name, Text, Required): Enter your child's name.
* **Child's Date of Birth** (Child's date of birth, Date, Required): Enter your child's date of birth.
* **Child's Age** (Child's age, Number, Required): Enter your child's age.
* **Childcare Provider Name** (Child care provider name, Text, Required): Enter the name of the child care provider.
* **Facility Name (if applicable)** (Facility name, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the facility where care will be provided.
* **Provider Address** (Provider address, Text, Required): Enter the address of the care provider.
* **Provider Phone Number** (Provider phone number, Text, Required): Enter the phone number of the care provider.
* **Start Date of Care** (Start date of care, Date, Required): Enter the start date of care for your child.
* **End Date of Care (if applicable)** (End date of care, Date, Optional): Enter the end date of care for your child, if applicable.
* **Care Schedule (days and hours)** (Care schedule, Text, Required): Specify the care schedule for your child (e.g., Monday to Friday, 8-3).
* **Type of Care** (Type of care, Select one, Required): Choose the type of care (Full-time, Part-time, After-school, Weekend, Emergency only, or Other).
* **Emergency Contact Information** (Emergency contact information, Text, Required): Enter the emergency contact information for your child.
* **Authorization for Medical Decisions** (Authorization for medical decisions, Select one, Required): Decide on medical authorization (Authorized, Not authorized, Limited authorization).
* **Approved Disciplinary Methods** (Approved disciplinary methods, Text, Required): Specify approved disciplinary methods.
* **Special Needs, Allergies, or Restrictions** (Special needs, allergies, or restrictions, Text, Optional): List any special needs, allergies, or restrictions.
* **Authorization for Regular Activities and Outings** (Authorization for regular activities and outings, Select one, Required): Choose whether you authorize regular activities and outings (Authorized, Requires advance permission, Not authorized).
* **Permission for Photos or Videos** (Permission for photos or videos, Select one, Required): Choose whether you grant permission for photos or videos (Authorized, Not authorized).
* **Payment Terms and Fees** (Payment terms and fees, Text, Required): Agree to payment terms and fees.
* **I Authorize This Provider to Care for My Child** (I authorize this provider to care for my child, Select one, Required): Confirm your authorization for the care provider to care for your child.
