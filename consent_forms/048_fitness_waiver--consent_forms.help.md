# Fitness Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from new members of the fitness program, including emergency contact details, to ensure we can reach them in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **New Member Waiver**: Enter your name to confirm you are a new member of the fitness program.
2. **Emergency Contact Information**: Select one of the options to indicate who should be contacted in case of an emergency. Choose "Parent" if you are a minor, "Guardian" if you are a guardian, or "Other" if you would like to specify a different contact.
3. **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
4. **Emergency Contact Address**: Enter the address of your emergency contact.
5. **Relationship**: Briefly describe your relationship with your emergency contact (e.g., mother, father, sister, etc.).
