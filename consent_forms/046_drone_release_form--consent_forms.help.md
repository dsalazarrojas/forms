# Drone Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Drone Release Form is a document used to record the release of a drone. It captures essential information about the drone, its location, and the circumstances surrounding its release. Completing this form ensures that all necessary details are captured, making it easier to handle and maintain accurate records.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by providing the initial details about the drone, including its name, operator, and owner.
2. Enter the location where the drone was released.
3. Specify the date and time of the release.
4. Add a brief description of the reason for the release (if applicable).
5. If any comments are relevant, include them in the Comments section.
6. Choose whether the drone has been "Released" or "Not Released".
7. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Drone Name** (`drone_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the drone being released.
* **Operator** (`operator`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or entity responsible for releasing the drone.
* **Agent** (`agent`, text, optional): Enter the name or entity that will take custody of the drone after its release.
* **Owner** (`owner`, text, optional): Enter the name or entity that owns the drone being released.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the exact location where the drone was released.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the drone was released.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the release.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): This field is for recording the signature of the person releasing the drone.
* **Release** (`release`, select one, optional): Choose whether the drone has been released or not.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, optional): Add a brief explanation for the reason of the release.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional relevant comments about the drone release.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): This is the final button to complete the form.

Tips:
* Always provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure accurate records.
* Complete all fields correctly to avoid any confusion or delays.
* Review the form for any errors before submitting it.
* Ensure all relevant details are captured for easy tracking and reference.
