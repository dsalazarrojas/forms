# school_based_sealant_permission_slip_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain a sealant permission slip for a student participating in a school-based program. It collects information about the student, school, and program they are enrolled in, as well as any allergies, health conditions, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Complete each field as instructed, making sure to provide accurate and complete information.
2. Select the correct contact type for the person providing the information (Contact Person, Parent, School Administrator, Health Care Provider, or Other).
3. Enter the student's contact phone number in the format XXX-XXX-XXXX.
4. Enter the student's email address in a valid format (e.g. `user@example.com`).
5. Enter the student's address in the format XXXX XXXX, XXXX (e.g. 123 Main St, Anytown, USA 12345).
6. Enter the name of the school the student attends.
7. Enter the student's name.
8. Enter the student's date of birth.
9. Enter the name of the program the student is participating in.
10. Enter the start and end dates of the program.
11. (Optional) If the student has allergies or health conditions, enter them in the respective fields.
12. Enter any other relevant information about the student.
13. Select the type of sealant to be used.
14. Enter the frequency and dosage of the sealant to be used.
15. Enter the start and end dates for the sealant.
16. Enter any notes about the sealant.
17. Enter any allergies or health conditions related to the sealant.
18. Enter any other relevant information about the sealant.
19. Sign and date the form with the sealant signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **contact_name**: Select the type of contact person (Contact Person's Name, Parent's Name, School Administrator, Health Care Provider, or Other) that best describes the person providing the information.
* **contact_phone**: Enter the student's contact phone number in the format XXX-XXX-XXXX.
* **email**: Enter the student's email address in a valid format (e.g. `user@example.com`).
* **address**: Enter the student's address in the format XXXX XXXX, XXXX (e.g. 123 Main St, Anytown, USA 12345).
* **school_name**: Enter the name of the school the student attends.
* **student_name**: Enter the student's name.
* **student_dob**: Enter the student's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **program_name**: Enter the name of the program the student is participating in.
* **program_date**: Enter the start and end dates of the program.
* **school_administrator**: (Optional) If this field is filled out, enter the name of the school administrator.
* **health_care_provider**: (Optional) If this field is filled out, enter the name of the health care provider.
* **other**: (Optional) Enter any other relevant information about the student.
* **sealant_type**: Select the type of sealant to be used.
* **sealant_frequency**: Enter the frequency of the sealant to be used.
* **sealant_dosage**: Enter the dosage of the sealant to be used.
* **sealant_start_date**: Enter the start date of the sealant.
* **sealant_end_date**: Enter the end date of the sealant.
* **sealant_notes**: Enter any notes about the sealant.
* **sealant_allergies**: (Optional) If the student has allergies related to the sealant, enter them here.
* **sealant_important_health_conditions**: (Optional) If the student has health conditions related to the sealant, enter them here.
* **sealant_other**: (Optional) Enter any other relevant information about the sealant.
* **sealant_comments**: (Optional) Enter any comments about the sealant.
* **sealant_signature_date**: Sign and date the form with the sealant signature.
* **sealant_signature_name**: Enter the name of the person signing the form.
