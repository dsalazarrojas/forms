# Club Newsletter Story Feature Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from feature recipients for their story or feature to be published in the Club Newsletter. The purpose of the form is to ensure that feature recipients are aware of and agree to the terms of publication, including when and how their story will be shared.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out each section of the form with accurate and complete information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure that you provide all required information.
3. Submit the form when you have filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feature Recipient Name** (Feature Recipient Name, text, required): This is where you enter your name as the person being featured in the Club Newsletter.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further details.
* **Date of Consent** (Date of Consent, date, required): Select the date on which you are giving your consent for the feature to be published.
* **Story or Feature Topic** (Story or Feature Topic, text, required): Describe the topic of the story or feature you are providing.
* **Brief Story Overview** (Brief Story Overview, text, required): Provide a summary of the story or feature.
* **Publication Medium** (Publication Medium, select_multiple, required): Choose where you would like your story to be published (e.g. Newsletter, Website, Social Media).
* **Permission to Use Photos** (Permission to Use Photos, select_one, required): Choose whether you consent to use photos of you (Yes, Any Photos, Yes, Only Provided Photos, No Photos).
* **Provide Your Photo** (Provide Your Photo, text, optional): If you have given permission for photos to be used, you can upload a photo here.
* **Permission to Use Direct Quotes** (Permission to Use Direct Quotes, select_one, required): Choose whether you consent to use direct quotes (Yes, Direct Quotes, Yes, Paraphrased Only, No Quotes).
* **Name Display Preference** (Name Display Preference, select_one, required): Choose how you would like your name to be displayed (Full Name, First Name Only, Anonymous, No Preference).
* **Want to Review Draft Before Publication** (Want to Review Draft Before Publication, select_one, required): Choose whether you would like to review the draft before it is published (Yes, Please, No, Trust Your Discretion).
* **Acceptable Publication Timeline** (Acceptable Publication Timeline, select_one, optional): Choose when you would like your story to be published (Next Issue, Within 2-3 Weeks, Within 1 Month, Flexible).
* **Right to Request Removal** (Right to Request Removal, select_one, required): Choose how long you would like your story to remain published (Indefinite, One Year, One Issue Only, Other).
* **Permission for Third-Party Social Sharing** (Permission for Third-Party Social Sharing, select_one, required): Choose whether you consent to social sharing (True, False, Ask First).
* **Additional Notes or Restrictions** (Additional Notes or Restrictions, text, optional): If you have any additional notes or restrictions, enter them here.

## Tips
* Make sure to review each field carefully to ensure that you provide accurate and complete information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [contact email or phone number].
