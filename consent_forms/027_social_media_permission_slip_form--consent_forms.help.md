# Social Media Permission Slip Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Social Media Permission Slip Form is a tool for users to grant permission to an organization to share their content on social media platforms.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select a User Permission Type**: Choose whether you want to grant permission to an individual ("User") or to a business or organization ("Business").
2. **Choose Content Type**: Select the type of content you are granting permission to share: Photo, Video, Text, Audio, or Other.
3. **Specify Content Sharing Date**: Enter the date when you are granting permission to share your content.
4. **Share Permission**: This is optional. If you want to grant permission to share your content immediately, select the date when you would like to grant permission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Permission**: Choose whether you are granting permission as an individual ("User") or as a business/organization ("Business").
* **Content Type**: Select the type of content you are granting permission to share (e.g. Photo, Video, Text, Audio, or Other).
* **Content Sharing Date**: Enter the date when you are granting permission to share your content. This field is required.
* **Share Permission**: This is optional. If you want to grant permission to share your content immediately, select the date when you would like to grant permission.
