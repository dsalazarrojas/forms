# Anesthesia Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Anesthesia Consent Form is a crucial document used in medical settings to obtain informed consent from patients before undergoing anesthesia. It helps ensure that patients are fully aware of the risks and implications of the treatment, allowing them to make informed decisions about their care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your patient's information:
	* Patient name: Provide the patient's full legal name.
	* Date of birth: Enter the patient's date of birth.
2. Describe the procedure:
	* Procedure description: Write a brief description of the planned procedure.
3. Confirm discussion of risks:
	* Risks explained to patient: Indicate whether the patient has been informed about the risks associated with the procedure.
4. Provide contact information:
	* Contact phone: Enter the phone number for contact with the patient during the procedure.
5. Confirm consent:
	* Signature printed name: Enter the patient's printed name to signify their consent.
	* Date signed: Enter the date of the consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient name**: Enter the patient's full legal name.
	+ *Required field*.
* **Date of birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
	+ *Required field*.
* **Procedure description**: Write a brief description of the planned procedure.
	+ *Required field*.
* **Risks explained to patient**: Confirm whether the patient has been informed about the risks associated with the procedure.
	+ *Required field*.
	+ Options: 'True' or 'False'.
* **Known allergies**: List any known allergies or adverse reactions.
	+ *Optional field*.
* **Signature printed name**: Enter the patient's printed name to signify their consent.
	+ *Required field*.
* **Date signed**: Enter the date of the consent.
	+ *Required field*.
* **Contact phone**: Enter the phone number for contact with the patient during the procedure.
	+ *Optional field*.

## Tips
- Make sure to review the form carefully before submission to ensure all required fields are completed.
- If a patient has allergies or adverse reactions, be sure to list them carefully.
- Ensure that the patient's printed name matches their legal name.
- Keep the procedure description brief and clear, to ensure the patient understands the treatment.
- Sign the form with a pen, and enter the date of consent.
