# digital_creator_highlight_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from digital content creators for collaborations, partnerships, or other business agreements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and ensure you understand its purpose.
2. Fill out the form according to your situation, being as accurate and detailed as possible.
3. If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification or guidance from the relevant authorities.
4. Submit the form once you have filled it out completely and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **digital_creator_highlight_consent_form** (`form_header`, label, required: false): This is the title of the form, which is used as a header to identify the purpose of the form.
* **creator_details** (`creator_details`, select_one, required: false): Select 'True' to indicate that you are a digital content creator, or 'False' to indicate otherwise.
* **creator_agreement** (`creator_agreement`, select_multiple, required: false): This field allows you to specify if you agree to certain conditions or not. Select 'True' for each condition you agree to, or 'False' for conditions you do not agree to. Leave 'pending' as an option if you are unsure or need clarification.
* **consent_details** (`consent_details`, text, required: false): This field is for providing additional details about your consent. Please be as specific and clear as possible.
* **creator_signature** (`creator_signature`, select_one, required: false): Select 'True' to indicate that you have signed the agreement, or 'False' to indicate that you have not signed it yet.
* **admin_notes** (`admin_notes`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is for administrators to take notes on your agreement. Select 'True' to indicate that you have read and understand the admin notes, or 'False' to indicate that you have not read them yet.
* **date** (`date`, date, required: false): Select a date for the agreement, if applicable.
* **time** (`time`, time, required: false): Select a time for the agreement, if applicable.

## Tips
* Please make sure to review and understand each field before filling it out.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask for guidance from the relevant authorities.
* Fill out the form accurately and truthfully to ensure a smooth and successful collaboration.
