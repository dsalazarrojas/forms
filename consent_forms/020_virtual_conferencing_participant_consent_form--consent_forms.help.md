# Virtual Conferencing Participant Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect participant consent for virtual conferencing events. It's essential to ensure that participants understand the purpose and terms of the event before proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Review the participant role dropdown list carefully and select the correct role for the participant.
3. Ensure that you have read and understood the privacy policy before signing the form.
4. Date and time of consent should be filled out according to your local time zone.
5. Participant consent should be filled out according to your understanding and agreement with the event terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`1`): This field is required and should be filled out with the participant's first name.
* **Last Name** (`2`): This field is required and should be filled out with the participant's last name.
* **Email Address** (`3`): This field is required and should be filled out with the participant's email address.
* **Company Name** (`4`): This field is not required but is useful for identification purposes.
* **Event Type** (`5`): This field should be filled out with the type of the event (Virtual, In-person, or Other).
* **Date of Event** (`6`): This field is required and should be filled out with the date of the event in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Time of Event** (`7`): This field is required and should be filled out with the time of the event in the format HH:MM.
* **Location of Event** (`8`): This field is not required but is useful for identification purposes.
* **Purpose of Conference** (`9`): This field is not required but is useful for understanding the event's purpose.
* **Participant Role** (`10`): This field is required and should be filled out with the participant's role (Host, Participant).
* **Privacy Policy URL** (`11`): This field is required and should be filled out with the URL of the event's privacy policy.
* **Contact Name** (`12`): This field is required and should be filled out with the contact person's name.
* **Contact Email** (`13`): This field is required and should be filled out with the contact person's email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`14`): This field is required and should be filled out with the contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Organization** (`15`): This field is not required but is useful for identification purposes.
* **Consent Date** (`16`): This field is required and should be filled out with the date of consent in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Consent Time** (`17`): This field is required and should be filled out with the time of consent in the format HH:MM.
* **Consent Note** (`18`): This field is not required but is useful for additional comments.
* **Participant Consent** (`19`): This field is required and should be filled out according to your understanding and agreement with the event terms.
* **Participant Consent Signed** (`20`): This field is required and should be filled out according to the participant's understanding and agreement with the event terms.
* **Participant Consent Signed Date** (`21`): This field is required and should be filled out with the date of signing in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Participant Consent Signed Time** (`22`): This field is required and should be filled out with the time of signing in the format HH:MM.
* **Participant Consent Signed By** (`23`): This field is not required but is useful for identification purposes.
* **Participant Consent Signed By Phone** (`24`): This field is not required but is useful for identification purposes.
* **Participant Consent Signed By Email** (`25`): This field is not required but is useful for identification purposes.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Ensure that you understand the event terms before signing the form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please contact the event organizers.
