# Travel Disclaimer And Insurance Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from clients or travelers regarding their travel plans, insurance details, and contact information for travel agencies and suppliers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form carefully, ensuring all required fields are completed accurately.
2. Start by filling out the "Travel Agency Reservation" field, which requires your travel agency reservation number or details.
3. Proceed to the "Client Safety" field, where you can provide any special safety considerations or concerns you may have.
4. In the "Emergency Contact Information" section, please provide the contact details of your emergency contact person.
5. The "Contact Information" field is used for your contact information, which will be used to get in touch with you during your travel.
6. Read and agree to the "Terms and Conditions" section carefully, which outlines your responsibilities and expectations as a traveler.
7. The "Cancellation Policy" section explains how and when cancellations can be made, which will help in avoiding any misunderstandings.
8. Travel insurance details can be provided in the "Travel Insurance" field, including your policy number and provider.
9. In "Travel Insurer", you can mention the name and contact information of your travel insurer.
10. Supplier information and contact details are provided in the "Supplier Information" and "Supplier Contact" fields, which are essential for any supplier-related matters.
11. Finally, sign and date the "Client Signature" field, which confirms your acknowledgement of the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Travel Agency Reservation** (`travel_agency_reservation`, `text`, **required: false**): This field is used to provide your travel agency reservation number or details. Please enter your reservation number if you have one, or describe your reservation in a few words.
* **Client Safety** (`client_safety`, `text`, **required: false**): In this field, you can provide any special safety considerations or concerns you may have while traveling.
* **Emergency Contact Information** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, **required: false**): Here, please provide the contact details of your emergency contact person, including their name, phone number, and email address.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, **required: false**): Enter your contact information to help travel agencies or suppliers get in touch with you during your travel.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, `text`, **required: false**): Read and agree to this section carefully, which outlines your responsibilities and expectations as a traveler.
* **Cancellation Policy** (`cancellation_policy`, `text`, **required: false**): This section explains how and when cancellations can be made to avoid any misunderstandings.
* **Travel Insurance** (`travel_insurance`, `text`, **required: false**): Provide your travel insurance details, including your policy number and provider, if applicable.
* **Travel Insurer** (`travel_insurer`, `text`, **required: false**): Mention the name and contact information of your travel insurer here.
* **Supplier Information** (`supplier_information`, `text`, **required: false**): Enter any supplier information related to your travel, including their name, address, and contact details.
* **Supplier Contact** (`supplier_contact`, `text`, **required: false**): Provide the contact details of your supplier, including their name, phone number, and email address.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, `note`, **required: false**): Sign and date this field to confirm your acknowledgement of the terms and conditions.

## Tips
- Always read the "Terms and Conditions" section carefully before signing.
- Ensure all fields are completed accurately to avoid any issues during the travel process.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask your travel agency for clarification.
- Travel insurance details are essential for your travel; ensure you provide accurate information.
- Supplier information and contact details are vital for communication; enter them accurately.
- Sign and date the "Client Signature" field only after reading and agreeing to the terms and conditions.
