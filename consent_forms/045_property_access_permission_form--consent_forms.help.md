# Property Access Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Access Permission Form is used to gather contact information and details of property access requests for properties. This helps us understand how to best communicate with users about their access requests and ensure that the property is available for inspection or access.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information in the following pages:

1. **Property Access Permission Form Page 1**: This page is for entering basic contact and property information.
2. **Contact Information**: This page is for providing more detailed contact information for the user.
3. **Access Request Details**: This page is for providing a description of the access request.
4. **Preferred Contact Method**: This page is for choosing a preferred method of contact.
5. **Contact Information 2**: This page is for entering phone number (if preferred contact method is phone) and email address (if preferred contact method is email).
6. **Access Request Date**: This page is for selecting the date of the access request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Access Permission Form Page 1**: This page is for entering basic contact and property information.
  * **property_access_permission_form_page_1_label** (`Property Access Permission Form Page 1`: Enter your property's basic contact and information here):
    * Enter your property's name and address in the text fields.
* **contact_information_label** (`Contact Information`: Provide more detailed contact information):
    * Enter your full name, title, department/agency, and contact phone number in the text fields.
* **access_request_details_label** (`Access Request Details`: Describe the access request):
    * Enter a brief description of your access request, including the type of access and any other relevant details.
* **preferred_contact_method_label** (`Preferred Contact Method`: How do you want to be contacted?):
    * Select one of the available options: Phone, Email, or Text.
* **contact_phone_label** (`Contact Phone Number`: Enter your phone number if preferred contact method is phone):
    * Enter your phone number.
* **contact_email_label** (`Contact Email`: Enter your email address if preferred contact method is email):
    * Enter your email address.
* **access_request_date_label** (`Access Request Date`: Select the date of the access request):
    * Select the date of the access request.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure proper communication and access to your property.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
