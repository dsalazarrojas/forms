# Automated Service Scheduling Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Automated Service Scheduling Consent Form is designed to capture customer information and preferences for automated scheduling of services. This form is used to ensure that customers understand how their data will be used and that they consent to the use of automation features.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by reading the introduction and providing your consent for automated service scheduling.
2. Enter your customer information, including first name, last name, email address, phone number, and street address.
3. Select your preferred state of residence and ZIP code.
4. Indicate the type of service you are interested in (e.g., Home Maintenance, HVAC Service, etc.).
5. Provide your service provider name and account number (if applicable).
6. Review and understand the explanation of how automated scheduling works.
7. Confirm your consent to automated scheduling.
8. Select all features you are consenting to (e.g., automatic appointment booking, automatic reminders, etc.).
9. Choose your preferred contact methods (e.g., email, phone call, etc.).
10. Indicate your preferred scheduling preferences (e.g., days of the week, time windows, etc.).
11. Review and confirm your understanding of the cancellation policy.
12. Confirm your understanding of the liability waiver.
13. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information (id: 2)**: Provide your details, such as first name, last name, email address, phone number, and street address.
* **First Name (id: 3)**: Enter your legal first name.
* **Last Name (id: 4)**: Enter your legal last name.
* **Email Address (id: 5)**: Enter your primary email address.
* **Phone Number (id: 6)**: Enter your best contact number.
* **Street Address (id: 7)**: Enter your residential address.
* **City (id: 8)**: Enter the city of your residence.
* **State (id: 9)**: Select your state of residence from the provided options.
* **ZIP Code (id: 10)**: Enter your 5-digit ZIP code.
* **Type of Service (id: 12)**: Select the type of service you are interested in (e.g., Home Maintenance, HVAC Service, etc.).
* **Service Provider Name (id: 13)**: If you have an account with a service provider, enter their name or ID number.
* **Account Number (id: 14)**: If you have an account, enter your account number.
* **Automatic Scheduling (id: 15)**: Please read and understand the explanation of how automated service scheduling works.
* **Understanding of Automated Scheduling (id: 16)**: Confirm that you understand how automated scheduling works.
* **Consent to Automated Scheduling (id: 17)**: Confirm your consent to automated scheduling.
* **Features You are Consenting To (id: 18)**: Select all features you are consenting to (e.g., automatic appointment booking, automatic reminders, etc.).
* **Scheduling Preferences (id: 19)**: Choose your preferred contact methods (e.g., email, phone call, etc.).
* **Preferred Days of Week (id: 20)**: Select the days of the week you prefer for scheduling.
* **Preferred Time Windows (id: 21)**: Choose the time windows you prefer for scheduling.
* **Minimum Advance Notice (id: 22)**: Indicate the minimum advance notice you require.
* **Blackout Dates (id: 23)**: If you have any dates you cannot schedule appointments during, enter them.
* **Communication Preferences (id: 24)**: Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g., email, phone call, etc.).
* **Preferred Contact Method (id: 25)**: Select your preferred contact method (e.g., email, phone call, etc.).
* **Reminder Frequency (id: 26)**: Select how often you would like to receive reminders.
* **Confirmation Required (id: 27)**: Confirm whether you need to confirm appointments or not.
* **Cancellation Policy (id: 28)**: Review and understand the cancellation policy.
* **Understanding of Cancellation Policy (id: 29)**: Confirm that you understand the cancellation policy.
* **How Can You Cancel (id: 30)**: Select how you would like to cancel appointments (e.g., online portal, phone call, etc.).
* **Cancellation Fee Acknowledgment (id: 31)**: Confirm that you understand that late cancellations may incur fees.
* **Data Privacy (id: 32)**: Review and understand how your data is used.
* **Data Usage Consent (id: 33)**: Confirm that you consent to the use of your data for scheduling.
* **Data Sharing (id: 34)**: Confirm that you consent to sharing your data with service providers.
* **Privacy Policy Acknowledgment (id: 35)**: Confirm that you have read the privacy policy.
* **Terms and Conditions (id: 36)**: Review the terms and conditions of service.
* **Terms Acceptance (id: 37)**: Confirm that you accept the terms and conditions.
* **Service Level Agreement (id: 38)**: Confirm that you understand the service level agreement.
* **Liability Waiver (id: 39)**: Confirm that you understand the liability waiver.
* **Modification Rights (id: 40)**: Confirm that you understand that the service provider may modify the schedule.
* **Emergency Contact (id: 41)**: Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Name (id: 42)**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (id: 43)**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Additional Authorizations (id: 44)**: Enter any special instructions or authorizations.
* **Customer Signature (id: 45)**: Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.
* **Date of Signature (id: 46)**: Enter the date of signature.
* **Witness Name (id: 47)**: If applicable, enter the name of a witness.

Note: This help guide is intended to be used as a reference for end-users to complete the form correctly and confidently.
