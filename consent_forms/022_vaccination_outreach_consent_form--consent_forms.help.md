# Vaccination Outreach Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from patients for vaccination outreach activities. It collects patient contact information, vaccination type, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification.
2. Provide the patient's date of birth.
3. Enter the patient's phone number.
4. Enter the patient's email address.
5. Choose the type of vaccination you are offering.
6. Enter your name as a provider.
7. Enter your license number.
8. Choose the date you sign this form.
8. Sign your name, indicating you have reviewed the information provided and that the patient is aware of the vaccination details.
9. Confirm your consent by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Patient Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Patient Phone**: Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Patient Email**: Enter the patient's email address.
* **Vaccination Type**: Choose the type of vaccination you are offering.
* **Provider Name**: Enter your name as a provider.
* **Provider License**: Enter your license number.
* **Signature Date**: Choose the date you sign this form in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Signature**: Sign your name, indicating you have reviewed the information provided and that the patient is aware of the vaccination details.
* **Consent**: Confirm your consent by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
