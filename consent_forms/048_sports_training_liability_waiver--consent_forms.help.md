# Sports Training Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a liability waiver for sports training, used to gather necessary information and gain consent for minors participating in sports activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the requested information for the minor:
	* First name
	* Last name
	* Email
	* Birth date
2. Provide emergency contact information:
	* Emergency contact name
	* Emergency contact phone number
	* Relationship with the minor
3. Confirm parent permission:
	* Select the correct option for parent permission
4. Enter facility information:
	* Facility name
	* Facility address
	* Facility phone number
	* Facility email
	* Facility website
	* Facility license
5. Report any medical history or conditions:
	* Medical history
	* Medical conditions
6. Authorize emergency contact:
	* Select the correct option for emergency contact authorization
7. Waiver:
	* Select the correct option for the waiver
8. Sign and date the form:
	* Signature of the parent or guardian
	* Date signed

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter the first name of the minor.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter the last name of the minor.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the parent or guardian.
* **Birth date** (`birth_date`, date, required: false): Enter the birth date of the minor.
* **Emergency contact name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency contact phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required: false): Describe the relationship between the minor and the emergency contact person.
* **Parent permission** (`parent_permission`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the correct option for parent permission.
* **Facility** (`facility`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the facility where the minor will be participating in sports training.
* **Facility address** (`facility_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the facility.
* **Facility phone** (`facility_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the facility.
* **Facility email** (`facility_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the facility.
* **Facility website** (`facility_website`, text, required: false): Enter the website of the facility.
* **Facility license** (`facility_license`, text, required: false): Enter the license number or certificate of the facility.
* **Medical history** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): Report any medical history or conditions of the minor.
* **Medical condition** (`medical_condition`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose any medical conditions relevant to the minor.
* **Emergency contact authorized** (`emergency_contact_authorized`, select_one, required: false): Choose the correct option for emergency contact authorization.
* **Waiver** (`waiver_signed`, select_one, required: false): Choose the correct option for the waiver.
* **Date signed** (`date_signed`, date, required: false): Enter the date the form is signed.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required: false): Sign the form with your signature.
* **Emergency contact signature** (`emergency_contact_signature`, note, required: false): Sign on behalf of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency contact address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
* **Facility business name** (`facility_business_name`, text, required: false): Enter the business name of the facility.
* **Facility phone** (`facility_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the facility phone number.
* **Emergency contact authorized** (`emergency_contact_authorized`, select_one, required: false): This field is redundant and not used.
* **Date signed** (`date_signed`, date, required: false): This field is not used.
