# Caregiver Story Publication Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed for caregivers to provide consent for the publication of their stories and experiences. This form ensures that the caregiver's story can be safely and accurately shared with the intended audience while respecting the caregiver's boundaries and rights. The form helps ensure that the publication process is conducted transparently and responsibly, with the caregiver's informed consent and understanding.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Carefully read each field explanation and ensure you understand the purpose and implications of each question.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and honest information.
3. Select the relevant options for each field according to your preferences and needs.
4. Read and review each section carefully before proceeding to the next one.
5. Make sure to sign and date the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Caregiver Name**: <mark>Enter your full name as it appears on your identification or government-issued ID.</mark>
* **Email Address**: <mark>Enter your email address where you can be reached for updates and inquiries.</mark>
* **Phone Number**: <mark>Include your phone number, including the area code, so that you can be contacted for further information.</mark>
* **Care Recipient Name**: <mark>This is the name of the person you are caring for. This will be kept confidential and only used with their consent.</mark>
* **Date of Consent**: <mark>Enter the date you are providing this consent.</mark>
* **Purpose of Story Publication**: <mark>Select the reason why you are sharing your story. This will help the publisher understand the context and intentions behind your story.</mark>
	+ **Educational purposes**: Sharing your story for educational purposes, such as teaching or training.
	+ **Awareness campaign**: Sharing your story to raise awareness about caregiving or related issues.
	+ **Advocacy and support**: Sharing your story to advocate or support others in similar caregiving situations.
	+ **Resource documentation**: Sharing your story for documentation and reference purposes.
	+ **News or media article**: Sharing your story for news or media publication.
	+ **Book or documentary**: Sharing your story for book or documentary publication.
	+ **Website or blog**: Sharing your story on a website or blog.
	+ **Training materials**: Sharing your story for training or educational materials.
	+ **Other purpose**: Select "Other purpose" if you have a specific reason not listed above.
* **Where Will Story Be Published?**: <mark>Select all applicable platforms where you would like your story to be published.</mark>
	+ **Organization website**: Publishing your story on the organization's website.
	+ **Social media**: Sharing your story on social media platforms.
	+ **Print publication**: Publishing your story in print media.
	+ **Online blog or news site**: Sharing your story on online blogs or news sites.
	+ **Book or eBook**: Publishing your story in a book or eBook.
	+ **Video or film**: Creating a video or film based on your story.
	+ **Educational materials**: Using your story for educational materials or resources.
	+ **Presentations**: Sharing your story in presentations or talks.
	+ **News media**: Sharing your story with news media outlets.
	+ **Other platform**: Select "Other platform" if you have a specific platform in mind.
* **What Content Will Be Shared?**: <mark>Select all applicable content areas you are willing to share.</mark>
	+ **Personal caregiving experience**: Sharing your personal experiences and stories.
	+ **Challenges faced**: Sharing the challenges you have faced as a caregiver.
	+ **Solutions or strategies used**: Sharing solutions or strategies you have found effective.
	+ **Emotional journey**: Sharing your emotional experiences and perspectives.
	+ **Lessons learned**: Sharing any lessons you have learned from your caregiving experience.
	+ **Photos or videos**: Sharing photos or videos related to your caregiving experience.
	+ **Quotes from interviews**: Sharing quotes from interviews or conversations related to your caregiving experience.
	+ **Contact information**: Sharing your contact information for further inquiry or contact.
	+ **Health information about care recipient**: Sharing health information about the person you are caring for.
	+ **Other content**: Select "Other content" if you have specific content that you would like to share.
* **Anonymity and Name Use**: <mark>Select how you would like to be identified in your story.</mark>
	+ **Use my real name**: Publishing your story using your real name.
	+ **Use pseudonym or initials only**: Using a pseudonym or your initials in your story.
	+ **Remain completely anonymous**: Not using your name or any identifying information.
	+ **Let publisher decide**: Let the publisher decide how to handle your anonymity.
* **Care Recipient Anonymity**: <mark>Select how you would like the care recipient to be identified in your story.</mark>
	+ **Include name and identifying information**: Including the care recipient's name and identifying information.
	+ **Use pseudonym**: Using a pseudonym for the care recipient.
	+ **Remain anonymous - no identifying details**: Not including any identifying information about the care recipient.
	+ **Not applicable - no care recipient mentioned**: Not mentioning the care recipient at all.
* **Permission to Use Photos or Videos**: <mark>Decide if you would like your photos or videos to be used in your story.</mark>
	+ **Yes - use photos or videos**: Allowing the use of your photos or videos.
	+ **Yes - use videos only**: Allowing the use of videos, but not photos.
	+ **Yes - use photos only**: Allowing the use of photos, but not videos.
	+ **No - text only**: Only sharing text content, without photos or videos.
* **Permission to Share Medical Details**: <mark>Decide if you are willing to share medical details related to your caregiving experience.</mark>
	+ **Yes - share specific details**: Sharing specific medical details related to your caregiving experience.
	+ **Yes - general information only**: Sharing general medical information, but not specific details.
	+ **No - no medical details**: Not sharing any medical information related to your caregiving experience.
* **Permission to Share Emotional Content**: <mark>Decide if you are willing to share emotional experiences and perspectives from your caregiving journey.</mark>
	+ **Yes - share personal emotional experiences**: Sharing your emotional experiences and perspectives.
	+ **Yes - but with some limits**: Sharing emotional experiences, but with some restrictions or boundaries.
	+ **No - keep emotional content private**: Not sharing any emotional content related to your caregiving experience.
* **Editorial Review and Approval**: <mark>Decide if you would like to review and approve your story before it is published.</mark>
	+ **Yes - I want to review and approve**: Reviewing and approving your story before it is published.
	+ **Yes - I want minor review only**: Reviewing your story briefly before it is published.
	+ **No - publisher can decide content**: Allowing the publisher to decide the content and publication process.
* **Accuracy and Fact-Checking**: <mark>Decide if you are willing to take responsibility for the accuracy of your story.</mark>
	+ **Responsible for accuracy review**: Taking responsibility for reviewing and ensuring the accuracy of your story.
	+ **No accuracy review process**: Not taking responsibility for reviewing or ensuring the accuracy of your story.
	+ **Unsure of process**: Unsure or unclear about the accuracy review process.
* **Duration of Consent**: <mark>Decide how long you would like your consent to last.</mark>
	+ **One-time use only**: Your consent is only valid for one-time use.
	+ **Limited time - specify duration**: Specifying a limited time frame for your story to be used.
	+ **Ongoing - indefinite use**: Allowing your story to be used indefinitely.
	+ **Until I request removal**: Allowing your story to be used until you request its removal.
* **Compensation or Payment**: <mark>Decide if you expect any compensation or payment for your story.</mark>
	+ **Yes - will be paid**: Expecting payment or compensation for your story.
	+ **No - no compensation**: Not expecting any payment or compensation for your story.
	+ **Other arrangement - to be discussed**: Discussing other compensation arrangements.
* **Right to Request Removal**: <mark>Decide if you would like to have the right to request the removal of your story.</mark>
	+ **Yes - can request removal**: Having the right to request the removal of your story.
	+ **Limited - only before publication**: Limited to requesting removal before the story is published.
	+ **No - no removal option**: Not having the right to request the removal of your story.
* **Permission for Third-Party Sharing**: <mark>Decide if you are willing for your story to be shared with other organizations.</mark>
	+ **Yes - can share with others**: Allowing your story to be shared with other organizations.
	+ **With permission only**: Sharing your story with other organizations only with your permission.
	+ **No - use by publisher only**: Only allowing your story to be shared by the publisher.
* **Permission for Follow-Up Contact**: <mark>Decide if you are willing for the publisher to contact you later.</mark>
	+ **Yes - may contact**: Allowing the publisher to contact you for further information or updates.
	+ **Yes - limited contact only**: Limited contact only for specific purposes.
	+ **No - do not contact**: Not allowing the publisher to contact you.
* **Understanding of Privacy Protections**: <mark>Confirm that you understand how your privacy will be protected.</mark>
	+ **Yes - understand privacy measures**: Confidently understanding and agreeing to the privacy measures.
	+ **Partially - have some understanding**: Partially understanding the privacy measures, but have questions or concerns.
	+ **No - unclear about privacy**: Unclear or unsure about the privacy measures.
* **All Questions Have Been Answered**: <mark>Confirm that you are satisfied with the explanations and understand the form.</mark>
	+ **Yes - all questions answered**: Confidently stating that all questions have been answered.
	+ **No - still have questions**: Still having questions or concerns about the form.
* **I Give Informed Consent for Story Publication**: <mark>Finally, confirm that you provide your consent for the publication of your story.</mark>
	+ **Yes, I give full consent**: Confidently providing full consent for story publication.
	+ **Yes, I consent with conditions**: Providing consent with specific conditions or limitations.
	+ **No, I do not consent**: Declining to provide consent for story publication.
* **Additional Notes or Conditions**: <mark>Include any additional notes or conditions you would like to add.</mark>
	+ <type>Enter any additional information or conditions you would like to include.</type>

Note: This help guide is intended to be a comprehensive explanation of the form and its fields. It is recommended to read each field explanation carefully and make informed decisions based on your understanding. If you have any questions or concerns, please ask the publisher or your point of contact for further clarification.
