# Campus Sustainability Study Filming Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Campus Sustainability Study Filming Consent Form is used to gather informed consent from participants to film interviews and activities for academic research purposes. This form ensures that participants are aware of the intended use of the footage and are willing to participate in the study.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the study description to understand the purpose and scope of the filming.
2. Provide your full name, email address, and phone number as required.
3. Select your consent to being filmed, including the type of filming (video or photography).
4. Choose the permitted uses of the footage, such as academic research, educational presentations, or social media sharing.
5. Indicate your preference for anonymity in the footage.
6. Agree to the assignment of intellectual property rights to the research team.
7. Confirm that you understand your right to withdraw from the study at any time.
8. Confirm that you have read and understand the privacy policy.
9. Optionally, provide your student ID or employee ID.
10. Sign and date the consent form to complete the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Please provide your complete name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your campus or personal email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Provide your best contact number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Please enter your date of birth to confirm you are 18 or older.
* **Filming Consent** (`consent_video`, `select_one`, required): Select your choice for filming.
* **Photography Consent** (`consent_photography`, `select_one`, required): Select your choice for photography.
* **Permitted Uses** (`permitted_uses`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable uses of the footage.
* **Anonymity Preference** (`anonymity_preference`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you want to be identified in the footage.
* **Rights and Release** (`intellectual_property`, `select_one`, required): Select to agree to assign all intellectual property rights to the research team.
* **Compensation** (`compensation`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether you accept compensation for the study.
* **Right to Withdraw** (`right_to_withdraw`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you understand your right to withdraw from the study at any time.
* **Privacy Confirmation** (`privacy_confirmation`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you have read and understand the privacy policy.
* **Researcher Information** (`researcher_contact`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether you want to receive contact information for the research team.
* **Consent Acknowledgment** (`consent_acknowledgment`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you have read and understand the consent form and agree to participate.
* **Date of Consent** (`signature_date`, `date`, required): Enter today's date to confirm your consent.

## Tips
Please read each field carefully and select the correct options to ensure that you are providing informed consent. If you have any questions or concerns, contact the research team for assistance.
