# Restaurant Training Guide Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to verify that employees of a restaurant have completed the training guide and confirm their understanding of its content.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have read and understood the restaurant training guide.
2. Select the correct option for "employee_signature" to confirm that you have read and understand the guide.
3. Select the correct option for "manager_signature" to confirm that your manager has reviewed and accepted your understanding.
4. Fill in your "employee_name" and "employee_title" fields with your name and job title.
5. Enter the name of the restaurant in the "restaurant_name" field.
6. Select the date of the training in the "training_date" field.
7. Choose "Yes" or "No" in the "completion_record" field to indicate whether you have completed the training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_signature** (Select Multiple): Select "1" if you have read and understood the guide, "2" if you have not.
* **manager_signature** (Select Multiple): Select "1" if your manager has reviewed and accepted your understanding, "2" if not.
* **employee_name** (Text): Enter your name as it appears on your official documentation.
* **employee_title** (Text): Enter your job title.
* **restaurant_name** (Text): Enter the name of the restaurant you work for.
* **training_date** (Date): Enter the date you completed the training.
* **completion_record** (Select Multiple): Select "Yes" if you have completed the training, "No" otherwise.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete this form accurately and honestly.
* Ensure your manager has reviewed and accepted your understanding before selecting "1" for "manager_signature".
* This form is for internal use only and is not intended for external use.
