# Animation Test Footage Release Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain release and consent permissions from individuals who have contributed to animation test footage. The purpose of this form is to ensure that all parties involved are aware of and agree to the usage and dissemination of their work. By filling out this form, contributors will be able to specify their preferences for the release and use of their test footage, including permission for public sharing, educational use, and archival purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name and email address in the "Full Name" and "Email Address" fields.
2. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field. This is required for verification purposes.
3. Select your role in the project from the dropdown menu in the "Your Role in the Project" field.
4. If you have a specific responsibility or description of your role, enter it in the "Specific Responsibility Description" field.
5. Enter the dates you contributed to the project in the "Dates of Work on Project" field.
6. Select the name of the project you contributed to in the "Project Name" field.
7. Choose the type of test footage you are releasing in the "Type of Test Footage" field.
8. Provide a brief description of what the footage shows in the "Brief Description of Footage" field.
9. Describe how you appear in the footage in the "Your Appearance in Footage" field.
10. Make selections for the release and consent permissions in the "Permission to Release Footage", "Permission to Use for Publicity", "Permission to Share on Social Media", "Permission for Educational Use", and "Permission to Archive Footage" fields.
11. Select how you would like to be credited in the "Credit Preference" field.
12. If you have a pseudonym or a preferred name to be used for credits, enter it in the "Name or Pseudonym for Credits" field.
13. Finally, agree to the compensation terms as discussed in the "I Understand Compensation Terms as Discussed" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter the email address where you can be contacted and correspondence will be sent.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Required): Enter your date of birth for verification purposes.
* **Your Role in the Project** (Select One, Required): Select the role you played in the project from the dropdown menu.
* **Specific Responsibility Description** (Text, Optional): Describe your specific responsibilities or contributions if applicable.
* **Dates of Work on Project** (Text, Optional): Enter the dates you contributed to the project.
* **Project Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the project you contributed to.
* **Type of Test Footage** (Select One, Required): Select the type of test footage you are releasing.
* **Brief Description of Footage** (Text, Optional): Provide a brief description of what the footage shows.
* **Your Appearance in Footage** (Text, Optional): Describe how you appear in the footage.
* **Permission to Release Footage** (Select One, Required): Choose whether to grant release of the test footage.
* **Permission to Use for Publicity** (Select One, Required): Choose whether to allow use of the test footage for public use.
* **Permission to Share on Social Media** (Select One, Required): Choose whether to allow sharing of the test footage on social media.
* **Permission for Educational Use** (Select One, Required): Choose whether to allow educational use of the test footage.
* **Permission to Archive Footage** (Select One, Required): Choose whether to allow archiving of the test footage.
* **Credit Preference** (Select One, Required): Select how you would like to be credited in the footage.
* **Name or Pseudonym for Credits** (Text, Optional): Enter a pseudonym or preferred name to be used for credits if not your real name.
* **I Understand Compensation Terms as Discussed** (Select One, Required): Confirm that you understand the compensation terms as discussed.
