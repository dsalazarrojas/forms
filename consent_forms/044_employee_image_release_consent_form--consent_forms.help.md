# Employee Image Release Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain an employee's consent for the use of their images, videos, or audio recordings in various company communications and external sharing channels. The form helps ensure that employees are aware of and agree to the use of their likeness in different contexts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Employee ID Number in the "Employee ID Number" field.
2. Select your choice of consent for each media type (photo, video, audio) by clicking on the corresponding button.
3. Decide if you want to share your image, video, or audio with company communications, marketing campaigns, social media, or other organizations.
4. Choose your company to share with by selecting "No" or "Yes" in the "Share with Company Communications" field.
5. Fill in your employee details such as your name, title, department, and manager's name.
6. Sign and date the form using the provided date and time fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID Number** (`Employee ID Number`, `text`, required): Enter your employee ID number as issued by the company.
* **Release Photo Consent** (`Release Photo Consent`, `select_multiple`, required): Select "Yes" if you consent to the use of your photo; select "No" if you do not.
* **Release Video Consent** (`Release Video Consent`, `select_multiple`, required): Select "Yes" if you consent to the use of your video; select "No" if you do not.
* **Release Audio Consent** (`Release Audio Consent`, `select_multiple`, required): Select "Yes" if you consent to the use of your audio; select "No" if you do not.
* **Share with Company Communications** (`Share with Company Communications`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select "Yes" if you consent to sharing your image, video, or audio with company communications.
* **Share with Marketing Campaigns** (`Share with Marketing Campaigns`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select "Yes" if you consent to sharing your image, video, or audio with marketing campaigns.
* **Share Publicly** (`Share Publicly`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select "Yes" if you consent to sharing your image, video, or audio publicly.
* **Share on Social Media** (`Share on Social Media`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select "Yes" if you consent to sharing your image, video, or audio on social media.
* **Share with Other Organizations** (`Share with Other Organizations`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select "Yes" if you consent to sharing your image, video, or audio with other organizations.
* **Employee Name** (`Employee Name`, `text`, optional): Fill in your name as an employee.
* **Employee Title** (`Employee Title`, `text`, optional): Fill in your title as an employee.
* **Department** (`Department`, `text`, optional): Fill in your department as an employee.
* **Manager Name** (`Manager Name`, `text`, optional): Fill in your manager's name.
* **Manager Title** (`Manager Title`, `text`, optional): Fill in your manager's title.
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required): Date of completion of this form.
* **Time** (`Time`, `time`, required): Time of completion of this form.
* **Company Name** (`Company Name`, `text`, optional): Fill in the name of your company.
* **Employee Signature** (`Employee Signature`, `note`, optional): Sign this form by writing your name.
* **Date Signed** (`Date Signed`, `date`, optional): Date of signing of this form.
* **Time Signed** (`Time Signed`, `time`, optional): Time of signing of this form.
* **Manager Signature** (`Manager Signature`, `note`, optional): Sign this form by writing your name as manager.
* **Employee ID Type** (`Employee ID Type`, `text`, optional): Fill in the type of your employee ID (e.g., employee number, social security number, etc.).
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes for your consent.

Please note that all fields marked as required must be filled in accurately to complete this form. If you're unsure about any field, please consult your HR department.
