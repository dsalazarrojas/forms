# Shared Workspace Policy Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be a consent form for shared workspaces, used to collect information from users about their policy consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. On the first page, provide your **Staff Details**.
   - This includes providing some basic information about yourself to help us identify you correctly.
2. On the second page, indicate your **Policy Consent**.
   - Select all the policies that you consent to by choosing the corresponding options.
3. On the third page, provide a **Signature**.
   - A signature is required to confirm that you have read and understood the policies.
4. On the fourth page, select the **Review Policy** that you wish to review.
   - This is where you confirm which policy you want to review before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Staff Details
* **Staff Details** (`shared_workspace_policy_consent_form_1`, text, optional): 
  This field collects basic information about you. Please fill in your name and any other relevant details that can help us identify you correctly.

### Policy Consent
* **Policy Consent** (`policy_consent_1`, select_multiple, optional): 
  Please select all the policies that you consent to. This will help us understand what you are comfortable with in the shared workspace.

### Signature
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, optional): 
  A signature is required to confirm that you have read and understood the policies. Please sign your name in this field.

### Review Policy
* **Review Policy** (`review_policy`, select_one, optional): 
  Please select the policy that you wish to review. This will help us direct you to the relevant policy document.

## Tips
Please take a few minutes to carefully review each field to ensure you are providing accurate information. If you are unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
