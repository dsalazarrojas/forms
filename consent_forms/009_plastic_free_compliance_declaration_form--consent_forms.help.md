# plastic_free_compliance_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The plastic-free compliance declaration form is used to collect information from vendors about their plastic-free compliance status. This form helps vendors declare their compliance with environmental regulations related to plastic use and waste management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the instructions carefully and understand the purpose of the form.
2. Fill in your vendor name, address, and contact information (email and phone number) in the respective fields.
3. Provide the date of the declaration, when you signed the form, and your signature.
4. Choose the correct declaration status from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, text, optional): Your official business name or the name under which you are operating.
* **Vendor Address** (`vendor_address`, text, optional): Your business address where you are operating from.
* **Declaration Date** (`declaration_date`, date, optional): The date you are declaring your compliance with plastic-free regulations.
* **Declaration Signed By** (`declaration_signed_by`, text, optional): Your official name and title, as printed on your business license or ID.
* **Vendor Email** (`vendor_email`, email, optional): Your email address where you can be reached.
* **Vendor Phone** (`vendor_phone`, text, optional): Your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Declaration Status** (`declaration_status`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following statuses:
	+ Status 1: Compliance
	+ Status 2: Non-Compliance
	+ Status 3: In Progress

## Tips
- Ensure that you have the authority to sign this declaration on behalf of your company.
- Be accurate and honest when filling out this form.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
- This form is meant for plastic-free compliance declarations only; it's not intended for other purposes.
