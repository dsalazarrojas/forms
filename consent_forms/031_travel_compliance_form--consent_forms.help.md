# Travel Compliance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture information about travel-related activities within the organization, ensuring compliance with company regulations and policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the destination country from the options provided.
2. Enter the start and end dates of your travel.
3. Provide a brief reason for the travel.
4. Select the status of your travel (Not Started, Completed, or Cancelled).
5. Attach any relevant documents related to the travel.
6. Check the agreement box to confirm compliance with travel regulations.
7. Provide any additional comments about the travel.
8. Attach a signature from the person who signed the agreement.
9. Check the status of the attachment (Active, Completed, or Cancelled).
10. Provide any comments about the attachment.
11. Check the status of the signature (Signed or Not Signed).
12. Enter the date the attachment was signed.
13. Check the status of the attachment (Active, Completed, or Cancelled).
14. Provide any comments about the attachment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Destination Country**: Select the country you are traveling to from the options provided. 
* **Travel Date**: Enter the start and end dates of your travel. 
* **Reason for Travel**: Provide a brief explanation for your travel. 
* **Status**: Select the current status of your travel (Not Started, Completed, or Cancelled). 
* **Documents**: Attach any relevant documents related to the travel. 
* **Agreement**: Check this box to confirm that you have read and understood the travel regulations. 
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments about your travel. 
* **Signature**: Attach a signature from the person who signed the agreement. 
* **Attachment Status**: Check the status of the attachment (Active, Completed, or Cancelled). 
* **Attachment Comments**: Provide any comments about the attachment. 
* **Signature Status**: Check the status of the signature (Signed or Not Signed). 
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date the attachment was signed. 
* **Attachment Status**: Check the status of the attachment (Active, Completed, or Cancelled). 
* **Attachment Comments**: Provide any comments about the attachment. 
* **Signed By**: Enter the name of the person who signed the attachment. 
* **Signed By Title**: Enter the title of the person who signed the attachment. 
* **Signed By Email**: Enter the email of the person who signed the attachment. 
* **Signed By Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person who signed the attachment.
