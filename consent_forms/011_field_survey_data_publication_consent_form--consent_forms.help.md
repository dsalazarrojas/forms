# Field Survey Data Publication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect participant consent for the publication of field survey data. It is a legal requirement for many field surveys, ensuring that participants understand and agree to the terms of data publication.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you are authorized to complete this form. Only personnel authorized by your organization should be using this form.
2.  Review each section of the form carefully to understand what information is required.
3.  Fill in the participant information (Section 1) with the participant's details as per the organization's records.
4.  Choose one of the options in "Field Survey Data Use" (Section 2) that best describes your intentions for publishing the data.
5.  Select "Yes" or "No" in "Consent Given" (Section 3) to indicate participant consent.
6.  Fill in the remaining sections with the relevant information, such as data collection details, publication details, participant contact information, and comments (Sections 4-9).
7.  Date and sign the form in "Field Survey Data Publication Consent Form Dated" (Section 13) and "Field Survey Data Publication Consent Form Signed By" (Section 12).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, optional):
    This section is for filling in the participant's information as per the organization's records.
*   **Field Survey Data Use** (`field_survey_data_use`, select_multiple, optional):
    This section is for choosing one of the options that best describes your intentions for publishing the data. Please select one of the following options:
    +   'Yes'
    +   'No'
*   **Consent Given** (`consent_given`, select_one, optional):
    This is to select "Yes" or "No" to indicate participant consent.
*   **Data Collection** (`data_collection`, text, optional):
    This section is for filling in data collection details.
*   **Data Publication** (`data_publication`, text, optional):
    This section is for filling in data publication details.
*   **Participant Contact** (`participant_contact`, text, optional):
    This section is for filling in participant contact information.
*   **Participant Contact Email** (`participant_contact_email`, email, optional):
    This section is for filling in participant contact email.
*   **Participant Survey Response** (`participant_survey_response`, text, optional):
    This section is for filling in participant survey response.
*   **Participant Comments** (`participant_comments`, text, optional):
    This section is for filling in participant comments.
*   **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, note, optional):
    This section is for participant signature.
*   **Field Survey Data Publication Consent Form Reviewed By** (`field_survey_data_publication_consent_form_reviewed_by`, date, optional):
    This section is for the date when the form was reviewed.
*   **Field Survey Data Publication Consent Form Signed By** (`field_survey_data_publication_consent_form_signed_by`, date, optional):
    This section is for the date when the form was signed.
*   **Field Survey Data Publication Consent Form Dated** (`field_survey_data_publication_consent_form_dated`, date, optional):
    This section is for the date when the form was created or issued.
*   **Field Survey Data Publication Consent Form Issued By** (`field_survey_data_publication_consent_form_issued_by`, text, optional):
    This section is for the person who issued the form.
*   **Field Survey Data Publication Consent Form Reviewd By** (`field_survey_data_publication_consent_form_reviewed_by`, text, optional):
    This section is for the person who reviewed the form.
*   **Field Survey Data Publication Consent Form Signed By** (`field_survey_data_publication_consent_form_signed_by`, text, optional):
    This section is for the person who signed the form.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that you have reviewed the form carefully before completing it.
*   Only fill in the necessary sections.
*   Ensure that the form is signed and dated correctly.
*   Review the form before submitting it to the data management team.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or the data management team.
