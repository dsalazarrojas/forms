# embroidery_waiver_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The embroidery waiver form is a document that requires the customer to provide their signature, order number, and other relevant details. It's essential to fill this form correctly to ensure a smooth transaction experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the `customer_name` field.
2. Provide the order number, if you have one.
3. Read and agree to the `waiver_terms`.
4. Sign the `signature` field with your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **customer_name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **order_number** (`order_number`, number, optional): If you have an order number, please enter it here.
* **waiver_terms** (`waiver_terms`, text, required): Please read and agree to the terms stated below.
* **signature_date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date you sign this form.
* **signature** (`signature`, text, required): Sign this document by providing your name and date.
* **form_footer** (`form_footer`, text, optional): This is a space for any additional comments or information.
