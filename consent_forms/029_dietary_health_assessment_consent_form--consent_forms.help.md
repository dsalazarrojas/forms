# Dietary Health Assessment Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from clients for a dietary health assessment. This information will be used to tailor the assessment to the individual's needs and provide personalized recommendations for their diet.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name and contact information.
2. If applicable, enter the client's medical conditions, allergies, and relationship with the emergency contact.
3. Select the relationship with the emergency contact.
4. Enter any medical notes and insurance information.
5. Specify the client's dietary restrictions and assessment frequency.
6. Date and time the assessment is to take place.
7. Sign and date the form as the client and provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Please enter the client's name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the client's email address for communication.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the client's phone number for contact.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, optional): Enter the client's home or work address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the client's date of birth.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_multiple, optional): Select how the client is related to the emergency contact (e.g. Client, Spouse, Parent).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact's address.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, required): Select the relationship between the client and emergency contact (e.g. Spouse, Child, Parent, Sibling, Other).
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): Enter any medical conditions the client may have.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, optional): Select any allergies the client may have (e.g. Food, Medications, Environmental).
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, text, optional): Enter any medical notes for the client.
* **Insurance Information** (`insurance_information`, text, optional): Enter the client's insurance information.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, optional): Specify any dietary restrictions the client may have.
* **Dietary Assessment** (`dietary_assessment`, text, optional): Describe the client's dietary needs and preferences.
* **Dietary Assessment Frequency** (`dietary_assessment_frequency`, select_one, required): Select how frequently the dietary assessment is needed (e.g. Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
* **Dietary Assessment Date** (`dietary_assessment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the dietary assessment.
* **Dietary Assessment Time** (`dietary_assessment_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the dietary assessment.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, text, required): Sign the form as the client.
* **Provider Signature** (`provider_signature`, text, required): Sign the form as the provider.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the form.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the form.
* **Signature Reason** (`signature_reason`, text, required): Explain why you are signing the form.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required): Confirm that the client has read and understood the consent statement.
