# photography_contract_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients, including their details, session details, session terms, and payment terms, to ensure both parties have a clear understanding of the photography contract.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. If you have any information about the client, such as their name, email, or phone number, please fill in the "Client Details" page. This is optional.
2. Move on to the "Session Details" page, where you can provide information about the session, such as date, time, location, and other specifics.
3. If you have any terms or conditions to share with the client, please include them on the "Session Terms" page.
4. If payment is required, please fill in the "Payment Terms" page with the payment amount, method, and any other payment-related details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details** (`Client Details`, text, optional):
	+ This page is for you to provide any information you have about the client, such as their name, email, or phone number. This helps us know who we're working with.
* **Session Details** (`Session Details`, text, optional):
	+ Here, you can provide the date, time, location, and other specific details about the session.
* **Session Terms** (`Session Terms`, text, optional):
	+ If you have any terms or conditions to share with the client, such as cancellation policies or equipment requirements, please include them here.
* **Payment Terms** (`Payment Terms`, text, optional):
	+ If payment is required, please fill in the payment amount, method, and any other payment-related details here.

## Tips
Remember, the more information you provide, the better we can serve you and your clients. If you're unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask!
