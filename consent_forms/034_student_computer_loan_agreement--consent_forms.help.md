# Student Computer Loan Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Computer Loan Agreement form is a document used to outline the terms and conditions of a computer loan between a student and the school or organization providing the loan.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the following information:
	* **Student ID** (id: 3): Enter your student ID number as provided by the school.
	* **Date Issued** (id: 6): Enter the date the computer was issued to you.
	* **Date Signed** (id: 9): Enter the date you signed this agreement.
	* **Student Signature** (id: 10): Sign your name to confirm you understand the terms of the loan.
	* **Loan Agreement Signed By** (id: 8): Sign your name of the person providing the loan.
2. Fill in the optional fields with the following information:
	* **Loan Amount** (id: 2): If applicable, enter the amount of the loan.
	* **Purpose of Loan** (id: 4): Briefly describe the purpose of the loan.
	* **Loan Details** (id: 5): Add any additional details about the loan.
	* **Loan Terms and Conditions** (id: 7): If applicable, add any specific terms and conditions related to the loan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID** (id: 3): Enter your student ID number as provided by the school.
* **Loan Amount** (id: 2): If applicable, enter the amount of the loan.
* **Purpose of Loan** (id: 4): Briefly describe the purpose of the loan.
* **Loan Details** (id: 5): Add any additional details about the loan.
* **Date Issued** (id: 6): Enter the date the computer was issued to you.
* **Loan Terms and Conditions** (id: 7): If applicable, add any specific terms and conditions related to the loan.
* **Loan Agreement Signed By** (id: 8): Sign your name of the person providing the loan.
* **Date Signed** (id: 9): Enter the date you signed this agreement.
* **Student Signature** (id: 10): Sign your name to confirm you understand the terms of the loan.
* **Loaner Signature** (id: 11): Sign the name of the person providing the loan.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately.
- Review the form carefully before signing to ensure you understand the terms and conditions of the loan.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to your loan provider.

Please note that while it's a text-based format, the fields with "date" type will be displayed as calendar fields in the actual application. 

The fields with "text" type are open for any additional comments or details about the loan.
