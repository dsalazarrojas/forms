# Teen Support Group Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teen Support Group Agreement is a form designed to capture the consent and information of both the teen and their guardian/parent in the context of a support group. It ensures that both parties are aware of their roles, responsibilities, and expectations within the group.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form accurately and honestly.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand its purpose and implication.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement and consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **teen_support_group_agreement** (`text`, required: false): This field is for the teen to provide a brief description of their understanding of the support group's purpose, goals, and expectations.
* **guardian_info** (`text`, required: false): This field is for the guardian/parent to provide information about their relationship with the teen and their involvement in the support group.
* **guardian_relationship** (`text`, required: false): This field is for the guardian/parent to describe their relationship with the teen and how they will support and communicate with the teen within the group.
* **guardian_signature** (`text`, required: false): This field is for the guardian/parent to sign and date their consent to the teen's involvement in the support group.
* **teen_signature** (`text`, required: false): This field is for the teen to sign and date their consent to be part of the support group.
* **parent_info** (`text`, required: false): This field is for the guardian/parent to provide additional information about themselves and their role in the support group.
* **parent_consent** (`text`, required: false): This field is for the guardian/parent to provide additional consent and understanding of the support group's purpose and expectations.

## Tips
* Ensure you understand each field's purpose and implication before completing the form.
* Review the form carefully to avoid errors or misinterpretations.
* Complete the form accurately and honestly to maintain a positive and safe environment within the group.
