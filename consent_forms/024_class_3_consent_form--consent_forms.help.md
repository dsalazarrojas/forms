# Class 3 Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a mandatory requirement for parents or guardians to provide consent for their children's participation in Class 3 activities. It includes sections for student information, parent or guardian information, and permissions for various activities, as well as medical information and general consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's information, including their name, ID or roll number, and class and section.
2. Provide your relationship to the student and your contact information, including email and phone number.
3. Answer each of the activity permission questions, choosing either "Yes, I consent", "No, I do not consent", or "Conditional" for each question.
4. Confirm your agreement to the school rules and regulations and give your final consent for your child to participate in Class 3 activities.
5. Certify that you are the parent or legal guardian by selecting "Yes, I certify".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Name** (`student_name`, Text, required): Enter your child's name.
- **Student ID or Roll Number** (`student_id`, Text, required): Enter your child's school ID or roll number.
- **Class and Section** (`class_section`, Text, required): Choose your child's class and section.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, optional): Enter your child's date of birth if required.
- **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, Text, required): Enter your full name as the parent or guardian.
- **Parent Email Address** (`parent_email`, Email, required): Enter your email address.
- **Parent Phone Number** (`parent_phone`, Text, required): Enter your phone number.
- **Relationship to Student** (`relationship_to_student`, Select One, required): Choose how you are related to the student (Mother, Father, Guardian, Other).
- **Permission for Field Trips and Outings** (`field_trips`, Select One, required): Choose whether you consent for field trips and outings.
- **Permission for Sports and Physical Activities** (`sports_activities`, Select One, required): Choose whether you consent for sports and physical activities.
- **Permission for Cultural Programs and Performances** (`cultural_programs`, Select One, optional): Choose whether you consent for cultural programs and performances.
- **Permission for Photography and Videography** (`photography_video`, Select One, required): Choose whether you consent for photography and videography.
- **Permission to Use Images in School Materials** (`media_usage`, Select One, optional): Choose whether you consent for images to be used in school materials.
- **Permission to Seek Emergency Medical Treatment** (`medical_emergency`, Select One, required): Choose whether you consent for emergency medical treatment.
- **Medical Conditions or Allergies** (`medical_conditions`, Text, optional): Enter any medical conditions or allergies your child may have.
- **Medications or Special Care Needed** (`medications`, Text, optional): Enter any medications or special care your child may need.
- **School Rules and Regulations Agreement** (`school_rules_agreement`, Select One, required): Choose whether you agree for your child to follow school rules and regulations.
- **Final Consent** (`final_consent`, Select One, required): Give final consent for your child to participate in Class 3 activities.
- **Parent or Guardian Signature Consent** (`parent_signature_consent`, Select One, required): Certify that you are the parent or legal guardian.
