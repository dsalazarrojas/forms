# pain_management_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about patients undergoing pain management treatment plans. It helps healthcare providers understand patient needs and preferences for pain management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the following required fields:
   - **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name.
   - **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY or DD/MM/YYYY).
   - **Pain Management Treatment Options**: Select all the treatment options that apply to the patient (e.g., "Yes" for each applicable option).
   - **Risks and Benefits**: Provide a brief summary of the treatment risks and benefits.
   - **Informed Consent**: Select either "I have read and understand the form" to acknowledge understanding of the form's content or "I have not read and do not understand the form" to indicate a need for further explanation.
2. You can leave the **Patient Address** and **Doctor Name** fields blank if not applicable.
3. Sign your **Signature** to confirm you have completed and understand the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (Patient Name, text, required): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format or DD/MM/YYYY format.
* **Patient Address** (Patient Address, text, optional): If the patient is not staying at the hospital, enter their home or current location address.
* **Doctor Name** (Doctor Name, text, optional): Enter the name of your doctor or healthcare provider.
* **Pain Management Treatment Options** (Pain Management Treatment Options, select multiple, required): Select all the treatment options that apply to the patient. For example, select "Yes" for each option that is relevant.
* **Risks and Benefits** (Risks and Benefits, text, required): Provide a brief summary of the treatment risks and benefits, including any side effects, contraindications, or other relevant information.
* **Informed Consent** (Informed Consent, select one, required): Select either "I have read and understand the form" to acknowledge understanding of the form's content or "I have not read and do not understand the form" to indicate a need for further explanation.
* **Signature** (Signature, note, optional): Sign your name to confirm you have completed and understand the form.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are completed accurately and honestly.
- Review the form carefully before signing to understand the treatment options and risks and benefits.
- If you have not read and do not understand the form, ask a healthcare provider for further explanation.
- Keep a copy of the completed form for your personal records.
