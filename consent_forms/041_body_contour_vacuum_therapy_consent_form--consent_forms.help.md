# Body Contour Vacuum Therapy Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Body Contour Vacuum Therapy Consent Form is a critical document that requires clients' consent and understanding before undergoing body contouring treatments. This form ensures that clients are aware of the procedures, risks, and potential side effects associated with the treatment. It is essential to complete this form accurately and truthfully to maintain a safe and successful treatment process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Please fill in your personal details:
    *   Enter your first name in the "Client First Name" field.
    *   Enter your last name in the "Client Last Name" field.
    *   Enter your contact email in the "Client Email" field.
    *   Enter your contact phone number in the "Client Phone" field.
2.  Provide necessary information:
    *   Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
    *   Enter your mailing address in the "Street Address" field.
    *   Enter your city in the "City" field.
    *   Enter your state in the "State" field.
    *   Enter your zip code in the "ZIP Code" field.
3.  Select relevant treatment areas:
    *   Choose the area that needs treatment in the "Treatment Area" field.
    *   Select how many sessions are planned in the "Number of Sessions" field.
4.  Disclose medical information:
    *   List any medical conditions you have in the "Medical Conditions" field.
    *   List any current medications in the "Current Medications" field.
    *   List any allergies in the "Allergies" field.
5.  Disclose pregnancy and blood information:
    *   Confirm your pregnancy status in the "Pregnancy Status" field.
    *   Confirm any blood disorders in the "Blood Disorders" field.
6.  Confirm treatment information:
    *   Confirm if the procedure has been explained to you in the "Procedure Explained" field.
    *   Confirm if you understand the risks associated with the treatment in the "Risks Understood" field.
    *   Confirm if you have understood potential side effects in the "Potential Side Effects Understood" field.
7.  Confirm other information:
    *   Confirm if alternative treatments have been discussed with you in the "Alternative Treatments Discussed" field.
    *   Confirm if all your questions have been answered in the "All Questions Answered" field.
8.  Finalize the form:
    *   Sign your name in the "Client Signature" field.
    *   Date the signature in the "Signature Date" field.
    *   Sign and date your provider's name in the "Provider Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client First Name** (text, required): This is the first name of the client.
*   **Client Last Name** (text, required): This is the last name of the client.
*   **Client Email** (email, required): This is the client's contact email.
*   **Client Phone** (text, required): This is the client's contact phone number.
*   **Date of Birth** (date, required): This is the client's date of birth.
*   **Street Address** (text, required): This is the client's mailing address.
*   **City** (text, required): This is the city where the client resides.
*   **State** (text, required): This is the state where the client resides.
*   **ZIP Code** (text, required): This is the client's zip code.
*   **Treatment Area** (select one, required): Choose the area of the body that requires treatment.
*   **Number of Sessions** (select one, required): Select the number of planned sessions for the treatment.
*   **Medical Conditions** (text, optional): List any medical conditions that may affect the treatment.
*   **Current Medications** (text, optional): List all current medications.
*   **Allergies** (text, optional): List any allergies that may affect the treatment.
*   **Pregnancy Status** (select one, required): Confirm if you are pregnant or nursing.
*   **Blood Disorders** (select one, required): Confirm if you have any blood disorders.
*   **Skin Conditions** (select one, required): Confirm if you have any skin conditions in the treatment area.
*   **Recent Surgeries** (select one, required): Confirm if you have had any surgeries in the last 6 months.
*   **Procedure Explained** (select one, required): Confirm if the procedure has been explained to you.
*   **Risks Understood** (select one, required): Confirm if you understand the risks associated with the treatment.
*   **Potential Side Effects Understood** (select multiple, required): Confirm if you have understood the potential side effects.
*   **Alternative Treatments Discussed** (select one, required): Confirm if alternative treatments have been discussed with you.
*   **All Questions Answered** (select one, required): Confirm if all your questions have been answered.
*   **Voluntary Consent** (select one, required): Confirm if you understand that this is a voluntary consent.
*   **Client Signature** (text, required): Sign your name in this field.
*   **Signature Date** (date, required): Date the signature.
*   **Provider Signature** (text, optional): Sign and date your provider's name.
