# School Media Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a school media release, which is a formal declaration that grants the school permission to use a student's image, voice, or work in educational media. It's used to ensure that parents/guardians understand and agree to the school's media use policy. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. **Read the form carefully**: Before filling out the form, take a moment to read the entire form and understand its purpose.
2. **Enter your name**: Fill in your name as a parent/guardian.
3. **Enter your child's name**: Fill in your child's name.
4. **Enter the school name**: Fill in the name of the school your child attends.
5. **Enter your child's grade**: If applicable, fill in your child's grade.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **media_release**: This field is for you to write a brief message, typically a signature, confirming that you grant permission for the school to use your child's media.
* **parent_name**: This field is where you enter your name as a parent or guardian.
* **school_name**: This field is for you to enter the name of the school your child attends.
* **student_name**: This field is for you to enter your child's name.
* **student_grade** (if applicable): This field is for you to enter your child's grade level, typically used when the media release is specific to a particular grade.

## Tips
* Make sure to read the form carefully and understand its purpose before filling it out.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact your school administrator.
* If you're unsure about your child's grade level, please leave this field blank or contact your school administrator for clarification.
