# Nostalgic Media Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nostalgic Media Submission Form is a form for submitting historical media items for preservation, sharing, or other purposes. This form helps gather relevant information about the creator and the media item being submitted.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the creator(s) of the media item in the "Creator" field.
2. Provide the contact information of the creator(s) in the "Contact" field.
3. Enter the name of the organization associated with the media item in the "Organization" field (if applicable).
4. Enter the location where the media item was created or is relevant to.
5. Upload the media item file by selecting it from your device or providing a URL to the file.
6. Describe the media item in up to 2 paragraphs in the "Submission" field.
7. Grant permission for Jotform to share or use the submitted material by selecting "Yes" in the "Permission" field.
8. Provide an email address for contact and sharing in the "Email" field.
9. If desired, enter a phone number and address for further contact and sharing.
10. Enter any additional contact information, such as social media handles and website links.
11. Enter any additional notes about the media item.
12. Enter the date and time of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Creator** (`creator`, text, required): Enter the name of the creator(s) of the media item.
* **Contact** (`contact`, text, required): Provide the contact information of the creator(s) for further communication.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required): Enter the name of the organization associated with the media item (if applicable).
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location where the media item was created or is relevant to.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of submission.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time of submission.
* **File** (`file`, text, required): Upload the media item file or provide a URL to the file.
* **File URL** (`file_url`, text, required): Enter the URL to the media item file (if uploaded via URL).
* **File Name** (`file_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the media item file.
* **File Type** (`file_type`, text, required): Enter the type of the media item file.
* **File Size** (`file_size`, text, required): Enter the size of the media item file.
* **File Description** (`file_description`, text, required): Describe the media item in up to 2 paragraphs.
* **Permission** (`permission`, select_one, required): Grant permission for Jotform to share or use the submitted material by selecting "Yes".
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter an email address for contact and sharing.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter a phone number for contact and sharing.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter an address for contact and sharing.
* **Social Media** (`social_media`, text, required): Enter any social media handle(s) associated with the creator(s).
* **Website** (`website`, text, required): Enter any website link(s) associated with the creator(s).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Enter any additional notes about the media item.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date of submission.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required): Enter the time of submission.
* **Form Footer** (`form_footer`, note, required): Enter any final comments or notes about the submission.
* **Creator Info** (`creator_info`, text, required): Enter any additional information about the creator(s).
