# League Player Profile Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The League Player Profile Consent Form is used to collect the essential information from league players, ensuring they are aware of their involvement and responsibilities within the league. This form is a mandatory part of the onboarding process for new players.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your player information in the "Player Information" section.
2. List your emergency contacts in the "Emergency Contacts" section.
3. Select your consent status in the "Consent" section.
4. Provide any relevant participation information in the "Participation Information" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Player Information
* **Player Information** (`Player Information`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your player name, email address, and any other relevant details that you would like to share with the league.

### Emergency Contacts
* **Emergency Contacts** (`Emergency Contacts`, `text`, required/optional): List the names and contact details of people you would like the league to contact in case of an emergency. This could be a family member, friend, or emergency contact that you would like to be reached.

### Consent
* **Consent** (`Consent`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select "Yes" to indicate that you have read, understand, and agree to the league's terms of use. You may select "No" if you do not wish to provide consent. If you have any concerns or questions, please reach out to our support team.

### Participation Information
* **Participation Information** (`Participation Information`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any relevant information about your participation in the league, such as your availability for events or any medical conditions that the league should be aware of.
