# Air Show Team Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Air Show Team Waiver Form is a comprehensive document designed to collect necessary information from teams and pilots participating in air shows. This form is essential for risk assessment, safety protocols, and liability purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read through each section to ensure you understand what each field is asking for.
- Fill out the form completely and accurately, as incomplete or incorrect information may cause delays or affect your participation.
- Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
- Sign the form with your full name, as this is a required field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Name** (`Team Name`, text, required): Enter your team or aerobatic team's name.
* **Pilot Name** (`Pilot Name`, text, required): Enter your full legal name as per your ID documents.
* **Aircraft Type** (`Aircraft Type`, text, required): Enter the make and model of the aircraft you are flying.
* **Aircraft Registration Number** (`Aircraft Registration Number`, text, required): Enter the tail number of your aircraft.
* **Pilot License Number** (`Pilot License Number`, text, required): Enter your FAA or CAA ID number, as relevant to your region.
* **Insurance Policy Number** (`Insurance Policy Number`, text, required): Enter your liability insurance policy number.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`Emergency Contact Name`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`Emergency Contact Phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Waiver of Liability** (Waiver of Liability, select_one, required): You must agree to hold harmless the organizers and their agents for any injury, damages, or losses that may occur during the air show.
* **Media Release** (Media Release, select_one, required): You grant permission to use photos and videos taken during the air show for promotion purposes.
* **Signature** (`Signature`, text, required): Type your full name to sign the form.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, required): Enter the date on which you are signing this form.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
- In case of any doubts or questions, contact the air show organizers or your emergency contact person.
- Keep a copy of the signed form for your records.
