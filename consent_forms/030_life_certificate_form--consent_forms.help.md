# Life Certificate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Life Certificate Form is used to create an official record of a person's death.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Form Information" section with your name and contact information.
2. Enter the "Person Information" section with the deceased person's details.
3. Select the correct "Person Contributed To" from the drop-down menu.
4. Enter the "Date of Birth" of the deceased person.
5. Enter the "Social Security Number" of the deceased person.
6. Enter the "Date of Death" of the deceased person.
7. Select the "Cause of Death" from the drop-down menu.
8. Enter the "Place of Death" where the person passed away.
9. Fill out the "Form Submitter" information with your name, role, and contact information.
10. Select the "Certificate Type" from the drop-down menu.
11. Enter the "Certificate Date".
12. Select the "Certificate Status" as active or inactive.
13. Fill out the "Form Submitter Declaration" with a brief statement.
14. Enter the "Form Submitter Date".
15. Select the "Form Submitter Declaration Status" as active or inactive.
16. Enter any additional comments about the form submission.
17. Enter the "Form Submitter Name".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Information**: This is the general information about this form submission. Please fill out your name and contact information.
	+ <name>: This is your name.
	+ <type>: This is a text field.
	+ <required/optional>: This field is not required.
* **Person Information**: This section collects information about the person who passed away.
	+ <name>: This is the name of the person who passed away.
	+ <type>: This is a text field.
	+ <required/optional>: This field is not required.
* **Person Contributed To**: Select the person who contributed to the life certificate.
	+ <options>: John, Jane, Jack.
	+ <required/optional>: This field is not required.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the date of birth of the deceased person.
	+ <type>: This is a date field.
	+ <required/optional>: This field is not required.
* **Social Security Number**: Enter the social security number of the deceased person.
	+ <type>: This is a number field.
	+ <required/optional>: This field is not required.
* **Date of Death**: Enter the date of death of the deceased person.
	+ <type>: This is a date field.
	+ <required/optional>: This field is not required.
* **Cause of Death**: Select the cause of death from the drop-down menu.
	+ <options>: This field is not provided in the YAML output.
* **Place of Death**: Enter the location where the person passed away.
	+ <type>: This is a text field.
	+ <required/optional>: This field is not required.
* **Form Submitter**: This section collects information about the person submitting the form.
	+ <name>: This is your name.
	+ <type>: This is a text field.
	+ <required/optional>: This field is not required.
* **Form Submitter Role**: This field collects information about your role in submitting the form.

* **Form Submitter Contact**: This field collects your contact information.
	+ <type>: This is a text field.
	+ <required/optional>: This field is not required.
* **Life Certificate Number**: This is the unique identifier for this life certificate.
	+ <type>: This is a number field.
