# Courier Service Driver Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Courier Service Driver Agreement form is a collection of personal and vehicle information required for new or existing drivers working with our courier services. It ensures that all necessary details are captured accurately and securely for our records and compliance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Driver Name** in the "Driver Name" field (required).
2. Enter your **Contact Number** (optional).
3. Provide your **Email Address** (optional).
4. Enter your **Driver License Number** (required).
5. Select your **Driver License State** (optional).
6. Enter your **Driver License Expiration Date** (optional).
7. Enter your **Vehicle License Number** (required).
8. Select your **Vehicle License State** (optional).
9. Enter your **Vehicle License Expiration Date** (optional).
10. Enter your **Vehicle Registration Number** (required).
11. Select your **Vehicle Registration State** (optional).
12. Enter your **Vehicle Registration Expiration Date** (optional).
13. Enter your **Address** (optional).
14. Enter your **City** (optional).
15. Select your **State** (optional).
16. Enter your **Zip Code** (optional).
17. Enter your **Vehicle Make** (optional).
18. Enter your **Vehicle Model** (optional).
19. Enter your **Vehicle Year** (optional).
20. Select your **Vehicle Color** (optional).
21. Enter your **Vehicle License Plate** (required).
22. Sign with your name in the **Driver Signature** field (required).
23. Sign with your name in the **Driver Signature Confirmation** field and date your signature in the **Agreement Date** field (both required).
24. Review your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Driver Name**: The name of the driver working with our courier services (required).
* **Contact Number**: The phone number where the driver can be reached (optional).
* **Email Address**: The email address of the driver for secure communication (optional).
* **Driver License Number**: The driver's license number as issued by the government (required).
* **Driver License State**: The state where the driver's license was issued (optional).
* **Driver License Expiration Date**: The date when the driver's license is set to expire (optional).
* **Vehicle License Number**: The vehicle's license plate number as registered with the state (required).
* **Vehicle License State**: The state where the vehicle's license was issued (optional).
* **Vehicle License Expiration Date**: The date when the vehicle's license is set to expire (optional).
* **Vehicle Registration Number**: The vehicle's registration number as registered with the state (required).
* **Vehicle Registration State**: The state where the vehicle's registration was issued (optional).
* **Vehicle Registration Expiration Date**: The date when the vehicle's registration is set to expire (optional).
* **Address**: The driver's address for secure delivery purposes (optional).
* **City**: The city where the driver resides (optional).
* **State**: The state where the driver resides (optional).
* **Zip Code**: The zip code of the driver's address (optional).
* **Vehicle Make**: The make of the vehicle used for courier services (optional).
* **Vehicle Model**: The model of the vehicle used for courier services (optional).
* **Vehicle Year**: The year the vehicle was manufactured (optional).
* **Vehicle Color**: The color of the vehicle used for courier services (optional).
* **Vehicle License Plate**: The license plate number of the vehicle used for courier services (required).
* **Driver Signature**: The driver's signature confirming agreement (required).
* **Driver Signature Confirmation**: Confirmation of the driver's signature (required for record-keeping).
* **Agreement Date**: The date the driver's agreement is made (required).
