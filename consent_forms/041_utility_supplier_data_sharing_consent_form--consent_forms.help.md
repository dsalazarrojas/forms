# Utility Supplier Data Sharing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect data sharing consent from customers for their utility supplier data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please review the form and ensure you understand the purpose and implications of the consent you are about to give.
2. Fill in your details in the corresponding fields, such as customer name, email, and phone number.
3. Select the relevant options for data sharing purpose, duration of consent, and customer permission.
4. Indicate your consent to share data with third-party suppliers.
5. Finally, review the form for accuracy and submit it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, required): This field is for you to enter your name, email, and phone number, which will be used to identify you as the customer.
* **Supplier Information** (`supplier_info`, text, optional): This field is for you to enter your supplier's name or ID, if applicable.
* **Data Sharing Purpose** (`data_sharing_purpose`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if you agree to share your data with third-party suppliers, 'No' otherwise.
* **Duration of Consent** (`duration_of_consent`, number, required): Enter the length of time you consent to share your data, in years or months.
* **Customer Permission** (`customer_permission`, select_multiple, required): Select the types of data you agree to share with third-party suppliers.
* **List of Suppliers** (`suppliers_list`, select_multiple, required): Indicate which third-party suppliers you are willing to share your data with.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, note, optional): This field is for you to attach a digital signature, if desired.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Customer E-mail** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Utility Supplier Data Sharing Consent Form ID** (`utility_supplier_data_sharing_consent_form_id`, number, required): A unique identifier for this form.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter the tool assigned to you for this form.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, text, optional): Enter the user assigned to you for this form.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, required): A unique identifier for this form.
* **Category** (`category`, text, optional): Enter the category under which this form is stored.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of this form.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, optional): Enter the path to the output file, if generated.
* **Assigned Tool 2** (`assigned_tool_2`, text, optional): Enter the second tool assigned to you for this form.
* **Output File 2** (`output_file_2`, text, optional): Enter the path to the second output file, if generated.
* **Customer Note** (`customer_note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes you may have for the customer.
* **Supplier Note** (`supplier_note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes for the supplier.
* **Utility Supplier Data Sharing Consent Form Id 2** (`utility_supplier_data_sharing_consent_form_id_2`, number, required): A unique identifier for this form.
* **Assigned Form ID** (`assigned_form_id`, text, optional): Enter the ID of the form you are assigned to.
* **Output File 3** (`output_file_3`, text, optional): Enter the path to the third output file, if generated.
* **Assigned Tool 3** (`assigned_tool_3`, text, optional): Enter the third tool assigned to you for this form.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you understand the implications of sharing your data with third-party suppliers.
* Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your assigned user or contact the customer support team.
