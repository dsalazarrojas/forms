# Community Impact Study Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather consent from participants in a community impact study, ensuring they understand the study's purpose, methods, and data usage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please read the **Description of study** field, which explains the brief summary of the study's purpose and methods.
2. In the **Consent to participate** field, select "I consent" if you agree to participate in the study.
3. In the **Data usage permissions** field, select the options that apply to how you would like your data to be used.
4. If you wish to withdraw from the study, please follow the **Withdrawal process** field.
5. In the **Signature name** and **Signature date** fields, enter your printed name and the date of consent, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name** (`Participant name`, `text`, required): Your full legal name.
* **Email address** (`Email address`, `email`, required): Enter the email address you would like us to use for study correspondence.
* **Description of study** (`Description of study`, `text`, required): A brief summary of the study's purpose and methods.
* **Consent to participate** (`Consent to participate`, `select_one`, required): Select "I consent" to agree to participate in the study.
* **Data usage permissions** (`Data usage permissions`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the options that apply to how you would like your data to be used.
	+ Research only: The data will be used only for research purposes.
	+ Anonymized public reporting: The data will be used for anonymized public reporting.
	+ Linked follow up contact: The data will be used for linked follow-up contact.
* **Duration of participation** (`Duration of participation`, `text`, optional): Enter the expected time commitment for your participation in the study.
* **Withdrawal process** (`Withdrawal process`, `text`, optional): Explain the process for withdrawing from the study.
* **Signature name** (`Signature name`, `text`, required): Enter your printed name to confirm consent.
* **Signature date** (`Signature date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of consent.
