# Glamorous Event Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for event planners and participants to complete a waiver form for events. This form gathers essential information for event planning and participant identification. 

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Use the provided fields to enter your information.
- Each field must be filled out as required (marked by an asterisk *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Planner** (`event_planner`, text, required *): Enter the name of the person or organization responsible for planning the event.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required *): Enter the name of the participant or person attending the event.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required *): Enter a valid email address for the participant.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required *): Enter the date of the event in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required *): Enter the time of the event in the format `HH:MM am/pm`.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the event (if applicable).
* **Participant Age** (`participant_age`, number, required *): Enter the age of the participant.
* **Participant Height** (`participant_height`, number, required *): Enter the height of the participant.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required *): Choose the type of event (Yes/No).
* **Event Category** (`event_category`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the event category (if applicable).
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the event (if applicable).
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, note, required *): Sign and date the form to acknowledge understanding the waiver.
* **Event Planner Signature** (`event_planner_signature`, note, required *): Sign and date the form to acknowledge responsibility for the event.
* **I have read and understand the waiver** (`waiver_accepted`, select_multiple, required *): Confirm that you have read and understand the waiver (Yes/No).

Note: All fields marked with an asterisk (*) are required to be filled out.
