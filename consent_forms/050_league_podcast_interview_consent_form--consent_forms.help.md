# League Podcast Interview Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The League Podcast Interview Consent Form is used to collect information and obtain consent from guests and hosts for recording and publishing their interviews.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Interview Date**: Enter the date of the interview.
	* **Interview Time**: Enter the time of the interview.
	* **Guest Name**: Enter the name of the guest.
	* **Guest Email**: Enter the guest's email address.
	* **Guest Phone**: Enter the guest's phone number.
	* **Guest Address**: Enter the guest's address.
	* **Host Name**: Enter the name of the host.
	* **Host Email**: Enter the host's email address.
	* **Host Phone**: Enter the host's phone number.
	* **Recording Date**: Enter the date of the recording.
	* **Recording Time**: Enter the time of the recording.
	* **Publish Date**: Enter the date of publication.
	* **Publish Time**: Enter the time of publication.
	* **Consent to Recording**: Select "Consent to Recording" to confirm that the guest is aware of and agrees to the recording.
	* **Consent to Publish**: Select "Consent to Publish" to confirm that the guest is aware of and agrees to the publication.
	* **Recording Location**: Enter the location of the recording.
	* **Recording Duration**: Enter the duration of the recording.
	* **Recording File**: Enter the link to the recording file.
	* **Consent Form Signed**: Select "Signed" to confirm that the guest has signed the consent form.
	* **Consent to Share**: Select "Share with Host" to confirm that the guest is willing to share the recording with the host.
	* **Share With**: Select "Host and Guests" to confirm who the recording will be shared with.

2. Review the form carefully and make sure all required fields are filled out correctly.
3. Click submit to complete the form.
