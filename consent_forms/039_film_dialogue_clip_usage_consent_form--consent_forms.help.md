# Film Dialogue Clip Usage Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Film Dialogue Clip Usage Consent Form is used to capture consent from participants for the usage of their film dialogue clips in a variety of contexts, including academic or research settings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked for.
2. Enter your details in the required fields.
3. Select the relevant options for each field as required.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Details
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required: false): This field is used to capture your details as a participant. Please enter your name and contact information.

### Clip Description
* **Clip Description** (`clip_description`, text, required: false): This field is used to provide a brief description of the clip you are consenting to use.

### Clip Details
* **Clip Details** (`clip_details`, text, required: false): This field is used to capture any additional information about the clip you are consenting to use.

### Media Type
* **Media Type** (`media_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the type of media you are consenting to use the clip in. Choose from the available options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
	+ Option 4

### Participant Consent
* **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`, select_one, required: false): Select your level of consent for the usage of your clip. Choose from the available options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
	+ Option 4

### Signed By
* **Signed By** (`signed_by`, text, required: false): This field is used to capture the signature of the participant or representative of the participant who has signed this form.

### Date Served
* **Date Served** (`date_served`, date, required: false): This field is used to capture the date the consent was given.

### Time Served
* **Time Served** (`time_served`, time, required: false): This field is used to capture the time the consent was given.

### Tips
* Please ensure you have read and understood the form carefully before completing it.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for clarification.
* Please sign and date the form to confirm your consent.
