# Estate Planning Records Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Estate Planning Records Release Form is used to collect information for estate planning purposes, allowing you to release records to authorized individuals or entities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the provided fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Make sure to sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Release Form** (`estate_planning_records_release_form_page`, text, required: false): This field is the main section of the form, where you can provide details about the records you wish to release.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Relationship** (`relationship`, text, required: false): Describe your relationship to the individual or entity you are releasing records for (e.g., spouse, parent, child, etc.).
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the individual or entity you are releasing records for.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): If available, provide the phone number of the individual or entity you are releasing records for.

## Tips
* Be accurate and thorough in filling out the form to ensure correct information is provided to the recipient.
* If you are unsure about any field or its meaning, please contact your contact person or relevant authorities for assistance.
