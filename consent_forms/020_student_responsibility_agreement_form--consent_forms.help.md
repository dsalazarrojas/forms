# Student Responsibility Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture student and parent information and signatures for agreements or commitments made by students.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please fill in the required fields and review the content carefully before submitting.
- Make sure to have a parent or guardian sign the form if the student is a minor.
