# Student Responsibility Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture student and parent information and signatures for agreements or commitments made by students.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please fill in the required fields and review the content carefully before submitting.
- Make sure to have a parent or guardian sign the form if the student is a minor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Information**: Please provide general information about the student and their parent/guardian relationship. This field is optional.
  - **Label**: General Information
  - **Type**: Text
  - **Required/Optional**: Optional
  - **Explanation**: Briefly describe the student's relationship with their parent/guardian.
- **Student Signature**: Please provide your signature as a student. This field is a required signature.
  - **Label**: Student Signature
  - **Type**: Text
  - **Required/Optional**: Required
  - **Explanation**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
- **Student Name**: Please enter your name as a student. This field is optional.
  - **Label**: Student Name
  - **Type**: Text
  - **Required/Optional**: Optional
  - **Explanation**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Parent Signature**: Please provide your signature as a parent/guardian. This field is a required signature if the student is a minor.
  - **Label**: Parent Signature
  - **Type**: Text
  - **Required/Optional**: Optional (required if student is a minor)
  - **Explanation**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
- **Parent Name**: Please enter your name as a parent/guardian. This field is optional.
  - **Label**: Parent Name
  - **Type**: Text
  - **Required/Optional**: Optional
  - **Explanation**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.

## Tips
- Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting.
- If the student is a minor, please ensure that a parent or guardian signs the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask for assistance.
