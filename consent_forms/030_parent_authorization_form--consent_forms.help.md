# Parent Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parent Authorization Form is used to obtain medical authorization from a parent or guardian for a minor to receive medical treatment. This form helps verify the parent's or guardian's permission for the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Parent Name**, which is required to identify the parent or guardian.
2. Provide the **Parent Phone** number, which is essential for communication purposes.
3. Enter the **Child Name**, which is the minor's name who will be receiving treatment.
4. Specify the **Date of Treatment**, which is the date when the treatment will be administered.
5. Choose the **Treatment Time**, which is the scheduled time of the treatment.
6. Sign the **Parent Signature** field, which is a required field to confirm the parent's authorization.
7. Enter the **Date Completed**, which is the date when the form was submitted.
8. Optionally, provide the **Doctor Name**, which is the name of the doctor administering the treatment.
9. Optionally, provide the **Medical History**, which is a brief description of the minor's medical history.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name** (Parent Name, text, required): Enter the name of the parent or guardian giving authorization.
* **Parent Phone** (Parent Phone, text, required): Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Child Name** (Child Name, text, required): Enter the name of the minor receiving treatment.
* **Date of Treatment** (Date of Treatment, date, required): Enter the date when the treatment will be administered.
* **Treatment Time** (Treatment Time, time, required): Choose the scheduled time of the treatment.
* **Parent Signature** (Parent Signature, note, required): Sign your name as the parent or guardian giving authorization.
* **Date Completed** (Date Completed, date, required): Enter the date when the form was submitted.
* **Doctor Name** (Doctor Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the doctor administering the treatment.
* **Medical History** (Medical History, text, optional): Briefly describe the minor's medical history, if necessary.

## Tips

* Be thorough and accurate when filling out this form to ensure that the parent's or guardian's authorization is valid.
* If you are unsure about any field, it is best to consult with the healthcare provider.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the healthcare provider for assistance.
