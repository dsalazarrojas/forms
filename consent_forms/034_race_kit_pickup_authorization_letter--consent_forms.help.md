# Race Kit Pickup Authorization Letter - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Race Kit Pickup Authorization Letter" is a form used to collect information from participants or their authorized representatives for kit pickup.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and last name in the "Participant First Name" and "Participant Last Name" fields.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Enter the name of the person authorized to pick up the kit in the "Authorized Contact" field.
4. Choose the pickup time from the available options or type in a custom time in the "Pickup Time" field.
5. Select the location where the kit will be picked up in the "Pickup Location" field.
6. Provide the date the kit will be picked up in the "Pickup Date" field.
7. Optionally, you may add any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field.
8. Finally, describe the contents of the kit in the "Kit Contents" field. This information will help us ensure that the correct kit is picked up and that all necessary materials are present.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant First Name** (`participant_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID or driver's license.
* **Participant Last Name** (`participant_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID or driver's license.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Authorized Contact** (`authorized_contact`, text, required): Enter the name of the person authorized to pick up the kit.
* **Pickup Time** (`pickup_time`, time, required): Select the time you would like to pick up the kit.
* **Pickup Location** (`pickup_location`, text, required): Enter the location where the kit will be picked up (e.g., office, home, etc.).
* **Pickup Date** (`pickup_date`, date, required): Provide the date you would like to pick up the kit.
* **Pickup Signature** (`pickup_signature`, note, optional): If you're picking up the kit, please sign your name here.
* **Kit Contents** (`kit_contents`, text, required): Describe the contents of the kit to ensure accuracy during pickup.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional information or comments about your kit pickup.
