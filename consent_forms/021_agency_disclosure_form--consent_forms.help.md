# Agency Disclosure Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Agency Disclosure Form is a document used to disclose the relationships between real estate professionals, brokerages, and clients in real estate transactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing the form's purpose and understanding the importance of accurately completing it.
2. Complete the required fields, starting with the **Licensee Name**, **Real Estate Brokerage Name**, and **Client Name** fields. These fields are essential for identifying the parties involved in the transaction.
3. Select the **Brokerage Role**, which indicates the capacity in which the broker is acting (e.g., Seller's Agent, Buyer's Agent, Dual Agent, etc.). This field ensures that all parties understand the broker's role and responsibilities.
4. Check the **Acknowledged Statutory Duties** field, which lists the duties that the licensee must perform with honesty, fair dealing, and reasonable care. Select all the duties that apply to your situation.
5. Finally, sign the **Signature Acknowledgment** field, which confirms that you have received a copy of this disclosure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
The following is a detailed explanation of each field:

* **Licensee Name** (Required): Please enter your name as the real estate professional.
	+ This field is required to identify the person responsible for the transaction.
* **Real Estate Brokerage Name** (Required): Enter the name of the brokerage firm involved in the transaction.
	+ This field is required to identify the brokerage firm's involvement in the transaction.
* **Client Name** (Required): Enter the name of the client involved in the transaction.
	+ This field is required to identify the person or entity involved in the transaction.
* **Brokerage Role** (Required): Select the capacity in which the broker is acting (e.g., Seller's Agent, Buyer's Agent, Dual Agent, etc.).
	+ This field ensures that all parties understand the broker's role and responsibilities.
* **Acknowledged Statutory Duties** (Required): Select all the duties that apply to your situation (Loyalty and Obedience, Confidentiality, Disclosure of Material Facts, Accounting for Funds, Reasonable Skill and Care).
	+ This field is required to ensure that you understand and acknowledge your statutory duties.
* **Disclosure Date** (Required): Enter the date on which the disclosure was provided.
	+ This field is required to record the date of the disclosure.
* **Property Address** (Optional): Enter the address of the property involved in the transaction (if applicable).
	+ This field is not required but may be necessary for certain transactions.
* **Signature Acknowledgment** (Optional): Sign to confirm that you have received a copy of this disclosure.
	+ This field is not required but recommended to confirm receipt of the disclosure.

## Tips
* Please review the form carefully and ensure that you complete all required fields accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or colleague for guidance.
* Complete the form as soon as possible after the initial consultation, ideally on the first meeting with the client.
* Make a copy of the disclosure available to the client and store it in their file for future reference.
* Remember to keep the disclosure up to date and modify it as necessary to reflect any changes in the client's circumstances or the transaction status.
