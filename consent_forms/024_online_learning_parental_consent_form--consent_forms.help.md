Online Learning Parental Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from the parent or guardian of a student before enrolling or continuing their online learning activities. This is an important step to ensure that the student is properly registered and accounted for in our system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in all the required fields. The form will not be saved until all the required fields are filled in.
2. Enter the student's name in the "student_name" field.
3. Enter the student's email address in the "student_email" field.
4. Enter the parent's email address in the "parent_email" field.
5. Enter the parent's phone number in the "parent_phone" field.
6. Enter the student's identifier (e.g., a unique student ID or student number) in the "student_identifier" field.
7. Sign and date the "parent_signature" field with the parent's signature.
8. Leave the "student_name_on_record" field blank if the student's name is already on record. Otherwise, enter the student's name.
9. Add any additional comments or notes for the parent in the "parent_note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Parent Consent (parent_consent)**: This is the text input where the parent or guardian signs and dates their consent.
  (Required)
  
- **Student Name (student_name)**: The full name of the student who will be participating in the online learning activity.
  (Required)

- **Parent Email (parent_email)**: The email address of the parent or guardian.
  (Required)

- **Parent Phone (parent_phone)**: The phone number of the parent or guardian.
  (Required)

- **Student Email (student_email)**: The email address of the student.
  (Required)

- **Student Name on Record (student_name_on_record)**: If the student's name is already on record, leave this field blank. Otherwise, enter the student's name.
  (Optional)

- **Parent Name (parent_name)**: The full name of the parent or guardian.
  (Required)

- **Student Identifier (student_identifier)**: A unique identifier for the student (e.g., a student ID or student number).
  (Required)

- **Parent Signature (parent_signature)**: This is where the parent or guardian signs and dates their consent.
  (Required)

- **Parent Note (parent_note)**: This is an optional field for the parent or guardian to add any additional comments or notes.
  (Optional)

This guide will help ensure that the form is completed accurately and efficiently, reducing errors and ensuring that the student's online learning activities are properly registered in our system.
