# fitness_assessment_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from clients before undergoing a fitness assessment. It ensures the client has been informed about the risks and consequences of the assessment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand all the questions and fields.
2. Answer all required fields accurately and truthfully.
3. Sign and date the form, indicating your agreement to the terms and conditions outlined.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Informed Consent** (`client_informed_consent`, text, required): Please provide a written statement indicating that you have been informed about the risks and consequences of the fitness assessment process.
* **Risks and Consequences** (`risks_and_consequences`, text, optional): This is an optional field for you to describe any risks or consequences you are aware of or concerned about related to the fitness assessment.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Please enter the date you sign this form.
* **Signature Time** (`signature_time`, time, required): Please enter the time you sign this form.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, note, required): This is a space for you to sign your name, indicating your agreement to the terms and conditions.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Please provide your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Fitness Professional Name** (`fitness_professional_name`, text, required): Please provide the name of the fitness professional performing the assessment.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required): Please provide the name of the organization providing the fitness assessment services.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): This is an optional field for you to provide contact information for the organization or fitness professional.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, required): Please provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, select_one, required): Please select who your emergency contact is (spouse, parent, other).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Please provide the phone number of your emergency contact (if selected).
