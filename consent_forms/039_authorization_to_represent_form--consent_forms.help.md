# Authorization To Represent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals to grant authorization to a representative, allowing them to make decisions and act on their behalf in various matters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields, marked with a asterisk (*), with accurate and complete information.
2. For optional fields, you may skip them if they do not apply to your situation.
3. Review each field carefully and ensure that you understand its implications on the authorization being granted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Authorization Date** (`authorization_date`, date, required): Enter the date of this authorization.
  - This field is the date when the authorization starts.
- **Principal Name** (`principal_name`, text, required): Enter the full legal name of the person being represented.
  - This is the name of the individual who is granting the authorization.
- **Principal Address** (`principal_address`, text, required): Enter the residential address of the person being represented.
  - This is the address of the individual who is granting the authorization.
- **Principal Email** (`principal_email`, email, required): Enter the email contact of the person being represented.
  - This is the email address where the representative can contact the individual.
- **Principal Phone** (`principal_phone`, text, required): Enter the contact phone number of the person being represented.
  - This is the phone number where the representative can contact the individual.
- **Representative Name** (`representative_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the person representing the principal.
  - This is the name of the representative who will be acting on behalf of the principal.
- **Relationship to Principal** (`representative_relationship`, select_one, required): Select the relationship between the representative and the principal.
  - This defines how the representative is related to the principal (e.g., Spouse, Family Member, Attorney, etc.).
- **Representative Address** (`representative_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the representative.
  - This is the address of the representative who will be acting on behalf of the principal.
- **Representative Email** (`representative_email`, email, required): Enter the email contact of the representative.
  - This is the email address where the principal or other parties can contact the representative.
- **Representative Phone** (`representative_phone`, text, required): Enter the contact phone number of the representative.
  - This is the phone number where the principal or other parties can contact the representative.
- **Scope of Representation** (`scope_of_authority`, select_one, required): Select the scope of authority granted to the representative.
  - This defines what matters the representative is authorized to handle (e.g., Specific Matter Only, Limited Matters, General Representation, etc.).
- **Specific Matters of Representation** (`specific_matters`, text, required): Describe the specific matters that the representative is authorized to handle.
  - This is a more detailed description of what the representative is allowed to handle on behalf of the principal.
- **Type of Representation** (`representation_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of representation.
  - This defines what kind of representation the representative will be performing (e.g., Legal Representation, Financial/Business, Medical Decisions, etc.).
- **Start Date of Representation** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the authorization starts.
  - This is the date when the representative's authority begins.
- **End Date of Representation** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the authorization expires.
  - This is the date when the representative's authority ends.
- **Duration of Authorization** (`duration_type`, select_one, required): Select the duration of the authorization.
  - This defines how long the authorization is valid (e.g., One-Time/Single Matter, Temporary - Specific Period, Ongoing Until Revoked, etc.).
- **Financial Decision Authority** (`financial_authority`, select_one, required): Select the level of financial decision authority granted.
  - This defines what kind of financial decisions the representative can make (e.g., No Financial Authority, Limited Financial Authority, Specific Amount Limit, etc.).
- **Financial Decision Limit** (`financial_limit`, number, optional): Enter the maximum amount of financial decisions the representative can make.
  - This is the maximum amount of money the representative can handle.
- **Authority to Sign Documents** (`signing_authority`, select_one, required): Select the level of authority to sign documents.
  - This defines whether the representative can sign on behalf of the principal.
- **Medical Decision Authority** (`medical_authority`, select_one, optional): Select the level of medical decision authority granted.
  - This defines what kind of medical decisions the representative can make (e.g., No Medical Authority, Limited Medical Decisions, Emergency Medical Decisions, etc.).
- **Authority to Communicate on Behalf** (`communication_authority`, select_one, optional): Select the level of authority to communicate on behalf.
  - This defines whether the representative can communicate on behalf of the principal.
- **Specific Limitations** (`limitation_details`, text, optional): Enter any restrictions on the authorization.
  - This is where you can specify any limitations or conditions that apply.
- **Representative Duties and Obligations** (`duties_and_obligations`, text, optional): Enter any duties or obligations the representative must perform.
  - This defines any actions or tasks the representative must complete.
- **Termination Conditions** (`termination_conditions`, text, optional): Enter conditions under which the authorization can be terminated.
  - This defines the circumstances under which the authorization ends.
- **Principal Authorization** (`principal_authorization`, select_one, required): Select whether you authorize this representation.
  - This is where you confirm or deny the authorization, or confirm with modifications if needed.

## Tips
- Be sure to double-check all fields before submitting the form to avoid mistakes.
- Ensure that you understand the implications of each field on the authorization being granted.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact your representative or relevant authorities.
