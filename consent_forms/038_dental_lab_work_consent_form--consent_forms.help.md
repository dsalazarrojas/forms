# Dental Lab Work Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from patients for dental lab work. By filling out this form, you're confirming that you understand the purpose and scope of the dental lab work, and that you're willing to participate.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and make sure you understand each field.
2. Fill out the "Patient Information" field with your name and any other relevant details.
3. In the "Lab Information" field, provide any necessary information about the dental lab work, such as the type of work or the expected duration.
4. In the "Consent" field, select one of the two options: "I am willing to participate in this study" or "I am not willing to participate in this study".
5. If you wish to be contacted by the lab for any reason, fill out the "Contact Information" field with your contact details.
6. If you have an address, fill out the "Address" field, so the lab can contact you if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information**: Please fill out your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Lab Information**: Please provide any necessary information about the dental lab work you're undergoing.
* **Consent**: Please choose one of the two options: "I am willing to participate in this study" or "I am not willing to participate in this study". This decision will affect the lab's actions regarding your work.
* **Contact Information**: If you want to be contacted by the lab for any reason, please fill out this field with your contact details.
* **Address**: If you have an address, please fill out this field so the lab can contact you if necessary.
