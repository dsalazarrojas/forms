# Hair Foiling Highlight Retouch Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from clients about their hair foiling and retouching procedures. This form is used to ensure that clients understand the risks and benefits associated with these procedures and provide a record of their approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure that you understand the questions and options presented.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Client Approvals" question, indicating whether you approve of the procedure.
3. Provide a brief description of your hair history in the "Hair History" field.
4. List any known risks or complications in the "Risks" field.
5. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Client Approvals 2" question, indicating whether you re-approve of the procedure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Approvals** (`client_approvals`, Select Multiple, required/optional): Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you approve of the procedure. This will help us ensure that you are aware of and understand the process.
* **Hair History** (`hair_history`, Text, optional): Please provide a brief description of your hair history, including any previous procedures you have undergone or medications you are taking. This will help us better understand your individual needs.
* **Risks** (`risks`, Text, optional): If you are aware of any potential risks or complications associated with the procedure, please list them here.
* **Client Approvals 2** (`client_approvals_2`, Select Multiple, required/optional): Please select "Yes" or "No" to re-approve of the procedure. This is an opportunity for you to re-evaluate your decision and ensure that you are still comfortable with the process.

## Tips
* Please answer each question carefully and honestly, as this will help us provide the best possible service.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of the procedure, please don't hesitate to ask us for clarification.
* This form is a record of your consent, so please review it carefully before signing.
