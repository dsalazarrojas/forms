# Healthcare Data Audit Research Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Healthcare Data Audit Research Consent Form is a document that requires patients to provide their consent for the use of their healthcare data in research studies. This form helps ensure that patients understand the purpose and risks associated with participating in research studies and that their data is handled securely and confidentially.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. **General Information**: Fill in the patient's basic details, such as their name and contact information.
2. **Patient Data**: If applicable, fill in the patient's date of birth and any relevant medical information.
3. **Consent Status**: Indicate whether the patient has given their consent for the use of their data in research studies.
4. **Contact Person and Department**: Provide the name and contact information of the person responsible for the research study.
5. **Patient Signature and Healthcare Provider Signature**: Both the patient and their healthcare provider must sign the form to confirm their consent.
6. **Date and Time**: Fill in the date and time of the form completion.
7. **Medical Record Number and Study ID**: Fill in the patient's medical record number and the study ID.
8. **Institution and Location**: Fill in the name of the institution and location where the research study is being conducted.
9. **Research Study Title, Description, and PI**: Fill in the title, description, and principal investigator (PI) of the research study.
10. **Date of Last Contact and Time of Last Contact**: If applicable, fill in the date and time of the last contact with the patient.
11. **Contact Person Last Contact**: If applicable, fill in the name of the contact person responsible for the last contact.
12. **Patient Signature and Healthcare Provider Signature**: Both the patient and their healthcare provider must sign the form to confirm their consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (1)**: This field is for the patient's basic details, such as their name and contact information.
* **Patient Data (2)**: This field is for the patient's date of birth and any relevant medical information.
* **Consent Status (3)**: This field requires the patient to indicate whether they have given their consent for the use of their data in research studies.
* **Email (4)**: This field is for the patient's email address.
* **Phone (5)**: This field is for the patient's phone number.
* **Contact Person (6)**: This field is for the name of the person responsible for the research study.
* **Contact Department (7)**: This field is for the department responsible for the research study.
* **Contact Email (8)**: This field is for the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone (9)**: This field is for the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Address (10)**: This field is for the address of the contact person.
* **Patient Signature (11)**: This field requires the patient to sign the form to confirm their consent.
* **Healthcare Provider Signature (12)**: This field requires the patient's healthcare provider to sign the form to confirm their consent.
* **Date (13)**: This field is for the date of the form completion.
* **Time (14)**: This field is for the time of the form completion.
* **Medical Record Number (15)**: This field is for the patient's medical record number.
* **Study ID (16)**: This field is for the study ID.
* **Study Version (17)**: This field is for the version of the study.
* **Institution (18)**: This field is for the name of the institution where the research study is being conducted.
* **Location (19)**: This field is for the location where the research study is being conducted.
* **Research Study Title (20)**: This field is for the title of the research study.
* **Research Study Description (21)**: This field is for a brief description of the research study.
* **Research Study PI (22)**: This field is for the principal investigator (PI) of the research study.
* **Date of Last Contact (23)**: If applicable, this field is for the date of the last contact with the patient.
* **Time of Last Contact (24)**: If applicable, this field is for the time of the last contact with the patient.
* **Contact Person Last Contact (25)**: If applicable, this field is for the name of the contact person responsible for the last contact.

## Tips

* Please fill in all fields accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the contact person listed on the form.
* The patient must sign the form to confirm their consent.
