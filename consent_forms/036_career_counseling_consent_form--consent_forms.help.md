# Career Counseling Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Career Counseling Consent Form is used to obtain the client's consent for career counseling services. It ensures that the client understands and agrees to the terms of their counseling session, including the use of recordings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Client Name**: Enter the full name of the client.
2. **Counselor Name**: Enter the name of the assigned counselor (if applicable).
3. **Services Description**: Provide an overview of the counseling services being offered.
4. **Confidentiality Explanation**: Describe the limits of confidentiality for the client's information.
5. **Consent for Service**: Select "I consent" to indicate that the client agrees to counseling.
6. **Consent for Recording**: Choose "True" or "False" to indicate whether the client consents to recording sessions.
7. **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact (if applicable).
8. **Signature Printed Name**: Type the client's printed name as their signature.
9. **Date Signed**: Enter the date of the consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (Required, Text): Enter the full name of the client, as it appears on their ID or identification.
* **Counselor Name** (Optional, Text): Enter the name of the assigned counselor, if applicable.
* **Services Description** (Required, Text): Briefly describe the counseling services being offered to the client.
* **Confidentiality Explanation** (Optional, Note): Summarize the limits of confidentiality for the client's information.
* **Consent for Service** (Required, Select One): Select "I consent" to indicate that the client agrees to counseling.
* **Consent for Recording** (Optional, Select One): Choose "True" or "False" to indicate whether the client consents to recording sessions.
* **Emergency Contact** (Optional, Text): Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact, if applicable.
* **Signature Printed Name** (Required, Text): Type the client's printed name as their signature.
* **Date Signed** (Required, Date): Enter the date of the consent.
