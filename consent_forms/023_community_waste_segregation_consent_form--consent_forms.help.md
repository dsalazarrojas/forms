# Community Waste Segregation Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from community residents to participate in the waste segregation program. To complete this form, please follow these steps.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out each section as instructed and make sure to provide accurate and complete information. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`resident_full_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name, including your first and last name.
* **Email Address** (`resident_email`, email, required): Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`resident_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Household Address** (`household_address`, text, required): Enter your household address, including street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Number of People in Household** (`household_size`, number, required): Enter the total number of residents in your household.
* **Which waste segregation program are you joining?** (`program_name`, select_one, required): Select one of the available programs:
	+ Curbside recycling program
	+ Compost collection program
	+ Hazardous waste disposal
	+ E-waste recycling
	+ Bulk item pickup
	+ Full waste segregation package
* **Program Details** (`section_program_details`, note, required): Read and understand the program details.
* **I understand the requirements of the waste segregation program** (`understand_program_requirements`, select_one, required): Select "Yes, I Understand" if you comprehend the program requirements.
* **I understand that bins must be placed curbside by 7am on pickup day** (`understand_bin_placement`, select_one, required): Select "Yes, I Understand" to confirm you know bin placement requirements.
* **I understand that contaminated bins may not be collected** (`understand_contamination_policy`, select_one, required): Select "Yes, I Understand" to confirm you know the contamination policy.
* **I agree to properly sort waste according to guidelines** (`agree_proper_sorting`, select_one, required): Select "Yes, I Agree" to confirm your commitment to proper sorting.
* **I agree to keep bins clean and in good condition** (`agree_bin_maintenance`, select_one, required): Select "Yes, I Agree" to confirm your commitment to bin maintenance.
* **I agree to place bins in accessible locations for collection** (`agree_accessible_placement`, select_one, required): Select "Yes, I Agree" to confirm your commitment to accessible bin placement.
* **I agree to report any contamination issues promptly** (`report_contamination`, select_one, required): Select "Yes, I Agree" to confirm your commitment to reporting contamination.
* **I agree to the collection of my waste disposal data** (`consent_data_collection`, select_one, required): Select "Yes, I Consent" to confirm your consent to data collection.
* **I understand my data will be used for program evaluation** (`understand_data_use`, select_one, required): Select "Yes, I Understand" to confirm your understanding of data use.
* **I consent to occasional home visits for education purposes** (`consent_home_visits`, select_one, required): Select "Yes, I Consent" to confirm your consent to home visits.
* **I understand the fee structure for this program** (`understand_fees`, select_one, required): Select "Yes, I Understand" to confirm your understanding of fees.
* **I understand that additional pickups may incur extra charges** (`understand_additional_charges`, select_one, required): Select "Yes, I Understand" to confirm your understanding of additional charges.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select one of the available payment methods:
	+ Monthly utility bill
	+ Credit card auto-pay
	+ Check
	+ Online payment
	+ Cash at office
* **When would you like to receive your bins?** (`bin_delivery_preference`, select_one, required): Select one of the available options:
	+ As soon as possible
	+ Within 2 weeks
	+ Within 1 month
	+ Specific date (note below)
* **If specific date, please specify** (`bin_delivery_date`, date, optional): If you selected a specific date, enter it here.
* **Any special instructions for bin placement at your home?** (`bin_placement_instructions`, text, optional): Enter any special instructions for bin placement.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, required): Select one of the available contact methods:
	+ Email
	+ Phone call
	+ Text message
	+ Mail
	+ No preference
* **Would you like to receive our newsletter?** (`newsletter_signup`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes, please" to receive our newsletter.
* **Any additional comments or questions?** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions.
* **Date of Consent** (`consent_date`, date, required): Enter today's date.
* **Electronic Signature** (`electronic_signature`, text, required): Type your full name.

## Tips
- Please answer all required fields accurately and completely.
- Review each field carefully to ensure you understand its purpose and requirements.
- If you are unsure about any part of the form, please ask for clarification.

Note: This guide is intended to help you complete the form accurately. However, if you are still unsure about any part of the form, please contact your program administrator for further guidance.
