# Educational Program Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Program Waiver Form is used to obtain a waiver from participants in an educational program, acknowledging the risks associated with it.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read and understand the form before filling it out.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and truthful information.
3. Review each field to ensure you have filled out all required information.
4. Sign the form to confirm your participation and understanding of the risks involved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Program Details** (`program_details`, text, optional): This field is used to provide a brief description of the educational program you are participating in.
  • Please type a brief summary of the program.
  • This field is optional but helps us understand your context.
- **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, optional): This field is used to collect participant information.
  • Please provide your name and any other relevant contact information.
  • This field is optional but helps us stay in touch with you.
- **Waiver** (`waiver`, text, optional): This field is used to acknowledge the risks associated with the educational program.
  • Please type a short statement indicating your understanding of the risks.
  • This field is optional but crucial for your safety.
- **Risk Acknowledgement** (`risk_acknowledgement`, text, optional): This field is used to formally acknowledge the risks involved.
  • Please type your name and date to confirm your understanding of the risks.
  • This field is optional but essential for your protection.
- **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): This field is used to provide emergency contact information.
  • Please provide the name and contact information of a person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
  • This field is optional but helps us reach you or someone who can assist you.
- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): This field is used to collect additional contact information.
  • Please provide any other relevant contact information.
  • This field is optional but helps us communicate with you effectively.
- **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): This field is used to sign the form.
  • Please type your name and date to confirm your participation.
  • This field is optional but necessary for your official participation.
- **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, optional): This field is used to sign the form as a participant.
  • Please type your name and date to confirm your participation.
  • This field is optional but necessary for your official participation.
- **Date** (`date`, date, optional): This field is used to record the date you completed the form.
  • Please select a date to confirm the completion date.
  • This field is optional but helps us track changes.
- **Time** (`time`, time, optional): This field is used to record the completion time of the form.
  • Please select the time when you completed the form.
  • This field is optional but helps us track completion timing.
- **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, optional): This field is used to record the participant's signature.
  • Please sign your name and date to confirm your participation.
  • This field is optional but necessary for your official participation.

Note: The fields marked as "optional" are not required for form submission, but it's recommended to fill them out for a complete and accurate record.
