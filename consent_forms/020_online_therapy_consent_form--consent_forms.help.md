The "online-therapy-consent-form" form is used to obtain consent from patients for online therapy sessions. It is a critical document that ensures patients understand and agree to the terms and conditions of their online therapy sessions.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the form's required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form's structure. These fields include patient name, date of birth, email, address, phone number, and signature.
2.  Select the appropriate options for the "Patient Consent Status," "Next of Kin Consent," "Healthcare Provider," and "Online Therapy Frequency" fields.
3.  Enter the dates of "Online Therapy Start Date" and "Online Therapy End Date."
4.  Optionally, provide contact information for the healthcare provider and next of kin.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Patient Name** (text, required): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification documents.
*  **Patient Date of Birth** (date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in the standard format (MM/DD/YY).
*  **Online Therapy Start Date** (date, required): Enter the start date of the online therapy sessions.
*  **Online Therapy End Date** (date, required): Enter the end date of the online therapy sessions.
*  **Online Therapy Frequency** (text, required): Specify the frequency of online therapy sessions.
*  **Patient Email** (email, required): Enter the patient's email address.
*  **Next of Kin Email** (email, optional): Enter the next of kin's email address (if applicable).
*  **Healthcare Provider Email** (email, required): Enter the healthcare provider's email address.
*  **Patient Address** (text, required): Enter the patient's address.
*  **Next of Kin Address** (text, required): Enter the next of kin's address (if applicable).
*  **Healthcare Provider Address** (text, required): Enter the healthcare provider's address.
*  **Patient Phone** (text, required): Enter the patient's phone number.
*  **Next of Kin Phone** (text, required): Enter the next of kin's phone number (if applicable).
*  **Healthcare Provider Phone** (text, required): Enter the healthcare provider's phone number.
*  **Patient Consent Status** (select multiple, required): Select "Yes" if the patient has given consent; "No" otherwise.
*  **Next of Kin Consent** (select multiple, required): Select "Yes" if the next of kin has given consent; "No" otherwise.
*  **Healthcare Provider** (select multiple, required): Select "Yes" if the healthcare provider has given consent; "No" otherwise.
*  **Patient Notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional comments or concerns from the patient.
*  **Next of Kin Contact Info** (text, optional): Enter contact information for the next of kin (if applicable).
*  **Healthcare Provider Contact Info** (text, optional): Enter contact information for the healthcare provider.
*  **Healthcare Provider Notes** (note, optional): Enter any comments or concerns from the healthcare provider.
*  **Patient Signature** (text, required): Sign the form to confirm patient consent.
*  **Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of submission or signing of the form.

Note that all required fields are marked as such in the form's structure and must be filled in to complete the form properly.
