# Physician Supervision Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physician Supervision Agreement form is used to document the supervision of physicians within a practice. This form is essential for ensuring that physicians are properly supervised and monitored in their practice, which is critical for maintaining high-quality patient care and meeting regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the **Practice Name**, which should be the name of the practice where the supervision will take place.
2. Enter the **Physician Name**, which should be the name of the physician being supervised.
3. Select the **Supervising Physician** who will be responsible for overseeing the physician's practice.
4. Choose the **Effective Date** when the supervision will begin.
5. Select the **Supervision Level** that best describes the level of supervision required. You can choose from Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3.
6. Enter the **Practice Address**, where the supervision will take place.
7. Enter the **Supervising Doctor** name, phone number, and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Practice Name**: This is the name of the practice where the supervision will take place. Enter the exact name of the practice as it appears on their business records.
* **Physician Name**: This is the name of the physician being supervised. Enter the correct name of the physician as it appears on their medical license.
* **Supervising Physician**: This is the physician who will be overseeing the supervisee's practice. Enter their name, title, and contact information (phone and email).
* **Effective Date**: Enter the date when the supervision will begin. This date should be a specific date (MM/DD/YY) when the supervision will start.
* **Supervision Level**: This field indicates the level of supervision required. Choose from Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3, which describe the intensity of supervision needed.
* **Practice Address**: Enter the address of the practice where the supervision will take place.
* **Supervising Doctor**: Enter the name, phone number, and email address of the supervising doctor.
* **Supervising Doctor Phone**: Enter the phone number of the supervising doctor.
* **Supervising Doctor Email**: Enter the email address of the supervising doctor.
* **Practice Location**: Enter the location of the practice (e.g., office building, hospital, etc.).
* **Practice Hours**: Enter the practice's hours of operation.
* **Practice Phone**: Enter the practice's phone number.
* **Physician Signature**: This is a required field where the supervising doctor signs to acknowledge their agreement to supervise.
* **Supervising Doctor Signature**: This is a required field where the supervising doctor signs to acknowledge their agreement to supervise.
* **Supervising Doctor Date**: Enter the date the supervising doctor signs the agreement.
* **Supervising Doctor Level**: This field indicates the level of supervision required. Choose from Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3.
* **Supervising Doctor Title**: Enter the title of the supervising doctor.
* **Supervisor Signature Date**: Enter the date the supervisor signs the agreement.
* **Supervisor Signature**: This is a required field where the supervisor signs to acknowledge their agreement to supervise.
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of the supervisor.
* **Supervisor Level**: This field indicates the level of supervision required. Choose from Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3.
* **Supervisor Doctor**: Enter the name of the supervisor doctor.
* **Supervisor Phone**: Enter the supervisor's phone number.
* **Physician Name**: Enter the name of the supervisee (physician being supervised).
* **Practice Name Label**: Enter the label for the practice name (not actually used in the form).
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of the supervisor.
* **Supervisor Level**: Enter the level of supervision required. Choose from Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3.

Note: Some fields are not actually used in the form but are included in the help guide to provide clarity.
