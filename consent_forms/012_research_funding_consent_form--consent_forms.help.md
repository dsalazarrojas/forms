# Research Funding Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from research participants in exchange for research funding. It helps researchers to ensure that participants are aware of and understand the research being conducted, its risks, and the implications of participating.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and contact information (address, telephone number, and email) as requested.
2. Briefly describe the research study in which you are participating.
3. Sign and date this form to indicate your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, Text, Required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **contact address** (`contact_address`, Text, Optional): Enter your address where you can be contacted regarding this research study.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, Date, Optional): Date of your consent to participate in the research study.
* **contact telephone** (`contact_telephone`, Text, Optional): Enter your telephone number where you can be contacted.
* **contact email** (`contact_email`, Text, Optional): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Research Study Description** (`research_study_description`, Text, Optional): Briefly describe the research study in which you are participating. 

## Tips
- Make sure to read and understand the research study description carefully before signing this form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the researcher or institution conducting the study.
- Keep a copy of this signed form for your records.
