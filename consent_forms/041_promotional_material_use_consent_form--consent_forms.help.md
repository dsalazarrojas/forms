# promotional_material_use_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect your consent for businesses to use your promotional materials featuring your name or likeness. Fill out this form to provide consent for the use of your promotional materials on social media or other marketing channels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1. Ensure you have the necessary permissions or approvals before filling it out.
2. Fill in your business name as it appears on your promotional materials.
3. Provide your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
4. Enter a valid email address for easy communication.
5. Mention your current or past phone number, if applicable.
6. Share a brief testimonial about your experience with the business (optional).
7. Indicate your agreement on using your promotional materials.
8. Select whether or not you've used images featuring your likeness in promotional materials.
9. Specify how you've used those images (e.g., on your own social media, allowed the business to use them).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name (`business_name`, Text, Required):** Enter your business name as it is displayed on your promotional materials.
* **Contact Info (`contact_info`, Text, Optional):** This is where we'll reach out to you if we need further information or clarification.
* **Email (`email`, Email, Required):** A valid email address for easy communication and follow-up.
* **Phone Number (`phone`, Text, Optional):** Mention any current or past phone number you'd like to share.
* **Testimonial (`testimonial`, Text, Optional):** Share a brief testimonial about your experience with the business (if you'd like).
* **Agreement (`agreement`, Select One, Required):** Indicate whether you agree or do not agree to the use of your promotional materials.
* **Images (`images`, Select Multiple, Optional):** Select how you've used or allowed the use of images featuring your likeness.

Note: The agreement field requires a clear "yes" or "no" answer, while the images field allows you to select multiple options.
