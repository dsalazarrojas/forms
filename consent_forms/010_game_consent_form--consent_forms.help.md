# Game Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Game Consent Form is designed to collect necessary information and consent for game-related activities. This form is used to gather relevant details and preferences from users, helping us better understand their needs and provide an enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with your information.
2. Review and select your consent options carefully.
3. Provide additional comments if you have any concerns or suggestions.
4. Submit the form to proceed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Game Consent Form** (`title`, optional): Enter a title for this form, if desired.
* **Name** (`game_consent_form_name`, optional): Enter your name to be associated with your consent and feedback.
* **Email** (`game_consent_form_email`, optional): Enter your email address if you want to receive updates or notifications.
* **Phone** (`game_consent_form_phone`, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted directly.
* **Topics to Talk to GM About** (`game_consent_form_topics_to_talk_to_gm`, optional): Specify the topics you'd like to discuss with the Game Master (GM) or project team.
* **Topics** (`game_consent_form_topics`, optional, select multiple): Select one or more topics that interest you or are relevant to your feedback.
* **Consent** (`game_consent_form_consent`, optional, select one): Choose how you feel about the game-related activity. Options are: Agree, Disagree, or Abstain.
* **Additional Comments** (`game_consent_form_additional_comments`, optional): Share any additional thoughts, concerns, or suggestions you have about the game.

## Tips
- Be honest when selecting your consent options.
- Provide as much detail as possible in the "Topics to Talk to GM About" field.
- For "Topics", select all relevant topics that apply.
- If unsure about any field, ask a team member for assistance.
- Review your form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
