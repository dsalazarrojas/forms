# occupational_therapy_innovation_feature_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The occupational therapy innovation feature consent form is used to track the consent of occupational therapy innovation features. This form is used to collect information about a patient's consent to participate in occupational therapy innovation features.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's information on the "patient_info" field.
2. Select "yes" or "no" for the "consent" field, indicating whether the patient has given their consent to participate in the innovation feature.
3. Enter any additional information about the innovation feature in the "innovation" field.
4. Provide contact information for the patient in the "contact_info" field.
5. Date and time of submission is required for "submission_date" and "submitted_at_user".
6. Select the status of the submission from "published", "draft", or "pending" for "status".
7. Enter any comments or feedback in the "comments" field.
8. If required, enter the user's name who created, updated, submitted, or assigned the form in the respective fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_info** (text): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, or any other relevant information.
* **consent** (select_multiple): Select "yes" to indicate the patient has given their consent, "no" otherwise.
* **innovation** (text): Enter any additional information about the innovation feature, e.g., description, purpose, or expected benefits.
* **contact_info** (text): Enter the patient's contact information, e.g., phone number, email address, or address.
* **submission_date** (date): Date of submission in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **created_at** (time): Date and time when the form was created.
* **updated_at** (time): Date and time when the form was last updated.
* **created_by** (text): Name of the user who created the form.
* **updated_by** (text): Name of the user who last updated the form.
* **submitted_at** (date): Date of submission in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **submitted_by** (text): Name of the user who submitted the form.
* **submitted_to** (text): Name of the user to whom the form was submitted.
* **assigned_to** (text): Name of the user to whom the form was assigned.
* **status** (select_multiple): Select "published", "draft", or "pending" to indicate the status of the submission.
* **comments** (note): Enter any comments or feedback about the submission.
* **comments_user** (text): Enter the user's name who created the comments.
* **created_by_user** (text): Name of the user who created the submission.
* **updated_by_user** (text): Name of the user who last updated the submission.
* **submitted_by_user** (text): Name of the user who submitted the form.
* **assigned_to_user** (text): Name of the user to whom the form was assigned.
* **submitted_to_user** (text): Name of the user to whom the form was submitted.
* **submitted_at_user** (time): Date and time of submission in YYYY-MM-DD format.
