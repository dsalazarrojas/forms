# Academic Reflection Podcast Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from participants for their participation in an academic reflection podcast. It includes fields for personal information, consent details, and conditions for participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review and read the form carefully to understand the purpose and consent process.
2.  Enter your full name and email address in the respective fields.
3.  Choose your role in the academic context (e.g. Student, Faculty, Researcher, etc.).
4.  Provide your institution or organization affiliation.
5.  Enter the title of the podcast episode or series.
6.  Choose the date of recording.
7.  Provide your consent to having your voice recorded for the podcast.
8.  Select the platforms where you consent to the publication and distribution of the podcast.
9.  Choose how you wish to be identified on the podcast (full name, first name only, or anonymous).
10.  Confirm your understanding of the editing process and conditions for withdrawal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Academic Reflection Podcast Consent Form --** (Page 1):
    *   This is the form header, indicating the purpose and context of the form.
* **Participant full name** (Page 2):
    *   Please enter your full name as it should appear on the podcast.
* **Email address** (Page 3):
    *   Enter the email address where you can be reached for any updates or follow-ups.
* **Phone number** (Page 4):
    *   This field is optional, but providing a phone number can help with communication and scheduling.
* **Your role** (Page 5):
    *   Choose your role in the academic context (e.g. Student, Faculty, etc.).
* **Institution or organization affiliation** (Page 6):
    *   Enter your affiliation with the institution or organization.
* **Title of the podcast episode or series** (Page 7):
    *   Enter the title of the podcast episode or series you are participating in.
* **Date of recording** (Page 8):
    *   Enter the date of the recording.
* **-- Consent Details --** (Page 9):
    *   This section provides information about the consent process.
* **I consent to having my voice recorded for this academic podcast** (Page 10):
    *   Select "Yes - I consent" to confirm your agreement to being recorded.
* **I consent to the publication and distribution of the podcast on the following platforms (select all that apply)** (Page 11):
    *   Choose the platforms where you consent to the podcast being published and distributed.
* **I consent to my name being disclosed in connection with the podcast** (Page 12):
    *   Select how you wish to be identified on the podcast (full name, first name only, or anonymous).
* **I understand that the recording may be edited for clarity and length** (Page 13):
    *   Confirm your understanding of the editing process.
* **I understand I may withdraw my consent at any time before publication by contacting the project coordinator** (Page 14):
    *   Confirm your understanding of the withdrawal conditions.
* **Date of consent** (Page 15):
    *   Enter the date of consent.
* **Any additional conditions or requests regarding your participation** (Page 16):
    *   Enter any additional conditions or requests you may have for your participation.
