### # Blood Donor Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from blood donors, ensuring they understand the blood donation process, risks, and consequences. It's crucial to carefully review and fill out this form accurately before proceeding with the donation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by reading and understanding the consent sections, which are located at the top of the form.
2. Fill out your name and ID number (if previously registered) on pages 2 and 3.
3. Enter your date of birth on page 4.
4. Provide your contact number on page 5.
5. Answer the questions on pages 6-13, agreeing or disagreeing with the statements as applicable.
6. Sign and date your consent on page 15.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Name** (Page 2, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Donor ID Number** (Page 3, text, optional): If you have a previously registered ID number, enter it here.
* **Date of Birth** (Page 4, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contact Number** (Page 5, text, required): Enter your contact number for follow-up purposes.
* **I understand the blood donation process and risks** (Page 6, select_one, required): Check the box to confirm you understand the risks and process.
* **I consent to testing of my blood for infectious diseases** (Page 7, select_one, required): Check the box to agree to testing for infectious diseases.
* **I consent to health screening and questioning** (Page 8, select_one, required): Check the box to agree to health screening and questioning.
* **I accept deferral if I am deemed ineligible** (Page 9, select_one, required): Check the box to confirm you understand you may be deferred if deemed ineligible.
* **I acknowledge the risks of blood donation** (Page 10, select_one, required): Check the box to confirm you understand the risks of blood donation.
* **I confirm I am in good health** (Page 11, select_one, required): Check the box to confirm you are in good health.
* **I confirm I am not pregnant** (Page 12, select_one, required): Check the box to confirm you are not pregnant.
* **I have not traveled to high-risk areas recently** (Page 13, select_one, required): Check the box to confirm you have not traveled to high-risk areas recently.
* **I waive anonymity for follow-up if needed** (Page 14, select_one, optional): Check the box to confirm you are willing to waive anonymity (not required).
* **Date of Consent** (Page 15, date, required): Enter the date and time you signed this consent form.
* **Healthcare Provider or Witness Name** (Page 16, text, optional): Enter the name of the healthcare provider or witness who verified your identity.

Note: Ensure you carefully review and understand each field before filling out the form, and do not hesitate to ask questions or seek clarification if needed.
