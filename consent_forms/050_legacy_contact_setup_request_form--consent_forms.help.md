# Legacy Contact Setup Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide information about the setup of a legacy contact for an account or organization. Fill in the form with the required details to ensure that your legacy contact preferences and wishes are respected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the name of the trusted person who will be the legacy contact.
2.  Enter the email address of the trusted person.
3.  Enter the phone number of the trusted person.
4.  Enter any other account information, such as the account name and type.
5.  Specify the reason why you are setting up this legacy contact.
6.  Choose the method by which you would like to contact the legacy contact.
7.  Select the legacy contact option.
8.  Enter the date of birth of the trusted person (if applicable).
9.  Enter the date of death of the trusted person (if applicable).
10. Enter any additional notes about the legacy contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Legacy Contact Setup**: This is the text input field where you can type in the name of the trusted person who will be the legacy contact.
* **Trusted Person**: Enter the name of the person who will be contacted in case of emergencies or when the account holder is unreachable.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the trusted person.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the trusted person.
* **Account**: Enter any account information, such as the account name and type.
* **Account Name**: Enter the name of the account that this legacy contact is for.
* **Account Type**: Enter the type of account this is (e.g., individual, business, organization).
* **Reason**: Specify the reason why you are setting up this legacy contact.
* **Legacy Contact Method**: Choose how you would like to contact the legacy contact, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Legacy Contact**: Select one or more options to determine whether the legacy contact should be contacted in specific circumstances.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the date of birth of the trusted person (if applicable).
* **Date of Death**: Enter the date of death of the trusted person (if applicable).
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the legacy contact.

## Tips
* Make sure you have permission to use this form for this purpose.
* Double-check the information you enter to ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any part of this form, contact the appropriate authority for assistance.
