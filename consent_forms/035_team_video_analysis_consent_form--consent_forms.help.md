# Team Video Analysis Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from team members for video analysis or recording. It is essential to fill out this form carefully to ensure you understand what information is being requested and what it will be used for.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please ensure you are filling out this form for the correct purpose: video analysis or recording for your team.
2. Fill out your basic information (First Name, Last Name, Email Address) and, if you have a parent or guardian who is responsible for your well-being, provide their details as well (Parent First Name, Parent Last Name, Parent Email Address). This information helps us ensure we have the correct contact details for your parents or guardians.
3. If you're under 18, you will also need to fill out your Date of Birth.
4. Please answer the consent questions carefully. "True" means you consent to the specified action, while "False" means you do not consent.
   - Consent to Record Video: If you select "True", this means you consent to being recorded during the video analysis.
   - Consent to Train Coaches: If you select "True", you consent to having your video analyzed by coaches to aid their training.
   - Consent to Share Video with Coaches: If you select "True", you agree to have your recorded video shared with coaches for analysis.
   - Consent to Share Video with Parents: If you select "True", you agree to have your recorded video shared with your parents for feedback or other purposes.
5. Finally, fill out the Date of Consent, which indicates the date you have provided consent for these actions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required: false): Your email address for communication.
* **Parent First Name** (`parent_first_name`, `text`, required: false): If you have a parent or guardian responsible for your well-being, enter their first name here.
* **Parent Last Name** (`parent_last_name`, `text`, required: false): If you have a parent or guardian responsible for your well-being, enter their last name here.
* **Parent Email Address** (`parent_email_address`, `email`, required: false): If you have a parent or guardian responsible for your well-being, enter their email address here.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): If you're under 18, please enter your date of birth to help ensure consent is provided with proper oversight.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, `text`, required: false): The name of the team you are representing or participating with.
* **Consent to Record Video** (`consent_to_record_video`, `select_one`, required: false): Please choose "True" if you consent to being recorded during video analysis.
* **Consent to Train Coaches** (`consent_to_train_coaches`, `select_one`, required: false): Please choose "True" if you consent to having your video analyzed by coaches for their training.
* **Consent to Share Video with Coaches** (`consent_to_share_video_with_coaches`, `select_one`, required: false): Please choose "True" if you agree to have your recorded video shared with coaches for analysis.
* **Consent to Share Video with Parents** (`consent_to_share_video_with_parents`, `select_one`, required: false): Please choose "True" if you agree to have your recorded video shared with your parents for feedback or other purposes.
* **Date of Consent** (`date_of_consent`, `date`, required: false): The date on which you provided consent for these actions.
 
## Tips
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask the responsible team or person conducting the analysis for clarification.
- Ensure you understand what each field means and what implications it may have.
- If you're under 18, please ensure someone assists you in filling this form, especially if there's a parent or guardian you're responsible for contacting.
