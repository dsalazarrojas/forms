# AI Ethics In Education Research Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help researchers apply for permission to conduct studies that involve the use of AI in educational settings. The form aims to ensure that researchers address various ethical considerations and follow best practices in AI development and deployment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, email, and institution affiliation.
2. Provide a brief title and description of your research project.
3. Explain the primary goals and objectives of your study.
4. Specify the area of AI application in education (e.g., personalized learning, assessment, administrative tasks, content creation, student support systems, or other).
5. Describe how you will address ethical considerations, such as data privacy protection and bias mitigation.
6. Outline your estimated research duration and timeline.
7. If applicable, provide information on the funding source and IRB or ethics committee approval status.
8. Finally, describe how you will obtain and manage participant consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Principal Investigator Full Name**: Please enter your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Principal Investigator Email**: Enter your professional email address for research correspondence.
* **Institution Affiliation**: Specify the university or organization conducting the research.
* **Research Project Title**: Briefly describe the title of your research study.
* **Research Objectives**: Explain the primary goals and objectives of your study.
* **Area of AI Application in Education**: Select the specific educational context where AI will be applied (e.g., personalized learning, assessment, administrative tasks, content creation, student support systems, or other).
* **Ethical Considerations Addressed**: Describe how you will address ethical implications, including data protection and bias mitigation.
* **Data Privacy Protection Measures**: Explain how you will protect participant data (e.g., anonymization, encryption, secure data storage).
* **Bias Mitigation Strategies**: Describe how you will prevent or address algorithmic bias.
* **Estimated Research Duration**: Provide an estimated timeline for the study.
* **Funding Source**: If applicable, provide information on the organization providing financial support.
* **IRB or Ethics Committee Approval Status**: Select whether your study has received institutional approval or if it's pending.
* **Description of Consent Process**: Explain how you will inform and obtain participant consent.

**Tips**
Please ensure that you provide accurate and honest information throughout the form. This form will be reviewed to ensure that your research meets the required standards for AI ethics in education.
