# Title Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Title Release Form" is a document used for transferring the ownership of a vehicle. It's an essential step in the vehicle ownership transfer process.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  To start, ensure you have all the necessary information ready, including the vehicle's title number, plate number, make, model, and any other relevant vehicle details.
2.  Select the correct vehicle title from the provided options.
3.  Fill in the "Title Number" field with the actual title number.
4.  Enter the "Plate Number" if it's available.
5.  Select the "Title Type" to indicate the type of title (e.g., clean, salvage, etc.).
6.  Enter the "Date Issued" if applicable.
7.  Fill in the issuer's details, including "Issuer ID Number," "Issuer Address," "Issuer DOB," "Issuer Phone," and "Signature."
8.  Ensure that "Party A" and "Party B" sections are filled correctly, as per the context of the vehicle transfer.
9.  Fill in the "Date Completed" and "Completed By" sections, which may involve dates and details of the completion process.
10.  Finally, review all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **1. Vehicle Title** (`<name>: Title Number`, `<type>: number`, required/optional): Enter the actual title number of the vehicle.
*   **2. Party A** (`<name>: Party A`, `<type>: select_one`, required/optional): Select "Party A" as the party involved in the title transfer.
*   **3. Party B** (`<name>: Party B`, `<type>: select_one`, required/optional): Select "Party B" as the party involved in the title transfer.
*   **4. Date Signed** (`<name>: date_signed`, `<type>: date`, required/optional): Enter the date on which you sign the form.
*   **5. Signature A** (`<name>: signature_a_label`, `<type>: note`, required/optional): This is where Party A signs the form, typically by their signature or a stamp.
*   **6. Signature B** (`<name>: signature_b_label`, `<type>: note`, required/optional): This is where Party B signs the form, typically by their signature or a stamp.
*   **7. Vehicle Title** (`<name>: vehicle_title_label`, `<type>: select_one`, required/optional): Select the correct vehicle title from the options provided.
*   **8. Date Issued** (`<name>: date_issued_label`, `<type>: date`, required/optional): Enter the date the vehicle title was issued, if applicable.
*   **9. Issuer ID Number** (`<name>: issuer_id_number_label`, `<type>: number`, required/optional): Enter the ID number of the issuing authority.
*   **10. Issuer Address** (`<name>: issuer_address_label`, `<type>: text`, required/optional): Enter the address of the issuing authority.
*   **11. Issuer DOB** (`<name>: issuer_dob_label`, `<type>: date`, required/optional): Enter the date of birth of the issuing authority.
*   **12. Issuer Phone** (`<name>: issuer_phone_label`, `<type>: text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the issuing authority.
*   **13. Party A Signature** (`<name>: party_a_signature_label`, `<type>: note`, required/optional): This is where Party A signs the form, typically by their signature or a stamp.
*   **14. Party B Signature** (`<name>: party_b_signature_label`, `<type>: note`, required/optional): This is where Party B signs the form, typically by their signature or a stamp.
*   **15. Date Completed** (`<name>: date_completed_label`, `<type>: date`, required/optional): Enter the date of completion of the form.
*   **16. Completed By** (`<name>: completed_by_label`, `<type>: text`, required/optional): Enter the name and signature of the person completing this form.
*   **17. Completed By Phone** (`<name>: completed_by_phone_label`, `<type>: text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the person completing this form.
*   **18. Title Number** (`<name>: title_number_label`, `<type>: number`, required/optional): Enter the title number of the vehicle.
*   **19. Plate Number** (`<name>: plate_number_label`, `<type>: text`, required/optional): Enter the plate number of the vehicle.
*   **20. Year** (`<name>: year_label`, `<type>: text`, required/optional): Enter the year of manufacture of the vehicle.
*   **21. Make** (`<name>: make_label`, `<type>: text`, required/optional): Enter the make of the vehicle.
*   **22. Model** (`<name>: model_label`, `<type>: text`, required/optional): Enter the model of the vehicle.
*   **23. VIN** (`<name>: vin_label`, `<type>: text`, required/optional): Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) of the vehicle.
*   **24. Title Type** (`<name>: title_type_label`, `<type>: text`, required/optional): Select the type of title (e.g., clean, salvage, etc.).
*   **25. Title Release Date** (`<name>: title_release_date_label`, `<type>: date`, required/optional): Enter the date the title was released.
