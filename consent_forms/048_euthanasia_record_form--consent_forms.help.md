# Euthanasia Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Euthanasia Record Form is a standardized template used to document the events surrounding the euthanasia of an animal. This form is designed to collect information from the owner and veterinarian involved in the procedure, ensuring accurate and transparent record-keeping.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  The form is divided into several sections. Please read through each section carefully and fill in the required information accurately and completely.
2.  The form is organized as follows:
    *   General Information: This section is for providing general details about the animal and its owner.
    *   Pet Information: This section is for providing specific information about the animal, including its age, breed, and health status.
    *   Reason for Euthanasia: This section is for selecting the reason why the euthanasia was performed.
    *   Veterinarian Information: This section is for providing details about the veterinarian who performed the euthanasia.
    *   Euthanasia Details: This section is for recording specific details about the euthanasia procedure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### General Information
*   **Euthanasia Record Form**: This is the title of the form.
*   **General Information**: This section is where you can add any additional comments or details about the euthanasia procedure.
    *   **Owner Name**: The name of the animal's owner.
    *   **Owner Email**: The email address of the animal's owner.
    *   **Owner Phone**: The phone number of the animal's owner.

### Pet Information
*   **Pet Information**: This section is where you can provide specific information about the animal.
    *   **Owner Name**: The name of the animal's owner (same as in the General Information section).
*   **Age**: The age of the animal at the time of euthanasia.
*   **Breed**: The breed of the animal.
*   **Weight**: The weight of the animal.
*   **Reason For Euthanasia**: This section is for selecting the reason why the euthanasia was performed.
    *   **Reason For Euthanasia**: A list of possible reasons for euthanasia, including terminal illness, severe injury, or behavioral issues.

### Reason for Euthanasia
*   **Reason For Euthanasia**: Please select the reason for euthanasia from the list provided.

### Veterinarian Information
*   **Veterinarian Name**: The name of the veterinarian who performed the euthanasia.
*   **Veterinarian Email**: The email address of the veterinarian.
*   **Veterinarian Phone**: The phone number of the veterinarian.
*   **Veterinarian Signature**: The signature of the veterinarian.

### Euthanasia Details
*   **Date of Euthanasia**: The date on which the euthanasia was performed.
*   **Time of Euthanasia**: The time at which the euthanasia was performed.
*   **Method of Euthanasia**: The method used for euthanasia, such as injection, anesthesia, or other.
*   **Cause of Death**: The cause of death, such as cancer, injury, or other.
*   **Additional Comments**: Any additional comments or details about the euthanasia procedure.

## Tips

*   Make sure to provide accurate and complete information when filling out the form.
*   If the form requires a signature, make sure to sign it with your name and title.
*   If you are unsure about any of the information on the form, please contact the veterinarian or animal owner for clarification.
