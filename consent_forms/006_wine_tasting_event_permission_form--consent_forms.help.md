# Wine Tasting Event Permission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect permission and information from participants attending a wine tasting event.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the date of the event.
2. Enter the time of the event.
3. Enter your name as a participant.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Enter your email address.
6. Enter your address.
7. Add any additional notes about your participation.
8. Enter the name of the event organizer.
9. Confirm your consent to participate by selecting "Yes" or "No".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Date (`event_date`, date, required: false)**: Please enter the date of the event in MM/dd/yy format.
* **Event Time (`event_time`, time, required: false)**: Enter the time of the event in 12-hour format (e.g., 3:00 PM).
* **Participant Name (`participant_name`, text, required: false)**: Enter your name as a participant in the event.
* **Participant Phone (`participant_phone`, text, required: false)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Participant Email (`participant_email`, email, required: false)**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Participant Address (`participant_address`, text, required: false)**: Enter your address where you can be reached.
* **Participant Notes (`participant_notes`, note, required: false)**: Add any additional notes or comments about your participation.
* **Event Organizer (`event_organizer`, text, required: false)**: Enter the name of the person or organization hosting the event.
* **Participant Consent (`participant_consent`, select_one, required: false)**: Confirm your participation by selecting "Yes" or "No".
