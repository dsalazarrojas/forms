# Virtual Exchange Student Story Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from virtual exchange students to share their stories and media with specified individuals or organizations. The form is a crucial part of the virtual exchange process, ensuring that students' personal information and stories are shared with the right people at the right time.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, select the category you wish to apply (e.g., "Consent Forms").
2. Provide a brief description of your story and the media you are willing to share (if applicable).
3. Choose whether you are allowing media or story-sharing permissions (or both).
4. Specify with whom you are willing to share your story (e.g., "family and friends" or "publicly on social media").
5. Select the duration for which you are willing to share your story (e.g., "ongoing" or "only until [specific date]").
6. You can also specify a form submission date if desired.
7. If you do not wish to use the code-based form builder, please let us know.
8. Finally, you can provide your email address and phone number for future contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (`Category`, Select One, Optional): Select the category that applies to your story (e.g., "Consent Forms").
* **Description** (`Description`, Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of your story (e.g., "I had an amazing experience abroad").
* **Media and Story-Sharing Permissions** (`Media and Story-Sharing Permissions`, Select One, Optional): Choose whether you are allowing media or story-sharing permissions (or both).
* **Story-Sharing** (`Story-Sharing`, Text, Optional): Enter the story you are willing to share (e.g., "I learned how to make traditional [country-specific] dishes").
* **Story-Sharing With Whom** (`Story-Sharing With Whom`, Text, Optional): Specify with whom you are willing to share your story (e.g., "family and friends").
* **Story-Sharing On** (`Story-Sharing On`, Date, Optional): Select the duration for which you are willing to share your story (e.g., "ongoing" or "only until [specific date]").
* **Story-Sharing Duration** (`Story-Sharing Duration`, Time, Optional): Enter the duration for which you are willing to share your story (e.g., "1 hour" or "1 day").
* **Form Submission** (`Form Submission`, Text, Optional): Enter the date you are submitting this form (e.g., "2024-03-15").
* **No Code Form Builder** (`No Code Form Builder`, Text, Optional): If you do not wish to use the code-based form builder, please let us know.
* **Drag-and-Drop Interface** (`Drag-and-Drop Interface`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the drag-and-drop interface (e.g., "I am using the drag-and-drop interface for [reason]").
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address for future contact (e.g., "john.doe@example.com").
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for future contact (e.g., "123-456-7890").
