# Access System Technology Use Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a mandatory legal requirement to confirm your consent for the use of access system technologies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name as it appears on your ID documents.
2. Enter your email address as your primary contact.
3. Provide your employee or student ID, as it is a unique identifier for you within the institution.
4. Read the information provided under the "This form covers..." section to understand what data will be collected and used.
5. Check the consent options for biometric data and location tracking, and select your choice for each.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official ID documents.
* **Email Address (Primary Contact)**: Enter your primary contact email for access and communication purposes.
* **Employee or Student ID**: Enter your unique identifier for the institution.
* **This form covers the use of biometric scanners and mobile access apps and electronic tracking for facility access**: Read this section carefully to understand what data will be collected and used.
* **I consent to the collection and storage of my biometric data for access purposes**: Choose "I Consent" to confirm your agreement.
* **I consent to the logging of my entry and exit times at facility access points**: Choose "I Consent" to confirm your agreement.
* **I understand that my data will be retained for 1 year after my relationship with the institution ends**: Choose "I Understand" to confirm.
* **I understand that data may be processed by a third-party security provider**: Choose "I Understand" to confirm.
* **I agree to maintain the security of any mobile device used for access**: Choose "I Agree" to confirm.
* **By signing - I provide formal consent for the use of access technologies as described**: Choose "I Consent" to confirm.
* **Type Name to Sign**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID documents.
* **Date**: Enter the current date.

Please note that all required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*) in their labels. Fill out all required fields carefully and accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities within your institution.
