# smart_meter_remote_monitoring_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect remote monitoring consent from customers, ensuring that both the customer and the utility provider are aware of and agree to monitor the customer's utility usage.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, ensuring all required fields are filled out.
2. Provide your name and address in the "Customer Name" and "Customer Address" fields.
3. Identify your utility provider in the "Utility Provider" field.
4. Enter the start and end dates for monitoring in the "Monitor Start Date" and "Monitor End Date" fields, if applicable.
5. Sign and date the form in the "Customer Signature" and "Utility Provider Signature" fields.
6. Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate your agreement to monitor your utility usage in the "Monitor Agreement" field.
7. Optional: Provide a brief explanation for your monitor agreement decision in the "Monitor Agreement Explain" field.
8. If necessary, add any additional notes in the "Utility Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Customer Address** (text, optional): Enter your address, including street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Utility Provider** (text, required): Identify your utility provider (e.g., electricity, gas, water).
* **Monitor Start Date** (date, optional): Enter the start date for monitoring, if applicable.
* **Monitor End Date** (date, optional): Enter the end date for monitoring, if applicable.
* **Customer Signature** (note, required): Sign and date this section to indicate your agreement to monitor.
* **Utility Provider Signature** (note, required): Sign and date this section to indicate your agreement to monitor.
* **Monitor Agreement** (select_one, required): Choose "Yes" to indicate your agreement to monitor your utility usage.
* **Monitor Agreement Explain** (text, optional): Briefly explain your decision for monitoring or lack thereof.
* **Utility Notes** (text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes regarding the form or your agreement.
