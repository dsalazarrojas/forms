# Medical Induction Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect medical information from patients to ensure their safety and well-being during their stay in the facility. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and completely, as it helps medical staff provide the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's information, including name, date of birth, and other relevant details.
2. Provide accurate and up-to-date information about the patient's medical history, allergies, and current medical conditions.
3. List any medications the patient is currently taking.
4. Identify their emergency contact person and their relationship to the patient.
5. Provide contact information for the emergency contact, including phone number and email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Patient Name** (text, required)
Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification or medical records.

### 2. **Date of Birth** (date, required)
Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### 3. **Email** (email, optional)
Enter the patient's email address (if available).

### 4. **Phone** (text, optional)
Enter the patient's phone number (if available).

### 5. **Medical History** (text, optional)
Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses, surgeries, or medical conditions.

### 6. **Allergies** (text, optional)
List any known allergies the patient has.

### 7. **Medical Conditions** (text, optional)
Describe the patient's current medical conditions, such as diabetes, hypertension, etc.

### 8. **Medications** (text, optional)
List any medications the patient is currently taking.

### 9. **Emergency Contact Name** (text, optional)
Enter the name of the emergency contact person.

### 10. **Emergency Contact Relationship** (text, optional)
Describe the relationship between the patient and the emergency contact person (e.g., parent, spouse, friend).

### 11. **Emergency Contact Phone** (text, optional)
Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.

### 12. **Emergency Contact Email** (email, optional)
Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.

## Tips
* Be accurate and thorough when providing information to ensure the best possible care for the patient.
* If you're unsure about any information, consult with medical staff or the patient's doctor.
* Update this form if the patient's medical information changes during their stay.
