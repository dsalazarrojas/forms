# Media Publication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Media Publication Consent Form is used to collect consent from individuals for the publication of media related to them. It helps ensure that individuals understand and agree to the terms and conditions of the media publication.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, please fill out this form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure you provide your name, a clear photo of yourself, and the title of the publication.
3. Enter the date of the publication in the correct format (yyyy-mm-dd).
4. Once you have filled out all the required fields, click the submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Media Publication Consent Form** (`media_publication_consent_form`): Enter a brief description of the form purpose (e.g., "I consent to the publication of my media image and name in the [Publication Title] publication").
* **Individual Name** (`individual_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Individual Image** (`individual_image`): Upload a clear and legible photo of yourself.
* **Publication Title** (`publication_title`): Enter the title of the publication that includes your media image or name.
* **Publication Date** (`publication_date`): Enter the date of the publication in the format yyyy-mm-dd (e.g., 2022-01-01).
