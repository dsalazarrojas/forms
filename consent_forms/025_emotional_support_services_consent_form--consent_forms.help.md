# emotional_support_services_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The emotional support services consent form is a document that gathers information from the client to provide them with the best possible services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the label and instructions carefully.
2. Fill out all the required fields (marked as "Required" in the form).
3. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Consent** (User Consent, text, Required): This is a statement of the client's consent to receive emotional support services.
* **Description of Emotional Support Services** (Description of Emotional Support Services, text, Required): Please describe the type of services you are eligible for.
* **Date of Consent** (Date of Consent, date, Required): The date on which you gave your consent for these services.
* **Type of Emotional Support Services** (Type of Emotional Support Services, select_one, Optional): Choose the type of services you are eligible for (In person, Phone, Virtual, Text).
* **Support Frequency** (Support Frequency, select_multiple, Optional): Select the frequency at which you prefer to receive support (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Bi-Monthly).
* **Contact Method** (Contact Method, select_one, Optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, Text, In Person).
* **Start Date** (Start Date, date, Optional): The date when you want to start receiving services.
* **End Date** (End Date, date, Optional): The date when you want to end receiving services.
* **Contact Person** (Contact Person, text, Required): Provide the name and contact information of the person you would like to be in contact with.
* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, text, Required): Provide the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email, email, Required): Provide the email of the contact person.
* **Consent Status** (Consent Status, select_one, Optional): Choose the status of your consent (Accepted, Rejected, Pending).
* **Consent Note** (Consent Note, note, Optional): Add any additional comments about your consent.
* **Emotional Support Services Note** (Emotional Support Services Note, note, Optional): Add any additional comments about the services you are eligible for.
