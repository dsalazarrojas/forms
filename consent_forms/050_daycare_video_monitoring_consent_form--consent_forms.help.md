# Daycare Video Monitoring Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daycare Video Monitoring Consent Form is a document that must be completed and signed by a parent or guardian to provide consent for video recording of their child while in the daycare facility. This form is necessary to ensure that parents/guardians are aware of and agree to the video monitoring process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure that you understand the purpose and contents of the form.
2. Fill in the required information, including your name and your child's date of birth and time of day.
3. Indicate your consent to video monitoring by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as a parent or guardian of the child.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This is your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This is your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Please enter your child's date of birth.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Please enter your preferred time for monitoring.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_multiple, optional): Please select 'Yes' if you agree to video monitoring of your child or 'No' if you do not.
