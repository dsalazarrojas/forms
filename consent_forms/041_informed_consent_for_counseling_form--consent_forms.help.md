# Informed Consent For Counseling Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from clients for counseling services. It ensures that clients understand their rights, responsibilities, and the nature of the therapy sessions. The form is a crucial document for establishing a healthy and informed relationship between the client and the therapist.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in your name and the name of your therapist.
*   Enter the date and time of your first therapy session.
*   Specify the frequency and length of your sessions.
*   Indicate the type of therapy you will be receiving.
*   Choose the type of consent you are giving (informed or non-informed).
*   Select the areas you are willing to share with your therapist.
*   Provide an emergency contact person's name and contact information.
*   Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Informed Consent For Counseling Form** (`title`): This is the title of the form and should not be filled in.
*   **Client Name** (`client_name`): Please enter your full name as you would like it to be listed on records.
*   **Therapist Name** (`therapist_name`): Enter your therapist's full name as they will be working with you.
*   **Therapy Start Date** (`therapy_date`): Enter the date of your first session with the therapist.
*   **Therapy Time** (`therapy_time`): Enter the length of each therapy session in hours.
*   **Therapy Location** (`therapy_location`): Please enter the location of your therapy sessions.
*   **Therapy Goals** (`therapy_goals`): Briefly describe the issues you are seeking help for.
*   **Consent Type** (`consent_type`): Choose whether you are giving informed or non-informed consent.
*   **Informed Consent** (`informed_consent`): This section outlines your understanding of confidentiality, your responsibilities, and your rights as a client.
*   **Therapy Frequency** (`therapy_frequency`): Enter the frequency of your sessions per week or month.
*   **Session Length** (`session_length`): Enter the average length of your sessions.
*   **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`): Provide the name and contact information of a person to contact in case of an emergency.
*   **Client Rights** (`client_rights`): This is a text field for any other rights or concerns you have.
*   **Therapy Type** (`therapy_type`): Enter the type of therapy you are seeking.
*   **Notes** (`notes`): Add any notes or comments about your sessions or therapy.
*   **Client Signature** (`client_sign`): Please sign your name to confirm you have read and agreed to this consent form.
*   **Therapist Signature** (`therapist_sign`): Please sign your name as the therapist to confirm you have reviewed and will follow this consent form.
*   **Completed Date** (`completed_date`): The date this form was completed.
*   **Completed By** (`completed_by`): The name and time of the person who completed this form.
*   **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): The name of the person who submitted this form.
*   **Reviewed By** (`reviewed_by`): The name of the person who reviewed this form.
*   **Reviewed Date** (`reviewed_date`): The date this form was reviewed.
*   **Reviewed By Other** (`reviewed_by_other`): The name of another person who reviewed this form.
*   **Reviewed Date Other** (`reviewed_date_other`): The date this form was reviewed by someone other than the therapist.
*   **Reviewed By Other Signature** (`reviewed_by_other_signature`): The signature of the person who reviewed this form.

Please note that some fields are not required and can be left blank if not applicable. Always refer to your therapist for any questions or clarification.
