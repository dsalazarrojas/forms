# Teacher Interview Publication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain a teacher's consent for a publication related to an interview. It is a crucial step in ensuring that the teacher's information and likeness are used correctly and with their permission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the first name field.
2. Fill in your last name in the last name field.
3. Enter your email address in the email field.
4. Select your department from the department field (e.g. English, Math, Science, History, or Other).
5. Choose the type of publication you are consenting to (e.g. Book, Article, Online Post, or Other).
6. Briefly explain why you are consenting to the publication in the publication consent why field.
7. Confirm your agreement to the publication in the publication consent agreement field.
8. Sign your name in the signature field.
9. Choose the date and time of the interview in the interview date and interview time fields.
10. Choose the date and time of the publication in the publication date and publication time fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (Text, required): Enter your first name.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (Text, required): Enter your last name.

### Email
* **Email** (Email, required): Enter your email address.

### Department
* **Department** (Select Multiple, optional): Select your department from the options (e.g. English, Math, Science, History, or Other).

### Publication Consent
* **Publication Consent** (Text, required): Briefly explain why you are consenting to the publication.

### Type of Publication
* **Type of Publication** (Select One, required): Choose the type of publication you are consenting to (e.g. Book, Article, Online Post, or Other).

### Publication Consent Why
* **Publication Consent Why** (Text, optional): Briefly explain why you are consenting to the publication.

### Publication Consent Agreement
* **Publication Consent Agreement** (Select Multiple, required): Confirm your agreement to the publication (True or False).

### Signature
* **Signature** (Text, required): Sign your name.

### Interview Date
* **Interview Date** (Date, required): Choose the date of the interview.

### Interview Time
* **Interview Time** (Time, required): Choose the time of the interview.

### Publication Date
* **Publication Date** (Date, required): Choose the date of the publication.

### Publication Time
* **Publication Time** (Time, required): Choose the time of the publication.
