# Farmers Insurance Cancellation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Farmers Insurance Cancellation Form is designed to gather necessary information from farmers for the cancellation of their insurance policy. This form aims to streamline the cancellation process, ensuring that all required details are captured accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Please ensure that your internal systems do not already have a cancellation request process in place for the policy. If so, do not use this form.
2.  Go through the form page by page, filling out each section as requested.
3.  Start from the first page, which is the "Customer Details" page. Fill out the "Customer Details" field with accurate and up-to-date information about the farmer's policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details** (`Customer Details`, text, optional):
    This field is for the farmer to provide their personal details, such as name, address, and contact information.
* **Cancellation Reason** (`Cancellation Reason`, select_one, required):
    This field asks the farmer to choose the reason for cancelling their policy. Please select one of the provided options: Reason 1 or Reason 2.
* **Policy Number** (`Policy Number`, text, optional):
    Enter the policy number associated with the insurance policy you wish to cancel.
* **Cancellation Date** (`Cancellation Date`, date, optional):
    This field is for the farmer to enter the date they wish to cancel their policy. Please ensure the date format is MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Confirm Cancellation** (`Confirm Cancellation`, note, optional):
    This field is for the farmer to confirm their cancellation, indicating that they have read and understood the cancellation instructions.

## Tips
*   Please ensure all information is accurate and up-to-date for a smooth cancellation process.
