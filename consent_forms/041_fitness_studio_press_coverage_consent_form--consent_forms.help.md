# Fitness Studio Press Coverage Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Studio Press Coverage Consent Form is used to obtain consent from Fitness Studio's members for the use of their media for press coverage purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Member Information**: This section is used to collect information about the member. Please enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
2. **Media Use Consent**: Select all the types of media you are consenting to use (Image, Video, Audio, Publication, Broadcast).
3. **Why Would You Like to Give Media Use Consent?**: Please provide a brief explanation for why you would like to give media use consent.
4. **Release Options**: Select the specific type of media you would like to release (Image Release, Video Release, Audio Release, Publication Release, Broadcast Release).
5. **Publication Name or Description**: If you selected "Publication Release", please enter the name or description of the publication.
6. **Signature**: The member's signature is required to indicate that they have reviewed and agreed to the terms.
7. **Manager Signature**: The manager's signature is required to indicate that they have reviewed and agreed to the terms.
8. **Date**: Enter the date of the agreement.
9. **Staff Information**: This section is used to collect information from staff members.
	* **Staff Signature**: The staff member's signature is required to indicate that they have reviewed and agreed to the terms.
	* **Staff Name**: The staff member's name.
	* **Date**: The date of the agreement.
10. **Other Press Coverage**: If you would like to release media for other purposes, please select "Other" and provide additional information.
	* **Agreed on Other**: Select whether you agree to release media for other purposes.
	* **Date**: Enter the date of the agreement.
	* **Staff**: Enter the staff member's name.
	* **Additional Notes**: Provide any additional notes.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Review the form carefully before signing to ensure that you understand the terms.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact Fitness Studio's management for clarification.
* This form is only for use with Fitness Studio's members. If you are not a member, please do not complete this form.
