# Virtual Talent Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect basic information from virtual talent for registration purposes. The form is meant to gather relevant details such as contact information, experience, education, and other notable achievements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Scroll down to each page to fill out the requested information.
2. Enter your name and contact information in the "User Input" page.
3. In the "contact_info" field, enter your contact details such as email address, phone number, and location.
4. For "headshot", "demo_reel", "resume", "bio", "experience", "education", "awards_and_accomplishments", you can enter text descriptions of your relevant details.
5. For "headshot_attachment", "experience_attachment", "education_attachment", you can upload attachments related to your headshot, experience, and education respectively.
6. In the "headshot_link" and "demo_reel_link" fields, enter links to your online profiles or websites where your headshot and demo reel can be found.
7. In the "resume_link" field, enter a link to your resume.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **contact_info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact details such as email address, phone number, and location.
* **headshot** (`headshot`, text, optional): Describe your headshot in detail.
* **Demo Reel** (`demo_reel`, text, optional): Describe your demo reel in detail.
* **Headshot Attachment** (`headshot_attachment`, text, optional): Attach any relevant images or files related to your headshot.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, optional): Describe your resume in detail.
* **bio** (`bio`, text, optional): Describe yourself in detail.
* **Experience** (`experience`, text, optional): Describe your experience in detail.
* **Education** (`education`, text, optional): Describe your education in detail.
* **Awards and Accolades** (`awards_and_accomplishments`, text, optional): List any notable achievements.
* **Headshot Link** (`headshot_link`, text, optional): Enter a link to your online profile or website where your headshot can be found.
* **Resume Link** (`resume_link`, text, optional): Enter a link to your resume.
* **Experience Attachment** (`experience_attachment`, text, optional): Attach any relevant files or images related to your experience.
* **Education Attachment** (`education_attachment`, text, optional): Attach any relevant files or images related to your education.
* **Demo Reel Link** (`demo_reel_link`, text, optional): Enter a link to your demo reel.

## Tips
* Please ensure all attachments are in a supported format (e.g. pdf, docx, jpg, png, etc.).
* Ensure that all links are valid and accessible.
* Use a clear and concise format to provide details in the text fields.
