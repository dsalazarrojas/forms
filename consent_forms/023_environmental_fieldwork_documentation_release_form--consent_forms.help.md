# Environmental Fieldwork Documentation Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Environmental Fieldwork Documentation Release Form is a comprehensive document used to capture essential information about fieldwork activities, including the location, team members involved, research objectives, and other relevant details. This form helps ensure that all necessary information is recorded accurately and consistently, making it a vital tool for project managers and team leaders.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form title with the specific title of the fieldwork project.
2. Provide the location of the fieldwork, including the country, state, or region where the work took place.
3. Select the team members who were involved in the fieldwork from the list of available team options.
4. If applicable, add any existing documentation related to the fieldwork, such as photos, videos, or reports.
5. Date and time of consent, if applicable.
6. Enter a valid email address and phone number, if available.
7. Choose the research area(s) of focus, such as Research, Conservation, or Education.
8. Add any additional project details, including specific objectives and goals.
9. Add any notes or observations about the fieldwork, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form-title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a concise title for the fieldwork project.
* **fieldwork-location** (`fieldwork_location`, text, optional): Provide the location of the fieldwork, including the country, state, or region.
* **team-members** (`team_members`, select_multiple, optional): Select the team members who were involved in the fieldwork from the available options:
	+ Team A
	+ Team B
	+ Team C
* **documentation** (`documentation`, text, optional): If applicable, add any existing documentation related to the fieldwork, such as photos, videos, or reports.
* **consent-date** (`consent_date`, date, optional): Date of consent, if applicable.
* **consent-time** (`consent_time`, time, optional): Time of consent, if applicable.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address for contact or further information.
* **phone-number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter a valid phone number for contact or further information.
* **research--conservation** (`research--conservation`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the research area(s) of focus:
	+ Research
	+ Conservation
	+ Education
* **project-details** (`project_details`, text, optional): Add any additional project details, including specific objectives and goals.
* **notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any notes or observations about the fieldwork, if necessary.
