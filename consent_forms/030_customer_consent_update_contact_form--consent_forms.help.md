# customer_consent_update_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The customer_consent_update_contact_form is used to update customer contact information such as First Name, Last Name, Email, Consent, and Phone Number. Additionally, it is used to record customer consent status and assign a tool to follow up with the customer. This form should be completed when a customer's consent status needs to be updated or a new tool needs to be assigned to a customer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the First Name, Last Name, and Email fields with the customer's updated information.
2. Select the customer's current consent status from the "Consent" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the customer's phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. If applicable, assign a tool to follow up with the customer in the "Assigned To" field.
5. Add any additional notes regarding the customer's consent update in the "Notes" field.
6. If the customer's consent status is "Yes", select at least one tool to assign to the customer in the "Assigned Tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (required): Enter the customer's first name.
- **Last Name** (required): Enter the customer's last name.
- **Email** (required): Enter the customer's email address.
- **Consent** (required): Select the customer's current consent status from the dropdown menu.
  - "Yes": The customer has given consent.
  - "No": The customer has not given consent.
  - "Maybe": The customer's consent status is unknown or pending.
- **Phone Number** (required): Enter the customer's phone number.
- **Notes** (optional): Add any additional notes regarding the customer's consent update.
- **Assigned To** (required): Enter the name of the authorized personnel assigned to follow up with the customer.
- **Assigned Tool** (optional): Select at least one tool to assign to the customer for follow up, if the customer's consent status is "Yes".
  - ChatJimmy: A chat-based tool.
  - Emailer: An email-based tool.
  - PhoneBot: A phone-based tool.
- **Output File** (optional): This field is not used in the form's functionality, it is a placeholder for potential future development.

## Tips
- Only authorized personnel should complete this form.
- Ensure that the customer's consent status and assigned tools are accurate and up-to-date.
- If the customer's consent status changes, update the "Consent" field accordingly.
- If the customer's consent status is "Yes", ensure at least one tool is selected in the "Assigned Tool" field.
