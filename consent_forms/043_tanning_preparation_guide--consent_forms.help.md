# Tanning Preparation Guide - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tanning Preparation Guide form is designed to collect client details and obtain consent for communication from the salon. Completing this form ensures that your information is accurately recorded for the tanning preparation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your client details in the "Client Details" section.
2. Read and select the prep instructions that apply to you.
3. Choose your consent preference for receiving emails from the salon.
4. (Optional) Provide additional comments or information in the "Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details (required)**: This section collects your personal details, such as your name and other relevant information. Please ensure that the information is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Prep Instructions (required)**: Select the prep instructions that you have read and understood from the provided options. This is essential for a successful tanning preparation process.
* **Consent (required)**: Choose your preference for receiving emails from the salon. Select one or both options, as per your preference.
	+ I consent to receiving emails from the salon.
	+ I do not consent to receiving emails from the salon.
	+ Unknown: If you are unsure or do not have a preference, you can select this option.
* **Appointment Details (optional)**: If you have any additional comments or information about your appointment, you can provide them in this section.
* **Comments (optional)**: You can use this section to add any additional comments or questions you may have.
