# Dog Adoption Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals or entities adopting dogs from the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **User Input** field with your relevant details.
2. Provide your **Contact Details** in the **Contact Details** field.
3. Enter the **Owner Details** of the adopted dog.
4. Specify the **Dog Details** of the adopted dog.
5. Select the **Adoption Date** of the dog adoption.
6. Enter the **Adoption Fee** paid for the dog adoption (if applicable).
7. Indicate whether you have signed the waiver for the dog adoption by selecting **Yes** or **No** in the **Waiver Signed** field.
8. Leave a **Signature** to acknowledge your understanding of the waiver.
9. Enter your **Email** address for communication purposes.
10. Enter your **Phone** number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Input** (1) (<type:text>): Enter your name and other personal details that we need to know about you.
- **Contact Details** (2) (<type:text>): Provide your address, phone number, and other contact information.
- **Owner Details** (3) (<type:text>): Enter the owner's name, address, and other relevant details about the dog's previous owner.
- **Dog Details** (4) (<type:text>): Enter the dog's name, breed, age, and any other relevant details about the dog.
- **Adoption Date** (5) (<type:date>): Select the date of the dog adoption.
- **Adoption Fee** (6) (<type:number>): Enter the adoption fee paid for the dog adoption (if applicable).
- **Waiver Signed** (7) (<type:select_one>): Select 'Yes' to indicate that you have signed the waiver for the dog adoption.
- **Signature** (8) (<type:note>): Leave a signature to acknowledge your understanding of the waiver.
- **Email** (9) (<type:email>): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
- **Phone** (10) (<type:text>): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
