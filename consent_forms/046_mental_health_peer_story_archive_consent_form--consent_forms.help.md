# Mental Health Peer Story Archive Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Peer Story Archive Consent Form is designed to collect mental health peer stories from contributors. The form collects information such as contributor name, story, consent for publication, and other relevant details. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the contributor's name in the "Contributor Name" field.
2. Type or paste your mental health story in the "Story" field.
3. Select your consent for publication by choosing either "True" or "False" in the "Consent" field.
4. Optionally, select the non-profit organizations that you are associated with in the "Non-profit" field.
5. If you are a member of a support group, enter the name of the group in the "Support Group" field.
6. If you are associated with an advocacy project, enter the name of the project in the "Advocacy Project" field.
7. Review the "Form Instructions" field for any additional guidance on completing the form.
8. Enter the version of the form you are submitting in the "Form Version" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, false): This field allows you to enter a title for your story.
* **Contributor Name** (`contributor_name`, text, true): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the archive.
* **Story** (`story`, text, true): Share your mental health story, describing your experiences and challenges.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, true): Select "True" if you consent to the use of your story in the archive, and "False" if you do not consent.
* **Non-profit** (`non_profit`, select_multiple, false): Select the non-profit organizations that you are associated with, if applicable.
* **Support Group** (`support_group`, text, false): If you are a member of a support group, enter the name of the group here.
* **Advocacy Project** (`advocacy_project`, text, false): If you are associated with an advocacy project, enter the name of the project here.
* **Form Instructions** (`form_instructions`, note, false): Review this section for any additional guidance or instructions on completing the form.
* **Form Version** (`form_version`, text, false): Enter the version of the form you are submitting.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have not submitted a similar story in the past before proceeding.
* Review each field carefully to ensure accurate and complete information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the archive administrators for assistance.
