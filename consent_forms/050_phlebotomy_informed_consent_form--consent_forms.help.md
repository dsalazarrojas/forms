# phlebotomy_informed_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from patients for phlebotomy procedures.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the **Patient Name** field.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth in the **Date of Birth** field.
3. Enter the patient's medical history in the **Medical History** field.
4. Select the type of consent from the **Consent Type** dropdown menu.
5. Enter the medical procedure that will be performed in the **Medical Procedure** field.
6. Enter a detailed explanation of the informed consent in the **Informed Consent** field.
7. Sign and date the form in the **Signature** field.
8. Enter the medical facility where the procedure will be performed in the **Medical Facility** field.
9. Enter the date and time of the medical procedure in the **Medical Procedure Date** and **Medical Procedure Time** fields.
10. Enter the patient's email address in the **Email** field.
11. Enter the patient's phone number in the **Phone** field.
12. Enter the patient's address, city, state, zip, and country in the **Address**, **City**, **State**, **Zip**, and **Country** fields.
13. Enter the name of the medical staff member who is providing the consent in the **Medical Staff Name** field.
14. Enter the ID number of the medical staff member in the **Medical Staff ID** field.
15. Enter the title of the medical staff member in the **Medical Staff Title** field.
16. Enter the department of the medical staff member in the **Medical Staff Department** field.
17. Enter the phone number of the medical staff member in the **Medical Staff Phone** field.
18. Enter the email address of the medical staff member in the **Medical Staff Email** field.
19. Enter the medical facility where the medical staff member works in the **Medical Staff Facility** field.
20. Enter any additional notes from the medical staff member in the **Medical Staff Note** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`Patient Name`, text, required): Enter the patient's name.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, text, optional): Enter the patient's medical history.
* **Consent Type** (`Consent Type`, select_one, required): Select the type of consent from the dropdown menu.
* **Medical Procedure** (`Medical Procedure`, text, required): Enter the medical procedure that will be performed.
* **Informed Consent** (`Informed Consent`, text, required): Enter a detailed explanation of the informed consent.
* **Signature** (`Signature`, note, required): Sign and date the form.
* **Medical Facility** (`Medical Facility`, text, optional): Enter the medical facility where the procedure will be performed.
* **Medical Procedure Date** (`Medical Procedure Date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the medical procedure.
* **Medical Procedure Time** (`Medical Procedure Time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the medical procedure.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Address** (`Address`, text, optional): Enter the patient's address.
* **City** (`City`, text, optional): Enter the patient's city.
* **State** (`State`, text, optional): Enter the patient's state.
* **Zip** (`Zip`, text, optional): Enter the patient's zip code.
* **Country** (`Country`, text, optional): Enter the patient's country.
* **Medical Staff Name** (`Medical Staff Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the medical staff member who is providing the consent.
* **Medical Staff ID** (`Medical Staff ID`, text, optional): Enter the ID number of the medical staff member.
* **Medical Staff Title** (`Medical Staff Title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the medical staff member.
* **Medical Staff Department** (`Medical Staff Department`, text, optional): Enter the department of the medical staff member.
* **Medical Staff Phone** (`Medical Staff Phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the medical staff member.
* **Medical Staff Email** (`Medical Staff Email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the medical staff member.
* **Medical Staff Facility** (`Medical Staff Facility`, text, optional): Enter the medical facility where the medical staff member works.
* **Medical Staff Note** (`Medical Staff Note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes from the medical staff member.
