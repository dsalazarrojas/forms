# Team Social Media Posting Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Social Media Posting Consent Form is a mandatory form that requires team members to give their consent before their information is posted on social media. This form ensures that each team member has been informed and has agreed to have their data shared publicly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are a team member with access to your own information.
2. Review the form questions carefully and answer them accurately.
3. If you are unsure about any question, ask your team lead or HR representative for clarification.
4. Fill out your name, email address, and other required fields correctly.
5. Select the correct options for each question, taking into account your team's policies and guidelines.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Has User Given Consent** (`user_consent`, select_one, required):
	+ This question is a critical part of the form, and you must select one of the two options: "Consent Given" or "Consent Not Given".
	+ If you select "Consent Given", your information will be posted on social media.
	+ If you select "Consent Not Given", your information will not be posted.
* **Name** (name, text, optional):
	+ Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents or ID cards.
	+ If you are not sure about your name, please contact your team lead or HR representative for guidance.
* **Organization Name** (organization, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select the name of your current or previous organization(s) from the list.
	+ This information helps the team to understand your background and expertise.
* **Job Title** (job_title, text, optional):
	+ Enter your current or previous job title(s) as it appears on your official documents or ID cards.
	+ This information helps the team to understand your role and responsibilities.
* **Email Address** (email, email, required):
	+ Enter your official email address as it appears on your official documents or ID cards.
	+ This is the address that will be used for social media posting.
* **Phone** (phone, text, optional):
	+ Enter your contact phone number as it appears on your official documents or ID cards.
	+ This is not a required field, but it may be helpful for team communication.
* **Job Title (Duplicate)** (job_title, text, optional):
	+ This field is a duplicate of the previous "Job Title" field and serves the same purpose.
* **Team Lead Name** (team_lead_name, text, optional):
	+ Enter your team lead's name as it appears on your official documents or ID cards.
	+ This is not a required field, but it may be helpful for team communication.
