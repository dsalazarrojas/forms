# Photoshoot Client Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photoshoot Client Agreement Form is designed to capture essential information from clients before a photoshoot session. This information will help ensure that both you and your client are on the same page regarding the photoshoot.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Session Details** section with the date and any other relevant details about the photoshoot.
2. Enter your **Photographer's Name** in the corresponding field.
3. Fill in the **Client's Name** field with your name or the name of the person responsible for the client.
4. Enter your **Contact Information** in the **Contact Info** field, including your name, phone number, and email address.
5. For **Photo Deliverables**, select the options that apply to your needs:
	* Photos from the session
	* Raw images
	* Retouched images
	* All of the above
6. If you need any specific **Photo Retouching** level, select the corresponding option:
	* None
	* Basic
	* Standard
	* Advanced
7. Choose the method of **Photo Delivery** that suits you best:
	* Email
	* CD/DVD
	* USB
	* Cloud
8. Fill in any additional details about your photo delivery preferences in the **Photo Delivery Details** field.
9. Finally, fill in the **Session Date** field with the actual date of the photoshoot.
