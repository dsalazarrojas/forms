# Veterinary Study Publication Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to obtain informed consent from participants in a veterinary study. It ensures that participants understand the study's procedures, risks, and benefits before agreeing to participate.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and carefully.
2. Enter a brief description of the veterinary study in the "Veterinary Study" field.
3. Provide your consent date and time in the "Consent Date" field.
4. Select the time of day for the consent in the "Consent Time" field.
5. Sign your name in the "Signature" field.
6. Answer the series of statements in the "I Understand..." section, confirming your understanding of the study's terms and conditions.
7. Choose your response to the final statement in the "I Agree/Decline" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Study Publication Consent** (`study_publication_consent`, required, note): This field is for the participant to enter a brief description of the study.
* **Veterinary Study** (`veterinary_study`, required, text): Please enter a brief description of the study.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, required, date): Provide the date you are giving your consent.
* **Consent Time** (`consent_time`, required, time): Select the time of day for your consent.
* **Signature** (`signature`, required, text): Please sign below to indicate that you have read and agree to the consent form.
* **I Understand...** (`study_publication_consent_1`, required, text): I understand that by participating in this study, I will be providing sensitive information about my pet(s).
* **I Am Willing...** (`study_publication_consent_2`, required, text): I am willing to participate in the study and provide the required information about my pet(s).
* **I Am Aware...** (`study_publication_consent_3`, required, text): I am aware of the potential risks and benefits associated with participating in this study.
* **I Am Willing to Give...** (`study_publication_consent_4`, required, text): I am willing to give my pet's information to the researchers.
* **I Agree/Decline** (`study_publication_consent_5`, required, select one): I agree/decline to the terms and conditions of the study.
