# Covid Questionnaire For Customers - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your travel history, contact with infected individuals, symptoms, and other relevant details related to COVID-19. This information will help us assess your risk of exposure and inform decisions regarding store policies and procedures to minimize that risk.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
- Fill in all required fields.
- If a field is not applicable, mark "No" or select "False" where relevant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (Customer Name, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your ID or driver's license.
* **Have you traveled internationally?** (Have you traveled, select_one, required): Select "True" if you have traveled to another country in the past 14 days.
* **Have you traveled to which countries?** (Have you traveled countries, select_multiple, optional): If you selected "True" above, list the countries you have visited.
* **Have you traveled recently?** (Have you traveled recently, date, required): If you selected "True" above, enter the date of your most recent international travel.
* **How often do you travel?** (Have you traveled frequency, time, optional): If you selected "True" above, enter how often you travel.
* **Have you had contact with an infected individual?** (Contact with infected, select_one, required): Select "True" if you have had contact with someone who has tested positive for COVID-19.
* **Contact with infected details** (Contact with infected details, text, optional): If you selected "True" above, describe your contact with the individual.
* **Symptoms** (Symptoms, select_multiple, required): Select any of the listed symptoms you are currently experiencing.
* **Exposure date** (Exposure date, date, optional): If you selected "True" for contact with infected, enter the date of exposure.
* **Exposure start date** (Exposure start date, date, optional): If you selected "True" for contact with infected, enter the start date of exposure.
* **Exposure end date** (Exposure end date, date, optional): If you selected "True" for contact with infected, enter the end date of exposure.
* **Have you visited a hospital?** (Have you visited hospital, select_one, required): Select "True" if you have visited a hospital in the past 14 days.
* **Have you visited other countries?** (Have you visited countries, select_multiple, optional): If you selected "True" above, list the countries you have visited.
* **Travel history countries** (Travel history countries, select_multiple, optional): If you have visited other countries, list them here.
* **Customer is mildly symptomatic?** (Customer is mildly symptomatic, select_one, optional): Select "True" if you are experiencing symptoms.
* **Customer is symptomatic?** (Customer is symptomatic, select_one, optional): Select "True" if you are experiencing severe symptoms.
* **Customer is severely symptomatic?** (Customer is severely symptomatic, select_one, optional): Select "True" if you are experiencing severe symptoms.
* **Store staff wear masks?** (Store staff wear masks, select_one, optional): Select "True" if store staff wear masks.
* **Store staff wear gloves?** (Store staff wear gloves, select_one, optional): Select "True" if store staff wear gloves.
* **Store staff wash hands?** (Store staff wash hands, select_one, optional): Select "True" if store staff wash hands before interacting with customers.
* **Customer wear masks?** (Customer wear masks, select_one, optional): Select "True" if you wear a mask when interacting with store staff.
* **Customer wear gloves?** (Customer wear gloves, select_one, optional): Select "True" if you wear gloves when interacting with store staff.
* **Customer wash hands?** (Customer wash hands, select_one, optional): Select "True" if you wash your hands before interacting with store staff.
* **Store temperature check?** (Store temperature check, select_one, optional): Select "True" if the store checks your temperature before allowing entry.
* **Store distance check?** (Store distance check, select_one, optional): Select "True" if the store checks for social distancing before allowing entry.
