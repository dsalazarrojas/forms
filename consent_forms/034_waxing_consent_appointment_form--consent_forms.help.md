# Waxing Consent & Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Waxing Consent & Appointment Form is a document that requires the client's consent and schedule an appointment for waxing services. The form helps us gather essential information about the client's preferences and schedule the appointment in a respectful and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your client's name in the Client Name field.
2. Provide your client's email address in the Client Email field.
3. Enter your client's phone number in the Client Phone field.
4. Select your preferred date in the Preferred Date field.
5. Select your preferred time in the Preferred Time field.
6. Indicate whether your client has given consent to the waxing services in the Consent Given field.
7. Choose the waxing services your client is interested in, such as Eyebrow Waxing, Chin/Upper Lip, or Lip/Chin.
8. Enter the date and time of your scheduled appointment in the Appointment Date and Appointment Time fields, respectively.
9. Provide any additional notes about your client's appointment in the Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter your client's full name.
* **Client Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your client's email address for further communication.
* **Client Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your client's phone number for easier contact.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, date, optional): Select the date you prefer to schedule the appointment.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, time, optional): Select the time you prefer to schedule the appointment.
* **Consent Given** (`consent_given`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether your client has given consent to the waxing services (True or False).
* **Service** (`service_brow_waxing`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the waxing services your client is interested in (Eyebrow Waxing, Chin/Upper Lip, or other options).
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the scheduled appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the scheduled appointment.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional notes about your client's appointment.
