# Community Event Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for participants to provide consent for attending a community event. It gathers important personal and emergency contact information for the event organizers. It also requires a waiver of liability in case of an incident.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the form carefully before filling it out.
- Ensure you have all necessary information and permissions required.
- Fill out the form with accurate and honest information.
- Sign and date the form when prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First Name** (`participant_first_name`, required): Enter your legal first name.
- **Last Name** (`participant_last_name`, required): Enter your legal last name.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, required): Enter your birthdate in MM/DD/YYYY format.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, required): Enter your primary contact email address.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, required): Enter your primary contact phone number.
- **Street Address** (`address`, required): Enter your home address.
- **City, State, ZIP** (`city_state_zip`, required): Enter your city, state, and ZIP code.
- **Event Name** (`event_name`, required): Choose the name of the event you are attending.
- **Event Date** (`event_date`, required): Choose the date of the event.
- **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, required): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
- **Medical Conditions or Allergies** (`medical_conditions`, optional): If you have any medical conditions or allergies, list them here.
- **Current Medications** (`medications`, optional): If you take any medications, list them here.
- **Acknowledgment of Activity Risks** (`activity_risks`, required): Acknowledge that you understand the risks involved in the event.
- **Consent for Medical Treatment** (`medical_treatment_consent`, required): Provide consent for emergency medical treatment.
- **Photo and Video Consent** (`photo_video_consent`, required): Choose whether you give consent for photos and videos to be taken of you.
- **Liability Waiver** (`liability_waiver`, required): Waive liability claims against the event organizers.
- **Transportation Consent** (`transportation_consent`, required): Understand that transportation is your responsibility.
- **Agreement to Follow Rules** (`rules_agreement`, required): Agree to follow the event rules.
- **Parent or Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, optional): If you are a minor, enter your parent or guardian's name.
- **Parent or Guardian Signature** (`guardian_signature`, optional): If you are a minor, have your parent or guardian sign this field.
- **Date of Signature** (`signature_date`, required): Enter the current date.
