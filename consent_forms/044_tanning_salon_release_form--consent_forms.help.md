# Tanning Salon Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Tanning Salon Release Form is used to collect your information for data collection and record-keeping purposes for a third-party tanning salon.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your **First name** (`form_1`) - enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. Fill in your **Last name** (`form_2`) - enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
3. Enter your **Email address** (`form_3`) - enter your email address for communication purposes.
4. Enter your **Phone number** (`form_4`) - enter your phone number for communication purposes.
5. Select your **Date of visit** (`form_5`) - select the date you plan to visit the tanning salon.
6. Select your **Time of visit** (`form_6`) - select the time you plan to visit the tanning salon.
7. Write any **notes/agreement** (`form_7`) - provide any additional comments or agreements you'd like to share with the tanning salon.
8. Sign with your **Signature** (`form_8`) - provide your signature for consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name (form_1)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last name (form_2)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email address (form_3)**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone number (form_4)**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Date of visit (form_5)**: Select the date you plan to visit the tanning salon.
* **Time of visit (form_6)**: Select the time you plan to visit the tanning salon.
* **Notes/agreement (form_7)**: Provide any additional comments or agreements you'd like to share with the tanning salon.
* **Signature (form_8)**: Provide your signature for consent.

## Tips

* Please make sure to provide accurate and complete information for each field.
