# Night_Driving_Waiver_Request_Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Night Driving Waiver Request Form is a form used to request a waiver for night driving, which is typically used for individuals who are required to drive at night but do not have a valid driver's license or are experiencing difficulties driving in low light conditions. This form is intended for those who are unable to obtain a regular driver's license due to medical reasons, lack of licensure, or other extenuating circumstances.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the relevant option for "Night Driving Waiver Request" to choose the reason you are requesting the waiver.
2.  Enter your name and contact information in the "Name" and "Contact Number" fields.
3.  Provide your date of birth.
4.  Enter your email address.
5.  Check the box "Waiver" if you are requesting a waiver.
6.  Optionally provide any additional comments.
7.  Finally, press the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Night Driving Waiver Request** (`night_driving_waiver_request`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select one or more options that apply to your request for a night driving waiver. Please choose the options that best describe why you are requesting a waiver.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name in this field.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Waiver** (`waiver`, select_one, required/optional): Check this box to indicate that you are requesting a waiver.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or information that may help us process your request.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required/optional): Once you have completed all the fields, press this button to submit your request.
