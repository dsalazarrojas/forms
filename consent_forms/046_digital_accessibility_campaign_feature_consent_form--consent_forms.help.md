# Digital Accessibility Campaign Feature Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a critical component of the Digital Accessibility Campaign, ensuring that participants are aware of and agree to participate in the campaign.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your participant details, including your email and phone number.
2.  Decide whether you want to be featured in the campaign or not.
3.  Review and agree to the consent statement.
4.  Specify your campaign contribution.
5.  Choose whether you want to be featured in the campaign or not.
6.  Enter a valid email address.
7.  Provide your phone number.
8.  Enter your date of birth.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant details** (`participant_details`, text, required): Please enter your name and any other details you want to share about yourself.
* **Be featured** (`featured`, select_one, required): Check 'Yes' if you're willing to have your contribution featured in the campaign.
* **Consent statement** (`consent_statement`, text, required): Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the campaign.
* **Campaign contribution** (`campaign_contribution`, text, required): Describe your campaign contribution, including any relevant details about the work or content you're submitting.
* **Be featured in campaign** (`featured_in_campaign`, select_one, required): Check 'Yes' if you're willing to have your contribution featured in the campaign.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address to receive updates and communications about the campaign.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number to be contacted for campaign-related activities.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth for age-related campaign requirements.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you enter a valid email address in the correct format.
* Review the consent statement carefully and ask questions if you're unsure about any part of it.
* If you're unsure about any aspect of the form, please ask the campaign administrators for clarification.
