# Data Privacy Policy Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Data Privacy Policy Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from users about the company's data privacy practices. Your input will help us identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions to better protect your data and ensure transparency in our operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields (First Name, Last Name, Email, question1, question2, question3, question4, question5).
2. Select one of the options for questions 1, 2, 3, and 4.
3. Select only one option for question 1.
4. Select multiple options for questions 2, 3, and 4.
5. If you have any additional comments or thoughts, use the Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter a valid email address where you can be reached for follow-up questions or communications.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): If you are providing feedback on behalf of a company or organization, please enter the name of the company.
* **What do you think about our data privacy practices?** (`question1`, select_one, required): Select one of the options to express your honest opinion about our data privacy practices.
* **How would you rate our data collection methods?** (`question2`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply to express your opinion about the effectiveness of our data collection methods.
* **How would you rate our data use practices?** (`question3`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply to express your opinion about the effectiveness of our data use practices.
* **How would you rate our data security practices?** (`question4`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply to express your opinion about the effectiveness of our data security practices.
* **Have you contacted us about our data privacy practices before?** (`question5`, select_one, required): Select 'True' if you have contacted us in the past, 'False' otherwise.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): This field is optional, but if you have any additional thoughts or comments, you can share them here.
