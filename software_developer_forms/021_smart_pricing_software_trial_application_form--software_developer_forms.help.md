# Smart Pricing Software Trial Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help software developers apply for a trial of our smart pricing software. We will use the information provided to assess your suitability for the trial and to provide you with further instructions and information about the trial process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the following information in the fields below. Make sure to fill out all the required fields (if any) to ensure that we receive your application successfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Details (`1`)
* **User Details**: This is where you can input your personal details, including your name, email, and any other relevant contact information. Please make sure to provide your correct contact information so that we can get in touch with you.

### Business Details (`2`)
* **Business Details**: This is where you can input your company's name, address, and any other relevant business information. Please make sure to provide accurate information so that we can assess your business's suitability for the trial.

### Contact Details (`3`)
* **Contact Details**: This is where you can input the contact person's name and contact information for your business. Please make sure to provide the contact person's name and their relevant contact information.

### Company Details (`4`)
* **Company Details**: This is where you can input your company's website, industry, and any other relevant company information. Please make sure to provide accurate information so that we can assess your company's suitability for the trial.

### Trial Preferences (`5`)
* **Trial Preferences**: This is where you can specify your preferences for the trial, such as the duration and any specific features you'd like to try. Please make sure to be specific about your preferences so that we can tailor the trial to your needs.

### Submission (`6`)
* **Submission**: This is where you can input your submission, which will be reviewed by our team to determine your suitability for the trial. Please make sure to provide a clear and concise submission that includes all the necessary information.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields (if any) to ensure that we receive your application successfully.
* Please be specific and accurate when inputting your information to avoid any misunderstandings.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to contact us.
