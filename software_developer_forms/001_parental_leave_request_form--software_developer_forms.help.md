## Parental Leave Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information for a parental leave request. It is used to notify the management and HR department of an employee's intention to take parental leave. The form is a necessary step in the process of requesting parental leave, which is a type of leave that allows employees to take care of their children.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
3. Select the start date of your parental leave in the "Start Date" field.
4. Select the end date of your parental leave in the "End Date" field.
5. Write a brief explanation for your request in the "Reason for Request" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID badge or your official name used in the company records.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title as it is currently listed in the company's directory or HR system.
* **Start Date**: Select the date you would like your parental leave to start. This is the date you will be leaving work to begin your parental leave.
* **End Date**: Select the date you would like your parental leave to end. This is the date you will return to work after your parental leave.
* **Reason for Request**: Write a brief explanation for your request. This could include the reason for your request, any necessary details about your leave, or any other relevant information.
