# Cloud Based Inventory Pilot Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from companies that are interested in participating in a cloud-based inventory pilot application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have all the necessary information required.
2. Complete each section accurately and thoroughly, as it will help the project team better understand your company's needs and requirements.
3. Ensure you have the necessary permission or authority to provide the information required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (2): Please enter your company's name.
* **Company Size - Number of Employees** (3): Please enter the approximate number of employees in your company.
* **Industry - Sector** (4): Please select the industry sector your company belongs to.
* **Company Website** (5): This is an optional field for your company's website.
* **Full Name** (7): Please enter your full name as the primary contact person for this application.
* **Job Title** (8): Please enter your job title.
* **Email Address** (9): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (10): Please enter your phone number.
* **What will be tracked in inventory?** (12): Please describe the type of items or products you want to track in inventory.
* **Approximate number of items to track** (13): Please enter the approximate number of items you want to track.
* **Number of locations to track** (14): Please enter the number of locations you want to track.
* **Required update frequency** (15): Please select the frequency at which you want to update the inventory data.
* **Current inventory management system** (17): Please describe your current inventory management system (e.g., software, manual method).
* **Pain points with current system** (18): Please describe the pain points or areas where your current system is not meeting your needs.
* **Primary goals for pilot program** (20): Please select the primary goals for the pilot program (e.g., cost reduction, improved accuracy).
* **Expected benefits from cloud solution** (21): Please describe the expected benefits from implementing a cloud-based inventory management solution.
* **Desired pilot start date** (23): Please enter the desired start date for the pilot program.
* **Proposed pilot duration - Months** (24): Please enter the proposed duration of the pilot program.
* **Organization readiness for change** (25): Please select how ready your organization is for change and implementation.

By following these instructions, you will be able to complete the form accurately and provide the necessary information for the project team to understand your company's needs and requirements for the pilot program.
