# Developer End Of Day Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help developers record their daily work activities, including the projects they are working on, the tasks completed, and any important notes or comments they need to share. The goal is to provide a quick and easy way for developers to keep track of their daily progress and communicate with their team or manager.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the projects you have been working on during the day.
2. Select the relevant projects from the "Project" field.
3. Select the tasks you completed during the day from the "Task" field.
4. Enter the start and end times of your workday in the "Start Time" and "End Time" fields, respectively.
5. Optionally, add any notes or comments in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Daily Update** (`daily_update`, text, optional): A field where you can provide a brief summary of your daily activities.
* **Project** (`project_id`, select_multiple, optional): Select the projects you have been working on during the day. This field allows you to keep track of the projects you are currently working on.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the day you are checking in. This field is required for reporting purposes.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of your workday. This field is optional, but helps to keep track of your work hours.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of your workday. This field is optional, but helps to keep track of your work hours.
* **Task** (`task_id`, select_multiple, optional): Select the tasks you completed during the day. This field allows you to keep track of the tasks you have finished.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): A field where you can add any notes or comments about your workday. This field is optional, but can be useful for reference or follow-up discussions.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address. This field is optional, but can be useful for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number. This field is optional, but can be useful for urgent matters.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): A field where you can add any additional comments or feedback. This field is optional, but can be useful for future reference.
