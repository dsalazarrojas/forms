# user_story_item_entry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to update existing stories. It allows users to enter details such as the story ID, description, priority, category, status, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Story ID** of the story to be updated.
2. Provide a brief **Description** of the story.
3. Select the **Priority** level of the story, if applicable.
4. Choose the **Category** of the story.
5. Select the **Status** of the story.
6. (Optional) Enter the **Created At** date and time of the story.
7. (Optional) Determine if a tool is **Assigned Tool**.
8. (Optional) Enter the **Submitted By** name of the person submitting the story.
9. (Optional) Enter the **Created By** name of the person creating the story.
10. (Optional) Add any **Comments** to the story.
11. (Optional) Add any **Notes** to the story.
12. (Optional) Enter **Feedback** or suggestions for the story.
13. (Optional) Enter the **Submitted By Email** of the person submitting the story.
14. (Optional) Enter the **Submitted At** date and time of the story.
15. (Optional) Enter the **Updated At** date and time of the story.
