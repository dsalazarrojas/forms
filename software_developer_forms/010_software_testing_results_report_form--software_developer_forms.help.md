# software_testing_results_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The software testing results report form is used to report software testing results. This helps our testing team improve our software products.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the project name in the **project_name** field.
2. Choose the test environment from the dropdown menu in the **test_environment** field.
3. Enter your test results in the **test_results** field. This can be any relevant information about your testing experience.
4. Add any additional notes in the **notes** field.
5. Enter your email address in the **email** field.
6. Enter your phone number in the **phone** field.
7. Select the person who should be notified about your submission in the **assigned_to** field.
8. Choose the date and time when you submitted the form in the **submitted_at** field.
9. Choose the date and time when the form was last updated in the **updated_at** field.
10. Choose the date and time when the form was created in the **created_at** field.
11. Select all relevant team members who should be notified about your submission in the **submitted_by** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **project_name** (text, required: false): Enter the name of the project you were testing.
* **test_environment** (select_one, required: false): Choose the environment where you conducted the tests (e.g., development or production).
* **release_number** (number, required: false): Enter the release number of the software you were testing.
* **test_results** (text, required: false): Enter any relevant information about your testing experience.
* **notes** (note, required: false): Add any additional notes about your testing experience.
* **email** (email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **assigned_to** (select_one, required: false): Select the person who should be notified about your submission.
* **submitted_at** (date, required: false): Choose the date and time when you submitted the form.
* **updated_at** (date, required: false): Choose the date and time when the form was last updated.
* **created_at** (date, required: false): Choose the date and time when the form was created.
* **submitted_by** (select_multiple, required: false): Select all relevant team members who should be notified about your submission.
