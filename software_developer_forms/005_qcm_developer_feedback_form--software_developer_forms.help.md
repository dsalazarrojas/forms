# QCM Developer Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The QCM Developer Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from software developers about their experience with the QCM system. Your feedback will help us understand what we do well and what we can improve to better serve you and other developers in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and email address in the first two fields.
2. In the "Feedback" field, describe your experience with the QCM system, including what you liked and disliked about it.
3. Rate your experience by selecting a number from 1 to 10 in the "Rating" fields.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, you can provide them in the "Comments" field.
5. Finally, select "Yes" or "No" in the "Submit" field to indicate whether you are willing to share your feedback with the development team.
