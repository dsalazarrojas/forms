# Eye Tracking Interaction User Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Eye Tracking Interaction User Feedback Form is a tool used to collect feedback from users after interacting with our product or service that utilizes eye tracking technology. This form is designed to gather specific information about the user's experience, which will help us identify areas for improvement and optimize the user interface.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and make sure you understand what each field is asking for.
2. Answer each field accurately and honestly, as this will help us improve the product or service.
3. Fill out all required fields (if any).
4. Review your submission before submitting it to ensure everything is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_feedback**: This field is used to collect your feedback on the interaction session. Describe your thoughts, feelings, and any challenges you encountered during the session.
* **feedback_rating**: Provide a rating from 1 to 5 to express your satisfaction level with the interaction session. A higher rating indicates a more positive experience.
* **user_interaction_time**: Enter the total time spent interacting with the product or service during the session.
* **user_interaction_day**: Enter the day of the interaction session.
* **user_interaction_time_of_day**: Enter the time of day when the interaction session took place.
* **user_interaction_date**: Enter the date of the interaction session.
* **eye_tracking_type**: Describe the type of eye tracking technology used during the session.
* **interaction_details**: Provide any additional details about the interaction session, such as specific features or functionalities you used.
* **eye_tracking_device**: Describe the device used for eye tracking during the session.
* **interaction_device**: Describe the device used for interaction during the session.
* **user_device**: Enter the type of device used for the interaction session (e.g., smartphone, tablet, desktop).
* **browser**: Select the browser used for the interaction session.
* **system**: Select the operating system used for the interaction session.
* **resolution**: Select the screen resolution used for the interaction session.
* **screen_size**: Enter the screen size used for the interaction session.
* **operating_system**: Enter the operating system used for the interaction session.
* **interaction_speed**: Describe the speed of interaction during the session.
