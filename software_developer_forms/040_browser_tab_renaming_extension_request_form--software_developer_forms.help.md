# Browser Tab Renaming Extension Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to request the addition of a browser tab renaming extension feature to our platform. Please fill out this form to provide detailed information about the feature you are requesting, so we can better understand your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To ensure that we receive the information we need to process your request, please complete this form carefully and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester name** (`1`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your company or personal documentation.
* **Contact email** (`2`, `email`, required): Please provide an email address where we can contact you for status updates and follow-up questions.
* **Browser platform** (`3`, `select_one`, required): Choose the browser platform you are requesting the feature for. This will help us tailor the feature to your specific needs.
* **Feature description** (`4`, `text`, required): Describe the desired tab renaming behavior in detail. This will help us understand how the feature should work and what benefits it would bring.
* **Use cases** (`5`, `text`, optional): Provide typical scenarios where this feature would be useful. This will help us understand how the feature would be used in real-world situations.
* **Priority** (`6`, `select_one`, optional): If you have a pressing need for this feature, please select the highest priority level that applies to you. This will help us prioritize our development tasks.
* **Attachment links** (`7`, `text`, optional): If you have screenshots, designs, or prototypes that showcase your desired feature, please provide links to them here. This will help us better understand your vision.
* **Follow up preference** (`8`, `select_one`, optional): Please choose how you would prefer to be followed up on the status of your request. This will ensure that we keep you informed about our progress.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when describing your desired feature.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out.
* We will review your request and get back to you as soon as possible.
* Thank you for taking the time to submit your request!
