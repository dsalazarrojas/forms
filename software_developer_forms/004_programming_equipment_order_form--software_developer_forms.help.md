# Programming Equipment Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Programming Equipment Order Form is used to document and track the ordering of programming equipment and resources for software development projects.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with all required fields.
2. Provide the name of the customer placing the order.
3. Enter the contact person's name and phone number.
4. Choose the type of equipment ordered (Laptop, Software, or Hardware).
5. Select the programming language and operating system used.
6. Specify the equipment status (New, Refurbished, or Used).
7. Enter the total cost of the ordered equipment.
8. Assign a tool or resource (if necessary).
9. Add any comments or notes about the order.
10. Submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order form** (`order_form`): A text field to enter any additional comments or notes about the order.
* **Customer** (`customer`): The name of the customer placing the order.
* **Contact person** (`contact_person`): The name of the contact person for the customer.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`): The phone number of the contact person.
* **Email** (`email`): The email address of the contact person (optional).
* **Equipment ordered** (`equipment_ordered`): Select one or more types of equipment ordered (Laptop, Software, or Hardware).
* **Quantity ordered** (`quantity_ordered`): The quantity of each equipment type ordered.
* **Date ordered** (`date_ordered`): Enter the date the order was placed.
* **Equipment requested** (`equipment_requested`): Select one type of equipment requested (Laptop, Software, or Hardware).
* **Programming language** (`programming_language`): Select the programming language used.
* **Operating system** (`operating_system`): Select the operating system used.
* **Equipment status** (`equipment_status`): Select the status of the ordered equipment (New, Refurbished, or Used).
* **Total cost** (`total_cost`): Enter the total cost of the ordered equipment.
* **Assigned tool** (`assigned_tool`): Enter the tool or resource assigned to the order.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Add any additional comments or notes about the order.
* **Form id** (`form_id`): A unique identifier for the form (optional).
* **Submitted at** (`submitted_at`): The date and time the form was submitted.
* **Updated at** (`updated_at`): The date and time the form was last updated.
* **User** (`user`): The name of the user who submitted the form (optional).
* **Created at** (`created_at`): The date and time the form was created.
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`): The name of the person or team assigned to the order (optional).
* **Updated by** (`updated_by`): The name of the person or team that last updated the form (optional).
* **Equipment ordered status** (`equipment_ordered_status`): Select the status of the ordered equipment (Ordered, In progress, or Shipped).
* **Form version** (`form_version`): The version of the form used (optional).

Please note that this form is for internal use only and is subject to change as the company's needs evolve.
