# Computer Games Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather information about computer game players' preferences, habits, and experiences. It's designed to help us understand your gaming habits, interests, and opinions to improve our services. By completing this survey, you'll help us tailor our games to better meet your needs and preferences.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **Survey ID**, a unique identifier to help us track your responses.
2. Provide the **Survey Date**, the date you're completing this form.
3. If you'd like, enter your **Respondent Name** or gamertag for identification purposes.
4. Enter your **Email Address** for any follow-up communication from us.
5. Select your **Age Range** from the options provided.
6. Choose your **Gender** from the options provided.
7. Indicate your **Primary Gaming Platform** from the available options.
8. Select how often you **Gaming Frequency**.
9. Choose the **Average Session Length** of your typical gaming sessions.
10. Select your preferred **Game Genres** from the options provided.
11. If you'd like, enter your **Favorite Game** name.
12. Rate your **Overall Game Satisfaction** from the options provided.
13. Rate the **Graphics Quality** of games you've played.
14. Rate the **Gameplay Mechanics** of games you've played.
15. Rate the **Story and Narrative** of games you've played (if applicable).
16. Rate the **Sound and Music** of games you've played.
17. Choose your preferred **Difficulty Level** for games.
18. Indicate your preference for **Multiplayer Games**.
19. Select how much you spend on **Monthly Gaming**.
20. Select the factors that influence your **Purchases** of games.
21. If applicable, suggest **New Features** you'd like to see in games.
22. Report any **Bugs Encountered** during gameplay.
23. Rate your **Likelihood to Recommend** our games to others.
24. Provide any **Additional Feedback** or suggestions for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Survey ID** (`survey_id`, text, required): A unique identifier for your survey responses.
- **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, required): The date you're completing this form.
- **Respondent Name** (`respondent_name`, text, optional): Your name or gamertag for identification.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Your email for follow-up communication.
- **Age Range** (`age_range`, select_one, required): Select your age group.
- **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, optional): Choose your gender.
- **Primary Gaming Platform** (`gaming_platform`, select_one, required): Select your primary gaming platform.
- **Gaming Frequency** (`gaming_frequency`, select_one, required): Indicate how often you game.
- **Average Session Length** (`average_session_length`, select_one, required): Choose your typical session length.
- **Preferred Game Genres** (`preferred_game_genres`, select_multiple, required): Select your favorite game genres.
- **Favorite Game** (`favorite_game`, text, optional): Enter your favorite game name.
- **Overall Game Satisfaction** (`game_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction with current games.
- **Graphics Quality Rating** (`graphics_quality_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the graphics quality of games.
- **Gameplay Mechanics Rating** (`gameplay_mechanics_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the gameplay mechanics of games.
- **Story and Narrative Rating** (`story_narrative_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate the story and narrative (if applicable).
- **Sound and Music Rating** (`sound_music_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the sound and music of games.
- **Difficulty Level** (`difficulty_level`, select_one, required): Select your preferred difficulty level.
- **Multiplayer Preference** (`multiplayer_preference`, select_one, required): Indicate your preference for multiplayer games.
- **Monthly Gaming Spending** (`spending_habits`, select_one, required): Select how much you spend on gaming monthly.
- **Factors Influencing Purchases** (`purchase_factors`, select_multiple, optional): Select factors that influence your game purchases.
- **New Features Desired** (`new_features_desired`, text, optional): Suggest new features you'd like to see in games.
- **Bugs Encountered** (`bugs_encountered`, select_one, required): Report any bugs encountered during gameplay.
- **Likelihood to Recommend** (`likelihood_to_recommend`, select_one, required): Rate your likelihood to recommend our games.
- **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, text, optional): Provide any additional feedback or suggestions.

Tips:
* Please answer honestly and to the best of your abilities.
* If you're unsure or don't know an answer, choose the most likely option.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, use the **Additional Feedback** field.
