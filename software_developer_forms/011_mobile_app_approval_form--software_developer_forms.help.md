# Mobile App Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mobile App Approval Form is designed to collect information about mobile apps for approval purposes. It helps the approval team quickly gather the necessary details to assess and review the apps.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the App Name, which is the title of the mobile app.
2. Enter the Developer Name and their contact information (Email and Phone).
3. Select the Category of the app (e.g., Apple or Google).
4. Fill in the Version of the app.
5. Add a Description of the app.
6. Enter the Upload Date and Release Date of the app (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **App Name** (`app_name`, text, optional): The title of the mobile app.
* **Developer Name** (`developer_name`, text, optional): The name and contact information of the developer.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): The email address of the developer.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): The phone number of the developer.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): The category of the mobile app (e.g., Apple or Google).
* **Version** (`version`, text, optional): The version of the mobile app.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): A description of the mobile app.
* **Upload Date** (`upload_date`, date, optional): The date the app was uploaded.
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, date, optional): The release date of the mobile app.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Double-check the accuracy of the information provided.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it.
