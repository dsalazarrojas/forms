# Recommendation System Metrics Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track metrics for a recommendation system. It helps users to monitor and analyze the system's performance, providing valuable insights to improve its effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for the form in the `form_title` field. This is an optional field, but it's a good idea to provide a brief description of the project or the specific metrics being tracked.
2. Enter the `user_id` field with your unique identifier (e.g., your username or ID number).
3. Select the `project_id` field with the relevant project ID from the dropdown list.
4. Choose the relevant metrics from the `metric_1` field (a select multiple field).
5. Enter the date of completion in the `date` field.
6. Enter the time of completion in the `time` field.
7. Add any additional comments or information in the `note` field.
8. If applicable, enter your email address in the `email` field.
9. Enter your phone number in the `phone` field.
10. Finally, enter a unique identifier for this form in the `form_id` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form_title** (`text`, optional): A brief title for this form, describing the project or the specific metrics being tracked.
* **user_id** (`number`, optional): A unique identifier for you as a user, such as your username or ID number.
* **project_id** (`select_one`, optional): Select the relevant project ID from the dropdown list.
* **metric_1** (`select_multiple`, optional): Choose the relevant metrics from the dropdown list.
* **date** (`date`, optional): The date of completion.
* **time** (`time`, optional): The time of completion.
* **note** (`note`, optional): Additional comments or information about the project.
* **email** (`email`, optional): If applicable, enter your email address.
* **phone** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **form_id** (`text`, optional): A unique identifier for this form.

## Tips

* Make sure to select all relevant metrics for the project in the `metric_1` field.
* If you're having trouble selecting a project from the `project_id` field, double-check your project list.
* You can add any additional comments or information in the `note` field.
* This form is for tracking progress and metrics, so be as accurate as possible.
