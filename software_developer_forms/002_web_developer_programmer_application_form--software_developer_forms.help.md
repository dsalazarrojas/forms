# Web Developer Programmer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Web Developer Programmer Application Form is designed for web developers to apply for opportunities, recognition, or funding. This form helps the application process by gathering necessary information about the applicant's basic information, programming skills, project experience, social media presence, application details, and website information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your **Basic Information** (page 1):
	* Enter your name and contact details in the provided fields.
2.  Describe your **Programming Skills** (page 2):
	* List your relevant programming languages, tools, and frameworks you're familiar with.
3.  Provide **Project Information** (page 3):
	* Share details about your previous projects, including the technologies used, and your role in the project.
4.  Share your **Social Media** (page 4):
	* List your professional social media profiles (e.g., LinkedIn, Twitter, GitHub).
5.  Include **Application Information** (page 5):
	* Explain why you're applying for this opportunity and what you hope to achieve.
6.  Add **Website Information** (page 6):
	* Provide a link to your personal website or portfolio, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Basic Information**: Enter your name and contact details to allow us to get in touch with you about your application.
* **Programming Skills**: List your experience and proficiency in programming languages, tools, and frameworks to help us understand your technical expertise.
* **Project Information**: Share details about your past projects, including the technologies used and your role in the project.
* **Social Media**: Share your professional online presence to connect with us.
* **Application Information**: Explain why you're applying for this opportunity and what you hope to achieve.
* **Website Information**: Share a link to your personal website or portfolio to demonstrate your work.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all the required fields to avoid any issues with your application.
- Use clear and concise language when answering the questions.
- Proofread your answers for any errors or typos before submitting your application.
- If you have any supporting documents or attachments, you can upload them separately.
