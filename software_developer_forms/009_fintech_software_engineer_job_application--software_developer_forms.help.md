# Fintech Software Engineer Job Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fintech Software Engineer Job Application form is used to collect information from individuals interested in applying for a Fintech Software Engineer position. The form is designed to assess the qualifications and preferences of applicants in order to match them with suitable job openings.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Name**: Enter your full name as it should be displayed on your resume and in any future correspondence with us.
2. **Email**: Enter your valid email address. This will be used for communication and potential follow-up questions.
3. **Technical Skills**: Please select "Yes" if you possess the necessary technical skills for the position.
4. **Experience**: Indicate your level of experience with "Yes" if you have relevant experience and "No" otherwise.
5. **Education**: Select your highest achieved education level (Bachelor, Master, or Work Experience).
6. **Job Preferences**: Indicate your job preferences by selecting the options that best suit your needs and interests.
7. **Attachments**: If you have any additional information that supports your application, you can attach it here. This can include your resume, portfolio, or any other relevant documentation.
8. **Submission**: Enter a brief description of your submission. Explain why you are applying for this position and what you bring to the table.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (text, required): Enter your full name to help us identify you and contact you regarding your application.
* **Email** (email, optional): A valid email address is required for communication and potential follow-up questions.
* **Technical Skills** (select one, required): Indicate if you possess the necessary technical skills for the position.
* **Experience** (select multiple, optional): Indicate your level of experience with "Yes" if you have relevant experience and "No" otherwise.
* **Education** (select multiple, optional): Select your highest achieved education level.
* **Job Preferences** (select multiple, optional): Indicate your job preferences by selecting the options that best suit your needs and interests.
* **Attachments** (note, optional): Attach any additional information that supports your application, such as your resume or portfolio.
* **Submission** (text, required): Enter a brief description of your submission, explaining why you are applying for this position and what you bring to the table.
