# Blockchain Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to collect information about blockchain technology and its applications from individuals in the industry. The goal is to understand respondents' professional backgrounds, knowledge, and experience with blockchain, as well as their interest in various aspects of blockchain technology.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, please follow the instructions below:

1.  Answer all required fields (marked as `*required*` in the form).
2.  Provide genuine and accurate information for all other fields (marked as `optional`).
3.  Do not leave any field blank unless instructed to do so.
4.  If you have any questions or need help with any field, please contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Survey Introduction
* **-- Blockchain Technology Survey --** (`survey_intro`): This section is an introduction to the survey and its purpose.

### Page 2: Respondent Information
* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`): Please provide your name. (Optional)
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`): If you'd like to receive follow-up questions or surveys, please provide your email address. (Optional)

### Page 4: Interest and Experience
* **Interest in blockchain technology - Not Interested (1) to Very Interested (5)** (`interest_in_blockchain`): Please rate your interest in blockchain technology. (Required)

* **Level of concern about blockchain regulation** (`regulatory_concerns`): Please select your level of concern about blockchain regulation. (Optional)

### Page 7: Expected Timeline and Company Interest
* **When will blockchain have major impact?** (`expected_timeline`): Please select when you expect blockchain to have a major impact. (Required)

* **Survey Completion Date** (`survey_completion_date`): Please provide the date of completing this survey. (Required)
