# Nth Largest Number Calculation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nth Largest Number Calculation Form is a user-friendly tool designed to calculate the Nth largest number from a list of input numbers. It guides users through a simple process of inputting numbers, selecting which numbers to consider as the Nth largest, and displaying the final result.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Input Numbers**: Enter one or multiple numbers in the input field below. You can input numbers separated by spaces or commas.
2. **Select Nth Largest**: Choose which numbers to consider for the calculation. Select 'Yes' for the number(s) you want to include or 'No' for those you do not.
3. **Select Nth Largest**: Once you've selected 'Yes' for the numbers you wish to consider, click on 'Select Nth Largest' to proceed.
4. **Result**: The form will then display the Nth largest number you've selected.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Input Numbers** (`input_numbers`, text, optional): This field is where you input one or multiple numbers for the form to process. You can separate numbers with spaces or commas to help the form understand your input correctly.
* **Select Nth Largest** (`select_nth_largest`, select_multiple, optional): In this field, choose 'Yes' to include a number in the calculation or 'No' to exclude it. Make your selections carefully to ensure you're considering the numbers relevant to your analysis.
* **Result** (`result`, number, optional): This field displays the calculated Nth largest number after your selections. You may not see any output if no numbers are selected or if no numbers are input.
