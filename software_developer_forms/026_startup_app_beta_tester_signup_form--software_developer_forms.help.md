# Startup App Beta Tester Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from potential beta testers about their interest in our startup app. By filling out this form, you will be helping us to better understand our target audience and improve the app for future releases.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your title, name, and email address in the "User Information" section.
3. Provide your contact details in the "Contact Info" section.
4. Describe your interest in the beta testing process in the "Beta Tester Info" section.
5. Review your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (text):
	+ **Title** (`title`, text, required): Enter your title or job title.
	+ **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name as it should be displayed in our database.
* **Contact Info** (text):
	+ **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
	+ **Phone** (phone, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Beta Tester Info** (text):
	+ **Date** (date, date, required): Enter the date you would be available for beta testing.
	+ **Time** (time, time, required): Enter the time you would be available for beta testing.
	+ **Note** (note, note, required): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Company** (text):
	+ **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Role** (text):
	+ **Role** (`role`, text, required): Enter your role in your company.
* **How did you hear about us?** (text):
	+ **How did you hear about us?** (`how_did_you_hear`, text, required): Enter how you heard about us.
* **Comments** (note):
	+ **Comments** (`comments`, note, required): Enter any additional comments or feedback.
* **Form Owner** (select_one):
	+ **Form Owner** (`form_owner`, select_one, required): Select who you are interested in being assigned to. Options: Jimmy, Other

**Submit**
Click the "Submit" button to send your information to the development team. We appreciate your interest in our startup app!
