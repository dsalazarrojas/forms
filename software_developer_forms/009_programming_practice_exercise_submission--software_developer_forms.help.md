# Programming Practice Exercise Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for users to submit their programming practice exercises, allowing you to track and analyze their submissions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your user information:
    *   **User Input** (`user_input`): Enter a brief description of your exercise submission.
    *   **User Email** (`user_email`): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
    *   **User Phone** (`user_phone`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
    *   **User Notes** (`user_notes`): Enter any additional information you want to share about your exercise submission.
*   Enter exercise information:
    *   **Exercise Name** (`exercise_name`): Enter the name of the exercise you're submitting.
    *   **Exercise Description** (`exercise_description`): Enter a description of the exercise you're submitting.
    *   **Solution** (`solution`): Enter your code solution to the exercise.
*   Review and feedback:
    *   **Mentor Feedback** (`mentor_feedback`): Enter any feedback you'd like to share with your mentor.
    *   **Review** (`review`): Enter a review of your submission.
    *   **Exercise Language** (`exercise_language`): Choose the programming language you used to solve the exercise.
*   Submission details:
    *   **Submission Date** (`submission_date`): Enter the date you're submitting the exercise.
    *   **Submission Time** (`submission_time`): Enter the time you're submitting the exercise.
*   Rating and feedback:
    *   **Exercise Rating** (`exercise_rating`): Rate how well you think you've done on the exercise (Good/Fair).
    *   **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`): Choose how you rate your exercise submission (Good/Fair).
    *   **Mentor Rating** (`mentor_rating`): Rate how well your mentor thinks you've done on the exercise (Good/Fair).
    *   **Mentor Rating Scale** (`mentor_rating_scale`): Choose how your mentor rates your exercise submission (Good/Fair).
