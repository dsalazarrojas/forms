# Rust Developer Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey for Rust developers to share their opinions and feedback on the language.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read the form carefully and answer each question based on your genuine experience and opinions.
* Make sure you are answering on behalf of yourself, not on behalf of someone else.
* If you are not satisfied with Rust, please provide specific reasons why.
* For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply.
* Make sure to check the "I agree" option at the end of the form to submit your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rust Developer Survey Form Introduction**: This is a brief introduction to the form, explaining the purpose and importance of the survey.
* **How long have you been using Rust?**: This question is asking about your experience with Rust, from "Less than 6 months" to "More than 2 years".
* **How satisfied are you with Rust?**: This question is asking about your level of satisfaction, from "Very Satisfied", "Satisfied", "Neutral", or "Dissatisfied".
* **What team or organization do you work for?**: This question is asking for the name of the team or organization you work for.
* **Which Rust communities or teams are you part of?**: This question is asking for specific Rust communities or teams you are actively involved with. You can select multiple options.
* **Is there any specific feature you would like to see in Rust?**: This question is asking for any specific features you would like to see in Rust.
* **Other needs for Rust?**: This question is for any other needs or requirements you have for Rust.
* **Any further feedback about Rust?**: This question is for any additional comments or feedback you may have about Rust.
* **Would you like to share your email address with us?**: This question is asking whether you would like to share your email address with the Rust team for further communication.
* **Would you like to share your phone number with us?**: This question is asking whether you would like to share your phone number with the Rust team for further communication.
* **Any other comments?**: This question is for any other comments or feedback you may have.
* **By clicking submit, I agree to the terms of service**: This question is asking for your agreement to the terms of service before submitting the form.
