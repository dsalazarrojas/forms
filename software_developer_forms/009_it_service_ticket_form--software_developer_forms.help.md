# IT Service Ticket Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The IT Service Ticket Form is a tool used to create and manage IT service tickets. This form helps collect essential information about each ticket, including the request type, description, priority, and other relevant details. It also enables the efficient assignment of tickets to the right personnel and tracking of progress.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, select the appropriate "Request Type" that best describes the nature of your request.
2.  In the "Description" field, provide a clear and concise explanation of the issue or request.
3.  Choose the "Status" of your ticket from the available options.
4.  Select the "Priority" level of your request from Low, Medium, or High.
5.  Assign the ticket to a specific person or team using the "Assigned to" field.
6.  Optionally, enter a "Due Date" for the ticket if it's a deadline-bound request.
7.  Select the person responsible for creating the ticket using the "Created by" field.
8.  Add any additional "Notes" or comments related to the ticket.
9.  If needed, provide your "Email" address for further correspondence.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Request Type** (Select One: IT Request, Service Request, Other): This field helps identify the category of your request, ensuring it's properly routed to the right team or individual.
*   **Description** (Text): Write a clear and detailed description of the issue or request to help the assigned person understand the problem accurately.
*   **Status** (Select One: Open, In Progress, Closed, Rejected): Indicate the current status of your ticket to reflect its progression or resolution.
*   **Priority** (Select One: Low, Medium, High): Assign a priority level to your request based on its urgency or importance.
*   **Assigned to** (Select Multiple: chatjimmy, Other): Choose the person or team responsible for handling your ticket.
*   **Due Date** (Date): If applicable, enter a specific date by which the ticket should be resolved.
*   **Created** (Date): This field is automatically populated with the date and time the ticket was created.
*   **Updated** (Date): Displays the last time the ticket was updated.
*   **Closed** (Date): Shows the date the ticket was closed or resolved.
*   **Created by** (Select Multiple: chatjimmy, Other): Identify the person creating the ticket.
*   **Notes** (Note): Add any additional comments or notes related to the ticket.
*   **Email** (Email): Optionally, enter your email address for further communication about the ticket.

## Tips

- Be as detailed as possible when describing your issue to ensure accurate resolution.
- Make sure to select the correct status and priority to facilitate proper ticket management.
- Only select relevant options for "Assigned to" and "Created by" to avoid confusion.
- Enter a Due Date if your request has a specific deadline.
- Keep "Notes" field for any additional information or comments that may aid in resolving the ticket.
