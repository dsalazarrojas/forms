# Tax Software User Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tax Software User Registration form is designed to collect user information and preferences for access and management of tax software features.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. This field is required.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field. This field is required.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This field is required.
4. Select your **User Type** from the dropdown menu. This field is optional.
5. Enter your **Job Title** in the "Job Title" field. This field is optional.
6. Enter the name of your **Company** in the "Company" field. This field is optional.
7. If you are not a representative of a company, enter a brief description of your **Job Title Other** in the "Job Title Other" field. This field is optional.
8. Select your **User Role** from the dropdown menu. This field is optional.
9. Select your **Contact Method** from the dropdown menu. This field is optional.
10. Enter any additional **Contact Details** you would like to include in the "Contact Details" field. This field is optional.
11. Enter the name of the **Tax Platform** you are using in the "Tax Platform" field. This field is required.
12. Enter the name of your **Accounting Firm** in the "Accounting Firm" field. This field is required.
13. Enter the name of your **Finance Team** in the "Finance Team" field. This field is required.
14. Enter the name of the **Software** you are using in the "Software" field. This field is required.
15. Enter any additional **User Notes** you would like to include in the "User Notes" field. This field is optional.
16. Enter the **Registration Date** in the "Registration Date" field. This field is required.
17. Enter the **Registration Time** in the "Registration Time" field. This field is required.
18. Enter the name of the user who **Created By** in the "Created By" field. This field is required.
19. Enter the name of the user who **Updated By** in the "Updated By" field. This field is required.
20. Enter the date and time the **Updated At** in the "Updated At" field. This field is required.
21. Enter the name of the user who is **Assigned To** in the "Assigned To" field. This field is required.
22. Select the status of your **Status** from the dropdown menu. This field is optional.
23. Enter any **Comments** you would like to include in the "Comments" field. This field is optional.
24. Select whether you have any **Attachments** from the dropdown menu. This field is optional.
25. Enter any additional **Tags** you would like to include in the "Tags" field. This field is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address.
* **User Type** (Select One, Optional): Select your user type.
* **Job Title** (Text, Optional): Enter your job title.
* **Company** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of your company.
* **Job Title Other** (Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of your job title if you are not a representative of a company.
* **User Role** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select your user role.
* **Contact Method** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select your contact method.
* **Contact Details** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional contact details.
* **Tax Platform** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the tax platform you are using.
* **Accounting Firm** (Text, Required): Enter the name of your accounting firm.
* **Finance Team** (Text, Required): Enter the name of your finance team.
* **Software** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the software you are using.
* **User Notes** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional user notes.
* **Registration Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date of registration.
* **Registration Time** (Time, Required): Enter the time of registration.
* **Created By** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the user who created the registration.
* **Updated By** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the user who updated the registration.
* **Updated At** (Date, Required): Enter the date and time the registration was updated.
* **Assigned To** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the user who is assigned to the registration.
* **Status** (Select One, Optional): Select the status of your registration.
* **Comments** (Text, Optional): Enter any comments you would like to include.
* **Attachments** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select whether you have any attachments.
* **Tags** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional tags you would like to include.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure of any information, please reach out to the support team for assistance.
* This form is for new registrations only. If you need to update an existing registration, please contact the support team for assistance.
