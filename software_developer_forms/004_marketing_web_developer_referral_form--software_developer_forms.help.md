# Marketing Web Developer Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect referrals for marketing web development opportunities from potential clients or partners.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. **Marketing Web Developer Referral Form**: Enter a descriptive title for the referral.
2. **name**: Enter your name as the person making the referral.
3. **Email**: Enter a valid email address for follow-up contact.
4. **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company you represent or are affiliated with.
5. **Job Title**: Enter your job title or position within the company.
6. **Contact Number**: Enter a valid phone number for contact purposes.
7. **LinkedIn URL**: If applicable, enter your LinkedIn profile URL for networking and verification.
8. **Personal URL**: If applicable, enter a personal website or portfolio URL for further review.
9. **Project Details**: Indicate if the referred project is ongoing (yes) or not (no).
10. **Project Start Date**: Enter the start date of the project or a specific date for the project inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Marketing Web Developer Referral Form** (`title`, text, not required): Enter a descriptive title for the referral to help our team quickly understand the opportunity.
* **name** (`name`, text, required): Your name as the person making the referral.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter a valid email address for follow-up contact and communication.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): The name of the company you represent or are affiliated with.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Your job title or position within the company.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, not required): Enter a valid phone number for contact purposes.
* **LinkedIn URL** (`linkedin_url`, text, not required): If applicable, enter your LinkedIn profile URL for networking and verification.
* **Personal URL** (`personal_url`, text, not required): If applicable, enter a personal website or portfolio URL for further review.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, select_multiple, required): Indicate if the referred project is ongoing (yes) or not (no).
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, date, not required): Enter the start date of the project or a specific date for the project inquiry.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and valid contact information to ensure we can get in touch with you.
* If you have a LinkedIn profile, include your URL to facilitate networking and verification.
* Be specific and clear when describing the project details, if applicable.
