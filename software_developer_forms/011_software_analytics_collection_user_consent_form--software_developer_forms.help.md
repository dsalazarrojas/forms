# software_analytics_collection_user_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect user details and consent for software analytics. It is meant for users who are familiar with the software and its usage and are willing to provide their data for analytics purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your user details, including any relevant information about yourself.
2. Indicate whether or not you are willing to provide your data for software analytics.
3. Enter the date and time of when you used the software (if applicable).
4. Provide your phone number (optional).
5. Enter any additional notes or comments (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details**: Please fill in your user details, including your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Software Analytics**: Please select 'True' to indicate that you are willing to provide your data for software analytics, or 'False' to decline.
* **Date**: If you have used the software, enter the date of when you used it.
* **Time**: If you have used the software, enter the time of when you used it.
* **Consent**: This is a duplicate field of "Software Analytics" that asks for your consent for data collection.
* **Email**: Please enter your email address (optional).
* **Phone**: Please enter your phone number (optional).
* **Note**: Please enter any additional notes or comments about your usage of the software (optional).
* **Software Analytics Data**: This field is a duplicate of "Software Analytics" and is not necessary to fill in. Please ignore it.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields, even if they are optional in this form.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure it is accurate.
* If you have any issues with the form or its purpose, please contact the form administrators for further assistance.
