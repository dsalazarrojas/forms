# DevOps Engineer Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from candidates applying for the DevOps Engineer position. It will help us assess your qualifications, experience, and fit for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your personal details, such as name, email, and phone number, on the first page.
2.  Select your educational background on the second page.
3.  Provide your work history and relevant experience on the third page.
4.  Check the boxes indicating your proficiency in various skills on the fourth page.
5.  Share your work experience and achievements on the fifth page.
6.  Indicate whether you have provided personal referees or not on the sixth page.
7.  Finally, review your application carefully and submit it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Personal Details** (`personal_details`, Text, Required): Please fill in your name, email, and phone number to ensure we can contact you for further information.
*   **Education** (`education`, Select Multiple, Required): Select all the degrees you have obtained, such as Bachelor's or Master's, relevant to the DevOps Engineer position.
*   **Work History** (`work_history`, Text, Optional): Describe your previous work experience, focusing on your achievements and skills acquired in previous roles.
*   **Skills** (`skills`, Select Multiple, Required): Indicate which skills you have acquired or are proficient in, such as automation, cloud computing, or containers. This will help us assess your suitability for the role.
*   **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, Text, Optional): Provide a detailed account of your work experience, including job titles, company names, and duration of employment. This will help us understand your background and qualifications.
*   **Personal Referees** (`personal_referees`, Select Multiple, Required): Please indicate whether you have provided personal referees or not. If yes, provide their names, job titles, and contact information. This will help us verify your claims and assess your network.
*   **Submit** (`submit`, Text, Required): Once you have completed all fields, please review your application carefully and submit it.

## Tips

*   Please provide accurate and concise information to ensure your application is thoroughly reviewed.
*   Be specific about your skills and experience to demonstrate your suitability for the DevOps Engineer position.
*   Ensure you have completed all required fields before submitting your application.
*   Do not hesitate to reach out to us if you need any clarification or assistance during the application process.
