# developer_job_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a comprehensive application form for software developers to showcase their qualifications and apply for a developer position. It is designed to gather detailed information about your background, skills, and experience to ensure that you meet the requirements for the role.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete each section carefully and thoroughly.
2. Make sure to fill in all required fields.
3. If a field is optional, feel free to fill it in if you have relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Resume
* **Resume** (`resume`, `text`, required): Attach a clear and concise resume that highlights your education, experience, and skills.

### Skills
* **Skills** (`skills`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check the skills that you possess, and provide a brief explanation of your proficiency level.

### Project details
* **Project details** (`project_details`, `text`, required): Describe your relevant projects, including the technologies used and your role.

### Experience
* **Experience** (`experience`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check the boxes that apply to your experience and provide a brief explanation.

### Education
* **Education** (`education`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check the boxes that apply to your education and provide any relevant information.

### Work sample
* **Work sample** (`work_sample`, `text`, optional): If you have any relevant work samples, describe them and attach files as necessary.

### References
* **References** (`references`, `text`, optional): Provide any relevant references or contact information.

### Additional Info
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `text`, optional): Use this field to provide any additional information that you think is relevant to your application.

### Upload Resume
* **Upload Resume** (`upload_resume`, `text`, optional): If you have a resume that you would like to upload, attach it as a PDF or docx file.

### Upload Work sample
* **Upload Work sample** (`upload_work_sample`, `text`, optional): If you have a work sample that you would like to upload, attach it as a PDF or docx file.
