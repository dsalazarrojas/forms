# retail_pos_system_beta_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The retail_pos_system_beta_registration_form is designed for retail stores interested in testing the beta version of the Point of Sale (POS) system. By filling out this form, you will be providing essential information about your business that will help us tailor the system to your store's needs and ensure a smooth transition to the beta phase.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information about your business ready.
2. Start by filling out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. For fields with options, select the one that best describes your store.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name**: Enter your company name (\*). This will be used as a reference for our team and to identify your store in the beta program.
* **Contact Person**: Enter your contact person's name (\*). This will be the primary contact for communication and support.
* **Email**: Enter your email address (\*). We will use this to send important updates and information about the beta program and the POS system.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional). This will help us contact you in case we need to discuss any questions or issues.
* **Store Name**: Enter your store name (\*). This will be used to identify your store in the beta program and to provide you with personalized support.
* **Location**: Enter your store location (\*). This will help us understand your store's unique needs and preferences.
* **Store Type**: Select the type of store you have (brick and mortar, online, or both) (\*). This will influence how we configure the system for your store's specific requirements.
* **Store Size**: Select the size of your store (small, medium, or large) (\*). This will help us tailor the system's features and settings for your store.
* **Store Products**: Enter your store products (\*). This will help us ensure that the system's catalog is tailored to your store's specific products.
* **Consent**: Accept or decline receiving emails from us (\*). This will allow us to communicate with you and keep you informed about the beta program and the POS system's updates.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out the form carefully, as the information will be used to configure the system for your store's specific needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us after submitting the form.
* We will be in touch with you shortly after submitting the form to confirm your registration and to provide you with further instructions.
