# e-commerce_developer_api_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register e-commerce developers to our API platform, allowing us to collect information about your platform preferences and details for registration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure that all required fields are filled out.
2. Fill out the fields as instructed, and click submit to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Platform** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): 
Select 'Yes' if you wish to be registered for the e-commerce API on one or more platforms. 
* **Developer Details** (`text`, required/optional): 
Enter any additional details about yourself as the developer, such as your name, company name, or contact information.
* **Review** (`note`, required/optional): 
Use this field to provide any comments or notes about your registration.

## Tips
- Please note that this form is designed to be completed by e-commerce developers only.
- Ensure that your platform preferences are accurate, as these will be used to provide you with relevant information and services.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, please don't hesitate to contact us.
- We will review your registration and follow up with you shortly after submission.
