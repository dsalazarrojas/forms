# Pricing Software Trial Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to register for a software trial, allowing users to provide their contact information and select a plan for their trial. The form is intended to gather necessary details for the software trial registration process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form fields with your information:
	* Enter your First Name and Last Name (if you want to complete this form).
	* Enter your Email address.
	* Enter your Phone number.
	* Enter your Password (and confirm it for verification).
	* Enter your Company name (if applicable).
	* Select your Role (Administrator or User).
	* Enter the name of the product you are registering for.
	* Choose a plan that suits your needs (Trial, Premium, or Enterprise).
	* Select the date and time you signed up for the trial.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **trial_name**: Enter the name of the software trial you are registering for.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Password**: Enter the password for your software trial account.
* **Confirm Password**: Re-enter your password for verification.
* **Company**: Enter your company name (if applicable).
* **Role**: Select your role in the software trial (Administrator or User).
* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product you are registering for.
* **Plan**: Choose the plan that suits your needs (Trial, Premium, or Enterprise).
* **Sign Up Date**: Select the date you signed up for the trial.
* **Sign Up Time**: Select the time you signed up for the trial.

## Tips

- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure successful submission.
- Enter accurate and correct information for verification purposes.
- If you have any issues with the form or trial registration process, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
