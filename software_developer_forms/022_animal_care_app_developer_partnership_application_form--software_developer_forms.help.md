# Animal Care App Developer Partnership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect information from software developers interested in partnering with us for the development of animal care technology solutions. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the required contact information (pages 1-4).
2. Select the type of partnership you are interested in (pages 5-6).
3. Describe your experience in animal care or veterinary technology (pages 7-8).
4. Share your proposed solution or partnership idea (pages 9-10).
5. Explain the value you bring to the partnership (pages 11-12).
6. Provide additional information about your company and team (pages 13-15).
7. Confirm your availability and interest in the partnership (pages 16-17).
8. Review and sign the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Contact Person Full Name (Page 1)**: Your name as the primary contact person for the partnership.
	+ Type your full name as you would like it to appear on a partnership agreement.
* **2. Email Address (Page 2)**: Your business email address.
	+ Enter your professional email address.
* **3. Phone Number (Page 3)**: Your direct contact number.
	+ Enter your phone number without any special characters.

	+ Enter your company's name as you would like it to appear on a partnership agreement.
* **5. Company Website (Page 4)**: Your company's website URL.
	+ Enter your website's URL.

	+ Type your interest in partnering with us.

	+ Select the number of employees your company has.
