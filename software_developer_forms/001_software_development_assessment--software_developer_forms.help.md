# Software Development Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information from software developers to assess their skills, experience, and preferences. It's meant to be filled out by software developers applying for a project.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Select all relevant options for each field where applicable.
3. Be as detailed as possible when filling out the 'Project Details' field.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, contact the project manager/client.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Page** (`id: 1`): This is the main introduction page where you can add any additional information or context about your application.
* **Software Development Skills** (`id: 2`): Select all the software development skills you have experience with, such as Development, Deployment, and Testing.
* **Development Tools** (`id: 3`): Choose the development tools you use in your work, such as Jira, GitHub, and Slack.
* **Software Development Methodologies** (`id: 4`): Select the software development methodologies you're familiar with, such as Agile and Waterfall.
* **Software Development Teams** (`id: 5`): Choose the size of the teams you've worked with, such as Large, Medium, and Small.
* **Communication Channels** (`id: 6`): Select the channels you use for communication, such as Email, Phone, and Chat.
* **Software Development Technologies** (`id: 7`): Choose the programming languages and technologies you're skilled in, such as Java, Python, and JavaScript.
* **Collaboration Tools** (`id: 8`): Select the collaboration tools you use, such as Google Drive and Trello.
* **Feedback Mechanism** (`id: 9`): This field is not used in this form.
* **Time to Complete** (`id: 10`): This field is not used in this form.
* **Notes** (`id: 11`): Add any additional notes or comments about your application.
* **Email** (`id: 12`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`id: 13`): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`id: 14`): Enter your address.
* **User Name** (`id: 15`): Enter your name.
* **User Email** (`id: 16`): Enter your email address.
* **User Phone** (`id: 17`): Enter your phone number.
* **User Address** (`id: 18`): Enter your address.
* **User Notes** (`id: 19`): Add any additional notes or comments about yourself.
* **Software Development Era** (`id: 20`): Choose the era of software development you're most experienced with, such as Era 1 and Era 2.
* **Project Details** (`id: 21`): Describe your experience and qualifications for the project.
* **Team Size** (`id: 22`): Choose the size of the teams you've worked with, such as Small, Medium, and Large.
* **Team Location** (`id: 23`): Select where you've worked with teams, such as Remote, On Site, or Hybrid.
* **Collaboration Frequency** (`id: 24`): Select how often you collaborate with teams, such as Daily, Weekly, and Monthly.
* **Submission Comments** (`id: 25`): Add any final comments or feedback about your application.

Please note that some fields are not used in this form and can be ignored.
