# Equipment Safety Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equipment Safety Assessment Form is designed to evaluate the safety status of equipment and identify potential hazards. It helps inspectors and maintenance personnel assess the condition of equipment, document findings, and provide recommendations for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page, providing clear and concise information about the equipment's safety status.
2. Ensure that you answer all required fields. If a field is not required, you may skip it.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all relevant information is included.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Equipment Safety Assessment (page 1)
• **Equipment Safety Assessment** (page_1, note, required: false): This page is for providing a general overview of the equipment's safety status. Write a brief description of the equipment's safety status, including any notable hazards or concerns.

### Safety Risks (page 2)
• **Safety Risks** (page_2, select_one, required: false): On this page, select "Yes" if you have identified any significant safety risks related to the equipment. If there are no risks, select "No".

### Safety Risks - Select Multiple (page 3)
• **Safety Risks - Select Multiple** (page_3, select_multiple, required: false): On this page, select all the safety risks that apply to the equipment. This will help inspectors and maintenance personnel understand the scope of the risks.

### Inspection Date (page 4)
• **Inspection Date** (page_4, date, required: false): Enter the date of the inspection, ensuring it is in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).

### Inspector Name (page 5)
• **Inspector Name** (page_5, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person who performed the inspection.

### Equipment ID (page 6)
• **Equipment ID** (page_6, text, required: false): Enter the unique identifier of the equipment being assessed (e.g., serial number, model number, etc.).

### Inspection Frequency (page 7)
• **Inspection Frequency** (page_7, select_one, required: false): On this page, select the frequency of inspections for the equipment (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly, etc.).

### Inspection Date (page 8)
• **Inspection Date** (page_8, date, required: false): Enter the date of the most recent inspection (or the planned date for the next inspection).

Note: This form is designed to help identify potential hazards and ensure equipment safety. Please be accurate and thorough in your responses.
