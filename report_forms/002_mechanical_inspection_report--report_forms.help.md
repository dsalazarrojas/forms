# Mechanical Inspection Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mechanical Inspection Report form is a standardized template used to document the results of a vehicle's mechanical inspection. This form is used to gather information on the vehicle's condition, test results, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter the Vehicle Registration Number in the designated field.
2.  Enter the Vehicle Year.
3.  Describe the vehicle's Mechanical Condition in the provided text field.
4.  Provide detailed Test Drive Details in the text field.
5.  Enter the name of the person or team member who performed the inspection, the Inspection Date, and the Vehicle Type.
6.  Enter the Odometer Reading (if applicable).
7.  Enter the Vehicle Condition (if applicable).
8.  Enter the Test Engineer's name and other relevant details.
9.  Enter the Vehicle Make, Model, and Year.
10. Enter the Vehicle Model Year.
11. Enter the Vehicle Type ID (if applicable).
12. Enter the Transmission Type ID (if applicable).
13. Enter the Engine Type ID (if applicable).
14. Enter the Vehicle Condition ID (if applicable).
15. Enter the Inspection Result in the text field.
16. Provide any additional Comments on the vehicle's condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Vehicle Registration Number** (`vehicle_registration_number`, `text`, required): Enter the vehicle's registration number to identify the vehicle.
*   **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, `number`, required): Enter the year of the vehicle.
*   **Mechanical Condition** (`mechanical_condition`, `text`, required): Describe the vehicle's mechanical condition in detail.
*   **Test Drive Details** (`test_drive_details`, `text`, required): Provide detailed notes on the vehicle's test drive.
*   **Recorded By** (`recorded_by`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person or team member who performed the inspection.
*   **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the inspection.
*   **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, `text`, optional): Enter the type of vehicle (if applicable).
*   **Odometer Reading** (`odometer_reading`, `number`, optional): Enter the odometer reading (if applicable).
*   **Vehicle Condition** (`vehicle_condition`, `text`, required): Describe the vehicle's overall condition.
*   **Test Engineer** (`test_engineer`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the test engineer.
*   **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, `text`, required): Enter the vehicle's make.
*   **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, `text`, required): Enter the vehicle's model.
*   **Test Notes** (`test_notes`, `text`, required): Provide detailed notes on the vehicle's test.
*   **Inspection Report Number** (`inspection_report_number`, `text`, required): Enter the inspection report number.
*   **Vehicle Color** (`vehicle_color`, `text`, optional): Enter the vehicle's color (if applicable).
*   **Transmission Type** (`transmission_type`, `text`, optional): Enter the vehicle's transmission type (if applicable).
*   **Engine Type** (`engine_type`, `text`, optional): Enter the vehicle's engine type (if applicable).
*   **Vehicle Year Make Model** (`vehicle_year_make_model`, `text`, required): Enter the vehicle's year, make, and model.
*   **Vehicle Year Model Year** (`vehicle_year_model_year`, `text`, required): Enter the vehicle's year and model year.
*   **Vehicle Type ID** (`vehicle_type_id`, `text`, optional): Enter the vehicle's type ID (if applicable).
*   **Transmission Type ID** (`transmission_type_id`, `text`, optional): Enter the vehicle's transmission type ID (if applicable).
*   **Engine Type ID** (`engine_type_id`, `text`, optional): Enter the vehicle's engine type ID (if applicable).
*   **Vehicle Condition ID** (`vehicle_condition_id`, `text`, optional): Enter the vehicle's condition ID (if applicable).
*   **Inspection Result** (`inspection_result`, `text`, required): Enter the inspection result.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required): Provide any additional comments on the vehicle's condition.

## Tips

*   Ensure all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
*   Double-check the vehicle's registration number, year, and other details for accuracy.
*   Provide detailed descriptions for each field, especially for the Test Drive Details and Test Notes.
*   Use a clear and concise format for the Inspection Result and Comments.
*   Ensure all optional fields are completed accurately (if applicable).
