# Loan Performance Quarterly Metrics Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Loan Performance Quarterly Metrics Report is used to report on the performance of loans over a specific quarter.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the quarter you are reporting on from the options provided.
2. Enter the relevant data for the selected quarter in the fields below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **quarter** (Select One, Required): Select the quarter you are reporting on.
* **year** (Number, Required): Enter the year for which you are reporting.
* **total_loans** (Number, Required): Enter the total number of loans.
* **new_origination** (Number, Required): Enter the number of new loan originations.
* **outstanding_principal** (Number, Required): Enter the total outstanding principal of loans.
* **outstanding_interest** (Number, Required): Enter the total outstanding interest of loans.
* **interest_income** (Number, Required): Enter the total interest income.
* **servicing_fees** (Number, Required): Enter the total servicing fees.
* **provision_for_doubt** (Number, Required): Enter the provision for doubt.
* **delinquency_rate** (Number, Required): Enter the delinquency rate.
* **charge_off_rate** (Number, Required): Enter the charge-off rate.
* **return_rate** (Number, Required): Enter the return rate.
* **default_rate** (Number, Required): Enter the default rate.
* **charge_off_amts** (Number, Required): Enter the total charge-off amounts.
* **recoveries** (Number, Required): Enter the total recoveries.
* **recoveries_as_a_percentage** (Number, Required): Enter the recoveries as a percentage.
* **average_recovery** (Number, Required): Enter the average recovery.
* **average_recovery_rate** (Number, Required): Enter the average recovery rate.
* **loan_value** (Number, Required): Enter the total loan value.
* **total_value** (Number, Required): Enter the total value.
* **net_writedown** (Number, Required): Enter the net writedown.
* **net_writedown_rate** (Number, Required): Enter the net writedown rate.
* **cash_flow** (Number, Required): Enter the cash flow.
* **cash_flow_rate** (Number, Required): Enter the cash flow rate.
