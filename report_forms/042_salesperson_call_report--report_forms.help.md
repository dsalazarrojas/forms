# Salesperson Call Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Salesperson Call Report form, ensuring you have all the necessary information to track sales calls effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Date of Sale field with the date of the sales call.
2. Enter the Time of Sale field with the time of the sales call.
3. Enter the Salesperson Name field with the name of the salesperson who made the call.
4. Enter the Customer Name field with the name of the customer being called.
5. Enter the Customer Address field, if applicable.
6. Enter the Sale Amount field with the total amount of the sale.
7. Select the Sale Status field with the current status of the sale (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
8. Provide any additional notes in the Notes field.
9. Include any additional notes from the salesperson in the Salesperson Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Date of Sale (2)** (`date`, required/optional: required): Enter the date of the sales call, which is the day when the sale was made.
* **Time of Sale (3)** (`time`, required/optional: required): Enter the time of the sales call, which is the time when the sales call was made.
* **Salesperson Name (4)** (`text`, required/optional: required): Enter the name of the salesperson who made the call.
* **Customer Name (5)** (`text`, required/optional: required): Enter the name of the customer being called.
* **Customer Address (6)** (`text`, required/optional: required): Enter the customer's address, if applicable.
* **Sale Amount (7)** (`number`, required/optional: required): Enter the total amount of the sale.
* **Sale Status (8)** (`select_one`, required/optional: required): Select the current status of the sale from the options: Active, Inactive, or Pending.
* **Notes (9)** (`note`, required/optional: optional): Provide any additional notes about the sale.
* **Salesperson Notes (10)** (`note`, required/optional: optional): Include any additional notes from the salesperson about the sale.
