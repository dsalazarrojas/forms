# Damage Assessment Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Damage Assessment Report" form is used to collect information and track progress on damage assessments. This form is used to report damage, track progress, and update status in various business operations, such as construction or facilities management.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the **Report Type** field with the type of report you are submitting (e.g., Damage Assessment or Assessment Report).
*   Enter the **Report Date** in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
*   Provide the **Reported By** field with the name of the person reporting the damage.
*   Enter the **Reported By Email** and **Reported By Phone** fields with the email and phone number of the person reporting the damage.
*   Enter the **Reported By Organization** field with the name of the organization reporting the damage.
*   Describe the **Report Details** in the text field.
*   Enter the **Damage Type** field with the type of damage (e.g., structural, water damage, etc.).
*   Enter the **Damage Description** field with a brief description of the damage.
*   Enter the **Damage Date** field with the date of the damage in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
*   Enter the **Damage Location** field with the location of the damage.
*   Enter the **Damage Type Description** field with a description of the type of damage.
*   Select the **Report Status** field with the current status of the report (e.g., Pending Review, In Review, Complete, Draft, or Approved).
*   Enter the **Submitter** field with the name of the person submitting the report.
*   Enter the **Submitter Phone** field with the phone number of the person submitting the report.
*   Enter the **Submitter Email** field with the email of the person submitting the report.
*   Enter the **Report Note** field with any additional notes or comments about the report.
*   Enter the **Report Status Update** field with any updates to the report status.
*   Enter the **Review Note** field with any comments or notes from the reviewer.
*   Enter the **Reviewer** field with the name of the reviewer.
*   Enter the **Reviewer Phone** field with the phone number of the reviewer.
*   Enter the **Reviewer Email** field with the email of the reviewer.
*   Enter the **Review Date** field with the date the report was reviewed.
*   Enter the **Completed Date** field with the date the report was completed.
