# Coach Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coach Report Form is a tool used by coaches to provide detailed feedback on athlete or client sessions. It helps coaches evaluate performance, identify areas for improvement, and set goals for the next session.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the athlete or client name in the **Athlete or Client Name** field.
2. Select the **Type of Training** that best describes the session (e.g., Individual, Group, Tactical, Strength and Conditioning, or Skill Specific).
3. Enter the **Date of Session**.
4. Select the **Physical Performance** level of the athlete or client (e.g., Below Avg, Avg, Above Avg, or Exceptional).
5. Select the **Technical Skill Progress** level (e.g., Below Avg, Avg, Above Avg, or Exceptional).
6. Select the **Mental Focus and Attitude** level (e.g., Below Avg, Avg, Above Avg, or Exceptional).
7. Provide a brief description of **Key Achievements this Session** (if applicable).
8. Describe the **Areas Needing Improvement** (if applicable).
9. Enter the **Goal for Next Session** (if applicable).
10. Sign the form with your **Coach Signature**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Athlete or Client Name**: Enter the name of the athlete or client who had the session. This will help us associate the feedback with the correct individual.
* **Date of Session**: Enter the date of the session you are reporting on. This helps us track progress over time.
* **Coach Name**: Enter your name as the coach who conducted the session. This ensures we know who provided the feedback.
* **Type of Training**: Select the type of training or session that took place (e.g., Individual, Group, Tactical, Strength and Conditioning, or Skill Specific).
* **Physical Performance**: Rate the athlete or client's physical performance (e.g., Below Avg, Avg, Above Avg, or Exceptional).
* **Technical Skill Progress**: Rate the athlete or client's technical skill progress (e.g., Below Avg, Avg, Above Avg, or Exceptional).
* **Mental Focus and Attitude**: Rate the athlete or client's mental focus and attitude (e.g., Below Avg, Avg, Above Avg, or Exceptional).
* **Key Achievements this Session**: Describe any notable achievements or successes from the session (if applicable).
* **Areas Needing Improvement**: Identify any areas that require further work or improvement (if applicable).
* **Goal for Next Session**: Set a specific objective for the next session (if applicable).
* **Coach Signature**: Sign the form with your name as the coach who provided the feedback.

## Tips
- Ensure each session report is filled out individually and not duplicated or copied.
- Select the most accurate options for performance metrics and skills.
- If unsure, refer to the provided options or your best judgment.
- Provide specific, concrete feedback to help the athlete or client improve.
- Keep the **Goal for Next Session** concrete and achievable.
- Sign the form with your **Coach Signature** to ensure accountability.
