# Agricultural Cooperative Member Satisfaction Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from agricultural cooperative members on their overall satisfaction with the services provided by the cooperative. It will help identify areas of improvement and understand what services are most valued by members.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member ID** (1, text, required): Enter your unique cooperative membership identification number.
* **Member Name** (2, text, required): Provide your full name as a member of the cooperative.
* **Membership Duration** (3, select_one, required): Select how long you have been a member of the cooperative.
* **Farm Type** (4, select_one, required): Choose the type of farming operation you are engaged in.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (5, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the cooperative.
* **Service Quality Rating** (6, select_one, required): Rate the quality of the services provided by the cooperative.
* **Pricing Satisfaction** (7, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction with the cooperative pricing.
* **Benefits Utilized** (8, select_multiple, required): Select all cooperative benefits you use.
* **Most Valuable Service** (9, select_one, not required): Indicate which service provided by the cooperative has been the most valuable to you.
* **Areas Needing Improvement** (10, select_multiple, required): Select areas that need improvement within the cooperative.
* **Recommendation Likelihood** (11, select_one, required): Indicate how likely you are to recommend the cooperative to others.
* **Participation Level** (12, select_one, required): Indicate your level of participation in cooperative activities.
* **Additional Feedback** (13, text, not required): Provide any additional feedback or comments about the cooperative.
* **Report Date** (14, date, required): Enter the date of this satisfaction report.

## Tips

* Please provide honest and candid feedback to help us improve our services.
* You can select multiple benefits you use under "Benefits Utilized".
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional Feedback" field.
* Enter the correct date of this satisfaction report in the "Report Date" field.
