# Health Status Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Status Report Form is used to gather information about an individual's health status, including their medical history, symptoms, and diagnosis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Scroll to the first page.
2. Enter your medical history in the text box provided on the first page.
3. On the second page, select all the symptoms that apply to you.
4. On the third page, select the diagnosis that best matches your condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical History**: This field is where you enter your medical history, including any relevant information about your past medical conditions or procedures. This field is a text box where you can provide a brief description of your medical history.
* **Symptoms**: This field is where you select all the symptoms that apply to you. Select "Yes" for each symptom that you are experiencing. If you are not experiencing any symptoms, select "No" for each option.
* **Diagnosis**: This field is where you select the diagnosis that best matches your condition. Select "Yes" if your diagnosis is positive, and "No" if your diagnosis is negative.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when describing your medical history.
* Only select symptoms that you are currently experiencing.
* Choose the diagnosis that best matches your condition.
* If you are unsure about any of the options, consult with the relevant department or supervisor for guidance.
