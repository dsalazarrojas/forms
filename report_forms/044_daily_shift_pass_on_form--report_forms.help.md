# Daily Shift Pass On Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for reporting shifts that have already started or are about to start. It is used to record accurate information about shifts, including start and end times, and replacement details if applicable. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the start and end times of your shift accurately, as this information is used for various business processes.
2. If there is a replacement, fill in their name and phone number.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
4. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shift Start Time** (`shift_start_time`, required/optional): Enter the start time of your shift.
* **End Shift Time** (`end_shift_time`, required/optional): Enter the end time of your shift.
* **Replacement Name** (`replacement_name`, required/optional): Enter the name of the person replacing you for your shift, if applicable.
* **Replacement Phone** (`replacement_phone`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the person replacing you for your shift, if applicable.
