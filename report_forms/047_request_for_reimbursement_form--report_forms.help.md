# Request For Reimbursement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Request For Reimbursement Form is used to submit requests for reimbursement for company expenses. This form ensures that employees provide accurate and detailed information about their expenses, making it easier for the company to process and approve reimbursement requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, email, and phone number to provide contact information.
2. Upload a clear and readable receipt image of the expense.
3. Enter the receipt date and a detailed description of the expense.
4. Enter the amount of the expense and the category it falls under.
5. Choose the category of the expense (e.g., business travel, equipment, etc.).
6. Enter any additional comments or notes about the expense.
7. Click the submit button to send the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Name
* **Name** (Text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID.
### Email
* **Email** (Text, required): Enter your work email address.
### Phone
* **Phone** (Text, required): Enter your work phone number.
### Receipt Date
* **Receipt Date** (Date, required): Enter the date the receipt was received.
### Receipt Image
* **Receipt Image** (Text, required): Upload a clear and readable image of the receipt.
### Description
* **Description** (Text, optional): Enter a detailed description of the expense, including any relevant details or context.
### Amount
* **Amount** (Number, required): Enter the total amount of the expense.
### Expense Item
* **Expense Item** (Text, required): Enter the item or service that was purchased or acquired.
### Expense Date
* **Expense Date** (Date, required): Enter the date of the expense.
### Expense Category
* **Expense Category** (Text, required): Choose the category of the expense (e.g., business travel, equipment, etc.).
### Expense Amount
* **Expense Amount** (Number, required): Enter the cost of the expense.
### Expense Reason
* **Expense Reason** (Text, required): Enter a brief explanation of why the expense was incurred.
### Expense Image
* **Expense Image** (Text, required): Upload a clear and readable image of the expense.
### Expense Note
* **Expense Note** (Text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the expense.
### Expense Status
* **Expense Status** (Text, optional): Enter the current status of the expense (e.g., submitted, approved, etc.).
### Expense Comment
* **Expense Comment** (Text, optional): Enter any comments about the expense.
### Form Category
* **Form Category** (Text, required): Choose the category of the form (e.g., travel, expense, etc.).
### Form Comments
* **Form Comments** (Text, optional): Enter any comments about the form.
### Form Notes
* **Form Notes** (Text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the form.
### Form Submit
* **Form Submit** (Text, not required): Click to submit the form for review and approval.
### Form Status
* **Form Status** (Text, not required): View the current status of the form.
### Form Reimbursement Amount
* **Form Reimbursement Amount** (Text, not required): Enter the amount of the reimbursement.
### Form Reimbursement Status
* **Form Reimbursement Status** (Text, not required): View the status of the reimbursement.

Please note that the form will guide you through each step, and you can also ask your supervisor or HR representative for help if you have any questions or need assistance.
