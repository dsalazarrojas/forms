# Field Inspection Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Field Inspection Report Form is used to document findings, observations, and notes during field inspections.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the **Field Inspection Area ID** to identify the area of inspection.
2. Describe the **Field Inspection Area** being inspected.
3. Enter the **Field Inspection Date** and **Field Inspection Time** of the inspection.
4. Check the **Field Inspection Weather** conditions if necessary.
5. Report the **Field Inspection Condition** observed during the inspection.
6. Enter any **Field Inspection Note** or additional information related to the inspection.
7. Add any **Field Inspection Additional Notes** for further details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Field Inspection Area ID** (`field_inspection_area_id`, text, optional): Enter the ID of the field inspected.
* **Field Inspection Area** (`field_inspection_area`, text, optional): Describe the area inspected.
* **Field Inspection Date** (`field_inspection_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the inspection, in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Field Inspection Time** (`field_inspection_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the inspection, in 24-hour format.
* **Field Inspection Weather** (`field_inspection_weather`, text, optional): Check the weather conditions during the inspection, if applicable.
* **Field Inspection Condition** (`field_inspection_condition`, text, optional): Report the condition observed during the inspection.
* **Field Inspection Note** (`field_inspection_note`, note, optional): Enter any note or comment related to the inspection.
* **Field Inspection Additional Notes** (`field_inspection_additional_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional information related to the inspection. 

Note that all fields are optional and can be completed as needed.
