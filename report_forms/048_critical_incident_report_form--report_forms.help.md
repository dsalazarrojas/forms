# critical_incident_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Critical Incident Report Form is a tool for reporting and managing critical incidents in the workplace. It's designed to gather essential information about an incident, including its location, cause, and resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Report Date**: Enter the date the incident occurred.
2. **Incident Location**: Provide a brief description of the incident location.
3. **Incident Brief**: Give a concise description of what happened.
4. **Incident Details**: Offer more detailed information about the incident.
5. **Incident Cause**: Identify the reason for the incident.
6. **Incident Resolution**: Explain how the incident was resolved.
7. **Incident Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the incident.
8. **Incident Follow-up**: Record any follow-up actions taken.
9. **Incident Review**: Add any review or feedback about the incident.
10. **Reopened**: Note if the incident was reopened.
11. **Closed**: Confirm if the incident is closed.
12. **Archived**: Indicate if the incident was archived.
13. **Incident Reporter**: Enter the name of the person reporting the incident.
14. **Incident Reviewer**: Enter the name of the person reviewing the incident.
15. **Resolved**: Confirm if the incident is resolved.
16. **Reviewed**: Confirm if the incident was reviewed.
17. **Closed Date**: Enter the date the incident was closed.
18. **Incident ID**: Enter a unique identifier for the incident.
