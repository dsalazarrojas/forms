# Health Examination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a patient's health examination, including their medical history, vital signs, allergies, current medical conditions, medication, medical procedures, medical diagnosis, treatment plans, and health insurance information. This form is designed to ensure that healthcare providers have all the necessary information to conduct a thorough examination and treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above. Make sure to complete each field accurately and thoroughly, and do not repeat any information that is already present in a previous field.
