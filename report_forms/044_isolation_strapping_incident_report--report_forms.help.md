# Isolation Strapping Incident Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report incidents involving isolation and/or quarantine of patients in the facility. Please complete this form to document the incident and provide essential details for follow-up actions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
*   Select the date of the incident in the "Incident Date" field.
*   Enter the location where the incident occurred in the "Incident Location" field.
*   Provide a detailed description of the incident in the "Description" field.
*   Choose the type of strapping applied in the "Strapping Type" field.
*   Enter the patient's contact information in the "Patient Contact" field.
*   Enter the facility's name in the "Facility" field.
*   Enter the name of the person reporting the incident in the "Reporting Person" field.
*   Specify the number of witnesses involved in the "Witness Count" field.
*   Provide a detailed description of the witnesses in the "Witness Details" field.
*   Select a date for the strapping in the "Strapping Date" field.
*   Enter the name of the facility manager in the "Facility Manager" field.
*   Choose any follow-up actions that will be taken in the "Follow-up Action" field.
*   Select the status of the incident in the "Incident Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Name (page 1)**: Enter the patient's name who was involved in the incident.
*   **Incident Date (page 1)**: Select the date of the incident using the date picker.
*   **Incident Location (page 1)**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
*   **Description (page 1)**: Provide a detailed description of what happened.
*   **Strapping Type (page 2)**: Choose the type of strapping applied to the patient, such as "Isolation", "Quarantine", or "Isolation and Quarantine".
*   **Patient Contact (page 2)**: Enter the patient's contact information, including their name and contact details.
*   **Facility (page 2)**: Enter the name of the facility where the incident occurred.
*   **Reporting Person (page 2)**: Enter the name of the person reporting the incident.
*   **Witness Count (page 3)**: Specify the number of witnesses involved in the incident.
*   **Witness Details (page 3)**: Provide a detailed description of the witnesses.
*   **Strapping Date (page 4)**: Select a date for the strapping procedure.
*   **Facility Manager (page 4)**: Enter the name of the facility manager.
*   **Follow-up Action (page 4)**: Choose the follow-up actions that will be taken, such as "None", "Follow-up Call", or "Follow-up Visit".
*   **Incident Status (page 5)**: Select the status of the incident, such as "Open" or "Closed".
