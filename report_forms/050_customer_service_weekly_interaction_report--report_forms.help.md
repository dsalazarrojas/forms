# Customer Service Weekly Interaction Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This report is a weekly summary of customer service interactions, allowing managers to track and analyze performance and provide feedback and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the customer service interactions that took place during the week and gather all the relevant information.
2. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, ensuring that all required fields are completed.
3. Select the correct options for each field based on your observations and experiences.
4. Enter comments and manager notes as needed, providing feedback and suggestions for improvement.
5. Review and approve the report before submitting it to the team or management.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Interaction Report** (`customer_service_weekly_interaction_report`): This is the main section of the form where you report the customer service interactions for the week. Enter a brief summary of the interactions, including any notable issues or events.
* **Weekly Support Performance Trends** (`weekly_support_performace_trends`): Select the performance trends for the week, which should reflect the overall quality of service provided.
	+ Highly Satisfactory: Excellent service with no major issues.
	+ Satisfactory: Good service with minor issues.
	+ Unsatisfactory: Poor service with significant issues.
* **Interaction Number** (`customer_service_weekly_interaction_num_1`): Enter the number of customer interactions for the week.
* **Resolution Number** (`customer_service_weekly_interaction_num_2`): Enter the number of customer interactions resolved for the week.
* **Average Duration** (`customer_service_weekly_interaction_duration_mean`): Enter the average time spent on customer interactions for the week.
* **Resolution Rate** (`customer_service_weekly_interaction_resolution_rate`): Enter the percentage of customer interactions resolved for the week.
* **Satisfaction Rate** (`customer_service_weekly_interaction_satisfaction_rate`): Enter the percentage of customers satisfied with the service.
* **Top Issue** (`customer_service_weekly_interaction_top_issue`): Select the top issue faced by customers during the week.
	+ Communication: Issues related to communication, such as unclear information or poor responsiveness.
	+ Product: Issues related to the product or service offered.
	+ Service: Issues related to the service provided.
* **Comments** (`customer_service_weekly_interaction_comments`): Enter any additional comments or feedback from customers or team members.
* **Manager Notes** (`customer_service_weekly_interaction_manager_notes`): Enter any notes or feedback from the manager for the week.
* **Manager Recommendations** (`customer_service_weekly_interaction_manager_recommendations`): Enter any recommendations or suggestions from the manager for improvement.
* **Review Date** (`customer_service_weekly_interaction_review_date`): Enter the date of the review for the week.
* **Review Time** (`customer_service_weekly_interaction_review_time`): Enter the time of the review for the week.
* **Reviewer** (`customer_service_weekly_interaction_reviewer`): Enter the name and title of the person conducting the review.
* **Reviewer Email** (`customer_service_weekly_interaction_reviewer_email`): Enter the email of the person conducting the review.
* **Manager Name** (`customer_service_weekly_interaction_manager_name`): Enter the name of the manager approving the report.
* **Manager Job Title** (`customer_service_weekly_interaction_manager_job_title`): Enter the job title of the manager approving the report.
* **Manager Email** (`customer_service_weekly_interaction_manager_email`): Enter the email of the manager approving the report.
* **Manager Phone** (`customer_service_weekly_interaction_manager_phone`): Enter the phone number of the manager approving the report.
* **Manager Approval Date** (`customer_service_weekly_interaction_manager_approval_date`): Enter the date of the manager's approval.
* **Manager Approval Time** (`customer_service_weekly_interaction_manager_approval_time`): Enter the time of the manager's approval.
* **Manager Approval Status** (`customer_service_weekly_interaction_manager_approval_status`): Select the status of the manager's approval.
	+ Approved: Approved by the manager.
	+ Rejected: Rejected by the manager.
	+ Pending: Waiting for manager approval.
* **Manager Approval Notes** (`customer_service_weekly_interaction_manager_approval_notes`): Enter any additional notes or feedback from the manager for the week.

Note: This help guide is provided as a general overview of the form and its fields. If you have any specific questions or concerns, please reach out to your manager or supervisor for guidance.
