# Financial Needs Analysis Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help assess and analyze financial situations by gathering information on income, expenses, assets, liabilities, and other key financial metrics. It's a tool for financial planning and review purposes, likely used by financial advisors or planners.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each page carefully and complete each section as instructed.
2. Enter your current income and expenses in the respective fields.
3. List your assets and liabilities, and provide any additional information as needed.
4. Answer the questions about cash flow, risk assessment, and other financial metrics with honesty.
5. Choose whether you need a financial planner or have any next steps to take.
6. Provide your contact information and any additional notes if necessary.
7. Sign and date the form to indicate that you've completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Income** (`income`, number, required): Enter your current monthly or yearly income. This will help us understand your financial capabilities.
* **Expenses** (`expenses`, number, required): List your regular monthly or yearly expenses, including rent, food, transportation, and other essential costs.
* **Assets** (`assets`, text, required): List your assets, such as cash, stocks, bonds, or other valuable items.
* **Liabilities** (`liabilities`, text, required): List any debts, loans, or financial obligations you have.
* **Cash Flow** (`cashflow`, number, required): This field will help us understand your financial stability. If you have trouble, you can enter "0" and explain the issue in the "notes" field.
* **Risk Assessment** (`risk_assessment`, select one, required): Choose "Yes" if you feel you're at risk of financial instability.
* **Financial Planner** (`financial_planner`, select one, required): Select "Yes" if you think you need help with financial planning.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, note, required): Provide any additional information about next steps you plan to take to improve your financial situation.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, select multiple, required): Provide your contact information so we can reach out to you for any follow-up questions or discussions.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required): Add any other relevant information or comments you'd like to share.
* **Review** (`review`, select one, required): Choose "Yes" if you've completed the review process.
* **Financial Needs Analysis** (`financial_needs_analysis`, select one, required): Select "Yes" if you've completed the financial needs analysis.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Sign and date the form to confirm completion.

## Tips

- Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
- If you're unsure about any field, ask your financial advisor for clarification.
- Take your time and complete the form thoroughly.
- Review your responses carefully before submitting the form.
