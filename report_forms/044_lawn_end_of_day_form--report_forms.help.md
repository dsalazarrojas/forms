# Lawn End Of Day Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to report on daily activities and conditions of a lawn. It helps lawn maintenance staff or individuals responsible for the lawn's upkeep to capture information on activities completed, lawn conditions, issues encountered, equipment used, and additional comments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the "Activities Completed" field with a brief description of the work done on the lawn during the day.
2. Report the current condition of the lawn in the "Lawn Condition" field.
3. Mention any issues encountered in the "Issues Encountered" field.
4. List the equipment used for the tasks in the "Equipment Used" field.
5. Add any additional comments or observations in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Activities Completed** (`lawn_activities_completed`, text, optional): Describe the activities done on the lawn, such as mowing, pruning, or fertilizing.
* **Lawn Condition** (`lawn_condition`, text, optional): Report the current condition of the lawn, including any notable features or changes.
* **Issues Encountered** (`issues_encountered`, text, optional): List any problems or concerns observed on the lawn.
* **Equipment Used** (`equipment_used`, text, optional): List the equipment used for the activities, such as lawn mowers, trimmers, or fertilizers.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any other relevant information or observations about the lawn's condition or activities.
