# Issue Suggestion Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to allow users to suggest new issues and ideas for improvement. By filling out this form, you can provide valuable feedback and help us better understand your needs and concerns. Before submitting, please make sure that the issue you are suggesting is new and not already reported or discussed elsewhere.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your input in the "User input" field. This field is for any additional information you want to share about your suggestion.
2. Select a "Suggestion type" from the options provided. This will help us understand the nature of your issue.
3. Choose the relevant "Category" from the available options. This will help us group similar issues together.
4. Provide a "Description" of the issue you are suggesting, including any relevant details or context.
5. Optionally, if you are certain that your issue is not already reported or discussed, select "Yes" to "Suggestion type" and "No" to "Category".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User input (1, User input, required)**: Enter any additional information you think is relevant to your suggestion.
* **Suggestion type (2, suggestion_type, optional)**: Select "Yes" to suggest a new idea, or "No" to report a problem or bug.
* **Category (3, category, optional)**: Choose a category that best describes the issue you are reporting.
* **Issue Suggestion (4, issue_suggestion, optional)**: Describe the issue you are suggesting in detail.
* **Description (5, description, optional)**: Add any additional details or context to support your suggestion.

## Tips
* Be as descriptive as possible in the "Description" field to help us understand the issue.
* If you are unsure about the category, select all that apply.
* If you are reporting a duplicate issue, please provide the original issue number or a link to the original report.
