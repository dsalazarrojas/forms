# Closing Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The closing checklist is designed to ensure that all tasks and procedures are completed during store closing. This form is used to document the progress and status of various closing tasks, including cash handling, inventory management, cleanliness, and security procedures. The checklist helps management and staff track and review the completeness of closing tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields for each section: Store Name or Location, Closing Date, Closing Time, Manager on Duty, All registers counted and balanced, and Manager Signature.
2. Choose the correct status for each task in the sections: Cash Handling, Inventory, Cleanliness, and Security Procedures.
3. Provide any necessary comments or issues encountered in the issues encountered field.
4. Make sure to complete all fields before submitting the form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Store Name or Location (`store_name`):
**Store Name or Location**: Name of the retail location where the closing checklist is being completed.
### Store ID or Code (`store_number`):
**Store ID or Code**: Optional field for Store ID or code.
### Manager on Duty (`closing_manager`):
**Manager on Duty**: Name of the manager on duty during the closing period.
### Closing Date (`closing_date`):
**Closing Date**: Date of closing.
### Closing Time (`closing_time`):
**Closing Time**: Store closing time.
### Cash Handling (`registers_balanced`):
**All registers counted and balanced**: Select one of the options: Yes, all balanced, Yes, with overages, or Yes, with shortages.
### Cash Discrepancy (`cash_discrepancy`):
**Cash discrepancy amount if any**: Optional field for cash discrepancy amount.
### Cash Handling (`cash_deposited`):
**Cash deposited or secured**: Choose one of the options: Deposited in safe, Ready for bank deposit, or Locked in till.
### Credit Card Transactions (`credit_card_reconciliation`):
**Credit card transactions reconciled**: Choose one of the options: Completed, In progress, or Not completed.
### Inventory and Stock (`section_registers`):
**-- Inventory and Stock --**: This section includes procedures for inventory management.
### Shelves Restocked (`shelves_restocked`):
**Shelves restocked for next day**: Choose one of the options: Completed fully, Partially completed, or Not started.
### Markdown Items (`markdown_items`):
**Markdown items processed**: Choose one of the options: Completed, Pending, or Not needed.
### Inventory Shrinkage (`inventory_shrink`):
**Any unusual inventory shrinkage noted**: Choose one of the options: No issues, Minor discrepancies, Significant discrepancies, or Potential theft.
### Cleanliness and Maintenance (`section_cleanliness`):
**-- Cleanliness and Maintenance --**: This section includes procedures for store cleaning and upkeep.
### Sales Floor Cleaned (`sales_floor_cleaned`):
**Sales floor swept and cleaned**: Choose one of the options: Completed thoroughly, Completed adequately, or Partial.
### Fitting Rooms Checked (`fitting_rooms_checked`):
**Fitting rooms cleared and cleaned**: Choose one of the options: Completed, Completed with issues, or Partial.
### Bathrooms Cleaned (`bathrooms_cleaned`):
**Bathrooms cleaned and restocked**: Choose one of the options: Completed, Completed adequately, or Partial.
### Trash Removed (`trash_removed`):
**All trash removed from store**: Choose one of the options: Completed, Partial, or Not started.
### Security Procedures (`section_security`):
**-- Security Procedures --**: This section includes procedures for store security and access.
### Doors Locked (`doors_locked`):
**All doors locked and secured**: Choose one of the options: Completed, In progress, or Not completed.
### Alarm Set (`alarm_set`):
**Alarm system activated**: Choose one of the options: Activated successfully, In progress, or Not completed.
### Lights Off (`lights_off`):
**All lights turned off**: Choose one of the options: Completed, In progress, or Not completed.
### Issues Encountered (`issues_encountered`):
**Any issues or problems encountered**: Optional field for any issues or problems encountered.
### Manager Signature:
**Manager Signature (print name)**: Manager's signature to acknowledge checklist completion.

Note: This help guide is a comprehensive document that covers all the fields and sections of the closing checklist. It is essential to review this guide carefully to ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
