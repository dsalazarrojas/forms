> ## Accounting Report Generation Request Form - Help Guide
### Purpose
The Accounting Report Generation Request Form is used to request the generation of various accounting reports, such as Profit and Loss, Balance Sheet, Aging Report, and Budget vs Actual reports. Users can also request custom reports with specific parameters. The form helps in streamlining the reporting process by collecting necessary information from the requester, ensuring timely and accurate report generation.

### How To Complete This Form
1.  **Fill out the form completely and accurately**. This will ensure that your report request is processed efficiently and effectively.
2.  **Select the Report Type** from the options provided, such as Profit and Loss, Balance Sheet, Aging Report, Budget vs Actual, or Custom Report.
3.  **Specify the Report Frequency**, whether it's One-time, Monthly Recurring, or Quarterly Recurring.
4.  **Choose the Level of Detail** for your report, such as Summary or Detailed with Transactions.
5.  **Select the Output Format**, including PDF, Excel, CSV, or Online Dashboard.
6.  **Choose the Distribution List** to send the report to.
7.  **Set the Desired Delivery Date** for the report.
8.  **Mark the Internal Approval Status** as required.

### Field-by-Field Explanation
*   *Requester Name*: The name of the person requesting the report, used for identification and contact purposes. **(Required)**
*   *Report Type*: Choose the type of report you need, such as Profit and Loss or Balance Sheet. **(Required)**
*   *Custom Report Details* (Optional): If you selected Custom Report, describe the specific parameters you need for your report.
*   *Reporting Frequency*: Specify how often you want the report generated, such as One-time, Monthly Recurring, or Quarterly Recurring. **(Required)**
*   *Start Date for Data*: The beginning of the report range. **(Required)**
*   *End Date for Data*: The end of the report range. **(Required)**
*   *Level of Detail*: Choose the level of detail for your report, such as Summary or Detailed with Transactions. **(Required)**
*   *Output Format*: Select the format of your report, including PDF, Excel, CSV, or Online Dashboard. **(Required)**
*   *Needed By Date*: The desired delivery date for the report. **(Required)**
*   *Distribution List*: List the email addresses of those who need to receive the report.
*   *Internal Approval Status*: Mark the internal approval status, which can be Pending or Approved.
