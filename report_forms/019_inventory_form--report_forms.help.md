# Inventory Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inventory Form is used to collect and store information about items in the inventory, making it easier to manage and analyze the inventory.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter general information about the item, including the product name, location, and description.
2. Select the item condition (New, Used, Refurbished) and status (Available, Not Available, Discontinued).
3. Choose the item category (Electronics, Clothing, Furniture) and subcategory (optional).
4. Enter the item's price, quantity, and amount.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about the item.
6. Enter the item's URL, email, phone number, and image.
7. Finally, review and submit the form to add the item to the inventory.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (ID 1)**: Enter a brief description of the item. (Text, Optional)
* **Product Name (ID 2)**: Enter the name of the product. (Text, Optional)
* **Product Quantity (ID 3)**: Enter the quantity of the product. (Number, Optional)
* **Product Location (ID 4)**: Enter the location of the product. (Text, Optional)
* **Item Description (ID 5)**: Enter a brief description of the item. (Text, Optional)
* **Item Condition (ID 6)**: Select the condition of the item (New, Used, Refurbished). (Select One, Optional)
* **Item Status (ID 7)**: Select the status of the item (Available, Not Available, Discontinued). (Select Multiple, Optional)
* **Item Category (ID 8)**: Select the category of the item (Electronics, Clothing, Furniture). (Select Multiple, Optional)
* **Item Subcategory (ID 9)**: Enter the subcategory of the item (optional). (Text, Optional)
* **Date Added (ID 10)**: Enter the date the item was added to the inventory. (Date, Optional)
* **Time Added (ID 11)**: Enter the time the item was added to the inventory. (Time, Optional)
* **Note (ID 12)**: Add any additional notes or comments about the item. (Note, Optional)
* **Email (ID 13)**: Enter the contact email of the item's owner. (Email, Optional)
* **Phone (ID 14)**: Enter the contact phone number of the item's owner. (Text, Optional)
* **Item Image (ID 15)**: Add an image of the item. (Note, Optional)
* **Item URL (ID 16)**: Enter the URL of the item. (Text, Optional)
* **Item Price (ID 17)**: Enter the price of the item. (Number, Optional)
* **Item Quantity (ID 18)**: Enter the quantity of the item. (Number, Optional)
* **Item Amount (ID 19)**: Enter the amount of the item. (Number, Optional)
* **Item Location (ID 20)**: Enter the location of the item. (Text, Optional)
* **Item Status 2 (ID 21)**: Select the status of the item (Available, Not Available, Discontinued). (Select One, Optional)
* **Item Condition 2 (ID 22)**: Select the condition of the item (New, Used, Refurbished). (Select One, Optional)
* **Item Subcategory 2 (ID 23)**: Enter the subcategory of the item (optional). (Text, Optional)
* **Item Category 2 (ID 24)**: Select the category of the item (Electronics, Clothing, Furniture). (Select Multiple, Optional)
* **Item Note (ID 25)**: Add any additional notes or comments about the item. (Note, Optional)

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Use a clear and concise description of the item.
* Use the correct category and subcategory for the item.
* Double-check the item's condition and status.
* Use the correct price and quantity for the item.
* Add any additional notes or comments that may be relevant to the item.
