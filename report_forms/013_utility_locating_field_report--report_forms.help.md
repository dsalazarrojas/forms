# Utility Locating Field Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your experiences in the field, specifically during utility locating field activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "what_is_your_name" field. This will help us know who is submitting the report.
2. Provide the name of the company you represent in the "company_name" field.
3. Enter your contact number in the "contact_number" field so we can reach out to you with follow-up questions.
4. Enter your email address in the "email" field to enable us to contact you.
5. Describe the location where you found the utility in the "location" field.
6. Enter the date and time you found the utility in the "date_found" and "time_found" fields respectively.
7. Note down the coordinates of the location in the "location_coordinates" field.
8. Select the type of utility you found from the options in the "utility_type" field.
9. Add any additional comments or notes about your experience in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **what_is_your_name** (text, required/optional): Enter your name to help us identify you as the person submitting the report.
* **company_name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the company you represent.
* **contact_number** (text, required/optional): Enter your contact number so we can reach out to you for follow-up.
* **email** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **location** (text, required/optional): Describe the location where you found the utility.
* **date_found** (date, required/optional): Enter the date you found the utility.
* **time_found** (time, required/optional): Enter the time you found the utility.
* **location_coordinates** (text, required/optional): Note down the coordinates of the location where you found the utility.
* **utility_type** (select_one, required/optional): Select the type of utility you found from the options provided.
* **notes** (note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your experience.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you only complete this form if you have actual experience in the field and not if you are just duplicating information.
- If you have any doubts or concerns while filling this form, do not hesitate to contact us for assistance.
