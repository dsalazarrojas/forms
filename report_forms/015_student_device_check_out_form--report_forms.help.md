# Student Device Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Device Check Out Form is used to track and manage student devices, including their current status, check-out and return dates, and any issues that may arise during this process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter the student's first name.
3. Enter the student's last name.
4. Enter the student's ID number.
5. Select the device type (if applicable).
6. Enter the device's serial number.
7. Specify the current status of the device (Checked Out, Available, Lost, or Stolen).
8. Choose the check-out date (if applicable).
9. Choose the return date (if applicable).
10. Enter the teacher's first name (if applicable).
11. Enter the teacher's last name (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student First Name**: The first name of the student who checked out the device.
* **Student Last Name**: The last name of the student who checked out the device.
* **Student ID**: A unique identifying number for the student.
* **Device Type**: The type of device being checked out (e.g., laptop, tablet, etc.). 
* **Device Serial Number**: A unique identifier for the device, usually found on the device itself or in the device's documentation.
* **Device Status**: Select the current status of the device (Checked Out, Available, Lost, or Stolen).
* **Check Out Date**: The date the device was checked out by the student.
* **Return Date**: The date the device is due to be returned.
* **Teacher First Name**: The first name of the teacher responsible for the device.
* **Teacher Last Name**: The last name of the teacher responsible for the device.

## Tips
* Always ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If a device is lost or stolen, select the corresponding status to indicate this to the relevant parties.
* Be as accurate as possible when filling out the form to ensure that device tracking and management are effective.
