# Vessel Daily Log Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Vessel Daily Log Form is used to log information about a vessel's daily activities. This form captures basic data about the vessel's movements, crew status, and remarks or comments.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Vessel ID**: Enter a unique identifier for the vessel.
2. **Date**: Record the date of the vessel's activity.
3. **Vessel Name**: Enter the name of the vessel.
4. **Departure Time**: Record the time the vessel departed.
5. **Arrival Time**: Record the time the vessel arrived.
6. **Crew**: Indicate if the crew is present or not.
7. **Remarks**: Enter any additional comments or remarks about the vessel's activity.
8. **Latitude**: Record the latitude of the vessel's location.
9. **Longitude**: Record the longitude of the vessel's location.
10. **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the vessel's activity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vessel ID**: Enter a unique identifier for the vessel. This is a required field for tracking the vessel's activities.
* **Date**: Record the date of the vessel's activity. This field is not required.
* **Vessel Name**: Enter the name of the vessel. This field is not required.
* **Departure Time**: Record the time the vessel departed. This field is not required.
* **Arrival Time**: Record the time the vessel arrived. This field is not required.
* **Crew**: Indicate if the crew is present or not. This field is not required.
* **Remarks**: Enter any additional comments or remarks about the vessel's activity. This field is not required.
* **Latitude**: Record the latitude of the vessel's location. This field is not required.
* **Longitude**: Record the longitude of the vessel's location. This field is not required.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the vessel's activity. This field is not required.
