# Student Driver Progress Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Student Driver Progress Report Form is used to record and track student progress in learning to drive. This form helps instructors to evaluate student progress and provide feedback.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter the student's name and grade level.
3. Select the student's skills from the list provided.
4. Provide feedback and comments for the student.
5. Enter the evaluation date and time.
6. Enter the student's progress status.
7. Enter the assigned instructor's name and email.
8. Enter the assigned instructor's comments.
9. Enter the form ID and version.
10. Enter the submitted date and time.
11. Enter the submitted IP address.
12. Enter the submitted user.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's name as it appears on their record.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, number, required): Enter the student's current grade level.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the assigned instructor.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required): Enter any feedback or comments for the student.
* **Student Comments** (`student_comments`, text, required): Enter any comments from the student.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the evaluation.
* **Instructor Comments** (`instructor_comments`, text, required): Enter any comments from the instructor.
* **Student Skills** (`student_skills`, select_one, required): Select the skills that the student has demonstrated.
*   *Defensive Driving*
*   *Skidpan*
*   *Vehicle Control*
*   *Turn Signals*
*   *Backing*
*   *Parallel Parking*
*   *Three Point Turns*
*   *Merging*
*   *Intersecting*
*   *Parking*
*   *Right Turns*
*   *Night Driving*
*   *Left Turns*
*   *Forward*
*   *Reverse*
*   **Other Skills** (`student_skills_other`, text, required): Enter any other skills not listed above.
* **Student Progress Status** (`student_progress_status`, select_one, required): Enter the student's progress status.
*   *Not Started*
*   *In Progress*
*   *Complete*
* **Assigned Instructor** (`assigned_instructor`, text, required): Enter the name of the assigned instructor.
* **Assigned Instructor Email** (`assigned_instructor_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the assigned instructor.
* **Assigned Instructor Phone** (`assigned_instructor_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the assigned instructor.
* **Assigned Instructor Comments** (`assigned_instructor_comments`, text, required): Enter any comments from the assigned instructor.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, required): Enter a unique identifier for the form.
* **Form Version** (`form_version`, number, required): Enter the version of the form.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the user who submitted the form.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, required): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`, time, required): Enter the time the form was submitted.
* **Submitted IP** (`submitted_ip`, text, required): Enter the IP address of the user who submitted the form.
* **Submitted User** (`submitted_user`, text, required): Enter the user who submitted the form.

Note: The fields marked as required must be filled out.
