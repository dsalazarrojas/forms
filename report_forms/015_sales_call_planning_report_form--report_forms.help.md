# Sales Call Planning Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a structured approach to planning and recording sales calls. It helps in gathering essential details about each call, including customer information, call schedule, and outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form efficiently, follow these steps:
1. Enter the `Sales Call Date` of the scheduled or conducted call.
2. Input the `Sales Call Time` of the call.
3. Provide the name and contact information of the `Customer`.
4. Mention the `Product` or services discussed during the call.
5. List the `Contact Person` and their role or department.
6. Specify the `Territory` where the call was conducted.
7. Enter any `Notes` about the call, including the discussion, decisions, and next steps.
8. If applicable, mention the `Sales Manager` involved in the call.
9. Record the `Next Call Date` if scheduled.
10. Identify the `Assigned Sales Person` and `Assigned Sales Team` responsible for the call.
11. Enter the `Sales Call Duration` if applicable.
12. Add `Sales Call Planning Notes` to document any specific details about the call.
13. Provide the `Customer Email`, `Customer Phone`, and `Customer Address` if available.
14. Enter any `Customer Notes` about the call.
15. Mention the `Customer Website` and `Customer Fax` if applicable.
16. If required, add `Other Notes` about the call.
17. Choose the `Sales Call Outcome` from the options: `Correct`, `Incorrect`, `Cancelled`, or `Other`.
18. Provide additional `Sales Call Outcome Notes` to elaborate on the outcome.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Sales Call Planning Report Form
* **Sales Call Date**: Enter the date of the scheduled or conducted sales call.
* **Sales Call Time**: Input the start time of the call.
* **Customer**: Enter the customer's name, email, phone number, and address.
* **Product**: Mention the product or services discussed during the call.
* **Contact Person**: List the contact person's name and role or department.
* **Territory**: Specify the territory where the call was conducted.
* **Notes**: Add any additional information about the call, including discussion, decisions, and next steps.
* **Sales Manager**: If applicable, mention the sales manager involved in the call.
* **Next Call Date**: Record the date of the next call if scheduled.
* **Assigned Sales Person**: Identify the sales person assigned to the call.
* **Assigned Sales Team**: Specify the sales team assigned to the call.
* **Sales Call Duration**: Enter the duration of the call if applicable.
* **Sales Call Planning Notes**: Add specific details about the call planning process.
* **Customer Email**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone**: Input the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Address**: Enter the customer's address.
* **Customer Notes**: Add any notes about the customer.
* **Customer Website**: Mention the customer's website (if available).
* **Customer Fax**: Input the customer's fax number.
* **Other**: Add any other information about the call.
* **Other Notes**: Add any additional notes about the call.
* **Sales Call Outcome**: Choose the outcome of the call from the options: Correct, Incorrect, Cancelled, or Other.
* **Sales Call Outcome Notes**: Elaborate on the outcome of the call if needed.
