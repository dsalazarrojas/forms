# Lost And Found Pet Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to report a lost pet and gather essential information for the reunion process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately as much as possible.
2. Enter the reason you are submitting this form.
3. Provide the date and time the pet went missing.
4. Choose the type of pet and its breed.
5. Enter the city and street where the pet was last seen.
6. Enter the pet's name and other identifying features (gender, color, and age).
7. Enter the contact information (name, phone number, and email) of the person or organization that found/owns the pet.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Why are you submitting this form?**: This field is the reason you are submitting this form. Please enter the reason for reporting the lost pet.
* **Date Lost**: Enter the date and time the pet went missing.
* **Type of Pet**: Choose the type of pet that was lost (cat, dog, or other).
* **Breed of Pet**: Enter the breed of the pet (optional).
* **City**: Enter the city where the pet was last seen.
* **Street**: Enter the street where the pet was last seen.
* **Pet's Name**: Enter the pet's name.
* **Gender**: Choose the gender of the pet (male, female, or unknown).
* **Color of Pet**: Enter the color of the pet (optional).
* **Age of Pet**: Enter the age of the pet.
* **Size of Pet**: Enter the size of the pet (optional).
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person or organization that found/owns the pet.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person or organization that found/owns the pet.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email of the person or organization that found/owns the pet.
* **Upload Pictures**: Decide whether to upload pictures of the pet (yes or no).

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and detailed information about the pet and its loss.
* Upload pictures of the pet if possible to help identify it.
* Use a specific format for date and time entry (e.g., MM/DD/YY HH:MM:SS).
* Check and correct any mistakes before submitting the form.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
