# Daily Expense Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Expense Report Form is used to document and report expenses incurred daily by employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you are using this form for its intended purpose and do not duplicate it for tasks or expenses already logged elsewhere.
2. Fill out each field as prompted, using the provided explanations below for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Date of Service**: Enter the date you incurred the expenses you are reporting.
* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your personnel file or ID card.
* **Service Type**: Indicate whether your daily service includes "Yes" or "No" service.
* **Expense Item**: List the items or services you are claiming as expenses. Select "Yes" for each applicable item.
* **Expense Amount**: Enter the total amount of your expenses, including taxes and other fees.
* **Currency**: Indicate whether your expenses are in US dollars or some other currency. Select "Yes" for the currency used.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or explanations about your expenses.
* **Email**: Your work email address.
* **Phone Number**: Your work phone number.

Please note that all fields are optional, but providing all necessary information ensures a complete and accurate expense report.
