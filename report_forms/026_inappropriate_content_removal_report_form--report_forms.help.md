# Inappropriate Content Removal Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inappropriate Content Removal Report Form is used to report content that is deemed as inappropriate or offensive. It helps in the removal of such content and to identify the user who submitted the report.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow the steps below:

- **Title**: Enter a brief title for the report, describing the content you wish to report. This should be a clear and concise title that describes the content without giving away too much information.
- **Description**: Provide a brief description of the content. This should be a detailed explanation of the content you are reporting.
- **Category**: Select the type of content you are reporting. Options include "Inappropriate User Post", "Inappropriate User", "Inappropriate Content", or "Other".
- **Content**: Provide a detailed description of the content you are reporting. This should be a descriptive field where you can provide more information about the content.
- **URL**: If the reported content has a URL, provide it here.
- **Date**: Enter the date you first encountered the content.
- **Submitted By**: Enter your name and your email address or phone number (both are optional). This will help us identify who submitted the report.
- **Assigned To**: Select who this content should be reviewed by. You can select multiple users.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_title**: A brief title for the report, describing the content you wish to report.
* **Description**: A detailed description of the content you are reporting.
* **Category**: The type of content you are reporting.
* **Content**: A descriptive field where you can provide more information about the content.
* **URL**: The URL of the content you are reporting.
* **Date**: The date you first encountered the content.
* **Submitted By**: Your name and email address or phone number, which helps us identify who submitted the report.
* **Assigned To**: The user(s) that this content should be reviewed by.

## Tips

- Always fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
- Ensure that you do not misuse this form for malicious purposes.
- If you are unsure about a field, you can contact your supervisor or HR department for assistance.
