# Incident Investigation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Incident Investigation Form is used to document and investigate incidents in a structured and detailed manner.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the requested information about the incident.
2. Ensure that each field is filled out according to its label.
3. For select fields, choose the most relevant option that describes the situation.
4. For fields with hints, use them as guidance to provide clear and concise information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, `text`, optional):
	+ This field is for collecting any other relevant information about the incident.
	+ Please provide a brief description of the incident, including any relevant details.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, `date`, optional):
	+ Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, `time`, optional):
	+ Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, `text`, optional):
	+ This field is for collecting the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, `text`, optional):
	+ This field is for collecting a detailed description of the incident.
* **Witness Name** (`witness_name`, `text`, optional):
	+ If a witness was present, enter their name.
* **Witness Email** (`witness_email`, `email`, optional):
	+ If a witness was present, enter their email address.
* **Witness Phone** (`witness_phone`, `text`, optional):
	+ If a witness was present, enter their phone number.
* **Investigation Date** (`investigation_date`, `date`, optional):
	+ Enter the date the investigation started.
* **Investigation Time** (`investigation_time`, `time`, optional):
	+ Enter the time the investigation started.
* **Investigation Location** (`investigation_location`, `text`, optional):
	+ This field is for collecting the location of the investigation.
* **Safety Measures** (`safety_measures`, `select_multiple`, required):
	+ Select all relevant safety measures applied during the investigation.
* **Root Cause** (`root_cause`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Choose the root cause of the incident.
* **Prevention Plan** (`prevention_plan`, `text`, optional):
	+ Describe the measures taken to prevent similar incidents in the future.
* **Incident Reported By** (`incident_reported_by`, `text`, optional):
	+ If the incident was reported by someone, enter their name.
* **Incident Reported Date** (`incident_reporting_date`, `date`, optional):
	+ Enter the date the incident was reported.
* **Investigation Status** (`investigation_status`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Choose the status of the investigation.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, `text`, optional):
	+ This field is for collecting follow-up information.
* **Follow Up Date** (`follow_up_date`, `date`, optional):
	+ Enter the date of the follow-up.
* **Follow Up Time** (`follow_up_time`, `time`, optional):
	+ Enter the time of the follow-up.
* **Investigation Duration** (`investigation_duration`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter the duration of the investigation.
* **Investigation Notes** (`investigation_notes`, `text`, optional):
	+ This field is for collecting any notes about the investigation.
* **Witness Details** (`witness_details`, `text`, optional):
	+ This field is for collecting any information about the witness.
* **Incident ID** (`incident_id`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter the incident ID.
* **Incident Number** (`incident_number`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter the incident number.
