# Daily Vehicle Inspection Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Vehicle Inspection Report form is used to collect information about the current state of a vehicle during an inspection. The form will help inspectors report on the vehicle's status and condition, ensuring accurate records are kept up-to-date.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **Enter the Vehicle Registration Number**: Please enter the vehicle's registration number in the Vehicle Registration Number field. This is a required field.
2. **Enter the Inspector's Name**: Enter the name of the inspector who performed the inspection in the Inspector Name field. This is a required field.
3. **Enter Vehicle Details**: Fill in the Vehicle Make, Vehicle Year, and Engine Number fields with the relevant information about the vehicle.
4. **Enter Vehicle Condition**: Select the correct option from the Vehicle Condition field to describe the vehicle's current condition. This field is a multiple choice question with options: New, Used, Fair, Poor.
5. **Enter Inspection Details**: Enter the Date of Inspection and Time of Inspection fields with the details of the inspection. This field is for recording the date and time of the inspection.
6. **Enter Inspection Location**: Enter the Location of Inspection field with the location where the inspection was performed.
7. **Add Comments**: If you have any comments or additional information about the vehicle, enter it in the Comments field. This field is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Registration Number**: Enter the vehicle's registration number as recorded on the vehicle's documentation.
* **Inspector Name**: Enter the name of the person who performed the inspection.
* **Vehicle Make**: Enter the make of the vehicle, e.g. Ford, Toyota, Honda, etc.
* **Vehicle Year**: Enter the year the vehicle was manufactured.
* **Engine Number**: Enter the engine number as recorded on the vehicle's documentation.
* **Mileage**: Enter the current mileage of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Condition**: Select the option that best describes the vehicle's current condition.
* **Date of Inspection**: Enter the date the inspection was performed.
* **Time of Inspection**: Enter the time the inspection was performed.
* **Location of Inspection**: Enter the location where the inspection was performed.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information about the vehicle that may be relevant to the inspection.
