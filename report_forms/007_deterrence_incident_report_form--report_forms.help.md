# Deterrence Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about incidents that require attention from the organization. Fill out this form to report incidents, including details, location, and timestamp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of the incident in the **description** field.
2. Provide details about the incident in the **incident_details** field.
3. Enter the date and time of the incident in the **date** and **time** fields, respectively.
4. Specify the location where the incident occurred in the **location** field.
5. Enter your name and title or position as the incident reporter in the **incident_reporter** field.
