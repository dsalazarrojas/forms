# Restaurant Operating Expense Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Restaurant Operating Expense Claim Form is a document that allows restaurant businesses to claim operating expenses related to their day-to-day activities. This form is designed to gather necessary information to process claims efficiently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Business Name** field with the name of your restaurant business.
2. Select the **Claim Period** for which you are claiming expenses.
3. Enter the **Total Claimed** amount of expenses you are submitting.
4. Choose the types of **Restaurant Expenses** you are claiming (e.g., Rent, Food and Beverage, Labor, etc.).
5. Indicate if you have **Supporting Documents** to attach.
6. If claiming **Other Expenses**, fill in the relevant details.
7. Review your information for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claim Form** (Page 1):
	+ This is the title of the form. Enter your business name as it appears on your license.
* **Date Claimed** (Page 2):
	+ Enter the date you are submitting this claim.
* **Business Name** (Page 2):
	+ Enter the name of your restaurant business (as it appears on your license).
* **Claim Period** (Page 3):
	+ Select the claim period for which you are submitting this claim (e.g., quarterly or annually).
* **Total Claimed** (Page 3):
	+ Enter the total amount you are claiming for expenses.
* **Restaurant Expenses** (Page 4):
	+ Select all applicable types of restaurant expenses you are claiming (e.g., Rent, Food and Beverage, Labor, etc.).
* **Supporting Documents** (Page 4):
	+ Check "True" if you have supporting documents for your claim, and "False" otherwise.
* **Other Expenses** (Page 5):
	+ Enter any additional expenses not listed in the "Restaurant Expenses" field.
* **Supporting Documents** (Page 5):
	+ Attach any documents supporting your claim.
