# School Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a student's school attendance and performance. The purpose of this form is to provide an accurate and comprehensive report of the student's situation, which can be used by teachers, administrators, or other relevant parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "school_name" field.
2. Enter the student's ID number in the "student_id" field.
3. Enter the teacher's name in the "teacher name" field.
4. Select the subject that the student is reporting on from the "subject" field.
5. Enter the student's score or progress in the "score" field.
6. Enter the date of the report in the "date" field.
7. Enter the time of the report in the "time" field.
8. Enter any additional note or comment in the "note" field.
9. Enter the teacher's email address in the "email" field (optional).
10. Enter the teacher's phone number in the "phone" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): This field is where you enter the name of the school where the student attends.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, optional): This field is where you enter the student's ID number.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): This field is where you enter the teacher's name who is reporting on the student.
* **Subject** (`subject`, select_one, optional): This field allows you to select the subject that the student is being reported on.
	+ Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
* **Score** (`score`, number, optional): This field is where you enter the student's score or progress.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): This field is where you enter the date of the report.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): This field is where you enter the time of the report.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): This field is where you enter any additional note or comment.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This field is where you enter the teacher's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This field is where you enter the teacher's phone number.
