# Fire Investigation Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Investigation Report Form is designed to gather essential information about a fire incident. This form is used to document the details of the incident, including the date and time it occurred, the location, and any information about the suspect(s) involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Describe the incident in the "What happened" field.
2.  Provide the date and time of the incident in the "When did it happen" field.
3.  Specify the location of the incident in the "Where did it happen" field.
4.  Provide details about the incident in the "What happened details" field.
5.  If available, provide the suspect's name and any relevant identifying information in the "Suspect name", "Suspect description", "Suspect address", "Suspect phone", "Suspect relationship", "Suspect demeanor", "Suspect clothing", and "Suspect vehicle" fields.
6.  Take note of any additional information or comments about the suspect in the "Suspect notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What happened** (incident_description): A brief description of the incident, including the circumstances leading up to and following the incident.
*   **When did it happen** (incident_date): The date and time of the incident.
*   **What time** (incident_time): The time of the incident.
*   **Where did it happen** (incident_location): The location of the incident.
*   **What happened details** (incident_description_details): Additional details about the incident, such as any notable events or circumstances surrounding it.
*   **Suspect name** (suspect_name): The name of the suspect.
*   **Suspect description** (suspect_description): A description of the suspect, including any identifying features such as height, hair color, etc.
*   **Suspect address** (suspect_address): The suspect's address.
*   **Suspect phone** (suspect_phone): The suspect's phone number.
*   **Relationship to the suspect** (suspect_relationship): The relationship between the suspect and the victim or others involved.
*   **Suspect demeanor** (suspect_demeanor): A description of the suspect's behavior or attitude.
*   **Suspect clothing** (suspect_clothing): A description of the suspect's clothing or attire.
*   **Suspect vehicle** (suspect_vehicle): The suspect's vehicle make and model.
*   **Suspect notes** (suspect_notes): Any additional information or comments about the suspect.

## Tips
Please complete the form as thoroughly and accurately as possible to support the investigation process. If you're unsure about any field, refer to the incident report guidelines or seek guidance from your supervisor.
