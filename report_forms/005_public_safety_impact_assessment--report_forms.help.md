# Public Safety Impact Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Safety Impact Assessment form is used to evaluate and document the potential risks and consequences of a project that may impact public safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with relevant information about your project.
2. Be as specific as possible when providing details.
3. Review and update your answers as necessary throughout the project's lifecycle.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Details** (`project_details`): Enter a brief description of the project.
* **Project Risks** (`project_risks`): List any identified risks related to the project.
* **Mitigation Measures** (`mitigation_measures`): Describe the measures taken to mitigate the identified risks.
* **Submit** (`submit`): Click this button to submit your completed form.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`): Indicate the current status of the project.
* **Risk Level** (`risk_level`): Select the level of risk associated with the project (High, Low).
* **Risk Probability** (`risk_probability`): Enter the probability of the risk occurrence (e.g., 1-10, 1-100, etc.).
* **Risk Impact** (`risk_impact`): Describe the potential impact of the risk on public safety.
* **Risk Frequency** (`risk_frequency`): Indicate how often the risk is likely to occur.
* **Risk Description** (`risk_description`): Provide a detailed description of the risk.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`): Enter the name of the project.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`): Enter the location(s) where the project will take place.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`): Enter a brief description of the project.
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`): Enter the start date of the project.
* **Project End Date** (`project_end_date`): Enter the end date of the project.

## Tips
* Review and update your answers as necessary throughout the project's lifecycle.
* Consult with project managers or risk management specialists if you're unsure about any section.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Keep in mind that this form is for public safety impact assessment only, and do not use it for general project information or recurring issues.
