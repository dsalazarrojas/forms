This form is intended for users to report damage to their property. The form collects information about the property, the damage description, and related contact information. The purpose of the form is to provide a structured way for users to report damage to their properties, which can be used for insurance claims or other purposes. To avoid duplication, users should only submit one report per property per reportable event. If a user needs to report damage to a property multiple times, they should not resubmit the same form multiple times.

# Property Damage Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Property Damage Report Form is designed to collect information about property damage. It will help you submit a report for insurance claims, repair work, or other purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select "Yes" or "No" to answer if the property has been damaged.
2. Provide the required information about the property, including its description and contact information.
3. Describe the damage in detail, including any relevant details about what happened and what was affected.
4. Enter the date the damage occurred.
5. Enter the time the damage occurred.
6. Enter the location of the damaged property.
7. Enter the contact information for the insurance company, if applicable.
8. Check if an insurance agent was present at the scene of the damage.
9. Enter the policy number, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Form (<name>report_form</name>, <type>select_one</type>, required/false>):** 
  Select "Yes" if the property has been damaged, otherwise select "No".
* **Property Info (<name>property_info</name>, <type>text</type>, required/false>):** 
  Enter a detailed description of the property that was damaged.
* **Damage Description (<name>damage_description</name>, <type>text</type>, required/false>):** 
  Provide a detailed description of the damage, including any relevant details about what happened and what was affected.
* **Date (<name>property_damage_report_form_date</name>, <type>date</type>, required/false>):** 
  Enter the date the damage occurred.
* **Time (<name>property_damage_report_form_time</name>, <type>time</type>, required/false>):** 
  Enter the time the damage occurred.
* **Location (<name>property_damage_report_form_location</name>, <type>text</type>, required/false>):** 
  Enter the location of the damaged property.
* **Contact Info (<name>property_damage_report_form_contact_info</name>, <type>text</type>, required/false>):** 
  Enter contact information for the property owner.
* **Insurance Company (<name>property_damage_report_form_insurance_company</name>, <type>text</type>, required/false>):** 
  Enter the name of the insurance company for the damaged property.
* **Agent (<name>property_damage_report_form_agent</name>, <type>select_one</type>, required/false>):** 
  Select "Yes" if an insurance agent was present at the scene of the damage, otherwise select "No".
* **Policy Number (<name>property_damage_report_form_policy_number</name>, <type>text</type>, required/false>):** 
  Enter the policy number for the damaged property.

Note: The fields with "required/false" are not actually required fields in the original YAML, but they should be treated as such in the help guide for the sake of clarity and usability.
