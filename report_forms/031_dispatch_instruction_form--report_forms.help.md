# Dispatch Instruction Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Dispatch Instruction Form is used to document the details of each dispatch instruction, including items received, delivery date, delivery time, delivery note, customer contact information, and order details. This form ensures that all relevant information is accurately recorded, enabling efficient and effective communication with customers and resolution of any issues that may arise during delivery.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the relevant options for each field from the dropdown menu, if available.
2. Enter the customer's name, items received, order ID, delivery date, delivery time, delivery note, customer phone number, email, and address in the corresponding fields.
3. Double-check that all required fields are filled in accurately and correctly.
4. Review the form carefully before submission to ensure that all necessary information is provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dispatch Instruction Form 1**: Select the relevant options for this dispatch instruction from the dropdown menu.
* **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's name accurately and clearly.
* **Items Received**: Record the items received from the customer or the items being dispatched.
* **Order ID**: Enter the order ID associated with the dispatch instruction.
* **Delivery Date**: Select the date of the delivery.
* **Delivery Time**: Select the time of the delivery.
* **Delivery Note**: Record any relevant notes about the delivery.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number for easy contact.
* **Customer Email**: Enter the customer's email for efficient communication.
* **Customer Address**: Enter the customer's address for accurate delivery location.
