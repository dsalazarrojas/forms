# Report Fake Business Profile - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report fake or suspicious business profiles for further investigation and action.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
- Fill in the "Profile Picture" and "Business Name" fields with relevant information about the fake business profile.
- Select the correct "Reason for Reporting" and "Fake Business Profile" options, depending on the specific nature of the report.
- Enter the "Reason for Reporting" and "Reporting Reason" fields with a clear explanation of why this profile is fake or suspicious.
- Choose the correct "Social Media" options, if applicable.
- Select the correct "Reporting Date" and "Reporting Time" fields to provide the date and time of the report.
- Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Header**: This field is a placeholder for a title or a header for the form.
* **Profile Picture** (`profile_picture`, text, required): Upload an image or enter a URL for the profile picture that you believe is fake or suspicious.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the business that you believe is fake or suspicious.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the business that you believe is fake or suspicious.
* **Website** (`website`, text, required): Enter the website of the business that you believe is fake or suspicious.
* **Social Media** (`social_media`, select_one, required): Select the social media platform where the fake or suspicious business profile exists (e.g., Facebook, Instagram, Twitter).
* **Other Social Media** (`other_social_media`, text, required): Enter any other social media platforms where the fake or suspicious business profile exists.
* **Reason for Reporting** (`reason_for_reporting`, text, required): Enter a clear explanation of why you believe the business profile is fake or suspicious.
* **Reporting Reason** (`reporting_reason`, select_one, required): Select the reason for reporting the fake or suspicious business profile (e.g., Scam, Suspicious Activity, Other).
* **Fake Business Profile** (`fake_business_profile`, select_multiple, required): Select all the characteristics that apply to the fake business profile (e.g., Fake Address, Suspicious Name, Suspicious Logo).
* **Reporting Date** (`reporting_date`, date, required): Enter the date you became aware of the fake or suspicious business profile.
* **Reporting Time** (`reporting_time`, time, required): Enter the time you became aware of the fake or suspicious business profile.
* **Fake Business Profile Options**: Select all the options that apply to the fake business profile.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click to submit the report.

**Tips:**
- Be as specific as possible when filling in the fields.
- Make sure to select all relevant options that apply to the fake or suspicious business profile.
- Enter a clear and concise explanation for the "Reason for Reporting" and "Reporting Reason" fields.
- Double-check the form for accuracy before submitting the report.
