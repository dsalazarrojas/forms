# Air Conditioning Daily Requisition Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to record a technician's daily report on their work with air conditioning systems. It collects information on tasks completed, materials used, hours spent on site, and any issues or defects found during the work. The report is used to track progress and ensure accurate record-keeping.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your name and title as the technician completing the report.
2. Select the date of the work completed.
3. Enter the site location or address where the work was done.
4. List the tasks you completed during the day.
5. If applicable, describe any materials used and their quantities.
6. Enter the total hours spent on site.
7. If any issues or defects were found, describe them in detail.
8. Indicate if additional visits are needed.
9. Enter any comments or approvals from your supervisor.
10. Finally, confirm that the report is complete and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Technician name** (text, required)
* Enter your name as the technician completing the report.

### 2. **Report date** (date, required)
* Select the date of the work completed.

### 3. **Site location** (text, required)
* Enter the address or site identifier where the work was done.

### 4. **Tasks completed today** (text, required)
* Provide a brief list of tasks you completed during the day.

### 5. **Materials used** (text, optional)
* Describe any materials or parts used during the work. If none were used, you can leave this field blank.

### 6. **Hours worked today** (number, optional)
* Enter the total hours spent on site, if applicable.

### 7. **Issues found during work** (text, optional)
* Describe any unexpected problems or defects found during the work.

### 8. **Follow up required** (select_one, required)
* Indicate whether additional visits are needed. Choose 'True' or 'False'.

### 9. **Supervisor notes** (text, optional)
* Enter any comments or approvals from your supervisor.

### 10. **Acknowledgement of report** (note, required)
* Confirm that the report is complete and accurate by typing your signature or comments.

### 11. **Supervisor comments** (text, optional)
* Enter any additional comments or remarks from your supervisor, if any.

Note: This field is a note field that requires a signature or comments to be entered, it's not a text field with options.
