# credit_agency_dispute_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The credit_agency_dispute_form is used to report a dispute related to a credit agency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details: **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Address** are required fields that will help us identify you and verify your account.
2. Select the **Credit Agency** involved in the dispute.
3. Choose the **Report Type** that best describes your dispute.
4. If applicable, enter your **Credit Score** and **Account Number**.
5. Indicate whether the dispute is related to a **Reason for Dispute**.
6. Upload a **Supporting Document** if necessary.
7. Add any **Additional Comments** regarding the dispute.
8. Select whether the dispute has been **Dispute Agreed** by the credit agency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Your first name is required to identify you.
* **Last Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Your last name is required to identify you.
* **Address** (`name`, `text`, required): Your address is required to verify your account.
* **Credit Agency** (`name`, `text`, required): The credit agency involved in the dispute.
* **Report Type** (`name`, `text`, required): Choose the type of dispute that best describes the situation.
* **Credit Score** (`name`, `text`, optional): If you want to include your credit score in the report.
* **Account Number** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your account number with the credit agency.
* **Reason for Dispute** (`name`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' if the dispute is related to this reason, 'No' otherwise.
* **Supporting Document** (`name`, `text`, required): Attach any relevant documents that support your dispute claim.
* **Additional Comments** (`name`, `text`, required): Add any additional information about the dispute.
* **Dispute Agreed** (`name`, `select_one`, required): Select 'True' if the credit agency has agreed to the dispute, 'False' otherwise.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you have any supporting documents, please attach them to the form.
* If you have any additional comments, please provide them in the space provided.
* Make sure to select the correct options for **Reason for Dispute** and **Dispute Agreed**.
