# Predictive Maintenance Insight Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information on the predictive maintenance status of assets to provide insights for maintenance planning and scheduling.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form fields carefully and fill in all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for asset selection if the asset requires maintenance.
3. Choose the maintenance frequency of the selected asset by selecting "Yes" or "No" for multiple frequencies.
4. Enter the relevant date for the asset's maintenance history.
5. Input the time of the last maintenance or inspection.
6. Provide notes about the asset's condition, status, or any other relevant information.
7. Enter the contact email address for further communication.
8. (Optional) Enter your phone number for additional communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **select_asset (Yes/No)**: Indicates whether the asset requires maintenance.
* **report_frequency (Yes/No)**: Selects the maintenance frequencies for the asset (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, monthly).
* **date_field**: Enter the relevant date for the asset's maintenance history.
* **time_field**: Enter the time of the last maintenance or inspection.
* **asset_notes**: Provide notes about the asset's condition, status, or any other relevant information.
* **email**: Enter the contact email address for further communication. 
* **phone**: (Optional) Enter your phone number for additional communication.
