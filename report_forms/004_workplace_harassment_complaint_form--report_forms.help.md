# Workplace Harassment Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information about workplace harassment incidents. Please complete this form to report incidents of harassment, bullying, or related behaviors that affect your work environment. Your report will help us ensure a safe and respectful workplace.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is required.
2. Please provide a detailed report of the incident, including as much information as you know.
3. Provide your contact information so we can follow up with you, if necessary.
4. If you're reporting a harassment incident involving multiple witnesses, please provide details about each witness.
5. Make sure to include the perpetrator's employer, as this information is crucial for our investigation.
6. If you've witnessed the incident, please provide your details as a witness.
7. If you're reporting on behalf of someone else, please ensure you have their consent.
8. If you have additional information or supporting evidence, please attach it to this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Information** (`report_information`, text, optional): Provide a detailed report of the incident, including what happened, where, when, and how it affected you.
* **Reporter Details** (`reporter_details`, text, optional): Please provide your name, title, department, and contact information (email, phone number, or extension).
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, text, optional): Provide as much information as you know about the incident, including what happened, why, and any other relevant details.
* **Perpetrator Details** (`perpetrator_details`, text, optional): Provide the name, title, department, and contact information (email, phone number, or extension) of the person you are reporting.
* **Perpetrator's Employer** (`perpetrator_employer`, text, required): Please provide the name and address of the person's employer.
* **Complainant Details** (`complainant_details`, text, optional): If you're reporting on behalf of someone else, please provide their name, title, department, and contact information.
* **Complaint Date** (`complaint_date`, date, optional): The date the incident occurred.
* **Number of Witnesses** (`witness_count`, number, optional): If you witnessed the incident, how many other people saw or heard about it.
* **Witness 1 Details** (`witness_1_details`, text, optional): If you're reporting as a witness, please provide your name, title, department, and contact information.
* **Witness 2 Details** (`witness_2_details`, text, optional): If you're reporting as a witness, please provide your name, title, department, and contact information.
* **Witness 3 Details** (`witness_3_details`, text, optional): If you're reporting as a witness, please provide your name, title, department, and contact information.
* **Witness 4 Details** (`witness_4_details`, text, optional): If you're reporting as a witness, please provide your name, title, department, and contact information.

Note:
* Please be as thorough as possible when providing details about the incident.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact your HR representative or supervisor for guidance.
* This form is confidential and will be reviewed and addressed by the relevant authorities.
