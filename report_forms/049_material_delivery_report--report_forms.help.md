# Material Delivery Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Material Delivery Report form is used to track the delivery of materials to different departments within an organization. It captures information about the delivery date, location, personnel responsible, and any issues that may have arisen during the delivery process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the delivery date and time of the material delivery.
2. Enter the location where the material delivery was made.
3. Identify the personnel responsible for delivering the materials (e.g., Construction, Warehouse, Facilities).
4. If any issues arose during the delivery, select them from the options provided.
5. Describe the issue(s) that occurred in the "Issue Description" field.
6. Sign and timestamp the form to confirm that it has been completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **delivery_date** (date): Enter the date when the material delivery took place.
  - This field captures the date of the material delivery.
- **delivery_location** (text): Enter the location where the material delivery was made.
  - This field captures the location where the material delivery was executed.
- **delivered_by** (text): Identify the personnel or department responsible for delivering the materials.
  - This field is used to identify the personnel responsible for the delivery.
- **material_received** (select one): Select the status of material delivery (Received, Not received, On order).
  - This field indicates the status of material delivery.
- **comments** (text): Enter any additional comments or notes about the delivery.
  - This field allows for any additional comments or notes about the delivery.
- **issue** (select multiple): Select any issues that occurred during the material delivery (None, Delay in Delivery, Shortage, Damage, Incorrect item, Other, Other (specify)).
  - This field is used to capture any issues that occurred during the delivery.
- **issue_description** (text): Describe the issue that occurred in more detail.
  - This field provides a space to describe the issue in more detail.
- **signature** (time): Date and time when the form was completed.
  - This field is used to timestamp when the form was completed.
- **assigned** (select one): Identify the department responsible for material delivery (Construction, Warehouse, Facilities).
  - This field identifies the department responsible for material delivery.
