# Weekly Clinic Activity Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weekly Clinic Activity Report form is used to collect information about the clinic's operations, staffing, services, and patient outcomes. Complete this form to provide an accurate and concise overview of the clinic's activity during the week.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully before starting to ensure you understand each field's purpose.
* Answer each field based on your actual experiences and observations.
* Be accurate and precise when entering data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Clinic Operations
* **Clinic Operations** (`Clinic Operations`, note, not required): This field allows you to enter any notable events or issues related to clinic operations. Use this field to mention any significant happenings, such as staff absences, equipment malfunctions, or other notable events that might impact the clinic's activity.

### Staffing Notes
* **Staffing Notes** (`Staffing Notes`, select multiple, not required): In this field, select all the days that staffing notes apply, i.e., the days when staffing was an issue. For example, if staffing was a problem on Monday and Tuesday, select both "Monday" and "Tuesday".

### Staffing Schedule
* **Staffing Schedule** (`Staffing Schedule`, time, not required): Enter the time when the first staff member arrived at the clinic and the time when the last staff member left.

### Service Activity
* **Service Activity** (`Service Activity`, select one, not required): Check this field if there were any issues with service activity during the week. Choose the option that best describes the situation.

### Clinic Services
* **Clinic Services** (`Clinic Services`, select multiple, not required): This field allows you to select the services provided during the week. Choose all the services that apply to your situation.

### Patient Outcomes
* **Patient Outcomes** (`Patient Outcomes`, select one, not required): Choose the option that best describes the patient outcomes during the week. For example, if all patients were satisfied, choose "All Patients Were Satisfied".

### Total Services Provided
* **Total Services Provided** (`Total Services Provided`, number, not required): Enter the total number of services provided during the week.

### Total Services Billed
* **Total Services Billed** (`Total Services Billed`, number, not required): Enter the total number of services billed during the week.

### Billing Issues
* **Billing Issues** (`Billing Issues`, select multiple, not required): If you encountered any billing issues, select all the days they occurred. For example, if billing issues arose on Monday and Wednesday, select both "Monday" and "Wednesday".

### Additional Notes
* **Additional Notes** (`Additional Notes`, note, not required): Enter any additional comments or notes about the week's activity that are not captured in other fields.
