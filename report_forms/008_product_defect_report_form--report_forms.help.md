# Product Defect Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Defect Report Form is used for reporting product defects to maintain product quality and efficiency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Defect Image**: Attach a clear image of the product defect.
* **Additional Comment**: Provide any additional comments or details about the product condition or defect type.
* **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the customer who reported the defect.
* **Customer Email**: Enter the email address of the customer who reported the defect.
* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product that is defective.
* **Product Category**: Enter the category of the product (e.g., electronics, clothing, etc.).
* **Product ID**: Enter a valid product ID number.
* **Defect Type**: Select the type of defect (Defected, Not Defected, Partially Defected).
* **Product Condition**: Describe the product's condition before and after the defect.
* **Product Description**: Add a brief description of the product.
* **Product Comment**: Add any comments related to the product defect.
* **Product Status**: Select the product status (Active, Inactive, Partially Inactive).
* **Product Rating**: Enter a rating for the product (e.g., 1-5).
* **Product Price**: Enter the price of the product.
* **Product Date**: Enter the date the product was produced or manufactured.
* **Product Time**: Enter the time the product was produced or manufactured.
* **Product Image**: Attach a relevant product image.
* **Product Video**: Attach a relevant product video.
* **Product Audio**: Attach a relevant product audio file.
* **Product Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Product Phone 2**, **Product Phone 3**, **Product Phone 4**, **Product Phone 5**, **Product Phone 6**: Enter any additional phone numbers, if necessary.

Note: Some fields may be irrelevant to some types of products or situations. Please use your best judgment when filling out this form.
