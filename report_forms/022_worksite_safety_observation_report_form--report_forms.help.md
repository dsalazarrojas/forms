# Worksite Safety Observation Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about safety observations made during site visits, helping to identify and mitigate potential hazards and improve safety protocols.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Answer each question thoroughly and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
* If a field is optional, only fill it out if it is relevant to your observation.
* Use the options provided for the "Hazard Identification" field only if they accurately describe the hazard you have observed.
* Sign and date the form to confirm that you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (1)**: Provide your name and any other general information relevant to your observation.
* **Hazard Information (2)**: Briefly describe the hazard you observed, including any relevant details.
* **Observation Details (3)**: Describe the specific details of your observation, including what you saw, when and where it occurred, and any relevant context.
* **Safety Recommendations (4)**: Provide any recommendations for safety improvements or mitigations you suggest to address the hazard you observed.
* **Signature (5)**: Sign and date the form to confirm that you have completed it.
* **Date (6)**: Enter the date of your observation.
* **Time (7)**: Enter the time of your observation.
* **Location (8)**: Describe the location where your observation was made.
* **Observation Notes (9)**: Add any additional notes or information that may be relevant to your observation.
* **Hazard Identification (10)**: Select all options that accurately describe the hazard you observed. If none of the options match, select "Other" and provide a description of the hazard in the text field.
