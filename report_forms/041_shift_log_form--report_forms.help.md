# Shift Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for logging and reporting on shifts. Shifts are periods of time worked by employees, typically during work hours.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Click on "Submit" to start a new shift log entry.
2. Select the date of the shift from the calendar input field (shift_date).
3. Enter the start time of your shift in the shift_start_time field.
4. Enter the end time of your shift in the shift_end_time field.
5. If you took a break during your shift, enter the start and end times of your break in the break_start_time and break_end_time fields.
6. Choose the type of shift you worked from the shift_type field.
7. Add any notes or comments about your shift in the notes and comments fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **shift_date**: Enter the date of the shift (MM/DD/YYYY). This field is not required.
* **shift_start_time**: Enter the start time of your shift (HH:MM:SS). This field is not required.
* **shift_end_time**: Enter the end time of your shift (HH:MM:SS). This field is not required.
* **break_start_time**: Enter the start time of your break (HH:MM:SS). This field is not required.
* **break_end_time**: Enter the end time of your break (HH:MM:SS). This field is not required.
* **shift_duration**: Enter the duration of your shift in minutes. This field is not required.
* **shift_type**: Choose the type of shift you worked (Shift 1, Shift 2, or Shift 3). This field is not required.
* **notes**: Add any notes or comments about your shift. This field is not required.
* **comments**: Add any additional comments about your shift. This field is not required.

Note: This form can be completed multiple times, and each entry will be saved separately.
