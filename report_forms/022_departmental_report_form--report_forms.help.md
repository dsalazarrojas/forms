# Departmental Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Departmental Report Form is a tool for departmental representatives to report internal issues, payroll information, and other relevant data within their department.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant category for your report from the "Category" field.
2. Enter a brief description of the report in the "Description" field.
3. Fill in any relevant departmental information, such as the department name and contact details, in the "Departmental Contact Information" field.
4. If necessary, upload any relevant documents or additional comments in the "Upload Necessary Documents" and "Upload Additional Comments" fields.
5. Enter any notes or observations related to the report in the "Notes" field.
6. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Department** (`departmental_report_form_department`, text, required): Enter the name of the department that the report is related to.
* **Internal Issues** (`internal_issues`, text, optional): Enter any internal issues or problems that need to be addressed.
* **Payroll** (`payroll`, text, optional): Enter any relevant payroll information.
* **Departmental Contact Information** (`departmental_contact`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more contacts from the available options: Manager, HR, or Director.
* **Manager Contact** (`manager_contact`, text, optional): Enter the contact details of the manager or relevant person.
* **Upload Necessary Documents** (`upload_necessary_documents`, text, optional): Upload any necessary documents or files related to the report.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any notes or observations related to the report.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Confirm that you have completed and reviewed the form before submitting it.
* **Upload Additional Comments** (`upload_additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information that is not covered by the other fields.
* **Category** (`departmental_report_form_category`, select_one, optional): Select the category for the report from the available options: Report Forms or Other.
* **Description** (`departmental_report_form_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the report.
