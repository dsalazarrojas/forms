# Bone Marrow Biopsy Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the results of a bone marrow biopsy procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's demographic details: Report Date, Patient Name, Medical Record Number, Date of Birth.
2. Fill in the details of the biopsy procedure: Biopsy Date, Biopsy Time, Biopsy Site.
3. Fill in the results of the pathology examination: Specimen Description, Specimen Adequacy, Cellularity, Myeloid to Erythroid Ratio, Blast Cell Percentage, Erythropoiesis, Granulopoiesis, Megakaryocytes.
4. Report on any dysplasia or fibrosis present in the marrow: Dysplasia Present, Dysplasia Description, Marrow Fibrosis.
5. Record any additional studies performed: Iron Stain Result, Cytochemical Studies Performed, Special Studies.
6. Provide a final diagnosis: Diagnostic Impression.
7. Make any clinical correlations: Clinical Correlation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Report Date (1)**: The date on which the biopsy was performed.
* **Patient Name (2)**: The name of the patient undergoing the procedure.
* **Medical Record Number (3)**: The patient's medical record number.
* **Date of Birth (4)**: The date of birth of the patient.
* **Biopsy Date (5)**: The date on which the biopsy was performed.
* **Biopsy Time (6)**: The time of the biopsy.
* **Biopsy Site (7)**: The site from which the bone marrow was taken (e.g. Posterior Iliac Crest, Anterior Iliac Crest, etc.).
* **Physician Performing Procedure (8)**: The name of the physician who performed the biopsy.
* **Pathologist Reviewing Sample (9)**: The name of the pathologist who reviewed the sample.
* **Specimen Description (10)**: A description of the sample, including color, consistency, and length.
* **Specimen Adequacy (11)**: An assessment of whether the sample was sufficient for diagnostic purposes.
* **Cellularity (12)**: An assessment of the cellularity of the marrow, expressed as a percentage.
* **Myeloid to Erythroid Ratio (13)**: An optional field for recording the myeloid to erythroid ratio.
* **Blast Cell Percentage (14)**: An optional field for recording the blast cell percentage.
* **Erythropoiesis (15)**: Findings on erythropoiesis, e.g. normal, abnormal, decreased, etc.
* **Granulopoiesis (16)**: Findings on granulopoiesis, e.g. normal, abnormal, decreased, etc.
* **Megakaryocytes (17)**: Findings on megakaryocytes, e.g. increased, normal, decreased, etc.
* **Dysplasia Present (18)**: An indication of whether dysplasia is present.
* **Dysplasia Description (19)**: A description of the dysplasia, if present.
* **Marrow Fibrosis (20)**: An assessment of the degree of marrow fibrosis.
* **Iron Stain Result (21)**: The result of the iron stain, if performed.
* **Cytochemical Studies Performed (22)**: A list of any cytochemical studies performed.
* **Special Studies (23)**: A list of any special studies performed (e.g. flow cytometry, cytogenetics, etc.).
* **Diagnostic Impression (24)**: The final diagnosis.
* **Clinical Correlation (25)**: Recommendations for the clinician.
