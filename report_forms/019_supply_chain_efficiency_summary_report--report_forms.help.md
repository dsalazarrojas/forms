# Supply Chain Efficiency Summary Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information on the performance and efficiency of a team within the supply chain. It helps track progress, identify bottlenecks, and plan improvement actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the reporting period for which you want to gather information.
2. Enter the name of the team responsible for the supply chain process.
3. Choose the relevant Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) that apply to your team.
4. Enter any bottlenecks or challenges that are affecting the supply chain process.
5. Provide any improvement actions that have been taken or plan to take to address bottlenecks.
6. Choose how to proceed with the report: submit now, save for later, or attach files.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reporting Period**: Select the time period for which you want to gather information. This could be a specific date or a range of dates.
* **Team Name**: Enter the name of the team responsible for the supply chain process.
* **KPIs (Key Performance Indicators)**: Choose the relevant KPIs that apply to your team. Select one or more of the available options to track progress on specific metrics.
* **Bottlenecks**: Enter any challenges or obstacles that are affecting the supply chain process. Use this field to provide context and details on areas that need improvement.
* **Improvement Actions**: Provide any actions that have been taken or plan to take to address bottlenecks. This could include specific steps, timelines, or resources allocated.
* **Submit**: Choose how to proceed with the report. You can submit the report now, save it for later, or attach files for further review.
* **Date**: Enter the date on which the report is being submitted.
* **Time**: Enter the time of day the report is being submitted.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the person submitting the report.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the report.
* **Location**: Use this field to provide any additional context or information about the location where the report is being submitted.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the report.
* **Attachments**: Attach any relevant files or documents to the report.
* **User**: Choose the user responsible for submitting the report.
* **Notes**: Use this field to provide any additional comments or information about the report.

Tips:

* Be as specific as possible when filling out the form to ensure accurate information is gathered.
* If unsure about any field, refer to your team's guidelines or policies for clarification.
* Attach relevant files to support your report and provide context.
