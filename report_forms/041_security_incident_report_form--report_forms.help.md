# Security Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Security Incident Report Form is a tool used to document and report security incidents that have occurred in the workplace. This form ensures that all necessary information is gathered to facilitate prompt and effective response to the incident.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the incident date and type from the respective fields.
2. Provide a detailed description of the incident in the "Incident Description" field.
3. Choose the location where the incident occurred from the "Incident Location" field.
4. Enter your contact information, including title, phone number, and email address, in the "Submitter" section.
5. Enter the contact information for the incident responder in the "Incident Responder" section.
6. Fill out any other relevant fields as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, required): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, required): Choose the type of incident from the dropdown menu.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, required): Describe the incident in detail.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, required): Select the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, optional): Select the current status of the incident.
* **Incident Category** (`incident_category`, optional): Choose the categories of the incident.
* **Other Category** (`incident_category_other`, optional): If the incident does not fit into the above categories, enter a brief description here.
* **Incident Responder** (`incident_responder`, required): Enter the contact information for the incident responder.
* **Responder Title** (`incident_responder_title`, optional): Enter the title of the incident responder.
* **Responder Phone** (`incident_responder_phone`, required): Enter the phone number of the incident responder.
* **Responder Email** (`incident_responder_email`, required): Enter the email address of the incident responder.
* **Responder Location** (`incident_responder_location`, optional): Enter the location of the incident responder.
* **Responder Department** (`incident_responder_department`, optional): Enter the department of the incident responder.
* **Responder Supervisor** (`incident_responder_supervisor`, optional): Enter the supervisor of the incident responder.
* **Responder Manager** (`incident_responder_manager`, optional): Enter the manager of the incident responder.
* **Responder Notes** (`incident_responder_notes`, optional): Add any notes or comments from the incident responder.
* **Submitter** (`incident_submitter`, required): Enter your contact information.
* **Submitter Title** (`incident_submitter_title`, optional): Enter your title.
* **Submitter Phone** (`incident_submitter_phone`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Email** (`incident_submitter_email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Location** (`incident_submitter_location`, optional): Enter your location.
* **Submitter Department** (`incident_submitter_department`, optional): Enter your department.
* **Submitter Supervisor** (`incident_submitter_supervisor`, optional): Enter your supervisor.
* **Submitter Manager** (`incident_submitter_manager`, optional): Enter your manager.
* **Submitter Notes** (`incident_submitter_notes`, optional): Add any notes or comments from the submitter.
* **Responder Notes** (`incident_responder_notes`, optional): Add any notes or comments from the incident responder.
