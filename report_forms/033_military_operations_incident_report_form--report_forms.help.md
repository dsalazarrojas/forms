# Military Operations Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about incidents that have occurred during military operations. It is intended to be used by military personnel to report and document incidents, allowing for prompt and effective response and resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the incident in the "Incident Date" field.
2. Provide the location of the incident in the "Location" field.
3. List the unit(s) involved in the incident in the "Unit Involved" field.
4. Choose the type of incident in the "Type" field.
5. Describe the incident in the "Description" field.
6. Select the status of the incident in the "Status" field.
7. Identify the unit responding to the incident in the "Unit Responding" field.
8. Provide any relevant information about the resolution of the incident in the "Resolution" field.
9. Outline any follow-up actions that have been taken in the "Follow-up Actions" field.
10. Add any additional notes or comments about the incident in the "Incident Notes" field.
11. Enter the name and designation of the person reviewing the incident in the "Incident Reviewer" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Date (1)**: Date of the incident.
* **Location (2)**: City, province, state or region where the incident occurred.
* **Unit Involved (3)**: List the unit(s) involved in the incident. For example, "1st Platoon, Bravo Company, 3rd Battalion".
* **Type (4)**: Type of incident, such as "Security Breach", "Equipment Failure", or "Environmental Hazard".
* **Description (5)**: A brief description of what happened during the incident.
* **Status (6)**: Select the current status of the incident (In Progress, Completed, or Ongoing).
* **Unit Responding (7)**: Name and designation of the unit responding to the incident, such as "4th Platoon, Delta Company, 5th Battalion".
* **Resolution (8)**: Provide information about what was done to resolve the incident.
* **Follow-up Actions (9)**: Outline any follow-up actions that were taken or planned.
* **Incident Notes (10)**: Add any additional notes or comments about the incident.
* **Incident Reviewer (11)**: Enter the name and designation of the person reviewing the incident.
