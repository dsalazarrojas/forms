# Service Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to record and track the service and maintenance history of a vehicle. It captures information about the vehicle's usage dates, service history dates, mileage, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form by following the instructions below.
2. Each field is labeled and explained clearly below.
3. Make sure to enter accurate information for each field.
4. Review and edit the form as needed before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Record** (`service_record_form`): Enter a brief description of the service or maintenance performed on the vehicle.
* **Vehicle ID** (`vehicle_id`): Enter a unique identifier for the vehicle, e.g., a number or a name.
* **VIN** (`vin`): Enter the Vehicle Identification Number of the vehicle. The VIN is a 17-character code used to identify a vehicle's make, model, and year.
* **Vehicle Usage Dates** (`vehicle_usage_dates`): Enter the dates when the vehicle was used, e.g., "Jan 1, 2022 - Dec 31, 2022".
* **Service History Dates** (`service_history_dates`): Enter the dates when service or maintenance was performed on the vehicle.
* **Maintenance Dates** (`maintenance_dates`): Enter the dates when maintenance was performed on the vehicle.
* **Mileage** (`mileage`): Enter the current mileage of the vehicle.
* **Service Frequency** (`service_frequency`): Select whether the vehicle requires service on a "Yes" or "No" basis.
* **Frequency** (`service_frequency_frequency`): Select the frequency at which the vehicle is due for service, e.g., "Monthly", "Quarterly", "Semi-annual", "Yearly".
* **Service Type** (`service_type`): Select the type of service performed on the vehicle, e.g., "Oil Service", "Tire Service", "Maintenance Service", "Inspection Service".
* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter notes about the service or maintenance performed on the vehicle.
