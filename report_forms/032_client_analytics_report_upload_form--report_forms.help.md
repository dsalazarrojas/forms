# Client Analytics Report Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect analytics data from clients for reporting purposes. It allows clients to upload their reports for the current reporting period, which can be monthly, quarterly, or annual. The form helps our analysts to review and analyze the data efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Client/Account Name in the "Client/Account Name" field.
2. Select the reporting period for which you are uploading the data (e.g., Monthly, Quarterly, etc.).
3. Choose the primary analytics platform used to collect the data.
4. Provide a description of the files being uploaded (e.g., GA4 Traffic Report, Conversion Data).
5. Confirm that you have successfully uploaded the files to the secure portal.
6. If applicable, provide the link to the uploaded assets.
7. Describe any specific anomalies or concerns in the data.
8. Let us know if you would like to schedule a follow-up meeting to review the data in more detail.
9. Enter your email address for reporting follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client/Account Name** (`client_name`, Text, required): Enter the name of your client or account.
* **Today's Date** (`submission_date`, Date, required): Select the date on which you are submitting the report.
* **Reporting Period** (`reporting_period`, Select One, required): Choose the reporting period for which you are uploading the data. This can be Monthly, Quarterly, or Annual.
* **Primary Analytics Platform** (`analytics_platform`, Select One, required): Select the primary analytics platform used to collect the data. This can be Google Analytics 4, Adobe Analytics, Mixpanel, Shopify Analytics, or Facebook Ads Manager.
* **Description of Files Being Uploaded** (`report_details_header`, Text, required): Provide a description of the files being uploaded, such as GA4 Traffic Report, Conversion Data.
* **Have you successfully uploaded the files to the secure portal?** (`file_upload_confirmation`, Select One, required): Confirm that you have uploaded the files successfully.
* **Link to Uploaded Assets (if applicable)** (`portal_link`, Text, optional): If you have uploaded the assets to a public link, provide it here.
* **Any specific anomalies or concerns in this period's data?** (`data_concerns`, Text, optional): Describe any specific anomalies or concerns you have about the data.
* **Would you like to schedule a deep-dive review call?** (`follow_up_meeting_requested`, Select One, required): Let us know if you would like to schedule a follow-up meeting to review the data.
* **Best email for reporting follow-up** (`contact_person_email`, Email, required): Enter your email address for reporting follow-up.
* **Internal Campaign ID** (`internal_campaign_id`, Text, optional): Provide any internal campaign ID, if applicable.
