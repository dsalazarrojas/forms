# Unemployment Insurance Fraud Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Unemployment Insurance Fraud Reporting Form is designed for reporting potential cases of unemployment insurance fraud. It allows you to provide information about a suspect employee and the report's details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields with the correct information.
2. Provide as much detail as possible to support your report.
3. Attach any relevant documentation to support your report.
4. Choose how you want to submit the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Suspect Employee Information** (`suspect_employee_info`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional information about the suspect employee, such as their name, job title, or position.
* **Suspect Employee ID** (`suspect_employee_id`, number, optional): If you have the suspect employee's ID number, enter it here.
* **Suspect Job Title** (`suspect_job_title`, text, optional): Enter the suspect employee's job title.
* **Suspect DOB** (`suspect_dob`, date, optional): Enter the suspect employee's date of birth.
* **Suspect Termination Date** (`suspect_termination_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the suspect employee was terminated.
* **Suspect Last Seen At Work** (`suspect_last_seen_at_work`, date, optional): Enter the date the suspect employee last worked.
* **Suspect Termination Reason** (`suspect_termination_reason`, text, optional): Select the reason for the suspect employee's termination.
* **Suspect Last Known Address** (`suspect_last_known_address`, text, optional): Enter the suspect employee's last known address.
* **Report Employee Information** (`report_employee_info`, text, optional): Provide information about the employee making this report, such as their name and contact information.
* **Report Employee ID** (`report_employee_id`, number, optional): If you have the report employee's ID number, enter it here.
* **Report Job Title** (`report_job_title`, text, optional): Enter the report employee's job title.
* **Report DOB** (`report_dob`, date, optional): Enter the report employee's date of birth.
* **Report Termination Date** (`report_termination_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the report employee was terminated.
* **Report Last Seen At Work** (`report_last_seen_at_work`, date, optional): Enter the date the report employee last worked.
* **Report Termination Reason** (`report_termination_reason`, text, optional): Select the reason for the report employee's termination.
* **Report Last Known Address** (`report_last_known_address`, text, optional): Enter the report employee's last known address.
* **Report Date of Fraudulent Activity** (`report_date_of_fraudulent_activity`, date, optional): Enter the date the report fraudulent activity took place.
* **Report Date of Discovery** (`report_date_of_discovery`, date, optional): Enter the date the report was made.
* **Report Description** (`report_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the report.
* **Report Attachments** (`report_attachments`, text, optional): Attach any supporting documentation or evidence to support your report.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, optional): Choose how you want to submit the report:
	+ Report the above information and attach any supporting documentation.
	+ Submit without submitting.

## Tips
- Ensure that you provide accurate and complete information about the suspect employee and the report details.
- Attach any relevant documentation to support your report.
- Choose how you want to submit the report, as described above.
