# Inside Sales Email Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Inside Sales Email Report Form is used to report sales interactions with customers, such as email conversations or meetings. This form is designed to help track and document these interactions for sales team reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as follows:

1. Enter the customer's First Name.
2. Enter the customer's Email address.
3. Enter the customer's Phone number.
4. Enter the customer's Company name.
5. Indicate if the Sales Person was involved in the interaction (Yes/No).
6. Select the Date of the interaction (YYYY-MM-DD).
7. Select the Time of the interaction (HH:MM AM/PM).
8. Indicate if the Sales Person was involved in the interaction (Yes/No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text): Enter the customer's first name.
* **Email** (email): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone** (text): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Company** (text): Enter the customer's company name.
* **Sales Person** (select one): Check "Yes" if the Sales Person was involved in the interaction, or "No" if not.
* **Date** (date): Select the date of the interaction (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Time** (time): Select the time of the interaction (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Sales Person** (select one): Check "Yes" if the Sales Person was involved in the interaction, or "No" if not.

## Tips
* Make sure to only fill out this form for sales interactions that have occurred.
* Only fill out this form if you have not already reported this sales interaction.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your sales team for clarification.
* This form is meant to be a quick and easy way to report sales interactions, so keep your answers concise and focused on the details of the interaction.
