# mail_service_disruption_report_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to report a mail service disruption that has already occurred.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the user(s) affected by the disruption.
2. Provide detailed information about the issue in the "Issue Details" field.
3. Choose the facility, mailroom, and customer service team involved.
4. Select the type of issue that occurred (delayed, disrupted, or lost).
5. Provide the start and end times of the disruption.
6. Choose the status of the issue (new, in progress, or done).
7. Add any additional comments or notes about the issue.
8. Choose a follow-up time and status.
9. Provide any comments about the follow-up.
10. Select the facilities and mailrooms affected by the disruption.
11. Describe the issue and its impact.
12. Provide a solution to the issue.
13. Finally, select "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User** (`user`, required/optional): Select the user(s) involved in the disruption.
* **Issue Details** (`issue_details`, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of the issue in this field.
* **Facility** (`facility`, required/optional): Choose the facility where the disruption occurred.
* **Mailroom** (`mailroom`, required/optional): Select the mailroom affected by the disruption.
* **Customer Service** (`customer_service`, required/optional): Choose the customer service team involved.
* **Issue Type** (`issue_type`, required/optional): Select the type of issue that occurred (delayed, disrupted, or lost).
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, required/optional): Enter the start time of the disruption.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, required/optional): Enter the end time of the disruption.
* **Status** (`status`, required/optional): Choose the status of the issue (new, in progress, or done).
* **Notes** (`notes`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the issue.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, required/optional): Choose a follow-up time and status.
* **Follow-up Comment** (`follow_up_comment`, required/optional): Provide any comments about the follow-up.
* **User Comment** (`user_comment`, required/optional): Add any additional comments from the user.
* **Facility Comment** (`facility_comment`, required/optional): Add any additional comments from the facility.
* **Mailroom Comment** (`mailroom_comment`, required/optional): Add any additional comments from the mailroom.
* **Customer Service Comment** (`customer_service_comment`, required/optional): Add any additional comments from the customer service team.
* **Follow-up Status** (`follow_up_status`, required/optional): Choose the follow-up status (done or in progress).
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, required/optional): Describe the issue and its impact.
* **Cause** (`cause`, required/optional): Provide a description of the cause of the issue.
* **Solution** (`solution`, required/optional): Provide a solution to the issue.
* **Submit** (`submit`, required/optional): Select "Submit" to complete the form.

## Tips
Please make sure to complete the form accurately and thoroughly to help us understand the disruption and improve our services. If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
