# E Bike Repair Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The E Bike Repair Report Form is used to report maintenance, repairs, or issues with e-bikes. This form helps mechanics and service staff gather necessary information about the e-bike's condition, any problems during the repair process, and the current status of the e-bike.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide details about the e-bike being repaired, including its model, make, and serial number (if available).
2. Describe the issue or problem that needed attention, including any symptoms or error messages.
3. Write a brief summary of the work done and the current status of the e-bike.
4. Add any comments from the mechanic or service staff that may be helpful for future reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **E-bike Details** (e_bike_details): 
    * Type: Text
    * Required: No
    * Explanation: Enter the e-bike's model, make, and serial number (if available) to help identify the bike and its repair history.
* **Description** (description): 
    * Type: Text
    * Required: No
    * Explanation: Describe the e-bike's condition, including any pre-repair notes or observations.
* **Issue** (issue): 
    * Type: Text
    * Required: No
    * Explanation: Enter a brief description of the problem or issue that needed attention.
* **Mechanic Comments** (mechanic_comments): 
    * Type: Text
    * Required: No
    * Explanation: Add any comments from the mechanic or service staff that may be helpful for future reference.
* **Status** (status): 
    * Type: Text
    * Required: No
    * Explanation: Update the e-bike's status after the repair, such as "Fixed", "Needs further repair", or "Still under repair".

## Tips
* Make sure to include all relevant information about the e-bike and the issue.
* Use a clear and concise format to ensure easy reading and understanding.
* If you need to append to an existing report, please make sure to include a clear explanation of the changes or additions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
