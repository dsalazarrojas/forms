# Referee Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Referee Evaluation Form is used by evaluators to assess a referee's performance during a game. This form is essential for evaluating a referee's abilities, such as their decision-making, communication, and overall performance. It will help in identifying areas of improvement and provide valuable feedback to the referee.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the relevant information for each field.
2. Provide your name and email address in the "Evaluators Name" and "Evaluators Email" fields, respectively.
3. Enter the name of the referee being evaluated in the "Referee Name" field.
4. Select the date of the game evaluation from the calendar provided.
5. Choose the game time from the dropdown menu.
6. Evaluate the referee's performance in the following areas:
	* Dress & Appearance
	* Pre-Game
	* Fitness
	* Attitude
	* Courage
	* Character
	* Consistency
	* Mechanics
	* Signals
	* Accuracy of Decisions
	* Game Control
7. Enter any additional comments about the evaluation in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Evaluators Name**: This is the field where you, as the evaluator, enter your name.
* **Evaluators Email**: This is the field where you enter your email address.
* **Referee Name**: Enter the name of the referee being evaluated.
* **Date**: Choose the date of the game evaluation from the calendar provided.
* **Game Time**: Select the game time from the dropdown menu.
* **Competition Level**: Evaluate the referee's performance at a competition level (High or Low).
* **Dress & Appearance**: Rate the referee's dress and appearance on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Pre-Game**: Rate the referee's pre-game preparation and organization on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Fitness**: Rate the referee's physical fitness level on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Attitude**: Rate the referee's attitude and demeanor on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Courage**: Rate the referee's courage and decisiveness on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Character**: Rate the referee's character and sportsmanship on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Consistency**: Rate the referee's consistency and ability to follow rules on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Mechanics**: Rate the referee's mechanical skills on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Signals**: Rate the referee's ability to communicate effectively on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Accuracy of Decisions**: Rate the referee's accuracy of decisions on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Game Control**: Rate the referee's game control and ability to manage the game on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about the evaluation.

## Tips
* Be honest and provide constructive feedback to help the referee improve.
* Provide specific examples or incidents that support your evaluation.
* Keep the comments clear and concise.
* Proofread your responses before submitting the form.
