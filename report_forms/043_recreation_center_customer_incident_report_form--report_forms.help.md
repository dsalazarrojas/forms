# Recreation Center Customer Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recreation Center Customer Incident Report Form is a tool used to document and report incidents that occur within the Recreation Center. This form helps staff to gather and record information about incidents, which can aid in identifying and addressing potential safety and security concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the date and time the incident occurred in the "Incident Date" field.
2.  Enter the location where the incident occurred in the "Incident Location" field.
3.  Provide a detailed description of the incident in the "Incident Description" field.
4.  If applicable, indicate if any injuries were sustained in the "Incident Injuries" field.
5.  Describe any damages or losses sustained due to the incident in the "Incident Damage" field.
6.  Identify the staff member or customer who reported the incident in the "Incident Reporter" field.
7.  Specify which department is responsible for the incident in the "Incident Department" field.
8.  Mark the current status of the incident as "Active" or "Inactive" in the "Incident Status" field.
9.  Add any additional notes about the incident in the "Incident Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, optional): Enter the date and time the incident occurred in the format `YYYY-MM-DD` or select from a calendar.
- **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, optional): Enter the exact time the incident occurred.
- **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, optional): Provide the location where the incident occurred, e.g., "Main Floor".
- **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, optional): Describe the incident in as much detail as possible.
- **Incident Injuries** (`incident_injuries`, text, optional): If applicable, indicate if any injuries were sustained due to the incident.
- **Incident Damage** (`incident_damage`, text, optional): Describe any damages or losses sustained due to the incident.
- **Incident Reporter** (`incident_reporter`, text, optional): Identify the staff member or customer who reported the incident.
- **Incident Department** (`incident_department`, text, optional): Specify which department is responsible for the incident.
- **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, select_one, optional): Mark the current status of the incident as "Active" or "Inactive".
- **Incident Notes** (`incident_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about the incident.
