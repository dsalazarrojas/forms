# Virtual Assistant End Of Day Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help Virtual Assistants document their daily activities, accomplishments, and experiences. By using this form, Virtual Assistants can provide valuable insights to their team members, managers, or clients, ensuring that everyone stays informed and up-to-date with the progress made during the day. This report helps in identifying successes, challenges, and areas for improvement, enabling better decision-making and planning for future tasks.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible for each section.
2. Fill in your start and finish times to track your working hours.
3. Enter your contact information (email and phone number) so that the report can be easily reached out to you.
4. Add any additional comments or observations in the "Note" section.
5. Optionally, report on anything that didn't go as planned.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What did you accomplish today?** (`what_did_you_achieve`, text, optional)
	+ This section is for you to report on your accomplishments and successes of the day.
* **What were the biggest challenges you faced?** (`biggest_challenges`, text, optional)
	+ Share with us the most significant obstacles you encountered today.
* **What did you learn today?** (`what_did_you_learn`, text, optional)
	+ Reflect on any new knowledge, skills, or insights gained during the day.
* **What did you do differently than usual?** (`did_anything_different`, text, optional)
	+ Describe any deviations from your usual routine or approach.
* **What was the most significant success you achieved?** (`greatest_success`, text, optional)
	+ Identify the most notable accomplishment of the day.
* **What didn't go as planned?** (`what_didnt_go_as_planned`, text, optional)
	+ Share any unexpected events or setbacks that occurred.
* **What time did you start working?** (`start_time`, time, optional)
	+ Log your start time for the day.
* **What time did you finish working?** (`finish_time`, time, optional)
	+ Log your finish time for the day.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional)
	+ Provide your email address so that we can reach out to you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional)
	+ Enter your phone number so that we can contact you.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional)
	+ Add any additional comments or observations about your day.
* **What did you do today?** (`did_you_do_anything`, text, optional)
	+ Briefly describe your daily activities.
* **What did you not do today?** (`didnt_do_anything`, text, optional)
	+ Share any activities or tasks that you didn't complete.
* **Date of the report** (`date_of_report`, date, optional)
	+ Enter the date of the report.
* **Time of the report** (`time_of_report`, time, optional)
	+ Log the time of the report.
* **What would you like to report?** (`report_what`, select_one, optional)
	+ Choose one of the options: Nothing, Something, or Everything.
* **How did you accomplish your goals today?** (`accomplished_goals`, text, optional)
	+ Describe your approach to achieving your goals.
* **Would you like to report on anything else?** (`anything_else`, select_one, optional)
	+ Choose one of the options: Nothing, Something, or Everything.

Note: The fields marked as "optional" can be filled in or left blank, depending on your preference.
