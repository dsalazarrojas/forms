# Treasury Reporting Approval Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Treasury Reporting Approval Application Form is used to submit treasury reports for approval. This form is intended for employees who need to submit reports for review and approval by the relevant department or manager.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the report type from the "Report Type" field.
2. Enter the start and end dates of the reporting period in the "Reporting Start Date" and "Reporting End Date" fields.
3. Enter your name and email in the "Submitter Name" and "Submitter Email" fields.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Submitter Phone" field.
5. Ensure that all fields are accurate and complete before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Report Form Details
* **Report Form Details** (`report_form_details`, text, optional): This field is a text field where you can enter any additional information about the report you are submitting.

### Report Type
* **Report Type** (`report_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of report you are submitting. Please refer to the options provided to choose the correct report type.

### Reporting Period
* **Reporting Period** (`reporting_period`, date, optional): Enter the date range for which you are submitting the report.

### Reporting Start Date
* **Reporting Start Date** (`reporting_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the reporting period.

### Reporting End Date
* **Reporting End Date** (`reporting_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the reporting period.

### Submitter Name
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID badge.

### Submitter Email
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, optional): Enter your work email address.

### Submitter Phone
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, optional): Enter your work phone number.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have already checked with the relevant department or manager to verify the need for and approval process of this report.
* Double-check that you haven't already submitted a similar report within the specified reporting period.
* Enter all fields accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your report can be processed efficiently.
