# Small Estate Affidavit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Small Estate Affidavit Form is used to declare the ownership of a small estate and provide information about the property's location and relevant contacts. This form is typically used for legal or administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form in a quiet, distraction-free environment.
- Ensure you have all necessary information and contacts ready before starting.
- Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy.
- Enter your responses in the corresponding fields.
- Review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Estate Owner** (`estate_owner`, select_one, optional): Check "Yes" if you are the owner of the small estate, or "No" if someone else is. This indicates who is responsible for the estate's management and maintenance.
* **Relationship** (`relationship`, text, optional): Describe your relationship with the estate's owner or manager, if applicable.
* **Owner Address** (`owner_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the estate's owner or manager.
* **Owner City** (`owner_city`, text, optional): Enter the city of the estate's owner or manager.
* **Owner State** (`owner_state`, text, optional): Enter the state of the estate's owner or manager.
* **Owner Zip** (`owner_zip`, text, optional): Enter the zip code of the estate's owner or manager.
* **Owner Phone** (`owner_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the estate's owner or manager.
* **Estate Address** (`estate_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the small estate.
* **Estate City** (`estate_city`, text, optional): Enter the city of the small estate.
* **Estate State** (`estate_state`, text, optional): Enter the state of the small estate.
* **Estate Zip** (`estate_zip`, text, optional): Enter the zip code of the small estate.
* **Executor Name** (`executor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the executor or person responsible for the estate's management.
* **Executor Address** (`executor_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the executor or person responsible for the estate's management.
* **Executor City** (`executor_city`, text, optional): Enter the city of the executor or person responsible for the estate's management.
* **Executor State** (`executor_state`, text, optional): Enter the state of the executor or person responsible for the estate's management.
* **Executor Zip** (`executor_zip`, text, optional): Enter the zip code of the executor or person responsible for the estate's management.
* **Executor Phone** (`executor_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the executor or person responsible for the estate's management.
* **Heir Address** (`heir_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the heir or person inheriting the estate.
* **Heir City** (`heir_city`, text, optional): Enter the city of the heir or person inheriting the estate.
* **Heir State** (`heir_state`, text, optional): Enter the state of the heir or person inheriting the estate.
* **Heir Zip** (`heir_zip`, text, optional): Enter the zip code of the heir or person inheriting the estate.
* **Heir Phone** (`heir_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the heir or person inheriting the estate.
* **Other Executors** (`other_executors`, select_multiple, optional): Check "Yes" if there are other executors of the estate, or "No" if not.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, optional): This is a space for your signature to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.
