# Outpatient Psychiatric Progress Note Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This template is designed to document the progress of a patient's treatment in an outpatient psychiatric setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the field labeled "patient name".
2. Enter the patient's medical record number in the field labeled "medical record number".
3. Select the date of service using the date field.
4. Enter the patient's date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
5. Select the patient's mental status from the options "Active" or "Inactive".
6. Enter the patient's chief complaint in the text field.
7. Enter the location of the chief complaint in the text field.
8. Enter the patient's history of present illness in the text field.
9. Enter the patient's treatment plan in the text field.
10. Enter the patient's social history in the text field.
11. Enter the patient's physical exam in the text field.
12. Enter any allergies in the text field.
13. Enter any medications in the text field.
14. Select whether the patient has any allergies/medications from the options "Yes" or "No".
15. Enter the patient's review of systems in the text field.
16. Enter the patient's assessment in the text field.
17. Enter the patient's plan in the text field.
18. Enter the follow-up information in the text field.
19. Enter any additional notes in the text field.
20. Enter your signature as the physician.
21. Enter your title as the physician.
22. Enter the date the form was completed.
23. Enter the time the form was completed.
24. Enter the time the form was entered.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **patient name** (text): The patient's name.
- **medical record number** (text): The patient's medical record number.
- **date of service** (date): The date of the patient's visit.
- **date of birth** (date): The patient's date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
- **mental status** (select_one): The patient's mental status, selecting "Active" or "Inactive".
- **chief complaint** (text): The patient's chief complaint.
- **chief complaint location** (text): The location of the patient's chief complaint.
- **history of present illness** (text): The patient's history of present illness.
- **treatment plan** (text): The patient's treatment plan.
- **social history** (text): The patient's social history.
- **physical exam** (text): The patient's physical exam.
- **allergies** (text): Any allergies the patient has.
- **medications** (text): Any medications the patient is taking.
- **allergies/medications** (select_multiple): Whether the patient has any allergies/medications, selecting "Yes" or "No".
- **review of systems** (text): The patient's review of systems.
- **assessment** (text): The patient's assessment.
- **plan** (text): The patient's plan.
- **follow-up** (text): Any follow-up information.
- **note** (note): Any additional notes.
- **physician signature** (text): Your signature as the physician.
- **physician title** (text): Your title as the physician.
- **date completed** (date): The date the form was completed.
- **time completed** (time): The time the form was completed.
- **time entered** (time): The time the form was entered.
- **date of birth** (date): The patient's date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
- **physical exam** (text): The patient's physical exam.
