# Shipping Load Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shipping Load Report Form is used to report shipping details, including pickup and delivery locations, shipment type, weight, volume, and more. This information is essential for tracking and managing shipments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the shipment details in the fields provided.
2. Select the correct shipment type from the drop-down menu.
3. Enter the pickup location and delivery location in the respective fields.
4. Enter the shipment number, weight, volume, and length/width/height of the shipment.
5. Select the delivery date and time.
6. Choose the shipment service type (Standard, Express, or Other) and the shipment carrier (UPS, FedEx, or Other).
7. Enter any additional notes about the shipment.
8. Save or submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Shipment Details** (`shipment_details`, `text`, not required): This field is for entering any additional details about the shipment.
* Enter any relevant information about the shipment in this text field.

### 2. **Pickup Location** (`pickup_location`, `text`, not required): Enter the location from where the shipment was picked up.
* Enter the name of the pickup location.

### 3. **Pickup Date and Time** (`pickup_datetime`, `date`, not required): Enter the date and time of the shipment pickup.
* Enter the date and time of the shipment pickup.

### 4. **Shipment Number** (`shipment_number`, `number`, not required): Enter the number of the shipment.
* Enter a unique number for the shipment.

### 5. **Shipment Type** (`shipment_type`, `select_one`, not required): Select the type of shipment (LTL, LCL, FTL, or Other).
* Choose the correct shipment type from the drop-down menu.

### 6. **Pickup Date** (`pickup_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the date of the shipment pickup.
* Enter the date of the shipment pickup.

### 7. **Shipment Weight** (`shipment_weight`, `number`, not required): Enter the weight of the shipment.
* Enter the weight of the shipment in pounds or kilograms.

### 8. **Shipment Volume** (`shipment_volume`, `number`, not required): Enter the volume of the shipment.
* Enter the volume of the shipment in cubic feet or meters.

### 9. **Shipment Length** (`shipment_length`, `number`, not required): Enter the length of the shipment.
* Enter the length of the shipment in feet or meters.

### 10. **Shipment Width** (`shipment_width`, `number`, not required): Enter the width of the shipment.
* Enter the width of the shipment in feet or meters.

### 11. **Shipment Height** (`shipment_height`, `number`, not required): Enter the height of the shipment.
* Enter the height of the shipment in feet or meters.

### 12. **Delivery Location** (`delivery_location`, `text`, not required): Enter the location where the shipment will be delivered.
* Enter the name of the delivery location.

### 13. **Delivery Date and Time** (`delivery_datetime`, `date`, not required): Enter the date and time of the shipment delivery.
* Enter the date and time of the shipment delivery.

### 14. **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the date of the shipment delivery.
* Enter the date of the shipment delivery.

### 15. **Shipment Status** (`shipment_status`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select the status of the shipment (In Transit, Delivered, On Hold, Cancelled, or Other).
* Choose the correct status of the shipment from the drop-down menu.

### 16. **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, not required): Enter any additional notes about the shipment.
* Enter any additional comments or information about the shipment.

### 17. **Created By** (`created_by`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the person who created the shipment.
* Enter the name of the person who created the shipment.

### 18. **Updated By** (`updated_by`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the person who updated the shipment.
* Enter the name of the person who updated the shipment.

### 19. **Created At** (`created_at`, `time`, not required): Enter the time the shipment was created.
* Enter the time the shipment was created.

### 20. **Updated At** (`updated_at`, `time`, not required): Enter the time the shipment was last updated.
* Enter the time the shipment was last updated.

### 21. **Created By User** (`created_by_user`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the user who created the shipment.
* Enter the name of the user who created the shipment.

### 22. **Updated By User** (`updated_by_user`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the user who updated the shipment.
* Enter the name of the user who updated the shipment.

### 23. **Shipment Service** (`shipment_service`, `select_one`, not required): Select the service type (Standard, Express, or Other).
* Choose the correct service type from the drop-down menu.

### 24. **Shipment Carrier** (`shipment_carrier`, `select_one`, not required): Select the carrier (UPS, FedEx, or Other).
* Choose the correct carrier from the drop-down menu.

### 25. **Shipment Tracking Number** (`shipment_tracking_number`, `text`, not required): Enter the tracking number of the shipment.
* Enter the tracking number of the shipment.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Use the correct format for dates (MM/DD/YYYY) and times (HH:MM AM/PM).
* Use a unique shipment number for each shipment.
* Choose the correct shipment type, service, and carrier for accurate tracking and delivery.
