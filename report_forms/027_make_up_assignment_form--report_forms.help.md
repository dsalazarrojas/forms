# Make Up Assignment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report the absence of a student from an assignment or activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter the assignment or activity the student was absent from in the "Assignment Name" field.
3. Enter the reason for the student's absence in the "Reason" field.
4. Enter the date the student was absent in the "Date Absent" field. This field is for the date of the absence, and should be in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
5. Enter the time the student was absent in the "Time Absent" field. This field is for the time of the absence, and should be in the 24-hour clock format (HH:MM).
6. If you want to leave a note for the student or teacher, you can enter it in the "Note" field.
7. If you want to provide the student's contact information, you can enter it in the "Email" and "Phone" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name (student_name)** (Required): Enter the student's name as it is on their ID card or in the school database.
* **Assignment Name (assignment_name)** (Optional): Enter the name of the assignment or activity the student was absent from.
* **Reason (reason)** (Required): Enter a brief description of the reason for the student's absence.
* **Date Absent (date_absented)** (Required): Enter the date of the absence in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time Absent (time_absented)** (Required): Enter the time of the absence in the 24-hour clock format (HH:MM).
* **Note (note)** (Optional): Enter any additional information about the student's absence, such as any supporting documents or further explanation.
* **Email (email)** (Optional): Enter the student's email address, if you want to contact them directly.
* **Phone (phone)** (Optional): Enter the student's phone number, if you want to contact them directly.
