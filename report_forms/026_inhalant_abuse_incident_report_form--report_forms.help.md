# Inhalant Abuse Incident Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Inhalant Abuse Incident Report Form is designed to collect information about incidents of inhalant abuse. This form is intended to gather details about the suspect, the incident, and any relevant background information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the form's sections in a clear and accurate manner. 
2. Ensure all required fields are completed.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Report Form**: This is the main text field for providing a brief description of the incident.
* **Suspect Name**: Enter the name of the individual suspected of inhalant abuse.
* **Suspect Inhalant Use**: Select 'True' if the suspect has used inhalants, 'False' otherwise.
* **Reporting Officer**: Provide the name and title of the officer reporting the incident.
* **Suspect Employer**: Enter the name of the suspect's employer.
* **Suspect Employer Employees**: Select 'Yes' if the suspect's employer has employees, 'No' otherwise.
* **Suspect Job Title**: Enter the suspect's job title.
* **Suspect Work Site**: Enter the suspect's workplace.
* **Incident Description**: Provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **Suspect Phone**: Enter the suspect's phone number.
* **Suspect Work Phone**: Enter the suspect's work phone number.
* **Suspect Email**: Enter the suspect's email address.
* **Suspect Address**: Enter the suspect's address.
* **Suspect Work Address**: Enter the suspect's workplace address.
* **Suspect Employer Address**: Enter the suspect's employer's address.
* **Suspect Job Title Employer**: Enter the suspect's job title at their employer.
* **Suspect Employer**: Enter the suspect's employer's name again.
* **Suspect DUI**: Enter any details about the suspect's DUI history.
* **Suspect Drug Screen**: Enter any details about the suspect's drug screen history.
* **Suspect Last DUI Date**: Enter the date of the suspect's last DUI.
* **Suspect Last Drug Screen Date**: Enter the date of the suspect's last drug screen.
* **Suspect Last Drug Screen Result**: Enter the result of the suspect's last drug screen.
* **Suspect License Class**: Enter the suspect's license class.

**Tips**:
