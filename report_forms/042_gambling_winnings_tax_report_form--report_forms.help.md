# Gambling Winnings Tax Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for reporting your gambling winnings for tax purposes. Please fill out the form with accurate and complete information about your winnings.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your individual details, including your name, email, phone number, and address.
2. Enter the date and time of your gambling activity.
3. Report the amount of money you have won.
4. Specify the payment method used (e.g., Bank, Cheque, Credit Card, or Other).
5. Add any additional payment details if necessary.
6. Provide any notes about your transaction.
7. Select "Submit" to complete and submit your report.
8. Enter the date and time you submitted your report.
9. Confirm that you submitted the report and who submitted it.
10. Confirm the submit date and time of your report.
11. If applicable, provide additional submit dates, times, and submitters.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Individual Form** (`individual_form`, `text`, required): Enter your personal details, such as name, email, and phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, `required`): Report the date of your gambling activity.
* **Time** (`time`, `required`): Report the time of your gambling activity.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, `required`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Amount** (`amount`, `number`, `required`): Report the amount of money you have won.
* **Payout Amount** (`payout_amount`, `number`, `required`): Report the amount paid out.
* **Total Amount** (`total_amount`, `number`, `required`): Report the total amount.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, `required`): Specify the payment method used (e.g., Bank, Cheque, Credit Card, or Other).
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, `text`, `optional`): Add any additional payment details if necessary.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, `optional`): Provide any notes about your transaction.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `select_one`, `required`): Select "Submit" to complete and submit your report.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date you submitted your report.
* **Submit Time** (`submit_time`, `time`, `required`): Enter the time you submitted your report.
* **Submit By** (`submit_by`, `text`, `required`): Confirm who submitted the report.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `text`, `required`): Confirm that you submitted the report.
* **Submit Date/Time** (`submit_date_time`, `date`, `required`): Confirm the submit date and time of your report.
* **Submit Date/Time 2** (`submit_date_time_2`, `date`, `optional`): If applicable, report an additional submit date and time.
* **Submit Time 2** (`submit_time_2`, `time`, `optional`): If applicable, report an additional submit time.
* **Submit By 2** (`submit_by_2`, `text`, `optional`): If applicable, report who submitted the report.
* **Submitted By 2** (`submitted_by_2`, `text`, `optional`): If applicable, confirm that you submitted the report.
* **Submit Date/Time 3** (`submit_date_time_3`, `date`, `optional`): If applicable, report an additional submit date and time.

Please note that some fields are marked as optional, but it's essential to fill out all required fields for a successful submission.
