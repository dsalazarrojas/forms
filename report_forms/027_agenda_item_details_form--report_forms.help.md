# Agenda Item Details Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the outcomes and timing of specific agenda items in meetings. By filling out this form, meeting participants can provide detailed information about the discussions, decisions, and actions generated from each agenda item.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as the meeting rapporteur (the person recording the details).
2. Select the date of the report (when this form was completed).
3. Fill out the following sections with the relevant information for each agenda item:
   a. Agenda Item Title: Enter the title of the agenda item.
   b. Presenter's Name: Enter the name of the person who led the discussion on this agenda item.
   c. Start Time: Enter the actual time the discussion began.
   d. End Time: Enter the actual time the discussion ended.
   e. Was the item completed? (Select one of the options: Fully Completed, Partially Completed (Carried Forward), Postponed, or Withdrawn).
   f. Summary of discussion: Provide a brief summary of the key points raised by participants.
   g. Key decisions reached: List the formal outcomes or votes.
   h. Follow-up actions required: Select all the tasks that need to be done as a result of this agenda item.
   i. Responsible parties for follow-up: Enter the names or roles of the people who will handle the follow-up actions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Rapporteur Name**: Enter your name as the person recording the details.
* **Date of Report**: Enter the date when this form was completed.
* **Agenda Item Title**: Enter the title of the agenda item being reported.
* **Presenter Name**: Enter the name of the person who led the discussion on this agenda item.
* **Start Time**: Enter the actual time the discussion began.
* **End Time**: Enter the actual time the discussion ended.
* **Was the item completed?** (Select one of the options: Fully Completed, Partially Completed (Carried Forward), Postponed, or Withdrawn):
	+ Choose the status of the agenda item.
* **Summary of discussion**: Provide a brief summary of the key points raised by participants.
* **Key decisions reached**: List the formal outcomes or votes.
* **Follow-up actions required**: Select all the tasks that need to be done as a result of this agenda item.
* **Responsible parties for follow-up**: Enter the names or roles of the people who will handle the follow-up actions.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If an agenda item is partially completed, be sure to indicate this in the "Was the item completed?" field.
* If follow-up actions are required, make sure to list all relevant tasks and enter the responsible parties.
* If you need help or have questions, contact form@gic.mx.
