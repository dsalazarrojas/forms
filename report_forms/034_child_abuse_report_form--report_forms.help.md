# Child Abuse Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report suspected child abuse incidents. It's crucial to fill out this form accurately and thoroughly, providing all the necessary details to ensure a proper response to the situation.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each field carefully and answer all questions as completely and accurately as possible.
- Ensure you have all the necessary information before starting to fill out the form.
- If you are unsure about any part of the process, please do not hesitate to contact the relevant authorities or our support team.
- Save a copy of the completed form for your records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Report date** (ID: 2, Label: Report date, Required: true): Enter the date of the suspected child abuse incident.
* **Report time** (ID: 3, Label: Report time, Required: true): Enter the time of the suspected child abuse incident.
* **Reporter type** (ID: 4, Label: Reporter type, Required: true): Select the type of reporter:
	+ Mandatory reporter: A person or organization with a legal or professional obligation to report child abuse.
	+ Voluntary reporter: An individual who is not legally or professionally required to report child abuse.
	+ Anonymous: The reporter's identity is not known or not disclosed.
* **Reporter name (if not anonymous)** (ID: 5, Label: Reporter name (if not anonymous), Required: false): If the reporter is not anonymous, enter their name.
* **Contact phone number** (ID: 6, Label: Contact phone number, Required: false): Enter the reporter's contact phone number.
* **Contact email** (ID: 7, Label: Contact email, Required: false): Enter the reporter's contact email.
* **Occupation or professional role** (ID: 8, Label: Occupation or professional role, Required: false): Enter the reporter's occupation or professional role.
* **Child's full name** (ID: 9, Label: Child's full name, Required: true): Enter the child's full name.
* **Child's age or date of birth** (ID: 10, Label: Child's age or date of birth, Required: true): Enter the child's age or date of birth.
* **Child's address or location** (ID: 11, Label: Child's address or location, Required: true): Enter the child's address or location.
* **Type of suspected abuse** (ID: 12, Label: Type of suspected abuse, Required: true): Select all that apply:
	+ Physical abuse
	+ Emotional abuse
	+ Sexual abuse
	+ Neglect
	+ Abandonment
	+ Exposure to domestic violence
	+ Substance abuse
	+ Other
* **Detailed description of suspected abuse** (ID: 13, Label: Detailed description of suspected abuse, Required: true): Provide a detailed description of the suspected abuse.
* **Date of incident (if known)** (ID: 14, Label: Date of incident (if known), Required: false): Enter the date of the incident if known.
* **Location of incident** (ID: 15, Label: Location of incident, Required: false): Enter the location of the incident.
* **Visible injuries or marks** (ID: 16, Label: Visible injuries or marks, Required: true): Select the relevant answer:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Unknown
* **Description of injuries** (ID: 17, Label: Description of injuries, Required: false): Provide a description of the injuries.
* **Information about suspected perpetrator** (ID: 18, Label: Information about suspected perpetrator, Required: false): Provide any information about the suspected perpetrator.
* **Relationship to child (if known)** (ID: 19, Label: Relationship to child (if known), Required: false): Select the relevant answer:
	+ Parent
	+ Caregiver
	+ Family member
	+ Authority figure
	+ Peer
	+ Unknown
* **Information about household situation** (ID: 20, Label: Information about household situation, Required: false): Provide any information about the household situation.
* **What the child has said or communicated** (ID: 21, Label: What the child has said or communicated, Required: false): Provide any relevant information about what the child has said or communicated.
* **Any previous reports or concerns** (ID: 22, Label: Any previous reports or concerns, Required: true): Select the relevant answer:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Unknown
* **Does child need immediate protection?** (ID: 23, Label: Does child need immediate protection?, Required: true): Select the relevant answer:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Unclear
* **Witness information (if any)** (ID: 24, Label: Witness information (if any), Required: false): Provide any witness information.
* **Additional details or observations** (ID: 25, Label: Additional details or observations, Required: false): Provide any additional details or observations.
* **I declare that this report is made in good faith** (ID: 26, Label: I declare that this report is made in good faith, Required: true): Select the relevant answer:
	+ I agree
	+ I do not agree

This form is a serious and sensitive tool to gather critical information about suspected child abuse. Please fill it out carefully and truthfully to ensure the best possible response to the situation.
