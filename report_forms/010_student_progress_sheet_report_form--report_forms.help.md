# Student Progress Sheet Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Progress Sheet Report Form is used to collect and track student information and progress for educational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter the student's name in the "student_name" field.
*   Enter the student's ID number in the "student_id" field.
*   Select the date of the progress sheet in the "progress_sheet_date" field.
*   Enter the student's attendance rate in the "attendance_rate" field.
*   Enter the student's attendance status in the "attendance" field.
*   Enter the student's progress rate in the "progress_rate" field.
*   Enter the teacher's name in the "teacher_name" field.
*   Enter the teacher's ID number in the "teacher_id" field.
*   Select the student's section or group in the "student_section" field.
*   Enter the student's group or class in the "student_group" field.
*   Enter the student's subject or course in the "student_subject" field.
*   Enter the student's grade or level in the "grade" field.
*   Enter any comments or observations about the student in the "comments" field.
*   Select the student's status in the "status" field.
*   Enter the student's grade scale in the "grade_scale" field.
*   Enter the student's status scale in the "status_scale" field.
*   Enter the student's progress scale in the "progress_scale" field.
*   Enter any additional comments or observations about the student in the "comments" field.
*   Enter the student's section scale in the "section_scale" field.
*   Enter the student's progress in the "student_progress" field.
*   Enter the student's status progress in the "status_progress" field.
*   Enter the student's subject scale in the "subject_scale" field.
*   Enter the student's subject status in the "subject_status" field.
*   Enter the student's subject status scale in the "subject_status_scale" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **student_name** (text, required): Enter the student's name.
*   **student_id** (number, optional): Enter the student's ID number. If this field is not filled in, it is not a requirement.
*   **progress_sheet_date** (date, optional): Select the date of the progress sheet. If this field is not filled in, it will be treated as the current date.
*   **attendance_rate** (number, optional): Enter the student's attendance rate.
*   **attendance** (text, optional): Enter the student's attendance status.
*   **progress_rate** (number, optional): Enter the student's progress rate.
*   **teacher_name** (text, optional): Enter the teacher's name.
*   **teacher_id** (number, optional): Enter the teacher's ID number. If this field is not filled in, it is not a requirement.
*   **student_section** (select_one, optional): Select the student's section or group.
*   **student_group** (text, optional): Enter the student's group or class.
*   **student_subject** (text, optional): Enter the student's subject or course.
*   **grade** (text, optional): Enter the student's grade or level.
*   **comments** (text, optional): Enter any comments or observations about the student.
*   **status** (select_one, optional): Select the student's status.
*   **grade_scale** (number, optional): Enter the student's grade scale.
*   **status_scale** (text, optional): Enter the student's status scale.
*   **progress_scale** (number, optional): Enter the student's progress scale.
*   **student_section_scale** (text, optional): Enter the student's section scale.
*   **student_progress** (text, optional): Enter any comments or observations about the student's progress.
*   **status_progress** (text, optional): Enter the student's status progress.
*   **subject_scale** (text, optional): Enter the student's subject scale.
*   **subject_status** (text, optional): Enter the student's subject status.
*   **subject_status_scale** (text, optional): Enter the student's subject status scale.
