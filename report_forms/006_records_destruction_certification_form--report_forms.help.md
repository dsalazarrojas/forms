# Records Destruction Certification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Records Destruction Certification Form is a tool used to certify the destruction of records in accordance with relevant laws and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select who was responsible for destroying the records in the "Destroyed by" section.
2. Describe the records that were destroyed in the "Destroyed Records" section.
3. Record the date on which the records were destroyed in the "Destroyed Date" section.
4. Select the method used to destroy the records in the "Destruction Method" section.
5. Select the reason for destroying the records in the "Reason for Destruction" section.
6. If applicable, capture the witness signature and date in the "Witness Signature" sections.
7. If applicable, capture the destroyer's signature and date in the "Destroyer Signature" sections.
8. Record the certification date in the "Certification Date" section.
9. Capture the certification title in the "Certification Title" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Destroyed by

* **Destroyed by**: (User 1, Select, Required): Select who was responsible for destroying the records. This will help to identify the person accountable for the destruction process.

### Destroyed Records

* **Destroyed Records**: (Text, Optional): Provide a detailed description of the records that were destroyed. This will help to ensure that all relevant records are accounted for and properly destroyed.

### Destroyed Date

* **Destroyed Date**: (Date, Optional): Record the date on which the records were destroyed. This will help to track when the records were destroyed and ensure that the destruction process was followed correctly.

### Destruction Method

* **Destruction Method**: (Select, Optional): Select the method used to destroy the records, such as shredder or incinerator. This will ensure that the destruction process was done in accordance with relevant laws and regulations.

### Reason for Destruction

* **Reason for Destruction**: (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the reason for destroying the records, such as non-compliant or non-essential. This will help to ensure that the records were destroyed for the correct reasons.

### Witness Signature

* **Witness Signature**: (Text, Optional): If a witness is present, capture their signature in this section. This will provide an additional layer of accountability for the destruction process.

### Witness Signature Date

* **Witness Signature Date**: (Date, Optional): Record the date on which the witness signed the form. This will help to track when the witness signed the form.

### Witness Signature Title

* **Witness Signature Title**: (Text, Optional): Capture the title of the person signing as a witness. This will help to identify who the witness was.

### Destroyer Signature

* **Destroyer Signature**: (Text, Optional): Capture the signature of the person responsible for destroying the records. This will ensure that the person accountable for the destruction process has signed off on it.

### Destroyer Signature Date

* **Destroyer Signature Date**: (Date, Optional): Record the date on which the person responsible for destroying the records signed the form. This will help to track when the destroyer signed the form.

### Destroyer Signature Title

* **Destroyer Signature Title**: (Text, Optional): Capture the title of the person responsible for destroying the records. This will help to identify who the destroyer was.

### Certification Date

* **Certification Date**: (Date, Optional): Record the date on which the certification was signed. This will help to track when the certification was completed.

### Certification Title

* **Certification Title**: (Text, Optional): Capture the title of the person certifying the destruction. This will help to identify who certified the destruction process.
