> Meeting Report Template - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Meeting Report Template is a form used to capture meeting details for reporting and record-keeping. Please review the form and ensure that the information provided is relevant and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the meeting date (Field 2) and time (Field 3) of the meeting.
2. Enter the location (Field 4) where the meeting was held.
3. Describe the meeting objectives (Field 5) that were discussed.
4. Summarize the meeting outcomes (Field 6) or key takeaways.
5. Identify if all attendees (Field 7) were present during the meeting.
6. Provide any additional meeting notes (Field 8) that you'd like to record.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the meeting.
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the meeting.
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location where the meeting was held.
* **Meeting Objectives** (`meeting_objectives`, text, required/optional): Describe the meeting's main goals or objectives.
* **Meeting Outcomes** (`meeting_outcomes`, text, required/optional): Summarize the key takeaways or outcomes from the meeting.
* **Meeting Attendees** (`meeting_attendees`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check if all attendees were present during the meeting.
* **Meeting Notes** (`meeting_notes`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the meeting.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when filling in the form to ensure accurate reporting.
* Verify that the information provided is relevant to the meeting and your role.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
