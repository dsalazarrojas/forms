# School Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Incident Report Form is used to collect information about incidents that occur within the school setting. This form helps school administrators gather details about incidents such as accidents, fights, or other issues that require attention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form when you are in a situation where an incident has occurred or is about to occur.
* Provide your contact information so that the responder can reach out to you for additional questions or clarification.
* Choose the correct staff member to report the incident to (e.g. Assistant Principal, Counselor, Teacher).
* Provide as much detail as possible about the incident in the "Incident Details" field.
* Indicate if any staff members were affected by the incident.
* Select the correct options for "Report To" based on your school's incident reporting process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`): Enter the name of the student involved in the incident.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`): Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`): Provide as much detail as possible about the incident. This could include what happened, who was involved, and any other relevant information.
* **Staff Affected** (`staff_affected`): Indicate if any staff members were affected by the incident.
	+ Select "Yes" if any staff members were involved in or affected by the incident.
	+ Select "No" if no staff members were involved in or affected by the incident.
* **Responder Name** (`responder_name`): Enter your name so that the responder can contact you for additional questions or clarification.
* **Responder Contact Info** (`responder_contact`): Enter your contact information so that the responder can reach out to you for additional questions or clarification.
* **Report To** (`report_to`): Select the staff member you would like to report the incident to (e.g. Assistant Principal, Counselor, Teacher).
	+ Select "Assistant Principal" if you are unsure or the incident is severe.
	+ Select "Counselor" if you are unsure or need additional support services.
	+ Select "Teacher" if you are reporting an incident involving a teacher or students.
	+ Select "Other" if your school has designated another staff member to handle incident reports.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Enter any additional information that is not covered in the previous fields. This could include any relevant context, details, or follow-up questions you have.
