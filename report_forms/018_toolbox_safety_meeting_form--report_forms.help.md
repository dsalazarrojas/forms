# Toolbox Safety Meeting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to report on the status of toolboxes and toolkits used in various work areas to ensure that they are properly maintained and inspected according to company safety policies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Toolbox Meeting Details**: Enter a brief description of the toolbox meeting held, including the date and time of the meeting, the location, and the attendees present.
2. **Tool Kit**: Choose the correct options for toolkits that are available or not available in the toolbox.
3. **Tool List**: List the tools present in the toolbox. Be as detailed as possible, including the quantity and condition of each tool.
4. **Tool Location**: Enter the location where the toolbox is stored, including the building, floor/room number, and aisle/rack number.
5. **Tool Kit Responsible**: Indicate whether the tool kit is the responsibility of the current shift or a designated person.
6. **Inspection Frequency**: Choose the recommended frequency for inspecting the toolbox, based on the guidelines provided by the company safety policies.
7. **Last Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the last inspection, if any.
8. **Last Inspection Result**: Choose the result of the last inspection, indicating whether the toolbox was in good condition or not.
