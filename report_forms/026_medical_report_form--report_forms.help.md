# Medical Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Medical Report Form is a tool used to gather information about a patient's medical condition, symptoms, treatment, and outcomes. This form helps healthcare professionals gather data for further analysis and treatment recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Medical Report Form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by filling in the patient information section.
2. Describe the patient's symptoms in the "symptoms" section. Select all relevant symptoms that apply.
3. Provide a detailed description of the patient's medical history.
4. Report any medications the patient is currently taking.
5. Document the patient's current medical treatment and outcome.
6. Add any additional notes or comments if necessary.
7. Indicate whether you have access to a medical specialist or not.
8. Answer the questions regarding medical specialists for each specialist that applies.
9. Complete the contact information section, indicating whether you have contact information for the patient.
10. Finally, review your answers for accuracy and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Patient Information
* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text): Provide a brief description of the patient, including name, age, and any relevant medical ID numbers.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, select_multiple): Check all symptoms that apply to the patient's current condition, such as Headache, Fatigue, Nausea, Vomiting, or Fever.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text): Describe the patient's medical history, including any relevant illnesses, allergies, or surgeries.
* **Medications** (`medications`, select_one): Select the medications the patient is currently taking, such as Aspirin, Ibuprofen, or Vitamin C.
* **Medical Treatment** (`medical_treatment`, text): Document the patient's current medical treatment and any relevant details.
* **Patient Outcome** (`patient_outcome`, select_one): Report the patient's current outcome, such as Good, Fair, or Poor.

### Page 2: Additional Information
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, note): Add any additional comments or notes about the patient's condition.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, select_multiple): Indicate whether you have access to the patient's contact information, such as phone numbers or email addresses.
