# Civil Drawings Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for submitting civil drawings to the organization. It captures the essential details about the project, drawing references, and the submission itself.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Click on "Start Submission" to begin the form.
2.  Fill in the project details, drawing references, and submitter information in the corresponding fields.
3.  Upload the required files via the provided instructions.
4.  Confirm that the drawings meet the required standards by checking the certification statement.
5.  Finally, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project name** (project_name, text, required): Enter the official title of the project.
* **Drawing reference number** (drawing_reference_number, text, required): Enter the internal reference or revision number of the drawing.
* **Submitter name** (submitter_name, text, required): Enter the full name of the engineer or firm submitting the drawing.
* **Contact email** (contact_email, email, required): Enter the email address for any clarifications or questions regarding the submission.
* **Drawing scale** (drawing_scale, text, optional): If applicable, provide the scale used in the drawing.
* **Revision number** (revision_number, number, optional): If applicable, enter the revision number of the drawing.
* **File upload instructions** (file_upload_note, note, required): Read the instructions on how to upload files via the portal.
* **Purpose of submission** (purpose_of_submission, text, required): Briefly describe the purpose of the submission, allowing for permit review of tender documentation.
* **Certification statement** (certification_statement, note, required): Confirm that the drawings meet the required standards by checking the checkbox.
* **Submission date** (submission_date, date, required): Enter the current date.

## Tips

- Be sure to double-check the project and drawing details to avoid errors.
- Ensure you have all the required files and information before submitting the form.
- Review and confirm the certification statement to ensure the drawings meet the required standards.
