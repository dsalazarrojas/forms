# ridgewood_behavior_amp_incident_documentation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used for documenting incidents that have occurred within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Incident Location (1)**: Enter a detailed description of the location where the incident occurred.
2. **Incident Time (2)**: Select the date and time of the incident from the calendar.
3. **Reporter (3)**: Enter the name and role of the person who reported the incident.
4. **Description (4)**: Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
5. **Behavior (5)**: Describe the behavior or actions that led to the incident.
6. **Actions Taken (6)**: Document the actions taken by the staff in response to the incident.
7. **Parent Communication (7)**: Describe any communication made with the parent or guardian regarding the incident.
8. **Parent Signature (8)**: If the parent or guardian is present, obtain their signature as confirmation of their awareness of the incident.
9. **Coordinator Signature (9)**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the coordinator has reviewed and agreed to the incident documentation.
10. **Incident Date (10)**: Select the date of the incident from the calendar.
11. **Incident Time (11)**: Select the time of the incident from the clock.
12. **Location (12)**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Location (1)**: The location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time (2)**: The date and time of the incident.
* **Reporter (3)**: The person who reported the incident, including their name and role.
* **Description (4)**: A detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Behavior (5)**: A description of the behavior or actions that led to the incident.
* **Actions Taken (6)**: A record of the actions taken by the staff in response to the incident.
* **Parent Communication (7)**: A description of any communication made with the parent or guardian regarding the incident.
* **Parent Signature (8)**: The signature of the parent or guardian, confirming their awareness of the incident (optional).
* **Coordinator Signature (9)**: A confirmation from the coordinator that they have reviewed and agreed to the incident documentation (yes/no).
* **Incident Date (10)**: The date of the incident.
* **Incident Time (11)**: The time of the incident.
* **Location (12)**: The location where the incident occurred.

## Tips

* Ensure that you are filling this form for a valid incident and that it is not a duplicate entry.
* If you are unsure whether an incident has already been reported, contact your supervisor or the incident management team for clarification.
* Please review the form carefully and provide detailed information for each field to ensure accurate documentation.
