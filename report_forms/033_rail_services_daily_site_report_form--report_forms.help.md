# Rail Services Daily Site Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rail Services Daily Site Report Form is a tool used to report on the daily activities, incidents, and site information of rail services. This form is used to gather essential data for site management, quality control, and incident reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Site ID number at the top of the form, which is a unique identifier for the site.
2. Enter the Site Location, which is the geographical location of the site.
3. Choose the date of the report, which is the date the report is being submitted on.
4. Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if equipment was used during the reporting period.
5. Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if an incident was reported during the reporting period.
6. Provide any additional notes or comments about the site in the Notes field.
7. Enter any comments or notes about the site in the Comments field.
8. Enter your name and other contact information in the User Contact Info field.
9. Choose the Date of Submission, which is the date the form is being submitted.
10. Enter your name in the User Name field.
11. Enter your email address in the User Email field.
12. Enter your phone number in the User Phone field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Site ID** (number): Enter the unique ID number of the site.
* **Site Location** (text): Enter the geographical location of the site.
* **Date of Report** (date): Enter the date of the report.
* **Equipment Used** (select_multiple): Answer 'Yes' if equipment was used during the reporting period, or 'No' if not.
* **Incident Reported** (select_multiple): Answer 'Yes' if an incident was reported during the reporting period, or 'No' if not.
* **Notes** (note): Provide any additional notes or comments about the site.
* **Comments** (text): Enter any additional comments or notes about the site.
* **Submitted By** (text): Enter your name.
* **Location Map** (select_one): Answer 'Yes' if the location is on a map, or 'No' if not.
* **Date of Submission** (date): Enter the date the form is being submitted.
* **User Contact Info** (text): Enter your name, email, and phone number.
* **Submission Date** (date): Enter the date the form is being submitted.
* **User Name** (text): Enter your name.
* **User Email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **User Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate information in the Site ID field to ensure correct identification of the site.
* Use the Notes field to add any additional information that does not fit in the other fields.
* Use the User Contact Info field to provide your contact information, which will be used for follow-up purposes.
