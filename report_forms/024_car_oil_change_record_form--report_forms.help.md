# Car Oil Change Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Car Oil Change Record Form is designed to collect and record essential details about car oil changes, ensuring that maintenance and repair records are up-to-date and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the vehicle make and model to identify the vehicle being recorded.
2. Enter the vehicle year of manufacture.
3. Provide the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) if available.
4. Enter the date and mileage of the oil change.
5. Choose the brand and type of oil used.
6. Indicate if the oil filter was replaced, and provide the brand and model if known.
7. Record the total cost of the oil change.
8. Specify the service provider or shop where the oil change was performed.
9. Enter the recommended date and mileage for the next oil change.
10. Acknowledge the accuracy of the oil change record entry.
11. Confirm your name for the oil change record.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Make and Model** (`Car Make and Model`, `text`, required): This field helps identify the vehicle being recorded. Please enter the exact make and model of the vehicle, e.g., Toyota Corolla or Ford F-150.
* **Vehicle Year** (`Vehicle Year`, `number`, required): Enter the year of manufacture of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Identification Number (VIN)** (`Vehicle Identification Number (VIN)`, `text`, optional): If available, provide the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) code, which is typically 17 characters.
* **Date of Oil Change** (`Date of Oil Change`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the oil change was performed.
* **Mileage at Oil Change** (`Mileage at Oil Change`, `number`, required): Record the odometer reading during the oil change.
* **Brand of Oil Used** (`Brand of Oil Used`, `text`, required): Select the brand of oil used, e.g., Mobil 1, Castrol, Pennzoil.
* **Oil Viscosity (Weight)** (`Oil Viscosity (Weight)`, `select_one`, required): Choose the oil viscosity (weight) used, e.g., 5W-30, 0W-20.
* **Type of Oil Used** (`Type of Oil Used`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of oil used, e.g., Full Synthetic, Synthetic Blend, Conventional (Mineral).
* **Was the oil filter replaced?** (`Was the oil filter replaced?`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if the oil filter was replaced during the oil change.
* **Oil Filter Brand and Model** (`Oil Filter Brand and Model`, `text`, optional): If known, provide the brand and model of the oil filter used.
* **Total Cost of Oil Change ($)** (`Total Cost of Oil Change ($)` , `number`, optional): Record the total amount paid for the oil change, including labor.
* **Service Provider / Shop Name** (`Service Provider / Shop Name`, `text`, required): Specify the service provider or shop where the oil change was performed.
* **Next Oil Change Due Date** (`Next Oil Change Due Date`, `date`, optional): Enter the recommended date for the next oil change.
* **Next Oil Change Due Mileage** (`Next Oil Change Due Mileage`, `number`, optional): Record the recommended mileage for the next oil change.
* **I acknowledge the oil change record entry** (`I acknowledge the oil change record entry`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that the oil change record entry is accurate.
* **Type your name for oil change record confirmation** (`Type your name for oil change record confirmation`, `text`, required): Confirm your name for the oil change record entry.
* **Any additional comments or requests?** (`Any additional comments or requests?`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional details or comments about the oil change record.
