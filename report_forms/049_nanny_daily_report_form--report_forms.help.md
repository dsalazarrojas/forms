# Nanny Daily Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help nannies or caregivers report on their daily activities and events related to the children in their care, providing a record of important events and progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the question "Morning activities" by selecting either 'Yes' or 'No' from the options provided.
2. In the "Child duties" field, provide a text description of the tasks or milestones achieved by the child(ren) during the day.
3. In the "Notes" field, provide any additional comments or notes about the day's events or incidents.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6. Select the date of the report in the "Date" field.
7. Select the time of the report in the "Time" field.
8. In the "Assigned Tool" field, enter any tools or equipment used during the day.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Morning Activities** (`morning_activities`, select_multiple, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the child(ren) participated in morning activities.
* **Child Duties** (`child_duties`, text, required): Enter a text description of the child's duties or milestones achieved during the day.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about the day's events or incidents.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select the date of the report.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Select the time of the report.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter any tools or equipment used during the day.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Please be accurate and clear in your report.
* Please enter 'Yes' or 'No' for morning activities.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask the child's parent or guardian for clarification.
