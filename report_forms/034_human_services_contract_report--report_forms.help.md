# Human Services Contract Report - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Human Services Contract Report is a crucial document for tracking and verifying the status of human services contracts.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you're providing accurate information.
2. Fill in all required fields ( marked as "required" in red).
3. Double-check your submissions for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Provider (`Service Provider`, text, **Required**):** Enter the name of the service provider, as it appears on their contract or agreement with our organization.
* **Reporting Period (`Reporting Period`, date, **Required**):** Select a date range for the report, indicating when the services were provided.
* **Service Deliveries (`Service Deliveries`, select_multiple):** Choose all applicable options (Yes/No) to indicate whether service deliveries were made during the reporting period.
* **Contract Details (`Contract Details`, select_one):** Select (Yes/No) to confirm whether the contract details are accurate and up-to-date.
* **Service Updates (`Service Updates`, text, **Optional**):** Add any relevant updates or comments about the services provided.
* **Additional Comments (`Additional Comments`, text, **Optional**):** Enter any additional comments or observations about the report.
* **Reporting Status (`Reporting Status`, select_one):** Select (Active/Inactive) to indicate the current status of the report.
* **Reporting Start Date (`Reporting Start Date`, date, **Required**):** Enter the start date of the reporting period.
* **Reporting End Date (`Reporting End Date`, date, **Required**):** Enter the end date of the reporting period.
* **Submission Date (`Submission Date`, date, **Required**):** Select the date the report was submitted.
* **Submitter (`Submitter`, text, **Required**):** Enter your name and title as the person submitting this report.
* **Reporting Period Frequency (`Reporting Period Frequency`, select_one):** Confirm (Yes/No) whether the reporting period applies to all submissions.
* **Reporting Frequency (`Reporting Frequency`, text, **Optional**):** Provide any additional frequencies or intervals related to the reporting period.
* **Reporting Start Date Frequency (`Reporting Start Date Frequency`, select_one):** Confirm (Yes/No) whether the reporting start date applies to all submissions.
* **Reporting End Date Frequency (`Reporting End Date Frequency`, select_one):** Confirm (Yes/No) whether the reporting end date applies to all submissions.
* **Submitter Email (`Submitter Email`, email, **Optional**):** Enter your work email for contact purposes.
* **Submitter Phone (`Submitter Phone`, text, **Required**):** Enter your work phone number for contact purposes.
* **Submitter Comments (`Submitter Comments`, text, **Optional**):** Add any comments or feedback about the report.
* **Service Provider Notes (`Service Provider Notes`, text, **Optional**):** Enter any notes or comments from the service provider.
* **Service Provider Signature (`Service Provider Signature`, text, **Optional**):** Capture the service provider's digital signature for verification purposes.
* **Service Provider Name (`Service Provider Name`, text, **Required**):** Enter the name of the service provider.
* **Service Provider Email (`Service Provider Email`, email, **Optional**):** Enter the service provider's email address.
* **Service Provider Phone (`Service Provider Phone`, text, **Optional**):** Enter the service provider's phone number.
* **Contract Signed (`Contract Signed`, select_one):** Confirm (Yes/No) whether the contract is signed.
* **Contract Status (`Contract Status`, select_one):** Select (Active/Inactive) to indicate the current status of the contract.

Remember to review each field carefully and complete the form according to the provided guidance. If you have any doubts or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
