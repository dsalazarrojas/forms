# Housekeeping Hotel Lost And Found Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Housekeeping Hotel Lost And Found Form is used to report lost items found during a hotel staff member's shift. This form helps track the details of the item, the owner's information, and the person who found and reported the item.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the form instructions and purpose carefully before starting.
- Enter the details of the item found, including its description and value.
- Select the correct option for who found the item (Housekeeping Staff, Security Guard, Manager, or Guest).
- Enter the owner's name, email, and phone number (if available).
- Select who reported the item (Housekeeping Staff, Security Guard, Manager, or Guest).
- Choose the date and time the item was reported.
- Complete all required fields and review the form before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Report Form** (`report_form`, text, optional): A brief description of the item found.
* **Item Details** (`item_details`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of the item found, including its value.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text, optional): The name of the item's owner, if known.
* **Owner Email** (`owner_email`, email, optional): The email address of the item's owner, if available.
* **Owner Phone** (`owner_phone`, text, optional): The phone number of the item's owner, if available.
* **Found By** (`found_by`, select_one, optional): Choose who found the item (Housekeeping Staff, Security Guard, Manager, or Guest).
* **Reported By** (`reported_by`, select_multiple, optional): Choose who reported the item (Housekeeping Staff, Security Guard, Manager, or Guest).
* **Reported Date** (`reported_date`, date, optional): The date the item was reported.
* **Reported Time** (`reported_time`, time, optional): The time the item was reported.

Note: Completing this form thoroughly and accurately will help in reuniting the lost item with its rightful owner.
