# E Commerce Customer Service Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report any issues or concerns customers have experienced with our e-commerce services. It helps us to track and resolve problems efficiently, ensuring that our customers receive the best possible service.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Click on the "Customer Information" page to enter your name, email, and any other relevant details.
2. Click on the "Order Information" page to provide details about the order that relates to the issue you're reporting.
3. Click on the "Issue Information" page to describe the problem you experienced.
4. On the "Resolution Status" page, let us know how we resolved the issue or if it's still ongoing.
5. Finally, if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up, please provide your preferred contact method on the "Follow up" page.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, required/optional): This is the first page where you'll enter your contact information, including your name and email address. This will help us identify you and get in touch with you to discuss your issue.
* **Order Information** (`order_info`, text, required/optional): This page is for providing details about the order that's related to the issue you're experiencing. Please include the order number, date, and any other relevant information.
* **Issue Information** (`issue_info`, text, required/optional): Describe the problem you experienced with your order, including any error messages or other details that might be helpful for us to understand the issue.
* **Resolution Status** (`issue_resolution`, text, required/optional): Let us know how we resolved the issue, or if it's still ongoing. This will help us track the progress and ensure that we've addressed your concern.
* **Follow up** (`follow_up`, text, required/optional): If you'd like to be contacted back for follow-up, please provide your preferred contact method here.
