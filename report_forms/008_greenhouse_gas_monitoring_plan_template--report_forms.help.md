# Greenhouse Gas Monitoring Plan Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential information for a Greenhouse Gas Monitoring Plan, which is crucial for tracking and managing greenhouse gas emissions in various settings, such as research sites or facilities. The data collected will be used to understand emissions levels, patterns, and trends, and to inform strategies for mitigation and reduction.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling in the general information section, which includes details about the project title, facility name, site location, and other basic information.
2. Move on to the monitoring plan section, where you'll select the appropriate monitoring actions and actions that will be taken at the site.
3. Complete the monitoring actions section by selecting from the list and adding any additional actions as needed.
4. Ensure that the project start and end dates are accurately recorded.
5. Fill out the rest of the form with relevant site and project details.
