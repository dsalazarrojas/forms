# Time And Effort Reporting Form For Federal Grants - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Time And Effort Reporting Form for Federal Grants is a report form designed to capture information about time and effort related to federal grants. This form helps grant recipients provide accurate and compliant reports to their sponsors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Report Federal Grants" page and enter the required information in the corresponding fields.
2. For each field, enter the relevant details as required by the federal grant regulations.
3. Make sure to complete all the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the field label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Federal Grants** (`report_federal_grants`, text, required): This is the main section of the form where you report your grant activities. Enter a clear and concise description of the grant you are reporting.
* **Grant Funding** (`grant_funding`, text, required): Enter the total amount of funding received or expected for the grant.
* **Reporting Process** (`reporting_process`, text, required): Describe the process you followed to report your grant activities, including any relevant documentation or records.
* **Time and Effort Reporting Form** (`time_and_effort_reporting_form`, text, required): Enter the name of the form you are using to report your time and effort.
* **Accurate and Compliant Reports** (`accurate_and_compliant_reports`, text, required): Confirm that your reports are accurate and compliant with federal regulations.
* **Save Time and Resources** (`save_time_and_resources`, text, required): Indicate how the reporting process has helped you save time and resources.
* **Hassle-free Solution** (`hassle_free_solution`, text, required): Describe any hassles or challenges you experienced while using the reporting form.
* **Federal Funding** (`federal_funding`, text, required): Enter any additional federal funding sources or sources not already reported.

## Tips
- Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your sponsor or grant administrator.
- Keep a record of your reports to ensure easy reference and audit trail.
