# inventory_system_failure_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The inventory system failure contact form is designed for users to report failures or issues related to the inventory system. This form helps to gather information about the problem, its priority, and the contact details of the user reporting the issue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select whether the inventory system has failed (`Inventory System Failure` field).
2. Choose the priority of the report (`Report Priority` field).
3. Provide a detailed description of the issue (`Report Details` field).
4. Select how urgent the report is (`Report Urgency` field).
5. Choose who should be assigned to address the issue (`Assigned To` field).
6. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number (`Contact Information` and `Contact Phone` fields).
7. Specify the location where the incident occurred (`Incident Location` field).
8. Enter the date of the incident (`Incident Date` field).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inventory System Failure** (`Inventory System Failure`): This field allows you to indicate whether the inventory system has failed. You can select 'Yes' or 'No' to respond to this question.
* **Report Priority** (`Report Priority`): Choose how important you think this issue is by selecting High, Medium, or Low.
* **Report Details** (`Report Details`): Provide a clear and detailed description of the issue you are reporting. This field is used to gather more information about the problem.
* **Report Urgency** (`Report Urgency`): Select how urgently you need the issue to be addressed. You can choose 'Now', 'Within 24 Hours', 'Within 48 Hours', or 'As Soon As Possible'.
* **Assigned To** (`Assigned To`): Choose who you want to be assigned to address the issue. You can select 'chatjimmy' or 'other' to specify another team member or person.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`): Enter your contact email address so we can get in touch with you to follow up on the issue.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`): Enter your phone number so we can contact you directly to discuss the issue.
* **Incident Location** (`Incident Location`): Provide the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Date** (`Incident Date`): Enter the date when the incident occurred.
