# Hostile Network Investigation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Hostile Network Investigation Form is used to collect and document information about hostile network incidents, including summaries, dates, locations, incident types, and other relevant details. This form is used to facilitate investigation and resolution of incidents.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Provide a brief summary of the incident in the "Incident Summary" field.
2.  Enter the date and time of the incident in the "Incident Date" and "Incident Time" fields.
3.  Select the correct time zone where the incident occurred in the "Time Zone" field.
4.  Choose the location where the incident occurred from the "Incident Location" dropdown.
5.  Select the type of incident from the "Incident Type" dropdown.
6.  Provide any additional details about the incident in the "Incident Details" field.
7.  Identify the person who reported the incident in the "Incident Reporter" field.
8.  Enter any notes from the incident reporter in the "Incident Reporter Notes" field.
9.  Select any members of the security team involved in the investigation from the "Security Team" dropdown.
10.  Update the investigation status in the "Investigation Status" field.
11.  Enter the resolution date and time in the "Resolution Date" and "Resolution Time" fields.
12.  Select the resolution status in the "Resolution Status" field.
13.  Enter any final notes about the investigation in the "Investigation Notes" field.
14.  Attach any supporting files or documents related to the incident in the "Attach File" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report**: The main report field, where you can enter a brief description of the incident.
	+ Type: text
	+ Purpose: Provide a brief summary of the incident.
* **Incident Summary**: Enter a brief description of the incident.
	+ Type: text
	+ Purpose: Provide a brief summary of the incident.
* **Incident Date**: Enter the date of the incident.
	+ Type: date
	+ Purpose: Record the date of the incident.
* **Incident Time**: Enter the time of the incident.
	+ Type: time
	+ Purpose: Record the time of the incident.
* **Time Zone**: Select the time zone where the incident occurred.
	+ Type: number
	+ Purpose: Specify the time zone for correct timestamping.
* **Incident Location**: Select the location where the incident occurred.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Purpose: Choose the location of the incident.
* **Incident Type**: Select the type of incident.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Purpose: Choose the type of incident.
* **Incident Details**: Enter any additional details about the incident.
	+ Type: text
	+ Purpose: Provide any additional information about the incident.
* **Incident Reporter**: Identify the person who reported the incident.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Purpose: Identify the person who reported the incident.
* **Incident Reporter Notes**: Enter any notes from the incident reporter.
	+ Type: text
	+ Purpose: Record any notes from the incident reporter.
* **Security Team**: Select any team members involved in the investigation.
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Purpose: Choose team members involved in the investigation.
* **Investigation Status**: Update the status of the investigation.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Purpose: Track the status of the investigation.
* **Resolution Date**: Enter the date of the resolution.
	+ Type: date
	+ Purpose: Record the date of the resolution.
* **Resolution Time**: Enter the time of the resolution.
	+ Type: time
	+ Purpose: Record the time of the resolution.
* **Resolution Status**: Update the status of the resolution.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Purpose: Track the status of the resolution.
* **Investigation Notes**: Enter any final notes about the investigation.
	+ Type: text
	+ Purpose: Record any final notes about the investigation.
* **Attach File**: Attach any supporting files or documents.
	+ Type: text
	+ Purpose: Attach any supporting files or documents.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you enter accurate and relevant information to facilitate the investigation and resolution of the incident.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the security team for assistance.
* Please keep the investigation notes up to date to ensure that the incident is properly tracked.
