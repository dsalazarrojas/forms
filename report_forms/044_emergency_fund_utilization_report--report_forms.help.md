# emergency_fund_utilization_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report on the utilization of emergency funds.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the date of the emergency.
2. Enter the amount allocated for the emergency.
3. Indicate whether supporting documents are available.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **emergency_fund_utilization_report**: This is the title of the report. (Note: This field is required.)
* **date_of_emergency**: Enter the date of the emergency. (Date field)
* **amount_allocated**: Enter the amount allocated for the emergency. (Number field)
* **supporting_documents**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether supporting documents are available. (Multiple selection field)

## Tips
* If you're unsure about the amount allocated, refer to your organization's financial records for accurate information.
* If you're missing supporting documents, make sure to gather them before submitting this report.
* Double-check your submission to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
