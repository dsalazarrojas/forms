# Basketball Referee Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect data for basketball games, including game details, team and player information, and game outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for the game date, game time, game venue, head referee, home team name, away team name, and game outcome.
2. Provide the scores for both teams.
3. Report any notable incidents that occurred during the game.
4. Enter any comments or observations from the head referee.
5. Confirm that the report is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report date** (`report_date`): Enter the date you are submitting this report.
* **Game date** (`game_date`): Enter the date of the game being reported.
* **Game time** (`game_time`): Enter the time of the game being reported.
* **Game venue** (`game_venue`): Enter the name of the game venue where the game took place.
* **Head referee** (`head_referee`): Enter the name of the head referee for the game.
* **Home team name** (`home_team_name`): Enter the name of the home team.
* **Away team name** (`away_team_name`): Enter the name of the away team.
* **Home team final score** (`home_final_score`): Enter the final score of the home team.
* **Away team final score** (`away_final_score`): Enter the final score of the away team.
* **Game outcome** (`game_outcome`): Select the correct outcome of the game from the options provided.
* **Report confirmation** (`report_confirmation`): Confirm that the report is accurate and complete by selecting the "I Confirm" option.

## Tips

* Be accurate and complete when filling out the form to ensure the data is useful for analysis and decision-making.
* Double-check the scores and game outcome to ensure they are correct.
* Provide any notable incidents or observations that occurred during the game to help improve future game management.
