# Submarine Defect Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Submarine Defect Report Form is used to collect information about defects found on submarines, allowing for efficient tracking and reporting of issues. This form ensures that defects are documented and communicated effectively, facilitating corrective actions and preventing potential safety hazards.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select a category or subcategory that best describes the nature of the defect, if applicable.
2. Enter general information about the defect, including the name of the affected submarine and any relevant details.
3. Provide a detailed description of the defect, including its location, severity, and any contributing factors.
4. Fill in the report date and report time to indicate when the defect was observed.
5. Enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
6. Review your submission to ensure it is complete and accurate before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (Page 1)** (`general_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the defect, including the submarine's name and any relevant context.
* **Report Header (Page 1)** (`report_header`, text, optional): Enter a unique header for the report, if desired.
* **Defect Description (Page 1)** (`defect_description`, text, optional): Please provide a detailed description of the defect, including its location, severity, and any contributing factors.
* **Report Date (Page 1)** (`report_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of when the defect was observed.
* **Report Time (Page 1)** (`report_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of when the defect was observed.
* **Contact Information (Page 2)** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter any relevant contact information, such as a supervisor or responsible person's name.
* **Name (Page 2)** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Email (Page 2)** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone (Page 2)** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.

## Tips
- Ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or a designated authority.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid missing essential information.
