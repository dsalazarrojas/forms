# production_department_activity_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to report activities performed by employees in the production department.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the start date of the activity in the "Start Date" field.
2. Enter the end date of the activity in the "End Date" field.
3. Enter the employee's name who performed the task in the "Employee Name" field.
4. Enter the task's unique identifier in the "Task ID" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the activity started.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the activity ended.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee who performed the task.
* **Task ID** (`task_id`, number, optional): Enter the unique identifier for the task being performed.

## Tips

- Ensure that you're accurate with the dates and task IDs to ensure that the data is properly recorded and reported.
- Double-check that you're not already using this form for another task or in another context.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the production department or your supervisor.
