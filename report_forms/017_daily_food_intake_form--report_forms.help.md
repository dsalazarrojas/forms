# Daily Food Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to record an individual's food intake for a specific day. It helps users track their daily consumption of various food items, which can be useful for nutritional planning, dietary tracking, or other purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the food items you consumed on the day from the dropdown menu provided.
2. Choose the date of the day for which you are reporting your food intake.
3. If desired, enter notes about your food intake in the text area provided.
4. If you're unsure or need to report multiple food items, use the second "Select Food Intake Amount" field.
5. Enter the time of day for which you are reporting your food intake.
6. Enter the quantities of the selected food items in the "Select Food Intake Amount" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Food** (`daily_food_intake_form_2`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the food items you consumed during the day from the dropdown menu.
* **Food Intake Form Notes** (`daily_food_intake_form_3`, `note`, required:false): Enter any additional notes or comments about your food intake.
* **Select Date** (`daily_food_intake_form_4`, `date`, required:false): Select the date for which you are reporting your food intake.
* **Select Time** (`daily_food_intake_form_5`, `time`, required:false): Select the time of day for which you are reporting your food intake.
* **Select Food Intake Amount** (`daily_food_intake_form_6`, `number`, required:false): Enter the quantity of the selected food item you consumed.
* **Select Food Intake Amount (2)** (`daily_food_intake_form_7`, `number`, required:false): Enter the quantity of the selected food item you consumed, if unsure or need to report multiple food items.
* **Select Food Intake Amount (3)** (`daily_food_intake_form_8`, `number`, required:false): Enter the quantity of the selected food item you consumed, if unsure or need to report multiple food items.

## Tips

* Please ensure to report accurate and complete information about your food intake to get the most out of this form.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact your supervisor or a designated representative.
