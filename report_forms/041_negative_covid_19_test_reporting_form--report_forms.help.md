# Negative COVID 19 Test Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to report negative COVID-19 test results from healthcare settings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the requested information for each field.
2. Ensure that you have the necessary permissions to report on the patient's behalf.
3. Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Test Date (1)**: Enter the date of the COVID-19 test in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Patient Name (2)**: Enter the name of the patient who was tested for COVID-19.
* **Test Result (3)**: Choose "Yes" if the test result was negative.
* **Result Details (4)**: Enter any additional comments or details about the test result, if necessary.
* **Patient Location (5)**: Enter the location where the test was conducted.
* **Test Type (6)**: Choose all applicable test types (e.g., PCR, Antigen, etc.).
* **Test Site (7)**: Choose "Yes" if the test was conducted at the main test site.
* **Staff Member Name (8)**: Enter the name of the staff member reporting the test result.
* **Staff Member Email (9)**: Enter the email address of the staff member reporting the test result.
* **Staff Member Phone (10)**: Enter the phone number of the staff member reporting the test result.

## Tips
* Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
* Ensure that you are authorized to report on the patient's behalf.
* If you need help or have questions, contact your supervisor or HR department.
