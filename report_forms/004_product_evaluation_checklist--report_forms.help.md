# Product Evaluation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect product evaluation data from customers or employees. It is meant to be filled out by individuals who have evaluated a product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
2. Select the type of product you evaluated from the "Product Type" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the product name, price, and image URL (if applicable).
4. Enter your evaluation date and time.
5. Enter your contact email address.
6. Enter your contact phone number and address.
7. Enter your rating of the product (1-5).
8. Enter your weighted rating (optional).
9. Provide any additional notes or comments about the product in the "Evaluation Comments" field.
10. Enter any additional product evaluation details in the "Evaluation Details" field.
11. Enter any product evaluations or comments about the product in the "Product Evaluations" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Product Type** (`product_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of product you evaluated.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, optional): Enter your rating of the product (1-5).
* **Weighted Rating** (`weighted_rating`, `number`, optional): Enter your weighted rating (optional).
* **Evaluation Comments** (`evaluation_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments or feedback about the product.
* **Evaluation Details** (`evaluation_details`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional evaluation details.
* **Product Evaluations** (`product_evaluations`, `note`, optional): Enter any product evaluations or comments.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the product.
* **Product Price** (`product_price`, `text`, optional): Enter the price of the product.
* **Product Image** (`product_image`, `text`, optional): Enter the URL of the product image.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the evaluation.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact address.
* **Evaluation Notes** (`evaluation_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional evaluation notes.
