# Infant Daily Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Infant Daily Report Form is used to report on the daily activities and health status of infants in a clinical setting. This form helps healthcare professionals track the progress of infant patients and monitor any changes or concerns that may arise.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the date and time of the day's activities.
2. Report any morning activities that occurred, such as feeding, bathing, or playtime.
3. Indicate whether the infant was fed with a bottle or breast, and provide any relevant details.
4. Record the number of diaper changes performed.
5. Note the infant's sleep duration and schedule.
6. Report any medication administered, including the dosage and timing.
7. List any medical conditions or concerns that arose during the day.
8. Report any allergies or sensitivities the infant may have.
9. Provide any medical notes or observations from the day.
10. Report any other notable events or activities.
11. Sign and date the form to confirm completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Morning Activities** (`morning_activities`, text, optional): Report any activities that occurred in the morning, such as feeding, bathing, or playtime.
* **Feeding bottle/Feeding breast** (`feeding_bottle/feeding_breast`, text, optional): Indicate whether the infant was fed with a bottle or breast, and provide any relevant details.
* **Diaper changes** (`diaper_changes`, number, optional): Record the number of diaper changes performed.
* **Sleep Duration** (`sleep_duration`, number, optional): Note the infant's sleep duration in hours.
* **Sleep Schedule** (`sleep_schedule`, select one, optional): Choose from 8 hours, 12 hours, or 16 hours to report the infant's sleep schedule.
* **Medication** (`medication`, text, optional): Report any medication administered, including the dosage and timing.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): List any medical conditions or concerns that arose during the day.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): Report any allergies or sensitivities the infant may have.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, text, optional): Provide any medical notes or observations from the day.
* **Other Events** (`other_events`, text, optional): Report any other notable events or activities.
* **Observations** (`observations`, text, optional): Provide any observations or comments about the infant's behavior or condition.
* **Signature** (`signature`, time, optional): Sign and date the form to confirm completion.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the report.
