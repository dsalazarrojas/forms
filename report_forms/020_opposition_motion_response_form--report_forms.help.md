# Opposition Motion Response Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Opposition Motion Response Form is used to submit a formal response to a motion presented to a governing body.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review the information presented on the first page.
2.  Select the relevant information about the motion (opposition motion or other) on the second page.
3.  Provide a written response to the motion on the third page.
4.  Select any supporting documents that are relevant to the response.
5.  Enter the date of the motion.
6.  Enter the time of the motion.
7.  Enter the opposition motion number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Opposition Motion Response Form Page 1
*   **Opposition Motion Response Form Page 1** (`opposition_motion_response_form_page_1`, text, optional): This is the main body of the response form where you can enter your response.
*   **Motion Details** (`motion_details`, select_one, optional): Select the type of motion the response is addressing. Options include: Opposition Motion and Other.
*   **Written Response** (`written_response`, text, optional): Provide a clear and concise written response to the motion.
*   **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, select_multiple, optional): Select any documents that are relevant to the response.
*   **Motion Date** (`motion_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the motion.
*   **Motion Time** (`motion_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the motion.
*   **Opposition Motion Number** (`opposition_motion_number`, number, optional): Enter the opposition motion number.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that the response is accurate and complete.
*   If a response has already been submitted, do not resubmit.
*   If unsure about any field or have any questions, please reach out to the governing body for clarification.
*   Be concise and provide clear, relevant information.
