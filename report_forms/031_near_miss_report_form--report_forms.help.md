# near_miss_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for reporting near misses, close calls, or significant incidents that have occurred during work hours, operations, or events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the description of the incident in the **incident_description** field.
2. Enter the date and time of the incident in the **incident_date** field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Enter the time of the incident in the format HH:MM AM/PM in the **incident_time** field.
4. Provide a location where the incident occurred in the **location** field.
5. Describe the root cause of the incident in the **root_cause** field.
6. List any preventive actions taken or planned to prevent future occurrences in the **preventive_actions** field.
7. Identify who was involved in reporting the incident in the **report_who** field.
8. Describe what was reported (e.g., equipment, process, etc.) in the **report_what** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **incident_description** (Note): Provide a detailed description of the incident that occurred.
* **incident_date** (Date): Enter the date of the incident in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **incident_time** (Time): Enter the time of the incident in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **location** (Text): Describe the location where the incident occurred.
* **root_cause** (Text): Identify the root cause of the incident.
* **preventive_actions** (Text): List any preventive actions taken or planned to prevent future occurrences.
* **report_who** (Text): Identify who reported the incident.
* **report_what** (Text): Describe what was reported (e.g., equipment, process, etc.).
