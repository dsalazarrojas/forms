# Police Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This Police Report form is used to document and track incidents reported to the police department. It captures key information about the incident, including details about the witness, the incident location, and the officer who reported the incident.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the required information for the witness and incident details on the first page.
2. If the incident date and time are known, enter it on the first page. If not, you can leave it blank or fill it in on a later page.
3. Enter the name of the officer who reported the incident on the second page.
4. Select the contact method used to contact the witness on the second page.
5. If you have any additional information about the suspect, enter it on the second page.
6. Enter the details of the incident as reported by the witness on the third page, including the phone number and email address of the witness.
7. Review and submit the form when you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Incident Details
* **Title** (`police_report_form_1`, text, required): Enter a brief title or description of the incident.
* **Witness** (`witness`, text, required): Enter the name of the witness who reported the incident.
* **Incident Date Time** (`incident_date_time`, date, optional): Enter the date and time of the incident. Use the "YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS" format.
* **Officer Name** (`officer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the officer who is responsible for investigating the incident.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the incident.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of the incident.

### Page 2: Witness Contact and Officer Details
* **Witness Contact Method** (`witness_contact_method`, select multiple, optional): Select how you would like to contact the witness (Phone, Email, or Other).
* **Suspect Details** (`suspect_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the suspect.
* **Police Officer Details** (`police_officer_details`, text, required): Enter the details of the officer who is investigating the incident.
* **Incident Reported By** (`incident_reported_by`, text, required): Enter your name as the person who reported the incident.
* **Incident Reported By Phone** (`incident_reported_by_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number as the person who reported the incident.
* **Incident Reported By Email** (`incident_reported_by_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address as the person who reported the incident.
* **Incident Reported By Other** (`incident_reported_by_other`, text, optional): Enter any other contact information for the person who reported the incident.

### Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields carefully and accurately.
* Use the correct format for date and time fields.
* If you need to contact the witness, select the preferred method from the dropdown menu.
* Review and verify all information before submitting the form.
