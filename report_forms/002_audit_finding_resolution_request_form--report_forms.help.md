# Audit Finding Resolution Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Audit Finding Resolution Request Form" is used to update the status of internal audit findings. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the finding ID to identify the audit finding.
2. Enter the name of the auditor or reporter who identified the finding.
3. Select the department responsible for resolving the finding.
4. Provide a summary of the audit finding and evidence in the "description of finding" field.
5. Suggest a corrective action to resolve the finding in the "corrective action" field.
6. Enter the target date to complete the corrective action in the "proposed resolution date" field.
7. Select the current status of the resolution request from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Finding ID**: Enter the internal audit finding identifier.
* **Reported by**: Enter the name of the auditor or reporter who identified the finding.
* **Department Responsible**: Select the department assigned to resolve the finding. This field should only be completed by the department responsible for the finding.
* **Description of Finding**: Provide a summary of the audit finding and evidence. 
* **Corrective Action**: Suggest a corrective action to resolve the finding. 
* **Proposed Resolution Date**: Enter the target date to complete the corrective action.
* **Resolution Status**: Select the current status of the resolution request from the options provided.
