# Business Automation Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help businesses assess their current state of automation, identify areas for improvement, and set realistic goals for future automation implementation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct answers for each field based on your company's current state and goals.
2. For fields with multiple options, select all applicable answers.
3. If a field is marked as "required," ensure that you provide a valid response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Department or Division** (`department`, `text`, required): Identify the department or division you are representing.
* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name.
* **Your Email** (`respondent_email`, `email`, required): Enter your business email address.
* **Current Automation Readiness Level** (`automation_readiness`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of automation implementation your business currently has:
	+ Just Getting Started: Beginning to explore automation options.
	+ Exploring Options: Evaluating various automation tools and techniques.
	+ In Planning Phase: Developing a plan for automation implementation.
	+ Currently Implementing: Partially or fully automated processes.
	+ Partially Automated: Some automation is in place, but not fully integrated.
	+ Highly Automated: Almost all processes are automated.
* **Which processes are currently automated?** (`current_automated_processes`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select all processes that are currently automated:
	+ Finance and Accounting
	+ Human Resources
	+ Sales Management
	+ Customer Service
	+ Inventory Management
	+ Marketing
	+ Data Entry
	+ Reporting
* **Priority Areas for Automation** (`automation_priority_areas`, `select_multiple`, required): Identify the areas where you want to automate first:
	+ Reduce Manual Work
	+ Improve Efficiency
	+ Reduce Costs
	+ Improve Quality
	+ Enhance Customer Experience
* **Biggest Automation Challenges** (`biggest_challenges`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select all challenges your business faces:
	+ Limited Budget
	+ Lack of Technical Knowledge
	+ Change Resistance
	+ Legacy Systems
	+ Data Integration Issues
	+ Time Constraints
* **Estimated Budget for Automation** (`budget_allocated`, `number`, not required): Enter your estimated annual budget for automation (in currency).
* **Expected Implementation Timeline** (`timeline`, `select_one`, required): Select the expected time frame for automation implementation:
	+ Immediate (0-3 months)
	+ Short-term (3-6 months)
	+ Medium-term (6-12 months)
	+ Long-term (12+ months)
* **Currently Used Business Tools** (`existing_tools`, `text`, not required): List the software tools your business currently uses.
* **Expected ROI Timeframe** (`roi_expectations`, `select_one`, not required): Select the expected timeframe for return on investment:
	+ Less than 6 months
	+ 6-12 months
	+ 1-2 years
	+ 2+ years
	+ Unsure
* **Staff Training Willingness** (`staff_training`, `select_one`, required): Assess your team's receptiveness to training:
	+ Very Willing
	+ Willing
	+ Neutral
	+ Reluctant
	+ Very Reluctant
* **Additional Comments or Specific Needs** (`additional_comments`, `text`, not required): Provide any additional information or specific needs you'd like to share.
