# Mileage Log And Expense Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help employees accurately track and report their business mileage and expenses. By completing this form, you will be able to record the details of your trips, including the mileage log, date, odometer reading, business use, expense category, and amount spent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the trip in the "Date" field.
2. Record the odometer reading at the beginning and end of the trip in the "Odometer Reading" field.
3. Indicate whether the trip was for business use or not by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Business Use" field.
4. If the trip was for business use, describe the purpose of the trip in the "Business Use Description" field.
5. Select the category of expense incurred during the trip from the "Expense Category" field.
6. Enter the amount spent during the trip in the "Amount" field.
7. Add any additional notes or comments about the trip in the "Notes" field.
8. Enter your name in the "Submitted By" field.
9. Enter the date you submitted the form in the "Submitted On" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the trip.
* **Mileage Log** (`text`, optional): Record the details of the trip, including the miles driven, routes taken, and any notable events.
* **Odometer Reading** (`number`, optional): Record the odometer reading at the beginning and end of the trip.
* **Business Use** (`select_one`, optional): Indicate whether the trip was for business use or not.
* **Business Use Description** (`text`, optional): Describe the purpose of the trip if it was for business use.
* **Expense Category** (`select_one`, optional): Select the category of expense incurred during the trip.
* **Amount** (`number`, optional): Enter the amount spent during the trip.
* **Notes** (`text`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the trip.
* **Submitted By** (`text`, optional): Enter your name.
* **Submitted On** (`date`, optional): Enter the date you submitted the form.

## Tips
Please ensure that you have all necessary receipts or supporting documentation to substantiate your expenses.
