# Evacuation Drill Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Evacuation Drill Report Form is a document used to gather information about the details of an evacuation drill conducted by the organization. It is a crucial tool for tracking and improving emergency response procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the form carefully and accurately**, providing the following information:
	* Your name and contact details
	* Details about the drill, including location and date/time
	* Any observations or issues encountered during the drill
2. Ensure that you fill out the form once per drill, and do not submit duplicate reports.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, Required: False): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, Required: False): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, Required: False): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, Required: False): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, Required: False): Enter the location where the drill took place.

## Tips
- Ensure that you fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Review your submission before submitting to ensure that all required fields are filled out.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the relevant department.
