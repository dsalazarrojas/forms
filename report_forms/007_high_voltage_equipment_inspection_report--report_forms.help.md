# High Voltage Equipment Inspection Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
The High Voltage Equipment Inspection Report is a document used to report on the inspection of high voltage equipment, such as electrical equipment, generators, or other machinery that operates at high voltage levels. This form is used to collect information about the equipment's inspection date, equipment ID, voltage level, inspection location, inspection result, any notes taken during the inspection, the assigned inspector's name, and their signature.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Inspection Date** field with the date of the inspection in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
2. Enter the **Equipment ID** in the corresponding field, which should match the identifier assigned to the equipment being inspected.
3. Select the **Voltage Level** of the equipment from the dropdown menu:
	* Low: Equipment with a voltage level below 1000V.
	* Medium: Equipment with a voltage level between 1000V and 2000V.
	* High: Equipment with a voltage level above 2000V.
4. Enter the **Inspection Location** where the equipment was inspected, such as a room number or a specific area within a facility.
5. Select the **Inspection Result** from the dropdown menu:
	* Pass: The equipment passed the inspection.
	* Fail: The equipment failed the inspection.
	* N/A: The equipment was not applicable to the inspection.
6. Add any **Notes** regarding the inspection, such as observations, actions taken, or further recommendations.
7. Enter the name of the **Assigned Inspector** who performed the inspection.
8. Sign the form with your **Signature**, indicating that you have reviewed and approved the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, required): Date of the inspection in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
- **Equipment ID** (`equipment_id`, required): Identifier for the equipment being inspected. This should match the identifier assigned to the equipment by the manufacturer or facility management.
- **Voltage Level** (`voltage_level`, required): Select the voltage level of the equipment from the dropdown menu.
- **Inspection Location** (`inspection_location`, optional): Location where the inspection was conducted.
- **Inspection Result** (`inspection_result`, required): Select the outcome of the inspection from the dropdown menu.
- **Notes** (`notes`, optional): Any comments or observations made during the inspection.
- **Assigned Inspector** (`assigned_inspector`, optional): Name of the person who performed the inspection.
- **Signature** (`signature`, required): Your signature, indicating that you have reviewed and approved the inspection.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
- Select the correct voltage level and inspection result to accurately reflect the equipment's condition.
- Use the **Notes** field to provide any additional information that may not fit in the other fields.
- Ensure that your **Signature** is accurate and legible.
