# Risk Based Internal Audit Plan Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you create a risk-based internal audit plan for your organization. The plan will guide the identification, evaluation, and mitigation of potential risks to ensure the accuracy and reliability of your financial statements and operations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the type of audit you want to perform (e.g., Audit of Financial Statements, Audit of Compliance with Laws and Regulations, Audit of IT System Security, or Other).
2. Identify any additional audit types that you want to include (e.g., multiple types of audits).
3. Prioritize the selected audits (e.g., high, medium, low).
4. Specify any dates and times for the audit (e.g., start and end dates, audit duration).
5. Provide any relevant contact information (e.g., email, phone number).
