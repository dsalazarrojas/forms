# Risk Based Internal Audit Plan Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you create a risk-based internal audit plan for your organization. The plan will guide the identification, evaluation, and mitigation of potential risks to ensure the accuracy and reliability of your financial statements and operations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the type of audit you want to perform (e.g., Audit of Financial Statements, Audit of Compliance with Laws and Regulations, Audit of IT System Security, or Other).
2. Identify any additional audit types that you want to include (e.g., multiple types of audits).
3. Prioritize the selected audits (e.g., high, medium, low).
4. Specify any dates and times for the audit (e.g., start and end dates, audit duration).
5. Provide any relevant contact information (e.g., email, phone number).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Risk Based** (`risk_based_internal_audit_plan_template_page_4`): Enter a brief description of the risk-based internal audit plan, if applicable.
* **Select One** (`risk_based_internal_audit_plan_template_page_2`): Choose one of the listed audit types (e.g., Audit of Financial Statements, Audit of Compliance with Laws and Regulations, Audit of IT System Security, or Other).
* **Select Multiple** (`risk_based_internal_audit_plan_template_page_3`): Choose multiple audit types from the list.
* **Priority** (`risk_based_internal_audit_plan_template_page_5`): Enter a numerical value to indicate the priority level of the selected audits (e.g., 1-5, where 1 is high, and 5 is low).
* **Date** (`risk_based_internal_audit_plan_template_page_6`): Enter the start and end dates of the audit, in the format MM/DD/YY or MM/DD/YY.
* **Time** (`risk_based_internal_audit_plan_template_page_7`): Enter the start and end times of the audit, in the format HH:MM or HH:MM.
* **Email** (`risk_based_internal_audit_plan_template_page_8`): Enter any relevant email addresses for communication.
* **Phone** (`risk_based_internal_audit_plan_template_page_9`): Enter any relevant phone numbers for communication.
* **Note** (`risk_based_internal_audit_plan_template_page_10`): Enter any additional notes or comments about the audit.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide clear and concise information for each field.
* Review each field carefully to ensure you're answering the right questions.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or assistance.
