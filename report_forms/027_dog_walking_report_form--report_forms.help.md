# Dog Walking Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record information about a dog walking session, including visit details, pet information, and walker and owner contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the appropriate page from the drop-down menu or by clicking on the "Visit Information" page.
2. Fill out the required fields for the "Visit Information" page, including:
	* Date and start time of the walk.
	* Weather conditions during the walk.
	* Route taken during the walk.
3. For the "Pet Information" page, fill out the required fields, including:
	* Dog breed and age.
	* Dog's health condition.
4. For the "Owner Information" page, fill out the required fields, including:
	* Owner's phone number and email.
5. Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Visit Information** (`visit_info`, text, required): Enter the date and start time of the walk.
* **Pet Information** (`pet_info`, text, required): Enter the dog's breed and age.
* **Walk Details** (`visit_details`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the walk, including the route taken and any notable events.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the walk.
* **Walk Date** (`walk_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the walk.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of the walk.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required): Enter the end time of the walk.
* **Walk Duration** (`walk_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of the walk in minutes.
* **Walk Distance** (`walk_distance`, number, required): Enter the distance of the walk.
* **Weather** (`weather`, select_one, optional): Choose the weather conditions experienced during the walk.
* **Route** (`route`, select_multiple, optional): Select the route taken during the walk.
* **Dog Condition** (`dog_condition`, select_one, optional): Choose the dog's condition after the walk.
* **Walker Notes** (`walker_notes`, note, optional): Enter any notes or comments from the walker.
* **Client Updates** (`client_updates`, note, optional): Enter any updates or comments from the client.
* **Dog ID** (`dog_id`, number, required): Enter the dog's ID number.
* **Dog Name** (`dog_name`, text, required): Enter the dog's name.
* **Dog Breed** (`dog_breed`, text, optional): Enter the dog's breed.
* **Dog Age** (`dog_age`, number, required): Enter the dog's age.
* **Dog Health Condition** (`dog_health_condition`, select_one, optional): Choose the dog's health condition.
* **Vet Notes** (`vet_notes`, note, optional): Enter any notes from the vet.
* **Owner Phone** (`owner_phone`, text, optional): Enter the owner's phone number.
* **Owner Email** (`owner_email`, email, optional): Enter the owner's email.
* **Walker Phone** (`walker_phone`, text, optional): Enter the walker's phone number.
* **Walker Email** (`walker_email`, email, optional): Enter the walker's email.
* **Walk Status** (`walk_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the status of the walk.

Note: This guide assumes that the form will be filled out by a single user, but it may be reviewed and edited by multiple users in the future.
