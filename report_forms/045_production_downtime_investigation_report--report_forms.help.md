# Production Downtime Investigation Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about production downtime incidents, including their details, equipment affected, and corrective actions taken.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Use the text fields for descriptive answers.
3. Choose the correct options in the select multiple and select one fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Production Downtime Investigation Report** (1, text, required: false): This is the title of the form, which should be completed with a clear and descriptive title of the production downtime incident.
* **Incident Details** (2, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the incident, including what happened and when.
* **Equipment Affected** (3, text, required: false): List the equipment that was affected by the production downtime incident.
* **Incident Description** (4, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Root Cause** (5, select_multiple, required: false): Check all that apply to indicate the root cause of the incident. This field is a multiple-select field, where you can choose multiple options.
	+ 'Yes' indicates that the selected option is a root cause.
* **Duration** (6, number, required: false): Enter the duration of the production downtime incident, in hours.
* **Corrective Action** (7, select_one, required: false): Check the box to indicate if a corrective action was taken during the incident.
	+ 'Yes' indicates that a corrective action was taken.
* **Follow-up Actions** (8, note, required: false): Use this field to document any follow-up actions that were taken after the incident.

## Tips
* Be concise and clear in your answers, and ensure that the information collected is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you're unsure about any aspect of this form or the process, please reach out to your team lead or supervisor for guidance.
