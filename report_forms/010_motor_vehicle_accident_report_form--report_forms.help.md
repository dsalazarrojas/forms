# motor_vehicle_accident_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report vehicle accidents. It is used to gather information about the accident, including vehicle registration numbers, accident dates and times, locations, and descriptions. It also collects information about witnesses, vehicle damage, and police reports.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the vehicle registration number of the vehicle involved in the accident.
2. Enter the year of the vehicle.
3. Enter the date and time of the accident.
4. Enter the location of the accident.
5. Enter a description of what happened during the accident.
6. Enter any details about injuries sustained during the accident.
7. (Optional) Enter any witness information, including their names and statements.
8. (Optional) Enter any vehicle damage or made damage.
9. Enter the police report number (if applicable).
10. Enter the name and title of the officer issuing the report.
11. Enter the date and time of when the report was completed.
12. Select if the report is completed or not.
13. Choose all that apply for report status (Completed, Incomplete, Pending).
14. Enter the vehicle owner's name.
15. Enter the vehicle owner's address.
16. Enter the vehicle make.
17. Enter the vehicle model.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Registration Number (1)**: Enter the registration number of the vehicle involved in the accident.
* **Vehicle Year (2)**: Enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Accident Date (3)**: Enter the date of the accident.
* **Accident Time (4)**: Enter the time of the accident.
* **Location (5)**: Enter the location of the accident.
* **Accident Description (6)**: Enter a description of what happened during the accident.
* **Injuries (7)**: Enter any details about injuries sustained during the accident.
* **Witnesses (8)**: (Optional) Enter any witness information, including their names and statements.
* **Vehicle Damage (9)**: (Optional) Enter any vehicle damage or made damage.
* **Vehicle Made (10)**: (Optional) Enter any vehicle made damage.
* **Witness (11)**: (Optional) Enter any witness information, including their names and statements.
* **Witness Statement (12)**: Enter the witness statement.
* **Police Report Number (13)**: (Optional) Enter the police report number.
* **Officer Issuing Report (14)**: Enter the name and title of the officer issuing the report.
* **Report Date (15)**: Enter the date of the report.
* **Report Time (16)**: Enter the time of the report.
* **Assigned Officer (17)**: Enter the name of the assigned officer.
* **Officer Signature (18)**: Enter the officer's signature.
* **Officer Title (19)**: Enter the officer's title.
* **Report Completed (20)**: Select if the report is completed or not.
* **Report Status (21)**: Choose all that apply for report status (Completed, Incomplete, Pending).
* **Vehicle Owner (22)**: Enter the name of the vehicle owner.
* **Owner Address (23)**: Enter the vehicle owner's address.
* **Vehicle Make (24)**: Enter the vehicle's make.
* **Vehicle Model (25)**: Enter the vehicle's model.
