# Energy Audit Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Energy Audit Checklist form is a data collection tool used to gather information about the energy usage and audit status of a particular site. This form helps users to identify areas of improvement and provide a comprehensive overview of the site's energy efficiency status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the site's name in the "Site Name" field.
2. Select the current building status from the "Building Status" field.
3. Select all applicable equipment from the "Checklist Equipment" field.
4. Identify any safety concerns and select all applicable options from the "Safety Concerns" field.
5. Enter the total energy consumption for the site in the "Energy Consumption" field.
6. Select energy conservation measures that have been implemented or planned for the site from the "Energy Conservation Measures" field.
7. Enter the count of equipment in the "Equipment Count" field.
8. Enter the total energy consumed by the site in the "Total Energy Consumed" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Site Name** (site_name, text, optional): Enter the name of the site being audited.
* **Admin** (admin, text, optional): Enter any administrative information about the site.
* **Building Status** (building_status, select_one, optional): Select the current status of the building from the options "Building under construction", "Active", or "Decommissioned".
* **Checklist Equipment** (checklist_equipment, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable equipment from the options "Lighting", "HVAC", "Electrical system", "Plumbing system", "Fire alarm system", or "Safety devices".
* **Facility** (facility, text, optional): Enter any facility-specific details about the site.
* **Safety Concerns** (safety_concerns, select_multiple, optional): Identify any safety concerns from the options "Fire suppression system", "Fire alarm system", "Safety devices", "Fire extinguishers", or "Emergency exit signs".
* **Comments** (comments, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the site.
* **Energy Consumption** (energy_consumption, number, optional): Enter the total energy consumption for the site.
* **Energy Conservation Measures** (energy_conservation_measures, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable energy conservation measures from the options "Lighting", "HVAC", "Electrical system", "Plumbing system", or "Fire alarm system".
* **Equipment Count** (equipment_count, number, optional): Enter the count of equipment at the site.
* **Total Energy Consumed** (total_energy_consumed, number, optional): Enter the total energy consumed by the site.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to get a complete picture of the site's energy efficiency status.
* Use the "Checklist Equipment" and "Energy Conservation Measures" fields to identify areas of improvement.
* Double-check the "Safety Concerns" field to ensure all applicable concerns are selected.
* Enter accurate energy consumption and equipment count data to ensure accurate results.
