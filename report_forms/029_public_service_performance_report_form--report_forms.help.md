# Public Service Performance Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from users about their experiences with public services. It helps public service providers to understand how well their services are meeting user needs and to identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the required information in the fields as follows:
	* Enter the name of the service you are reporting on in the **Service Name** field.
	* Rate your experience with the service using the **Rating** field (1-5 stars).
	* Enter any comments or suggestions in the **Comment** field.
	* If you have any further suggestions, add them in the **Suggestions** field.
	* Enter your email address in the **Email** field (if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions).
	* Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field (if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions).
	* Enter your name in the **Submitter Name** field.
	* Enter your email address in the **Submitter Email** field.
	* Enter the date and time you visited the service in the **Date** and **Time** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Report Form Data (Page 1)
* **Service Name**: Enter the name of the public service you are reporting on.
### Service Name
* **Rating**: Rate your experience with the service using the provided stars (1-5).
* **Rating Comment**: Enter any comments about your rating.
### Service Performance
* **Suggestions**: Enter any suggestions for improvement you have for the service.
### Contact Information
* **Email**: Enter your email address to receive follow-up questions or responses.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number to receive follow-up questions or responses.
* **Submitter Name**: Enter your name.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date**: Enter the date you visited the service.
* **Time**: Enter the time you visited the service.
* **Comment**: Enter any additional comments about your experience.

Please note that all fields are optional, unless otherwise stated.
