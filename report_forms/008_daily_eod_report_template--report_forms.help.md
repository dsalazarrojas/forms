# Daily EOD Report Template - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Daily EOD Report Template is a form designed to help users document their daily activities and progress. This template collects information on the date, user ID, activity type, location, and any additional notes. It's meant to be filled out daily and submitted to provide a clear record of daily work.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Select the date on which the report is being submitted from the calendar widget.
3. Enter your user ID in the `user_id` field.
4. Describe the activity you completed or worked on in the `activity` field.
5. Choose the locations where you were active in the `location` field by selecting multiple options from the dropdown menu.
6. Add any additional notes about your day in the `notes` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **eod_date** (`eod_date`, date, required): Enter the date on which you are submitting this report. This is a calendar widget that will help you select the correct date.
* **user_id** (`user_id`, number, optional): Your user ID, which identifies you within the system. If you're unsure, you can leave this field blank.
* **activity** (`activity`, text, optional): Describe the main activity you completed or worked on today. Be as detailed as possible to give yourself a clear record of your progress.
* **location** (`location`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate the locations where you were active today. Choose one or multiple options from the dropdown menu. 
* **notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or observations about your day. This is a free-form text field where you can write down anything that's relevant to your work.

## Tips

* If you're unsure about any of the fields, just leave them blank or ask your supervisor for guidance.
* Make sure to submit the form before the end of the day to ensure it's recorded in a timely manner.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact your supervisor or support team for help.
