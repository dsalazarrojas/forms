# Call Center Lead Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential customers or leads to facilitate communication and sales. It helps the sales team understand their interests, needs, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields: First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and Products or Services Interested In.
2. Select your Job Title, Industry, and Budget Range if applicable.
3. Choose how you heard about us and your preferred contact method.
4. Optionally, provide additional information on specific areas of interest and a preferred contact time.
5. Provide any additional comments or questions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your primary email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your primary phone number where we can contact you.
* **Company Name** (`company`, text, optional): Enter the name of your company (if applicable).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title or position within your company (if applicable).
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your office or work address (if applicable).
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city of your office or work location (if applicable).
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the state of your office or work location (if applicable).
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, optional): Enter the zip code of your office or work location (if applicable).
* **Products or Services Interested In** (`products_services`, text, required): Select the products or services you are interested in.
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_one, optional): Select the industry you belong to.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, select_one, optional): Select the budget range you are looking to invest or spend.
* **Expected Purchase Timeline** (`timeline`, select_one, optional): Select the expected purchase timeline.
* **How Did You Hear About Us?** (`how_heard`, select_one, optional): Select how you heard about us.
* **Specific Areas of Interest** (`specific_interest`, text, optional): Enter specific areas of interest or topics you would like to learn more about.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`best_contact_method`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred contact method.
* **Best Time to Contact** (`best_contact_time`, select_one, optional): Select the best time to contact you.
* **Additional Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you may have.
