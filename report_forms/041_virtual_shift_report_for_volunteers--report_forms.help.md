# Virtual Shift Report For Volunteers - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for reporting volunteer shifts worked by volunteers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your information for the shift you just completed or are about to complete.
2. Make sure to enter your hours worked accurately to ensure your time is recorded correctly.
3. Double-check your email and phone number are correct before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Shift Report** (`volunteer_shift_report`, note, not required): This is a text field for any notes or comments about your shift.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, not required): Enter the date you started your shift.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, not required): Enter the date you ended your shift.
* **Hours Worked** (`hours_worked`, number, not required): Enter the total number of hours you worked during your shift.
* **Volunteer Name** (`volunteer_name`, text, not required): Enter your name as a volunteer.
* **Volunteer Email** (`volunteer_email`, email, not required): Enter your email address.
* **Volunteer Phone** (`volunteer_phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Please fill out this form accurately and honestly.
* If you've worked multiple shifts in one day, you can use this form to report on each shift separately.
* Make sure to keep a record of your completed shifts for your own reference.
