# Annual Project Progress Report - Help Guide
## Purpose

This report aims to gather information on a project's progress, milestones, risks, and challenges. It provides a snapshot of the project's current status, enabling project managers and stakeholders to track progress, identify potential issues, and make data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Project Name**: Enter the official name of the project, as recognized by the organization.
2. **Project ID or Code**: Provide the unique identifier assigned to the project (optional).
3. **Project Manager Name**: Enter the name of the person overseeing the project.
4. **Project Manager Email**: Enter the contact email of the project manager.
5. **Reporting Period**: Specify the year or date range of the report (e.g., "2022" or "Q1 2022").
6. **Report Submission Date**: Enter the date this report is being submitted.
7. **Department or Business Unit**: If applicable, enter the department or business unit responsible for the project.
8. **Project Start Date**: Enter the date when the project began.
9. **Expected Project Completion Date**: Specify when the project is expected to be completed.
10. **Current Project Status**: Choose one of the following options:
	* On track
	* Minor delays
	* Significant delays
	* At risk
	* On hold
	* Completed
11. **Project Completion Percentage**: Enter an estimated percentage of project completion.
12. **Budget Status**: Choose one of the following options:
	* On budget
	* Slightly over budget
	* Significantly over budget
	* Under budget
13. **Total Project Budget**: Enter the allocated budget amount for the project.
14. **Actual Spending to Date**: Enter the amount spent on the project so far.
15. **Schedule Status**: Choose one of the following options:
	* On schedule
	* Minor delays
	* Significant delays
	* Ahead of schedule
16. **Key Accomplishments This Year**: Describe major milestones or deliverables achieved this year.
17. **Number of Deliverables Completed**: Count of completed items.
18. **Outstanding Issues or Challenges**: Describe any problems encountered or remaining.
19. **Risks Identified**: List potential threats to project success.
20. **Risk Mitigation Strategies**: Describe plans to address identified risks.
21. **Project Team Size**: Enter the number of team members involved.
22. **Overall Team Performance**: Assess the project team's performance as:
	* Excellent
	* Good
	* Satisfactory
	* Needs improvement
	* Poor
23. **Stakeholder Satisfaction**: Rate stakeholder satisfaction as:
	* Very satisfied
	* Satisfied
	* Neutral
	* Dissatisfied
	* Very dissatisfied
24. **Next Steps or Planned Activities**: Outline upcoming activities or milestones.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the report.
* Be as accurate and thorough as possible when providing information.
* If any field is not applicable, indicate "N/A" or "Not Applicable."
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your project manager or the relevant department.
