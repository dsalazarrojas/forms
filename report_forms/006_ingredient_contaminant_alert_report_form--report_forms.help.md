# Ingredient Contaminant Alert Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ingredient Contaminant Alert Report Form is designed to report incidents of suspected contaminants in ingredients or products. This form is intended to be used by personnel or quality control staff to ensure that potential contaminants are identified and documented, and corrective actions are taken to prevent reoccurrences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the specific ingredient that was found to be potentially contaminated.
2. Select the type of contaminant that was identified (e.g., Bacteria, Virus, Other, Mold/Toxin).
3. Indicate the severity level of the contaminant (if applicable).
4. Choose the location where the contaminant was found (e.g., Warehouse, Factory, Distributor, Retail).
5. Add any additional comments or photos related to the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **ingredient**: Enter the specific ingredient that was found to be potentially contaminated.
* **contaminant_type**: Select the type of contaminant that was identified. This field will provide a list of options to choose from, including:
	+ Bacteria
	+ Virus
	+ Other
	+ Mold/Toxin
* **severity**: Indicate the severity level of the contaminant (if applicable). This field will accept a numerical value.
* **location**: Choose the location where the contaminant was found. This field will provide a list of options to choose from, including:
	+ Warehouse
	+ Factory
	+ Distributor
	+ Retail
* **photos**: Add any additional photos or notes related to the incident. This field will accept multiple text entries. The photos will be displayed as additional text fields, but they are intended to be images uploaded or URLs.

Note: This form may seem lengthy, as there are multiple text fields for photos. However, these fields are intended to allow users to add up to 19 different photos or notes related to the incident.
