# Retail Visit Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Retail Visit Report form is used to document visits from sales representatives or other visitors in retail stores. This form is used to gather information about each visit, including store details and visit specifics.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Store Name**, where the visit took place.
2. Select the **Date of Visit**, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
3. Choose the **Time of Visit**, in the format hh:mm.
4. Submit the form once you have filled out all the required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Store Name** (Store Name, text, required): Enter the name of the store where the visit took place.
* **Date of Visit** (Date of Visit, date, required): Select the date of the visit in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Time of Visit** (Time of Visit, time, required): Choose the time of the visit in hh:mm format.

## Tips
* Be accurate when filling out the form, as the information will be used to evaluate sales performance and customer service.
* Ensure that you have permission from the store owner before submitting the form.
* Do not submit duplicate reports for the same visit.
