# Cleaning Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cleaning Report Form is designed to document the details of cleaning services provided to a client or property. It gathers information about the service date, type, areas cleaned, and client feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and fill in the requested information as it applies to your specific cleaning service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Report Date** (`report_date`, required): Enter the date of the cleaning service.
* **Cleaning Service Period** (`report_period`, required): Describe the date range of the service provided.
* **Property/Client Name** (`property_name`, required): Enter the name of the property or client.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, required): Enter the address of the cleaned property.
* **Crew Leader Name** (`crew_leader`, required): Enter the name of the supervisor on duty.
* **Crew Members Present** (`crew_members`, optional): List the names of all team members.
* **Type of Service Performed** (`service_type`, required): Select the type of cleaning service provided (e.g., Regular Cleaning, Deep Cleaning).
* **Duration of Service** (`service_duration`, required): Enter the hours spent on cleaning.
* **Areas Serviced** (`areas_serviced`, required): Select all areas cleaned (e.g., Kitchen, Bathrooms, Bedrooms).
* **Was all planned work completed?** (`work_completed`, required): Choose whether all planned work was completed.
* **Percentage of Work Completed** (`completion_percentage`, optional): Estimate the percentage of work completed.
* **Reason for Incomplete Work** (`work_not_completed_reason`, optional): If work was not fully completed, describe the reason.
* **Kitchen cleaning completed** (`kitchen_completed`, required): Select whether kitchen cleaning was completed.
* **Bathroom cleaning completed** (`bathroom_completed`, required): Select whether bathroom cleaning was completed.
* **Floor cleaning completed** (`floor_cleaning_completed`, required): Select whether floor cleaning was completed.
* **Were there any issues during service?** (`any_issues`, required): Choose whether any issues arose during the service.
* **Describe Any Issues Encountered** (`issues_description`, optional): Provide details about any problems that occurred.
* **Any damage to property reported?** (`damage_reported`, required): Select whether any damage to the property was reported.
* **Damage Description** (`damage_description`, optional): Describe any damage to the property.
* **Quality of Work Performed** (`work_quality`, required): Select how well the work was completed (e.g., Excellent, Very Good).
* **Client Satisfaction Level** (`client_satisfaction`, required): Choose the client's satisfaction level with the service (e.g., Very Satisfied, Satisfied).
* **Client Feedback or Special Comments** (`feedback_notes`, optional): Enter any feedback from the client.
* **Supplies/Products Used** (`supplies_used`, optional): List the materials and products used during the service.
* **Any equipment problems or needs?** (`equipment_issues`, optional): Describe any equipment problems or needs.
* **Follow-up cleaning needed?** (`follow_up_needed`, required): Select whether follow-up cleaning is needed.
* **Were there any issues during service?** (`any_issues`, required): Select whether any issues arose during the service (this field is likely intended to be a duplicate of `any_issues`).
* **Any damage to property reported?** (`damage_reported`, required): Select whether any damage to the property was reported (this field is likely intended to be a duplicate of `damage_reported`).
* **Client Feedback or Special Comments** (`feedback_notes`, optional): Enter any feedback from the client (this field is likely intended to be a duplicate of `feedback_notes`).
