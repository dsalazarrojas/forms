# Weekly Environmental Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weekly Environmental Assessment form is designed to collect information about site conditions, hazards, and other relevant data from site visits.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Complete each section of the form, providing information as instructed for each section.
2. Be accurate and truthful when filling out the form.
3. Review and revise as necessary before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Site Conditions (1)** (Text, Optional): Describe the current site conditions, including any notable weather, maintenance, or other relevant factors.
* **Hazards (2)** (Select Multiple, Optional): Identify any hazards present at the site, such as uneven terrain, slippery surfaces, or other potential safety risks.
* **Sustainability Metrics (3)** (Number, Optional): Record any relevant sustainability metrics, such as water consumption, energy usage, or waste management.
* **Comments (4)** (Text, Optional): Add any additional comments or observations about the site visit.
* **Weekly Site Visit (5)** (Date, Optional): Record the date of the site visit.
* **Weekly Site Visit 2 (6)** (Time, Optional): Record the time of the site visit.
* **Photos (7)** (Note, Optional): Add any photos or images from the site visit.
* **Notes (8)** (Text, Optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the site visit.
* **Email (9)** (Email, Optional): Enter the email address of the person filling out the form.
* **Phone Number (10)** (Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the person filling out the form.
* **Assigned (11)** (Select One, Optional): Identify the person or team assigned to the site visit.
* **Submitter (12)** (Select One, Optional): Identify the person or team submitting the form.
* **Created By (13)** (Select One, Optional): Identify the person or team creating the form entry.
* **Created Date (14)** (Date, Optional): Record the date the form was created.
* **Submitted Date (15)** (Date, Optional): Record the date the form was submitted.
* **Created Time (16)** (Time, Optional): Record the time the form was created.
* **Submitted Time (17)** (Time, Optional): Record the time the form was submitted.
* **Site Location (18)** (Text, Optional): Enter the site location or address.
* **Latitude (19)** (Number, Optional): Record the latitude of the site location.
* **Longitude (20)** (Number, Optional): Record the longitude of the site location.
* **Uploaded By (21)** (Select One, Optional): Identify the person or team uploading the form.
* **Uploaded Date (22)** (Date, Optional): Record the date the form was uploaded.
* **Uploaded Time (23)** (Time, Optional): Record the time the form was uploaded.
* **Comments 2 (24)** (Text, Optional): Add any additional comments or observations.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submission.
