# Google Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report incidents, including details such as the incident's type, summary, description, and status. It also gathers relevant information from the reporter, including their name, contact information, and attachment details. Fill out this form to ensure that your incident report is complete and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Incident Details** page with the relevant information about the incident, including the **Summary**, **Description**, **Time**, **Date**, **Location**, and **Status**.
2. On the **Reporter Information** page, provide your **Name**, **Email**, and **Phone Number**.
3. Select the **Type** of incident from the provided options.
4. Choose the correct **Priority** level for the incident.
5. Add any relevant **Attachments** to support your report.
6. Select any relevant **Tags** related to the incident.
7. Finally, review your submission for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (`title`, `text`, not required): This is the title of the form, which will be displayed as "Google Incident Report Form".
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, `text`, not required): This is the main section for describing the incident. Please include as much information as possible to help the team respond efficiently.
* **Summary** (`incident_summary`, `text`, required): Provide a brief summary of the incident.
* **Type** (`incident_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of incident from the provided options.
* **Description** (`incident_description`, `text`, required): This is a more detailed description of the incident.
* **Time** (`incident_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time when the incident occurred.
* **Date** (`incident_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Location** (`incident_location`, `text`, not required): If the incident occurred at a specific location, please provide it here.
* **Status** (`incident_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose the current status of the incident.
* **Reporter** (`incident_reporter`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the reporter of this incident.
* **Email** (`incident_reporter_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`incident_reporter_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Comments** (`incident_comments`, `text`, required): Provide any additional comments or context related to the incident.
* **Attachments** (`incident_attachments`, `note`, not required): You can upload any relevant attachments or supporting documents.
* **Priority** (`incident_priority`, `select_one`, required): Choose the priority level for the incident from the provided options.
* **Tags** (`incident_tags`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select any relevant tags related to the incident.

Please note that some fields are marked as **required** or **not required** in the form. Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
