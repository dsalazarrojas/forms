**Medical Alert Form - Help Guide**
## Purpose

The Medical Alert Form is a tool for healthcare professionals to report medical alerts related to patients under their care. The form is designed to gather essential information in a structured and efficient manner, ensuring that critical medical alerts are addressed promptly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Patient Information**: Enter the name of the patient for whom the medical alert is being reported.
2. **Medical Alert Date**: Select the date of the medical alert.
3. **Medical Alert Type**: Choose the type of medical alert from the list provided (Critical, Non-critical, or Urgent).
4. **Reason for Alert**: Provide a brief explanation for the medical alert, if necessary.
5. **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person or team responsible for contact.
6. **Contact Information**: Enter the phone number and email of the contact person.
7. **Patient Status**: Enter any relevant patient status information.
8. **Medical Team Information**: Enter the name of the medical team or department responsible for the patient's care.
9. **Medical Team Contact**: Enter the name of the person or team contact.
10. **Medical Team Contact Information**: Enter the phone number and email of the medical team contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: The name of the patient for whom the medical alert is being reported.
* **Medical Alert Date**: The date on which the medical alert occurred or is expected to occur.
* **Medical Alert Type**: A classification of the medical alert as Critical, Non-critical, or Urgent.
*   **Reason for Alert**: A brief explanation of why the medical alert is being reported, if required.
* **Contact Person**: The name of the person or team responsible for contact.
* **Contact Phone**: The phone number of the contact person.
*   **Contact Email**: The email of the contact person.
*   **Patient Status**: Any relevant information about the patient's status.
*   **Medical Team Name**: The name of the medical team or department responsible for the patient's care.
*   **Medical Team Contact**: The name of the person or team contact.
*   **Medical Team Contact Phone**: The phone number of the medical team contact.
*   **Medical Team Email**: The email of the medical team contact.
*   **Medical Team Location**: The location of the medical team or department.

**Tips**

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check the medical alert type to ensure it is accurately classified.
* Use the provided contact information to ensure that the medical alert is addressed promptly.
