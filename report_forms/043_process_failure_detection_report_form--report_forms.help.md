# process_failure_detection_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report and track process failures, allowing users to document details about the failure, including the severity level, evidence, and investigation status.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the user information section (Section 1) with your details.
2. In the failure details section (Section 2), describe the failure you experienced or witnessed.
3. Select the severity level of the failure from the dropdown menu (Section 3).
4. Provide any evidence related to the failure (Section 4).
5. Date and time of the incident (Section 5).
6. Status of the investigation (Section 6).
7. Add any comments about the investigation (Section 7).
8. If applicable, specify the tool or equipment involved (Section 8).
9. Add any additional notes about the incident (Section 9).
10. Provide a comment about the severity level of the incident (Section 10).
11. Finally, assign the investigation to a specific person or team (Section 11).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (Section 1, Text)**: Enter your name, title, and contact information.
* **Failure Details (Section 2, Text)**: Describe the failure you experienced or witnessed, including the steps leading up to the failure.
* **Severity (Section 3, Select One)**: Choose the severity level of the failure:
	+ Critical
	+ High
	+ Low
* **Evidence (Section 4, Text)**: Attach any supporting documents or photos related to the failure.
* **Incident Date (Section 5, Date)**: Enter the date of the incident.
* **Incident Time (Section 6, Time)**: Enter the time of the incident.
* **Investigation Status (Section 7, Select One)**: Choose the status of the investigation:
	+ In Progress
	+ Completed
* **Investigation Comment (Section 8, Text)**: Add any comments or updates about the investigation.
* **Assigned Tool (Section 9, Text)**: If applicable, specify the tool or equipment involved in the failure.
* **Incident Note (Section 10, Text)**: Add any additional notes about the incident.
* **Severity Comment (Section 11, Text)**: Provide a comment about the severity level of the incident.
* **Assigned To (Section 11, Text)**: Specify the person or team responsible for investigating the incident.
