# Electrical Appliance Issue Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about issues with electrical appliances. It helps our team understand the problem, prioritize and schedule visits, and communicate with customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Describe the appliance and the issue you're experiencing.
2. Provide details about the issue, such as its impact and any relevant context.
3. If applicable, select the type of appliance involved.
4. Schedule a visit for our team to assist with the issue.
5. Indicate the priority level of the issue (Low, Medium, High).
6. If you'd like, provide your contact information or phone number for follow-up communication.
7. Finally, update the issue status as it changes (e.g., New, In Progress, Resolved).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Appliance Issue** (`appliance_issue`, `text`, required): Briefly describe the issue with the electrical appliance.
- **Issue Details** (`issue_details`, `text`, required): Provide more details about the issue, including its impact.
- **Appliance Type** (`appliance_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of appliance involved (Appliance 1, Appliance 2, or Appliance 3).
- **Schedule Visit** (`schedule_visit`, `date`, required): Select a date for our team to visit and assist with the issue.
- **Issue Priority** (`issue_priority`, `select_one`, required): Indicate the priority level of the issue (Low, Medium, or High).
- **Customer Contact** (`customer_contact`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for follow-up communication.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): If you'd like, provide your phone number for faster communication.
- **Issue Status** (`issue_status`, `select_multiple`, required): Update the issue status as it changes (New, In Progress, Resolved).
- **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required): Add any additional comments or information about the issue.

## Tips
- Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form to ensure our team can efficiently address the issue.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
- Review the form before submitting it to ensure you haven't missed any essential information.
