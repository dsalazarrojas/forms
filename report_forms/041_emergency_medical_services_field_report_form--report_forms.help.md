# Emergency Medical Services Field Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about emergency medical services field reports.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure that you are reporting an incident that occurred in the field and not for other types of medical events or non-emergency incidents.
2.  Select the relevant medical condition that occurred during the incident.
3.  Indicate whether the patient is alive or deceased.
4.  Enter the incident number assigned to the case.
5.  Enter your name and department as the incident reporter.
6.  Enter the date and time when the incident occurred.
7.  Enter the patient's name.
8.  Enter the patient's date of birth.
9.  Enter the patient's address.
10.  Enter a brief description of the injury.
11.  Indicate whether the patient is alive or deceased.
12.  Enter the incident number assigned to the case.
13.  Enter your name and department as the incident reporter.
14.  Enter the date and time when the incident occurred.
15.  Enter the patient's name.
16.  Enter the patient's date of birth.
17.  Enter the patient's address.
18.  Enter your department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Medical Condition** (`emergency_medical_services_field_report_form_8`, `select_one`, required): Select the relevant medical condition that occurred during the incident. This will help to identify the type of medical condition that required emergency services.
*   **Patient Status** (`patient_status`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether the patient is alive or deceased. This will help to track the outcome of the incident.
*   **Incident Number** (`incident_number`, `number`, required): Enter the incident number assigned to the case. This is a unique identifier for the incident and helps with tracking and reporting.
*   **Incident Reporter** (`incident_reporter_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the incident reporter. This will help to identify who reported the incident.
*   **Incident Reporter Department** (`incident_reporter_department`, `text`, required): Enter your department as the incident reporter. This will help to identify the department that reported the incident.
*   **Date of Event** (`date_of_event`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the incident occurred. This will help to track the timing of the incident.
*   **Time of Event** (`time_of_event`, `time`, required): Enter the time when the incident occurred. This will help to track the timing of the incident.
*   **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's name. This will help to identify the patient involved in the incident.
*   **Patient Date of Birth** (`patient_date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter the patient's date of birth. This will help to identify the patient's demographic information.
*   **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's address. This will help to identify the patient's location.
*   **Injury Description** (`injury_description`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the injury. This will help to document the details of the incident.
*   **Other Name** (`patient_name_other`, `text`, required): Enter any other names associated with the incident. This can be useful in cases where there are multiple parties involved.
