# E Commerce Fake Review Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help detect and report fake reviews, suspicious products, or other e-commerce-related issues. It is not a real review form, but rather a tool for reporting such incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the form's fields as per the instructions below.
- Make sure to fill in all required fields (marked as "true").

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Suspicious Product Name** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, required): Enter the name of the product that you suspect is fake or suspicious. This could be a product name, product description, or any other relevant information.
- **Report Date** (`id: 2`, `type: date`, required): Fill in the date when you first noticed or reported this issue. This helps us track the timeline of the report.
- **Suspicious Product Image** (`id: 3`, `type: text`, required): Upload or describe the product image that you suspect is fake or suspicious. This helps us verify the product's authenticity.
- **Marketplace Platform** (`id: 4`, `type: select_one`, required): Select the platform where you found the product (e.g., Amazon, eBay, Etsy, Facebook, Google Play, or Other).
- **Review Rating** (`id: 5`, `type: select_multiple`, required): Choose the review rating you suspect is fake or suspicious (1-5 stars).
- **Report Description** (`id: 6`, `type: text`, required): Provide a detailed description of the issue you're reporting, including any evidence or supporting information.
- **Suspicious User Name** (`id: 7`, `type: text`, required): Enter the name of the user you suspect is fake or involved in the issue.
- **Report Reason** (`id: 8`, `type: text`, required): Explain the reason for your report, including any relevant context or evidence.
- **Report Details** (`id: 9`, `type: text`, required): Provide any additional details that might help us investigate the issue.
- **Submitted By** (`id: 10`, `type: text`, required): Enter your name or username.
- **Submit Date** (`id: 11`, `type: date`, required): Fill in the date and time you submitted this report.
- **Review Type** (`id: 12`, `type: select_one`, required): Choose the type of review you're reporting (Negative, Positive, Neutral, or Other).
- **Report Status** (`id: 13`, `type: select_one`, required): Select the status of your report (Open, Closed, or In Progress).
- **Product Type** (`id: 14`, `type: select_multiple`, required): Choose the type of product you suspect is fake or suspicious (e.g., Electronics, Fashion, Beauty, Home, Kitchen, or Other).
- **Report ID** (`id: 15`, `type: text`, required): Enter a unique identifier for your report.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when filling out the form.
- Use real names and dates where applicable.
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- If you have any additional information, attach it to the "Report Details" field.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact us for assistance.
