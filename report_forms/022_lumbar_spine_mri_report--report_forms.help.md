# Lumbar Spine MRI Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lumbar Spine MRI Report form is designed to collect and document information for patients undergoing lumbar spine MRI exams. This form is intended for medical professionals to record and report the results of these exams.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form accurately and completely with the required information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Double-check the information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification card or medical record.
* **Referring Provider Name** (`referring_provider_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the provider who referred the patient for this exam.
* **Date of Exam** (`date_of_exam`, date, required): Enter the date of the lumbar spine MRI exam.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, text, required): Enter the patient's medical record number.
* **Lumbar Spine MRI Report** (`lumbar_spine_mri_report`, text, required): Describe the lumbar spine MRI exam results, including any relevant findings or diagnoses.
* **Findings** (`findings`, text, required): Summarize any notable findings from the lumbar spine MRI exam.
* **Imaging Center** (`imaging_center`, text, required): Enter the name of the imaging center where the MRI exam was performed.
* **Radiologist Report** (`radiologist_report`, text, required): Enter any relevant information from the radiologist's report.
* **Referring Provider Note** (`referring_provider_note`, text, required): Add any additional comments from the referring provider.
* **Radiologist Note** (`radiologist_note`, text, required): Add any additional comments from the radiologist.
* **Lumbar Spine MRI Report Signed By** (`lumbar_spine_mri_report_signed_by`, text, required): Include the name and title of the person signing the form.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, optional): Enter the date the form was signed.
* **Time Signed** (`time_signed`, time, optional): Enter the time the form was signed.
* **Version** (`version`, text, optional): Enter the version number of the form, if necessary.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text, required): Have the patient sign and date the form.
* **Radiologist Signature** (`radiologist_signature`, text, required): Have the radiologist sign and date the form.
* **Medical Review** (`medical_review`, text, required): Enter any medical review comments.
* **Medical Review Date** (`medical_review_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the medical review.
* **Medical Review Time** (`medical_review_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the medical review.
* **Radiologist Review** (`radiologist_review`, text, required): Enter any radiologist review comments.
* **Date Reviewed** (`date_reviewed`, date, optional): Enter the date of the radiologist review.
* **Time Reviewed** (`time_reviewed`, time, optional): Enter the time of the radiologist review.
* **Report Version** (`report_version`, text, required): Enter the version number of the report.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, optional): Enter the patient's address, if necessary.

Note: Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely before submitting the form.
