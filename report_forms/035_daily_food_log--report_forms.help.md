# Daily Food Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Food Log form is designed to collect information about your daily food consumption. This form is intended for users to report or log their food intake on a daily basis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your date of food consumption in the Date Entry field.
2. Select the foods you consumed by choosing from the options provided in the Food field.
3. If applicable, enter the quantity of food consumed and the serving size in the Quantity and Serving Size fields, respectively.
4. Enter the total calorie intake in the Calorie field.
5. Provide any additional notes or comments in the Notes field.
6. Finally, confirm that you've completed the log by selecting "Yes" in the Submit field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date Entry (id: 2)**: Enter the date of food consumption. This field is required and should be in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Food (id: 3)**: Select all the foods you consumed from the available options. This field is required.
* **Quantity (id: 4)**: If you'd like to enter the quantity of food consumed, use this field. This field is optional.
* **Serving Size (id: 5)**: If you'd like to specify the serving size of the food, use this field. This field is optional.
* **Calorie (id: 6)**: Enter the total calorie intake for the day. This field is optional.
* **Notes (id: 7)**: Provide any additional notes or comments about your food consumption. This field is optional.
* **Submit (id: 8)**: Confirm that you've completed the log by selecting "Yes". This field is required.
