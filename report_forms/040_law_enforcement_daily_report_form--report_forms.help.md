# Law Enforcement Daily Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Law Enforcement Daily Report Form is used for law enforcement personnel to document their daily activities, incidents, and events, providing a clear and concise record of the day's events for better tracking and reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the run count for the day.
2. Enter the date of the incident or event being reported.
3. Enter the start time of each run.
4. Enter the end time of each run.
5. Enter the location of each run.
6. Provide a brief description of the incident or event.
7. Enter any relevant address details.
8. Enter any notes about the incident or event.
9. Enter any additional notes from the officer.
10. Enter the officer's name.
11. Enter the ID of the officer assigned to the unit.
12. Enter the ID of the unit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Run Count** (1, number, required: false): Enter the total number of runs completed during the day.
* **Date** (2, date, required: false): Select the date of the incident or event being reported.
* **Start Time** (3, time, required: false): Enter the start time of each run.
* **End Time** (4, time, required: false): Enter the end time of each run.
* **Location** (5, text, required: false): Enter the location of each run.
* **Incident Description** (6, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the incident or event.
* **Address** (7, text, required: false): Enter any relevant address details.
* **Notes** (8, note, required: false): Enter any notes about the incident or event.
* **Officer Notes** (9, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes from the officer.
* **Officer Name** (10, text, required: false): Enter the officer's name.
* **Assigned Officer ID** (11, text, required: false): Enter the ID of the officer assigned to the unit.
* **Unit ID** (12, text, required: false): Enter the ID of the unit.

## Tips
* Ensure that the form is filled out correctly and thoroughly to provide accurate information for the department's record-keeping and reporting needs.
* Review your assigned unit's policies and procedures before completing this form.
* Double-check for accurate and up-to-date information before submitting the form.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
