# store_tasting_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The store_tasting_report_form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience at a store after tasting products. The feedback collected will help store owners and managers make informed decisions to improve customer experience and product offerings.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the required fields, such as customer details and event date and time.
2.  Choose the store where you visited and tasted products.
3.  Select the type of product you tasted.
4.  Rate the customer service, quality, presentation, value, taste, and overall experience.
5.  Enter any comments or feedback you have for the store manager and staff.
6.  Finally, enter any score you feel represents the store's performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **customer**: Enter your customer name.
*   **store_name**: Enter the name of the store where you visited and tasted products.
*   **store_location**: Enter the location of the store.
*   **event_date**: Enter the date of your visit to the store.
*   **event_time**: Enter the time of your visit to the store.
*   **product_name**: Enter the name of the product you tasted.
*   **product_type**: Select the type of product you tasted from the dropdown menu.
*   **comments**: Enter any comments or feedback you have about your experience.
*   **customer_service**: Rate the customer service provided by the store manager and staff.
*   **quality**: Evaluate the quality of the products you tasted.
*   **presentation**: Evaluate the presentation of the products you tasted.
*   **value**: Evaluate the value of the products you tasted.
*   **taste**: Evaluate the taste of the products you tasted.
*   **overall**: Rate your overall experience at the store.
*   **store_tasting_score**: Enter a score for the store's performance.
*   **store_manager**: Select the title of the store manager who attended to your needs.
*   **store_manager_email**: Enter the email of the store manager.
*   **store_manager_phone**: Enter the phone number of the store manager.
*   **store_manager_title**: Enter the title of the store manager.
*   **customer_service**: Rate the customer service provided by the store manager and staff.
*   **store_manager_comments**: Enter any comments or feedback you have for the store manager.
*   **customer_comments**: Enter any comments or feedback you have about your experience.
*   **customer_rating**: Enter a rating for your overall experience at the store.

**Tips**

*   Be honest and objective when rating and evaluating your experience.
*   Provide specific details about your experience, such as the products you tasted and the staff that interacted with you.
*   Use the form as a tool to improve your experience and provide feedback that will help the store improve.
