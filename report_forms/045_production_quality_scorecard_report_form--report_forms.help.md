# Production Quality Scorecard Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Production Quality Scorecard Report Form is used to collect information about the quality of production shifts, including the shift name, date, and product name. It also includes scores and ratings for different criteria, as well as notes and action items. This form helps to provide a comprehensive overview of the production quality during the shift.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Shift Name in the designated field.
2. Select the Shift Date from the calendar picker.
3. Enter the Product Name in the designated field.
4. Fill in the Score 1 field with a score from 1 to 10.
5. Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to the four criteria questions, indicating if the criteria were met.
6. Enter a score from 1 to 10 in the Score 2 field.
7. Enter any additional Notes if necessary.
8. Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if there were any Quality Issues.
9. Enter a brief description of the Quality Issues, if any.
10. Enter the Overall Rating.
11. Enter the Batch Name in the designated field.
12. Enter the Batch Rating.
13. Enter the Shift Assessment, selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
14. Enter the Shift Rating.
15. Enter the Follow-up Date, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shift Name (1)**: Enter the name of the shift that this report is for.
	+ This field is required and should be in the format `Shift X`.
* **Shift Date (2)**: Select the date of the shift that this report is for.
	+ This field is required and should be in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Product Name (3)**: Enter the name of the product being produced.
	+ This field is required.
* **Score 1 (4)**: Enter a score from 1 to 10 for the first criteria.
* **Criteria 1 (5)**: Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the first criteria was met.
* **Criteria 2 (6)**: Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the second criteria was met.
* **Criteria 3 (7)**: Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the third criteria was met.
* **Criteria 4 (8)**: Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the fourth criteria was met.
* **Score 2 (9)**: Enter a score from 1 to 10 for the second criteria.
* **Notes (10)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the shift.
* **Photos (11)**: Attach any photos or images that are relevant to the shift.
* **Shift Notes (12)**: Enter any notes or comments about the shift.
* **Shift Assessment (13)**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the shift met the required standards.
* **Shift Rating (14)**: Enter a score from 1 to 10 for the shift.
* **Batch Name (15)**: Enter the name of the batch produced during the shift.
	+ This field is required.
* **Batch Rating (16)**: Enter a score from 1 to 10 for the batch.
* **Overall Rating (17)**: Enter a score from 1 to 10 for the overall quality of the production.
* **Quality Issues (18)**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if there were any quality issues.
* **Issues Description (19)**: Enter a brief description of the quality issues, if any.
* **Action Items (20)**: Enter any action items that need to be addressed.
* **Action Plan (21)**: Enter a plan to address the action items.
* **Follow-up (22)**: Enter any follow-up comments or notes.
* **Follow-up Date (23)**: Enter the date for follow-up, if necessary.
* **Assigned To (24)**: Enter the person assigned to address the action items or follow-up.

Note: Some fields are optional, but are intended to provide additional information or context.
