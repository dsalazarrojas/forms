# Surveillance Tour Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Surveillance Tour Checklist is a form used to gather information about the details of a surveillance tour, including incidents, checkpoints, and patrol notes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Patrol Details**: Enter a brief description of the patrol or tour you are reporting on.
2. **Incidents**: Select all incidents that occurred during the patrol or tour. You can select multiple options.
3. **Checkpoints**: Select the checkpoints that were checked during the patrol or tour.
4. **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the patrol or tour.
5. **Patrol Notes**: Enter any notes or comments about the patrol or tour.
6. **Patrol Comments**: Enter any comments or observations about the patrol or tour.
7. **Incident Details**: Enter a description of any incidents that occurred during the patrol or tour.
8. **Next Patrol**: Enter the date and time of the next patrol or tour.
9. **Notes 2**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the patrol or tour.
10. **Signature**: Enter your signature to confirm that you completed the form.
11. **Email**: Enter your email address for contact information.
12-14, 16-19, 21-25: **Patrol Vehicle**: Enter the details of the patrol vehicle used during the tour.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patrol Details (1)**: A text field where you can enter a brief description of the patrol or tour.
* **Incidents (2)**: A select multiple field where you can choose from the following options: No Incidents, Minor Incident, Major Incident, Critical Incident.
* **Checkpoints (3)**: A select one field where you can choose from the following options: Check all, Check some, Check no.
* **Notes (4)**: A text field where you can enter any additional notes or comments about the patrol or tour.
* **Patrol Notes (5)**: A text field where you can enter any notes or comments about the patrol or tour.
* **Patrol Comments (6)**: A text field where you can enter any comments or observations about the patrol or tour.
* **Incident Details (7)**: A text field where you can enter a description of any incidents that occurred during the patrol or tour.
* **Next Patrol (8)**: A date field where you can enter the date and time of the next patrol or tour.
* **Notes 2 (9)**: A text field where you can enter any additional notes or comments about the patrol or tour.
* **Signature (10)**: A text field where you can enter your signature to confirm that you completed the form.
* **Email (11)**: An email field where you can enter your email address for contact information.
* **Patrol Vehicle 2-14, 16-19, 21-25**: Text fields where you can enter the details of the patrol vehicle used during the tour.

**Tips**

* Make sure to select all incidents that occurred during the patrol or tour.
* Select the correct options for checkpoints and incidents.
* Enter clear and concise notes and comments about the patrol or tour.
* Enter a valid signature and email address.
* Enter the correct details for the patrol vehicle.
