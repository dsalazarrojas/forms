# CrowdStrike Global Threat Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CrowdStrike Global Threat Report Form is used to collect information about security incidents. This form helps in analyzing and reporting threats to ensure the safety and security of your organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the Threat Level of the incident.
2. Choose whether the incident is of type Yes or No.
3. Enter the Incident ID number.
4. Enter the Incident Date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. Enter the Threat Name.
6. Enter the Threat Source.
7. Enter the Incident Location.
8. Enter a brief description of the threat.
9. Choose whether the threat is severe or not.
10. Select a date range for the incident.
11. Enter any additional Incident Notes.
12. Classify the threat as Yes or No.
13. Enter the Threat Classification Source.
14. Enter a brief Threat Classification Notes.
15. Enter the Threat Classification Ownership.
16. Enter the Threat Classification Responsible Party.
17. Choose whether you have confidence in the classification or not.
18. Select the level of confidence in the classification (High or Low).
19. Choose the status of the classification (Active or Inactive).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Threat Level**: Select the level of severity of the incident (High or Low).
* **Threat Type**: Choose whether the incident is of type Yes or No.
* **Incident ID**: Enter the unique identifier number for the incident.
* **Incident Date**: Enter the date of the incident (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Threat Name**: Enter a name for the threat.
* **Threat Source**: Enter where the threat originated from.
* **Incident Location**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Threat Description**: Enter a brief description of the threat.
* **Threat Severity**: Choose whether the threat is severe or not.
* **Date Range**: Select a date range for the incident.
* **Incident Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the incident.
* **Threat Classification**: Classify the threat as Yes or No.
* **Threat Classification Source**: Enter the source of the threat classification.
* **Threat Classification Notes**: Enter a brief note about the threat classification.
* **Threat Classification Ownership**: Enter who owns the threat classification.
* **Threat Classification Responsible Party**: Enter the party responsible for the threat classification.
* **Threat Classification Confidence**: Choose whether you have confidence in the classification or not.
* **Threat Classification Confidence Level**: Select the level of confidence in the classification (High or Low).
* **Threat Classification Status**: Choose the status of the classification (Active or Inactive).
