# Weekly Tutor Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weekly Tutor Report is a form used to report on the progress of students and lessons. It is used to track the progress of students who have been tutored and to provide additional comments or feedback.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the students who have been tutored from the list provided.
2. Report the progress of lessons for each student.
3. Enter the date and time of reporting.
4. Enter any comments or feedback.
5. Enter any additional comments or feedback from the tutor.
6. Record the date and time of submission.
7. Specify if the form was submitted by the student or the tutor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **students** (students_list): Select students who have been tutored. Choose from the list of available students.
* **Lessons Progress** (lessons_progress): Report the progress of lessons for each student.
* **Date Of Reporting** (date_of_reporting): Report the date of reporting. Enter the date when the lessons progress was reported.
* **Time Of Reporting** (time_of_reporting): Report the time of reporting. Enter the time when the lessons progress was reported.
* **Comments** (comments): Enter any comments or feedback.
* **Tutor Comments** (tutor_comments): Enter any additional comments or feedback from the tutor.
* **Submit Date** (submit_date): Record the date of submission. This is when the form was submitted.
* **Submit Time** (submit_time): Record the time of submission. This is when the form was submitted.
* **Form Submitted By** (form_submitted_by): Indicates if the form was submitted by the student or the tutor. Select 'Yes' if the form was submitted by the student, otherwise select 'No'.
* **Form Completed By** (form_completed_by): Indicates if the form was completed by the student or the tutor. Select 'Yes' if the form was completed by the student, otherwise select 'No'.

## Tips

- Make sure to report the progress of lessons for each student accurately.
- Enter the correct date and time of reporting.
- Use the comments fields to provide additional comments or feedback.
- Specify who submitted and completed the form for internal tracking purposes.
