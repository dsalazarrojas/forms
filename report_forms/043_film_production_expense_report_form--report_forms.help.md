# film production expense report form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to be used by film production team members to report and track expenses related to specific films.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the correct **Expense Type** from the provided options:
	*   Film
	*   Equipment
	*   Talent
	*   Miscellaneous
2.  Enter the **Expense Date** of the reported expense.
3.  Enter the **Expense Amount** (in numeric format).
4.  Provide a detailed description of the expense in the **Expense Description** field.
5.  Calculate the **Total Expenses** (optional).
6.  Select the **Category** of the expense:
	*   Film
	*   Production
	*   Post
	*   Pre
7.  Enter the **Film Name** (if applicable).
8.  Enter the **Production Company** (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Expense Type** (`Expense Type`, select_one, required): Select the type of expense you are reporting (e.g., Film, Equipment, Talent, or Miscellaneous).
*   **Expense Date** (`Expense Date`, date, required): Enter the date of the expense.
*   **Expense Amount** (`Expense Amount`, number, required): Enter the cost of the expense in numeric format.
*   **Expense Description** (`Expense Description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the expense, including details about what the expense covers.
*   **Total Expenses** (`Total Expenses`, number, optional): Enter the total expenses for the current film (optional).
*   **Category** (`Category`, select_one, required): Choose the category of the expense from the provided options.
*   **Film Name** (`Film Name`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the film the expense relates to.
*   **Production Company** (`Production Company`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the production company related to the expense.
