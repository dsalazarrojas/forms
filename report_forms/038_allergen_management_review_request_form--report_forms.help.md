# Allergen Management Review Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Allergen Management Review Request Form is used to request a review of an allergen issue. This form helps to gather information, coordinate resources, and ensure a thorough investigation and review process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information.
2.  Select the relevant options and answers for each field.
3.  Ensure that you have attached all necessary documentation and supporting information.
4.  Review the form before submitting it to ensure that all fields are filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Request ID (1)** (`text`, `required`): Enter a unique identifier for your request. This will help track and reference your issue.
*   **Request Date (2)** (`date`, `required`): Enter the date of your request.
*   **Requester Name (3)** (`text`, `required`): Enter your full name as the person making the request.
*   **Requester Email (4)** (`email`, `required`): Enter your contact email address.
*   **Department (5)** (`select_one`, `required`): Select the department relevant to your issue (e.g., Quality Assurance, Food Safety, etc.).
*   **Facility Location (6)** (`text`, `required`): Enter the location where the issue occurred.
*   **Type of Review Requested (7)** (`select_one`, `required`): Select the type of review you are requesting (e.g., routine, incident investigation, new product assessment, etc.).
*   **Priority Level (8)** (`select_one`, `required`): Select the level of urgency for the review (e.g., immediate, within 24 hours, etc.).
*   **Allergens Involved (9)** (`select_multiple`, `required`): Select all allergens that are involved in the issue.
*   **Products Affected (10)** (`text`, `required`): List the products affected by the issue.
*   **Incident Description (11)** (`text`, `required`): Provide a detailed description of what happened.
*   **Incident Date (12)** (`date`, `optional`): Enter the date of the incident, if applicable.
*   **Incident Location (13)** (`text`, `optional`): Enter the location of the incident, if applicable.
*   **Root Cause Known (14)** (`select_one`, `required`): Indicate whether the root cause is known or suspected.
*   **Suspected Root Cause (15)** (`text`, `optional`): Enter the suspected root cause, if applicable.
*   **Customer Impact (16)** (`select_one`, `required`): Select how the customer was impacted (e.g., no impact, potential exposure, etc.).
*   **Product Distribution Status (17)** (`select_one`, `required`): Select the status of the product distribution (e.g., in production, in warehouse, etc.).
*   **Recall Consideration (18)** (`select_one`, `required`): Indicate whether recall is being considered.
*   **Regulatory Notification Required (19)** (`select_one`, `required`): Indicate whether regulatory notification is required.
*   **Previous Similar Incidents (20)** (`select_one`, `optional`): Indicate whether this is a similar issue that has happened before.
*   **Documentation Attached (21)** (`text`, `optional`): List any attached documents that support your request.
*   **Team Members Needed (22)** (`select_multiple`, `optional`): Select the team members who should be involved in the review process.
*   **Review Deadline (23)** (`date`, `required`): Enter the deadline for the review.
*   **Expected Outcomes (24)** (`text`, `required`): Describe the expected outcomes from the review.
*   **Corrective Actions Initiated (25)** (`select_one`, `required`): Indicate whether any corrective actions have been taken.
*   **Actions Description (26)** (`text`, `optional`): Enter a description of the actions taken, if applicable.
*   **Communication Plan Needed (27)** (`select_one`, `required`): Indicate whether communication with stakeholders is required.
*   **Additional Information (28)** (`text`, `optional`): Provide any additional relevant details.
*   **Management Notified (29)** (`select_one`, `required`): Indicate whether management has been informed.

This form is designed to ensure that all necessary information is gathered and that the review process is thorough and effective. Please review the form carefully and ensure that all fields are filled out accurately before submitting it. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to the relevant team members for guidance.
