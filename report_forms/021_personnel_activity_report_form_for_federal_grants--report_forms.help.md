# Personnel Activity Report Form For Federal Grants - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report the time spent by employees on specific grant-related activities. It helps to track and gather information about employee activities to ensure accurate reporting and compliance with federal grant regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Grant Number, which identifies the federal grant that the employee worked on.
2. Select the report period for the activities reported, which is usually the quarter or fiscal year.
3. Enter the Employee Name, which is the name of the employee who performed the activities.
4. Select the Employee Department, which is the department where the employee works.
5. Enter the Start Date and End Date of the activities reported.
6. Enter the total hours spent on the activities.
7. Provide additional notes about the activities, if necessary.
8. Enter your name and email address as the submitter.
9. Enter your phone number as the submitter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Grant Number** (`grant_number`, text, optional): Enter the unique identifier of the federal grant that you worked on.
* **Report Period** (`report_period`, select_one, optional): Select the report period for the activities reported. Options are: 1, 2, or 3.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee who performed the activities.
* **Employee Department** (`employee_department`, text, optional): Enter the department where the employee works.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the activities reported.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the activities reported.
* **Hours** (`hours`, number, optional): Enter the total hours spent on the activities.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide additional notes about the activities, if necessary.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the submitter.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address as the submitter.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number as the submitter.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have the correct Grant Number to report.
* Check the report period carefully to ensure you are reporting for the correct quarter or fiscal year.
* Enter accurate information for all fields, including Employee Name, Employee Department, Start Date, End Date, and Hours.
* Provide clear and concise additional notes, if necessary.
* Enter your name, email address, and phone number correctly as the submitter.
