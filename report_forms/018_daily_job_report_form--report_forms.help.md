# daily job report form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Daily Job Report Form is designed to capture daily work details from employees, allowing managers to track work hours, tasks completed, and other relevant information. This form is used for performance evaluation and job assignment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the report date for the day's activities.
2. Enter your name and employee ID as required fields.
3. Provide the number of hours worked and any completed works/tasks.
4. Enter any comments or notes about your work activities.
5. Select the option for "Submitted To" and "Selected Option" as required fields.
6. Select the department and job title for your job assignment.
7. Add any additional comments from your manager or supervisor as needed.
8. Sign the form with your employee signature and manager's signature (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Date (1)**: Enter the date for which you are reporting your work activities.
* **Employee Name (2)**: Enter your full name as an employee in the company.
* **Employee ID (3)**: Enter your employee ID number as required.
* **Hours Worked (4)**: Report the number of hours you worked during the day.
* **Works Completed (5)**: Describe any notable tasks or works you completed during the day.
* **Comments (6)**: Add any additional comments or notes about your work activities.
* **Manager Comments (7)**: Add comments from your manager or supervisor, if applicable.
* **Submitted By (8)**: Enter your name as the person submitting this report.
* **Submitted On (9)**: Select the date you are submitting this report.
* **Submitted To (10)**: Select the manager to whom this report is submitted.
* **Selected Option (11)**: Select one or more options from the list.
* **Department (12)**: Select your department for your job assignment.
* **Section (13)**: Enter any additional details about your section or job assignment.
* **Sub Section (14)**: Enter any additional details about your sub-section or job assignment.
* **Supervisor Comments (15)**: Add comments from your supervisor or manager, if applicable.
* **Manager Signature (16)**: Add your manager's signature, if required.
* **Employee Signature (17)**: Add your employee signature.
* **Employee Number (18)**: Enter your employee number as required.
* **Job Title (19)**: Select your job title for your job assignment.
* **Supervisor Number (20)**: Enter your supervisor's number, if applicable.
* **Submitted By Manager (21)**: Enter your manager's name, if required.
* **Submitted To Manager (22)**: Enter the manager to whom this report is submitted.
* **Department Manager (23)**: Enter your department manager's name, if required.
* **Section Manager (24)**: Enter your section manager's name, if required.
* **Manager Comments 2 (25)**: Add comments from your manager or supervisor, if applicable.

Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
