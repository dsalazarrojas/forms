# Accommodation Shift Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report the details of a shift worked by an employee in an accommodation setting, such as a hotel or inn.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the shift in the "Shift Date" field.
2. Enter the start and end times of the shift in the "Shift Start Time" and "Shift End Time" fields, respectively.
3. Enter your full name in the "Employee Name" field.
4. Enter your employee identification number in the "Employee ID" field.
5. Select your position title from the "Position Title" dropdown menu.
6. Enter the name of the accommodation property where you worked in the "Accommodation Property" field.
7. Select the type of shift you worked from the "Shift Type" dropdown menu.
8. Enter the number of rooms assigned to you for the shift in the "Rooms Assigned" field.
9. Enter the number of rooms cleaned during the shift in the "Rooms Cleaned" field.
10. Enter the number of check-ins and check-outs you assisted during the shift in the "Check Ins Assisted" and "Check Outs Assisted" fields, respectively.
11. Enter the number of maintenance issues reported during the shift in the "Maintenance Issues Reported" field.
12. Provide a brief description of any maintenance issues reported in the "Maintenance Issues Description" field, if applicable.
13. Enter the number of guest complaints received during the shift in the "Guest Complaints" field.
14. Provide a brief description of any guest complaints received in the "Guest Complaints Description" field, if applicable.
15. Enter the number of incidents reported during the shift in the "Incidents Reported" field.
16. Provide a brief description of any incidents reported in the "Incidents Description" field, if applicable.
17. Enter any notes from your supervisor about the shift in the "Supervisor Notes" field, if applicable.
18. Finally, sign and date the form with your electronic signature in the "Employee Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shift Date** (`shift_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the shift.
* **Shift Start Time** (`shift_start_time`, time, required): Enter the time when the shift started.
* **Shift End Time** (`shift_end_time`, time, required): Enter the time when the shift ended.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required): Enter your employee identification number.
* **Position Title** (`position_title`, text, required): Select your job title from the dropdown menu.
* **Accommodation Property** (`accommodation_property`, text, required): Enter the name of the accommodation property where you worked.
* **Shift Type** (`shift_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of shift you worked from the dropdown menu.
* **Rooms Assigned** (`rooms_assigned`, number, required): Enter the number of rooms assigned to you for the shift.
* **Rooms Cleaned** (`rooms_cleaned`, number, required): Enter the number of rooms cleaned during the shift.
* **Check Ins Assisted** (`check_ins_assisted`, number, required): Enter the number of check-ins you assisted during the shift.
* **Check Outs Assisted** (`check_outs_assisted`, number, required): Enter the number of check-outs you assisted during the shift.
* **Maintenance Issues Reported** (`maintenance_issues_reported`, number, required): Enter the number of maintenance issues reported during the shift.
* **Maintenance Issues Description** (`maintenance_issues_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of any maintenance issues reported, if applicable.
* **Guest Complaints** (`guest_complaints`, number, required): Enter the number of guest complaints received during the shift.
* **Guest Complaints Description** (`guest_complaints_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of any guest complaints received, if applicable.
* **Incidents Reported** (`incidents_reported`, number, required): Enter the number of incidents reported during the shift.
* **Incidents Description** (`incidents_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of any incidents reported, if applicable.
* **Supervisor Notes** (`supervisor_notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes from your supervisor about the shift, if applicable.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, text, required): Sign and date the form with your electronic signature.

## Tips

* Make sure to complete all required fields.
* If a field is not required, you can leave it blank or enter any applicable information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or HR representative.
