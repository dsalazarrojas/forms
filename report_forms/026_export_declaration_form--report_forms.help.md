## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the main information (Page 1).
2. Enter the export details (Page 2).
3. Enter the shipping information (Page 3).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Main Information (Page 1)
* **Main Information (main_info)**: Enter a brief description of the export shipment, including the type of goods being exported and the reason for the shipment.

### Export Details (Page 2)
* **Export Details (export_details)**: Provide a detailed description of the export shipment, including the quantity, weight, and value of the goods being exported.

### Shipping Information (Page 3)
* **Shipping Information (shipping_info)**: Enter the shipping details, including the carrier's name, flight or vessel number, and expected arrival date.
