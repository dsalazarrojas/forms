# work_from_home_activity_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The work_from_home_activity_report_form is a reporting tool for employees to document their work from home activities. It helps track work hours, compliance with company policies, and provides a record of work from home activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the date you worked from home.
2. Enter the start time of your workday.
3. Enter the end time of your workday.
4. Type the location where you are working from home.
5. Enter the duration of your workday.
6. Describe your work break in the box provided.
7. Add any additional notes about your workday.
8. Enter your name and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Home Date**: The date you worked from home.
  - This field is a calendar input field where you can select the date you worked from home.
  - Please select the correct date from the calendar dropdown.
- **Work Start Time**: The start time of your workday.
  - This field is for entering the start time of your workday in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
  - Please enter the start time of your workday.
- **Work End Time**: The end time of your workday.
  - This field is for entering the end time of your workday in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
  - Please enter the end time of your workday.
- **Work Location**: The location where you are working from home.
  - This field is for entering the location where you are working from home, such as your home address or a coffee shop.
  - Please enter the location where you worked from home.
- **Work Duration**: The duration of your workday.
  - This field is for entering the total number of hours you worked.
  - Please enter the duration of your workday in decimal format (e.g. 2.5 for 2 hours and 30 minutes).
- **Work Break**: A brief description of your work break.
  - This field is for entering a brief description of your work break, such as "coffee break" or "exercise".
  - Please enter a brief description of your work break.
- **Notes**: Any additional notes about your workday.
  - This field is for entering any additional notes about your workday.
  - Please add any relevant information you want to be included in your report.
- **Submitted By**: The name of the employee submitting the report.
  - This field is for entering the name of the employee submitting the report.
  - Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
