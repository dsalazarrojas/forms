# sustainability_and_supplier_diversity_spend_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for a report on sustainability and supplier diversity spend. It helps the company track and analyze data on its suppliers, their sustainability practices, and other related details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is required.
2. Enter your company's information (Supplier Name, Supplier Address, Supplier City, Supplier State, Supplier Country, Supplier Zip, Supplier Email, and Supplier Phone).
3. Select the relevant sustainability and diversity spend details for your suppliers (Yes or No).
4. Choose the report date and end date for the period you want to analyze.
5. Enter any other information as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Info**: Enter your company's name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Supplier Info**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether your supplier practices sustainability.
* **Sustainability Info**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the supplier's level of sustainability practice.
* **Spend Info**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the supplier's spend on diversity and sustainability initiatives.
* **Report Date**: Enter the date you want the report to cover (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the report period (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Supplier Name**: Enter the name of your supplier.
* **Supplier Address**: Enter the supplier's address.
* **Supplier City**: Enter the city of the supplier's location.
* **Supplier State**: Enter the state of the supplier's location.
* **Supplier Country**: Enter the country of the supplier's location.
* **Supplier Zip**: Enter the zip code of the supplier's location.
* **Supplier Email**: Enter the supplier's email address.
* **Supplier Phone**: Enter the supplier's phone number.
* **Supplier Type**: Enter the type of supplier (optional).
