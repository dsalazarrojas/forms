# Swimming Pool Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about incidents or accidents that occurred in a swimming pool environment. It will help pool administrators or staff to document and investigate incidents to ensure pool safety and maintenance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Pool Manager Name** field with the name of the person in charge of the pool area where the incident occurred.
2. Select the **Date and Time** of the incident using the calendar icon.
3. Provide a detailed description of what happened in the **Incident Description** field.
4. Enter the name and contact details of the staff member involved in the incident.
5. List any witnesses and their details in the **Witness Details** field.
6. Check the status of the pool equipment in the **Pool Equipment Status** field.
7. Describe the **Incident Outcome**.
8. Choose the type of incident that occurred from the options provided in the **Incident Type** field.
9. Indicate if there was any **Pool Equipment Damage**.
10. Check if a staff member conducted an investigation into the incident.
11. Describe the **Incident Cause**.
12. Enter any **Action Taken** in response to the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, text, required: false): Enter a brief summary of the incident.
* **Pool Manager Name** (`pool_manager_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person in charge of the pool area where the incident occurred.
* **Date and Time** (`incident_date_time`, date, required: false): Select the date and time of the incident using the calendar icon.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of what happened.
* **Staff Name** (`staff_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the staff member involved in the incident.
* **Staff Contact Details** (`staff_contact_details`, text, required: false): Enter the contact details of the staff member.
* **Witness Details** (`witness_details`, text, required: false): List any witnesses and their details.
* **Pool Equipment Status** (`pool_equipment_status`, text, required: false): Check the status of the pool equipment.
* **Incident Outcome** (`incident_outcome`, text, required: false): Describe the incident outcome.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of incident from the options provided.
* **Pool Equipment Damage** (`pool_equipment_damage`, select_multiple, required: false): Check if any pool equipment was damaged.
* **Staff Conducted Investigation** (`staff_conducted_investment`, select_multiple, required: false): Check if a staff member conducted an investigation into the incident.
* **Incident Cause** (`incident_cause`, text, required: false): Describe the incident cause.
* **Action Taken** (`action_taken`, text, required: false): Enter any action taken in response to the incident.

## Tips

* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the pool staff for guidance.
* Please fill in all fields as much as possible, but do not fill in fields that you are not sure or do not know the answer to.
* If the incident is ongoing, please update the form as more information becomes available.
* If you need help with the form, please contact the pool administrators or staff for assistance.
