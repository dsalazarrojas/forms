# Wildlife Rescue Incident Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wildlife Rescue Incident Report form is used to document incidents of wildlife rescue, gather information about the species involved, and track the response by the wildlife rescue team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Species ID ( Species ID, Number, Required)**: Enter the species' unique identifier, such as a species code or a reference number.
* **Species ( Species, Text, Optional)**: Provide the common name of the species, such as "American Bald Eagle" or "Red Fox".
* **Shelter ID (Shelter ID, Number, Optional)**: Enter the shelter's identifier, if the animal was rescued from a shelter.
* **Shelter Name (Shelter Name, Text, Optional)**: Enter the name of the shelter where the animal was rescued from.
* **Animal Type (Animal Type, Text, Optional)**: Describe the type of animal, such as "mammal" or "bird".
* **Animal Status (Animal Status, Text, Optional)**: Provide a brief description of the animal's status, such as "injured" or "orphaned".
* **Date of Event (Date of Event, Date, Optional)**: Enter the date of the event, in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
* **Time of Event (Time of Event, Time, Optional)**: Enter the time of the event, in the format "HH:MM AM/PM".
* **Location of Event (Location of Event, Text, Optional)**: Enter the location where the event occurred, including city, state, and zip code.
* **Rescue Details (Rescue Details, Text, Optional)**: Provide a detailed description of the rescue, including any challenges faced and how they were overcome.
* **Injury Description (Injury Description, Text, Optional)**: Describe any injuries or conditions the animal was in at the time of rescue.
* **Medical Attention Given (Medical Attention Given, Text, Optional)**: If the animal received medical attention, describe the treatment or medication given.
* **Shelter Notes (Shelter Notes, Text, Optional)**: Enter any notes or comments from the shelter staff or management.
* **Wildlife Rescue Team (Wildlife Rescue Team, Text, Optional)**: If the wildlife rescue team was contacted, provide their name and contact information.
* **Contact Info (Contact Info, Text, Optional)**: Enter your contact information, including name, phone number, and email address.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure proper tracking and follow-up.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or seek guidance from your supervisor or team lead.
* If you need to submit multiple reports, please fill out a separate form for each incident.
