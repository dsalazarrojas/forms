# Pool Chemicals Check Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pool Chemicals Check Form is used to track and report the levels of pool chemicals, such as chlorine, pH, and alkalinity, to ensure that the pool's water quality is within safe and healthy ranges.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form once a week or as needed.
2. Check the pool's water levels and test results before submitting the form.
3. Answer the questions honestly and accurately based on your observations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pool Size** (`pool_size`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the size of your pool (e.g., small, medium, large).
* **Pool Type** (`pool_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check the boxes for the type of pool you have (e.g., above ground, in-ground, saltwater).
* **Water Temperature** (`water_temperature`, `number`, required: false): Enter the current water temperature of your pool in degrees Fahrenheit (e.g., 78° F).
* **Pool Water Level** (`pool_water_level`, `number`, required: false): Enter the current water level of your pool (e.g., 2ft, 3ft, etc.).
* **Chemical Level** (`chemical_level`, `number`, required: false): Enter the current levels of pool chemicals such as chlorine, pH, and alkalinity (e.g., 3ppm, 7.2, etc.).
* **Chlorine Level** (`chlorine_level`, `number`, required: false): Enter the current level of free chlorine in your pool (e.g., 3ppm).
* **Test Results** (`test_results`, `select_one`, required: false): Check the box if you have conducted tests for pool chemicals (e.g., 'Yes').
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about your pool's water quality.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and honestly based on your observations.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, please consult the pool's manual or contact the pool's maintenance personnel.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the pool's maintenance personnel or the pool's administrator.
