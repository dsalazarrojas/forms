# Research Grant Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Research Grant Audit Form is used to review and verify the details of a research grant. It is a critical component of the research grant management process, ensuring that all relevant information is accurately recorded and reported. This form is typically completed by grant administrators or auditors to assess the status of a grant and ensure compliance with grant guidelines and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary information and documentation for the grant in question.
2. Select the correct project ID from the dropdown list.
3. Enter the project name accurately and completely.
4. Enter the grant amount in numerical format (e.g., $10,000.00).
5. Choose the current grant status from the dropdown list (Active or Inactive).
6. Enter the review date in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2022-01-01).
7. Provide a detailed explanation of the evidence supporting the grant's current status.
8. If applicable, enter any findings or issues that need to be addressed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project ID** (`project_id`, number, required): Enter the unique identifier assigned to the project by the grant administration. This field is used to track and reference the project within the grant management system.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the project accurately and completely, as it appears on official documents and records.
* **Grant Amount** (`grant_amount`, number, required): Enter the total amount of funding allocated for the project. Ensure the format is numerical (e.g., $10,000.00).
* **Grant Status** (`grant_status`, select_one, required): Choose the current status of the grant from the dropdown list (Active or Inactive).
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the last review or audit.
* **Evidence** (`evidence`, text, required): Provide a detailed explanation of the evidence supporting the grant's current status.
* **Findings** (`findings`, text, optional): If there are any issues or concerns regarding the grant, enter a brief description of the findings. This field is not mandatory, but it can help identify potential problems or areas for improvement.
* **Grant Status** (`grant_status`, select_one, required) - Note: This field has a duplicate label. The correct field to use is the one on page 2 (Grant Status), which has the same label and same options as this field.
