# hr_case_note_submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hr_case_note_submission form is used to capture information about employee-submitted HR case notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee's name in the "Employee" field.
2. Enter the date of the incident in the "Incident Date" field. This should be the date the incident occurred.
3. Provide a brief description of the incident in the "Incident Details" field.
4. Describe the actions you took or plan to take in response to the incident in the "Actions Taken" field.
5. If applicable, attach any relevant documents or evidence to the "Attachments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee** (Employee, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee who experienced or witnessed the incident.
* **Incident Date** (Incident Date, date, optional): Enter the date the incident occurred in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Incident Details** (Incident Details, text, optional): Describe the incident in a brief and clear manner.
* **Actions Taken** (Actions Taken, text, optional): Describe the actions you took or plan to take in response to the incident.
* **Attachments** (Attachments, text, optional): If applicable, attach any relevant documents or evidence related to the incident.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide as much detail as possible about the incident.
* If you are unsure about any information, do not hesitate to ask for help.
* Remember to attach any relevant attachments that can support your report.
