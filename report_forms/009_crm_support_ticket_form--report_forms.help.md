# CRM Support Ticket Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from reporters who are experiencing issues with their CRM system. It is intended to be used by CRM system users to report problems or incidents that require attention from the support team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the "Reporter name" and "Reporter email" fields.
2. Select the category of the issue you are experiencing from the "Issue category" dropdown menu.
3. Choose the priority of the issue you are experiencing from the "Priority" dropdown menu.
4. Describe the issue you are experiencing in the "Issue description" field.
5. If applicable, provide any attachments or links related to the issue in the "Attachment reference or link" field.
6. If known, select an expected resolution date from the "Expected resolution date" field.
7. If necessary, provide any additional follow-up notes for internal use in the "Follow up notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Reporter name**: Enter your name as the person filing the ticket.
  Required field.
  Please provide your name as it will be used for internal communication and follow-up.
- **Reporter email**: Enter your email address as it will be used for follow-up communication.
  Required field.
  Please ensure that your email is accurate and up-to-date.
- **Issue category**: Select the category that best describes the area affected by the issue.
  Required field.
  Choose from: Data issue, Access problem, Integration failure, or UI bug.
- **Priority**: Select the level of impact on operations.
  Required field.
  Choose from: Low, Medium, High, or Critical.
- **Issue description**: Provide a detailed description of the issue you are experiencing.
  Required field.
  Please be as specific as possible to help the support team understand the problem.
- **Attachment reference or link**: If applicable, provide any attachments or links related to the issue.
  Optional field.
  This field is for screenshots, log files, or other relevant attachments.
- **Expected resolution date**: If known, select an expected date for resolution.
  Optional field.
  Please be as accurate as possible.
- **Follow up notes**: If necessary, provide any additional follow-up notes for internal use.
  Optional field.
  This field is for internal use only and will not be shared with the reporter.
