# Credit Report Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Credit Report Request Form is a document used to gather information from customers or clients for credit reporting or other business purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address accurately.
2. Enter your Phone number.
3. Provide your Date of Birth.
4. Enter your Social Security Number (if applicable).
5. If you have a Credit Card, enter the number.
6. Enter your Address.
7. Select the type of credit report you need (Individual or Business).
8. Choose how often you would like to receive credit reports (One time, Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`user_input`, text, Optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, Optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Optional): Enter a valid email address associated with your identity.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, Optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Social Security Number** (`social_security_number`, text, Optional): Enter your social security number (if applicable).
* **Credit Card Number** (`credit_card_number`, text, Optional): Enter your credit card number (if applicable).
* **Address** (`address`, text, Optional): Enter your current address.
* **Credit Report Type** (`credit_report_type`, select_one, Optional): Select the type of credit report you need (Individual or Business).
* **Credit Report Frequency** (`credit_report_frequency`, select_multiple, Optional): Choose how often you would like to receive credit reports (One time, Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly).

## Tips
Please ensure you fill in all required fields accurately and truthfully to avoid delays or errors in the credit reporting process. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team for assistance.
