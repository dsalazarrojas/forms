# Security Incident Response Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Security Incident Response Log form is used to document and manage security incidents, ensuring that all relevant information is collected and recorded.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Security Incident Response Log form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date and time the incident occurred in the "Incident Date" field.
2. Provide a brief description of the incident in the "Incident Description" field.
3. Indicate the type of incident that occurred in the "Incident Type" field.
4. Identify the assets affected by the incident in the "Affected Assets" field.
5. Enter the email of the person who reported the incident in the "Incident Reporter Email" field.
6. Update the incident status in the "Incident Status" field.
7. Enter any additional details or comments about the incident in the "Incident Details" field.
8. Enter the responder's information, including name, email, and comments in the "Responder" fields.
9. Update the resolution status in the "Resolution" field.
10. Enter any comments about the resolution in the "Resolution Comments" field.
11. Enter the date and time the incident was closed in the "Incident Closed Date" and "Incident Closed Time" fields.
12. Enter any comments about the incident closure in the "Incident Closed Comments" field.
13. Update the reviewer's information, including name and date in the "Reviewer" fields.
14. Update the review date and time in the "Review Date" and "Review Time" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date**: Enter the date and time the incident occurred. This field is a date field.
* **Incident Type**: Choose the type of incident that occurred from the drop-down menu. Options include Type of Incident, Other, Unknown, Network/Internet, System/Network, Physical, Personnel, Environmental, and Social.
* **Incident Description**: Provide a brief description of the incident. This field is a text field.
* **Incident Location**: Enter the location where the incident occurred. This field is a text field.
* **Affected Assets**: Identify the assets affected by the incident. Choose multiple options from the drop-down menu.
* **Incident Reporter**: Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
* **Incident Reporter Email**: Enter the email of the person who reported the incident. This field is an email field.
* **Incident Status**: Update the status of the incident to Active, Resolved, In Progress, or Unknown.
* **Incident Details**: Enter any additional details or comments about the incident. This field is a note field.
* **Incident Follow-up**: Enter any follow-up information or comments about the incident. This field is a text field.
* **Category**: Enter the category of the incident. This field is a text field.
* **Severity**: Choose the severity of the incident from the drop-down menu. Options include High, Medium, and Low.
* **Responder**: Enter the name of the person who responded to the incident.
* **Responder Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person who responded to the incident. This field is a text field.
* **Responder Email**: Enter the email of the person who responded to the incident. This field is an email field.
* **Responder Comments**: Enter any comments or details about the responder's actions. This field is a note field.
* **Resolution**: Choose the resolution status from the drop-down menu. Options include No Resolution, Not Applicable, In Progress, and Resolved.
* **Resolution Comments**: Enter any comments about the resolution. This field is a note field.
* **Incident Closed Date**: Enter the date the incident was closed.
* **Incident Closed Time**: Enter the time the incident was closed.
* **Incident Closed Comments**: Enter any comments about the incident closure. This field is a note field.
* **Reviewer**: Enter the name of the person who reviewed the incident.
* **Review Date**: Enter the date the incident was reviewed.
* **Review Time**: Enter the time the incident was reviewed.
