# Component Integrity Verification Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Component Integrity Verification Report form is a standardized template for recording inspection findings and corrective actions taken on a component or asset. It is designed to provide a systematic and structured approach to documenting the component's integrity and the actions taken to maintain or improve its condition.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Section 1: Inspection Summary**
   - This section is a brief overview of the inspection findings and summary of the corrective actions taken.
   - Use this section to provide a high-level overview of the inspection results and any notable issues or concerns.

2. **Section 2: Inspector Information**
   - Enter your name and title in this field. This is the person who completed the report.
   - Make sure to include your name and title accurately to ensure proper attribution and accountability.

3. **Section 3: Inspection Date**
   - Enter the date of the inspection in this field. This is the date when the inspection was conducted.
   - Use the date format as specified in your organization's standard practices.

4. **Section 4: Component Identifier**
   - Enter the component's serial number or asset tag in this field. This helps identify the specific item being inspected.
   - Use the exact serial or asset tag number to ensure accurate tracking and referencing.

5. **Section 5: Integrity Findings**
   - Describe any defects or anomalies found during the inspection in this field. Provide a detailed account of the issues identified.
   - Be as specific as possible when documenting the findings, including any images or videos that support your claims.

6. **Section 6: Severity Level**
   - Rate the severity of the issues found during the inspection using the provided options: Low, Moderate, High, or Critical.
   - Use the severity level to prioritize and classify the issues identified.

7. **Section 7: Corrective Actions Taken**
   - Describe any corrective actions taken to mitigate or resolve the issues identified in this field.
   - Be as detailed as possible, including any photos or documents that support your claims.

8. **Section 8: Recommended Follow-up**
   - Suggest any next steps or follow-up actions that should be taken to maintain or improve the component's integrity.
   - Be specific and provide a clear timeline for the recommended actions.

9. **Section 9: Attachments**
   - Upload any photos, documents, or other attachments that support your claims or findings.
   - Make sure to include any relevant images or documents that provide context and evidence to support your report.

## Tips

* Use standard formatting and practices for dates and formatting.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Use attachments only for relevant and supporting materials.
* Proofread and review your report before submission to ensure accuracy and clarity.
