# Dog Care Progress Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report on the progress of a dog's care, including feeding, exercise, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the dog's name in the "dog_name" field.
2. Select the type of pet (dog, cat, or other) from the "pet_type" dropdown menu.
3. Select the food given to the dog from the "food_given" dropdown menu.
4. Enter the amount of time spent outside for the dog in the "time_spent_outside" field.
5. Enter any notes or comments about the dog's care in the "notes" field.
6. Enter the contact number of the owner or caregiver in the "contact_number" field.
7. Enter the name of the owner or caregiver in the "owner_name" field.
8. Enter the email of the owner or caregiver in the "owner_email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### dog_name
*   **Dog Name**: (`dog_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the dog being reported on.
### pet_type
*   **Pet Type**: (`pet_type`, select one, optional): Select the type of pet (dog, cat, or other).
### food_given
*   **Food Given**: (`food_given`, select multiple, optional): Select the food given to the dog from the list.
### time_spent_outside
*   **Time Spent Outside**: (`time_spent_outside`, time, optional): Enter the amount of time spent outside for the dog.
### notes
*   **Notes**: (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any comments or events related to the dog's care.
### contact_number
*   **Contact Number**: (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the contact number of the owner or caregiver.
### owner_name
*   **Owner Name**: (`owner_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the owner or caregiver.
### owner_email
*   **Owner Email**: (`owner_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the owner or caregiver.
