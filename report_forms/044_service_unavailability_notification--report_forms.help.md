# Service Unavailability Notification - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to notify stakeholders of service unavailability, allowing them to report incidents, describe their impact, and provide updates on the service recovery status. This form is intended for internal use, and completion is optional but recommended for maintaining accurate records and facilitating communication among teams.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Service Unavailability Notification" form from the available options.
2. Fill out the form fields as required or as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Unavailability Notification Form** (`service_unavailability_notification_form`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the service unavailability.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the time when the service became unavailable.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the time when the service is expected to be restored or is currently available.
* **Impact Assessment** (`impact_assessment`, text, optional): Describe the impact of the service unavailability on users or operations.
* **Affected Teams** (`affected_teams`, select multiple, optional): Select the teams that are impacted by the service unavailability.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the issue.
* **Update** (`update`, text, optional): Enter any updates or changes to the service recovery status.
* **Service Recovery Time** (`service_recovery_time`, time, optional): Enter the estimated or actual time when the service is expected to be restored.
* **Service Recovery Status** (`service_recovery_status`, select one, optional): Select the current service recovery status:
	+ Service Recovered
	+ Still Unavailable
	+ Under Investigation
	+ Unknown
* **Root Cause** (`root_cause`, text, optional): Enter the root cause of the issue, if known.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure efficient communication and record-keeping.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to your supervisor or the relevant team for guidance.
* Completion is optional, but it is recommended for maintaining accurate and comprehensive records.
* Use this form to report service unavailability for internal services only.
