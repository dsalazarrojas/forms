# teacher_weekly_plan_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for teachers to plan their weekly activities and tasks in an organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the subject(s) you will be teaching this week.
2. Choose the class(es) you will be teaching this week.
3. Fill in the date and time for your weekly activities (if applicable).
4. Add any additional notes or comments.
5. Enter your contact information (email and phone number, if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Subject Selection (Select Subject)
* **Select Subject (teacher_weekly_plan)** (`Select Subject`, select_one, required): Select the subject(s) you will be teaching this week. Choose one or more subjects from the provided options.

### Class Selection (Select Class)
* **Select Class (class_list)** (`Select Class`, select_multiple, required): Choose the class(es) you will be teaching this week. Select one or more classes from the provided options.

### Date (date)
* **Date (date)** (`Date`, date, required): Enter the date of your weekly activities (if applicable).

### Time (time)
* **Time (time)** (`Time`, time, required): Fill in the time for your weekly activities (if applicable).

### Notes (notes)
* **Notes (notes)** (`Notes`, note, required): Add any additional notes or comments about your weekly activities.

### Contact Information (email)
* **Email (email)** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.

### Contact Information (phone)
* **Phone (phone)** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
