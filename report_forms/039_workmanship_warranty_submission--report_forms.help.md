# Workmanship Warranty Submission - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers who are seeking a workmanship warranty for a specific job. The information provided in this form will be used to process the warranty claim.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete all the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Ensure that the customer name, email, and phone number match the customer's official records.
3. Provide detailed information about the job, including the job number and date.
4. Describe the specific claim you are making about the workmanship of the job.
5. Review and submit the form once you have provided all the necessary details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): This field is used to collect the customer's name. Please enter your name as it appears on your official records.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): This field is used to collect your email address. Please enter your work email or any other email that is associated with your official records.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required): This field is used to collect your phone number. Please enter your primary phone number that is associated with your official records.
* **Job Details** (`job_details`, text, required): This field is used to collect information about the job in question. Please provide a brief description of the job, including any relevant details or context.
* **Warranty Claimant** (`warranty_claimant`, text, required): This field is used to collect information about who is making the warranty claim. Please describe yourself or the person making the claim.
* **Claim Details** (`claim_details`, text, required): This field is used to collect information about the specific claim you are making. Please provide a detailed description of the issue or problem you are experiencing with the job.
* **Job Number** (`job_number`, number, required): This field is used to collect the unique number associated with the job. Please ensure that this number is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Job Date** (`job_date`, date, required): This field is used to collect the date the job was completed. Please ensure that this date is accurate and corresponds to the actual completion date of the job.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or need assistance with the form, please contact us.
* If you have already submitted a warranty claim for this job, please do not submit another claim.
