# los_santos_daily_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect daily reports from players or staff of the "Los Santos" organization. The goal is to gather information about player activities, interactions, or incidents that occurred during a specific reporting period. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date you want to report for in the "Report Date" field.
2. Choose the time you want to report for in the "Report Time" field.
3. Enter your player name in the "Player Name" field.
4. Describe your player character in the "Player Character" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Report Date (Report Date)**: Select the date you want to report for.
* **Report Time (Report Time)**: Choose the time you want to report for.
* **Player Name (Player Name)**: Enter your player name.
* **Player Character (Player Character)**: Describe your player character.
