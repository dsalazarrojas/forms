# Daily OPD Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily OPD Report Form is used to report on Outpatient Department (OPD) activities for a given day. This form captures essential information about patients, their treatments, and hospital visits. It helps healthcare professionals track and analyze daily activities in the OPD, making it easier to manage patient care and optimize services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the first name, middle name, and last name of the patient in the respective fields.
2.  Type the patient's age in the 'Age' field.
3.  Select the patient's sex from the dropdown list.
4.  Enter the contact number of the patient in the 'Contact Number' field.
5.  Enter the name of the hospital where the patient is admitted in the 'Hospital Name' field.
6.  Enter the department where the patient is being treated in the 'Department Name' field.
7.  Type the bed number of the patient in the 'Bed Number' field.
8.  Enter the total number of patients treated on the 'Patient Count' field.
9.  Enter the total number of visits made on the 'Visit Count' field.
10.  Enter the duration of the patient's admission in the 'Admission Duration' field.
11.  Select the reason for admission from the dropdown list.
12.  Enter the date of discharge in the 'Discharge Date' field.
13.  Select multiple reasons for admission in the 'Reason for Admission' field.
14.  Enter any additional diagnosis or comments in the 'Diagnosis' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter the first name of the patient.
* **Middle Name** (`middle_name`, `text`, required): Enter the middle name of the patient.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter the last name of the patient.
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, required): Type the age of the patient.
* **Sex** (`sex`, `select_one`, required): Select the sex of the patient from the dropdown list.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter the contact number of the patient.
* **Hospital Name** (`hospital_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the hospital where the patient is being treated.
* **Department Name** (`department_name`, `text`, required): Enter the department where the patient is being treated.
* **Bed Number** (`bed_number`, `number`, required): Type the bed number of the patient.
* **Patient Count** (`patient_count`, `number`, required): Enter the total number of patients treated.
* **Visit Count** (`visit_count`, `number`, required): Enter the total number of visits made.
* **Admission Duration** (`admission_duration`, `time`, required): Enter the duration of the patient's admission.
* **Discharge Date** (`discharge_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of discharge.
* **Reason for Admission** (`reason_for_admission`, `select_multiple`, required): Select multiple reasons for admission from the dropdown list.
* **Diagnosis** (`diagnosis`, `text`, required): Enter any additional diagnosis or comments.

Note: All fields are required for proper completion of the form. Please ensure that all fields are filled according to the specified data types.
