# Travel Agent Income Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a travel agent's income. It is an essential tool for travel agents to report their income accurately and on time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information for your travel agency.
2. Make sure to fill out each field accurately and completely.
3. Review the form before submitting to ensure all fields are filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **client_name** (`client_name`, `text`, required: false): This field is for the name of the client or the individual who earned the income. Enter the full name as it appears on their official documents.
* **income** (`income`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total income earned by the client.
* **income_date** (`income_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date on which the income was earned.

## Tips
* Double-check the date field for accuracy, as this is a specific date.
* Ensure that the client's name is spelled correctly to avoid any discrepancies.
