# Mental Health Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect mental health information from users. It has 8 pages with various fields for users to input their responses.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by reviewing each page of the form, taking note of the fields and options available.
2. For each field, carefully read the label and the options provided to ensure you understand what is being asked.
3. Enter your responses for each field, making sure to follow the instructions for the specific field type.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure they are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. mental_health_survey_page_1** (text): Enter your response to this field.
* **2. mental_health_survey_page_2** (select_one): Choose one of the options provided.
* **3. mental_health_survey_page_3** (select_multiple): Select one or more of the options provided.
* **4. mental_health_survey_page_4** (number): Enter a numerical value.
* **5. mental_health_survey_page_5** (number): Enter another numerical value.
* **6. mental_health_survey_page_6** (date): Enter a date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **7. mental_health_survey_page_7** (time): Enter a time in the format HH:MM:SS.
* **8. mental_health_survey_page_8** (note): Enter a note or comment about your mental health.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer each field carefully and accurately.
* Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure they are complete and make sense.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact the form administrators for assistance.
