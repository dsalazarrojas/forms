# Landscaping General Time Sheet Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Landscaping General Time Sheet Form is a tool for collecting employment information related to landscaping work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Select your Job Type from the Job Type dropdown menu.
4. Enter the Date you worked in the Date Worked field.
5. Enter the number of Hours you worked in the Hours Worked field.
6. Enter your Wage in the Wage field.
7. Select your Pay Period from the Pay Period dropdown menu.
8. Add any additional Notes about your work in the Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name in this field.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, optional): Select your job type from the list:
	+ Landscaping
	+ Yard Cleaning
	+ Lawn Mowing
	+ Snow Removal
* **Date Worked** (`date_worked`, date, optional): Enter the date you worked in this format: `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Hours Worked** (`hours_worked`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours you worked for this pay period.
* **Wage** (`wage`, number, optional): Enter your hourly wage.
* **Pay Period** (`pay_period`, select_one, optional): Select your pay period from the list:
	+ Weekly
	+ Bi-Weekly
	+ Monthly
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about your work, such as special tasks or conditions that affect your pay.
