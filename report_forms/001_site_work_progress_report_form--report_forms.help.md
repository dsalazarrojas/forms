# Site Work Progress Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Site Work Progress Report Form is a tool used to track the status of ongoing site work projects. This form helps site workers to provide regular updates on their project progress and allows for the collection of relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the project status of your assigned projects.
2.  Select the current project status from the dropdown menu for "Project Status".
3.  Indicate whether "Work Completed" is applicable to your project by selecting the relevant options from the dropdown menu.
4.  Provide a brief labor summary in the text field for "Labor Summary".
5.  Choose whether "Equipment Summar" is relevant to your project by selecting the correct option.
6.  Enter any relevant labor costs in the "Labor Costs" field.
7.  Enter any relevant equipment costs in the "Equipment Costs" field.
8.  Calculate and enter the total project costs in the "Total Costs" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Project Status (Active/Inactive)**: Select the current status of your project. This can be either Active or Inactive.
*   **Work Completed (Yes/No)**: Indicate whether work has been completed for your project.
*   **Labor Summary**: Provide a brief summary of the labor work completed for your project.
*   **Equipment Summary (Yes/No)**: Choose whether equipment is being used for your project.
*   **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your project progress.
*   **Labor Costs**: Enter the total labor costs for your project.
*   **Equipment Costs**: Enter the total equipment costs for your project.
*   **Total Costs**: Calculate and enter the total project costs.
