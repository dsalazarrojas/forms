# Neighbor Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Neighbor Complaint Form is used to report and track issues or disputes between neighbors. It helps our team gather essential information to understand the situation and potential resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in the address field with the address of the issue location.
- Provide a clear and concise description of the problem in the problem_statement field.
- Enter the date of the dispute in the dispute_date field.
- Choose the most suitable resolution action from the select_one options in the resolution_action field.
- Fill in your contact information in the contact_number, contact_name, and contact_email fields.
- If there's a witness involved, fill in their statement in the witness_statement field, their name in the witness_name field, and their contact information in the contact_number, contact_name, and contact_email fields.
- Choose the most suitable resolution status from the select_one options in the resolution_status field.
- Fill in the assigned user's name in the assigned_user field and the tool used for resolution in the assigned_tool field.
- Enter the date the issue was assigned to the assigned_user in the assigned_date field.
- Provide any additional notes in the notes field.
- Choose the most suitable complaint type from the select_one options in the complaint_type field.
- Choose the most suitable report type from the select_one options in the report_type field.
- Fill in any report details in the report_details field.
- If a witness is involved, provide their details in the witness_details field.
- Fill in the witness's contact information in the contact_number, contact_name, and contact_email fields.
- Choose the most suitable resolution follow-up option from the select_one options in the resolution_follow_up field.
- Choose the most suitable witness agreement from the select_one options in the witness_agreement field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **address**: Fill in the address where the issue or dispute occurred.
* **problem_statement**: Provide a clear and concise description of the problem.
* **dispute_date**: Enter the date the issue started or when it was noticed.
* **resolution_action**: Choose the most suitable action to take for resolution (e.g., mediation, arbitration, or resolution with a specific party).
* **contact_number**: Fill in your contact phone number.
* **contact_name**: Fill in your name.
* **contact_email**: Fill in your email address.
* **witness_statement**: If a witness is involved, provide their statement about the issue.
* **witness_name**: Fill in the witness's name.
* **witness_contact_number**: Fill in the witness's contact phone number.
* **witness_email**: Fill in the witness's email address.
* **witness_address**: Fill in the witness's address.
* **resolution_date**: Enter the date the issue was resolved.
* **resolution_status**: Choose the most suitable status for the resolution (e.g., resolved, pending, or unresolved).
* **assigned_user**: Fill in the name of the user assigned to handle the issue.
* **assigned_tool**: Fill in the tool used for resolution (e.g., mediation or arbitration).
* **assigned_date**: Enter the date the issue was assigned to the user.
* **notes**: Provide any additional information or comments about the issue.
* **complaint_type**: Choose the most suitable type of complaint (e.g., noise, parking, or property damage).
* **report_type**: Choose the most suitable type of report (e.g., neighbor dispute or property issue).
* **report_details**: Fill in any additional details about the report.
* **witness_details**: Provide any details about the witness.
* **witness_contact_number**: Fill in the witness's contact phone number.
* **witness_name**: Fill in the witness's name.
* **witness_email**: Fill in the witness's email address.
* **resolution_follow_up**: Choose the most suitable follow-up action for the resolution (e.g., schedule a call or meeting).
* **witness_agreement**: Choose the most suitable agreement with the witness (e.g., they're willing to cooperate or not).
