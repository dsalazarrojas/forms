# Manhole Cover Replacement Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manhole Cover Replacement Report is used for tracking and documenting manhole cover replacement activities. This form helps ensure that accurate records are kept of when and where manhole covers are replaced, supporting the maintenance of the city's infrastructure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose whether the manhole cover was replaced (Yes or No).
2. Enter the date the manhole cover was replaced (in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`).
3. Select whether the location of the replaced manhole cover was recorded (Yes or No).
4. Choose the type of manhole cover that was replaced (Yes or No).
5. If relevant, add photo evidence of the replaced manhole cover or its location.
6. Select whether the manhole cover was properly signed off (Yes or No).
7. If the manhole cover was opened, choose whether this was recorded (Yes or No).
8. Select whether the location where the manhole cover was opened was recorded (Yes or No).
9. Choose the type of manhole cover that was opened (Yes or No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Manhole Cover Replacement Report** (`manhole_cover_replacement_report`, Select Multiple, Not Required): Choose whether the manhole cover was replaced (Yes or No).
* **Date Replaced** (`date_replaced`, Date, Not Required): Enter the date the manhole cover was replaced (in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`).
* **Location Replaced** (`location_replaced`, Select One, Not Required): Select whether the location of the replaced manhole cover was recorded (Yes or No).
* **Manhole Cover Type Replaced** (`manhole_cover_type_replaced`, Select Multiple, Not Required): Choose the type of manhole cover that was replaced (Yes or No).
* **Photo Evidence (URL or Attachment)** (`photo_evidence`, Note, Not Required): If relevant, add photo evidence of the replaced manhole cover or its location.
* **Sign Off** (`sign_off`, Select Multiple, Not Required): Select whether the manhole cover was properly signed off (Yes or No).
* **Date Opened** (`date_opened`, Date, Not Required): If the manhole cover was opened, enter the date it was opened (in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`).
* **Location Opened** (`location_opened`, Select One, Not Required): Select whether the location where the manhole cover was opened was recorded (Yes or No).
* **Manhole Cover Type Opened** (`manhole_cover_type_opened`, Select Multiple, Not Required): If the manhole cover was opened, choose the type of manhole cover that was opened (Yes or No).

# Tips
* Make sure to fill out all fields accurately and completely, as this information is used to keep track of the city's infrastructure maintenance activities.
* If you're unsure about any field, please refer to your department's guidelines for reporting procedures.
