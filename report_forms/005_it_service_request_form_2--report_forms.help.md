# it_service_request_form_2 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "it_service_request_form_2" is a form designed to collect information about IT service requests. It is intended for reporting technical issues or problems related to IT services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Select the relevant Category (Yes/No) for the issue you are reporting.
5. Describe the problem you are experiencing in the "Problem Description" field.
6. Provide any additional comments about the issue in the "Comments" field.
7. If necessary, enter your Contact Number for communication purposes.
8. Enter your Contact Time for the next available time to contact you.
9. Enter the Date of the issue (if known).
10. If needed, provide a brief Repair Request for the issue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your official email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Category** (Category, select multiple, optional): Choose whether the issue is Yes or No, to help us understand the severity of the problem.
* **Problem Description** (Problem Description, text, required): Describe the technical issue you are experiencing in detail.
* **Comments** (Comments, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information about the issue.
* **Contact Number** (Contact Number, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for more information.
* **Contact Time** (Contact Time, time, optional): Enter the next available time to contact you for more information.
* **Date** (Date, date, optional): Enter the date of the issue (if known).
* **Repair Request** (Repair Request, text, optional): If you have a specific request for the repair, please enter it here.

## Tips
* If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to ask for help.
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, contact us using the provided contact information.
