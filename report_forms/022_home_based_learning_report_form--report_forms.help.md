# Home Based Learning Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Based Learning Report Form is designed to collect information from parents and teachers about a student's progress in home-based learning programs. This form is meant to be filled out after a specific time period, such as at the end of each month, to track a student's development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. The form is divided into several sections, each with a specific field that you need to fill in:
	* **Parent Name**: Please enter your name as the parent or guardian of the student.
	* **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student you are reporting on.
	* **Contact Number**: Provide your contact number in case the school needs to reach out to you.
	* **Email**: Enter your email address for correspondence.
	* **Comments From Parent**: Share any comments or feedback you have about the student's progress.
	* **Comments From Teacher**: If applicable, add any comments from the teacher about the student's progress.
	* **Progress Update**: Describe the current progress of the student, including any notable achievements or challenges.
	* **Date Last Progress Update**: Enter the date of the last progress update.
	* **Next Progress Update**: Schedule the next progress update date.
	* **Parent Signature**: Sign this form to confirm that it is accurate.
	* **Teacher Signature**: If applicable, sign this form to confirm that it is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name** (Parent Name, text, required/optional): Enter your name as the parent or guardian of the student.
* **Student Name** (Student Name, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the student you are reporting on.
* **Contact Number** (Contact Number, text, required/optional): Provide your contact number in case the school needs to reach out to you.
* **Email** (Email, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for correspondence.
* **Comments From Parent** (Comments From Parent, note, required/optional): Share any comments or feedback you have about the student's progress.
* **Comments From Teacher** (Comments From Teacher, note, required/optional): If applicable, add any comments from the teacher about the student's progress.
* **Progress Update** (Progress Update, text, required/optional): Describe the current progress of the student, including any notable achievements or challenges.
* **Date Last Progress Update** (Date Last Progress Update, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the last progress update.
* **Next Progress Update** (Next Progress Update, date, required/optional): Schedule the next progress update date.
* **Parent Signature** (Parent Signature, text, required/optional): Sign this form to confirm that it is accurate.
* **Teacher Signature** (Teacher Signature, text, required/optional): If applicable, sign this form to confirm that it is accurate.

Note: All fields are required unless otherwise specified as "optional".
