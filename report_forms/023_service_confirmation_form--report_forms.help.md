# Service Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Confirmation Form is used to record and confirm the details of a service provided to a company. It helps ensure that all necessary information is recorded accurately and consistently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the company's name in the "Company Name" field.
2. Enter the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
3. Enter the service date in the "Service Date" field.
4. Enter the service time in the "Service Time" field.
5. Enter the completion date in the "Completion Date" field.
6. Select the service confirmation status in the "Service Confirmation" field.
7. Enter any additional notes in the "Notes" field.
8. Enter the email of the person submitting the form in the "Email" field.
9. Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form in the "Phone" field.
10. Enter the name of the person who submitted the form in the "Submitted By" field.
11. Enter the date the form was submitted in the "Submitted At" field.
12. Enter the name of the person who created the form in the "Created By" field.
13. Enter the date the form was created in the "Created At" field.
14. Enter the name of the person who updated the form in the "Updated By" field.
15. Enter the date the form was updated in the "Updated At" field.
16. Enter the name of the person assigned to complete the service in the "Assigned To" field.
17. Enter the name of the person assigned by in the "Assigned By" field.
18. Enter the name of the person who completed the service in the "Completed By" field.
19. Enter the date the service was completed in the "Completed At" field.
20. Select the current service status in the "Service Status" field.
21. Enter the name of the person or department the form was submitted to in the "Submitted To" field.
22. Enter the date the form was submitted in the "Submitted Date" field.
23. (Optional) Enter the name of the person who created the form in the "Created By 2" field.
24. (Optional) Enter the name of the person who updated the form in the "Updated By 2" field.
25. (Optional) Enter the date the form was submitted in the "Submitted At 2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name (Company Name, text, required)**: Enter the name of the company that received the service.
* **Employee Name (Employee Name, text, required)**: Enter the name of the employee who provided the service.
* **Service Date (Service Date, date, required)**: Enter the date the service was provided.
* **Service Time (Service Time, time, required)**: Enter the time the service was provided.
* **Completion Date (Completion Date, date, required)**: Enter the date the service was completed.
* **Service Confirmation (Service Confirmation, select_one, required)**: Select the status of the service confirmation.
* **Notes (Notes, note, required)**: Enter any additional notes about the service.
* **Email (Email, email, required)**: Enter the email of the person submitting the form.
* **Phone (Phone, text, required)**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted By (Submitted By, text, required)**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted At (Submitted At, date, required)**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Created By (Created By, text, required)**: Enter the name of the person who created the form.
* **Created At (Created At, date, required)**: Enter the date the form was created.
* **Updated By (Updated By, text, required)**: Enter the name of the person who updated the form.
* **Updated At (Updated At, date, required)**: Enter the date the form was updated.
* **Assigned To (Assigned To, text, required)**: Enter the name of the person assigned to complete the service.
* **Assigned By (Assigned By, text, required)**: Enter the name of the person assigned by.
* **Completed By (Completed By, text, required)**: Enter the name of the person who completed the service.
* **Completed At (Completed At, date, required)**: Enter the date the service was completed.
* **Service Status (Service Status, select_multiple, required)**: Select the current status of the service.
* **Submitted To (Submitted To, text, required)**: Enter the name of the person or department the form was submitted to.
* **Submitted Date (Submitted Date, date, required)**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Created By 2 (Created By 2, text, optional)**: (Optional) Enter the name of the person who created the form.
* **Updated By 2 (Updated By 2, text, optional)**: (Optional) Enter the name of the person who updated the form.
* **Submitted At 2 (Submitted At 2, date, optional)**: (Optional) Enter the date the form was submitted.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and consistently.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all necessary information is included.
* Use the "Created By", "Updated By", "Created At", and "Updated At" fields for tracking and auditing purposes.
* Use the "Assigned To", "Assigned By", "Completed By", and "Completed At" fields to track progress and completion of the service.
* Use the "Service Status" field to select the current status of the service.
* Use the "Submitted To" field to track who the form was submitted to.
