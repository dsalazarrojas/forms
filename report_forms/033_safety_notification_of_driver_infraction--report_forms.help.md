# Safety Notification Of Driver Infraction - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on a driver infraction, which is a formal notice or warning given to a driver for a specific offense or infraction while driving. The form is intended for reporting a driver infraction that occurred.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the driver information section (fields 11-17) with details about the vehicle involved in the infraction.
2. Fill in the infraction details section (fields 2-5, 18-20) with information about the infraction itself.
3. Fill in the report details section (fields 6-9, 21-24) with your own information as the person reporting the infraction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report a driver infraction** (field 1):
	+ This is the title of the form, and it doesn't require any input.
* **Driver infraction type** (field 2):
	+ Select the type of infraction that occurred (Driving under the influence, Speeding, or Reckless driving).
* **Date of infraction** (field 3):
	+ Enter the date when the infraction occurred.
* **Time of infraction** (field 4):
	+ Enter the time when the infraction occurred.
* **Location of infraction** (field 5):
	+ Enter the location where the infraction happened.
* **Description of infraction** (field 6):
	+ Provide a brief description of what happened during the infraction.
* **Attach a photo** (field 7):
	+ This field is not required. If you have a photo, you can attach it here.
* **Reporter details** (field 8):
	+ Enter your name and any other details you'd like to provide about yourself as the reporter.
* **Contact number** (field 9):
	+ Enter your contact number so that we can reach you for further questions or information.
* **Contact email** (field 10):
	+ Enter your email address so that we can contact you.
* **License number** (field 11):
	+ Enter the license number of the vehicle involved in the infraction.
* **Vehicle info** (field 12):
	+ Provide information about the vehicle involved, including its type, color, registration number, model, year, and make.
* **Infraction vehicle type** (field 13):
	+ Select the type of vehicle involved (Car, Truck, or Motorbike).
* **Infraction vehicle color** (field 14):
	+ Select the color of the vehicle involved.
* **Infraction vehicle registration number** (field 15):
	+ Enter the registration number of the vehicle involved.
* **Infraction vehicle model** (field 16):
	+ Enter the model of the vehicle involved.
* **Infraction vehicle year** (field 17):
	+ Enter the year of the vehicle involved.
* **Infraction vehicle make** (field 18):
	+ Enter the make of the vehicle involved.
* **Infraction vehicle license plate** (field 19):
	+ Enter the license plate of the vehicle involved.
* **Infraction status** (field 20):
	+ Select the status of the infraction (Pending, Active, or Completed).
* **Infraction status reason** (field 21):
	+ Provide a reason for the infraction status.
* **Infraction comments** (field 22):
	+ Enter any comments about the infraction.
* **Submitter details** (field 23):
	+ Enter your details as the person submitting the form.
* **Infraction reporting officer** (field 24):
	+ Select who reported the infraction (Police Officer, Traffic Officer, or Other).
* **Infraction reporting officer name** (field 25):
	+ Enter the name of the person who reported the infraction.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field's label or hint for guidance.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact our support team.
