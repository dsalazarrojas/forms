# Smart Meter Accuracy Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Meter Accuracy Report Form is used to collect data about smart meter inspections, ensuring that meters are accurately recording consumption data. This form helps identify potential errors or discrepancies in meter readings and ensures that meters are functioning correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the required information.
2. Fill in the Inspect Date field with the date of the inspection.
3. Enter the Meter ID of the smart meter being inspected.
4. Enter the Site ID where the meter is located.
5. Enter your name as the Inspector Name.
6. Enter the meter readings for Meter Reading 1, Meter Reading 2, and Meter Reading 3. These readings are used to verify the accuracy of the meter.
7. Enter any additional Notes about the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inspect Date**: Enter the date of the inspection in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Meter ID**: Enter the unique identifier of the smart meter being inspected.
* **Site ID**: Enter the location where the meter is installed (e.g., building number or address).
* **Inspector Name**: Enter your name as the person performing the inspection.
* **Meter Reading 1**, **Meter Reading 2**, **Meter Reading 3**: Enter the readings from the meter for these three specific points in time. These readings are used to verify the accuracy of the meter.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or observations about the inspection, such as any issues found or recommendations for maintenance.

## Tips
* Ensure that the meter readings entered are accurate and match the meter's recorded readings.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please consult with the relevant authorities or seek further clarification.
* This form is for reporting purposes, so please fill it out thoroughly and accurately.
