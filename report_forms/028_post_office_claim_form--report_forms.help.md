# post_office_claim_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect post office claims from customers. This includes lost or damaged parcels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the customer details section with the customer's name and contact information.
2. Select the type of claim (Lost Parcel or Damaged Parcel).
3. Provide a brief description of the issue with the parcel.
4. Enter the date and time the parcel was received.
5. Enter the signature of the person receiving the parcel.
6. Enter the customer's signature.
7. Enter the contact number and email address of the customer.
8. Provide additional delivery details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details**: Enter the customer's name, contact number, and email address. 
* **claim_type**: Select the type of claim (Lost Parcel or Damaged Parcel).
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of the issue with the parcel.
* **Date Received**: Enter the date the parcel was received.
* **Time Received**: Enter the time the parcel was received.
* **Signature**: Enter the signature of the person receiving the parcel.
* **Customer Signature**: Enter the customer's signature.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the customer's contact number.
* **Email Address**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **Delivery Details**: Provide additional delivery details.
* **Post Office Details**: Enter any post office details relevant to the claim. 

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate information to ensure that the claim is processed correctly.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact our customer service team.
