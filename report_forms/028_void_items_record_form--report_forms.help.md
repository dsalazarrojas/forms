# Void Items Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and document the voiding of items that have already been processed and are no longer valid or active.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill out the "Customer Name" field with the name of the customer who was originally associated with the item.
3. Enter the "Date Of Return" field with the date the item was initially scheduled to return to the customer.
4. In the "Item Description" field, provide a brief description of the item being voided.
5. In the "Reason For Void" field, explain the reason for voiding the item.
6. Enter the "Voided By" field with the name of the employee who is voiding the item.
7. Fill out the "Voided Date" field with the date the item was voided.
8. In the "Voided Time" field, enter the time the item was voided.
9. If the customer has a contact email, enter it in the "Customer Email" field.
10. If the customer has a phone number, fill out the "Customer Phone" field.
11. Enter the name of the tool assigned to the voided item in the "Assigned Tool" field.
12. Use the "Notes" field to add any additional information about the voided item.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the customer who was originally associated with the item.
* **Date Of Return**: Enter the date the item was initially scheduled to return to the customer.
* **Item Description**: Provide a brief description of the item being voided.
* **Reason For Void**: Explain the reason for voiding the item.
* **Voided By**: Enter the name of the employee who is voiding the item.
* **Voided Date**: Enter the date the item was voided.
* **Voided Time**: Enter the time the item was voided.
* **Customer Email**: If the customer has a contact email, enter it here.
* **Customer Phone**: If the customer has a phone number, enter it here.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the name of the tool assigned to the voided item.
* **Notes**: Add any additional information about the voided item.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have all the necessary information before completing this form to avoid duplication of efforts and ensure accurate records.
* If the reason for voiding is not listed in the provided options, please select "Other" and provide a clear explanation in the "Reason For Void" field.
