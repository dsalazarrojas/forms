# Blast Vibration Monitoring Log - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Blast Vibration Monitoring Log is used to collect data from blast vibration monitoring activities. This data will be used to track and analyze the effectiveness of blast monitoring systems and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the site name where the blast vibration monitoring activity took place.
2. Select the date of the monitoring activity.
3. Choose the time of the monitoring activity.
4. Enter the internal blast identifier.
5. Record the peak and average vibration reading in mm/s.
6. Provide the instrument's serial number (if available).
7. Enter any observer notes or comments.
8. Indicate whether further follow-up is required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Site name**: Location of the blasting activity.
* **Date**: Date of the monitoring activity.
* **Time**: Time of the monitoring activity.
* **Blast identifier**: Internal reference for the blast.
* **Vibration reading peak mm/s**: Record the peak vibration reading.
* **Vibration reading average mm/s**: Record the average vibration reading (if available).
* **Instrument serial number**: Monitor device serial number (if available).
* **Observer notes**: Enter any observations or comments made during the blast.
* **Follow up required**: Indicate if further investigation is needed.

## Tips
- Be accurate and consistent in filling out the form.
- Ensure that you have filled out the form for a new site and blast identifier.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant team for assistance.
