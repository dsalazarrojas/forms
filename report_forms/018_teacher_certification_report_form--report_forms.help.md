# Teacher Certification Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for teachers to report their certification information, including training details and certification status. It helps to track and document a teacher's certifications, which can be useful for administrative purposes or professional development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Teacher Name**: Enter the name of the teacher being reported on.
	* **Training Institution**: Provide the name of the institution where the teacher received the training.
2. Select the certification status:
	* Choose from the options: "Certified", "Not Certified", or "In Progress".
3. Enter the certification details:
	* **Certification Date**: Enter the date the teacher was certified.
	* **Training Hours**: Enter the total number of training hours completed.
	* **Certification Number**: Enter the certification number, if applicable.
	* **Certification Type**: Enter the type of certification (e.g., "Basic", "Advanced", etc.).
	* **Training Level**: Enter the level of training completed (e.g., "Basic", "Intermediate", etc.).
4. Add any additional comments or notes:
	* **Comments**: Provide any relevant comments or notes about the certification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher Information**: Enter the details of the teacher being reported on.
* **Course Name**: Enter the name of the course the teacher completed.
* **Course Start Date**: Enter the date the course started.
* **Course End Date**: Enter the date the course ended.
* **Certification Date**: Enter the date the teacher was certified.
* **Training Hours**: Enter the total number of training hours completed.
* **Training Hours Total**: This field is not required, but it may be used to track total training hours across multiple certifications.
* **Certification Status**: Choose from the options: "Certified", "Not Certified", or "In Progress".
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the certification.
* **Submitted By**: The field is automatically populated with the username of the user submitting the form.
* **Created By**: The field is automatically populated with the username of the user creating the form.
* **Updated By**: The field is automatically populated with the username of the user updating the form.
* **Created At**: The field is automatically populated with the date and time the form was created.
* **Updated At**: The field is automatically populated with the date and time the form was last updated.
* **Deleted At**: The field is automatically populated with the date the form was deleted.
* **Training Institution**: Enter the name of the institution where the teacher received the training.
* **Certification Institution**: Enter the name of the institution issuing the certification.
* **Certification ID**: Enter the certification ID, if applicable.
* **Certification Code**: Enter the certification code, if applicable.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional notes about the certification.
* **Certification Number**: Enter the certification number, if applicable.
* **Training Program**: Enter the name of the training program completed.
* **Certification Type**: Enter the type of certification (e.g., "Basic", "Advanced", etc.).
* **Training Level**: Enter the level of training completed (e.g., "Basic", "Intermediate", etc.).
