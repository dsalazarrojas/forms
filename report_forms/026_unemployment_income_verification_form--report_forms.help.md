# unemployment_income_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The unemployment income verification form is used to confirm an individual's employment status and income. It gathers information about an individual's employment history, current employment status, and other relevant details to verify their income.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Select the correct options for each field.
- Ensure all required fields are filled out.
- Review and correct any errors before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`): Select your current employment status from the provided options. This field is required as it helps determine your employment status at the time of verification.
* **Last Employer's Name** (`last_employer_name`): Enter the name of your last employer. This field is required as it helps identify your previous employment.
* **Last Employer's Address** (`last_employer_address`): Enter the address of your last employer. This field is required as it helps identify your previous employment location.
* **Last Employer's Phone** (`last_employer_phone`): Enter your last employer's phone number. This field is required as it helps verify your previous employment.
* **Last Employer's Start Date** (`last_employer_start_date`): Enter the start date of your last employment. This field is required as it helps determine the duration of your previous employment.
* **Last Employer's End Date** (`last_employer_end_date`): Enter the end date of your last employment. This field is required as it helps determine the duration of your previous employment.
* **Last Employer's Reason for Leaving** (`last_employer_reason_for_leaving`): Explain why you left your last employment. This field is required as it helps understand the circumstances surrounding your previous job separation.
* **Current Employer's Name** (`current_employer_name`): Enter the name of your current employer. This field is required as it helps identify your current employment.
* **Current Employer's Address** (`current_employer_address`): Enter the address of your current employer. This field is required as it helps identify your current employment location.
* **Current Employer's Phone** (`current_employer_phone`): Enter your current employer's phone number. This field is required as it helps verify your current employment.
* **Current Employer's Start Date** (`current_employer_start_date`): Enter the start date of your current employment. This field is required as it helps determine the duration of your current employment.
* **Current Employer's End Date** (`current_employer_end_date`): Enter the end date of your current employment. This field is optional as it helps determine the duration of your current employment.
* **Current Employer's Reason for Leaving** (`current_employer_reason_for_leaving`): Explain why you might leave your current employment. This field is required as it helps understand the circumstances surrounding your current employment status.
* **Income Proof** (`income_proof`): Select 'Yes' if you have proof of income. This field is required as it helps confirm your income status.
* **Employment Agency** (`employment_agency`): Select 'Yes' if you have an employment agency. This field is optional as it helps identify any employment agency involvement.
* **Other Income Proof** (`other_income_proof`): Select 'Yes' if you have proof of other income. This field is optional as it helps confirm additional income sources.
* **Current Employment Status** (`current_employment_status`): Select your current employment status from the provided options. This field is required as it helps determine your current employment status.
* **Active/Inactive** (`current_employment_status`): Select the correct status for your current employment.
