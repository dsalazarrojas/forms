# sexually_transmitted_infection_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sexually Transmitted Infection Report Form is a tool for healthcare professionals to collect information about a patient's STI diagnosis and treatment. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal identifiable information.
2. Select all applicable STI symptoms that the patient is experiencing.
3. Choose the correct diagnosis based on the patient's symptoms.
4. Enter any relevant test results.
5. Describe the treatment plan for the patient's STI.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Details**: Enter the patient's first and last name, date of birth, and contact information for your own records.
* **STI Symptoms** (`STI_symptoms`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the symptoms that the patient is experiencing related to STI. For example, 'Yes' for a symptom the patient is experiencing, and 'No' for one they are not.
* **Diagnosis** (`diagnosis`, select_one, required: false): Choose the correct diagnosis based on the patient's symptoms. This field should be filled out in accordance with your medical training and expertise.
* **Test Results** (`test_results`, text, required: false): Enter any relevant test results related to the patient's STI diagnosis.
* **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, text, required: false): Describe the treatment plan for the patient's STI, including any recommended medications, follow-up appointments, or other necessary information.

## Tips
* Make sure to obtain the patient's consent before filling out this form.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy, especially when selecting the diagnosis.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to your medical training or consult with a colleague.
* This form may be used for internal tracking and research purposes, but be aware of any local laws or regulations regarding patient data protection.
