# Emergency Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Claim Form is used to report incidents that occurred during work hours or work-related activities. This form helps the team to understand the details of the incident and respond promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by filling out page 1: "emergency_claim_form_page_1" (First Page). This section is a general introduction to the incident.
2.  Move on to page 2: "emergency_claim_form_page_2" (Contact Info). Here, you need to provide your contact information so that we can get in touch with you for further discussion or updates.
3.  On page 3: "emergency_claim_form_page_3" (Accident Details), enter a detailed description of what happened, including the location, date, and time of the incident.
4.  On page 4: "report_type" (Report Type), select the category that best describes the incident (injury, car accident, or other).
5.  On page 5: "claim_details" (Claim Details), describe the impact of the incident, including any medical expenses, time lost, or other relevant costs.
6.  On page 6: "contact_us" (Contact Us), provide any additional information about yourself or the incident, if needed.
7.  On page 7: "file_report" (File Report), attach any supporting documents, such as photos or videos, that help to illustrate the incident.
