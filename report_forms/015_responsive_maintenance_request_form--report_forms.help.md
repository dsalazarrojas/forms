# Responsive Maintenance Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for reporting maintenance requests, allowing you to provide detailed descriptions of maintenance issues, categorize them, and assign them to specific personnel. It also offers an option to sync the request with over 100 apps for easy tracking and management.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by selecting a category for your maintenance request from the dropdown menu:
	* Report Forms: For reporting general maintenance concerns.
	* Request Forms: For submitting specific requests for maintenance.
2. Enter a detailed description of the issue you are experiencing. Please be as descriptive as possible to help the assigned personnel understand the request.
3. If you have any further contact information, such as an email or phone number, you can provide it in the respective fields for easier communication.
4. Choose who you would like to assign the maintenance request to from the dropdown menu.
5. If you want to sync the request with more than 100 apps, please enter the relevant information in the "Sync with 100+ apps" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Maintenance Request Form** (`maintenance_request_form`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of the issue you are experiencing.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Select a category for your maintenance request. This will help the assigned personnel understand the type of issue you are reporting.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): A more detailed explanation of the issue.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide your email address for easier contact.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for quicker communication.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, optional): Choose who you would like to assign the maintenance request to. This will determine who receives the request.
* **Sync with 100+ apps** (`sync_apps`, text, optional): Enter the relevant information to sync the request with more than 100 apps.
