# Annual Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Annual Report Form is a comprehensive report that provides an overview of a company's or organization's financial and operational performance over the past year. This form helps leadership and stakeholders assess progress, identify areas of improvement, and make informed decisions for future growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Company or Organization Name
	* Reporting Year
	* Report Prepared By
	* Report Submission Date
	* Executive Summary
	* Key Highlights and Achievements
	* Total Annual Revenue
	* Net Income or Profit
	* Outlook for Next Year
	* Overall Financial Position
2. Provide optional fields if applicable:
	* Revenue Change from Previous Year
	* Total Annual Expenses
	* Major expense categories (Expenses Breakdown)
	* Total Number of Employees
	* Employee Growth Rate
	* Market Performance Assessment
	* Strategic Initiatives Completed
	* New Products or Services Launched
	* Geographic or Market Expansion
	* Strategic Partnerships or Acquisitions
	* Challenges and Obstacles Faced
	* Solutions and responses to challenges
	* Sustainability and Social Responsibility (ESG initiatives or community involvement)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company or Organization Name** (`company_name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the legal name of the company or organization.
* **Reporting Year** (`reporting_year`, `number`, **Required**): Enter the fiscal year being reported.
* **Report Prepared By** (`report_preparer`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the name and title of the person compiling the report.
* **Report Submission Date** (`report_date`, `date`, **Required**): Enter the date this report is being filed.
* **Executive Summary** (`executive_summary`, `text`, **Required**): Briefly summarize the company's or organization's performance and accomplishments over the past year.
* **Key Highlights and Achievements** (`key_highlights`, `text`, **Required**): List the major accomplishments and milestones achieved by the company or organization.
* **Total Annual Revenue** (`total_revenue`, `number`, **Required**): Enter the gross revenue for the year.
* **Revenue Change from Previous Year** (`revenue_change`, `number`, **Optional**): Enter the percentage change from the previous year.
* **Total Annual Expenses** (`total_expenses`, `number`, **Required**): Enter the sum of all expenses.
* **Net Income or Profit** (`net_income`, `number`, **Required**): Calculate revenue minus expenses.
* **Major expense categories** (`expenses_breakdown`, `text`, **Optional**): List the largest spending areas.
* **Total Number of Employees** (`employee_count`, `number`, **Optional**): Enter the headcount at year end.
* **Employee Growth Rate** (`employee_growth`, `number`, **Optional**): Enter the change in headcount from previous year.
* **Market Performance Assessment** (`market_performance`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select Improved, Maintained, Declined, or New market entry.
* **Strategic Initiatives Completed** (`strategic_initiatives`, `text`, **Required**): List the major projects or plans executed.
* **New Products or Services Launched** (`new_products`, `text`, **Optional**): List any new products or services added to the product line.
* **Geographic or Market Expansion** (`market_expansion`, `text`, **Optional**): List any new markets or regions entered.
* **Strategic Partnerships or Acquisitions** (`partnerships_formed`, `text`, **Optional**): List any new partnerships or business combinations.
* **Challenges and Obstacles Faced** (`challenges_faced`, `text`, **Optional**): List any difficulties encountered during the year.
* **Solutions and responses to challenges** (`challenges_resolved`, `text`, **Optional**): Describe how challenges were addressed.
* **Sustainability and Social Responsibility** (`sustainability_initiatives`, `text`, **Optional**): List any ESG initiatives or community involvement.
* **Outlook for Next Year** (`outlook_next_year`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the expectations and goals for the coming year.
* **Overall Financial Position** (`financial_position`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select Strong, Healthy, Adequate, Challenged, or At risk.
