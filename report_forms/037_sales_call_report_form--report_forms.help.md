# Sales Call Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Call Report Form is a tool used to document sales calls and their outcomes. It helps in collecting and analyzing data about customer interactions, customer types, and sales results.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the date of the sales call in the "Sales Call Date" field.
2. Enter the name and email of the customer in the respective fields.
3. Provide the customer's phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
4. If applicable, select the relevant option for "Sales Rep", indicating whether a sales representative was involved in the call or not.
5. If applicable, select the relevant option for "Customer Location", indicating whether the customer's location was visited or not.
6. Similarly, select the relevant option for "Customer Type" if it applies.
7. Describe the outcome of the sales call in the "Sales Outcome" field.
8. Enter the opportunity ID if it is applicable.
9. Review and submit the form once you have filled in all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sales Call Date** (sales_call_date, date, Optional): This field should be filled with the date on which the sales call took place.
* **Customer Name** (customer_name, text, Optional): Enter the name of the customer who was involved in the sales call.
* **Customer E-mail** (customer_email, text, Optional): Enter the email address of the customer.
* **Customer Phone** (customer_phone, text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the customer.
* **Sales Rep** (sales_rep, select_one, Optional): If a sales representative was involved in the call, select 'Yes' from the options. If not, select 'No'.
* **Customer Location** (customer_location, select_one, Optional): If the sales representative visited the customer's location, select 'Yes' from the options. If not, select 'No'.
* **Customer Type** (customer_type, select_one, Optional): If the customer is of a certain type, select the appropriate option.
* **Sales Outcome** (sales_outcome, text, Optional): Describe the outcome of the sales call, e.g. lead generated, opportunity created, etc.
* **Opportunity Id** (opportunity_id, text, Optional): If applicable, enter the opportunity ID related to the sales call.
* **Opportunity Type** (opportunity_type, text, Optional): This field is currently not used in this form but will be used in other forms. Please do not fill it out.

## Tips
* Ensure that all the fields are filled in correctly to ensure that the form is complete and accurate.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or colleague for guidance.
* The form should not be used for duplicate reporting, i.e., only report sales calls that have already taken place.
