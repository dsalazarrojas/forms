# Basketball Game Score Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and report the details of a basketball game, including scores, team information, and game-related events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter the date of the game using the date field.
2. Select the time of the game using the time field.
3. Enter the venue or location of the game using the game venue/location field.
4. Enter the names of the home and away teams.
5. Enter the names of the coaches for both teams.
6. Enter the final scores for both teams.
7. Enter the scores for each quarter (Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4) for both teams.
8. Identify the MVPs (Most Valuable Players) for both teams.
9. Enter any game highlights or notes.
10. Confirm the accuracy of the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Game date** (`game_date`): Enter the date of the game in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Game time** (`game_time`): Enter the time of the game.
* **Game venue/location** (`game_venue`): Enter the location of the game, including the stadium or arena name.
* **Home team name** (`home_team`): Enter the name of the home team.
* **Away team name** (`away_team`): Enter the name of the away team.
* **Home team coach** (`home_coach`): Enter the name of the home team coach.
* **Away team coach** (`away_coach`): Enter the name of the away team coach.
* **Home team final score** (`home_team_score`): Enter the final score of the home team.
* **Away team final score** (`away_team_score`): Enter the final score of the away team.
* **Home team Q1 score** (`q1_home`): Enter the score of the home team in the first quarter.
* **Away team Q1 score** (`q1_away`): Enter the score of the away team in the first quarter.
* **Home team Q2 score** (not included in the original YAML): Enter the score of the home team in the second quarter.
* **Away team Q2 score** (not included in the original YAML): Enter the score of the away team in the second quarter.
* **Home team Q3 score** (not included in the original YAML): Enter the score of the home team in the third quarter.
* **Away team Q3 score** (not included in the original YAML): Enter the score of the away team in the third quarter.
* **Home team Q4 score** (not included in the original YAML): Enter the score of the home team in the fourth quarter.
* **Away team Q4 score** (not included in the original YAML): Enter the score of the away team in the fourth quarter.
* **Home team MVP** (`home_mvp`): Enter the name of the home team's MVP.
* **Away team MVP** (`away_mvp`): Enter the name of the away team's MVP.
* **Leading scorer - Home team** (`leading_scorer_home`): Enter the name of the leading scorer for the home team.
* **Leading scorer - Away team** (`leading_scorer_away`): Enter the name of the leading scorer for the away team.
* **Game highlights or notes** (`highlights`): Enter any notable events or highlights from the game.
* **Attendance count** (`attendance`): Enter the attendance count for the game.
