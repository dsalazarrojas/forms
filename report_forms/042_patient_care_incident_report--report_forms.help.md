# Patient Care Incident Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Care Incident Report form is designed to provide a structured and detailed account of incidents that occur in the context of patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the date of the incident in the `incident_date` field.
2.  Enter the patient's name in the `patient_name` field.
3.  Provide a detailed description of the incident in the `incident_description` field.
4.  Select the category of the incident from the dropdown menu in the `category` field.
5.  Enter the location of the incident in the `incident_location` field.
6.  Select the type of incident from the dropdown menu in the `incident_type` field.
7.  Enter a detailed account of the incident's cause in the `incident_cause` field.
8.  Select the outcome of the incident from the dropdown menu in the `incident_outcome` field.
9.  Enter the name of the doctor who reported the incident in the `reporting_doctor` field.
10. Enter the name of the nurse who reported the incident in the `reporting_nurse` field.
11. Enter the name of the patient care team involved in the incident in the `patient_care_team` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Incident Date (`incident_date`):
*   A field to enter the date of the incident.
*   Enter the date in a format that is easily readable and understood, such as mm/dd/yyyy.

### 2. Patient Name (`patient_name`):
*   A field to enter the patient's name.
*   Enter the full name of the patient, including their first name, last name, and any middle name or title.

### 3. Incident Description (`incident_description`):
*   A field to provide a detailed description of the incident.
*   Enter a clear and concise account of what happened, including any relevant details.

### 4. Category (`category`):
*   A dropdown menu with options to select the category of the incident.
*   Select the most appropriate category from the options provided.

### 5. Incident Location (`incident_location`):
*   A field to enter the location of the incident.
*   Enter the location where the incident occurred, including any relevant details.

### 6. Incident Type (`incident_type`):
*   A dropdown menu with options to select the type of incident.
*   Select the most appropriate type from the options provided.

### 7. Incident Cause (`incident_cause`):
*   A field to enter a detailed account of the incident's cause.
*   Enter a clear and concise explanation of why the incident occurred.

### 8. Incident Outcome (`incident_outcome`):
*   A dropdown menu with options to select the outcome of the incident.
*   Select the most appropriate outcome from the options provided.

### 9. Reporting Doctor (`reporting_doctor`):
*   A field to enter the name of the doctor who reported the incident.
*   Enter the doctor's name as it appears on their identification badge or personnel file.

### 10. Reporting Nurse (`reporting_nurse`):
*   A field to enter the name of the nurse who reported the incident.
*   Enter the nurse's name as it appears on their identification badge or personnel file.

### 11. Patient Care Team (`patient_care_team`):
*   A field to enter the name of the patient care team involved in the incident.
*   Enter the names of the team members, including their titles or positions.

## Tips
*   Always complete this form as thoroughly and accurately as possible.
*   Ensure that all relevant information is captured and documented.
*   Consult with your supervisor or relevant authority if you are unsure about the form's purpose or how to complete it.
*   Review and verify the form before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
