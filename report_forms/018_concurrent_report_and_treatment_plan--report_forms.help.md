# Concurrent Report And Treatment Plan - Help Guide

## Purpose

The "Concurrent Report And Treatment Plan" form is a comprehensive tool used to document the assessment, treatment goals, and progress of a client in a clinical setting. It captures essential information from the clinician and client to ensure accurate and effective treatment planning.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, enter the client's name in the "Client name" field, located on page 1 of the form.
2.  Next, enter the date of the assessment in the "Date of assessment" field, also on page 1.
3.  Describe the presenting issues and symptoms of the client in the "Presenting issues and symptoms" field.
4.  If applicable, enter the diagnosis or clinical impression in the "Diagnosis or clinical impressions" field.
5.  Specify the client's treatment goals in the "Treatment goals" field.
6.  Outline the interventions and methods planned for the client in the "Interventions and methods planned" field.
7.  Indicate the frequency and duration of sessions in the "Frequency and duration of sessions" field.
8.  If necessary, detail any safety concerns or plans in the "Risk assessment and safety plan" field.
9.  Enter the name of the clinician completing the form in the "Clinician name" field.
10. Enter the follow-up and review date in the "Follow up and review date" field.
11. Finally, confirm the accuracy and completeness of the information entered before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client name**: Enter the full name of the client, following the format used in your institution or organization.
    *   Type: Text
    *   Required: Yes
*   **Date of assessment**: Enter the date on which the assessment was conducted.
    *   Type: Date
    *   Required: Yes
*   **Presenting issues and symptoms**: Describe the concerns and symptoms presented by the client.
    *   Type: Text
    *   Required: Yes
*   **Diagnosis or clinical impressions**: Enter the primary diagnosis or clinical impression if applicable.
    *   Type: Text
    *   Required: No
*   **Treatment goals**: Specify the client's short-term and long-term goals.
    *   Type: Text
    *   Required: Yes
*   **Interventions and methods planned**: Outline the planned interventions and methods for the client.
    *   Type: Text
    *   Required: Yes
*   **Frequency and duration of sessions**: Indicate the frequency and duration of sessions planned for the client.
    *   Type: Text
    *   Required: Yes
*   **Risk assessment and safety plan**: Describe any safety concerns or plans if necessary.
    *   Type: Text
    *   Required: No
*   **Clinician name**: Enter the name of the clinician completing the form.
    *   Type: Text
    *   Required: Yes
*   **Follow up and review date**: Enter the next review or reassessment date.
    *   Type: Date
    *   Required: No

## Tips

*   Ensure that the information entered is accurate and up-to-date.
*   Double-check the form for completeness before submission.
*   If you are unsure about any field or have questions, consult with a relevant expert or refer to the relevant guidelines and protocols.
