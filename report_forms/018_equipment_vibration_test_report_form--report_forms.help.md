# Equipment Vibration Test Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for recording the results of equipment vibration testing. It is used to collect information on the test location, date, frequency range, acceleration range, vibration type, amplitude range, test conditions, and any notes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the form with the test location, date, and any other relevant information.
2. Select the frequency range and acceleration range of the test.
3. Choose the type of vibration tested (e.g., sine, random, etc.).
4. Enter the amplitude range of the vibration.
5. Describe the test conditions, including any relevant environmental factors.
6. Add any additional notes on the test.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Test Location**: The location where the vibration test was performed. This could be a specific room or area within a building, or a specific piece of equipment.
* **Test Date**: The date on which the vibration test was performed.
* **Frequency Range**: The range of frequencies tested during the vibration test.
* **Acceleration Range**: The range of acceleration measured during the test.
* **Vibration Type**: The type of vibration tested (e.g., sine, random, etc.).
* **Amplitude Range**: The range of amplitude measured during the test.
* **Test Conditions**: Any environmental factors that may have affected the test, such as temperature, humidity, etc.
* **Notes**: Any additional information or comments about the test.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields (if any).
* Use clear and concise language when filling in the form.
* Double-check the accuracy of the data entered.
* Save a copy of the completed form for future reference.
