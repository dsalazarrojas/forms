# Marine Wildlife Sighting Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Marine Wildlife Sighting Report form is designed to collect information about marine wildlife sightings for research and conservation efforts. This form is for collecting personal experiences and observations of marine species.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Sighting Time field with the exact date and time of your encounter.
2. Enter the Sighting Location details in the Sighting Location field.
3. Choose the species you observed from the Species dropdown menu.
4. Enter the Species ID number for the observed species.
5. Fill in the Sighting Date field with the date of your encounter.
6. Enter the Sighting Coordinates, which can be the latitude and longitude of the location.
7. Answer if you are associated with a Conservation Group by selecting "Yes" or "No".
8. Enter the Conservation Group ID number for the conservation group you are associated with.
9. Enter your name in the Researcher Name field.
10. Provide your contact information in the Researcher Contact Info field.
11. Add any additional notes or comments in the Researcher Notes field.
12. If you are associated with a conservation group, enter their website URL in the Conservation Group URL field.
13. If you have a direct email address for the conservation group, enter it in the Conservation Group Email field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Report Form**: Enter a brief description of your sighting, if necessary.
* **Sighting Time**: Enter the exact date and time of your marine wildlife sighting.
* **Sighting Location**: Provide detailed location information where the sighting occurred.
* **Species**: Choose the species you observed from the dropdown menu.
* **Species ID**: Enter the species ID number for the observed species.
* **Sighting Date**: Enter the date of your marine wildlife sighting.
* **Sighting Coordinates**: Enter the latitude and longitude of the location where you observed the species.
* **Conservation Group**: Answer "Yes" if you are associated with a conservation group, or "No" if not.
* **Conservation Group ID**: Enter the conservation group ID number, if applicable.
* **Researcher Name**: Enter your name as the researcher who conducted the sighting.
* **Researcher Contact Info**: Provide your contact information.
* **Researcher Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your sighting.
* **Conservation Group URL**: Enter the website URL of the conservation group, if applicable.
* **Conservation Group Email**: Enter the direct email address of the conservation group, if applicable.

## Tips
* Be as accurate as possible when filling out the fields.
* Make sure to include all necessary information to ensure the conservation group receives your report correctly.
* If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to the researchers or conservation groups for clarification.
* Ensure the integrity and accuracy of the collected data by not filling out any field with incorrect information.
