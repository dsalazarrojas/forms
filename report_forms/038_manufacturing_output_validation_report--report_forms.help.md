# manufacturing_output_validation_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The manufacturing_output_validation_report form is used to collect information from the manufacturing process for output validation reports.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the batch details, including the batch ID and any other relevant information.
2. Enter the inspection results for each batch.
3. Enter the quantity produced for each batch.
4. Select the production team and quality team involved in the batch.
5. Enter the date and time of production.
6. Add any notes or observations about the batch.
7. Enter your email address and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Batch Details** (`batch_details`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the batch.
* **Batch ID** (`batch_id`, number, optional): Enter the ID of the batch.
* **Inspection Results** (`inspection_results`, select multiple, optional): Select the results of the inspection from the options provided.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity produced for the batch.
* **Production Team** (`production_team`, select one, optional): Select the team involved in producing the batch.
* **Quality Team** (`quality_team`, select one, optional): Select the team responsible for the quality of the batch.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of production.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of production.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any notes or observations about the batch.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and relevant information for each field.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any doubts or questions, contact your supervisor or support team.
