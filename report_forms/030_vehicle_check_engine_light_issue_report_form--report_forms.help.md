# Vehicle Check Engine Light Issue Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for individuals reporting issues related to their vehicle's check engine light coming on. Please use this form to provide detailed information about your vehicle and the problem you are experiencing with the check engine light.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the year of your vehicle in the "Vehicle Year" field.
2. Select the make of your vehicle from the list of options in the "Vehicle Make" field.
3. Provide the model of your vehicle in the "Vehicle Model" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Year (<name>)** (`text`, required): Enter the year of your vehicle using four digits (e.g., 2015). This will help us understand the age and model year of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make (<name>)** (`select_one`, required): Select the make of your vehicle from the list of options. If your vehicle's make is not listed, please choose "Other" and provide the correct make in the "Vehicle Model" field.
* **Vehicle Model (<name>)** (`text`, optional): If the make of your vehicle is listed, enter its model here. If the make is not listed, provide the correct model in this field.
