# Newsletter Details Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Newsletter Details Form is used to collect personal details for newsletter subscribers. This form gathers information such as name, contact, and preferences for the newsletter.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Newsletter Details Form" link to access the form.
2. Fill out the form with the requested information.
3. Review the form before submitting to ensure accuracy.
4. Submit the form to complete the subscription process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Newsletter Interest** (`newsletter_interest`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more interests from the list to help us tailor the content of the newsletter to your preferences.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD format.
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, `time`, required: false): Enter your time of birth in 24-hour format.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional information or notes about yourself.
* **Email Subscription** (`email_subscription`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how you would like to receive the newsletter - Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, or Option 4.
* **Newsletter Subscription Frequency** (`newsletter_subscription_frequency`, `select_one`, required: false): Select how often you want to receive the newsletter - Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
* **Newsletter Category** (`newsletter_category`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose a category that best describes the newsletter content you are interested in.
* **Newsletter Language** (`newsletter_language`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more languages in which you prefer to receive the newsletter content.
