# Fire Department Incident Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Department Incident Report is a standardized form used to document and report incidents reported to the Fire Department. This form ensures that all relevant information is captured accurately and efficiently, supporting prompt and effective response and investigation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant Call Type from the dropdown menu:
	* Fire: Reports of fires or fires that were contained or have been put out.
	* Rescue: Reports involving rescues or extrications.
	* Medical: Reports of medical emergencies or incidents.
	* Other: Reports that do not fit into the above categories.
2. Provide a brief description of the situation found, including any relevant details.
3. Enter the name of the officer in charge, or the person responsible for the incident response.
4. List any units involved in the incident response, including their role or unit type.
5. Enter the call number, which is a unique identifier for the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Call Type**: Select the most relevant category for the reported incident. This will help the Fire Department prioritize and allocate resources effectively.
* **Situation Found**: Describe the situation as found by responders, including any notable observations, findings, or hazards.
* **Officer In Charge**: Enter the name of the officer who led the incident response or is responsible for the investigation.
* **Units Involved**: List any units or teams that participated in the incident response, their roles or unit types, and any special equipment or resources used.
* **Call Number**: Enter a unique identifier for the incident, which helps track and reference the incident response.

## Tips
* Be as concise and accurate as possible when filling out this form to ensure that the Fire Department can quickly and effectively respond to the incident.
* If you are unsure about any information or need clarification, please contact the Fire Department directly for assistance.
* This form is intended for official incident reporting purposes only and is not to be used for personal or marketing purposes.
