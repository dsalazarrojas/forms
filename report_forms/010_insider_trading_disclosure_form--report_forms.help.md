# Insider Trading Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report trades or transactions made by insiders, such as executives, directors, or other individuals with a significant ownership stake in the company. This form ensures that all trades are disclosed in a timely and accurate manner.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the Company Name of the entity involved in the trade.
2. Enter the name of the Reporting Person, which is the individual making the trade.
3. Enter the Title of Security being traded.
4. Enter the Date of Transaction, which is the date the trade was made.
5. Enter the Date of Disclosure, which is the date the trade is being reported.
6. Enter the Security Symbol, which is a unique identifier for the security being traded.
7. Enter the Security Name, which is the name of the security being traded.
8. Enter the Number of Shares, which is the quantity of shares traded.
9. Enter the Price of Shares, which is the price at which the shares were traded.
10. Enter the Total Amount, which is the total value of the trade.
11. Select the Form Type, which is the type of form being submitted (e.g., Form 4, Form 3, etc.).
12. Enter the Filing Date, which is the date the form is being filed.
13. Enter the Reporting Person's Title.
14. Enter the Reporting Person's Name.
15. Enter the Reporting Person's Address.
16. Enter the Reporting Person's City.
17. Enter the Reporting Person's State.
18. Enter the Reporting Person's Zip.
19. Enter the Reason for Transaction, which is a brief description of why the trade was made.
20. Enter the Location, which is the location of the trade.
21. Enter the Date of Purchase, which is the date the shares were purchased.
22. Enter the Date of Sale, which is the date the shares were sold.
23. Enter a Description of the trade.
24. Select the Form Submit option to confirm that the form is complete and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the company involved in the trade.
* **Reporting Person** (`reporting_person`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the individual making the trade.
* **Title of Security** (`title_of_security`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the security being traded.
* **Date of Transaction** (`date_of_transaction`, `date`, required): Enter the date the trade was made.
* **Date of Disclosure** (`date_of_disclosure`, `date`, required): Enter the date the trade is being reported.
* **Security Symbol** (`security_symbol`, `text`, optional): Enter the unique identifier for the security being traded.
* **Security Name** (`security_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the security being traded.
* **Number of Shares** (`number_of_shares`, `number`, optional): Enter the quantity of shares traded.
* **Price of Shares** (`price_of_shares`, `number`, optional): Enter the price at which the shares were traded.
* **Total Amount** (`total_amount`, `number`, optional): Enter the total value of the trade.
* **Form Type** (`form_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the type of form being submitted (e.g., Form 4, Form 3, etc.).
* **Filing Date** (`filing_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the form is being filed.
* **Reporting Person Title** (`reporting_person_title`, `text`, optional): Enter the title of the reporting person.
* **Reporting Person Name** (`reporting_person_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the reporting person.
* **Reporting Person Address** (`reporting_person_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the address of the reporting person.
* **Reporting Person City** (`reporting_person_city`, `text`, optional): Enter the city of the reporting person.
* **Reporting Person State** (`reporting_person_state`, `text`, optional): Enter the state of the reporting person.
* **Reporting Person Zip** (`reporting_person_zip`, `text`, optional): Enter the zip code of the reporting person.
* **Reason for Transaction** (`reason_for_transaction`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of why the trade was made.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location of the trade.
* **Date of Purchase** (`date_of_purchase`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the shares were purchased.
* **Date of Sale** (`date_of_sale`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the shares were sold.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, optional): Enter a description of the trade.
* **Form Submit** (`form_submit`, `select_one`, optional): Select to confirm that the form is complete and accurate.
