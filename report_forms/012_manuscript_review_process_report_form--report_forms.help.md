# Manuscript Review Process Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manuscript Review Process Report Form is used to gather feedback from peer-reviewers on manuscripts submitted for publication.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form for each manuscript you have reviewed.
2. Enter your reviewer details, including your name, email, and phone number (optional).
3. Provide a rating for the manuscript, choosing from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
4. Select the status of the manuscript, choosing from Published, Accepted, Rejected, In Progress, or Withdrawn.
5. Indicate whether the manuscript is a resubmission or not.
6. Enter the date and time of manuscript submission (optional).
7. Select the status of the manuscript submission, choosing from Active or Inactive.
8. Enter any additional comments or observations about the manuscript.
9. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Manuscript Review Form**: Enter your comments, ratings, and suggestions for improvement for the manuscript you are reviewing.
* **Reviewer Details**: Enter your name, email, and phone number (optional). This information will be recorded for record-keeping purposes.
* **Manuscript Submission ID**: Enter the ID number of the manuscript you are reviewing.
* **Reviewer Comments**: Enter any additional comments or observations about the manuscript.
* **Reviewer Rating**: Provide a rating for the manuscript, choosing from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Manuscript Status**: Select the status of the manuscript, choosing from Published, Accepted, Rejected, In Progress, or Withdrawn.
* **Manuscript Type**: Indicate whether the manuscript is a resubmission or not.
* **Manuscript Version**: Enter the version number of the manuscript (optional).
* **Reviewer Name**: Enter your name as the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Email**: Enter your email address (optional).
* **Reviewer Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Submission Date Range**: Enter the date and time of manuscript submission (optional).
* **Manuscript Reviewer Notes**: Enter any additional comments or observations about the manuscript.
* **Reviewer Observations**: Enter any additional comments or observations about the manuscript.
* **Reviewer Recommendation**: Indicate whether you recommend the manuscript for publication, choosing from Yes or No.
* **Reviewer Recommendation Reason**: Enter the reason for your recommendation (optional).
* **Reviewer Signature**: Sign and date the form to indicate that you have completed the review.
* **Manuscript Status Date**: Enter the date of the manuscript status change (optional).
* **Submitted By**: Indicate whether the manuscript was submitted by you, choosing from Yes or No.
* **Manuscript Submitter**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the manuscript (optional).
* **Manuscript Submission Date**: Enter the date of manuscript submission (optional).
* **Manuscript Submission Time**: Enter the time of manuscript submission (optional).
* **Manuscript Submission Status**: Select the status of the manuscript submission, choosing from Active or Inactive.
* **Manuscript Submission Comments**: Enter any additional comments or observations about the manuscript.
