# Crime Scene Investigation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Crime Scene Investigation Form is a standardized form used by law enforcement agencies to gather and document information about crime scenes and investigations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the required information before filling out the form.
2. Start by filling out the **Crime Description** field, which should provide a detailed description of the crime scene and the incident.
3. Next, answer the **Crime Type** question by selecting the correct category from the provided options.
4. If applicable, provide the **Crime Location**, **Crime Time**, and **Crime Date** fields with the relevant information.
5. For each suspect and witness, provide a brief description, including their name, age, and address (if applicable).
6. Ensure to include the **Officer Name** and **Officer Department** fields with the correct information.
7. In the **Crime Scene Investigation Note** field, provide a detailed account of the investigation process and findings.
8. If applicable, upload images of the suspect and witness photos.
9. Finally, in the **Officer Additional Comments** field, provide any additional information relevant to the investigation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Crime Description
**Crime Description** (required): A detailed description of the crime scene and the incident.

*   **Crime Type** (`Crime Type`, `select_one`, `required`): A category of the crime.

    *   This field helps in categorizing the crime and aids in the investigation process.
*   **Crime Location** (`Crime Location`, `text`, `false`): The location of the crime scene.

    *   This field helps in identifying the crime scene and aids in the investigation process.
*   **Crime Time** (`Crime Time`, `time`, `false`): The time of the crime.

    *   This field helps in determining the timeline of the crime and aids in the investigation process.
*   **Crime Date** (`Crime Date`, `date`, `false`): The date of the crime.

    *   This field helps in establishing the timeline of the crime and aids in the investigation process.

## Tips
When filling out this form, ensure to provide accurate and detailed information. Avoid using jargon or technical terms that may be confusing to others. Double-check your responses for errors or inconsistencies. If you are unsure about any field, consult with a supervisor or colleague before proceeding.
