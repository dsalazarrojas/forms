# Surgical Pathology Report Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about surgical pathology samples for reporting and documentation purposes. It captures details about the specimen, fixation and staining procedures, and other relevant information for accurate and standardized reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Specimen Details section with a description of the specimen, including any notable features or characteristics.
2. Provide information about the Sample Size, including the size of the sample or tissue block.
3. Enter the Fiture Size, if applicable.
4. Specify the Tumor Location, describing where the tumor is located within the specimen.
5. Enter your diagnosis or conclusion about the specimen.
6. Choose the Fixation Method used, e.g., paraffin, glutaraldehyde, or other.
7. Select the Staining Method used, e.g., H&E, IHC, or other.
8. Describe the Tumor Characteristics, including any notable features or findings.
9. Enter the Date of Fixation, the date the specimen was fixed.
10. Enter the Time of Fixation, the time the specimen was fixed.
11. Enter the Date of Staining, the date the staining was performed.
12. Enter the Time of Staining, the time the staining was performed.
13. Describe the Tissue Block Size, if applicable.
14. Specify the Fixation Technique used, e.g., decalification, or other.
15. Enter the Fixation Agent used.
16. Select the Embedding Medium used, e.g., paraffin, resin, or other.
17. Enter the Section Thickness, if applicable.
18. Describe the Staining Procedure used, e.g., manual, automated, or other.
19. Enter the Specimen Condition, e.g., fresh, frozen, or other.
20. Add any additional Comments about the specimen or findings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Specimen Details** (Specimen Details, text, required): Enter a detailed description of the specimen, including any notable features or characteristics.
* **Sample Size** (Sample Size, text, optional): Enter the size of the sample or tissue block, e.g., 1 cm x 1 cm.
* **Fiture Size** (Fiture Size, text, optional): Enter the size of the fixed tissue, if applicable.
* **Tumor Location** (Tumor Location, text, optional): Describe where the tumor is located within the specimen.
* **Diagnosis** (Diagnosis, text, optional): Enter your conclusion or diagnosis about the specimen.
* **Fixation Method** (Fixation Method, text, optional): Select the method used to fix the specimen, e.g., paraffin, glutaraldehyde, or other.
* **Staining Method** (Staining Method, text, optional): Select the method used for staining, e.g., H&E, IHC, or other.
* **Tumor Characteristics** (Tumor Characteristics, text, optional): Describe any notable features or findings about the tumor.
* **Date of Fixation** (Date of Fixation, date, optional): Enter the date the specimen was fixed.
* **Time of Fixation** (Time of Fixation, time, optional): Enter the time the specimen was fixed.
* **Date of Staining** (Date of Staining, date, optional): Enter the date the staining was performed.
* **Time of Staining** (Time of Staining, time, optional): Enter the time the staining was performed.
* **Tissue Block Size** (Tissue Block Size, text, optional): Describe the size of the tissue block, if applicable.
* **Fixation Technique** (Fixation Technique, text, optional): Enter the technique used for fixation, e.g., decalification, or other.
* **Fixation Agent** (Fixation Agent, text, optional): Enter the agent used for fixation.
* **Embedding Medium** (Embedding Medium, text, optional): Select the medium used for embedding, e.g., paraffin, resin, or other.
* **Section Thickness** (Section Thickness, text, optional): Enter the thickness of the section, if applicable.
* **Staining Procedure** (Staining Procedure, text, optional): Describe the procedure used for staining, e.g., manual, automated, or other.
* **Specimen Condition** (Specimen Condition, text, optional): Enter the condition of the specimen, e.g., fresh, frozen, or other.
* **Comments** (Comments, text, optional): Add any additional comments or remarks about the specimen or findings.
