# Distribution Timeliness Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for tracking and reporting on the timeliness of distributions. It captures relevant details about on-time delivery performance, reasons for delays, distribution schedules, and other essential information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Enter the `On Time Delivery Performance` field, which measures the percentage of on-time deliveries. This field is represented as a number and can be filled in manually. 
2. In the `Delay Reasons` field, describe the reasons for any delays that occurred during the distribution process. This field is for free text and can be used to provide detailed explanations for the delays.
3. Enter the `Distribution Date` field to record the date when the distribution took place. This field is a calendar date field for inputting dates.
4. Input the `Distribution Time` field to record the time of the distribution. This field is for inputting time values.
5. In the `Delivery Location` field, specify the location where the delivery occurred. This field is for free text and can be used to provide detailed information about the location.
6. Enter the `Reporting Period` field to describe the time frame over which the distribution took place. This field is for free text and can be used to provide context for the report.
7. Use the `Notes` field to add any additional comments or observations that may be relevant to the distribution. This field is for free text and can be used to capture extra information.
8. Finally, enter your `Email` address, which will be used for contact purposes in case the form is submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **On Time Delivery Performance** (`Number`, required: false): A measure of the percentage of on-time deliveries. This field represents a number that indicates the rate of successful deliveries.
* **Delay Reasons** (`Text`, required: false): An optional field for providing reasons for any delays that occurred during the distribution process.
* **Distribution Date** (`Date`, required: false): A field for recording the date when the distribution took place.
* **Distribution Time** (`Time`, required: false): A field for recording the time of the distribution.
* **Delivery Location** (`Text`, required: false): A field for specifying the location where the delivery occurred.
* **Reporting Period** (`Text`, required: false): A field for describing the time frame over which the distribution took place.
* **Notes** (`Text`, required: false): A field for adding comments or observations that may be relevant to the distribution.
* **Email** (`Email`, required: false): A field for entering your contact email address in case the form is submitted.
