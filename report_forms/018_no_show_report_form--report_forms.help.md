## No Show Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The No Show Report Form is used to report instances of a person not showing up to an event. It is used to capture relevant information about the event and the person who was scheduled to attend.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Click on the "Submit" button to begin the form.
* Fill out each field as prompted. Each field is described below.
* Once you have completed all the fields, click "Submit" to submit your report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name** (event_name): Enter the name of the event that the person was scheduled to attend.
* **Attendee Name** (attendee_name): Enter the name of the person who was scheduled to attend the event.
* **Attendee Email** (attendee_email): Enter the email address of the person who was scheduled to attend the event.
* **Attendee Phone** (attendee_phone): Enter the phone number of the person who was scheduled to attend the event.
* **Event Date** (event_date): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (event_time): Enter the time of the event.
* **Scheduled Date** (scheduled_date): Enter the date the event was originally scheduled.
* **Scheduled Time** (scheduled_time): Enter the time the event was originally scheduled.
* **No Show Status** (no_show_status): Select all of the statuses that apply to this no show. This will help us understand why this person did not show up.
* **Reason for No Show** (reason): Select the reason why the person did not show up. This will help us understand the cause of the no show.
* **Notes** (notes): Add any additional comments or details that might be helpful in understanding the no show.
* **No Show Report** (no_show_report): Enter any additional information that might be helpful in understanding the no show.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you are using this form to report genuine no shows and not to abuse the system by reporting false or fabricated no shows.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the event organizer or your manager for clarification.
* Please be as detailed as possible when filling out the form, as it will help us understand the cause of the no show and prevent future occurrences.
