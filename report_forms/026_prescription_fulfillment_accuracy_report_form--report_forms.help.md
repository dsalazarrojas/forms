# Prescription Fulfillment Accuracy Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prescription Fulfillment Accuracy Report Form is used to report any discrepancies or issues with prescription fulfillment, ensuring that any problems are identified and addressed promptly. This form is intended to help pharmacists and staff detect errors and improve the accuracy of prescription fulfillment processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the details of the issue or discrepancy you have encountered in the **Prescription Fulfillment Accuracy Report** field.
2. Select a **Report Date** and choose the date when you first noticed the issue.
3. Select a **Report Time** and choose the time when you first noticed the issue.
4. Enter the name or identifier of the person who reported the issue in the **Reported By** field.
5. Assess the accuracy of the prescription fulfillment by entering a **Prescription Fulfillment Accuracy** score (e.g., 0-100).
6. Document any **Improvement Actions** taken to address the issue, such as changes to workflow or communication with staff.
7. Describe any **Corrective Actions** taken to resolve the issue, such as additional training or process changes.
8. Enter any additional comments or information about the issue in the **Additional Comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Prescription Fulfillment Accuracy Report** (text): This field is for entering details about the issue or discrepancy you have encountered. Please be as specific as possible so that the issue can be quickly identified and addressed.
* **Report Date** (date): Enter the date when you first noticed the issue. This helps track the timing and progression of the problem.
* **Report Time** (time): Enter the time when you first noticed the issue. This helps identify any time-sensitive problems.
* **Reported By** (text): Enter the name or identifier of the person who reported the issue. This helps track who identified the problem and ensures that the correct person is informed of any actions taken.
* **Prescription Fulfillment Accuracy** (number): Assess the accuracy of the prescription fulfillment by entering a score between 0-100. A higher score indicates better accuracy.
* **Improvement Actions** (text): Describe any changes or improvements you have made to the prescription fulfillment process in response to the issue.
* **Corrective Actions** (text): Describe any actions taken to resolve the issue, such as additional training or process changes.
* **Additional Comments** (text): Enter any additional comments or information about the issue that may help identify the root cause or suggest further actions.
