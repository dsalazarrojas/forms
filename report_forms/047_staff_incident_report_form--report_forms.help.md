# Staff Incident Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for documenting and tracking incidents in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2.  Enter your name and your role or department for accurate tracking.
3.  Fill out each section in order, from top to bottom.
4.  If a field is not relevant to the incident, please enter N/A (Not Applicable) in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, `date`, required: false): Please enter the date when the incident occurred.
* **Reported By** (`reported_by`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name, role, and/or department that reported the incident.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the exact location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select all relevant categories that best describe the incident (Safety, Security, Health, Other).
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, `text`, required: false): Please provide a detailed description of what happened.
* **Action Taken** (`action_taken`, `text`, required: false): Please describe any actions taken to address the incident.
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select the status of the incident (Open, Closed, Pending).
*   Please note that these statuses are intended to track the incident's resolution and are usually updated by the relevant authorities.
* **Incident Category** (`incident_category`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select all relevant categories that best describe the incident (Safety, Security, Health, Other).
*   Please note that these categories are intended to track the incident's nature and are usually updated by the relevant authorities.
*   Please do not select categories that are not applicable.
* **Reported To** (`reported_to`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the name of the person or department that the incident was reported to.
* **Incident Outcome** (`incident_outcome`, `text`, required: false): Please enter a detailed description of the outcome of the incident.
*   Please note that this field is intended for recording any follow-up or resolution information.
* **Witness Statement** (`witness_statement`, `text`, required: false): Please enter a witness's statement, if available.
* **Investigation Notes** (`investigation_notes`, `text`, required: false): Please enter any investigation notes or details that may be useful for the investigation process.
* **Incident ID** (`incident_id`, `number`, required: false): This field is used for internal tracking purposes only. Please do not alter it, as it is automatically generated.

## Tips

*   Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
*   Do not hesitate to ask for help if you need assistance or clarification.
*   Please report all incidents, no matter how minor they may seem.
