# Travel Claim Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Travel Claim Form is used to report travel expenses for business or vacation trips.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct type of travel (Business or Vacation).
2. Enter the start and end dates of your trip.
3. Enter the destination of your trip.
4. Choose the mode of transportation used (Air, Train, Bus).
5. If applicable, select the modes of transportation used.
6. Enter the total distance and hours traveled.
7. Enter the total number of days and nights.
8. Select all expenses related to your trip, including Airfare, Hotel, Meals, and Other.
9. Click Submit to complete and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header** (Note): This section serves as a title for the form.
* **What type of travel was taken** (Select One, required): Choose whether your travel was for Business or Vacation.
* **Date** (Date, required): Enter the start date of your trip.
* **Start Date** (Date, required): Enter the start date of your trip.
* **End Date** (Date, required): Enter the end date of your trip.
* **Destination** (Text, required): Enter the destination of your trip.
* **Mode of Transportation** (Select Multiple, required): Choose all modes of transportation used during your trip (Air, Train, Bus).
* **Distance** (Number, required): Enter the total distance traveled.
* **Hours** (Number, required): Enter the total hours of travel.
* **Days** (Number, required): Enter the total number of days of travel.
* **Nights** (Number, required): Enter the total number of nights of travel.
* **Travel Expenses** (Select Multiple, required): Select all expenses related to your trip (Airfare, Hotel, Meals, Other).
* **Submit** (Select One, required): Choose to submit your form or cancel.
