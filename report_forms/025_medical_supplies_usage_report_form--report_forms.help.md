# Medical Supplies Usage Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Medical Supplies Usage Report Form is a tool used to report the usage of medical supplies across various locations. It helps track and monitor the quantity of medical supplies used, quantity still stocked, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with the relevant information.
2. Ensure that you submit only one form per location, per stock ID.
3. Double-check your information before submitting to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Supplies Stock ID (<stock_id>)**: Enter the unique ID assigned to the medical supplies stock.
* **Medical Supplies Product ID (<product_id>)**: Enter the ID of the medical supply product being used.
* **Quantity Used (<quantity_used>)**: Enter the quantity of the medical supply used during the reporting period.
* **Quantity Still Stocked (<quantity_still_stocked>)**: Enter the quantity of the medical supply still stocked after usage.
* **Truck Number (<truck_number>)**: Enter the number of the truck used for transportation.
* **Submitter Name (<submitter_name>)**: Enter your name as the submitter.
* **Submitter Email (<submitter_email>)**: Enter your email address as a point of contact.
* **Submitter Phone (<submitter_phone>)**: Enter your phone number as a point of contact.
* **Submitter Comments (<submitter_comments>)**: Enter any additional comments or information about the report.
* **Submitter Department (<submitter_department>)**: Select the department you belong to from the options provided. 

Please note that some fields are optional and can be left blank if not applicable.
