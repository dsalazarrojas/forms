# Factory Cleanliness Audit Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Factory Cleanliness Audit Report form is used to gather information and feedback on the cleanliness of the factory and its facilities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring that all required fields are completed.
2. Select the correct cleanliness rating for the areas inspected.
3. Enter the date and time of the audit.
4. Specify the personnel who inspected the factory, including EHS, Quality, and Operations personnel.
5. Add any comments or observations about the cleanliness of the factory.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Factory Cleanliness Audit Report Form** (`factory_cleanliness_audit_form`, text, not required): This is the form header and title.
* **Cleanliness Rating** (`rating`, number, not required): Select one of the provided options (5, 10, 20) to indicate the cleanliness level of the factory.
* **Clean Areas** (`clean_areas`, select_multiple, not required): Choose the areas of the factory that were inspected, from the options 5, 10, or 20.
* **Areas Inspected** (`areas_inspected`, text, not required): Describe the areas of the factory that were inspected during the audit.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, not required): Add any comments or observations about the cleanliness of the factory.
* **Date** (`date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the audit.
* **Time** (`time`, time, not required): Enter the time of the audit.
* **EHS Person Inspected** (`ehs_person_inspected`, text, not required): Specify the EHS personnel who conducted the audit.
* **Quality Person Inspected** (`quality_person_inspected`, text, not required): Specify the Quality Assurance personnel who conducted the audit.
* **Operations Person Inspected** (`operations_person_inspected`, text, not required): Specify the Operations personnel who conducted the audit.

# Tips
* Ensure that you complete the form accurately and honestly.
* Review all fields carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant personnel.
* Repeat this form for each audit or inspection to track cleanliness levels over time.
