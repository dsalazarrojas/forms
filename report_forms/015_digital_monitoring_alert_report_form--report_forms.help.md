# Digital Monitoring Alert Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Monitoring Alert Report Form is used to report and track incidents or issues that have occurred in a digital monitoring system.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, paying attention to the required fields.
2. Be honest and thorough in your reporting, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Review and edit your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Details**: This field is used to provide a brief description of the incident or issue.
* **Time**: Record the date and time that the incident occurred or was discovered.
* **Source**: Enter the source of the incident, such as a specific IP address or URL.
* **Summary**: Provide a concise summary of the incident or issue.
* **Priority**: Select the priority level of the incident, which will help the team determine its importance and urgency.
* **Evidence**: Attach any relevant evidence or documentation related to the incident.
* **Responsible Team**: Identify the team responsible for resolving the incident.
* **Status**: Select the status of the incident, such as Open, Closed, or Pending.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or information about the incident.
* **Date**: Record the date that the incident occurred or was discovered.
* **Time**: Record the time that the incident occurred or was discovered.
* **Description**: Provide a detailed description of the incident or issue.
* **Category**: Select the category of the incident, such as System, Network, or Application.
* **Reporter**: Enter your name and contact information.
* **Escalation**: Check if the incident requires escalation or follow-up actions.
* **Frequency**: Select whether the incident is a one-time issue or recurring.
* **Impact**: Select the impact level of the incident, which will help the team determine its severity.
* **Severity**: Select the severity level of the incident, which will help the team determine its priority.
* **Status Escalation**: Check if the incident status has been escalated.
* **Report Status**: Select the status of the report, such as Draft, Submitted, or Approved.
* **Escalation Count**: Enter the number of times the incident has been escalated.
* **Escalation Time**: Record the time that the incident was escalated.
* **Escalation Status**: Select the status of the escalation, such as Open or Closed.
* **Responsible User**: Enter the name and contact information of the user responsible for resolving the incident.
* **Comment**: Add any additional comments or information about the incident.
