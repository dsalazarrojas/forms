# Kosher Kitchen Inspection Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kosher Kitchen Inspection Report is a form used to document the findings and outcomes of a kosher kitchen inspection. It is completed by a kitchen inspector and contains information about the inspection, including the inspector's details, the kitchen's condition, and the results of the inspection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name and title as the inspector.
2. Enter the location of the kitchen being inspected.
3. Record the date of the inspection.
4. Indicate whether the kitchen has a valid kosher certificate.
5. Describe any critical findings or issues discovered during the inspection.
6. Document any corrective actions taken or recommended.
7. Provide an assessment of the kitchen's cleanliness.
8. Indicate whether the kitchen is in compliance with kosher regulations.
9. Add any comments or additional information about the inspection.
10. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspector Name (text)**: Enter your name and title as the inspector.
* **Kitchen Location (text)**: Enter the location of the kitchen being inspected.
* **Date of Inspection (date)**: Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Kosher Certificate (select one)**: Choose 'Kosher' if the kitchen has a valid kosher certificate, 'Non-Kosher' otherwise.
* **Critical Findings (text)**: Describe any critical issues or findings discovered during the inspection.
* **Corrective Actions Taken (text)**: Document any corrective actions taken or recommended.
* **Cleanliness Outcomes (text)**: Assess the kitchen's cleanliness.
* **Kosher Compliance (select one)**: Indicate whether the kitchen is in compliance with kosher regulations.
* **Comments (text)**: Add any additional comments or information about the inspection.
* **Signature (text)**: Sign and date the form to confirm completion.
* **Kitchen ID (number)**: Enter the kitchen's ID number.
* **Inspector ID (number)**: Enter your ID number.
* **Form Submitted At (date)**: This field is automatically populated and cannot be edited.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Use clear and concise language when describing critical findings and corrective actions.
- Ensure that the kitchen's cleanliness outcomes are accurate and thorough.
- Sign and date the form to confirm completion.
