# technology_issue_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users report technology-related issues, such as bugs, defects, or other technical problems. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your issue is reported effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Issue Description**: Briefly describe the issue you are experiencing, including any error messages or symptoms.
2. **Issue Category**: Select one or more categories related to the issue. This will help the support team understand the type of issue and allocate resources accordingly.
3. **Issue Priority**: Indicate the priority of the issue, which will determine the response time and urgency.
4. **Issue Type**: Identify the type of technology involved (hardware or software).
5. **Affected Department**: Specify the department or team affected by the issue.
6. **Issue Date**: Enter the date when the issue occurred or was first noticed.
7. **Start Time** and **End Time**: Enter the start and end times of the issue, if applicable.
8. **User Input**: Add any additional comments or details about the issue.
9. **Attachment 1** and **Attachment 2**: Add any attachments, such as screenshots or error logs, that will help support the issue report.
10. **User Name**: Enter your name as the person reporting the issue.
11. **User E-mail**: Enter your email address for follow-up communication.
12. **Issue Status**: Update the status of the issue as Open, Closed, or In Progress.
