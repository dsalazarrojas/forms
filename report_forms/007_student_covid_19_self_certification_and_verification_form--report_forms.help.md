# Student COVID 19 Self Certification And Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a student's current health status and travel history, ensuring that the student is safe and healthy to participate in classes.
## How To Complete This Form
This form has multiple pages, each with different fields. Please read each section carefully and fill out the required fields accordingly.
- Page 1: This page requires information about the student's name, parent name, student ID number, and symptoms.
- Date: Please fill out the current date in the date field.
- Student Name: Enter the student's full name.
- Parent Name: Enter the parent's full name.
- Student ID Number: Enter the student's ID number.
- COVID-19 Symptoms: Select all the symptoms the student is experiencing.
- Temperature: Enter the student's current temperature.
- Student Travel: Indicate if the student has traveled in the past week.
- Page 2: This page requires information about the teacher's status and travel history.
- Teacher Name: Enter the teacher's full name.
- Teacher Status: Enter the teacher's current status.
- Teacher Travel: Indicate if the teacher has traveled in the past week.
- Teacher Travel History: Enter the teacher's travel history.
- Last Contact with COVID-19: Enter the date of the last contact with COVID-19.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Student's full name.
* **Parent Name**: Parent's full name.
* **Student ID Number**: Student's ID number.
* **Date**: Date of submission.
* **COVID-19 Symptoms**: Select all symptoms the student is experiencing.
* **Temperature**: Student's current temperature.
* **Student Travel**: Indicate if the student has traveled in the past week.
* **Student Travel History**: Date of travel history.
* **Teacher Name**: Teacher's full name.
* **Teacher Status**: Teacher's current status.
* **Teacher Travel**: Indicate if the teacher has traveled in the past week.
* **Teacher Travel History**: Date of travel history.
* **Last Contact with COVID-19**: Date of last contact with COVID-19.
## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
- This form should not be filled out more than once a week.
- If you are unsure about any of the information, please consult with your school administration or healthcare provider.
