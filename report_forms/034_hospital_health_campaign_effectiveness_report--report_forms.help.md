# Hospital Health Campaign Effectiveness Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Health Campaign Effectiveness Report form is designed to collect data on the outcomes of health campaigns conducted by hospitals. This information will help us evaluate the success and areas of improvement for these campaigns.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Ensure that you have accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Make sure to submit the form only once from the same hospital.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hospital Campaign Outcomes** (`hospital_campaign_outcomes`, text, required): Enter a brief summary of the campaign's outcome.
* **Campaign Improvement Ideas** (`campaign_improvement_ideas`, text, required): Suggest ideas for future improvement of the campaign.
* **Submission Dates** (`submission_dates`, select multiple, optional): Check 'Yes' if the form was submitted on the correct dates.
* **Notes and Comments** (`notes_and_comments`, note, required): Add any additional comments or notes about the campaign.
* **Campaign Name** (`campaign_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the campaign.
* **Campaign Start Date** (`campaign_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the campaign.
* **Campaign End Date** (`campaign_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the campaign.
* **Total Outreaches** (`total_outreaches`, number, required): Enter the total number of outreaches.
* **Total Contacts** (`total_contacts`, number, required): Enter the total number of contacts.
* **Campaign Status** (`campaign_status`, select one, required): Choose the status of the campaign (Active or Inactive).
* **Campaign Target** (`campaign_target`, text, required): Enter the target of the campaign.
* **Campaign Goal** (`campaign_goal`, text, required): Enter the goal of the campaign.
* **Hospital Name** (`hospital_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the hospital.
* **Hospital ID** (`hospital_id`, text, required): Enter the ID of the hospital.
* **Submitted by** (`submitted_by`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, required): Enter the date of submission.
* **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`, time, required): Enter the time of submission.
* **Hospital Address** (`hospital_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the hospital.
* **Hospital Phone** (`hospital_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the hospital.
* **Hospital Email** (`hospital_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the hospital.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Campaign Location** (`campaign_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the campaign.
* **Campaign Location Notes** (`campaign_location_notes`, text, required): Enter any notes about the location.
* **Hospital Map** (`hospital_map`, text, required): Enter a map of the hospital location.
* **Follow-up Notes** (`follow_up_notes`, note, required): Add any follow-up comments or notes.

Note: This form is only to be filled out by hospital staff members who have direct involvement with the campaign.
