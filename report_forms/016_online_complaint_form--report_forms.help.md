# Online Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to report any issues or concerns they have regarding our products or services. It will help us gather information about your complaint and assist us in addressing and resolving the issue as soon as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of complaint you are experiencing from the "Complaint Type" dropdown menu.
2. Provide a description of your issue in the text box labeled "Description".
3. Enter the category of the product related to your complaint from the "Product Category" dropdown menu.
4. Enter the date you experienced the issue.
5. Enter your name, email, phone number, and address.
6. Write a message describing your complaint.
7. Submit your complaint.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Complaint Type** (`id: 1`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Select the type of complaint you are experiencing. You can choose from the following options: "Complaint Type:" or "Other".
* **Description** (`id: 2`, `type: text`, required: false): This is a text box where you can provide more details about your complaint.
* **Product Category** (`id: 3`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Select the category of the product related to your complaint from the dropdown menu. You can choose from the following options: "Product Category:" or "Other".
* **Date** (`id: 4`, `type: date`, required: false): Enter the date you experienced the issue.
* **Customer Name** (`id: 5`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`id: 6`, `type: email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`id: 7`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`id: 8`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter your address.
* **Message** (`id: 9`, `type: note`, required: false): This is a text box where you can provide a more detailed message about your complaint.
* **Submit** (`id: 10`, `type: text`, required: false): Click this button to submit your complaint.
* **Cancel** (`id: 11`, `type: text`, required: false): Click this button to cancel your complaint.
* **Error Message** (`id: 12`, `type: text`, required: false): This field is not editable and will display any error messages that may occur while submitting your complaint.
* **Customer Email** (`id: 13`, `type: email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the customer.
* **Customer Phone** (`id: 14`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the customer.
* **Customer Address** (`id: 15`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the address of the customer.
* **Customer Message** (`id: 16`, `type: text`, required: false): Provide a message from the customer.
* **Name** (`id: 17`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the name of the customer.
* **Customer Description** (`id: 18`, `type: text`, required: false): Provide a description of the customer.
* **Customer Complaint Type** (`id: 19`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Select the type of complaint from the dropdown menu. You can choose from the following options: "Complaint Type:" or "Other".
* **Customer Product Type** (`id: 20`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Select the type of product related to the complaint from the dropdown menu. You can choose from the following options: "Product Category:" or "Other".
* **Customer Date** (`id: 21`, `type: date`, required: false): Enter the date of the complaint.
* **Customer Error Message** (`id: 22`, `type: text`, required: false): Display any error messages that may occur while submitting the complaint.
* **Product Id** (`id: 23`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the ID of the product related to the complaint.
* **Order Id** (`id: 24`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the ID of the order related to the complaint.
* **Complaint Message** (`id: 25`, `type: text`, required: false): Provide a message describing your complaint.

Please note that this form is not required for all customers. You can submit your complaint without filling out any fields if you do not have any issues.
