# Noise Exposure Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Noise Exposure Monitoring Form is designed to track noise exposure of employees during their work periods. It is crucial to fill out this form accurately to ensure accurate records and proper safety measures.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form for each shift you work. This will help track your noise exposure over time.
2.  Make sure to fill out all required fields. Required fields are marked with a star (*) in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   *Employee Name*: Please enter your name as it appears on your employee ID.
*   *Start Time*: Enter the start time of your work period (e.g., 08:00).
*   *End Time*: Enter the end time of your work period (e.g., 16:00).
*   *Work Location*: Enter the specific location where you performed your work duties (e.g., warehouse, office, etc.).
*   *Noise Level*: If possible, enter the measured noise level of your work environment (e.g., 80 decibels).
*   *Personal Protective Equipment*: If you wore any personal protective equipment (PPE) during work, please list the type(s) (e.g., earplugs, earmuffs, etc.).
*   *Hearing Protection*: Check if you used hearing protection during work. Options are: "Hearing protection used" or "No hearing protection used."
*   *Monitoring Area*: Please enter the specific area where you were monitored for noise exposure (e.g., machine shop, production floor, etc.).
*   *Observer Name*: Please enter the name of the person who observed or monitored your noise exposure (e.g., supervisor, safety officer, etc.).
