# Process Downtime Log Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Process Downtime Log Report Form is used to record and review machine downtimes, ensuring that all relevant information is documented for analysis and future reference.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your Machine ID number
2.  Enter the Date of the incident
3.  Enter the time the machine was turned off (Time In)
4.  Enter the time the machine was turned back on (Time Out)
5.  Select the type of machine affected
6.  Describe the status of the machine (Active or Inactive)
7.  Add any relevant comments
8.  Indicate whether the issue was assigned to someone
9.  Get the supervisor and employee signatures
10.  Enter the date the form was received
11.  Enter the date the form was reviewed
12.  Indicate whether the form was reviewed by someone
13.  Add any supervisor comments
14.  Add any employee comments
15.  Add any manager comments
16.  Indicate the current status of the machine (Active or Inactive)
17.  Indicate any action taken on the machine
18.  Indicate whether the form was completed by someone
19.  Indicate whether the form was submitted
20.  Add any additional comments

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Machine ID** (`machine_id`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to identify the machine affected by the downtime. Enter the machine's unique ID number.
*   **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the downtime incident. This field helps in tracking the timeline of events.
*   **Time In** (`time_in`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time the machine was turned off. This helps in calculating the duration of downtime.
*   **Time Out** (`time_out`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time the machine was turned back on. This helps in determining the duration of downtime.
*   **Machine Type** (`machine_type`, `text`, required: false): Describe the type of machine affected by the downtime. This helps in identifying common issues with specific machine types.
*   **Machine Status** (`machine_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the current status of the machine (Active or Inactive) after the incident.
*   **Reason** (`reason`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the reason for the downtime. This helps in understanding the root cause of the issue.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about the incident. This helps in keeping a record of the discussion or action taken.
*   **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate whether the issue was assigned to someone for resolution. This helps in tracking responsibility and deadlines.
*   **Supervisor Signature** (`supervisor_signature`, `text`, required: false): Get the supervisor's signature to confirm review and approval.
*   **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, `text`, required: false): Get the employee's signature to confirm their involvement in the resolution.
*   **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, `text`, required: false): Get the manager's signature to confirm review and approval.
*   **Date Received** (`date_received`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the form was received by the supervisor or team lead.
*   **Date Reviewed** (`date_reviewed`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the form was reviewed by the supervisor or team lead.
*   **Reviewed By** (`reviewed_by`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate whether the form was reviewed by someone.
*   **Supervisor Comments** (`supervisor_comments`, `text`, required: false): Add any supervisor comments or notes about the incident.
*   **Employee Comments** (`employee_comments`, `text`, required: false): Add any employee comments or notes about the incident.
*   **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, `text`, required: false): Add any manager comments or notes about the incident.
*   **Status** (`status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the current status of the machine (Active or Inactive) after resolution.
*   **Action** (`action`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate any action taken on the machine after the incident.
*   **Completed By** (`completed_by`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate whether the form was completed by someone.
*   **Completed** (`completed`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate whether the form was completed.
*   **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate whether the form was submitted.
*   **Submitted** (`submitted`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate whether the form was submitted.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments about the incident.

## Tips

*   Always fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that all relevant information is captured.
*   Verify that you are filling out the correct form for your situation and do not duplicate information by checking with your supervisor or team lead if unsure.
*   Get the required signatures to confirm review and approval.
*   Review and comment on any relevant issues to help in the resolution and prevention of future incidents.
