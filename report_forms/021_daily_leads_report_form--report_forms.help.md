# daily_leads_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and report on daily leads, providing essential information for the sales team to make informed decisions and prioritize their actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Review the sections and fields carefully to ensure you are filling out the correct information.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely, taking into consideration the required fields.
3. Review and submit your report when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Assigned Leads
* **Assigned Leads**: Select all relevant leads that you have been assigned to work on, using the "Yes" or "No" options.
### Action Taken
* **Action Taken**: Describe the actions you have taken or plan to take on the assigned leads, providing relevant details in the text field.
### Notes
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or observations you would like to share about the leads.
### Lead Status
* **Lead Status**: Choose the current status of the lead, using the "Active" or "Inactive" options.
### Contact Method
* **Contact Method**: Select the method used to contact the lead, using the "Phone" or "Email" options.
### Lead Assigned To
* **Lead Assigned To**: Indicate whether the lead has been assigned to you, using the "Yes" or "No" options.

## Tips
* Be accurate and thorough when filling out the form to ensure the sales team has the most up-to-date information.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or team lead for clarification.
* Make sure to submit the form on time to ensure timely reporting and follow-up actions.
