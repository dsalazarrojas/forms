# Flight Punctuality And Delay Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Flight Punctuality And Delay Report Form is used to collect information about flight status and potential delays, and to gather details about the submitter's contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the flight ID from the drop-down menu.
2. Enter the flight number.
3. Select the flight status (On time, Delayed, Cancelled, or Scheduled).
4. Enter the delay time.
5. Optionally, select the cause of the delay (Weather, Technical Issues, Air Traffic Control, or Bird Strike).
6. Enter the delay duration.
7. Enter your name, email address, phone number, and department (optional).
8. Provide any comments or additional information.
9. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Flight Details**: This is the first page of the form. Select the flight ID from the drop-down menu.
* **Flight ID**: Enter the unique identifier for the flight.
* **Flight Number**: Enter the flight number as it appears on the ticket or itinerary.
* **Flight Status**: Select the current status of the flight (On time, Delayed, Cancelled, or Scheduled).
* **Delay Time**: Enter the actual delay time in minutes.
* **Cause of Delay**: Select the reason for the delay (Weather, Technical Issues, Air Traffic Control, or Bird Strike).
* **Delay Duration**: Enter the length of the delay in minutes.
* **Travel Agent**: Provide your name and email address as a travel agent (optional).
* **Submitter**: Enter your name, email address, phone number, and department (optional).
* **Submitter Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information.
* **Travel Agent Name**: Enter the name of the travel agent (optional).
* **Travel Agent Email**: Enter the email address of the travel agent (optional).
* **Travel Agent Phone**: Enter the phone number of the travel agent (optional).
* **Travel Agent Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information from the travel agent.
* **Travel Agent Department**: Enter the department of the travel agent (optional).
* **Travel Agent Title**: Enter the title of the travel agent (optional).
* **Submitter Title**: Enter your title (optional).
* **Submitter Role**: Enter your role (optional).
* **Submitter Company**: Enter the name of your company (optional).
* **Submitter Position**: Enter your position (optional).
* **Submitter Job Title**: Enter your job title (optional).
* **Submitter Organization**: Enter the name of your organization (optional).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Enter accurate and clear information for all fields.
* Provide any additional comments or information to help with investigation.
