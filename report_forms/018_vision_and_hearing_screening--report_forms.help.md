# Vision And Hearing Screening - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record vision and hearing test results for students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter the teacher's name in the "Teacher Name" field.
3. Enter the student's grade level in the "Grade" field.
4. Enter any comments about the student's vision and hearing test in the "Comments" field.
5. Enter the date of the vision test in the "Date of Vision Test" field.
6. Enter the results of the vision test in the "Results of Vision Test" field.
7. Enter the date of the hearing test in the "Date of Hearing Test" field.
8. Enter the results of the hearing test in the "Results of Hearing Test" field.
9. Finally, add any additional comments about the student's vision and hearing test in the "Comments" field.
10. Sign and date the form with the nurse's signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the student's name who underwent the vision and hearing test.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the teacher who administered the test.
* **Grade** (`grade`, number, required/optional): Enter the student's grade level.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any comments about the student's vision and hearing test.
* **Date of Vision Test** (`date_of_vision_test`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the vision test was performed.
* **Results of Vision Test** (`results_of_vision_test`, text, required/optional): Enter the results of the vision test.
* **Date of Hearing Test** (`date_of_hearing_test`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the hearing test was performed.
* **Results of Hearing Test** (`results_of_hearing_test`, text, required/optional): Enter the results of the hearing test.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments about the student's vision and hearing test.
* **Nurse's Signature** (`nurse_signature`, text, required/optional): Sign and date the form with the nurse's signature.
