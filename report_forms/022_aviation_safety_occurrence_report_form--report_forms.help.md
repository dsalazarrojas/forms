This form is designed to collect information related to aviation safety occurrences, which are events that can potentially threaten the safety of people, aircraft, or equipment involved in aviation operations. The form aims to gather detailed information about the occurrence, including its metadata, reporter details, occurrence specifics, and recommended corrective actions. This form will help gather relevant data to enhance aviation safety and minimize the risk of such incidents in the future.

The Aviation Safety Occurrence Report Form is a crucial tool for reporting and analyzing safety-related events in the aviation industry. To ensure accurate and comprehensive reporting, please follow the following steps:

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully and ensure you have all necessary information before starting.
2.  Fill out the "Date of Occurrence" field with the date the event happened.
3.  Fill out the "Time of Occurrence (UTC)" field with the exact time the event occurred.
4.  Enter the "Location of Occurrence" field with the FIR, airport, or position where the event took place.
5.  In the "Reporter Details" section, provide your information as the person reporting the event.
6.  Enter your "Reporter Full Name" and "Reporter Email Address" accurately.
7.  Select your "Reporter Position" from the provided options, as this will help categorize your role in the event.
8.  Fill out the "Airline or Company Code" field if applicable.
9.  In the "Occurrence Specifics" section, categorize and describe the event in detail.
10.  Select the "Occurrence Category" that best describes the level of the event.
11.  Enter the "Aircraft Registration" number if applicable.
12.  Choose the "Phase of Flight" when the occurrence happened.
13.  Provide a clear and factual account of the event in the "Description of Occurrence" field.
14.  Describe the immediate action taken to manage the event in the "Immediate Action Taken" field.
15.  Assess the initial severity of the event in the "Initial Severity Assessment" field.
16.  Suggest recommended corrective actions to prevent recurrence in the "Recommendations for Corrective Action" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date of Occurrence**: Enter the date the event happened.
* **Time of Occurrence (UTC)**: Fill out the exact time the event occurred.
* **Location of Occurrence**: Enter the FIR, airport, or position where the event took place.
* **Reporter Full Name**: Enter your full name accurately.
* **Reporter Email Address**: Enter your email address for follow-up and verification purposes.
* **Reporter Position**: Select your primary role in the context of the event.
* **Airline or Company Code**: Enter the code if applicable.
* **Occurrence Category**: Select the level of event from the provided options.
* **Aircraft Registration**: Enter the tail number of the aircraft involved, if applicable.
* **Phase of Flight**: Choose the phase when the occurrence happened.
* **Description of Occurrence**: Provide a clear and factual account of the event.
* **Immediate Action Taken**: Describe the immediate actions taken to manage the event.
* **Initial Severity Assessment**: Assess the initial severity of the event.
* **Recommendations for Corrective Action**: Suggest actions to prevent recurrence.

## Tips

*   Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
*   Enter all required fields to avoid incomplete submissions.
*   Use clear and concise language when providing descriptions and recommendations.
*   Be thorough when assessing the severity of the event.
*   Follow up with your submission if you have any further questions or concerns.
