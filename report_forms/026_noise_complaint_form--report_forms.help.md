# Noise Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Noise Complaint Form is a tool used to report noise disturbances to the relevant authorities. It collects necessary information about the complainant and the noise issue, which will be used to address the problem and prevent future occurrences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and address in the first two fields.
2. Select the type of noise disturbance (e.g., Construction, Animal, Music, or Other).
3. Provide a detailed description of the issue in the text field.
4. Enter the date and time the noise disturbance started or is currently occurring.
5. Submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Complainant Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Address**: Provide the street, city, and state where the noise disturbance is happening. Please include the zip code for accurate location identification.
* **Type of Noise**: Select the category that best describes the noise disturbance you are reporting.
	+ **Construction**: Loud construction noise, such as from a building site.
	+ **Animal**: Disturbances caused by animals, such as barking dogs or wild animals.
	+ **Music**: Excessive music noise, such as from a party or music events.
	+ **Other**: Any other type of noise disturbance not listed above.
* **Details**: Describe the noise disturbance in as much detail as possible, including the frequency, duration, and location of the noise.
* **Date and Time**: Enter the date and time the noise disturbance started or is currently occurring.
