**gdpr_breach_reporting_form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
This form is designed to report breaches of personal data in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). It helps you document the incident, assess its impact, and track the process of remediation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form sections in sequential order.
2. Provide detailed descriptions for each section, as required.
3. Select relevant options from dropdown menus where applicable.
4. Upload supporting documents if necessary.
5. Submit the form when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
Please find below an explanation for each field:

* **Breach Description** (1): Enter a detailed description of the data breach, including its nature and scope.
* **Affected System** (2): Select the affected system or system component involved in the breach.
* **Data Classification** (3): Choose the type of data compromised in the breach.
* **Date of Discovery** (4): Enter the date you discovered the breach.
* **Date of Reporting** (5): Enter the date you reported the breach.
* **Reported By** (6): Enter the name of the person or department reporting the breach.
* **Description of Event** (7): Provide an additional description of the events surrounding the breach.
* **Affected Data** (8): Select the types of data affected by the breach.
* **Impact on Privacy** (9): Assess the impact on privacy and select the level of severity.
* **Remediation Status** (10): Report the status of remediation efforts.
* **Remediation Plan** (11): Outline a plan for remediating the breach.
* **Notes** (12): Add any additional comments or notes about the breach.
* **Additional Information** (13): Provide any supplementary information about the breach.
* **Supporting Documents** (14): Upload relevant documents supporting your report.
* **Submitted By** (15): Enter your name and contact information.
* **Submitted Date** (16): Enter the date you submitted the report.
* **Reviewed By** (17): Enter the name of the person reviewing the report.
* **Review Date** (18): Enter the date the report was reviewed.
* **Approval Status** (19): Select the status of approval for the report.
* **Reviewer Comments** (20): Enter any comments from the reviewer.
* **Approval Date** (21): Enter the date the report was approved.
* **Escalation Status** (22): Report if the breach has been escalated to a higher authority.
* **Escalated To** (23): Enter the name or department to which the breach was escalated.
* **Escalated Date** (24): Enter the date the breach was escalated.
* **Action Taken** (25): Describe any actions taken to address the breach.

By following these steps and explanations, you should be able to complete the GDPR Breach Reporting Form accurately and efficiently. If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
