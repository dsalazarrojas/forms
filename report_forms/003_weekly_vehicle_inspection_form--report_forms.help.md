# Weekly Vehicle Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a vehicle's condition after a weekly inspection. The inspecting officer will assess the vehicle's condition, mileage, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that you have the necessary information and resources to complete the form accurately.
2. Select the correct inspection officer from the list of available officers.
3. Enter the vehicle's make, year, and mileage in the corresponding fields.
4. Provide a description of the vehicle's condition, including any notable issues or concerns.
5. Enter the date and time of the inspection.
6. Describe the condition of each vehicle component (e.g., engine, transmission, brakes, etc.).
7. Provide any comments or additional information about the vehicle's condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **vehicle_make**: Enter the make of the vehicle (e.g., Toyota, Ford, etc.).
* **vehicle_year**: Enter the year of the vehicle (e.g., 2015, 2020, etc.). (Required)
* **vehicle_model**: Enter the model of the vehicle (e.g., Camry, Mustang, etc.). (Optional)
* **vehicle_license_plate**: Enter the vehicle's license plate number.
* **vehicle_vin**: Enter the vehicle's Vehicle Identification Number (VIN).
* **inspection_date**: Enter the date of the inspection (MM/DD/YY). (Required)
* **inspection_time**: Enter the time of the inspection (HH:MM AM/PM). (Optional)
* **vehicle_condition**: Describe the overall condition of the vehicle, including any notable issues or concerns.
* **mileage**: Enter the current mileage of the vehicle. (Required)
* **engine_condition**: Describe the condition of the vehicle's engine.
* **brakes**: Describe the condition of the vehicle's brakes.
* **suspension_system**: Describe the condition of the vehicle's suspension system.
* **steering_system**: Describe the condition of the vehicle's steering system.
* **transmission**: Describe the condition of the vehicle's transmission.
* **exhaust_system**: Describe the condition of the vehicle's exhaust system.
* **body**: Describe the condition of the vehicle's body.
* **glass_condition**: Describe the condition of the vehicle's glass (e.g., windshield, side windows, etc.).
* **tires**: Describe the condition of the vehicle's tires.
* **lights_and_signals**: Describe the condition of the vehicle's lights and signals (e.g., headlights, taillights, etc.).
* **license_plate**: Enter the vehicle's license plate number. (Duplicate of vehicle_license_plate)
* **vehicle_status**: Enter any additional information about the vehicle's status (e.g., sold, in storage, etc.).
* **inspection_officer**: Select the inspecting officer from the list of available officers. (Required)
* **vehicle_location**: Enter the location where the vehicle was inspected.
* **vehicle_make**: Enter the make of the vehicle (e.g., Toyota, Ford, etc.). (Duplicate of vehicle_make)
* **comments**: Provide any additional comments or information about the vehicle's condition.

Note: If you're unsure about any field, please consult the inspecting officer or the relevant documentation for guidance.
