# Employee Complaint Investigation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and document employee complaints, including reports of misconduct and investigations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the relevant options for "Report Forms and Complaint Forms" to indicate the type of complaint or report being filed.
* Enter details of the employee misconduct or incident in the "Employee Misconduct" field.
* Provide an accurate date and time of the incident in the "Date and Time" field.
* List the names of any witnesses in the "Witness Names" field.
* Enter the name of the employee involved in the "Employee Name" field.
* Choose the correct department related to the complaint in the "Department" field.
* Select the current status of the investigation in the "Investigation Status" field.
* Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Forms and Complaint Forms (1)**: Select the type of complaint or report being filed. This field is used to categorize the complaint.
* **Employee Misconduct (2)**: Enter a detailed description of the misconduct or incident.
* **Date and Time (4)**: Enter the date and time of the incident.
* **Witness Names (5)**: List the names of any witnesses who may have seen or heard something related to the complaint.
* **Employee Name (6)**: Enter the name of the employee involved in the complaint.
* **Department (7)**: Choose the department related to the complaint.
* **Investigation Status (8)**: Select the current status of the investigation.
* **Notes (9)**: Add any additional notes or comments about the complaint or investigation.
* **Category (10)**: This field is currently empty, but you can use it to add any additional information related to the complaint.
