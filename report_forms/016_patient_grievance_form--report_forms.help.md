# Patient Grievance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient feedback and grievances, ensuring that their concerns are heard and addressed promptly by healthcare providers. It is an official form used to gather information and support patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Report Form Patient Info" page:
	* Provide your name and contact information so we can contact you for follow-up.
	* Include your healthcare provider's name (if known).
2. Describe your symptoms on the "Report Form Symptoms" page:
	* Be as detailed as possible to help us better understand your condition.
3. Outline your complaint in the "Report Form Complaint" page:
	* Explain what you are unhappy about with your care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Report Form Patient Info:
* **Patient Info**: Required - Your full name and contact information so we can get in touch with you.
* **Healthcare Provider**: If you know the healthcare provider's name, please enter it here. It helps us to direct your complaint.

### Report Form Symptoms:
* **Symptoms**: Required - Describe your symptoms and any relevant health issues you are experiencing.

### Report Form Complaint:
* **Complaint**: Required - Explain your complaint in detail. What are you unhappy about with your care? What would you like us to do about it?

## Tips
- Please be as detailed as possible when filling out this form.
- Attach any supporting documents or evidence you believe is relevant to your complaint.
- If you experience any difficulties filling out this form, please contact us for assistance.
