# Police Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Police Complaint Form is designed to collect information about incidents or complaints reported to the police department. This form helps to gather details about the incident, including the date, location, description, suspect information, and other relevant details. The form is intended for use by individuals who want to report a crime or incident to the police.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Police Complaint Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the incident date, which is the date the incident occurred.
2. Provide a brief description of the incident, including as much detail as possible.
3. If applicable, enter the suspect's name and any other relevant information.
4. If applicable, provide the police officer's name and badge number (if known).
5. Enter the case number (if assigned by the police department).
6. Choose the incident type (e.g., theft, assault, etc.).
7. Enter the police department that received the complaint.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Provide the location where the incident took place.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required: false): A brief description of what happened.
* **Suspect** (`suspect`, text, required: false): If known, provide the suspect's name and any other relevant information.
* **Police Officer** (`police_officer`, text, required: false): If known, provide the police officer's name and badge number.
* **Case Number** (`case_number`, text, required: false): Enter the case number assigned by the police department (if applicable).
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, text, required: false): Choose the type of incident (e.g., theft, assault, etc.).
* **Police Department** (`police_department`, text, required: false): Enter the police department that received the complaint.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide as much detail as possible when filling out the form.
* If unsure about any information, leave it blank and note "Unknown" in the field.
* Review and double-check your answers before submitting the form.
* Contact the police department if you have any questions or need further assistance.
