# Boat Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record details about a boat's journey, including departure and arrival information, weather conditions, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the date of this log entry in the "Log Date" field.
2. Enter the name of the vessel in the "Boat Name" field.
3. Enter the official registration number of the boat in the "Boat Registration Number" field.
4. Enter the name of the person in command in the "Captain Name" field.
5. Enter the departure location in the "Departure Location" field.
6. Enter the time of departure in the "Departure Time" field.
7. Enter the destination in the "Destination" field.
8. Enter the expected time of arrival in the "Estimated Arrival Time" field (if applicable).
9. Enter the actual time of arrival in the "Actual Arrival Time" field (if applicable).
10. Enter the number of passengers on board in the "Number of Passengers" field.
11. If there are passengers on board, list their names in the "Passenger Names" field.
12. If there is a crew on duty, list their names in the "Crew Members" field.
13. Enter the level of fuel at departure in the "Fuel Level at Departure" field.
14. Enter the level of fuel at arrival in the "Fuel Level at Arrival" field.
15. Enter the amount of fuel added during the trip in the "Fuel Added" field (if applicable).
16. Enter the total cost of fuel for the trip in the "Fuel Cost" field (if applicable).
17. Enter the engine hours at the start of the trip in the "Engine Hours at Start" field.
18. Enter the engine hours at the end of the trip in the "Engine Hours at End" field.
19. Enter the distance traveled during the trip in the "Distance Traveled" field.
20. Describe the weather conditions during the trip in the "Weather Conditions" field.
21. Describe the sea state during the trip in the "Sea Conditions" field.
22. Enter the wind speed in knots in the "Wind Speed" field (if applicable).
23. If any issues were encountered during the trip, enter them in the "Issues Encountered" field.
24. If any maintenance was performed during the trip, enter it in the "Maintenance Performed" field.
25. If any incidents or accidents occurred during the trip, enter them in the "Incidents or Accidents" field.
26. Enter any additional notes or comments about the trip in the "Notes and Comments" field.
27. Enter your name as the person completing this log in the "Submitter Name" field.
28. Type your name as the signature in the "Submitter Signature" field.
29. Enter the date this log was submitted in the "Submission Date" field.

* **Distance Traveled**: Enter the distance traveled during the trip.

* **Wind Speed**: Enter the wind speed in knots (if applicable).
