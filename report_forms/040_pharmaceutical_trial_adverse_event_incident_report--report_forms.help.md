# Pharmaceutical Trial Adverse Event Incident Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pharmaceutical Trial Adverse Event Incident Report form is used to document and track adverse events that occur during a pharmaceutical trial or study. This form helps gather essential information about the event, including its description, severity, and any actions taken to address it.

## How To Complete This Form
To ensure accurate and thorough completion of the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand its purpose and the information required.
2. Ensure you have all relevant information and data before starting the form.
3. Complete each field accurately and truthfully, using the provided fields as guidelines.
4. Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Study ID** (study_id)
The **Study ID** field should be populated with the corresponding study ID number.

### **Report Title** (report_title)
Enter a brief title describing the reason for the report.

### **Study Phase** (study_phase)
Select the relevant study phase: 
*   Phase 1
*   Phase 2

### **Study Status** (study_status)
Select the relevant study status:
*   Active
*   Completed
*   Terminated

### **Date Incident Occurred** (date_incident_occurred)
Enter the date the adverse event occurred.

### **Time Incident Occurred** (time_incident_occurred)
Enter the time the adverse event occurred.

### **Event Description** (event_description)
Describe the adverse event that occurred.

### **Subject ID** (subject_id)
Enter the subject ID number.

### **Study Title** (study_title)
Enter the study title as listed on the protocol.

### **Study Start Date** (study_start_date)
Enter the study start date.

### **Study End Date** (study_end_date)
Enter the study end date if applicable.

### **Study Location** (study_location)
Enter where the adverse event occurred.

### **Adverse Reaction** (adverse_reaction)
Enter the adverse reaction or event.

### **Severity** (severity)
Select the relevant severity:
*   Mild
*   Moderate
*   Severe

### **Action Taken** (action_taken)
Describe the action taken to address the adverse event.

### **Action Taken By** (action_taken_by)
Enter the name of the person who took action.

### **Study Sponsor** (study_sponsor)
Enter the name of the sponsor.

### **Study Sponsor Contact** (study_sponsor_contact)
Enter the contact name.

### **Study Sponsor Org** (study_sponsor_org)
Enter the sponsor org.

### **Study Sponsor Address** (study_sponsor_addr)
Enter the sponsor org address.

### **Study Sponsor Phone** (study_sponsor_phone)
Enter the sponsor phone number.

### **Study Sponsor Email** (study_sponsor_email)
Enter the sponsor email.

### **Adverse Event ID** (adverse_event_id)
Enter the Adverse Event ID number.

### **Event Cause** (adverse_event_cause)
Describe the cause of the adverse event.

### **Tips**

*   Please ensure all relevant information is recorded accurately and completely.
*   Review the form for completeness and consistency before submission.
*   If you are unsure or have any questions, please contact the study sponsor or relevant stakeholders for guidance.
