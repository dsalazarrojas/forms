# Platform Metrics Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Platform Metrics Report Form is a tool used to collect and analyze data on platform metrics. This form is intended for internal purposes only and should not be used for external communication or reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the reporting period for which you want to gather metrics.
2. Enter the name of the platform or product for which you are gathering metrics.
3. Enter the product ID number.
4. Enter any relevant performance metrics.
5. Enter any notes or comments.
6. Provide your email address.
7. Provide your phone number.
8. Select the submit options to confirm your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reporting Periods** (`reporting_periods`): Select the period for which you want to gather metrics (e.g., Q1 2024, Q2 2024, etc.).
* **Product Name** (`product_name`): Enter the name of the platform or product for which you are gathering metrics.
* **Product ID** (`product_id`): Enter the product ID number.
* **Metric 1** (`metric_1`): Enter the first performance metric.
* **Metric 2** (`metric_2`): Enter the second performance metric.
* **Metric 3** (`metric_3`): Enter the third performance metric.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional comments or notes you have regarding the metrics.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Submit Options** (`platform_metrics_report_form_submit`): Confirm that you have read and understand the terms of this form submission and that it is used for internal purposes only. You can select multiple options to confirm your submission.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`): Enter the date of submission.
