# Free Police Incident Report Template - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to document and report incidents that have occurred at a specific location. This form helps you provide detailed information to the authorities, ensuring they can respond and address the issue promptly and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the Date of occurrence field with the date on which the incident took place.
2. Enter the Time of incident field with the exact time the incident occurred.
3. Provide the Location of incident, specifying where the incident happened.
4. In the Details of incident field, describe the incident in detail, including what happened and any relevant details.
5. If applicable, enter the Name of person involved field with the name of the person or people involved in the incident.
6. Fill out the Contact number field with the contact information of the person reporting the incident or the person involved.
7. Enter the Email address field with the email address of the person reporting the incident or the person involved.
8. In the Additional details field, provide any further information that may be relevant to the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date of occurrence**: Enter the date on which the incident occurred in the format mm/dd/yyyy or mm/dd/yyyy hh:mm AM/PM.
* **Time of incident**: Enter the exact time the incident occurred in the 24-hour format hh:mm AM/PM.
* **Location of incident**: Describe the location where the incident took place, including the address, building, or area.
* **Details of incident**: Provide a detailed description of what happened, including any relevant context or circumstances.
* **Name of person involved**: If applicable, enter the name of the person or people involved in the incident.
* **Contact number**: Enter the contact information of the person reporting the incident or the person involved.
* **Email address**: Enter the email address of the person reporting the incident or the person involved.
* **Additional details**: Provide any further information that may be relevant to the incident, such as any witnesses or evidence.
