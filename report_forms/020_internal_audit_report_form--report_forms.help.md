# Internal Audit Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Internal Audit Report Form is a tool used to document internal audit findings, report on dates and times of audits, locations, auditor names, and other relevant details. This form is typically used for quality assurance, risk management, or compliance purposes within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields as indicated by the required tag in each section.
2. Enter the date and time of the audit in the corresponding fields.
3. Select the location where the audit was conducted from the options provided.
4. Choose the status of the audit from the options provided.
5. Enter the name and contact information of the auditor.
6. Add any additional notes related to the audit in the notes field.
7. Optionally, select an output file to attach to the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Audit Form**:
	+ (Required): This field is the main description of the audit. Describe the scope, objective, and methodology of the audit.
* **Date of Audited**:
	+ (Date): Enter the date of the audit, using the format MM/DD/YY (Month, Day, Year).
* **Time of Audited**:
	+ (Time): Enter the time of the audit, using 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Location**:
	+ (Select Multiple): Choose the location where the audit was conducted from the provided options.
* **Status**:
	+ (Select One): Choose the status of the audit from the provided options.
* **Auditor**:
	+ (Text): Enter the name of the auditor who conducted the audit.
* **Contact**:
	+ (Text): Enter the contact information of the auditor, including phone number, email, or other relevant details.
* **Notes**:
	+ (Note): Add any additional notes related to the audit, including findings, recommendations, or follow-up actions.
* **Output File**:
	+ (Text): Optionally, select an output file to attach to the report, such as a document or image related to the audit.
