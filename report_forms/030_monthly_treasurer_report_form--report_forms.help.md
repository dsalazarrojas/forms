# Monthly Treasurer Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Monthly Treasurer Report Form is a tool for reporting monthly financial activities, including income, expenses, and balance. It is used to track and analyze financial data for the month.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief summary of the month's activities in the "Monthly Report" field.
2. Enter income amounts in the "Income" field.
3. Select the type of income in the "Income Type" field.
4. Enter expense amounts in the "Expense" field.
5. Select the type of expense in the "Expense Type" field.
6. Calculate the total income and expenses balance in the "Balance" field.
7. Enter the date and time of submission in the "Submission Date" and "Submission Time" fields.
8. Provide contact information, if necessary, in the "Contact Info" and "Email" fields.
9. Select a category for the report in the "Category" field.
10. Confirm whether the category should be repeated in the "Repeat category selection" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Monthly Report** (text, optional): Enter a brief summary of the month's activities.
* **Income** (number, optional): Enter income amounts.
* **Income Type** (select_one, required): Select the type of income (Salary, Investments, Business Income).
* **Expense** (number, optional): Enter expense amounts.
* **Expense Type** (select_one, required): Select the type of expense (Rent, Food, Transportation).
* **Balance** (number, required): Calculate the total income and expenses balance.
* **Submission Date** (date, required): Enter the date of submission.
* **Submission Time** (time, required): Enter the time of submission.
* **Contact Info** (text, optional): Enter phone number.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter email.
* **Category** (select_one, required): Select a category (Yes, No).
* **Repeat category selection** (select_one, optional): Confirm whether the category should be repeated (Yes, No).
