# daily_management_report_and_performance_log_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The daily management report and performance log form is used to track and log information about tasks, employee performance, and management activities on a daily basis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the task title and description.
2. Select the status of the task: completed, pending, in progress, or cancelled.
3. Select the status of the employee: active, inactive, or on leave.
4. Enter the date and time of the task completion or update.
5. Enter the email and phone number of the employee.
6. Add any additional notes or comments about the task or employee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **task_title** (text, required): Enter the title of the task.
* **employee_tasks** (number, required): Enter the number of tasks completed by the employee.
* **task_performance** (text, required): Enter a brief description of the task performance.
* **employee_performance** (number, required): Enter the performance rating of the employee.
* **task_status** (select one, required): Select the status of the task: completed, pending, in progress, or cancelled.
* **employee_status** (select one, required): Select the status of the employee: active, inactive, or on leave.
* **date** (date, required): Enter the date of the task completion or update.
* **time** (time, required): Enter the time of the task completion or update.
* **employee** (select one, required): Select the employee who completed the task.
* **employee_description** (text, required): Enter a brief description of the employee's work or performance.
* **manager** (select one, required): Select whether the task is managed by you or not.
* **form_title** (text, required): Enter a title for the form.
* **form_description** (text, required): Enter a description of the form.
* **form_category** (text, required): Enter the category of the form.
* **form_issuer** (text, required): Enter the name of the person who issued the form.
* **form_id** (number, required): Enter the ID of the form.
* **form_assigned_tool** (text, required): Enter the tool assigned to the form.
* **form_updated_by** (text, required): Enter the person who updated the form.
* **form_created_by** (text, required): Enter the person who created the form.

Note: The fields marked with a star (*) are required fields.
