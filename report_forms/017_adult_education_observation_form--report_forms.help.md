# Adult Education Observation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adult Education Observation Form is designed to assess the performance of educators in adult education settings, focusing on specific behaviors and characteristics that demonstrate their teaching effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields for:
	* Observer Name: Enter your professional name.
	* Educator Name Being Observed: Enter the name of the educator you are observing.
	* Subject or Course Title: Enter the title of the subject or course being observed.
	* Observation Date: Enter the date of the observation.
	* Observation Start Time: Enter the start time of the observation.
	* Lesson Preparation and Clarity: Choose how well the educator prepared the lesson and presented it to the adult learners.
	* Engagement with Adult Learners: Choose how well the educator facilitated discussion and involvement among the adult learners.
	* Subject Matter Expertise: Choose how well the educator demonstrated subject matter expertise.
	* Classroom Management and Environment: Choose how well the educator managed the classroom and created a positive learning environment.
	* Overall Performance Rating: Choose a rating that best summarizes your overall impression of the educator's performance.
	* Overall Performance Rating: Enter any final thoughts or comments on the observation.
2. If applicable, fill in the optional fields for:
	* Observer Strengths Noted: Provide feedback on areas of strength observed.
	* Areas for Improvement: Suggest specific areas for the educator's growth.
	* Observer Summary Comments: Add any final thoughts or comments on the observation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Observer Name** (`observer_name`, required): Enter your professional name.
* **Educator Name Being Observed** (`educator_name_being_observed`, required): Enter the name of the educator you are observing.
* **Subject or Course Title** (`subject_or_course_title`, required): Enter the title of the subject or course being observed.
* **Observation Date** (`observation_date`, required): Enter the date of the observation.
* **Observation Start Time** (`observation_start_time`, required): Enter the start time of the observation.
* **Lesson Preparation and Clarity** (`lesson_preparation_and_clarity`, required):
	+ Excellent: The educator demonstrated exceptional lesson preparation and clarity in presenting the material.
	+ Good: The educator demonstrated adequate lesson preparation and clarity, but could improve.
	+ Satisfactory: The educator demonstrated some lesson preparation and clarity, but room for improvement.
	+ Needs Improvement: The educator did not demonstrate sufficient lesson preparation and clarity.
	+ Poor: The educator's lesson preparation and clarity was lacking.
* **Engagement with Adult Learners** (`engagement_with_adult_learners`, required):
	+ Excellent: The educator effectively facilitated discussion and involvement among the adult learners.
	+ Good: The educator facilitated some discussion and involvement, but could improve.
	+ Satisfactory: The educator facilitated some discussion and involvement, but room for improvement.
	+ Needs Improvement: The educator did not facilitate sufficient discussion and involvement.
	+ Poor: The educator's engagement with adult learners was lacking.
* **Subject Matter Expertise** (`subject_matter_expertise`, required):
	+ Excellent: The educator demonstrated exceptional subject matter expertise.
	+ Good: The educator demonstrated adequate subject matter expertise, but could improve.
	+ Satisfactory: The educator demonstrated some subject matter expertise, but room for improvement.
	+ Needs Improvement: The educator did not demonstrate sufficient subject matter expertise.
	+ Poor: The educator's subject matter expertise was lacking.
* **Use of Instructional Technology** (`use_of_instructional_technology`, optional):
	+ Excellent: The educator effectively used instructional technology during the lesson.
	+ Good: The educator used some instructional technology during the lesson, but could improve.
	+ Satisfactory: The educator used some instructional technology during the lesson, but room for improvement.
	+ Needs Improvement: The educator did not use sufficient instructional technology during the lesson.
	+ Poor: The educator's use of instructional technology was lacking.
* **Classroom Management and Environment** (`classroom_management_and_environment`, required):
	+ Excellent: The educator effectively managed the classroom and created a positive learning environment.
	+ Good: The educator managed the classroom and created some learning environment, but could improve.
	+ Satisfactory: The educator managed the classroom and created some learning environment, but room for improvement.
	+ Needs Improvement: The educator did not manage the classroom and create a positive learning environment.
	+ Poor: The educator's classroom management and environment were lacking.
* **Observer Strengths Noted** (`observer_strengths_noted`, optional): Provide feedback on areas of strength observed.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, optional): Suggest specific areas for the educator's growth.
* **Overall Performance Rating** (`overall_performance_rating`, required):
	+ Exceeds Expectations: The educator's performance far exceeded expectations.
	+ Meets Expectations: The educator's performance met expectations.
	+ Developing: The educator's performance demonstrated some areas of growth.
	+ Unsatisfactory: The educator's performance did not meet expectations.
* **Observer Summary Comments** (`observer_summary_comments`, optional): Add any final thoughts or comments on the observation.
* **Use of Instructional Technology** (`use_of_instructional_technology`, optional): This field is not used in the form as it is not required.

* **Overall Performance Rating** (`overall_performance_rating`, required): This field is meant to summarize the overall performance rating.
