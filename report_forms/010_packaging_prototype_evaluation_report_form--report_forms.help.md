# Packaging Prototype Evaluation Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on packaging prototypes. It's meant for packaging evaluators to document their assessment of a prototype's packaging concept, design, and performance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Packaging Prototype Evaluation Report Form** page with your feedback on the packaging prototype.
2. Choose your response for each question in the **Packaging Concept Matrix**.
3. Add any additional comments about the prototype.
4. Sign and date the form as the Evaluator and Reviewer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Packaging Prototype Evaluation Report Form** (`packaging_prototype_evaluation_report_form`, text, required): This is the main section where you write your overall thoughts and feedback about the packaging prototype. 
* **Packaging Concept Matrix** (`packaging_concept_matrix`, select_multiple, required): Use this section to rate how well the prototype's packaging concept meets the required criteria. You can choose from "Strongly Disagree", "Neutral", and "Strongly Agree" options.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback about the prototype that you didn't mention in the main section.
* **Evaluator Signature** (`evaluator_signature`, note, optional): Sign and date the form as the evaluator who filled it out.
* **Reviewer Signature** (`reviewer_signature`, note, optional): Sign and date the form as the reviewer who verified the report.
* **Date Reviewed** (`date_reviewed`, date, optional): Record the date when you reviewed the packaging prototype.
* **Time Reviewed** (`time_reviewed`, time, optional): Record the time when you reviewed the packaging prototype.
* **Packaging Prototype Evaluation Report Form Version** (`packaging_prototype_evaluation_report_form_version`, number, optional): Record the version of the packaging prototype evaluation report form.

**Tips**
* Please be as specific as possible when filling out the **Packaging Concept Matrix**.
* Make sure to date and time stamp the **Date Reviewed** and **Time Reviewed** accurately.
* The **Evaluator Signature** and **Reviewer Signature** should be signed and dated by the respective individuals.
