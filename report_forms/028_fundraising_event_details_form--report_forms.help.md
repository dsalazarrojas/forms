# Fundraising Event Details Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fundraising Event Details Form is designed to collect and record crucial information about fundraising events, enabling efficient management and follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name**: <event_name> 
  * This field is used to enter the name of the fundraising event.
  * Enter a brief and descriptive title for the event.
* **Date**: <date>
  * This field is for entering the date of the fundraising event.
  * Select the correct date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Event Description**: <event_description>
  * This field is for providing a detailed description of the event.
  * Enter a brief but descriptive paragraph about the event.
* **Event Location**: <event_location>
  * This field is for entering the location of the fundraising event.
  * Enter the exact location where the event will take place.
* **Event Time**: <event_time>
  * This field is for entering the time of the fundraising event.
  * Select the correct time in the format HH:MM.
* **Target Goal**: <target_goal>
  * This field is for entering the target amount for the fundraising event.
  * Enter the total amount the event aims to raise.
* **Actual Goal**: <actual_goal>
  * This field is for entering the actual amount raised during the event.
  * Enter the total amount collected from the event.
* **Event Type**: <event_type>
  * This field is for selecting the type of the fundraising event.
  * Choose between "Charity event" or "Fundraiser event".
* **Contact Name**: <contact_name>
  * This field is for entering the name of the contact person.
  * Enter the name of the person responsible for the event.
* **Contact Email**: <contact_email>
  * This field is for entering the email of the contact person.
  * Enter the email address of the person responsible for the event.
* **Contact Phone**: <contact_phone>
  * This field is for entering the phone number of the contact person.
  * Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the event.
* **Event Status**: <event_status>
  * This field is for selecting the status of the fundraising event.
  * Choose between "Active", "Inactive", or "Completed".
* **Notes**: <notes>
  * This field is for entering any additional notes about the event.
  * Enter any relevant comments or observations about the event.
* **Form ID**: <form_id>
  * This field is for entering the unique identifier of the form.
  * This field is not typically used by end-users.
* **Assigned To**: <assigned_to>
  * This field is for selecting the person assigned to the event.
  * Choose between "John Doe" or "Jane Doe". 

Please refer to this guide for accurate and efficient filling out of the form.
