# Marketing Client Project Outcome Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about marketing client projects, specifically their outcomes, performance insights, and client feedback. The goal is to gather data that will help assess the success or progress of marketing projects.
 
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Read the instructions carefully and ensure you understand what information is being requested.
- Fill out each field with accurate and relevant information about the project.
- Review and update your answers as needed.
 
## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, optional):
  - Provide a brief description of the project, including its goals, objectives, and any relevant context.
- **Outcomes** (`outcomes`, text, optional):
  - Report on the overall outcome of the project, including any achievements or results.
- **Performance Insights** (`performance_insights`, text, optional):
  - Share any insights or data that demonstrate the project's performance, such as metrics or statistics.
- **Client Feedback** (`client_feedback`, select_multiple, optional):
  - Choose the client's feedback about the project, which can be:
    - Client was happy with the project
    - Client was neutral with the project
    - Client was unhappy with the project
- **Project Outcomes** (`project_outcomes`, text, optional):
  - Provide more details on the project outcomes, including any specific results or achievements.
- **Client Performance Insights** (`client_performance_insights`, text, optional):
  - Share any insights about the client's performance during or after the project.
- **Close Date** (`close_date`, date, optional):
  - Enter the date when the project was closed or completed.
 
## Tips
- Ensure you provide clear and accurate information in each field.
- If unsure about any field, use your best judgment or estimate based on available data.
- Review and update your answers as necessary to ensure they reflect the project's actual status.
