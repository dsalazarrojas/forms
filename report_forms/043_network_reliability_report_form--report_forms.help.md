# Network Reliability Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Network Reliability Report Form is designed to collect information about network-related incidents that occur within the network. This form helps identify and analyze network reliability issues to improve overall network performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, such as incident date, incident time, and incident location.
2. Select the affected services that were impacted during the incident.
3. Choose the incident impact and outcome.
4. Provide a description of the incident.
5. Analyze the root cause of the incident and suggest corrective actions.
6. Identify any preventive measures taken to avoid similar incidents.
7. Select the category, type, and subtype of the incident.
8. Enter the status of the incident.
9. Select who reported the incident.
10. Enter the incident report ID and the reporter's name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, required): Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Duration** (`incident_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of the incident.
* **Location** (`incident_location`, text, required): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Affected Services** (`affected_services`, select_multiple, required): Select the services that were impacted during the incident.
* **Incident Impact** (`incident_impact`, select_one, required): Select the impact of the incident (High, Medium, Low).
* **Incident Outcome** (`incident_outcome`, select_one, required): Select the outcome of the incident (Resolved, Ongoing, Unknown).
* **Description** (`incident_description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **Root Cause** (`root_cause`, text, required): Analyze and enter the root cause of the incident.
* **Corrective Action** (`corrective_action`, text, required): Suggest a corrective action to avoid similar incidents.
* **Preventive Measures** (`preventive_measures`, text, required): Identify any preventive measures taken to avoid similar incidents.
* **Category** (`incident_category`, text, required): Enter the category of the incident.
* **Type** (`incident_type`, text, required): Enter the type of the incident.
* **Subtype** (`incident_subtype`, text, required): Enter the subtype of the incident.
* **Status** (`incident_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the incident (Open, Closed, In Progress).
* **Reported by** (`reported_by`, select_one, required): Select who reported the incident (IT, Management, Team Member).
* **Incident Report ID** (`incident_report_id`, text, required): Enter a unique ID for the incident report.
* **Reporter** (`incident_reporter`, text, required): Enter the name of the reporter.
* **Date Reported** (`incident_date_reported`, date, required): Enter the date the incident was reported.
* **Last Updated** (`incident_last_updated`, date, required): Enter the date the incident was last updated.
* **Priority** (`incident_priority`, select_one, required): Select the priority of the incident (High, Medium, Low).
* **Comment** (`incident_comment`, text, required): Provide a comment about the incident.
* **Commenter** (`incident_commenter`, text, required): Enter the name of the commenter.
* **Date Reported** (`incident_date_reported`, date, required): Enter the date the incident was reported.
* **Last Updated** (`incident_last_updated`, date, required): Enter the date the incident was last updated.
* **Reported by** (`incident_reported_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
* **Incident Reporter** (`incident_reporter`, text, required): Enter the name of the reporter.
