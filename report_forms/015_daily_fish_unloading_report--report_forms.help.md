# Daily Fish Unloading Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect data about fish unloading activities, including the date, vessel, landing site, fish species, quantity, condition, and time of unloading.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the date of the unloading activity in the "Unloading Date" field.
2. Enter the name of the vessel that unloaded the fish in the "Vessel" field.
3. Enter the location where the fish were unloaded in the "Landing Site" field.
4. Select the type of fish species that was unloaded in the "Fish Species" field.
5. Enter the total quantity of fish unloaded in the "Quantity" field.
6. Indicate the condition of the fish unloaded in the "Condition" field, by selecting one or more options.
7. Enter any additional remarks or comments about the unloading activity in the "Remarks" field (this field is optional).
8. Enter the time of day the fish were unloaded in the "Unloading Time" field.
9. Enter the date and time you are submitting this report in the "Submit Date" and "Submit Time" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Unloading Date* (Date):** Enter the date of the unloading activity.
* **Vessel* (Text):** Enter the name of the vessel that unloaded the fish.
* **Landing Site* (Text):** Enter the location where the fish were unloaded.
* **Fish Species* (Select One):** Select the type of fish species that was unloaded.
*   - **Salmon:** Unloaded salmon
   - **Cod:** Unloaded cod
   - **Trout:** Unloaded trout
* **Quantity* (Number):** Enter the total quantity of fish unloaded.
* **Condition* (Select Multiple):** Indicate the condition of the fish unloaded.
  - **Good:** The fish are in good condition.
  - **Bad:** The fish are in bad condition.
  - **Damaged:** The fish are damaged.
*   **Remarks (Note):** Enter any additional remarks or comments about the unloading activity (this field is optional).
* **Unloading Time* (Time):** Enter the time of day the fish were unloaded.
* **Submit Date* (Date):** Enter the date you are submitting this report.
* **Submit Time* (Time):** Enter the time you are submitting this report.
