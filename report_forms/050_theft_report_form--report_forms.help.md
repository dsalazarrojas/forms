# Theft Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for a theft report, which can be used for investigation or insurance purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the date and time of the incident in the "Date & Time" field.
2. Provide a brief description of what happened in the "Description" field.
3. If the suspect is a vehicle, provide their vehicle make and model in the "Suspect Vehicle Make" and "Suspect Vehicle Model" fields.
4. If the suspect is a person, provide their name, address, and contact information in the "Suspect Name", "Suspect Address", and "Suspect Phone" fields.
5. Provide your contact information and signature as the witness in the "Witness Contact Details" and "Witness Signature" fields.
6. The police officer will review and sign the report in the "Police Officer Name", "Police Officer ID", and "Police Officer Signature" fields.
7. Ensure that the report is accurate and complete before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Theft Report**: This is the main section where you will describe what happened.
* **Location**: This field is optional and should be filled out if the incident occurred in a specific location.
* **Date & Time**: This field should be filled out in the format MM/DD/YYYY HH:MM:SS.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of what happened.
* **Witness Details**: This field is for the witness to provide their details, including their name, address, and contact information.
* **Suspect Name**: If the suspect is a person, provide their name.
* **Suspect Address**: If the suspect is a person, provide their address.
* **Suspect Phone**: If the suspect is a person, provide their phone number.
* **Suspect Vehicle Details**: If the suspect is a vehicle, provide their vehicle make and model.
* **Suspect Vehicle License**: If the suspect is a vehicle, provide their license plate number.
* **Witness Signature**: This field is for the witness to sign and date the report, acknowledging that they have provided accurate information.
* **Witness Signature Image**: This field is optional and allows the witness to upload an image of their signature.
