# inside_sales_daily_emails_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for inside sales teams to report their daily activities, such as sending and responding to emails.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your contact name and email address.
2. Provide a brief description of the email subject.
3. Write a concise summary of the email body.
4. Specify the action taken on the email (e.g., sent, responded, pending).
5. Add any additional comments about the email interaction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the person you interacted with.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the person you interacted with.
* **Subject** (`subject`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the topic or subject of the email.
* **Email Body** (`email_body`, `note`, required/optional): Provide a brief summary of the email content.
* **Action Taken** (`action_taken`, `text`, required/optional): Specify the action taken on the email, such as sent, responded to, or pending.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the email interaction.

## Tips
* Be as concise as possible when filling out the email subject and body fields.
* Use the action taken field to track the status of the email.
* Use the additional comments field to add any relevant context or notes about the email.
