# sprintercrv_pre_trip_inspection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for pre-trip inspections to document important details of the trip. This information will help us identify any potential issues and ensure a smooth and safe trip for our clients.
## How To Complete This Form
- Read the form carefully and ensure all required fields have been completed.
- Answer each field accurately and concisely.
- Double-check your answers for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Trip Details** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the trip, including the purpose and any specific details relevant to the inspection.
  This field is used to provide a summary of the trip details, such as the destination and any notable events or issues.

- **Driver Name** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the driver conducting the trip.
  This field is used to capture the driver's name for reference and audit purposes.

- **Driver License Number** (`3`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the driver's license number.
  This field is used to verify the driver's identity and ensure compliance with company regulations.

- **Vehicle License Number** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the vehicle's license number.
  This field is used to verify the vehicle's registration and ensure it is in good standing.

- **Trip Start Time** (`5`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the start time of the trip.
  This field is used to track the start time of the trip and ensure timely departure.

- **Trip Start Location** (`6`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the location where the trip started.
  This field is used to document the starting point of the trip.

- **Trip End Time** (`7`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the end time of the trip.
  This field is used to track the end time of the trip and ensure timely arrival.

- **Trip End Location** (`8`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the location where the trip ended.
  This field is used to document the ending point of the trip.

- **Odometer Reading** (`9`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the odometer reading at the start of the trip.
  This field is used to track the vehicle's mileage before the trip.

- **Fuel Type** (`10`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the type of fuel used for the trip.
  This field is used to track the type of fuel used during the trip.

- **Fuel Level** (`11`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the current fuel level.
  This field is used to document the fuel level at the start of the trip.

- **Vehicle Condition** (`12`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the vehicle's current condition.
  This field is used to document any pre-existing conditions that may impact the trip.

- **Notes** (`13`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the trip.
  This field is used to capture any important information that doesn't fit in other fields.

- **Trip Start Mileage** (`14`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the odometer reading at the start of the trip.
  This field is used to track the vehicle's mileage before the trip.

Note: Please ensure all fields are completed accurately and concisely to ensure a thorough pre-trip inspection.
## Tips
- Be thorough when filling out the form to ensure all details are accounted for.
- Double-check your answers for accuracy to avoid errors.
- If you're unsure about any field, please consult your supervisor or relevant policies and procedures.
- Keep the form up to date with current information, as this will help us improve our processes and procedures.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor.
