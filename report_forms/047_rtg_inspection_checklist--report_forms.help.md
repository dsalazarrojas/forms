# rtg_inspection_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The rtg_inspection_checklist form is used to record and manage inspection information for equipment and systems.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form fields and ensure you understand what each field represents.
2. Answer each question or field according to your inspection findings and knowledge.
3. Enter your responses in the relevant fields, taking care to select the correct options for select fields.
4. Save your completed form for future reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Is the equipment in good working order** (equipment_status, number, required): Answer "yes" or "no" to indicate the current status of the equipment.
* **Safety features** (safety_features, select_one, required): Select one or more safety features present, such as alarm system, warning system, or safety valve.
* **How often are you inspecting** (inspection_frequency, select_multiple, required): Select from Daily, Weekly, or Monthly to indicate the frequency of inspections.
* **Last inspection date** (last_inspection_date, date, required): Enter the date of the last inspection.
* **Next inspection due date** (next_inspection_due_date, date, required): Enter the date of the next scheduled inspection.
* **Maintenance status** (maintenance_status, number, required): Answer "yes" or "no" to indicate if maintenance is required.
* **Maintenance frequency** (maintenance_frequency, select_multiple, required): Select from Daily, Weekly, or Monthly to indicate the frequency of maintenance.
* **Last maintenance date** (maintenance_last_date, date, required): Enter the date of the last maintenance performed.
* **Next maintenance due date** (maintenance_next_due_date, date, required): Enter the date of the next scheduled maintenance.
* **Comments** (comments, note, required): Enter any comments or notes about the inspection or maintenance.
* **Comments 1** (comments_1, note, optional): Enter any additional comments.
* **Contact Person** (contact_person, text, required): Enter the name of the person responsible for maintenance or inspection.
* **Contact Person Email** (contact_person_email, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Person Phone** (contact_person_phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
