# Crash Risk Analysis Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Crash Risk Analysis Form is designed to help analyze and report on crashes, enabling informed decision-making and risk mitigation strategies.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form for a single crash incident.
2.  Select your relevant contact information for the incident from the options provided.
3.  Choose the type and severity of the crash from the options list.
4.  Enter a detailed assessment of the crash, providing relevant information and observations.
5.  Add any additional notes or comments about the incident, if necessary.
6.  Review and confirm that you have provided all the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contact Info (contact_info)**: Select your contact information related to the crash incident, such as your name or your team's contact.
*   **Crash Info (crash_info)**: Select the type and severity of the crash from the provided options.
*   **Assessment (assessment)**: Enter a detailed assessment of the crash, including relevant information and observations.
*   **Notes (notes)**: Add any additional comments or notes about the incident, if necessary.
