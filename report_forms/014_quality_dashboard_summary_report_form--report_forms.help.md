# Quality Dashboard Summary Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quality Dashboard Summary Report Form is used to generate a quality dashboard summary report for a team. It will help assess team performance, participation, and other quality metrics. This report will be used to identify areas of improvement and track progress over time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the team you want to report on from the "Team Performance" page.
2. Choose the correct status for team performance (Good or Poor).
3. Check the "Team Participation" box for team members who are participating.
4. Enter the number of issues reported and resolved.
5. Enter the total score.
6. Select the report date.
7. Enter the report time.
8. Enter the team's email address.
9. Enter the team's phone number.
10. Add any additional notes for the team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Performance**: Select the status of the team's performance (Good or Poor).
	+ This field will help us assess the team's overall performance.
* **Team Participation**: Check the box for each team member who is participating.
	+ This field will help us identify team members who are actively contributing to the team.
* **Number of Issues Reported**: Enter the total number of issues reported for the team.
	+ This field will help us understand the volume of issues reported for the team.
* **Number of Issues Resolved**: Enter the total number of issues resolved for the team.
	+ This field will help us track the progress made in resolving issues.
* **Total Score**: Enter the total score for the team.
	+ This field will help us assess the team's overall score.
* **Report Date**: Select the date of the report.
	+ This field will help us understand when the report was generated.
* **Report Time**: Select the time of the report.
	+ This field will help us understand when the report was generated.
* **Team Email**: Enter the team's email address.
	+ This field will help us contact the team for further information.
* **Team Phone**: Enter the team's phone number.
	+ This field will help us contact the team for further information.
* **Additional Note for Team**: Add any additional notes about the team.
	+ This field will help us capture any extra information about the team.
* **Quality KPIs**: Select the quality KPIs (Good or Poor) for the team.
	+ This field will help us assess the team's quality metrics.
