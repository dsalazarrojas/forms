# Menu Popularity Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you collect and analyze data on menu popularity. It consists of four pages: Main Page, Menu Item Form, Submit Form, and Confirm Form. Please use this guide to navigate each page and submit your report.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start on the **Main Page**.
2. Click on the "Menu Item Form" link to proceed to the next page.
3. On the **Menu Item Form**, fill out the required field with information about the menu item.
4. Click on the "Submit Form" link to submit your report.
5. Review and confirm your submission on the **Confirm Form** page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Menu Item Form** (Menu Item Form, `text`, required): This field is where you enter the name of the menu item you are reporting on.
* **Menu Item Form** (Menu Item Form, `text`, required): This field is not present twice in the original YAML, it seems to be a duplicate. If you intended to have two separate fields for menu item information, please clarify. Assuming it's a single field, it's for entering additional details about the menu item.
* **Submit Form** (Submit Form, `text`, required): This field is for submitting your report.
* **Confirm Form** (Confirm Form, `text`, required): This field is to confirm your submission.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the "Menu Item Form" field accurately to provide valuable feedback.
* Review your submission before confirming to ensure everything is correct.
