# Fashion Retail Customer Service Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fashion Retail Customer Service Report is a form used to gather detailed information about customer service incidents in the fashion retail sector. This report is used to record customer complaints, provide resolutions, and track employee involvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Customer Service Incident**: This section is for the main incident that the customer service team member is reporting. Enter a brief description of the incident.
2. **Date**: Enter the date the incident occurred.
3. **Time**: Enter the time the incident occurred (optional).
4. **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee who handled the customer's issue.
5. **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the customer involved in the incident.
6. **Description**: Enter a detailed description of the incident, including relevant details such as the issue and resolution.
7. **Customer Email**: Enter the customer's email (if available).
8. **Customer Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number (if available).
9. **Issue Category**: Select the category that best describes the issue (e.g., "Order Issue", "Product Return", etc.).
10. **Issue Description**: Enter a detailed description of the issue.
11. **Store Manager Name**: Enter the name of the store manager involved in the incident.
12. **Customer Service Resolution**: Select all applicable resolutions used to resolve the customer's issue (e.g., "Refund", "Replacement", etc.).
13. **Customer Service Coaching**: Select all applicable coaching actions taken by the customer service team member (e.g., "Verbal Warning", "Written Warning", etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Service Incident**: A brief description of the customer's complaint or issue.
* **Date**: The date the incident occurred.
* **Time**: The time the incident occurred (if applicable).
* **Employee Name**: The name of the employee who handled the customer's issue.
* **Customer Name**: The name of the customer involved in the incident.
* **Description**: A detailed description of the incident, including the issue and resolution.
* **Customer Email**: The customer's email address (if available).
* **Customer Phone**: The customer's phone number (if available).
* **Issue Category**: A selection of the category that best describes the issue (e.g., "Order Issue", "Product Return", etc.).
* **Issue Description**: A detailed description of the issue.
* **Store Manager Name**: The name of the store manager involved in the incident.
* **Customer Service Resolution**: Selection of all resolutions used to resolve the customer's issue (e.g., "Refund", "Replacement", etc.).
* **Customer Service Coaching**: Selection of coaching actions taken by the customer service team member (e.g., "Verbal Warning", "Written Warning", etc.).

## Tips
* Ensure you fill out each field accurately and thoroughly, as this information will be used to improve customer service and resolve future incidents.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to your customer service guidelines and policies.
* Remember to keep your descriptions clear and concise, providing enough detail for the customer service team to understand the incident.
