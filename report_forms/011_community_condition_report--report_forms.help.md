# Community Condition Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Community Condition Report form is designed to gather information about community issues or problems reported by residents. This form is used to collect data on the location, type, severity, and other relevant details of these issues, which helps community administrators prioritize and address them effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your full name as the reporter in the "Reporter Name" field.
2. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" field.
3. Enter the date you are submitting this report in the "Date of Report" field.
4. Specify the location of the issue in the "Location of Issue" field.
5. Select the primary category of the issue in the "Type of Issue" field.
6. Choose the severity level of the issue from the "Severity Level" options.
7. Describe the issue in detail in the "Detailed Description" field.
8. (Optional) If the issue poses an immediate safety hazard, select "True" in the "Is This a Safety Hazard" field.
9. Specify who or what is affected by the issue in the "Area Affected" field.
10. Indicate how long the issue has been present in the "Duration of Issue" field.
11. If you have reported this issue before, select "True" in the "Have You Reported This Before" field.
12. (Optional) If applicable, enter the date of the previous report in the "Previous Report Date" field.
13. Suggest a recommended action to address the issue in the "Recommended Action" field.
14. (Optional) Enter any additional comments or information in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reporter Name (Reporter Name)**: Enter your full name as the person reporting the issue.
* **Contact Information (Contact Information)**: Provide your contact phone number or email address.
* **Date of Report (Date of Report)**: Enter the date you are submitting this report.
* **Location of Issue (Location of Issue)**: Specify the exact address or location where the issue is occurring.
* **Type of Issue (Type of Issue)**: Select the primary category of the issue from the provided options.
* **Severity Level (Severity Level)**: Choose the level of severity from the provided options.
* **Detailed Description (Detailed Description)**: Provide a detailed description of the issue.
* **Is This a Safety Hazard (Is This a Safety Hazard)**: Indicate whether the issue poses an immediate safety hazard.
* **Area Affected (Area Affected)**: Specify who or what is affected by the issue.
* **Duration of Issue (Duration of Issue)**: Indicate how long the issue has been present.
* **Have You Reported This Before (Have You Reported This Before)**: Indicate if you have reported this issue before.
* **Previous Report Date (Previous Report Date)**: Enter the date of the previous report if applicable.
* **Recommended Action (Recommended Action)**: Suggest a recommended action to address the issue.
* **Additional Comments (Additional Comments)**: Enter any other relevant information or comments.

## Tips

* Please be as detailed as possible when describing the issue in the "Detailed Description" field.
* If you have photos or other relevant attachments, please describe them in the "Photo Description" field.
* If you've reported the issue before, please indicate that in the "Have You Reported This Before" field.
* Ensure that you're answering each question truthfully and providing accurate information.
* If you have any additional comments or information, please enter them in the "Additional Comments" field.
