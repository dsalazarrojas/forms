# Property Market Analysis Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and report property market analysis information from real estate professionals, such as property managers, appraisers, inspectors, and agents. The purpose of this form is to streamline the reporting process and ensure consistent data collection. By completing this form, you will be able to provide detailed analysis of properties, including their features, price ranges, and market status. This information will be used for internal reporting and decision-making purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of property (Residential, Commercial, or Industrial) and its subtype (House, Condo, Townhouse, or Apartment).
2. Choose the property's price range and select multiple features that apply (Parking, Swimming Pool, Gym, or View).
3. Enter the report date and report time.
4. Provide the property manager's name, contact number, and email (if applicable).
5. Enter the agent's name and organisation (if applicable).
6. Enter the appraiser's name, license number, contact number, and email (if applicable).
7. Enter the property inspector's name, license number, contact number, and email (if applicable).
8. Add any additional report notes and upload any relevant attachment.
9. Enter the report submission date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Address**: Enter the property's address, which will help us identify the location of the property for analysis.
* **Property Type**: Select the type of property (Residential, Commercial, or Industrial) to determine its market classification.
* **Property Subtype**: Choose the subtype of the property (House, Condo, Townhouse, or Apartment) for more specific market analysis.
* **Property Status**: Indicate the property's current status (Active, Sold, or Leased) to understand its market position.
* **Property Price Range**: Select the price range of the property ($100,000-$200,000, $200,000-$500,000, $500,000-$1,000,000, or $1,000,000-$2,000,000).
* **Property Features**: Select multiple features that apply to the property (Parking, Swimming Pool, Gym, or View).
* **Report Date**: Enter the date of the report.
* **Report Time**: Enter the time of the report.
* **Property Manager Name**: Enter the name of the property manager (if applicable).
* **Property Manager Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of the property manager (if applicable).
* **Property Manager Email**: Enter the email of the property manager (if applicable).
* **Agent Name**: Enter the name of the agent (if applicable).
* **Agent Organisation**: Enter the organisation of the agent (if applicable).
* **Appraiser Name**: Enter the name of the appraiser (if applicable).
* **Appraiser License Number**: Enter the license number of the appraiser (if applicable).
* **Appraiser Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of the appraiser (if applicable).
* **Appraiser Email**: Enter the email of the appraiser (if applicable).
* **Property Inspector Name**: Enter the name of the property inspector (if applicable).
* **Property Inspector License Number**: Enter the license number of the property inspector (if applicable).
* **Property Inspector Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of the property inspector (if applicable).
* **Property Inspector Email**: Enter the email of the property inspector (if applicable).
* **Report Notes**: Add any additional report notes for further analysis.
* **Report Attachment**: Upload any relevant attachment for the report.
* **Report Submission Date**: Enter the date of the report submission.
* **Report Submission Time**: Enter the time of the report submission.

Note: All fields marked as required will be mandatory to fill out for a successful submission.
