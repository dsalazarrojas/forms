# Heamatology Monthly Reports - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Heamatology Monthly Reports form is used to record and report on patient information and lab test results. This form is designed to help hematologists keep track of patient data and provide a comprehensive overview of patient health status. It is essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that patient information is up-to-date and reliable.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's first name in the **first_name** field.
2. Provide the patient's date of birth in the **date_of_birth** field.
3. For each lab test, select either **Yes** or **No** from the **lab_tests** field.
4. Enter the patient's address in the **patient_address** field.
5. Enter the doctor's name in the **doctor_name** field.
6. Select the date for the report in the **report_date** field.
7. Enter any lab results in the **lab_results** field.
8. Add any additional medical notes in the **medical_notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name**: A text field for the patient's first name. This is a required field.
* **date_of_birth**: A date field for the patient's date of birth. This is a required field.
* **lab_tests**: A select multiple field where you can choose whether a patient has undergone specific lab tests (Yes/No).
* **patient_address**: A text field for the patient's address. This field is not required.
* **doctor_name**: A text field for the doctor's name. This field is not required.
* **report_date**: A date field for the report date. This field is not required.
* **lab_results**: A text field for any lab results. This field is not required.
* **medical_notes**: A text field for any additional medical notes. This field is not required.

## Tips
* Make sure to accurately fill in all required fields (first name and date of birth).
* Double-check the selected lab tests to ensure they are up-to-date.
* Enter the patient's address and doctor's name if applicable.
* Select a date for the report that reflects the current month.
* Enter any relevant lab results or medical notes to provide a comprehensive patient report.
