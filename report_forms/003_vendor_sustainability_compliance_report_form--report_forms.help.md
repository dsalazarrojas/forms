# Vendor Sustainability Compliance Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from vendors about their sustainability practices and compliance with certain standards. It is intended for vendors who have been identified as needing to provide sustainability-related information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete each field on the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Review each page carefully before submitting the form.
3. Make sure to select the correct options and provide the necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Main Page
* **Title**: This is the title of the form, which you cannot change.
*   **Hint**: This field is not editable and displays the form title.

### Supplier Information
* **Supplier Information** (`supplier_information_page`, text, optional): Please enter any relevant information about your company or organization that we may need to know for follow-up questions or issues.

### Sustainability Practices
* **Sustainability Practices** (`sustainability_practices_page`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the sustainability practices that your company follows, such as "Yes" if you have a recycling program.

### Certifications and Documents
* **Certifications and Documents** (`certifications_documents_page`, select_one, optional): Please select "Yes" if you have any certifications or documents related to sustainability, such as ISO 14001.

### Reporting Period
* **Reporting Period** (`reporting_period_page`, date, optional): Select the reporting period for this form. This is the date range during which you are reporting your sustainability practices.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info_page`, text, optional): Please provide contact information for follow-up questions or issues, such as a phone number and email address.

### Review and Submit
* **Review and Submit** (`review_and_submit_page`, text, optional): This is the final step to review and submit your sustainability report. Make sure to review all the information you have entered carefully before submitting the form.
