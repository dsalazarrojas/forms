# Data Breach Enquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Data Breach Enquiry Form is designed to report and document data breaches that have occurred within the organization. This form helps to gather information about the breach, including the description of the incident, the date and time it occurred, the location of the breach, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the "Report A Data Breach" question. If you are reporting a data breach, select the corresponding option from the dropdown list. If you are not reporting a data breach, select "No".
2. Describe the data breach in the "Description Of Breach" field. Please provide as much detail as possible about the incident.
3. Enter the date and time of the breach in the "Date Of Breach" field. This should be in the format of dd/mm/yyyy hh:mm:ss.
4. Enter the location of the breach in the "Location Of Breach" field. This can include the building, room, or specific area where the breach occurred.
5. Enter the name of the person who reported the breach in the "Reported By" field.
6. If you are reporting an internal incident, select "Yes" or "No" in the "Type Of Report" field.
7. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Report A Data Breach** (`data_breach_enquiry_form`, select_multiple, required): Answer this question to indicate whether a data breach has occurred.
* **Description Of Breach** (`description_of_breach`, text, required): Describe the data breach in detail, including what happened and what was affected.
* **Date Of Breach** (`date_of_breach`, date, required): Enter the date and time of the breach in the format of dd/mm/yyyy hh:mm:ss.
* **Location Of Breach** (`location_of_breach`, text, required): Enter the location where the breach occurred.
* **Reported By** (`reported_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the person who reported the breach.
* **Type Of Report** (`report_type`, select_one, required): Indicate whether the incident is internal or external.
