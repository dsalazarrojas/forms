# Hazard Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hazard Assessment Form is used to document and record hazards identified in the workplace. This form is a tool for employees and supervisors to communicate and record potential hazards, assess their level of severity, and take corrective actions to mitigate risks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide the address of the workplace where the hazard was identified.
2.  Enter the name of the supervisor who is overseeing the assessment.
3.  List all hazards identified in the workplace.
4.  Select the level of hazard (Low, Medium, or High) based on the severity of the hazard.
5.  Specify the type of hazard (if applicable).
6.  Describe the corrective action taken or planned.
7.  Record the date and time the assessment was conducted.
8.  Enter the employee's name and ID number (if applicable).
9.  Provide the employee's job title and department (if applicable).
10. Enter the supervisor's phone number and email (if applicable).
11. Enter the supervisor's address (if applicable).
12. Record the person who reviewed the assessment.
13. Record the date and time the assessment was reviewed.
14. Enter the workplace location (if different from the workplace address).
15. Enter any notes about the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Address of Workplace**: Enter the address of the location where the hazard was identified.
*   **Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of the person overseeing the assessment.
*   **Hazards Identified**: List all hazards identified in the workplace.
*   **Hazard Level**: Select the level of the hazard (Low, Medium, or High) based on its severity.
*   **Hazard Type**: Enter the type of hazard (if applicable).
*   **Corrective Action**: Describe the action taken or planned to mitigate the hazard.
*   **Review Date**: Record the date the assessment was reviewed.
*   **Review Time**: Record the time the assessment was reviewed.
*   **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee who identified the hazard.
*   **Employee ID Number**: Enter the employee's ID number (if applicable).
*   **Job Title**: Enter the employee's job title (if applicable).
*   **Department**: Enter the department where the employee works (if applicable).
*   **Supervisor Phone**: Enter the supervisor's phone number (if applicable).
*   **Supervisor Email**: Enter the supervisor's email (if applicable).
*   **Supervisor Address**: Enter the supervisor's address (if applicable).
*   **Reviewed by**: Record the name of the person who reviewed the assessment.
*   **Date**: Record the date the assessment was recorded.
*   **Time**: Record the time the assessment was recorded.
*   **Workplace**: Enter the name of the workplace (if different from the workplace address).
*   **Address of Workplace**: Enter the address of the workplace.
*   **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the assessment.
