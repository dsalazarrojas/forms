## Daily Operations Log For Crews - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Operations Log For Crews form is used to track and record daily operations, shifts, and events for crews. This form is used to gather information about each crew's activities, notes, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Shift** number, which identifies the shift being logged.
2. Enter the **Operational Note**, a brief description of the operations or events that occurred during the shift.
3. Enter the **Crew ID**, a unique identifier for the crew.
4. Enter the **Supervisor ID**, a unique identifier for the supervisor.
5. Enter the **Shift Notes**, any additional notes about the shift.
6. Choose the **Weather** for the day.
7. Enter the **Notes** for any additional comments or observations.
8. Choose the **Date** for the shift.
9. Choose the **Time** for the shift.
10. Select the **Location** of the operations or events.
11. Enter the **Site ID**, a unique identifier for the site.
12. Enter the **Department ID**, a unique identifier for the department.
13. Enter the **Site Name**, the name of the site.
14. Enter the **Shift ID**, a unique identifier for the shift, if applicable.
15. Enter the **Operational Note**, a brief description of the operations or events.
16. Enter the **Weather ID**, a unique identifier for the weather.
17. Enter the **Site Location**, the location of the site.
18. Select the **Department** for the shift, if applicable.
19. Enter the **Crew Name**, the name of the crew.
20. Enter the **Site**, the location of the site.
21. Enter the **Notes**, any additional comments or observations.
22. Enter the **Supervisor Name**, the name of the supervisor.
23. Select the **Crew**, the crew that worked the shift.
24. Enter the **Site Shift ID**, a unique identifier for the site shift, if applicable.
25. Select the **Site Department**, the department for the site, if applicable.

* **Shift** (1, number, required): Enter the shift number.
* **Operational Note** (2, text, required): A brief description of the operations or events.
* **Crew ID** (3, number, required): A unique identifier for the crew.
* **Supervisor ID** (4, number, required): A unique identifier for the supervisor.
* **Shift Notes** (5, text, optional): Additional notes about the shift.
* **Weather** (6, select_one, optional): Choose the weather for the day.
* **Notes** (7, text, optional): Additional comments or observations.
* **Date** (8, date, required): Choose the date for the shift.
* **Time** (9, time, required): Choose the time for the shift.
* **Location** (10, select_multiple, optional): Select the location of the operations or events.
* **Site ID** (11, number, required): A unique identifier for the site.
* **Department ID** (12, number, required): A unique identifier for the department.
* **Site Name** (13, text, optional): The name of the site.
