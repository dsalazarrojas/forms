# Daily Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Daily Report Form is designed to help team members document their daily activities, progress, and any issues or challenges they faced. This form will help management and team leaders track and analyze daily performance and address any challenges or obstacles that may arise.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Daily Report Form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the date in the "date" field.
2. Select the tasks you completed by checking the corresponding boxes in the "tasks_completed" field.
3. In the "tasks_in_progress" field, enter any tasks that you are currently working on.
4. In the "issues_encountered" field, briefly describe any issues or problems you encountered during the day.
5. In the "challenges_faced" field, describe any significant challenges or obstacles you faced.
6. If you have any comments or additional information, enter them in the "comments" field.
7. Finally, enter your name or identifier in the "submitted_by" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **date** (date): Enter the current date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **tasks_completed** (select multiple): Select all tasks you completed today.
- **tasks_in_progress** (text): Enter any tasks that you are currently working on.
- **issues_encountered** (text): Briefly describe any issues or problems you encountered during the day.
- **challenges_faced** (text): Describe any significant challenges or obstacles you faced.
- **comments** (text): Enter any additional comments or information.
- **submitted_by** (text): Enter your name or identifier.
