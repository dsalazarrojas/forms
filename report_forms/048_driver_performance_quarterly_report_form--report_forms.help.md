# driver_performance_quarterly_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to monitor and evaluate driver performance on a quarterly basis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date for the report using the "driver_performance_quarterly_report" field.
2. Enter the name of the supervisor who evaluated the driver in the "supervisor_name" field.
3. Enter the email address of the supervisor in the "supervisor_email" field.
4. Enter the name of the driver being evaluated in the "driver_name" field.
5. Provide feedback to the driver by filling in the "feedback_to_driver" field.
6. Assess the driver's overall performance by choosing "Yes" or "No" in the "overall_performance" field.
7. Indicate if the driver engaged in any "dangerous_driving" behaviors by choosing "Yes" or "No".
8. Record the distance the driver followed in the "following_distance" field.
9. Record the distance the driver left in the "leaving_distance" field.
10. Record the time the driver arrived at their destination in the "arrival_time" field.
11. Provide notes on the driver's performance in the "driver_notes" field.
12. Add any additional comments about the driver's performance in the "comments" field.
13. Record the location the driver left at the end of their shift in the "leaving_location" field.
14. Sign the form with your official supervisor signature in the "supervisor_signature" field.
15. Sign the form with the driver's signature in the "driver_signature" field.
16. Set the date the report was submitted using the "date_submitted" field.
17. Set the time the report was submitted using the "time_submitted" field.
18. Record the assigned tool used for the report in the "assigned_tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **driver_performance_quarterly_report** (date): This field is used to select the date for the report.
* **supervisor_name** (text): Enter the name of the supervisor who evaluated the driver.
* **supervisor_email** (email): Enter the email address of the supervisor.
* **driver_name** (text): Enter the name of the driver being evaluated.
* **feedback_to_driver** (text): Provide feedback to the driver on their performance.
* **overall_performance** (select one): Assess the driver's overall performance.
* **dangerous_driving** (select one): Indicate if the driver engaged in any dangerous driving behaviors.
* **following_distance** (number): Record the distance the driver followed.
* **leaving_distance** (number): Record the distance the driver left.
* **arrival_time** (time): Record the time the driver arrived at their destination.
* **driver_notes** (text): Provide notes on the driver's performance.
* **comments** (text): Add any additional comments about the driver's performance.
* **leaving_location** (text): Record the location the driver left at the end of their shift.
* **supervisor_signature** (text): Sign the form with your official supervisor signature.
* **driver_signature** (text): Sign the form with the driver's signature.
* **date_submitted** (date): Set the date the report was submitted.
* **time_submitted** (time): Set the time the report was submitted.
* **assigned_tool** (text): Record the assigned tool used for the report.
* **comments** (text): Add any additional comments about the report.
