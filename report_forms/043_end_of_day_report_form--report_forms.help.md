# End Of Day Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The End Of Day Report Form is a daily report that employees use to document their work hours, breaks, and activities. This form helps management track employee productivity and ensure compliance with labor laws.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Work Summary**: This field is for providing a brief overview of your work activities for the day.
2. **Employee**: Select 'Yes' if you worked on this day, 'No' otherwise.
3. **Date Worked**: Enter the date you worked.
4. **Start Time**: Enter the time you started work.
5. **End Time**: Enter the time you ended work.
6. **Work Hours**: Enter the total number of hours you worked.
7. **Break Durations**: Select 'Yes' if you took a break, 'No' otherwise.
8. **Work/Break**: Select 'Yes' for work and 'Yes' for break if you engaged in both, 'No' for work only, 'No' for break only, or other combinations of 'Yes' and 'No' for different activities.
9. **Notes**: Provide any additional comments or notes about your work activities.
10. **Submitted By**: Select 'Yes' if you submit this form, 'No' otherwise.
