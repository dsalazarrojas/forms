# Patient Handover Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Handover Form is used to gather information about a patient being transferred to another healthcare professional or department. This form serves as a standardized template to ensure that critical patient information is accurately and efficiently communicated during the handover process.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the relevant patient details from the dropdown menu.
2.  Enter the patient's name in the text field.
3.  Enter the patient's date of birth (DOB) using the date picker.
4.  Choose the patient's gender from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Details**: 
   Select the correct title (Mr, Mrs, Dr, Miss, Ms, Rev) to match the patient's identity.
   This field is used to identify the patient's title.
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name.
   This field is used to enter the patient's name.
* **Patient DOB**: Enter the patient's date of birth using the date picker.
   This field is used to enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Patient Gender**: Choose the patient's gender (Male, Female).
   This field is used to identify the patient's gender.
* **Patient Handover Form Section**: This is a placeholder section for notes or other information related to the handover process.
   This field is not required to be filled out.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that the receiving healthcare professional or department receives all the necessary information.
* If the form is not complete, it may lead to delays or errors during the handover process.
