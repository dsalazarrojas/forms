> # Scuba Diving Medical Clearance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect medical clearance information for scuba diving activities. It assesses an individual's medical history, physical examination, and diving experience to ensure they are fit to dive safely.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully and answer each question truthfully.
2. Complete all required fields (indicated by asterisk \*).
3. Enter all information in a clear and legible format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **intro** (1): Introduce yourself and provide a brief medical history.
	+ Please provide a short introduction about your medical history, including any relevant medical conditions or allergies.
* **medical_history** (2): Describe your medical history.
	+ Provide a detailed account of any medical conditions, allergies, or surgeries you have had.
* **medication** (3): List any medications you are currently taking.
	+ Enter the name and dosage of any medications you are currently taking.
* **medical_condition** (4): List any medical conditions you have.
	+ Provide a list of any medical conditions you have, including any current or past conditions.
* **physical_exam** (5): Describe your physical examination results.
	+ Provide a detailed account of any physical examination results, including blood pressure, heart rate, etc.
* **diving_experience** (6): Describe your diving experience.
	+ Provide a detailed account of your diving experience, including any certifications or training you have received.
* **medical_certificate** (7): Attach any relevant medical certificates.
	+ Provide any relevant medical certificates, such as a doctor's note or medical clearance letter.
* **emergency_contacts** (8): List your emergency contact information.
	+ Enter the name, phone number, and address of your emergency contact person.
* **emergency_contact_info** (9): Provide any additional emergency contact information.
	+ Provide any additional information about your emergency contact person, such as their medical history or contact numbers.
* **emergency_contact_relationship** (10): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
	+ Explain your relationship with your emergency contact, such as their role in your life.
* **dive_site** (11): Describe the dive site.
	+ Provide a detailed account of the dive site, including location and conditions.
* **dive_date** (12): Enter the date of the dive.
	+ Enter the date of the dive in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **dive_time** (13): Enter the time of the dive.
	+ Enter the time of the dive in HH:MM format.
* **dive_location** (14): Describe the dive location.
	+ Provide a detailed account of the dive location, including coordinates or a map link.
* **dive_depth** (15): Enter the maximum depth of the dive.
	+ Enter the maximum depth of the dive in meters.
* **dive_time_limit** (16): Enter the maximum dive time.
	+ Enter the maximum time of the dive in hours.
* **emergency_procedures** (17): Describe any emergency procedures.
	+ Provide a detailed account of any emergency procedures that will be used during the dive.
* **emergency_procedure** (18): Describe any emergency procedure.
	+ Provide a detailed account of any emergency procedure that will be used during the dive.
* **emergency_return_procedure** (19): Describe the emergency return procedure.
	+ Provide a detailed account of any emergency return procedures that will be used after the dive.
* **emergency_return_procedure_info** (20): Provide any additional emergency return procedure information.
	+ Provide any additional information about the emergency return procedure.
* **medical_condition_2** (21): Describe any additional medical conditions.
	+ Provide a detailed account of any additional medical conditions.
* **medical_condition_3** (22): Describe any additional medical conditions.
	+ Provide a detailed account of any additional medical conditions.
* **medical_condition_4** (23): Describe any additional medical conditions.
	+ Provide a detailed account of any additional medical conditions.
* **medical_certificate_2** (24): Attach any additional medical certificates.
	+ Provide any additional medical certificates, such as a doctor's note or medical clearance letter.
* **medical_certificate_3** (25): Attach any additional medical certificates.
	+ Provide any additional medical certificates, such as a doctor's note or medical clearance letter.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
* Review and double-check your answers before submitting the form.
* If unsure about any question, ask your diving instructor or dive master for guidance.
