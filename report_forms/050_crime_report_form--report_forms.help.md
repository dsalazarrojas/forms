# Crime Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Crime Report Form is a tool used to collect standardized information about reported crimes. It is designed to gather data that can aid in the investigation and processing of reported incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review the form questions carefully and ensure you are submitting information related to a reported crime.
2.  Begin by selecting the options for each field based on the incident.
3.  Fill out all required fields, marked as such.
4.  For fields not marked as required, provide information if it is relevant to the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Date of the crime** (`crime_report_form_date_of_crime`): Enter the date of the crime in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time of the crime** (`crime_report_form_time_of_crime`): Enter the time of the crime using a 24-hour clock (e.g., 14:30 for 2:30 PM).
* **Location of the crime** (`crime_report_form_location_of_crime`): Provide a detailed description of the location where the crime occurred, including the address or nearest landmark.
* **Suspects** (`crime_report_form_suspects`): Indicate if there were any suspects involved in the crime. Select 'Yes' if there is at least one suspect.
* **Victims** (`crime_report_form_victims`): Indicate if there were any victims involved in the crime. Select 'Yes' if there is at least one victim.
* **Offenders** (`crime_report_form_offenders`): Indicate if there were any offenders involved in the crime. Select 'Yes' if there is at least one offender.
* **Injuries sustained by victims** (`crime_report_form_injuries`): Describe any injuries sustained by victims during the crime.
* **Damage caused by the crime** (`crime_report_form_damage`): Describe any damage caused by the crime, including the extent and nature of the damage.
* **Evidence collected** (`crime_report_form_evidence`): List any evidence collected related to the crime.
* **Notes** (`crime_report_form_notes`): Add any additional information that may be relevant to the investigation.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when providing information about the crime.
* If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to ask for assistance.
* Ensure all fields are filled out completely and accurately.
