# Department Recap Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Department Recap Form is designed to collect essential information from department teams, facilitating a comprehensive understanding of their performance, challenges, and achievements. This form helps the management and leadership team to make informed decisions and provide targeted support to their departments.

## How To Complete This Form

- **Step 1:** Review the form carefully before starting.
- **Step 2:** Ensure you're completing the form for your department and not for another department or external entity.
- **Step 3:** Complete the form with accurate and honest answers.
- **Step 4:** Submit the form when finished, ensuring to keep a record of your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **prophets**: A field to note any recent updates or significant events within your department. This can be a single point or a brief description.
* **departmental**: Enter a text-based description of your department's performance metrics, providing a clear and concise overview.
* **key**: Indicate your department's key achievements, which can be 'Yes' if you have any notable accomplishments or 'No' otherwise.
* **reporting**: Provide a short description of your department's internal reporting practices, detailing how you communicate your results and progress to your team and management.
* **transparency**: Enter a text-based description of how effectively you maintain transparency within your department, outlining methods and tools used.
* **boost**: If you've experienced a productivity boost, enter the number of percentage points (e.g., 10, 20, 30). This helps to gauge the impact of any initiatives taken.
* **system**: Select the internal reporting system your department uses for reporting and communication. This will help in identifying the tools and methods used across departments for comparison and improvement.
* **kpis**: Describe your department's key performance indicators (KPIs) in the provided text field, ensuring to be specific and concise.
* **system**: Select only one internal reporting system your department utilizes for reporting purposes. This helps in tracking the diversity of systems used across departments. 

## Tips
- Please be honest and accurate when filling this form.
- Ensure your answers are specific and concise.
- If unsure, reach out to your department head or management for clarification.
- This form is a valuable resource for departmental insights; your input contributes to a better understanding of your team's performance.
