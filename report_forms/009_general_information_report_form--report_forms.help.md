# General Information Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and store general information about reported issues, making it easier to track and analyze them. It's intended for internal use by help desk or support teams.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the general information fields (page 1) with a brief description of the issue.
2. Provide a detailed description of the issue (field "Issue Description") on the second page.
3. Enter the date and time when the issue occurred (fields "Issue Date" and "Issue Time" on the third page).
4. Specify the location where the issue occurred (field "Issue Location" on the fourth page).
5. Categorize the issue by department and category (fields "Issue Department" and "Issue Category" on the fifth page).
6. Determine the issue's priority level (field "Issue Priority" on the sixth page).
7. Check the issue's status (field "Issue Status" on the seventh page).
8. Enter your contact information (field "Reporter" on the last page).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information (Page 1)**: This section is for entering a brief description of the issue. You can include any relevant context or background information to help us understand the problem.
* **Issue Description (Page 2)**: Provide a detailed explanation of the issue, including what happened and any steps you took to resolve it.
* **Issue Date (Page 3)**: Enter the date when the issue occurred. This helps us track the issue's timeline.
* **Issue Time (Page 3)**: Enter the time when the issue occurred. If you're unsure, you can leave this field blank.
* **Issue Location (Page 4)**: Specify the location where the issue occurred. This could be a physical location or a system/resource identifier.
* **Issue Department (Page 5)**: Select the department associated with the issue. This helps us route the issue to the correct team for resolution.
* **Issue Category (Page 5)**: Choose one or multiple categories that apply to the issue. This helps us further categorize and track similar issues.
* **Issue Priority (Page 6)**: Select the priority level of the issue. This determines how quickly we respond and resolve the issue.
* **Issue Status (Page 6)**: Check the status of the issue (Active or Inactive). This helps us track issue progress and resolution.
* **Reporter (Page 7)**: Enter your contact information for follow-up or additional questions. This is optional but recommended for context and communication.
