# Employee End Of Day Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to be completed by employees at the end of their workday to report their activities, work hours, and any other relevant information. The form is used to track employee work hours, ensure accuracy in reporting, and provide a record of completed activities.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for your report, a brief description of your activities, and select the category for this report (e.g. "Report Forms" or "Other Forms"). This will help the management team understand the nature of your report.
2. Enter your name as it should be displayed on the report.
3. If necessary, enter your employee ID for identification purposes.
4. Enter the start and end times of your workday to ensure accurate time tracking.
5. Enter the number of hours you worked during your shift.
6. Add any additional notes about your activities or tasks completed during the day.
7. If you're submitting the report, select "Yes" or "No" to confirm.
8. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief title for your report.
* **Form Category** (`form_category`, select_one, required): Select the category for this report from the dropdown list.
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your activities.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as it should be displayed on the report.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required/optional): Enter your employee ID for identification purposes.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the start time of your workday.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the end time of your workday.
* **Work Time** (`work_time`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of hours you worked during your shift.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes about your activities or tasks completed during the day.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_multiple, required/optional): Confirm or decline submitting the report.
* **Email** (`employee_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`employee_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure accurate time tracking and reporting.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
* This form is intended for reporting your daily activities, so be as detailed as possible in your descriptions and notes.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us for assistance.
