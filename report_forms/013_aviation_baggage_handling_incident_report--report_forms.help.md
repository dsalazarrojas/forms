# Aviation Baggage Handling Incident Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to report and document incidents related to aviation baggage handling, ensuring that all relevant information is collected and documented in a structured and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your full name and email address as the reporting agent.
2. Enter the airline code (e.g. AA, BA, DL) related to the incident.
3. Enter the flight number associated with the incident.
4. Provide the details of the incident in the "Incident Details" section.
5. Enter the date and time when the incident was identified.
6. Select the main category of the incident (e.g. Damaged Baggage, Delayed or Mishandled, etc.).
7. Enter the unique tag number of the affected bag.
8. Specify the location where the incident was identified (e.g. Check-in Counter, Aircraft Loading, etc.).
9. Describe the incident in detail, including its current state.
10. If applicable, provide the physical description of the affected bag.
11. Enter the estimated damage value (in USD or local currency).
12. Select all immediate actions taken to resolve the issue (e.g. creating a Property Irregularity Report (PIR), rerouting baggage, etc.).
13. Choose the current case status (e.g. Open - Investigation Pending, Resolved - Baggage Located/Delivered, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reporting Agent Full Name**: Enter your full name as the person reporting the incident.
* **Agent Email Address**: Enter your email address for further contact or follow-up.
* **Airline Code**: Enter the airline code (e.g. AA, BA, DL) associated with the incident.
* **Flight Number**: Enter the flight number related to the incident.
* **Incident Details**: Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Incident Date**: Enter the date when the incident was identified.
* **Incident Time**: Enter the approximate time when the incident was identified.
* **Type of Incident**: Select the main category of the incident (e.g. Damaged Baggage, Delayed or Mishandled, etc.).
* **Baggage Tag Number**: Enter the unique identifier for the affected bag.
* **Point of Discovery**: Specify the location where the incident was identified (e.g. Check-in Counter, Aircraft Loading, etc.).
* **Detailed Description of Incident**: Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Physical Description of Baggage**: If applicable, provide the color, make, and model of the affected bag.
* **Estimated Damage Value**: Enter the estimated damage value in USD or local currency.
* **Immediate Actions Taken**: Select all immediate actions taken to resolve the issue (e.g. creating a Property Irregularity Report (PIR), rerouting baggage, etc.).
* **Current Case Status**: Choose the final disposition of this report (e.g. Open - Investigation Pending, Resolved - Baggage Located/Delivered, etc.).
* **Witness Name**: If applicable, enter the name of other staff or customers involved.
