# pediatrics_schedule_error_reporting - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report any scheduling errors that occurred during pediatric care. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to help identify and correct any issues that may have impacted patient care.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Incident Date" field with the date of the scheduling error.
2. Enter the "Incident Time" if applicable.
3. Describe the "Impact on Care" and how the scheduling mistake affected the patient's care.
4. Provide a detailed "Incident Description" of the error and its impact.
5. Identify the "Schedule Error Cause" if known.
6. Specify the "Root Cause" if known.
7. Suggest any "Recommended Correction" to prevent similar mistakes in the future.
8. Enter the "Reported by" information, including title, department, position, and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Schedule Error** (Required): Describe the scheduling error that occurred.
* **Incident Date** (Required): Enter the date when the scheduling error occurred.
* **Incident Time** (Optional): Enter the time when the scheduling error occurred.
* **Impact on Care** (Required): Describe how the scheduling mistake affected the patient's care.
* **Incident Description** (Required): Provide a detailed description of the scheduling error and its impact on care.
* **Schedule Error Cause** (Optional): Identify the cause of the scheduling mistake.
* **Root Cause** (Optional): If known, specify the root cause of the scheduling error.
* **Recommended Correction** (Optional): Suggest any corrections or actions to be taken to prevent similar scheduling errors in the future.
* **Reported by** (Optional): Enter the name, title, department, position, and contact information of the person reporting the error.
* **Report Date** (Required): Enter the date when the report was submitted.
* **Reported by Email** (Optional): Enter the email address of the person reporting the error.
* **Reported by Phone** (Optional): Enter the phone number of the person reporting the error.
* **Reported by Extension** (Optional): Enter the extension of the person reporting the error.
* **Reported by Facsimile** (Optional): Enter the facsimile number of the person reporting the error.
* **Reported by Address** (Optional): Enter the address of the person reporting the error.
* **Reporting Facility** (Optional): Enter the name of the facility where the report is submitted.
* **Reporting Facility Name** (Optional): Enter the name of the facility where the report is submitted.
* **Reporting Facility Address** (Optional): Enter the address of the facility where the report is submitted.
* **Reporting Facility City** (Optional): Enter the city of the facility where the report is submitted.
* **Reporting Facility State** (Optional): Enter the state of the facility where the report is submitted.
* **Reporting Facility Zip** (Optional): Enter the zip code of the facility where the report is submitted.
* **Reporting Facility Phone** (Optional): Enter the phone number of the facility where the report is submitted.
