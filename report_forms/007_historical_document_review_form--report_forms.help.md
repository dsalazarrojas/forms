# Historical Document Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Historical Document Review Form is designed to help reviewers assess the quality and accuracy of historical documents. This form ensures that each review is thorough and transparent, and that no two reviews are identical.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by reading and familiarizing yourself with the document's content and context.
2. Fill out the form with the following information:
	* **Document Title**: A brief title of the document.
	* **Document Date**: The date of the document.
	* **Document Author**: The name of the document author.
	* **Review Rating**: On a scale of 1-5, rate the document's quality and accuracy.
	* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback on the document.
3. Be thorough and clear in your answers, and make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Document Title** (`document_title`, text, optional): Provide a brief title of the document.
* **Document Date** (`document_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the document.
* **Document Author** (`document_author`, text, optional): Enter the name of the document author.
* **Review Rating** (`review_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate the document's quality and accuracy on a scale of 1-5.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback on the document.

## Tips
* Please be objective and unbiased in your review.
* Ensure that you are familiar with the document's content and context before starting the review.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
* You can use this form for any historical document, academic paper, or research material.
