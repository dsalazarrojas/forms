# Free Police Incident Report Template - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help you document and report incidents, accidents, or events that require official attention. It's a flexible template that can be used for various types of incidents, such as police reports, crime reports, incident reports, or even workplace accidents.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form on a computer or mobile device with a web browser.
2. Enter the date of the incident in the "Date" field.
3. Enter the time the incident occurred in the "Time" field.
4. Describe the location where the incident took place in the "Location" field.
5. Provide further details about the incident in the "Further details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the incident in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`. This will help you keep track of when the incident occurred.
* **Time** (`time`, required/optional): Enter the time the incident occurred in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`. This will help you record the exact time of the incident.
* **Location** (`text`, required/optional): Describe the location where the incident happened. This could be a physical address, a building, or a specific room.
* **Further details** (`text`, required/optional): Provide as much detail as possible about what happened, including any circumstances or factors that might be relevant to the incident. Be as specific and objective as you can.

## Tips

* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* Use clear and concise language in your descriptions to ensure your report is easy to understand.
* If you're unsure about any field, you can always ask for help or clarification.
