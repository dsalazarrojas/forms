# COVID 19 Compliance Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID-19 Compliance Report Form is used to track and report on various aspects of COVID-19 compliance within a healthcare facility, including staff and patient vaccination statuses, infection rates, and safety protocols.

## How to Complete This Form
1. Fill in the facility information, including the facility name, address, and facility type.
2. Enter the contact person's details, including name, title, email, and phone number.
3. Select the reporting period for the form (e.g., weekly, monthly, quarterly, or annually).
4. Enter the start and end dates of the reporting period.
5. For staff and patient data, enter the number of staff and patients, respectively, for each category (e.g., fully vaccinated, boosted, medically exempt, and unvaccinated).
6. Report any new staff and patient cases, hospitalizations, and deaths due to COVID-19.
7. Select the current masking policy, screening policy, and PPE availability status.
8. Indicate the current outbreak status.
9. Enter any compliance issues and corrective actions taken.
10. Add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facility Name** (ID: 1): Enter the name of the healthcare facility.
* **Facility Address** (ID: 2): Enter the complete address of the healthcare facility.
* **Facility Type** (ID: 3): Select the type of healthcare facility (e.g., hospital, nursing home, etc.).
* **Contact Person** (ID: 4): Enter the name of the person responsible for reporting.
* **Contact Title** (ID: 5): Enter the title of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (ID: 6): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (ID: 7): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Reporting Period** (ID: 8): Select the reporting period (e.g., weekly, monthly, etc.).
* **Period Start Date** (ID: 9): Enter the start date of the reporting period.
* **Period End Date** (ID: 10): Enter the end date of the reporting period.
* **Report Date** (ID: 11): Enter the date the report was submitted.
* **Total Healthcare Personnel** (ID: 13): Enter the total number of healthcare personnel.
*   **Fully Vaccinated Staff** (ID: 14): Enter the number of staff who have completed the primary series.
*   **Boosted Staff** (ID: 15): Enter the number of staff who have received the booster dose.
*   **Medically Exempt Staff** (ID: 16): Enter the number of staff who are medically exempt from vaccination.
*   **Religiously Exempt Staff** (ID: 17): Enter the number of staff who are religiously exempt from vaccination.
*   **Unvaccinated Staff** (ID: 18): Enter the number of staff who have not received any doses.
*   **Total Patients** (ID: 20): Enter the total number of patients.
*   **Fully Vaccinated Patients** (ID: 21): Enter the number of patients who have completed the primary series.
*   **Boosted Patients** (ID: 22): Enter the number of patients who have received the booster dose (optional).
*   **Medically Exempt Patients** (ID: 23): Enter the number of patients who are medically exempt from vaccination.
*   **Unvaccinated Patients** (ID: 24): Enter the number of patients who have not received any doses.
*   **New Staff Cases** (ID: 26): Report any new positive staff cases this period.
*   **New Patient Cases** (ID: 27): Report any new positive patient cases this period.
*   **Staff Hospitalizations** (ID: 28): Report any staff hospitalizations due to COVID-19.
*   **Patient Hospitalizations** (ID: 29): Report any patient hospitalizations due to COVID-19.
*   **Staff Deaths** (ID: 30): Report any staff deaths due to COVID-19.
*   **Patient Deaths** (ID: 31): Report any patient deaths due to COVID-19.
*   **Masking Policy** (ID: 33): Select the current masking policy.
*   **Screening Policy** (ID: 34): Select the current screening policy.
*   **PPE Availability** (ID: 35): Select the current PPE availability status.
*   **Outbreak Status** (ID: 36): Select the current outbreak status.
*   **Compliance Issues** (ID: 37): Enter any compliance issues.
*   **Corrective Actions** (ID: 38): Enter any corrective actions taken.
*   **Additional Notes** (ID: 39): Enter any additional notes or comments.
*   **Administrator Name** (ID: 40): Enter the name of the facility administrator.
*   **Administrator Signature** (ID: 41): Enter the administrator's signature.
*   **Date Signed** (ID: 42): Enter the date the report was signed.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the facility administrator or contact person.
* Double-check the data for accuracy before submitting the report.
* Keep the report up-to-date and accurate to ensure the best possible response to the COVID-19 pandemic.
