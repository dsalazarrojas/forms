# Agency Mid Year Financial Report - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Agency Mid Year Financial Report is a form used to provide a mid-year financial review for agencies. It is used to report on financial performance from January to June and to provide projections for the remainder of the year.

## How to Complete This Form

1.  Enter the **Agency Name**: This is the name of the agency submitting the report.
2.  Enter the **Reporting Officer Name**: This is the name of the person submitting the report.
3.  Enter the **Submission Date**: This is the date the report is being submitted.
4.  Enter the **Total Revenue Year-to-Date**: This is the total revenue received from January to June.
5.  Enter the **Total Operating Expenses Year-to-Date**: This is the total operating expenses from January to June.
6.  Enter the **Net Profit or Loss**: This is the difference between revenue and operating expenses.
7.  Enter the **Projected Revenue for Remainder of Year**: This is the forecasted revenue for the second half of the year.
8.  Select the **Budget Status**: Choose from the options "Under Budget", "On Budget", "Over Budget" (within 10%), or "Significantly Over Budget".
9.  Enter an **Explanation for Budget Variances**: This is an optional field for providing additional details if the budget was exceeded.
10.  Enter the **Number of Active Client Accounts**: This is the number of active client accounts as of the report date.
11.  Enter the **Number of Full-time Employees**: This is the number of full-time employees as of the report date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Agency Name** (`agency_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the agency submitting the report.
*   **Reporting Officer Name** (`officer_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person submitting the report.
*   **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the report is being submitted.
*   **Total Revenue Year-to-Date** (`total_revenue`, `number`, required): Enter the total revenue received from January to June.
*   **Total Operating Expenses Year-to-Date** (`operating_expenses`, `number`, required): Enter the total operating expenses from January to June.
*   **Net Profit or Loss** (`net_profit_loss`, `number`, required): Enter the difference between revenue and operating expenses.
*   **Projected Revenue for Remainder of Year** (`projected_revenue`, `number`, optional): Enter the forecasted revenue for the second half of the year.
*   **Budget Status** (`budget_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose from the options "Under Budget", "On Budget", "Over Budget" (within 10%), or "Significantly Over Budget".
*   **Explanation for Budget Variances** (`variance_explanation`, `text`, optional): Provide additional details if the budget was exceeded.
*   **Number of Active Client Accounts** (`client_accounts`, `number`, required): Enter the number of active client accounts as of the report date.
*   **Number of Full-time Employees** (`employee_count`, `number`, required): Enter the number of full-time employees as of the report date.
