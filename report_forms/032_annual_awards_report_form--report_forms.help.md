# Annual Awards Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Annual Awards Report Form is a template to collect information from organizations about their awards and recognition programs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your organization's information, including the organization name, department or division name (if applicable), and your name and title.
2. Enter the email address and phone number of the contact person submitting the report.
3. Provide details about the reporting year and the date of submitting the report.
4. Report the total number of awards given, the number of award categories, and the total number of award recipients.
5. Provide details about the awards ceremony, including the date and location (if known).
6. Describe the highlights and memorable moments of the ceremony.
7. Share any feedback or testimonials from award winners.
8. If you have any suggestions or recommendations for next year, add them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, `text`, required): Enter your organization's name.
* **Department or Division Name** (`department_name`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the department or division submitting the report.
* **Reporting Person Name** (`reporting_person_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name.
* **Reporting Person Title** (`reporting_person_title`, `text`, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for contact.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Reporting Year** (`reporting_year`, `number`, required): Enter the year being reported on.
* **Report Submission Date** (`report_submission_date`, `date`, required): Enter today's date for submission.
* **Total Number of Awards Given** (`total_awards_given`, `number`, required): Enter the count of all awards presented.
* **Number of Award Categories** (`number_of_categories`, `number`, required): Enter the count of different categories.
* **Total Number of Award Recipients** (`total_recipients`, `number`, required): Enter the count of people who won awards.
* **Awards Ceremony Date** (`ceremony_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the awards ceremony.
* **Awards Ceremony Location** (`ceremony_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location of the awards ceremony (if known).
* **Number of Attendees** (`attendance_count`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of people who attended the ceremony.
* **Key Highlights of the Ceremony** (`ceremony_highlights`, `text`, optional): Describe the notable moments or achievements of the ceremony.
* **Most Memorable Moments** (`standout_moments`, `text`, optional): Enter any special or significant moments from the ceremony.
* **Impact of Awards on Organization** (`award_impact`, `text`, optional): Explain how the awards impacted your organization.
* **Recipient Feedback or Testimonials** (`recipient_feedback`, `text`, optional): Collect comments from award winners.
* **Recommendations for Next Year** (`future_recommendations`, `text`, optional): Provide any suggestions for improvement for next year's awards program.
