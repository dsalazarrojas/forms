# organic_source_audit_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about an organic source audit report for suppliers and their organic sources. It is a general template and does not specify which type of organic source it is auditing.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide Supplier Information**
	* Fill in the supplier's details on the first page.
2. **Enter Audit Details**
	* Describe the audit performed on the supplier's organic sources.
3. **Document Findings**
	* List any issues or concerns found during the audit.
4. **Submit Supporting Evidence**
	* Attach any relevant documents or photos that support the findings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supplier Information (id: 1)**:
	* Enter the supplier's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Audit Details (id: 2)**:
	* Describe the audit performed, including the date, time, and location.
* **Findings (id: 3)**:
	* List any issues or concerns found during the audit, such as non-compliance with regulations.
* **Supporting Evidence (id: 4)**:
	* Attach any documents, photos, or videos that support the findings.

## Tips

* Make sure to keep the form concise and focused on the required information.
* Use the supporting evidence field to attach relevant documents or photos that support the findings.
* Review the form before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled in.
* If you need to add any additional fields or sections, consider consulting with the form owner or the audit team.
