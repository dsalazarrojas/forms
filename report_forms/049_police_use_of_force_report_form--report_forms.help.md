# Police Use Of Force Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Police Use Of Force Report Form is a critical tool used to document and report incidents where force was used by police officers. This form helps to gather detailed information about the incident, ensuring that all relevant details are captured and reported.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) as you go through the form.
2. Be thorough and accurate when filling out each field, as the information provided will be used to document and report the incident.
3. If you are unsure about any field or require clarification, please don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Police Report** (`police_force_report_form_section_1`, text, optional): This field is used to provide a brief summary of the incident.
* **Force Details** (`police_force_report_form_section_2`, text, optional): This field is used to provide additional details about the incident, such as any specific actions taken by the officer.
* **Officer's Name** (`officer_name`, text, required): This field requires the name of the officer who used force during the incident.
* **Force Used** (`force_used`, select_one, required): This field is used to select the type of force used during the incident.
* **Date Of Occurrence** (`date_of_occurrence`, date, required): This field is used to record the date of the incident.
* **Time Of Occurrence** (`time_of_occurrence`, time, required): This field is used to record the time of the incident.
* **Location Of Occurrence** (`location_of_occurrence`, text, required): This field is used to specify the location where the incident occurred.
* **Description Of Event** (`description_of_event`, text, required): This field is used to provide a detailed description of what occurred during the incident.
* **Witness Statement** (`witness_statement`, text, required): This field is used to capture any witness statements or accounts from individuals who observed the incident.
* **Witness Statement 2** (`witness_statement_2`, text, optional): This field is used to capture additional witness statements or accounts.
* **Witness Statement 3** (`witness_statement_3`, text, optional): This field is used to capture any additional information from witnesses.
* **Police Force Report** (`police_force_report_form_section_3`, text, optional): This field is used to provide any additional comments or information about the incident.
* **Police Force Report** (`police_force_report_form_section_4`, text, optional): This field is used to provide any additional comments or information about the incident.
* **Officer In Charge** (`officer_in_charge`, text, optional): This field is used to capture the name and contact information of the officer in charge of the incident.
* **Officer In Charge Phone** (`officer_in_charge_phone`, text, optional): This field is used to capture the phone number of the officer in charge.
* **Officer In Charge Email** (`officer_in_charge_email`, email, optional): This field is used to capture the email address of the officer in charge.
* **Officer Signature** (`officer_signature`, note, required): This field is used to obtain the officer's signature, acknowledging that they have completed the form accurately.
* **Incident Report Number** (`incident_report_number`, text, required): This field is used to capture the incident report number.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required): This field is used to record the date of the incident.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, required): This field is used to record the time of the incident.
* **Officer S Name** (`officer_s_name`, text, required): This field is used to capture the name of the officer who used force during the incident.
* **Officer S Id** (`officer_s_id`, text, required): This field is used to capture the ID number of the officer who used force during the incident.
