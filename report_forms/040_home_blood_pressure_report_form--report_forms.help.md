# Home Blood Pressure Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your home blood pressure readings, which can be useful for your healthcare providers to track and monitor your cardiovascular health.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "User Name" field.
2. Select the date on which you took your blood pressure reading in the "Date" field.
3. Enter the time on which you took your blood pressure reading in the "Time" field.
4. Enter your systolic blood pressure reading in the "Systolic Reading" field.
5. Enter your diastolic blood pressure reading in the "Diastolic Reading" field.
6. Enter your pulse rate in the "Pulse Rate" field.
7. If applicable, list any medications you took on the day you took your blood pressure reading in the "Medications Taken" field.
8. Optionally, provide any comments or additional information about your blood pressure reading in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Name
**User Name** (`user_name`, text, not required): Your name.
### Date
**Date** (`date`, date, not required): The date on which you took your blood pressure reading.
### Time
**Time** (`time`, time, not required): The time on which you took your blood pressure reading.
### Systolic Reading
**Systolic Reading** (`systolic_reading`, number, not required): Your systolic blood pressure reading.
### Diastolic Reading
**Diastolic Reading** (`diastolic_reading`, number, not required): Your diastolic blood pressure reading.
### Pulse Rate
**Pulse Rate** (`pulse_rate`, number, not required): Your pulse rate.
### Medications Taken
**Medications Taken** (`medications_taken`, text, not required): If you took any medications on the day you took your blood pressure reading, list them here.
### Comments
**Comments** (`comments`, text, not required): If you have any additional information about your blood pressure reading, provide it here.
