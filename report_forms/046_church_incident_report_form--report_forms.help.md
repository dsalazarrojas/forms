# Church Incident Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Church Incident Report Form is a tool used to gather information about incidents that have occurred within the church community. This form is used to report incidents such as medical emergencies, injuries, property damage, theft, security disturbances, missing items, and other incidents. The form helps in documenting the incident, providing necessary information for further investigation and follow-up actions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Person Reporting Incident" section with your personal details: Your Full Name, Position/Role in the Church, and Phone Number.
2. Provide a detailed description of the incident, including the date and time it occurred.
3. Specify the location of the incident.
4. Select the category of the incident from the list provided (e.g., Medical Emergency, Injury/Accident, etc.).
5. Provide a detailed description of what happened.
6. If applicable, specify the primary person involved in the incident and the names and contacts of any witnesses.
7. Answer the questions related to emergency services and first aid rendered.
8. Provide a summary of the immediate actions taken.
9. If required, specify if further follow-up is needed.
10. Finally, sign and date the form as verification of the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Your Full Name** (Text, Required)
Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.

### 2. **Your Position/Role in Church** (Text, Required)
Describe your role or position within the church (e.g., pastor, staff member, member, etc.).

### 3. **Your Phone Number** (Text, Required)
Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further information or follow-up.

### 4. **Date of Incident** (Date, Required)
Enter the date the incident occurred in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

### 5. **Time of Incident** (Time, Required)
Enter the time the incident occurred in the 12-hour clock format (e.g., 02:30 PM).

### 6. **Precise Location of Incident** (Text, Required)
Describe the exact location within the church where the incident occurred (e.g., parking lot, sanctuary, classroom 102).

### 7. **Incident Category** (Select One, Required)
Choose the category of the incident from the list provided:
  - Medical Emergency
  - Injury/Accident
  - Property Damage
  - Theft
  - Security/Disturbance
  - Missing Item
  - Other

### 8. **Detailed Description of Incident** (Text, Required)
Provide a factual account of what happened, including who, what, when, and where.

### 9. **Name of Primary Person Involved** (Text, Optional)
Enter the name of the primary person involved in the incident.

### 10. **Names and Contacts of Witnesses** (Text, Optional)
List the names and contacts of any witnesses.

### 11. **Was First Aid Rendered?** (Select One, Required)
Choose whether first aid was provided:
  - Yes - Specify below
  - False

### 12. **Were Emergency Services Called?** (Select One, Required)
Choose whether emergency services were called:
  - Yes - Police
  - Yes - EMS/Fire
  - Yes - Both
  - False

### 13. **Summary of Immediate Actions Taken** (Text, Required)
Describe the immediate actions taken as a result of the incident.

### 14. **Is Further Follow-up Required?** (Select One, Required)
Choose whether further follow-up is needed:
  - True
  - False

### 15. **Electronic Signature of Reporter** (Text, Required)
Type your name as verification of the report.

### 16. **Date of Report** (Date, Required)
Enter the date you are submitting this report in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
