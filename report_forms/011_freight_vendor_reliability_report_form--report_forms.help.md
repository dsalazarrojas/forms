# Freight Vendor Reliability Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report on the reliability of freight vendors for past shipments. The data collected will help identify patterns and trends in vendor performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the shipment details, including the freight vendor's name and the shipment ID.
2. Select the reliability of the freight vendor from the options provided.
3. Enter the date of shipment delivery.
4. Fill in any additional comments about the shipment.
5. Calculate and enter the reliability score based on your assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shipment Details**: This section collects information about the shipment, including the freight vendor's name and ID.
* **Reliability**: This field asks you to rate the reliability of the freight vendor, with options: Carrier, On-time, Late.
* **Delivered Date**: Enter the date the shipment was delivered.
* **Freight Vendor**: Enter the name of the freight vendor who handled the shipment.
* **Shipment ID**: Enter the unique ID of the shipment.
* **Shipment Type**: Select the type of shipment, from options: Air, Ground, Train, Truck.
* **Freight Weight**: Enter the weight of the shipment.
* **Freight Cost**: Enter the cost of the shipment.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments about the shipment.
* **Reliability Score**: Enter a numerical score based on your assessment of the freight vendor's reliability, using your discretion.
