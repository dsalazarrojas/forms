# Wildlife Egg Transfer Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the details of wildlife eggs transferred between individuals or groups, such as wildlife sanctuaries, research facilities, or conservation organizations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form for each transfer, ensuring that all fields are accurately and completely filled out.
2. Be sure to sign and date the form to indicate your authority and responsibility for the transfer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Transfer Location**: This field is used to indicate the location where the egg transfer took place. Enter the exact address, room number, or building name where the transfer occurred.
* **Donor**: This field is used to record the name of the individual or organization donating the eggs. If you are the donor, enter your name or the name of your organization.
* **Species**: This field is used to confirm whether the eggs being transferred are of a wild species. Select 'Yes' if the eggs are of a wild species, 'No' otherwise.
* **Quantity**: This field is used to record the number of eggs transferred. Enter the exact number of eggs transferred.
* **Collector**: This field is used to record the name of the individual or organization collecting the eggs. This is typically the person or organization receiving the eggs.
* **Transfer Date**: This field is used to record the date the eggs were transferred. Use the date format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Collector Signature**: This field is used to record your signature as the collector, confirming that you received the eggs.
* **Transfer Location Details**: This field is used to record any additional details about the transfer location, such as room number or building name.
* **Recipient**: This field is used to record the name of the individual or organization receiving the eggs. This is typically yourself, the collector.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form, as it will be used for tracking and record-keeping purposes.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult with your supervisor or someone in charge of record-keeping.
