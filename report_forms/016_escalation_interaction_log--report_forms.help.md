Escalation Interaction Log - Help Guide
================

## Purpose
The Escalation Interaction Log is a form used to document interactions between users and support teams when an issue is escalated from one team to another. This form helps track the escalation process and ensures that all parties involved are informed and up-to-date on the status of the issue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Escalation Interaction Log, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your input in the **User Input** field, which is for your comments or description of the issue.
2.  Select the **Escalation Type** that best describes how the issue was escalated (e.g., Phone, Email, None, Chat, Other).
3.  Enter the **Interaction Time** to note when the interaction occurred.
4.  Enter **Escalation Details** to provide further context on the issue.
5.  Select **Resolution Status** to indicate the current status of the issue (e.g., Resolved, Pending, Closed, Not Applicable).
6.  Enter **Action Taken** to describe any actions taken to resolve or escalate the issue.
7.  Enter **Action Details** to provide more information on the actions taken.
8.  Enter **Outcome Details** to describe the outcome or results of the interaction.
9.  Enter **Comments** for any additional comments or notes on the issue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Input** (`user_input`, `text`, required: false): This field is for entering your input or comments on the issue.
*   **Escalation Type** (`escalation_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the escalation type that best describes how the issue was escalated.
*   **Interaction Time** (`interaction_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time when the interaction occurred.
*   **Escalation Details** (`escalation_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter further details on the issue.
*   **Resolution Status** (`resolution_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the current status of the issue.
*   **Action Taken** (`action_taken`, `text`, required: false): Describe any actions taken to resolve or escalate the issue.
*   **Action Details** (`action_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter more information on the actions taken.
*   **Outcome Details** (`outcome_details`, `text`, required: false): Describe the outcome or results of the interaction.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes on the issue.
