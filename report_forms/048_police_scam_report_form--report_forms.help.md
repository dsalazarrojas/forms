# Police Scam Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report incidents of police scams, including details about the incident, suspect information, evidence, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Incident Details, including a brief description of what happened.
2. Provide information about the suspect, including any relevant details.
3. Upload any evidence related to the incident.
4. Enter the date and time of the incident.
5. Enter the location where the incident occurred.
6. Add any contact information for you or the individual affected.
7. Provide a detailed description of what happened.
8. If you have any additional supporting evidence, upload it here.
9. Choose whether to report the incident to the police (optional).
10. If you're reporting this incident, enter your information (optional).
11. Select the type of incident that occurred (e.g., Police Scam, Other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Details (1)**: Enter a brief description of the incident, including the nature of the scam and any relevant details.
* **Suspect Info (2)**: Provide any information about the suspect, such as their name, address, or any other identifying details.
* **Evidence (3)**: Upload any supporting evidence related to the incident, such as photographs or videos.
* **Incident Date (4)**: Enter the date the incident occurred (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Incident Time (5)**: Enter the time the incident occurred (hh:mm AM/PM).
* **Location (6)**: Enter the location where the incident occurred, including the city, state, and zip code.
* **Contact Info (7)**: Add any contact information for you or the individual affected, such as a name and phone number.
* **Incident Description (8)**: Provide a detailed description of what happened, including any relevant details.
* **Supporting Evidence (9)**: Upload any additional evidence that supports your report, such as photographs or videos.
* **Report to Police (10)**: Choose whether to report this incident to the police (optional).
* **Submitted by (11)**: If you're reporting this incident, enter your name and any other relevant information (optional).
* **Incident Type (12)**: Select the type of incident that occurred (e.g., Police Scam, Other).
