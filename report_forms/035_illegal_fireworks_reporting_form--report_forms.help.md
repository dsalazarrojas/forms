# Illegal Fireworks Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report illegal fireworks incidents in the community. To ensure that your report is processed efficiently, please fill out the form in a clear and concise manner and do not report duplicate incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your contact information: First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone Number.
2. Provide the incident details: Date and Time of the incident, the location (City, State, Zip), and a brief Description of what happened.
3. Indicate whether the incident was a "Yes" or "No" for Select One and Select Multiple, as these relate to the presence of illegal fireworks.
4. If applicable, provide any additional information or notes about the incident in the Note field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name for contact purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further information.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the incident in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the incident in 24-hour format `HH:MM`.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate whether you observed or know of any illegal fireworks during the incident.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate whether you observed or know of multiple illegal fireworks during the incident.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional information or notes about the incident if applicable.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter the address where the incident took place.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, optional): Enter the city where the incident took place.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, optional): Enter the state where the incident took place.
* **Zip** (`zip`, `text`, optional): Enter the zip code where the incident took place.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the incident.
