# Member Engagement Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and information on the level of engagement and participation of members within your community. It is meant to help the organization understand the needs and preferences of its members, providing valuable insights for improvement and development of programs and activities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select the options that best match your experiences and opinions.
3. Ensure that you fill out all required fields, denoted by a asterisk (\*).
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
