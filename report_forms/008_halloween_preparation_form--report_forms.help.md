# Halloween Preparation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Halloween Preparation Form is a tool used by event organizers to provide information to the staff about their upcoming Halloween event. This form helps the staff to better prepare for the event and ensure that everything is in order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information about your event.
2. Enter your name, the date and time of your event, the venue where it will be held, and the type of event it is (e.g. Trick-or-Treat, Costume Party, Horror Movie Night).
3. Estimate the number of guests and staff attending your event.
4. Select the types of food that will be served during the event.
5. Add any additional notes about your event.
6. Enter the contact information for your event (email and phone number).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (1)**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Date (2)**: Enter the date of the event (e.g. October 31st).
* **Time (3)**: Enter the time of the event (e.g. 6:00 PM).
* **Venue (4)**: Enter the location where the event will be held.
* **Event Type (5)**: Select the type of event (e.g. Trick-or-Treat, Costume Party, Horror Movie Night).
* **Guests (6)**: Estimate the number of guests expected to attend.
* **Staff (7)**: Estimate the number of staff attending.
* **Food (8)**: Select the types of food that will be served during the event.
* **Notes (9)**: Add any additional information about your event.
* **Email (10)**: Enter the email address of the person submitting the form.
* **Phone (11)**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.
* **Contact (12)**: Enter the name and contact information of the person submitting the form.

Note: All fields are optional, but it's best to fill out as much information as possible to help the staff prepare for your event.
