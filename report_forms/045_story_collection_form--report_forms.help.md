# Story Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Story Collection Form is designed to collect information about a story from a submitter. This form is used for reporting or feedback purposes and should be filled out by the person submitting the story.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields:
	* **Submitter Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
	* **Submitter Agreement**: Select "I have read and agree to the terms of this form" to proceed.
2. Fill out any additional required fields:
	* **Story**: Provide a detailed description of your story.
3. Fill out any optional fields as desired:
	* **Submitter Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
	* **Submitter Email**: Enter your contact email address.
	* **Submitter Date**: Enter the date of your story submission.
	* **Submitter Time**: Enter the time of your story submission.
	* **Submitter Note**: Add any additional notes about your story.
	* **Submitter Comments**: Provide any additional comments about your story.
	* **Submitter Attachments**: Attach any relevant files or documents to your story.
	* **Submitter Signature**: Include your signature (if required).
	* **Submitter Organization**: Enter your company or organization name.
	* **Submitter Department**: Enter your department name.
	* **Submitter Designation**: Enter your designation or role.
	* **Submitter Position**: Enter your position or job title.
	* **Submitter Title**: Enter your title or position title.
	* **Submitter Address**: Enter your address or location.
	* **Submitter City**: Enter your city name.
	* **Submitter State**: Enter your state or region name.
	* **Submitter Postal Code**: Enter your postal or zip code.
