## Interview Notes Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of the Interview Notes Form is to collect and record information about an interview session, including details about the candidate, the conversation, and the rating of the interview. This form is used to facilitate the collection of structured and objective feedback from the interviewer, ensuring consistency and accuracy in assessing candidate performance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Interview Notes Form, follow these steps:

1. **Select the relevant information**: Choose the correct form page (Main Form, Candidate Information, Interview Notes, Feedback, Rating, or Interviewer Information) depending on the specific information you need to capture.
2. **Fill in the required fields**: Ensure you fill in all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the label.
3. **Provide accurate and detailed information**: Enter clear and concise notes about the candidate and the interview session.
4. **Rate the interview**: If applicable, select a rating from the provided options (1, 2, 3, 4, 5) to assess the candidate's performance.
5. **Save your changes**: Click "Submit" to save your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Form (\*)**:
	+ This is the first page of the form where you can enter a brief description of the candidate and the interview session.
* **Candidate Information**:
	+ Enter any additional details about the candidate, such as their name, position, or relevant skills.
* **Interview Notes**:
	+ Provide a detailed and concise summary of the interview conversation.
* **Feedback**:
	+ Enter any comments or feedback you have for the candidate, which can be useful for future improvement.
* **Rating (\*)**:
	+ Select the rating of the candidate's performance based on the provided options (1, 2, 3, 4, 5).
* **Options**:
	+ Select one or more of the provided options that best describe the candidate's performance.
* **Date (\*)**:
	+ Enter the date of the interview session.
* **Time (\*)**:
	+ Enter the time of the interview session.
* **Interviewer Information**:
	+ Enter any relevant information about yourself, such as your name or ID number.

## Tips

* Be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling in the form.
* Use clear and concise language when entering notes and feedback.
* Select the rating and options that best reflect the candidate's performance.
* Ensure you complete all required fields before submitting the form.
* Save your changes after completing the form to avoid losing your work.
