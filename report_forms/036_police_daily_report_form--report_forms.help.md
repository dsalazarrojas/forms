# Police Daily Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Police Daily Report Form is designed to record and document daily activities, incidents, and other important details by police officers. This form helps in monitoring and analyzing officer performance, ensuring that all tasks are completed efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is required.
2. Fill in all the relevant fields with the correct information.
3. Ensure all fields are accurately and truthfully filled.
4. Review your form before submitting to ensure everything is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Daily Activity
* **Daily Activity** (`daily_activity`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the daily activity performed by the officer, including any notable events or incidents that occurred.

### Page 2: Incidents
* **Incidents** (`incidents`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all incidents that occurred during the day from the provided options.

### Page 3: Operations
* **Operations** (`operations`, `text`, required/optional): Describe any operations performed by the officer, including the location and details of the operation.

### Page 4: Officer Name
* **Officer Name** (`officer_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the officer submitting the report.

### Page 5: Shift
* **Shift** (`shift`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the shift that the officer was working during the incident from the provided options.

### Page 6: Date
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the incident or activity reported.

### Page 7: Time
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of the incident or activity reported.

### Page 8: Location
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the location where the incident or activity occurred.

### Page 9: Details
* **Details** (`details`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any additional details about the incident or activity.

### Page 10: Assigned Tool
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the tool or equipment assigned to the officer for the incident or activity.

### Page 11: Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required/optional): Add any further comments or notes about the incident or activity.

### Page 12: Submit
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required/optional): Review and submit the form.

Note: This form is designed to be completed on a daily basis by police officers to ensure accurate and complete reporting of daily activities and incidents.
