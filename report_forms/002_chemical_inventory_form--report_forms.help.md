# Chemical Inventory Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Chemical Inventory Form is used to record and track chemical inventory in your laboratory or storage facility. It helps to maintain an accurate record of the chemical's properties, storage location, and custodian information. This form is essential for ensuring the safe handling and management of chemicals.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Chemical Details

*   **Chemical Name**: Enter the full IUPAC or common name of the chemical.
*   **CAS Registry Number**: Enter the CAS Registry Number of the chemical. This is a unique identifier for the chemical substance.
*   **Physical State**: Select the physical state of the chemical at room temperature (Solid, Liquid, Gas).
*   **GHS Hazard Classification**: Select the primary hazard category of the chemical (Flammable, Toxic, Corrosive, Oxidizer, Non-Hazardous).

### Step 2: Storage and Location

*   **Storage Location - Room and Shelf**: Provide a detailed description of the storage location, including the room and shelf where the chemical is stored.

### Step 3: Quantity and Measurement

*   **Current Quantity**: Enter the current quantity of the chemical, as a numeric value only.
*   **Unit of Measure**: Select the unit used to measure the quantity (Grams (g), Milliliters (ml), Kilograms (kg), Liters (L)).

### Step 4: Custodian Information

*   **Person Responsible for Inventory**: Enter the full name of the lab manager or PI responsible for maintaining the inventory.

## Tips

*   Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
*   Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors or omissions.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or lab manager for guidance.
