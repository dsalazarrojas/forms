# Patient Progress Notes Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document and track patient progress during medical visits, consultations, or appointments. It captures key information about the patient's condition, medical history, and current status, as well as the visit details and staff involvement. This form helps healthcare professionals maintain accurate and up-to-date records, facilitating better patient care and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant information for each field, ensuring accuracy and relevance to the patient's current status.
2. Fill in the required fields with the necessary information, using the options provided for select and select_multiple fields.
3. Enter any additional notes or comments in the free-text fields, providing a clear and concise account of the patient's condition.
4. Sign the form to confirm completion and review, and ensure the patient signs it as well (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Id** (`patient_id`):
	+ A unique identifier for the patient, helping to distinguish between multiple patients.
	+ Enter the patient's ID number, if available.
* **Date Visited** (`date_visited`):
	+ The date of the medical visit or consultation.
	+ Enter the date in the format `DD/MM/YYYY`.
* **Visit Type** (`visit_type`):
	+ The type of visit, which can be Checkup, Consult, or Appointment.
	+ Select the most relevant visit type based on the patient's condition and treatment.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`):
	+ The patient's full name, as recorded in their medical records.
	+ Enter the patient's name in the format "Last Name, First Name".
* **Condition** (`condition`):
	+ A brief description of the patient's current medical condition.
	+ Enter a concise summary of the patient's symptoms, diagnosis, or treatment progress.
* **Medication** (`medication`):
	+ Any prescribed medication, including dosage and administration details.
	+ Enter the medication name, dosage, and frequency of administration.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`):
	+ A list of known allergies or sensitivities the patient has.
	+ Enter any allergies or sensitivities that may impact their treatment or medical care.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`):
	+ A brief summary of the patient's medical history, including previous treatments or conditions.
	+ Enter any relevant medical history that may influence the patient's current condition or treatment.
* **Vital Signs** (`vital_signs`):
	+ Measurements of the patient's vital signs, such as blood pressure or temperature.
	+ Enter the patient's vital signs, as measured during the visit or consultation.
* **Patient Status** (`patient_status`):
	+ A selection of the patient's current status, which can be Stable, Critical, or Discharged.
	+ Select the most accurate status based on the patient's condition and treatment.
* **Physician Notes** (`physician_notes`):
	+ Additional comments or observations from the healthcare provider.
	+ Enter any important information that may impact the patient's care or treatment.
* **Staff Name** (`staff_name`):
	+ The name and title of the healthcare staff member involved in the patient's care.
	+ Enter the staff member's name and title.
* **Signed By** (`signed_by`):
	+ The name of the healthcare staff member who signed the form.
	+ Enter the staff member's name.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`):
	+ The date and time the form was signed.
	+ Enter the date and time in the format `DD/MM/YYYY HH:MM`.
* **Time Signed** (`time_signed`):
	+ The time the form was signed.
	+ Enter the time in the format `HH:MM`.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`):
	+ The patient's signature, confirming they have read and understood the form.
	+ Enter the patient's signature.
* **Staff Signature** (`staff_signature`):
	+ The healthcare staff member's signature, confirming they have reviewed and completed the form.
	+ Enter the staff member's signature.
* **Completed By** (`completed_by`):
	+ The name of the healthcare staff member who completed the form.
	+ Enter the staff member's name.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`):
	+ The date the form was completed.
	+ Enter the date in the format `DD/MM/YYYY`.
* **Time Completed** (`time_completed`):
	+ The time the form was completed.
	+ Enter the time in the format `HH:MM`.
* **Patient Progress** (`patient_progress`):
	+ A summary of the patient's progress, including any notable changes or improvements.
	+ Enter a concise account of the patient's progress.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`):
	+ Any additional comments or observations from the healthcare provider.
	+ Enter any important information that may impact the patient's care or treatment.
* **Patient Contact** (`patient_contact`):
	+ The patient's contact information, including phone number, email, or address.
	+ Enter the patient's contact details.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`):
	+ The patient's home or mailing address.
	+ Enter the patient's address.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`):
	+ The patient's email address.
	+ Enter the patient's email address.

Please note that this form is a sample form, and actual fields and content may vary based on your organization's specific requirements and procedures. It's essential to review and customize the form according to your medical facility's policies and guidelines.
