## Weekly Installer Work Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weekly Installer Work Report is a form for installers to document their hours worked and notable events/ issues during the week. This report should only be submitted if the user has actually conducted work at the specified job sites.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are filling out this form for work conducted at the job sites specified.
2. Enter the name of each job site where you worked.
3. Enter the total hours worked for the week.
4. Provide any additional notes about your work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Sites** (`job_sites`, select_multiple, required: false): Enter the names of the job sites where you worked this week. For example, "123 Main St" or "ABC Corp HQ".
* **Hours Worked** (`hours_worked`, number, required: true): Enter the total hours worked this week.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: true): Enter any additional information about your work, such as notable events or issues that occurred.

## Tips
* Make sure to only report work conducted at the job sites specified in this form.
* Double-check your hours and accuracy of your reporting.
