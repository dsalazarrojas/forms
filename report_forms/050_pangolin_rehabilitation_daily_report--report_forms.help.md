# Pangolin Rehabilitation Daily Report - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Pangolin Rehabilitation Daily Report is designed to aid veterinarians and medical professionals in monitoring and recording the progress of pangolins under their care. This form helps in maintaining a consistent and detailed record of patient care, ensuring that all necessary data is collected for follow-up and future reference.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the **Patient Name** and select the **Species** of the pangolin.
2. Provide the **Rehabilitation Location** where the patient is being treated.
3. Report any **Treatment Given** to the patient, including dosage and frequency.
4. Record any **Medication Administered**, including dosage and frequency.
5. Note the **Patient Condition**, including any changes or updates.
6. Outline the **Care Plan**, detailing specific steps for the patient's recovery.
7. Enter the **Follow Up Date**, the scheduled next appointment date.
8. Add any **Notes** regarding the patient's care or your own observations.
9. Sign with your **Veterinarian Signature**, or provide a reason for not signing in the **Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the name of the pangolin patient.
* **Species**: Identify the species of the pangolin.
* **Rehabilitation Location**: Enter the location where the patient is undergoing rehabilitation.
* **Treatment Given**: Report any medical treatment provided to the patient, including dosage and frequency.
* **Medication Administered**: Record any medication administered to the patient, including dosage and frequency.
* **Patient Condition**: Note any significant changes or updates on the patient's health status.
* **Care Plan**: Outline specific steps or tasks for the patient's recovery and well-being.
* **Follow Up Date**: Enter the date of the next follow-up appointment.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or observations regarding the patient's care.
* **Veterinarian Signature**: Sign with your professional signature, or explain reasons for not signing in the **Notes** field.
