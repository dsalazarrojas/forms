# Chiropractic Business Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chiropractic Business Report Form is a tool used by chiropractors to report on their business performance, including financial metrics, patient statistics, and operational data. This form helps chiropractors track their progress, identify areas of improvement, and make data-driven decisions to optimize their practice.

## How To Complete This Form

### Report Information (Page 1)
- Select the report date and reporting period (e.g., month, quarter, or year)
- Enter the practice name

### Report Date
- Enter the report date in the format `yyyy-mm-dd`
- **Required field**

### Reporting Period
- Choose the reporting period (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually)
- **Required field**

### Period Start and End Dates
- Enter the start date of the reporting period in the format `yyyy-mm-dd`
- Enter the end date of the reporting period in the format `yyyy-mm-dd`
- **Required fields**

### Basic Practice Information
- Enter the name of your practice
- This is the name of your chiropractic business that appears on the report

### Report Prepared By
- Enter the name of the individual preparing this report
- This can be your name, the practice owner, or a staff member preparing the report

### Financial Metrics (Page 2)
- Enter the total revenue earned during the reporting period
- Enter the total amount collected
- **Required fields**

### Patient and Practice Metrics (Page 2)
- Enter the number of new patients acquired during this period
- Enter the total number of active patients currently under your care
- Enter the total number of patient visits, including all appointments
- Enter the average number of visits per patient
- Enter the number of patients who missed appointments (no-shows)
- Enter the number of patients who cancelled their appointments
- **Required fields**

### Additional Patient and Staff Metrics (Page 3)
- Enter the total number of adjustments performed
- Enter the number of physical therapy sessions provided
- Enter the number of X-rays taken
- Enter the number of new patient consultations held
- Enter other services provided by your staff or doctors

### Marketing and Operational Metrics (Page 4)
- Enter the total amount spent on marketing for this period
- Enter the number of new leads generated
- Enter the conversion rate of leads to patients
- Enter the top sources of referrals for new patients
- Enter the total number of staff hours worked
- Enter the total hours spent by doctors

### Operational and Performance Metrics (Page 5)
- Enter the total overhead costs for this period
- Enter the total supply costs for this period
- Enter the total equipment expenses
- Enter the performance analysis for this period (including areas of improvement)
- Enter an overall performance rating (e.g., Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor)
- Enter the key strengths observed during this period
- Enter the challenges or difficulties faced during this period
- Enter action items or planned improvements for the next period
- Enter goals for the next reporting period

### Additional Comments (Page 6)
- Enter any additional comments or observations about your practice

### Final Signature and Verification (Page 6)
- Sign this report with your name and title to confirm accuracy

This comprehensive guide covers all the fields within the Chiropractic Business Report Form. Each section is designed to help chiropractors collect and report on essential metrics for their business performance, including financial, patient, and operational metrics.
