# Lost And Found Property Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lost And Found Property Form is used to report a lost item within the institution's premises. Please fill out this form with accurate and clear information about the lost property.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the contact information section with your details.
2. Provide the address, email, and phone number of the contact person.
3. Describe the property in the property details section.
4. Specify the location where the property was lost.
5. Add any additional details about the property's condition.
6. Upload an image of the lost property (if available).
7. Choose the name and relationship of the contact person.
8. Enter the date you last had the property.
9. Enter the date the property was lost.
10. Indicate the gender of the contact person.
11. Add any additional information about the property (if needed).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required): Enter your name, title, or other identification.
* **Address** (`contact_information_address`, text, required): Enter the address where you can be contacted.
* **Email** (`contact_information_email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (`contact_information_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Property Details** (`property_details`, text, optional): Describe the lost property in as much detail as possible.
* **Location** (`found_property_location`, text, required): Specify the location where the property was lost.
* **Description** (`found_property_description`, text, required): Describe the lost property in as much detail as possible.
* **Condition** (`found_property_condition`, text, required): Describe the condition of the lost property.
* **Image** (`found_property_image`, text, required): Upload an image of the lost property (if available).
* **Name** (`contact_information_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Relationship** (`contact_information_relationship`, text, required): Specify the relationship of the contact person (e.g., student, staff, parent).
* **Date** (`lost_property_date`, date, required): Enter the date you last had the property.
* **Date** (`found_property_date`, date, required): Enter the date the property was lost.
* **Gender** (`contact_information_gender`, select_one, required): Choose the gender of the contact person.
* **Additional Info** (`property_details_additional_info`, text, optional): Add any additional information about the property (if needed).
