# Library Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Library Incident Report Form is used to document and report incidents that occur within the library. This form helps the library management team to identify, assess, and respond to these incidents in a timely and effective manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your details in the required fields.
2. Provide a brief description of the incident in the "Incident Description" field.
3. Specify the location of the incident in the "Location" field.
4. Describe the action taken in response to the incident in the "Action Taken" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`): Enter the date of the incident in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Reported By** (`reported_by`): Enter your name or title as the person reporting the incident.
* **Reported On** (`reported_on`): Enter the date you are reporting the incident.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`): Provide a detailed description of the incident, including any relevant details such as what happened, how it happened, and any injuries or damages involved.
* **Location** (`location`): Enter the location where the incident occurred within the library.
* **Action Taken** (`action_taken`): Describe the actions taken to respond to the incident, such as reporting it to the library management or taking immediate action to mitigate any damage.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, as this information will help the library management team assess and respond to the incident effectively.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, please don't hesitate to ask for help or clarification.
* Remember that the information collected in this form will be used solely for the purpose of improving library safety and incident management.
