# College Expense Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The College Expense Report is designed to gather accurate and complete records of expenses for students, including dates, amounts, categories, and descriptions of expenses, as well as details about the student, department, and funding source.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your report submission date.
2.  Enter your name as a student.
3.  Enter your student ID number.
4.  Enter your email address.
5.  Enter your phone number.
6.  Enter the department or organization you are submitting for.
7.  Enter the start and end dates of the expense report period.
8.  Select the purpose of the expense (e.g., conference, travel, supplies).
9.  If applicable, enter the name and dates of the event or project.
10.  Enter the date of the first expense.
11.  Select the category of the expense (e.g., transportation, meals).
12.  Enter a description of the expense.
13.  Enter the amount of the expense.
14.  Enter the name of the vendor or merchant.
15.  Indicate if you have a receipt or invoice for the expense.
16.  Enter additional expenses (if any).
17.  Enter the total amount of expenses being claimed.
18.  Select the funding source (e.g., student activity fee, department budget).
19.  Enter the budget approval number or code (if applicable).
20.  Enter the name of your supervisor or advisor.
21.  Enter your supervisor's email address.
22.  Select your preferred method of reimbursement (e.g., check, direct deposit).
23.  If you selected direct deposit, enter your banking information.
24.  Finally, certify that the expenses are accurate and necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Report Submission Date** (`report_date`, date, required): The date on which you are submitting this report.
*   **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Your full name as a student.
*   **Student ID Number** (`student_id`, text, required): Your student ID number.
*   **Email Address** (`student_email`, email, required): Your email address.
*   **Phone Number** (`student_phone`, text, required): Your phone number.
*   **Department or Organization** (`department_organization`, text, required): The department or organization you are submitting for.
*   **Expense Report Period Start Date** (`report_period_start`, date, required): The start date of the expense report period.
*   **Expense Report Period End Date** (`report_period_end`, date, required): The end date of the expense report period.
*   **Purpose of Expenses** (`expense_purpose`, select_one, required): The purpose of the expenses (e.g., conference, travel, supplies).
*   **Event or Project Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): If applicable, the name of the event or project.
*   **Expense Date** (`expense_1_date`, date, required): The date of the first expense.
*   **Expense Category** (`expense_1_category`, select_one, required): The category of the expense (e.g., transportation, meals).
*   **Expense Description** (`expense_1_description`, text, required): A description of the expense.
*   **Expense Amount** (`expense_1_amount`, number, required): The cost of the expense in dollars.
*   **Vendor or Merchant Name** (`expense_1_vendor`, text, required): The name of the vendor or merchant.
*   **Receipt or Invoice Attached** (`expense_1_receipt`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have a receipt or invoice for the expense.
*   **Additional Expenses** (`additional_expenses`, number, optional): The total amount of other expenses.
*   **Total Expenses Being Claimed** (`total_expenses`, number, required): The sum of all expenses.
*   **Funding Source** (`funding_source`, select_one, required): The source of the funding (e.g., student activity fee, department budget).
*   **Budget Approval Number or Code** (`budget_approval`, text, optional): If applicable, the budget approval number or code.
*   **Supervisor or Advisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): The name of your supervisor or advisor.
*   **Supervisor Email Address** (`supervisor_email`, email, required): The email address of your supervisor or advisor.
*   **Preferred Reimbursement Method** (`reimbursement_method`, select_one, required): Your preferred method of reimbursement (e.g., check, direct deposit).
*   **Banking Information for Direct Deposit** (`bank_account_info`, text, optional): If you selected direct deposit, enter your banking information.
*   **I Certify These Expenses Are Accurate and Necessary** (`certification`, select_one, required): Confirm that the expenses are accurate and necessary.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that you have all necessary information and approvals before completing this form.
*   Double-check your entries for accuracy to avoid errors.
*   If you encounter any issues, contact your supervisor or advisor for assistance.
