# Trash Violation Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trash Violation Report Form is used to report instances of trash-related issues on public or private property.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter a brief description of the trash violation you are reporting in the **Report a Trash Violation** field.
2. Provide any additional details about the incident in the **Report Details** field.
3. Enter your neighbor's or property owner's contact information in the **Neighbor's Details** field.
4. Enter your neighbor's or property owner's email address in the **Neighbor's Email** field.
5. Enter your neighbor's or property owner's phone number in the **Neighbor's Phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report a Trash Violation (trash_violation_report_form)**:
	+ Enter a clear and concise description of the trash-related issue you're reporting. Be as specific as possible about the location and the nature of the problem.
* **Report Details (report_details)**:
	+ Provide any additional details or context about the incident that may help address the problem. This can include photos or descriptions of the trash or the property in question.
* **Neighbor's Details (neighbor_details)**:
	+ Enter your neighbor's or property owner's name, address, and any other identifying information.
* **Neighbor's Email (neighbor_email)**:
	+ Enter your neighbor's or property owner's email address. This will help facilitate communication and resolution of the issue.
* **Neighbor's Phone (neighbor_phone)**:
	+ Enter your neighbor's or property owner's phone number. This will help ensure that you can be contacted for further information or to follow up on the issue.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you're reporting on behalf of someone else, please ensure that you have their permission to report this information.
* If you have any photos or documentation that support your report, please attach them to the report for reference.
