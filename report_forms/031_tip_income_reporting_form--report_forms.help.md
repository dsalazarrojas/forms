# Tip Income Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information about employee income earned during shifts, including shifts worked, income earned, and submission details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form correctly, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your employee ID and name.
2. Select your shift(s) worked during the reporting period.
3. Enter your total income earned, including tax deductions.
4. Specify the date and time you are submitting the form.
5. Identify who you are submitting the form to (e.g., Manager or Shift Manager).
6. Fill in the shift manager's ID and name (if applicable).
7. Add any additional notes, if necessary.
8. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shift** (Shift, text, required): Enter the shift(s) you worked during the reporting period.
* **Workday** (Workday, date, required): Select the date of the shift(s) you worked.
* **Income** (Income, number, required): Enter the total income earned during the shift(s).
* **Shift/Workday** (Shift/Workday, select_one, required): Select the combination of shift and workday.
* **Tip Earned** (Tip Earned, number, required): Enter the total tips earned during the shift(s).
* **Tax Deductions** (Tax Deductions, number, required): Enter any tax deductions applied to your income.
* **Tip Income** (Tip Income, number, required): Enter the total tips earned before tax deductions.
* **Other Income** (Other Income, number, optional): Enter any other income earned during the shift(s).
* **Shift 1** (Shift 1, select_multiple, required): Select the shifts you worked during the shift 1 period (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
* **Shift 2** (Shift 2, select_multiple, optional): Select the shifts you worked during the shift 2 period (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
* **Shift 3** (Shift 3, select_multiple, optional): Select the shifts you worked during the shift 3 period (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
* **Shift 4** (Shift 4, select_multiple, optional): Select the shifts you worked during the shift 4 period (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
* **Submit** (Submit, text, required): Enter a brief description of the submission (e.g., "Daily Report").
* **Submit Date** (Submit Date, date, required): Select the date you are submitting the form.
* **Submit Time** (Submit Time, time, required): Select the time you are submitting the form.
* **Employee ID** (Employee ID, text, required): Enter your employee ID.
* **Employee Name** (Employee Name, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Manager ID** (Manager ID, text, optional): Enter the manager's ID (if applicable).
* **Manager Name** (Manager Name, text, optional): Enter the manager's full name (if applicable).
* **Branch** (Branch, text, required): Enter the branch name.
* **Shift Manager ID** (Shift Manager ID, text, required): Enter the shift manager's ID.
* **Shift Manager Name** (Shift Manager Name, text, required): Enter the shift manager's full name.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Submit By** (Submit By, select_one, required): Select who you are submitting the form to (e.g., Manager or Shift Manager).
* **Submit To** (Submit To, select_one, optional): Select who to submit the form to (e.g., Shift Manager or Manager).
* **Shift 1** (Shift 1, select_multiple, optional): Select the shifts you worked during the shift 1 period (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
* **Shift 2** (Shift 2, select_multiple, optional): Select the shifts you worked during the shift 2 period (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
* **Shift 3** (Shift 3, select_multiple, optional): Select the shifts you worked during the shift 3 period (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
* **Shift 4** (Shift 4, select_multiple, optional): Select the shifts you worked during the shift 4 period (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Evening).

## Tips
- Make sure to accurately report your income earned during each shift.
- Double-check your submission details before submitting the form.
- Review and understand the submission process and who to submit the form to.
- Keep your notes field for any additional comments or details.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your manager or shift manager for assistance.
