# Smart Water Usage Report Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Water Usage Report Request Form is designed to gather information from property managers or owners to request a detailed water usage report for a specific property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Property Address field with the exact address of the property for which you are requesting the report.
2. Select a date range for the report in the Report Period field. This will determine the specific time frame for which you want the report to cover.
3. Enter your email address in the Property Manager Email field to receive the report when it's ready.
4. Fill in your name in the Report Requester Name field.
5. Enter your phone number in the Report Requester Phone field.
6. Provide a note in the Report Requester Note field if you have any specific instructions or comments for the report.
7. If you are a representative of a company, select the corresponding designation in the Report Requester Designation field.
8. If you want to receive the report at a different email address, enter it in the Report Requester Email field (this should be a valid email address).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Form** (`report_form`, text, optional): This field is for entering any additional comments or information not covered by the other fields.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, optional): Enter the exact address of the property for which you are requesting the report.
* **Report Period** (`report_period`, select_one, optional): Select a date range for the report. Please choose from the provided options, such as January 1 to January 31, February 1 to February 28, and so on.
* **Property Manager Email** (`property_manager_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to receive the report when it's ready.
* **Report Requester Name** (`report_requester_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the person requesting the report.
* **Report Requester Phone** (`report_requester_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Report Requester Note** (`report_requester_note`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or instructions for the report.
* **Report Requester Company** (`report_requester_company`, text, optional): If you are representing a company, enter the name of the company.
* **Report Requester Designation** (`report_requester_designation`, select_multiple, optional): Select your designation as the representative of the company or property. Options include Homeowner, Landlord, and Utility Representative.
* **Report Requester Email** (`report_requester_email`, email, optional): If you want to receive the report at a different email address, enter it here.
