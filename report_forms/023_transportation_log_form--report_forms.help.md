# Transportation Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Transportation Log Form is a tool for collecting data about vehicle trips, helping you to track and monitor vehicle usage, performance, and related metrics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the **Route ID**, a unique identifier for the route taken by the vehicle.
2. Enter the **Vehicle ID**, a unique number assigned to the vehicle.
3. Provide the **Start Time** and **Start Date** of the trip.
4. Enter the **End Time** and **End Date** of the trip.
5. Input the **Route Distance**, the total distance traveled during the trip.
6. Enter the **Start Miles**, the odometer reading at the beginning of the trip.
7. Enter the **End Miles**, the odometer reading at the end of the trip.
8. Record the **Drive Time**, the duration of the trip.
9. Choose the **Fuel Type** used during the trip from the options provided.
10. Enter the amount of **Fuel Used** during the trip.
11. Add any additional **Delivery Notes** about the trip.
12. Evaluate the **Vehicle Condition** at the end of the trip, using the provided options.
13. Optionally, add **Notes** about the trip.
14. Enter the **Vehicle Make** and **Vehicle Model**, if not already recorded in the system.
15. Enter the **Driver Name**, if the driver is not already recorded in the system.
16. Add any **Route Notes** about the trip.
17. Evaluate the **Weather Condition** during the trip, using the provided options.
18. Enter the **Vehicle Year**, if not already recorded in the system.
19. Enter the **Vehicle Year Made**, if not already recorded in the system.
20. Record the **Drive Miles**, the total distance traveled during the trip.
21. Record the **Drive Hours**, the duration of the trip.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Route ID** (`route_id`, number, required/optional): Enter a unique identifier for the route taken by the vehicle.
* **Vehicle ID** (`vehicle_id`, number, required/optional): Enter a unique number assigned to the vehicle.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, date, required/optional): Provide the start time of the trip.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required/optional): Provide the start date of the trip.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the end time of the trip.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the end date of the trip.
* **Route Distance** (`route_distance`, number, required/optional): Enter the total distance traveled during the trip.
* **Start Miles** (`start_miles`, number, required/optional): Enter the odometer reading at the beginning of the trip.
* **End Miles** (`end_miles`, number, required/optional): Enter the odometer reading at the end of the trip.
* **Drive Time** (`drive_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the duration of the trip.
* **Fuel Type** (`fuel_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of fuel used during the trip.
* **Fuel Used** (`fuel_used`, number, required/optional): Enter the amount of fuel used during the trip.
* **Delivery Notes** (`delivery_notes`, text, required/optional): Add any additional notes about the trip.
* **Vehicle Condition** (`vehicle_condition`, select_one, required): Evaluate the vehicle condition at the end of the trip.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required/optional): Add any additional notes about the trip.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, required/optional): Enter the vehicle make, if not already recorded in the system.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, required/optional): Enter the vehicle model, if not already recorded in the system.
* **Driver Name** (`driver_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the driver's name, if not already recorded in the system.
* **Route Notes** (`route_notes`, text, required/optional): Add any additional route-related notes.
* **Weather Condition** (`weather_condition`, select_one, required): Evaluate the weather condition during the trip.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, required/optional): Enter the vehicle year.
* **Vehicle Year Made** (`vehicle_year_made`, number, required/optional): Enter the year the vehicle was made.
* **Drive Miles** (`drive_miles`, number, required/optional): Enter the total distance traveled during the trip.
* **Drive Hours** (`drive_hours`, number, required/optional): Enter the duration of the trip.
