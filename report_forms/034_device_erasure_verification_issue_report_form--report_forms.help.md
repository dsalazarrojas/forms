# Device Erasure Verification Issue Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report issues related to device erasure, such as hardware or software erasure, and to request verification of the erasure process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of erasure issue you are reporting (hardware, software, or both).
2. Provide a detailed description of the issue and the asset involved.
3. Enter the date of the issue and the date of the asset's erasure.
4. Choose the status of the asset (available, unavailable, lost/stolen, damaged, or other).
5. Select the type of wipe method used (factory reset, hard reset, or both).
6. Enter the name and title of the user and the reportee (if applicable).
7. Enter the asset tag, serial number, model, vendor, manufacturer, and location.
8. Enter the reportee's department, location, job title, and supervisor's name and job title (if applicable).
9. Enter the asset's destruction date and warranty expiration date (if applicable).
10. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Device Erasure Verification Issue Report** (1): Enter a brief description of the issue.
* **Report Date** (2): Enter the date you are submitting this report.
* **Description** (3): Provide a detailed description of the issue and the asset involved.
* **Issue Description** (4): Repeat the description of the issue.
* **Issue Date** (5): Enter the date of the issue.
* **Device Status** (6): Choose the status of the asset (available, unavailable, lost/stolen, damaged, or other).
* **Wipe Type** (7): Select the type of erasure (hardware, software, or both).
* **Wipe Method** (8): Choose the type of wipe method used (factory reset, hard reset, or both).
* **User Name** (9): Enter the name of the user who reported the issue.
* **User Title** (10): Enter the user's title.
* **Reportee Name** (11): Enter the name of the reportee (if applicable).
* **Reportee Title** (12): Enter the reportee's title (if applicable).
* **Asset Tag** (13): Enter the asset's tag number.
* **Asset Serial Number** (14): Enter the asset's serial number.
* **Asset Model** (15): Enter the asset's model.
* **Asset Vendor** (16): Enter the asset's vendor.
* **Asset Manufacturer** (17): Enter the asset's manufacturer.
* **Reportee Department** (18): Enter the reportee's department.
* **Reportee Location** (19): Enter the reportee's location.
* **Reportee Job Title** (20): Enter the reportee's job title.
* **Asset Location** (21): Enter the asset's current location.
* **Asset Destruction Date** (22): Enter the date of the asset's destruction.
* **Asset Warranty Expiration Date** (23): Enter the asset's warranty expiration date (if applicable).
* **Reportee Supervisor Name** (24): Enter the reportee's supervisor's name (if applicable).
* **Reportee Supervisor Job Title** (25): Enter the supervisor's job title (if applicable).

Please note that the fields marked as "optional" are not mandatory, but it is recommended to fill them in if you have the necessary information.
