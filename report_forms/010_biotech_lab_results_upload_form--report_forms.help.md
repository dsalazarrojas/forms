# Biotech Lab Results Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Biotech Lab Results Upload Form is used to upload lab results in a structured and consistent manner, ensuring that all required information is provided for lab results submission.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the submission information, including the submission date and time. Ensure that the submission date is accurate.
2. Move on to the lab information section, where you will provide the laboratory name, lab facility code or ID, lab contact person's name, email, and phone number.
3. In the sample information section, provide the sample ID, sample type, sample collection date, and storage condition. Ensure that you select the correct sample type from the available options.
4. In the test information section, provide the test type or analysis performed, testing method or protocol, test start and completion dates, and quality control standards met. Ensure that you select the correct test type from the available options.
5. In the results upload section, provide a brief summary of the results, raw data file (if applicable), analysis or report file, and any additional supporting files.
6. Finally, in the interpretation and approval section, provide the name of the scientist or analyst who interpreted the results, the interpretation date, the name of the reviewer, and the review date. Ensure that you confirm that all information is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submission Date**: The date when the lab results were submitted. This field is required and must be accurate.
* **Submission Time**: The time when the lab results were submitted. This field is optional and not required.
* **Laboratory Name**: The name of the laboratory where the lab results were conducted. This field is required and must be accurate.
* **Lab Facility Code or ID**: A unique identifier for the laboratory. This field is required and must be accurate.
* **Lab Contact Person**: The name of the primary contact person at the laboratory. This field is required and must be accurate.
* **Lab Contact Email**: The email address of the primary contact person at the laboratory. This field is required and must be accurate.
* **Lab Contact Phone**: The phone number of the primary contact person at the laboratory. This field is required and must be accurate.
* **Sample ID**: A unique identifier for the sample. This field is required and must be accurate.
* **Sample Type**: The type of sample analyzed. This field is required and must be accurate.
* **Sample Collection Date**: The date when the sample was collected. This field is required and must be accurate.
* **Sample Quantity**: The amount or volume of the sample. This field is optional and not required.
* **Storage Condition**: The storage condition of the sample. This field is required and must be accurate.
* **Test Type or Analysis Performed**: The type of analysis conducted. This field is required and must be accurate.
* **Testing Method or Protocol**: The specific method or protocol used for testing. This field is required and must be accurate.
* **Test Start Date**: The date when testing began. This field is required and must be accurate.
* **Test Completion Date**: The date when testing was completed. This field is required and must be accurate.
* **Quality Control Standards Met**: Whether the sample passed quality control standards. This field is required and must be accurate.
* **QC Notes or Issues**: Any issues or special notes regarding quality control. This field is optional and not required.
* **Results Summary**: A brief summary of the findings. This field is required and must be accurate.
* **Results Status**: The current status of the results. This field is required and must be accurate.
* **Raw Data File**: An optional field to upload raw data file if applicable.
* **Analysis or Report File**: An optional field to upload analysis or report file if applicable.
* **Additional Supporting Files**: An optional field to upload any other relevant files.
* **Results Interpreted By**: The name of the scientist or analyst who interpreted the results. This field is required and must be accurate.
* **Interpretation Date**: The date when the results were interpreted. This field is required and must be accurate.
* **Reviewed and Approved By**: The name of the reviewer who reviewed and approved the results. This field is required and must be accurate.
* **Review Date**: The date when the results were reviewed and approved. This field is required and must be accurate.
* **Confidentiality Acknowledgment**: A confirmation that all information in the report is accurate and complete. This field is required and must be accurate.
