# Ventilation System Maintenance Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and track information about ventilation system maintenance for facility management and operations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter general information about the ventilation system in the **System Info** field.
2. Rate the system's condition in the **System Condition** field by selecting one or more of the following options: Good, Fair, Poor.
3. Record the date of previous services or visits in the **Service Visits** field.
4. Categorize the type of maintenance performed in the **Maintenance History** field by selecting one of the following options: No Maintenance, Scheduled Maintenance, Urgent Maintenance, Deferred Maintenance.
5. Enter notes or comments from the technician in the **Technician Notes** field.
6. Provide feedback or comments from the facility manager in the **Facility Manager Comments** field.
7. Select the technician assigned to the task in the **Assigned Technician** field by choosing one of the following options: chatjimmy, John Doe, Jane Doe.
8. Record the date of the service or maintenance visit in the **Service Date** field.
9. Record the next scheduled maintenance date in the **Next Due Date** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **System Info**: A text field to capture general information about the system.
* **System Condition**: A multi-select field to rate the system's condition.
* **Service Visits**: A date field to record the date of previous services or visits.
* **Maintenance History**: A single-select field to categorize the type of maintenance performed.
* **Technician Notes**: A text field for technicians to record their notes or comments.
* **Facility Manager Comments**: A text field for facility managers or other stakeholders to provide feedback or comments.
* **Assigned Technician**: A single-select field to select the technician assigned to the task.
* **Service Date**: A date field to record the date of the service or maintenance visit.
* **Next Due Date**: A date field to record the next scheduled maintenance date.

## Tips

* Make sure to complete all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Use the correct format for dates (DD/MM/YYYY).
* Review and verify the information before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with your supervisor or maintenance staff for guidance.
