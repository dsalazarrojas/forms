# Security Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for submitting security requests to your organization's security team. It gathers information about the request, the employee requesting it, and the reason behind the request. The form will be reviewed and actioned by the appropriate personnel to ensure the security of your organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the requested information in each field.
* Make sure to submit the form with all required information.
* Review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Security Request** (`security_request_form`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your security request.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you submitted your security request.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your name as an employee making the request.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the department you belong to.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location where you work.
* **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`, text, required): Enter a brief reason for your security request.
* **Security Level** (`security_level`, text, required): Enter the security level required for your request.
* **Requestor** (`requestor`, text, required): Enter the name of the person making the request.
* **Approver** (`approver`, text, required): Enter the name of the person or group that will review and action your request.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Enter any comments or notes for the reviewer.
* **Approved/Denied** (`approved`, select_one, required): Select the status of your request (approved or denied).
* **Reason Denied** (`reason_denied`, text, required): Enter the reason why your request was denied.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required): Enter any additional notes about your request.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required): Enter the tool or resource assigned to you for your request.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, required): Enter the name of the person or group that will work on your request.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, required): Select the status of your request (pending, approved, or denied).
* **Comments Status** (`comments_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of your comments (pending, approved, or denied).
* **Created At** (`created_at`, date, not required): This field is automatically generated and shows when your request was created.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, date, not required): This field is automatically generated and shows when your request was last updated.
* **Deleted At** (`deleted_at`, date, not required): This field is automatically generated and shows when your request was deleted.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, not required): Enter the name of the person who submitted the request.
* **Reviewer ID** (`reviewer_id`, text, not required): Enter the ID of the person or group that reviewed your request.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, date, not required): This field is automatically generated and shows when your request was created.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, date, not required): This field is automatically generated and shows when your request was last updated.
* **Deleted At** (`deleted_at`, date, not required): This field is automatically generated and shows when your request was deleted.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information to help reviewers make informed decisions.
* Use clear and concise language in your comments and notes.
* If your request is denied, provide the reason why it was denied.
* If your request is approved, acknowledge the assigned tool and person.
* Review and update your request regularly to ensure it is completed on time.
