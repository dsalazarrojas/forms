# Sleep Disturbance Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sleep Disturbance Report Form is a tool used to collect data on sleep-related issues experienced by subjects. It helps in gathering detailed information on the subject's sleep patterns, quality, and any associated disturbances.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the report date in the format specified.
2. Provide the subject's name.
3. Select the sleep stages that the subject experienced during their sleep.
4. Enter the duration of the subject's sleep.
5. Choose the quality of the subject's sleep.
6. Select any disturbances that the subject experienced during their sleep.
7. Specify the location where the subject slept.
8. Enter any additional comments or observations about the subject's sleep.
9. Choose the tool assigned to the subject for their sleep analysis.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Date (<report_date>):** Enter the date on which the report is being submitted.
* **Subject Name (<subject_name>):** Enter the subject's name.
* **Sleep Stages (<sleep_stages>):** Select all stages of sleep that the subject experienced, including:
	+ Stage 1: Non-REM Sleep
	+ Stage 2: Light Sleep
	+ Stage 3: Deep Sleep
	+ Stage 4: REM Sleep
* **Sleep Duration (<sleep_duration>):** Enter the duration of the subject's sleep in hours.
* **Sleep Quality (<sleep_quality>):** Choose the quality of the subject's sleep, including:
	+ Poor
	+ Fair
	+ Good
	+ Excellent
* **Disturbances (<disturbances>):** Select all disturbances that the subject experienced during their sleep, including:
	+ Snoring
	+ Sleepwalking
	+ Sleep Talking
	+ Sleep Paralysis
	+ Sleep Apnea
	+ Sleep Terrors
* **Sleep Location (<sleep_location>):** Choose the location where the subject slept, including:
	+ Home
	+ Bed
	+ Hotel
	+ Clinic
	+ Lab
	+ Other
* **Comments (<comments>):** Enter any additional comments or observations about the subject's sleep.
* **Assigned Tool (<assigned_tool>):** Choose the tool assigned to the subject for their sleep analysis, including:
	+ chatjimmy
	+ chatjimmy1
	+ chatjimmy2

## Tips
Please ensure that each field is completed accurately and thoroughly to provide a detailed understanding of the subject's sleep disturbance.
