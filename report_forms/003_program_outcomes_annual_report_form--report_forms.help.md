# Program Outcomes Annual Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Program Outcomes Annual Report Form" is used to collect information on program outcomes and achievements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer each question truthfully.
2. For each question, provide a clear and concise response.
3. Be sure to answer all required questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Report Form** (`report_form`, text, optional): 
    This field is for you to provide a brief description of your program's report. Please enter a few sentences summarizing your program's key accomplishments and challenges.
* **Program Outcomes/Goals** (`program_outcomes`, select_multiple, optional): 
    Choose whether your program achieved its outcomes/goals for the past year. Select all applicable options from the dropdown menu.
* **Annual Outcome/Achievements** (`annual_outcome_achievements`, select_multiple, optional): 
    Indicate whether your program achieved its annual outcomes/achievements for the past year. Select all applicable options from the dropdown menu.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your reporting.
* If you are unsure about a particular outcome or achievement, please consult with your team or superiors.
* If you have any additional comments or concerns, please include them in the report form field.
