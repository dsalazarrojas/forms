# eod-report-for-client-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for clients to report any end-of-day issues or concerns to the provider. It helps the provider to understand and address client's concerns efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Client's Name" field.
2. If applicable, provide the name of the Visiting Associate (VA) who interacted with you in the "Va's Name" field.
3. Enter the date of the incident or issue in the "Date" field.
4. Optionally, provide a brief note about the issue or concern in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client's Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the client reporting this issue.
* **Va's Name** (`va_name`, text, optional): If applicable, provide the name of the VA who interacted with you.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the incident or issue.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Provide a brief note about the issue or concern if you wish to add additional context.
