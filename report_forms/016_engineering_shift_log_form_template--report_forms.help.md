# Engineering Shift Log Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help engineers record and track their shifts, including start and end times, break times, and any notes or comments about their work. It is intended to be used for quality control and administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow these steps:

* Fill in your start time in the "start_time" field.
* Enter your end time in the "end_time" field.
* If you took a break, enter your break start time in the "break_start_time" field and your break end time in the "break_end_time" field.
* Check the "assigned_tool" field to indicate if any tools were assigned to you during your shift. Choose "Yes" if a tool was assigned, and "No" if not.
* If applicable, enter the name or email of the person you were assigned to in the "assigned_to" field.
* Add any additional comments or notes about your shift in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **start_time**: Enter the start time of your shift.
* **end_time**: Enter the end time of your shift.
* **break_start_time**: Enter the time you took your break, if applicable.
* **break_end_time**: Enter the time your break ended, if applicable.
* **notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your shift.
* **assigned_tool**: Check "Yes" if a tool was assigned to you during your shift, and "No" if not.
* **assigned_to**: If applicable, enter the name or email of the person you were assigned to during your shift.
