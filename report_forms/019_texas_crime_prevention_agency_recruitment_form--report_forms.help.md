# Texas Crime Prevention Agency Recruitment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and manage information about job openings and applicants within the Texas Crime Prevention Agency. It helps in organizing and categorizing job postings for efficient recruitment and selection processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form effectively, please follow these steps:
1. Provide new employee details, including their name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2. Fill in the contact person's name and email address.
3. Specify the job title, job description, and job opening category.
4. Choose the job opening location, status, and type.
5. Select the job opening category and status.
6. Add any additional notes about the job opening.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Employee Details** (new_employee_details): Enter the name and contact information of the employee applying for the job.
* **Contact Info** (contact_info): Provide the name and email address of the contact person responsible for the job opening.
* **Job Description** (job_description): Write a brief description of the job posting.
* **Job Title** (job_title): Enter the title of the job opening.
* **Education** (education): Specify the educational background required for the job opening.
* **Work Experience** (work_experience): Provide any relevant work experience for the job opening.
* **Job Opening ID** (job_opening_id): Enter a unique identifier for the job opening.
* **Job Start Date** (job_start_date): Choose the start date of the job opening.
* **Job Status** (job_status): Select the status of the job opening from the provided options.
* **Job Category** (job_category): Choose the category of the job opening from the options provided.
* **Department** (department): Select the department where the job opening is located.
* **Job Opening Date** (job_opening_date): Choose the date of the job opening.
* **Job Opening Type** (job_opening_type): Select the type of the job opening.
* **Job Opening Status** (job_opening_status): Select the status of the job opening.
* **Job Opening Category** (job_opening_category): Choose the category of the job opening.
* **Job Opening Description** (job_opening_description): Write a brief description of the job opening.
* **Job Opening Location** (job_opening_location): Select the location of the job opening.
* **Job Opening Notes** (job_opening_notes): Add any additional notes about the job opening.
* **Job Opening Status Date** (job_opening_status_date): Choose the date of the job opening status update.
* **Contact Person** (contact_person): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the job opening.
* **Contact Person Email** (contact_person_email): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Job Status** (job_status): Select the status of the job opening.
* **Job Status Note** (job_status_note): Add any notes about the job status.
* **Job Opening Notes** (job_opening_notes): Add any additional notes about the job opening.
* **Job Opening Notes 2** (job_opening_notes2): Add any additional notes about the job opening.
* **Submit** (submit): Confirm that you have read the form and are ready to submit it.

## Tips
- Ensure you fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
- Use proper formatting and grammar when filling out the form.
- Double-check your information before submitting the form.
