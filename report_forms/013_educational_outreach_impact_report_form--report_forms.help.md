# Educational Outreach Impact Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Educational Outreach Impact Report Form is used to track and report on the impact of educational outreach programs. This form helps us understand the effectiveness of our outreach efforts, identify areas for improvement, and make data-driven decisions for future program development.

## How To Complete This Form

Before filling out this form, please have the following materials ready:

* Your school's name and website URL (if applicable)
* A clear understanding of the outreach goals and objectives
* Information on the programs you are involved with (type, length, etc.)
* Start and end dates for the program(s)
* Number of students participating
* Any relevant notes or supporting documents (e.g., photos, videos, certificates)
* Your contact email and phone number

Follow these steps to complete the form:

1. Enter your school's name in the "School Name" field.
2. Provide the URL of your school's website in the "School Website" field (if applicable).
3. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if your school has outreach goals.
4. Select all the programs you are involved with in the "School Partners" field.
5. If you are involved with other school programs not listed, enter them in the "Other School Partners" field.
6. Choose the type of program you are involved with (e.g., "Yes", "No") in the "Program Type" field.
7. Select the length of your program (e.g., "Yes", "No") in the "Program Length" field.
8. Enter the start date of your program in the "Start Date" field.
9. Enter the end date of your program in the "End Date" field.
10. Select all the staff members involved in your program in the "Staff Involved" field.
11. Enter the total number of staff hours spent on the program in the "Total Staff Hours" field (if applicable).
12. Enter the number of students participating in the program in the "Student Participants" field.
13. If there are other student participants not listed, enter them in the "Other Student Participants" field.
14. Enter any additional notes or comments about your program in the "Notes" field.
15. Upload any supporting documents for your program in the "File Uploads" field.
16. Enter your contact email in the "Email" field.
17. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Form** (`report_form`, text, required): A text input field where you can briefly describe your program or outreach efforts.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your school.
* **School Website** (`school_website`, text, optional): Provide the URL of your school's website (if applicable).
* **Outreach Goals** (`outreach_goals`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if your school has outreach goals.
* **School Partners** (`school_partners`, select_multiple, required): Select all the programs you are involved with.
* **Other School Partners** (`school_partners_other`, text, optional): If you are involved with other school programs not listed, enter them here.
* **Program Type** (`program_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of program you are involved with (e.g., "Yes", "No").
* **Program Length** (`program_length`, select_one, required): Select the length of your program (e.g., "Yes", "No").
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of your program.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of your program.
* **Staff Involved** (`staff_involved`, select_multiple, required): Select all the staff members involved in your program.
* **Total Staff Hours** (`total_staff_hours`, number, optional): Enter the total number of staff hours spent on the program.
* **Student Participants** (`student_participants`, number, required): Enter the number of students participating in the program.
* **Other Student Participants** (`student_participants_other`, text, optional): If there are other student participants not listed, enter them here.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Enter any additional comments or notes about your program.
* **File Uploads** (`file_uploads`, text, required): Upload any supporting documents for your program.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your contact email.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Email Notifications** (`email_notifications`, text, optional): If you want to receive email notifications, enter your email address here.
* **Send Reminders** (`send_reminders`, text, optional): Enter any reminders or instructions for staff or students.
