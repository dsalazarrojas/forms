# outstation_requisition_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Outstation Requisition Form is a request form for employees to submit outstation travel details. This form is used to gather information for outstation travels, including travel dates, reasons, and cost details. The form helps the management team to approve or reject outstation travel requests in a centralized and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

*   Enter the required information accurately and completely, as this will help the management team to review and process your outstation travel request efficiently.
*   Ensure that you select the correct options for Outstation Type and Department from the provided drop-down menu.
*   If you are submitting this form on behalf of another employee, please enter their name, supervisor, and approver details.
*   Enter the travel start and end dates in the correct format (yyyy-mm-dd).
*   Enter the travel start and end times in the correct format (HH:MM).
*   Enter any additional comments or details about your outstation travel.
*   Submit the form once you have filled in all the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Outstation Travel Details** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, required: false): This field is a brief description of your outstation travel details.
*   **Employee Name** (`id: 2`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the name of the employee requesting the outstation travel.
*   **Department** (`id: 3`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false): Select the department the employee belongs to from the provided options.
*   **Travel Start Date** (`id: 4`, `type: date`, required: false): Enter the date when the employee will start their outstation travel (yyyy-mm-dd).
*   **Travel End Date** (`id: 5`, `type: date`, required: false): Enter the date when the employee will end their outstation travel (yyyy-mm-dd).
*   **Reason for Outstation** (`id: 6`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the reason for the outstation travel.
*   **Employee Supervisor** (`id: 7`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the name of the employee's supervisor.
*   **Employee Approver** (`id: 8`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the name of the employee who will be approving this outstation travel.
*   **Manager Approver** (`id: 9`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the name of the manager who will be approving this outstation travel.
*   **Approval Status** (`id: 10`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Select the approval status of your outstation travel from the provided options.
*   **Comments** (`id: 11`, `type: note`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or details about your outstation travel.
*   **Travel Start Time** (`id: 12`, `type: time`, required: false): Enter the time when the employee will start their outstation travel (HH:MM).
*   **Travel End Time** (`id: 13`, `type: time`, required: false): Enter the time when the employee will end their outstation travel (HH:MM).
*   **Outstation Type** (`id: 14`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false): Select the type of outstation travel from the provided options.
*   **Travel Location** (`id: 15`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the location of the outstation travel.
*   **Outstation Duration** (`id: 16`, `type: number`, required: false): Enter the duration of the outstation travel.
*   **Outstation Cost** (`id: 17`, `type: number`, required: false): Enter the cost of the outstation travel.
*   **Approval Date** (`id: 18`, `type: date`, required: false): Enter the date of approval for this outstation travel.
*   **Approval Time** (`id: 19`, `type: time`, required: false): Enter the time of approval for this outstation travel.
*   **Comments 2** (`id: 20`, `type: note`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or details about your outstation travel.
*   **Outstation Requisition Form 2** (`id: 21`, `type: text`, required: false): This field is not used (appears to be a duplicate of the first form).
*   **Outstation Requester** (`id: 22`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person requesting this outstation travel.
*   **Outstation Requester Email** (`id: 23`, `type: email`, required: false): Enter the email of the person requesting this outstation travel.
*   **Outstation Requester Phone** (`id: 24`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the person requesting this outstation travel.
*   **Form Submitted By** (`id: 25`, `type: text`, required: false): This field is not used (appears to be a duplicate of another field).

Note: Please ensure that you fill in all the required fields and submit the form accurately for efficient processing.
