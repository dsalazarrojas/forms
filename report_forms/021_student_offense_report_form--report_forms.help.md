# Student Offense Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document incidents involving students that have resulted in disciplinary actions. It helps gather relevant information about the incident, which can be used to inform future decisions or actions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the student's name in the "student_name" field. This is the name of the student who was involved in the incident.
2. Enter the student's grade level in the "student_grade" field. This is the grade level of the student at the time of the incident.
3. Provide a detailed description of the offense in the "offense_description" field. This should include any relevant details about the incident, including any witnesses or evidence.
4. Describe any actions that were taken in response to the incident in the "actions_taken" field. This should include any meetings or conversations with the student, or any other steps taken to address the incident.
5. Provide a summary of any conversations with the student in the "conversation_summary" field. This can help inform future interactions with the student.
6. Enter the date of the incident in the "offense_date" field. This is the date on which the incident occurred.
7. Enter the time of the incident in the "offense_time" field. This is the time at which the incident occurred.
8. Enter the time zone of the incident in the "offense_time_zone" field. This is the time zone in which the incident occurred.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **student_name** (Text, Required, Text): Enter the name of the student who was involved in the incident.
* **student_grade** (Number, Optional, Number): Enter the grade level of the student at the time of the incident.
* **offense_description** (Text, Optional, Text): Provide a detailed description of the incident, including any relevant details or evidence.
* **actions_taken** (Text, Optional, Text): Describe any actions that were taken in response to the incident, including any meetings or conversations with the student.
* **conversation_summary** (Text, Optional, Text): Provide a summary of any conversations with the student, which can help inform future interactions.
* **offense_date** (Date, Optional, Date): Enter the date on which the incident occurred.
* **offense_time** (Time, Optional, Time): Enter the time at which the incident occurred.
* **offense_time_zone** (Time, Optional, Time): Enter the time zone in which the incident occurred.
