# Crowdstrike Outage Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report an outage on a service, providing details about the impact, duration, cause, and resolution of the outage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the service impact from the options provided.
2. Enter the date and time when the outage started.
3. Enter the date and time when the outage ended.
4. Enter the duration of the outage.
5. Enter a brief description of the outage.
6. Select whether the outage affected teams.
7. Select the root cause of the outage.
8. Select whether the outage was reported by someone.
9. Enter the time when the report was submitted.
10. Select whether the outage has been closed.
11. Enter the time when the outage was closed.
12. Select whether the closed report was done by someone.
13. Enter the name of the person who closed the report.
14. Enter the email address of the person who submitted the report.
15. Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the report.
16. Enter any comments from the team lead.
17. Enter any comments from the customer.
18. Enter any next steps that need to be taken.
19. Select whether the outage was closed by someone.
20. Select the name of the person who closed the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Impact**: Select the impact of the outage on the service.
* **Date of Outage**: Enter the date and time when the outage started.
* **Start Time**: Enter the time when the outage started.
* **End Time**: Enter the time when the outage ended.
* **Duration**: Enter the length of time the outage lasted.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of the outage.
* **Cause**: Enter a description of what caused the outage.
* **Affected Teams**: Select which teams were affected by the outage.
* **Root Cause**: Select the root cause of the outage.
* **Reported By**: Select whether the report was made by a person.
* **Report Time**: Enter the time when the report was submitted.
* **Closed**: Select whether the outage has been closed.
* **Closed Time**: Enter the time when the outage was closed.
* **Closed By**: Select whether the report was closed by someone.
* **Closed By Name**: Enter the name of the person who closed the report.
* **Report By Name**: Select whether the report was submitted by someone.
* **Report By Email**: Enter the email address of the person who submitted the report.
* **Report By Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the report.
* **Report By Phone 2**: Enter a phone number of a second person who submitted the report.
* **Team Lead Comments**: Enter any comments from the team lead.
* **Customer Comments**: Enter any comments from the customer.
* **Next Steps**: Enter any next steps that need to be taken.
* **Closed By**: Select whether the report was closed by someone.
* **Closed By Name**: Enter the name of the person who closed the report.
