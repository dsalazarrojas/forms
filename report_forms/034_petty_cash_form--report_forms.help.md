# Petty Cash Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Petty Cash Form is used to record and track petty cash transactions, ensuring accurate and transparent record-keeping.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form accurately and completely.
2.  Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3.  Review and verify the information before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as a reference for submission.
*   **Date** (`date`, date, required: true): Enter the date of the transaction.
*   **Amount** (`amount`, number, required: true): Enter the amount of the transaction.
*   **Account Number** (`account_number`, text, required: false): Enter the account number related to the transaction (if applicable).
*   **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, text, required: true): Enter the name of the vendor or supplier.
*   **Description** (`description`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the transaction.
*   **Account Manager Name** (`account_manager_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the account manager responsible for the account (if applicable).
*   **Department** (`department`, text, required: false): Enter the department related to the transaction (if applicable).
*   **Amount Type** (`amount_type`, select_one, required: true): Select whether the transaction is for "Cash" or "Petty Cash".
*   **Amount Submitted** (`amount_submitted`, number, required: true): Enter the amount submitted for the transaction.
*   **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person submitting the transaction (if applicable).
*   **Date Submitted** (`submitted_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date the transaction was submitted.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about the transaction.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the person responsible for the transaction (if applicable).
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the transaction (if applicable).
*   **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the person responsible for the transaction (if applicable).
*   **Amount Approved** (`amount_approved`, number, required: false): Enter the amount approved for the transaction.
*   **Approved By** (`approved_by`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person approving the transaction (if applicable).
*   **Date Approved** (`approved_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date the transaction was approved.
*   **Status** (`status`, text, required: false): Enter the status of the transaction (if applicable).
*   **Amount Pending** (`amount_pending`, number, required: false): Enter the amount pending for the transaction.
*   **Amount Pending Status** (`amount_pending_status`, text, required: false): Enter the status of the amount pending (if applicable).
*   **Amount Reimbursed** (`amount_reimbursed`, number, required: false): Enter the amount reimbursed for the transaction.
*   **Reimbursed By** (`reimbursed_by`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person reimbursing the transaction (if applicable).
*   **Reimbursed Date** (`reimbursed_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date the transaction was reimbursed.

## Tips

*   Review all fields carefully before submission to ensure accuracy.
*   Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
*   Verify that the transaction aligns with company policies and procedures.
