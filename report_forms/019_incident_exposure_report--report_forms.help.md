# Incident Exposure Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about incidents that may have exposed an individual or a group to a potential hazard or risk. The form is designed to gather essential details about the incident, including date, time, location, and incident type, to facilitate prompt action and resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the date of the incident.
2. Enter the time of the incident.
3. Provide the location where the incident occurred.
4. Describe the type of incident.
5. Give a detailed account of what happened during the incident.
6. Indicate who reported the incident (Reporting Person).
7. If necessary, get supervisor's approval.
8. Report the incident status.
9. Provide a follow-up on the incident resolution.
10. If a safety meeting was held, report the date and time of the meeting and include any notable notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exposure Report** (id: 1): This is the main section where you can provide a brief description of the incident.
* **Date** (id: 2): Enter the date of the incident in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Time** (id: 3): Enter the time of the incident in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Location** (id: 4): Provide the exact location where the incident occurred.
* **Type** (id: 5): Describe the type of incident that happened.
* **Incident Details** (id: 6): Give a detailed account of what happened during the incident, including any contributing factors.
* **Reporting Person** (id: 7): Indicate who reported the incident.
* **Supervisor Approval** (id: 8): If necessary, get your supervisor's approval by selecting 'Yes' to confirm.
* **Status** (id: 9): Report the current status of the incident.
* **Follow-up** (id: 10): Provide a follow-up on the incident resolution or any action taken.
* **Safety Meeting** (id: 12): If a safety meeting was held, report the date and time of the meeting.
* **Safety Meeting Notes** (id: 13): Include any notable notes from the safety meeting.
* **Additional Information** (id: 14): Provide any additional information that might be helpful for incident resolution.
* **Supervisor** (id: 8): This is where you can select whether your supervisor has acknowledged and approved the incident report.
* **Resolution** (id: 11): Describe the resolution or action taken to address the incident.

## Tips

* Always refer to your organization's incident reporting guidelines and procedures before filling out this form.
* Ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete.
* Keep the form up-to-date and current to reflect any changes or updates to the incident status.
* Review this form with your supervisor before submitting it for their approval.
