# Immigration Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Immigration Intake Form is a tool used to collect essential information from individuals seeking immigration services. This form is designed to gather personal and contact information, as well as details about the individual's background and citizenship status. The form is intended to be completed by individuals who are seeking immigration-related assistance or services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Immigration Intake Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. This is the name by which you are known.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field. This is the surname by which you are known.
3. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the "Date Of Birth" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/1990).
4. Select your **Place Of Birth** from the dropdown options. This is the country where you were born.
5. Select your **Country Of Birth** from the dropdown options. This is the country of your citizenship.
6. Select your **citizenship_status** from the dropdown options. This indicates your current citizenship status.
7. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field. This is your personal email address.
8. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone Number" field. This is your personal phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your given or commonly used name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter the date you were born in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Place Of Birth**: Select the country where you were born.
* **Country Of Birth**: Select the country of your citizenship.
* **citizenship_status**: Select your current citizenship status.
* **Email**: Enter your email address in the format example@domain.com.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your personal phone number.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Use the correct format for the "Date Of Birth" field.
* Verify that your email address is formatted correctly.
* Make sure to select a country of birth that matches your actual birthplace.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with an immigration expert or contact us for assistance.
* Please double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
