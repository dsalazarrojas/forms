# Report Storm Drain Issues In Your Community - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to report issues related to storm drains in your community, such as blockages, debris, or other maintenance concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by describing the issue you are reporting in the **description** field.
2. Provide the **location** where the issue is occurring.
3. If you have taken photos of the issue, select the **photos** field and upload them.
4. In the **issue_type** field, select all the types of issues that are present (e.g. storm drain, street, park, or other).
5. Enter your contact information in the **submitter**, **submitter_email**, and **submitter_phone** fields to ensure that follow-up can be made with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **description**: A brief description of the issue you are reporting.
* **location**: The location of the issue, including the street address, park or other relevant details.
* **photos**: Up to 3 photos of the issue to help illustrate the problem.
* **issue_type**: Select all types of issues that are present (e.g. storm drain, street, park, or other).
* **photos**: Up to 3 photos of the issue to help illustrate the problem.
* **submitter**: Your name or alias.
* **submitter_email**: Your email address.
* **submitter_phone**: Your phone number.

## Tips
* Please make sure to provide clear and concise descriptions and photos to help us understand the issue.
* If you are reporting multiple issues, please submit separate forms for each issue.
* If you are reporting an issue that has already been addressed, please do not submit this form.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* Please ensure that you have permission to upload any photos you take, and that they are in a format that can be easily downloaded (e.g. JPEG or PNG).
