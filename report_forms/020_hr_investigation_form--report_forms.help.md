# HR Investigation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Investigation Form is used to document and investigate employee incidents within your organization. It gathers essential information to help the HR team identify the issue, interview relevant parties, and take steps to resolve the matter.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Provide detailed descriptions for each field.
3. Review and submit the form for further action.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee**: Enter the employee's name and other relevant details.
* **Investigation Date**: Enter the date when the incident occurred or was reported.
* **Event Description**: Provide a detailed description of what happened, including any relevant circumstances.
* **Location**: Specify the location where the incident occurred.
* **Action Taken**: Describe any actions taken to address the issue.
* **Resolution Date**: Enter the date when the issue was resolved (if applicable).
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or information about the incident.
* **Employee Response**: Get feedback from the employee involved.
* **Supervisor Response**: Get feedback from the employee's supervisor.
* **Manager Response**: Get feedback from the employee's manager.
* **Investigator Name**: Enter the name of the person leading the investigation.
* **Investigator Title**: Enter the title of the person leading the investigation.
* **Investigator Department**: Enter the department of the person leading the investigation.
* **Investigator Extension**: Enter the extension of the person leading the investigation.
* **Investigator Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person leading the investigation.
* **Investigator Email**: Enter the email address of the person leading the investigation.
* **Employee ID**: Enter the employee ID number.
* **Employee Job Title**: Enter the employee's job title.
* **Employee Department**: Enter the employee's department.
* **Employee Phone**: Enter the employee's phone number.
* **Employee Extension**: Enter the employee's extension.
* **Employee Email**: Enter the employee's email address.
* **Witness Employees Response**: Get feedback from witnesses or other employees involved in the incident.
* **Witness Managers Response**: Get feedback from managers who witnessed or were involved in the incident.
* **Witness Supervisors Response**: Get feedback from supervisors who witnessed or were involved in the incident.

Note: All fields are required unless specified as optional. Please fill out the form carefully and thoroughly, as it will help the HR team to investigate the incident efficiently.
