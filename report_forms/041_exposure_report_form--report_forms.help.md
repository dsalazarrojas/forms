# exposure_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for reporting exposure incidents, gathering information about incidents, witnesses, and witnesses' statements. It's crucial to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a proper investigation and response.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide Incident Details: Enter a detailed description of the incident, including location, date, and any other relevant information.
2. Enter Witness Information: Gather and enter witness details, including names, contacts, relationships, and descriptions.
3. Reporting Officer Information: Enter your own contact information, including title, email, phone number, and department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Details**: Enter a detailed description of the incident, including location and any other relevant information.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the incident.
* **Location**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Substance**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the incident involved a hazardous substance.
* **Symptoms**: Describe any symptoms the individual experienced due to the incident.
* **Medical Treatment**: Describe any medical treatment received by the individual.
* **Witness Details**: Enter witness information, including name, contact, relationship, and description.
* **Reporting Date**: Enter the date on which this report is being submitted.
* **Reporting Time**: Enter the time of day when this report is being submitted.
* **Reporting Officer**: Enter your name and contact information.
* **Witness Names**: Enter the names of any witnesses.
* **Witness Contacts**: Enter witness contact information.
* **Witness Relationship**: Describe the relationship between you and the witness.
* **Witness Description**: Describe the witness's appearance or circumstances.
* **Witness Signature**: Witness's signature (not required, but recommended).
* **Reporting Officer Signature**: Your signature (required).
* **Reporting Officer Title**: Enter your title.
* **Witness Title**: Enter witness title (if applicable).
* **Reporting Officer Email**: Enter your email address (required).
* **Witness Email**: Enter witness's email address (not required).
* **Reporting Officer Phone**: Enter your phone number (required).
* **Witness Phone**: Enter witness's phone number (not required).
* **Witness Address**: Enter witness's address (not required).
* **Witness Job Title**: Enter witness's job title (not required).
* **Reporting Officer Department**: Enter your department (not required).

Please review each field carefully and fill in the required information accurately to ensure a thorough report. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or department for assistance.
