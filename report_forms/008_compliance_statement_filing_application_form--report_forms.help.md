# Compliance Statement Filing Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Compliance Statement Filing Application Form is a regulatory form used to collect information from filers for official compliance statement filings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your filer information, including your name and contact email.
2. Choose the type of compliance filing you are submitting (e.g. Initial filing, Amendment, Annual report).
3. Enter the date of submission.
4. Briefly describe the statement being filed.
5. List any supporting documents or links attached.
6. Declare that the information provided is true and accurate.
7. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Filer name** (`filer_name`, text, required): Enter your individual or organization name as it appears on official records.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for filing confirmation and any further contact.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter an optional phone number for contact.
* **Filing type** (`filing_type`, select_one, required): Select from the options:
	+ Initial filing
	+ Amendment
	+ Annual report
* **Filing date** (`filing_date`, date, required): Enter the date of submission in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Summary of statement** (`summary_of_statement`, text, required): Briefly describe the purpose and contents of the statement being filed.
* **Supporting documents** (`supporting_documents`, text, optional): List any supporting documents or links attached to this submission.
* **Declaration** (`declaration`, select_one, required): Select from the options:
	+ I declare
	+ Other (provide a brief explanation)
