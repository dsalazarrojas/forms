# Benefit Investigation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Benefit Investigation Form is used to collect information about a specific issue or incident that requires investigation. Its purpose is to gather relevant data to analyze and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the investigator's information: Investigator Name and Investigator ID (if required).
2. Provide the date and time of the investigation.
3. Enter the subject's information: Subject Name and Date of Birth (if required).
4. Describe the alleged incident and its type (e.g., Fraud, Misrepresentation, Unauthorized Use, etc.).
5. Document the evidence gathered during the investigation, including witnesses interviewed.
6. Summarize the findings of the investigation.
7. Determine if the allegation was substantiated or not, and if there were any fraud indicators.
8. Set the priority level of the issue (Low, Medium, High, or Urgent).
9. Add any additional notes or comments if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Investigator Name** (text, required): Enter your name as the person conducting the investigation.
* **Investigator ID** (text, optional): Enter your ID number (if applicable).
* **Date of Investigation** (date, required): Enter the date the investigation started.
* **Time of Investigation** (time, optional): Enter the time the investigation started.
* **Subject Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the person being investigated.
* **Date of Birth** (date, optional): Enter the subject's date of birth (if required).
* **Case Number** (text, required): Enter a unique number for the case.
* **Type of Allegation** (select_one, required): Choose the type of issue being investigated (e.g., Fraud, Misrepresentation, etc.).
* **Description of Allegation** (text, required): Provide a detailed description of the alleged incident.
* **Date Alleged Incident Occurred** (date, optional): Enter the date the alleged incident occurred.
* **Complainant Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the person who reported the issue.
* **Evidence Gathered** (text, required): Document any evidence collected during the investigation.
* **Witnesses Interviewed** (text, optional): List the names of people spoken to during the investigation.
* **Investigation Findings** (text, required): Summarize the results of the investigation.
* **Was Allegation Substantiated** (select_one, required): Determine if the allegation was substantiated or not.
* **Recommended Action** (text, required): Suggest a course of action to take next.
* **Priority Level** (select_one, required): Set the priority level of the issue (Low, Medium, High, or Urgent).
* **Fraud Indicators Identified** (select_one, optional): Indicate if any fraud indicators were found.
* **Additional Notes** (text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes if needed.

This form is designed to be user-friendly and straightforward. By following these steps and understanding the purpose of each field, you'll be able to complete the form correctly and efficiently.
