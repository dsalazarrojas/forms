# submission_count_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to record and track submissions for internal purposes, such as monitoring employee performance or tracking progress on specific tasks. It captures essential information to ensure that submissions are accurately and consistently recorded.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Input your Name**: Enter your name in the "Name" field. This is a required field to help identify you as the submitter.
2. **Enter your Email**: Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This is an optional but recommended field to help us contact you in case we need further information.
3. **Choose Timestamp**: Select the date and time when the submission was made in the "Timestamp" field. This is an optional field but recommended for accurate tracking.
4. **Select Department**: Enter your department in the "Department" field. This helps categorize submissions for better tracking.
5. **Provide Brief Purpose**: Enter a brief description of the submission in the "Purpose" field. This helps us understand the reason behind the submission.
6. **Select Submitter**: Choose who actually submitted the form in the "Submitted By" field. Please select from the available options or type "Other" if not listed.
7. **Optional Submission Count**: If you know the number of submissions, enter it in the "Submission Count" field. This is an optional field but helps in tracking progress.
8. **Submission Count Type**: Select the type of submission count from the options provided in the "Submission Count Type" field. This ensures accurate tracking and categorization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your name to identify yourself as the submitter.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Timestamp** (`timestamp`, `date`, optional): Select the date and time of the submission for accurate tracking.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, optional): Enter your department for categorization.
* **Purpose** (`purpose`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the submission.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `select_one`, optional): Choose who submitted the form from the options or type "Other" if not listed.
* **Submission Count** (`submission_count`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of submissions if applicable.
* **Submission Count Type** (`submission_count_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the type of submission count for accurate tracking and categorization.
