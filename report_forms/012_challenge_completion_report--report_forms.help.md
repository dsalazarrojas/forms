# Challenge Completion Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Challenge Completion Report is used to gather information about the completion of a challenge from participants. This report helps us understand the challenges participants faced, the solutions they implemented, and their overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to access the form.
2. Select the report date and fill in the full name of the participant.
3. Provide the unique identifier number if applicable.
4. Enter the name of the challenge completed.
5. Select the challenge start and end dates.
6. Choose the completion status of the challenge.
7. If partially completed, enter the percentage complete.
8. Upload or provide supporting evidence or documentation.
9. Describe the goals met during the challenge.
10. List challenges or obstacles encountered.
11. Explain how the challenges were overcome.
12. Summarize the key takeaways or lessons learned.
13. Reflect on personal growth or development.
14. Rate the overall effort level required.
15. Rate the difficulty of the challenge.
16. Express satisfaction with completion.
17. Indicate interest in repeating the challenge.
18. Provide recommendations for future participants.
19. Finally, certify that the report is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Date** (`report_date`): Enter the date the report was submitted.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`): Enter the full name of the challenge participant.
* **Participant ID Number** (`participant_id`): Provide the unique identifier number if applicable.
* **Challenge Name** (`challenge_name`): Enter the name of the challenge completed.
* **Challenge Start Date** (`challenge_start_date`): Enter the date the challenge began.
* **Challenge End Date** (`challenge_end_date`): Enter the date the challenge was completed.
* **Completion Status** (`completion_status`): Select the completion status of the challenge (Fully Completed, Partially Completed, Not Completed, Completed Early).
* **Percentage Complete** (`completion_percentage`): If the challenge was partially completed, enter the percentage complete.
* **Supporting Evidence or Documentation** (`supporting_evidence`): Upload or provide photos, screenshots, or files proving challenge completion.
* **Goals Met During Challenge** (`goals_met`): Describe the goals achieved during the challenge.
* **Challenges or Obstacles Encountered** (`challenges_encountered`): List the challenges or obstacles faced during the challenge.
* **Solutions Implemented** (`solutions_implemented`): Explain how the challenges were overcome.
* **Lessons Learned** (`lessons_learned`): Summarize the key takeaways or lessons learned from the challenge.
* **Personal Growth or Development** (`personal_growth`): Reflect on personal growth or development experienced during the challenge.
* **Effort Level Required** (`effort_level`): Rate the overall effort level required to complete the challenge (Minimal Effort, Moderate Effort, Significant Effort, Extreme Effort).
* **Difficulty Rating** (`difficulty_rating`): Rate the difficulty of the challenge (Very Easy, Easy, Moderate, Difficult, Very Difficult).
* **Satisfaction with Completing Challenge** (`satisfaction_level`): Express satisfaction with completing the challenge (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
* **Would You Do This Challenge Again** (`would_do_again`): Indicate interest in repeating the challenge (Yes, Maybe, No).
* **Recommendations for Future Participants** (`recommendations`): Provide advice for future participants.
* **I Certify This Report is Accurate and Complete** (`signature_acknowledgment`): Acknowledge the truthfulness of the report and agree that it is accurate and complete.
