# Meeting Debrief Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This template is designed to collect information about a meeting, including its organizer, date, time, location, and notes. The form helps ensure that all important details are recorded and can be used for reference or follow-up action in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and fill in the required information for your meeting.
2. Select the correct meeting organizer from the options.
3. Choose the meeting date and time from the calendar or clock interface.
4. Enter the meeting location.
5. Add any relevant meeting notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Organizer** (`meeting_organizer`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Select the person who coordinated or facilitated the meeting.
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date, required: false):
	+ Enter the date the meeting took place.
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, time, required: false):
	+ Enter the start time of the meeting in 24-hour format.
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, text, required: false):
	+ Provide the address or venue of the meeting.
* **Meeting Notes** (`meeting_notes`, text, required: false):
	+ Write down any relevant comments, insights, or outcomes from the meeting.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you fill in all necessary information accurately to ensure effective debriefing and follow-up action.
- If the meeting was cancelled or rescheduled, please update the meeting date and time accordingly.
- Use the meeting notes field to record important action items, decisions, or next steps.
