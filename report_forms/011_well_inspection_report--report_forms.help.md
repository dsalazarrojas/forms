# Well Inspection Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the inspection results of a well and identify any necessary actions to be taken for its maintenance and repair. It provides a structured approach to capturing critical information about the well's condition and associated tasks or actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter a title for the report in the "well_inspection_report" field.
* Provide the facility's address in the "facility_address" field.
* Enter the name of the inspector in the "inspector_name" field.
* Enter the name of the well in the "well_name" field.
* Select the well's current status from the "well_status" field.
* Enter the date of the inspection in the "inspection_date" field.
* Attach any photos or files related to the inspection in the "photos_and_files" field.
* Enter any notes and recommendations in the "notes_and_recommendations" field.
* Select any recommended actions from the "recommended_actions" field. If "Other" is selected, use the "recommended_actions_others" field to enter a detailed description.
* Select the assigned inspector(s) from the "assigned_inspector" field. If "Other" is selected, use the "assigned_inspector_others" field to enter their name.
* Select the assigned tool from the "assigned_tool" field.
* Select the assigned manager(s) from the "assigned_manager" field. If "Other" is selected, use the "assigned_manager_others" field to enter their name.
* Enter any additional notes in the "notes" field.
* Enter the facility's manager's name in the "facility_manager" field.
* Enter the facility owner's name in the "facility_owner" field.
* Enter the facility's email address in the "facility_email" field.
* Enter the facility's phone number in the "facility_phone" field.
* Enter the facility's fax number in the "facility_fax" field.
* Enter any additional notes about the facility in the "facility_notes" field.
* Enter the location of the well in the "well_location" field.
* Enter the well's ID in the "well_id" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **well_inspection_report (report_form_title)**: Enter a brief title for the report.
* **facility_address**: Enter the facility's address where the well is located.
* **inspector_name**: Enter the name of the inspector who performed the inspection.
* **well_name**: Enter the name of the well being inspected.
* **well_status**: Select the current status of the well, which can be Good, Caution, Critical, or N/A.
* **inspection_date**: Enter the date the inspection was performed.
* **photos_and_files**: Attach any photos or files related to the inspection.
* **notes_and_recommendations**: Enter any notes or recommendations for the well's maintenance or repair.
* **recommended_actions**: Select any recommended actions for the well's maintenance or repair. If "Other" is selected, use the "recommended_actions_others" field to enter a detailed description.
* **recommended_actions_others**: Enter a detailed description of any recommended actions not listed in the previous field.
* **assigned_inspector (assigned_inspector)**: Select the assigned inspector(s) for the well's maintenance or repair. If "Other" is selected, use the "assigned_inspector_others" field to enter their name.
* **assigned_inspector_others**: Enter the name of the assigned inspector if they are not listed in the previous field.
* **assigned_tool (assigned_tool)**: Select the assigned tool for the well's maintenance or repair.
* **assigned_manager (assigned_manager)**: Select the assigned manager(s) for the well's maintenance or repair. If "Other" is selected, use the "assigned_manager_others" field to enter their name.
* **assigned_manager_others**: Enter the name of the assigned manager if they are not listed in the previous field.
* **notes**: Enter any additional notes about the well's condition or inspection.
* **photos**: Attach any photos related to the inspection.
* **facility_manager (facility_manager)**: Enter the facility manager's name.
* **facility_owner (facility_owner)**: Enter the facility owner's name.
* **facility_email (facility_email)**: Enter the facility's email address.
* **facility_phone (facility_phone)**: Enter the facility's phone number.
* **facility_fax (facility_fax)**: Enter the facility's fax number.
* **facility_notes (facility_notes)**: Enter any additional notes about the facility.
* **well_location (well_location)**: Enter the location of the well.
* **well_id (well_id)**: Enter the well's ID.

**Tips**

* Always fill in the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure the well is properly inspected and any necessary actions are taken.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the facility's maintenance or repair manual or consult with the facility manager.
* Attach any supporting photos or files to provide a clear understanding of the well's condition.
* Use the "Other" option only when necessary and provide a detailed description in the relevant field.
