# Patient Safety Quarterly Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report incidents related to patient safety within the quarter. It is a crucial tool for tracking and addressing potential safety concerns that may impact patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the incident date and time of day when the incident occurred.
2. Specify the location where the incident occurred.
3. Provide a brief description of what happened.
4. Identify the type of incident.
5. Indicate the department(s) involved in the incident.
6. Select whether the incident was reported by the patient or not.
7. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Page** (First Page, note, not required): This is the introductory page that explains the purpose and instructions for the form. Please read this page carefully before starting the form.
* **Incident Date** (incident_date, date, not required): Enter the date when the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (incident_time, time, not required): Enter the time of day when the incident occurred (e.g., 14:30 for 2:30 PM).
* **Time Zone** (incident_time_zone, text, not required): Enter the time zone where the incident occurred (e.g., EST for Eastern Standard Time).
* **Location** (location, text, not required): Enter the location where the incident occurred (e.g., ward, room number, or department).
* **Incident Description** (incident_description, text, not required): Provide a brief description of what happened during the incident.
* **Incident Type** (incident_type, select_one, not required): Check the box to indicate whether the incident was of the type 'Yes' or 'No'. This helps to categorize incidents for further analysis.
* **Department Involved** (department, select_multiple, not required): Check the box to indicate which departments were involved in the incident.
* **Incident Reporter** (incident_reporter, select_multiple, not required): Check the box to indicate who reported the incident ('Yes' for patient, 'No' for non-patient).

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when providing dates, times, and locations to help track incidents accurately.
* If unsure about an incident type, refer to your unit or department's incident reporting guidelines.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
