# Ministry Leader Monthly Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from ministry leaders about their team's activities and progress over the course of a month.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the previous month's report to ensure that you have a clear understanding of what has changed.
2. Select the relevant information for the current month from the provided options.
3. Fill out all required fields with accurate and relevant information.
4. Double-check your work for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **general_info** (Select One): Select the general information relevant to your team, such as the team's current status or any notable events that occurred.
* **monthly_report** (Select Multiple): Select all relevant events or activities that occurred within the team during the month, such as meetings, events, or projects.
* **date** (Date): Enter the date of the event or activity being reported.
* **time** (Time): Enter the time of the event or activity being reported.
* **note** (Note): Enter any additional notes or comments about the event or activity.
* **email** (Email): Enter your email address for contact information.
* **phone** (Text): Enter your phone number for contact information.
* **category** (Select Multiple): Select all relevant categories for the event or activity, such as type of project or type of event.
* **subcategory** (Select One): Select the specific subcategory for the event or activity.
* **location** (Select One): Select the location where the event or activity occurred.
* **team** (Select One): Select the team that was involved in the event or activity.
* **event_type** (Select One): Select the type of event or activity that occurred.
* **event_date** (Date): Enter the date of the event or activity.
* **event_time** (Time): Enter the time of the event or activity.
* **event_note** (Note): Enter any additional notes or comments about the event or activity.
* **team_size** (Number): Enter the number of team members involved in the event or activity.
* **team_hours** (Time): Enter the total hours worked by the team during the event or activity.
* **location_note** (Note): Enter any additional notes or comments about the location.
* **comments** (Note): Enter any additional comments or suggestions about the team's performance.
* **follow_up** (Select One): Select the follow-up action that is required or recommended.
* **follow_up_note** (Note): Enter any additional notes or comments about the follow-up action.
* **next_step** (Select One): Select the next step that is recommended for the team.
* **next_step_note** (Note): Enter any additional notes or comments about the next step.
* **signature** (Select One): Select the person who will sign off on the report.

Note: This form is designed to be filled out on a monthly basis, and all fields should be completed as accurately and comprehensively as possible. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or administrator.
