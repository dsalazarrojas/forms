# Waterway Safety Work Method Statement - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Waterway Safety Work Method Statement is a template used to document and record the safety protocols and procedures for work performed on waterway sites. This form is essential for ensuring that all stakeholders are aware of potential hazards and risks associated with the work and have taken necessary precautions to mitigate them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Contractor Info** (`contractor_info`): This field is required. Please enter the contractor's name and contact information, as this will be used for communication and reference purposes.
2. **Site Details** (`site_details`): Briefly describe the location of the work site, including any relevant details that may impact safety.
3. **Safety Plan** (`safety_plan`): Outline the safety measures and protocols that will be implemented during the work.
4. **Hazards** (`hazards`): Indicate if there are any hazards present on the site. Select 'Yes' for any hazards identified.
5. **Risk Assessment** (`risk_assessment`): Provide a detailed assessment of the risks associated with the work, including any measures taken to mitigate them.
6. **Control Measures** (`control_measures`): Describe the control measures put in place to mitigate or eliminate identified hazards.
7. **Safety Measures** (`safety_measures`): Outline any additional safety measures implemented to ensure a safe working environment.
8. **Review and Approval** (`review_and_approval`): This field is not applicable as it seems to be an optional field.
9. **Revision History** (`revision_history`): Record any changes or updates made to the safety work method statement.
10. **Job Completion** (`job_completion`): Enter the date the work was completed.
11. **Supervisor Signature** (`supervisor_signature`): The supervisor's signature is required to confirm that they have reviewed and approved the safety work method statement.
12. **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`): Enter the supervisor's name.
13. **Contractor Signature** (`contractor_signature`): The contractor's signature is required to confirm that they have reviewed and implemented the safety work method statement.
14. **Contractor Name** (`contractor_name`): Enter the contractor's name.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Review and revise the safety work method statement regularly to ensure it remains relevant and up-to-date.
