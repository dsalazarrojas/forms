# SWMS Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SWMS form is used to report and manage work activities that may pose a risk to workers' safety and wellbeing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:
1. Provide a brief description of the work activity being reported.
2. Indicate whether the activity is part of the SWMS system ("SWMS" field).
3. Select all applicable tasks that are part of the activity.
4. Enter the date and time the activity started and ended.
5. Enter the date and time the activity was completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Work Description** (`form_data`, text, required/optional): A brief description of the work activity being reported.
- **SWMS** (`swms`, select_one, required/optional): Indicates whether the activity is part of the SWMS system.
  - Choose 'Yes' if the activity is part of the SWMS system.
  - Choose 'No' if the activity is not part of the SWMS system.
- **Task List** (`task_list`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all applicable tasks that are part of the activity.
  - Choose 'Yes' for tasks that apply.
  - Choose 'No' for tasks that do not apply.
- **Date Started** (`date_started`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the activity started.
- **Time Started** (`time_started`, time, required/optional): Enter the time the activity started.
- **Date Ended** (`date_ended`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the activity ended.
- **Time Ended** (`time_ended`, time, required/optional): Enter the time the activity ended.
- **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the activity was completed.
- **Time Completed** (`time_completed`, time, required/optional): Enter the time the activity was completed.
- **Task 1 Name** (`task_1_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the first task in the activity (if applicable). 

## Tips
- Ensure to fill in all fields accurately and completely.
- Verify that the activity is correctly reported as part of the SWMS system.
- Review the task list carefully to ensure all applicable tasks are selected.
- Use correct dates and times for the activity start, end, and completion.
