# ehs_incident_reporting--report_forms - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to report incidents that occur within the organization. This form collects detailed information about incidents, including their type, date, time, description, location, status, priority, and the person who reported them.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form fields carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information about the incident.
2. Fill out the form fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
3. Select the correct Incident Type, Incident Date, Incident Time, Incident Description, Incident Location, Incident Status, and Incident Priority from the provided options.
4. Enter the name of the person who reported the incident in the Incident Reporter field.
5. Complete the form and review it before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Details** (`report_details`, `text`, required: false): A text field where you can enter any relevant information about the incident.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of incident that occurred. Please select from the provided options.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, `text`, required: false): A text field where you can describe the incident in detail.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the status of the incident. Please select from the provided options.
* **Incident Priority** (`incident_priority`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the priority of the incident. Please select from the provided options.
* **Incident Reporter** (`incident_reporter`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
