# Trainee Progress Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for trainees to submit their progress reports for evaluation and tracking. It collects information about the trainee's name, contact details, and status of their progress. This form helps the training administrators and supervisors to monitor and assess the trainee's performance regularly, ensuring timely feedback and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **Enter your First Name**: Fill in your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. **Enter your Last Name**: Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. **Enter your Email Address**: Type in your email address in the "email" field.
4. **Enter your Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. **Select Report Date**: Choose the date you are submitting your progress report in the "report_date" field.
6. **Choose Your Progress Status**: Select the status of your progress from the options provided in the "progress_status" field. Options are:
	* Not Started
	* In Progress
	* Completed
7. **Add Any Additional Notes**: Type any relevant notes about your progress in the "notes" field.
8. **Enter Supervisor's Name**: Enter the name of your supervisor in the "supervisor_name" field.
9. **Enter Supervisor's Email Address**: Enter your supervisor's email address in the "supervisor_email" field.
10. **Enter Supervisor's Phone Number**: Enter your supervisor's phone number in the "supervisor_phone" field.
11. **Name the Person Who Received the Report**: Enter the name of the person who received the report in the "report_sent_to" field.
12. **Name the Person Who Reviewed the Report**: Enter the name of the person who reviewed the report in the "report_reviewed_by" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Report Date** (`report_date`, date, required: false): Choose the date you are submitting your progress report.
* **Progress Status** (`progress_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the status of your progress. Options are:
	+ Not Started
	+ In Progress
	+ Completed
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Type any relevant notes about your progress.
* **Supervisor's Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Supervisor's Email Address** (`supervisor_email`, email, required: false): Enter your supervisor's email address.
* **Supervisor's Phone Number** (`supervisor_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your supervisor's phone number.
* **Report Sent To** (`report_sent_to`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person who received the report.
* **Report Reviewed By** (`report_reviewed_by`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person who reviewed the report.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* Select the correct progress status to reflect your current status.
* Enter any additional notes to provide context to your progress report.
* Ensure that the report is sent to and reviewed by the correct person.
