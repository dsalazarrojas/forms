# Vehicle Handover Check Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Handover Check Form is used to report the handover of a vehicle. It's a simple form that helps us collect basic information about the customer and the vehicle's handover details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your details as requested in the "Customer Details" field. Please select "True" or "False" based on the options provided.
2. Enter your information about the vehicle in the "Vehicle Details" text box.
3. Select a date for the handover in the "Handover Date" field.
4. Enter a time for the handover in the "Handover Time" field.
5. Add any remarks or comments about the handover in the "Remarks" text box.
6. Sign your name in the space provided in the "Customer Signature" field.
7. The dealer or representative should sign their name in the space provided in the "Dealer Signature" field.
8. The vehicle owner should sign their name in the space provided in the "Vehicle Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details** (`customer_details_form`): Enter your name, contact number and email in this field.
* **Vehicle Details** (`vehicle_details_form`): Enter any information you'd like about the vehicle being handovered.
* **Handover Date** (`handover_date`): Select a date for the handover in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Handover Time** (`handover_time`): Select a time for the handover in the format `HH:MM` or `HH:MM:SS`.
* **Remarks** (`remarks_form`): Add any comments or remarks about the handover.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`): Sign your name in the space provided.
* **Dealer Signature** (`dealer_signature`): The dealer or representative should sign their name in the space provided.
* **Vehicle Signature** (`vehicle_signature`): The vehicle owner should sign their name in the space provided.

## Tips

* Please ensure you complete the form accurately and truthfully.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact our support team for assistance.
* This form is for one-time use only, please do not reuse it.
