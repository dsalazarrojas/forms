# Christmas Light Removal Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your Christmas light installation and removal needs. Please fill out this form to provide us with the necessary information to plan and perform the removal of your Christmas lights.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out each section carefully and thoroughly. Start by entering your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number. Then, provide information about the property where the lights are located, such as the service address, city, postal code, and type of property. Next, you'll need to describe the types of lights present, estimated count, and who installed them. You'll also have to select a preferred date and time for removal and decide if you need storage services. Finally, let us know how you heard about us and if you have any additional requests or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as the customer or representative filling out this form.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): If you're a business representative, provide your company name for our records.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Provide your email address for service confirmation and communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for scheduling and communication.
* **-- Customer Information --** (section_customer_info, note, optional): This section is for any additional customer information.
* **Service Address** (`service_address`, text, required): Enter the address where the lights are installed on.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Provide the city where the service address is located.
* **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, text, required): Enter the postal code where the service address is located.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of property where the lights are installed, such as a residential single-family home or a commercial office building.
* **-- Property Information --** (section_property, note, optional): This section is for any additional property information.
* **Who Installed the Lights** (`lights_installed_by`, select_one, required): Select who installed the lights, such as your company or another company.
* **Types of Lights Present** (`light_types_present`, select_multiple, required): Select all the types of lights present on the property.
* **Estimated Light Count** (`estimated_light_count`, select_one, required): Select the estimated number of bulbs present.
* **-- Light Details --** (section_light_details, note, optional): This section is for any additional light details.
* **Storage Needed** (`storage_needed`, select_one, required): Decide if you need storage services for the lights after removal.
* **Preferred Removal Date** (`preferred_removal_date`, date, required): Select a preferred date for the removal of the lights.
* **Preferred Time Window** (`preferred_time_window`, select_one, required): Select a preferred time window for the removal of the lights.
* **-- Scheduling --** (section_scheduling, note, optional): This section is for any scheduling information.
* **Access Instructions** (`access_instructions`, text, optional): Provide any access instructions, such as gate codes or lockbox information.
* **Safety Concerns** (`safety_concerns`, text, optional): Describe any safety concerns, such as steep roofs or power lines.
* **Pets on Property** (`pets_on_property`, select_one, required): Select if there are pets on the property.
* **Any Existing Damage** (`damage_report`, text, optional): Note any pre-existing damage on the property.
* **Additional Services** (`additional_services`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional services you'd like to request.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_heard`, select_one, required): Select how you heard about us.
* **Special Notes** (`special_notes`, text, optional): Provide any special notes or additional information.
