# Fire Modification Cert - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a certification document for modifications made to a fire. It is used to record and document the date and time of the modification, the status of the modification, and any additional details or signature of the individual making the modification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure that you are in the correct context to fill out this form.
* Gather all necessary information, including:
	+ Modification date and time
	+ Modification status (e.g., Modification Requested, In Progress, Completed, Cancelled)
	+ Modification details (if necessary)
	+ Signature of the individual making the modification
* Enter the modification number (if already assigned)
* Enter the date and time the modification was received
* Enter the date and time the modification was received by
* Enter the name of the individual or team receiving the modification

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **fire_modification_cert_form**: Enter a brief description of the modification made to the fire.
* **modification_date**: Enter the date the modification was made.
* **modification_number**: Enter the modification number (if already assigned).
* **modification_status**: Select the status of the modification from the following options:
	+ Modification Requested
	+ In Progress
	+ Completed
	+ Cancelled
* **modification_date**: Enter the date the modification was made.
* **modification_time**: Enter the time the modification was made.
* **modification_details**: Enter any additional details about the modification.
* **signature**: Enter your signature as the individual making the modification.
* **received_date**: Enter the date the modification was received.
* **received_time**: Enter the time the modification was received.
* **received_by**: Enter the name of the individual or team receiving the modification.

## Tips

* Be sure to enter accurate and true information for certification purposes.
* If you need to modify or update existing information, please submit a new modification cert form with the new information.
* Do not reuse the existing modification number for new modifications.
* Ensure that you have permission to enter information on behalf of another individual if required.
