# Teaching Method Effectiveness Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information on the effectiveness of teaching methods from teachers, coaches, or academic teams. This will help in assessing the quality and improvement of teaching techniques.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in your input on the teachers' experiences and feedback.
*   Select the category that best fits your role: "Classroom", "Coaching", or "Academic Team".
*   Optionally provide specific examples or anecdotes about your experiences in the feedback fields.
*   Select a date and time for the feedback (if applicable).
*   Optionally provide additional comments or feedback.
*   Submit your report by selecting "Yes" or "No" depending on whether your report is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Teachers Input** (`teacher_input`, text, required: false):
    *   This field is for the teacher to input their experiences and feedback on the teaching method.
*   **What type of teacher** (`category_select`, select_one, required: false):
    *   Select the category that best fits your role: "Classroom", "Coaching", or "Academic Team".
*   **Feedback** (`feedback_input`, note, required: false):
    *   Please provide specific examples or anecdotes about your experiences with the teaching method.
*   **Date** (`date`, date, required: false):
    *   Enter the date when the feedback was given or the experience occurred.
*   **Time** (`time`, time, required: false):
    *   Enter the time when the feedback was given or the experience occurred.
*   **Teachers Input 2** (`teacher_input_2`, text, required: false):
    *   This field is for the teacher to input additional experiences and feedback.
*   **Submit** (`submit`, select_multiple, required: false):
    *   Select "Yes" to submit your report, indicating it's accurate and complete.
    *   Select "No" if your report is not accurate or complete.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false):
    *   Provide any additional comments or feedback.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required: false):
    *   Enter your email address for contact purposes.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false):
    *   Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **What type of coach** (`category_select_2`, select_one, required: false):
    *   Select the category that best fits your role: "Classroom", "Coaching", or "Academic Team".
*   **Feedback** (`feedback_input_2`, note, required: false):
    *   Please provide specific examples or anecdotes about your experiences with the coaching method.
*   **Date 2** (`date_2`, date, required: false):
    *   Enter the date when the feedback was given or the experience occurred.
*   **Time 2** (`time_2`, time, required: false):
    *   Enter the time when the feedback was given or the experience occurred.
*   **What type of team** (`category_select_3`, select_one, required: false):
    *   Select the category that best fits your role: "Classroom", "Coaching", or "Academic Team".
