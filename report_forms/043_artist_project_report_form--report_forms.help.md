# Artist Project Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Artist Project Report Form is designed to gather information about an artist's project, providing a comprehensive overview of their work. This form helps project managers, curators, and administrators understand the project's scope, goals, and outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your artist name and email address in the respective fields.
2. Provide a brief overview of the project, including its title, description, and type.
3. Specify the project's start and end dates, as well as the total hours spent on the project.
4. Describe the project's outcomes and results achieved, including attendance, reach, or impact metrics.
5. Share critical reception or feedback received from the project.
6. List the materials or resources used, project budget, and funding sources.
7. Reflect on the lessons learned or challenges faced during the project.
8. Outline future plans or next steps for the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist Name** (`artist_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as an artist.
* **Email Address** (`artist_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Organization or Institution** (`organization_affiliation`, text, optional): If you are affiliated with an organization or institution, enter its name.
* **Project Title** (`project_title`, text, required): Enter a brief title of the project.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, required): Provide a detailed overview of the project.
* **Type of Project** (`project_type`, select_one, required): Select the category of the project from the provided options.
* **Project Goals and Objectives** (`project_goal`, text, required): Describe the project's goals and objectives.
* **Project Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the project.
* **Project End Date or Completion Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date or completion date of the project.
* **Total Hours Spent on Project** (`total_hours`, number, optional): Enter an approximate time investment in hours.
* **Outcomes and Results Achieved** (`outcomes_achieved`, text, required): Describe the measurable outcomes and results achieved.
* **Attendance, Reach, or Impact Metrics** (`attendance_impact`, text, optional): Enter attendance, reach, or impact metrics.
* **Critical Reception or Feedback** (`critical_reception`, text, optional): Share critical reception or feedback received.
* **Materials or Resources Used** (`materials_used`, text, optional): List the materials or resources used.
* **Project Budget** (`budget_amount`, number, optional): Enter the total funding or costs.
* **Funding Sources** (`funding_sources`, text, optional): List the funding sources.
* **Lessons Learned or Challenges** (`lessons_learned`, text, optional): Describe the lessons learned or challenges faced.
* **Future Development or Next Steps** (`future_development`, text, optional): Outline future plans or next steps for the project.
