# Utilities Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Utilities Form is designed to collect personal details from customers for the purpose of customer communication and service purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Name** in the text field provided.
2. Enter your **Contact Number** in the specified format (e.g., phone number, including international dialing codes if necessary).
3. Provide your **Email** address in the correct format (e.g., [username]@domain.com).
4. Select your **Date Of Birth** in the format of month, day, year, or use the calendar picker.
5. Enter the time you became a **Customer Since**, in the format of hour, minute, and am/pm.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (customer_name, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Number** (contact_number, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number, including international dialing codes if necessary.
* **Email** (email, email, required/optional): Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., [username]@domain.com).
* **Date Of Birth** (date_of_birth, date, required/optional): Select your date of birth using the month, day, and year format or use the calendar picker.
* **Customer Since** (customer_since, time, required/optional): Enter the time you became a customer in the format of hour, minute, and am/pm.

## Tips
* Ensure the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, do not hesitate to contact us for assistance.
* You can use the calendar picker for **Date Of Birth** to select your date of birth easily.
* You can enter international dialing codes for your **Contact Number** if necessary.
