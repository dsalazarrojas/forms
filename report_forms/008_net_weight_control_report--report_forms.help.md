# Net Weight Control Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Net Weight Control Report is used to track and monitor the weight of products throughout the production process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the product using the Product Id field.
2. Enter the product description in the product description field.
3. Enter the weight control ID.
4. Enter the weight control date.
5. Enter the product weight.
6. Select the unit of weight (e.g. grams, kilograms, etc.).
7. Enter the production ID.
8. Select the operator who performed the weight control.
9. Enter the net weight.
10. Enter the total weight.
11. Enter the product weight variance.
12. Enter the production weight variance.
13. Enter the production net weight variance.
14. Enter the product net weight.
15. Enter the production net weight.
16. Enter the production total weight.
17. Enter the production product weight.
18. Enter the production total product weight.
19. Enter the total product weight.
20. Enter the net production weight.
21. Enter the production net product weight.
22. Enter the total net production weight.
23. Enter the weight difference.
24. Enter the weight variance.
25. Enter the production weight difference.
</br>

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Id** (`product_id`, `number`, required): Enter the unique identifier of the product.
* **Product description** (`product_description`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the product.
* **Weight control id** (`weight_control_id`, `number`, required): Enter the weight control ID number.
* **Weight control date** (`weight_control_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of weight control.
* **Product weight** (`product_weight`, `number`, required): Enter the weight of the product.
* **Weight unit** (`weight_unit`, `text`, required): Select the unit of weight (e.g. grams, kilograms, etc.).
* **Production id** (`production_id`, `number`, required): Enter the production ID number.
* **Operator id** (`operator_id`, `select_one`, required): Select the operator who performed the weight control.
* **Net weight** (`net_weight`, `number`, required): Enter the net weight.
* **Total weight** (`total_weight`, `number`, required): Enter the total weight.
* **Product weight variance** (`product_weight_variance`, `number`, required): Enter the product weight variance.
* **Production weight variance** (`production_weight_variance`, `number`, required): Enter the production weight variance.
* **Production net weight variance** (`production_net_weight_variance`, `number`, required): Enter the production net weight variance.
* **Product net weight** (`product_net_weight`, `number`, required): Enter the product net weight.
* **Production net weight** (`production_net_weight`, `number`, required): Enter the production net weight.
* **Production total weight** (`production_total_weight`, `number`, required): Enter the production total weight.
* **Production product weight** (`production_product_weight`, `number`, required): Enter the production product weight.
* **Production total product weight** (`production_total_product_weight`, `number`, required): Enter the production total product weight.
* **Total product weight** (`total_product_weight`, `number`, required): Enter the total product weight.
* **Net production weight** (`net_production_weight`, `number`, required): Enter the net production weight.
* **Production net product weight** (`production_net_product_weight`, `number`, required): Enter the production net product weight.
* **Total net production weight** (`total_net_production_weight`, `number`, required): Enter the total net production weight.
* **Weight difference** (`weight_difference`, `number`, required): Enter the weight difference.
* **Weight variance** (`weight_variance`, `number`, required): Enter the weight variance.
* **Production weight difference** (`production_weight_difference`, `number`, required): Enter the production weight difference.
