# Daily Patient Census Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Daily Patient Census Report is used to collect information about the number and characteristics of patients in a healthcare setting, such as a hospital or nursing home.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Navigate through each page of the form, completing the fields as necessary.
* For each field, enter the required or optional information as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Prophets** (Page 1): Enter a brief description of the page or section being reported on.
* **Unit (Page 2)**: Select the type of unit or department (e.g., ICU, general medical-surgical) that the patients are being reported on.
* **Page2 Unit** (Page 2): Enter the specific unit or department (e.g., Med-Surg, ICU) that the patients are being reported on.
* **Page2 Prophet** (Page 2): Select whether or not prophets are present on this unit.
* **Date (Page 3)**: Select the date for which the census is being reported.
* **Page3 Date** (Page 3): Enter the date for which the census is being reported (likely a backup or confirmation field).
* **Page3 Date 2** (Page 3): Select the time of day for which the census is being reported.
* **Page4** (Page 4): Enter any additional comments or notes regarding the census.
* **Page4 Prophets** (Page 4): Enter any prophets or special considerations that require attention.
* **Staffing (Page 5)**: Select the types of staff members present during the census (e.g., RN, LPN, MD).

Note: If you are unsure about any field or option, please consult with your supervisor or the form administrator for clarification.
