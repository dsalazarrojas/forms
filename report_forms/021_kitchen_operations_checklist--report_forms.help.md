# Kitchen Operations Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Kitchen Operations Checklist" form is a centralized platform for kitchen staff to report on the status of kitchen equipment, cleaning, and operations. It allows for efficient tracking and management of kitchen activities.
## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through the pages and select the corresponding status or options for each field. You can choose from pre-defined options for each field, or input your own text or dates as required.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Kitchen Equipment** (`id: 1`, `type: select_multiple`, `options: Cleaned, Available, Not Available`): Select the current status of kitchen equipment, such as whether it's cleaned, available, or not available.
* **Cleaning Schedule** (`id: 2`, `type: date`, `options: []`): Input the date of the next scheduled cleaning.
* **Cleaning Supplies** (`id: 3`, `type: select_one`, `options: Sponges, Mops, Brushes`): Select the type of cleaning supplies needed or used for the kitchen.
* **Kitchen Tools** (`id: 4`, `type: select_multiple`, `options: Sharp, Dull, Other`): Report the status of kitchen tools, such as whether they are sharp or dull.
* **Cleaning Frequency** (`id: 5`, `type: select_one`, `options: Daily, Weekly, Monthly`): Select how often the cleaning is performed.
* **Kitchen Staff** (`id: 6`, `type: select_one`, `options: Manager, Supervisor, Team Member`): Identify the kitchen staff member responsible for the task.
* **Cleaning Status** (`id: 7`, `type: select_one`, `options: Completed, In Progress, Not Started`): Report the completion status of the cleaning task.
* **Notes** (`id: 8`, `type: note`, `options: []`): Add any additional comments or notes about the task.
* **Kitchen Operations Completed** (`id: 9`, `type: select_multiple`, `options: Pre-Opening, Pre-Service, Post-Service, Pre-Closing`): Select the kitchen operations that have been completed.
* **Kitchen Operations In Progress** (`id: 10`, `type: select_multiple`, `options: Pre-Opening, Pre-Service, Post-Service, Pre-Closing`): Select the kitchen operations that are currently in progress.
* **Kitchen Operations Not Completed** (`id: 11`, `type: select_multiple`, `options: Pre-Opening, Pre-Service, Post-Service, Pre-Closing`): Select the kitchen operations that have not been completed.
* **Assigned To** (`id: 12`, `type: text`, `options: []`): Identify the person or team assigned to complete the task.
* **Assigned Date** (`id: 13`, `type: date`, `options: []`): Input the date assigned to complete the task.
* **Closed Date** (`id: 14`, `type: date`, `options: []`): Input the date the task was completed.
* **Closed Status** (`id: 15`, `type: select_one`, `options: Completed, In Progress, Not Started`): Report the completion status of the task.
* **Notes** (`id: 16`, `type: note`, `options: []`): Add any additional comments or notes about the task.
* **Kitchen Operations Status** (`id: 17`, `type: text`, `options: []`): Report the status of the kitchen operations, in case of any unusual or exceptional circumstances.
* **Comments** (`id: 18`, `type: text`, `options: []`): Add any comments or feedback.
* **Tool Status** (`id: 19`, `type: select_multiple`, `options: Cleaned, Available, Not Available`): Select the status of kitchen tools, such as whether they are cleaned, available, or not available.
* **Equipment Status** (`id: 20`, `type: select_multiple`, `options: Cleaned, Available, Not Available`): Select the status of kitchen equipment, such as whether it is cleaned, available, or not available.
* **Tool Count** (`id: 21`, `type: number`, `options: []`): Report the number of tools available or required.
* **Equipment Count** (`id: 22`, `type: number`, `options: []`): Report the number of equipment available or required.
* **Tool Type** (`id: 23`, `type: text`, `options: []`): Report the type of tool.
* **Tool Condition** (`id: 24`, `type: select_one`, `options: Good, Fair, Poor`): Report the condition of the tool.
* **Tool Maintenance Required** (`id: 25`, `type: select_one`, `options: 'True', 'False'`): Report whether the tool requires maintenance.
## Tips

* Make sure to fill out each field accurately to ensure efficient tracking and reporting.
* Use the "Notes" fields to provide any additional information or comments.
* Refer to the form's purpose and scope to ensure you're completing the form correctly.
* If you're unsure about any field or section, refer to your supervisor or manager for guidance.
