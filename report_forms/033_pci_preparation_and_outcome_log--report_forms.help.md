# pci_preparation_and_outcome_log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a medical procedure's preparation and outcome in a cardiology department or lab.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the details of the procedure, including its name, date, and time.
2.  Select the type of procedure that was performed (e.g., Cardiology, Electrophysiology).
3.  Choose the outcome of the procedure (e.g., Good, Fair, Poor).
4.  Enter any comments about the procedure.
5.  Select the team members who were involved in the procedure (e.g., Cardiology Team, Radiology Team).
6.  Choose the physician who performed the procedure (e.g., Cardiology Physician).
7.  Enter any additional comments about the procedure.
8.  Enter the date the form was submitted.
9.  Enter the date of the procedure's outcome.
10. Enter the outcome of the procedure.
11. Enter any additional comments about the procedure.
12. Select the team members who were involved in the procedure.
13. Choose the physician who performed the procedure.
14. Enter any additional comments about the procedure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Procedure Details** (`procedure_details`, `text`, `required`): Enter a brief description of the procedure.
* **Procedure Type** (`pci_type`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the type of procedure that was performed (e.g., Cardiology, Electrophysiology).
* **Procedure Name** (`procedure_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of the procedure.
* **Procedure Date** (`procedure_date`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date the procedure was performed.
* **Procedure Time** (`procedure_time`, `time`, `required`): Enter the time the procedure was performed.
* **Outcome** (`outcome`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Choose the outcome of the procedure (e.g., Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, `required`): Enter any comments about the procedure.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `text`, `required`): Enter the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **PCI Preparation Steps** (`pci_preparation_steps`, `text`, `required`): Enter the steps taken for the procedure's preparation.
* **Cardiology Team** (`cardiology_team`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select the team members who were involved in the procedure (e.g., Cardiology Team, Other).
* **Radiology Team** (`radiology_team`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select the team members who were involved in the procedure (e.g., Radiology Team, Other).
* **Cardiology Physician** (`cardiolphys`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose the physician who performed the procedure (e.g., Cardiology Physician, Other).
* **Comments 2** (`comments_2`, `text`, `required`): Enter any additional comments about the procedure.
* **Submitted On 2** (`submitted_on_2`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **PCI Outcome Date** (`pci_outcome_date`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date of the procedure's outcome.
* **PCI Outcome** (`pci_outcome`, `text`, `required`): Enter the outcome of the procedure.
* **Comments 3** (`comments_3`, `text`, `required`): Enter any additional comments about the procedure.
* **Cardiology Team 2** (`cardiology_team_2`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select the team members who were involved in the procedure (e.g., Cardiology Team, Other).
* **Cardiology Physician 2** (`cardiolphys_2`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose the physician who performed the procedure (e.g., Cardiology Physician, Other).

## Tips
* Be thorough when entering information about the procedure to ensure accurate records.
* Make sure to select the correct team members and physician involved in the procedure.
* Enter any comments or notes about the procedure to help future reference.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
