# ice_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "ice_submission_form" is a form used to collect and record incident details for reporting and tracking purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form fields below and ensure you have all necessary information about the incident before proceeding.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and detailed information about the incident.
3. Select the correct options from drop-down fields where applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`): Enter a brief description of the incident, including any relevant context or details.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`): Enter the date the incident occurred, in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`): Enter the time the incident occurred, in the format `HH:MM:SS` (24-hour clock).
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`): Enter a detailed description of the incident, including any relevant context or information.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`): Select 'Yes' to indicate that the incident is of type 'Yes', or 'No' otherwise.
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`): Select 'Active' to indicate that the incident is active, or 'Inactive' otherwise.
* **Incident Severity** (`incident_severity`): Select 'Yes' to indicate that the incident is of severity 'Yes', or 'No' otherwise.
* **Incident Priority** (`incident_priority`): Select 'Yes' to indicate that the incident has priority 'Yes', or 'No' otherwise.
* **Incident Category** (`incident_category`): Select 'Yes' to indicate that the incident belongs to category 'Yes', or 'No' otherwise.
* **Incident Submitter** (`incident_submitted_by`): Enter the name or identifier of the person who submitted the incident report.
* **Comments** (`incident_comments`): Enter any additional comments or information related to the incident.

**Tips**

* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the designated authority for guidance.
* This form is used for internal purposes only, do not submit sensitive or confidential information.
* If the incident requires urgent handling, notify the relevant authorities immediately.
