# Warehouse Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Warehouse Incident Report Form is used to report incidents that occur within the warehouse environment. This form is used to gather information about the incident, including details, type, location, and witnesses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the Incident Details section with any relevant information about the incident.
2. Select the type of incident that occurred (Fire, Theft, Vandalism, or Damage).
3. Enter the date and time the incident occurred.
4. Provide a brief description of what happened.
5. If there were witnesses, enter the number of witnesses and provide their details.
6. If you have any supporting images or videos, upload them to the Incident Image field.
7. Enter any witness statements or notes.
8. Describe any actions taken after the incident.
9. Enter your contact information (phone, email, and department) and the location and shift you were on.
10. Finally, enter any additional notes about the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Details**: Please enter a brief description of the incident, including any relevant details such as the area where it occurred.
* **Incident Type**: Select the type of incident that occurred from the list (Fire, Theft, Vandalism, or Damage).
* **Incident Date**: Enter the date the incident occurred in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Incident Time**: Enter the time the incident occurred in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Incident Location**: Enter a brief description of the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description**: Provide a detailed description of what happened.
* **Witness Count**: Enter the number of witnesses to the incident.
* **Witness Details**: Enter any details about the witnesses, including their names and contact information.
* **Witness Names**: Enter the names of any witnesses.
* **Witness Contacts**: Enter any contact information for the witnesses.
* **Incident Image**: If you have any supporting images or videos, upload them here.
* **Witness Statement**: Enter any statements or notes from witnesses.
* **Action Taken**: Describe any actions taken after the incident.
* **Incident Reporter**: Enter your name and title.
* **Incident Supervisor**: Enter the supervisor's name and title.
* **Incident Date Submitted**: Enter the date this report was submitted.
* **Incident Time Submitted**: Enter the time this report was submitted.
* **Incident Reporter Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Incident Reporter Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Incident Reporter Department**: Enter your department.
* **Incident Reporter Shift**: Enter the shift you were on.
* **Incident Reporter Location**: Enter the location you were at when the incident occurred.
* **Incident Note**: Enter any additional notes about the incident.
