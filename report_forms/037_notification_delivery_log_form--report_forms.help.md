# Notification Delivery Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and record notifications, including the recipient's information, notification details, and follow-up status. It helps in monitoring and managing notifications for follow-up actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the notification in the "form_title" field.
2. Select the date of the notification in the "notification_date" field.
3. Enter the time of the notification in the "notification_time" field.
4. Enter the recipient's name in the "recipient_name" field.
5. Select the type of notification in the "notification_type" field.
6. Indicate the delivery method of the notification in the "delivery_method" field.
7. Specify the follow-up status in the "follow_up_status" field by selecting all applicable options.
8. Enter any additional notes in the "notes" field (optional).
9. Enter the recipient's email address in the "recipient_email" field.
10. Enter the recipient's phone number in the "recipient_phone" field.
11. Select the follow-up method in the "follow_up_method" field.
12. Enter the date of the follow-up action in the "follow_up_date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Title** (form_title, text, required): Enter a brief title of the notification, which will be recorded in the log.
- **Notification Date** (notification_date, date, required): Select the date the notification was sent or received.
- **Notification Time** (notification_time, time, required): Enter the time of the notification, in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:30 for 2:30 PM).
- **Recipient Name** (recipient_name, text, required): Enter the name of the individual who received the notification.
- **Notification Type** (notification_type, select_one, required): Indicate the type of notification (e.g., Yes/No).
- **Delivery Method** (delivery_method, select_one, required): Select the method used to deliver the notification (e.g., Yes/No).
- **Follow-up Status** (follow_up_status, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable follow-up status options (Active/Inactive).
- **Notes** (notes, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the notification.
- **Recipient Email** (recipient_email, email, required): Enter the email address of the recipient.
- **Recipient Phone** (recipient_phone, text, required): Enter the phone number of the recipient.
- **Follow-up Method** (follow_up_method, select_one, required): Indicate if follow-up is required (e.g., Yes/No).
- **Follow-up Date** (follow_up_date, date, required): Enter the date of the follow-up action.
- **Follow-up Status** was removed as it is not a field present in the schema.
