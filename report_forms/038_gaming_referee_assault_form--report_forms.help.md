# Gaming Referee Assault Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gaming Referee Assault Form is designed to collect information about incidents of referee assaults that have occurred during games, tournaments, or other gaming events. This form is used to report these incidents to the relevant authorities, ensuring a prompt and fair investigation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the report date on the calendar provided.
2. Enter the name of the referee who was assaulted.
3. Enter the name of the team involved in the incident.
4. Describe the location where the incident occurred.
5. Provide a brief description of the incident.
6. Indicate if the injury was caused by the referee's actions or not.
7. Select the severity of the injury sustained.
8. Describe any action taken or recommended for prevention in the future.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Report Date
* **Report Date** (date, required): Enter the date the incident was reported or observed.

### 2. Referee Name
* **Referee Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the referee who was assaulted.

### 3. Team Name
* **Team Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the team involved in the incident.

### 4. Location
* **Location** (text, required): Enter the location where the incident occurred, such as the game field or arena.

### 5. Description
* **Description** (text, required): Provide a clear and detailed description of the incident, including any relevant details about what happened.

### 6. Injury Type
* **Injury Type** (select multiple, optional): Indicate if the injury was caused by the referee's actions or not. Select all relevant answers, such as "Yes" or "No".

### 7. Injury Severity
* **Injury Severity** (select one, optional): Indicate the severity of the injury sustained, choosing from "Yes" or "No" options, depending on whether the injury was severe or not.

### 8. Action Taken
* **Action Taken** (note, optional): Describe any action taken or recommended to prevent similar incidents in the future, including any steps the referee or team took, or suggested measures for improvement.

## Tips
- Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form to ensure a fair and prompt investigation.
- Report incidents promptly, as delays can impact the effectiveness of the investigation.
- If you're unsure about any information, please reach out to your local gaming association or event organizers for guidance.
