# Smart Energy Report Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Energy Report Request Form is designed to collect information about energy-related services for a property. This form helps in scheduling or requesting follow-up reports for the property, ensuring accurate and efficient report generation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Smart Energy Report Request Form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the property details in the **Property Details** field.
2. Select your delivery preferences in the **Delivery Preferences** field.
3. Provide your contact information, including your **Email** and **Phone Number**.
4. Specify the report details in the **Report Details** field.
5. Choose the consultants you would like to work with in the **Consultant** field.
6. Select the report type in the **Report Type** field.
7. Choose the report frequency in the **Report Frequency** field.
8. Provide the report date and time in the **Report Date** and **Report Time** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Details**: A field to enter the property's details. Please enter the relevant information about the property you are requesting reports for.
* **Delivery Preferences**: A field to select how you would like to receive the report. Please choose one of the provided options for delivery method.
* **Email**: A field to enter your email address. This will be used for communication and report delivery.
* **Phone Number**: A field to enter your phone number. This will be used for phone calls or reminders.
* **Report Details**: A field to enter any additional details about the report you are requesting.
* **Consultant**: A field to select the consultants you would like to work with on this report. Please choose one or more consultants from the provided options.
* **Report Type**: A field to select the type of report you are requesting. Please choose one of the provided options.
* **Report Frequency**: A field to select how often you would like to receive reports. Please choose one of the provided options.
* **Report Date**: A field to enter the date when you would like to receive the report.
* **Report Time**: A field to enter the time when you would like to receive the report.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out completely.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* Review your selections for the consultant, report type, and report frequency to ensure they align with your needs.
* Be sure to enter the correct report date and time.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us for assistance.
