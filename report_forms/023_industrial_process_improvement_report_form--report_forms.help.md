# Industrial Process Improvement Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions on industrial process improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question thoroughly and concisely.
2. Provide concrete and practical suggestions for improvement.
3. Review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form_1** (Text, Optional): This field is where you can provide a brief description of the industrial process you want to improve.
* **report_form** (Text, Optional): Use this field to provide more detailed information about the report, such as the process involved and the specific issues you want to improve.
* **date_field** (Date, Optional): Provide the date of the incident or the timeframe you observed the process.
* **time_field** (Time, Optional): Enter the time of the incident or the specific time you observed the process.
* **note_field** (Note, Optional): Use this field to add any additional comments or notes about the process.
* **email_field** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted for further discussion or clarification.
* **phone_field** (Text, Optional): Provide your contact number for further communication.
* **select_one** (Select One, Required): Choose one of the options to indicate the level of urgency and priority of the process improvement.
* **select_multiple** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select all the options that apply to the process improvement.

## Tips
* Please use a clear and concise format for your submission.
* Avoid duplicating or repeating information.
* Provide concrete and actionable suggestions for improvement.
* Proofread your submission before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
