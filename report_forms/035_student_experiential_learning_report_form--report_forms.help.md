# Student Experiential Learning Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and evaluations from students on their learning experiences during experiential learning activities. The purpose of this form is to gather information on student outcomes, challenges, and feedback on instructors and materials, which helps in improving the quality of learning experiences and enhancing student learning outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's name and course name fields with the relevant information.
2. Select the learning activity name from the options provided.
3. Choose the desired learning outcome achievement from the options provided.
4. Describe the benefits and challenges of the learning activity in the text fields.
5. Evaluate the learning activity, instructor, and materials by providing time estimates.
6. Leave comments for the student and instructor in the text fields.
7. Sign and date the form.
