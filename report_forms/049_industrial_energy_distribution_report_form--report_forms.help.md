The purpose of this form is to collect information about industrial energy distribution reports. This form allows users to report any issues related to industrial energy distribution, such as operational issues or problems with energy sources. The goal is to provide a comprehensive report that captures all relevant details for further investigation and resolution.

## industrial_energy_distribution_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The industrial energy distribution report form is designed to collect reports of any issues related to industrial energy distribution. This includes problems with energy sources, operational issues, and other concerns.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for the report in the "Form Title" field.
2. Select all the energy sources affected by the issue in the "Energy Sources" field.
3. Enter the total energy distributed in the "Total Energy Distributed" field.
4. Describe the operational issues encountered in the "Operational Issues" field.
5. Provide additional comments about the issue in the "Issue Comments" field.
6. Attach an image of the issue, if relevant, in the "Issue Image" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Form Title** (form_title, text, required/optional: false)
Enter a brief title of the report, describing the issue or concern.

### 2. **Energy Sources** (energy_sources, select_multiple, required/optional: false)
Select all energy sources affected by the issue, such as Electricity, Steam, Gas, or Water.

### 3. **Total Energy Distributed** (total_energy_distributed, number, required/optional: false)
Enter the total amount of energy distributed, if applicable.

### 4. **Operational Issues** (operational_issues, text, required/optional: false)
Provide a detailed description of the operational issues encountered.

### 5. **Issue Comments** (issue_comments, text, required/optional: false)
Offer additional comments or context about the issue, if applicable.

### 6. **Issue Image** (issue_image, text, required/optional: false)
Attach an image of the issue, if possible, to help with further investigation.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when describing operational issues and energy sources.
* If multiple energy sources are affected, select all relevant options in the "Energy Sources" field.
* Use the "Issue Comments" field to provide additional context or information that may be helpful for resolution.
* Attach an image of the issue, if possible, to help with further investigation.
