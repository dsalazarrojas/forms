# town_festival_contact_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for collecting contact information and messages for events or festivals, and for event planners or organizers to reach out to attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the event in the **Event Name** field.
2. Enter your name as an attendee in the **Attendee Name** field.
3. Select the date of the event in the **Event Date** field.
4. Select your preferred contact method (e.g., **Phone**, **Email**, or **In-Person**) in the **Contact Method** field. If you select **Other**, you can provide more information in the **Contact Method (Other)** field.
5. Enter any message or feedback you have in the **Message** field.
6. Select the category of the event (e.g., **Festival**, **Concert**, or **Parade**) in the **Event Category** field.
7. If you selected **Other** in the **Contact Method** field, enter the contact method you will be using in the **Contact Method (Other)** field.
8. Enter the location of the event in the **Event Location** field (optional).
9. Enter a brief description of the event in the **Event Description** field (optional).
10. Enter the name of the contact person or organization in the **Contact Name** field (optional).
11. Enter the email of the contact person or organization in the **Contact Email** field.
12. Enter the phone number of the contact person or organization in the **Contact Phone** field.
13. If applicable, select the tool that will be used for communication from the **Assigned Tool** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event or festival you are interested in.
* **Attendee Name**: Enter your name as the attendee or participant.
* **Event Date**: Select the date of the event or festival.
* **Contact Method**: Select how you would like to be contacted (e.g., phone, email, or in-person).
* **Message**: Enter any message or feedback you have regarding the event or festival.
* **Event Category**: Select the category of the event (e.g., festival, concert, or parade).
* **Contact Method (Other)**: If you selected **Other** in the **Contact Method** field, enter your preferred contact method here.
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event or festival (optional).
* **Event Description**: Enter a brief description of the event or festival (optional).
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person or organization (optional).
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email of the contact person or organization.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person or organization.
* **Assigned Tool**: If applicable, select the tool that will be used for communication with you.
