# Report Nuisance Calls And Texts - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report and document nuisance calls and texts that have occurred, providing essential details for tracking and resolving these incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the "Report Nuisance Calls and Texts Form" section with a clear and concise description of the incident.
2. Add any relevant screenshots or evidence related to the call or text.
3. In the "Report Nuisance Calls Details" field, provide as much detail as possible about the call or text.
4. For the "Report Nuisance Call Date" and "Report Nuisance Call Time" fields, select the date and time the call or text occurred.
5. In the "Report Nuisance Call Phone Number" field, enter the phone number involved in the call or text.
6. In the "Report Nuisance Calls Followup Actions" field, select all follow-up actions that apply to the incident, such as follow-up with a call, text message, email, or letter.
7. In the "Report Nuisance Calls Followup Actions Comments" field, add any additional comments or notes about the follow-up actions taken.
8. Finally, submit the form to complete the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Nuisance Calls and Texts Form** (Text, Optional): This is the main form section where you provide a description of the incident.
* **Report Nuisance Calls Details** (Text, Optional): Add as much detail as possible about the call or text.
* **Report Nuisance Calls Screenshots** (Text, Optional): Attach any relevant screenshots related to the incident.
* **Report Nuisance Call Evidence** (Text, Optional): Upload or link to evidence related to the call or text.
* **Report Nuisance Calls Followup** (Text, Optional): Select follow-up actions to take, such as follow-up with a call, text message, email, or letter.
* **Report Nuisance Calls Followup Actions Actions** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose one or more follow-up actions from the dropdown list.
* **Report Nuisance Calls Followup Actions Comments** (Text, Optional): Add comments or notes about the follow-up actions taken.
* **Report Nuisance Call Date** (Date, Optional): Select the date the call or text occurred.
* **Report Nuisance Call Time** (Time, Optional): Select the time the call or text occurred.
* **Report Nuisance Call Phone Number** (Text, Optional): Enter the phone number involved in the call or text.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide as much detail as possible in the "Report Nuisance Calls Details" field to ensure accurate and complete reporting.
* Attach relevant screenshots or evidence to support your report.
* Select all follow-up actions that apply to the incident in the "Report Nuisance Calls Followup Actions" field.
* Keep the "Report Nuisance Calls Followup Actions Comments" field concise and clear for future reference.
