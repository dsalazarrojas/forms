# Equipment Breakdown Summary Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equipment Breakdown Summary Report Form is used to document and report equipment failures, allowing maintenance teams to efficiently track and prioritize repairs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter the date the equipment failure occurred in the **Failure Date** field.
* Identify the specific **Equipment ID** and **Equipment Location** where the failure occurred.
* Provide a brief description of the **Failure Reason**.
* If applicable, specify the **Repair Details**, such as the root cause of the failure or any relevant maintenance history.
* Determine the **Failure Frequency** of the equipment's failure, if applicable.
* Select the **Incident History**, if the equipment has had previous breakdowns.
* Choose who the equipment was **Assigned To** (Maintenance Team, Operations Team, Both, or Not Specified).
* Report the **Repair Status** (Completed, In Progress, Not Started, Cancelled).
* Specify any **Comments** related to the breakdown.
* Choose the **Equipment Status** (Operational, Maintenance, Not Available, Other, Unknown).
* Indicate the **Priority** level of the repair (High, Medium, Low).
* Tag the **Equipment Tag** (Critical, Important, Non-Critical, Not Tagged, Unknown).
* Add any additional **Notes** related to the breakdown.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Failure Date** (Failure Date, date): Enter the date the equipment failure occurred.
* **Equipment ID** (Equipment ID, number): Enter the unique identifier for the equipment.
* **Failure Reason** (Failure Reason, text): Provide a brief description of the failure.
* **Repair Details** (Repair Details, text): Specify any details related to the repair, such as the root cause of the failure or any relevant maintenance history.
* **Failure Frequency** (Failure Frequency, number): Select if the equipment's failure has occurred frequently.
* **Equipment Location** (Equipment Location, text): Enter the location where the equipment is situated.
* **Incident History** (Incident History, note): Choose if the equipment has had previous breakdowns.
* **Assigned To** (Assigned To, select): Select who is responsible for the repair (Maintenance Team, Operations Team, Both, or Not Specified).
* **Repair Status** (Repair Status, select): Report the status of the repair (Completed, In Progress, Not Started, Cancelled).
* **Comments** (Comments, text): Enter any additional comments related to the breakdown.
* **Equipment Status** (Equipment Status, select): Choose the current status of the equipment (Operational, Maintenance, Not Available, Other, Unknown).
* **Priority** (Priority, select): Indicate the priority level of the repair (High, Medium, Low).
* **Equipment Tag** (Equipment Tag, select): Tag the equipment as Critical, Important, Non-Critical, Not Tagged, or Unknown.
* **Notes** (Notes, text): Enter any additional notes or observations about the breakdown.
