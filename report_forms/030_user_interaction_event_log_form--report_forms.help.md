# User Interaction Event Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The User Interaction Event Log Form is used to collect detailed information about user interactions with products or services. This form helps us identify and address any issues that users may have, ensuring a better overall customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the issue(s) you encountered with the product from the "Product Issues" page.
2. Choose the type of interaction you had with the product from the "User Interaction Log" page.
3. Indicate your agreement or disagreement with the statement on the "Interaction" page.
4. Select the user who received the interaction from the "Issued To" page.
5. Choose the type of interaction you had with the product from the "Interaction Type" page.
6. Select the product(s) involved in the interaction from the "Product" page.
7. (Optional) Add any additional comments or notes about the interaction in the "Notes" field.
8. Finally, confirm your consent to report the interaction on the "Consent" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Issues** (user_interaction_page_1): Select all issues you encountered with the product. This will help us understand the specific problems you experienced.
* **User Interaction Log** (user_interaction_page_2): Choose the type of interaction you had with the product (e.g., issue, product, user). This will help us identify the nature of your interaction.
* **Interaction** (interaction_page): Indicate your agreement or disagreement with the statement "I agree" or "I do not agree". This will help us understand your perspective on the interaction.
* **Issued To** (issued_to_page): Select the user who received the interaction. This is the user who will be receiving your feedback.
* **Interaction Type** (interaction_type_page): Choose the type of interaction you had with the product (e.g., issue, product, user). This will help us identify the nature of your interaction.
* **Product** (product_page): Select all products involved in the interaction. This will help us understand which products were involved in the issue.
* **Notes** (notes_page): (Optional) Add any additional comments or notes about the interaction. This is an open field for you to provide any relevant information.
* **Consent** (consent_page): Confirm your consent to report the interaction. This ensures we have your permission to process the information.
