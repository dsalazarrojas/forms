# Firewise Activity Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Firewise Activity Report Form is a user-facing form designed to collect data from users on their work activities, including hours worked, overtime, total hours, and expense details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Report Form Data" section with any additional information you want to include in the report.
2. Enter the start and end dates of the period you are reporting on in the "Report Start Date" and "Report End Date" fields, respectively.
3. Enter the total hours worked in the "Hours Worked" field.
4. Enter any overtime hours worked in the "Hours Overtime" field.
5. Enter the total expense amount in the "Expense Amount" field.
6. Enter a brief description of the expense in the "Expense Description" field.
7. Select the category of the expense from the dropdown menu in the "Expense Category" field.
8. Enter the date of the expense in the "Expense Date" field.
9. Select the type of report you are submitting from the dropdown menu in the "Report Type" field.
10. Enter the name of the tool assigned to you in the "Assigned Tool" field.
11. Enter your user name in the "User Name" field.
12. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
13. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
14. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
15. Enter your city in the "City" field.
16. Enter your state in the "State" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Form Data**: Enter any additional information you want to include in the report.
* **Report Start Date**: Enter the start date of the period you are reporting on.
* **Report End Date**: Enter the end date of the period you are reporting on.
* **Hours Worked**: Enter the total hours you worked during the reporting period.
* **Hours Overtime**: Enter any overtime hours you worked during the reporting period.
* **Total Hours**: This field is not editable. You can only view this field.
* **Expense Amount**: Enter the total amount of your expenses for the reporting period.
* **Expense Description**: Enter a brief description of the expense.
* **Expense Category**: Select the category of the expense from the dropdown menu (NFPA, Other, or Travel).
* **Expense Date**: Enter the date of the expense.
* **Report Type**: Select the type of report you are submitting from the dropdown menu (Abatement or Other).
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the name of the tool assigned to you.
* **User Name**: Enter your user name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **City**: Enter your city.
* **State**: Enter your state.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy.
* Submit the form once you have filled out all the necessary fields.
