### Fire Safety Equipment Maintenance Report - Help Guide
#### Purpose
The Fire Safety Equipment Maintenance Report form is used to record and report on the maintenance of fire safety equipment at various locations within a site. This form ensures that all fire safety equipment is properly maintained and functioning correctly.

#### How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the Site ID of the location where the fire safety equipment is installed.
2. Select the type of equipment being maintained.
3. Enter the location of the fire safety equipment.
4. Provide a brief description of the fire safety equipment.
5. Enter the date of the last inspection.
6. Select the outcome of the last inspection.
7. Choose the recommended maintenance frequency for the equipment.
8. Enter the date of the next maintenance.
9. Enter the date of the last maintenance.
10. Select the current status of the maintenance.
11. Enter the contact person responsible for the maintenance.

#### Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Site ID** (1): Enter the unique identifier for the site where the fire safety equipment is installed.
* **Equipment Type** (2): Select the type of fire safety equipment being maintained. For example, 'Fire Extinguisher', 'Fire Alarm', etc.
* **Location** (3): Enter the exact location of the equipment, such as 'Main Building, Room 123'.
* **Description** (4): Provide a brief description of the equipment, including its type and model number.
* **Last Inspection Date** (5): Enter the date of the last inspection of the equipment.
* **Last Inspection Result** (6): Select the outcome of the last inspection, either 'Failed' or 'Passed'.
* **Maintenance Frequency** (7): Choose the recommended maintenance frequency for the equipment, such as 'Daily', 'Weekly', 'Monthly', etc.
* **Next Maintenance Date** (8): Enter the date of the next scheduled maintenance.
* **Last Maintenance Date** (9): Enter the date of the last maintenance.
* **Maintenance Status** (10): Select the current status of the maintenance, either 'In Progress', 'Completed', or 'Not Scheduled'.
* **Contact Person** (13): Select the person responsible for the maintenance, such as 'Facility Manager', 'Maintenance Staff', or 'Other'.

#### Tips
* Ensure you accurately report the site ID to ensure correct identification of the equipment.
* Choose the correct equipment type to ensure accurate reporting.
* Clearly enter the location to avoid confusion.
* Provide a detailed description of the equipment.
* Enter the correct date of the last inspection to ensure accurate tracking.
* Ensure you select the correct outcome of the last inspection.
* Choose the correct maintenance frequency to ensure regular maintenance.
* Enter the correct date of the next maintenance to ensure it is scheduled correctly.
* Select the correct maintenance status to reflect the current state.
* Choose the correct contact person to ensure they are informed of the maintenance.
