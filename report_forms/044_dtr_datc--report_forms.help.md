# Dtr_Datc - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dtr_Datc form is designed to collect information about a worker's time and performance data. It's meant to be used by managers or supervisors to track and document work hours, time-in and time-out, and any notable events or incidents during a shift.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply fill in the required information and notes as needed. The form has 10 fields that you can fill out. Here's a step-by-step guide:

1. Fill in the time you started work (`Time-in`).
2. Fill in the time you finished work (`Time-out`).
3. Add any notes about your start time (`Time-in Note`).
4. Add any notes about your finish time (`Time-out Note`).
5. Indicate if you were late (`Late`).
6. Fill in any overtime hours worked (`Overtime`).
7. Report any incidents or events that occurred during your shift, such as accidents or issues with equipment (`Grooming`).
8. Fill in any additional work performance notes (`Work Performance`).
9. If you need to report any incidents or issues, add them in the `Late` field, which can also be used for other notable events.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Time-in (1)**: Enter the time you started work in 24-hour format.
* **Time-in Note (2)**: Add any notes about your start time, such as if you were running late or had to wait for a supervisor.
* **Time-out (3)**: Enter the time you finished work in 24-hour format.
* **Time-out Note (4)**: Add any notes about your finish time, such as if you had to extend your shift or had trouble with equipment.
* **Late (5)**: Check this box if you were late to work or had any issues with arrival times.
* **Overtime (6)**: Fill in any overtime hours worked, if applicable.
* **Grooming (7)**: Report any incidents or events that occurred during your shift, such as accidents or equipment malfunctions.
* **Work Performance (8)**: Fill in any additional work performance notes, such as if you completed tasks efficiently or met deadlines.
* **Time-in (9)**: This field is a duplicate of the first field, and you can ignore it.
