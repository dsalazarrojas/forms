# Suspicious Activity Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Suspicious Activity Report Form is designed to collect information about reported incidents or behaviors that are suspicious or warrant further investigation. This form is intended for use in reporting potential security threats or irregularities. Please complete this form carefully and honestly to provide accurate and useful information for further investigation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form title, "Suspicious Activity Report".
2. Enter the Report Date, in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Provide a detailed description of the suspect.
4. Upload or enter the Suspect Photo (if available).
5. Enter the Report Location.
6. Add any additional details regarding the report.
7. Choose whether the report has been Reported or Unreported.
8. Provide additional comments, if any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Suspicious Activity Report** (`suspicious_activity_report`, text, **required**): Enter a brief title for the report.
* **Report Date** (`report_date`, date, **required**): Enter the date the report was made, in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Suspect Description** (`suspect_description`, text, **optional**): Provide a detailed description of the suspect, including any relevant information such as their name, alias, or description.
* **Suspect Photo** (`suspect_photo`, text, **required**): Upload or enter the suspect's photo, if available. This can be a physical photo or a description of the suspect's appearance.
* **Report Location** (`report_location`, text, **required**): Enter the location where the suspicious activity occurred or was observed.
* **Report Details** (`report_details`, text, **optional**): Add any additional information that may be relevant to the report.
* **Submit Report** (`submit_report`, select_one, **required**): Choose whether the report has been Reported or Unreported.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, **optional**): Enter any additional comments or information that may be helpful in understanding the report.

Note: Please be honest and thorough when completing this form. The information provided will help in investigating and resolving potential security threats or irregularities.
