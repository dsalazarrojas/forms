# Geodetic Mark Recovery Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Geodetic Mark Recovery Report form is used to collect information for geodetic marks, which are signs or marks left on surfaces. Surveyors or teams input their findings, notes, and additional information related to geodetic marks using this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Geodetic Mark Recovery Report form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the mark recovery information, including the geodetic location, recovery date, and status.
2. Select the surveyor and team who conducted the survey.
3. Add any additional information or notes related to the mark recovery.
4. Upload any supporting photos or additional information as needed.
5. Review and submit the form for official review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Geodetic Mark Recovery** (`geodetic_mark_recovery_report_form_1`, text, required): Enter the mark recovery information, including the details of the recovery process.
* **Geodetic Mark Recoveries** (`geodetic_mark_recoveries`, select_multiple, required): Select the types of marks that were recovered, if applicable.
* **Geodetic Location** (`geodetic_location`, text, required): Enter the location where the mark was found.
* **Geodetic Recovery Date** (`geodetic_recovery_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the mark recovery.
* **Geodetic Status** (`geodetic_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the mark, such as "Recovered" or "Not Recovered".
* **Upload Photo** (`upload_photo`, text, optional): Upload any supporting photos or images related to the mark recovery.
* **Surveyor** (`geodetic_surveyor`, text, optional): Enter the name of the surveyor who conducted the survey.
* **Additional Information** (`upload_additional_info`, text, optional): Add any additional information related to the mark recovery.
* **Survey Team** (`geodetic_survey_team`, text, optional): Enter the name of the survey team that conducted the survey.
* **Survey Note** (`geodetic_survey_note`, note, optional): Add any notes or comments related to the survey.
* **Survey Date** (`geodetic_survey_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the survey.
* **Geodetic Recovery Date 2** (`geodetic_recovery_date_2`, date, optional): Enter the date of the recovery of additional marks.
* **Upload Additional Info 2** (`upload_additional_info_2`, text, optional): Upload any additional information related to the mark recovery.
* **Surveyor 2** (`geodetic_surveyor_2`, text, optional): Enter the name of the surveyor who conducted the survey.
* **Survey Team 2** (`geodetic_survey_team_2`, text, optional): Enter the name of the survey team that conducted the survey.
* **Survey Note 2** (`geodetic_survey_note_2`, note, optional): Add any notes or comments related to the survey.
* **Geodetic Location 2** (`geodetic_location_2`, text, optional): Enter the location where the additional marks were found.
* **Geodetic Status 2** (`geodetic_status_2`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the additional marks, if applicable.
* **Geodetic Recovery Date 3** (`geodetic_recovery_date_3`, date, optional): Enter the date of the recovery of further marks.
* **Survey Date 2** (`geodetic_survey_date_2`, date, optional): Enter the date of the further survey.
* **Surveyor 3** (`geodetic_surveyor_3`, text, optional): Enter the name of the surveyor who conducted the further survey.
* **Survey Team 3** (`geodetic_survey_team_3`, text, optional): Enter the name of the survey team that conducted the further survey.
* **Geodetic Location 3** (`geodetic_location_3`, text, optional): Enter the location where the further marks were found.
* **Geodetic Status 3** (`geodetic_status_3`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the further marks, if applicable.
* **Survey Note 3** (`geodetic_survey_note_3`, note, optional): Add any notes or comments related to the further survey.
