# Crop Scouting Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Crop Scouting Report Form is designed to collect information on the current state of crops in the field, helping field agents to report on their observations and findings. This data will aid in making informed decisions about crop care and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the "Farm Location" field with the location where the scouting was conducted.
2. Enter the type of plant being scouted in the "Plant type" field.
3. Enter the date of the scouting in the "Scouting Date" field.
4. Enter the time of the scouting in the "Scouting Time" field.
5. Describe the weather conditions in the "Weather Conditions" field.
6. Enter the current soil moisture level in the "Soil Moisture Level" field.
7. Enter the current soil temperature in the "Soil Temperature" field.
8. Describe any pest or disease issues in the "Pest and Disease" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Farm Location (farm_location)
* **Farm Location**: Enter the name or code of the farm where the scouting was conducted.

### Plant Type (plant_type)
* **Plant Type**: Describe the type of plant being scouted, e.g., corn, soybean, wheat, etc.

### Scouting Date (scouting_date)
* **Scouting Date**: Enter the date of the scouting, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Scouting Time (scouting_time)
* **Scouting Time**: Enter the time of the scouting, in the format HH:MM AM/PM.

### Weather Conditions (weather_conditions)
* **Weather Conditions**: Describe the current weather conditions, e.g., sunny, rainy, cloudy, etc.

### Soil Moisture Level (soil_moisture_level)
* **Soil Moisture Level**: Enter the current soil moisture level, e.g., dry, moist, flooded, etc.

### Soil Temperature (soil_temperature)
* **Soil Temperature**: Enter the current soil temperature in degrees Fahrenheit.

### Pest and Disease (pest_and_disease)
* **Pest and Disease**: Describe any pest or disease issues observed, e.g., aphids, powdery mildew, etc.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields (if any).
* Use the format specified for dates and times.
* Be as detailed as possible in the "Weather Conditions" and "Pest and Disease" fields.
* If unsure about any field, ask for help or clarification.
* This form is meant to be used for reporting, not for collecting sensitive or confidential information.
