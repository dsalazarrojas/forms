# Supplier Onsite Audit Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from suppliers about their on-site audit. It includes fields to capture the supplier's name, contact number, audit status, audit findings, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that the supplier's name and contact number are accurate and up-to-date.
2. Choose the audit status from the available options (Active or Inactive).
3. Enter the date of the onsite visit.
4. Provide a clear and concise description of the audit findings.
5. Rate the supplier's performance (Good, Fair, or other options if available).
6. Enter a conclusion based on the audit findings.
7. Add any additional comments if necessary.
8. Indicate if photos or videos were taken during the audit.
9. Enter any additional comments or notes about the audit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supplier Name (1)**: Enter the supplier's name as it appears on their business registration or contract.
* **Supplier Contact Number (2)**: Enter the supplier's contact number for communication and follow-up.
* **Date (3)**: Enter the date of the onsite audit visit.
* **Audit Status (4)**: Select the current status of the audit (Active or Inactive).
* **Onsite Auditor Name (5)**: Enter the name of the person conducting the onsite audit.
* **Onsite Visit Date (7)**: Enter the date of the onsite audit visit.
* **Audit Findings (8)**: Provide a detailed description of the audit findings.
* **Rating (9)**: Rate the supplier's performance (Good, Fair, or other options if available).
* **Audit Conclusion (10)**: Enter a clear and concise conclusion based on the audit findings.
* **Additional Comments (11)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the audit.
* **Photos (12)**: Indicate if photos were taken during the audit.
* **Videos (13)**: Indicate if videos were taken during the audit.
* **Audit Status Notes (14)**: Enter any additional notes about the audit status.
* **Onsite Auditor Title (15)**: Enter the title of the person conducting the onsite audit (if applicable).
* **Email (16)**: Enter the supplier's email address.
* **Phone (17)**: Enter the supplier's phone number.
* **Supplier Website (18)**: Enter the supplier's website.
* **Supplier Facebook (19)**: Enter the supplier's Facebook handle.
* **Supplier Twitter (20)**: Enter the supplier's Twitter handle.
* **Supplier LinkedIn (21)**: Enter the supplier's LinkedIn handle.
* **Supplier Instagram (22)**: Enter the supplier's Instagram handle.
* **Supplier Email (23)**: Enter the supplier's email address.
* **Supplier Phone (24)**: Enter the supplier's phone number.
* **Onsite Auditor Signature (25)**: Enter the signature of the person conducting the onsite audit.
 
 Note: Fields with a red star (*) are required fields.
