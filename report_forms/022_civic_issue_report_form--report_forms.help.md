# Civic Issue Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Civic Issue Report Form is designed to allow citizens to report issues affecting their community, such as public safety concerns, infrastructure problems, environmental issues, public health concerns, and zoning or property management matters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Decide if you wish to remain anonymous or provide your contact information.
2.  If you wish to remain anonymous, select "Yes, submit anonymously" in the "Do you wish to remain anonymous?" field.
3.  If you wish to provide your contact information, select "No, I will provide contact info" and enter your full name and email address in the respective fields.
4.  Select the category of the issue from the list of options.
5.  Enter the exact location or address of the issue.
6.  Enter the neighborhood name.
7.  Choose the date when the issue was first observed.
8.  Provide a detailed description of the issue.
9.  Indicate the urgency level of the issue.
10.  If applicable, include a photo of the issue (note the photo will be sent via email).
11.  If you would like to receive a follow-up on the status of your report, select "Yes, please".
12.  Finally, certify that the information provided is accurate to the best of your knowledge.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Do you wish to remain anonymous? (Anonymous submission)** (`anonymous_submission`, required/optional): Decide if you wish to remain anonymous or provide your contact information.
* **Your Full Name** (`reporter_name`, required/optional): Enter your full name. This field is only required if you do not select "Yes, submit anonymously".
* **Your Email Address** (`reporter_email`, required/optional): Enter your email address. This field is only required if you do not select "Yes, submit anonymously".
* **Category of the Issue** (`issue_category`, required): Select the category of the issue from the list of options.
* **Exact Location or Address** (`issue_location`, required): Enter the exact location or address of the issue.
* **Neighborhood Name** (`neighborhood_name`, required): Enter the neighborhood name.
* **Date when the issue was first observed** (`date_first_observed`, required): Choose the date when the issue was first observed.
* **Detailed Description of the Issue** (`issue_description`, required): Provide a detailed description of the issue.
* **Urgency of this Issue** (`urgency_level`, required): Indicate the urgency level of the issue.
* **Note - If you have photos, please send them to reports-civic.gov with the tracking ID provided after submission** (`photo_note`, required/optional): If you have photos, please note that they will be sent to reports-civic.gov via email.
* **Would you like a follow-up on the status of this report?** (`follow_up_permission`, required/optional): If you would like to receive a follow-up, select "Yes, please".
* **I certify that this information is accurate to the best of my knowledge** (`report_verification`, required): Confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Date of Report** (`report_date`, required): Enter the date of submission.
