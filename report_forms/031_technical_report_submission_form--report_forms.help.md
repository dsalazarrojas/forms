# technical-report-submission-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The technical-report-submission-form is designed to collect information from users for internal reporting and tracking purposes. Please ensure that you only submit the form once, as it is intended for reporting and tracking progress.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information before submitting the form.
2. Complete the form in a single session, as it's essential to prevent duplicate submissions.
3. Fill in each field accurately, following the explanations provided below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Technical Report** (`report_form`, text, required): Provide a brief summary of the technical report, including a concise description of the issue or problem.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, text, optional): Attach any relevant documents that support your report, such as images, videos, or links to related resources.
* **Author Details** (`author_details`, select_one, optional): Select one of the available authors for your report. This helps us track who submitted the report.
* **Report Comments** (`report_comments`, note, optional): Provide additional context or explanation for your report, if needed.
* **Submission Date** (`technical_report_submission_form_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are submitting the report.
* **Submission Time** (`technical_report_submission_form_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you are submitting the report.
Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required. Please fill in only the necessary information to avoid duplication.
## Tips
* Double-check your input before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
* If you have any issues or need assistance, reach out to the support team.
