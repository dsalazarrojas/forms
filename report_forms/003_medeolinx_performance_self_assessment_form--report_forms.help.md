# MedeoLinx Performance Self Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The MedeoLinx Performance Self Assessment Form is designed to collect performance evaluation metrics, allowing managers to provide feedback and employees to reflect on their performance level and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

### To complete the form, follow the steps below:

*   **Step 1: Review the form sections**
    -   The form consists of a series of questions and fields that you will answer on a page-by-page basis.
*   **Step 2: Answer the questions and fields**
    -   Fill in your answers to the questions, providing as much detail as possible.
    -   Select the relevant options for each question.
*   **Step 3: Review and submit the form**
    -   Review the answers and fields to ensure they are accurate and complete.
    -   Submit the form once you are satisfied with your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Manager Rating (ID: 2)**
*   **Manager Rating**: Select the manager's rating for your overall performance.
*   **Options:** 
    *   **Excellent**: Indicates an exceptional performance with minimal areas for improvement.
    *   **Needs Improvement**: Suggests room for improvement and requires focused effort.
    *   **Unsatisfactory**: Indicates a subpar performance that requires substantial improvement.

### 2. **Overall Performance Level (ID: 3)**
*   **Overall Performance Level**: Select your overall performance level for your position.
*   **Options:** 
    *   **High**: Indicates an outstanding performance at your level.
    *   **Medium**: Indicates a satisfactory performance with some areas for improvement.
    *   **Low**: Suggests a less-than-average performance requiring improvement.

### 3. **Areas of Improvement (ID: 4)**
*   **Areas of Improvement**: Select the areas where you need improvement for your performance.
*   **Options:** 
    *   **Communication**: Effective communication with colleagues, team members, and customers.
    *   **Time Management**: Effective time management to meet deadlines and tasks.
    *   **Teamwork**: Collaborative team work and cooperation.

### 4. **Date (ID: 5)**
*   **Date**: Enter the date this assessment is being completed.
*   **Type**: Date field to record the date of the assessment.

### 5. **Manager Comments (ID: 6)**
*   **Manager Comments**: Add any comments or feedback from your manager.
*   **Type**: Text field for comments.

### 6. **Employee Rating (ID: 7)**
*   **Employee Rating**: Select your own rating for your performance.
*   **Options:** 
    *   **High**: Indicates an exceptional performance with minimal areas for improvement.
    *   **Medium**: Suggests satisfactory performance with some areas for improvement.
    *   **Low**: Indicates a subpar performance requiring improvement.

### 7. **Manager Signature (ID: 8)**
*   **Manager Signature**: Sign and date the form with the manager's signature.
*   **Type**: Text field for signature.

### 8. **Employee Signature (ID: 9)**
*   **Employee Signature**: Sign and date the form with the employee's signature.
*   **Type**: Text field for signature.

## Tips
-   Make sure to fill out the form carefully, as the information is for internal use and performance evaluation.
-   Review the form before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
-   Ensure that both employee and manager have signed and dated their respective signature fields for verification purposes.
