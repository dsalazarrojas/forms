# Rheumatology Clinic Monthly Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The form is designed for Rheumatology Clinic staff to report on their monthly patient visits, capturing essential information about patient visits, including name, visit date, condition, treatment, and medical notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name on the "Patient Name" field (Page 1).
2. Enter the visit date on the "Visit Date" field (Page 2).
3. Fill out the "Patient Condition" and "Patient Treatment" fields (Page 3).
4. Record your doctor's signature and any relevant medical history, medication, or other notes (Page 4).
5. Enter any additional medical notes, if necessary (Page 5).
6. Enter the patient's ID number (Page 5).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's full name as it appears in their medical records.
* **Visit Date** (`visit_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the patient's visit.
* **Patient Condition** (`patient_condition`, text, required): Describe the patient's medical condition(s) being treated during the visit.
* **Patient Treatment** (`patient_treatment`, text, optional): Record any treatments or therapies administered to the patient during the visit.
* **Doctor Signature** (`doctor_signature`, text, required): Sign your name as the attending doctor.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Record any relevant medical history or allergies the patient has.
* **Medication** (`medication`, text, optional): Record any medications the patient is currently taking or was taking during the visit.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the patient's visit.
* **Patient ID** (`patient_id`, number, required): Enter the patient's unique ID number.

## Tips

* Ensure you have the necessary permission to access and report on patient data before filling out the form.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy to maintain patient confidentiality and medical record integrity.
* Consult with the relevant authorities or superiors if you have any doubts or concerns about the form or the information being collected.
* Please ensure that all fields have been completed before submitting the form.
