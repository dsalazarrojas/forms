# Occupational Therapy Progress Note Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Occupational Therapy Progress Note Template is used to document a patient's progress in occupational therapy. This form is designed to provide a structured way to record and track patient information, helping therapists and clinicians to assess and plan treatment effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Complete all required fields marked as "required."
2. For the "patient_progress" field (page 1), provide a brief description of the patient's progress in occupational therapy, including any notable achievements or concerns.
3. For the "diagnosis" field (page 2), enter the relevant diagnosis or condition being treated, if applicable.
4. For the "treatment_plan" field (page 3), outline the treatment plan and goals for the patient, if applicable.
5. For the "evaluation" field (page 4), indicate whether the patient has made progress or not by selecting "Yes" or "No."
6. For the "treatment_goals" field (page 5), select all applicable goals that the patient has achieved or is working towards.
7. For the "patient_outcome" field (page 6), enter the date of the last progress update.
8. For the "provider_notes" field (page 7), enter any additional comments or notes from the therapist or clinician.
9. For the "next_steps" field (page 8), enter the next planned steps or actions for the patient's care.
