# Leasing Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Leasing Checklist is a report form designed to gather information from users regarding their leasing activities. It helps in reporting and analysis of leasing-related data.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review each section carefully and choose the correct options for each field.
2.  Select the relevant check boxes for each section based on your activities.
3.  Double-check your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Check Voicemail** (`voicemail_checklist`, select_multiple, required/optional): Please check the boxes for voicemail activities that you have performed. Options are `True` and `False`.
* **Check Email** (`email_checklist`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check the boxes for email activities that you have performed. Options are `True` and `False`.
* **Open Work Order** (`work_order_checklist`, select_one, required/optional): Please choose if you have opened a work order for your activities. Options are `False` and `True`.
* **Payment** (`payment_checklist`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check the boxes for payment activities that you have performed. Options are `True` and `False`.
* **Update Key Log** (`key_log_checklist`, select_one, required/optional): Please choose if you have updated the key log for your activities. Options are `False` and `True`.
* **Update Email Checklist** (`update_email_checklist`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check the boxes for email checklist updates that you have performed. Options are `True` and `False`.
* **Voicemail Checklist 2** (`voicemail_checklist_2`, select_multiple, required/optional): Please check the boxes for voicemail activities that you have performed. Options are `True` and `False`.
* **Update Key Log Checklist** (`update_key_log_checklist`, select_one, required/optional): Please choose if you have updated the key log checklist for your activities. Options are `False` and `True`.
* **Open Work Order Checklist** (`open_work_order_checklist`, select_one, required/optional): Please choose if you have opened a work order for your activities. Options are `False` and `True`.
* **Check Email 2** (`check_email_2`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check the boxes for email activities that you have performed. Options are `True` and `False`.
* **Check Voicemail 2** (`check_voicemail_2`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check the boxes for voicemail activities that you have performed. Options are `True` and `False`.
* **Payment 2** (`payment_2`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check the boxes for payment activities that you have performed. Options are `True` and `False`.
