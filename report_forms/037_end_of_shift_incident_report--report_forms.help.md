# End Of Shift Incident Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "End Of Shift Incident Report" form is used to collect information about incidents that occurred during a shift. This form helps to report and track incidents, ensuring that all relevant details are documented and easily accessible for quality control, employee safety, and other purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Shift start time** and **Shift end time** to define the time period of the shift.
2. Choose the **Incident date** from the calendar picker or enter it manually.
3. Provide a detailed **Incident description** of what happened.
4. Select the **Incident location** where the incident occurred.
5. Select the **Incident type** from the provided options (Equipment failure, Employee error, Weather, or Other).
6. List the **Incident Employees involved** who were present during the incident.
7. If applicable, enter the **Witness** statement or contact information.
8. Enter any additional **Incident comments** or notes.
9. Choose the **Reported** status (True or False).
10. If required, enter the **Shift start** and **Shift end** coordinates (latitude and longitude).
11. Enter any **Supervisor** comments or notes.
12. If required, select the **Incident cause**.
13. Enter any additional **Action plan** for addressing the incident.
14. Enter any additional **Incident notes**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shift start time** (Time): Enter the time when the shift started.
* **Shift end time** (Time): Enter the time when the shift ended.
* **Incident date** (Date): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Incident description** (Text): Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Incident location** (Text): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident type** (Select one): Choose from the options (Equipment failure, Employee error, Weather, or Other).
* **Incident Employees Involved** (Select multiple): List the employees who were present during the incident.
* **Witness** (Text): Enter the witness statement or contact information.
* **Witness contact info** (Text): Enter the contact information of the witness.
* **Incident cause** (Text): Enter the cause of the incident.
* **Incident reporter** (Text): Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
* **Supervisor** (Text): Enter the name of the supervisor who is handling the incident.
* **Supervisor contact info** (Text): Enter the contact information of the supervisor.
* **Action plan** (Text): Enter any action plan for addressing the incident.
* **Incident comments** (Text): Enter any additional comments about the incident.
* **Incident notes** (Text): Enter any additional notes about the incident.
* **Reported** (Select one): Choose whether the incident was reported or not.
* **Shift start lat** (Number): Enter the latitude of the shift start location.
* **Shift start lng** (Number): Enter the longitude of the shift start location.
* **Shift end lat** (Number): Enter the latitude of the shift end location.
* **Shift end lng** (Number): Enter the longitude of the shift end location.
* **Supervisor notes** (Text): Enter any additional comments from the supervisor.
* **Incident date utc** (Text): Enter the UTC offset of the incident date.
* **Created by** (Text): Enter the name of the person who created the report.
* **Incident reported** (Select one): Choose whether the incident was reported or not.
