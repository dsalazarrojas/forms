Emergency Distribution SWOT Analysis - Help Guide
========================================

## Purpose

This form is designed to aid in the completion of a SWOT (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats) analysis for emergency distribution operations. The form collects information about the strengths and weaknesses of a response, potential opportunities and threats, details about the nature and location of the emergency distribution operation, and contact information for emergency contacts and submitters.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your response to the following questions:
	* **Strengths**: Describe the strengths of this response.
	* **Weaknesses**: Describe the weaknesses of this response.
	* **Opportunities**: Describe any opportunities you see for this response.
	* **Threats**: Describe any threats you see for this response.
	* **Emergency Distribution Operation**: Select the nature of the emergency distribution operation: Emergency Distribution for Disaster Response, Emergency Distribution for Humanitarian Response, or Emergency Distribution for Other.
	* **Location**: Describe where the emergency distribution operation will occur.
	* **Date**: Enter the date of the emergency distribution operation.
	* **Time**: Enter the time of the emergency distribution operation.
	* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about the operation.
	* **Email**: Enter the email of the emergency contact person.
	* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
	* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
	* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Describe the relationship between the submitter and the emergency contact person.
	* **Emergency Contact Notes**: Add any additional notes about the emergency contact person.
	* **Emergency Contact Position**: Describe the position of the emergency contact person.
	* **Emergency Distribution Team**: Describe the team involved in the emergency distribution operation.
	* **Submitter**: Enter your name.
	* **Submitter Email**: Enter your email.
	* **Submitter Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
2. Review your responses and confirm submission by checking the "Confirm Submission" box.
