# Trainee Progress Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trainee Progress Report Form is used to track the progress of trainees in various training programs. It is essential to fill in the form with accurate and up-to-date information to provide valuable feedback and insights for the trainees.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in the form with accurate and honest information about the trainee's progress.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled in.
- Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Trainee Details (1)
* **Trainee Details** (`trainee_progress_report_form`, text, not required): This field is for you to provide any general information about the trainee you are reporting on.
* **Trainee Title** (`trainee_title`, text, not required): Please enter the trainee's job title or relevant professional title.
* **Trainee Name** (`trainee_name`, text, not required): Enter the trainee's name.
* **Training Program** (`training_program`, text, not required): Provide the name of the training program the trainee is enrolled in.
* **Trainee Level** (`trainee_level`, select_one, not required): Choose the trainee's level of experience or seniority, either Junior or Senior.
* **Trainee Progress** (`trainee_progress`, text, not required): Describe the trainee's progress in detail.
* **Trainee Comments** (`trainee_comments`, text, not required): Enter any additional comments about the trainee's progress.
* **Trainee Email** (`trainee_email`, email, not required): Enter the trainee's email address (optional).
* **Trainee Phone** (`trainee_phone`, text, not required): Enter the trainee's phone number (optional).

### Trainer Information

* **Trainer Comments** (`trainer_comments`, text, not required): Enter any comments or feedback from the trainer about the trainee's progress.
* **Trainer Email** (`trainer_email`, email, not required): Enter the trainer's email address (optional).
* **Trainer Phone** (`trainer_phone`, text, not required): Enter the trainer's phone number (optional).

### Evaluation Details

* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, not required): Enter the time of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Period** (`evaluation_period`, number, not required): Enter the number of days or hours for the evaluation period.
* **Evaluation Type** (`evaluation_type`, select_one, not required): Choose the evaluation type, either On-site or Off-site.
* **Trainee Rating** (`trainee_rating`, number, not required): Enter a rating for the trainee's performance.
* **Trainer Rating** (`trainer_rating`, number, not required): Enter a rating for the trainer's performance.
* **Trainee Progress Percentage** (`trainee_progress_percentage`, number, not required): Enter the trainee's progress percentage.
* **Evaluation Comments** (`evaluation_comments`, text, not required): Enter any additional comments about the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Status** (`evaluation_status`, select_one, not required): Choose the evaluation status, either Complete or Not Complete.
* **Evaluation Date Status** (`evaluation_date_status`, select_one, not required): Choose the evaluation date status, either On Time or Late.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, not required): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, text, not required): Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.

## Tips
Please ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately before submitting the form. If a field is not required, it is optional, so feel free to leave it blank if it's not relevant.
