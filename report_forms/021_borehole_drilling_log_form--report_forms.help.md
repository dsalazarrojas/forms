Borehole Drilling Log Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Borehole Drilling Log Form is designed to document the details of borehole drilling operations. This form is used to collect and store information on drilling projects, including project identification, drilling specifications, stratigraphy and sampling, groundwater, and observations. It helps track and analyze the progress, quality, and results of drilling projects.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the required information before starting the form.
2. Fill out the project identification section with the project number, borehole ID, and site location.
3. Enter the date of drilling and the name of the driller.
4. In the drilling specifications section, select the drilling method used.
5. Enter the hole diameter and final depth in millimeters and meters, respectively.
6. In the stratigraphy and samples section, enter the sample depth from and to in meters.
7. Choose the sample type from the available options.
8. Provide a brief description of the soil or rock.
9. If applicable, report the water level struck and rest in meters.
10. Add any observations or drilling remarks.
11. Finally, select the log completion date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Number** (`project_no`): Enter the project number as assigned by your project management team.
* **Borehole ID** (`borehole_id_code`): Enter the unique code assigned to this borehole.
* **Site Location** (`site_loc`): Provide the geographical location of the drilling site.
* **Date of Drilling** (`drill_dt`): Enter the date when the drilling was performed.
* **Driller Name** (`driller_full_name`): Enter the name of the driller or person performing the drilling operation.
* **Drilling Method** (`drill_meth`): Select the method used for drilling, for example, Rotary, Percussion, etc.
* **Hole Diameter** (`hole_diam_mm`): Enter the diameter of the borehole in millimeters.
* **Final Depth** (`final_depth_m`): Enter the final depth of the borehole in meters.
* **Sample Depth From** (`depth_from_m`): Enter the depth from which the sample was collected in meters.
* **Sample Depth To** (`depth_to_m`): Enter the depth up to which the sample was collected in meters.
* **Sample Type** (`sample_type_select`): Select the type of sample collected, for example, Disturbed, Undisturbed, Core, etc.
* **Soil/Rock Description** (`soil_desc`): Provide a brief description of the soil or rock collected.
* **Water Level Struck** (`water_struck_m`): If applicable, enter the water level struck in meters.
* **Water Level Rest** (`water_rest_m`): If applicable, enter the water level rest in meters.
* **Drilling Remarks and Observations** (`drill_remarks`): Add any important notes or remarks about the drilling process.
* **Log Completion Date** (`log_completion_dt`): Enter the date when the log was completed.

Please note that all fields marked as required must be filled in accurately to ensure the form is submitted correctly.
