# Lost Receipt Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lost Receipt Form is a tool for reporting lost or misplaced receipts, such as store receipts, invoices, or other financial documents. It helps collect important information that may be required for reimbursement or other purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the receipt date you last had the item or service.
2. Enter the receipt number, if available.
3. Provide a brief description of the item or service purchased.
4. Enter the cost of the item or service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Receipt Date (1)
* **Receipt Date**: The date you last had the item or service. Please enter the date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

### Receipt Number (2)
* **Receipt Number**: The number on the receipt, if available. This may be a unique identifier assigned by the vendor or merchant.

### Item Description (3)
* **Item Description**: Briefly describe the item or service you purchased. This will help identify the specific item or service.

### Item Cost (4)
* **Item Cost**: Enter the cost of the item or service. This should match the amount on the receipt.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete the form as accurately as possible, as incorrect information may delay or affect reimbursement.
* If you have any trouble completing the form, contact the relevant authorities or support team for assistance.
