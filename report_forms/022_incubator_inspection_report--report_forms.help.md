# Incubator Inspection Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Incubator Inspection Report form is designed to collect information on the condition of incubators at a facility during an inspection. This form helps to identify any issues or concerns that need to be addressed, and ensures that the facility is meeting the necessary standards for safe and proper incubator usage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Inspect Incubators**: Start by reviewing the incubators at the facility to note any issues or concerns.
2. **Fill in the Form**: Answer each field as accurately as possible based on your findings.
3. **Submit the Form**: Once completed, submit the form for review and follow up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incubator Name**: Please enter the name of the incubator being inspected.
* **Incubator Type**: Select the type of incubator being inspected from the options provided.
* **Date Of Inspection**: Enter the date of the inspection in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Time Of Inspection**: Enter the time of the inspection in the correct format (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Completed**: Check off any completed actions or issues addressed during the inspection.
* **Facility Address**: Enter the address of the facility where the inspection was conducted.
* **Facility Contact**: Select the contact person at the facility responsible for the incubators.
* **Comments**: Check off any comments or notes about the inspection.
* **Issues Found**: Enter any issues or concerns discovered during the inspection.
* **Action Planned**: Select the action planned to address any issues discovered.
* **Facility Signature**: Enter the signature of the facility representative.
* **Inspector Signature**: Enter your signature as the inspector. 

Note: Ensure that the facility representative signs and dates their signature before completing this form.
 
## Tips
* Always ensure that the form is completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Make sure to follow the facility's policies and procedures for incubator usage and maintenance.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that it is complete and accurate.
