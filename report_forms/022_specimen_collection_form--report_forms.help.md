**Specimen Collection Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The Specimen Collection Form is a document used to collect information about specimens, such as patient data and laboratory results. This form is essential for maintaining accurate records and facilitating smooth specimen processing and analysis. Ensure that you have the right to access and collect specimens before filling out this form, as it is a critical part of medical practice. Duplicate entries should be avoided to prevent inconsistencies in specimen records.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. **Patient ID**: Enter the unique identifier assigned to the patient.
2. **Patient Name**: Provide the full name of the patient, including any middle name(s) or alias(es) if applicable.
3. **Patient Date-Time**: Enter the date and time when the specimen was collected or the appointment time for collection.
4. **Specimen ID**: Enter the unique identifier assigned to the specimen.
5. **Collector Name**: Provide the name of the person who collected the specimen.
6. **Collector Signature**: Include your signature as the person collecting the specimen.
7. **Laboratory ID**: Enter the unique identifier of the laboratory processing the specimen.
8. **Laboratory Name**: Provide the name of the laboratory processing the specimen.
9. **Collector Phone**: Enter your phone number for any further communication regarding the specimen.
10. **Patient Address**: Enter the address of the patient, including their postal code for delivery or pickup.
11. **Patient E-mail**: Enter the email address of the patient for communication purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient ID** (ID): Unique identifier assigned to the patient.
* **Patient Name** (Text): Full name of the patient.
* **Patient Date-Time** (Date): Collection date and time or appointment time for collection.
* **Specimen ID** (ID): Unique identifier assigned to the specimen.
* **Collector Name** (Text): Name of the person collecting the specimen.
* **Collector Signature** (Text): Your signature as the person collecting the specimen.
* **Laboratory ID** (Text): Unique identifier of the laboratory processing the specimen.
* **Laboratory Name** (Text): Name of the laboratory processing the specimen.
* **Collector Phone** (Text): Your phone number for further communication.
* **Patient Address** (Text): Address of the patient including postal code for delivery or pickup.
* **Patient E-mail** (Email): E-mail address of the patient for communication purposes.

## Tips

* Double-check your input for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure all required fields have valid information to avoid submission errors.
* If unsure about any field, consult your supervisor or refer to relevant policies and procedures.
* This form is designed for collecting specimen information; do not use it for other purposes.

Please refer to your local policies and procedures for further guidance on specimen collection, processing, and storage. 

This form is intended to be filled out by authorized personnel only. If you are not authorized, please do not proceed with completing this form.
