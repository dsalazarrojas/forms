# weekly_clinic_activity_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "weekly_clinic_activity_report_form" is a form used to report activities that occur on a weekly basis within a clinic setting. It is used to document and track activities that need to be reported and followed up on.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the relevant category of the activity from the provided options.
2. Provide a brief description of the activity.
3. Enter the date the activity occurred.
4. Enter the time the activity occurred.
5. Add any notes about the activity.
6. Enter the email address of the person assigned to follow up on the activity.
7. Enter the phone number of the person assigned to follow up on the activity.
8. Select the person assigned to the activity from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Category
* **Category** (`category`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the relevant category of the activity from the provided options.

### Description
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of what happened.

### Date
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the activity occurred.

### Time
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Enter the time the activity occurred.

### Notes
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required): Add any additional notes about the activity.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the person assigned to follow up on the activity.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the person assigned to follow up on the activity.

### Assigned To
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `select_one`, required): Select the person assigned to the activity from the provided options.

## Tips

* Make sure to select the correct category for the activity.
* Double-check the date and time entered to ensure accuracy.
* Be sure to enter the correct email and phone number of the person assigned to follow up on the activity.
* Select the correct person assigned to the activity.
* Use the notes field to add any additional information about the activity that may be helpful to follow up on.
* This form is used for reporting activities that occur on a weekly basis within a clinic setting, so be clear and concise in your reporting.
