# Meal Packing Financial Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report financial details about events, such as funding sources, expenses, and uploads. It's essential to provide accurate financial information to ensure correct data recording and prevent duplication of reports.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Event Details" field with any relevant event information.
2. Select the funding sources for your event from the list of options.
3. Enter the total expenses for your event.
4. Choose the type of upload you want to associate with your report from the available options.
5. Enter the event expenses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details**: Enter a brief description of the event, including its name, date, and location.
* **Funding Sources**: Select the sources of funding for your event from the options provided. You can choose one or multiple sources.
* **Expenses**: Enter the total expenses for your event.
* **Uploads**: Select the type of upload you want to associate with your report from the available options.
* **Event Expenses**: Enter the expenses specific to the event.

## Tips
- Double-check the fields for accuracy before submitting the report.
- Make sure to review existing reports to prevent duplication.
- If you're unsure about any field, it's best to contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
