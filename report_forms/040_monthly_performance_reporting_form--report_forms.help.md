# monthly_performance_reporting_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The monthly performance reporting form is a tool for managers to evaluate and document the performance of their team members. This form helps in tracking hours worked, project status, and providing feedback on the team member's progress.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the manager's name and email in the respective fields.
2. Select the status of each team member from the 'Project Status' dropdown menu.
3. Fill in the hours worked for each team member.
4. Enter any additional notes or comments for each project.
5. Rate the performance of each team member from 'Good', 'Fair', and 'Poor'.
6. Rate the overall satisfaction from 'Satisfied', 'Neutral', and 'Dissatisfied'.
7. Enter any next steps for each project.
8. Rate the manager's satisfaction with the team member's performance.
9. Enter any additional comments for the manager and employee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name as the manager.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the status of each team member from 'Yes', 'No', 'Maybe'.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the status of each project from 'Active', 'Inactive'.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes for each project.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the start date of each project.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the end date of each project.
* **Hours Worked** (`hours_worked`, `number`, optional): Enter the total hours worked by each team member.
* **Total Hours** (`total_hours`, `number`, optional): Enter the total hours worked on each project.
* **Project Hours Worked** (`project_hours_worked`, `number`, optional): Enter the hours worked on each project.
* **Project Total Hours** (`project_total_hours`, `number`, optional): Enter the total hours worked on each project.
* **Hours Over Budget** (`hours_over_budget`, `number`, optional): Enter the hours worked over budget.
* **Budgeted Hours** (`budgeted_hours`, `number`, optional): Enter the budgeted hours.
* **Hours Worked Overtime** (`hours_worked_overtime`, `number`, optional): Enter the hours worked overtime.
* **Hours Overtime** (`hours_overtime`, `number`, optional): Enter the total hours worked overtime.
* **Project Hours Overtime** (`project_hours_overtime`, `number`, optional): Enter the hours worked overtime on each project.
* **Budgeted Overtime** (`budgeted_overtime`, `number`, optional): Enter the budgeted overtime hours.
* **Total Hours Overtime** (`total_hours_overtime`, `number`, optional): Enter the total hours worked overtime.
* **Project Notes** (`project_notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments for each project.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, `note`, optional): Enter any comments from the manager.
* **Employee Comments** (`employee_comments`, `note`, optional): Enter any comments from the employee.
* **Project Status Comments** (`project_status_comments`, `note`, optional): Enter any comments for each project status.
* **Performance Rating** (`performance_rating`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the performance of each team member from 'Good', 'Fair', 'Poor'.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the overall satisfaction from 'Satisfied', 'Neutral', 'Dissatisfied'.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, `note`, optional): Enter any next steps for each project.
* **Manager Rating** (`manager_rating`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the manager's satisfaction with the team member's performance from 'Good', 'Fair', 'Poor'.
