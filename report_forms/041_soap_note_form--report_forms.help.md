# soap_note_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about patients who are being treated in a medical setting. It's intended for healthcare providers to document their interactions with patients, including patient demographics, medical history, and visit details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Select the date of the visit in the "Date Entered" field.
3. Choose the patient's gender in the "Patient Gender" field.
4. If applicable, select the patient's race in the "Patient Race" field.
5. Enter the provider's name in the "Provider Name" field.
6. Enter the reason for the visit in the "Reason for Visit" field.
7. Describe the patient's condition in the "Patient Condition" field.
8. Record any medical notes about the patient in the "Medical Notes" field.
9. If applicable, enter any allergies or medical conditions in the "Allergies" field.
10. Record any medications being taken by the patient in the "Medications" field.
11. Enter any notes about the patient's vital signs in the "Vital Signs" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name.
* **Date Entered**: Enter the date the patient was visited, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Patient Gender**: Select the gender of the patient (Male, Female, or Other).
* **Patient Race**: Select one or more of the options (White, Black, Asian, Other) if the patient's race is known.
* **Provider Name**: Enter your name as the healthcare provider.
* **Provider Title**: You don't need to fill this field if you're not sure what it represents.
* **Visit Date**: Enter the date of the visit, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Visit Time**: Enter the time of the visit, using 24-hour format.
* **Patient Status**: Select the visit type (Inpatient, Outpatient, ER).
* **Reason for Visit**: Describe the reason for the patient's visit.
* **Patient Condition**: Describe the patient's condition.
* **Medical Notes**: Enter any relevant medical notes about the patient.
* **Allergies**: Enter any allergies or medical conditions the patient has.
* **Medications**: Enter any medications the patient is taking.
* **Vital Signs**: Enter any notes about the patient's vital signs, or leave this field blank if not applicable.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- If you're unsure about a field, you can leave it blank and fill it in when more information becomes available.
- This form is intended for healthcare providers to document patient interactions, so please follow your institution's policies and procedures for completing forms like this.
