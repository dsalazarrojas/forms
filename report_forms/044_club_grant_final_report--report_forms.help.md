# Club Grant Final Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Club Grant Final Report is a form used by clubs to report on the completion of a grant they have received. It is essential to provide accurate and detailed information about the grant to ensure that the club's progress and financial status are properly documented and reported.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the Grant Program Name from the available options.
2. Enter the total grant amount received.
3. Enter the start and end dates of the grant period.
4. Enter the Grant ID or Reference Number if provided by the funder.
5. Enter the name of your club.
6. Enter the name of your club's president.
7. Enter your contact email address.
8. Enter your contact phone number (optional).
9. Describe the original project goals.
10. Describe the goals that were achieved.
11. Estimate or enter the exact number of people served or participated.
12. Describe the key outcomes and results of your project.
13. Describe any unexpected results or changes that occurred.
14. Enter the total amount spent.
15. Enter the remaining unspent funds.
16. Describe the major expense categories.
17. Explain any budget variance.
18. Select the type of supporting documentation attached (e.g., receipts, invoices, photos, attendance records, evaluation surveys, or other).
19. Describe your club's plan for follow-up and sustainability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Grant Program Name**: Enter the name of the grant program you received.
* **Grant Amount Received - USD**: Enter the total amount of money you received from the grant.
* **Grant Period Start Date**: Enter the date when the grant period began.
* **Grant Period End Date**: Enter the date when the grant period ended.
* **Grant ID or Reference Number**: Enter the Grant ID or Reference Number if provided by the funder.
* **Club Name**: Enter the name of your club.
* **Club President Name**: Enter the name of your club's president.
* **Contact Email Address**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number (optional).
* **Original Project Goals**: Describe the original project goals you aimed to achieve.
* **Goals Achieved**: Describe the goals that were successfully completed.
* **Number of People Served or Participated**: Estimate or enter the exact number of people served or participated.
* **Key Outcomes and Results**: Describe the key outcomes and results of your project.
* **Unexpected Results or Changes**: Describe any unexpected results or changes that occurred.
* **Total Amount Spent - USD**: Enter the total amount of money spent.
* **Remaining Unspent Funds - USD**: Enter the remaining unspent funds.
* **Major Expense Categories**: Describe the breakdown of how money was spent.
* **Budget Variance Explanation**: Explain any budget variance that occurred.
* **Documentation Attached**: Select the type of supporting documentation attached (e.g., receipts, invoices, photos, attendance records, evaluation surveys, or other).
* **Documentation Type**: This field is not explicitly mentioned in the original YAML, but based on the options, it seems to be a select_multiple field where you can choose one or more options.
* **Follow-up and Sustainability**: Describe your club's plan for follow-up and sustainability.
