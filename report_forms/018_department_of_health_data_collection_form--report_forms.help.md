# Department Of Health Data Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect general health data from individuals for reporting and data analysis purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

3. Ensure you fill out all required fields (if any) before submitting the form.
