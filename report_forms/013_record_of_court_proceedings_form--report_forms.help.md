# record_of_court_proceedings_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "record_of_court_proceedings_form" is a form used to collect and report details of court proceedings, court cases, or hearings. It helps in maintaining a structured and organized record of judicial activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

1. Review the form's purpose and ensure you have the relevant information required for the specific court proceeding you are documenting.
2. Fill in the form with the corresponding details:
	* **Court Date**: Enter the date of the court proceeding.
3. Enter **Case Number**: Provide the case number associated with the proceeding.
4. Enter **Court Number**: If applicable, input the specific court number where the proceeding took place.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Court Date** (1): Enter the date of the court proceeding (e.g., 2022-02-16).
* **Case Number** (2): Enter the unique number assigned to the case.
* **Court Number** (3): Enter the specific court number where the proceeding took place, if applicable.
