# customer_complaint_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather customer complaints about products and services. Please use this form to report any issues you are experiencing with our products or services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you are experiencing a problem with a product.
2. Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in our records.
3. Describe the issue you are experiencing with our product in the text field.
4. Choose how you would like to be contacted (yes/no).
5. Enter your contact information (e.g., phone number, email).
6. Enter your email address.
7. Select the type of product you are experiencing issues with (yes/no, one or multiple selections).
8. Click submit to submit your complaint.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **customer_complaint_form**: This is a placeholder page to help you navigate the form.
* **product_id**: Select 'Yes' to indicate if you are experiencing issues with a product. If you are not sure if the issue is with a product, select 'No'.
* **customer_name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in our records.
* **complaint_description**: Describe the issue you are experiencing with our product in this text field. Please provide as much detail as possible.
* **contact_method**: Choose how you would like to be contacted: by phone, email, or in person. Please note that we will try to contact you in the method you select.
* **contact_info**: Enter your contact information (e.g., phone number, email) so we can reach you.
* **customer_complaint_email**: Enter a valid email address so we can contact you.
* **product_type**: Select the type of product you are experiencing issues with. You can select 'Yes' for one product or multiple products that you are experiencing issues with.
