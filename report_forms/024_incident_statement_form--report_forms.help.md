# Incident Statement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Incident Statement Form is used to document and report incidents that occur within your department or team. This form helps to gather detailed information about the incident, which can be used for investigation, reporting, and follow-up actions.

## How To Complete This Form
This form is designed to be completed by staff members who have witnessed or been involved in the incident. To complete the form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Statement Form 1** (`incident_statement_form_1`, text, optional): Enter a brief title for the incident statement form. This will help with organization and categorization of the form.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the incident using the calendar format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the incident in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the incident, including what happened, how it happened, and any relevant details.
* **Injuries** (`incident_injuries`, select multiple, optional): If there were any injuries as a result of the incident, select the relevant options from the dropdown list. You can select multiple options if necessary.
* **Damages** (`incident_damages`, number, optional): Enter the amount of damages or financial impact from the incident.
