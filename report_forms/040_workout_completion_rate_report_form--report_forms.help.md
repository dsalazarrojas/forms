# Workout Completion Rate Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect data on the completion rate of workouts for participants in a fitness program. This helps fitness professionals track the progress and effectiveness of their programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the participant's name in the **Participant Name** field.
2. Select the start and end dates of the reporting period using the **Start Date** and **End Date** fields.
3. If the participant has a contact email address, enter it in the **Email** field.
4. If the participant has a contact phone number, enter it in the **Phone** field.
5. Select the participant's gender from the **Gender** dropdown menu.
6. Select the type(s) of workouts the participant completed during the reporting period from the **Workout Type** dropdown menu.
7. Enter the number of workouts the participant completed per week in the **Workout Frequency** field.
8. Enter the number of minutes spent on each workout session in the **Workout Minutes Per Session** field.
9. If you have any additional information about the participant, enter it in the **Participant Notes** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Report Form**: This is the first page of the form where you'll enter general information about the participant.
* **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant who completed the workouts.
* **Start Date**: Select the date when the participant began working out.
* **End Date**: Select the date when the participant completed the workouts.
* **Email**: If the participant has a contact email address, enter it here.
* **Phone**: If the participant has a contact phone number, enter it here.
* **Gender**: Select the participant's gender (Male, Female, or Other).
* **Workout Type**: Select the type of workout(s) the participant completed (Strength Training, Cardio, Both).
* **Workout Frequency**: Enter the number of workouts the participant completed per week.
* **Workout Minutes Per Session**: Enter the total number of minutes spent on each workout session.
* **Participant Notes**: Enter any additional information about the participant, such as their progress or challenges.
* **Participant Notes 2**: Enter any additional information about the participant, such as their goals or objectives.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information for each field to ensure reliable data for analysis.
* If a participant's workout schedule varies, you may need to enter multiple rows for each week or month.
* If a participant has multiple contact information (e.g., email and phone number), enter all of it.
