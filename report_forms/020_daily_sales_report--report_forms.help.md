# Daily Sales Report - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Daily Sales Report form is used to record and track sales activities for a specific day. It helps sales teams to monitor their performance, identify patterns and trends, and make data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Ensure you are using the correct date for the report.
2.  Select the correct customer ID for the sales interaction.
3.  Enter the product or service sold, if applicable.
4.  Choose the sales person who handled the sale.
5.  Enter the total sales amount.
6.  Add any additional notes about the sale, if necessary.
7.  Select the days of the week the sale was made.
8.  Choose the month in which the sale occurred.
9.  Select the year the sale occurred.
10.  Enter the time of the sale.
11.  Optionally, provide your email address and/or phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sales Date**: Enter the date of the sale (YYYY-MM-DD format).
* **Customer ID**: Select the ID of the customer the sale was made to.
* **Product Name**: Optional field for noting the product or service sold.
*   *Sales Person*: Choose the sales representative responsible for the sale.
*   *Sales Amount*: Enter the total sales amount.
*   *Notes*: Add any additional comments or remarks about the sale.
*   *Daily*: Select the day of the week the sale occurred.
*   *Week*: Select the days of the week the sale occurred.
*   *Month*: Choose the month in which the sale happened.
*   *Year*: Select the year the sale took place.
*   *Time*: Enter the time of the sale.
*   *Email*: Optionally, enter your email address for contact purposes.
*   *Phone*: Optionally, enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips

- Always ensure that all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
- If a product was not sold, it's okay to leave this field empty, as it's not required.
- Double-check your entries for accuracy to avoid errors in the data analysis.
