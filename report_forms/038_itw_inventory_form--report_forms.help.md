# itw_inventory_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ITW Inventory Form is used to gather information about inventory items. This form is used for internal tracking and reporting purposes, and is typically completed by authorized personnel. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Enter your name and any other required information in the fields provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **inventory_form_1** (text, optional): Enter a brief description of the inventory item.
* **inventory_form_2** (text, optional): Enter a detailed description of the inventory item.
* **inventory_form_3** (number, optional): Enter the quantity of the inventory item.
* **inventory_form_4** (number, optional): Enter the value of the inventory item.
* **inventory_form_5** (text, optional): Enter any additional information about the inventory item.
* **inventory_form_6** (text, optional): Enter any further information about the inventory item.
* **inventory_form_7** (text, optional): Enter any comments or notes about the inventory item.
* **inventory_form_8** (text, optional): Enter any other relevant details about the inventory item.
* **inventory_form_9** (text, optional): Enter any other information about the inventory item.
* **inventory_form_10** (text, optional): Enter any final information about the inventory item.
* **inventory_form_11** (text, optional): Enter any last comments or notes about the inventory item.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and clear information to ensure accurate inventory tracking.
* If you are unsure about any information, consult with the relevant personnel before completing this form.
* This form is for internal use only, and all fields are optional, however, required fields are marked as such. 

Please note that some fields may be marked as optional but still required for specific tasks or reporting purposes. Always review the form and its requirements before starting.
