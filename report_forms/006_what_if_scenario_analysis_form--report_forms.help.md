# what_if_scenario_analysis_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to analyze what-if scenarios, providing a structured framework to assess the potential impact and risks associated with a given situation. It guides users in identifying critical factors and mitigation steps to prepare for and respond to potential outcomes. The form is intended for scenarios that require careful consideration of various risks and consequences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a name for the scenario in the "Scenario Name" field.
2. Provide a detailed description of the scenario in the "Description" field.
3. Select the potential impacts and risks associated with the scenario from the respective dropdown menus.
4. Describe the specific risks and mitigation steps for each selected risk in the "Risks and Mitigation Steps" field.
5. Choose the severity and value of the impact, using the "Impact Severity" and "Impact Value" dropdown menus.
6. Specify the frequency of the impact, using the "Impact Frequency" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Scenario Name** (`1`, text, required): Enter a brief and descriptive name for the scenario, which will help you identify and track different scenarios.
* **Description** (`2`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the scenario, including relevant details and context.
* **Impact** (`3`, select_multiple, optional): Assess the potential impacts associated with the scenario. Select multiple options to account for various possible outcomes.
* **Risks and Mitigation Steps** (`4`, select_multiple, optional): For each selected risk, provide specific mitigation steps to address or minimize the risk.
* **Risks** (`5`, select_multiple, optional): Identify the potential risks associated with the scenario.
* **Mitigation Steps** (`6`, select_multiple, optional): For each selected risk, provide specific steps to mitigate or minimize the risk.
* **Impact Description** (`7`, text, optional): Describe the impact of the selected risk in detail, including its characteristics and consequences.
* **Impact Severity** (`8`, select_one, optional): Choose the severity level of the impact, indicating its potential extent and likelihood.
* **Impact Value** (`9`, select_one, optional): Select the value of the impact, representing its relative importance or consequence.
* **Impact Frequency** (`10`, select_one, optional): Specify the frequency of the impact, indicating how often it is expected to occur.
