# Executive Order Violation Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Executive Order Violation Report Form is used to report an incident where an executive order has been violated. This form is meant to be completed by an individual who has witnessed or experienced the violation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the requested information. If a field is optional, you may skip it if it is not applicable to your situation. Make sure to submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Form** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, required: false`): 
  This is the main text area where you can describe the incident of executive order violation.
* **Complainant Information** (`id: 2`, `type: text`, required: false`): 
  Please provide information about the person who was affected by the violation, such as their name, contact information, and address.
* **Violation Information** (`id: 3`, `type: text`, required: false`): 
  This field is for providing details about the violation itself, such as what happened and when.
* **Violation Date** (`id: 4`, `type: date`, required: false`): 
  Enter the date of the violation.
* **Time of Violation** (`id: 5`, `type: time`, required: false`): 
  Enter the time of the violation.
* **Time** (`id: 6`, `type: time`, required: false`): 
  This field is intended to record the time of day when the violation occurred, but it seems to be a duplicate of "Time of Violation". Please use the latter field instead.
* **Reporter ID** (`id: 7`, `type: number`, required: false`): 
  Enter your ID number if applicable.
* **Contact Information** (`id: 8`, `type: text`, required: false`): 
  Provide any relevant contact information that can be used to reach out to you for follow-up questions.
* **Address Information** (`id: 9`, `type: text`, required: false`): 
  Enter any relevant address information related to the incident.
* **Comments** (`id: 10`, `type: text`, required: false`): 
  Enter any additional comments or details about the violation.
* **Executive Order Violation Report Form Comments** (`id: 11`, `type: text`, required: false`): 
  Enter any additional comments or details about the report itself.

## Tips
- Make sure to complete all required fields.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, contact the relevant authorities or personnel.
