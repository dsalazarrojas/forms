# Employee Injury Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Injury Report Form is designed to gather information about incidents that have occurred to employees within your company. It helps to document the incident, track its details, and ensure that the necessary steps are taken to support the employee's recovery and the company's risk management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by filling in your company's name in the "company" field.
2. Enter the date of the incident in the "incident date" field. If the incident is ongoing, use the current date.
3. Provide the time of the incident in the "incident time" field.
4. Enter the name of the employee who was injured in the "employee" field.
5. Identify the supervisor or manager who is responsible for handling the incident in the "supervisor" field.
6. Choose the type of injury that occurred from the options provided in the "injury type" field.
7. Briefly describe the injury in the "injury description" field.
8. Enter the location where the incident occurred in the "incident location" field.
9. If the incident location is not listed, enter the alternative location in the "incident location other" field.
10. Finally, enter the date of the incident again in the "incident date" field (this is a duplicate field for consistency and accuracy).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **company** (text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **incident_date** (date, required): Enter the date of the incident.
* **incident_time** (time, required): Enter the time of the incident.
* **employee** (text, required): Enter the name of the employee who was injured.
* **supervisor** (text, required): Enter the name of the supervisor or manager responsible for handling the incident.
* **injury_type** (select_one, required): Choose the type of injury that occurred from the options provided:
	+ Employee injured
	+ Property damage
	+ Third-party injury
* **injury_description** (text, required): Briefly describe the injury.
* **incident_location** (text, required): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **incident_location_other** (text, optional): If the incident location is not listed, enter the alternative location.
* **incident_date** (date, required): Duplicate field for consistency and accuracy.
