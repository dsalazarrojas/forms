# Daily Mentor Score Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from mentors on the progress of mentees in their sessions. This feedback is used to track and analyze the progress of mentees over time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Session ID to identify the session you are reporting on.
2. Enter the Mentor Name, as recorded in your session notes.
3. Enter the Mentee Name, as recorded in your session notes.
4. Provide a brief description of what happened during the session in Session Notes.
5. Rate the Mentee's progress on a scale of 1-5 and 6-10, using the corresponding fields.
6. Specify any next steps for the Mentee's growth and development.
7. Add any comments or additional notes about the session.
8. Enter the name of the person submitting the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session ID** (1): Enter the unique ID of the session you are reporting on.
* **Mentor Name** (2): Enter the name of the mentor who conducted the session.
* **Mentee Name** (3): Enter the name of the mentee who attended the session.
* **Session Notes** (4): Describe what happened during the session, including any notable events or discussions.
* **Rating 1-5** (5): Evaluate the mentee's progress on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Rating 6-10** (6): Evaluate the mentee's progress on a scale of 6-10, where 6 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* **Progress** (7): Enter the mentee's progress as a number, where 0 is no progress and 100 is full progress.
* **Next Steps** (8): Specify any next steps or recommendations for the mentee's growth and development.
* **Comments** (9): Add any additional comments or notes about the session.
* **Submitted By** (10): Enter the name of the person submitting the report.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely for each session.
* Do not duplicate entries for the same session.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or support team.
