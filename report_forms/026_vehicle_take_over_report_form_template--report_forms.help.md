# Vehicle Take Over Report Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record information about a vehicle being returned to the company after it has been used by an employee or driver.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with the relevant information about the vehicle's condition and usage.
2. Make sure to sign the form to indicate that the information provided is accurate and complete.
3. Submit the form to the designated person or department for review and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Transfer Date** (`transfer_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the vehicle was transferred or taken over.
* **Odometer Reading** (`odometer_reading`, number, optional): Enter the vehicle's odometer reading at the time of transfer.
* **Mileage Logged** (`mileage_logged`, number, optional): Enter the total mileage logged during the vehicle's usage.
* **Fuel Level** (`fuel_level`, number, optional): Enter the vehicle's fuel level at the time of transfer.
* **Vehicle Damage Description** (`vehicle_damage_description`, text, optional): Describe any damage or issues found with the vehicle.
* **Additional Remarks** (`mileage_remarks`, text, optional): Enter any additional remarks or comments about the vehicle's condition.
* **Previous Driver's Odometer Reading** (`mileage_reading_previous_driver`, number, optional): Enter the odometer reading of the previous driver.
* **Driver's Odometer Reading** (`mileage_reading_this_driver`, number, optional): Enter the odometer reading of the current driver.
* **Fuel Consumed** (`fuel_consumed`, number, optional): Enter the total fuel consumed during the vehicle's usage.
* **Fuel Cost** (`fuel_cost`, number, optional): Enter the total fuel cost incurred during the vehicle's usage.
* **Total Fuel Consumed** (`total_fuel_consumed`, number, optional): Enter the total fuel consumed during the vehicle's usage.
* **Average Fuel Consumption** (`average_fuel_consumption`, number, optional): Calculate the average fuel consumption per vehicle usage.
* **Vehicle Returned** (`vehicle_returned`, date, optional): Enter the date when the vehicle was returned.
* **Remarks** (`remarks`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or remarks about the vehicle's condition.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, optional): Select the person or department assigned to handle the vehicle.
* **Remarks Vehicles Returned** (`remarks_vehicles_returned`, select_multiple, optional): Select the status of the vehicles returned.
* **Return Date** (`vehicle_return_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the vehicle was returned.
* **Remarks Returned** (`remarks_returned`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or remarks about the vehicle's condition.
* **Remarks Returned Vehicle** (`remarks_returned_vehicle`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or remarks about the vehicle's condition.
* **Fuel Consumption Returned** (`fuel_consumption_returned`, number, optional): Enter the total fuel consumed during the vehicle's usage.
* **Remarks Returned Fuel** (`remarks_returned_fuel`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or remarks about the fuel consumed.
* **Remarks Returned Vehicle 2** (`remarks_returned_vehicle_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or remarks about the vehicle's condition.
* **Vehicle Status** (`vehicle_return_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the status of the vehicle after return.
* **Vehicle Return Status** (`vehicle_return_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the status of the vehicle after return.
* **Remarks Returned Vehicle Status 2** (`remarks_returned_vehicle_status_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or remarks about the vehicle's condition.

Note: The fields marked as "select_one" or "select_multiple" allow you to choose from a dropdown list of options, while fields marked as "text" or "number" require you to enter a free-form text or numeric value.
