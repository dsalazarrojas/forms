# Defamation Reporting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Defamation Reporting Form is a tool for reporting incidents of defamation. The form is designed to gather information about the defamation incident, including the defamed individual, the nature of the defamation, and any relevant circumstances surrounding the incident.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name**: Enter your first name.
*   **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
*   **Email**: Enter your email address.
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
*   **Date of Incident**: Enter the date of the defamation incident, if known.
*   **Description of Incident**: Describe the defamation incident, including any relevant details.
*   **Location**: Enter the location of the defamation incident, if known.
*   **Defamed Public Figure**: Indicate whether the defamed individual is a public figure or not.
*   **Defamed Privately**: Specify whether the defamation was private or public.
*   **Defamed By**: Provide details about who defamed the individual.
*   **Defamed At**: Indicate the time of the defamation, if known.
*   **Action Taken**: Specify any actions taken in response to the defamation.
*   **Defamed By Person**: Enter any relevant details about the person who defamed the individual.
*   **Defamed By Entity**: Enter any relevant details about the entity that defamed the individual.
*   **Defamation Source**: Enter any relevant details about the source of the defamation.
*   **Defamed By URL**: Enter any relevant details about the URL associated with the defamation.
*   **Defamed On**: Indicate the time of the defamation, if known.
*   **Defamed At 2**: Enter the date of the defamation incident, if known.
*   **Defamed At By**: Indicate the time of the defamation, if known.
*   **Defamed At By Entity**: Enter any relevant details about the entity that defamed the individual.
*   **Defamed By Person At**: Enter any relevant details about the person who defamed the individual.
*   **Defamed At URL**: Enter any relevant details about the URL associated with the defamation.
*   **Defamed On Time**: Indicate the time of the defamation, if known.
*   **Defamed By Entity At**: Enter any relevant details about the entity that defamed the individual.
*   **Defamed By Entity URL**: Enter any relevant details about the URL associated with the defamation.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and complete.
*   Please note that all fields are optional, but providing as much detail as possible will help us better understand the incident.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for clarification or guidance.
