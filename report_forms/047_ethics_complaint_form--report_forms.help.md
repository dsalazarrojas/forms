# Ethics Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ethics Complaint Form is used to report incidents or complaints related to ethics issues within the organization. This form helps to gather information and investigate potential concerns in a structured and systematic way, ensuring that all reported incidents are handled fairly and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Report Form** (`report_form`, text, optional): This is the main body of your complaint. Please provide a clear and concise description of the incident or concern you are reporting.
2. **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when you observed or experienced the incident or became aware of it.
3. **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, text, optional): Add any additional information about the incident, such as the location, circumstances, or people involved.
4. **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a more detailed explanation of the incident or concern.
5. **Reportee** (`reportee_name`, text, optional): If the incident involved another person, please specify their name.
6. **Reportee Contact Info** (`reportee_contact_info`, text, optional): If you know the reportee's contact information, please provide it.
7. **Reporter** (`reporter_name`, text, optional): Provide your name as the person reporting the incident or concern.
8. **Reporter Contact Info** (`reporter_contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact information, so we can get in touch with you about your complaint.
9. **Participants** (`incident_participants`, text, optional): If others were involved in the incident, list their names.

## Tips

* Please be as objective and factual as possible when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to contact us for clarification.
* All information provided will be handled confidentially and investigated promptly.
