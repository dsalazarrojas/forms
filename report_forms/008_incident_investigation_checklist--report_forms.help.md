# incident_investigation_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The incident investigation checklist is a tool to aid in the process of conducting a thorough investigation into an incident. It helps gather essential information to understand the incident's causes, impacts, and potential next steps.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Initial Steps" page and provide a brief description of the incident, including any relevant details.
2. On the "employee_info" page, fill in the employee's details, providing any relevant information about their involvement or role in the incident.
3. On the "incident_info" page, describe the incident in more detail, including dates, times, and locations.
4. On the "Investigation Plan" page, outline the steps you plan to take to further investigate the incident and identify potential root causes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Initial Steps
• **Initial Steps** (`Initial Steps`, `note`, required: false): This page is the starting point for the investigation. Provide a brief description of the incident, including any relevant details that may help in understanding the incident's context.

### Employee Information
• **employee_details** (`employee_details`, `text`, required: false): This field is for providing details about the employee involved in the incident. This could include their role, department, or any other relevant information that may aid in the investigation.

### Incident Details
• **incident_details** (`incident_details`, `text`, required: false): This field is where you can provide a detailed description of the incident. Include dates, times, locations, and any other relevant information that may help in understanding the incident.

### Investigation Plan
• **Investigation Plan** (`Investigation Plan`, `text`, required: false): This field is for outlining the steps you plan to take to further investigate the incident and identify potential root causes. Be sure to include any relevant tasks, deadlines, and responsibilities.

## Tips
* Be thorough and detailed in your responses to ensure a comprehensive investigation.
* Keep the investigation focused on the facts and avoid speculation.
* Review and update the form as needed throughout the investigation.
* Ensure that all necessary information is gathered before closing the investigation.
