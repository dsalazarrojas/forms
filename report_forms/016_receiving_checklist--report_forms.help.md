# Receiving Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Receiving Checklist is a form used to document and verify the receipt and condition of delivered vehicles. It's essential for accurate and efficient processing of vehicle deliveries.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form with the provided information about the vehicle delivery, following the guidelines below.
- Fill in the **Job Number** of the delivery. If this information is not available, you can skip this field.
- Indicate if the **Delivery Company** was present during the delivery. This is a critical piece of information to ensure accurate credit for the delivery company.
- Select whether or not the **vehicle_type** was delivered.
- If you have any remarks or comments about the vehicle condition, type them in the **remarks** field, but this is not a required field.
- Check the **Side Lights**, **Fog Lights** and **Main Beam** to confirm if they are functioning or not.
- Enter the **Date** of delivery.
- Provide the **Delivery Company** name and the person who **Delivered By**.
- Finally, enter the **Date Received** to keep track of when the vehicle was received.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Number** (1, number, required): The identification number of the delivery job.
* **delivery_company** (2, text, required): The name of the company that delivered the vehicle.
* **vehicle_type** (3, select_one, required): Indicate if the vehicle type was delivered. 
* **vehicle_check** (4, note, optional): Any remarks or comments about the vehicle condition. 
* **Side Lights** (5, select_multiple, optional): Confirm if the side lights are functioning. 
* **Fog Lights** (6, select_multiple, optional): Confirm if the fog lights are functioning. 
* **Main Beam** (7, select_multiple, optional): Confirm if the main beam is functioning.
* **Date** (8, date, required): The date of delivery.
* **Delivery Company** (9, text, required): The name of the delivery company that delivered the vehicle.
* **Date Received** (10, date, required): The date the vehicle was received.
* **Delivered By** (11, text, required): The person who delivered the vehicle.
* **Remarks** (12, note, optional): Any further remarks or comments about the delivery event.
