# Regional Manager Monthly Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Regional Manager Monthly Report Form is a tool to help Regional Managers track their sales performance and other activities within their region. This form helps to gather necessary information from Regional Managers to evaluate and improve sales targets, sales performance, and regional sales activities.

## How To Complete This Form

This form is divided into several sections, each requiring different information. Please follow the steps below to fill out the form:

1.  **Manager Name**: Enter your name as the Regional Manager.
2.  **Reporting Period**: Enter the period for which you are submitting this report (e.g., "January 2024").
3.  **Sales Target**: Enter your target sales for the reporting period.
4.  **Sales Actual**: Enter the actual sales figures for the reporting period.
5.  **Sales Difference**: Enter the difference between your sales target and actual sales figures.
6.  **Region**: Enter your region name.
7.  **Product A**: Enter the sales figure for Product A.
8.  **Product B**: Enter the sales figure for Product B.
9.  **Product C**: Enter the sales figure for Product C.
10.  **Other Products**: Enter the sales figure for other products.
11.  **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or remarks you have for this reporting period.
12.  **Date Submitted**: Enter the date when you submitted this report.
13.  **Time Submitted**: Enter the time when you submitted this report.
14.  **Email**: Enter your contact email address.
15.  **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
16.  **Sales Manager Name**: Enter the name of your Sales Manager.
17.  **Sales Manager Email**: Enter the email address of your Sales Manager.
18.  **Sales Manager Phone**: Enter the phone number of your Sales Manager.
19.  **Sales Manager Notes**: Enter any additional remarks or feedback from your Sales Manager.
20.  **Regional Manager Name**: Enter your regional manager's name.
21.  **Regional Manager Designee**: Enter the name of the regional manager to whom you report.
22.  **Regional Manager Designee Title**: Enter the title of the regional manager.
