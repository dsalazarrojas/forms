# Outpatient Volume Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Outpatient Volume Report Form is designed to collect data on patient visits, doctor interactions, and other relevant information for outpatient services. This form helps to track and analyze the volume of outpatient visits, allowing for better patient care and resource management.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the required fields:
	* Patient Name: Enter the name of the patient being visited.
	* Visit Date: Enter the date of the visit in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
	* Clinic Shift: Enter the shift during which the clinic was visited.
2. Fill in any additional required fields:
	* Doctor Name: Enter the name of the doctor who attended the visit.
	* Doctor Specialty: Enter the specialty of the doctor who attended the visit.
3. Optional fields can be filled in if available:
	* Department: Enter the department where the patient was visited.
	* Doctor 2: Enter the name of the doctor who assisted or consulted on the visit.
	* Doctor Spec: Enter any specializations of the doctor who attended the visit.
	* Patient ID: Enter the ID of the patient (if available).
	* Patient Phone: Enter the phone number of the patient.
	* Doctor Phone: Enter the phone number of the doctor.
	* Doctor Email: Enter the email of the doctor.
	* Doctor Address: Enter the address of the doctor.
	* Patient Address: Enter the address of the patient.
	* Reason for Visit: Enter a brief description of the reason for the visit.
	* Visit Type: Select the type of visit (e.g., new patient, follow-up, etc.).
	* Patient Status: Select the status of the patient (e.g., active, inactive, etc.).
	* Duration: Enter the duration of the visit.
	* Notes: Enter any additional comments or notes on the visit.
