# Online Learning Platform Effectiveness Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather user feedback and report on the effectiveness of our online learning platform. It helps us understand how well the platform is meeting user needs and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose one of the options for "User Feedback" based on your recent experience with the platform.
2. Select one outcome that best describes your experience with the platform.
3. If you have any suggestions for areas where the platform needs improvement, describe them in the "Improvement Needs" text field.
4. If you have thoughts on training needs, type them in the "Training Needs" text field.
5. Rate your level of satisfaction with the platform (scale: 0-100).
6. Report your time spent on the platform for the selected outcome (e.g., hours, days, weeks, etc.).
7. Enter the date of the report (this will be the date you're submitting this form).
8. Finally, click the submit button to send the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Feedback (Select Multiple):**
  * User Feedback: This field asks how you feel about your recent experience with the platform.
  * Very Satisfied: You are extremely satisfied with your experience.
  * Satisfied: You are somewhat satisfied with your experience.
  * Somewhat Satisfied: You are somewhat satisfied with your experience.
  * Very Dissatisfied: You are extremely dissatisfied with your experience.
  * Dissatisfied: You are somewhat dissatisfied with your experience.
  * Not Satisfied: You are not satisfied with your experience.
* **User Outcome (Select One):**
  * User Outcome: This field asks how your experience with the platform met your expectations.
  * Improved: Your experience with the platform has positively impacted your knowledge or skills.
  * Not Improved: Your experience with the platform did not positively impact your knowledge or skills.
  * Unchanged: Your experience with the platform did not change your situation.
* **Improvement Needs (Text Field):**
  * Improvement Needs: If you have thoughts on where the platform could be improved, type them here.
* **Training Needs (Text Field):**
  * Training Needs: If you have thoughts on how the platform could be improved for future users, type them here.
* **User Satisfaction (Number Field):**
  * User Satisfaction: Rate your level of satisfaction with the platform on a scale of 0-100.
* **User Outcome Outcomes (Time Field):**
  * User Outcome Outcomes: Report the time you spent on the platform for the selected outcome.
* **Training Outcome Outcomes (Time Field):**
  * Training Outcome Outcomes: Report the time you spent on the platform for the training you received.
* **Report Date (Date Field):**
  * Report Date: Enter the date when you're submitting this report.
