# Receipt Reporting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Receipt Reporting Form is designed for employees, customers, or clients to report and record receipt information, including date, merchant, purchase amount, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Receipt Date` field with the date of the receipt.
2. Enter the `Merchant Name` of the establishment where the receipt was received.
3. Enter the `Purchase Amount` of the transaction.
4. If applicable, select the `Receipt Type` (e.g., credit card, reimbursement).
5. Add any additional information about the receipt in the `Description` field.
6. Enter the `Submitted By` details, including name, email, phone number, department, title, and date.
7. Enter the `Report To` details, including email, phone number, department, and title.
8. Add any attachments related to the receipt.
9. Finally, click the submit button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Receipt Date (1)** (`receipt_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the receipt.
* **Merchant Name (2)** (`merchant_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the merchant or establishment where the receipt was received.
* **Purchase Amount (3)** (`purchase_amount`, number, optional): Enter the total purchase amount.
* **Receipt Number (4)** (`receipt_number`, text, optional): Enter the receipt number or any other identifying number for the receipt.
* **Description (5)** (`description`, text, optional): Add any additional information about the receipt.
* **Receipt Type (6)** (`receipt_type`, select multiple, optional): Select the type of receipt, such as credit card or reimbursement.
* **Submitted By (7)** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter your name, email, phone number, department, or title.
* **Submitted On (8)** (`submitted_on`, date, optional): Enter the date the receipt was submitted.
* **Submitted To (9)** (`submitted_to`, text, optional): Enter the name or email of the person or department the receipt was submitted to.
* **Submitted Note (10)** (`submitted_note`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about the submission.
* **Report To (11)** (`report_to`, text, optional): Enter the name or email of the person or department to report to.
* **Report Date (12)** (`report_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the receipt was reported.
* **Report Note (13)** (`report_note`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about the report.
* **Category (14)** (`category`, text, optional): Enter any category or tag for the receipt.
* **Tags (15)** (`tags`, text, optional): Enter any relevant tags or keywords for the receipt.
* **Attachments (16)** (`attachments`, text, optional): Attach any relevant files or documents related to the receipt.
* **Submitted By Email (17)** (`submitted_by_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Submitted By Phone (18)** (`submitted_by_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Submitted By Department (19)** (`submitted_by_department`, text, optional): Enter your department or team.
* **Submitted By Title (20)** (`submitted_by_title`, text, optional): Enter your title or position.
* **Report To Email (21)** (`report_to_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person or department to report to.
* **Report To Phone (22)** (`report_to_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person or department to report to.
* **Report To Department (23)** (`report_to_department`, text, optional): Enter the department to report to.
* **Report To Title (24)** (`report_to_title`, text, optional): Enter the title or position of the person or department to report to.
* **Submitted By Date (25)** (`submitted_by_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the receipt was submitted.

* Attach relevant files or documents when available.
