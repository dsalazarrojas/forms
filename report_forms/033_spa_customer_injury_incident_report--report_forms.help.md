# Spa Customer Injury Incident Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document incidents that may have occurred at a spa, such as injuries or illnesses that affected customers or employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields accurately and completely.
2. Provide as much detail as possible about the incident.
3. Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and approvals before filling out the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false)
 Enter the name of the customer involved in the incident.

### 2. **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, required: false)
 Describe the location where the incident occurred.

### 3. **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required: false)
 Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.

### 4. **Injury Date** (`injury_date`, date, required: false)
 Enter the date of the incident.

### 5. **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, required: false)
 Enter the time of the incident.

### 6. **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required: false)
 Enter the email address of the customer involved in the incident.

### 7. **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required: false)
 If an employee was involved in the incident, enter their name.

### 8. **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required: false)
 If an employee supervisor was involved in the incident, enter their name.

### 9. **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_one, required: false)
 Choose the type of incident that occurred:
  - Injury
  - Illness
  - Other

### 10. **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, text, required: false)
 Provide additional information about the incident.

### 11. **Follow-up Plan** (`follow_up_plan`, text, required: false)
 Describe the follow-up actions that will be taken to address the incident.

### 12. **Internal Review** (`internal_review`, text, required: false)
 Enter any internal review or investigation information.

### 13. **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, required: false)
 Enter the date when the incident was reviewed.

### 14. **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, select_multiple, required: false)
 Choose the status of the incident:
  - Active
  - Closed
  - On Hold

### 15. **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, note, required: false)
 Enter your employee signature if you are responsible for filling out this form.

### 16. **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, note, required: false)
 Enter the customer's signature if you are able to obtain it.

### 17. **Employee Name Signature** (`employee_name_signature`, note, required: false)
 Enter the employee's name and signature if you are signing as an employee.

### 18. **Customer Name Signature** (`customer_name_signature`, note, required: false)
 Enter the customer's name and signature if you are able to obtain it.

### 19. **Employee Signature Date** (`employee_signature_date`, date, required: false)
 Enter the date of the employee's signature.

### 20. **Customer Signature Date** (`customer_signature_date`, date, required: false)
 Enter the date of the customer's signature.

### 21. **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, note, required: false)
 Enter the manager's signature if they are approving the incident report.

### 22. **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required: false)
 Enter the manager's name.

### 23. **Employee Supervisor Name** (`employee_supervisor_name`, text, required: false)
 Enter the employee supervisor's name.

### 24. **Incident Reported by** (`incident_reported_by`, text, required: false)
 Enter the person who reported the incident.

### 25. **Review Status** (`review_status`, text, required: false)
 Enter any review status information related to the incident.

## Tips
* If you are unsure about any information or field, consult with your supervisor or a designated personnel for guidance.
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and approvals before filling out this form.
* Fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a thorough understanding of the incident.
* Review and follow up on this form regularly to ensure that the incident status is up-to-date.
