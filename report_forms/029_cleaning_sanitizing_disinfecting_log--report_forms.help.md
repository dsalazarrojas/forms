# Cleaning Sanitizing Disinfecting Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Cleaning Sanitizing Disinfecting Log" form is designed to document cleaning, sanitizing, and disinfecting activities in facilities. It helps track the process details, such as facility information, log date and times, cleaning staff, and areas sanitized.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the "Facility Information" section:
    * Enter the "Facility Name" to identify the location being serviced.
    * Enter the "Facility Location/Address" or a unique identifier for the location.
    * Provide the "Facility Manager" name or contact person.
2.  Move on to the "Log Information" section:
    * Record the "Date of Log" for the sanitizing work performed.
    * Enter the "Start Time" when the sanitizing began.
    * Enter the "End Time" when the sanitizing ended.
    * List the "Cleaning Staff Name(s)" who performed the work.
3.  If a supervisor was present, enter their name in the "Supervisor Name" field.
4.  In the "Products Used" section:
    * If applicable, select the primary "Disinfectant Type" used.
    * Enter the "Disinfectant Concentration" if applicable.
    * Enter the "Contact Time" the disinfectant was left on surfaces if applicable.
    * If applicable, enter the "Sanitizer Type" used.
    * List any "Additional Chemicals or Products" used.
5.  In the "Areas Sanitized" section:
    * Select the relevant areas sanitized from the provided options (e.g., "Entryways and Doorways," "Bathrooms," "Offices and Workstations," etc.).
6.  In the "Specific Surfaces Treated" section:
    * Enter detailed items (e.g., "light switches, keyboards, phones") that were sanitized.
7.  In the "Quality and Verification" section:
    * Select the "Verification Method Used" to verify the effectiveness of the process (e.g., "Visual Inspection," "ATP Testing," etc.).
    * Enter the "Verification Results" of the quality control process.
8.  Enter any "Issues or Concerns During Sanitizing" encountered and provide "Additional Comments or Notes" as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the facility being serviced.
* **Facility Location/Address**: Enter the address or a unique identifier for the location.
* **Facility Manager**: Enter the name of the manager or contact person.
* **Date of Log**: Record the date the sanitizing work was performed.
* **Start Time**: Enter the time sanitizing began.
* **End Time**: Enter the time sanitizing ended.
* **Cleaning Staff Name(s)**: List the names of personnel performing the work.
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of the supervising personnel.
* **Primary Disinfectant Used**: Select the primary disinfectant used.
* **Disinfectant Concentration**: Enter the dilution ratio or concentration level of the disinfectant.
* **Contact Time Applied**: Enter the duration the disinfectant was left on surfaces.
* **Sanitizer Type**: Enter the type of sanitizer used.
* **Additional Chemicals or Products**: List any other products used.
* **Entryways and Doorways**: Select the relevant area sanitized.
* **Bathrooms**: Select the relevant area sanitized.
* **Break/Rest Rooms**: Select the relevant area sanitized.
* **Offices and Workstations**: Select the relevant area sanitized.
* **Conference and Meeting Rooms**: Select the relevant area sanitized.
* **Hallways and Common Areas**: Select the relevant area sanitized.
* **High-Touch Surfaces**: Select the relevant area sanitized.
* **Specific Surfaces or Items Treated**: Enter detailed items that were sanitized.
* **Verification Method Used**: Select the method used to verify the effectiveness of the process.
* **Verification Results**: Enter the results of the quality control process.
* **Issues or Concerns During Sanitizing**: Enter any encountered problems.
* **Additional Comments or Notes**: Enter any other relevant information.
