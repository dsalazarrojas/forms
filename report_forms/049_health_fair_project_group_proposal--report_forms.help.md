# Health Fair Project Group Proposal - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Fair Project Group Proposal form is designed to collect feedback and information about health-related projects from various groups. This form is part of a larger process for evaluating and improving projects within the health fair context. Please ensure that your submission is accurate and relevant to your group's project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring that all required fields are completed.
*   Enter your project's title and details in the designated fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Project Title** (`project_title`, text, required: false): Enter a brief title for your project.
*   **Project Group** (`project_group`, text, required: false): Identify the group associated with your project.
*   **Feedback Type** (`feedback_type`, select_one, required: false):
    +   Select the type of feedback you are providing for your project: Excellent, Fair, or Poor.
*   **Feedback Details** (`feedback_details`, text, required: false): Provide any additional details regarding your feedback.
*   **Category** (`category`, text, required: false): Enter the category of your project, if applicable.
*   **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, required: false): The date you are submitting this form.
*   **Project Status** (`project_status`, text, required: false): Briefly describe the current status of your project.
*   **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, required: false): Summarize your project's purpose and objectives.
*   **Project Outcomes** (`project_outcomes`, text, required: false): List the expected outcomes of your project.

## Tips

*   Double-check your entries for accuracy and relevance before submitting.
*   Ensure that your feedback is clear and concise.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please consult with your project group or the health fair team for clarification.
