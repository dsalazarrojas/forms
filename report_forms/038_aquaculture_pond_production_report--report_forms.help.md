# Aquaculture Pond Production Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Aquaculture Pond Production Report is a form used to collect information about the production of aquaculture ponds, including facility details, production data, and water quality parameters. This report helps to track and monitor the health and productivity of aquaculture ponds, ensuring that they are operating within optimal conditions for healthy fish growth and production.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the facility where the pond is located.
	* **Pond ID**: Enter the unique ID of the pond.
	* **Reporting Period**: Enter the date range for which you are reporting production data (e.g., "Jan 1 - Dec 31").
	* **Report Date**: Enter the date this report is submitted.
	* **Stock Quantity**: Enter the current stock quantity of fish in the pond.
	* **Water Temperature (Fahrenheit)**: Enter the current water temperature of the pond.
	* **Water pH Level**: Enter the current pH level of the pond.
	* **Dissolved Oxygen (mg/L)**: Enter the current dissolved oxygen level of the pond.
2. Provide optional fields as necessary:
	* **Average Fish Weight (pounds)**: If available, enter the average weight of fish harvested during this reporting period.
	* **Mortality Rate (percent)**: If available, enter the mortality rate (percent) of fish during this reporting period.
	* **Feed Amount Used (pounds)**: If applicable, enter the total amount of feed used during this reporting period.
	* **Any Health or Disease Issues**: If any health or disease issues arise, describe them in this field.
	* **Production Data**: Add any additional production data or notes about the pond's production.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of the facility where the pond is located.
* **Pond ID** (`pond_id`, `text`, `required`): Enter the unique ID of the pond.
* **Reporting Period** (`reporting_period`, `text`, `required`): Enter the date range for which you are reporting production data (e.g., "Jan 1 - Dec 31").
* **Report Date** (`report_date`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date this report is submitted.
* **Stock Quantity** (`stock_quantity`, `number`, `required`): Enter the current stock quantity of fish in the pond.
* **Average Fish Weight (pounds)** (`average_weight`, `number`, `optional`): If available, enter the average weight of fish harvested during this reporting period.
* **Mortality Rate (percent)** (`mortality_rate`, `number`, `optional`): If available, enter the mortality rate (percent) of fish during this reporting period.
* **Water Temperature (Fahrenheit)** (`water_temperature`, `number`, `required`): Enter the current water temperature of the pond.
* **pH Level** (`ph_level`, `number`, `optional`): Enter the current pH level of the pond.
* **Dissolved Oxygen (mg/L)** (`dissolved_oxygen`, `number`, `optional`): Enter the current dissolved oxygen level of the pond.
* **Feed Amount Used (pounds)** (`feed_amount`, `number`, `optional`): If applicable, enter the total amount of feed used during this reporting period.
* **Any Health or Disease Issues** (`health_issues`, `text`, `optional`): If any health or disease issues arise, describe them in this field.
* **Production Data** (`production_data`, `note`, `optional`): Add any additional production data or notes about the pond's production.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
- If unsure about any field, consult with your facility's aquaculture expert or refer to your facility's best practices manual.
- Report any health or disease issues promptly to prevent further harm to the fish and facility.
