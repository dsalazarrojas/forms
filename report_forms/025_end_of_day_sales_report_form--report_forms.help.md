# End Of Day Sales Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather end-of-day sales data and performance information from sales representatives, providing a daily summary of sales activities and allowing for analysis and tracking of progress.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form title to identify the specific sales report being submitted.
2. Select the relevant category for this sales report.
3. Provide a brief description of the sales activities and performance for this report.
4. Enter the category name related to this sales report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter a brief title for this sales report, e.g., "Sales Report for XYZ Product Line".
* **Category** (`form_category`, text, required): Select the category this sales report is related to, e.g., "ABC Product Line", "DEF Product Line", etc.
* **Description** (`form_description`, text, required): Describe the sales activities and performance for this report, e.g., "Sold 10 units of Product A and 20 units of Product B".
* **Category Name** (`form_category_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the category this sales report is related to, e.g., "ABC Product Line".
