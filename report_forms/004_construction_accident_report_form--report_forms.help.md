# Construction Accident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and report construction accidents, ensuring that necessary information is collected for incident investigation, risk assessment, and compliance with regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Provide the report date** (`Report Date`):
	* Enter the date this report is being filed.
2. **Provide the report time** (`Report Time`):
	* Enter the time this report is being filed.
3. **Specify the incident date** (`Date of Incident`):
	* Enter the date the accident occurred.
4. **Specify the incident time** (`Time of Incident`):
	* Enter the approximate time the accident occurred.
5. **Location of the incident** (`Location of Incident`):
	* Enter the specific location on the construction site where the incident occurred.
6. **Project name** (`Project Name`):
	* Enter the name of the construction project.
7. **Injured person's name** (`Injured Person Name`):
	* Enter the full name of the injured party.
8. **Injured person's role** (`Injured Person Role`):
	* Select the job title or position of the injured person from the list.
9. **Type of injury** (`Type of Injury`):
	* Select all types of injury that apply to the incident.
10. **Injury severity** (`Injury Severity`):
	* Select the level of severity of the injury from the list.
11. **Body parts affected** (`Body Parts Affected`):
	* Select all body parts affected by the incident.
12. **Detailed accident description** (`Accident Description`):
	* Provide a detailed description of what happened.
13. **Witnesses present** (`Were There Witnesses`):
	* Indicate if anyone saw the accident.
14. **Witness names** (`Witness Names`):
	* If applicable, list the names and contact information of witnesses.
15. **Equipment involved** (`Equipment or Tools Involved`):
	* If applicable, describe any equipment involved in the incident.
16. **Weather conditions** (`Weather Conditions`):
	* Select the weather conditions at the time of the incident.
17. **Safety equipment used** (`Safety Equipment in Use`):
	* Select any safety equipment being worn by the injured person.
18. **Immediate action taken** (`Immediate Action Taken`):
	* Describe any immediate actions taken after the incident.
19. **Medical treatment** (`Medical Treatment Provided`):
	* Describe any medical treatment given to the injured person.
20. **Reported to supervisor** (`Reported to Supervisor`):
	* Indicate if the supervisor was notified.
21. **Photos available** (`Photos Available`):
	* Indicate if photos of the incident are available.
22. **Reporter's name** (`Reporter Name`):
	* Enter the name of the person filing this report.
23. **Reporter's signature** (`Reporter Signature`):
	* Sign this report with your full name.
