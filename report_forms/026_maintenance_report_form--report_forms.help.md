# Maintenance Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Maintenance Report Form is used to collect and record information about maintenance activities within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and title to identify yourself as the form owner.
2. Enter your name as the owner of the maintenance activity.
3. Select the relevant organization that this report is for.
4. If applicable, select the designation related to the maintenance activity.
5. Enter a brief description of the maintenance activity.
6. Select the equipment used for the maintenance activity.
7. Provide a detailed account of the work done during the maintenance activity.
8. Add your signature to confirm that you've completed the form.
9. Enter the date and time of the maintenance activity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Owner**: Enter your name and title to identify yourself as the form owner. (Optional)
* **Owner Name**: Enter your name as the owner of the maintenance activity. (Optional)
* **Organization**: Select the relevant organization for which this report is being submitted. (Required)
* **Designation**: If applicable, select the designation related to the maintenance activity. (Optional)
* **Location**: Enter the location where the maintenance activity took place. (Optional)
* **Purpose**: Enter a brief description of the maintenance activity. (Optional)
* **Description**: Enter a detailed account of the work done during the maintenance activity. (Optional)
* **Equipment**: Select all the equipment used for the maintenance activity. (Required)
* **Work Details**: Enter a detailed account of the work done during the maintenance activity. (Optional)
* **Signature**: Add your signature to confirm that you've completed the form. (Optional)
* **Date**: Enter the date of the maintenance activity. (Optional)
* **Time**: Enter the time of the maintenance activity. (Optional)
