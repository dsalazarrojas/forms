# Quarterly Financial Performance Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to report and track the financial performance of a quarter. It helps users to provide information about the current financial status, such as sales revenue, cost of goods sold, and operating income, as well as other important details like comments and comments. The form is meant to be completed by users to provide accurate and up-to-date financial information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the quarter for which you are reporting financial performance (Q1, Q2, Q3, or Q4).
2. Enter the year for which you are reporting financial performance.
3. Choose the current financial status (On Track, At Risk, or Not On Track).
4. Enter sales revenue, cost of goods sold, and operating income for the selected quarter.
5. Add any additional comments or feedback in the comments field.
6. Select the person who will be assigned to review and follow up on this report.
7. Enter the email of the person who submitted this report.
8. Enter the phone number of the person who submitted this report.
9. Select the person who submitted this report.
10. Select the date when the report was submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Quarter** (`quarter`, select_one, required): Select the quarter for which you are reporting financial performance (Q1, Q2, Q3, or Q4).
* **Year** (`year`, number, required): Enter the year for which you are reporting financial performance.
* **Financial Status** (`financial_status`, select_one, required): Choose the current financial status (On Track, At Risk, or Not On Track).
* **Sales Revenue** (`sales_revenue`, number, required): Enter the sales revenue for the selected quarter.
* **Cost of Goods Sold** (`cost_of_goods_sold`, number, required): Enter the cost of goods sold for the selected quarter.
* **Operating Income** (`operating_income`, number, required): Enter the operating income for the selected quarter.
* **Net Income** (`net_income`, number, required): Enter the net income for the selected quarter.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required): Add any additional comments or feedback for the selected quarter.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required): Select the person who will be assigned to review and follow up on this report.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email of the person who submitted this report.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the person who submitted this report.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required): Select the person who submitted this report.
* **Submitted** (`submitted`, date, required): Select the date when the report was submitted.
