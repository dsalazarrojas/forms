# Secondary School Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Secondary School Report Form is a tool used to gather information from teachers about individual students in secondary school. It allows teachers to provide a quick reference for parent-teacher conferences and to track student performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Choose "Yes" or "No" for the student's subject status.
3. Enter the student's grade level.
4. Select "Yes" or "No" for the student's attendance record.
5. Choose the student's conduct level.
6. Enter comments from the teacher or parent in the designated sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: The student's name is a required field that should be completed with the student's full name.
* **Subject**: A select menu allowing you to choose "Yes" or "No" for the student's subject status.
* **student_grade**: A numeric field to enter the student's grade level, usually between 0 and 9.
* **student_attendance**: A select menu allowing you to choose "Yes" or "No" for the student's attendance record.
* **student_conduct**: A select menu allowing you to choose "Yes" or "No" for the student's conduct level.
* **teacher_comment**: A free-text field for the teacher to provide any necessary comments or observations.
* **parent_comment**: A free-text field for the parent to provide any necessary comments or observations.

## Tips
- Be as specific and accurate as possible when providing information.
- Only fill out fields that are applicable to the student's situation.
- Use the provided options for select menus to make selection easier.
- Remember to save your progress after filling out the form to avoid losing your work.
