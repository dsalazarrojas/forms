# Report Abuse - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to report abusive or problematic content. To submit a report, please follow the steps below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out all the required fields. If you are reporting abuse or a problem, provide a detailed explanation in the "Reason" field. For other reports, select the "Other" option in the "Report Type" field. You can submit your report at any time, and it will be reviewed and processed accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`User Input`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional details or thoughts about the abuse.
* **Name** (`Name`, text, optional): Please enter your name or the name of the person affected by the abuse.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): If you want to be contacted about this report, please enter your email address.
* **Reason** (`Reason`, text, optional): Provide a detailed explanation of the abuse or problem.
* **Page Number** (`Page Number`, number, optional): If the abuse was found on a specific page, please enter the page number.
* **Report Type** (`Report Type`, select-one, optional): Choose between "Report Type" and "Other" to categorize your report.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information about the report.
* **Report Form ID** (`Report Form ID`, number, optional): This field is not intended for manual entry and will be filled in automatically.
* **Created At** (`Created At`, date, read-only): This field displays the timestamp when the report was created.
* **Updated At** (`Updated At`, date, read-only): This field displays the last updated timestamp of the report.
* **Submitted At** (`Submitted At`, date, read-only): This field displays the timestamp when the report was submitted.
* **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`, select-one, optional): Choose between "System" and "User" to indicate who submitted the report.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have a valid reason for submitting a report before filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about how to complete this form, you can contact our support team for assistance.
* Reports submitted through this form will be reviewed and processed according to our abuse reporting policies.
