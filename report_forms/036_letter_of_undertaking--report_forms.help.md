# Letter Of Undertaking - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers seeking a loan, including contact and financial details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Name** in the corresponding field.
2. Provide your **Contact Number** for us to reach out to you regarding your loan application.
3. Enter your **Address** for us to verify your location.
4. Enter your **Email** address for us to communicate with you about your loan application.
5. In the **Purpose of Loan** field, describe the reason you are applying for a loan.
6. Select your **Employment Status** from the options provided.
7. Enter your **Annual Income** to provide your financial details.
8. Enter the number of **Employment Years** you have with your current employer.
9. Provide a brief description of your **Employment History** in the space provided.
10. Enter your **Credit Score** to provide more financial information.
11. Enter the **Loan Amount** you are seeking.
12. Enter the **Loan Term** in months.
13. If you are providing **Collateral** for the loan, provide a brief description of it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number or other contact information we can use to reach you.
* **Address**: Enter your physical address where we can send any important documents or information.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for us to communicate with you about your loan application.
* **Purpose of Loan**: Describe the reason you are applying for a loan.
* **Employment Status**: Select your current employment status from the options provided.
* **Annual Income**: Enter your annual income to provide more financial information.
* **Employment Years**: Enter the number of years you have been employed with your current employer.
* **Employment History**: Provide a brief description of your employment history.
* **Credit Score**: Enter your credit score to provide more financial information.
* **Loan Amount**: Enter the loan amount you are seeking.
* **Loan Term**: Enter the loan term in months.
* **Collateral**: If you are providing collateral, provide a brief description of it.
* **Address**: Enter your home or business address where we can send any important documents or information.
* **Since**: Not applicable for this form.
* **Loan Amount**: Enter the loan amount you are applying for.
* **Annual Income**: Enter your annual income for the past 12 months.
* **Employment Status**: Select your current employment status.
* **Loan Term**: Enter the loan term in months.
* **Credit Score**: Enter your credit score. 

Note: The fields marked as "Not Applicable" will not be used in the loan application process.
