# Dental Excuse Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dental Excuse Form is used to document a student's absence from school due to a visit to the dentist. This form is used to provide a valid reason for the student's absence and to obtain a doctor's note to support the excuse.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Student Name field with the student's name as it appears on their school records.
2. Enter the Student Email address associated with the student's account.
3. Enter the Date of Absence, which is the date of the student's visit to the dentist.
4. Enter the Start Time and End Time of the student's visit.
5. Select the Reason for Absence from the available options or enter a custom reason.
6. Enter the name of the Dental Provider (dental clinic or office) that the student visited.
7. Enter the student's Phone number for contact purposes.
8. Enter the Doctor's Name and Doctor's License number from the visit.
9. Select the Student Grade from the available options (3rd, 4th, 5th, 6th, 7th, 8th).
10. Select the Student Subjects that the student is absent from (English, Math, Science, History, PE).
11. Enter the Doctor's Phone number for contact purposes.
12. Enter the Doctor's Office address where the visit took place.
13. Enter the Doctor's Note, which should include a brief explanation of the student's condition.
14. Enter your own signature as the student.
15. Enter the Doctor's Signature to confirm the visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name (1)**: The student's name as it appears on their school records.
* **Student Email (2)**: The student's email address associated with their account.
* **Date of Absence (3)**: The date of the student's visit to the dentist.
* **Start Time (4)**: The start time of the student's visit.
* **End Time (5)**: The end time of the student's visit.
* **Reason for Absence (6)**: Select or enter a valid reason for the student's absence.
* **Dental Provider (7)**: The name of the dental clinic or office visited.
* **Student Phone (8)**: The student's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Doctor's Name (9)**: The name of the doctor who provided the note.
* **Doctor's License (10)**: The doctor's license number (A, B, or C).
* **Student Grade (11)**: Select the student's grade level (3rd, 4th, 5th, 6th, 7th, 8th).
* **Student Subjects (12)**: Select all subjects that the student is absent from (English, Math, Science, History, PE).
* **Doctor's Name (13)**: The name of the doctor who provided the note.
* **Doctor's License (14)**: The doctor's license number (A, B, or C).
* **Doctor's Phone (15)**: The doctor's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Doctor's Office (16)**: The address of the doctor's office.
* **Doctor's Note (17)**: A brief explanation of the student's condition by the doctor.
* **Student Signature (18)**: Enter your own signature as the student.
* **Doctor's Signature (19)**: Enter the doctor's signature to confirm the visit.
