# Vehicle Tracking Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and report vehicle-related data. It is designed to collect information about each vehicle's trip, including trip dates, destinations, driver details, and total costs associated with the trip. Fill out this form to help us keep accurate and up-to-date records of our vehicle usage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the trip date using the calendar picker.
2. Enter the destination(s) in the text field.
3. Fill in the driver information in the text field.
4. Enter the total cost of the trip in the number field.
5. If you have a vehicle number, enter it in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trip Date** (date): Select the date of the trip.
* **Destination** (text): Enter the destination of the trip.
* **Driver Info** (text): Enter the details of the driver, such as their name or ID.
* **Total Cost** (number): Enter the total cost of the trip.
* **Vehicle Number** (text): Enter the vehicle number, if applicable.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate reporting.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor for clarification.
