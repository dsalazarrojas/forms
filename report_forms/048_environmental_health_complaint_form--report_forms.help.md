# Environmental Health Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about environmental health complaints, allowing users to report issues related to air, water, land, or noise.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your complaint details.
2. Select the type of complaint you are reporting (Environmental Concerns, Complaint, or Concerns or Complaints).
3. Provide a brief description of your complaint in the complaint_details field.
4. Indicate the type of environmental issue affected (Air, Water, Land, or Noise).
5. Enter the location where the complaint occurred.
6. Enter your name and email address for contact purposes.
7. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
8. Provide any additional information about the complaint in the additional_information field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your complaint.
* **Complaint Type** (`complaint_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of complaint you are reporting (Environmental Concerns, Complaint, or Concerns or Complaints).
* **Complaint Details** (`complaint_details`, note, optional): Provide a brief description of your complaint.
* **Environmental Issue** (`environmental_issue`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more environmental issues affected (Air, Water, Land, or Noise).
* **Complaint Location** (`complaint_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the complaint occurred.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter your name for contact purposes.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, note, optional): Provide any additional information about the complaint.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when filling out the form to help us understand your complaint better.
* You can provide attachments or supporting documents if you have any.
* Our team will review your complaint and get back to you as soon as possible.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
