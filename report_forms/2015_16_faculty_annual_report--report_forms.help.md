# 2015 16 Faculty Annual Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from faculty members about their activities, achievements, and experiences during the 2015-16 academic year. It is intended to help faculty reflect on their work and provide insights for departmental and institutional decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details:
	* Full name (required)
	* Faculty ID (required)
	* Email address (required)
2. Report your academic activities:
	* Courses taught this year (list course codes separated by commas)
	* Total credit hours taught
	* Number of students supervised
	* Average grade of supervised students (e.g., A-, B+)
3. Update your research output:
	* Number of peer-reviewed publications
	* Number of conference papers presented
	* Total grant amount awarded (USD)
	* Ongoing research projects (briefly describe each project)
4. Share your service and outreach contributions:
	* Committees served on (list committee names)
	* Community outreach activities (provide a short summary)
5. Reflect on your professional development:
	* Workshops or training attended (list titles and dates)
	* Professional certifications earned (include awarding body)
6. Provide feedback on your experience:
	* How satisfied are you with the 2015-16 Faculty Annual Report process?
	* Overall rating (1-10)
	* Which aspects applied to your experience with the report? (select all)
	* What would you improve or change about the Faculty Annual Report? (provide specific suggestions)
	* Additional comments or notes

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your official university records.
* **Faculty ID** (`faculty_id`, `text`, required): Provide your unique faculty ID number.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your official university email address.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, `phone`, optional): Include your country code and phone number.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required): Enter your department name (e.g., Computer Science, History).
* **Position** (`position`, `select_one`, required): Select your current position (e.g., Assistant Professor, Senior Lecturer).
* **Courses taught this year** (`courses_taught`, `text`, required): List course codes separated by commas.
* **Total credit hours taught** (`credit_hours`, `integer`, required): Enter the total credit hours taught this year.
* **Number of students supervised** (`student_supervision`, `integer`, required): Include PhD, Masters, and undergraduate students.
* **Average grade of supervised students** (`average_student_grade`, `text`, optional): Enter the average grade of supervised students (e.g., A-, B+).
* **Number of peer-reviewed publications** (`publications`, `integer`, required): Count only journal articles.
* **Number of conference papers presented** (`conference_papers`, `integer`, optional): Count only oral presentations.
* **Total grant amount awarded (USD)** (`grants_awarded`, `decimal`, optional): Sum of all grant money received.
* **Ongoing research projects** (`research_projects`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe each project.
* **Committees served on** (`committee_memberships`, `text`, optional): List committee names.
* **Community outreach activities** (`community_outreach`, `text`, optional): Provide a short summary.
* **Workshops or training attended** (`workshops_attended`, `text`, optional): List titles and dates.
* **Professional certifications earned** (`certifications`, `text`, optional): Include awarding body.
* **How satisfied are you with the 2015-16 Faculty Annual Report process?** (`satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Select your level of satisfaction (e.g., Very satisfied, Dissatisfied).
* **Overall rating (1-10)** (`overall_rating`, `integer`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction (1 = worst, 10 = best).
* **Which aspects applied to your experience with the report?** (`experience_aspects`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all aspects that applied to your experience.
* **What would you improve or change about the Faculty Annual Report?** (`open_feedback`, `text`, optional): Provide specific suggestions.
* **Additional comments or notes** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Any other remarks.
