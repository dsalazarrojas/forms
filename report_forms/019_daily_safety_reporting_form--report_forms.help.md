# daily-safety-reporting-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect daily safety reporting information to maintain a centralized record of incidents, facilitating incident reporting, investigation, and closure.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2.  Provide as much detail as possible for optional fields.
3.  Ensure that the date and time fields are formatted correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Incident Report** (`incident_report`, text, required): Briefly describe the incident, including what happened and any injuries or near misses.
*   **Date of Incident** (`date_of_incident`, date, required): Enter the date the incident occurred in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
*   **Location of Incident** (`location_of_incident`, text, required): Specify the location where the incident occurred.
*   **Description of Incident** (`description_of_incident`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
*   **Cause of Incident** (`cause_of_incident`, text, required): Identify the reason or root cause of the incident.
*   **Corrective Action Planned** (`corrective_action_planned`, text, optional): Describe the corrective action planned to prevent similar incidents in the future.
*   **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, select_one, required): Choose the status of the incident (Open, Closed, Pending).
*   **Reporting Person** (`reporting_person`, text, optional): Specify the person reporting the incident.
*   **Reporting Date** (`reporting_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the incident was reported in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
*   **Reporting Time** (`reporting_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the incident was reported in the format HH:MM:SS.
*   **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of incident (Hazard, Near Miss, Incident).
*   **Incident Severity** (`incident_severity`, select_one, optional): Choose the severity of the incident (Low, Medium, High).
*   **Incident Criticality** (`incident_criticality`, select_one, optional): Choose the criticality of the incident (Low, Medium, High).
*   **Incident Investigation Status** (`incident_investigation_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the status of the investigation (Underway, Completed, Not Underway).
*   **Incident Cause Verified** (`incident_cause_verified`, select_one, optional): Choose whether the incident cause has been verified (True, False).
*   **Incident Action Planned** (`incident_action_planned`, select_one, optional): Choose whether a corrective action has been planned (True, False).
*   **Incident Review Status** (`incident_review_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the review status (Reviewed, Not Reviewed).
*   **Incident Status Updated By** (`incident_status_updated_by`, text, optional): Specify who updated the incident status.
*   **Incident Corrective Action Implementation Status** (`incident_corrective_action_implementation_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the implementation status (Implemented, Not Implemented).
*   **Incident Corrective Action Complete Status** (`incident_corrective_action_complete_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the completion status (Complete, Not Complete).
*   **Incident Criticality Assessed By** (`incident_criticality_assessed_by`, text, optional): Specify who assessed the incident criticality.
*   **Incident Reported By** (`incident_reported_by`, text, optional): Specify who reported the incident.
*   **Incident Date Closed** (`incident_date_closed`, date, optional): Enter the date the incident was closed in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
*   **Incident Time Closed** (`incident_time_closed`, time, optional): Enter the time the incident was closed in the format HH:MM:SS.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional information or comments about the incident.

## Tips

*   Ensure that all required fields are filled out completely and accurately.
*   If unsure about any field, please consult with your supervisor or safety officer.
*   Use the correct date and time formats when filling out the date and time fields.
*   Provide as much detail as possible for optional fields to help with investigation and closure.
