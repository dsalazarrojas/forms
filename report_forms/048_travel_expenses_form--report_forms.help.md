# Travel Expenses Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report travel expenses for accounting and reporting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your travel dates** (<id: 1, label: Travel Dates, required: false>): Enter the dates of your trip in the format `mm/dd/yyyy - mm/dd/yyyy`.
2. **Select your trip purpose** (<id: 2, label: Trip Purpose, required: false>): Choose the reason for your trip from the list:
	* Business
	* Personal
	* Other (Please specify)
3. **Enter your destination** (<id: 3, label: Destination, required: false>): Type in the location you traveled to.
4. **Select your expense type(s)** (<id: 4, label: Expense Type, required: false>): Choose the type(s) of expenses you are submitting, from the list:
	* Accommodation
	* Food
	* Transportation
	* Other (Please specify)
5. **Enter the expense amount** (<id: 5, label: Amount, required: false>): Type in the total amount of your expenses.
6. **Attach a receipt** (<id: 6, label: Receipt, required: false>): You can attach a receipt or a note with details about your expenses.
7. **Enter your name** (<id: 7, label: Submitter, required: false>): Type in your name as the person submitting this form.
8. **Enter your email** (<id: 8, label: Submitter Email, required: false>): Type in your email address.
9. **Enter your phone number** (<id: 9, label: Submitter Phone, required: false>): Type in your phone number.
10. **Select your approver(s)** (<id: 10, label: Approver, required: false>): Choose the person(s) who will review and approve your expenses, from the list:
	* Manager
	* Supervisor
	* Accountant
11. **Enter your approver's email** (<id: 11, label: Approver Email, required: false>): Type in the email address of the person you selected as your approver.
12. **Enter your approver's phone number** (<id: 12, label: Approver Phone, required: false>): Type in the phone number of the person you selected as your approver.
13. **Select the status of your request** (<id: 13, label: Status, required: false>): Choose the status of your request, from the list:
	* Approved
	* Rejected
	* Pending

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Dates**: Enter the dates of your trip in the format `mm/dd/yyyy - mm/dd/yyyy`.

* **Submitter Phone**: Type in your phone number.

* **Approver Email**: Type in the email address of the person you selected as your approver.
* **Approver Phone**: Type in the phone number of the person you selected as your approver.
