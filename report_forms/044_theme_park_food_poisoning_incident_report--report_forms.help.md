# Theme Park Food Poisoning Incident Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to report food poisoning incidents that have occurred in a theme park. It will help theme park staff to quickly gather all the necessary information to handle the situation effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the theme park where the incident occurred.
2. Enter the date and time when the incident happened.
3. Describe the food that was consumed by the person who experienced the symptoms.
4. Describe the symptoms that the person experienced.
5. Provide the name and contact information of the person who experienced the symptoms.
6. Provide any other relevant details about the incident location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Food Consumed** (`theme_park_food_poisoning_incident_report`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the food that was consumed by the person who experienced the symptoms.
* **Park** (`park_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the theme park where the incident occurred.
* **Report Date** (`report_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when the incident was reported, which is the date when the incident occurred plus one or more days.
* **Time of Incident** (`incident_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time when the incident occurred.
* **Symptoms Description** (`symptoms_description`, text, required: false): Describe the symptoms that were experienced by the person.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person who experienced the symptoms.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the contact phone number of the person who experienced the symptoms.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter the contact email of the person who experienced the symptoms.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location where the incident occurred.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the fields accurately and completely.
* If there is any additional information that is not included in this form, please provide it in the "Symptoms Description" field.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact the theme park staff for assistance.
