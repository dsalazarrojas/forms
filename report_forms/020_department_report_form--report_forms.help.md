# Department Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Department Report Form is a tool for employees to report on any issues they experienced during the week and the actions they took to address them. The goal is to gather information for management to review and improve department processes.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Select the page you wish to report on: You will have the option to choose between two pages: "Weekly Issues" and "Actions Taken".
* On the "Weekly Issues" page, select all the issues that occurred during the week. You can choose from the following options: 'Yes' or 'No'.
* On the "Actions Taken" page, describe any actions you took to address the issues. You can type as much or as little as necessary.
* On both pages, indicate if you are submitting the report or not. You can choose from the following options: 'Yes' or 'No'.
* Enter your email address to receive a copy of the report.
* Finally, confirm who the report is submitted to. You can choose from the following options: 'Yes' or 'No'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Weekly Issues
* **Weekly Issues** (`weekly_issues`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all the issues that occurred during the week. You can choose from the options 'Yes' or 'No'.
### Actions Taken
* **Actions Taken** (`actions_taken`, `note`, required/optional): Describe any actions you took to address the issues.
### Weekly Report
* **Weekly Report** (`week_report`, `select_one`, required/optional): Indicate if you are submitting the report.
### Submitted By
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address to receive a copy of the report.
### Submitted To
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, `select_one`, required/optional): Indicate who the report is submitted to.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
* Be concise when answering the questions.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the report.
* Use this form only for reporting purposes, and do not submit duplicate reports.
* Ensure that you are submitting the report and that you enter the correct email address to receive a copy of the report.
