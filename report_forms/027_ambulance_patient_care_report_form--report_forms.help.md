# Ambulance Patient Care Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ambulance Patient Care Report Form is a critical tool used by emergency medical services (EMS) personnel to document the care and transport of patients in emergency situations. The form helps to ensure that all necessary information is captured and communicated effectively between emergency responders, hospitals, and medical professionals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these general steps:

1. Review the patient's demographic information (Section 1) to ensure accuracy and completeness.
2. Record the call and transport details (Section 2) to provide context for the patient's care.
3. Document the patient's medical condition and treatment (Sections 3-5) to provide a clear account of their care and response.
4. Complete the patient's response to treatment and transport information (Sections 6-7) to ensure that all necessary details are captured.
5. Review and update the form as necessary during transport.
6. Complete the handoff report (Section 8) to ensure a smooth transfer of care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Run/Call Number** (Section 1): The unique identifier for the patient's emergency call.
* **Call Date** (Section 1): The date and time the call was received.
* **Call Time Received** (Section 1): The time the call was received.
* **Dispatched Time** (Section 1): The time the patient was dispatched to the scene.
* **Arrived Scene Time** (Section 1): The time the ambulance arrived at the scene.
* **Departed Scene Time** (Section 1): The time the patient was transported away from the scene.
* **Arrived at Facility Time** (Section 1): The time the patient arrived at the destination facility.
* **Transferred Care Time** (Section 1): The time the patient was transferred to a new care provider.
* **Available for Service Time** (Section 1): The time the patient was available for service.
* **Patient First Name** (Section 3): The patient's first name.
* **Patient Last Name** (Section 3): The patient's last name.
* **Patient Date of Birth** (Section 3): The patient's date of birth.
* **Patient Age** (Section 3): The patient's age.
* **Patient Gender** (Section 3): The patient's gender.
* **Patient Race/Ethnicity** (Section 3): The patient's self-identified race and/or ethnicity.
* **Location Type** (Section 4): The type of location where the incident occurred (e.g., private residence, public place, etc.).
* **Incident Location** (Section 4): The address or location where the incident occurred.
* **Chief Complaint** (Section 4): The patient's chief complaint or reason for seeking medical attention.
* **Mechanism of Injury** (Section 4): A description of how the injury occurred (if applicable).
* **Nature of Illness** (Section 4): The underlying medical condition or illness (if applicable).
* **Transport Priority** (Section 6): The priority of the transport (e.g., emergency, non-priority/routine).
* **Transport Mode** (Section 6): The mode of transportation used (e.g., lights and sirens, no lights and sirens).
* **Destination Facility** (Section 6): The hospital or facility where the patient was transported.
* **Reason for Facility Choice** (Section 6): The reason for selecting the destination facility.
* **Patient Position During Transport** (Section 6): The patient's position during transport (e.g., supine, sitting, etc.).
* **Receiving Nurse Name** (Section 7): The name of the nurse receiving the patient at the destination facility.
* **Receiving Physician Name** (Section 7): The name of the physician receiving the patient at the destination facility.
* **Handoff Report Time** (Section 7): The time the handoff report was completed.
* **Patient Belongings Transferred** (Section 7): A list of any personal belongings transferred with the patient.
* **Driver Name** (Section 8): The name of the driver or crew member completing the form.
* **Driver Certification** (Section 8): The certification level of the driver (e.g., EMT-B, EMT-I, Paramedic).
* **Attendant Name** (Section 8): The name of the attendant or crew member accompanying the patient.
* **Attendant Certification** (Section 8): The certification level of the attendant (e.g., EMT-B, EMT-I, Paramedic).
* **Third Crew Member** (Section 8): The name of any additional crew member present (if applicable).
* **Patient Care Narrative** (Section 9): A detailed account of the patient's care and response to treatment.
* **Special Circumstances** (Section 9): Any unusual or notable circumstances related to the patient's care.
* **Report Completed By** (Section 10): The name of the person completing the report.
* **Report Date** (Section 10): The date the report was completed.

## Tips

* Ensure that all necessary information is captured accurately and completely.
* Review and update the form as necessary during transport.
* Complete the form in a clear and legible manner.
* Verify that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Ensure that the form is signed and dated by the person completing it.
* Store the form in a secure and accessible location for future reference.
