# Library Patron Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Library Patron Incident Report Form is designed to collect information about incidents involving library patrons, including dates, times, locations, and descriptions of incidents. This form helps library staff to track and manage incidents efficiently, ensuring that necessary follow-up actions are taken to maintain a safe and respectful environment for all patrons and staff.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the patron's name in the "Patron Name" field.
2. Select the date of the incident in the "Incident Date" field. This field is a date picker that allows you to select a date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
3. Enter the time of the incident in the "Incident Time" field. This field expects a 12-hour clock format (e.g., 9:00 AM or 10:45 PM).
4. Specify the location where the incident occurred in the "Location" field.
5. Describe the incident in the "Incident Description" field. This is a free-text field where you can provide as much detail as necessary.
6. Indicate whether staff were notified about the incident in the "Staff Notified" field.
7. Choose the type of incident that occurred from the list (e.g., "Other", "Patron behavior", "Patron property damage", etc.).
8. List any follow-up actions taken in response to the incident in the "Follow-up Actions" field. Select all applicable actions, such as sending a note to the patron, making a phone call, or scheduling a follow-up meeting.
9. Identify who to contact for follow-up in the "Follow-up Contacts" field. Choose one or more individuals, such as the patron or library staff.
10. Provide any comments or notes about the incident in the "Follow-up Comments" field.
11. Attach any supporting documents or photos related to the incident to the corresponding "Attachment" fields (e.g., "Attachment 1", "Attachment 2", etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patron Name**: Enter the name of the patron involved in the incident.
* **Incident Date**: Select the date of the incident using the date picker.
* **Incident Time**: Enter the time of the incident in 12-hour clock format.
* **Location**: Specify the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description**: Describe the incident in as much detail as necessary.
* **Staff Notified**: Check if staff were notified about the incident.
* **Incident Type**: Choose the type of incident from the list.
* **Follow-up Actions**: Select all applicable follow-up actions taken in response to the incident.
* **Follow-up Contacts**: Identify who to contact for follow-up, choosing one or more individuals.
* **Follow-up Comments**: Provide any comments or notes about the incident.
* **Attachment 1** to **Attachment 12**: Attach any supporting documents or photos related to the incident.
