# Breach or Non Compliance Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Breach or Non-Compliance Reporting Form is designed to capture reports of incidents or issues that may be in breach of organizational policies, laws, or regulations. This form helps to ensure that all relevant information is captured and addressed in a structured and timely manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the "Report date" field with the date of the incident or issue being reported.
2.  Enter your name in the "Reporter name" field. This will help us track your report and follow up with you for updates.
3.  Identify the department related to the report in the "Department" field. If unsure, you can leave this field blank.
4.  In the "Policy or legislation affected" field, describe the specific policy or law that may have been breached.
5.  Provide a detailed description of the incident or issue in the "Description of breach" field.
6.  Assess the immediate risk level of the incident or issue in the "Immediate risk" field. Choose from High, Medium, or Low risk.
7.  If you've already taken any steps to address the issue, provide them in the "Actions taken" field.
8.  If you have any suggested next steps or responsible parties for follow-up, provide them in the "Recommended follow up" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Report date (report_date)**: The date of the incident or issue being reported.
    \*\* This date helps us track the timeline of events and ensure that your report is processed in a timely manner. \*\*
*   **Reporter name (reporter_name)**: Name of person submitting report.
    \*\* This field is used to track who reported the incident and for follow-up purposes. \*\*
*   **Department (department)**: Department related to the report.
    \*\* If unsure, you can leave this field blank. \*\*
*   **Policy or legislation affected (policy\_or\_legislation)**: Identify the policy or law impacted.
    \*\* This field is used to understand the specific policy or law that may have been breached. \*\*
*   **Description of breach (description\_of\_breach)**: Detailed description of the incident.
    \*\* This field should provide a clear and concise account of what happened. \*\*
*   **Immediate risk (immediate\_risk)**: Assess immediate risk level.
    \*\* Choose from High, Medium, or Low risk. \*\*
*   **Actions taken (actions\_taken)**: Steps already taken to address the issue.
    \*\* If you've already taken any steps, please provide them here. \*\*
*   **Recommended follow up (recommended\_follow\_up)**: Suggested next steps and responsible parties.
    \*\* If you have any suggestions for follow-up actions, please provide them here. \*\*

## Tips

*   Make sure to provide as much detail as possible in each field to ensure that your report is thorough and accurate.
*   If you're unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to ask your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
*   If you've reported the same issue previously, please make sure to update your report with any new information or changes.
