# Intervention Ticket Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Intervention Ticket Submission Form is used to report incidents, issues, or problems that require attention from designated authorities. It helps to ensure that your concerns are properly documented and addressed in a timely manner.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Select the correct options from the dropdown menus where applicable.
3. Enter relevant information in the text fields (e.g., First Name, Last Name, Description).
4. Make sure to select the correct category and subcategory for your issue.
5. Click the "Submit" button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted for further communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for follow-up communication.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you want to confirm that the issue is significant.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple values that apply to your issue.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the issue.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the issue.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter a brief note about the issue.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Enter a descriptive title for your ticket.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Select the category of the issue.
* **Subcategory** (`subcategory`, select_one, optional): Select the subcategory of the issue.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of the issue.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_multiple, required): Select who the issue is assigned to.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Confirm your submission by clicking the "Submit" button.

**Tips**

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to help ensure that your issue is addressed efficiently.
* Review the form before submitting to ensure that you have entered all necessary information.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with your supervisor or a designated authority.
