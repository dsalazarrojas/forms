# tenant_harassment_complaint_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Harassment Complaint Form is designed to report incidents of harassment within a housing or residential setting. This form is used to gather essential information to investigate and address these incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of harassment that occurred.
2. Provide the date and time of the incident.
3. Describe the incident in as much detail as possible.
4. If you witnessed the incident, provide your name, phone number, and email.
5. If you have any supporting documentation, such as a police report number, provide it.
6. Specify the location where the incident occurred.
7. If you are the tenant who experienced the incident, provide a statement describing what happened.
8. If you are the witness, provide a statement describing what you saw or heard.
9. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Harassment Type**: Select the type of harassment that occurred. Please choose one of the following:
	+ Racial Harassment
	+ Religious Harassment
	+ Sexual Orientation Harassment
	+ Disability Harassment
* **Incident Date**: Enter the date of the incident, using the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Incident Time**: Enter the time of the incident, using the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Incident Description**: Describe the incident in as much detail as possible, including what happened and any witnesses.
* **Witness Name**: If you witnessed the incident, enter your name.
* **Witness Phone**: If you witnessed the incident, enter your phone number.
* **Witness Email**: If you witnessed the incident, enter your email address.
* **Police Report Number**: If you have a police report number, enter it here.
* **Incident Location**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Witness Statement**: If you witnessed the incident, enter a brief statement describing what you saw or heard.
* **Tenant Statement**: If you are the tenant who experienced the harassment, enter a brief statement describing what happened.
* **Tenant Contact**: Enter your contact information, including your name and phone number.
* **Witness Signature**: Sign and date the form.
* **Tenant Signature**: Sign and date the form.
