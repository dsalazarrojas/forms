# Patient Visit Summary Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Patient Visit Summary Report is a form used to gather information about patient visits, which is crucial for reporting and summarizing encounters for medical record-keeping purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by ensuring you have access to the form and any necessary information about the patient's visit.
2. Select the provider (the individual who saw the patient) from the options provided.
3. Enter the date and time of the patient's visit.
4. Record the patient's age.
5. Enter the duration of the visit.
6. Provide any relevant notes from the provider regarding the visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Encounter Details** (`encounter_details`, text, optional): This field is used to enter a brief description of the patient's visit.
* **Provider** (`provider`, select_one, optional): Select the provider who saw the patient to associate the encounter with the correct healthcare professional.
* **Patient** (`patient`, text, optional): Enter the patient's name or identifier for accurate tracking.
* **Visit Date** (`visit_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the patient's visit.
* **Visit Time** (`visit_time`, time, optional): Record the time of the patient's visit.
* **Patient Age** (`patient_age`, text, optional): Enter the patient's age as of the visit.
* **Visit Duration** (`visit_duration`, number, optional): Record the length of the patient's visit.
* **Provider Notes** (`provider_notes`, text, optional): Enter any important notes from the provider about the visit.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are completed accurately to provide a clear and comprehensive summary of the patient's visit.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled in.
* If the patient's visit was particularly significant or unusual, include detailed notes in the "Provider Notes" field to maintain a record of the encounter.
* Consult with other healthcare team members or the patient's records if unsure about any information.
