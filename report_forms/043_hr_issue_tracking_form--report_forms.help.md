# HR Issue Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Issue Tracking Form is used to report and manage HR-related issues within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the employee who is experiencing the issue.
2.  Choose the type of contact for the issue (Manager, Team Leader, HR, or Other).
3.  Choose whether the issue is related to the employee's division (Yes or No).
4.  Select the type of problem (Recruiting, Hiring, Training, or Other).
5.  Provide a brief description of the issue.
6.  Choose the tool that will be used to address the issue (chatjimmy, Other).
7.  Enter the date the issue was identified.
8.  Choose the status of the issue (Open, Closed, or In Progress).
9.  Set the priority of the issue (Low, High, or Medium).
10. Describe the resolution to the issue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Employee**: Select the employee who is experiencing the issue. This will help the issue be properly routed to the relevant team or department.
	+ Options: List of employees in the organization
* **contact_type**: Choose the type of contact for the issue (Manager, Team Leader, HR, or Other). This will help determine who will be notified about the issue.
	+ Options: Manager, Team Leader, HR, Other
* **division**: Choose whether the issue is related to the employee's division (Yes or No). This will help determine the team that needs to be involved in resolving the issue.
	+ Options: Yes, No
* **Problem Type**: Select the type of problem (Recruiting, Hiring, Training, or Other). This will help the relevant team determine the best course of action for resolving the issue.
	+ Options: Recruiting, Hiring, Training, Other
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of the issue. This will help the issue be understood by the relevant teams and teams involved.
	+ Type: Text field
* **Assigned Tool**: Choose the tool that will be used to address the issue (chatjimmy, Other). This will help ensure that the right tool is used for the resolution.
	+ Options: chatjimmy, Other
* **Date**: Enter the date the issue was identified.
	+ Type: Date field
* **Issue Status**: Choose the status of the issue (Open, Closed, or In Progress). This will help track the progress of the issue.
	+ Options: Open, Closed, In Progress
* **Priority**: Set the priority of the issue (Low, High, or Medium). This will help determine the urgency and importance of the issue.
	+ Options: Low, High, Medium
* **Resolution**: Describe the resolution to the issue. This will help ensure that the issue is properly documented and tracked.
	+ Type: Text field
