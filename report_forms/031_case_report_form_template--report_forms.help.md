# Case Report Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a comprehensive case report form template designed to collect detailed information about a patient's incident or injury. The form is used to record the essential facts about the incident, including the date and time it occurred, the patient's symptoms, treatment, and outcome.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that all required fields are completed.
2. Enter the patient's name and ID number accurately.
3. Provide the correct date of birth and symptoms.
4. Select the correct incident type.
5. Describe the incident location and time.
6. Answer questions regarding first aid and medical attention provided.
7. Provide any additional medical information as necessary.
8. Sign and date the form to indicate completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report ID Number** (`report_id`, text, required): Enter the unique identifier for this case report.
* **Report Date** (`report_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the report.
* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, note, required): Enter any relevant patient details.
* **Patient Full Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Patient ID Number** (`patient_id`, text, required): Enter the patient's ID number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Age** (`age`, number, required): Enter the patient's age in years.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): Select the patient's gender.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the best contact number for the patient.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name of an emergency contact (if applicable).
* **Incident Information** (`incident_info`, note, optional): Enter additional incident details.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the incident.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the incident.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of incident.
* **Detailed Description of Incident** (`incident_description`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of the incident.
* **Were There Witnesses** (`witnesses_present`, select_one, required): Select whether there were witnesses present.
* **Witness Names and Contact Information** (`witness_names`, text, optional): Enter the names and contact information of witnesses (if applicable).
* **Signs and Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, required): Enter the patient's symptoms.
* **When Did Symptoms Begin** (`symptom_onset`, select_one, required): Select when symptoms began.
* **Severity of Condition** (`severity`, select_one, required): Select the severity of the condition.
* **Was First Aid Administered** (`first_aid_given`, select_one, required): Select whether first aid was administered.
* **Was Medical Attention Required** (`medical_attention`, select_one, required): Select whether medical attention was required.
* **Follow-Up Appointment Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of a follow-up appointment (if scheduled).
* **Body Parts Affected** (`body_parts_affected`, select_multiple, optional): Select the body parts affected by the incident (if applicable).
* **Vital Signs** (`vital_signs`, note, optional): Enter any relevant vital signs (if taken).
* **Blood Pressure** (`blood_pressure`, text, optional): Enter the patient's blood pressure (if measured).
* **Heart Rate** (`heart_rate`, number, optional): Enter the patient's heart rate (if measured).
* **Temperature** (`temperature`, number, optional): Enter the patient's temperature (if measured).
* **Respiratory Rate** (`respiratory_rate`, number, optional): Enter the patient's respiratory rate (if measured).
* **Treatment Information** (`treatment_info`, note, optional): Enter any treatment details.
* **Was First Aid Given** (`first_aid_given`, select_one, required): Select whether first aid was administered.
* **Describe First Aid Provided** (`first_aid_description`, text, optional): Enter a description of the first aid provided.
* **Was Medical Attention Required** (`medical_attention`, select_one, required): Select whether medical attention was required.
* **Treatment Facility Name** (`treatment_facility`, text, optional): Enter the name of the treatment facility (if applicable).
* **Diagnosing Provider Name** (`diagnosing_provider`, text, optional): Enter the name of the diagnosing provider (if applicable).
* **Diagnosis** (`diagnosis`, text, optional): Enter the diagnosis (if known).
* **Prognosis** (`prognosis`, select_one, optional): Select the expected prognosis.
* **Work or Activity Impact** (`work_impact`, note, optional): Enter any work or activity limitations.
* **Time Lost from Work or Activities** (`time_lost`, number, optional): Enter the number of days missed from work or activities.
* **Activity Restrictions** (`restrictions`, text, optional): Enter any activity restrictions.
* **Is Follow-Up Care Needed** (`follow_up_needed`, select_one, required): Select whether follow-up care is needed.
* **Follow-Up Appointment Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of a follow-up appointment (if scheduled).
* **Cause Analysis** (`cause_analysis`, text, optional): Enter any contributing factors.
* **Recommended Preventive Measures** (`preventive_measures`, text, optional): Enter any recommended preventive measures.
* **Reporter Information** (`reporter_info`, note, optional): Enter any additional reporter information.
* **Reporter Name** (`reporter_name`, text, required): Enter the reporter's full name.
* **Reporter Title** (`reporter_title`, text, required): Enter the reporter's job title.
* **Reporter Signature Date** (`reporter_signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date the report was signed.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submission.
* Enter accurate and complete information to provide a thorough and accurate record of the incident.
* Use this form as a guide for reporting and documenting incidents or injuries.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the relevant authorities or healthcare professionals.
* This form is for the purpose of reporting and documenting incidents or injuries and should not be used for medical billing or insurance purposes.
