# Fire Safety Information Collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect fire safety information from sites, ensuring the safety of employees, visitors, and assets.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the site details in the Site Details field.
2. Check the status of fire equipment and select the correct options for Equipment Status and Exits.
3. Provide the last and next inspection dates, if applicable.
4. Enter the name of the safety officer and contact person.
5. Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Site Details
* **Site Details** (text, required/optional): Enter the site details, including the location, address, or any other relevant information.

### Equipment Status
* **Equipment Status** (select_multiple, required/optional): Check the status of fire equipment on the site, selecting 'True' for equipment that is currently functioning and 'False' for equipment that is not functioning.

### Exits
* **Exits** (select_multiple, required/optional): Check the number and status of exits on the site, selecting 'True' for exits that are currently functioning and 'False' for exits that are not functioning.

### Last Inspection Date
* **Last Inspection Date** (date, required/optional): Enter the date of the last fire safety inspection, if applicable.

### Next Inspection Date
* **Next Inspection Date** (date, required/optional): Enter the date of the next scheduled fire safety inspection, if applicable.

### Safety Officer Name
* **Safety Officer Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the safety officer responsible for the site's fire safety.

### Contact Person
* **Contact Person** (text, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for fire safety on the site.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
* If you're unsure about any field, please seek clarification from your supervisor or safety officer.
