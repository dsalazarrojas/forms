# Transportation Infrastructure Condition Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about the condition of various types of transportation infrastructure, such as bridges, roadways, railways, and pathways. It will be used to provide an objective, data-driven evaluation of each infrastructure's condition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Transportation Infrastructure Condition Report" link to access the form.
2. Fill out the required fields with accurate and relevant information:
   - **Inspection Date**: The date of the inspection.
   - **Location**: The specific location of the infrastructure (e.g., "Main St. Bridge").
   - **Road Name**: The name of the road or pathway being inspected.
   - **Asset Type**: The type of transportation infrastructure being inspected (e.g., "Bridge", "Roadway", "Railway").
   - **Condition Score**: A numerical value between 1 and 5 indicating the condition of the infrastructure.
   - **Notes**: Any additional comments or observations.

   Note: The **Asset Type** field is not always required, depending on the specific infrastructure being inspected.
3. Click the "Submit" button to save your changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspection Date**: The date you inspected the transportation infrastructure. Enter the date using the standard date format.
* **Location**: Type in the name of the specific location of the infrastructure you inspected (e.g., "Main St. Bridge").
* **Road Name**: Enter the name of the road or pathway being inspected.
* **Asset Type**: Select the type of transportation infrastructure being inspected from the dropdown menu (e.g., "Bridge", "Roadway", "Railway").
* **Condition Score**: Enter a numerical value between 1 and 5 indicating the condition of the infrastructure. A score of:
   - 1: Poor
   - 2: Fair
   - 3: Good
   - 4: Excellent
   - 5: Excellent (no issues)
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or observations about the inspected infrastructure.

Note: The **Notes** field is optional but can be helpful in providing more context about the condition of the infrastructure.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you're unsure about any information, please double-check before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your supervisor or manager for guidance.
