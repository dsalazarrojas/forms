# Engineer Call Out Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Engineer Call Out Report Form is used to report call-outs from field engineers for site management to track and record call-out details and related information. This form ensures that all key fields are captured and helps in maintaining accurate and standardized call-out records.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the required information for each field as prompted.
*   Ensure that all fields marked as "required" are completed before proceeding to the next page.
*   Review the entire form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Name (1)**: Enter the name of the customer who requested the call-out. This is a critical field that helps in identifying the customer and their request.
*   **Engineer Name (2)**: Enter the name of the engineer who performed the call-out. This field is used to track the engineer's involvement and ensures that they receive credit for their work.
*   **Call Out Date (3)**: Enter the date when the call-out took place. This field helps in recording the timeline of the call-out and its related activities.
*   **Site Manager Name (4)**: Enter the name of the site manager who oversaw the call-out. This field ensures that the site manager's involvement is documented and recorded.
*   **Call Out Details (5)**: Provide a brief description of the call-out, including any notable events or issues encountered. This field helps in creating a comprehensive record of the call-out.
*   **Engineer Hours (6)**: Enter the number of hours spent on the call-out. This field helps in tracking the time spent on the call-out and related expenses.
*   **Organization Name (7)**: Enter the name of the organization or company that requested the call-out. This field ensures that the correct organization is associated with the call-out record.
*   **Assigned Tool (8)**: Select the tool that was assigned to the engineer for the call-out from the provided options. This field helps in tracking the tools used for the call-out and related expenses.
*   **Comments (9)**: Provide any additional comments or notes related to the call-out. This field is optional and provides additional context to the call-out record.
*   **Site Name (10)**: Enter the name of the site where the call-out took place. This field helps in tracking the location of the call-out and related activities.

## Tips

*   Always ensure that all required fields are completed before proceeding to the next page.
*   Review the entire form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
*   Use clear and concise language when entering comments or call-out details.
