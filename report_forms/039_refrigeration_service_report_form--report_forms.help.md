# Refrigeration Service Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Refrigeration Service Report Form is designed to collect information about refrigeration services performed by a service business. This form helps service businesses to document their services and keep track of their activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Service Business ID field with your service business's ID number or unique identifier.
2. Enter the date the service was performed in the Service Date field.
3. Provide a description of the service performed in the Maintenance Service Description field.
4. Add any additional notes or comments about the service in the Maintenance Service Notes field.
5. Enter the ID number of the refrigeration equipment serviced in the Refrigeration Equipment ID field.
6. Describe the equipment serviced in the Equipment Description field.
7. Enter the service address, where the service was performed in the Service Address field.
8. Provide the name of the customer who received the service in the Customer Name field.
9. Enter the customer's email address in the Customer Email field.
10. Enter the customer's phone number in the Customer Phone field.
11. Enter the name and title of the person responsible for performing the service in the Assigned To field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Business ID** (`service_business_id`, text, not required): This is the unique identifier or ID number of your service business.
* **Service Date** (`service_date`, date, not required): Enter the date the service was performed.
* **Maintenance Service Description** (`maintenance_service_description`, text, not required): Describe the service performed in detail.
* **Maintenance Service Notes** (`maintenance_service_notes`, text, not required): Add any additional notes or comments about the service.
* **Refrigeration Equipment ID** (`refrigeration_equipment_id`, text, not required): Enter the ID number of the refrigeration equipment serviced.
* **Equipment Description** (`equipment_description`, text, not required): Describe the equipment serviced in detail.
* **Service Address** (`service_address`, text, not required): Enter the address where the service was performed.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, not required): Provide the name of the customer who received the service.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, text, not required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, not required): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, not required): Enter the name and title of the person responsible for performing the service.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
- If a field is not required, you can leave it blank if not applicable.
- If you need help with any of the fields, please contact our support team.
- This form is for internal use only and is not intended for public consumption.
