# Event Completion Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Completion Report Form is a tool used to report on the completion of events, providing a concise and organized way to gather information about the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the event name, date, time, and location in the respective fields.
2. Provide a brief summary of the event outcomes in the "Event Outcomes" field.
3. Give feedback on the event, including suggestions for improvement in the "Event Feedback" field.
4. Describe how you would plan to follow up on this event in the "Event Planning" field.
5. Finally, provide any additional notes or next steps you plan to take in the "Event Next Steps" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the event being reported on.
* **Event Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Location** (Text, Optional): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Outcomes** (Text, Optional): Describe the outcomes or results of the event.
* **Event Feedback** (Text, Optional): Provide feedback on the event, including any suggestions for improvement.
* **Event Improvement** (Text, Optional): Suggest areas for improvement for future events.
* **Event Planning** (Text, Optional): Describe how you would plan to follow up on this event.
* **Event Next Steps** (Text, Optional): Provide any additional notes or next steps you plan to take.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and detailed information in each field to ensure a comprehensive report.
* If a field is not required, it is still important to provide information if possible.
* If you have any difficulties or questions, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
