# Management Feedback Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Management Feedback Report is a form designed to collect feedback from staff members about their manager's performance. It helps in providing a structured and objective assessment of the manager's effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Report Date field with the date of the feedback session or the last evaluation period.
2. Enter the Manager Name in the corresponding field.
3. In the What are your thoughts about the manager's performance? field, provide your honest and constructive feedback about the manager's performance.
4. Answer the Are there any areas of concern regarding the manager's behavior? question with a simple "True" or "False".
5. Select the level of staff motivation in the Is staff motivated? field with one of the three options: High, Medium, or Low.
6. In the Areas of concern field, mention any specific areas that you feel need improvement.
7. You can add any additional comments in the Comments field. However, this is not a required field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Date** (`report_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the feedback session or the last evaluation period. This will help in tracking the progress and development over time.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your manager whom you are providing feedback for.
* **What are your thoughts about the manager's performance?** (`feedback_questions`, text, required): Share your honest and constructive feedback about the manager's performance. Be specific and provide examples to support your comments.
* **Are there any areas of concern regarding the manager's behavior?** (`manager_behavior`, select_multiple, required): Select "True" if you have any areas of concern regarding the manager's behavior. If not, select "False".
* **Is staff motivated?** (`staff_motivation`, select_one, required): Choose the level of staff motivation with one of the three options: High, Medium, or Low.
* **Areas of concern** (`areas_of_concern`, text, required): Mention any specific areas that you feel need improvement.
* **Comments** (`report_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments that you would like to share with the person providing this form. This field is not required but can be useful for further discussion or clarification.
