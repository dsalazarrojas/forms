# Mineral Expenditure Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mineral Expenditure Reporting Form is used by project managers and team members to report project costs, expenditures, and progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the General Info section with your project details.
2. Provide a brief summary of your project in the Project Summary field.
3. Enter your project costs in the Mineral Costs field.
4. Select the reporting period in the Reporting Period field.
5. Choose the type of cost you are reporting in the Cost Type field.
6. Enter the amount of the cost in the Cost Amount field.
7. Select the currency type in the Currency Type field.
8. Enter the currency code in the Currency Code field (if applicable).
9. Enter your project name and team member name in the respective fields.
10. Provide your email and phone number (if applicable).
11. Select the reporting status (Draft, Submitted, or Approved).
12. Enter the submission date.
13. Add any additional comments or attachments as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of your project, including project name, project status, and any relevant project details.
* **Project Summary** (`id: 2`, `type: text`, required: false): Provide a short summary of your project, including its objectives, goals, and expected outcomes.
* **Mineral Costs** (`id: 3`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the costs associated with your project, including any expenses, materials, or labor costs.
* **Reporting Period** (`id: 4`, `type: text`, required: false): Select the period during which you are reporting your project expenses (e.g., Quarter, Semi Annual, Monthly, Yearly).
* **Cost Type** (`id: 5`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of cost you are reporting (e.g., labor, materials, equipment).
* **Cost Amount** (`id: 6`, `type: number`, required: false): Enter the actual cost amount for the selected cost type.
* **Currency Type** (`id: 7`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Select the currency type for your project costs (e.g., Australian Dollar, US Dollar, Canadian Dollar).
* **Currency Code** (`id: 8`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the currency code for the selected currency type (if applicable).
* **Project Name** (`id: 9`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the name of your project.
* **Team Member** (`id: 10`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the name of the team member associated with your project.
* **Email** (`id: 11`, `type: email`, required: false): Enter your email address (if applicable).
* **Phone** (`id: 12`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Reporting Status** (`id: 13`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Select the status of your report (Draft, Submitted, Approved).
* **Submission Date** (`id: 14`, `type: date`, required: false): Enter the date you are submitting your report.
* **Notes** (`id: 15`, `type: note`, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes related to your project.
* **Comments** (`id: 16`, `type: note`, required: false): Enter any comments or feedback from team members or administrators.
* **Attachments** (`id: 17`, `type: note`, required: false): Attach any relevant documents or files to your report.
* **Project Status** (`id: 18`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false): Select the status of your project (Active, Inactive, Suspended).
* **Reporting Period/Element Type** (`id: 19`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false): Select the type of reporting period (e.g., Quarter, Semi Annual, Monthly, Yearly).
* **Reporting Period Element** (`id: 20`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Select the specific element within the reporting period (e.g., Mineral Type A, Mineral Type B, Mineral Type C).
