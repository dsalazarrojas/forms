# Incident Injury Reporting Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about workplace incidents, including injuries that occurred during work hours. This information will be used to document the incident, track progress, and ensure that proper reporting and follow-up procedures are followed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information about the incident.
2. Provide a brief description of what happened, including the date and time of the incident.
3. If applicable, describe the injuries sustained and any medical treatment received.
4. Attach any supporting documentation, such as witness statements or incident investigation reports.
5. Provide contact information for the incident reporter and any supervisors involved.
6. Report the current status of the incident, including any changes in status over time.
