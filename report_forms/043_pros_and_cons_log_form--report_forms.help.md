# Pros And Cons Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect and log the pros and cons of something or someone. It's intended for decision-making or evaluation purposes, where you need to weigh the advantages and disadvantages of a particular option or person.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Start by filling out the required fields**: You will need to enter the date and time when you completed this form.
2. **List the pros**: Enter the advantages or benefits of the option or person you are evaluating in the "pro1", "pro2", "pro3", "pro4", and "pro5" fields.
3. **List the cons**: Enter the disadvantages or drawbacks of the option or person you are evaluating in the "con1", "con2", "con3", "con4", and "con5" fields.
4. **Submit the form**: Once you have filled out all the required fields, click the "submit" button to save your entries.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **pro1**: Enter the first advantage or benefit of the option or person you are evaluating.
* **pro2**: Enter the second advantage or benefit of the option or person you are evaluating.
* **pro3**: Enter the third advantage or benefit of the option or person you are evaluating.
* **pro4**: Enter the fourth advantage or benefit of the option or person you are evaluating.
* **pro5**: Enter the fifth and final advantage or benefit of the option or person you are evaluating. **This field is required**.
* **con1**: Enter the first disadvantage or drawback of the option or person you are evaluating.
* **con2**: Enter the second disadvantage or drawback of the option or person you are evaluating.
* **con3**: Enter the third disadvantage or drawback of the option or person you are evaluating.
* **con4**: Enter the fourth disadvantage or drawback of the option or person you are evaluating.
* **con5**: Enter the fifth and final disadvantage or drawback of the option or person you are evaluating. **This field is required**.
* **submit**: Click this button to save your entries and complete the form.
* **date**: Enter the date when you completed this form.
* **time**: Enter the time when you completed this form. **This field is required**.
