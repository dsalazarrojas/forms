# Maintenance Request Closure Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Maintenance Request Closure Report Form is used to document and track the closure of maintenance tasks. This form should only be used for closed maintenance requests and not for new requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct page to fill out the form. There are two pages in this form.
2. Fill out the required fields as follows:
	* Maintenance Requester: Enter the name of the person who submitted the maintenance request.
	* Maintenance Requester Email: Enter the email address of the person who submitted the maintenance request.
	* Maintenance Requester Phone: Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the maintenance request.
	* Maintenance Requester Department: Enter the department associated with the maintenance request.
	* Maintenance Requester Location: Enter the location associated with the maintenance request.
	* Maintenance Request Details: Enter a brief description of the maintenance request.
	* Maintenance Requester Date: Enter the date the maintenance request was submitted.
	* Maintenance Requester Time: Enter the time the maintenance request was submitted.
	* Maintenance Requester Comments: Enter any additional comments related to the maintenance request.
3. On the second page, fill out the following fields:
	* Maintenance Item Status: Select the current status of the maintenance item (Active or Inactive).
	* Maintenance Item ID: Enter the ID of the maintenance item.
	* Maintenance Item Description: Enter a brief description of the maintenance item.
	* Maintenance Item Status Updated By: Enter the person who updated the maintenance item status.
	* Maintenance Item Status Updated Date: Enter the date the maintenance item status was updated.
	* Maintenance Item Status Updated Time: Enter the time the maintenance item status was updated.
	* Maintenance Item Status Updated By: Enter the person who updated the maintenance item status.
	* Maintenance Item Closures: Enter any additional comments related to the maintenance item closures.
	* Maintenance Item Closures Count: Enter the number of times the maintenance item has been closed.
	* Maintenance Item Closures Count Updated By: Enter the person who updated the maintenance item closures count.
