# Journal Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Journal Entry Form is a standardized form used for recording patient information and data entry in a medical setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's personal details, including their name, address, and date of birth (if applicable).
2. Select or enter the patient's medical status, specialty, and medical practice details.
3. Enter the date and time of the journal entry.
4. Enter any additional demographic information, such as height and weight.
5. Review and confirm all entered information for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Journal Entry Form** (`1`, `number`, `required`: false): This is the form title, which is displayed to indicate the purpose of the form.
* **Date** (`2`, `date`, `required`: false): Enter the date of the journal entry.
* **Time** (`3`, `time`, `required`: false): Enter the time of the journal entry.
* **Patient Name** (`4`, `text`, `required`: false): Enter the patient's name.
* **Patient Address** (`5`, `text`, `required`: false): Enter the patient's address.
* **Medical Practice** (`6`, `text`, `required`: false): Enter the patient's medical practice information.
* **Medical Practice ID** (`7`, `number`, `required`: false): Enter the patient's medical practice ID.
* **Medical Specialty** (`8`, `text`, `required`: false): Enter the patient's medical specialty.
* **Medical Specialty ID** (`9`, `number`, `required`: false): Enter the patient's medical specialty ID.
* **Patient Status** (`10`, `text`, `required`: false): Enter the patient's status.
* **Patient Status ID** (`11`, `number`, `required`: false): Enter the patient's status ID.
* **Patient Type** (`12`, `text`, `required`: false): Enter the patient's type.
* **Patient Type ID** (`13`, `number`, `required`: false): Enter the patient's type ID.
* **Journal Entry Date** (`14`, `date`, `required`: false): Enter the journal entry date.
* **Journal Entry Time** (`15`, `time`, `required`: false): Enter the journal entry time.
* **Patient First Name** (`16`, `text`, `required`: false): Enter the patient's first name.
* **Patient Last Name** (`17`, `text`, `required`: false): Enter the patient's last name.
* **Patient Date of Birth** (`18`, `date`, `required`: false): Enter the patient's date of birth.

* **Patient Notes** (`25`, `text`, `required`: false): Enter any additional notes about the patient.

## Tips
* Double-check all entered information for accuracy before submitting.
* If any fields are required, make sure to fill them in completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult the relevant medical documentation or seek guidance from a medical professional.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, contact your healthcare provider or IT support.
