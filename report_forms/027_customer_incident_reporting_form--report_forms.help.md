# Customer Incident Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for customers to report incidents that have occurred with their interactions with the company. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that the incident is reported correctly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the question "customer_incident_reporting_form" by selecting one of the following options:
	* 'Yes' if you are reporting an incident
	* 'No' if you are not reporting an incident

2. Enter your name in the text field "customer_name" to identify yourself as the customer.

3. Provide a detailed description of the incident in the "incident_description" text area. Please be as specific as possible about what happened.

4. Indicate whether or not you were involved in the incident by selecting "reported_by" and choosing 'Yes' or 'No'.

5. If you reported the incident, enter the date it occurred in the "reported_on" field. Please use the format MM/DD/YYYY.

6. If you reported the incident, enter the time it occurred in the "reported_time" field. Please use the format 12:00 PM or 13:00 PM.

7. Select the status of the incident from the "incident_status" dropdown menu:
	* Active
	* Inactive

8. If you have any relevant contact information, select 'Yes' for "customer_contact_info" to provide it. If you have no contact information, select 'No'.

9. If you have any relevant contact information for the employee involved, select 'Yes' for "employee_contact_info" to provide it. If you have no contact information, select 'No'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **customer_incident_reporting_form** (Select One, required): This field asks whether or not you are reporting an incident. Please select 'Yes' if you are reporting an incident.
* **customer_name** (Text, required): Enter your name here to identify yourself as the customer.
* **incident_description** (Note, required): Please provide a detailed description of what happened, including any relevant details that may be helpful for our team.
* **reported_by** (Select Multiple, required): Indicate whether or not you were involved in the incident by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **reported_on** (Date, required): Enter the date the incident occurred in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **reported_time** (Time, optional): If you were involved in the incident, enter the time it occurred. Please use the format 12:00 PM or 13:00 PM.
* **incident_status** (Select One, required): Choose the status of the incident from the dropdown menu:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
* **customer_contact_info** (Select Multiple, required): If you have any relevant contact information, select 'Yes' to provide it. If you have no contact information, select 'No'.
* **employee_contact_info** (Select Multiple, required): If you have any relevant contact information for the employee involved, select 'Yes' to provide it. If you have no contact information, select 'No'.
