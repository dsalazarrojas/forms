# Consumer Attendance Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Consumer Attendance Report form is designed to collect detailed information about events, allowing for analysis and improvement of future events. This form should be completed by event organizers or coordinators to document and review the success and challenges of their events.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. For select fields, choose the most applicable option from the provided list.
3. Enter any additional details or comments in the text fields.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name (1)** (text, required): Enter the name of the event or session.
* **Event Date (2)** (date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Start Time (3)** (time, required): Enter the start time of the event.
* **End Time (4)** (time, required): Enter the end time of the event.
* **Event Location (5)** (text, required): Enter the location where the event was held.
* **Event Type (6)** (select_one, required): Choose the type of event from the provided list (e.g., Workshop, Training Session, Community Meeting).
* **Organizer Name (7)** (text, required): Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Organizer Email (8)** (email, required): Enter the contact email of the event organizer.
* **Organizer Phone (9)** (text, optional): Enter the contact phone number of the event organizer (if applicable).
* **Total Registered (10)** (number, required): Enter the total number of people registered for the event.
* **Total Attended (11)** (number, required): Enter the total number of people who attended the event.
* **Attendance Rate (12)** (number, optional): Enter the percentage of attendees (if available).
* **Attendee Demographics (13)** (text, optional): Provide a brief description of the attendee profile.
* **Age Groups Represented (14)** (select_multiple, optional): Select the applicable age groups.
* **Event Objectives (15)** (text, required): Enter the goals or objectives of the event.
* **Topics Covered (16)** (text, required): List the main topics discussed during the event.
* **Materials Distributed (17)** (text, optional): List any handouts or materials distributed during the event.
* **Participant Engagement Level (18)** (select_one, required): Rate the overall engagement level of participants.
* **Feedback Summary (19)** (text, optional): Provide a summary of participant feedback.
* **Challenges Encountered (20)** (text, optional): List any issues or challenges encountered during the event.
* **Key Successes (21)** (text, optional): List what went well or successful aspects of the event.
* **Follow-up Actions Needed (22)** (text, optional): List any follow-up actions or tasks required after the event.
* **Next Event Date (23)** (date, optional): Enter the scheduled date for the next event.
* **Additional Notes (24)** (text, optional): Enter any other relevant information.
* **Report Submitted By (25)** (text, required): Enter the name of the person submitting the report.
* **Report Submission Date (26)** (date, required): Enter the date of the report submission.
