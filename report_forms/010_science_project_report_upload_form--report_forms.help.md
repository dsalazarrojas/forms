# Science Project Report Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Science Project Report Upload Form is designed to allow students to upload reports for their science projects, providing a structured and organized way to submit their work. This form is intended for students to submit their project titles, descriptions, and supporting files, as well as their contact information. The form also allows teachers to review and provide feedback on the projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Science Project Report Upload Form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your project title in the "Project Title" field.
2.  Enter your student name in the "Student Name" field.
3.  Enter your teacher's name in the "Teacher Name" field.
4.  Provide a brief description of your project in the "Project Description" field.
5.  Select the category of your project from the "Category" dropdown menu.
6.  Upload supporting files for your project in the "File Upload" field (optional).
7.  Upload an image of your project in the "Image Upload" field (optional).
8.  Upload a video of your project in the "Video Upload" field (optional).
9.  Upload an audio file of your project in the "Audio Upload" field (optional).
10. Upload any other supporting file in the "Other File Upload" field (optional).
11. Enter the date and time of submission in the "Date of Submission" field.
12. Enter your contact information in the "Email" and "Phone" fields.
13. Enter any additional notes in the "Notes" field.
14. Select the current status of your project in the "Project Status" dropdown menu.
15. Finally, review your submission in the "Review" field before clicking "Submit".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Title** (`Project Title`, text, required): Enter a brief title for your science project.
* **Student Name** (`Student Name`, text, optional): Enter your student name for identification purposes.
* **Teacher Name** (`Teacher Name`, text, optional): Enter your teacher's name for collaboration and feedback.
* **Project Description** (`Project Description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your science project.
* **Category** (`Category`, select multiple, optional): Select the category of your project (e.g., physics, biology, chemistry).
* **File Upload** (`File Upload`, text, optional): Upload supporting files for your project.
* **Image Upload** (`Image Upload`, text, optional): Upload an image of your project.
* **Video Upload** (`Video Upload`, text, optional): Upload a video of your project.
* **Audio Upload** (`Audio Upload`, text, optional): Upload an audio file of your project.
* **Other File Upload** (`Other File Upload`, text, optional): Upload any other supporting file for your project.
* **Date of Submission** (`Date of Submission`, date, optional): Enter the date and time of submission.
* **Time of Submission** (`Time of Submission`, time, optional): Enter the time of submission.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your project.
* **Project Status** (`Project Status`, select one, optional): Select the current status of your project (e.g., active, inactive).
* **Science Project Report Upload** (`Science Project Report Upload`, text, optional): This field is used for uploading the project report.
* **Review** (`Review`, note, optional): Enter any review or feedback about your project.
