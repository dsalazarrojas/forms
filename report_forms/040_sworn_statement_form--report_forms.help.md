# Sworn Statement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sworn Statement Form is a crucial step in ensuring the objectivity and integrity of user input in our system. This form is designed to help users declare that they have not been influenced by any external sources while providing input.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required information in the provided fields.
2. Make sure to fill in all fields as accurately as possible.
3. Submit the form once you have filled in all necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### User Input

* **User Input** (user_input, text, optional): This field is where you can input any relevant information that you think may help us better understand your input. Please be as specific as possible while keeping it concise.
 
### User Data

* **User Data** (user_data, text, optional): This field is for any additional information that you think may be helpful for us to know about you.
 
### User Email

* **User Email** (user_email, email, optional): This field is to collect your email address for further communication or any updates from our end. Please ensure that you input your active and correct email address.
 
### User Phone

* **User Phone** (user_phone, text, optional): This field is for your phone number. Please input your active and correct phone number for any further communication.
 
### Form Submit

* **Form Submit** (form_submit, text, optional): This field is to confirm that you have filled in all necessary information accurately and honestly. Please type 'submit' to confirm.
 
### Tips

* Make sure to review all fields before submitting the form.
* Fill in all fields as accurately and truthfully as possible.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please contact us for further assistance.
