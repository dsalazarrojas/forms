# Bogging Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bogging Incident Report Form is designed to collect detailed information about a bogging incident, which includes the date and time of the incident, vehicle details, location, and circumstances of the incident, damage assessment, and rescue assistance required.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form with the required information**:
	* Ensure you provide accurate and detailed information about the incident.
	* Review each field carefully and ensure you have answered all required questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Report Date**: <date> (Required)
	* Enter the date of the report in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Report Time**: <time> (Required)
	* Enter the time of the report in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Reporter Name**: <text> (Required)
	* Enter the name of the person reporting the incident.
* **Reporter Title or Position**: <text> (Required)
	* Enter the title or position of the reporter.
* **Date of Incident**: <date> (Required)
	* Enter the date of the incident in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time of Incident**: <time> (Required)
	* Enter the time of the incident in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Vehicle Type**: <select> (Required)
	* Select the type of vehicle involved in the incident (Car, Light Truck, Heavy Truck, Motorbike, ATV, Equipment Vehicle, Other).
* **Vehicle Registration Number**: <text> (Required)
	* Enter the vehicle registration number.
* **Driver Name**: <text> (Required)
	* Enter the driver's name.
* **Driver Contact Number**: <text> (Required)
	* Enter the driver's contact number.
* **Location of Incident**: <text> (Required)
	* Enter the address or description of the location where the incident occurred.
* **GPS Coordinates if Available**: <text> (Optional)
	* If available, enter the GPS coordinates of the incident location.
* **Road Conditions**: <select> (Required)
	* Select the road conditions at the time of the incident (Sealed Road, Unsealed Road, Dirt Track, Muddy Conditions, Wet Conditions, Flooded Area, Soft Ground, Other).
* **Weather Conditions**: <select multiple> (Required)
	* Select all applicable weather conditions at the time of the incident (Clear, Rainy, Muddy, Wet, Windy, Foggy, Hot, Cold).
* **Estimated Speed When Bogged**: <number> (Optional)
	* Enter the estimated speed of the vehicle at the time of the incident.
* **Depth Vehicle Was Bogged**: <text> (Optional)
	* Enter the depth in inches or centimeters that the vehicle was bogged.
* **Apparent Cause of Bogging**: <select> (Required)
	* Select the apparent cause of the bogging (Soft Ground, Mud, Water, Sand, Snow or Ice, Rut or Hole, Equipment Failure, Driver Error, Unknown, Other).
* **Detailed Description of Cause**: <text> (Optional)
	* Enter a detailed description of the cause of the bogging.
* **Vehicle Damage Assessment**: <select> (Required)
	* Assess the damage to the vehicle (No Damage, Minor Damage, Moderate Damage, Severe Damage, Total Loss).
* **Description of Damage**: <text> (Optional)
	* Enter a detailed description of the damage.
* **Rescue Assistance Required**: <select> (Required)
	* Indicate if rescue assistance was required (Yes - Professional Recovery, Yes - Other Vehicles Helped, No - Self Recovered, Yes - Pending Assistance).
* **Recovery Method Used**: <text> (Optional)
	* Enter the recovery method used.
* **Estimated Recovery Cost**: <number> (Optional)
	* Enter the estimated recovery cost in dollars.
* **Any Injuries Reported**: <select> (Required)
	* Indicate if any injuries were reported (No Injuries, Minor Injuries, Serious Injuries, Fatalities).
* **Detailed Incident Description**: <text> (Required)
	* Enter a detailed description of the incident.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form.
* Use clear and concise language in all fields.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
* If unsure, contact your supervisor or a team member for clarification.
