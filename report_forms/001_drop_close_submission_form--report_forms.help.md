# Drop Close Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Drop Close Submission Form" is designed to gather information about the closure of a project or task. This form helps to ensure that all required details are collected accurately and consistently, making it easier for the operations team to review and process the submission.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the "Closure Date" field with the actual date of the project closure.
2. Enter the "Closure Time" in the format "HH:MM".
3. In the "Closure Details" section, select the correct option for the reason of closure.
4. In the "Logistics Details" section, provide any additional information about the logistics involved in the closure.
5. In the "Facilities Details" section, provide any relevant details about the facilities used or affected during the closure.
6. In the "Supporting Evidence" section, upload any supporting documents or evidence related to the closure.
7. In the "Operations Team" section, select "Yes" if an operations team member was involved in the closure.
8. In the "Facilities Team" section, select "Yes" if a facilities team member was involved in the closure.
9. In the "Logistics Team" section, select "Yes" if a logistics team member was involved in the closure.
10. In the "Operations Team Email" field, enter the email address of the operations team member responsible for the closure.
11. In the "Operations Team Phone" field, enter the phone number of the operations team member responsible for the closure.
12. In the "Submit" field, enter a brief summary of the submission.
13. Finally, review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Closure Date** (`page3`, `date`, required): Enter the actual date of the project closure in the format "MM/DD/YY".
* **Closure Time** (`page4`, `time`, required): Enter the closure time in the format "HH:MM".
* **Closure Details** (`page2`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the reason for the closure from the provided options.
* **Logistics Details** (`page5`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional details about the logistics involved in the closure.
* **Facilities Details** (`page6`, `text`, optional): Provide any relevant details about the facilities used or affected during the closure.
* **Supporting Evidence** (`page12`, `text`, required): Upload any supporting documents or evidence related to the closure.
* **Operations Team** (`page9`, `select_one`, optional): Select "Yes" if an operations team member was involved in the closure.
* **Facilities Team** (`page10`, `select_one`, optional): Select "Yes" if a facilities team member was involved in the closure.
* **Logistics Team** (`page11`, `select_one`, optional): Select "Yes" if a logistics team member was involved in the closure.
* **Operations Team Email** (`page13`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the operations team member responsible for the closure.
* **Operations Team Phone** (`page14`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the operations team member responsible for the closure.
* **Submit** (`page18`, `text`, required): Enter a brief summary of the submission.
