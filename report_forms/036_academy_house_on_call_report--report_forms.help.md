# Academy House On Call Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document on-call incidents, actions taken, and follow-up items for the reporting period. It's crucial for staff members to report accurately and thoroughly to maintain a safe and efficient working environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the following personal details:
	* **On-call staff member name**: Enter the name of the staff member who was on call during the shift.
	* **Email address**: Enter the email address of the on-call staff member.
	* **Contact phone number**: Enter the contact phone number of the on-call staff member.
2. Report the on-call shift details:
	* **Date of on-call shift**: Select the date of the on-call shift from the calendar.
	* **Shift start time**: Enter the start time of the on-call shift.
	* **Shift end time**: Enter the end time of the on-call shift.
	* **House or building name**: Enter the name of the house or building where the shift took place.
3. Document any incidents that occurred during the shift:
	* **Were there any incidents during the shift?**: Check "Yes" or "No" to indicate if any incidents occurred.
	* **Type of incident (select all that apply)**: Select all types of incidents that occurred during the shift.
	* **Time of incident**: Enter the time of the incident (if applicable).
	* **Detailed description of the incident**: Provide a detailed description of what happened, including who was involved and where.
	* **Actions taken in response**: Describe the actions taken in response to the incident.
	* **Who was contacted or notified**: List the names and roles of individuals who were contacted or notified about the incident.
4. Follow-up and observations:
	* **Is any follow-up action required?**: Check "Yes" or "No" to indicate if any follow-up actions are needed.
	* **If yes, describe the follow-up needed**: Provide a detailed description of the follow-up actions required.
	* **General observations or notes**: Enter any general observations or notes from the shift, such as student wellbeing or building conditions.
