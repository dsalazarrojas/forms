# Abdsrsm Daily Performance Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Abdsrsm Daily Performance Report" is a form used to evaluate an employee's performance during a shift, focusing on tasks completed, quality issues, safety incidents, and other key metrics. This report helps managers and supervisors assess an employee's performance, identify areas for improvement, and provide feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee full name and ID in the corresponding fields.
2. Select your department and shift from the provided options.
3. List all tasks completed during your shift, including any tasks still in progress or pending for the next shift.
4. Indicate if you achieved your daily targets or goals and describe any quality issues or incidents that occurred.
5. If there were any safety incidents, describe them in the designated field.
6. Report any equipment malfunctions or issues that affected your performance.
7. Provide any additional supervisor comments or remarks.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Employee full name
* **Employee full name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID.

### Employee ID
* **Employee ID**: Enter your unique employee ID number.

### Department
* **Department**: Select your department from the provided options: Operations, Maintenance, Safety, Administration, Logistics, or Other.

### Shift
* **Shift**: Select your shift from the provided options: Morning, Afternoon, or Night.

### List of tasks completed during the shift
* **List of tasks completed during the shift**: Describe all tasks you completed during your shift.

### Tasks still in progress or pending for the next shift
* **Tasks still in progress or pending for the next shift**: List any tasks not yet completed or pending for the next shift.

### Daily targets or goals achieved
* **Were daily targets or goals achieved**: Indicate if you achieved your daily targets or goals.

### Number of units produced or processed
* **Number of units produced or processed**: Enter the number of units produced or processed during your shift, or 0 if not applicable.

### Quality issues
* **Were any quality issues identified**: Indicate if you encountered any quality issues during your shift.

### Quality issue details
* **If quality issues were found, describe them**: Describe any quality issues that occurred during your shift.

### Safety incidents
* **Were there any safety incidents during the shift**: Indicate if there were any safety incidents during your shift.

### Incident details
* **If an incident occurred, provide details**: Describe any safety incidents that occurred during your shift.

### Equipment malfunctions or issues
* **Were there any equipment malfunctions or issues**: Indicate if you experienced any equipment malfunctions or issues during your shift.

### Equipment issue details
* **If yes, describe the equipment issue**: Describe any equipment malfunctions or issues that affected your performance.

### Supervisor remarks or comments
* **Supervisor remarks or comments**: Provide any additional comments or remarks from your supervisor.
