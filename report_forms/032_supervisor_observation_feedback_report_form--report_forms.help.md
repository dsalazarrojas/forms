# Supervisor Observation Feedback Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to facilitate the collection of observation feedback from supervisors, allowing staff to evaluate and assess their supervisor's performance. It is used to document the supervisor's strengths and areas for improvement, and to provide a quantitative rating of their performance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the form ID to associate this feedback with the relevant observation period.
2. Enter the supervisor's ID to identify the supervisor being observed.
3. Enter the staff ID to identify the staff member being observed.
4. Enter the date and time of the observation, using the "Date Observed" and "Time Observed" fields.
5. Describe the location where the observation took place.
6. Provide a brief description of the behavior observed during the feedback period.
7. Enter any positive comments about the supervisor's performance.
8. List any areas for improvement for the supervisor.
9. Rate the supervisor's performance on the following dimensions:
	* Supervisor Observation
	* Supervisor Quality
	* Adequacy of Support
	* Safety
	* Wellness
	* Equity

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form ID (1)**: Enter the unique identifier for this feedback report.
* **Supervisor ID (2)**: Enter the ID of the supervisor being observed.
* **Staff ID (3)**: Enter the ID of the staff member being observed.
* **Date Observed (4)**: Enter the date of the observation, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time Observed (5)**: Enter the time of the observation, in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Location Observed (6)**: Describe the location where the observation took place.
* **Behavior Observed (7)**: Describe the specific behavior observed during the feedback period.
* **Positive Comments (8)**: Enter any positive comments about the supervisor's performance.
* **Areas for Improvement (9)**: List any areas where the supervisor can improve their performance.
* **Rating Supervisor Observation (10)**: Rate the supervisor's performance on their ability to observe and assess.
* **Rating Supervisor Quality (11)**: Rate the supervisor's performance on their ability to provide high-quality feedback.
* **Rating Adequacy of Support (12)**: Rate the supervisor's ability to provide adequate support.
* **Rating Safety (13)**: Rate the supervisor's performance on maintaining a safe work environment.
* **Rating Wellness (14)**: Rate the supervisor's performance on maintaining a healthy work environment.
* **Rating Equity (15)**: Rate the supervisor's performance on promoting equity and inclusivity.
