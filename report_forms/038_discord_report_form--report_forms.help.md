# Discord Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Discord Report Form is designed to collect information from Discord users about a specific incident or issue they experienced. This form helps the community moderators to understand the context and details of the incident, allowing them to take appropriate action to resolve the issue.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your details, such as your name and email address.
2. Provide a brief description of the incident or issue you experienced.
3. If you have any attachments (e.g. screenshots, images, or files) related to the incident, you can upload them using the "Attachements" field.
4. Select "I agree to the terms" if you have read and understand the terms of service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reporter Name** (`report_form_reporter_name`, text, required): Your name, please.
* **Reporter Email** (`report_form_reporter_email`, email, required): Your email address, please.
* **Reporter Username** (`report_form_reporter_username`, text, required): Your Discord username, please.
* **Reporter Discord Tag** (`report_form_reporter_discord_tag`, text, required): Your Discord tag, please.
* **Reporter Message** (`report_form_reporter_message`, text, required): Please provide a brief description of the incident or issue you experienced.
* **Reported User Input** (`report_form_reported_user_input`, text, required): The username or ID of the user involved in the incident.
* **Reason** (`report_form_reason_other`, text, required): If you have a specific reason for the report, please provide it here.
* **Agree to Terms** (`report_form_agreed_terms`, select_one, required): Select "I agree to the terms" if you have read and understand the terms of service.
* **Additional Comments** (`report_form_additional_comments`, text, optional): Any additional comments or information you would like to provide.
* **Attachments** (`report_form_uploads`, text, optional): If you have any attachments (e.g. screenshots, images, or files) related to the incident, you can upload them here.
* **Attachment 1-13** (`report_form_uploads_file_1-13`, text, optional): You can upload up to 13 attachments if needed.

Note: Please ensure to fill in all the required fields to help the community moderators to resolve the issue effectively.
