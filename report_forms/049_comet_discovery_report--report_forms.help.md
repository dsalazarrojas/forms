# Comet Discovery Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Comet Discovery Report form is a tool used to collect data on comet observations from users. This form guides observers through the process of reporting their findings, including details such as observer name, observation date and time, location coordinates, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name as the observer in the "Observer name" field.
2. Select the date and time of observation using the "Observation date and time" field in the format YYYY-MM-DD and time.
3. Enter the location coordinates in the "Location coordinates" field in the format of latitude and longitude.
4. For the "Brightness magnitude" field, enter an estimate of the comet's apparent brightness.
5. The "Tail length estimate" field should be filled with a visual estimate of the comet's tail length in degrees.
6. If you used any instrument (e.g., telescope or binoculars) during observation, select it from the "Instrument used" field.
7. Indicate if photos were provided or available in the "Photos provided" field.
8. Add any additional observations or details about the comet in the "Additional observations" field.
9. If you wish to be contacted by the research team for further follow-up, enter your email address in the "Contact email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Observer name**: Enter your full name as the observer in this field.
- **Observation date and time**: Select the date and time of observation using the format YYYY-MM-DD and time.
- **Location coordinates**: Enter the latitude and longitude of the observation location.
- **Brightness magnitude**: Estimate the comet's apparent brightness in this field.
- **Tail length estimate**: Provide a visual estimate of the comet's tail length in degrees.
- **Instrument used**: If you used any instrument (e.g., telescope or binoculars) during observation, select it from this field.
- **Photos provided**: Indicate if photos were attached or are available for further study.
- **Additional observations**: Add any additional details or observations about the comet.
- **Contact email**: If you wish to be contacted by the research team for further follow-up, enter your email address in this field.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as "Required" in the form).
- If you have any doubts or questions, please reach out to the research team for further clarification.
- Ensure that the information provided is accurate and true to the best of your knowledge.
