# Student Device Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Device Check In Form is used to collect information about student devices used for educational purposes. This form helps to track and monitor student devices used in learning environments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct device type from the "User Device Type" dropdown menu.
2. Enter the serial number of the device.
3. Enter the MAC address of the device.
4. Enter the device configuration (optional).
5. Enter any additional notes about the device (optional).
6. Enter the student ID number.
7. Enter the user's name.
8. Select the current status of the device ("Available" or "Unavailable").
9. Enter the date and time the device was used.
10. Enter the user's location.
11. Enter the user's IP address.
12. Enter the user's browser type.
13. Enter the user's operating system.
14. Enter the user's browser version.
15. Enter the user's language.
16. Enter the user's country.
17. Enter the user's state.
18. Enter the user's city.
19. Enter the user's zip code.
20. Enter the region where the device is located.
21. Enter the make of the device.
22. Enter the model of the device.
23. Enter the type of the device.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, `text`, required): Enter a description of the device's purpose or the user's comment about the device.
* **User Device Type** (`user_device_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of device being used (e.g. laptop, tablet, phone).
* **Serial Number** (`serial_number`, `text`, required): Enter the serial number of the device.
* **MAC Address** (`mac_address`, `text`, required): Enter the MAC address of the device.
* **Device Configuration** (`device_config`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional configuration details about the device (e.g. operating system, browser type).
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the device.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, `number`, required): Enter the student ID number.
* **User Name** (`user_name`, `text`, required): Enter the user's name.
* **Device Status** (`device_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the current status of the device ("Available" or "Unavailable").
* **Date Used** (`date_used`, `date`, required): Enter the date and time the device was used.
* **Time Used** (`time_used`, `time`, required): Enter the time the device was used.
* **User Location** (`user_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location where the device is being used.
* **User IP Address** (`user_ip_address`, `text`, required): Enter the user's IP address.
* **User Browser** (`user_browser`, `text`, required): Enter the user's browser type.
* **User OS** (`user_os`, `text`, required): Enter the user's operating system.
* **User Browser Version** (`user_version`, `text`, required): Enter the user's browser version.
* **User Language** (`user_language`, `text`, required): Enter the user's language.
* **User Country** (`user_country`, `text`, required): Enter the user's country.
* **User State** (`user_state`, `text`, required): Enter the user's state.
* **User City** (`user_city`, `text`, required): Enter the user's city.
* **User Zip** (`user_zip`, `text`, required): Enter the user's zip code.
* **User Region** (`user_region`, `text`, required): Enter the user's region.
* **Device Make** (`user_device_make`, `text`, required): Enter the make of the device.
* **Device Model** (`user_device_model`, `text`, required): Enter the model of the device.
* **Device Type** (`user_device_type`, `text`, required): Enter the type of the device.
