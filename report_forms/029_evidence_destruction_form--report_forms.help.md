# evidence_destruction_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "evidence_destruction_form" is used to submit evidence for destruction. This form collects information about the submitter, including their contact details and logistical information. This form helps facilitate the submission and collection of evidence for destruction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your information:
	* **Submitter Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your badge or ID.
	* **Submitter Email**: Enter your work email address.
	* **Submitter Phone**: Enter your work phone number.
	* **Submitter Notes**: Enter any relevant notes or comments about the submission.
2. Choose the correct **Form Type** for your submission.
3. Enter the **Evidence Description**, **Quantity**, and **Weight** of the evidence being submitted.
4. Enter your **Submitter Department**, **Submitter Position**, and **Submitter Date** and **Submitter Time** of submission.
5. Complete the **Submitter Address** and **Submitter City**, **Submitter State**, and **Submitter Zip** fields with your contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submitter**: This is the name as it appears on your badge or ID.
* **Form Type**: Choose the type of form you are submitting (e.g. Option 1 or Option 2).
* **Evidence Description**: A brief description of the evidence being submitted.
* **Quantity**: The amount or quantity of the evidence being submitted.
* **Weight**: The weight or size of the evidence being submitted.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter your work email address.
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter your work phone number.
* **Submitter Notes**: Enter any relevant notes or comments about the submission.
* **Submitter Department**: Enter your department name.
* **Submitter Position**: Enter your position or title.
* **Submitter Date**: Enter the date of submission.
* **Submitter Time**: Enter the time of submission.
* **Submitter Address**: Enter your work address.
* **Submitter City**: Enter your city or town.
* **Submitter State**: Enter your state or province.
* **Submitter Zip**: Enter your postal code or zip code.

## Tips

* Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or IT department.
* This form should not be used for any other purpose other than submitting evidence for destruction.
