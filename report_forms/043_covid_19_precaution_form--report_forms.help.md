# COVID 19 Precaution Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help organizations report their COVID-19 precautions and measures to ensure public health and safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your organization's name and type (e.g., Business, Healthcare facility, School or University, Government office, Community center, or Other).
2. Enter the location or address of your organization.
3. Provide the date of the report.
4. Enter the name of the person submitting the report.
5. Check the COVID-19 precautions and measures that are currently in place, such as mask requirements, social distancing markers, handwashing stations, and so on.
6. Indicate whether staff have been trained on COVID-19 safety protocols.
7. Report if masks have been implemented among staff members (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
8. Confirm if enhanced cleaning protocols are in place.
9. Report if there have been any COVID-19 cases or exposure incidents reported in the past 30 days.
10. If cases or incidents have been reported, provide the number of cases or details of incidents.
11. Confirm if a communication plan has been established to inform staff and the public of any updates or concerns.
12. Indicate the frequency of updates or communication provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Organization Name** (Organization Name, text, required): Enter your organization's name.
- **Organization Type** (Organization Type, select_one, required): Select the type of organization you represent (e.g., Business, Healthcare facility, School or University, Government office, Community center, or Other).
- **Location or Address** (Location, text, required): Enter the address or location of your organization.
- **Report Date** (Report Date, date, required): Enter the date of the report.
- **Reporting Person Name** (Reporting Person Name, text, required): Enter the name of the person submitting the report.
- **COVID-19 Precautions in Place** (COVID-19 Precautions in Place, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable COVID-19 precautions and measures in place, such as mask requirements, social distancing markers, handwashing stations, etc.
- **Staff Trained on COVID-19 Safety** (Staff Trained on COVID-19 Safety, select_one, required): Confirm if staff members have received training on COVID-19 safety protocols.
- **Mask Compliance** (Mask Compliance, select_one, required): Report the level of mask compliance among staff members (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
- **Enhanced Cleaning Protocol** (Enhanced Cleaning Protocol, select_one, required): Confirm if enhanced cleaning protocols are in place.
- **COVID-19 Cases Reported** (COVID-19 Cases Reported, select_one, required): Report if COVID-19 cases have been reported in the past 30 days.
- **Number of Cases** (Number of Cases, number, optional): If cases were reported, enter the number of cases.
- **Exposure Incidents** (Exposure Incidents, select_one, required): Report if exposure incidents have been reported in the past 30 days.
- **Details of Incidents** (Details of Incidents, text, optional): If incidents were reported, provide details of these incidents.
- **Communication Plan** (Communication Plan, select_one, required): Confirm if a communication plan has been established to inform staff and the public of any updates or concerns.
- **Frequency of Updates** (Frequency of Updates, select_one, optional): Report the frequency of updates or communication provided.
- **Additional Notes** (Additional Notes, text, optional): Provide any additional notes or precautions that your organization has taken or is concerned about.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are filled in thoroughly and accurately.
- Review and confirm all information before submitting the form.
- Report all incidents and cases accurately and truthfully.
