# Food Inventory Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Inventory Form is used to track and record information related to food inventory, including quantity ordered, received, and in use, as well as reorder points and frequencies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the category and subcategory of the food item from the corresponding dropdown menus.
2. Enter the quantity ordered and received for the item.
3. Enter the quantity in use and the quantity on hand.
4. Set the reorder point and frequency for the item.
5. Enter the last 3 months' ordered and received quantities.
6. Set the reorder frequency for the last 3 months.
7. Enter any notes for the item.
8. Select "Yes" or "No" for supplier.
9. Enter the last reorder date if applicable.
10. Repeat the above steps for each food item.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Food Inventory**: This is the category for the food item. Select the relevant category from the dropdown menu.
* **Item**: This is the subcategory for the food item. Select the relevant subcategory from the dropdown menu.
* **Quantity Ordered**: Enter the quantity of the food item ordered.
* **Quantity Received**: Enter the quantity of the food item received.
* **Quantity In Use**: Enter the current quantity of the food item in use. (Optional)
* **Quantity On Hand**: Enter the current quantity of the food item on hand.
* **Reorder Point**: Set the reorder point for the food item.
* **Reorder Frequency**: Set the reorder frequency for the food item.
* **Quantity Low Point**: Set the low point quantity for the food item.
* **Quantity Ordered Last 3 Months**: Enter the total quantity ordered in the last 3 months.
* **Quantity In Last 3 Months**: Enter the total quantity in use in the last 3 months.
* **Inventory Category**: Select the category for the food item. Choose from the dropdown menu.
* **Inventory Subcategory**: Select the subcategory for the food item. Choose from the dropdown menu.
* **Inventory Subcategory 2**: Select the subcategories (if applicable) for the food item. Choose from the dropdown menu.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes for the food item.
* **Date Ordered**: Enter the date the food item was ordered.
* **Date Received**: Enter the date the food item was received.
* **Date Low Point**: Enter the date the food item reached its low point.
* **Supplier**: Select "Yes" or "No" if the food item has a supplier.
* **Reorder Frequency Last 3 Months**: Enter the reorder frequency for the last 3 months.
* **Notes Last 3 Months**: Enter any additional notes for the last 3 months.
* **Notes 3 Months Ago**: Enter any additional notes for 3 months prior.
* **Last Reorder Date**: Enter the date the food item was last reordered.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Use the correct inventory category and subcategory for each food item.
* Enter accurate quantities for ordered, received, and in use.
* Set the correct reorder points and frequencies.
* Enter accurate dates for ordered, received, and low points.
* Use the notes field for any additional information.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all information is accurate and complete.
