# Infectious Disease Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about infectious diseases reported by medical providers and facilities. It's crucial to fill out this form accurately to help prevent the spread of diseases and ensure proper reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information about the patient before starting.
2. Fill out the required fields (indicated by *) as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
3. Review and verify the information you've entered before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First and Last Name**: Please enter the patient's complete name, including their first and last name. (Patient Name) (Text) (*)
* **Medical Provider**: Enter the name of the medical provider who reported the disease. (Medical Provider) (Text) (*)
* **Onset Date**: Select the date when the disease was first detected. (Onset Date) (Date) (*)
* **Disease Name**: Specify the name of the disease reported. (Disease Name) (Text) (*)
* **Symptoms**: Provide a detailed description of the patient's symptoms. (Symptoms) (Note) (*)
* **Contact Number**: Enter the patient's contact number for communication purposes. (Contact Number) (Text) (optional)
* **Email Address**: Enter the patient's email address for further communication. (Email Address) (Email) (optional)
* **Reporting Health Care Provider**: Select the name of the healthcare provider who reported the disease. (Reporting Health Care Provider) (Multiple Selection) (optional)
* **Reporting Facility**: Select the name of the facility where the disease was reported. (Reporting Facility) (Single Selection) (optional)
* **Reporting Date**: Enter the date of reporting. (Reporting Date) (Date) (optional)
* **Reporting Time**: Enter the time of reporting. (Reporting Time) (Time) (optional)
* **Signature**: Enter your signature as proof of reporting. (Signature) (Note) (optional)
* **Age**: Enter the patient's age. (Age) (Number) (optional)

## Tips
* Make sure to enter all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check the information you've entered to avoid errors.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact your administrator for guidance.
