# Volunteer Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Report Form is used to gather information from volunteers about their activities and hours worked. It helps us understand their contributions and impact.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you are only reporting on activities that have not been previously reported on.
2. If you are unsure or have any questions, please reach out to us for assistance.
3. Fill out the form with the required and optional fields as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Report Form** (`volunteer_report`, text, optional): This is the main title of the report.
* **Hours Worked** (`hours_worked`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours you have worked on the reported activity.
* **Impact Activities** (`impact_activities`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the activities that you have contributed to, selecting multiple options if applicable.
* **Date Last Report** (`date_last_report`, date, optional): Enter the date of the last report you have made, if any.

## Tips
* Please note that all fields are optional unless marked as required. However, please report accurately and honestly.
