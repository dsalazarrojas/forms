# Data Integrity Compliance Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report and track incidents related to data integrity compliance. It helps ensure that incidents are properly reported and documented, and that necessary actions are taken to rectify the situation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (denoted by *) with your name, email, phone number, incident type, and incident status.
2. Enter the incident date and time in the format MM/DD/YYYY HH:MM.
3. Provide a detailed description of the incident in the incident description field.
4. Select the appropriate incident category and subcategory.
5. Check with the "Checked By" field to identify who checked the incident.
6. Enter any notes or comments as necessary.
7. Review and update the form as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name for reporting purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name for reporting purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of incident (Data Loss, Security Breach, Policy Violation, or Other).
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the incident in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the incident in the format HH:MM.
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, note, optional): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **Checked By** (`checked_by`, select_one, optional): Select who checked the incident (IT, Security, or Compliance Team).
* **Checked By User** (`checked_by_user`, select_one, optional): Select the user who checked the incident.
* **Action Taken** (`action_taken`, text, optional): Enter any actions taken to address the incident.
* **Action Date** (`action_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the action taken in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Action Time** (`action_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the action taken in the format HH:MM.
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the incident (Active, Resolved, or Closed).
* **Incident Notes** (`incident_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the incident.
* **Checked By Manager** (`checked_by_manager`, select_one, optional): Select who checked the incident with the manager.
* **Incident Comments** (`incident_comments`, note, optional): Enter any comments or notes about the incident.
* **Action Comments** (`action_comments`, note, optional): Enter any comments or notes about the action taken.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, note, optional): Enter any comments or notes from the manager.
* **Incident Category** (`incident_category`, select_one, optional): Select the category of the incident (Data Loss, Security Breach, or Policy Violation).
* **Incident Subcategory** (`incident_subcategory`, select_one, optional): Select the subcategory of the incident (Yes or No).
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of the incident.
* **Manager Status** (`manager_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the incident with the manager (Active, Resolved, or Closed).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Review and update the form as needed.
* Use the notes field to provide additional comments or information.
