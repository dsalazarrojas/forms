# Covid 19 Preliminary Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect preliminary information and data related to COVID-19 cases. The form gathers patient information, vaccination details, and follow-up schedules for patients with COVID-19. It is intended for healthcare providers to record and track patient data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the required information in each field.
2. Provide accurate and detailed answers to each question.
3. Make sure to fill out every field marked as "required" (indicated by a red asterisk \*).
4. Use the provided hints to help guide your answers.
5. Review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information* (1, text, required):** Enter the patient's name, age, and contact information.
* **Vaccine Details (2, text, required):** Record the type and dosage of the vaccine administered.
* **Vaccine Administration (3, text, required):** Specify the date and time of the vaccine administration.
* **Vaccine Efficacy (4, text, required):** Describe the patient's response to the vaccine, including any side effects.
* **Follow Up Schedule (5, text, required):** Set up the follow-up schedule for the patient, including date and time of next appointment.
* **Vaccine Side Effects (6, text, required):** Document any side effects experienced by the patient after vaccination.
* **Patient Outcome (7, text, required):** Record the patient's overall condition or outcome after vaccination.
* **Medical History (8, text, required):** Provide a brief medical history of the patient, including previous medical conditions.
* **Next Steps (9, text, required):** Outline the next steps or follow-up actions for the patient.
* **Follow-up (10, text, required):** Record any follow-up actions or appointments scheduled.
* **Patient Comments (11, text, required):** Allow the patient to provide any comments or feedback.
* **Clinician Comments (12, text, required):** Enable the clinician to add any comments or notes.
* **Date (13, date, required):** Enter the date of the form completion.
* **Time (14, time, required):** Record the time of the form completion.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check the accuracy of the information entered.
* Review and update the form regularly to reflect any changes in the patient's condition.
* Use this form as a template to track multiple patient cases.
* Contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for any questions or concerns.
