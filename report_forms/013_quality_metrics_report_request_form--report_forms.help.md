# Quality Metrics Report Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect relevant information for quality metrics reporting. It helps quality teams and managers to submit requests for specific reports that contain performance indicators such as dates, scores, and categories.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To submit a report request, start by selecting the type of report you need. Choose between "Quality Report" and "Business Report" to ensure that your request is processed correctly.
2.  Enter the date you are requesting the report for.
3.  If you need to schedule the report for a specific time, enter that here.
4.  If you have a quality score you'd like to report, enter it here.
5.  If you have a specific metric name for your report, enter it here.
6.  Enter the value of the metric if you have one.
7.  If you'd like to report a category for your metric, enter it here.
8.  Provide a brief description of your report request, including any additional information that would be helpful for the report.
9.  Enter your contact details, including your email and a phone number if necessary.
10.  Optionally provide your address to receive reports or follow-up information.
10.  Review and submit your report request.
