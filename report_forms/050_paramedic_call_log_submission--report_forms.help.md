# Paramedic Call Log Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Paramedic Call Log Submission form is designed to collect information about paramedic calls, including incident dates, patient details, interventions, and outcomes. This form will help paramedics document their calls in a standard format, making it easier to review and analyze incident data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the incident date of the call.
2. Enter the patient's name.
3. Select the type of call (Ambulance, Fire, or Police).
4. Choose the type of incident (Injury, Illness, or Other).
5. Choose the patient's details (Age, Sex, or Other).
6. Enter the details of the intervention performed.
7. Select the type of intervention (First Aid, Medical, or Trauma).
8. Choose the outcomes of the call (Discharged, Hospitalized, or Deceased).
9. Enter any additional notes about the call.
10. Select the status of the call (Complete, Incomplete, or In Progress).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Date (1)**: The date of the paramedic call.
* **Patient Name (2)**: The name of the patient involved in the call.
* **Call Type (3)**: The type of call made by the paramedic (Ambulance, Fire, or Police).
* **Incident Type (4)**: The type of incident that occurred during the call (Injury, Illness, or Other).
* **Patient Details (5)**: The patient's details, such as Age or Sex.
* **Intervention (6)**: A description of the intervention performed by the paramedic.
* **Intervention Type (7)**: The type of intervention (First Aid, Medical, or Trauma).
* **Outcomes (8)**: The outcome of the call (Discharged, Hospitalized, or Deceased).
* **Notes (9)**: Any additional comments or notes about the call.
* **Call Status (10)**: The status of the call (Complete, Incomplete, or In Progress).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in the incident date accurately.
* Select the correct call type and incident type to ensure accurate data collection.
* Provide clear and concise notes about the call.
* Review and update the call status regularly to reflect the current status of the call.
