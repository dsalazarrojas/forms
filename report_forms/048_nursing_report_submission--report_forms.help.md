# nursing_report_submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The nursing report submission form is designed to collect essential information from healthcare professionals regarding patient care and vital signs, medical history, medications, and other relevant details. This form helps ensure accurate and comprehensive documentation of patient treatment and care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1.  Select the relevant patient information and patient ID from the provided options.
2.  Provide accurate and detailed information in the respective fields for:
    *   Shift Details
    *   Patient Info
    *   Observations
    *   Interventions
    *   Vital Signs
    *   Medications
    *   Allergies
    *   Medical History
    *   Physical Exam
    *   Laboratory Results
    *   Radiology Results
    *   Medical Imaging (optional)
    *   Other (optional)
    *   Signature
    *   Date and Time of submission
3.  Choose the correct nurse ID from the options.
4.  Select the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Shift Details**: Provide the shift details of the nursing professional who is submitting the report.
*   **Patient Info**: Enter the patient's information, including their name, relevant details, and any other essential information.
*   **Observations**: Document any observations made during the patient's care, including any relevant medical conditions or concerns.
*   **Interventions**: Describe any interventions made during the patient's care, including any treatments or actions taken.
*   **Vital Signs**: Enter the patient's vital signs, such as temperature, blood pressure, pulse, and respiratory rate.
*   **Medications**: Select the medications administered to the patient.
*   **Allergies**: Choose any allergies the patient has.
*   **Medical History**: Document the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses or conditions.
*   **Physical Exam**: Describe the patient's physical exam, including any notable findings.
*   **Laboratory Results**: Enter any laboratory results, such as blood test or imaging results.
*   **Radiology Results** (optional): Enter any radiology results, such as X-ray or MRI results.
*   **Medical Imaging** (optional): Enter any medical imaging results.
*   **Other** (optional): Provide any additional information not captured in the other fields.
*   **Signature**: The nurse's signature confirms that the report is accurate and complete.
*   **Date**: Enter the date the report is submitted.
*   **Time**: Enter the time the report is submitted.
*   **Phone**: Enter the patient's contact phone number.
*   **Email**: Enter the patient's contact email.
*   **Fax**: Enter the patient's fax number (if applicable).
*   **Address**: Enter the patient's address.
*   **Unit**: Enter the unit or department where the patient is being cared for.
*   **Location**: Enter the location where the patient is being cared for.
*   **Patient ID**: Select the correct patient ID from the provided options.
*   **Nurse ID**: Select the correct nurse ID from the provided options.
*   **Submit**: Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit the report.

Note: This guide is not intended to be a substitute for medical training or advice. If you have questions or concerns about any information on this form, consult with your supervisor or healthcare provider.
