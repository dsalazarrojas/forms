# Service Report Rite Price - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a service report, including the service details and the price charged for the service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the customer's name and email in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the start and finish dates for the service.
3. Enter the start and finish times for the service.
4. Select the type of service provided (e.g., A/C Maintenance, A/C Installation, A/C Repair).
5. Enter a brief description of the service.
6. Enter the rite price for the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Report Rite Price Page** (`service_report_rite_price_page`, text, required): This is the title of the form.
* **customer name** (`customer name`, text, required): Enter the name of the customer who received the service.
* **customer email** (`customer email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the customer who received the service.
* **service start date** (`service start date`, date, required): Enter the date on which the service started.
* **service start time** (`service start time`, time, required): Enter the time at which the service started.
* **service finish date** (`service finish date`, date, required): Enter the date on which the service finished.
* **service finish time** (`service finish time`, time, required): Enter the time at which the service finished.
* **technician name** (`technician name`, text, required): Enter the name of the technician who provided the service.
* **technician email** (`technician email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the technician who provided the service.
* **service description** (`service description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the service provided.
* **rite price** (`rite price`, number, required): Enter the price charged for the service.
* **service type** (`service type`, select_one, required): Select the type of service provided from the options A/C Maintenance, A/C Installation, or A/C Repair.
