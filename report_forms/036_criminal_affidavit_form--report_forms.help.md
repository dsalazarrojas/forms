# Criminal Affidavit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Criminal Affidavit Form is used by law enforcement officers to create and manage reports and cases related to criminal activity. It captures essential information about the police case number, case date, suspect name and address, suspect contact information, police officer's name and station, description of the crime, witness statement, and the current status of the case.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the **police case number**, a unique identifier for the case.
2. Provide the **case date**, the date the case was created or updated.
3. Enter the **suspect name** of the individual involved in the crime.
4. Enter the **suspect address**, the location of the suspect's residence.
5. (Optional) Enter the **suspect contact information** if available.
6. Enter the **police officer name** and **police station** responsible for the case.
7. Provide a detailed **crime description**, a summary of the incident.
8. Record the **witness statement**, if available.
9. Select the current **police case status**, whether the case is active or inactive.
10. (Optional) Add any additional **police case notes** or **attachments** relevant to the case.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **police_case_number**: Enter a unique number assigned to the case.
* **police_case_date**: Enter the date the case was created or last updated.
* **suspect_name**: Enter the name of the individual involved in the crime.
* **suspect_address**: Enter the address of the suspect's residence.
* **suspect_contact** (optional): Enter any contact information for the suspect, if available.
* **police_officer_name**: Enter the name of the police officer handling the case.
* **police_station**: Enter the name of the police station where the case is being handled.
* **crime_description**: Enter a detailed description of the crime.
* **witness_statement**: Enter a statement from a witness, if available.
* **police_case_status**: Select the current status of the case, either active or inactive.
* **police_case_notes** (optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the case.
* **police_case_attachment** (optional): Attach any relevant files or documents to the case.
* **police_witness_statement_attachment** (optional): Attach any witness statements or supporting documents.
