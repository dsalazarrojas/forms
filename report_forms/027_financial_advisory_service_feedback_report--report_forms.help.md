# Financial Advisory Service Feedback Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from clients who have received financial advisory services. It helps us assess their satisfaction with the service provided and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the client's name in the `Client Name` field.
2. Choose the date of the advice session from the calendar.
3. Select the outcome of the session by choosing one of the options provided.
4. Rate the quality of the service provided on a scale of 1-5, answering each rating question individually (Rating 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the client's name to identify the individual receiving the financial advisory service.
* **Date Of Advice Session** (`date_of_advice_session`, `date`, optional): Choose the date of the advice session from the calendar to provide a specific timeframe for the feedback.
* **Session Outcome** (`session_outcome`, `select_one`, optional): Select the outcome of the session by choosing one of the provided options: Satisfied, Unsatisfied, or Neutral.
* **Rating 1** (`rating_1`, `number`, optional): Rate the quality of the service provided on a scale of 1-5. 
* **Rating 2** (`rating_2`, `number`, optional): Rate the quality of the service provided on a scale of 1-5.
* **Rating 3** (`rating_3`, `number`, optional): Rate the quality of the service provided on a scale of 1-5.
* **Rating 4** (`rating_4`, `number`, optional): Rate the quality of the service provided on a scale of 1-5.
* **Rating 5** (`rating_5`, `number`, optional): Rate the quality of the service provided on a scale of 1-5.

Note: The ratings are not cumulative, meaning that each rating question should be answered individually on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
