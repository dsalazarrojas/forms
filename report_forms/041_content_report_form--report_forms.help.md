# Content Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about issues with content on the platform.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields, which are marked as such in the form.
2. Provide as much detail as possible in the free text fields.
3. If you have screenshots, please attach them to support your report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (id: 1): Please enter your full name to help us identify you.
* **Email for Follow-up** (id: 2): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further information or assistance.
* **Report Date** (id: 3): The date when you noticed the issue.
* **Content URL** (id: 4): The URL of the content being reported.
* **Content Title** (id: 5): The title of the content being reported.
* **Report Category** (id: 6): Select the nature of your report, such as Inaccurate Information or Broken Links.
* **Issue Description** (id: 7): Describe the issue in detail.
* **Issue Location** (id: 8): Specify where the issue is located.
* **Severity Level** (id: 9): Indicate the severity of the issue, such as Low, Medium, High, or Critical.
* **First Observed** (id: 10): The date when you first noticed the issue (optional).
* **Affected Users** (id: 11): Indicate who is affected by this issue, such as All Users or Specific User Group.
* **Browser Used** (id: 12): The browser you were using when you encountered the issue (optional).
* **Device Used** (id: 13): The device you were using when you encountered the issue (optional).
* **Screenshots Attached** (id: 14): If you have screenshots to support your report, select "Yes" (optional).
* **Suggested Fix** (id: 15): If you have a suggestion for fixing the issue, enter it here (optional).
* **Similar Issues** (id: 16): If you have seen similar issues elsewhere, describe them here (optional).
* **Impact Description** (id: 17): Explain how the issue affects users.
* **Urgency** (id: 18): Indicate how quickly the issue should be addressed.
* **Willing to Help** (id: 19): If you are willing to assist in resolving the issue, select "Yes" (optional).
* **Additional Comments** (id: 20): Any other information you think would be helpful for us to know.
