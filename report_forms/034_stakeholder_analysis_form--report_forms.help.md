# Stakeholder Analysis Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stakeholder Analysis Form is designed to help project managers and team leaders to identify and analyze the key stakeholders involved in a project, their interests, influences, and capacities. This form serves as a tool to prevent or mitigate potential conflicts and ensure that all stakeholders are engaged and informed throughout the project lifecycle.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review your project and identify the stakeholders who will be affected by the project outcome.
2. Enter their names and classifications in the "Stakeholders" field.
3. Evaluate their influence on the project by selecting their power and interest levels.
4. Assess their capacity for action and ability to influence project outcomes.
5. Provide any additional comments or notes on their relationships and other relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. What is Stakeholder Analysis**: This field should be used to briefly describe the purpose and scope of the project, including the expected outcomes and any relevant background information.
* **2. Background Information**: This field is intended for any additional context or history about the project that may be relevant for the analysis.
* **3. Objectives of the Project**: In this field, please list the main objectives of the project, including any specific goals or deliverables.
* **4. Stakeholder Classification**: Select all stakeholders that apply from the list of available options. This will help you identify the different types of stakeholders involved in the project.
* **5. Stakeholder Relationships**: Describe the relationships between stakeholders, including their roles, responsibilities, and any potential conflicts.
* **6. Stakeholder Influence**: Evaluate the level of influence each stakeholder has on the project, selecting one option that best describes their influence.
* **7. Stakeholder Power**: Provide a numerical value indicating the level of power each stakeholder has in the project.
* **8. Stakeholder Interest**: Describe the level of interest each stakeholder has in the project, including their potential level of engagement.
* **9. Stakeholder Mission**: Explain how each stakeholder contributes to the project goals and objectives.
* **10. Stakeholder Capacity**: Assess each stakeholder's capacity for action, including their ability to influence project outcomes.
* **11. Comments**: Use this field for any additional notes or comments about each stakeholder, including any concerns or observations.

Note: This form is not intended to be a comprehensive or exhaustive tool, but rather a starting point for further analysis and discussion. It is recommended to adapt this form according to the specific needs and requirements of your project.
