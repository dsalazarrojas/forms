# Asset Disposal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Asset Disposal Form is used to document the formal disposal of company assets. It ensures that all relevant information is captured accurately and follows established procedures to maintain asset management records.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, as required fields are marked with a red asterisk \*.
2. Enter the request date (step 1) when the asset disposal request was made.
3. Enter the requester's name (step 2) and department (step 3) accurately.
4. Fill in the asset's details (steps 4-7), including its unique identifier (step 4), name (step 5), category (step 6), and original purchase date (step 8).
5. Determine the reason (step 10) for the asset's disposal from the available options.
6. Briefly describe the reason for disposal (step 11) and how the asset will be disposed (step 12).
7. If the disposed asset is electronic, confirm whether a data wipe (step 17) and data wipe certificate (step 20) is required.
8. Ensure you receive approval from all required departments (steps 20-22).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. Request Date**: Enter the date when the disposal request was made. This ensures that all relevant information is captured accurately and follows established procedures.
* **2. Requester Name**: Enter the name of the person requesting the disposal. This helps identify the responsible individual for the asset disposal process.
* **3. Department**: Enter the department that requested the disposal. This ensures the asset is properly accounted for within the department's assets.
* **4. Asset ID Number**: Enter the unique identifier for the asset being disposed. This helps track the asset's history and ensures accurate disposal records.
* **5. Asset Name**: Enter a brief description of the asset being disposed. This helps identify the asset and ensure its accurate disposal.
* **6. Asset Category**: Select the type of asset from the available options. This helps categorize the asset for disposal and asset management purposes.
* **7. Serial Number**: If applicable, enter the manufacturer's serial number for the asset. This provides detailed information for the asset.
* **8. Original Purchase Date**: Enter the date the asset was initially purchased. This tracks the asset's acquisition history.
* **9. Original Purchase Cost**: Enter the price paid for the asset. This is essential for financial records and asset management.
* **10. Reason for Disposal**: Select the reason for disposing the asset from the available options. This ensures that all asset disposals follow established procedures.
* **11. Detailed Description**: Enter a brief explanation for the reason of disposal. This helps justify the asset's disposal.
* **12. Proposed Disposal Method**: Select the method of disposal from the available options. This ensures that the asset is disposed of in a manner that aligns with company policies.
* **13. Estimated Disposal Value**: Enter the expected recovery value of the asset. This helps with financial considerations.
* **14. Estimated Disposal Cost**: Enter the estimated cost for disposing the asset. This ensures accurate financial planning.
* **15. Environmental Concerns**: Confirm if there are any environmental concerns related to the asset's disposal.
* **16. Environmental Details**: Provide a description of the environmental concerns. This helps address any specific issues related to disposal.
* **17. Is Data Wipe Required**: Confirm whether a data wipe is required for electronic devices. This ensures sensitive data is properly erased.
* **18. Data Wipe Certificate Required**: Confirm if a data wipe certificate is required for the asset. This ensures proper documentation.
* **19. Department Head Approval**: Ensure the department head approves the asset disposal. This confirms departmental approval.
* **20. Finance Department Approval**: Ensure the finance department approves the asset disposal. This confirms financial considerations.
* **21. Management Approval**: Confirm management approval for the asset disposal. This finalizes the disposal process.
* **22. Disposal Completed Date**: Enter the date when the asset was disposed. This captures the completion date for asset management records.
* **23. Disposal Notes**: Enter final notes regarding the asset disposal. This provides a record of the disposal process.
* **24. Department Head Approval**: Confirm if the department head has approved the asset disposal. This ensures all departments agree on the disposal.
* **25. Disposal Completed Date**: Enter the date when the asset was disposed. This captures the completion date for asset management records.

## Tips
* Always ensure that you've completed all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check the asset's details and ensure they are accurate.
* Follow established procedures for asset disposal, including obtaining necessary approvals.
* Review the form before submitting it to ensure all information is complete and accurate.
