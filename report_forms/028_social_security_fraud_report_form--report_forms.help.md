# Social Security Fraud Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to report cases of suspected social security fraud. Please use this form to provide detailed information about the case, including the suspect's name, contact details, and IP addresses. Your input will help us investigate and prevent such fraudulent activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information about the suspect.
2. Provide a clear description of the fraud case in the "Description" field.
3. Enter your contact information in the fields provided.
4. Double-check your input for any errors or missing information.
5. Click the "Submit" button to send the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the person reporting the fraud case.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Provide your phone number so we can contact you for further questions or verification.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Suspect Name** (`suspect_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person suspected of social security fraud.
* **Suspect Phone** (`suspect_phone`, text, required): Enter the suspect's phone number.
* **Suspect Email** (`suspect_email`, email, required): Enter the suspect's email address.
* **Fraud Date** (`fraud_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the fraud occurred or is suspected to have occurred.
* **Fraud Time** (`fraud_time`, time, required): Enter the time when the fraud occurred or is suspected to have occurred.
* **Description** (`fraud_description`, text, required): Provide a clear and detailed description of the fraud case.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the person submitting the report.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Company** (`submitter_company`, text, required): Enter your company or organization name (if applicable).
* **Submitter Title** (`submitter_title`, text, required): Enter your title or position.
* **Submitter Department** (`submitter_department`, text, required): Enter your department or team name (if applicable).
* **Submitter Position** (`submitter_position`, text, required): Enter your position or job title.
* **Submitter Location** (`submitter_location`, text, required): Enter your location or office address.
* **Submitter Comment** (`submitter_comment`, text, required): Provide any additional comments or information about the report.
* **Submitter IP** (`submitter_ip`, text, required): Enter your IP address.
* **Submitter User Agent** (`submitter_user_agent`, text, required): Enter your user agent (if applicable).
* **Suspect IP** (`suspect_ip`, text, required): Enter the suspect's IP address.
* **Suspect User Agent** (`suspect_user_agent`, text, required): Enter the suspect's user agent (if applicable).
* **Referral Source** (`submitter_referral_source`, text, required): Enter the source of the referral (e.g. email, phone, etc.).
* **Referral URL** (`submitter_referral_url`, text, required): Enter the URL of the referral (if applicable).
* **Submitter Notes** (`submitter_notes`, text, required): Provide any additional notes or information about the report.
