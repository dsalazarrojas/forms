# suspected_child_abuse_and_neglect_report_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Suspected Child Abuse and Neglect Report Form is designed to collect information from individuals who have witnessed or suspect child abuse or neglect. This form helps to gather details about the incident, including the date and time of the report, the location where the incident occurred, and any relevant information about the suspect and witnesses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the report details, including the date and location of the incident.
2. Enter the time of the report (if applicable).
3. Describe the suspect(s) involved in the incident.
4. Provide information about any witnesses to the incident.
5. Write a brief summary of the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Form Details**: 
    - **Report Form Details** (`report_form_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the incident, including any relevant details about the child's condition or situation.
* **Report Date**: 
    - **Report Date** (`report_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the report. This can be the date the report was completed or the date the incident occurred.
* **Report Time**: 
    - **Report Time** (`report_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the report, if applicable.
* **Report Location**: 
    - **Report Location** (`report_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Suspect Info**: 
    - **Suspect Info** (`suspect_info`, text, optional): Enter any information about the suspect(s) involved in the incident.
* **Witness Info**: 
    - **Witness Info** (`witness_info`, text, optional): Enter any information about witnesses to the incident.
* **Report Summary**: 
    - **Report Summary** (`report_summary`, text, optional): Enter a brief summary of the incident.
* **Suspect DOB**: 
    - **Suspect DOB** (`suspect_dob`, date, optional): Enter the date of birth of the suspect, if known.
