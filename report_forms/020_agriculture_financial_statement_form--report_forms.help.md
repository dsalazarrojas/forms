# Agriculture Financial Statement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect financial data from agricultural businesses. It gathers information on income and expenses, and assets and liabilities to provide a snapshot of a business's financial health.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your business name and the fiscal year you are reporting for.
2.  Select the period you are reporting (Quarter or Annual).
3.  Enter your total revenue from crop sales.
4.  Enter your total revenue from livestock sales.
5.  Enter any government payments you have received (such as subsidies or disaster relief).
6.  Enter any insurance proceeds you have received (such as crop insurance payouts).
7.  Fill in your operating expenses:
    *   Seeds and plants (cost of inputs)
    *   Fertilizer and chemicals (soil amendments and protection)
    *   Labor hired (wages and benefits)
    *   Fuel and oil (diesel, gas, lubricants)
    *   Repairs and maintenance (machinery and buildings)
8.  Enter the current market value of your land.
9.  Enter the depreciated value of your machinery.
10. Enter the total amount of loan liabilities you have outstanding.
11.  Calculate your net cash income (this will be calculated manually).
12.  Certify that the numbers you have entered are accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Business Name (2)**: This is the name of your agricultural business.
*   **Fiscal Year (3)**: This is the year you are reporting for.
*   **Reporting Period (4)**: Select the period you are reporting for (Quarter or Annual).
*   **Crop Sales (6)**: Enter your total revenue from crop sales.
*   **Livestock Sales (7)**: Enter your total revenue from livestock sales.
*   **Government Payments (8)**: Enter any government payments you have received (such as subsidies or disaster relief).
*   **Insurance Proceeds (9)**: Enter any insurance proceeds you have received (such as crop insurance payouts).
*   **Seeds and Plants (11)**: Enter the cost of seeds and plants used in your operation.
*   **Fertilizer and Chemicals (12)**: Enter the cost of fertilizer and chemicals used in your operation.
*   **Labor Hired (13)**: Enter the amount of money you have spent on labor (wages and benefits).
*   **Fuel and Oil (14)**: Enter the amount of money you have spent on fuel and oil (diesel, gas, lubricants).
*   **Repairs and Maintenance (15)**: Enter the amount of money you have spent on repairs and maintenance (machinery and buildings).
*   **Total Land Value (17)**: Enter the current market value of your land.
*   **Total Machinery Value (18)**: Enter the deprecitated value of your machinery.
*   **Total Loan Liabilities (19)**: Enter the amount of money you owe on outstanding loans.
*   **Net Cash Income (20)**: This field will be calculated manually.
*   **Certification (21)**: Enter yes if you certify the numbers you have entered are accurate, or other if you need to specify why they are not.
