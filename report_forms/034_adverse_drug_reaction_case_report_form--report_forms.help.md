# Adverse Drug Reaction Case Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adverse Drug Reaction Case Report Form is a tool used by healthcare professionals to report adverse reactions to medications. This form collects demographic information about the patient, details about the drug, and the adverse reaction experienced. Its primary purpose is to aid regulatory agencies and pharmaceutical companies in identifying potential risks associated with medications and improving medication safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Patient Information" (Page 1) to start with demographic details of the patient.
2. Fill out the patient's name, date of birth, and patient ID under "Patient Information".
3. Proceed to "Reporting Healthcare Professional" (Page 2) to report your details.
4. Enter your name, professional title, and contact information under "Reporting Healthcare Professional".
5. Fill out the drug details under "Drug Information" (Page 3).
6. Enter the drug name, dosage, and route of administration under "Drug Information".
7. Describe the adverse reaction experienced by the patient under "Adverse Reaction" (Page 4).
8. Provide a detailed description of the reaction, its severity, and outcome under "Adverse Reaction" (Page 4).
9. Complete the form by providing any additional comments under "Additional Comments" (Page 5).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Full Name**: Enter the legal full name of the patient.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Gender**: Select the gender of the patient.
* **Patient ID**: Enter a unique patient identification number.
* **Reporter Name**: Enter your name as a healthcare professional.
* **Reporter Title**: Enter your professional title.
* **Reporter Contact**: Enter your contact information.
* **Drug Name**: Enter the name of the drug involved.
* **Drug Dosage**: Enter the dosage of the drug taken.
* **Route of Administration**: Select how the drug was administered.
* **Duration of Treatment**: Enter how long the patient was on the drug before the reaction.
* **Reaction Description**: Provide a detailed description of the adverse reaction.
* **Reaction Severity**: Select the severity of the reaction.
* **Onset Date**: Enter the date when the reaction started.
* **Outcome**: Select the outcome of the reaction.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional information about the case.

## Tips

* Always use your professional judgment when filling out this form.
* Ensure that the patient's information is accurate and confidential.
* If you're unsure about any information or have further questions, please contact our support team for assistance.
* If you're reporting multiple reactions, fill out a new form for each case to avoid duplication of information.
