# Security Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect personal and security clearance information from users who are undergoing a security screening process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your first name, last name, email address, phone number, and address in the respective fields.
2. Select your security clearance level (Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3).
3. Choose the reason for your security clearance (Background Check, Security Interview, or Other).
4. Select the status of your security clearance (Approved, Denied, or Pending).
5. Indicate the result of your background check (Passed, Failed, or Pending).
6. If applicable, add any comments or signature about the security screening process.
7. Finally, confirm that you agree to the user agreement and sign the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **City**: Enter your city.
* **State**: Enter your state or province.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **Country**: Select your country of residence.
* **Security Clearance**: Choose your security clearance level (Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3).
* **Reason for Clearance**: Select the reason for your security clearance (Background Check, Security Interview, or Other).
* **Clearance Date**: Enter the date of your security clearance.
* **Clearance Time**: Enter the time of your security clearance.
* **Security Clearance Status**: Select the status of your security clearance (Approved, Denied, or Pending).
* **Background Check Result**: Select the result of your background check (Passed, Failed, or Pending).
* **Security Screening Comments**: Add any additional comments about the security screening process.
* **Security Screening Signature**: Add any signature or comments about the security screening process.
* **User Agreement**: Confirm that you agree to the user agreement.
* **Date**: (Optional) Enter the date of this form completion.
* **Time**: (Optional) Enter the time of this form completion.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback.
* **Security Screening Agency**: Enter the name of the security screening agency that conducted the screening.
* **Security Screening Comments**: Add any comments or feedback about the security screening process.
* **Security Screening Review Date**: (Optional) Enter the date of the security screening review.
* **Security Screening Review By**: (Optional) Enter the name of the person or agency that reviewed the security clearance.
