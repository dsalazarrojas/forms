# seo_business_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and document SEO reports for businesses. It helps in organizing and tracking these reports, allowing for better analysis and decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*), such as keywords, URL, date, time, email, phone, assigned tool, submitted by, submitted to, organization, department, team, location, submitted at, submitted by id, and submitted to id.
2. Select relevant options from the dropdown menus, such as search engines used (e.g., Google, Bing, Yahoo), tools employed (e.g., Ahrefs, SEMrush, Moz), and SEO techniques applied (e.g., on-page optimization, off-page optimization, link building).
3. Enter any additional comments or remarks in the comments field, if necessary.
4. Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Keywords** (`keywords`, `text`, required): Enter the keywords related to the report.
* **URL** (`url`, `select_one`, required): Select the URL type (http://, https://, or ftp://).
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the report was submitted.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Enter the time the report was submitted.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the submitter.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the submitter.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, required): Select the tool assigned to the report.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `select_one`, required): Select the person who submitted the report.
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, `select_one`, required): Select the person to whom the report was submitted.
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the organization.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required): Enter the department name.
* **Team** (`team`, `text`, required): Enter the team name.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required): Enter the location.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the comments about the report.
* **Assigned By** (`assigned_by`, `select_one`, required): Select the person who assigned the report.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, `date`, required): Enter the date the report was submitted.
* **Submitted By Id** (`submitted_by_id`, `number`, optional): Enter the ID of the person who submitted the report.
* **Submitted To Id** (`submitted_to_id`, `number`, optional): Enter the ID of the person to whom the report was submitted.

Note: Some fields may require additional information or explanations.
