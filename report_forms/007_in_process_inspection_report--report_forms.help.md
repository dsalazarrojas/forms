# in_process_inspection_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the progress and observations made during an in-process inspection of a product or product line in a manufacturing or production quality control process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the date of the inspection from the calendar or enter the date in the format MM/DD/YY.
2. Enter the name of the inspector or person conducting the inspection.
3. Choose the type of inspection being conducted (e.g., production, non-conformance, quality, or other).
4. Select the production lines or areas within the production process that are being inspected.
5. Provide any additional information about the location of the inspection.
6. Describe the defects or issues found during the inspection.
7. Document any corrective actions taken or planned to address the defects or issues.
8. Provide any supporting evidence or additional context for the findings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **in_process_inspection_date**: Enter the date of the inspection in MM/DD/YY format.
* **inspector_name**: Enter the name of the person conducting the inspection.
* **inspection_type**: Select the type of inspection being conducted (e.g., production, non-conformance, quality, or other).
* **production_line**: Select the production lines or areas within the production process that are being inspected.
* **inspection_location**: Enter any additional information about the location of the inspection.
* **defect_description**: Describe the defects or issues found during the inspection.
* **corrective_action**: Document any corrective actions taken or planned to address the defects or issues.
* **supporting_evidence**: Provide any additional context or evidence to support your findings.
