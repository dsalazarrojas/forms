# Research Conclusion And Recommendations Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture the research conclusion and recommendations of a project or study. It is intended for researchers to provide a brief summary of their findings and suggest potential next steps for future projects. The form helps to track the status of research projects, assign them to specific teams, and monitor progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Research Type** field with the type of research you have conducted (e.g., Research 1, Research 2, or Research 3).
2. Choose the **Research Status** that best represents the current stage of your project (e.g., In Progress, Complete, or Not Started).
3. Select the team member who is assigned to the research project (e.g., Team A, Team B, or Team C).
4. Choose the person who originally created the research project (e.g., User 1, User 2, or User 3).
5. (Optional) Provide a **Research Conclusion** summarizing your findings.
6. (Optional) Suggest **Recommendations** for future research projects.
7. (Optional) Describe the **Action Plan** for the recommendations you have suggested.
8. (Optional) Enter the **Date Completed** when the research project was completed.
9. (Optional) Enter the **Time Completed** when the research project was completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Research Type** (Select One, **research_type**): Choose the type of research that was conducted.
* **Research Status** (Select One, **research_status**): Select the current status of the research.
* **Assigned To** (Select One, **assigned_to**): Choose the team member assigned to the research project.
* **Created By** (Select One, **created_by**): Choose the person who originally created the research project.
* **Research Conclusion** (Text, **research_conclusion**): Provide a brief summary of your research findings (optional).
* **Recommendations** (Select Multiple, **recommendations**): Suggest potential next steps for future research projects (optional).
* **Action Plan** (Note, **action_plan**): Describe the action plan for the recommendations you have suggested (optional).
* **Date Completed** (Date, **date_completed**): Enter the date when the research project was completed (optional).
* **Time Completed** (Time, **time_completed**): Enter the time when the research project was completed (optional).

## Tips
Please ensure that you are accurately selecting the research type, status, and assigned team member to ensure that the form accurately tracks project progress. Additionally, please use the **Date Completed** and **Time Completed** fields only when the research project is actually completed, and not for ongoing projects.
