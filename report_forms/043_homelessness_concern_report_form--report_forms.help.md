# Homelessness Concern Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Homelessness Concern Report Form is a crucial tool for reporting concerns related to homelessness in your community. This form collects information about the situation, allowing authorities or service providers to take action and address these concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Report Date" and "Time of Day" fields to provide context about when you witnessed or experienced homelessness.
2. Enter a detailed description of the situation in the "Description" field.
3. Choose the "Select Concern" field to indicate the type of homelessness concern you are reporting.
4. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" field so that we can follow up with you if needed.
5. Confirm that you are reporting the concern by selecting either "True" or "False" in the "Report" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Community Report**: This field is for providing a brief summary of the situation. Enter a clear and concise description of what you saw or experienced.
* **Report Date**: Enter the date when you witnessed or experienced homelessness.
* **Time of Day**: Enter the time when you witnessed or experienced homelessness.
* **Location**: Enter the specific location where you witnessed or experienced homelessness.
* **Description**: This field is for providing detailed information about the situation. Describe what happened, how many people were affected, and any other relevant details.
* **Select Concern**: Choose the best option from the list to indicate the type of homelessness concern you are reporting. Options include "Homelessness Concerns" and "Other" (if your concern does not fit into one of the listed categories).
* **Contact Information**: Enter your name, email, and phone number so that we can follow up with you if needed.
* **Report**: Confirm that you are reporting the concern by selecting "True" or "False". If you are unsure, please explain in the "Description" field.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when describing the situation to help authorities or service providers take accurate action.
* If you are reporting a concern for someone else, ensure you have their permission to report on their behalf.
* If you are unsure about the type of concern, select "Other" and provide a detailed description in the "Description" field.
