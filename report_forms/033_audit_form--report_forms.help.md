# Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Audit Form is a tool for capturing key information and findings from audits conducted within an organization. It helps in maintaining records and ensuring accuracy and completeness of audits.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) to ensure the audit is considered complete.
2. Ensure that the audit title, number, and date are correctly filled out.
3. Select the correct audit type and scope according to the organization's audit types and scope.
4. Select all applicable areas reviewed from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Audit Title** (`audit_title`, text, *): This is the name or title of the audit.
* **Audit Number** (`audit_number`, text, *): This is the unique reference number of the audit.
* **Audit Date** (`audit_date`, date, *): This is the date of the audit.
* **Auditor Name** (`auditor_name`, text, *): This is the name of the lead auditor.
* **Auditor Email** (`auditor_email`, email, *): This is the contact email for the auditor.
* **Organization Audited** (`organization_audited`, text, *): This is the name of the organization or department being audited.
* **Audit Period Start** (`audit_period_start`, date, *): This is the start date of the audit period.
* **Audit Period End** (`audit_period_end`, date, *): This is the end date of the audit period.
* **Audit Type** (`audit_type`, select_one, *): This is the type of audit being conducted (Financial, Internal, Compliance, IT, Operational, Quality, Safety, or Environmental).
* **Audit Scope** (`audit_scope`, text, *): This is a brief description of the audit scope.
* **Audit Objectives** (`audit_objectives`, text, *): These are the main objectives of the audit.
* **Areas Reviewed** (`areas_reviewed`, select_multiple, *): This is a list of areas reviewed during the audit (Financial records, Internal controls, Compliance procedures, IT systems, Operational processes, Risk management, Human resources, or Procurement).
* **Findings Summary** (`findings_summary`, text, *): This is a brief summary of the key findings.
* **Findings Severity** (`findings_severity`, select_one, *): This is the overall severity of the findings (Critical, High, Medium, Low, or No findings).
* **Number of Findings** (`number_of_findings`, number, *): This is the total number of findings.
* **Compliance Status** (`compliance_status`, select_one, *): This is the overall compliance status (Fully Compliant, Mostly Compliant, Partially Compliant, Non-Compliant, or Not Assessed).
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, text, *): This is a list of key recommendations.
* **Management Response** (`management_response`, text, false): This is the management response to the findings.
* **Follow-Up Required** (`follow_up_required`, select_one, *): This is whether follow-up audit is needed (Yes, within 30 days, Yes, within 60 days, Yes, within 90 days, or No follow-up needed).
* **Audit Conclusion** (`audit_conclusion`, text, *): This is the overall conclusion of the audit.
* **Auditor Signature** (`auditor_signature`, text, *): This is the electronic signature of the auditor.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, *): This is the date of signature.

Note: The fields marked with an asterisk (\*) are required for the audit to be considered complete.
