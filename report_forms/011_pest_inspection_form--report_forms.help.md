# Pest Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for pest control professionals to report on pest inspections and provide essential information for follow-up actions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the treatment date in the `treatment_date` field. This is the date on which the treatment was conducted.
2. Fill out the `customer_name`, `customer_email`, and `customer_phone` fields with the relevant contact details for the property owner or manager.
3. Provide the address of the inspection location in the `inspection_location` field.
4. Select the type of pest found during the inspection from the `pest_type` dropdown menu.
5. Describe the pest issue in the `pest_description` field, including any observations or notes from the inspection.
6. Choose the treatment methods applied from the `treatment` dropdown menu.
7. Outline the next steps for follow-up or next actions in the `next_steps` field.
8. (Optional) Enter the follow-up date in the `followup_date` field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Treatment Date (treatment_date)

* **Treatment Date**: Enter the date on which the treatment was conducted.

### 2. Customer Information (customer_name)

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`): Enter the name of the property owner or manager.

### 3. Customer Email (customer_email)

* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`): Enter the email address of the property owner or manager.

### 4. Customer Phone (customer_phone)

* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`): Enter the phone number of the property owner or manager.

### 5. Inspection Location (inspection_location)

* **Inspection Location**: Enter the address of the inspection location.

### 6. Pest Type (pest_type)

* **Pest Type** (`pest_type`): Select the type of pest found during the inspection from the dropdown menu:
	+ Ants
	+ Bees
	+ Rats
	+ Roaches
	+ Termites

### 7. Pest Description (pest_description)

* **Pest Description** (`pest_description`): Describe the pest issue, including any observations or notes from the inspection.

### 8. Treatment (treatment)

* **Treatment** (`treatment`): Choose the treatment methods applied from the dropdown menu:
	+ Baiting
	+ Sealing
	+ Trapping

### 9. Next Steps (next_steps)

* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`): Outline the next steps for follow-up or next actions.

### 10. Follow-up Date (followup_date)

* **Follow-up Date** (`followup_date`): (Optional) Enter the date for next follow-up actions.
