# Daily Office Cleaning Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help office staff quickly and easily track and report the daily cleaning activities that are completed in the office.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the name of your office location on page 1.
2. Select the frequency of cleaning you would like to perform on page 1.
3. Select all applicable cleaning tasks on page 2.
4. Optionally, add the date and time you completed the tasks on pages 3 and 4.
5. Enter the name of the staff member who completed the tasks on page 4.
6. Add any additional comments or notes on page 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Office Location** (`office_cleaning_checklist`, text, optional): Enter the name of your office location.
* **How often would you like to clean?** (`cleaning_frequency`, select_one, required): Select how often you would like to clean the office:
	+ Every morning
	+ After every meeting
	+ At closing of business
	+ Never
* **Select all applicable tasks** (`office_cleaning_tasks`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the cleaning tasks that you would like to perform:
	+ Sweep and mop floors
	+ Empty trash
	+ Clean counters and sinks
	+ Dust and dust
	+ Wipe down surfaces
	+ Vacuum and clean furniture
* **Select all applicable tasks** (`office_cleaning_tasks`, select_one, required): Choose all the cleaning tasks that you have performed:
	+ Sweep and mop floors
	+ Empty trash
	+ Clean counters and sinks
	+ Dust and dust
	+ Wipe down surfaces
	+ Vacuum and clean furniture
* **Date completed** (`date_completed`, date, optional): Optionally, enter the date you completed the cleaning tasks.
* **Time completed** (`time_completed`, time, optional): Optionally, enter the time you completed the cleaning tasks.
* **Completed by** (`completed_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the staff member who completed the tasks.
* **Additional comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the cleaning tasks.
