# Administrative Probation Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help supervisors evaluate and provide feedback on an employee's performance during a probationary period. It helps in identifying strengths and areas for improvement, and making informed decisions about the employee's future in the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the employee's name, ID, position, and department.
2.  Provide dates for the start and end of the probationary period, as well as the reporting period.
3.  Rate the employee's attendance, punctuality, work quality, productivity, communication skills, teamwork, initiative, and policy compliance during the reporting period.
4.  List any incidents or disciplinary actions that occurred during this period.
5.  Identify the employee's strengths and areas for improvement.
6.  Specify any training or development needs.
7.  Provide an overall recommendation regarding the employee's future in the organization.
8.  Add any additional comments.
9.  Sign and date the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Employee Name** (`employee_name`): Enter the full name of the employee on probation.
*   **Employee ID** (`employee_id`): Enter the employee's identification number.
*   **Position Title** (`position_title`): Enter the employee's current position title.
*   **Department** (`department`): Select the department where the employee works.
*   **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`): Enter the name of the employee's supervisor.
*   **Supervisor Position** (`supervisor_position`): Enter the supervisor's position title.
*   **Probation Start Date** (`probation_start_date`): Enter the date when the probationary period began.
*   **Probation End Date** (`probation_end_date`): Enter the date when the probationary period ends.
*   **Report Period Start Date** (`report_period_start`): Enter the beginning date of this reporting period.
*   **Report Period End Date** (`report_period_end`): Enter the ending date of this reporting period.
*   **Attendance Rating** (`attendance_rating`): Rate the employee's attendance during this period. Choose from "Excellent", "Very Good", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
*   **Punctuality Rating** (`punctuality_rating`): Rate the employee's punctuality during this period. Choose from "Excellent", "Very Good", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
*   **Work Quality Rating** (`work_quality_rating`): Rate the quality of the employee's work during this period. Choose from "Excellent", "Very Good", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
*   **Productivity Rating** (`productivity_rating`): Rate the employee's productivity during this period. Choose from "Excellent", "Very Good", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
*   **Communication Skills Rating** (`communication_rating`): Rate the employee's communication skills during this period. Choose from "Excellent", "Very Good", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
*   **Teamwork Rating** (`teamwork_rating`): Rate the employee's ability to work with others during this period. Choose from "Excellent", "Very Good", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
*   **Initiative Rating** (`initiative_rating`): Rate the employee's initiative and proactivity during this period. Choose from "Excellent", "Very Good", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
*   **Policy Compliance Rating** (`policy_compliance_rating`): Rate the employee's compliance with policies during this period. Choose from "Excellent", "Very Good", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
*   **Incidents or Disciplinary Actions** (`incidents_disciplinary_actions`): List any incidents or disciplinary actions that occurred during this period.
*   **Strengths Identified** (`strengths_identified`): Identify the employee's strengths during this period.
*   **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`): Identify areas for improvement for the employee.
*   **Training and Development Needs** (`training_development_needs`): Specify any training or development needs for the employee.
*   **Overall Recommendation** (`overall_recommendation`): Provide an overall recommendation regarding the employee's future in the organization. Choose from "Extend Probation", "Continue as Planned", "Early Confirmation", or "Termination Recommended".
*   **Supervisor Comments** (`supervisor_comments`): Add any additional comments from the supervisor.
*   **Report Date** (`report_date`): Enter the date when this report is completed.
*   **Supervisor Signature** (`supervisor_signature`): Sign and date the report electronically.
