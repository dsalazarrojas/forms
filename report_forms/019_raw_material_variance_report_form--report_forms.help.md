# Raw Material Variance Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Raw Material Variance Report Form is used to document and track any variances in raw material usage during a process.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you are completing the form for a valid reason and that you are not duplicating an existing entry.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Raw Material Variance Report** (`raw_material_variance_form`, text, required): A brief description of the variance reported.
* **Material Name** (`material_name`, text, optional): The name of the raw material involved in the variance.
* **Actual Usage** (`actual_usage`, number, optional): The actual usage of the material during the reported period.
* **Reported Usage** (`reported_usage`, number, optional): The reported usage of the material during the reported period.
* **Usage Variance** (`usage_variance`, number, optional): The difference between actual and reported usage.
* **Material Description** (`material_description`, text, optional): A brief description of the material.
* **Root Cause** (`root_cause`, text, optional): The reason behind the variance.
* **Corrective Action** (`corrective_action`, text, optional): Any actions taken to mitigate or correct the variance.
* **Reported By** (`reported_by`, text, optional): The person who reported the variance.
* **Date Reported** (`date_reported`, date, optional): The date the variance was reported.
* **Time Reported** (`time_reported`, time, optional): The time the variance was reported.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Any additional comments about the variance.
