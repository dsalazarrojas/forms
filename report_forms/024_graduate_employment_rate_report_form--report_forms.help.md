# Graduate Employment Rate Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Graduate Employment Rate Report Form is a tool used to track the employment status of graduates from different programs. This report provides valuable insights into the labor market trends and helps to inform decisions related to recruitment, job placement, and graduate outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Program Info field with the specific program you are reporting on.
2. Enter the Cohort Start Year, which is the year the graduate cohort began their studies.
3. Provide the Graduate Name, as it appears on their record.
4. Enter any relevant Contact Info for follow-up purposes.
5. Report the current Employment Status of the graduate, which can be one or multiple of the following:
   - New Hire
   - Unemployed
   - Part-time
   - Full-time
   - Other

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Info (Program Info)** (`program_info`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the program you are reporting on, such as "Computer Science" or "Software Engineering".
* **Cohort Start Year (Cohort Start Year)** (`cohort_start_year`, number, required: false): Enter the year when the graduate cohort began their studies.
* **Graduate Name (Graduate Name)** (`graduate_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the graduate as it appears on their record.
* **Contact Info (Contact Info)** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter any relevant contact information for follow-up purposes, such as an email or phone number.
* **Employment Rate (Employment Rate)** (`employment_rate`, text, required: false): Enter the current employment status of the graduate, which can be one or multiple of the following:
   - New Hire
   - Unemployed
   - Part-time
   - Full-time
   - Other
* **Graduate Status (Graduate Status)** (`graduate_status`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the statuses that apply to the graduate's current employment situation.
   - New Hire
   - Unemployed
   - Part-time
   - Full-time
   - Other
