# Patient Readmission Rate Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect data on patients who have been readmitted to the hospital. The information collected will help us track and analyze readmission rates, identify areas for improvement, and improve patient care.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question carefully and accurately to provide complete information.
2. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your supervisor or the patient's primary care team for guidance.
3. Please ensure that all required fields are filled out, as indicated by the required label next to each field.
4. If you need to provide additional information not covered by the form, please add it in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How would you like to get in touch with the patient?** (`patient_readmission_rate_report_form_1`, text, optional): This field allows you to specify how you would like to get in touch with the patient, if needed.
* **Date of Event** (`patient_readmission_rate_report_form_2`, date, optional): Enter the date of the readmission event.
* **Time of Event** (`patient_readmission_rate_report_form_3`, time, optional): Enter the time of the readmission event.
* **Length of Stay** (`patient_readmission_rate_report_form_4`, number, optional): Enter the number of days the patient stayed in the hospital during their most recent admission.
* **Admitting Doctor** (`patient_readmission_rate_report_form_5`, select_one, optional): Select the doctor who admitted the patient during their most recent admission.
* **Reason for Readmission** (`patient_readmission_rate_report_form_6`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the reason for the patient's readmission.
* **Patient Information** (`patient_readmission_rate_report_form_7`, note, optional): Use this field to provide any additional patient information that may be relevant to their readmission.
* **Medical Record Number** (`patient_readmission_rate_report_form_8`, text, optional): Enter the patient's medical record number.
* **Hospital** (`patient_readmission_rate_report_form_9`, select_one, optional): Select the hospital where the patient was readmitted.
* **Department** (`patient_readmission_rate_report_form_10`, select_multiple, optional): Select all departments where the patient was treated during their most recent admission.
* **Date of Birth** (`patient_readmission_rate_report_form_11`, date, optional): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Contact Information** (`patient_readmission_rate_report_form_12`, email, optional): Enter the patient's contact information, such as their email or phone number.
* **Phone Number** (`patient_readmission_rate_report_form_13`, text, optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Additional Comments** (`patient_readmission_rate_report_form_14`, note, optional): Use this field to provide any additional comments or information about the patient's readmission.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or the patient's primary care team.
* This form is for reporting patient readmissions only; if you need to report other types of patient interactions, please use the corresponding form.
