# Hotel Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a guest's experience at the hotel, allowing us to identify and resolve any issues they may have encountered during their stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Enter a brief title summarizing your complaint.
2.  Describe the issue you experienced in as much detail as possible.
3.  Enter the date of your visit to the hotel.
4.  Enter the time of your visit to the hotel.
5.  Enter your room number, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Complaint Title** (Complaint Title): A short title that summarizes your issue.
- **Complaint Description** (Complaint Description): Describe the issue you experienced in as much detail as possible. This will help us understand what happened and how to address it.
- **Date of Visit** (Date of Visit): The date you stayed at the hotel.
- **Time of Visit** (Time of Visit): The approximate time you arrived at and left the hotel.
- **Room Number** (Room Number): If you were staying in a room, please enter the room number. This will help us identify the location where the issue occurred.

## Tips
*   Be as detailed as possible when describing your complaint, as this will help us understand and address the issue promptly.
*   If you're reporting on behalf of another guest, please make sure to include their contact information so we can follow up with them directly.
*   We'll do our best to respond to your complaint as soon as possible, but please allow 24-48 hours for us to investigate and resolve the issue.
