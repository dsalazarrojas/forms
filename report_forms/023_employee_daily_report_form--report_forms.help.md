# Employee Daily Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Daily Report Form is a daily record of an employee's activities and progress. It helps management and team leaders to track employee tasks, work status, and issues, and make informed decisions about task allocation and resource utilization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" next to each task to indicate your progress.
2. Choose the status of your active tasks by selecting "Active" or "Inactive".
3. Enter the start time of your workday.
4. Submit your report once you have completed all the required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **report_form** (`report_form`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your activities and progress for the day.
* **task_list** (`task_list`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" next to each task to indicate your progress. This will help management and team leaders to track your work status.
* **task_status** (`task_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the status of your active tasks by selecting "Active" or "Inactive". This will help identify tasks that require attention or need to be reassigned.
* **start_time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of your workday. This will help track your work hours and schedule.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when completing this form to ensure that your work is properly documented and accounted for.
- Complete this form daily to provide up-to-date information about your activities and progress.
- If you encounter any issues or challenges during the day, please include them in the "report_form" field for reference.
