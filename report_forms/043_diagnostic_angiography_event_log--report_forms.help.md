# Diagnostic Angiography Event Log - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Diagnostic Angiography Event Log is a form used to document the events that occur during a diagnostic angiography procedure. This form captures key information about the patient, the medical history, the diagnostic procedure, and the angiography procedure. It is an essential tool for medical professionals to accurately record and report the events of the procedure.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's information in the "Patient Info" section.
2. Record any relevant medical history in the "Medical History" section.
3. Document the diagnostic procedure performed in the "Diagnostic Procedure" section.
4. Describe the angiography procedure in the detail in the "Angiography Procedure" section.
5. Provide additional details about the cath lab in the "Cath Lab Info" section.
6. Summarize any review and discussion in the "Review and Discussion" section.
7. Sign and date the form in the "Physician Signature" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any relevant identifying information.
* **Medical History**: Describe any relevant medical history, including allergies, medications, or previous conditions.
* **Diagnostic Procedure**: Document the diagnostic procedure performed, including any notable findings or issues.
* **Angiography Procedure**: Describe the angiography procedure in detail, including any notable events or complications.
* **Cath Lab Info**: Provide details about the cath lab, including the equipment used and any notable issues.
* **Review and Discussion**: Summarize any review and discussion between the medical team.
* **Physician Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm completion.
* **Date Submitted**: Enter the date the form was completed.

## Tips

* Please ensure that each field is completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Review the form before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are filled out.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the relevant medical staff.
