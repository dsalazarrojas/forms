# Product Defect Report Form 2 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Defect Report Form 2 is used to gather more detailed information about product defects that have been previously reported in the Product Defect Report Form 1.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the information you submitted in the Product Defect Report Form 1.
2. Fill out this form with as much detail as possible about the defect.
3. Ensure that the information you provide is consistent with the original report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Product Defect Report Form 2** (`product_defect_report_form_2_page_1`, `text`, required/optional: false): This is the title of the form, which is not a field to fill out but a heading.
- **Defect Details** (`product_defect_details`, `text`, required/optional: false): Provide a detailed description of the defect, including the date and time it was found, the product name, and any other relevant information.
- **Defect Image** (`product_defect_image`, `text`, required/optional: false): If you have an image of the defect, please upload it here. This will help the team to better understand the defect and identify the root cause.

## Tips
- If you are unsure about any of the information, please ask your supervisor or colleague for guidance.
- If you have any other information that you think is relevant to the defect, please include it in the `Defect Details` field.
- Please be as detailed as possible when filling out this form to ensure that the defect is properly documented and addressed.
