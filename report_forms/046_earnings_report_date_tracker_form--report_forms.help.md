# Earnings Report Date Tracker Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Earnings Report Date Tracker Form is a tool used to track and manage earnings reports for a company or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields carefully and ensure you understand their relevance to your needs.
2. Enter the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
3. Fill out the optional fields only if necessary and relevant to your situation.
4. Review your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date of Earnings Report** (`date_of_earnings_report`, date, optional): Enter the date of the earnings report. If you're unsure, leave this field blank or enter the most recent date.
* **Expected Earnings Announcement Date** (`expected_earnings_announcement_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the earnings announcement is expected to occur. If left blank, the system will use the actual date from the previous field or an estimated date.
* **Actual Earnings Announcement Date** (`actual_earnings_announcement_date`, date, optional): Enter the actual date of the earnings announcement. If this field is used, it will be considered more authoritative than the expected date.
* **Expected Filing Date** (`expected_filing_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the earnings report is expected to be filed. If left blank, the system will use the actual date from the next field or an estimated date.
* **Actual Filing Date** (`actual_filing_date`, date, optional): Enter the actual date of the earnings report filing. If this field is used, it will be considered more authoritative than the expected date.
* **Quarterly Earnings Schedule** (`quarterly_earnings_schedule`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate which quarterly periods the report falls under. Choose "Yes" for periods applicable or "No" if not applicable.
* **Annual Earnings Schedule** (`annual_earnings_schedule`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate which annual periods the report falls under. Choose "Yes" for periods applicable or "No" if not applicable.
* **Fiscal Year** (`fiscal_year`, number, optional): Enter the fiscal year relevant to the report. If unsure, leave blank or enter the most recent fiscal year.
* **Reporting Period** (`reporting_period`, select_one, optional): Choose the reporting period from the options provided. This field is typically Q1, Q2, Q3, or Q4.
* **Report Frequency** (`report_frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose how frequently the report is released. Options include Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Bi-Quarterly, and Annually.
* **Reporting Status** (`reporting_status`, select_one, optional): Indicate the status of the report. Options are Pending, Released, Delayed, or Cancelled.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments related to the report.
