## Foreman Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Foreman Report Form is used to gather information from foremen about their work shifts, including details about their shift start and end times, hours worked, and any comments or notes they have about their work. This form is an essential tool for supervisors and project managers to stay informed and manage work activities efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields:
   - **Foreman Name**: Enter your name as foreman on the current project.
   - **Shift Start Time**: Enter the start time of your shift in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
   - **Shift End Time**: Enter the end time of your shift in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
   - **Shift Hours Worked**: Enter the total hours you worked during the shift.
   - **Foreman Signature**: Sign and date your report.
2. Provide optional information:
   - **Shift Leader Comments**: If you were working as shift leader, add any comments about your experience during the shift.
   - **Project Manager Comments**: Add any comments or feedback about the project or your work.
   - **Supervisor Comments**: Add any comments or feedback about your work or performance.
   - **Contractor Comments**: If you worked with a contractor, add any comments or feedback about their work.
   - **Other Comments**: Add any other comments or feedback about your work or experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Foreman Name** (foreman_name, text, required): Enter your name as foreman on the current project.
* **Shift Start Time** (shift_start_time, time, required): Enter the start time of your shift in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Shift End Time** (shift_end_time, time, required): Enter the end time of your shift in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Shift Hours Worked** (shift_hours_worked, number, required): Enter the total hours you worked during the shift.
* **Notes** (notes, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your work.
* **Foreman Signature** (foreman_signature, text, required): Sign and date your report.
* **Project Manager Comments** (project_manager_comments, text, optional): Add any comments or feedback about the project or your work.
* **Supervisor Comments** (supervisor_comments, text, optional): Add any comments or feedback about your work or performance.
* **Shift Leader Comments** (shift_leader_comments, text, optional): If you were working as shift leader, add any comments about your experience during the shift.
* **Assigned Tool** (assigned_tool, select_one, optional): Check if you were assigned a tool during the shift.
* **Contractor Comments** (contractor_comments, text, optional): If you worked with a contractor, add any comments or feedback about their work.
* **Other Comments** (other_comments, text, optional): Add any other comments or feedback about your work or experience.
