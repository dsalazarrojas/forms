# Monthly Distribution Partner Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Monthly Distribution Partner Report is used to track the sales performance of distribution partners for each month. This report helps to monitor and analyze sales data for all distribution partners, making it easier to identify trends and adjust strategies accordingly. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the name of the distribution partner in the "Distribution Partner Name" field.
2. Enter the partner's ID in the "Partner Id" field.
3. Enter the product's ID in the "Product Id" field.
4. Enter the sales date in the "Sales Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Distribution Partner Name** (Distribution Partner Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the distribution partner.
*   **Partner Id** (Partner Id, number, optional): Enter the partner's unique ID.
*   **Product Id** (Product Id, number, optional): Enter the product's unique ID.
*   **Sales Date** (Sales Date, date, optional): Enter the date of the sales event.

## Tips
*   Ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
*   If a field is not required, you can leave it blank.
*   Review the completed form before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
