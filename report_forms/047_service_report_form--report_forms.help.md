# service_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Report Form is a document used to capture and track information about services performed on equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by entering the customer's details, including their name and email address (if applicable).
- Enter the details of the equipment being serviced, including the air conditioning system ID and its status (active/inactive).
- Record the date and time of the last service performed on the equipment.
- Enter notes about the next scheduled service and any notes from the technician.
- Select the status of the equipment (new, refurbished, used, or obsolete).
- Add any additional comments.
- Finally, enter your details and submit the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Report Form** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the report.
* **Customer** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the customer's name.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the customer's email address (if applicable).
* **Phone** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Air Conditioning System ID** (`5`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the ID number of the air conditioning system.
* **Last Service Date** (`6`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the last service performed on the equipment.
* **Next Service Schedule** (`7`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the next scheduled service.
* **Next Service Notes** (`8`, `note`, required/optional): Enter notes about the next scheduled service.
* **Technician** (`9`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the technician who performed the service.
* **Technician Notes** (`10`, `note`, required/optional): Enter notes from the technician about the service performed.
* **Equipment Status** (`11`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the status of the equipment (active/inactive).
* **Equipment Status** (`12`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the equipment status.
* **Equipment Condition** (`13`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the condition of the equipment (new, refurbished, used, or obsolete).
* **Comments** (`14`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments.
* **Submitted By** (`15`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name.
* **Submitted Date** (`16`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you submitted the report.
* **Submitted Time** (`17`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time you submitted the report.
* **Submitted User** (`18`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name.
* **Submitted User Email** (`19`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address (if applicable).
* **Submitted User Phone** (`20`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Submitted User Name** (`21`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name.
* **Submitted User Department** (`22`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your department.
* **Submitted User Title** (`23`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your title.
* **Submitted User Job Title** (`24`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your job title.
* **Submitted User Notes** (`25`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes.

## Tips
- Double-check all fields for accuracy before submission.
- Use the correct format for dates and times.
- Avoid using ambiguous or unclear language in comments and notes. 

Please note that some fields are marked as "required" but this does not necessarily mean they are required for the form to be submitted. It is intended to indicate that the field contains information that is critical for the report to be processed properly.
