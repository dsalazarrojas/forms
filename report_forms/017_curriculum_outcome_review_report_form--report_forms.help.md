# Curriculum Outcome Review Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Curriculum Outcome Review Report Form" is used to report on the curriculum outcomes achieved by students. This form is intended for educators to review and assess the progress of students towards meeting the desired learning objectives. It helps to identify areas of strength and weakness, and inform instruction and support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Report Form" page.
2. Select the "School Department" where the student is enrolled.
3. Choose the relevant term for the report.
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Form** (`report_form`, text, optional): This is a free-form text field where you can enter any relevant information about the student's progress.
* **School Department** (`school_department`, select_one, optional): Choose the department where the student is enrolled from the list of options.
* **Term** (`term`, select_multiple, required): Select the term(s) that the report covers. This is a crucial field as it helps in tracking progress over time.
* **Outcome Achievement** (`outcome_achievement`, note, optional): This field is intended for any additional notes or comments on the student's outcome achievement.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about the student's progress or areas of improvement.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields (Term) to ensure accurate tracking of student progress.
* Be concise in your comments, focusing on relevant and actionable feedback.
