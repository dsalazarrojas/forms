# Substitute Teacher Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Substitute Teacher Reporting Form is used to report on the details of a substitute teacher's presence at a school, including information about students, any issues or concerns, and relevant notes from the lesson.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand the required and optional fields.
2. Complete the form accurately and thoroughly, including all required fields.
3. Ensure that all fields are filled out correctly before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Lesson Notes
* **Lesson Notes** (`lesson_notes`, text, required/optional): A text field where you can enter any relevant notes or comments about the lesson.

### Student Names
* **Student Names** (`student_names`, select_multiple, required): A dropdown field where you select multiple student names that were present during the lesson.

### Student Ages
* **Student Ages** (`student_ages`, select_one, required): A dropdown field where you select the age of the students.

### Student Subjects
* **Student Subjects** (`student_subjects`, select_multiple, required): A dropdown field where you select the subjects that the students are studying.

### Lesson Objectives
* **Lesson Objectives** (`lesson_objectives`, text, optional): A text field where you can enter any relevant information about the lesson objectives, if applicable.

## Tips
* Ensure that you complete all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Use the dropdown fields to select relevant and correct information.
* If you have any issues or concerns, enter them in the Lesson Notes field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the school administration.
