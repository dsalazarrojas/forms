# Weekly Retail Performance Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weekly Retail Performance Report is a tool used to track and analyze retail store performance across various metrics. This report is used to monitor sales, customer segments, inventory status, and other key performance indicators (KPIs) to provide insights for retail management and decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the store name in the "Store Name" field.
2. Select the start and end dates of the week for which you want to report on.
3. Enter the corresponding sales figures in the "Sales" field.
4. Enter the number of transactions for the selected period in the "Transactions" field.
5. Choose the current inventory status ("In Stock", "Out of Stock", or "Damaged") in the "Inventory Status" field.
6. Select the store locations that you are reporting on.
7. Choose the sales channels (Online, Offline, or Both) in the "Sales Channels" field.
8. (Optional) Enter any additional sales channels in the "Sales Channels 2" field.
9. Select the customer segments you are reporting on (Young Adults, Seniors, Families) in the "Customer Segment" field.
10. (Optional) Enter any additional customer segments in the "Customer Segments 2" field.
11. Leave a note or any additional comments in the "Notes" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Store Name** (`Store Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the store you are reporting on.
*   **Week Start Date** (`Week Start Date`, date, required): Select the start date of the reporting week.
*   **Week End Date** (`Week End Date`, date, required): Select the end date of the reporting week.
*   **Sales** (`Sales`, number, required): Enter the total sales figures for the selected period.
*   **Transactions** (`Transactions`, number, required): Enter the number of transactions for the selected period.
*   **Foot Traffic** (`Foot Traffic`, number, required): Enter the foot traffic figures for the selected period.
*   **Inventory Status** (`Inventory Status`, select_one, required): Choose the current inventory status (In Stock, Out of Stock, Damaged).
*   **Store Locations** (`Store Locations`, select_multiple, required): Select the store locations you are reporting on.
*   **Sales Channels** (`Sales Channels`, select_one, required): Choose the sales channels (Online, Offline, Both).
*   **Sales Channels 2** (`Sales Channels 2`, select_multiple, optional): (Optional) Enter any additional sales channels.
*   **Customer Segment** (`Customer Segment`, select_multiple, required): Select the customer segments you are reporting on (Young Adults, Seniors, Families).
*   **Customer Segments 2** (`Customer Segments 2`, select_multiple, optional): (Optional) Enter any additional customer segments.
*   **Average Ticket** (`Average Ticket`, number, optional): Enter the average ticket value (optional).
*   **Average Tickets** (`Average Tickets`, number, optional): Enter the average tickets per customer (optional).
*   **Total Revenue** (`Total Revenue`, number, optional): Enter the total revenue (optional).
*   **Total Customers** (`Total Customers`, number, optional): Enter the total customers (optional).
*   **Repeat Customers** (`Repeat Customers`, select_one, optional): Choose the repeat customer rate (High, Medium, Low).
*   **Customer Acquisition Costs** (`Customer Acquisition Costs`, number, optional): Enter the customer acquisition costs (optional).
*   **Customer Acquisition Costs 2** (`Customer Acquisition Costs 2`, number, optional): Enter the additional customer acquisition costs (optional).
*   **Customer Retention Costs** (`Customer Retention Costs`, number, optional): Enter the customer retention costs (optional).
*   **Customer Retention Costs 2** (`Customer Retention Costs 2`, number, optional): Enter the additional customer retention costs (optional).
*   **Average Order Value** (`Average Order Value`, number, optional): Enter the average order value (optional).
*   **Average Order Value 2** (`Average Order Value 2`, number, optional): Enter the additional average order value (optional).
*   **Order Value Distribution** (`Order Value Distribution`, select_multiple, optional): Select the order value distribution (Low, Medium, High).
*   **Customer Segments 2** (`Customer Segments 2`, select_multiple, optional): (Optional) Enter any additional customer segments.
*   **Notes** (`Notes`, note, optional): Leave any additional comments or notes (optional).
