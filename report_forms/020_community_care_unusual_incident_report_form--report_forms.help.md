# Community Care Unusual Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Care Unusual Incident Report Form is designed to collect information about unexpected or unusual events that occur in community care settings. This form helps ensure that incidents are documented accurately and in a standardized format for administrative review and follow-up actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure that you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Enter your full name and job title or role of the reporting staff member in sections 2 and 3.
3. Provide the date and time of the incident in sections 4 and 5.
4. Describe the exact location of the incident in section 6.
5. Select the most accurate category for the incident in section 8.
6. Provide a detailed description of the incident in section 9.
7. List the names of individuals involved in the incident in section 10.
8. Check whether anyone was injured in section 11.
9. If injuries were sustained, provide a description in section 12.
10. Describe any immediate actions taken in section 14.
11. Check whether emergency services were contacted in section 15.
12. List the parties notified of the incident in section 16.
13. Provide the names of any witnesses to the incident in section 17.
14. Confirm that a supervisor has been notified in section 19.
15. Sign and date the report in section 20.
16. Enter the date of report submission in section 21.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name of Reporting Staff Member**: Enter your full name as a staff member reporting the incident.
* **Job Title or Role of Reporting Staff**: Enter your job title or role as a staff member.
* **Date of Incident**: Enter the date of the incident (YYYY-MM-DD format).
* **Time of Incident**: Enter the time of the incident (HH:MM format).
* **Exact Location of Incident**: Describe the exact location where the incident occurred.
* **Type of Incident**: Select the most accurate category for the incident from the options provided.
* **Detailed Description of the Incident**: Provide a factual description of what happened, without opinions or assumptions.
* **Names of Individuals Involved**: List the names of all individuals involved in the incident.
* **Were there any Injuries Sustained?**: Check if anyone was injured during the incident.
* **Description of Injuries (If any)**: If injuries were sustained, provide a description of the injuries.
* **Immediate Actions Taken**: Describe any immediate actions taken in response to the incident.
* **Were Emergency Services (911) Contacted?**: Check if emergency services were contacted.
* **Who was Notified of the Incident**: List the parties notified of the incident.
* **Names of Witnesses (If any)**: Provide the names of any witnesses.
* **Has a Supervisor Been Notified?**: Check if a supervisor has been notified of the incident.
* **I certify that this is a true and accurate report of the incident**: Sign and date the report with your full name.
* **Date of Report Submission**: Enter the date you submit this report.
* **Recommended Follow-up or Preventive Actions**: Suggest any follow-up or preventive actions for administrative review.
