# Wax Center Shift Change Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wax Center Shift Change Form is designed to document staff shifts and interactions with clients. This form helps to track shift start and end times, client arrivals and departures, staff members' names and signatures, and other important details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Shift Start Time** field with the time you started your shift.
2. In the **Staff Member Arrived** field, write the name of the staff member who arrived to work with you.
3. In the **Client Arrived** field, write the name of the client who arrived during your shift.
4. In the **Client Departed** field, write the name of the client who departed during your shift.
5. Select the **Staff Attending To** field with the number of the staff member who will be taking over your shift.
6. Select the **Staff Attending From** field with the number of the staff member who worked the previous shift.
7. Choose the type of client you interacted with in the **Client Type** field (e.g., Male, Female, Couple, Senior).
8. Fill in the **Shift End Time** field with the time you ended your shift.
9. In the **Staff Attending Note** field, write any additional comments or notes about the shift.
10. In the **Client Note** field, write any comments or notes about the client.
11. In the **Notes** field, write any other important events or issues that occurred during your shift.
12. Fill in the **Staff Name** field with your name.
13. Leave the **Staff Signature** field blank, as it is intended for your signature.
14. Leave the **Client Signature** field blank, as it is intended for the client's signature.
15. Leave the **Manager Signature** field blank, as it is intended for the manager's signature.
16. In the **Date** field, select the date you completed this form.
17. Fill in the **Staff** field with your name.
18. In the **Shift Hours** field, fill in the number of hours you worked.
19. In the **Total Hours Worked** field, fill in the total hours worked during your shift.
20. In the **Break Minutes** field, fill in the number of break minutes taken.
21. In the **Break Start Time** field, fill in the start time of your break.
22. In the **Break End Time** field, fill in the end time of your break.
23. In the **Break Length** field, fill in the length of your break.
24. In the **Client Count** field, fill in the number of clients you interacted with.
