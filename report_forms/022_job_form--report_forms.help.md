# Job Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Form is a simple form used for recording or reporting on jobs performed by staff members or workers. It captures basic information about a job, such as job title, staff name, task date, and times, materials used, and related files.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the Job Title field with the title of the job or task.
* Enter the Staff Name responsible for completing the job.
* Choose a Task Date for the job or task.
* Select the Task Start Time when the job or task began.
* Select the Task End Time when the job or task finished.
* Briefly describe the Work Description of the job or task.
* List any Materials Used for the job or task.
* Provide any Related Files associated with the job or task.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title (Job Title)** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title of the job or task.
* **Staff Name (Staff Name)** (`staff_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the staff member responsible for the job or task.
* **Task Date (Task Date)** (`task_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the job or task took place.
* **Task Start Time (Task Start Time)** (`task_start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the job or task.
* **Task End Time (Task End Time)** (`task_end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the job or task.
* **Work Description (Work Description)** (`work_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the work performed.
* **Materials Used (Materials Used)** (`materials_used`, text, optional): List any materials or resources used for the job or task.
* **Related Files (Related Files)** (`related_files`, text, optional): Provide any files or documents related to the job or task.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields (if any) before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, you can always ask your supervisor or team lead for guidance.
* This form is for your personal record-keeping purposes only; you can use it as you see fit to report your work or tasks.
