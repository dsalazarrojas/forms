# Tree Climbing Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tree Climbing Log form is used to record and track student tree climbing activities. This includes recording dates of climbs, student details, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (none are required, but they will be marked as such below).
2. For each field, enter the corresponding information.
3. Review and submit the form to log your climb.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Date of Climb**
*   Date of Climb (`date`, required: false): Enter the date you climbed the tree.

### 2. **Student Name**
*   Student Name (`text`, required: false): Enter your name as the climber.

### 3. **Email**
*   Email (`email`, required: false): Enter your email address.

### 4. **Student Danger Level**
*   Student Danger Level (`select_one`, required: false):
    *   Choose your danger level, either High or Low.

### 5. **Tree Height**
*   Tree Height (`number`, required: false): Enter the height of the tree you climbed.

### 6. **Climb Type**
*   Climb Type (`select_multiple`, required: false):
    *   Choose whether or not the climb was successful.

### 7. **Route Completed**
*   Route Completed (`select_one`, required: false):
    *   Choose whether or not you completed the route.

### 8. **Notes**
*   Notes (`note`, required: false): Add any additional information about your climb.

### 9. **Email to**
*   Email to (`email`, required: false): Enter the email address to which you'd like your notes sent.

### 10. **Tree Type**
*   Tree Type (`select_multiple`, required: false):
    *   Choose whether or not the tree type was present during your climb.

### 11. **Comments**
*   Comments (`text`, required: false): Add any additional comments about your climb.

## Tips

*   Be accurate when entering your date of climb.
*   Use the correct email format for your email address.
*   Choose the correct danger level and climb type for your climb.
*   Review your form for accuracy before submitting.
