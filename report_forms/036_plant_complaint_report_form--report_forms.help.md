# Plant Complaint Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Plant Complaint Report Form is a tool used to report issues or complaints related to plants or plant-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. **Complaint Date**: Enter the date of the complaint in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
2. **Complaint Location**: Describe the location where the complaint occurred.
3. **Complaint Description**: Provide a detailed description of the issue or problem.
4. **Photo Evidence**: If applicable, upload a photo of the issue. This will help us to better understand the problem.
5. **Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your ID.
6. **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
7. **Email**: Enter your contact email address.
8. **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information about the issue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Complaint Date** (`complaint_date`, date, required/optional: required): Enter the date of the complaint in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Complaint Location** (`complaint_location`, text, required/optional: required): Describe the location where the complaint occurred.
* **Complaint Description** (`complaint_description`, text, required/optional: required): Provide a detailed description of the issue or problem.
* **Photo Evidence** (`photo_evidence`, text, required/optional: required): If applicable, upload a photo of the issue to help us better understand the problem.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required/optional: required): Please enter your name as it appears on your ID.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional: required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional: required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Comments** (`submitter_comments`, text, required/optional: required): Provide any additional comments or information about the issue.
