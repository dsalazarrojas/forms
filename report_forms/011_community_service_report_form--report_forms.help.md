# Community Service Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used for students, volunteers, and staff to report their service experiences, hours, and reflections on specific community service initiatives. It is a requirement for service learning hours tracking and evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the relevant section based on your experience (e.g., Volunteer Information, Service Activity Report, Final Verification)
2.  Fill in the required information, including:
    *   Full Name
    *   Email Address
    *   Supervisor Name (the person who oversaw your work)
    *   Project Name (name of the service initiative)
    *   Start Date (when you began the reporting period)
    *   End Date (when you finished the reporting period)
    *   Total Hours Completed (hours spent on the service initiative)
    *   Description of Activities Performed (details of your tasks and contributions)
    *   Skills Utilized (select all relevant skills)
    *   Rate Your Experience (evaluation of your experience, using 1-5 scale)
    *   Submission Date (date of form submission)
3.  Optionally, provide:
    *   Description of Challenges Faced
    *   Suggestions for Improvement

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required):
    \*   This field is for your name, which will be used to identify you in the report.
*   **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required):
    \*   Enter your email address associated with your service experience.
*   **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required):
    \*   This is the name of the person who supervised or coordinated your service experience.
*   **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, required):
    \*   Enter the name of the service initiative or project you participated in.
*   **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required):
    \*   This is the start date of the service period you are reporting on.
*   **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required):
    \*   This is the end date of the service period you are reporting on.
*   **Total Hours Completed** (`total_hours_completed`, number, required):
    \*   Report the total hours you spent on the service initiative.
*   **Description of Activities Performed** (`activity_description`, text, required):
    \*   Provide detailed descriptions of your tasks, events, and contributions during the service period.
*   **Skills Utilized** (`skills_utilized`, select_multiple, required):
    \*   Select all the skills you utilized during the service period (e.g., Leadership, Teamwork, Problem Solving, etc.).
*   **Rate Your Experience** (`project_rating`, select_one, required):
    \*   Evaluate your experience using the 1-5 scale provided.
*   **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required):
    \*   Enter the date and time of your form submission.
*   **Challenges Faced** (`challenges_faced`, text, optional):
    \*   If you encountered any challenges, describe them here.
*   **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, text, optional):
    \*   Share ideas on how the service experience could be improved.
*   **Information Accuracy Confirmation** (`information_accuracy_confirmation`, select_one, required):
    \*   Confirm that the information provided is accurate and true.
*   **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, optional):
    \*   Enter the name of your supervisor for the service experience.
*   **Describe Any Challenges You Faced** (`challenges_faced`, text, optional):
    \*   If you faced any difficulties or obstacles during the service period.
