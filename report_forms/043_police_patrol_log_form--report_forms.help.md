# Police Patrol Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Police Patrol Log Form is a standardized report used by police officers to document and record incidents that occur during their patrols. This form helps ensure consistency and accuracy in reporting and tracking incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Officer Name** in the "Officer Name" field. This field is used to identify who is submitting the report.
2. Enter the **Incident Date** in the "Incident Date" field. This field is used to record the date the incident occurred.
3. Enter the **Incident Time** in the "Incident Time" field. This field is used to record the time the incident occurred.
4. Provide a brief description of the **Incident Details** in the "Incident Details" field. This field is used to gather additional information about the incident.
5. If the incident involved the officer's shift, select **"Yes"** or **"No"** in the "Officer Shift" field.
6. Classify the **Incident Type** in one of the following categories:
	* Officer in distress
	* Equipment failure
	* Suspicious person
	* Vehicle accident
	* Other
7. Report the **Incident Status** by selecting one of the following:
	* Active
	* Resolved
	* In Progress
	* Closed
	* On Hold
8. Enter the **Incident Location** in the "Incident Location" field.
9. Provide a detailed description of the incident in the "Incident Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Officer Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your name and rank/ID number for identification purposes.
* **Incident Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date the incident occurred in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Incident Time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time the incident occurred in the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Incident Details** (Text, Optional): A brief description of the incident, including any relevant circumstances or observations.
* **Officer Shift** (Select One, Optional): Check "Yes" if your shift was involved in the incident, "No" otherwise.
* **Incident Type** (Select One, Optional): Choose from the following options:
	+ Officer in distress
	+ Equipment failure
	+ Suspicious person
	+ Vehicle accident
	+ Other
* **Incident Status** (Select One, Optional): Select from the following options:
	+ Active
	+ Resolved
	+ In Progress
	+ Closed
	+ On Hold
* **Incident Location** (Text, Optional): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description** (Text, Optional): A detailed description of the incident, including any relevant details or circumstances.
