# Employee Timesheet Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Timesheet Template is a form used to collect employee data, including work hours, pay rates, and status updates. This template is designed to help employees and administrators track and manage employee work hours, pay rates, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each field carefully and select the correct options for each section.
2. Ensure that you have the necessary permissions to access and submit this form.
3. Select the correct work week status for the employee.
4. Enter the correct work hours and pay rate for the employee.
5. Choose the correct pay frequency and type for the employee.
6. Select the correct work location for the employee.
7. Enter the correct pay start and end dates for the employee.
8. Choose the correct submitted by status and enter the submitted date and time.
9. Enter the submitted by name and email for the form submission.
10. Select the correct employee work status and pay status for the employee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Work Week** (id: 1, select_multiple, required): This field allows you to select whether the employee works during the week. Please choose 'Yes' if the employee works, and 'No' if they do not.
* **Work Hours** (id: 2, number, required): Enter the number of hours the employee worked during the week. Please ensure that this is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Pay Rate** (id: 3, number, required): Enter the pay rate for the employee. This should be a decimal value (e.g., 10.00).
* **Employee ID** (id: 4, text, required): Enter the employee's unique ID number.
* **Pay Frequency** (id: 5, select_one, required): This field asks whether the employee is paid on a frequency basis. Please choose 'Yes' if they are paid on a frequency basis, and 'No' if they are not.
* **Work Location** (id: 6, select_multiple, required): Select the location where the employee works. Please choose 'Yes' if the employee works, and 'No' if they do not.
* **Pay Frequency Type** (id: 7, select_one, required): This field asks for the type of pay frequency. Please choose 'Daily', 'Weekly', 'Bi-weekly', 'Monthly', or 'Annually'.
* **Pay Start Date** (id: 8, date, required): Enter the start date of the pay period.
* **Pay End Date** (id: 9, date, required): Enter the end date of the pay period.
* **Submitted By** (id: 10, select_one, required): This field asks whether the submission is made by the employee or someone else. Please choose 'Yes' if the submission is made by the employee, and 'No' if it is made by someone else.
* **Submitted Date** (id: 11, date, required): Enter the date of submission.
* **Submitted Time** (id: 12, time, required): Enter the time of submission.
* **Submitted By Name** (id: 13, text, required): Enter the name of the person who made the submission.
* **Submitted By Email** (id: 14, email, required): Enter the email of the person who made the submission.
* **Employee Work Status** (id: 15, select_one, required): This field asks for the employee's current work status. Please choose 'Active', 'Inactive', or 'Leave'.
* **Pay Status** (id: 16, select_one, required): This field asks for the employee's current pay status. Please choose 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
