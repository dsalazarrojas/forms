# Infrastructure Maintenance Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for reporting on infrastructure maintenance activities, including projects, location, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the maintenance project from the provided options.
2. Enter the date of the maintenance activity.
3. Provide the location of the maintenance activity.
4. Enter a brief description of the maintenance activity.
5. Select the maintenance activity type from the provided options.
6. Choose the status of the maintenance activity from the provided options.
7. Enter the priority level of the maintenance activity.
8. Identify the person assigned to the maintenance activity.
9. Add any comments regarding the maintenance activity.
10. Add any tags related to the maintenance activity.
11. Attach any files relevant to the maintenance activity.
12. Enter notes about the maintenance activity.
13. Enter the contact email for the maintenance activity.
14. Enter the contact phone number for the maintenance activity.
15. Select the time of the maintenance activity.
16. Select the date range for the maintenance activity.
17. Choose the maintenance schedule from the provided options.
18. Select the frequency of the maintenance activity from the provided options.
19. Select the recurrence of the maintenance activity from the provided options.
20. Enter the due date for the maintenance activity.
21. Select the completion status of the maintenance activity from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Maintenance Project**: Enter the name of the maintenance project.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the maintenance activity.
* **Location**: Enter the location of the maintenance activity.
* **Description**: Enter a brief description of the maintenance activity.
* **Maintenance Activity**: Select the type of maintenance activity from the provided options.
* **Maintenance Status**: Choose the status of the maintenance activity from the provided options.
* **Priority**: Enter the priority level of the maintenance activity.
* **Assigned To**: Identify the person assigned to the maintenance activity.
* **Comments**: Add any comments regarding the maintenance activity.
* **Tags**: Add any tags related to the maintenance activity.
* **Files**: Attach any files relevant to the maintenance activity.
* **Notes**: Enter notes about the maintenance activity.
* **Email**: Enter the contact email for the maintenance activity.
* **Phone**: Enter the contact phone number for the maintenance activity.
* **Time**: Select the time of the maintenance activity.
* **Date Range**: Select the date range for the maintenance activity.
* **Maintenance Schedule**: Choose the maintenance schedule from the provided options.
* **Frequency**: Select the frequency of the maintenance activity from the provided options.
* **Recurrence**: Select the recurrence of the maintenance activity from the provided options.
* **Due Date**: Enter the due date for the maintenance activity.
* **Completed**: Select the completion status of the maintenance activity from the provided options.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled accurately and thoroughly.
* Use relevant tags and files to keep track of maintenance activities.
* Consult the team lead or team members if you are unsure about any information.
