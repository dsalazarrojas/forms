# Product Aging Monitoring Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about products from merchandisers or suppliers. It aims to monitor and track product aging, which is a critical process in maintaining accurate product inventory levels and ensuring timely product delivery.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Provide the required information for each field as follows:
   - **Product Aging Monitoring Form**: Enter a title for the form, which can be any descriptive text.
   - **CATEGORY**: Enter the category of the product being monitored.
   - **DESCRIPTION**: Briefly describe the product being monitored.
   - **REMARKS**: Provide any additional comments or remarks about the product.
   - **Merchandiser Details**: Enter any additional information about the merchandiser or supplier.
   - **Related Product Info**: Enter any related product information that might be useful for monitoring.
   - **Signature of the submitter**: Sign and date your name as the person submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Aging Monitoring Form**: Enter a title that briefly describes the purpose of the form and the product being monitored. This field is not required.
* **CATEGORY**: The category of the product being monitored.
* **DESCRIPTION**: A brief description of the product being monitored.
* **REMARKS**: Additional comments or remarks about the product.
* **Merchandiser Details**: Enter any additional information about the merchandiser or supplier.
* **Related Product Info**: Any related product information that might be useful for monitoring.
* **Signature of the submitter**: Sign and date your name as the person submitting the form.

## Tips

- Always fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure proper product monitoring.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required information is provided.
- If you have any doubts or questions about the form, please contact your supervisor or the form's creator for further guidance.
