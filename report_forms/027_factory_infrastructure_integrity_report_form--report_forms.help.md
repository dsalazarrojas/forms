# Factory Infrastructure Integrity Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Factory Infrastructure Integrity Report Form is used to collect information on the condition and status of equipment and facilities within the factory. This report is completed by inspectors who conduct regular inspections and is used by the factory management team to monitor and address any issues or concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the inspection date for the visit.
2. Enter the name of the inspector who conducted the inspection.
3. Choose the status of the equipment from the list of options provided.
4. Enter the ID number of the equipment.
5. Select the status of the equipment from the list of options provided.
6. Enter the type of equipment.
7. Add any additional comments or notes about the equipment or facilities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **inspection_date** (Date, Required): The date of the inspection visit.
* **inspector_name** (Text, Not Required): The name of the inspector who conducted the inspection.
* **equipment_status** (Select Multiple, Not Required): A list of the current status of the equipment, e.g., functional, non-functional, or awaiting repairs.
* **equipment_id** (Number, Not Required): The identification number of the equipment.
* **status** (Select One, Not Required): The overall status of the equipment, e.g., working, not working, or awaiting maintenance.
* **type** (Text, Not Required): The type of equipment being inspected.
* **notes** (Note, Not Required): Any additional comments or notes about the equipment or facilities.
