# Wastewater Treatment Output Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report on the performance and status of a wastewater treatment plant. It captures data on water quality, treatment capacity, and operational status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the requested information for the specified period.
2. Ensure all fields are filled out completely.
3. Review and edit your submission before saving it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Plant Performance** (`plant_performance`): A text field for noting any notable performance issues or events.
* **Wastewater Influent** (`wastewater_influent`): Enter the amount of water entering the treatment plant.
* **Wastewater Treatment** (`wastewater_treatment`): Select the type of treatment used (Chemical, Biological, etc.).
* **Effluent Quality** (`effluent_quality`): Enter any notes or comments on the quality of the treated water.
* **Turbidity** (`turbidity`): Enter the turbidity reading of the treated water.
* **pH** (`ph`): Enter the pH reading of the treated water.
* **Temperature** (`temperature`): Enter the temperature reading of the treated water.
* **Flow Rate** (`flow_rate`): Enter the flow rate of the treated water.
* **Treatment Capacity** (`treatment_capacity`): Enter the current treatment capacity of the plant.
* **Treatment Capacity Units** (`treatment_capacity_units`): Select the unit of measurement for the treatment capacity (Ml/min, m3/d, etc.).
* **Treatment Capacity Status** (`treatment_capacity_status`): Indicate the current status of the treatment capacity (Online, Offline, etc.).
* **Comments** (`comments`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the plant's performance.
* **Date** (`date`): Select the date the report applies to.

Note: This form is best completed using the specified units and format to ensure accurate data capture and entry.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out completely and accurately.
* Check with system administrators if unsure about any data entry.
* Submit the form for review and approval before saving it as official.
