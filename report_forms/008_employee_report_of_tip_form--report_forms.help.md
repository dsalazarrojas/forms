# Employee Report Of Tip Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from employees about their work shifts, including tips earned. The information collected will be used for internal purposes such as payroll or accounting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **employee ID** to identify you as the employee reporting your tips.
2. Enter the **date** you worked the shift.
3. Enter your **name** as it appears on your employee records.
4. Enter the **total tips** you earned for the shift.
5. For the shift where you earned tips, select the **tip type** (e.g. food, beverage, etc.).
6. Enter the **shift start time** when your shift began.
7. Enter the **shift end time** when your shift ended.
8. Enter the **tip status** (e.g. approved, pending, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_id** (Text, Optional):
	+ This field is used to identify you as the employee reporting your tips.
* **date** (Text, Optional):
	+ This field is used to record the date you worked the shift.
* **employee_name** (Text, Optional):
	+ This field is used to record your name as it appears on your employee records.
* **total_tips** (Text, Optional):
	+ This field is used to record the total tips you earned for the shift.
* **tip_type** (Text, Optional):
	+ This field is used to select the type of tip (e.g. food, beverage, etc.) you earned.
* **shift_start** (Text, Optional):
	+ This field is used to record the start time of your shift.
* **shift_end** (Text, Optional):
	+ This field is used to record the end time of your shift.
* **tip_status** (Text, Optional):
	+ This field is used to select the status of your tip (e.g. approved, pending, etc.).

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your information is recorded correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or HR representative.
