# Customer Complaint Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about customer complaints, enabling us to understand the issues and provide a satisfactory resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Report Form** field with the specific details of your complaint, including the incident that led to the issue.
2. In the **Description Of Complaint** field, provide a clear and concise description of the issue you are experiencing.
3. Indicate your **Contact Information** by selecting the relevant options from the list.
4. Enter the **Date Of Complaint** when the issue occurred.
5. Enter the **Time Of Complaint** when the issue occurred.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Report Form** (Required, Text): Enter the specific details of your complaint, including the incident that led to the issue. This field should provide enough information for our support team to understand the problem.
* **Description Of Complaint** (Optional, Text): This field should provide a clear and concise description of the issue you are experiencing. Be as specific as possible so we can better understand the problem.
* **Contact Information** (Required, Select Multiple): Indicate the relevant options from the list to provide your contact details for us to reach out to you.
* **Date Of Complaint** (Required, Date): Enter the date when the issue occurred.
* **Time Of Complaint** (Required, Time): Enter the time when the issue occurred.

## Tips
* Be specific and clear when filling out this form to ensure our support team can understand the problem.
* Avoid resubmitting information that has already been submitted.
* If you have any supporting documentation or attachments related to your complaint, please attach them to your form submission.
* Our support team will review and process your submission in a timely and efficient manner. We appreciate your cooperation and patience during this process.
