# School Club Monthly Report Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose

The School Club Monthly Report Form Template is used to record and report on the activities and events of a school club, including events, club activities, budget, and financial information. This template helps club advisors and administrators to track and manage the club's performance and progress throughout the month.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required information:
	* **School Code** (`school_code`): Choose the school code from the provided options.
	* **School Name** (`school_name`): Enter the name of the school.
	* **Advisor Name** (`advisor_name`): Enter the name of the club advisor.
	* **Advisor Email** (`advisor_email`): Enter the email address of the club advisor.
	* **Club Name** (`club_name`): Enter the name of the club.
	* **Club Email** (`club_email`): Enter the email address of the club.
	* **Club Phone** (`club_phone`): Enter the phone number of the club.
	* **Event Dates** (`event_dates`): Enter the dates of the events in MM/DD format.
	* **Total Members** (`total_members`): Enter the total number of members in the club.
	* **Event Description** (`event_description`): Describe the event or activity.
	* **Budget Amount** (`budget_ammount`): Enter the budget amount for the event.
	* **Actual Budget** (`actual_budget`): Enter the actual budget spent for the event.
	* **Event Type** (`event_type`): Choose the type of event (Event, Activity, or Event and Activity).
	* **Club Type** (`club_type`): Choose the type of club (Sports, Arts, or Culture).
	* **Event Location** (`event_location`): Enter the location of the event.
	* **Event Time** (`event_time`): Enter the time of the event.
	* **Event Budget** (`event_budget`): Enter the budget for the event.
	* **Event Cost** (`event_cost`): Enter the cost of the event.
	* **Event Income** (`event_income`): Enter the income from the event.
	* **Event Profit** (`event_profit`): Enter the profit from the event.
	* **Club Activities** (`club_activities`): Choose the club activities (Sports, Arts, or Culture).
	* **Club Events** (`club_events`): Choose the club events (Event, Activity, or Event and Activity).
	* **Submission** (`submission`): Choose the submission status (Submit or Review).
2. Choose the correct options:
	* **School Code** (`school_code`): Choose the school code from the provided options.
	* **Event Type** (`event_type`): Choose the type of event (Event, Activity, or Event and Activity).
	* **Club Type** (`club_type`): Choose the type of club (Sports, Arts, or Culture).
	* **Club Activities** (`club_activities`): Choose the club activities (Sports, Arts, or Culture).
	* **Club Events** (`club_events`): Choose the club events (Event, Activity, or Event and Activity).
	* **Submission** (`submission`): Choose the submission status (Submit or Review).

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all the required fields.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information entered.
* Choose the correct options for each field.
* Review and submit the form carefully before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the club advisor or administrator for guidance.
