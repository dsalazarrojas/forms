# Hard Copy Report Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hard Copy Report Request Form is used to request a hard copy of a report from the system. This form captures information about the report request, including the delivery method, confirmation, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter a brief description of the report you are requesting in the "Report Request" field.
2.  Choose the delivery method for your report from the "delivery_preferences" field.
3.  Enter your delivery address in the "delivery_address" field.
4.  Enter your contact phone number in the "contact_phone_number" field.
5.  Enter your email address in the "contact_email" field.
6.  Schedule a confirmation and confirmation by selecting a date and time in the "confirm_date" and "confirm_time" fields.
7.  Choose how you would like to be confirmed (e.g., phone, email, or mail) in the "confirm_by" field.
8.  Select the preferred method for confirmation (e.g., phone, email, or mail) in the "confirm_via" field.
9.  Add any additional comments or notes about your request in the "comments" field.
10. Click the "Submit" button to send your request.
