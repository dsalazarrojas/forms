# Service Refusal Documentation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Service Refusal Documentation Form is used to document the details of a service refusal, including the reason for refusal, the date of refusal, and the status of the refusal. This form is an essential tool for staff members to record and manage service refusals in a structured and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Service Refusal Details in the text field at the top of the form.
2. Select the Refused By option to indicate whether the service was refused by staff or not.
3. Choose the Reason For Refusal option to specify the reason for the service refusal.
4. Enter the Service Refusal Date in the date field.
5. Select the Staff Confirmed option to indicate whether staff has confirmed the service refusal.
6. Enter the Refused Service Status and Refused Service Status Description options to describe the status of the service refusal.
7. Select the Service Refusal Form Completed option to indicate whether the form has been completed.
8. Enter the Reason For Service Refusal Form Completed option to describe the reason for completing the form.
9. Enter the Next Steps option to specify the next steps to be taken.
10. Enter the Staff Signature and Staff Signature Date fields to confirm that the form has been reviewed and signed by staff.
11. Select the Staff Signature Confirmed option to indicate whether the staff signature has been confirmed.
12. Enter the Staff Signature Confirmed Date field to specify the date of confirmation.
13. Select the Service Refusal Reviewed option to indicate whether the service refusal has been reviewed.
14. Select the Review By option to indicate who reviewed the service refusal.
15. Enter the Review Comments field to add any comments related to the review.
16. Enter the Next Review Date field to specify the next review date.
