# Coronavirus Case Report Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information on suspected or confirmed cases of COVID-19 for reporting and tracking purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **case_report_form** field (Page 1) with the relevant case details.
2. In the **report_details** field (Page 2), provide any additional information that may be relevant to the case.
3. In the **contact_info** field (Page 3), select the relevant contact information that applies to the case.
4. In the **symptoms** field (Page 4), list the symptoms associated with the case.
5. In the **case_reporter_info** field (Page 5), provide your contact information.
6. In the **location** field (Page 6), enter the location where the case was reported.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **case_report_form** (Page 1):
	+ **Name:** case_report_form
	+ **Type:** text
	+ **Required:** no
	+ **Explanation:** This is the main field for reporting the case details. Enter the relevant information in this field.
* **report_details** (Page 2):
	+ **Name:** report_details
	+ **Type:** text
	+ **Required:** no
	+ **Explanation:** Provide any additional information that may be relevant to the case.
* **contact_info** (Page 3):
	+ **Name:** contact_info
	+ **Type:** select_multiple
	+ **Required:** yes
	+ **Explanation:** Select the relevant contact information for the case. This may include phone numbers, email addresses, or other contact methods.
* **symptoms** (Page 4):
	+ **Name:** symptoms
	+ **Type:** text
	+ **Required:** no
	+ **Explanation:** List the symptoms associated with the case.
* **case_reporter_info** (Page 5):
	+ **Name:** case_reporter_info
	+ **Type:** text
	+ **Required:** yes
	+ **Explanation:** Enter your contact information so that we can follow up with you regarding the case.
* **location** (Page 6):
	+ **Name:** location
	+ **Type:** text
	+ **Required:** no
	+ **Explanation:** Enter the location where the case was reported.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have filled in all required fields before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for clarification.
- If you have already submitted a case report, please do not submit another report unless there have been significant changes to the case or new information has been received that warrants an update.
