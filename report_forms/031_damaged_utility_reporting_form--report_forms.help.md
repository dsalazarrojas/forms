This is a help guide for the "Damaged Utility Reporting Form". The form is intended to collect information from users about damages to utility services, such as power outages or water leaks. It is crucial to ensure that the submitted information is accurate and non-duplicative.

When filling out the form, please note that each field is designed to collect specific details about the incident. The "Utility Name" field is the most critical piece of information, as it will help the team determine the correct utility provider for the incident. The "Location" field should be filled in with the exact address or area where the damage occurred.

To complete the form, follow the steps below:

## Purpose
The "Damaged Utility Reporting Form" is designed to report incidents of damages to utility services, such as power outages or water leaks. The form helps the team identify and respond to these incidents quickly and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the "Utility Name" field with the name of the utility service affected (e.g., electricity, water, gas).
2.  Enter the exact location where the damage occurred.
3.  Provide a brief description of the incident.
4.  If available, attach photos of the damage as evidence.
5.  Mark "Yes" or "No" for "when_occurred" to indicate if the incident occurred during normal operating hours or not.
6.  Mark "Yes" or "No" for "how_occurred" to specify how the incident occurred.
7.  Enter any additional information about where the incident occurred in the "where_occurred" field.
8.  Specify the name of the user reporting the incident in the "reporting_user" field.
9.  Enter the date you are reporting the incident in the "reporting_date" field.
10.  Select the tool assigned to investigate or repair the damage from the "assigned_tool" options.
11.  Mark "Yes" or "No" for "submitted_by" to indicate if you are submitting this report on behalf of someone else.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Utility Name** (Text, Required): The name of the utility service affected (e.g., electricity, water, gas).
*   **Location** (Text, Required): Enter the exact address or area where the damage occurred.
*   **Date** (Date, Required): The date you are reporting the incident.
*   **Description** (Text, Required): A brief description of the incident.
*   **Photos** (Note, Optional): Attach photos of the damage as evidence, if available.
*   **When Occurred** (Select One, Optional): Mark "Yes" if the incident occurred during normal operating hours, or "No" if it did not.
*   **How Occurred** (Select Multiple, Optional): Specify how the incident occurred (e.g., "Yes" for a natural disaster, "No" for human error).
*   **Where Occurred** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional information about where the incident occurred.
*   **Reporting User** (Text, Optional): Specify the name of the user reporting the incident.
*   **Reporting Date** (Date, Optional): The date you are reporting the incident.
*   **Assigned Tool** (Select One, Optional): Select the tool assigned to investigate or repair the damage.
*   **Submitted By** (Select One, Optional): Mark "Yes" if you are submitting this report on behalf of someone else, or "No" if you are the actual reporter.

**Tips:**

*   Be sure to enter accurate and complete information to help the team respond quickly and effectively to the incident.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for assistance or clarification before proceeding.
*   Please ensure that you are submitting this report with the authority to do so, if you are submitting on behalf of someone else.
