# Fire Drill Coordination Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Drill Coordination Request Form is used to request coordination for fire drills in the workplace. This form helps the facility management team to plan and execute fire drills efficiently and safely.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Provide your email address for contact purposes.
3. Select the type of fire drill event you are requesting, such as regular drill or special event.
4. Fill in the start and end time of the fire drill.
5. Specify the location of the fire drill.
6. Provide the contact name and information (email and phone number) of the person or team in charge of the fire drill.
7. Enter any additional notes or comments about the fire drill.
8. Choose a date for the fire drill.
9. Select the time of the fire drill.
10. Specify the location of the fire drill.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Event Date**: Choose a date for the fire drill event.
* **Event Time**: Choose a time for the fire drill event.
* **Start Time**: Choose a start time for the fire drill event.
* **Location**: Specify the location of the fire drill event.
* **Event Type**: Select the type of fire drill event from the options provided.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person or team in charge of the fire drill.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person or team.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person or team.
* **Assigned To**: Enter the name of the person or team assigned to the fire drill.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or information about the fire drill.
* **Fire Drill Date**: Choose a date for the fire drill event.
* **Fire Drill Time**: Choose a time for the fire drill event.
* **Fire Drill Location**: Specify the location of the fire drill event.
