# Consent Refusal Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the refusal of consent by a client or patient. It is an essential tool for recording and tracking the reason for refusal, the circumstances surrounding the refusal, and the actions taken by the healthcare provider. This information helps healthcare providers understand the client's or patient's perspective and can inform future interactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Report Date**, which is the date you are completing the form.
2. Provide the **Report ID Number**, which is a unique identifier for this report.
3. Enter your **Reporter Name**, **Reporter Role**, and **Department**, which provide context about who is documenting the refusal.
4. Enter the **Client or Patient Name**, which is the individual who refused consent.
5. Enter the **Client or Patient ID**, which is a unique identifier for the individual.
6. Enter the **Date of Birth** and **Location of Refusal**, which help to identify and document the context of the refusal.
7. Select the **Type of Consent Refused**, which indicates what type of consent was refused (e.g., medical treatment, surgery, etc.).
8. If **Other** was selected for the type of consent refused, provide a brief explanation in the **Other Consent Type** field.
9. Describe the **Specific Procedure or Treatment** that was being consented to.
10. Enter the **Reason for Refusal**, which explains why the consent was refused (e.g., personal beliefs, fear or anxiety, etc.). Select the relevant option from the drop-down list or provide a brief explanation in the **Refusal Reason Details** field.
11. Indicate whether **Capacity to Decide** was assessed and, if so, provide details in the **Capacity Assessment Details** field.
12. Check whether **Information was Provided** to the client or patient, and if so, whether they **Demonstrated Understanding** of the information.
13. Check whether **Risks of Refusal were Explained** to the client or patient.
14. Check whether **Alternatives were Offered** to the client or patient.
15. Check whether **Witnesses were Present** and, if so, list their names.
16. Check whether **Family was Involved** and, if so, provide details about their involvement.
17. Check whether **Follow-Up is Planned** and, if so, schedule the follow-up date.
18. Describe the **Expected Impact of Refusal**, which indicates the consequences of the refusal.
19. Sign your name as the **Reporter Signature**, ensuring your identity as the person documenting the refusal.
20. Check whether **Supervisor was Notified**.
21. Select the **Report Status**, which indicates the current status of the report (e.g., submitted, under review, reviewed, action required, closed).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Date (1)**: Enter the date you are completing the form.
* **Report ID Number (2)**: Provide a unique identifier for this report.
* **Reporter Name (3)**: Your name as the person documenting the refusal.
* **Reporter Role (4)**: Your role or title in the healthcare organization.
* **Department (5)**: The department or division where you work.
* **Client or Patient Name (6)**: The name of the individual who refused consent.
* **Client or Patient ID (7)**: A unique identifier for the individual.
* **Date of Birth (8)**: The date of birth of the individual for identification purposes.
* **Location of Refusal (11)**: Where the refusal occurred.
* **Type of Consent Refused (12)**: Select the type of consent refused (e.g., medical treatment, surgery, etc.).
* **Other Consent Type (13)**: If **Other** was selected, provide a brief explanation of what type of consent was refused.
* **Specific Procedure or Treatment (14)**: Describe the specific procedure or treatment being consented to.
* **Reason for Refusal (15)**: Explain why the consent was refused (e.g., personal beliefs, fear or anxiety, etc.).
* **Refusal Reason Details (16)**: Provide a detailed explanation of the reason for refusal.
* **Was Capacity to Decide Assessed (17)**: Indicate whether decision-making capacity was evaluated.
* **Capacity Assessment Details (18)**: If capacity to decide was assessed, provide details about the assessment.
* **Was Information Provided (19)**: Indicate whether information about risks and benefits was provided.
* **Did Client Demonstrate Understanding (20)**: Check whether the client or patient demonstrated understanding.
* **Were Risks of Refusal Explained (21)**: Check whether risks of refusal were explained.
* **Were Alternatives Offered (22)**: Check whether alternative options were provided.
* **Were Witnesses Present (23)**: Indicate whether witnesses were present.
* **Witness Names (24)**: If witnesses were present, list their names.
* **Was Family Involved (25)**: Indicate whether family members were involved.
* **Family Involvement Details (26)**: If family was involved, describe their role.
* **Is Follow-Up Planned (27)**: Indicate whether follow-up is planned.
* **Follow-Up Date (28)**: If follow-up is planned, schedule the follow-up date.
* **Expected Impact of Refusal (29)**: Describe the expected consequences of the refusal.
* **Reporter Signature (30)**: Sign your name as the person documenting the refusal.
* **Was Supervisor Notified (31)**: Indicate whether supervisor was informed.
* **Report Status (32)**: Select the current status of the report (e.g., submitted, under review, reviewed, action required, closed).

Note: This help guide is for the user to understand the purpose and requirements of the form, as well as the specific fields and their corresponding explanations.
