# Project Safety Compliance Monthly Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Project Safety Compliance Monthly Report is used to document and track incidents that occurred on-site during the reporting period. This includes incidents such as injuries, near misses, or other safety-related events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the month and year of the incident.
2. Enter a description of the incident, including the date it occurred.
3. Choose the category and type of the incident.
4. Select the severity of the incident.
5. Describe the action taken to address the incident.
6. Choose the outcome of the investigation.
7. Select the status of the incident.
8. Enter follow-up actions taken.
9. Enter the date of the follow-up action.
10. Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
11. Enter the name of the person who followed up on the incident.
12. Select the follow-up status.
13. Enter any follow-up comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Safety Compliance Monthly Report** (Project): This is the title of the report.
* **Site** (Site): Enter the site where the incident occurred.
* **Month** (Month): Select the month when the incident occurred. The options are: January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November, December.
* **Year** (Year): Enter the year when the incident occurred.
* **Date Incident Occurred** (Date Incident Occurred): Enter the date when the incident occurred.
* **Description of Incident** (Description of Incident): Enter a brief description of the incident.
* **Severity of Incident** (Severity of Incident): Select the severity of the incident. The options are: Low, Medium, High.
* **Action Taken** (Action Taken): Enter any action taken to address the incident.
* **Investigation Outcome** (Investigation Outcome): Choose the outcome of the investigation. The options are: None, Pending, Complete, Dismissing.
* **Incident Status** (Incident Status): Select the status of the incident. The options are: Pending, Resolved, Closed.
* **Follow-up Action** (Follow-up Action): Enter any follow-up actions taken.
* **Follow-up Date** (Follow-up Date): Enter the date of the follow-up action.
* **Incident Reported By** (Incident Reported By): Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
* **Follow-up By** (Follow-up By): Enter the name of the person who followed up on the incident.
* **Follow-up Status** (Follow-up Status): Select the follow-up status. The options are: None, Pending, Complete.
* **Follow-up Comment** (Follow-up Comment): Enter any follow-up comments.
* **Category** (Category): Enter the category of the incident.
* **Type** (Type): Enter the type of the incident.
* **Location** (Location): Enter the location of the incident.
* **Date Incident Occurred** (Date Incident Occurred): Enter the date when the incident occurred.
* **Description of Incident** (Description of Incident): Enter a brief description of the incident.
* **Follow-up Notes** (Follow-up Notes): Enter any follow-up notes.
* **Incident Image URL** (Incident Image URL): Enter a URL to an image related to the incident.
* **Incident Video URL** (Incident Video URL): Enter a URL to a video related to the incident.
