# Sales And Advertising Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture information about sales and advertising efforts, including strategies, budgets, and performance metrics. The data collected will be used to analyze and optimize sales and advertising activities.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out all the required fields. Required fields are marked as such.
- Enter a brief description in each field, using plain English to explain your answers.
- If you are unsure about a field, please ask your supervisor or contact person for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Marketing Strategy** (`marketing_strategy`, text, optional): Briefly describe the marketing strategy employed in the sales and advertising efforts.
* **Marketing Budget** (`marketing_budget`, text, optional): Enter the total marketing budget for the sales and advertising efforts.
* **Sales Tactics** (`sales_tactics`, text, optional): Describe the sales tactics used in the sales and advertising efforts.
* **Sales Tactics Details** (`sales_tactics_details`, text, optional): Provide more information about the sales tactics used in the sales and advertising efforts.
* **Marketing Channels** (`marketing_channels`, text, optional): Specify the marketing channels used in the sales and advertising efforts (e.g. social media, email, phone).
* **Sales Channels** (`sales_channels`, text, optional): Describe the sales channels used in the sales and advertising efforts (e.g. phone, sales team, customer service).
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, text, optional): Identify the target audience for the sales and advertising efforts (e.g. age, location, interests).
* **Target Segment** (`target_segment`, text, optional): Specify the target segment for the sales and advertising efforts (e.g. demographic, psychographic).
* **Advertising Budget** (`advertising_budget`, number, optional): Enter the total advertising budget for the sales and advertising efforts.
* **Sales Goals** (`sales_goals`, text, optional): Define the sales goals and objectives for the sales and advertising efforts.
* **Sales Outcomes** (`sales_outcomes`, text, optional): Describe the sales outcomes and results from the sales and advertising efforts.
* **Sales Performance** (`sales_performance`, number, optional): Enter the sales performance metrics (e.g. conversion rate, customer acquisition cost).

Note: Some fields have been left blank for future expansion, but for now, please fill out all the provided fields. If you have any questions or need further clarification, do not hesitate to ask your supervisor or contact person.
