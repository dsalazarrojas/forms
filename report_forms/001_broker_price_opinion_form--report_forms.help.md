# Broker Price Opinion Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Broker Price Opinion Form is a comprehensive tool for real estate professionals to provide an opinion on the market value of a property. It gathers detailed information about the property, the market, and the appraiser's opinion of value.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the property address in the "Property Address" field (Page 1).
2. Select the city and state in the "City" and "State" fields, respectively (Page 1).
3. Enter the zip code in the "Zip Code" field (Page 1).
4. Choose the type of property from the "Property Type" dropdown menu (Page 1).
5. Enter the square footage of the property (Page 2).
6. Enter the number of bedrooms and bathrooms (Page 2).
7. Enter the year the property was built (Page 2).
8. Select the overall condition of the property from the "Property Condition" dropdown menu (Page 2).
9. Describe any recent improvements or renovations in the "Recent Improvements or Renovations" field (Page 3).
10. Enter the number of comparable sales analyzed (Page 3).
11. Choose the date of the market analysis (Page 3).
12. Enter your recommended market value for the property (Page 4).
13. Provide justification for your recommended price (Page 4).
14. Enter your name, license number, phone number, and email address in the relevant fields (Page 4).
15. Finally, enter the date of your opinion and sign your name (Page 5).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Address (Page 1)**: Enter the physical address of the property being appraised. This will help identify the location of the property and its associated amenities.
* **City (Page 1)**: Select the city where the property is located. This will help identify the local market conditions and trends.
* **State (Page 1)**: Select the state where the property is located. This will help identify the broader market conditions and trends.
* **Zip Code (Page 1)**: Enter the zip code of the property's location. This will help identify the local area and associated amenities.
* **Property Type (Page 1)**: Choose the type of property from the dropdown menu. This will help identify the property's characteristics and its market potential.
* **Square Footage (Page 2)**: Enter the gross living area of the property. This will help estimate the property's value based on its size.
* **Number of Bedrooms (Page 2)**: Enter the number of bedrooms in the property. This will help identify the property's livability and market potential.
* **Number of Bathrooms (Page 2)**: Enter the number of bathrooms in the property, including half baths. This will help identify the property's livability and amenities.
* **Year Built (Page 2)**: Enter the year the property was built. This will help identify the property's age and potential for renovations or updates.
* **Property Condition (Page 2)**: Select the overall condition of the property from the dropdown menu. This will help identify the property's potential for renovation or repair.
* **Recent Improvements or Renovations (Page 3)**: Describe any recent upgrades or improvements made to the property. This will help identify the property's potential for increased value.
* **Number of Comparable Sales Analyzed (Page 3)**: Enter the number of comparable sales used to inform your opinion of value. This will help demonstrate the accuracy of your analysis.
* **Market Analysis Date (Page 3)**: Choose the date of the market analysis. This will help identify the date of your opinion and the accuracy of your analysis.
* **Recommended Market Value (Page 4)**: Enter your recommended market value for the property. This will help identify your opinion of the property's value.
* **Justification for Recommended Price (Page 4)**: Provide a clear and detailed explanation for your recommended price. This will help support your opinion and demonstrate your expertise.
* **Broker Name (Page 4)**: Enter your name, as the broker providing the opinion. This will help identify the source of the opinion.
* **Broker License Number (Page 4)**: Enter your broker license number. This will help verify your qualifications and expertise.
* **Broker Phone Number (Page 4)**: Enter your phone number. This will help facilitate communication and potential follow-up questions.
* **Broker Email Address (Page 4)**: Enter your email address. This will help facilitate communication and potential follow-up questions.
* **Date of Opinion (Page 5)**: Enter the date of your opinion. This will help identify the date of your analysis.
* **Date of Signature (Page 5)**: Sign your name, confirming your opinion and expertise. This will help verify the authenticity of your opinion.
