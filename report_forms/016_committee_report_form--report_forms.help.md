# Committee Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate regular reporting from committee chairs and members on the activities, progress, and decisions made during the reporting period.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the required fields:
	* **Name of Committee**: Enter the name of the committee you are reporting for.
	* **Report Prepared By**: Enter your full name and role within the committee.
	* **Reporting Period**: Select the reporting period (e.g., monthly, quarterly, semi-annually, or annually).
	* **Date of the Last Meeting**: Enter the date of the last meeting.
	* **Number of Members Present**: Enter the number of members present.
	* **Was a Quorum present**: Select whether a quorum was present.
	* **Key Decisions Made during the Reporting Period**: Summarize major outcomes and votes made during the reporting period.
	* **Progress on Committee Goals**: Reference specific objectives from the strategic plan and describe progress made.
	* **Projects Completed or Major Milestones Achieved**: List any significant projects completed or milestones achieved.
	* **Current Challenges or Roadblocks**: Describe any challenges or roadblocks preventing progress.
	* **Key Items for the Next Meeting**: Highlight key items to be discussed in the next meeting.
	* **Actions or Approvals Requested from the Board/Leadership**: List any actions or approvals requested from the board or leadership.
	* **Any Budgetary or Resource Requests**: List any budgetary or resource requests.
	* **Date of Submission**: Enter the date of submission.
	* **Committee Chair Approval Check**: Confirm that this report has been reviewed and approved by the committee chair.
2. If applicable, fill in any additional sections:
	* **Meeting Summary**: Provide a brief summary of the last meeting.
	* **Upcoming Agenda Items**: List key items for the next meeting.
	* **Link to Supporting Documents or Minutes**: Enter a link to any supporting documents or minutes.
