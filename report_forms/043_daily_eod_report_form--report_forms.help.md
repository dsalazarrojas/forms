# Daily Eod Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Eod Report Form is a tool for users to report their daily activities, time spent, and other relevant details. This form is designed to be completed once a day, at the end of the workday.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date you are reporting for.
2. Provide a detailed Activity Log of all your tasks, meetings, and interactions.
3. Record the time you spent on various activities.
4. Add any Notes that you consider important.
5. Enter your Email address for verification purposes.
6. Provide your Phone Number (or any other relevant contact information).
7. Enter the name of the Company you are representing.
8. Specify the Location where you were working.
9. Briefly describe your main Activity for the day.
10. Click "Submit" to save your report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date** (1): Enter the date you are reporting for. This field should match the date you have worked on.
* **Activity Log** (2): Provide a detailed list of all your tasks, meetings, and interactions. Include as much information as possible, such as:
	+ Tasks completed
	+ Meetings attended
	+ Interactions with colleagues or customers
* **Time** (3): Record the time you spent on various activities. Estimate the time spent on each task or meeting to the best of your ability.
* **Notes** (4): Add any important notes about your day. This field is for any additional comments or information that you consider significant.
* **Email** (5): Enter your Email address for verification purposes. This is a one-time requirement for our records.
* **Phone Number** (6): Provide your Phone Number (or any other relevant contact information) for communication.
* **Company** (8): Enter the name of the company you are representing. This is for our records.
* **Location** (9): Specify the Location where you were working. This helps us track your work location.
* **Activity** (10): Briefly describe your main Activity for the day. Summarize your work in a few words. 

Please note that this form is for internal use only and is not intended for external sharing.
