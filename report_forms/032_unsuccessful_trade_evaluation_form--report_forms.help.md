The Unsuccessful Trade Evaluation Form is used to evaluate a trade that has not been completed as expected. To use this form, please note that the following information should be filled out only when a trade has failed to meet the expected outcome.

To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the ticket number of the trade in question.
2. Provide the reason for the unsuccessful trade.
3. Enter any further review comments or suggestions.
4. Select the user who submitted the trade and the user who reviewed it.
5. Choose the review status of the trade (if applicable).
6. Enter the date the trade was submitted and the date it was reviewed.
7. Add any additional notes about the trade.

The following fields are used to capture the relevant information:
* **Ticket Number** (`ticket_number`, number, required): Enter the ticket number associated with the trade.
* **Reason for Unsuccessful Trade** (`reason_for_unsuccessful_trade`, text, required): Enter the reason why the trade was not completed as expected.
* **Further Review** (`further_review`, text, required): Enter any additional comments or suggestions for further review.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required): Enter the user who submitted the trade.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, optional): Select the user who was assigned to review the trade (options: ChatJimmy, ChatBob).
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, select_one, optional): Select the review status of the trade (options: Not Reviewed, Reviewed, In Progress).
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, required): Enter the date the trade was submitted.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, required): Enter the date the trade was reviewed.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required): Enter any additional notes about the trade.
* **Reviewed By** (`review_by`, text, required): Enter the user who reviewed the trade.
