# Jury Duty Excuse Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Jury Duty Excuse Form is a document used to report instances of jury duty and provide reasons for exclusion. This form is designed to gather information from individuals who have been called to serve on a jury and cannot fulfill their duty due to various reasons such as unavailability, conflict of interest, or other valid reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide the reason for your exclusion in the "Reason for Exclusion" field.
4. If you are requesting to be excused for an extended period, enter the available start date in the "Available Start Date" field.
5. If you need to return from the jury duty, enter the return date in the "Return Date" field.
6. Select the duration of your jury duty period from the "Jury Duty Period" dropdown menu.
7. Indicate your preferred contact method by selecting one or multiple options from the "Contact Method" dropdown menu (optional).
8. If necessary, provide additional comments or information in the "Additional Comments" field.
9. Sign the form with your juror signature in the "Juror Signature" field.
10. Sign the form with your employer's signature in the "Employer Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Reason for Exclusion** (`Reason for Exclusion`, text, required): Provide a valid reason for your exclusion from jury duty, such as personal or professional conflicts.
* **Available Start Date** (`Available Start Date`, date, optional): If you are requesting to start your available period after your original jury duty start date, enter the date you are available to start.
* **Return Date** (`Return Date`, date, required): Enter the date you expect to return from jury duty.
* **Jury Duty Period** (`Jury Duty Period`, select_one, required): Select the duration of your jury duty period from the provided options (1-2 weeks, 2-4 weeks, 4-6 weeks, 6-8 weeks, 8-12 weeks).
* **Contact Method** (`Contact Method`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or multiple methods to contact you (Email, Phone, Mail).
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional information that may be helpful for your case.
* **Juror Signature** (`Juror Signature`, note, required): Sign the form with your juror signature.
* **Employer Signature** (`Employer Signature`, note, required): Sign the form with your employer's signature.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the date you are providing the information.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, optional): If applicable, enter the time you are providing the information.
* **Juror Note** (`Juror Note`, text, optional): Provide any additional information that may be helpful for your case.
* **Employer Note** (`Employer Note`, text, optional): Provide any additional information that may be helpful for your case.

Note: This form is for internal use only. Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
