# Volunteer Hours Donation Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Hours Donation Record Form is used to record volunteer hours donated for a specific volunteer, organization, and project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct **Volunteer** and **Organization** from the lists.
2. Enter the number of hours donated (**Volunteer Hours Donated**) and their value (**Hours Donated Value**).
3. Enter the date and time (**Volunteer Hours Donated Date** and **Volunteer Hours Donated Time**) of the donation.
4. Enter your **Contact Phone** and **Contact Email** to ensure we can reach you if needed.
5. Choose the relevant **Volunteer** and **Project** from the lists.
6. Enter any additional comments or notes about the donation (**Volunteer Hours Donated Comment** and **Volunteer Hours Donated Comment**).
7. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Organization**
* Label: organization
* Type: text
* Required: true

* Label: volunteer_hours_donated

* Required: true
