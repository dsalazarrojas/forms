# flight_log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "flight_log" form is designed to gather details about flight activities, including dates, flight numbers, airports, times, and crew information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that you have all necessary information ready before starting the form.
2. Complete each field according to the following guidelines:
   - **Flight Date**: Enter the date of the flight in date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
   - **Flight Number**: Enter the flight number or any other identifying code.
   - **Start Airpor**: Enter the airport where the flight started.
   - **Start Time**: Enter the time the flight began.
   - **End Airpor**: Enter the airport where the flight ended.
   - **End Time**: Enter the time the flight ended.
   - **Crew Members**: Select the crew members that were present on the flight (Yes or No).
   - **Aircraft Model**: Enter the model of the aircraft used for the flight.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Flight Date** (Page 1, flight_date): A date field where you should enter the date of the flight.
* **Flight Number** (Page 1, flight_number): Enter the flight number or any other identifying code.
* **Start Airpor** (Page 1, start_airport): Enter the airport where the flight started.
* **Start Time** (Page 1, start_time): Enter the time the flight began.
* **End Airpor** (Page 1, end_airport): Enter the airport where the flight ended.
* **End Time** (Page 1, end_time): Enter the time the flight ended.
* **Crew Members** (Page 1, crew_members): Select the crew members that were present on the flight by choosing from "Yes" or "No".
* **Aircraft Model** (Page 1, aircraft_model): Enter the model of the aircraft used for the flight.

## Tips
- Double-check all fields before submitting the form.
- If a flight has multiple crew members, select "Yes" for "Crew Members" to indicate that there were multiple crew members on the flight.
