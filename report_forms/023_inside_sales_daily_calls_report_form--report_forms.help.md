# Inside Sales Daily Calls Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used by sales representatives to report on their daily calls, providing information on calls completed, tasks escalated, and other key metrics. The report helps management to track performance and identify areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your first name and last name in the first two fields.
2. Enter the date of the call and its duration.
3. Report the number of calls completed, tasks escalated, and tasks completed.
4. Provide a list of phone numbers and email addresses of the clients you called.
5. Select the call status (Active or Inactive).
6. Indicate whether you completed tasks (Yes or No).
7. Select the account and customer type.
8. Record the outcome of the call, such as "Scheduled," "Rescheduled," "Completed," etc.
9. Provide any additional comments or notes about the call.
10. Enter the name of the sales representative, sales manager, and region assigned to the account.
11. Enter the account name, number, and type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (1): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (2): Enter your last name.
* **Date of call** (3): Enter the date of the call in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Call length** (4): Report the duration of the call in minutes.
* **Calls completed** (5): Report the number of calls completed.
* **Reach outs** (6): Report the number of reach outs made.
* **Tasks escalated** (7): Report the number of tasks escalated.
* **Tasks completed** (8): Report the number of tasks completed.
* **Email** (9): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (10): Enter your phone number.
* **Comments** (11): Provide any additional comments about the call.
* **Assigned to** (12): Enter the name of the sales representative assigned to the account.
* **Task type** (13): Indicate whether tasks were completed (Yes or No).
* **Call status** (14): Select the call status (Active or Inactive).
* **Sales rep** (15): Enter your name as a sales representative.
* **Sales manager** (16): Enter the name of the sales manager assigned to the account.
* **Region** (17): Enter the region where the account is located.
* **Territory** (18): Enter the territory where the account is located.
* **Account name** (19): Enter the name of the account.
* **Account number** (20): Enter the account number.
* **Account type** (21): Select the type of account.
* **Customer type** (22): Select the type of customer.
* **Call outcome** (23): Record the outcome of the call.
* **Call outcome other** (24): If the call outcome is "other," provide a brief description.
* **Notes** (25): Provide any additional comments or notes about the call.
