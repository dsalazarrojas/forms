# ncr_employee_product_reporting_tool - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ncr_employee_product_reporting_tool is a form designed to collect information from employees on products they have had issues with. This data will help us identify trends and areas for improvement in our products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
- Fill in your name and report date.
- Select the product you experienced issues with.
- Provide a brief description of the issue.
- If applicable, report the date and time you encountered the issue.
- Choose the person you would like to report this to (either Jimmy or Other).
- Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (text, optional): Enter your name so we can contact you if needed.
* **Report Date** (date, optional): Enter the date you are reporting the issue.
* **Product Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the product you are reporting issues with.
* **Description** (text, optional): Describe the issue you experienced with the product.
* **Product ID** (text, optional): Enter the product's identification number, if known.
* **Recipient Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the person you would like to report this to.
* **Issue Description** (text, optional): Describe the problem you encountered with the product.
* **Issue Date** (date, optional): Enter the date you experienced the issue.
* **Issue Time** (time, optional): Enter the time you encountered the issue.
* **Assigned To** (select_one, optional): Choose the person you would like to report this issue to (Jimmy or Other).
