# Artists And Producer Strategy Session Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to document and plan sessions between artists and producers. It covers essential details such as session objectives, project progress, and next steps. By filling out this form, you'll be able to keep track of your project's progress and ensure that you're on the right track.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Lead Artist Name and Lead Producer Name to identify the key individuals involved in the project.
2. Choose the current phase of the project from the provided options.
3. Enter the Date of Session to keep track of the timeline.
4. Enter the Project or Album Title to give a clear name to the project.
5. Select the Primary Objectives for the session from the options provided.
6. Enter a brief summary of the action items and tasks that need to be completed.
7. If relevant, add any additional comments or notes for the producer.
8. Finally, rate the productivity of the session to help identify areas for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lead Artist Name** (`artist_lead`, text, required): Enter the name of the artist or person in charge of the project.
* **Lead Producer Name** (`producer_lead`, text, required): Enter the name of the producer leading the project.
* **Date of Session** (`session_date`, date, required): Select the date of the session.
* **Project or Album Title** (`project_name`, text, required): Enter a clear name for the project or album.
* **Current Phase** (`current_phase`, select_one, required): Choose the current phase of the project from the options provided.
* **Primary Objectives for this Session** (`session_objectives`, text, required): Enter the primary objectives for the session, such as what needs to be decided today.
* **Budget Allocation Review** (`budget_update`, text, optional): Enter the current budget allocation and compare it to the remaining budget if necessary.
* **Marketing and Distribution Strategy** (`marketing_notes`, text, optional): Provide details on the marketing and distribution strategy for the project, including social media, streaming, and physical sales.
* **List of Action Items** (`action_items`, text, required): Enter a list of action items and tasks that need to be completed.
* **Proposed Next Meeting Date** (`next_session_date`, date, optional): Enter the proposed date for the next meeting.
* **Rate the Productivity of this Session** (`session_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate the productivity of the session from 1 to 5.
* **Producer Notes and Observations** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes from the producer.

## Tips
- Double-check the fields for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
- If you're unsure about any field, consult with the artist or producer for clarification.
- Use this form as a template for similar sessions in the future to keep track of progress and changes.
