# IT Service Ticket Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The IT Service Ticket Form is a tool used to submit and report IT service requests to our team. This form collects information about the issue or problem you're experiencing, allowing us to understand and resolve it efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Submit Ticket**: Enter a brief description of the problem or issue you're experiencing in this field.
2. **Description**: Provide more details about the problem, including any relevant information that might help us understand and resolve it.
3. **Ticket Type**: Select one or both of the options for the type of ticket you're submitting. This helps us categorize and prioritize your request.
4. **Priority**: Enter a number value to indicate the severity or priority of the issue.
5. **Other Details**: Enter any additional information you think might be relevant to resolving your issue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Submit Ticket**: This field is the main entry point for your report. Enter a clear and concise description of the problem or issue you're experiencing.
  - **Type**: A short text field for you to provide a brief summary of the problem.
  - **Required**: This field is not required, but it's recommended to provide as much information as possible.
- **Description**: This field is where you can provide more details about the problem, including any relevant information that might help us understand and resolve it.
  - **Type**: A text field for you to enter as much information as necessary.
  - **Required**: This field is not required, but it's recommended to provide as much information as possible.
- **Ticket Type**: This field allows you to select one or both of the options for the type of ticket you're submitting. This helps us categorize and prioritize your request.
  - **Type**: A select multiple field with the options 'Yes' and 'No'.
  - **Required**: This field is not required.
- **Priority**: This field allows you to indicate the severity or priority of the issue.
  - **Type**: A number field.
  - **Required**: This field is not required.

## Tips
* Be as detailed and clear as possible when filling out this form to ensure we have a good understanding of your issue.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask for help or clarification.
* You can submit multiple tickets if you have multiple issues to report.
