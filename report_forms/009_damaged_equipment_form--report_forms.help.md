# Damaged Equipment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to report damaged equipment. The form helps users to document and report any equipment that is no longer in good working condition due to various reasons such as wear and tear, accident, or natural disaster.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the relevant details for each section, ensuring you are as accurate as possible.
* Make sure you understand what information is being requested and provide relevant explanations where needed.
* Review and validate your information before submitting the form.
