# Political Event Protest Incident Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Political Event Protest Incident Report Form is a tool for reporting and documenting incidents related to political events and protests. This form captures essential information about the incident, including its date, location, type, and status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information about the incident.
2. If necessary, add supporting evidence and documentation to provide additional context.
3. Enter witness statements and names.
4. Update the investigation progress.
5. Select the incident status and category.
6. Add tags and comments as needed.
7. Enter the details of the incident organizer and responder.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **protest_date (1)**: Enter the date of the protest event.
* **protest_time (2)**: Enter the time of the protest event.
* **incident_location (3)**: Enter the location of the protest event.
* **incident_description (4)**: Describe the incident in as much detail as possible.
* **incident_type (5)**: Indicate whether the incident was a 'Yes' or 'No' type event.
* **incident_details (6)**: Enter any additional details about the incident.
* **incident_photos (7)**: Upload photos related to the incident.
* **incident_videos (8)**: Upload videos related to the incident.
* **incident_evidence (9)**: Upload evidence files related to the incident.
* **incident_documents (10)**: Upload documents related to the incident.
* **witness_names (11)**: Enter the names of witnesses.
* **witness_statements (12)**: Enter witness statements.
* **investigation_progress (13)**: Indicate the current investigation progress ('Yes' or 'No').
* **incident_status (14)**: Select the current status of the incident ('Active' or 'Inactive').
* **incident_category (15)**: Select the category of the incident ('Yes' or 'No').
* **incident_subcategory (16)**: Select multiple subcategories for the incident.
* **incident_tags (17)**: Enter any relevant tags.
* **incident_comments (18)**: Enter comments about the incident.
* **incident_notes (19)**: Enter any notes about the incident.
* **incident_organizer_name (20)**: Enter the name of the incident organizer.
* **incident_organizer_email (21)**: Enter the email of the incident organizer.
* **incident_organizer_phone (22)**: Enter the phone number of the incident organizer.
* **incident_organizer_notes (23)**: Enter any notes about the incident organizer.
* **incident_responder_name (24)**: Enter the name of the incident responder.
* **incident_responder_email (25)**: Enter the email of the incident responder.
* **incident_responder_phone (not present)**: There is no field for this label, it seems to be a mistake in the provided YAML.
