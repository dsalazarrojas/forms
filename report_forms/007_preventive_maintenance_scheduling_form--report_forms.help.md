# Preventive Maintenance Scheduling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information for scheduling and documenting preventive maintenance activities for vehicles and equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Form Title** (1) to briefly describe the maintenance activity.
2. Select a **Date** (2) for when the maintenance is scheduled to occur.
3. Add a **Station Location** (3) to indicate where the maintenance will take place.
4. Provide **Vehicle Information** (4) to ensure accurate identification of the equipment.
5. Specify **Service Requirements** (5) to outline the necessary maintenance tasks.
6. Decide on **Image Attachments** (6) to be uploaded, such as pictures or videos, to support the maintenance record.
7. Add your **Reporter's Signature** (7) to confirm that the form has been completed correctly.
8. Choose the person or team assigned to perform the maintenance, **Assigned To** (8).
9. Enter any additional **Notes** (9) about the maintenance activity.
10. Finally, submit the form by clicking the **Submit** button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Title (1)**: Enter a brief description of the maintenance activity.
- **Date (2)**: Select the date when the maintenance is scheduled.
- **Station Location (3)**: Identify where the maintenance will take place.
- **Vehicle Information (4)**: Provide a clear description of the equipment to be maintained.
- **Service Requirements (5)**: Specify the specific tasks required for maintenance.
- **Images (6)**: Select whether images or videos will be attached to support the maintenance record.
- **Reporter's Signature (7)**: Confirm that the form has been completed correctly.
- **Assigned To (8)**: Choose the person or team responsible for the maintenance.
- **Notes (9)**: Add any additional details about the maintenance activity.
- **Submit (10)**: Complete the form by clicking this button.
