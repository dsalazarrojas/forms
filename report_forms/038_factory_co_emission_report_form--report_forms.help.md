# Factory CO₂ Emission Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Factory CO₂ Emission Report Form is designed to collect detailed information about a factory's carbon emissions. This form helps factories report their emissions data for compliance, environmental tracking, or internal business purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. Review each field to ensure you have provided the correct information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Factory Details (factory_details)**: Enter a brief description of your factory.
* **Reporting Period (reporting_period)**: Choose the reporting period (1, 2, or 3 months, 6 months, 9 months, etc.) for which you are reporting emissions. Note that the reporting period is a required field and must be selected correctly.
* **Emission Totals (emission_totals)**: Enter the total CO₂ emissions for the selected reporting period. This field requires the correct unit of measurement (Tonnes CO₂ or Tonnes CO₂e).
* **Factory Id (factory_id)**: Optionally, enter a unique identifier for your factory. This field is not required but can help in identifying your factory.
* **Emission Factor (emission_factor)**: Optionally, enter a factor for the emission calculations. This field is not required but can be used for internal calculations.
* **Reporting Period Id (reporting_period_id)**: Optionally, enter the ID for the reporting period. This field is not required but can help in identifying the period.
* **Reporting Start Date (reporting_start_date)**: Select the start date of the reporting period.
* **Reporting End Date (reporting_end_date)**: Select the end date of the reporting period.
* **Reporting Frequency (reporting_frequency)**: Choose the frequency of the reporting (Quarterly, Monthly, Annually). This field is required.
* **Emission Value (emission_value)**: Enter the value of the emissions for the selected reporting period. This field requires a numerical value.
* **Emission Unit (emission_unit)**: Choose the unit for the emission value (Tonnes CO₂, Tonnes CO₂e, Tonnes CO₂e (default)). This field is required.
* **Reporting Period Unit (reporting_period_unit)**: Choose the unit for the reporting period (Year, Month, Day). This field is required.
* **Notes (notes)**: Enter any additional comments or information about the report. This field is not required but can be useful for further explanations.
