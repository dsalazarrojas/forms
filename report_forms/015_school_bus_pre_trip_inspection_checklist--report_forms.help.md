# School Bus Pre Trip Inspection Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to be a pre-trip inspection checklist for school buses. The purpose of this form is to ensure that the vehicle meets all necessary safety and regulatory requirements before it can be used for transportation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully to ensure that you are answering accurately and truthfully.
2. Answer each question according to the options provided.
3. Use the "Valid" option for fields that are present and in good condition, "Expired" for fields that are expired or not present, and "None" for fields that are not present or not applicable.
4. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Comments" field, if necessary.
