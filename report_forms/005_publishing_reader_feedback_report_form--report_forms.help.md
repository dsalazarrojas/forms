# Publishing Reader Feedback Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from readers about a published magazine. The feedback will help the editorial team identify areas for improvement, understand reader preferences, and make data-driven decisions about future publications. 

## How To Complete This Form
To provide feedback, please fill out this form with the required information. You can find the form fields explained below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Magazine Name** (magazine_name): Please enter the name of the magazine you are providing feedback on.
- **Issue Date** (issue_date): Enter the date of the issue of the magazine you are providing feedback on.
- **Feedback Type** (feedback_type): Choose one of the following types of feedback:
    - Option 1
    - Option 2
    - Option 3
- **Comment** (comment): Share your thoughts and suggestions about the magazine.
- **Email** (email): Provide your email address so we can contact you directly for any follow-up questions or discussions.
- **Phone** (phone): Enter your phone number so we can contact you by phone.
- **Page 1** (page_1): Enter your thoughts about the first page of the magazine.
- **Page 2** (page_2): Enter your thoughts about the second page of the magazine.
- **Page 3** (page_3): Enter your thoughts about the third page of the magazine.
- **Page 4** (page_4): Enter your thoughts about the fourth page of the magazine.
- **Page 5** (page_5): Enter your thoughts about the fifth page of the magazine.
- **Page 6** (page_6): Enter your thoughts about the sixth page of the magazine.
- **Page 7** (page_7): Enter your thoughts about the seventh page of the magazine.

## Tips
Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback. Your input will help us improve the magazine, and we appreciate your honesty!
