# Qualified Report Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Qualified Report Submission Form is designed to gather detailed information about report submissions, making it easier for the team to track and analyze reports.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant report category from the list of options.
2. Choose the priority level of the report, which will help the team assess its urgency.
3. Enter the date and time of the report submission.
4. Upload any supporting files that are relevant to the report.
5. Enter your name and email address to authenticate your submission.
6. Select the team associated with the report.
7. Enter any comments about the report.
8. Confirm your submission by filling out the "Submit" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Category**: This is the main category of the report. Please choose the most relevant category from the list of options.
* **Report Priority**: This indicates the level of urgency associated with the report. Choose from Low, Medium, or High to reflect its importance.
* **Report Date**: Enter the date of the report submission in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Report Time**: Enter the time of the report submission in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Supporting Files**: Upload any files that are relevant to the report, such as images, documents, or videos.
* **Report File**: Upload the main report file.
* **Supporting File**: Upload any additional files that support the report.
* **Comments**: Enter any comments or observations about the report.
* **Submitter**: Enter your name to authenticate your submission.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to contact you about the report.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so that others can contact you about the report.
* **Priority**: This is not a field to be filled out for this form. It is a leftover from a previous version and has been removed.
* **Notes**: This field is not used in this form and has been removed.
* **Submit**: This is a required field that confirms your submission.
* **Report Category 2**: This is an additional report category that can be used to further categorize the report.
* **Report Priority 2**: This is an additional priority level that can be used to assess the urgency of the report.
* **Report Date 2**: Enter the date of the report submission in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Report Time 2**: Enter the time of the report submission in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Submitter Email**: This is not a field to be filled out for this form. It is a leftover from a previous version and has been removed.
* **Submitter Phone**: This is not a field to be filled out for this form. It is a leftover from a previous version and has been removed.
* **Team 2**: Select the team associated with the report.
* **Report Comments**: Enter any comments or observations about the report.
* **Submitter Name**: Enter your name to authenticate your submission.

## Tips

* Make sure to submit a new report only once. If you have already submitted a similar report, please do not duplicate it.
* Fill out all required fields to ensure that your report is thoroughly documented.
* Use supporting files to provide more context and information about the report.
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the report category, priority level, and submission date and time.
