# skin_check_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **skin_check_form** is a medical questionnaire designed to collect information about a patient's skin concerns and medical history. This form is intended for use by healthcare professionals to gather accurate and comprehensive data about patients' skin health.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form's sections carefully to understand what information is being requested.
2. Answer each question truthfully and as accurately as possible.
3. Enter your answers in the provided text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Skin Concerns** (`Skin Concerns`, text, required: false):
	+ This section is where you can describe your current skin concerns, such as acne, eczema, or any other skin issues you're experiencing.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, text, required: false):
	+ In this section, please provide your medical history, including any past surgeries, medications, or allergies.
* **Skin Findings** (`Skin Findings`, text, required: false):
	+ Describe any skin findings or observations you've noticed, such as lesions, redness, or unusual growths.
* **Patient Info** (`Patient Info`, text, required: false):
	+ This section is for providing basic patient information, such as your name, contact details, or any other relevant contact information.
* **Physician Comments** (`Physician Comments`, text, required: false):
	+ Use this section to provide any additional comments or notes about the patient's skin health or medical history.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough when filling out this form.
* If you're unsure about any question, consult with the healthcare professional.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* Make sure to save a copy of the form for future reference.
