# School Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Incident Report Form is designed to collect information about incidents that occur within the school, enabling staff and students to report and document incidents in a structured and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options for the incident from the "Incident Report" page.
2. Enter the student's name and date of the incident on the corresponding pages.
3. Enter the location and description of the incident on the corresponding pages.
4. Select the staff and students involved in the incident on the corresponding pages.
5. Check if any injuries or damages were sustained.
6. Report the status of the incident on the "Incident Status" page.
7. Document any follow-up actions or dates on the "Follow-up" page.
