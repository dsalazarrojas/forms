# Casting Progress Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Casting Progress Report Form is a tool used by casting directors and associates to report their progress during casting sessions. It is an essential part of the casting process, providing a comprehensive record of the progress made during the session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the **Report Date** field and enter the date of the casting session.
2. Choose the **Shift** option from the dropdown menu, e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or All Day.
3. Enter the **Production Title** in the text field.
4. Enter the **Reporter Name** as the casting director or associate's name.
5. Select the **Current Stage of Casting** from the dropdown menu, e.g., Pre-Screening, First Audition, Callbacks, Final Selection, Contracting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Report Date
* **Report Date**: A field to enter the date of the casting session. Enter the date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### 2. Shift
* **Shift**: A dropdown menu to select the shift type, e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Evening, All Day.

### 3. Production Title
* **Production Title**: A text field to enter the title of the production, e.g., "Movie: The Great Adventure".

### 4. Reporter Name
* **Reporter Name**: A text field for the casting director or associate's name, e.g., "John Smith".

### 5. Number of Auditions Conducted Today
* **Number of Auditions Conducted Today**: A numeric field to enter the number of auditions conducted during the session.

### 6. Roles Reviewed/Casting Runs
* **Roles Reviewed/Casting Runs**: A text field to list the roles focused on during this shift.

### 7. Overall Talent Quality Today
* **Overall Talent Quality Today**: A dropdown menu to select the overall quality of the talent, e.g., Excellent, Above Average, Average, Below Average, Poor.

### 8. Number of Callbacks Selected
* **Number of Callbacks Selected**: A numeric field to enter the number of callbacks selected.

### 9. Technical or Scheduling Issues
* **Technical or Scheduling Issues**: A text field to describe any technical or scheduling issues encountered during the session.

### 10. Required Follow-up Actions
* **Required Follow-up Actions**: A text field to list any required follow-up actions, e.g., Rescheduling, checking references.

### 11. Current Stage of Casting
* **Current Stage of Casting**: A dropdown menu to select the current stage of casting, e.g., Pre-Screening, First Audition, Callbacks, Final Selection, Contracting.

### 12. Additional Shift Notes
* **Additional Shift Notes**: A text field for any additional notes about the shift, e.g., weather conditions, equipment issues.
