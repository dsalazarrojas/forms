# Building Observation Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Building Observation Report is a form used to document and report on the conditions and observations of a building's overall condition, safety, and maintenance needs. This report helps to identify potential issues and recommendations for repairs or maintenance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields: 
   - Report date
   - Observer name
   - Building name
   - Building address
   - Type of observation or inspection (e.g., Routine, Safety, Damage assessment)
   - Type of observation or inspection (e.g., Building condition, Systems)
2. Answer the rating questions with the following options:
   - Excellent
   - Good
   - Fair
   - Poor
   - Critical

   These rating questions will help evaluate the overall condition, safety, and effectiveness of each system.

3. Provide detailed text descriptions for the following fields:
   - Building information
   - Exterior observations
   - Foundation and structure observations
   - Exterior walls and materials observations
   - Floor conditions
   - Floor condition rating
   - Interior surfaces
   - Air quality and ventilation
   - Mold or mildew observations
   - Pest or rodent observations
   - Electrical system observations
   - Plumbing system observations
   - HVAC system observations
   - Fire safety equipment observations
   - Hazards or safety concerns observed
   - Severity of identified hazards
   - Maintenance items needed
   - Overall building condition
   - Safe for occupancy
