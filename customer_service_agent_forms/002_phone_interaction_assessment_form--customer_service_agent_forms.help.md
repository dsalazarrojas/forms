# Phone Interaction Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the details of phone interactions with customers, including the agent handling the interaction, the customer's details, and the outcome of the interaction. This information is used to improve customer service quality and agent performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the customer service agent who handled the interaction from the drop-down menu.
2. Select the customer who was being interacted with from the drop-down menu.
3. Choose a date for the interaction (if applicable).
4. Enter a time for the interaction (if applicable).
5. Provide a rating for the interaction (if applicable).
6. Enter any comments or notes about the interaction.
7. Enter any additional comments or notes.
8. Enter any customer complaints or concerns.
9. Enter any comments or feedback from the customer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Initial Interaction** (`phone_interaction`, `note`, `optional`): This section is intended for any notes or comments about the initial interaction with the customer.
* **Customer Service Agent** (`customer_service_agent`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the customer service agent who handled the interaction from the drop-down menu.
* **Customer** (`customer`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the customer who was being interacted with from the drop-down menu.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, `optional`): Enter the date of the interaction if applicable.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, `optional`): Enter the time of the interaction if applicable.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, `optional`): Enter a rating for the interaction (e.g., 1-5).
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, `optional`): Enter any comments or notes about the interaction.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, `optional`): Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **Customer Complaints** (`customer_complaints`, `text`, `optional`): Enter any customer complaints or concerns.
* **Customer Comments** (`customer_comments`, `text`, `optional`): Enter any comments or feedback from the customer.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Be as specific as possible when entering comments or notes.
* Use the drop-down menus to select the correct customer service agent and customer.
* If entering a date, use the standard date format (MM/DD/YY).
* If entering a time, use the standard time format (HH:MM AM/PM).
* Take your time to review the interaction and provide as much detail as possible.
