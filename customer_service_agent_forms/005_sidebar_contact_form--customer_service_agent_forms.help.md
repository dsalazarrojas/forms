# sidebar_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sidebar_contact_form is a simple contact form used to collect basic contact information from customers or end-users for support requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First name** in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your **Last name** in the "Last name" field.
3. Provide your **Email** address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name to help us identify you.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name to help us identify you.
* **Email**: Enter your email address that we can use to respond to your support request or send you updates.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.

## Tips
This form is for support purposes only. If you're looking for product information or sales, please visit our product page.
