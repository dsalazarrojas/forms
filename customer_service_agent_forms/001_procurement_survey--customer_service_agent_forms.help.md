# Procurement Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Procurement Survey form is used to gather information from customers regarding their procurement needs and preferences. This form helps us understand their requirements, which enables us to improve our products and services. Please complete this form to help us better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant category for your request from the "Category" dropdown menu.
2. Provide a brief description of your needs or requirements in the "Description" field.
3. If applicable, specify the product you are interested in and enter the relevant details in the "Product" field.
4. If applicable, specify the service you are interested in and enter the relevant details in the "Service" field.
5. Enter the desired pricing for your request.
6. Enter your email address.
7. Enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category (Select one)**: Choose the type of form related to your request (e.g., Customer Service Agent Forms, Customer Service Forms, or Product Forms).
* **Description (Note)**: Briefly describe the purpose or requirements of your request.
* **Product (Text)**: If applicable, specify the product you are interested in and enter relevant details (e.g., product name, version, or configuration).
* **Service (Text)**: If applicable, specify the service you are interested in and enter relevant details (e.g., service name, description, or duration).
* **Pricing (Number)**: Enter the desired pricing for your request.
* **Please Enter Your Email (Email)**: Enter your valid email address so we can contact you regarding your request.
* **Please Enter Your Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number so we can quickly discuss your request with you if needed.

## Tips
* Please ensure you provide accurate information to help us better serve you.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us directly.
* We appreciate your feedback and will use it to improve our products and services.
