# customer_service_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customer service agents to collect feedback from customers.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, fill out each section carefully and accurately.
- Start by filling out the "Customer Survey Title" field with a brief description of your interaction with the customer.
- Next, fill out the "Customer Name" field with the customer's name.
- Then, fill out the "Customer Email" field with the customer's email address.
- If the customer has provided you with their phone number, fill out the "Customer Phone" field with it.
- Finally, answer the "Interaction Type" question by selecting the appropriate option from the dropdown menu. 
- You can select multiple options if applicable.
- Select the "Interaction Frequency" from the dropdown menu to indicate how often the customer has interacted with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Survey Title** (`customer_survey_title`, text, optional): A brief description of your interaction with the customer.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_survey_name`, text, optional): The customer's name.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_survey_email`, email, optional): The customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_survey_phone`, text, optional): The customer's phone number.
* **Interaction Type** (`customer_survey_interaction_type`, select_multiple, optional): The type of interaction you had with the customer. Select all applicable options.
* **Interaction Frequency** (`customer_survey_interaction_frequency`, select_one, optional): How often the customer has interacted with us. Select one option.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out each field carefully and accurately.
- If a field is not applicable, you can leave it blank.
- If you are unsure about a field, you can ask a supervisor or colleague for assistance.
- Remember to save the form after completion.
