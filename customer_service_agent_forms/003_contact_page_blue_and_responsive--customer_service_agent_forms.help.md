# Contact Page Blue and Responsive - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to submit requests to our customer service team for assistance with their account, products, or services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Contact Page Blue and Responsive" link or button.
2. Scroll to the relevant section and fill in the required fields with the requested information.
3. Make sure to provide as much detail as possible in the "Detailed Description" field to help us assist you faster.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name, including any titles or initials.
* **Account ID (if applicable)** (`account_id`, `text`, optional): If you have an account ID, please enter it to help us locate your account quickly.
* **Primary Email Address** (`primary_email`, `email`, required): Enter your primary email address associated with your account.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Provide your contact phone number if you'd like to receive a call-back.
* **Support Category** (`support_category`, `select_one`, required): Select the most relevant department that best describes your issue:
	+ Technical Support
	+ Billing and Payments
	+ Sales Inquiry
	+ Product Feedback
	+ Account Management
	+ Website Bug
* **Product or Service Name** (`product_service_name`, `select_one`, required): Choose the product or service you're contacting us about:
	+ Standard Plan
	+ Premium Plan
	+ Enterprise Solution
	+ Add-on Service
	+ Legacy Support
* **How Severe is your Issue?** (`issue_severity`, `select_one`, required): Choose the severity of your issue:
	+ Minor (Question)
	+ Moderate (Feature missing)
	+ Major (Performance issue)
	+ Critical (System down)
* **Request Subject** (`request_subject`, `text`, required): Briefly describe the subject of your request.
* **Detailed Description** (`detailed_description`, `text`, required): Provide as much detail as possible to help us solve your issue faster.
* **Preferred Callback Time** (`preferred_callback_time`, `select_one`, optional): Choose a preferred callback time:
	+ Morning (8AM-12PM)
	+ Afternoon (12PM-5PM)
	+ Evening (5PM-8PM)
* **Service Experience** (`section_feedback`, `note`, optional): If you have any feedback about our service, please let us know.
* **How easy was it to find this contact page?** (`initial_ease_rating`, `select_one`, optional): Rate how easy it was to find this contact page:
	+ 1 (very difficult)
	+ 2
	+ 3
	+ 4
	+ 5 (very easy)
* **Join our monthly product update newsletter?** (`sign_up_newsletter`, `select_one`, optional): If you'd like to stay updated on our services, choose "True" to join our newsletter.

Please review and fill out the form carefully to ensure that your request is handled efficiently. If you have any questions or need assistance, don't hesitate to contact us.
