# Post Office Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for claimants to report lost or damaged parcels to the postal service provider.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
2. Select the relevant fields for your situation. For example, if your parcel was lost, select "Yes" for "Lost or Damaged Parcel".
3. Enter your name and address as they appear on your identification.
4. Select your postal service provider from the dropdown list.
5. Attach a photo or scanned copy of your identification and your signature to the relevant fields.
6. Enter any necessary contact information for yourself and the postal service provider.
7. Provide a detailed description of what happened.
