# Contact Us Ocean Deep Theme - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for customers who need assistance with Ocean Deep Theme's products or services.

## How To Complete This Form
To get assistance with Ocean Deep Theme, follow these steps:
1. Select the "Inquiry category" that best describes your issue or question.
2. Enter your full name and email address in the required fields.
3. If you prefer to be contacted, select a preferred contact method.
4. Describe your issue or question in the "Message" field.
5. If necessary, choose the urgency level and provide additional information to help our team better understand your concern.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your full name for correspondence purposes.
* **Email address** (`email`, email, required): Use the email address we'll use to reply to your concern.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number for urgent contact if you want to be reached directly.
* **Inquiry category** (`inquiry_category`, select_one, required): Select the category that best describes your issue or question.
* **Message** (`message`, text, required): Clearly describe your issue or question.
* **Urgency level** (`urgency`, select_one, optional): Choose how quickly you need assistance (e.g., Low, Medium, High).
* **Preferred contact method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, optional): Select how our team should contact you (e.g., Email, Phone, Chat).
* **Additional information** (`additional_information`, text, optional): Provide any other details that may help our team better understand your concern.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields for the customer service team to respond efficiently.
* If you have a non-emergency issue, it's recommended to choose the "Low" urgency level.
* If you prefer to be contacted via phone, ensure that your phone number is correct and up-to-date.
* For complex issues, provide as much additional information as possible to help the customer service team provide accurate assistance.
