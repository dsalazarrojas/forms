# funny_complaint_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "funny_complaint_form" is designed to collect feedback and complaints from customers about any issues they've encountered with our services or products. This form aims to gather detailed information about the complaint, allowing us to address the issue efficiently and provide the best possible resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Customer Service Agent Form" page.
2. Fill out the fields as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Service Agent Form** (`1`, `text`, required: false): 
    This field is the first section of the form and is optional. It is meant to be a header for the form, but you are not required to fill this out.
* **Complaint Details** (`2`, `text`, required: false): 
    Please provide a clear and concise description of your complaint. Be as specific as possible to help us understand the issue.
* **Contact Information** (`3`, `select_one`, required: false): 
    Select how you would like to be contacted regarding your complaint. Options are:
    * Name: We will contact you by phone or in person.
    * Phone: We will contact you by phone.
    * Email: We will contact you by email.
    * Other: Specify a different contact method in the "Complaint Subject" field.
* **Complaint Subject** (`4`, `text`, required: false): 
    Briefly describe the issue you are having so we can quickly understand the nature of your complaint.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide as much detail as possible about your complaint.
- If you have a preferred method of contact, select it in the "Contact Information" field.
- If you have additional information about your complaint, feel free to include it in the "Complaint Subject" field.
