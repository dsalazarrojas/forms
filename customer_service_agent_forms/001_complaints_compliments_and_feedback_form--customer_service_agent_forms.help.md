# Complaints, Compliments And Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for customers to provide their feedback, complaints, and suggestions to our team. We value your opinion and take all feedback seriously. Please use this form to let us know if you had a positive or negative experience with our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide feedback, please follow these steps:
1. **Fill in your personal information**: Enter your full name, email address, and phone number (if you'd like us to contact you). This will help us identify you and follow up on your feedback.
2. **Select the interaction date**: Enter the date of your service interaction.
3. **Choose the interaction channel**: Please indicate which channel you used for your service interaction (e.g., phone, email, chat, or in person).
4. **Choose the feedback type**: Select the type of feedback you'd like to provide (complaint, compliment, suggestion, or other).
5. **Provide details of your feedback**: Describe your experience in as much detail as possible.
6. **State your desired outcome**: If you'd like us to achieve something specific, let us know.
7. **Grant permission for follow-up**: Indicate whether you'd like us to contact you to discuss your feedback.
8. **Rate your overall experience**: Give us an overall rating from 1 to 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (id: 2, label: Full name, type: text, required: true): Enter your first and last name.
* **Email address** (id: 3, label: Email address, type: email, required: true): Enter a valid email address to which we can follow up on your feedback.
* **Phone number** (id: 4, label: Phone number, type: text, required: false): Enter your phone number if you'd like us to contact you.
* **Interaction date** (id: 5, label: Interaction date, type: date, required: true): Enter the date of the service interaction.
* **Interaction channel** (id: 6, label: Interaction channel, type: select_one, required: true): Select the channel used for your service interaction (phone, email, chat, in person).
* **Feedback type** (id: 7, label: Feedback type, type: select_one, required: true): Choose the type of feedback you'd like to provide (complaint, compliment, suggestion, or other).
* **Details of feedback** (id: 8, label: Details of feedback, type: text, required: true): Describe your experience in as much detail as possible.
* **Resolution sought** (id: 9, label: Resolution sought, type: text, required: false): If you'd like us to achieve something specific, let us know.
* **Permission for follow-up** (id: 10, label: Permission for follow up, type: select_one, required: true): Indicate whether you'd like us to contact you to discuss your feedback.
* **Overall rating** (id: 11, label: Overall rating, type: select_one, required: false): Give us an overall rating from 1 to 5 of your experience.
