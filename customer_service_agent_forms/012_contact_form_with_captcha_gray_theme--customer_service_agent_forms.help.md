# Contact Form With Captcha Gray Theme - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and feedback from customers or users who need support with their inquiries or issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out all required fields**:
	* Full name: Provide your full name so that we can address you properly.
	* Email address: Enter a valid email address where we can contact you back.
	* Subject: Briefly describe the topic or issue you're contacting us about.
	* Message: Describe the problem or question you need help with in detail.
2. **Complete CAPTCHA verification**: This is a security measure to ensure we can verify your identity.
3. **Optional but recommended**:
	* Attach supporting files via email: If you have large files like screenshots or logs, you can attach them via email.
	* Additional details: Share any extra context that might help us better understand your issue.
4. **Select your preferred contact method**:
	* Email: We'll respond to your email address.
	* Phone: We'll contact you on your preferred phone number.
