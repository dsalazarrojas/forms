# Report Spam Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Report Spam Form is designed to help users report instances of spam and phishing activity on our platform. This form allows you to report suspicious or unwanted emails, messages, or activities you've encountered, helping us to take action and keep our platform safe and secure.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To report spam, please provide your email address to receive updates on the status of your report.
2.  Describe the issue you're reporting, including any relevant details.
3.  Specify the type of report you're submitting, choosing from "Spam", "Phishing", "Spam/Phishing", or "Other".
4.  Indicate the status of the reported content, selecting from "Active", "Passive", or "Unknown".
5.  Select all types of spam or phishing you've encountered, choosing from "Spam", "Phishing", or "Other" (for multiple selections).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Report Spam Form (1)**: This is the introduction to the report form.
*   **email (2)**: Provide your email address to receive updates on the status of your report.
    \*   Please use your company email for business reports.
*   **report_description (3)**: Describe the issue you're reporting, including any relevant details.
*   **report_subject (4)**: Specify the subject of the report.
*   **report_details (5)**: Provide any additional information about the issue you're reporting.
*   **report_type (6)**: Choose the type of report you're submitting:
    \*   Spam
    \*   Phishing
    \*   Spam/Phishing
    \*   Other
*   **report_spam_status (7)**: Indicate the status of the reported content:
    \*   Active
    \*   Passive
    \*   Unknown
*   **report_spam_type (8)**: Select all types of spam or phishing you've encountered:
    \*   Spam
    \*   Phishing
    \*   Other (for multiple selections)
*   **report_spam_status (9)**: Indicate the status of the reported content:
    \*   Active
    \*   Passive
    \*   Unknown

## Tips

*   Please report specific details about the issue you're reporting.
*   Use this form to report spam and phishing activities encountered on our platform.
*   Reports will be kept confidential and used to take action on the reported issue.
