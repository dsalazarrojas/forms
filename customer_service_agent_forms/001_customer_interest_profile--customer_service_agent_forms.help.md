# Customer Interest Profile - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Customer Interest Profile" form is a critical tool for our team to provide effective customer service. It helps us gather information about a customer's demographics, interests, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your basic customer information (client_information) on **Page 1**.
2. Select your primary **category** (category) from the options on **Page 2**.
3. Choose your subcategory from the options on **Page 2**.
4. Select your **interests** from the options on **Page 3** (you can choose multiple).
5. Enter your **phone number** on **Page 4**.
6. Enter your **email address** on **Page 5**.
7. Add any additional notes or comments about your customer on **Page 6**.
8. Finally, review all the information you've entered to ensure it is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information (client_information)**: Enter your basic customer information, such as name and address.
* **Category (category)**: Select your primary category (e.g., Computers, Mobile Phones, Electronics, Music).
* **Subcategory (subcategory)**: Choose a specific subcategory related to your primary category.
* **Interests (interests)**: Select all the interests that apply to you (e.g., Computers, Mobile Phones, Electronics, Music).
* **Phone Number (phone_number)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Note (note)**: Add any additional notes or comments about your customer.
* **Subcategory (subcategory)**: This field is required for our team to better understand your customer's needs and preferences.
 
## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, please do not hesitate to ask our support team for assistance.
* Please review all fields before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you would like to save a copy of your completed form for future reference, you can print it out or save it as a PDF.
