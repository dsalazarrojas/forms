# post_installation_customer_feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from customers who have recently had our company's installation service. It helps us understand their experience and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To provide feedback, please select the options that best reflect your experience with our installation service. You may select one or more options from the list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Post Installation Feedback (Yes/No)** (`customer_feedback_form`, Required)
	+ This is the main question we're trying to answer with this form. Please select 'Yes' if you are satisfied with our service and 'No' if you are not.
* **Overall Satisfaction (1-5)** (`installation_rating`, Optional)
	+ On a scale from 1 to 5, rate how satisfied you are with our service. A 1 indicates a very poor experience, while 5 indicates an excellent experience.
* **What Factors Influenced Your Satisfaction? (Select Multiple)** (`rating_reasons`, Optional)
	+ Select all the factors that influenced your satisfaction or dissatisfaction with our installation service. You can choose one or more options.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Optional)
	+ Please enter any additional comments or thoughts you have about your experience with our installation service. This is a free-text field where you can provide as much or as little information as you like.
