# Customer Service Agent Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customer service agents applying for a position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Fill in your last name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
4. Fill in your phone number in the **Phone** field.
5. Enter your address in the **Address** field.
6. Fill in your job title in the **Job Title** field.
7. Select your job type from the options provided in the **Job Type** field.
8. Choose your work status from the available options in the **Work Status** field.
9. Select your job status from the options provided in the **Job Status** field.
10. Enter your availability in the **Availability** field.
11. Enter your work hours in the **Work Hours** field.
12. Fill in your start date in the **Start Date** field.
13. Fill in your end date in the **End Date** field.
14. Select your skills and languages you possess in the **Skills** and **Languages** fields, respectively.
14. Fill in your social security number in the **Social Security Number** field.
15. Fill in your work experience in the **Work Experience** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address in the format `username@example.com`.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number in the format `(123) 456-7890`.
* **Address** (text, required): Enter your home or work address.
* **Job Title** (text, required): Enter your current or desired job title.
* **Job Type** (select_one, required): Select the type of job you are applying for (Customer Service, Sales, Marketing, Operations).
* **Work Status** (select_multiple, required): Choose your current work status (Available, Part-time, Full-time).
* **Job Status** (select_one, required): Select your current job status (Available, Inactive, Active).
* **Availability** (time, required): Enter your availability in the format `HH:MM-HH:MM`.
* **Work Hours** (time, required): Enter your work hours in the format `HH:MM-HH:MM`.
* **Start Date** (date, required): Enter your start date in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **End Date** (date, required): Enter your end date in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Social Security Number** (text, required): Enter your social security number (XXX-XX-XXXX).
* **Skills** (text, optional): List your skills and qualifications.
* **Languages** (text, optional): List your languages you possess.
