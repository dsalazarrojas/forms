# Customer Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from customers regarding their travel preferences, including their name, contact details, travel dates, destination, and special requests. This information will help us tailor our services to meet their specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer's name in the `Name` field.
2. Enter the customer's email address in the `Email` field.
3. Provide the customer's phone number in the `Phone` field.
4. Specify the customer's destination in the `Destination` field.
5. Select the travel dates in the `Travel Dates` field, using the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
6. Choose the travel type(s) from the options provided in the `Travel Type` field.
7. Select the travel arrangement from the options provided in the `Travel Arrangement` field.
8. Optionally, enter any special requests in the `Special Requests` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information (customer_info)**: Enter a short description of the customer's information in this field.
* **Name (name)**: Enter the customer's name.
* **Email (email)**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Destination (destination)**: Enter the customer's destination or travel location.
* **Travel Dates (travel_dates)**: Select the travel dates using the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Travel Type (travel_type)**: Choose one or more travel types from the options:
	+ Airport Transfer
	+ Bus
	+ Train
* **Travel Arrangement (travel_arrangement)**: Select one of the following travel arrangements:
	+ Hotel
	+ Flight
	+ Cruise
* **Special Requests (special_requests)**: Enter any special requests or additional details the customer may have.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed to ensure accurate and efficient service.
* Double-check the travel dates and type to ensure they match the customer's needs.
* Be thorough when listing special requests to provide the best possible service.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
