# Basic Email Support Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help you submit a request for assistance with an issue related to your email account or services. Please provide as much information as possible so that our support team can better understand and address your concern.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information, starting with your personal details.
2. Select the issue category that best describes your concern.
3. Provide a brief subject of your issue.
4. Describe the issue in detail.
5. If applicable, provide additional information such as the email provider, device type, browser or email application, and error messages.
6. Specify when the issue started and how often it occurs.
7. If you have attachments or screenshots, let us know if you will attach them later.
8. Choose your preferred resolution method.
9. If the issue is urgent, provide a reason for the urgency.
10. Finally, add any additional notes or information that will help our support team better assist you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (Text, Required): Please enter your name as it appears on your account.
* **Your Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter your email address associated with your account.
* **Account Number** (Text, Optional): If you have an account number, please enter it here.
* **Contact Phone Number** (Text, Optional): If you would like to be contacted via phone, please provide your phone number.
* **Issue Category** (Select One, Required): Choose the category that best describes your issue. (Login Problems, Email Not Receiving, Email Not Sending, Spam Issues, Account Issues, Configuration Help, Other)
* **Priority Level** (Select One, Required): Indicate the urgency of your issue. (Low, Medium, High, Urgent)
* **Subject of Your Issue** (Text, Required): Briefly describe the issue you are experiencing.
* **Detailed Description of the Issue** (Text, Required): Provide as much detail as possible about the issue.
* **Email Provider** (Select One, Optional): If your issue is related to your email provider, please select it here. (Gmail, Yahoo, Outlook, AOL, Other)
* **Device Type** (Select One, Optional): If your issue is related to your device, please select it here. (Computer - Windows, Computer - Mac, Smartphone - iOS, Smartphone - Android, Tablet)
* **Browser or Email Application** (Text, Optional): If your issue is related to your browser or email application, please describe it here.
* **Error Messages** (Text, Optional): If you have received any error messages, please enter them here.
* **When Did the Issue Start?** (Date, Optional): If you know when the issue started, please enter the date.
* **How Often Does This Occur?** (Select One, Optional): If you can, please describe how often you experience this issue. (Once, Occasionally, Frequently, Constantly)
* **Steps You Have Already Taken to Resolve** (Text, Optional): If you have tried any solutions, please describe them here.
* **Do You Have Attachments or Screenshots?** (Select One, Optional): If you have attachments or screenshots, let us know if you will attach them later.
* **Preferred Resolution Method** (Select One, Optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted or communicated with. (Email Response, Phone Call, Video Call, Chat Support)
* **Reason for Urgency (If Applicable)** (Text, Optional): If your issue is urgent, please explain why.
* **Additional Notes or Information** (Text, Optional): If you have any additional information that will help us better assist you, please enter it here.
