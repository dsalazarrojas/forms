# Online Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Complaint Form is a means for customers to submit their complaints and issues to the company's customer service team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Online Complaint Form** field with a title that describes the nature of your complaint.
2. In the **Customer Complaint** field, provide a detailed description of the issue you are experiencing.
3. Ensure you enter your **Customer Email** address accurately, as this is how we will contact you to discuss your complaint.
4. Enter your **Customer Phone Number** if you wish to be contacted by phone.
5. Enter your **Customer Name**, which will help us identify you and your complaint.
6. In the **Customer Message** field, provide any additional information that may be helpful in resolving your complaint.
7. Choose a **Customer Service Agent** from the dropdown list to ensure that your complaint is handled by someone familiar with your issue.
8. Finally, click the **Submit** button to send your complaint to our customer service team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Online Complaint Form** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a title for your complaint that briefly describes the nature of your issue.
* **Customer Complaint** (`customer_complaint`, text, required): Describe your issue or problem in as much detail as possible.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you to discuss your complaint.
* **Customer Phone Number** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): If you prefer to be contacted by phone, enter your 10-digit phone number.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your name so we can identify you and your complaint.
* **Customer Message** (`customer_message`, text, required): Provide any additional information that may help us resolve your complaint.
* **Date** (`form_date`, date, required): Choose the date on which you experienced the issue.
* **Time** (`form_time`, time, required): Choose the time when you experienced the issue.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, optional): Click this button to send your complaint to our customer service team.
* **Customer Service Agent** (`customer_service_agent`, select_one, optional): Select a customer service agent who will handle your complaint from the dropdown list.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when describing your complaint.
* Ensure that your email address and phone number are accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any additional information or evidence that may be helpful in resolving your complaint, please attach it to your form submission.
