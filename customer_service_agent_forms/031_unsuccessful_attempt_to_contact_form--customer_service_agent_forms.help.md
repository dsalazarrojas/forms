## unsuccessful_attempt_to_contact_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document unsuccessful attempts to contact customers, allowing us to track and analyze reasons for failed communication. This information helps us identify areas for improvement and ensure that our customer support processes are working effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Customer" field and enter the customer's name.
2. Select the "Agent" field and choose the name of the agent who made the attempt to contact the customer.
3. Select the "reason_for_unsuccessful_attempt" field and choose the reason for the unsuccessful attempt from the dropdown menu. Common reasons include:
	* The customer was unavailable.
	* The number or email address was wrong.
	* The customer was not available.
	* The customer did not respond.
4. In the "Customer Message" field, provide any additional information about the customer's message or interaction, such as any specific details that might be helpful for future contact attempts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer**: Enter the customer's name to identify the individual who was not successfully contacted.
* **Agent**: Choose the name of the agent who made the attempt to contact the customer.
* **reason_for_unsuccessful_attempt**: Select the reason why the attempt to contact the customer was unsuccessful. This helps us understand why our processes are not working effectively and identify areas for improvement.
* **Customer Message**: Provide any additional information about the customer's message or interaction that might be helpful for future contact attempts.
