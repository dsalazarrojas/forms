# Customer Satisfaction Survey Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with our product or service. It helps us understand what we're doing well and where we need to improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the introduction section with a brief description of your experience with our product or service.
2.  Choose how you rate the product or service in the "product_rating" field.
3.  Select all the features that were available on our product or service in the "product_features" field.
4.  Rate the service you received in the "service_rating" field.
5.  Choose how satisfied you are overall with our product or service in the "overall_satisfaction" field.
6.  Enter your email address in the "email" field, if you'd like us to follow up with you about your feedback.
7.  Enter your phone number in the "phone_number" field, if you'd like us to follow up with you about your feedback.
8.  Share any additional comments you have about your experience in the "additional_comments" field.
9.  Enter any feedback you have about your experience in the "feedback" field.
10. Choose the category of your feedback in the "category" field.
11. Select which tool was assigned to you in the "assigned_tool" field.
12. Enter a unique identifier for this form in the "form_id" field.
13. Enter the file name you'd like to save your feedback as in the "output_file" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`1`, text, required: false): This is where you describe your experience with our product or service. Keep it short and concise.
* **Product Rating** (`2`, select_one, required: false): Rate the quality of our product or service. Choose from "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **Product Features** (`3`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the features that were available on our product or service.
* **Service Rating** (`4`, number, required: false): Rate the service you received. Enter a number between 1 and 10.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`5`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose how satisfied you are overall with our product or service. Select from "Very High", "High", "Medium", "Low", or "Very Low".
* **Email** (`6`, email, required: false): Enter your email address if you'd like us to follow up with you about your feedback.
* **Phone Number** (`7`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number if you'd like us to follow up with you about your feedback.
* **Additional Comments** (`8`, text, required: false): Share any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.
* **Feedback** (`9`, note, required: false): Enter any feedback about your experience.
* **Category** (`10`, select_one, required: false): Choose the category of your feedback. Select from "Customer Service" or "Other".
* **Assigned Tool** (`11`, select_one, required: false): Select which tool was assigned to you.
* **Form ID** (`12`, number, required: false): Enter a unique identifier for this form.
* **Output File** (`13`, text, required: false): Enter the file name you'd like to save your feedback as.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all the fields that apply to your experience.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please share them in the "Additional Comments" field.
* If you'd like us to follow up with you about your feedback, enter your email or phone number in the respective fields.
* Choose the category and assigned tool that best describe your feedback.
* Enter a unique identifier for this form and a file name to save your feedback.
