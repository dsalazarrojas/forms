# Contact Us Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contact Us form is designed to allow customers to easily contact our support team for help with various topics, including technical support, general questions, and other inquiries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your first name, last name, and email address.
2. Select the subject of your message from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter a brief description of your issue or question in the message field.
4. Choose how you would like to be contacted (email, phone, or no preference).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Please enter your last name.
* **Your Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Your Phone Number** (Text, Optional): If you would like to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number.
* **Country** (Text, Optional): Enter your country of residence.
* **City** (Text, Optional): Enter your city of residence.
* **Subject** (Select One, Required): Choose a subject that best describes your issue or question.
* **Message / Description of the Issue** (Text, Required): Enter a brief description of your issue or question.
* **Sign up for our newsletter?** (Select One, Optional): Choose to opt-in or opt-out of our newsletter.
* **How should we contact you?** (Select One, Required): Choose how you would like us to contact you (email, phone, or no preference).
