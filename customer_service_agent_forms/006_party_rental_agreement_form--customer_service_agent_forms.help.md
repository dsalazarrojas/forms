# Party Rental Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Party Rental Agreement Form is a document used to outline the terms and conditions of a rental space or equipment rental between the party hosting and the party renting the space or equipment. This form helps to establish a clear understanding between the parties involved, ensuring a smooth and successful rental experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Party Rental Agreement" field with a brief description of the rental agreement.
2. Provide details about the rental space or equipment in the "Party Space Rental Details" field.
3. Specify the equipment being rented and its details in the "Rental Equipment Details" field.
4. If applicable, describe the catering services provided in the "Catering Details" field.
5. Review and agree to the payment terms in the "Payment Terms" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Party Rental Agreement
* **Party Rental Agreement** (`party_rental_form`, text, required): Briefly describe the rental agreement, including the terms and conditions of the rental.

### Party Space Rental Details
* **Party Space Rental Details** (`party_space_rental_details`, text, required): Provide details about the rental space, such as the address, size, and amenities.

### Rental Equipment Details
* **Rental Equipment Details** (`rental_equipment_details`, text, required): Specify the equipment being rented, including its type, condition, and any additional features or accessories.

### Catering Details
* **Catering Details** (`catering_details`, text, optional): If applicable, describe the catering services provided, including the type of food, quantity, and any specific requirements.

### Payment Terms
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, required): Review and agree to the payment terms, including the rental fee, payment method, and any late payment fees.
