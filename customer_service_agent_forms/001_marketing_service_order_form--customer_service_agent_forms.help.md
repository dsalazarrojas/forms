# Marketing Service Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about marketing services ordered by clients or customers. It is designed to be completed by the client or customer, and the gathered information will be used to provide services as per their requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide a detailed description of your project in the Project Details field.
2. Enter the name and contact information of the lead person responsible for the project in the Lead Person field.
3. Specify the services you require in the Service Requests field.
4. Choose the type of service you need from the Service Type dropdown list.
5. Enter the project deadline in the Project Deadline field.
6. Select the desired start date for the service in the Service Start Date field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, required/optional): This field is where you can provide a detailed description of your project.
* **Lead Person** (`project_lead`, text, required/optional): This field is for the name and contact information of the person responsible for the project.
* **Service Requests** (`service_requests`, text, required/optional): Here, you can list the specific services you require for your project.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of service you need from the dropdown list.
* **Project Deadline** (`project_deadline`, date, required/optional): Enter the deadline for the project.
* **Service Start Date** (`service_start_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date when you want the service to start.
