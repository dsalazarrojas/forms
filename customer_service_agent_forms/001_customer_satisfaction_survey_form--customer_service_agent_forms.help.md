# customer_satisfaction_survey_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The customer_satisfaction_survey_form is a form used to collect customer feedback and satisfaction data. This form helps us understand how well we are meeting our customer's expectations and identifies areas where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer the questions on each page of the form to the best of your ability.
2.  Ensure that all required fields are completed. If a field is marked as "required", it must be filled out.
3.  If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page**: This page is used to start the form. Please complete any required fields on this page.
* **Second Page (Select One)**: Select one of the options provided to indicate your satisfaction with this experience.
* **What is your satisfaction with this experience? (Select Multiple)**: Please select all the options that apply to your experience.
* **Feedback**: This field is used to provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Email**: If you have an email address associated with your account, please enter it here. This will help us follow up with you if needed.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by our team.
* **Date**: If you have a specific date related to your experience, please enter it here.
* **Time**: If you have a specific time related to your experience, please enter it here.
* **Customer ID**: This field is used to identify the customer you are providing feedback on. If you are unsure, please leave this field blank.
* **Customer Name**: This field is used to identify the customer you are providing feedback on. Please enter the customer's name as it is listed in our system.
* **Customer ID (optional)**: This field is used to identify the customer you are providing feedback on. If you are unsure, please leave this field blank. 

Please note that some fields are not required, but it is essential to complete them if you can. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team.
