# service enquiry form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Enquiry Form is a tool used to collect and manage customer service enquiries. It is designed to help our team efficiently resolve customer issues and provide a good experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the provided fields.
2. Select the type of service you are enquiring about.
3. Provide a clear description of your concern.
4. If applicable, select the date and time of your service enquiry.
5. Add any additional remarks or information that may be helpful for our team to understand your issue.
6. Choose the option of whether you would like to be assigned to a specific team member.
7. Review your form carefully before submitting to ensure all fields are completed accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **service_enquiry_form** (Text Field, Required: False): This field is the main title of the form and serves as a label to identify the type of form you are completing.
* **name** (Text Field, Required: False): Please enter your full name so our team can address you properly.
* **e-mail** (Email Field, Required: False): Provide your email address so our team can contact you regarding your enquiry.
* **phone** (Text Field, Required: False): If you would like to be contacted via phone, please enter your phone number.
* **service_enquiry_type** (Select Multiple, Required: False): Choose the type of service you are enquiring about. This will help our team direct your issue to the right department.
* **description** (Note Field, Required: False): Describe your issue or concern in detail. This will enable our team to understand your problem and resolve it efficiently.
* **service_enquiry_date** (Date Field, Required: False): If your issue is related to a specific date, please select the date of your service enquiry.
* **service_enquiry_time** (Time Field, Required: False): If applicable, select the time of your service enquiry.
* **service_enquiry_remarks** (Note Field, Required: False): Add any additional information that may be helpful for our team to understand your issue.
* **assigned_to** (Select One, Required: False): Choose whether you would like to be assigned to a specific team member. This will help our team prioritize your issue accordingly.
