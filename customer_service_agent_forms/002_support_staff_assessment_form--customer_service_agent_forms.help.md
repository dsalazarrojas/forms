# Support Staff Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the performance and development needs of support staff members. It helps gather essential information about their role, employment history, and areas for improvement to inform decisions about staff development and performance evaluations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the **Introduction** page, which provides a brief overview of the form's purpose.
2. On the **Staff Name** page, enter the name of the staff member being assessed.
3. On the **Role** page, select the role of the staff member from the provided options (Support, Customer Service, Operations, or Other).
4. On the **Start Date** page, enter the date the staff member started their employment.
5. On the **End Date** page, enter the date the staff member ended their employment (if applicable).
6. On the **Manager** page, enter the name of the staff member's manager.
7. On the **Evaluation Date** page, enter the date of the last evaluation.
8. On the **Overall Performance** page, describe the staff member's overall performance.
9. On the **Strengths** page, list the staff member's strengths.
10. On the **Weaknesses** page, list the staff member's weaknesses.
11. On the **Development Plan** page, outline the plan for the staff member's development and areas of improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Staff Name (Text)**: Enter the name of the staff member being assessed.

* **Manager (Text)**: Enter the name of the staff member's manager.

* **Weaknesses (Text)**: List the staff member's weaknesses.
