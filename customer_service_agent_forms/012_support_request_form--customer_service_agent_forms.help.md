# Support Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The support request form is designed to collect information from customers who are seeking support or assistance with an issue they are experiencing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form in the order provided.
2. Complete all required fields (those marked as "required" in the form).
3. Provide as much detail as possible for optional fields.
4. Review your submission before submitting it for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **support_request_form** (`support_request_form`, text, required: false): This is the title of your support request. Please enter a brief summary of your issue.
* **customer_name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification or contact information.
* **problem_description** (`problem_description`, text, required: false): Describe the problem you are experiencing in as much detail as possible.
* **request_question** (`request_question`, text, required: false): If you have a specific question or request related to your issue, please enter it here.
* **issue_date** (`issue_date`, date, required: false): If applicable, please enter the date you first experienced the issue.
* **issue_time** (`issue_time`, time, required: false): If applicable, please enter the time you first experienced the issue.
* **am_pm** (`am_pm`, select_multiple, required: true): Please select the AM/PM of the issue time.
* **email** (`email`, email, required: false): Please enter your email address so we can contact you with updates on your support request.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): If you would like to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number.
* **category** (`category`, text, required: false): If you have a specific category for your support request, please enter it here.
* **description** (`description`, text, required: false): If you have any additional information or context about your issue, please enter it here.
* **assigned_to** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required: false): Select the customer support agent you would like to be assigned to.

Please note that some fields are optional, but providing additional information will help us assist you more efficiently. If you are unsure about what to enter in a field, please don't hesitate to contact us for further clarification.
