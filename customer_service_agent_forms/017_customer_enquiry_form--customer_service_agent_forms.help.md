# customer_enquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The customer enquiry form is a tool used to collect and process customer feedback, concerns, or questions regarding a product or service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields and provide as much detail as possible.
2. Be as clear and concise as possible in your responses.
3. Ensure your contact information is up-to-date and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **customer_enquiry_form_1** (`customer_enquiry_form_1_label`, text, required: false)
	* This field is not required, but it is useful for providing any general comments or questions you might have about the product or service.
### 2. **customer_enquiry_name** (`customer_enquiry_name_label`, text, required: false)
	* This field is not required, but it is useful for the customer service agent to know who is inquiring.
### 3. **customer_enquiry_email** (`customer_enquiry_email_label`, email, required: false)
	* This field is not required, but if you have a valid email address, it would be helpful for the customer service agent to contact you directly.
### 4. **customer_enquiry_form_2** (`customer_enquiry_form_2_label`, text, required: false)
	* This field is not required, but it is useful for providing more information about your inquiry.
### 5. **customer_enquiry_form_3** (`customer_enquiry_form_3_label`, text, required: false)
	* This field is not required, but it is useful for providing additional details about your concern or suggestion.
