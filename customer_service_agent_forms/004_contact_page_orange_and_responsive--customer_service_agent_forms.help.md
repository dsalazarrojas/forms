# Contact Page Orange and Responsive - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contact Page Orange and Responsive form is designed to allow customers to get in touch with our team, provide feedback, share their experience, or inquire about our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Please enter your name in the "Your Name" field.
2. Provide your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
3. Describe the subject of your message in the "Subject of your message" field.
4. Write your message in the "Your Message" field.
5. Choose a feedback category that best describes your experience or feedback.
6. Rate your overall experience with us by selecting one of the options provided.
7. Indicate whether you would recommend us to a friend or colleague.
8. If applicable, provide your permission to use your feedback as a testimonial.
9. Let us know if you require a response to your message.
10. Select your preferred response channel (if applicable).
11. Finally, decide if you would like to subscribe to our newsletter (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`visitor_name`, **text**, required): Enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, **email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Subject of your message** (`inquiry_subject`, **text, required): Briefly describe the topic of your message.
* **Your Message** (`main_message`, **text, required): Share your thoughts, questions, or comments with us.
* **Feedback Category** (`feedback_category`, **select_one, required): Choose a category that best describes your experience or feedback.
* **How would you rate your overall experience with us?** (`experience_rating`, **select_one, required): Rate your experience using the provided scale.
* **Would you recommend us to a friend or colleague?** (`recommendation_likelihood`, **select_one, required): Indicate your likelihood of recommending us.
* **May we use your feedback as a testimonial?** (`testimonial_permission`, **select_one, required): Provide your consent for us to use your feedback as a testimonial.
* **Do you require a response to this message?** (`follow_up_needed`, **select_one, required): Let us know if you need a response to your message.
* **If yes, how should we reach you?** (`preferred_response_channel`, **select_one, optional): Choose your preferred response channel (if applicable).
* **Stay updated with our orange newsletter!** (`newsletter_subscription`, **select_one, optional): Decide if you would like to subscribe to our newsletter.
