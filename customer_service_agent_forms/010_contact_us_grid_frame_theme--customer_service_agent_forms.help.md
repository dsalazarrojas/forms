# Contact Us Grid Frame Theme - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow customers to contact the company's customer service team with any questions or issues they may have. It is intended to be a comprehensive contact form that gathers essential information from customers to effectively respond to their inquiries.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "First Name" field with your first name.
2. Fill out the "Last Name" field with your last name.
3. Enter your valid email address in the "Your Email" field.
4. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Your Phone Number" field. This is optional.
5. Select the subject of your inquiry from the "Inquiry Subject" field.
6. Enter a message or description of the issue in the "Message / Description of the Issue" field.
7. Indicate whether you would like a response to this message by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Do you require a response to this message?" field.
8. Choose how you would like to be contacted by selecting your preferred method from the "How should we contact you?" field.
9. Decide whether you would like to sign up for our newsletter by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Sign up for our newsletter?" field. This is optional.
10. Finally, indicate how you heard about us by selecting the relevant option from the "How did you hear about us?" field. This is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name in this field.
* **Your Email** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your valid email address in this field. This will be used for follow-up communication.
* **Your Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you have one. This is optional.
* **Inquiry Subject** (`subject_header`, select_one, required): Select the subject of your inquiry from the provided options.
* **Message / Description of the Issue** (`message_details`, text, required): Enter a message or description of the issue in this field.
* **Do you require a response to this message?** (`follow_up_needed`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you would like a response to this message by selecting "True" or "False".
* **How should we contact you?** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, required): Select how you would like to be contacted from the options provided.
* **Sign up for our newsletter?** (`newsletter_opt_in`, select_one, optional): Decide whether you would like to sign up for our newsletter by selecting "True" or "False". This is optional.
* **How did you hear about us?** (`referral_source`, select_one, optional): Indicate how you heard about us by selecting the relevant option.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- If you have any issues with your inquiry, do not hesitate to contact us.
- We will respond to your inquiry as soon as possible, if you require a response.
