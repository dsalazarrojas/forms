# Customer Service Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to gather feedback from customers on their experience with the company's service.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Rate your satisfaction level with the company's service.
2. Describe your experience with the company's service.
3. Specify who you spoke with during your interaction.
4. If you have any additional comments, provide them.
5. Rate your satisfaction level with the company's service.
6. Describe what the company did well during your interaction.
7. Describe what the company could do better.
8. Specify who contacted you.
9. Provide any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How satisfied are you with our service?** (`customer_service_satisfaction_survey`, number, optional): This question asks you to rate your overall satisfaction level with the company's service. Choose a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is not satisfied and 5 is very satisfied.
* **What was your experience like during your interaction with us?** (`customer_service_satisfaction_level`, text, optional): Describe your experience with the company's service. Be as detailed as possible to help us understand what we did well or poorly.
* **Who did you speak with during your interaction?** (`customer_service_agent`, select_one, optional): Specify who you spoke with during your interaction with the company's service. Choose from the options provided.
* **What could we improve on?** (`customer_service_satisfaction_why`, text, optional): Describe what you think the company could improve on based on your experience.
* **What did we do well?** (`customer_service_satisfaction_what_we_did`, text, optional): Describe what the company did well during your interaction.
* **What could we do better?** (`customer_service_satisfaction_what_we_could_do`, text, optional): Describe what the company could do better during your interaction.
* **Who contacted you?** (`customer_service_satisfaction_who_contacted`, select_multiple, optional): Specify who contacted you. Choose from the options provided.
* **Additional comments** (`customer_service_satisfaction_additional_comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments about your experience with the company's service.
* **How would you rate your satisfaction level?** (`customer_service_satisfaction_satisfaction_level_2`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction level with the company's service. Choose a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is not satisfied and 5 is very satisfied.
* **What did we do well 2?** (`customer_service_satisfaction_what_we_did_2`, text, optional): Describe what the company did well during your interaction.
* **What could we do better 2?** (`customer_service_satisfaction_what_we_could_do_2`, text, optional): Describe what the company could do better during your interaction.
