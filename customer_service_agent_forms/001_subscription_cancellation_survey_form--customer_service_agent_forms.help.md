# Subscription Cancellation Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers who are cancelling their subscription services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the reason(s) for your subscription cancellation from the list.
   1. If you select "Other", please provide a detailed explanation in the "Other Cancellation Reason" field.

2. If you are cancelling multiple services, please select all relevant services from the "What Service Are You Cancelling" dropdown list.
   1. If you select "Other" from this dropdown, please provide a detailed explanation in the "What Service Are You Cancelling" field.

3. Rate our service on a scale from 1 to 4 (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
   1. If you selected "Fair" or "Poor", please explain why in the "Why Did You Rate Our Service" field.

4. Select your level of satisfaction with our service and support.
   1. If you selected "Fair" or "Poor", please explain why in the "Why Were Our Support" field.

5. Provide any final comments or suggestions on how we can improve our service and support.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Why Are You Cancelling** (`Cancellation Reason`, select_multiple): Select the reason(s) for your subscription cancellation.
* **What Was Your Last Payment Date** (`Cancellation Date`, date): If you are cancelling your subscription, please provide the date of your last payment.
* **What Service Are You Cancelling** (`Cancellation Service`, select_multiple): If you are cancelling multiple services, please select all relevant services.
* **Other Cancellation Reason** (`Cancellation Reason Other`, text): Provide a detailed explanation for cancelling your subscription.
* **Were You Satisfied** (`Cancellation Satisfied`, select_one): Select whether you were satisfied with our service.
* **Why Were You Not Satisfied** (`Cancellation Satisfied Note`, text): If you selected "False" for satisfaction, please explain why.
* **Would You Recommend** (`Cancellation Recommend`, select_one): Select whether you would recommend our service.
* **Why Would You Recommend** (`Cancellation Recommend Note`, text): If you selected "False" for recommendation, please explain why.
* **Comments** (`Cancellation Comments`, text): Provide any additional comments or suggestions on how we can improve our service and support.
* **How Rate Our Service** (`Cancellation Service Rating`, select_one): Rate our service on a scale from 1 to 4 (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Why Did You Rate Our Service** (`Cancellation Service Rating Note`, text): If you selected "Fair" or "Poor", please explain why.
* **How Can We Improve** (`Cancellation Our Service Improve`, text): Provide suggestions on how we can improve our service.
* **What Actions Should We Take** (`Cancellation Our Service Action`, text): Provide specific actions we can take to improve our service.
* **How Was Our Support** (`Cancellation Support`, select_one): Select whether our support was satisfactory.
