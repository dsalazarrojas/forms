# Agent Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agent Feedback Form is a crucial tool for customers to provide feedback on their experience with our agents. It helps us identify areas of strength and weakness in our agent's performance and allows us to make data-driven decisions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Enter your information**: Please fill in your full name, email address, and phone number (if you provide a phone number).
2. **Choose the type of service**: Select the type of service you received from the agent (e.g., Buying, Selling, Renting, Property Management, Consultation, or Other).
3. **Select the transaction date**: Enter the date of the transaction or service provided.
4. **Rate the agent's performance**: Evaluate the agent's communication skills, responsiveness, professionalism, knowledge and expertise, and punctuality.
5. **Provide feedback on what you liked most**: Share what you appreciated most about working with the agent.
6. **Identify areas for improvement**: Suggest areas where the agent could improve.
7. **Recommend the agent**: Decide if you would recommend this agent to friends or family.
8. **Add any additional comments**: Share any additional thoughts or comments about your experience with the agent.
9. **Rate your likelihood to use again**: Assess how likely you are to use this agent in the future.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents (e.g., driver's license, passport).
* **Customer Email**: Enter your valid email address.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number (if you wish to share it).
* **Agent Name**: Enter the name of the agent you interacted with.
* **Type of Service**: Select the type of service you received from the agent (e.g., Buying, Selling, Renting, Property Management, Consultation, or Other).
* **Transaction Date**: Enter the date of the transaction or service provided (if you can recall it).
* **Overall Satisfaction Rating**: Evaluate your overall satisfaction with the agent's service, choosing from Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.
* **Communication Rating**: Rate the agent's communication skills as Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, or Poor.
* **Responsiveness Rating**: Assess the agent's responsiveness as Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, or Poor.
* **Professionalism Rating**: Evaluate the agent's professionalism as Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, or Poor.
* **Knowledge and Expertise Rating**: Rate the agent's knowledge and expertise as Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, or Poor.
* **Negotiation Skills Rating**: Evaluate the agent's negotiation skills as Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, or Poor.
* **Punctuality Rating**: Assess the agent's punctuality as Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, or Poor.
* **What Did You Like Most?**: Share what you appreciated most about working with the agent.
* **Areas for Improvement**: Suggest areas where the agent could improve.
* **Would You Recommend?**: Decide if you would recommend this agent to friends or family (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Not Sure, Probably No, Definitely No).
* **Additional Comments**: Share any additional thoughts or comments about your experience with the agent.
* **Likelihood to Use Again**: Assess how likely you are to use this agent in the future (Very Likely, Likely, Neutral, Unlikely, Very Unlikely).
