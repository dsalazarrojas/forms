# return_and_credit_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The return and credit form is used to document and process returns and credits for customers and sellers. It captures essential information for both the customer and the seller, ensuring that the return and credit process is handled efficiently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the details of the returned product in the "Returned Product" field.
2. Specify the reason for the return in the "Reason For Return" field.
3. Enter the reason for the refund in the "Reason For Refund" field.
4. Enter the refund amount in the "Refund Amount" field.
5. Add any additional seller message or comments in the "Seller Message" field.
6. Add any additional buyer message or comments in the "Buyer Message" field.
7. Enter the return date in the "Return Date" field.
8. Enter the return time in the "Return Time" field.
9. Enter the product ID in the "Product ID" field.
10. Enter any additional customer notes in the "Customer Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Returned Product** (Returned Product, text, required): Enter the product being returned, including its description and any relevant details.
* **Reason For Return** (Reason For Return, text, optional): Specify the reason why the product is being returned. This can include issues with the product, incorrect ordering, or other relevant details.
* **Reason For Refund** (Reason For Refund, text, optional): Explain the reason for the refund. This can include issues with the product, incorrect payment, or other relevant details.
* **Refund Amount** (Refund Amount, number, optional): Enter the amount to be refunded for the returned product.
* **Seller Message** (Seller Message, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes from the seller, such as shipping updates or further instructions.
* **Buyer Message** (Buyer Message, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes from the buyer, such as shipping updates or further instructions.
* **Return Date** (Return Date, date, optional): Enter the date the product was returned.
* **Return Time** (Return Time, time, optional): Enter the time the product was returned.
* **Product ID** (Product ID, number, optional): Enter the ID of the product being returned.
* **Customer Note** (Customer Note, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments from the customer, such as product issues or other relevant information.
