# Refund Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to process customer refund requests. It allows customers to provide information about their refund request, including the reason for the request, the amount to be refunded, and other relevant details. The form is also used by customer service agents to process and manage customer refund requests.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*). 
2. Select the relevant options from the dropdown menus, if applicable.
3. Enter any additional comments or notes as needed.
4. Review your submission before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info** (`customer_service_agent_forms_customer_info`, Text, Optional): Enter your customer information here.
* **Reason for Refund** (`customer_service_agent_forms_reason_for_refund`, Text, Optional): Describe the reason for your refund request.
* **Refund Amount** (`customer_service_agent_forms_refund_amount`, Number, Optional): Enter the amount you are requesting to be refunded.
* **Customer Service Agent** (`customer_service_agent_forms_customer_service_agent`, Select One, Optional): Choose the customer service agent who will be handling your request.
* **Customer Service Agent Comments** (`customer_service_agent_forms_customer_service_agent_comments`, Text, Optional): Enter any comments or feedback for the customer service agent.
* **Customer Approval** (`customer_service_agent_forms_customer_approval`, Select One, Optional): Choose whether your refund request is approved or declined.
* **Customer Refund Status** (`customer_service_agent_forms_customer_refund_status`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the current status of your refund request.
* **Customer Service Agent Notes** (`customer_service_agent_forms_customer_service_agent_notes`, Note, Optional): Enter any notes or comments from the customer service agent.
* **Refund Request Date** (`customer_service_agent_forms_refund_request_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date of your refund request.
* **Refunded Amount** (`customer_service_agent_forms_refunded_amount`, Number, Optional): Enter the amount that has been refunded.
* **Agent Refund Comments** (`customer_service_agent_forms_agent_refund_comments`, Text, Optional): Enter any comments or feedback from the customer service agent.
* **Customer Refund Type** (`customer_service_agent_forms_customer_refund_type`, Select One, Optional): Choose the type of refund you are requesting.
