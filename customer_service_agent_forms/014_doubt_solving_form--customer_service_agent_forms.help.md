# Doubt Solving Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool for customers to submit their doubts and inquiries about their account to the customer service team.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your customer details, including your name and other relevant information.
2. Describe your doubt or inquiry in the "What's your doubt?" field.
3. If you have a preferred email address, provide it in the "Email" field. This is useful for the customer service team to contact you directly.
4. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field.
5. Once you have completed all the fields, click on the "Submit" button to send your doubt to the customer service team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details**: Enter your name and other relevant information about yourself.
* **What's your doubt?**: Describe your doubt or inquiry in detail, so that the customer service team can understand your concerns.
* **Email**: Enter your preferred email address, which the customer service team can use to contact you directly.
* **Phone**: If you have a phone number, enter it here so that the customer service team can call you back.
* **Submit**: Click this button to send your doubt to the customer service team for resolution.
