# Full Width Contact Page Responsive - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for customers who want to get in touch with our team or provide feedback on our services. It's meant to help our customer service agents quickly and efficiently assist customers with their inquiries or concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the Customer Name field with your name as it appears on your identification.
2. Enter a valid email address in the Customer Email field, which will be used for communication.
3. If you have a phone number, provide it in the Customer Phone Number field for our convenience.
4. In the Message/Comments field, type any comments or questions you have, and if applicable, provide your Customer ID Number.
5. Answer "Yes" or "No" to "How Did You Hear About Us?" depending on your source of information.
6. Select the methods by which you would like us to contact you (e.g., "Yes", "No", or "Other").
7. Choose your preferred contact method from the options provided.
8. If you have any additional information to share, type it in the Additional Information field. This is optional.
9. If you have any other questions or concerns, type them in the Any Other Question field. This is optional.
10. Confirm your email address in the Confirm Customer Email field.
11. Finally, let us know if you're happy with our service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Name (1)**: A text field where you can type your name as it appears on your identification.
  - Type in your full name.
- **Customer Email (2)**: A field for your valid email address.
  - Enter a real email address that we can use to contact you.
- **Customer Phone Number (3)**: A text field for your phone number (if you have one).
  - If you don't have a phone number, you can skip this field.
- **Message/Comments (4)**: A text field for any comments or questions you might have.
  - Be as detailed as possible about your inquiry or concern.
- **Customer ID Number (5)**: A field for your customer ID (if applicable).
  - If you don't have a customer ID, you can skip this field.
- **How Did You Hear About Us? (6)**: A single-select field with three options ("Yes", "No", "Other").
  - Select "Yes" if you heard about us through a specific method, "No" if you didn't, or "Other" if it was something else.
- **How Can We Contact You? (7)**: A multi-select field with "Yes", "No", "Other" options.
  - Select any method by which you'd like to be contacted.
- **Preferred Contact Method (8)**: A single-select field with options provided.
  - Choose the method you'd like to use to contact you.
- **Additional Information (9)**: A text field for any extra details you'd like to share (optional).
  - If you have something to add, type it in here.
- **Any Other Question (10)**: A text field for any other inquiries or concerns you might have (optional).
  - If you have something to ask, type it in here.
- **Confirm Customer Email (11)**: A field to confirm your email address.
  - Retype your email address for verification purposes.
- **Are You Happy with Our Service? (12)**: A radio field with three options ("Yes", "No", "Other").
  - Answer truthfully about your experience with our service. 

## Tips
- Please ensure your contact information (email and phone number) is accurate and up to date.
- If you have trouble filling out a field, try to type it in as it should be filled out.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, don't hesitate to ask.
