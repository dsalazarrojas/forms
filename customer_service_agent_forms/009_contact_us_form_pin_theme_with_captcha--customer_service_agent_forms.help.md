# Contact Us Form Pin Theme with Captcha - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow customers to reach out to the support team and receive help with any issues or questions they may have. Please fill out the form below to get started.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as prompted. Please note that some fields are required, while others are optional. Make sure to review the instructions for each field carefully before submitting your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Full Name** (full_name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Your Email Address** (contact_email, email, required): Enter the email address you wish to use for follow-up communication.
* **Your Phone Number** (contact_phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you wish to provide it.
* **Inquiry Subject** (subject_header, text, required): Briefly describe the subject of your inquiry.
* **Message / Description of the Issue** (message_content, text, required): Please provide a detailed description of your issue or question.
* **Security Verification** (section_security, note, required): Please read and comply with the security verification process.
* **Captcha Verification** (user_captcha, text, required): Enter the characters shown in the image below to verify your human identity.
* **Preferred PIN for Follow-up** (security_pin, number, optional): Enter a 4-digit numeric code for future authentication (optional).
* **Do you consent to the collection of your IP address for security purposes?** (user_ip_consent, select_one, required): Choose 'True' to allow the collection of your IP address for security purposes.
* **I agree to the terms and conditions** (privacy_agreement, select_one, required): Choose 'True' to indicate your agreement to the terms and conditions.
