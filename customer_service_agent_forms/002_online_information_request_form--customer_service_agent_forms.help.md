# Online Information Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Information Request Form is designed to collect information from customers on their service needs and requirements. It's a simple form that guides them to provide essential details about their inquiry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the "Name" field with your full name or the name of the organization you represent.
2. Enter your "Contact Email" in the format of `example@email.com`.
3. Provide your "Contact Phone" number.
4. Select at least one service you are interested in from the "Services" dropdown menu. This could be Environmental Design, Landscaping, or Treatment.
5. If the above services do not apply, you may type in "Other Services" to specify your requirements.
6. Indicate what "Materials or Training" you require, by selecting either "Materials" or "Training" from the dropdown menu.
7. In the "Additional Comments" section, type any extra information or comments about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Online Information Request Form**: This is the title of the form.
* **Name**: Enter your full name or the name of the organization you represent.
	+ Required: No
	+ Type: text
* **Organization**: Enter the name of the organization you are from.
	+ Required: No
	+ Type: text
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address in the format `example@email.com`.
	+ Required: No
	+ Type: email
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number.
	+ Required: No
	+ Type: text
* **Services**: Select at least one service you are interested in:
	+ Required: Yes
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Options: Environmental Design, Landscaping, Treatment
* **Other Services**: If the above services do not apply, enter your specific service requirement here.
	+ Required: No
	+ Type: text
* **Materials or Training**: Select what you require:
	+ Required: Yes
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Options: Materials, Training
* **Additional Comments**: In this section, type any extra information or comments about your request.

## Tips
* Please ensure your contact email address is valid and can be reached by our team.
* Be specific when indicating services or materials requested to ensure accurate information is sent to the right teams.
* If you have any doubts or questions about the form, please contact us directly.
