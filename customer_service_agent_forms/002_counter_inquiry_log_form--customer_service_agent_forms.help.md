# Counter Inquiry Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Counter Inquiry Log Form is used to record and document customer inquiries in a consistent and structured way.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Customer Name** in the first field. This is the name of the person who made the inquiry.
2. Enter the **Customer Phone** in the second field. This is the contact phone number of the customer.
3. Enter the **Customer Email** in the third field. This is the email address of the customer (if provided).
4. Select the **Inquiry Type** from the dropdown menu. This indicates the type of inquiry made by the customer.
5. Enter the **Date** of the inquiry. This is the date the customer made the inquiry.
6. Enter the **Customer Message** in the text field. This is the message or description of the inquiry made by the customer.
7. Enter your **Name** and any **Agent Comment** if applicable.
8. You may also add a **Prophets Message** if required.
9. Finally, list any **Follow-up Actions** taken by you as the agent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Service Agent** (text, optional): This field is for you, the customer service agent, to identify yourself on the form.
  - **Customer Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the customer who made the inquiry.
  - **Customer Phone** (text, required): Enter the contact phone number of the customer.
  - **Customer Email** (email, optional): Enter the email address of the customer (if provided).
  - **Inquiry Type** (select_one, required): Select the type of inquiry made by the customer. Options are 'Yes' and 'No'.
  - **Date** (date, required): Enter the date the customer made the inquiry.
  - **Customer Message** (text, required): Enter a description of the inquiry made by the customer.
  - **Agent Name** (text, optional): Enter your name as the customer service agent for reference.
  - **Agent Comment** (text, optional): Add any comments or notes about your interaction with the customer.
  - **Prophets Message** (note, optional): Add a note or message from the Prophet (or any other relevant information).
  - **Follow-up Actions** (text, optional): List any follow-up actions taken by you as the agent.
