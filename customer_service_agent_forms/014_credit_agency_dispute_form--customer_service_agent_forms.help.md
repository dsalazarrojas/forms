# Credit Agency Dispute Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Credit Agency Dispute Form is used to gather information about a credit agency dispute, which can include issues with creditors, account status, or debt-related problems. This form helps customer service agents collect necessary details to effectively resolve these disputes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name and contact information as the customer service agent.
2.  Provide the date of the dispute.
3.  Choose the type of dispute (e.g., dispute on my report with a creditor, dispute on my account status, or dispute on my debt).
4.  Select the creditor involved in the dispute.
5.  Enter a brief description of the dispute.
6.  Provide contact information for the customer who is disputing the credit agency.
7.  Attach any supporting documents, such as proof of payment or letters from the creditor or collector.
8.  Enter any additional notes or comments about the dispute.
