# New Customer Set Up Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Customer Set Up Form Template is a comprehensive form designed to gather necessary information about new customers. This form is intended for use by customer service agents to create accurate and up-to-date customer profiles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's name in the "customer_name" field.
2. Provide the customer's email address in the "customer_email" field.
3. Enter the customer's phone number in the "phone_number" field.
4. Enter the customer's date and time of birth in the "date_of_birth" and "time_of_birth" fields, respectively.
5. Enter the customer's address in the "customer_address" field.
6. If necessary, add any relevant customer notes in the "customer_note" field.
7. Indicate whether a tool is assigned to the customer by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the "assigned_tool" field.
8. Select whether the customer is assigned to a customer service agent by selecting multiple options in the "customer_service_agent" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **new_customer_set_up_form_template** (Required): This field is the title of the form that you are currently completing.
* **customer_name**: Enter the name of the customer you are setting up.
* **customer_email**: Enter the email address of the customer for communication purposes.
* **phone_number**: Enter the customer's phone number for further contact.
* **date_of_birth**: Enter the customer's date of birth in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **time_of_birth**: Enter the customer's time of birth in 24-hour format "HH:MM".
* **customer_address**: Enter the customer's address for delivery purposes.
* **customer_note**: Add any relevant notes about the customer, if necessary.
* **assigned_tool**: Select 'Yes' if a tool is assigned to the customer, 'No' otherwise.
* **customer_service_agent**: Select multiple options to assign the customer to customer service agents, or leave blank for no assignment.
