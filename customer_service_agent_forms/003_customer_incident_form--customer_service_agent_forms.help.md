# customer_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The customer incident form is used to document and manage customer incidents reported to our company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please ensure you have a clear understanding of the customer's issue and the context surrounding the incident.
2. Complete each field as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **customer_name** (text, required): Enter the customer's name in this field.
* **customer_email** (email, required): Enter the customer's email address in this format: `example@example.com`.
* **incident_description** (text, required): Provide a detailed description of the incident, including relevant context and any specific details that may be helpful for issue resolution.
* **incident_date** (date, required): Enter the date the incident occurred in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **incident_time** (time, required): Enter the time the incident occurred in the format `HH:MM:SS`.
* **customer_phone** (text, optional): If the customer has a phone number, enter it here.
* **customer_service_agent** (text, optional): Enter your name or the name of the customer service agent handling the incident.
* **issue_resolution** (text, optional): If the issue has been resolved, provide a brief description of the resolution.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any information, please contact the customer service team for guidance.
* Please keep the description concise but informative, providing relevant details to aid in issue resolution.
