# Bra Size Detection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bra Size Detection Form is designed to collect information from customers to provide personalized bra size recommendations based on their physical characteristics, preferences, and lifestyle.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the customer information section, including full name and email address.
2. Provide your underbust and bust measurements in inches.
3. Select your preferred coverage, cup shape, and strap style.
4. Indicate any current fit issues you may be experiencing.
5. Choose your primary activity level and preferred material.
6. Select your budget range and whether you consent to receive size recommendations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`customer_name`, required/true): Provide your first and last name for identification purposes.
* **Email Address** (`customer_email`, required/true): Enter your email address to receive tailored size and style suggestions.
* **Phone Number** (`customer_phone`, required/false): Provide your phone number for any follow-up communication.
* **Underbust Measurement** (`band_measurement`, required/true): Measure directly under your bust in inches.
* **Full Bust Measurement** (`bust_measurement`, required/true): Measure around the fullest part of your bust in inches.
* **Preferred Coverage** (`coverage_preference`, required/false): Choose from Full, Moderate, or Minimal coverage.
* **Preferred Cup Shape** (`cup_shape_preference`, required/false): Select from Balconette, Full cup, or Demi-cup.
* **Strap Preference** (`strap_preference`, required/false): Choose from Standard, Convertible, Strapless, or Racerback.
* **Current Fit Problems** (`fit_issues`, required/false): Select any issues you are experiencing, such as Band too tight, Band too loose, or Cups overflow.
* **Primary Activity Level** (`activity_level`, required/false): Choose from Everyday wear, Athletic activity, Work/professional, Special occasions, or Mixed activities.
* **Sports or High Impact Activities** (`sports_type`, required/false): If applicable, list any sports or activities that may affect your bra fit.
* **Preferred Material** (`material_preference`, required/false): Select from Cotton, Microfiber, Lace, Satin, or Gel.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, required/false): Choose from Under 30 dollars, 30-50 dollars, 50-75 dollars, 75-100 dollars, or Over 100 dollars.
* **I Consent to Sizing Recommendations** (`sizing_consent`, required/true): Check to receive personalized size recommendations.

Note: Fields marked as required/true must be filled out to proceed with the size detection process.
