# Contact Page Black and Responsive - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contact Page Black and Responsive form is designed to collect information from customers who need assistance or would like to provide feedback. Please refer to the form's introduction for more information on the types of inquiries that can be submitted.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Contact Our Support Team" button to access the form.
2. Enter your Full Name in the "Full Name" field.
3. Enter your Email Address in the "Email Address" field.
4. Select the relevant "Inquiry Type" that best describes your question or issue.
5. Provide your contact number in the "Phone Number" field (if applicable).
6. Enter a detailed description of your inquiry or issue in the "Message Details" field.
7. Optionally, rate your overall experience with the "Overall Website Satisfaction" field.
8. Indicate how you found us with the "How did you find us?" field.
9. Select your preferred response time with the "Preferred Response Time" field.
10. Optionally, provide feedback on how to improve our service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (2)**: Your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address (3)**: Your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number (4)**: If you would like to be called back, please provide your phone number.
* **Inquiry Type (5)**: Select from the dropdown menu to indicate the type of inquiry you have. Examples include:
	+ General Support: General questions or concerns.
	+ Billing Question: Questions about your bill or payment.
	+ Technical Issue: Technical problems with the service.
	+ Feedback: Suggestions for improvement.
	+ Feature Request: Ideas for new features.
	+ Other: Anything else not listed above.
* **Priority Level (6)**: Select from the dropdown menu to indicate the urgency level of your inquiry.
	+ Low: Non-urgent issues.
	+ Medium: Issues that need attention within 1-2 days.
	+ High: Urgent issues that need immediate attention.
	+ Urgent: Extremely urgent issues that need immediate attention.
* **Subject (7)**: Briefly describe the purpose of your inquiry.
* **Message Details (8)**: Provide as much detail as possible about your inquiry or issue.
* **Overall Website Satisfaction (10)**: Rate your overall experience with our website. Select from:
	+ Very Satisfied: We're happy to hear that!
	+ Satisfied: Good experience, but some room for improvement.
	+ Neutral: No strong feelings either way.
	+ Unsatisfied: Not a great experience.
	+ Very Unsatisfied: A poor experience.
* **How did you find us? (11)**: Select from the dropdown menu to indicate how you found us. Examples include:
	+ Search Engine: Found us through a search engine.
	+ Social Media: Found us through social media.
	+ Referral: A friend or colleague referred us.
	+ Advertisement: Found us through an advertisement.
	+ Other: Anything else not listed above.
* **Preferred Response Time (12)**: Select from the dropdown menu to indicate your preferred time frame for a response. Examples include:
	+ Within 24 Hours: Response within 24 hours.
	+ 1-3 Business Days: Response within 1-3 business days.
	+ No Rush: No specific time frame.

Tips:

* Please fill out the form as thoroughly and accurately as possible.
* Be detailed in your description to help us better understand your issue or concern.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, feel free to include them.
