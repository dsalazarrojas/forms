# online-consumer-complaint-and-demand-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a record of online customer complaints and demands, helping our customer service team to track and resolve issues efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

* Enter a clear and concise description of your complaint or demand in the "Complaint" field.
* Provide your name in the "Name" field.
* Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This is used to contact you for further discussion or updates.
* Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field. This is used to contact you directly if required.
* In the "Description" field, provide more details about your complaint or demand.
* If you have attached any files or documents related to your issue, upload them in the "Attachment" field.
* Select "Yes" in the "Category" field if your complaint or demand falls under a specific category.
* Select "Yes" in the "Channel" field if you would like to be contacted through a specific channel (e.g., phone, email, etc.).
* Select "Yes" in the "Priority" field to indicate the urgency level of your issue.
* Select the current status of your issue in the "Status" field.
* Enter any relevant tags or keywords related to your complaint or demand in the "Tags" field.
* Select the date and time when you first created this issue in the "Created At" field.
* Enter the name of the person who created this issue in the "Created By" field.
* Select the date and time when this issue was last updated in the "Modified At" field.
* Enter the name of the person who last updated this issue in the "Modified By" field.
* If you have assigned this issue to a specific person, enter their name in the "Assigned To" field.
* If you have assigned this issue to someone, enter their name in the "Assigned By" field.
* Enter the time of day when you first created this issue in the "Created At Time" field.
* Enter the time of day when this issue was last updated in the "Modified At Time" field.
* Finally, click "Submit" to submit your complaint or demand.
