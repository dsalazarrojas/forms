# General Inquiry Contact Form With Dark Theme - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for customers who need to submit general inquiries, feedback, or requests to the customer service team. It collects information such as email, name, phone number, and message to allow the team to contact and address the customer's issue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of inquiry you would like to submit (e.g., Support, Feedback, or Other)
2. Enter your email address
3. Enter your name
4. Enter your phone number (optional)
5. Describe the issue or request in the Customer Message field
6. Add any relevant attachments or files (optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Inquiry Form Data** (`general_inquiry_form_data`, `text`, optional): This field is a placeholder to gather any additional information you would like to share with our team.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name so we can address you personally.
* **Customer Phone Number** (`customer_phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone.
* **Customer Message** (`customer_message`, `text`, optional): Describe the issue or request you would like to submit.
* **Customer Subject** (`customer_subject`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief subject or title for your message.
* **Customer Inquiry Type** (`customer_inquiry_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the type of inquiry you are submitting (e.g., Support, Feedback, or Other).
* **Customer Inquiry Type Other** (`customer_inquiry_type_other`, `text`, optional): If you selected "Other" above, enter a brief description of the type of inquiry.
* **Customer Attachment** (`customer_attachment`, `text`, optional): Attach any relevant files or documents to support your inquiry.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your email address and name are accurate and up-to-date to ensure we can contact you.
* You can attach relevant files or documents to support your inquiry.
* Please be descriptive in the "Customer Message" field so we can effectively address your issue.
* If you have a preferred contact method (e.g., phone, email, or chat), please indicate in the "Customer Message" field.
