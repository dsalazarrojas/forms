# Dog Walking Client Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from dog walking clients, allowing us to efficiently manage our services and provide a better customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your contact details, including your email and phone number. These are required fields and must be completed.
2. Provide details about your pet, including any relevant information about its needs and habits.
3. Specify the appointment details, if applicable.
4. Select the services you require for your pet, choosing from 10, 30, or 60 minute walk options.
5. Add any additional notes or instructions in the notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect your contact information, including your name and address. Please fill out your name and email address, and provide a phone number if you have one.
* **Pet Details** (`pet_details`, text, required/optional): This field is used to provide information about your pet, such as its name, age, breed, and any health or behavioral issues.
* **Appointment Details** (`appointment_details`, text, required/optional): If you have a specific appointment or appointment time in mind, please provide details about it in this field.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): This field is used to collect your email address, which we will use to contact you about your appointment or any updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): This field is used to collect your phone number, which we will use to contact you about your appointment or any updates.
* **Services** (`services`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more options from 10, 30, or 60 minute walks to specify the services you require for your pet.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): This field is used to provide any additional information or instructions about your pet's needs or appointment.
