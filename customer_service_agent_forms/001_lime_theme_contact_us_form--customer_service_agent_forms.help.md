# Lime Theme Contact Us Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a contact channel for customers to reach out to the customer service team with any queries, feedback, or concerns they may have regarding the Lime Theme.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields with your information and click "Submit" to send your message to the customer service team.
2. If you are attaching a file to your message, make sure it is in a valid format and not exceeding the file size limit.
3. You can repeat the "Category" field if you need to select multiple categories for your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (text)**: Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name (text)**: Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address in this field. This will help the customer service team contact you back.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number in this field.
* **Category (select_multiple)**: Select one or more categories that best describe your inquiry. You can repeat this field if needed.
* **Message (text)**: Describe your concern, question, or feedback in this field.
* **Attachment (note)**: Upload a file to attach to your message if necessary. Note that this field is required.
* **Subject (text)**: Enter a brief subject for your message.
* **Category (repeat) (select_multiple)**: Select one or more categories that best describe your inquiry.
* **Attachment Description (text)**: Briefly describe the attachment you uploaded, if any.
* **Attachment Upload (note)**: Upload any additional files you want to attach to your message. This field is optional.
* **Subject (repeat) (text)**: Enter a brief subject for your message.
