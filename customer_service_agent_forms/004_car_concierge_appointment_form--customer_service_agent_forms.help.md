> # Car Concierge Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Car Concierge Appointment Form is designed to schedule appointments and collect details about the services you require from our concierge service. This form helps us to understand your needs and preferences, ensuring that we provide you with the best possible experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with the required information in the spaces provided.
2. Make sure to enter your email address and phone number correctly.
3. Select your preferred appointment date and time from the calendar.
4. Choose the type of concierge service you require.
5. Enter your vehicle details, including make, model, year, and VIN (if applicable).
6. Provide instructions on how the concierge will access your vehicle.
7. If you have any additional service requests, please list them in the space provided.
8. Enter your emergency contact information.
9. Acknowledge our concierge service fees.
10. Finally, type your name for signature confirmation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Please enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): For confirmation and updates, please enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): To contact you on the day of service, please enter your phone number.
* **Address for Concierge Service** (`address`, text, required): Where the concierge should meet you, please enter your address.
* **Preferred Appointment Date** (`preferred_date`, date, required): Select your preferred day for the appointment.
* **Preferred Appointment Time** (`preferred_time`, time, required): Select your preferred time for the appointment.
* **Type of Concierge Service Needed** (`concierge_service_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of concierge service you require.
* **Car Make and Model** (`car_make_model`, text, required): Enter the make and model of your vehicle.
* **Car Year** (`car_year`, number, required): Enter the year of manufacture of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Identification Number (VIN)** (`vehicle_vin_concierge`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the 17-character VIN code.
* **Vehicle Location at Home** (`vehicle_vin_concierge_location`, select_one, required): Choose where your vehicle is located at home.
* **Keys and Access Instructions** (`keys_access_instructions`, text, required): How the concierge will access your vehicle, e.g. garage, driveway, street parking.
* **Any Additional Service Requests** (`additional_service_requests`, text, optional): If you have any additional requests, please list them here.
* **Emergency Contact Information** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, required): Enter your emergency contact's name and phone number.
* **I Acknowledge the Concierge Service Fees** (`concierge_acknowledgement`, select_one, required): Confirm that you acknowledge the concierge service fees.
* **Signature Confirmation** (`signature_confirmation`, text, required): Type your name for signature confirmation.
