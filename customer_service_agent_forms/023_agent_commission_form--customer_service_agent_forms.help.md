# Agent Commission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Agent Commission Form is used to collect data and calculate commissions for customer service agents. It's essential to fill in all required fields accurately to ensure correct commission calculations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the **Agent Full Name**, **Agent ID**, and **Agent Email** fields with the corresponding information.
2. Enter the **Reporting Period** (month/year) for which commission is being calculated.
3. Enter the date of commission calculation in the **Commission Calculation Date** field.
4. In the **Sales Transactions** section, enter the total number of transactions completed by the agent and the total dollar amount of all sales.
5. Enter the **Commission Rate (%)** applicable to the agent.
6. Enter the **Base Commission Amount** before adjustments.
7. In the **Commission Adjustments** section, enter any **Performance Bonus**, **Penalty Deductions**, **Referral Bonuses**, and **Other Adjustments**.
8. Enter the **Total Commission Earned** after all adjustments.
9. Select the **Commission Payment Status** from the options provided.
10. Choose the **Payment Method** from the available options.
11. If applicable, enter the expected **Payment Date**.
12. Select **Manager Approval** status.
13. If necessary, enter the name of the **Manager Name** who approved this commission calculation.
14. Add any additional notes in the **Additional Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Agent Full Name**: Enter the full name of the agent. (Required)
* **Agent ID**: Enter the unique identifier for the agent. (Required)
* **Agent Email**: Enter the agent's email address. (Required)
* **Reporting Period**: Enter the month/year for which commission is being calculated. (Required)
* **Commission Calculation Date**: Enter the date when the commission was calculated. (Required)
* **Sales Transactions**: Enter the total number of transactions completed by the agent. (Required, but not a numerical input, this field only labels the section)
* **Number of Transactions**: Enter the total number of transactions completed by the agent. (Required)
* **Total Sales Amount**: Enter the total dollar amount of all sales. (Required)
* **Commission Rate (%)**: Enter the commission rate applicable to the agent. (Required)
* **Base Commission Amount**: Enter the base commission amount before adjustments. (Required)
* **Commission Adjustments**: Enter any performance bonuses earned (Required)
* **-- Commission Adjustments --**: This section is for additional commission adjustments. Enter any **Performance Bonus**, **Penalty Deductions**, **Referral Bonuses**, and **Other Adjustments** as applicable. (Optional)
* **Performance Bonus**: Enter any performance bonuses earned. (Optional)
* **Penalty Deductions**: Enter any deductions due to penalties. (Optional)
* **Referral Bonuses**: Enter any bonuses from referrals. (Optional)
* **Other Adjustments**: Enter any other commission adjustments. (Optional)
* **Total Commission Earned**: Enter the total commission after all adjustments. (Required)
* **Commission Payment Status**: Select the status of commission payment. (Required)
* **Payment Method**: Choose the method for commission payment. (Required)
* **Expected Payment Date**: Enter the expected date of commission payment. (Optional)
* **Manager Approval**: Select the status of manager approval. (Required)
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of the approving manager if applicable. (Optional)
* **Additional Notes**: Add any additional notes about the commission calculation. (Optional)

Please ensure all required fields are filled in accurately for correct commission calculations.
