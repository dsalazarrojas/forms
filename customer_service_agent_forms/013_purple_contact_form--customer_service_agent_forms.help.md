# Purple Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Purple Contact Form is a tool for customers to share their thoughts, concerns, or questions with us. It's meant to be a simple and user-friendly way for them to get in touch and for our agents to process and respond to their queries efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the form to open it.
2. Choose the appropriate answer to "Contact Method" to indicate how you'd like to be contacted.
3. Fill in the "Customer Name", "Company", and "Company Name" fields with the relevant information.
4. Enter your "Email" address to allow us to contact you.
5. Choose a "Date" and "Time" for the meeting or appointment (if applicable).
6. Add any additional notes in the "Note" field.
7. Select the correct answer to "Contact Info" depending on your needs.
8. Click submit to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Method** (`Contact Method`, select_one, required): Choose how you'd like us to contact you. Select either 'Yes' if you're willing to be contacted, or 'No' if you prefer not to be contacted.
* **Customer Name** (`Customer Name`, text, optional): Enter your name for our reference.
* **Company Name** (`Company Name`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of your company.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like us to contact you by phone.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, optional): Choose the date for the meeting or appointment.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, optional): Choose the time for the meeting or appointment.
* **Note** (`Note`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your request.
* **Company** (`Company`, text, optional): Enter the name of your company, if applicable.
* **Role** (`Role`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your role or position within the company.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' if you're willing to provide your contact information, or 'No' if you're not.
