# Support Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with our support team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the overall experience question with a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
2. Provide a brief description of your satisfaction level with the support you received.
3. Select the support agent who assisted you.
4. Describe your communication skills with the support agent.
5. Answer the question about whether the problem was solved.
6. Estimate the time it took to respond to your issue.
7. Answer the question about whether you would like to be contacted again.
8. Provide any additional comments or feedback.
9. Enter your email address (if you would like to be contacted for follow-up).
10. Enter your phone number (if you have one).
11. Select the support agent who assisted you (if different from the first agent).
12. Answer the question about the level of support you received.
13. Select the category of the issue you encountered.
14. Select the sub-category of the issue.
15. Select the follow-up actions you would like to receive.
16. Answer the question about the resolution of the issue.
17. Estimate the time spent on the issue.
18. Describe any issues or problems you encountered.
19. Provide additional comments.
20. Enter your email address (if you would like to be contacted for follow-up).
21. Enter your phone number (if you have one).
22. Select the support agent who assisted you (if different from the first agent).
23. Answer the question about the level of support you received.
24. Select the category of the issue.
25. Select the sub-category of the issue.
26. Select the follow-up actions you would like to receive.

## Tips
- Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
- If you have any additional comments or feedback, please enter them in the "Comments" field.
- If you would like to be contacted for follow-up, please enter your email address and phone number.
