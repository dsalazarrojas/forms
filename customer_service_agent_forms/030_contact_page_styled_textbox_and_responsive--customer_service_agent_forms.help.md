# Contact Page Styled Textbox And Responsive - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to contact the customer service team for assistance with issues or inquiries related to products or services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the category of issue you are experiencing (Account Issue, Billing Question, Product Support, Service Request, Technical Problem, or General Inquiry).
2.  Choose the product or service related to your issue.
3.  Provide a detailed description of the problem you are experiencing.
4.  If applicable, describe the steps you have already taken to try and resolve the issue.
5.  Specify your preferred method of contact (email, phone call, or text message).
6.  Indicate the best time for the support team to contact you.
7.  If applicable, specify whether you have screenshots available to attach.
8.  Finally, select the urgency level of your issue (Low, Medium, High, or Critical).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name for our reference.
- **Customer ID** (`customer_id`, `text`, optional): If you have an account number or ID, please enter it for our reference.
- **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address we should use to contact you.
- **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number we can use to contact you.
- **Issue Type** (`issue_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the category of issue you are experiencing (Account Issue, Billing Question, Product Support, Service Request, Technical Problem, or General Inquiry).
- **Product or Service** (`product_service`, `select_one`, required): Choose the product or service related to your issue (Product A, Product B, Product C, Service Package, or Other).
- **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, `text`, required): Describe the problem you are experiencing in as much detail as possible.
- **Steps Already Taken** (`steps_taken`, `text`, optional): If you have already tried to resolve the issue, describe the steps you have taken.
- **Error Message** (`error_message`, `text`, optional): If you have an error message, please enter it here.
- **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone Call, Text Message).
- **Best Time to Contact** (`availability`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate the best time for our support team to contact you (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Weekend).
- **Priority Level** (`priority_level`, `select_one`, required): Select the urgency level of your issue (Low, Medium, High, Critical).
- **Screenshots** (`screenshots`, `select_one`, optional): If you have screenshots, choose whether you will attach them or not (Yes - Will Attach, Yes - Will Send Later, False).
- **Additional Comments** (`error_message`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional comments, enter them here.

## Tips
- Please provide clear and concise descriptions of your issues.
- If you have screenshots, please attach them when prompted.
- If you have already taken steps to resolve the issue, please describe them.
- Be as detailed as possible when selecting the issue type, product or service, and priority level.
