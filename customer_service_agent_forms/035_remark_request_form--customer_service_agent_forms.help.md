# Remark Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Remark Request Form is a tool used by customer service agents to gather information from customers about their issues with a student's work or performance. This form helps to identify potential problems and provide a resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Gather information from the customer** about their issue with the student's work or performance.
2. **Fill out the form** with the relevant details, including:
	* The student's name and ID
	* A brief description of the issue
	* Whether or not to remark the student's work
	* A reason for your decision
	* Any supporting documentation
3. **Submit the form** to escalate the issue and provide a resolution.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Remark Request Form**
* **Remark Request Form** (id: 1, type: text, required: false): This is the title of the form.
### 2. **Request Details**
* **Request Details** (id: 2, type: text, required: false): This field is where you will describe the issue or problem with the student's work or performance.
### 3. **Student Name**
* **Student Name** (id: 3, type: text, required: false): This field is where you will enter the student's name.
### 4. **Student ID**
* **Student ID** (id: 4, type: text, required: false): This field is where you will enter the student's ID.
### 5. **Remarks**
* **Remarks** (id: 5, type: select_multiple, required: false): This field allows you to select whether or not to remark the student's work. Options are "Yes" and "No".
### 6. **Reason for Remarks**
* **Reason for Remarks** (id: 6, type: text, required: false): This field is where you will enter a brief explanation for your decision on whether or not to remark the student's work.
### 7. **Supporting Document**
* **Supporting Document** (id: 7, type: text, required: false): This field is where you will upload any supporting documentation that may be relevant to the issue.
### 8. **Submit**
* **Submit** (id: 8, type: text, required: false): This button submits the form and sends it to the next level of review.
