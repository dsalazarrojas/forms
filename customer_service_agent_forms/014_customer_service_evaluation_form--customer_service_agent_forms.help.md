# Customer Service Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the performance of customer service agents, providing a channel for customers to rate and comment on their experiences. Its purpose is to collect feedback to help improve customer service quality and ensure that customers receive satisfactory support. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Contact Us**: Select the reason for your visit to our customer service team from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter your **Email** or phone number so we can contact you back.
3. Leave your **Phone Number** so we can call or text you if needed.
4. Choose your overall **Rating** to indicate how satisfied you are with our service.
5. If you wish to leave a more detailed **Comment**, please do so in the space provided.
6. Finally, enter your **Customer Name**, which will help us address your feedback directly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Us**: Choose the reason for your visit from the dropdown menu.
	+ Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you back.
	+ Please enter a valid email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number so we can call or text you if needed.
	+ Please enter a valid phone number.
* **Rating**: Choose a rating from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Comment**: If you have any additional comments or feedback, please enter them in this space.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name so we can address your feedback directly.
	+ Please enter your real name. 

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible in your comment to help us improve our service.
* You can choose to be contacted by phone call or text.
