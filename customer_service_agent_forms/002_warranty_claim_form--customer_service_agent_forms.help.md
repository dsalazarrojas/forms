# Warranty Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to submit a warranty claim for a product that they believe is defective or malfunctioning. Please fill out the form with accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth claim process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (indicated by *) with accurate information.
2. Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
3. Ensure that the date and time fields are in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY for dates and HH:MM AM/PM for times).
4. Choose the correct status for your claim from the dropdown list.
5. Sign and date the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (First Name, text, required)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name (Last Name, text, required)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address (Email Address, email, required)**: Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you about your claim.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number, text, required)**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you if needed.
* **Product Serial Number (Product Serial Number, text, optional)**: If you have a serial number for the product, enter it here.
* **Problem Description (Problem Description, text, optional)**: Describe the issue with your product in as much detail as possible.
* **Claim Date (Claim Date, date, required)**: Enter the date you are submitting this claim.
* **Claim Time (Claim Time, time, optional)**: Enter the time you are submitting this claim.
* **Product Model (Product Model, text, optional)**: If you know the model of your product, enter it here.
* **Purchase Date (Purchase Date, date, required)**: Enter the date you purchased the product.
* **Purchase Time (Purchase Time, time, required)**: Enter the time you purchased the product.
* **Purchase Location (Purchase Location, text, optional)**: Enter the location where you purchased the product.
* **Warranty Provider (Warranty Provider, text, optional)**: If you know the warranty provider, enter it here.
* **Warranty Issue (Warranty Issue, text, optional)**: Describe the issue with your warranty.
* **Claim Status (Claim Status, select_one, optional)**: Choose the status of your claim from the list.
* **Reason For Claim (Reason For Claim, text, optional)**: If you know why you are making this claim, enter it here.
* **Resolution Note (Resolution Note, text, optional)**: If your claim has been resolved, enter any notes here.
* **Customer Name (Customer Name, text, required)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Product Image (Product Image, text, optional)**: If you have an image of the product, you can upload it here.
* **Customer Signature (Customer Signature, text, required)**: Sign your name to confirm that you are submitting this claim.
* **Claim Date (Claim Date, date, optional)**: Enter the date you are submitting this claim (this field is a duplicate of Claim Date above, please use the first one).
* **Claim Time (Claim Time, time, optional)**: Enter the time you are submitting this claim (this field is a duplicate of Claim Time above, please use the first one).

Note: All fields marked as required are mandatory and must be filled out for the form to be processed.
