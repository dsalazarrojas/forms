# Customer Service Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer feedback from customers who have interacted with a customer service agent. The feedback is used to evaluate the performance of customer service agents, gather feedback, and understand areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (*). You can skip any fields that are not required. Follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Feedback" field by selecting one or more options that best describe your experience.
2. Rate your experience using the rating scale.
3. If you'd like to provide more details, type them in the "Message" field.
4. Attach any relevant files or documents to support your feedback by using the "Attachments" field.
5. Finally, submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback** (`customer_service_feedback`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more options that best describe your experience, such as "Feedback", "Suggestions", or "Comments".
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required): Rate your experience on a scale.
* **Message** (`customer_service_feedback_form_message`, text, required): If you'd like to provide more details about your experience, type them in this field.
* **Attachments** (`customer_service_feedback_form_uploads`, text, required): Attach any relevant files or documents to support your feedback.
* **Customer Service Agent** (`customer_service_agent_name`, text, optional): If you remember the customer service agent's name, feel free to type it in.
* **Date** (`customer_service_feedback_form_date`, date, optional): If you remember the date of the interaction, type it in.
* **Time** (`customer_service_feedback_form_time`, time, optional): If you remember the time of the interaction, type it in.
* **Subject** (`customer_service_feedback_form_subject`, text, optional): Briefly describe the subject of your feedback.
* **Very Satisfied** (`customer_service_feedback_form_satisfied`, select_one, optional): Select the level of satisfaction you felt about your experience.
* **Attachments** (`customer_service_feedback_form_uploads`, text, optional): Attach any supporting files or documents to your feedback.
