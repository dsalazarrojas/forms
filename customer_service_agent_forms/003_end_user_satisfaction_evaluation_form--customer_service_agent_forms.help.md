# end_user_satisfaction_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "end_user_satisfaction_evaluation_form" is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with the customer service team. This form helps gather information on various aspects of the customer service interaction, including communication, resolution, satisfaction, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully and make sure you are familiar with the questions and options provided.
* Answer each question honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
* For questions with multiple options, select the one that best describes your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer service** (`Customer service`, `note`, required: false): This section is a note area where you can provide any comments or feedback about your experience with the customer service team. Be as detailed as possible.
* **Communication** (`Communication`, `text`, required: false): Describe your experience with communication from the customer service team. How clear and responsive were they?
* **Resolution** (`Resolution`, `number`, required: false): Rate your experience with how quickly and effectively the issue was resolved. Use a number between 1-5 to indicate your level of satisfaction.
* **Satisfaction** (`Satisfaction`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the customer service team's help. Use a number between 1-5 to indicate your level of satisfaction.
* **Recommendation** (`Recommendation`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the option that best describes how likely you are to recommend our service to others.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): If you would like to be contacted directly about your feedback, please provide your email address.
* **Phone number** (`Phone number`, `text`, required: false): If you would like to be contacted directly about your feedback, please provide your phone number.
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your name so we can address you personally.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide as much detail as possible in your answers.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, feel free to include them in the "Comments" field.
* If you would like to be contacted directly, please provide your email address or phone number.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us directly.
