# NGO Educational Workshop Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information and donations for an NGO educational workshop. It's essential to understand that the form is intended for workshops, not for other types of events or activities. If you are not registering for a workshop, please do not use this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) next to the label.
2. Enter your donation amount in the "Donation Amount" field.
3. Choose how often you'd like to donate (if applicable).
4. Select the type of workshop you're interested in (if applicable).
5. Provide your name, email, and phone number (if you are registering for a workshop).
6. Indicate how often you'd like to register for a workshop (if applicable).
7. Confirm your registration status (if applicable).
8. Add any comments or notes about your registration (if applicable).
9. Select your preferred payment method (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donation Amount** (`Donation Amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount you'd like to donate to the workshop.
* **Donation Frequency** (`Donation Frequency`, select_one, optional): Select how often you'd like to donate. You can choose between "Monthly" or "One-time".
* **Workshop Type** (`Workshop Type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the type of workshop you're interested in. You can select one or multiple options, such as "Basic Workshop", "Advanced Workshop", or "Workshop Series".
* **Attendee Name** (`Attendee Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the attendee (if you are registering for a workshop).
* **Attendee E-mail** (`Attendee E-mail`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the attendee (if you are registering for a workshop).
* **Attendee Phone** (`Attendee Phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the attendee (if you are registering for a workshop).
* **Registration Frequency** (`Registration Frequency`, select_one, optional): Indicate how often you'd like to register for a workshop. You can choose between "One-time" or "Regular".
* **Registration Status** (`Registration Status`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm your registration status. You can select one or multiple options, such as "Not Registered", "Registered", or "Cancelled".
* **Comments** (`Comments`, note, optional): Add any comments or notes about your registration (if applicable).
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred payment method. You can choose between "Bank Transfer", "Credit Card", or "PayPal".
