# Client Success Framework Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals interested in attending the Client Success Framework Training. It collects contact information, personal details, and preferences for the training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Enter your company name and job title.
3. Select your preferred training date and location (if in-person).
4. Indicate your experience level with client success and your primary goal for attending the training.
5. Select up to 3 topics you are interested in learning about.
6. If attending in-person, provide any dietary requirements or accessibility needs.
7. Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 3. First Name
* **First Name**: Your first name as it should appear on the training materials and certificates.

### 4. Last Name
* **Last Name**: Your last name as it should appear on the training materials and certificates.

### 5. Email Address
* **Email Address**: We will send training details to this email, so please ensure it is accurate and checked regularly.

### 6. Phone Number
* **Phone Number**: This will be used to contact you about the training schedule or any updates.

### 8. Company Name
* **Company Name**: Your company name as it should appear on the training materials and certificates.

### 9. Department or Team
* **Department or Team**: Your department or team name as it should appear on the training materials and certificates.

### 10. Job Title
* **Job Title**: Your current job title as it should appear on the training materials and certificates.

### 11. Size of your team
* **Size of your team**: The number of people you manage or work with.

### 12. Years in your current role
* **Years in your current role**: The number of years you have been in your current position.

### 14. Preferred Training Date
* **Preferred Training Date**: Choose a date for the training from the available options. Note that these dates are subject to change.

### 15. Preferred Training Format
* **Preferred Training Format**: Select the format you prefer for the training: In-Person, Virtual, or Hybrid.

### 16. Preferred Location (if in-person)
* **Preferred Location (if in-person)**: Choose the location for the in-person training. Note that locations may change depending on the date selected.

### 17. Have you completed client success training before?
* **Have you completed client success training before?**: Indicate if you have prior experience with client success training.

### 19. Your experience level with client success
* **Your experience level with client success**: Select your level of experience with client success: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert.

### 20. Primary goal for attending this training
* **Primary goal for attending this training**: Describe what you hope to learn or achieve from this training.

### 21. Which topics are you most interested in learning about?
* **Which topics are you most interested in learning about?**: Select up to 3 topics you are most interested in learning about. These topics include Client Onboarding Best Practices, Building Client Relationships, Managing Client Expectations, Measuring Client Success, Proactive Problem Solving, Growth and Expansion Strategies, Communication Frameworks, and Technology and Tools.

### 22. Current challenges in client success
* **Current challenges in client success**: Describe any challenges you are currently facing in client success.

### 24. Dietary requirements or restrictions
* **Dietary requirements or restrictions**: If you have any dietary restrictions, please list them here. This will help us provide suitable meals during in-person events.

### 25. Any accessibility needs or accommodations needed?
* **Any accessibility needs or accommodations needed?**: Indicate if you have any accessibility needs or requests for the training.

### 26. Do you need hotel accommodations?
* **Do you need hotel accommodations?**: If attending in-person, select if you need hotel accommodations.

### 28. How did you hear about this training?
* **How did you hear about this training?**: Select how you heard about this training, such as Email, Website, Social Media, Colleague Recommendation, Manager Recommendation, Industry Event, or Other.

### 29. I agree to the terms and conditions
* **I agree to the terms and conditions**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions of the training.

### 30. Additional comments or questions?
* **Additional comments or questions?**: Any additional comments or questions you may have about the training.
