# ux-workshop-registration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The UX workshop registration form is a tool for workshop organizers to collect information about upcoming workshops. This form helps organizers gather relevant data about workshop attendees, including workshop names, dates, times, locations, and other important details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*). 
2. Enter the workshop name, date, time, location, type, and other relevant details.
3. If you have any comments or notes, use the corresponding fields to provide additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Name** (`workshop_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the workshop.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`workshop_date`, date, required/optional): Choose the date of the workshop.
* **Time** (`workshop_time`, time, required/optional): Select the time of the workshop.
* **Location** (`workshop_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location of the workshop.
* **Duration** (`workshop_duration`, text, required/optional): Enter the duration of the workshop.
* **Type** (`workshop_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of workshop (e.g., Workshop A, Workshop B, or Workshop C).
* **Capacity** (`workshop_capacity`, text, required/optional): Enter the capacity of the workshop.
* **Notes** (`workshop_notes`, note, required/optional): Use this field to add any additional notes or comments.
* **Comments** (`workshop_comments`, text, required/optional): Use this field to provide any additional comments.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required/optional): Click the submit button to complete the form.
