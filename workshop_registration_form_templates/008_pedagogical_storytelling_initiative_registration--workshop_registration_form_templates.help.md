# Pedagogical Storytelling Initiative Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Pedagogical Storytelling Initiative workshops. Please follow the instructions below to provide your relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields with your relevant information. Please note that some fields are optional and only apply to specific categories of participants.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Roles**: Choose the role that best describes your position within the Pedagogical Storytelling Initiative. This will help us tailor the workshop to your needs.
* **Experiences Years**: If you are a Program Director, please enter the number of years you have experience in this role.
* **Motivations**: Describe your motivations for attending the workshop in a few words. This will help us understand your goals for the workshop.
* **Workshop Name**: Provide the name of the workshop you are registering for. If unsure, please leave this field blank and we will provide a list of available workshops.
* **Participants**: If you are registering as a Program Director, choose all that apply. This will help us gauge interest for specific workshops.
  * Number of Participants: Number of participants you are expected to bring to the workshop.
  * Age Range: Age range of participants you are expecting.
  * Location: Location of the workshop participants will be held.

**Tips**: Please fill out the form accurately and honestly to ensure that we can tailor the workshop to your needs. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
Note: Please do not register for a workshop with incomplete or false information.
