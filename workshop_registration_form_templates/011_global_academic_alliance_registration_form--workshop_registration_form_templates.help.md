# Global Academic Alliance Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect registration information from partners who wish to join the Global Academic Alliance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. Select the correct option for each field from the dropdown menu (if applicable).
3. Make sure to provide accurate dates in the date fields.
4. If any information is missing or outdated, please update it as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, `text`, optional):
	+ Please enter the name of your institution or partner.
* **Partner Type** (`partner_type`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Select the type of partner you are: Partner University, Research Centre, or Company.
* **Department Name** (`department_name`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the department related to your partner.
* **Department Type** (`department_type`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Select the type of department: Academic, Research, or Other.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the partnership.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional):
	+ Enter the email address of your partner's contact person.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter the phone number of your partner's contact person.
* **Country** (`country`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Select the country of your partner's location.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter the city of your partner's location.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter the state or region of your partner's location.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter the physical address of your partner's location.
* **Zip** (`zip`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter the zip code of your partner's location.
* **Partner Since** (`partner_since`, `date`, optional):
	+ Enter the date your partner joined the Global Academic Alliance.
* **Last Updated** (`last_updated`, `time`, optional):
	+ Enter the last date your partner's information was updated.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional):
	+ Enter any additional notes or comments about your partner.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Indicate if the partner's information was submitted by you.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, `date`, optional):
	+ Enter the date the partner's information was submitted.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Indicate if the partner's information was updated by you.
* **Updated Date** (`updated_date`, `date`, optional):
	+ Enter the date the partner's information was updated.
* **Submitted User** (`submitted_user`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Indicate if the partner's information was submitted by the partner.
* **Updated User** (`updated_user`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Indicate if the partner's information was updated by you.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Indicate if the partner's information was created by you.
* **Created Date** (`created_date`, `date`, optional):
	+ Enter the date the partner's information was created.
* **Deleted By** (`deleted_by`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Indicate if the partner's information was deleted by you.
* **Deleted Date** (`deleted_date`, `date`, optional):
	+ Enter the date the partner's information was deleted.

Note: All fields are optional, but providing accurate and complete information will help us better serve the partnership. If you're unsure about any field or would like to clarify any specific details, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
