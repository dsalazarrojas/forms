# Butterfly Preservation Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering participants for a butterfly preservation workshop. Please fill out the form below to provide your details and preferences for the workshop.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field. This will be used for workshop communications.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. If you are affiliated with an organization, enter its name in the "Organization Name" field (optional).
5. Select your participant type (Student, Educator, Researcher, Community Member, Professional, or Other) in the "Type of Participant" field.
6. Choose your experience level with butterflies (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert) in the "Experience Level with Butterflies" field.
7. Select your preferred workshop session (morning, afternoon, or evening) in the "Preferred Workshop Session" field.
8. Choose a date for the workshop in the "Workshop Date" field.
9. If you have any special interests (e.g., monarch butterflies, habitat conservation, butterfly gardening, etc.), select them in the "Special Areas of Interest" field (optional).
10. If you have any dietary restrictions, please enter them in the "Dietary Restrictions" field (optional).
11. If you have any accessibility needs, please describe them in the "Accessibility Needs" field (optional).
12. Enter the size of your group in the "Group Size" field, and if applicable, list the names of other group members in the "Names of Other Group Members" field.
13. If you need transportation assistance, select the appropriate option in the "Transportation Needed" field.
14. Finally, agree to the workshop waiver and policies by selecting "Yes, I agree" in the "I agree to Workshop Waiver and Policies" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`participant_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`participant_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for workshop communications.
* **Phone Number** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact number.
* **Organization Name** (`organization`, text, optional): If you are affiliated with an organization, enter its name.
* **Type of Participant** (`participant_type`, select_one, required): Select your participant category (Student, Educator, Researcher, Community Member, Professional, or Other).
* **Experience Level with Butterflies** (`experience_level`, select_one, required): Choose your experience level with butterflies (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert).
* **Preferred Workshop Session** (`workshop_session`, select_one, required): Select your preferred workshop session (morning, afternoon, or evening).
* **Workshop Date** (`registration_date`, date, required): Choose a date for the workshop.
* **Special Areas of Interest** (`special_interests`, select_multiple, optional): Select any special areas of interest (e.g., monarch butterflies, habitat conservation, butterfly gardening, etc.).
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, optional): If you have any dietary restrictions, please enter them.
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, text, optional): If you have any accessibility needs, please describe them.
* **Group Size** (`group_size`, number, required): Enter the size of your group.
* **Names of Other Group Members** (`group_members`, text, optional): If applicable, list the names of other group members.
* **Transportation Needed** (`transportation_needed`, select_one, required): Select whether you need transportation assistance.
* **I agree to Workshop Waiver and Policies** (`waiver_agreement`, select_one, required): Agree to the workshop waiver and policies by selecting "Yes, I agree".
