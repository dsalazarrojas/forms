# strategic-investment-forum-registration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering participants for a strategic investment forum. Please fill out the required fields to complete your registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the participant information fields, including company name, job title, contact phone, email, and department.
2. Select your participant status and job title from the available options.
3. Enter your contact details, including email and participant status.
4. Choose your preferred date and time for the forum.
5. Add any additional notes or comments.
6. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Company Name** (`company`, text, optional): Enter your company's name.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title within the company.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
- **E-mail** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
- **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter your department or team within the company.
- **Participant Status** (`participant_status`, text, optional): Select your participant status from the available options.
- **Job Title 2** (`job_title_2`, text, optional): Enter your second job title within the company.
- **E-mail 2** (`email_2`, email, optional): Enter your second email address.
- **Participant Status 2** (`participant_status_2`, text, optional): Select your second participant status from the available options.
- **Select 1** (`select_1`, select_one, optional): Choose one option from the list.
- **Select 2** (`select_2`, select_multiple, optional): Choose multiple options from the list.
- **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the forum.
- **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the forum.
- **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
- **Select 3** (`select_3`, select_one, optional): Choose one option from the list.
- **Time 2** (`time_2`, time, optional): Enter the second time of the forum.
- **Select 4** (`select_4`, select_one, optional): Choose one option from the list.
- **Date 2** (`date_2`, date, optional): Enter the second date of the forum.
- **Participant Status 3** (`participant_status_3`, text, optional): Select your third participant status from the available options.
- **Select 5** (`select_5`, select_one, optional): Choose one option from the list.

Please note that all fields are optional, but filling out the required fields is crucial for a successful registration.
