# Team Development Coaching Session Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Development Coaching Session Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from team members to facilitate a successful and productive coaching session. This form will help us understand your team's needs, preferences, and availability, ensuring a tailored and effective coaching experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your Team Member Name in the first field.
2. Provide your email address in the second field.
3. Enter your phone number in the third field.
4. Indicate your team size in the fourth field.
5. Select a date for the coaching session in the fifth field.
6. Choose a time for the coaching session (if available) in the sixth field.
7. Optionally, add any additional information or notes about your team's goals and requirements in the seventh field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Member Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name as a team member.
* **email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, number, required/optional): Indicate the number of team members in your team.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select a date for the coaching session.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Choose a time for the coaching session (if available).
* **Additional Information** (`note`, note, required/optional): If you have any additional information or notes about your team, you can add them here.

## Tips

* Please make sure to check the "Date" field to schedule the coaching session on a date that works for you.
* If you have any specific preferences or requirements for the coaching session, please note them in the "Additional Information" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
