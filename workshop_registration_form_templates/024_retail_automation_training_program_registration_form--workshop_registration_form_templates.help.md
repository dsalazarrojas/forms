# Retail Automation Training Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals or companies interested in registering for the Retail Automation Training Program. Please complete the form accurately and honestly to ensure proper program planning and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all required fields.
2. Double-check your details before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Retail Automation Training** (`retail_automation_training_program_registration_form`, text, required: false): 
   Please enter a brief description of your training request or interest.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): 
   Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in our communication.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): 
   Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): 
   Enter your phone number for further contact and communication.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required: false): 
   If you are representing a company, please enter its name for our records.
* **Position** (`position`, text, required: false): 
   Describe your role or title within the company for better understanding.
* **Training Type** (`training_type`, select_multiple, required: false):
   Select all applicable types of training you are interested in:
   - Retail
   - On-Site
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): 
   Enter the date when you plan to attend the training, if available.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): 
   If available, enter the time slot you prefer for the training.
   
## Tips
- Please fill in all fields accurately and honestly.
- Double-check your details before submission.
- If you experience any issues while filling this form, contact us directly.
