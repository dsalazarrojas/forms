# Creative Assessment Practice Exchange Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Creative Assessment Practice Exchange is a platform where creative professionals can share their experiences, learn from others, and collaborate on creative projects. This form is used to register for the platform and provide personal and professional information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, email address, and other required fields.
2. Select your experience level, interest areas, and other relevant details.
3. Review and agree to the terms of participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (id: 1, type: text, required: true): Enter your name in the format "First Name Last Name".
* **Email Address** (id: 2, type: email, required: true): Enter your valid email address that will be used for communication and login purposes.
* **Experience Level** (id: 3, type: select_multiple, required: true):
	+ Select "Yes" if you have relevant experience in creative work.
	+ Select "No" if you don't have relevant experience.
* **Interest Areas** (id: 4, type: select_multiple, required: true):
	+ Select the areas where you have experience and interest in.
	+ You can select multiple areas.
* **Creative Assessment Interests** (id: 5, type: select_one, required: true):
	+ Select "Yes" if you are interested in creative assessment.
	+ Select "No" if you are not interested.
* **Training Background** (id: 6, type: text, required: true): Enter your background in training or relevant courses.
* **Creative Assessment Experience** (id: 7, type: select_multiple, required: true):
	+ Select "Yes" if you have experience with creative assessment.
	+ Select "No" if you don't have experience.
* **Collaboration Preferences** (id: 8, type: select_multiple, required: true):
	+ Select the collaboration preferences that suit you best.
* **Participant Type** (id: 9, type: select_one, required: true):
	+ Select the type of participant you are.
* **Language** (id: 10, type: text, required: true): Enter your preferred language of communication.
* **Country** (id: 11, type: text, required: true): Enter your country of residence.
* **Organization** (id: 12, type: text, required: true): Enter your current organization.
* **Phone Number** (id: 13, type: text, required: true): Enter your phone number.
* **Additional Notes** (id: 14, type: note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Registration Date** (id: 20, type: date, required: true): Enter the date you are available to participate.
* **Time of Day** (id: 21, type: time, required: true): Enter the time of day you are available to participate.
* **Time Zone** (id: 18, type: text, required: true): Enter your time zone.
* **Location** (id: 19, type: text, required: true): Enter your location.
* **Time Zone Offset** (id: 22, type: select_one, required: true):
	+ Select "Yes" if you have a unique time zone offset.
	+ Select "No" if you don't have a unique time zone offset.
* **Agreed Participation Terms** (id: 24, type: select_one, required: true):
	+ Select "Yes" to agree to the participation terms.
	+ Select "No" to indicate that you don't agree.
* **Agreed Participation Terms 2** (id: 25, type: select_one, required: true):
	+ Select "Yes" to agree to the second participation terms.
	+ Select "No" to indicate that you don't agree.

**Tips**

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure correct submission.
* Review and agree to the participation terms before submitting the form.
* Double-check your information for accuracy and consistency.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the platform administrators.
