# Professional Development Workshop Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Professional Development Workshop Appointment Form is a registration form for upcoming workshops. It is used to collect information from participants about their availability and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the desired workshop date from the available options.
2.  Choose your preferred workshop time from the available options.
3.  Enter the workshop duration.
4.  Enter the name of the workshop trainer.
5.  Enter your name as a participant.
6.  Enter your email address.
7.  Enter your phone number.
8.  Confirm your email address.
9.  Choose whether you agree or decline with the participant agreement terms.
10.  Add any additional notes or comments.
11.  Select the submit option to finish the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Date**: Select the date of the workshop you are interested in attending.
* **Workshop Time**: Choose the time of the workshop that suits you best.
* **Workshop Duration**: Enter the duration of the workshop you are interested in (e.g., full-day, half-day, etc.).
* **Trainer Name**: Enter the name of the workshop trainer.
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as a participant.
* **Participant E-mail**: Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Confirm E-mail**: Confirm your email address for verification purposes.
* **Participant Agreement**: Select "Agree" if you agree to the participant agreement terms, or "Decline" if you do not.
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your participation.
* **Participant Availability**: Enter your availability for the workshop (not applicable in this case, but included for completeness).
* **Submit**: Select "Submit" to complete the form and finish the registration process.

Note: The fields with "required: true" are mandatory fields and must be filled out for the form to be successfully submitted.
