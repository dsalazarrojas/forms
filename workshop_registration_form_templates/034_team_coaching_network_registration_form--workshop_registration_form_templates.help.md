# Team Coaching Network Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Coaching Network Registration Form is designed to collect registration information from individuals interested in joining the Team Coaching Network. This form is used to gather relevant details such as name, email, phone number, and other information to facilitate the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Provide a valid email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select a date from the calendar in the "Date" field.
5. Choose a time that suits you best in the "Time" field.
6. If you have any additional notes or comments, you can enter them in the "Additional Notes" field.
7. Select one or more services you are interested in joining from the list in the "Which of the following services do you want to join?" field.
8. If you want to join multiple services, select them in the "Select multiple services" field.
9. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
10. Enter your age in the "Age" field.
11. If you have a coach, you can enter their name in the "Coach" field.
12. If you want to specify a team, you can enter it in the "Team" field.
13. Choose your position on the team from the list in the "Position" field.
14. Select your role within the team from the list in the "Role" field.
15. Read and agree to the terms and conditions in the "Terms & Conditions" field.
16. Finally, read and agree to the agreement terms in the "Agreement" field by selecting "Yes" or "No".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name (1)**: Enter your full name.
* **Email (2)**: Provide a valid email address to ensure we can contact you.
* **Phone (3)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date (4)**: Select a date from the calendar.
* **Time (5)**: Choose a time that suits you best.
* **Additional Notes (6)**: If you have any additional information or comments, you can enter them here.
* **Which of the following services do you want to join? (7)**: Select one or more services you are interested in joining.
* **Select multiple services (8)**: If you want to join multiple services, select them here.
* **Date of Birth (9)**: Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Age (10)**: Enter your age.
* **Coach (11)**: If you have a coach, enter their name here.
* **Team (12)**: If you want to specify a team, enter it here.
* **Position (13)**: Choose your position on the team from the list.
* **Role (14)**: Select your role within the team from the list.
* **Agreement (15)**: Read and agree to the agreement terms.
* **Terms & Conditions (16)**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Agreement (17)**: Read and agree to the agreement terms by selecting "Yes" or "No".
