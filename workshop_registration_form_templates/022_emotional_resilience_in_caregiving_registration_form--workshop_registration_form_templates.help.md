# Emotional Resilience In Caregiving Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from caregivers and individuals who provide emotional support and care for others. The information collected will be used to facilitate the workshop on "Emotional Resilience In Caregiving".

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill in the required information in each field. Make sure to provide accurate and truthful answers to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Registration Form
* **Registration Form** (`text`, `Registration Form`, `false`): A text field to collect the registrant's name or any other relevant information.

### Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (`text`, `Contact Info`, `false`): A text field to collect the registrant's contact details, such as email or phone number.

### Emotional Resilience
* **Emotional Resilience** (`text`, `Emotional Resilience`, `false`): A text field to collect information about the registrant's emotional resilience.

### Caregiver Info
* **Caregiver Info** (`text`, `Caregiver Info`, `false`): A text field to collect information about the caregiver.

## Tips
* Please make sure to review your submission before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
