# Pottery Studio Workshop Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pottery Studio Workshop Membership Form is used to capture information from potential workshop participants, including their workshop preferences and membership type.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your workshop preferences from the list of options.
2. Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed.
3. Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you.
4. Enter your phone number for us to reach out to you.
5. Choose your membership type from the list of options.
6. If you selected "Other" for your membership type, please specify in the "membership_type_other" field.
7. Add any additional notes or comments in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Application** (`workshop_application`, text, optional): This field is for a brief description of why you are interested in joining the workshop.
* **Workshop Preferences** (`workshop_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the workshop types that interest you, such as Pottery or Painting.
* **Workshop Preferences Other** (`workshop_preferences_other`, text, optional): If you have a specific workshop preference not listed, please specify it here.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for us to reach out to you.
* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, select_one, optional): Choose your membership type from the list of options.
* **Membership Type Other** (`membership_type_other`, text, optional): If you selected "Other" for your membership type, please specify it here.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments you would like to include.

## Tips

* Ensure your phone number is correct and up-to-date.
