# Team Dynamics Workshop Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Dynamics Workshop Attendance Form is designed to collect information from participants attending a workshop. This form will help the organizers to manage attendance, track participant interests, and provide a seamless experience to all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter your First Name in the text field labeled "First Name".
2. Enter your Last Name in the text field labeled "Last Name".
3. Enter your Email Address in the text field labeled "Email" (in the correct format of `username@example.com`).
4. Enter your Phone Number in the text field labeled "Phone Number".
5. Select the date you will be attending the workshop in the date field labeled "Workshop Date".
6. Select the time you will be attending the workshop in the time field labeled "Workshop Time".
7. Indicate if you are a member of a team in the select multiple field labeled "Team Members". You can select "Yes" if you are a member of a team or "No" if you are not.
8. Select the interests that apply to you in the select multiple field labeled "Participant Interests". You can select "Yes" for each interest that applies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your Email Address here, in the format `username@example.com`.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number here.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date you will be attending the workshop.
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, time, required/optional): Select the time you will be attending the workshop.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate whether you are a member of a team. Select "Yes" if you are and "No" if not.
* **Participant Interests** (`participant_interests`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the interests that apply to you. Select "Yes" for each interest that applies.
