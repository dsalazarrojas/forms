# DIY Ornament Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a DIY Ornament Workshop. Please complete the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure your spot in the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your workshop details.
2. Enter your contact information.
3. Enter attendee details.
4. Choose your workshop preferences.
5. Add any special requests.
6. Select the date and time of the workshop.
7. Enter your email address.
8. Enter your phone number.
9. Confirm your email address.
10. Confirm your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Details (1)** (`Workshop Details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the workshop you are interested in attending. This will help us understand your interests and preferences.
* **Contact Info (2)** (`Contact Info`, text, optional): Enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Attendee Details (3)** (`Attendee Details`, text, optional): Enter the details of the person attending the workshop. This may include their name, address, and any special requirements.
* **Workshop Preferences (4)** (`Workshop Preferences`, text, optional): Choose any special requests or preferences you have for the workshop.
* **Special Requests (5)** (`Special Requests`, text, optional): If you have any specific requests or needs, please list them here.
* **Date & Time (6)** (`Date & Time`, date, optional): Select the date and time you want to attend the workshop.
* **Email (7)** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter the email address you want to be contacted at.
* **Phone (8)** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Confirm Email (9)** (`Confirm Email`, email, optional): Confirm the email address you entered.
* **Confirm Phone (10)** (`Confirm Phone`, text, optional): Confirm your phone number.

## Tips

* Please make sure to double-check your email and phone number for accuracy.
* If you have any special requests or preferences, list them in the workshop preferences field.
* Make sure to select a date and time that works for you.
* We will contact you at the email address and phone number you provide.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
