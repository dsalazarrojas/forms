# EdTech Integration Fellowship Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from potential participants of the EdTech Integration Fellowship program. Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your password in the "Password" field.
5. Confirm your email address in the "Confirm Email" field.
6. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field (in the format YYYY-MM-DD).
7. Enter your time of birth in the "Time of Birth" field (in the format HH:MM:SS).
8. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
9. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
10. Select your country of origin from the "Country of Origin" field.
11. If you wish to select multiple countries, you can do so in the "Select Multiple Countries" field (optional).
12. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field (optional).
13. Confirm your phone number in the "Confirm Phone" field.
14. Enter the date of your email confirmation (in the format YYYY-MM-DD) in the "Email Confirmation" field.
15. Select your time of selection (in the format HH:MM:SS) in the "Time Selection" field.
16. Select your country of origin from the "Country 2" field.
17. If you wish to select multiple countries, you can do so in the "Select Multiple 2" field (optional).
18. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Note 2" field (optional).
19. Confirm your email address in the "Confirm Email 2" field.
20. Select multiple countries in the "Select Multiple 3", "Select Multiple 4", "Select Multiple 5", "Select Multiple 6", and "Select Multiple 7" fields (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to receive important updates and communications from the EdTech Integration Fellowship team.
* **Password**: Enter a password to secure your account.
* **Confirm Email**: Confirm your email address to ensure accuracy.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 1990-01-01).
* **Time of Birth**: Enter your time of birth in the format HH:MM:SS (e.g., 12:00:00).
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address**: Enter your address for contact purposes.
* **Country of Origin**: Select your country of origin to help us understand your background.
* **Select Multiple Countries**: If you have a dual citizenship or multiple countries of residence, select all the countries that apply.
* **Note**: Enter any additional comments or questions you may have about the program or your application.
* **Confirm Phone**: Confirm your phone number to ensure accuracy.
* **Email Confirmation**: Enter the date of your email confirmation in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Time Selection**: Select your preferred time of selection for the program.
* **Country 2**: Select your country of origin for the second instance.
* **Select Multiple 2**: If you have a dual citizenship or multiple countries of residence, select all the countries that apply for the second instance.
* **Note 2**: Enter any additional comments or questions you may have about the program or your application.
* **Confirm Email 2**: Confirm your email address to ensure accuracy.
* **Select Multiple 3**, **Select Multiple 4**, **Select Multiple 5**, **Select Multiple 6**, **Select Multiple 7**: If you have a dual citizenship or multiple countries of residence, select all the countries that apply for the multiple instances.

Note that some fields are optional and do not affect your eligibility or participation in the fellowship. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for clarification.
