# Kayaking_Safety_Course_Registration_Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kayaking_Safety_Course_Registration_Form is a registration form designed for participants in a kayaking safety course. This form helps to gather essential information about the participant, including contact details, medical conditions, and emergency contact information, to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for all.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Participant Details fields (fields 2-5, 7-12, 16, 17-18) with the requested information.
2. If you are registering someone else, please ensure you have their permission to share this information.
3. Fill in the Waiver Agreement field (field 6) to acknowledge that you understand and agree to the course rules and regulations.
4. Fill in the Emergency Contact field (field 7) with accurate information.
5. Fill in the Participant's Phone and Email fields (fields 12 and 13) with your contact information.
6. Fill in the Additional Notes and Information fields (fields 9-10) with any relevant details.
7. Check the Medical Conditions field (field 11) if you have any conditions that may affect your participation in the course.
8. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details 1** (`participant_details_1`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the participant's details.
* **Participant Details 2** (`participant_details_2`, text, required): Enter the participant's name.
* **Participant Details 3** (`participant_details_3`, text, required): Enter the participant's address.
* **Participant Details 4** (`participant_details_4`, text, required): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Details 5** (`participant_details_5`, text, optional): Enter any additional participant details.
* **Waiver Agreement** (`waiver_agreement`, text, optional): Acknowledge that you understand and agree to the course rules and regulations.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the emergency contact information (name, phone number, and email).
* **Participant's Phone** (`participant_details_8`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Participant's Email** (`participant_details_9`, text, required): Enter your contact email.
* **Additional Notes** (`participant_details_10`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or information.
* **Additional Information** (`participant_details_11`, text, optional): Enter any additional information.
* **Medical Conditions** (`participant_details_12`, text, optional): Check if you have any medical conditions that may affect your participation.
* **Participant's Age** (`participant_details_13`, number, optional): Enter your age.
* **Participant's Date of Birth** (`participant_details_14`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Submit 1** (`submit`, text, required): Review and submit the form.
* **Thank You!** (`form_conclusion_2`, note, optional): A confirmation message after submission.

Tips:

* Be sure to double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* If you have any medical conditions that may affect your participation, please list them in the Medical Conditions field.
* If you are registering someone else, please ensure you have their permission to share this information.
