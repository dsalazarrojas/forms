# Renewable Process Engineering Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals or organizations registering for a workshop or training session focused on renewable process engineering. It will help workshop organizers track attendance, gather demographic information, and collect necessary details for the attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form correctly, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your first and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field to ensure you can be contacted.
3. If applicable, select your program of interest from the "Program" field.
4. If applicable, enter your department or affiliation in the "Department" field.
5. If applicable, enter the course you are registered for in the "Course" field.
6. Enter your registration date in the "Registration Date" field.
7. If applicable, enter the time you are registered for in the "Time" field.
8. If you have any additional comments or information, please use the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address to ensure you can be contacted.
* **Program** (`program`, `text`, required/optional): If applicable, select your program of interest.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required/optional): If applicable, enter your department or affiliation.
* **Course** (`course`, `text`, required/optional): If applicable, enter the course you are registered for.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you are registered for the workshop or training session.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required/optional): If applicable, enter the time you are registered for.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required/optional): If you have any additional comments or information, please use this field.
