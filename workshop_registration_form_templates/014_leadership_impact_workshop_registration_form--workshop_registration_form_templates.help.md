# leadership_impact_workshop_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register participants for a leadership impact workshop. It collects demographic information, event details, and any additional notes from the participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **First Name** (page 1, field 1).
2. Enter your **Last Name** (page 1, field 2).
3. Provide your **Email** (page 1, field 3).
4. Specify whether you will be attending the **Event** (page 1, field 5).
5. Choose the **Event Dates** (page 1, field 6) you will be attending, if applicable.
6. Select the **Event Time** (page 1, field 7) you will be attending, if applicable.
7. Choose the type of **Event Type** (page 1, field 8) you will be attending.
8. Provide the **Event Location** (page 1, field 9) of the event.
9. Describe the **Event Details** (page 1, field 10) of the event, if necessary.
10. Add any **Notes** (page 1, field 11) about your registration.
11. Specify which tool has been **Assigned** (page 1, field 12) to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (page 1, field 1, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (page 1, field 2, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (page 1, field 3, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (page 1, field 4, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Event Attendance** (page 1, field 5, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" if you will be attending the event.
*   Note: This field will only be shown if you have selected "Yes" in the above field.
* **Event Dates** (page 1, field 6, date, required: false): Choose the dates you will be attending, if applicable.
* **Event Time** (page 1, field 7, time, required: false): Choose the time you will be attending, if applicable.
* **Event Type** (page 1, field 8, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or more event types (Event 1, Event 2, Event 3).
* **Event Location** (page 1, field 9, text, required: false): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Details** (page 1, field 10, text, required: false): Enter any details about the event.
* **Notes** (page 1, field 11, note, required: false): Add any additional notes.
* **Assigned Tool** (page 1, field 12, select_one, required: false): Select the tool assigned to you.
