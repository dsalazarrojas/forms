# Veterinary Supply Chain Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from individuals interested in attending the Veterinary Supply Chain Workshop. The collected information will be used to tailor the workshop experience and keep attendees informed about the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the respective text fields.
2. Input your Email address to receive updates about the workshop and related events.
3. Select your preferred Date of attendance.
4. Choose the correct Event Type, which could be at the clinic, supplier, school, or association.
5. Select the type(s) of attendee you are (e.g., Clinician, Supplier, Student, or Association member).
6. Indicate if you give your consent to share your information with the workshop organizers.
7. If you have any additional comments or requests, use the text area for Additional Comments.
8. Enter your Contact Number for future communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (1)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name (2)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email (3)**: Input your valid email address to receive updates and notifications about the workshop.
* **Date (4)**: Select the date you prefer to attend the workshop.
* **Time (5)**: Select the time slot you prefer to attend the workshop.
* **Event Type (6)**: Choose where you would like to attend the workshop, such as a clinic, supplier, school, or association.
* **Attendee Type (7)**: Select all the types that apply to you, such as Clinician, Supplier, Student, or Association member.
* **Consent (8)**: Indicate if you give your consent to share your information with the workshop organizers.
* **Additional Comments (9)**: Enter any additional comments or requests you may have regarding your attendance.
* **Contact Number (10)**: Enter your contact number for future communication with the workshop organizers.
