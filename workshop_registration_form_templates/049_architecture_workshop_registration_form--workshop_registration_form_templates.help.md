# Architecture Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from participants registering for an architecture workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your basic information such as name, email, phone number, job title, and organization or company name.
2. Select the workshop you are registering for.
3. Choose your experience level in the field of architecture or relevant area.
4. If you have any expectations from the workshop, write them down in the "Workshop Expectations" section.
5. If you have any special accessibility needs, please mention them in the "Accessibility Needs" section.
6. If you have dietary restrictions, please specify them in the "Dietary Restrictions" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Organization or Company**: Enter your organization or company name (if applicable).
* **Job Title or Role**: Enter your job title or role (if applicable).
* **Workshop Name**: Choose the workshop you are registering for.
* **Workshop Date**: Enter the date of the workshop you are registering for.
* **Workshop Time**: Enter the start time of the workshop (if applicable).
* **Instructor or Facilitator Name**: Enter the name of the instructor or facilitator (if applicable).
* **Your Experience Level**: Choose your experience level in the field of architecture or relevant area.
* **Prior Workshop Attendance**: Confirm if you have attended similar workshops before.
* **What do you want to learn?**: In the "What do you want to learn?" field, write down your specific goals and expectations from the workshop.
* **Specific Topics of Interest**: If there are any specific areas you want to focus on, mention them here.
* **Registration fee payment status**: Confirm whether you have paid the registration fee or if you require payment information.
* **Special Accessibility Needs**: If you have any special accessibility needs, mention them here.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: If you have any dietary restrictions, mention them here.
