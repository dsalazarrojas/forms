# Animal Training Workshop Scheduling Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us schedule and plan an animal training workshop that meets your specific needs. By filling out this form, you will be able to select the type of workshop, desired dates, budget, and other details that are essential for a successful event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields marked with a red star (*). These fields are essential for us to understand your needs and schedule the workshop accordingly.
2. Choose the type of workshop you are interested in from the "Type of Workshop Requested" field. This will help us determine the content and duration of the workshop.
3. Select your preferred workshop format from the "Workshop Format" field. This will help us decide on the logistics of the event.
4. Choose your preferred workshop dates from the "Preferred Workshop Dates" field. You can select up to 3 dates.
5. Enter your contact information, including your full name, email, phone number, and address.
6. Provide information on the number of participants you expect, their experience level, and the species focus of the workshop.
7. Select any specific topics you would like to cover during the workshop from the "Specific Topics to Cover" field.
8. If you have any special requirements or accommodations, please enter them in the "Special Requirements or Accommodations" field.
9. Finally, review your answers to ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Full Name (<*>) 
This field is required and should be filled out with your full name.

*   **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.

### 2. Organization Name 
This field is optional and should be filled out if you are representing an organization.

*   **Organization Name**: Enter the name of your organization.

### 3. Email Address (<*>) 
This field is required and should be filled out with your contact email.

*   **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address.

### 4. Phone Number (<*>) 
This field is required and should be filled out with your contact phone number.

*   **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.

### 5. Street Address 
This field is optional and should be filled out if you are providing a venue.

*   **Street Address**: Enter the address of the workshop location.

### 6. City (<*>) 
This field is required and should be filled out with the city of the workshop location.

*   **City**: Enter the city of the workshop location.

### 7. State or Province (<*>) 
This field is required and should be filled out with the state or province of the workshop location.

*   **State or Province**: Enter the state or province of the workshop location.

### 8. ZIP or Postal Code (<*>)

*   **ZIP or Postal Code**: Enter the zip or postal code of the workshop location.

### 9. Type of Workshop Requested (<*>)

### 12. Earliest Acceptable Date

### 13. Latest Acceptable Date
