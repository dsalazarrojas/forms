# Educational Neuroscience Exploration Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants about their background, interests, and experiences in the field of neuroscience. This information will help us tailor our educational content to meet the needs of our participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form with your information.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked with an asterisk *).
3. Review and submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form**: This field is used to capture any additional information about your workshop registration.
* **Participant Name**: This field is used to capture your name.
* **Participant E-mail**: This field is used to capture your e-mail address.
* **Participant Phone**: This field is used to capture your phone number.
* **Participant Notes**: This field is used to capture any additional comments or notes about your participation.
* **Participant Employment Status**: This field is used to select your current employment status.
	+ Employed: You are employed.
	+ Unemployed: You are not employed.
	+ Retired: You are retired.
* **Participant Degree Level**: This field is used to select your educational degree level.
	+ High School: You have a high school diploma.
	+ Bachelor: You have a bachelor's degree.
	+ Master: You have a master's degree.
	+ PhD: You have a doctorate degree.
* **Participant Education Field Of Interest**: This field is used to select multiple fields you are interested in.
	+ Neurology: You are interested in neurology.
	+ Psychology: You are interested in psychology.
	+ Physics: You are interested in physics.
	+ Biology: You are interested in biology.
* **Participant Education Level**: This field is used to select your educational level.
	+ High School: You have a high school diploma.
	+ Bachelor: You have a bachelor's degree.
	+ Master: You have a master's degree.
	+ PhD: You have a doctorate degree.
* **Participant Experience With Neuroscience**: This field is used to select your experience level with neuroscience.
	+ Beginner: You have little to no experience with neuroscience.
	+ Intermediate: You have some experience with neuroscience.
	+ Advanced: You have extensive experience with neuroscience.
* **Participant Additional Info**: This field is used to capture any additional comments or notes about your participation.
* **Participant Agreement**: This field is used to select if you agree to the participant agreement.
	+ True: You agree to the participant agreement.
	+ False: You do not agree to the participant agreement.
* **Participant Agreement Date**: This field is used to select the date of your agreement.
* **Participant Agreement Time**: This field is used to select the time of your agreement.
* **Participant Agreement Note**: This field is used to capture any additional comments or notes about your agreement.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your information before submitting your registration.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us.

Please submit your registration and we will get back to you shortly.
