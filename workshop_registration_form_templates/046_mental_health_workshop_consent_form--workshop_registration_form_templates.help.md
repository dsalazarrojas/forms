# mental_health_workshop_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects important contact and medical information to ensure the participant's safety and well-being during the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*): **title**, **age**, **email**, **name** of emergency contact, **emergency_name**, **workshop_name**, **workshop_time**, **workshop_date**.
2. For each question, provide accurate and thorough information.
3. If a medical condition, allergy, or special note is selected, please provide a brief explanation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **title** (text, required): Enter your name as you would like to be addressed during the workshop.
* **age** (number, required): Enter your age.
* **email** (email, required): Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **name** (text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **emergency_name** (text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **emergency_phone** (text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **emergency_email** (email, required): Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **workshop_name** (text, required): Enter the name of the workshop you are registering for.
* **workshop_date** (date, required): Enter the date of the workshop you are registering for.
* **workshop_time** (time, required): Enter the time of the workshop you are registering for.
* **agree_to_terms** (select_one, required): Confirm that you have read, understand, and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **medical_condition** (select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes, I have a medical condition' if you have any medical conditions that we should be aware of.
* **medical_note** (text, optional): Provide a brief explanation of any medical conditions, if applicable.
* **medical_allergies** (select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes, please list' if you have allergies.
* **medical_allergies_note** (text, optional): List any allergies, if applicable.
* **emergency_contact_instructions** (text, optional): Provide instructions for emergency contact.
* **emergency_contact** (text, optional): Provide a note about the emergency contact, if applicable.
* **consent** (select_one, required): Confirm that you have read, understand, and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **consent_note** (text, optional): Provide a note about the consent, if applicable.
* **participant_signature** (text, required): Sign this form to confirm that you have completed the workshop registration accurately and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Tips

- Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
- Double-check your contact and emergency contact information for accuracy and completeness.
- If you have any medical conditions, allergies, or special needs, please provide a brief explanation.
