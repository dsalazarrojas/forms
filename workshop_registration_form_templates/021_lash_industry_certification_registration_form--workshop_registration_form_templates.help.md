# Lash Industry Certification Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for certification in the Lash industry. It is meant to be filled out by individuals seeking certification, including workshop attendees or those looking to become certified in lash technology.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Ensure that you are truthful in your responses.
* Review your submission before submitting it for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Information (form_info)**: A text field to gather any additional information about the submission.
* **Applicant Name (applicant_name)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Previous Training (previous_training)**: If you have received any previous training in the Lash industry, select "Yes" and provide details in the "Training Details" field.
* **Verify Training (verify_training)**: Select "Yes" if you have previously verified your training, or "No" if you have not.
* **Training Details (training_details)**: If you selected "Yes" for "Previous Training", provide details about your previous training in this field.
* **Salon Information (salon_info)**: Enter your salon information.
* **Trainer Information (trainer_info)**: Enter the information of your trainer or instructor.
* **Training Program (training_program)**: If you are currently enrolled or have previously enrolled in a training program, select "Yes".
* **Date of Birth (date_of_birth)**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Time of Day (time_of_day)**: Enter the time of day you are submitting this form.
* **Note (note)**: If you have any additional comments or notes, enter them here. This field is optional.
* **Form ID (form_id)**: This field is for internal use only and will be filled out automatically. Do not fill it out.
