# virtual_shopping_assistant_demo_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registering for a virtual shopping assistant demo. The form is designed to gather user information and preferences for the demo.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the main page with your details.
2. Select your preferences for the next step of the demo.
3. Confirm your submission details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main Page** (`main`, `text`, required: false): Please fill out this page with your details.
* **Next Step 1** (`next_step_1`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your interest in participating in the next step of the demo.
* **Next Step 2** (`next_step_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to confirm your participation in the next step of the demo.
* **Submission** (`submission`, `note`, required: false): This is a space to include any additional comments or details about your submission.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and honest answers to ensure that the demo is tailored to your needs.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please contact the demo organizers for clarification.
* Please submit the form only once, to avoid duplicate submissions.
