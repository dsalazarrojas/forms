# Workshop Registration On Equality Issues - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users who want to register for a workshop on equality issues. This information is used by workshop organizers to tailor the workshop experience to meet the needs of all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant details in the "participant_details" field.
2. Select your session choices by choosing the options that apply to you in the "session_choices" field.
3. Indicate your accessibility needs in the "accessibility_needs" field.
4. Once you have filled out all the necessary information, click the "submit" button to submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_details** (text, required/optional): Please fill in your name, contact information, and any other details you would like us to know about you.
* **session_choices** (select_multiple, required/optional): Please select all the sessions that interest you. You can choose more than one option if applicable.
* **accessibility_needs** (select_one, required/optional): Please indicate if you have any accessibility needs that we should be aware of when organizing the workshop.
* **submit** (text, required/optional): Please submit your registration by clicking this button to confirm your participation.
