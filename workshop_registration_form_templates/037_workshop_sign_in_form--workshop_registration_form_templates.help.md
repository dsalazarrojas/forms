# Workshop Sign In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Workshop Sign In Form is used to collect attendee information for a workshop. It is a simple form that gathers basic details such as first and last name, email, phone number, and attendee type. This form is designed to be easy to fill out and provides a clear and straightforward way for attendees to register for a workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Email address in the Email field. This field is used to communicate with you about the workshop.
4. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
5. Select your Attendee Type from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (text)**: Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name (text)**: Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address in this field. This will be used for communication about the workshop.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number in this field.
* **Attendee Type (select_one)**: Choose your role for the workshop from the options:
	+ Participant
	+ Presenter
	+ Both

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information to ensure proper communication and workshop organization.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact the workshop organizers.
* If you need to make changes to your registration, please contact the workshop organizers for assistance.
