# Youth Interactive Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Interactive Workshop Registration Form is designed to collect participant information for a workshop, which is a series of interactive activities for young people. This form helps us to understand your preferences and needs for the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out each section carefully and thoroughly. You can save your progress and come back later if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details** (Page 1):
	+ **Participant Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
	+ **Participant Email**: Enter a valid email address to receive updates and information about the workshop.
	+ **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Contact Details** (Page 2):
	+ **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person responsible for your contact information.
	+ **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the person responsible for your contact information.
	+ **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person responsible for your contact information.
* **Workshop Preferences** (Page 3):
	+ **Select one or more options for your workshop preferences**: Choose the options that best fit your needs.
* **Participant Notes** (Page 4):
	+ **Additional information about you**: Enter any additional details that you think will help us understand you better.
* **Participant Gender** (Page 5):
	+ **Participant Gender**: Select your gender from the options provided.
	+ **Participant Gender Other (Description)**: If your gender is not listed, please describe it here.
* **Participant Occupation** (Page 6):
	+ **Enter your occupation**: Describe your occupation or job title.
* **School Details** (Page 7):
	+ **School Name**: Enter the name of your school.
	+ **Class**: Enter your class name or number.
	+ **Teacher Name**: Enter the name of your teacher.
	+ **Teacher Contact Info**: Enter your teacher's contact information.
* **Workshop Details** (Page 8):
	+ **Enter any additional information about the workshop**: Describe your expectations and needs for the workshop.
* **Workshop Schedule** (Page 9):
	+ **Participant Workshop Date**: Enter the date of your workshop.
	+ **Participant Workshop Time**: Enter the time of your workshop.
	+ **Participant Workshop Session**: Enter the session number of your workshop.
	+ **Participant Workshop Duration**: Enter the duration of your workshop in hours.
	+ **Participant Workshop Location**: Enter the location of your workshop.
* **Registration and Signature** (Page 10):
	+ **Participant Signature**: Please sign your name to confirm your registration.
	+ **Participant Terms of Service**: Select the option that best fits your understanding of the terms of service.

Please note that the fields and page layout may be subject to change, but the above explanation will guide you through the process. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.

This form is designed to be user-friendly, easy to understand, and efficient to complete.
