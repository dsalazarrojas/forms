# Data Ethics In Education Roundtable Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register attendees for the Data Ethics In Education Roundtable. It helps us understand who will be joining the discussion and what topics they are interested in. This information will be used to tailor the event to the needs and interests of all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the "User Details" page with your name, role, and contact information.
2. On the "Topic Interests" page, select all the topics you are interested in (you can select multiple options).
3. On the "Consent For Communication" page, choose whether you agree or do not agree to receive communications about future events.
4. You may optionally add any additional notes or comments on the "Notes" page.
5. On the "Attendee Details" page, please fill in your attendee information (name, role, and contact email).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **User Details (Page 1)**
* **User Details (User Details)** (`text`, Required): Please fill in your name, role (e.g., expert, participant, organizer), and contact information (email, phone number, etc.).
### **Topic Interests (Page 2)**
* **Topic Interests (Topic Interests)** (`select_multiple`, Optional): Select all topics you are interested in, such as:
	+ Machine Learning
	+ Data Protection
	+ Data Use and Sharing
### **Consent For Communication (Page 3)**
* **Consent For Communication (Consent For Communication)** (`select_one`, Optional): Choose whether you agree (`I agree`) or do not agree (`I do not agree`) to receive communications about future events.
### **Notes (Page 4)**
* **Notes (Notes)** (`note`, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your registration.
### **Attendee Details (Page 5)**
* **Attendee Details (Attendee Details)** (`text`, Required): Fill in your name, role, and contact email to complete your registration.
