# Responsible Beverage Service Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from participants for the Responsible Beverage Service Training program. It helps trainers or administrators track participant details and manage the training session effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Training Provider** name in the text field provided.
2. Provide your **Participant Name** in the text field.
3. Enter your **Contact Number** in the text field.
4. Enter your **Email** address.
5. Select a **Training Date** from the calendar or date picker.
6. Check the box for **Consent** (if applicable).
7. Choose your **Participant Details** from the select menu.
8. Select the **Assigned Tool** from the select menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Training Provider**: Enter the name of the training provider, such as the company or organization offering the training.
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for communication.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for updates or notifications.
* **Training Date**: Select a date for the training session you wish to attend. Please ensure you are available on the selected date.
* **Consent**: Check this box if you have read and understood the training program's terms and conditions.
* **Participant Details**: Choose one of the options provided, indicating your participation status (e.g., "Option 1: Attending", "Option 2: Not Attending").
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool assigned to you for the training session.

Note: Please complete all fields to ensure your registration is processed correctly.
