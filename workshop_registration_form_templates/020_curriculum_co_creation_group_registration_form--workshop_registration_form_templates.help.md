# Curriculum Co Creation Group Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Curriculum Co Creation Group Registration Form is a tool used to register and provide information about your group and meeting details. This form helps to keep track of your group's registration and ensures that all necessary information is captured accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **User Name** (required) in the first field. This is your name as it will be displayed in the system.
2. Enter your **Email Address** (required) in the second field. This will be the email address that the system will use to contact you.
3. Select your **Role** (required) in the third field. This can be either "Yes" or "No" indicating whether you are a member of the group.
4. Enter your **Group Name** (required) in the fourth field. This is the name of your group as it will be displayed in the system.
5. (Optional) Enter the **Meeting Date** in the fifth field.
6. (Optional) Enter the **Meeting Time** in the sixth field.
7. (Optional) Enter the **Meeting Duration** in the seventh field.
8. (Optional) Enter any **Meeting Notes** in the eighth field.
9. (Optional) Enter the **Submit Date** and **Submit Time** in the ninth and tenth fields.
10. (Optional) Enter any **Comments** about your registration in the eleventh field.
11. Enter the **Submitted By** in the twelfth field.
12. Enter the **Submitted On** and **Submitted At** in the thirteenth and fourteenth fields.
13. Enter your **Submitted By Name** in the fifteenth field.
14. Enter the **Submitted At Time** in the sixteenth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name (User Name, text, required)**: Enter your name as it will be displayed in the system.
* **Email Address (Email Address, email, required)**: Enter your email address that the system will use to contact you.
* **Role (Role, select_one, required)**: Select either "Yes" or "No" indicating whether you are a member of the group.
* **Group Name (Group Name, text, required)**: Enter the name of your group as it will be displayed in the system.
* **Meeting Date (Meeting Date, date, optional)**: Enter the date of your meeting.
* **Meeting Time (Meeting Time, time, optional)**: Enter the time of your meeting.
* **Meeting Duration (Meeting Duration, number, optional)**: Enter the duration of your meeting.
* **Meeting Notes (Meeting Notes, note, optional)**: Enter any notes about your meeting.
* **Submit Date (Submit Date, date, optional)**: Enter the date you submitted your registration.
* **Submit Time (Submit Time, time, optional)**: Enter the time you submitted your registration.
* **Comments (Comments, text, optional)**: Enter any comments about your registration.
* **Submitted By (Submitted By, text, optional)**: Enter your name as it will be displayed in the system.
* **Submitted On (Submitted On, date, optional)**: Enter the date you submitted your registration.
* **Submitted At (Submitted At, time, optional)**: Enter the time you submitted your registration.
* **Submitted By Name (Submitted By Name, text, optional)**: Enter your name as it will be displayed in the system.
* **Submitted At Time (Submitted At Time, time, optional)**: Enter the time you submitted your registration.
