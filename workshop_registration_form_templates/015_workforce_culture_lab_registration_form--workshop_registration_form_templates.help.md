# Workforce Culture Lab Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form captures essential details for workshop registrations, supporting the planning and execution of workshops within the Workforce Culture Lab.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. This is your given name.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field. This is your surname.
3. Provide your **Email** address in the "Email" field. This will be used for communication related to the workshop.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field for contact purposes.
5. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the "Date of Birth" field.
6. Select the **Workshop Type** that you wish to register for from the dropdown options.
7. Enter any **Additional Comments** related to your registration in the "Additional Comments" field.
8. Select your workshop **Participants** from the dropdown options (e.g., "Participant 1", "Participant 2", "Participant 3", etc.).
9. Enter the **Workshop Facilitators**' names or details if applicable.
10. Provide the **Workshop Location** where the workshop will take place.
11. Enter the **Workshop Duration** in hours.
12. Select the **Workshop Start Date** from the calendar or use the custom date format.
13. Select the **Workshop End Date** from the calendar or use the custom date format.
14. Enter the **Workshop Time** in 24-hour format.
15. Select the **Workshop Status** from the dropdown options (e.g., "Active", "Inactive", "Pending").
16. Enter any **Workshop Notes** related to the event.
17. Provide an update on the **Workshop Status Update** if needed.
18. Enter **Workshop Additional Details** if necessary.
19. Enter **Participant Additional Details** if applicable.
20. Enter the **Registration Date** when you registered.
21. Enter the **Registration Time** in 24-hour format.
22. Select the **Registration Status** from the dropdown options (e.g., "Active", "Inactive", "Pending").
23. Enter any **Registration Notes** related to your registration.
24. Ensure all fields are completed accurately and according to the specified formats.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your surname.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Optional): Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Workshop Type** (Select One, Optional): Choose the workshop type you wish to register for.
* **Additional Comments** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments related to your registration.
* **Workshop Participants** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select all participants that will be attending the workshop.
* **Workshop Facilitators** (Text, Optional): Enter the facilitators' names or details if applicable.
* **Workshop Location** (Text, Optional): Provide the workshop location where it will take place.
* **Workshop Duration** (Number, Optional): Enter the workshop duration in hours.
* **Workshop Start Date** (Date, Optional): Select the start date of the workshop from the calendar or use the custom date format.
* **Workshop End Date** (Date, Optional): Select the end date of the workshop from the calendar or use the custom date format.
* **Workshop Time** (Time, Optional): Enter the workshop time in 24-hour format.
* **Workshop Status** (Select One, Optional): Choose the workshop status (e.g., "Active", "Inactive", "Pending").
* **Workshop Notes** (Text, Optional): Enter any notes related to the workshop.
* **Workshop Status Update** (Text, Optional): Provide an update on the workshop status if needed.
* **Workshop Additional Details** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional details about the workshop.
* **Participant Additional Details** (Text, Optional): Enter any details about the participants.
* **Registration Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date you registered.
* **Registration Time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time you registered in 24-hour format.
* **Registration Status** (Select One, Optional): Choose your registration status (e.g., "Active", "Inactive", "Pending").
* **Registration Notes** (Text, Optional): Enter any notes related to your registration.

## Tips

- Double-check that all fields are filled in accurately before submitting the form.
- If you encounter any issues, contact the workshop organizers for assistance.
- Ensure that all dates and times are in the correct formats (YYYY-MM-DD for dates, 24-hour format for times).
