# public_speaking_workshop_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from potential participants in a public speaking workshop. It helps workshop organizers track and manage registrations, ensuring accurate records and communication with attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the first_name field.
2. Enter your last name in the last_name field.
3. Provide your email address in the email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the phone field.
5. Select the workshop date in the workshop_date field (format: dd/mm/yyyy).
6. Choose the workshop time in the workshop_time field.
7. Enter your name as a participant in the participant_name field.
8. Provide your email address as a participant in the participant_email field.
9. Enter your phone number as a participant in the participant_phone field.
10. Indicate your attendance status in the workshop_attendance field by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
11. Add any additional notes or comments in the additional_notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name** (text, Required: no): Enter your first name as it should be displayed in our records.
* **last_name** (text, Required: no): Enter your last name as it should be displayed in our records.
* **email** (email, Required: no): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **phone** (text, Required: no): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **workshop_date** (date, Required: no): Select the date of the workshop you will attend.
* **workshop_time** (time, Required: no): Choose the time slot of the workshop you will attend.
* **participant_name** (text, Required: no): Enter your name as it will be displayed on our records for your participation.
* **participant_email** (email, Required: no): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you as a participant.
* **participant_phone** (text, Required: no): Enter your phone number as a participant.
* **workshop_attendance** (select_one, Required: no): Indicate your attendance status by choosing 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **additional_notes** (note, Required: no): Add any additional comments or information that might be useful for us to know.

## Tips
* Please make sure to double-check your details before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you are entering accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
