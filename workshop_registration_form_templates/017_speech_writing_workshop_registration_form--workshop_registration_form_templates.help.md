# Speech Writing Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from workshop participants, including their participant details, workshop preferences, and submissions. It is intended for use during the Speech Writing Workshop to streamline data collection and enhance the participant experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Participant Information** section with your details as prompted.
2. Provide **Workshop Information** about the workshop you are participating in.
3. Share your **Participant Preferences** with us.
4. Submit your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, text, required: false)
• This section is where you can provide any additional information about yourself that you think is relevant for the workshop organizers.
### **Workshop Information** (`workshop_information`, text, required: false)
• In this section, please provide any information about the workshop you are participating in, such as the topic or speaker.
### **Participant Preferences** (`participant_preferences`, text, required: false)
• Share with us any specific preferences or requirements you have, such as dietary needs or equipment requirements.
### **Submission** (`submission`, text, required: false)
• This is where you can upload any supporting materials or submissions you have prepared for the workshop, such as a written statement or a draft of your speech.
## Tips
• Please ensure that your submissions are accurate and relevant to the workshop.
• If you have any questions or concerns, contact form@gic.mx for assistance.
