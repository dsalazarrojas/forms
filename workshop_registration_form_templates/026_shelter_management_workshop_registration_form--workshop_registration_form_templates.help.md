# Shelter Management Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shelter Management Workshop Registration Form is used to collect information from participants who wish to attend the workshop. This includes session preferences, contact information, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by selecting the session that best suits your schedule and preference.
- Enter your contact information, including name, phone number, and email.
- Provide any additional notes or information about yourself.
- Select your participant type.
- Choose the type of sessions you would like to attend.
- Enter your date of availability.
- Select your preferred time slot.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details (1)**: Enter your name as it appears on your ID.
* **Session Preferences (2)**: Select the session that best suits your schedule and preference.
* **Logistics Information (3)**: Enter any additional details about travel and accommodation.
* **Contact Information (4)**: Enter your contact information, including name, phone number, and email.
* **Name (5)**: Enter your name as it appears on your ID.
* **Phone (6)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Email (7)**: Enter your email address.
* **Note (8)**: Provide any additional information about yourself.
* **Participant Type (9)**: Select your participant type.
* **Session Type (10)**: Select the type of sessions you would like to attend.
* **Date (11)**: Enter your date of availability.
* **Time (12)**: Select your preferred time slot.

## Tips
- Be accurate and thorough when filling out the form to avoid delays in processing.
- Ensure that you have all necessary information before starting the form.
- If you have any doubts or questions, contact us for assistance.
