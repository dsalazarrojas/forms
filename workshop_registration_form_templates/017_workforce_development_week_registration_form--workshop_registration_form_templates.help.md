# Workforce Development Week Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering participants for the Workforce Development Week, a professional development event. Please fill it out accurately and completely to ensure we can better manage the event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address (fields 1 & 2).
2. Choose whether you have a company affiliation (field 3). If you do, enter your company name. If not, you can leave this field blank.
3. Check off any roles you hold within your organization (field 4).
4. Select your job title (field 5).
5. Identify any departments you're associated with (field 6).
6. Specify your manager's status (field 7).
7. List any teams you're a part of (field 8).
8. Enter your name (field 9).
9. Enter your emergency contact's relationship to you (field 10).
10. Enter your phone number (field 12).
11. Enter your address (field 13).
12. Check off whether you have an emergency contact (field 14).
13. Enter your emergency contact's name (field 15).
14. Enter your emergency contact's phone number (field 16).
15. Enter your emergency contact's email (field 17).
16. Specify your confirmation status (field 18).
17. Select your confirmation date (field 19).
18. Select your confirmation time (field 20).
19. Add any additional notes (field 21).
20. Check off your emergency contact relationship 2 (field 22).
21. Enter your emergency contact's name 2 (field 23).
22. Enter your emergency contact's phone number 2 (field 24).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (field 1): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (field 2): Enter your business email address.
* **Company** (field 3): If you have a company, please enter its name. If you don't have a company, leave this field blank.
* **Role** (field 4): Check off any roles you hold within your organization.
* **Job Title** (field 5): Select your job title from the options provided.
* **Department** (field 6): Identify any departments you're associated with.
* **Manager** (field 7): Specify your manager's status.
* **Team** (field 8): List any teams you're a part of.
* **Name 2** (field 9): Enter your name (if different from field 1).
* **Email 2** (field 10): Enter your secondary email address (if you have one).
* **Phone** (field 11): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (field 12): Enter your full address.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (field 13): Check off whether you have an emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (field 14): Enter your emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (field 15): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (field 16): Enter your emergency contact's email address.
* **Confirmation Status** (field 17): Specify your confirmation status.
* **Confirmation Date** (field 18): Select your confirmation date from the calendar.
* **Confirmation Time** (field 19): Select your confirmation time.
* **Notes** (field 20): Add any additional notes about your registration.
* **Additional Notes** (field 21): Add any additional information.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship 2** (field 22): Check off whether you have an emergency contact 2.
* **Emergency Contact Name 2** (field 23): Enter your emergency contact's name 2.
* **Emergency Contact Phone 2** (field 24): Enter your emergency contact's phone number 2.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact the event organizers for assistance.
* Please note that this form is for the purpose of event registration only; any personal or sensitive data will be treated with respect and confidentiality.
