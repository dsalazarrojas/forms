# diy_workshop_reservation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The DIY Workshop Reservation Form is designed to gather information from participants who are interested in attending a workshop. This form will help event organizers collect relevant details about the participants, such as their contact information and workshop preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your participant information on the first page.
2. Enter your contact details on the second page.
3. Choose your preferred workshop(s) on the third page.
4. Select the date you would like to attend the workshop on the fourth page.
5. Choose the time slot for the workshop on the fifth page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`Participant Information`, text, required: false): This page is where you can enter your participant information, such as your name, email, or any other relevant details.
* **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, text, required: false): In this section, please provide your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
* **Workshop Preferences** (`Workshop Preferences`, select multiple, required: false): Select the workshops you are interested in attending from the list. You can choose more than one workshop if you're interested.
* **Workshop Date** (`Workshop Date`, date, required: false): Please select the date you would like to attend the workshop. You can choose a specific date from the available options.
* **Workshop Time** (`Workshop Time`, time, required: false): Select the time slot you prefer for the workshop. The available time slots will depend on the workshop date you selected.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information to help us better communicate with you.
* If you have any specific needs or requirements, please let us know in the participant information section.
* You can choose more than one workshop if you're interested in attending multiple sessions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
