# Ingredient Quality Symposium Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for attendees to register for the Ingredient Quality Symposium.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
* Fill in your first and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
* Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
* If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field.
* If you have a date of birth, enter it in the "Date of Birth" field.
* Enter your address in the "Address" field.
* If you have a company, enter it in the "Company" field.
* If you have a position, enter it in the "Position" field.
* Select your "Registration Status" and "Payment Method" from the respective dropdown menus.
* Select your "Payment Status" from the dropdown menu.
* Choose which attendees (Symposium) you will attend from the "Attendees (Symposium)" multiple-select field.
* Provide any additional information in the "Other Information" field.
* Accept or decline the "Terms of Service" by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Terms of Service" dropdown menu.
* Finally, submit your registration by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, Required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, Required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, Optional): If you have a phone number, enter it here.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, `date`, Optional): If you have a date of birth, enter it here.
* **Address** (`Address`, `text`, Required): Enter your address.
* **Company** (`Company`, `text`, Optional): If you have a company, enter it here.
* **Position** (`Position`, `text`, Optional): If you have a position, enter it here.
* **Registration Status** (`Registration Status`, `select_one`, Required): Select your current registration status.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, `select_one`, Required): Select your payment method.
* **Payment Status** (`Payment Status`, `select_one`, Required): Select your payment status.
* **Attendees (Symposium)** (`Attendees (Symposium)`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Choose which attendees you will attend.
* **Other Information** (`Other Information`, `note`, Optional): Provide any additional information here.
* **Terms of Service** (`Terms of Service`, `select_one`, Required): Accept or decline the terms of service.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, `text`, Required): Click this button to submit your registration.
