# Customer Journey Mapping Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Journey Mapping Workshop Registration form is designed to collect information from customers to help us better understand their needs and preferences for the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your Email address in the "Email" field. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
4. Choose the date of the workshop you wish to attend in the "Workshop Date" field.
5. Select the time slot of the workshop you prefer in the "Workshop Time" field.
6. Specify the duration you expect to attend in the "Workshop Duration" field.
7. Enter the location of the workshop in the "Workshop Location" field.
8. Select the date of your registration in the "Registration Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you about the workshop and any updates or notifications.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, required/optional): Choose the date you wish to attend the workshop. This is when we will be holding the workshop.
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, time, required/optional): Select the time slot you prefer for the workshop. We will be offering multiple time slots for your convenience.
* **Workshop Duration** (`workshop_duration`, number, required/optional): Please specify how many hours you plan to attend the workshop. This will help us plan for the right number of attendees and resources.
* **Workshop Location** (`workshop_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location of the workshop. This will be the venue where the workshop will take place.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date and time you registered for the workshop. This will help us track who has registered and when. 

## Tips

Please ensure you enter accurate and valid information for each field, as this will help us serve you better throughout the workshop. If you have any queries or changes to your registration, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
