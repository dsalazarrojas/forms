# health_data_transparency_forum_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help the Health Data Transparency Forum organizers gather information from users interested in registering for the workshop. Please fill out this form to let us know about your interest in the workshop.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each of the sections below. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to help us best assist you in planning the workshop.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (1, text, required): Please enter your name to help us identify you in our records.
* **Workshop Info** (2, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the workshops you are interested in attending. This will help us gauge your interest in each workshop and plan accordingly.
* **Date** (3, date, optional): If you have a preferred date for the workshop, please let us know. This will help us plan the workshop schedule.
* **Time** (4, time, optional): If you have a preferred time for the workshop, please let us know. This will help us plan the workshop schedule.
* **Address** (5, text, required): Please enter a valid address to help us contact you in case of any updates or changes to the workshop.
* **Notes** (6, note, optional): If you have any personal notes or preferences that you would like to share with us, please use this field to do so.

**Tips:**
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to help us plan the workshop effectively.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to us at [contact email/phone number].
