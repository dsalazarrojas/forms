# Educational Workshops Stay Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register users for educational workshops, providing information about themselves and their workshop preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the User Information section with your details.
2. Provide stay details in the Stay Details section.
3. Add workshop information in the Workshop Information section.
4. Enter participant information for yourself and any others who will be participating.
5. Review your submission in the Review section.
6. Confirm your submission in the Confirm Submission section.
7. Review your submission history in the Submission History section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, `text`, optional): Enter your name, email, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Stay Details** (`stay_details`, `text`, optional): Provide the details of your stay, including dates and accommodation preferences.
* **Workshop Information** (`workshop_info`, `text`, optional): Enter the name and description of the workshop you wish to attend.
* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, `text`, optional): List the names and contact information of any participants accompanying you.
* **Submission** (`submission`, `text`, optional): This is a free-text field for any additional comments or information.
* **Confirm Submission** (`confirm`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm that you have completed the form accurately.
* **Review** (`review`, `text`, optional): Review your submission to ensure it is accurate and complete.
* **Submission History** (`submission_history`, `text`, optional): Review your previous submissions for any relevant information.

Note: The required/optional status of each field indicates whether it is a required field or not. Some fields, like **Confirm Submission**, are required to confirm that the form is accurate, but others, like **Submission History**, are not required.
