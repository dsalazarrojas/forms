# Product Customization Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users who wish to attend a product customization workshop. Fill out the form to register your interest and provide any additional comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your personal contact information: first name, last name, and email.
2. Select your preferred workshop details: workshop name, date, start and end times, and venue.
3. Add any additional comments to provide more information about your attendance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): This is your given first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): This is your surname.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): This is the email address we'll use to contact you about the workshop.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): This is your phone number for additional communication.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Select the date you'd like to attend the workshop.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, required: false): Choose the start time of the workshop you'd like to attend.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`, required: false): Select the end time of the workshop you'd like to attend.
* **Workshop Name** (`workshop_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the workshop you're interested in attending.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, `date`, required: false): Choose the date of the workshop you'd like to attend.
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, `time`, required: false): Select the start and end times of the workshop you'd like to attend.
* **Workshop Venue** (`workshop_venue`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location of the workshop you'd like to attend.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional information about your attendance or preferences.

## Tips
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy to ensure smooth communication.
* Be specific when selecting workshop details to ensure you attend the correct workshop.
* Use the comments field to provide any additional context about your attendance.
