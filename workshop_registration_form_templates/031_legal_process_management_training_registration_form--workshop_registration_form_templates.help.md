# Legal Process Management Training Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your interests in attending the Legal Process Management Training session. We want to gather information about you, such as your contact details and preferences for the training session, so we can best accommodate your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields as instructed below.
2. Review and confirm your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **First Name**
* **Label:** First Name
* **Type:** text
* **Purpose:** Enter your first name for identification purposes.

### 2. **Last Name**
* **Label:** Last Name
* **Type:** text
* **Purpose:** Enter your last name for identification purposes.

### 3. **Email**
* **Label:** Email
* **Type:** email
* **Purpose:** Enter your email address for us to get in touch with you about the training session.

### 4. **Phone Number**
* **Label:** Phone Number
* **Type:** text
* **Purpose:** Enter your phone number so we can reach you about any further details regarding the training.

### 5. **Address**
* **Label:** Address
* **Type:** text
* **Purpose:** Enter your address for us to get in touch with you in case of any last-minute updates.

### 6. **Province**
* **Label:** Province
* **Type:** select_one
* **Purpose:** Indicate if you are interested in attending a training session in a specific province.

### 7. **Training Date**
* **Label:** Training Date
* **Type:** date
* **Purpose:** Select a date for the training session you are interested in.

### 8. **Training Time**
* **Label:** Training Time
* **Type:** time
* **Purpose:** Select a time slot for the training session you are interested in.

### 9. **Training Location**
* **Label:** Training Location
* **Type:** select_multiple
* **Purpose:** Indicate which training locations you are interested in, if any.

### 10. **Payment Method**
* **Label:** Payment Method
* **Type:** select_one
* **Purpose:** Indicate your preferred payment method, if you need to pay for the training session.

### 11. **Notes**
* **Label:** Notes
* **Type:** note
* **Purpose:** Add any additional comments or requests regarding your participation.

### 12. **Confirm Email**
* **Label:** Confirm Email
* **Type:** email
* **Purpose:** Re-enter your email address to confirm it with us.

### 13. **Confirm Phone**
* **Label:** Confirm Phone
* **Type:** text
* **Purpose:** Re-enter your phone number to confirm it with us.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields with a red asterisk (\*) are mandatory.
* Use the correct format for the date (YYYY-MM-DD) and time (HH:MM).
* If you have any specific requirements or requests for the training session, please mention them in the notes section.
