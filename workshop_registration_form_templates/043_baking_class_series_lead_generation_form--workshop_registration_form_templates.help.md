# Baking Class Series Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to generate leads for a baking class series. It will help us understand your interests, skills, and preferences to tailor the class series to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide your Email Address and Phone Number (optional).
3. Select all that apply from the "Type of Baking Class Interested In" list.
4. Choose your current baking skill level.
5. Select your preferred class format (in-person, online, or hybrid).
6. Choose your preferred days of the week for the class.
7. Select your preferred time slots for the class.
8. Choose your preferred series duration.
9. Select your commitment level for regular attendance.
10. Indicate how often you'd like to attend classes.
11. Describe your learning goals.
12. (Optional) Provide your baking experience.
13. Provide your preferred class location or online platform.
14. (Optional) Specify your budget range per class.
15. (Optional) Mention any special requirements or accommodations.
16. Provide any additional comments or questions.
17. Indicate whether you'd like to receive information about the class series.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`id: 2`): Enter your first name as it is written on your documents.
* **Last Name** (`id: 3`): Enter your last name as it is written on your documents.
* **Email Address** (`id: 4`): Enter your valid email address to receive updates and information about the class series.
* **Phone Number** (`id: 5`): Optional phone number to help us contact you if needed.
* **Type of Baking Class Interested In** (`id: 7`): Select all that apply from the list (e.g., Bread Making, Pastry and Desserts, etc.).
* **Your Current Baking Skill Level** (`id: 8`): Choose your current skill level (e.g., Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
* **Preferred Class Format** (`id: 9`): Select your preferred class format (e.g., In-Person, Online, Hybrid).
* **Preferred Days** (`id: 11`): Select all that apply from the list (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, etc.).
* **Preferred Times** (`id: 12`): Select your preferred time slot (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Weekend).
* **Preferred Series Duration** (`id: 13`): Choose your preferred series duration (e.g., 4 Weeks, 8 Weeks, 12 Weeks, Ongoing).
* **Can You Commit to Regular Attendance?** (`id: 15`): Indicate your commitment level for regular attendance (e.g., Yes, All Classes, Mostly, Some Flexibility, Uncertain).
* **How Often Would You Like Classes?** (`id: 16`): Indicate how often you'd like to attend classes (e.g., Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly, Flexible).
* **What Are Your Learning Goals?** (`id: 18`): Describe your learning goals (e.g., Improve baking skills, Learn new recipes, etc.).
* **Describe Your Baking Experience** (`id: 19`): (Optional) Provide your baking experience to help us tailor the class series.

* **Additional Comments or Questions** (`id: 24`): Provide any additional comments or questions.
