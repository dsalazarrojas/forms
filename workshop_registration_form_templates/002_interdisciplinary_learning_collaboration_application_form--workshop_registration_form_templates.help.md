## Interdisciplinary Learning Collaboration Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help teams and organizations collaborate on projects that bring together diverse disciplines and expertise. It guides project proposals, team details, project status updates, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all necessary information.
2. Enter your project proposal in the "Project Proposal" field.
3. Provide team details in the "Team Details" field.
4. Enter project details in the "Project Details" field.
5. Describe your evaluation criterion in the "Evaluation Criterion" field.
6. Enter your evaluation results in the "Evaluation Results" field.
7. Upload your project submission in the "Submission" field.
8. Review and update your project status in the "Review" field.
9. Enter your project status in the "Project Status" field.
10. Provide notes and updates in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Registration Form**: This is the main form section.
	+ **Project Proposal**: A brief description of your project proposal.
		- Enter a short paragraph explaining your project idea and goals.
	+ **Team Details**: A list of team members involved in your project.
		- List the names and affiliations of team members.
	+ **Project Details**: A detailed description of your project.
		- Provide a comprehensive overview of your project's goals, objectives, and scope.
* **Project Status**: An update on your project's progress.
	+ **Project Status Update**: A brief description of your project's current status.
* **Evaluation Criterion**: A description of how you will evaluate project progress.
	+ **Evaluation Results**: The outcome of your evaluation process.
* **Submission**: Attach any supporting materials for your project.
	+ **Submission**: Upload any relevant files or documents.
* **Review**: Review and update your project status and notes.
* **Notes**: A field for notes and updates on your project.
* **Approval**: This field is not required for completion.
* **Submit**: This field is not required for completion.

## Tips

* Make sure to review the form carefully before submission.
* Use clear and concise language in all fields.
* Attach relevant supporting materials for your project.
* Keep your project proposal and project details comprehensive and up-to-date.
* Regularly update your project status and notes.
* Ensure that your evaluation criterion and evaluation results are accurate and fair.
