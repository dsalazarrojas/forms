# Cultural Immersion Workshop Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for registering for a cultural immersion workshop. It captures essential details about you and your preferences to help us accommodate your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To ensure a smooth registration process, please carefully read through the following steps:

1. Fill out each field with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Select the correct options for your language, level of participation, and availability preferences.
3. Review the form thoroughly before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Registration Form 1** (`workshop_registration_form_1`, text, required: false): This field is a free-text area where you can provide any additional information you'd like to share about your registration.
* **Workshop Details 1** (`workshop_details_1`, text, required: false): Here, you can provide any relevant details about the workshop you're interested in, such as specific topics or activities you'd like to focus on.
* **Workshop Location 1** (`workshop_location_1`, text, required: false): Please enter the name of the workshop location you're interested in.
* **Workshop Date 1** (`workshop_date_1`, date, required: false): Select the date you'd like to attend the workshop.
* **Workshop Time 1** (`workshop_time_1`, time, required: false): Choose the time slot you prefer for the workshop.
* **Participant Details 1** (`participant_details_1`, text, required: false): This field is available for any additional comments or requests regarding your participant's needs.
* **Participant First Name 1** (`participant_first_name_1`, text, required: false): Enter the first name of the participant.
* **Participant Last Name 1** (`participant_last_name_1`, text, required: false): Enter the last name of the participant.
* **Participant Email 1** (`participant_email_1`, email, required: false): Please enter a valid email address for the participant.
* **Participant Phone 1** (`participant_phone_1`, text, required: false): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Address 1** (`participant_address_1`, text, required: false): Provide the participant's address.
* **Participant Postal Code 1** (`participant_postal_code_1`, text, required: false): Enter the participant's postal code.
* **Workshop Language 1** (`workshop_language_1`, select_one, required: false): Choose the language in which you'd like to participate in the workshop.
* **Workshop Level 1** (`workshop_level_1`, select_multiple, required: false): Select your level of participation, such as Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **Workshop Notes 1** (`workshop_notes_1`, text, required: false): Share any additional comments or requests regarding your workshop experience.
* **Workshop Venue 1** (`workshop_venue_1`, text, required: false): If you have a preferred workshop venue, enter it here.
* **Workshop Capacity 1** (`workshop_capacity_1`, number, required: false): Select the number of participants in your group.
* **Workshop Availability 1** (`workshop_availability_1`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the time slot you're available for the workshop, such as Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you've provided all necessary information to facilitate a smooth registration process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the facilitators.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
