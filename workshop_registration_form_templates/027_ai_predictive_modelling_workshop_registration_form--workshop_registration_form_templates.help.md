# AI Predictive Modelling Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AI Predictive Modelling Workshop Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from attendees to ensure a successful and enjoyable experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the step-by-step instructions below:

1. Fill in your **Full name** in the "Full name" field. This is a required field, so make sure to enter your first and last names.
2. If you are affiliated with an **Organization**, enter the name of your company or affiliation.
3. Provide your **Email address** to receive confirmation and any necessary information about the workshop. This is a required field.
4. Choose your **Experience level with predictive modelling** from the list:
	* Beginner
	* Intermediate
	* Advanced
	* Expert
5. Select all **Programming languages** you are familiar with from the list:
	* Python
	* R
	* SQL
	* Julia
	* None (if none of the above apply)
6. Provide a short description of your **Goals for attending the workshop**.
7. Choose your **Session preference** for the workshop:
	* Morning
	* Afternoon
	* Both days
8. If you have any **Dietary restrictions**, please enter the details in the text field.
9. If you have any **Accessibility needs**, please enter the details in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your first and last name.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): Enter the name of your company or affiliation.
* **Email address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address to receive confirmation and workshop information.
* **Experience level with predictive modelling** (`experience_level`, select_one, required): Choose your level of experience with predictive modelling:
	+ Beginner
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced
	+ Expert
* **Programming languages** (`programming_languages`, select_multiple, optional): Select all programming languages you are familiar with:
	+ Python
	+ R
	+ SQL
	+ Julia
	+ None
* **Goals for attending the workshop** (`goals_for_workshop`, text, optional): Provide a short description of your goals for attending the workshop.
* **Session preference** (`session_preference`, select_one, required): Choose your session preference:
	+ Morning
	+ Afternoon
	+ Both days
* **Dietary restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, optional): Enter any dietary restrictions you may have.
* **Accessibility needs** (`accessibility_needs`, text, optional): Enter any accessibility needs you may have.
