# Worker Technical Certification Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Worker Technical Certification Registration Form is used to collect information about a worker's technical certification status and upload supporting documents. This form helps ensure that workers have the necessary technical certifications to perform their job tasks safely and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal information (Page 1)
2. Enter your contact information (Page 1)
3. (Optional) Enter any technical certification information you have received (Page 2)
4. Choose your technical certification status (Page 2)
5. Upload any supporting documents you need to submit (Page 3)
6. Submit your form for review

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Personal Information
* **worker information** (`worker_name`, `text`, required):
    Please enter your name and any other personal details you wish to share.
* **contact information** (`email`, `text`, required):
    Enter your work email and phone number so we can contact you for further instructions or clarification.

### Page 2:
* **technical certification** (`certification`, `text`, optional):
    If you have received any technical certifications, please enter a brief description of the certification and its expiration date.
* **technical certification status** (`status`, `select_multiple`, required):
    Choose the status of your technical certification:
    *   Active: I have an active technical certification.
    *   Inactive: I do not have an active technical certification.
*   Please note that having an active technical certification is required to perform certain job tasks.

### Page 3:
* **uploaded documents** (`document`, `text`, required):
    Please upload any supporting documents that you have received for your technical certification, such as a scanned copy of your certification certificate or proof of completion of a training course.

Note: You can upload multiple files or documents by clicking on the "Upload" button and following the on-screen instructions.
