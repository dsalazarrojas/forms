# Academic Innovation Sprint Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Innovation Sprint Application Form is a tool used to gather information from applicants for an academic innovation sprint. It is used to review and evaluate applications and select projects for the innovation sprint.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Provide your contact email address in the "Contact email" field.
2.  Enter your full name in the "Full name" field.
3.  Provide a "Department or affiliation" (e.g. unit or lab name).
4.  Enter a "Project title" that describes the innovation project you are applying for.
5.  Write a brief "Project summary" that describes the objectives and expected outcomes of your project.
6.  List "Team members" (if applicable) separated by commas.
7.  Describe the "Expected impact" of your project, focusing on its academic or educational impact.
8.  If needed, select one or more types of support from the "Support needed from organizers" dropdown (e.g., mentoring, funding, technical support).
9.  Enter your availability during the sprint using the "Availability during sprint" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`Full name`, text, required): Enter your full name to identify yourself as an applicant.
* **Contact email** (`Contact email`, email, required): Use your institutional email address (e.g., @u.edu) for verification purposes.
* **Department or affiliation** (`Department or affiliation`, text, required): Provide the name of the department, lab, or unit you are associated with.
* **Project title** (`Project title`, text, required): Enter the title of your innovation project.
* **Project summary** (`Project summary`, text, required): Briefly describe the objectives and expected outcomes of your project.
* **Team members** (`Team members`, text, optional): List team members separated by commas, if applicable.
* **Expected impact** (`Expected impact`, text, required): Describe the academic or educational impact of your project.
* **Support needed from organizers** (`Support needed from organizers`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more types of support needed (e.g., mentoring, funding, technical support).
* **Availability during sprint** (`Availability during sprint`, text, required): Enter your availability during the sprint using dates or days you can participate.

## Tips
- Double-check your contact email address and project title to ensure they are correct.
- Be as concise as possible in your project summary and expected impact descriptions.
- If you have any questions or need help, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
