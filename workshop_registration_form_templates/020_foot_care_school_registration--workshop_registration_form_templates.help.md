# Foot Care School Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Foot Care School Registration form is designed to collect registration information from users for a workshop focused on foot care. The form has multiple pages with various fields for collecting different types of information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please navigate through each page and enter your details in the required fields. Ensure that you provide accurate and relevant information in each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Information** (`user_info`, text, required): This is the first page of the form where you can enter your details, including any other information that might be relevant to your registration.
*   **Course Information** (`course_info`, text, required): On this page, you can select the courses you are interested in attending.
*   **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required): This page is for providing contact details, including phone numbers and email addresses.
*   **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, required): Enter your payment details on this page.
*   **Any other information** (`note`, text, required): This page is for any additional comments or details you might have.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address on this page.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number on this page.
*   **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select a date on this page.
*   **Time** (`time`, time, required): Select a time on this page.
*   **What is the purpose of your registration?** (`select_one`, select_one, required): Select one of the options to indicate the purpose of your registration.
*   **Which courses are you interested in attending?** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required): Select multiple courses you are interested in attending.
*   **Description** (`text`, text, required): Enter a description on this page.
*   **Quantity** (`integer`, number, required): Enter a quantity on this page.
*   **Price** (`decimal`, number, required): Enter a price on this page.
*   **What type of registration is this?** (`select_one`, select_one, required): Select one of the options to indicate the type of registration.

Note: This form has no required fields marked as `true`, so all the fields are optional.
