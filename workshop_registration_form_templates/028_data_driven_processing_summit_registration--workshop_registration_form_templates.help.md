# Data Driven Processing Summit Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Data Driven Processing Summit Registration Form is designed to collect necessary information from participants for the event. This form includes fields to gather details such as name, email, phone number, attendance preferences, dietary restrictions, and payment method, as well as any additional information or special requests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the registration form completely and accurately. All fields are optional, but filling out all necessary fields will help us provide a better experience for you.
2. Select your attendance preferences, including whether you prefer to attend in-person or virtually.
3. Choose your preferred payment method: online or offline.
4. Provide any additional information or special requests you may have.
5. Review your answers carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **name** (text, required): Enter your full name.
- **email** (email, required): Enter your valid email address.
- **phone** (text, optional): Provide your phone number for communication purposes.
- **terms_and_agreement** (select_one, optional): Indicate your acceptance of the terms and conditions of the summit.
    - Options: I accept the terms and conditions, I do not accept the terms and conditions
- **Attendance preferences** (select_multiple, optional): Choose your preferred attendance preference.
    - Options: Virtual, In-person, Both
- **Dietary restrictions** (select_multiple, optional): Select any dietary restrictions you may have.
    - Options: Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-free, None of the above
- **Dietary restrictions note** (text, optional): If you have any specific dietary restrictions, provide additional information here.
- **Payment method** (select_one, optional): Choose your preferred payment method.
    - Options: Online payment, Offline payment
- **Additional Information** (select_multiple, optional): Provide any additional information or special requests.
    - Options: I have any food allergies, I have any medical conditions, None of the above
- **Special requests** (text, optional): If you have any other special requests, provide them here.
- **Dietary restrictions note** (text, optional): This field is only visible if you select any dietary restrictions. Please provide any additional information about your dietary restrictions.
