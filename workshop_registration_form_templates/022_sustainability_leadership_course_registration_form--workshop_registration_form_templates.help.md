# Sustainability Leadership Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sustainability Leadership Course Registration Form is used to register for the Sustainability Leadership Course. It's a form that captures the basic contact information of the participant and their preferences for the course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your First Name.
2. Provide your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email Address.
4. Enter your Phone Number.
5. Describe your goals for the course.
6. Select the type(s) of course you would like to attend (required).
7. (Optional) Select a Start Date for the course.
8. (Optional) Select a Start Time for the course.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Please enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone**: Please enter your phone number.
* **What are your goals for this course?**: This is a space for you to describe your goals for the course. Be as specific as possible to help the course administrators tailor the course to your needs.
* **What type of course would you like to attend?**: Please select the type(s) of course that best fit your needs from the options provided.
* **Start Date (optional)**: Please select a date for which you would like to start the course. If this field is left blank, the course will default to the earliest available date.
* **Start Time (optional)**: Please select a time for which you would like to start the course. If this field is left blank, the course will default to the earliest available time.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct course type(s) that fit your needs.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* Be specific with your goals to help us tailor the course to your needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the course administrators.
