# Mini Album Class Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data for course registration, specifically for mini album classes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name**, which is your personal name.
2. Enter your **Last Name**, which is your surname.
3. Provide your **Email** address, which will be used for communication related to the course.
4. Enter your **Phone Number**, which will be used for contact purposes.
5. Enter the **Course Name**, which is the title of the mini album class you are interested in.
6. Choose the **Course Date** you plan to attend, which will be in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
7. Select the **Course Time**, which is the schedule time for the course.
8. Enter the **Course Duration**, which is the length of the course in hours.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your personal name. (Required: False)
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname. (Required: False)
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes. (Required: False)
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes. (Required: False)
* **Course Name**: Enter the title of the mini album class you are interested in. (Required: False)
* **Course Date**: Select the date you plan to attend the course, in the format MM/DD/YYYY. (Required: False)
* **Course Time**: Select the schedule time for the course. (Required: False)
* **Course Duration**: Enter the length of the course in hours. (Required: False)

## Tips

* Ensure that you have entered all required information accurately.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions, please contact us for assistance.
