# Business Continuity Planning Session Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Continuity Planning Session Registration form is designed to collect information from participants for a workshop or session focused on business continuity planning. This guide will help you understand the form's purpose and walk you through the process of completing it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Provide your participant information, including your first name, last name, email address, phone number, company name, job title, and department (if applicable).
2. Choose a preferred date and time for the session.
3. Select how long you would like to attend the session (e.g., half-day, full-day, multi-day workshop).
4. Indicate your preferred delivery format (e.g., in-person, virtual, hybrid).
5. Identify your primary continuity challenge and any relevant goals you hope to achieve from the session.
6. Indicate the key stakeholders you would like to involve in the session.
7. Describe your current status with business continuity planning.
8. Provide any special requirements or accommodations you may need.
9. Specify the expected number of participants from your team.
10. Let us know if you need pre-session materials.
11. Finally, provide any additional information or questions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Required): Enter your first name as you would like it to be displayed in the session materials.
* **Last Name** (Required): Enter your last name as you would like it to be displayed in the session materials.
* **Email Address** (Required): Enter your business email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your contact phone number that we can use to reach you.
* **Company Name** (Required): Enter your organization's name as it will be displayed in the session materials.
* **Job Title** (Required): Enter your position or title within your organization.
* **Department** (Optional): If you belong to a specific department within your organization, enter it here.
* **Preferred Session Date**: Choose a date that works best for you for the session.
* **Preferred Session Time**: Select the time slot you prefer for the session (morning, afternoon, or evening).
* **Expected Session Duration**: Choose how long you would like to attend the session (half-day, full-day, or multi-day workshop).
* **Preferred Delivery Format**: Select how you would like to receive the session materials (in-person, virtual, or hybrid).
* **Primary Continuity Challenge**: Describe your main concern or challenge related to business continuity planning.
* **Planning Goals**: Select the goals you hope to achieve from the session (e.g., develop a continuity strategy, identify critical functions, create a recovery plan).
* **Key Stakeholders to Involve**: Choose the stakeholders you would like to involve in the session (e.g., executive leadership, department heads, IT department).
* **Current Continuity Planning Status**: Indicate your current status with business continuity planning (no plan in place, plan under development, plan exists but needs update).
* **Special Requirements or Accommodations**: If you have any special needs or requirements, such as accessibility or dietary needs, enter them here.
* **Expected Number of Participants**: Specify the number of participants you expect from your team.
* **Need Pre-Session Materials**: Let us know if you would like to receive materials before the session.
* **Additional Information or Questions**: Finally, provide any additional information or questions you have.
