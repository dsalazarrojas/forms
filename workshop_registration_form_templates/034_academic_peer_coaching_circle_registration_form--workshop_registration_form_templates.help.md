# Academic Peer Coaching Circle Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Peer Coaching Circle Registration Form is designed to register participants for a peer coaching circle. This form gathers essential information from participants, including their contact details, coaching focus areas, and availability for sessions. The form is used to create a list of registered participants for the peer coaching circle.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Participant name" field. This is the full name by which you will be registered in the peer coaching circle.
2. Enter your email address in the "Contact email" field. This will be used for meeting invites and other notifications.
3. Select your department or unit in the "Department or unit" field. This will help the peer coaching circle organizers understand your affiliation.
4. In the "Coaching focus areas" field, select up to three focus areas (e.g., Teaching practice, Research mentoring, Career development, Work life balance) that you would like to focus on during the peer coaching circle.
5. Check the "Prior coaching experience" field and select whether you have any previous facilitation or coaching experience (None, Some, or Extensive).
6. Enter your availability for sessions in the "Availability for sessions" field. This is crucial for scheduling purposes.
7. If you have any accessibility needs, enter them in the "Accessibility needs" field.
8. Provide an emergency contact name and phone number in the "Emergency contact" field.
9. Check the "Consent to share contact" field and select whether you permit your contact details to be shared among participants.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant name (Participant name)**: Enter your full name for registration purposes.
* **Contact email (Contact email)**: Enter the email address you use for meeting invites and other notifications.
* **Department or unit (Department or unit)**: Select your department or affiliation for peer coaching circle organization.
* **Coaching focus areas (Coaching focus areas)**: Select up to three areas you would like to focus on during the peer coaching circle (e.g., Teaching practice, Research mentoring, Career development).
* **Prior coaching experience (Prior coaching experience)**: Check whether you have prior coaching or facilitation experience.
* **Availability for sessions (Availability for sessions)**: Enter your typical days or times available for sessions.
* **Accessibility needs (Accessibility needs)**: If you have any accessibility needs, list them here.
* **Emergency contact (Emergency contact)**: Provide an emergency contact name and phone number.
* **Consent to share contact (Consent to share contact)**: Check whether you permit your contact details to be shared among participants.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions and approval from your department or unit to participate in the peer coaching circle.
* Make sure to select accurate availability for sessions to avoid scheduling conflicts.
* If you have any accessibility needs, please inform the organizers so they can make necessary arrangements.
* Check the "Consent to share contact" field carefully, as this determines how your contact details are shared among participants.
