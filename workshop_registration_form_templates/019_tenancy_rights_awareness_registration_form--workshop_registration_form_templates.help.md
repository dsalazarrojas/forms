# tenancy_rights_awareness_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The tenancy rights awareness registration form is used to register participants for a workshop focused on tenancy rights awareness. The form collects information from participants about their details, reasons for joining, consent to updates, contact details, and signature.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your participant details in the **Participant Details** field.
2. Indicate your **Reason for Joining** by selecting from the provided options.
3. Select your **Consent to Updates** options as applicable.
4. Fill out your **Contact Details** section with your relevant contact information.
5. Enter your **Contact Number** and **Contact Email** for communication purposes.
6. Sign and date your **Participant Signature** in the designated field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details**: Enter your name, title, and other relevant details as required.
* **Reason for Joining**: Select the reason(s) why you are joining the workshop. This helps us understand your motivations and interests.
* **Consent to Updates**: Check the box(es) that indicate your consent to receive updates, news, and information about the workshop and related topics.
* **Contact Details**: Provide your contact information, including your name, company/organization, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Participant Signature**: Sign and date your signature in the designated field, indicating your consent to participate in the workshop and acknowledge the terms and conditions.

## Tips
Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your details are recorded correctly. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please do not hesitate to reach out to the workshop organizers or administrators for clarification.
