# Retail Innovation Lab Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Innovation Lab Registration Form is designed to collect information from workshop participants and organizers. This form helps us track attendance, preferences, and other relevant details to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **First Name**: Enter your first name in this field.
2. **Last Name**: Enter your last name in this field.
3. **Email**: Enter your email address in this format: `username@example.com`.
4. **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
5. **Role**: Select your role in the workshop (Yes/No).
6. **If Other Specify**: If you selected "No" for Role, please specify your role in this field.
7. **Workshop Date**: Enter the date of the workshop you are attending.
8. **Workshop Time**: Enter the time of the workshop you are attending.
9. **Workshop Duration**: Enter the duration of the workshop (optional).
10. **Number of Participants**: Enter the number of participants in your group.
11. **Innovation Lab Participants**: Enter the number of innovation lab participants in your group.
12. **Workshop Location**: Select the location of the workshop (Yes/No).
13. **Workshop Facilitators**: Select the facilitators of the workshop (Yes/No).
14. **Workshop Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the workshop.
15. **Workshop Agreement**: Enter any agreements or terms related to the workshop (optional).
16. **Email Agreement**: Select whether you agree to receive emails from us (Yes/No).
17. **If Other Specify**: If you selected "No" for Email Agreement, please specify your email agreement in this field.
18. **Workshop Rating**: Rate your experience with the workshop (optional).
19. **Workshop Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback about the workshop.
20. **Innovation Lab Goals**: Enter your goals for the innovation lab (optional).
21. **Innovation Lab Objectives**: Enter your objectives for the innovation lab (optional).
22. **Innovation Lab Outcomes**: Enter any outcomes or results from the innovation lab (optional).
23. **Innovation Lab Participants Interests**: Enter the interests of the innovation lab participants (optional).
24. **Innovation Lab Resources**: Enter any resources used in the innovation lab (optional).
25. **Innovation Lab Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the innovation lab.
