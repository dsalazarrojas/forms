# Workshop Participant Access Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Workshop Participant Access Form is a tool used to grant access to a workshop for its participants. The form is used to collect and record the details of workshop participants, including their name, contact information, and status. This form helps workshop organizers to efficiently manage participant registrations and track participant status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "Workshop Participant Access Form" link to open the form.
2.  Fill in the required fields with the relevant information:
    *   **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant, as provided by the participant or their representative.
    *   **Participant Email**: Enter the email address of the participant, which will be used for communication purposes.
    *   **Workshop Title**: Enter the title of the workshop the participant is joining.
3.  Fill in the optional fields with relevant information:
    *   **Participant Phone**: Enter the phone number of the participant, which can be used for further communication.
    *   **Participant Status**: Choose the status of the participant from the dropdown menu: Pending, Approved, Rejected, or Completed.
    *   **Workshop Date**: Enter the date of the workshop the participant is joining.
    *   **Participant Time**: Enter the time of the participant's workshop participation.
    *   **Workshop Participants**: Choose the role of the participant in the workshop: Admin, Leader, or Member.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Details**: This field is not explicitly used in the form, but it is likely intended to be a brief description or notes field for the participant.
*   **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant.
*   **Participant Email**: Enter the email address of the participant.
*   **Participant Phone**: Enter the phone number of the participant.
*   **Workshop Title**: Enter the title of the workshop the participant is joining.
*   **Participant Status**: Choose the status of the participant from the dropdown menu: Pending, Approved, Rejected, or Completed.
*   **Workshop Date**: Enter the date of the workshop the participant is joining.
*   **Participant Time**: Enter the time of the participant's workshop participation.
*   **Workshop Participants**: Choose the role of the participant in the workshop: Admin, Leader, or Member.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill in all required fields with accurate information.
*   Double-check the participant status before submitting the form.
*   Enter the workshop date and time accurately to avoid conflicts.
*   Choose the correct role for the participant in the workshop dropdown menu.
