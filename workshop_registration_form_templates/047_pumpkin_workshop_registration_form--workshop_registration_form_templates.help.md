# Pumpkin Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect details from users regarding their interest in attending the Pumpkin Workshop. It is essential to fill this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the correct information is captured.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields with your accurate information.
2. Make sure to choose the correct option for the select_one and select_multiple fields carefully.
3. Review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Details** (`workshop_registration_form`, text): Enter a brief description of your interest in the workshop.
* **Name** (`name`, text): Enter your full name as you would like it to appear on the workshop list.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address, which will be used for communication regarding the workshop.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Time** (`time`, time): Select your available time slot for the workshop.
* **Date** (`date`, date): Select your preferred date for the workshop.
* **Note** (`note`, note): Add any additional information or comments about your interest in the workshop.
* **Select Workshop** (`select_one_workshop`, select_one): Choose 'Yes' if you are interested in the workshop.
* **Select Multiple Workshops** (`select_multiple_workshops`, select_multiple): Choose 'Yes' if you would like to attend multiple workshops.
* **Submission** (`workshop_registration_form_submission`, text): Add any additional comments or information regarding your submission.
* **Submit** (`workshop_registration_form_submit`, text): Confirm your registration by submitting this form.
* **Select Workshop Submission** (`select_one_workshop_submission`, select_one): Choose 'Yes' if you are ready to submit your workshop registration.
* **Confirm Registration**: Note that this field is not a field in the actual form but is mentioned here for completeness and to ensure clarity in the guide. 

## Tips
- Please make sure to enter accurate and complete information in all fields.
- Double-check your entries before submitting the form.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out for clarification.
- The completion of this form is essential for your registration in the Pumpkin Workshop.
