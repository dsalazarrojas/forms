# Women's Empowerment Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from participants for the Women's Empowerment Workshop. It will help us better understand who is attending, their contact details, event preferences, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the "Participant Details" section.
2. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Info" section.
3. Describe the event you are registering for in the "Event Details" section.
4. Confirm your registration information in the "Registration Info" section.
5. Review and agree to our payment terms if you are registering with payment options.
6. If applicable, provide information about your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact" section.
7. Finally, review and submit your form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

• **Participant Details** (Participant Details, text, required):
The first section collects your personal details such as name, email, and any other information you want to share with us.

• **Contact Info** (Contact Info, text, required):
This section collects your contact details such as phone number and address so we can get in touch with you.

• **Event Details** (Event Details, text, required):
Here, you can describe the event you are registering for in as much detail as possible.

• **Registration Info** (Registration Info, text, required):
Please confirm your registration by providing your registration information, including your name and event details.

• **Payment Terms** (Payment Terms, text, required):
If you are registering with payment options, please review and agree to our payment terms before proceeding.

• **Participant Preferences** (Participant Preferences, text, optional):
If you have any special preferences or requirements, please let us know in this section.

• **Emergency Contact** (Emergency Contact, text, optional):
Provide information about your emergency contact, if applicable.

• **Submit and Review** (Submit and Review, text, required):
Finally, review your form and submit it for review.
