# Veterinary Imaging Technology Seminar Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Imaging Technology Seminar Registration form is designed to gather information from veterinary professionals who want to attend the seminar. It helps the event organizers to tailor the session to their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your details:
	* **Name**: Enter your name in this field.
	* **Email**: Enter your email address, which will be used to communicate with you about the seminar.
	* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, which will be used to verify your registration.
2. Choose your session preferences:
	* **Session Preferences**: Select "Yes" if you would like to attend the session, or "No" if you are not interested.
3. Choose your provider preferences:
	* **Clinic or Training Provider**: Select "Yes" if you are representing a clinic or training provider, or "No" if you are not.
4. Consent to attend:
	* **Attendee Consent**: Read and agree to the terms of attendance by selecting "Yes" or "No".
5. Review and submit:
	* Review all the fields to ensure you have filled in all the required information.
	* Click "Submit" to submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (text)**: This field is for your name.
	+ Required: No
* **Email (email)**: This field is for your email address, which will be used to contact you about the seminar.
	+ Required: No
* **Phone (text)**: This field is for your phone number, which will be used to verify your registration.
	+ Required: No
* **Session Preferences (select_multiple)**: This field allows you to select whether you would like to attend the session or not.
	+ Options: "Yes", "No"
	+ Required: No
* **Session Preferences 2 (select_multiple)**: This field is similar to the previous one, allowing you to select whether you would like to receive more information about the session or not.
	+ Options: "Yes", "No"
	+ Required: No
* **Attendee Consent (note)**: This field is to confirm that you agree to attend the session.
	+ Options: "Yes", "No"
	+ Required: No
* **Clinic or Training Provider (select_one)**: This field is to let us know if you are representing a clinic or training provider.
	+ Options: "Yes", "No"
	+ Required: No
* **Session Preferences 1 (select_multiple)**: This field is similar to the previous one, allowing you to select whether you would like to attend the session or not.
	+ Options: "Yes", "No"
	+ Required: No

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields.
* Review all the fields before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or issues with the form, contact the event organizers.
