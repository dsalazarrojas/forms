# Creative Program Participation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals wishing to participate in a creative program. Please fill out this form to share your interest and preferences for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit your request, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all necessary information ready before starting the form.
* Review each field carefully to ensure you provide the correct information.
* If you need help or clarification on any field, please contact us at form@gic.mx.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Your name is required to be entered here. Please type your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email**: Your email address is required for communication purposes. Please enter a valid email address you own or control.
* **Program Type**: This is where you specify the type of program you wish to participate in. Choose from the available options provided (Creative, Other, Art, Education, or Entertainment).
* **Program Details**: Here, describe briefly the type of program you are interested in, such as specific projects, themes, or requirements.
* **Date**: Please select the date you wish to participate in the program.
* **Time**: Select the time you are available to participate in the program.
* **Location**: Enter the location where you wish to participate in the program.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of your expectations and goals for the program.
* **Submit**: Once you have filled out all the required fields, click this button to submit your request.
* **Cancel**: If you need to stop the form before submission, click this button to clear your entries. 

Note: This form is designed to be completed online, and it is recommended to review each field carefully before submitting. If you need help or clarification, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
