# effective_questioning_workshop_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Effective Questioning Workshop Registration" form is designed to register participants for workshops, collect their information, and manage payment details. This form is used to organize and track workshop registrations, ensuring that participants are accurately accounted for and payment methods are recorded.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you have all the required participant information, including name, email, phone number, and participant role.
2. Fill in the participant's name, email address, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
3. Select the participant's role and attendance status from the dropdown menus.
4. Provide any additional participant notes, if necessary.
5. Enter the workshop date, time, and name in the respective fields.
6. Choose the payment method and status from the dropdown menu.
7. Fill in the payment terms and amount, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for collecting general information about the participant. Enter the participant's name, email address, and phone number here.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the workshop.
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of the workshop.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the participant's name.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes about the participant, if necessary.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `text`, required/optional): Select the payment method used by the participant.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any payment terms or conditions, if applicable.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the participant's payment status from the dropdown menu.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the payment amount, if applicable.
* **Participant ID** (`participant_id`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a unique participant ID, if necessary.
* **Workshop Name** (`workshop_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the workshop.
* **Workshop Start Date** (`workshop_start_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the start date of the workshop.
* **Workshop End Date** (`workshop_end_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the end date of the workshop.
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of the workshop.
* **Workshop Address** (`workshop_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the address of the workshop location.
* **Workshop Notes** (`workshop_notes`, `note`, required/optional): Add any notes about the workshop.
* **Participant Status** (`participant_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the participant's status from the dropdown menu.
* **Participant Role** (`participant_role`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the participant's role from the dropdown menu.
* **Participant Attendance** (`participant_attendance`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose the participant's attendance status from the dropdown menu.
