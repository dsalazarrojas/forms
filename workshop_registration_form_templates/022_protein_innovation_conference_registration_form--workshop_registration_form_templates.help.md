# Protein Innovation Conference Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Protein Innovation Conference Registration Form is a crucial tool for registering attendees for the Protein Innovation Conference. This form helps us collect essential information about each attendee, ensuring that they receive accurate and relevant information about the conference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields marked as "required" in the form.
2. Be accurate and concise with your responses.
3. Make sure to review your form before submitting it to ensure it's complete and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Name** (Text, Required): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Company Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the company you are representing, or the name of your institution.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Job Title** (Text, Required): Enter your job title or role.
* **Company** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of your company or institution.
* **Role** (Text, Required): Enter your role in the company or institution.
* **Message** (Note, Optional): This is an optional field where you can include any additional information about yourself or your company that you'd like to share.
* **Agree to Terms** (Select One, Required): Select "Yes" if you agree to the terms and conditions of the conference. 

## Tips
- Please ensure that you fill out the form accurately and completely.
- If you have any issues or questions while filling the form, do not hesitate to contact us.
- The information you provide will be kept confidential and used only for the purposes of the conference.
