# Health Coaching Certification Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from participants who are registering for the Health Coaching Certification Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the participant details, including their name and any other relevant information.
2.  Select the preferred date for the workshop.
3.  Agree to the terms and conditions by selecting one of the options.
4.  Enter your participant email address.
5.  Enter your participant phone number.
6.  Choose your workshop preferences from the provided options.
7.  Add any comments or remarks about the workshop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Details**: This field is used to collect basic participant information such as their name. 
*   **Preferred Date**: Please select the date for which you would prefer to attend the workshop. If you have a particular date in mind, please let us know and we will do our best to accommodate your request.
*   **Agreement**: By selecting one of the options, you are agreeing to the terms and conditions of the workshop. Please take a moment to review the terms and conditions before making a selection.
*   **Participant Email**: This field is used to collect your participant's email address. Please ensure that the email address is valid and up-to-date.
*   **Participant Phone**: This field is used to collect your participant's phone number. Please enter your phone number in the format (XXX) XXX-XXXX.
*   **Workshop Agreement**: Select one of the provided options to agree to the workshop terms and conditions. Please read the terms and conditions carefully before making a selection.
*   **Workshop Preferences**: Please choose your workshop preferences from the provided options. This will help us tailor the workshop to your needs.
*   **Comments**: If you have any comments or suggestions about the workshop, please add them here. This will help us improve the workshop for future participants.

## Tips

*   Before submitting the form, please make sure you have carefully reviewed the information you have provided.
*   If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact us and we will be happy to assist you.
*   Please note that we will do our best to accommodate your preferred date, but we reserve the right to make changes to the schedule as needed.
