# Team Leader Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from team leaders who want to participate in the Team Leader Training program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Registration Information page with your details.
2. Proceed to the Participant Information page and fill in the details of the team leader(s) participating in the training.
3. On the Team Details page, provide a brief description of your team.
4. Fill in the Session details on the Session page.
5. Finally, enter any Additional Information you would like to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Information (<name>, <type>: text, required)**: This page is where you will enter your registration details. Please fill in your name and any other contact information you would like to provide.
* **Participant Information (<name>, <type>: text, required)**: On this page, please enter the details of the team leader(s) participating in the training. This includes their name, email, and other relevant information.
* **Team Details (<name>, <type>: text, required)**: Here, you will provide a brief description of your team, including its name and members.
* **Session (<name>, <type>: text, required)**: Please select the session you would like to attend from the available options.
* **Additional Information (<name>, <type>: text, optional)**: If there is any additional information you would like to provide or any specific requirements you have, please enter it here.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all the required fields accurately.
- Double-check the session details to ensure you have selected the correct one.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the training organizers.
- Please review your submission before submitting, as it will be used to tailor the training experience to your needs.
