# Human Centered Learning Design Fellowship Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals interested in participating in the Human Centered Learning Design Fellowship program. It collects information about your personal details, fellowship preferences, and background information to help us understand your profile and ensure a smooth program execution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, such as your First Name and Last Name.
2. Enter your Email address, which will be used to communicate with you throughout the program.
3. Provide your Phone number for any further contact or communication.
4. Choose your organization and Position for your current work or studies.
5. Write a brief Bio that describes a little about yourself.
6. Select the type of fellowship you're interested in (Fellowship Type).
7. Choose your interests (Interests) that align with the program's focus.
8. Enter the date (Date) and time (Time) you submitted the form.
9. Select who submitted the form (Submitted By).
10. Enter the submitted date (Submitted Date) and time (Submitted Time).
11. Enter the device you used to submit the form (Submitted Device).
12. Enter any additional information about the form ID (Form ID).
13. Enter the category for your submission (Category).
14. Briefly describe your submission (Description).
15. Finally, upload or enter the Output File (Output File).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you use for official communications.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for any further contact or communication.
* **Organization**: Enter the name of your current work or study organization.
* **Position**: Enter your current work or study position.
* **Bio**: Write a brief description about yourself, including your background and interests.
* **Fellowship Type**: Choose the type of fellowship that interests you.
* **Interests**: Select the interests that align with the program's focus.
* **Date**: Enter the date you submitted the form.
* **Time**: Enter the time you submitted the form.
* **Submitted By**: Select who submitted the form.
* **Submitted Date**: Enter the date you submitted the form.
* **Submitted Time**: Enter the time you submitted the form.
* **Submitted Device**: Enter the device you used to submit the form.
* **Form ID**: Enter any additional information about the form ID.
* **Category**: Enter the category for your submission.
* **Description**: Briefly describe your submission.
* **Output File**: Upload or enter the output file related to your submission.
