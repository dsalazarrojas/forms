# Retail Inventory Forecasting Webinar Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information for the Retail Inventory Forecasting Webinar. Please complete the form accurately to ensure that you receive the necessary materials and have a great experience during the webinar.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your personal details: first name, last name, and email address.
2. If you are registering on behalf of your company, provide the company name.
3. Select your job title and department from the provided options.
4. Confirm your role in the company.
5. Provide the necessary information about your manager, if applicable.
6. Select your job function and business unit from the provided options.
7. Fill in any additional job titles that are relevant to your role.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your official identification.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your official identification.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address that you use to receive notifications and communications.
- **Company** (`company`, `text`, required): Enter your company name as it is officially registered.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Select your primary job title.
- **Department** (`department`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the department(s) you are associated with.
- **Job Function** (`job_function`, `select_one`, required): Select your job function.
- **Business Unit** (`business_unit`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your business unit(s).
- **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, required): Enter your manager's name.
- **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, `text`, required): Enter your manager's email address.
- **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your manager's phone number.
- **Job Description** (`job_description`, `note`, required): Briefly describe your job responsibilities.
- **Manager Title** (`manager_title`, `text`, required): Enter your manager's title.
- **Manager Department** (`manager_department`, `text`, required): Enter your manager's department.
- **Manager Business Unit** (`manager_business_unit`, `text`, required): Enter your manager's business unit.
- **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter your company name.
- **Role** (`role`, `select_one`, required): Select your role in the company.
- **Job Title 2** (`job_title2`, `text`, required): Select any secondary job title.
- **Job Title 3** (`job_title3`, `text`, required): Select any additional job title.
