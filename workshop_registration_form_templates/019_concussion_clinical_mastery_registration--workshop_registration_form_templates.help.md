# Concussion Clinical Mastery Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Concussion Clinical Mastery Registration form, explaining each field and its importance in the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Participant Name** in the text field. This will be how your name appears on the certificate and other materials related to the workshop.
2. If applicable, enter your **Professional Title**. This field is optional and allows you to specify your professional title (e.g., MD, PT, AT, RN, etc.).
3. Enter your **Organization or Employer** if you are representing an institution or private practice. This field is also optional.
4. Provide your **Email Address**. This is necessary for registration confirmation and to receive materials related to the workshop.
5. Enter your **Professional License Number** if you have one. This is optional and used for CE tracking purposes.
6. Select the **Session Selection** you wish to attend from the provided options. You can choose one or multiple sessions.
7. If applicable, specify your **Dietary Requirements** for catered events.
8. Choose your **Payment Status** from the options provided:
	* **Paid**: Indicates that you have already paid for the workshop.
	* **Pending**: Indicates that you have not yet paid or are awaiting payment confirmation.
	* **Invoice Required**: Suggests that you need an invoice or further payment instructions.
9. If needed, describe any **Special Accommodations** you require for the workshop, such as accessibility or learning accommodations.
10. Read and agree to the **Consent and Terms** statement, which outlines how your data will be used and the policies related to the workshop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Your full name as it should appear on the certificate.
* **Professional Title** (`professional_title`, text, optional): Your professional title, such as MD, PT, AT, RN, etc.
* **Organization or Employer** (`organization`, text, optional): The name of your institution or private practice.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Your email address for registration confirmation and materials.
* **Professional License Number** (`license_number`, text, optional): Your professional license number for CE tracking.
* **Session Selection** (`session_selection`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or multiple sessions to attend from the provided options (Core clinical mastery, Advanced diagnostics, Return to play protocols, Case review lab).
* **Dietary Requirements** (`dietary_requirements`, text, optional): Specify any dietary requirements for catered events.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, required): Choose your payment status (Paid, Pending, Invoice required).
* **Special Accommodations** (`special_accommodations`, text, optional): Describe any special accommodations you require for the workshop.
* **Consent and Terms** (`consent_terms`, note, required): Read and agree to the statement outlining data use and policies.

## Tips

* Make sure to select the correct sessions you plan to attend to ensure you receive the relevant materials.
* If you have any dietary requirements, please specify them so we can arrange for the necessary accommodations.
* Ensure you choose the correct payment status to avoid any delays or misunderstandings.
* Review the consent and terms statement carefully before agreeing to it.
