# Job Readiness Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for a job readiness workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To register for the workshop, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure that you are eligible to register for the workshop.
3. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Name
* **Participant Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID documents (required).
### Email
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address (required).
### Phone
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where we can reach you (optional).
### Job Readiness Score
* **Job Readiness Score**: Enter your job readiness score (optional).
### Job Title
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title as it appears on your ID documents (required).
### Job Description
* **Job Description**: Enter a brief description of your job (required).
### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy (required).
### Participant Photo
* **Participant Photo**: You may upload a photo of yourself (optional).
### Workshop Type
* **Workshop Type**: Select the workshop type you are registering for (required).
### Workshop Date
* **Workshop Date**: Select the date you plan to attend the workshop (required).
### Participant Details
* **Participant Details**: Select the participant details that apply to you (optional).
### Participant Status
* **Participant Status**: Select your participant status (required).
### Participant Notes
* **Participant Notes**: Enter any additional notes about your registration (optional).

## Tips
* Be careful when filling out the form, as any mistakes may affect your registration.
* If you have any issues while filling out the form, contact us for assistance.
* Ensure that you have the necessary information ready before submitting the form.
