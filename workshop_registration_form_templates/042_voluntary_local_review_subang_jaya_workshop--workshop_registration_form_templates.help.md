# Voluntary Local Review Subang Jaya Workshop - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture relevant information about a local workshop held in Subang Jaya. It will help us understand the details of the workshop, such as its name, date, and time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the workshop name in the "Workshop Name" field. This is the title of the workshop that you are registering.
2. Enter the date of the workshop in the "Date Of Workshop" field in the correct format (e.g., MM/DD/YY or DD MMMM YYYY).
3. Select or enter the time of the workshop in the "Time Of Workshop" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Name** (`workshop_name`, text, optional): Enter the title of the workshop you are registering.
* **Date Of Workshop** (`date_of_workshop`, date, optional): Enter the date of the workshop in the correct format (e.g., MM/DD/YY or DD MMMM YYYY).
* **Time Of Workshop** (`time_of_workshop`, time, optional): Select or enter the time of the workshop.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in the fields accurately to ensure that we have the correct information about the workshop.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for clarification.
* This form is designed to be completed by one person per workshop, so please do not fill in the form more than once.
