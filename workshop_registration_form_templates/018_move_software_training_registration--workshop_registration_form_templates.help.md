# MOVE Software Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register users for a software training session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Ensure you have not already registered for the same session.
* Fill in your contact information in the "User Information" page.
* Select your choice of session in the "Session Choice" page.
* Indicate if you have any additional training session in the "Training Session" page.
* Enter any specific goals for the training in the "Goals" page.
* Enter your contact email address in the "Email" page.
* Enter your phone number in the "Phone" page.
* Select the date and time of your preferred training session in the "Date" and "Time" pages.
* Leave any notes for the training team in the "Notes" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information (text)**: Enter your name and any other relevant information that will help us contact you.
* **Session Choice (select one)**: Choose the session you are interested in registering for. Please select 'Yes' if you would like to register for this session.
* **Training Session (select multiple)**: Indicate if you have any additional training sessions you would like to attend. Select 'Yes' for each session you would like to register for.
* **Goals (text)**: Enter any specific goals or objectives you have for the training. This will help us tailor the training to your needs.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about the training session.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number so we can reach you about any last-minute updates.
* **Date (date)**: Select the date of your preferred training session.
* **Time (time)**: Select the time of your preferred training session.
* **Notes (note)**: Leave any notes or comments about your registration. This is an optional field, but it's helpful for us to know about any special needs or requirements you may have.

## Tips
* Please make sure to select the correct email address and phone number to ensure we can contact you about the training session.
* If you have any special needs or requirements, please leave a note in the "Notes" field so we can take them into account.
