# Multilingual Instruction Research Group Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Multilingual Instruction Research Group Registration Form is designed to collect information about research groups that conduct multilingual instruction research. This form helps administrators and researchers to track and manage the registration of these groups.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Research Group Name** field with the name of your research group.
2. Enter your **Name** in the corresponding field.
3. Provide your **Email** address to ensure that you can be contacted for any further information or updates.
4. Enter your **Phone** number to facilitate communication.
5. Describe your **Research Group Description** in a few sentences, explaining the main goals and objectives of your research group.
6. Select the **Research Group Type** that best describes your group's focus (Multilingual, Bilingual, or Monolingual).
7. Choose the **Research Group Language**(s) that your group operates in.
8. Select the **Research Group Leaders** who are responsible for your group's activities.
9. Enter the **Research Group Start Date** and **Research Group End Date**, if applicable.
10. Select who **Form Submitted By** (Self, Researcher, or Admin).
11. Optionally, enter any additional comments about your research group in the **Research Group Additional Comments** field.
12. Enter any **Research Group Comments** in the **Research Group Comments** field.
13. Finally, select the **Research Group Status** (Active, Inactive, or Pending) that best describes your group's current status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Research Group Name** (`research_group_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your research group.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Provide your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for communication.
* **Research Group Description** (`research_group_description`, text, required: false): Describe your research group's main goals and objectives.
* **Research Group Type** (`research_group_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of your research group (Multilingual, Bilingual, or Monolingual).
* **Research Group Language** (`research_group_language`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the languages your research group operates in (Spanish, English, French, Mandarin, Arabic, Russian, Japanese, German, Portuguese, Italian).
* **Research Group Leaders** (`research_group_leaders`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the leaders of your research group (Lead Researcher, Co-Researcher, Team Member).
* **Research Group Start Date** (`research_group_start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the start date of your research group.
* **Research Group End Date** (`research_group_end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the end date of your research group.
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, select_one, required: false): Select who submitted the form (Self, Researcher, Admin).
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when you submitted the form.
* **Research Group Additional Comments** (`research_group_additional_comments`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments about your research group.
* **Research Group Comments** (`research_group_comments`, note, required: false): Enter any comments about your research group.
* **Research Group Status** (`research_group_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the status of your research group (Active, Inactive, Pending).
