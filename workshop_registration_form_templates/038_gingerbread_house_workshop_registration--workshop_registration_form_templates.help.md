# Gingerbread House Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering to attend the Gingerbread House Workshop. Please fill out the form to provide your registration information, payment details, and confirm your attendance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the registration form with your name, email, phone number, and any additional note if needed.
2. Select your preferred workshop schedule from the options provided.
3. Choose your preferred payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
4. Enter the payment amount.
5. Confirm that you agree to the Terms and Conditions.
6. Review and confirm your registration details, including email and phone contact information.
7. Indicate whether you have been sent email or phone notifications.
8. Enter the dates of email and phone notifications sent, if any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form** (`text`, `required`: false): Fill in your attendee's name.
* **Attendee Information** (`text`, `required`: false): Provide any additional information about your attendance.
* **Workshop Schedule** (`select_one`, `required`: false): Choose the date you would like to attend the workshop.
* **Payment Info** (`text`, `required`: false): Enter your payment details.
* **Payment Method** (`select_one`, `required`: true): Select your preferred payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
* **Payment Amount** (`number`, `required`: false): Enter the payment amount.
* **Attendee Name** (`text`, `required`: false): Fill in your attendee's name again for confirmation.
* **Email** (`email`, `required`: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`text`, `required`: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Note** (`note`, `required`: false): Provide any additional note about your attendance.
* **Email Confirmation** (`email`, `required`: false): Confirm your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Confirmation** (`text`, `required`: false): Confirm your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Note Confirmation** (`note`, `required`: false): Confirm any additional note about your attendance.
* **Payment Confirmation** (`note`, `required`: false): Confirm your payment details.
* **Terms & Conditions** (`select_multiple`, `required`: false): Confirm that you agree to the Terms and Conditions.
* **Attendee Info Confirmation** (`text`, `required`: false): Confirm your attendee's information.
* **Registration Confirmation** (`text`, `required`: false): Confirm your registration details.
* **Workshop Registration Number** (`number`, `required`: false): Enter a unique number for your registration.
* **Email Sent** (`select_multiple`, `required`: false): Indicate whether email notifications have been sent.
* **Phone Sent** (`select_multiple`, `required`: false): Indicate whether phone notifications have been sent.
* **Note Sent** (`select_multiple`, `required`: false): Indicate whether note notifications have been sent.
* **Payment Method Sent** (`select_multiple`, `required`: false): Indicate whether payment method notifications have been sent.
* **Email Sent Date** (`date`, `required`: false): Enter the date email notifications were sent.
* **Phone Sent Date** (`date`, `required`: false): Enter the date phone notifications were sent.
* **Note Sent Date** (`date`, `required`: false): Enter the date note notifications were sent.

Note: Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately to ensure smooth registration and payment processing.
