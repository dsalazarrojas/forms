# Scholarship Workshop Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from workshop participants about their experience at the scholarship workshop. Your feedback is crucial in helping us improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and email address in the designated fields.
2. Provide your rating of the workshop experience out of 5 stars.
3. Answer the question about whether you selected to attend or not.
4. Select the dates and times you attended the workshop from the list provided.
5. Write any comments or suggestions about the workshop in the 'Workshop Comments' and 'Workshop Suggestions' fields.
6. Confirm your attendance at the workshop by indicating the correct location and time.
7. Click the 'Submit' button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required: false): This field is for you to provide a title for your feedback. You can choose a title that best describes your experience.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your name so that we can address you in our response.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Provide your email address so that we can contact you if needed.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required: false): Rate your overall experience of the workshop out of 5 stars. Be honest, as your feedback is valuable to us.
* **Select** (`select`, select_one, required: true): Choose whether you selected to attend the workshop or not. This will help us confirm your attendance.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required: true): Choose all dates and times you attended the workshop. This will help us track your attendance accurately.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Select the date you attended the workshop. This will help us keep a record of attendance.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Choose the time you attended the workshop. This will help us track the timing of your attendance.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Write any comments or thoughts about your experience with the workshop.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number so that we can contact you if needed.
* **Workshop Name** (`workshop_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the workshop you attended.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, required: false): Select the date you attended the workshop.
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, time, required: false): Choose the time you attended the workshop.
* **Workshop Location** (`workshop_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location where the workshop took place.
* **Workshop Attendance** (`workshop_attendance`, text, required: false): Confirm your attendance at the workshop.
* **Workshop Comments** (`workshop_comments`, note, required: false): Write any comments or suggestions about the workshop.
* **Workshop Suggestions** (`workshop_suggestions`, note, required: false): Provide any suggestions you have for future workshops.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required: false): Click this button to send your feedback.
