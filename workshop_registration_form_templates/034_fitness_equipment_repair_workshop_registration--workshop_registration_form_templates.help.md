# Fitness Equipment Repair Workshop Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from workshop attendees. Please fill it out completely to facilitate a smooth and successful workshop experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To register for the Fitness Equipment Repair Workshop, follow these steps:

### Step 1: Fill out the Workshop Registration Page

* **Workshop registration**: Enter a brief description or name for your registration (this field is not mandatory but recommended for internal tracking purposes).
* **Session Preferences**: Select the session preference that suits your needs (e.g., yes or no).
* **Repair Shop**: Choose the correct repair shop from the provided options (gym, repair shop, or training center).

### Step 2: Provide Attendee Details

* **Attendee details**: Enter a brief description or name of the person attending the workshop.
* **Email**: Enter the attendee's email address to facilitate communication.
* **Phone**: Enter the attendee's phone number (if applicable).
* **Date**: Choose or enter a date for the workshop (if you need to attend on a specific date).
* **Session time**: If applicable, choose a specific session time (not required).
* **Session date**: If you need to attend on a specific date for this workshop (not required).

### Step 3: Additional Information

* **Equipment issues**: Describe any equipment-related issues you'd like to address or discuss during the workshop.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or questions you have for the workshop facilitators.
* **Confirm email**: Confirm that the email address provided is accurate.
* **Confirm phone**: Confirm that the phone number provided is accurate.
* **Gym equipment**: Indicate if you are bringing gym equipment for repair or discussion during the workshop.
* **Repair equipment**: Indicate if you are bringing repair equipment for demonstration or discussion during the workshop.
* **Training equipment**: Indicate if you are bringing training equipment for demonstration or discussion during the workshop.

## Tips

- Make sure to double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the workshop facilitators for clarification.
- Ensure you have the necessary permissions and arrangements made before attending the workshop with equipment.
