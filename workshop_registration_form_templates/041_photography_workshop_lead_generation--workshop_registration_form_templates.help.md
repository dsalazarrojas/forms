# Photography Workshop Lead Generation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help generate leads for photography workshops. It's meant to collect information from potential attendees about their interests, preferences, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Are you interested in our workshops?" to indicate your interest level.
2. Enter the name of the workshop you're referring to, if applicable.
3. Choose the date of the workshop.
4. Select the time of the workshop.
5. Enter your email address.
6. Provide your phone number.
7. Enter the location of the workshop.
8. Type in the workshop venue (if applicable).
9. Describe your workshop in detail, including any specific features or requirements.
10. Choose your level of interest in the workshop (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
11. Provide the price of the workshop.
12. Enter the capacity of the workshop.
13. Set the duration of the workshop.
14. Choose how often the workshop will be offered (Once, Repeating, or Monthly).
15. Select the status of the workshop (Upcoming, Ongoing, or Closed).
16. If the workshop is Closed, select the reason for the status (Full, Cancelled, Postponed, or Other).
17. Choose the lead status (New, Follow-up, Converted, or Converted and Rescheduled).
18. Enter the lead ID (if applicable).
19. Specify the source of the lead (Website, Social Media, Referral, or Other).
20. If Other, enter a brief description of the lead source.
21. Optionally, add any additional comments about the lead.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Are you interested in our workshops?** (`workshop_interest`): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate your level of interest in the workshop.
* **Workshop Name** (`workshop_name`): Enter the name of the workshop (if applicable).
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`): Choose the date of the workshop (format MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`): Select the time of the workshop (format HH:MM).
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Provide your phone number for further communication.
* **Workshop Location** (`workshop_location`): Enter the location of the workshop.
* **Workshop Venue** (`workshop_venue`): Type in the workshop venue (if applicable).
* **Workshop Details** (`workshop_details`): Describe your workshop in detail, including any specific features or requirements.
* **What level of interest do you have?** (`workshop_interest_level`): Choose your level of interest (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
* **Workshop Price** (`workshop_price`): Enter the price of the workshop.
* **Workshop Capacity** (`workshop_capacity`): Set the capacity of the workshop.
* **Workshop Duration** (`workshop_duration`): Set the duration of the workshop.
* **How often will this workshop be offered?** (`workshop_frequency`): Choose how often the workshop will be offered (Once, Repeating, or Monthly).
* **Status of Workshop** (`workshop_status`): Select the status of the workshop (Upcoming, Ongoing, or Closed).
* **Please select the reason of the workshop status** (`workshop_status_reason`): If the workshop is Closed, select the reason (Full, Cancelled, Postponed, or Other).
* **Lead Status** (`lead_status`): Choose the lead status (New, Follow-up, Converted, or Converted and Rescheduled).
* **Lead ID** (`workshop_lead_id`): Enter the lead ID (if applicable).
* **Lead Source** (`workshop_lead_source`): Specify the source of the lead (Website, Social Media, Referral, or Other).
* **Other (please specify)** (`workshop_lead_source_other`): If Other, enter a brief description of the lead source.
* **Workshop Lead Comment** (`workshop_lead_comment`): Optionally, add any additional comments about the lead.
