# Memoir Writing Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants who want to register for the Memoir Writing Workshop. By filling out this form, you will be able to reserve a spot in the workshop and provide the necessary details for the organizers to prepare for your presence.

## How To Complete This Form
To ensure that you provide the correct information, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out your **Participant Name** (Field 1: participant_name) with your real name.
2. Enter your **Participant Email** (Field 2: participant_email) address correctly.
3. Provide your **Participant Phone Number** (Field 3: participant_phone) if you want to be contacted for any reason.
4. Choose your **Writing Level** (Field 4: writing_level) from the options: Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
5. Select the sessions you prefer for the workshop, including **Morning**, **Afternoon**, and **Evening** (Field 5: session_preferences). You can choose one or multiple sessions.
6. In the **Requirements** field (Field 6: requirements), provide any additional information that the instructor should know about you.
7. In the **Instructor Bio** field (Field 7: instructor_bio), write a brief introduction about yourself and your writing experience, so the instructors can get a feel for your writing style and preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name (Field 1: participant_name)**: Your full name, as it appears on your ID or driver's license. This is the primary contact point for workshop updates and communication.
* **Participant Email (Field 2: participant_email)**: Your valid email address that we can use to send you workshop updates and instructions.
* **Participant Phone (Field 3: participant_phone)**: If you want to receive phone calls or texts from us, please provide a valid phone number. This is optional.
* **Writing Level (Field 4: writing_level)**: Select the level that best describes your writing skills and experience. This will help the instructor tailor the workshop to your needs.
* **Session Preferences (Field 5: session_preferences)**: Choose one or more sessions that you prefer to attend. You can select **Morning**, **Afternoon**, or **Evening** sessions. Please note that the workshop schedule may change, and we will inform you about any changes.
* **Requirements (Field 6: requirements)**: If you have any specific needs or requirements, please let us know here. This could be a medical condition, a writing style, or any other information that might affect your workshop experience.
* **Instructor Bio (Field 7: instructor_bio)**: Briefly introduce yourself, mentioning your writing experience and background. This will help the instructor get to know you better and provide more personalized guidance during the workshop.
