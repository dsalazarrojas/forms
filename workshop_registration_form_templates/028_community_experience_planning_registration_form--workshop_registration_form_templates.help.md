# Community Experience Planning Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering participants for a community experience planning event. Please fill it out accurately to ensure proper registration and event planning.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read each field explanation to understand its purpose.
- Fill in your personal information (First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Phone Number).
- Select your preferred Experience Type, Date, Experience Level, and Role in Community.
- If applicable, provide your Organization or Company name.
- If registering a group, select "True" for Group Registration and enter the Group Size.
- Select your preferred Payment Method.
- Optional fields are not required but can help us better understand your needs and preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (Required): Enter your legal first name.
- **Last Name** (Required): Enter your legal last name.
- **Email Address** (Required): Enter the email address we'll use for event communications.
- **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your primary contact number.
- **Organization or Company** (Optional): If you're representing an organization, enter its name.
- **Job Title** (Optional): Enter your position or role.
- **Experience Type** (Required): Select the type of experience you're registering for (e.g., Planning Workshop, Design Thinking Session).
- **Preferred Date** (Required): Select your preferred date for the experience.
- **Experience Level** (Required): Choose your level of familiarity with planning (e.g., Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
- **Your Role in Community** (Required): Select how you contribute to the community (e.g., Resident, Business Owner, Community Leader).
- **Planning Goals** (Optional): Describe what you hope to achieve during the experience.
- **Challenges Faced** (Optional): Share any obstacles you're currently facing.
- **Registering a Group** (Required): Select "True" if you're registering multiple people, "False" otherwise.
- **Group Size** (Optional): Enter the number of people in your group.
- **Accessibility Needs** (Optional): If you require accommodations, describe your needs.
- **Dietary Restrictions** (Optional): If refreshments are provided, list any dietary restrictions.
- **Payment Method** (Required): Select how you'll pay (e.g., Credit Card, Invoice, Purchase Order).
- **How Did You Hear About This** (Optional): Choose where you learned about this event.
- **Additional Comments** (Optional): Share any additional information.
