# workshop-application-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The workshop-application-form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in participating in a workshop. This form will help workshop organizers assess applicants' suitability for the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your name and any other relevant contact information.
2. Select your current employment status from the list provided.
3. Specify your expectations from the workshop.
4. Provide details about yourself as an applicant.
5. Enter your email address (required).
6. Enter your phone number (optional).
7. If you are currently employed, provide details about your current employment.
8. Specify your workshop interests (select multiple options).
