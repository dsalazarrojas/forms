# Access Bars Workshop Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who wish to register for the Access Bars Workshop. The form gathers details such as name, contact information, workshop preferences, and payment methods to facilitate smooth registration and communication processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Full Name**, which should include both first and last name.
2. Provide your **Email Address**, which will be used for workshop confirmation and reminders.
3. Enter your **Phone Number**, which should be your mobile number.
4. Select your preferred **Workshop Date** from the available dates.
5. Choose your **Experience Level** with Access Bars, as this will help us tailor the workshop experience to your needs.
6. Select your preferred **Payment Method**, such as credit card, PayPal, or cash on day.
7. If you have any dietary restrictions, please select one from the options (e.g., vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free).
8. Let us know how you **Hear About This Workshop**, so we can understand your referral source.
9. Read and agree to the **Legal and Consent** section, which includes our privacy and cancellation policies.
10. Finally, give us your **Digital Signature**, by typing your full name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name, including both first and last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address that will be used for workshop confirmation and reminders.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your mobile number, which will be used for communication purposes.
* **Workshop Date**: Select the preferred date for the workshop from the available dates.
* **Experience Level**: Choose your current level of experience with Access Bars:
	+ Beginner (First Class)
	+ Repeat Student
	+ Practitioner
	+ Facilitator
* **Payment Method**: Select how you plan to pay for the workshop:
	+ Credit Card
	+ PayPal
	+ Cash on Day
	+ Bank Transfer
* **Dietary Requirements**: Select your dietary requirements:
	+ None
	+ Vegetarian
	+ Vegan
	+ Gluten-Free
	+ Nut Allergy
* **Referral Source**: How did you hear about this workshop? (e.g., social media, friend, website)
* **Legal and Consent**: Read and agree to our privacy and cancellation policies.
* **Class Recording Consent**: Decide whether you consent to being included in class photos or videos for marketing and educational use.
* **Privacy Policy Agreement**: Agree to our privacy and cancellation policies.
* **Digital Signature**: Type your full name to digitally sign the form. 
* **Registration Date**: This field is automatically populated with today's date and is required for registration purposes.
