# HR Modernization Seminar Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a registration form for the HR Modernization Seminar. It gathers relevant information from participants, including contact details, seminar details, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the registration form (Field 1) with your name and any additional details you'd like to provide.
2. Provide your contact information (Fields 2-8) to allow us to get in touch with you.
3. Confirm your seminar details (Field 6 and Field 9-10) to ensure that we have accurate information about the seminar you are attending.
4. Specify your payment method (Field 22) and confirm your payment (Field 21) to secure your spot.
5. Confirm your attendance (Field 24) to let us know that you will be attending the seminar.
6. Add any additional notes or comments (Field 25) you'd like to share with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This is the main registration form where you can provide any additional details about yourself.
* **Contact Form** (`2`, `text`, required: false): This section gathers your contact information, including your email and phone number.
* **Seminar Details** (`3`, `text`, required: false): This field is for you to provide any additional details about the seminar you are attending.
* **Payment Details** (`4`, `text`, required: false): This section is for you to provide payment information, but it is not required.
* **Additional Information** (`5`, `text`, required: false): This field is for you to provide any additional information you'd like to share with us.
* **Confirm Seminar** (`6`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select "Yes" to confirm that you will be attending the seminar.
* **Email** (`7`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone** (`8`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your phone number so we can get in touch with you.
* **Seminar Date** (`9`, `date`, required: false): Please select the date of the seminar you will be attending.
* **Seminar Time** (`10`, `time`, required: false): Please select the time of the seminar you will be attending.
* **Company** (`11`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your company name.
* **Job Title** (`12`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your job title.
* **Role** (`13`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your role.
* **Name** (`14`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your name.
* **Department** (`15`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your department.
* **Address** (`16`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your address.
* **City** (`17`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your city.
* **State** (`18`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your state.
* **Zip** (`19`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your zip code.
* **Seminar Location** (`20`, `text`, required: false): Please provide the location of the seminar.
* **Confirm Payment** (`21`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select "Yes" to confirm that you have made payment.
* **Payment Method** (`22`, `text`, required: false): Please select your payment method.
* **Confirm Seminar Details** (`23`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select "Yes" to confirm that you have reviewed the seminar details.
* **Confirm Attendance** (`24`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select "Yes" to confirm that you will attend the seminar.
* **Notes** (`25`, `text`, required: false): Please add any additional notes or comments you'd like to share with us.
