# Virtual Card Class Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is to assist users in completing the Virtual Card Class Registration form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. On the first page, select the classes you are interested in.
2. On the subsequent pages, enter your email address, first name, last name, and any additional details about yourself.
3. Confirm your consent to participate.
4. Click the "Submit" button to finalize your registration.

		- Click this button to finalize your registration.
