# Virtual Card Class Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is to assist users in completing the Virtual Card Class Registration form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. On the first page, select the classes you are interested in.
2. On the subsequent pages, enter your email address, first name, last name, and any additional details about yourself.
3. Confirm your consent to participate.
4. Click the "Submit" button to finalize your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* Class Choices (page 1):
	+ This is where you select the classes you are interested in. You can select one or more classes.
* Registration Form (page 2):
	+ Email:
		- This field is for your email address. Please make sure it is accurate.
	+ First Name:
		- This is your first name.
	+ Last Name:
		- This is your last name.
* Registration Form (page 3):
	+ Attendee Details:
		- This is a free-text field for any additional details about yourself.
* Registration Form (page 4):
	+ Consent:
		- Please confirm that you have read and agree to the terms of participation.
* Registration Form (page 4):
	+ Submit:
		- Click this button to finalize your registration.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and complete information in the required fields.
- Select the classes you are genuinely interested in.
- Review your information carefully before submitting the form.
- If you have any issues or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
