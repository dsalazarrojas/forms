This form is intended to register users for a population health data science workshop. It collects basic contact and demographic information, such as name, email, phone number, and date of birth. Additionally, it captures more specific information like time zone and workshop attendance preferences. The form should only be filled out if the user intends to participate in the workshop.

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential details from workshop registrants, allowing us to manage attendee information and preferences effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the form:

1. Fill out the required fields marked as "required" (First Name, Email, and Time Zone).
2. Enter your preferred First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
3. Enter your valid email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
5. Select your Date of Birth in the Date of Birth field.
6. Choose your preferred Time Zone from the select options.
7. Optionally, add a Note if you have any additional comments or requests.
8. Select which workshops you would like to attend from the Workshop Attendance options.
9. Finally, indicate your agreement to participate in the workshop by selecting "I Agree".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): This is your first name, which will be used for registration and communication purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): This is your last name, which will be used for registration and communication purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): This is the email address where you will receive important workshop notifications and updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): This will help us understand your time zone preferences.
* **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, select_one, optional): Select your time zone from the options to help us plan workshop schedule accordingly.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or requests you may have.
* **Which workshop will you attend?** (`workshop_attendance`, select_multiple, optional): Choose which workshop(s) you wish to participate in.
* **I agree** (`agreement`, select_one, optional): Indicate your understanding and agreement to participate in the workshop.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are filled out to register successfully.
* Select only relevant options for workshop attendance and time zone.
* Use the Note field to provide any additional context or preferences.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
