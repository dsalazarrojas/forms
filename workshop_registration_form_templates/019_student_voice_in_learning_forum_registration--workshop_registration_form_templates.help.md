# Student Voice In Learning Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students for registration purposes. The form will help gather data on student name, email, interests, and any questions they may have for the Student Voice In Learning Forum.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you are eligible to register for the event.
2. Fill in your Full name in the "Student Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Student Email" field.
4. Describe your interests in the "Interests" field.
5. If you have any questions, type them in the "Questions" field.
6. Optionally, you can add more details about your questions in the "Questions Details" field.
7. Choose your sign up option from the dropdown list.
8. Click on the "Submit" button to finish the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Fill in your full name as you would like to be known in the form.
- **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
- **Interests** (`student_interests`, text, optional): Describe your interests to help us know you better.
- **Questions** (`questions`, text, optional): If you have any questions or concerns, please type them here.
- **Questions Details** (`questions_details`, text, optional): Add more information about your questions if needed.
- **Sign Up** (`sign_up`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your sign up option from the list:
  - Sign Up for the event
  - Not signing up
  - Not sure
- **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click on this button to finish the registration process.
- **Sign Up** (`sign_up`, text, required): This button will not be visible on the live form but is used as a placeholder for the submit button.
