# Asset Risk Evaluation Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants for the Asset Risk Evaluation Workshop. It is crucial to fill out this form accurately and honestly to ensure that we can tailor the workshop experience to meet your needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Choose the correct options for each select_one or select_multiple field.
3. Enter the correct information for text fields.
4. Ensure that your email address is accurate and up-to-date.
5. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: Please enter your full legal name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Job Title**: Enter your position held in your organization.
* **Organization**: Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Industry**: Select the industry that best describes your work area.
* **Email Address**: Enter your current email address for contact and confirmation purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Work Address**: Enter your mailing address for workshop purposes.
* **Workshop Date**: Select the date you plan to attend the workshop.
* **Workshop Location**: Choose the location where the workshop will be held.
* **Risk Management Experience**: Select your level of experience in risk management.
* **Current Role Related to Risk**: Select the role you have in risk management.
* **Learning Objectives**: Select all the topics you want to learn about during the workshop.
* **Asset Types of Focus**: Select all the asset types that interest you.
* **Dietary Requirements**: Select any dietary restrictions you have.
* **Special Accommodations Needed**: If you have any special needs, please enter them here.
* **Payment Method**: Select how you will pay for the workshop.
* **Registration Fee**: Enter the registration fee for the workshop.
* **Early Bird Discount Applied**: If you have applied for an early bird discount, select 'Yes'.
* **Group Discount Applied**: If you have registered with a group, select 'Yes'.
* **Need Certificate of Completion**: If you need a certificate of completion for continuing education, select 'Yes'.
* **Continuing Education Credits Needed**: If you need continuing education credits, select 'Yes'.
* **Consent to Receive Marketing**: Select whether you consent to receive marketing materials from us.
* **How Did You Hear About Workshop**: Select where you heard about the workshop.
* **Special Requests**: If you have any other needs, please enter them here.
* **I Accept Terms and Conditions**: Select whether you accept the terms and conditions of the workshop.
* **I Understand Cancellation Policy**: Select whether you have read and understood the cancellation policy.
* **Registration Date**: Enter the date you are registering for the workshop.

Note: This guide is meant to be a general explanation and might not cover every single possible scenario or question. If you have any further questions or concerns, please feel free to reach out to us for assistance.
