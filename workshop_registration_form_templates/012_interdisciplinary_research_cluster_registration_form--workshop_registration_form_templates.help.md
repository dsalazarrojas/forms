# Interdisciplinary Research Cluster Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register and gather information about Interdisciplinary Research Clusters. It provides a structured way to collect relevant details about the cluster, its leader, and associated projects.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the **Research Cluster Information** section with a brief description of the cluster, including its name and any relevant details.
2. For each participant in the cluster, select their involvement status (**Yes** or **No**) in the **Cluster Participants** field.
3. Provide a brief explanation of the cluster's goals and objectives in the **Cluster Goals** field.
4. Enter the email address of the cluster leader in the **Cluster Leader Email** field.
5. Enter the phone number of the cluster leader in the **Cluster Leader Phone** field.
6. Enter the email address of the project leader in the **Project Leader Email** field.
7. Enter the phone number of the project leader in the **Project Leader Phone** field.
8. Enter the website of the cluster leader in the **Cluster Leader Website** field.
9. Enter the website of the project leader in the **Project Leader Website** field.
10. Enter any additional notes or comments about the cluster in the **Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Research Cluster Information**: Enter a brief description of the cluster, including its name and any relevant details.
* **Cluster Participants**: Select **Yes** for each participant who is involved with the cluster.
* **Cluster Goals**: Explain the cluster's goals and objectives.
* **Cluster Leader**: Enter the email address of the cluster leader.
* **Cluster Leader Phone**: Enter the phone number of the cluster leader.
* **Cluster Leader Website**: Enter the website of the cluster leader.
* **Project Leader**: Enter the email address of the project leader.
* **Project Leader Phone**: Enter the phone number of the project leader.
* **Project Leader Website**: Enter the website of the project leader.
* **Cluster Status**: Select the status of the cluster (**Active** or **Inactive**).
* **Cluster Duration**: Enter the duration of the cluster in the desired unit (e.g., days, weeks, months, etc.).
* **Cluster Duration Unit**: Select the unit for the cluster duration.
* **Cluster Dates**: Enter the start and end dates of the cluster.
* **Cluster Time**: Enter the time of the cluster (if applicable).
* **Cluster Location**: Enter the location where the cluster will take place (if applicable).
* **Cluster Funding**: Enter any funding information about the cluster (if applicable).
* **Cluster Leader Email**: Enter the email address of the cluster leader.
* **Cluster Leader Phone**: Enter the phone number of the cluster leader.
* **Cluster Leader Website**: Enter the website of the cluster leader.
* **Project Leader**: Enter the name of the project leader.
* **Project Leader Email**: Enter the email address of the project leader.
* **Project Leader Phone**: Enter the phone number of the project leader.
* **Project Leader Website**: Enter the website of the project leader.
* **Project Website**: Enter the website of the project.
* **Tags**: Enter any relevant tags or keywords about the cluster.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the cluster.

Note: All required fields are marked as **required** in the form. Please fill out all required fields accurately to ensure proper processing.
