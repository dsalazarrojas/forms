# Palliative Care Communication Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Palliative Care Communication Training Registration form is used to collect participant details for palliative care communication training programs. It helps administrators to efficiently manage and organize registrations.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
- Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
- Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
- Select your role in the "Roles" field.
- Choose the type of workshop you wish to attend in the "Workshop Type" field.
- Select your availability options in the "Availability" field.
- Enter your workshop details, if applicable.
- Select your preferred location in the "Location" field.
- Choose your preferred time slot in the "Time" field.
- Select your communication workshop preferences in the "Communication Workshop" field.
- Fill in your enrollment status in the "Enrollment" field.
- Select the type of workshop you wish to attend in the "Workshop Type" field.
- Finally, click on "Submit Registration" to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Details (Page 1)**: Enter a brief description of the workshop you wish to attend.
* **Participant Details (Page 2)**: Enter your name, email, and phone number to allow us to contact you.
* **Roles (Page 3)**: Select your role in the palliative care communication training program.
* **Availability (Page 4)**: Choose your availability options for the workshop.
* **Workshop Details (Page 5)**: Enter any additional workshop details if needed.
* **Enrollment (Page 6)**: Specify your enrollment status in the training program.
* **Communication Workshop (Page 7)**: Select your preferences for the communication workshop.
* **Submit Registration (Page 8)**: Review your registration and submit to complete your registration.
* **Contact Details (Page 9)**: Enter your contact details to allow us to contact you.
* **Name (Page 10)**: Enter your name.
* **Email (Page 11)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (Page 12)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Role (Page 13)**: Select your role in the workshop.
* **Workshop Type (Page 14)**: Select the type of workshop you wish to attend.
* **Date (Page 15)**: Enter your preferred date for the workshop.
* **Time (Page 16)**: Select your preferred time for the workshop.
* **Location (Page 17)**: Select your preferred location for the workshop.
