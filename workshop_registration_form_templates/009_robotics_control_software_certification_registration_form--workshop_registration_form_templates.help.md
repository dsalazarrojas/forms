# robotics_control_software_certification_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The robotics control software certification registration form is used to collect information from candidates for certification purposes. This includes details about the candidate, their contact information, certification requirements, and certification information. The form is designed to be filled out once for each certification attempt.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required and optional fields as requested. Please ensure that all fields with a "required" attribute are filled out before proceeding. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Details** (`candidate_details`, `text`, required): Please enter the candidate's name and any other relevant details. This field is required for certification purposes.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required): Enter the candidate's contact information, including email and phone number. This field is required for communication purposes.
* **Certification Requirements** (`certification_requirements`, `text`, required): Describe the specific requirements for the certification, such as courses taken or skills demonstrated. This field is required for certification evaluation.
* **Certification Information** (`certification_info`, `text`, required): Provide any additional certification information, such as certification dates or numbers. This field is required for certification verification.
* **Agreement Terms** (`agreement_terms`, `text`, required): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the certification. This field is required for legal purposes.
* **Upload File** (`upload_file`, `text`, required): If required, upload any supporting files for certification purposes. This field is not required but recommended for submission.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for contact and communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact and communication purposes.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the certification attempt.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the certification attempt.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, `text`, required): Once all fields are filled out, click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* If required, upload any supporting files before submitting the form.
* Review your information carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or issues, contact our support team.
