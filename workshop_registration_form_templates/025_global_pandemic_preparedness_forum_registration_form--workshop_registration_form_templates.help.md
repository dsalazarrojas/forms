# Global Pandemic Preparedness Forum Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Pandemic Preparedness Forum Registration Form is designed to collect contact information and other relevant details from individuals who want to participate in the forum.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact details, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select your country of origin from the list provided.
3. Choose the organization you are affiliated with (if any).
4. Select your role in the forum (if any).
5. Provide a confirm message (optional).
6. Fill in any additional confirm details, such as a confirm text, date, and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form** (`1`): This is the main form where you can fill in your contact details.
* **Contact Details** (`2`): Enter your email address to receive important updates and messages from the forum organizers.
* **Email** (`3`): Enter a valid email address that can be used to communicate with you.
* **Phone** (`4`): Enter your phone number to receive important updates and messages from the forum organizers.
* **Date** (`5`): Choose the date you plan to attend the forum.
* **Time** (`6`): Choose the time you plan to attend the forum.
* **Name** (`7`): Enter your name as you would like to be known on the forum.
* **Country** (`8`): Select your country of origin from the list provided.
* **Organization** (`9`): If you are affiliated with an organization, select it from the list provided. This is optional.
* **Role** (`10`): If you have a specific role in the forum, select it from the list provided. This is optional.
* **Confirm** (`11`): This is an optional section where you can confirm your participation.
* **Confirm Text** (`12`): Enter any additional confirm text if required.
* **Confirm Text Area** (`13`): Enter any additional confirm text if required.
* **Confirm Date** (`14`): Choose the confirm date (if required).
* **Confirm Time** (`15`): Choose the confirm time (if required).
* **Confirm Message** (`16`): Enter any confirm message if required.
* **Submit** (`17`): Click this button to submit your registration form.
* **Cancel** (`18`): Click this button to cancel your registration form.
* **Back** (`19`): Click this button to go back to the previous page.
* **Next** (`20`): Click this button to go to the next page.
* **Help** (`21`): Click this button to view this help guide again.
* **Error** (`22`): This is a feedback section for errors encountered while submitting the form.
* **Success** (`23`): This is a feedback section for successful submissions.
* **Warning** (`24`): This is a feedback section for warnings or important messages.
* **Confirm** (`25`): This is an optional confirm section where you can confirm your participation. 

**Tips:**

* Make sure to double-check your contact details and confirm information before submitting the form.
* Use a valid email address and phone number to ensure you receive important updates and messages from the forum organizers.
* If you have any issues or questions, click on the "Help" button to view this help guide again.
