# Community Hobby Workshop Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to record participant information and feedback from each community hobby workshop session.

## How To Complete This Form

- Start by filling out the required fields: Participant name, Workshop title, and Date attended.
- Optional fields include Email address, Materials needed, Skill level, Feedback on session, Would you attend again, and Interested in follow up events. These fields provide valuable insights but are not mandatory.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask the workshop organizers for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Participant name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as you would like to be recorded in our attendance tracking.
  - This field is used to identify you as a participant in the workshop.
  - Please ensure your name is spelled correctly to avoid confusion.

- **Email address** (`email`, email, optional): If you would like to be contacted for future updates or follow-up events, please provide your email address.
  - This field is not required but helps us to keep you informed about upcoming workshops and events.

- **Workshop title** (`workshop_title`, text, required): Enter the name of the hobby workshop you attended.
  - This field helps us to track the specific workshop and its attendees.

- **Date attended** (`date_attended`, date, required): Enter the date you attended the workshop.
  - This field is used to track the session's attendance dates.

- **Materials needed** (`materials_needed`, text, optional): List any materials or resources you needed to complete the workshop.
  - If you had any issues or difficulties with materials, please describe them here.

- **Skill level** (`skill_level`, select_one, optional): Select your skill level as it relates to the workshop.
  - This field helps us to gauge the workshop's difficulty level and identify areas for improvement.

- **Feedback on session** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share your thoughts on the workshop's content and organization.
  - Your feedback is valuable in helping us to refine the workshop's structure and content.

- **Would you attend again** (`would_return`, select_one, optional): Indicate how likely you are to attend another workshop of this type.
  - This field gives us an idea of the workshop's popularity and its potential for future sessions.

- **Interested in follow up events** (`follow_up_interest`, email, optional): If yes, please enter your email address so we can contact you about upcoming events.
  - If you are interested in following up on other workshops, please let us know your email address, and we'll be in touch!
