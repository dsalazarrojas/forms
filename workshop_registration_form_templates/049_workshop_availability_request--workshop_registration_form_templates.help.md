# Workshop Availability Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workshop Availability Request form is used to inquire about the availability of a workshop or event. The form helps to gather information from potential attendees about their preferred date, contact details, group size, availability, and any additional notes they may have. The form is designed to be simple and straightforward, making it easy to submit your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your contact details in the "Contact Details" field.
2.  Specify your preferred date for the workshop in the "Preferred Date" field.
3.  Indicate the number of people in your group that will be attending in the "Group Size" field.
4.  Let us know if you are available to attend the workshop by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Availability" field.
5.  Add any additional comments or notes you have in the "Notes" field.
6.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details**: A field where you can provide your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Preferred Date**: Select a date you would like to attend the workshop. This is where you can specify a date you are available for the workshop.
* **Group Size**: Enter the number of people that will be attending with you.
* **Availability**: If you are available to attend the workshop, select "Yes", otherwise select "No".
* **Notes**: This is an optional field where you can provide any additional information or comments about your request.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your workshop request.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that you provide accurate information in all the fields.
*   You can specify multiple dates for availability if needed.
*   If you have any special requests or requirements, make sure to include them in the "Notes" field.
*   If you have any questions or need help, contact us at your email address provided.
