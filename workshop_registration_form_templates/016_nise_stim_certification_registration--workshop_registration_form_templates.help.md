# nise_stim_certification_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering for a certification program provided by the National Institute for Safety and Technology (NISEST) Stimulation (STIM) program. The form is used to collect information from applicants, including their details, training history, and preferred certification date and time. This information will be used to process and validate certification applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Applicant Details section with your information, including your name and contact details.
2. In the Training Provider section, enter the name of the training provider you are registering with.
3. In the Training Details section, enter a brief description of your training experience.
4. Select your Applicant Credentials from the drop-down menu in the Applicant Credentials section.
5. In the Preferred Certification Date and Time section, enter your preferred date and time for certification.
6. Finally, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Details (1)** (`title`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name and contact information so we can get in touch with you about your certification application.
* **Training Provider (2)** (`name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the training provider you are registering with.
* **Training Details (3)** (`name`, note, required/optional): Briefly describe your training experience, highlighting relevant skills or qualifications.
* **Applicant Credentials (4)** (`name`, select_one, required/optional): Select your level of experience from the provided options.
* **Preferred Certification Date (5)** (`name`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you prefer to be certified.
* **Preferred Certification Time (6)** (`name`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you prefer to be certified.
* **Certification Date (7)** (`name`, date, required/optional): This field is not actively used in the form, but is required for consistency.
* **Certification Time (8)** (`name`, time, required/optional): This field is not actively used in the form, but is required for consistency.

Note: The form is currently set up to be optional for all fields, however, it is assumed that all fields will be completed to ensure accurate and efficient processing of certification applications. 

**Tips**

* Make sure to enter accurate information to avoid delays or errors in processing your application.
* If you have any issues or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.

Please let us know if you have any questions or need further clarification.
