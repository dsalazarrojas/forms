# Academic Equity In Leadership Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering participants to the Academic Equity In Leadership Training. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure that your information is accurate and complete.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting, make sure you have all the necessary information ready, including your personal details, professional experience, and training preferences. If you are not interested in attending the workshop, do not submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Academic Equity in Leadership Training Registration

* **Academic Equity in Leadership Training Registration**: This is the title of the workshop, and it's not a field that needs to be filled out.

### Page 2: Participant Information

* **Full Name**: Please enter your legal name as it appears on official documents. This is a required field.
* **Email Address**: Enter your professional email address for workshop communications. This is a required field.
* **Institution or Organization**: Enter the name of your university, college, or organization. This is a required field.
* **Current Position**: Enter your current role or title. This is a required field.
* **Department or Division**: Enter your academic department or administrative division. This is not a required field.

### Page 3: Professional Experience

* **Years of Leadership Experience**: If you have held leadership positions, enter the number of years you have been in those roles. This is not a required field.
* **Areas of Interest in Equity Leadership**: Select all topics you are interested in from the list. This is not a required field.
	+ Racial equity in academic leadership
	+ Gender equity in faculty promotion
	+ LGBTQ+ inclusion in academic settings
	+ Disability access in educational environments
	+ Socioeconomic equity in admissions
	+ International scholar support
	+ First-generation student success
	+ Faculty diversity recruitment

### Page 4: Additional Information

* **Current Challenges in Equity Leadership**: If you face any equity challenges in your current role, describe them briefly. This is not a required field.
* **Learning Objectives**: Describe what you hope to gain from the training. This is not a required field.

### Page 5: Session Preferences

* **Preferred Session Format**: Choose your preferred way of participating in the training. This is a required field.
	+ In-person only
	+ Virtual only
	+ Hybrid option
	+ No preference

### Page 6: Accessibility and Dietary Information

* **Accessibility Requirements**: If you have any accessibility needs for the training, describe them briefly. This is not a required field.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: If you have any dietary restrictions for meals provided, describe them briefly. This is not a required field.

### Page 7: Emergency Contact Information

* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person. This is a required field if the "Consent to Recordings" option is selected.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number for the emergency contact. This is a required field if the "Consent to Recordings" option is selected.

### Page 8: Recording Consent

* **Consent to Recordings**: Decide whether you consent to be recorded during the training for educational purposes. This is not a required field.
