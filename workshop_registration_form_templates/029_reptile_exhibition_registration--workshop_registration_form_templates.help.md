# Reptile Exhibition Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reptile Exhibition Registration form is a crucial tool used by exhibitors to provide essential information about their exhibit to the Reptile event organizers. This form helps ensure that all necessary details are collected accurately and efficiently, allowing for smooth event planning and execution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Exhibit Name** field with the name of your exhibit, which should be a brief description of what you will be showcasing at the Reptile exhibition.
2. Provide details about your **Show Details** in the text field. This is an optional field, but including this information will help the organizers understand your exhibit better.
3. Enter your **Contact Information** in the **Contact Info** field, which should include your email and any other relevant contact details.
4. Select the type of **Exhibit Type** that best describes your display.
5. Choose the **Animal Species** that your exhibit is about.
6. Choose the **Show Location** where your exhibit will be displayed.
7. Enter the size of your **Exhibit Size** in feet.
8. Set the **Exhibit Setup Time**, **Show Start Time**, and **Show Finish Time** according to the required time slots provided.
9. Enter the **Setup Cost** and **Tear-down Cost** for your exhibit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Exhibit Name** (`exhibit_name`, text, required): The name of your exhibit, which should be a brief description of what you will be showcasing at the Reptile exhibition.
* **Show Details** (`show_details`, text, optional): A text field for providing additional details about your show.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, email, optional): Enter your contact information, including your email and any other relevant contact details.
* **I Agree** (`rule_agreement`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply from the list of agreements.
* **Exhibit Type** (`exhibit_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of exhibit that best describes your display.
* **Animal Species** (`animal_species`, select_one, required): Choose the animal species related to your exhibit.
* **Show Location** (`show_location`, select_one, required): Choose the location where your exhibit will be displayed.
* **Exhibit Size** (`exhibit_size`, number, required): Enter the size of your exhibit in feet.
* **Exhibit Setup Time** (`exhibit_setup_time`, time, required): Set the time when you would like to start setting up your exhibit.
* **Show Start Time** (`show_start_time`, time, required): Set the time when you would like your show to start.
* **Show Finish Time** (`show_finish_time`, time, required): Set the time when you would like your show to finish.
* **Setup Cost** (`setup_cost`, number, required): Enter the cost for setting up your exhibit.
* **Tear-down Cost** (`teardown_cost`, number, required): Enter the cost for taking down your exhibit.
