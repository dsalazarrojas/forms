# Faculty Collaboration Accelerator Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Faculty Collaboration Accelerator Registration Form is a tool for faculty members to express their interest in collaborating with other departments and programs on campus. This form helps to facilitate resource sharing and expertise collaboration among faculty members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the form title.
2. Enter your name.
3. Provide your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Specify your department.
6. Mention your affiliation with the university.
7. Select your collaboration interests from the available options.
8. Choose a program that aligns with your interests.
9. Enter the date you would like to submit your proposal.
10. Provide your name as the person submitting the proposal.
11. (Optional) Add any additional comments or remarks.
12. Confirm your submission by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required): Please enter a brief title for your proposal.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Please enter your full name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter your university email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Please enter your phone number.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Please specify the department you belong to.
* **Affiliation** (`affiliation`, text, required): Please mention your affiliation with the university.
* **Collaboration Interests** (`collaboration_interests`, select_multiple, required): Please select the collaboration interests that align with your proposal.
* **Program** (`program`, select_one, required): Please choose a program that aligns with your collaboration interests.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Please enter the date you would like to submit your proposal.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required): Please enter your name as the person submitting the proposal.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Please add any additional comments or remarks about your proposal.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to submit your proposal.
* **Confirm Submit** (`confirm_submit`, text, required): Confirm that you have reviewed and agreed to the submission.

Note: The "Submit" and "Confirm Submit" fields are for internal use only and are not intended for end user completion.
