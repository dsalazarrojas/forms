# Evidence Based Curriculum Development Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering for a workshop on evidence-based curriculum development. Please fill out the form to provide your details and register for the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your personal information, including your first name, last name, email, and other relevant details.
2. Provide the title of the workshop you want to register for.
3. Select the type of curriculum you are interested in developing.
4. Choose the date and time you want to submit your registration.
5. Fill out any additional comments or information you would like to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Your first name.
### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Your last name.
### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Your email address.
### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): The date of your birth.
### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Your phone number.
### Address
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Your address.
### Workshop Title
* **Workshop Title** (`workshop_title`, `text`, required: false): The title of the workshop you want to register for.
### Curriculum Type
* **Curriculum Type** (`curriculum_type`, `select_one`, required: false): The type of curriculum you are interested in developing.
### Submit Date
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, `date`, required: false): The date you want to submit your registration.
### Submit Time
* **Submit Time** (`submit_time`, `time`, required: false): The time you want to submit your registration.
### Registered By
* **Registered By** (`registered_by`, `text`, required: false): The person who registered you for the workshop.
### Registered On
* **Registered On** (`registered_on`, `date`, required: false): The date you were registered for the workshop.
### Registered At
* **Registered At** (`registered_at`, `time`, required: false): The time you were registered for the workshop.
### Updated By
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, `text`, required: false): The person who updated your registration.
### Updated On
* **Updated On** (`updated_on`, `date`, required: false): The date your registration was updated.
### Updated At
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, `time`, required: false): The time your registration was updated.
### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Any additional comments or information you would like to provide.
### Created By
* **Created By** (`created_by`, `text`, required: false): The person who created your registration.
### Created On
* **Created On** (`created_on`, `date`, required: false): The date your registration was created.
### Created At
* **Created At** (`created_at`, `time`, required: false): The time your registration was created.
### Form Submitted By
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, `text`, required: false): The person who submitted the form.
### Form Submitted On
* **Form Submitted On** (`form_submitted_on`, `date`, required: false): The date the form was submitted.
### Form Submitted At
* **Form Submitted At** (`form_submitted_at`, `time`, required: false): The time the form was submitted.
### Assigned To
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `text`, required: false): The person assigned to your registration.
### Assigned At
* **Assigned At** (`assigned_at`, `time`, required: false): The time assigned to your registration.

Note: All fields are optional, but please fill out as much information as possible to ensure a smooth registration process.
