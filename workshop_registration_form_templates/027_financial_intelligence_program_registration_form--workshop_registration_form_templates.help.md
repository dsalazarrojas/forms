# Financial Intelligence Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for the Financial Intelligence Program. It collects information about the participant's selection of modules, program details, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out this form to register for the Financial Intelligence Program.
- Ensure you provide accurate contact information to receive program updates and notifications.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Registration Module**: 
  * Choose whether you are registering for the program or not (Yes/No).
* **Participants Name**: 
  * Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Participants Email**: 
  * Enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you.
* **Participants Phone**: 
  * Enter your phone number that can be reached at.
* **Program Date**: 
  * Enter the date you plan to attend the program.
* **Program Time**: 
  * Enter the time you plan to attend the program.
* **Program Duration**: 
  * Enter the expected duration of the program (e.g., half-day, full-day, etc.).
* **Participants Consent**: 
  * Confirm that you have read and understood the program's terms and conditions, and agree to the program's policies.
* **Module Selection 1**: 
  * Select the modules you are interested in attending. You can choose multiple modules.
* **Module Selection 2**: 
  * Select the additional modules you are interested in attending. You can choose multiple modules.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure you have the necessary permissions and approvals to register for the program.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the program administrators.
