# Innovative Pedagogy Symposium Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for attendees to register and provide their information for the Innovative Pedagogy Symposium.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the conference name.
2. Select your session interests.
3. Indicate any accessibility needs you may have.
4. Enter your email address.
5. Enter your phone number.
6. Provide any additional details about yourself (if necessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Conference Name** (`conference_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the conference you are registering for. This will help us associate your registration with the correct event.
* **Session Interests** (`session_interests`, select_multiple, required): Select all the sessions you are interested in attending. This will help us allocate seating and ensure you get the most out of the event.
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, select_one, required): Indicate any accommodations or assistive technologies you may need to attend the event.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about event updates and communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number, which will be used for internal communication during the event.
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about yourself, such as dietary restrictions or special requirements.

## Tips

* Be accurate and thorough when filling out your session interests, as this will help us plan the event effectively.
* If you have any specific accessibility needs, please ensure you select the correct option to ensure we can accommodate you properly.
* Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy to prevent any delays in communication.
