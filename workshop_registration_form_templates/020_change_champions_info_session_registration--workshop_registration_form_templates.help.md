# Change Champions Info Session Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for registering participants for a "Change Champions Info Session" event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Fill in the Registration Date field with the date you would like to register.
2.  Enter the Participant Full Name in the format "First Name Last Name".
3.  Enter the primary contact email address in the Email Address field.
4.  Enter the phone number in the Phone Number field.
5.  (Optional) Enter the Organization or Company Name if you are registering on behalf of an organization.
6.  (Optional) Enter the Job Title or Position if you are registering as an individual.
7.  (Optional) Enter the Department or Division if you are registering as part of an organization.
8.  Select the Preferred Info Session Date from the available options.
9.  (Optional) Select your preferred Time for the info session, or choose "No Preference" if you don't have a preference.
10. Select whether you would like to attend the info session In-Person or Virtually.
11. Select your Role in the Change Initiative. If you don't identify with any of the options, select "Other".
12. (Optional) Select the level of experience you have with change management.
13. (Optional) Enter your objectives for attending the info session.
14. (Optional) Enter any current challenges or concerns you are facing related to change.
15. (Optional) Select the level of readiness your organization has for change.
16. (Optional) Select the level of engagement your stakeholders have with the change initiative.
17. (Optional) Enter any dietary restrictions you may have for the event.
18. (Optional) Select any accessibility accommodations you may need.
19. (Optional) Enter any additional details about the accessibility needs you selected.
20. (Optional) Select whether you would like to receive updates about the change champion program.

*   **Registration Date**: Enter the date you would like to register.
*   **Participant Full Name**: Enter the full name of the participant.
*   **Email Address**: Enter the primary contact email address.
*   **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number.

*   **Time Preference**: Select your preferred time for the info session (if you don't have a preference, select "No Preference").
*   **Attendance Format**: Select whether you would like to attend in-person or virtually.
*   **Role in Change Initiative**: Select your role in the change initiative.
*   **Prior Change Management Experience**: Select the level of experience you have with change management.
*   **What Do You Hope to Learn**: Enter your objectives for attending the info session.
*   **Current Change Challenges or Concerns**: Enter any current challenges or concerns you are facing related to change.
*   **Organization Readiness for Change**: Select the level of readiness your organization has for change.
