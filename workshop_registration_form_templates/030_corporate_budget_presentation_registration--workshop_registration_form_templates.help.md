# Corporate Budget Presentation Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Budget Presentation Registration form is used to collect information from interested parties about their financial presentations. This form will help our team assess the feasibility of potential collaborations or partnerships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the attendee details, including any necessary contact information.
2. Select the session choices that best represent the presentation content.
3. Provide any special requirements or conditions that need to be considered.
4. Choose the financial presentation type (e.g., time of day, duration).
5. Select the event details that best fit the presentation.
6. Enter your contact information.
7. Provide company information (if applicable).
8. Indicate whether or not the presentation will include any additional presentations.
9. Add any additional comments or notes about the presentation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Details** (1, text, required/optional): Please enter your name, title, and any other relevant details about yourself or your representative.
* **Session Choices** (2, select_multiple, required/optional): Select up to three options that best represent the content of your presentation. This will help us understand the type of presentation you plan to offer.
* **Special Requirements** (3, text, required/optional): If there are any specific requirements or conditions that need to be considered during the presentation, please enter them here.
* **Financial Presentation** (4, time, required/optional): Choose the time of day and duration for your presentation. This will help us schedule the event accordingly.
* **Event Details** (5, select_one, required/optional): Select the event that best fits the content of your presentation.
* **Contact Info** (6, email, required/optional): Enter your email address and any other contact information that you'd like to share.
* **Company Info** (7, text, required/optional): If you represent a company, please enter the company name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Presentation Info** (8, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate whether or not the presentation will include any additional presentations.
* **Attendee Requirements** (9, note, required/optional): Use this field to note any attendee requirements or special conditions that need to be considered during the presentation.
* **Additional Comments** (10, text, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your presentation that you'd like to share with us.
