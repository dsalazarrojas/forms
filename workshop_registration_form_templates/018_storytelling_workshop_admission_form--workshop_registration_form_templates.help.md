# Storytelling Workshop Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Storytelling Workshop Admission Form is a registration form for users interested in attending a workshop on storytelling. The form collects essential information to ensure a smooth and efficient workshop experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the required information.
2. Enter your First Name, Last Name, and Email Address in the respective fields.
3. Provide a valid Phone Number if you would like to be contacted.
4. Specify the Workshop Date and Time you would like to attend.
5. Select your interest in the Workshop Topic.
6. Select any additional Workshop Topics you are interested in.
7. Provide any Special Requirements you may have.
8. Specify your Workshop Preferences (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (Text): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (Email): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (Text): Enter a valid phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Workshop Date** (Date): Enter the date you would like to attend the workshop.
* **Workshop Time** (Time): Enter the time you would like to attend the workshop.
* **Workshop Topic** (Select One): Select 'Yes' if you are interested in the workshop topic.
* **What topics are you interested in?** (Select Multiple): Select 'Yes' for any additional workshop topics you are interested in.
* **Special Requirements** (Note): Enter any special requirements you may have, such as accommodations or dietary restrictions.
* **Workshop Preferences** (Text): Enter any additional information about your workshop preferences.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the workshop organizers.
* Review the form carefully before submission to avoid missing any important information.
