# Sustainable Logistics Council Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Sustainable Logistics Council Registration Form is designed for users to register their interest in the Sustainable Logistics Council, a platform that promotes sustainable logistics and supply chain management practices. This form helps users provide their contact information, preferences, and additional details to get involved with the council.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out all required fields.
2.  Review the fields carefully before submitting.
3.  Ensure the accuracy of your contact information and additional details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on our communications.
*   **User Email** (`user_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where you would like to receive updates and communications from the council.
*   **Company** (`user_company`, text, optional): If you are representing a company, enter the company name.
*   **Title** (`user_title`, text, optional): Enter your title or position within your organization (e.g., CEO, Sustainability Manager).
*   **Phone** (`user_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
*   **Notes** (`user_notes`, text, optional): Share any additional notes or comments about your interest in the Sustainable Logistics Council.
*   **Submit** (`user_submit`, text, optional): Click to submit your registration.
*   **Category** (`category`, text, optional): Select the category that best describes your interest in the Sustainable Logistics Council.
*   **Agree** (`user_agree`, select_one, optional): Select whether you agree or disagree to the terms and conditions.
*   **Category** (`user_category`, select_multiple, optional): Select all categories that apply to your interest in the Sustainable Logistics Council.
*   **Comments** (`user_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or additional information about your interest in the Sustainable Logistics Council.
*   **Date** (`user_date`, date, optional): Enter your birthdate or the date you became interested in sustainable logistics.
*   **Time** (`user_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of day you typically work or are active.
*   **Address** (`user_address`, text, optional): Enter your work or home address for us to contact you.
*   **City** (`user_city`, text, optional): Enter the city where you work or reside.
*   **State** (`user_state`, text, optional): Enter the state where you work or reside.
*   **Zip** (`user_zip`, number, optional): Enter your zip or postal code for us to contact you.
*   **Country** (`user_country`, text, optional): Enter the country where you work or reside.
*   **Agreement** (`user_agreement`, select_one, optional): Select the agreement that best represents your commitment to sustainable logistics.
*   **Agreement Details** (`user_agreement_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about your agreement with the Sustainable Logistics Council.
*   **Additional Information** (`user_additional_information`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your interest in sustainable logistics.
*   **Uploads** (`user_uploads`, select_multiple, optional): Select all uploads that apply to your interest in the Sustainable Logistics Council.

## Tips

1.  The fields marked as "required" are necessary for registration.
2.  Review all fields carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
3.  Use a valid email address and phone number to ensure we can contact you.
4.  Be honest about your interests and preferences to help us tailor communications and opportunities to your needs.
