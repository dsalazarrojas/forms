# Workforce Robotics Upskilling Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for participants to register for a workforce robotics upskilling program. It gathers information about the participant's job title, role, contact details, and training and workshop preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant information on page 1.
2. Provide your contact information on page 2.
3. Enter your job title and job role on page 3 and 4, respectively.
4. Share any relevant training information on page 5.
5. If necessary, provide robot information on page 6.
6. Add workshop information on page 7.
7. Optionally, include any notes or comments on page 8.
8. Review and submit your completed form on page 9.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information**: Enter your name and any additional participant information on this page.
* **Contact Information**: Provide your full name, email address, phone number, and any other contact preferences on this page.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title related to robotics upskilling, such as robotic process automation or robotics engineer.
* **Job Role**: Describe your current job role in a brief sentence or two, focusing on your key responsibilities and areas of expertise.
* **Training Information**: Share any relevant training or courses you've completed related to robotics upskilling, or information about the training you're seeking.
* **Robot Information**: If applicable, provide details about the robot(s) you're working with or interested in. This can include robot model, type, or specific tasks it performs.
* **Workshop Information**: Specify the workshop topic(s) or area(s) of focus you're interested in, such as programming, mechanics, or AI integration.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or questions you have about the program or form.
* **Submit**: Review and submit your completed form to register for the upskilling program.
