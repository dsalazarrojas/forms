# Vendor Governance Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Governance Workshop Registration form is a single-page form designed to register attendees for a workshop session. 

## How To Complete This Form

This form consists of the following fields:

1. **workshop_registration** (text): Enter the registration number for the workshop session you are registering for.

2. **workshop_session** (select one): Select 'Yes' to indicate that you are registering for a workshop session, or 'No' if you're not.

3. **attendee_name** (text): Enter your name as it appears on your business card or ID.

4. **attendee_email** (email): Enter your email address.

5. **attendee_phone** (text): Enter your phone number.

6. **attendee_company** (text): Enter your company name.

7. **session_date** (date): Enter the date of the workshop session.

8. **session_time** (time): Enter the time of the workshop session.

9. **attendee_interests** (text): Describe your interests or reasons for attending the workshop.

10. **company** (text): Enter your company name.

11. **position** (text): Enter your position or title within your company.

12. **department** (text): Enter your department name.

13. **job_title** (text): Enter your job title.

14. **role** (text): Enter your role within the company.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the workshop organizers.
* You can only register for one workshop session at a time.
* Please note that this form is not meant for internal use and is intended for external vendors only.
