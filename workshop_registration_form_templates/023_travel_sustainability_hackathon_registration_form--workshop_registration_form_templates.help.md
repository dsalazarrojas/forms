# Travel Sustainability Hackathon Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for the Travel Sustainability Hackathon event.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out all the required fields on each page. Some pages may not have any required fields. 
- Click the "Submit" button once you have filled out all the required fields.
- Review your submission before submitting it. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Personal Info (Page 1)
* **Personal Info**: Enter your personal information, including your name and other relevant details. (text)

### Contact Info (Page 2)
* **Contact Info**: Enter your contact information, including your email and phone number. (text)

### Travel Info (Page 3)
* **Travel Info**: Enter your travel information, including flight number or travel dates. (text)

### Registration Info (Page 4)
* **Registration Info**: Enter your registration information, including any additional details about your participation. (text)

### Emergency Contact (Page 5)
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the contact information of your emergency contact, including their name and phone number. (text)

### Payment Info (Page 6)
* **Payment Info**: Enter your payment method information, including payment details. (text)

### Payment Method (Page 7)
* **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method from the available options. (Select One)
  *   Options: Visa, Mastercard, Payone, Bank Transfer

### Terms and Conditions (Page 8)
* **Terms and Conditions**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the event. (Note)
  *   This is a non-editable field and cannot be filled out. 

Please note that some fields might be required for payment processing. 

If you need help or have questions while filling the form, feel free to contact the support team.
