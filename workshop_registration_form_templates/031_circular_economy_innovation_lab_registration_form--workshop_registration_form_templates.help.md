# Circular Economy Innovation Lab Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Circular Economy Innovation Lab Registration Form is designed to capture essential information from participants to facilitate a successful and effective lab experience. This form helps us understand your background, project, and expectations, ensuring you get the most out of our lab sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details, including First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and Organization or Company.
2. Select the relevant information about your Professional Background, such as your Industry and Years of Professional Experience.
3. Indicate your Areas of Expertise (Select all that apply).
4. Choose the lab session you'd like to participate in and select your Participation Level.
5. Provide a brief overview of your circular economy project or idea in Project Description.
6. Describe your Project Stage, Project Goals and Expected Outcomes, and Expected Environmental or Social Impact.
7. If necessary, mention any Resources or Support Needed for your project.
8. Indicate your interest in collaborating with other participants.
9. Specify the hours you can dedicate to the lab per week.
10. Choose your preferred start date and mention any Availability Constraints or Preferences.
11. Define your Key Learning Goals from this lab.
12. Describe how you will measure success in this lab.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (required): Your first name as it appears on your personal identification documents.
* **Last Name** (required): Your last name as it appears on your personal identification documents.
* **Email Address** (required): Your work or personal email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (required): Your work or mobile phone number for contact.
* **Organization or Company** (required): The name of your organization or company.
* **Job Title** (required): Your job title within your organization.
* **Industry** (required): The sector your organization or company operates in (e.g., Manufacturing, Retail, Technology).
* **Years of Professional Experience** (optional): The number of years you've been working in your field.
* **Areas of Expertise** (optional): Select all the areas you're experienced in (e.g., Product design, Supply chain management, Sustainability).
* **Select Lab Session** (required): Choose the lab session you'd like to participate in (e.g., Session 1 - Fundamentals, Session 2 - Advanced Design).
* **Participation Level** (required): Choose how you plan to engage in the lab (e.g., Observer, Active participant, Team lead).
* **Project Description** (required): A brief overview of your circular economy project or idea.
* **Project Stage** (required): The current stage of your project (e.g., Idea stage, Early development, Pilot testing).
* **Project Goals and Expected Outcomes** (required): Describe what you hope to achieve through this lab.
* **Expected Environmental or Social Impact** (optional): The potential positive impact of your project on the environment and society.
* **Resources or Support Needed** (optional): Select all the resources or support you're seeking (e.g., Technical expertise, Business mentoring, Networking opportunities).
* **Collaboration Interest** (optional): Indicate if you're interested in collaborating with other participants.
* **Hours per Week You Can Dedicate** (required): Choose how many hours you can dedicate to the lab per week.
* **Preferred Start Date** (required): Choose a date you'd like to start participating in the lab.
* **Availability Constraints or Preferences** (optional): Mention any constraints or preferences you have regarding your availability.
* **Key Learning Goals from This Lab** (required): Describe the skills or knowledge you hope to gain from this lab experience.
* **How Will You Measure Success in This Lab** (optional): Explain how you plan to measure the success of your project or idea in this lab.
