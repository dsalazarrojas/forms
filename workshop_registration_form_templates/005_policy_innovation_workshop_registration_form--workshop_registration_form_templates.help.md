# policy_innovation_workshop_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The policy innovation workshop registration form is designed to collect information from workshop participants, speakers, and organizers. It serves as a central hub for storing and managing registration data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Select the correct participant type from the options provided.
3. Choose the type of workshop you are interested in attending.
4. Enter the workshop date and time in the required format.
5. Provide additional notes if necessary.
6. Review your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Form Title
* **Policy Innovation Workshop Registration Form** (`form_title`, text, required): This is the title of the form, which is a placeholder to be filled in with the workshop name. It is a required field.

### Participant Information
* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, text, optional): This field allows you to provide any additional information about yourself as a participant. It is an optional field.

### Participant Type
* **Participant Type** (`participant_type`, select_one, required): This field allows you to select whether you are a Speaker, Participant, or Organizer. This helps the workshop organizers to categorize and manage registration data.

### Workshop Type
* **Workshop Type** (`workshop_type`, select_one, required): This field allows you to select the type of workshop you are interested in attending, which can be either Policy Innovation or Other. This helps the workshop organizers to manage workshop offerings and resources.

### Workshop Date
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the workshop you are interested in attending. This should be in the standard date format.

### Workshop Time
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the workshop you are interested in attending. This should be in the standard time format.

### Venue
* **Venue** (`venue`, text, required): Enter the location where the workshop will take place. This helps the workshop organizers to manage logistics and venue selection.

### Additional Notes
* **Additional Notes** (`note`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or requests, you can enter them in this field.

### Assigned User
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, text, optional): This field is intended for workshop organizers to assign a participant to a specific user, but it is not clear what this means. If you are unsure, please do not use this field.
