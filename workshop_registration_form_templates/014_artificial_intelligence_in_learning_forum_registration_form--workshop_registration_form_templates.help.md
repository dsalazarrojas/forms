# Artificial Intelligence In Learning Forum Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from educators, researchers, and industry professionals interested in AI applications in learning. We will use this information to create a valuable and engaging experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the "Participant Information" section.
2. Provide your professional information, including your institution or organization name, job title, and professional role category.
3. Select your areas of interest and experience level with AI.
4. Indicate your preferred session format and interest in networking opportunities.
5. Share your learning objectives and any current challenges you are facing with AI in learning.
6. Provide any dietary requirements or accessibility needs.
7. Consent to receive forum updates and agree to abide by the forum code of conduct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`<name>`, `text`, required): Please provide your first name.
* **Last Name** (`<name>`, `text`, required): Please provide your last name.
* **Email Address** (`<email>`, `email`, required): Please provide your email address. Registration confirmation will be sent here.
* **Phone Number** (`<phone_number>`, `text`, optional): Please provide your phone number, if desired. This will help us with event updates.
* **Institution or Organization** (`<institution_name>`, `text`, required): Please provide the name of your school, university, company, or organization.
* **Job Title or Position** (`<job_title>`, `text`, required): Please provide your job title or position.
* **Professional Role Category** (`<professional_role>`, `select_one`, required): Please select your primary professional role category:
	+ Educator or Teacher
	+ Administrator
	+ Instructional Designer
	+ Learning Technologist
	+ Researcher
	+ AI Specialist
	+ Student
	+ Other
* **Years of Experience in Education** (`<years_experience>`, `select_one`, optional): Please select your years of experience in education:
	+ Less than 1 year
	+ 1-3 years
	+ 4-7 years
	+ 8-15 years
	+ 15+ years
* **Topics You Are Most Interested In** (`<interest_areas>`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select all the topics you are most interested in:
	+ AI in Personalized Learning
	+ Intelligent Tutoring Systems
	+ Natural Language Processing in Education
	+ AI Assessment Tools
	+ Automated Grading Systems
	+ Predictive Analytics in Learning
	+ Ethical AI in Education
	+ AI Policy and Governance
* **Your Experience with AI** (`<ai_experience_level>`, `select_one`, required): Please select your level of experience with AI:
	+ No prior experience
	+ Basic familiarity
	+ Some hands-on experience
	+ Extensive experience
	+ I am an AI expert
* **Preferred Session Format** (`<session_preferences>`, `select_one`, optional): Please select how you prefer to attend:
	+ In-person only
	+ Virtual only
	+ Hybrid (both options)
	+ No preference
* **Interest in Networking Opportunities** (`<networking_interest>`, `select_one`, optional): Please select your level of interest in networking opportunities:
	+ Very interested
	+ Somewhat interested
	+ Not interested
* **What Do You Hope to Learn?** (`<learning_objectives>`, `text`, required): Please share your learning objectives for attending this forum.
* **Current Challenges with AI in Learning** (`<current_challenges>`, `text`, optional): Please share any current challenges you are facing with AI in learning.
* **Dietary Requirements or Restrictions** (`<dietary_requirements>`, `select_one`, optional): Please select any dietary requirements or restrictions:
	+ No restrictions
	+ Vegetarian
	+ Vegan
	+ Gluten-free
	+ Kosher
	+ Halal
	+ Other
* **Accessibility or Accommodation Needs** (`<accessibility_needs>`, `text`, optional): Please provide any accessibility or accommodation needs you may have.
* **I agree to receive forum updates and communications** (`<communications_consent>`, `select_one`, optional): Please select whether you consent to receive forum updates and communications:
	+ Yes, I Consent

* **I agree to abide by the forum code of conduct** (`<code_of_conduct>`, `select_one`, required): Please select whether you agree to abide by the forum code of conduct:
	+ I Agree
