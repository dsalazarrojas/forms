# Intercultural Dialogue Series Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Intercultural Dialogue Series Registration Form is a tool used to collect information from participants and help the organizers manage the event.
## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, start by selecting the "Registration Form" page.
2. Fill out each field in the order presented.
3. Ensure that all required fields (marked as "required: true") are completed before proceeding.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form** (`registration_form`, text, required: false): This is the first page of the form. It contains a general introduction and instructions for the registration process. You can read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Fill in your full name as you would like it to be displayed in the event materials.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address to receive updates about the event and any necessary information.
* **Date** (`date`, time, required: false): Select the date you plan to attend the event.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Select the time you plan to arrive at the event.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, required: false): Choose one option from the list that best suits your availability or preferences.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the options that apply to you from the list.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): If there is any important information you would like to share or discuss, enter it in the note field.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number so that the organizers can contact you if needed.
