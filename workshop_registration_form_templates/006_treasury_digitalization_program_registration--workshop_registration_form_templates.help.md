# Treasury Digitalization Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect demographic and professional information from individuals who are interested in the Treasury Digitalization Program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined in the previous section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Department** (Text, optional): If you are affiliated with a department, enter the name of your department. This will help us get a better understanding of your work environment.
* **Email** (Email, optional): Enter a valid email address that you regularly check.
* **Phone** (Text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Date Of Birth** (Date, optional): Enter your date of birth. This will help us get a better understanding of your background.
* **Time Zone** (Select One, optional): Select your time zone from the dropdown menu. This will help us get a better understanding of your location.
* **Organization** (Text, optional): Enter your organization name. This will help us get a better understanding of your work environment.
* **Job Title** (Text, optional): Enter your job title. This will help us get a better understanding of your professional background.
* **Job Description** (Text, optional): Enter a brief description of your job. This will help us get a better understanding of your job responsibilities.
* **Location** (Text, optional): Enter the location of your work.
* **Country** (Text, optional): Enter the country of your location.
* **Time Zone** (Select One, optional): Select your time zone from the dropdown menu. This will help us get a better understanding of your location.
* **Role** (Text, optional): Select your role from the dropdown menu. This will help us get a better understanding of your job responsibilities.
* **Role Type** (Text, optional): Enter your role type. This will help us get a better understanding of your job role.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill in all required fields to ensure that your application is processed efficiently.
* If you are unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
