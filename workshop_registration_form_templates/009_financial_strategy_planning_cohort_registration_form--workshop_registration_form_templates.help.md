# Financial Strategy Planning Cohort Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for registering participants in Financial Strategy Planning cohorts. It is intended to capture essential information from each participant, including their name, email, phone number, and other relevant details. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information ready before starting the form.
2. Fill in your name, email address, and phone number in the respective fields.
3. Select the correct Financial Strategy Planning Cohort and Location from the provided options.
4. Choose the assigned tool for your cohort, or select "Other Tool" if not assigned.
5. If applicable, add any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Name
 * **Name** (<label> `name`, <type> `text`, required/optional): Enter your full name in this field.
### 2. Email
 * **Email** (<label> `email`, <type> `email`, required/optional): Enter a valid email address to receive updates and communications about your participation.
### 3. Phone
 * **Phone** (<label> `phone`, <type> `text`, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number for further communication.
### 4. Date
 * **Date** (<label> `date`, <type> `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you wish to participate in the cohort.
### 5. Financial Strategy Planning Cohort
 * **Financial Strategy Planning Cohort** (<label> `financial_strategy_planning_cohort`, <type> `select_one`, required/optional): Select the correct cohort you wish to join. Options include Financial Strategy Planning Cohort 1, Financial Strategy Planning Cohort 2, and Financial Strategy Planning Cohort 3.
### 6. Location
 * **Location** (<label> `location`, <type> `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select one or more locations you are interested in participating in. Options include Location 1, Location 2, and Location 3.
### 7. Assigned Tool
 * **Assigned Tool** (<label> `assigned_tool`, <type> `select_one`, required/optional): Select the tool assigned to your cohort, or choose "Other Tool" if not assigned.
### 8. Comments
 * **Comments** (<label> `comments`, <type> `text`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or information about your participation.
### 9. Notes
 * **Notes** (<label> `notes`, <type> `text`, required/optional): Enter any personal notes or reminders about your participation.
### 10. Additional Comments
 * **Additional Comments** (<label> `additional_comments`, <type> `text`, required/optional): Add any further comments or information about your participation.

## Tips
- Ensure the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact the form administrator for assistance.
- Review and submit the form carefully before submission.
- If you have any issues with the form or need help, contact the form administrator for support.
