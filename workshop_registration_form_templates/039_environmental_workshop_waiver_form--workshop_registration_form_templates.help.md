# Environmental Workshop Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for an environmental workshop and sign a waiver. It is recommended to fill it out for the workshop organizers to have the necessary information to contact you in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the answer "Yes" or "No" for each of the options on the "Workshop Registration Form" page.
2. Provide your participant consent by selecting a date on the "Participant Consent" page.
3. Write down your waiver terms in the text field on the "Waiver Terms" page.
4. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Emergency Contact Details" page.
5. Enter your participant details in the text field on the "Participant Details" page.
6. Sign the form by providing your signature on the "Participant Signature" page.
7. Enter your organization's name in the text field on the "Organization Name" page.
8. Enter your organization's phone number in the text field on the "Organization Phone" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Registration Form**: This page asks you to select whether you will be attending the workshop ("Yes" or "No"). Please choose the answer that applies to you.
* **Participant Consent**: This page asks you to select a date, which confirms that you have given your consent to participate in the workshop.
* **Waiver Terms**: This page asks you to write down any terms or conditions that you would like to apply to your participation in the workshop.
* **Emergency Contact Details**: This page asks you to select "Yes" if you have an emergency contact person or "No" if you do not have one.
* **Participant Details**: This page asks you to enter your name, email, and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Participant Signature**: This page asks you to provide your signature by selecting a date.
* **Organization Name**: This page asks you to enter the name of your organization.
* **Organization Phone**: This page asks you to enter your organization's phone number.

## Tips
* Please fill out this form carefully and completely, as it is recommended but not mandatory.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact the workshop organizers.
* Please make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
