# Employee Branding Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for the Employee Branding Workshop. It gathers essential information about each participant, including name, email, job title, department, and other relevant details. The information collected will be used for workshop planning and execution.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to enter your correct email address, as it will be used for communication related to the workshop.
3. If you need help with any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address that can be used for communication.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your official job title.
- **Department** (`department`, `text`, required): Enter your department name.
- **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YY).
- **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the workshop you are registering for.
- **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Enter the workshop time (HH:MM AM/PM).
- **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, `time`, required): Enter the duration of the workshop you are registering for.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
- **Select One** (`select_one`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the options provided.
- **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose multiple options from the list.
- **Note** (`note`, `note`, required): Add any relevant comments or notes about your registration.
- **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YY).
- **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Enter the time for your availability.
- **Decimal** (`decimal`, `number`, required): Enter a decimal value.
- **Integer** (`integer`, `number`, required): Enter an integer value.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address that can be used for communication.
- **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your contact address.
- **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter your city name.
- **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter your state name.
- **Zip** (`zip`, `number`, required): Enter your zip or postal code.
- **Country** (`country`, `text`, required): Enter your country name.
- **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required): Add any comments about your registration.
- **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, `note`, required): Confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions of the workshop.

## Tips
- Verify the accuracy of all fields before submitting the form.
- Use a valid email address for communication purposes.
- Ensure that your contact details are up to date and correct.
