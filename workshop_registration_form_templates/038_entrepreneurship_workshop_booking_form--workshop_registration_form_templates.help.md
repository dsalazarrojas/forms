# Entrepreneurship Workshop Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for workshop booking purposes, focusing on entrepreneurship-related workshops.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your Participant Information.
2. Provide details about the workshop you are interested in.
3. Fill out your Booking Information.
4. Enter any additional information you would like to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, required: false): 
  Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information as requested.
* **Workshop Information** (`workshop_info`, text, required: false): 
  Describe the workshop you are interested in, or provide any specific details about the session(s) you would like to attend.
* **Booking Information** (`booking_info`, text, required: false): 
  Provide details about the booking, such as the date and time you prefer to attend.
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required: false): 
  Include any additional details about yourself that might be helpful for the workshop organizers, such as your experience with the topic or any relevant skills you bring.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text, required: false): 
  If there's anything else you'd like to mention or ask, use this section to provide it.
