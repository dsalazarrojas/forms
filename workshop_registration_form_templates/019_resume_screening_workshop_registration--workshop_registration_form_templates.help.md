# Resume Screening Workshop Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in attending a Resume Screening Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Registration Form**: This field is the form title and is required for identification purposes.
*   **Attendee Details**: This field is used to gather your name, email, and other relevant attendee details.
*   **Session Preferences**: In this field, you can specify your preferred workshop session.
*   **Expectations**: This field is used to understand your expectations from the workshop.
*   **Resume Review**: Please upload or attach your resume for review by the workshop facilitators.
*   **Workshop Session**: Select your preferred workshop session from the available options.
*   **Contact Info**: Provide your contact information, including phone, email, and address details.
*   **Session Preferences List**: Select your preferred workshop session from the available options.
*   **Session Preferences 1-4**: Select your preferred workshop session from the available options.
*   **Resume Preferences**: Specify your preferred resume preferences.
*   **Session Preferences List 2**: Select your preferred workshop session from the available options.
*   **Contact Info Phone**: Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Contact Info Email**: Provide your email address for contact purposes.
*   **Contact Info Work Email**: Provide your work email address for contact purposes.
*   **Contact Info Mobile**: Provide your mobile number for contact purposes.
*   **Contact Info Work Number**: Provide your work phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Contact Info Address**: Provide your address for contact purposes.
*   **Contact Info Work Address**: Provide your work address for contact purposes.
*   **Contact Info City**: Provide your city for contact purposes.
*   **Contact Info State**: Provide your state for contact purposes.
*   **Contact Info Postal Code**: Provide your postal code for contact purposes.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all required fields are filled in to complete the form successfully.
*   If you have any questions or need assistance, don't hesitate to contact us.
*   We will contact you soon after submitting this form to confirm your registration.
