# Data Analytics Retail Bootcamp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Data Analytics Retail Bootcamp Registration Form" is designed to collect information from participants for a retail data analytics training program. This form helps us to understand your background, preferences, and goals, ensuring an engaging and relevant learning experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form carefully, providing accurate information for each field.
2. Start by providing your email address, so we can contact you with updates and event details.
3. Enter your name and address to help us tailor the event to your needs.
4. If you're attending as part of a team, you can select multiple options from the "Select Multiple" field.
5. Choose your preferred date and time for the training program. Please note that availability may vary.
6. Finally, add any additional notes or comments about your interests and expectations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about the training program and its schedule.
* **Full Name** (`name`, text, required): Please enter your full name, which will be used for event registration and communication.
* **Age** (`age`, number, optional): If you're willing, please share your age to help us tailor the content to your needs.
* **Full Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your full address to help us with travel or logistics arrangements, if needed.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Add your phone number for easy communication, if needed.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, optional): Select one option that best describes you or your role in the organization.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_many`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple options that apply to your team or role in the organization.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Please choose a date you're available for the training program. Availability may be limited, so be sure to choose wisely.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select a preferred time for the training program, taking into account our schedule.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your preferences or expectations. This helps us tailor the experience to your needs.
