# Corporate Renewal Planning Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for teams or individuals who want to participate or attend the Corporate Renewal Planning event. If you're a representative of your company or a team member looking for renewal and growth opportunities, this form is for you. Fill it out carefully to ensure accurate information is collected.

## How To Complete This Form
- Click on each field to focus on the input section.
- Fill in the required information, following each field's explanation.
- Click "submit" when you're done.
