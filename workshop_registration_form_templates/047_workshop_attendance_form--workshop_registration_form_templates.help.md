# Workshop Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workshop Attendance Form is a tool used to collect information from workshop attendees, ensuring accurate attendance tracking and record keeping for events, conferences, or workshops.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the workshop in the "workshop" field.
2. Select the date of the workshop from the calendar or enter it manually in the "date" field.
3. Enter the time of the workshop in the "time" field.
4. Fill in the name of the attendee in the "attendee_name" field.
5. Enter the email of the attendee in the "email" field (make sure to include the "@domain" for a valid email address).
6. Enter the phone number of the attendee in the "phone" field.
7. Add any additional notes about the attendee in the "notes" field.
8. Select whether the attendee is present or not by choosing one of the provided options in the "is_present" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **workshop** (`workshop`, text, required): Enter the title of the workshop you are attending.
* **date** (`date`, date, required): Select the date of the workshop or enter it manually in the format of "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time of the workshop in 24-hour format ("HH:MM").
* **attendee_name** (`attendee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the attendee.
* **email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the attendee, including the "@" symbol for a valid email address.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the attendee.
* **notes** (`notes`, note, required): Add any additional notes about the attendee.
* **is_present** (`is_present`, select_one, required): Select whether the attendee is present or not, with provided options.
