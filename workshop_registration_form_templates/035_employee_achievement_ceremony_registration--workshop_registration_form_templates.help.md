# Employee Achievement Ceremony Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Achievement Ceremony Registration form is used to collect information from employees and their guests attending a special event, such as an achievement ceremony.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name and employee number (if applicable).
2. Enter the guest's name and guest number (if applicable).
3. Choose an RSVP option for the employee and guest.
4. Enter the event date and time (if applicable).
5. Add any special requirements or messages for the employee and guest (if applicable).
6. Enter the email address and phone number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee who will be attending the event.
	+ This field is optional.
* **Employee Number**: Enter the employee number of the employee who will be attending the event (if applicable).
	+ This field is optional.
* **Guest Name**: Enter the name of the guest who will be attending the event with the employee.
	+ This field is optional.
* **Guest Number**: Enter the guest number of the guest who will be attending the event with the employee (if applicable).
	+ This field is optional.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event (if applicable).
	+ This field is optional.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event (if applicable).
	+ This field is optional.
* **RSVP**: Choose whether the employee and guest will be attending the event (RSVP: "Yes" or "No").
	+ This field is optional.
* **Message**: Add any special requirements or messages for the employee and guest (if applicable).
	+ This field is optional.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the employee and guest (if applicable).
	+ This field is optional.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the employee and guest (if applicable).
	+ This field is optional.
* **Special Requirements**: Enter any special requirements or requests from the employee and guest (if applicable).
	+ This field is optional.
* **Guest Special Requirements**: Enter any special requirements or requests from the guest (if applicable).
