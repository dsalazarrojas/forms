# Workforce Collaboration Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register employees for workforce collaboration training sessions. It collects essential information about the employee, their job status, training location, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the first two fields. These are required fields and should be accurate to your official name.
2. Enter your **Email** and **Manager Email** to ensure that communication with the training team is possible.
3. Select your **Job Status** as either **Active** or **Inactive** to provide the training team with the relevant information.
4. Choose the **Training Location** where you will attend the training session (if you have multiple locations, please select all that apply).
5. Select the **Training Type** for the session you wish to attend (if you have multiple types, select all that apply).
6. Enter your **Manager Phone** to allow the training team to contact you directly.
7. Enter your **Manager Title** to provide additional context about your role.
8. Select your current **Job Status** and **Training Status** as relevant to your situation.
9. If the training location is not listed, you can enter the details of the **Other Training Location**.
10. If the training type is not listed, you can enter the details of the **Other Training Type**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter your work email address where the training team can contact you.
* **Manager Email**: Enter your manager's email address where the training team can contact them.
* **Company**: If you wish to provide your company name for the training team's reference.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title as per your official documents.
* **Department**: Choose "Yes" if you are in a department, otherwise choose "No".
* **Manager**: If you have a manager, enter their name.
* **Manager Phone**: Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Training Location**: Choose the location(s) where you will attend the training session.
* **Training Type**: Choose the type of training you wish to attend.
* **Training Location Other**: If the training location is not listed, enter the details of the other location.
* **Training Type Other**: If the training type is not listed, enter the details of the other type.
* **Training Status**: Select your current training status as per your situation.
* **Training Status Date**: Enter the date of your current training status.
* **Manager Title**: Enter your manager's title.
* **Manager Phone**: Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Training Status Date**: Enter the date of your current training status.
* **Other Training Location**: If the training location is not listed, enter the details of the other location.
* **Other Training Type**: If the training type is not listed, enter the details of the other type.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for the training team's reference.
* **Submit**: This is the submit button. Please review your information carefully before submitting the form.
