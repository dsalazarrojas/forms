# Student Led Learning Reform Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students for a student-led learning reform initiative. The form asks for student name, school, parent name, and other relevant details. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name as a student.
2. Enter your school name.
3. Add your parent's name.
4. Provide your school email address and your personal email address.
5. Specify your interests and motivations for learning reform initiatives.
6. Indicate if you are an organizer participant or not.
7. Choose the program name, dates, start and end times, and duration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (text): Enter your name as a student.
* **School** (text): Enter your school name.
* **Parent Name** (text): Enter your parent's name (if applicable).
* **School Email** (email): Enter your school email address.
* **Student Email** (email): Enter your personal email address.
* **Interests and Motivations** (text): Specify your interests and motivations for learning reform initiatives. This could include what topics you are interested in, what you want to achieve, or what problems you want to solve.
* **Program Name** (text): Choose the name of the program you are registering for.
* **Organizer Participants?** (select multiple): Indicate if you will be an organizer participant for the program. This helps the organizers to plan and manage the program.
* **Program Dates** (date): Choose the start and end dates of the program.
* **Program Start Time** (time): Choose the start time of the program.
* **Program End Time** (time): Choose the end time of the program.
* **Program Duration** (number): Specify the duration of the program in hours.
* **Teacher Name** (text): This is not a required field, but if you are registering as a teacher, please enter your name.
* **Parent Name** (text): This is not a required field, but if you are registering as a parent, please enter your name.
