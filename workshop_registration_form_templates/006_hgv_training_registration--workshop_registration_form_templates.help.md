# HGV Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect contact details and schedule preferences from users who are interested in HGV training. This information will be used by training providers to follow up with the users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields as instructed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): This is a field where you can enter your contact details, such as your name, phone number, and email address.
* **License Status** (`license_status`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the options: Driver License Number, Driving School ID, or Other. If you don't have a license number or a driving school ID, select Other.
* **Schedule Preferences** (`schedule_preferences`, number, required: false): Enter a number indicating your preferred schedule for the training (e.g., 1, 2, etc.).
* **Training Provider** (`training_provider`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the options: Driving Schools, Training Providers, or Other.
* **Driving School** (`driving_school`, select_one, required: false): Select Yes or No to indicate if you are affiliated with a driving school.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the contact person (e.g., your name).
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, note, required: false): Enter your address in the space provided.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes you may want to add about your training preferences or any other information.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields correctly to ensure your details are captured accurately.
* You can add notes if you have any specific requirements or preferences.
* If you are not sure about any of the options, select the most relevant one or add a note explaining your preference.
