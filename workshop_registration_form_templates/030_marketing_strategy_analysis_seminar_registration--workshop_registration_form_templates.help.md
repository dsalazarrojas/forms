# Marketing Strategy Analysis Seminar Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketing Strategy Analysis Seminar Registration form is designed to gather details from interested attendees about their company and their marketing strategy. This information will help us create a more valuable experience for all attendees by tailoring the seminar content.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out each field with your information.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled.
3. Select relevant options for the select fields.
4. Submit the form once you have filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **User Inputs**
This is the section where you input your details as a representative of your company.
### 2. **Marketing Strategy Seminar**
Please provide the title of the marketing strategy seminar you are interested in.
### 3. **Name**
Enter your name.
### 4. **Company**
Enter your company's name.
### 5. **Email**
Please enter your company's email address.
### 6. **Phone**
Enter your company's phone number.
### 7. **Position**
Enter your job position in the company.
### 8. **Job Function**
Enter your job function in the company.
### 9. **Company URL**
Enter your company's website URL.
### 10. **Job Title**
Enter your job title.
### 11. **Job Description**
Enter a brief description of your job.
### 12. **Company Size**
Select the size of your company.
### 13. **Company Type**
Select the type of your company.
### 14. **Event Interest Level**
Select how interested you are in attending the event on a low, moderate, or high level.
### 15. **Seminar Interest Level**
Select how interested you are in the seminar on a low, moderate, or high level.
### 16. **Follow Up**
Choose a date to follow up on your seminar registration.
### 17. **Follow Up Time**
Choose a time to follow up on your seminar registration.
### 18. **Marketing Channels**
Select your preferred marketing channel.
### 19. **Lead Source**
Select where you found out about this seminar.
### 20. **Lead Source Other**
If your lead source is "other", please provide more details.
### 21. **Marketing Channels Other**
If your marketing channel is "other", please provide more details.
### 22. **Marketing Channels Other Description**
If your marketing channel is "other", please provide a more detailed description.
### 23. **Event Date**
Choose a date you will be able to attend the seminar.
### 24. **Event Time**
Choose a time you will be able to attend the seminar.
### 25. **Submit**
Review and submit the form.
