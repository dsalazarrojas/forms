# Sustainability Forum Panelist Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sustainability Forum Panelist Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from potential panelists for the sustainability forum. This form is a crucial step in the registration process, and its completion is required to participate in the forum.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  On the first page, provide your panelist details in the "Panelist Details" field.
2.  On the second page, enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number in the "Contact Information" field.
3.  On the third page, select all the expertise fields that apply to you or your organization in the "Expertise Fields" field.
4.  If you have any additional information to share, you can do so in the "Additional Information" field on the last page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Panelist Details** (required): Enter your name and title or position to be displayed on the forum.
* **Contact Information** (required): Enter your email address and phone number to allow us to contact you with further details.
* **Expertise Fields** (optional): Select all the areas of expertise that you or your organization specializes in. This will help us match you with the most relevant topics and discussions.
* **Email** (required): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (optional): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Additional Information** (optional): This field is for any additional information you want to share with us, such as your background or qualifications.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have not already participated in previous sustainability forums to avoid duplication.
* Review your information carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
