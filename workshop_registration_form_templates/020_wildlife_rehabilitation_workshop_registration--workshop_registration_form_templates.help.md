# Wildlife Rehabilitation Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect registration information from interested individuals for the Wildlife Rehabilitation Workshop. 

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Provide Personal Details
Please enter your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields. 

### Step 2: Enter Contact Information
Enter your Email address, as we will use this to communicate with you about the workshop details. 

### Step 3: Choose Your Preferred Date
If possible, select the date you would like to attend the workshop. Keep in mind that the event schedule will be updated according to your selection. 

### Step 4: Share Your Interests
Please indicate if you are interested or not in participating in any of the activities offered during the workshop. 

### Step 5: Provide Your Availability and Consent
You can choose to consent or not to participate in any activities. 

### Step 6: Enter Contact Information (Again)
Enter your Phone number for us to get in touch with you if needed. 

### Step 7: Provide Additional Information (Optional)
If you have any additional comments or notes, you can add them here. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address for workshop updates and communication.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, `date`, optional): Select the date you would like to attend the workshop. Note that this is optional, but it helps us to schedule the event better.
* **Interests** (`interests`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the activities you are interested in participating in during the workshop.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number for any updates or last-minute changes.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter your address if you have any special requirements for attending the workshop.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or information that might be helpful for us to know about your needs. 

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and honestly. We will use this information to tailor the workshop to suit your needs and preferences.
* If you have any dietary requirements, please mention them in your notes or contact us separately.
* You can cancel your registration if you are unable to attend. Just let us know as soon as possible so we can adjust our schedules accordingly.
