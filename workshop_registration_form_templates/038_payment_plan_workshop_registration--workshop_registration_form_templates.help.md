# Payment Plan Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Payment Plan Workshop Registration form is designed to gather user information and payment plan preferences for the Payment Plan Workshop. It is a simple form that helps you provide details and choices for the workshop organizers to prepare for the event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully and honestly to ensure accurate information is submitted.
2. Make sure to provide all required fields.
3. Double-check your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (1)** (<type>): 
  * This field is used to collect basic contact information about you.
  * Please enter your name, email address, and any other relevant details.
* **Payment Information (2)** (<type>): 
  * This field is used to capture your payment method and details.
  * Please enter your payment method and any other relevant payment details.
* **Fee Information (3)** (<type>): 
  * This field is used to provide more details about your payment fees.
  * Please enter any additional information or concerns about your payment fees.
* **Fee Selection (4)** (<type>): 
  * This field is a dropdown menu to select your preferred payment plan.
  * Choose one or more options from the list to proceed with your payment plan.
  * Options: Payment Plan 1, Payment Plan 2, Payment Plan 3, Payment Plan 4
* **Payment Terms (5)** (<type>): 
  * This field is used to provide any further payment terms or conditions.
  * Please enter any additional payment terms or conditions you may have.
* **Registration Information (6)** (<type>): 
  * This field is used to capture any additional registration details.
  * Please enter any additional information about your registration.

## Tips

* Double-check your responses before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact the workshop organizers.
* You can always reach out to the workshop organizers for further assistance.
