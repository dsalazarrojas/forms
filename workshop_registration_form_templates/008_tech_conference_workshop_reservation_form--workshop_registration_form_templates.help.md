# Tech Conference Workshop Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tech Conference Workshop Reservation Form is designed to collect information from attendees and workshop organizers to plan and manage workshops during the conference. This form helps us understand workshop preferences, attendee details, and session schedules, ensuring a smooth and organized experience for all attendees and organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Workshop Details" page.
2. Fill out the "Workshop Details" field with a brief description of the workshop you're interested in.
3. Proceed to the "Attendee Info" page.
4. Provide your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information in the "Attendee Info" field.
5. Move on to the "Workshop Reservation" page.
6. Enter your preferred workshop session time in the "Workshop Reservation" field.
7. On the "Session Capacity" page, enter the number of attendees you expect to join you in the workshop session.
8. Go to the "Attendee Preferences" page and describe any specific preferences or requirements you may have for the workshop session.
9. On the "Session Details" page, add any additional details about the workshop session.
10. Finally, enter your contact details on the "Contact Details" page and confirm the workshop dates on the "Workshop Dates" page.
11. Don't forget to include any notes or comments you'd like to share in the "Notes" field at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Details (Workshop Details)**: A brief description of the workshop you're interested in, including the name and topic of the workshop.
* **Attendee Info (Attendee Info)**: A field to enter your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information to help us reach you regarding the workshop.
* **Workshop Reservation (Workshop Reservation)**: Select your preferred workshop session time for the chosen workshop.
* **Session Capacity (Session Capacity)**: Enter the number of attendees you expect to join you in the workshop session.
* **Attendee Preferences (Attendee Preferences)**: A text area for you to describe any specific preferences or requirements you may have for the workshop session.
* **Session Details (Session Details)**: Add any additional details about the workshop session that might be helpful for the organizers.
* **Contact Details (Contact Details)**: Enter your name, email address, and phone number (if available) to help us contact you about the workshop.
* **Session Time (Session Time)**: Choose your preferred session time for the workshop.
* **Workshop Dates (Workshop Dates)**: Confirm the dates of the workshop you've chosen.
* **Notes (Notes)**: A field for you to include any additional comments or notes about the workshop.
