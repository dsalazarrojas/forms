# Customer Insights Academy Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Insights Academy Registration Form is designed to collect information about individuals who wish to participate in the Customer Insights Academy, a platform providing valuable insights and knowledge on customer behavior and preferences. This form helps us understand your interests, goals, and background to tailor our resources and services to better support your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the "Participant Details" section (page 1).
2. Provide your registration details in the "Registration Details" section (page 2).
3. Add any additional comments or notes about your participation in the "Notes" section (page 3).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, optional):
  This section is where you can provide your personal details, such as your name, email, and any other information you'd like to share about yourself.
* **Registration Details** (`registration_details`, text, optional):
  In this section, please provide your registration details, including your role, department, and company, so we can better understand your background and interests.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional):
  Here, you can add any additional comments or questions you have about the Customer Insights Academy. We're always looking for ways to improve, so please let us know if there's anything we can do differently to support you!

**Tips**

* Please fill in your details accurately to ensure we can contact you with relevant information and resources.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
* We'll get back to you shortly after submitting your form to confirm your registration and provide further instructions.
