# urban-guest-experience-lab-registration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a workshop at the Urban Guest Experience Lab. It collects necessary details from each guest to ensure a successful and organized experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before proceeding.
2. Fill out the required fields (marked as "required") completely and accurately.
3. For fields with dropdown options, select the most relevant option for your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Information** (`user_information`, text, optional): This section is intended to provide general information about you.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required): Enter your contact information to enable us to reach out to you regarding your workshop registration.
- **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required): Select the date you wish to register for the workshop.
- **Registration Time** (`registration_time`, time, required): Choose the time slot you prefer for the workshop.
- **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number to enable us to reach out to you.
- **Message** (`message`, note, optional): This is an opportunity for you to provide any additional comments or questions.
- **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, required): Choose 'Yes' if you can attend the workshop, and 'No' otherwise.
- **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, optional): Select all options that apply to you.
- **Registration Status** (`registration_status`, select_one, required): Choose the status of your registration.
- **Registration Status Options** (`registration_status_options`, select_one, optional): Choose the status of your registration from the options provided.
- **Workshop Title** (`workshop_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the workshop you wish to register for.
- **Workshop Description** (`workshop_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the workshop that you wish to register for.

## Tips
* Ensure the workshop title is accurately entered as it appears on your workshop registration materials.
* If your workshop registration is complete, select 'Registration Complete' in the Registration Status Options.
* If you have any questions or comments, feel free to use the Message field to communicate them to us.
