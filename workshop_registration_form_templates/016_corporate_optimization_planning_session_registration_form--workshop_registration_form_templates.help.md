# Corporate Optimization Planning Session Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from individuals who wish to attend a corporate optimization planning session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your details:
	* Enter your name in the "Name" field.
	* Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
	* Enter your company name in the "Company" field.
2. Describe your goals and objectives:
	* In the "Goals" field, write a brief description of what you hope to achieve or accomplish during the session.
3. Provide your preferences:
	* In the "Preferences" field, let us know any specific preferences or requirements you may have for the session.
4. Review and submit:
	* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (text, required):
	+ Your name, which will be used to address you during the session.
* **Email** (email, required):
	+ Your email address, which will be used for correspondence and communication.
* **Company** (text, required):
	+ The name of the company you represent.
* **Goals** (text, required):
	+ A brief description of your goals and objectives for the session.
* **Preferences** (text, required):
	+ Any specific preferences or requirements you may have for the session.

Note: The required fields are marked as such to help ensure we get accurate and complete information from each attendee. If a field is marked as required, it is essential that you fill it in for your submission to be accepted.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill in all required fields accurately to ensure proper communication and planning for the session.
- You can enter as much or as little information as you feel comfortable with, but please keep in mind that we need some basic information to plan and facilitate the session effectively.
