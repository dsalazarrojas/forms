# Smart Lodging Technology Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from workshop registrants to ensure smooth workshop organization and facilitation. It gathers details on registrant's preferences, interests, and technical skills for a better workshop experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the User Information fields with your details (name, contact number, etc.)
2. Choose your session interests (Hotel, Hospitality, or Lodging Technology)
3. Enter any special requirements you might have for the workshop
4. Provide your phone number
5. Enter your email address
6. Write any additional comments or notes
7. Select a session date for the workshop
8. Choose your arrival and departure dates
9. Select your arrival and departure times
10. Choose your assigned tool from the provided options (e.g., chatjimmy or chatbuster)
11. Select the category for your workshop registration (Workshop Registration Form Templates, Hotel and Hospitality Templates, or Lodging Templates)
12. Enter a brief description of your workshop or project
13. If required, fill out any additional fields for assigned tools 2 and form IDs 2

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information**: Fill out your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Session Interests**: Choose one or more from the options (Hotel Technology, Hospitality Technology, or Lodging Technology) that interest you.
* **Special Requirements**: Mention any specific requirements you have for the workshop (e.g., wheelchair accessibility).
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact number for us to reach out to you.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for workshop updates.
* **Additional Information**: Provide any additional comments or notes about your workshop experience.
* **Session**: Select a date for the workshop session you wish to attend.
* **Arrival Date**: Choose your arrival date at the workshop.
* **Arrival Time**: Select your arrival time at the workshop.
* **Departure Date**: Choose your departure date from the workshop.
* **Departure Time**: Select your departure time from the workshop.
* **Assigned Tool**: Choose one of the provided tool options (chatjimmy or chatbuster).
* **Form ID**: Enter a unique ID for your workshop registration (optional).
* **Category**: Select the category for your workshop registration (Workshop Registration Form Templates, Hotel and Hospitality Templates, or Lodging Templates).
* **Description**: Enter a brief description of your workshop or project.
* **Output File**: Provide the output file for your workshop registration (optional).
* **Assigned Tool 2**: Enter the name of the assigned tool 2 (optional).
* **Form Id 2**: Enter a unique ID for your workshop registration 2 (optional).
* **Category 2**: Select the category 2 for your workshop registration 2 (optional).
