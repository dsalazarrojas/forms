# raffle_participation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information for a raffle drawing. Participants are asked to provide their name, contact email, phone number, and contribution amount (if any) in order to participate. Once the form is submitted, the participant's details will be stored and they will be contacted for further information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with a asterisk (*) below.
2. Enter your participant information accurately and thoroughly.
3. Review your submission before clicking the submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_name** (Participant Name, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **participant_email** (Participant Email, email, required): Enter a valid email address that you use regularly.
* **participant_phone** (Participant Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **contribution_amount** (Contribution Amount, number, optional): If you're making a contribution to participate, please enter the amount you're contributing. This field is optional and is not required for participation.
* **participant_info** (Participant Info, text, optional): Please provide any additional information about yourself that you'd like to share with us.
* **participant_phone** should be filled in only if you wish to be contacted through phone. Please be aware that your contact number will be used only for further information and will not be used for marketing purposes. 

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date contact information so that we can reach you with further updates.
* If you're having trouble filling out the form, do not hesitate to contact us for assistance. 
* If you're participating in multiple raffles, please fill out a separate form for each raffle.
