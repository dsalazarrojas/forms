5. Enter the name of your organization in the "Organization Name" field.
6. Select the field in which you work (e.g., Healthcare, Technology, Research, Education, Government, or Other) in the "Industry Sector" field.
7. Rate your current expertise level with AI technologies (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert) in the "Current Expertise Level with AI" field.
8. If applicable, specify the years of experience you have with diagnostic systems in the "Experience with Diagnostic Tools" field.
9. In the "Expected Learning Outcomes" field, describe what you hope to gain from this workshop.
10. If you have any food allergies or dietary requirements, please specify them in the "Dietary Restrictions" field.
11. If you require any accommodations for participation, please describe them in the "Accessibility Needs" field.
12. Finally, confirm your consent for data use in the "Consent for Data Use" field.
