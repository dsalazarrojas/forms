# legal_compliance_workshop_attendance_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register your interest in attending a legal compliance workshop. It will help us track your attendance and provide you with information about the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the registration information section with your contact details.
2. Provide your attendance details, such as the date and time you plan to attend.
3. Select one option from the "Select One" field, which might be your preferred option or role within the workshop.
4. Choose multiple options from the "Select Multiple" field, which might be relevant to your attendance or participation in the workshop.
5. If you have any additional notes or comments, you can add them to the "Note" fields.
6. Finally, review your information and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Info (1)**: Enter your registration details, such as your name, organization, or other relevant information.
* **Contact Info (2)**: Provide your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
* **Attendance Info (3)**: Enter the date and time you plan to attend the workshop.
* **Date (4)**: Enter the date you plan to attend the workshop.
* **Time (5)**: Enter the time you plan to attend the workshop.
* **Location (6)**: Enter the location of the workshop, if applicable.
* **Organization (7)**: Enter the name of your organization.
* **Role (8)**: Select the role you will be playing at the workshop.
* **Email (9)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (10)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Select One (11)**: Choose one option that best describes your reason for attending the workshop.
* **Select Multiple (12)**: Choose multiple options that apply to your attendance or participation in the workshop.
* **Note (13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your attendance or participation in the workshop.
