# innovation_in_preservation_forum_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The innovation_in_preservation_forum_registration_form is used to register your details for the Innovation in Preservation forum.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the required and optional fields as instructed.
2. Be sure to review your responses for accuracy before submission.
3. If you have comments or additional information, use the provided fields to communicate them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your name?** (`general_details`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **How can we contact you?** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): Provide your contact information so we can reach out to you regarding forum updates and interactions.
* **What is your phone number?** (`phone_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number as it is currently listed in your official records.
* **Do you have any comments about this form?** (`comments`, note, required/optional): Share any additional comments or feedback you may have about the form or the forum.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name again for reference purposes.
* **How can we reach you?** (`contact_method`, text, required/optional): Specify how you would like to be contacted (e.g., email, phone, etc.).
* **Is this your phone number?** (`phone_verification`, checkbox, required/optional): Confirm that the phone number you provided is accurate and yours.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or information that you would like to share.
