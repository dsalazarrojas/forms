**Digital Ethics For Educators Registration - Help Guide**
=============================================

## Purpose
----------------

This form is designed to collect your registration details for the Digital Ethics For Educators workshop. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
-------------------------

### Step 1: Review the form carefully before starting to fill it out.

### Step 2: Ensure you have all the necessary details ready, including your name, email, phone number, and any other requested information.

### Step 3: Enter your details into the form accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
-------------------------

* **Workshop Registration Form**:
  * `title`: Digital Ethics For Educators Registration
  * `name`: Enter your name as you would like it to appear in the workshop's records.
  * `email`: Enter your valid email address where you can be reached for workshop updates and notifications.
  * `phone`: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for workshop-related matters.
  * `select_one_organizer`: Please select whether you will be attending as a single or multiple organizer.
  * `select_multiple_organizers`: Please select whether you will be attending as a single or multiple organizer. 
  * `date`: Enter the date of the workshop you are registering for.
  * `time`: Enter the time of the workshop you are registering for.
  * `note`: Enter any additional notes or comments you have about your registration.

## Tips
---------

* Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly to avoid any issues during the registration process.
* If you encounter any difficulties or have questions, please contact the workshop organizers for assistance.
* Review your form carefully before submitting it to ensure everything is complete and correct.
