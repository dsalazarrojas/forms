# Agile Enterprise Transformation Course Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in attending the Agile Enterprise Transformation course. It will help the course administrators to understand the participant's background, preferences, and expectations for the course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Participant Full Name, which is your full name as it appears on your ID.
2. Enter your Participant Email address, which will be used for course communication.
3. Provide your Participant Phone number for future contact.
4. Enter your current Job Title and the name of your Company.
5. Select your Company's Industry from the provided list.
6. Enter your Years of Professional Experience.
7. Choose your Agile Experience Level from the options provided.
8. Indicate whether you currently hold a Leadership Role.
9. Enter the name of the Agile Enterprise Transformation course you are interested in.
10. Select your preferred session dates from the available options.
11. Choose how you would like to attend the course (In-Person, Virtual, or Hybrid).
12. If attending in-person, select your preferred location from the options provided (if applicable).
13. Identify your Primary Learning Goals for attending the course.
14. Specify the specific challenges you are facing in your organization's Agile transformation (if applicable).
15. State your expected outcomes from the course.
16. If applicable, specify any special accommodations you need.
17. If applicable, indicate any dietary restrictions for course meals.
18. Indicate your interest in pursuing certification after the course.
19. Finally, enter any additional comments or information you would like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name** (`participant_full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for course communication.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for future contact.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your current job title.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Company Industry** (`company_industry`, select_one, required): Select your company's industry from the provided list.
* **Years of Professional Experience** (`years_of_experience`, number, required): Enter how many years of professional experience you have.
* **Agile Experience Level** (`agile_experience_level`, select_one, required): Choose your current level of experience with Agile methodologies.
* **Leadership Role** (`leadership_role`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you currently hold a leadership position.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the Agile Enterprise Transformation course you are interested in.
* **Preferred Session Dates** (`preferred_session_dates`, select_multiple, required): Select your preferred dates for the course from the available options.
* **Attendance Format** (`attendance_format`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to attend the course (In-Person, Virtual, or Hybrid).
* **Location Preference** (`location_preference`, select_one, required): If attending in-person, select your preferred location from the options provided (if applicable).
* **Primary Learning Goals** (`primary_learning_goals`, select_multiple, required): Identify your primary goals for attending the course.
* **Specific Challenges** (`specific_challenges`, text, optional): If applicable, specify the specific challenges you are facing in your organization's Agile transformation.
* **Expected Outcomes** (`expected_outcomes`, text, required): State your expected outcomes from the course.
* **Special Accommodations** (`special_accommodations`, text, optional): If applicable, specify any special accommodations you need.
