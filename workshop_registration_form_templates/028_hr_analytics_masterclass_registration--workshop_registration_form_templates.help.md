# HR Analytics Masterclass Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Analytics Masterclass Registration form is used to collect information from participants for internal organization and communication purposes. It is not a public-facing form and its responses are not submitted to any third party.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select whether you are attending the workshop by choosing between 'Yes' and 'No' in the first field.
* Fill in your name and job title accurately and truthfully.
* Indicate your experience level by choosing between 'High' and 'Low'.
* Choose your preferred session preferences by selecting 'Yes' and 'No'.
* Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you with updates and information.
* Enter your phone number to enable us to reach you effectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attending Workshop** (`workshop_registration_form_template`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field allows you to decide if you are attending the workshop or not. Choose 'Yes' if you plan to attend, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Name** (name, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed for communication purposes.
* **Job Title** (job_title, text, required/optional): Please enter your job title as you would like it to be displayed for communication purposes.
* **Experience Level** (experience_level, select_one, required/optional): Choose between 'High' and 'Low' to indicate your level of experience with HR Analytics.
* **Session Preferences** (session_preferences, select_multiple, required/optional): Select your preferred session preferences by choosing 'Yes' or 'No'. This will help us tailor the workshop content and structure to your needs.
* **Email** (email, email, required/optional): Enter your email address accurately to receive updates, information, and communication about the workshop.
* **Phone** (phone, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you with any last-minute updates or reminders.
