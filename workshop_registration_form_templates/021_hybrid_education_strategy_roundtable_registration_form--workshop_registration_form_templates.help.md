# Hybrid Education Strategy Roundtable Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from participants interested in attending the Hybrid Education Strategy Roundtable. Completing this form helps us organize and facilitate a productive discussion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the `participant_name` field.
2. Provide your email address in the `email` field.
3. Enter your phone number in the `phone` field.
4. Select a time that works for you in the `time` field.
5. Choose a date for the roundtable in the `date` field.
6. Choose one option from the `select_one` field.
7. Select all applicable options from the `select_multiple` field.
8. Add any additional comments or notes about your participation in the `note` field.
9. Click the `submit` button to finalize your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Registration Form** (`workshop_registration_form`): This is the main form where you can enter your registration information.
* **participant_name** (`participant_name`): Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed at the roundtable.
* **email** (`email`): Enter your valid email address where you can be reached.
* **phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **time** (`time`): Select a time that works for you to attend the roundtable.
* **date** (`date`): Choose a date for the roundtable.
* **select_one** (`select_one`): Select one option from the list.
* **select_multiple** (`select_multiple`): Select all applicable options from the list.
* **note** (`note`): Enter any additional comments or notes about your participation.
* **submit** (`submit`): Click this button to finalize your registration.
