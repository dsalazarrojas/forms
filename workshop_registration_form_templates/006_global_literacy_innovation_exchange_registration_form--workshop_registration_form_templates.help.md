# Global Literacy Innovation Exchange Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Global Literacy Innovation Exchange Registration Form is designed to collect information from participants for the Global Literacy Innovation Exchange event. This form is necessary for registering participants and ensuring that we have all the necessary details for the event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Email Address, which is a required field.
2. Enter your Participant Phone number, if you want to be contacted for any updates or reminders.
3. Select the Date of the Event when you will be attending.
4. Choose your Participant Location and Time Zone.
5. Select your Country of origin.
6. Choose your method of Registration, i.e., Online, On-Site, or by Phone.
7. Enter any additional Participant information that you feel is relevant.
8. Click on the Submit button to save your registration.
9. Fill in the Payment Amount and select your preferred Payment Method.
10. Confirm your payment details.
11. Enter the Event Date when the event will take place.
12. Enter the Event Time when the event will take place.
13. Choose the Event Location where the event will be held.
14. Enter any Event Details that you think are important.
15. Enter any Participant Notes that you want to share with the organizers.
16. Sign the Participant Agreement, which outlines the terms and conditions of the event.
17. Sign your Participant Agreement with your signature.
18. Enter the date when you signed the agreement.
19. Enter your name as the person signing the agreement.
20. Enter the date and time when you signed the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Email Address** (email, required): This is your email address where we will send you updates and reminders.
* **Participant Phone** (text, optional): If you want to be contacted for any updates or reminders.
* **Date of Event** (date, required): The date when the event will take place.
* **Participant Location** (text, required): The location where you will be participating in the event.
* **Time Zone** (select one, optional): The time zone you are in.
* **Country** (select multiple, required): Your country of origin.
* **Registration Method** (select one, optional): How you want to register, i.e., Online, On-Site, or by Phone.
* **Participant Additional Info** (text, optional): Any additional information you feel is relevant.
* **Submit** (text, required): Click this button to save your registration.
* **Payment Amount** (number, required): The amount you are willing to pay for the event.
* **Payment Method** (select one, required): The method of payment you prefer.
* **Confirm Payment** (text, optional): Confirm your payment details.
* **Event Date** (date, required): The date when the event will take place.
* **Event Time** (time, required): The time when the event will take place.
* **Event Location** (text, required): The location where the event will be held.
* **Event Details** (text, optional): Any important details about the event.
* **Participant Notes** (text, optional): Any notes you want to share with the organizers.
* **Participant Agreement** (text, required): This is a required field, but it is actually the **Participant Agreement Terms**, not just any agreement.
* **Participant Signature** (text, required): Sign your name to agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Participant Agreement Terms** (text, required): This is actually the **Participant Agreement**, not terms.
* **Participant Agreement Date** (date, required): The date when you signed the agreement.
* **Participant Agreement Sign** (text, required): Sign your name to agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Participant Agreement Signed By** (text, required): Enter your name as the person signing the agreement.
* **Participant Agreement Signed At** (date, required): The date and time when you signed the agreement.
