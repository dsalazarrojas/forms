# Digital Merchandising Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Merchandising Workshop Registration form is used to register a workshop with the following details:

* Session name and type
* Start and end dates for single or multiple sessions
* Location and contact information
* List of required attendees with their contact details

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the session type: Single Session or Multiple Session
2. Enter the session name and details
3. Choose the start and end dates for the session
4. Enter the location of the workshop
5. Provide the contact person's information
6. List the required attendees with their contact details

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Name** (`session_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the workshop session.
* **Session Type** (`session_type`, select_one, required): Choose between Single Session or Multiple Session.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required for Single Session): Enter the start date of the session.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required for Single Session): Enter the end date of the session.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the workshop session.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location where the workshop will be held.
* **Organizer** (`organizer`, text, required): Enter the name of the person organizing the workshop.
* **Attendees** (`attendees`, note, required): List all required attendees separated by commas.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): Enter the organization of the contact person.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title of the contact person.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department of the contact person.
* **Required Attendee** (`required_attendee`, text, required): List the name of the required attendee separated by commas.
* **Required Attendee Phone** (`required_attendee_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the required attendee.
* **Required Attendee Email** (`required_attendee_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the required attendee.
* **Session ID** (`session_id`, text, optional): Enter a unique identifier for the workshop session.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the workshop session.
* **Contact Title** (`contact_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the contact person.
* **Required Attendee** (`required_attendee`, text, optional): This field is not used as it is a duplicate of `required_attendee`.
* **Required Attendee Phone** (`required_attendee_phone`, text, optional): This field is not used as it is a duplicate of `required_attendee_phone`.
* **Required Attendee Email** (`required_attendee_email`, email, optional): This field is not used as it is a duplicate of `required_attendee_email`.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled accurately and completely.
* Review the form for any errors before submitting.
* If you are registering multiple sessions, ensure that you provide the start and end dates for each session.
