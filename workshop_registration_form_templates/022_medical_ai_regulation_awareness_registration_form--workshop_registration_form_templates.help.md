# Medical AI Regulation Awareness Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from attendees for the Medical AI Regulation Awareness Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Provide your attendee details, including your role in the workshop.
2.  Confirm your consent to participate in the workshop by selecting 'Yes' in the 'Consent' field.
3.  Enter your contact number.
4.  Enter your email address.
5.  Confirm your medical licensing status by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' accordingly.
6.  Indicate your attendance status at the workshop.
7.  If you have any special needs or requirements, please specify in the 'Special Needs' field.
8.  Provide any additional information that you think would be helpful for the organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Attendee Details (ID 1)**: This field is for your personal details, including your name or title.
*   **Role (ID 2)**: This field is for you to select whether you are a healthcare professional, researcher, or another relevant role for the workshop.
*   **Expectations (ID 3)**: This field is where you can specify any special expectations you may have for the workshop content or any specific topics you'd like to cover.
*   **Consent (ID 4)**: Please select 'Yes' to confirm that you're aware of and in compliance with relevant medical regulations.
*   **Contact Number (ID 5)**: This field is for your contact number that the workshop organizers may reach out to you with further questions or for any clarification.
*   **Email (ID 6)**: Enter your email address so that we can keep you updated about the workshop and any additional information you may need.
*   **Medical Licensing (ID 7)**: This field is to confirm whether you have medical licensing or not. Please select 'Yes' or 'No' accordingly.
*   **Workshop Attendance (ID 8)**: Please select 'Yes' if you'll be attending the workshop.
*   **Special Needs (ID 9)**: If you have any special requirements or needs, please specify in this field.
*   **Additional Information (ID 10)**: This field is for any additional information you'd like to provide about yourself or your participation in the workshop.

## Tips

*   Please fill out this form with accurate and relevant information to ensure that you get the most out of the workshop.
*   If you're unsure about any of the fields, please don't hesitate to reach out to the workshop organizers for clarification.
