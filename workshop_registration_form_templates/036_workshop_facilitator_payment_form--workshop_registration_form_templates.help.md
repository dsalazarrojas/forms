# Workshop Facilitator Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workshop Facilitator Payment Form is a critical document that helps workshop facilitators provide accurate and timely payment information for their workshops. This form ensures that payment details are correctly recorded and processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you are a registered facilitator and have a clear understanding of the workshop details.
2. Select "Yes" for "Facilitator Information" (Field 2) to indicate that you are submitting payment information for yourself or your organization.
3. Enter the workshop name in the "Workshop Name" field (Field 3).
4. Enter your name as the facilitator in the "Facilitator Name" field (Field 4).
5. Enter the payment amount in the "Payment Amount" field (Field 5).
6. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Payment Method" (Field 6) to indicate whether payment is received or not.
7. Enter any additional payment terms or conditions in the "Payment Terms" field (Field 7).
8. Provide a brief description of the workshop in the "Workshop Description" field (Field 8).
9. Enter the workshop date in the "Workshop Date" field (Field 9).
10. Enter the workshop time in the "Workshop Time" field (Field 10).
11. Enter the workshop location in the "Workshop Location" field (Field 11).
12. Enter the workshop email address in the "Workshop Email" field (Field 12).
13. Enter your email address in the "Facilitator Email" field (Field 13).
14. Enter your phone number in the "Facilitator Phone" field (Field 14).
15. Enter the workshop address in the "Workshop Address" field (Field 15).
16. Enter the postal code for the workshop location in the "Workshop Postal Code" field (Field 16).
17. Enter the workshop country in the "Workshop Country" field (Field 17).
18. Enter the workshop state in the "Workshop State" field (Field 18).
19. Enter the workshop city in the "Workshop City" field (Field 19).
20. Enter the workshop zip code in the "Workshop Zip" field (Field 20).
21. Enter any additional workshop notes in the "Workshop Notes" field (Field 21).
22. Select "Active" or "Inactive" for "Payment Status" (Field 22) to indicate the current payment status.
23. Enter the payment date in the "Payment Date" field (Field 23).
24. Enter the name of the person submitting the form in the "Form Submitted By" field (Field 24).
25. Enter a reference for the payment in the "Payment Reference" field (Field 25).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payment Information (Field 1):** The section where you enter payment details for your workshop.
	+ **Payment Amount (Field 5):** Enter the total amount paid for the workshop.
	+ **Payment Method (Field 6):** Select "Yes" if payment has been received, "No" otherwise.
* **Facilitator Information (Field 2):** Confirm that you are a registered facilitator or represent the organization.
* **Workshop Name (Field 3):** Enter the name of the workshop.
* **Facilitator Name (Field 4):** Enter your name as the facilitator.
* **Payment Terms (Field 7):** Enter any additional payment terms or conditions.
* **Workshop Description (Field 8):** Provide a brief description of the workshop.
* **Workshop Date (Field 9):** Enter the date of the workshop.
* **Workshop Time (Field 10):** Enter the time of the workshop.
* **Workshop Location (Field 11):** Enter the location of the workshop.
* **Workshop Email (Field 12):** Enter the email address of the workshop.
* **Facilitator Email (Field 13):** Enter your email address.
* **Facilitator Phone (Field 14):** Enter your phone number.
* **Workshop Address (Field 15):** Enter the address of the workshop location.
* **Workshop Postal Code (Field 16):** Enter the postal code for the workshop location.
* **Workshop Country (Field 17):** Enter the country of the workshop location.
* **Workshop State (Field 18):** Enter the state of the workshop location.
* **Workshop City (Field 19):** Enter the city of the workshop location.
* **Workshop Zip (Field 20):** Enter the zip code of the workshop location.
* **Workshop Notes (Field 21):** Enter any additional notes about the workshop.
* **Payment Status (Field 22):** Select "Active" if payment is active, "Inactive" otherwise.
* **Payment Date (Field 23):** Enter the date of payment.
* **Form Submitted By (Field 24):** Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Payment Reference (Field 25):** Enter a reference for the payment.

## Tips
When filling out this form, please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and truthfully. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your workshop administrator for guidance.
