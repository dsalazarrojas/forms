# HR Professional Certification Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register HR professional certifications for tracking and record-keeping purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details (Form Details, Name, Surname, Email, Phone).
2. Provide your certification details (Certification Date, Certification Type, Certification Number, Certification Name, Status of Certification, Expiration Date, Expiration Status of Certification).
3. Select the certifying body for each certification (Certifying Body, Certifying Body 2, etc.).
4. Fill in any other details required (Certification Status, Other Certification Status, Other Certifying Body, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Details** (`id: 1`): This field is for entering the title of this form, which is already pre-filled as "HR Professional Certification Registration Form". You can leave it as is.
* **Name** (`id: 2`): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Surname** (`id: 3`): Enter your surname as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`id: 4`): Enter your email address as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Phone** (`id: 5`): Enter your phone number as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Certification Date** (`id: 6`): Enter the date you obtained your certification.
* **Certification Type** (`id: 7`): Select the type of certification you have (e.g. Certified HR Professional, Certified Recruiter, etc.).
* **Certification Number** (`id: 8`): Enter the number assigned to your certification.
* **Certification Name** (`id: 9`): Enter the name of your certification.
* **Status of Certification** (`id: 10`): Select the status of your certification (e.g. Active, Inactive).
* **Other Certification Status** (`id: 11`): Enter any other status of your certification if not listed above.
* **Certifying Body** (`id: 12`): Enter the name of the certifying body for your certification.
* **Certification ID** (`id: 13`): Enter the ID number assigned to your certification.
* **Other Certifying Body** (`id: 14`): Enter any other certifying body for your certification if not listed above.
* **Certifying Body Other 2** (`id: 15`): Enter any other certifying body for your certification if not listed above.
* **Date of Certification 2** (`id: 16`): Enter the date of your second certification.
* **Expiration Date** (`id: 17`): Enter the date your certification expires.
* **Status of Certification 2** (`id: 18`): Select the status of your second certification (e.g. Active, Inactive).
* **Certifying Body 2** (`id: 19`): Enter the name of the certifying body for your second certification.
* **Certifying Body Other 3** (`id: 20`): Enter any other certifying body for your second certification if not listed above.
* **Expiration Status of Certification** (`id: 21`): Select the expiration status of your second certification (e.g. Active, Inactive).
* **Other Expiration Status of Certification** (`id: 22`): Enter any other status of your certification if not listed above.
* **Certifying Body 2 2** (`id: 23`): Enter any other certifying body for your second certification if not listed above.
* **Other Certifying Body 2** (`id: 24`): Enter any other certifying body for your second certification if not listed above.
* **Certifying Body 2_2** (`id: 25`): Enter any other certifying body for your second certification if not listed above.
