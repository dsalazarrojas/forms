# Pottery Workshop Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a pottery workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your registration details, including the registration date, instructor name, and class time.
2. Enter your student name and contact information, including email and phone number.
3. Choose a payment method, such as cash, credit/debit, PayPal, or bank transfer.
4. Select the status of your payment, such as paid, not paid, or partially paid.
5. Choose the status of your booking, such as confirmed, pending, or canceled.
6. Provide any additional information about the workshop, such as the duration and location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required): Enter the date you want to register for the workshop.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the instructor who will be leading the workshop.
* **Class Time** (`class_time`, text, required): Enter the time of the class.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your name as a student.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose a payment method, such as cash, credit/debit, PayPal, or bank transfer.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, required): Choose the status of your payment, such as paid, not paid, or partially paid.
* **Booking Status** (`booking_status`, select_one, required): Choose the status of your booking, such as confirmed, pending, or canceled.
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the workshop.
* **Registration Date Range** (`registration_date_range`, date, required): Enter a date range for the workshop.
* **Workshop Schedules** (`workshop_schedules`, text, required): Enter any additional workshop schedules.
* **Workshop Duration** (`workshop_duration`, text, required): Enter the duration of the workshop.
* **Instructor** (`instructor`, text, required): Enter the name of the instructor.
* **Workshop Capacity** (`workshop_capacity`, text, required): Enter the capacity of the workshop.
* **Total Participants** (`total_participants`, text, required): Enter the total number of participants.
* **Registration Fee** (`registration_fee`, text, required): Enter the registration fee.
* **Fee Status** (`fee_status`, text, required): Enter the status of the registration fee.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the payment.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Enter any additional notes.
* **Student Capacity** (`student_capacity`, text, required): Enter the student capacity.
* **Workshop Address** (`workshop_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the workshop.
* **Workshop Location** (`workshop_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the workshop.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Payment Total** (`payment_total`, number, required): Enter the total payment amount.

Note: Please fill out all required fields to ensure that your workshop registration is processed correctly.
