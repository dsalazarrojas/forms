# Sustainable Food Systems Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sustainable Food Systems Workshop Registration Form is designed to collect participant information for a workshop on sustainable food systems. This form helps us to understand who will be attending the workshop and tailor the experience accordingly.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow the steps outlined above in the internal reasoning block.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information (registration_form_participant)**: This field is used to gather information about you as a participant. Please provide your details in this field.
* **Which Workshop (workshop_selection)**: Choose one of the available workshops: Workshop A, Workshop B, or Workshop C.
* **Select Dates (workshop_dates)**: Choose one or multiple dates for the workshop that suit you best.
* **Contact Information (contact_information)**: Provide your email address and/or phone number for us to contact you.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address to stay updated on workshop details and schedules.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter your phone number to receive reminders and notifications about the workshop.
* **Additional Notes (note)**: Share any additional notes or comments about your registration or requirements.
