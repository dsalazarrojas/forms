# workforce_digital_literacy_workshop_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant and workshop information for the Workforce Digital Literacy Workshop. It is used to gather essential details about each participant, including their name, contact information, workshop preferences, and more.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form title to describe the workshop.
2. Enter your first and last name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
3. Provide your email and phone number for contact purposes.
4. Select whether you have attended a workshop before.
5. Choose the date and time of the workshop you want to attend.
6. Enter the workshop location.
7. Agree to the participant agreement (yes or no).
8. Provide any additional comments if necessary.
9. Select the workshop facilitators, coordinators, and sponsors.
10. Provide your comments and any additional comments.
11. Verify that the participant agreement form was filled out correctly.
12. Add your participant comments, if needed.
13. Enter your name for signature.
14. Finally, review your submission and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title (1)**: A title for the workshop, e.g. "Workshop Title".
* **Participant First Name (2)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Participant Last Name (3)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email (4)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone (5)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Workshop Type (6)**: Select whether you have attended a workshop before.
* **Workshop Date (7)**: Choose the date of the workshop you want to attend.
* **Workshop Time (8)**: Select the time of the workshop you want to attend.
* **Notes (9)**: Enter any additional comments or information about the workshop.
* **Workshop Location (10)**: Enter the location of the workshop.
* **Participant Agreement (11)**: Select 'Yes' to agree to the participant agreement.
* **Participant Agreement Other (12)**: Enter any other information about the participant agreement.
* **Participant Agreement Other Hint (13)**: A hint for the participant agreement other field.
* **Confirm Participant Agreement (14)**: Select 'Yes' to confirm the participant agreement.
* **Workshop Facilitator (15)**: Enter the name of the workshop facilitator.
* **Workshop Coordinators (16)**: Enter the names of the workshop coordinators.
* **Workshop Sponsor (17)**: Enter the name of the workshop sponsor.
* **Sponsored by (18)**: Enter the sponsor's name (if any).
* **Organizers (19)**: Enter the names of the workshop organizers.
* **Participant Comments (20)**: Enter any comments from the participant.
* **Workshop Comments (21)**: Enter any comments about the workshop.
* **Additional Comments (22)**: Enter any additional comments about the workshop.
* **Participant Additional Comments (23)**: Enter any additional comments from the participant.
* **Form Submitted By (24)**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Participant Signature (25)**: Enter your signature as a participant.
