# Entrepreneurship Workshop Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Entrepreneurship Workshop Attendance Form is a registration form used to collect information from participants who plan to attend the workshop. It helps the event organizers to keep track of registered participants and their details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Registration Info field with your relevant details.
2. Provide your Participant Name and Participant Email for identification purposes.
3. Enter your Participant Phone number for contact purposes.
4. Choose your Participant Interest from the options provided.
5. Add any additional notes about yourself in the Participant Notes field.
6. Enter your Registration Info Participant Id (if you have one).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Info**: Enter your relevant registration information such as your name, email, or other details that you feel are necessary to share with the event organizers.
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name, which will be used to identify you as a participant in the workshop.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address, which will be used for communication purposes with the event organizers and other participants.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number, which will be used for contact purposes.
* **Workshop Date**: This field is not required but if you know the workshop date, you can fill it in to help the event organizers with planning.
* **Participant Interest**: Select one of the options that best describes your interest in the workshop.
* **Participant Notes**: This field is for any additional information you want to share with the event organizers. You can use it to provide more details about your background, expectations, or anything else you think is relevant.
* **Registration Info Participant Id**: Enter your participant ID if you have one, which will help the event organizers to identify you as a registered participant.
