# nonprofit_tech_skills_training_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Nonprofit Tech Skills Training Registration Form is designed to collect information from participants interested in attending nonprofit tech skills training sessions. This form helps us understand the participant's skills, interests, and availability to better tailor the training sessions to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the form submission page, which will ask for your name, email, phone number, and other basic information.
2.  On the second page, select your skill level, which will help us understand your current level of expertise.
3.  On the third page, select the skills you are interested in learning more about, as well as the topics you are interested in.
4.  On the fourth page, select the specific workshops you are interested in attending.
5.  Finally, provide any additional comments or notes you'd like to share with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Form Submission** (`form_submission`, note, required: false): This is the introduction page where you can provide any additional information about your participation in the training session.
*   **Participant Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Participant Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
*   **Participant Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number where we can reach you.
*   **Skill Level 1** (`skill_level_1`, select_one, required: false): Select your current level of expertise in a specific skill, such as beginner, intermediate, advanced, or expert.
*   **Skill Level 2** (`skill_level_2`, select_one, required: false): Choose your level of expertise in another skill.
*   **Skill Level 3** (`skill_level_3`, select_one, required: false): Select your current level of expertise in yet another skill.
*   **Skill Level 4** (`skill_level_4`, select_one, required: false): Select your level of expertise in yet another skill.
*   **Workshop Skills** (`skills_workshop`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the skills you are interested in learning more about.
*   **Topic Interests** (`topic_interests`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the topics you are interested in exploring.
*   **Workshop Topics** (`workshop_topics`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the specific topics you are interested in attending.
*   **Workshop Date/Time** (`workshop_date_time`, date/time, required: false): Select a date and time that works best for you to attend.
*   **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, time, required: false): Choose the duration of the workshop you prefer.
*   **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required: false): If you have a preferred tool or technology, please let us know.
*   **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, required: false): This is an internal field and should be left blank.
*   **Output File** (`output_file`, text, required: false): Upload any relevant files or documents that support your registration.
*   **Category** (`category`, text, required: false): Enter any additional categories you'd like to be considered.
