# Unity Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Unity Event Registration form is used to collect information from users who want to attend or participate in Unity events. This form helps event organizers collect relevant details from attendees, such as their names, email addresses, and roles within the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields:
	* First name
	* Email
2. Select your interests and accessibility needs for the event
3. Choose your role in the event (e.g., participant, organizer, or volunteer)
4. Confirm your email address, name, and role
5. Confirm your interest in attending the event

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your official email address.
* **Session Interests** (`attendee_session_interests`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the sessions you are interested in attending by choosing 'Yes' for each relevant session.
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any accessibility needs you have by choosing 'Yes' for each applicable need.
* **Role** (`role`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your role in the event (e.g., participant, organizer, or volunteer).
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, `select_one`, optional): If applicable, choose the type of event (e.g., conference, meetup, or workshop).
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the event if it's been determined by the event organizer.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the event if it's been determined by the event organizer.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or information about the event.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm that your email address is correct.
* **Confirm Name** (`confirm_name`, `text`, optional): Confirm your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Confirm Role** (`confirm_role`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm your role in the event.
* **Confirm Event Type** (`confirm_event_type`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm the type of event.
* **Confirm Date** (`confirm_date`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm the date of the event.
* **Confirm Time** (`confirm_time`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm the time of the event.
