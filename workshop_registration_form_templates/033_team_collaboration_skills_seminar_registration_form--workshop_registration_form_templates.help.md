# Team Collaboration Skills Seminar Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for registering teams for the Team Collaboration Skills Seminar.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your team's first name and last name.
2. Enter your team's email address and phone number.
3. Select the seminar date and time that suits your team best.
4. Choose the number of team members that will be attending.
5. Provide your team's name, lead's name, and role.
6. Add any additional information you want to share about your team.
7. Review the form carefully and make sure all information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required: false): Enter your team's first name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required: false): Enter your team's last name.
* **Email** (3, email, required: false): Enter your team's email address.
* **Phone** (4, text, required: false): Enter your team's phone number.
* **Date** (5, date, required: false): Select the seminar date that suits your team best.
* **Time** (6, time, required: false): Select the seminar time that suits your team best.
* **Select One** (7, select_one, required: false): Choose the option that best describes your team's registration status.
* **Select Multiple** (8, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the options that best describe your team's interests and needs.
* **Note** (9, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about your team.
* **Seminar Date** (10, date, required: false): Enter the date of the seminar.
* **Seminar Time** (11, time, required: false): Enter the time of the seminar.
* **Team Size** (12, number, required: false): Enter the number of team members that will be attending.
* **Team Name** (13, text, required: false): Enter your team's name.
* **Team Lead** (14, text, required: false): Enter your team's lead's name.
* **Team Role** (15, text, required: false): Enter your team's role.
* **Team Description** (16, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of your team.
* **Seminar Location** (17, text, required: false): Enter the location of the seminar.
* **Seminar Facilitator** (18, text, required: false): Enter the facilitator's name for the seminar.
* **Additional info** (19, note, required: false): Add any additional information about your team.
* **Team E-mail** (20, email, required: false): Enter your team's email address.
* **Team Phone** (21, text, required: false): Enter your team's phone number.
* **Registration Form Completed** (22, select_one, required: false): Mark that the registration form has been completed.
* **Hours** (23, number, required: false): Enter the hours spent completing the registration form.
* **Date** (24, date, required: false): Enter the date the registration form was completed.
* **Time** (25, time, required: false): Enter the time the registration form was completed.
