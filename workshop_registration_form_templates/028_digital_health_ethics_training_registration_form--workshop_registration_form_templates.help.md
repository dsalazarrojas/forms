# Digital Health Ethics Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about individuals who are interested in attending digital health ethics training. The training focuses on handling digital health data in an ethical manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
* Provide your Email address for communication and updates.
* Enter your Phone number for contact purposes.
* Select your Date of Birth and Time of Birth.
* Enter your Medical License Number and type.
* Choose your Medical Specialty from the list of options.
* Choose the Training Location where you would like to attend the training.
* Select your preferred Training Language.
* Enter the Training Date and Time if you have been assigned one.
* Add any additional Note if you have any specific requests or requirements.
* Finally, confirm the Training Location and Language.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required)
Your first name is required to complete this form. Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.

### **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required)
Your last name is required to complete this form. Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.

### **Email** (`email`, email, required)
Your email address is required for communication and updates related to the training. Please enter a valid email address that you check regularly.

### **Phone** (`phone`, text, required)
Your phone number is required for contact purposes. Please enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.

### **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required)
Your date of birth is required for age verification purposes. Please enter your date of birth in the format dd/mm/yyyy.

### **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, required)
Your time of birth is required for demographic purposes. Please enter your time of birth in the format 12:00 PM/AM/PM.

### **Medical License Number** (`medical_license_number`, number, required)
Your medical license number is required to complete this form. Please enter your valid medical license number.

### **Medical License Type** (`medical_license_type`, select_multiple, required)
Select the type of medical license you hold. You can choose from:
* Physician
* Nurse
* Other

### **Medical Specialty** (`medical_specialty`, select_one, required)
Choose your medical specialty from the list of options:
* Cardiovascular
* Dermatology
* Emergency Medicine
* Endocrinology
* Gastroenterology
* General Medicine
* Hematology
* Neurology
* Palliative Care
* Rheumatology
* Other

### **Training Location** (`training_location`, select_one, required)
Choose the location where you would like to attend the training. You can choose from:
* Virtual
* On-site
* Other (please specify)

### **Training Language** (`training_language`, select_one, required)
Select your preferred language for the training:
* English
* Spanish
* Other

### **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, required)
Enter the date of the training you have been assigned.

### **Training Time** (`training_time`, time, required)
Enter the time of the training you have been assigned.

### **Note** (`note`, note, optional)
Add any additional note or comment you have regarding the training.

Note: This form is designed to collect the necessary information for the digital health ethics training. Please fill it in accurately and completely to avoid any errors or omissions.
