# Webinar Registration For Leaders - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants about their registration for a webinar, specifically for leaders. It aims to gather their contact details, preferences, and confirmations of their selections.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Full Name and Email address on the first page.
2. Provide your phone number and preferred date and time on the second page.
3. Select your preference on the third page.
4. Confirm your email address and phone number on the fourth page.
5. Re-select your preferred date and time on the fifth page.
6. Confirm your selections on the sixth page.
7. Accept the Terms and Conditions on the last page.
8. Finally, click on the Submit button to submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name**: Enter your full name as you want it to be displayed.
- **Email**: Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number, if you have one.
- **Preferred Date**: Select a date that suits your schedule.
- **Preferred Time**: Select a time that suits your schedule.
- **Select Your Preference**: Choose an option from the list.
- **Select Your Preferred**: Choose multiple options from the list.
- **Additional Note**: Enter any additional information you would like to provide.
- **Confirm Email**: Re-enter your email address to confirm it.
- **Confirm Phone**: Re-enter your phone number to confirm it.
- **Confirm Date**: Re-select your preferred date.
- **Confirm Time**: Re-select your preferred time.
- **Confirm Your Selection**: Re-choose the options you selected earlier.
- **Confirm Your Preferred**: Re-choose the options you selected earlier.
- **Terms and Conditions**: Accept the terms and conditions of the webinar.
- **Submit**: Click the Submit button to complete your registration.
