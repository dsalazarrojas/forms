# Underfloor Heating Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering participants for an underfloor heating training session. Please fill in the required information to complete your registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the training provider by choosing "Yes" or "No" for "Training Provider" field.
2. If "Yes" is selected for "Training Provider", enter the name of the provider in the "Provider Name" field.
3. Choose "Yes" or "No" for "Training Session" field.
4. If "Yes" is selected for "Training Session", enter the name of the session in the "Session Name" field.
5. Fill in your attendee details:
	* "Attendee Name" is the name of the person attending the training session.
	* "Attendee Email" is the email address of the attendee.
	* "Attendee Phone" is the phone number of the attendee.
6. Enter your address:
	* "Address" is the physical address of the attendee.
	* "City" is the city where the attendee is located.
	* "State" is the state or region where the attendee is located. Choose "Yes" or "No" for this field.
	* "Postcode" is the postal code or ZIP code of the attendee's location.
	* "Country" is the country where the attendee is located. Choose "Yes" or "No" for this field.
7. Enter the session details:
	* "Registration Date" is the date the attendee registered for the session.
	* "Registration Time" is the time the attendee registered for the session.
	* "Session Start Date" is the start date of the session.
	* "Session End Date" is the end date of the session.
	* "Session Start Time" is the start time of the session.
	* "Session End Time" is the end time of the session.
	* "Session Duration" is the duration of the session.
8. Enter the training provider's details:
	* "Training Provider Email" is the email address of the training provider.
	* "Training Provider Phone" is the phone number of the training provider.
	* "Provider Address" is the physical address of the training provider.
	* "Provider City" is the city where the training provider is located.
	* "Provider State" is the state or region where the training provider is located. Choose "Yes" or "No" for this field.
	* "Provider Postcode" is the postal code or ZIP code of the training provider's location.
9. Review your entries to ensure everything is correct before submitting the form.
