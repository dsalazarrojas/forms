# Anti Racism Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for anti-racism training programs. It collects personal and professional details to confirm and process participant registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Participant Full Name** in the "First and last name" field. This is the full name of the participant registering.
2. Enter your **Email Address** for confirmation and materials related to the training.
3. Provide your **Phone Number** for contact purposes.
4. Enter your **Organization or Affiliation** if you are registering as part of an organization.
5. Select the type of **Organization** you belong to.
6. Enter your **Job Role or Position**.
7. Choose the **Industry** related to your work.
8. Select the size of your **Team or Organization**.
9. Choose the type of **Training** you are registering for.
10. Enter the **Number of Participants** registering.
11. Select a **Preferred Training Date**.
12. Choose an **Alternate Date** (optional).
13. Select the **Delivery Format** of the training (in-person, virtual, or hybrid).
14. Choose the **Preferred Duration** of the training (half-day, full-day, or custom duration).
15. Select your **Timezone** for scheduling purposes.
16. Select the primary **Learning Objectives** you hope to achieve.
17. Describe your current **DEI Initiatives** in place.
18. If applicable, list the **Specific Challenges** you want to address during the training.
19. Specify any **Accessibility Requirements**.
20. If necessary, provide **Accommodation Details**.
21. Choose the **Payment Method** (credit card, invoice, or other).
22. Enter the **Billing Contact Name** if different from the participant's name.
23. Enter the **Billing Email** for invoices.
24. If applicable, enter a **Promotional Code** for discounts.
25. Select how you **Hear About Us**.
26. Accept or decline the **Terms and Conditions** of registration.
27. Acknowledge the **Cancellation Policy**.
28. Finally, enter the **Registration Date**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name** (`participant_name`): The full name of the participant registering. 
* **Email Address** (`participant_email`): The email address used for confirmation and materials related to the training.
* **Phone Number** (`participant_phone`): The contact phone number of the participant.
* **Organization or Affiliation** (`organization_name`): The name of the organization or school the participant belongs to.
* **Organization Type** (`organization_type`): The type of organization the participant's affiliation is with.
* **Job Role or Position** (`job_role`): The job title or role of the participant.
* **Industry** (`industry`): The sector of the participant's work.
* **Team or Organization Size** (`team_size`): The approximate size of the participant's team or organization.
* **Training Type** (`training_type`): The type of training the participant is registering for.
* **Number of Participants** (`number_of_participants`): The number of people registering for the training.
* **Preferred Training Date** (`preferred_date`): The preferred date for the training.
* **Alternate Date** (`alternate_date`): A backup or alternate date for the training (optional).
* **Delivery Format** (`delivery_format`): The mode of the training delivery (in-person, virtual, or hybrid).
* **Preferred Duration** (`preferred_duration`): The preferred duration of the training session.
* **Timezone** (`timezone`): The timezone of the participant's location for scheduling.
* **Primary Learning Objectives** (`learning_objectives`): The main goals the participant hopes to achieve during the training.
* **Current DEI Initiatives** (`current_initiatives`): The current diversity, equity, and inclusion initiatives of the participant's organization.
* **Specific Challenges** (`specific_challenges`): Any particular issues or challenges the participant wants to address during the training.
* **Accessibility Requirements** (`accessibility_requirements`): Any accommodations required for the participant's access to the training.
* **Accommodation Details** (`accommodation_details`): A detailed explanation of the participant's accommodation requirements.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): The method of payment for the training (credit card, invoice, or other).
* **Billing Contact Name** (`billing_contact`): The name of the person responsible for payment.
* **Billing Email** (`billing_email`): The email address for invoices.
* **Promotional Code** (`promotional_code`): A code for any applicable discounts.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_did_you_hear`): The source of the participant's referral to the training.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_accepted`): The participant's acceptance or declination of the registration terms.
* **Cancellation Policy Acknowledged** (`cancellation_policy_acknowledged`): The participant's acceptance or declination of the cancellation policy.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`): The date of registration.

Note: This help guide provides general guidance and should be reviewed for completeness and accuracy in relation to the form's fields and the provided context.
