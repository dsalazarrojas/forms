# Vision Board Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register attendees for the Vision Board Workshop. It collects registration details, attendee preferences, and consent to attend the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the registration form on the first page.
2. Provide your attendee details on the second page.
3. Indicate your session preferences on the third page.
4. Confirm your consent to attend the workshop on the fourth page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Registration Form (Page 1)
* **Registration Form** (`registration_form`, text, optional): Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant registration information.
*   *Note: This field is optional, but providing your contact information will help us reach out to you with any important updates or reminders.*

### Attendee Details (Page 2)
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, text, optional): Share a little about yourself, such as your interests or any relevant background information that might help us tailor the workshop experience to your needs.
*   *Note: This field is optional, but providing your personal details will help us create a more engaging and personalized experience for you.*

### Session Preferences (Page 3)
* **Session Preferences** (`session_preferences`, text, optional): Let us know about your preferred session format, pace, or topics. This will help us create a more enjoyable and effective experience for you.
*   *Note: This field is optional, but indicating your preferences will help us tailor the sessions to your needs and interests.*

### Consent (Page 4)
* **Consent** (`consent`, text, optional): Confirm that you have read and agree to our workshop terms and conditions. This is a required field, as we need your consent to proceed with your registration.
*   *Note: This field is required to ensure that you are aware of and agree to our workshop policies and guidelines.*
