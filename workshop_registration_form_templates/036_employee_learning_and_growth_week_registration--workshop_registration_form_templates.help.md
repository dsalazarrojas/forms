# Employee Learning And Growth Week Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for the Employee Learning And Growth Week program. Please fill out this form to provide your information and preferences for the event. If you have already registered for this event, please do not submit the form again. Duplicate registrations are not accepted.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Main Page** section with any additional comments or information about your registration.
2. In the **Participant Information** section, enter your participant details:
	* Enter your **E-mail** address to help us contact you with event updates and reminders.
	* Enter your **Phone** number if you would like to be contacted by phone.
	* Add any **Notes** about your participation or special requests.
3. Select your **Participant Type** from the options provided:
	+ **Regular Employee**: If you are a regular employee of our organization.
	+ **Management Team**: If you are a member of our management team.
	+ **Leadership Team**: If you are a member of our leadership team.
	+ **Other**: If you don't fit into any of the above categories, please select this option and provide a brief description in the **Other (please specify)** field.
4. If you selected **Other** as your participant type, please provide a brief description of your role in the **Other (please specify)** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Page**: The main page is a section where you can enter any additional comments or information about your registration.
* **Participant Information - E-mail**: This field is used to collect your email address, which will be used to contact you with event updates and reminders.
* **Participant Information - Phone**: This field is used to collect your phone number, which will be used to contact you by phone if needed.
* **Participant Information - Notes**: This field is used to collect any additional comments or notes about your participation in the event.
* **Participant Information - Participant Type**: This field is used to determine your role within our organization, which will help us understand your needs and preferences for the event.
* **Participant Information - Other (please specify)**: This field is used to provide a brief description of your role if you selected **Other** as your participant type.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your registration is processed correctly.
* If you have any questions or need assistance with your registration, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* You will receive an email confirmation of your registration once you have completed and submitted this form.
