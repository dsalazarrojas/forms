# Employee Satisfaction Survey Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect registration information for the Employee Satisfaction Survey Workshop. This form is for employees who are interested in attending the workshop and want to provide their details, dietary requirements, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and fill in the required fields. You can navigate between pages using the "Next" and "Previous" buttons. Make sure to review each page carefully before proceeding to the next one.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Information** (`employee_info`, text, Optional): This is the page to fill in your personal details. Please provide a brief introduction about yourself, including your name, position, and any other relevant information.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, date, Optional): Choose a date for the workshop that suits you best. You can select a date from the calendar or enter a specific date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Dietary Requirement** (`dietary_requirement`, select_one, Optional): If you have any dietary restrictions, please select the relevant option (Veggie, Gluten-free, Peanut-free, or Non).
* **Discussion Topics** (`discussion_topics`, select_multiple, Optional): Choose the topics you are interested in discussing during the workshop. You can select one or more topics from the list.
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, text, Optional): This is a free-text field where you can provide any additional information about yourself or your needs.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, time, Optional): If you have a preferred time for the workshop, please select it from the available options.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Optional): Provide your email address for any follow-up communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number for any urgent communication.
* **No Code** (`no_code_field`, note, Optional): This field is not applicable for this form.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, Optional): Choose one option from the list. This field is not relevant for this form and you can select any option.

## Tips
- Please make sure to review each page carefully before proceeding to the next one.
- If you need help or have any questions, you can contact our support team.
- You can save your progress and come back later to complete the form if needed.
- Please note that this form is for workshop registration only and not for general feedback or discussion.
