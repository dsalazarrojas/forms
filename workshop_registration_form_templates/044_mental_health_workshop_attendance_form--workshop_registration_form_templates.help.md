# Mental Health Workshop Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Workshop Attendance Form is designed to collect information about participants' attendance and experiences during a workshop. This form is essential for workshop organizers to track attendance, gather feedback, and improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Workshop Name**: Enter the name of the workshop you attended.
2. **Attendee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
3. **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
4. **Date**: Select the date you attended the workshop.
5. **Time**: Select the time you attended the workshop.
6. **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number (optional).
7. **Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your experience.
8. **Workshop Attendance**: Choose whether you attended the workshop, did not attend, or were not decided about attending.
9. **Participant Notes**: Enter any thoughts, comments, or feedback about the workshop.
10. **Workshop Rating**: Rate the workshop on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "very dissatisfied" and 5 is "very satisfied".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Name** (Workshop Name, text, required): Enter the name of the workshop you attended to help the organizers track attendance.
* **Attendee Name** (Attendee Name, text, required): Enter your name for contact and tracking purposes.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Provide your email address for contact and follow-up information.
* **Date** (Date, date, required): Select the date you attended the workshop from the calendar.
* **Time** (Time, time, required): Select the time you attended the workshop to help with scheduling.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes (optional).
* **Note** (Note, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your experience.
* **Workshop Attendance** (Workshop Attendance, select multiple, required): Choose whether you attended, did not attend, or were not decided about attending the workshop.
* **Participant Notes** (Participant Notes, text, required): Enter your thoughts, comments, or feedback about the workshop.
* **Workshop Rating** (Workshop Rating, number, required): Rate the workshop on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "very dissatisfied" and 5 is "very satisfied".
* **Workshop Comments** (Workshop Comments, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about the workshop (optional).

## Tips
* Ensure that you have not already submitted an attendance record for this workshop to avoid duplication.
* Be honest and thorough when providing your feedback and comments.
* Use the rating scale to provide constructive feedback about the workshop's effectiveness.
* If you have any concerns or questions, contact the workshop organizers for assistance.
