# Mental Health Innovation Lab Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Innovation Lab Registration Form is designed to gather information from workshop registrants for effective workshop organization and participant administration. Please review the form carefully and ensure you have all necessary information before submitting.

## How To Complete This Form

- Carefully review each field for its required status. Mandatory fields are marked as "Required."
- Fill out the form completely and accurately. Incomplete or inaccurate submissions may delay or prevent your registration.
- Ensure that you have provided valid and up-to-date contact information to facilitate communication and event follow-up.
- If you are unsure about any field, you can ask the form administrators for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Registration Form** (id: 1): This field is a text entry where you can provide a brief introduction or any specific workshop-related information you'd like to mention.
* **Participant Info** (id: 2): Here, you can enter your name, title, and any other relevant participant details, if applicable.
* **Contact Info** (id: 3): Enter your contact details, such as a name, email, and phone number, for easy communication and follow-up.
* **Email** (id: 4): Enter your valid email address. This is a required field for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (id: 5): Provide your phone number for further communication and updates. This is a required field.
* **Date** (id: 6): Enter the date on which you would like to attend the workshop or program.
* **Time** (id: 7): Specify the time slot you prefer for the workshop.
* **Innovation Programs** (id: 8): Choose whether you have interest in any of the mentioned innovation programs.
* **Program Interest** (id: 9): Indicate your interest level for any of the mentioned programs. 
* **Participant Name** (id: 10): This is where you can add your name as it appears on the official registration records.
* **Organization** (id: 11): If applicable, enter your organization name for networking and identification purposes.
* **Role** (id: 12): Add your professional role or department within your organization, if applicable.
* **Description** (id: 13): Briefly describe your background or interest in mental health innovation, if you wish.
* **Additional Info** (id: 14): Provide any additional information that might be useful for workshop organizers or participants.
* **Terms and Conditions** (id: 15): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the workshop and associated activities.
* **Privacy Policy** (id: 16): Understand and agree to the workshop's privacy policy regarding collected data.

## Tips

- Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately to avoid unnecessary delays or complications.
- If you are unsure about any field, you can reach out to the workshop organizers or support staff for clarification.
- This form is designed to collect data for workshop planning and management; it is not intended for commercial gain or external use without consent.
