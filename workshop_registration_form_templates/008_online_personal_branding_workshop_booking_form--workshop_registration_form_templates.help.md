# Online Personal Branding Workshop Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Online Personal Branding Workshop Booking Form" is designed to collect essential information from participants who want to register for the online personal branding workshop. The form helps to easily manage workshop registrations and bookings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select your registration form category.
2. Choose whether or not to assign a chatbot to the participant.
3. Briefly describe your interest in the workshop.
4. Provide a unique ID for the workshop registration.
5. Specify whether or not you want the participant to receive an output file.
6. Enter the date you submitted the form.
7. Enter your participant name.
8. Enter your participant surname.
9. Enter your participant email address.
10. Enter your participant phone number.
11. Select whether or not the participant is attending the workshop.
12. Enter the date of the booking.
13. Choose your payment method.
14. Enter the time of the booking.
15. Enter any additional notes about the participant's booking.
16. Specify whether or not the submission was made by the participant themselves.
17. Specify whether or not the submission was made to a specific person or team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Forms** (Page 1):
	* **Registration Forms**: Select whether or not this form is for the main registration form.
* **Chatjimmy** (Page 2):
	* **Chatjimmy**: This field is for specifying whether or not to assign a chatbot to the participant. Please select "Yes" if you want to assign a chatbot.
* **The Online Personal Branding Workshop Booking Form allows participants...** (Page 3):
	* **The Online Personal Branding Workshop Booking Form allows participants...**: This field is for describing your interest in the workshop in brief.
* **Workshop Registration Form IDs** (Page 4):
	* **Workshop Registration Form IDs**: Enter a unique ID for the workshop registration.
* **Output File** (Page 5):
	* **Output File**: Specify whether or not you want the participant to receive an output file. Please select "Yes" if you want to send a file.
* **Submit Date** (Page 6):
	* **Submit Date**: Enter the date you submitted the form.
* **Participant Name** (Page 7):
	* **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant.
* **Participant Surname** (Page 8):
	* **Participant Surname**: Enter the surname of the participant.
* **Participant E-mail** (Page 9):
	* **Participant E-mail**: Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone** (Page 10):
	* **Participant Phone**: Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Workshop Title** (Page 11):
	* **Workshop Title**: Select whether or not the participant is attending the workshop. Please select "Yes" if they are attending.
* **Booking Date** (Page 12):
	* **Booking Date**: Enter the date of the booking.
* **Payment Method** (Page 13):
	* **Payment Method**: Choose your payment method. Please select "Yes" if you've already paid.
* **Booking Time** (Page 14):
	* **Booking Time**: Enter the time of the booking.
* **Notes** (Page 15):
	* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the participant's booking.
* **Submitted By** (Page 16):
	* **Submitted By**: Specify whether or not the submission was made by the participant themselves. Please select "Yes" if it was made by the participant.
* **Submitted To** (Page 17):
	* **Submitted To**: Specify whether or not the submission was made to a specific person or team. Please select "Yes" if it was made to a team.

Please note that this form is intended to manage the online workshop registrations and bookings, so it's crucial to provide accurate and detailed information for the participant's booking. If you're unsure about any field, please contact the workshop administrators for clarification.
