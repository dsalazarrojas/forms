# AI Project Management Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AI Project Management Workshop Registration form is used to collect information from attendees to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience during the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email address" field, which will receive confirmation of your registration.
3. If you have experience managing projects, enter the number of years you've been doing so in the "Years experience managing projects" field.
4. Provide a brief description of your goals for attending the workshop in the "Goals for attendance" field.
5. Select your preferred session format in the "Session preference" field.
6. If you have any dietary restrictions, please mention them in the "Dietary restrictions" field.
7. If you have any accessibility needs, specify them in the "Accessibility needs" field.
8. Provide emergency contact details in the "Emergency contact" field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (name, text, required): Enter your full name, including both first and last names.
* **Organization** (organization, text, optional): Enter your company or team name.
* **Email address** (email, email, required): Enter your email address where you will receive confirmation of your registration.
* **Years experience managing projects** (years_experience, number, optional): Enter the number of years you've been managing projects.
* **Goals for attendance** (goals_for_attendance, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your goals for attending the workshop.
* **Session preference** (session_preferred, select_one, required): Select your preferred session format: Weekday intensive, Weekend bootcamp, or Remote self-paced.
* **Dietary restrictions** (dietary_restrictions, text, optional): If you have any dietary restrictions, enter them here.
* **Accessibility needs** (accessibility_needs, text, optional): If you have any accessibility needs, specify them here.
* **Emergency contact** (emergency_contact, text, optional): Provide emergency contact details, if applicable.
