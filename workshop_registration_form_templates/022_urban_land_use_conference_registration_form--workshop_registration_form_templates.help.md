# Urban Land Use Conference Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from participants and sponsors for the Urban Land Use Conference. Fill out this form to provide your personal details, conference registration information, and sponsorship details (if applicable). Your input will help us organize the conference and make it a success.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Scroll through each page of the form, which includes three pages in total.
2. On the first page (Personal Details), provide your:
	* Name and contact information.
3. On the second page (Registration Details), enter your:
	* Conference registration information, including dates and payment details.
4. On the third page (Sponsorship Details), if you are a sponsor, provide:
	* Your company and sponsor information.
5. Review your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
