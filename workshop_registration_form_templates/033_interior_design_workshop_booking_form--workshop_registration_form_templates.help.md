# interior_design_workshop_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The interior design workshop booking form is used to collect information about workshop participants and their registration status for the interior design workshop.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the workshop name and any other required information for your workshop.
* Provide your participant name, email address, and phone number.
* Enter the event date and start/end times.
* Select the workshop duration.
* Choose your registration type (Workshop A, B, or C).
* Indicate the number of participants attending with you.
* If you are registering for the workshop with other participants, select their names from the list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Name (id: 1)** (`Workshop Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the workshop you are registering for.
* **Participant Name (id: 2)** (`Participant Name`, text, optional): Enter your name as a participant.
* **Email (id: 3)** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number (id: 4)** (`Phone Number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Event Date (id: 5)** (`Event Date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the workshop.
* **Start Time (id: 6)** (`Start Time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the workshop.
* **End Time (id: 7)** (`End Time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the workshop.
* **Workshop Duration (id: 8)** (`Workshop Duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the workshop.
* **Registration Type (id: 9)** (`Registration Type`, select_one, optional): Select the registration type (Workshop A, B, or C).
* **Participant Count (id: 10)** (`Participant Count`, number, optional): Enter the number of participants attending with you.
* **Participant List (id: 11)** (`Participant List`, select_multiple, optional): Select the names of participants attending with you from the provided list.
