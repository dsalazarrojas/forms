# Student Research Publishing Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students attending the Student Research Publishing Workshop. It helps us understand your background, preferences, and expectations from the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email.
2.  Choose your status as a student (Undergraduate, Graduate, or PhD).
3.  Select the topics you are interested in learning about during the workshop (e.g., Research Methodology, Data Analysis, or Research Writing).
4.  Provide your preferred Date and Time for the workshop.
5.  Leave any additional notes or comments in the "Additional Notes" field.
6.  Rate how useful you found the workshop (Useful, Somewhat Useful, or Not Useful at all).
7.  Decide if you would recommend this workshop to others (True, False, or Unsure).
8.  Indicate which topics you would like to learn more about (Workshop Organization, Research Ethics, or Time Management).
9.  Provide the Date and Time of your next workshop visit.
10.  Write any additional comments or notes in "Additional Notes 2".
11.  Let us know if you would like to be contacted for future workshops (True, False, or Maybe).
12.  Enter your email address and phone number (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it is on your official documents.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it is on your official documents.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address that we can use to communicate with you.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for future contact.
*   **What kind of student are you?** (`select_one`, optional): Choose your status as a student (Undergraduate, Graduate, or PhD).
*   **What topics do you want to learn about?** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select all the topics you are interested in learning about (e.g., Research Methodology, Data Analysis, or Research Writing).
*   **Date** (`date`, optional): Choose the date you would like to attend the workshop.
*   **Time** (`time`, optional): Choose the time you would like to attend the workshop.
*   **Additional Notes** (`note`, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about your registration.
*   **How did you find this workshop?** (`select_one`, optional): Rate how useful you found the workshop (Useful, Somewhat Useful, or Not Useful at all).
*   **Would you recommend this workshop to others?** (`select_one`, optional): Decide if you would recommend this workshop to others (True, False, or Unsure).
*   **Which topics would you like to learn more about?** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select all the topics you would like to learn more about (e.g., Workshop Organization, Research Ethics, or Time Management).
*   **Next Workshop Date** (`date`, optional): Provide the date of your next workshop visit.
*   **Next Workshop Time** (`time`, optional): Provide the time of your next workshop visit.
*   **Additional Notes 2** (`note`, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about your registration.
*   **Would you like to be contacted for future workshops?** (`select_one`, optional): Let us know if you would like to be contacted for future workshops (True, False, or Maybe).
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for future communication.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for future contact.

Note: Please fill in all the required fields to ensure that your registration is complete and accurate.
