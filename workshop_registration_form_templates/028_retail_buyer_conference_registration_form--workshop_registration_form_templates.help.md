# Retail Buyer Conference Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Buyer Conference Registration Form is designed to gather information from attendees for a productive and enjoyable experience at the conference. Please take a few minutes to fill out the form accurately, and we'll take care of the rest.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields (marked as "required") with your personal and company information.
2. Select your preferred date and time of attendance from the given options.
3. Choose your dietary restrictions from the options provided (Meat, Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-free, Dairy-free, or Other).
4. Indicate your company size and type (if applicable).
5. Select the sessions you plan to attend (Morning, Afternoon, Full Day, or Half Day).
6. Provide any additional details or comments in the message field.
7. Read and agree to the terms and conditions by typing in the "Terms Accepted" field.

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.

* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title.
