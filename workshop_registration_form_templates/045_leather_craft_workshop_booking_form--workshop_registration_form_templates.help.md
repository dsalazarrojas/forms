# Leather Craft Workshop Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leather Craft Workshop Booking Form is designed for workshop participants to provide accurate and complete information about themselves and their workshop preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your personal details: first and last name, date of birth, email, phone number, and emergency contact information.
2. Select the workshop type and confirm your attendance.
3. Provide information about any tools needed for the workshop.
4. Enter the workshop details: date, time, venue, and facilitator.
5. Submit the form once all fields are filled in accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
• **First Name**: A required field to collect your first name. Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Last Name
• **Last Name**: A required field to collect your last name. Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Date of Birth
• **Date of Birth**: A required field to collect your date of birth. Please enter the date of birth in the format 'mm/dd/yyyy'.

### Email
• **Email**: A required field to collect your email address. Please enter your primary email address.

### Phone Number
• **Phone Number**: A required field to collect your phone number. Please enter your primary phone number.

### Workshop Type
• **Workshop Type**: A required field to select the type of workshop you wish to attend. Please select one of the provided options: Leather Crafting, Pattern Making, or Leather Finishing.

### Workshop Date
• **Workshop Date**: A required field to select the date of the workshop. Please select the date of the workshop from the provided options.

### Workshop Time
• **Workshop Time**: A required field to select the start and end time of the workshop. Please select the start and end time in the 24-hour format.

### Participant Number
• **Participant Number**: A required field to select the number of participants you will be bringing to the workshop.

### Note
• **Note**: An optional field to provide additional information or notes about the workshop or any special requirements.

### Confirm Workshop
• **Confirm Workshop**: A required field to confirm your attendance in the workshop. Please select 'Yes' to confirm.

### Confirm Email
• **Confirm Email**: A required field to confirm the workshop type in email. Please select the relevant workshop type from the options.

### Tools Needed
• **Tools Needed**: A required field to select any tools or equipment needed for the workshop. Please select the relevant tools from the provided options.

### Emergency Contact
• **Emergency Contact**: A required field to provide the name of the emergency contact person. Please enter the name of the emergency contact.

### Emergency Contact Number
• **Emergency Contact Number**: A required field to enter the phone number of the emergency contact. Please enter the phone number of the emergency contact.

### Emergency Contact Relationship
• **Emergency Contact Relationship**: A required field to specify the relationship between you and the emergency contact. Please enter a brief description of the relationship.

### Emergency Contact Email
• **Emergency Contact Email**: An optional field to enter the email address of the emergency contact. Please enter the email address of the emergency contact.

### Workshop Location
• **Workshop Location**: A required field to provide the location of the workshop. Please enter the venue or location of the workshop.

### Workshop Facilitator
• **Workshop Facilitator**: A required field to provide the name of the facilitator. Please enter the name of the facilitator leading the workshop.

### Workshop Venue
• **Workshop Venue**: A required field to provide the venue of the workshop. Please enter the venue or location of the workshop.

### Workshop Venue Address
• **Workshop Venue Address**: A required field to provide the address of the workshop venue. Please enter the address of the workshop venue.

### Workshop Start Time
• **Workshop Start Time**: A required field to select the start time of the workshop. Please select the start time in the 24-hour format.

### Workshop End Time
• **Workshop End Time**: A required field to select the end time of the workshop. Please select the end time in the 24-hour format.

### Workshop Notes
• **Workshop Notes**: An optional field to provide any additional notes or information about the workshop. Please enter any relevant notes or information about the workshop.

### Submit
• **Submit**: A required field to submit the form once all fields are filled in accurately.

Note: This guide is subject to change, and additional information may be added or modified at any time. Please ensure that you read this guide carefully before completing the form.
