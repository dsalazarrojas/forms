# School To Work Pathways Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School To Work Pathways Workshop Registration Form is designed for workshop registrants to provide their information in order to participate in a school-to-work workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student information on the first page (Student Information).
2. Provide your contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
3. Choose your workshop preferences, if applicable.
4. Select the workshop details, including name, date, start date, end date, time, location, duration, and the name of the workshop organizer.
5. Confirm your student interests and workshop interests, if applicable.
6. Add any comments or remarks about the workshop.
7. Finally, submit your registration by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information** (`student_info`, text, required): Please fill in your student name, grade level, and other relevant details.
* **contact** (`contact`, text, required): Please enter your name, email address, and phone number to help us contact you regarding the workshop.
* **Workshop Preferences** (`student_workshop_preferences`, text, optional): If you have any specific requests or preferences for the workshop, please mention them here.
* **Workshop Information** (`workshop_info`, text, required): Please select the workshop you would like to attend, including the workshop name, date, start date, end date, time, and location.
* **Student Interests** (`student_interests`, text, optional): If you have any specific interests or skills you would like to share or discuss during the workshop, please mention them here.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, required): Please enter your student ID number, if applicable.
* **Workshop Duration** (`workshop_duration`, number, required): Please select the duration of the workshop you are interested in.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Please select the start date of the workshop you are interested in.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Please select the end date of the workshop you are interested in.
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, time, required): Please select the time of the workshop you are interested in.
* **Workshop Location** (`workshop_location`, text, required): Please select the location of the workshop you are interested in.
* **Workshop Organizer** (`workshop_organizer`, text, optional): If you have any questions or need more information about the workshop, please mention the name of the workshop organizer.
* **Workshop Comments** (`workshop_comments`, text, optional): If you have any comments or remarks about the workshop, please mention them here.

Note: The "Submit" button will save your registration and send it to us. Make sure to review your information carefully before submitting your form.
