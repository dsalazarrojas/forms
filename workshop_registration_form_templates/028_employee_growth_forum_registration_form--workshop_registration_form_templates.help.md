# Employee Growth Forum Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Growth Forum Registration Form is designed to collect information from employees for an Employee Growth Forum workshop or event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Please ensure you have not already registered for this workshop.
- Fill in your First Name.
- Enter your Last Name.
- Provide a valid Email address.
- Enter your Phone number.
- Choose a valid date for the Workshop Date (mm/dd/yyyy format).
- Select a Registration Time (hh:mm format).
- Provide a brief description of the Event Name.
- Enter a brief description of the Event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (1): Enter your first name as it is displayed on your official identification or documents.
- **Last Name** (2): Enter your last name as it is displayed on your official identification or documents.
- **Email** (3): Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
- **Phone** (4): Enter your contact phone number.
- **Workshop Date** (5): Choose the date on which you wish to attend the workshop. Please use the mm/dd/yyyy format (e.g., 02/15/2024).
- **Registration Time** (6): Select the time at which you plan to attend the workshop. Please use the hh:mm format (e.g., 14:30).
- **Event Name** (7): Enter a brief title of the event or workshop.
- **Event Description** (8): Enter a brief description of the event or workshop.

## Tips
- Double-check your information before submitting the form.
- Ensure your email and phone number are valid and up-to-date.
- If you have any issues or concerns, contact your HR or event organizer.
