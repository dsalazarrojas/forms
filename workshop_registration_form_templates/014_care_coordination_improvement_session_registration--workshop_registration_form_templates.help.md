# Care Coordination Improvement Session Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect registration information from participants for a care coordination improvement session. It's essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience.
## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by selecting the session date you would like to attend from the "Session date" field.
2.  Fill in your participant name, organization (if applicable), and role or job title in the respective fields.
3.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field, which will be used for session confirmation and future communications.
4.  Indicate any dietary restrictions or accessibility needs you may have in the "Dietary restrictions" and "Accessibility needs" fields, respectively.
5.  Provide the name and contact information for your emergency contact person in the "Emergency contact" field.
6.  Choose whether or not you consent to share your contact information with the session organizers in the "Consent to share contact" field.
7.  Submit the form once you have filled in all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name**: Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Organization**: If you're representing an organization, enter the name of your company or institution.
* **Email**: A valid email address that will be used for session confirmation and future communications.
* **Role or job title**: A brief description of your position related to care coordination.
* **Session date**: Select the date you would like to attend the care coordination improvement session.
* **Dietary restrictions**: Indicate any special dietary requirements, such as vegetarian or gluten-free.
* **Accessibility needs**: Describe any accommodations you may need to fully participate in the session.
* **Emergency contact**: Enter the name and contact information of a person to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Consent to share contact**: Select "True" to allow session organizers to share your contact information for coordination purposes.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the email address you provide is valid and active.
* If you have any special needs or requirements, please indicate them in the "Dietary restrictions" and "Accessibility needs" fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the session organizers for assistance.
