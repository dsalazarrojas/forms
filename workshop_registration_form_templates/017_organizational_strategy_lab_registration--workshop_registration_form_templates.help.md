# Organizational Strategy Lab Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from participants in the Organizational Strategy Lab. It helps us understand the goals, preferences, and other details of each participant, which will aid us in creating a productive and engaging experience for everyone. Please note that you must be registered as a participant in this lab to access this form. If you're not registered, you won't be able to access this form.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to access the form.
2. Fill out the form fields as prompted:
	* Enter your participant details (id 1)
	* Enter your goals for participating in the lab (id 2)
	* Select your session preferences (id 3)
	* Enter your personal goals (id 4)
	* Select your participant session preferences (id 5)
	* Select your participant details for the session (id 6)
	* Select your participant preferences (id 7)
	* Enter your contact information (id 8)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details (id 1)**: Please enter your name, department or team name, and title.
* **Goals (id 2)**: Briefly describe the goals you aim to achieve during the lab. This will help us tailor the experience to your needs.
* **Session Preferences (id 3)**: Choose the time slots you are available to attend during the lab.
	+ Select all applicable options to indicate the time slots you prefer.
* **Participant Goals (id 4)**: Describe your personal goals for participating in the lab, such as what you hope to learn, what you want to achieve, or what questions you have.
* **Participant Session Preferences (id 5)**: Select the session preferences that align with your needs.
	+ Choose all applicable options to indicate your preferences.
* **Participant Details Session (id 6)**: Select whether you want to share your participant details with other lab attendees.
	+ Select "Yes" if you are okay with sharing, or "No" if you prefer not to.
* **Participant Preferences (id 7)**: Select your participant preferences for the lab.
	+ Choose "Yes" if you have specific needs or requirements, or "No" if not.
* **Contact (id 8)**: Enter your email address to receive updates and communications about the lab.
