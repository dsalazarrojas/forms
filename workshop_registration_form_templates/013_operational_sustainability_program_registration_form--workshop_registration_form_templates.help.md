# Operational Sustainability Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an Operational Sustainability Program, which is a structured initiative aimed at ensuring the long-term sustainability of business operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information about your program.
2. Review the form carefully to ensure all fields are accurate and complete.
3. Submit the form once you have filled in all the necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Program Details
* **Program Details** (`program_details`, `text`, optional): This field is a general description of your program. Please enter a brief statement that outlines the key aspects of your program.

### Program Name
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your program, which should be clear and descriptive.

### Program Date
* **Program Date** (`program_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your program, which should be in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.

### Program Time
* **Program Time** (`program_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of your program, which should be in the format of HH:MM AM/PM.

### Program Start Time
* **Program Start Time** (`program_start_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the start time of your program, which should be in the format of HH:MM AM/PM.

### Program End Time
* **Program End Time** (`program_end_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the end time of your program, which should be in the format of HH:MM AM/PM.

### Program Location
* **Program Location** (`program_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location of your program, which should be a specific address or a brief description.

### Program Duration
* **Program Duration** (`program_duration`, `text`, optional): Enter the duration of your program, which should be a brief statement of how long it will last.

## Tips

* Please make sure to double-check the information provided before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your program manager or a relevant authority for clarification.
* This form is for internal use only and will not be shared with anyone outside the organization.
* The information collected from this form will be used to create a schedule and track the program's progress.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for assistance.
