# mental_health_workshop_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects essential information from participants registering for a mental health workshop. It's designed to be completed by workshop attendees to ensure we have the necessary details for planning and organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:
1. Provide your name and any other relevant details in the `Participant Details` section.
2. Indicate your availability and preferences for the workshop in the `Workshop Preferences` section.
3. Enter your emergency contact and medical details in the respective sections.
4. Choose your registration status and sign the `Registration Form` to confirm.
5. Sign the `Emergency Form` to confirm your emergency contact's understanding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`Participant Details`): Please enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Workshop Preferences** (`Workshop Preferences`): Indicate your availability and any preferences you may have for the workshop, such as dates, timings, or topics.
* **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`): Provide the contact details of the person or organization that will be responsible for your care in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Details** (`Emergency Details`): Enter any relevant information about the emergency contact, such as their name, phone number, and address.
* **Medical Details** (`Medical Details`): Please provide any medical details that might be essential for our team.
* **Registration Form** (`Registration Form`): Sign this section to confirm your registration and commitment to attend the workshop.
* **Emergency Form** (`Emergency Form`): Please have your emergency contact sign this section to confirm their understanding and commitment.
* **Registration Status** (`Registration Status`): Choose your current registration status, whether it's Registered, Pending, or Cancelled.
* **Participant Signature** (`Participant Signature`): Sign this section to confirm your commitment to attend the workshop.
* **Emergency Contact Signature** (`Emergency Contact Signature`): Sign this section to confirm your emergency contact's understanding and commitment.
