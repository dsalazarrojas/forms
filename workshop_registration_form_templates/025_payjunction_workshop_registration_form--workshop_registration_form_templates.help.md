# PayJunction Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants for the workshop registration process. It's essential to provide accurate and complete information to facilitate a smooth workshop experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill in all required fields**: Please make sure to complete all the fields marked as "required".
2. **Select the correct options**: When selecting options, choose the relevant ones that apply to your situation.
3. **Submit the form**: Once you've filled out the form, submit it to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form (1)** (`text`, required: false): This field is where you can enter any relevant comments or details about your registration.
* **Name (2)** (`text`, required: false): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email (3)** (`email`, required: false): Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications about the workshop.
* **Phone (4)** (`text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for any necessary contact.
* **Select One (5)** (`select_one`, required: false): Choose "Yes" if this option applies to you, or "No" otherwise.
* **Select Multiple (6)** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all the relevant options that apply to you.
* **Date (7)** (`date`, required: false): Enter the date you plan to attend the workshop.
* **Time (8)** (`time`, required: false): Enter the time you plan to attend the workshop.
* **Note (9)** (`note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about your registration.
* **Decimal Number (10)** (`number`, required: false): Enter a decimal number (e.g., 3.5) if required.
* **Integer Number (11)** (`number`, required: false): Enter an integer number (e.g., 1, 2, 3).
* **Text Input (12)** (`text`, required: false): Enter any additional text information.
* **Workshop Name (13)** (`text`, required: false): Enter the name of the workshop you are registering for.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as "required". Please fill out all required fields to ensure a successful registration.
