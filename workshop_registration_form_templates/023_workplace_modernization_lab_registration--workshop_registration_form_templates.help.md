# Workplace Modernization Lab Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register participants for the Workplace Modernization Lab. It collects personal and demographic information, including name, email, and interests, to ensure that participants understand their roles and responsibilities in the program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Indicate your interests by selecting one or more options from the "Interests" dropdown menu.
4. Choose your preferred session time from the "Preferred Session Time" dropdown menu.
5. Read and agree to the "Terms Agreement" and "Participant Agreement" by selecting the corresponding options from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title (Required)**: This field is for the title of the form, which will be used for administrative purposes.
* **Name (Required)**: Enter your name as it is spelled on your official documents. This will help us identify you as a participant in the program.
* **Email (Required)**: Enter your official email address, which will be used for communication purposes within the program.
* **Interests (Required)**: Select one or more interests that you have, such as "Yes" or "No". This will help us tailor the program to your needs.
* **Preferred Session Time (Required)**: Choose a time of day when you are most available to participate in the program, such as "Morning", "Afternoon", or "Evening".
* **Terms Agreement (Required)**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the program. Select "I agree to the terms" to proceed.
* **Participant Agreement (Required)**: Read and agree to the participant terms. Select "I agree to the participant terms" to proceed.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Review your selections before submitting the form to ensure they are correct.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact the program administrators for assistance.
