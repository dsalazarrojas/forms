# Organizational Coaching Mastermind Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Organizational Coaching Mastermind Registration Form is a tool designed to collect registration details from individuals interested in attending the Organizational Coaching Mastermind event. It's essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
2. **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
3. **Email**: Enter your active email address to receive updates and notifications about the event.
4. **Phone**: Provide your contact phone number for any follow-up communications.
5. **Event Title**: Enter the title of the specific event you are registering for (e.g., Organizational Coaching Mastermind).
6. **Event Date**: Select the date of the event you are registering for (if applicable).
7. **Event Start Time**: If applicable, enter the start time of the event.
8. **Event End Time**: If applicable, enter the end time of the event.
9. **Workshop Title**: Enter the title of the workshop you are registering for (if applicable).
10. **Workshop Date**: If applicable, enter the date of the workshop you are registering for.
11. **Workshop Start Time**: If applicable, enter the start time of the workshop you are registering for.
12. **Workshop End Time**: If applicable, enter the end time of the workshop you are registering for.
13. **Payment Method**: Choose how you plan to pay for the event or workshop (options: Stripe, PayPal, Bank Transfer).
14. **Payment Amount**: Enter the payment amount for the selected method.
15. **Payment Currency**: Select the currency for your payment (options: USD, CAD, EUR).
16. **Payment Notes**: If necessary, provide any additional comments or notes for your payment method.
