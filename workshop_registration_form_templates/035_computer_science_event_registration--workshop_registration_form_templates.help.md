# Computer Science Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from attendees and event stakeholders for a Computer Science event. It's essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth registration process and effective event planning.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the registration process.
2. Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Review and correct any errors before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee name** (`Attendee name`, text, **required**): This is the name of the person registering for the event. Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email address** (`Email address`, email, **required**): Enter your valid email address to receive event updates, materials, and any necessary communication from the organizers.
* **Affiliation** (`Affiliation`, text, **optional**): If you are representing an organization or school, please provide the name and details of your affiliation.
* **Ticket type** (`Ticket type`, select_one, **required**): Choose the type of ticket that best suits your purpose for attending the event. Options include:
	+ Attendee: Regular attendee with no special privileges.
	+ Speaker: Speaker or presenter at the event.
	+ Student: Student attending the event.
	+ Volunteer: Volunteer helping with event organization or logistics.
* **Sessions selected** (`Sessions selected`, select_multiple, **optional**): Select the sessions you plan to attend during the event. Choose from:
	+ Keynote: The main session with a keynote speaker.
	+ Workshop A: A workshop with practical exercises.
	+ Workshop B: Another workshop with practical exercises.
	+ Panel: A discussion session with multiple speakers.
* **Dietary requirements** (`Dietary requirements`, text, **optional**): If you have specific dietary needs or requirements, please let us know so we can arrange for catering accordingly.
* **Accessibility needs** (`Accessibility needs`, text, **optional**): If you have any special accessibility needs or requirements, please inform us in this field so we can provide necessary arrangements.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for a smooth event planning process.
- If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us at [insert contact information].
- Review your form carefully before submission to avoid errors and ensure a successful registration process.
