# Grant Proposal Coaching Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals interested in participating in a grant proposal coaching program. It will help us understand your goals, preferences, and contact information to provide personalized coaching services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form by providing the required information.
2. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Grant Proposal Coaching Registration Form** (`1`, `text`, not required): This is the form title, which is not required to be filled out but will help us identify the purpose of your submission.
* **participant_info** (`2`, `text`, not required): Please provide a brief description of yourself, including your name, title, and contact information (email address and phone number). This will help us tailor coaching services to your needs.
* **scheduling_preferences** (`3`, `text`, not required): In this field, specify your preferred dates and times for coaching sessions. Be as specific as possible to help us schedule coaching appointments that suit you best.
* **goals** (`4`, `text`, not required): Describe your grant proposal coaching goals, such as improving proposal writing skills, developing grant research ideas, or refining your grant writing process.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting.
* Be as specific as possible when providing contact information and scheduling preferences.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, feel free to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
