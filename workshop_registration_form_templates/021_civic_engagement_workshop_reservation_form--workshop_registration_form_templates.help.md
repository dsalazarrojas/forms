# Civic Engagement Workshop Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect reservations for the Civic Engagement Workshop. To complete this form, please carefully review the following instructions and provide accurate information for each field.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details: Enter your primary participant's name, email address, and select your preferred date for the workshop.
2. Choose a workshop session: Select the workshop that interests you the most from the provided options.
3. Provide group details: If you are attending with a group, enter the number of people in your group.
4. Check your accessibility needs: If you have any accessibility or dietary needs, please provide details for in-person attendance.
5. Share how you heard about the workshop: Select how you heard about this workshop.
6. Stay informed: Select whether you would like to receive updates on future workshops.
7. Agree to terms: Confirm that you understand that space is limited and will notify the organizer if you cannot attend.
8. Complete the submission: Click submit to finalize your reservation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Primary Participant Name**: Enter the name of the primary participant.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Select a Workshop**: Choose the workshop that interests you the most.
* **Preferred Date**: Select the date that suits you best for the workshop.
* **How Will You Attend?**: Choose whether you will attend in-person or virtually.
* **Number of People in Your Group**: Enter the number of people in your group, if applicable.
* **Organization/Group Name**: If you are attending with an organization, enter their name.
* **Primary Goal for this Workshop**: Enter your primary reason for attending the workshop.
* **Accessibility or Dietary Needs**: If you have any accessibility or dietary needs, please provide details for in-person attendance.
* **How Did You Hear about This Workshop?**: Select how you heard about this workshop.
* **Would You Like to Receive Updates?**: Select whether you would like to receive updates on future workshops.
* **I Understand and Agree**: Confirm that you understand that space is limited and will notify the organizer if you cannot attend.
* **Submission Date**: This field is auto-generated and should not be filled out by the user.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for all fields.
* If you have any special accessibility or dietary needs, please provide detailed information for in-person attendance.
* Select the workshop that best aligns with your interests and goals.
* If you are unable to attend, please notify the organizer in a timely manner to ensure that they can offer the space to others.
