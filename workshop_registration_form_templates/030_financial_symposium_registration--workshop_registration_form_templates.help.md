# Financial Symposium Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Financial Symposium, providing essential information for the symposium organizers to process your registration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting it.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
4. If you have any issues or questions, refer to this guide for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Details** (`Attendee Details`, text, required/optional): This field is for providing your contact information and any other relevant details about yourself as an attendee.
* **Session Choices** (`Session Choices`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the sessions you would like to attend by selecting multiple options. Please note that you can select up to [insert number] sessions.
* **Payment Details** (`Payment Details`, text, required/optional): This field is for providing information about your payment method, such as your name and payment amount. 

## Tips
* Be aware that incomplete or inaccurate information may lead to issues with your registration.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
* Please review your submission carefully before submitting the form.
* If you need to make changes to your registration, contact the symposium organizers.
