# editing_techniques_workshop_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Editing Techniques Workshop Registration Form" is designed to collect registration information from participants in a workshop about editing techniques.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "Workshop Details" field with the relevant workshop information.
2. Proceed to the "Participant Info" section, where you will provide your personal contact details, including your name, email, and phone number.
3. Select the workshop date and time that suits you best in the "Workshop Date" and "Workshop Time" fields.
4. Choose your preferred payment method in the "Payment Method" field.
5. Finally, select the workshop type that interests you in the "Workshop Type" field.
6. Review your entries and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Details**: Enter the name of the workshop and any additional information required.
* **Participant Info**:
	+ **Participant Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
	+ **Participant Email**: Enter your valid email address.
	+ **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Workshop Date**: Select the date of the workshop you wish to attend.
* **Workshop Time**: Select the time of the workshop you wish to attend.
* **Payment Details**: Enter your payment details, such as payment method and amount.
* **Registration Date**: This field is automatically populated with the current date.
* **Payment Method**: Select one of the options provided, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Workshop Type**: Select one of the options provided, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Id**: This field is automatically populated with a unique ID number.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Review your entries before submitting the form to ensure that everything is correct.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact the workshop organizers or administrators for assistance.
