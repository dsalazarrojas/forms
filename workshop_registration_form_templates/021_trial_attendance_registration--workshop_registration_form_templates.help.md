# Trial Attendance Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register your attendance at a trial event. Please fill out this form to let us know you're attending.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Name
• **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documentation.

### Email
• **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted regarding the trial event.

### Phone
• **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted regarding the trial event.

## Tips
* If you're having trouble filling out the form, please contact us for assistance.
* Make sure to double-check your email and phone number for accuracy.
