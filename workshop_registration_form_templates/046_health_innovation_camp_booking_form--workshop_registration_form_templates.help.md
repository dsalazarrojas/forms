# health_innovation_camp_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Health Innovation Camp event. You will be asked for your contact information and preferences for the event. This will help us tailor the event to your needs and make sure we have enough resources for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each of the fields with the requested information. If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the field-by-field explanation below for more details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact** (`contact`, `email`, required: **false**): Please enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: **false**): Enter your name so we can personalize your event experience.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: **false**): Select the date of the event you would like to register for.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: **false**): Select your preferred time slot for the event. 
* **Booking Type** (`booking_type`, `select_one`, required: **false**): Choose the reason for your booking (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Attendees** (`attendees`, `select_multiple`, required: **false**): Select the number of attendees you will be bringing with you (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required: **false**): If you have any specific requests or needs for your booking, type them in here.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: **false**): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you about any further details regarding the event.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: **false**): Enter your phone number, in case we need to get in touch with you urgently.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required: **false**): Please click on this button to submit your registration for the event. 

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- If you have any special requests or needs, use the "Note" field to let us know.
- Our team will contact you with further details regarding your event registration. 

Note: This help guide assumes that the form is used for registration purposes, but it may also be used for other purposes such as feedback or inquiry, in which case some fields or the overall process may vary.
