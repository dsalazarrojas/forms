Service Management Training Registration - Help Guide
==============================================

## Purpose
The Service Management Training Registration form is designed to collect participant information for service management training courses. This form helps us to track participant details and preferences for upcoming workshops. It is intended for training participants to register for a workshop and provide necessary contact and workshop information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Registration Form** fields, which include participant details such as participant name, email, phone number, and registration information.
2. Choose your preferred dates for the workshop using the **Preferred Dates** field.
3. Select your **Consent** by choosing 'yes' or 'no' to acknowledge our training terms and conditions.
4. Fill in any additional **Notes** or **Participant Comments** if necessary.
5. Provide workshop information, including the name of the course and workshop, using the **Workshop Info** fields.
6. Finally, click submit to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Registration Form
* **Registration Form** (<id: 1>): A section where you can enter your general registration information.
* **Participant Details** (<id: 2>): Enter your participant details such as participant name, email, and phone number.
* **Participant Info** (<id: 3>): Enter any additional participant information.
* **Preferred Dates** (<id: 4>): Choose your preferred dates for the workshop.
* **Consent** (<id: 5>): Select 'yes' or 'no' to acknowledge our training terms and conditions.
* **Phone** (<id: 6>): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (<id: 7>): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Notes** (<id: 8>): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Registration Info** (<id: 9>): Enter any additional registration information.
* **Registration Info Participant** (<id: 10>): Enter any additional registration information specific to the participant.
* **Participant Status** (<id: 11>): Select your participant status (active, inactive, or pending).
* **Participant Status Note** (<id: 12>): Enter any notes regarding your participant status.
* **Participant Info 2** (<id: 13>): Enter any additional participant information.
* **Participant Name** (<id: 14>): Enter your name as the participant.
* **Course Name** (<id: 15>): Enter the name of the course you're enrolling in.
* **Workshop Name** (<id: 16>): Enter the name of the workshop.
* **Participant Status Update** (<id: 17>): Enter any updates regarding your participant status.
* **Participant Comments** (<id: 18>): Enter any comments or feedback you may have.
* **Participant Comments Info** (<id: 19>): Enter any additional comments information.
* **Workshop Info** (<id: 20>): Enter any information regarding the workshop.
* **Workshop Info 1** (<id: 21>): Enter additional workshop information.
* **Workshop Info 1 Date** (<id: 22>): Enter the date of the workshop info 1.
* **Workshop Info 1 Time** (<id: 23>): Enter the time of the workshop info 1.
* **Workshop Info 2** (<id: 24>): Enter additional workshop information.
* **Workshop Info 2 Date** (<id: 25>): Enter the date of the workshop info 2.
