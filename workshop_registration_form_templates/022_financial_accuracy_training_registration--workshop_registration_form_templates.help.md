# Financial Accuracy Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering for financial accuracy training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To register for financial accuracy training, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
2.  Choose the date and time of the session you wish to attend.
3.  Indicate the duration of the session.
4.  State your goals for attending the financial accuracy training.
5.  Specify the location of the session.
6.  List the names of the session leaders.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person attending the session, as per official identification. Please use the format as per the official identification (e.g., first name last name).

* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required: false): Select the date of the session you wish to attend from the available dates.

* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, required: false): Choose the time of the session you wish to attend from the available time slots.

* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, number, required: false): Indicate the duration of the session in hours or minutes, as applicable.

* **Goals** (`goals`, text, required: false): Describe your personal goals for attending the financial accuracy training session.

* **Session Location** (`session_location`, text, required: false): Indicate the location of the session venue.

* **Session Leaders** (`session_leaders`, text, required: false): Provide the names of the leaders or instructors conducting the session.

Note: Please review the form carefully to ensure you have filled out all required fields and provided accurate information. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the session organizer for assistance.
