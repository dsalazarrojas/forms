# leadership_and_resilience_workshop_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Leadership and Resilience Workshop Registration Form" is used to collect information from individuals who wish to participate in the workshop. This form is required to ensure that we can manage and organize the workshop effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address to ensure we can contact you with important updates and information.
3. Add your Phone number so we can contact you in case of any last-minute changes.
4. Enter your Date of Birth for our records.
5. Add your Address and City to help us locate you easily.
6. Enter your State and Zip code for further contact and location purposes.
7. Mention your Organization and your Role in our Organization to help us understand your position and involvement.
8. Select the number of attendees from your organization that will be participating in the workshop.
9. Add any comments you may have, if you wish to share your thoughts or preferences.
10. Specify the Event Date and Time you will attend the workshop.
11. Finally, select the length of time you plan to stay for the workshop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is written on any official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is written on any official documents.
* **Email**: A valid email address where we can contact you with workshop updates and information.
* **Phone**: A phone number where we can contact you in case of any last-minute changes.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth for our records.
* **Address**: Your physical address where we can reach you.
* **City**: The city where you are from.
* **State**: Your state of residence.
* **Zip**: Your zip code for further contact and location purposes.
* **Organization**: The name of your organization you are representing.
* **Role in our organization**: Your position or title within the organization.
* **Workshop attendees**: Select how many people from your organization will be attending the workshop.
* **Comments**: Any additional information or comments you wish to share with us.
* **Event Date**: The date you plan to attend the workshop.
* **Event Time**: The time you plan to attend the workshop.
* **Workshop Duration**: Select the length of time you plan to stay for the workshop.

## Tips
Please make sure to complete the form accurately and truthfully. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
