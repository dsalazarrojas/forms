# Prototyping Workshop Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Prototyping Workshop Absence Form" is used to report the absence of participants in a workshop. Please fill in the form accurately to ensure attendance records are updated correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all fields as instructed, and click "Submit" to complete the form.
2. Ensure that you are submitting the form for the correct participant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Submission** (`form_submission`, `text`, required: false): Enter your comments about your absence, if any.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name as it is known to the workshop organizers.
* **Date of Absence** (`date_of_absence`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you were absent from the workshop.
* **Time of Absence** (`time_of_absence`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you were absent from the workshop.
* **Reason for Absence** (`reason_for_absence`, `text`, required: false): Explain the reason for your absence, if applicable.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact number so that workshop organizers can reach out to you.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address so that workshop organizers can contact you.
* **Participant Attendance** (`participant_attendance`, `select_one`, required: false): Select if you are attending or not attending the workshop.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments about your attendance.
* **Form submitted by** (`form_submitted_by`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name as it is known to the workshop organizers.
* **Participant** (`participant`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name or participant ID.
* **Form submitted on** (`form_submitted_on`, `date`, required: false): This field is automatically populated when you submit the form.

## Tips
* Ensure that you submit the form accurately and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the workshop organizers for clarification.
* Please fill in all required fields to ensure correct attendance records.
