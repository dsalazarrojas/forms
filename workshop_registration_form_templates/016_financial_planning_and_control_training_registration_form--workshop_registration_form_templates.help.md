# Financial Planning And Control Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a registration form for the Financial Planning and Control Training course. It is designed to collect necessary information from participants to ensure they have the required knowledge and skills in financial planning and control.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your name and any other relevant information.
2. Provide your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
3. Describe your finance background, including your current role and any relevant experience.
4. Indicate whether you are interested in attending the training program.
5. Select your available dates for attending the training.
6. Select your preferred dates for attending the training.
7. Provide any additional information about the financial planning and control training program you would like to know.
8. Fill in any additional details you would like to share with the training organizers.
9. Provide any additional details about your training course requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal details**: Provide your personal details, including your name and any other relevant information.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your email address and phone number.
* **Finance Background**: Describe your finance background, including your current role and any relevant experience.
* **Training Interests**: Select "Yes" if you are interested in attending the training program.
* **Available Dates**: Select the dates you are available to attend the training. Note that these are the dates the training is offered, and selecting "Yes" indicates you are available on those dates.
* **Preferred Dates**: Select the dates you would prefer to attend the training. These dates are not necessarily available, but rather your preferences.
* **Financial Planning and Control Training Info**: Provide any additional information about the financial planning and control training program you would like to know.
* **Finance Background Details**: You may provide additional details about your finance background if relevant.
* **Training Program Information**: You may provide additional details about the training program you are interested in.
* **Course Details**: You may provide any additional details about your training course requirements.
* **Other details**: You may provide any additional details you would like to share with the training organizers.
* **Submit**: Click the submit button to submit your registration.
