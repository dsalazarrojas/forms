# Nature Creative Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Nature Creative Workshop Registration" form is designed to collect participant information and preferences for the Nature Creative Workshop. Fill out this form to register for the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your registration information, including your participant name and email.
2. Select your preferred workshop date.
3. Fill out the participant information sections as needed to provide more details about yourself.
4. Review your selection and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Information** (workshop_registration, text, required): This field is where you enter your participant name.
* **Registration Date** (registration_date, date, required): Enter the date you are registering for the workshop.
* **Participant Name** (participant_name, text, required): Enter your name as a participant.
* **Participant Email** (participant_email, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone** (participant_phone, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Workshop Preferences** (workshop_preferences, select_multiple, required): Select your preferred workshop preferences.
* **Participant Notes** (participant_notes, note, required): Enter any additional notes about yourself.
* **Participant Preferences** (participant_preferences, text, required): Enter any additional preferences about the workshop.
* **Participant Affiliations** (participant_affiliations, text, required): Enter any relevant participant affiliations.
* **Submit Form** (submit_form, select_one, required): Review and submit your completed form.
* **Form Submitted** (form_submitted, select_one, required): A read-only field to confirm form submission.
* **Participant Survey** (participant_survey, select_multiple, required): Select your preferred participant survey options.
* **Workshop Location** (workshop_location, text, required): Enter the workshop location.
* **Workshop Date** (workshop_date, date, required): Enter the workshop date.
* **Participant Agreement** (participant_agreement, select_one, required): Review and agree to the participant agreement.
* **Participant Additional Info 1-10** (participant_additional_info_1-10, text, required): Enter any additional information about yourself.

Note: Some fields are marked as "required" and should be filled out accordingly.
