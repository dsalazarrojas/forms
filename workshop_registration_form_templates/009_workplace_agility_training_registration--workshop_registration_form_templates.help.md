# Workplace Agility Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information and preferences for the Workplace Agility Training program. Please note that this form is not to be filled out by participants who have already registered or have attended the training in the past.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure you have not already registered for this training program. If you have, please do not fill out this form again.
2. Fill out the form with your participant details, including your contact information and any special needs you may have.
3. Select your preferred session time (Morning, Afternoon, or All Day).
4. Enter any special needs or requirements you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, `text`, required/optional): Fill out your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Session Preferences** (`session_preferences`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose your preferred session time (Morning, Afternoon, or All Day).
* **Special Needs** (`special_needs`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any special needs or requirements you may have.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required/optional): Fill out any additional contact information you would like to provide.
* **Trainer Details** (`trainer_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any information related to the trainer you are registering with.

## Tips
* Please ensure you are registering under the correct category and session time.
* If you have any special needs or requirements, please enter them in the "Special Needs" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
