## Grant Writing Training Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Grant Writing Training Form is designed to collect information from participants who attend a workshop on grant writing. This form helps us understand the demographics and preferences of the participants, as well as the details of the workshop they are attending.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant name in the text field.
2. Provide your email address in the email field.
3. Enter your phone number in the phone field.
4. Enter the title of the workshop you are attending in the workshop title field.
5. Choose the start and end dates for the workshop in the start date and end date fields.
6. Provide a brief description of the workshop in the workshop description field.
7. Add any notes you have about the participant in the participant notes field.
8. Enter any notes about the grant writer in the grant writer notes field.
9. Enter the location of the workshop in the workshop location field.
10. Choose the start and end times for the workshop in the start time and end time fields.
11. Indicate the number of attendees for the workshop in the participant attendee count field.
12. List any allergies or medical conditions the participant has in the participant allergies field.
13. Indicate any dietary restrictions the participant has in the participant dietary restrictions field.
14. List any special accessibility needs the participant has in the participant accessibility needs field.
15. Enter the name of the grant writer instructor in the grant writer instructor field.
16. Enter the name of the grant writer coordinator in the grant writer coordinator field.
17. Enter the email of the grant writer in the grant writer email field.
18. Enter the phone number of the grant writer in the grant writer phone field.
19. Indicate the length of the workshop in the workshop length field.
20. Indicate the type of workshop in the workshop type field.
21. Choose the funding source for the workshop in the participant funding source field.
22. Enter the amount of funding for the workshop in the participant funding amount field.
23. Check if the participant has agreed to terms in the participant agreed to terms field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name (participant_name)**: Enter your name in this text field.
* **Email (participant_email)**: Enter your email address in this email field.
* **Phone (participant_phone)**: Enter your phone number in this text field.
* **Workshop Title (workshop_title)**: Enter the title of the workshop you are attending in this text field.
* **Workshop Start Date (workshop_start_date)**: Choose the start date for the workshop in this date field.
* **Workshop End Date (workshop_end_date)**: Choose the end date for the workshop in this date field.
* **Workshop Description (workshop_description)**: Enter a brief description of the workshop in this text field.
* **Participant Notes (participant_notes)**: Add any notes you have about the participant in this note field.
* **Grant Writer Notes (grant_writer_notes)**: Add any notes you have about the grant writer in this note field.
* **Workshop Location (workshop_location)**: Enter the location of the workshop in this text field.
* **Workshop Start Time (workshop_start_time)**: Choose the start time for the workshop in this time field.
* **Workshop End Time (workshop_end_time)**: Choose the end time for the workshop in this time field.
* **Participant Attendant Count (participant_attendee_count)**: Indicate the number of attendees for the workshop in this number field.
* **Participant Allergies (participant_allergies)**: List any allergies or medical conditions the participant has in this text field.
* **Participant Dietary Restrictions (participant_dietary_restrictions)**: Indicate any dietary restrictions the participant has in this text field.
* **Participant Accessibility Needs (participant_access_needs)**: List any special accessibility needs the participant has in this text field.
* **Grant Writer Instructor (grant_writer_instructor)**: Enter the name of the grant writer instructor in this text field.
* **Grant Writer Coordinator (grant_writer_coordinator)**: Enter the name of the grant writer coordinator in this text field.
* **Grant Writer Email (grant_writer_email)**: Enter the email of the grant writer in this email field.
* **Grant Writer Phone (grant_writer_phone)**: Enter the phone number of the grant writer in this text field.
* **Workshop Length (workshop_length)**: Indicate the length of the workshop in this number field.
* **Workshop Type (workshop_type)**: Indicate the type of workshop in this text field.
* **Participant Funding Source (participant_funding_source)**: Choose the funding source for the workshop in this select multiple field.
* **Participant Funding Amount (participant_funding_amount)**: Enter the amount of funding for the workshop in this number field.
* **Participant Agreed to Terms (participant_agreed_to_terms)**: Check if the participant has agreed to terms in this text field.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill in all the required fields to ensure that we can process your information correctly.
* If you have any special requests or needs, please make sure to include them in the participant notes field.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please feel free to ask for help.
