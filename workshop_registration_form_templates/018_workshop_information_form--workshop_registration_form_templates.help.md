# Workshop Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and store information about a workshop, which will be displayed to attendees and staff.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the workshop in the "Workshop Title" field.
2. Provide a brief description of the workshop in the "Workshop Description" field.
3. Select the category for the workshop from the available options.
4. Enter the time of the workshop in the "Workshop Time" field.
5. Enter the date of the workshop in the "Workshop Date" field.
6. Enter the location of the workshop.
7. Enter the name and contact information of the workshop organizer.
8. Enter the capacity (number of attendees) for the workshop.
9. Enter the registration deadline for the workshop.
10. Enter the price of the workshop.
11. Add any additional notes about the workshop in the "Workshop Notes" field.
12. Enter the workshop email contact information.
13. Enter the workshop phone number.
14. Upload any relevant workshop files.
15. Upload any workshop images.
16. Upload any workshop audio files.
17. Upload any workshop video files.
18. Add any other relevant information about the workshop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Workshop Title (Workshop Title)
• This is the title of the workshop, which will be displayed to attendees and staff.

### Workshop Description (Workshop Description)
• A brief description of the workshop, which should provide context and information to attendees.

### Workshop Category (Workshop Category)
• Select the category for the workshop from the available options.

### Workshop Time (Workshop Time)
• Enter the time of the workshop, which should be in 24-hour format.

### Workshop Date (Workshop Date)
• Enter the date of the workshop.

### Workshop Location (Workshop Location)
• Enter the location of the workshop.

### Workshop Organizer (Workshop Organizer)
• Enter the name and contact information of the workshop organizer.

### Workshop Capacity (Workshop Capacity)
• Enter the number of attendees the workshop is designed for.

### Workshop Registration Deadline (Workshop Registration Deadline)
• Enter the date by which attendees must register for the workshop.

### Workshop Price (Workshop Price)
• Enter the price of the workshop.

### Workshop Notes (Workshop Notes)
• Add any additional notes or comments about the workshop.

### Workshop Email (Workshop Email)
• Enter the email contact information of the workshop organizer.

### Workshop Phone (Workshop Phone)
