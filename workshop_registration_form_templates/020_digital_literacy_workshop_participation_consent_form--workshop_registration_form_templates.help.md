# Digital Literacy Workshop Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect participant information and obtain consent for participation in the digital literacy workshop. It ensures that participants understand their role and responsibilities during the workshop.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact details in the "Contact Details" section.
2. Provide emergency contact information in the "Emergency Info" section.
3. If you have any accessibility needs, please mention them in the "Accessibility Needs" section.
4. Read and acknowledge the "Participation Consent" section.
5. Provide participant details, including your name, title, and organisation, in the "Participant Details" section.
6. Enter your contact information, including your email and phone number, in the "Contact Info" section.
7. Identify your emergency contacts in the "Emergency Contacts" section.
8. Add any additional comments or notes about your participation in the "Additional Comments" section.
9. Review the "Workshop Details" section for important information about the workshop.
10. Check the "Consent to Share" box to agree to share your data with the workshop organizers.
11. Enter any additional information about your participation in the "Additional Info" section.
12. Finally, review the form carefully and submit it to complete your registration.
