# net_zero_construction_training_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from workshop attendees about their interest in attending net-zero construction training sessions. The training team will use this information to create a schedule for the training sessions based on the attendee's preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your details in the "attendee_details" field.
2. Select your session preferences by choosing "Yes" or "No" in the "select_session" field.
3. Select any requirements for the training session you are interested in by choosing "Yes" or "No" in the "select_requirements" field.
4. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Attendee Details**: <name> (<type>): This field is used to collect your name and any other information you'd like to provide about yourself.
  - **Select Session**: <name> (<type>): This field is used to select which session you are interested in attending. You can choose "Yes" for each session you'd like to attend or "No" if you are not interested.
  - **Select Requirements**: <name> (<type>): This field is used to select any requirements for the training session you are interested in. You can choose "Yes" for any requirements you need help with or "No" otherwise.
  - **Attendee Requirements**: <name> (<type>): This field is used to provide any other information about your needs or requirements for the training session.
  - **Submit**: <name> (<type>): This field is a submit button to submit your form and complete the registration process. 

Note: The "select_session" and "select_requirements" fields are both select multiple fields where you can choose multiple options by selecting "Yes" for each relevant option. 

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your information is accurately recorded.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact the training team.
- This form will be used to create a schedule for the training sessions, so please make sure to be accurate with your selections.
