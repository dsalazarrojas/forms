# holiday_crafts_workshop_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for participant registration to provide accurate and complete data for the workshop organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the Participant Details section, providing your first name and last name.
2. Proceed to the Event Details section and enter the relevant information about the event.
3. In the Workshop Details section, enter any additional details about the workshop.
4. In the Participant Permissions section, check all the boxes of the permissions you grant to the workshop organizers.
5. In the Emergency Contact section, provide the emergency contact information.
6. In the Participant Declaration sections, sign and date each section to confirm your participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details (1)**: Enter your first name and last name to identify yourself as a participant.
* **Event Details (2)**: Enter the name of the event and other relevant details.
* **Workshop Details (3)**: Enter any additional details about the workshop.
* **Participant Permissions (4)**:
	+ Check "First Name" to allow sharing of your first name.
	+ Check "Last Name" to allow sharing of your last name.
	+ Check "Phone Number" to allow sharing of your phone number.
	+ Check "Email Address" to allow sharing of your email address.
	+ Check "Parent/Guardian Info" to allow sharing of parent/guardian information.
* **Emergency Contact (5)**:
	+ Enter emergency contact name.
	+ Enter emergency contact phone number.
	+ Enter emergency contact relation (e.g., Mother, Father, Brother, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Info (6)**:
	+ Enter emergency contact address.
* **Relation (8)**:
	+ Select the relation between you and the emergency contact (e.g., Mother, Father, Brother, etc.).
* **Participant Declaration (9)**:
	+ Sign below to confirm you are attending.
* **Signature (10)**:
	+ Sign your name to confirm you are attending.
* **Participant Declaration Text (11)**:
	+ Enter a text to confirm you are aware of and permit the workshop organizers to collect and disclose information about you.
* **Date of Declaration (12)**:
	+ Enter the date you are making this declaration.
* **Time of Declaration (13)**:
	+ Enter the time you are making this declaration.
* **Participant Iphone (14)**:
	+ Enter your iPhone.
* **Participant Phone (15)**: 
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Workshop Declaration (16)**:
	+ Enter a text to confirm you are aware of and agree to the workshop terms and conditions.
* **Participant Declaration Text 2 (17)**:
	+ Enter a text to confirm you are aware of and permit the workshop organizers to share your work.
* **Signature 2 (18)**:
	+ Sign your name to confirm you are aware of and permit the workshop organizers to share your work.
* **Date of Declaration 2 (19)**:
	+ Enter the date you are making this declaration.
* **Time of Declaration 2 (20)**:
	+ Enter the time you are making this declaration.
* **Participant Iphone 2 (21)**:
	+ Enter your iPhone number.
* **Participant Phone 2 (22)**:
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Participant Declaration Text 3 (23)**:
	+ Enter a text to confirm you are aware of and permit the workshop organizers to share your work.
* **Signature 3 (24)**:
	+ Sign your name to confirm you are aware of and permit the workshop organizers to share your work.
* **Date of Declaration 3 (25)**:
	+ Enter the date you are making this declaration.

## Tips

* Make sure to sign each participant declaration section with your signature.
* Enter the correct date and time for each participant declaration.
* Check all the participant permissions that apply to you.
* Enter accurate and up-to-date information about yourself and your emergency contact.
* Review each section carefully before submitting the form.
