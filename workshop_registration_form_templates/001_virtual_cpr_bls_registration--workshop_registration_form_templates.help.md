# Virtual CPR/BLS Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants for virtual CPR/BLS (Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation/Basic Life Support) registration. It helps us understand who's attending, their details, and session preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on each field to focus on it.
2. Read the label and hint for each field.
3. Fill in the required information accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Session Scheduling** (`1`, `note`, required: false):
	* This is a brief description of the session you're registering for.
- **Participant Details** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	* Check "Yes" if you're a participant, "No" otherwise. This helps us understand who's attending.
- **Session Date** (`3`, `date`, required: false):
	* Enter the date of the session you're registering for.
- **Participant Name** (`4`, `text`, required: false):
	* Enter your name as it appears on your registration materials.
- **Participant Email** (`5`, `email`, required: false):
	* Enter your email address for correspondence and updates.
- **Participant Phone** (`6`, `text`, required: false):
	* Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Session Time** (`7`, `time`, required: false):
	* Enter the time of the session you're registering for.
- **Instructor Name** (`8`, `text`, required: false):
	* Enter the name of the instructor or presenter.
- **Course Name** (`9`, `text`, required: false):
	* Enter the name of the course you're registering for.
- **Course Location** (`10`, `text`, required: false):
	* Enter the location of the course you'll be attending.
