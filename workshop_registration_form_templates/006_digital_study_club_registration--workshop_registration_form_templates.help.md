# Digital Study Club Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who wish to register for a digital study club. The form gathers details about the user's membership type, interests, format preferences, session format, preferred date and time, session preferences, email, phone number, and any additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your membership type by choosing from the options provided.
2. Enter your interests in the text field.
3. Choose your format preferences from the available options.
4. Select your session format (30 minutes, 1 hour, or 2 hours) in the dropdown menu.
5. Choose your preferred date and time from the available options.
6. Select your session preferences (Virtual, In-person, or Both) in the dropdown menu.
7. Enter your email address in the format "example@example.com".
8. Enter your phone number in the format "123-456-7890".
9. Add any additional notes if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Details** (User Details, text): Enter your name, contact information, or any relevant details.
- **Membership Type** (Member, Non-Member, Guest, select multiple): Select whether you're a member, non-member, or guest of the digital study club.
- **Interests** (text): Describe your interests or areas of study.
- **Format Preferences** (Online, Offline, Both, select one): Choose how you prefer to participate in the study club (online or offline).
- **Session Format** (30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours, select one): Select the duration of your desired session.
- **Preferred Date and Time** (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, select one): Choose a day and time that suits you best.
- **Session Preferences** (Virtual, In-person, Both, select multiple): Select how you prefer to attend sessions (virtually or in-person).
- **Email** (text, email): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
- **Phone** (text, text): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Notes** (text, note): Add any additional information or comments that may be helpful for the study club.
