# Plant Forward Innovation Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering your interest in the Plant Forward Innovation Program. The program aims to support innovative projects that drive positive change. 
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the fields in the following order:
1. "Program Name" field (id: 1)
2. "Email" field (id: 2)
3. Optional fields (id: 3-9) as you see fit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Name (id: 1, text, required)**: Enter your name which will be used as a reference for your registration.
* **Email (id: 2, email, required)**: Enter your email address for communication and notifications.
* **Date of Birth (id: 3, date, optional)**: Enter your date of birth for identity verification.
* **Phone (id: 4, text, optional)**: Enter your contact number for future communication.
* **Address (id: 5, text, optional)**: Enter your address for location verification.
* **Bio (id: 6, text, optional)**: Describe yourself and your interests in the program.
* **Research areas of interest (id: 7, text, optional)**: Specify your area of research interest.
* **Program/Institution (id: 8, text, optional)**: Verify your affiliation with a specific program or institution.
* **Program Location (id: 9, text, optional)**: Enter the location of your program or research.
## Tips
Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly. The program will review your submission and contact you with further instructions.
