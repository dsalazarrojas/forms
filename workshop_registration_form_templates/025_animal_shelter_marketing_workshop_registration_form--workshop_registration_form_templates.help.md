# Animal Shelter Marketing Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to gather information about the participant, their organization, and their preferences for the workshop to ensure a tailored and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Full Name**: Enter your first and last name.
2. **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
3. **Organization Name**: Enter the name of your organization (shelter or rescue).
4. **Organization Type**: Choose the type of organization you represent (Animal Shelter, Rescue Organization, Animal Sanctuary, etc.).
5. **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address.
6. **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
7. **Street Address**: Enter your organization's street address (optional).
8. **City**: Enter the city where your organization is located (optional).
9. **State or Province**: Enter the state or province where your organization is located (optional).
10. **ZIP or Postal Code**: Enter your organization's ZIP or postal code (optional).
11. **Select Workshop Date**: Choose a preferred workshop date (in-person or virtual).
12. **Marketing Experience Level**: Choose your level of marketing experience (Beginner - New to Marketing, Intermediate - Some Experience, etc.).
13. **Current Marketing Challenges**: Select all relevant marketing challenges you're facing (Limited Budget, Limited Staff Time, etc.).
14. **Topics of Interest**: Select all topics that interest you the most (Social Media Strategy, Content Marketing, etc.).
15. **Organization Size**: Choose the number of staff you have in your organization.
16. **Annual Marketing Budget**: Approximate the annual marketing budget of your organization (optional).
17. **Social Media Platforms**: Select all the social media platforms you use.
18. **Learning Goals**: Enter your goals for attending the workshop (what you hope to achieve).
19. **Registration Type**: Choose the type of registration you're using (Individual, Team, etc.).
20. **Number of Attendees**: Enter the total number of attendees from your organization (if registering a team).
21. **Workshop Fee**: Note that the workshop fee is based on your registration type (optional).
22. **Payment Method**: Select how you'd like to pay for the workshop (Credit Card, Debit Card, etc.).
23. **Dietary Requirements**: Select any dietary requirements for in-person sessions (None, Vegetarian, etc.).
24. **Accessibility Needs**: If you have any accessibility needs, please enter them in the text field.
25. **Referral Source**: If you were referred by a colleague or another source, select that option.
26. **Continuing Education Units**: If you need continuing education units, select that option (if applicable).
27. **Additional Comments or Questions**: If you have any other comments or questions, enter them here (optional).
28. **Terms and Cancellation Policy Acceptance**: Confirm that you accept the workshop terms and cancellation policy.

## Tips
- Please fill out all required fields accurately.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact your workshop representative for guidance.
- Review your answers before submitting to ensure accuracy.
