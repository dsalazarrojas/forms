# Occupational Therapy Innovation Forum Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from users interested in attending the Occupational Therapy Innovation Forum. It will help us create a record of attendees and organize the event more effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your contact information in the "contact info" field.
2. Provide details about the forum you are interested in attending in the "forum details" field.
3. Share event-related information in the "event details" field.
4. Enter your registration information in the "registration info" field.
5. Click the "submit" button to send your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **contact info** (contact_info, text, optional):
    This field is where you'll enter your name and contact details to help us reach out to you after the event.
* **forum details** (forum_details, text, optional):
    Share information about the forum, such as the topic or purpose, to help us better understand your interest in the forum.
* **event details** (event_details, text, optional):
    Provide any relevant details about the event you are interested in attending, like the date, time, or location.
* **registration info** (registration_info, text, optional):
    Enter any registration information you'd like to share with us, such as your role in the field or any specific requirements you may have.
* **submit** (submit, text, optional):
    Click this button to send your completed form to us.
