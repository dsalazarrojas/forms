# hr_process_innovation_workshop_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register individuals for an HR Process Innovation Workshop, a workshop focused on process improvement for HR teams and professionals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your details, including name, email, phone number, department, job title, manager's name, and manager's email.
2. Choose the workshop type (A, B, or C) that suits your interests.
3. Select the workshop date and start time that fits your schedule.
4. Click "Submit" to register for the workshop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Title** (`workshop_title`, text, optional): This is the title of the workshop, for informational purposes only.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Please enter your full name as you would like it to appear on our records.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your work email address to receive updates and communications about the workshop.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your work phone number, in case we need to contact you about the workshop.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Specify your department or team to help us better understand your interests and preferences.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title to help us better understand your role and position.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): If you would like, please enter your manager's name, so they can be informed about your registration.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, optional): Enter your manager's email address, in case we need to communicate with them about the workshop.
* **Workshop Type** (`workshop_type`, select one, optional): Choose the workshop type that best fits your interests (A, B, or C).
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, optional): Select the date you would like to attend the workshop.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Choose the start time that suits your schedule.

Please note that all fields are optional, but it's recommended to fill in as much information as possible to ensure we can provide you with the best experience at the workshop.
