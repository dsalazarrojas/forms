# Educational Philosophy Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Philosophy Forum Registration form is designed to collect participant information for educational workshops.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the category of the workshop you want to participate in.
2.  Enter your participant name.
3.  Provide your email address for contact purposes.
4.  Enter your contact number.
5.  Indicate your interests in the workshop (e.g., Yes/No).
6.  Read and consent to the code of conduct.
7.  Choose the workshop attendance option (Yes/No).
8.  Enter the workshop name.
9.  Enter the participant ID.
10.  Select the form ID.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Category** (`1`, Text, required): Select the category of the workshop.
*   **Participant Name** (`2`, Text, required): Enter your participant name.
*   **Email** (`3`, Email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
*   **Contact Number** (`4`, Text, required): Enter your contact number.
*   **Participant Interests** (`5`, Select Multiple, required): Indicate your interests in the workshop (Yes/No).
*   **Code of Conduct Consent** (`6`, Note, required): Read and consent to the code of conduct.
*   **Workshop Attendance** (`8`, Select One, required): Choose your workshop attendance option (Yes/No).
*   **Workshop Name** (`23`, Text, required): Enter the name of the workshop you are registering for.
*   **Participant ID** (`21`, Text, required): Enter a unique participant ID for tracking purposes.
*   **Form ID** (`25`, Text, required): Enter a unique form ID for workshop registration.

Note: Fields marked as "required" are mandatory and must be filled in order to complete the form.
