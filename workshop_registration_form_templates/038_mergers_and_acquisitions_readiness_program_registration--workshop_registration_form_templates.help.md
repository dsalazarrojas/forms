# Mergers And Acquisitions Readiness Program Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals or teams to register for the Mergers And Acquisitions Readiness Program. The program's purpose is to provide guidance and resources for organizations preparing for or in the process of mergers and acquisitions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your First Name.
2.  Provide your Email.
3.  Select your Role.
4.  Choose your Team Size.
5.  Enter your Objectives.
6.  Choose your Target Date Commitment.
7.  Optionally, enter your Contact Number, Organization, Department, Team Members, Objectives Details, and Additional Comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
*   **Role** (select_one, required): Choose your role from the provided options: Advisor, Consultant, or Corporate Team.
*   **Team Size** (select_multiple, required): Select the number of people in your team:
    + 1-10
    + 11-20
    + 21-50
    + 51+
*   **Objectives** (text, required/optional): Briefly describe your objectives for the program.
*   **Target Date Commitment** (time, required): Choose a target date for your commitment.
*   **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
*   **Contact Number** (text, required/optional): Enter your contact number.
*   **Organization** (text, required/optional): Enter your organization's name.
*   **Department** (text, required/optional): Enter your department name.
*   **Team Members** (text, required/optional): List the names and titles of your team members.
*   **Objectives Details** (text, required/optional): Provide further details about your objectives.
*   **Additional Comments** (text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you have.

Note: The Target Date Commitment field (ID 13) is likely redundant and could be removed or consolidated with the Target Date field (ID 6).
