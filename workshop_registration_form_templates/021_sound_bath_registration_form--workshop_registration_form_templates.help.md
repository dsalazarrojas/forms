# Sound Bath Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sound Bath Registration Form is used to register attendees for a sound bath workshop. It collects personal and preference information to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your title and select the correct session time and date that suit your schedule.
2. Enter your first and last name in the respective fields.
3. Provide a valid email address and phone number where you can be contacted.
4. Share any health information that we need to know about you (optional).
5. In the "Attendee Details" field, provide any additional information about your preferences or requirements (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form-title** (Form Title, text, required: false): Enter the title you would like to be displayed on your sound bath session.
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required: false): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Health Information** (Health Information, text, required: false): Share any health information that we need to know about you.
* **Preferred Session Time** (Preferred Session Time, select_one, required: false): Select your preferred session time from the options provided (08:00, 720, 840, 960, 1080, 1200).
* **Attendee Details** (Attendee Details, text, required: false): Provide any additional information about your preferences or requirements.
* **Preferred Session Date** (Preferred Session Date, date, required: false): Select your preferred session date.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate information, especially your email address and phone number.
* If you have any health concerns or requirements, please share them with us so we can ensure a comfortable experience for you.
* Please select your preferred session time and date carefully, as these will be used to schedule your sound bath session.
