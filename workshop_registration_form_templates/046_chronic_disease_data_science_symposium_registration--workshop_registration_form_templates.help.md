# Chronic Disease Data Science Symposium Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chronic Disease Data Science Symposium Registration form is designed to collect information from registrants to provide a tailored experience. This form gathers data about registrants' background and preferences for the symposium.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal and professional details.
2. Select your relevant background, experience, and interests.
3. Choose your session preferences and registration type.
4. Provide any additional comments or questions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (<code>first_name</code>, <type>text</type>): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (<code>last_name</code>, <type>text</type>): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (<code>email</code>, <type>email</type>): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (<code>phone</code>, <type>text</type>): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Organization/Institution** (<code>affiliation</code>, <type>text</type>): Enter your work or institution name.
* **Job Title or Position** (<code>job_title</code>, <type>text</type>): Enter your job title or position.
* **Primary Professional Role** (<code>professional_role</code>, <type>select_one</type>): Select your primary professional role, such as:
	+ Data Scientist
	+ Healthcare Provider
	+ Researcher
	+ Public Health Official
	+ Industry Professional
	+ Student
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Years of Experience in Field** (<code>experience_level</code>, <type>select_one</type>): Select the number of years you have been working in the field, such as:
	+ Less than 1 year
	+ 1-3 years
	+ 3-5 years
	+ 5-10 years
	+ Over 10 years
* **Area of Expertise** (<code>expertise_area</code>, <type>select_multiple</type>): Select all areas you have expertise in:
	+ Data Analysis
	+ Machine Learning
	+ Public Health
	+ Clinical Research
	+ Disease Epidemiology
	+ Health Informatics
	+ Data Visualization
	+ Statistical Analysis
* **Symposium Date** (<code>s Symposium_date</code>, <type>date</type>): Enter the date of the symposium.
* **Registration Type** (<code>registration_type</code>, <type>select_one</type>): Select your registration type, such as:
	+ Full Conference
	+ Single Day
	+ Workshop only
	+ Virtual attendance
	+ Group registration
* **Session Preferences** (<code>sessions_interest</code>, <type>select_multiple</type>): Select the sessions you are interested in, such as:
	+ Keynote Address
	+ Data Analytics in Healthcare
	+ Machine Learning Applications
	+ Policy and Prevention
	+ Case Studies
	+ Networking Session
	+ Workshop 1
	+ Workshop 2
* **Dietary Restrictions** (<code>dietary_restrictions</code>, <type>text</type>): If you have dietary restrictions, please specify.
* **Accessibility Requirements** (<code>accessibility_needs</code>, <type>text</type>): If you have accessibility requirements, please specify.
* **Any Other Accommodation Needs?** (<code>accommodation_needs</code>, <type>text</type>): If you have any other accommodation needs, please specify.
* **Current Research Focus or Interest** (<code>research_focus</code>, <type>text</type>): Please specify your current research focus or interest.
* **Interested in Networking Opportunities?** (<code>networking_interest</code>, <type>select_one</type>): Indicate your interest in networking opportunities, such as:
	+ True
	+ Maybe
	+ False
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (<code>additional_comments</code>, <type>text</type>): If you have any additional comments or questions, please provide them here.
