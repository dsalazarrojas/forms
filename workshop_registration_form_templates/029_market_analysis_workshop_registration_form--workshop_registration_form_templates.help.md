# market_analysis_workshop_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The market analysis workshop registration form is designed for participants to register for the market analysis workshop. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:

1. Ensure you have all required information before starting the form. While most fields are not required, providing your correct information will help us better serve you.
2. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, Email, Organization, and Role (if applicable).
3. If you have a phone number, enter your phone number.
4. Select your preferred payment method (bank transfer, credit card, or PayPal).
5. Confirm your acceptance of the terms and conditions.
6. Choose your payment status (paid, pending, or not paid).
7. Finally, provide your Date of Birth.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (text, required: false): Enter your first name as you would like it to be displayed.
- **Last Name** (text, required: false): Enter your last name as you would like it to be displayed.
- **Email** (email, required: false): Enter your valid email address. 
- **Organization** (text, required: false): If you are registering as part of an organization, enter the name of your organization.
- **Role** (text, required: false): If you have a specific role within your organization, enter it here. 
- **Phone** (text, required: false): Enter your phone number if you want us to contact you.
- **Payment Method** (select one, required: false): Select one of the following options: bank transfer, credit card, or PayPal. 
- **Terms and Conditions** (select multiple, required: false): Confirm your acceptance of the terms and conditions. 
  * Please note that this field has a binary answer of 'Yes' or 'No', and it's a required field. 
- **Payment Status** (select one, required: false): Choose your current payment status: paid, pending, or not paid. 
- **Date of Birth** (date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD). 
  * Please note that this field might be used for internal tracking purposes.
