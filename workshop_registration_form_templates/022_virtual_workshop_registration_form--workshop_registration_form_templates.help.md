# Virtual Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for participants to register their interest in attending a virtual workshop. It collects necessary information to provide a smooth and enjoyable experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name.
2. Enter your E-mail address to ensure we can contact you with updates and details.
3. Provide your Phone number for easy communication.
4. Choose the name of the workshop you are interested in.
5. Select the date and time you plan to attend the workshop.
6. Enter the URL of the workshop (if available).
7. Provide any additional details about the workshop.
8. Choose your participant type (Participant or Organizer).
9. Enter any additional details specific to your participant type (e.g., for Organizers).
10. Select your preferred workshop language (English, French, Spanish, or Chinese).
11. Enter your gender (Male, Female, or Other).
12. Enter any additional details specific to your gender (e.g., for Participants who are part of a specific group).
13. Provide your participant's language.
14. Enter your occupation.
15. Enter your position in your institution.
16. Enter your institution's name.
17. Enter your address.
18. Enter your zip code.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **E-mail**: Enter your valid email address to receive updates and notifications.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for easy communication.
* **Workshop Name**: Choose the name of the workshop you are interested in.
* **Date**: Select the date you plan to attend the workshop.
* **Time**: Select the time you plan to attend the workshop.
* **Workshop URL**: Enter the URL of the workshop (if available).
* **Workshop Details**: Provide any additional details about the workshop.
* **Participant Type**: Choose whether you are a Participant or an Organizer.
* **Participant Type Option1**, **Participant Type Option2**, **Participant Type Option3**: These fields are specific to Organizers and provide additional details about their role.
* **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant.
* **Participant E-mail**: Enter the email of the participant.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter the phone number of the participant.
* **Participant Country**: Enter the country of the participant.
* **Participant Company**: Enter the name of the participant's company.
* **Workshop Language**: Select the language you would like to attend the workshop in (English, French, Spanish, or Chinese).
* **Participant Language**: Enter the language spoken by the participant.
* **Participant Gender**: Select your gender (Male, Female, or Other).
* **Participant Gender Option1**, **Participant Gender Option2**, **Participant Gender Option3**: These fields are specific to participants and provide additional details about their gender.
* **Participant Occupation**: Enter your occupation.
* **Participant Position**: Enter your position in your institution.
* **Participant Institution**: Enter the name of your institution.
* **Participant Address**: Enter your address.
* **Participant Zip**: Enter your zip code.

Note: Fields marked as "Participant Type Option" and "Participant Gender Option" are only visible when the participant is of a specific type or gender.
