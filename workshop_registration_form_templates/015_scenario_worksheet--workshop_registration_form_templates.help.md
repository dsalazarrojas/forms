# Scenario Worksheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Scenario Worksheet" form is designed to collect information from workshop participants, specifically regarding their experience with a particular scenario or problem. The form helps to understand individual responses and team collaboration, and provides valuable insights for workshop facilitators to tailor their sessions accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Begin by providing base information (page 1). 
2. Answer questions about team composition (page 2).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Base Info (Page 1)
* **Base Info** (Text field, Optional): Please provide a brief background about your professional experience and expertise related to the scenario. This will help workshop facilitators understand your context and prepare for your participation in the workshop.

### Team Member (Page 2)
* **Team Member** (Select Multiple, Optional): Indicate whether you are a member of a team that will be participating in the workshop. This will help the facilitators to understand the team dynamics and tailor the workshop activities accordingly.

### Workshop Details (Page 3)
* **Workshop Details** (Date field, Optional): Note down any relevant details about the workshop schedule or requirements. This will help the facilitators to prepare for your needs and expectations.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling in your responses.
- If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask your workshop facilitator for clarification.
- Your input will help create a better experience for you and your team.
