# Online Assessment Innovation Challenge Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Assessment Innovation Challenge Registration Form is a form used to collect registration data from participants of the Online Assessment Innovation Challenge.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the first field.
2. Enter your last name in the second field.
3. Enter your email address in the third field, following the standard email format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
4. Select your date of birth using the date picker input.
5. Enter your phone number in the fifth field.
6. Select your preferred language from the dropdown menu.
7. If you have any additional comments or notes, enter them in the last field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`form_first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`form_last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`form_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address in the standard format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
* **Date of Birth** (`form_date_of_birth`, date, optional): Select your date of birth using the date picker input.
* **Phone** (`form_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Country** (`form_select_country`, select_one, optional): Select your country from the dropdown menu (e.g., USA, Canada, Mexico).
* **Language** (`form_select_language`, select_multiple, optional): Select your preferred language from the dropdown menu (e.g., English, Spanish, French, Arabic).
* **Note** (`form_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes you have about your registration.
