# Educational Leadership In Crisis Management Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register participants in educational leadership crisis management workshops. It collects essential information from individuals, including their name, contact details, and department affiliation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your first name and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your valid email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. If you are participating in the workshop, fill in the "Date" field with the date you intend to attend.
5. If applicable, select your preferred time slot for the workshop in the "Time" field.
6. Optionally, add any additional notes about your participation in the "Note" field.
7. Select your department from the "Department" dropdown menu, which is a required field. Currently, options are Department of Education, Department of Health, and Department of Public Safety.
8. If you have a preferred department affiliation but it's not listed, you can select "Other" in the "Department" select multiple field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address you check regularly.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Select the date you intend to participate in the workshop.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Choose a time slot for the workshop if applicable.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes about your participation.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required): Select your department from the options: Department of Education, Department of Health, or Department of Public Safety.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_multiple`, optional): If your department is not listed, select "Other" to specify your department.
