# Data Literacy Workshop Broadcast Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect participant information and consent for a data literacy workshop broadcast. By filling out this form, participants will be able to provide their details, consent to broadcast, and indicate their preferences for contact and recording.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your participant information, including your name and email address.
2. Indicate your consent to broadcast by selecting the relevant options.
3. Choose your contact preference for the broadcast.
4. Indicate your preference for recording and additional comments.
5. Provide any additional notes or information if necessary.
6. Add your signature as a participant.
7. Add your trainer's signature and name.
8. Provide the workshop details, including date, time, location, and length.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`):
	+ Please enter your name and any other relevant participant details.
* **Email** (`email`):
	+ Enter your email address so we can contact you about the workshop.
* **Name** (`name`):
	+ Enter your name as it should appear on the broadcast.
* **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you consent to be broadcasted during the workshop.
* **Consent to Broadcast** (`consent_to_broadcast`):
	+ Select the platforms you consent to be broadcasted on (e.g., television, radio, internet).
* **Recording Consent** (`recording_consent`):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you consent to be recorded during the workshop.
* **Contact Preference** (`contact_preference`):
	+ Choose how you would like to be contacted after the workshop (e.g., email, phone, mail).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`):
	+ Add any additional comments or information about your participation.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`):
	+ Enter the date of the workshop (optional).
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`):
	+ Enter the time of the workshop (optional).
* **Workshop Location** (`workshop_location`):
	+ Enter the location of the workshop (optional).
* **Broadcast Television** (`broadcast_television`):
	+ Select the platforms you consent to be broadcasted on (e.g., television).
* **Broadcast Radio** (`broadcast_radio`):
	+ Select the platforms you consent to be broadcasted on (e.g., radio).
* **Broadcast Internet** (`broadcast_internet`):
	+ Select the platforms you consent to be broadcasted on (e.g., internet).
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`):
	+ Add any additional notes or information about your participation.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`):
	+ Sign this form as a participant.
* **Trainer Signature** (`trainer_signature`):
	+ Sign this form as the trainer.
* **Trainer Name** (`trainer_name`):
	+ Enter your name as the trainer.
* **Trainer Title** (`trainer_title`):
	+ Enter your title as the trainer.
* **Workshop Coordinators** (`workshop_coordinators`):
	+ Enter the names of the workshop coordinators.
* **Workshop Sponsors** (`workshop_sponsors`):
	+ Enter the names of the workshop sponsors.
* **Workshop Participants** (`workshop_participants`):
	+ Enter the number of workshop participants.
* **Workshop Length** (`workshop_length`):
	+ Enter the length of the workshop in minutes.
* **Workshop Duration** (`workshop_duration`):
	+ Enter the duration of the workshop in minutes.
* **Workshop Language** (`workshop_language`):
	+ Enter the language of the workshop (e.g., English, Spanish, French).

Note: Some fields may have limited options or are optional, but they are all included in this explanation to provide a comprehensive understanding of the form.
