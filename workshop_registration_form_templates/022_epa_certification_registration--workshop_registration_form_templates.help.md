# epa_certification_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from employees about their Employee Professional Association (EPA) certification process. This form is used to track and manage employee certification registrations, ensuring that all required details are captured and stored accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Ensure you have all required information before starting the form.
2. Fill out the form in a clear and accurate manner.
3. Review the form for any errors or missing information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Details** (`applicant_details`): This field is for entering your name and other identifying information.
* **Training Date** (`training_date`): Please enter the date on which you completed the training required for the certification.
* **Training Provider** (`training_provider`): Enter the name of the training provider that conducted the certification training.
* **Proof of Completion** (`proof_of_completion`): Upload or enter proof of completion of the training (e.g., certificate, diploma).
* **Completion Date** (`completion_date`): Enter the date on which you completed the certification process.
* **Submission** (`submission`): Describe your submission or upload supporting documentation for your certification.
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`): If you have any comments or feedback, please provide them here.
* **Review Status** (`review_status`): Select the status of your review from the options provided (Active or Inactive).

## Tips

- Double-check all fields for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant personnel.
