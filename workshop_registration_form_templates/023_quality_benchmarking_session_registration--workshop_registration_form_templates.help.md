# Quality Benchmarking Session Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from participants for a quality benchmarking session. It will help us understand your details, topic interests, and session preferences to better organize and facilitate the session.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on each field to focus on it.
2. Fill out the required fields (indicated by a star <\*).
3. Select one or more options for the "Topic interests" field.
4. Choose your preferred session time from the dropdown menu.
5. Enter any comments or additional information you'd like to provide.
6. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant details** (<participant_details>): Enter your name and any other details you'd like to share with us.
* **Topic interests** (<topic_interests>): Select one or more options that best represent your interests in this quality benchmarking session. 
    + Quality Improvement
    + Benchmarking
    + Other (please specify in the "Comments" field)
* **Session time** (<session_time>): Choose your preferred session time from the following options:
    + 9:00-11:00
    + 11:00-13:00
    + 13:00-15:00
* **Contact info** (<contact_info>): Enter any contact information you'd like to share with us, such as your email or phone number.
* **Comments** (<comments>): Provide any additional comments or information about your participation in this quality benchmarking session.
