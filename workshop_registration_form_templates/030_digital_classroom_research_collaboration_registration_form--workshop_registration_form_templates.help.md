# Digital Classroom Research Collaboration Registration Form - Help Guide

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Input your mobile number (if applicable).
4. Choose your department affiliation.
5. Provide the title of your research project.
6. Indicate whether you have collaborators on your project.
7. Select your role in the project.
8. Enter the name of your institution (if applicable).
9. Select your university (if applicable).
10. Enter your departmental affiliation (if applicable).
11. Specify your research area.
12. Provide a brief description of your research project.
13. Enter the submission date of your research.
14. Select the deadline for your research completion.
15. If desired, specify the time slot for your project.
16. Include any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**
	* A required field where you enter your first name.
* **Last name**
	* A required field where you enter your last name.
* **Email**
	* Enter your email address.
* **Mobile number**
	* Enter your mobile number (if applicable).
* **Department**
	* Choose your department affiliation.
* **Research title**
	* Enter the title of your research project.
* **Collaborators**
	* Indicate whether you have collaborators on your project.
* **Role**
	* Select your role in the project (Principal Investigator, Co-Principal Investigator, Research Assistant, or Other).
* **Institution**
	* Enter the name of your institution (if applicable).
* **University**
	* Select your university (if applicable).
* **Departmental affiliation**
	* Enter your departmental affiliation (if applicable).
* **Research area**
	* A required field where you specify your research area.
* **Research description**
	* A brief description of your research project.
* **Submission date**
	* Enter the submission date of your research.
* **Deadline**
	* Select the deadline for your research completion.
* **Time**
	* If desired, specify the time slot for your project.
* **Note**
	* Include any additional comments or notes.

## Tips

* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submission.
* Make sure to specify your research area and project title clearly.
* If you have collaborators, ensure you select 'Yes' in the 'Collaborators' field.
* Enter the correct submission date and deadline for your research project.
* Use the 'Note' field for any additional comments or clarifications. 

This guide is intended to be user-friendly and assist you in navigating the form efficiently. If you need further clarification on any field, please do not hesitate to contact us.
