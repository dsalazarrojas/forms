# VLSI Training Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The VLSI Training Program Registration form is used to gather information from users who want to register for the program. Please complete this form carefully to ensure that we have an accurate understanding of your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

* Read each section carefully and fill in the required fields.
* Make sure to select the correct options for each field.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant details** (`participant_details`, text, required/optional): This section is intended to capture basic information about the participant, such as name, contact number, and other relevant details.
* **Session preferences** (`session_preferences`, select_one, required/optional): Please select one option from the list that best suits your needs.
* **Motivations** (`motivations`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the reasons why you want to participate in the VLSI Training Program.
* **Participant notes** (`participant_notes`, note, required/optional): Write any additional information about your needs and preferences that are not captured in the above fields.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further details.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Provide your contact number for any additional communication.
* **Session date** (`session_date`, date, required/optional): Select a date for your session from the available dates.
* **Session time** (`session_time`, time, required/optional): Select a time for your session from the available times.
* **Session time AM/PM** (`session_time_am_pm`, select_one, required/optional): Choose AM or PM to confirm the session time.
* **Session duration** (`session_duration`, number, required/optional): Select the duration of your session from the available options.
* **Participant details form** (`participant_details_form`, select_one, required/optional): Choose a form type to capture additional participant details.

## Tips

* Make sure to select the correct options for each field to ensure that we have accurate information about your needs and preferences.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, please contact us at [insert contact email or phone number].
