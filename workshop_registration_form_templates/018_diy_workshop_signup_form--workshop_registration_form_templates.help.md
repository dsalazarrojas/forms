# DIY Workshop Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants to register for a DIY workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Create" button to start filling out the form.
2. Provide the required information for each field:
	* Workshop Name: Enter the name of the workshop you want to sign up for.
	* Instructor Name: Enter the name of the instructor or staff member who will lead the workshop.
	* Email: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
	* Phone Number: Enter your phone number for follow-up communications.
	* Date: Select the date you plan to attend the workshop.
	* Time: Select the time you plan to attend the workshop.
	* Participant Details: Add any additional details about your participation or special requests.
3. Review and submit the form once you've filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Workshop Name (`workshop_name`)
* Enter the name of the workshop you want to sign up for.

### Instructor Name (`instructor_name`)
* Enter the name of the instructor or staff member who will lead the workshop.

### Email (`email`)
* Enter your email address for contact purposes.

### Phone Number (`phone_number`)
* Enter your phone number for follow-up communications.

### Date (`date`)
* Select the date you plan to attend the workshop.

### Time (`time`)
* Select the time you plan to attend the workshop.

### Participant Details (`participant_details`)
* Add any additional details about your participation or special requests.

## Tips
* Please ensure to fill out all required fields to avoid any issues with your registration.
* If you need to make any special requests or have any questions, feel free to contact us.
* Our team will be in touch with you shortly after submission of this form.
