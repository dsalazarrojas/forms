# Machine Learning In Production Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Machine Learning In Production Training Registration form is designed to collect registration information from attendees for the Machine Learning In Production Training program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your Email in the "Email" field, which must be a valid email address.
4. Select your Experience Level from the "Experience Level" dropdown menu:
	* Beginner: No prior experience in machine learning.
	* Intermediate: Some experience in machine learning.
	* Advanced: Extensive experience in machine learning.
5. Choose your Payment Method from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu:
	* PayPal: Payment will be processed through PayPal.
	* Stripe: Payment will be processed through Stripe.
	* Other: Specify other payment methods (please provide details in the "Note" field).
6. Enter the Date you would like to attend the training in the "Date" field.
7. Select the Time you would like to attend the training in the "Time" field.
8. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
9. Specify the Payment Amount for the training.

* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name.
