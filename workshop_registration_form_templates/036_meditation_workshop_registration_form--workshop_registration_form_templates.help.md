# meditation_workshop_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users interested in registering for a meditation workshop. It is essential to fill out this form to help us better understand your needs and provide the best experience for you.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out all the required fields marked as required.
- Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed on any communication related to the workshop.
- Enter a valid email address that can be used to communicate with you about the workshop details.
- If you have a phone number, please enter it for us to contact you in case of any updates or changes.
- Select the desired workshop date from the provided options.
- Select the desired workshop time from the provided options.
- Enter the number of participants you plan to bring with you.
- Add any additional comments or notes about your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (Full Name, text, required): Please enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed on any communication related to the workshop.
- **email_address** (email, email, required): Enter a valid email address that can be used to communicate with you about the workshop details.
- **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): If you have a phone number, please enter it for us to contact you in case of any updates or changes.
- **Date** (Date, date, required): Select the desired workshop date from the provided options.
- **Time** (Time, time, required): Select the desired workshop time from the provided options.
- **Location** (Location, text, optional): This field is not currently used but may be used in the future for additional information about the workshop location.
- **Workshop Date** (Workshop Date, date, required): This is the date of the meditation workshop.
- **Workshop Time** (Workshop Time, time, required): This is the time of the meditation workshop.
- **Number of Participants** (Number of Participants, number, required): Enter the number of participants you plan to bring with you to the workshop.
- **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your registration.
