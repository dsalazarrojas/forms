# Automation Standards Conference Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from attendees, speakers, sponsors, and exhibitors for the Automation Standards Conference.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form in its entirety, making sure to provide all required information.
- For each field, provide accurate and relevant information.
- If a field is marked as optional, you can leave it blank if not applicable.
- Review your answers for spelling and grammar before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required)
Enter your first name as it is written on your identification.

### 2. **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required)
Enter your last name as it is written on your identification.

### 3. **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required)
Enter your email address where confirmation will be sent.

### 4. **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required)
Enter your phone number where contact can be made.

### 5. **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required)
Enter the name of your company as it is known.

### 6. **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required)
Enter your job title within the company.

### 7. **Department** (`department`, `text`, optional)
Enter the department you belong to if applicable.

### 8. **Company Industry** (`company_industry`, `select_one`, required)
Select the industry your company belongs to from the list.

### 9. **Name on Badge** (`badge_name`, `text`, required)
Enter how you would like your name to be displayed on your badge.

### 10. **Attendee Type** (`attendee_type`, `select_one`, required)
Select whether you are attending as a general attendee, speaker, sponsor, or exhibitor.

### 11. **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, `date`, required)
Enter your expected date of registration.

### 12. **Day 1 Sessions** (`day_1_sessions`, `select_multiple`, required)
Select all preferred sessions for Day 1.

### 13. **Day 2 Sessions** (`day_2_sessions`, `select_multiple`, required)
Select all preferred sessions for Day 2.

### 14. **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, `select_multiple`, required)
Select any dietary restrictions you have.

### 15. **Accessibility Requirements** (`accessibility_needs`, `text`, optional)
If you have any accessibility needs, enter them here.

### 16. **Areas of Interest** (`areas_of_interest`, `select_multiple`, required)
Select all areas of interest that apply.

### 17. **T-Shirt Size** (`t_shirt_size`, `select_one`, required)
Select your t-shirt size.

### 18. **Subscribe to Future Newsletters** (`newsletter_signup`, `select_one`, required)
Select whether you want to subscribe to future newsletters.

### 19. **Additional Comments or Questions** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional)
Enter any additional comments or questions you have.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled out to avoid any issues with submission.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer back to this help guide for guidance.
- If you have any further questions, contact the conference organizers.
