# Glasswork Presentation Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Glasswork Presentation Registration form is used to collect attendee information for a workshop presentation. This form is used to gather essential details such as name, email, phone number, event information, and more. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Provide event information in the "Event Info" field.
5. Select one option from the "Select One" dropdown list.
6. Select multiple options from the "Select Multiple" dropdown list. 
7. Optionally, add any additional comments or notes in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input (Required: False)**: Enter any relevant information about yourself.
* **Email (Required: False)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (Required: False)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Event Info (Required: False)**: Provide any additional event information.
* **Name (Required: False)**: Enter your name.
* **Date (Required: False)**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Time (Required: False)**: Enter the time of the event.
* **Select One (Required: True)**: Select one option from the dropdown list.
* **Select Multiple (Required: True)**: Select multiple options from the dropdown list.
* **Note (Required: False)**: Add any additional comments or notes about your attendance.
