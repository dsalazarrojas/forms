# Workshop Proposal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals or organizations propose and plan a workshop, providing essential details about the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields with the following information:
	* The contact name and email.
	* The contact phone number (if applicable).
	* The workshop title and description.
	* The workshop date and time.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
3. If you have any questions or need assistance, please do not hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Workshop Proposal Form
* **Proposition (1)**: Briefly introduce the workshop, sharing its purpose, topic, and expected outcomes.
* **Contact Name (2)**: Enter the name of the person responsible for this workshop proposal.
* **Contact Email (3)**: Provide the email address of the person responsible for this workshop proposal.
* **Contact Phone (4)**: Enter the contact phone number of the person responsible for this workshop proposal (if applicable).
* **Workshop Title (5)**: Enter a clear and concise title for the workshop.
* **Description (6)**: Give an overview of the workshop, explaining its content, structure, and expected outcomes.
* **Date (7)**: Enter the date for the workshop, using the standard date format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Time (8)**: Enter the time for the workshop, using the standard 12-hour format (e.g., 02:00 PM).

## Tips
- Be as clear and concise as possible when filling out this form.
- Ensure accuracy in the information provided.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, please reach out to the contact person.
