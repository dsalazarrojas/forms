# Interdisciplinary Learning Summit Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to assist users in understanding how to properly complete the Interdisciplinary Learning Summit Registration form, ensuring accurate and relevant submissions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the registration form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the form instructions to understand the requirements and purpose of each section.
2. Carefully read each field explanation below to ensure you understand the expected input.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Session Interest (Select Multiple, Required: False)
* **Session Interest** (`session_interest`, Select Multiple): This field allows you to select the sessions that interest you. Please choose from the available options.

### Session Preferences (Select Multiple, Required: False)
* **Session Preferences** (`session_preferences`, Select Multiple): Similar to Session Interest, this field helps us understand your session preferences. Select the options that apply.

### Interdisciplinary Interests (Select One, Required: False)
* **Interdisciplinary Interests** (`session_interests`, Select One): This field is for indicating your interest in interdisciplinary topics.

### Contact Name (Text, Required: False)
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, Text): Please enter your name for registration purposes.

### University/Organization (Text, Required: False)
* **University/Organization** (`university_organisation`, Text): Indicate if you are representing a university or organization.

### Role (Select One, Required: False)
* **Role** (`role`, Select One): Select your role from the available options to help us understand your affiliation.

### Email (Email, Required: False)
* **Email** (`email`, Email): Enter your valid email address to ensure we can contact you.

### Phone (Text, Required: False)
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.

### Accessibility Needs (Note, Required: False)
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, Note): If you have any accessibility needs, please describe them in this field.

### Dietary Restrictions (Select Multiple, Required: False)
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, Select Multiple): Select any dietary restrictions you have for our catering purposes.

## Tips
- Please review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
