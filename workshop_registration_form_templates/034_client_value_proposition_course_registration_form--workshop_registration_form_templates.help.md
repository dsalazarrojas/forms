# Client Value Proposition Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Client Value Proposition (CVP) course.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Personal Information Section**: Fill out your name, email address, and phone number in this section.
2. **Company Information Section**: Provide your company's name, job title, department, and industry.
3. **Course Information**: Select your preferred course start date and format.
4. **Experience and Goals**: Answer questions about your experience with value proposition development and your primary goal for taking this course.
5. **Logistics and Special Requirements**: If needed, indicate any special requirements or accommodations you require.
6. **Certificate of Completion**: Indicate whether you are interested in obtaining a certificate of completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for course access and communications.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Company Name**: Enter your company's name.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Department**: Enter your department or team name.
* **Industry**: Enter your company's industry.
* **Company Size**: Select the number of employees in your company.
* **Preferred Course Start Date**: Select your preferred course start date.
* **Preferred Course Format**: Select your preferred course format (e.g. live virtual, self-paced online, hybrid, in-person).
* **Time Zone**: Enter your time zone if you are interested in in-person or hybrid courses (not required).
* **Experience with Value Proposition Development**: Indicate your level of experience with value proposition development.
* **Primary Goal for Taking This Course**: Enter what you want to learn from this course.
* **Which Topics are You Most Interested In?**: Select up to three topics you are most interested in learning about (e.g. crafting compelling value props, customer pain points, differentiation strategies).
* **Business Challenges You Are Trying to Solve**: Enter any business challenges you are trying to solve.
* **Your Role Type**: Select your role type (e.g. sales, marketing, product management).
* **Will Others from Your Team Participate?**: Indicate whether others from your team will participate.
* **How Many Team Members Might Be Interested?**: Enter the number of team members who might be interested.
* **Technology Comfort Level**: Indicate your comfort level with online learning platforms.
* **Special Requirements or Accommodations**: Enter any special requirements or accommodations you require.
* **Certificate of Completion**: Indicate whether you are interested in obtaining a certificate of completion.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have answered all required fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
