# Leadership Workshop Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for a leadership workshop. It captures essential details such as booking date, time, and participant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Booking Form" section with the date and time of the workshop you wish to attend.
2. Provide your participant details in the "Participant Details" section. This includes your name, email, phone, job title, and organization.
3. If necessary, include any special requests or messages in the "Participant Request" field.
4. Confirm your booking by filling out the "Confirm" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Booking Form** (`booking_form`, text, optional): Enter the date and time of the workshop you wish to attend.
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, optional): Provide your participant details, including your name, email, phone number, job title, and organization.
* **Confirm** (`confirm_form`, text, optional): Confirm that you are attending the workshop.
* **Participant Request** (`participant_request`, text, optional): If you have any special requests or messages, type them here.
* **Booking Date** (`booking_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you wish to book the workshop for.
* **Booking Time** (`booking_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you wish to book the workshop for.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the participant.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Participant Organization** (`participant_organization`, text, optional): Enter your organization's name.
* **Participant Job Title** (`participant_job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Participant Message** (`participant_message`, note, optional): Enter any special messages or requests.
* **Confirm Date** (`confirm_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you confirm your booking.
* **Confirm Time** (`confirm_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you confirm your booking.
* **Confirm Message** (`confirm_message`, note, optional): Enter any messages or comments about your confirmation.
