# Sustainable Workforce Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for registration in the Sustainable Workforce Program. Please fill out the necessary fields accurately to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Ensure each field is filled out according to the guidelines provided for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Data** (Page 1): This is the main form data. Please provide any relevant information about your registration.
* **Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your ID or as you would like to be referred to.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address. This will be used for communication regarding your registration.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number. This can be used for follow-up calls or messages.
* **Form Data 2**: This is an additional note field for any other relevant information you would like to provide.
* **Company**: If you are registering on behalf of your company, please enter the name of your company.
* **Department**: If you are registering on behalf of your company, please enter your department name.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title or position within your company.
* **Form Data 3**: This field is for a date. Please enter a date in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Form Data 4**: This field is for a time. Please enter a time in the correct format (HH:MM).
* **Form Data 5**: This field is a select multiple question. Please choose either 'Yes' or 'No' according to your answer.
* **Form Data 6**: This is an additional note field for any other relevant information you would like to provide.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact our support team.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid any issues with processing your registration.
