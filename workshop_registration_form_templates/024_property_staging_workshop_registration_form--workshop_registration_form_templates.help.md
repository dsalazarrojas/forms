# Property Staging Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Property Staging Workshop. It collects attendee information, payment details, and other relevant details for the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information before starting the form.
2. Fill out the form in sections, following the guidelines for each field.
3. Review your entries carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Attendee Details (Property Staging Workshop Registration)
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address as a unique identifier for your registration.
* **Phone** (`phone`, number, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Attendee Preferences** (`attendee_preferences`, text, optional): If applicable, describe any specific needs or requirements you have as an attendee.
* **Payment** (`payment_details`, text, optional): If applicable, describe the payment terms or conditions.
* **Choose Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional):
	+ **Stripe**: Choose Stripe as your payment method.
	+ **PayPal**: Choose PayPal as your payment method.
	+ **Bank Transfer**: Choose Bank Transfer as your payment method.
* **Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount you are paying for the workshop. (Note: This field is optional, but please fill it out for accurate billing purposes.)
* **Currency** (`payment_currency`, select_one, optional):
	+ **USD**: Select USD as your currency.
	+ **EUR**: Select EUR as your currency.
	+ **GBP**: Select GBP as your currency.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, optional):
	+ **Pending**: Select Pending if payment is not yet processed.
	+ **Success**: Select Success if payment is processed successfully.
	+ **Failed**: Select Failed if payment processing failed.
* **Payment Method (again)** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional):
	+ (Note: This field is a repeat of the "Choose Payment Method" field above, for your convenience.)
* **Payment Status (again)** (`payment_status`, select_one, optional):
	+ (Note: This field is a repeat of the "Payment Status" field above, for your convenience.)

**Tips**

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please ask your supervisor or the workshop organizers for clarification.
* Review your entries carefully before submitting the form.
