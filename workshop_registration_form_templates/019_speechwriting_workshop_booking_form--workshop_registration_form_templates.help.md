# Speechwriting Workshop Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to book and register for a Speechwriting Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your First Name and Last Name in the fields "First Name" and "Last Name" respectively.
2. Enter your valid email address in the field "email".
3. Enter your phone number in the field "phone".
4. Select the workshop you would like to attend from the list of available options in the field "which workshop".
5. Choose the start and end dates and times for your workshop attendance.
6. Add any additional comments or messages in the field "message".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (text)**: Enter your first name for registration purposes.
* **Last Name (text)**: Enter your last name for registration purposes.
* **Email (email)**: Enter a valid email address for communication with the workshop organizers.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **which workshop (select_one)**: Choose one of the three available workshops: Workshop 1, Workshop 2, or Workshop 3.
* **start_date (date)**: Choose the start date of your workshop attendance.
* **start_time (time)**: Choose the start time of your workshop attendance.
* **end_date (date)**: Choose the end date of your workshop attendance.
* **end_time (time)**: Choose the end time of your workshop attendance.
* **message (note)**: Add any additional comments or messages for the workshop organizers.

## Tips
* Make sure to check your email address and phone number for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Select the correct workshop option that suits your interests and availability.
* Double-check your start and end dates and times before submitting the form.
* If you have any additional comments or queries, use the "message" field to communicate with the workshop team.
