# Environmental Leadership Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Environmental Leadership Program Registration form is designed to collect participant details and preferences for the program. This form is intended for participants who have been invited to the program or who are registering on their own initiative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1.  Fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2.  Make sure to provide accurate and complete information for each field.
3.  If you are unsure about any field, please contact the program administrator for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Participant Details**: This field is for providing your basic contact information, such as your name and title. Please fill out your name and title accurately.
*   **Availability**: This field asks about your availability for the program. Please select the dates you are available to participate.
*   **Commitments**: This field asks about your commitments and responsibilities. Please select the options that apply to you.
*   **Program Details**: This field is for providing more details about your interest in the program. Please fill out this field accurately and concisely.
*   **Participant Preferences**: This field is for providing your preferences for the program. Please fill out this field accurately and concisely.
*   **Contact Details**: This field is for providing your contact information. Please fill out this field accurately.
*   **Program Preferences**: This field is for providing your preferences for the program. Please fill out this field accurately and concisely.
*   **Submit**: Please make sure to review all fields before submitting the form.
