# Skincare Workshop Planning Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you plan and organize a workshop related to skincare. It collects information about the workshop, its participants, and appointment details to ensure a smooth and successful event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "workshop_planning_form" page with any relevant information about the workshop.
2. Provide details about the participants on the "participant_details" page.
3. Enter information about the workshop session on the "workshop_session_details" page.
4. Finally, fill out the "appointment_details" page with any relevant appointment information.

* **workshop_session_details** (`workshop_session_details`, text, optional): This page is for collecting information about the workshop session. You can add any details about the session, including the schedule, duration, and any specific instructions.
