# Guest Relations Enhancement Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guest Relations Enhancement Training Registration Form is designed to collect information from individuals who are interested in attending the Guest Relations Enhancement Training. This form will help us understand who has expressed interest in the training and provide a record of their registration details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately with your details.
2. Ensure you enter the correct information in the fields specified.
3. Review your submission carefully before clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Registration Form** (Text): Enter your general information, such as name, job title, department, location, and notes about yourself.
  - This is the first section of the form where you can provide general information about yourself.
- **Contact Details** (Text): Enter your email, phone number, and other relevant contact information.
  - This is where you can enter your contact details so we can get in touch with you.
- **Training Details** (Text): Enter the training level, trainee status, and other relevant details.
  - This is where you can provide information about the training level and other details you would like to share.
- **Training Level** (Select One): Select one of the available training levels.
  - Please choose the relevant training level for your needs.
- **Trainee Status** (Select Multiple): Select one or multiple trainee statuses that apply to you.
  - Choose the status that best describes your situation.
- **Training Start Date** (Date): Enter the date you wish to start the training.
  - Please enter a valid date for the training start date.
- **Training End Date** (Date): Enter the date you wish the training to end.
  - Please enter a valid date for the training end date.
- **Training Duration** (Text): Enter the duration of the training you are interested in.
  - Please provide a duration for the training.
- **Training Topic** (Text): Enter the training topic you are interested in.
  - Please choose the relevant training topic.
- **Trainee Name** (Text): Enter your name.
  - This will help us keep track of the trainee's details.
- **Trainee Email** (Text): Enter your email address.
  - We will use this to keep in touch with you about the training.
- **Trainee Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number.
  - We may use this to reach out to you about the training.
- **Trainee Department** (Text): Enter the department you work in.
  - Please provide this for our reference.
- **Trainee Location** (Text): Enter the location where you work.
  - This will help us keep track of your location.
- **Trainee Notes** (Text): Enter any additional notes or comments.
  - Feel free to add any additional comments or details.
- **Trainee Guest** (Text): Enter the name of your guest.
  - Please enter the name of your guest if applicable.
- **Submit** (Text): Click this button to submit your form.

Note: Please review each field carefully and make sure to enter accurate information to ensure a smooth registration process.
