# Restaurant Management Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restaurant Management Workshop Registration Form is for restaurant owners or management personnel to register for the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form completely, but remember that some fields are not required.
* You can fill out the form at any time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Restaurant Management Workshop Registration** (`registration_form`): This is the main form where you can write a brief description about why you are interested in the workshop.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address so we can keep in touch with you.
* **Workshop Attendance** (`workshop`): Select 'Yes' if you are attending the workshop, 'No' if you are not.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`): Enter the date you would like to start the workshop.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all the required fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can contact us for clarification.
