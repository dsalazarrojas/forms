# Social Innovation in Learning Cohort Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants for the Social Innovation in Learning Cohort. It will help us gather essential details about you and your program preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled.
3. Review your information carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your personal name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address. This will be used for communication about the cohort.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact number for any additional information or updates.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address**: Enter your current address for delivery purposes or any other relevant information.
* **City**: Enter the city where you currently reside.
* **Zip**: Enter your postal code.
* **Cohort Type**: Select whether you want to join the cohort in-person or online.
* **Start Date**: If you're registering for a specific cohort, enter the start date.
* **End Date**: If you're registering for a specific cohort, enter the end date.
* **Program Name**: Enter the name of the program you're interested in.
* **Program Description**: Describe the program you're interested in, providing more details about the content and format.
* **Program Location**: Enter the location where the program will take place.
* **Cohort Name**: Enter the name of the cohort you're registering for.
* **Cohort Description**: Describe the cohort you're interested in, providing more details about the content and format.
* **Cohort Type Description**: Provide a brief description of the type of cohort you're registering for (in-person or online).
* **Program Duration**: Enter the duration of the program you're interested in.
* **Program Fees**: Enter any fees associated with the program you're registering for.
* **Payment Terms**: Select the payment terms you're comfortable with (Cash, Credit Card, or Bank Transfer).
* **Application Status**: Select the status of your application (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
* **Application Comments**: Provide any additional comments about your application.
* **Payment Terms**: Select the payment terms you're comfortable with (Cash, Credit Card, or Bank Transfer).
