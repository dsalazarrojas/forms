# Cultural Workshop Facilitator Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cultural Workshop Facilitator Registration Form is used to collect essential details from cultural workshop facilitators. This information helps us ensure a well-organized and engaging experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
4. Enter your phone number without any symbols or special characters in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Briefly describe your cultural workshop facilitation interest in the "Cultural Workshop Facilitator Interest" field.
6. Write a brief bio, including your experience, background, and qualifications, in the "About You" field.
7. Describe your relevant workshop experience in the "Workshop Experience" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number without any symbols or special characters.
* **Cultural Workshop Facilitator Interest** (`cultural_workshop_facilitator_interest`, text, required): Briefly describe your cultural workshop facilitation interest.
* **About You** (`bio`, text, required): Write a brief bio, including your experience, background, and qualifications.
* **Workshop Experience** (`workshop_experience`, text, required): Describe your relevant workshop experience.
