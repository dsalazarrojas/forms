# Sound Mixing Masterclass Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sound Mixing Masterclass Booking Form is designed to collect information from users who want to book a workshop or masterclass in sound mixing. This form helps us understand the user's needs and preferences for the workshop, ensuring we can tailor the event to their interests and provide a better experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the first field.
2. Enter your email address in the second field. Please make sure it is a valid email address.
3. In the third field, input your phone number.
4. Select a date for the workshop or masterclass you wish to attend.
5. Choose a time slot for the event.
6. Select the type of workshop or masterclass you are interested in (Sound Mixing Masterclass, Audio Mixing, or Music Production).
7. Provide any additional notes or comments about your attendance.
8. Confirm your booking by selecting 'True' or 'False' from the options, or choose 'Undecided' if you are still unsure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Please enter your name as it is to be used in our records.
* **email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you about the event and any updates.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
* **date**: Choose a date for the workshop or masterclass from the calendar.
* **time**: Select a time slot from the available options.
* **event**: Select which type of workshop or masterclass you are interested in (Sound Mixing Masterclass, Audio Mixing, or Music Production).
* **notes**: Provide any additional comments or questions you have about the event.
* **confirm**: Confirm your booking by selecting 'True' or 'False', or if you are still unsure, choose 'Undecided'.

## Tips

* Please make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form, as you will not be able to edit it after submission.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, feel free to contact us at any time.
