## Mastermind Event Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide is for users who wish to register for the Mastermind Event, a knowledge-sharing platform that brings together experts and thought leaders from various industries to share insights and ideas.

## How To Complete This Form
To register for the Mastermind Event, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the **Attendee Details** page (page 1) with your name, email address, and any other relevant information as requested.
2.  On the **Preferences** page (page 2), let us know about any special requirements or requests you have for the event, such as dietary restrictions or accessibility needs.
3.  In the **Consent** page (page 3), confirm that you have read and agree to the event's terms and conditions.
4.  Finally, provide your **Participant Data** (page 4) if you wish to participate in any additional activities or surveys during the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Attendee Details** (`id: 1`, `name: attendee_details`, `label: Attendee Details`, `type: text`, `required: false`):
    *   Enter your name and email address to identify yourself as an attendee.
*   **Preferences** (`id: 2`, `name: preferences`, `label: Preferences`, `type: text`, `required: false`):
    *   If you have any special requirements or requests, such as dietary restrictions or accessibility needs, please let us know here.
*   **Consent** (`id: 3`, `name: consent`, `label: Consent`, `type: text`, `required: false`):
    *   Confirm that you have read and agree to the event's terms and conditions.
*   **Participant Data** (`id: 4`, `name: participant_data`, `label: Participant Data`, `type: text`, `required: false`):
    *   If you wish to participate in any additional activities or surveys during the event, please provide your relevant data here.

## Tips
Make sure to double-check your information before submitting your registration. If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
