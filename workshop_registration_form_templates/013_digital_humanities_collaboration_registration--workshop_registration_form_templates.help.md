# Digital Humanities Collaboration Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a digital humanities collaboration workshop. Please provide accurate details to ensure a successful collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Ensure you select the correct options for each field accurately.
3. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Title**: Enter the title of the workshop you are registering for.
* **Name**: Enter your name as a participant.
* **role**: Choose your role in the collaboration (Director, Manager, or Team Member).
* **Research Interests**: This is a note field for you to list your research interests. You may leave this blank if not applicable.
* **Collaboration Preferences**: Select all that apply for the types of collaborations you are interested in.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone Number**: Enter your phone number. 
* **Collaboration Preferences Hint**: Please leave a hint for others about your collaboration preferences (optional).
* **Workshop Date**: Enter the date of the workshop.
* **Workshop Time**: Enter the time of the workshop.
* **Workshop Location**: Enter the location of the workshop.
* **Workshop Duration**: Enter the duration of the workshop.
* **Workshop Capacity**: Enter the capacity of the workshop.
* **Participant Details**: Enter any additional details about the participant.
* **Research Partners**: Choose your research partner type (Lab, Center, or Network).
* **Research Focus**: Choose the area of research focus (Humanities, Social Sciences, or Natural Sciences).
* **Research Objectives**: Select all that apply for the research objectives.
* **Participant Requirements**: Enter any requirements for the participant.
* **Workshop Outcomes**: Select all that apply for the workshop outcomes.
* **Workshop Evaluations**: Select all that apply for the workshop evaluations.
* **Participant Contribution**: Select all that apply for the participant contribution.
* **Workshop Fees**: Enter the workshop fees (if applicable).
* **Workshop Notes**: Leave any additional workshop notes.
* **Follow-up Information**: Leave any additional follow-up information.
