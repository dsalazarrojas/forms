# Counseling Skills Workshop Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Counseling Skills Workshop Enrollment Form is used to collect information from participants for the workshop enrollment process. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First name.
2. Fill in your Last name.
3. Enter your Date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
4. Enter the Workshop code.
5. Enter your Email address.
6. Enter your Phone number.
7. Select your Participant status (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
8. Choose the Workshop type (Basic, Advanced, or Expert).
9. Enter the Workshop date.
10. Enter the Workshop time.
11. Enter the Workshop location.
12. Add any additional notes.
13. Enter the Assigned tool.
14. Enter the Created by field (leave blank for system-generated value).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Workshop code** (`workshop_code`, `text`, required: false): Enter the workshop code for the session.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Participant status** (`participant_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your participant status (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **Workshop type** (`workshop_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the type of workshop (Basic, Advanced, or Expert).
* **Workshop date** (`workshop_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the workshop date.
* **Workshop time** (`workshop_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the workshop time.
* **Workshop location** (`workshop_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the workshop location.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes for the participant.
* **Assigned tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required: false): Enter the assigned tool for the workshop.
* **Created by** (`created_by`, `text`, required: false): Enter the created by field (leave blank for system-generated value).
