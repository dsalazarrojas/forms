# Sustainability Reporting Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for the Sustainability Reporting Workshop. It collects information about each participant, including their name, email, phone number, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide any additional information about yourself in the "Participant Notes" field.
3. If you are representing a company, enter the company name.
4. Enter your role and job title within the company.
5. If you have a manager, provide their name, email address, and phone number.
6. Click on the "Submit" button to register your participation.
