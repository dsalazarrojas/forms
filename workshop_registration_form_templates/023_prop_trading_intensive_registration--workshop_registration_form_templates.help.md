# Prop Trading Intensive Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Prop Trading Intensive Registration" form is used to collect participant details and program information for the prop trading intensive program. This form helps us to understand the participant's experience level, goals, contact information, and program details, which enables us to provide a tailored experience for each participant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Fill in your participant details, including your name.
3. Choose your experience level (beginner, intermediate, or advanced).
4. Describe your goals for the program.
5. Provide your contact information (e.g., email, phone number).
6. Enter your program information (e.g., dates, location).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details**: This is the first page of the form where you'll enter your name and any other relevant details.
* **Experience Level**: This field allows you to choose your level of experience in prop trading. Please select the option that best describes your level of expertise:
	+ Beginner: You have little to no experience in prop trading.
	+ Intermediate: You have some experience in prop trading, but you're looking to improve.
	+ Advanced: You have extensive experience in prop trading and are looking to refine your skills.
* **Goals**: Describe your goals for the program. What do you hope to achieve or learn from the experience?
* **Contact Info**: Enter your email address, phone number, or any other contact method we can use to reach you.
* **Program Info**: Enter any additional information about the program, such as dates or location.
* **Program Details**: This field is used to capture any further details about the program, such as specific dates or dates.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields.
* Be as detailed as possible when describing your goals and program information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
