# Mother Daughter Retreat Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register a mother and her daughter for a Mother Daughter Retreat event. Please fill out this form to provide us with essential details about your daughter and yourself.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Mother Daughter Event Details page, providing information about the event you are attending.
2. Provide your and your daughter's personal information on the Mother Name and Daughter Age pages.
3. Choose the dates you will be attending on the Dates Attending page.
4. Indicate whether there are any special needs or conditions on the Special Needs page.
5. Enter your and your daughter's contact information on the Mother Contact Info and Daughter Contact Info pages.
6. Provide any additional notes or comments on the Event Details page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mother Daughter Event Details**: This page is used to provide general details about the event you are attending. Please fill in the required information to ensure we have a clear understanding of the event.
* **Mother's Name** (`mother_name`): This field is used to collect the mother's name. Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Age of Daughter** (`daughter_age`): This field is used to collect the age of your daughter. Please enter her age as of the date you are attending the event.
* **Dates Attending** (`dates_attending`): Select the dates you plan to attend the event. Please choose the dates you are available.
* **Mother Consent** (`mother_consent`): Please indicate whether you have reviewed and agreed to the terms of the event. Please select one or more of the options provided.
* **Special Needs** (`special_needs`): If there are any specific conditions or requirements for you or your daughter, please select "Yes" to indicate this on the Special Needs page.
* **Mother Contact Info** (`mother_contact_info`): This field is used to collect your contact information, including your phone and email. Please ensure your contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Daughter Contact Info** (`daughter_contact_info`): This field is used to collect your daughter's contact information, including her phone and email. Please ensure her contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`): This field is used to provide any additional notes or comments about the event. Please use this space to share any important information with us.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out to avoid any delays in processing your registration.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy to ensure we can reach you and your daughter effectively.
* If you have any special needs or requirements, please indicate them on the Special Needs page to help us prepare for your needs.
