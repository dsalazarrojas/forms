# Smart Energy System Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Smart Energy System Training session. Please fill in the required information to complete your registration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant session details (Session Topic, Start Date, End Date, Start Time, End Time, and Duration).
2. Enter the participant details (Participant Name, Participant Email, Participant Phone, Participant Country, Participant Address, Participant City, Participant State, Participant Postal Code, and Participant Language).
3. Select the participant's level of experience (0-2 years, 2-5 years, 5-10 years, 10-20 years, or More than 20 years).
4. Choose the session location (Online, In-person, or Phone).
5. If applicable, choose the tool to be assigned to you during the session.
6. Review all the information entered and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Details** (`Session Topic`, `Start Date`, `End Date`, `Start Time`, `End Time`, `Duration`): These fields are used to select the relevant session details. Choose the correct session topic and dates for your training session.
* **Participant Details** (`Participant Name`, `Participant Email`, `Participant Phone`, `Participant Country`, `Participant Address`, `Participant City`, `Participant State`, `Participant Postal Code`, `Participant Language`): These fields are used to collect your contact information.
* **Session Choices** (`Session Choices`): Select your session choices (Yes or No).
* **Consent** (`Consent`): This field is currently not used in this guide.
* **Participant Name** (`Participant Name`): Enter your name.
* **Participant Email** (`Participant Email`): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`Participant Phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Participant Country** (`Participant Country`): Enter your country of residence.
* **Participant Address** (`Participant Address`): Enter your address.
* **Participant City** (`Participant City`): Enter your city.
* **Participant State** (`Participant State`): Enter your state or province.
* **Participant Postal Code** (`Participant Postal Code`): Enter your postal code or zip code.
* **Participant Country** (`Participant Country`): Enter your country of residence.
* **Participant Language** (`Participant Language`): Choose your language (English, French, Spanish, German, Japanese, Chinese, or Arabic).
* **Participant Gender** (`Participant Gender`): Choose your gender (Male, Female, or Other).
* **Participant Education** (`Participant Education`): Choose your level of education (Bachelor of Arts, Bachelor of Science, Bachelor of Engineering, Bachelor of Education, Bachelor of Business, High School, College, Postgraduate, Doctorate, or Diploma).
* **Participant Experience** (`Participant Experience`): Select your level of experience (0-2 years, 2-5 years, 5-10 years, 10-20 years, or More than 20 years).
* **Session Location** (`Session Location`): Choose your preferred session location (Online, In-person, or Phone).
* **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`): Choose if you want to be assigned a tool (Yes or No).

## Tips

* Ensure you have filled in all the required fields.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact the training session organizers.
