# Winter Winds Clinic Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Winter Winds Clinic Registration form is designed to collect information from participants and instructors regarding their workshop participation. It is essential for workshop organization and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Details, including your name and any other relevant information.
2. Select the instrument you will be playing during the workshop from the provided options or specify "Other" if your instrument is not listed.
3. Choose the school or workshop where you will be participating.
4. If you are a teacher, provide details about the instrument you will be teaching.
5. If your instrument is not listed, specify it in the "If other, please specify" field.
6. Confirm your consent to participate in the workshop.
7. Enter your email address.
8. Provide your phone number.
9. Enter your address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details**: A text field for you to input your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Instrument**: A select multiple field where you can choose from a list of available instruments or specify "Other" for instruments not listed.
* **School/Workshop**: A select one field to choose the school or workshop where you will be participating.
* **Instrument Details**: A text field for you to provide more information about the instrument you will be playing or teaching.
* **Instrument Name**: A text field to input the name of your instrument, if different from the instrument type selected above.
* **Teacher Instrument**: A text field to input the instrument you will be teaching, if different from your own instrument.
* **If other, please specify**: A text field to input a different instrument if it is not listed above.
* **Consent**: A note field to confirm your participation in the workshop. Please read and agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Email**: An email field to input your email address.
* **Phone**: A text field to input your phone number.
* **Address**: A text field to input your address.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions to access and use this form.
- If you are unsure, contact your supervisor or manager to clarify.
- This form should not be duplicated or shared without required permissions.
