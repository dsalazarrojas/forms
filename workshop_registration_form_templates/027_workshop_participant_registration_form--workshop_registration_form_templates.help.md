# Workshop Participant Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workshop Participant Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from workshop participants, including their personal details and preferences.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your first name in the first name field.
2. Provide your last name in the last name field.
3. Enter your email address in the email field.
4. Provide your phone number.
5. Choose one option from the "Select One" field.
6. Select multiple options from the "Select Multiple" field.
7. If applicable, choose a date and time for the workshop.
8. You can add a note with any additional information you'd like to share with the workshop organizers.
9. If applicable, choose an assigned tool for the workshop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name (e.g., John).
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name (e.g., Doe).
* **Email**: Enter your email address in the format "example@example.com".
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number (e.g., +1-123-456-7890).
* **Select One**: Choose one option from the dropdown menu (e.g., Option 1).
* **Select Multiple**: Select multiple options from the dropdown menu by holding down the Ctrl key while selecting (e.g., Option 1, Option 3).
* **Date**: If applicable, choose a date for the workshop (e.g., 2024-03-16).
* **Time**: If applicable, choose a time for the workshop (e.g., 10:00 AM).
* **Note**: Add any additional information you'd like to share with the workshop organizers.
* **Assigned Tool**: If applicable, choose an assigned tool for the workshop (e.g., Tool 1).
