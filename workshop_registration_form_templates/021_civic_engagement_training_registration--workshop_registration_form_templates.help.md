# Civic Engagement Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Civic Engagement Training Registration form is designed to collect essential information from participants who wish to register for a Civic Engagement Training program. The form gathers details such as participant name, organization, contact email, training date, role in the community, accommodation requirements, payment status, emergency contact, consent for photos, and submission date. This information helps the training organizers to manage registrations and provide necessary support to the participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Participant Name**: Please type your full legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. Enter your **Organization**: If you are a representative of an organization, please type the name of your employer or affiliation.
3. Enter your **Contact Email**: Please provide a valid email address where you can be reached for training materials and confirmation.
4. Choose your **Training Date**: Select the date of the training session you would like to attend.
5. Enter your **Role in Community**: Describe your role in the community, such as volunteer, student, or staff.
6. (Optional) Specify any **Accommodation Requirements**: If you have any specific needs or requirements, such as dietary restrictions or accessibility needs, please describe them.
7. Confirm your **Payment Status**: If you have already paid the fee, select "Paid". If you would like to receive an invoice, select "Invoice". If you would like to request a waiver, select "Waiver requested".
8. Provide your **Emergency Contact**: If you would like someone to contact in case of an emergency, please provide their name and phone number.
9. (Optional) Grant **Consent for Photos**: If you agree to allow photos to be taken during the training, select "True". If you would like to opt-out, select "False".
10. Submit your form by selecting the current date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): This is the full legal name of the participant. Please ensure it is accurate and complete.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): If you are representing an organization, please type its name. If not, leave blank.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): This is the email address where you can be reached for training materials and confirmation.
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, required): Choose the date of the training session you would like to attend.
* **Role in Community** (`role_in_community`, text, optional): Describe your role in the community, such as volunteer, student, or staff.
* **Accommodation Requirements** (`accommodation_requirements`, text, optional): If you have any special needs or requirements, please describe them.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, required): Select the payment status that applies to you:
	+ "Paid" if you have already paid the fee
	+ "Invoice" if you would like to receive an invoice
	+ "Waiver requested" if you would like to request a waiver
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): If you would like someone to contact in case of an emergency, please provide their name and phone number.
* **Consent for Photos** (`consent_for_photos`, select_one, optional): Select "True" if you agree to allow photos to be taken during the training, and "False" if you would like to opt-out.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): This is the date and time when the form is submitted.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and completely to ensure a successful registration process.
