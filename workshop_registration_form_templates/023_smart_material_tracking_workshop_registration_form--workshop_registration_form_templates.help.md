# Smart Material Tracking Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users interested in attending a workshop on smart material tracking. It's a one-time registration form to collect your contact and workshop preference information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and company (if applicable) on the first page.
2. Enter your email address and phone number on the next page.
3. Select your preferred workshop type on the third page.
4. Choose your time preference and select the start and end dates of the workshop on the fourth and fifth pages respectively.
5. Add any additional notes or comments on the last page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (text)**: Enter your full name.
* **Company (text)**: If you represent a company, enter it here. This is not required.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your business email address.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Workshop Type (select_one)**: Choose from the options "Material Tracking", "Product Development", "Project Management", "Other", or "Workshop Type 1".
* **Time Preference (time)**: Choose your preferred time of day.
* **Start Date (date)**: Select the start date of the workshop you are interested in.
* **End Date (date)**: Select the end date of the workshop you are interested in.
* **Note (note)**: Add any additional comments or notes about your registration. This is optional, but helps us understand your needs better.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Be specific when selecting your workshop type and time preference.
* If you have any special requests or questions, feel free to add them in the "Note" field.
