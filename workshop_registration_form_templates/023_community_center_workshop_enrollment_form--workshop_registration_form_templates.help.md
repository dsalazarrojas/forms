# Workshop Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from individuals interested in enrolling in our workshops. It's a crucial part of our enrollment process to ensure that we have accurate contact details, payment information, and other necessary data to facilitate a smooth and enjoyable workshop experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in the Participant Name field**: Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
2. **Enter your Email address**: Type in your email address that we can use for confirmations, materials, and other notifications.
3. **Provide your Phone number (optional)**: If you'd like to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number. This is not a required field.
4. **Select your preferred Workshop**: Choose the workshop you'd like to enroll in from the dropdown menu.
5. **Choose your Session date**: Select the date of the workshop session you've chosen.
6. **Confirm your Payment status**: Let us know if you've made a payment or your payment status is pending or waived.
7. **Special Requirements or Accommodations**: If you have any accessibility, dietary, or equipment needs, provide them here.
8. **Read and Agree to our Terms**: Confirm that you've read and agree to our workshop policies and attendance requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name**: This is your full name as it appears on official documents. Please type it in carefully.
* **Email address**: This is the email address we'll use for all confirmations, materials, and other workshop-related notifications.
* **Phone number**: If you'd like to be contacted by phone, type your phone number here. (Note: This field is optional.)
* **Workshop choice**: Select the workshop you'd like to attend from the dropdown menu.
* **Session date**: Choose the date of the workshop session you've selected.
* **Payment status**: Confirm your payment status: paid, pending, or waived.
* **Special requirements or accommodations**: If you have any special needs, such as accessibility requirements or dietary restrictions, describe them here.
* **Consent and agreement**: Confirm that you've read and agree to our workshop policies and attendance requirements.
