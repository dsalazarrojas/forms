# Expense Allocation Roundtable Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Expense Allocation Roundtable Registration Form is designed to collect information about users who want to register for the expense allocation roundtable. This form helps us gather details such as name, organization, role, and contact information, as well as event type, budget, and expense category.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **Full Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Organization** in the second field.
3. Choose your **Role** from the options provided.
4. Provide your **Email** address to ensure we can contact you about the event.
5. Enter your **Phone** number for additional contact information.
6. Select your **Team** affiliation from the options provided.
7. Choose the type of **Event Type** you are registering for (Registration or Other).
8. Enter your **Budget Amount** for the event.
9. Select a **Budget Start Date** and **Budget End Date** for your expenses.
10. Choose the category of **Expense Category** that applies to your allocation (Travel, Accommodation, Food, or Other).
11. Add any additional information about the **Expense Note** if needed.
12. Finally, fill in any additional information about who **Submitted By**, **Submitted On**, **Approved By**, and **Approved On**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as you would like to be referred to.
* **Organization**: Enter your organization or company name.
* **Role**: Choose your role in the expense allocation roundtable.
* **Email**: Provide a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for additional contact information.
* **Team**: Select your team affiliation from the options provided.
* **Event Type**: Choose the type of event you are registering for (Registration or Other).
* **Budget Amount**: Enter the amount allocated for the event.
* **Budget Start Date**: Select the start date of the budget period.
* **Budget End Date**: Select the end date of the budget period.
* **Expense Category**: Choose the category of expense (Travel, Accommodation, Food, or Other).
* **Expense Note**: Add any additional information about your expenses.
* **Submitted By**: Enter the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted On**: Enter the date and time you submitted the form.
* **Approved By**: Enter the person who approved the form.
* **Approved On**: Enter the date and time the form was approved.
* **Updated By**: Enter the person who updated the form.
* **Updated On**: Enter the date and time the form was updated.
* **Updated At**: Enter the timestamp of the last update.
* **Status**: Select the status of the form (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
* **Created By**: Enter the person who created the form.
* **Created On**: Enter the date and time the form was created.
* **Updated By**: Enter the person who last updated the form.
* **Updated On**: Enter the date and time the form was last updated.
* **Updated At**: Enter the timestamp of the last update.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure a successful form submission.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
* If you have any questions or issues with the form, contact our support team.
* The form is designed to be completed in a few minutes, so take your time and fill it out carefully.
