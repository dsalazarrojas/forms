# cybersecurity_workshop_lead_generation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The cybersecurity_workshop_lead_generation form is designed to collect information about workshop attendees to facilitate efficient lead generation and follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please go through the following steps:
1. Provide your **Workshop Lead Generation** information, which is a brief description of the workshop you are leading.
2. Enter your **Name** in the required field.
3. Enter your **Email** in the required field.
4. Confirm your **Email** to ensure it is accurate.
5. Enter your **Phone** number.
6. Enter your **Company** name.
7. Enter your **Job Title**.
8. Enter your **Department**.
9. Enter the **Event Date** of the workshop.
10. Enter the **Event Time** of the workshop.
11. Enter the **Event Location** where the workshop will be held.
12. Enter the **Event Duration**, which is the length of time the workshop will last.
13. Enter your **Follow-up** information to help us contact you after the workshop.
14. Enter the **Lead Source**, which is where you heard about the workshop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Lead Generation**:
	+ A text field for a brief description of the workshop.
* **Name**:
	+ A field to enter your name.
* **Email**:
	+ A field to enter your email address.
	+ **Hint**: Ensure your email is accurate to receive updates about the workshop.
* **Confirm Email**:
	+ A field to confirm your email address.
* **Phone**:
	+ A field to enter your phone number.
* **Company**:
	+ A field to enter your company name.
* **Job Title**:
	+ A field to enter your job title.
* **Department**:
	+ A field to enter your department.
* **Event Date**:
	+ A field to enter the date of the workshop.
* **Event Time**:
	+ A field to enter the time of the workshop.
* **Event Location**:
	+ A field to enter the location of the workshop.
* **Event Duration**:
	+ A field to enter the length of time the workshop will last.
	+ **Hint**: Ensure the duration is accurate.
* **Follow-up**:
	+ A field to enter follow-up information.
* **Lead Source**:
	+ A field to enter where you heard about the workshop.
	+ **Hint**: Help us understand how you found out about the workshop.

**Tips**:
* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Double-check your email address to avoid errors.
* Enter a brief description of the workshop in the **Workshop Lead Generation** field.
* If you have any questions, contact us.
* Please review the fields for completeness before submitting.
