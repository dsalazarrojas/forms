# Educational Design Thinking Workshop Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you request educational design thinking workshops tailored to your specific needs. Fill out this form to provide us with information about your workshop requirements, and we will do our best to accommodate your requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form in a new tab or window.
2. Start with the first page, "Workshop Request Form", and provide a clear and concise description of your workshop request.
3. Move on to the subsequent pages, "Contact Details", "Dates", "Goals", "Workshop Logistics", "Custom Questions", and "Form Builder".
4. Answer each question on each page thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
5. Review your completed form for accuracy before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Request Form**: This is the main section where you describe your workshop request. Please provide a brief but detailed explanation of what you are looking for. This will help us understand your needs and tailor the workshop to your requirements.
* **Contact Details**: Please provide your name, email address, and phone number so that we can reach out to you to confirm your request and discuss any further details.
* **Dates**: Specify the ideal dates for your workshop, including the start and end dates. Be as specific as possible to ensure we can accommodate your request.
* **Goals**: Outline the specific goals and objectives of your workshop. What do you hope to achieve through this workshop? Provide as much detail as possible.
* **Workshop Logistics**: Describe the logistics of your workshop, including the number of participants, location, and any special requirements (e.g., equipment, facilities, etc.).
* **Custom Questions**: If you have any custom questions or requirements that are not covered in the above sections, please include them here.
* **Form Builder**: This section is not currently being used in this form. Please leave it blank.
* **Organized Data Collection**: This section is also not currently being used. Please leave it blank.

## Tips
* Be thorough and concise in your answers to ensure we can best understand your requirements.
* If you have any doubts or need clarification on any section, do not hesitate to contact us.
* Once you have completed the form, review it carefully for accuracy and completeness before submitting it.
