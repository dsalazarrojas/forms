# Retail Growth Strategy Session Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees for a Retail Growth Strategy Session. The session aims to provide a comprehensive overview of retail growth strategies and tailor the content to attendees' backgrounds and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely.
2. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your preferred date of attendance from the "Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for communication and follow-up.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for communication and follow-up.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Select the date you prefer to attend the session.
