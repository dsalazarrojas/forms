# Livestock Disease Prevention Forum Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for the Livestock Disease Prevention Forum workshop registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as requested and follow these general guidelines:
- Fill in your contact information accurately.
- Choose your preferences for the Livestock Disease Prevention Course.
- Select your availability for the workshop.
- Provide any additional notes or information for the workshop facilitators.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth for age verification purposes.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, required/optional): Enter your available time for the workshop.
* **Livestock Disease Prevention Course** (`Livestock Disease Prevention Course`, select_one, required/optional): Choose if you are interested in the course.
* **Workshop Date** (`Workshop Date`, date, required/optional): Enter your available date for the workshop.
* **Workshop Time** (`Workshop Time`, time, required/optional): Enter your available time for the workshop.
* **Workshop Location** (`Workshop Location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location of the workshop.
* **Workshop Facilitator** (`Workshop Facilitator`, text, required/optional): Enter the facilitator's name for the workshop.
* **Workshop Notes** (`Workshop Notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes for the workshop.
* **Livestock Disease Prevention Certificate** (`Livestock Disease Prevention Certificate`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose if you require the certificate.

Note: Please ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately and completely. If you need assistance or have questions, do not hesitate to reach out to the workshop organizers.
