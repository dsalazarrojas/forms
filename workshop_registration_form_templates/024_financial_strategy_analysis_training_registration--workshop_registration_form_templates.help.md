# Financial Strategy Analysis Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registration of attendees to the Financial Strategy Analysis Training session. It is designed to gather essential information to tailor the session to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the session preferences options carefully and select the options that best suit your needs.
2. Fill in your session preference details, such as selecting multiple options or specifying your time and date.
3. Provide your attendee details, including any relevant text information.
4. If applicable, list any special requirements, such as accessibility needs or equipment requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Preferences**: 
  * `Session Preference 1` (`session_preferences_1`, select_multiple, optional):
    + Select one or more options from the list (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3) that best describe your session preferences.
* **Session Preference 2** (`session_preferences_2`, time, optional):
    + Specify the time of your session preference.
* **Session Preference 3** (`session_preferences_3`, date, optional):
    + Specify the date of your session preference.
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, text, optional):
    + Provide any additional information regarding your session preferences.
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, text, optional):
    + List any special requirements you may have, such as accessibility needs or equipment requirements.

## Tips
Please fill out the form accurately and completely. The training organizers will use the information provided to tailor the session to your needs. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the organizers.
