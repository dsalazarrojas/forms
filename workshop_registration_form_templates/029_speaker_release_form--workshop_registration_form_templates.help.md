# Speaker Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Speaker Release Form is a document that allows speakers to release their intellectual property, including their name, likeness, and any other proprietary rights, for use in a workshop setting. This form helps to establish a clear understanding between the speaker and the workshop organizers, ensuring that all parties are aware of the terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your speaker information in the Speaker Information section.
2.  Provide more details about yourself in the Speaker Details section.
3.  Write a description of your content in the Speaker Content section.
4.  Read and agree to the Terms and Conditions.
5.  Confirm your consent to release your content.
6.  Enter your email address in the Speaker Email field.
7.  Enter your name in the Speaker Name field.
8.  If you have a phone number, enter it in the Speaker Phone field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Speaker Information** (`text`, Optional): Enter your name and any other relevant speaker information.
*   **Speaker Details** (`text`, Optional): Provide any additional details about yourself that you feel are relevant to your workshop presentation.
*   **Speaker Content** (`text`, Optional): Write a brief description of the content you will present during the workshop.
*   **Terms and Conditions** (`text`, Optional): Read through the terms and conditions carefully and check the box to indicate your acceptance.
*   **Speaker Consent** (`text`, Optional): Confirm your consent to release your content for use in the workshop.
*   **Speaker Email** (`text`, Optional): Enter your email address so that we can contact you with any questions or concerns.
*   **Speaker Name** (`text`, Optional): Enter your name for identification purposes.
*   **Speaker Phone** (`text`, Optional): If you have a phone number, enter it so we can contact you for any last-minute questions. 

## Tips
*   Make sure to read the terms and conditions carefully and ask any questions if you're unsure about anything.
*   Ensure that you understand what you're consenting to before signing this form.
*   If you have any concerns or questions, contact us at form@gic.mx.
