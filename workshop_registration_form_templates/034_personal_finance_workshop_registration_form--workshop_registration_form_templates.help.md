# Personal Finance Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from participants for the Personal Finance Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Enter your personal details, such as First Name, Last Name, and Date of Birth.
3. Provide your Email and Phone numbers.
4. Select your preferred Workshop Date.
5. Choose your payment method and terms.
6. Select your Participant Type and Workshop Preferences.
7. Check the boxes for any Special Needs, Accommodation Preferences, and Dietary Restrictions you may have.
8. Provide Emergency Contact information, including Relationship and Phone number.
9. Review the terms and conditions of the workshop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Please enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Please enter your date of birth if you wish to provide it.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, required): Select the date you would like to attend the workshop.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, optional): This is the date the form was filled out.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, required): Choose how you plan to pay for the workshop, such as 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, select_one, required): Choose whether you have agreed to the payment terms.
* **Participant Type** (`participant_type`, select_multiple, required): Select your participant type, such as 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Workshop Preferences** (`workshop_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Select any preferences you have for the workshop.
* **Special Needs** (`special_needs`, select_multiple, optional): Check if you have any special needs.
* **Accommodation Preferences** (`accommodation_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Select your accommodation preferences.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_multiple, optional): Check any dietary restrictions you have.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's name and relationship with you.
* **Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, required): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Relationship** (`emergency_relationship`, text, optional): Enter the relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, select_multiple, optional): Check if you have any participant information to share.
* **Emergency Authorization** (`emergency_authorization`, select_one, optional): Check if you authorize the emergency contact to make decisions for you.
* **Emergency Permission** (`emergency_permission`, select_one, required): Check if you grant permission for the emergency contact to make decisions for you in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Message** (`emergency_message`, text, optional): Enter an emergency message for the emergency contact.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, optional): Review the terms and conditions of the workshop.
* **Participant Declaration** (`participant_declaration`, note, optional): Review the participant declaration.
* **Workshop Information** (`workshop_info`, select_multiple, optional): Check any workshop information you wish to share.
* **Submission** (`submission`, select_multiple, required): Select whether you agree to submit the form.

Please note that this help guide is just an example, and you may want to review and customize it according to your specific needs.
