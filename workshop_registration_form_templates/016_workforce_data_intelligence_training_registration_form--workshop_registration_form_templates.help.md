# Workforce Data Intelligence Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register users for a workforce data intelligence training program. It collects necessary information for the training process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your details on the "User Details" page.
2. Enter your contact information on the "Contact Info" page.
3. Fill out the "Session Info" page with your session details.
4. Add your "Training Details" on the next page.
5. If necessary, provide "Submission Info" about your work or project.
6. If necessary, enter "Payment Details" for payment information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Details** (`user_details_page`, text, required): Enter your name and any other relevant personal details that you would like to share with the training team.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info_page`, text, required): This is where you enter your contact information such as your email, phone number, and any other contact methods that you would like to share with us.
- **Session Info** (`session_info_page`, text, required): Provide details about your training session such as date, time, location, and any other relevant information.
- **Training Details** (`training_details_page`, text, required): Enter your training goals, objectives, or any other information that you would like to share with the training team.
- **Submission Info** (`submission_page`, text, optional): This field is optional, but if you have a specific work or project that you would like to submit for review or feedback, provide details about it here.
- **Payment Details** (`payment_page`, text, optional): Enter any payment method, amount or information here if necessary.
- **Review Form** (`review_page`, text, optional): This is an optional field where you can provide feedback, comments, or any other thoughts about the form or the training experience.
