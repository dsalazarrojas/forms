# Collaborative Teaching Residency Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in participating in a Collaborative Teaching Residency program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name and valid email address in the required fields.
2. Provide your phone number and residency term to ensure your application is complete.
3. Select your preferred grade level and subject focus.
4. Enter the name of your university or teacher preparation program and your expected graduation date.
5. Enter your valid GPA.
6. Select your preferred mentoring schedule.
7. Choose your preferred school type.
8. Provide a valid contact phone number and name for an emergency contact.
9. Select your preferred transportation method.
10. Choose your background check status.
11. Select your TB test status.
12. Enter any special needs or accommodations you may require.
13. Write a statement of interest explaining why you want to participate in the residency program.
14. Confirm if you can provide references.
15. Add any additional comments or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Resident Teacher Full Name**: Enter your full name to ensure that the program has accurate contact information for you.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address to receive important updates and communication from the program.
* **Phone Number**: Provide a valid phone number in case the program needs to contact you.
* **Residency Term**: Select which term you are applying for to ensure proper placement in the program.
* **Grade Level Preference**: Select your preferred grade level to match your teaching interests.
* **Subject Area**: Choose your primary subject focus to ensure proper pairing with mentors and peers.
* **University or Teacher Preparation Program**: Enter the name of your university or teacher preparation program to verify your affiliation.
* **Degree Program**: Enter the degree program you are pursuing to ensure proper placement.
* **Expected Graduation Date**: Enter your expected graduation date to track your progress.
* **GPA**: Enter your current GPA to ensure you meet program requirements.
* **Prior Teaching Experience**: If applicable, describe any previous teaching experiences to showcase your background.
* **Mentoring Preference**: Select your preferred mentoring schedule to match your needs.
* **Interested in Being a Mentor Teacher**: Choose if you are interested in being a mentor to other teachers.
* **School Type Preference**: Select your preferred school type to match your interests.
* **Geographic Location Preference**: If applicable, enter your preferred location for the residency.
* **Availability Schedule**: Enter your availability schedule to ensure proper matching with mentors and peers.
* **Desired Residency Duration**: Select how long you would like to participate in the program.
* **Transportation Method**: Choose how you plan to commute to the program.
* **Background Check Status**: Select your background check status to ensure proper clearance.
* **Tuberculosis Clearance**: Select your TB test status to ensure safety and health protocols.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Special Needs or Accommodations**: If applicable, describe any special needs or accommodations required.
* **Statement of Interest**: Write a statement explaining why you want to participate in the program.
* **References Available**: Confirm if you can provide references.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any other relevant information about your application.

Note: This form will be reviewed and verified by the program organizers before making any decisions on your application. Please ensure that all fields are accurate and complete to avoid any issues during the application process.
