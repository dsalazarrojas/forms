# Expressive Lens Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture information from participants for an expressive lens workshop. It is intended to be completed by workshop participants to register themselves for the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your workshop information.
2. Provide your participant details.
3. Select your preferred lens options.
4. Consent to participate.
5. Enter your email address.
6. Enter your phone number.
7. Enter the name of the workshop you are registering for.
8. Select the start date of the workshop.
9. Select the end date of the workshop.
10. Enter the location of the workshop.
11. Select the time zone for the workshop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Information** (`Workshop Information`, text, optional): Enter the workshop information if you have any.
* **Participant Details** (`Participant Details`, text, optional): Enter your participant details, if you have any specific information to share.
* **Lens Preferences** (`Lens Preferences`, select multiple, optional): Select one or more options that represent your preferred lens options.
* **Consent** (`Consent`, select one, optional): Select one of the options to indicate your consent to participate.
* **Participant Email** (`Participant Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about the workshop.
* **Participant Phone** (`Participant Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about the workshop.
* **Workshop Name** (`Workshop Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the workshop you are registering for.
* **Workshop Start Date** (`Workshop Start Date`, date, optional): Select the start date of the workshop.
* **Workshop End Date** (`Workshop End Date`, date, optional): Select the end date of the workshop.
* **Workshop Location** (`Workshop Location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the workshop.
* **Workshop Time Zone** (`Workshop Time Zone`, select one, optional): Select the time zone for the workshop.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure a complete and accurate registration.
- Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask the workshop organizers.
