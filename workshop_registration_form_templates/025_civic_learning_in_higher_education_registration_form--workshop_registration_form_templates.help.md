# Civic Learning In Higher Education Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students to facilitate their participation in civic learning programs and activities in higher education. It is used to record student details for training sessions, accommodate special needs, and gather consent for media use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your **Full name** (Student name) in the `Student name` field.
2. Provide your **University identifier** (Student ID) in the `Student ID` field (if applicable).
3. Enter your **Student email** address for course material distribution in the `Student email` field.
4. Choose your preferred **Training session** from the provided options.
5. If necessary, specify any **Accommodation needs** you may have.
6. Choose your **Credit preference**, if you require academic credit or a certificate.
7. Provide contact information for your **Emergency contact name and phone number** (if required).
8. Select whether you **Consent for media use** (yes/no).
9. Finally, use the current date for the **Submission date**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Student name 
* **Student name**: Enter your full name (First and last name). This is a mandatory field.
### Student ID 
* **University identifier**: Provide your university-issued student ID, if applicable. If not required, this field is not mandatory.
### Student email 
* **Student email**: Enter your student email for course materials and other relevant communications.
### Program of study 
* **Major or department**: Specify your academic program or department. If no program is specified, this field is not mandatory.
### Training session 
* **Choose preferred session**: Select the training session you wish to attend (Module 1, 2, or 3).
### Accommodation needs 
* **Accessibility or assistance**: If you require any accommodations or assistance during the training, specify them in this field.
### Credit preference 
* **Request academic credit or certificate**: Choose your preference for academic credit or a certificate.
### Emergency contact 
* **Contact name and phone**: Provide contact information for a person who can be reached in case of emergencies.
### Consent for media 
* **Allow photos recordings for materials**: Select whether you consent to being photographed or recorded for course materials.
### Submission date 
* **Use today's date**: Use the current date for the submission.
