# Public Health Digital Campaign Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects necessary information from potential attendees to register for public health digital campaign training events. It is used to gather contact details, event preferences, and payment information to provide a smooth experience for attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps:

1. Provide your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
5. Select your preferred event type from the Event Type dropdown (Healthcare Provider, Policy Maker, Public Health Professional, or Other).
6. If you have a preferred Training Topic, enter it in the Training Topic field.
7. Add any Additional Information you would like to share with the organizers in the Additional Information field.
8. Choose your preferred Event Location from the Event Location dropdown (Chicago, IL, Los Angeles, CA, New York City, NY, or Other (Please specify)).
9. Select how you would like to Register (In-person, Online, or Phone).
10. Enter the date you would like to Register on in the Registration Date field.
11. Enter your Confirmation Number.
12. Select your Payment Method (Full payment, Partial Payment, or Payment plan).
13. If you have any Special Requests, describe them in the Special Request field.
14. Confirm that you have made a Special Request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Event Type** (select_multiple, required: false): Select your preferred event type. Options: Healthcare Provider, Policy Maker, Public Health Professional, Other.
* **Training Topic** (text, required: false): If you have a preferred training topic, enter it here.
* **Additional Information** (note, required: false): Add any additional information you would like to share with the organizers.
* **Event Location** (select_one, required: false): Choose your preferred event location. Options: Chicago, IL, Los Angeles, CA, New York City, NY, Other (Please specify).
* **Registration Method** (select_one, required: false): Select how you would like to register (In-person, Online, or Phone).
* **Registration Date** (date, required: false): Enter the date you would like to register on.
* **Confirmation Number** (text, required: false): Enter your confirmation number.
* **Payment Information** (select_multiple, required: false): Select your payment method. Options: Full payment, Partial Payment, Payment plan.
* **Special Request** (text, required: false): If you have any special requests, describe them here.
* **Confirm Special Request** (select_one, required: false): Confirm that you have made a special request.

## Tips

- Ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
- Choose your preferred options carefully, as they will affect your registration experience.
- If you have any special requests, be specific and clear in your description.
- Review your form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
