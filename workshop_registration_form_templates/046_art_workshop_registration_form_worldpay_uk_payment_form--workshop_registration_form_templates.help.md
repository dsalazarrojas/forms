# Art Workshop Registration Form WorldPay UK Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering participants for an art workshop. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure your registration is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your personal information, including your name, email, phone number, and emergency contact information.
2. Select the workshop you wish to attend from the available options.
3. Choose your preferred workshop date and time.
4. If you have any accessibility needs, please select the appropriate option and provide a brief description.
5. Select your payment method and provide any required payment information.
6. Review and agree to the terms and conditions, cancellation policy, and data protection terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, `date`, **required**): Enter the date you are registering for the workshop.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your full name.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, **required**): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, **required**): Enter your phone number.
* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, `text`, **required**): Enter your full address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, `required`): Enter your city.
* **Postcode** (`postcode`, `text`, **required**): Enter your UK postcode.
* **Country** (`country`, `select_one`, **required**): Select your country of residence.
* **Workshop Selection** (`workshop_selection`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the workshop you wish to attend.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, `date`, **required**): Select your preferred workshop date.
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, `select_one`, **required**): Select your preferred workshop time.
* **Experience Level** (`experience_level`, `select_one`, **required**): Select your experience level.
* **Materials Option** (`materials_option`, `select_one`, **required**): Select your materials option.
* **Refreshments** (`refreshments`, `select_one`, **optional**): Select whether you would like to add a refreshments package to your registration.
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, `select_one`, **required**): Select whether you have any accessibility needs.
* **Accessibility Details** (`accessibility_details`, `text`, **optional**): If you have accessibility needs, provide a brief description.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, **required**): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Workshop Fee** (`workshop_fee`, `number`, **required**): Enter the base workshop fee.
* **Materials Fee** (`materials_fee`, `number`, **optional**): Enter the materials cost.
* **Refreshments Fee** (`refreshments_fee`, `number`, **optional**): Enter the refreshments cost.
* **Subtotal** (`subtotal`, `number`, **optional**): The subtotal before VAT.
* **VAT Rate** (`vat_rate`, `number`, **optional**): The VAT percentage.
* **VAT Amount** (`vat_amount`, `number`, **optional**): The VAT amount.
* **Total Amount** (`total_amount`, `number`, **required**): The total amount due in GBP.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, **required**): Select your payment method.
* **Card Type** (`card_type`, `select_one`, **optional**): Select your card type.
* **Cardholder Name** (`cardholder_name`, `text`, **optional**): Enter the name on your card.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your billing address if different from your mailing address.
* **Terms Accepted** (`terms_accepted`, `select_one`, **required**): Confirm you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Cancellation Policy** (`cancellation_policy`, `select_one`, **required**): Confirm you understand the cancellation policy.
* **Data Protection** (`data_protection`, `select_one`, **required**): Confirm you agree to the data protection terms.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, **optional**): Enter any additional information you would like to provide.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure your registration is processed correctly.
* If you have any accessibility needs, please provide a brief description to ensure we can provide the necessary accommodations.
* Please review and agree to the terms and conditions before proceeding with your registration.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [insert contact information].
