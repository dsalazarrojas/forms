# Birdwatching Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering for a birdwatching workshop. It consists of several sections that gather essential information from participants, which is necessary for workshop organization and management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the workshop you are registering for from the provided options.
2. Choose your preferred workshop date and start time.
3. Enter your name, email address, phone number, and age group.
4. Indicate your birdwatching experience level and the reason for attending the workshop.
5. Specify any equipment you will bring or need.
6. Select how you will pay for the workshop.
7. Confirm your willingness to be photographed during the workshop and acknowledge the risks associated with outdoor activities.
8. Confirm that you agree to follow the code of conduct.
9. Provide any additional comments or questions you might have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Title** (id: 2): Choose the workshop you are registering for from the provided options. This will help us tailor the experience to your needs and interests.
* **Workshop Date** (id: 3): Select the date for the workshop you prefer. Please note that dates might be limited, so choose carefully.
* **Workshop Start Time** (id: 4): Select the start time for the workshop you prefer.
* **Registration Date** (id: 5): Enter the current date to ensure timely registration.
* **First Name** (id: 7): Enter your first name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Last Name** (id: 8): Enter your last name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address** (id: 9): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (id: 10): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Age Group** (id: 11): Choose your age group to help us cater to your needs and interests.
* **Birdwatching Experience Level** (id: 13): Indicate your level of birdwatching experience to help us provide suitable guidance and support.
* **Reason for Attending Workshop** (id: 14): Select the reasons why you are interested in attending the workshop.
* **Specific Topics of Interest** (id: 15): If you have specific topics you would like to focus on during the workshop, enter them here.
* **Favorite Bird Groups or Species** (id: 16): If you have a particular interest in certain bird groups or species, enter them here.
* **Attendance Type** (id: 18): Choose how you plan to participate in the workshop (in-person, virtual, etc.).
* **Number Attending Together** (id: 19): If you are bringing family or friends, indicate the number of attendees.
* **Names of Companions** (id: 20): List the names of your companions if you are bringing others.
* **Need Transportation** (id: 22): If you require assistance with transportation, select the option that best suits your needs.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (id: 23): If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, enter them here.
* **Accessibility Accommodations Needed** (id: 24): If you require any special accessibility accommodations, enter them here.
* **Equipment Needed** (id: 25): Indicate what equipment you are bringing or need (e.g., binoculars, etc.).
* **Workshop Registration Cost** (id: 27): Enter the fee amount for the workshop, which you will pay through one of the selected payment methods.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (id: 28): Choose your preferred method of payment (online, check, etc.).
* **May Workshop Photos Include You** (id: 30): Confirm your consent for workshop photos, which will be taken with your understanding of being photographed.
* **Acknowledge Outdoor Activity Risks** (id: 31): Confirm that you understand the risks associated with the workshop's outdoor activities.
* **Agree to Code of Conduct** (id: 32): Confirm that you agree to follow the code of conduct.
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (id: 35): Enter any additional comments or questions you might have.
* **May We Contact You with Updates** (id: 36): Confirm that you consent to be contacted for workshop updates.

## Tips
* Make sure to carefully select the workshop date and start time that suits your needs and schedule.
* Enter accurate and complete information for each field to ensure smooth processing.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to us.
