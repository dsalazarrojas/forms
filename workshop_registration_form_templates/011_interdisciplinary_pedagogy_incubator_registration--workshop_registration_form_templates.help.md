# Interdisciplinary Pedagogy Incubator Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Interdisciplinary Pedagogy Incubator Registration Form is designed to gather essential information from individuals interested in participating in the program. This form will help the program coordinators understand the participants' general information, their areas of interest, and their consent to participate.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the **General Info** section, which is a text field for you to provide your general details.
2.  In the **Participant Info** section, enter your participant information, if applicable.
3.  In the **Interests** section, specify your relevant interests or areas of expertise.
4.  Lastly, in the **Consent** section, indicate your consent to participate in the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info (1)** (`General Info`, `text`, required: false): This is a text field where you can provide any general information about yourself.
* **Participant Info (2)** (`Participant Info`, `text`, required: false): If you are a participant, enter your participant information in this text field.
* **Interests (3)** (`Interests`, `text`, required: false): List your relevant interests or areas of expertise that align with the program's focus.
* **Consent (4)** (`Consent`, `text`, required: false): Please indicate your consent to participate in the program by entering a simple "yes" or "I consent" in this text field.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out all fields that apply to you.
*   If you have any special requests or accommodations, please let us know in the **General Info** section.
*   Be as specific as possible when listing your interests in the **Interests** section.
*   A simple "yes" or "I consent" in the **Consent** section is sufficient to indicate your agreement to participate.
