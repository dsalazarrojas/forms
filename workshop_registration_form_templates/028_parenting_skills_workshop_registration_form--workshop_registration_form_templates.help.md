# Parenting Skills Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for registering participants for a workshop on parenting skills. It is used to collect essential information about each participant to tailor the workshop experience to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the workshop you wish to attend.
2. Choose a time slot that suits you best for the workshop.
3. Fill in your name, email address, phone number, and address.
4. Add any additional notes or comments you have about your participation in the workshop.
5. Select your preferred language (English, Spanish, French, or Mandarin).
6. Choose how you would prefer to participate (Online, In-person, or Phone).
7. Select your preferred time for the workshop (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
8. Choose the workshop topic that interests you the most (Parenting Skills, Child Development, or Parenting Strategies).
9. Enter your age.
10. Select your gender (Male, Female, or Other).
11. If you have children, enter their age.
12. Indicate your current parenting status (Married, Single, or Divorced).
13. Enter the size of your family.
14. Check if you are employed or not.
15. Enter your annual income.
16. Select your education level (Primary, Secondary, or Tertiary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the workshop you wish to attend.
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, `time`, required/optional): Choose a time slot that suits you best for the workshop.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required/optional): Fill in your name.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Fill in your phone number.
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your address.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your participation in the workshop.
* **Participant Preferred Language** (`participant_preferred_language`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select your preferred language (English, Spanish, French, or Mandarin).
* **Participant Preferred Method** (`participant_preferred_method`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose how you would prefer to participate (Online, In-person, or Phone).
* **Participant Preferred Time** (`participant_preferred_time`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your preferred time for the workshop (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
* **Workshop Topic** (`workshop_topic`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the workshop topic that interests you the most (Parenting Skills, Child Development, or Parenting Strategies).
* **Participant Age** (`participant_age`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your age.
* **Participant Gender** (`participant_gender`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your gender (Male, Female, or Other).
* **Participant Child Age** (`participant_child_age`, `number`, required/optional): If you have children, enter their age.
* **Participant Parenting Status** (`participant_parenting_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Indicate your current parenting status (Married, Single, or Divorced).
* **Participant Family Size** (`participant_family_size`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the size of your family.
* **Participant Employed** (`participant_employed`, `select_one`, required/optional): Check if you are employed or not.
* **Participant Income** (`participant_income`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your annual income.
* **Participant Education Level** (`participant_education_level`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your education level (Primary, Secondary, or Tertiary).
