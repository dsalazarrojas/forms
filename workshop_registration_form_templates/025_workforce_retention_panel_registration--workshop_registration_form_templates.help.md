# Workforce Retention Panel Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic information and areas of expertise from participants to help the workforce retention panel better understand its members' needs and concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Participant Email" field.
3. Select all the expertise areas that apply to you from the "Expertise Areas" field.
4. Choose your consent option from the "Consent" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, **required**): Please enter your full name as it appears on your business card or email.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, **required**): Enter your work email address.
* **Expertise Areas** (`expertise_areas`, select_multiple, **required**): Select all the areas of expertise that you have or are interested in developing. This will help us understand your strengths and how you can contribute to the workforce retention panel. You can select multiple options.

	+ Option 2

* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, **required**): Please select one of the consent options to indicate your understanding and agreement to participate in the workforce retention panel.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your name and email address as it appears on your business card or email.
* Only select expertise areas that are true and relevant to your current or desired role.
* If you are unsure about any of the options, please contact your HR representative for guidance.
