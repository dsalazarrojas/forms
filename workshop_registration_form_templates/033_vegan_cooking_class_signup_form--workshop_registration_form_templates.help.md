# Vegan Cooking Class Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather details about individuals who wish to participate in a vegan cooking class.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your User Information (Section 1). 
    * Please fill out this section completely, as it's a required field.
2. Enter your Class Information (Section 2), if you want to register for a specific class.
    * You can skip this section if you don't have any particular class in mind.
3. Fill out Participant Information (Section 3). 
    * Ensure this section is completed as it's a required field.
4. Enter your Payment Information (Section 4). 
    * Complete this section accurately for payment processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Information** (Required): This section collects basic information about you, the registrant.
  - This includes your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about you that we might need to contact you.
- **Class Information** (Optional): If you're registering for a specific class, please provide the class details here.
  - You can skip this section if you're interested in general registration or want to be contacted about upcoming classes.
- **Participant Information** (Required): In this section, please list the names of all participants (you and any other attendees).
  - Ensure to enter accurate participant names and contact information.
- **Payment Information** (Required): Please enter your payment method and other details for processing your payment.
  - This is an essential field and must be completed for successful registration and payment processing.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form carefully and correctly. 
- Ensure all required fields are completed to avoid any issues during the registration process.
- Review your entries before submission to confirm accuracy.
- Contact us if you need any clarification or have questions about any field.
