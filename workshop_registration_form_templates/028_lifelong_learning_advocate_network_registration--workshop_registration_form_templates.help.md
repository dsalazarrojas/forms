# Lifelong Learning Advocate Network Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lifelong Learning Advocate Network Registration form is designed to collect information from potential registrants who are interested in joining the network. It will help us understand their interests, motivations, and agreement to participate in the network.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out each field carefully and accurately. You can ask for help if you need it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Organization Name** (`form_organization_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your organization or institution.
* **Organization Type** (`form_organization_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of your organization from the options provided:
	+ Community Organization
	+ Public Institution
	+ Private Institution
	+ Other
* **First Name** (`form_first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`form_last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`form_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`form_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`form_address`, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **City** (`form_city`, text, optional): Enter the city where you are located.
* **State** (`form_state`, text, optional): Enter the state or province where you are located.
* **Country** (`form_country`, text, optional): Enter the country where you are located.
* **Interests** (`form_interests`, text, optional): Describe your interests related to lifelong learning.
* **Motivation** (`form_motivation`, text, optional): Describe why you are interested in joining the Lifelong Learning Advocate Network.
* **Agreement** (`form_agree`, select_one, optional): Select whether you agree or do not agree to participate in the Lifelong Learning Advocate Network.
	+ I agree to participate in the Lifelong Learning Advocate Network.
	+ I do not agree to participate in the Lifelong Learning Advocate Network.
* **Additional Information** (`form_additional_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional information you would like to share.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`form_terms_conditions`, select_one, optional): Select whether you agree or do not agree to the terms and conditions of the Lifelong Learning Advocate Network.
	+ I agree to the terms and conditions.
	+ I do not agree to the terms and conditions.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and carefully.
* If you need help, do not hesitate to ask.
* Please agree to the terms and conditions before proceeding.
* If you have any additional information or comments, please use the "Additional Information" field.
* If you need to contact us, please use the email address provided at the top of this page.
