# National Online Forensics Competition Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The National Online Forensics Competition Registration form is designed to collect necessary information from participants and their coaches to ensure a smooth and successful registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant details in the Participant Details section.
2. Select the events you wish to participate in.
3. Provide your coach's contact information in the Coach Contacts section.
4. Enter your participant's email address in the Participant Email field.
5. Enter your participant's phone number in the Participant Phone field.
6. Enter the event name in the Event Name field.
7. Select the event start and end dates in the Event Start Date and Event End Date fields, respectively.
8. Enter the event location in the Event Location field.
9. Select the event time in the Event Time field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details (Participant Details)**: This is where you provide your basic details such as name, etc.
* **Events (Events)**: Please select the events you wish to participate in for the competition.
* **Coach Contacts (Coach Contacts)**: Enter your coach's contact information such as name and email.
* **Participant Email (Participant Email)**: Enter your participant's email address to enable us to communicate with you.
* **Participant Phone (Participant Phone)**: Enter your participant's phone number.
* **Event Name (Event Name)**: Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Start Date (Event Start Date)**: Select the start date of the event.
* **Event End Date (Event End Date)**: Select the end date of the event.
* **Event Location (Event Location)**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Time (Event Time)**: Select the time of the event.

## Tips

- Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately to avoid any delays or issues with the registration process.
- If you are experiencing any difficulties with the form, please contact our support team for assistance.
- Please note that we will be in touch with you shortly after you submit the form to confirm your registration details.
