# RSO Online Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The RSO Online Training Registration Form is used to collect registration information from workshop participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date of your workshop session.
2. Select the start time of your workshop session.
3. Enter your RSO (Regular Session Organizer) Name.
4. Enter your Email.
5. Enter your Phone number.
6. Enter your Organization Name.
7. Enter your Job Title.
8. Enter your Department.
9. Select your preferred Training Location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Session Date
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required: false): Select the date of your workshop session.

### Start Time
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required: false): Select the start time of your workshop session.

### Session Time
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, text, required: false): Enter the session time of your workshop session. Please note that this field is not related to the start time, but rather to the total duration of the session.

### RSO Name
* **RSO Name** (`rso_name`, text, required: false): Enter your RSO (Regular Session Organizer) name.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your Email address for communication.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for communication.

### Organization
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required: false): Enter your organization name.

### Job Title
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Enter your job title in your organization.

### Department
* **Department** (`department`, text, required: false): Enter your department in your organization.

### Training Location
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the available Training Locations (Training Center 1, Training Center 2, Training Center 3).
