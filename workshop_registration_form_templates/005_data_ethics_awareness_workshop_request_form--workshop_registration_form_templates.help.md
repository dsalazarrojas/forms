# Data Ethics Awareness Workshop Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Data Ethics Awareness Workshop Request Form is designed to help us understand your interest in attending our Data Ethics Awareness Workshop. It's a form to gather your details and preferences to tailor the workshop content and logistics to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out this form with accurate and relevant information.
2. Ensure that you have not already submitted a workshop request in the past year.
3. If you have any special requests or accommodations, please include them in the "Workshop Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Details** (workshop_details, text, optional): Briefly describe the type of workshop you are interested in attending. For example, "Data Ethics for Business Professionals" or "Data Ethics for Academics and Researchers".
* **Contact Details** (contact_details, text, optional): Provide your name, title, and institution/organization to help us contact you and tailor the workshop content to your needs.
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you with workshop updates and details.
* **Phone** (phone, text, optional): Provide your phone number to enable us to contact you with last-minute updates or changes.
* **Workshop Date** (workshop_date, date, optional): If you have a specific date in mind for the workshop, please enter it in the format YYYY-MM-DD. If you don't have a specific date, we will contact you with available dates.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have not already submitted a workshop request in the past year to avoid duplicating effort and provide the best experience for everyone.
* If you have any special requests or accommodations, please include them in the "Workshop Details" field.
* We will contact you with workshop updates and details via the email address you provide.
* We will use the information you provide to tailor the workshop content and logistics to your needs.
