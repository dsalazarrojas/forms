# Ethical Research In Education Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect necessary information from attendees to provide them with a great experience at the workshop. Please note that some fields are optional and will not affect your registration. Fill out the form as completely as possible to ensure we can tailor the workshop to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Attendee Name in the first field. This is the name we will address you by during the workshop.
2. Enter your Email Address in the second field. We will use this to communicate with you before, during, and after the workshop.
3. Enter your Phone Number in the third field. This will help us reach out to you in case of any changes or updates.
4. Choose if you give Consent to Share Data by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the fourth field. This will allow us to keep you updated on the workshop and its activities.
5. Choose your interests by selecting any number of items from the list in the fifth field. This will help us tailor the workshop content to your needs.
6. Select your Workshop Preferences, choosing one or more time slots from the list in the sixth field. 
7. Choose your Dietary Preferences by selecting one or more options from the list in the seventh field.
8. If you have any Special Requests, enter them in the eighth field. This is an optional field.
9. Add any Additional Comments you may have in the ninth field. This is also an optional field.
10. Click on the Submit button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Name**: Enter your name as you would like to be addressed during the workshop. This is the required information to complete your registration.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address to receive important updates and information before, during, and after the workshop.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number to help us reach out to you in case of any last-minute changes or updates.
* **Consent to Share Data**: Choose 'Yes' to consent to share your data with other attendees, and 'No' if you do not want to share your data.
* **Interests**: Select any number of interests that you have, from the list of available options.
* **Workshop Preferences**: Select one or more time slots that you prefer for the workshop. 
* **Dietary Preferences**: Select one or more options that you prefer for meal times during the workshop. 
* **Special Requests**: Enter any special requests you may have, such as accommodations or equipment needs.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or information you may want to provide.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your registration form.
