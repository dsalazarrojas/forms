# Special Edition Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from users for a workshop or event registration. It is a crucial part of the event planning process and helps organizers keep track of participants' details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select one option from the "Select One" dropdown list.
5. Choose multiple options from the "Select Multiple" dropdown list.
6. Enter a date in the "Date" field.
7. Enter a time in the "Time" field.
8. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
9. Enter an email address in the "Email 2" field.
10. Fill out the "Required Field" field with your name or any other required information.
11. Fill out the "Required Field 2" field with your name or any other required information.
12. Enter a decimal number in the "Decimal" field.
13. Enter an integer number in the "Integer" field.
14. Enter a text description in the "Text Area" field.
15. Enter a text description in the "Text Area 2" field.
16. Enter a decimal number in the "Decimal 2" field.
17. Enter an integer number in the "Integer 2" field.
18. Select one option from the "Select One 2" dropdown list.
19. Choose multiple options from the "Select Multiple 2" dropdown list.
20. Enter a date in the "Date 2" field.
21. Enter a time in the "Time 2" field.
22. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Note 2" field.
23. Enter an email address in the "Email 2" field.
24. Enter a phone number in the "Phone 2" field.
25. Fill out the "Required Field 3" field with your name or any other required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, optional): Choose one option from the dropdown list.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, optional): Choose multiple options from the dropdown list.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter a date in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter a time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Email 2** (`email2`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Required Field** (`required_field`, text, required): Fill out this field with your name or any other required information.
* **Required Field 2** (`required_field_2`, text, required): Fill out this field with your name or any other required information.
* **Decimal** (`decimal`, number, optional): Enter a decimal number.
* **Integer** (`integer`, number, optional): Enter an integer number.
* **Text Area** (`text_area`, text, optional): Enter a text description.
* **Text Area 2** (`text_area_2`, text, optional): Enter a text description.
* **Decimal 2** (`decimal_2`, number, optional): Enter a decimal number.
* **Integer 2** (`integer_2`, number, optional): Enter an integer number.
* **Select One 2** (`select_one_2`, select_one, optional): Choose one option from the dropdown list.
* **Select Multiple 2** (`select_multiple_2`, select_multiple, optional): Choose multiple options from the dropdown list.
* **Date 2** (`date_2`, date, optional): Enter a date in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Time 2** (`time_2`, time, optional): Enter a time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Note 2** (`note_2`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Email 2** (`email_2`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone 2** (`phone_2`, text, optional): Enter a phone number for contact purposes.
* **Required Field 3** (`required_field_3`, text, required): Fill out this field with your name or any other required information.
