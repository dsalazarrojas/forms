# HR Policy Modernization Roundtable Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Policy Modernization Roundtable Registration form is designed to collect personal and professional information from workshop registrants. This will enable us to provide a tailored experience for each participant, accommodate any accessibility needs, and ensure that everyone is properly informed about the sessions they will be attending.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow the steps below:

1.  Enter your First Name and Last Name in their respective fields.
2.  Enter your official Email address for contact purposes.
3.  Specify your Organization, Job Title, and Role.
4.  Enter your Company name.
5.  Enter your Contact Email and Contact Phone number.
6.  Select your preferred Session Date.
7.  Choose your Session Preferences.
8.  Specify any Accessibility Needs you may have.
9.  Provide any Accommodations you require.
10. Enter any Special Requests you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1): Enter your first name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Last Name** (2): Enter your last name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email** (3): Enter your official email address for contact purposes.
* **Organization** (4): Enter your current or affiliated organization's name.
* **Job Title** (5): Enter your current job title or position.
* **Role** (6): Enter your role or position within your organization.
* **Company** (7): Enter your company or organization's name.
* **Contact Email** (8): Enter an email address where we can reach you for further communication.
* **Contact Phone** (9): Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Preferred Session Date** (10): Select the date you prefer for the workshop sessions.
* **Session Preferences** (11): Specify any preferences you have for the workshop sessions, e.g., topics or time slots.
* **Accessibility Needs** (12): Specify any accessibility needs you have for participating in the workshop, e.g., wheelchair access, audio equipment.
* **Accommodations** (13): Specify any accommodations you require for the workshop, e.g., dietary restrictions.
* **Special Requests** (14): Enter any special requests you have for the workshop, e.g., specific session topics or speakers.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required.
