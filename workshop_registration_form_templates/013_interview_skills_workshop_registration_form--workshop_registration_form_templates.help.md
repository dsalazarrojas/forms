# Interview Skills Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Interview Skills Workshop. It collects information such as name, email, phone number, and preferred workshop type.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address to ensure we can contact you regarding the workshop.
3. Provide your Phone number so we can reach out to you if needed.
4. Select the type of interview skills workshop you are interested in from the dropdown options.
5. Choose the skills you would like to improve from the dropdown options.
6. Add any Additional Comments if you have any specific needs or requests.
7. Click on the "Submit" button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Name**: Enter the name of the workshop you are registering for.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you if needed.
* **Time**: Enter the preferred time for the workshop (note: format should be in 24-hour format, e.g., 14:00).
* **Date**: Enter the preferred date for the workshop (note: format should be in YYYY-MM-DD format, e.g., 2024-02-15).
* **What type of interview skills workshop are you interested in?**: Select the type of workshop from the dropdown options.
* **What type of skills do you want to improve?**: Select the skills you want to improve from the dropdown options.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or requests you have regarding the workshop.
* **Submit**: Click this button to complete your registration.
* **Cancel**: Click this button to cancel your registration. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and complete registration information.
* Double-check your email address for accuracy before submitting.
* Be specific with your preferred workshop time and date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [insert contact information].
