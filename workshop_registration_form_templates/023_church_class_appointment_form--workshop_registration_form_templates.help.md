# Church Class Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the registration process for students interested in participating in church classes. It aims to collect essential information to ensure a smooth and effective class experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your Student Full Name in the first field.
2. Enter your Email Address for course materials and links related to the class.
3. Provide your Phone Number for contact purposes.
4. Select the Course and Section you are interested in by choosing the relevant option.
5. Specify your Preferred Class Section/Time by choosing from the available options.
6. (Optional) If you have previously completed Bible or Theology classes, provide the details in this field.
7. (Optional) If you have specific goals or objectives for taking the class, please provide a brief description.
8. Choose whether you are taking the class for Credit (Certificate Program) or Audit (Personal Growth).
9. (Optional) If you are not affiliated with a church, please let us know your current church affiliation.
10. If you require any special accommodations, please specify in this field.
11. Let us know how you heard about the class by selecting the relevant option.
12. Confirm your registration by selecting "I Confirm" or "Other" if you need to provide more information.
13. Finally, please provide the Date of Registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Student Full Name** (`student_name`, `text`, `required`)
The Student Full Name field is where you should enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.

*   Please provide your full name as it is registered in our records.

### 2. **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, `required`)
The Email Address field is used to provide your email address for course materials and links.

*   Enter the email address you use for your primary communication.

### 3. **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, `required`)
The Phone Number field is for contact purposes, and we will use it to reach you.

*   Enter your phone number as you would like to be contacted.

### 4. **-- Course and Section Selection --** (`header_class_selection`, `note`, `required`)
This section allows you to select the course and section you are interested in.

*   Please choose the class that aligns with your interests and schedule.

### 5. **Course Name** (`course_of_interest`, `select_one`, `required`)
The Course Name field is where you select the specific class you wish to attend.

*   Choose the course that aligns with your interests and needs.

### 6. **Preferred Class Section/Time** (`section_preference`, `select_one`, `required`)
The Preferred Class Section/Time field is where you specify your preferred class time.

*   Select the class time that suits your schedule.

### 7. **Previous Bible or Theology Classes Completed** (`previous_classes_completed`, `text`, `required`)
This field is for students who have previously taken classes in the same subject area.

*   If you have completed previous classes, please provide the details.

### 8. **-- Personal Goals --** (`header_goals`, `note`, `optional`)
This section is for you to specify your personal goals for taking the class.

*   If you have specific goals, please provide a brief description.

### 9. **What do you hope to learn in this class?** (`learning_objectives`, `text`, `required`)
The What do you hope to learn in this class? field is where you specify your objectives.

*   Please provide a brief description of what you hope to achieve.

### 10. **Are you taking this class for credit or audit?** (`audit_or_credit`, `select_one`, `required`)
The Are you taking this class for credit or audit? field is where you choose your purpose.

*   If you are taking the class for credit, please choose "For Credit (Certificate Program)".

### 11. **Current Church Affiliation** (`church_affiliation`, `text`, `optional`)
The Current Church Affiliation field is for students not affiliated with a church.

*   If you do not have a church affiliation, please specify.

### 12. **Any special accommodations?** (`special_accommodations`, `text`, `required`)
The Any special accommodations? field is where you specify any special needs.

*   If you need any special accommodations, please specify in this field.

### 13. **How did you hear about this class?** (`hear_about_class`, `select_one`, `required`)
The How did you hear about this class? field is where you choose your source.

*   Choose how you heard about the class.

### 14. **I confirm my registration for the selected section** (`registration_confirm`, `select_one`, `required`)
The I confirm my registration for the selected section field is where you confirm your registration.

*   Please select "I Confirm" to confirm your registration.

### 15. **Date of Registration** (`date_submitted`, `date`, `required`)
The Date of Registration field is where you provide the date of your registration.

*   Please enter the date of your registration.

## Tips
*   Please review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy.
*   If you have any doubts or questions, please contact the relevant authorities.
*   Be honest in your responses, and provide accurate information.
*   If you need any special accommodations, please specify them in the relevant field.
*   Please confirm your registration to ensure a smooth class experience.
