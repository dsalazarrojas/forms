# Sustainability In Business Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for participants to register for the Sustainability In Business Workshop, a workshop focused on sustainable practices and strategies for business.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the **Participant Information** section (Page 1) with your name and any other details you want to share about yourself.
2.  Move on to the **Participant Details** (Page 2) and provide any additional information that you think would be useful for us to know about your experience, background, or expertise.
3.  In the **Contact Details** (Page 3) section, provide your contact information, such as your email address, phone number, or any other contact method you prefer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information (Page 1)**
	+ **Label: participant_information**
	+ **Type: text**
	+ **Required:** No
	+ **Description:** This is where you can write a little bit about yourself, such as your name, profession, or any other information that you think would be helpful for us to know about your background.
* **Participant Details (Page 2)**
	+ **Label: participant_details**
	+ **Type: text**
	+ **Required:** No
	+ **Description:** Share any additional information about your experience, background, or expertise that you think would be useful for us to know.
* **Contact Details (Page 3)**
	+ **Label: contact_details**
	+ **Type: text**
	+ **Required:** No
	+ **Description:** Enter your contact information, such as your email address, phone number, or any other contact method you prefer.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete the form accurately and honestly.
* If you are not sure about something, don't hesitate to contact us for help.
* We will keep your data private and secure, and will only use it to manage the workshop registrations.
