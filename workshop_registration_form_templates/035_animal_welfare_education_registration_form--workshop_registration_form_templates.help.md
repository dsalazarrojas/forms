# Animal Welfare Education Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect registration information from participants for an animal welfare education workshop.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Please fill out each field carefully and accurately.** Each field is required for a specific reason, and we appreciate your attention to detail.
2. **Participant Full Name:** Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
3. **Participant Age or Guardian Name:** If you are an adult, enter your age. If you are a minor, enter the name of your guardian.
4. **Contact Email Address:** Enter your email address where you can be reached for updates and information.
5. **Primary Interest Area:** Select the area of animal welfare that interests you most (e.g., Responsible Pet Ownership, Wildlife Conservation and Habitat, etc.).
6. **Workshop Date Selection:** Choose your preferred workshop date from the available options.
7. **Previous Animal Welfare Education:** If you have previously attended any classes or workshops related to animal welfare, list them here (optional).
8. **Dietary Restrictions for Provided Lunch:** If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, please list them here (optional).
9. **Photography and Media Consent:** Select "Yes" if you consent to be photographed or appear in media related to the workshop, or "No" if you do not.
10. **Emergency Contact Name:** Enter the name of the person you would like us to contact in case of an emergency.
11. **Emergency Contact Phone Number:** Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
12. **Digital Signature:** Enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name** (<participant_name_full>): This is a required field where you enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Participant Age or Guardian Name** (<participant_age_status>): This field is required and should be filled with your age if you are an adult or your guardian's name if you are a minor.
* **Contact Email Address** (<participant_contact_email>): This field is required and should be filled with your email address where you can be reached for updates and information.
* **Primary Interest Area** (<primary_interest_area>): This is a required field where you select the area of animal welfare that interests you most (e.g., Responsible Pet Ownership, Wildlife Conservation and Habitat, etc.).
* **Workshop Date Selection** (<workshop_date_selection>): This field is required and should be selected from the provided options (e.g., Session A, Session B, Session C).
* **Previous Animal Welfare Education** (<previous_education_summary>): This field is optional and should be used to list any previous classes or workshops attended related to animal welfare.
* **Dietary Restrictions for Provided Lunch** (<dietary_needs_lunch>): This field is optional and should be used to specify any dietary restrictions or allergies.
* **Photography and Media Consent** (<photo_consent_status>): This field is required and should be selected to indicate whether you consent to be photographed or not.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (<emergency_contact_full_name>): This field is required and should be filled with the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (<emergency_contact_phone_number>): This field is required and should be filled with the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Digital Signature** (<participant_signature_digital>): This field is required and should be filled with your name as it appears on your government-issued ID.

## Tips

* Please review each field carefully and fill out all required fields to ensure a smooth registration process.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
* We appreciate your attention to detail and cooperation in ensuring that your registration is accurate and complete.
