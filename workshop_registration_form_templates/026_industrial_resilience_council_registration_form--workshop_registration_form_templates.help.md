This form is for registering members for the Commercial Resilience Council. This is an internal registration form used to capture relevant information about an individual's work profile. The form captures the essential details about the member's personal and professional information, including name, contact details, job profile, and commercial resilience. Please ensure that you have the required permissions to complete this form, and that you have not already registered with the Commercial Resilience Council before.

# Commercial Resilience Council Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Commercial Resilience Council Registration Form is designed to collect and store information about individuals who are part of the Commercial Resilience Council. This form is used for internal purposes only and is not intended for external use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Email in the Email field.
4. Enter your Job Title in the Job Title field.
5. Enter your Company in the Company field.
6. Enter your Contact Number in the Contact Number field.
7. Enter your Time In Office in the Time In Office field in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
8. Enter your Time Out in the Time Out field in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
9. Enter your Date of Joining in the Date field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
10. Enter your Notes about your role in the Notes field.
11. Enter your Job Description in the Job Description field.
12. Enter your Work Email in the Work Email field.
13. Enter your Work Phone in the Work Phone field.
14. Enter your Work Address in the Work Address field.
15. Enter your Work Phone Number in the Work Number field.
16. Enter your Work Zip in the Work Zip field.
17. Select your Job Type from the options provided.
18. Select your Role Type from the options provided.
19. Enter a brief description of your commercial resilience in the Commercial Resilience field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as per your official records.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as per your official records.
* **Email** (`email`, text, required: false): Enter your official work email address.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Enter your job title as per your official designation.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required: false): Enter your official company name.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter your official contact number.
* **Time In Office** (`time_in_office`, time, required: false): Enter your official time of joining the office in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Time Out** (`time_out`, time, required: false): Enter your official time of leaving the office in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter your official date of joining in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information about your role.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of your job responsibilities.
* **Work Email** (`work_email`, text, required: false): Enter your official work email address.
* **Work Phone** (`work_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your official work phone number.
* **Work Address** (`work_address`, text, required: false): Enter your official work address.
* **Work Number** (`work_number`, text, required: false): Enter your official work phone number.
* **Work Zip** (`work_zip`, text, required: false): Enter your official work zip code.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, text, required: false): Select your job type from the options provided.
* **Role Type** (`role_type`, text, required: false): Select your role type from the options provided.
* **Commercial Resilience** (`commercial_resilience`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of your commercial resilience.

Note: The fields with 'required: false' are optional, but it is recommended to fill them for better information retrieval. Ensure that you have the necessary permissions to complete this form.
