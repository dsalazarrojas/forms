# Product Launch Team Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register team members for the product launch team. It helps the team to have a clear understanding of who is joining the team and their roles and skills.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all fields that are marked as "required". These will be indicated by an asterisk (\*).
2. For "Roles", select all roles that apply to you. You can select multiple roles.
3. For "Skills", select the skills that you have or are interested in.
4. Enter your "Availability" in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
5. Enter your "Email" address.
6. Enter your "Phone" number.
7. If you want to add any additional information, use the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Launch Team** (`product_launch_team_registration`, text, required): This field should be filled out with the name of the team member or a brief description of the team member.
* **Roles** (`product_launch_team_registration_roles`, select_multiple, required): Select all roles that apply to you, such as Marketing, Development, Design, Product.
* **Skills** (`product_launch_team_registration_skills`, select_one, required): Select the skills that you have or are interested in, such as Writing, Design, Development, Analysis, Testing.
* **Availability** (`product_launch_team_registration_availability`, date, required): Enter your availability in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
* **Email** (`product_launch_team_registration_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`product_launch_team_registration_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Note** (`product_launch_team_registration_note`, note, required): If you want to add any additional information, use this field.
* **Time** (`product_launch_team_registration_time`, time, optional): This field is not required and is not explained in the provided yaml file.
