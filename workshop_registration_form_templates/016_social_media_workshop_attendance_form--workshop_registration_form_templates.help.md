# Social Media Workshop Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the fields and requirements of the Social Media Workshop Attendance Form to help you complete it accurately and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Registration Form**: This section is for providing basic information about yourself.
2. **Social Media**: Please enter your social media handles (e.g., Twitter, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, etc.).
3. **Contact Info**: Enter your contact information, including Email and Phone.
4. **Workshop Info**: Enter the workshop name, start and end dates, start and end times, and location.
5. **Participant Info**: Enter any additional participant information.
6. **Payment**: Enter payment information (Note: This field is not currently in use).
7. **Workshop Attendance**: Enter your attendance status for the workshop.
8. **Workshop Participants**: Enter any additional workshop participant information.
9. **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the workshop.
10. **Submit**: Click this button to submit your completed form.
