# Production Intelligence Certification Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Production Intelligence Certification Registration form is used to register for the Production Intelligence Certification program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address.
2. Select your desired Certification Level from the dropdown options.
3. (Optional) Enter your Motivation for obtaining certification.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Select the date and time when you intend to submit your application.
6. Enter the email address of the person submitting the application.
7. Enter the email address of the person creating the application.
8. Enter the email address of the person updating the application.
9. (Optional) Leave the Updated At field empty, as it is not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (text)**: This field is used to capture the user's first name.
* **Last Name (text)**: This field is used to capture the user's last name.
* **Email (email)**: This field is used to capture the user's email address. Please ensure that the email is entered correctly.
* **Certification Level (select_one)**: This field is used to specify the level of certification desired. Please select the appropriate level from the dropdown options.
* **Motivation (note)**: This field is optional and is used to capture the user's motivation for obtaining certification.
* **Phone (text)**: This field is required to capture the user's phone number.
* **Submission Date (date)**: This field is required to capture the date when the user intends to submit their application.
* **Submission Time (time)**: This field is required to capture the time when the user intends to submit their application.
* **Submitter (email)**: This field is required to capture the email address of the person submitting the application.
* **Created By (email)**: This field is required to capture the email address of the person creating the application.
* **Updated By (email)**: This field is required to capture the email address of the person updating the application.
* **Updated At (date)**: This field is not required and is used to capture the date when the application was last updated.
* **Deleted At (date)**: This field is not required and is used to capture the date when the application was deleted.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately to avoid any errors or rejections.
* If you encounter any issues while filling out the form, please do not hesitate to reach out to the support team.
