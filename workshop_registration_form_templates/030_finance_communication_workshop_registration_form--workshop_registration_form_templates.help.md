# finance_communication_workshop_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a finance communication workshop. The information collected will be used by workshop organizers to track attendee data and ensure a smooth workshop experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields:
	* **User Data**: Enter any relevant data about yourself (e.g., name, email).
	* **Name**: Enter your full name.
	* **Email**: Enter your email address.
	* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
	* **Workshop Type**: Choose if you will be attending as a "Yes" or "No".
	* **Date**: Choose a date for the workshop.
	* **Time**: Choose a time for the workshop.
	* **Workshop Duration**: Choose a duration for the workshop.
	* **Time Zone**: Choose a time zone for the workshop.
2. Make sure to review your entries for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **User Data**
* **User Data**: A field for entering any relevant data about yourself.

### 2. **Name**
* **Name**: Enter your full name.

### 3. **Email**
* **Email**: Enter your email address.

### 4. **Phone Number**
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.

### 5. **Workshop Type**
* **Workshop Type**: Choose "Yes" if you will be attending the workshop, "No" otherwise.

### 6. **Date**
* **Date**: Choose a date for the workshop.

### 7. **Time**
* **Time**: Choose a time for the workshop.

### 8. **Workshop Duration**
* **Workshop Duration**: Choose the duration of the workshop.

### 9. **Workshop Time Zone**
* **Workshop Time Zone**: Choose a time zone for the workshop.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Review your entries for accuracy before submitting.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, contact the workshop organizers.
