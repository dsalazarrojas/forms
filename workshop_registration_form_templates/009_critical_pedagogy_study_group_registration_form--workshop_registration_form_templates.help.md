# Critical Pedagogy Study Group Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Critical Pedagogy Study Group Registration Form is used to gather information from participants interested in joining the study group. This form is designed to collect relevant details, including name, contact information, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow the steps below:

1. Ensure you have read and understood the form's purpose and any relevant instructions.
2. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective text fields.
3. Enter a valid email address in the Email field (we recommend using a professional or university email).
4. If you have a Phone number, enter it in the Phone field.
5. Select a suitable Time for the study group meeting using the time field.
6. Choose a Date for the study group meeting using the Date field.
7. For the Select One field, choose the relevant option (Yes or No) based on your interests.
8. For the Select Multiple field, choose one or more relevant options (Yes or No).
9. Add any additional comments or notes in the Note field.
10. Enter a valid second email address for verification purposes in the Email 2 field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents. This is a required field.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents. This is a required field.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you check regularly. This will be used for communication and updates. This is a required field.
* **Phone**: Enter a phone number where you can be reached for any questions or updates. This is an optional field.
* **Time**: Select a suitable time for the study group meeting. This is an optional field.
* **Date**: Choose a suitable date for the study group meeting. This is an optional field.
* **Select One**: Choose the option that best represents your interests. This is an optional field.
* **Select Multiple**: Choose one or more options that best represent your interests. This is an optional field.
* **Note**: Add any additional comments or notes about your participation. This is an optional field.
* **Email 2**: Enter a valid second email address for verification purposes. This is an optional field.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the relevant authorities or support team.
* This form is for registration purposes only and should not be used for any other activities.
