# Social Security Benefits Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register attendees for a training session on Social Security Benefits. Fill out the form to let us know you're attending the session and provide your contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the session information and ensure you have the correct session ID.
2. Fill out the form with your details, including attendee name, contact email, phone number, and role (if any).
3. If applicable, fill out any additional session-specific fields.
4. Provide any additional comments or questions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Registration Form** (`workshop_registration_form`, `text`, optional): This field is not required but is a placeholder for any additional information you may need to provide about your registration.
* **Attendee Information** (`attendee_info`, `text`, optional): Please fill out this field with your details, including your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Session Information** (`session_info`, `text`, optional): If you have any special requests or requirements for the session, please mention them in this field.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, optional): Fill out your contact email and phone number so we can get back to you with any updates.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Please ensure this is a valid email address we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Provide your phone number for any follow-up calls or reminders.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): If you have any specific date requirements or preferences, please mention them in this field.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): If you have any time-specific requirements or preferences, please mention them in this field.
* **Session ID** (`session_id`, `text`, optional): Please ensure you have the correct session ID provided by the session administrators.
* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, `text`, optional): Fill out your name for the session.
* **Role** (`role`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your role for the session (Admin, Moderator, Speaker, Other).
* **Session Name** (`session_name`, `text`, optional): If you're attending a specific session, please mention the name of it.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional information or comments, please share them in this field.

Note: All fields are optional, but please ensure you fill out the required fields (email and phone) for us to get back to you.
