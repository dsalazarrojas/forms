# videography_workshop_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for workshop attendance. It collects details about the workshop, the attendee, and the schedule.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the workshop you wish to enroll in.
2. Provide your first and last names.
3. Enter your email address for communication purposes.
4. Enter your phone number (optional).
5. Select the date of the workshop you wish to attend.
6. Choose the time slot you prefer for the workshop.
7. Select the workshops you would like to attend.
8. Add any additional notes about your attendance.
9. Confirm your email address to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Name** (`workshop_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the workshop you wish to attend.
* **first**, (`first_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your first name.
* **last**, (`last_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your last name.
* **email**, (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address for communication.
* **phone**, (`phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **date**, (`workshop_date`, Date, Optional): Select the date of the workshop you wish to attend.
* **time**, (`workshop_time`, Time, Optional): Choose your preferred time slot for the workshop.
* **select_workshops**, (`selected_workshops`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the workshops you would like to attend.
* **note**, (`note`, Text, Optional): Add any additional notes about your attendance.
* **confirm_email**, (`confirm_email`, Text, Optional): Confirm your email address for accuracy.

## Tips
- Ensure your email address is entered correctly to receive communication about the workshop.
- If you select multiple workshops, ensure you have space and are available for each selected workshop.
- Use the notes field to communicate any specific needs or requirements you have for the workshops you selected.
