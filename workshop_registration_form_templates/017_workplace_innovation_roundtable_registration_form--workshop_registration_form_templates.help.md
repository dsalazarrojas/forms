# Workplace Innovation Roundtable Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Innovation Roundtable Registration Form is designed to collect registration information from attendees for events where multiple attendees are expected.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form completely and accurately with your registration information.
2. Ensure that you register yourself and not multiple people at once.
3. If you have already registered for the event, you do not need to re-register.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info**: This field is for your personal details such as name and any other relevant information you'd like to share.
* **Contact Info**: Provide your contact information so that we can reach out to you with any last-minute updates or information about the event.
* **Registration Info**: Confirm your registration and provide any additional information about your registration.
* **Event Info**: If this is a recurring event, please provide the information about the event such as date, time, location, etc.
* **Company**: If you are representing a company, please provide the company name and any relevant information about your company.
* **Role**: Please provide your role within the organization.
* **Email**: Provide your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: Provide your phone number for any additional contact purposes.
* **Event Attendees**: Please select who will be attending the event from the provided options. This is to help us with seating and catering arrangements.
* **Event Attendee 1-15**: These fields are for providing details for each of the attendees you selected. Please provide the name, email, and phone number for each attendee.

Tips:
* Ensure that you register each attendee separately to avoid any registration confusion.
* Double-check your registration information for accuracy.
* If you have any dietary restrictions or special requests, please let us know in the registration info field.
