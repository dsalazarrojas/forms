# Faculty And Staff Workshop On Gender Equality And Implicit Bias - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to register faculty and staff for a workshop on gender equality and implicit bias. It collects essential information from registrants, including their name, email, phone number, and any additional notes or faculty-specific information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "phone" field.
4. Select the desired workshop date from the "workshop_date" field.
5. Choose the workshop time that suits you best from the "workshop_time" field.
6. Add any additional notes or comments in the "notes" field.
7. If you are a faculty member, provide any relevant faculty-specific notes in the "faculty_notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name (text, required)**: Please enter your full name as it appears on official records.
* **email (email, required)**: Enter your institutional email address where you can be reached for workshop-related matters.
* **phone (text, optional)**: Provide your contact phone number to reach you regarding the workshop.
* **date (date, optional)**: Select the date you prefer for the workshop. Please choose from the available options: Thursday, Dec 14 or Friday, Dec 15.
* **workshop_date (select_one, optional)**: This field is a duplicate of the "date" field. Please disregard this field.
* **workshop_time (time, optional)**: Select the time slot that suits you best for the workshop.
* **notes (note, optional)**: Add any additional comments or questions you have regarding the workshop.
* **faculty (text, optional)**: If you are a faculty member, provide any relevant information about your faculty-specific needs or requirements.
* **faculty_notes (note, optional)**: Add any faculty-specific comments or questions.
* **phone (text, optional)**: This field is a duplicate of the "phone" field. Please disregard this field.

Tips:
* Ensure you fill out all mandatory fields correctly to ensure successful submission.
* Double-check your details before submitting the form.
* If you have any difficulties or questions, contact the workshop organizers for assistance.
