# Online Woodworking Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Woodworking Training Registration form is used to collect participant information for online woodworking sessions. It is intended for workshop organizers or administrators to register participants and manage session details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, start by providing the required information for each field. This includes:
	* Session Name
	* Date of Session
	* Time of Session
	* Participant Name
	* Email
	* Phone
2. Select the correct Workshop Provider from the dropdown menu.
3. Add any Additional Notes if necessary, but this is optional.
4. Once you have completed all the required fields, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Name** (`session_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the session you are registering participants for.
* **Date of Session** (`session_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the session in a standard date format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Time of Session** (`session_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the session in a 24-hour format (e.g., 14:30).
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter the participant's name.
* **Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Workshop Provider** (`workshop_provider`, select_one, required): Select the provider of the workshop from the available options:
	+ Woodworking Workshop
	+ Training Center
	+ DIY Class
	+ Online Course
* **Additional Notes** (`participant_notes`, note, optional): This field is for any additional notes or comments about the participant.
