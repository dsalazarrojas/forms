# Training Venue Expression Of Interest - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form allows training venues to express their interest in being listed on our platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the venue information section, providing the details about your training venue.
2. Provide your contact information so we can get in touch with you for further details.
3. Compare your options with our platform to express your interest.
4. Click on the "Submit" button to submit your Expression Of Interest.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Venue Information** (Venue Information, text, required: false): This section is for you to provide details about your training venue, such as its name, address, and description.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, required: false): Please provide your name, email, and phone number so we can get in touch with you for further details.
* **Compare Options** (Compare Options, text, required: false): Here, you can compare your training offerings with our platform's requirements to express your interest.
* **Venue Details** (Venue Details, text, required: false): If you have any additional information about your venue that you'd like to share, please include it here.
* **Submit** (Submit, note, required: false): This is a confirmation button to submit your Expression Of Interest. Click on it to send your application.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all fields that apply to your situation.
* If you have already submitted an Expression Of Interest form for the same venue, please do not resubmit.
* You can use this form multiple times if you have multiple training venues to list.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at <form@gic.mx>.
