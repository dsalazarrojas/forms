# Landscaping Association Workshop Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Landscaping Association Workshop Membership Form is designed to collect information from potential members who wish to participate in a workshop. It is intended for new or renewed membership applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the Membership Application page.
2. Provide details about your workshop preferences in the Workshop Participant Details page.
3. Select relevant options for the Participant Information page.
4. Choose your preferred workshop details in the Workshop Details page.
5. Enter your membership information and payment details on the Payment Details page.
6. Enter the contact person and phone number on the Contact Person and Phone Number pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Membership Application
* **Membership Application** (Text): This is the main section where you will provide a brief description of your membership application. Please keep it concise and relevant to your application.

### Workshop Participant Details
* **Workshop Participant Details** (Text): This field is for providing information about your workshop preferences. Please type out your thoughts and details about which workshop you are interested in.

### Participant Information
* **Participant Information** (Select Multiple): Please select all the options that apply to your situation. This will help us understand your specific needs and preferences.

### Workshop Details
* **Workshop Details** (Select One): Choose the workshop details that best match your preferences.

### Membership Information
* **Membership Information** (Number): This field is for entering a numeric value related to your membership status. Please enter the correct number according to your status.

### Payment Details
* **Payment Details** (Text): This field is for providing payment-related information, such as the payment method or amount. Please type out your payment details.

### Contact Person
* **Contact Person** (Email): Please enter the email address of the contact person for your workshop.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (Text): This field is for entering your phone number. Please type out your phone number.

Note: This form is designed to be filled out by the potential member themselves, and we will review and process the information accordingly. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
