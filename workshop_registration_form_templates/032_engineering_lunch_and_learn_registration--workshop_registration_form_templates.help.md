## engineering_lunch_and_learn_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect user information and preferences for an "Engineering Lunch and Learn" event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Scroll down to the first page** (User Details)
2. Fill in your **User Details**:
  	* Enter your **Name** and **Company** in the respective fields.
3. Scroll down to the second page (Session Preferences)
4. Choose your **Session Preferences** for the event.
5. Scroll down to the third page (Event Details)
6. Fill in the **Event Details** (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details** (`Name`, `Company`, required): Enter your full name and your company name to help us get to know you better.
* **Session Preferences** (`Session Preferences`, required): Select your preferred session topic or topic of interest to tailor the event to your needs.
* **Event Details** (`Event Details`, optional): Provide any additional comments or information about your event preferences.

## Tips
* Please fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please include them in the **Event Details** field.
* Once you have completed the form, click submit to reserve your spot at the event.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, please don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
