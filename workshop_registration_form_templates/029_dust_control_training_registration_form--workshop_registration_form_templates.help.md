# Dust Control Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for the dust control training program. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure successful registration.

## How To Complete This Form
- Click on each page to start filling out the form.
- Fill in your **Name**, **Company**, **Position** fields with your relevant information.
- Enter your **Email** address in the format of `example@email.com`.
- Enter your **Phone** number without any special characters.
- Select a valid **Date** from the calendar provided.
- Select a valid **Time** from the time options provided.
- Choose your **Attendance** status from the options "Option 1" or "Option 2".
- Optionally, add any **Comments** about your registration or training needs.
- If you agree to the terms of the participation agreement, type "I agree" in the **Participation Agreement** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Fill in your full name as you would like to be addressed in the training materials and communication.
- **Company** (`company`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your company if applicable.
- **Position** (`position`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your job title or position within your company.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter a valid email address to receive training materials and communication.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Date** (`date`, `date`, required/optional): Select a date from the calendar provided that you plan to attend the training session.
- **Time** (`time`, `time`, required/optional): Select a time from the time options provided for the training session.
- **Attendance** (`attendance`, `select_one`, required): Choose your attendance status by selecting one of the options.
  • Option 1: I will attend the training session.
  • Option 2: I will not attend the training session.
- **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required/optional): Optionally, add any comments or notes about your registration or training needs.
- **Participation Agreement** (`agreement`, `text`, required/optional): Type "I agree" to confirm your understanding of the terms of participation.
