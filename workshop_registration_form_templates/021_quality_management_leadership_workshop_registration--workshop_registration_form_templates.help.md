# Quality Management Leadership Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register attendees for a Quality Management Leadership Workshop. It collects personal details, attendance preferences, and relevant dates to ensure accurate registration and organization of the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Contact Number for easy communication.
3. Select your preferred Workshop Date and Time.
4. Specify the Workshop Duration.
5. If necessary, provide any Special Requirements.
6. Choose your preferred Attendee Details.
7. Add any Notes if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your official email address for communication.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your contact number for easy communication.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, required): Select the date you prefer for the workshop.
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, time, required): Select the time slot for the workshop.
* **Workshop Duration** (`workshop_duration`, number, required): Specify the duration of the workshop (e.g., 1 hour, 2 days, etc.).
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, select_multiple, optional): Choose any additional requirements you may need for the workshop (e.g., wheelchair accessibility, etc.).
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, select_one, required): Select who you are representing (e.g., yourself, a team member, etc.).
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you registered for the workshop.
* **Registration Time** (`registration_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you registered for the workshop.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your attendance.

## Tips
* Double-check your input for accuracy before submitting.
* Ensure you have the necessary approvals or permissions before registering.
* Review the workshop details and terms before submitting.
