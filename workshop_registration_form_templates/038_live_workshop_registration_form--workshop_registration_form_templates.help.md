# Live Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering participants in live workshops, ensuring that organizers have the necessary information to prepare for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Participant name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Select the workshop you wish to attend from the list of available workshops.
4. Provide your workshop date of interest.
5. Enter your participant email (if different from your personal email).
6. Enter your participant phone number(s) for easy contact.
7. Enter your participant's phone number 2 (if applicable).
8. Enter your participant's phone number 3 (if applicable).
9. Choose the time slot you prefer for the workshop.
10. Enter your participant's address.
11. Enter your participant's city.
12. Enter your participant's state.
13. Enter your participant's zip code.
14. Enter your participant's country.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant name**: Enter your name as it should appear in our records.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you regarding the workshop.
* **Workshop name**: Choose the workshop you wish to attend from the list. 
* **Workshop date**: Enter the date you are interested in attending the workshop.
* **Participant email**: Enter your email address for our records (if different from the email you entered in the "Email" field).
* **Participant phone**: Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Participant phone 2**: Enter your secondary phone number (if applicable).
* **Participant phone 3**: Enter your tertiary phone number (if applicable).
* **Workshop time**: Choose the time slot you prefer for the workshop.
* **Participant address**: Enter your full address for our records.
* **Participant city**: Enter your city of residence.
* **Participant state**: Enter your state of residence.
* **Participant zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **Participant country**: Enter your country of residence.
* **Participant note**: Enter any additional information you would like to provide about your participation.

## Tips
* Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any inconvenience during the workshop.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us at [insert contact information].
