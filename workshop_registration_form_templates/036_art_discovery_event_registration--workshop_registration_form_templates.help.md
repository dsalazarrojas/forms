# Art Discovery Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Discovery Event Registration form is designed to collect information from attendees for an art event. This information will help the organizers create a unique and enjoyable experience for each attendee.

## How To Complete This Form

### Section 1: Attendee Information
#### <ul>
* **Full Name** (Full Name): This is the name of the attendee who is registering.
* **Email Address** (Email Address): This is the email address of the attendee who is registering. Please ensure it is correct as this is how we will contact you.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number): This is an optional field for the attendee to provide their phone number. This will be used for contact purposes.
</ul>

### Section 2: Event Information
#### <ul>
* **Event Name** (Event Name): This is the name of the event that the attendee is interested in attending.
* **Event Date** (Event Date): This is the date of the event that the attendee is interested in attending.
* **Event Time** (Event Time): This is the time of the event that the attendee is interested in attending.
* **Event Location or Venue** (Event Location or Venue): This is the location of the event that the attendee is interested in attending.
</ul>

### Section 3: Art Interests
#### <ul>
* **Types of Art Interested In** (Types of Art Interested In): Please select all types of art that interest you the most. 
    + Contemporary art
    + Classical art
    + Photography
    + Sculpture
    + Digital art
    + Installation art
    + Performance art
    + Mixed media
* **Art Experience Level** (Art Experience Level): Please select your level of experience in art.
    + Beginner
    + Casual enthusiast
    + Experienced collector
    + Artist
    + Professional in field
</ul>

### Section 4: Accessibility and Special Needs
#### <ul>
* **Accessibility or Mobility Needs** (Accessibility or Mobility Needs): If you have any accessibility needs, please let us know so we can accommodate you.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (Dietary Restrictions or Allergies): If the event includes refreshments, please let us know if you have any dietary restrictions or allergies.
* **Will you be bringing an assistant or companion** (Will you be bringing an assistant or companion): If yes, please select the number of people who will be attending with you.
</ul>

### Section 5: Preferences
#### <ul>
* **Number of Guests Including You** (Number of Guests Including You): Please select the number of people, including the attendee who is registering, who will be attending.
* **Preferred Communication Method** (Preferred Communication Method): Please select the method you would like to be contacted for further event updates.
    + Email
    + Phone
    + Text message
    + No further contact
</ul>

### Section 6: Additional Information
#### <ul>
* **How Did You Hear About This Event** (How Did You Hear About This Event): Please select how you heard about this event.
    + Social media
    + Website
    + Email newsletter
    + Friend or colleague
    + Venue listing
    + Other
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (Additional Comments or Questions): If you have any further comments or questions, please provide them here.
</ul>
