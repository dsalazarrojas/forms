# Climate Action Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Climate Action Workshop Registration Form is used to register for the Climate Action Workshop, which aims to educate and engage individuals in climate action initiatives. The form collects information about the individual's personal details, workshop preferences, and expectations from the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the personal information section, which includes your first name, last name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select your workshop format preference (in-person, virtual, or hybrid).
3. Indicate your level of experience with climate action (beginner, intermediate, advanced, or expert).
4. Choose your primary areas of interest related to climate action (select all that apply).
5. If you have current climate initiatives in your organization, provide a brief description.
6. Specify your dietary requirements.
7. Mention any accessibility needs you may have.
8. Share what you hope to gain from this workshop.
9. Provide any additional information or requests you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name as it appears on official documents. 
* **Last Name**: Your surname or family name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address**: Your email address where you can be reached for updates and notifications.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number in case we need to reach you.
* **Organization/Institution**: The name of your organization or institution you represent.
* **Job Title**: Your job title within your organization.
* **Workshop Name**: The title of the workshop you wish to attend.
* **Workshop Date and Time**: The date and time of the workshop you wish to attend.
* **Workshop Format**: The format of the workshop you prefer (in-person, virtual, or hybrid).
* **Climate Action Experience Level**: Your level of experience with climate action (beginner, intermediate, advanced, or expert).
* **Primary Areas of Interest**: Select all the primary areas of interest related to climate action (carbon reduction, renewable energy, sustainable agriculture, water conservation, policy and advocacy, corporate sustainability, community action, or other).
* **Current Climate Initiatives**: Briefly describe current climate initiatives in your organization.
* **Dietary Requirements**: Specify any dietary requirements you may have (none, vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, or other).
* **Accessibility Needs**: Mention any accessibility needs you may have.
* **Learning Goals**: Share what you hope to gain from this workshop.
* **Specific Topics**: Provide any specific topics you would like to be covered during the workshop.
* **Additional Information**: Share any additional information or requests you may have.
