# Executive Business Review Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Executive Business Review Program Registration Form is designed to collect information for the registration process of the Executive Business Review Program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your personal details in the corresponding fields.
3. If you are representing a company, provide the company name and job title.
4. Select your nationality from the dropdown menu.
5. Choose your country and state from the respective dropdown menus.
6. Upload a file (optional).
7. Review your details carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Name** (`program_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the program you are registering for.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, optional): Enter your company name (if applicable).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, optional): Enter your department.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, `text`, optional): Enter your time of birth.
* **Nationality** (`nationality`, `text`, optional): Enter your nationality.
* **Passport Number** (`passport_number`, `number`, optional): Enter your passport number.
* **Date of Issue** (`passport_date_of_issue`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of issue.
* **Nationality (Other)** (`nationality_other`, `text`, optional): Enter your nationality (if not listed).
* **Country** (`country`, `select_one`, optional): Select your country.
* **State** (`state`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your state.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter your city.
* **Zip** (`zip`, `number`, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Address** (`address`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your address.
* **Upload File** (`upload_file`, `text`, optional): Upload a file (optional).
* **File Name** (`uploaded_file_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the file name (if uploading).
* **File Size** (`uploaded_file_size`, `text`, optional): Enter the file size (if uploading).
* **File Type** (`uploaded_file_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the file type (if uploading).
* **Date of Upload** (`uploaded_file_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of upload (if uploading).
* **File** (`uploaded_file`, `text`, optional): Upload a file (optional).

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
- If you are uploading a file, ensure that it is in the correct format and size.
- Review your details carefully before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
