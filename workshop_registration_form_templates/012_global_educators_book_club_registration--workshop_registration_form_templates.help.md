# global_educators_book_club_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The global educators book club registration form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in joining the club. This form aims to gather data on personal details, interests, and preferences for meetings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the first name field with your full first name.
2. Enter your last name in the second field.
3. Provide a valid email address in the third field.
4. Indicate your role in the fourth field.
5. List your interests in the fifth field.
6. Specify your availability in the sixth field.
7. Enter your member details in the seventh field.
8. Mention your university in the eighth field.
9. List other interests in the ninth field.
10. Specify other member interests in the tenth field.
11. Provide details about your availability in the eleventh field.
12. Indicate your meeting preferences in the twelfth field.
13. Specify the frequency of meetings in the thirteenth field.
14. Mention the time of meetings in the fourteenth field.
15. Specify the meeting time for members in the fifteenth field.
16. Enter the meeting day in the sixteenth field.
17. Specify the meeting date in the seventeenth field.
18. Enter the meeting location in the eighteenth field.
19. Mention the meeting location for members in the nineteenth field.
20. Provide other meeting details in the twentieth field.
21. Complete the registration form in the twenty-first field.
22. If you are not ready to submit, select the "No Submission" option.
23. If you wish to cancel your registration, select the "Cancel" option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Provide a valid email address.
* **Role** (`role`, text, required/optional): Indicate your role.
* **Interests** (`interests`, text, required/optional): List your interests.
* **Availability** (`availability`, text, required/optional): Specify your availability.
* **Member Details** (`member_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your member details.
* **University** (`university`, text, required/optional): Mention your university.
* **Other Interests** (`interests_other`, text, required/optional): List other interests.
* **Other Member Interests** (`member_interests`, text, required/optional): Specify other member interests.
* **Availability Details** (`availability_details`, text, required/optional): Provide details about your availability.
* **Meeting Preferences** (`meeting_preferences`, text, required/optional): Indicate your meeting preferences.
* **Meeting Frequency** (`meeting_frequency`, text, required/optional): Specify the frequency of meetings.
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, text, required/optional): Mention the time of meetings.
* **Member Meeting Time** (`member_meeting_time`, text, required/optional): Specify the meeting time for members.
* **Meeting Day** (`meeting_day`, text, required/optional): Enter the meeting day.
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, text, required/optional): Specify the meeting date.
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the meeting location.
* **Member Meeting Location** (`member_meeting_location`, text, required/optional): Mention the meeting location for members.
* **Other Meeting Details** (`other_meeting_details`, text, required/optional): Provide other meeting details.
* **Registration Form** (`registration_form`, text, required): Complete this form to register.
* **Member Registration Form** (`member_registration_form`, text, required/optional): Complete this form to register as a member.
* **Submit** (`submit`, note, required/optional): Click to submit your registration.
* **No Submission** (`no_submission`, note, required/optional): Select if you are not ready to submit.
* **Cancel** (`cancel`, note, required/optional): Select to cancel your registration.
