# Data Literacy For Teachers Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from teachers who wish to register for the Data Literacy Program. It helps us understand their interest in the program, gather relevant details, and ensure they are eligible to participate. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure we can contact you and schedule the program according to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select your program signups. Please indicate if you are interested in signing up for the program.
2.  Provide your participant details. Choose whether you have signed up for the program before or not.
3.  Enter your name. If you have signed up before, you can leave this field blank.
4.  Enter your email address. This will be used for communication and follow-up purposes.
5.  Enter your phone number (if applicable). This is optional, but it will help us contact you more easily.
6.  Enter a brief follow-up note (if applicable).
7.  Select the start date of the program you want to begin with.
8.  Select the end date of the program you want to complete.
9.  Enter the name of your training coordinator (if applicable).
10.  Choose whether you are part of the education team or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Signups** (Program Signups, select_multiple, required): Please select 'Yes' if you are interested in signing up for the program.
* **Participant Details** (Participant Details, select_one, required): Please choose 'Yes' if you have signed up for the program before.
* **Name** (Name, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Follow-up** (Follow-up, note, optional): Enter a brief note about your follow-up preferences.
* **Program Start Date** (Program Start Date, date, required): Select the start date of the program you want to begin with.
* **Program End Date** (Program End Date, date, required): Select the end date of the program you want to complete.
* **Training Coordinator** (Training Coordinator, text, optional): Enter the name of your training coordinator.
* **Education Team** (Education Team, select_one, required): Please choose 'Yes' if you are part of the education team.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
- If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to ask.
- We will contact you once you submit this form to discuss your registration further.
- Please check your email for updates and follow-up communications.
