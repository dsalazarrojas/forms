# Workforce Inclusion Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workforce Inclusion Workshop Registration Form is a crucial tool to help us tailor our workshop content and preparation to your needs. By filling out this form, you will be able to participate in a workshop experience that is relevant and meaningful to you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your details in the **Contact Information** field, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
3. Enter your phone number in the **Phone Number** field.
4. Select your desired workshop date and time in the **Date** and **Time** fields.
5. Add any additional comments or notes in the **Note** field.
6. Choose the purpose of your workshop attendance from the **What is the purpose of your workshop attendance?** dropdown menu.
7. Select the languages you speak from the **Which languages do you speak?** dropdown menu.
8. Enter the number of years of experience you have in your field in the **How many years of experience do you have in your field?** field.
9. Enter your annual salary range in the **What is your annual salary range?** field.
10. Add any additional information about yourself in the **Additional Information** text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (Registration Form)**: This is the main form that asks for your basic details.
	+ **Name (Text)**: Your full name as you would like to be addressed.
	+ **Email (Email)**: The email address you want us to contact you with.
	+ **Phone Number (Text)**: Your contact phone number.
* **Contact Information**: This field is the same as the **Contact Information** field above, but you have the option to enter more information here if you'd like.
* **Email**: This is your email address.
* **Phone Number**: This is your contact phone number.
* **Date**: Choose your desired workshop date. Please use the date format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Time**: Choose your desired workshop time. Please use the 24-hour format.
* **Note**: Add any additional comments or notes about your attendance.
* **What is the purpose of your workshop attendance?**: Choose one of the options below.
	+ Option 1: Personal Interest
	+ Option 2: Professional Development
	+ Option 3: Company Requirements
* **Which languages do you speak?**: Choose all the languages you speak.
	+ Option 1: Language 1
	+ Option 2: Language 2
	+ Option 3: Language 3
* **How many years of experience do you have in your field?**: Enter the number of years you have worked in your field.
* **What is your annual salary range?**: Enter the range of your annual salary.
* **Additional Information**: Add any additional information about yourself.
