# Strategic Transformation Lab Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect necessary information from participants to facilitate communication and coordination for the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant details on page 1.
2. Indicate your goals and expectations for the workshop on page 2.
3. Choose your preferences for the workshop, including any dietary or accessibility needs, on page 3.
4. Provide your contact information, including email and phone number, on page 4.
5. Choose a date and time for the workshop that suits you on page 5.
6. Add any additional notes or information about your participation on page 6.
7. Review and confirm your preferences and contact information on page 7.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required: false): Enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
- **Participant Goals** (`goals`, text, required: false): Share your goals and expectations for the workshop. This will help us understand how we can best support you.
- **Participant Preferences** (`participant_preferences`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose your preferences for the workshop, including any dietary or accessibility needs. Please select all that apply.
  - Yes: You have specific requirements or needs that we should be aware of.
  - No: You do not have any specific requirements or needs.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter your contact information, including your email and phone number.
- **Email** (`participant_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
- **Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can reach you.
- **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Choose a date for the workshop that suits you. Please use the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
- **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Choose a time for the workshop that suits you. Please use the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes or information about your participation.
- **Participant Preferences 2** (`participant_preferences_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose your preferences for the workshop, including any further preferences or requirements. Please select all that apply.
  - Yes: You have specific requirements or needs that we should be aware of.
  - No: You do not have any specific requirements or needs.
- **Contact Information 2** (`contact_info_2`, text, required: false): Enter any additional contact information, such as a different email or phone number.
