# Cultural Responsiveness Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cultural Responsiveness Training Registration form is designed to collect necessary information from attendees who are interested in or have already registered for the Cultural Responsiveness Training. This form helps ensure that attendees have the necessary permissions and preferences set for the training session.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select your session preferences**: Choose one or more of the options that best fit your preferences.
2. Fill in your **Attendee Details** (name) and **Email** fields as they are required for registration and communication purposes.
3. Confirm your **Participation Consent** and decide whether you would like to **Submit** your registration.
4. Review your form submission before confirming your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Select your session preferences

*   **Session Preferences**: Select one or more of the options that best fit your preferences. You can choose 'Yes' or 'No' from the options listed.

### 2. Attendee Details (name)

*   **Name**: Please provide your name as it will be used for registration and communication purposes.

### 3. Email

*   **Email**: Please enter your email address as it will be used for registration and communication purposes.

### 4. Phone

*   **Phone Number**: Please enter your phone number as it will be used for registration and communication purposes.

### 5. Confirm your participation consent

*   **Participation Consent**: Confirm your consent to participate in the Cultural Responsiveness Training.

### 6. Submission

*   **Submit**: Review and confirm your form submission to complete the registration process.

Note: Some fields have been removed for brevity and clarity, but their original labels are still used as they are in the provided YAML.
