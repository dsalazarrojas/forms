# Health Data Privacy Seminar Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect relevant information from attendees for the Health Data Privacy Seminar. The collected data will be used to tailor the seminar content and facilitate connections among attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring all required fields are filled.
2. Provide accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Review the form before submitting to ensure it is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form** (`Registration Form`, text, required/optional): This is the main registration form where you can provide your name, organization, and contact details.
* **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name, job title, and department or institution.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, text, required/optional): This field is not relevant for this seminar. You can leave it blank.
* **Selection 1** (`Selection 1`, text, required/optional): Choose one or more from the options provided (if any).
* **Selection 2** (`Selection 2`, text, required/optional): Choose one or more from the options provided (if any).
* **Additional Information** (`Additional Information`, text, required/optional): If you have any special requests or additional information, please enter it here.
* **Final** (`Final`, text, required/optional): This is the final field. Please enter any final comments or questions.
