# Advanced Mastocytosis Workshop Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants who wish to register for the Advanced Mastocytosis Workshop. The form gathers essential details to ensure a smooth and safe workshop experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have all the necessary information required in the form before proceeding.
2.  Start by filling in your details, such as participant name, affiliation, and contact email.
3.  Indicate your dietary requirements if any.
4.  Select your professional role in the field of mastocytosis.
5.  Confirm your payment status.
6.  If necessary, specify any special accommodations you require.
7.  Finally, provide the emergency contact information for yourself or a representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Participant name** (participant_name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID.
- **Affiliation or organization** (affiliation, text, optional): If you are representing an institution or organization, enter the name here.
- **Contact email** (email, email, required): Enter your contact email address for workshop materials and communications.
- **Dietary requirements** (dietary_requirements, text, optional): If you have any food allergies or restrictions, list them here.
- **Professional role** (professional_role, select_one, required): Choose your role in the field of mastocytosis:
  - Physician
  - Nurse
  - Researcher
  - Patient
  - Other
- **Payment status** (payment_status, select_one, required): Confirm your payment status:
  - Paid
  - Invoice me
  - Sponsorship
- **Special accommodations needed** (special_accommodations, text, optional): If you need any special accommodations, such as accessibility or other needs, list them here.
- **Emergency contact name and phone** (emergency_contact, text, required): Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact for safety purposes.
