# Farm To Retail Innovation Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering new and innovative farm-to-retail products or ideas. Please fill it out to showcase your ideas or collaborate with us on a project.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please submit this form to initiate the registration process.
2. Fill out the form completely, including any requested fields.
3. Review your form before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Submit** (Text, Required): Enter your submission.
* **Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your name or the name of your farm.
* **Organization** (Text, Optional): Enter your organization or farm name.
* **Contact Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Product Category** (Text, Optional): Select the category of your product (e.g. Fresh Produce, Processed, etc.).
* **Product Description** (Text, Optional): Briefly describe your product.
* **Innovation Challenge** (Select One, Optional): Select one innovation challenge you face (e.g. Accelerators, Challenges, etc.).
* **Accelerator(s)** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select one or multiple accelerators you have access to.
* **Challenge(s)** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select one or multiple challenges you face.
* **Collaboration(s)** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select one or multiple collaborations you have or are interested in.
* **Program(s)** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select one or multiple programs you have participated in.
* **Product Type** (Select One, Optional): Select the type of your product (e.g. Fresh, Processed, etc.).
* **Product Weight** (Number, Optional): Enter the weight of your product.
* **Product Size** (Select One, Optional): Select the size of your product (e.g. Small, Medium, Large).
* **Farm Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your farm name.
* **Farm Location** (Text, Optional): Enter your farm location.
* **Start Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the start date of your project or innovation.
* **Start Time** (Time, Optional): Enter the start time of your project or innovation.
* **End Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the end date of your project or innovation.
* **End Time** (Time, Optional): Enter the end time of your project or innovation.
* **Duration** (Text, Optional): Briefly describe the duration of your project or innovation.
* **Notes** (Note, Optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Assigned Tool** (Text, Optional): Enter any assigned tool or equipment used in your project or innovation.
* **Form ID** (Text, Optional): This field is for our internal use only.

This guide is intended to help you understand how to fill out this form correctly. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
