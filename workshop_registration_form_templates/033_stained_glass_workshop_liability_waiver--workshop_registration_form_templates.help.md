# Stained Glass Workshop Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose

This is a liability waiver form for participants of a stained glass workshop. It's essential to complete this form before attending the workshop to acknowledge and agree to the terms and conditions of the event.

## How To Complete This Form

* Read the entire form carefully before starting to fill it out.
* Answer all required fields truthfully and accurately.
* Ensure you've checked the "Participant Agreement Confirmed" box to indicate that you've read and understood the workshop terms.
* Sign the "Signed Consent" field with your signature.
* Fill out any additional fields as prompted, such as emergency contact information and workshop details.

* **Participant Details**
	+ (Participant Details): Enter your name, email, phone number, and date of birth.
* **Signed Consent**
	+ (Signed Consent): Sign your name to confirm you've read and understood the workshop terms.

* **Relationship**

* **Phone**
	+ (Phone): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Participant Agreement**
	+ (Participant Agreement): This field is for any additional comments or remarks about your participation in the workshop.
* **Participant Agreement Text**
	+ (Participant Agreement Text): This field is for any additional information about your participation in the workshop.

	+ (Workshop Details): Enter the workshop name, location, start and end dates.

* **Class End Date**
	+ (Class End Date): Enter the end date of the workshop.
* **Instructor Name**
	+ (Instructor Name): Enter the name of the workshop instructor.
