# Cross Functional Management Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for registering participants in the Cross Functional Management Workshop. Please ensure you have all necessary information before filling out this form. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your workshop organizer for clarification. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Provide your email address.
3. Enter your phone number (if available).
4. Add a brief description of your company and address (if applicable).
5. Select the workshop date and time you plan to attend.
6. Choose your workshop location (if applicable).
7. Add any workshop details you'd like to share with the organizers.
8. Select your participant type (e.g., Option A, Option B, Option C, or other).
9. If your participant type is "other", provide a brief description of your participant type.
10. Select your registration status (e.g., option a, option b, option c, or other).
11. If your registration status is "other", provide a brief description of your registration status.
12. Enter the date and time of your workshop registration.
13. Choose your payment method (e.g., option a, option b, option c, or other).
14. If your payment method is "other", provide a brief description of your payment method.
15. Add any workshop attendance details (if applicable).
16. Select your workshop attendance status (e.g., option a, option b, option c, or other).
17. If your workshop attendance status is "other", provide a brief description of your attendance status.
18. Enter the date and time of your planned workshop attendance.
19. Add any workshop attendance notes (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name**: First name of the participant. 
* **last_name**: Last name of the participant. 
* **email**: Email address of the participant.
* **phone**: Phone number of the participant (if available).
* **participant_info**: Brief description of the participant.
* **company_name**: Name of the company the participant belongs to (if applicable).
* **company_address**: Address of the company (if applicable).
* **workshop_date**: Date of the workshop. 
* **workshop_time**: Time of the workshop. 
* **workshop_location**: Location of the workshop (if applicable).
* **workshop_details**: Brief description of the workshop details.
* **participant_type**: Type of participant (e.g., Option A, Option B, Option C, or other).
* **participant_type_other**: Brief description of the participant type (if participant type is "other").
* **registration_status**: Status of the registration (e.g., option a, option b, option c, or other).
* **registration_status_other**: Brief description of the registration status (if registration status is "other").
* **workshop_registration_date**: Date of the workshop registration.
* **workshop_registration_time**: Time of the workshop registration.
* **payment_method**: Method of payment (e.g., option a, option b, option c, or other).
* **payment_method_other**: Brief description of the payment method (if payment method is "other").
* **notes**: Additional notes from the participant.
* **workshop_attendance**: Brief description of the workshop attendance details.
* **workshop_attendance_other**: Brief description of the workshop attendance details (if attendance is "other").
* **workshop_attendance_notes**: Additional notes about workshop attendance.
* **workshop_attendance_status**: Status of the workshop attendance (e.g., option a, option b, option c, or other).
* **workshop_attendance_status_other**: Brief description of the workshop attendance status (if attendance status is "other").
* **workshop_attendance_date**: Date of the workshop attendance.
* **workshop_attendance_time**: Time of the workshop attendance.
* **workshop_attendance_notes_other**: Brief description of the workshop attendance notes (if attendance notes is "other").
