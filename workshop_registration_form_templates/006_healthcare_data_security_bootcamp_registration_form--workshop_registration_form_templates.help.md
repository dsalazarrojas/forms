# Healthcare Data Security Bootcamp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registering participants to the Healthcare Data Security Bootcamp. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by filling out the "Registration Form" on the first page.
2. On the second page, fill out "Full Name", "Email Address", and "Phone" fields as required for registration. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form** (`registration_form`, text, optional): 
  This field is where you start your registration process by providing a brief description of your purpose or intention in attending the bootcamp.
* **Full Name** (`name`, text, required): 
  Please enter your full name as you would like to be registered in the bootcamp.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required):
  This is your contact email address. Please ensure it's valid.
  Please enter a valid email address as you would like it to be associated with your registration. 
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required):
  This is your contact phone number. 

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately to ensure your smooth registration process.
- If you're having trouble with your registration, contact us at your provided email address.
