# Livestock Welfare Workshop Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Livestock Welfare Workshop Recording Consent Form is a document used to obtain informed consent from workshop participants for the recording of their sessions. This form is designed to ensure that participants understand and agree to the recording and use of their session materials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Workshop Participant Info**: Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself, such as your title or position.
2. **Participant Contact**: Provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number (if applicable).
3. **Participant Consent**: Select "Yes" if you agree to the recording and use of your session materials. This includes the recording of your voice, image, and any other materials you may create during the workshop.
4. **Recording Details**: Briefly describe the recording you are requesting to be made, including the purpose, duration, and any specific requirements or restrictions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Participant Info** (`Workshop Participant Info`, text, required/optional)
	+ This field is used to collect your name and any other relevant information about yourself, such as your title or position. Please provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* **Participant Contact** (`Participant Contact`, text, required/optional)
	+ This field is used to gather your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number (if applicable). This information will be used to follow up with you regarding the recording and any other workshop-related matters.
* **Participant Consent** (`Participant Consent`, select one, required/optional)
	+ Select "Yes" to indicate that you agree to the recording and use of your session materials. This includes the recording of your voice, image, and any other materials you may create during the workshop.
* **Recording Details** (`Recording Details`, text, required/optional)
	+ In this field, provide a brief description of the recording you are requesting to be made, including the purpose, duration, and any specific requirements or restrictions. This will help us understand your needs and ensure that the recording is completed according to your specifications.
