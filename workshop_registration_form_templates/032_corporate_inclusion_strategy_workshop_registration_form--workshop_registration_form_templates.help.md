# Corporate Inclusion Strategy Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who want to register for the Corporate Inclusion Strategy Workshop. It's essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first and last name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
4. Choose your role from the dropdown menu.
5. Optionally, select your team size and department if applicable.
6. Enter your organization name.
7. Choose your company size from the dropdown menu.
8. Confirm your email address by re-entering it in the "confirm email" field.
9. Confirm your phone number by re-entering it in the "confirm phone" field.
10. Confirm your role by re-selecting it from the dropdown menu.
11. Confirm your organization name by re-entering it in the "confirm organization" field.
12. Confirm your company size by re-selecting it from the dropdown menu.
13. Optionally, select the workshop date and time if you're registering for a specific workshop.
14. Optionally, enter the workshop location if you're registering for a specific workshop.
15. Enter any additional notes about your registration.
16. Confirm the note by re-entering it in the "confirm note" field.
17. Finally, agree to the terms and conditions by selecting the corresponding option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Choose your role from the dropdown menu.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, number, optional): Optionally, select your team size.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Optionally, enter your department.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required): Enter your organization name.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, required): Choose your company size from the dropdown menu.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, optional): Optionally, select the workshop date.
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, time, optional): Optionally, select the workshop time.
* **Workshop Location** (`workshop_location`, text, optional): Optionally, enter the workshop location.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about your registration.
* **Confirm Email** (`email_confirmation`, email, required): Re-enter your email address for confirmation.
* **Confirm Phone** (`phone_confirmation`, text, required): Re-enter your phone number for confirmation.
* **Confirm Role** (`role_confirmation`, select_one, optional): Re-select your role for confirmation.
* **Confirm Department** (`department_confirmation`, text, optional): Re-enter your department for confirmation.
* **Confirm Organization** (`organization_confirmation`, text, required): Re-enter your organization name for confirmation.
* **Confirm Company Size** (`company_size_confirmation`, select_one, optional): Re-select your company size for confirmation.
* **Confirm Workshop Date** (`workshop_date_confirmation`, date, optional): Re-select the workshop date.
* **Confirm Workshop Time** (`workshop_time_confirmation`, time, optional): Re-select the workshop time.
* **Confirm Workshop Location** (`workshop_location_confirmation`, text, optional): Re-enter the workshop location for confirmation.
* **Confirm Note** (`note_confirmation`, note, optional): Re-enter your note for confirmation.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, select_multiple, required): Select the option to agree to the terms and conditions.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure you've filled in all required fields before submitting.
* If you're registering for a specific workshop, please enter the correct workshop date and time.
