# ประก Healthcare Design Thinking Lab Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for the registration process of the "Healthcare Design Thinking Lab" workshop. It is designed to collect personal and team-related information from attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your First Name, Last Name, and Email address in the respective fields.
2. Provide your Phone number for contact purposes.
3. Choose a Date and Time for your participation in the workshop.
4. If necessary, provide additional Participant Details.
5. Enter your Team Name and Team Size if you are part of a team.
6. Select your role within the team (Team Member, Educator, or Other).
7. Indicate whether you are an Educator, Staff, Student, or Extern.
8. Mark yourself as the Team Lead if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (Text)** (Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (Text)** (Required): Enter your last name.
* **Email (Email)** (Required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone (Text)**: Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date (Date)**: Choose a date for your participation in the workshop.
* **Time (Time)**: Choose a time for your participation in the workshop.
* **Participant Details (Text)**: If necessary, provide additional information about the participant.
* **Team Name (Text)**: Enter your team's name if you are part of a team.
* **Team Size (Number)**: Enter the size of your team.
* **Role (Select One)** (Required): Select your role within the team (Team Member, Educator, or Other).
* **Is Educator (Select Multiple)**: Indicate if you are an educator.
* **Is Member (Select Multiple)**: Indicate if you are a team member.
* **Is Extern (Select Multiple)**: Indicate if you are an external participant.
* **Is Student (Select Multiple)**: Indicate if you are a student.
* **Is Staff (Select Multiple)**: Indicate if you are a staff member.
* **Team Lead (Select Multiple)**: Mark yourself as the team lead if applicable.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out this form accurately and completely.
* If you are part of a team, make sure to include all team members' information.
* Double-check your role and affiliation before submitting the form.
