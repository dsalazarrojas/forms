# Enablement Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Enablement Training Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from attendees for enablement training programs. The form is for internal use only and is intended to help tailor the training content according to attendee preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal and professional details in the Attendee Details section.
2. Select the training programs you are interested in from the Training Programs section.
3. Choose the date and time of the training session from the Dates and Time section.
4. Enter the address or location of the training session from the Venue section.
5. Add any additional comments or notes you would like to share with the training team from the Comments section.
6. Provide your email address and phone number for communication purposes.
7. Select the trainer or facilitator's name and training duration from the Training Programs section.
8. Provide the name and details of your team from the Attendee Team section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`):
	+ Please enter your name, company, job title, department, manager's name, director's name, and team.
* **Training Programs** (`training_programs`):
	+ Select the training programs you are interested in.
* **Dates** (`dates`):
	+ Choose the date and time of the training session.
* **Time** (`time`):
	+ Select the time of the training session.
* **Venue** (`venue`):
	+ Enter the address or location of the training session.
* **Comments** (`comments`):
	+ Add any additional comments or notes you would like to share with the training team.
* **Attendee Email** (`attendee_email`):
	+ Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Attendee Phone** (`attendee_phone`):
	+ Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Training Program** (`training_program`):
	+ Select the training program you are interested in.
* **Training Date** (`training_date`):
	+ Choose the date of the training session.
* **Training Time** (`training_time`):
	+ Select the time of the training session.
* **Training Venue** (`training_venue`):
	+ Enter the address or location of the training session.
* **Trainer Name** (`trainer_name`):
	+ Select the trainer or facilitator's name.
* **Training Duration** (`training_duration`):
	+ Select the duration of the training session.
* **Training Location** (`training_location`):
	+ Enter the address or location of the training session.
* **Training Language** (`training_language`):
	+ Select the language of the training session.
* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`):
	+ Enter your name.
* **Attendee Company** (`attendee_company`):
	+ Enter your company name.
* **Attendee Job Title** (`attendee_job_title`):
	+ Enter your job title.
* **Attendee Department** (`attendee_department`):
	+ Enter your department.
* **Attendee Manager** (`attendee_manager`):
	+ Enter your manager's name.
* **Attendee Director** (`attendee_director`):
	+ Enter your director's name.
* **Attendee Team** (`attendee_team`):
	+ Enter your team details.
* **Enablement Training Registration Form Comments** (`enablement_training_registration_form_comments`):
	+ Add any additional comments or notes you would like to share with the training team.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`):
	+ Select the assigned trainer or facilitator's name.

Note: All fields are optional and are intended to be filled in according to the attendee's preference.
