# livestock_care_conference_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering attendees, speakers, or sponsors for the Livestock Care Conference. 
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field according to the instructions provided.
2. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: A text field to input your name.
* **Email**: A text field to input your email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: A text field to input your contact phone number.
* **Date**: A date field to select the date of your visit or attendance.
* **Time**: A time field to select the time of your visit or attendance.
* **Event Type**: A text field to specify the type of event you're attending.
* **Registration Type**: A select-one field with options to select the type of registration (Livestock Care, Livestock Management, Veterinary Care, Other).
* **Registration Number**: A text field to input any registration number associated with your registration.
* **Comments**: A note field for any additional comments or messages.
* **Confirm**: A select-multiple field to confirm your registration (Yes/No).
* **Additional Comments**: A text field for any additional comments or requests.
* **Special Requests**: A text field to input any special requests.
* **Emergency Contact**: A text field to input the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Phone**: A text field to input the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Email**: A text field to input the email of the emergency contact person.
* **Organization**: A text field to input your organization's name.
* **Role in Organization**: A text field to input your role within your organization.
* **Job Position**: A text field to input your job title or position.
* **Department**: A text field to input your department's name.
* **Address**: A text field to input your organization's or business's address.
* **City**: A text field to input your city or location.
* **State**: A text field to input your state or province.
* **Zip**: A text field to input your zip or postal code.
* **Country**: A text field to input your country of residence.
