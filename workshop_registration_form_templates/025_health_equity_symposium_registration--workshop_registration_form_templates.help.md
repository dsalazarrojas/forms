# Health Equity Symposium Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Equity Symposium Registration form is designed to collect essential information from attendees to create a positive and inclusive experience at the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Details, including any necessary information such as contact preferences and emergency contact information.
2. Select your session interests to help the organizers tailor the event to your needs.
3. Choose your accessibility needs to ensure that the event accommodations can be made accordingly.
4. Specify your session preferences for a more enjoyable experience.
5. Fill in any additional comments or remarks you may have.
6. Provide the date and time of the event, if necessary.
7. Enter any additional information about the event that you would like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Details
* **User Details** (text, User Details, required/optional): Fill in your name, email, and any other necessary contact information to help us get in touch with you.

### Session Interests
* **Session Interests** (select multiple, Session Interests, required/optional): Choose the sessions you are interested in attending to help us plan the event accordingly.

### Accessibility Needs
* **Accessibility Needs** (select one, Accessibility Needs, required): Select any accessibility needs you may have, such as wheelchair accessibility or sign language interpretation.

### Session Preferences
* **Session Preferences** (select multiple, Session Preferences, required): Choose the session preferences, such as morning or afternoon sessions, to ensure a comfortable and enjoyable experience.

### Contact Preferences
* **Contact Preferences** (text, Contact Preferences, optional): Fill in any contact preferences you may have, such as phone or email.

### Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact** (text, Emergency Contact, optional): Enter the contact information of an emergency contact in case of an unexpected situation.

### Contact Preferences 2
* **Contact Preferences 2** (text, Contact Preferences 2, optional): Fill in any additional contact preferences you may have.

### Emergency Contact 2
* **Emergency Contact 2** (text, Emergency Contact 2, optional): Enter the contact information of another emergency contact.

### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (text, Additional Comments, optional): Enter any additional comments or remarks you may have about your participation in the event.

### Date of Event
* **Date of Event** (date, Date of Event, optional): Enter the date of the event if necessary.

### Time of Event
* **Time of Event** (time, Time of Event, optional): Enter the time of the event if necessary.

### Session Details
* **Session Details** (text, Session Details, optional): Enter any additional session details you would like to share.

### Event Location
* **Event Location** (text, Event Location, optional): Enter the location of the event if necessary.

### Additional Info
* **Additional Info** (text, Additional Info, optional): Fill in any additional information you would like to share about the event.

### User Details 2
* **User Details 2** (text, User Details 2, optional): Fill in any additional user details you would like to share.
