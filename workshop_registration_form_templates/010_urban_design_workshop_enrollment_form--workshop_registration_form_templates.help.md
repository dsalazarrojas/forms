# Urban Design Workshop Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Urban Design Workshop Enrollment Form is used to collect enrollment data for the Urban Design Workshop. This form gathers information from workshop attendees, including their contact details, workshop participation status, and assigned tool.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
2. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
3. If you have any issues or concerns, contact the workshop organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you wish to attend the workshop.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time you plan to attend the workshop.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes related to your enrollment.
* **Workshop Name** (`workshop_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the workshop you are enrolling in.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the workshop you are enrolling in.
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the workshop you are enrolling in.
* **Participant Number** (`participant_number`, number, required: false): Enter the number of participants you are enrolling.
* **Enrollment Status** (`enrollment_status`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Select "Active" if you are currently enrolled in the workshop.
	+ Select "Inactive" if you are not currently enrolled in the workshop.
	+ Select "Pending" if your enrollment is still pending.
* **Category** (`category`, text, required: false): Enter any relevant category related to your enrollment.
* **Tags** (`tags`, text, required: false): Add any relevant tags related to your enrollment.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Select "chatjimmy" for chatjimmy tool.
	+ Select "tool1" for tool1.
	+ Select "tool2" for tool2.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact the workshop organizers.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accurate information.
