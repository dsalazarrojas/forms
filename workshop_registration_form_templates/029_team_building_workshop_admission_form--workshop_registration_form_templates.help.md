# Team Building Workshop Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Building Workshop Admission Form is a form designed to collect information from participants who are interested in attending a team building workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Main Information" section with any relevant details about yourself or your team.
2. Provide your "Participant Details" in the next section, including any necessary information about yourself or your team members.
3. If you have any "Special Requirements", please list them in the "Special Requirements" section.
4. Finally, provide your "Contact Information" so we can get in touch with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Main Information
* **Main Information** (`main_information`, `text`, required/optional): This is a text-based field where you can provide any relevant details about yourself or your team.

### Participant Details
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, `text`, **required**): Please provide your name(s) or your team's name(s) and any other necessary information about yourself or your team members.

### Special Requirements
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, `text`, required/optional): If you have any special requirements or requests, please list them here.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, required/optional): Provide your contact information so we can get in touch with you.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure your spot in the workshop is secured.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
* We will review your submission and get back to you as soon as possible.
