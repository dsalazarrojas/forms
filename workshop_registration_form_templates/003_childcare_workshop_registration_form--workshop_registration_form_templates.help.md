# Childcare Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather necessary information for registering a child for a workshop.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully and thoroughly, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. Review each field's purpose and guidelines to ensure accurate and successful registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent or Guardian Name**: Enter the full name of the parent or guardian who will be contacted for registration confirmation and emergency purposes.
* **Child Name**: Enter the first and last name of the child attending the workshop.
* **Child Age**: Enter the child's age in years.
* **Contact Email**: Enter a valid email address for registration confirmation and important workshop updates.
* **Workshop Selection**: Choose the workshop session to be attended by the child (Early Years Playtime, Toddler Social Skills, Creative Storytelling, Parenting Strategies).
* **Preferred Session Date**: Select the date of the chosen workshop session.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of an alternate contact person in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Payment Method**: Choose the method for paying the workshop fee (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, Pay on Arrival).
