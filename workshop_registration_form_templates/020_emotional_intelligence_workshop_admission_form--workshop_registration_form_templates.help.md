# emotional_intelligence_workshop_admission_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect information about an individual's participation in an emotional intelligence workshop. This includes details such as their name, contact email, date of birth, and emergency contact information, as well as status as a participant or instructor. The form also aims to gather information about the workshop itself, including its type, date, time, location, and any relevant notes. By filling out this form, participants can provide essential information to help the workshop organizers understand their needs and tailor the experience accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **participant_name** in the text field.
2. Provide your **contact_email** in the email format (e.g., example@example.com).
3. Enter your **date_of_birth** in the date format (e.g., 01/01/1990).
4. Choose your **workshop_type** from the dropdown menu (e.g., Yes or No).
5. Enter the **workshop_date** in the date format (e.g., 01/01/2024).
6. Choose the **workshop_time** from the dropdown menu (e.g., 09:00).
7. Enter the **workshop_location** in the text field.
8. Select your current status as **participant_status** from the dropdown menu (e.g., Active or Inactive).
9. Enter your **contact_phone** in the text field.
10. Provide the **emergency_contact**'s name in the text field.
11. Describe your relationship with the **emergency_contact** in the text field.
12. Enter the **emergency_phone** in the text field.
13. Add any additional **workshop_notes** that might be helpful for the organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (<participant_name>, text): Enter your name as you want to be referred to in the workshop.
* **Contact Email** (<contact_email>, email): Enter your email address to receive any necessary information or updates.
* **Date of Birth** (<date_of_birth, date>): Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Workshop Type** (<workshop_type, select_one>): Choose whether you are participating as Yes or No.
* **Workshop Date** (<workshop_date, date>): Enter the date of the workshop in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Workshop Time** (<workshop_time, time>): Choose the time of the workshop from the dropdown menu (e.g., 09:00).
* **Workshop Location** (<workshop_location, text>): Enter the location of the workshop.
* **Participant Status** (<participant_status, select_multiple>): Select your current status as Active or Inactive.
* **Contact Phone** (<contact_phone, text>): Enter your phone number for any necessary contact.
* **Emergency Contact** (<emergency_contact, text>): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Relationship** (<emergency_relationship, text>): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone** (<emergency_phone, text>): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Workshop Notes** (<workshop_notes, note>): Add any additional notes that might be helpful for the workshop organizers.
* **Participant Comments** (<participant_comments, text>): Enter any comments you may have about your participation in the workshop.
* **Workshop Status** (<workshop_status, select_multiple>): Select the status of the workshop as Active or Inactive.

Note: This guide is intended to help non-technical users complete the form accurately and efficiently.
