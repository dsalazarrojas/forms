# Community Health Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Health Workshop Registration form is designed for users to register for the workshop. It collects personal and workshop preferences to provide a smooth and organized experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:
1. **Provide your full name**: Enter your legal name in the field labeled "Full name". This is necessary for registration confirmation and certificate purposes.
2. Choose your preferred **session**: Select the workshop session you would like to attend from the "Session selection" field.
3. **Enter your email address**: Provide a valid email address to receive workshop updates and confirmations.
4. **Optionally provide your organization**: If applicable, enter your employer or affiliated organization.
5. **Provide contact information**: Enter your phone number and an emergency contact name and phone number (if applicable).
6. **Consent to be photographed**: Choose whether you consent to be photographed during the event.
7. **Special requests or needs**: If you have any accessibility, dietary, or special requests, enter them in the text field "Special requests".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your legal name.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): If you are registering as an employee or from an affiliated organization, provide the name.
* **Email address** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address to receive workshop updates and confirmations.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Emergency contact name and phone** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): If you have an emergency contact, provide their name and phone number.
* **Session selection** (`session_selection`, select_one, required): Choose the workshop session you prefer to attend.
* **Role in healthcare** (`role_in_healthcare`, select_one, optional): Select your role in healthcare (e.g., Clinician, Community worker, Student, General public).
* **Consent to be photographed** (`consent_photo`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you consent to be photographed during the event.
* **Special requests or needs** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Enter any accessibility, dietary, or special requests you may have.
