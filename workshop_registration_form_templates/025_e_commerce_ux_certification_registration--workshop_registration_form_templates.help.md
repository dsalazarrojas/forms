# E Commerce UX Certification Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The E Commerce UX Certification Registration form is used to collect registration information from individuals interested in taking the e-commerce UX certification course. This form is designed to help the certification authority keep records of registrants and ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "E-mail" field.
3. Choose your experience level in the "Experience level" field.
4. Select your preferred contact method in the "Contact Method" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).
6. Provide your location in the "Location" field.
7. Type any additional notes in the "Notes" field (optional).
8. Click the "Submit" button to submit your registration information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **E Commerce UX Certification Registration**: This is the title of the form, which is also the title of the course you are registering for.
* **Name**: Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed in your registration record.
* **E-mail**: Enter your email address that you would like to use for communication related to the course.
* **Experience level**: Choose your level of experience in e-commerce UX, which is used to determine the level of the course you need.
* **Motivation**: This field is not strictly necessary, but provides additional context about your motivation for taking the course.
* **Contact Method**: Choose how you would like to be contacted, which could be phone, email, or chat.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you have any (optional).
* **Location**: Enter your location, which is used to determine the best way to contact you.
* **Notes**: If you have any additional comments or requests, you can type them here (optional).
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your registration information and complete the process.
