# Organizational Agility Workshop Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to register participants for an organizational agility workshop. It captures information about the participant's interests, preferences, and details to ensure a smooth and effective workshop experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form accurately and completely, providing the necessary information.
2.  For each field, please read the explanations carefully to ensure you understand the purpose of the field.
3.  If you're unsure about any field, please contact the workshop organizers for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Registration** (`workshop_registration`, text, optional): Enter a brief title of the workshop you are registering for.
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about the participant, such as their role in the organization or their specific interests.
* **Participant Interests** (`interests`, select_multiple, required): Select all the interests that apply to you (Agile, Leadership, Transformation, Innovation).
* **Participant Preferences** (`preferences`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred workshop format (Online, In-person, Hybrid).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your registration.
* **How many participants do you want to bring along?** (`workshop_participants`, number, optional): Enter the number of participants you want to bring along to the workshop.
* **What topics are you interested in?** (`workshop_topics`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the topics that interest you (Digital Transformation, Organizational Design, Leadership Development, Communication).
* **Workshop Venue** (`workshop_venue`, select_one, required): Choose the workshop venue (Online, In-person, Hybrid).
* **Workshop Duration** (`workshop_duration`, number, optional): Enter the desired duration of the workshop.
* **Scheduling Preferences** (`workshop_scheduling`, select_multiple, optional): Select your preferred scheduling options (Morning, Afternoon, Full-day).
* **Are you an employee of the organization?** (`participant_employees`, select_one, required): Confirm if you are an employee of the organization.
* **Who will be paying for this workshop?** (`participant_employer`, select_one, required): Confirm who will be paying for this workshop (Self, Organization).
* **Additional Notes** (`participant_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about your registration.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have all the necessary information before submitting the form.
- If you're unsure about any field, please contact the workshop organizers for clarification.
- Please register only once per participant.
