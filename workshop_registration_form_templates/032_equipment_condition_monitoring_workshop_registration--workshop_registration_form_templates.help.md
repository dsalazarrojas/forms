## Equipment Condition Monitoring Workshop Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for workshop registration for the Equipment Condition Monitoring (ECM) workshop. The workshop is intended for equipment operators and condition monitoring specialists to learn about condition monitoring techniques and best practices. The form is meant to be completed by workshop participants to provide necessary information for workshop organization and planning.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the `first_name` field.
2. Fill in your last name in the `last_name` field.
3. Enter your email address in the `email` field.
4. Provide your phone number in the `phone` field.
5. Select the workshop date in the `workshop_date` field.
6. If you have any special requirements, such as dietary restrictions or mobility needs, enter them in the `special_requirements` field.
7. Use the `attendee_details` field to provide any additional details about your attendance.
8. Click the "Submit" button to submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name** (text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **last_name** (text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **email** (email, optional): Enter your email address. This will be used to communicate with you about the workshop.
* **phone** (text, optional): Provide your phone number. This will be used to contact you in case of last-minute changes.
* **workshop_date** (date, optional): Select the date you plan to attend the workshop. Please ensure you select a valid date within the workshop schedule.
* **special_requirements** (text, optional): If you have any special requirements, such as dietary restrictions or mobility needs, enter them here.
* **attendee_details** (note, optional): Use this field to provide any additional details about your attendance.
* **submit** (text, optional): Click this button to submit your registration.
