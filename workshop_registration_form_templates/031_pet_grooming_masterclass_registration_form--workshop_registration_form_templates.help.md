# pet_grooming_masterclass_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pet grooming masterclass registration form is designed for attendees to provide essential details about their session preferences and personal information to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience at the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Provide session details by filling in the "Session Details" field.
2. Enter your attendee details in the "Attendee Info" field.
3. Enter your pet's details in the "Pet Info" field.
4. Select your preferred session from the "Session Preferences" field.
5. Enter your payment details in the "Payment Info" field.
6. If you have any additional information you'd like to share, please use the "Additional Info" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Session Details**: Please enter your desired session details, such as date and time of the session.
- **Attendee Info**: Please provide your personal details, including your name, contact number, and email address.
- **Pet Info**: Please enter your pet's details, including its name, age, breed, and any health conditions it may have.
- **Session Preferences**: Select the session you would like to attend from the available options (Session A, Session B, Session C).
- **Payment Info**: Enter your payment details, including the method and amount.
- **Additional Info**: This field is optional, where you can provide any additional information you'd like to share with us, such as any special requests or preferences.
