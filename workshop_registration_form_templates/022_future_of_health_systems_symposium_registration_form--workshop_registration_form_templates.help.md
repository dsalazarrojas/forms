# Future Of Health Systems Symposium Registration Form - Help Guide

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form effectively, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (indicated as "required" in the form).
2. Double-check your email address for correctness before submitting the form.
3. Select your country and role correctly from the provided options.
4. Choose the sessions you plan to attend.
5. Make sure to submit your form once you've filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your first name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **Email** (`email`, text, required/optional): Enter your official email address where you can be reached for symposium updates and communications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter a phone number where you can be reached for symposium-related matters.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your department or organization.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): Enter your official job title or position within your organization.
* **Institution** (`institution`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your institution or organization.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, text, required/optional): Enter your job function or role within your organization.
* **Country** (`country`, select_one, required/optional): Select your country of residence or work.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required/optional): Choose between "Attendee" or "Speaker" depending on your role in the symposium.
* **Session** (`session`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the sessions you plan to attend during the symposium.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time at which you plan to arrive or participate in the event. Please use a 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date on which you plan to participate in the event.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or remarks regarding your attendance or participation.
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether someone is assigned to handle your registration or not.
* **Submitted by** (`submitted_by`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose who will be submitting the registration on your behalf.

This help guide provides a clear understanding of each field's purpose and expected input. Please review it carefully before submitting your registration to ensure accurate and complete information.
