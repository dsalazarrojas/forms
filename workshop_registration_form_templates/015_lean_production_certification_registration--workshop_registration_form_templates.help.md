# Lean Production Certification Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Lean Production Certification Registration" form is designed to collect participant registration information for Lean production certification training sessions. This information includes participant details, session preferences, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant details section with your name, email address, and other relevant information.
2. Select your preferred session options for the training.
3. Provide any additional notes or comments in the participant details notes field.
4. Choose your preferred training provider.
5. Add any additional notes or comments in the participant notes field.
6. Select your preferred session options for the preferred sessions field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required: false): This field is for entering your name and other relevant participant details.
* **Session Preferences** (`session_preferences`, select_multiple, required: false): Select your preferred session options for the training. You can choose one or more sessions.
* **Experience** (`experience`, text, required: false): This field is for providing any relevant work experience or background information.
* **Notes** (`participant_notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about your participation in the training.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Participant ID** (`participant_id`, number, required: false): Enter your participant ID (if applicable).
* **Training Provider Details** (`training_provider_details`, select_one, required: false): Choose your preferred training provider from the options provided.
* **Participant Details Notes** (`participant_details_notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about your participant details.
* **Preferred Sessions** (`preferred_sessions`, select_multiple, required: false): Select your preferred session options from the options provided.
