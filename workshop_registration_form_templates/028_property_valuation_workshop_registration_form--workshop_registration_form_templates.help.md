# Property Valuation Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Valuation Workshop Registration Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in attending a Property Valuation Workshop. This form helps us gather essential details about the attendee and workshop specifics to ensure a smooth and successful workshop experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your valid email address in the Email field.
3. If you have a phone number, enter it in the Phone field (optional).
4. Fill in the Date and Time of the workshop you would like to attend.
5. Select the correct Workshop Date and Workshop Time from the calendar.
6. Choose the duration of the workshop in the Workshop Duration field.
7. Enter the workshop location in the Workshop Location field.
8. Select the workshop topic from the provided options.
9. Choose the workshop capacity from the options (10, 20, 30, 40, or 50).
10. Add any additional comments in the Workshop Comments field (optional).
11. Fill in the Additional Information field (optional).
12. Read and agree to the Workshop Agreement fields 1-9.
13. Select the Property Type field.
14. Read and agree to the Workshop Agreement 2 field.
15. Fill in the Important Dates field.
16. Read and agree to the Workshop Agreement 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, and 9 fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it (optional).
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Fill in the date of the workshop you would like to attend.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Select the time of the workshop you would like to attend.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, `date`, required): Select the correct Workshop Date from the calendar.
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, `time`, required): Select the correct Workshop Time from the calendar.
* **Workshop Duration** (`workshop_duration`, `number`, required): Choose the duration of the workshop (e.g., 1 hour, 2 hours, etc.).
* **Workshop Location** (`workshop_location`, `text`, required): Enter the workshop location.
* **Workshop Topic** (`workshop_topic`, `text`, required): Select the topic of the workshop from the provided options.
* **Workshop Capacity** (`workshop_capacity`, `select_one`, required): Choose the capacity of the workshop (10, 20, 30, 40, or 50).
* **Workshop Comments** (`workshop_comments`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments about the workshop.
* **Additional Information** (`workshop_additional_info`, `text`, optional): Fill in any additional information about your attendance.
* **Workshop Agreement 1-9** (`workshop_agreement_1-9`, `text`, required): Read and agree to each of the workshop agreements.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, `text`, required): Select the type of property you are interested in.
* **Important Dates** (`workshop_agreement_7`, `date`, required): Fill in the important dates for the workshop.
* **Workshop Agreement 3-9** (`workshop_agreement_3-9`, `text`, required): Read and agree to each of the workshop agreements 3-9.
