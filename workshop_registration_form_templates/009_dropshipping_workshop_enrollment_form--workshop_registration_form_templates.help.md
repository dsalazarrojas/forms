# Dropshipping Workshop Enrollment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Dropshipping Workshop Enrollment Form" is designed for registration to a workshop related to dropshipping, a training or educational event. This form will help us gather the necessary information from you to provide a better experience and ensure you can join the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address to receive important updates and information about the workshop.
3. Provide your Company name (if applicable).
4. Enter your Phone Number for contact purposes.
5. Enter your Street Address and City where you can be reached.
6. Choose your State of residence or origin from the dropdown menu.
7. Enter your Zip Code for further contact and logistical purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for updates and important information about the workshop.
- **Company (Optional)**: If you represent a company, please enter your company name for identification purposes.
- **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further contact.
- **Email Address**: Enter the email address where you can be reached for workshop updates and information.
- **Street Address**: Enter your street address where you can be reached.
- **City**: Enter the city where you can be reached.
- **State**: Choose your state of residence or origin from the dropdown menu.
- **Zip Code**: Enter your zip code for further contact and logistical purposes.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your registration is complete.
- If you have any issues or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us for assistance.
- We will use your information to provide a better experience for the workshop.
