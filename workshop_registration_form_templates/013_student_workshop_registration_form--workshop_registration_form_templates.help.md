# Student Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Workshop Registration Form is a simple registration form designed to collect relevant information from students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and other contact details in the `student_details` field.
2. Provide your contact information in the `contact_details` field.
3. Enter the workshop details you are interested in in the `workshop_details` field.
4. Give your consent to the terms and conditions of the workshop in the `consent` field.
5. You may be asked to provide additional questions in the `additional_questions` field.
6. If required, you may need to upload a file in the `file_upload` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Details** (`student_details`): Enter your name and other relevant details about yourself as a student.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`): Provide your contact information, including your address, phone number, and email.
* **Workshop Details** (`workshop_details`): Enter the specific workshop you are interested in, including the date and time you would like to attend.
* **Consent** (`consent`): Check the box to indicate that you agree to the terms and conditions of the workshop.
* **Additional Questions** (`additional_questions`): Answer any additional questions that may be asked by the workshop organizers.
* **File Upload** (`file_upload`): If required, upload a file to support your registration, such as a resume or a letter of introduction.
