# Lightsaber Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect workshop registration information from users who are interested in attending a lightsaber workshop. It will help the workshop organizers gather relevant details from attendees, including their name, contact information, and session preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Choose your session date and time in the "Session Date" and "Session Time" fields respectively.
5. Select your preferred session time (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening) in the "Session Preferences" field.
6. If you have any specific session length requirements, enter the desired length in the "Session Length" field.
7. Choose the session speaker you would like to attend (John Doe, Jane Doe, or Jim Doe) in the "Session Speaker" field.
8. If you would like to register on a specific date, enter it in the "Registration Date" field.
9. If you have any additional notes about your registration, you can enter them in the "Notes" field.
10. Enter the category and tags relevant to your workshop in the "Category" and "Tags" fields.
11. Provide a brief description of yourself or your session in the "Description" field.
12. Choose the session topic (Lightsaber, Lightsaber Combat, or Lightsaber Dueling) that interests you in the "Session Topic" field.
13. Enter the location of the session in the "Session Location" field.
14. Select the status of your session (Pending, Confirmed, or Cancelled) in the "Session Status" field.
15. Enter the capacity of the session in the "Session Capacity" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the workshop materials and communication.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for workshop communication and updates.
* **Phone**: Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Session Date**: Choose the date you would like to attend the workshop.
* **Session Time**: Select the time you would like to attend the workshop.
* **Session Preferences**: Choose your preferred session time (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
* **Session Length**: If you have a specific session length requirement, enter it here.
* **Session Speaker**: Select the speaker you would like to attend (John Doe, Jane Doe, or Jim Doe).
* **Registration Date**: If you would like to register on a specific date, enter it here.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about your registration.
* **Category**: Enter the category relevant to your workshop.
* **Tags**: Enter the tags relevant to your workshop.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of yourself or your session.
* **Session Topic**: Choose the session topic that interests you (Lightsaber, Lightsaber Combat, or Lightsaber Dueling).
* **Session Location**: Enter the location of the session.
* **Session Status**: Select the status of your session (Pending, Confirmed, or Cancelled).
* **Session Capacity**: Enter the capacity of the session.

Note: This guide is designed to be user-friendly and clear, with each field explained in detail to help users understand what to expect from the form.
