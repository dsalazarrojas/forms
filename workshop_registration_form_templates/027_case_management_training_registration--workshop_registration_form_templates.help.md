# Case Management Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for case management training programs. It collects essential information from potential participants, including contact details, professional background, training preferences, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read through each section carefully and complete all required fields.
- Fill in your personal and professional details accurately.
- Select your preferred training format and course.
- Choose your payment method.
- Confirm your terms and conditions acceptance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`): Enter the date you plan to sign up for the training.
* **Full Name** (`participant_name`): Enter your full legal name.
* **Professional Title** (`professional_title`): Enter your job title or professional position.
* **Organization or Agency** (`organization`): Enter your current organization or agency name.
* **Organization Type** (`organization_type`): Choose the category of your organization.
* **Business Address** (`address`): Enter your work or office address.
* **City** (`city`): Enter your city name.
* **State or Province** (`state`): Enter your state or province.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (`zip_code`): Enter your postal code or zip code.
* **Email Address** (`email`): Enter a professional email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`): Enter your best contact phone number.
* **Professional Background** (`professional_background`): Briefly describe your professional background and experience.
* **Highest Education Level** (`highest_education`): Select your highest completed education level.
* **Field of Study** (`field_of_study`): Enter your major or area of concentration.
* **Professional Certifications** (`professional_certifications`): Select any relevant professional licenses or credentials.
* **Years of Case Management Experience** (`years_experience`): Choose how many years of case management experience you have.
* **Current Role Description** (`current_role`): Briefly describe your current job responsibilities.
* **Population Served** (`population_served`): Select the types of clients you currently serve.
* **Training Selection** (`training_selection`): Select the training programs you are interested in.
* **Type of Training** (`training_type`): Select the type of training you prefer.
* **Preferred Training Format** (`training_format`): Choose your preferred training delivery method.
* **Preferred Training Date** (`preferred_date`): Enter your preferred training date.
* **Alternate Date** (`alternate_date`): Enter a secondary preferred training date (if applicable).
* **Primary Learning Objectives** (`learning_objectives`): Describe the key takeaways you hope to gain from the training.
* **Special Accommodations Needed** (`special_needs`): If necessary, specify any accessibility requirements.
* **Dietary Requirements** (`dietary_requirements`): If meals will be provided, indicate any dietary restrictions.
* **Continuing Education Requirements** (`ceu_requirements`): Specify any CEU requirements for your profession (if applicable).
* **Do You Need Continuing Education Units?** (`ceu_needed`): Confirm if you require CEUs for the training.
* **Type of CEU Credit Needed** (`ceu_type`): Select the certification body requiring CEUs.
* **License Number** (`license_number`): Enter a relevant professional license number (if applicable).
* **License State** (`license_state`): Enter the state that issued your license (if applicable).
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`): You will be prompted to enter payment information if applicable.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select the payment method for your registration.
* **Are You Applying for a Scholarship?** (`scholarship_needed`): Indicate if you are applying for a scholarship.
* **Are Multiple People Registering?** (`group_discount`): If registering with others, indicate the number of people in your group.
* **How Did You Hear About This Training?** (`how_heard`): Select how you discovered the training.
* **Have You Attended Our Training Before?** (`previous_training`): Confirm if you have attended our training previously.
* **I Accept Terms and Conditions** (`terms_accepted`): Confirm agreement to the training terms and conditions.
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (`additional_comments`): If you have any other information, enter it here.
