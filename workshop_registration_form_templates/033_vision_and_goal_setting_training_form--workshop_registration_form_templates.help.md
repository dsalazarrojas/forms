# vision_and_goal_setting_training_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help participants register for a workshop and set their learning and workshop goals. It will collect essential information about the participant and the workshop to ensure a smooth and productive experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your participant information in the "Participant Info" section.
2. Enter details about the workshop in the "Workshop Details" section.
3. Specify your learning goals in the "Learning Goals" section.
4. (Optional) Describe your workshop goals in the "Workshop Goals" section.
5. (Optional) Add any additional comments or evaluations in the "Evaluation" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, `text`, required/optional): Provide your name, title, or any other identifier as a participant in the workshop.
* **Workshop Details** (`workshop_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the workshop, its description, or any other relevant information about the event.
* **Learning Goals** (`learning_goals`, `text`, required/true): Specify your learning objectives or what you aim to achieve from the workshop.
* **Workshop Goals** (`workshop_goals`, `text`, optional/optional): If applicable, describe your expectations from the workshop, what you want to accomplish, or what you hope to achieve.
* **Evaluation** (`evaluation`, `text`, optional/optional): Add any additional comments or evaluations about your experience at the workshop.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and clear information to help the workshop organizers and facilitators tailor the event to your needs.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please mention them in the "Workshop Goals" section.
* Be specific and concrete when describing your learning and workshop goals. This will help you get the most out of the workshop and track your progress.
