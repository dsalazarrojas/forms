# Multicultural Classroom Development Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather user information for the Multicultural Classroom Development program. The program aims to create a more inclusive and diverse learning environment by bringing together educators, experts, and community members from various backgrounds. The form will be used to register participants for the program, collecting essential details about each user. This form is intended to be used for the Multicultural Classroom Development initiative. If you're unsure whether you need to fill this form, please refer to your local program coordinator or project lead for clarification.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely, but you're not required to do so. You can save your progress and continue later.
2. Enter your contact information, including your email address in the "Email" field and your name in the "Name" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field, if you'd like to be reached.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. User Details (Page 1)

* **User Details** (`user_details`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of yourself, including your background, interests, or any relevant experience you'd like to share. This will help us get to know you better and tailor the program to your needs.

### 2. Email (Page 2)

* **Email** (`contact_email`, text, optional): Enter your email address where you'd like to be contacted for updates and information about the program. If you're already registered, please make sure it's accurate.

### 3. Name (Page 3)

* **Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on official documents. Please ensure it matches the name on your email address, if applicable.

### 4. Phone (Page 4)

* **Phone** (`contact_number`, text, optional): If you'd like to be reached by phone, please enter your phone number.
