# Organizational Trust Building Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from participants who are interested in joining the Organizational Trust Building Program. By filling out this form, you will be able to register your interest and provide details about your expectations and participant requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Participant Details section, which includes information about yourself as a participant.
2. In the Expectations section, describe your expectations from the program.
3. In the Scheduling Preferences section, select your preferred time slot for the program.
4. If you have any specific participant requirements, you can include them in the Participant Requirements section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Details**: This section is where you provide information about yourself as a participant. Please fill out this field with your name, title, and any other relevant details.
- **Expectations**: Here, you can describe your expectations from the program. Be specific about what you hope to achieve or learn from the program.
- **Scheduling Preferences**: Select your preferred time slot for the program from the options provided:
  * 9am - 1pm prophets
  * 9:30am - 12:30pm
  * 1pm - 3pm
- **Participant Requirements**: If you have any specific requirements or needs as a participant, please describe them here. This could include any accommodations or equipment you may need to participate in the program.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your registration is complete.
* Be clear and concise in your answers to ensure that the organizers can accommodate your needs.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to contact us for assistance.
