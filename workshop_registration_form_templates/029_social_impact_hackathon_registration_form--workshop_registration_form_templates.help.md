# Social Impact Hackathon Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from participants about their registration for the Social Impact Hackathon. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure a smooth and successful registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with your personal and professional details. If you're unsure about any field, please refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your official email address associated with your organization.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your work phone number for contact purposes.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the name of your department within your organization.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your official job title within your organization.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Choose your role at the hackathon (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Role Options** (`role_options`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable roles that you'd like to participate in (e.g., "CEO", "Founder", "Manager").
* **Prophets** (`prophets`, date, optional): Please select the proposed date for your participation (if applicable).
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the preferred time for your participation (if applicable).
* **Note** (`note`, note, required): Leave any additional notes or comments for the organizers.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately.
* Double-check your contact information for correctness.
* Select all applicable roles for participation in the Role Options field.
* If you're unsure about any field, please refer to the field-by-field explanation above.
