# kundalini_activation_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from participants who have expressed interest in attending a kundalini yoga workshop. The form should only be used for this purpose and should not be used for any other reason.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the participant details section with your accurate and complete information.
2. Select your consent to participate in the workshop.
3. Enter the session details, including date and time.
4. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.
5. Fill out the remaining fields with your accurate and complete information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_details** (`text`, required: false): This is the participant's details section. Enter your accurate and complete information, including your name, gender, date of birth, address, city, state, and zip code.
* **session_details** (`text`, required: false): Enter the details of the workshop session you are interested in attending. Be accurate and complete with the date and time.
* **kundalini_activation_details** (`text`, required: false): Enter any additional information about your kundalini activation experience.
* **participant_consent** (`select_one`, required: false): Select either "I consent to the kundalini activation" or "I do not consent to the kundalini activation". If you are unsure, you can consult with our support team.
* **session_date** (`date`, required: false): Enter the date of the session you are interested in attending. Be accurate and complete.
* **session_time** (`time`, required: false): Enter the time of the session you are interested in attending. Be accurate and complete.
* **participant_email** (`email`, required: false): Enter your email address. This will be used to contact you with further information about the workshop.
* **participant_phone** (`text`, required: false): Enter your phone number. This will be used to contact you with further information about the workshop.
* **session_schedule** (`text`, required: false): Enter any additional information about the session schedule you are interested in.
* **participant_name** (`text`, required: false): Enter your name.
* **participant_gender** (`text`, required: false): Enter your gender.
* **participant_dob** (`date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth.
* **participant_address** (`text`, required: false): Enter your address.
* **participant_city** (`text`, required: false): Enter your city.
* **participant_state** (`text`, required: false): Enter your state.
* **participant_zip** (`text`, required: false): Enter your zip code.
* **participant_country** (`text`, required: false): Enter your country.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all information is accurate and complete.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact our support team.
* This form should only be used for kundalini yoga workshop registration.
* Do not use this form for any other purpose.
