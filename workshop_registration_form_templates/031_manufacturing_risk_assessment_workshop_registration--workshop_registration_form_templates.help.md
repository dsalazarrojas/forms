# Manufacturing Risk Assessment Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from workshop attendees to help us better understand their needs and preferences for the Manufacturing Risk Assessment Workshop. Please fill out this form to register for the workshop and provide us with your details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your session choices by choosing one or more from the list.
2. Provide your attendee details, including your name and email address.
3. Enter your phone number (optional).
4. Choose the session date for the workshop.
5. Indicate whether you will be attending workshop sessions.
6. Provide a risk assessment and course materials details.
7. Mention any equipment you might need during the workshop.
8. If applicable, provide session choice details.
9. Specify the workshop location (optional).
10. Choose the workshop time (optional).
11. Mention the workshop duration (optional).
12. Indicate the capacity of the workshop (required for organizers).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Session choices** (Select one or more): This section allows you to choose from available sessions for the workshop. Select the sessions that interest you the most.
- **Name** (Text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your business card or identification.
- **Email** (Email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you with workshop updates and information.
- **Phone** (Text, optional): Provide your phone number in case we need to reach you.
- **Session date** (Date, required): Choose the session date for the workshop you plan to attend.
- **Workshop sessions** (Select one, required): Indicate whether you will be attending workshop sessions.
- **Risk Assessment** (Text, required): Please provide your risk assessment details.
- **Course materials** (Text, required): Describe any course materials you think you might need during the workshop.
- **Equipment** (Text, optional): If you have any equipment requirements, please list them here.
- **Other** (Text, optional): If you have any other specific needs or requests, please mention them here.
- **Session choice details** (Text, optional): If you have specific session choice details, please provide them in this field.
- **Workshop location** (Text, optional): Specify the workshop location, if different from the default location.
- **Workshop time** (Time, optional): Choose the workshop time that suits you best.
- **Workshop duration** (Number, optional): Indicate the expected duration of the workshop.
- **Capacity** (Number, required): Please specify the capacity of the workshop to help us plan accordingly.
