# Women's Winter Study Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Women's Winter Study Registration form is designed to collect information from external participants for a Women's Winter Study session. It is intended to gather participant preferences and demographics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, paying attention to the required fields.
2. For each field, select the most relevant option that reflects your true preferences and demographics.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Preferences** (<label>Session Preferences</label>): Select all of the session preferences that apply to you.
* **Participant Data** (<label>Participant Data</label>): Choose your participant data from the dropdown menu.
* **Study Type** (<label>Study Type</label>): Select the type of study that best fits your preferences.
* **Participant Name** (<label>Participant Name</label>): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email** (<label>Email</label>): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (<label>Phone</label>): Enter your phone number.
* **Study Session** (<label>Study Session</label>): Select the date you plan to attend the study session.
* **Session Time** (<label>Session Time</label>): Select the time you plan to attend the study session.
* **Participant Comments** (<label>Participant Comments</label>): Enter any additional comments or preferences you'd like to share with us.
* **Participant Diet** (<label>Participant Diet</label>): Select your dietary preferences.
* **Participant Abilities** (<label>Participant Abilities</label>): Enter any additional information about your abilities or needs that we should be aware of.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form.
* Make sure to select the correct options for each field.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact us for clarification.
* This form should only be filled out by external participants, not internal staff members.
