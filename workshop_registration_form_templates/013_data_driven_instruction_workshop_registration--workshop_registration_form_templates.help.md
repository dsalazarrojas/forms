# Data Driven Instruction Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants to a workshop or training program. It gathers essential information about each participant, including their school name, contact person, email, phone number, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the school name where the participant is from.
2. Provide the contact person's name and email address.
3. Enter the participant's phone number.
4. Enter the participant's date of birth (Date).
5. Enter the participant's time of registration (Time).
6. Select the workshop date and time.
7. Enter any additional participant details in the text fields.
8. Choose whether to schedule the workshop (Yes/No).
9. Select the management option for registration (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
10. Choose whether to schedule the workshop dates (Yes/No).
11. Enter any notes or comments about the participant.
12. Select the participant's ID status (Yes/No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the school where the participant is from.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the participant's date of birth.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the participant's time of registration.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, required): Select the workshop date.
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, time, required): Select the workshop time.
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, note, required): Enter any additional details about the participant.
* **Schedule Workshop** (`schedule_workshop`, select_multiple, required): Choose whether to schedule the workshop (Yes/No).
* **Manage Registration** (`manage_registration`, select_one, required): Select the management option for the workshop (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Schedule Workshop Dates** (`schedule_workshop_dates`, select_multiple, required): Choose whether to schedule the workshop dates (Yes/No).
* **Participant Details 1-10** (`participant_details_1-10`, text, required): Enter any additional details about the participant.
* **Workshop Location** (`workshop_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the workshop.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Enter any additional notes or comments about the participant.
* **Participant ID** (`participant_id`, select_one, required): Select the participant's ID status (Yes/No).
