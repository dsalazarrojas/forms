# linkedin_workshop_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from workshop attendees for a workshop registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the attendee's details in the **Attendee Details** section.
2. Provide information about the workshop you are registering for in the **Workshop Details** section.
3. Enter your contact details in the **Contact Details** section.
4. If required, fill in your payment information in the **Payment Details** section.
5. Confirm your attendance in the **Confirm Attendance** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Details (<name>)**: Enter your name and any other relevant details that you want to share with the workshop organizers.
* **Workshop Details (<name>)**: Enter the title or name of the workshop you are registering for.
* **Session Details (<name>)**: Enter the session number, date, or other relevant information about the workshop session you are registering for.
* **Contact Details (<name>)**: Enter your contact information, including email address and phone number.
* **Payment Details (<name>)**: Enter your payment information, including the payment method and amount (if applicable).
* **Confirm Attendance (<name>)**: Confirm that you are attending the workshop by selecting "Yes, I will attend" or "No, I won't attend".

Note: The form does not have any conditional logic, so all fields are optional.
