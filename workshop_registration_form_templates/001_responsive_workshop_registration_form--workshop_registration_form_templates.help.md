# Responsive Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect registration information from workshop attendees, including basic details and scheduling preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact information in the "Registration Information" section.
2. Provide your basic details in the "Basic Information" section.
3. Choose your preferred scheduling options in the "Scheduling" section.
4. If you have more scheduling information to share, enter it in the "More Scheduling Information" section.
5. If necessary, enter additional information in the "Additional Information" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Registration Information
* **Registration Information (text, optional)**: Enter your name and contact details, such as phone number and email address, so we can get in touch with you about workshop updates.

### Basic Information
* **Basic Information (text, optional)**: Provide any other relevant details about yourself that you feel is necessary for the workshop organizers to know.

### Scheduling
* **Scheduling (text, optional)**: Choose your preferred workshop time slot from the available options.

### More Scheduling Information
* **More Scheduling Information (text, optional)**: If you have any further scheduling preferences or requirements, enter them here.

### Additional Information
* **Additional Information (text, optional)**: If you have any other information that you feel is relevant to share with the workshop organizers, enter it here.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled in to avoid any issues with your registration.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us directly.
