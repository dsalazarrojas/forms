# Textile Training Course Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Textile Training Course Registration form is a simple, multi-page form designed to collect essential information from participants to enroll in the course. This form helps us understand the participant's course enrollment status and gather any additional details they may need to know before attending the course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On the first page, select either "Yes" or "No" to indicate your enrollment status for the course.
2. On the second page, provide any additional details about yourself as a participant.
3. On the third page, confirm that you have read and understand the consent to enroll in the course.
4. On the final page, if you have any additional information that may be relevant to your participation, please provide it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Enrollment** (`Yes/No`, optional): This question is to confirm your enrollment status for the course.
	+ If you have already enrolled, select "Yes" to move forward with the form.
	+ If you haven't enrolled yet, select "No" and you will be asked to provide your contact information on the next page.
* **Participant Details** (Text, optional): This is a text field where you can provide any additional details about yourself as a participant.
	+ You can include your name, job title, or any other relevant information that may help us get to know you better.
* **Consent** (Text, optional): This field is to confirm that you have read and understood the terms and conditions of the course.
	+ Please read the provided text carefully and type "Yes, I have read and understand the terms and conditions of the course" to confirm your consent.
* **Additional Info** (Text, optional): This field is for any additional information that may be relevant to your participation in the course.
	+ You can use this space to share any questions, concerns, or information that you feel is important to your participation.

## Tips
- Please be accurate and truthful when answering the form questions.
- If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
- We appreciate your participation and look forward to seeing you at the course!
