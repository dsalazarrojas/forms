# material_innovation_workshop_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The material innovation workshop registration form is a tool for workshop organizers to collect relevant information from attendees. By completing this form, you will help the workshop team understand your registration details and interests, ensuring a smooth and engaging experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the registration details section, providing your name and any other relevant contact information.
2. Select your personal details, such as name, email address, and phone number.
3. Choose your topic interests from the list provided.
4. If you have any special needs, please select them from the list.
5. Confirm your email address by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
6. Provide any additional information you feel is necessary for the workshop team to know.
7. Review the terms and conditions, acknowledging that you have read and understood them.
8. Confirm that you have read and understood the terms and conditions by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Details** (`1` , `text`, required): This section is where you provide your registration details, including your name and any other contact information.
* **Personal Details** (`2` , `text`, optional): This section is optional and allows you to provide any personal details you feel are relevant.
* **Topic Interests** (`3` , `select_multiple`, optional): This section allows you to choose which topics you are interested in for the workshop. Select as many as apply to you.
* **Special Needs** (`4` , `select_multiple`, optional): If you have any special needs, such as wheelchair accessibility or hearing impairment, please select them from the list.
* **Confirm** (`5` , `select_one`, optional): Please confirm that you have read and understood the terms and conditions.
* **Additional Info** (`6` , `text`, optional): This section is for any additional information you feel is necessary for the workshop team to know.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`7` , `note`, optional): This section outlines the guidelines for the workshop. Please review and acknowledge that you have read and understood them.
* **Confirm Email** (`8` , `select_one`, optional): Please confirm that your email address is accurate.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Be as specific as possible when selecting your topic interests.
* If you have any special needs, please let the workshop team know so they can provide necessary accommodations.
* Review the terms and conditions carefully before confirming that you have read and understood them.
* Double-check your email address to ensure it is accurate.
