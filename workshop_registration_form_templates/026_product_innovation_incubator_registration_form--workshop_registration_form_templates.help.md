# Product Innovation Incubator Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Innovation Incubator Registration Form is used to collect information from potential applicants to the Product Innovation Incubator program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your **Form Title** in the **Form Title** field. This field is not required but it's a good idea to have a descriptive title for your application.
2. Enter your name in the **Founder Name** field. This field is not required but it's a good idea to be honest about who you are.
3. Add your email address in the **Founder Email** field. This is a required field to ensure we can contact you about your application.
4. Provide a brief description of your idea in the **Idea Description** field. This field is not required, but it will help us understand your idea better.
5. Let us know if your idea is still in the development stage by selecting the corresponding option in the **Idea Stage** field. This field is not required, but it will help us evaluate your idea.
6. Choose one or more relevant options from the **Industry Sector** field. This field is not required, but it will help us understand your idea's context.
7. Indicate the number of team members you have for your idea in the **Team Members** field. This field is not required, but it's a good idea to be honest about your team size.
8. If your team is located in the same location as the program, select **Yes** in the **Team Location** field. This field is not required, but it will help us evaluate your idea.
9. If your idea is active, select **Active** in the **Idea Status** field. This field is not required, but it will help us understand your idea's status.
10. If your idea targets a specific market, select **Yes** in the **Target Market** field. This field is not required, but it will help us evaluate your idea.
11. If your product is in a specific stage, select **Yes** in the **Product Stage** field. This field is not required, but it will help us understand your product's stage.
12. Choose the type of program you are applying for in the **Program Type** field. This field is not required, but it will help us understand the type of program you are applying for.
13. Specify how many months you are looking to stay in the program in the **Program Length** field. This field is not required, but it will help us plan the program duration.
14. Enter the date you are submitting your application in the **Application Date** field. This field is not required, but it will help us track the application timeline.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): A brief title for your application.
* **Founder Name** (`founder_name`, text, optional): The name of the founder or applicant.
* **Founder Email** (`founder_email`, email, required): A valid email address that we can use to contact you about your application.
* **Idea Description** (`idea_description`, text, optional): A brief description of your idea.
* **Idea Stage** (`idea_stage`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more options that describe the stage of your idea.
* **Industry Sector** (`industry_sector`, select_one, optional): Choose a relevant industry sector for your idea.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, number, optional): The number of team members you have for your idea.
* **Team Location** (`team_location`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if your team is located in the same location as the program.
* **Idea Status** (`idea_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the status of your idea.
* **Target Market** (`target_market`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if your idea targets a specific market.
* **Product Stage** (`product_stage`, select_multiple, optional): Select the stage of your product.
* **Program Type** (`program_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of program you are applying for.
* **Program Length** (`program_length`, number, optional): Specify how many months you are looking to stay in the program.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, optional): The date you are submitting your application.
