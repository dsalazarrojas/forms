# Senior Safety Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for a senior safety workshop. Please ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields marked as "required" in each page.
2. Ensure that the email address provided is valid and in the correct format (e.g., username@domain.com).
3. Enter your phone number in the correct format (e.g., 123-456-7890).
4. Enter your date of birth in the correct format (DD-MM-YYYY).
5. Select the workshop date you are attending from the available options.
6. Select the time slot you prefer for the workshop.
7. Enter the workshop location.
8. Fill out any additional notes about your participant experience or needs.
9. Enter your name in the correct format (e.g., First Last).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Registration Form**: 
  A text field to provide additional information about your registration.
- **Participant Details**: 
  A text field to provide additional information about the participant.
- **Workshop Details**: 
  A text field to provide additional information about the workshop.
- **Participant Notes**: 
  A text field for any additional comments or notes about the participant.
- **Email**: 
  A field to provide your email address. Please ensure it is valid and in the correct format.
- **Participant Phone**: 
  A text field to provide your phone number. Please enter it in the correct format.
- **Date of Birth**: 
  A field to provide your date of birth. Please enter it in the correct format (DD-MM-YYYY).
- **Workshop Dates**: 
  A field to select the date you are attending the workshop.
- **Workshop Time**: 
  A field to select the time slot you prefer for the workshop.
- **Workshop Location**: 
  A text field to provide the location of the workshop.
- **Participant Name**: 
  A text field to provide your name. Please enter it in the correct format (e.g., First Last).
