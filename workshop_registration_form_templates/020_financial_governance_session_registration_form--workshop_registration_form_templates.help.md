# Financial Governance Session Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for attendees to provide their details and information for a financial governance session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name and other relevant details in the first page of the form.
2. Provide your details in the "Registration Form" section.
3. Enter the session details in the "Session Details" section.
4. If you have any additional information to share, you can do so in the "Additional Information" section.
5. Finally, enter a brief description of yourself in the "Attendee Bio" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Details**: This page is where you enter your name and other relevant details.
  * This page is the first step in the form and requires basic information such as your name.
* **Registration Form**: This section is where you provide your details for the session.
  * Please enter your details accurately and completely.
* **Session Details**: This section is where you enter the session details.
  * Please provide the details of the session you are registering for.
* **Additional Information**: This section is where you can provide any additional information you want to share with the session organizers.
  * This is an optional section, but it helps the organizers understand your needs and preferences.
* **Attendee Bio**: This section is where you enter a brief description of yourself.
  * Please enter a brief description of your background and experience.
