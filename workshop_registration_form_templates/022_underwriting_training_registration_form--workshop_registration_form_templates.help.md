The "underwriting_training_registration_form" is a form designed to register participants for the Underwriting and Training Program. This form collects the necessary information to identify the participant, their training details, and their preferred training topics.

Before completing this form, please ensure that you are registering for a specific training session and that you have not already registered for this training. To complete this form, please follow the instructions below.

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register participants for the Underwriting and Training Program. It is used to collect information on the participant's training details, such as their name, email, phone number, and training date and time. The form also includes fields for selecting the preferred training topics and submitting the completed form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your training team in the "Training Team" field.
2. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Select the training date for which you are registering in the "Training Date" field.
6. Select the training time for which you are registering in the "Training Time" field.
7. Enter the training location in the "Training Location" field.
8. Enter the name of the organization you represent in the "Organization" field.
9. Select the role you are registering for in the "Role" field.
10. If you have any comments, you can enter them in the "Comments" field.
11. Select the training topic(s) that interests you in the "Training Topic" field.
11. Select the topic(s) for which you want to receive training in the "Submit" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Training Team**: This field is used to identify the training team you will be joining. Please enter your training team name accurately.
* **Name**: This field is used to collect your name. Please enter your full name.
* **Email**: This field is used to collect your email address. Please enter a valid email address.
* **Phone Number**: This field is used to collect your phone number. Please enter a valid phone number.
* **Training Date**: This field is used to select the date of the training session. Please select the date for which you are registering.
* **Training Time**: This field is used to select the time of the training session. Please select the time for which you are registering.
* **Training Location**: This field is used to enter the location of the training session. Please enter the location accurately.
* **Organization**: This field is used to collect the name of the organization you represent. Please enter the name of your organization.
* **Role**: This field is used to select your role in the training session. Please select the role you are registering for.
* **Comments**: This field is optional and used to enter any comments or additional information you want to provide. Please enter any comments you have.
* **Training Topic**: This field is used to select the training topic that interests you. Please select the topic(s) that you want to learn more about.
* **Submit**: This field is used to submit the completed form. Please select the topics for which you want to receive training.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have selected the correct training date and time.
* Please enter valid phone number and email address.
* Please select a topic that is relevant to your role and interests.
* Please review your form carefully before submitting it.
