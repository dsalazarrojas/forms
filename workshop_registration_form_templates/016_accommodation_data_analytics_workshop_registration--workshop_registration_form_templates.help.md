# Accommodation Data Analytics Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect registration information for the Accommodation Data Analytics Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name and email address in the designated fields.
2. Provide your phone number, organization or company name, and job title.
3. Choose your preferred industry sector and session type.
4. Indicate your experience level with data analytics and select the topics you are interested in learning about.
5. Specify the software tools you use, if any.
6. If applicable, note any dietary requirements and accessibility needs.
7. Mention how you heard about the workshop.
8. Provide any additional comments or questions you may have.
