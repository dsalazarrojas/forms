# Lash Technician Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register for the Lash Technician Course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your preferred date from the "Date" field.
6. Select your preferred time from the "Time" field.
7. Choose one or more sessions from the "Which session" field.
8. If you have any additional notes or comments, you can use the "Note" fields (Note 1 to Note 18) to provide them.
