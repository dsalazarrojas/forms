# Payroll Insight Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users interested in attending the Payroll Insight Workshop. The form helps us understand the attendees' needs and preferences, allowing us to tailor the workshop content to their expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To register for the Payroll Insight Workshop, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "User Information" page to provide basic contact details.
2. Move to the "Session Information" page to select your preferred session date and time.
3. On the "Attendee Information" page, provide any additional comments or questions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information (<name>: <type> - <optional>):** This section collects your basic contact details such as your name, email, and phone number. Please fill in your details accurately so we can get in touch with you.
* **Session Information (<name>: <type> - <optional>):** Choose your preferred session date and time from the available options. If you don't have a preference, select "No preference".
* **Attendee Information (<name>: <type> - <optional>):** If you have any additional comments or questions, provide them here. This section is optional, but feel free to use it to let us know if you have any specific requirements or requests for the workshop.
