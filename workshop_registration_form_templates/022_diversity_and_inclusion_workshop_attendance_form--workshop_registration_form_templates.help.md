# diversity_and_inclusion_workshop_attendance_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from workshop attendees for diversity and inclusion workshops. It is essential to provide accurate and comprehensive data for planning and execution purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please provide the name of the workshop you are registering for.
2.  Enter the date you plan to attend the workshop, if applicable.
3.  Enter the time you plan to attend the workshop, if applicable.
4.  Enter your email address for contact purposes.
5.  Provide your phone number for any further communication.
6.  Add any additional notes regarding your attendance.
7.  For workshops offering options, select one from the provided choices.
8.  For workshops offering multiple options, select all applicable options.
9.  Enter your age in the designated field.
10. Enter a decimal value, if required, in the designated field.
11. Enter a text description, if required, in the designated field.
12. If there are multiple dates for the workshop, enter the next date you plan to attend.
13. If there are multiple times for the workshop, enter the next time you plan to attend.
14. If there are multiple email addresses for registration, enter the next email address you would like to use.
15. Select one option from the second set of choices.
16. Select all applicable options from the second set of choices.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Workshop Name** (`workshop_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the workshop you are registering for. This helps us ensure that we are aware of the specific workshop you are attending.
- **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): If applicable, enter the date you plan to attend the workshop.
- **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): If applicable, enter the time you plan to attend the workshop.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for any further communication. We may use this to send updates or reminders about the workshop.
- **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Provide your phone number for any further communication. We may use this to send updates or reminders about the workshop.
- **Note** (`note`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your attendance.
- **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, required/optional): For workshops offering options, select one from the provided choices.
- **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required/optional): For workshops offering multiple options, select all applicable options.
- **Age** (`integer`, number, required/optional): Enter your age for demographic purposes.
- **Decimal Value** (`decimal`, number, required/optional): Enter a decimal value, if required, for any specific purpose.
- **Text Description** (`text`, text, required/optional): Enter a text description, if required, for any specific purpose.
- **Date 2** (`date_2`, date, required/optional): If there are multiple dates for the workshop, enter the next date you plan to attend.
- **Time 2** (`time_2`, time, required/optional): If there are multiple times for the workshop, enter the next time you plan to attend.
- **Email 2** (`email_2`, email, required/optional): If there are multiple email addresses for registration, enter the next email address you would like to use.
- **Select One 2** (`select_one_2`, select_one, required/optional): Select one option from the second set of choices.
- **Select Multiple 2** (`select_multiple_2`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all applicable options from the second set of choices.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are providing accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any doubts or questions, feel free to contact us.
* This form is designed to guide you through the process, so please take your time and fill it out carefully.
