# dental_extractions_workshop_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering attendees for a dental extractions workshop. The workshop is a medical procedure where attendees will be undergoing dental extractions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the session you are registering for by choosing "Yes" or "No" from the "Select Session" dropdown menu.
2. Fill out the attendee details form with your information.
3. Sign the Terms of Service by typing "Yes" to confirm.
4. Provide session details in the text box.
5. Enter your payment details in the text box.
6. Share medical information in the text box.
7. Provide emergency contact information in the text box.
8. Add any additional information in the text box.
9. (Optional) Confirm signing the agreement by selecting "Yes" or "No".
10. List any medical conditions that may affect your participation in the workshop in the "Medical Conditions" select_multiple field.
11. If applicable, list any allergies or medications that may impact your workshop experience in the "Allergies or Medications" select_multiple field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Session** (required): Choose "Yes" if you are registering for a session, or "No" if you are not.
* **attendee_details** (required): Fill out this form with your name, email, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Signed Terms** (required): Type "Yes" to confirm that you have read and agree to the terms of service.
* **session_details** (required): Provide the details of the session you are registering for, such as date, time, and location.
* **Payment Details** (required): Enter your payment information, including payment method and amount.
* **Medical Information** (required): Share any relevant medical information that may impact your participation in the workshop.
* **Emergency Contact** (required): Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Additional Information** (required): Add any additional information that may be helpful for your registration.
* **Signed Agreement** (optional): If you have not already signed the agreement, confirm by selecting "Yes".
* **Medical Conditions** (optional): If you have any medical conditions that may affect your participation, list them here.
* **Allergies or Medications** (optional): If you have any allergies or medications that may impact your workshop experience, list them here.

## Tips
* Make sure to choose the correct session you are registering for.
* Provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth registration process.
* If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please disclose them to ensure your safety and well-being.
