# Regenerative Farming Program Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from farmers interested in registering their farm for the Regenerative Farming Program. By filling out this form, you will provide essential data about your farm, its practices, and your participation in the program.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name as per your identification.
2. Provide your Email, which will be used for communication and notifications.
3. Enter your farm's name and address.
4. Select your Experience Level as per your farm's history with regenerative practices.
5. Specify the size of your farm.
6. Choose your farm's type (Organic, Conventional, or Other).
7. Decide whether you accept the program's terms and conditions (Program Acceptance).
8. Enter your farm's location.
9. Provide your contact number.
10. Select the type of program you are interested in (Regenerative Agriculture or Conservation Agriculture).
11. If you have any additional comments or information, you can add them in the "Additional Comments" field.
12. If you wish to provide more details about your farm, you can do so in the "Farm Details" and "Experience Details" fields.
13. Finally, review and agree to the program's terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your personal name.
* **Last Name**: Your surname.
* **Email**: A valid email address for communication and notifications.
* **Farm Name**: The name of your farm.
* **Experience Level**: Select the level of your farm's experience with regenerative practices (Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3).
* **Farm Size**: Enter the size of your farm (in units of hectares or acres).
* **Farm Type**: Choose the type of farm you have (Organic, Conventional, or Other).
* **Program Acceptance**: Confirm your acceptance of the program's terms and conditions.
* **Farm Location**: The location of your farm.
* **Contact Number**: A valid contact number for communication.
* **Program Type**: Select the type of program you are interested in (Regenerative Agriculture or Conservation Agriculture).
* **Additional Comments**: Any additional information you want to provide.
* **Farm Details**: More details about your farm (optional).
* **Experience Details**: More details about your experience with regenerative practices (optional).
* **Agreement**: Review and agree to the program's terms and conditions.

# Note: This form is designed to be completed in collaboration with the program team for a successful and smooth registration process.
