# Smart Access Control Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Access Control Workshop Registration form is designed to gather information from attendees registering for the workshop. Please take a few minutes to review the form and provide your details correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Select the relevant options for each field, ensuring that all required fields are filled out correctly.
3. Double-check that you have filled out all fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (1, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification or as you would like to be known.
* **Email** (2, email, required/optional): Enter your email address that you use for communication.
* **Topic Interests** (4, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the topics you are interested in learning about during the workshop. You can choose from the following options: 
	+ 'Yes' 
	+ 'No'
* **Preferred Dates** (5, date, required/optional): Select your preferred dates for attending the workshop. This will help us better understand your schedule and preferences.
* **Notes** (6, note, required/optional): If you have any additional information that you would like to share with us, please enter it here. This is an optional field, but it will help us get to know you better.
* **Name** (7, text, required/optional): This field seems to be a duplicate of the first field "Name". Please ignore this field and fill out the first one instead.
