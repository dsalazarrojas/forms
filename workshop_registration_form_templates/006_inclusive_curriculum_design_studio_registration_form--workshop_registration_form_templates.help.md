# Inclusive Curriculum Design Studio - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for workshop participants to register for the Inclusive Curriculum Design Studio. It collects essential information to ensure a smooth and inclusive experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First name** in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field. This will be used to communicate with you about the workshop details.
3. Choose a **Workshop date** from the calendar.
4. Enter the **Participant email** of the person attending the workshop.
5. Optionally, enter the **Participant phone** number if you want to be contacted.
6. Add any **Participant notes** about the participant's needs or requirements.
7. Indicate if the participant has **Special needs** and provide additional details if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): A valid email address is required for communication purposes.
* **Workshop date** (`workshop_date`, date, required): Select the workshop date you are interested in attending.
* **Participant email** (`participant_email`, text, required): Enter the email of the person attending the workshop.
* **Participant phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person attending the workshop, if you want to be contacted.
* **Participant notes** (`participant_notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional notes about the participant's needs or requirements.
* **Special needs** (`participant_special_needs`, select_one, optional): Indicate if the participant has any special needs (Yes/No).
* **Additional details** (`special_needs_details`, text, optional): If the participant has special needs, provide additional details here.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure we can contact you and the participant about the workshop details.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us using the provided email address.
