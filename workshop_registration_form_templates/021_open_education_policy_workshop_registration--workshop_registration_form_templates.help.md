# Open Education Policy Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information from participants attending the Open Education Policy Workshop. It helps us understand your preferences and needs, allowing us to provide a better experience for you during the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand what information is required.
2. Fill in your details accurately and completely.
3. Select your session choices and preferences.
4. Submit your registration to complete the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details**
  * **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed during the workshop.
  * **Participant Institution** (`participant_institution`, `text`, required: false): If you are representing an institution, please enter its name here.
  * **Participant Position** (`participant_position`, `text`, required: false): Describe your role or position in your institution.
  * **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your institutional email address.
  * **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.
  * **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional information you would like to share with us.
  * **Minimalist Form** (`minimalist_form`, `note`, required: false): This field is intended for additional notes or comments.
* **Session Choices**
  * **Session Choices** (`session_choices`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select up to 5 session choices that suit your needs and preferences.
* **Consent**
  * **Consent** (`consent`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate your consent to participate in the workshop by selecting 'Yes'.
* **Diet**
  * **Participant Diet** (`participant_diet`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select your dietary preference (Vegan, Vegetarian, Omnivore).
* **Language**
  * **Participant Language** (`participant_language`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the language in which you are most comfortable communicating during the workshop.
* **Date and Time**
  * **Session Start Time** (`session_start_time`, `time`, required: false): Select the preferred start time for your sessions.
  * **Session End Time** (`session_end_time`, `time`, required: false): Select the preferred end time for your sessions.
  * **Workshop Start Date** (`workshop_start`, `date`, required: false): Choose the start date for the workshop.
  * **Workshop End Date** (`workshop_end`, `date`, required: false): Choose the end date for the workshop.
  * **Session Start** (`session_start`, `time`, required: false): Select the start time of your session.
  * **Session End** (`session_end`, `time`, required: false): Select the end time of your session.
* **Session Preferences**
  * **Participant Session Preferences** (`participant_session_preferences`, `text`, required: false): Enter any specific session preferences or requirements.
  * **Participant Session** (`participant_session`, `text`, required: false): Add any other session-specific details.
  * **Participant Session Choice 1-5** (`participant_session_choice_1`, `text`, `participant_session_choice_2`, `text`, `participant_session_choice_3`, `text`, `participant_session_choice_4`, `text`, `participant_session_choice_5`, `text`, required: false): Enter up to 5 session choices with specific details.
