# Water Conservation Challenge Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Water Conservation Challenge Registration form is a tool to register schools and their students for a water conservation challenge or competition. This form collects information from schools to track their participation, students, and water sources.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `School Name` field with the name of your school.
2. Enter the `Student Name` of the students participating in the challenge.
3. Provide your school's `Email Address` and `Phone Number` as contact methods.
4. Choose the `School Location` from the available options to track the geographical location of your school.
5. Enter the `Number of Students Participating` to track the number of students participating in the challenge.
6. Enter the `Teacher Name` who will be responsible for your school's participation.
7. Fill in the `School Address` to track the location of your school.
8. Select the `Water Source` used by your school or students from the available options.
9. Choose the `Water Quality` of the water source used by your school or students from the available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Name**: Enter the name of your school. (Required)
* **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student participating in the challenge. (Required)
* **Email Address**: Enter a contact email address for your school. (Required)
* **Phone Number**: Enter a contact phone number for your school. (Required)
* **School Location**: Select the location of your school. (Not required)
* **Number of Students Participating**: Enter the number of students participating in the challenge. (Required)
* **Teacher Name**: Enter the name of the teacher responsible for your school's participation. (Required)
* **School Address**: Enter the address of your school. (Required)
* **Water Source**: Select the water source used by your school or students. (Required)
* **Water Quality**: Select the quality of the water source used by your school or students. (Required)

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check the information provided before submitting.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact the contact method provided in the form.
