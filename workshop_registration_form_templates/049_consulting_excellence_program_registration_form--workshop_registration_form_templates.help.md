# Consulting Excellence Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for participants to register for the Consulting Excellence Program. Fill out the form to provide your information and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information (marked as "required" in the form). You can leave the optional fields blank if not applicable.
2. Select your preferred workshop session from the dropdown menu.
3. Provide any dietary requirements or special accommodation needs in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name for registration.
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, optional): If you have an affiliation with an organization, enter its name here.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for registration confirmation.
* **Workshop session** (`workshop_session`, `select_one`, required): Select one preferred workshop session from the dropdown menu:
	+ Session A
	+ Session B
	+ Session C
* **Dietary requirements** (`dietary_requirements`, `text`, optional): If you have any food allergies or preferences, enter them here.
* **Special accommodation** (`special_accommodation`, `text`, optional): If you have accessibility needs or other requirements, enter them here.
