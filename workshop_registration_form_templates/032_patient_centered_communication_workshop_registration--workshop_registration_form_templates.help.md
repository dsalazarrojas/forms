# Patient Centered Communication Workshop Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information for registering patients or their caregivers for a patient-centered communication workshop. The collected data will be used for workshop organization, session planning, and attendee management.

## How To Complete This Form
To register for the workshop, please fill out this form completely and accurately. Ensure you provide the correct session title, start and end dates, workshop location, and necessary attendee details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Title** (`session_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief but descriptive title of the workshop session.
* **Session Start Date** (`session_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the workshop session is scheduled to begin.
* **Session End Date** (`session_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the workshop session is scheduled to end.
* **Workshop Location** (`workshop_location`, text, optional): Provide the location where the workshop will take place.
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the attendee, such as their role or any special requirements.
* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the patient or their caregiver registering for the workshop.
* **Attendee E-mail** (`attendee_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the patient or their caregiver.
* **Attendee Phone** (`attendee_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the patient or their caregiver.

## Tips
- Ensure that the session title is clear and descriptive to help workshop organizers understand the topic and schedule the session correctly.
- Double-check the dates entered for the start and end of the session to avoid conflicts or scheduling issues.
- If you are registering on behalf of someone else, ensure that you have their permission to do so.
- If you have any special requests or requirements, please include them in the 'Attendee Details' field.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the workshop organizers for assistance.
