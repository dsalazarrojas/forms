# Machine Health Monitoring Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Machine Health Monitoring Training Registration Form is used to register participants for a Machine Health Monitoring Training program. It collects information such as name, contact details, and category preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
3. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
4. Select a Date of Registration from the calendar.
5. Select a Time of Registration from the clock.
6. Add any additional Note about your registration.
7. Choose a Category that best describes your interest.
8. If applicable, enter the name of the Tool assigned to you.
9. If applicable, select the output file name.
10. Enter your Form IDs for reference.
11. Enter a Description of your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (id: 1, First Name, text, optional)
* This field is used to collect your first name. Please enter your first name as you prefer to be addressed.

### Email (id: 2, Email, email, optional)
* This field is used to collect your email address. Enter your valid email address to receive updates and communications.

### Phone (id: 3, Phone, text, optional)
* This field is used to collect your phone number. Enter your phone number for further contact.

### Date of Registration (id: 4, Date of Registration, date, optional)
* This field is used to select a date for your registration.

### Time of Registration (id: 5, Time of Registration, time, optional)
* This field is used to select a time for your registration.

### Note (id: 6, Note, text, optional)
* This field is used to add any additional notes about your registration.

### Category (id: 7, Category, select_multiple, optional)
* This field is used to select a category that best describes your interest in the training.

### Assigned Tool (id: 8, Assigned Tool, text, optional)
* This field is used to enter the name of the tool assigned to you.

### Output File (id: 9, Output File, text, optional)
* This field is used to select the output file name.

### Form IDs (id: 10, Form IDs, text, optional)
* This field is used to enter your Form IDs for reference.

### Description (id: 11, Description, text, optional)
* This field is used to enter a description of your registration.

### Category Description (id: 12, Category Description, text, optional)
* This field is used to enter a description of your selected category.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, reach out to the training team.
* Review your submission carefully before submitting the form.
