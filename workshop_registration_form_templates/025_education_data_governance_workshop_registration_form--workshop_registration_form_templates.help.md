# education_data_governance_workshop_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants of an education data governance workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure that you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill out each field accurately and truthfully.
3. Don't hesitate to ask for clarification if you're unsure about any field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Details
* **Participant Details**: This field is for general information about the participant.

### Participant Information
* **Participant Name**: Your name as a participant in the workshop.
* **Participant Email**: Your email address.
* **Participant Phone**: Your phone number.
* **Participant Organization**: Your organization's name.
* **Title**: Your job title or title in your organization.
* **Role**: Your role in your organization.
* **Job Title**: Your job title or position.
* **Department**: The department you work in.
* **Manager**: The name of your manager.
* **Manager Title**: The title of your manager.
* **Manager Phone**: The phone number of your manager.
* **Manager Email**: The email address of your manager.

### Workshop Information
* **Workshop Name**: The name of the workshop you're registering for.
* **Workshop Date**: The date of the workshop.
* **Workshop Time**: The time of the workshop.
* **Workshop Duration**: The duration of the workshop.
* **Workshop Location**: The location of the workshop.
* **Workshop Organizers**: A multiple-choice question indicating whether the workshop is organized by multiple people.
* **Submission Status**: A multiple-choice question indicating the submission status of your workshop.
* **Submission Date**: The date of your workshop submission.
* **Submission**: A general text field for any additional information.

### Notes
* **Notes**: Any additional information about your workshop or participation.
