# Weekly_Discussion_Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weekly_Discussion_Registration form is used to collect information from workshop participants for the purpose of assigning them to a specific group or discussion session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your topic interests from the "Topic Interests" section. This will help us understand what topics you are interested in discussing.
2. Choose your recurring session preferences from the "Recurring Sessions" section. This will help us schedule sessions that fit your needs.
3. Select your preferred sign-up option from the "Sign up" section.
4. Add any additional topic interests in the "Additional Topic Interests" section (if applicable).
5. Fill in your name, email, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
6. Choose a date and time for the session from the "Date" and "Time" sections.
7. Provide any comments or additional information in the "Comments" section (if applicable).
8. Select the user you would like to be assigned to (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Topic Interests** (`topic_interests`, select_one, required): Select your topic interests from the provided options.
* **Recurring Sessions** (`recurring_sessions`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your recurring session preferences.
* **Sign up** (`signup`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred sign-up option.
* **Additional Topic Interests** (`topic_interests_2`, note, optional): Add any additional topic interests you have.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Choose a date for the session.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Choose a time for the session.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or information.
* **Assigned** (`assigned_user`, select_one, optional): Select the user you would like to be assigned to.
