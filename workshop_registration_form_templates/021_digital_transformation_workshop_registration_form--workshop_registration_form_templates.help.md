# Digital Transformation Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help you complete the Digital Transformation Workshop Registration Form accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Go through each section in order: Registration information, Attendee details, Payment information, Preferences, and Contact details.
2. Fill out each field as prompted, and make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by an asterisk *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Information** (`registration_form`, text, required: false): This section is where you enter your registration details for the workshop. Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, text, required: false): Here, you can fill out any additional information about the attendee, such as their role or job title.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_details`, text, required: false): This section is for payment details, such as the method of payment and amount paid.
* **Preferences** (`preferences`, text, required: false): You can indicate your preferences for the workshop, such as language or dietary needs.
* **Email** (`email`, text, required: false): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the attendee.
* **Email** (`email`, text, required: false): Please enter the email address of the attendee.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the attendee.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required: false): Enter the company name of the attendee.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required: false): Select the role of the attendee from the options provided.
* **Location** (`location`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the location(s) where the attendee will be attending the workshop.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required: false): Select the payment method from the options provided.
* **Amount** (`amount`, number, required: false): Enter the amount paid for the workshop.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of payment.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of payment.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Status** (`payment_status`, text, required: false): Enter the status of the payment.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the company of the attendee.
* **City** (`city`, text, required: false): Enter the city of the attendee.
* **State** (`state`, text, required: false): Enter the state of the attendee.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required: false): Enter the zip code of the attendee.
* **Country** (`country`, text, required: false): Enter the country of the attendee.
* **Email Confirmation** (`email_confirmation`, email, required: false): Enter the email confirmation.
* **Phone Confirmation** (`phone_confirmation`, text, required: false): Enter the phone confirmation.

Please note that some fields are marked as optional, but please ensure that you fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (*). If you are unsure about any field, please refer to your workshop confirmation email for more information.
