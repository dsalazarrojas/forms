# Digital Room Allocation Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for trainers, coordinators, or other authorized personnel to collect and record details about upcoming training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **User Information (1)**: Fill in your name, email, and any other relevant user details.
2. **Training Info (2)**: Provide a description of the training session, topic, and type.
3. **Room Information (3)**: Enter the details about the allocated room, including its type and capacity.
4. **Training Dates (4-5)**: Select the start and end dates for the training session.
5. **Training Duration (6)**: Specify the number of hours or days the training will last.
6. **Training Capacity (7)**: Enter the maximum number of attendees that can be accommodated for the training session.
7. **Location (8)**: Enter the address or location of the training session.
8. **Trainer Information (9-10)**: Enter the trainer's name, email, and phone number.
9. **Training Type (11)**: Choose between the options for the type of training: 'Training Type', 'On-the-job', 'Online', or 'Blended'.
10. **Room Type (12)**: Choose if the room allocated is yes or no.
11. **Room Details (13-14)**: Enter the capacity of the training room and its description.
12. **Training Status (15)**: Choose the status of the training event as 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
13. **Training Status Date (16)**: Enter the date the training status was updated.
14. **Training Status Comment (17)**: Leave any comments or notes about the training status.
15. **Room Notes (18)**: Enter any additional notes about the allocated room.
16. **Training Notes (19)**: Leave any notes about the training event.
17. **User Agreement (20)**: Confirm if the user has agreed to the terms and conditions.
18. **Terms and Conditions (21)**: Enter the terms and conditions for the training event.

Note that most fields are optional, so fill in only what's necessary for your specific use case. If you're unsure about any field, feel free to reach out to your supervisor or refer to our organizational guidelines.
