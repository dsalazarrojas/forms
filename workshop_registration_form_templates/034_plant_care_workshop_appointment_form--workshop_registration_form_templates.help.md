# Plant Care Workshop Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect user registration and appointment details for the Plant Care Workshop. This form will be used to organize and manage participant information for the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and fill in the required information.
2. Enter your name and any other relevant details in the User Input field.
3. Provide appointment details in the Appointment Details field.
4. Confirm your participation in the Participant Confirmation field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input (Name, Text, Optional)**: Enter your name or any other relevant information you'd like to share with the workshop organizers.
* **Appointment Details (Text, Optional)**: Provide any necessary details about your appointment, such as date and time preferences, contact information, or special requests.
* **Participant Confirmation (Text, Optional)**: Confirm your participation in the workshop by entering your name and a brief message if you'd like to express your interest or any questions you have.
