# skill_building_session_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registering participants for skill-building workshops. It gathers information on workshop and session details, as well as participant personal and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the workshop details section, including Workshop Name, Session Date, Start Time, and Session Length.
2. Provide your participant information, including Participant Name, Email, Contact Phone, Participant Company, Participant Title, Job Title, Participant Department, Participant Position, and any other relevant details.
3. Choose your session location and facilitators from the options provided.
4. Indicate if you have attended the workshop before or not.
5. Provide any additional comments or notes you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Name** (`1`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the name of the workshop you are interested in attending.
* **Session Date** (`2`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter the date of the workshop session.
* **Start Time** (`3`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the start time of the workshop session.
* **Participant Name** (`4`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your name as a participant.
* **Email** (`5`, `email`, required: `false`): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`6`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Participant Company** (`7`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the name of your company if applicable.
* **Participant Title** (`8`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your job title or role.
* **Participant Job Title** (`9`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your job title.
* **Participant Department** (`10`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your department name.
* **Participant Position** (`11`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your position or role within your department.
* **Session Length** (`12`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the length of the workshop session.
* **Session Length Unit** (`13`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the unit of the session length (e.g. hours, minutes).
* **Session Location** (`14`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the location of the workshop session.
* **Participant Comments** (`15`, `note`, required: `false`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the workshop session.
* **Workshop Facilitator** (`16`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Choose the facilitator for the workshop session.
* **Participant Attended** (`17`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Indicate if you have attended the workshop before or not.
* **Participant Attended Before** (`18`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Indicate if you attended the workshop before or not.
* **Workshop Name 2** (`19`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the name of the second workshop you are interested in.
* **Session Date 2** (`20`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter the date of the second workshop session.
* **Session Time** (`21`, `time`, required: `false`): Enter the start time of the second workshop session.
* **Session Notes** (`22`, `note`, required: `false`): Enter any notes or additional comments about the second workshop session.
* **Email 2** (`23`, `email`, required: `false`): Enter your email address for the second workshop session.
* **Participant Phone** (`24`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your contact phone number for the second workshop session.
* **Workshop Facilitator 2** (`25`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Choose the facilitator for the second workshop session.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have all the necessary information before filling out the form.
* Use the hints or suggestions provided for each field for guidance.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask your workshop facilitator or contact person for clarification.
