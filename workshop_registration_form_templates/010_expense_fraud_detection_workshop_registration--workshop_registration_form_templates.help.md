# Expense Fraud Detection Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Expense Fraud Detection Workshop Registration" form is designed to collect registration information from individuals interested in or requiring training on identifying fraudulent activities within financial transactions, specifically related to expenses.

## How To Complete This Form
To register for the workshop, please follow these steps:

1. **Workshop Registration** (`id: 1`): Enter a brief description of why you're registering for the workshop. This could be a personal interest in the topic or a requirement from your company.
2. **Name** (`id: 2`): Enter your name as it appears on your identification or contact information.
3. **Email** (`id: 3`): Provide your email address for workshop updates and communication.
4. **Company** (`id: 4`): If applicable, enter your company name for identification and workshop coordination.
5. **Discount Amount** (`id: 5`): If you're eligible for a discount (10% or more), enter the amount you qualify for. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
6. **Payment Method** (`id: 6`): Select your preferred method of payment for the workshop (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, PayPal).
7. **Payment Details** (`id: 7`): Enter any additional information required for the payment method you selected, such as payment confirmation or transaction details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Workshop Registration**
- **Workshop Registration** (`id: 1`): A space to briefly describe why you're registering for the workshop.
### **Name**
- **Name** (`id: 2`): Enter your personal or company name as it's known to the workshop.
### **Email**
- **Email** (`id: 3`): Your email address for workshop updates and notifications.
### **Company**
- **Company** (`id: 4`): If relevant, enter your company name for identification and workshop coordination purposes.
### **Discount Amount**
- **Discount Amount** (`id: 5`): If you've been offered a discount of 10% or more, enter the amount here to receive the discount. Otherwise, you can leave it blank.
### **Payment Method**
- **Payment Method** (`id: 6`): Select how you'd like to pay for the workshop (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, PayPal).
### **Payment Details**
- **Payment Details** (`id: 7`): Enter any required information for your chosen payment method.

## Tips
- Ensure your contact information (name and email) are accurate for proper workshop communication.
- If you've been offered a discount, please enter the exact amount to receive it.
