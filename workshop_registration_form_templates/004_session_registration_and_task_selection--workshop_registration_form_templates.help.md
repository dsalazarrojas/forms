# Session Registration And Task Selection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for participants to register and select sessions and tasks for a workshop. It is an essential part of the workshop registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Participant Details page with your information.
2. On the Session Choices page, select the sessions you would like to attend by choosing "Yes" from the available options.
3. On the Task Preferences page, provide your task preferences by typing in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Details
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, `text`, required: false): This page is for you to enter your personal details such as your name and any other relevant information.

### Session Choices
* **Session Choices** (`session_choices`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Here, you can select the sessions you would like to attend. Choose "Yes" for each session that interests you.

### Task Preferences
* **Task Preferences** (`task_preferences`, `text`, required: false): In this field, you can provide any task preferences you have, such as specific skills or expertise you would like to bring to the workshop.

### Tips
- Make sure to select "Yes" for each session you would like to attend.
- You can add multiple tasks to your preferences.
- If you have any specific requirements or concerns, feel free to mention them in the Task Preferences field.
