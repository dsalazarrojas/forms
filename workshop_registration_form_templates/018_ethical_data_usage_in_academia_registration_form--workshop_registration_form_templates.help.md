# ethical_data_usage_in_academia_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register users' involvement with ethical data in academic settings. It helps to track who is actively using or handling ethical data within the institution and ensures that all registered participants understand and agree to the ethical guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your student type from the options provided.
2. Enter your name if you are a student or staff member.
3. Enter your email for correspondence purposes.
4. Provide your department (e.g., Faculty of Arts, School of Engineering).
5. Mention your role within the department (e.g., Professor, Research Assistant).
6. If you are a faculty member, select your faculty status (Active or Inactive).
7. If you are a faculty member, provide your department name.
8. Briefly describe the research project you are involved with, if any.
9. If you are handling or using ethical data, provide a brief description of the guidelines you follow for handling it.
10. Finally, review and confirm the information you have provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Type** (Select One):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you are actively using or handling ethical data.
	+ Select 'No' if you are not actively involved with ethical data.
* **Name** (Text):
	+ Enter your name if you are a student or staff member.
* **Email** (Email):
	+ Enter your email address for institutional correspondence purposes.
* **Department** (Text):
	+ Provide your department name (e.g., Faculty of Arts, School of Engineering).
* **Role** (Text):
	+ Mention your role within the department (e.g., Professor, Research Assistant).
* **Faculty Status** (Select One):
	+ Select 'Active' if you are an active faculty member.
	+ Select 'Inactive' if you are an inactive faculty member.
* **Faculty Department** (Text):
	+ If you are a faculty member, provide your department name.
* **Research Project** (Text):
	+ Briefly describe the research project you are involved with, if any.
* **Ethical Guidelines** (Text):
	+ Provide a brief description of the guidelines you follow for handling ethical data, if you are handling or using it.
* **Submit** (Text):
	+ Review the information you have provided before submitting.
* **Confirm** (Note):
	+ Confirm that you have reviewed and agree to the ethical guidelines before submitting the form.

## Tips
- Please fill in all required fields.
- If you are unsure about any field, refer to your departmental guidelines or seek assistance from your supervisor.
- Review the form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy.
