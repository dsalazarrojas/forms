# Tourism Resilience Council Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tourism Resilience Council Registration Form is designed to collect information from participants to provide a more personalized experience during the workshops.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps outlined below:

1. Fill out the form with the required information for each field.
2. Review the form carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
3. Submit the form once you've finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Page** (`registration_page`, `text`, required: `false`): This is the starting point of the form. Please ensure you've read the form instructions before proceeding.
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, `text`, required: `false`): Please provide your name, email, and phone number for future reference.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter any additional contact information, such as your LinkedIn profile or other relevant details.
* **Workshop Preferences** (`workshop_preferences`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select your preferred workshop (Workshop 1 or Workshop 2).
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: `false`): If you'd like to specify a particular date for the workshop, please enter it here.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: `false`): If you'd like to specify a particular time for the workshop, please enter it here.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required: `false`): Provide your name as you'd like to be referred to during the workshop.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, required: `false`): Enter your email address for future communication.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your phone number for future reference.
* **Message** (`message`, `note`, required: `false`): If you have any additional information or messages for the organizers, please enter it here.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, `note`, required: `false`): Enter any additional notes or information about yourself.
* **Workshop Attendance** (`workshop_attendance`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select which workshops you'd like to attend (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Participant Status** (`participant_status`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select your participant status (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select your payment method (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select your payment status (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Participant Type** (`participant_type`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select your participant type (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Participant Group** (`participant_group`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select your participant group (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Participant Country** (`participant_country`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select your country of origin (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Workshop Name** (`workshop_name`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select the workshop you'd like to attend (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, `date`, required: `false`): If you'd like to specify a particular workshop date, please enter it here.
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, `time`, required: `false`): If you'd like to specify a particular workshop time, please enter it here.
* **Participant Language** (`participant_language`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select your preferred language (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Participant Level** (`participant_level`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select your participant level (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Participant Gender** (`participant_gender`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select your gender (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required: `false`): Review and submit your form once you've completed it.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Review the form carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
