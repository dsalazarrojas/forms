# iphoto_photography_workshop_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect registration information from users who are interested in attending an iPhoto Photography Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you have filled out all required fields (indicated by a red asterisk \*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form** (`1`, `text`, not required): This field is where you will enter your registration information.
* **Workshop Date** (`2`, `date`, not required): Enter the date you would like to attend the workshop. This should be in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Session Time** (`3`, `number`, not required): Select the session time you prefer for the workshop.

## Tips
* Please note that the workshop date and session time are not required fields, but it's recommended that you provide this information so that we can better schedule the workshop according to your preferences.
* If you have any specific requests or preferences, please include them in the `Registration Form` field.
