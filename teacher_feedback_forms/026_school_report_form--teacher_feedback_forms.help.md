# School Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Report Form is a tool for teachers to provide feedback and reports on student performance, progress, and behavior.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for the student:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Teacher Name
	* Date Submitted
2. Provide an update on the student's progress:
	* Student Progress
3. Note any concerning behavior:
	* Student Behavior
4. Document any notable achievements:
	* Student Achievements
5. Select the subject area the observation is related to:
	* Subject Area
6. Indicate the class period the observation took place in:
	* Class Period
7. Fill in the date and time the form was submitted:
	* Date Submitted
	* Time Submitted

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter the student's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter the student's last name.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the teacher.
* **Student Progress** (`student_progress`, text, optional): Provide an update on the student's progress, such as recent accomplishments, goals, or areas of concern.
* **Student Behavior** (`student_behavior`, text, optional): Note any concerning behavior observed in the student, such as disruptions, conflicts, or difficulties with classroom management.
* **Student Achievements** (`student_achievements`, text, optional): Document any notable achievements or accomplishments of the student.
* **Subject Area** (`subject_area`, text, optional): Select the subject area the observation is related to, such as math, science, or English.
* **Class Period** (`class_period`, text, optional): Indicate the class period the observation took place in, such as 1st period or 5th period.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, optional): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`, time, optional): Enter the time the form was submitted.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when providing updates on student progress and behavior.
* Use clear and concise language to ensure accurate and easy-to-read notes.
* Use the correct date and time for the submission to ensure accurate timestamping.
