# Teacher Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students about their teachers, aiming to provide a comprehensive understanding of the teacher's performance, strengths, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant questions that apply to your experience with the teacher.
2. Provide detailed answers to each question, explaining your thoughts and feelings about the teacher's performance.
3. Use the available options or text boxes to describe your answers in a clear and concise manner.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the necessary questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How was the teacher's performance? (teacher_performance)**: This question allows you to evaluate the overall performance of the teacher, describing their strengths and weaknesses in a general manner.
* **What are your thoughts on the teacher's teaching styles and techniques? (teaching_styles_and_techniques)**: Share your insights on the teacher's approach to teaching, including any effective or ineffective methods they employ.
* **How engaged do you feel with the teacher? (student_engagement)**: Rate your level of engagement with the teacher, indicating how interested and invested you are in the subject matter.
* **What is your feedback frequency on the teacher's availability? (teacher_availability)**: Describe how often you receive feedback from the teacher, providing insight into their responsiveness and availability.
* **How often do you receive feedback from the teacher? (feedback_frequency)**: Rate how frequently the teacher provides feedback, helping to evaluate their communication style.
* **How does the teacher support you in your learning? (teacher_support)**: Share your thoughts on the teacher's support and guidance, including any positive or negative experiences.
* **How helpful do you find the teacher's responses to your questions? (teacher_helpfulness)**: Rate the teacher's response time and helpfulness, providing insight into their effectiveness in addressing your needs.
* **Does the teacher demonstrate professional behavior? (teacher_professionalism)**: Check if the teacher exhibits professional conduct in their interactions with you.
* **How effective is the teacher in their role? (teacher_effectiveness)**: Rate the teacher's effectiveness in their position, considering factors like communication, planning, and execution.
* **How does the teacher communicate effectively with you? (teacher_communication)**: Rate the teacher's communication style, considering clarity, effectiveness, and responsiveness.
* **How friendly and approachable is the teacher? (teacher_friendly)**: Assess the teacher's friendliness and approachability, indicating how welcoming and accessible they are.
* **How responsive is the teacher to your needs? (teacher_responsive)**: Describe the teacher's responsiveness to your queries and concerns.
* **Does the teacher provide supportive guidance and feedback? (teacher_supportive)**: Rate the teacher's provision of guidance and feedback, considering frequency and effectiveness.
* **Does the teacher take responsibility for their actions and decisions? (teacher_responsible)**: Check if the teacher exhibits accountability in their work.
* **How prepared is the teacher for classes and lessons? (teacher_prepared)**: Rate the teacher's preparedness for classes and lessons, considering factors like planning and execution.
* **How professional is the teacher in their interactions with you? (teacher_professionalism_2)**: Assess the teacher's professional conduct in interactions with you.
* **Does the teacher exhibit punctuality in their work? (punctuality)**: Check if the teacher is punctual in their work.

Note: This form is designed to gather feedback in a way that is comprehensive yet not overly burdensome. Please take your time and provide honest, detailed answers to help the teacher understand their strengths and areas for improvement.
