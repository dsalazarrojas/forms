# Teaching Session Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from students after a teaching session. This feedback will help the trainer to improve their teaching methods and provide better experiences for future students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Session Title** field with the title of the session you attended.
2. Rate the session using the **Overall Rating** field. This is a numerical rating out of [insert maximum number, e.g. 5].
3. Provide feedback on the session using the **Feedback** field.
4. Enter the name of the trainer in the **Trainer Name** field.
5. Enter your own name in the **Student Name** field.
6. Enter the date of the session using the **Session Date** field.
7. Enter the time of the session using the **Session Time** field.
8. If you have any additional comments, please enter them in the **Comments** field.
9. If you have any contact information for the trainer, please enter their phone number in the **Trainer Phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Title** (`session_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the session you attended.
* **Overall Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate the session on a scale of 1-5.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Provide feedback on the session, such as what you liked and disliked.
* **Trainer Name** (`trainer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the trainer.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the student who attended the session.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the session.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the session.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments you have about the session.
* **Trainer Phone** (`trainer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the trainer.

Note: All fields are optional, but providing feedback and your name is encouraged.
