# Lesson Plan Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback on lesson plans, helping teachers share their experiences and insights with colleagues.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete the form, follow these steps:
   1. Select the teacher who completed the lesson plan.
   2. Enter the student's name as it appears in the class register.
   3. Enter any additional feedback you have regarding the lesson plan.
   4. If applicable, select the lesson name or title as it appears in your records.
   5. Optionally, provide the date when the lesson plan was completed.
   6. If the lesson plan utilized a specific tool or resource, select the tool used (e.g., chatjimmy).
   7. Optionally, rate the lesson plan with a numerical rating (1-5).
   8. Add any additional comments about the lesson plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **teacher**: A field to select the teacher who completed the lesson plan.
- **student name**: A field to enter the student's name as it appears in the class register.
- **feedback**: A field to enter any additional feedback you have regarding the lesson plan.
- **lesson name**: A field to enter the lesson plan's name or title as it appears in your records.
- **date**: A field to enter the date when the lesson plan was completed.
- **time**: A field to enter the time when the lesson plan was completed.
- **rating**: A field to rate the lesson plan with a numerical rating (1-5).
- **comments**: A field to add any additional comments about the lesson plan.
- **tool**: A field to select the tool or resource used during the lesson plan (e.g., chatjimmy).
- **comments**: A field to add any additional comments about the lesson plan.
