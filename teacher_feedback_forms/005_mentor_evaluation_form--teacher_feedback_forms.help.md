# Mentor Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on a teacher or educator's performance as a mentor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the title of the evaluation, e.g., "Midterm Progress Update" or "Final Evaluation".
2. Type in the name of the mentor being evaluated.
3. Choose a date for the evaluation.
4. Rate the mentor on a scale of 1 to 5, and provide a brief explanation for your rating.
5. Provide any feedback or comments about the mentor's performance.
6. If applicable, mention any challenges you've encountered while working with the mentor.
7. Select areas where the mentor needs improvement, such as communication, time management, or problem-solving skills.
8. Add any additional evaluation comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Mentor Evaluation Form**
This is the title of the evaluation and is used to help identify the purpose of the form.
* **Required**: Optional (you can leave it blank)
### 2. **Mentor's Name**
This is where you enter the name of the person being evaluated.
* **Label**: The name of the mentor, e.g., "John Doe"
* **Required**: No, but it's essential to fill this out for proper identification.
### 3. **Evaluation Date**
This is the date when the evaluation was conducted or is relevant to the evaluation.
* **Label**: The date of the evaluation
* **Required**: No
### 4. **Overall Rating (1-5)**
Rate the mentor on a scale of 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Label**: The overall rating of the mentor
* **Required**: No
### 5. **Why?**
Provide a brief explanation for your overall rating.
* **Label**: A short explanation for the rating
* **Required**: No
### 6. **Feedback**
This is an open-ended field for providing any feedback or comments about the mentor's performance.
* **Label**: Comments or feedback about the mentor
* **Required**: No
### 7. **Challenges Encountered**
If you've faced any challenges while working with the mentor, list them here.
* **Label**: Challenges or difficulties encountered while working with the mentor
* **Required**: No
### 8. **Evaluation Comments**
Provide any additional comments or thoughts about the mentor's performance.
* **Label**: Comments or feedback about the mentor
* **Required**: No
### 9. **Areas for Improvement**
Select one or more areas where the mentor needs improvement, such as communication or time management.
* **Label**: Areas where the mentor needs to improve
* **Required**: No
  * Communication: The mentor could improve on how they communicate with you or others.
  * Time Management: The mentor could improve on managing their time effectively.
  * Organization: The mentor could improve on organizing tasks or projects.
  * Problem Solving: The mentor could improve on solving problems.
  * Teaching Method: The mentor could improve on their teaching method.
  * Adaptability: The mentor could improve on adapting to new situations.
  * Interpersonal Skills: The mentor could improve on interacting with others.
  * Leadership: The mentor could improve on leading others.
  * Teamwork: The mentor could improve on working collaboratively with the team.
  * Self-Management: The mentor could improve on managing themselves.
  * Initiative: The mentor could improve on initiating tasks or projects.
  * Creativity: The mentor could improve on being creative.
  * Self-Initiative: The mentor could improve on taking the initiative.
  * Self-Organization: The mentor could improve on organizing themselves.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form as accurately and honestly as possible.
* Be specific when providing feedback, especially on areas for improvement.
* Keep the feedback professional and constructive.
