# Teacher Favorite Things Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a teacher's personal preferences and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select your favorite options from the provided lists.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Favorite Food** (`favorite_food`, select_multiple): Select all the foods that you enjoy eating.
* **Favorite Holiday** (`favorite_holiday`, select_one): Choose your favorite holiday.
* **Favorite Activity** (`favorite_activity`, select_multiple): Select all the activities you enjoy doing.
* **Favorite Color** (`favorite_color`, select_one): Choose your favorite color.
* **Favorite Book** (`favorite_book`, select_multiple): Select all the books that you enjoy reading.
* **Favorite Movie** (`favorite_movie`, select_one): Choose your favorite movie.
* **Favorite Music** (`favorite_music`, select_multiple): Select all the types of music that you enjoy listening to.
* **Favorite Game** (`favorite_game`, select_one): Choose your favorite game.
* **Favorite Game Character** (`favorite_game_character`, select_one): Choose your favorite character from a game.
* **Favorite Game Series** (`favorite_game_series`, select_multiple): Select all the game series that you enjoy playing.
* **Favorite Book Character** (`favorite_book_character`, select_multiple): Select all the characters from books that you enjoy reading about.

## Tips
- Be honest and have fun with it!
- You can select as many or as few options as you like for each field.
- If you need more information or clarification on any of the fields, feel free to ask your administrator.
