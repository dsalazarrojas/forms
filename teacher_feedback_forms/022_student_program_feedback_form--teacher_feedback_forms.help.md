# Student Program Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Program Feedback Form is a tool used to collect insights from students on the effectiveness of a program or course. This form helps teachers evaluate their teaching methods and materials, identify areas that need improvement, and develop future programs that cater to the needs of their learners.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Select all the options that apply to your feedback on the program.
2. Provide a detailed explanation for your feedback in the "Suggestions For Improvement" and "Comments" fields.
3. Fill out the "Name Of Student," "Email Of Student," and "Contact Number Of Student" fields with the student's information.
4. Indicate your overall satisfaction with the program by selecting "Satisfied" or "Dissatisfied" in the "Overall Program Satisfaction" field.
5. Provide feedback on your teacher's performance by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Teacher Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Feedback** (`program_feedback_form`, select_multiple): Select all the options that apply to your feedback on the program. This section asks for your opinion on how well the program has met your needs.
* **Feedback On Program** (`feedback_on_program`, text): Provide a detailed explanation of your feedback on the program. Be specific about what you liked or disliked about the program.
* **Suggestions For Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, text): Suggest ways to improve the program or course. This will help the teacher and program developers create a more effective learning experience for you.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text): Add any additional comments or feedback you think are relevant to the program.
* **Name Of Student** (`name_of_student`, text): Enter the name of the student who is filling out this form.
* **Email Of Student** (`email_of_student`, email): Enter the email address of the student who is filling out this form.
* **Contact Number Of Student** (`contact_number_of_student`, text): Enter the contact number of the student who is filling out this form.
* **Course Program Feedback** (`course_program_feedback`, select_one): Select "Yes" if you're satisfied with the course program, otherwise select "No".
* **Course Program Satisfaction** (`course_program_satisfaction`, select_one): Select "Satisfied" if you're satisfied with the course program, otherwise select "Dissatisfied".
* **Teacher Feedback** (`teacher_feedback`, select_one): Select "Yes" if you're satisfied with your teacher's performance, otherwise select "No".
* **Overall Program Satisfaction** (`overall_program_satisfaction`, select_one): Select "Satisfied" if you're satisfied with the overall program, otherwise select "Dissatisfied".
