# Tutoring Services Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from students about the tutoring services provided by teachers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the title of the tutoring services you are providing feedback on.
2. Enter the name of the teacher you are providing feedback on.
3. Select whether or not the teacher's teaching style is effective.
4. Select whether or not you found the services helpful.
5. If you have any suggestions for improvement, select one from the options provided.
6. Enter the date and time you submitted the form.
7. Enter your email address if you want to be contacted for further discussion.
8. Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted for further discussion.
9. Provide any additional comments about your experience with the services.
10. Click the "Submit" button to submit your feedback.
11. Click the "Cancel" button to discard your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Tutoring Services Feedback Form** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the tutoring services you are providing feedback on.
- **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the teacher you are providing feedback on.
- **Teaching Style** (`teaching_style`, select_multiple, required): Select whether or not the teacher's teaching style is effective.
- **Student Feedback** (`student_feedback`, select_multiple, required): Select whether or not you found the services helpful.
- **Suggestion for Improvement** (`suggestion_for_improvement`, select_one, required): If you have any suggestions for improvement, select one from the options provided.
- **Date** (`form_submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date you submitted the form.
- **Time** (`form_submission_time`, time, required): Enter the time you submitted the form.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted for further discussion.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted for further discussion.
- **Comment** (`comment`, text, required): Provide any additional comments about your experience with the services.
- **Form Submit** (`form_submit`, text, required): Click the "Submit" button to submit your feedback.
- **Form Cancel** (`form_cancel`, text, required): Click the "Cancel" button to discard your feedback.
- **Page** (`page`, text, required): Enter the page number you are providing feedback on.
- **Page Number** (`page_number`, text, required): Enter the page number you are providing feedback on.
- **Page Count** (`page_count`, text, required): Enter the page count you are providing feedback on.
- **Page Size** (`page_size`, text, required): Enter the page size you are providing feedback on.
- **Page Numbering** (`page_numbering`, select_multiple, required): Select whether or not you found the page numbering clear.
- **Feedback** (`feedback_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of feedback you are providing.
- **Teacher** (`teacher_id`, select_one, required): Select whether or not you have feedback for the teacher.
- **Student** (`student_id`, select_one, required): Select whether or not you have feedback for the student.
- **Student Name** (`student_name`, select_one, required): Select the name of the student you are providing feedback on.
- **Teacher Name 2** (`teacher_name_2`, select_one, required): Select the name of the teacher you are providing feedback on.
- **Form Submit 2** (`form_submit_2`, text, required): Click the "Submit" button to submit your feedback.
- **Form Cancel 2** (`form_cancel_2`, text, required): Click the "Cancel" button to discard your feedback.
- **Comments** (`form_comments`, text, required): Provide any additional comments about your experience with the services.
