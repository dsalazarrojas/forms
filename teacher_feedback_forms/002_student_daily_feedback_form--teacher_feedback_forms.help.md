# Student Daily Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Daily Feedback Form is designed to gather daily feedback from students about their learning experience. Teachers can use this form to get insights into what works well and what does not, helping them improve their teaching methods and provide better support to their students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Select the date of the feedback in the "Feedback Date" field.
3. Write your feedback in the "Feedback" field.
4. If applicable, enter the teacher's name and email address in the "Teacher" and "Teacher Email" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Student Name
* **Student Name** (text, required): Enter the student's name.
### Feedback Date
* **Feedback Date** (date, required): Select the date of the feedback.
### Feedback
* **Feedback** (text, required): Write a brief description of your feedback.
### Teacher
* **Teacher** (text, optional): Enter the teacher's name (if applicable).
### Teacher Email
* **Teacher Email** (email, optional): Enter the teacher's email address (if applicable).
