# Online Instructors Mini Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from students about their experience with an online instructor.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Select the relevant options for each field based on your experience with the instructor.
*   You can provide feedback on multiple aspects of the instructor's performance.
*   Be honest and provide constructive feedback that will help the instructor improve.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Instructors Performance**: This field is not applicable as per the form design.
*   **How well was the course organized**: Rate your experience with the course organization by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
*   **Effective Communication**: Rate your experience with the instructor's communication skills by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
*   **Technical Skills**: Rate your experience with the instructor's technical skills by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
*   **How quickly did the instructor respond to questions**: Rate your experience with the instructor's response time by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
*   **How difficult was the course**: Rate your experience with the course difficulty by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
*   **How much support did the instructor provide**: Rate your experience with the instructor's support by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
*   **Overall satisfaction with instructor**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the instructor by selecting 'Satisfied' or 'Dissatisfied'.
*   **Would you take this class again**: Rate your willingness to take the class again by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
*   **Rating for Instructor**: Rate your experience with the instructor using a numerical value (1-5).
*   **Comments for Instructor**: Provide any additional comments or feedback for the instructor.
