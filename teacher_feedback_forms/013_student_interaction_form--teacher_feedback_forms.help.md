# Student Interaction Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to capture observations and feedback from teachers regarding interactions with students. It helps to document and analyze these interactions to improve future interactions and student outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter a title for the form in the **Student Interaction Form** field. This title should be a brief description of the interaction, such as a class meeting or project review.
2. Provide a detailed description of the interaction in the **Description** field. This can include any relevant context or background information.
3. Enter the date of the interaction in the **Date** field.
4. Select the subject that the interaction was related to.
5. Select the teacher who was interacting with the student.
6. Select the students involved in the interaction.
7. Choose the type of interaction that occurred, such as Academic or Social.
8. Add any additional notes about the interaction in the **Interaction Notes** field.
9. Enter the teacher's email address for contact.
10. Enter the teacher's phone number for contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Interaction Form**: A brief title for the form describing the interaction. Required.
* **Description**: A detailed description of the interaction, including any relevant context or background information. Not required.
* **Date**: The date of the interaction. Not required, but recommended for accurate recording.
* **Subject**: The subject that the interaction was related to. Required.
* **Teacher**: The teacher who was interacting with the student. Not required, but recommended for accurate attribution.
* **Student**: The student(s) involved in the interaction. Not required, but recommended for accurate recording.
* **Interaction Type**: The type of interaction that occurred, such as Academic or Social. Not required, but recommended for analysis.
* **Interaction Notes**: Additional notes about the interaction. Not required, but recommended for capturing important details.
* **Email**: The teacher's email address for contact. Not required, but recommended for follow-up communication.
* **Phone**: The teacher's phone number for contact. Not required, but recommended for immediate communication.
