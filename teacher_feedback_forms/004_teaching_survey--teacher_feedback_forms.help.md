# Teaching Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Teaching Survey is a form designed to collect feedback and insights from students about their teaching experience. The goal is to gather information that helps teachers improve their teaching methods and make informed decisions about their practice.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Teaching Survey, follow these steps:

1. Review the form questions carefully and answer them based on your honest experience as a student.
2. Enter your responses in the text fields provided.
3. Make sure to answer all questions, as they are optional (except for any marked as required).
4. Once you've completed the form, submit it to provide your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **teacher_feedback_form_1**
	* Label: (No label provided)
	* Type: text
	* Required: False
	* Explanation: This field is for you to share any thoughts or comments about your teaching experience. Please be as detailed and specific as possible.

### 2. **teacher_feedback_form_2**
	* Label: (No label provided)
	* Type: text
	* Required: False
	* Explanation: This field is for you to provide any feedback on the effectiveness of the teaching methods used in class.

### 3. **teacher_feedback_form_3**
	* Label: (No label provided)
	* Type: text
	* Required: False
	* Explanation: This field is for you to suggest any areas where the teacher could improve their teaching practice.

## Tips
* Be honest and sincere in your responses.
* Try to be as specific as possible when sharing your thoughts.
* If you have any concerns or questions, don't hesitate to reach out to your teacher or the form administrators.
