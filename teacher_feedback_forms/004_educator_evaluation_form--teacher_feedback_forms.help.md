# Educator Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Educator Evaluation Form is used to gather feedback from teachers regarding the overall performance and effectiveness of the assigned educator in their role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each question or field carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Enter your feedback in the provided text fields.
3. For numerical fields (communication_frequency, time_management, communication_frequency_2, time_management_2), provide a number between 1-5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **general** (`general`, `text`, required/optional): A text field for any general comments about the educator's performance.
* **knowledge** (`knowledge`, `text`, required/optional): A text field for feedback on the educator's knowledge base and teaching expertise.
* **classroom_management** (`classroom_management`, `text`, required/optional): A text field for feedback on the educator's classroom management skills.
* **communication** (`communication`, `text`, required/optional): A text field for feedback on the educator's communication skills.
* **communication_frequency** (`communication_frequency`, `number`, required/optional): A numerical field to rate how frequently the educator communicates with students, with 1 being "rarely" and 5 being "frequently".
* **time_management** (`time_management`, `number`, required/optional): A numerical field to rate how well the educator manages their time, with 1 being "poorly" and 5 being "well".
* **organization** (`organization`, `text`, required/optional): A text field for feedback on the educator's organizational skills.
* **classroom_environment** (`classroom_environment`, `text`, required/optional): A text field for feedback on the classroom environment.
* **communication_style** (`communication_style`, `text`, required/optional): A text field for feedback on the educator's communication style.
* **communication_frequency_2** (`communication_frequency_2`, `number`, required/optional): A numerical field to rate how frequently the educator communicates with parents and colleagues, with 1 being "rarely" and 5 being "frequently".
* **time_management_2** (`time_management_2`, `number`, required/optional): A numerical field to rate how well the educator manages their time, with 1 being "poorly" and 5 being "well".
* **organization_2** (`organization_2`, `text`, required/optional): A text field for feedback on the educator's organizational skills.
* **classroom_environment_2** (`classroom_environment_2`, `text`, required/optional): A text field for feedback on the classroom environment.
* **communication_style_2** (`communication_style_2`, `text`, required/optional): A text field for feedback on the educator's communication style.
* **overall_rating** (`overall_rating`, `text`, required/optional): A text field for the overall rating of the educator's performance.
* **assigned_tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required): A text field for the assigned tool or platform used by the educator.

## Tips
* Be honest and specific in your feedback to help the educator grow and improve.
* Keep your feedback concise and clear, focusing on specific behaviors or actions.
* If you have any concerns or observations that are not addressed in the form, please feel free to add them in the general comments field.
