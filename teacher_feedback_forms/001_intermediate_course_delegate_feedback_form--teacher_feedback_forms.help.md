# Intermediate Course Delegate Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Intermediate Course Delegate Feedback Form is used to gather feedback from course providers and participants in an intermediate course. This form helps course providers to understand the strengths and weaknesses of their courses and to improve their delivery. Participants are asked to provide their input and feedback on the course provider and the course itself.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Introduction
To start, simply fill in the form with your feedback and input for the course provider and the course. Make sure to answer all required fields to ensure that your feedback can be accurately captured and analyzed.

### Step 2: Answering Questions
- **Course Provider (1)**: This field is used to indicate if the course provider is available for the course. Please select 'Yes' if the course provider is available, and 'No' otherwise.
- **Feedback (2)**: In this field, you can provide any comments or feedback about the course provider.
- **Participant Feedback (3)**: Please select 'Yes' if you have given feedback on the course, and 'No' otherwise.
- **Participant Name (4)**: This field is used to provide your name, this is optional but it will help course provider to address you directly.
- **Participant Contact Information (5)**: This field is used to provide your contact information, this is optional but will help course provider to reach out to you directly.
- **Participant E-mail (6)**: In this field, please provide your e-mail address.
- **Participant Phone (7)**: This field is used to provide your phone number.
- **Course Provider Comment (8)**: This field is used to provide any additional comments for the course provider.
- **Participant Comment (9)**: In this field, you can provide any additional comments for the course provider.
- **Participant Satisfaction (10)**: Please select all that apply regarding your satisfaction level:
    - Satisfied
    - Dissatisfied
- **Course Provider Rating (11)**: This field is used to rate the course provider from 0-10.
- **Participant Satisfaction Level (12)**: Please select all that apply regarding the participant's satisfaction level:
    - Satisfied
    - Dissatisfied
- **Course Rating (13)**: This field is used to rate the course from 0-10.
- **Provider Satisfaction Level (14)**: Please select all that apply regarding the provider's satisfaction level:
    - Satisfied
    - Dissatisfied
- **Provider Comment (15)**: This field is used to provide any additional comments for the course provider.
- **Course (16)**: Please select 'Yes' if the course is active, 'No' otherwise.
- **Level (17)**: Please select the level of the course:
    - High
    - Low
- **Status (18)**: Please select the status of the course:
    - Active
    - Inactive
- **Course Provider (19)**: Please select 'Yes' if the course provider is available, 'No' otherwise.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer all required fields to ensure that your feedback can be accurately captured and analyzed.
* Use the 'Participant Name' field to provide your name if you want to receive direct communication from the course provider.
* Use the 'Participant E-mail' field to provide your e-mail address if you want the course provider to be able to contact you directly.
