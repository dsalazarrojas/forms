# Teachers Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teachers Assessment Form is a tool used to collect teacher feedback and ratings for students or subject matters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Teacher ID** (a unique identifier assigned to you) in the "Teacher ID" field.
2. Provide your **Teacher Name** in the "Teacher Name" field.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field.
4. Select the **Subject** being assessed in the "Subject" field.
5. Choose the date of the assessment in the **Assessment Date** field.
6. Describe the student's or subject matter's **Strengths** in the "Strengths" field.
7. Describe the student's or subject matter's **Weaknesses** in the "Weaknesses" field.
8. Provide any additional **Comments** about the assessment in the "Comments" field.
9. Rate the teacher's performance using the **Teacher Rating** dropdown menu (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
10. Optionally, provide any additional **Teacher Feedback** in the "Teacher Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Teacher ID**: Enter your unique identifier assigned to you as a teacher.
* **Teacher Name**: Provide your name as a teacher.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Subject**: Select the subject being assessed.
* **Assessment Date**: Choose the date of the assessment.
* **Strengths**: Describe the student's or subject matter's strengths.
* **Weaknesses**: Describe the student's or subject matter's weaknesses.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments about the assessment.
* **Teacher Rating**: Rate the teacher's performance using the dropdown menu.
* **Teacher Feedback**: Optionally, provide additional feedback about the assessment.
