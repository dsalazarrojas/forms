# Course Instructor Feedback Form MASTER - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Course Instructor Feedback Form MASTER is a tool for course instructors to provide feedback and suggestions about their teaching experience. This form helps to collect valuable insights and data that can be used to improve courses and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your comments in the "comments" field (Field 1).
2. Rate your experience with the "rating" field (Field 2).
3. Provide a rating score for your experience with the "rating_score" field (Field 4).
4. Select the tool that you used for the course with the "assigned_tool" field (Field 7).
5. You can submit this form at any time, and the "created_at", "updated_at", and "submitted_at" fields will be automatically populated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Course Instructor (instructor_feedback)
*   **Course Instructor** (Field 1, **note**, required: no):
    Enter a brief description of the course or your feedback on the course.

### 2. Comments
*   **Comments** (Field 2, **text**, required: no):
    Enter your comments and suggestions about the course.

### 3. Rating
*   **Rating** (Field 3, **number**, required: no):
    Rate your experience with the course.

### 4. Rating Score
*   **Rating Score** (Field 4, **number**, required: no):
    Provide a numerical rating score for your experience with the course.

### 5. Feedback Date
*   **Feedback Date** (Field 5, **date**, required: no):
    This field will be automatically populated and is not editable.

### 6. Feedback Time
*   **Feedback Time** (Field 6, **time**, required: no):
    This field will be automatically populated and is not editable.

### 7. Assigned Tool
*   **Assigned Tool** (Field 7, **select_one**, required: no):
    Select the tool that you used for the course, e.g., "chatjimmy".

### 8. Form ID
*   **Form ID** (Field 8, **number**, required: no):
    This field is automatically generated and not editable.

### 9. Created At
*   **Created At** (Field 9, **number**, required: no):
    This field will be automatically populated and is not editable.

### 10. Updated At
*   **Updated At** (Field 10, **number**, required: no):
    This field will be automatically populated and is not editable.

### 11. Submitted At
*   **Submitted At** (Field 11, **number**, required: no):
    This field will be automatically populated and is not editable.

## Tips
*   Please be as specific and detailed as possible when providing your comments and ratings.
*   Select the correct tool that you used for the course to help course administrators track tool usage.
*   You can submit this form at any time, and the automatically populated fields will be updated accordingly.
