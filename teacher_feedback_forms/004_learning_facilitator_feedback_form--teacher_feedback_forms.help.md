# learning_facilitator_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "learning_facilitator_feedback_form" is a form designed to collect feedback from learning facilitators about their experience with teachers in a specific department. This form is used to gather information on the effectiveness of the teacher's teaching style, the quality of the lesson, and the overall satisfaction with the department's management of the learning experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name.
2. Enter the name of the Teacher you are providing feedback about.
3. Provide the Teacher's Title if applicable.
4. Rate your satisfaction with the Teacher's performance on a scale of 1-5.
5. Type any additional comments or feedback you have about the Teacher.
6. Enter your Email address (this will help us contact you if we need more information).
7. If you are providing feedback about a specific department, please enter the name of the Department.
8. Type any additional comments or feedback you have about the department.
9. If you have any further comments, please type them in the text field below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the teacher you are providing feedback about.
* **Teacher Title** (`teacher_name_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the teacher (e.g., Dr., Professor, etc.).
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the teacher's performance on a scale of 1-5.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Type any additional comments or feedback you have about the teacher.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address (this will help us contact you if we need more information).
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): If you are providing feedback about a specific department, enter the name of the department.
* **Comments 2** (`comments_2`, text, optional): Type any additional comments or feedback you have about the department.
* **Department 2** (`department_2`, text, optional): You can enter any additional department-specific comments.
* **Comments 3** (`comments_3`, text, optional): You can enter any final comments or feedback you have about the learning experience.

## Tips
* Please be as specific and detailed as possible when providing feedback.
* You can enter any additional comments or feedback you have at the bottom of the form.
* If you need any further assistance, please do not hesitate to contact us.
