# Substitute Teacher Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Substitute Teacher Reporting Form is a tool designed to gather information from substitutes about their experience and observations when subbing for a regular teacher in a classroom setting. This form will help the absent teacher stay informed and prepared for their return, and also assist in maintaining consistency and quality in the classroom environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete the form in a thorough and honest manner, providing as much detail as possible about your experience as a substitute.
2. Start by filling out the first section on **Lesson Notes**, which provides space to briefly describe the lessons or activities that were covered in class.
3. Move on to the **Classroom Setup** section, where you can describe any issues or challenges you faced with the room or equipment.
4. Next, report on any notable **Student Behaviors** you observed, such as positive comments or concerns.
5. The **Student Attendance** section is where you can note any students who were absent or had other issues that impacted their participation.
6. If you encountered any **Classroom Setup Issues**, please describe them in this section.
7. If you observed any **Student Behavior Issues**, provide a brief explanation of what happened.
8. In **Student Absences**, list any students who were absent during your visit.
9. Finally, provide any additional **Teacher Comments** or **Student Comments** that may be helpful for the absent teacher to know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lesson Notes** (text, optional): A brief description of the lessons or activities that were covered in class.
* **Classroom Setup** (text, optional): Describe any issues or challenges you faced with the room or equipment.
* **Student Behaviors** (text, optional): Report any notable student behaviors you observed, such as positive comments or concerns.
* **Student Attendance** (text, optional): Note any students who were absent or had other issues that impacted their participation.
* **Classroom Setup Issues** (text, optional): Describe any issues or challenges you faced with the room or equipment.
* **Student Behavior Issues** (text, optional): Provide a brief explanation of any student behavior issues you observed.
* **Student Absences** (text, optional): List any students who were absent during your visit.
* **Teacher Comments** (text, optional): Provide any additional comments or information that may be helpful for the absent teacher to know.
* **Student Comments** (text, optional): If you had any interactions or notable comments from students, please include them here.
