# Special Education Teacher Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Special Education Teacher Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from students about their experience with a special education teacher. This form will help the teacher understand their teaching methods and strategies, identify areas for improvement, and make data-driven decisions to enhance their teaching practices.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field. This will help us contact you if we need any additional information.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field. This will help us reach out to you if we need to clarify any feedback.
5. Share your feedback about your experience with the special education teacher in the "Feedback" field.
6. If applicable, select the date and time you had the experience with the teacher.
7. Enter the name of your teacher in the "Teacher" field.
8. If you have any comments or thoughts about the class, enter them in the "Class" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name to provide your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name to provide your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share your thoughts and experiences with the teacher to help them improve.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): If you have a specific date in mind, enter it here. This will help the teacher understand when you had the experience.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): If you have a specific time in mind, enter it here. This will help the teacher understand when you had the experience.
* **Teacher** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your special education teacher.
* **Class** (`class`, text, optional): Share any comments or thoughts about the class you had with the teacher.

## Tips
Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form. Your feedback is crucial in helping special education teachers provide better services to students. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
