# medical_teaching_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for providing feedback on medical teaching sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you are in the correct session.
2. Fill in the relevant fields with as much detail as possible.
3. Use the hints to guide you on what to write.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Medical Teaching Feedback** (`feedback_given`, text, required): Describe what was given to the students during the session.
* **Overall Feedback Given** (`feedback_received`, text, required): Describe the feedback given by the students.
* **Timing** (`feedback_received_time`, date, required): Date and time the feedback was given.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): Your name as a teacher.
* **Medical Teaching Feedback (2)** (`medical_teaching_feedback`, text, optional): Additional feedback on the medical teaching session (optional).
* **Teacher Name Hint** (`teacher_name_hint`, text, optional): A hint for writing your teacher name (optional).
* **Feedback Received Hint** (`feedback_received_hint`, text, optional): A hint for writing the feedback given by students (optional).
* **Teacher Name 2** (`teacher_name_2`, text, optional): Another field for writing teacher name (optional).
* **Feedback Time Hint** (`feedback_time_hint`, text, optional): Another hint for writing the time of feedback (optional).
* **Teacher ID** (`teacher_id`, number, optional): A unique ID for the teacher (optional).
* **Medical Teaching Feedback Form** (`feedback_form_id`, text, required): The unique ID of this feedback form.
* **Teacher Name 3** (`teacher_name_3`, text, optional): Another field for writing teacher name (optional).

## Tips
- Be specific and detailed in your feedback.
- Use the hints to guide you on what to write.
- Only fill in the fields that are relevant to you.
- Save your progress regularly to avoid losing your work.
- Review and revise your work before submitting.
