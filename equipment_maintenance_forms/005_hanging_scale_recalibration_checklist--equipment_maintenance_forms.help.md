# Hanging Scale Recalibration Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information for the recalculation of a hanging scale.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review the form and understand its purpose and the information required.
2.  Ensure you have all the necessary information and authorization to perform the recalculation.
3.  Fill out the form accurately and completely.
4.  Submit the form for review and further action.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### General Information (Page 1)
* **General Information**: This field is for providing any general information about the hanging scale, such as its location or any relevant notes.

### Equipment Identification (Page 1)
* **Equipment Identification**: Enter the specific model number or ID of the hanging scale being recalibrated.

### Current Values (Page 2)
* **Current Values**: Enter the current measurement values of the hanging scale, including any previous recalculation values.

### Recalibration Values (Page 2)
* **Recalibration Values**: Enter the new recalculation values for the hanging scale.

### Recalibration Date (Page 2)
* **Recalibration Date**: Enter the date the hanging scale was last recalibrated.

### Next Recalibration Due (Page 2)
* **Next Recalibration Due**: Enter the next date the hanging scale is due for recalculation.

### Technician Signoff (Page 3)
* **Technician Signoff**: Select 'Yes' if the technician has signed off on the recalculated values.

### Notes (Page 3)
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the recalculation process.

### Email Notifier (Page 3)
* **Email Notifier**: Enter the email address of the person or team who should be notified about the recalculation completion.
Please note that this guide is a general explanation and may need to be adjusted based on the specific implementation and requirements of your organization.
