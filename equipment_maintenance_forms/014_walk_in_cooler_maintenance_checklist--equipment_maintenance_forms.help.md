# Walk In Cooler Maintenance Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help maintenance personnel quickly and effectively assess the status of a walk-in cooler's various components. This checklist covers key aspects, such as compressor condition, condenser coils, and defrost timer settings, among others. By filling out this form, users will be able to identify areas that require attention or repair, facilitating efficient maintenance and extending the lifespan of the walk-in cooler.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the options provided for each field and choose the most accurate answer based on your inspection.
2. Enter the required information for each field.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Walk In Cooler Maintenance Checklist**: This is the title of the form, indicating that this is the main maintenance checklist for the walk-in cooler.
* **Cooler Model**: Enter the model number or name of the walk-in cooler being inspected.
* **Cooler Type**: Identify the type of walk-in cooler (e.g., "1 Door", "2 Door", etc.).
* **Compressor Condition**: Rate the condition of the compressor as either "Good" or "Bad".
* **Condenser Coils Condition**: Rate the condition of the condenser coils as either "Clean" or "Dirty".
* **Evaporator Coils Condition**: Rate the condition of the evaporator coils as either "Good" or "Bad".
* **Defrost Timer**: Select the current defrost timer setting from the available options (1 hour, 2 hours, etc.).
* **Defrost Interval**: Enter the current defrost interval setting.
* **Last Defrost Time**: Record the time of the last defrost cycle.
* **Last Defrost Duration**: Enter the length of the last defrost cycle.
* **Last Defrost Temperature**: Record the temperature reached during the last defrost cycle.
* **Temperature**: Enter the current temperature inside the walk-in cooler.
* **Door Probs**: Enter any issues with the door, such as leaks or misalignment.
* **Door Sensors**: Check if the door sensors are functioning correctly and select "True" or "False" accordingly.
* **Door Hinges**: Rate the condition of the door hinges as either "Good" or "Bad".
* **Drain**: Check if the drain is functioning correctly and select "Good" or "Bad".
* **Electrical Outlets**: Rate the condition of the electrical outlets as either "Good" or "Bad".
* **Fans**: Rate the condition of the fans as either "Good" or "Bad".
* **Floor Drain**: Check if the floor drain is functioning correctly and select "Good" or "Bad".
* **Floor Drain Pump**: Rate the condition of the floor drain pump as either "Good" or "Bad".
* **Gaskets**: Rate the condition of the gaskets as either "Good" or "Bad".
* **Motors**: Rate the condition of the motors as either "Good" or "Bad".
* **Pipes**: Rate the condition of the pipes as either "Good" or "Bad".
* **Pipes Fittings**: Rate the condition of the pipes fittings as either "Good" or "Bad".
* **Refrigerant**: Rate the condition of the refrigerant as either "Good" or "Bad".
