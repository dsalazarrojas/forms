## Purpose
The Hydraulic System Maintenance Log form is used for recording and maintaining a record of regular maintenance activities on hydraulic systems in a facility. The form captures essential information about the equipment, its description, serial number, type, and maintenance details such as the date, technician performing the maintenance, inspection items, and any additional comments. This form aids in keeping a comprehensive record of maintenance activities, making it easier to track and analyze maintenance history and perform future repairs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Hydraulic System Maintenance Log form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the equipment serial number of the hydraulic system being maintained.
2.  Provide a brief description of the hydraulic system in the designated field.
3.  Select the correct type of equipment being maintained.
4.  Specify the date of the maintenance activity.
5.  Choose the person responsible for performing the maintenance activity (Technician or Team).
6.  Identify all items checked during the inspection (select multiple items).
7.  Add any additional comments about the maintenance activity.
8.  Enter your signature for verification and record-keeping purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Equipment Serial Number** (`equipment_serial_number`, text, required): Enter the serial number of the hydraulic system being maintained. This is a unique identifier that helps track and distinguish between different systems.
*   **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, text, required): Choose the type or category of the hydraulic equipment being maintained. This field helps to categorize and filter the maintenance data for easier analysis.
*   **Hydraulic System Description** (`hydraulic_system_description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the hydraulic system, including its purpose and usage. This description will aid in quick identification and referencing of the system.
*   **Maintenance Date** (`maintenance_date`, date, required): Enter the exact date of the maintenance activity. This is essential for tracking and recording the maintenance timeline.
*   **Maintenance By** (`maintenance_by`, select_one, required): Select whether the maintenance activity was performed by a Technician or Team member. This field is vital for accountability and tracking who performed the maintenance.
*   **Inspection Item** (`inspection_item`, select_multiple, required): Select multiple items from the list that were inspected or maintained during the activity. This field allows for comprehensive recording of all inspection items.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional information or comments about the maintenance activity, such as the reasons for maintenance or observations.
*   **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Sign and date the form to authenticate and record the completion of the maintenance activity.

## Tips

*   Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
*   Review the form before submission to ensure that all necessary information is included.
*   Regularly review and update the form to maintain a comprehensive and accurate record of maintenance activities.
*   Consider using the form's digital version to facilitate easier data entry, storage, and analysis.
