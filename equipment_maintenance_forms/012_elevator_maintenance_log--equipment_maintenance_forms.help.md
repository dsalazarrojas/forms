# elevator_maintenance_log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The elevator_maintenance_log form is used to track maintenance activities for elevators in a facility. It collects information on the last service date, next service due date, and any notes or observations related to the elevator's maintenance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Select the relevant information for each field.
2. Fill in the Last Service Date with the date the elevator was last serviced.
3. Enter the Next Service Due date, which is the scheduled date for the next service.
4. Provide any additional Notes or observations about the elevator's condition.
5. Sign the Form Signature field to indicate that the form has been reviewed and approved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Header** (`form_header`, text, optional): Enter a header or title for the form, if desired.
* **facility_info** (`facility_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the facility where the elevator is located.
* **Elevator Make** (`elevator_make`, text, optional): Enter the make of the elevator, if known.
* **Elevator Model** (`elevator_model`, text, optional): Enter the model of the elevator, if known.
* **Elevator Location** (`elevator_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the elevator within the facility.
* **Last Service Date** (`last_service_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the elevator was last serviced.
* **Next Service Due** (`next_service_due`, date, optional): Enter the scheduled date for the next service.
* **Service History** (`service_history`, text, optional): Enter any relevant notes or comments about the elevator's service history.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the elevator's condition.
* **Form Signature** (`form_signature`, note, optional): Sign the form to indicate that it has been reviewed and approved.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for the Last Service Date and Next Service Due fields.
* If unsure about any of the fields, refer to the facility's maintenance records or consult with a supervisor.
* Use the Service History field to document any notable issues or repairs performed on the elevator.
* Review and sign the Form Signature field to ensure that the form is complete and accurate.
