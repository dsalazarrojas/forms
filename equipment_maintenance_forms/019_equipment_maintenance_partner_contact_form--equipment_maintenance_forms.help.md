# equipment_maintenance_partner_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The equipment maintenance partner contact form is designed to collect contact information and details about partners for equipment maintenance. This form helps to ensure that all necessary contact information is collected for future reference and to streamline communication with partners.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the contact information of the partner, including their name, phone number, email address, and address.
2. Select the services offered by the partner, whether it's maintenance, repair, or both.
3. Provide a date and time for the availability of the partner.
4. Add any additional notes or comments.
5. Select the current submission status of the form, whether it's pending or approved.
6. Add any additional notes or comments if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (Page 1)**:
	+ **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person.
	+ **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
	+ **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
	+ **Contact Address**: Enter the physical address of the contact person.
* **Partner Form (Page 2)**:
	+ **Service Coverage**: Select the services offered by the partner, whether it's maintenance, repair, or both.
* **Availability (Page 3)**:
	+ **Availability**: Select a date and time for the availability of the partner.
	+ **Availability Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the partner's availability.
* **Submission Status (Page 4)**:
	+ **Submission Status**: Select the current submission status of the form, whether it's pending or approved.
* **Additional Notes (Page 5)**:
	+ **Additional Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the partner.
* **Notes (Page 6)**:
	+ **Notes**: Add any general notes or comments about the partner.
