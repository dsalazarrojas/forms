# Weekly Equipment Maintenance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weekly Equipment Maintenance Checklist is a form used to track and manage equipment maintenance activities. This form helps ensure that equipment is maintained regularly, reducing the risk of downtime and improving overall equipment health.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of maintenance activity being performed (Maintenance activity, Repair, or Inspect).
2. Enter the Equipment ID, which is a unique identifier for the equipment being maintained.
3. Choose the category of equipment (General, HVAC, Electrical, Plumbing, or Piping).
4. Select the date of the maintenance activity.
5. Enter any comments or notes related to the maintenance activity.
6. Specify who is assigned to perform the maintenance.
7. Choose the condition of the equipment (Good, Bad, Needs Repair, or Needs Inspection).
8. Select the maintenance priority (High, Medium, or Low).
9. Set the maintenance status (Completed, Pending, Ongoing).
10. Choose the next maintenance schedule (Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Never).
11. Add any additional notes or comments.
12. Upload any photos or attachments related to the maintenance activity.
13. Set the maintenance scheduling (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly).
14. Enter the date of the last maintenance scheduled.
15. Count the number of equipment items.
16. Choose the maintenance frequency (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Never).
17. Enter the date of the last maintenance performed.
18. Enter the name of the tool assigned to the maintenance activity.
19. Enter the location of the equipment.
20. Enter any remarks or comments.
21. Enter the type of equipment.
22. Enter the name of the person who performed the last maintenance.
23. Enter any additional remarks.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Maintenance Activity
* **Maintenance Activity** (`select_one`): Select the type of maintenance activity being performed (Maintenance activity, Repair, or Inspect).

### Equipment ID
* **Equipment ID** (`text`): Enter the unique identifier for the equipment being maintained.

### Equipment Category
* **Equipment Category** (`select_multiple`): Choose the category of equipment (General, HVAC, Electrical, Plumbing, or Piping).

### Maintenance Date
* **Maintenance Date** (`date`): Select the date of the maintenance activity.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`note`): Enter any comments or notes related to the maintenance activity.

### Assigned To
* **Assigned To** (`text`): Enter the name of the person assigned to perform the maintenance activity.

### Maintenance By

### Maintenance Status
