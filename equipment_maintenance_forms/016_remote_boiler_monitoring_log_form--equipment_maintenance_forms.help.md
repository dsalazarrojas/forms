The purpose of this form is to record and track the remote monitoring data of a boiler. The form is used by maintenance teams to log temperature and pressure readings from the boiler system, ensuring that the equipment is properly maintained and operating within safe parameters. It's crucial for the maintenance teams to accurately report these readings to guarantee the reliability and efficiency of the boiler system.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

### How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all required fields for each page.
2. Select "location" and enter the specific location where the remote boiler monitoring system is located.
3. Record the temperature reading for the boiler in the "temperature_reading" field.
4. Record the pressure reading for the boiler in the "pressure_reading" field.
5. Enter the date and time of the reading in the "date_and_time" field.
6. Add any additional comments or notes about the boiler's condition in the "notes" field.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Location** (location, text, required): Enter the specific location where the remote boiler monitoring system is installed. This could be a building, plant, or other facility.
* **Temperature Reading** (temperature_reading, number, required): Record the current temperature reading of the boiler, typically in degrees Celsius or Fahrenheit.
* **Pressure Reading** (pressure_reading, number, required): Record the current pressure reading of the boiler, typically in pounds per square inch (PSI) or other relevant units.
* **Date and Time** (date_and_time, date, required): Enter the date and time when the readings were taken.
* **Notes** (notes, note, required): Add any comments or notes about the boiler's condition, including any issues or concerns that need to be addressed.

### Tips

* Make sure to report accurate and reliable data for each field.
* Keep the "notes" field concise and focused on essential comments.
* Use the correct units for the temperature and pressure readings.
* Review and update this form regularly to ensure that the boiler system is properly maintained.
