1. Review the form and ensure that you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Enter the medical device information, including model, serial number, and manufacturer.
3. Document the service history, including the maintenance date and time.
4. Enter the maintenance records, including any relevant notes or comments.
5. Select the person who completed the maintenance task and the person assigned to the device.
6. Enter the date and time the maintenance was completed.
