# ROV Pre Launch Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ROV Pre Launch Checklist is a form designed to be used by ROV crew members to ensure that all necessary preparations are made before a mission. This form helps guarantee that the crew and equipment are ready for a safe and successful operation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure that you understand the purpose and requirements.
2. Answer each field according to the instructions provided.
3. Make sure to review the form thoroughly before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Deployment Preparations** (`1`, text, optional): A text field where you can describe any preparation you have made for the deployment, such as checking the weather forecast, reviewing the navigation plan, or performing routine maintenance on the ROV.
* **Equipment Status** (`2`, select multiple, optional): Select all equipment that are currently active or inactive, such as propulsion, navigation, communication, etc.
* **Crew Training** (`3`, select one, optional): Select either 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the crew has received training on the specific ROV procedures and protocols.
* **Emergency Procedure** (`4`, text, optional): A text field where you can describe the emergency procedures that you have reviewed and understood.
* **Weather Conditions** (`5`, select one, optional): Select the current weather conditions, such as 'Option 1', 'Option 2', or 'Option 3', to indicate the conditions that you expect during the mission.
* **Navigation Planning** (`6`, date, optional): Enter the date when the navigation plan was reviewed and updated.
* **Communication Planning** (`7`, select multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether communication plans are in place, such as radio checks and communication protocols.
* **Safety Equipment** (`8`, select one, optional): Select either 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether all necessary safety equipment is available and accounted for.
* **Emergency Response** (`9`, text, optional): A text field where you can describe the emergency response procedures that you have reviewed and understood.
* **Review and Approval** (`10`, date, optional): Enter the date when this checklist was reviewed and approved by the mission leader or designated authority.
* **Comments** (`11`, text, optional): A text field where you can add any additional comments or notes that are relevant to the mission.

Note: All fields are optional unless specified as required. It is essential to complete this form carefully and accurately to ensure a safe and successful mission.
