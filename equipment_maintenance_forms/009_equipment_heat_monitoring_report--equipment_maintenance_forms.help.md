# equipment_heat_monitoring_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The equipment heat monitoring report form is a critical document used for tracking and analyzing temperature-related issues with equipment in facilities. This form collects essential information to aid facility management and maintenance teams in identifying trends, preventing future occurrences, and making informed decisions for maintenance and repairs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Issue Status" dropdown menu and choose the correct severity level for the issue.
2. Fill in the "Issue Description" field with a brief summary of the problem.
3. Enter the "Issue Date" and "Issue Time" fields with the exact dates and times the issue was observed.
4. In the "Issue Evidence" field, provide any supporting photographs or descriptions of the problem.
5. Enter the "Asset Name", "Asset Location", "Asset Temperature", and "Issue Temperature" fields with accurate information about the affected equipment.
6. Choose the "Issue Priority" level from the dropdown menu.
7. Select the "Issue Type" from the dropdown menu.
8. Enter the "Facility Name" and "Facility Location" fields with the relevant facility details.
9. Enter the "Issue Reporter" field with your name or identifier.
10. Enter the "Issue Responsible" field with the name or identifier of the person responsible for handling the issue.
11. (Optional) Provide any additional information in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Issue Status**: Choose the correct severity level for the issue from the dropdown menu.
* **Issue Description**: Provide a brief summary of the problem.
* **Issue Date**: Enter the exact date the issue was observed.
* **Issue Time**: Enter the exact time the issue was observed.
* **Issue Evidence**: Upload or describe any supporting evidence or photographs of the problem.
* **Asset Name**: Enter the name of the affected equipment.
* **Asset Location**: Enter the location of the affected equipment.
* **Asset Temperature**: Enter the current temperature reading of the affected equipment.
* **Issue Temperature**: Enter the temperature reading associated with the issue.
* **Issue Priority**: Choose the correct priority level for the issue from the dropdown menu.
* **Issue Type**: Select the correct type of issue from the dropdown menu.
* **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the facility where the issue occurred.
* **Facility Location**: Enter the location of the facility where the issue occurred.
* **Issue Reporter**: Enter your name or identifier.
* **Issue Responsible**: Enter the name or identifier of the person responsible for handling the issue.
* **Issue Reviewed**: (Optional) If the issue has been reviewed, indicate yes or no.
* **Issue Closed**: (Optional) If the issue has been closed, indicate yes or no.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional information or comments about the issue.
