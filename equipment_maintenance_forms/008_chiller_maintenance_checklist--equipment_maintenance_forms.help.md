# Chiller Maintenance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chiller Maintenance Checklist form is designed to aid technicians in completing a comprehensive assessment of a chiller system's condition. This form ensures that all necessary checks are performed and documented for routine maintenance, quarterly inspections, and annual maintenance of chillers.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Chiller Maintenance Checklist form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Service Information** section with details about the service visit.
2. Select the **Type of Service** from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter the **Technician Name** and **Technician ID Number**.
4. Enter the **Service Company Name**.
5. Fill in the **Customer Information** section with site details.
6. Enter the **Customer Name**, **Site Address**, and **Site Contact Person** and **Site Contact Phone**.
7. Fill in the **Chiller Information** section with chiller details.
8. Perform and document the **Electrical System Checks** (voltage, amperage, contactor checks).
9. Perform and document the **Mechanical System Checks** (compressor inspection, oil level, refrigerant level, belt inspections).
10. Document the **Cleaning Tasks** (condenser coil cleaning, evaporator coil cleaning, equipment area cleaning).
11. Record **Operational Readings** (suction pressure, discharge pressure, evaporator temperature, condenser temperature).
12. Summarize **Findings and Recommendations**.
13. Document the **Overall Equipment Status**.
14. Document any **Issues Found During Service** and **Recommendations Made**.
15. List any **Parts Replaced**.
16. Enter the **Recommended Next Service Date**.
17. Sign and date the **Technician Signature**.
18. Sign and date the **Customer Acknowledgment**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Service Information (Required: False)
* **Service Information**: Details about this maintenance visit.
	+ A note-type field for technicians to record notes and comments about the service visit.

### Service Date (Required: True)
* **Service Date**: Date of the maintenance visit.
	+ A date field where technicians should enter the date of the visit.

### Type of Service (Required: True)
* **Type of Service**: Type of maintenance or inspection performed.
	+ A dropdown menu to select the type of service (routine, quarterly, annual, etc.).

### Technician Name (Required: True)
* **Technician Name**: Name of the technician performing the service.
	+ A text field for the technician's name.
