# Fire Safety Equipment Maintenance Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report the maintenance status of fire safety equipment in a site. It helps ensure that the equipment is properly maintained and compliant with safety regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and submit the report. The form is divided into several sections:

1. Site Name: Enter the name of the site where the fire safety equipment is located.
2. Equipment Type: Identify the type of equipment being maintained (e.g., fire extinguisher, sprinkler system).
3. Equipment ID: Enter the unique ID of the equipment being reported on.
4. Last Inspection Date: Record the date the equipment was last inspected or checked.
5. Maintenance Due Date: Enter the date the equipment is due for maintenance.
6. Last Maintenance Date: Record the date the equipment was last maintained.
7. Next Maintenance Date: Enter the date the equipment is due for next maintenance.
8. Maintenance Status: Select the current status of the equipment (Active, Due, Completed, or Overdue).
9. Notes: Add any additional comments or notes about the equipment.
10. Email: Enter your contact email address if you want to be contacted for follow-up.
