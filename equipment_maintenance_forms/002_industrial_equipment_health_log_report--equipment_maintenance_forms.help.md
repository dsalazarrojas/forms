# industrial_equipment_health_log_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The industrial equipment health log report form is designed to gather and record information about the maintenance and inspection history of industrial equipment. This form helps track the status of equipment, identify potential issues, and schedule future inspections.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select the equipment you are logging the health report for**:
	* Choose the industrial equipment you are reporting on from the available options.
2. **Update the equipment status**:
	* Choose the current status of the equipment (Active or Inactive).
3. **Record the last inspection date**:
	* Enter the date of the last inspection or maintenance performed on the equipment.
4. **Schedule the next inspection**:
	* Enter the date when the next inspection or maintenance is due.
5. **Document any issues or problems**:
	* Describe any issues or problems encountered during the inspection or maintenance.
6. **Indicate if there is an issue reported**:
	* Check if there is an issue or problem with the equipment that needs to be addressed.
7. **Select the priority of the issue**:
	* Choose a priority level for the issue, if applicable.
8. **Assign a team member to address the issue**:
	* Check if a team member is assigned to handle the issue or problem.
9. **Add any comments or notes**:
	* Provide any additional comments or notes about the issue or problem.
10. **Add any additional notes**:
	* Provide any other relevant notes about the equipment or issue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header** (`form_header`, text, optional): This field is not used in the form's functionality, but provides a title for the form header section.
* **Equipment Name** (`equipment_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the industrial equipment being logged.
* **Equipment Description** (`equipment_description`, text, optional): Describe the type or purpose of the equipment.
* **Equipment Status** (`equipment_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the current status of the equipment (Active or Inactive).
* **Last Inspection Date** (`last_inspection_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the last inspection or maintenance performed on the equipment.
* **Next Inspection Due Date** (`next_inspection_due_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the next inspection or maintenance is due.
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, text, optional): Describe any issues or problems encountered during the inspection or maintenance.
* **Issue Reported** (`issue_reported`, select_multiple, optional): Check if there is an issue or problem with the equipment that needs to be addressed.
* **Priority** (`priority`, number, optional): Choose a priority level for the issue, if applicable.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, optional): Check if a team member is assigned to handle the issue or problem.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about the issue or problem.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any other relevant notes about the equipment or issue.
* **Priority** (`priority`, number, optional): This field is repeated, please ignore.
