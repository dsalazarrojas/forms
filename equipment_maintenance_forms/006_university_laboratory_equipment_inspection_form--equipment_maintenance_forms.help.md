# University Laboratory Equipment Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for inspecting and maintaining university laboratory equipment. It helps track the status of equipment, identify maintenance needs, and record inspection dates.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form for each piece of equipment in your care.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed before proceeding.
3. Enter the correct equipment ID to identify the equipment being inspected.
4. Select the correct equipment type (e.g., pending, completed, not needed).
5. Describe the equipment condition using the provided options (e.g., ประก, good, fair, poor).
6. Determine if maintenance is needed, and select the correct status (e.g., pending, completed, not needed).
7. Enter the last maintenance date and the next inspection date, if applicable.
8. Specify the equipment location and the person responsible for it.
9. Add any additional comments or remarks about the equipment in the dedicated fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Equipment ID** (`equipment_id`): Enter the unique ID of the equipment being inspected.
  - Enter the text-based ID provided by the equipment manufacturer or inventory system.
- **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`): Select the type of equipment being inspected.
  - Choose from the options: pending, completed, not needed.
- **Equipment Condition** (`equipment_condition`): Describe the condition of the equipment.
  - Select from the options: ประก, good, fair, poor.
- **Maintenance Needed** (`maintenance_needed`): Determine if maintenance is required.
  - Select from the options: pending, completed, not needed.
- **Maintenance Status** (`maintenance_status`): Update the maintenance status.
  - Select from the options: pending, completed, not needed.
- **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`): Record the date of the current inspection.
  - Enter the date in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
- **Inspection Time** (`inspection_time`): Record the time of the current inspection.
  - Enter the time in the format HH:MM TT.
- **Inspector ID** (`inspector_id`): Enter the ID of the person conducting the inspection.
  - This may be your employee ID or a unique identifier.
- **Equipment Model** (`equipment_model`): Enter the equipment model.
  - Enter the model number or name as provided by the manufacturer.
- **Equipment Brand** (`equipment_brand`): Enter the equipment brand.
  - Enter the brand name as provided by the manufacturer.
- **Equipment Serial Number** (`equipment_serial_number`): Enter the equipment serial number.
  - Enter the serial number as provided by the manufacturer.
- **Last Maintenance** (`last_maintenance`): Record the date of the last maintenance.
  - Enter the date in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
- **Next Inspection Date** (`next_inspection_date`): Record the date of the next scheduled inspection.
  - Enter the date in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
- **Equipment Location** (`equipment_location`): Specify the location of the equipment.
  - Enter the room number, department, or floor where the equipment is kept.
- **Responsible Person** (`responsible_person`): Enter the person responsible for the equipment.
  - Enter the name and contact information of the person in charge.
- **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional comments or remarks about the equipment.
  - Use these fields to note any issues, comments, or recommendations.
- **Remark 1** (`remarks1`): Additional comments or remarks about the equipment.
  - Enter any specific observations or notes about the equipment.
- **Remark 2** (`remarks2`): Further comments or remarks about the equipment.
  - Add any additional notes or comments.
- **Remark 3** (`remarks3`): More comments or remarks about the equipment.
  - Enter any last-minute notes or recommendations.
- **Remark 4** (`remarks4`): Additional comments or remarks about the equipment.
  - Enter any final thoughts or observations.
- **Remark 5** (`remarks5`): More comments or remarks about the equipment.
  - Add any last-minute notes or comments.
- **Remark 6** (`remarks6`): Comments or remarks about the equipment.
  - Enter any final thoughts or observations.
- **Remark 7** (`remarks7`): Final comments or remarks about the equipment.
  - Enter any last-minute notes or comments.
- **Remark 8** (`remarks8`): Last comments or remarks about the equipment.
  - Add any final thoughts or observations.
