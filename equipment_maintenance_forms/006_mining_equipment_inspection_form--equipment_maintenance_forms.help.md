# Mining Equipment Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mining Equipment Inspection Form is used to record and track the maintenance and inspection history of mining equipment. This helps ensure that equipment is regularly checked, maintained, and inspected, reducing the risk of downtime and improving overall equipment efficiency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Equipment ID and Location fields with relevant information.
2. Provide a brief Description of the equipment.
3. Record the Date Last Maintenance and specify the next maintenance Due Date.
4. Select the Equipment Status and choose whether the equipment is Running, Maintenance Required, or Offline.
5. For each piece of equipment, choose the Maintenance Frequency and Inspection Frequency from the provided options.
6. Record the costs associated with Last Maintenance and Next Maintenance.
7. Add any additional Notes for Maintenance Frequency, Inspection Frequency, Next Maintenance, and Next Inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Equipment ID**: A unique identifier for each piece of equipment.
* **Location**: Where the equipment is located, either On-site or Off-site.
* **Description**: A brief description of the equipment.
* **Date Last Maintenance**: Record the date of the last maintenance.
* **Next Maintenance Due**: Date of the next maintenance scheduled.
* **Equipment Status**: Select the current status of the equipment, either Running, Maintenance Required, or Offline.
* **Maintenance Frequency**: Select the maintenance frequency for the equipment, such as Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
* **Last Maintenance Cost**: Cost of the last maintenance.
* **Next Maintenance Cost**: Estimated cost of the next maintenance.
* **Maintenance Frequency Notes**: Any additional notes regarding the maintenance frequency.
* **Inspection Frequency**: Select the inspection frequency for the equipment, such as Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
* **Inspection Frequency Notes**: Any additional notes regarding the inspection frequency.
* **Next Maintenance Notes**: Any notes on the next maintenance.
* **Equipment Model**: Model of the equipment.
* **Manufacturer**: Manufacturer of the equipment.
* **Serial Number**: Serial number of the equipment.
* **Next Inspection Due**: Date of the next inspection scheduled.
* **Maintenance Location**: Location where the equipment will be maintained, either On-site or Off-site.
