# Factory Maintenance Scheduling Device Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Factory Maintenance Scheduling Device Order Form is used to schedule maintenance for devices in a factory setting. This form ensures that equipment is properly maintained to prevent downtime and increase productivity.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the contact details section with your name, contact number, and email address.
2. Select the equipment needed for maintenance from the list.
3. Choose the maintenance scheduling type from the available options.
4. Add any additional notes or comments regarding the maintenance.
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, optional): Enter your name, contact number, and email address so that we can get in touch with you regarding your maintenance request.
* **Equipment Needed** (`equipment_needed`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the equipment that requires maintenance. Select multiple options if you need to schedule maintenance for multiple pieces of equipment.
* **Maintenance Scheduling** (`maintenance_scheduling`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of maintenance you need for your chosen equipment (e.g., routine, emergency, or scheduled maintenance).
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes regarding the maintenance. This can include specific maintenance instructions or any other relevant information.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct equipment and maintenance type to ensure that your maintenance request is processed accurately.
* If you have multiple equipment that require maintenance, select all relevant options for the "Equipment Needed" field.
* If you need to schedule maintenance for a specific date or time, please include this information in the "Notes" field.
