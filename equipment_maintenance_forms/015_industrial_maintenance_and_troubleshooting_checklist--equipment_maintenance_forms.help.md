# industrial_maintenance_and_troubleshooting_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The industrial maintenance and troubleshooting checklist is a form designed for documenting and addressing technical issues related to equipment in industrial settings. It helps to ensure that maintenance tasks are properly recorded and communicated, promoting efficient and effective troubleshooting and maintenance activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the troubleshooting category that best describes the issue (Equipment, Electrical, or Mechanical).
2. List the types of equipment involved (Pump, Compressor, or Generator).
3. Provide a brief description of the issue.
4. Enter the maintenance date, start time, and end time if applicable.
5. Add any relevant notes about the issue or maintenance activity.
6. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Troubleshooting Category** (`troubleshooting_category`, select_one, required/optional): Select the category that best describes the issue (e.g., Equipment, Electrical, or Mechanical).
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, select_multiple, required/optional): List the types of equipment involved in the issue (e.g., Pump, Compressor, or Generator).
* **Description** (`description`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the issue.
* **Maintenance Date** (`maintenance_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when the maintenance activity was performed.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time when the maintenance activity started.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time when the maintenance activity ended.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Add any relevant notes about the issue or maintenance activity.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
