# Bill Acceptor Troubleshooting Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to standardize the troubleshooting process for bill acceptors, guiding technicians through a systematic approach to identify and resolve issues. It helps in recording and tracking problems, ensuring better maintenance and quality control.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your details as a service technician.
2.  Fill in the required fields, such as technician name, ID, and location information.
3.  Describe the reported problem and select the relevant issue from the dropdown list.
4.  Complete each troubleshooting step, checking relevant boxes or selecting issues found.
5.  Report test results and any parts replaced.
6.  Provide additional notes and follow-up requirements (if any).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Technician Name** (`technician_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as the service technician.
*   **Technician ID** (`technician_id`, text, required): Enter your employee or contractor ID number.
*   **Service Date** (`service_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the troubleshooting session.
*   **Service Start Time** (`service_time_start`, time, required): Record the start time of the service.
*   **Service End Time** (`service_time_end`, time, required): Record the completion time of the service.
*   **Location Information** (`location_information`, note, optional): Enter any additional location details.
*   **Location Name** (`location_name`, text, required): Enter the business or facility name.
*   **Location Address** (`location_address`, text, required): Enter the full street address.
*   **Machine ID** (`machine_id`, text, required): Enter the vending or gaming machine identifier.
*   **Bill Acceptor Model** (`bill_acceptor_model`, text, required): Enter the manufacturer and model number of the bill acceptor.
*   **Bill Acceptor Serial Number** (`bill_acceptor_serial`, text, required): Enter the serial number of the bill acceptor unit.
*   **Issue Identification** (`issue_identification`, note, optional): Describe the reported problem.
*   **Reported Issue** (`reported_issue`, select_one, required): Select the reported problem from the dropdown list, such as Bills Not Accepted, Bills Jamming, etc.
*   **Detailed Issue Description** (`issue_description`, text, required): Provide more details about the problem.
*   **Troubleshooting Checklist** (`troubleshooting_checklist`, note, optional): Complete each troubleshooting step (see below).
*   **Check Power Connection** (`check_power_connection`, select_one, required): Verify the power cable is secure. Options: Checked - OK, Checked - Issue Found, Not Applicable.
*   **Inspect Bill Path** (`check_bill_path`, select_one, required): Check for debris or obstructions. Options: Clean - No Issues, Debris Found - Cleaned, Damaged - Needs Replacement.
*   **Check Sensors** (`check_sensors`, select_one, required): Verify sensor cleanliness and function. Options: Clean and Functional, Dirty - Cleaned, Malfunctioning - Replaced.
*   **Check Bill Stacker** (`check_stacker`, select_one, required): Inspect stacker mechanism. Options: Working Properly, Jammed - Cleared, Damaged - Needs Repair.
*   **Check Firmware** (`check_firmware`, select_one, required): Verify firmware is up-to-date. Options: Up to Date, Updated During Service, Update Failed.
*   **Test Results** (`test_results`, select_one, required): Report test results. Options: All Tests Passed, Some Issues Remain, Requires Additional Service.
*   **Denominations Tested** (`bills_tested`, select_multiple, required): Select which bills were tested. Options: $1, $5, $10, $20, $50, $100.
*   **Resolution Status** (`resolution`, select_one, required): Determine if the issue was resolved. Options: Fully Resolved, Partially Resolved, Not Resolved - Parts Needed, Not Resolved - Escalated.
*   **Parts Replaced** (`parts_replaced`, text, optional): If applicable, list any parts replaced.
*   **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Provide any other observations or recommendations.
*   **Follow-up Required** (`follow_up_required`, select_one, required): Determine if additional service is needed. Options: No Follow-up Needed, Follow-up Scheduled, Customer to Monitor, Escalate to Manufacturer.

**Tips**: Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that any issues are properly documented and resolved.
