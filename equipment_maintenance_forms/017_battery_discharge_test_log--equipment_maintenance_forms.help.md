# Battery Discharge Test Log - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to record the results of a battery discharge test. The purpose of this test is to evaluate the performance of a battery under a controlled load, typically to assess its capacity and overall health. By recording the results of this test, technicians can identify potential issues with a battery and ensure that it meets the required standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information:
	* `Test ID` (unique identifier for the test)
	* `Test Date` (date of the test)
	* `Start Time` (time the test began)
	* `Technician Name` (name of the technician who performed the test)
	* `Battery ID` (identifier for the battery being tested)
	* `Battery Type` (type of battery, e.g., Lithium-Ion, Lead-Acid, etc.)
	* `Battery Capacity` (rated capacity of the battery)
	* `Nominal Voltage` (rated voltage of the battery)
	* `Serial Number` (manufacturer's serial number of the battery)
	* `Battery Age` (age of the battery)
	* `Initial Voltage Reading` (initial voltage reading before discharge)
	* `Initial Temperature` (initial temperature of the battery in Celsius)
	* `Discharge Rate` (current draw during the test)
	* `Load Applied` (test load)
	* `Cutoff Voltage` (end voltage threshold)
	* `Final Voltage Reading` (final voltage reading after discharge)
	* `Final Temperature` (final temperature of the battery in Celsius)
	* `Discharge Duration` (duration of the test)
	* `Capacity Delivered` (actual capacity of the battery)
	* `Test Result` (pass or fail result of the test)
	* `Test Equipment Used` (equipment ID)
2. Select the type of battery and age from the provided options
3. Enter any anomalies observed during the test in the `Anomalies Observed` field (optional)
4. Review and submit the form

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Test ID**: Unique identifier for the test, used to reference the test in future queries.
* **Test Date**: Date the test was performed.
* **Start Time**: Time the test began.
* **Technician Name**: Name of the person who performed the test.
* **Battery ID**: Identifier for the battery being tested.
* **Battery Type**: Type of battery, e.g., Lithium-Ion, Lead-Acid, etc.
* **Battery Capacity**: Rated capacity of the battery.
* **Nominal Voltage**: Rated voltage of the battery.
* **Serial Number**: Manufacturer's serial number of the battery.
* **Battery Age**: Age of the battery, with options for new (less than 6 months), 6 months to 1 year, 1 to 2 years, 2 to 3 years, or over 3 years.
* **Initial Voltage Reading**: Initial voltage reading before discharge.
* **Initial Temperature**: Initial temperature of the battery in Celsius.
* **Discharge Rate**: Current draw during the test.
* **Load Applied**: Test load.
* **Cutoff Voltage**: End voltage threshold for the test.
* **Final Voltage Reading**: Final voltage reading after discharge.
* **Final Temperature**: Final temperature of the battery in Celsius.
* **Discharge Duration**: Duration of the test.
* **Capacity Delivered**: Actual capacity of the battery.
* **Test Result**: Pass or fail result of the test.
* **Test Equipment Used**: Equipment ID used for the test.
* **Anomalies Observed**: Any issues or anomalies observed during the test (optional).
* **Equipment Used**: Equipment ID used for the test.

Note: This form is designed to be used for recording the results of a battery discharge test. Completing this form will help ensure accurate and consistent tracking of test results.
