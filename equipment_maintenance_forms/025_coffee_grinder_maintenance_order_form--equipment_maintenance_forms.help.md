# Coffee Grinder Maintenance Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form gathers information needed for efficient and effective maintenance and service of your coffee grinder. Please fill out this form accurately and completely.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Gather the required information from the customer, including name, email, phone number, and other contact details.
2.  Complete the grinder information section with details on the grinder's brand, model, and age.
3.  Describe the issue or problem with the grinder and select the primary service needed.
4.  Specify the preferred service date and time, and your contact method for scheduling.
5.  Optionally, select whether you need a loaner grinder during service.
6.  Review the form before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Full Name** (customer_name, text, required): Fill out the customer's name as it appears on their ID or business card.
- **Email Address** (email, email, required): Enter the customer's email address for communication.
- **Phone Number** (phone, text, required): Enter the customer's phone number for communication.
- **Phone Number** (phone, text, required): Repeat the customer's phone number for backup.
- **Café or Business Name** (business_name, text, required): Enter the name of the business or establishment.
- **Service Address** (address, text, required): Enter the address of the business location.
- **Grinder Brand** (grinder_brand, select_one, required): Select the manufacturer of the coffee grinder.
- **Grinder Model** (grinder_model, text, required): Enter the model number of the grinder.
- **Grinder Age** (grinder_age, select_one, required): Select the approximate age of the grinder.
- **Daily Usage Frequency** (usage_frequency, select_one, required): Select how often the grinder is used.
- **Service Needed** (service_type, select_one, required): Select the primary service needed.
- **Problem Description** (problem_description, text, required): Describe the issue with the grinder in detail.
- **Issue Severity** (issue_severity, select_one, required): Select the severity of the issue.
- **Current Symptoms** (current_symptoms, select_multiple, optional): Check any symptoms currently experienced with the grinder.
- **Preferred Service Date** (preferred_service_date, date, required): Select the preferred date for service.
- **Preferred Service Time** (service_window, select_one, required): Select the preferred time for service.
- **Service Urgency** (urgency, select_one, required): Select how soon the service is needed.
- **Do you need a loaner grinder** (loaner_grinder, select_one, optional): Check whether a loaner grinder is needed during service.
- **Interested in Replacement** (replacement_option, select_one, optional): Check whether you're interested in replacing the grinder if not repairable.
- **Best Contact Method** (best_contact_method, select_one, required): Select the best way to reach you for scheduling service.
- **Special Instructions** (special_instructions, text, optional): Enter any special instructions or information for the service tech. 

This form ensures accurate and efficient gathering and processing of service and maintenance requests for coffee grinders.
