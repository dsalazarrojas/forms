# Laser Quality Check Confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to confirm the quality of laser equipment after an inspection. It helps ensure that the equipment is in good working order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of equipment being inspected from the "Equipment Type" dropdown menu. Choose from options like "Laser Cutter", "Laser Engraver", or "Laser Etcher". If the equipment type is not listed, choose "Other" to provide a description.
2. Enter the name of the equipment being inspected in the "Equipment Name" field.
3. Provide the serial number of the equipment, if applicable.
4. Enter the date of the inspection using the date picker.
5. Select the inspection result from the "Inspection Result" dropdown menu. Choose from options like "Passed", "Failed", or "In Progress".
6. Enter any remarks or comments about the inspection in the "Remarks" field.
7. Schedule a follow-up inspection using the "Follow-up" date picker, if required.
8. Enter the name of the person who performed the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equipment Type**: Select the type of laser equipment being inspected. Choose from options like "Laser Cutter", "Laser Engraver", or "Laser Etcher". If the equipment type is not listed, choose "Other" to provide a description.
* **Equipment Name**: Enter the name of the laser equipment being inspected.
* **Serial Number**: Enter the serial number of the laser equipment, if applicable.
* **Date of Inspection**: Enter the date of the inspection using the date picker.
* **Inspection Result**: Select the outcome of the inspection from options like "Passed", "Failed", or "In Progress".
* **Remarks**: Enter any comments or remarks about the inspection.
* **Follow-up**: Schedule a follow-up inspection, if required.
* **Checked by**: Enter the name of the person who performed the inspection.
