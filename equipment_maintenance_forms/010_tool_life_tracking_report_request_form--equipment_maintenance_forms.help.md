# Tool Life Tracking Report Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tool Life Tracking Report Request Form is used to request a tool life tracking report for equipment with specific status and usage details. This form is intended to be filled out by the equipment owner or authorized personnel to request a report on the tool's condition and usage history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
2. Select the correct equipment status from the dropdown menu.
3. Provide any additional usage details in the provided text box.
4. Add any supporting attachments as needed.
5. Select the assigned tool and technician from the dropdown menus.
6. Choose the review status of the report.
7. Enter the date the form was submitted.
8. Enter the date the form was updated.
9. Review and verify the form for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **tool_life_tracking_report_request_form**: This is the title of the form.
* **equipment_id**: Enter the ID number of the equipment you are requesting a report for.
* **equipment_status**: Select the current status of the equipment from the dropdown menu:
	+ Active: The equipment is currently in use.
	+ Offline: The equipment is currently not in use.
	+ Retired: The equipment has been removed from service.
	+ Lost: The equipment has been lost or misplaced.
	+ Other: Select this option if the equipment status does not match the above categories.
* **tool_id**: Enter the ID number of the tool being reported.
* **request_date**: Enter the date the report is requested for. This field is not displayed on the form but is automatically populated when the form is submitted.
* **usage_details**: Provide a brief description of the tool's usage history, including any notable events or issues.
* **notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the tool or report.
* **attachments**: Attach any relevant documents or images that support the report.
* **assigned_tool**: Enter the tool number assigned to the technician or user.
* **assigned_technician**: Enter the name or initials of the assigned technician.
* **review_status**: Select the current review status of the report from the dropdown menu:
	+ In Progress: The report is currently being worked on.
	+ Done: The report has been completed.
	+ Not Started: The report has not yet been started.
	+ On Hold: The report is currently paused.
	+ Cancelled: The report has been cancelled.
* **submitted_by**: Enter the name or initials of the person submitting the report.
* **created_by**: Enter the name or initials of the person creating the form.
* **updated_by**: Enter the name or initials of the person updating the form.
* **updated_at**: Enter the date the form was last updated.
* **form_id**: Enter a unique identifier for the form.
* **submitted_at**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **reviewed_by**: Enter the name or initials of the person reviewing the report.
* **reviewed_at**: Enter the date the report was last reviewed.
* **comments**: Enter any comments or feedback on the report.
* **form_version**: Enter the version of the form.
* **revision_id**: Enter a unique identifier for the revision of the form.
* **created_at**: Enter the date the form was created.
* **submitted_by_group**: Enter the group or department submitting the report.
* **updated_at**: Enter the date the form was last updated.
