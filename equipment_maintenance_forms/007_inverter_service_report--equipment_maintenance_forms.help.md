# Inverter Service Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inverter Service Report form is used to document the details of inverter service visits, including the service person's presence, completed work, and any additional notes. This form is essential for tracking and maintaining accurate records of equipment maintenance and service history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the appropriate answer from the "Service Visit" field to indicate whether a service visit occurred.
2. Provide a brief description of the inverter details in the "Inverter Details" field.
3. Enter the service visit date in the "Service Visit Date" field in the desired format.
4. Enter the name of the service person in the "Service Person" field.
5. Enter the start and finish times of the service visit in the "Service Start Time" and "Service Finish Time" fields, respectively.
6. Describe any completed work in the "Completed Work" field.
7. Enter any additional notes in the "Notes" field.
8. Sign and date the "Service Person Signature" field to confirm completion of the service visit.
9. Enter your name and title in the "Service Person Name" and "Service Person Title" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Visit** (`service_visit`, select_multiple, not required): Select "Yes" if a service visit occurred, or "No" otherwise.
* **Inverter Details** (`inverter_details`, text, not required): Describe the inverter details, such as model number or serial number.
* **Service Visit Date** (`service_visit_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the service visit in the desired format (e.g., MM/DD/YY).
* **Service Person** (`service_person`, text, not required): Enter the name of the service person.
* **Service Start Time** (`service_start_time`, time, not required): Enter the start time of the service visit in the desired format (e.g., 08:00).
* **Diagnostics** (`diagnostics`, text, not required): Describe any diagnostics performed during the service visit.
* **Completed Work** (`completed_work`, text, not required): Describe any work completed during the service visit.
* **Service Finish Time** (`service_finish_time`, time, not required): Enter the finish time of the service visit in the desired format (e.g., 17:00).
* **Service Person Signature** (`service_person_signature`, text, not required): Sign and date the form to confirm completion of the service visit.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, not required): Enter any additional notes from the service visit.
* **Service Person Name** (`service_person_name`, text, not required): Enter your name.
* **Service Person Title** (`service_person_title`, text, not required): Enter your title or position.
