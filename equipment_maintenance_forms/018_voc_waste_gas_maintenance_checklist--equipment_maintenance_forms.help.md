# voc_waste_gas_maintenance_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about the maintenance and inspection of gas systems in facilities. It ensures that all necessary components are inspected and properly functioning, and that all maintenance activities are up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the components of the gas system that you want to inspect and maintain. You can choose from the following options:
	* Valve 1
	* Valve 2
	* Valve 3
2. Next, select the maintenance activities that need to be performed on the chosen components. You can choose from the following options:
	* Maintenance Activity 1
	* Maintenance Activity 2
	* Maintenance Activity 3
3. If you have any additional comments or information, you can add them in the "Additional Comments" section.
4. Enter the facility's email address and phone number in the corresponding fields.
5. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inspection of Gas System Components** (`gas_system_inspection`): Select all the components of the gas system that you want to inspect.
* **Gas Inlet** (`gas_inlet`): Enter the gas inlet information.
* **Gas Outlet** (`gas_outlet`): Enter the gas outlet information.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Enter any additional comments or information you have about the maintenance and inspection of the gas system.
* **Additional Comments** (`page_4`): Enter any additional comments or information you have about the maintenance and inspection of the gas system.
* **Facility Email** (`facility_email`): Enter the facility's email address.
* **Facility Phone** (`facility_phone`): Enter the facility's phone number.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`): Click this button to complete the form and submit the information.
* **Facility Email 2** (`facility_email_2`): Enter the facility's secondary email address.
* **Facility Phone 2** (`facility_phone_2`): Enter the facility's secondary phone number.
* **Submit Button 2** (`submit_button_2`): Click this button to complete the form and submit the information. 

## Tips
* Make sure to inspect all the necessary components of the gas system.
* Select all the required maintenance activities for the inspected components.
* Enter all the necessary contact information for the facility.
* Double-check all the entered information before submitting the form.
