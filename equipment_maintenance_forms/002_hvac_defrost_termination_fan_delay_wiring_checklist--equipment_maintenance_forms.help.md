# hvac_defrost_termination_fan_delay_wiring_checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to document the termination of a fan delay wiring for HVAC (heating, ventilation, and air conditioning) equipment maintenance. It captures essential information about the termination location, wiring details, and other relevant specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "General Information" page with any applicable details.
2. On the "Wiring Diagram" page, describe the current wiring configuration.
3. In the "Termination Location" page, select the specific location where the fan delay wiring is to be terminated.
4. On the "Termination Type" page, choose the type of termination used.
5. Select the wire gauge and color for the termination on the corresponding pages.
6. Enter the fan delay date on the "Fan Delay" page.
7. Sign and date the form, including providing your customer signature and photograph, if possible.
8. Add any additional comments or notes on the "Notes" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`1`, required/optional): This page provides space to enter any general information about the termination.
* **Wiring Diagram** (`2`, required/optional): Describe the current wiring configuration, including any relevant details about the fan delay wiring.
* **Termination Location** (`3`, required): Select the specific location where the fan delay wiring is to be terminated.
* **Wire Gauge** (`4`, required): Select the wire gauge used for the termination.
* **Wire Color** (`5`, required): Select the wire color used for the termination.
* **Termination Type** (`6`, required): Choose the type of termination used.
* **Fan Delay** (`7`, required): Enter the date for the fan delay.
* **Signature** (`8`, required): Sign and date your signature to confirm completion of the form.
* **Photos** (`9`, optional): Provide photographs of the termination, if possible.
* **Comments** (`10`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the termination.
* **Date** (`11`, required): Enter the date when the fan delay wiring is terminated.
* **Time** (`12`, required): Enter the time when the fan delay wiring is terminated.
* **Technician Comments** (`13`, required): Enter any comments or notes from the technician performing the termination.
* **Customer Signature** (`14`, required): Select how the customer signed the form.
* **Customer Signature Date** (`15`, required): Enter the date when the customer signed the form.
* **Job Number** (`16`, optional): Enter the job number related to the termination.
* **Equipment ID** (`17`, optional): Enter the equipment ID or serial number.
* **Job Type** (`18`, optional): Describe the type of job or task performed.
* **Location** (`19`, required): Enter the exact location where the termination occurred.
* **Termination Date** (`20`, required): Enter the date when the fan delay wiring is terminated.
* **Technician Notes** (`21`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments from the technician.
* **Customer Signature Photo** (`22`, optional): Provide a photograph of the customer signature, if possible.
* **Technician Signature Photo** (`23`, optional): Provide a photograph of the technician signature, if possible.
* **Notes** (`24`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the termination.
* **Equipment Condition** (`25`, optional): Describe the condition of the equipment after the termination.

Note that all required fields must be filled out before submitting the form.
