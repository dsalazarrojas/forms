# Mining Equipment Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information on the inspection of mining equipment, ensuring it is in good working order or in need of maintenance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the type of equipment being inspected (e.g. Mining Equipment, Heavy Equipment, Other Equipment, or Equipment Type).
2. Confirm the equipment's status (Operational, Non-Operational, Offline, or Maintenance).
3. If applicable, enter the date of inspection.
4. Enter your name as the inspector.
5. Provide any additional comments about the equipment's condition (if necessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Equipment Type** (`Equipment Type`, `select_one`): Select the type of equipment being inspected to help identify the correct inspection procedures and requirements.
* **Equipment Status** (`Equipment Status`, `select_one`, required): Confirm the current status of the equipment (Operational, Non-Operational, Offline, or Maintenance). This is a required field to ensure accurate data collection.
* **Inspection Date** (`Inspection Date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the inspection if it's different from the current date.
* **Inspector's Name** (`Inspector's Name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name as the person conducting the inspection.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments about the equipment's condition or any concerns.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and complete data collection.
- If the equipment is Non-Operational or Maintenance, provide a brief explanation of the reasons.
- Keep comments concise and focused on the specific issues with the equipment.
