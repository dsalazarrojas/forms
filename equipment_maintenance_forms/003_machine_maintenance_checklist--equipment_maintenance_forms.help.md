# Machine Maintenance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Machine Maintenance Checklist is a form used to document and track the maintenance status of equipment. It ensures that maintenance personnel and supervisors can quickly identify any required maintenance actions and plan accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the equipment maintenance checklists to identify any necessary maintenance tasks.
2. Select the status of each checklist item as either "Yes" or "No" for multiple items, or choose the specific "Yes" or "No" option for individual items.
3. Record the date of the last inspection or maintenance action for each item.
4. Select the next maintenance date for each item.
5. Indicate the current maintenance status of the equipment as "Active" or "Inactive".
6. Add any additional notes or comments from technicians or supervisors.
7. Record the name of the person who last performed the maintenance action.
8. Record the date and time of the last maintenance action.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Maintenance Checklists** (`equipment_maintenance_checklist_1`, note, not required): A note section for listing specific maintenance checklists related to the equipment.
* **Inspections** (`equipment_maintenance_checklist_2`, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" if the inspection is due or has been completed.
* **Replacements** (`equipment_maintenance_checklist_3`, select_multiple, required: true): Select the types of replacements required or performed on the equipment (e.g., "Yes" for replacement of parts, "No" for no replacement).
* **Last Inspected** (`equipment_maintenance_checklist_4`, date, required: true): Record the date of the last inspection or maintenance action.
* **Next Inspected** (`equipment_maintenance_checklist_5`, date, required: true): Record the date of the next inspection or maintenance action.
* **Maintenance Status** (`equipment_maintenance_checklist_6`, select_one, required: false): Select the current status of the maintenance as "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Technicians Notes** (`equipment_maintenance_checklist_7`, text, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments from technicians.
* **Supervisor Notes** (`equipment_maintenance_checklist_8`, text, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments from supervisors.
* **Last Done By** (`equipment_maintenance_checklist_9`, text, required: false): Record the name of the person who last performed the maintenance action.
* **Last Done On** (`equipment_maintenance_checklist_10`, time, required: true): Record the date and time of the last maintenance action.
