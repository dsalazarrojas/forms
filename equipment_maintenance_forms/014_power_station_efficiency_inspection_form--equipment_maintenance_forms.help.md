# Power Station Efficiency Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Power Station Efficiency Inspection Form is designed to gather information about the current status of a power station's equipment, condition, and any necessary maintenance or repairs. This form helps maintenance staff evaluate the efficiency of the station and identify potential issues to prevent downtime and ensure smooth operation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the inspection in the "Inspection Date" field (field 1).
2. Enter the name of the inspector in the "inspector_name" field (field 2).
3. Confirm whether the power station's site ID is active (field 3) - select "Yes" if it is, "No" otherwise.
4. Enter the name of the power station (field 4).
5. Indicate the type of equipment being inspected (field 5) - select all applicable options.
6. Specify the location of the inspection (field 6).
7. Enter any comments or notes about the inspection (field 7).
8. Evaluate the efficiency of the power station (field 8) - select all applicable options.
9. Document any findings from the inspection (field 9).
10. Enter the date of the next scheduled inspection (field 10).
11. Add any maintenance planning notes (field 11).
12. Provide an operational overview of the power station (field 12).
13. Add any additional comments (field 13).
14. Confirm if the site manager is involved in the inspection (field 14) - select "Yes" if they are, "No" otherwise.
15. Enter the name of the person responsible for the inspection (field 15).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspection Date** (field 1): Enter the date of the inspection in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **inspector_name** (field 2): Enter the name of the person conducting the inspection.
* **site_id** (field 3): Confirm whether the power station's site ID is active. Select "Yes" if it is, "No" otherwise.
* **power_station_name** (field 4): Enter the name of the power station.
* **equipment_type** (field 5): Indicate the type of equipment being inspected. Select all applicable options.
* **inspection_location** (field 6): Specify the location where the inspection took place.
* **comments** (field 7): Enter any comments or notes about the inspection.
* **efficiency_review** (field 8): Evaluate the efficiency of the power station. Select all applicable options.
* **documented_findings** (field 9): Document any findings from the inspection.
* **next_inspection_date** (field 10): Enter the date of the next scheduled inspection.
* **maintenance_planning_notes** (field 11): Add any maintenance planning notes.
* **operational_overview** (field 12): Provide an operational overview of the power station.
* **additional_comments** (field 13): Enter any additional comments.
* **assigned_to** (field 14): Confirm if the site manager is involved in the inspection. Select "Yes" if they are, "No" otherwise.
* **site_manager** (field 15): Enter the name of the person responsible for the inspection.
