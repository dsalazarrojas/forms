# H2S_Safety_Equipment_Sign_Out_Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the signing out of safety equipment to ensure accurate tracking and accountability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined in the form instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **sign_out_equipment** (`sign_out_equipment`, select_multiple, required): Select the equipment you are signing out from the available options.
*   **equipment_description** (`equipment_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the equipment, including its model and serial number.
*   **location** (`location`, select_one, required): Choose the location where the equipment will be used.
*   **date_signed_out** (`date_signed_out`, date, required): Enter the date you signed the equipment out.
*   **time_signed_out** (`time_signed_out`, time, required): Enter the time you signed the equipment out.
*   **user_signed_out** (`user_signed_out`, select_one, required): Select the user who is signing the equipment out.
*   **notes** (`notes`, note, required): Add any notes or remarks related to the equipment.
*   **assigned_to** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required): Choose the person or department that will be assigned to use the equipment (if applicable).
*   **assigned_date** (`assigned_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the equipment is expected to be returned.
*   **assigned_time** (`assigned_time`, time, required): Enter the time when the equipment is expected to be returned.
*   **location_2** (`location_2`, select_one, required): Select the location where the equipment will be returned (if applicable).
*   **equipment_status** (`equipment_status`, select_one, required): Choose the equipment status (e.g., available, in use, etc.).
*   **assigned_by** (`assigned_by`, select_one, required): Choose the person who is assigning the equipment out (if applicable).
*   **assigned_user** (`assigned_user`, select_one, required): Select the user to whom the equipment is assigned (if applicable).
*   **notes_2** (`notes_2`, note, required): Add any additional notes.
*   **equipment_type** (`equipment_type`, text, required): Enter the type of equipment.
*   **equipment_model** (`equipment_model`, text, required): Enter the model of the equipment.
*   **equipment_description_2** (`equipment_description_2`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the equipment.
*   **remarks** (`remarks`, text, required): Enter any remarks related to the equipment.
*   **equipment_number** (`equipment_number`, text, required): Enter the equipment number.
*   **notes_3** (`notes_3`, note, required): Add any additional notes.
*   **assigned_to_2** (`assigned_to_2`, select_one, required): Choose the person or department that will be assigned to use the equipment (if applicable).
