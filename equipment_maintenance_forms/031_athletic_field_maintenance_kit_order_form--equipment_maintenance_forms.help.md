# Athletic Field Maintenance Kit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for ordering athletic field maintenance kits. This form is used to ensure that we receive all necessary details for processing your order efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Organization Name**: Enter your organization's name, such as a school, facility, or organization.
2. **Department**: Select the department within your organization responsible for ordering.
3. **Contact Person Name**: Enter the primary contact person's name for this order.
4. **Contact Email**: Enter the contact person's email address for order communications.
5. **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact person's phone number for order questions.
6. **Order Date**: Enter the date when you need the supplies.
7. **Needed By Date**: Enter the date when you need the supplies delivered.
8. **Field Type**: Select the type of field you maintain (e.g., natural grass, artificial turf, etc.).
9. **Maintenance Kit Type**: Choose the type of maintenance kit you need (e.g., basic, standard, premium, etc.).
10. **Quantity of Kits**: Enter the number of kits you need to order.
11. **Supplies Needed**: Select all the supplies you require for the maintenance (e.g., paint, line striping machine, etc.).
12. **Paint Color Preference**: Choose your preferred paint color for field marking.
13. **Paint Quantity**: If you need paint, enter the quantity in gallons.
14. **Equipment Size Preference**: If you need equipment, choose your preferred size (e.g., walk-behind, ride-on, etc.).
15. **Brand Preference**: If you prefer a specific brand, enter it here.
16. **Replacement Parts Needed**: If you need replacement parts, list them.
17. **Safety Equipment Needed**: Select any safety equipment you need.
18. **Estimated Unit Price**: Enter the estimated price per kit in USD.
19. **Total Budget**: Enter your total budget for the order.
20. **Shipping Method**: Choose how you want the order to be shipped (e.g., standard, express, etc.).
21. **Delivery Address**: Enter the address where you want the order delivered.
22. **Delivery Instructions**: Provide any special delivery instructions or notes.
23. **Budget or Account Code**: If applicable, enter your budget or account code for billing purposes.
24. **Purchase Order Number**: If applicable, enter your purchase order number.
25. **Payment Method**: Choose how you will pay for the order (e.g., credit card, check, etc.).
26. **Special Instructions**: Provide any special requests or notes.
27. **Rush Order**: If this is a rush order, select yes.
