# Heat Pump Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Heat Pump Inspection Checklist is designed to assist with the routine maintenance and evaluation of heat pumps. This form will help ensure that all necessary information is collected and documented during each inspection, facilitating accurate records and informed decision-making for maintenance and repair.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the general information about the heat pump in the "General Info" section, including any relevant details about the equipment model and installation date.
2. Enter the serial number of the heat pump in the "Serial Number" field.
3. Enter the date of the last inspection in the "Last Inspection" field.
4. Select the type of pump from the options provided in the "Pump Type" field.
5. Describe the current operating condition of the heat pump, selecting all applicable options in the "Operating Condition" field.
6. Enter the operating hours of the heat pump in the "Operating Hours" field.
7. Add any additional comments or notes about the inspection in the "Notes" field.
8. Enter the contact number and email address for the individual performing the inspection, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info**: Provides a space to enter general information about the heat pump, such as the equipment model and installation date.
* **Equipment Model**: Enter the specific model of the heat pump being inspected.
* **Serial Number**: Enter the unique serial number assigned to the heat pump.
* **Installation Date**: Enter the date the heat pump was installed.
* **Last Inspection**: Enter the date of the last inspection performed on the heat pump.
* **Pump Type**: Select the type of pump, from the options provided, that best describes the heat pump.
* **Operating Condition**: Describe the current operating condition of the heat pump, selecting all applicable options.
* **Operating Hours**: Enter the operating hours of the heat pump, which will help estimate the remaining lifespan.
* **Notes**: Provide additional comments or notes about the inspection, if applicable.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number for the individual performing the inspection, if applicable.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address for the individual performing the inspection, if applicable.
* **Submit**: Once all fields are completed, click "Submit" to save the information.
