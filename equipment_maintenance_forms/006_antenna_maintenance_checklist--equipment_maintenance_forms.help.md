# Antenna Maintenance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and track antenna maintenance activities. It gathers information on the technician performing the maintenance, the maintenance date, and the details of the work performed.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter the technician's name and ID**: Enter the name and ID number of the technician performing the maintenance.
2. **Enter the site location and antenna ID**: Enter the address or location of the antenna site and the unique identifier for the antenna being serviced.
3. **Select the antenna type**: Choose the type of antenna being maintained from the dropdown list.
4. **Enter the maintenance date**: Select the date of the maintenance activity.
5. **Select the maintenance type**: Choose the type of maintenance being performed (e.g., routine inspection, preventive maintenance, etc.).
6. **Select the weather conditions**: Choose the current weather conditions at the time of maintenance.
7. **Complete the safety checks**: Answer the questions regarding the safety checks performed, including whether a safety briefing was conducted.
8. **Check the antenna mounting**: Select the condition of the antenna's mounting hardware.
9. **Check for physical damage**: Select the level of physical damage observed on the antenna.
10. **Check for corrosion**: Select the level of corrosion observed on the antenna.
11. **Perform the electrical inspection**: Answer questions about the condition of the antenna's electrical components.
12. **Check the cable and connector condition**: Select the condition of the antenna's cables and connectors.
13. **Test signal strength**: Record the measured signal strength.
14. **Check the grounding system**: Select the condition of the antenna's grounding system.
15. **Check for cleaning**: Select whether cleaning was performed during maintenance.
16. **List any replaced parts**: List any parts that were replaced during maintenance.
17. **Describe any issues found**: Describe any problems discovered during the maintenance.
18. **Note any corrective actions taken**: Describe what was done to address any issues.
19. **Determine if follow-up is required**: Indicate whether additional work is needed.
20. **Schedule follow-up date**: Enter the recommended date for next maintenance.
21. **Enter the next scheduled inspection date**: Enter the date for the next routine inspection.
22. **Enter the technician's signature**: Confirm the technician's signature or name.
23. **Enter the supervisor review status**: Indicate the status of the supervisor's review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Technician Name** (1): Enter the name of the technician performing the maintenance.
* **Technician ID** (2): Enter the ID number of the technician performing the maintenance.
* **Site Location** (3): Enter the address or location of the antenna site.
* **Antenna ID or Tag Number** (4): Enter the unique identifier for the antenna being serviced.
* **Antenna Type** (5): Select the type of antenna being maintained.
* **Maintenance Date** (6): Enter the date of the maintenance activity.
* **Maintenance Type** (7): Select the type of maintenance being performed.
* **Weather Conditions** (8): Select the current weather conditions at the time of maintenance.
* **-- Safety Checks --** (9): Answer questions regarding the safety checks performed, including whether a safety briefing was conducted.
* **Personal Protective Equipment Used** (10): Select all the Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) worn during maintenance.
* **Site Safety Briefing** (11): Select whether a safety briefing was conducted.
* **-- Structural Inspection --** (12): Answer questions about the condition of the antenna's structural components.
* **Antenna Mounting Condition** (13): Select the condition of the antenna's mounting hardware.
* **Physical Damage Observed** (14): Select the level of physical damage observed on the antenna.
* **Corrosion Check** (15): Select the level of corrosion observed on the antenna.
* **-- Electrical Inspection --** (16): Answer questions about the condition of the antenna's electrical components.
* **Cable and Connector Condition** (17): Select the condition of the antenna's cables and connectors.
* **Signal Strength Test** (18): Record the measured signal strength.
* **Grounding System Check** (19): Select the condition of the antenna's grounding system.
* **Cleaning Performed** (20): Select whether cleaning was performed during maintenance.
* **Parts Replaced** (21): List any parts that were replaced during maintenance.
* **Issues Found** (22): Describe any problems discovered during the maintenance.
* **Corrective Actions Taken** (23): Describe what was done to address any issues.
* **Follow-up Required** (24): Indicate whether additional work is needed.
* **Follow-up Date** (25): Enter the recommended date for next maintenance.
* **Next Scheduled Inspection** (26): Enter the date for the next routine inspection.
* **Technician Signature** (27): Confirm the technician's signature or name.
* **Supervisor Review Status** (28): Indicate the status of the supervisor's review.

## Tips

* Be thorough and accurate when completing this form to ensure accurate tracking and record-keeping.
* Review the form carefully to ensure that all required fields are completed.
* Attach supporting documents or photos as necessary to support the maintenance activity.
