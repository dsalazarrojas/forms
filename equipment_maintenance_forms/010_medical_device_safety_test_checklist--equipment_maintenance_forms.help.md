# medical_device_safety_test_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and maintain information about the safety testing of medical devices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields:
	* Serial number
	* Last inspection date
	* Next due date
2. Optional fields:
	* Medical device description
	* Medical device model
	* Medical device manufacturer

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. medical_device_info
* **medical_device_info** (`medical_device_info`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to provide a brief description of the medical device being tested.
* **Medical Device Information** is a text field where you can enter a short description of the medical device, such as its model, manufacturer, and any other relevant details.

### 2. serial_number
* **medical_device_serial_number** (`serial_number`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field allows you to choose whether the medical device has a serial number or not.
* **Serial Number** is a field with options 'Yes' and 'No' to indicate if the device has a serial number.

### 3. last_inspection_date
* **last_inspection_date** (`last_inspection_date`, `date`, required/optional): This field is used to record the date of the last inspection of the medical device.
* **Last Inspection Date** is a date field where you can enter the date of the last inspection.

### 4. next_due_date
* **next_due_date** (`next_due_date`, `date`, required/optional): This field is used to record the date when the next inspection is due.
* **Next Due Date** is a date field where you can enter the date when the next inspection is due.

### 5. next_calibration_date
* **next_calibration_date** (`next_calibration_date`, `date`, required/optional): This field is used to record the date of the next calibration of the medical device.
* **Next Calibration Date** is a date field where you can enter the date of the next calibration.

### 6. calibration_frequency
* **calibration_frequency** (`calibration_frequency`, `number`, required/optional): This field is used to record the frequency of calibration for the medical device.
* **Calibration Frequency** is a number field where you can enter the frequency of calibration (e.g., every 6 months).

### 7. calibration_status
* **calibration_status** (`calibration_status`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field allows you to choose the status of the calibration.
* **Calibration Status** is a field with options On Schedule, Past Due, Never Due to indicate the status of the calibration.

### 8. inspection_result
* **inspection_result** (`inspection_result`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field is used to record the result of the inspection.
* **Inspection Result** is a field with options Passed, Failed, Deferred, Pending to indicate the result of the inspection.

### 9. comments
* **comments** (`comments`, `note`, required/optional): This field is used to record any comments or notes about the inspection.
* **Comments** is a text field where you can enter any comments or notes.

### 10. photos
* **photos** (`photos`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to upload photos of the medical device.
* **Photos** is a text field where you can upload photos of the device.

### 11. signature

* **Signature** is a text field where you can enter the signature.

### 12. email
