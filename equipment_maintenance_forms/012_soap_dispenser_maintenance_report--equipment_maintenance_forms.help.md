# Soap Dispenser Maintenance Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Soap Dispenser Maintenance Report is a form used to collect information about the maintenance status of soap dispensers in a facility. Maintenance personnel will complete this form to report on the condition of each soap dispenser, including its functionality, soap level, restocking status, and any additional notes or comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Soap Dispenser Maintenance Report, follow these steps:

1. Enter the location of the soap dispenser being reported on.
2. Select the condition of the soap dispenser from the available options (Fully Functional, Partially Functional, or Not Functional).
3. Enter the current soap level in the dispenser.
4. Select the restocking status of the soap dispenser (Restocked, Not Restocked).
5. Enter any additional comments or notes about the soap dispenser.
6. Enter the name of the person assigned to complete the maintenance task.
7. Save the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **location** (Text, Required): Enter the location of the soap dispenser being reported on. This is a required field and should be entered accurately to ensure that the correct location is associated with the maintenance report.
* **dispenser_condition** (Select One, Required): Select the condition of the soap dispenser from the available options (Fully Functional, Partially Functional, or Not Functional). This field is required and will help maintenance personnel understand the status of the soap dispenser.
* **soap_level** (Number, Required): Enter the current soap level in the dispenser. This field is required and should be entered accurately to ensure that the correct soap level is recorded.
* **restocking_status** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the restocking status of the soap dispenser (Restocked, Not Restocked). This field is required and will help maintenance personnel understand the restocking status of the soap dispenser.
* **maintenance_activity** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the soap dispenser. This field is not required but can provide valuable additional information about the dispenser.
* **notes** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the soap dispenser. This field is not required but can provide valuable additional information about the dispenser.
* **assigned_to** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the person assigned to complete the maintenance task. This field is required and should be entered accurately to ensure that the correct person is assigned to the task.

## Tips
- Make sure to complete the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that maintenance personnel can take the necessary steps to address any issues with the soap dispenser.
- Double-check the location and soap level fields to ensure that they are entered correctly.
- Select the correct restocking status and condition of the dispenser to ensure that maintenance personnel have a clear understanding of the dispenser's status.
- Assign the maintenance task to the correct person to ensure that the task is completed in a timely and efficient manner.
