# weekly_audio_visual_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

The **weekly_audio_visual_checklist** is designed to aid in the regular maintenance and inspection of audio-visual equipment. This form ensures that users can quickly and easily record issues, checks, and other relevant information to keep the equipment in good working condition.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the issues encountered during the inspection in the **Issues Encountered** field.
2. For each issue, select the relevant **Checked Items** and **Issues Issued** in the respective fields.
3. Enter the date and time of the inspection in the **Date** and **Time** fields.
4. Provide any additional comments or remarks in the **Comments** field.
5. Sign and date the form in the **Signature** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Checklist Items**: Enter the items checked or inspected during the audit. Select multiple items if necessary.
* **Issues Encountered**: Choose the issues or problems found during the inspection.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the inspection.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or remarks about the inspection.
* **Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm the inspection was conducted.
* **Checked Items**: Select the items checked or verified during the inspection.
* **Issues Issued**: Select the issues or problems identified during the inspection.
* **Comments Issued**: Enter any additional comments or remarks about the issues.
* **Signature Issued**: Sign and date the form to confirm the issues were addressed.
* **Checked Items Issued**: Select the items checked or verified after the issues were addressed.
* **Comments Issued 2**: Enter any additional comments or remarks after the issues were addressed.
* **Signature Issued 2**: Sign and date the form to confirm the issues were addressed.

## Tips

- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Select all relevant issues and items checked.
- Enter accurate and clear dates and times.
- Provide detailed comments for each section.
- Sign and date each section as required.
