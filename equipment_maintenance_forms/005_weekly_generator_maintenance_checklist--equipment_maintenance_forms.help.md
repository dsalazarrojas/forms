# Weekly Generator Maintenance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document and track generator maintenance activities on a weekly basis. It helps in monitoring the status of various components and ensuring that the generator is running smoothly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the provided checklist for each section to understand what needs to be checked.
2. Select the correct status for each maintenance item from the provided options.
3. Fill in the date for each action taken.
4. Add any additional notes if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Checklist Item 1: Check the oil level
* **Check the oil level** (`checklist_item_1`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to select the status of the oil level. Please choose from the options: Below 0.5, 0.5 to 0.8, 0.8 to 1.0, Above 1.0.

### Checklist Item 2: Check the oil filter
* **Check the oil filter** (`checklist_item_2`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to select the status of the oil filter. Please choose from the options: Not replaced, Replaced.

### Checklist Item 3: Check the coolant level
