# Daily Pre Operation Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Daily Pre-Operation Checklist is a form used to document and track the status of pre-operation checklists for equipment. It is intended to ensure that all necessary steps are taken before operating the equipment, and to provide a record of the checklist completion status and any changes to that status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Location**: Enter the location where the pre-operation checklist is being completed.
	* **Equipment Name**: Enter the name of the equipment being checked.
	* **Date Completed**: Enter the date the pre-operation checklist was completed.
	* **Time Completed**: Enter the time the pre-operation checklist was completed.
	* **Completed By**: Enter the person who completed the pre-operation checklist.
	* **Submitted By**: Enter the person who submitted the pre-operation checklist.
	* **Submitted By Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the pre-operation checklist.
	* **Submitted By Location**: Enter the location of where the pre-operation checklist was submitted from.
	* **Equipment Serial Number**: Enter the serial number of the equipment being checked.
	* **Equipment Id**: Enter the ID of the equipment being checked.
2. If necessary, fill in the optional fields:
	* **Pre-Operation Checklist Status**: Select the current status of the pre-operation checklist (Completed, Not Completed, Pending, Cancelled).
	* **Reason Not Completed**: Enter a reason for why the pre-operation checklist is not completed.
	* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes related to the pre-operation checklist.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled in and accurate. Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting it.
