# Refrigerant Log Sign Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Refrigerant Log Sign Off Form is a document that records the status of refrigerant systems in your organization. It is used to track and document the sign off of refrigerant from various machines and equipment. The form is used to ensure that the refrigerant is properly signed off, which is an essential aspect of maintaining the safety and efficiency of your equipment and systems.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide the General Info page with the required information for the sign off.
2. Enter the Sign Off Info on the second page.
3. Add any additional notes about the sign off on the third page.
4. Indicate whether the sign off is supervised or not on the fourth page.
5. Date and time of the sign off on the last page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info** (`general_info`, `text`, required: false): This page provides a general description of the sign off. Please enter the relevant details such as the machine or equipment that was signed off, the technician's name, and any other relevant information.
* **Sign Off Info** (`sign_off_info`, `text`, required: false): This page provides additional information about the sign off, such as the reason for the sign off and any other relevant notes.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, required: false): This page is for any additional notes or comments about the sign off. Please use this field to provide any further details that are not included in the previous fields.
* **Supervisor Sign Off** (`supervisor_signoff`, `select_one`, required: false): This field indicates whether the sign off was supervised or not. Please select either 'True' or 'False' depending on the situation.
* **Date and Time** (`date_and_time`, `date`, required: false): This field is for recording the date and time of the sign off. Please ensure that this field is completed accurately. 

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and consistently with the company's standard operating procedures.
* This form is a mandatory document for all refrigerant log sign offs. Failure to complete this form may result in equipment malfunctions, safety risks, and other serious consequences. 
* This form should be used for all types of refrigerant log sign offs, including but not limited to, routine maintenance, emergency repairs, and planned maintenance. 

Note: This form is an essential tool for maintaining the safety, efficiency, and integrity of your refrigerant systems. Completing this form will ensure that you are documenting and tracking the status of your refrigerant systems as required. 

Please review this document carefully and follow the instructions for completing the form accurately and consistently.
