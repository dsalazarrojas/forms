# equipment_functional_test_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equipment Functional Test Checklist is a form used to collect and record results of functional tests on equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Complete the form in a quiet, distraction-free area.
2. Ensure you have the necessary knowledge and authority to complete this form.
3. Select the correct options for each field as accurately and truthfully as possible.
4. Fill out the form in its entirety before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### General Information (Page 1)
* **General Information**: This field is where you provide a brief description of the equipment being tested, including its current status and any relevant context.

### Equipment Type (Page 1)
* **Equipment Type**: Indicate whether the equipment has been tested for its intended purpose or if it's a test unit.

### Equipment Model Number (Page 1)
* **Model Number**: Enter the equipment's model number if it has one.

### Equipment Location (Page 1)
* **Equipment Location**: Select the location where the equipment is currently situated. Choose the option that best matches where the equipment can be found.

### Functional Test Date (Page 2)
* **Functional Test Date**: Enter the date the functional test was performed.

### Tester Name (Page 2)
* **Tester Name**: Provide the name of the person who performed the functional test.

### Test Result (Page 2)
* **Test Result**: Select the result of the test. Choose from the available options: "Pass", "Fail", or "Not Tested".

### Maintenance Frequency (Page 2)
* **Maintenance Frequency**: Enter the recommended maintenance frequency for the equipment, if applicable.

### Notes (Page 2)
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the equipment's current status or the test process.

## Tips
* Always fill out the form in its entirety before submitting it.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy and truthfulness.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the relevant equipment documentation or consult with a subject-matter expert.
* Keep the form up-to-date and consistent with other equipment maintenance documents.
