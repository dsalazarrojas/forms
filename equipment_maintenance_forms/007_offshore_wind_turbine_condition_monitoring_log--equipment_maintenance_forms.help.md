# Offshore Wind Turbine Condition Monitoring Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to monitor and document the condition of offshore wind turbines. It helps to gather data on the turbine's performance, issues, and maintenance activities, which can be used to optimize maintenance planning, identify potential problems, and improve overall turbine reliability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for the form in the "Form Title" field. This title will be used to identify the form and help you and others quickly understand the content.
2. Select the category of the form in the "Category" field. You can choose either "Equipment Maintenance Forms" or "Other".
3. Provide a brief description of the form contents in the "Description" field.
4. Enter the date and time of the form submission in the "Form Submission Date" and "Form Submission Time" fields.
5. Enter your name or user ID in the "Form Submission User" field.
6. Add any relevant issue tracking information in the "Issue Tracking" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a title for the form that describes the contents of this log.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Select the category for this form. Choose "Equipment Maintenance Forms" if this log is related to wind turbine maintenance or "Other" if it's for a different purpose.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief summary of the contents of this log.
* **Form Submission Date** (`form_submission_date`, date, optional): Enter the date on which this form was completed.
* **Form Submission Time** (`form_submission_time`, time, optional): Enter the time on which this form was completed.
* **Form Submission User** (`form_submission_user`, text, optional): Enter your name or user ID for auditing and logging purposes.
* **Issue Tracking** (`issue_tracking`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about issue tracking, such as ticket numbers, work orders, or other relevant information.
