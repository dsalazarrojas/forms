# Engine Intake Manifold Torque Sequence Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Engine Intake Manifold Torque Sequence Checklist is a form used to document the steps taken during the Engine Intake Manifold Torque Sequence process. This process involves applying torques to specific parts of the engine intake manifold. By filling out this form, technicians can ensure they have followed the correct sequence and document any issues or comments that arose during the process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before starting the intake manifold torque sequence process.
2. Fill out the form step-by-step, following the sequence as instructed.
3. Select all the steps that were performed in the "Torque Sequence Steps" field.
4. Document any comments or issues that arose during the process in the "Comments" field.
5. Review your work for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intake Manifold Data** (`Intake Manifold Page`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant information about the intake manifold, such as the engine type or model.
* **Torque Sequence** (`Manifold Torque Sequence`, `text`, required: false): Enter the sequence of torques applied to the intake manifold.
* **Torque Values** (`Manifold Torque Values`, `text`, required: false): Enter the actual torque values applied to the intake manifold.
* **Technician Information** (`Technician Information`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name, ID number, or other relevant information.
* **Intake Manifold Inspection** (`Intake Manifold Inspection`, `note`, required: false): Enter any issues or comments about the intake manifold inspection.
* **Torque Sequence Steps** (`Torque Sequence Steps`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the steps that were performed during the torque sequence process.

	+ Option 2: Step 2 - Description: Select this option if Step 2 was performed.
	+ Option 3: Step 3 - Description: Select this option if Step 3 was performed.
* **Comments** (`Torque Sequence Comments`, `note`, required: false): Enter any comments or issues that arose during the process.
