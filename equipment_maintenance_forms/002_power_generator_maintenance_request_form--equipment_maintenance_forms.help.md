# Power Generator Maintenance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Power Generator Maintenance Request Form is designed to provide a structured format for reporting and tracking maintenance requests for the power generator equipment. This form helps the maintenance team to efficiently manage and prioritize maintenance tasks, ensuring that the equipment is properly maintained to function correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  To start, select the status of the generator from the dropdown menu. This will help the maintenance team to understand the urgency and priority of the request.
2.  If the generator is inactive, provide the model number in the text field.
3.  Describe the issue with the generator in the field provided.
4.  If necessary, select the date for the scheduled maintenance visit.
5.  If you need assistance during the maintenance visit, indicate the expected time for the visit in the field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Generator Status** (`generator_status`, select_one, required): This field allows you to report the current status of the generator. Please select "Active" if the generator is currently operational, or "Inactive" if it is not. This will help the maintenance team prioritize the maintenance requests.
* **Generator Model** (`generator_model`, text, required only when generator is inactive): If the generator is inactive, provide the model number for reference.
* **Generator Issue** (`generator_issue`, text, required): Describe the issue with the generator, being as specific as possible.
* **Maintenance Schedule** (`maintenance_schedule`, date, optional): If you have a specific date in mind for the maintenance visit, provide it. This will help the maintenance team plan the visit accordingly.
* **Service Visit Time** (`service_visit`, time, optional): If you need assistance during the maintenance visit, please provide the expected time for the visit. This will help the maintenance team schedule their work accordingly.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide as much detail as possible about the generator issue to help the maintenance team to efficiently diagnose and fix the problem.
* Ensure that you have consulted with the relevant stakeholders before filling out this form.
* This form should only be used for legitimate maintenance requests, not for routine or unnecessary tasks.
