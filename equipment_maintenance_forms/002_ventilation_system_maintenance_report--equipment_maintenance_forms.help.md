# ventilation_system_maintenance_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ventilation system maintenance report is used to document the maintenance visit of a ventilation system, ensuring that the system is properly maintained and records are kept.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the form instructions carefully before starting the maintenance visit.
- Ensure you have access to the relevant system documentation and records.
- Fill out the form with the required information, including the maintenance visit date, system condition, and maintenance history.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **service_visits** (`maintenance_visit`, note, optional): This field is used to record a brief description of the maintenance visit.
* **system_condition** (`system_condition`, text, optional): Please describe the current condition of the ventilation system, including any issues or concerns.
* **maintenance_history** (`maintenance_history`, date, optional): This field is used to record the date of the last maintenance visit.
* **technician_signature** (`technician_signature`, text, optional): After completing the visit, please sign and date your signature to confirm the maintenance work has been done.
