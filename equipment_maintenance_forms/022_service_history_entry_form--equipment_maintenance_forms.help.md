# Service History Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service History Entry Form is designed to track the service history of equipment assets, providing essential information for maintenance planning and resource management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that you have a clear understanding of the service event you are reporting, including the equipment involved and the purpose of the maintenance or repair.
2. Fill in the required fields, which include:
	* Service History (page 1)
	* Equipment details (page 2)
	* Serial number, model, and revenue information (page 3)
	* Revenue and cost information (page 4)
3. Be accurate when filling in the fields to ensure that the information is reliable and easy to reference later.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service History** (page 1):
	+ Enter a brief description of the service event.
* **Equipment** (page 2):
	+ Provide the name of the equipment being serviced.
* **Serial Number** (page 3):
	+ Enter the serial number of the equipment, if applicable.
* **Model** (page 3):
	+ Enter the model number or type of the equipment.
* **Revenue Code** (page 3):
	+ Enter the code associated with the revenue generated from the equipment's use.
* **Revenue Type** (page 4):
	+ Select "Yes" if revenue was generated from the equipment's use, "No" otherwise.
* **Revenue Date** (page 4):
	+ Enter the date when revenue was generated or is expected.
* **Revenue Amount** (page 4):
	+ Enter the amount of revenue generated or expected.
* **Cost Code** (page 4):
	+ Enter the code associated with any costs incurred for the equipment's maintenance.
* **Cost Type** (page 4):
	+ Select "Yes" if costs were incurred for the equipment's maintenance, "No" otherwise.
* **Cost Date** (page 4):
	+ Enter the date when costs were incurred or are expected.
* **Cost Amount** (page 4):
	+ Enter the amount of costs incurred or expected.
* **Next Service** (page 4):
	+ Enter the date when the next scheduled maintenance or service is due.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have the necessary information before filling in the form.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with your supervisor or colleague for guidance.
