# Catering Equipment Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Catering Equipment Assessment Form is used to evaluate the condition and maintenance status of catering equipment. This form helps assessors identify potential issues and make recommendations for maintenance, repairs, or replacement to ensure safe and efficient equipment operation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name as the assessor.
2.  Select the assessment date and location of the equipment.
3.  Choose the equipment category.
4.  Enter the specific equipment name.
5.  Provide the equipment's identification number.
6.  Enter the equipment's manufacturer and model number (if known).
7.  Record the purchase date.
8.  Rate the overall condition, cleanliness, and functionality of the equipment.
9.  Indicate if the safety check passed or if there are safety concerns.
10. List any safety concerns.
11. Identify the maintenance needed (e.g., cleaning, calibration, or replacement).
12. Indicate the urgency of repair or replacement.
13. Enter any estimated repair cost (if known).
14. Determine if the equipment should be replaced (and when).
15. Record the last and next service dates.
16. Check if the equipment is under warranty.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assessor Name** (`assessor_name`): <u>Enter your name as the person conducting this assessment.</u>
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`): <u>Enter the date of this assessment.</u>
* **Location** (`location`): <u>Enter the location where the equipment is situated.</u>
* **Equipment Category** (`equipment_category`): <u>Choose the type of equipment (e.g., cooking, refrigeration, or food preparation).</u>
* **Equipment Name** (`equipment_name`): <u>Enter the specific name of the equipment (e.g., oven, dishwasher, or refrigeration unit).</u>
* **Equipment ID or Serial Number** (`equipment_id`): <u>Enter any identification number or serial number of the equipment.</u>
* **Manufacturer** (`manufacturer`): <u>Enter the brand or manufacturer of the equipment (if known).</u>
* **Model Number** (`model_number`): <u>Enter the model number of the equipment (if known).</u>
* **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`): <u>Enter the date the equipment was purchased.</u>
* **Age in Years** (`age_years`): <u>Enter the age of the equipment in years.</u>
* **Overall Condition** (`overall_condition`): <u>Rate the overall condition of the equipment on a scale of Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Very Poor.</u>
* **Cleanliness Rating** (`cleanliness_rating`): <u>Rate the cleanliness of the equipment on a scale of Very Clean, Clean, Acceptable, Needs Cleaning, or Very Dirty.</u>
* **Functionality Status** (`functionality_status`): <u>Rate the functionality of the equipment as Fully Functional, Minor Issues, Major Issues, Not Functional, or other.</u>
* **Safety Check Passed** (`safety_check_passed`): <u>Indicate if the safety check passed or if there are safety concerns.</u>
* **Safety Concerns** (`safety_concerns`): <u>List any safety concerns or issues with the equipment.</u>
* **Maintenance Needed** (`maintenance_needed`): <u>Select the type of maintenance needed (e.g., cleaning, calibration, or replacement).</u>
* **Repair Urgency** (`repair_urgency`): <u>Indicate the urgency of repair or replacement (e.g., Not Urgent, Low Priority, Medium Priority, High Priority, or Critical).</u>
* **Estimated Repair Cost** (`estimated_repair_cost`): <u>Enter any estimated repair cost (if known).</u>
* **Replacement Recommended** (`replacement_recommended`): <u>Determine if the equipment should be replaced (and when).</u>
* **Last Service Date** (`last_service_date`): <u>Enter the date the equipment was last serviced.</u>
* **Next Service Due** (`next_service_due`): <u>Enter the date when the equipment is due for its next service.</u>
* **Warranty Status** (`warranty_status`): <u>Indicate if the equipment is under warranty.</u>
* **Assessor Notes** (`assessor_notes`): <u>Enter any additional observations or comments about the equipment.</u>
* **Follow-up Required** (`follow_up_required`): <u>Indicate if follow-up is required (yes or no).</u>
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`): <u>Enter the date for follow-up (if required).</u>
