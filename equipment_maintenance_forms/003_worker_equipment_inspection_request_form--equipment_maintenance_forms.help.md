# Worker Equipment Inspection Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Worker Equipment Inspection Request Form is used to collect information about equipment that requires inspection. This form helps to identify the equipment, schedule an inspection, and track the progress of the inspection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the equipment that needs inspection from the options provided in the "category" field.
2. Choose the date of the inspection from the "inspection_date" field.
3. Provide any supporting photos of the equipment that may be relevant to the inspection.
4. Enter a brief description of the equipment and the reason for the inspection.
5. Optionally, select a condition (e.g., "Good", "Needs Maintenance", etc.) and enter a number to indicate its severity.
6. Select the date when the request was made.
7. Enter a detailed description of the issue with the equipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **equipment** (text): Enter a brief description of the equipment that needs inspection.
* **inspection_date** (date): Enter the date when the inspection is requested.
* **supporting_photos** (text): Attach any photos that may be helpful for the inspection.
* **category** (select_multiple): Choose the category under which the equipment falls (e.g., "Equipment Maintenance", "Safety", etc.).
* **condition** (number): Select the condition of the equipment (e.g., "Good", "Needs Maintenance", etc.). If you have a specific condition in mind, enter the corresponding number.
* **request_date** (date): Enter the date when the request was made.
* **description** (text): Enter a detailed description of the issue with the equipment.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide clear and concise information to help the inspector understand the issue.
* Attach any supporting photos that can help identify the problem.
* Select the correct category for accurate tracking and reporting.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the equipment manager for assistance.
