# Hydraulic System Monitoring Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hydraulic System Monitoring Log Form is used to track and report on the monitoring data and comments related to hydraulic systems. It helps maintenance personnel, operators, or supervisors document the status of the system, record issues or problems, and provide contact information for follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form
- Begin by selecting the correct option for "Equipment Type" and filling in the relevant equipment details.
- Provide any general information about the equipment and its current status.
- Record any monitoring data related to the equipment.
- Leave any comments or notes about the equipment's condition.
- Complete the submitter details, including name, email, and phone number (if applicable).
- Enter the submitter's organization or company (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Equipment Type** (`Type`, select_one): Select the type of equipment being monitored.
* **Equipment Identification** (`Equipment ID`, select_one): Select the specific equipment being monitored using its ID.
* **Monitoring Data** (`monitoring_data`, text): Enter any monitoring data relevant to the equipment.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note): Leave any notes or comments about the equipment's condition.
* **Submitter Details** (`submitter_details`, text): Leave a brief description of why you are submitting this form.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text): Enter your name as the submitter.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email): Enter your email address as the submitter.
* **Submitter Phone Number** (`submitter_phone_number`, text): Enter your phone number as the submitter.
* **Submitter Organization** (`submitter_organization`, text): Enter the name of your organization or company as the submitter.
