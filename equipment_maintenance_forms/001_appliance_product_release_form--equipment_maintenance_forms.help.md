Appliance Product Release Form - Help Guide
=====================================

## Purpose

The Appliance Product Release Form is used to record the release of an appliance for shipment or distribution. This form ensures that all relevant information is captured and verified before the appliance is sent to the receiving department, guaranteeing a smooth delivery process and preventing any potential issues.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the release date and time of the appliance.
2. Provide the full name of the appliance being released.
3. Enter the manufacturer's model number.
4. Enter the unique serial number of the appliance.
5. Specify the facility where the appliance was manufactured.
6. Select the current quality inspection status.
7. Enter the name of the inspector who performed the quality check.
8. Enter the date when the quality inspection was completed.
9. Provide any defect notes or null if none found during inspection.
10. Select the approval status for product release.
11. Enter the name of the manager approving the release.
12. Specify the department receiving the released appliance.
13. Enter the name of the contact person in the receiving department.
14. Enter the email address of the contact person.
15. (Optional) Enter the phone number of the contact person.
16. Select the condition of the appliance packaging.
17. Select all documentation that accompanies this release.
18. Enter any special care or handling requirements.
19. Enter any additional comments or notes about this release.
20. Enter the exact release time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Release Date** (1, `date`, required): Date when the appliance is being released.
* **Product Name** (2, `text`, required): Full name of the appliance being released.
* **Model Number** (3, `text`, required): Manufacturer model identification number.
* **Serial Number** (4, `text`, required): Unique serial number for this unit.
* **Manufacturing Location** (5, `text`, required): Facility where the appliance was manufactured.
* **Quality Check Status** (6, `select_one`, required): Current quality inspection status.
* **Quality Inspector Name** (7, `text`, required): Name of the inspector who performed the quality check.
* **Quality Inspection Date** (8, `date`, required): Date when quality inspection was completed.
* **Defect Notes** (9, `text`, optional): Any defects found during inspection or null if none.
* **Release Approval Status** (10, `select_one`, required): Approval status for product release.
* **Approving Manager** (11, `text`, required): Name of manager approving the release.
* **Handoff Department** (12, `select_one`, required): Department receiving the released appliance.
* **Handoff Contact Person** (13, `text`, required): Primary contact in receiving department.
* **Handoff Contact Email** (14, `email`, required): Email address of the contact person.
* **Handoff Contact Phone** (15, `text`, optional): Phone number of the contact person.
* **Packaging Condition** (16, `select_one`, required): Condition of the appliance packaging.
* **Documentation Included** (17, `select_multiple`, required): Select all documentation that accompanies this release.
* **Special Handling Instructions** (18, `text`, optional): Any special care or handling requirements.
* **Release Notes** (19, `text`, optional): Additional comments or notes about this release.
* **Release Timestamp** (20, `time`, required): Exact time of release.
