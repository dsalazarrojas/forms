# Process Grounding Check Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Process Grounding Check Form is used to ensure the grounding system of a facility is functioning correctly and safely. It is a crucial form for maintenance and inspection purposes to verify that the grounding points are properly grounded and do not pose any electrical safety risks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information about the facility's grounding system.
2. Provide a detailed description of the grounding system and its type.
3. Record the count and description of the grounding points.
4. Report the inspection results for each grounding point.
5. Enter the date and time of the inspection.
6. Provide a summary of the issues identified during the inspection.
7. Create an action plan to address any issues found.
8. Enter your contact information as the submitter and reviewer.
9. Complete the review comments and date/time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Facility Name
• **Facility Name** (required): Enter the name of the facility being inspected.

### 2. Inspector Name
• **Inspector Name** (required): Enter your name as the inspector who is filling out this form.

### 3. Grounding System Description
• **Grounding System Description** (required): Provide a detailed description of the grounding system.

### 4. Grounding System Type
• **Grounding System Type** (select one): Select the type of the grounding system (Electrical, Mechanical, or Grounding).

### 5. System Location
• **System Location** (required): Enter the location of the grounding system.

### 6. System Elevation
• **System Elevation** (required): Enter the elevation of the grounding system.

### 7. Grounding Points Count
• **Grounding Points Count** (required): Enter the count of grounding points.

### 8. Grounding Points Description
• **Grounding Points Description** (required): Provide a detailed description of each grounding point.

### 9. Grounding Points Inspection
• **Grounding Points Inspection** (select multiple, optional): Report the inspection results for each grounding point (Pass, Fail, or N/A).

### 10. Inspection Date
• **Inspection Date** (required): Enter the date of the inspection.

### 11. Inspection Time
• **Inspection Time** (required): Enter the time of the inspection.

### 12. Issues Identified
• **Issues Identified** (required): Provide a summary of any issues identified during the inspection.

### 13. Action Plan
• **Action Plan** (required): Create a summary of the steps to address any issues found.

### 14. Submitter
• **Submitter** (required): Enter your name and contact information as the submitter.

### 15. Submitter E-mail
• **Submitter E-mail** (required): Enter your email address as the submitter.

### 16. Submitter Phone
• **Submitter Phone** (required): Enter your phone number as the submitter.

### 17. Submitter Department
• **Submitter Department** (optional): Select the department of the submitter (Maintenance, EHS, or Engineering).

### 18. Submitter Job Title
• **Submitter Job Title** (optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' if you have a job title.

### 19. Reviewer Name
• **Reviewer Name** (required): Enter the name of the reviewer who will review this form.

### 20. Reviewer Department
• **Reviewer Department** (optional): Select the department of the reviewer (Maintenance, EHS, or Engineering).

### 21. Reviewer Job Title
• **Reviewer Job Title** (optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' if the reviewer has a job title.

### 22. Submitter Comments
• **Submitter Comments** (required): Provide any additional comments from the submitter.

### 23. Reviewer Comments
• **Reviewer Comments** (required): Provide comments from the reviewer.

### 24. Review Date
• **Review Date** (required): Enter the date of the review.

### 25. Review Time
• **Review Time** (required): Enter the time of the review.

**Tips:** Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, and ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting.
