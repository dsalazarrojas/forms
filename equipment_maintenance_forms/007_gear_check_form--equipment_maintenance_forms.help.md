# Gear Check Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gear Check Form is used to track and manage equipment maintenance, including reporting on equipment condition, identifying issues, and scheduling follow-up checks or actions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Gear Check Form, follow these steps:

1.  **Introduction**: Start by reading and agreeing to the introduction section.
2.  **Equipment Details**: Report on the make, model, serial number, and other relevant information about the equipment.
3.  **Condition Rating**: Assess the overall condition of the equipment, using the ratings "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
4.  **Issue Description**: Describe any issues or problems found with the equipment.
5.  **Next Check Due**: Schedule the next maintenance check or follow-up, by selecting a date.
6.  **Issue Priority**: Determine the urgency or severity of the issue, with levels like "High", "Medium", or "Low".
7.  **Category**: Categorize or group similar equipment or issues.
8.  **Equipment Condition at Last Check**: Record the condition of the equipment at the last check or maintenance visit.
9.  **Equipment Condition**: Report the current condition of the equipment.
10. **Next Check Frequency**: Set the schedule for future checks or maintenance.
11.  **Issue Status**: Track the status of the issue, such as "Open", "Closed", or "In Progress".
12.  **Issue Status Comments**: Provide additional comments or notes about the issue.
13.  **Equipment Serial Number**: Record the unique identifier for the equipment.
14.  **Equipment Model Number**: Record the model number or specific type of equipment.
15.  **Equipment Asset Tag**: Record any asset tags or tracking numbers assigned to the equipment.
16.  **Equipment Make**: Record the manufacturer of the equipment.
17.  **Equipment Type**: Categorize or group similar equipment.
18.  **Location**: Record the location where the equipment is used or stored.
19.  **Notes**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the equipment or issue.
20.  **Category 2**: Additional categorization or grouping.
21.  **Follow-up Frequency**: Set the schedule for follow-up checks or actions.
22.  **Follow-up Due Date**: Schedule a follow-up check or action, by selecting a date (if applicable).
23.  **Follow-up Completed**: Record whether the follow-up check or action was completed.
24.  **Follow-up Next Due Date**: Schedule the next follow-up check or action, by selecting a date.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and complete maintenance tracking.
*   Use the correct rating for the condition of the equipment to reflect its true status.
*   Be specific when describing issues or problems with the equipment.
*   Set realistic and achievable schedules for follow-up checks or actions.
*   Keep the issue status up-to-date to reflect the current status.
