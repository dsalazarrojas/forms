# valve_inspection_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The valve_inspection_checklist form is designed to standardize the inspection process for valves, ensuring that all relevant details are captured and compared over time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the condition of the valve (Excellent, Good, Poor, Very Poor).
2. Check if the valve has any leaks (False or True).
3. Record the date and time of the inspection.
4. Check if photo evidence is present (True or False).
5. Select whether or not to attach a photo evidence file (True or False).
6. Enter any additional notes regarding the inspection (optional).
7. Enter the name of the person assigned to follow-up on the inspection (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Valve Condition** (`valve_condition`, select_one, required): Select the current condition of the valve from the dropdown menu, indicating its overall performance level.
* **Valve Leak** (`valve_leak`, select_multiple, required): Check if the valve has any leaks by selecting True or False from the dropdown menu.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the inspection in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Inspection Time** (`inspection_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the inspection in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Photo Evidence** (`photo_evidence`, select_multiple, required): Check if photo evidence is present by selecting True or False from the dropdown menu.
* **Photo Evidence File** (`photo_evidence_file`, select_multiple, required): Select whether or not to attach a photo evidence file.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person assigned to follow-up on the inspection (if applicable).
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes regarding the inspection (if necessary).
* **Notes 2** (`notes_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes regarding the inspection (if necessary).

## Tips
* Take note of any specific issues or observations during the inspection and document them in the notes field.
* Attach photo evidence when it will help facilitate the inspection process or document any findings.
* Complete all required fields to ensure accurate and comprehensive inspection reports.
* Store the form for reference and future inspection planning purposes.
