# Skid Steer Equipment Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skid Steer Equipment Inspection Form is designed to assess the condition of skid steer equipment, including its general condition, tires, hydraulic pressure, hydraulic leakage, steering system, hydraulic system, oil level, oil color, engine condition, transmission condition, electrical system, electrical hazards, and other notable conditions. This form helps to identify areas that require attention and maintenance to ensure the equipment remains in good working order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the equipment's history and usage to understand its condition and any previous issues.
2. Evaluate the equipment's general condition, paying attention to any wear and tear, damage, or other notable issues.
3. Check the tires for any signs of wear, damage, or uneven wear.
4. Inspect the hydraulic pressure and hydraulic system for any leaks or issues.
5. Evaluate the steering system's performance and condition.
6. Check the hydraulic system's performance and condition.
7. Record the oil level and color, and look for any signs of leakage.
8. Inspect the engine and transmission for any issues or wear and tear.
9. Evaluate the electrical system's performance and condition.
10. Check for any electrical hazards.
11. Document any other notable conditions or issues.
12. Enter the date and time of the last inspection.
13. Sign and date the form to indicate completion and approval.
14. Enter any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Condition** (`general_condition`, text, required: false): A brief description of the equipment's overall condition, including any notable issues or concerns.
* **Tires Condition** (`tires_condition`, text, required: false): A description of the tires' condition, including any wear, damage, or other issues.
* **Hydraulic Pressure** (`hydraulic_pressure`, number, required: false): Record the current hydraulic pressure reading.
* **Hydraulic Leakage** (`hydraulic_leakage`, text, required: false): A description of any hydraulic leaks or issues.
* **Hydraulic Leakage Note** (`hydraulic_leakage_note`, note, required: false): Additional notes or comments regarding hydraulic leakage.
* **Steering System** (`steering_system`, select_one, required: true): Evaluate the steering system's performance and condition as Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Hydraulic System** (`hydraulic_system`, select_multiple, required: true): Evaluate the hydraulic system's performance and condition as Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Oil Level** (`oil_level`, text, required: false): Record the current oil level reading.
* **Oil Color** (`oil_color`, text, required: false): A description of the oil color and any concerns.
* **Engine Condition** (`engine_condition`, text, required: false): A description of the engine's condition, including any notable issues.
* **Transmission Condition** (`transmission_condition`, text, required: false): A description of the transmission's condition, including any notable issues.
* **Electrical System** (`electrical_system`, select_one, required: true): Evaluate the electrical system's performance and condition as Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Electrical Hazards** (`electrical_hazards`, select_multiple, required: true): Check for any electrical hazards, selecting "Electrical Hazards" if present.
* **Other Conditions Note** (`other_conditions_note`, note, required: false): Additional notes or comments regarding other notable conditions.
* **Date of Last Inspection** (`date`, date, required: false): Record the date of the last inspection.
* **Time of Last Inspection** (`time`, time, required: false): Record the time of the last inspection.
* **Technician Signature** (`technician_signature`, text, required: false): The technician's signature to indicate completion and approval.
* **Inspector Signature** (`inspector_signature`, text, required: false): The inspector's signature to indicate completion and approval.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter contact information for follow-up or further communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter contact information for follow-up or further communication.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Additional notes or comments regarding the inspection.
* **Equipment Number** (`equipment_number`, text, required: false): Enter the equipment's identification number.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, text, required: false): A description of the equipment type.
* **Equipment Location** (`equipment_location`, text, required: false): A description of the equipment's location.

## Tips
* Be thorough and detailed in your evaluation and reporting.
* Take photos or videos to document any issues or conditions.
* Review and update this form as needed to ensure it remains relevant and effective.
* Store this form in a secure location to ensure access and record-keeping.
