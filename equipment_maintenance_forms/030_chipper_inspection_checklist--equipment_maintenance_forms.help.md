# Chipper Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chipper Inspection Checklist is a comprehensive form used to evaluate the condition and safety of chipper equipment. This form helps identify any issues or concerns that need attention to prevent accidents, ensure optimal performance, and extend the lifespan of the equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Ensure you have the latest version of the Chipper Inspection Checklist.
2. Review the form carefully and answer all questions accurately and thoroughly.
3. Complete each field according to the guidelines and explanations provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Inspection Date** (`Inspection Date`, date, required): Enter the date of the inspection.
- **Inspector Name** (`Inspector Name`, text, required): Type the name of the person performing the inspection.
- **Company Name** (`Company Name`, text, required): Enter the company name related to the equipment.
- **Chipper Model Number** (`Chipper Model Number`, text, required): Specify the model number of the chipper.
- **Serial Number** (`Serial Number`, text, required): Enter the serial number of the chipper.
- **Installation Date** (`Installation Date`, date, optional): If known, enter the date the chipper was installed.
- **Operating Hours Since Last Service** (`Operating Hours Since Last Service`, number, optional): If known, enter the operating hours since the last service.
- **Chipper Type** (`Chipper Type`, select_one, required): Select the type of chipper (Drum, Disc, Screw auger, Knife-style, or Other).
- **Engine Type** (`Engine Type`, select_one, required): Select the engine type (Gasoline, Diesel, Electric, PTO powered).
- **Overall Condition** (`Overall Condition`, select_one, required): Evaluate the overall condition of the chipper as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
- **Rust or Corrosion** (`Rust or Corrosion`, select_one, required): Determine if there is any rust or corrosion present (None, Minor, Moderate, Severe).
- **Visible Damage** (`Visible Damage`, select_one, required): Check for visible damage such as dents, cracks, or bending (Yes, No).
- **Description of Damage** (`Description of Damage`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of any visible damage.
- **Safety Features** (`Safety Features`, note, optional): Note any safety features or concerns.
- **Guard or Safety Screen Integrity** (`Guard or Safety Screen Integrity`, select_one, required): Evaluate the integrity of the guard or safety screen (Intact, Damaged, Missing).
- **Emergency Stop Button Functions** (`Emergency Stop Button Functions`, select_one, required): Check if the emergency stop button functions properly (Yes, No, Not present).
- **Blade Condition** (`Blade Condition`, select_one, required): Determine the blade condition (Sharp and good, Slightly dull, Very dull, Damaged).
- **Engine Starts and Operates Normally** (`Engine Starts and Operates Normally`, select_one, required): Check if the engine starts and operates normally (Yes, No, Not tested).
- **Feed System Functions Properly** (`Feed System Functions Properly`, select_one, required): Check if the feed system functions properly (Yes, No, Not tested).
- **Inspection Result** (`Inspection Result`, select_one, required): Determine the inspection result (Approved - Safe to use, Conditional - Minor repairs needed, Failed - Do not use until repairs made, Needs professional service).

**Tips:** Take notes or photos as necessary to document any findings or concerns during the inspection.
