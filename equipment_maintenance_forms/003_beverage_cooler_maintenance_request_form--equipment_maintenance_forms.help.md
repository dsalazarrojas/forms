# Beverage Cooler Maintenance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report issues with a beverage cooler, ensuring that the necessary maintenance or repair is performed to minimize downtime and maintain the equipment's functionality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your name, email address, and phone number so that we can contact you for follow-up communication.
2. Specify the location of the cooler within your facility.
3. Describe the issue with the cooler in detail, including its age and model number if known.
4. Indicate the type and severity of the issue.
5. Explain how the issue affects your business operations.
6. Specify when the issue first occurred and its urgency level.
7. Choose a preferred service time and ensure that someone will be available to receive the technician.
8. If the cooler has received previous service, indicate the frequency of such service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (Reporter Name, text, required): Please enter your full name for follow-up communication.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required): Provide your contact email for us to get in touch with you.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required): Enter your contact phone number for urgent issues.
* **Date of Report** (Date of Report, date, required): Indicate when you first noticed the issue.
* **Cooler Location** (Cooler Location, text, required): Specify the location of the cooler within your facility.
* **Cooler Model Number** (Cooler Model Number, text, optional): Provide the model or serial number of the cooler, if available.
* **Equipment Age** (Cooler Age, select_one, optional): Estimate the approximate age of the cooler.
* **Type of Issue** (Issue Type, select_one, required): Select the main problem with the cooler.
* **Detailed Description** (Detailed Description, text, required): Describe the problem in as much detail as possible.
* **Impact on Operations** (Impact on Operations, select_one, required): Indicate how the issue affects your business operations.
* **When Did This Issue First Occur** (When Did This Issue First Occur, text, optional): Approximate date or time when the issue started.
* **Priority Level** (Priority Level, select_one, required): Specify the urgency level of the repair.
* **Preferred Service Time** (Preferred Service Time, select_one, optional): Choose a convenient time for service.
* **Will Contact Person Be Available** (Will Contact Person Be Available, select_one, required): Ensure that someone will be available to receive the technician.
* **Has This Equipment Had Previous Service** (Has This Equipment Had Previous Service, select_one, optional): Indicate if the cooler has received previous service.
* **Additional Notes** (Additional Notes, text, optional): Provide any other relevant information for the technician.

Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that we can efficiently address the issue with your beverage cooler. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
