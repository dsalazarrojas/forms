# R&D Testing And Repair Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The R&D Testing and Repair Survey form is used to document the testing and repair process of equipment. This information is used to track the performance of equipment, identify areas for improvement, and make data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by entering the equipment's current condition.
2. Next, enter the location where the equipment was used.
3. Enter the date the equipment was tested.
4. Enter the date the equipment was repaired.
5. Select the test result (Good, Bad, or Not Applicable).
6. Enter any test result notes.
7. Select the repair result (Good, Bad, or Not Applicable).
8. Enter any repair result notes.
9. Enter the root cause of the issue (if known).
10. Enter any corrective action taken.
11. Enter the type of equipment.
12. Enter the equipment model.
13. Enter any test equipment used.
14. Enter any repair equipment used.
15. Enter the serial number of the equipment.
16. Enter the vendor of the equipment.
17. Enter the model number of the equipment.
18. Enter the software version of the equipment.
19. Enter the hardware version of the equipment.
20. Enter the duration of the test.
21. Enter the duration of the repair.
22. Enter any labor cost incurred.
23. Enter any material cost incurred.
24. Enter any test notes.
25. Enter any repair notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equipment Condition**: Enter the current condition of the equipment.
* **Location Used**: Enter the location where the equipment was used.
* **Testing Date**: Enter the date the equipment was tested.
* **Repair Date**: Enter the date the equipment was repaired.
* **Test Result**: Select the result of the test (Good, Bad, or Not Applicable).
* **Test Result Notes**: Enter any notes related to the test result.
* **Repair Result**: Select the result of the repair (Good, Bad, or Not Applicable).
* **Repair Result Notes**: Enter any notes related to the repair result.
* **Root Cause**: Enter the root cause of the issue (if known).
* **Corrective Action**: Enter any corrective action taken.
* **Equipment Type**: Enter the type of equipment.
* **Equipment Model**: Enter the model of the equipment.
* **Test Equipment**: Enter any test equipment used.
* **Repair Equipment**: Enter any repair equipment used.
* **Serial Number**: Enter the serial number of the equipment.
* **Vendor**: Enter the vendor of the equipment.
* **Model Number**: Enter the model number of the equipment.
* **Software Version**: Enter the software version of the equipment.
* **Hardware Version**: Enter the hardware version of the equipment.
* **Test Duration**: Enter the duration of the test.
* **Repair Duration**: Enter the duration of the repair.
* **Labor Cost**: Enter any labor cost incurred.
* **Material Cost**: Enter any material cost incurred.
* **Test Notes**: Enter any test notes.
* **Repair Notes**: Enter any repair notes.
