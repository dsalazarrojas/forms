# Machine Calibration Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Machine Calibration Report Form is used to document the maintenance activities performed on equipment, including calibration, repair, and inspection. The information collected helps track and manage equipment maintenance and performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the Machine Calibration Report Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the unique Equipment ID of the equipment being maintained.
2. Provide the name of the technician who performed the maintenance.
3. Describe the equipment being maintained, including its purpose and current status.
4. Enter the serial number of the equipment.
5. Record the date of the maintenance activity (e.g., calibration, repair, or inspection).
6. Select the type of maintenance performed (e.g., calibration, repair, inspection, or other).
7. Enter the frequency of the calibration activity, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equipment ID** (`equipment_id`, Text, Optional): Enter the unique identifier of the equipment being maintained.
* **Technician Name** (`technician_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the technician who performed the maintenance.
* **Machine Description** (`machine_description`, Text, Optional): Describe the equipment being maintained, including its purpose and current status.
* **Serial Number** (`serial_number`, Text, Optional): Enter the serial number of the equipment.
* **Maintenance Date** (`maintenance_date`, Date, Optional): Record the date of the maintenance activity.
* **Maintenance Type** (`maintenance_type`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the type of maintenance performed (e.g., calibration, repair, inspection, or other).
* **Calibration Frequency** (`calibration_frequency`, Number, Optional): Enter the frequency of the calibration activity, if applicable.
