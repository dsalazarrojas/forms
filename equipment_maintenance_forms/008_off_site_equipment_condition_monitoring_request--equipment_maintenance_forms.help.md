## off_site_equipment_condition_monitoring_request - Help Guide

**Purpose:**

This form is designed to gather information for monitoring equipment condition off-site. The form is used for submitting a request for scheduling maintenance and monitoring of equipment that is not currently located on-site.

**How To Complete This Form:**

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the necessary details about the equipment in question, including its ID, type, and any access notes.
2. Provide a date and time for the scheduled maintenance.
3. Specify the maintenance team assigned to perform the task.
4. Outline the next steps to be taken after the maintenance is completed.
5. If necessary, add any additional information about who the maintenance task is assigned to.

**Field-by-Field Explanation:**

* **off_site_equipment_details**:
	+ (text)
	+ A brief description of the equipment in question.
* **equipment_id**:
	+ (text)
	+ The unique identifier for the equipment.
* **equipment_type**:
	+ (text)
	+ The type of equipment being monitored.
* **site_access_notes**:
	+ (text)
	+ Any additional notes about accessing the equipment site.
* **scheduling_details**:
	+ (text)
	+ A description of the scheduled maintenance plan.
* **maintenance_schedule_date**:
	+ (date)
	+ The date on which the maintenance is scheduled.
* **maintenance_schedule_time**:
	+ (time)
	+ The time of the scheduled maintenance.
* **maintenance_team**:
	+ (text)
	+ The team assigned to perform the maintenance task.
* **next_steps**:
	+ (text)
	+ A list of actions to take after the maintenance is completed.
* **assigned_to**:
	+ (text)
	+ The person or team responsible for handling the maintenance task.

**Tips:**

* Ensure that you provide accurate and complete information about the equipment to facilitate efficient maintenance.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required details are included.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, reach out to the maintenance team for guidance.
