# ice_cream_freezer_maintenance_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture order details for maintaining the ice cream freezer equipment. It is intended for users to provide necessary information for the maintenance team to process and schedule the work required for the equipment's upkeep.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:
1. Go to the "Order Details" page and provide a brief description of the issue with your ice cream freezer.
2. Proceed to the "Contact Details" page and enter your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information.
3. In the "Job Details" page, describe the specific maintenance job that needs to be done, including any necessary details about the equipment or location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details (1)** (text, required): Please provide a clear and concise description of the issue with your ice cream freezer, including any error messages or symptoms you have observed.
* **Contact Details (2)** (text, required): Enter your name and email address to help us contact you with any questions or updates about your maintenance request. You can also include any other relevant contact information, such as phone number or extension.
* **Job Details (3)** (text, required): Describe the specific maintenance job that needs to be done, including any necessary details about the equipment or location, such as model number, serial number, or room number.
