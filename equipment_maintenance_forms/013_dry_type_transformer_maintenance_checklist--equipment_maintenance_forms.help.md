# Dry Type Transformer Maintenance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the maintenance and inspection of dry type transformers. It gathers information on various aspects of the transformer, including its site address, type, and condition. The data collected will help in scheduling and conducting regular site visits and maintenance activities for the transformer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the site address where the transformer is located.
2. Select the type of transformer being inspected.
3. Record the inspection date.
4. Enter the name of the person conducting the inspection.
5. Provide details on any notes or comments about the transformer's condition.
6. Choose the frequency of site visits for the transformer.
7. Select the current condition of the transformer (Good, Fair, Poor, or Bad).
8. Specify the status of the transformer (Active, Inactive, or Offline).
9. Enter the transformer's voltage (RMS) rating.
10. Enter the transformer's current rating.
11. Enter the transformer's power factor.
12. Enter the transformer's impedance rating.
13. Enter the transformer's impedance ratio.
14. (Optional) Provide additional notes or comments about the transformer's condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Site Address** (`1`, `text`, `Required`): Enter the physical address where the transformer is located.
* **Transformer Type** (`2`, `text`, `Required`): Specify the type of transformer being inspected.
* **Inspection Date** (`3`, `date`, `Required`): Record the date of the inspection.
* **Inspector Name** (`4`, `text`, `Required`): Enter the name of the person conducting the inspection.
* **Notes** (`5`, `note`, `Optional`): Provide any notes or comments about the transformer's condition.
* **Site Visit Frequency** (`6`, `select_multiple`, `Required`): Choose the frequency of site visits for the transformer (Weekly, Daily, Monthly).
* **Transformer Condition** (`7`, `select_one`, `Required`): Select the current condition of the transformer (Good, Fair, Poor, Bad).
* **Transformer Status** (`8`, `select_one`, `Required`): Specify the status of the transformer (Active, Inactive, Offline).
* **Transformer Voltage (RMS)** (`9`, `text`, `Required`): Enter the transformer's voltage rating (RMS).
* **Transformer Current Rating** (`10`, `text`, `Required`): Enter the transformer's current rating.
* **Transformer Power Factor** (`11`, `text`, `Required`): Enter the transformer's power factor.
* **Transformer Impedance** (`12`, `text`, `Required`): Enter the transformer's impedance rating.
* **Transformer Impedance Ratio** (`13`, `text`, `Required`): Enter the transformer's impedance ratio.
* **Notes 2** (`14`, `note`, `Optional`): Provide additional notes or comments about the transformer's condition.
