# Dewatering Pump Maintenance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dewatering Pump Maintenance Checklist is a form used to track and record the maintenance status of dewatering pumps. This form helps in keeping track of the pump's ID, model, location, status, and other important details to ensure regular maintenance and prolong the pump's lifespan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Pump ID" field and enter the unique identifier of the dewatering pump.
2. Enter the "Pump Model" field with the specific model number of the pump.
3. Enter the "Pump Location" field with the location where the pump is installed.
4. For "Pump Status", select one of the provided options: "Running", "Not Running", or "Under Maintenance".
5. If required, enter the "Date Completed" field with the date when the maintenance was completed.
6. In the "Comments" field, provide any additional comments or notes about the pump's maintenance.
7. If necessary, enter any additional details about the pump in the "Additional Pump Details" field.
8. Finally, describe the job details in the "Job Details" field, which is a required field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pump ID** (`pump_id`, text, optional): This field is where you enter the unique identifier of the dewatering pump.
* **Pump Model** (`pump_model`, text, optional): Enter the specific model number of the pump.
* **Pump Location** (`pump_location`, text, optional): Indicate where the pump is installed.
* **Pump Status** (`pump_status`, select one, optional): Select one of the provided options to indicate the current status of the pump.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the date when the maintenance was completed.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about the pump's maintenance.
* **Additional Pump Details** (`additional_pump_details`, text, optional): Enter any extra details about the pump.
* **Job Details** (`job_details`, text, required): This field is required and should contain a description of the job details related to the pump's maintenance.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all fields accurately and honestly to ensure the effectiveness of the pump's maintenance tracking.
* If you're not sure about any field, try to provide as much information as possible or ask the relevant personnel for guidance.
