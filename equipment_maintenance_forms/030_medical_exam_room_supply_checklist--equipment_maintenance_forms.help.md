# Medical Exam Room Supply Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to ensure that the exam room is well-maintained and equipped for a smooth and safe medical examination process. The form helps staff to assess the current state of the exam room and identify any areas that require improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the current status of each item on the form.
2. Make sure to answer each question honestly, as it is a self-assessment tool.
3. If you are unsure about any item, use your best judgment and explain your answer if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exam Room Supply** (`exam_room_supply`, text, not required): This section is for general comments about the state of the exam room, such as cleanliness, organization, and overall condition.
* **Air Quality** (`air_quality`, select_multiple, not required): Please select one or both of the options to indicate whether the air quality in the exam room is good or poor.
* **Temperature** (`temperature`, select_one, not required): Select "Yes" if the temperature in the exam room is comfortable, and "No" if it is not.
* **Humidity** (`humidity`, select_multiple, not required): Please select one or both of the options to indicate whether the humidity in the exam room is good or poor.
* **Ventilation** (`ventilation`, select_multiple, not required): Select one or both of the options to indicate whether the ventilation system is working properly or not.
* **Medical Equipment** (`medical_equipment`, select_one, not required): Select "Yes" if all medical equipment is in good working order, and "No" if any equipment is not functioning properly.
* **Trash Baskets** (`trash_baskets`, select_multiple, not required): Select one or both of the options to indicate whether trash baskets are available and functioning properly.
* **Medical Supplies** (`medical_supplies`, select_multiple, not required): Please select one or both of the options to indicate whether medical supplies are available and in stock.
* **Handwashing** (`handwashing`, select_one, not required): Select "Yes" if handwashing facilities are available and functional, and "No" if they are not.
* **Water Quality** (`water_quality`, select_multiple, not required): Select one or both of the options to indicate whether the water quality in the exam room is good or poor.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when completing this form.
* If you are unsure about any item, use your best judgment and explain your answer if necessary.
* This form is a tool to help maintain the exam room, please use it regularly to ensure the room remains in good condition.
