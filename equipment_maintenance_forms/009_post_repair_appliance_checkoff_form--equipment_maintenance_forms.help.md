# Post-Repair Appliance Checkoff Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to document the post-repair process of an appliance, ensuring that all necessary information is collected and communicated effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the form as thoroughly as possible, providing accurate information for each field.
2. Enter the technician's name who performed the repair.
3. Record the date of the repair.
4. List any parts replaced during the repair.
5. Enter the serial number of the appliance.
6. Specify the model of the appliance.
7. Describe the issue with the appliance before the repair.
8. Review and complete the pre-repair checklist to ensure that all necessary steps were taken before starting the repair.
9. Review and complete the post-repair checklist to ensure that all necessary steps were taken after completing the repair.
10. Review and complete the additional post-repair checklist sections as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Heading**: This field is for the title of the form. It is a required field but can be left empty if not applicable.
* **Technician Name** (`technician_name`, text, required): The name of the technician who performed the repair.
	+ Please enter your name as it should be printed on the repair document.
* **Date of Repair** (`date_of_repair`, date, required): The date the repair was performed.
	+ Use the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Part Replaced** (`part_replaced`, text, required): A list of parts replaced during the repair.
	+ Be as specific as possible and include part numbers or models if available.
* **Serial Number** (`serial_number`, text, required): The serial number of the appliance.
	+ If the serial number is not available, please leave this field empty.
* **Appliance Model** (`appliance_model`, text, required): The model of the appliance.
	+ Be as specific as possible and include any relevant details.
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, text, required): A detailed description of the issue with the appliance before the repair.
	+ Be as descriptive as possible to help future reference.
* **Pre-Repair Checklist** (`pre_repair_checklist`, note, optional): A list of steps taken before starting the repair.
	+ Review and complete this section to ensure all necessary steps were taken.
* **Post-Repair Checklist** (`post_repair_checklist`, note, optional): A list of steps taken after completing the repair.
	+ Review and complete this section to ensure all necessary steps were taken.
* **Post-Repair Checklist 2** (`post_repair_checklist_2`, note, optional): A second list of additional steps taken after completing the repair.
	+ Review and complete this section as required.
* **Post-Repair Checklist 3** (`post_repair_checklist_3`, note, optional): A third list of additional steps taken after completing the repair.
	+ Review and complete this section as required.
* **Post-Repair Checklist 4** (`post_repair_checklist_4`, note, optional): A fourth list of additional steps taken after completing the repair.
	+ Review and complete this section as required.

Note: The fields marked as "required" must be filled out in order to complete the form properly. The fields marked as "optional" can be left empty if not applicable.
