# Air Filtration System Maintenance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is a comprehensive maintenance checklist for the air filtration system, designed to ensure that all necessary checks and inspections are performed during routine and emergency maintenance services. The form is divided into sections that cover various aspects of the system's condition, including filters, motors, and other critical components.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and employee number in sections 1 and 2.
2. Record the date of the maintenance service in section 3.
3. Enter the name and address of the facility where the system is located in sections 4 and 5.
4. Specify the system's identifier or name in section 6.
5. Select the type of filtration system you are inspecting in section 7.
6. Select the type of maintenance service you are performing in section 9.
7. Evaluate the condition of each filter, including the HEPA and carbon filters, and record your findings in sections 10-13.
8. Check the UV lamp's status and hours of operation in sections 15 and 16.
9. Inspect the system's seals and gaskets in section 18.
10. Measure and record the airflow and pressure differential in sections 19 and 20, if available.
11. Inspect the motor and belt condition in sections 21 and 22.
12. Check the electrical connections and control panel in sections 23 and 24.
13. Report any air leaks found in section 25.
14. Assess the system's noise level in section 26.
15. Record any replacement parts used in section 27.
16. List any part numbers used in section 28.
17. Provide any recommended actions or repairs in section 29.
18. Schedule the next service date in section 30.
19. Sign and date the form in sections 31 and 32.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Technician Name** (`technician_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as the maintenance technician performing this service.
* **Technician ID** (`technician_id`, text, required): Enter your employee number or ID.
* **Maintenance Date** (`maintenance_date`, date, required): Record the date of the maintenance service.
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the facility where the system is located.
* **Facility Address** (`facility_address`, text, required): Enter the full address of the facility.
* **System ID or Name** (`system_id`, text, required): Enter the system's identifier or name.
* **System Type** (`system_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of filtration system being inspected (e.g., HEPA, Carbon, UV-C, etc.).
* **Maintenance Type** (`maintenance_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of maintenance service being performed (e.g., routine, repair, upgrade, etc.).
* **Pre-Filter Condition** (`pre_filter_condition`, select_one, required): Evaluate the current condition of the pre-filter (e.g., good, fair, poor, replaced).
* **Pre-Filter Replaced** (`pre_filter_replaced`, select_one, required): Check if the pre-filter has been replaced.
* **HEPA Filter Condition** (`hepa_filter_condition`, select_one, required): Evaluate the current condition of the HEPA filter (e.g., good, fair, poor, replaced).
* **HEPA Filter Replaced** (`hepa_filter_replaced`, select_one, required): Check if the HEPA filter has been replaced.
* **Carbon Filter Condition** (`carbon_filter_condition`, select_one, required): Evaluate the current condition of the carbon filter (e.g., good, fair, poor, replaced).
* **UV Lamp Status** (`uv_lamp_status`, select_one, required): Check the status of the UV lamp (e.g., working properly, diminished output, not working, replaced).
* **UV Lamp Hours** (`uv_lamp_hours`, number, optional): Record the hours of operation of the UV lamp, if available.
* **Housing Cleaned** (`housing_cleaned`, select_one, required): Check if the housing has been cleaned.
* **Seals and Gaskets** (`seals_gaskets`, select_one, required): Evaluate the condition of the seals and gaskets (e.g., good condition, fair condition, poor condition, replaced).
* **Airflow Measurement (CFM)** (`airflow_measurement`, number, optional): Record the measured airflow, if available.
* **Pressure Differential (inches WG)** (`pressure_differential`, number, optional): Record the filter pressure drop, if available.
* **Motor Condition** (`motor_condition`, select_one, required): Evaluate the condition of the motor (e.g., good, fair, poor, requires attention).
* **Belt Condition** (`belt_condition`, select_one, required): Evaluate the condition of the belt (e.g., good, fair, worn, replaced).
* **Electrical Connections** (`electrical_connections`, select_one, required): Check the condition of the electrical connections (e.g., all secure, some loose, issues found, not checked).
* **Control Panel Check** (`control_panel`, select_one, required): Inspect the control panel (e.g., all functions working, minor issues, major issues, not checked).
* **Air Leaks Detected** (`leaks_detected`, select_one, required): Report any air leaks found (e.g., none, minor, major).
* **Noise Level** (`noise_level`, select_one, required): Assess the system's noise level (e.g., normal, slightly elevated, abnormal, attention needed).
* **Parts Used** (`parts_used`, text, optional): List any replacement parts used.
* **Part Numbers** (`parts_part_numbers`, text, optional): List any part numbers used.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, text, required): Provide any suggested actions or repairs.
* **Next Service Date** (`next_service_date`, date, required): Schedule the next service date.
* **Technician Signature** (`technician_signature`, text, required): Sign and date the form with your name and employee number.
* **Supervisor Approval** (`supervisor_approval`, text, optional): Signature of the supervisor or authorized personnel.
* **Checklist Status** (`checklist_status`, select_one, required): Evaluate the overall status of the system (e.g., complete, minor issues noted, incomplete, follow-up required).

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly.
- If a field is not applicable, select 'Not applicable' from the options.
- Review the form carefully before signing and submitting it.
- Use this checklist as a guide to ensure consistent and thorough maintenance services.
