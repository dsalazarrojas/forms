# Quick Release Steering Wheel Installation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather and record information about the installation of a quick release steering wheel on a vehicle. It will help mechanics to standardize the process and ensure that all necessary information is collected.

## How To Complete This Form

* Start by selecting the "Customer Info" page.
* Enter the customer's name, address, and contact information.
* Proceed to the "Vehicle Info" page.
* Enter the vehicle's make, model, and year.
* On the next page, enter the "Mechanic Info" page, which is for the mechanic performing the installation.
* Enter your name, company, and any relevant contact information.
* Select the "Vehicle Model" page.
* Enter the correct model of the vehicle being serviced.
* Move on to the "Vehicle Year" page.
* Enter the year of the vehicle being serviced.
* On the "Steering Wheel Serial Number" page, enter the serial number of the installed steering wheel.
* On the "Steering Wheel Part Number" page, enter the part number of the installed steering wheel.
* Enter any installation notes on the "Installation Notes" page.
* Finally, sign and date the "Signature" page.
* Confirm the "Date" of the installation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for entering the customer's name and contact information.
* **Vehicle Info** (`vehicle_info`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for entering the vehicle's make, model, and year.
* **Mechanic Info** (`mechanic_info`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for entering the mechanic's name and contact information.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for entering the correct model of the vehicle being serviced.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, required: false)
	+ This field is for entering the year of the vehicle being serviced.
* **Steering Wheel Serial Number** (`steering_wheel_serial_number`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for entering the serial number of the installed steering wheel.
* **Steering Wheel Part Number** (`steering_wheel_part_number`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for entering the part number of the installed steering wheel.
* **Installation Notes** (`installation_notes`, note, required: false)
	+ This field is for taking notes during the installation process.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for obtaining the mechanic's signature and confirmation of the installation.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false)
	+ This field is for entering the date of the installation.
