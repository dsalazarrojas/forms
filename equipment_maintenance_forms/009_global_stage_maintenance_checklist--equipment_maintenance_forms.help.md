# Global Stage Maintenance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Stage Maintenance Checklist is a form used by venue staff or authorized personnel to perform regular maintenance and inspections of stage equipment. It helps record and report any issues or defects found during these inspections and provides follow-up information for subsequent actions taken.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Complete each field according to the instructions below.
2. Enter the relevant information for each field based on your observations and inspections.
3. Make sure to sign and date the form to ensure accurate record-keeping.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Stage Inspection** (`stage_inspection`, number, required): Enter the number of stage inspections performed.
* **Stage Area** (`stage_area`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the stage area inspected.
* **Stage Equipment** (`stage_equipment`, select_one, optional): Select the type of stage equipment inspected (Lighting, Sound, Rigging).
* **Stage Equipment Condition** (`stage_equipment_condition`, select_multiple, optional): Select the condition of the inspected stage equipment (Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Stage Inspection Frequency** (`stage_inspection_frequency`, date, required): Enter the date of the last inspection or the planned inspection date.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the next scheduled follow-up inspection.
* **Follow-up Status** (`follow_up_status`, select_one, optional): Select the follow-up status (Completed, In Progress, Not Started).
* **Venue Staff Name** (`venue_staff_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the venue staff member performing the inspection.
* **Venue Staff Email** (`venue_staff_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the venue staff member performing the inspection.
* **Venue Staff Phone** (`venue_staff_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the venue staff member performing the inspection.
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, text, optional): Describe the issue or defect found during the inspection.
* **Issue Priority** (`issue_priority`, select_one, optional): Select the priority level of the issue (High, Medium, Low).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments related to the inspection.
* **Issue Category** (`issue_category`, select_one, optional): Select the category of the issue (Technical, Safety, Maintenance).
* **Issue Subcategory** (`issue_subcategory`, select_one, optional): Select the subcategory of the issue (Lighting, Sound, Rigging).
* **Issue Location** (`issue_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the issue was found.
* **Issue Reported By** (`issue_reported_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person reporting the issue.
* **Issue Reported Date** (`issue_reported_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the issue was reported.
* **Action Taken** (`action_taken`, text, optional): Describe the action taken to address the issue.
* **Action Date** (`action_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the action was taken.
* **Follow-up Details** (`follow_up_details`, text, optional): Enter any follow-up information related to the issue.
* **Venue Name** (`venue_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the venue where the inspection was performed.
* **Venue Address** (`venue_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the venue where the inspection was performed.
* **Venue City** (`venue_city`, text, optional): Enter the city of the venue where the inspection was performed.
* **Follow-up Contact** (`follow_up_contact`, text, optional): Enter any follow-up contact information for the issue.
