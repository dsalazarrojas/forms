# CPR Chest Compression Feedback Device Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect data on the inspection of a CPR Chest Compression Feedback Device, ensuring its continued functionality and effectiveness in a healthcare or medical setting.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in your name and title as the person performing the inspection, which will help identify who conducted the check.
2. Enter the date of the inspection, which is essential for tracking and record-keeping purposes.
3. Enter the serial number from the device's label, which helps identify the device being inspected.
4. Check the battery condition according to the options provided: Good, Replace soon, or Replace now.
5. Verify the sensor's calibration status: Calibrated, Requires calibration, or Unknown.
6. Describe any physical damage observed during the inspection, such as dents, cracks, or loose parts.
7. Perform a functionality test on the device and select Pass or Fail, indicating whether the checks passed or not.
8. If any corrective actions were taken, describe the actions and date performed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspector name**: This field is where you enter your name as the person performing the inspection.
* **Inspection date**: This is where you enter the date of the inspection, which is essential for tracking and record-keeping purposes.
* **Device serial number**: Enter the serial number from the device's label to identify the device being inspected.
* **Battery condition**: Check the battery condition according to the options provided. This helps ensure the device is functioning correctly.
	+ Good: The battery is in a good condition and can be used for future inspections.
	+ Replace soon: The battery is nearing its end-of-life and should be replaced soon.
	+ Replace now: The battery is already failed and should be replaced immediately.
* **Sensor calibration status**: Verify the sensor's calibration status according to the options provided. This ensures accurate readings from the device.
	+ Calibrated: The sensor is calibrated and ready for use.
	+ Requires calibration: The sensor needs to be calibrated before use.
	+ Unknown: The calibration status is unknown or not checked.
* **Physical damage observed**: Describe any physical damage found during the inspection, such as dents, cracks, or loose parts.
* **Functionality test result**: Perform a functionality test on the device and select Pass or Fail, indicating whether the checks passed or not.
* **Corrective actions**: Describe any actions taken as a result of the inspection, including the date and details of the actions.
