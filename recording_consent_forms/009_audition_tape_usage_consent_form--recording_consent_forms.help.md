# Audition Tape Usage Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for performers to provide consent for the use and distribution of their audition recordings. It ensures that you understand how your recorded audition will be handled.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, please read each section carefully and select the relevant options. You can skip sections that do not apply to you.

1. Enter your date of signing at the top of the form (`Consent Form Date` field).
2. Enter your full name, email address, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
3. Provide the title of the project and the date of the audition recording in the respective fields.
4. Choose how the recording was made (`How Was the Recording Made`).
5. Enter the name of the person conducting the audition (`Casting Director Name`), if applicable.
6. Select your consent for the distribution of your recording.
7. Choose your consent for internal use, including casting decisions.
8. Decide whether your recording can be used in the final production.
9. Choose your consent for promotional and marketing use.
10. Indicate if you are entitled to residual payments.
11. Select if you require on-screen credit if your recording is used.
12. Choose your consent for data collection.
13. Decide on the storage duration of your recording.
14. Indicate if you can request deletion after the audition.
15. Choose your union status.
16. Agree or disagree with the compensation terms.
17. Sign the legal signature authorization to confirm your consent.
18. Add any additional terms or conditions in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Consent Form Date** (1): Enter the date this consent form is being signed. This date is a required field.
* **Performer Name** (2): Enter your full legal name. This is a required field.
* **Performer Email** (3): Enter your email address. This is a required field.
* **Performer Phone** (4): Enter your contact phone number. This is a required field.
* **Production Title** (5): Enter the name of the project (film, show, etc.) you are auditioning for. This is a required field.
* **Audition Recording Date** (6): Enter the date of the audition recording. This is a required field.
* **How Was the Recording Made** (7): Select how the recording was made (In-Person at Studio, Self-Tape Submission, Virtual/Zoom Audition, etc.). This is a required field.
* **Casting Director Name** (8): Enter the name of the person conducting the audition, if applicable. This is an optional field.
* **Production Company Name** (9): Enter the name of the production company. This is a required field.
* **Consent to Distribute Recording** (10): Select your consent for the distribution of your recording. This includes choosing between Full Consent, Limited Distribution, No Consent, or Need to Review Contract. This is a required field.
* **Internal Use - Casting Only** (11): Choose your consent for internal use, including casting decisions. This is a required field.
* **Use in Final Production** (12): Decide whether your recording can be used in the final production. You can choose between Yes - Agree, No - Do Not Use, or Only if Cast in Role. This is a required field.
* **Promotional and Marketing Use** (13): Choose your consent for promotional and marketing use. You can select between Yes - Full Promotional Use, Yes - Limited Promotional Use, or No - Do Not Promote. This is a required field.
* **Residual Payments for Use** (14): Indicate if you are entitled to residual payments. You can choose between Yes - Entitled to Residuals, No - Waive Residual Rights, TBD - To Be Negotiated, or Not Applicable. This is an optional field.
* **On-Screen Credit** (15): Select if you require on-screen credit if your recording is used. You can choose between Yes - Credit Required, No - Not Required, or Flexible. This is an optional field.
* **Consent to Data Collection** (16): Choose your consent for data collection. You can select between Agree or Disagree. This is a required field.
* **Recording Storage Duration** (17): Decide on the storage duration of your recording. You can choose between Indefinitely, Project Duration Only, 1 Year, 2 Years, or Other - Specify. This is a required field.
* **Request Recording Deletion** (18): Indicate if you can request deletion after the audition. You can select between Yes - Can Request Deletion, No - Cannot Request, or After Casting Complete Only. This is a required field.
* **Union Affiliation** (19): Enter your union status. You can choose between SAG-AFTRA Member, Non-Union, or Eligible to Join. This is an optional field.
* **Compensation Terms Agreed** (20): Agree or disagree with the compensation terms. You can choose between Yes - Agree, No - Do Not Agree, or Need Clarification. This is a required field.
* **Legal Signature Authorization** (21): Sign the legal signature authorization to confirm your consent. This is a required field.
* **Additional Terms or Conditions** (22): Add any special requests or modifications in this text field. This is an optional field.
