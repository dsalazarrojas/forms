## Academic Reflection Video Publication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from individuals who have recorded videos as part of an academic reflection project. It ensures that they understand and agree to the terms and conditions of publishing their video reflections.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to answer all required fields.
3. Choose the correct options for each field based on your preferences.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of the Video Project** (`project_title`): Enter the official title of the recording.
* **Name of Person Featured in Video** (`student_faculty_name`): Enter the name of the person featured in the video.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address for correspondence regarding the video.
* **I authorize the publication of the video reflection for educational purposes** (`publication_consent`): Choose whether you authorize or do not authorize the publication of the video reflection for educational purposes.
	+ "Authorized": You agree to the publication of your video reflection.
	+ "Not Authorized": You do not agree to the publication of your video reflection.
* **Select platforms where the video may be published** (`platforms_allowed`): Check all platforms where you agree to the video being published.
	+ "University YouTube Channel"
	+ "Institutional Repository"
	+ "Course Management System"
	+ "Social Media Handles"
* **Date from which consent is effective** (`effective_date`): Enter the start date for the permissions.
* **How long may the video remain published?** (`duration_of_consent`): Choose the duration for which you agree to the video being published.
	+ "Indefinitely"
	+ "For 1 Year"
	+ "For 5 Years"
	+ "Until End of Current Semester"
* **Do you wish to review the final edit before publication?** (`right_to_review`): Choose whether you want to review the final edit before publication.
	+ "True": You want to review the final edit.
	+ "False": You do not want to review the final edit.
* **I have read and agree to the video publication terms** (`terms_acceptance`): Choose whether you have read and agree to the terms.
	+ "I Agree": You have read and agree to the terms.
	+ "Other": You have not read or do not agree to the terms.
* **Digital Signature - Type Name** (`digital_signature`): Enter your name for verification.
* **Signing Date** (`signing_date`): Enter the current date.

Please note that all fields are required, and you must fill them out completely and accurately to ensure that your consent is valid.
