# Team Recruitment Video Appearance Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from participants for their appearance in team recruitment videos.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the participant's name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Enter the name of the school the participant is associated with in the "School Name" field.
3. Select the participant's role in the video in the "Role" field.
4. Choose the media location where the video will be recorded (Online, In-person, or Phone) in the "Media Location" field.
5. Enter the participant's email address in the "Email" field.
6. Enter the participant's phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Select the date of consent in the "Date of Consent" field.
8. Confirm if consent has been given or not in the "Consent Given" field.
9. Enter any additional note if necessary in the "Note" field.
10. Enter the participant's phone number in the "Participant Phone" field.
11. Enter the participant's email address in the "Participant Email" field.
12. Enter the school contact person's name in the "School Contact" field.
13. Enter the school contact person's phone number in the "School Contact Phone" field.
14. Enter the school contact person's email address in the "School Contact Email" field.
15. Enter the name of the school (if different from the participant's school) in the "School Name 2" field.
16. Enter the participant's address in the "Participant Address" field.
17. Enter the school's address in the "School Address" field.
18. Select the role of the school contact person in the "Role 2" field.
19. Choose the media location where the school contact person is associated with (Online, In-person, or Phone) in the "Media Location 2" field.
20. Enter the date of consent given by the school contact person in the "Consent Date" field.
21. Select the media location where the participant will be recorded (Online, In-person, or Phone) in the "Participant Media Location" field.
22. Confirm if the participant has given consent in the "Participant Consent" field.
23. Enter the participant's school name in the "Participant School Name" field.
24. Enter the participant's school contact person's name in the "Participant School Contact" field.
25. Enter the participant's school address in the "Participant School Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (Participant Name, Text, Required): Please enter the participant's name.
* **School Name** (School Name, Text, Required): Enter the name of the school the participant is associated with.
* **Role** (Role, Select One, Required): Select the role of the participant in the video.
* **Media Location** (Media Location, Select Multiple, Required): Choose where the video will be recorded (Online, In-person, or Phone).
* **Email** (Email, Email, Required): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, Text, Required): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Date of Consent** (Date, Date, Required): Select the date of consent.
* **Consent Given** (Consent Given, Select One, Required): Confirm if consent has been given or not.
* **Note** (Note, Note, Optional): Enter any additional note if necessary.
* **Participant Phone** (Participant Phone, Text, Required): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Email** (Participant Email, Email, Required): Enter the participant's email address.
* **School Contact** (School Contact, Text, Required): Enter the school contact person's name.
* **School Contact Phone** (School Contact Phone, Text, Required): Enter the school contact person's phone number.
* **School Contact Email** (School Contact Email, Email, Required): Enter the school contact person's email address.
* **School Name 2** (School Name 2, Text, Required): Enter the name of the school (if different from the participant's school).
* **Participant Address** (Participant Address, Text, Required): Enter the participant's address.
* **School Address** (School Address, Text, Required): Enter the school's address.
* **Role 2** (Role 2, Select One, Required): Select the role of the school contact person.
* **Media Location 2** (Media Location 2, Select Multiple, Required): Choose where the school contact person is associated with (Online, In-person, or Phone).
* **Consent Date** (Consent Date, Date, Required): Enter the date of consent given by the school contact person.
* **Participant Media Location** (Participant Media Location, Select Multiple, Required): Select the media location where the participant will be recorded (Online, In-person, or Phone).
* **Participant Consent** (Participant Consent, Select One, Required): Confirm if the participant has given consent.
* **Participant School Name** (Participant School Name, Text, Required): Enter the participant's school name.
* **Participant School Contact** (Participant School Contact, Text, Required): Enter the participant's school contact person's name.
* **Participant School Address** (Participant School Address, Text, Required): Enter the participant's school address.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and truthfully.
* If unsure about any field, double-check the instructions above or ask for help.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, contact the relevant team or authority.
