# Medical Ethics Education Series Recording Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to capture the consent of individuals participating in medical ethics education series, ensuring that they understand and agree to the terms and conditions of their participation. This form is a crucial step in the educational process, and it helps to establish a record of informed consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Education Provider**: Enter the name of the education provider who is responsible for the medical ethics education series.
2. **Participant Name**: Enter your name as the participant in the medical ethics education series.
3. **Date Signed**: Enter the date on which you signed this consent form. If you're unsure, refer to the current date.
4. **Time Signed**: Enter the time at which you signed this consent form.
5. **Session ID**: Enter the unique ID assigned to this session. This ID will help us keep track of your participation.
6. **Participant Signature**: Provide your signature, as a verification of your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Education Provider**: This is the name of the organization or person providing the medical ethics education series. Enter the name as it appears on their letterhead or business card.
* **Participant Name**: This is your name as the participant in the medical ethics education series. Please enter your full name to ensure accurate record-keeping.
* **Date Signed**: This field is used to record the date on which you signed this consent form. It's essential to enter the correct date to avoid any issues with our records.
* **Time Signed**: Enter the time at which you signed this consent form. This helps us keep accurate records of when you gave your consent.
* **Session ID**: This field is for the unique ID assigned to this session. It will help us track your participation accurately.
* **Participant Signature**: This is a required field where you must sign your name as a verification of your consent. Please use the space provided for your signature.
