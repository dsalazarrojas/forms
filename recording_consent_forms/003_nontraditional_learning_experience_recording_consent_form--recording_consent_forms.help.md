# Nontraditional Learning Experience Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information and consent for non-traditional learning experiences, which are programs or activities that fall outside of traditional classroom settings. The form is designed to ensure that participants understand the recording and use of their non-traditional learning experiences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the participant's name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the participant's parent or guardian has given permission for the recording.
3. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the participant has given their own permission for the recording.
4. Enter the participant's age (if applicable).
5. Enter the participant's email address.
6. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the participant has had a learning experience.
7. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the participant has participated in a training program.
8. Select one of the following:
	* High: The participant has a high level of education or experience.
	* Low: The participant has a low level of education or experience.
9. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the participant has participated in an internship.
10. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate the purpose of the recording.
11. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate the method of recording (e.g. video, audio, etc.).
12. Enter the duration of the recording in minutes.
13. Select the date and time of consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant who is signing this form.
* **Parent Permission**: Select "Yes" if the participant's parent or guardian has given permission for the recording.
* **Participant Permission**: Select "Yes" if the participant has given their own permission for the recording.
* **Participant Age**: Enter the age of the participant, if applicable.
* **Participant Email**: Enter the participant's email address.
* **Learning Experience**: Select "Yes" if the participant has had a learning experience.
* **Training Program**: Select "Yes" if the participant has participated in a training program.
* **Education Level**: Select one of the following:
	+ High: The participant has a high level of education or experience.
	+ Low: The participant has a low level of education or experience.
* **Internship Type**: Select "Yes" if the participant has participated in an internship.
* **Recording Purpose**: Select "Yes" if the participant has consented to the recording for this purpose.
* **Recording Method**: Select "Yes" if the recording method is acceptable to the participant.
* **Recording Duration**: Enter the duration of the recording in minutes.
* **Consent Date**: Enter the date and time of consent.
