# Educational Video Licensing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from individuals or organizations for the use of educational videos. It typically includes contact information, the purpose of the use, a consent text, a signature, and a date of consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. On the first page, provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" field.
2. In the "Purpose of Use" field, describe the intended use of the educational video.
3. In the "Consent Text" field, type in the specific text you wish to be displayed, if any, to be included in the license agreement.
4. In the "Signature" field, sign your name to indicate that you have read and agreed upon the terms.
5. In the "Date" field, select the date on which you are giving your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`consent_form_contact_info`, `text`, optional): Enter your name and contact information, including your email address and phone number, so that we can get in touch with you if needed.
* **Purpose of Use** (`consent_form_purpose`, `text`, optional): Describe the intended use of the educational video, such as for a research project, a presentation, or a educational course.
* **Consent Text** (`consent_form_consent_text`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional text you wish to be included in the license agreement, such as specific terms and conditions.
* **Signature** (`consent_form_consent_signature`, `text`, optional): Sign your name to confirm that you have read and agreed upon the terms.
* **Date** (`consent_form_consent_date`, `date`, optional): Select the date on which you are giving your consent, which will be used to track the agreement.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate contact information so that we can reach out to you if needed.
* Clearly describe the purpose of use to ensure that we can understand your intended use of the video.
* Be specific and clear in your consent text to avoid any confusion.
* Sign your name to confirm your agreement.
* Select the correct date to ensure that the consent is up-to-date.
