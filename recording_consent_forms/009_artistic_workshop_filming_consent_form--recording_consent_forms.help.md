# Artistic Workshop Filming Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to obtain consent from participants for video recording and photography during artistic workshops or events. It ensures that participants are aware of and agree to the use of their image and likeness for various purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your full name in the "Participant Full Name" field.
2. Provide a brief description of the workshop or event in the "Workshop or Event Title" field.
3. Indicate whether you are under 18 years of age by checking "True" or "False" in the "Is the participant under 18 years of age" field.
4. If you are under 18, provide the name of your parent or guardian in the "Parent or Guardian Name" field.
5. Check "True" or "False" to indicate your consent to being video recorded in the "I consent to being video recorded" field, and to being photographed in the "I consent to still photography" field.
6. Select all specific uses that you grant permission for in the "Permission for specific uses" field (e.g., social media, workshop archives, promotional brochures).
7. Specify how you would like to be credited in the "How would you like to be credited" field (e.g., full name, first name only, anonymous).
8. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
9. Date and sign the form in the "Date Signed" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop or Event Title**: A brief description of the workshop or event you are participating in.
* **Participant Full Name**: Your full name as a participant.
* **Is the participant under 18 years of age**: Check "True" if you are under 18, or "False" if you are 18 or older.
* **Parent or Guardian Name**: The name of your parent or guardian if you are under 18.
* **I consent to being video recorded**: Check "True" to give your consent to being video recorded, or "False" to decline.
* **I consent to still photography**: Check "True" to give your consent to being photographed, or "False" to decline.
* **Permission for specific uses**: Check all specific uses that you grant permission for (e.g., social media, workshop archives, promotional brochures).
* **How would you like to be credited**: Specify how you would like to be credited (e.g., full name, first name only, anonymous).
* **Email Address**: Your email address for contact purposes.
* **Date Signed**: Date and sign the form to complete the consent.
