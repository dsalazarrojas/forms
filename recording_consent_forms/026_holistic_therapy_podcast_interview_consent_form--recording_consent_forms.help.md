# Holistic Therapy Podcast Interview Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holistic Therapy Podcast Interview Consent Form is a necessary step before each episode is recorded. Please take a moment to review this guide to ensure you complete the form accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
4. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
5. Choose your consent for the recording and sharing of your interview from the "Consent Given" dropdown menu.
6. If you have any additional comments or questions, type them in the "Additional Comments" field.
7. Enter the name of the podcast you are appearing on in the "Podcast Name" field.
8. Enter the URL of the podcast in the "Podcast URL" field.
9. Provide your email address in the "Guest Email" field.
10. Enter your contact number in the "Guest Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your first name here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Please enter your email address here.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Please enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Please enter your contact number here.
* **Consent Given** (`consent_given`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose how you wish to be contacted regarding the recording and sharing of your interview.
*   Option 1: I am willing to share my interview on the podcast
*   Option 2: I am willing to share my interview on the podcast, but only with a specific group of people
*   Option 3: I am not willing to share my interview on the podcast
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, required: false): If you have any additional comments or questions, please type them here.
* **Podcast Name** (`podcast_name`, text, required: false): Please enter the name of the podcast you are appearing on.
* **Podcast URL** (`podcast_url`, text, required: false): Please enter the URL of the podcast you are appearing on.
* **Guest Email** (`guest_email`, email, required: false): Please enter your email address here.
* **Guest Number** (`guest_number`, text, required: false): Please enter your contact number here.
