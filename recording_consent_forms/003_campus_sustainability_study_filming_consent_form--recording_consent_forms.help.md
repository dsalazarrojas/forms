# Campus Sustainability Study Filming Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Campus Sustainability Study Filming Consent Form is a crucial document that ensures participants in campus filming activities have their rights respected and their data protected. This form outlines the terms of use for the footage and the participant's preferences for how they wish to be identified.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full legal name in the Participant Name field.
2. Select your role or affiliation (e.g., Student, Faculty, Staff, Community Member) in the Role or Affiliation field.
3. Enter your email address in the Email Address field.
4. Enter your phone number in the Phone Number field.
5. Select the project title and researcher or filmmaker name in the respective fields.
6. Choose the date of filming and location of filming in the respective fields.
7. Select the types of recordings you authorize (e.g., Video, Audio, Photographs).
8. Choose how you wish to be identified (e.g., Use my real name, Use a pseudonym, Keep me anonymous).
9. Confirm that you agree to the terms of the study.
10. Sign and date the consent form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: Enter your full legal name as per your identification documents.
* **Role or Affiliation**: Select your current role or affiliation with the university or institution (e.g., Student, Faculty, Staff, Community Member).
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Project Title**: Enter the title of the project you are participating in (e.g., Campus Sustainability Documentary).
* **Researcher or Filmmaker Name**: Enter the name of the researcher or filmmaker associated with the project.
* **Date of Filming**: Choose the date when filming will take place.
* **Location of Filming**: Enter the location on campus where filming will occur (e.g., Campus building or outdoor area).
* **Recording Types**: Select which types of recordings you authorize (e.g., Video, Audio, Photographs).
* **Use of Footage**: Choose all intended uses of the footage you authorize (e.g., Educational Use, Research Presentation, Public Website, Social Media, Not for Public Release).
* **Consent to be Recorded**: Indicate whether you give your consent to be recorded.
* **Use of Name**: Choose how you wish to be identified (e.g., Use my real name, Use a pseudonym, Keep me anonymous).
* **Agreement to Terms**: Confirm that you agree to the terms of the study.
* **Confirmation Date**: Sign and date the consent form to confirm your agreement.
