# Talent Demo Reel Usage Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from talent for the usage of their demo reels. It is intended for use by talent agencies, management companies, and other professionals to ensure that the talent has given their consent and agreement for the usage of their demo reels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Select a date for the **Date of Agreement**.
6. Select a time for the **Date of Agreement Time**.
7. Provide your **Signature** in the "Signature" field.
8. Select one of the options for the **Consent Agreement** field, indicating your acknowledgement of the terms of the usage of your demo reel.
9. In the **Consent** field, provide a brief statement indicating your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Please enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name**: Please enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Agreement**: Select a date when you agree to the terms of the usage of your demo reel.
* **Date of Agreement Time**: Select a time when you agree to the terms of the usage of your demo reel.
* **Signature**: Please provide your signature as proof of your agreement.
* **Consent Agreement**: Please select one of the options to indicate your acknowledgement of the terms of the usage of your demo reel. If you have any questions or concerns about the terms, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* **Consent**: Please enter a brief statement indicating your consent to the usage of your demo reel.
