# Literacy Program Filming Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Literacy Program Filming Consent Form is used to gather consent from participants in the Literacy Program and to collect information about the filming of their program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant's details in the **Participant Details** field.
2. Enter the participant's **Name**.
3. Enter the participant's **Age**.
4. Select the participant's **Education Level**.
5. If applicable, select the device used for filming, such as **Audio Recorder**, **Camera**, or **Phone**. If not, select **Other** and fill in the details.
6. Enter the date the recording took place (**Recording Date**).
7. Enter the time the recording took place (**Recording Time**).
8. Write a note about the filming, including any relevant details (optional).
9. Sign the form with your name in the **Consent Signed By** field.
10. Sign the form with an authorized representative of the organization in the **Organization Signature** field.
11. Enter the date you signed the form (**Date Signed**).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details**:
  * This field is used to collect participant's basic information.
* **Name**:
  * Enter the participant's full name.
* **Age**:
  * Enter the participant's age.
* **Education Level**:
  * Select the level of education that the participant has completed (e.g. elementary school, high school, university, etc.).
* **Filmed By**:
  * Select the device used for filming, or if not applicable, select **Other** and fill in the details.
* **Recording Device**:
  * Select the device used for recording (e.g. **Audio Recorder**, **Camera**, or **Phone**).
* **Recording Date**:
  * Enter the date the recording took place.
* **Recording Time**:
  * Enter the time the recording took place.
* **Filming Consent**:
  * Write a note about the filming, including any relevant details.
* **Consent Signed By**:
  * Sign the form with your name.
* **Organization Signature**:
  * Sign the form with an authorized representative of the organization.
* **Date Signed**:
  * Enter the date you signed the form.
