# environmental_research_drone_footage_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The environmental research drone footage consent form is a document used to record the details of a drone-based research project, including the equipment used, location, and purpose of the filming.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of drone used for the project from the "Research Drone" field.
2.  Enter the date on which the footage was captured or will be captured in the "Date of Capture" field.
3.  Enter the time at which the footage was captured or will be captured in the "Time of Capture" field.
4.  Enter the location where the footage was captured or will be captured in the "Location" field.
5.  List the individuals who have reviewed and approved the project's research goals and objectives in the "Prophets" field.
6.  Enter a brief description of the research goals and objectives in the "Research Goals" field.
7.  Enter a detailed description of the research objectives in the "Research Objectives" field.
8.  Select the type of filming equipment used from the "Filming Equipment" field.
9.  Enter the location where filming took place or will take place in the "Filming Location" field.
10. Enter the duration of the filming session in the "Filming Duration" field.
11. Enter the date on which filming took place or will take place in the "Filming Date" field.
12. Enter the time at which filming took place or will take place in the "Filming Time" field.
13. Enter a brief description of the reason for filming in the "Filming Reason" field.
14. Confirm whether filming permission was obtained in the "Filming Permission" field.
15. Confirm whether filming supervision was provided in the "Filming Supervision" field.
16. (Optional) Enter the type of recording equipment used in the "Recording Equipment" field.
17. (Optional) Enter the location where recording took place or will take place in the "Recording Location" field.
18. (Optional) Enter the duration of the recording session in the "Recording Duration" field.
