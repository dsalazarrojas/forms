# film_dialogue_clip_usage_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect consent from participants for the use of their dialogue clips in film or media projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant details (page 1).
2. Provide clip details (page 2).
3. Choose your consent option (page 3).
4. Write your agreement (page 4).
5. Sign the agreement (page 5).
6. Enter your name and date signed (pages 6 and 7).
7. Add your participant signature (page 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required: false): Fill in your name, email, and other relevant contact information.
* **Clip Details** (`clip_details`, text, required: false): Describe the clip you're providing, including its title, location, and any other relevant information.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required: false): Choose between "I consent to allow my dialogue clip to be used in film or media projects" and "I do not consent to allow my dialogue clip to be used in film or media projects".
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, text, required: false): Write a brief statement explaining your agreement and any conditions or restrictions.
* **Signed By** (`signed_by`, text, required: false): Enter your name as the participant.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required: false): Enter the date you sign this form.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, required: false): Add your signature as proof of your consent.
* **Agreement Page** (`agreement_page`, text, required: false): Enter the text of your agreement, including any necessary terms and conditions.
