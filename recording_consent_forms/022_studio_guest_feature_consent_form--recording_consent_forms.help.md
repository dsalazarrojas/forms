# Studio Guest Feature Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Studio Guest Feature Consent Form is used to collect information from guests on their permissions and contact details. This form is part of the studio feature and is used to manage guest interactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for Guest Permissions. This determines the level of access and interaction you are willing to give to the guest.
2. If you are not a guest, you can skip this field.
3. Sign your name for the Signature field. This is a one-time requirement for guests.
4. Fill in your contact details in the Contact Details field. This includes your name, email, and any other relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Permissions** (`guest_permissions`, select_multiple, optional): This field asks you to select whether you give the guest permission to interact with you in the studio. You can choose either 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required): This is a one-time requirement where you are asked to sign your name. This field is a note type, which means you can enter any information you want, but it is a required field.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): This field is for you to enter any contact information you want to provide, such as your name, email, and phone number.
