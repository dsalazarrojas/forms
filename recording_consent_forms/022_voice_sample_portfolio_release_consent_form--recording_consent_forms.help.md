# Voice Sample Portfolio Release Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Voice Sample Portfolio Release Consent Form is a crucial document that outlines the agreement between the submitter and the organization regarding the release of their voice sample portfolio. This form ensures that the submitter is aware of the terms and conditions of the release, which is essential for the proper use and protection of their intellectual property.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review and confirm your agreement to release the voice sample portfolio on page 1.
2. Select the type of release that applies to you on page 2.
3. Provide any additional conditions or notes related to the release on page 3.
4. Enter the date when the release will start on page 4.
5. Specify how the recording will be released on page 5.
6. Enter your name on page 6.
7. Enter your email address on page 7.
8. Enter your phone number on page 8.
9. Add any additional notes about this release on page 9.
10. Review and confirm that you have completed the form correctly on page 10.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submission Agreement** (page 1, checkbox):
	+ This field confirms your agreement to release the voice sample portfolio. Please select "I agree" to proceed.
* **Release Type** (page 2, select_one):
	+ Select one of the options below:
		+ General Release: This type of release allows for the general use of your voice sample portfolio without any restrictions.
		+ Specialized Release: This type of release allows for the use of your voice sample portfolio for specific purposes, such as commercial use, educational use, or research purposes.
* **Release Details** (page 3, note):
	+ Please provide any additional conditions or notes related to the release, such as any restrictions or limitations on how the portfolio can be used.
* **Release Date** (page 4, date):
	+ Enter the date when the release will start. This is the date when the voice sample portfolio can be released for use.
* **Recording Release** (page 5, note):
	+ Please specify how the recording will be released, such as through a public performance, educational use, or online publication.
* **Submitter's Name** (page 6, text):
	+ Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Submitter's Email** (page 7, email):
	+ Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Submitter's Phone** (page 8, text):
	+ Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Confirmation Notes** (page 9, note):
	+ Add any additional notes about this release, such as any questions or concerns you have.
* **Submission Complete** (page 10, text):
	+ Review and confirm that you have completed the form correctly. Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
