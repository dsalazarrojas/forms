# Eco Documentary Interview Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Eco Documentary Interview Consent Form is designed to obtain consent from interview participants for recording and sharing their conversations during an environmental or documentary project. The form ensures that participants understand how their data will be used and are willing to participate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and read all the fields.
2. For each field, make sure you understand what is being asked before providing an answer.
3. Fill in your answers according to your preferences and needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Recording Consent Forms 
* **Recording Consent Forms** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select all the types of recording consent forms that apply to your project. Options are 'Yes' and 'No'. This field is not required, but it is essential to select the correct options to ensure that participants understand the scope of the recording.

### Sharing Consent Forms 
* **Sharing Consent Forms** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select all the types of consent forms that apply to your project. Options are 'Yes' and 'No'. This field is not required, but it is essential to select the correct options to ensure that participants understand how their data will be shared.

### Project Description 
* **Project Description** (`text`, optional): A brief description of the project. This field is not required, but it is helpful to provide a clear and concise overview of the project.

### Project Location 
* **Project Location** (`text`, optional): Where the project will be conducted. This field is not required, but it is useful to specify where the project will take place.

### Project Duration 
* **Project Duration** (`number`, optional): Length of the project. This field is not required, but it can provide useful information for planning and scheduling purposes.

### Recording Consent 
* **Recording Consent** (`select_one`, optional): Check if you are willing to be recorded during the interview. Options are 'True' and 'False'. This field is not required, but it is essential to ensure that participants understand if they will be recorded.

### Sharing Consent 
* **Sharing Consent** (`select_one`, optional): Check if you are willing to have your recorded data shared. Options are 'True' and 'False'. This field is not required, but it is essential to ensure that participants understand how their data will be used.

### Project Name 
* **Project Name** (`text`, optional): Title of the project. This field is not required, but it is helpful to specify the project title.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by each participant, and it is essential to ensure that all participants understand their rights and consent to the recording and sharing of their data.
