# Community Health Story Archive Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Health Story Archive Consent Form is used to collect consent and preferences from individuals who want to share their stories with the community health story archive.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please review and understand the purpose of this form.
2. Enter your full legal name in the "Storyteller name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email address" field.
4. Choose your consent for recording and storing your story.
5. Select the permitted uses of your story.
6. Choose your preference for anonymity.
7. If you wish to withdraw your consent, please follow the instructions in the "How to withdraw consent" field.
8. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Storyteller name** (`storyteller_name`, text, required): Enter your full legal name as you would like it to be displayed on your story.
* **Email address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact and permissions purposes.
* **Permission to record story** (`permission_recording`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes I give permission" if you agree to record your story, or "No I do not give permission" if you do not agree.
* **Permission to store story in archive** (`permission_storage`, select_one, required): Choose "True" to allow long-term archival of your recording, or "False" to not allow it.
* **Rights for use of story** (`usage_rights`, select_multiple, required): Select all permitted uses of your story, such as "Research", "Educational materials", "Promotional materials", and "Public archive".
* **Preference for anonymity** (`anonymity_preference`, select_one, required): Choose "Use my name", "Use initials", or "Anonymize my story" to specify your preference.
* **How to withdraw consent** (`withdraw_instructions`, text, required): If you change your mind, follow these instructions to withdraw your consent.
* **Signature name** (`signature_name`, text, required): Type your full name as a signature confirmation.
* **Signature date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Date and sign to confirm your consent.
