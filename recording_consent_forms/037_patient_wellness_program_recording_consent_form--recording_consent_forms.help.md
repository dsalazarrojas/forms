# Patient Wellness Program Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain the patient's consent for recording their data for the Patient Wellness Program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete the form with your personal details.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure that you understand the purpose and requirements.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information**: This field is used to collect your personal details, including your name and any other identifying information.
	+ **Label**: Patient Information
	+ **Type**: Text
	+ **Required**: No
* **Contact Information**: This field is used to collect your contact details, including your address and phone number.
	+ **Label**: Contact Information
	+ **Type**: Text
	+ **Required**: No
* **Phone Number**: This field is used to collect your phone number for contact purposes.
	+ **Label**: Phone Number
	+ **Type**: Text
	+ **Required**: No
* **Email**: This field is used to collect your email address for contact purposes.
	+ **Label**: Email
	+ **Type**: Email
	+ **Required**: No
* **Recording Permissions**: This field is used to determine whether you agree to record your sessions/series.
	+ **Label**: Recording Permissions
	+ **Type**: Select One
	+ **Required**: No
* **Emergency Contact Name**: This field is used to collect the name of the emergency contact person.
	+ **Label**: Emergency Contact Name
	+ **Type**: Text
	+ **Required**: No
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: This field is used to collect the phone number of the emergency contact person.
	+ **Label**: Emergency Contact Phone
	+ **Type**: Text
	+ **Required**: No
* **Relationship**: This field is used to describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.
	+ **Label**: Relationship
	+ **Type**: Select Multiple
	+ **Required**: No
* **Emergency Contact Address**: This field is used to collect the address of the emergency contact person.
	+ **Label**: Emergency Contact Address
	+ **Type**: Text
	+ **Required**: No
* **Patient Signature**: This field is used to collect your signature, indicating that you have read and understood the form.
	+ **Label**: Patient Signature
	+ **Type**: Note
	+ **Required**: No
* **Provider Signature**: This field is used to collect the provider's signature, indicating that they have reviewed and agreed to the form.
	+ **Label**: Provider Signature
	+ **Type**: Note
	+ **Required**: No
* **Date**: This field is used to record the date of the form.
	+ **Label**: Date
	+ **Type**: Date
	+ **Required**: No
* **Recorded Date**: This field is used to record the date and time that the form was recorded.
	+ **Label**: Recorded Date
	+ **Type**: Date
	+ **Required**: No
* **Recorded Time**: This field is used to record the time that the form was recorded.
	+ **Label**: Recorded Time
	+ **Type**: Time
	+ **Required**: No
* **Recorded By**: This field is used to record the person that recorded the form.
	+ **Label**: Recorded By
	+ **Type**: Text
	+ **Required**: No
* **Recording Type**: This field is used to determine the type of recording (Session, Series, Series + Session).
	+ **Label**: Recording Type
	+ **Type**: Select Multiple
	+ **Required**: No
* **Recording Method**: This field is used to determine the method of recording (Manual, Automated).
	+ **Label**: Recording Method
	+ **Type**: Select One
	+ **Required**: No
* **Recording Location**: This field is used to record the location of the recording.
	+ **Label**: Recording Location
	+ **Type**: Text
	+ **Required**: No
* **Recording Device**: This field is used to record the device used for recording.
	+ **Label**: Recording Device
	+ **Type**: Text
	+ **Required**: No
* **Recording Notes**: This field is used to record any additional notes about the recording.
	+ **Label**: Recording Notes
	+ **Type**: Text
	+ **Required**: No
* **Release of Information**: This field is used to determine whether you agree to release your information.
	+ **Label**: Release of Information
	+ **Type**: Select Multiple
	+ **Required**: No
* **Release of Instrumental**: This field is used to determine whether you agree to release your instrumental data.
	+ **Label**: Release of Instrumental
	+ **Type**: Select One
	+ **Required**: No
* **Patient Agreement**: This field is used to determine whether you agree to share your patient data.
	+ **Label**: Patient Agreement
	+ **Type**: Select Multiple
	+ **Required**: No
* **Provider Agreement**: This field is used to determine whether your provider agrees to share your data.
	+ **Label**: Provider Agreement
	+ **Type**: Select Multiple
	+ **Required**: No
* **Notes**: This field is used to record any additional notes.
	+ **Label**: Notes
	+ **Type**: Text
	+ **Required**: No

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have read and understood each field before completing the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
* The form should be signed and dated by both you and your healthcare provider.
* This form is used for the Patient Wellness Program and should not be used for any other purpose.
