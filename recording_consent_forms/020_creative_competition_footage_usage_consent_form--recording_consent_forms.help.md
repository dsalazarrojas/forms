# Creative Competition Footage Usage Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Creative Competition Footage Usage Consent Form is used to collect information about your consent and agreement on the usage of your submitted footage in various contests, festivals, and campaigns.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each field carefully and fill in the required information.
2. Answer all fields marked as "required".
3. Choose relevant options for select fields.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Permissions** (`participant_permissions`, text, required): Provide your participant permissions in this field. This is your agreement on the terms and conditions of your submitted footage.
* **Usage Agreement** (`usage_agreement`, text, required): Describe the usage agreement for your submitted footage. This is a critical part of your consent.
* **Submission Rights** (`submission_rights`, text, required): Define the submission rights for your footage. This will help us understand your ownership and control over your work.
* **Contests, Festivals, and Campaigns** (`contests_festivals_campaigns`, select_multiple, false): Select all contests, festivals, or campaigns you are willing to participate in. This field is optional.
* **Organizers** (`organizers`, select_multiple, false): Choose all organizers you would like to work with for your submitted footage. This field is optional.
* **Submitted Video Content** (`submitted_video_content`, select_multiple, false): Select all submitted video content you want to be associated with. This field is optional.
* **Campaign Duration** (`campaign_duration`, date, false): Provide the duration of the campaign your footage will be used in. This field is optional.
* **Campaign Budget** (`campaign_budget`, number, false): Enter the budget for the campaign your footage will be used in. This field is optional.
* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, required): Provide your participant information, including any relevant contact details.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, false): Choose your preferred contact method for communication about your submitted footage. This field is optional.
