# Video Commentary Publication Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Video Commentary Publication Consent Form is used to gather consent from individuals who will be recorded or published in video content. This consent form ensures that all parties are aware of and approve the use of their likeness in the video.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely, ensuring all required fields are filled.
2. Review the form carefully to ensure accuracy and understanding of the consent being given.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address. This email will be used for communication related to the video.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number. This will be used for contacting you about the video.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date on which the video will be recorded or published.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time on which the video will be recorded or published.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, required/optional): Select 'Yes' to indicate that you consent to being recorded and/or published in the video.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select 'Yes' to indicate that you consent to being recorded and/or published in multiple videos.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your consent.
* **Email 2** (`email_2`, text, required/optional): Enter an additional email address for any further communication.
* **Select One 2** (`select_one_2`, select_one, required/optional): Select 'Yes' to indicate that you consent to being recorded and/or published in specific circumstances.
* **Select One 3** (`select_one_3`, select_one, required/optional): Select 'Yes' to indicate that you consent to specific uses of the video content.

## Tips
- Ensure you understand the purpose and implications of the video recording and publication.
- Read the form carefully and ask questions if you are unsure about any field.
- Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.
