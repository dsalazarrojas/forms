# Dance Choreography Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the consent for dance choreography. It is intended to be filled out by the individual giving consent for the recording of their dance class or rehearsal. This form is not for general use and should only be used for the specific recording of dance choreography.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, select the correct options for the following fields:

* **Dance Class/Rehearsal**: Select the correct option for the type of dance class or rehearsal you are recording.
* **Name**: Enter your name as it should be displayed on any recordings made.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Parent Consent**: Enter any additional notes for your parent's consent.
* **Student Consent**: Enter any additional notes for your student's consent.
* **Consent Date**: Select the date you are giving consent for the recording.
* **Consent Time**: Select the time you are giving consent for the recording.
* **Recording Type**: Select the reason for the recording from the options provided.
* **Special Requests**: Enter any special requests you have for the recording.
* **Consent to Share**: Select whether you want your recording to be shared with others.
* **Share with**: Select who you want to share the recording with.
* **Consent to Publish**: Select whether you want your recording to be published.
* **Publish with**: Select who you want to publish the recording with.
* **Note**: Enter any additional notes for the recording.
