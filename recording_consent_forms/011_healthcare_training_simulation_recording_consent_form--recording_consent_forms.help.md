# Healthcare Training Simulation Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from users to record their healthcare training simulations. The form gathers user information and preferences regarding recording methods to ensure that users are aware of how their simulations will be recorded and stored.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your basic contact information, including your name and email address.
2. Enter the course name and location where the simulation will take place.
3. Record the start and end dates of the simulation.
4. If you are willing, please indicate your willingness to be recorded through audio only, video only, screen only, or a combination of these methods. You can select one or more options as per your preference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information**: Enter your name and email address to contact you about the simulation and any recording activities.
* **Name**: Enter the name of the course you are participating in.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you.
* **Phone**: Leave this field blank unless you want to provide an additional contact method.
* **Course Information**: Enter a brief description of the course you are participating in.
* **Location**: Enter the location where the simulation will take place.
* **Simulation Start**: Record the start date and time of the simulation.
* **Simulation End**: Record the end date and time of the simulation.
* **Record Screener**: If you are willing, please indicate your willingness to be recorded through screen.
* **Record Audio**: If you are willing, please indicate your willingness to be recorded through audio.
* **Record Video**: If you are willing, please indicate your willingness to be recorded through video.
* **Record Screen and Video**: If you are willing, please indicate your willingness to be recorded through both screen and video.
* **Record Screen and Audio**: If you are willing, please indicate your willingness to be recorded through both screen and audio.
* **Record All**: If you are willing, please indicate your willingness to be recorded through all three methods (screen, audio, and video).

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are aware of the recording methods and their implications before completing this form.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please contact us at [insert contact email or phone number].
