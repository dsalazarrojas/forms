# Healthcare Design Thinking Lab Filming Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain consent from participants in the Healthcare Design Thinking Lab for filming purposes, ensuring they understand the implications and purpose of filming.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, including the purpose, use, participant's name, and email.
2. Select the relevant options for filming consent, including whether the participant consents to filming, the type of filming, and potential uses (education, research, or promotional).
3. Enter any additional notes or comments as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **filming_consent_form** (Required): A text field where you can enter the name of the filming form or project.
* **Purpose** (Optional): A text field where you can enter a brief description of the filming purpose.
* **use** (Required): A select-one field with two options, "Yes" or "No", indicating whether the participant consents to filming.
* **Name** (Optional): A text field where the participant's name can be entered.
* **relationship** (Optional): A text field where any relevant relationship to the participant can be entered.
* **email** (Required): A text field where the participant's email can be entered, as per email format.
* **contact** (Optional): A text field where any additional contact information can be entered.
* **filming_consent_participants** (Required): A select-multiple field where you can choose whether the participant consents to filming, with options "Yes" or "No".
* **filming_consent_informed** (Required): A select-one field where you can choose whether the participant has been informed, with options "Yes" or "No".
* **filming_consent_participants_education** (Optional): A select-multiple field where you can choose the participant's education-related filming consents, with options "Yes" or "No".
* **filming_consent_participants_research** (Optional): A select-multiple field where you can choose the participant's research-related filming consents, with options "Yes" or "No".
* **filming_consent_participants_promotional** (Optional): A select-multiple field where you can choose the participant's promotional filming consents, with options "Yes" or "No".
* **filming_consent_participants_other** (Optional): A select-multiple field where you can choose other filming consents, with options "Yes" or "No".
* **filming_consent_participants_other_other** (Optional): A text field where any other filming consents can be described.
* **filming_consent_other** (Optional): A text field where any other filming consents can be described.
* **filming_consent_participants_other_notes** (Optional): A text field where any additional filming consents can be noted.
* **filming_consent_other_notes** (Optional): A text field where any additional filming consents can be noted.
* **filming_consent_participants_education_uses** (Optional): A text field where any education filming uses can be described.
* **filming_consent_participants_research_uses** (Optional): A text field where any research filming uses can be described.
* **filming_consent_participants_promotional_uses** (Optional): A text field where any promotional filming uses can be described.
* **filming_consent_participants_other_uses** (Optional): A text field where any other filming uses can be described.
* **filming_consent_other_uses** (Optional): A text field where any other filming uses can be described.
* **filming_consent_participants_notes** (Optional): A text field where any filming consents can be noted.
* **filming_consent_other_notes** (Optional): A text field where any other filming consents can be noted.
* **filming_consent_agreement** (Required): A select-one field where you can choose whether the participant consents to the filming agreement, with options "Yes" or "No".
