# veterinary_continuing_education_recording_consent - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record participants' consent for continuing education sessions, ensuring they are aware and agreeable to being recorded.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Read the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and implications of the consent.
2.  Input your name in the 'Participant Name' field, which is the field labeled as 'Participant Name'.
3.  Provide your valid email address in the 'Email' field for communication purposes.
4.  If you have a phone number, enter it in the 'Phone' field for contact purposes.
5.  Specify the date you gave your consent in the 'Consent Date' field.
6.  Add your digital signature in the 'Electronic Signature' field.
7.  If you are affiliated with an organization, specify it in the 'Organization' field.
8.  If you are an educator, speaker, or attendee, select your role in the 'Role' field.
9.  If applicable, specify your department in the 'Department' field.
10. Specify the location of the session in the 'Location' field.
11. Provide any other contact information in the 'Other Contact Info' field if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Recording Consent**: This is the main field to capture participants' consent for recording.
    (`Recording Consent`, note, optional)
*   **Participant Name**: This is where you should input your name. (`Participant Name`, text, required)
*   **Email**: Use this field for your contact email. (`Email`, email, optional)
*   **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number here. (`Phone`, text, optional)
*   **Consent Date**: Date you gave your consent. (`Consent Date`, date, optional)
*   **Electronic Signature**: This field is for your digital signature. (`Electronic Signature`, text, optional)
*   **Organization**: If you are affiliated with an organization, specify it here. (`Organization`, text, optional)
*   **Role**: Choose your role in the session as an 'Educator', 'Speaker', or 'Attendee'. (`Role`, select_multiple, optional)
*   **Department**: If you are affiliated with a department within an organization, specify it here. (`Department`, text, optional)
*   **Location**: Specify the location of the session. (`Location`, text, optional)
*   **Contact Number**: Your contact phone number. (`Contact Number`, text, optional)
*   **Other Contact Info**: Any other contact information you'd like to provide. (`Other Contact Info`, text, optional)
