# Compassion Fatigue Research Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains the purpose and requirements for completing the Compassion Fatigue Research Recording Consent Form. This form is designed to gather your consent for participating in a research study on compassion fatigue. Please read carefully and provide accurate information to ensure a smooth and successful research experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your personal and contact information.
2. Select the method of recording you are willing to participate in (e.g., audio, video, written transcription, photography).
3. Choose how you would like your data to be used (e.g., data analysis only, presentation at academic conferences, publication in peer-reviewed journals, educational purposes).
4. Decide on your anonymity preference (e.g., use your real name, pseudonym, fully anonymized).
5. Confirm your understanding of your right to pause or stop the recording at any time.
6. Select the duration of data storage for the research recordings (e.g., 1 year, 3 years, 5 years, indefinitely).
7. Provide your contact email for follow-up information.
8. Finally, date and sign this form digitally.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Title of the Research Study** (study_title): Enter the title of the research study you are participating in.
- **Principal Investigator** (principal_investigator): Enter the name of the lead researcher for this project.
- **Participant Full Name** (participant_name): Enter your full legal name.
- **Method of Recording** (recording_method_consent): Choose which formats of recording you are willing to participate in (e.g., audio, video, written transcription, photography).
- **Intended Use of Recordings** (intended_use_of_recordings): Select how you would like your data to be used (e.g., data analysis only, presentation at academic conferences, publication in peer-reviewed journals, educational purposes).
- **Anonymity and Confidentiality** (anonymity_preference): Choose how you would like your identity to be managed in the recordings (e.g., use your real name, use a pseudonym, fully anonymized).
- **Right to Pause or Stop Recording** (right_to_stop_recording): Confirm your understanding of your right to pause or stop the recording at any time without penalty.
- **Data Retention Period** (data_storage_duration): Select the duration of data storage for the research recordings (e.g., 1 year, 3 years, 5 years, indefinitely).
- **Contact Email for Participant** (contact_for_questions): Provide your contact email for follow-up information.
- **Date of Consent** (consent_signature_date): Date and sign this form digitally, confirming your consent to participate in the research.
- **Digital Signature Acknowledgment** (digital_signature_acknowledgment): Confirm that by submitting this form, you are providing your legal consent to be recorded for this research.
