# Music Workshop Participant Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture the necessary information from workshop participants to ensure we have their recording consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the title of the workshop.
2.  Enter the date of the workshop.
3.  Provide your name as a participant.
4.  Enter your email address (if you'd like to be contacted by us).
5.  Enter your phone number (if you have one).
6.  Select your recording consent preference from the options provided.
7.  Sign your name to acknowledge your consent.
8.  Date your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Workshop Title** (Workshop Title, text, required/optional): Enter the title of the workshop you're participating in.
*   **Workshop Date** (Workshop Date, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the workshop.
*   **Participant Name** (Participant Name, text, required/optional): Enter your name as a participant.
*   **Email** (Email, email, required/optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted by us.
*   **Phone** (Phone, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number if you have one.
*   **Recording Consent** (Recording Consent, select\_one, required): Select whether or not you're okay with being recorded during the workshop.
*   **Participant Signature** (Participant Signature, note, required/optional): Sign your name to acknowledge your consent.
*   **Date** (Date, time, required/optional): Date your signature.
*   **Workshop Title** (Workshop Title, text, required/optional): This field seems to be a duplicate of the first "Workshop Title" field. Please ignore this field.
 
 Note that the "Workshop Title" field is not necessary as it is a duplicate of the first "Workshop Title" field.
