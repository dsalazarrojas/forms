# Production Design Showcase Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Production Design Showcase Consent Form is a tool used to collect information and consent from participants in a production design showcase event. This form is designed to gather essential details about the participant and their project, as well as their consent to showcase their work. It is a crucial document that helps ensure a smooth and respectful experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "Participant Details" page, which includes fields for the participant's name, email, and other relevant contact information.
2. Proceed to the "Project Info" page, where you will provide details about your project, such as its title, description, and relevant images or videos.
3. The "Consent Agreements" page is where you will review and agree to the terms of the showcase, including the use of your project's intellectual property.
4. Finally, sign and date the "Signatures" page to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Details
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your business card or identification documents.
* **Email**: Provide a valid email address where you can be reached for follow-up information or questions.
* **Other Contact Info**: Add any additional contact information you'd like to provide, such as a phone number or website.

### Project Info
* **Project Title**: Briefly describe your project, including its title and any relevant information about the project.
* **Project Description**: Elaborate on your project, including its objective, scope, and any notable features or achievements.
* **Images/Videos**: Upload any relevant images or videos of your project to help showcase its design.

### Consent Agreements
* **Consent Agreement**: Review and agree to the terms of the showcase, including the use of your project's intellectual property.

### Signatures
* **Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm your consent to the terms outlined above.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly to avoid any issues with your project's representation.
* Review your project's intellectual property rights before agreeing to the consent terms.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us at <form@gic.mx>.
