# Academic Study Video Diary Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Study Video Diary Consent Form is a critical component of the research study, ensuring that participants provide informed consent for the recording and use of their video data. This form helps to establish a clear understanding of the study's objectives, data collection methods, and how their contribution will be used and protected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address accurately, as they will be used to communicate with you throughout the study.
2. Assign a Study ID Number provided by the research team for identification purposes.
3. Select the date of your consent, which is today's date.
4. Agree to record and submit video diaries as part of the study by selecting "I Agree".
5. Authorize the use of your video data for specific purposes, such as Research Analysis, Academic Presentations, Journal Publications, or Educational Training.
6. Confirm your understanding that you can withdraw from the study at any time by selecting "Yes - I Understand".
7. Verify that you understand your data will be stored securely and your name will be anonymized by selecting "True".
8. Indicate your preference for facial blurring in public presentations of the data by selecting "True" or "False".
9. Acknowledge that you have read the participation information sheet and agree to the terms by selecting "I Agree".
10. Sign the form by typing your full name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name** (`participant_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Use your active email address for study communication.
* **Assigned Study ID Number** (`study_id_number`, `text`, required): Enter the Study ID Number provided by the research team for identification.
* **Date of Consent** (`consent_date`, `date`, required): Select today's date to indicate when you provided your consent.
* **I Agree to Record and Submit Video Diaries** (`video_diary_consent`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your agreement to participate by selecting "I Agree".
* **Data Use Authorization** (`data_use_authorization`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the approved purposes for the use of your video data.
* **Right to Withdraw** (`withdraw_understanding`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your understanding of your right to withdraw from the study at any time.
* **Data Security and Anonymization** (`confidentiality_assurance`, `select_one`, required): Verify your understanding of data protection and anonymization.
* **Facial Blurring Preference** (`facial_blur_request`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your preference for facial blurring in public presentations of the data.
* **Acknowledgement** (`legal_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your understanding and agreement to the participation terms.
* **Electronic Signature** (`participant_signature`, `text`, required): Sign the form by typing your full name.

## Tips

* Ensure you review the participation information sheet thoroughly before completing this form.
* Ask questions or clarify any doubts about the form or study objectives with the research team.
* Be aware that your data will be treated with the highest level of security and confidentiality.
* Familiarize yourself with the approved purposes for data use and indicate your preferences accordingly.
