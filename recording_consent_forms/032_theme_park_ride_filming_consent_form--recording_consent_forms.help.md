Theme Park Ride Filming Consent Form is used for documenting and organizing filming activities in a theme park. It captures details of the theme park ride where filming took place, including the category of the footage, description, theme park name, filming team involved, guest name, and other essential filming details. This form is designed to be filled out by filming crew members or park staff to ensure that all filming activities are properly recorded and documented, which can help improve the efficiency and accuracy of the park's operations.

Before proceeding, kindly check if the form is a duplicate. If you've already submitted a filming consent form, your details and the current form will be merged.

This form guides you through the required steps to complete the filming details for a theme park ride.
## Purpose
To document and organize filming activities in the theme park rides, this form captures essential details of the filming sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To ensure that all filming activities are properly documented, please go through the following steps:

1. Fill in the category of the footage being captured, e.g., 'Ride', 'Interview', 'Event'.
2. Add a brief description of the filming activity, including what was captured and where.
3. Specify the name of the theme park where the filming took place.
4. Mention the name of the team involved in the filming, including crew members and their roles.
5. Enter the name of the guest(s) involved in the filming, if any.
6. Fill in their contact information, including a valid email address and phone number.
7. Specify the date and time of the filming session, including the start and end time.
8. Identify the location where the camera was situated during filming.
9. Choose the type of camera used for the filming.
10. Enter the height of the camera from the ground level.
11. Specify the duration of the filming session.
12. Indicate the location where the filming took place within the theme park.
13. Choose the time of day when the filming occurred.
14. Describe the weather conditions during the filming.
15. Add any additional notes regarding the filming activity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category**: This field asks for the type or category of footage being captured, e.g., 'Ride', 'Interview', 'Event'. Please select the most relevant option.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of the filming activity, including what was captured and where it took place.
* **Theme Park**: Enter the name of the theme park where the filming happened.
* **Filming Team**: Specify the name of the team involved in the filming, including crew members and their roles.
* **Guest Name**: If there were any guests involved in the filming, fill in their name here.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address for the contact person.
* **Phone**: Enter a valid phone number for the contact person.
* **Filming Date**: Fill in the date of the filming session, including start and end times.
* **Time**: Specify the time of the filming session, including start and end times.
* **Camera Location**: Identify the location where the camera was situated during filming.
* **Camera Type**: Choose the type of camera used for the filming.
* **Camera Height**: Enter the height of the camera from the ground level.
* **Filming Length**: Specify the duration of the filming session.
* **Filming Location**: Indicate the location within the theme park where the filming took place.
* **Filming Time of Day**: Choose the time of day when the filming occurred.
* **Filming Weather**: Describe the weather conditions during filming.
* **Note**: Add any additional notes or details regarding the filming activity.
* **Consent**: This is a note field for any consent or permissions required for the filming activity.

Note: This form is a critical document and should be completed accurately for efficient and secure processing.
