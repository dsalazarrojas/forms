# esports_replay_publication_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The esports replay publication consent form is a critical component of our recording process. It is used to collect informed consent from participants to publish their esports replays, ensuring that their identities, replays, and other sensitive information are handled properly and in compliance with relevant laws and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name in the "Name" field. This will be used to identify you in the publication process.
2.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This will be used to communicate with you regarding the publication of your replay.
3.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field. This will be used to contact you in case we need to confirm any details.
4.  Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Publication Consent" field to indicate your willingness to publish your replay. If you select "Yes", your replay will be published and made available to the public.
5.  If applicable, enter your date of birth in the "Date" field. This is not required, but will be helpful in case we need to confirm your age.
6.  If applicable, enter the date and time you wish to sign the form. This is not required, but will be helpful for our records.
7.  Finally, enter your signature in the "Signature" field. This will confirm that you have read and understood the terms of this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details 1 (Name)**: Enter your name to identify yourself in the publication process.
* **Participant Details 2 (Email)**: Enter your email address to receive communications regarding the publication of your replay.
* **Participant Details 3 (Phone)**: Enter your phone number to be contacted in case we need to confirm any details.
* **Publication Consent**: Select "Yes" to publish your replay, or "No" to keep it private.
* **Participant Details 4 (Date)**: Enter your date of birth, if applicable, to help us confirm your age.
* **Participant Details 5 (Signature)**: Enter your signature to confirm that you have read and understood the terms of this form.

## Tips
Please ensure that you read and understand the terms of this form before signing it. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
