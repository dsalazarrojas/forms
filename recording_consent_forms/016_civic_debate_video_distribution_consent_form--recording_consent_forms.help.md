# Civic Debate Video Distribution Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect the consent of participants in a debate event for video and audio recording purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **Participant Full Name** in the "Participant Full Name" field.
2. Enter your **Participant Email** in the "Participant Email" field.
3. Provide the **Title of the Debate Event** in the "Title of the Debate Event" field.
4. Select the **Date of the Debate** from the calendar in the "Date of the Debate" field.
5. Choose your answer for **I consent to being video and audio recorded during the debate** in the "I consent to being video and audio recorded during the debate" dropdown menu.
6. Select whether you **consent to the distribution of these recordings** in the "I consent to the distribution of these recordings" dropdown menu.
7. Choose all the **Approved Distribution Channels** that you agree to in the "Approved Distribution Channels" dropdown menu.
8. If you are under 18 years old, select **Are you under 18 years of age?** as "True".
9. If you are under 18 years old, your parent or guardian must sign below in the "If under 18 - A parent or legal guardian must sign below" field.
10. Provide your **Digital Signature (Full Name)** by typing it in the "Digital Signature (Full Name)" field.
11. Select the **Date of Signature** from the calendar in the "Date of Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name** (`participant_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Title of the Debate Event** (`debate_event_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the debate event you are participating in.
* **Date of the Debate** (`debate_date`, `date`, required): Select the date of the debate event from the calendar.
* **I consent to being video and audio recorded during the debate** (`consent_recording`, `select_one`, required): Choose "I Consent" to allow recording or "I Do Not Consent" to decline.
* **I consent to the distribution of these recordings** (`consent_distribution`, `select_one`, required): Choose "I Consent" to allow distribution or "I Do Not Consent" to decline.
* **Approved Distribution Channels** (`distribution_channels`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the distribution channels you are comfortable with, such as Educational Websites, Social Media (YouTube, Facebook, etc.), Local Television, or Internal Archive.
* **Are you under 18 years of age?** (`age_verification`, `select_one`, required): Select "True" if you are under 18 years old.
* **If under 18 - A parent or legal guardian must sign below** (`guardian_consent_note`, `note`, optional): If you are under 18, your parent or guardian must sign below.
* **Digital Signature (Full Name)** (`digital_signature`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as a digital signature.
* **Date of Signature** (`signature_date`, `date`, required): Select the date of your digital signature from the calendar.

## Tips
* Please answer all the questions truthfully and accurately.
* Make sure to review all the fields carefully before submitting the form.
* If you are under 18 years old, make sure your parent or guardian signs the form before you submit it.
