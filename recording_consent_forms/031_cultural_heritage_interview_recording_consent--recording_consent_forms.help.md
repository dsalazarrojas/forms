# Cultural Heritage Interview Recording Consent - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture the recording consent of participants in cultural heritage interviews. It ensures that all parties involved are aware of and agree to the terms of the recording.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and the name of your organisation as the researcher.
2. Select the date of the interview.
3. Specify the method of recording (audio, video, or both).
4. Confirm that you, as the participant, have given your consent for the recording.
5. Provide the date of your consent.
6. Sign your name as the participant, indicating that you have given your consent.
7. Sign your name as the researcher, indicating that you are collecting and storing the recording.
8. (Optional) Provide the location of the recording.
9. (Optional) Select the license type for the recording file.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Researcher Name**: Please fill in your name as the researcher.
* **Researcher Organisation**: This is your research organisation's name. It's a required field and should be filled in accurately.
* **Interview Date**: The date of the interview. Please select the date when the interview took place.
* **Recording Location**: If you want to specify the location of the recording, please provide it here. This field is optional.
* **Recording Method**: Select the method of recording (audio, video, or both).
* **Participant Consent**: Confirm that you, as the participant, give your consent for the recording. Select 'True' to indicate your consent.
* **Consent Date**: The date when you gave your consent.
* **Participant Signature**: Sign your name here, indicating that you give your consent for the recording.
* **Researcher Signature**: Sign your name here, indicating that you are collecting and storing the recording.
* **Recording File**: If you have a recording file, provide the URL or path where it can be found.
* **Recording File License**: Select the license type for the recording file (Public or Private).
* **Additional Notes**: If you need to provide any additional information, please use this field.
* **Contact Information**: If you need to provide any further contact information, please do so here. This field is optional.
