# Performance Archive Preservation Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information and consent for the preservation and management of audio recordings. It is intended for use by individuals who wish to store and manage their own recordings or those of others. Please review the form carefully and ensure that you understand each field before submitting it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand each field and its purpose.
2. Select "True" or "False" for "Performance Archive Consent" to indicate your agreement or refusal to store and manage the recording.
3. Choose one or multiple locations to store the recording from the provided options.
4. Select one or multiple methods to access the recording from the provided options.
5. Add any additional notes or comments you may have.
6. Enter your contact information to ensure we can reach you with any further questions or concerns.
7. Enter the name of the performance or event you are submitting.
8. Enter the date and time of the performance or event.
9. Enter the location where the performance or event took place.
10. Add any additional notes or comments you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Performance Archive Presentation** (`performance_archive_presentation`): This field is not required, but if you would like to provide a brief description of the performance or event, please enter it here.
* **Performance Archive Consent** (`performance_archive_consent`): Please select "True" if you consent to store and manage the recording, or "False" if you do not.
* **Where will you store the recording?** (`performance_archive_stored`): Choose one or multiple locations to store the recording. This could be a cloud storage service, a local device, or other.
* **How will you access the recording?** (`performance_archive_access`): Select one or multiple methods to access the recording, such as publicly or privately.
* **Additional Notes** (`performance_archive_notes`): Enter any additional comments or notes you may have.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Enter your name as the primary contact person for this recording.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address so that we can reach you with any further questions or concerns.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Other Contact Info** (`contact_other_contact`): Enter any other contact information you may have.
* **Other Contact Info URL** (`contact_other_contact_url`): Enter the URL of your other contact information.
* **Performance Name** (`performance_name`): Enter the name of the performance or event.
* **Performance Date** (`performance_date`): Enter the date of the performance or event.
* **Performance Time** (`performance_time`): Enter the time of the performance or event.
* **Location** (`location`): Enter the location where the performance or event took place.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional comments or notes you may have.
* **Submit** (`submit`): Click this button to submit the form.
