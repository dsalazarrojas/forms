# creative_direction_interview_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain consent from participants for recording an interview, specifying the purpose and use of the recording.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the title of the form as desired.
2. Fill in your participant's name and email address accurately.
3. If applicable, provide a title and description of the project.
4. Indicate your agreement to participate in the recording by selecting one of the options provided.
5. Sign your name as an acknowledgment of your agreement.
6. Specify the date of the project's title.
7. Choose how you agree to have the recording used (e.g., internal or external project development, presentation).
8. Select an option for the intended use of the recording.
9. (Optional) Add any additional notes for the participant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Title** (1, text, required: false): Enter a title for the form as desired.
  This field is for the title of the form, which is not required but can help identify the form's purpose.
- **Participant Name** (2, text, required: true): Enter your full name as the participant.
  This is a required field to identify you as the person participating in the recording.
- **Participant Email** (3, email, required: true): Enter your email address associated with your name.
  This field is required to ensure you can be contacted and reached regarding the recording.
- **Project Title** (4, text, required: false): If applicable, enter the title of the project you are participating in.
  This field is optional and helps in identifying the project's name.
- **Project Description** (5, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the project you are participating in.
  This field is optional and gives more context about the project.
- **Participant Agreement** (6, select multiple, required: true): Select your agreement to participate in the recording.
  You must choose one of the provided options to indicate your agreement to participate.
- **Participant Signature** (7, note, required: true): Sign your name as an acknowledgment of your agreement.
  This field is required to validate your agreement to participate.
- **Project Title Date** (8, date, required: true): Enter the date of the project's title.
  This field is required to ensure the recording is accurately timestamped.
- **Recording Agreement** (9, select multiple, required: true): Select how you agree to have the recording used.
  You must choose one of the provided options for the use of the recording.
- **Recording Use** (10, select one, required: true): Select the intended use of the recording.
  Choose one of the provided options for the intended use of the recording.
- **Participant Notes** (11, note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments for the participant.
  This field is optional and can be used to provide any further comments or information.
