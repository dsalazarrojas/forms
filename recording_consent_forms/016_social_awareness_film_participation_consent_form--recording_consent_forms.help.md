# social_awareness_film_participation_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The social awareness film participation consent form is a tool used to collect information from participants in film projects for educational and advocacy purposes. It's meant to gather demographic data from individuals to provide insight into the social awareness campaigns or films.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user information (page 1).
2. Enter your participant's name and contact information (pages 2-3).
3. Choose the public use of the film (page 4).
4. If other use is selected, describe the purpose (page 5).
5. Provide your consent date and time (pages 6-7).
6. Sign the consent statement (page 8).
7. Enter your participant demographic information (pages 9-14).
8. Complete the address and postal code (pages 15-16).
9. Sign the participant signature (page 17).
