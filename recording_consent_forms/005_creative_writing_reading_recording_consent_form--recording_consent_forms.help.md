# Creative Writing Reading Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Creative Writing Reading Recording Consent Form is a text-based form used to record a user's consent for creative writing or other activities. This form helps to clarify the user's understanding of the recording process and provides a clear, concise way to obtain their consent for the recording to be shared or used in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these simple steps:

1. Start by entering your school name in the `school_name` field.
2. Provide your email address in the `email` field.
3. Add your phone number in the `phone` field.
4. Record the date and time of the recording in the `date` and `time` fields.
5. Finally, confirm your consent in the `recording_consent` field by entering a simple "yes" or "I agree" in the text area.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Submission** (`1`, `text`, optional): This field is not actually a field for the user to fill out, but rather a placeholder or label for the form submission. It appears to be a leftover from the form design and can be ignored.
* **School Name** (`2`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the school you are affiliated with. This can be a public or private school, and is used for identification purposes only.
* **Usage Permissions** (`3`, `note`, optional): This field is a note field where you can optionally specify any usage permissions or restrictions for the recording. This can include any relevant details about what the recording can be used for and under what circumstances.
* **Recording Consent** (`4`, `text`, optional): Confirm your consent for the recording by entering a simple "yes" or "I agree" in this field. This is the primary purpose of the form: to obtain your consent for the recording to be shared or used in the future.
* **Email** (`5`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address, which can be used for any follow-up communication related to the recording.
* **Phone** (`6`, `text`, optional): Add your phone number, which can be used for any additional contact or follow-up related to the recording.
* **Date** (`7`, `date`, optional): Record the date and time of the recording in this field. This should be the actual date and time when the recording takes place.
* **Time** (`8`, `time`, optional): Record the time of the recording in this field. This should match the time recorded in the `date` field.
