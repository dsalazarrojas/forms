# Educational Outreach Film Recording Consent - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used for recording consent from participants and schools to record a film for educational outreach purposes. It ensures that the participants and schools understand their roles, responsibilities, and expectations during the filming process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read the form carefully and fill out all required fields.
2. If you are a participant, provide your contact information and confirm your consent to be recorded.
3. If you are a school representative, provide your school's contact information and confirm that the participant has given their consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (Page 1, Participant Information):
    * **participant_information** (`participant_information`, Text, optional): Enter your name, email, phone number, and address for the participant.
* **Film Location** (Page 1, Film Location):
    * **film_location** (`film_location`, Text, optional): Specify the location where the film will be recorded.
* **Participant Permission** (Page 1, Participant Permission):
    * **participant_permission** (`participant_permission`, Select Multiple, optional): Indicate whether you have given permission to be recorded (Yes or No).
* **School Nonprofit Program** (Page 1, School Nonprofit Program):
    * **school_nonprofit_program** (`school_nonprofit_program`, Select One, optional): Confirm whether your school has a nonprofit program (Yes or No).
* **Participant Age** (Page 1, Participant Age):
    * **participant_age** (`participant_age`, Number, optional): Enter your age.
* **Participant Age Group** (Page 1, Participant Age Group):
    * **participant_age_group** (`participant_age_group`, Select Multiple, optional): Indicate your age group (Yes or No).
* **School Nonprofit Programs** (Page 1, School Nonprofit Programs):
    * **school_nonprofit_programs** (`school_nonprofit_programs`, Select Multiple, optional): Confirm whether your school offers nonprofit programs (Yes or No).
* **Filming Details** (Page 2, Filming Details):
    * **filming_details** (`filming_details`, Text, optional): Provide a brief description of the filming process and equipment to be used.
* **Film Length** (Page 2, Film Length):
    * **film_length** (`film_length`, Number, optional): Specify the expected duration of the film.
* **Filming Date** (Page 2, Filming Date):
    * **filming_date** (`filming_date`, Date, optional): Choose a date for filming.
* **Filming Time** (Page 2, Filming Time):
    * **filming_time** (`filming_time`, Time, optional): Choose a time for filming.
* **Consent** (Page 2, Consent):
    * **consent** (`consent`, Select One, optional): Confirm that you have given your consent to be recorded (Yes or No).
* **Signature Date** (Page 3, Signature Date):
    * **signature_date** (`signature_date`, Date, optional): Enter the date of your signature.
* **Signature Time** (Page 3, Signature Time):
    * **signature_time** (`signature_time`, Time, optional): Enter the time of your signature.
* **Signature** (Page 3, Signature):
    * **signature** (`signature`, Note, optional): Sign your name to confirm your consent.
* **School Contact Information** (Page 4, School Contact Information):
    * **school_contact_info** (`school_contact_info`, Text, optional): Enter your school's contact information.
* **School Email** (Page 4, School Email):
    * **school_email** (`school_email`, Email, optional): Enter your school's email address.
* **School Phone** (Page 4, School Phone):
    * **school_phone** (`school_phone`, Text, optional): Enter your school's phone number.
* **Participant Phone** (Page 4, Participant Phone):
    * **participant_phone** (`participant_phone`, Text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Participant Email** (Page 4, Participant Email):
    * **participant_email** (`participant_email`, Email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Address** (Page 4, Participant Address):
    * **participant_address** (`participant_address`, Text, optional): Enter your address.
* **Film Title** (Page 5, Film Title):
    * **film_title** (`film_title`, Text, optional): Enter a title for the film.
* **Film Description** (Page 5, Film Description):
    * **film_description** (`film_description`, Text, optional): Provide a brief description of the film.
* **Film Purpose** (Page 5, Film Purpose):
    * **film_purpose** (`film_purpose`, Text, optional): Specify the purpose of the film.
* **Additional Comments** (Page 5, Additional Comments):
    * **additional_comments** (`additional_comments`, Text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes.

## Tips

* Please read each field carefully and ensure you understand its purpose before filling out the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your school representative or the filmmaking team.
* Remember to review and sign your consent form to confirm your agreement to be recorded.
