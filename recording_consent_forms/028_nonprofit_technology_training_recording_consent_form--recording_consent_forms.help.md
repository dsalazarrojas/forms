# Nonprofit Technology Training Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nonprofit Technology Training Recording Consent Form is designed to gather consent from nonprofit training participants for recording their sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the applicable fields as follows:
- Answer yes or no to the question "Do you have consent to record this session?"
- Select the participants who have given consent
- Specify the type of recording device used
- Choose whether the recording is of audio or video type
- Enter a message to communicate your consent to the participant
- Enter your email address
- Enter your phone number

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **RecordingConsentForm** (Text): This is the main section header for the form.
* **NonprofitTechnoloyTrainingRecordingConsentForm** (Text): A title for the form, describing the purpose.
* **training_participants** (Select One): Choose which participant(s) have given consent to the recording.
* **hasVideoRecordingConsent** (Select Multiple): Answer yes or no to whether you have consent to record video.
* **hasAudioRecordingConsent** (Select One): Answer yes or no to whether you have consent to record audio.
* **recorded_at** (Date): Enter the date of the recording.
* **recording_device** (Select One): Specify the type of device used for recording.
* **recording_file** (Select Multiple): Choose the type of recording file (audio or video).
* **recording_consent_message** (Note): Enter a message to communicate your consent to the participant.
* **email** (Email): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (Text): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
Please make sure to answer each question honestly and accurately, as this form is a legal document.
