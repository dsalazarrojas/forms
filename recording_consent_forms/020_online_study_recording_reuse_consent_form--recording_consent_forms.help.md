# online_study_recording_reuse_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The online study recording reuse consent form is used to obtain consent for the reuse of online study recordings. It ensures that all parties involved in the study agree to the terms of reusing the study recordings.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required information, including:
	* Study Title
	* Consent Date
	* Consent Time
	* Submitter Reuse Agreement (Yes/No)
	* Submitter Reuse Agreement Date
	* Researcher Reuse Permission (Yes/No)
	* Research Team Reuse Permission (Yes/No)
	* Study Coordinator Reuse Permission (Yes/No)
	* Data Manager Reuse Permission (Yes/No)
	* Submitter Reuse Agreement Other Description (optional)
2. Choose the relevant options for:
	* Study Reuse Status (Active/Inactive)
	* Participant Reuse Status (Active/Inactive)
	* Submitter Reuse Permission (Yes/No)
	* Researcher Reuse Permission (Yes/No)
	* Research Team Reuse Permission (Yes/No)
	* Study Coordinator Reuse Permission (Yes/No)
	* Data Manager Reuse Permission (Yes/No)
3. Fill in any additional comments as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Study Title** (`study_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the study being recorded.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, required): Enter the date on which consent was given.
* **Consent Time** (`consent_time`, time, required): Enter the time at which consent was given.
* **Reuse Consent** (`reuse_consent`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you consent to the reuse of the recording.
* **Study Reuse Status** (`study_reuse_status`, select_multiple, required): Choose the status of the study recording (Active or Inactive).
* **Participant Reuse Status** (`participant_reuse_status`, select_multiple, required): Choose the status of the participant in the study (Active or Inactive).
* **Study Reuse Comments** (`study_reuse_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about the study recording.
* **Participant Reuse Comments** (`participant_reuse_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about the participant in the study.
* **Submitter Information** (`submitter_information`, text, optional): Enter any information about the submitter of the form.
* **Submitter Reuse Permission** (`submitter_reuse_permission`, select_multiple, required): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the submitter consents to the reuse of the recording.
* **Submitter Reuse Agreement** (`submitter_reuse_agreement`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the submitter agrees to the conditions of reusing the recording.
* **Submitter Reuse Agreement Other** (`submitter_reuse_agreement_other`, text, optional): Enter any additional information if choosing "No" for Submitter Reuse Agreement.
* **Submitter Reuse Agreement Other Description** (`submitter_reuse_agreement_other_description`, text, optional): Enter a description of any other conditions agreed upon.
* **Researcher Reuse Permission** (`researcher_reuse_permission`, select_multiple, required): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the researcher consents to the reuse of the recording.
* **Research Team Reuse Permission** (`research_team_reuse_permission`, select_multiple, required): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the research team consents to the reuse of the recording.
* **Study Coordinator Reuse Permission** (`study_coordinator_reuse_permission`, select_multiple, required): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the study coordinator consents to the reuse of the recording.
* **Data Manager Reuse Permission** (`data_manager_reuse_permission`, select_multiple, required): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the data manager consents to the reuse of the recording.
* **Submitter Reuse Agreement Date** (`submitter_reuse_agreement_date`, date, required): Enter the date on which the submitter's agreement was given.
* **Submitter Reuse Agreement Time** (`submitter_reuse_agreement_time`, time, required): Enter the time at which the submitter's agreement was given.
