# Sports Conference Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain consent from speakers or attendees for their sessions to be recorded during a sports conference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the required fields.
2. Indicate your role as a speaker or attendee.
3. Choose whether you grant permission for your session to be recorded.
4. Select the duration of the recording in hours and minutes.
5. Choose whether you grant permission for your session to be recorded.
6. Specify the location of the recording (if any).
7. Provide any additional notes or comments about the recording.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required):
  Enter your name as it appears on your name tag or business card.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required):
  Enter your email address so we can contact you about the recording.
* **Role** (`role`, `select_one`, optional):
  Select 'Yes' if you are speaking at the conference, 'No' otherwise.
* **Permission** (`permission`, `select_multiple`, optional):
  Select 'Yes' if you want to grant recording permission for your session, 'No' for other attendees. You can select multiple options if applicable.
* **Recording Date** (`recording_date`, `date`, required):
  Enter the date of the recording in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Recording Time** (`recording_time`, `time`, required):
  Enter the start time of the recording in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Recording Duration** (`recording_duration`, `number`, required):
  Enter the duration of the recording in hours and minutes (e.g., 1:30 for 1 hour 30 minutes).
* **Recording Type** (`recording_type`, `select_one`, optional):
  Select 'Yes' if your session will be recorded, 'No' otherwise.
* **Recording Location** (`recording_location`, `select_multiple`, optional):
  Select 'Yes' if your session will be recorded at a specific location, 'No' otherwise.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional):
  Enter any additional notes or comments about the recording.
