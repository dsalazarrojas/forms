# Culinary Class Filming Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Culinary Class Filming Release Form is designed to capture the necessary information and consent from participants involved in a culinary class filming. This form ensures that participants are aware of and agree to the terms of filming, including their photo, video, and audio releases. It also collects essential details about the event, such as date, location, and time, to facilitate smooth event planning and management.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the participant's name in the "Participant Name" field.
2.  Provide the participant's email address in the "Email" field.
3.  Enter the participant's contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
4.  Confirm or deny the participant's consent for filming by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Filming Consent" field.
5.  Indicate the participant's consent for photo release by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Participant Photo Release" field.
6.  Similarly, select "True" or "False" for video and audio releases in the "Participant Video Release" and "Participant Audio Release" fields, respectively.
7.  Specify the participant's school or chef affiliation in the "School or Chef" field.
8.  Enter the participant's school or chef ID in the "School or Chef ID" field.
9.  Fill in the filming date in the "Filming Date" field.
10. Enter the filming location in the "Filming Location" field.
11. Specify the filming time in the "Filming Time" field.
12. Provide additional event details in the "Event Details" field.
13. Sign the "Participant Consent Signature" field as a proof of the participant's consent.
14. Identify the chef or instructor in the "Chef or Instructor" field.
15. Describe the filming capture in the "Filming Capture" field.
16. Provide more participant information in the "Participant Information" fields.
17. Optionally, enter any additional participant information, participant conversation, or other event details as required and necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant in this field.
- **Email**: Type the participant's email address.
- **Contact Number**: Enter the participant's contact number.
- **Filming Consent**: Select "True" if you consent to filming, "False" otherwise.
- **Participant Photo Release**: Select "True" if you consent to being photographed, "False" otherwise.
- **Participant Video Release**: Select "True" if you consent to being video recorded, "False" otherwise.
- **Participant Audio Release**: Select "True" if you consent to being audio recorded, "False" otherwise.
- **Participant Other Release**: Select "True" or "False" for other media releases not explicitly mentioned, if any.
- **School or Chef**: Enter the participant's school or chef affiliation.
- **School or Chef ID**: Enter the participant's school or chef ID.
- **Filming Date**: Enter the date of the filming.
- **Filming Location**: Enter the location where the filming will take place.
- **Filming Time**: Enter the time of the filming.
- **Event Details**: Provide any additional details about the event.
- **Participant Consent Signature**: Sign here to confirm your consent.
- **Chef or Instructor**: Enter the name of the chef or instructor.
- **Filming Capture**: Describe the filming capture in this field.
- **Participant Information**: Enter more information about the participant.
- **Participant Information 2**: Enter more participant information, if necessary.
- **Participant Information 3**: Enter even more participant information, if required.
- **Participant Information 4**: Enter any additional participant information, if any.
- **Event Details 2**: Enter any additional event details, if necessary.
- **Event Details 3**: Enter even more event details, if required.
- **Event Details 4**: Enter any additional event details, if any.
- **Participant Conversation**: Enter any participant conversation, if necessary.
- **Participant Other Release**: If not explicitly covered in other releases, select "True" or "False" for other media releases.
