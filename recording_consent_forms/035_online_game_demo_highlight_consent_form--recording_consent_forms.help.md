# Online Game Demo Highlight Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain consent and gather information from players who participate in online game demos. It aims to showcase their demos online and collect relevant data to enhance their experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Form Title** in the text field. This field is required.
2. Provide your **First Name** in the text field. This field is required.
3. Enter your **Email** address, which is also required.
4. If you want, you can provide your **Gamer Tag**, which is not required.
5. Select your preferred **Highlight Type** from the dropdown menu:
	* **Highlight**: For a highlight reel of your gameplay.
	* **Demo**: For a demo reel showcasing your gameplay.
	* **Game**: For a game-specific demo reel.
6. If you want to upload a file, enter the **Highlight Upload** in the text field. This field is not required.
7. Check the boxes for your desired **Permissions** for the upload:
	* **True**: Grant permission for your demo to be showcased.
	* **False**: Do not grant permission for your demo to be showcased.
	* **Maybe**: Undecided or unsure.
8. Enter the **Game Name** where you played the game in the text field. This field is required.
9. Enter the **Game Platform** where you played the game in the text field. This field is not required.
10. Enter the **Upload Date** in the date field. This field is required.
11. Enter the **Upload Time** in the time field. This field is required.
12. Enter any additional **Note** about your demo in the text field. This field is not required.
13. Confirm that your upload is correct by selecting **Confirm Upload**:
	* **True**: Confirm that the upload is correct.
	* **False**: Refuse to confirm the upload.
	* **Maybe**: Undecided or unsure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title**: Enter a title for your demo to be showcased online.
* **First Name**: Enter your player name in the text field.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Gamer Tag**: Enter your gamer tag if you have one.
* **Highlight Type**: Select the type of demo you want to showcase.
* **Highlight Upload**: Upload a file if you want to showcase it online.
* **Permissions**: Check the boxes for your desired permissions.
* **Game Name**: Enter the game name where you played the game.
* **Game Platform**: Enter the platform where you played the game.
* **Upload Date**: Enter the date of your upload.
* **Upload Time**: Enter the time of your upload.
* **Note**: Enter any additional information about your demo.
* **Confirm Upload**: Confirm that your upload is correct.
