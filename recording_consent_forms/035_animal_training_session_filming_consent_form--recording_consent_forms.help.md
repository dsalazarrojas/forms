# Animal Training Session Filming Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from animal owners for filming and recording of their training sessions. It ensures that all parties involved are aware of their rights and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form correctly, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your information as the animal owner.
	* Enter your first name in the "Owner First Name" field.
	* Enter your last name in the "Owner Last Name" field.
	* Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
	* Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
2. Provide information about your dog.
	* Enter your dog's name in the "Dog Name" field.
	* Enter your dog's breed in the "Dog Breed" field.
	* Enter your dog's age in the "Dog Age" field.
3. Choose the type of training your dog is receiving.
	* Select the type of training from the "Type of Training" dropdown menu.
4. Choose the trainer and training facility.
	* Enter the name of your trainer in the "Trainer Name" field.
	* Enter the name of the training facility in the "Training Facility Name" field.
5. Choose the date and time of the training session.
	* Enter the date of the session in the "Session Date" field.
	* Enter the start time of the session in the "Session Start Time" field.
6. Choose how the footage will be used.
	* Select how the footage will be used from the "Use For" dropdown menus.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Owner First Name**: Your first name as the animal owner.
* **Owner Last Name**: Your last name as the animal owner.
* **Email Address**: Your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number.
* **Owner Last Name**: Your last name as the animal owner.
* **Dog Name**: Name of your dog.
* **Dog Breed**: Breed of your dog.
* **Dog Age**: Age of your dog.
* **Type of Training**: Select the type of training your dog is receiving.
* **Trainer Name**: Name of your trainer.
* **Training Facility Name**: Name of the training facility.
* **Session Date**: Date of the session.
* **Session Start Time**: Time session begins.
* **Session Duration**: Estimated duration of the session.
* **Consent to Video Recording**: Allow video recording of the session.
* **Consent to Audio Recording**: Allow audio recording of the session.
* **Consent to Still Photography**: Allow photos to be taken during the session.
* **Use For Progress Documentation**: Use footage for documentation purposes.
* **Use For Trainer Education**: Use footage for trainer education and training.
* **Use For Marketing and Promotion**: Use footage for marketing and promotion purposes.
* **Use For Social Media Posting**: Post footage on social media platforms.
* **Use For Online Content Creation**: Use footage in online content creation.
* **Use For Research Purposes**: Use footage in professional research.
* **Anonymization Preference**: Anonymize names and identifying information.
* **Review Footage Before Public Use**: Right to review footage before sharing.
* **Withdrawal of Consent**: Understand you can withdraw consent at any time.
* **Understand Rights and Options**: Acknowledge understanding of rights and options.
* **Release of Liability**: Release facility from liability.
* **I Agree to These Terms**: Accept all terms of consent.
* **Date of Consent**: Date of consent.
* **Additional Notes or Comments**: Any other information or requests.
