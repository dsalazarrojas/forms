# film_archival_inclusion_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The film archival inclusion consent form is used to obtain consent from participants in a film archiving project. The form is designed to collect information from participants about their willingness to include their film in the archiving project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your name and email address in the provided fields.
2. Read the consent statement carefully and choose your response.
3. Provide any relevant project details if you wish.
4. Fill in your contact information.
5. Optionally, leave a note with your signature to confirm.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name (`participant_name`):** Enter your full name in this field.
* **Participant Email (`participant_email`):** Enter your email address. This is where we will contact you if needed.
* **Consent Given (`consent_given`):** Choose "I give my consent" if you are willing to include your film in the archiving project, or "I do not give my consent" if you are not.
* **Project Details (`project_details`):** If you wish to provide more information about your project, you can do so in this field.
* **Contact Information (`contact_info`):** Enter your contact information, such as phone number and address.
* **Signature (`signature`):** Optionally, leave a note with your signature to confirm your identity and confirm your consent.

## Tips
* Please read the consent statement carefully before making your selection.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
* You can modify this form according to your project's specific needs, but be sure to give credit to the original adaptation.
