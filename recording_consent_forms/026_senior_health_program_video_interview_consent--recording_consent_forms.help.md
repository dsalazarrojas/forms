# senior_health_program_video_interview_consent - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Senior Health Program Video Interview Consent Form is used to record the consent of participants in the Senior Health Program for video interviews.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Ensure you are filling out this form as an authorized representative of the Senior Health Program.
2. Fill out the required fields: program name, interview date/time, schedule preferences, and consent to share.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program name** (`program_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the program you are involved with.
* **Interview Date/Time** (`interview_date_time`, time, required): Select the date and time of the video interview.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, note, optional): Enter any additional contact information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Schedule Remote Interview** (`schedule_remote_interview`, select_multiple, required): Indicate if you are available for a remote interview.
* **Schedule Date** (`schedule_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the video interview.
* **Schedule Time** (`schedule_time`, time, optional): Select the time of the video interview.
* **Schedule Duration** (`schedule_duration`, number, optional): Select the duration of the video interview.
* **Schedule Frequency** (`schedule_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose the frequency of the video interview (Morning, Afternoon, Afternoon/Weekend).
* **Schedule Day Of Week** (`schedule_day_of_week`, select_one, required): Choose the day of the week of the video interview (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday).
* **Consent To Share** (`consent_to_share`, select_one, required): Indicate if you are consenting to share your information.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or details.
## Tips
This form will be used as a record of your consent for the video interview. Make sure to fill it out thoroughly and accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your representative.
Note: This form is not intended to be a substitute for any other forms or documentation required by the Senior Health Program.
