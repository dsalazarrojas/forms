# Live Audience Interview Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information and consent from participants before conducting a live audience interview. The form is designed to ensure that participants understand the terms and conditions of the recording and have given their explicit consent to be recorded.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. If applicable, provide your contact information (email, phone number) in the "Contact Info" field.
3. Sign your name in the "Signature" field, indicating your agreement to be recorded.
4. Provide your participant signature in the "Participant Signature" field.
5. Enter the date of signing in the "Date" field.
6. Fill in the production details and your relationship with the participant in the respective fields.
7. If applicable, fill in the participant's email and other contact information in the respective fields.
8. Select your choice of recording consent from the "Recording Consent" dropdown menu.
9. Confirm that you have agreed to be recorded and understand that you may be seen by the public in the "I have agreed to be recorded and understand that I may be seen by the public" field.
10. If applicable, fill in your date of birth and emergency contact information.
11. Enter the participant's address, city, and zip code.
12. If applicable, select the date and time of understanding.
13. Add any additional comments in the "Any additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (Participant Name, text, required): Please enter your name.
* **Contact Info** (Contact Info, text, optional): If you have any contact information (email, phone number), please provide it.
* **Signature** (Signature, note, required): This is a field for you to sign your name, indicating your agreement to be recorded.
* **Participant Signature** (Participant Signature, note, required): This is a field for the participant to sign their name, indicating their agreement to be recorded.
* **Date** (Date, date, required): Enter the date of signing.
* **Producer Name** (Producer Name, text, optional): If you are a producer, please enter your name.
* **Production Details** (Production Details, text, optional): Please enter any details related to the production.
* **School or Employer** (School or Employer, text, optional): If applicable, please enter your school or employer information.
* **Participant Email** (Participant Email, email, optional): If you have an email, please provide it.
* **Producer Email** (Producer Email, email, optional): If you are a producer, please enter your email.
* **Recording Consent** (Recording Consent, select_one, required): Choose how you want to be recorded.
* **I have agreed to be recorded and understand that I may be seen by the public** (I have agreed to be recorded and understand that I may be seen by the public, select_multiple, required): Confirm your agreement to be recorded and understood that you may be seen by the public.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, optional): If applicable, please enter your date of birth.
* **Emergency Contact** (Emergency Contact, text, optional): If you have an emergency contact, please provide their information.
* **Relationship** (Relationship, text, optional): If applicable, describe your relationship with the participant.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): If you have a phone number for the emergency contact, please provide it.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): If you have an email for the emergency contact, please provide it.
* **Address** (Address, text, optional): If applicable, enter the participant's address.
* **City** (City, text, optional): If applicable, enter the participant's city.
* **State** (State, text, optional): If applicable, enter the participant's state.
* **Zip** (Zip, text, optional): If applicable, enter the participant's zip code.
* **Date of Understanding** (Date of Understanding, date, optional): If applicable, select the date and time of understanding.
* **Time** (Time, time, optional): If applicable, select the time of understanding.
* **Any additional comments** (Any additional comments, note, optional): If you have any additional comments, please enter them.
* **Producer Comments** (Producer Comments, note, optional): If you are a producer, please enter any comments you have.
