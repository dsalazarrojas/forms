# virtual_meeting_recording_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The virtual meeting recording consent form is used to obtain consent from meeting participants for the recording of virtual meetings. This form helps ensure that all participants are aware of and agree to the recording of their meeting for reference or training purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and make sure you understand its purpose and requirements.
2. Answer the questions in the form truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Make sure to select the correct options for the recording permission, recording method, and recording purpose.
4. Enter your name and title (if applicable) in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Recording Consent** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): This field is a free-text input where you can provide your consent for the recording of the meeting.
* **Recording Permission** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required): This field asks for the permission to record the meeting, and you can choose one or more of the provided options:
	+ Option 1: I give my consent to record this meeting.
	+ Option 2: I do not give my consent to record this meeting.
	+ Option 3: I need to discuss with my manager/leader before giving consent.
* **Recording Method** (`3`, `select_one`, required): This field asks for the method of recording, and you can choose one of the provided options:
	+ Option 1: Audio only.
	+ Option 2: Video and audio.
	+ Option 3: Screen recording.
	+ Option 4: Live streaming.
	+ Option 5: Other (please specify).
* **Recording Purpose** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): This field is a free-text input where you can specify the purpose of the recording (e.g., training, reference, or review).
* **Meeting ID** (`5`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for entering the meeting ID or reference number.
* **Meeting Title** (`6`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for entering the title of the meeting.
* **Recording Date** (`7`, `date`, required/optional): This field is for entering the date of the recording.
* **Recording Time** (`8`, `time`, required/optional): This field is for entering the time of the recording.
* **Recording Location** (`9`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for entering the location of the recording.
* **Additional Comments** (`10`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for any additional comments or notes you have regarding the recording.
* **Name** (`11`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for entering your name.
* **Title** (`12`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for entering your title (if applicable).
