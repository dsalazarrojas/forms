# performance_lighting_test_recording_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to capture the consent and demographic information of participants in a performance lighting test recording. This form is used to ensure that participants have provided their consent to participate in the test recording and to collect relevant information about their demographic details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure that you have read and understood the purpose of this form.
2.  Fill in your test date in the "Test Date" field.
3.  Enter your participant name in the "Participant Name" field.
4.  Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Test Recording" field to indicate whether you consent to participate in the test recording.
5.  Enter your participant's email address in the "Participant Email" field.
6.  Enter your participant's phone number in the "Participant Phone" field.
7.  Enter the location of the test in the "Test Location" field.
8.  Enter the time of the test recording in the "Test Recording Time" field.
9.  Add any notes about the test in the "Test Notes" field.
10.  Enter any additional participant notes in the "Participant Notes" field.
11.  Confirm your participant's agreement to participate in the test recording in the "Participant Agreement" field.
12.  Confirm the test agreement in the "Test Agreement" field.
13.  Sign and date your signature in the "Signature Date" field.
14.  Sign your signature time in the "Signature Time" field.
15.  Add any notes about the test location in the "Test Location Notes" field.
16.  Enter your participant's address in the "Participant Address" field.
17.  Enter the event date in the "Event Date" field.
18.  Enter the event time in the "Event Time" field.
19.  Enter the second test date in the "Test Date 2" field.
20.  Enter the contact location in the "Contact Location" field.
21.  Enter the contact address in the "Contact Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Test Date** (Date): Enter the date of the test recording.
* **Participant Name** (Text): Enter your participant's name.
* **Test Recording** (Select One): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you consent to participate in the test recording.
* **Participant Email** (Email): Enter your participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone** (Text): Enter your participant's phone number.
* **Test Location** (Text): Enter the location of the test recording.
* **Test Recording Time** (Time): Enter the time of the test recording.
* **Participant Signature** (Note): Add any notes about your participant's signature.
* **Event Name** (Text): Enter the name of the event.
* **Contact Name** (Text): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (Email): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (Text): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Test Notes** (Text): Add any notes about the test recording.
* **Participant Notes** (Text): Add any notes about your participant's details.
* **Participant Agreement** (Text): Confirm your participant's agreement to participate in the test recording.
* **Test Agreement** (Text): Confirm the test agreement.
* **Signature Date** (Date): Sign and date your signature.
* **Signature Time** (Time): Sign your signature time.
* **Test Location Notes** (Text): Add any notes about the test location.
* **Participant Address** (Text): Enter your participant's address.
* **Event Date** (Date): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (Time): Enter the time of the event.
* **Test Date 2** (Date): Enter the second test date.
* **Contact Location** (Text): Enter the location of the contact person.
* **Contact Address** (Text): Enter the address of the contact person.
