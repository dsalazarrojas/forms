# Video Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Video Recording Consent Form is used to obtain consent from users for video recording purposes. This form is typically used by HR or management to record meetings, lectures, or other events where video recording is allowed, but requires consent from the users involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (User ID, User Name, and Recording Date) accurately.
2. Optionally, enter the User Name and Recording Date.
3. For the optional fields (Video Title, Recording Time, and Recording Location), you can leave them blank if not applicable.
4. Review your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **User ID** (User ID, number, required)
Enter your User ID, which is your unique identifier for this video recording.

### 2. **User Name** (User Name, text, optional)
Enter your name as it appears on your official documents, such as your ID card or passport.

### 3. **Video Title** (Video Title, text, optional)
If applicable, enter a brief title of the video recording, such as "Meeting with John Doe" or "Lecture on XYZ Topic".

### 4. **Recording Date** (Recording Date, date, required)
Enter the date of the recording, in the format dd/mm/yyyy.

### 5. **Recording Time** (Recording Time, time, optional)
Enter the time of the recording, in the format hh:MM AM/PM.

### 6. **Recording Location** (Recording Location, text, optional)
Enter the location of the recording, such as "Conference Room 101" or "Online via Zoom".

## Tips
- Make sure to review your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact the HR department or your management representative.
- The form should not be used for any purpose other than obtaining video recording consent.
- The submitted form will be kept confidential and used only for the intended purpose.
- You can ask for help if you need assistance or guidance on any of the fields.
- The form may be used in conjunction with other forms or procedures; however, this guide only addresses this specific form.

I hope this guide helps you complete the form successfully. If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out.
