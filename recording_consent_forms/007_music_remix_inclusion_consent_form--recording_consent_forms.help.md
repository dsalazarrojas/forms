# Music Remix Inclusion Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music Remix Inclusion Consent Form is used to collect user information and project details for music remix inclusion purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your information.
2. Provide project details, including the name of the project and any relevant context.
3. Specify whether you consent to share your project with other artists.
4. Provide any additional artist information, if applicable.
5. Enter release details, if applicable.
6. Confirm your consent to share your project.
7. Add any notes or comments you have.
8. Submit the form to complete the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (`user_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name, email, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your project, a brief description, and any other relevant context.
* **Remix Inclusion** (`remix_inclusion`, `text`, required/optional): Specify whether you consent to include your project in the remix process. If you don't select this option, your project will not be included.
* **Artist Info** (`artist_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any relevant information about the artist you wish to collaborate with.
* **Release Details** (`release_details`, `text`, required/optional): If applicable, enter the release details, such as date and time.
* **Sharing Consent** (`sharing_consent`, `text`, required/optional): Confirm your consent to share your project with other artists.
* **Notes Section** (`notes_section`, `text`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes you have about your project.
* **Confirmation** (`confirmation`, `text`, required/optional): Confirm that you have read and understand the terms and conditions of this form.

Note: The `required/optional` labels indicate whether the field is required or optional, but please note that all fields are actually optional, as all fields are marked as `required: false` in the form schema.
