# Academic Reflection Podcast Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Reflection Podcast Consent Form is a digital form used to capture participant consent and essential information for the recording of an academic podcast. This form is a crucial step in the recording process, ensuring that participants are aware of how their recording will be used and are willing to participate.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and usage of the recording.
2. Fill out the form with your accurate and complete information.
3. Select your consent and preferences for the recording's usage.
4. Sign the form digitally to validate your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name of Participant** (`participant_name`, text, required): Please enter your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Participant Email Address** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your primary email address where you can be reached.
* **Title of the Podcast Episode or Project** (`podcast_title`, text, required): Provide the title or reference name for the recording.
* **Date of Recording** (`recording_date`, date, required): Enter the date the session took place or will take place.
* **I grant permission to record my voice and image for academic purposes** (`grant_permission`, select_one, required): Select "Yes - I Consent" if you grant permission for the recording.
* **How can this recording be used?** (`scope_of_use`, select_multiple, required): Choose all approved uses for the recording.
* **Would you like your identity to remain anonymous?** (`anonymity_preference`, select_one, required): Select "Public Credit - Use my name", "Use a Pseudonym", or "Full Anonymity" depending on your preference.
* **I acknowledge that the recording is the property of the institution** (`ownership_acknowledgement`, select_one, required): Select "I Agree" to acknowledge the recording's ownership.
* **I understand that I will not receive financial compensation for this participation** (`remuneration_agreement`, select_one, required): Select "I Understand and Agree" if you acknowledge this condition.
* **Electronic Signature - Type Full Name** (`signature_name`, text, required): Enter your full name to validate your digital signature.
* **Date signed** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the current date to validate your digital signature.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when providing your contact information.
* Ensure you understand how your recording will be used before granting consent.
* Select all approved uses for the recording.
* Be mindful of your anonymity preference.
* Understand the institution's ownership and your remuneration agreement.
