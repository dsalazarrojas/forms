## Online Discussion Archiving Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain your consent for archiving and sharing your online discussions. This involves collecting and storing your personal data, such as your name and email, which will be made available to authorized personnel in our organization.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select "Yes" if you are willing to have your online discussions archived, or "No" if you are not.
2.  Enter your name in the "Name" field.
3.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4.  Provide a brief description of the online discussion in the "Discussion Details" field.
5.  Select "Signed" if you consent to have your online discussions archived and shared, or "Not Signed" if you do not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **General Information** (`Yes`, `No`, required: false): Select "Yes" if you are willing to have your online discussions archived, or "No" if you are not.
*   **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on official documents.
*   **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
*   **Discussion Details** (`Discussion Details`, `text`, required: false): Briefly describe the online discussion you are referring to.
*   **Signed Consent** (`Signed Consent`, `select_one`, required: false): Select "Signed" to indicate your consent to archive and share your online discussions, or "Not Signed" to indicate your refusal.
