# Rehabilitation App Usability Recording Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from participants in a rehabilitation app usability recording session. The form captures the participant's demographic information, consent for recording, and details about the recording device used.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Provide your demographic information, including your age, occupation, and contact details.
2. Confirm your consent to be recorded. This is a critical step, as it ensures you understand the purpose and implications of the recording.
3. Provide details about the recording device you used, including its model, serial number, and IP address.
4. Fill in any additional notes or comments as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (1)**: Enter your name and other relevant demographic information.
* **Contact Information (2)**: Provide your contact details, such as your email and phone number.
* **Participant Information (3)**: Enter your participant information, which will help us understand your demographics and experiences.
* **Recording Consent (4)**: Check "yes" if you agree to be recorded, or "no" if you do not. Note that "not applicable" is also an option for situations where recording is not possible.
* **Recording Consent Details (5)**: Explain why you agreed or disagreed to being recorded.
* **Recording Consent Signature (6)**: Sign your name to confirm your consent.
* **Participant Signature (7)**: Sign your name to confirm your identity.
* **Participant Date (8)**: Enter the date you participated in the recording session.
* **Recording Date (9)**: Enter the date the recording took place.
* **Recording Time (10)**: Enter the time the recording took place.
* **Participant Notes (11)**: Add any additional comments or notes about your experience.
* **Participant Signature Notes (12)**: Add any additional comments or notes about your signature.
* **Participant Phone Number (13)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Participant Email (14)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Participant Location (15)**: Enter your location where the recording took place.
* **Participant IP Address (16)**: Enter the IP address you used during the recording session.
* **Recording Device (17)**: Enter the model of the recording device you used.
* **Recording Device Notes (18)**: Add any additional comments or notes about your recording device.
* **Recording Device Model (19)**: Enter the model of the recording device you used.
* **Recording Device Version (20)**: Enter the version of the recording device you used.
* **Recording Device Serial Number (21)**: Enter the serial number of the recording device you used.
* **Recording Device IP Address (22)**: Enter the IP address of the recording device you used.
* **Participant Occupation (23)**: Enter your occupation.
* **Participant Age (24)**: Enter your age.
* **Recording Consent Checkbox (25)**: Check "yes" if you agree to be recorded, or "no" if you do not.
