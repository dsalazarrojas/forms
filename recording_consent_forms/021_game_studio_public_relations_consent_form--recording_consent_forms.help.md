# Game Studio Public Relations Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Game Studio Public Relations Consent Form is a confidential form used to collect information from game development team members for public relations purposes. This form helps us understand your public image and representation in public forums. Please note that the form is intended for internal use only.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name
2. Enter your email address
3. Enter your phone number
4. (Optional) Provide your company name if you are representing a company
5. (Optional) Enter your job title
6. Select your department within the company (choose from IT and Engineering, Marketing and Sales, or Operations)
7. (Optional) Enter your date of birth
8. (Optional) Enter your time of birth

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (First name, text, required): Enter your first name as it is commonly known to the public.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your public email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your public phone number.
* **Company** (Company, text, optional): If you represent a company, enter the name of the company.
* **Job title** (Job title, text, optional): Enter your job title as it is commonly known to the public.
* **Department** (Department, select_one, required): Select the department within your company that you represent (IT and Engineering, Marketing and Sales, or Operations).
* **Date of birth** (Date of birth, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of birth** (Time of birth, time, optional): Enter your time of birth in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
