# Student Faculty Roundtable Recording Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain consent from faculty members to record meetings or discussions with students, ensuring that both parties are aware of and agree to the recording process. This form helps maintain transparency and ensures that students have the necessary consent for recording purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer the questions accurately, ensuring you have the necessary information to complete the form correctly.
2. Select the correct options for each field as per your understanding and agreement.
3. Ensure you sign the "Recording Consent Signed" field to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Recording Consent** (`recording_consent`, `select_one`, required): This field asks if you agree to be recorded. Select "Yes" if you are comfortable with being recorded, and "No" if you are not.
* **Recording Location** (`recording_location`, `text`, optional): If you are recording, please specify the location where the recording will take place.
* **Faculty Member** (`faculty_member`, `text`, optional): You can enter the name of the faculty member you are interacting with.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, optional): If you are a student, please enter your name.
* **Recording Date** (`recording_date`, `date`, optional): You can specify the date of the recording.
* **Recording Time** (`recording_time`, `time`, optional): If applicable, please specify the time of the recording.
* **Recording Device** (`recording_device`, `text`, optional): You can mention the device or equipment used for recording.
* **Recording Purpose** (`recording_purpose`, `text`, optional): You can describe the reason or purpose of the recording.
* **Other Details** (`other_details`, `text`, optional): If there are any additional details or context about the recording, please share them in this field.
* **Faculty Agreement** (`faculty_agreement`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable options if you agree to be recorded. Options include "I understand and agree" or "Do not record".
* **Faculty Comments** (`faculty_comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any comments or concerns regarding the recording, please share them here.
* **Student Agreement** (`student_agreement`, `text`, optional): If you are a student, please enter your understanding and agreement to being recorded.
* **Student Comments** (`student_comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any comments or concerns as a student, please share them here.
* **Faculty Consent** (`faculty_consent`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" if you have obtained faculty consent, and "No" if not.
* **Recording Consent Signed** (`recording_consent_signed`, `select_one`, required): As the faculty member, please select "Signed" if you have signed the consent, and "Not Signed" if not.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
- Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
- If you're unsure about any field, please consult the faculty or student representative for guidance.
