# Academic Roundtable Livestream Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather consent from participants in an Academic Roundtable Livestream event for recording and livestreaming. This form is essential for ensuring that all participants have granted permission for their sessions to be recorded and distributed, and for any restrictions on the use of those recordings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Full Legal Name** in the **Participant Name** field (field 2).
2. Enter a valid email address in the **Contact Email** field (field 3). This will be used for confirmation and recording notices.
3. Select your **Role at the Event** (field 4). This will help identify your position in the event, which may impact how your recording is handled.
4. Select whether you **Consent to Livestream** (field 5) and **Consent to Record Session** (field 6). These options are for granting permission to stream and record your session, respectively.
5. If you grant consent, specify any **Restrictions on Use of Recording** (field 7) that you have.
6. Choose your **Publicity Preferences for Speaker Bios** (field 8), if applicable.
7. Type your **Signature Confirmation** (field 9), which should be your full name to confirm your consent.
8. Enter the **Date of Consent** (field 10), which is the date when you gave your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant name** (field 2): Enter your full legal name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Contact email** (field 3): Enter a valid email address that you check regularly. This will be used for confirmation and recording notices.
* **Role at event** (field 4):
  + **Speaker**: You will be presenting or delivering content during the event.
  + **Panellist**: You will be participating in a panel discussion during the event.
  + **Moderator**: You will be leading the event or discussion.
  + **Attendee**: You will be attending the event as a participant.
* **Consent to livestream** (field 5):
  + **True**: You grant permission for your session to be streamed online.
  + **False**: You do not grant permission for your session to be streamed online.
* **Consent to record session** (field 6):
  + **True**: You grant permission for your session to be recorded.
  + **False**: You do not grant permission for your session to be recorded.
* **Restrictions on use of recording** (field 7): If you have any specific limitations on how your recording can be used, enter them here.
* **Publicity preferences for speaker bios** (field 8):
  + Specify how you wish to be credited or described in promotional materials related to your session.
* **Signature confirmation** (field 9): Type your full name to confirm that you have read and understood the consent form.
* **Date of consent** (field 10): Enter the date when you gave your consent.
