# remote_workshop_recording_sharing_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "remote_workshop_recording_sharing_consent_form" is used to gather consent from workshop participants for sharing workshop recordings. This form is typically used in a workshop setting where recordings are made of the session and need to be shared with participants or third-party organizations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field and provide the necessary information.
2. Make sure to complete all required fields.
3. If a field is optional, you can leave it blank if you do not have the necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Recording Sharing Consent** (`workshop_recording_sharing_concent`, `select_multiple`, not required): Choose one or multiple options from the list of how workshop recordings can be shared. The options are: Recording and sharing of workshop recordings, Sharing workshop recordings with other workshop participants, and Sharing workshop recordings with third-party organizations.
* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, `text`, not required): This field is for any additional information about the participant. You can type any relevant details here.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, not required): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, not required): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, `note`, not required): This field is for any additional notes about the participant. You can type any relevant details here.
* **Sharing With Other Participants** (`sharing_with_other_participants`, `select_one`, not required): Choose whether or not to share the recording with other participants.
* **Workshop Recording Sharing Consent** (`workshop_recording_sharing_concent`, `text`, not required): This field is a duplicate of the first field, it should be left blank and is not required.

## Tips
* Make sure to review and complete all fields before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the workshop organizer or support team for assistance.
* This form is intended to be used in a workshop setting where recordings are made of the session and need to be shared with participants or third-party organizations.
