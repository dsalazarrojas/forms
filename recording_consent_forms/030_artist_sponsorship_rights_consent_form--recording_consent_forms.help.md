# Artist Sponsorship Rights Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Artist Sponsorship Rights Consent Form is a document that outlines the terms and conditions of a sponsorship agreement between an artist and a sponsor. It ensures that both parties have a clear understanding of their roles, responsibilities, and rights.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in all the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure that your answers accurately reflect your agreement.
3. Save or print the completed form for future reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Artist Name (1)
• **Artist Name** (`artist_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the artist as it appears on their contract or identification documents.
### Artist Email Address (2)
• **Artist Email Address** (`artist_email`, `email`, required): Enter the artist's email address where they can be contacted.
### Artist Phone Number (3)
• **Artist Phone Number** (`artist_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the artist's phone number where they can be contacted.
### Sponsor Name or Organization (4)
• **Sponsor Name or Organization** (`sponsor_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the sponsor or organization.
### Sponsor Representative Name and Title (5)
• **Sponsor Representative Name and Title** (`sponsor_representative`, `text`, required): Enter the name and title of the representative of the sponsor.
### Sponsor Contact Email (6)
• **Sponsor Contact Email** (`sponsor_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the sponsor or representative.
### Date of Agreement (7)
• **Date of Agreement** (`agreement_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date on which this agreement was made.
### Agreement Period - Months (8)
• **Agreement Period - Months** (`agreement_period`, `number`, required): Enter the duration of the sponsorship agreement in months.
### Type of Sponsorship (9)
• **Type of Sponsorship** (`sponsorship_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of sponsorship from the options provided.
### Artist Grants Image or Likeness Rights (10)
• **Artist Grants Image or Likeness Rights** (`image_rights_granted`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the artist grants image or likeness rights to the sponsor.
### Recording Rights Granted (11)
• **Recording Rights Granted** (`recording_rights`, `select_one`, required): Select whether recording rights are granted to the sponsor.
### Exclusive Terms (12)
• **Exclusive Terms** (`exclusive_terms`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the agreement is exclusive or not.
### Geographic Territory of Rights (13)
• **Geographic Territory of Rights** (`geographic_territory`, `text`, optional): This field is optional and only applies if you selected the type of sponsorship as "Financial" or "Mixed".
### Permission for Social Media Usage (14)
• **Permission for Social Media Usage** (`social_media_usage`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the artist grants permission for social media usage.
### Compensation or Benefit Amount (15)
• **Compensation or Benefit Amount** (`compensation_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the compensation or benefit amount agreed upon.
### Payment Terms (16)
• **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `text`, required): Describe the payment terms of the agreement.
### Early Termination Allowed (17)
• **Early Termination Allowed** (`termination_clause`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the agreement can be terminated early.
### Dispute Resolution Method (18)
• **Dispute Resolution Method** (`dispute_resolution`, `select_one`, optional): This field is optional and only applies if you selected "No" for "Exclusive Terms".
### Artist Grants Consent to All Terms Above (19)
• **Artist Grants Consent to All Terms Above** (`consent_granted`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the artist grants consent to all terms above.
### Date of Artist Signature or Consent (20)
• **Date of Artist Signature or Consent** (`signature_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date on which the artist signed or gave their consent.
### Additional Terms or Conditions (21)
• **Additional Terms or Conditions** (`additional_terms`, `text`, optional): This field is optional and can be used to add any additional terms or conditions.

## Tips
- Make sure to carefully read and understand each field before completing it.
- Double-check the accuracy of all information entered.
- Save or print the completed form for future reference.
