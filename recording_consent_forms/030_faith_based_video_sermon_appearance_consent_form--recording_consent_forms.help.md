# Faith Based Video Sermon Appearance Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information from individuals who wish to be recorded and livestreamed during a faith-based video sermon appearance. It ensures that the individual has provided their consent to being recorded and livestreamed, and that they are aware of any restrictions or limitations on recording and livestreaming their appearance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that all necessary information is collected.
2. Review each field carefully and make sure you understand what is being asked.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the production team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name?** (`name`, `text`, required): This field is where you should enter your name as you would like it to be displayed during the recording and livestreaming.
* **Contact Email Address** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Please enter your email address so we can contact you with any questions or concerns.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Please enter your phone number so we can contact you with any questions or concerns.
* **Do you consent to being recorded and livestreamed during the service?** (`recording_consent`, `select_one`, required): This field asks if you agree to being recorded and livestreamed during the service. Please select "True" if you consent, and "False" if you do not.
* **Do you have any concerns about being recorded and livestreamed?** (`recording_concerns`, `text`, optional): If you have any concerns or questions, please enter them in this field.
* **What are your available dates and times for recording and livestreaming?** (`available_dates_times`, `text`, optional): Please enter any available dates and times when you are available for recording and livestreaming.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional information or comments, please enter them in this field.
* **Do you have any restrictions or limitations on recording and livestreaming your appearance?** (`recording_restrictions`, `select_one`, optional): This field asks if you have any restrictions or limitations on recording and livestreaming your appearance. Please select "True" if you do, and "False" if you do not.
* **Do you consent to being recorded and livestreamed for the following services?** (`recording_services`, `select_multiple`, required): This field asks you to select which services you consent to being recorded and livestreamed for. Please select all that apply.
* **Do you have any additional information about your availability or recording requirements?** (`additional_info`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional information about your availability or recording requirements, please enter them in this field.
* **Is this a one-time recording or a regular series of recordings?** (`recording_type`, `select_one`, required): This field asks if this is a one-time recording or a regular series of recordings. Please select "One-time" if this is a one-time recording, and "Regular Series" if this is a regular series of recordings.
* **Do you have any specific requirements or restrictions for the recording and livestreaming settings?** (`recording_settings`, `text`, optional): If you have any specific requirements or restrictions for the recording and livestreaming settings, please enter them in this field.
