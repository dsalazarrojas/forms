# Open Access Academic Video Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Open Access Academic Video Consent Form is used to collect participant consent for video recordings in academic settings. This form ensures that participants understand the purpose of the recording and agree to its use in academic settings.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate participant information option.
2. Choose the purpose of the video recording.
3. Provide your participant's consent by selecting "Yes".
4. Date of consent (optional).
5. Provide any additional participant notes if necessary.
6. Enter the researcher's information.
7. Enter participant notes if necessary.
8. Provide participant's video consent.
9. Sign your participant consent.
10. The researcher should provide their video consent if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, select_one): Select "Yes" if you are the participant, "No" if you are not.
* **Purpose of the Video Recording** (`purpose_video_recording`, select_multiple): Choose the reason for the video recording (e.g., Research, Teaching, Presentation, or Other).
* **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`, select_one): Select "Yes" to indicate your consent for the recording.
* **Date** (`participant_consent_date`, date): If you are selecting "Yes" for participant consent, please enter the date of your consent.
* **Signed** (`participant_consent_signed`, select_one): Select "Yes" if you have signed the consent form, "No" otherwise.
* **Information about the participant** (`participant_information_2`, text): Enter any additional information about yourself as a participant.
* **Notes** (`participant_consent_note`, note): Enter any notes or comments related to your consent.
* **Researcher Information** (`researcher_information`, text): Enter your researcher's information.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_consent_note_2`, note): Enter any notes or comments related to the participant.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text): Enter your contact information.
* **Participant's Video Consent** (`participant_video_consent`, select_one): Select "Yes" to indicate your video consent.
* **Signature** (`participant_consent_signature`, text): Sign your name as a participant.
* **Researcher's Video Consent** (`researcher_video_consent`, select_one): Select "Yes" if you, as a researcher, consent to the recording.
* **Participant Information 3** (`participant_information_3`, text): Enter any additional information about the participant (optional).
