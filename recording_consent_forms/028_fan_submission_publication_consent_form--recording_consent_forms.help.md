# Fan Submission Publication Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect consent from fans to publish their submissions, such as music, videos, or images. It is used to verify that the fan understands and agrees to the terms of publication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your name, email, and submission type.
2. Choose whether you consent to the publication of your submission.
3. Provide any additional comments you would like to share.
4. Enter your email address for verification purposes.
5. Enter the name and email of the artist or creator.
6. Select the type of submission you are making (music, video, image, or other).
7. Choose the date and time of the submission (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fan Submission Publication Consent Form**: This is the title of the form, which explains its purpose.
* **Fan Content**: This field is optional, and you can enter any additional comments about your submission.
* **Publication Permission**: Select "Yes" if you consent to the publication of your submission, or "No" if you do not. Please note that not selecting an option will be treated as "Not Stated" and may not be publishable.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for verification purposes.
* **Fan Name**: This field is optional, but it's the name of the person submitting the content.
* **Fan Email**: Enter your email address, which will be used for verification purposes.
* **Artist Name**: This field is optional, and it's the name of the artist or creator.
* **Artist Email**: Enter the email address of the artist or creator.
* **Submission Type**: Select the type of submission you are making (music, video, image, or other).
* **Date Submission**: Enter the date of the submission (if applicable).
* **Time Submission**: Enter the time of the submission (if applicable).

## Tips
Please note that not selecting "Yes" for publication permission in the "Publication Permission" field will be treated as "Not Stated", and your submission may not be publishable.
