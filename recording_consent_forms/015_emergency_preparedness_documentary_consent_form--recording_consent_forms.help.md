# Emergency Preparations Documentary Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information, consent, and other relevant details for emergency preparations. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and honestly to ensure that your participation is recorded correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your participant details, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Select your roles played in the emergency preparations.
3. Specify your consent for video, audio, and photo recording.
4. Provide additional contact information for media purposes.
5. Fill in your consent date, time, and location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, `text`, required): This field is where you provide your personal details, including your name.
* **Roles Played** (`roles`, `text`, required): List the specific roles you played in the emergency preparations.
* **Emergency Preparations Films** (`emergency_preparations_films`, `text`, required): Describe the emergency preparations you were involved in.
* **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`, `number`, required): Indicate your consent for video, audio, and photo recording.
* **Participant Role Consent** (`participant_role_consent`, `number`, required): Specify your consent for your role in the emergency preparations.
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, `text`, required): This field is similar to the first "Participant Details" field, but you can provide any additional details you think are necessary.
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, required): If you are affiliated with an organization, provide its name and details.
* **Organization Role** (`organization_role`, `text`, required): Describe your role within the organization.
* **Participant Organization** (`participant_organization`, `text`, required): If you are representing an organization, provide its name and details.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, required): Provide your phone number for contact.
* **Participant Media Contact** (`participant_media_contact`, `text`, required): Specify your media contact information, such as a website or social media handle.
* **Participant Note** (`participant_note`, `note`, required): Add any additional notes about your participation.
* **Other Contact** (`participant_other_contact`, `text`, optional): If you have an alternate contact person, provide their name and details.
* **Other Phone** (`participant_other_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the other contact's phone number.
* **Other Note** (`participant_other_note`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes about the other contact.
* **Participant Video** (`participant_video_consent`, `number`, required): Indicate your consent for video recording.
* **Participant Audio** (`participant_audio_consent`, `number`, required): Specify your consent for audio recording.
* **Participant Photo** (`participant_photo_consent`, `number`, required): Indicate your consent for photo recording.
* **Participant Video Audio** (`participant_video_audio_consent`, `number`, required): Consent for video and audio recording.
* **Participant Photo Audio** (`participant_photo_audio_consent`, `number`, required): Consent for photo and audio recording.
* **Consent Date** (`participant_consent_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you granted your consent.
* **Consent Time** (`participant_consent_time`, `time`, required): Specify the time you granted your consent.
* **Consent Timezone** (`participant_consent_timezone`, `text`, required): Indicate your time zone for the consent.
* **Consent Location** (`participant_consent_location`, `text`, required): Provide the location where you granted your consent.

## Tips

* Ensure that you fill out the form accurately and honestly.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant authorities or the form administrators.
