# film_trailer_distribution_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The film trailer distribution consent form is used to collect details from distributors for promotional trailer distribution, including trailer details, distributor information, and agreement upload.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field as needed for your trailer distribution.
2. Upload your trailer file.
3. Select your distribution authorization.
4. Choose your digital signature.
5. Upload a sample agreement.
6. Provide your distributor contact information.
7. Fill out the trailer notes fields as needed.
8. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trailer Details** (`trailer_details`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the trailer.
* **Distributor Details** (`distributor_details`, text, required): Enter your name, email, and phone number as the authorized distributor.
* **Trailer File** (`trailer_file`, text, required): Upload or enter the path to your trailer file.
* **Date Uploaded** (`upload_date`, date, required): Enter the date you uploaded the trailer.
* **Time Uploaded** (`upload_time`, time, required): Enter the time you uploaded the trailer.
* **Distributor Name** (`distributor_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the distributor.
* **Distributor Email** (`distributor_email`, email, required): Enter your email address as the distributor.
* **Distributor Phone** (`distributor_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number as the distributor.
* **Trailer ID** (`trailer_id`, text, optional): Enter a unique trailer ID if necessary.
* **Distribution Authorization** (`distribution_authorization`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the authorization level for this trailer.
* **Digital Signature** (`digital_signature`, select_one, optional): Choose your digital signature.
* **Distributor Agreement** (`distributor_agreement`, text, optional): Upload a sample agreement for this trailer.
* **Agreement Upload** (`upload_agreement`, text, optional): Upload any additional agreements related to this trailer.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, optional): Enter the date you signed this form.
* **Signed By** (`signed_by`, text, optional): Enter your name as the signer.
* **Time Signed** (`signed_at`, time, optional): Enter the time you signed this form.
* **Agreement Text** (`upload_agreement_text`, text, optional): Enter any additional text related to this agreement.
* **Distributor Notes** (`distributor_notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes from the distributor.
* **Trailer Notes** (`trailer_notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes from the trailer owner.
* **Trailer Notes 2** (`trailer_notes_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional trailer notes.
* **Trailer Notes 3** (`trailer_notes_3`, text, optional): Enter any more trailer notes.
* **Trailer Notes 4** (`trailer_notes_4`, text, optional): Enter any additional trailer notes.
* **Trailer Notes 5** (`trailer_notes_5`, text, optional): Enter any more trailer notes.
* **Distributor Notes 2** (`distributor_notes_2`, text, optional): Enter any notes from the distributor.

Note: The required fields are marked as required in the form schema.
