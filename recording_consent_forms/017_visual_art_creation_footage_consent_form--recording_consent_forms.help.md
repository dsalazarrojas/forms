# Visual Art Creation Footage Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from artists for the creation and use of their footage in visual art projects. It's essential for artists to provide accurate and truthful information about themselves, the footage, and their consent. Please review each field carefully before submitting the form.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the first page with basic artist information (page **1**). You can enter any relevant details, but this is not a required field.
2. On the second page, provide details about the footage you are consenting to use (page **2**). Again, accuracy is key in this field, but it is optional.
3. On page 3, make sure to select "Consent" if you are willing to grant permission for your footage to be used in visual art projects. You can also choose "No Consent" if you wish to decline.
4. If you have any additional information that you want to share, you can add it in the fourth page (page **4**), which is a free-text field.
5. Finally, sign your name in the fifth page (page **5**) to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist Information** (`artist_info`, text, optional): Enter your name, email, and any other contact details relevant to your art.
* **Footage Information** (`footage_info`, text, optional): Briefly describe the footage you are consenting to use. Include any relevant details such as location, date, or equipment used.
* **Consent Information** (`consent_info`, select_one, required): Choose "Consent" if you agree to permit the use of your footage. If you decline, select "No Consent".
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, note, optional): Add any extra details you want to share about your artwork, the project, or your consent.
* **Signature** (`signature_info`, text, required): Your signature confirms that the information provided is true and accurate. Please sign your name and date the form.
