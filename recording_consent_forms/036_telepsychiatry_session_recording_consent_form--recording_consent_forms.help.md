# Telepsychiatry Session Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture the client's consent for recording of telepsychiatry sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and select the correct options for each field.
2. Answer all questions truthfully to ensure accurate recording of your session.
3. Sign and date the form before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Recording Consent Form (Page 1)**
	+ **Session Recording Consent Form**: This is the main question asking if you agree to the recording of your session. Please select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your consent.
* **Client Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Client Phone**: If you have a phone number, please enter it. This is an optional field.
* **Client Email**: If you have an email address, please enter it. This is an optional field.
* **Client Address**: Please enter your current address if you have one. This is an optional field.
* **Consent Reason**: This is an optional field where you can provide a reason for your consent or non-consent.
* **Date of Birth**: Please enter your date of birth in the required format (dd/mm/yyyy).
* **Gender**: Please select your gender from the options provided.
* **Session Type**: This question is related to the session type, but it seems to be a duplicate of the first question. To avoid confusion, please select 'Yes' or 'No' for this question as well.
* **Client IP**: Please enter your IP address if you have one. This is an optional field.
* **Consent Date**: Please enter the date you are giving your consent.
* **Session Date**: Please enter the date of your session.
* **Time**: Please enter the time of your session.
* **Consent Time**: Please enter the time of your consent.
* **Client Agreed Session Recording**: Please select 'True' or 'False' to indicate if you agree to the recording of your session.
* **Client Agreed Recording IP**: Please select 'True' or 'False' to indicate if you agree to the recording of your session via IP address.
* **Client Agreed Recording Phone**: Please select 'True' or 'False' to indicate if you agree to the recording of your session via phone.
* **Session Agreed Recording**: Please select 'True' or 'False' to indicate if you agree to the recording of your session.
* **Recording Agreed**: Please select 'True' or 'False' to indicate if you agree to the recording of your session.
* **Client Agreed IP**: Please select 'True' or 'False' to indicate if you agree to the recording of your session via IP address.
