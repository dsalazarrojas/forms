# Data Literacy Workshop Broadcast Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Data Literacy Workshop Broadcast Consent Form is used to gather essential information and consent from participants before the start of a Data Literacy Workshop broadcast. This form ensures that participants are aware of and agree to the terms and conditions, including the recording of their participation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each field carefully and provide the required information.
2. Fill in the form completely and accurately.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the workshop organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional):
	+ Provide your phone number where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Permission to Record** (`permission_to_record`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select "True" to indicate that you are aware of and agree to be recorded during the workshop.
* **Permission to Broadcast** (`permission_to_broadcase`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select one or more options to indicate which types of broadcasts you are willing to participate in (Audio, Video, Live).
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date you are giving your consent for the workshop.
* **Consent Time** (`consent_time`, time, optional):
	+ Enter the time you are giving your consent for the workshop.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional):
	+ Add any additional comments or concerns you may have.
* **Agreement to Terms and Conditions** (`agreement_to_terms_and_conditions`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select "True" to indicate that you have read, understand, and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Agreement to Release** (`agreement_to_release`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select "True" to indicate that you are willing to release your recordings and materials for further education and development.
* **Participant Agreement** (`participant_agreement`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select one or more options to indicate which agreements you are willing to make regarding the workshop (True or False).
* **Participant Agreement Date** (`participant_agreement_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date you are agreeing to the participant agreement.
* **Participant Agreement Time** (`participant_agreement_time`, time, optional):
	+ Enter the time you are agreeing to the participant agreement.
