# team_video_analysis_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from team members for video analysis and other purposes. It ensures that participants are aware of the recording and consent to its creation, analysis, and sharing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand its purpose and each field's significance.
2. Select the appropriate "Form Purpose" from the options provided.
3. Provide your name, "Participant Information", and other required contact details in the "Participant Information" field.
4. Check the box indicating your consent to record and review the video in the "Consent to Record and Review" field.
5. Select the options for "Consent to Share" and "Consent to Share with Others" according to your preferences.
6. Enter your "Date of Birth" in the required format "DD/MM/YY".
7. Sign the "Participant Signature" field with your name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Purpose** (`form_purpose`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Select the correct purpose for which this form is being filled out. Options are "Analysis" and "Training".
* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your full name and any other necessary information for your participant status.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Check the box indicating your consent to record and review the video. Options are "True" and "False".
* **Recording Purpose** (`recording_purpose`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the purpose of the video recording, if necessary.
* **Recording Location** (`recording_location`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the location where the recording will take place.
* **Recording Duration** (`recording_duration`, number, required: false):
	+ Enter the duration of the recording in hours.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your contact details, such as phone number, email, or address.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the name and contact details of an emergency contact.
* **Consent to Record and Review** (`consent_to_record_and_review`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Check the box indicating your consent to record and review the video. Options are "True" and "False".
* **Consent to Share** (`consent_to_share`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select the options for sharing the video with others. Options are "True" and "False".
* **Consent to Share with Others** (`consent_to_share_with_others`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select the options for sharing the video with specific individuals or groups.
* **Date of Birth (DD/MM/YY)** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false):
	+ Enter your date of birth in the required format.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, note, required: false):
	+ Sign the form with your name, indicating your acceptance of the terms and conditions.

## Tips
* Ensure you understand each field's purpose and significance before filling out the form.
* Review the form carefully to avoid missing any important sections.
* Be truthful and honest when providing your contact details and other information.
* Keep in mind that providing false information or failing to provide required details may lead to consequences in the future.
