# Animation Scene Commentary Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect consent from animation participants for the use of their commentary in various contexts. It includes fields that cover the participant's name, professional details, project information, recording medium, and permissions for use in different materials.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please start by filling out all required fields carefully and accurately.
2. For each field, select the correct option that reflects your agreement or preference.
3. Enter your full name in the "Digital Signature" field as it appears on your official government-issued ID.
4. Date and sign the form to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official government-issued ID.
* **Professional Title and Project Role**: Describe your title and role in the project, e.g., Director, Lead Animator.
* **Scene or Sequence ID**: Enter the ID or title of the scene or sequence you are participating in.
* **Animation Project Title**: Enter the title of the animation project you are participating in.
* **Commentary Recording Type**: Select the type of commentary recording you are participating in:
	+ Audio Only
	+ Audio and Video - On-camera
	+ Written Commentary - Textual
* **Permission to Use Commentary in Behind-the-Scenes Material**: Select "Yes - I agree" to grant permission for your commentary to be used in behind-the-scenes materials.
* **Permission to Use in Educational or Training Materials**: Select "Yes - I agree" to grant permission for your commentary to be used in educational or training materials.
* **Grant of Intellectual Property for Commentary**: Select "I grant all rights to the studio" to grant all rights to your recorded commentary to the studio.
* **Authorization to Edit Commentary**: Select "I authorize the studio to edit" to allow the studio to edit your commentary for length, clarity, or style.
* **Digital Signature**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official government-issued ID.
* **Date Signed**: Enter the date you are signing this form.
