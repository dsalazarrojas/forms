# data_security_awareness_video_production_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture employees' consents for video recordings, ensuring that they are aware of and agree to the terms of the recording. It is a critical document for maintaining data security and employee trust within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct answer for "Video Production Consent" (question 1) to indicate whether you agree to the video production.
2. If you choose "Yes" for "Video Production Consent", you will be asked to select the type of consent you are giving, including "Consent to video tape" and "Consent to audio recording" (questions 2 and 3). Select all applicable options.
3. For "Video Tape Duration" (question 3), enter the length of the video recording, in minutes.
4. Specify the "Recording Location" (question 4) where the recording will take place.
5. Enter the "Recording Date" (question 5) and "Recording Time" (question 6) for the video recording.
6. In the "Employee Consent Statement" (question 7), type your consent statement, ensuring that it accurately reflects your understanding and agreement to the terms of the recording.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Video Production Consent (1)** (`Video Production Consent`, Select One, Required): 
Indicate whether you give consent to video production.

* **Consent to Video Tape (2)** (`Consent to video tape`, Select Multiple, Not Required):
Select from the options "Consent to video tape" or "Consent to audio recording" if applicable.

* **Video Tape Duration (3)** (`Video Tape Duration`, Number, Not Required):
Enter the length of the video recording in minutes.

* **Recording Location (4)** (`Recording Location`, Text, Not Required):
Specify the location where the recording will take place.

* **Recording Date (5)** (`Recording Date`, Date, Not Required):
Enter the date of the video recording.

* **Recording Time (6)** (`Recording Time`, Time, Not Required):
Enter the time of the video recording.

* **Employee Consent Statement (7)** (`Employee Consent Statement`, Text, Not Required):
Type a brief statement expressing your consent to the terms of the recording. 

## Tips
Please fill out the form carefully and truthfully. If you are unsure about any of the questions, contact your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
