# Performance Lighting Design Documentation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Lighting Design Documentation Consent Form is a critical component of our project management process. It ensures that all contributors understand and agree to the terms of their involvement in a project. This form is used to collect essential information for transparency and clarity on our project documentation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, ensuring that all required fields are filled out.
2. Enter your contributor information, including your name, email, and role.
3. Select the type of consent you are providing (materials, events, productions, educational, or promotional).
4. Choose a consent date.
5. Sign and date your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contributor** (`contributor`, text, required): Enter your name to identify you as a contributor to the project.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name if you wish to have your name associated with your contribution.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address. This will be used to communicate project updates and notifications.
* **Role** (`role`, text, optional): Describe your role in the project, such as designer, engineer, or manager.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter your company or institution name.
* **Contact** (`contact`, text, optional): Enter a contact method, such as phone number or address.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, text, optional): Enter an email address to contact you.
* **Consent Type** (`consent_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of consent you are providing. You can select multiple types.
	+ Materials: Permission to share materials related to the project.
	+ Events: Permission to include your name and contribution in events related to the project.
	+ Productions: Permission to include your contribution in production-related activities.
	+ Educational: Permission to share educational content related to the project.
	+ Promotional: Permission to use your contribution in promotional materials.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, required): Enter the date of consent.
* **Consent Signature** (`consent_signature`, note, required): Sign and date your consent.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* Keep a record of your consent, as it may be required for future reference.
