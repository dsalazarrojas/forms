# Academic Peer Exchange Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Peer Exchange Recording Consent Form is designed to collect information from academic peers about their consent to participate in a recording session. This form is intended to ensure that all participants are aware of and agree to the terms and conditions of the recording.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully and ensure you understand all the questions and options.
2. Answer all the required questions, which are marked with a red asterisk (*).
3. Provide your full name and email address as requested.
4. Indicate your institution affiliation as relevant.
5. Provide the title and date of the exchange session.
6. Select the types of recording you consent to.
7. Provide consent to video and audio recording.
8. Choose how you consent to recording distribution.
9. Choose how you consent to recording publication.
10. Specify the intended purpose of the recording.
11. Select your preference for access restrictions.
12. Choose your preferred recording retention period.
13. If applicable, provide any special requests.
14. Confirm that you understand your withdrawal rights.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Institution** (text, required): Enter your university or organization affiliation.
* **Exchange Session Title** (text, required): Enter the title of the academic peer exchange session.
* **Exchange Date** (date, required): Enter the date when the exchange session will take place.
* **Exchange Time** (text, required): Enter the start and end time of the exchange session.
* **Types of Recording** (select_multiple, required): Select all the types of recording that apply to you.
* **Consent to Video Recording** (select_one, required): Indicate your consent to video recording.
* **Consent to Audio Recording** (select_one, required): Indicate your consent to audio recording.
* **Consent to Distribution** (select_one, required): Indicate how you consent to the distribution of recordings.
* **Consent to Publication** (select_one, required): Indicate how you consent to the publication of recordings.
* **Purpose of Recording** (text, required): Specify the intended purpose of the recording.
* **Access Restriction Preferences** (select_one, required): Select your preference for access restrictions.
* **Recording Retention Period** (select_one, required): Select your preferred recording retention period.
* **Special Requests** (text, optional): Provide any special requests you have regarding the recording.
* **Withdrawal Rights Acknowledged** (select_one, required): Confirm that you understand your withdrawal rights.
