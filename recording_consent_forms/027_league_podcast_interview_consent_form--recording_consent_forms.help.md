# League Podcast Interview Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The League Podcast Interview Consent Form is a document used to collect information about individuals being interviewed on a podcast episode for the League organization. This form is essential to ensure that the participant's details and consent are recorded accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
6. Select the date of the interview from the calendar in the "Date of interview" field.
7. Select the time of the interview from the time picker in the "Time of interview" field.
8. Choose your recording consent preference in the "Recording consent" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your mailing address.
* **Date of interview**: Select the date of the interview from the calendar.
* **Time of interview**: Select the time of the interview from the time picker.
* **Recording consent**: Choose whether you consent to being recorded during the podcast interview. You can choose between "Record interview" or "Do not record interview."
