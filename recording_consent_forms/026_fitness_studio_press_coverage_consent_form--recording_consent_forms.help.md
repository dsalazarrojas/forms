# Fitness Studio Press Coverage Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals, usually photographers or staff members, for media coverage purposes, including obtaining their consent for the use of their likeness in various media.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the below steps:

1. **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
2. **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
3. **Email**: Enter your official email address.
4. **Phone**: Enter your official phone number.
5. **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD format (e.g., 01/01/1990).
6. **Consent Given**: Choose one of the options:
	* Option 1
	* Option 2
	* Option 3
7. **Consent Expiration Date**: If you choose Option 1 or Option 2, enter a date after which your consent will expire. This is optional.
8. **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information related to your consent.
9. **Media Type**: If you are participating in media coverage, choose the type of media that applies to you (e.g., photo, video, interview).
	* Option 1
	* Option 2
10. **Media Outlets**: If you are participating in media coverage, choose the media outlets where your image or likeness will be used (e.g., news article, social media).
	* Option 1
	* Option 2
	* Option 3
11. **Release Date**: If you have chosen to participate in media coverage, enter the date after which your image or likeness will be released.
12. **Photographer**: Enter the name of the photographer who will be capturing your image or likeness.
13. **Studio**: Enter the name of the studio where you will be participating in media coverage.
14. **Location**: Enter the location where the media coverage will take place.
15. **Contact**: Enter the contact information of the person or entity responsible for contacting you.
16. **Consent Status**: Choose the status of your consent (e.g., active, inactive, expired).
	* Option 1
	* Option 2
	* Option 3
17. **Consent Note**: Enter any additional notes or information related to your consent status.
18. **Release Note**: Enter any additional notes or information related to your release status.
19. **Media Outlet**: If you are participating in media coverage, choose the media outlet where your image or likeness will be used (e.g., news article, social media).
	* Option 1
	* Option 2
	* Option 3
20. **Image Capture**: Enter any information about the image capture, such as the location or circumstances.
21. **Photo Release Date**: If you have chosen to participate in media coverage, enter the date after which your photo will be released.
22. **Image Capture Location**: Enter the location where the image was captured.
23. **Audio Capture**: Enter any information about the audio capture, such as the location or circumstances.
24. **Audio Release Date**: If you have chosen to participate in media coverage, enter the date after which your audio will be released.
25. **Other**: Enter any other relevant information not captured in the previous fields.
