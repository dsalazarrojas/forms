# Boudoir Photography Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to gather information and obtain consent from clients for boudoir photography sessions. It covers essential details such as contact information, session specifics, and consent for image usage. Please complete the form carefully and thoroughly to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for both you and the photographer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through each page carefully and fill in the required information. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask the photographer for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (1): Please enter your legal first name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Last Name** (2): Please enter your legal last name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Date of Birth** (3): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Age Confirmation** (4): Confirm that you are at least 18 years old.
- **Address** (5): Provide your complete home address.
- **Phone Number** (6): Enter your primary contact number.
- **Email Address** (7): Enter your email address for session communications.
- **Photographer Name** (8): Enter the full name of the photographer assigned to your session.
- **Studio Name** (9): Enter the name of the photography studio where the session will take place.
- **Session Date** (10): Enter the scheduled date of your session.
- **Session Type** (11): Select the type of boudoir session you are interested in (Classic boudoir, Implied nude, Artistic nude, Couples boudoir, Bridal boudoir, or Other).
- **Comfort Level** (12): Describe your comfort level with the type of photography you have chosen.
- **Image Usage Consent** (13): Indicate how you wish your images to be used (Portfolio use only, Portfolio and social media, Portfolio, social media, and marketing, Private use only, or Private with photographer discretion).
- **Third Party Sharing** (14): Decide whether you permit the sharing of your images with third parties.
- **Retouching Preferences** (15): Specify the level of retouching you prefer (Minimal - natural look, Moderate - smooth skin only, or Extensive - full retouching).
- **Image Delivery Format** (16): Select how you would like to receive your images (Digital download, USB drive, Printed album, or Wall art).
- **Privacy Concerns** (17): If you have specific privacy concerns, please express them here. (Not required but recommended).
- **Support Person** (18): Indicate if you will bring a support person to the session.
- **Wardrobe Changes** (19): Estimate how many outfit changes you plan to make during the session.
- **Special Requests** (20): Make any additional requests or requirements known to the photographer.
- **Cancellation Policy** (21): Confirm that you understand the cancellation policy.
- **Payment Terms** (22): Confirm that you understand the payment terms.
- **Voluntary Participation** (23): Verify that you are participating voluntarily without coercion.
- **Right to Withdraw** (24): Confirm that you understand you can withdraw consent at any time during the session.
- **Client Signature** (25): Sign your name with your full legal name as verification of your identity.
- **Signature Date** (26): Date of signing as proof of your consent.
- **Photographer Signature** (27): The photographer signs as verification of their acceptance of your consent and to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.
