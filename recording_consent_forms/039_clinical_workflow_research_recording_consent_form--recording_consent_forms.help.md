# Clinical Workflow Research Recording Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent for audio or video recordings of clinical workflow for research purposes. It ensures that participants understand the scope and purpose of the recordings and provides a legal and ethical basis for the recordings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Read through each section carefully and answer the questions truthfully.
2.  Provide all required information and ensure you understand the purpose and scope of the recordings.
3.  Sign and date the form to indicate your consent.
4.  Have a witness (if applicable) sign and date the form.
5.  Return the form to the investigator for review and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Study Title** (`study_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the study you are participating in.
- **Study ID** (`study_id`, `text`, required): Enter the unique identifier for the study.
- **Principal Investigator** (`principal_investigator`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the principal investigator.
- **Institution** (`institution`, `text`, required): Enter the institution or facility where you are participating in the study.
- **Participant Full Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name.
- **Your Role** (`participant_role`, `select_one`, required): Choose your role in the study from the list provided.
  - This is used to help classify and organize the recordings for the study.
- **Department or Unit** (`department`, `text`, required): Enter the department or unit you are participating in.
- **I Understand the Purpose of Recording** (`recording_purpose`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes I Understand' to confirm you understand the purpose of the recordings.
  - This ensures you understand that recordings will take place for research purposes.
- **Types of Recording** (`recording_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable types of recordings that will take place.
  - This includes video recording, audio recording, screen capture, and motion tracking.
- **Recording Locations** (`recording_locations`, `text`, required): Enter the locations where recordings will take place.
- **I Understand the Recording Duration** (`recording_duration`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes I Understand' to confirm you understand the recording duration.
  - This ensures you understand how long recordings will take place.
- **Recording Schedule** (`recording_schedule`, `text`, optional): Enter the schedule of the recordings.
- **I Understand What Will Be Recorded** (`what_will_be_recorded`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes I Understand' to confirm you understand what will be recorded.
  - This ensures you understand what will be recorded during the study.
- **I Understand How Patient Interactions Will Be Handled** (`patient_interactions`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes I Understand' to confirm you understand how patient interactions will be handled.
  - This ensures you understand how interactions with patients will be managed during recordings.
- **I Understand How PHI Will Be Protected** (`phi_protection`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes I Understand' to confirm you understand how protected health information will be handled.
  - This ensures you understand how PHI will be protected.
- **I Understand How Recordings Will Be Stored** (`data_storage`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes I Understand' to confirm you understand how recordings will be stored.
  - This ensures you understand how the recordings will be secured.
- **I Understand Who Will Have Access to Recordings** (`data_access`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes I Understand' to confirm you understand who will have access to recordings.
  - This ensures you understand who can access the recordings.
- **I Understand How Long Recordings Will Be Kept** (`data_retention`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes I Understand' to confirm you understand how long recordings will be kept.
  - This ensures you understand the retention period of the recordings.
- **I Understand How Recordings Will Be Destroyed** (`data_destruction`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes I Understand' to confirm you understand how recordings will be destroyed.
  - This ensures you understand the process of destroying the recordings.
- **I Understand How Recordings Will Be Used** (`usage_of_recordings`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes I Understand' to confirm you understand how recordings will be used.
  - This ensures you understand for what purposes recordings will be used.
- **Use of De-identified Clips in Publications** (`publication_consent`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you consent to use de-identified clips in publications.
  - This ensures you understand how your recordings may be used for educational or research purposes.
- **Use of De-identified Clips in Presentations** (`presentation_consent`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you consent to use de-identified clips in presentations.
  - This ensures you understand how your recordings may be used for presentations at conferences or meetings.
- **I Understand Participation Is Voluntary** (`voluntary_participation`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes I Understand' to confirm you understand that participation is voluntary.
  - This ensures you understand that you can withdraw your consent at any time.
- **I Can Request to Pause Recording** (`right_to_pause_recording`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes I Understand' to confirm you understand you can pause recordings at any time.
  - This ensures you understand you have control over the recording process.
- **I Can Withdraw at Any Time** (`right_to_withdraw`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes I Understand' to confirm you understand you can withdraw your consent at any time.
  - This ensures you understand you have the right to change your mind.
- **My Employment Will Not Be Affected** (`employment_not_affected`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes I Understand' to confirm you understand your employment will not be affected by participation.
  - This ensures you understand that your participation will not impact your employment status.
- **I Have Contact Information for Questions** (`contact_information`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes I Have It' to confirm you have a contact number or method where questions can be directed.
  - This ensures that questions can be directed to you if needed.
- **Have All Your Questions Been Answered** (`questions_answered`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes All Questions Answered' to confirm your understanding.
  - This ensures you understand your rights and responsibilities.
- **Please List Remaining Questions** (`remaining_questions`, `text`, optional): If you still have questions, list them here.
- **Have You Received a Copy of This Form** (`copy_of_form`, `select_one`, required): Select 'True' to confirm you have received a copy of this form.
  - This ensures you have a record of your consent.
- **Your Decision** (`participant_decision`, `select_one`, required): Select 'I Agree to Participate' to confirm your decision.
  - This ensures you are aware of your rights and responsibilities.
- **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, `text`, required): Sign your full name.
  - This is your confirmation of consent.
- **Date of Signature** (`signature_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your signature.
  - This ensures you understand the date of your consent.
- **Witness Name** (`witness_name`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the witness.
  - This ensures a witness can verify your consent if needed.
- **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`, `text`, optional): If applicable, sign the witness's full name.
  - This is the witness's confirmation of your consent.
- **Investigator Name** (`investigator_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the investigator.
  - This ensures they can be contacted if needed.
- **Investigator Signature** (`investigator_signature`, `text`, required): Sign the investigator's full name.
  - This is the investigator's confirmation of your consent.
- **Date** (`investigator_signature_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the investigator's signature.
  - This ensures you understand the date of their consent.

## Tips

- Answer truthfully and accurately.
- If you have questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask the investigator.
- Ensure you understand your rights and responsibilities.
- Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.
- Ensure a witness (if applicable) signs and dates the form as well.
- Return the form to the investigator for review and processing.
