# Audio Recording Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain permission from participants to record their audio, including details about the recording project and the scope of permissions granted.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully and ensure you understand each field.
2.  Fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3.  If you are a minor, please have your parent or guardian sign the form.
4.  For each field, enter the required information as prompted.
5.  Once complete, review the form for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name**: \* Your legal name as stated on your identification. This will be used for verification and identification purposes.
    *Example: John Doe*

* **Email Address**: Your contact email address for communication and updates.
    *Example: johndoe@example.com*

* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number for further questions or clarification.
    *Example: 123-456-7890*

* **Mailing Address**: Your full address for correspondence and mail delivery.
    *Example: 123 Main St, Anytown USA 12345*

* **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth for age verification.
    *Example: 01/01/1999*

* **Minor Status**: Check if you are 18 years or older or a minor (under 18 years).
    *Example: No, I am 18 or older*

* **Parent or Guardian Name**: If you are a minor, enter your parent or guardian's name.
    *Example: John Smith*

* **Parent or Guardian Signature**: If you are a minor, type your parent or guardian's signature as electronic signature.
    *Example: John Smith*

* **Organization Name**: The name of the organization requesting permission for the recording project.
    *Example: ABC Productions*

* **Project Title**: The title of the recording project.
    *Example: My Voice*

* **Project Description**: A brief description of the recording project.
    *Example: A 5-minute podcast*

* **Recording Date**: The scheduled date of the recording.
    *Example: 2023-02-01*

* **Recording Location**: The location of the recording.
    *Example: New York City*

* **Content Type**: Select the type of content to be recorded (e.g., spoken word, singing, etc.).
    *Example: Spoken word*

* **Permission Scope**: Select the permissions granted for the recording (e.g., record, edit, distribute, etc.).
    *Example: Record my voice, distribute publicly*

* **Distribution Channels**: Select where the recording will be distributed (e.g., podcast, streaming services, etc.).
    *Example: Podcast platforms, streaming services*

* **Credit Requirement**: Select if you require attribution or credit for your recording.
    *Example: Yes, full credit required*

* **Compensation Agreement**: Select how you will be compensated (e.g., paid session, royalty agreement, etc.).
    *Example: Paid session*

* **Exclusivity Clause**: Select if this recording is exclusive.
    *Example: No, non-exclusive*

* **Termination Clause**: Acknowledge that you understand the terms for terminating this agreement.
    *Example: Yes, I understand*

* **Participant Signature**: Type your full name as electronic signature.
    *Example: John Doe*

* **Signature Date**: The date of permission grant.
    *Example: 2023-02-01*
