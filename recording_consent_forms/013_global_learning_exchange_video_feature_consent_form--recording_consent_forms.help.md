# global_learning_exchange_video_feature_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The global_learning_exchange_video_feature_consent_form is designed to collect user consent for video features and personal information, ensuring that users understand how their data will be used.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Choose whether you want to give consent for the video feature (yes/no).
2.  Select how you want the video feature to be used (Internal/External).
3.  Indicate your consent for recording (yes/no).
4.  Enter your email address.
5.  Enter your phone number.
6.  Provide your date of birth.
7.  Provide the time of capture.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **video Feature Consent** (`video_feature_consent`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose whether you want to give consent for the video feature.
*   **How will the video feature be used** (`video_feature_use`, select_one, required/optional): Select how you want the video feature to be used. This will be used for Internal or External purposes.
*   **recording Consent** (`recording_consent`, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate your consent for recording.
*   **email address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
*   **phone number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
*   **date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Provide your date of birth.
*   **time of capture** (`time_of_capture`, time, optional): Provide the time of capture.
