# Club Highlight Reel Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Club Highlight Reel Consent Form is a form used to capture the consent of participants to be recorded or photographed for a club's highlight reel. It's essential to complete this form accurately to ensure that the participant's information and consent are recorded correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling in your full name and email address as required fields.
2.  Next, provide your age and date of consent as required fields.
3.  Answer the question about parental consent if you are under 18 years old.
4.  If you are under 18, provide your parent or guardian's name and email address.
5.  Select the options for each permission type (video, photo, audio, social media, public use, and duration limit) to indicate your consent.
6.  If you are under 18 and have selected "Yes, I am Under 18", enter any additional terms or restrictions.
7.  Finally, indicate if you are aware of any potential compensation for participating in the highlight reel.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name (`participant_name`):** Your full name as the participant.
* **Email Address (`participant_email`):** Your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Age (`participant_age`):** Your age as the participant.
* **Date of Consent (`consent_date`):** The date you are giving your consent to be recorded.
* **Parental Consent Required (`guardian_required`):** If you are under 18, select "Yes, I am Under 18" to indicate that you need parental consent.
* **Parent or Guardian Name (`guardian_name`):** If you are under 18, enter the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Email (`guardian_email`):** If you are under 18, enter your parent or guardian's email address.
* **Permission to Record Video (`video_permission`):** Select "Yes, I Give Permission" to allow us to record video of you.
* **Permission to Take Photos (`photo_permission`):** Select "Yes, I Give Permission" to allow us to take photos of you.
* **Permission to Record Audio (`audio_permission`):** Select "Yes, I Give Permission" to allow us to record audio of you.
* **Permission to Use on Social Media (`social_media_permission`):** Select "Yes, Full Social Media Permission" to allow us to post about you on social media or select other options as needed.
* **Permission for Public Use (`public_use_permission`):** Select "Any Public Use" to allow us to use your recording publicly or other options as needed.
* **Duration Limit for Use (`duration_limit`):** Select the duration limit for how long your recording can be used.
* **Awareness of Compensation (`compensation_awareness`):** Indicate if you are aware of any potential compensation for participating in the highlight reel.
* **Additional Terms or Restrictions (`additional_terms`):** If you have any additional terms or restrictions, enter them here.

Note: Please fill out the form accurately and honestly. If you have any questions or concerns, contact us.
