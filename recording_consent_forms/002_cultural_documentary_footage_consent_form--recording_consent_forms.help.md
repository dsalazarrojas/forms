# Cultural Documentary Footage Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for obtaining consent from participants in a documentary footage recording project. It helps us to gather necessary information from participants, such as their contact details and filming location.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide general information about yourself, such as your name and title/occupation.
2. Fill out your participant information, including your role in the documentary and any relevant contact information.
3. Specify your contact information, including your email and phone number.
4. Grant us permission to film you, including specifying any filming details.
5. If you are a parent or guardian, grant us permission for the participant to be filmed.
6. Fill out any additional filming details.
7. Submit your completed form to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information
* **General Information** (general_information, text, optional)
  This field is where you can provide general information about yourself, such as your name and title/occupation.

### Participant Information
* **Participant Information** (participant_info, text, optional)
  This field is where you can specify your role in the documentary and any other relevant contact information.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (contact_info, text, optional)
  This field is where you can provide your contact information, including your email and phone number.

### Participant Permission
* **Participant Permission** (participant_permission, text, optional)
  This field is where you can grant us permission to film you, including specifying any filming details.

### Filming Location
* **Filming Location** (filming_location, text, optional)
  This field is where you can specify the filming location.

### Filming Details
* **Filming Details** (filming_details, text, optional)
  This field is where you can specify the details of the filming location.

### Parent/Guardian Permission
* **Parent/Guardian Permission** (participant_permission_2, text, optional)
  This field is where you can grant permission for a parent or guardian to be filmed.

### Submit
* **Submit** (submit, text, required)
  This field is where you submit your completed form. Please make sure to fill out all other fields before submitting.
