# Maintenance Professional Award Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Maintenance Professional Award Submission form is used to collect information from nominees who have been nominated for a maintenance professional award.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields (indicated by a asterisk \*). Optional fields can be left blank if not applicable.
2. Enter your name in the Nominator Name field.
3. Enter your email address in the Nominator Email field.
4. If you wish to include additional information, enter it in the Nomination Details field.
5. If you have supporting documents, select "Yes" in the Supporting Documents field.
6. Indicate whether or not you have reviewed and agree to the consent policy.
7. Select your submission status.
8. Enter your company name in the Nominator Company field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nominator Name** (`nominator_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the nominator.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date of submission.
* **Nominator Email** (`nominator_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Nomination Category** (`nomination_category`, select_one, required): Select the category of the nomination.
* **Nominator Phone** (`nominator_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number as the nominator.
* **Nomination Details** (`nomination_details`, text, required): Enter any additional information about the nomination.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether or not you have supporting documents.
* **Consent** (`consent`, note, required): Review and agree to the consent policy.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the submission.
* **Nominator Company** (`nominator_company`, text, optional): Enter your company name.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
* If you have any supporting documents, make sure to select "Yes" in the Supporting Documents field.
* If you have reviewed and agree to the consent policy, select "Yes" in the Consent field.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
