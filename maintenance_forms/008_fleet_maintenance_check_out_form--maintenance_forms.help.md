# Fleet Maintenance Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and track vehicle maintenance activities, ensuring accurate and up-to-date information about each vehicle's status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the vehicle's information:
	* Vehicle Make: Enter the make of the vehicle (e.g., Toyota, Ford, Honda).
	* Vehicle Model: Enter the model of the vehicle (e.g., Corolla, Mustang, Civic).
	* Vehicle Year: Enter the year the vehicle was made.
2. Select the maintenance type:
	* Choose the type of maintenance performed (e.g., oil change, tire rotation, brakes).
3. Enter service dates:
	* Service Start Date: Enter the date the maintenance started.
	* Service End Date: Enter the date the maintenance was completed.
4. Record odometer and mileage readings:
	* Odometer Reading: Record the current odometer reading.
	* Mileage Reading: Record the current mileage.
5. Check the fuel level:
	* Select the current fuel level of the vehicle (Full, Quarter, Half, or Empty).
6. Assign a technician:
	* Select the technician who performed the maintenance.
7. Update service status:
	* Select the status of the maintenance (Complete, Incomplete, Not Started, or Awaiting Approval).
8. Add any additional notes:
	* Provide any additional comments or notes about the maintenance.
9. Record costs:
	* Service Cost: Enter the total cost of the service.
	* Labor Cost: Enter the labor cost associated with the maintenance.
	* Total Cost: Enter the total cost of the maintenance.
10. Enter service start and end locations:
	* Service Start Location: Enter the location where the maintenance started.
	* Service End Location: Enter the location where the maintenance was completed.
11. Enter service start and end times:
	* Service Start Time: Enter the time when maintenance started.
	* Service End Time: Enter the time when maintenance was completed.
12. Enter vehicle registration and other details:
	* Vehicle Registration: Enter the vehicle registration number.
	* Vehicle Year Made: Enter the year the vehicle was made.
	* Vehicle VIN: Enter the vehicle's VIN number.
