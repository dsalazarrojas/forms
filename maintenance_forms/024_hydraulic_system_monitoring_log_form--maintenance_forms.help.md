# Hydraulic System Monitoring Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hydraulic System Monitoring Log Form is used to collect and record important information about hydraulic systems, including equipment status, maintenance activities, and relevant data points. This form helps maintenance and monitoring teams track and report on the status of hydraulic systems, ensuring timely and accurate maintenance activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all necessary information and resources before proceeding.
* Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly for each hydraulic system.
* Review and submit the completed form for review and action.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equipment ID** (1): Enter the unique identification number of the hydraulic system.
* **Equipment Name** (2): Provide the name of the hydraulic system, e.g., pump, valve, etc.
* **Location** (3): Specify where the hydraulic system is located, e.g., workshop, office, etc.
* **Last Checked** (4): Record the date of the last scheduled maintenance or check.
* **Comments** (5): Enter any additional comments or notes about the hydraulic system.
* **Next Due Date** (6): Record the date when the next maintenance or check is due.
* **Notes** (7): Enter any additional notes or comments about the hydraulic system.
* **Checked By** (8): Record the name of the person who last checked or performed maintenance on the hydraulic system.
* **Goals** (9): Enter any specific goals or objectives for maintenance or monitoring activities.
* **Contact Person** (10): Record the name and contact information of the person responsible for the hydraulic system.
* **Contact Number** (11): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Email** (12): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Submitted By** (13): Record the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted Date** (14): Record the date the form was submitted.
* **Assigned To** (15): Record the name of the person or team assigned to perform maintenance or monitoring activities.
* **Priority** (16): Indicate if the hydraulic system requires urgent attention or maintenance.
* **Equipment Status** (17): Record the status of the hydraulic system, e.g., active or inactive.
* **Hydraulic Fluid Type** (18): Indicate the type of hydraulic fluid used in the system.
* **Hydraulic Hose Type** (19): Record the type of hydraulic hose used in the system.
* **Hydraulic Tank Capacity** (20): Indicate if the hydraulic system uses a tank for fluid storage.

Tips: Always ensure you have permission to access and update the form, especially if you are not the primary contact person. Verify the accuracy of the information entered before submitting the form.
