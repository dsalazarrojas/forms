# Machine Maintenance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Machine Maintenance Checklist form is a systematic tool to record and track regular maintenance activities for machinery and equipment. It helps in ensuring that all necessary maintenance steps are completed and documented accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant maintenance actions that were taken during the maintenance activity.
2. Provide a brief description of the equipment, including its model, type, and current condition.
3. Choose the outcomes achieved from the maintenance activity.
4. Enter the maintenance last update date and time.
5. Update the maintenance status and record any comments from technicians involved.
6. Enter any additional comments or notes about the maintenance activity.
7. Set the maintenance due date and start date if applicable.
8. Update the maintenance status and record any comments from assigned users.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Equipment Inspection** (`equipment_inspection`, `text`, required: false): A brief description of the equipment being inspected, including its current condition.
* **Maintenance Actions** (`maintenance_actions`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all maintenance actions that were taken during the activity. For example, "Action 1", "Action 2", etc.
* **Replacements Made** (`replacements_made`, `text`, required: false): List any replacement parts or supplies used during the maintenance activity.
* **Outcomes** (`outcomes`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the outcome(s) achieved from the maintenance activity. For example, "Outcomes 1", "Outcomes 2", etc.
* **Site Location** (`site_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location of the equipment or site where the maintenance was performed.
* **Technicians Involved** (`technicians_involved`, `text`, required: false): List the names or IDs of technicians involved in the maintenance activity.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: false): Additional comments about the maintenance activity.
* **Equipment Model** (`equipment_model`, `text`, required: false): Enter the model or make of the equipment being maintained.
* **Maintenance Frequency** (`maintenance_frequency`, `time`, required: false): Enter the frequency of maintenance, e.g., monthly, weekly, etc.
* **Maintenance Last Update** (`maintenance_last_update`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date and time of the last maintenance update.
* **Maintenance Status** (`maintenance_status`, `text`, required: false): Update the maintenance status, e.g., "Completed", "In Progress", etc.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, `text`, required: false): Enter the type of equipment being maintained.
* **Equipment Condition** (`equipment_condition`, `text`, required: false): Describe the current condition of the equipment.
* **Maintenance Priority** (`maintenance_priority`, `number`, required: false): Enter the maintenance priority level, e.g., 1, 2, etc.
* **Equipment Description** (`equipment_description`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the equipment being maintained.
* **Maintenance Outcomes** (`maintenance_outcomes`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional outcomes or results from the maintenance activity.
* **Technician Comments** (`technician_comments`, `text`, required: false): Enter any comments from the technician involved.
* **Maintenance Notes** (`maintenance_notes`, `note`, required: false): Additional notes about the maintenance activity.
* **Maintenance Due Date** (`maintenance_due_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the scheduled maintenance due date.
* **Maintenance Start Date** (`maintenance_start_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the start date of the maintenance activity.
* **Maintenance End Date** (`maintenance_end_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the end date of the maintenance activity.
* **Maintenance Status Update** (`maintenance_status_update`, `text`, required: false): Update the maintenance status.
* **Maintenance Status Update Date** (`maintenance_status_update_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the maintenance status update.
* **Maintenance Status Update Time** (`maintenance_status_update_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the maintenance status update.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name or ID of the assigned user.
