# Truck Hour Meter Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to log the hours a truck has been driven, including various details about the driver and the vehicle.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant information for the truck you are logging.
2. Fill in the driver's name and confirm whether the vehicle is operational or not.
3. Record the total engine hours and select whether the truck took routes or not.
4. Provide the vehicle ID and confirm whether the truck is active or not.
5. Enter the start and end dates and times for the logged period.
6. Calculate or provide the total driving hours and enter it in the corresponding field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Truck Hour Meter Log** (Page 1, Truck Hour Meter Log): This is the title of the form.
* **Driver's Name** (driver_name): Enter the name of the truck driver who logged in the hours.
* **Engine Hours** (engine_hours): Enter the total number of hours the engine has been running.
* **Routes** (routes): Select the routes that the truck took during the logged period. Select 'Yes' if the truck took routes and 'No' if not.
* **Vehicle ID** (vehicle_id): Select whether the truck is operational ('Yes') or not ('No').
* **Start Date** (start_date): Enter the date when the truck started running.
* **Start Time** (start_time): Enter the time when the truck started running.
* **End Date** (end_date): Enter the date when the truck stopped running.
* **End Time** (end_time): Enter the time when the truck stopped running.
* **Total Driving Hours** (total_driving_hours): Calculate or enter the total number of hours the truck ran during the logged period.
