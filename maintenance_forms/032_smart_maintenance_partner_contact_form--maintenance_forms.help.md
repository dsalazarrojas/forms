# Smart Maintenance Partner Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to enable you to contact your maintenance partner for assistance or collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your details and description of the issue.
2. Select the relevant category for the issue you are experiencing.
3. Choose the partner type that best suits your needs.
4. Ensure you submit the form once you have completed all the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Data** (`form_data`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of your maintenance partner request.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Please enter your name or title for easy reference.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number for faster communication.
* **Description** (`description`, note, optional): Add any additional details or comments about your request.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Select the category that best describes your issue (Maintenance, Support, Technical).
* **Partner Type** (`partner_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of partner you are contacting (Maintenance, Support, Technical).
* **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, text, optional): If you are inquiring about a specific partner, please provide their name for reference.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and clear information to ensure efficient resolution of your issue.
* Double-check your contact details (email and phone) for correct formatting.
