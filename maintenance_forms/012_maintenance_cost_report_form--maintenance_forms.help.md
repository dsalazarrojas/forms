# maintenance_cost_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report maintenance costs for various activities such as labor, materials, and other expenses. It captures essential information like cost amount, frequency, and submission details for accurate record-keeping and accounting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields: `cost_type` (select labor, material, or other), `cost_amount`, and `submission_date` (date the cost was submitted).
2. Optionally, select the frequency of the maintenance activity (weekly, biweekly, or monthly) using `cost_frequency`.
3. Add any additional comments or notes about the maintenance activity in the `notes` field.
4. Ensure that the `email` and `phone` fields are filled with your contact information.
5. Finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Maintenance Cost Form** (`maintenance_cost_form`, text, not required): This field is not actually a field on the form, but it's the name of the first page.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, not required): Enter the date the cost was submitted.
* **Cost Type** (`cost_type`, select_one, not required): Select the type of cost (labor, material, or other).
* **Cost Amount** (`cost_amount`, number, not required): Enter the total cost amount.
* **Frequency** (`cost_frequency`, select_multiple, not required): Select the frequency of the maintenance activity (weekly, biweekly, or monthly).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, not required): Provide any additional comments or notes about the maintenance activity.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, not required): Enter the date the form is being submitted.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, text, not required): Enter the person assigned to the maintenance activity.
* **Assigned ID** (`assigned_id`, text, not required): Enter the assigned ID.
* **Assigned EK** (`assigned_ek`, text, not required): Enter the assigned EK.
* **Assigned User** (again) (`assigned_u`, text, not required): Enter the user again.
* **Assigned Jimmy** (`assigned_jimmy`, text, not required): Enter the assigned Jimmy.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, not required): Enter the person submitting the form.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, not required): Enter the form ID.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, text, not required): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Submitted EK** (`submitted_ek`, text, not required): Enter the submitted EK.
* **Submitted User** (again) (`submitted_u`, text, not required): Enter the user who submitted the form.
* **Submitted T** (`submitted_t`, text, not required): Enter the submitted T.
* **Submitted I** (`submitted_i`, text, not required): Enter the submitted I.
* **Submitted Form ID** (`submitted_form_id`, text, not required): Enter the submitted form ID.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, text, not required): Enter the submission date.
* **Submitted Jimmy ID** (`submitted_jimmy_id`, text, not required): Enter the submitted Jimmy ID.
