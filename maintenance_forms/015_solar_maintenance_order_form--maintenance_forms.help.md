# Solar Maintenance Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request or schedule maintenance service for a solar system. To complete this form, you will need to provide information about the solar system, service requirements, and scheduling details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Solar System Description**: Briefly describe the solar system you are submitting for maintenance.
2. **Service Type**: Indicate if the service is required (Yes) or not (No).
3. **Service Date**: Enter the date of the requested service.
4. **Service Time**: Enter the time of the requested service.
5. **Service Status**: Choose the current status of the service (Available, Not Available, or Unknown).
6. **Service Status Hint**: Provide any additional information about the service status.
7. **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the person or company requesting the service.
8. **Customer Email**: Enter the email address of the person or company requesting the service.
9. **Customer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person or company requesting the service.
10. **Address**: Enter the address of the location where the service is required.
11. **Service Address**: Enter the address where the service will be performed.
12. **Service Notes**: Provide any additional information about the service.
13. **Maintenance Request**: Enter the maintenance request details.
14. **Maintenance Description**: Describe the maintenance details.
15. **Schedule Appointment**: Indicate if you want to schedule an appointment for the service (Yes or No).
16. **Appointment Date**: Enter the date of the scheduled appointment.
17. **Appointment Time**: Enter the time of the scheduled appointment.
18. **Appointment Status**: Choose the status of the appointment (Confirmed, Tentative, or Not Confirmed).
19. **Appointment Status Hint**: Provide any additional information about the appointment status.
