# Power Supply Restoration Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for requesting power supply restoration when there is an outage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked as such.
2. Ensure that you are filling out the form for a genuine power supply outage.
3. Complete all sections accurately and thoroughly.
