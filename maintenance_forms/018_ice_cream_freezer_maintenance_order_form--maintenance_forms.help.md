# Ice Cream Freezer Maintenance Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help the maintenance team schedule appointments and maintenance for the company's ice cream freezers. Please use this form to communicate your maintenance requests to the team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your company type from the options provided.
2. Enter your name and contact information.
3. Choose the type of appointment you need (Scheduled or Urgent).
4. Select the type of maintenance required (Repair or Maintenance).
5. Choose the status of the maintenance task (Pending, Active, or Closed).
6. Provide any additional notes about the maintenance request.
7. Schedule the date for the maintenance appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Type** (`company_type`, Select Multiple, **required**):
  Indicates if the company needs regular or one-time maintenance.
* **Name** (`name`, Text, **required**):
  Enter your full name, or the name of your organization, if applicable.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, Text, **optional**):
  Provide the name of the person the maintenance team can contact for further questions.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Number, **required**):
  Enter your phone number so the maintenance team can contact you to discuss the maintenance request.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, **required**):
  Enter your email address so the maintenance team can contact you about the maintenance request.
* **Appointment Type** (`appointment_type`, Select One, **required**):
  Choose if the maintenance appointment is for a scheduled or urgent maintenance task.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, Select Multiple, **required**):
  Select the type of maintenance required, either repair or maintenance.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, Select One, **required**):
  Choose the status of the maintenance task (Pending, Active, or Closed).
* **Notes** (`notes`, Note, **optional**):
  Provide any additional comments or information about the maintenance request.
* **Scheduled Date** (`scheduled_date`, Date, **required**):
  Enter the date on which you would like the maintenance task to be completed.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you select the correct company type and job type to ensure that the maintenance team knows how to prioritize and schedule the task.
* If you need to contact the maintenance team, please provide a valid phone number and email address.
* Ensure that you select a scheduled date that is realistic and can be completed within the maintenance team's capacity.
