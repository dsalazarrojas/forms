# Toolbox Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document the status of a toolbox, including its assigned tool ID, the team members who have access to it, the tool's start and end dates, and the condition and location of the tool.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the `toolbox_id` number to identify the specific toolbox.
2. Indicate whether team members have access to the toolbox with the `team_members` field.
3. Confirm whether the team leader has access to the toolbox with the `team_leader` field.
4. Enter the start date and start time of the toolbox's availability with the `start_date` and `start_time` fields.
5. Enter the end date and end time of the toolbox's availability with the `end_date` and `end_time` fields.
6. Confirm the team leader's signature with the `team_lead_signature` field.
7. Indicate whether team members have signed the form with the `team_member_signature` field.
8. Enter any additional comments with the `notes` field.
9. Enter the contact email of the person completing the form with the `email` field.
10. Enter the contact phone number of the person completing the form with the `phone` field.
11. Confirm whether the tool is assigned to someone with the `assigned_to` field.
12. Confirm whether the tool was assigned by someone with the `assigned_by` field.
13. Choose the tool's status with the `status` field.
14. Confirm whether the tool is assigned to a specific tool with the `assigned_tool` field.
15. Enter the tool ID number with the `assigned_tool_id` field.
16. Indicate any comments on the tool with the `comments` field.
17. Enter the tool name with the `tool_name` field.
18. Enter the tool type with the `tool_type` field.
19. Enter the tool's location with the `location` field.
20. Enter the tool's condition with the `tool_condition` field.
21. Enter the asset number with the `asset_number` field.
22. Enter the asset tag with the `asset_tag` field.
23. Enter the asset serial number with the `asset_serial_number` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **toolbox_id**: Enter the number of the toolbox you are documenting.
* **team_members**: Check 'Yes' if team members have access to the toolbox.
* **team_leader**: Check 'Yes' if the team leader has access to the toolbox.
* **start_date**: Enter the date when the toolbox becomes available.
* **start_time**: Enter the time when the toolbox becomes available.
* **end_date**: Enter the date when the toolbox is no longer available.
* **end_time**: Enter the time when the toolbox is no longer available.
* **team_lead_signature**: Check 'Yes' if the team leader signs the form.
* **team_member_signature**: Check 'Yes' if team members sign the form.
* **notes**: Enter any additional comments about the toolbox.
* **email**: Enter the email of the person completing the form.
* **phone**: Enter the phone number of the person completing the form.
* **assigned_to**: Check 'Yes' if the tool is assigned to someone.
* **assigned_by**: Check 'Yes' if the tool was assigned by someone.
* **status**: Choose the status of the tool (Active or Inactive).
* **assigned_tool**: Check 'Yes' if the tool is assigned to a specific tool.
* **assigned_tool_id**: Enter the ID number of the assigned tool.
* **comments**: Enter any comments about the tool.
* **tool_name**: Enter the name of the tool.
* **tool_type**: Enter the type of the tool.
* **location**: Enter the location of the tool.
* **tool_condition**: Enter the condition of the tool.
* **asset_number**: Enter the asset number of the tool.
* **asset_tag**: Enter the asset tag of the tool.
* **asset_serial_number**: Enter the asset serial number of the tool.
