# Estate Maintenance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Estate Maintenance Request Form is designed to collect information from property owners or residents to request maintenance services or report issues with their estate. This form is intended to be filled out by the property owner or a designated representative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your information in the "Request Form" section, if required.
2. Provide your contact details in the "Contact Information" section.
3. In the "Maintenance Requests" section, select all the specific issues or services you need help with by checking the corresponding boxes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Form** (`text`, optional, `estate_maintenance_request_form`): If required, enter a brief description of the issue or maintenance request you are submitting.
* **Contact Information** (`text`, optional, `contact_details`): Please provide your name, phone number, and email to help us contact you regarding your maintenance request.
	+ Note: If you are submitting on behalf of someone else, please include their name and relationship to you.
* **Maintenance Requests** (`select_multiple`, required, `maintenance_requests`):
	+ Select all the issues or services you need help with, such as:
		- 123 Main St
		- 456 Elm St
		- 789 Oak St

## Tips
- Please be as specific as possible when requesting maintenance services.
- If you need help with multiple issues, select all relevant options in the "Maintenance Requests" section.
- If you have any supporting documents or photos, you can attach them to the form or email them to us separately.
