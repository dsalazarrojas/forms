# equipment_maintenance_partner_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The equipment_maintenance_partner_contact_form is used to capture contact information and service details of a partner who provides equipment maintenance services to the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the contact details of your partner in the `Contact Details` section.
2. Select the service coverage hours that your partner provides (e.g., AM/PM, 24/7, Weekends).
3. Enter the service hours of your partner (a date).
4. Enter the service availability of your partner (a time).
5. Select the coverage areas of your partner (e.g., Building 1, Building 2, Building 3).
6. Enter your partner's name and service name (e.g., John Doe - Maintenance Service).
7. Enter your partner's email address.
8. Enter your partner's phone number.
9. Optionally, review and edit your submissions as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details**: Enter your name and contact information.
* **Service Coverage**: Select the hours during which your service is available (e.g., AM/PM, 24/7, Weekends).
* **Service Hours**: Enter a date on which your service is available.
* **Service Availability**: Enter a time on which your service is available.
* **Coverage Areas**: Select the areas you provide service to (e.g., Building 1, Building 2, Building 3).
* **Vendor Name**: Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Service Name**: Enter a descriptive name for your service (e.g., Maintenance Service).
* **Vendor Service Email**: Enter your email address associated with your service.
* **Vendor Service Phone**: Enter your phone number associated with your service.

## Tips
* Make sure to review and edit your submissions as needed before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need help with completing this form, contact your supervisor or support team.
