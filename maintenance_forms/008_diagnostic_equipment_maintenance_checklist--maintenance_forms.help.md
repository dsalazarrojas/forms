# Diagnostic Equipment Maintenance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help maintain diagnostic equipment in proper working condition by tracking and documenting the maintenance activities performed on it. The goal is to prevent breakdowns, reduce maintenance costs, and ensure that the equipment is functioning correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form for each diagnostic equipment item as required or necessary.
2. Review and update the form regularly to reflect the current status of the equipment.
3. Use the form to document and track any maintenance activities, including dates and notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Equipment Serial Number (`equipment_serial_number`, Text, Optional)**: Enter the unique serial number of the equipment, if available.
* **Maintenance Date (`maintenance_date`, Date, Optional)**: Record the date of the last maintenance activity or inspection.
* **Maintenance Frequency (`maintenance_frequency`, Select One, Optional)**: Select the frequency of the maintenance, if any:
	+ Daily: Daily checks or routine maintenance.
	+ Weekly: Weekly checks or routine maintenance.
	+ Monthly: Monthly checks or routine maintenance.
* **Maintenance Status (`maintenance_status`, Select Multiple, Optional)**: Select the status of the maintenance, if any:
	+ Complete: Maintenance completed.
	+ Pending: Maintenance pending or scheduled.
	+ Incomplete: Maintenance incomplete or pending further actions.
* **Maintenance Type (`maintenance_type`, Select One, Optional)**: Select the type of maintenance, if any:
	+ Scheduled: Scheduled maintenance.
	+ Unscheduled: Unscheduled or emergency maintenance.
	+ Emergency: Emergency maintenance.
* **Equipment Condition (`equipment_condition`, Text, Optional)**: Enter a description of the current condition of the equipment.
* **Notes (`notes`, Note, Optional)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the maintenance activity.

## Tips
* This form is a general purpose form, and its fields can be adjusted or customized to fit specific needs.
* Always ensure that the form is reviewed and updated regularly to reflect the current status of the equipment.
* Use the form in conjunction with other maintenance activities and records to ensure accurate and consistent tracking.
