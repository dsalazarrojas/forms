# Chilled Water System Inspection Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to conduct a comprehensive inspection of the chilled water system, ensuring it is functioning properly and identifying any potential issues that require attention.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: **Facility Name**, **Inspection Date**, **Inspector Name**, **Chiller Model Number**, **Refrigerant Type**, and **Current Operating Hours**. These fields are essential for recording the basic information about the inspection.
2. Indicate the type of inspection being performed (e.g., routine maintenance, annual inspection, pre-season check, post-repair verification, or emergency assessment). This will help record the purpose of the inspection and aid in tracking future maintenance.
3. Record your findings for the visual inspection, noting any visible damage or leaks, the overall physical condition of the unit, and the presence of any visible damage or leaks.
4. Perform the operational checks, ensuring the unit starts and operates normally, has proper temperature control, and has pressure readings within normal range. Note any issues or concerns.
5. Verify the refrigerant level is appropriate for the system.
6. Review and update the maintenance records, including the date of last service. If necessary, make recommendations for future maintenance or repairs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the facility being inspected.
* **Inspection Date**: Record the date of the inspection in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Inspector Name**: Enter your name and title or designation as the inspector.
* **License or Certification Number**: Enter your professional license or certification number, if applicable.
* **Type of Inspection**: Select the type of inspection being performed (e.g., routine maintenance, annual inspection, pre-season check, post-repair verification, or emergency assessment).
* **Chiller Model Number**: Enter the model number of the chiller unit being inspected.
* **Chiller Serial Number**: Enter the serial number of the chiller unit.
* **Refrigerant Type**: Record the type of refrigerant used in the system.
* **Installation Date**: Record the date the unit was installed.
* **Current Operating Hours**: Record the total hours the unit has been in operation.
* **Overall Physical Condition**: Assess the overall condition of the chiller unit, selecting one of the following options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Visible Damage or Leaks**: Indicate whether there is any visible damage or leaks, selecting Yes or No.
* **Description of Damage (if present)**: If visible damage or leaks are present, describe the condition in detail.
* **Pipe Insulation Condition**: Assess the condition of the pipes, selecting one of the following options: Intact, Damaged, Missing, or Not Applicable.
* **Starts and Operates Normally**: Indicate whether the unit starts and operates normally, selecting Yes, No, or Not tested.
* **Temperature Control Functioning Properly**: Indicate whether temperature control is functioning properly, selecting Yes, No, or Minor Adjustment Needed.
* **Pressure Readings Within Normal Range**: Indicate whether pressure readings are within the normal range, selecting Yes, No, or Not checked.
* **Refrigerant Level Appropriate**: Indicate whether the refrigerant level is appropriate, selecting Correct, Low, High, or Not checked.
* **Last Service Date**: Record the date of the last service or maintenance.
* **Recommendations and Notes**: Make any necessary recommendations or notes for future maintenance or repairs.
