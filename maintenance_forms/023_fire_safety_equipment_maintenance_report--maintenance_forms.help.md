# Fire Safety Equipment Maintenance Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Safety Equipment Maintenance Report is used to document the maintenance and status of fire safety equipment within a facility. This report helps ensure that all necessary maintenance is performed on a regular basis to ensure the safety of occupants and visitors.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Determine if the site has been visited for maintenance, and select "True" or "False" accordingly.
2. Choose the type of maintenance performed (Inspection or Maintenance).
3. Enter the name of the fire safety equipment being maintained.
4. Indicate the current status of the equipment (Working, Offline, or Broken).
5. Select the date when the maintenance was performed.
6. Choose the maintenance time (if relevant).
7. Enter the next maintenance date.
8. Select the frequency of future maintenance (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
9. Select who will perform the next maintenance (Facility Team or External Contractor).
10. Enter the number of fire safety equipment items being maintained.
11. Enter the date of the last maintenance performed.
12. Select the frequency of next maintenance (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
13. Select who will perform the next maintenance (Facility Team or External Contractor).
14. Enter the total maintenance hours spent.
15. Enter the cost of the maintenance.
16. Enter a description of the current status of the equipment.
17. Enter any notes about the maintenance performed.
18. Enter any notes about the next maintenance.
19. Enter the model of the fire safety equipment being maintained.
20. Enter the make of the fire safety equipment being maintained.
21. Enter the serial number of the fire safety equipment being maintained.
22. Enter the year the fire safety equipment was purchased.
23. Enter the year the fire safety equipment was last maintained.
24. Enter any additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Site Visited** (`site_visited`, boolean, not required): Indicates whether the site has been visited for maintenance.
* **Maintenance Type** (`maintenance_type`, select_one, not required): Type of maintenance performed (Inspection or Maintenance).
* **Equipment Name** (`equipment_name`, text, not required): Name of the fire safety equipment being maintained.
* **Equipment Status** (`equipment_status`, select_one, not required): Current status of the equipment (Working, Offline, or Broken).
* **Maintenance Date** (`maintenance_date`, date, not required): Date when the maintenance was performed.
* **Maintenance Time** (`maintenance_time`, time, not required): Time when the maintenance was performed (if relevant).
* **Next Maintenance Date** (`next_maintenance_date`, date, not required): Date of the next maintenance.
* **Maintenance Frequency** (`maintenance_frequency`, select_one, not required): Frequency of future maintenance (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Maintenance By** (`maintenance_by`, select_one, not required): Who will perform the next maintenance (Facility Team or External Contractor).
* **Number of Fire Safety Equipment** (`safety_equipment_count`, number, not required): Number of fire safety equipment items being maintained.
* **Last Maintenance Date** (`last_maintenance_date`, date, not required): Date of the last maintenance performed.
* **Next Maintenance Frequency** (`next_maintenance_frequency`, select_one, not required): Frequency of next maintenance (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Next Maintenance By** (`next_maintenance_by`, select_one, not required): Who will perform the next maintenance (Facility Team or External Contractor).
* **Maintenance Hours** (`maintenance_hours`, number, not required): Total maintenance hours spent.
* **Maintenance Cost** (`maintenance_cost`, number, not required): Cost of the maintenance.
* **Equipment Status Description** (`equipment_status_description`, text, not required): Description of the current status of the equipment.
* **Maintenance Notes** (`maintenance_notes`, text, not required): Notes about the maintenance performed.
* **Next Maintenance Notes** (`next_maintenance_notes`, text, not required): Notes about the next maintenance.
* **Safety Equipment Model** (`safety_equipment_model`, text, not required): Model of the fire safety equipment being maintained.
* **Safety Equipment Make** (`safety_equipment_make`, text, not required): Make of the fire safety equipment being maintained.
* **Safety Equipment Serial Number** (`safety_equipment_serial_number`, text, not required): Serial number of the fire safety equipment being maintained.
* **Safety Equipment Year of Purchase** (`safety_equipment_year_of_purchase`, number, not required): Year the fire safety equipment was purchased.
* **Safety Equipment Year of Last Maintenance** (`safety_equipment_year_of_last_maintenance`, number, not required): Year the fire safety equipment was last maintained.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, not required): Any additional notes.
