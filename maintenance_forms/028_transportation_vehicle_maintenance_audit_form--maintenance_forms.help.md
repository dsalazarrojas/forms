# Transportation Vehicle Maintenance Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Transportation Vehicle Maintenance Audit Form is a tool used to gather information on the maintenance status and history of transportation vehicles. It is used by maintenance staff and fleet operators to record essential details on individual vehicles.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the vehicle's identification information, including its inspection date.
2. Record the vehicle's current mileage reading.
3. Indicate the vehicle's maintenance status (Active or Inactive).
4. Assess the vehicle's condition (Excellent, Fair, or Poor).
5. Describe any issues found with the vehicle.
6. Schedule the next maintenance or inspection date.
7. Record any maintenance costs associated with the vehicle.
8. Specify the maintenance location (Central, Northern, or Southern).
9. Add any relevant notes about the vehicle's maintenance history.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Information**: Enter the vehicle's identification details, such as its make, model, and registration number.
* **Inspection Date**: Select the date of the latest inspection or maintenance.
* **Mileage Reading**: Record the current mileage reading on the vehicle's odometer.
* **Maintenance Status**: Select the vehicle's current maintenance status (Active or Inactive).
* **Vehicle Condition**: Assess the vehicle's overall condition (Excellent, Fair, or Poor).
* **Issues Found**: Describe any issues or problems found with the vehicle.
* **Maintenance Schedule**: Enter the next scheduled inspection or maintenance date.
* **Next Inspection Date**: Select the date of the next planned inspection or maintenance.
* **Maintenance Cost**: Record any costs associated with the vehicle's maintenance.
* **Maintenance Location**: Specify the location where the vehicle's maintenance is performed (Central, Northern, or Southern).
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the vehicle's maintenance history.
