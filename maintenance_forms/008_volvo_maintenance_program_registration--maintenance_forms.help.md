# Volvo Maintenance Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers register their vehicle for maintenance programs such as Volvo Maintenance, Volvo Priority Service, or Volvo Express Service. By completing this form, customers can select the relevant maintenance program and schedule a service visit at a preferred service center.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the year of your vehicle from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the make of your vehicle.
3. Choose the maintenance program type that suits your needs (Volvo Maintenance, Volvo Priority Service, or Volvo Express Service).
4. Select the service center where you'd like to schedule your service visit.
5. If desired, add any additional information about your vehicle or service requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, required): Enter the year of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, required): Type the make of your vehicle (e.g., Volvo, Toyota, etc.).
* **Program Type** (`program_type`, select_one, required): Choose the maintenance program that suits your needs:
	+ Volvo Maintenance: Regular maintenance and inspections.
	+ Volvo Priority Service: Priority service and scheduling.
	+ Volvo Express Service: Fast-track service and repair.
* **Service Center** (`service_center`, select_one, required): Select the service center where you'd like to schedule your service visit:
	+ Service Center 1
	+ Service Center 2
	+ Service Center 3
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you'd like to start your maintenance schedule.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or requirements for your service visit.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter the correct vehicle year and make to ensure accurate maintenance scheduling.
* Selecting the correct maintenance program type will affect the type of service and inspections performed on your vehicle.
* Be sure to schedule your service visit at a service center that is convenient for you.
* If you have any special requests or comments, be sure to add them in the additional info field.
