# Maintenance Material Purchase Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request and track maintenance materials. It helps maintenance staff to efficiently manage their tasks by providing a structured way to submit and manage requests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Request Details" field and enter a brief description of the maintenance material you need.
2. Click on the "Request Date" field and select the date you submitted the request.
3. In the "Staff Name" field, enter the name of the staff member responsible for the maintenance task.
4. In the "Equipment Type" field, enter the type of equipment related to the maintenance material request.
5. In the "Purchase Request" field, enter any additional information about the purchase request.
6. Select the "Priority" of the request by choosing between "Yes" and "No" from the dropdown menu. This will help staff determine the urgency of the request.
7. Select the "Status" of the request by choosing between "In Progress" and "Done" from the dropdown menu. This will help track the progress of the request.
8. In the "Comments" field, enter any additional comments or notes about the request.
9. If applicable, select "Yes" in the "Assigned To" field to indicate that the request is assigned to a specific staff member.
10. Enter the name of the tool or equipment related to the maintenance task in the "Assigned Tool" field.
11. Enter the date the form was submitted in the "Form Submitted" field.
12. If applicable, enter the date the form was completed and approved in the "Form Completed" and "Form Approved" fields.
13. If the form was rejected or deleted, enter the date in the "Form Rejected" and "Form Deleted" fields.
14. Enter the version of the form in the "Form Version" field.
15. Select the current status of the form by choosing between "Active" and "Inactive" from the dropdown menu in the "Form Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Details (Text)**: Enter a brief description of the maintenance material you need.
* **Request Date (Date)**: Select the date you submitted the request.
* **Staff Name (Text)**: Enter the name of the staff member responsible for the maintenance task.
* **Equipment Type (Text)**: Enter the type of equipment related to the maintenance material request.
* **Purchase Request (Text)**: Enter any additional information about the purchase request.
* **Priority (Select)**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate the priority of the request.
* **Status (Select)**: Select "In Progress" or "Done" to track the progress of the request.
* **Comments (Note)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the request.
* **Assigned To (Select)**: If the request is assigned to a specific staff member, select "Yes".
* **Assigned Tool (Text)**: Enter the name of the tool or equipment related to the maintenance task.
* **Form Submitted (Date)**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Form Completed (Date)**: Enter the date the form was completed.
* **Form Approved (Date)**: Enter the date the form was approved.
* **Form Rejected (Date)**: Enter the date the form was rejected.
* **Form Deleted (Date)**: Enter the date the form was deleted.
* **Form Version (Text)**: Enter the version of the form.
* **Form Status (Select)**: Select "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the current status of the form.
