# Chiller Maintenance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chiller Maintenance Checklist is a form used to document the maintenance of chillers, ensuring consistent and thorough maintenance, and addressing any issues or concerns promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and title as the technician performing the maintenance.
2. Enter the date the maintenance was performed.
3. If you are reporting on a specific chiller model, enter the model number. (Optional but recommended for tracking purposes.)
4. Assess the coolant and refrigerant levels and select the appropriate status from the options provided.
5. Indicate if the filters were cleaned during the maintenance.
6. Describe the condition of the belts and pumps, and note any wear or replacements needed.
7. Confirm if electrical checks were completed.
8. Enter any notes or recommendations for future maintenance or issues.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Technician Name** (`technician_name`, text, required): Enter your name and title as the technician performing the maintenance.
* **Maintenance Date** (`maintenance_date`, date, required): Enter the date the maintenance was performed.
* **Chiller Model** (`chiller_model`, text, optional): If you are reporting on a specific chiller model, enter the model number for tracking purposes.
* **Coolant Level** (`coolant_level`, select_one, required): Assess the coolant and refrigerant levels and select the appropriate status from the options provided:
	+ Normal
	+ Low
	+ Refill required
* **Filters Cleaned** (`filters_cleaned`, select_one, required): Indicate if the filters were cleaned during the maintenance:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Belts and Pumps Condition** (`belts_and_pumps`, text, optional): Describe the condition of the belts and pumps, and note any wear or replacements needed.
* **Electrical Checks Completed** (`electrical_checks`, select_one, required): Confirm if electrical checks were completed:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Notes and Recommendations** (`notes_recommendations`, text, optional): Enter any notes or recommendations for future maintenance or issues.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure proper maintenance tracking and issue resolution.
- If you are unsure about any section, please consult with a supervisor or maintenance expert before proceeding.
- This form is intended to supplement regular maintenance activities, and is not a substitute for comprehensive maintenance plans or schedules.
