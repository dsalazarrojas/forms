# Van Maintenance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Van Maintenance Checklist form is designed to help van owners or operators keep track of their vehicle's maintenance activities. This form is a general checklist for tracking key maintenance items, including vehicle details and maintenance schedules.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the vehicle's details:
	* Enter the vehicle's make in the "Vehicle Make" field.
	* Enter the vehicle's model in the "Vehicle Model" field.
2. Update the maintenance schedule:
	* Enter the scheduled maintenance dates in the "Maintenance Schedule" field.
3. Record the last maintenance dates:
	* Enter the last maintenance date in the "Last Maintenance Date" field (there are two fields for this, but only one is required).
4. Check the vehicle's status:
	* Enter the current fuel level in the "Fuel Level" field.
	* Enter the current oil level in the "Oil Level" field.
	* Check the tire pressure in the "Tire Pressure" field.
	* Check the brake pad condition in the "Brake Pads" field.
	* Check the air filter condition in the "Air Filter" field.
	* Check the engine oil condition in the "Engine Oil" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Make** (`van_maintenance_checklist_page_1_field_1`, text, required/optional): Enter the make of your van, e.g., Ford, Toyota, or Mercedes-Benz.
* **Vehicle Model** (`van_maintenance_checklist_page_1_field_2`, text, required/optional): Enter the model of your van, e.g., Transit, Corolla, or Sprinter.
* **Maintenance Schedule** (`van_maintenance_checklist_page_1_field_3`, text, required/optional): Enter the scheduled maintenance dates for your van, including any upcoming or past maintenance activities.
* **Last Maintenance Date** (`van_maintenance_checklist_page_1_field_4`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the last maintenance performed on your van.
* **Last Maintenance Date** (`van_maintenance_checklist_page_1_field_5`, date, required/optional): (Duplicate of the previous field, but only one is required for the form to be considered complete)
* **Fuel Level** (`van_maintenance_checklist_page_1_field_6`, text, required/optional): Check the current fuel level of your van, e.g., full, half, or empty.
* **Oil Level** (`van_maintenance_checklist_page_1_field_7`, text, required/optional): Check the current oil level of your van, e.g., full, half, or empty.
* **Tire Pressure** (`van_maintenance_checklist_page_1_field_8`, text, required/optional): Check the current tire pressure of your van, ensuring it meets the recommended level.
* **Brake Pads** (`van_maintenance_checklist_page_1_field_9`, text, required/optional): Check the condition of your van's brake pads, e.g., good, worn, or replaced.
* **Air Filter** (`van_maintenance_checklist_page_1_field_10`, text, required/optional): Check the condition of your van's air filter, e.g., clean, dirty, or replaced.
* **Engine Oil** (`van_maintenance_checklist_page_1_field_11`, text, required/optional): Check the condition of your van's engine oil, e.g., full, low, or changed.

## Tips
- Always check the recommended maintenance schedule for your specific van make and model.
- Record any maintenance activities performed or scheduled, including dates and details.
- Review and update this form regularly to stay on top of your van's maintenance needs.
