# HVAC Unit Upgrade Approval Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request approval for upgrading HVAC (Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning) units. It helps facility managers, administrators, or maintenance personnel to formally submit a request for upgrades, providing detailed information about the upgrade proposal and supporting documentation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the form submission field**: Provide a brief description of the upgrade request.
2. **Provide Hvac Upgrade Details**: Describe the details of the upgrade, including the type of upgrade and the reasons for the request.
3. **Submit your Hvac Upgrade Proposal**: Write a detailed proposal, including the scope of work, timeline, and cost estimates.
4. **Attach Supporting Documents**: Attach any relevant documents, such as quotes, diagrams, or reports, that support your proposal.
5. **Enter Submitter Details**: Provide information about yourself, including your name, department, and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Submission** (`form_submission`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the upgrade request.
- **Hvac Upgrade Details** (`hvac_upgrade_details`, text, optional): Describe the details of the upgrade, including the type of upgrade and the reasons for the request.
- **Hvac Upgrade Proposal** (`hvac_upgrade_proposal`, text, optional): Write a detailed proposal, including the scope of work, timeline, and cost estimates.
- **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, text, optional): Attach any relevant documents, such as quotes, diagrams, or reports, that support your proposal.
- **Submitter Details** (`submitter_details`, text, optional): Provide information about yourself, including your name, department, and contact information.

## Tips
* Be sure to attach any supporting documents that are relevant to your proposal.
* Clearly describe the upgrade and its reasons in the Hvac Upgrade Details field.
* Keep your Hvac Upgrade Proposal concise and well-structured, including a detailed scope of work, timeline, and cost estimates.
* Ensure your Submitter Details are accurate and complete.
