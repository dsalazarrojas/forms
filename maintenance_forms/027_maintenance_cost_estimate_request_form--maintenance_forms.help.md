# Maintenance Cost Estimate Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Maintenance Cost Estimate Request Form is a tool used to estimate the time and cost required for maintenance tasks. This form is designed to be completed by technicians to provide accurate and detailed information about the maintenance process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate information about the job being completed.
	* Start by filling out the "Main Information" section, which includes details such as job number, equipment type, and location.
	* In the "Technician Information" section, enter your name, phone number, and email address.
	* In the "Review Information" section, provide details about the review process, including who reviewed the job and when.
2. Enter accurate estimated hours and costs for the job.
	* The "Estimated Hours" field is used to estimate the time required for the maintenance task.
	* The "Estimated Cost" field is used to estimate the cost of the maintenance task.
3. Provide any additional notes or attachments as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Information (1)**: This field is used to enter the main details of the job, including job number, equipment type, and location.
* **Job Number (2)**: Enter the job number assigned to the maintenance task.
* **Equipment Type (3)**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the equipment is applicable to the job.
* **Serial Number (4)**: Enter the serial number of the equipment being maintained.
* **Description (5)**: Enter a brief description of the maintenance task.
* **Location (6)**: Select multiple locations where the job was performed.
* **Estimated Hours (7)**: Enter the estimated time required for the maintenance task.
* **Estimated Hours Hint (8)**: Provide any additional information about the estimated hours.
* **Estimated Cost (9)**: Enter the estimated cost of the maintenance task.
* **Technician's Notes (10)**: Provide any additional notes about the maintenance task.
* **Attachments (11)**: Attach any relevant documents or images to the job.
* **Technician Information (12)**: Enter your name, phone number, and email address as the technician completing the form.
* **Technician's Name (13)**: Enter your name as the technician.
* **Technician's Phone (14)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Technician's Email (15)**: Enter your email address.
* **Technician's Phone 2 (16)**: Enter an alternate phone number (optional).
* **Technician's Email 2 (17)**: Enter an alternate email address (optional).
* **Technician's Phone 3 (18)**: Enter another alternate phone number (optional).
* **Technician's Email 3 (19)**: Enter another alternate email address (optional).
* **Review Information (20)**: Enter details about the review process, including who reviewed the job and when.
* **Job Reviewed By (21)**: Enter who reviewed the job.
* **Review Date (22)**: Enter the date the job was reviewed.
* **Review Time (23)**: Enter the time the job was reviewed.
* **Review Status (24)**: Select the review status, e.g., Review 1, Review 2, etc.

Note: The form is designed to be completed by technicians, but the "Review Information" section is used to document the review process, not the actual review itself. The "Review Status" field is used to indicate the stage of the review process.
