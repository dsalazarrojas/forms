# Bus Maintenance Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request maintenance service for a bus. Authorized personnel can use this form to submit a request for service, and it will be reviewed and processed accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your information and vehicle details.
2. Select the correct department and service type from the provided options.
3. Choose the priority level of the request, based on the urgency of the issue.
4. Describe the problem and any symptoms you have observed.
5. If applicable, provide additional information, such as the date and time when the issue first occurred, and any parts that may be needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requestor Information**
  * This section is for your personal details and contact information.
  * You will need to enter your full name, employee ID, and contact phone number and email address.
* **Department**
  * Select the department that the bus belongs to, such as Operations, Maintenance, or Fleet Management.
* **Vehicle Information**
  * Provide the bus number, make and model, and license plate number.
* **Service Information**
  * Describe the problem and any symptoms you have observed.
  * If the issue is recurring, select the frequency of occurrence.
* **Service Type**
  * Choose the type of service required, such as Routine Maintenance, Oil Change, or Brake Service.
* **Priority Level**
  * Select the level of urgency for the request, based on how quickly the service is needed.
  * Options include Low, Medium, High, Urgent, and Critical.
* **Problem Description**
  * Describe the problem in detail, including any relevant information.
* **Symptoms Observed**
  * If you have observed any symptoms, describe them here.
* **When Did Issue First Occur**
  * If you know the date and time when the issue first occurred, enter it here.
* **Frequency**
  * Select the frequency of the issue, such as Constant, Frequent, Occasional, or Rare.
* **Does This Affect Vehicle Operation**
  * Indicate if the issue affects the vehicle's operation, and if so, what impact it has.
* **Preferred Service Date**
  * Enter the date and time when you would like the service to be performed.
* **Preferred Service Time**
  * Enter the preferred time for the service.
* **Known Parts Needed**
  * If specific parts are required for the service, enter them here.
* **Previous Related Repairs**
  * If this issue has been addressed before, describe the previous repairs here.
* **Additional Notes**
  * Any other relevant information or comments can be entered here.
