# Predictive Retail Maintenance Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Predictive Retail Maintenance Application Form is designed to capture information from customers or staff about maintenance requests for retail stores or locations. This form helps the maintenance team to quickly identify and respond to maintenance needs.

## How to Complete This Form
1. To complete the form, please select the "Maintenance Type" of the issue you are experiencing. This will help the maintenance team to understand the nature of the issue.
2. Enter the "Location" where the maintenance is needed.
3. Briefly describe the "Problem Description" to help the maintenance team understand the issue.
4. Select "Yes" if the maintenance is active or "No" if not.
5. Enter the "Maintenance Date" when the maintenance is expected to be done.
6. Enter the "Maintenance Time" when the maintenance is expected to be done.
7. Enter your "Contact Name", "Contact Number", and "Contact Email" so that the maintenance team can reach out to you for further questions or updates.
8. Enter any additional "Contact Message" if you have any specific instructions or requests.
9. If the maintenance is assigned to someone, select "Yes" for "Assigned To".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Maintenance Type**: Select the type of maintenance needed (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Location**: Enter the location where the maintenance is needed.
* **Problem Description**: Briefly describe the issue you are experiencing.
* **Maintenance Date**: Enter the date when the maintenance is expected to be done.
* **Maintenance Time**: Enter the time when the maintenance is expected to be done.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name so that the maintenance team can contact you.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number so that the maintenance team can reach out to you.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address so that the maintenance team can contact you.
* **Contact Message**: Enter any additional information or instructions for the maintenance team.
* **Assigned To**: If the maintenance is assigned to someone, select "Yes".

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter accurate information to help the maintenance team respond to your request efficiently.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact the maintenance team.
* The maintenance team will review your request and follow up with you as soon as possible.
