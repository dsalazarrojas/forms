# Maintenance Downtime Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and verify maintenance activities that are causing a device or equipment to be unavailable for use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure that the activity is a maintenance activity causing the device or equipment to be unavailable for use.
2.  Enter the details of the maintenance activity.
3.  Enter the start and end dates for the maintenance activity.
4.  Enter the duration of the maintenance activity.
5.  Enter the start and end times for the maintenance activity.
6.  Enter the contact person responsible for the maintenance activity.
7.  Review and submit the form to confirm the maintenance activity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Maintenance Downtime Acknowledgement Form** (`maintenance_downtime_acknowledgement_form`, Text, Required):
    This field is the title of the form.
*   **Maintenance Activity** (`maintenance_activity`, Text, Optional):
    This field is used to describe the maintenance activity that is causing the device or equipment to be unavailable for use.
*   **Maintenance Details** (`maintenance_details`, Text, Required):
    This field is used to provide additional information about the maintenance activity, such as the nature of the activity or any relevant notes.
*   **Maintenance Start Date** (`maintenance_start_date`, Date, Optional):
    This field is used to input the start date of the maintenance activity.
*   **Maintenance End Date** (`maintenance_end_date`, Date, Optional):
    This field is used to input the end date of the maintenance activity.
*   **Maintenance Duration** (`maintenance_duration`, Number, Optional):
    This field is used to input the duration of the maintenance activity.
*   **Maintenance Start Time** (`maintenance_start_time`, Time, Optional):
    This field is used to input the start time of the maintenance activity.
*   **Maintenance End Time** (`maintenance_end_time`, Time, Optional):
    This field is used to input the end time of the maintenance activity.
*   **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, Text, Optional):
    This field is used to input the name and contact information of the person responsible for the maintenance activity.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that the activity is a maintenance activity before completing this form.
*   Ensure that you are not duplicating an already reported activity.
*   Provide accurate and detailed information about the maintenance activity to ensure proper tracking and reporting.
