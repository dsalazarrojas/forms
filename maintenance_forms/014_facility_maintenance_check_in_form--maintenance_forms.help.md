# Facility Maintenance Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to aid facility maintenance personnel in documenting and tracking maintenance activities, issues, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct options for 'Activities' and 'Issues' fields based on your observations or experiences during the maintenance process.
2. Provide a detailed note describing any maintenance activities, issues, or notable points related to the facility.
3. Choose whether 'Scheduled Activities' are relevant to your current work.
4. Identify if a technician is assigned for the maintenance task by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' for 'Assigned Tech'.
5. Leave additional notes or comments, if needed, in the 'Notes' field.
6. Confirm that you have completed the maintenance tasks by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' for 'Submit'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Maintenance Check In** (`facility_maintenance_check_in_form`): This is the title of the form that serves as a header.
* **Activities** (`maintenance_activities`): Select 'Yes' if you have performed any maintenance activities during the current shift. Select 'No' if no activities were performed.
* **Issues** (`maintenance_issues`): Select 'Yes' if any issues were identified during the maintenance process. Select 'No' if no issues were encountered.
* **Note** (`facility_maintenance_check_in_form_note`): This is a text field where you can provide a detailed description of any maintenance activities, issues, or notable points observed during the process.
* **Scheduled Activities** (`scheduled_activities`): Select 'Yes' if you are currently performing scheduled maintenance activities. Select 'No' if no scheduled activities are relevant to your current work.
* **Assigned Tech** (`assigned_technician`): Select 'Yes' if a technician is assigned to handle the maintenance task. Select 'No' if a technician is not assigned.
* **Notes** (`facility_maintenance_check_in_form_note_1`): This is another text field for leaving any additional notes or comments, if needed.
* **Submit** (`maintenance_form_submit`): Select 'Yes' to confirm that you have completed the maintenance tasks. Select 'No' to indicate that tasks are incomplete or pending. 

## Tips

- Be accurate and thorough when filling out the 'Note' fields to ensure clear communication with colleagues and management.
- Make sure to update the 'Activities', 'Issues', and 'Scheduled Activities' fields accurately based on your actual work and observations.
- If a technician is assigned, ensure you document their name or ID in the 'Notes' field for future reference.
- Review and submit the form only after you have completed your maintenance tasks to ensure accuracy.
