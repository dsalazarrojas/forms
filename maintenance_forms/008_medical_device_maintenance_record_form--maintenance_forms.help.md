# Medical Device Maintenance Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Device Maintenance Record Form is a document that helps maintain a record of medical device service and maintenance activities. It gathers crucial information related to the device, its maintenance schedule, and the personnel involved in the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the device name to identify the specific medical device being maintained or serviced.
2. Fill in the device serial number to track the device's unique identifier.
3. Specify the service frequency, indicating how often the equipment is serviced. 
4. Fill in the service date when the device was last serviced.
5. Update the service status to reflect the current state of the device (Available, In-Service, Out-of-Service, Overdue, or Unknown).
6. Optionally, add comments to provide additional context about the device or service activity.
7. Enter the user's name and department to identify the personnel involved.
8. Provide the contact number for easy communication.
9. Enter the email of the person in charge.
10. Fill in the date the device was received and the date it was last serviced.
11. Enter the user ID for reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Device Name** (`device_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the medical device being maintained or serviced.
* **Device Serial Number** (`device_serial_number`, text, required): Enter the unique identifier of the device.
* **Service Frequency** (`service_frequency`, text, required): Indicate how often the equipment is serviced (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
* **Service Date** (`service_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the device was last serviced.
* **Service Status** (`service_status`, select_one, required): Choose the current status of the device (Available, In-Service, Out-of-Service, Overdue, or Unknown).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Add any additional comments or notes about the service activity.
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the technician or engineer performing the service.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the department or team responsible for the device maintenance.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter the contact number for easy communication.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person in charge.
* **Date Received** (`date_received`, date, required): Enter the date the device was received.
* **Date Serviced** (`date_serviced`, date, required): Enter the date the device was last serviced.
* **User ID** (`user_id`, text, required): Enter the unique identifier of the technician or engineer.
* **User ID** (`user_id`, text, required): Enter the unique identifier of the user performing the service.
