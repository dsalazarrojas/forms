# UV Water System Installation And Maintenance Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request maintenance or installation services for the UV water system. To submit a valid request, make sure you have a valid reason for the request, either for maintenance or installation. Also, check that this request is not a duplicate.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting either the "Water System Installation" or "Maintenance Request" option from the dropdown menu. This will specify the reason for your request.
2. Provide a brief description of the service you need in the "Service Details" field, e.g. "Replace UV light".
3. Choose a date for the visit in the "Schedule Visit" field.
4. Choose a time for the visit in the "Service Time" field.
5. Enter your contact name in the "Contact Name" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **uv_water_system_request (Text)**: Enter a brief description of the UV water system installation or maintenance request. (Required)
* **request_type (Select One)**: Choose the type of service you need. Options are "Water System Installation" and "Maintenance Request". (Required)
* **service_details (Text)**: Provide a brief description of the service you need, e.g. "Replace UV light". (Required)
* **schedule_visit (Date)**: Choose a date for the visit. (Required)
* **service_date (Number)**: Not applicable (this field is not used).
* **service_time (Time)**: Not applicable (this field is not used).
* **contact_name (Text)**: Enter your name. (Required)
* **email_address (Email)**: Enter your email address. (Required)
* **contact_phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number. (Required)
* **form_section_10 (Number)**: Not applicable (this field is not used).
* **form_section_11 (Number)**: Not applicable (this field is not used).
* **form_section_12 (Number)**: Not applicable (this field is not used).
* **form_section_13 (Text)**: Not applicable (this field is not used).
* **form_section_14 (Select Multiple)**: This field is not used for scheduling; it seems to be intended for other purposes, so it's not explained here.

Note: Please make sure to review and submit your request before proceeding, as you won't be able to edit or cancel it later.
