# Fast Line Service Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fast Line Service Request form is used to collect essential information from customers for efficient service delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the relevant options for "Priority" and "Assigned To" fields.
2. Enter the customer's information, including their contact method, contact number, email address, and service location.
3. Provide a description of the problem or issue you are experiencing.
4. Choose a date for the service request and assignment of the task.
5. Add any relevant comments or attachments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fast Line Service Request Form** (`1`, `text`, not required): This is the main heading for the form.
* **Customer Information** (`2`, `text`, not required): Enter the customer's name, contact method, contact number, email address, and service location.
* **Service Request Information** (`3`, `text`, not required): Describe the issue or problem you are experiencing.
* **Contact Method** (`4`, `text`, not required): Enter the customer's preferred contact method (e.g., phone, email, or in-person).
* **Contact Number** (`5`, `text`, not required): Enter the customer's contact number.
* **Email Address** (`6`, `text`, not required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Service Location** (`7`, `text`, not required): Enter the location where the service will be performed.
* **Problem Description** (`8`, `text`, not required): Provide a detailed description of the problem or issue.
* **Service Request Date** (`9`, `date`, not required): Choose a date for the service request.
* **Assigned Date** (`10`, `date`, not required): Choose a date for the task assignment.
* **Priority** (`11`, `select_one`, required): Select the priority level for the service request (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Assigned To** (`12`, `select_one`, required): Choose the person assigned to handle the service request (ChatJimmy, John, or Jane).
* **Comments** (`13`, `text`, not required): Add any comments or notes about the service request.
* **Attachments** (`14`, `text`, not required): Upload or link any relevant attachments, such as images or documents.

## Tips
- Make sure to complete all required fields before submitting the form.
- Use clear and concise language when describing the problem or issue.
- Attach any relevant documents or images to support the service request.
- Prioritize the service request accordingly to ensure timely delivery.
