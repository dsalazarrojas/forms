# Inverter Service Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inverter Service Report form is used to document the services performed on an inverter, ensuring that maintenance tasks are properly recorded and followed up on.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the inverter's ID, model, and year to identify the device being serviced.
2. Record the date and time of the visit.
3. Indicate the duration of the visit.
4. Specify the technician who performed the visit.
5. Describe any diagnostics performed or issues identified.
6. Provide a description of the work done during the visit.
7. Schedule the next planned visit date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **inverter_id** (inverter_id, text, optional): Enter the unique identifier of the inverter being serviced.
* **inverter_model** (inverter_model, text, optional): Enter the model of the inverter.
* **inverter_year** (inverter_year, number, optional): Enter the year of the inverter.
* **visit_date** (visit_date, date, optional): Enter the date of the visit.
* **visit_time** (visit_time, time, optional): Enter the time of the visit.
* **technician** (technician, text, optional): Enter the name of the technician who performed the visit.
* **visit_duration** (visit_duration, number, optional): Enter the duration of the visit.
* **diagnostics** (diagnostics, text, optional): Describe any diagnostics performed or issues identified during the visit.
* **work_description** (work_description, text, optional): Describe the work done during the visit.
* **next_visit_date** (next_visit_date, date, optional): Schedule the next planned visit date.
