# Plant Maintenance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Plant Maintenance Request Form is a digital form used to collect information about maintenance requests for facilities, equipment, and assets. This form is intended for employees or staff members to report and manage maintenance tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields to provide accurate information about the facility, equipment, or asset in question.
2. Select the correct priority level for the maintenance request.
3. Provide a detailed description of the issue or maintenance needed.
4. Enter the date and time you are requesting maintenance to be performed.
5. Select the status of the maintenance request.
6. Choose the staff member assigned to perform the maintenance.
7. Select the facility type.
8. Enter any additional information or remarks about the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Name** (1): Enter the name of the facility, equipment, or asset that requires maintenance.
* **Priority** (2): Select the level of priority for the maintenance request. High, Medium, or Low.
* **Description** (3): Provide a detailed description of the issue or maintenance needed.
* **Date Requested** (4): Enter the date you are requesting maintenance to be performed.
* **Time Requested** (5): Enter the time you are requesting maintenance to be performed.
* **Scheduled Date** (6): Enter the date when the maintenance is scheduled to be performed.
* **Scheduled Time** (7): Enter the time when the maintenance is scheduled to be performed.
* **Status** (8): Select the current status of the maintenance request. Open, In Progress, or Closed.
* **Assigned To** (9): Choose the staff member assigned to perform the maintenance.
* **Facility Type** (10): Select the type of facility, equipment, or asset that requires maintenance.
* **Location** (11): Enter any additional information about the location of the facility, equipment, or asset.
* **Equipment Type** (12): Enter any additional information about the type of equipment that requires maintenance.
* **Asset Tag** (13): Enter the asset tag number of the equipment or asset that requires maintenance.
* **Serial Number** (14): Enter the serial number of the equipment that requires maintenance.
* **Remarks** (15): Provide any additional remarks or comments about the maintenance request.
* **Notes** (16): Enter any additional notes or information about the maintenance request.
* **Maintenance Type** (17): Enter any additional information about the type of maintenance required.
* **Category** (18): Enter any additional information about the category of the maintenance request.

**Tips**

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your maintenance request is processed correctly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or maintenance team for guidance.
* Please note that duplicate requests may be rejected.
