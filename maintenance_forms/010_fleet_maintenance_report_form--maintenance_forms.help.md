# fleet_maintenance_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The fleet_maintenance_report_form is used for reporting maintenance activities on specific vehicles within a fleet.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the vehicle's details:
	* Enter the Vehicle Number of the vehicle being reported.
	* Enter the Vehicle Year of the vehicle being reported.
2. Enter any additional information:
	* Enter the Vehicle Make of the vehicle being reported.
	* Enter the Vehicle Model of the vehicle being reported.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Number**: Enter the unique identifier of the vehicle being reported.
* **Vehicle Year**: Enter the year of the vehicle being reported.
* **Vehicle Make**: Enter the make of the vehicle being reported (e.g., Toyota, Ford, etc.).
* **Vehicle Model**: Enter the model of the vehicle being reported (e.g., Corolla, F-150, etc.).

## Tips
* Double-check that you have all necessary information about the vehicle before creating a report.
* If a report already exists for the vehicle, do not duplicate the effort by creating a new report.
* If you need to report on multiple vehicles, create separate reports for each vehicle.
