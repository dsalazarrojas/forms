# Facility Maintenance Certification Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to register their facility maintenance certification. It is used to verify competence in a particular area of facility maintenance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **User Information** (`User Information`, `text`, required: false): Provide your personal details as required by your certifying organization.
2. **Employer Details** (`Employer Details`, `text`, required: false): Enter your employer's name and contact information as relevant.
3. **Sessions** (`Sessions`, `text`, required: false): Specify the dates and locations of any relevant maintenance sessions or training events you've participated in.
4. **Certifications** (`Certifications`, `text`, required: false): List any relevant certifications you've obtained, such as safety training or maintenance licenses.
5. **Submit** (`Submit`, `text`, required: false): Review and submit your registration form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information**: Enter your name, address, and any other relevant personal details as required.
* **Employer Details**: If applicable, enter your employer's name, contact details, and address for contact purposes.
* **Sessions**: Describe the sessions or training events you've participated in, including dates and locations.
* **Certifications**: List all relevant certifications you've obtained, such as safety training or maintenance licenses.
* **Submit**: Once you've filled in all necessary fields, review your form and submit it for review.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy and completeness before submission.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in.
* Use a clear and consistent format in filling out the sessions and certifications fields.
