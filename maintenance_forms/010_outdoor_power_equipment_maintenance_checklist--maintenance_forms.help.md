# Outdoor Power Equipment Maintenance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and organize information on the maintenance history and schedule of outdoor power equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `equipment_checklist` field with a list of equipment that needs to be maintained.
2. Provide a brief description of the maintenance history in the `maintenance_history` field.
3. Enter the date of the last service in the `last_service_date` field.
4. Schedule the next service date in the `next_service_date` field.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about the equipment in the `notes` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Equipment Checklist (1)
* **Equipment Checklist**: List the outdoor power equipment that needs to be maintained, such as lawn mowers, trimmers, or chain saws. 

### Maintenance History (2)
* **Maintenance History**: Briefly describe any previous maintenance or repairs performed on the equipment, including dates and details of work completed.

### Last Service Date (3)
* **Last Service Date**: Enter the date when the outdoor power equipment was last serviced or maintained. This can be a specific date or a range of dates if maintenance was performed over a period of time.

### Next Service Date (4)
* **Next Service Date**: Schedule the date when the next service is due or when maintenance is recommended. This can be a specific date or a range of dates if maintenance is needed over a period of time.

### Notes (5)
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or information about the equipment, such as usage, issues, or recommendations for future maintenance.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when listing equipment and describing maintenance history and schedule.
- Use clear and concise language in the `notes` field to help with future reference and decision-making.
- Regularly review and update the form to stay on top of equipment maintenance and ensure continued performance and safety.
