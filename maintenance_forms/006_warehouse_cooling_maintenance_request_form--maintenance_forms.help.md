# Warehouse Cooling Maintenance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Warehouse Cooling Maintenance Request Form is used to request maintenance for your warehouse's cooling systems. This form will help us identify the required maintenance, assign resources, and schedule the necessary work to keep your cooling systems running smoothly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all the necessary equipment that require maintenance from the list of options for "Cooling Equipment."
2. Choose the appropriate "Maintenance Priority" based on the urgency of the maintenance.
3. Specify the type of maintenance required for the selected equipment by selecting the corresponding "Maintenance Type."
4. Fill out any additional comments or details regarding the maintenance in the "Comments" section.
5. Provide your contact information in the "Email" and "Phone" fields for follow-up and updates.
6. Ensure that the "Facility," "Equipment Number," and "Location" fields are completed with accurate information.
7. Select the current "Equipment Status" and "Equipment Condition" of the equipment.
8. If you are the one submitting the request, select "Yes" in the "Assigned To" field.
9. Select the dates for the "Scheduled Start Date" and "Scheduled End Date" based on your availability.
10. Enter the "Actual Start Date" and "Actual End Time" once the maintenance has started.
11. Select "Yes" if you are the one who submitted the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Cooling Equipment**: Choose the equipment requiring maintenance from the provided options.
  <small>This field helps us know which equipment is in need of maintenance.</small>
* **Maintenance Priority**: Select the urgency level for the maintenance (e.g., "Yes" if it's urgent or "No" if it can wait).
  <small>This helps us prioritize the maintenance based on the level of urgency.</small>
* **Maintenance Type**: Choose the type of maintenance needed for the equipment (e.g., repair, replacement, etc.).
  <small>This allows us to understand what specific work is required for the equipment.</small>
* **Comments**: Provide any additional details or comments regarding the maintenance request.
  <small>This gives us more context and information to help us complete the work efficiently.</small>
* **Email**: Enter your contact email address for updates and follow-up.
  <small>This ensures we can contact you for any further information or updates.</small>
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number for immediate contact.
  <small>This provides an additional means of communication in case of any last-minute questions or changes.</small>
* **Facility**: Enter the name of the facility where the equipment is located.
  <small>This helps us locate the equipment and schedule the maintenance accordingly.</small>
* **Equipment Number**: Enter the unique identifier for the equipment.
  <small>This ensures we can identify the specific equipment needing maintenance.</small>
* **Location**: Enter the location of the equipment within the facility.
  <small>This helps us locate the equipment in the facility.</small>
* **Equipment Status**: Select the status of the equipment (e.g., "Active" or "Inactive").
  <small>This helps us understand the current state of the equipment.</small>
* **Equipment Condition**: Select the condition of the equipment (e.g., "Yes" for working or "No" for not working).
  <small>This helps us understand the current state of the equipment.</small>
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or details regarding the maintenance request.
  <small>This gives us more context and information to help us complete the work efficiently.</small>
* **Assigned To**: If you are submitting the request, select "Yes."
  <small>This helps us know who to contact for further information or updates.</small>
* **Scheduled Start Date**: Enter the date when you'd like the maintenance to start.
  <small>This helps us schedule the maintenance accordingly.</small>
* **Scheduled End Date**: Enter the date when you'd like the maintenance to end.
  <small>This helps us plan the completion of the maintenance.</small>
* **Actual Start Date**: Enter the date when the maintenance started.
  <small>This ensures we track the progress of the maintenance accurately.</small>
* **Actual Start Time**: Enter the time when the maintenance started.
  <small>This helps us record the exact time the maintenance started.</small>
* **Actual End Time**: Enter the time when the maintenance ended.
  <small>This helps us track the completion time of the maintenance.</small>
* **Submitted By**: If you are submitting the request, select "Yes."
  <small>This helps us know who submitted the request and can contact them for further information or updates.</small>
* **Submitted By Role**: If you are submitting the request, select your role or position.
  <small>This helps us understand your role and position in submitting the request.</small>
