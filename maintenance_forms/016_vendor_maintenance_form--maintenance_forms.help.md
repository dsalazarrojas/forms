# Vendor Maintenance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Maintenance Form is used to gather and update information about a vendor's details for administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant vendor information to be updated or verified.
2. Fill out the form with the required and optional fields as described below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Company Name** (`vendor_maintenance_form_page_1`, text, **required**: false): Enter the vendor's name or company name.
  This field is used to identify the vendor's company name.
- **Address** (`vendor_maintenance_form_page_2`, text, **required**: false): Enter the vendor's address.
  This field is used to record the vendor's physical location.
- **Email** (`vendor_maintenance_form_page_3`, email, **required**: false): Enter the vendor's email address.
  This field is used to contact the vendor for communication purposes.
- **Phone Number** (`vendor_maintenance_form_page_4`, text, **required**: false): Enter the vendor's phone number.
  This field is used to contact the vendor for communication purposes.
- **Reimbursement Request** (`vendor_maintenance_form_page_5`, number, **required**: false): Enter the vendor's reimbursement amount or request (if applicable).
  This field is used to track any reimbursement requests made by the vendor.
- **Reimbursement Date** (`vendor_maintenance_form_page_6`, date, **required**: false): Enter the date of the vendor's reimbursement request (if applicable).
  This field is used to track the date of the vendor's reimbursement request.

## Tips
* Ensure that only one form is submitted per vendor to avoid data duplication.
* If a field is not relevant to the vendor's information, you can leave it blank.
* Review the form for accuracy before submitting it to the relevant team.
