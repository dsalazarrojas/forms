# Machine Maintenance Log Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Machine Maintenance Log Access Form is a structured log for tracking and reporting machine maintenance activities. It helps in organizing and documenting all maintenance tasks performed on machines, ensuring that records are accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Category** field with the type of maintenance activity (e.g., Maintenance, Repair, or Inspection).
2. Enter the **Machine Number** of the machine being maintained.
3. Select the **Maintenance Activity** type from the provided options (e.g., Maintenance, Repair, or Inspection).
4. Enter the **Date Completed** of the maintenance activity.
5. Enter the **Time Completed** of the maintenance activity.
6. Add any **Comments** about the activity (e.g., details about the work performed, notes, or any issues found).
7. Optionally, specify the **Assigned Tool** used during the maintenance activity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category (Category)**:
	+ A required field for identifying the type of maintenance activity (e.g., Maintenance, Repair, or Inspection).
* **Machine Number (machine_number)**:
	+ Enter the unique identifier of the machine being maintained.
	+ This helps in linking the maintenance activity to the specific machine.
* **Maintenance Activity (maintenance_activity)**:
	+ Select from the options provided (Maintenance, Repair, or Inspection).
	+ This ensures consistency in recording the type of maintenance activity.
* **Date Completed (date_completed)**:
	+ Enter the date when the maintenance activity was completed.
	+ Format: YYYY-MM-DD (Year, Month, Day).
* **Time Completed (time_completed)**:
	+ Enter the time when the maintenance activity was completed.
	+ Format: HH:MM (Hours, Minutes).
* **Comments (comments)**:
	+ Enter any comments, notes, or details about the maintenance activity.
* **Assigned Tool (assigned_tool)**:
	+ Enter the tool or equipment used during the maintenance activity.
	+ Optional, but helpful for future reference.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and consistently.
* Use the provided options for the **Maintenance Activity** field to maintain consistency.
* Keep the **Comments** field brief but informative for easy reference.
* Review and update the form regularly to maintain an accurate and up-to-date maintenance log.
