# Predictive Maintenance Insight Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Predictive Maintenance Insight Report Form is used to collect data on asset-related predictive maintenance insights. This information will help maintenance teams make informed decisions to ensure the optimal functioning of assets and prevent potential issues.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide a Report Name**: Enter a unique name for the report, such as a descriptive title or a reference number.
2. **Select the Asset Category**: Choose the category of the asset, e.g., Category 1, Category 2, or Category 3.
3. **Select the Asset Type**: Choose the type of asset, e.g., Type 1, Type 2, or Type 3.
4. **Describe the Asset Location**: Enter the location of the asset, if necessary.
5. **Specify the Asset Condition**: Select all applicable conditions for the asset, e.g., Condition 1, Condition 2, and Condition 3.
6. **Enter the Start and End Dates (Optional)**: Provide start and end dates for the maintenance period, if relevant.
7. **Add Notes (Optional)**: Provide any additional comments or observations.
8. **Enter Contact Information (Optional)**: Provide contact details, including name and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Report Name** (text, required): Enter a unique name for the report.
- **Asset Category** (select_one, required): Choose the category of the asset (Category 1, Category 2, or Category 3).
- **Asset Type** (select_one, required): Choose the type of asset (Type 1, Type 2, or Type 3).
- **Asset Location** (text, optional): Enter the location of the asset.
- **Asset Condition** (select_multiple, required): Select all applicable conditions for the asset (Condition 1, Condition 2, and Condition 3).
- **Start Date** (date, optional): Provide the start date for the maintenance period.
- **End Date** (date, optional): Provide the end date for the maintenance period.
- **Notes** (note, optional): Provide any additional comments or observations.
- **Contact Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
- **Contact Email** (email, optional): Enter the contact email address of the contact person.
