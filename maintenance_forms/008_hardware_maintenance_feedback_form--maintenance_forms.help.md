# Hardware Maintenance Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hardware Maintenance Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from users on the maintenance status of hardware equipment. This form helps us identify areas that need improvement and ensure that all equipment is well-maintained.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required and optional fields as instructed. Please make sure to submit the form only if you have experienced issues with equipment maintenance. If you are unsure about any field, you can ask for clarification or seek assistance from a support representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hardware Maintenance Feedback Form** (`form_title`, text, required): This is the title of the form that you are submitting. Please enter a brief description of the issue you are experiencing with hardware maintenance.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, optional): Choose 'Yes' if you are experiencing issues with a service-related maintenance problem, 'No' if you are not experiencing issues or if your issue is not service-related.
* **Equipment ID** (`equipment_id`, number, required): Please enter the unique ID of the equipment that you are experiencing issues with.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or details about your maintenance issue, please enter them here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted about your maintenance issue.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted via phone, enter your phone number here.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Your name or title for reference purposes.
