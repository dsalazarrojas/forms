# Property Maintenance Weighbridge Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Maintenance Weighbridge Log is designed to provide a comprehensive record of weighbridge usage. This form is used to track key details related to vehicles, drivers, and weighbridge readings, ensuring accurate and efficient management of weighbridge operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information required for the fields listed below.
2. Fill in the required fields accurately and completely.
3. Review your entries before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Site Id** (1, number, required: false): Enter a unique identifier for the site where the weighbridge was used. This can include a site code or a reference number.
* **Vehicle Id** (2, number, required: true): Enter the ID of the vehicle that was weighed. This helps in identifying and tracking vehicle-specific weighbridge usage.
* **Vehicle Type** (3, text, required: false): Enter the type of vehicle (e.g., trailer, truck, etc.) to further categorize and analyze weighbridge data.
* **Vehicle Registration Number** (4, text, required: true): Enter the vehicle's registration number for accurate identification.
* **Driver Id** (5, number, required: true): Enter the driver's ID for tracking driver-specific weighbridge usage.
* **Driver Name** (6, text, required: false): Enter the driver's name for reference and tracking purposes.
* **Weight** (7, number, required: true): Record the weight reading taken at the weighbridge. This helps in monitoring vehicle weight and ensuring accurate weighing practices.
* **Timestamp** (8, date, required: true): Enter the date and time of the weighbridge reading. This ensures accurate tracking and historical record-keeping.
* **Comments** (9, text, required: false): Add any relevant comments or notes about the weighbridge reading, such as weather conditions or any issues encountered.
* **Assigned Tool** (10, text, required: false): Record the tool or equipment used during the weighbridge reading, if applicable.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled accurately and completely.
- Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submission.
- Review and update this form regularly to ensure all information is up-to-date and accurate.
