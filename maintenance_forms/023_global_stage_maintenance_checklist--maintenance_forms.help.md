# global_stage_maintenance_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The global_stage_maintenance_checklist form is used to collect information about the status of a global stage, including its inspection, condition, and maintenance needs.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out this form for stages that require regular maintenance checks.
- Ensure you have the necessary information to complete each field accurately.
- Review each field and its corresponding explanation to understand what information is required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Stage Inspection** (`stage_inspection`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the stage's current inspection status. For example, "No issues found" or "Requires immediate attention."
* **Stage Condition** (`stage_condition`, `select_one`, required: false): Select 'Yes' if the stage is currently in use or has been recently used, or 'No' if it's not.
* **Issues Found** (`issues_found`, `text`, required: true): If any issues were found during inspection, enter a detailed description of the problem and any proposed solutions.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate if any follow-up actions are required, and select all applicable options (e.g., 'Yes' for additional inspections).
* **Venue Inspection Date** (`venue_inspection_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the last inspection if applicable.
* **Venue Inspector** (`venue_inspector`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name or initials of the person who performed the last inspection if applicable.
* **Production Team** (`production_team`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate if the production team is aware of the stage's condition and maintenance needs.
* **Venue Location** (`venue_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location of the stage where the inspection was performed if not already known.

## Tips
- Ensure you complete the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure the stage's status is up-to-date.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to the stage's maintenance history or consult with the relevant personnel.
- Review the form regularly to track the stage's maintenance needs and status.
