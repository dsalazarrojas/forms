# Inertial Measurement Unit Predictive Maintenance Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather data for predictive maintenance inspections on inertial measurement units (IMUs).

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the **IMU Type** field with the type of IMU being inspected (e.g., Accelerometer, Gyroscope, Magnetometer, or Other).
2. Enter the **IMU Location** where the inspection is taking place.
3. Record the **IMU Reading 1**, **IMU Reading 2**, and **IMU Reading 3** values from the IMU.
4. Select the status of the IMU from the **Status** dropdown menu (e.g., Pending, Completed, In Progress, or Cancelled).
5. Fill in any **Comments** or **Notes** regarding the inspection.
6. If you have a specific **Category** for the inspection, select from the dropdown menu (e.g., Maintenance, Repair, or Other).
7. Enter any additional **Subcategory** information related to the inspection.
8. If you need to contact someone for further discussion or clarification, enter their **Phone** number.
9. Fill in the **Email** address of the person responsible for the inspection.
10. Select the **Assigned To** field with the name of the person responsible for the inspection.
11. Review and update the **Date Created**, **Date Modified**, **Form ID**, and **Created At** fields (these are automatically populated and should not be edited).
12. If necessary, add to **Comments History** and **Status** fields to track progress and discussions.
13. Finally, fill in the **User ID** field with your own ID for reference.
14. Review and update the **Updated At**, **Deleted At**, and **Deleted By** fields if you need to make changes or mark the form as deleted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **IMU Inspection Form**: This is the main form title.
* **Date of Inspection**: Enter the date when the inspection is taking place.
* **IMU Reading 1**, **IMU Reading 2**, **IMU Reading 3**, **IMU Reading 4**: These fields are for recording the IMU's reading values.
* **IMU Type**: Choose the type of IMU being inspected (e.g., Accelerometer, Gyroscope, Magnetometer, or Other).
* **IMU Location**: Enter the exact location where the IMU is being inspected.
* **Notes**: Fill in any comments or notes related to the inspection.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the person responsible for the inspection.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the inspection.
* **Category**: Select the category of the inspection (e.g., Maintenance, Repair, or Other).
* **Subcategory**: Enter any additional subcategory information related to the inspection.
* **Comments**: Add any comments or notes regarding the inspection.
* **Status**: Select the status of the inspection (e.g., Pending, Completed, In Progress, or Cancelled).
* **Comments History**: Track any comments or discussions regarding the inspection.
* **User ID**: Enter your own ID for reference.
* **Form ID**: Review and do not edit.
