# Cleaning Robot Maintenance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your maintenance request for your cleaning robot. Please fill out the form with as much detail as possible to ensure that we can provide the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Customer Information" section with your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide details about your robot, including the model, serial number, and purchase date.
3. Describe the issue with your robot, including the type of issue and when it started.
4. Select the type of service you need (e.g. repair, maintenance, cleaning).
5. Choose your preferred service location and time.
6. Let us know how urgently you need service and your preferred contact method.
7. Add any additional notes or requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`): Enter your full name as the owner of the robot.
* **Email Address** (`customer_email`): Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates.
* **Phone Number** (`customer_phone`): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Service Address** (`customer_address`): Enter the address where the robot is located.
* **Robot Model** (`robot_model`): Enter the brand and model of your robot.
* **Serial Number** (`robot_serial_number`): Enter the serial number or product ID of your robot.
* **Date of Purchase** (`purchase_date`): Enter the date you purchased the robot (optional).
* **Warranty Status** (`warranty_status`): Select whether your robot is under warranty or not.
* **Type of Issue** (`issue_type`): Choose the type of issue you are experiencing with your robot.
* **Detailed Description of Issue** (`detailed_issue`): Describe the specific problem you are experiencing with your robot.
* **When Did the Problem Start?** (`when_issue_started`): Enter the approximate date or how long ago the problem started.
* **Error Messages or Codes** (`error_messages`): Enter any error messages or codes displayed on your robot.
* **Troubleshooting Steps Already Attempted** (`troubleshooting_attempted`): Describe any steps you have taken to troubleshoot the issue.
* **Type of Service Needed** (`service_type`): Choose the type of service you need (e.g. repair, maintenance, cleaning).
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency_level`): Select how urgently you need service.
* **Preferred Service Date** (`preferred_service_date`): Enter the date you would like service to be performed.
* **Preferred Service Time** (`preferred_service_time`): Choose the time of day you would like service to be performed.
* **Service Location Preference** (`service_location`): Choose where you would like service to be performed (e.g. at home, at our service center).
* **Need a Replacement Unit While Yours Is Serviced?** (`replacement_unit`): Select whether you need a replacement unit while your robot is being serviced.
* **Additional Notes or Special Requests** (`additional_notes`): Enter any additional information or special requests you have.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`): Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g. phone, email, text message).
* **Best Time to Contact You** (`best_contact_time`): Enter the best time to contact you.
* **Request Submission Date** (`request_date`): This field is automatically populated with the current date.
