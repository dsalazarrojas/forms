# On Site Repair Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The On Site Repair Appointment Form is used to schedule an on-site repair appointment with a technician for maintenance or repair services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name and contact number.
2. Provide your address where the technician will visit.
3. Choose a date for the appointment.
4. Select a time for the appointment.
5. If you have any specific reasons for the visit, provide them in the "Visit Reason" field.
6. Submit the form to schedule your appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Information (id: 1)
* **Contact Information (text):** Enter your name and contact information so we can reach you to confirm your appointment.

### Name (id: 2)
* **Name (text):** Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.

### Contact Number (id: 3)
* **Contact Number (text):** Enter your phone number so we can contact you to confirm your appointment.

### Address (id: 4)
* **Address (text):** Enter the address where the technician will visit you.

### Date (id: 5)
* **Date (date):** Choose a date for your appointment.

### Time (id: 6)
* **Time (time):** Choose a time for your appointment.

### Select Time (id: 7)
* **Select Time (select one):** Choose from the list of available times for your appointment: 9:00 AM, 10:00 AM, 11:00 AM, 12:00 PM, 1:00 PM, 2:00 PM, 3:00 PM, 4:00 PM, 5:00 PM, 6:00 PM.

### Select Date (id: 8)
* **Select Date (select multiple):** Choose one or more dates for your appointment.

### On-site Visit (id: 9)
* **On-site Visit (text):** Enter any specific details about the visit, such as equipment or asset to be repaired.

### Visit Reason (id: 10)
* **Visit Reason (text):** Enter any specific reasons for the visit, such as a description of the issue or problem you are experiencing.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate contact information and address to ensure we can reach you to confirm your appointment.
* Choose a date and time that works best for you.
* If you have any specific reasons for the visit, provide them in the "Visit Reason" field to help the technician prepare for the visit.
