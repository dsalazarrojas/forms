# Maintenance Philosophy Document Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Maintenance Philosophy Document Request Form is used to collect information for requests related to maintenance activities. This form helps the organization to understand the requirements and needs of the submitter, ensuring that the right information is gathered and provided to them in a timely manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Request Type**: Select the type of request you are submitting (Maintenance, Non-maintenance, or Other).
2.  **What is the purpose of the request?**: Briefly describe the purpose of your request in this field.
3.  **Urgency of Request**: Choose the level of urgency for your request (Low, Medium, or High).
4.  **Format Preference**: Select the format you prefer for the maintenance philosophy document (pdf, docx, or excel).
5.  **Facilities Involved**: Select the facilities that are involved in the request (Electrical, HVAC, Plumbing, or Other).
6.  **Operations Involved**: Choose the operations involved in the request (Maintenance, Operations, or Other).
7.  **Submitter Name**: Enter your name as the submitter of this request.
8.  **Submitter Role**: Select your role as the submitter (Manager, Team Leader, Supervisor, or Other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Request Type
*   **Request Type** (Maintenance, Non-maintenance, or Other): This field allows you to categorize the type of request you are submitting.

### What is the purpose of the request?
*   **What is the purpose of the request**: Describe the main reason or goal of your request.

### Urgency of Request
*   **Urgency of Request** (Low, Medium, or High): Indicate the level of urgency you require for this request.

### Format Preference
*   **Format Preference** (pdf, docx, or excel): Select the preferred format for the maintenance philosophy document.

### Facilities Involved
*   **Facilities Involved** (Electrical, HVAC, Plumbing, or Other): Select the facilities that are involved in the request.

### Operations Involved
*   **Operations Involved** (Maintenance, Operations, or Other): Choose the operations involved in the request.

### Submitter Name
*   **Submitter Name**: Enter your name as the submitter of this request.

### Submitter Role
*   **Submitter Role** (Manager, Team Leader, Supervisor, or Other): Select your role as the submitter of this request.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill out the form accurately and truthfully to ensure that your request is processed correctly.
*   If you have any doubts or need further clarification, please contact [contact email](form@gic.mx).
