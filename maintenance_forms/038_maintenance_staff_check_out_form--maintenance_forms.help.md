# Maintenance Staff Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Maintenance Staff Check Out Form is a tool used to track the check-out status of maintenance staff members. It helps the management team stay updated on the current status of staff members and their equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your staff ID in the "Staff ID" field.
2. Enter the equipment ID of the equipment you are checking out.
3. Select the date of check-out in the "Date" field.
4. Select the check-out time in the "Check Out Time" field.
5. Enter the reason for check-out in the "Reason" field (optional).
6. Enter any additional remarks in the "Remarks" field (optional).
7. Select your current status as "Active" or "Inactive" in the "Status" field.
8. Select the status of the equipment as "Pending" or "Confirmed" in the "Status" field.
9. Enter the name of the staff member who checked you out in the "Checked Out By" field.
10. Enter the name of the staff member who checked you out to in the "Checked Out To" field.
11. Enter the time when you checked out in the "Checked Out Time" field (optional).
12. Enter the name of the staff member who checked you in in the "Checked In By" field.
13. Select the time when you checked in in the "Checked In Time" field.
14. Enter any additional remarks in the "Remarks2" field (optional).
15. Enter any additional remarks in the "Remarks3" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Staff ID**: Enter your unique staff ID to identify yourself in the system.
* **Equipment ID**: Enter the ID of the equipment you are checking out.
* **Date**: Select the date of check-out using the mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Check Out Time**: Select the time of check-out using the 12h Minimalist format.
* **Reason**: Enter the reason for checking out (optional).
* **Status**: Select your current status as "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Status**: Select the status of the equipment as "Pending" or "Confirmed".
* **Checked Out By**: Enter the name of the staff member who checked you out.
* **Checked Out To**: Enter the name of the staff member who checked you out to.
* **Checked Out Time**: Enter the time when you checked out (optional).
* **Checked In By**: Enter the name of the staff member who checked you in.
* **Checked In Time**: Select the time when you checked in.
* **Remarks2**: Enter any additional remarks (optional).
* **Remarks3**: Enter any additional remarks (optional).

Note: This form is for internal use only and is not intended for external sharing.
