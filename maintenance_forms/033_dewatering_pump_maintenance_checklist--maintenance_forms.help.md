# Dewatering Pump Maintenance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and track maintenance information for dewatering pumps. It helps the maintenance team to identify and address potential issues, schedule maintenance tasks, and keep track of pump status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the site visit details, including the date and time of the visit.
2. Report the current status of the pump, selecting one of the three options: Good, Bad, or Unknown.
3. Indicate if there are any leaks associated with the pump.
4. Describe any inspection issues with the pump.
5. Specify if any maintenance action was taken on the pump.
6. Select the current maintenance status of the pump.
7. Enter the next scheduled maintenance date.
8. Enter any additional notes or comments about the pump.
9. Identify the team member assigned to perform maintenance on the pump.
10. Enter the date of the site visit.
11. Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
12. Enter the date and time the form was submitted.
13. Enter the job site where the pump is located.
14. Select the maintenance category for the pump.
15. Enter the time the form was submitted.
16. Enter the location of the pump.
17. Enter any additional notes or comments about the pump.
18. Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the form.
19. Select the type of pump being maintained.
20. Enter the capacity of the pump.
21. Enter any additional notes or comments about the pump in this section.
22. Enter any comments in the comments section.
23. Enter any additional comments in the comments section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Site Visit Details (1)**: Enter the details of the site visit, including the date and time.
* **Pump Status (2)**: Select the current status of the pump, indicating if it is in good working condition, has issues, or if it is unknown.
* **Pump Leaks (3)**: Check if there are any leaks associated with the pump.
* **Pump Inspection Issues (4)**: Describe any issues found during the inspection of the pump.
* **Maintenance Action Taken (5)**: Specify if any maintenance action was taken on the pump.
* **Maintenance Status (6)**: Select the current maintenance status of the pump.
* **Next Maintenance Date (7)**: Enter the next scheduled maintenance date for the pump.
* **Notes (8)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the pump.
* **Assigned Team Member (9)**: Identify the team member assigned to perform maintenance on the pump.
* **Site Visit Date (10)**: Enter the date of the site visit.
* **Submitted By (11)**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted Date (12)**: Enter the date and time the form was submitted.
* **Job Site (13)**: Enter the location where the pump is situated.
* **Maintenance Category (14)**: Select the category of maintenance for the pump.
* **Submitted Time (15)**: Enter the time the form was submitted.
* **Location (16)**: Enter the location of the pump.
* **Notes and Comments (17)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the pump.
* **Submitted By Phone (18)**: Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the form.
* **Pump Type (19)**: Select the type of pump being maintained.
* **Pump Location (20)**: Enter the location of the pump.
* **Pump Capacity (21)**: Enter the capacity of the pump.
* **Notes Comments (22)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the pump in this section.
* **Comments Section (23)**: Enter any comments about the pump.
* **Comments Section Comments (24)**: Enter any additional comments in the comments section.
* **Comments Section Comments 2 (25)**: Enter any further comments about the pump.
