# Service Activity Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Activity Log form is designed to record and track maintenance activities for services provided. It is used by service personnel to document their activities, ensuring accurate and detailed records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Review the form**: Understand the purpose and required information for the form.
2. Fill out the **Service Activity Log** field with a brief description of the service activity.
3. Select the **Activity Date** field to enter the date of the service activity.
4. Provide a detailed **Activity Description** of the service activity in the corresponding field.
5. Choose the **Activity Outcome** from the provided options (Completed, Cancelled, or Deferred).
6. Select **Follow-ups**, if applicable, from the options provided.
7. Enter the **Next Follow-up Date** for scheduled follow-ups.
8. Choose the **Assigned to** field from the options provided, or select "Other" for non-standard assignments.
9. Enter the **Service Name** and **Service Type** for the activity.
10. Provide the **Service Location** details for the activity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Service Activity Log
* **Service Activity Log** (`service_activity_log`, `text`, required/optional): Brief description of the service activity.
### Activity Date
* **Activity Date** (`activity_date`, `date`, required/optional): The date of the service activity.
### Activity Description
* **Activity Description** (`activity_description`, `text`, required/optional): Detailed description of the service activity.
### Activity Outcome
* **Activity Outcome** (`activity_outcome`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select from options: Completed, Cancelled, Deferred.
### Follow-ups
* **Follow-ups** (`follow_ups`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select applicable follow-ups from options: Yes, No.
### Next Follow-up Date
* **Next Follow-up Date** (`next_follow_up_date`, `date`, required/optional): Date for scheduled follow-ups.
### Assigned to
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose from options: chatjimmy, Other.
### Service Name
* **Service Name** (`service_name`, `text`, required/optional): Name of the service activity.
### Service Type
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, `text`, required/optional): Type of the service activity.
### Service Location
* **Service Location** (`service_location`, `text`, required/optional): Location of the service activity.

This help guide will assist service personnel in understanding the form's purpose and usage, ensuring accurate and detailed records of maintenance activities.
