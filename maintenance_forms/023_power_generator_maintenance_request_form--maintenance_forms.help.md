# Power Generator Maintenance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Power Generator Maintenance Request Form is designed to centralize the maintenance requests for power generators in industrial or commercial settings. It gathers information from facility managers to ensure that the maintenance is done on time and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your facility manager's information (name, email, phone number, and facility location).
2. Describe the maintenance issue with the equipment type in mind.
3. Schedule the visit date and select the service provider to be assigned.
4. Add any additional comments or notes.
5. Upload any relevant files.
6. Update the status of the maintenance request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facility Manager Name** (`facility_manager_name`, text, required): Please enter your name.
* **Facility Manager Email** (`facility_manager_email`, text, required): Enter your email address.
* **Facility Manager Phone** (`facility_manager_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Facility Location** (`facility_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the facility where the power generator is located.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, text, required): Describe the type of equipment that needs maintenance.
* **Maintenance Issue** (`maintenance_issue`, text, required): Describe the issue with the equipment.
* **Maintenance Schedule Visit** (`maintenance_schedule_visit`, date, required): Select the date for the maintenance visit.
* **Service Provider** (`service_provider`, select_one, required): Select the service provider to be assigned to the maintenance.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Uploaded Files** (`uploaded_files`, note, optional): Upload any files relevant to the maintenance issue.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person assigned to the maintenance task.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, text, optional): Not applicable.
* **Date Created** (`date_created`, date, optional): Not applicable.
* **Time Created** (`time_created`, time, optional): Not applicable.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Not applicable.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, optional): Not applicable.
* **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`, time, optional): Not applicable.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, optional): Not applicable.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Not applicable.
* **Status** (`status`, text, required): Select the status of the maintenance request.
* **Facility Manager Notes** (`facility_manager_notes`, text, optional): Add any notes from the facility manager.
* **Service Provider Notes** (`service_provider_notes`, text, optional): Add any notes from the service provider.
* **Uploaded Files** (`uploaded_files`, note, optional): Not applicable.
* **Facility ID** (`facility_id`, text, optional): Not applicable.
* **Service Provider Name** (`service_provider_name`, text, optional): Not applicable.

Note: Fields with `required: true` are essential to complete the form, while fields with `required: false` are optional and can be filled out at your discretion.
