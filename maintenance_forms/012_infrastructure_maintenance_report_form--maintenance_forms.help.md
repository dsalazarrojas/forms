# infrastructure_maintenance_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The infrastructure maintenance report form is used to collect maintenance report information from users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Enter your information in the corresponding fields.
2.  Fill out the user field with your name or ID.
3.  Enter the location where you performed the maintenance activities.
4.  Choose the date you performed the maintenance activities.
5.  Select the time you performed the maintenance activities.
6.  Choose whether you performed maintenance activities by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the "Maintenance Type" dropdown menu.
7.  Select the assets involved in the maintenance activities by choosing "Yes" or "No" from the "Asset" dropdown menu.
8.  Briefly describe the maintenance activities in the "Description" field.
9.  Enter any additional comments or notes about the maintenance activities in the "Notes" field.
10.  Enter your contact information, including email and phone number, in the "Email" and "Phone" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name or ID.
* **Location** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location where you performed the maintenance activities.
* **Date** (`name`, `date`, required: false): Choose the date you performed the maintenance activities.
* **Time** (`name`, `time`, required: false): Choose the time you performed the maintenance activities.
* **Maintenance Type** (`name`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether you performed maintenance activities by choosing "Yes" or "No".
* **Asset** (`name`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the assets involved in the maintenance activities by choosing "Yes" or "No".
* **Description** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Briefly describe the maintenance activities.
* **Notes** (`name`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes about the maintenance activities.
* **Email** (`name`, `email`, required: false): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all fields that are marked as "required" to ensure that your report is complete and accurate.
* If you have any issues or concerns while filling out this form, please contact the maintenance team for assistance.
