# Mechanical Maintenance Material Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mechanical Maintenance Material Log form is designed to record information about materials used during mechanical maintenance tasks. This form helps to track materials used, their quantities, and costs for future reference and record-keeping.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter a brief description of the material used (Material Entry).
2.  Provide details about the maintenance task performed (Maintenance Entry).
3.  Specify the materials used (Materials Used).
4.  Enter a detailed description of the material (Material Description).
5.  Enter the quantity of the material used (Quantity).
6.  Enter the unit price of the material (Unit Price).
7.  Calculate the total price of the material(s) used (Total Price).
8.  Specify the user who performed the maintenance task (User).
9.  Add any additional notes about the maintenance task or material usage (Notes).
10.  Enter the date and time the maintenance was performed (Date and Time).
11.  Select if the task was assigned to a specific user (Assigned User).
12.  Select if a specific tool was assigned to the task (Assigned Tool).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Material Entry** (Material Entry, text, required): Enter a brief description of the material used.
*   **Maintenance Entry** (Maintenance Entry, text, required): Provide details about the maintenance task performed.
*   **Materials Used** (Materials Used, text, required): Specify the materials used.
*   **Material Description** (Material Description, text, required): Enter a detailed description of the material.
*   **Quantity** (Quantity, number, required): Enter the quantity of the material used.
*   **Unit Price** (Unit Price, number, required): Enter the unit price of the material.
*   **Total Price** (Total Price, number, required): Calculate the total price of the material(s) used.
*   **User** (User, text, required): Specify the user who performed the maintenance task.
*   **Notes** (Notes, note, required): Add any additional notes about the maintenance task or material usage.
*   **Date** (Date, date, required): Enter the date the maintenance was performed.
*   **Time** (Time, time, required): Enter the time the maintenance was performed.
*   **Assigned User** (Assigned User, select_one, required): Select if the task was assigned to a specific user.
*   **Assigned Tool** (Assigned Tool, select_one, required): Select if a specific tool was assigned to the task.
