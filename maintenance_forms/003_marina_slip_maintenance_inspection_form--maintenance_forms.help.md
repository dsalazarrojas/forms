# Marina Slip Maintenance Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marina Slip Maintenance Inspection Form is used to record and track maintenance activities performed on marina slips, ensuring that all necessary tasks are completed and reported accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "assigned to" field to indicate who is responsible for performing the maintenance tasks. This could be the Chief Maintenance Officer, a Maintenance Worker, or another staff member.
2. Enter the date and time of the inspection in the "inspection_date" and "inspection_time" fields.
3. Record the location of the slip being inspected in the "slip_location" field.
4. Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether maintenance tasks were completed in the "maintenance_tasks" field.
5. Indicate the status of the inspection by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "inspection_completed" field.
6. Provide any comments or notes about the inspection in the "comments" field (optional).
7. Select the entity that submitted the form in the "submitted_by" field (optional).
8. Add any additional comments or observations about the inspection in the "comments_section" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **marina_slip_inspection_id** (Number): Enter a unique identifier for this inspection.
* **inspection_date** (Date): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **inspection_time** (Time): Enter the time of the inspection.
* **slip_location** (Text): Enter the location of the slip being inspected.
* **maintenance_tasks** (Select Multiple): Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether maintenance tasks were completed.
* **assigned_to** (Select One): Select who is responsible for performing the maintenance tasks.
* **inspection_completed** (Select One): Indicate the status of the inspection by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **comments** (Text): Provide any comments or notes about the inspection.
* **submitted_by** (Select One): Select the entity that submitted the form.
* **comments_section** (Text): Add any additional comments or observations about the inspection.
