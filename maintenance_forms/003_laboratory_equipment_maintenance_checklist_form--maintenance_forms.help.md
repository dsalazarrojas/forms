# Laboratory Equipment Maintenance Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for tracking and updating the maintenance status of laboratory equipment, ensuring it is regularly checked for any issues or problems, and its maintenance history is accurately recorded.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by identifying the laboratory equipment being maintained. Fill in the 'Laboratory Equipment ID' field with the correct equipment ID.
- Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each 'Maintenance Checklist Item' question, indicating whether the equipment is in need of maintenance. You may choose 'Yes' for any item that requires attention or repair.
- If the equipment requires maintenance, provide a date for the 'Date of Last Maintenance' and select 'Yes' for 'Last Maintenance By'. Otherwise, leave these fields blank.
- Select the 'Maintenance Status' of the equipment as 'Active' if it is currently in use or 'Inactive' if it is not in use.
- Choose any 'Maintenance Frequency' that applies to the equipment (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Laboratory Equipment ID** (`laboratory_equipment_id`): Enter the unique identifier for the equipment being maintained.
* **Maintenance Checklist Item** (`maintenance_checklist_item_id`): Select 'Yes' if the equipment is in need of maintenance, 'No' otherwise.
* **Maintenance Type** (`maintenance_type_id`): Select 'Yes' if the maintenance is necessary, 'No' otherwise.
* **Date of Last Maintenance** (`date_of_last_maintenance`): Enter the date the maintenance was last performed.
* **Last Maintenance By** (`last_maintenance_by`): Select 'Yes' if the last maintenance was performed by the current user, 'No' otherwise.
* **Maintenance Status** (`maintenance_status`): Select 'Active' if the equipment is currently in use, 'Inactive' if it is not in use.
* **Maintenance Frequency** (`maintenance_frequency`): Select any applicable maintenance frequencies (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
