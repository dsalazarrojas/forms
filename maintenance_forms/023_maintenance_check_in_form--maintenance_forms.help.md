# Maintenance Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Maintenance Check In Form is used to record and track the maintenance activities on equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Enter the details of the maintenance activity in the "Check In Details" field.
3. Select the date and time of the activity in the "Date and Time" field.
4. Enter the technician's name and their ID in the "Technician Name" field.
5. Enter any notes or comments about the activity in the "Technician Notes" field.
6. Enter the equipment ID and location of the activity in the "Equipment ID" and "Location" fields.
7. Select the status of the maintenance activity from the "Status" field.
8. If necessary, enter the due date of the activity.
9. Enter any comments or notes about the activity in the "Comments" field.
10. Select the "Maintenance Form" type from the field.
11. If necessary, assign the activity to a specific person.
12. Enter the date the activity was assigned.
13. Select who completed the activity.
14. Enter the date the activity was completed.
15. Enter the date the activity was created.
16. Enter the date the activity was updated.
17. If the activity is deleted, select "Yes" in the "Deleted" field.
18. Enter the date the activity was deleted.
19. Select whether the activity is checked by someone.
20. Enter the comments about the activity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Check In Details** (`check_in_details`, `text`, required): Enter the details of the maintenance activity.
* **Date and Time** (`date_and_time`, `date`, required): Enter the date and time of the maintenance activity.
* **Technician Name** (`technician_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the technician who performed the maintenance activity.
* **Technician Notes** (`technician_notes`, `text`, required): Enter any notes or comments from the technician about the activity.
* **Equipment ID** (`equipment_id`, `number`, required): Enter the ID of the equipment being maintained.
* **Work Order Number** (`work_order_number`, `text`, required): Enter the work order number for the maintenance activity.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required): Enter the location of the maintenance activity.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the maintenance activity.
* **Status** (`status`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of the maintenance activity from the options.
* **Due Date** (`due_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the due date of the maintenance activity.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments or notes about the activity.
* **Check Out Date** (`check_out_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the activity was checked out.
* **Closed Date** (`closed_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the activity was closed.
* **Maintenance Form** (`maintenance_form`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the type of maintenance form from the options.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `select_one`, required): Select the person assigned to perform the activity.
* **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the activity was assigned.
* **Completed By** (`completed_by`, `select_one`, required): Select who completed the activity.
* **Completed Date** (`completed_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the activity was completed.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, `date`, required): Enter the date the activity was created.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, `date`, required): Enter the date the activity was last updated.
* **Deleted At** (`deleted_at`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the activity was deleted.
* **Deleted By** (`deleted_by`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether the activity was deleted.
* **Deleted Date** (`deleted_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the activity was deleted.
* **Comments Date** (`comments_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the comments about the activity were made.
* **Checked By** (`checked_by`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether the activity was checked by someone.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Enter the comments about the activity.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all the required fields before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field's label and hint for more information.
* If you need help with the maintenance activity, contact the maintenance team.
