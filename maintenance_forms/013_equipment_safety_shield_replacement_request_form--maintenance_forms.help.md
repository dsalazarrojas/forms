# equipment_safety_shield_replacement_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request replacement of safety shields on equipment due to issues or malfunctions.

## How To Complete This Form
Before filling out this form, please ensure that:
- You are familiar with the equipment's status in the system
- This request is not a duplicate
- You have a clear understanding of the issue with the safety shield

To complete this form:
1. Click on the "Maintenance Details" page and enter any relevant maintenance information.
2. On the "Equipment Details" page, enter the necessary details about the equipment in question.
3. In the "Issue Description" field, describe the problem with the safety shield in as much detail as possible.
4. In the "Photos" field, upload photos of the safety shield and the issue with it, if possible.
5. On the "Safety Shield Details" page, enter any relevant details about the safety shield, such as its condition or any relevant certifications.
6. On the "Equipment Condition" page, select whether the equipment is in good or bad condition.
7. On the "Recommended Replacement" page, select whether a replacement is recommended or not.
8. In the "Comments" field, add any additional comments or notes about the issue with the safety shield.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Maintenance Details** (`maintenance_form`, text, optional): This field is for any general maintenance information about the equipment.
- **Equipment Details** (`equipment_details`, text, optional): Enter any necessary details about the equipment in question.
- **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, text, optional): Describe the problem with the safety shield in as much detail as possible.
- **Photos** (`photos`, text, optional): Upload photos of the safety shield and the issue with it, if possible.
- **Safety Shield Details** (`safety_shield_details`, text, optional): Enter any relevant details about the safety shield, such as its condition or any relevant certifications.
- **Equipment Condition** (`equipment_condition`, select_one, optional): Select whether the equipment is in good or bad condition.
- **Recommended Replacement** (`recommended_replacement`, select_one, optional): Select whether a replacement is recommended or not.
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the issue with the safety shield.
