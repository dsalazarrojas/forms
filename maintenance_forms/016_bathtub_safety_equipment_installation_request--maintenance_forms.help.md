# Bathtub Safety Equipment Installation Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bathtub Safety Equipment Installation Request form is designed to provide us with essential information about you and your property in order to schedule and install bathtub safety equipment that suits your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your contact information (Homeowner Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and Property Address) and make sure all required fields are filled out accurately.
* Select your Property Type and Current Bathtub Type from the options provided.
* Choose the safety equipment you need for installation (Select All That Apply).
* Indicate any mobility challenges you're experiencing or have experienced.
* Select your age group and any bathroom access considerations.
* Choose a preferred installation date and time, or select any time slot if it's not specified.
* Confirm your home ownership status and, if applicable, whether you've received landlord approval.
* Select the budget consideration for the installation.
* Add any additional comments or notes, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Homeowner Name** (`homeowner_name`, text, required): This is your full name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): This is the email address we'll use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): This is your contact phone number.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required): Enter the address where you reside.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of residence you live in.
* **Equipment Type Needed** (`equipment_type`, select_multiple, required): Select all the types of equipment you need for your safety.
* **Current Bathtub Type** (`bathtub_type`, select_one, required): Indicate the type of bathtub you currently have.
* **Mobility Challenges** (`mobility_challenges`, select_multiple, required): Check any difficulties you experience or have experienced.
* **Primary User Age Group** (`user_age_group`, select_one, required): Select your age range.
* **Bathroom Access** (`bathroom_access`, select_one, required): Check if there are any access considerations in your bathroom.
* **Preferred Installation Date** (`preferred_installation_date`, date, required): Pick a preferred installation date.
* **Preferred Time of Day** (`preferred_time`, select_one, required): Choose a preferred time or leave it open for any time.
* **Home Ownership** (`home_ownership`, select_one, required): Indicate your home ownership status.
* **Landlord Approval Obtained** (`landlord_approval`, select_one, required): If applicable, confirm if you've received landlord approval.
* **Budget Consideration** (`budget_consideration`, select_one, required): Choose how the installation will be paid.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Provide any extra information or comments you'd like to share with us.
