# Sewer Issues And Pest Control Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report sewer issues and pest control requests to the maintenance team. It helps the team to quickly identify and address the problems, ensuring that your property and neighborhood are safe and habitable.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of service you require (e.g., Sewer Service, Pest Control, or Other).
2. Describe the issue in detail, providing as much information as possible.
3. Attach any relevant photos or videos of the issue.
4. Indicate the priority level of the issue (Urgent or Non-Urgent).
5. Schedule a service date and time if you have a specific request.
6. Provide the time of day you would like the service to take place.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Type (Sewer Service, Pest Control, Other)**:
	+ Select the type of service you need from the provided options.
* **Issue Description (Describe the issue in detail)**:
	+ Provide a clear and concise description of the problem you are experiencing.
	+ Attach any relevant photos or videos to help the maintenance team understand the issue.
* **Attach Photos**:
	+ If you have photos or videos of the issue, please attach them to this field.
* **Priority Level (Urgent, Non-Urgent)**:
	+ Select the priority level of the issue based on its severity and impact.
* **Service Date (Schedule a service date and time)**:
	+ If you have a specific request for a service date and time, provide it here.
* **Service Time (Schedule a service time)**:
	+ If you have a specific request for a service time, provide it here.

## Tips
Please ensure that you have attached any relevant photos or videos to support your description. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please do not hesitate to contact the maintenance team for assistance.
