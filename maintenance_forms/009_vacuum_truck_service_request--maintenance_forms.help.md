# Vacuum Truck Service Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vacuum Truck Service Request form is a tool for customers to submit requests for maintenance services related to their equipment. Fill out this form to inform our team about your service needs and we'll take care of the rest.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Make sure you have a genuine request and not a duplicate.
2. Fill out the form with accurate information.
3. If you're not sure about any field, contact us for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Number** (1, Text, Optional): Enter your job or order number, if applicable.
* **Customer Name** (2, Text, Optional): Please enter your full name.
* **Customer Email** (3, Email, Optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further details.
* **Service Type** (4, Select One, Optional): Choose 'Yes' if you need any service from our vacuum truck team.
* **Equipment Type** (5, Select Multiple, Optional): Select all equipment types related to your request.
* **Location** (6, Text, Optional): Provide the location where the service is needed.
* **Job Description** (7, Select Multiple, Optional): Explain your maintenance request in detail.
* **Job Date** (8, Date, Optional): Enter the date when the job is scheduled or needs to be done.
* **Service Date** (9, Date, Optional): Enter the date when the service is required.
* **Technician Name** (10, Text, Optional): If you have a preferred technician, enter their name.
* **Phone Number** (11, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Notes** (12, Note, Optional): Leave any additional comments or details about your request. 

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible in the 'Job Description' field.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us directly.
