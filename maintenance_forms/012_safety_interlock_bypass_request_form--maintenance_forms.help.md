# Safety Interlock Bypass Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to request a safety interlock bypass for maintenance or repairs. This form is used to collect and document the necessary information for the review and approval process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Navigate to the "Safety Interlock Bypass Request Form" page.
2. Select the "Maintenance Form" page.
3. Complete the "Temporary Bypass Request" section with the required information.
4. Select the "Bypass Timing" for the maintenance request.
5. Choose the risks associated with the bypass in the "Bypass Risk" section.
6. Select the person or team responsible for approving the bypass in the "Approved By" section.
7. Enter the date and time the bypass was approved in the "Date Approved" and "Time Approved" sections.
8. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Comments" section.
9. Enter the email address of the person requesting the bypass in the "Email" section.
10. Enter the phone number of the person requesting the bypass in the "Phone" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Maintenance Form
* **Maintenance Form** (`maintenance_form_1`, text, optional): This is the main form section where the user can enter any additional information related to the maintenance request.

### Temporary Bypass Request
* **Temporary Bypass Request** (`maintenance_form_2`, select_one, optional): Select the reason for the temporary bypass request from the provided options.
	+ Option 1: Regular maintenance
	+ Option 2: Emergency repair
	+ Option 3: Other (please specify)

### Bypass Timing
* **Bypass Timing** (`maintenance_form_3`, date, optional): Enter the date for when the bypass is required.
* **Note:** If the date is not known, you can leave this field blank.

### Bypass Risk
* **Bypass Risk** (`maintenance_form_4`, select_multiple, optional): Select the risks associated with the bypass.
	+ Option 1: High risk
	+ Option 2: Low risk

### Approved By
* **Approved By** (`maintenance_form_5`, select_one, optional): Select the person or team responsible for approving the bypass.
	+ Option 1: John Doe
	+ Option 2: Jane Doe

### Date Approved
* **Date Approved** (`maintenance_form_6`, date, optional): Enter the date the bypass was approved.
* **Note:** If the date is not known, you can leave this field blank.

### Time Approved
* **Time Approved** (`maintenance_form_7`, time, optional): Enter the time the bypass was approved.
* **Note:** If the time is not known, you can leave this field blank.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`maintenance_form_8`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes regarding the maintenance request.

### Email
* **Email** (`maintenance_form_9`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person requesting the bypass.
* **Note:** This field is for contact purposes only and is not required for the bypass process.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`maintenance_form_10`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person requesting the bypass.
* **Note:** This field is for contact purposes only and is not required for the bypass process.
