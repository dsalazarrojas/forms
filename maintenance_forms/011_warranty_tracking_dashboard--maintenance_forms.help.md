# Warranty Tracking Dashboard - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Warranty Tracking Dashboard form is designed for tracking customer information, warranty details, and claim status. It's used for monitoring and updating customer data and claim resolutions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please ensure that you have all necessary information and data at hand before filling out this form.
2. Follow the instructions and guidelines provided for each field.
3. Fill out each field with the required and optional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer_info** (1, text, not required): Enter customer details such as name, contact info, or any other relevant information.
* **warranty_details** (2, text, not required): Provide warranty details such as policy number, start and end dates, or any other related info.
* **claim_status** (3, text, not required): Enter the current claim status, e.g., open, resolved, or canceled.
* **customer_contact** (4, text, not required): Enter customer contact information or details.
* **customer_notes** (5, text, not required): Record any relevant customer notes.
* **warranty_notes** (6, text, not required): Enter notes related to warranty or claim resolutions.
* **claim_resolution** (7, text, not required): Enter the resolution method or details.
* **warranty_status** (8, text, not required): Enter the warranty status or details.
* **warranty_cancel** (9, text, not required): Enter cancellation info such as date and method.
* **warranty_renewal** (10, text, not required): Enter renewal info such as date and method.
* **claim_resolution_date** (11, text, not required): Enter the date of claim resolution.
* **claim_resolution_time** (12, text, not required): Enter the resolution time or details.
* **resolution_method** (13, text, not required): Enter the resolution method or details.
* **resolution_notes** (14, text, not required): Enter resolution notes.
* **claim_resolution_by** (15, text, not required): Enter who resolved the claim.
* **claim_resolution_status** (16, text, not required): Enter the claim resolution status.
* **claim_resolution_date_2** (17, text, not required): Enter the second claim resolution date.
* **claim_resolution_time_2** (18, text, not required): Enter the second claim resolution time.
* **resolution_notes_2** (19, text, not required): Enter the second resolution notes.
* **warranty_cancel_date** (20, text, not required): Enter the warranty cancellation date.
* **warranty_cancel_time** (21, text, not required): Enter the warranty cancellation time.
* **warranty_cancel_method** (22, text, not required): Enter warranty cancellation method.
* **warranty_cancel_status** (23, text, not required): Enter the warranty cancellation status.
* **warranty_cancel_notes** (24, text, not required): Enter warranty cancellation notes.
* **warranty_cancel_resolution_status** (25, text, not required): Enter the warranty cancellation resolution status.

## Tips
* Be sure to double-check your inputs for accuracy and consistency.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
* If unsure about a field, please ask for clarification or guidance.
