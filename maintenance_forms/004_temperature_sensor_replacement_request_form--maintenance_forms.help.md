# Temperature Sensor Replacement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for temperature sensor replacement requests. If you are requesting a replacement for a faulty temperature sensor, use this form to submit your request.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Temperature Sensor Replacement Request**:
    *   Please provide a brief description of the reason for the replacement request.
    *   You can include any relevant details, such as the sensor's current condition or any issues you are experiencing.
2.  **Customer Information**:
    *   Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
    *   This will help us to contact you regarding the status of your request.
3.  **Faulty Sensor Information**:
    *   Describe the faulty sensor, including its current condition and any issues you are experiencing.
    *   Be as detailed as possible to help our team better understand the problem.
4.  **Replacement Information**:
    *   Provide any relevant details about the replacement sensor, such as its serial number or model number.
    *   This will help our team locate the correct replacement.
5.  **Serial Number**:
    *   Enter the serial number of the faulty sensor, if known.
    *   This is a unique identifier for the sensor and will help us to locate the correct replacement.
6.  **Sensor Model**:
    *   Provide the model number of the sensor.
    *   This will help our team to ensure we provide the correct replacement.
7.  **Sensor Type**:
    *   Choose the type of sensor you need to replace.
    *   This will help us to ensure we provide the correct replacement.
8.  **Location**:
    *   Select the location of the sensor, as listed in the options provided.
    *   This will help us to track the sensor's installation and replacement.
9.  **Description**:
    *   Provide a detailed description of the sensor's condition or any issues you are experiencing.
    *   This will help our team to better understand the problem.
10. **Date of Request**:
    *   Enter the date you are requesting the replacement.
    *   This will help us to track the request's timeline.
11. **Arrangement**:
    *   Choose the desired arrangement for the replacement, as listed in the options provided.
    *   This will help us to ensure timely delivery of the replacement.
12. **Follow Up**:
    *   Choose if you would like to be followed up regarding the request.
    *   This will help us to ensure you are updated on the status of your request.
