# Maintenance Audit For Operators - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Maintenance Audit for Operators form is used to conduct regular maintenance audits on equipment. It helps ensure that the equipment is in good working condition and identifies any potential issues that require attention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of equipment being audited from the options provided.
2. Enter the equipment ID, name, and location.
3. Select the status of the equipment (e.g., Available, Maintenance Required, or Inactive).
4. Select the operator who conducted the audit from the options provided.
5. Enter the operator's email and phone number.
6. Specify the maintenance frequency (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly).
7. Enter the date of the last maintenance and the date of the next maintenance.
8. Provide any remarks or notes about the maintenance.
9. Enter your signature and the date of completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`): Select the type of equipment being audited from the options provided.
* **Equipment ID** (`equipment_id`): Enter the unique ID of the equipment.
* **Equipment Name** (`equipment_name`): Enter the name of the equipment.
* **Equipment Location** (`equipment_location`): Enter the location where the equipment is installed or used.
* **Equipment Status** (`equipment_status`): Select the status of the equipment (e.g., Available, Maintenance Required, or Inactive).
* **Operator Name** (`operator_name`): Select the operator who conducted the audit from the options provided.
* **Operator Email** (`operator_email`): Enter the operator's email address.
* **Operator Phone** (`operator_phone`): Enter the operator's phone number.
* **Maintenance Frequency** (`maintenance_frequency`): Specify the maintenance frequency (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly).
* **Maintenance Date** (`maintenance_date`): Enter the date of the last maintenance.
* **Next Maintenance Date** (`next_maintenance_date`): Enter the date of the next maintenance.
* **Remarks** (`remarks`): Provide any additional remarks or notes about the maintenance.
* **Operator Note** (`operator_note`): Enter any notes or comments from the operator.
* **Maintenance Note** (`maintenance_note`): Enter any notes or comments about the maintenance.
* **Signature** (`signature`): Enter your signature to confirm completion of the audit.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`): Enter the date of completion of the audit.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately for each piece of equipment being audited.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
* Save a copy of the completed form for reference and record-keeping purposes.
