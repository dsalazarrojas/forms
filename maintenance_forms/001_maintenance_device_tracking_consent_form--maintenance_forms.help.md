# Maintenance Device Tracking Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track the maintenance activities performed on devices/equipment within an organization, ensuring accurate records and facilitating communication between the maintenance team and other stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information about the device/equipment being maintained, such as its serial number, asset number, and equipment type.
2. Select the tracking method used during the maintenance activity (e.g., manual, automatic).
3. Choose the status of the tracking (e.g., active, inactive, pending).
4. Enter the date and time of the maintenance activity.
5. Provide any additional comments or notes about the maintenance activity.
6. Enter the email and phone number of the individual responsible for the maintenance activity.
7. Specify the location where the maintenance activity was performed.
8. Enter the model year and model of the device/equipment being maintained.
9. Specify the type of device/equipment being maintained.
10. Describe the condition of the device/equipment before and after maintenance.
11. Identify any issues or problems encountered during the maintenance activity.
12. Specify any actions taken during the maintenance activity.
13. Enter any additional comments about the maintenance activity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Device Tracking Consent** (`device_tracking_consent`): Enter the consent or agreement from the device/equipment owner to track and maintain the device/equipment.
* **Maintenance Device ID** (`maintenance_device_id`): Enter the unique identifier for the device/equipment being maintained.
* **Tracking Method** (`tracking_method`): Select the method used to track the device/equipment (e.g., manual, automatic).
* **Tracking Status** (`tracking_status`): Specify the status of the tracking (e.g., active, inactive, pending).
* **Maintenance Date** (`maintenance_date`): Enter the date when the maintenance activity was performed.
* **Maintenance Time** (`maintenance_time`): Enter the time when the maintenance activity was performed.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the maintenance activity.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter the email address of the individual responsible for the maintenance activity.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter the phone number of the individual responsible for the maintenance activity.
* **Maintenance Location** (`maintenance_location`): Enter the location where the maintenance activity was performed.
* **Device Model** (`device_model`): Enter the model year and model of the device/equipment being maintained.
* **Device Type** (`device_type`): Specify the type of device/equipment being maintained.
* **Device Condition** (`device_condition`): Describe the condition of the device/equipment before and after maintenance.
* **Device Problem** (`device_problem`): Identify any issues or problems encountered during the maintenance activity.
* **Action Taken** (`action_taken`): Specify any actions taken during the maintenance activity.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Enter any additional comments about the maintenance activity.
* **Serial Number** (`serial_number`): Enter the serial number of the device/equipment being maintained.
* **Asset Number** (`asset_number`): Enter the asset number of the device/equipment being maintained.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`): Specify the type of equipment being maintained.
* **Equipment Name** (`equipment_name`): Enter the name of the equipment being maintained.
* **Equipment Location** (`equipment_location`): Enter the location where the equipment is located.
* **Equipment Status** (`equipment_status`): Specify the status of the equipment (e.g., active, inactive, pending).
* **Equipment Condition** (`equipment_condition`): Describe the condition of the equipment before and after maintenance.
* **Equipment Problem** (`equipment_problem`): Identify any issues or problems encountered during the maintenance activity.

By following this guide, you should be able to accurately complete the form and ensure that all necessary information is recorded correctly.
