# Rental Unit Management Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rental Unit Management Form is designed to collect essential information about the management and maintenance of rental units. This form aims to provide a structured approach to tracking and documenting crucial data related to rental units, ensuring accurate and efficient management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and effectively, follow these steps:

* Start by reviewing the form carefully and ensuring you understand each section and its relevance to your task or requirement.
* Fill in the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
* For fields with options, choose the most suitable option that applies to your situation.
* Use the "Additional Comments" field for any further information or clarification that may be relevant to the rental unit's management.
* Ensure that the "Lease Expiration Date" and "Lease Start Date" fields are populated with accurate dates in the correct format.
* Double-check your responses to ensure accuracy and consistency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant Details**: This field is used to collect the contact and identification information of the tenant. Provide the tenant's name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Lease Timelines**: Describe the lease agreement's terms and conditions, including any key milestones or events.
* **Rent Records**: Log and document all rent payments, including dates and amounts.
* **Additional Comments**: Use this field for any additional information or clarification that may be relevant to the rental unit's management.
* **Tenant Contact**: Indicate whether the tenant has provided contact information or not.
* **Rent Frequency**: Enter the frequency of rent payments (e.g., weekly, monthly, etc.).
* **Rent Amount**: Enter the amount of the rent payment.
* **Lease Expiration Date**: Enter the date when the lease agreement expires.
* **Lease Start Date**: Enter the date when the lease agreement starts.
* **Rent Frequency Label**: Use this field to label the rent frequency (e.g., "Monthly").
