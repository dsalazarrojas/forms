# Damage Report For Common Areas - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report damages or issues in shared spaces such as common areas within a building or community. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Shared Space Damage Report page (1) with a brief description of the issue.
2. Enter the location (2) where the issue was found.
3. Provide a detailed description of the damage (3) including any relevant details such as the extent of the damage.
4. Upload any relevant photos (4, 5) of the damage.
5. Enter the date (6) when the issue occurred or was noticed.
6. Enter the time (7) when the issue occurred or was noticed.
7. Enter the contact information (8, 9, 10) of the person who reported the issue, including their name, phone number, and email (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shared Space Damage Report (1)**: Enter a brief description of the issue found in the shared space.
* **Space Location (2)**: Enter the exact location of the issue, including the room number or a descriptive address.
* **Damage Description (3)**: Provide a detailed description of the damage or issue, including any relevant details such as the extent of the damage.
* **Photo 1 (4)**: Upload any relevant photos of the damage or issue.
* **Photo 2 (5)**: Upload any additional photos that may help maintenance teams understand the issue.
* **Damage Date (6)**: Enter the date when the issue occurred or was noticed.
* **Damage Time (7)**: Enter the time when the issue occurred or was noticed.
* **Contact Person (8)**: Enter the name of the person who reported the issue.
* **Phone Number (9)**: Enter the contact phone number of the person who reported the issue.
* **Email (10)**: Enter the contact email of the person who reported the issue (optional).
