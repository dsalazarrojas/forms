# Lockout Tagout Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lockout Tagout Safety Checklist is a form designed to ensure that critical systems, equipment, and machines are properly locked out and tagged out for safety inspection. This form is used to record the details of a lockout/tagout procedure, including the lockout/tagout status, location, and any issues or actions taken.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Lockout Tagout Options**: Select the type of lockout/tagout procedure you are performing. Options include "Lockout" and "Tagout".
2. **Facility**: Choose the location where the lockout/tagout is being performed. Options are "Electrical Room", "Piping System", and "Pump Room".
3. **Lockout Tagout Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the lockout/tagout inspection using the "YYYY-MM-dd" format.
4. **Lockout Tagout Issues**: If there are any issues with the lockout/tagout procedure, describe them in the text field.
5. **Lockout Tagout Actions**: Describe any actions taken to address the issues or complete the lockout/tagout procedure.
6. **Facility Inspector**: Select the person performing the lockout/tagout inspection. Options are "Lockout Tagout Inspector 1", "Facilities Team Member 1", and "Other".
7. **Facility Inspector Signature**: Sign and date the lockout/tagout inspection.
8. **Lockout Tagout Comments**: Add any additional comments regarding the lockout/tagout procedure.
9. **Issue Date**: If an issue is identified, enter the date it was discovered.
10. **Issue Type**: Select the type of issue identified. Options are "Safety", "Maintenance", and "Other".
11. **Issue Description**: Describe the issue in detail.
12. **Issue Resolution**: Describe the actions taken to resolve the issue.
13. **Lockout Tagout Comments**: Add any additional comments regarding the lockout/tagout procedure.
14. **Lockout Tagout Inspection Frequency**: Select the frequency of the lockout/tagout inspection. Options are "Daily", "Weekly", "Monthly", "Quarterly", and "Other".
15. **Lockout Tagout Safety Checklist Frequency**: Select the frequency of the lockout/tagout safety checklist completion. Options are "After each lockout/tagout", "Every 30 days", "Every 60 days", "Every 90 days", "Every 120 days", and "Other".
16. **Lockout Tagout Safety Checklist Completed**: Enter the date the safety checklist was completed, if applicable.
17. **Lockout Tagout Safety Checklist Reviewed**: Enter the date the safety checklist was reviewed, if applicable.
18. **Facility Manager Reviewed**: Enter the date the facility manager reviewed the safety checklist, if applicable.
19. **Supervisor Reviewed**: Enter the date the supervisor reviewed the safety checklist, if applicable.
20. **Reviewer Name**: Enter the name of the person reviewing the safety checklist.
21. **Reviewer Signature**: Sign and date the review of the safety checklist.
22. **Facility Inspector Reviewer Comments**: Add any comments from the facility inspector regarding the review.
23. **Supervisor Reviewer Comments**: Add any comments from the supervisor regarding the review.
24. **Facility Inspector Reviewer Signature**: Sign and date the review of the safety checklist by the facility inspector, if applicable.
25. **Supervisor Reviewer Signature**: Sign and date the review of the safety checklist by the supervisor, if applicable.
