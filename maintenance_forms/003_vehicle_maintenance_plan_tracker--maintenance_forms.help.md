# Vehicle Maintenance Plan Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Maintenance Plan Tracker form is designed to help users track and manage the maintenance plans for a fleet of vehicles. It allows users to create a maintenance schedule for each vehicle, taking into account its type, service history, and other relevant factors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of vehicle you want to track.
2. Enter the vehicle's registration number.
3. Enter the service history, if applicable.
4. Choose the service frequency (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly, etc.).
5. Enter the next service date, if applicable.
6. Enter the odometer reading, if applicable.
7. Set the service cost, if applicable.
8. Choose the maintenance tasks to be performed on the vehicle (e.g., Oil Change, Tire Rotation, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Type**: Enter the type of vehicle you want to track (e.g., Car, Truck, Motorcycle, etc.).
* **Vehicle Registration Number**: Enter the vehicle's registration number.
* **Service History**: Briefly describe the service history of the vehicle (if applicable).
* **Service Frequency**: Choose the service frequency for the vehicle (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly, etc.).
* **Next Service Date**: Enter the next service date for the vehicle (if applicable).
* **Odometer Reading**: Enter the current odometer reading of the vehicle (if applicable).
* **Service Cost**: Enter the service cost for the vehicle (if applicable).
* **Vehicle Maintenance Plan**: Select all the maintenance tasks to be performed on the vehicle (e.g., Oil Change, Tire Rotation, etc.).

## Tips

* Make sure to choose the correct service frequency and next service date for the vehicle.
* Select all the maintenance tasks that are applicable to the vehicle.
* Review and update the maintenance plan regularly to ensure the vehicle is properly maintained.
* Note: The service history field is only relevant if you want to track the vehicle's past service history.
* Note: The odometer reading field is only relevant if you want to track the vehicle's mileage.
* Note: The service cost field is only relevant if you want to track the vehicle's maintenance cost.
* Note: The maintenance tasks field is only relevant if you want to select specific maintenance tasks for the vehicle.
