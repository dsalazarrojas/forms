# Rail Temperature Monitoring Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rail Temperature Monitoring Log form is used to track temperature data from rail monitors. It is essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the data collected is reliable and useful for maintenance and monitoring activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct option for "Rail Temperature Monitors" to indicate whether any monitors were found on the rail.
2. Enter the number of monitors installed on the rail in the "Monitors Installed" field.
3. Record the date and time of the readings were taken in the "Readings Taken" field.
4. Enter any observation notes regarding the rail monitors in the "Observation Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Rail Temperature Monitors** (`rail_temperature_monitors`, select_multiple, required: false): Check all the rail temperature monitors that were found on the rail during the journey or inspection. You can select multiple monitors if needed.
* **Monitors Installed** (`rail_monitors_installed`, text, required: false): Enter the number of monitors installed on the rail.
* **Readings Taken** (`readings_taken`, date, required: false): Record the date and time of the readings were taken.
* **Observation Notes** (`observation_notes`, note, required: false): Enter any observation notes regarding the rail monitors, such as any irregularities or issues found during the journey or inspection.

## Tips
- Be thorough and accurate when completing this form.
- Review the rail monitors before completing this form to ensure that you are reporting accurate data.
- Complete this form for each journey or inspection, and do not reuse or duplicate entries.
