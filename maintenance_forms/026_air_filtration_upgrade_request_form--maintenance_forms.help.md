# Air Filtration Upgrade Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request upgrades to air filtration systems in facilities. It helps the maintenance team to assess and address your needs efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure that you fill out one form per facility.
3. Be as detailed as possible, especially when describing the current issues with your system and the areas that need upgrade.
4. If unsure about any field, don't hesitate to ask for help or clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requestor Name** (`requestor_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as the person requesting the upgrade.
* **Email Address** (`requestor_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`requestor_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): If you're representing a department, enter its name. If not, leave this field blank.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you're requesting the upgrade.
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the facility where the upgrade is needed.
* **Facility Address** (`facility_address`, text, required): Enter the full address of the facility.
* **Current System Type** (`current_system_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of your current air filtration system (e.g., Standard fiberglass, HEPA system, etc.).
* **Current System Age** (`current_system_age`, select_one, required): Choose the age of your current system.
* **Reason for Upgrade** (`upgrade_reason`, select_one, required): Choose the primary reason for the upgrade (e.g., Improved air quality needed, Health concerns, etc.).
* **Health Concerns** (`health_concerns`, select_multiple, optional): Select all health concerns that apply to you or the facility (e.g., Allergies, Asthma, etc.).
* **Desired Upgrade Type** (`desired_upgrade`, select_multiple, required): Choose the types of upgrades you desire (e.g., HEPA filtration, Activated carbon, etc.).
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency_level`, select_one, required): Choose the urgency level for the upgrade (e.g., Emergency, High, Moderate, etc.).
* **Budget Available** (`budget_available`, select_one, required): Indicate if budget for the upgrade is allocated (e.g., Yes, Budget request pending, etc.).
* **Estimated Budget Range** (`estimated_budget`, select_one, optional): If you know your budget, choose the range (e.g., Under $5,000, etc.).
* **Affected Area** (`affected_area`, text, required): Describe the areas that need upgrade.
* **Approximate Square Footage** (`square_footage`, select_one, optional): Choose the approximate size of the area that needs upgrade.
* **Occupancy Level** (`occupancy_level`, select_one, required): Choose the number of people that occupy the area.
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, text, optional): Enter any special requirements or needs for the upgrade.
* **Current Issues** (`current_issues`, text, required): Describe the problems with your current system.
* **Preferred Completion Timeline** (`preferred_timeline`, select_one, required): Choose when you prefer the work to be completed (e.g., ASAP, Within 2 weeks, etc.).
* **System Downtime Acceptable** (`downtime_acceptable`, select_one, required): Indicate if the system can be shut down during business hours.
* **On-site Assessment Needed** (`assessment_needed`, select_one, required): Indicate if an on-site assessment is required before proceeding with the upgrade.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional information that might be helpful for the maintenance team.
* **Requestor Signature** (`requestor_signature`, text, required): Enter a digital signature to confirm your request.
