# Laptop Maintenance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to submit a maintenance request for your laptop. Please fill it out to report any issues or request repairs, and our maintenance team will get in touch with you to schedule a maintenance slot.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Laptop Maintenance Request** (`laptop_maintenance_request`): Write a brief description of the issue with your laptop. This could be a hardware problem, software issue, or just a general concern.
2.  **Request Type** (`laptop_maintenance_request_form_type`): Select the type of maintenance request you're making:
    *   Request for repair: If your laptop has a hardware or software problem that needs fixing.
    *   Request for maintenance: If you need regular maintenance or updates performed on your laptop.
    *   Other: If it doesn't fit into either of the above categories.
3.  **Description** (`laptop_maintenance_request_description`): Provide a brief description of the maintenance request. Be as detailed as possible to help the maintenance team understand the problem.
4.  **Priority** (`laptop_maintenance_request_priority`): Indicate the priority level of your maintenance request:
    *   High: Urgent issues that need immediate attention.
    *   Medium: Issues that require attention within the next few days.
    *   Low: Less urgent issues that can wait for a while.
5.  **Scheduled Date** (`laptop_maintenance_request_scheduled_date`): If you know when you'd like to schedule the maintenance, please enter the date. Otherwise, leave this field blank and our maintenance team will contact you to schedule a suitable time.
6.  **Scheduled Time** (`laptop_maintenance_request_scheduled_time`): If you know when you'd like to schedule the maintenance, please enter the time. Otherwise, leave this field blank and our team will contact you to schedule a suitable time.
7.  **Who** (`laptop_maintenance_request_who`): Choose who the maintenance request is for:
    *   Admin: Maintenance request for an administrator.
    *   IT: Maintenance request for the IT department.
    *   Team Member: Maintenance request for a team member.
    *   Customer: Maintenance request for a customer.
8.  **Comments** (`laptop_maintenance_request_comments`): If you have any additional comments or information about the maintenance request, please enter them here.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out the form thoroughly and accurately.
*   If you're unsure about any of the fields or have questions, don't hesitate to reach out to the maintenance team for clarification.
*   If you need to cancel or change your maintenance request, please contact the maintenance team as soon as possible to avoid delays.

Note: This form was created using AI and is intended to help with laptop maintenance requests. Please use it for internal use only.
