# Leak Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The leak intake form is a tool used to report and gather essential information about a leak in a building or facility. This information will help the maintenance team to identify and resolve the issue.

## How To Complete This Form
This guide will walk you through each field of the leak intake form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Leak Form** (`leak_form`, text, optional): Please provide a detailed description of the leak.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the leak (e.g., building, room, floor, etc.).
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date the leak was discovered or occurred.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time the leak was discovered or occurred.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Add any additional information about the leak, such as its location, size, or any other relevant details.
* **Photo** (`photo`, text, optional): Attach any photos or images related to the leak that can help the maintenance team in identifying the issue.
* **Route to** (`route_to`, select_one, required): Please select the person responsible for addressing the leak:
  + **Facility Manager**
  + **Property Manager**
  + **Maintenance Team**
* **Follow-up** (`follow-up`, date, optional): If you would like to schedule a follow-up meeting or appointment to discuss the leak further, enter the date and time for it.
