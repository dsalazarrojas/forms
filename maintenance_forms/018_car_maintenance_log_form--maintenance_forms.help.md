The Car Maintenance Log Form is used to record and track maintenance activities performed on a vehicle. It's essential for fleet management, vehicle owners, and maintenance teams to keep track of service history, upcoming maintenance schedules, and any issues that may arise. This form can be used for both regular and critical vehicle maintenance tasks. 

Before starting to fill out this form, please ensure that you are using it for the correct vehicle and purpose. If you have already completed a log entry for this vehicle, please ensure you are not duplicating the entry. 

This guide will walk you through each field and explain what information is required and what it's used for.

## Purpose
The Car Maintenance Log Form is designed to gather information about a vehicle's maintenance history. It helps to track maintenance activities, service dates, mileage, and any issues that may have been encountered.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct vehicle information, including year, make, and model.
2. Enter the current mileage of the vehicle.
3. Fill in the service date and type of service performed.
4. Select the category of maintenance (routine, preventive, corrective, or emergency).
5. Record the mileage at the time of service.
6. Specify who performed the service.
7. Enter the total cost of the service.
8. Check if warranty coverage was used.
9. Schedule the next service due date and mileage.
10. Check for any issues found during the service.
11. Describe any issues that were found.
12. Enter any recommended future services.
13. Check if a receipt is attached.
14. Verify that fluid levels were checked and topped up.
15. List any fluids added.
16. Check the current condition of the tires and brakes.
17. Enter any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`): Enter the year of the vehicle. This helps to identify the vehicle's age and ensures that the correct information is being recorded.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`): Enter the manufacturer of the vehicle. This helps to identify the vehicle's make and model.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`): Enter the model of the vehicle. This helps to identify the vehicle's specific model.
* **Vehicle VIN** (`vehicle_vin`): Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) of the vehicle. This helps to uniquely identify the vehicle.
* **License Plate** (`license_plate`): Enter the license plate number of the vehicle. This helps to identify the vehicle's registration.
* **Current Mileage** (`current_mileage`): Enter the current odometer reading of the vehicle. This helps to track the vehicle's mileage history.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`): Enter the name of the vehicle's owner. This helps to identify the vehicle's ownership.
* **Maintenance Record** (`maintenance_record`): Enter any maintenance notes or details about the vehicle. This helps to keep a record of past services and any issues that may have been encountered.
* **Service Date** (`service_date`): Enter the date the service was performed. This helps to track the vehicle's maintenance schedule.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`): Select the type of service performed, such as oil change, tire rotation, or brake service.
* **Service Category** (`service_category`): Select the category of maintenance, such as routine, preventive, corrective, or emergency.
* **Mileage at Service** (`mileage_at_service`): Enter the odometer reading at the time of service.
* **Service Provider** (`service_provider`): Select who performed the service, such as a dealership, independent shop, or mobile service.
* **Service Provider Name** (`service_provider_name`): Enter the name of the service provider.
* **Service Location** (`service_location`): Enter the city and state where the service was performed.
* **Parts Replaced** (`parts_replaced`): List any parts that were replaced during the service.
* **Parts Cost** (`parts_cost`): Enter the cost of the parts replaced.
* **Labor Cost** (`labor_cost`): Enter the cost of labor.
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`): Enter the total cost of the service, including parts and labor.
* **Warranty Coverage** (`warranty_coverage`): Check if warranty coverage was used.
* **Next Service Due** (`next_service_due`): Enter the date and mileage for the next service due.
* **Next Service Mileage** (`next_service_mileage`): Enter the mileage for the next service due.
* **Service Notes** (`service_notes`): Enter any additional notes or comments from the service.
* **Issues Found** (`issues_found`): Check if any issues were found during the service.
* **Issues Description** (`issues_description`): Describe any issues that were found.
* **Technician Recommendations** (`technician_recommendations`): Enter any recommended future services.
* **Receipt Attached** (`receipt_attached`): Check if the receipt is attached.
* **Fluid Levels Checked** (`fluid_levels_checked`): Check if fluid levels were checked.
* **Fluids Topped Up** (`fluids_toped_up`): Select which fluids were added.
* **Tire Condition** (`tire_condition`): Check the condition of the tires.
* **Tire Pressure** (`tire_pressure`): Enter the tire pressure reading.
* **Brake Condition** (`brake_condition`): Check the condition of the brakes.
* **Battery Condition** (`battery_condition`): Check the condition of the battery.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Enter any additional comments or notes.

This form will help you keep accurate and comprehensive records of your vehicle's maintenance history. Remember to always keep the form updated and use it for future reference. 

Please note that some fields are optional or not required, but they are all relevant to maintaining accurate and complete records.
