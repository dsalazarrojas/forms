# Hostel Repair And Maintenance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to be filled by hostel staff when reporting a maintenance or repair issue. Its purpose is to gather information that helps to identify the problem, contact the responsible person, and facilitate prompt action to resolve the issue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the contact person's details in the "Contact Person" field.
2. Enter the contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
3. Provide the contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
4. Specify the location where the issue is reported in the "Location" field.
5. Provide a brief description of the location in the "Location Description" field.
6. Describe the maintenance or repair issue in the "Issue Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Hostel Repair Form** (`hostel_repair_form`, text, required/optional): This is the title of the form, which should not be changed.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person who reported the issue.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact phone number of the person who reported the issue.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the contact email address of the person who reported the issue.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required/optional): Specify the location where the issue is reported. This could be a room number or a specific section of the hostel.
* **Location Description** (`location_description`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the location to help with addressing the issue.
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, text, required/optional): Describe the maintenance or repair issue in as much detail as possible.
