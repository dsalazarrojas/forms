# Vehicle Maintenance Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Vehicle Maintenance Checklist Form is a tool used to document vehicle maintenance activities, ensuring consistency and accuracy in tracking and recording maintenance tasks, dates, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the maintenance tasks that need to be performed for the vehicle.
2. Fill in the vehicle maintenance date (if applicable).
3. Choose the assigned tool for the maintenance tasks.
4. Indicate the vehicle's status and type.
5. Optionally, enter any repair remarks or notes.
6. Review and submit the form for accurate and up-to-date maintenance records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Maintenance Tasks** (`maintenance_tasks`, select_multiple, required): Select all the maintenance tasks that need to be performed for the vehicle, such as oils and lubricants, tires, battery, etc.
* **Vehicle Maintenance Date** (`vehicle_maintenance_date`, date, optional): The date of the maintenance activity (if applicable).
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required): Select the tool or equipment used for the maintenance task, such as "chatjimmy" or "Other".
* **Vehicle Repair Remarks** (`vehicle_repair_remarks`, text, optional): Enter any remarks or notes about the repair or maintenance activity.
* **Vehicle Maintenance Schedule** (`vehicle_maintenance_schedule`, date, optional): The scheduled date for the next maintenance activity (if applicable).
* **Vehicle Status** (`vehicle_status`, select_one, required): Choose the vehicle's status, either "In-Service" or "Out-Of-Service".
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of vehicle, either "Car" or "Truck".
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, optional): Enter the year of the vehicle (if applicable).
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, optional): Enter the make of the vehicle (if applicable).
