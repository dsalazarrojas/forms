# Service History Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to record the service history of a vehicle, including details about the maintenance type, cost, and next service date. This information is vital for technicians to perform their tasks efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the correct **vehicle** type (Car, Truck, or Motorcycle) from the options provided.
2. Enter a detailed **description** of the maintenance performed.
3. Record the **date** of the maintenance service.
4. Enter the **time** of the maintenance service.
5. Select the **type of maintenance** (Preventative or Corrective).
6. Enter the **cost** of the maintenance service (if applicable).
7. Provide any additional **notes** about the service.
8. Select the **technician** who performed the service (Jimmy, Jill, or Bob).
9. Choose the **status** of the service (Complete, In Progress, or Cancelled).
10. Record the **next service date**.
11. Record the **last service date**.
12. Enter the **last service description** (if applicable).
13. Enter the **last service miles**.
14. Enter the **last service cost**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle** (Required): Select the type of vehicle (Car, Truck, or Motorcycle) that the maintenance service was performed on.
* **Description** (Required): Enter a detailed description of the maintenance performed, such as oil change, tire rotation, etc.
* **Date** (Required): Record the date the maintenance service was performed.
* **Time** (Required): Record the time the maintenance service was performed.
* **Maintenance Type** (Required): Select the type of maintenance (Preventative or Corrective).
* **Cost** (Optional): Enter the cost of the maintenance service (if applicable).
* **Notes** (Optional): Provide any additional notes about the service.
* **Technician** (Required): Select the technician who performed the service (Jimmy, Jill, or Bob).
* **Status** (Required): Choose the status of the service (Complete, In Progress, or Cancelled).
* **Next Service Date** (Required): Record the date of the next scheduled service.
* **Last Service Date** (Required): Record the date of the last maintenance service.
* **Last Service Description** (Optional): Enter a description of the last maintenance service (if applicable).
* **Last Service Miles** (Required): Record the mileage of the vehicle at the last service.
* **Last Service Cost** (Required): Record the cost of the last maintenance service.
