# Spa Repair Warranty Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spa Repair Warranty Registration form is designed to collect information about a customer's spa repair request. It gathers details about the customer, the spa, and the repair warranty.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Spa Repair Warranty Registration Form**:
	* This is the first field, where you can enter the title of the repair warranty registration.
2. **Customer Details**:
	* Enter the customer's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
3. **Spa Details**:
	* Provide details about the spa being repaired, such as its address, description, and any other relevant information.
4. **Repair Warranty**:
	* Describe the repair warranty, including its duration and any specific terms or conditions.
5. **Customer Contact Details**:
	* Enter the customer's contact information, including phone number and email, to ensure they can be reached for updates or inquiries.

## Tips
