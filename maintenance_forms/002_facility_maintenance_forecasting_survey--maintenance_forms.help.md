## Facility Maintenance Forecasting Survey - Help Guide
### Purpose
The Facility Maintenance Forecasting Survey is a tool used to collect data from facility owners or managers to forecast maintenance requirements and ensure the effective management of maintenance activities.

### How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the correct facility for which you want to report the maintenance needs.
2.  Provide a detailed description of the current conditions of the facility's assets.
3.  Specify the future maintenance needs of the facility, including any upcoming repairs, upgrades, or replacements.
4.  Enter the relevant contact information of the person responsible for maintenance.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Details (1)** (`Facility Details`, `text`, required): This field is where you will enter the name and identification of the facility. Please provide a brief description of the facility, including its location and any other relevant details.
* **Asset Conditions (2)** (`Asset Conditions`, `text`, required): In this field, describe the current conditions of the facility's assets, such as equipment, machinery, and buildings. This information will help identify potential issues that require maintenance.
* **Future Maintenance Needs (3)** (`Future Maintenance Needs`, `text`, required): Specify the future maintenance needs of the facility, including any upcoming repairs, upgrades, or replacements of assets.
* **Contact Information (4)** (`Contact Information`, `text`, optional): If necessary, enter the contact information of the person responsible for maintenance, including their name, phone number, and email address.

### Tips
*   Please make sure to answer all required fields (Facility Details, Asset Conditions, and Future Maintenance Needs).
*   Be as detailed as possible in describing the current conditions and future needs of the facility's assets.
*   If you are unsure about any field or information, please ask your maintenance team or supervisor for guidance.
*   You can save your progress and come back to complete the form later.
