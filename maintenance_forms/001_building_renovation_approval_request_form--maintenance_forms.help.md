# Building Renovation Approval Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for submitting a request for building renovation. It gathers information from the requestor and details about the proposed renovation project. The goal is to ensure that the renovation project aligns with the organization's policies and guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Read the form carefully and understand the requirements before submitting your request.
*   Enter your contact information (full name, department, email address, and phone number).
*   Provide a detailed description of the proposed renovation project.
*   Specify which building requires the renovation, the floor level or area, and the estimated start and completion dates.
*   Estimate the total cost of the project and identify the funding source.
*   Indicate if your supervisor has approved this request.
*   Specify if safety reviews are required for the project.
*   Enter any additional comments or information relevant to the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, **required**): Enter your full legal name.
*   **Department** (`department`, select_one, **required**): Select the department you belong to (Facilities, Administration, Operations, Human Resources, Finance, or IT).
*   **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, **required**): Enter your work email address.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, **required**): Enter your best contact number.
*   **Renovation Details** (`renovation_details`, note, **required**): Describe the renovation project in detail.
*   **Building Name** (`building_name`, text, **required**): Specify which building requires renovation.
*   **Floor Level** (`floor_level`, text, **required**): Enter the floor level or area where the renovation will take place.
*   **Renovation Type** (`renovation_type`, select_one, **required**): Select the type of renovation (Structural Changes, Cosmetic Updates, Electrical Systems, Plumbing Systems, HVAC Upgrades, Accessibility Improvements, or Other).
*   **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, **required**): Enter a detailed description of the proposed renovation project.
*   **Estimated Start Date** (`estimated_start_date`, date, **required**): Enter the estimated start date when work should begin.
*   **Estimated Completion Date** (`estimated_completion_date`, date, **required**): Enter the expected completion date.
*   **Budget Information** (`budget_information`, note, **required**): Enter cost estimates and funding information.
*   **Estimated Cost** (`estimated_cost`, number, **required**): Enter the total estimated cost of the project.
*   **Funding Source** (`funding_source`, select_one, **required**): Select where funding will come from (Capital Budget, Operating Budget, Department Budget, External Grant, or Other).
*   **Approvals** (`approvals`, note, **required**): List any required approvals for the project.
*   **Supervisor Approval Required** (`supervisor_approval`, select_one, **required**): Indicate if your supervisor has approved this request.
*   **Safety Review Required** (`safety_review_required`, select_one, **required**): Indicate if safety review is required for the project.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, **optional**): Enter any additional information or comments you wish to share about the project.
