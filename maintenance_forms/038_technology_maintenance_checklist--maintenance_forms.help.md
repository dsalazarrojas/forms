# Technology Maintenance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Technology Maintenance Checklist form is designed to collect information and feedback for technology maintenance, likely for an organization or company to track and manage their technology-related tasks and issues.

## How To Complete This Form
1. The form consists of three sections:
	* **Page 1** (`tech_maintenance_checklist_page_1`): This section is the main content area where you can enter your information and feedback.
	* **Page 2** (`tech_maintenance_checklist_page_2`): This is the second section where you can add additional comments or details.
	* **Notes** (`notes`): This is a dedicated field for any additional comments or notes you would like to make.
2. Simply type your response in the relevant section, and click "Submit" to save your input.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Page 1 (tech_maintenance_checklist_page_1)**: This is the main field where you can enter your maintenance information.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Purpose: Enter your maintenance information, such as tasks, issues, or progress.
* **Page 2 (tech_maintenance_checklist_page_2)**: This is the second field where you can add additional comments or details.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Purpose: Enter any additional comments or details related to your maintenance activities.
* **Notes (notes)**: This is a dedicated field for any additional comments or notes.
	+ Type: Note
	+ Purpose: Add any additional comments or notes you would like to make.
