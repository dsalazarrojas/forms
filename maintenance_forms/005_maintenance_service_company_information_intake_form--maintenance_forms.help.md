# Maintenance Service Company Information Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about maintenance service companies.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you are not already in our maintenance service company database.
2. Fill out the form with the requested information, following the instructions for each field.
3. Submit the form to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Vendor Name** (Vendor Name, text, required/optional): This is the name of your company. Please enter the name of your company as it appears on your business cards or website.
- **Address** (Address, text, required/optional): Enter the physical address of your company's main office. This can be a street address or a PO box.
- **Phone** (Phone, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the company's main office. This can be a standard 10-digit number or a full international number with country code.
- **Email** (Email, email, required/optional): Enter the primary email address of your company's main office. This should be a company-wide email address that receives general inquiries.
- **Fax** (Fax, text, required/optional): Enter the fax number of the company's main office. This can be a standard 10-digit number or a full international number with country code.
- **Contact Name** (Contact Name, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person in charge of communications with our organization.
- **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the person in charge of communications with our organization.
- **Contact Email** (Contact Email, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the person in charge of communications with our organization.
- **Alternate Contact Phone** (Alternate Contact Phone, text, required/optional): Enter an alternate phone number of the person in charge of communications with our organization.
- **Alternate Contact Email** (Alternate Contact Email, email, required/optional): Enter an alternate email address of the person in charge of communications with our organization.

## Tips
- Make sure to use a format that is easy to read and understand.
- Use a standard font and font size for better legibility.
- Proofread your submission before submitting it to ensure that all information is correct and accurate.
