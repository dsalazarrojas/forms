# Refrigerated Trailer Scheduled Maintenance Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to guide technicians in conducting a scheduled maintenance check on refrigerated trailers. The form will help ensure that all critical systems are checked and any necessary maintenance is performed to keep the trailers in good working condition.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Enter Trailer Number**: Enter the correct trailer number to ensure the form is associated with the correct trailer.
2. **Engine Oil Level**: Check the engine oil level and report it on the form.
3. **Oil Type**: Select the type of oil used for the engine from the options provided.
4. **Air Filter Condition**: Check the air filter condition and select from the options provided (Clean, Dirty, or Missing).
5. **Air Filter Last Replaced**: If the air filter is dirty or missing, report the date it was last replaced. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
6. **Engine Oil Last Replaced**: Report the date the engine oil was last replaced. If the oil has never been replaced, leave this field blank.
7. **Battery Condition**: Check the condition of the trailer battery and select from the options (Good, Bad, or Unknown).
8. **Brake Pads Condition**: Check the condition of the brake pads and select from the options (Good, Bad, or Unknown).
9. **Tire Condition**: Check the condition of the trailer tires and select from the options (Good, Bad, or Unknown).
10. **Suspension Condition**: Check the condition of the trailer suspension and select from the options (Good, Bad, or Unknown).
11. **Electrical Condition**: Check the condition of the trailer electrical system and select from the options (Good, Bad, or Unknown).
12. **Refrigerated System Condition**: Check the condition of the refrigerated system and select from the options (Good, Bad, or Unknown).
13. **Trailer Temperature**: Record the trailer temperature.
14. **Refrigerated System Temperature**: Record the refrigerated system temperature.
15. **Engine Oil Type**: Select the type of engine oil used from the options provided.
16. **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the trailer.
17. **Technician Name**: Enter the name of the technician performing the maintenance.
18. **Technician ID**: Enter the ID of the technician performing the maintenance.
19. **Date Of Service**: Enter the date of the maintenance service.
20. **Time Of Service**: Enter the time of the maintenance service.
21. **Signature**: Sign the form to confirm the maintenance was performed.
22. **Confirm Trailer Number**: Confirm that the trailer number entered is correct.
23. **Confirm Refrigerated System**: Confirm the condition of the refrigerated system.
24. **Confirm Electrical**: Confirm the condition of the electrical system.
25. **Confirm Trailer Condition**: Confirm the overall condition of the trailer.
26. **Confirm Maintenance Required**: Confirm if any maintenance is required.
