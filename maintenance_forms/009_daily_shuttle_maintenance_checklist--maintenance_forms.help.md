# Daily Shuttle Maintenance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Shuttle Maintenance Checklist is a tool used to record the status of the shuttle system before and after each trip, enabling maintenance teams to identify and address potential issues, ensuring the safety and efficiency of the shuttle operation.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

*   Ensure you have access to the form on your device.
*   Review each page of the form carefully.
*   Select the appropriate options for each field based on your observations and findings.
*   Enter any recorded issues or concerns in the `Recorded issues` field.
*   Enter your email address in the `Email` field to ensure that any issues recorded can be sent to the relevant personnel.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Pre-trip inspections** (`pre_trip_inspections`, select_multiple, required: false): Pre-trip inspections are performed before the shuttle departs. Select all that apply to indicate any issues that were observed or found during this time.
*   **Recorded issues** (`recorded_issues`, text, required: false): Enter any issues or concerns that were recorded during or after the trip. This could include mechanical issues, customer complaints, or other notable incidents.
*   **Readiness to operate** (`readiness_to_operate`, select_one, required: false): Evaluate the shuttle's readiness to operate based on your observations. Choose "Good" if the shuttle is ready, "Fair" if there are some concerns, or select "No" if it's not ready.
*   **Fleet** (`fleet`, select_one, required: false): Indicate whether the shuttle is part of the fleet or not.
*   **Campus** (`campus`, select_one, required: false): Indicate which campus the shuttle is operating on.
*   **Hotel** (`hotel`, select_one, required: false): Indicate whether the shuttle will be operating to or from a hotel during this trip.
*   **Shuttle service** (`shuttle_service`, select_one, required: false): Indicate whether the shuttle will be providing service during this trip.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about this trip.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so that any issues recorded can be sent to the relevant personnel.

## Tips
*   Always complete the form as soon as possible after each trip, while the information is fresh in your mind.
*   Be as detailed as possible when recording issues or concerns.
*   Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it, to ensure all fields are filled out correctly.
*   If unsure about any field, seek guidance from a supervisor or colleague.
