# Beverage Cooler Maintenance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beverage Cooler Maintenance Request Form is a tool used to report issues with your beverage cooler. Please fill out this form with as much detail as possible so that our maintenance team can efficiently diagnose and repair the problem.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and detailed information.
2. Be as specific as possible when describing the problem in the issue description field.
3. Choose the correct type of cooler and urgency level based on your needs.
4. Submit the form after you have filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (requester_name): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (requester_email): Enter your email address where you will receive updates.
* **Phone Number** (requester_phone): Enter your phone number for urgent communication.
* **Company or Store Name** (company_name): Enter your business name or location.
* **Location Address** (location_address): Enter the full address where the cooler is located.
* **City** (city): Enter the city where service is needed.
* **State** (state): Enter the state where service is needed.
* **ZIP Code** (postal_code): Enter the postal code for the service location.
* **Type of Cooler** (cooler_type): Select the type of cooler that needs service (e.g. Reach-In Refrigerator, Walk-In Cooler, Display Cooler, etc.).
* **Cooler Brand** (cooler_brand): If available, enter the brand of the cooler (optional).
* **Model Number** (model_number): If available, enter the model number of the cooler (optional).
* **Serial Number** (serial_number): If available, enter the serial number of the cooler (optional).
* **Type of Issue** (issue_type): Select the type of issue the cooler is experiencing (e.g. Not Cooling, Not Cooling Enough, etc.).
* **Detailed Issue Description** (issue_description): Describe the problem in as much detail as possible.
* **When Did the Issue Start** (issue_started): Enter the date when you first noticed the problem.
* **Urgency Level** (urgency_level): Choose how urgent you think the repair is (e.g. Emergency - Immediate Attention Needed, High Priority - Within 24 Hours, etc.).
* **Preferred Service Date** (preferred_service_date): Enter the date when you would like service to be performed.
* **Preferred Service Time** (preferred_service_time): Choose a time that works best for you (optional).
* **Access Instructions** (access_instructions): Provide any special instructions for the technician's access.
* **Additional Comments** (additional_comments): Add any other information about the issue.
* **Have There Been Previous Repairs** (previous_repairs): Describe any recent repairs or maintenance done on the cooler (optional).
* **Additional Comments** (additional_comments): Any other information about the issue.
