# refrigerator_maintenance_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the maintenance status of a refrigerator. It helps users track and report issues, providing valuable data for maintenance planning and decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options for each field based on your observations or inspections.
2. Fill in the required fields accurately and completely.
3. Review and update the form regularly to reflect changes in the refrigerator's condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Refrigerator Model**: Enter the model or name of the refrigerator being maintained.
* **Maintenance Date**: Enter the date of the last maintenance or inspection.
* **Defrosting Status**: Select 'Yes' if the refrigerator is currently being defrosted, 'No' if it's not being defrosted, or choose 'Unknown' if you're unsure.
* **Compressor Status**: Choose 'Good' if the compressor is working properly, 'Bad' if it's not working, or 'Unknown' if you're unsure.
* **Door Status**: Select 'Good' if the door is functioning correctly, 'Bad' if it's not, or 'Needs Repair' if it requires maintenance or replacement.
* **Temperature**: Enter the current temperature of the refrigerator, if applicable.
* **Power Status**: Choose 'Good' if the power is working properly, 'Bad' if it's not, or 'Unknown' if you're unsure.
* **Defrost Time**: Enter the estimated defrost time, if applicable.
 
## Tips
* Be accurate and honest when filling out this form to ensure reliable data for maintenance planning and decision-making.
* If you're unsure about any field, choose 'Unknown' and note any additional comments or concerns in the space provided.
* Review and update the form regularly to reflect changes in the refrigerator's condition.
