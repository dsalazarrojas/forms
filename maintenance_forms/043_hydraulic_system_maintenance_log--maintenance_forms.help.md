# Hydraulic System Maintenance Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hydraulic System Maintenance Log form is intended to collect data on the maintenance and inspection of hydraulic systems, allowing operators to track and record critical maintenance information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Fill out all required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
2. Enter the pump type in the "pump_type" field.
3. Input the serial number of the hydraulic system in the "serial_number" field.
4. Record the model year of the hydraulic system in the "model_year" field.
5. Enter the date of the last inspection in the "last_inspection_date" field.
6. Record the date when the next maintenance is due in the "next_due_date" field.
7. Input the date for the next scheduled service in the "next_service_date" field.
8. Enter any maintenance notes or issues in the "maintenance_log" field.
9. Provide your signature in the "signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pump Type** (`pump_type`, text, required): Enter the type of pump used in the hydraulic system.
* **Serial Number** (`serial_number`, text, required): Record the serial number of the hydraulic system.
* **Model Year** (`model_year`, number, required): Enter the model year of the hydraulic system.
* **Last Inspection Date** (`last_inspection_date`, date, required): Record the date of the last inspection of the hydraulic system.
* **Next Due Date** (`next_due_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the next maintenance is due for the hydraulic system.
* **Next Service Date** (`next_service_date`, date, required): Record the date for the next scheduled service of the hydraulic system.
* **Maintenance Log** (`maintenance_log`, text, optional): Enter any notes or issues related to the maintenance of the hydraulic system.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Provide your signature to confirm completion of the form.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Use the "maintenance_log" field to document any maintenance activities or issues that occurred during the inspection.
