# Meier Hall Maintenance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Meier Hall Maintenance Request Form is designed to collect information about maintenance requests related to Meier Hall. Fill out this form to notify maintenance personnel of any issues or concerns you have about the hall.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your Name**: Fill out this field with your name.
2. **Enter your Email**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you with updates about your request.
3. **Describe the Issue**: Provide a clear and concise description of the maintenance issue you are reporting.
4. **Submit Your Request**: Select "Yes" if you want to submit your request for maintenance. Select "No" if you do not want to proceed with your request.
5. **Optional: Provide Date and Time**: If you are reporting an issue that requires immediate attention, you can optionally select a specific date and time for the maintenance work to take place. If not provided, maintenance personnel will schedule the work at their discretion.
6. **Optional: Assigned Tool**: If the issue involves specific equipment or tools, select "Yes" if they need to be assigned for the maintenance task. Select "No" if not applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name** (text, required): Enter your name to help us contact you about your maintenance request.
* **email** (email, optional): Enter your email address so that we can contact you about your request.
* **description** (note, required): Provide a clear description of the maintenance issue you are reporting.
* **maintenance_request_form_submit** (select_one, required): Select "Yes" to submit your maintenance request. Select "No" to cancel your request.
* **maintenance_request_form_date** (date, optional): Optional date for maintenance to take place. If not provided, we will schedule the work at our discretion.
* **maintenance_request_form_time** (time, optional): Optional time for maintenance to take place. If not provided, we will schedule the work at our discretion.
* **assigned_tool** (select_one, required): Select "Yes" if a specific tool is required for the maintenance task. Select "No" if not applicable.
* **maintenance_request_form_submit** (select_one, required): This field is for submitting your maintenance request, not for assigned tool selection.
