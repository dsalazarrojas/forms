# Facility Maintenance Completion Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to report completion of facility maintenance tasks. It is designed to provide a structured way of recording completion dates and task IDs, ensuring accurate and timely maintenance records.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as you progress through the form. You will be notified of missing information.
2. Ensure you have access to the required information before starting the form.
3. Double-check your entries for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Maintenance Report Form** (`facility_maintenance_report_form_page_1`, text, optional): 
	+ Use this field to provide a brief description of the facility maintenance task completion.
* **Facility Maintenance Task ID** (`facility_maintenance_task_id`, number, optional): 
	+ Enter the unique identifier assigned to the maintenance task.
* **Completion Date** (`completion_date`, date, optional): 
	+ Select the date on which the maintenance task was completed.
* **Facility Maintenance Completion Report** (`facility_maintenance_completion_report_form_page_1`, text, optional): 
	+ This field seems to be a duplicate of the first field, `Facility Maintenance Report Form`. It is recommended to skip it for consistency.
