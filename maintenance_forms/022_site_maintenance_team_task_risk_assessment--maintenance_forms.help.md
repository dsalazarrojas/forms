# Site Maintenance Team Task Risk Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Site Maintenance Team Task Risk Assessment form is used to evaluate the risk level of a task assigned to a site maintenance team and provide essential information for effective communication and risk management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, select the site maintenance team that the task is assigned to.
2. Enter the site maintenance team task title.
3. Assess the risk level of the task by selecting one of the three options: Low, Medium, or High.
4. If necessary, provide additional comments or notes about the risk level.
5. Enter the date and time of the task.
6. If you have any additional comments or notes, enter them in the dedicated note field.
7. Select the team member or team to whom the task will be assigned.
8. Enter the email address of the team member or team to whom the task will be assigned.
9. Enter the phone number of the team member or team to whom the task will be assigned.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Site Maintenance Team Task** (`site_maintenance_team_task`, text, optional): Enter the title of the site maintenance team task.
* **Risk Assessment** (`risk_assessment`, select_one, optional): Assess the risk level of the task (Low, Medium, or High).
* **Risk Level Note** (`risk_level_note`, note, optional): Enter additional comments or notes about the risk level.
* **Date** (`site_maintenance_team_task_risk_assessment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the task.
* **Time** (`site_maintenance_team_task_risk_assessment_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the task.
* **Site Maintenance Team Risk Assessment Note** (`site_maintenance_team_task_risk_assessment_note`, note, optional): Enter additional comments or notes about the task.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, optional): Select the site maintenance team or team member to whom the task will be assigned.
* **Email To** (`email_to`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the team member or team to whom the task will be assigned.
* **Phone To** (`phone_to`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the team member or team to whom the task will be assigned.
