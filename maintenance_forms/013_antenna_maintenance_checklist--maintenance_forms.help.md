# Antenna Maintenance Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Antenna Maintenance Checklist is a form designed to collect information about the physical condition of antennas, identify potential issues, and schedule follow-up actions as needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the "Site id or name" field with the identifier or name of the site or location.
2.  Enter the name of the technician or person completing the form in the "Technician name" field.
3.  Enter the date of the inspection in the "Inspection date" field.
4.  Describe the current condition of the antenna in the "Antenna physical condition" field, choosing one of the provided options: "Good", "Minor wear", "Damaged", or "Requires replacement".
5.  Check the status of the connectors and cables in the "Connectors and cables status" field, selecting one of the options: "Good", "Repair needed", or "Replace".
6.  Confirm the grounding and bonding status in the "Grounding and bonding status" field, choosing one of the options: "Compliant", "Non-compliant", or "Not applicable".
7.  In the "Signal test results summary" field, enter a brief description of the signal test results, or attach relevant reports.
8.  If any corrective actions are required, provide a detailed description in the "Corrective actions required" field.
9.  If necessary, schedule a follow-up date in the "Follow up date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Site id or name**: Enter the unique identifier or name of the site or location.
* **Technician name**: Enter the name of the person completing the checklist.
* **Inspection date**: Enter the date of the maintenance inspection.
* **Antenna physical condition**: Assess the current condition of the antenna, choosing one of the options: "Good", "Minor wear", "Damaged", or "Requires replacement".
* **Connectors and cables status**: Check the status of the connectors and cables for corrosion or loose connections, selecting one of the options: "Good", "Repair needed", or "Replace".
* **Grounding and bonding status**: Confirm the grounding and bonding status, choosing one of the options: "Compliant", "Non-compliant", or "Not applicable".
* **Signal test results summary**: Enter a brief description of the signal test results, or attach relevant reports.
* **Corrective actions required**: If necessary, describe the repairs or parts needed to address any issues.
* **Follow up date**: Schedule a follow-up date if additional actions are needed.

## Tips

* Ensure this form is completed thoroughly and accurately.
* Refer to standard operating procedures and industry guidelines for antenna maintenance.
* Store completed forms securely and maintain records for future reference.
* Update this form as necessary to reflect changes in procedures or industry standards.
