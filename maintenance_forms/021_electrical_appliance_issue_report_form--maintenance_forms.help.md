# Electrical Appliance Issue Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Electrical Appliance Issue Report Form is designed to report issues with electrical appliances in your home or office. This form will help our team to identify and resolve the issue efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you are reporting a new issue and not a duplicate.
3. If you are reporting multiple issues, please submit each one separately.
4. If you are unsure about any field, contact our support team for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Appliance Name** (`appliance-name`, `text`, required): Please enter the name of the appliance that is experiencing the issue (e.g., "Fridgerator" or "TV").
* **Appliance Location** (`appliance-location`, `text`, required): Describe the location where the appliance is installed (e.g., "kitchen", "living room", or "office").
* **Issue Description** (`issue-description`, `text`, required): Briefly describe the problem with your appliance, including any error messages or symptoms you are experiencing (e.g., "Not turning on", "Leaks", or "Unresponsive").
* **Service Requested** (`service-requested`, `select_one`, required): If you would like us to contact you for service, select "Yes". Otherwise, select "No".
* **Scheduled Service** (`scheduled-service`, `select_one`, required): Choose a day when you would like to schedule the service.
* **Preferred Date and Time** (`preferred-date-time`, `date` and `time`, required): Enter the date and time you would like to schedule the service.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): If you have a preferred phone number for us to contact you, enter it here.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): If you have a preferred email address for us to contact you, enter it here.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned-tool`, `text`, required): Enter the tool or equipment that the technician will use to repair or replace your appliance.
* **Created By** (`created-by`, `text`, required): Enter your name or the name of the person who created this report.
