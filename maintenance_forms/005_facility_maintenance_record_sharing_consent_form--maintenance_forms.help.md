# Facility Maintenance Record Sharing Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Facility Maintenance Record Sharing Consent Form is a document used to obtain consent from facility users for sharing maintenance records with various teams or external entities. This form helps ensure that all parties involved in facility maintenance have a clear understanding of how and when records will be shared.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the type of consent you wish to grant (Maintenance Records or Facility Maintenance Records).
2. Choose the methods by which you want to share records (Internet, Email, Phone, Mail, or Dropbox).
3. Enter your email address to receive updates (optional).
4. Provide your name for identification purposes.
5. Select a date for the agreement (optional).
6. Choose the frequency of sharing (Once, Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Bi-Annual, or Annually).
7. Select who you want to share records with (Facility Management Team, Department Head, Facility Users, Facility Management, Maintenance Team, External Vendors, or All).
8. Select the facility maintenance team you want to share records with (Team 1, Team 2, Team 3, Team 4, Team 5, Team 6, Team 7, Team 8).
9. If desired, select to sign the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Facility Maintenance Record Sharing** (`maintenance_form`, text, optional): This field allows you to type in a short description of the maintenance records you are willing to share.
- **Type of Consent** (`consent_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of consent you wish to grant (Maintenance Records or Facility Maintenance Records).
- **Sharing Method** (`sharing_method`, select_multiple, optional): Select the methods you wish to use for sharing records (Internet, Email, Phone, Mail, or Dropbox).
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address to receive updates (optional).
- **Your Name** (`name`, text, optional): Provide your name for identification purposes.
- **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select a date for the agreement (optional).
- **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select a time for the agreement (optional).
- **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, optional): Type in the name of the facility.
- **Additional Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (optional).
- **Agreement** (`agreement`, number, required): Sign this agreement to confirm your consent.
- **How Often to Share** (`sharing_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Select the frequency of sharing (Once, Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Bi-Annual, or Annually).
- **Email Frequency** (`email_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Select how frequently you want to receive emails.
- **Who to Share with** (`sharing_with`, select_multiple, optional): Choose who you want to share records with (Facility Management Team, Department Head, Facility Users, Facility Management, Maintenance Team, External Vendors, or All).
- **Facility Maintenance Team** (`facility_maintenance_team`, select_multiple, optional): Select the facility maintenance team you want to share records with (Team 1, Team 2, Team 3, Team 4, Team 5, Team 6, Team 7, Team 8).
- **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, date, optional): Select a date for the agreement (optional).
- **Sign** (`signature`, select_multiple, optional): Select how you wish to sign the agreement (Sign, Sign and Date, Sign and Print, Sign and Email, or Sign and Fax).

## Tips

* Make sure to complete the form accurately to ensure that all parties understand the agreement.
* Review the form before submitting to ensure all fields are filled out correctly.
* Sign the agreement to confirm your consent.
