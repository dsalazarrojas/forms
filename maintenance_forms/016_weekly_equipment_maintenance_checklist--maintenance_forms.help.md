# Weekly Equipment Maintenance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool for maintenance personnel to track and manage the maintenance of equipment on a weekly basis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Equipment ID** (ID) of the equipment being maintained.
2. Choose the **Inspection Date** of the last maintenance activity.
3. Enter the **Maintenance Due Date** by which the equipment needs to be maintained.
4. Select the **Maintenance Performed** type from the list (e.g., Repair, Preventive, Scheduled, Emergency).
5. Enter a brief **Maintenance Description** of the work performed.
6. Select the **Equipment Status** (Active, Inactive, In Maintenance, Offline).
7. Enter the **Location** where the equipment is situated.
8. Add any **Notes** regarding the maintenance activity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Equipment ID**: Enter the unique ID of the equipment being maintained.
* **Inspection Date**: Choose the date of the last maintenance activity.
* **Maintenance Due Date**: Enter the date by which the equipment needs to be maintained.
* **Maintenance Performed**: Select the type of maintenance performed (e.g., Repair, Preventive, Scheduled, Emergency).
	+ This field helps to track the reason for maintenance.
* **Maintenance Description**: Enter a brief description of the maintenance activity.
* **Equipment Status**: Select the current status of the equipment (Active, Inactive, In Maintenance, Offline).
	+ This field helps to track the equipment's availability.
* **Location**: Enter the location where the equipment is situated.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes regarding the maintenance activity.
	+ This field is for any extra information that maintenance personnel may need to remember or report.
