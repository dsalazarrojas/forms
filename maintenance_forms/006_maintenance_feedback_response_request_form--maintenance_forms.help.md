# Maintenance Feedback Response Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by facility managers to provide feedback on maintenance requests. It helps to track and evaluate the quality of service offered by the maintenance team.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please enter the name of the Facility Manager who is submitting this feedback.
2. Enter the name of the Maintenance Technician who responded to the maintenance request.
3. Rate the response on a scale of 0-100.
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback.
5. Select the category for the feedback (Landlords, Operations, or Facility).
6. Enter the email address (if applicable).
7. Enter the phone number (if applicable).
8. Enter the date the response was completed.
9. Enter the time the response was completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facility Manager** (`facility_manager`, text, required): This field is used to enter the name of the Facility Manager submitting this feedback.
* **Maintenance Technician** (`maintenance_technician`, text, required): This field is used to enter the name of the Maintenance Technician who responded to the maintenance request.
* **Rating** (`ratings`, number, required): This field is used to rate the response on a scale of 0-100.
* **Comment** (`comment`, text, optional): This field is used to provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): This field is used to select the category of feedback (Landlords, Operations, or Facility).
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This field is used to enter an email address (if applicable).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This field is used to enter a phone number (if applicable).
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, date, optional): This field is used to enter the date the response was completed.
* **Time Completed** (`time_completed`, time, optional): This field is used to enter the time the response was completed.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Use clear and concise language when providing comments or feedback.
* Select the correct category for the feedback.
* Enter accurate and complete contact information if applicable.
