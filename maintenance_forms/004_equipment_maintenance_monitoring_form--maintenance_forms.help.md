# Equipment Maintenance Monitoring Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help users track and monitor equipment maintenance activities. It collects information on the maintenance schedule, equipment performance, maintenance downtime, maintenance frequency, and maintenance costs associated with equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the maintenance schedule for the equipment.
2. Enter the date of the last equipment performance check.
3. Enter the number of days the equipment has been in maintenance downtime.
4. Enter the maintenance frequency (how often the equipment is maintained).
5. Enter the total maintenance costs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Maintenance Schedule** (`maintenance_schedule`, select_multiple, required/optional): 
  This field allows you to select multiple maintenance schedules for the equipment. Choose from the available options: 'Yes' or 'No'.

- **Equipment Performance** (`equipment_performance`, date, required/optional): 
  This field is used to track the date of the last equipment performance check. Enter the date in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.

- **Maintenance Downtime** (`maintenance_downtime`, number, required/optional): 
  This field is used to track the number of days the equipment has been in maintenance downtime. Enter a whole number, for example, '5' for 5 days.

- **Maintenance Frequency** (`maintenance_frequency`, number, required/optional): 
  This field is used to track how often the equipment is maintained. Enter a whole number, for example, '4' for 4 times per year.

- **Maintenance Costs** (`maintenance_costs`, number, required/optional): 
  This field is used to track the total maintenance costs associated with the equipment. Enter a whole number, for example, '$10,000' for $10,000 dollars.

## Tips
- Make sure to review and update the maintenance schedule regularly to ensure timely maintenance is performed.
- Enter accurate and up-to-date performance check dates to track equipment performance effectively.
- Regularly review and update maintenance downtime, frequency, and costs to optimize equipment maintenance.
