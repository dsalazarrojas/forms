# Metal Cleaning Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to document the metal cleaning process, capturing details about the surface condition, metal condition, and cleaning method used. It ensures that important information is recorded accurately and comprehensively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing the "Surface" field and describe the current condition of the metal surface.
2. Move on to the "Metal" field and assess the metal's condition.
3. Take a photo or note the "Surface Before" field to record the initial state of the metal.
4. Describe the final state of the metal surface in the "Surface After" field.
5. Select the appropriate cleaning method used from the dropdown list in the "Cleaning" field.
6. Use the "Comments" field for any additional information or notes about the cleaning process.
7. Sign and date the form with your signature in the "Signature" field.
8. Provide your email address in the "Email" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Surface**: Describe the current condition of the metal surface, including any notable marks, scratches, or other features.
* **Metal**: Assess the overall condition of the metal, considering factors like rust, corrosion, or other forms of degradation.
* **Surface Before** (Photo or note): Record the initial state of the metal surface before cleaning.
* **Surface After**: Describe the final state of the metal surface after cleaning.
* **Cleaning**: Select the cleaning method used, such as chemical solution, steam cleaning, or ultrasonic cleaning.
* **Comments**: Add any additional notes or observations about the cleaning process.
* **Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm that the cleaning was completed accurately.
* **Email**: Provide your email address for contact or follow-up purposes (optional).

Note: This form is designed to be completed accurately and thoroughly, ensuring that all necessary information is captured to document the metal cleaning process.
