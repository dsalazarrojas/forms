# website_maintenance_cost_estimator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The website maintenance cost estimator form helps website administrators or owners to estimate the costs associated with maintaining a website, including bug fixes, content updates, and performance enhancements. The form collects basic information such as the user's report, estimated budget for maintenance, maintenance needs, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your User Report in the text box. This should be a brief summary of the issues or tasks you want to report for maintenance.
2. Select the maintenance needs for your website from the list provided. You can choose one or multiple options.
3. Enter the estimated budget for each maintenance task in the number field. This will help estimate the total cost of maintenance.
4. Describe your maintenance plan in the text box.
5. Enter your website's URL.
6. Enter your support email address.
7. Enter your support phone number (optional).
8. Add any additional notes if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Report**: This is a text box where you can enter a brief summary of the issues or tasks you want to report for maintenance.
- **Maintenance Needs**: This is a multiple-select field where you can choose one or multiple options from the list provided.
- **Estimated Budget**: This is a number field where you can enter the estimated budget for each maintenance task.
- **Maintenance Plan**: This is a text box where you can describe your maintenance plan.
- **Website URL**: This is a text field where you can enter your website's URL.
- **Support Email**: This is an email field where you can enter your support email address.
- **Support Phone**: This is a text field where you can enter your support phone number.
- **Additional Notes**: This is a text box where you can add any additional notes if required.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and clear information in the User Report.
- Be specific when selecting maintenance needs.
- Estimate the budget accurately for each maintenance task.
- Describe your maintenance plan clearly and concisely.
- Enter accurate contact information.
- Use the form to track and analyze website maintenance needs and costs over time.
