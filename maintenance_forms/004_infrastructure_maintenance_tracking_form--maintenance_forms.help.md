# Infrastructure Maintenance Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Infrastructure Maintenance Tracking Form is used to collect and track information about the maintenance needs of facilities within an organization. It is designed to provide a centralized repository for maintenance requests, allowing teams to prioritize and manage maintenance tasks more effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the facility that requires maintenance from the list of facilities.
2. Enter the date that the maintenance request was made.
3. Provide a detailed description of the maintenance request.
4. Choose the priority level of the maintenance request (High, Medium, or Low).
5. Select the category of the maintenance request.
6. Choose the person or people responsible for handling the maintenance request.
7. Select the status of the maintenance request (New, In Progress, or Done).
8. Enter the due date for the maintenance request.
9. Select the type of facility requiring maintenance.
10. Enter the location of the facility.
11. Enter the contact person's name and contact details (email and phone number).
12. Enter any additional contact notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facility Name (`facility_name`, text, optional):** Enter the name of the facility that requires maintenance.
* **Maintenance Request Date (`maintenance_request_date`, date, optional):** Enter the date that the maintenance request was made.
* **Description (`description`, text, optional):** Provide a detailed description of the maintenance request.
* **Priority (`priority`, select_one, optional):** Choose the priority level of the maintenance request (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Category (`category`, text, optional):** Select the category of the maintenance request.
* **Assigned To (`assigned_to`, select_multiple, optional):** Choose the person or people responsible for handling the maintenance request.
* **Status (`status`, select_one, optional):** Select the status of the maintenance request (New, In Progress, or Done).
* **Due Date (`due_date`, date, optional):** Enter the due date for the maintenance request.
* **Facility Type (`facility_type`, text, optional):** Select the type of facility requiring maintenance.
* **Location (`location`, text, optional):** Enter the location of the facility.
* **Contact Name (`contact_name`, text, optional):** Enter the contact person's name.
* **Contact Email (`contact_email`, email, optional):** Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Contact Phone (`contact_phone`, text, optional):** Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Notes (`contact_notes`, text, optional):** Enter any additional contact notes.
* **Facility Notes (`facility_notes`, text, optional):** Enter any additional facility notes.
* **Status Notes (`status_notes`, text, optional):** Enter any additional status notes.
* **Category Notes (`category_notes`, text, optional):** Enter any additional category notes.
* **Priority Notes (`priority_notes`, text, optional):** Enter any additional priority notes.
* **Assigned To Notes (`assigned_to_notes`, text, optional):** Enter any additional assigned to notes.
* **Due Date Notes (`due_date_notes`, text, optional):** Enter any additional due date notes.
* **Facility Type Notes (`facility_type_notes`, text, optional):** Enter any additional facility type notes.
* **Location Notes (`location_notes`, text, optional):** Enter any additional location notes.
* **Facility Status (`facility_status`, select_one, optional):** Select the status of the facility (Active or Inactive).
* **Facility Status Notes (`facility_status_notes`, text, optional):** Enter any additional facility status notes.
* **Facility Category (`facility_category`, text, optional):** Enter any additional facility category.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as "required" in the form schema.
