## Purpose

The Power Amplifier Unit Inspection Form is used to inspect and maintain power amplifier units. It helps ensure that the units are in good working condition and schedule follow-up inspections.

## How To Complete This Form

2. **Equipment Model**: Enter the model number of the power amplifier unit.
3. **Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the current inspection.
4. **Inspection Result**: Select the result of the inspection (Normal, Abnormal, Not Applicable, or Not Inspected).
5. **Signature**: Enter the signature of the person conducting the inspection.
6. **Comments**: Enter any comments or observations about the inspection.
7. **Assigned To**: Select the person responsible for maintaining the power amplifier unit.
8. **Follow-up Date**: Enter the date when the next inspection is scheduled.
9. **Inspection Signed Off**: Select whether the inspection is signed off or not.
10. **Equipment Status**: Select the current status of the power amplifier unit (In Good Working Condition, In Poor Condition, In Need Of Repair, Not Available For Inspection, or Not Inspected).
11. **Equipment Condition**: Enter any additional comments about the condition of the power amplifier unit.
12. **Inspection Type**: Select the type of inspection performed (General Inspection, Functional Test, Diagnostic Test, Performance Test, or Other).
13. **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the inspection.
14. **Email**: Enter the contact email of the person conducting the inspection.
15. **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of the person conducting the inspection.
16. **Inspection Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the inspection.
17. **Equipment Manufacturer**: Select whether the power amplifier unit has a manufacturer's logo or not.
18. **Follow-up Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the follow-up inspection.
19. **Inspection Frequency**: Select the frequency of the inspection (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annual).
