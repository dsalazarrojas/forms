# Power Amplifier Unit Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
## How To Complete This Form
## Field-by-Field Explanation
## Tips

### 1. **Equipment ID** (required)
Enter the unique identifier of the power amplifier unit being inspected.
### 2. **Equipment Model** (optional)
Enter the model number of the power amplifier unit.
### 3. **Inspection Date** (required)
Enter the date of the current inspection.
### 4. **Inspection Result** (required)
Select the result of the inspection (Normal, Abnormal, Not Applicable, or Not Inspected).
### 5. **Signature** (optional)
Enter the signature of the person conducting the inspection.
### 6. **Comments** (optional)
Enter any comments or observations about the inspection.
### 7. **Assigned To** (required)
Select the person responsible for maintaining the power amplifier unit.
### 8. **Follow-up Date** (optional)
Enter the date when the next inspection is scheduled.
### 9. **Inspection Signed Off** (required)
Select whether the inspection is signed off or not.
### 10. **Equipment Status** (optional)
Select the current status of the power amplifier unit (In Good Working Condition, In Poor Condition, In Need Of Repair, Not Available For Inspection, or Not Inspected).
### 11. **Equipment Condition** (optional)
Enter any additional comments about the condition of the power amplifier unit.
### 12. **Inspection Type** (optional)
Select the type of inspection performed (General Inspection, Functional Test, Diagnostic Test, Performance Test, or Other).
### 13. **Notes** (optional)
Enter any additional notes about the inspection.
### 14. **Email** (optional)
Enter the contact email of the person conducting the inspection.
### 15. **Contact Number** (optional)
Enter the contact number of the person conducting the inspection.
### 16. **Inspection Notes** (optional)
Enter any additional notes about the inspection.
### 17. **Equipment Manufacturer** (optional)
Select whether the power amplifier unit has a manufacturer's logo or not.
### 18. **Follow-up Notes** (optional)
Enter any additional notes about the follow-up inspection.
### 19. **Inspection Frequency** (required)
Select the frequency of the inspection (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annual).
### 20. **Last Inspection Date** (optional)
Enter the date of the last inspection.
### 21. **Next Inspection Date** (optional)
Enter the date of the next inspection.
### 22. **Next Inspection Due** (optional)
Enter the date when the next inspection is due.
### 23. **Last Inspection Status** (optional)
Enter the status of the last inspection.
### 24. **Last Inspection Result** (optional)
Enter the result of the last inspection.
### 25. **Last Signed Off** (optional)
Enter the status of the last signed off.
