# Manhole Cover Replacement Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manhole Cover Replacement Report form is used to report on the replacement of a manhole cover. This form is used to collect information about the replaced manhole cover's details and any related photo evidence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter the title of the form, "Manhole Cover Replacement Report".
*   If you have any photo evidence, you can upload it using the "Photo Evidence" field.
*   Select the type, material, and size of the replaced manhole cover using the "Manhole Cover Details" dropdown menu.
*   Provide your sign-off or approval using the "Sign Off" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Photo Evidence**: Upload any photo evidence related to the replaced manhole cover.
*   **Manhole Cover Details** (`Manhole Cover Type`, `Manhole Cover Material`, `Manhole Cover Size`): Select the type, material, and size of the replaced manhole cover.
*   **Sign Off**: Provide your sign-off or approval for the form submission.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure a complete report.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your supervisor or the original requestor for clarification.
*   This form is for reporting on manhole cover replacements only. If you need to report on other types of maintenance activities, please use the corresponding form.
