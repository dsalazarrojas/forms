# Beverage Production Equipment Maintenance Service Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for requesting maintenance services for beverage production equipment. It helps the maintenance team to track and schedule the necessary services to keep your equipment in good working condition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide information about the person submitting the maintenance request.
2. Provide information about the facility, including the name, location, and production area affected by the issue.
3. Describe the equipment involved, including its type, brand, model, and serial number.
4. Describe the issue with the equipment, including when it was first noticed and how often it occurs.
5. Specify the impact on production and the urgency level of the service required.
6. Provide any additional information about the maintenance history of the equipment.
7. Schedule the service by selecting a preferred time for the technician to arrive.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of Requester**: Enter the name of the person submitting the maintenance request. This is required information.
* **Position or Title**: Enter the person's role in the facility, such as "Production Manager" or "Maintenance Supervisor". This is required information.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the person submitting the maintenance request. This is required information.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the maintenance request. This is required information.
* **Date of Request**: Select the date when this maintenance request is being submitted. This is required information.
* **Facility or Plant Name**: Enter the name of the production facility where the issue is occurring. This is required information.
* **Facility Location**: Enter the city, state, or address of the production facility. This is required information.
* **Production Area or Line**: Enter the specific area or line of production affected by the issue. This is required information.
* **Type of Equipment**: Select the type of equipment involved, such as "Mixing equipment" or "Filling machine". This is required information.
* **Equipment Brand or Manufacturer**: Enter the brand or manufacturer of the equipment. This is required information.
* **Equipment Model Number**: Enter the model number of the equipment, if available. This is optional information.
* **Serial Number or Asset ID**: Enter the serial number or asset ID of the equipment, if available. This is optional information.
* **Equipment Age or Installation Year**: Enter the approximate year the equipment was installed. This is optional information.
* **Type of Issue**: Select the type of issue with the equipment, such as "Equipment not operating" or "Quality issues". This is required information.
* **Detailed Description of Problem**: Enter a detailed description of the issue with the equipment. This is required information.
* **When Was This Issue First Noticed**: Select the date or time period when the issue was first noticed. This is required information.
* **Frequency of Issue**: Select how often the issue occurs, such as "Constantly" or "Once in a while". This is required information.
* **Impact on Production**: Select the impact on production, such as "Complete shutdown - no production" or "Safety concern only". This is required information.
* **Urgency Level**: Select the urgency level of the service required, such as "Emergency - within 2 hours" or "Standard - within 1 week". This is required information.
* **Date of Last Maintenance Service**: Enter the date when the equipment was last serviced. This is optional information.
* **Is There a Maintenance Contract?**: Select if there is an active or expired contract for maintenance. This is required information.
* **Current Service Provider or Vendor**: Enter the name of the current service provider or vendor. This is optional information.
* **Preferred Time for Service**: Select the preferred time for the technician to arrive, such as "During production hours" or "After production hours". This is required information.
* **Will Someone Be Available for Technician Access?**: Select if someone will be available to provide access to the technician. This is required information.
* **Additional Notes or Special Instructions**: Enter any additional notes or special instructions for the maintenance service. This is optional information.

**Tips**

* Be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out this form to help the maintenance team understand the issue and schedule the necessary service.
* If you are unsure about any of the information, do not hesitate to ask for help or clarification.
* Please ensure that you provide the required information to ensure that the maintenance request is processed efficiently.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the maintenance team for assistance.
