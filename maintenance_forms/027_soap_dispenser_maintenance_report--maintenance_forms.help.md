# Soap Dispenser Maintenance Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Soap Dispenser Maintenance Report is used to collect information about the maintenance and condition of soap dispensers in a facility. This report helps identify issues, determine the need for restocking, and schedule maintenance activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the **Location** of the soap dispenser being reported on.
2. Describe the **Soap Dispenser Condition**, selecting the status that best matches your observation (e.g., Good, Needs Refill, Leaking, Empty, Faulty).
3. Record any **Maintenance Activity** performed or planned for the dispenser.
4. Add any **Notes** about the condition or maintenance performed.
5. Enter the **Date Of Inspection** if applicable.
6. If the dispenser needs restocking, select **Restocking Needed** and enter the date for **Restock Date**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Location**: This field is for entering the location where the soap dispenser is situated.
* **Soap Dispenser Condition**: This field allows you to report the status of the soap dispenser, which can be Good, Needs Refill, Leaking, Empty, or Faulty. Choose the option that best matches your observation.
* **Maintenance Activity**: Here, you can record any maintenance activity performed or planned for the soap dispenser.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional information or comments about the condition or maintenance performed.
* **Date Of Inspection**: Enter the date of the inspection, if applicable.
* **Restocking Needed**: Select this option if the soap dispenser needs restocking. This will help the facilities team to prioritize replenishment of soap supplies.
* **Restock Date**: If restocking is needed, enter the date by which the dispenser will be restocked.
