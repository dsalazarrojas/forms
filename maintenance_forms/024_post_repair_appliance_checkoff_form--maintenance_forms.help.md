## How To Complete This Form
This form has 9 fields, each with a specific purpose. To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the details of the appliance that was repaired.
2. Provide the details of the technician who performed the repair.
3. Describe the repair process, including any issues that arose during the process.
4. Check if the post-repair check was performed.
5. Select the outcome of the post-repair check.
6. Enter any comments or feedback from the customer.
7. Enter the date the repair was completed.
8. Enter the time the repair was completed.
9. Enter the name of the person who completed the repair.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. <label>Appliance Details</label> (`post_repair_appliance_checkoff_form_1`, text, optional)
Enter a brief description of the appliance that was repaired, including its make, model, and serial number.

### 2. <label>Techncian Details</label> (`post_repair_appliance_checkoff_form_2`, text, optional)
Enter the name, employee ID, and any other relevant information about the technician who performed the repair.

### 3. <label>Repair Details</label> (`post_repair_appliance_checkoff_form_3`, text, optional)
Describe the issues that arose during the repair process, including any challenges or complications that were encountered.

### 4. <label>Post-Repair Check</label> (`post_repair_appliance_checkoff_form_4`, select_one, optional)
Check if the post-repair check was performed.

### 5. <label>Customer Approval</label> (`post_repair_appliance_checkoff_form_5`, select_multiple, optional)
Select whether the customer approved the repair.

### 6. <label>Comments</label> (`post_repair_appliance_checkoff_form_6`, text, optional)
Enter any comments or feedback from the customer.

### 7. <label>Date</label> (`post_repair_appliance_checkoff_form_7`, date, optional)
Enter the date the repair was completed.

### 8. <label>Time</label> (`post_repair_appliance_checkoff_form_8`, time, optional)
Enter the time the repair was completed.

### 9. <label>Completed By</label> (`post_repair_appliance_checkoff_form_9`, text, optional)
Enter the name of the person who completed the repair.

## Tips
- Make sure to complete all fields accurately and truthfully.
- If a field is not required, it can be left blank.
- If you have any issues or questions, do not hesitate to reach out for assistance.
