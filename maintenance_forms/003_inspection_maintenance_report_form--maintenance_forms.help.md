# Inspection Maintenance Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to report on the condition and maintenance needs of various assets and government agencies.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and make sure you are familiar with the asset's current status and any relevant maintenance schedules or requirements.
2. Complete the form by providing the required information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspection Maintenance Report Form** (`page_1`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the inspection and maintenance report.
* **Maintenance Report Form** (`page_2`, text, required): Enter a detailed report on the maintenance needs of the asset.
* **Company Assets** (`page_3`, text, required): List the company assets that were inspected and maintained.
* **Business Associates** (`page_4`, text, required): List the business associates involved in the inspection and maintenance.
* **Government Agencies** (`page_5`, text, required): List the government agencies involved in the inspection and maintenance.
* **Assets List** (`page_6`, text, required): List the specific assets that were inspected and maintained.
* **Notes** (`page_7`, text, required): Provide any additional notes or comments on the inspection and maintenance.
* **Submit** (`page_8`, text, required): Confirm that the form has been completed accurately and submit it for review.

## Tips

* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with your supervisor or relevant documentation.
