# Disinfection Task Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Disinfection Task Log form is used to record and document disinfection tasks performed by staff members. This form is essential for tracking and managing disinfection tasks, ensuring that all necessary information is captured accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Date and Time of the task in the "Date and Time" field.
2. Specify the Location of where the task was performed in the "Location" field.
3. Select the type of disinfection task performed from the options provided in the "Disinfection Type" field.
4. If applicable, list the chemicals used during the task in the "Chemicals Used" field.
5. Provide details about the staff members involved in the task in the "Staff Details" field.
6. Add any comments or additional information about the task in the "Comments" field.
7. Sign off on the form by providing your signature in the "Signature" field.
8. Add any additional comments or feedback about the form in the "Disinfection Task Log Form Comments" field.
9. Choose the assigned tool(s) used for the task from the options provided in the "Assigned Tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Disinfection Task Log** (`disinfection_task_log`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the disinfection task performed.
* **Date and Time** (`date_and_time`, date, required/optional): Enter the date and time of when the task was performed.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required/optional): Specify the location where the task was performed.
* **Disinfection Type** (`disinfection_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of disinfection task performed from the options provided.
* **Chemicals Used** (`chemicals_used`, text, required/optional): If applicable, list the chemicals used during the task.
* **Staff Details** (`staff_details`, text, required/optional): Provide details about the staff members involved in the task.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Add any comments or additional information about the task.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required/optional): Sign off on the form by providing your signature.
* **Disinfection Task Log Form Comments** (`disinfection_task_log_form_comments`, text, required/optional): Add any additional comments or feedback about the form.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_multiple, required): Choose the assigned tool(s) used for the task from the options provided.
* **Disinfection Task Log Form Comments** (`disinfection_task_log_form_comments`, text, required/optional): Add any additional comments or feedback about the form.
