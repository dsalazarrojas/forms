# Material Handling Equipment Maintenance Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Material Handling Equipment Maintenance Log form is used to document and track the maintenance history of material handling equipment. This form helps ensure that equipment is properly maintained, reduces downtime, and improves overall efficiency in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Equipment ID**: Enter the unique identifier for the equipment being maintained. This could be a serial number, asset tag, or any other identifying code.
2. **Equipment Type**: Select the type of equipment being maintained from the dropdown list (e.g., Forklift, Hand Truck, etc.).
3. **Make/Model Year**: Enter the manufacturer, model, and year of the equipment.
4. **Description**: Provide a brief description of the equipment and any relevant details.
5. **Location**: Enter the location where the equipment is currently situated.
6. **Equipment Status**: Select the current status of the equipment (Active or Inactive).
7. **Maintenance Type**: Select the type of maintenance being performed (Preventive, Corrective, or Emergency).
8. **Maintenance Date**: Enter the date of the maintenance event.
9. **Hours**: Record the total hours spent on maintenance.
10. **Repair Costs**: Enter any costs associated with the maintenance.
11. **Repair Notes**: Add any additional notes about the maintenance process.
12. **Preventative Maintenance Schedule**: Enter the schedule for future maintenance.
13. **Performed By**: Record the name and contact information of the person performing the maintenance.
14. **Serial Number**: Enter the serial number of the equipment.
15. **Asset Tag**: Enter the asset tag number of the equipment.
16. **Maintenance Status**: Select the status of the maintenance (Complete or Incomplete).
17. **Notes**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the maintenance.
18. **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person for maintenance.
19. **Phone Number**: Enter the contact phone number.
20. **Email**: Enter the contact email.
21. **Asset Number**: Enter the asset number.
22. **Equipment Type ID**: Enter the ID of the equipment type.
23. **Asset Tag ID**: Enter the ID of the asset tag.
24. **Location ID**: Enter the ID of the location.
25. **Contact Info**: Record any additional contact information.
