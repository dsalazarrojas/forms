# Elevator Maintenance Log - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Elevator Maintenance Log form is used to record and track maintenance activities performed on elevators. It is essential for monitoring and managing elevator maintenance schedules, ensuring that elevators are properly maintained and repaired to ensure public safety. By filling out this form, maintenance personnel can record vital information about the maintenance activities, including the type of elevator, location, and technician involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of elevator being maintained (e.g., Car, Freight, Passenger, Service, or Service Lift).
2. Enter the location of the elevator (e.g., building number, floor number, or specific room).
3. Enter the date of the maintenance service (Service Date).
4. Enter the start and end dates of the maintenance period (Maintenance Start Date and Maintenance End Date).
5. Enter the name of the technician performing the maintenance.
6. Provide any additional notes about the maintenance activity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information**: This field is where you can enter any general information about the maintenance activity, such as the purpose of the maintenance or any specific details about the elevator.
* **Elevator Id**: This field is used to enter a unique identifier for the elevator, such as a serial number or a reference code.
* **Location**: Enter the location where the elevator is installed, including the building number, floor number, or specific room.
* **Type of Elevator**: Select the type of elevator being maintained, such as Car, Freight, Passenger, Service, or Service Lift.
* **Service Date**: Enter the date of the maintenance service, including the month, day, and year.
* **Maintenance Start Date**: Enter the start date of the maintenance period, including the month, day, and year.
* **Maintenance End Date**: Enter the end date of the maintenance period, including the month, day, and year.
* **Technician**: Enter the name of the technician who performed the maintenance work.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the maintenance activity, such as any issues encountered or resolutions applied.
* **Service Lift**: This field is empty in this template and does not need to be entered.
