# sensor_inspection_and_maintenance_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help maintainers and inspectors record inspection results, maintenance actions, and other relevant details about sensors in a system. It's an essential tool for ensuring sensor functionality and preventing malfunctions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by filling out the "inspections" page, which provides a space to record inspection details.
2. On the "results" page, enter the inspection results in the text field.
3. The "actions" page is where you'll record the maintenance actions taken.
4. On the "sensor_description" page, enter any additional details about the sensor.
5. Select the type of maintenance action taken from the dropdown list on the "maintenance_type" page.
6. Choose the status of the maintenance action from the dropdown list on the "maintenance_status" page.
7. If applicable, enter the date and time of inspection on the "date_inspected" page.
8. Enter the name of the technician or inspector on the "inspected_by" page.
9. Enter the email and phone number of the technician or inspector on the "inspected_by_email" and "inspected_by_phone" pages respectively.
10. If you have the sensor serial number, enter it on the "sensor_serial_number" page.
11. Finally, add any additional notes on the "notes" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **inspections** (inspections, note, optional): This page is for recording inspection details. Enter any comments or observations about the inspection.
* **results** (results, text, optional): Enter the results of the inspection in this text field.
* **actions** (actions, text, optional): Describe the maintenance actions taken on this page.
* **sensor_description** (sensor_description, text, optional): Provide any additional details about the sensor, such as its model or location.
* **maintenance_type** (maintenance_type, select_one, required): Select the type of maintenance action taken from the dropdown list.
* **maintenance_status** (maintenance_status, select_one, required): Choose the status of the maintenance action from the dropdown list.
* **date_inspected** (date_inspected, date, optional): Enter the date of inspection (if applicable).
* **time_inspected** (time_inspected, time, optional): Enter the time of inspection (if applicable).
* **inspected_by** (inspected_by, text, optional): Enter the name of the technician or inspector.
* **inspected_by_email** (inspected_by_email, email, optional): Enter the email of the technician or inspector.
* **inspected_by_phone** (inspected_by_phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the technician or inspector.
* **sensor_serial_number** (sensor_serial_number, text, optional): If available, enter the sensor's serial number.
* **notes** (notes, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the inspection and maintenance.
