# Maintenance Emergency Contact List Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to create a comprehensive list of emergency contacts for a team or organization. It helps identify and record critical contact information in the event of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select multiple emergency contacts from the emergency contact list.
2. Enter the team members involved.
3. Enter the phone number of the contact.
4. Enter the email address of the contact.
5. Enter the address of the contact.
6. Enter any additional notes about the contact.
7. Select the type of phone (home, office, or cell) for the contact.
8. Enter the name of another list of emergency contacts (if applicable).
9. Enter the team name and location.
10. Select the contact relationship (friend, family, or colleague) for the emergency contact.
11. Enter the name of the emergency contact.
12. Enter the contact number.
13. Select the contact relationship (friend, family, or colleague) for the emergency contact.
14. Enter the contact email.
15. Select the contact phone type (home, office, or cell).
16. Enter the contact location.
17. Enter any additional notes about the contact.
18. Enter the contact address.
19. Select the contact relationship (friend, family, or colleague) for the contact.
20. Select whether the contact is an emergency contact.
21. Select the contact relationship (friend, family, or colleague) for the contact.
22. Enter the number of the contact's emergency contact.
23. Enter the location of the contact's emergency contact.
24. Enter the email of the contact's emergency contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Emergency Contact List**: Select multiple emergency contacts from the predefined list.
* **Team Members**: Select the team members involved.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the contact.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the contact.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the contact.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the contact.
* **Phone**: Select the type of phone (home, office, or cell) for the contact.
* **Another List**: Select another list of emergency contacts (if applicable).
* **Team Name**: Enter the team name.
* **Team Location**: Enter the team location.
* **Contact Relationship**: Select the contact relationship (friend, family, or colleague) for the contact.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number.
* **Contact Relationship**: Select the contact relationship (friend, family, or colleague) for the contact.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the contact email.
* **Contact Phone**: Select the contact phone type (home, office, or cell).
* **Contact Location**: Enter the contact location.
* **Contact Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the contact.
* **Contact Address**: Enter the contact address.
* **Contact Relationship**: Select the contact relationship (friend, family, or colleague) for the contact.
* **Emergency Contact**: Select whether the contact is an emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Select the contact relationship (friend, family, or colleague) for the contact.
* **Emergency Contact Number**: Enter the contact number.
* **Emergency Contact Location**: Enter the location of the contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the contact email.

Note: Make sure to fill out all required fields marked with ** and enter accurate information for each field to ensure a comprehensive emergency contact list.
