# Newsletter Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users who wish to subscribe to a newsletter. The collected data includes name, contact information, and payment preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Select your desired Subscription Type from the dropdown menu.
5. Choose your preferred Payment Preference from the list (e.g., PayPal, Check/Postal).
6. Enter your Address, City, State, and Country in the corresponding fields.
7. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
8. Click the "Submit" button to complete your subscription.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (text)**: Enter your given name as it appears on your personal documents.
* **Last Name (text)**: Enter your surname as it appears on your personal documents.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your active email address where you can be reached.
* **Subscription Type (select_one)**: Choose one of the available subscription options to receive different types of newsletters.
* **Payment Preference (select_multiple)**: Select one or multiple payment methods you are willing to use for subscription payments.
* **Address (text)**: Enter your physical address where you can be reached.
* **City (text)**: Enter the name of the city where you reside.
* **State (text)**: Enter the name of the state where you reside.
* **Country (text)**: Enter the name of the country where you reside.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure a successful submission.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact our support team for assistance.
* We will not share your information with third parties without your consent.
