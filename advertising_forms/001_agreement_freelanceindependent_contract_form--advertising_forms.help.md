# Agreement - FreelanceIndependent Contract Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to establish an agreement between a contractor (freelancer) and a client. It outlines the terms and conditions of the project, including the services to be provided, payment terms, and confidentiality agreement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact information as the contractor.
2. Provide the client's name and address.
3. Specify the project description, start date, end date, and payment amount.
4. Select the payment terms and services to be provided.
5. Indicate your agreement to keep client information confidential.
6. If applicable, specify the termination notice period.
7. Sign the form with your full name and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contractor Name** (`contractor_name`, text, required): Enter your full legal name.
* **Contractor Address** (`contractor_address`, text, required): Enter your mailing address for the contractor.
* **Contractor Email** (`contractor_email`, email, required): Enter your primary contact email.
* **Contractor Phone** (`contractor_phone`, text, optional): Enter your best contact number (if applicable).
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the full legal name of the client.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the services to be provided.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date work will commence.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the expected completion date (if applicable).
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required): Enter the total agreed compensation.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, select_one, required): Select one of the following payment terms:
	+ Net 15: Payment is due 15 days after completion.
	+ Net 30: Payment is due 30 days after completion.
	+ Upon Receipt: Payment is due upon receipt of final deliverables.
	+ 50% Upfront: Payment is due 50% of the total amount upfront.
* **Services Provided** (`services_provided`, select_multiple, required): Select all services applicable to the project:
	+ Marketing Strategy
	+ SEO Services
	+ Content Creation
	+ Social Media Management
	+ Graphic Design
	+ Email Marketing
* **Confidentiality Agreement** (`confidentiality_agreement`, select_one, required): Indicate your agreement to keep client information confidential.
	+ 'True': You agree to keep client information confidential.
	+ 'False': You do not agree to keep client information confidential.
* **Termination Notice Period** (`termination_notice`, text, optional): Specify the termination notice period (e.g., 14 days).
* **Contractor Signature** (`signature_placeholder`, text, required): Sign the form with your full name as signature.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required): Enter the date of signature.
