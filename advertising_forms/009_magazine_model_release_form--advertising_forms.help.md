# Magazine Model Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain a model's consent and release for the use of their image in advertising and media purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all necessary information and signatures.
2. Make sure to sign the form as the model and have a witness sign it as well, if necessary.
3. Ensure that the photographer or authorized representative has signed the form, if required.

* **Model Name** (`model_name`, text, required): This is the name of the model who will be featured in the photo shoot.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required): This is the contact information for the model, including their address and phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): This is the address of the model, where they can be contacted.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): This is the email address of the model.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): This is the phone number of the model.
* **Photo Date** (`photo_date`, date, required): This is the date the photo was taken.
* **Photo Location** (`photo_location`, text, optional): This is the location where the photo was taken.
* **Model Agreement** (`model_agreement`, text, optional): This is where the model agrees to the terms and conditions of the photo shoot.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): This is the name of the client who commissioned the photo shoot.
