# mary-kay-beauty-profile-by-julie-potts - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about their beauty profile, specifically for advertising purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
2. Enter your First Name, Last Name, and Email addresses in the corresponding fields.
3. Enter your Phone number, Birth Date, and Zip Code in their respective fields.
4. If you have a business, enter your Business Owner, Business Name, and Business Email addresses in their fields.
5. Enter any relevant Social Media information, if applicable.
6. If applicable, enter any Consultant ID, Consultant Name, and Consultant Email addresses.
7. Select a Beauty Profile Image or document, if applicable.
8. Read and agree to the Beauty Profile Terms and Conditions.
9. Finally, provide any additional comments you have about your beauty profile.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address associated with your identification.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, required): Enter your birth date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Address 1** (`address_1`, text, optional): Enter your address line 1.
* **Address 2** (`address_2`, text, optional): Enter your address line 2.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter your city.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter your state.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Country** (`country`, text, optional): Enter your country.
* **Business Owner** (`business_owner`, text, optional): Enter your business owner's name.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, optional): Enter your business name.
* **Business Email** (`business_email`, email, optional): Enter your business email address.
* **Business Phone** (`business_phone`, text, optional): Enter your business phone number.
* **Social Media** (`social_media`, text, optional): Enter your social media information, if applicable.
* **Consultant ID** (`consultant_id`, text, optional): Enter your consultant ID, if applicable.
* **Consultant Name** (`consultant_name`, text, optional): Enter your consultant name, if applicable.
* **Consultant Email** (`consultant_email`, email, optional): Enter your consultant email address, if applicable.
* **Consultant Phone** (`consultant_phone`, text, optional): Enter your consultant phone number, if applicable.
* **Beauty Profile Image** (`beauty_profile_image`, text, optional): Select a beauty profile image, if applicable.
* **Beauty Profile Document** (`beauty_profile_document`, text, optional): Select a beauty profile document, if applicable.
* **Beauty Profile Terms and Conditions** (`beauty_profile_terms_and_conditions`, text, optional): Read and agree to the beauty profile terms and conditions.
* **Beauty Profile Comments** (`beauty_profile_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments about your beauty profile.
* **Beauty Profile Revisions** (`beauty_profile_revisions`, text, optional): Enter any revisions about your beauty profile.

Note: All fields marked as "optional" can be left blank if not applicable.
