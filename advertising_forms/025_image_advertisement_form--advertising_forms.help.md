# Image Advertisement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Image Advertisement Form is used to collect information about an image advertisement for advertising purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the form data.
2. Upload or enter the image.
3. Enter the ad URL.
4. Provide a description of the advertisement.
5. Select the category of the advertisement.
6. Choose the type of advertisement.
7. Enter the ad budget.
8. Enter the ad cost.
9. Specify the ad duration.
10. Enter the start date and time of the advertisement.
11. Enter the end date and time of the advertisement.
12. Select the advertisement's status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data** (1) (text, optional): Enter a brief text description of the advertisement.
* **Image** (2) (text, optional): Enter the image URL or upload an image file.
* **Ad URL** (3) (text, optional): Enter the URL of the advertisement.
* **Description** (4) (text, optional): Enter a brief text description of the advertisement.
* **Category** (5) (select one, optional): Choose a category for the advertisement.
* **Ad Type** (6) (select one, optional): Choose the type of advertisement.
* **Ad Budget** (7) (number, optional): Enter the budget for the advertisement.
* **Ad Cost** (8) (number, optional): Enter the cost of the advertisement.
* **Ad Duration** (9) (time, optional): Enter the duration of the advertisement.
* **Ad Start Date** (10) (date, optional): Enter the start date of the advertisement.
* **Ad Start Time** (11) (time, optional): Enter the start time of the advertisement.
* **Ad End Date** (12) (date, optional): Enter the end date of the advertisement.
* **Ad End Time** (13) (time, optional): Enter the end time of the advertisement.
* **Ad Status** (14) (select one, optional): Choose the status of the advertisement.
