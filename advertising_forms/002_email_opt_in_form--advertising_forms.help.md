# EMail Opt In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect contact information and preferences for users who wish to receive emails from the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the first field. This is a required field.
2. Provide your **Email** address. This is also a required field, and it should be a valid email address.
3. If you have a company, provide it in the **Company** field. This field is not required.
4. Enter your **Job Title**, if applicable. This field is not required.
5. Select your **Job Function** from the options provided, if applicable. This field is not required.
6. Enter your **Job Department**, if applicable. This field is not required.
7. Select your **Job Location** from the options provided, if applicable. This field is not required.
8. In the **Agreement** section, choose 'Yes' to agree to receive promotional emails and other communications.
9. In the **Consent** section, select one of the options to consent to the terms and conditions.
10. If you have a second **Job Title**, enter it in the corresponding field. This field is not required.
11. If you have a second **Job Function**, enter it in the corresponding field. This field is not required.
12. If you have a second **Job Location**, enter it in the corresponding field. This field is not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (required): Enter your first name as it is known to you.
* **Email** (required): Enter a valid email address to which you want to receive communications from us.
* **Company** (optional): If you represent a company, enter it here.
* **Job Title** (optional): Enter your job title as you want it to be represented.
* **Job Function** (optional): Select your job function from the options provided.
* **Job Department** (optional): Enter the department you work in, if applicable.
* **Job Location** (optional): Select your job location from the options provided.
* **Agreement** (required): Choose 'Yes' to agree to receive promotional emails and other communications.
* **Consent** (required): Select one of the options to consent to the terms and conditions.
* **Job Title 2** (optional): Enter your second job title, if applicable.
* **Job Function 2** (optional): Enter your second job function, if applicable.
* **Job Location 2** (optional): Enter your second job location, if applicable.
