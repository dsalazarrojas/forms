# Advertising Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from advertisers to create an effective advertising campaign. It gathers details about the advertiser and their campaign goals, target audience, budget, and other relevant information to help us better understand their needs and create a successful campaign.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your company name in the "Company Name" field.
2. Provide your name and title in the "Contact Person" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Enter your website URL in the "Website URL" field (if applicable).
6. Select your primary advertising goal from the list of options in the "Primary Advertising Goal" field.
7. Describe your target audience in the "Target Audience Description" field.
8. Choose the platforms where you would like your ads to appear from the list of options in the "Preferred Platforms" field.
9. Select your estimated budget for the campaign from the list of options in the "Estimated Budget" field.
10. Enter the desired start date for the campaign in the "Desired Start Date" field.
11. Enter the desired end date for the campaign (if applicable) in the "Desired End Date" field.
12. If applicable, enter any additional comments or instructions in the "Additional Instructions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter your name and title.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Website URL** (`website_url`, text, required): Enter your website URL (if applicable).
* **Campaign Name** (`campaign_name`, text, required): Enter a name for the campaign.
* **Primary Advertising Goal** (`advertising_goal`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ Brand Awareness
	+ Lead Generation
	+ Sales Conversion
	+ Website Traffic
	+ App Installs
* **Target Audience Description** (`target_audience`, text, required): Briefly describe who you want to reach.
* **Preferred Platforms** (`ad_platforms`, select_multiple, optional): Select the platforms where you want your ads to appear:
	+ Social Media (Facebook/Instagram)
	+ Search Engine (Google/Bing)
	+ Display Networks
	+ Email Newsletters
	+ Print Media
* **Estimated Budget** (`budget_range`, select_one, required): Select your budget range for the campaign:
	+ Less than $500
	+ $500 - $1,000
	+ $1,000 - $5,000
	+ $5,000 - $10,000
	+ $10,000+
* **Desired Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the desired start date for the campaign.
* **Desired End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the desired end date for the campaign (if applicable).
* **Additional Instructions** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any specific requirements or creative direction.
