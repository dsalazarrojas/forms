# Souvenir Booklet Advertising Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect advertising information from businesses for inclusion in a souvenir booklet.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact details, including your name and phone number.
2. Select the size of the advertisement you would like to have in the souvenir booklet.
3. Enter your business details, including your business name and phone number.
4. Choose a payment method (e.g. card, bank transfer, or check).
5. Enter payment details if necessary.
6. Specify how you would like to be contacted (e.g. phone, email, or mail).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details** (Text): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Souvenir Ad Size** (Select One): Choose the size of the advertisement you would like to have in the souvenir booklet. Options include Large, Medium, and Small.
* **Business Details** (Text): Enter any information about your business that is relevant to the advertisement.
* **Payment Method** (Select One): Choose the method you would like to use for payment. Options include Card, Bank Transfer, and Check.
* **Payment Details** (Text): Enter any additional payment information that may be required.
* **Contact Method** (Select One): Choose how you would like to be contacted. Options include Phone, Email, and Mail.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted via email.
* **Phone** (Number): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted via phone.
* **Business Name** (Text): Enter your business name for inclusion in the souvenir booklet.
* **Business Phone** (Text): Enter your business phone number for inclusion in the souvenir booklet.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your contact and business details for accuracy.
- Ensure that your payment method is correct and complete.
- Choose a contact method that is best for you, such as phone or email.
- Enter any necessary payment details to complete the payment method chosen.
