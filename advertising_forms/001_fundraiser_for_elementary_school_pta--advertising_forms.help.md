# Fundraiser For Elementary School PTA - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for fundraising events at elementary school PTAs. It collects data that will be used to facilitate communication and organization for these events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields as prompted. The form is self-explanatory, and each field has a clear label and description to help guide you through the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Fundraiser For Elementary School PTA** (`title`, string, required): Enter a descriptive title for the fundraising event.
* **school_info** (`school_info`, string, required): Enter information about the school, such as the school name, location, or grade level.
* **Fundraiser Info** (`fundraiser_info`, string, optional): Enter details about the fundraising event, such as the date, time, and type of event.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, string, optional): Enter contact information for the person handling the fundraising event, such as their name, phone number, and email.
* **Social Media** (`social_media`, string, optional): Enter social media links or handles where the fundraising event will be promoted.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a contact email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, string, optional): Enter a contact phone number.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter additional notes or comments about the fundraising event.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, string, optional): Enter the name of the school.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure successful communication and organization of the fundraising event.
* Review each field carefully to ensure that you're providing the correct type of information.
