# Email Design Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help you create an email that effectively communicates with your target audience. It will guide you through the process of selecting the right email design and content that suits your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1.  Fill out the form fields with the required information.
2.  Make sure to complete all the required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
3.  Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
4.  Once submitted, your answers will be reviewed and used to generate a personalized email design.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **customer_details** (`customer_details`, `text`, required: false): 
    This field is used to gather information about you as a customer. Please provide your name, phone number, and any other relevant details that will be used to personalize the email.
*   **email_content** (`email_content`, `text`, required: true): 
    This field is where you provide the content of your email. Please describe what you want to communicate to your target audience and what you want to include in your email.
*   **design_preferences** (`design_preferences`, `text`, required: true): 
    This field is where you specify your design preferences for the email. Please indicate what design style, color scheme, and other visual elements you prefer for your email.

## Tips

*   Make sure to be as specific as possible when filling out the form.
*   Use clear and concise language to ensure accuracy in the generated email.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can contact us for further clarification.
