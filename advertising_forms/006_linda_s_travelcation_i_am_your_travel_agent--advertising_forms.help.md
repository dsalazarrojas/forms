# linda_s_travelforms - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "linda_s_travelforms" form is designed to collect information about travel-related activities. It's likely used for travel agency or tour operator purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. The form is divided into two pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **form_data** (`form_data`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your travel plans or activities.
- **contact_info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide your contact information, such as your name and address.
- **travel_destination** (`travel_destination`, text, optional): Enter the travel destination or location.
- **checkin_date** (`checkin_date`, date, optional): Enter the check-in date for your travel plans.
- **checkout_date** (`checkout_date`, date, optional): Enter the check-out date for your travel plans.
- **travel_agent** (`travel_agent`, select_one, optional): Choose 'Yes' if you're traveling as part of a travel agency or tour operator group.
- **contact_name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person you'd like to be in contact with.
- **contact_email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person you'd like to be in contact with.
- **contact_phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person you'd like to be in contact with.
- **travel_notes** (`travel_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional travel-related notes or comments.

## Tips

- Make sure to fill out the required fields accurately.
- Double-check your contact information for correctness.
- Use clear and concise language when entering your travel notes.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact the form administrator for clarification.
