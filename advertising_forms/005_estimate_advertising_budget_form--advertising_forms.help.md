# Estimate Advertising Budget Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Estimate Advertising Budget Form" is designed to collect information from users about their advertising needs and preferences. This form will help us understand whether you are a potential customer for our advertising services and if so, what your estimated budget for advertising is, how often you want to see ads, and any additional comments or descriptions you may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Decide whether you are a "Yes" or "No" for being a potential customer.
2. Enter your estimated ad budget as a number.
3. Provide a brief description of your ad budget to help us understand your needs.
4. Choose "Yes" or "No" for how often you want to see ads.
5. Enter a brief description of your ad frequency preference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What type of user are you?**: This field is used to determine whether you are a potential customer for our advertising services. Please choose "Yes" if you are interested in our advertising services, and "No" otherwise.
* **Estimated ad budget**: This field is used to collect your estimated budget for advertising. Please enter a number that reflects your current budget for advertising.
* **Description for ad budget estimate**: This field is used to provide additional context about your ad budget. Please provide a brief description of your estimated budget to help us better understand your needs.
* **How often do you want ads?**: This field is used to determine your desired ad frequency. Please choose "Yes" if you want to see ads frequently, and "No" otherwise.
* **Description for ad frequency**: This field is used to provide additional context about your ad frequency preference. Please provide a brief description of your ad frequency preference to help us better understand your needs.

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and honest information to help us provide you with the best possible service.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* This form is for internal use only, so please do not share it with anyone else.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for clarification before submitting the form.
