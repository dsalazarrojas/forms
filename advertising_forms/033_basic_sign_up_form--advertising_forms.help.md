## Basic Sign Up Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Basic Sign Up Form is used to collect information from users who want to sign up for your service. This form is used to gather contact information, areas of interest, and preferences for communication and updates. It also includes a section for users to agree to the terms and conditions and the privacy policy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your Last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email address" field. Please make sure it is a valid email address.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone number" field.
5. If you have a company, enter it in the "Company name (optional)" field.
6. Select all the areas of interest that apply from the "Areas of interest" dropdown menu.
7. Enter what you are most interested in from the "What are you most interested in?" field.
8. Choose how you heard about us from the "How did you hear about us?" dropdown menu.
9. Decide on your communication preferences by selecting whether to receive email updates, marketing emails, and SMS notifications.
10. Agree to the terms and conditions and privacy policy by selecting "I Agree" from the respective dropdown menus.
11. Confirm that the information you provided is accurate by selecting "I Confirm" from the "I confirm this information is accurate" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Company name (optional)** (`company`, `text`, optional): Enter your company name if you have one.
* **Areas of interest** (`interest_areas`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the areas of interest that apply (Marketing, Sales, Technology, Finance, Customer Service, Other).
* **What are you most interested in?** (`product_interest`, `text`, optional): Enter what you are most interested in.
* **How did you hear about us?** (`how_heard`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how you heard about us (Search Engine, Social Media, Referral, Advertisement, Email, Other).
* **Receive email updates** (`email_updates`, `select_one`, optional): Decide whether to receive email updates.
* **Receive marketing emails** (`marketing_emails`, `select_one`, optional): Decide whether to receive marketing emails.
* **Receive SMS notifications** (`sms_notifications`, `select_one`, optional): Decide whether to receive SMS notifications.
* **I agree to the terms and conditions** (`terms_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Agree to the terms and conditions.
* **I agree to the privacy policy** (`privacy_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Agree to the privacy policy.
* **I confirm this information is accurate** (`signup_confirmation`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that the information you provided is accurate.
