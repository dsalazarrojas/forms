# Ad Spot Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ad Spot Booking Form is designed to collect information from advertisers to process their ad spot bookings effectively. It's essential to fill out this form carefully to ensure that your booking is processed correctly and promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **Company / Organization Name** in the "Company / Organization Name" field. This is a required field and should be completed accurately.
2.  Enter the **Contact Person Name** in the "Contact Person Name" field. This field is also required.
3.  Enter your **Contact Email** in the "Contact Email" field. This field is required and should be a valid email address.
4.  Enter your **Contact Phone** in the "Contact Phone" field. This field is required.
5.  Select the **Publication / Platform** where you'd like to advertise. This field is required and has options such as "Monthly Newsletter", "Website Homepage", "Podcast Audio Spot", "Quarterly Printed Magazine", and "Social Media Featured Post".
6.  Select the **Ad Size / Type** for your ad spot. This field is required and has options such as "Full Page / Banner", "Half Page / Sidebar", "Quarter Page / Inline", "Sponsored Content Article", and "30-Second Audio Clip".
7.  Enter the **Campaign Start Date** for your ad spot. This field is required and should be in the correct date format.
8.  Enter the **Campaign End Date** for your ad spot. This field is required and should be in the correct date format.
9.  Enter the number of **Placements / Rotations** for your ad spot. This field is required.
10. Select the status of your **Creative Assets**. This field is required and has options such as "Assets ready for upload", "Assets in development", and "Need design assistance".
11. If applicable, enter the **Destination URL (Click-through)** for your ad spot.
12. Enter any **Special Placement Requests or Notes** for your ad spot.
13. Select your **Billing Preference**. This field is required and has options such as "Invoice via Email", "Credit Card (Pre-pay)", and "Existing Corporate Account".
14. Finally, confirm that you agree to the **Advertising Terms and Conditions**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company / Organization Name**: Enter your company's or organization's name accurately.
* **Contact Person Name**: Enter the name of the person responsible for this booking.
* **Contact Email**: Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Publication / Platform**: Select where you'd like to advertise.
* **Ad Size / Type**: Select the size and type of your ad spot.
* **Campaign Start Date**: Enter the start date of your ad campaign.
* **Campaign End Date**: Enter the end date of your ad campaign.
* **Number of Placements / Rotations**: Enter the number of ad spots you'd like to book.
* **Creative Assets Status**: Select the status of your creative assets.
* **Destination URL (Click-through)**: Enter the URL for your ad spot, if applicable.
* **Special Placement Requests or Notes**: Enter any special requests or notes for your ad spot.
* **Billing Preference**: Select your billing preference.
* **Advertising Terms and Conditions**: Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions of our advertising policy.
