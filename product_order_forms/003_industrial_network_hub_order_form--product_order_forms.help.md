# industrial_network_hub_order_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The industrial network hub order form is a form used to place new orders for products. It collects customer information, purchase details, and notes for order completion. The purpose of this form is to ensure accurate and thorough information is collected to process the order efficiently and correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the industrial network hub order form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: **Customer Name**, **Company**, **Email**, and **Phone**.
2. Enter any additional information you want to provide in the **Notes** field.
3. If applicable, provide **Purchase References** and **Requested Drawing** details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **customer-name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter the customer's name as it appears on their business card or letterhead.
* **company** (`company`, text, required): Enter the customer's company name.
* **email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional information about the order, such as specific product requirements or special instructions.
* **purchase-references** (`purchase_references`, text, optional): If this is a follow-up order, provide reference numbers from previous orders to help with order processing.
* **requested-drawing** (`requested_drawing`, text, optional): If you have a specific drawing or design requirements, enter the details here.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Use the **Notes** field to provide any special instructions or requirements for the order.
* If you need to follow up on a previous order, use **Purchase References** to help our team locate the original order.
* If you have a specific drawing requirement, use **Requested Drawing** to provide the necessary details.
