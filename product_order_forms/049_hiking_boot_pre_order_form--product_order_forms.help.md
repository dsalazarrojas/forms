# Hiking Boot Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hiking Boot Pre Order Form is designed to collect information from customers who wish to pre-order hiking boots. This form helps us understand your preferences and requirements for your hiking boots, ensuring that we can better serve you in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **User Details** (`user_details`, text, optional): This section is for you to enter your personal details, such as your name, email address, and any other relevant information.
2. **Hiking Order** (`order_details`, text, optional): Provide your hiking order preferences, including your desired boot type, size, and color.
3. **Payment** (`payment_details`, text, optional): Enter your payment details, including your method of payment and any relevant account information.
4. **Shipping Preferences** (`shipping_preferences`, text, optional): Let us know your preferred shipping method and any special requirements.
5. **Review** (`review`, note, optional): This is a space for you to leave any comments or feedback about your order.
6. **Order Review** (`order_review`, text, optional): Review your order details before submitting.
7. **Payment Confirmation** (`payment_confirmation`, text, optional): Confirm your payment details.
8. **Review Confirmation** (`review_confirmation`, text, optional): Confirm your review details.
9. **Shipping Confirmation** (`shipping_confirmation`, text, optional): Confirm your shipping details.
10. **Order Confirmation** (`order_confirmation`, text, optional): Confirm your order details.
11. **Review Request** (`review_request`, text, optional): If you have any questions or need further clarification, use this section.
12. **Payment Request** (`payment_request`, text, optional): If you have any payment-related concerns, use this section.
13. **Shipping Request** (`shipping_request`, text, optional): If you have any shipping-related concerns, use this section.
14. **Review Request 2** (`review_request2`, text, optional): If you have any further questions or concerns, use this section.
