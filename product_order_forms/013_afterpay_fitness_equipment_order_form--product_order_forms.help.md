# Afterpay Fitness Equipment Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Afterpay Fitness Equipment Order Form is designed to collect necessary information from clients for afterpay fitness equipment orders. Completing this form ensures that your order is processed efficiently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. For client details, provide your full name as it should be spelled and formatted for delivery purposes.
3. Enter a valid email address for order confirmation and future correspondence.
4. Specify the model or SKU of the equipment you are interested in purchasing.
5. Enter the number of units you would like to order.
6. Choose your preferred delivery option from the list:
	* Standard
	* White glove
	* Pickup
7. If the equipment requires assembly, select 'True'. Otherwise, select 'False'.
8. Indicate whether you would like to use Afterpay payment options by selecting 'True' or 'False'.
9. (Optional) Provide any installation notes for the crew in the "Installation notes" field.
10. (Optional) Enter your daytime contact phone number for the installation crew.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client name** (`Client name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it should be spelled and formatted for delivery purposes.
* **Contact email** (`Contact email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address for order confirmation and future correspondence.
* **Equipment model** (`Equipment model`, `text`, required): Specify the model or SKU of the equipment you are interested in purchasing.
* **Quantity** (`Quantity`, `number`, required): Enter the number of units you would like to order.
* **Delivery option** (`Delivery option`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred delivery option from the list:
	+ Standard
	+ White glove
	+ Pickup
* **Assembly required** (`Assembly required`, `select_one`, required): If the equipment requires assembly, select 'True'. Otherwise, select 'False'.
* **Use Afterpay** (`Use Afterpay`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you would like to use Afterpay payment options by selecting 'True' or 'False'.
* **Installation notes** (`Installation notes`, `text`, optional): (Optional) Provide any notes for the installation crew.
* **Contact phone** (`Contact phone`, `text`, optional): (Optional) Enter your daytime contact phone number for the installation crew.
