# water_treatment_equipment_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The water treatment equipment order form is a necessary step in the ordering process for water treatment equipment. It captures essential customer information and order details to ensure accurate and efficient processing of the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in all required fields marked with an asterisk (*) symbol.
2. Enter your name and email address in the customer name and email fields, respectively.
3. Enter your phone number in the customer phone field.
4. Select the type of equipment from the list provided in the equipment type field.
5. Provide a detailed description of the product in the product details field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address that we can use to communicate with you about your order.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for any potential follow-up or verification purposes.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of equipment you are interested in ordering from the provided options (equipment a, equipment b, or equipment c).
* **Product Details** (`product_details`, text, required): Describe the product you are interested in ordering, including any specific features or requirements you need.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have reviewed all fields carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* Please note that submitting this form is the first step in the ordering process; we will follow up with you to confirm your order and provide any necessary further instructions.
