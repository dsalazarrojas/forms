# industrial_air_hose_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact and delivery information from customers interested in purchasing industrial air hoses for their business or industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Provide your contact details, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Enter your delivery address to ensure smooth and efficient delivery of the industrial air hose.
3. Select the industrial air hose you are interested in purchasing from the available options.
4. If you have any special instructions or requests for the delivery, please enter them in the "Special Instructions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name, email, and phone number so we can get in touch with you about your order.

### Delivery Address
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your delivery address to ensure that the industrial air hose is delivered to the right location.

### Product Selection
* **Product Selection** (`product_selection`, `text`, required/optional): Select the industrial air hose that you are interested in purchasing from the available options. Please make sure to choose the correct product according to your needs.

### Special Instructions
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, `text`, required/optional): If you have any special requests or instructions for the delivery, such as "deliver to the loading dock" or "please bring with a pump", enter them here.
