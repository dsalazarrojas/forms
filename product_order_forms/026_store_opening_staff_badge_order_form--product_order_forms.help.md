# store-opening-staff-badge-order-form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from store staff for issuing badges.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Staff details** page with the required information.
2. In the **Roles** page, select all roles assigned to the staff member.
3. In the **Payments** page, enter the payment information for the badge.
4. In the **Payment method** page, select the payment method used for the badge.
5. In the **Payment terms** and **Payment total** page, enter the payment terms and total amount.
6. In the **Billing address** page, enter the billing address information.
7. In the **Store information** page, enter the store name, phone number, email, and address.
8. In the **Manager information** page, enter the manager's name, phone number, email, title, and badge ID.
9. Finally, enter the store owner's name and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Staff details** (text, required): Enter the staff member's name and other basic details.
* **Roles** (select multiple, required): Select all roles assigned to the staff member.
* **Shipping** (text, not required): Enter additional shipping information.
* **Payments** (text, required): Enter payment information for the badge.
* **Payment method** (select one, required): Select the payment method used for the badge.
* **Payment terms** (number, required): Enter the payment terms for the badge.
* **Payment total** (number, required): Enter the total payment amount.
* **Store address** (text, not required): Enter additional store address information.
* **Billing address** (text, required): Enter the billing address.
* **Billing name** (text, required): Enter the name to be used for billing purposes.
* **Billing phone** (text, not required): Enter the phone number to be used for billing purposes.
* **Billing email** (email, not required): Enter the email to be used for billing purposes.
* **Store name** (text, required): Enter the name of the store.
* **Store phone** (text, required): Enter the phone number of the store.
* **Store email** (email, required): Enter the email of the store.
* **Store address** (text, required): Enter the address of the store.
* **Manager name** (text, required): Enter the name of the manager.
* **Manager phone** (text, required): Enter the phone number of the manager.
* **Manager email** (email, required): Enter the email of the manager.
* **Manager title** (text, required): Enter the title of the manager.
* **Manager badge ID** (text, not required): Enter additional manager badge ID information.
* **Manager badge number** (text, not required): Enter additional manager badge number information.
* **Manager badge pin** (text, not required): Enter additional manager badge pin information.
* **Store owner name** (text, required): Enter the name of the store owner.
* **Store owner phone** (text, required): Enter the phone number of the store owner.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all the required fields to complete the form successfully.
- Double-check the accuracy of the information entered.
- If you need help with any field, click on the help icon next to it.
