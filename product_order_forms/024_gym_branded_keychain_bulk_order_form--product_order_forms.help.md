# Gym Branded Keychain Bulk Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for bulk orders of gym-branded keychains.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product you wish to order.
2. **Quantity**: Enter the number of items you would like to order.
3. **Email**: Enter your email address for billing and communication purposes.
4. **Phone**: Enter your phone number for billing and communication purposes.
5. **Company Name**: Enter the name of your company or business.
6. **Order Type**: Select the type of order you are placing.
7. **Shipping Method**: Select the shipping method for your order.
8. **Shipping Address**: Enter the shipping address where the order will be delivered.
9. **Billing Address**: Enter the billing address associated with your company or business.
10. **Billing Email**: Enter the email address for billing purposes.
11. **Billing Phone**: Enter the phone number for billing purposes.
12. **Order Notes**: Enter any additional notes or instructions for the order.
13. **Sales Tax**: Enter the sales tax rate for your order.
14. **Discount**: Enter any applicable discount for your order.
15. **Order Date**: Enter the date you would like to place the order by.
16. **Order Time**: Enter the time you would like to place the order.
17. **Customer Name**: Enter your name as the customer placing the order.
18. **Customer Email**: Enter your email address.
19. **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number.
20. **Customer Address**: Enter your address.
21. **Customer Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments for your order.
