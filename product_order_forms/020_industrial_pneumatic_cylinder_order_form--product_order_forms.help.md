# industrial_pneumatic_cylinder_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers order industrial pneumatic cylinders. It gathers essential information from the customer and their desired product specifications, shipping details, and any additional notes. This form ensures that orders are processed accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Customer Info**: Enter your name and contact information.
2. **Product Info**: Provide details about the industrial pneumatic cylinder you wish to order, including its specifications or any special requirements.
3. **Shipping Info**: Enter your shipping address, including your preferred shipping method and any specific delivery instructions.
4. **Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info** (`Customer Info`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, phone number, and email address to help us contact you about your order.
* **Product Info** (`Product Info`, text, required/optional): Describe the specific industrial pneumatic cylinder you are looking to purchase, including its size, material, or other relevant details.
* **Shipping Info** (`Shipping Info`, text, required/optional): Enter your shipping address, including your street address, city, state, and ZIP code, as well as your preferred shipping method.
* **Note** (`Note`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or special instructions about your order, such as special handling requirements or delivery deadlines.

**Tips**: Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely. Incomplete or incorrect information may cause delays in processing your order. If you have any questions or need further assistance, please don't hesitate to contact us at `form@gic.mx`.
