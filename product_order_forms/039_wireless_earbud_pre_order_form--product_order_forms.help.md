# Wireless Earbud Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to pre-order wireless earbuds. Please fill out the form with your information to reserve your earbuds and we will contact you when they are available for shipping.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting, make sure you have all the required information ready. You can complete the form in the following steps:

1.  Fill in your information, including name and email address.
2.  Select the order date you would like to receive the earbuds on.
3.  Specify the quantity of earbuds you would like to order.
4.  Choose your preferred shipping option (Standard, Express, or Free).
5.  Select your preferred payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
6.  Enter the total cost of your order.
7.  Add any additional order notes or comments.
8.  Specify the shipping details if necessary.
9.  Enter your phone number (optional).
10.  Click on the "Submit" button to send your pre-order form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, optional): This is your name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): This is your email address where we will contact you when the earbuds are available.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, optional): This is the date you would like to receive the earbuds on.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, optional): This is the number of earbuds you would like to order.
* **Shipping Cost** (`shipping_cost`, `number`, optional): This is the shipping cost associated with your order.
* **Shipping Option** (`shipping_option`, `select_one`, optional): This is your preferred shipping option. You can choose between Standard, Express, or Free.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, optional): This is your preferred payment method. You can choose between Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, `number`, optional): This is the total cost of your order.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, `note`, optional): This is any additional notes or comments you may have about your order.
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, `text`, optional): This is any additional information about your shipping preferences.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): This is your phone number (optional).
* **Order ID** (`order_id`, `number`, optional): This is a unique ID for your order.
* **Status** (`status`, `select_multiple`, optional): This is the status of your order. You can choose between Pending, Processing, or Delivered.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are properly filled out.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [your contact information].
* We will contact you when the earbuds are available for shipping.
* You will receive a confirmation email when your order is processed.
