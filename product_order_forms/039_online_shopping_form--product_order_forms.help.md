# online_shopping_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate online shopping by allowing customers to input their order details, including product selection, quantity, price, shipping date, customer information, and payment method. It helps customers place orders efficiently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the product name in the "Product Name" field.
2. Enter the desired quantity of the product.
3. Enter the price of the product.
4. Choose a shipping date from the calendar or enter a date manually.
5. Enter your customer name.
6. Enter your customer email address.
7. Enter your customer phone number.
8. Select your payment method from the options provided (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
9. If you selected "Yes" as your payment method, provide more information in the "Payment Method Other" field.
10. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
11. Select the options for your order from the respective "Select One" and "Select Multiple" fields.
12. Finally, click "Submit" to complete your order.
