# Store Front Banner Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect order details for a store front banner order. It requests information about the banner's name, artwork, location, designer contact, payment method, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*). These fields must be completed in order to submit the form.
2. Enter the banner's name and location as specified.
3. Provide the designer's contact information, including email address, phone number, and other details as requested.
4. Choose the payment method and enter any additional payment details.
5. Agree to the terms and conditions.
6. Enter any additional comments if desired.
7. Select the currency and shipping location of the order.
8. Choose the shipping method from the available options.
9. Finally, review your order details and submit the form.
