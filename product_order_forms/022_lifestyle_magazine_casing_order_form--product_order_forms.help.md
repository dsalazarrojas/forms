# Lifestyle Magazine Casing Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lifestyle Magazine Casing Order Form is a tool used to order Lifestyle Magazine products. This form allows customers to select a magazine title, choose their preferred product casing size, enter their shipping and billing information, and confirm their order details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently, please follow these steps:

1. Select the magazine title from the dropdown menu that suits your needs.
2. Choose your preferred product casing size from the available options.
3. Enter the desired quantity of the product.
4. Provide your email address for communication and order confirmation.
5. Enter your shipping information, including shipping address, city, state, and zip code.
6. Enter your billing information, including billing name, address, city, state, and zip code.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Magazine Selection** (`magazine_selection`, select_one, required: false): Select the title of the magazine you would like to order. This will help us process your order accurately.
* **Product Casing Size** (`casing_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the size of the product casing that suits your needs.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required: false): Enter the number of products you would like to order.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you for order updates and confirmation.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for any additional contact or shipping information.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **Product Details** (`product_details`, text, required: false): Enter any additional details about the product you are ordering.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the product you are ordering.
* **Product Code** (`product_code`, text, required: false): Enter the product code or reference number.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the product you are ordering.
* **Product Price** (`product_price`, number, required: false): Enter the price of the product.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required: false): Enter the shipping address where the product will be delivered.
* **Shipping City** (`shipping_city`, text, required: false): Enter the city of the shipping address.
* **Shipping State** (`shipping_state`, text, required: false): Enter the state of the shipping address.
* **Shipping Zip** (`shipping_zip`, text, required: false): Enter the zip code of the shipping address.
* **Shipping Country** (`shipping_country`, text, required: false): Enter the country of the shipping address.
* **Billing Name** (`billing_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the billing contact.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, text, required: false): Enter the billing address.
* **Billing City** (`billing_city`, text, required: false): Enter the city of the billing address.
* **Billing State** (`billing_state`, text, required: false): Enter the state of the billing address.
* **Billing Zip** (`billing_zip`, text, required: false): Enter the zip code of the billing address.
* **Billing Country** (`billing_country`, text, required: false): Enter the country of the billing address.
* **Total Price** (`total_price`, text, required: false): The total price of the order will be calculated automatically.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, text, required: false): This field is for internal use and will display the status of your order.

Note: All fields marked as required: false can be left blank if not applicable.
