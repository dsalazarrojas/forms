# Farm Harvest Equipment Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Farm Harvest Equipment Order Form is used to collect information from farmers and agricultural businesses for the order of farm harvest equipment. This form is essential for ensuring that all necessary equipment is ordered, and the correct information is provided for each order.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, ensure that you have all the necessary information about your farm and order before filling out this form.
2.  Select all the machinery you require for the order.
3.  Provide the necessary details for the order, including the delivery location and any additional information.
4.  Confirm your order by providing your contact information and any other required details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Farm Harvest Equipment Order Form** (`farm_harvest_equipment_order_form_page`): This is the title of the form, which should not be filled out.
*   **Customer Details** (`customer_details_label`): Enter your farm name and any other relevant contact information.
*   **Select Machinery** (`select_machinery`): Choose all the equipment you need for the order, using the drop-down list.
*   **Machinery Details** (`machinery_details_label`): Enter any additional details about the equipment you have selected.
*   **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details_label`): Enter the delivery location and any other relevant details for the equipment delivery.
*   **Order Total** (`order_total_label`): Enter the total cost of the ordered equipment.
*   **Payment Details** (`payment_details_label`): Enter your payment method and any other necessary details.
*   **Confirmation** (`confirmation_label`): Confirm your order by providing your contact information and any other required details.

## Tips

*   Ensure that all fields are filled out completely and accurately.
*   Double-check your order details before submitting the form.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact your sales representative for assistance.
