# Order Form Product Detail - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Order Form Product Detail is a form used to collect information from customers about their product orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **product id** of the product being ordered.
2. Enter the **product name** of the product being ordered.
3. Enter the **order date** in the format MM/dd/yyyy (month/day/year).
4. Enter the **order time** in the format HH:MM (24-hour clock).
5. Enter the **order total** amount.
6. Enter the **customer name** as it appears on their billing invoice.
7. Enter the **customer email** in the format of [customer@example.com](mailto:customer@example.com).
8. Enter the **customer phone** number without any leading zeros.
9. Enter the **customer address** in a concise and clear format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **product_order** (`product_order_form`, text, optional): Enter the product being ordered.
* **product id** (`product_id`, number, optional): Enter the unique identifier for the product being ordered.
* **product name** (`product_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the product being ordered.
* **order date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the order was made in the format MM/dd/yyyy (month/day/year).
* **order time** (`order_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the order was made in the format HH:MM (24-hour clock).
* **order total** (`order_total`, number, optional): Enter the total cost of the order.
* **customer name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the customer as it appears on their invoice.
* **customer email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the customer in the format of [customer@example.com](mailto:customer@example.com).
* **customer phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the customer without any leading zeros.
* **customer address** (`customer_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the customer in a concise and clear format.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information entered.
