# Safety Lighting Control Unit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Safety Lighting Control Unit Order Form is a document used to gather information necessary to process an order for safety lighting control units.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, following the guidelines below.
2. Provide accurate information to ensure proper processing of your order.
3. Review your order carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Information** (id: 1, text, required: false): Enter your order details, including any relevant specifications or requirements.
* **Customer Info** (id: 2, text, required: false): Enter your name, company name, or any other relevant contact information.
* **Delivery Address** (id: 3, text, required: false): Enter the address where you would like the order delivered.
* **Delivery Date** (id: 4, date, required: false): Select the date you would like to receive the order.
* **Order Details** (id: 5, text, required: false): Enter any additional details or instructions related to the order.
* **Supplier Info** (id: 6, text, required: false): Enter the supplier's name or contact information, if relevant.
* **Payment Info** (id: 7, text, required: false): Enter payment details, if necessary.
* **Customer Email** (id: 8, email, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Customer Phone** (id: 9, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Supplier Email** (id: 10, email, required: false): Enter the supplier's email address, if necessary.
* **Supplier Phone** (id: 11, text, required: false): Enter the supplier's phone number, if necessary.
* **Delivery Note** (id: 12, text, required: false): Enter any special delivery instructions or notes.
* **Payment Note** (id: 13, text, required: false): Enter any relevant payment instructions or notes.
* **Order Notes** (id: 14, text, required: false): Enter any additional order notes or instructions.
* **Signature** (id: 15, note, required: false): Enter your signature to confirm the order.
