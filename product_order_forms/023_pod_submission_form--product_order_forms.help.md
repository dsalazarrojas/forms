# POD Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The POD Submission Form is a document used for submitting new product orders to be processed and reviewed by our team. This form should only be used for submitting original, accurate, and complete product orders. Duplicate submissions or submissions with incomplete or incorrect information may cause delays or issues with the ordering process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Submitter Information** (`submitter_info`): Enter your name and contact details to identify who is submitting the order.
	* Please fill in your name, email address, and phone number to ensure we can contact you for further information or clarification if needed.
2. **Product Order Details** (`product_details`): Provide a detailed description of the product ordered.
	* Please include the product type and category in the description.
3. **Quantities** (`quantities`): Enter the quantity of the product being ordered.
	* Enter the exact amount being ordered.
4. **Usage Confirmation** (`usage_confirmation`): Confirm whether the product is for intended usage or not.
	* Select "Yes" if the product is for your intended usage; select "No" if it is not.
5. **Usage Notes** (`usage_notes`): Additional notes about the product usage.
	* If needed, provide any additional information about the product usage.
6. **Submitter Comments** (`submitter_comments`): Any comments or questions you may have.
	* If you have any questions or need clarification, feel free to add them here.
7. **Address Information** (`submitter_address`, `submitter_city`, `submitter_state`, `submitter_zip`, `submitter_country`, `submitter_country_code`): Enter your address information for accurate shipping and billing purposes.
	* Please ensure the information is accurate for shipping and billing purposes.
8. **File Uploads** (`submitter_file_upload`, `submitter_product_file`, `submitter_design_file`, `submitter_usage_file`, `submitter_signature_file`): Upload the required files for the product order.
	* Please ensure the correct file formats and sizes are uploaded as specified.

## Tips
* Please make sure to review and double-check all entered information before submitting the form.
* Ensure all required fields are filled out.
* If you need help or clarification, contact the relevant department.
