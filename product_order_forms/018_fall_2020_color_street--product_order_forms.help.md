# Fall 2020 Color Street - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fall 2020 Color Street form is designed to collect customer information and order details for the Fall 2020 Color Street product. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field with the corresponding information.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed.
3. Double-check your input for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first name** (1, text, optional): Please enter your first name.
* **last name** (2, text, optional): Please enter your last name.
* **email** (3, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (4, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **address line 1** (5, text, optional): Enter your address line 1.
* **address line 2** (6, text, optional): Enter your address line 2.
* **city** (7, text, optional): Enter your city.
* **state** (8, text, optional): Enter your state.
* **zip** (9, number, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **country** (10, text, optional): Enter your country.
* **order date** (11, date, optional): Enter the date of your order.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are accurately completed.
* Double-check your input for accuracy before submitting.
* If you are having trouble with the form, please contact our support team.
