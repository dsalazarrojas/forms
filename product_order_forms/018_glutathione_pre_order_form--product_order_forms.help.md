# glutathione_pre_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The glutathione_pre_order_form is designed for customers to pre-order products related to glutathione supplements. The form is intended to gather information about the customer, their delivery preferences, payment details, order confirmation, contact information, delivery address, and product specifics, as well as any pre-order details they might need to provide.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the glutathione_pre_order_form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information about yourself.
2. Provide any additional information that might be helpful for the order, such as delivery details and pre-order specifics.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_info** (user_info, text, required): This field is for you to enter your name or any other identifying information you'd like to provide. Please ensure the information is accurate and up-to-date.
* **delivery_details** (delivery_details, text, required): In this field, provide your preferred delivery method, such as shipping address or delivery date. Be as specific as possible to ensure timely and correct delivery.
* **payment_info** (payment_info, text, required): Enter your preferred method of payment, whether it's credit card, PayPal, or any other accepted payment method. Please double-check that you have provided the correct payment information.
* **order_confirmation** (order_confirmation, text, required): Please confirm your order by reiterating the details of your pre-order. This ensures accuracy and reduces errors.
* **contact_info** (contact_info, text, required): Enter your contact information, such as phone number or email address, so we can reach out to you with updates and questions.
* **delivery_address** (delivery_address, text, required): Provide the exact delivery address where you would like the products shipped. Please include any necessary details, such as apartment number or building name.
* **product_info** (product_info, text, required): Describe the specific glutathione supplement you are pre-ordering, including the product name, size, or any special instructions.
* **pre_order_details** (pre_order_details, text, required): If you have any special instructions or requests for your pre-order, such as a preferred shipping date or special handling requests, please include them here.

## Tips
Please double-check all fields for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
