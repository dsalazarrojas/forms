# Keychain Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to place custom keychain orders. It collects customer information and customizes the keychain's design according to their preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your contact information:
	* First Name: Your first name
	* Last Name: Your last name
	* Email: Your email address
	* Phone (optional): Your phone number
2. Provide your order date:
	* Date: The date for your custom keychain order
3. Add any additional order message (optional):
	* Order Message: A note to the person creating your keychain
4. Choose your keychain type:
	* Keychain Type: Select from "Custom", "Default", or "Premium" options
5. Select your keychain size:
	* Size: Choose from "Small", "Medium", "Large" options (multiple selection allowed)
6. Choose your keychain shape:
	* Shape: Enter the shape of your keychain (e.g., heart, star, etc.)
7. Select your keychain text size:
	* Text Size: Enter the size of the text on your keychain
8. Select your keychain line height:
	* Line Height: Enter the height of the lines on your keychain
9. Choose your keychain font size:
	* Font Size: Enter the size of the font on your keychain (optional)
10. Select your keychain line weight:
	* Line Weight: Enter the weight of the lines on your keychain (optional)
11. Select your keychain color depth:
	* Color Depth: Enter the depth of the color on your keychain (optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name.
* **Last Name**: Your last name.
* **Email**: Your email address.
* **Phone (optional)**: Your phone number.
* **Date**: The date for your custom keychain order.
* **Order Message (optional)**: A note to the person creating your keychain.
* **Keychain Type**: Choose from "Custom", "Default", or "Premium" options.
* **Size**: Choose from "Small", "Medium", "Large" options (multiple selection allowed).
* **Shape**: Enter the shape of your keychain (e.g., heart, star, etc.).
* **Text Size**: Enter the size of the text on your keychain.
* **Line Height**: Enter the height of the lines on your keychain.
* **Font Size**: Enter the size of the font on your keychain (optional).
* **Line Weight**: Enter the weight of the lines on your keychain (optional).
* **Color Depth**: Enter the depth of the color on your keychain (optional).

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate information to ensure your order is processed correctly.
* If you have any issues or questions, please contact the keychain production team.
* The form is designed to be easy to use, and all fields are clearly labeled.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
