# Custom POS Display Stand Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to capture order information for a Custom POS Display Stand product.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer information in the "Customer Information" field.
2. Describe the product specifications in the "Specs" field.
3. Share any artwork requirements in the "Artwork" field.
4. Input the quantity of the product you want to order in the "Quantities" field.
5. Choose your preferred payment option from the "Payment Info" dropdown menu.
6. Add any additional comments or notes about your order in the "Notes" field (optional).
7. Input your email address in the "Email" field.
8. Input your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Stand Order Form**: This is the title of the form, and you can ignore it.
* **Customer Information**: This field is where you input your customer information. Please fill in your name and any other relevant details.
* **Specs**: Describe the product specifications you need, such as size, material, or color.
* **Artwork**: Share any artwork requirements, such as images or designs.
* **Quantities**: Input the quantity of the product you want to order.
