# Extension Bracket Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Extension Bracket Order Form is designed to collect essential information from customers to process their extension bracket orders efficiently. This form is meant to be filled out by customers to provide accurate and complete details about their order, payment, and other related information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the "Customer Information" page to provide your basic contact information.
2. Fill out the "Shipping Details" page with the address and other required details for shipping.
3. In the "Order Details" page, specify the extension brackets you would like to order, their quantity, and any additional comments or requirements.
4. Enter your payment information in the "Payment Info" page.
5. For shipping and billing purposes, provide your address information in the "Shipping Address" and "Billing Info" pages.
6. In the "Order Summary" page, review and confirm the order details before submitting.
7. If you have any additional notes or comments for the customer service team, you can add them in the "Customer Notes" page.
8. Finally, enter your email address for communication purposes in the "Customer Email" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information**: Please enter your name, phone number, and any other contact details as required.
* **Shipping Details**: Specify the shipping address where you would like the extension brackets to be delivered.
* **Order Details**: List the extension brackets you would like to order, including their quantity and any specific requirements.
* **Payment Info**: Enter your payment method and other required details to complete the transaction.
* **Shipping Address**: Provide the address where the extension brackets will be shipped to.
* **Billing Info**: If different from the shipping address, provide the billing address.
* **Order Summary**: Review and confirm the order details before submission.
* **Customer Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes for the customer service team.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately to avoid delays or errors in the order processing.
- Please review your order details carefully before submitting to ensure completeness and correctness.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the customer service team for assistance.
