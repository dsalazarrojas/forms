# Eco Friendly Bag Restock Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for restocking eco-friendly bags. The form is used by customers to provide their contact and order details, such as contact information, delivery address, and order status. The form also includes fields for customer notes, reorder reasons, and order due dates.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your contact and order details.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`): Enter your contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`): Enter the delivery address where the eco-friendly bags will be delivered.
* **Delivery Notes** (`delivery_notes`): Add any additional delivery notes or instructions.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`): Enter the date for the order.
* **Order Type** (`order_type`): Select the type of order.
* **Product ID** (`product_id`): Enter the ID of the product.
* **Quantity Ordered** (`quantity_ordered`): Enter the quantity of the product ordered.
* **Reorder Reason** (`reorder_reason`): Enter the reason for reordering the product.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`): Select the status of the order.
* **Order Due Date** (`order_due_date`): Enter the due date for the order.
* **Reorder Date** (`reorder_date`): Enter the date for the reordering of the product.
* **Next Reorder Date** (`next_reorder_date`): Enter the date for the next reorder.
* **Order Date Range** (`order_date_range`): Enter the date range for the order.
* **Customer Message** (`customer_message`): Enter any additional message for the customer.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`): Enter the total amount for the order.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`): Enter your name.
* **Customer Phone Number** (`customer_phone_number`): Enter your phone number.
* **Delivery Notes** (`delivery_notes`): Add any additional delivery notes or instructions.
