# healthy_life_program_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The healthy_life_program_application_form is designed to collect information about customers' contact details, previous experience, main motivation, product details, and other relevant information. This form helps us understand customer preferences and needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Please fill out the form carefully and accurately** with your contact details, previous experience, and main motivation.
2. Make sure to select the correct contact information type from the dropdown options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details**: Enter your name, email address, and any other contact information you would like to share with us.
* **Previous Experience**: Describe your previous experience with our program or any relevant background information that may help us understand your application.
* **Main Motivation**: Explain your primary motivation for applying to our program, and why you are interested in it.
* **Product Details**: Enter the product ID, name, and description of the product you are interested in.
* **Contact Information Type**: Select the type of contact information you would like to provide (Home, Business, or Other).
* **Product Order Form Label**: This field is not currently used in the form and is intended for future use.
* **Product Order Form Hint**: This field is not currently used in the form and is intended for future use.
* **Product ID**: Enter the ID of the product you are interested in (if applicable).
* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product you are interested in (if applicable).
* **Product Description**: Enter a brief description of the product you are interested in (if applicable).
* **Product Price**: Enter the price of the product you are interested in (if applicable).
* **Contact Information Type**: Select the type of contact information you would like to provide (Home, Business, or Other).
* **Contact Information Type Hint**: This field is not currently used in the form and is intended for future use.
* **Contact Information Label**: This field is not currently used in the form and is intended for future use.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and detailed information to help us understand your application better.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We value uniqueness in our program, so please ensure that you are not duplicating your application.
