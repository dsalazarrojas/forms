# simple_order_form_by_jezreel_collections - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about orders from customers. It contains fields for customer contact details, order status, and order amount to facilitate order processing and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "order info" page and enter the basic details about the order.
2. Move on to the "customer" page and fill in the customer's name, email, phone number, and other contact information.
3. Provide the customer's address, city, and state, if applicable.
4. Enter the customer's phone number and any additional notes about the customer.
5. On the "order products" page, confirm whether specific products are part of the order or not.
6. Enter the total value of the order in the "order total" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **order info**: Enter the order title, description, or other basic details.
* **customer name**: Enter the customer's full name.
* **customer email**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **customer phone**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **customer address**: Enter the customer's physical address.
* **customer city**: Enter the customer's city name.
* **customer state**: Enter the customer's state or province.
* **customer post al code**: Enter the customer's postal or zip code.
* **order products**: Confirm whether specific products are part of the order. Select 'Yes' for included products and 'No' for excluded products.
* **customer note**: Enter any additional notes about the customer.
* **order total**: Enter the total value of the order.
