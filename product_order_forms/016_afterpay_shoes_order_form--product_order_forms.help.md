# Afterpay Shoes Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Afterpay Shoes Order Form is a straightforward form to collect information from customers about their shoe orders. It is meant to be filled out by customers to provide clear and accurate details for their order processing and shipping.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the buyer details section, which includes providing your full name and email address.
2. Select the shoe model you are interested in purchasing.
3. Choose the size and number of pairs you would like to order.
4. Optionally, specify your preferred color.
5. Choose whether to use Afterpay as your payment method.
6. Provide your shipping address for delivery.
7. Acknowledge that you have read and understood the return policy.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Buyer name** (`buyer_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it will appear in our records.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for shipping confirmation and potential communication.
* **Shoe model** (`shoe_model`, text, required): Type or paste the item SKU or model name of the shoe you are interested in purchasing.
* **Shoe size** (`size`, text, required): Enter the size of the shoe, including width if applicable.
* **Color** (`color`, text, optional): Choose your preferred color for the shoe (if available).
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the number of pairs you would like to order.
* **Use Afterpay** (`afterpay`, select_one, required): Choose whether to use Afterpay as your payment method.
* **Shipping address** (`shipping_address`, text, required): Provide a clear and accurate delivery address for your order.
* **Return policy acknowledgment** (`return_policy_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Acknowledge that you have read and understood our return policy.

## Tips
- Double-check your order details for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Make sure to review and understand our return policy before proceeding with your order.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at your convenience.
