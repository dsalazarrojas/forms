# Camping Accessories Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for ordering camping accessories such as sleeping bags, tents, camping stoves, water filters, and grills. It is intended for customers to provide their information and select the items they would like to order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Provide your personal details in the "Customer Information" section.
2. Enter your shipping address in the "Shipping Address" section.
3. Select the camping accessories you would like to order in the "Order Items" section.
4. Choose your preferred shipping method and payment option in the "Additional Options" section.
5. Read and agree to the terms and conditions in the last step.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`customer_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Company Name (if applicable)** (`customer_company`, text, optional): If you are representing a company, enter the name of the company.
* **Street Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required): Enter the street address where you would like your order shipped to.
* **City** (`shipping_city`, text, required): Enter the city where you would like your order shipped to.
* **State or Province** (`shipping_state`, text, required): Enter the state or province where you would like your order shipped to.
* **Postal Code** (`shipping_zip`, text, required): Enter the postal code where you would like your order shipped to.
* **Country** (`shipping_country`, text, required): Enter the country where you would like your order shipped to.
* **Sleeping Bag (Winter) - Quantity** (`sleeping_bag_quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of sleeping bags you would like to order.
* **Tent 4-Person - Quantity** (`tent_quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of tents you would like to order.
* **Camping Backpack - Quantity** (`backpack_quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of camping backpacks you would like to order.
* **LED Camping Lantern - Quantity** (`lantern_quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of LED camping lanterns you would like to order.
* **Insulated Cooler - Quantity** (`cooler_quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of insulated coolers you would like to order.
* **Portable Camping Stove - Quantity** (`camping_stove_quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of portable camping stoves you would like to order.
* **Water Filter Bottle - Quantity** (`water_filter_quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of water filter bottles you would like to order.
* **Portable Camping Grill - Quantity** (`grill_quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of portable camping grills you would like to order.
* **Special Requests or Custom Orders** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Enter any special requests or custom orders you would like to make.
* **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred shipping method.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred payment method.
* **I agree to the terms and conditions** (`terms_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions.
