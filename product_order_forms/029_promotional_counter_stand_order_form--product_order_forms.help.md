# Promotional Counter Stand Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Promotional Counter Stand Order Form is designed to gather information from customers for orders of promotional counter stands. This form helps the business understand the customer's needs and preferences, ensuring accurate and efficient order fulfillment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the "Customer Name" and "Customer Email" fields, respectively.
2. Provide your phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
3. Describe the artwork details you would like to use for the promotional counter stand in the "Artwork Details" field.
4. Enter the delivery details, including the city and state for the delivery location.
5. Choose the payment method you would like to use from the drop-down menu.
6. Enter the payment amount you would like to pay.
7. Add any additional comments or instructions for the order in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required/optional): Enter the title of the promotional counter stand order.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name so we can address your order correctly.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you with order updates.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for easy communication.
* **Artwork Details** (`artwork_details`, text, required/optional): Describe the artwork details you would like to use for the promotional counter stand.
* **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, text, required/optional): Enter the city and state where you would like the promotional counter stand delivered.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the payment method you would like to use for the order.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required/optional): Enter the payment amount you would like to pay.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the delivery address so we can ensure accurate delivery.
* **Delivery City** (`delivery_city`, text, required/optional): Enter the city of the delivery location.
* **Delivery State** (`delivery_state`, text, required/optional): Enter the state of the delivery location.
* **Delivery Zip** (`delivery_zip`, text, required/optional): Enter the zip code of the delivery location.
* **Payment Method Details** (`payment_method_details`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional details about the payment method.
* **Comments** (`form_submit_comments`, text, required/optional): Add any comments or instructions for the order.
* **Date** (`form_submit_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you would like to receive the order.
* **Time** (`form_submit_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you would like to receive the order.
* **Location** (`form_submit_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location where you would like to receive the order.
* **Message** (`customer_message`, text, required/optional): Add any message you would like to include with the order.
* **Comments** (`form_submit_comments`, text, required/optional): Add any additional comments or instructions for the order.
* **Signature** (`form_submit_signature`, text, required/optional): Add a signature for the order.
* **Company** (`form_submit_company`, text, required/optional): Enter the company name.
* **Address** (`form_submit_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the company address.
* **City** (`form_submit_city`, text, required/optional): Enter the city of the company location.
* **State** (`form_submit_state`, text, required/optional): Enter the state of the company location.
