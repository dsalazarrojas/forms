# Door Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from customers for the creation of a door order. It ensures accurate and efficient processing of their order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Customer Information** section with your personal details.
2. Enter your **Address** and **Contact Information**.
3. Specify the **Delivery Date** and **Delivery Time** for your order.
4. Add any additional **Order Details** as necessary.
5. Select your preferred **Order Type** and **Delivery Time Slot**.
6. Enter your **Product Id** (if applicable).
7. Specify your **Customer Type** (Residential, Commercial, or Industrial).
8. Add any **Order Notes** as required.
9. Select the **Delivery Status** of your order (Confirmed, Pending, or Cancelled).
10. Confirm the **Assigned User** for your order (Yes or No).
