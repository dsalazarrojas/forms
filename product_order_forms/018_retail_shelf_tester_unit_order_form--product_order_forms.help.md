# Retail Shelf Tester Unit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect order information from customers to place orders for retail shelf tester units.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the following fields:
*   Fill out the "Order Form" page with all the required information.
*   Fill out the "Customer Information" page with your details.
*   Fill out the "Customer Address" page with your shipping address.
*   Fill out the "Shipping Info" page with your shipping details.
*   Enter the "Unit Price" of the shelf tester unit.
*   Select the "Order Date" for the order.
*   Enter the "Unit Quantity" of the shelf tester unit(s) you are ordering.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Order Form** (`order_form`, text, not required)
	+ This is where you can enter any additional comments or instructions for your order.
*   **Customer** (`customer`, text, not required)
	+ Enter your name and any other relevant customer information.
*   **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, not required)
	+ Enter your address where you would like the order to be shipped to.
*   **Shipping Info** (`shipping_info`, text, not required)
	+ Enter any shipping details or instructions, such as a shipping address or a specific delivery method.
*   **Unit Price** (`unit_price`, number, not required)
	+ Enter the price per unit of the shelf tester unit you are ordering.
*   **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, not required)
	+ Select the date you would like the order to be processed.
*   **Unit Quantity** (`unit_quantity`, number, not required)
	+ Enter the quantity of shelf tester unit(s) you would like to order.
