# Interactive Display Screen Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Interactive Display Screen Order Form is designed to gather information for customer orders related to interactive displays or screens.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Product Name** field with the name of the product or service you are ordering.
2. Enter your **Customer Name** in the field provided.
3. Enter your **Email** address to allow us to contact you regarding your order.
4. If you have a **Phone**, please enter it in the field.
5. If you have a specific **Address**, enter it here.
6. Select your **Order Items** from the options provided. You can select multiple items.
7. Enter the **Order Quantity**, which is the number of products or services you would like to order.
8. Finally, enter the **Total Cost**, which is the total amount you would like to pay for your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Name (Required)**: The name of the product or service you are ordering.
* **Customer Name (Required)**: Your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email (Required)**: A valid email address for us to contact you about your order.
* **Phone (Optional)**: Your phone number, which we may use to contact you if needed.
* **Address (Optional)**: Your physical address for delivery or shipping purposes.
* **Order Items (Required)**: Select one or more options to specify the products or services you are ordering.
* **Order Quantity (Required)**: The number of products or services you would like to order.
* **Total Cost (Required)**: The total amount you would like to pay for your order.
 
## Tips
* Please ensure you have filled in all required fields to submit a complete order.
* If you have any questions or need help with your order, please contact us at [support email/phone number].
* Make sure to double-check your order details before submitting to avoid errors.
