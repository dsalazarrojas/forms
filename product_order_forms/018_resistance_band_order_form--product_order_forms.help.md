# Resistance Band Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Resistance Band Order Form is designed to collect information from customers for their orders, helping us to process and fulfill their orders efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the product(s) you wish to order in the "Product Select" field.
2. Provide your shipping details, including your name, address, and any other required information in the "Shipping Address" field.
3. Choose your preferred payment method in the "Payment Method" field.
4. Add any additional order notes in the "Order Notes" field.
5. Enter the order total and tax rate in the "Order Total" and "Tax Rate" fields, respectively.
6. Select the desired ship date in the "Ship Date" field.
7. Choose the payment status and order status in the "Payment Status" and "Order Status" fields, respectively.
