# eco_cup_prototype_order_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information for an eco-cup product order. It's designed to be a basic template for users to provide product information, shipping details, and sample requests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required and optional information as needed.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
3. Double-check the terms confirmation to confirm your understanding of the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Product Information** (`product_info`, `text`, required: false)
*This field is for the user to enter any product-related information. Please provide any relevant details about the product you'd like to order, such as product name, description, or quantity.* 

### **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, `text`, required: false)
*Enter your shipping address details, including name, address, city, state, zip code, and any other relevant information for the shipping process.*

### **Sample Requests** (`sample_requests`, `text`, required: false)
*If you have any specific sample requests or special instructions, please describe them here. This could include any specific requirements or preferences for your order.*

### **Terms Confirmation** (`terms_confirmation`, `text`, required: false)
*Please read and agree to the terms of the order by typing "Yes, I confirm" in the field. This ensures you understand the terms and conditions of your order.*
