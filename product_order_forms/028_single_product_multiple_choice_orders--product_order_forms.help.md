# Single Product Multiple Choice Orders - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Single Product Multiple Choice Orders form is designed to help customers or sales representatives quickly place orders for a single product. This form is intended to capture the essential information required for the order, making it a convenient and efficient way to initiate a purchase.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields with the necessary information.
* Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the product choice.
* Enter the quantity of the product desired.
* Provide your customer's name.
* Enter your customer's email address.
* Enter your customer's phone number.
* Add any additional notes for the order (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Single Product Multiple Choice Orders** (id: 1, type: text, required: false): This is the title of the form, which is not a field to fill in, but rather a heading to guide you through the process.
* **product** (id: 2, type: select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you would like to order this product, or 'No' if you do not. This field is optional, but selecting 'Yes' will trigger the next step in the ordering process.
* **quantity** (id: 3, type: number, required: false): Enter the quantity of the product you would like to order. This field is optional, and leaving it blank will not affect your order.
* **customer_name** (id: 4, type: text, required: false): Enter the name of the customer placing the order.
* **customer_email** (id: 5, type: email, required: false): Enter the email address of the customer.
* **customer_phone** (id: 6, type: text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the customer.
* **customer_note** (id: 7, type: note, required: false): Add any additional notes about the order, such as special requests or instructions.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill in all required fields to complete the order successfully.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, contact our customer support team.
* This form is designed to be user-friendly and easy to use; if you encounter any issues, please let us know so we can improve the form for future users.
