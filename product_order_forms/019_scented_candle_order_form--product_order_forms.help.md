# scented_candle_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for a scented candle order. It includes sections for customer details, order information, shipping information, payment information, and review.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields (if any).
2. Enter your customer details, including name, email, and phone number.
3. Provide order information, such as the type of scented candle you're interested in.
4. Enter shipping information, including address and any additional notes.
5. Confirm your payment method and details.
6. Leave a review if you have any feedback or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details**: Enter your name, email, and phone number for us to contact you about your order.
* **Order Information**: Provide details about the type of scented candle you're interested in purchasing.
* **Shipping Information**: Enter your address to ensure smooth delivery of your order.
* **Payment Information**: Enter your payment method and details (e.g., credit card number, expiration date).
* **Review**: Leave a review or comment about your experience with our scented candles.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your order.
* **Note**: Leave any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Shipping**: Enter any additional shipping instructions or notes.

## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Make sure to enter your email address correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us through our customer support email.
* Our team will review and process your order promptly after submission.
