# e_commerce_return_label_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to provide information about their return order label, which will be used for return logistics and customer communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your order details in the "order_details" field.
2. Provide any relevant return details in the "return_details" field.
3. Select your customer details (Male, Female, Other) in the "customer_details" field.
4. Enter your return address in the "return_address" field.
5. If applicable, upload a photo in the "return_photo" field.
6. Enter any additional notes in the "notes" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **order_details** (Text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your order that requires a return label.
* **return_details** (Text, required/optional): Enter any additional information about the return, such as reason for return or any other relevant details.
* **customer_details** (Select One, required/optional): Select your gender (Male, Female, Other) to help us better understand your return requirements.
* **return_address** (Text, required/optional): Enter your return address where the return label can be sent to.
* **return_photo** (Text, required/optional): If you have a photo of the item, you can upload it here.
* **notes** (Note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about your return order, such as a personal message or special instructions.
* **email** (Email, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your return label.
* **phone** (Text, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your return label.

## Tips
Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and clearly. If you have any issues or questions, don't hesitate to contact us for further assistance.
