# easy_order_system - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "easy_order_system" form is designed to collect information about customer orders for internal use within an organization. Its purpose is to gather essential details about customers, their deliveries, and order comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form sections in order: customer details, delivery details, order information, and order comments.
* Make sure to fill out the "order_info" field as it is required.
* Double-check the form fields for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, optional): Fill out this section with the customer's name, address, phone number, or any other relevant contact information.
- **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, text, optional): Provide details about the delivery schedule, delivery address, or any specific delivery instructions.
- **Order Info** (`order_info`, text, required): This field is crucial in the order process. Please fill out this section with the relevant order information, including order number, order date, or any other critical details.
- **Order Comments** (`order_comments`, text, optional): If any additional comments or notes about the order are required, fill out this section with relevant information.
