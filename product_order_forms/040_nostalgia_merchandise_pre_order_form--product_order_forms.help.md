# Nostalgia Merchandise Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for pre-ordering merchandise from a nostalgic product line. Please fill out the form to provide your contact and order details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through the fields one by one, making sure to provide accurate and complete information. If you need help with any field, please refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Your full name is required to complete the order.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates on your order.
* **Phone**: Your contact phone number, optional but recommended for any further communication.
* **Address**: Provide your address for delivery purposes.
* **Payment Details**: Type in any additional payment information you'd like to mention.
* **Payment Method**: Select how you plan to pay for your order:
	+ Bank Transfer: You will be responsible for arranging the transfer with the bank.
	+ Other: Please specify your preferred payment method.
* **Order Details**: Describe the order you'd like to place, e.g., product name, quantity.
* **Product Name**: Type in the name of the product you'd like to pre-order.
* **Product Price**: Enter the price of the product (if known).
* **Quantity**: The number of items you'd like to order.
* **Delivery Details**: Describe the delivery details, e.g., delivery date, time, instructions.
* **Delivery Name**: Type in the name of the person receiving the delivery.
* **Delivery Address**: Provide the address where the delivery will be made.
* **Delivery Phone**: The phone number of the person receiving the delivery.
* **Delivery Email**: Enter the email address of the person receiving the delivery.
* **Delivery Date**: The date you'd like the delivery to be made.
* **Time**: The time of the delivery.
* **Delivery Instructions**: Any additional instructions for the delivery.
* **Note**: Any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Confirm**: Confirm that you've reviewed the form accurately.
* **Submit**: Submit your order for processing.</thinking>
