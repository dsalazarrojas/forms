# Fin Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a new product order. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth ordering process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields, marked with a star (*).
2. Ensure you provide a valid email address for contact and payment purposes.
3. Enter your preferred shipping details, including address and phone number.
4. Choose a payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank) and fill out the required information for your chosen method.
5. Provide any additional comments or notes you'd like to share with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info** (1, text, optional): Please enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Contact Name** (2, text, optional): Enter your name and contact details if different from above.
* **Contact Email** (3, email, optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Contact Phone** (4, text, optional): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Shipping Details** (5, text, optional): Describe your shipping preferences, including any special requests.
* **Shipping Address** (6, text, optional): Enter your shipping address if different from your contact address.
* **Product Preferences** (7, text, optional): Share any specific product preferences, including quantity and other relevant details.
* **Fin Quantity** (8, number, optional): Enter the quantity of the product you'd like to order.
* **Order Comments** (9, note, optional): Leave any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Payment Method** (10, text, optional): Choose your preferred payment method from the options below.
* **Payment Method Credit Card** (11, text, optional): Fill out the required information for credit card payment.
* **Payment Method PayPal** (12, text, optional): Fill out the required information for PayPal payment.
* **Payment Method Bank** (13, text, optional): Fill out the required information for bank payment.
