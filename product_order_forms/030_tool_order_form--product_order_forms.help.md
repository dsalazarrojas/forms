# Tool Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Tool Order Form is designed to gather information for tool orders from customers or users within our organization. This form is used to capture essential details such as address, city, state, zip, contact information, and order specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Tool Order Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the address where you would like to receive the tool(s) and any other relevant contact information, if applicable.
2. Select the city where you would like to receive the tool(s).
3. Choose the state where you would like to receive the tool(s).
4. Enter your zip code to help us ensure accurate delivery.
5. Provide your name to help us process the order.
6. Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your order.
7. Enter your phone number in case we need to reach you for any reason.
8. If you are ordering a damaged tool, enter the number of damaged tools.
9. Select the type of order you are placing (change, replace, new).
10. Add any additional notes about your order, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Address** (text, required/optional): This field is for you to enter your address where you would like to receive the tool(s).
* **City** (text, required/optional): Enter the city where you would like to receive the tool(s).
* **State** (select_one, required/optional): Choose the state where you would like to receive the tool(s) from the available options (CA, WA, TX, NY).
* **Zip** (text, required/optional): Enter your zip code to ensure accurate delivery.
* **Name** (text, required/optional): Provide your full name to help us process the order.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your order.
* **Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number in case we need to reach you.
* **Damaged tool** (number, required/optional): If you are ordering a damaged tool, enter the number of damaged tools.
* **Order type** (select_one, required/optional): Select the type of order you are placing (change, replace, new).
* **Note** (note, required/optional): Add any additional notes about your order, if needed.
