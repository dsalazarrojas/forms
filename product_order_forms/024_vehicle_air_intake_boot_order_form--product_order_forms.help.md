# Vehicle Air Intake Boot Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect information for vehicle boot orders, which is essential for processing and fulfilling orders accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information, ensuring that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
2. Review the form for completeness before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Info** (`vehicle_info`, text, optional): This field is used to capture any additional information about the vehicle being serviced. 
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, optional): Enter the year of the vehicle, e.g., 2015, 2018, etc.
* **Engine Type** (`engine_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of engine in your vehicle, i.e., Gasoline, Diesel, or Hybrid.
* **Transmission** (`transmission`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of transmission in your vehicle, i.e., Automatic, Manual.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, note, optional): Please provide a detailed description of the order, including any specific services required.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the order, e.g., 2022-01-01.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the order, e.g., 14:00.
* **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Customer Vehicle Make** (`customer_vehicle_make`, text, optional): Enter the make of the customer's vehicle.
* **Customer Vehicle Model** (`customer_vehicle_model`, text, optional): Enter the model of the customer's vehicle.
* **Customer Vehicle Year** (`customer_vehicle_year`, number, optional): Enter the year of the customer's vehicle.
* **Customer Vehicle Color** (`customer_vehicle_color`, text, optional): Enter the color of the customer's vehicle.
* **Customer Vehicle Plate** (`customer_vehicle_plate`, text, optional): Enter the plate number of the customer's vehicle.
* **Order Quantity** (`order_quantity`, text, optional): Enter the quantity of the order.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
