# Construction Product Catalog Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to catalog and describe construction products, including their technical specifications, pricing, and availability. The form helps ensure accurate and consistent product information is collected for sales and customer service purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the category under which the product will be listed (e.g., Building Materials, Electrical/Lighting, etc.).
2.  Enter the official name of the product, the manufacturer or brand name, and the SKU or model number.
3.  Provide a detailed description of the product, including its primary use and features.
4.  Specify the standard dimensions or size range of the product.
5.  Identify the material composition of the product.
6.  Enter the weight per unit (if applicable).
7.  If applicable, enter the load rating or capacity of the product.
8.  Check all certifications that apply for the product (e.g., ASTM, ANSI, UL, LEED, OSHA, ISO).
9.  Select the standard warranty period for the product.
10. Enter the list price per unit, excluding shipping and taxes.
11.  Specify the minimum order quantity (MOQ).
12.  Choose the estimated lead time for delivery.
13.  Enter any additional product or ordering notes.
14.  Enter the date the product was entered into the catalog.
15.  Specify the authorized person's name who entered the product information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Official Product Name** (product_name): Enter the official name of the product as it is known by the manufacturer or supplier.
*   **Manufacturer or Brand Name** (manufacturer_brand): Enter the name of the manufacturer or brand of the product.
*   **SKU or Model Number** (sku_model_number): Enter the SKU or model number of the product as assigned by the manufacturer or supplier.
*   **Product Category** (product_category): Select the primary category under which the product will be listed. This will help with search and filter functions.
*   **Detailed Product Description** (product_description): Provide a detailed description of the product, including its primary use and features.
*   **Standard Dimensions or Size Range** (dimensions_size_range): Enter the standard dimensions or size range of the product.
*   **Material Composition** (material_composition): Enter the material composition of the product (e.g., 60% Recycled Steel, High-Density Polyethylene).
*   **Weight per Unit** (weight_per_unit): Enter the weight per unit of the product (if applicable).
*   **Load Rating or Capacity** (load_rating_capacity): If applicable, enter the load rating or capacity of the product.
*   **Certified Compliance Standards** (compliance_standards_checklist): Check all certifications that apply for the product, such as ASTM, ANSI, UL, LEED, OSHA, ISO.
*   **Standard Warranty Period** (warranty_period): Select the standard warranty period for the product.
*   **List Price per Unit** (list_price_per_unit): Enter the list price per unit, excluding shipping and taxes.
*   **Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)** (minimum_order_qty): Specify the minimum order quantity required for the product.
*   **Estimated Lead Time for Delivery** (lead_time_estimate): Choose the estimated lead time for delivery.
*   **Additional Product or Ordering Notes** (additional_notes): Enter any additional product or ordering notes.
*   **Date of Catalog Entry** (form_entry_date): Enter the date the product was entered into the catalog.
*   **Authorized By (Name)** (authorized_by): Specify the name of the authorized person who entered the product information.
