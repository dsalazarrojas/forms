# product_list_order_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to generate a product list order template for customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Email**: Enter the customer's email.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date the order was placed.
* **Total Amount**: Enter the total amount of the order.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Order Items**: Select "Yes" if the order contains items, "No" otherwise.
* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product being ordered.
* **Item Price**: Enter the price of the item being ordered.
