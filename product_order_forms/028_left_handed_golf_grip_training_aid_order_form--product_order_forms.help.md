# left_handed_golf_grip_training_aid_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "left_handed_golf_grip_training_aid_order_form" is a form designed for customers to order a training aid for left-handed golfers. Customers can use this form to select a product and provide their shipping information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the product you would like to order from the "Product selection" field.
2. Choose your shipping preferences from the "Shipping preferences" field.
3. Enter your customer details in the "Customer details" field.
4. Select your shipping address from the "Shipping address" field.
5. Optionally, add any additional notes in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product selection** (`Product selection`, `select_one`, optional): Select the product you would like to order. Choose one of the available options.
* **Shipping preferences** (`Shipping preferences`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose how you would like to receive your order. Select all applicable options.
* **Customer details** (`Customer details`, `text`, optional): Enter your name, email, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Shipping address** (`Shipping address`, `select_one`, optional): Select your shipping address from the available options. Choose one of the following: Address Line 1, Address Line 2, Postal Code, Phone Number, or Email.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional information you would like to include with your order.
