# Nail Template 2020 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for a product order. Please complete the form with the required details to ensure accurate order processing.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, start by entering the **Product Name** in the "Product Name" field.
- Next, enter the **Product Price** in the "Product Price" field.
- Enter the **Product Quantity** in the "Product Quantity" field.
- Finally, enter your **Customer Name** in the "Customer Name" field.
- Review your entries carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Product Name** (`product_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the product you wish to order.
- **Product Price** (`product_price`, `number`, required): Enter the price of the product you wish to order.
- **Product Quantity** (`product_quantity`, `number`, required): Enter the quantity of the product you wish to order.
- **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the customer placing the order.
- **Product Order Form** (`product_order_form`, `text`, optional): This field is a free text area where you can enter any additional information related to the order.
