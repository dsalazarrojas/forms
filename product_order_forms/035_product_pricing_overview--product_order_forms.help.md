# Product Pricing Overview - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to provide an overview of product pricing information, allowing you to easily view and manage product prices, discounts, and promotional status. The form is meant to be a quick reference guide for sales teams, product managers, and other stakeholders to quickly access and understand product pricing details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter product name**: In the "product_name" field, enter the name of the product you are referencing. This will help you quickly identify the product.
2. **Enter product description**: In the "product_description" field, provide a brief description of the product, if necessary.
3. **Set product price**: In the "product_price" field, enter the price of the product, including tax. This should be a numerical value.
4. **Apply product discount**: In the "product_discount" field, enter the percentage off or promo code (if applicable) to apply to the product price.
5. **Choose promo status**: In the "product_promo_status" field, select the current promotional status of the product: Active, Inactive, or Pending.
6. **Add product positioning**: In the "product_positioning" field, provide notes on the product's competitive positioning (e.g., unique features, target market).
7. **Select product category**: In the "product_category" field, enter the category of the product.
8. **Add product image**: In the "product_image" field, enter any relevant images or links to images for the product.
9. **Link to product**: In the "product_link" field, enter any relevant links to the product, such as the website or product page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **product_name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the product.
- **product_description** (text, optional): Provide a brief description of the product.
- **product_price** (number, optional): Enter the price of the product, including tax.
- **product_discount** (number, optional): Enter the percentage off or promo code to apply to the product price.
- **product_promo_status** (select_multiple, optional): Choose the current promotional status of the product: Active, Inactive, or Pending.
- **product_positioning** (note, optional): Provide notes on the product's competitive positioning.
- **product_category** (text, optional): Enter the category of the product.
- **product_image** (text, optional): Enter any relevant images or links to images for the product.
- **product_link** (text, optional): Enter any relevant links to the product.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields, especially "product_name" and "product_price", as they are crucial for accurate product pricing information.
* Use the "product_promo_status" field to track changes in promotional status.
* Keep "product_positioning" notes concise and relevant to the product's competitive positioning.
* Use "product_category" to classify products and facilitate easy searching.
* Use "product_image" and "product_link" fields to provide additional context and references to the product.
