# Fireproof Flooring Tile Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fireproof Flooring Tile Order Form is a tool for customers to submit their orders and provide necessary information for the order processing process. This form is used to collect contact details, delivery information, and order specifics in order to process the order accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name and other contact information in the **Customer Details** section.
2. Provide your delivery address in the **Delivery Address** section.
3. Select the type of tile you wish to order from the **Tile Selection** options.
4. Enter any additional details regarding your order in the **Order Details** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Details** (Customer Details, text, required/optional):
	* This section is used to collect your name and other contact information.
	* Enter your name, email address, and phone number for communication purposes.
- **Delivery Address** (Delivery Address, text, required/optional):
	* Please enter your delivery address to ensure timely and accurate order delivery.
	* This information will be used by our delivery team to contact you with updates and for delivery confirmation.
- **Tile Selection** (Tile Selection, text, required/optional):
	* This section is used to select the type of tile you wish to order.
	* Choose from the provided options to ensure accurate order processing.
- **Order Details** (Order Details, text, required/optional):
	* Enter any additional details regarding your order, such as special requests or instructions.
	* This information will be reviewed by our team to ensure your order is processed correctly.
