# Dancewear Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect necessary information for dancewear orders from customers.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name, email, and phone number in the "Customer Information" section.
2. Select your preferred dance classes (Ballet, Jazz, Contemporary, Hip-Hop, etc.) in the "Dance Classes" section.
3. Choose the dance products you want to order (Leotard, Tights, Shoes, Socks, etc.) in the "Product Choice" section.
4. Enter your shipping address in the "Shipping Address" section.
5. Confirm your order in the "Customer Order Confirmation" section.
6. If needed, add any special instructions or comments in the "Special Instructions" section.
7. Enter any additional instructions or comments in the "Additional Instructions" section.
8. Review and confirm your order summary in the "Order Summary" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`name`, `type: text`, required/optional): Enter your name.
* **Dance Classes** (`dance_classes`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the dance classes you are interested in (Ballet, Jazz, Contemporary, Hip-Hop, etc.).
* **Product Choice** (`product_choice`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the dance products you want to order (Leotard, Tights, Shoes, Socks, etc.).
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your shipping address.
* **Customer Order Confirmation** (`customer_order_confirmation`, `text`, required/optional): Confirm your order details.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, `text`, required/optional): If needed, add any special instructions or comments.
* **Additional Instructions** (`additional_instructions`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional instructions or comments.
* **Order Summary** (`order_summary`, `text`, required/optional): Review and confirm your order summary.

## Tips

* Make sure to carefully review and confirm your order details before submitting the form.
* If you have any special requests or instructions, please enter them in the "Special Instructions" or "Additional Instructions" sections.
* Please double-check your shipping address for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
