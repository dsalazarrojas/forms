## Shoes Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shoes Order Form is a tool to gather information for processing online shoe orders. It captures essential details for fulfilling the customer's order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your preferred shoe design from the available options.
2. Choose the size of the shoe you would like to order.
3. Indicate the quantity of shoes you want to purchase.
4. If you have any further requests regarding the color of your shoe, provide them in the text field.
5. Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
6. Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you regarding your order.
7. Enter your phone number for any further communication.
8. Enter your delivery address to ensure correct delivery of your shoes.
9. Select your preferred payment method from the available options.
10. If you have a payment token for any reason, you can enter it here (this is optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Choose a shoe design** (`shoe_design`, `select_one`, required):
  Choose the color of the shoe you would like to order from the provided options.
* **Choose a shoe size** (`shoe_size`, `select_one`, required):
  Select the size of the shoe you would like to order.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, required):
  Indicate how many shoes you would like to order.
* **Further requests** (`color_request`, `text`, optional):
  If you have a specific color preference or have any other requests for your shoes, enter them in this field.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required):
  Please enter your name as it appears on your identification for verification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required):
  This is your contact email address to ensure we can get in touch regarding your order.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required):
  Enter your phone number for any further communication regarding your order.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, `text`, required):
  Enter your delivery address to ensure correct delivery of your shoes.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required):
  Select your preferred payment method for the purchase.
* **Payment Token** (`payment_token`, `text`, optional):
  If you have a payment token for any reason, you can enter it here.

## Tips
- Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy to ensure correct communication regarding your order.
- If you have any special requests regarding your order (such as color preferences), make sure to include them in the "Further requests" field.
- Select your preferred payment method to ensure a smooth transaction.
- Enter your delivery address correctly to avoid any delays in delivering your shoes.
- If you have a payment token, please include it for the payment process.
- Make sure to review your entire form before submitting to avoid any errors.
