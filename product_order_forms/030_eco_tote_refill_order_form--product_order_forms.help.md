# Eco Tote Refill Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to enable customers to place orders for refills on their reusable eco totes. By completing this form, customers can specify their contact information, product selections, and delivery details to initiate the refill process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your name in the **customerName** field. This is the name that will be used to address your order.
2. Provide your contact email address in the **contactEmail** field. This will be used for communication and order updates.
3. Enter your phone number in the **contactPhone** field. This will be used for contact and delivery purposes.
4. Enter the address of the service location in the **serviceAddress** field. This is the location where your order will be fulfilled.
5. Provide any additional delivery instructions in the **deliveryDetails** field. This can include special requests or delivery requirements.
6. Select the products you wish to refill from the **productSelections** field. Choose from the options available.
7. Check the box to indicate if you agree to the **customerAgreement** field. This is a requirement for the order process.
8. Confirm your order by selecting "Yes" in the **orderConfirmation** field.
9. Select "Yes" in the **deliveryWindow** field to indicate your preferred delivery timeframe.
10. Choose your preferred delivery method from the **deliveryMethod** field.
