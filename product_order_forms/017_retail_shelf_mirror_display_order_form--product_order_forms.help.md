# Retail Shelf Mirror Display Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Retail Shelf Mirror Display Order Form is designed to collect essential details for processing retail shelf mirror display orders. This form helps in streamlining the ordering process by gathering relevant information about the store, product, and order specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Store Information** in the first field, which includes essential details about the store.
2. Provide **Product Details** in the second field, specifying the product you want to order.
3. In the **Order Information** field, enter the order specifics, such as the order date and time.
4. Enter your **Contact Information**, including your email, phone number, and name.
5. Choose the correct **Order Date Format** from the dropdown menu.
6. Select the **Order Status** as either **Open** or **Closed**.
7. Optionally, enter additional details such as **Product Quantity**, **Shelf Number**, and **Store Address**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Store Information** (1) (`Store Information`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the essential details about the store.
* **Product Details** (2) (`Product Details`, `text`, required/optional): Provide specific details about the product you want to order.
* **Order Information** (3) (`Order Information`, `text`, required/optional): Enter order specifics, including date and time.
* **Contact Info** (4) (`Contact Info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your contact information, including email, phone number, and name.
* **Order Date** (5) (`Order Date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the order.
* **Order Time** (6) (`Order Time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of the order.
* **Product Id** (7) (`Product Id`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the product ID number.
* **Order Id** (8) (`Order Id`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the order ID number.
* **Product Type** (9) (`Product Type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of product you want to order from the dropdown menu.
* **Product Quantity** (10) (`Product Quantity`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the quantity of the product you want to order.
* **Shelf Number** (11) (`Shelf Number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the shelf number where the product will be displayed.
* **Store Address** (12) (`Store Address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the store's address.
* **Order Notes** (13) (`Order Notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the order.
* **Email** (14) (`Email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (15) (`Phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Store Name** (16) (`Store Name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the store.
* **Order Date Format** (17) (`Order Date Format`, `select_one`, required): Choose the format in which you want to display the order date from the dropdown menu.
* **Store Id** (18) (`Store Id`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the store ID number.
* **Store Phone** (19) (`Store Phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the store's phone number.
* **Order Id** (20) (`Order Id`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the order ID number again for verification.
* **Order Status** (21) (`Order Status`, `select_one`, required): Choose the status of the order as either **Open** or **Closed**.
* **Contact Name** (22) (`Contact Name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (23) (`Contact Phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (24) (`Contact Email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Store Address** (25) (`Store Address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the store's address again.
