# Thank-You-Insert-Order-Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help customers provide information on their orders, allow them to choose how they want to be followed up on, and offer feedback on their experience with our product. The form has multiple fields where customers can select options, enter contact information, and provide additional feedback. By providing this information, you will be helping us to improve our services and better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the available options for each field and choose the correct response that applies to your situation.
2.  For fields with multiple options, select all that apply.
3.  Enter your contact information in the corresponding fields (email and phone numbers).
4.  Provide any additional feedback in the "Follow up Orders Note" and "Photo Gallery Note" fields.
5.  Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Insert Order Form** (`insert_order_form`, `select_one`, required): This field allows you to select "Yes" or "No" on whether you would like to be inserted with your order.
*   **Follow up Orders** (`follow_up_orders`, `select_multiple`, required): This field allows you to select "Yes" or "No" on whether you want us to follow up on your order.
*   **Feedback Form** (`feedback_form`, `select_one`, required): This field allows you to select "Yes" or "No" on whether you have any feedback you would like to provide.
*   **Photo Gallery** (`photo_gallery`, `select_multiple`, required): This field allows you to select "Yes" or "No" on whether you want to be included in the photo gallery.
*   **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for our records.
*   **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for our records.
*   **Product Name** (`product_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the product or service you are ordering.
*   **Follow up Orders Note** (`follow_up_orders_note`, `note`, required): Provide any additional comments or information on how you want to be followed up on.
*   **Photo Note** (`photo_note`, `note`, required): Provide any additional comments or information on the photos you want to be included in the gallery.
*   **Customer Order Number** (`customer_order_number`, `text`, required): Enter the number of your order for our records.
*   **Customer Feedback** (`customer_feedback`, `select_one`, required): This field allows you to select "Yes" or "No" on whether you have any feedback you would like to provide.
*   **Customer Follow up Orders** (`customer_follow_up_orders`, `select_multiple`, required): This field allows you to select "Yes" or "No" on whether you want us to follow up on your order.
*   **Customer Photo Gallery** (`customer_photo_gallery`, `select_multiple`, required): This field allows you to select "Yes" or "No" on whether you want to be included in the photo gallery.
*   **Customer Email 2** (`customer_email_2`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for our records.
*   **Customer Phone 2** (`customer_phone_2`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for our records.

## Tips

- Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all fields are filled out correctly.
- Be honest and clear in your responses to help us better serve you and improve our services.
- If you have any issues or concerns, feel free to contact us at your convenience.
