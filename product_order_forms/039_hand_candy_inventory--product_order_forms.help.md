# Hand Candy Inventory - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Hand Candy Inventory form is used to collect information from customers for ordering hand candy products. It is designed to be completed by customers to provide their details and preferences for a smooth order process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Product Order Form" page.
2. Fill in the required and optional fields as requested.
3. Click "Submit" to continue with the next page.
4. Repeat the process until all pages are completed.
5. Review and submit your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Order Form** (`product_order_form`, text, required: false)
This page is where customers select the products they want to order. Please choose the product(s) you wish to purchase.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, required: false)
Please provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required: false)
Enter any special requests or notes about your order.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, required: false)
Enter your delivery address, including street address and any other relevant details.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, required: false)
Enter your payment information, including payment method and any additional details.
* **Shipping Info** (`shipping_info`, text, required: false)
Enter your shipping information, including shipping address and any other relevant details.
* **Order Summary** (`order_summary`, text, required: false)
Review your order summary to ensure everything is correct.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, text, required: false)
Add any additional notes or comments about your order.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required and optional fields as requested.
* Review your order summary before submitting your form.
* If you have any questions or issues, contact us for assistance.
* Please note that this form is for internal use only and is not intended for external sharing or use.
