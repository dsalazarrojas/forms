# Smart Robot Maintenance Kit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture information for the maintenance kit order for a smart robot.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer information section with your details.
2. Provide shipping information, including your address and any additional details.
3. Specify the type of service you require for your robot.
4. Enter the robot's serial number, model, and year of manufacture.
5. Choose your preferred payment method and enter the payment amount.
6. Add any special instructions or comments.
7. Check the order status and ensure it is correct before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information**: This section is used to capture your contact and order details.
    * **Customer Name**: Enter your full name.
    * **Customer Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
    * **Customer Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Shipping Information**: 
    * **Shipping Date**: Enter the date you would like to have the order shipped.
    * **Shipping Address**: Enter the address where you would like the order to be shipped.
* **Robot Information**: 
    * **Robot Serial Number**: Enter the serial number of your robot.
    * **Robot Model**: Enter the model number of your robot.
    * **Robot Year**: Enter the year of manufacture for your robot.
* **Service Type**: Choose the type of service you require for your robot. Options include:
    * General Inspection
    * Diagnostic Test
    * Complete Repair
    * Parts Replacement
    * Preventative Maintenance
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you would like to pay for the order. Options include:
    * Credit Card
    * PayPal
    * Bank Transfer
    * Cash
* **Order Quantity**: Enter the quantity of maintenance kits you require.
* **Order Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Special Instructions**: Add any special instructions or requests you have.
* **Contact Information**: Enter any additional contact information.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter the shipping address.
* **Billing Address**: Enter the billing address (if different from the shipping address).
* **Payment Terms**: Enter any payment terms or conditions.
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the payment amount.
* **Customer Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Customer Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Order Number**: Enter the order number.
* **Order Date**: Enter the order date.
* **Special Requests**: Enter any special requests or instructions.
