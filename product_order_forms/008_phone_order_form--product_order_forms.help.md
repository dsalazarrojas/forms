# phone_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to place phone orders with the necessary information, including contact details, order date, product list, and payment method. Please ensure that you are only using this form once per order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your phone number and email address.
2. Select the order date for your phone order.
3. If you have more than one phone number, please enter the second phone number.
4. Indicate the quantity of the product you wish to order.
5. Choose the product(s) from the list of available products (Phone, Accessories, etc.).
6. Choose the accessory you wish to order from the list (Headphones, Earbuds, etc.).
7. Enter any customer comments or messages for the sales representative.
8. Provide any order notes or special instructions.
9. Enter your billing address.
10. Enter your shipping address.
11. Choose your payment method (Credit Card, Cash, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info**: Enter your name, phone number, and email address to contact you for the order.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for the order.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address to contact you for the order.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date you wish to pick up or have the order delivered.
* **Second Phone Number**: If you have multiple phone numbers, enter the second one for the order.
* **Quantity**: Indicate the quantity of the product you wish to order.
* **Product List**: Choose the product(s) you wish to order from the list (Phone, Accessories, etc.).
* **Accessories List**: If you selected "Accessories" from the Product List, choose the specific accessory you wish to order (Headphones, Earbuds, etc.).
* **Customer Comments**: Enter any comments or messages for the sales representative.
* **Order Notes**: Enter any special notes or instructions for the order.
* **Billing Address**: Enter your billing address for the order.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter the shipping address for the order.
* **Payment Method**: Choose your payment method (Credit Card, Cash, etc.).
