# Furniture Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for furniture orders, including their personal details, order preferences, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your personal details, such as your email and phone number.
2. Select the product(s) you want to order.
3. Provide your delivery date and time.
4. Enter your delivery address, city, state, and zip code.
5. Select your payment method and provide any additional payment details.
6. Enter the order status (if applicable).
7. Add any additional order comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **User Input**: This field is for you to input your name and other personal details.
  * Type: text
  * Required: no

- **Select Product**: This field is for you to select the product(s) you want to order. Choose 'Yes' to select a product.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: no

- **Delivery Date**: This field is for you to input your desired delivery date.
  * Type: date
  * Required: no

- **Delivery Time**: This field is for you to input your desired delivery time.
  * Type: time
  * Required: no

- **Email**: This field is for you to input your email address.
  * Type: email
  * Required: no

- **Phone**: This field is for you to input your phone number.
  * Type: text
  * Required: no

- **Product Note**: This field is for you to add any additional notes about the product(s) you are ordering.
  * Type: note
  * Required: no

- **Order Comment**: This field is for you to add any additional comments about your order.
  * Type: text
  * Required: no

- **Payment Method**: This field is for you to select your preferred payment method. Choose from:
  * Payment by Card
  * Cash
  * Other
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: no

- **Payment Details**: This field is for you to input any additional payment details.
  * Type: text
  * Required: no

- **Order Status**: This field is for you to select the status of your order. Choose from:
  * Pending
  * In Progress
  * Completed
  * Type: select_multiple
  * Required: no

- **Product Price**: This field is for you to input the price of the product(s) you are ordering.
  * Type: number
  * Required: no

- **Quantity**: This field is for you to input the quantity of the product(s) you are ordering.
  * Type: number
  * Required: no

- **Delivery Address**: This field is for you to input your delivery address.
  * Type: text
  * Required: no

- **Delivery City**: This field is for you to input the city for your delivery address.
  * Type: text
  * Required: no

- **Delivery State**: This field is for you to input the state for your delivery address.
  * Type: text
  * Required: no

- **Delivery Zip**: This field is for you to input your zip code for your delivery address.
  * Type: text
  * Required: no

- **Delivery Country**: This field is for you to input the country for your delivery address.
  * Type: text
  * Required: no

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your order is processed correctly.
* Double-check your order details before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
