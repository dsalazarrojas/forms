# Product Authentication Label Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Authentication Label Order Form is designed to collect necessary information for product authentication labels. This form helps customers or users order labels for various purposes such as verification, tracking, or identification.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please fill out all the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2.  For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose one or multiple options from the provided list.
3.  If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Brand** (`brand`, `text`, required: false): Enter the brand of the product being ordered.
* **Product Code** (`product_code`, `text`, required: false): Enter the product code of the product being ordered.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the product being ordered.
* **Authentication Type** (`authentication_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of authentication label required for the product (Authentication Label, Authentication Number, Authentication Certificate, or Certificate of Origin).
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the shipping address where the label will be sent.
* **Shipping City** (`shipping_city`, `text`, required: false): Enter the city for the shipping address.
* **Shipping State** (`shipping_state`, `text`, required: false): Enter the state for the shipping address.
* **Shipping Postal Code** (`shipping_postal_code`, `text`, required: false): Enter the postal code for the shipping address.
* **Shipping Country** (`shipping_country`, `text`, required: false): Enter the country for the shipping address.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional order details or comments.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as the customer ordering the label.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address as the customer.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number as the customer.
* **Order Instructions** (`order_instructions`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional instructions for the order.
* **Authentication Label Type** (`authentication_label_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of authentication label (Standard Label, Premium Label, Basic Label).
* **Authentication Label Size** (`authentication_label_size`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the size of the label (Small, Medium, Large, Extra Large).
* **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the shipping method (Standard Shipping, Priority Shipping, Express Shipping).
* **Shipping Cost** (`shipping_cost`, `number`, required: false): Enter the shipping cost for the label.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the payment method (Bank Transfer, Credit Card, PayPal).
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional notes about the order.
* **Authentication Label Number** (`authentication_label_number`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the number of the authentication label required.
* **Authentication Label Quantity** (`authentication_label_quantity`, `number`, required: false): Enter the quantity of authentication labels needed.
* **Shipping Status** (`shipping_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the shipping status (Not Shipped, Shipped, Cancelled, Pending).
* **Authentication Label Status** (`authentication_label_status`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the status of the authentication label (Approved, Rejected, Pending).

## Tips
- Double-check all fields for any errors before submitting the form.
- Review the provided field-by-field explanations for any additional guidance.
- If you're unsure about any field, ask for assistance or clarification.
