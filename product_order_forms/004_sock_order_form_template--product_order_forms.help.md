# Sock Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sock Order Form Template is a tool used by sales teams to collect information from customers who want to place an order for socks. The form helps to ensure that all necessary details are collected accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your address in the "Address" field.
4. Enter your city in the "City" field.
5. Select your state from the available options in the "State" field.
6. Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field.
7. Choose a date for delivery from the calendar in the "Date of Delivery" field.
8. Enter the quantity of socks you wish to order in the "Quantity" field.
9. Select the color(s) you want to order from the available options in the "Color" field.
10. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
11. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
12. If you have any additional comments or messages for the sales team, enter them in the "Note" field.
13. Finally, select the order date from the calendar in the "Order Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your physical address for delivery purposes.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (`state`, select_one, required): Select your state of residence from the available options.
* **Zip** (`zip`, number, required): Enter your zip code for delivery purposes.
* **Date of Delivery** (`date_of_delivery`, date, required): Choose a date for delivery of your ordered socks.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the number of socks you wish to order.
* **Color** (`color`, select_multiple, optional): Select the color(s) of socks you wish to order.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or messages for the sales team.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required): Select the date you wish to place the order.
