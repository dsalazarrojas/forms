# halloween_pre_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for customers to submit their interest in purchasing products before the official sales period for Halloween. Please use this form to collect necessary information from customers.

## How To Complete This Form

To use this form, simply follow these steps:
* Review the form to ensure you have all necessary fields filled in.
* Fill in your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* Enter your email address where you can be contacted for order updates and information.
* Provide your phone number in case the vendor needs to contact you.
* Specify the product(s) you are interested in purchasing.
* Indicate the quantity of each product you wish to order.
* Choose a shipping date you would like for your order.
* If you wish to have extra charges, please specify in the total amount.
* You can add any special instructions for the vendor or sales team.
* If you have any additional comments for the vendor or sales team, please enter them in the customer notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address where you can be contacted for order updates and information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Provide your phone number in case the vendor needs to contact you.
* **Product** (`product`, `text`, required/optional): Specify the product(s) you are interested in purchasing.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, required/optional): Indicate the quantity of each product you wish to order.
* **Shipping Date** (`shipping_date`, `date`, required/optional): Choose a shipping date you would like for your order. This can be any date in the future.
* **Total Amount** (`total_amount`, `number`, required/optional): If you wish to have extra charges, please specify them in this field.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, `text`, required/optional): You can add any special instructions for the vendor or sales team.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, `text`, required/optional): If you have any additional comments for the vendor or sales team, please enter them in this field.
