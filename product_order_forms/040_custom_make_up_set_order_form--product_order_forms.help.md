# Custom Make Up Set Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom Make Up Set Order Form is designed for customers to place orders for custom make-up sets. This form collects the customer's name, order preferences, quantity, shipping address, city, state, and country.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Select your preferred make-up products from the "Order Preferences" field.
3. Enter the quantity of the make-up set you would like to order.
4. Provide your shipping address in the "Shipping Address" field.
5. Enter your city in the "Shipping City" field.
6. Enter your state in the "Shipping State" field.
7. Enter your postal code in the "Shipping Postal Code" field.
8. Enter your country in the "Shipping Country" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name (Customer Name, Text, Required)**: This is the field where you enter your name.
* **Order Preferences (Order Preferences, Select Multiple, Optional)**: Select one or more options from the list to choose your preferred make-up products.
	+ Foundation
	+ Tinted Moisturizer
	+ Concealer
* **Quantity (Quantity, Number, Optional)**: Enter the quantity of the make-up set you would like to order.
* **Shipping Address (Shipping Address, Text, Optional)**: Provide your shipping address in this field.
* **Shipping City (Shipping City, Text, Optional)**: Enter your city in this field.
* **Shipping State (Shipping State, Text, Optional)**: Enter your state in this field.
* **Shipping Postal Code (Shipping Postal Code, Text, Optional)**: Enter your postal code in this field.
* **Shipping Country (Shipping Country, Text, Optional)**: Enter your country in this field.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate order processing.
* Double-check your shipping address, city, state, and postal code to ensure accurate delivery.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact our customer support team.
