# Jacket Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Jacket Order Form is a document used to collect customer information for order processing purposes. It is essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure that your order is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by entering your customer information, including your name and email address.
2. Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
3. Choose your desired jacket size and color.
4. If necessary, add any special instructions for the order.
5. Select the desired date and time of delivery.
6. Choose your preferred payment method.
7. Enter your payment information in the designated field.
8. If you have any additional instructions or comments, please use the "Special Instructions 2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Jacket Size**: Choose the size of the jacket you would like to order.
* **Jacket Color**: Select the color of the jacket you would like to order.
* **Special Instructions**: If you have any special requests or comments, please enter them here.
* **Date of Delivery**: Select the date on which you would like to receive your order.
* **Time of Delivery**: Select the time on which you would like to receive your order.
* **Payment Method**: Choose the method you would like to use for payment.
* **Payment Info**: Enter your payment information accurately and securely.
* **Special Instructions 2**: If you have any additional comments or requests, please enter them here.
