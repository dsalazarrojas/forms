# Team Branded Water Jug Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Branded Water Jug Order Form is a critical document used to collect information about a team's order needs and preferences. It is essential to fill this form accurately to ensure that the team's order is processed efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the team's name in the "Team Name" field.
2. Select the team size from the provided options.
3. Enter the team's email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter the team's phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select the type of water the team requires from the "Water Type" field.
6. Choose the size of the water jug from the "Water Size" field.
7. Provide any additional order details in the "Order Details" field.
8. Select the date and time for the order in the "Order Date" and "Order Time" fields, respectively.
9. Indicate if the assigned tool is required or not.
10. (Optional) Select any additional form controls required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Name** (`Team Name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the team that is placing the order.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, number, required/optional): Select the size of the team from the provided options.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the team's email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the team's phone number for communication purposes.
* **Water Type** (`water_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of water required by the team (e.g., yes or no).
* **Water Size** (`water_size`, select_one, required/optional): Select the size of the water jug required by the team (e.g., yes or no).
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional details about the order, such as special requests or requirements.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date for the order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, required/optional): Select the time for the order.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required/optional): Indicate if the assigned tool is required or not.
* **Form Control** (`form_control`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select any additional form controls required for the order (e.g., yes or no).

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled accurately and truthfully to ensure proper processing of the order.
* Review all the options carefully before making selections.
* If you need assistance or have questions, feel free to contact the team's management for guidance.
