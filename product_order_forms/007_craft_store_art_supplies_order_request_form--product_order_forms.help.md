# craft_store_art_supplies_order_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers to process and fulfill their art supply orders. To use this form, customers must provide details about their order, including customer information, shipping address, shipping option, delivery information, order items, and any additional comments. The form will help the store staff to efficiently process and deliver the order.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your order details in the "Order Details" field.
2. Provide your customer information in the "Customer Info" field.
3. Enter your shipping address in the "Shipping Address" field.
4. Select your preferred shipping option from the "Shipping Option" field.
5. Enter any delivery information in the "Delivery Info" field.
6. List the items you want to order in the "Order Items" field.
7. Add any comments or notes about your order in the "Order Comments" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
9. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
10. Click on the "Form Submission" field to submit your order.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details** (`1`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of your order, including the items you want to purchase and any special requests.
* **Customer Info** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name, address, and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Shipping Address** (`3`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address where you want the order to be shipped to.
* **Shipping Option** (`4`, `select_one`, required: false): Select from the options: "Pickup", "Delivery", or "Other" (for special instructions).
* **Delivery Info** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Enter any specific delivery instructions or details, such as a delivery date or time.
* **Order Items** (`6`, `text`, required: false): List the items you want to order, using a comma-separated format (e.g., "Paint, Canvas, Brushes").
* **Order Comments** (`7`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about your order, such as special requests or questions.
* **Email** (`8`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.
* **Phone** (`9`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your order.
* **Form Submission** (`10`, `text`, required: false): Enter your form submission, which will help us process your order efficiently.
## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all fields accurately and completely.
* Use a comma-separated format for listing order items.
* If you have any special requests or comments, use the "Order Comments" field to add them.
