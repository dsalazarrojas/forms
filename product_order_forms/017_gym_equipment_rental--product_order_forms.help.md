# Gym Equipment Rental - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to rent gym equipment. To complete the form, please follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the type of equipment you want to rent (Bikes, Treadmills, Weights), choose the rental period (Week, Month, Quarter), and enter the desired start and end dates. You will also need to enter the quantity of equipment and any additional comments, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equipment Type** (`select_one_equipment_type`, **Bikes, Treadmills, Weights**): Select the type of equipment you want to rent.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number): Enter the number of equipment you want to rent.
* **Rental Period** (`select_one_rental_period`, **Week, Month, Quarter**): Choose the rental period for the equipment.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date): Enter the start date of the rental period.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date): Enter the end date of the rental period.
* **Form Comments** (`form_comments`, text): Enter any additional comments or information about your rental request.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your rental.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your rental.
* **Form Fields** (`form_fields`, text): If you have any additional information about your rental request, please enter it here.
