# specialty_oil_procurement_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Specialty Oil Procurement Order Form is designed to collect order details from customers for specialty oil products. This form is intended for businesses that sell specialty oils, such as oil refineries, distributers, or retailers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Go to the first page titled "Specialty Oil Order Details".
2. Provide your order details, such as the type of oil product you're interested in purchasing.
3. Proceed to the next page for customer information.
4. Fill out the customer info fields with your relevant details.
5. Continue to the shipping details page to enter your shipping information.
6. On the payment details page, provide your payment method and any other required payment information.
7. Add any additional information you'd like to include with your order.
8. Leave a note for the merchant or customer support (if needed).
9. Sign the form if necessary (e.g., for official documents).
10. Review and submit your order.

* **Specialty Oil Order Details**: This is the first page where you'll start filling out the form. Provide the type of specialty oil product you're interested in purchasing.
* **Customer Info**: This page requires you to fill out your customer information, including your name, address, and contact details.
* **Shipping Details**: Enter your shipping information, including your address and any other relevant details.
* **Payment Details**: Provide your payment method and any other required payment information.
* **Additional Info**: Add any additional information you'd like to include with your order.
* **Notes**: Leave a note for the merchant or customer support if you need to communicate something specific.
* **Signature**: Sign the form if necessary, such as for official documents.

* **Save**: This button will save your progress, but not submit your order.
