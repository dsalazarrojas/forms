# Race Day Medal Printing Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to process orders for race day medals. It's designed to collect information about the event, details about the medals, shipping, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the event details: Provide the event name, date, time, and location.
2. Specify the design type and quantity of medals to be printed.
3. Provide shipping address, city, state, and zip code.
4. Choose a payment method and specify payment details.
5. Review and submit the form to place your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event name**: This field is used to enter the name of the event.
* **Event date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event time**: Enter the time of the event.
* **Event location**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Design type**: Select one of the available design options for the medal.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of medals to be printed.
* **Shipping address**: Enter the shipping address where the medals will be sent.
* **City**: Enter the city for the shipping address.
* **State**: Enter the state for the shipping address.
* **Zip**: Enter the zip code for the shipping address.
* **Payment method**: Select one of the available payment options.
* **Payment details**: Enter any additional payment details.
* **Custom medal text**: Enter any custom text for the medal.
* **Custom medal designs**: Select one or more custom designs for the medal.
* **Custom medal colors**: Select one or more custom colors for the medal.
* **Custom medal designs 2**: Select one or more custom designs for the medal (this is a duplicate field, but it's used for backup or redundancy purposes).
* **Custom medal colors 2**: Select one or more custom colors for the medal (this is a duplicate field, but it's used for backup or redundancy purposes).
* **Shipping details**: Enter any additional shipping details.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Event description**: Enter a brief description of the event.
* **Payment status**: Select one of the available payment status options.
* **Payment method details**: Enter any additional payment method details.
* **Total cost**: This field will be filled in automatically after the order is processed.
* **Shipping details**: This field will be filled in automatically after the order is processed.
