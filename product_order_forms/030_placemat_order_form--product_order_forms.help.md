# placemat_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The placemat_order_form is a form used to collect customer information and order details for processing orders.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please complete the form by following these steps:
  1. Fill out the "Customer Information" page (customer_form) with the required details.
  2. Move on to the additional "Customer Form 1" and "Customer Form 2" pages (customer_form_section_1 and customer_form_section_2) if applicable.
  3. Provide delivery details in the "Delivery Details" field.
  4. Select the desired product from the "Product" drop-down list.
  5. Add any relevant customer notes in the "Customer Notes" field.
  6. Enter a date for the order in the "Order Summary" field.
  7. Add any final order notes in the "Order Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information (customer_form)**: Enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details. This is the main section of the form.
* **Customer Form 1 (customer_form_section_1)**: If this field is present, please provide additional customer information if necessary.
* **Customer Form 2 (customer_form_section_2)**: If this field is present, provide any extra customer information required.
* **Delivery Details**: Enter the delivery address, preferred delivery date, or any other relevant delivery information.
* **Product**: Select the product you wish to order from the available options.
* **Customer Notes**: Add any comments or special requests regarding the order.
* **Order Summary**: Enter a date for the order summary.
* **Order Notes**: Add any final comments or instructions regarding the order.

## Tips
- Please ensure all required fields are filled out.
- Double-check the information for accuracy before submitting.
- If a field is not required but applicable, feel free to add any additional information.
- If you have any issues or concerns with the form, please contact our support team.
