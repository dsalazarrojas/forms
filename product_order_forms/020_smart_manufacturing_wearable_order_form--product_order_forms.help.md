# Smart Manufacturing Wearable Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Manufacturing Wearable Order Form is used to collect information for smart manufacturing wearable orders. This form is designed to be completed by customers who want to place an order for a smart manufacturing wearable.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the company details section with your company's information.
2. Provide contact information for the person who will be receiving the order.
3. Select the specific wearable you want to order from the options provided.
4. Choose the delivery date and time that works best for you.
5. Add any additional delivery information if needed.
6. Specify how you want to receive your order (e.g., shipping or in-person pickup).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Details** (Company Details, text, required/optional): Please fill out your company's details, including your name and any other relevant information.
* **Contact Person** (Contact Person, text, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information of the person who will be receiving the order.
* **Delivery Info** (Delivery Info, text, required/optional): Provide any additional information about your delivery preferences.
* **Wearable Select** (Wearable Select, select_one, required/optional): Choose the specific smart manufacturing wearable you want to order from the options provided.
* **Delivery Date** (Delivery Date, date, required/optional): Select the date you would like to receive your order.
* **Delivery Time** (Delivery Time, time, required/optional): Choose the time you would like to receive your order.
* **Company Notes** (Company Notes, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **Email** (Email, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your order.
* **Company Address** (Company Address, text, required/optional): Enter your company's address.
* **Order Type** (Order Type, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of order you are placing (e.g., replacement, upgrade, etc.).
* **Delivery Details** (Delivery Details, text, required/optional): Provide any additional information about your delivery preferences.
* **Company** (Company, text, required/optional): Enter your company's name.
* **Address** (Address, text, required/optional): Enter your company's address.
* **City** (City, text, required/optional): Enter your city.
* **State** (State, text, required/optional): Enter your state.
* **Zip Code** (Zip Code, text, required/optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Delivery Method** (Delivery Method, select_one, required/optional): Choose how you want to receive your order (e.g., shipping, in-person pickup, etc.).
* **Delivery Status** (Delivery Status, select_one, required/optional): Choose the status of your order (e.g., pending, shipped, etc.).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately to ensure smooth order processing.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, don't hesitate to contact us.
* Please review your order carefully before submitting it to ensure it's accurate.
