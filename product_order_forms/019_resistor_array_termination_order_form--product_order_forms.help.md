# resistor_array_termination_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The resistor array termination order form is used for collecting customer and product information for resistor array terminations. This form is crucial for ensuring accurate and complete data capture for termination purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (*) in the customer information section.
2. Choose the correct product info that matches your product.
3. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for resistor type and termination type.
4. Enter the quantity of resistors needed.
5. Add any additional notes if necessary.
6. Enter the customer's email and phone number.
7. Select the delivery date and time.
8. Confirm the customer's signature.
9. Enter the customer's salesperson information and sales notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info** (1, text, required): Enter the customer's name, company, and contact information.
* **Product Info** (2, text, required): Enter the product name, quantity, and other relevant product details.
* **Resistor Type** (3, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' to indicate the resistor type is included in the order.
* **Termination Type** (4, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' to indicate the termination type is included in the order.
* **Quantity** (5, number, required): Enter the number of resistors needed.
* **Notes** (6, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Email** (7, email, required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone** (8, text, required): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Delivery Date** (9, date, required): Select the date when the customer needs the product.
* **Delivery Time** (10, time, required): Select the time when the customer needs the product.
* **Customer Signature** (11, select_one, required): Confirm the customer's signature on the order.
* **Customer Signature Date** (12, date, required): Enter the date of the customer's signature.
* **Customer Signature Time** (13, time, optional): Enter the time of the customer's signature.
* **Additional Info** (14, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the order.
* **Salesperson** (15, text, required): Enter the salesperson's name.
* **Termination Info** (16, text, required): Enter the termination details.
* **Termination Notes** (17, text, optional): Add any termination-related notes.
* **Order Date** (18, date, required): Enter the date of the order.
* **Salesperson Info** (19, text, required): Enter the salesperson's information.
* **Salesperson Notes** (20, text, optional): Enter the salesperson's notes.
* **Customer Notes** (21, text, optional): Enter any customer notes.
* **Customer Notes 2** (22, text, optional): Enter any additional customer notes.
* **Customer Info 3** (23, text, optional): Enter any additional customer information.
* **Resistor Array Termination Order Form Info** (24, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the order.
* **Customer Info 4** (25, text, optional): Enter any additional customer information.
