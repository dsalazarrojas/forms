# Smart Pressure Sensor Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Pressure Sensor Order Form is a user-facing form designed to collect necessary information from customers for the ordering process of smart pressure sensors. This form will help the order processing team understand the customer's requirements and ensure timely and accurate delivery of the product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. **Shipping Details**: In the Shipping Details section, provide the delivery address where the product will be shipped.
2. **Product Quantity**: In the Product Quantity field, enter the number of units you would like to order.
3. **Billing Address**: In the Billing Address field, enter your billing address for the order.
4. **Payment Details**: In the Payment Details field, enter the payment method and any other relevant information.
5. **Shipping Type**: Select the shipping type from the options provided (UPS, FedEx, USPS, DHL).
6. **Shipping Speed**: Select the shipping speed from the options provided (Standard, Express, Expedited, Priority).
7. **Contact Person**: Select the contact person's title (Mr., Mrs., Ms., Dr.).
8. **Contact Email**: Enter the contact email of the customer's representative.
9. **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the customer's representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shipping Details**
	+ **Shipping Address** (id: 3) (`text`, optional): Enter your shipping address, where the product will be delivered.
* **Product Quantity**
	+ **Product Quantity** (id: 2) (`number`, optional): Enter the number of products you would like to order.
* **Billing Address**
	+ **Billing Address** (id: 4) (`text`, optional): Enter your billing address for the order.
* **Payment Details**
	+ **Payment Details** (id: 5) (`text`, optional): Provide payment method and any other relevant details.
* **Shipping Type**
	+ **Shipping Type** (id: 6) (`select_one`, optional): Select one option from the list (UPS, FedEx, USPS, DHL).
* **Shipping Speed**
	+ **Shipping Speed** (id: 7) (`select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more options from the list (Standard, Express, Expedited, Priority).
* **Contact Person**
	+ **Contact Person** (id: 8) (`select_one`, optional): Select one option from the list (Mr., Mrs., Ms., Dr.).
* **Contact Email**
	+ **Contact Email** (id: 9) (`email`, optional): Enter the contact email of the customer's representative.
* **Contact Phone**
	+ **Contact Phone** (id: 10) (`text`, optional): Enter the contact phone number of the customer's representative.
