# Fireproof Cabinet Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fireproof Cabinet Order Form is designed to collect information from customers for their fireproof cabinet orders. This form helps us process orders efficiently and ensure accurate delivery of fireproof cabinets to our customers. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to facilitate a smooth order process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the required information.
* Fill out each field accurately and completely.
* Submit the form once you have filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Order Details
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional): This field is used to provide a brief description of the order. You can enter any relevant details about your order in this field.

* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, number, required): Enter your shipping address accurately and completely to ensure correct delivery of your fireproof cabinet.

* **Cabinet Selection** (`cabinet_selection`, select_one, required): Select the type of cabinet you want to order from the available options (Option 1, Option 2).

* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required): Select a date for delivery of the cabinet. This is an essential date for us to process the order accurately.

* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, required): Select a time slot for delivery of the cabinet. Please choose a time that works best for you.

* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to pay for the order from the available options (Option 1, Option 2).

* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for us to contact you regarding your order.

* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for us to contact you in case of any urgent matters related to your order.

* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, note, optional): If you have any additional information about your order, you can enter it in this field.

* **Chatjimmy** (`chatjimmy`, text, optional): This field is not required and can be left blank.

**Tips**

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* We will be in touch with you shortly after submitting the form.
