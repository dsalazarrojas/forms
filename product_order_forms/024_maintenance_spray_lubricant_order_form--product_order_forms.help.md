# Maintenance Spray Lubricant Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for the maintenance spray lubricant order. Please use this guide to ensure you fill out the form correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read through the form carefully before starting the submission process.
- Enter the order date in the format MM/DD/YY in the `order_date` field.
- Specify the order time in the format HH:MM if needed in the `order_time_` field.
- Enter the quantity ordered in the `quantity_ordered` field.
- Enter the delivery date in the format MM/DD/YY in the `delivery_date_` field.
- Enter the delivery time in the format HH:MM if needed in the `delivery_time_` field.
- Select the facility where the order is to be delivered by choosing one of the options in the `facility` field.
- Select multiple workshops where the order is to be delivered by choosing multiple options in the `workshop` field.
- Select the team who will be receiving the order by choosing one option in the `team` field.
- Enter any additional comments or notes related to the order in the `comments` field.
- Enter the email address of the contact person in the `email` field.
- Enter the phone number of the contact person in the `phone` field.
- Enter any additional notes related to the order in the `notes` field.
- Enter any additional comments related to the order in the `order_comments` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Maintenance Spray Lubricant Order Form** (Text, Required/Optional): This is the title of the form.
* **order_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date you want the order to be placed.
* **order_time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time you want the order to be placed.
* **quantity_ordered** (Number, Optional): Enter the quantity of maintenance spray lubricant ordered.
* **delivery_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date you want the delivery to be made.
* **delivery_time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time you want the delivery to be made.
* **facility** (Select One, Optional): Select the facility where the order is to be delivered.
* **workshop** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select multiple workshops where the order is to be delivered.
* **team** (Select One, Optional): Select the team who will be receiving the order.
* **comments** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes related to the order.
* **email** (Email, Optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **notes** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional notes related to the order.
* **order_comments** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments related to the order.
