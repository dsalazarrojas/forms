# Fall/Winter 2020 Color Street Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place orders for Fall and Winter 2020 Color Street products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct Color Street Collection from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter your Product Name, if applicable.
3. Provide a brief Product Description, if applicable.
4. Choose your desired Color Street Size and select all applicable sizes.
5. Enter the quantity you wish to order.
6. Add any special instructions for your order.
7. Enter your email address.
8. Enter your phone number.
9. Select the type of order you are placing (e.g., In-Store, Online, Phone, Email, or Mail).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Color Street Collection** (`color_street_collection`, select_one): Select the correct Color Street Collection for your order. This will help us ensure that we ship the correct products to you.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text): Enter the name of the product you are ordering, if applicable.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, text): Provide a brief description of the product you are ordering, if applicable.
* **Color Street Size** (`color_street_size`, select_multiple): Choose the size of the Color Street product you are ordering. Select all applicable sizes for your order.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number): Enter the quantity of the product you are ordering.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, note): Add any special instructions or comments about your order.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your order.
* **Order Type** (`order_type`, select_one): Select the type of order you are placing (e.g., In-Store, Online, Phone, Email, or Mail).

## Tips

* Please make sure to select the correct Color Street Collection and size for your order.
* If you have any special instructions, please provide them in the "Special Instructions" field.
* If you are placing an order by phone, please provide your phone number so we can contact you to confirm your order.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us at [contact email or phone number].
* This form is for ordering Fall and Winter 2020 Color Street products only. If you need to order other products, please contact us for assistance.
