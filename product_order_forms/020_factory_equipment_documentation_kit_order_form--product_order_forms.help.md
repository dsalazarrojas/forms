# Factory Equipment Documentation Kit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Factory Equipment Documentation Kit Order Form is used to collect information from users to prepare a customized documentation kit for their ordered equipment. This form helps our team to understand the user's requirements and preferences, ensuring that the delivered kit meets their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Information, including your name, contact number, and email address.
2. Specify your delivery details, such as date and time of delivery.
3. Choose the type of order you are placing (Equipment, Kit, or Other).
4. Select the specific equipment being ordered from the list provided.
5. Enter the delivery address and any additional order notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`): Enter your name, contact number, and email address.
* **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`): Specify the date and time you would like to receive your documentation kit.
* **Delivery Location** (`delivery_location`): Enter the address where you would like the kit to be delivered.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`): Choose the date you would like to receive your documentation kit.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`): Choose the time you would like to receive your documentation kit.
* **Order Type** (`order_type`): Choose the type of order you are placing (Equipment, Kit, or Other).
* **Equipment Ordered** (`equipment_ordered`): Select the specific equipment being ordered from the list provided.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`): Enter the address where you would like the kit to be delivered.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): Enter your contact number.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`): Enter your company name.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`): Enter your company address.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`): Add any additional notes or instructions for your order.

**Tips**

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate order fulfillment.
* Use the correct format for dates (mm/dd/yyyy) and times (HH:MM).
* If you need help or have any questions, please contact our support team.
