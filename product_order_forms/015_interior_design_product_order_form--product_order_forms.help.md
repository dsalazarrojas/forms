# interior_design_product_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The interior design product order form is used to collect information for interior design product orders. This form is designed to gather product details, client information, payment details, and delivery information from end-users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the product order form with the necessary product information, including the product name, description, and any other relevant details.
2. Enter the client's information, including their name, address, and any other relevant contact details.
3. Enter the payment details, including the payment method (credit card, PayPal, or bank transfer).
4. Input the payment method-specific details, such as the payment amount.
5. Enter the billing and shipping addresses.
6. Select the delivery date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Order Form (`product_order_form`)**: Enter the product information, including the product name, description, and other relevant details.
* **Client Information (`client_info`)**: Enter the client's name, address, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Product Information (`product_info`)**: Enter the product details, including any relevant information about the product.
* **Payment Details (`payment_details`)**: Enter the payment amount and method. Select the payment method from the drop-down list.
* **Order Total (`order_total`)**: Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Payment Method (`payment_method`)**: Select the payment method from the drop-down list (credit card, PayPal, or bank transfer).
* **Billing Address (`billing_address`)**: Enter the billing address.
* **Shipping Address (`shipping_address`)**: Enter the shipping address.
* **Delivery Date (`delivery_date`)**: Select the delivery date.
* **Delivery Time (`delivery_time`)**: Select the delivery time.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate order processing.
* Double-check the product information and client details to ensure accuracy.
* Enter the payment and billing addresses carefully to avoid errors.
* Select the correct payment method and delivery date and time.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the support team for assistance.
