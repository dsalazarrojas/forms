# Challenge Coin Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Challenge Coin Order Form is used to collect information required for producing custom challenge coins. It is essential for ensuring that your custom challenge coin order is accurate, complete, and meets your specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the order date for your challenge coin order.
2. Enter the customer's full name.
3. Provide the customer's email address for order confirmation.
4. Enter the customer's contact phone number.
5. Choose the name of the organization or unit associated with the customer.
6. Enter the customer's shipping address.
7. Select the type of challenge coin desired.
8. Select the coin size and shape.
9. Choose the type of metal and enamel used for the coin.
10. If applicable, upload a design file or indicate that you require design services.
11. Provide any design notes if you have a custom design.
12. Decide if a design proof is required before production.
13. Choose the packaging option for the challenge coins.
14. Indicate if this is a rush order.
15. Specify the delivery date if you need it expedited.
16. Select the payment method for your order.
17. Provide any special instructions or requirements.
18. Sign your name as confirmation of your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Date**: Enter the date you would like to have this order fulfilled.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your full name as the customer.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for order confirmation.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Organization Name**: Enter the name of your organization or unit (if applicable).
* **Shipping Address**: Enter your full shipping address.
* **Coin Type**: Choose the type of challenge coin you want to order (Custom, Stock, Military, Corporate, Commemorative, or Challenge Coin Set).
* **Coin Size**: Choose the diameter of your challenge coin (1.5, 1.75, 2, 2.5, 3, or Custom).
* **Coin Shape**: Choose the shape of your challenge coin (Round, Square, Rectangle, Oval, or Custom).
* **Metal Type**: Choose the type of metal for your challenge coin (Brass, Copper, Nickel, Zinc alloy, Silver plated, Gold plated, or Antique finish).
* **Enamel Type**: Choose the type of enamel coloring for your challenge coin (Soft Enamel, Hard Enamel, Printed Epoxy, or No Enamel).
* **Edge Type**: Choose the type of edge for your challenge coin (Smooth, Rope, Diamond Cut, Numbered, or Custom).
* **Design File Uploaded**: If you have a design file, upload it here, or indicate that you require design services.
* **Design Notes**: Provide any custom design requirements or notes if you have a custom design.
* **Proof Required**: Decide if you need a design proof before production (Yes - Send digital proof, Yes - Send physical proof, or No - Proceed to production).
* **Packaging Option**: Choose the packaging type for your challenge coins (Bulk, Individual Velvet Pouches, Individual Display Boxes, Presentation Case, or Custom).
* **Rush Order**: Indicate if this is a rush order (No - Standard production, Yes - Rush production).
* **Delivery Date Needed**: Specify the date you need delivery (if applicable).
* **Payment Method**: Choose the payment method for your order (Credit Card, Debit Card, Wire Transfer, Check, or Purchase Order).
* **Special Instructions**: Provide any special requirements or notes.
* **Customer Signature**: Sign your full name as confirmation of your order.

This form will guide you through the necessary steps to complete your custom challenge coin order accurately and efficiently.
