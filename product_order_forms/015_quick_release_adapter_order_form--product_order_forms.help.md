# quick_release_adapter_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The quick_release_adapter_order_form is used to collect information about a supplier's order, including contact details, product specifics, delivery details, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the supplier's name and contact number in the designated fields.
2. Provide the supplier's email address.
3. Select the product name from the list of options.
4. Choose the product type from the list of options.
5. Enter the quantity and price of the product.
6. Specify the delivery date and time.
7. Add any necessary delivery notes or instructions.
8. Select the payment method and payment status.
9. Enter the delivery address and any additional delivery instructions.
10. Review and submit the form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Name** (1, required): Enter the name of the supplier.
* **Contact Number** (2, required): Enter the supplier's contact number.
* **Supplier Email** (3, required): Enter the supplier's email address.
* **Product Name** (4, required): Select the name of the product from the list.
* **Product Type** (5, required): Select the type of product from the list.
* **Quantity** (6, required): Enter the quantity of the product ordered.
* **Price** (7, required): Enter the price of the product.
* **Delivery Date** (8, required): Enter the delivery date in date format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Delivery Time** (9, required): Enter the delivery time in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00).
* **Delivery Note** (10, optional): Add any additional delivery notes or instructions.
* **Payment Method** (11, required): Select the payment method from the list.
* **Payment Details** (12, optional): Enter any additional payment details.
* **Delivery Address** (13, required): Enter the delivery address.
* **Delivery Instructions** (14, optional): Add any additional delivery instructions.
* **Delivery Terms** (15, optional): Enter any delivery terms or conditions.
* **Supplier Comment** (16, optional): Add any comments from the supplier.
* **Payment Terms** (17, optional): Enter any payment terms or conditions.
* **Notes** (18, optional): Add any additional notes about the order.
* **Order ID** (19, optional): Enter the order ID number.
* **Customer** (20, optional): Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Note** (21, optional): Add any additional customer notes.
* **Order Status** (22, required): Select the order status from the list.
* **Payment Status** (23, required): Select the payment status from the list.
