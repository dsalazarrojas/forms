# Cosmetics Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cosmetics Order Form is a tool for customers to place orders for cosmetic products. To place an order, follow these steps.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. **Select Products**: Choose the products you want to purchase from the options provided. You may select multiple products.
2. **Provide Customer Information**: Enter your name and any other relevant information to help us serve you better.
3. **Enter Shipping Address**: Provide your shipping address to ensure that your order is delivered correctly.
4. **Enter Order Total (Optional)**: If you want to include any other products or services not listed, you can enter the total cost here.
5. **Choose Payment Method**: Select the payment method that best suits your preference. Please note that some payment methods may require further information.
6. **Choose Shipping Method**: Choose how you want your order to be shipped, considering options available.
7. **Confirm Quantity**: Specify the quantity of each product you would like to order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **products (Select Multiple)**: The list of available products for you to select from.
* **customer_info (Text)**: Enter your name, address, phone number, or any other relevant contact information to help us serve you better.
* **shipping_address (Text)**: Enter the address where you want your order to be shipped to.
* **order_total (Number)**: Enter the total cost for any additional products or services not listed.
* **payment_method (Select One)**: Choose the payment method that best suits your preference. Please note that some payment methods may require further information.
* **shipping_method (Select One)**: Choose how you want your order to be shipped, considering options available.
* **size (Select Multiple)**: Select the size of the product you want to order.
* **quantity (Number)**: Specify the quantity of each product you want to order.

## Tips
* Be accurate when entering your shipping address to ensure correct delivery.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* Some payment methods may have additional requirements or restrictions. Follow the on-screen instructions for further information.
* Double-check your order total and quantity to avoid errors.
