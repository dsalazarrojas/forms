# Spa Skincare Product Order Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about spa skincare product orders from customers. You can fill it out to provide details about your order, delivery preferences, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the delivery preferences that apply to your order.
2. Enter your delivery address, city, state, and postcode.
3. Add any customer notes you'd like to share with your spa.
4. Enter your contact information for the customer.
5. Choose the date and time for your order (optional).
6. Specify the quantity of products you'd like to order.
7. Select the products you'd like to order.
8. Enter the total amount of your order.
9. Provide the name of the spa where you'll be receiving the order.
10. Enter the contact information for the spa.
11. Enter any spa notes you'd like to include with your order.
12. Enter your name, address, phone number, and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Delivery Preferences** (select one): Choose whether you'd like to receive your order on a specific date and time or if you'd like to pick up your order in-store.
* **Delivery Address** (text): Enter your delivery address.
* **Delivery City** (text): Enter the city where you'd like to receive your order.
* **Delivery State** (text): Enter the state where you'd like to receive your order.
* **Delivery Postcode** (number): Enter the postcode where you'd like to receive your order.
* **Customer Notes** (text): Add any notes or comments you'd like to share with your spa.
* **Customer Contact** (text): Enter your contact information so the spa can reach out to you.
* **Order Date** (date): Enter the date you'd like to receive your order (optional).
* **Order Time** (time): Enter the time you'd like to receive your order (optional).
* **Order Quantity** (number): Enter the quantity of products you'd like to order.
* **Order Products** (select multiple): Choose the products you'd like to order from the options provided.
* **Product 1** (text): If you selected "Product 1", enter the product name.
* **Product 2** (text): If you selected "Product 2", enter the product name.
* **Product 3** (text): If you selected "Product 3", enter the product name.
* **Total Amount** (number): Enter the total amount of your order.
* **Spa Name** (text): Enter the name of the spa where you'll be receiving your order.
* **Spa Address** (text): Enter the address of the spa where you'll be receiving your order.
* **Spa Phone** (text): Enter the contact phone number of the spa.
* **Spa Email** (email): Enter the email address of the spa.
* **Spa Website** (text): Enter the website of the spa.
* **Spa Notes** (text): Add any notes or comments you'd like to share with the spa.
* **Customer Name** (text): Enter your name.
* **Customer Address** (text): Enter your address.
* **Customer Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **Customer Email** (email): Enter your email address.
