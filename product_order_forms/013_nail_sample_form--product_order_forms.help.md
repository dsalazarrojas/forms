# nail_sample_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "nail_sample_form" is a form designed to collect information related to nail sample orders, ensuring that the product is allocated correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Choose whether a nail sample is required ('Yes' or 'No').
2. Enter a brief description of the sample.
3. Provide the quantity of the order.
4. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
5. Enter your phone number.
6. Add any additional order details.
7. Choose the assignment method (Chat, Email, or Phone).
8. Enter the product ID for the allocated product.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Choose your nail sample**: Select 'Yes' if a sample is needed, 'No' otherwise. This determines the type of order.
* **Sample Description**: Enter a brief description of the sample required.
* **Quantity of order**: Enter the number of samples requested.
* **Your email**: Enter your email address for easy contact.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Order details**: Add any additional information about the order.
* **Assign to**: Choose how you would like to be contacted (Chat, Email, or Phone) for further instructions.
* **Product ID**: Enter the ID of the product to be allocated (if required).
## Tips
- Make sure you have the required information ready before filling out the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact your local support team for guidance.
