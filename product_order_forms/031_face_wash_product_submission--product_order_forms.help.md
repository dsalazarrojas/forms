# Face Wash Product Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Face Wash Product Submission form is a tool for brands to submit their face wash products for listing on a product catalog or database. This form gathers essential information about products, including their brand, name, type, ingredients, image, and other details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Face Wash Product Submission form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter Brand Name**: Provide the name of the brand that owns the face wash product.
2. **Enter Product Name**: Enter the name of the face wash product.
3. **Choose Face Wash Product Type**: Select the type of face wash product from the options provided (Cleansing, Moisturizing, Exfoliating, Soothing, Balancing, Anti-acne).
4. **Enter Ingredient Name**: List the ingredients used in the face wash product.
5. **Describe Ingredients**: Provide a brief description of the ingredients used.
6. **Upload Face Wash Image**: Upload an image of the face wash product.
7. **Enter Face Wash Image URL**: If you don't want to upload an image, enter the URL of the image.
8. **Enter Product Price**: Enter the price of the face wash product.
9. **Enter Brand URL**: Enter the website URL of the brand.
10. **Enter Brand Email**: Enter the email address of the brand.
11. **Enter Brand Phone**: Enter the phone number of the brand.
12. **Enter Brand Facebook**: Enter the brand's Facebook handle.
13. **Enter Brand Twitter**: Enter the brand's Twitter handle.
14. **Enter Face Wash Description**: Provide a brief description of the face wash product.
15. **Enter Face Wash Tags**: Enter tags or keywords associated with the face wash product.
16. **Enter Face Wash Keywords**: Enter keywords associated with the face wash product.
17. **Submit**: Click the submit button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Brand Name**: The name of the brand that owns the face wash product. 
* **Product Name**: The name of the face wash product. 
* **Face Wash Product Type**: The type of face wash product (Cleansing, Moisturizing, Exfoliating, Soothing, Balancing, Anti-acne).
* **Ingredient Name**: List of ingredients used in the face wash product.
* **Ingredients Description**: Brief description of the ingredients used.
* **Face Wash Image**: Upload an image of the face wash product.
* **Face Wash Image URL**: URL of the face wash product image (if not uploaded).
* **Product Price**: Price of the face wash product.
* **Brand URL**: Website URL of the brand.
* **Brand Email**: Email address of the brand.
* **Brand Phone**: Phone number of the brand.
* **Brand Facebook**: Brand's Facebook handle.
* **Brand Twitter**: Brand's Twitter handle.
* **Face Wash Description**: Brief description of the face wash product.
* **Face Wash Tags**: Tags or keywords associated with the face wash product.
* **Face Wash Keywords**: Keywords associated with the face wash product.
* **Face Wash Submitted By**: Name of the person submitting the product. 

Note: All fields are optional, but providing accurate and complete information will help ensure the product is listed correctly and efficiently.
