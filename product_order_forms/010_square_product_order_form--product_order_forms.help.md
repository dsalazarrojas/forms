# Square Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Square Product Order Form is a digital form used to collect information for product orders. This form is used to capture essential details about the product, payment method, and order details for a successful transaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First name** and **Last name** in the fields provided. These fields are required fields.
2. Enter your **Email** address. This field is also required.
3. Enter your **Phone number** in the field provided.
4. Provide the **Product name** of the item you would like to order.
5. Enter the **Quantity** of the product you would like to order.
6. Enter the **Price** of the product you would like to order.
7. Enter a brief description of the **Order details**.
8. Select your preferred **Payment method** from the dropdown menu (Card number, Cash, or Bank transfer).
9. Confirm your **Payment method** by selecting it from the dropdown menu.
10. Confirm your **Order** by selecting it from the dropdown menu.
11. Enter the **Order date** and **Order time** of your transaction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First name** (First name, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Last name** (Last name, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your personal email address.
- **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number.
- **Product name** (Product name, text, required): Enter the name of the product you would like to order.
- **Quantity** (Quantity, number, required): Enter the quantity of the product you would like to order.
- **Price** (Price, number, required): Enter the price of the product you would like to order.
- **Order details** (Order details, text, required): Describe the order in a few words.
- **Payment method** (Payment method, select_one, required): Select your preferred payment method from the options available.
- **Confirm payment** (Confirm payment, select_multiple, required): Confirm your selected payment method.
- **Confirm order** (Confirm order, select_multiple, required): Confirm your order by selecting it from the options available.
- **Order date** (Order date, date, required): Enter the date of your order.
- **Order time** (Order time, time, required): Enter the time of your order.
