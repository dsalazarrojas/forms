# Tncbeans Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Tncbeans Order Form is a general-purpose form for collecting information about a product order. It is likely to be used in a business or sales context where the user needs to provide details about their order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the requested information about yourself.
2. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
3. If you are placing an order, enter the product order ID and quantity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name (1)
* First Name: The field for your first name.
* Type: text
* Required: No (optional)

### Last Name (2)
* Last Name: The field for your last name.
* Type: text
* Required: No (optional)

### Email (3)
* Email: The field for your email address.
* Type: email
* Required: No (optional)

### Phone (4)
* Phone: The field for your phone number.
* Type: text
* Required: No (optional)

### Date (5)
* Date: The field for the date of the order.
* Type: date
* Required: No (optional)

### Time (6)
* Time: The field for the time of the order.
* Type: time
* Required: No (optional)

### Product Order ID (7)
* Product Order ID: The field for your product order ID.
* Type: text
* Required: No (optional)

### Quantity (8)
* Quantity: The field for the quantity of the order.
* Type: number
* Required: No (optional)

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields, if any.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask a sales representative for clarification.
- This form is for general use and may be subject to changes or updates in the future.
