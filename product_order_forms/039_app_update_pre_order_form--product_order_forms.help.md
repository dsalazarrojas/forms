# App Update Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers who are interested in pre-ordering the latest app update, allowing them to get early access to the update and provide valuable feedback.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field. This is how we will notify you when the update is ready.
3. If you are not currently using the app, you can skip this field.
4. Select your operating system/platform from the options provided.
5. Let us know if you are interested in participating in the beta test by selecting "Yes, I'd love to" from the options.
6. Choose your primary use case for the app from the options.
7. Specify the most anticipated feature in the upcoming update.
8. Enter the number of licenses you need in the "Number of licenses needed" field.
9. Select your preferred payment method.
10. Choose how you would like to be notified when the update is ready.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`customer_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where we will notify you when the update is ready.
* **Which version are you currently using?** (`current_version`, `text`, optional): If you are currently using a version of the app, enter it here for our reference.
* **Operating System / Platform** (`user_platform`, `select_one`, required): Select your operating system/platform from the options provided.
	+ iOS
	+ Android
	+ Windows
	+ MacOS
	+ Linux
* **Are you interested in participating in the Beta test?** (`beta_interest`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes, I'd love to" if you are interested in early access to new features.
	+ Yes, I'd love to
	+ No, I prefer the stable release
* **Primary Use Case** (`usage_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose your primary use case from the options.
	+ Personal Use
	+ Professional / Business Use
	+ Educational Use
* **Most anticipated feature in this upcoming update** (`anticipated_feature`, `text`, required): Tell us what feature you are most looking forward to in the new update.
* **Number of licenses needed** (`license_quantity`, `number`, required): Enter the number of licenses you need.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_preference`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred payment method.
	+ Credit Card
	+ PayPal
	+ Apple Pay / Google Pay
	+ Already covered by subscription
* **How should we notify you of the release?** (`notification_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you would like to be notified when the update is ready.
	+ Email
	+ SMS
	+ In-App Notification
* **Any feedback on the current version you'd like us to address?** (`feedback_current`, `text`, optional): Share your feedback on the current version of the app if you have any.
