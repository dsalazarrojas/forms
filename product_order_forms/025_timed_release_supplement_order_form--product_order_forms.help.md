# Timed Release Supplement Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Timed Release Supplement Order Form is used to collect customer information, delivery preferences, and payment details for the timed release supplement. This form is designed to gather essential data to facilitate the order process and ensure timely delivery of the supplement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer's information, including name, contact details, and any relevant notes.
2. Specify the delivery preferences, such as date and time of delivery.
3. Provide details about the timed release supplement, including any relevant medical conditions or allergies.
4. Enter payment information, including method and amount.
5. Enter billing and shipping information for the order.
6. Submit the form to complete the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information (id: 1)** (`customer_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the customer's name, email address, and phone number to ensure timely and secure communication.
* **Delivery Preferences (id: 2)** (`delivery_preferences`, text, required/optional): Specify the date and time of delivery to ensure the supplement is delivered on time.
* **Timed Release Supplement Form (id: 3)** (`timed_release_supplement_form`, text, required/optional): Enter any relevant details about the supplement, including medical conditions or allergies.
* **Customer Considerations (id: 4)** (`customer_considerations`, text, required/optional): Provide any customer considerations or special requests that may affect the order or delivery.
* **Payment Information (id: 5)** (`payment_info`, text, required/optional): Enter payment method and amount to complete the order.
* **Billing Information (id: 6)** (`billing_info`, text, required/optional): Enter billing address and contact details to ensure accurate billing.
* **Shipping Information (id: 7)** (`shipping_info`, text, required/optional): Enter shipping address and contact details to ensure timely delivery.
* **Submit Info (id: 8)** (`submit_info`, text, required/optional): Review and submit the form to complete the order.

## Tips
* Be accurate and clear when filling out the form to ensure timely and secure delivery.
* Check for errors before submitting the form to avoid delays or cancellations.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
