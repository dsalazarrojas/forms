# Color Street Shop My Stash - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Color Street Shop My Stash form is a crucial tool for customers to place their orders and communicate their preferences for product selection, shipping, and potential services. This guide will walk you through each section of the form to ensure you complete it accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Color Street Shop My Stash form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate information.
2. For each field, read the label and hint provided to understand what information is needed.
3. Review and complete each section (e.g., "Shop My Personal Stash", "Billing and Checkout") according to the instructions provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Name
* **Customer Name** (`stash_customer_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification or contact information.
### Email Address
* **Email Address** (`stash_email`, email, required): Enter the email address associated with your order or contact information. If different from the customer name, please use this email for invoicing purposes.
### Mailing Address
* **Mailing Address** (`stash_mailing_address`, text, required): Provide your complete mailing address where your order will be delivered.
### Select from Available Stock
* **Select from Available Stock** (`section_stash_selection`, note, required): Review the current stock list to identify the items you would like to purchase.
### List the Set Names
* **List the set names you would like to purchase** (`stash_items_list`, text, required): Enter the names of the sets you selected from the stock list.
### Total Number of Sets
* **Total Number of Sets** (`stash_total_sets`, number, required): Confirm the total number of sets you are ordering.
### Are you using the B3G1 Deal?
* **Are you using the B3G1 Deal?** (`stash_deal_check`, select_one, required): Choose 'True' if you are using the B3G1 Deal for this order.
### Billing and Checkout
* **Billing and Checkout** (`section_stash_billing`, note, required): This section is a placeholder for billing and checkout instructions. Please review carefully.
### PayPal Email for Invoicing
* **PayPal Email for Invoicing** (`stash_paypal_email`, email, required): Enter your PayPal email address for invoicing purposes.
### Shipping Choice
* **Shipping Choice** (`stash_shipping_speed`, select_one, required): Select the shipping speed that suits your needs and budget: Standard ($4.00), Priority ($8.00), or Local Pick-up (Free).
### Opportunities
* **Opportunities** (`section_stash_inquiries`, note, required): Review this section for potential opportunities related to hosting an online party or becoming a stylist.
### Are you interested in hosting an online party for free nails?
* **Are you interested in hosting an online party for free nails?** (`stash_hostess_interest`, select_one, required): Choose 'True' to express your interest.
### Are you interested in learning about being a Stylist?
* **Are you interested in learning about being a Stylist?** (`stash_stylist_interest`, select_one, required): Choose 'True' to express your interest.
### Who is your hostess or stylist?
* **Who is your hostess or stylist?** (`stash_referral`, text, required): Enter the name or contact information of your hostess or stylist.
### Comments or Questions
* **Comments or Questions** (`stash_comments`, text, required): Share any additional comments or questions you may have with us.

Remember, completing this form accurately and carefully will ensure that your order is processed efficiently and that you receive the best possible service from the Color Street Shop. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
