# race_participant_packet_printing_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create and manage orders for participant packets.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of packet you want to order from the "Packet Type" field.
2. Enter the quantity of packets you want to order in the "Order Quantity" field.
3. Provide your delivery address in the "Delivery Address" field.
4. Add any additional delivery details in the "Delivery Details" field.
5. Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" field.
6. Enter any order notes in the "Order Notes" field.
7. Finally, enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Packet Type (`packet_type`, required: false)**: Select one of the following packet types:
	+ Type A
	+ Type B
	+ Other

* **Order Quantity (`order_quantity`, required: false)**: Enter the quantity of packets you want to order. This field is optional, but ensure to fill it out if you have a specific order quantity.

* **Delivery Address (`delivery_address`, required: false)**: Enter your delivery address where you want the packets to be delivered.

* **Delivery Details (`delivery_details`, required: false)**: Add any additional delivery details, such as special instructions or requests.

* **Payment Method (`payment_method`, required: false)**: Select your preferred payment method:
	+ Online
	+ Offline

* **Order Notes (`order_notes`, required: false)**: Enter any additional notes about your order, such as special requests or instructions.

* **Contact Phone (`contact_phone`, required: false)**: Enter your contact phone number so we can reach you with any questions or updates.
