# in-store-music-licensing-order-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by store owners to request in-store music licensing for their business.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your store details.
2. Provide your usage information, including how you plan to use the licensed music.
3. Enter your payment information.
4. Add your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
5. Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate your consent to receive promotional materials and updates.
6. Provide product information, including the type of product you are requesting music for.
7. Review and agree to the terms and conditions.
8. Enter your billing information, including your billing name, address, and payment method.
9. Enter your shipping information, including your shipping name, address, and shipping method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Store Details**: Please enter your store name, address, and any other relevant details about your store.
* **Usage Information**: Describe how you plan to use the licensed music, e.g. background music, live performances, etc.
* **Payment Info**: Enter your payment method and any relevant payment details.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your name, email, and phone number so we can contact you for further questions or updates.
* **Consent**: Answer "Yes" if you want to receive promotional materials and updates from us. Answer "No" if you don't.
* **Product Info**: Enter the type of product you are requesting music for, e.g. CD, DVD, digital download, etc.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Please read and agree to our terms and conditions to continue with the licensing process.
* **Billing Information**: Enter your billing name, address, and payment method.
* **Shipping Information**: Enter your shipping name, address, and shipping method.
