# Paper Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Paper Order Form is designed to collect information for paper orders from customers. It helps us gather essential details about the customer, order, and delivery status, ensuring accurate and efficient processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) symbol. These fields are First Name, Last Name, Email, Date, Time, Address, Product Name, Order Number, Sales Person, Customer, Order Date, Order Time, and Delivery Status.
2.  Select the correct options for Sales Person, Customer, and Delivery Status.
3.  Enter your notes in the Notes 1 to Notes 5 fields if necessary.
4.  Choose a payment method from the available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name* (1)**: Enter your first name as per your identification.
* **Last Name* (2)**: Enter your last name as per your identification.
* **Email* (3)**: Type your email address for communication purposes.

* **Notes** (8)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your order. This field is not required.

* **Customer* (12)**: Select the customer's name from the available options.
