# Custom Automation Arm Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is meant to guide the customer through a step-by-step process of providing required information for their custom automation arm order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the product details in the first field.
2. Provide customer information in the second field.
3. Enter delivery details in the third field.
4. Enter payment information in the fourth field.
5. Upload any supporting files as required in the fifth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Product Details**
	* (Product Details, Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the custom automation arm you are interested in ordering. This will help us better understand your needs and preferences.

### 2. **Customer Info**
	* (Customer Info, Text, Optional): Please provide your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number. This will help us get in touch with you for any further questions or updates.

### 3. **Delivery Details**
	* (Delivery Details, Text, Optional): Specify the shipping address where you would like to receive your custom automation arm. You can also include any specific delivery instructions you may have.

### 4. **Payment Info**
	* (Payment Info, Text, Optional): Enter your payment method and any relevant payment information. This will help us process your order efficiently.

### 5. **File Uploader**
	* (File Uploader, Text, Optional): If you have any supporting files or documents related to your order, you can upload them here. This can help us better understand your needs and preferences.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are completed as thoroughly as possible.
