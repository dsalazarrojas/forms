# Adventure Map Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adventure Map Order Form is designed to collect information from customers to place an order for an adventure map. It is intended to be used by customers to provide their personal and order-specific details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the instructions below:

*   Fill in the customer's full name, email address, phone number, shipping address, and billing address in the respective fields.
*   Select the map region of interest from the dropdown menu.
*   Choose the map scale and format from the dropdown menus.
*   Select the features that are important for the customer's adventure from the dropdown menu.
*   Specify the quantity of maps ordered.
*   Indicate if custom annotations are needed and provide details if necessary.
*   Select the delivery timeline that suits the customer's needs.
*   Choose the payment method.
*   Confirm that the provided information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (Required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
*   **Email Address** (Required): Enter a valid email address for order confirmation and updates.
*   **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your contact phone number for any further questions or updates.
*   **Shipping Address** (Required): Enter the full shipping address for the map delivery.
*   **Billing Address** (Optional): If different from shipping address, enter the billing address associated with the payment method.
*   **Map Region** (Required): Select the region of interest for the map from the dropdown menu: National Parks, Mountain Ranges, Coastal Areas, Deserts, Forests, International, or Custom Region.
*   **Map Scale** (Required): Choose the scale of the map from the dropdown menu: 1:24,000 (Topographic), 1:62,500 (Regional), 1:250,000 (State), 1:1,000,000 (Country), or Custom Scale.
*   **Map Format** (Required): Select the format of the map from the dropdown menu: Paper Map, Waterproof Map, Digital Download, GPS Compatible File, Mobile App Access, or Bundle Package.
*   **Map Features** (Required): Select the features that are important for your adventure from the dropdown menu: Trail Markings, Elevation Contours, Water Sources, Campgrounds, Emergency Points, Geological Info, or Wildlife Areas.
*   **Quantity Ordered** (Required): Specify the number of maps ordered.
*   **Custom Annotations** (Required): Indicate if custom annotations are needed.
*   **Annotation Details** (Optional): Provide details about the custom annotations if necessary.
*   **Delivery Timeline** (Required): Select the delivery timeline from the dropdown menu: Standard (5-7 days), Express (2-3 days), Rush (1 day), Date specific:, or Flexible.
*   **Payment Method** (Required): Choose the payment method from the dropdown menu: Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Check.
*   **Order Total** (Required): Enter the estimated total cost of the order.
*   **Order Confirmation** (Required): Confirm that the provided information is accurate.
