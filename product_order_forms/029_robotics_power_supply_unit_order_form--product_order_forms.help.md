# Robotics Power Supply Unit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Robotics Power Supply Unit Order Form is a form designed to collect information from customers about their order for robotics power supply units. This form will help us to gather essential details about the customer, their shipping information, delivery preferences, payment information, order details, product selection, and any additional notes or instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your customer details, including name, address, and contact information.
2. Enter your shipping information, including address, phone number, and any other relevant details.
3. Specify your delivery preferences, such as delivery date and time.
4. Enter your payment information, including credit card details or any other payment method.
5. Provide details about the order, including product type and quantity.
6. Select the products you would like to order from the options provided.
7. Add any additional notes or instructions about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details** (customer_details): Enter your name, address, and contact information to help us know who to contact for your order.
* **Shipping Information** (shipping_info): Enter your shipping address, phone number, and any other relevant details to ensure prompt delivery.
* **Delivery Preferences** (delivery_preferences): Specify your delivery date and time to suit your needs.
* **Payment Information** (payment_info): Enter your credit card details or any other payment method to complete your payment.
* **Order Details** (order_details): Enter details about the products you are ordering, including type and quantity.
* **Product Selection** (product_selection): Select the products you would like to order from the provided options.
* **Notes** (notes): Add any additional notes or instructions about your order, such as special requests or instructions.
