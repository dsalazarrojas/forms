# Product Dimensions Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Dimensions Information Form is designed to collect factual data about product dimensions, which is essential for product design, manufacturing, and inventory management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Product Description" field and enter a brief description of the product.
2. Provide the product's material information (if applicable).
3. Enter the dimensional unit used for the product's dimensions (e.g., inches, mm, or cm).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Product Description** (`product_description_info`, Text, Required): This field is where you enter a brief description of the product. This information helps in understanding the product's purpose, design, or features.
- **Material Info** (`material_info`, Text, Optional): If the product is made from a specific material, enter the material name here. This field is not required but can be useful for further product analysis or documentation.
- **Dimensional Unit** (`dimensional_unit`, Text, Optional): Enter the unit of measurement used for the product's dimensions (e.g., inches, mm, or cm). This field is not required but helps in ensuring consistency in dimension reporting.
- **Product Dimensions Information** (`product_dimensions_info`, Text, Not Required): This field is not required but can be used for additional product information or notes that don't fit in other fields.
