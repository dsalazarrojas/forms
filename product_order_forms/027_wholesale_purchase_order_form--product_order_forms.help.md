# wholesale_purchase_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The wholesale purchase order form is used to collect information for large quantity purchases from customers. This form is intended for wholesale orders and should not be used for individual customer orders or retail purchases.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide your name and email address.
3. Enter your contact phone number.
4. Describe your order, including any necessary comments or notes.
5. If applicable, select the items you wish to purchase and indicate whether you'd like to purchase each item.
6. Enter the quantity of each item you wish to purchase.
7. Provide a description of each item you are ordering.
8. Review your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information (Contact Information)**: Enter your name, email address, and phone number.
* **Contact Name (Contact Name)**: Enter the name of the person filling out this form.
* **Contact E-mail (Contact E-mail)**: Enter the email address of the person filling out this form.
* **Contact Phone (Contact Phone)**: Enter the phone number of the person filling out this form.
* **Order Details (Order Details)**: Describe your order, including any necessary comments or notes.
* **Order Date (Order Date)**: Enter the date you expect to receive the order.
* **Customer PO (Customer PO)**: Enter your purchase order number (if applicable).
* **Order Comments (Order Comments)**: Enter any additional comments about your order.
* **Items (Items)**: Select the items you wish to purchase.
* **Item Number (Item Number)**: Indicate whether you'd like to purchase each item (yes or no).
* **Quantity (Quantity)**: Enter the quantity of each item you wish to purchase.
* **Item Description (Item Description)**: Enter a description of each item you are ordering.
* **No Item Description (Item Number)**: Select the description of each item you are ordering is not required.
 
## Tips
* Ensure you have the necessary internal approvals and processes in place before submitting this form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact your internal sales representative for assistance.
