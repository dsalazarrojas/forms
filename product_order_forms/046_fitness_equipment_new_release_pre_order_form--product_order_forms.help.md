# fitness_equipment_new_release_pre_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a new release of fitness equipment from customers. It will help us understand their needs and preferences for the product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the product details section with the product you are interested in, including its title and any other relevant information.
2. Provide your delivery address in the delivery address section.
3. Choose the order date and time you would like to receive the product.
4. Enter your payment information in the payment info section.
5. Add any additional delivery information and order notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Details** (text): Enter the title of the product you are interested in.
* **Delivery Address** (text): Enter your delivery address where the product will be shipped to.
* **Order Date** (date): Choose the date you would like to receive the product.
* **Order Time** (time): Choose the time you would like to receive the product.
* **Payment Info** (text): Enter your payment information, such as credit card details or bank transfer information.
* **Delivery Info** (text): Add any additional delivery information, such as special instructions or requirements.
* **Order Notes** (note): Add any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **Retailer Preferences** (select_one): Choose how you would like to receive the product, such as Retailer, Online Store, or Gym.
* **Gym Info** (text): If you chose Gym as your retailer, provide more information about the gym.
* **Store Info** (text): If you chose Online Store as your retailer, provide more information about the store.
* **Other Info** (text): Add any other relevant information about your order.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Shipping Cost** (number): Enter the shipping cost for your order.
* **Payment Method** (select_multiple): Choose the payment method you would like to use, such as Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Other.
* **Payment Amount** (number): Enter the payment amount for your order.
