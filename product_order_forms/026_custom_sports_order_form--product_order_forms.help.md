# Custom Sports Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom Sports Order Form is a tool used to collect user information and details for custom sports orders in an e-commerce or retail environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the User Information page with your details.
2.  Move on to the Order Details page to provide information about the order.
3.  On the Order Preferences page, specify any preferences or requests for your order.
4.  Enter your contact details on the Contact Details page.
5.  On the Payment Info page, provide your payment information.
6.  The Order Total page is where you'll see the total cost of your order.
7.  Finally, enter your Shipping Address and the Payment Status will be displayed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, email, and any other relevant personal details here. This is the first page of the form and will help us get in touch with you after you fill out the order.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required/optional): Provide information about the custom sports order, such as the type of sports equipment or service you are interested in.
* **Order Preferences** (`order_preferences`, text, required/optional): Specify any preferences or requests for your order, such as special instructions or requirements.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your address, phone number and any other contact preferences here.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your payment information, including card number and other payment details.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, required/optional): This will display the total cost of your order.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your shipping address here.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, text, required/optional): This will display the status of your payment after it is processed.
