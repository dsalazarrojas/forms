# Factory Power Backup Battery Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Factory Power Backup Battery Order Form is designed to collect user information and order details for the purpose of ordering power backup batteries from the factory. This form helps the factory to understand customer requirements and process orders efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the User Information section with your name, contact number, and email address.
2. Provide your delivery details, such as address and delivery dates.
3. Specify your preferred dates for delivery.
4. Enter your order details, including the type and quantity of batteries required.
5. Make a payment through the provided payment field.
6. Enter your contact information, including name and email address.
7. If you have any additional notes or comments, enter them in the Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, `text`, required: false): This is the first section of the form where you can enter your name, contact number, and email address.
* **Delivery Details** (`delivery_info`, `text`, required: false): In this section, please enter your address and delivery dates.
* **Preferred Dates** (`preferred_dates`, `text`, required: false): Specify your preferred dates for delivery.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter the type and quantity of power backup batteries you require.
* **Payment** (`payment`, `text`, required: false): Make a payment through this field.
* **Contact** (`contact`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name and email address for contact purposes.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): If you have any additional comments or notes, enter them here.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* Please review your order details carefully before submitting the form.
