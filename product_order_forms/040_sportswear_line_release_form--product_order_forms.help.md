# sportswear_line_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sportswear_line_release_form is designed to collect product and order information for the sportswear industry. The form is intended to gather details about the product's launch, marketing, and sales assets, as well as order information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide product information in the "Product Info" field.
2. Fill in the "Launch Details" field with relevant details about the product launch.
3. Enter marketing assets related to the product in the "Marketing Assets" field.
4. Provide sales assets related to the product in the "Sales Assets" field.
5. Enter order information in the "Order Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Info** (product_info, text, required/optional: optional): Enter a brief description of the product.
* **Launch Details** (launch_details, text, required/optional: optional): Describe the product's launch details, including dates, events, or other relevant information.
* **Marketing Assets** (marketing_assets, text, required/optional: optional): Upload or describe the marketing assets related to the product, such as images, videos, or social media posts.
* **Sales Assets** (sales_assets, text, required/optional: optional): Provide sales assets related to the product, including sales sheets, product sheets, or other sales-related materials.
* **Order Info** (order_info, text, required/optional: optional): Enter the order information, including order numbers, dates, or other relevant details.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all relevant fields to ensure accurate and complete information.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or issues, contact our support team.
