# Treadmill Pre Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Treadmill Pre Order Form is used to collect information from customers and suppliers before placing an order. This form ensures that both parties are aware of the order details and agree on the terms of the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
2. Enter your email address to ensure that we can contact you regarding your order.
3. If you wish to, enter your phone number for further communication.
4. Select the date when you want the order to be delivered.
5. Enter a brief description of the product you are pre-ordering.
6. Select the quantity of the product.
7. Enter the name of the retailer who you are pre-ordering from.
8. Confirm that you have received the order by selecting "Confirmed" from the options.
9. Confirm that the order meets your requirements by selecting "Confirmed" from the options.
10. Enter a unique identifier for the order.
11. Enter the name of the supplier who you are pre-ordering from.
12. Enter the phone number of the supplier.
13. Enter the email of the supplier.
14. Enter the address of the supplier.
15. Enter your address for delivery.
16. Enter any additional notes for the supplier.
17. Enter any additional notes for the customer.
18. Enter any additional comments for the supplier.
19. Enter any additional comments for the customer.
20. Enter any notes you have.
21. Enter the signature of the customer.
22. Enter the signature of the supplier.
23. Enter any supplier report.
24. Enter the customer report.
25. Enter the date of the supplier signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification. (<type: text, <required: true>)
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where we can contact you regarding your order. (<type: email, <required: true>)
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for further communication. (<type: text, <required: false>)
* **Order Date**: Select the date when you want the order to be delivered. (<type: date, <required: true>)
* **Product Description**: Briefly describe the product you are pre-ordering. (<type: text, <required: false>)
* **Quantity**: Select the quantity of the product. (<type: number, <required: false>)
* **Retailer**: Select the name of the retailer who you are pre-ordering from. (<type: number, <required: true>)
* **Supplier Confirmation**: Confirm that you have received the order by selecting "Confirmed". (<type: select_one, <required: true>)
* **Customer Confirmation**: Confirm that the order meets your requirements by selecting "Confirmed". (<type: select_one, <required: true>)
* **Form ID**: Enter a unique identifier for the order. (<type: text, <required: true>)
* **Supplier Name**: Enter the name of the supplier who you are pre-ordering from. (<type: text, <required: false>)
* **Supplier Phone**: Enter the phone number of the supplier. (<type: text, <required: false>)
* **Supplier Email**: Enter the email of the supplier. (<type: email, <required: false>)
* **Supplier Address**: Enter the address of the supplier. (<type: text, <required: false>)
* **Customer Address**: Enter your address for delivery. (<type: text, <required: false>)
* **Supplier Notes**: Enter any additional notes for the supplier. (<type: text, <required: false>)
* **Customer Notes**: Enter any additional notes for the customer. (<type: text, <required: false>)
* **Supplier Comments**: Enter any additional comments for the supplier. (<type: text, <required: false>)
* **Customer Comments**: Enter any additional comments for the customer. (<type: text, <required: false>)
* **Notes**: Enter any notes you have. (<type: text, <required: false>)
* **Customer Signature**: Enter the signature of the customer. (<type: text, <required: false>)
* **Supplier Signature**: Enter the signature of the supplier. (<type: text, <required: false>)
* **Supplier Report**: Enter any supplier report. (<type: text, <required: false>)
* **Customer Report**: Enter the customer report. (<type: text, <required: false>)
* **Supplier Signature Date**: Enter the date of the supplier signature. (<type: date, <required: false>)
