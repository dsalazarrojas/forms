# scent_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "scent_order_form" is a form designed to collect information about a customer's order for a scent product. This form is used to gather essential details such as customer contact information, order specifics, and review or feedback data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer information:
	* Enter your name in the "customer_name" field.
	* Enter your email address in the "customer_email" field.
	* Enter your phone number in the "customer_phone" field.
2. Select the product you ordered:
	* Enter the name of the product you are ordering in the "product_name" field.
	* Select the scent of the product you ordered in the "product_scent" field.
3. Enter order details:
	* Enter the date you placed the order in the "order_date" field.
	* Enter the time you placed the order in the "order_time" field.
4. Provide additional information or comments:
	* If you have any additional information or comments about your order, enter them in the "order_notes" field.
5. Rate your experience:
	* Select a rating from 1 to 5 in the "review_rating" field.
6. Leave feedback:
	* If you would like to provide feedback about your experience, enter your comments in the "review_comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **customer_name**: Enter your name.
- **customer_email**: Enter your email address.
- **customer_phone**: Enter your phone number.
- **product_name**: Enter the name of the product you are ordering.
- **product_scent**: Select the scent of the product you ordered.
- **order_date**: Enter the date you placed the order.
- **order_time**: Enter the time you placed the order.
- **order_notes**: Enter any additional information or comments about your order.
- **review_rating**: Select a rating from 1 to 5 for your experience.
- **review_comments**: Enter any feedback or comments about your experience.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for all fields to ensure efficient processing of your order.
- If you have any issues or concerns, please contact our support team.
- The form is designed to be completed in a few minutes, so please be concise and clear in your responses.
- If you are unable to complete the form, please contact our support team for assistance.
