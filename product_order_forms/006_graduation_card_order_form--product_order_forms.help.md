# Graduation Card Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Graduation Card Order Form is used to collect information for customers who wish to purchase graduation cards. The form includes fields for customer, shipping, graduation card, and payment information, as well as fields for order details and notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
2. Enter your customer information in the Customer Information section.
3. Enter your shipping information in the Shipping Info section.
4. Select the graduation card information in the Graduation Card Info section.
5. Enter your payment information in the Payment Information section.
6. Review and confirm your order summary.
7. Upload any necessary images or files for the graduation card.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information (Customer Information)** (`customer_info`, `text`, required: false)
    + Enter your name, email, and phone number.
* **Shipping Info (Shipping Info)** (`shipping_info`, `text`, required: false)
    + Enter your shipping address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Graduation Card Info (Graduation Card Info)** (`graduation_card_info`, `text`, required: false)
    + Select the type of graduation card you want to order.
* **Payment Information (Payment Information)** (`payment_info`, `text`, required: false)
    + Enter your payment method and details (e.g. credit card, PayPal).
* **Order Summary (Order Summary)** (`order_summary`, `text`, required: false)
    + Review and confirm your order details.
* **Graduation Card (Graduation Card)** (`graduation_card`, `select_one`, required: false)
    + Select the type of graduation card you want to order.
* **Graduation Card Quantity (Graduation Card Quantity)** (`graduation_card_quantity`, `number`, required: false)
    + Enter the quantity of graduation cards you want to order.
* **Graduation Card Text (Graduation Card Text)** (`graduation_card_text`, `text`, required: false)
    + Enter any additional text you want to include on the graduation card.
* **Graduation Card Image Url (Graduation Card Image Url)** (`graduation_card_image_url`, `text`, required: false)
    + Upload a URL for the graduation card image.
* **Graduation Card Image (Graduation Card Image)** (`graduation_card_image`, `text`, required: false)
    + Upload the graduation card image file.
* **Graduation Card Price (Graduation Card Price)** (`graduation_card_price`, `number`, required: false)
    + Enter the price of the graduation card.
* **Graduation Card Discount (Graduation Card Discount)** (`graduation_card_discount`, `number`, required: false)
    + Enter any applicable discounts.
* **Graduation Card Coupon (Graduation Card Coupon)** (`graduation_card_coupon`, `number`, required: false)
    + Enter any applicable coupons.
* **Customer Address (Customer Address)** (`customer_address`, `text`, required: false)
    + Enter your address.
* **Customer Phone (Customer Phone)** (`customer_phone`, `text`, required: false)
    + Enter your phone number.
* **Customer Email (Customer Email)** (`customer_email`, `email`, required: false)
    + Enter your email address.
* **Customer Message (Customer Message)** (`customer_message`, `text`, required: false)
    + Enter any message for the customer.
* **Customer Note (Customer Note)** (`customer_note`, `text`, required: false)
    + Enter any notes for the customer.
* **Order Date (Order Date)** (`order_date`, `date`, required: false)
    + Enter the order date.
* **Order Time (Order Time)** (`order_time`, `time`, required: false)
    + Enter the order time.
