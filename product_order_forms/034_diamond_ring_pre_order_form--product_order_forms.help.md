# Diamond Ring Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Diamond Ring Pre Order Form is a tool used to collect customer information and ring details for custom diamond ring pre-orders. This form is designed to guide users through the process of providing essential information for their order.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for **Customer Information** (page 1).
2. Describe the desired ring details in the **Ring Details** field (page 1).
3. Provide engraving information in the **Engrave Info** field (page 1).
4. Choose a delivery method by selecting one of the options in the **Delivery Method** field (page 2).
5. Provide any additional comments or messages in the **Message** field (page 2).
6. Review and submit the form to complete your pre-order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, optional): This field is for customers to provide their name, email address, and other contact details.
* **Ring Details** (`ring_details`, text, optional): Describe the type of diamond ring you are interested in pre-ordering, including any specific features or customizations you'd like.
* **Ring Engrave** (`ring_engrave`, text, optional): Enter any engraving information, such as names, dates, or messages, you'd like to be engraved on the ring.
* **Delivery Info** (`delivery_info`, text, optional): Enter any relevant delivery information, such as a specific address or gift message.
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, select_one, required): Select one of the following delivery options: Delivery by Mail, In-Store Pickup, or Local Delivery.
* **Engrave Info** (`engrave_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional engraving information, such as font styles or sizes.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): Leave any additional comments or messages for the customer support team.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in for accurate processing of your pre-order.
* If you have any questions or need assistance with your pre-order, don't hesitate to contact our customer support team.
* We'll be in touch with you shortly after submitting your pre-order form to confirm receipt of your order and provide further instructions.
