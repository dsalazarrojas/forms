# Gym Social Media Backdrop Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gym Social Media Backdrop Order Form is a tool used to create and manage social media content for your gym. This form helps you gather information about your brand, payment options, and shipping details, ensuring you have a consistent and accurate online presence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of content you want to order, choosing from the options 'Gym Logo', 'Branding', or 'Other'.
2. Provide the payment information, including the amount and method (cash, credit, or PayPal).
3. Enter any additional shipping information if required.
4. Fill in the shipping address if you are choosing to ship the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Gym Logo** (`gym_logo_order`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the type of content you want to order, such as a gym logo, branding material, or other items.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant payment details, such as any additional notes or information about the payment.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total payment amount for the ordered content.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the payment method you prefer, whether it's cash, credit, or PayPal.
* **Shipping Info** (`shipping_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional shipping information that might be relevant to your order.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, `text`, required: false): Fill in the shipping address if you have chosen to ship the ordered content to a different location.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the required fields, even though they are not marked as 'required', as they are still necessary for a complete order.
- If you are having trouble choosing the correct payment method or shipping address, refer back to your gym's payment and shipping policies for more information.
