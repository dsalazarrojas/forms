# Personalized Necklace Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect order information from customers for personalized necklaces.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer information, including name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide order details, such as product description and image (if applicable).
3. Select the shipping method and date (if applicable).
4. Fill in the shipping address and billing address (if applicable).
5. Enter the order total and status.
6. Add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional): Describe the order you would like to place.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, optional): Provide any additional description of the product you are ordering.
* **Product Image** (`product_image`, text, optional): Upload an image of the product (if applicable).
* **Customer Note** (`customer_note`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes for the customer.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Shipping Address** (`customer_shipping_address`, text, optional): Enter the customer's shipping address.
* **Customer Billing Address** (`customer_billing_address`, text, optional): Enter the customer's billing address.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, optional): Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the current status of the order (New, In Progress, Delivered, Cancelled).
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the order.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments for the customer.
* **Customer Shipping Date** (`customer_shipping_date`, date, optional): Select the shipping date.
* **Customer Shipping Time** (`customer_shipping_time`, time, optional): Select the shipping time.
* **Customer Shipping Method** (`customer_shipping_method`, select_one, optional): Select the shipping method (Standard Shipping, Express Shipping, Pickup).
* **Customer Shipping Address Line 1** (`customer_shipping_address_line_1`, text, optional): Enter the first line of the shipping address.
* **Customer Shipping Address Line 2** (`customer_shipping_address_line_2`, text, optional): Enter the second line of the shipping address.
* **Customer Shipping City** (`customer_shipping_city`, text, optional): Enter the city of the shipping address.
* **Customer Shipping State** (`customer_shipping_state`, text, optional): Enter the state of the shipping address.
* **Customer Shipping Zip** (`customer_shipping_zip`, text, optional): Enter the zip code of the shipping address.
* **Customer Shipping Country** (`customer_shipping_country`, text, optional): Enter the country of the shipping address.
* **Customer Billing Country** (`customer_billing_country`, text, optional): Enter the country of the billing address.
* **Customer Billing State** (`customer_billing_state`, text, optional): Enter the state of the billing address.
* **Customer Billing Zip** (`customer_billing_zip`, text, optional): Enter the zip code of the billing address.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* We will contact you with any further questions or concerns about your order.
