# Oh My Closet! Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Oh My Closet! Order Form is used by customers to submit their order information for the business.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the form with your name, shipping address, and contact information.
2.  Enter your order date in the "Order Date" field.
3.  Add any additional order details in the "Order Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Shipping Address 1** (`shipping_address1`, `text`, required: false): Enter your shipping address, including the address line 1.
*   **Shipping Address 2** (`shipping_address2`, `text`, required: false): Enter your shipping address, including the address line 2.
*   **Shipping City** (`shipping_city`, `text`, required: false): Enter the city of your shipping address.
*   **Shipping State** (`shipping_state`, `text`, required: false): Enter the state of your shipping address.
*   **Shipping Postal Code** (`shipping_postal_code`, `text`, required: false): Enter the zip code of your shipping address.
*   **Shipping Country** (`shipping_country`, `text`, required: false): Enter the country of your shipping address.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
*   **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of your order.
*   **Order Details** (`order_details`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional order information in this field.

## Tips

*   Double-check your contact information for accuracy before submitting the form.
*   Ensure that your order date is correct and reflects the date of your order.
*   Be sure to add any additional order details that may be helpful for the business to process your order.
