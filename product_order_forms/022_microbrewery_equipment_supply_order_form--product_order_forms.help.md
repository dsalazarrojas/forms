# Microbrewery Equipment Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help microbreweries order equipment and supplies from suppliers. It collects information about the equipment needed, delivery details, payment methods, contact information, and shipping addresses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of equipment you need to order.
2. Choose your delivery details, including the date and time you would like to receive the equipment.
3. Confirm your terms and conditions of the sale.
4. Enter your contact information, including name, email, phone number, and company.
5. Provide any additional contact notes or information.
6. Enter your shipping address, including city and zip code.
7. Confirm that you have accepted the terms and conditions.
8. Submit your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Equipment Type** (`Equipment Type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of equipment you are ordering, such as "Yes" for a specific type of equipment or "No" if you are unsure.
* **Delivery Details** (`Delivery Details`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your preferred delivery date and time, or select "Yes" to request a specific delivery time.
* **Delivery Date** (`Delivery Date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to receive the equipment, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Delivery Time** (`Delivery Time`, time, optional): Select the time you would like to receive the equipment.
* **Terms Confirmation** (`Terms Confirmation`, select_one, optional): Confirm that you have read and accepted the terms and conditions of the sale, by selecting "Yes".
* **Supplier Information** (`Supplier Information`, select_multiple, optional): Choose whether you would like to receive information from the supplier about their products and services.
* **Quantity Ordered** (`Quantity Ordered`, number, optional): Enter the number of equipment items you are ordering.
* **Unit Price** (`Unit Price`, number, optional): Enter the price per unit of equipment.
* **Total Cost** (`Total Cost`, number, optional): Enter the total cost of the equipment ordered.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, select_one, optional): Confirm whether you will be paying by credit card or other accepted payment method.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, select_multiple, optional): Choose whether you would like to receive contact information from the supplier about your order.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to receive updates about your order.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number to receive updates about your order.
* **Contact Name** (`Contact Name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the person contacting the supplier.
* **Contact Title** (`Contact Title`, text, optional): Enter your title or position.
* **Contact Company** (`Contact Company`, text, optional): Enter the name of your company.
* **Contact Notes** (`Contact Notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional contact notes or information.
* **Shipping Address** (`Shipping Address`, text, optional): Enter the address where you would like the equipment to be shipped.
* **Shipping City** (`Shipping City`, text, optional): Enter the city where you would like the equipment to be shipped.
* **Shipping State** (`Shipping State`, text, optional): Enter the state where you would like the equipment to be shipped.
* **Shipping Zip** (`Shipping Zip`, text, optional): Enter the zip code where you would like the equipment to be shipped.
* **Terms Accepted** (`Terms Accepted`, select_one, optional): Confirm that you have read and accepted the terms and conditions of the sale, by selecting "Yes".
* **Form Submit** (`Form Submit`, select_one, optional): Confirm that you are ready to submit your order, by selecting "Yes".
