# sims_files_purchase_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for placing an order for digital files. It's used to collect customer details, order details, and payment information to facilitate the order fulfillment and payment processing process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Email address.
3. Provide your Phone number.
4. Select your Country.
5. Enter the Delivery Details.
6. Enter the Delivery Address.
7. Enter the Delivery City.
8. Enter the Delivery State.
9. Enter the Delivery Zip.
10. Enter the Order Details.
11. Select the Purchase Price and currency.
12. Choose your Payment Method (e.g., PayPal, Stripe, Bank Transfer, or Credit/Debit Card).
13. Confirm that the purchase is true (this is the final confirmation of the order).
14. Confirm that the purchase confirmation email has been sent (this will ensure the customer receives the email).
15. Enter any additional Payment Status and Notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (required): Enter your first name as it is typically displayed.
* **Last Name** (required): Enter your last name as it is typically displayed.
* **Email** (required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (optional): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted.
* **Country** (optional): Select your country of residence.
* **Delivery Details** (optional): Enter any specific delivery instructions or requests.
* **Delivery Address** (optional): Enter the address where the digital file will be delivered.
* **Delivery City** (optional): Enter the city where the delivery address is located.
* **Delivery State** (optional): Enter the state where the delivery address is located.
* **Delivery Zip** (optional): Enter the postal/zip code of the delivery address.
* **Order Details** (optional): Enter any additional information about the order.
* **Purchase Price** (optional): Enter the price of the digital file (in the selected currency).
* **Purchase Currency** (optional): Select the currency of the digital file price.
* **Payment Method** (optional): Select the method of payment (e.g., PayPal, Stripe, Bank Transfer, or Credit/Debit Card).
* **Confirm Purchase** (required): Confirm that this is a real order.
* **Purchase Confirmation** (optional): Enter a confirmation message to the customer.
* **Purchase Confirmation Email** (optional): Enter the email address to send the purchase confirmation email to.
* **Purchase Confirmation Sent** (required): Confirm that the purchase confirmation email has been sent.
* **Payment Status** (optional): Enter the status of the payment.
* **Payment ID** (optional): Enter any additional payment information.
* **Purchase Date** (optional): Enter the date of the purchase.
* **Payment Method Note** (optional): Enter any additional information about the payment method.
* **Confirm Payment** (required): Confirm that the payment has been made.
* **Notes** (optional): Enter any additional notes about the order.
* **Form ID** (not required): This field is not necessary for completing the form, it seems to be an internal identification field.
