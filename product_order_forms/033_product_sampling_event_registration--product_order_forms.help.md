# Product Sampling Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from users interested in receiving free product samples. It is designed to collect event-specific details and user preferences to provide relevant samples.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Enter a brief description of the event you are interested in attending.
2.  Select any product samples you are interested in receiving.
3.  Provide your attendee details (name, email, and phone number).
4.  Indicate any dietary restrictions you have.
5.  If applicable, provide additional preferences or instructions.
6.  Finally, enter any additional information about the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **event_details (Text)**:
This field is used to provide a brief description of the event you are interested in attending.

### 2. **sample_preferences (Select Multiple)**:
Select any product samples you are interested in receiving. You can choose one or multiple options from the list.

### 3. **attendee_details (Text)**:
Enter your name, email, and phone number. This information will be used to contact you about the event.

### 4. **dietary_restrictions (Select Multiple)**:
Indicate any dietary restrictions you have, such as Vegan, Vegetarian, or Gluten-free.

### 5. **additional_sample_preferences (Note)**:
If you have any specific product preferences or instructions, provide additional information here. For example, if you are particularly interested in a certain type of product, you can mention it in this field.

### 6. **event_details (Text)**:
Enter any additional information about the event you are interested in attending, if applicable. This can include details such as your company name or department for the event.

## Tips
* Be specific when selecting product samples to ensure you receive the most relevant samples.
* If you have any additional preferences or instructions, be sure to include them in the "additional_sample_preferences" field.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
