# Slime Ingredient Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user information, order details, and payment information for the purchase of slime ingredients. It helps us process your order efficiently and ensure that we have all the necessary information to fulfill your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your user information in the "User Information" section.
2. Provide details about your order, including any additional items you'd like to purchase, in the "Order Details" section.
3. Choose your payment method and shipping method from the respective dropdown menus.
4. Enter your shipping address and any special instructions or comments about your order in the "Shipping Info" and "Order Comment" sections.
5. Review and confirm your total amount due.
6. Once you've completed all the fields, click submit to place your order.
