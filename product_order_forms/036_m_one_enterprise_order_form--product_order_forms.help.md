# M One Enterprise Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The M One Enterprise Order Form is designed for customers or business representatives to submit and manage orders for products or services. This form captures necessary information to track and fulfill order requests.

## How To Complete This Form
- Click on the "New Order" button to initiate the order creation process.
- Select the appropriate product type from the available options.
- Enter the desired quantity for the order.
- Provide the product name and description if needed.
- Upload a product image if required.
- Enter the customer's name, contact number, email, and address.
- Choose the order status and date and time of the order.
- Optionally, select the assigned tool for the order.
- Add any customer notes for further clarification.
- Fill in any other required fields as per the form's specifications.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Normal Essentials** (`new_normal_essentials_form`, Select): Select the type of product or service being ordered. Options include Normal, New, and Normalized.
* **Product Type** (`product_type`, Select Multiple): Choose multiple product types as needed.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, Number): Enter the desired quantity of the ordered product.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, Text): Enter the name of the product or service being ordered.
* **Price** (`price`, Number): Enter the price of the product or service being ordered.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, Text): Enter a brief description of the product or service being ordered.
* **Product Image** (`product_image`, Note): Upload an image of the product or service being ordered, if required.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, Text): Enter the name of the person placing the order.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, Text): Enter the customer's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, Email): Enter the customer's email address for communication.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, Select): Choose the status of the order. Options include Active, Inactive, and Cancelled.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, Date): Enter the date of the order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, Time): Enter the time of the order.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, Text): Select the tool assigned to the order, if required.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, Note): Add any additional notes from the customer for the order.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, Number): Enter the ID of the form, if required.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, Text): Enter the name of the person creating the form.
* **Created Date** (`created_date`, Date): Enter the date the form was created.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, Text): Enter the name of the person updating the form.
* **Updated Date** (`updated_date`, Date): Enter the date the form was last updated.
* **Form Version** (`form_version`, Text): Enter the version of the form.
* **Form Status** (`form_status`, Select): Choose the status of the form. Options include Active, Inactive, and Cancelled.
* **Form Notes** (`form_notes`, Note): Add any notes about the form.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, Text): Enter the customer's address.
* **Customer City** (`customer_city`, Text): Enter the customer's city.

## Tips
- Ensure that you fill in all required fields accurately to ensure a successful order submission.
- Review the form carefully to ensure that you have selected the correct product type and order status.
- Use the product image field to upload any required images for the order.
- Enter the customer's name and contact information accurately to ensure timely communication.
- Use the customer notes field to add any additional information about the order.
- Save the form before submitting it to avoid losing any entered data.
