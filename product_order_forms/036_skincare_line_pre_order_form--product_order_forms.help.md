# skincare_line_pre_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to create a pre-order for a skincare product line. It collects information about the customer, product selection, and order details. Please fill out this form accurately to ensure a smooth pre-order process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with the requested information.
2. Review each field for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address. This will be used for communication and order updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Product** (`product`, text, required): Select the specific skincare product you would like to pre-order.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Choose the quantity of the product you would like to pre-order.
* **Shipping Date** (`shipping_date`, date, required): Select the date you would like the product to be shipped.
* **Confirm Reservation** (`confirm_reservation`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and understood the pre-order terms and conditions.
