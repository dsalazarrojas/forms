# Smart Scale Equipment Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for the fulfillment of smart scale equipment orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by entering your customer information on Page 1.
2. Move on to Page 2 to enter your shipping details.
3. Finally, enter your payment information on Page 3.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`): Enter your name, company name (if applicable), and any other relevant details. This is where you will start your submission process.
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`): Enter your shipping address, contact phone number, and any other relevant shipping details. This information is important for delivering the ordered equipment to the correct location.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`): Enter your payment method and any other payment details as required. This is the final step before submitting your order.
 
## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check your submissions before finalizing your order.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
