# rare_plant_pre_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The rare plant pre-order form is designed for customers to pre-order rare plants from a supplier or online store. This form collects contact information, pre-order details, and payment information to process orders efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your contact information and pre-order details.
2. Provide your delivery preferences to ensure timely and convenient shipping.
3. Enter your payment details to complete the order.
4. Review and confirm your order before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information (id: 1)**: Provide your name, email address, and phone number so we can contact you regarding your order.
* **Rare Plant Pre-order (id: 2)**: Enter the name and quantity of the rare plant you'd like to pre-order. Please be specific about the plant species.
* **Delivery Preferences (id: 3)**: Let us know your preferred shipping method and any special instructions for delivery.
* **Payment Details (id: 4)**: Enter your payment method (e.g., credit card number, PayPal) and any other relevant payment information.
* **Order Confirmation (id: 5)**: Review your order details to ensure accuracy and completeness. This is your chance to double-check everything before submitting.
* **Contact Us (id: 6)**: If you have any questions or concerns, or if you'd like to inquire about your order, use this field to reach out to us.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date contact information to ensure timely communication about your order.
* Double-check your order details before submitting to avoid any mistakes.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us using the 'Contact Us' field.
