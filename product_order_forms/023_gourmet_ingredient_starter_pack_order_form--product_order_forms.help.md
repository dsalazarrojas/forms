# Gourmet Ingredient Starter Pack Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help customers create a customized order for a gourmet ingredient starter pack. Please take a few minutes to fill out the form below to create your order.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Order Information**: This section is for general information about your order. You can enter any relevant details you'd like to provide.
2. **Customer Details**: Provide your name and any other relevant contact information you'd like to share with us.
3. **Ingredient Selection**: Choose the ingredients you'd like to include in your starter pack from the list below:
	* Apple
	* Carrot
	* Celery
	* Cucumber
	* Garlic
	* Mushroom
	* Onion
	* Pepper
	* Tomatoes
4. **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of each ingredient you'd like to have in your starter pack. Please note that you can only enter numbers for this field.
5. **Delivery Address**: Enter your delivery address where you'd like to receive your order.
6. **Delivery Time**: Select the time you'd like your order to be delivered. Please choose from the available options (AM, PM, or 24-hour format).
7. **Special Instructions**: If you have any special requests or instructions for your order, please enter them here. This is a good place to provide any additional information that might help us fulfill your order.
8. **Order Notes**: If you have any other notes or comments about your order, please enter them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Information (order_information)**: This field is for any general information about your order that you'd like to provide.
* **Customer Details (customer_details)**: Please enter your name and any other contact information you'd like to share with us.
* **Ingredient Selection (ingredient_selection)**: Choose the ingredients you'd like to include in your starter pack from the list below. You can select multiple items.
	+ **Apple**: Select this option to include apples in your starter pack.
	+ **Carrot**: Select this option to include carrots in your starter pack.
	+ **Celery**: Select this option to include celery in your starter pack.
	+ **Cucumber**: Select this option to include cucumbers in your starter pack.
	+ **Garlic**: Select this option to include garlic in your starter pack.
	+ **Mushroom**: Select this option to include mushrooms in your starter pack.
	+ **Onion**: Select this option to include onions in your starter pack.
	+ **Pepper**: Select this option to include peppers in your starter pack.
	+ **Tomatoes**: Select this option to include tomatoes in your starter pack.
* **Quantity (quantity)**: Enter the quantity of each ingredient you'd like to have in your starter pack. Please enter a number for this field.
* **Delivery Address (delivery_address)**: Enter your delivery address where you'd like to receive your order.
* **Delivery Time (delivery_time)**: Select the time you'd like your order to be delivered. Please choose from the available options (AM, PM, or 24-hour format).
* **Special Instructions (special_instructions)**: If you have any special requests or instructions for your order, please enter them here.
* **Order Notes (order_notes)**: If you have any other notes or comments about your order, please enter them here.

## Tips

* Please make sure to enter accurate information to ensure timely and correct delivery of your order.
* If you have any special requests or instructions, please include them in the Special Instructions field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
