# industrial_control_panel_assembly_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect customer information, order details, and payment and shipping information for industrial control panel assembly orders.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Customer Information section with the customer's contact details.
2. Provide order details, including any specific requirements or requests.
3. Enter delivery information, including the date and time of delivery.
4. Specify payment information, including the payment method and amount.
5. Confirm the order total, shipping cost, and any additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the customer's name.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the specific requirements or requests for the order.
* **Delivery Information** (`delivery_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the delivery date and time.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date of delivery.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, `time`, required/optional): Select the time of delivery.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required/true): Select the payment method, which can be Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the amount to be paid.
* **Shipping Cost** (`shipping_cost`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the shipping cost.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Order Config Notes** (`order_config_notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any notes or comments about the order configuration.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the current order status, which can be New Order, In Progress, Completed, or Cancelled.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the payment status, which can be Paid, Pending, or Cancelled.
* **Customer Info Notes** (`customer_info_notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any notes or comments about the customer's information.
* **Order Config Info** (`order_config_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any notes or comments about the order configuration.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the order.
* **Delivery Notes** (`delivery_notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any notes or comments about the delivery.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the total amount of the order.
* **Shipping Information** (`shipping_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the shipping information, including any additional notes or comments.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the customer's address.

Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely. If you have any doubts or questions, please reach out to the order processing team.
