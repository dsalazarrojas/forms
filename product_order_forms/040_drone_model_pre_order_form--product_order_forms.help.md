# Drone Model Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers who want to pre-order a drone model. It is intended for customers who are interested in purchasing a drone and need to provide their personal details, drone preferences, and order specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Select a delivery date in the "Delivery Date" field.
7. Select a delivery time in the "Delivery Time" field.
8. Enter the quantity of drones you want to order in the "Quantity" field.
9. Select the color of your preferred drone model from the "Color" dropdown menu.
10. Select one or multiple drone models from the "Model" multi-select menu.
11. If you have any additional comments or questions, enter them in the "Message" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, *): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, *): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, *): Enter your email address, which we will use to contact you for order updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, *): Enter your phone number, which we will use to contact you for order updates.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, *): Select a date for your drone delivery.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, *): Select a time for your drone delivery.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, *): Enter the number of drones you want to order.
* **Color** (`color`, select_one, *): Select one of the available colors for your drone model.
* **Model** (`model`, select_multiple, *): Select one or multiple drone models you are interested in pre-ordering.
* **Message** (`message`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you have about your order.
