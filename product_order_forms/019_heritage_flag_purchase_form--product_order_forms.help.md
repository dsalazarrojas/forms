# Heritage Flag Purchase Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Heritage Flag Purchase Form is designed to collect information from customers for the purchase of heritage flags. The form is used to gather customer information, shipping details, payment information, and order details to process the order accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the customer information section with your name and contact details.
* Provide shipping details, including your address and any additional instructions for the delivery.
* Fill in the payment information section with your payment method and details.
* Enter the flag information, including any special message or occasion.
* Select the order status (if you want to update the order status).
* Add any additional notes or comments in the note section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, `text`, optional): Enter your name and contact details to help us process your order accurately.
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, `text`, optional): Provide your shipping address and any additional instructions for the delivery.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, `text`, optional): Enter your payment method and details to complete the order.
* **Flag Information** (`flag_info`, `text`, optional): Enter any special message or occasion for the flag.
* **Total** (`order_total`, `number`, optional): Enter the total cost of the flag purchase.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the current status of your order (e.g., Order Placed, Order Shipped, Order Cancelled).
* **Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address to contact you.
* **Phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Note** (`customer_note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **Additional Note** (`customer_note`, `note`, optional): Add any other additional comments about your order.

Please note that some fields are not required, but filling them in will help us process your order more efficiently.
