# Party Favours Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Party Favours Order Form is designed to collect order details for party favours. This form helps us efficiently manage party favours orders and ensures that we get the required information for successful delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the order details (page 1).
2. Provide the guests' number and their names (page 1).
3. Select the gift type and add a message (page 1).
4. Schedule the delivery date and time (page 1).
5. Choose the delivery method and specify the address (page 1).
6. Enter any additional information, such as email and phone number (page 1).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Order Details
* **Order Details** (Page 1, `order_details`): Enter a brief description of the order.
* **Guests Number** (Page 1, `guests_number`): Enter the number of guests attending the party.
* **Guest Name** (Page 1, `guest_name`): Enter the name of the guest(s).
* **Gift Type** (Page 1, `gift_type`): Select the type of gift you want to order (Gift, Card, or Other).
* **Gift Message** (Page 1, `gift_message`): Add a personal message with the gift.
* **Delivery Date** (Page 1, `delivery_date`): Enter the date you want the gift delivered.
* **Delivery Time** (Page 1, `delivery_time`): Enter the time you want the gift delivered.
* **Party Date** (Page 1, `party_date`): Enter the date of the party.
* **Party Time** (Page 1, `party_time`): Enter the time of the party.
* **Delivery Method** (Page 1, `delivery_method`): Select how you want the gift delivered (In Person, Courier, or Postal).
* **Delivery Address** (Page 1, `delivery_address`): Enter the address where the gift should be delivered.
* **Delivery Location** (Page 1, `delivery_location`): Enter the location of the delivery.
* **Email** (Page 1, `email`): Enter the email address of the recipient.
* **Phone** (Page 1, `phone`): Enter the phone number of the recipient.
* **Note** (Page 1, `note`): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Assigned Tool** (Page 1, `assigned_tool`): Enter the tool or equipment assigned to the order.
* **Assigned By** (Page 1, `assigned_by`): Enter the person or team assigned to the order.
