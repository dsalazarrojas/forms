## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Date (id: 1)**: The date on which the order is being placed. 
* **Customer Name (id: 2)**: The name of the customer, as they wish to be known on the invoice. 
* **Customer Address (id: 3)**: A text input for the customer's address, which could include their street, city, and state. This field is for internal use, as the customer will likely already be entered in their account information, but we need this on the order form for order fulfillment. 
* **Customer Email (id: 4)**: An email address for the customer to receive order notifications. 
* **Customer Phone (id: 5)**: A phone number for the customer to receive order updates or for any urgent communication. 
* **Order Description (id: 6)**: A description of the order, including any specific details about the product, shipping, or other relevant information. 

## Tips
* Be sure to enter accurate customer information to ensure smooth order fulfillment and communication.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are completed.
* If you're unsure about any part of the form, contact the order department for assistance.
