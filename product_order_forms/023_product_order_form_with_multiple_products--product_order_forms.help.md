# Product Order Form With Multiple Products - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about ordered products, allowing customers to specify the details of their orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the product fields with the desired product names.
2. Enter the corresponding quantity for each product in the respective quantity field (e.g. Quantity One for Product One, etc.).
3. Review your inputs and click "Save" to save your order or "Back" to reset your inputs and try again.
4. When ready, click "Submit" to complete your order.
