# stripe_clothing_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a product order, including order details, customer information, shipping details, payment information, and other relevant fields.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the order details page with the necessary information.
2. Move on to the customer information page and fill in the required fields.
3. Proceed to the shipping details page and provide the required information.
4. Move to the payment information page and fill in the necessary fields.
5. Select the items ordered on the items ordered page.
6. Choose shipping options on the shipping options page.
7. Make any special requests on the special requests page.
8. Add any notes on the notes page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Order Details**: Enter the details of the order, such as the order date and time.
  - **title (Page 1, id: 1)**: Enter a brief description of the order.
- **Customer Info**: Enter the customer's contact information, including their email and phone number.
  - **customer_info (Page 2, id: 2)**: Enter the customer's name and contact information.
- **Shipping Details**: Enter the shipping details, including the shipping address.
  - **shipping_details (Page 3, id: 3)**: Enter the shipping details, including the name and address of the recipient.
- **Payment Info**: Enter the payment information, including the payment method and status.
  - **payment_info (Page 4, id: 4)**: Enter the payment method and status.
- **Items Ordered**: Select the items ordered.
  - **items_ordered (Page 5, id: 5)**: Select the items ordered, multiple selection allowed.
- **Shipping Options**: Select the shipping options.
  - **shipping_options (Page 6, id: 6)**: Select the shipping options, multiple selection allowed.
- **Special Requests**: Make any special requests.
  - **special_requests (Page 7, id: 7)**: Enter any special requests.
- **Notes**: Add any notes.
  - **notes (Page 8, id: 8)**: Enter any additional comments or notes.
- **Email**: Enter the customer's email address.
  - **email (Page 9, id: 9)**: Enter the customer's email address.
- **Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number.
  - **phone (Page 10, id: 10)**: Enter the customer's phone number.
- **Address**: Enter the shipping address.
  - **address (Page 11, id: 11)**: Enter the shipping address.
- **Country**: Select the country of the shipping address.
  - **country (Page 12, id: 12)**: Select the country of the shipping address.
- **State**: Select the state of the shipping address.
  - **state (Page 13, id: 13)**: Select the state of the shipping address.
- **Zip**: Select the zip code of the shipping address.
  - **zip (Page 14, id: 14)**: Select the zip code of the shipping address.
- **City**: Enter the city of the shipping address.
  - **city (Page 15, id: 15)**: Enter the city of the shipping address.
- **Order Date**: Enter the order date.
  - **order_date (Page 16, id: 16)**: Enter the date of the order.
- **Order Time**: Enter the order time.
  - **order_time (Page 17, id: 17)**: Enter the time of the order.
- **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the ordered items.
  - **quantity (Page 18, id: 18)**: Enter the quantity of the ordered items.
- **Price**: Enter the price of the ordered items.
  - **price (Page 19, id: 19)**: Enter the price of the ordered items.
- **Total Cost**: Select the total cost.
  - **total_cost (Page 20, id: 20)**: Select the total cost.
- **Payment Method**: Select the payment method.
  - **payment_method (Page 21, id: 21)**: Select the payment method.
- **Payment Status**: Select the payment status.
  - **payment_status (Page 22, id: 22)**: Select the payment status.
- **Order Status**: Select the order status.
  - **order_status (Page 23, id: 23)**: Select the order status.
- **Order Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes.
  - **order_notes (Page 24, id: 24)**: Enter any additional comments or notes.
- **Assigned Tool**: Enter any assigned tool information.
  - **assigned_tool (Page 25, id: 25)**: Enter any assigned tool information.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check the shipping address and contact information.
* Select the correct payment method and status.
* Review the total cost and make sure it matches the total price of the items ordered.
