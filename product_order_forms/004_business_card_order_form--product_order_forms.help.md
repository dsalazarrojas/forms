# Business Card Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Card Order Form is designed to collect necessary information for producing and shipping custom business cards.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the type of account (new or existing) and submit.
2.  Enter the company name, contact person's name, email, phone number, and select "Submit".
3.  Enter the quantity of cards, choose a card size, select a paper stock, finish type, and enter card information for printing.
4.  Enter shipping address, city, and select shipping speed and payment method.
5.  Select the logo or brand files provided and submit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Account Type**: Select whether this is a new or existing account.
    *   New Account: This is a new account for your business.
    *   Existing Customer: You already have an account with us.
*   **Company Name**: Enter your business' legal name.
*   **Contact Person Name**: Enter the full name of the contact person.
*   **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
*   **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact person's phone number.
*   **Company Website**: Enter your business website URL if you have one.
*   **Logo or Brand Files Provided**: Select whether you have logo or brand files ready to be used.
    *   Yes, Files Attached: You have the files ready.
    *   Yes, Will Provide Later: You will provide the files later.
    *   No, Need Design Help: You will need help with designing the logo or brand files.
*   **Quantity of Cards**: Enter the quantity of cards you want to order.
*   **Card Size**: Select the standard or custom size of the card.
    *   Standard 3.5 x 2 inches: This is our standard size.
    *   Standard 85 x 55mm: This is our standard international size.
    *   Custom Size: You want a custom size.
*   **Paper Stock**: Select the paper quality of the card.
    *   Standard 14pt: Our standard paper quality.
    *   Premium 16pt: Our premium paper quality.
    *   Luxury 18pt: Our luxury paper quality.
*   **Finish Type**: Select the finish type of the card.
    *   Matte: A non-reflective finish.
    *   Glossy: A shiny, reflective finish.
    *   Linen: A textured, fabric-like finish.
    *   Silk: A smooth, glossy finish.
*   **Card Information for Printing**: Enter any additional information that needs to be printed on the card.
*   **Shipping Address**: Enter the full delivery address.
*   **City**: Enter the city name.
*   **State and Zip Code**: Enter the state and postal code.
*   **Shipping Speed**: Select the shipping speed.
    *   Standard 7-10 Days: Our standard shipping speed.
    *   Expedited 3-5 Days: Faster shipping speed.
    *   Rush 1-2 Days: Fastest shipping speed.
*   **Payment Method**: Select the payment method.
    *   Credit Card: Pay with your credit card.
    *   Purchase Order: Pay with a purchase order.
    *   Bank Account: Pay with your bank account.
