# Fitness Club Supplement Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you order supplements from our fitness club. It's a simple form that collects your personal details and preferences for your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Fill in your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field. This is where we'll contact you about your order.
4. Choose a date for your order in the "order_date" field.
5. Select your preferred pickup or delivery method in the "pickup_delivery" field.
6. Choose your preferred payment method in the "payment_method" field.
7. If required, fill in the payment date in the "payment_date" field.
8. Add any additional notes or comments about your order in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
*   **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name here.
### Last Name
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name here.
### Email
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address here. This is where we'll contact you about your order.
### Order Date
*   **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, required: false): Choose a date for your order.
### Pickup/Delivery
*   **Pickup/Delivery** (`pickup_delivery`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose whether you'd like to pick up your order or have it delivered.
### Payment Method
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your preferred payment method: cash, credit card, check, or PayPal.
### Payment Date
*   **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, `date`, required: false): If you've chosen a payment method that requires a date (like a check or bank transfer), enter the date of the payment.
### Notes
*   **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about your order here.
