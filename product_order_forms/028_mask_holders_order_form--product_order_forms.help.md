# mask_holders_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture order details for mask holders from customers.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling in the "Order Details" field with a brief description of the order you want to place.
2.  Select the product you want to order from the "Select Product" dropdown menu.
3.  Enter the quantity of the product you want to order in the "Quantity" field.
4.  Enter the price of the order in the "Price" field.
5.  Enter the total amount of the order in the "Total Amount" field.
6.  Fill in your contact details in the "Contact Details" field (including name, email, etc.).
7.  Enter your shipping address in the "Shipping Address" field (where the order will be delivered).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Order Details** (`order_details`, `text`, optional): A brief description of the order you want to place. 
*   **Select Product** (`product_select`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the product you want to order from the provided options.
*   **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of units you want to order.
*   **Price** (`price`, `number`, optional): Enter the price of the order.
*   **Total Amount** (`total_amount`, `number`, optional): Enter the total amount of the order. This should be calculated automatically based on the quantity and price selected.
*   **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact details (name, email, etc.). 
*   **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the address where the order will be delivered.

## Tips

*   Make sure to provide accurate contact and shipping information to ensure timely delivery and smooth communication.
*   Double-check your order details and total amount to avoid errors.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact the support team for assistance.
