# Polish On Point Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect order information from customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the desired product in the "order_form" field.
2. Indicate if the customer is interested in Fall Collections by selecting "Yes" or "No".
3. Provide the customer's details in the "customer_details" field.
4. Enter the customer's address in the "customer_address" field.
5. Enter the order details in the "order_details" field.
6. Select the payment options by selecting "Yes" or "No" for the available options.
7. Enter the customer's phone number in the "customer_phone" field.
8. Enter the customer's email address in the "customer_email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **order_form** (order_form, text, required): Enter the product that the customer is interested in purchasing.
- **fall_collections** (fall_collections, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if the customer is interested in fall collections, otherwise select "No".
- **customer_details** (customer_details, text, required): Enter the customer's name and any other relevant details.
- **customer_address** (customer_address, text, required): Enter the customer's address for delivery purposes.
- **order_details** (order_details, text, required): Enter the order details, such as quantity and special requests.
- **payment_options** (payment_options, select_multiple, required): Select the payment options that apply to the order.
- **customer_phone** (customer_phone, text, required): Enter the customer's phone number for contact purposes.
- **customer_email** (customer_email, email, required): Enter the customer's email address for communication purposes.
