# precision-balancing-weight-order-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for precision-balancing weights orders. It's used to calculate the total quantity of weights, shipping details, and payment methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the weight classes that apply to your order.
2.  Choose the selected weight class from the provided options.
3.  Select the weight unit (Grams, Kilograms, or Pounds) for your order.
4.  Enter the quantity of weights you are ordering.
5.  Select the order date and time.
6.  Enter your contact number.
7.  Enter your customer name (optional).
8.  Enter your customer email (optional).
9.  Enter your customer phone number (optional).
10.  Enter your customer address (optional).
11.  Select the shipping date.
12.  Enter the total quantity of weights.
13.  Select the shipping total (options: $0-$10, $10-$20, $20-$30).
14.  Select the payment method (options: Cash, Bank Transfer, Credit/Debit Card).
15.  Enter any additional comments (optional).
16.  Indicate if the customer is verified (options: True, False).
17.  Select the order status (options: Pending, Shipped, Cancelled).
18.  Select the shipping status (options: Pending, Shipped, Cancelled).
19.  Indicate if the customer signature is required (options: True, False).
20.  If applicable, enter the signature date.
21.  Select the shipping method (options: Standard Shipping, Express Shipping).
22.  Enter any special instructions (optional).
23.  Enter an order note (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Weight Classes** (`weight_classes`): Select the weight classes that apply to your order.
* **Selected Class** (`weight_class_selected`): Choose the selected weight class from the provided options.
* **Weight Unit** (`weight_unit`): Select the weight unit (Grams, Kilograms, or Pounds) for your order.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`): Enter the quantity of weights you are ordering.
* **Order Date/Time** (`order_date_time`): Select the date and time of the order.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): Enter your contact number.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`): Enter your customer name (optional).
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`): Enter your customer email (optional).
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`): Enter your customer phone number (optional).
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`): Enter your customer address (optional).
* **Shipping Date** (`shipping_date`): Select the shipping date.
* **Total Quantity** (`total_quantity`): Enter the total quantity of weights.
* **Shipping Total** (`shipping_total`): Select the shipping total (options: $0-$10, $10-$20, $20-$30).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select the payment method (options: Cash, Bank Transfer, Credit/Debit Card).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Enter any additional comments about the order (optional).
* **Customer Verified** (`customer_verified`): Indicate if the customer is verified (options: True, False).
* **Order Status** (`order_status`): Select the order status (options: Pending, Shipped, Cancelled).
* **Shipping Status** (`shipping_status`): Select the shipping status (options: Pending, Shipped, Cancelled).
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`): Indicate if the customer signature is required (options: True, False).
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`): If applicable, enter the signature date.
* **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`): Select the shipping method (options: Standard Shipping, Express Shipping).
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`): Enter any special instructions about the order (optional).
* **Order Note** (`order_note`): Enter an order note (optional).
