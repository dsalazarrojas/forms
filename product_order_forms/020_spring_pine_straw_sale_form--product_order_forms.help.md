# Spring Pine Straw Sale Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spring Pine Straw Sale Form is used to place an order for spring pine straws. It collects information about the customer's unit number, shipping details, and payment and shipping methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your unit number in the "Unit Number" field.
2.  Select the straw type from the list in the "Straw Type" field.
3.  Enter the quantity of straws you would like to order in the "Quantity" field.
4.  Enter your shipping information, including address, city, state, zip, and country in the "Ship To Address 1", "Ship To City", "Ship To State", "Ship To Zip", and "Ship To Country" fields.
5.  Select the payment method (yes/no) and shipping method (yes/no) in the "Payment Method" and "Ship Method" fields.
6.  If applicable, enter the unit leader's details in the "Unit Leader" field.
7.  Enter the contact name, phone number, and email address in the "Contact Name", "Phone Number", and "Email" fields.
8.  Enter the BSA number 1 and BSA number 2 details in the "BSA 1" and "BSA 2" fields.
9.  Select the BSA number 2 type from the list in the "BSA Number Type" field.
10. Enter the submit button to submit your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Unit Number** (1, number, required): Enter the unit number where you would like to receive your straws.
*   **Straw Type** (2, select_one, required): Select the type of straw you would like to order.
*   **Quantity** (3, number, required): Enter the quantity of straws you would like to order.
*   **Ship To Address 1** (4, text, required): Enter the shipping address.
*   **Ship To City** (5, text, required): Enter the city to ship to.
*   **Ship To State** (6, text, required): Enter the state to ship to.
*   **Ship To Zip** (7, text, required): Enter the zip code to ship to.
*   **Ship To Country** (8, text, required): Enter the country to ship to.
*   **BSA Number** (9, number, required): Enter the BSA number.
*   **Straw Price** (10, number, required): Enter the price of the straws.
*   **Payment Method** (11, select_one, required): Select the payment method (yes/no).
*   **Ship Method** (12, select_one, required): Select the shipping method (yes/no).
*   **Ship Comments** (13, note, optional): Enter any additional comments about shipping.
*   **Unit Type** (14, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of unit (yes/no).
*   **Unit Number** (15, number, required): Enter the unit number again.
*   **Ship To Town** (16, text, required): Enter the town to ship to.
*   **Unit Leader** (17, text, optional): Enter the unit leader's name.
*   **Contact Name** (18, text, required): Enter the contact name.
*   **Phone Number** (19, text, required): Enter the phone number.
*   **Email** (20, email, required): Enter the email address.
*   **BSA Number 2** (21, text, required): Enter the BSA number 2.
*   **BSA Number Type** (22, select_multiple, optional): Select the BSA number 2 type (yes/no).
*   **BSA 1** (23, date, optional): Enter the BSA 1 date.
*   **BSA 2** (24, time, optional): Enter the BSA 2 time.
*   **Submit** (25, text, required): Click the submit button to complete your order.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and complete information in all fields.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact our customer support team.
- Please review all fields carefully before submitting your order.
