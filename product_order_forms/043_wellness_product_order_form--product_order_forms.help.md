# wellness product order form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers to process their orders for wellness products.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the field labeled "First Name".
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the field labeled "Last Name".
3. Enter your **Email Address** in the field labeled "Email".
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the field labeled "Phone".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your valid email address to receive updates on your order.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number to contact you with any updates on your order.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter a valid email address to receive any updates on your order.
* You can enter a phone number to contact you with any updates on your order.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact our customer support team.
