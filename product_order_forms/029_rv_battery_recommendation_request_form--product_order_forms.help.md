# RV Battery Recommendation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The RV Battery Recommendation Request Form is designed to help users find the right battery for their RV based on their specific needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the relevant options for each field and submit the form. If you need help with a field, see below for detailed explanations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Camping style**
`rv_batteries_camping_style` (`select_one`, required: false)
Select the type of camping or off-grid activity you will be doing with your RV. This helps us understand your power needs and recommend the right battery for your RV.

### **Power goals**
`power_goals_batteries` (`select_multiple`, required: false)
Choose the level of power you need for your RV. You can select multiple options to indicate your power needs.

### **Battery warranty**
`battery_warranty` (`select_one`, required: false)
Select the length of battery warranty you are looking for. This will help us understand your expectations for the battery's lifespan.

### **Preferred location of installation**
`preferred_location` (`select_multiple`, required: false)
Indicate where you would like the battery to be installed. This could be at a dealer, remotely, at home, or another location of your choice.

### **Preferred installation method**
`preferred_installation_method` (`select_multiple`, required: false)
Choose how you would like the battery installed. Options include professional installation, DIY, or both.

### **Battery size**
`battery_size` (`select_one`, required: false)
Select the size of battery you are looking for.

### **Battery type**
`battery_type` (`select_multiple`, required: false)
Choose the type of battery you are interested in. This will help us recommend the right battery for your RV.

### **Battery brand**
`battery_brand` (`select_multiple`, required: false)
Select the brand of battery you are interested in.

### **Special features**
`special_features` (`select_multiple`, required: false)
Check any special features you are looking for in a battery, such as cold cranking, deep cycle, or off-grid capabilities.

### **Would you like to be contacted for follow-up?**
`follow_up` (`select_one`, required: false)
Indicate if you would like to be contacted for follow-up or not.

### **Phone number**
`contact_number` (`text`, required: false)
If you would like to be contacted, please enter your phone number for follow-up communication.

### **Email**
`email` (`email`, required: false)
If you would like to be contacted, please enter your email address for follow-up communication.

## Tips
* Please select all applicable options for each field to give us a better understanding of your needs.
* If you are unsure about any field, please leave it blank and our team will contact you for further clarification.
* This form is designed to help us provide you with the best battery recommendation for your RV.
