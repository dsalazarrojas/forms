# Product Purchase Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect the necessary information from customers to process and fulfill product purchase orders. It's meant to be used by customers who want to buy a product from your business.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Make sure to select the correct payment method.
3. Provide any additional order notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, `text`, Optional): This field is where you can enter your name and contact information.
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, `text`, Optional): Enter your shipping address and any other relevant details.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, `text`, Optional): Describe the product you want to order, including any special instructions.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, Optional): Choose your preferred payment method from the options: Visa, Master Card, American Express, Discover, or PayPal.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, `note`, Optional): Add any additional comments or instructions for the order.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the required information.
- Double-check your payment method and order details to ensure accuracy.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
