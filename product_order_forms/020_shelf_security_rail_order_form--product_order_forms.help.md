# shelf_security_rail_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The shelf_security_rail_order_form is used to collect information from customers for their product orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the shipping details fields with information about the shipping method and destination.
2. Select the shipping company and provide any additional information required.
3. Provide comments or notes about the order, if necessary.
4. Enter the product details, including whether the product has any special instructions or notes.
5. Enter the customer contact information, including whether the customer has a physical address or prefers contact through email or phone.
6. Provide any additional customer order details, such as order quantity or total cost.
7. Select whether the customer has a physical address or prefers contact through email or phone.
8. Enter the date the order was placed.
9. Enter any additional customer contact information, such as email or phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **shipping_details**: Enter the shipping method and destination for the order. This field is required.
* **shipping_company**: Enter the name of the shipping company used for this order. This field is optional.
* **ship_from**: Enter the ship-from date and time for this order. This field is optional.
* **order_comments**: Enter any comments or notes about the order, such as special instructions or requests. This field is optional.
* **product_details**: Enter any additional details about the product being ordered, such as product number or instructions. This field is optional.
* **contact_info**: Enter the contact information for the customer, including their address, phone number, or email. This field is optional.
* **customer_order_details**: Enter any additional details about the customer's order, such as order quantity or total cost. This field is optional.
* **product_info**: Select whether the product has any special instructions or notes. This field is optional.
* **order_total**: Enter the total cost of the order. This field is optional.
* **customer_address**: Select whether the customer has a physical address, email, or phone number. This field is optional.
* **order_date**: Enter the date the order was placed. This field is optional.
* **customer_contact**: Enter any additional customer contact information, such as email or phone number. This field is optional.
