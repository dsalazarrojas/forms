# Nailstrip Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nailstrip Order Form is used to process orders for nail products. Please use this form to provide accurate and complete information to ensure timely and correct order fulfillment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Double-check the information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your order.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you regarding your order.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to receive the product.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to receive the product.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to start using the product.
* **Product ID** (`product_id`, text, optional): Enter the product ID number to associate with your order.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the number of products you would like to order.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the product you are ordering.
 

## Tips
* Please double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [support email/phone number].
* If you need to make changes to your order, please let us know as soon as possible so we can make adjustments accordingly.
