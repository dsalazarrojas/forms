# Handbag Product Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Handbag Product Registration Form is designed to collect detailed information about a handbag product, including ownership and purchase details, contact information, and shipping information. This form helps ensure accurate and comprehensive data is recorded for product registration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the ownership details of the handbag product, including relevant information about the product's identity and status.
2. Provide purchase information, such as the date and location of purchase.
3. Enter your contact details, including your name, email, and phone number.
4. Enter shipping information, including the name and address of the shipper.
5. Optional: If you have relevant product information, such as a description or image, you can upload it as a file.
6. If you have another file to attach, you can upload it as well.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Ownership Details** (`Ownership Details`, `text`, required): Please enter the relevant information about the handbag product's ownership, such as its identity and status.
* **Purchase Information** (`Purchase Information`, `text`, required): Enter the date and location of purchase.
* **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, `text`, required): Enter your name, email, and phone number.
* **Shipping Information** (`Shipping Information`, `text`, required): Enter the name and address of the shipper.
* **Product Information** (`Product Information`, `text`, optional): If you have relevant information about the product, such as a description or image, you can upload it as a file.
* **File 1** (`File`, `text`, optional): If you have another file to attach, you can upload it here.
* **File 2** (`File`, `text`, optional): You can upload another file if needed.
