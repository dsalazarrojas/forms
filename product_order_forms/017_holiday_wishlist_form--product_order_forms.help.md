# Holiday Wishlist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holiday Wishlist Form is used to collect information about your holiday wishes and preferences for a specific product. This information will help the company understand your needs and provide a better experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, please follow these steps:
2. Carefully review each field and enter the requested information.
3. Make sure to answer all required fields (indicated by a red star).
4. Submit the form when you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Gift Requests (Gift Requests)
* **Gift Requests** (`gift_requests`, text, optional): This field allows you to write a message about what you would like as a gift. Please use this area to express your wishes for the holiday season.

### Product Wishlist (Product Wishlist)
* **Product Wishlist** (`product_wishlist`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the products you are interested in. This field is a list of possible options, and you can select multiple items.

### Friend Requests (Friend Requests)
* **Friend Requests** (`friend_requests`, text, optional): This field is for you to write a message about any friends or family members you would like to request gifts for.

### Customer Requests (Customer Requests)
* **Customer Requests** (`customer_requests`, number, optional): This field is likely for you to input some kind of numerical value, such as a specific quantity or amount. Please enter a valid number.

### Shipping Details (Shipping Details)
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, select_one, optional): This field asks if you want to ship the gift. Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your preference.

### Special Instructions (Special Instructions)
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, optional): In this text area, you can provide any special instructions or additional information about your gift requests.

### Contact Details (Contact Details)
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide your contact details, such as name, email, and phone number.

### Email Details (Email Details)
* **Email Details** (`email_details`, email, optional): In this field, please enter your email address to facilitate communication.

### Wishlist Category (Wishlist Category)
* **Wishlist Category** (`wishlist_category`, select_one, optional): This field asks you if you want to specify a category for your wishes. Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your preference.

### Submit (Submit)
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): This is the final step to submit your form. Please ensure you have filled out all required fields before clicking "Submit".

### Product ID (Product ID)
* **Product ID** (`product_id`, text, optional): This field is for you to input a Product ID, if required.

### Price (Price)
* **Price** (`price`, number, optional): This field is likely for you to input a price, if required. Please enter a valid numerical value.
