# Collar Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers order custom-made collars with various options such as collar type, fabric, and shipping information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Customer Full Name** in the **Customer Full Name** field.
2. Enter your **Email Address** in the **Email Address** field. This is your contact email.
3. Enter your **Phone Number** in the **Phone Number** field.
4. Enter your **Shipping Address** in the **Shipping Address** field.
5. Enter your **City** in the **City** field.
6. Enter your **State or Province** in the **State or Province** field.
7. Enter your **ZIP or Postal Code** in the **ZIP or Postal Code** field.
8. Select your **Country** from the dropdown menu.
9. Select your **Collar Type** from the dropdown menu.
10. Enter your **Collar Size** in inches or cm in the **Collar Size** field.
11. Select your **Shirt Size** from the dropdown menu.
12. Select your **Sleeve Length** from the dropdown menu.
13. Select your **Fabric Type** from the dropdown menu.
14. Select your **Fabric Pattern** from the dropdown menu.
15. Enter your **Color** preference in the **Color** field.
16. Enter the **Quantity** of collars you would like to order.
17. If you have a **Monogram or Embroidery**, enter it in the **Monogram or Embroidery** field.
18. If you have any **Special Instructions**, enter them in the **Special Instructions** field.
19. Select **Yes Please Wrap** if you would like the order to be gift wrapped.
20. If you would like to include a **Gift Message**, enter it in the **Gift Message** field.
21. Enter your **Required Delivery Date** in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
22. Select your **Shipping Method** from the dropdown menu.
23. Select your **Payment Method** from the dropdown menu.
24. If you have a **Promo Code**, enter it in the **Promo Code** field.
25. Select **Yes I Accept** to agree to the **Terms and Conditions**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Full Name**: Enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your contact email.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter your complete street address.
* **City**: Enter your city name.
* **State or Province**: Enter your state or province name.
* **ZIP or Postal Code**: Enter your postal code.
* **Country**: Select your country of residence.
* **Collar Type**: Select the style of collar you would like to order.
* **Collar Size**: Enter your neck size in inches or cm.
* **Shirt Size**: Select your overall shirt size.
* **Sleeve Length**: Select your desired sleeve length.
* **Fabric Type**: Select your preferred material for the collar.
* **Fabric Pattern**: Select your preferred pattern for the fabric.
* **Color**: Enter your preferred color.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of collars you would like to order.
* **Monogram or Embroidery**: If desired, enter your monogram or embroidery details.
* **Special Instructions**: If you have any custom requests, enter them here.
* **Gift Wrapping**: Select if you would like the order to be gift wrapped.
* **Gift Message**: If you would like to include a message, enter it here.
* **Required Delivery Date**: Enter the date you need the order to be delivered.
* **Shipping Method**: Select your desired shipping speed.
* **Payment Method**: Select how you would like to pay for the order.
* **Promo Code**: If you have a promo code, enter it here.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Select **Yes I Accept** to agree to the terms.
* **Card Number**, **Card Expiry**, **Card CVV**, **Cardholder Name**, **Thread Color for Monogram**: These fields are only required if you select **Credit Card** as your payment method.
* **Special Instructions**: Enter any custom requests or details for your order.
* **Monogram Position**: Select where you would like the monogram to be placed.
* **Thread Color for Monogram**: Select your desired thread color for the monogram.
* **Shipping Method**: Select your desired shipping speed.
* **Promo Code**: Enter any promo code you have.
* **Cardholder Name**: Enter the name to be displayed on the card.
* **Terms Accepted**: Select **Yes I Accept** to agree to the terms.
* **Gift Message**: Enter the message to be included with the gift.
* **Required Delivery Date**: Enter the date you need the order delivered.
* **Delivery Method**: Select your desired shipping speed.
