# Custom Basket Creation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect detailed information from customers for custom basket creation. This form is designed to help businesses create a custom basket for each customer based on their preferences and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each field carefully to ensure you provide accurate and complete information.
- Enter your name and contact details in the Contact Information Form.
- Select your preferred products and quantities in the Product Selection Form.
- Provide any special instructions or notes for your order in the Order Notes Form.
- Select your preferred shipping and payment methods in the Shipping Method Form and Payment Method Form.
- Review and confirm your order details in the Order Review Form.
- Submit the form to complete your order.
