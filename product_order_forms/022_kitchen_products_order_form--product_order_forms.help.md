# Kitchen Products Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Kitchen Products Order Form" is designed to collect essential information about products ordered in the kitchen. This form helps the kitchen staff to efficiently manage orders, track product details, and provide accurate reports for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the product's **Product Name** (Field 1).
2. Select the **Product Quantity** (Field 2) ordered for each product.
3. Enter the **Company** name associated with the order (Field 3).
4. Provide the **Client's** name and contact information in the **Client** field (Field 4).
5. Choose the correct **Product Type** from the list (Field 5).
6. Enter the **Product Price** for each product (Field 6).
7. Select the **Ordered On** date for the product (Field 7).
8. Add any additional **Order Notes** (Field 8).
9. Enter the customer's **Email** (Field 9) and **Phone Number** (Field 10).
10. Record the **Order Total** amount (Field 11).
11. Choose the payment method(s) used for the order (Field 12).
12. Select the **Payment Date** for the order (Field 13).
13. Enter the **Delivery Address** for the order (Field 14).
14. Set the **Delivery Status** (Field 15) to "Pending," "Completed," or "Canceled" as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Name (Field 1)**: Enter the name of the product ordered.
* **Product Quantity (Field 2)**: Type the number of products ordered.
* **Company (Field 3)**: Enter the company associated with the order.
* **Client (Field 4)**: Enter the client's name and contact information.
* **Product Type (Field 5)**: Select the correct product type from the list.
* **Product Price (Field 6)**: Enter the price of each product.
* **Ordered On (Field 7)**: Select the date the order was placed.
* **Order Notes (Field 8)**: Add any additional comments or notes about the order.
* **Email (Field 9)**: Enter the client's email address.
* **Phone Number (Field 10)**: Enter the client's phone number.
* **Order Total (Field 11)**: Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Payment Method (Field 12)**: Select the payment method used for the order (e.g., Credit Card, PayPal, Cash).
* **Payment Date (Field 13)**: Select the date of payment for the order.
* **Delivery Address (Field 14)**: Enter the delivery address for the order.
* **Delivery Status (Field 15)**: Set the delivery status to "Pending," "Completed," or "Canceled."

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out each field accurately to ensure correct data entry.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult with your supervisor or team lead.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your kitchen staff.
