# paypal_business_product_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PayPal Business Product Order Form is designed for businesses to gather customer contact information, shipping details, and product specifics to facilitate seamless product ordering through PayPal or other accepted payment methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact details in the "contact_details" field.
2. Provide shipping details in the "shipping_details" field.
3. Specify the product you wish to purchase in the "product" field.
4. Choose your preferred payment method from the "payment_method" field, which is a dropdown menu with options for PayPal, Credit Card, and Bank Transfer.
5. Enter the product name in the "product_name" field.
6. If applicable, enter the product price in the "product_price" field.
7. If needed, indicate the quantity of the product being ordered in the "quantity" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Details**: This is where you enter your name, email, or phone number, so we can get back to you about your order. (Required: no)
- **Shipping Details**: Enter your address and any other relevant shipping information for our delivery process. (Required: no)
- **Product**: Briefly describe the product you are interested in purchasing. (Required: no)
- **Payment Method**: Choose how you want to pay for your order: PayPal, Credit Card, or Bank Transfer. (Required: yes)
- **Product Name**: Enter the exact name of the product you are ordering. This helps us identify what you are looking for. (Required: no)
- **Quantity**: If you're ordering more than one product, enter the number here. (Required: no)
- **Product Price**: If you know the price of the product, enter it here to save time during checkout. (Required: no)

## Tips
- Always ensure you are using the correct payment method to avoid issues during checkout.
- Double-check your contact and shipping details for accuracy.
- Enter product-specific information to help us process your order efficiently.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us directly after completing this form.
- This form is designed for business-to-business sales, so please ensure you have the necessary authority to make purchases on behalf of your company.
