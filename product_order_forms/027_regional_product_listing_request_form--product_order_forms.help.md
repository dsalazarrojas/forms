# regional_product_listing_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The regional_product_listing_request_form is used to request a new product to be listed in our regional product catalog. This form is used for both internal and external stakeholders to submit product information for review and potential inclusion in our product catalog. Please ensure you are using this form for its intended purpose and not duplicating efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

* Review the form fields carefully to ensure you have the necessary information.
* Fill in the required fields (customer_name, product_name, price) as this information is necessary for processing your request.
* Fill in the optional fields (description) with any additional details you feel are relevant to the product.
* Submit the form when you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Product Request**: Enter a brief description of the product you are requesting to be listed.
* **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the customer submitting the request. This will help our team track the origin of the request and follow up with the customer.
* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product being requested.
* **Description**: Enter a brief description of the product, including any relevant details that will help our team understand the product's features and value.
* **Price**: Enter the price of the product. This will help our team understand the value of the product and ensure it meets our catalog's pricing guidelines.

## Tips
* Please ensure the product information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any additional information or attachments to support your request, please attach them separately and provide a link to them in this form.
* Our team will review your request and follow up with you regarding the status of your request.
