# Personalized Egg Basket Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information for a personalized egg basket order. Customers will provide details such as egg type, quantity, color, egg size, and shipping information to fulfill their order.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields: egg type, quantity, egg size, basket type, shipping method, payment method, delivery date, and order ID.
2. Choose the desired egg type from the options provided.
3. Select the desired shipping method.
4. Choose the desired payment method.
5. Enter the delivery date.
6. Enter the order ID.
7. Enter the customer's email address.
8. Enter the customer's phone number.
9. Review and submit the form to place your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **egg type** (Egg type, select_one, required): Select the type of egg you would like to order. Choose from Eggs, Egg whites, Egg whites with yolk, Egg yolk, or Eggs with yolk.
* **quantity** (Quantity, number, required): Enter the number of eggs you would like to order.
* **color** (Color, select_multiple, optional): Select the desired color for your egg basket from Brown, White, Gray, Black, Green, Blue, Red, or Yellow.
* **egg size** (Egg size, select_one, required): Choose the desired egg size from Small, Medium, Large, or Extra Large.
* **basket type** (Basket type, select_one, required): Select the type of basket you would like to use for your order from Plastic, Wood, Paper, or Bamboo.
* **shipping address** (Shipping address, select_multiple, required): Enter the customer's shipping address.
* **shipping method** (Shipping method, select_one, required): Choose the shipping method from Shipping via USPS, Shipping via FedEx, or Shipping via UPS.
* **payment method** (Payment method, select_one, required): Select the payment method from Credit Card, PayPal, or Cash.
* **delivery date** (Delivery date, date, required): Enter the date you would like to receive your order.
* **delivery time** (Delivery time, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to receive your order.
* **special requests** (Special requests, text, optional): Enter any special requests you may have.
* **customer info** (Customer info, text, optional): Enter any customer information that is relevant to your order.
* **special requests 2** (Special requests 2, text, optional): Enter any additional special requests you may have.
* **notes** (Notes, text, optional): Enter any notes that are relevant to your order.
* **order date** (Order date, date, required): Enter the date of your order.
* **order time** (Order time, time, optional): Enter the time of your order.
* **delivery time 2** (Delivery time 2, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to receive your order.
* **notes 2** (Notes 2, text, optional): Enter any additional notes that are relevant to your order.
* **order status** (Order status, select_one, required): Choose the status of your order from In Progress, Completed, Cancelled, or Pending.
* **payment status** (Payment status, select_one, required): Choose the status of your payment from Pending, Paid, or Unpaid.
* **payment method 2** (Payment method 2, select_one, required): Select the method of payment from Cash, Credit Card, or PayPal.
* **notes 3** (Notes 3, text, optional): Enter any additional notes that are relevant to your order.
* **order id** (Order ID, number, required): Enter the unique ID of your order.
* **customer email** (Customer email, email, required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **customer phone** (Customer phone, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.

Note: Please review each field carefully and enter the correct information to ensure accurate order processing.
