# Beverage Truck Branding Wrap Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information for a custom branding wrap order. It is designed to guide you through the process of ordering a custom wrap for your beverage truck or other vehicle.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the client information section with your company name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the type of vehicle that needs branding.
3. Provide information about the vehicle's color and dimensions.
4. Choose the coverage area for the wrap.
5. Describe your design concept and theme.
6. Specify the brand elements to include in the design.
7. Choose the primary color scheme for the wrap.
8. If you have existing artwork or need a new design created, select the corresponding option.
9. If you have a designer or source for the design, provide their information.
10. If you have specific material requirements, select the preferred wrap material.
11. If you need a special finish or effect, specify it.
12. Choose the installation location and preferred installation date.
13. Select the desired project timeline.
14. Determine whether you need professional installation or will install it yourself.
15. Indicate your total budget for the project.
16. Choose a preferred payment method.
17. If you have additional requirements, such as special effects or finishes, specify them.
18. Finally, add any additional notes or special requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Enter your company or business name. This will be used to identify you and your brand.
* **Company or Brand Name**: Enter your company or brand name as it appears on your logo or marketing materials.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted for updates and communications.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for urgent matters.
* **Vehicle Type**: Select the type of vehicle that needs branding: Beverage truck, Food truck, Delivery van, Car or sedan, Box truck, Trailer, or Other.
* **Vehicle Year and Make**: Enter the year, brand, and model of your vehicle.
* **Current Vehicle Color**: Enter the current color of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Approximate Dimensions**: Enter the approximate length and width of your vehicle in feet.
* **Wrap Coverage Desired**: Select how much of the vehicle you want covered: Full wrap - all sides, Partial wrap - sides and back, Box wrap - cargo area only, Door wraps only, Hood and doors, Custom coverage.
* **Design Concept or Theme**: Describe your design concept or theme in a few words.
* **Brand Elements to Include**: Specify the brand elements to include in the design, such as your logo, colors, taglines, etc.
* **Primary Color Scheme**: Enter the main colors you want to feature in the wrap design.
* **Do You Have Artwork or Design**: Select whether you have existing artwork or design, or need a new design created.
* **Design Source or Designer**: If you have a designer or source for the design, enter their information.
* **Number of Design Revisions Included**: If you expect revisions to the design, specify the number of rounds you are willing to accept.
* **Preferred Wrap Material**: Select the type of material you prefer for the wrap: Standard vinyl wrap, Premium cast vinyl, Specialty finish (matte, gloss), Perforated vinyl, Clear protective layer preferred, No preference.
* **Expected Wrap Durability**: Select how long you expect the wrap to last: 3-5 years, 5-7 years, 7+ years, No specific requirement.
* **Special Weather Resistance Needed**: If you need extreme sun, salt, or other weather resistance, specify it.
* **Installation Location**: Enter the location where the wrap will be installed.
* **Preferred Installation Date**: Enter the preferred date for installation.
* **Project Timeline**: Select the desired project timeline: Urgent - within 1 week, Standard - 2-4 weeks, No specific deadline, Need for specific event.
* **Installation Service Needed**: Select whether you need professional installation: Yes, professional installation required, Yes, but we can coordinate, No, we will install ourselves, No, vehicle to be picked up.
* **Budget Range**: Select your total budget for the project: Under $1,000, $1,000 - $2,500, $2,500 - $5,000, $5,000 - $10,000, Over $10,000, Flexible.
* **Preferred Payment Method**: Select how you prefer to pay: Credit card, Check, Wire transfer, Installment plan, Other.
* **Special Effects or Finishes**: If you need any special effects or finishes, specify it.
* **Additional Notes or Special Requests**: Enter any additional notes or special requests you have for the project.

Note: Please review each field carefully and provide the most accurate information possible to ensure the success of your project.
