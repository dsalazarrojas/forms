# Action Figure Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to pre-order action figures, ensuring they receive their preferred variant and quantity before they are released or become available in stores.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your customer information, including your full name, contact email, and shipping address.
2. Enter your contact phone number (optional).
3. Select the variant you want to pre-order (Standard, Deluxe, or Limited).
4. Enter the quantity you wish to pre-order.
5. Choose the payment method you prefer (Credit card, PayPal, or Bank transfer).
6. Confirm that you accept the pre-order terms and refund policy.
7. If applicable, enter a promotional code.
8. Add any additional instructions or notes about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer name**: Your full name for order confirmation.
* **Contact email**: Your email for order and shipping updates.
* **Shipping address**: Your full shipping address including postal code.
* **Phone number**: Optional phone number for delivery issues.
* **Figure variant**: Select the variant you want to pre-order (Standard, Deluxe, or Limited).
* **Quantity**: The number of units you want to pre-order.
* **Expected release date**: Estimated release or shipping date.
* **Payment method**: Choose how you will pay for your pre-order (Credit card, PayPal, or Bank transfer).
* **Pre order terms acceptance**: Confirm that you accept the pre-order terms and refund policy.
* **Promotional code**: Enter any applicable promotional code.
* **Additional instructions**: Add any shipping or order notes.

## Tips
* Be aware that pre-orders are subject to availability and release dates.
* Keep an eye on this form for updates on new releases and variants.
* Pre-orders are non-refundable and subject to change, so be prepared for potential changes.
