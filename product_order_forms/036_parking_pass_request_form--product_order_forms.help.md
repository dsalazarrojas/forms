# parking_pass_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The parking pass request form is used to collect information from customers for parking pass requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your customer information in the **Customer Info** field.
2.  Enter your **Contact Number**.
3.  Enter your **Contact Name**.
4.  Select the desired **Parking Pass** options.
5.  Enter your **Email** address.
6.  Enter your **Phone** number.
7.  Provide the **Date** and **Time** for the parking pass request.
8.  Enter a **Description** of the request.
9.  Confirm your **Parking Info** and **Address**.
10. Select the type of **Vehicle** you will be using.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info**: Enter your name, address, and other relevant customer details.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number, which will be used for follow-up communication.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name, as this will help us confirm your identity.
* **Parking Pass**: Select the parking pass option that best suits your needs.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for us to contact you about your parking pass request.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you about your parking pass request.
* **Date**: Enter the date you will be using the parking pass.
* **Time**: Enter the time you will be using the parking pass.
* **Description**: Enter a short description of your parking pass request.
* **Parking Info**: Confirm the parking details you selected earlier.
* **Address**: Enter your address for parking pass confirmation.
* **Vehicle Info**: Enter your vehicle information, such as make and model.
* **Vehicle Type**: Select the type of vehicle you will be using.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for all fields.
* Review your form carefully before submitting it to ensure all fields are filled out correctly.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact us at [support email or phone number].
