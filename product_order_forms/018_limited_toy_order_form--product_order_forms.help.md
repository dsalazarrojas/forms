# limited_toy_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The limited_toy_order_form is designed to collect information for a small-scale toy purchase or order. It helps streamline the ordering process for limited toy orders.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the limited_toy_order_form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name, email address, phone number, address, city, state, and zip code for contact and shipping purposes.
2. Enter the quantity of the toy ordered.
3. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the option related to the toy order.
4. Enter any additional comments or instructions for the order.
5. Enter any additional notes or comments for the order.
6. Confirm your email address and phone number for verification purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Header**: Enter any additional text for the form header.
- **Name**: Enter your name for the order.
- **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Address**: Enter your address for shipping and delivery purposes.
- **City**: Enter your city for shipping and delivery purposes.
- **State**: Enter your state for shipping and delivery purposes.
- **Zip**: Enter your zip code for shipping and delivery purposes.
- **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the toy ordered.
- **Option 3**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' for a specific option related to the toy order.
- **Submit**: Enter any additional comments or instructions for the order.
- **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments for the order.
- **Confirm Email**: Confirm your email address for verification purposes.
- **Confirm Phone**: Confirm your phone number for verification purposes.
- **Confirm Phone Number**: Confirm your phone number for verification purposes.
- **Confirm Phone Extension**: Confirm your phone extension for verification purposes.
- **Confirm Phone Context**: Confirm the context of your phone number for verification purposes.
- **Confirm Phone Type**: Confirm the type of your phone number for verification purposes.
- **Confirm Phone Prefix**: Confirm the prefix of your phone number for verification purposes.
- **Confirm Phone Suffix**: Confirm the suffix of your phone number for verification purposes.
- **Confirm Phone Country Code**: Confirm the country code of your phone number for verification purposes.
- **Confirm Phone Area Code**: Confirm the area code of your phone number for verification purposes.
- **Confirm Phone Exchange**: Confirm the exchange of your phone number for verification purposes.
- **Confirm Phone Line Number**: Confirm the line number of your phone number for verification purposes.
- **Confirm Phone Special**: Confirm any special characters of your phone number for verification purposes.

Note: The confirmation fields for phone number may seem redundant, but are included to ensure accurate phone number information.
