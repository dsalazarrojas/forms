# Smart Shelf Refill Device Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Shelf Refill Device Order Form is used to place an order for a Smart Shelf device refill. To complete the form, follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields. The Product ID is a unique identifier for the product you are ordering and is required to process the order.
2. Provide the customer's name as it appears on their order records.
3. Enter the customer's email address if you have it.
4. Enter the customer's phone number if you have it.
5. Choose the date you want the order to be processed.
6. Choose the time you want the order to be processed.
7. Enter the customer's address.
8. Provide any additional details about the order.
9. Choose the shipping method.
10. Enter the shipping address, city, and state.
11. Enter the shipping zip code.
12. Add any additional order notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Product ID** (`product_id`, number, required): Enter the unique identifier for the product you are ordering.
- **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the customer, as it appears on their order records.
- **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, text, optional): Enter the email address of the customer, if available.
- **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the customer, if available.
- **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Choose the date you want the order to be processed.
- **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, optional): Choose the time you want the order to be processed.
- **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, optional): Enter the customer's address.
- **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional details about the order.
- **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, text, optional): Choose the shipping method you prefer.
- **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, optional): Enter the shipping address.
- **Shipping City** (`shipping_city`, text, optional): Enter the shipping city.
- **Shipping State** (`shipping_state`, text, optional): Enter the shipping state.
- **Shipping Zip** (`shipping_zip`, text, optional): Enter the shipping zip code.
- **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional order notes.

## Tips
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, check with the relevant records before submitting.
- Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
- Double-check your input for accuracy before submitting.
