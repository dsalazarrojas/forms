# Culinary Knife Sharpening Service Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place an order for the culinary knife sharpening service. It collects information necessary to process the order correctly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk \* or a "required" label in the field description.
- Select the preferred method of payment and drop-off or pick-up option.
- Enter the date and time of the desired order.
- Specify the type of business for which the knives are to be sharpened.
- Enter the number of knives to be sharpened.
- Provide any additional information about the order in the "Sharpening Info" field.
- Enter a valid email address and phone number for communication.
- Read and confirm the order details before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Details (1)**: Enter a brief description of your order to help us process it correctly.
* **Order Info (2)**: Enter any relevant information about your order that may help us process it efficiently.
* **Drop-Off or Pick-Up (3)**: Choose whether you would like to drop off the knives at our location or have us pick up the knives from your location.
* **Order Date (4)**: Select the date on which you would like to have your knives sharpened.
* **Order Time (5)**: Select the time on which you would like to have your knives sharpened.
* **Payment Method (6)**: Choose how you would like to pay for the service, either by credit card or PayPal.
* **Payment Info (7)**: If you selected "Other" as your payment method, enter the details about the payment method you would like to use.
* **Business Type (8)**: Select the type of business for which you are sharpening the knives, either a sharpener, mobile sharpening service, or a kitchen.
* **Knives (9)**: Enter the number of knives that need to be sharpened.
* **Sharpening Info (10)**: Enter any additional information about the sharpening process.
* **Notes (11)**: Enter any notes or instructions you would like to provide about the order.
* **Email (12)**: Enter a valid email address for communication.
* **Phone (13)**: Enter a valid phone number for communication.
* **Confirm (14)**: Review and confirm the order details before submitting the form.
* **Submit (15)**: Once you have reviewed and confirmed the order details, click the "Submit" button to place the order.
