# Sneaker Limited Edition Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers interested in pre-ordering a limited edition sneaker. The form gathers customer details, delivery preferences, payment information, and shipping data to process the pre-order request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Make sure to select the correct delivery date and preferred shipping address.
3. Choose your preferred payment method (Cash, Credit Card, or PayPal).
4. Select your desired shipping options.
5. Review and adjust your order details according to your preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details** (Customer Details): This section is used to collect contact information about the customer, including Email, Phone, and Name.
* **Delivery Preferences** (Delivery Preferences): Here, you can select the delivery date and preferred shipping address for your order.
* **Payment Details** (Payment Details): In this section, you can enter your payment amount and choose between three payment methods: Cash, Credit Card, and PayPal.
* **Shipping** (Shipping): Choose whether you would like to have your order shipped or not.
* **Payment Terms** (Payment Terms): Provide any additional notes or instructions for payment.
* **Currency** (Currency): Select the payment currency from the available options.
* **State** (State): Select the state where the shipping address is located.
* **Zip** (Zip): Enter the zip code for the shipping address.
* **Country** (Country): Choose the country for the shipping address.
* **Name** (Name): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Address** (Address): Enter the shipping address, including the street number and name.
* **Phone** (Phone): Enter your phone number.
* **Payment Amount** (Payment Amount): Enter the amount you would like to pay for the order.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method): Choose the payment method you prefer.
* **Payment Notes** (Payment Notes): Add any additional notes or instructions for payment.
* **City** (City): Enter the city for the shipping address.
* **Zip** (Zip): Enter the zip code for the shipping address.

Note: Make sure to fill out all required fields for successful pre-order processing.
