# homemade_mask_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow you to order custom-made masks. Please complete the following fields to ensure accurate and efficient processing of your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Select the color of the mask you would like to order.
2. Choose the material for your mask.
3. Enter the quantity you need.
4. Select the size of the mask.
5. Provide any special instructions for your order.
6. Enter your email address for communication purposes.
7. Provide your phone number for us to contact you.
8. Add any additional customer notes.
9. Select the shipping date.
10. Enter your form submission email (will be used for confirmation).
11. Enter your form submission phone number (will be used for confirmation).
12. Enter the URL of the form submission (this might not be necessary).
13-25. Please ignore these last fields as they seem to be related to internal server-side functionality and not for user input.
