# Annual Inventory Clearance Sale Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to place an order for one or more items at a discounted price.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name and email address so we can contact you about your order.
2.  Enter your company name if you are placing the order on behalf of a business or organization.
3.  Enter your phone number so we can contact you about delivery coordination.
4.  Enter your billing address for accounting and tax purposes.
5.  Enter your shipping address if it is different from your billing address.
6.  Enter the description of each item you are ordering.
7.  Enter the quantity of each item you are ordering.
8.  Enter the regular price and sale price of each item.
9.  Choose a preferred shipping method.
10.  Choose a preferred delivery date, if desired.
11.  Enter any special instructions or packaging requests.
12.  Select a payment method for your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Full Name**: <em>Enter the name of the person placing the order.</em>
* **Company Name**: <em>Enter the name of the business or organization placing the order, if applicable.</em>
* **Email Address**: <em>Enter the email address to receive order confirmations and updates.</em>
* **Phone Number**: <em>Enter a contact number for delivery coordination.</em>
* **Billing Address**: <em>Enter the street address for billing and accounting purposes.</em>
* **Shipping Address**: <em>Enter the delivery address, if different from the billing address.</em>
* **Item 1 - Product Description**: <em>Enter the description of the first item being ordered.</em>
*   **Item 1 - Quantity**: <em>Enter the number of units of the first item being ordered.</em>
*   **Item 1 - Regular Price per Unit**: <em>Enter the original price of the first item before the discount.</em>
*   **Item 1 - Clearance Sale Price**: <em>Enter the discounted price of the first item per unit.</em>
*   **Item 2 - Product Description**: <em>Enter the description of the second item being ordered, if applicable.</em>
*   **Item 2 - Quantity**: <em>Enter the number of units of the second item being ordered, if applicable.</em>
*   **Item 2 - Clearance Price per Unit**: <em>Enter the discounted price of the second item per unit, if applicable.</em>
*   **Item 3 - Product Description**: <em>Enter the description of the third item being ordered, if applicable.</em>
*   **Item 3 - Quantity**: <em>Enter the number of units of the third item being ordered, if applicable.</em>
*   **Item 3 - Clearance Price per Unit**: <em>Enter the discounted price of the third item per unit, if applicable.</em>
*   **Preferred Shipping Method**: <em>Choose the shipping method that best suits your needs.</em>
*   **Preferred Delivery Date**: <em>Enter the date you would like the items delivered.</em>
*   **Special Instructions or Packaging Requests**: <em>Enter any special instructions or requests for packaging or handling.</em>
*   **Payment Method**: <em>Select the method you will use to pay for the order.</em>
*   **I agree to the terms and conditions**: <em>Check the box to agree to the terms and conditions of the sale.</em>
