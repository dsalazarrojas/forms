# Athletic Training Manual Printing Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Athletic Training Manual Printing Order Form is a critical tool for customers to provide necessary information for printing their athletic manuals. It ensures that all essential details are collected accurately and efficiently, enabling our production team to create and deliver the manuals on time.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and fill in all required fields.
2. Enter your order date in the `Order Date` field.
3. Provide a reference number for tracking in the `Order Number` field (optional).
4. Enter the date by which you would like to receive the manuals in the `Requested Delivery Date` field.
5. Fill in your organization/school name in the `Organization/School Name` field.
6. Enter the name of the authorized contact person in the `Contact Person Name` field.
7. Provide your email address in the `Email Address` field.
8. Enter your phone number in the `Phone Number` field.
9. Enter your shipping address in the `Shipping Address` field.
10. Specify your city in the `City` field.
11. Enter your state in the `State` field.
12. Enter your ZIP code in the `ZIP Code` field.
13. Enter the title of the manual in the `Manual Title` field.
14. Specify the quantity of manuals to print in the `Quantity` field.
15. Enter the approximate page count of the manual in the `Page Count` field.
16. Choose the binding type (e.g., Perfect Binding, Spiral Binding, etc.) in the `Binding Type` field.
17. Select the paper type (e.g., Standard 20lb, Heavy 28lb, etc.) in the `Paper Type` field.
18. Choose if you want color printing (Full Color, Black and White, etc.) or not in the `Color Printing` field.
19. Select the type of cover material (e.g., Same as Pages, Cardstock, etc.) in the `Cover Material` field.
20. Choose any special finishes you would like to apply to the manual (e.g., Lamination, etc.) in the `Special Finishes` field.
21. Choose how you will submit the file (e.g., Email PDF, Online Upload, etc.) in the `How will the file be submitted?` field.
22. If you need expedited service, choose from the available options in the `Rush Delivery` field.
23. Add any additional notes or instructions in the `Additional Notes` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Date**: Enter the date of this printing order to ensure timely production and delivery.
* **Order Number**: Provide a reference number for tracking your order (optional).
* **Requested Delivery Date**: Enter the date by which you would like to receive the manuals.
* **Organization/School Name**: Enter your organization/school name for identification purposes.
* **Contact Person Name**: Enter the name of the authorized contact person.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter your shipping address for delivery purposes.
* **City**: Enter your city for delivery purposes.
* **State**: Enter your state for delivery purposes.
* **ZIP Code**: Enter your ZIP code for delivery purposes.
* **Manual Title**: Enter the title of the manual or topic.
* **Quantity**: Specify the number of manuals to print.
* **Page Count**: Enter the approximate number of pages.
* **Binding Type**: Choose the binding type (e.g., Perfect Binding, Spiral Binding, etc.).
* **Paper Type**: Choose the paper type (e.g., Standard 20lb, Heavy 28lb, etc.).
* **Color Printing**: Choose if you want color printing or not.
* **Cover Material**: Choose the type of cover material (e.g., Same as Pages, Cardstock, etc.).
* **Special Finishes**: Choose any special finishes you would like to apply to the manual.
* **How will the file be submitted?**: Choose how you will submit the file (e.g., Email PDF, Online Upload, etc.).
* **Rush Delivery**: Choose if you need expedited service.
* **Additional Notes**: Add any special instructions or requests.

Note: It is essential to fill in all required fields accurately for proper production and delivery of your athletic training manuals.
