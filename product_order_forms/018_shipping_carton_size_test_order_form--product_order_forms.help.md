# shipping_carton_size_test_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The shipping_carton_size_test_order_form is a test order form used to collect information for determining the size of a carton for shipping. It's designed for testing purposes and should not be used for actual order placement or shipping.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you're filling out this form for testing purposes only.
2. Provide all the required and optional details as prompted.
3. Fill in the test_corton_size field with the test carton size details.
4. Select the delivery preferences that apply to you.
5. Enter the delivery date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
6. Enter the delivery time in the format HH:MM:SS.
7. Fill in your delivery location.
8. Add any relevant test carton notes.
9. Provide your email address.
10. Enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Test Carton Size** (<name>, <type>, required/optional): Fill in the size of the test carton, this will help determine the correct size for shipping.
* **Delivery Preferences** (<name>, <type>, required/optional): Select all the delivery preferences that apply to you. This will help determine the correct delivery options.
* **Delivery Date** (<name>, <type>, required/optional): Enter the date you want to receive the test carton in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Delivery Time** (<name>, <type>, required/optional): Enter the time you want to receive the test carton in the format HH:MM:SS.
* **Delivery Location** (<name>, <type>, required/optional): Fill in the address where you want to receive the test carton.
* **Test Carton Note** (<name>, <type>, required/optional): Add any relevant notes about the test carton.
* **Test Carton Email** (<name>, <type>, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Test Carton Phone** (<name>, <type>, required/optional): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Ensure that you're filling out this form for testing purposes only.
* Double-check your input for accuracy before submitting the form.
* In case of any discrepancies, please contact the relevant team members for assistance.
