# pick_ka_deli_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pick_ka_deli_order_form is used to place an order at a deli.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Order Details page with the relevant information about your order.
2. Enter the Customer Info page with your contact details.
3. Provide the Contact Details, including phone and address.
4. Select the Payment Method and choose the correct payment option.
5. Enter the Order Total, Delivery Date, and Delivery Time, if applicable.
6. Add any additional Notes if required.
7. Provide the Email and Phone number of the customer.
8. Enter the Address, City, and Zip Code of the customer.
9. Select the Payment Method Options and Order Total Currency, if applicable.
10. Enter the Store Info and Store Hours, if required.
11. Finally, review the form to ensure all fields are completed accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required: false): Enter the details of your order, including the products or items you'd like to purchase.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, required: false): Enter the customer's contact details, including their email and phone number.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): Enter the customer's contact address, phone number, and email.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required: false): Select the payment method for your order, choosing between 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, required: false): Enter the total cost of your order.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you'd like to receive your order.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time you'd like to receive your order.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or instructions for the staff.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter the customer's address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required: false): Enter the city of the customer.
* **State** (`state`, text, required: false): Enter the state of the customer.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required: false): Enter the customer's zip code.
* **Order Instructions** (`order_instructions`, text, required: false): Enter any additional instructions for the staff.
* **Order Comments** (`order_comments`, text, required: false): Enter any comments or messages for the customer.
* **Store Info** (`store_info`, text, required: false): Enter the store information, including name and address.
* **Store Hours** (`store_hours`, text, required: false): Enter the store hours of operation.
* **Payment Method Options** (`payment_method_options`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the payment method options, choosing between 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Order Total Currency** (`order_total_currency`, select_one, required: false): Select the currency for the order total, choosing between 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, required: false): Enter the total cost of your order.
* **Store** (`store`, text, required: false): Enter the store name.
* **Manager** (`manager`, text, required: false): Enter the manager's name.
* **Customer** (`customer`, text, required: false): Enter the customer's name.

This guide should help users complete the form accurately and efficiently. If you have any further questions or need clarification, please don't hesitate to ask.
