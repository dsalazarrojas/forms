# Order And Pickup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Order and Pick-up Form is used to place new orders and schedule pick-up for customers. It is intended for internal use and should only be used for new orders, not for editing or updating existing orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:

1. Select a time slot that suits your needs from the "Time Slot" dropdown menu.
2. Choose the products you want to order from the "Products" list.
3. Enter your name, email address, and phone number in the respective fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order and Pick Up Form** (`order_and_pickup_form`): This field is not a field for input but a label for the form itself.
* **Time Slot** (`time_slot`): Select a time slot from the dropdown menu that suits your needs for pick-up. Available options are 8am-12pm, 12pm-4pm, and 4pm-8pm.
* **Products** (`products`): Choose the products you want to order from the list. Multiple product selection is allowed.
* **Pickup Date** (`pickup_date`): This field is currently not in use but will be populated automatically after the form is submitted.
* **Pickup Time** (`pickup_time`): This field is currently not in use but will be populated automatically after the form is submitted.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`): Enter your name as it will be displayed on the order.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter a valid email address to contact you for order updates and pickup confirmation.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you about your order or pick-up.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct time slot that suits your needs.
* Double-check your order details before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact our customer service team.
* This form will be processed and reviewed upon submission, and you will receive a confirmation email and a call or text to confirm your pick-up time and details.
