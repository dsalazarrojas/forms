# Toy Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers when placing an order for toys. Use this form to accurately gather details about the customer and their order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the 'First Name' field.
2. Enter your last name in the 'Last Name' field.
3. Enter your email address in the 'Email' field.
4. Enter your address in the 'Address' field.
5. Select the type of order you are placing in the 'Order Type' field. You can choose from 'Book', 'Soft Toy', or 'Board Game'.
6. If applicable, enter the name of the product you are ordering in the 'Product Name' field.
7. Enter the quantity of the product you are ordering in the 'Product Quantity' field.
8. Select a delivery date for your order in the 'Delivery Date' field.
9. Select a delivery time for your order in the 'Delivery Time' field.
10. Enter the total cost of your order in the 'Total Cost' field.
11. Select a payment method for your order from the 'Payment Method' field. You can choose from 'Credit Card', 'PayPal', or 'Bank Transfer'.
12. If you have any additional notes or comments, enter them in the 'Note' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address.
- **Address** (Text, Required): Enter your address.
- **Order Type** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the type of order you are placing.
- **Product Name** (Text, Optional): If you are ordering a specific product, enter its name here.
- **Product Quantity** (Number, Required): Enter the quantity of the product you are ordering.
- **Delivery Date** (Date, Required): Select a date for delivery.
- **Delivery Time** (Time, Required): Select a time for delivery.
- **Total Cost** (Number, Required): Enter the total cost of your order.
- **Payment Method** (Select One, Required): Select a payment method from 'Credit Card', 'PayPal', or 'Bank Transfer'.
- **Note** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your order.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
- Double-check the 'Order Type' field to ensure you select the correct option.
- If you have any questions or concerns about your order, use the 'Note' field to provide additional information.
- Ensure the 'Total Cost' field is accurate and matches the products and quantities you have ordered.
