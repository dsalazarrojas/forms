# Shop My Color Street Stock! - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer information and product details for orders. Fill out this form to submit your order for the "My Color Street Stock!" product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information. You can submit your order without creating an account, but please note that you will need to provide your email and phone number to process your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your order.
- **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted about your order.
- **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you about your order.
- **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the product you wish to order.
- **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, optional): Briefly describe the product you are ordering.
- **Product Price** (`product_price`, number, optional): Enter the price of the product you are ordering (if applicable).

## Tips
- Please ensure all fields are filled out with accurate and complete information.
- If you have any questions or issues while filling out this form, you can contact us at [insert contact information].
