# Retail Tablet Display Stand Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for ordering a retail tablet display stand. The form helps to collect your customer information and preferences to ensure we provide you with the right stand that suits your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer details on **page 1: Customer Details**.
2. Enter your shipping address on **page 2: Shipping Address**.
3. Choose the size of the stand that you need on **page 3: Stand Selection**.
4. Fill in your shipping city and postcode on **page 4: Shipping City and Postcode**.
5. If you have a preferred contact method, you can enter your email address or phone number on **page 6: Email and Phone**.
6. If you have any additional comments or requests, you can include them on **page 8: Additional Comments**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, `text`, required):
    This is where you enter your customer name and other relevant information.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, `text`, required):
    Please fill in your shipping address, including your address line, city, and postcode.
* **Stand Selection** (`stand_selection`, `select_one`, required):
    Choose the size of the stand you need from the options: Tablet Display Stand 10", Tablet Display Stand 20", or Tablet Display Stand 30".
* **Shipping City** (`shipping_city`, `text`, required):
    Enter your city for shipping purposes.
* **Postcode** (`shipping_postcode`, `text`, required):
    Enter your postcode for shipping purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional):
    If you prefer to be contacted via email, enter your email address here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional):
    If you prefer to be contacted via phone, enter your phone number here.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, optional):
    Any additional comments or requests you may have can be included here.
