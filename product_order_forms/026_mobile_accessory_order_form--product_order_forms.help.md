# Mobile Accessory Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information necessary for ordering mobile accessories. Please ensure you only need to use this form for this specific purpose and do not duplicate efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional)
Order Details is a free-text field where you can provide any additional information related to your order.

### 2. **Select Product** (`product_select`, select_one, optional)
Select the product you are interested in purchasing.

### 3. **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional)
Enter the quantity of the product you are ordering.

### 4. **Unit Price** (`unit_price`, number, optional)
Enter the unit price of the product you are ordering.

### 5. **Payment** (`payment`, select_multiple, optional)
Select the payment option you prefer.

### 6. **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, optional)
Enter your shipping address if different from your contact information.

### 7. **Contact** (`contact`, text, required)
Enter your contact information, including your name and any other relevant contact details.

### 8. **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required)
Enter your email address.

### 9. **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required)
Enter your phone number.

Please note that fields marked "required" are mandatory fields that must be filled out for the form to be submitted correctly.
