# Course Material Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Course Material Order Form is used to place orders for course materials, such as textbooks, equipment, or other learning resources. This form helps customers or staff members quickly and accurately submit requests for course materials, ensuring that the necessary materials are ordered and delivered on time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter the course material you wish to order in the "Course Material" field.
* Select the quantity of materials you need.
* Enter the price of the materials (if applicable).
* Choose the shipping cost (if applicable).
* Provide your contact information, including your email and phone number (if applicable).
* Add any additional order notes.
* Select the payment gateway to be used for the order.
* Choose the order status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Material** (Select Multiple, required: false): Choose the type of course material you wish to order, such as textbooks, equipment, or other learning resources.
* **Quantity** (Number, required: false): Enter the number of materials you need to order.
* **Price** (Number, required: false): Enter the price of the materials (if applicable).
* **Shipping Cost** (Text, required: false): Enter the shipping cost (if applicable).
* **Customer Email** (Email, required: false): Enter the email address of the customer or staff member placing the order.
* **Customer Phone** (Text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the customer or staff member placing the order.
* **Order Notes** (Note, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about the order.
* **Payment Gateway** (Select One, required: false): Select the payment gateway to be used for the order, such as Gateway 1, Gateway 2, or Gateway 3.
* **Order Status** (Select One, required: false): Choose the status of the order, such as Status 1, Status 2, or Status 3.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate contact information to ensure timely delivery and communication about the order.
* Choose the correct payment gateway to ensure secure and reliable payment processing.
* Add any additional order notes to provide context or instructions for the order.
* Select the correct order status to reflect the current state of the order.
