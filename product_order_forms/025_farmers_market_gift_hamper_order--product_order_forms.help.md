# Farmers Market Gift Hamper Order - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to place an order for a customized gift hamper filled with locally-sourced products from a farmers market.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the gift hamper in the **Form Title** field.
2. Fill in the recipient's name in the **Recipient Name** field.
3. Enter the recipient's email address in the **Recipient Email** field.
4. Choose a delivery date for the gift hamper in the **Delivery Date** field.
5. Select the type of hamper you would like to order in the **Hamper Selection** field.
6. Select the options for the contents of the hamper in the **Hamper Selection Option** field.
7. If you would like to include a gift message, enter it in the **Gift Message** field.
8. Enter the delivery time for the gift hamper in the **Delivery Date Time** field.
9. Enter the delivery address in the **Delivery Address** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (Text, Optional): Enter a title for the gift hamper, if desired.
* **Recipient Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the person receiving the gift hamper.
* **Recipient Email** (Email, Required): Enter the email address of the person receiving the gift hamper.
* **Delivery Date** (Date, Required): Choose the date for delivery of the gift hamper.
* **Hamper Selection** (Select One, Required): Select the type of hamper you would like to order.
* **Hamper Selection Option** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the options for the contents of the hamper.
* **Gift Message** (Text, Optional): If you would like to include a gift message, enter it here.
* **Delivery Date Time** (Time, Optional): Choose the delivery time for the gift hamper.
* **Delivery Address** (Text, Required): Enter the address where the gift hamper should be delivered.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter the recipient's email address correctly to ensure proper delivery of the gift hamper.
* Select a delivery date and time that works best for you.
* Choose a hamper type and options that fit the recipient's preferences.
* If you have any special requests or instructions, include them in the **Gift Message** field.
