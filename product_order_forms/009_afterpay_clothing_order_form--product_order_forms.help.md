# Afterpay Clothing Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Afterpay Clothing Order Form is designed to help customers place an order for clothing items using the Afterpay payment option. This form requires the following information: customer name, shipping address, item description or SKU, size, color, quantity, unit price, accessories, payment method, and special shipping instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your information, including your customer name and shipping address.
2. Select the item you wish to order, providing the item description or SKU.
3. Choose the size and color of the item.
4. Enter the quantity of the item you wish to order.
5. Select any optional accessories you would like to add to your order.
6. Choose your payment method (Afterpay, Credit Card, or PayPal).
7. If necessary, provide special shipping instructions.
8. Review and submit your form to complete the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Customer Name
* **Customer Name**: Enter your full name as you would like it to appear on your order.
### 2. Shipping Address
* **Shipping Address**: Enter your shipping address, including street address, apartment number, city, state, and ZIP code.
### 3. -- Item Selection --
### 4. Item Description or SKU
* **Item Description or SKU**: Enter the name or stock code of the item you wish to order.
### 5. Size
* **Size**: Select the size of the item from the options provided: Extra Small (XS), Small (S), Medium (M), Large (L), Extra Large (XL), or XXL.
### 6. Color
* **Color**: Select the color of the item from the options provided: Black, White, Navy Blue, Charcoal, Olive Green, or Burgundy.
### 7. Quantity
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of items you wish to order.
### 8. Unit Price
* **Unit Price**: Enter the price per individual item.
### 9. Accessories
* **Accessories**: Select any optional accessories you would like to add to your order, such as Gift Wrap, Matching Belt, Spare Buttons, or Garment Bag.
### 10. Payment Method
* **Payment Method**: Choose your payment method: Afterpay, Credit Card, or PayPal.
### 11. Special Shipping Instructions
* **Special Shipping Instructions**: If you have any special shipping instructions, such as "Leave at the back door", enter them here.
