# Recycled Display Stand Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recycled Display Stand Order Form is used to collect order details from customers for a recycled display stand. This form helps our customer service team to process and fulfill orders efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete the form, simply fill out the required fields with the requested information.
2. Make sure to enter a valid email address to help us contact you with any updates or questions about your order.
3. For the "Order Items" field, select the items you would like to order from the available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (1, `text`, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
- **Phone** (2, `text`, optional): Provide your phone number for easy contact.
- **Email** (3, `email`, optional): Enter a valid email address to receive updates on your order.
- **Address** (4, `text`, optional): Enter your shipping address for accurate order delivery.
- **Order Items** (5, `select_multiple`, required): Select the items you would like to order from the available options.
  - Select "Yes" to include an item in your order.
  - Select "No" if you do not want to include an item.
- **Special Instructions** (6, `note`, optional): Add any special instructions or requests you have for your order.
- **Quantity** (7, `number`, optional): Enter the quantity of each item you would like to order.
- **Price** (8, `number`, optional): Enter the price of each item you would like to order.
- **Total Price** (9, `number`, optional): The total price of your order will be calculated automatically.
