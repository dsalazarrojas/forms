# Safety Floor Marking Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Safety Floor Marking Supply Order Form is used to place orders for safety floor markings with authorized suppliers. This form is an essential tool for facilities to communicate their needs and manage orders effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling in the required fields, indicated by the **(required)** label in the field explanation section below.
2.  Enter the name of the facility where the order is being placed in the **facility_name** field.
3.  Provide the name of the authorized supplier for the order in the **supplier_name** field.
4.  Enter the email address of the facility contact person in the **facility_contact_email** field (not required but recommended).
5.  Enter the facility phone number in the **facility_phone_number** field (not required but recommended).
6.  Fill in the facility address in the **facility_address** field (not required but recommended).
7.  Choose the date for the order in the **facility_order_date** field (not required but recommended for order tracking).
8.  Choose the time of the order in the **facility_order_time** field (not required but recommended for order tracking).
9.  Enter the quantity of the required safety floor markings in the **facility_order_quantity** field.
10.  Fill in any additional notes regarding the order in the **facility_order_notes** field (not required but recommended for order clarification).
11.  Enter the name of the contact person for the supplier in the **supplier_contact_name** field (not required but recommended).
12.  Enter the phone number of the supplier contact person in the **supplier_contact_phone** field (not required but recommended).
13.  Enter the email address of the supplier contact person in the **supplier_contact_email** field (not required but recommended).
14.  Fill in any additional notes for the supplier in the **supplier_notes** field (not required but recommended).
15.  Choose the date for the delivery in the **delivery_date** field (not required but recommended for order tracking).
16.  Choose the time of the delivery in the **delivery_time** field (not required but recommended for order tracking).
17.  Select the delivery method from the options **in-person**, **ship**, or **pickup** in the **delivery_method** field.
18.  Select the delivery status from the options **Pending** or **Completed** in the **delivery_status** field.
19.  Select the payment status from the options **Pending**, **Completed**, or **Cancelled** in the **payment_status** field.
20.  Select the payment method from the options **Cash**, **Credit Card**, or **Online Payment** in the **payment_method** field.
21.  Enter the supplier's price for the order in the **supplier_price** field (not required but recommended for cost tracking).
