# Home Appliance Stock Order Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Appliance Stock Order Request Form is designed for customers to provide details about their stock order requests. It helps the customer service team collect relevant information to efficiently fulfill the orders.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill in the form with accurate and complete information about your order.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all necessary fields are filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **delivery_details**: Please enter a brief description of the delivery details required for your order.
  - This field is for providing any special delivery instructions or requests.
  - If you have any specific delivery details, such as delivery to a different address or location, please enter them here.
  - This information will help the customer service team to ensure accurate delivery.

- **customer_details**: Please enter a brief description of the customer and their order requirements.
  - This field is for providing any relevant customer information, such as name, contact number, or order specifics.
  - If you have any specific customer requests or preferences, please enter them here.
  - This information will help the customer service team to efficiently manage the order.

- **delivery_date**: Please select the date for delivery of the ordered stock.
  - This field is for providing the specific date when the ordered stock is required to be delivered.
  - If the delivery date is flexible, you can select 'Any' to indicate this.
  - Please ensure to select a realistic delivery date based on your needs.

- **delivery_time**: Please select the time for delivery of the ordered stock.
  - This field is for providing the specific time when the ordered stock is required to be delivered.
  - If the delivery time is flexible, you can select 'Any' to indicate this.
  - Please ensure to select a realistic delivery time based on your needs.

- **delivery_address**: Please enter the delivery address of the ordered stock.
  - This field is for providing the address where the ordered stock is to be delivered.
  - Please ensure to enter a complete and accurate address for efficient delivery.

- **stock_items**: Please enter a brief description of the stock items ordered.
  - This field is for providing the specific items ordered, including their quantities or types.
  - If you have ordered multiple items, please list them separated by commas or on new lines.
  - This information will help the customer service team to accurately manage the order.

- **stock_item_count**: Please enter the quantity of each stock item ordered.
  - This field is for providing the exact quantity of each stock item ordered.
  - If you have ordered multiple items, please ensure to enter the correct quantity for each item.
  - This information will help the customer service team to efficiently manage the order.

- **customer_order_number**: Please enter the customer order number for your reference.
  - This field is for providing a reference number for your order.
  - If you have a unique order number, please enter it here.
  - This will help the customer service team to track your order easily.

- **delivery_method**: Please select the delivery method for the ordered stock.
  - This field is for providing the delivery method preference.
  - Please choose either 'Yes' or 'No' based on whether delivery is required.
  - This information will help the customer service team to arrange for delivery efficiently.

- **order_notes**: Please enter any additional order notes or comments.
  - This field is for providing any specific notes or comments about your order.
  - If you have any special requests or requirements, please enter them here.
  - This information will help the customer service team to better understand your needs.

## Tips
- Please ensure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
- If you have any doubts or questions, please contact the customer service team.
- Providing complete and accurate information will enhance the efficiency of the order fulfillment process.
