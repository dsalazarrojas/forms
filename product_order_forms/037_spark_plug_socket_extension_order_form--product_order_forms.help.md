# Spark Plug Socket Extension Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spark Plug Socket Extension Order Form is used to gather information about the customer's requirements for a spark plug socket extension. This form is intended for customers who need to order spark plug socket extensions for their own use, such as for maintenance, repair, or replacement purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the main_page option that best describes your order: "Yes" or "No". This will help us understand whether you're placing a new order or inquiring about a previous one.
2. Enter your customer information in the customer_info field. This includes your name, company name, and contact information.
3. Specify the order details in the order_details field, answering "Yes" or "No" to indicate the type of order you're placing.
4. Provide the shipping address in the shipping_address field.
5. Choose the payment method (if applicable) in the payment_info field, answering "Yes" or "No".
6. Add any additional customer notes in the customer_notes field.
7. Confirm your submission by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the form_submit field.
8. Confirm your order by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the customer_confirmation field.
9. Enter the number of spark plug socket extensions required in the spark_plug_socket_extension field.
10. Specify the quantity of the ordered item in the quantity field.
11. Choose the color of the spark plug socket extension in the color field, selecting from the available options.
12. Specify the type of socket you need in the socket_type field, selecting from the available options.
13. Enter the size of the socket in the socket_size field.
14. Choose the shape of the socket in the socket_shape field, selecting from the available options.
15. Specify the material of the socket in the socket_material field.
16. Choose the color of the socket in the socket_color field, selecting from the available options.
17. Enter the shipping cost in the shipping_cost field.
18. Choose the currency for the shipping cost in the shipping_cost_currency field, selecting from the available options.
19. Enter the shipping cost symbol in the shipping_cost_currency_symbol field.
20. Select the shipping method (if applicable) in the shipping_method field, answering "Yes" or "No".
21. Add any additional notes about the order in the order_notes field.
22. Enter a confirmation code (if applicable) in the confirmation_code field.
23. Enter a label for the confirmation code in the confirmation_code_label field.
24. Enter the customer's email address in the customer_email field.
25. Enter the customer's phone number in the customer_phone field.
