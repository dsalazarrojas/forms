# PayU Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PayU Product Order Form is a form designed to collect order information from customers to enable them to place an order with PayU.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form by entering your email address in the "Email" field.
2. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
3. Type in the name of the product you are ordering in the "Product Name" field.
4. Enter the date of the order in the "Order Date" field.
5. Enter the time of the order in the "Order Time" field.
6. Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown list.
7. Enter the total amount of the order in the "Total Amount" field.
8. If you have any additional comments or requests, type them in the "Customer Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Email (customer_email)**: Enter your email address to enable us to contact you about your order. This field is optional.
* **Phone (customer_phone)**: Enter your phone number to enable us to contact you about your order. This field is optional.
* **Product Name (product_name)**: Type in the name of the product you are ordering. This field is optional.
* **Order Date (order_date)**: Enter the date of the order. This field is optional.
* **Order Time (order_time)**: Enter the time of the order. This field is optional.
* **Payment Method (payment_method)**: Select your preferred payment method from the dropdown list. The options are PayU, PayPal, and Bank Transfer.
* **Total Amount (total_amount)**: Enter the total amount of the order. This field is optional.
* **Customer Message (customer_message)**: If you have any additional comments or requests, type them in this field. This field is optional.
