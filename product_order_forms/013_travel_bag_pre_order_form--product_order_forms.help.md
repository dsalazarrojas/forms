# Travel Bag Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a pre-order form for travel bags. It will help us understand your preferences and specifications for your pre-ordered bag.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your personal details:
	* First Name: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
	* Last Name: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
	* Email: Enter your contact email address.
	* Phone: Enter your phone number for easy contact.
2. Select your travel details:
	* Travel Date: Enter the date you plan to travel on.
3. Choose your delivery details:
	* Delivery Address: Enter the address where you want your bag to be delivered.
	* Delivery City: Enter the city where your bag will be delivered.
	* Delivery State: Enter the state where your bag will be delivered.
	* Delivery Zip: Enter the zip code where your bag will be delivered.
	* Delivery Country: Enter the country where your bag will be delivered.
4. Add travel bag specifications:
	* Travel Details: Enter any specific requirements for your travel bag, such as color or size.
	* Product Type: Choose the type of product you want to pre-order (Backpack, Handbag, Travel Bag).
	* Product Size: Choose the size of the product (Small, Medium, Large).
	* Product Color: Choose the color of the product (Blue, Red, Green).
	* Product Material: Choose the material of the product (Canvas, Leather, Mesh).
	* Product Weight: Enter the weight of the product.
	* Product Size X, Y, Z: Enter the dimensions of the product.
	* Product Height: Enter the height of the product.
	* Product Width: Enter the width of the product.
	* Product Length: Enter the length of the product.
5. Confirm your order:
	* Confirm: Select "I confirm that I have read and agree to the Terms and Conditions" to complete the form.
