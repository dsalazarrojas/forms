# fathers_day_diy_kits_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Fathers Day DIY Kits Form** is used to collect information from customers who are interested in purchasing a customized gift for their fathers on Father's Day. This form helps the customer provide their personal details so that we can create a unique and thoughtful gift.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out your First Name and Last Name.
2. Provide your Address to ensure we can contact you with any updates or information about your order.
3. Enter a valid Email address so we can send you confirmation emails.
4. If you have a Phone number, please add it to help us contact you quickly.
5. Once you've filled out all fields, click the "Submit" button to send us your details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, Required/Optional: False): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents. This will help us personalize your gift.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Required/Optional: False): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents. This will help us personalize your gift.
* **Address** (`address`, Text, Required/Optional: False): Please enter your full address to ensure we can contact you with any updates or information about your order.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required/Optional: False): Please enter a valid email address where we can send you confirmation emails about your order.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Required/Optional: False): If you have a phone number, please enter it to help us contact you quickly about your order.
