# Character Merchandise Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit character merchandise for approval. It collects relevant information about the product, including the creator or vendor's details, product characteristics, and submission confirmation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all necessary information available before starting the submission process.
2. Fill in your creator or vendor information in the **Creator and Vendor Information** section.
3. Provide a detailed description of the merchandise in the **Merchandise Details** section.
4. Specify the product category, title, and brief description in the **Product Category**, **Product Title and Brief Description**, and **Character Featured in the Merchandise** fields.
5. Choose the manufacturing method used and estimated retail price point in the **Manufacturing Method Used** and **Target Sale Price Point** fields.
6. Specify the availability timeline and whether a physical sample is available for review in the **Availability Timeline Estimate** and **Physical Sample Availability** fields.
7. Provide links to product images or portfolio in the **Link to Product Images or Portfolio** field.
8. Confirm the accuracy of your submission details in the **Confirmation of Submission Details** field.
9. Select the date of submission in the **Date of Submission** field.
10. Add any additional comments or context in the **Additional Merchandise Comments** field.
11. Provide a brief marketing plan summary in the **Marketing Plan Summary** field (optional).
12. Confirm your submission by selecting **I Confirm and Submit Proposal** in the **Confirmation of Submission Details** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Creator or Vendor Full Name**: Enter your full name as the creator or authorized vendor of the merchandise.
* **Business or Brand Name** (optional): If applicable, enter the business or brand name associated with the merchandise.
* **Contact Email for Submission**: Enter an email address where you can be contacted for submission status and follow-up.
* **Creator Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Character Featured in the Merchandise**: Describe which character the product is based on.
* **Product Category**: Choose the most accurate description for the product category from the list.
* **Product Title and Brief Description**: Enter a catchy title and a brief description of the product.
* **Manufacturing Method Used**: Choose how the product is being made (Handmade by Creator, Locally Produced, Mass-Produced by Third Party, or Print-on-Demand).
* **Target Sale Price Point**: Enter the estimated retail price per unit.
* **Availability Timeline Estimate**: Choose when the product will be ready for sale.
* **Link to Product Images or Portfolio**: Provide a URL where we can see the product images or portfolio.
* **Is a physical sample available for review?**: Choose whether a physical sample is available for review (Yes, ready to ship, Available upon request, or No, digital concept only).
* **Confirmation of Submission Details**: Confirm that you are the creator or authorized vendor for this merchandise. Select **I Confirm and Submit Proposal** to submit your proposal.
* **Date of Submission**: Enter today's date.
* **Additional Merchandise Comments**: Add any other relevant information or context.
* **Marketing Plan Summary** (optional): Briefly describe how you plan to promote this product.
* **Submission Confirmation**: Confirm that you have accurately filled out all fields and are the creator or authorized vendor of the merchandise.
