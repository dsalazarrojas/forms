# Monthly Order Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the completion of the Monthly Order Submission Form. The form is intended to streamline the process of submitting monthly orders, allowing you to provide essential details about the product, delivery, and your business.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you are in the correct context to submit a monthly order.
2.  Select the "Repeatable Order" field carefully, choosing one or multiple options that apply to your order.
3.  Provide a brief note for your repeatable order selection, if necessary.
4.  Enter the required information for "Business Name," "Contact Number," and "Contact Email."

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Product Order Form** (`product_order_form`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the product you are ordering, including any specific details or quantities.
*   **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, text, required/optional): Describe the delivery method, such as shipping or in-person pickup.
*   **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you would like the order to be delivered.
*   **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you would like the order to be delivered.
*   **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, required/optional): If you have any special requests or requirements for your order, enter them here.
*   **Business Info** (`business_info`, text, required/optional): Enter any relevant business information, such as a reference number or order ID.
*   **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your business name or the name of the company you are representing.
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact number where you can be reached.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact email address.
*   **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional details about the order, such as a purchase order number.
*   **Repeatable Order** (`repeatable_order`, select_multiple, required): Select the applicable repeatable order options for your submission.
*   **Repeatable Order Note** (`repeatable_order_note`, text, required/optional): If necessary, enter a brief note about your repeatable order selection.

## Tips
*   Be specific and detailed in your responses to ensure accuracy and efficiency in processing your order.
*   Proofread your form before submission to ensure all required fields are completed.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to the relevant department for assistance.
