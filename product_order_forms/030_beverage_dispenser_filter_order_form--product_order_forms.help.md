# Beverage Dispenser Filter Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beverage Dispenser Filter Order Form is used to collect customer information and details for beverage dispenser filters. This form is essential to provide accurate and efficient processing of orders and ensure seamless customer service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your full name to identify yourself.
2. Provide your email address for order updates and notifications.
3. Enter your phone number for further contact if needed.
4. For business or organization orders, enter your company name if applicable.
5. Enter your shipping address for accurate delivery.
6. Specify the city for delivery.
7. Enter your state for delivery.
8. Provide your ZIP code for delivery.
9. Select the brand of your beverage dispenser.
10. If known, enter the model number of your dispenser.
11. Choose the type of filter you need.
12. If applicable, enter the part number of your old filter.
13. Specify how many filters you need.
14. Select how often you replace your filters.
15. Choose how you would like your order shipped.
16. Select how you would like to pay for your order.
17. If applicable, enter your purchase order number.
18. Decide if you need professional installation.
19. Enter any special requests.
20. Provide any additional comments about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: `customer_name` (text, required): Enter your full name for identification.
* **Email Address**: `customer_email` (email, required): We will use this email for order updates and notifications.
* **Phone Number**: `customer_phone` (text, required): Enter your phone number for further contact if needed.
* **Company Name**: `company_name` (text, optional): If you represent a business, enter your company name here.
* **Shipping Address**: `shipping_address` (text, required): Enter your full shipping address for accurate delivery.
* **City**: `city` (text, required): Specify the city for delivery.
* **State**: `state` (text, required): Enter your state for delivery.
* **ZIP Code**: `postal_code` (text, required): Provide your ZIP code for delivery.
* **Dispenser Brand**: `dispenser_brand` (select_one, required): Select the brand of your beverage dispenser.
* **Dispenser Model**: `dispenser_model` (text, optional): If known, enter the model number of your dispenser.
* **Filter Type Needed**: `filter_type` (select_one, required): Choose the type of filter you need.
* **Filter Part Number**: `filter_part_number` (text, optional): If applicable, enter the part number of your old filter.
* **Quantity**: `quantity` (number, required): Specify how many filters you need.
* **Replacement Frequency**: `replacement_frequency` (select_one, optional): Select how often you replace your filters.
* **Shipping Method**: `shipping_method` (select_one, required): Choose how you would like your order shipped.
* **Payment Method**: `payment_method` (select_one, required): Select how you would like to pay for your order.
* **Purchase Order Number**: `purchase_order_number` (text, optional): If applicable, enter your purchase order number.
* **Installation Service**: `installation_service` (select_one, required): Decide if you need professional installation.
* **Special Requests**: `special_requests` (text, optional): Enter any special requirements for your order.
* **Additional Comments**: `additional_comments` (text, optional): Provide any additional comments about your order.
 
## Tips

* Ensure you enter accurate contact information for order updates and notifications.
* Double-check your shipping and payment details for correct delivery and payment processing.
* If you need installation, ensure that you have selected the correct options.
* If you have any special requests, please enter them in the "Special Requests" field.
* For any questions or concerns, contact us at your shipping address or email.
