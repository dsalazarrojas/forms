# kitchen ventilation filter order form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "kitchen ventilation filter order form" is designed to collect information for ordering or customizing kitchen ventilation filters. This form guides you through selecting the type, size, shape, location, number, type, and other specific details to ensure you receive the right filters for your kitchen.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form successfully, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of filter you need from the options (Standard, Premium, or Ultra).
2.  Choose the shape of your filter from the available options (Round, Square, or Rectangular).
3.  Select the location of your filter from the available options (Downtown, Uptown, or Suburbs).
4.  Input the number of filters you need.
5.  If you need filters of different types, select all that apply from the "Filter Type" options (Ceramic, Metal, or Plastic).
6.  In the "Other Filters" field, list any other filters you may need.
7.  Use the "Special Instructions" field to provide any specific details or requests you have.
8.  Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
9.  Provide your email address for communication.
10. Enter the date you prefer to receive your filters (mm/dd/yyyy format).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Filter Type** (`kitchen_ventilation_filter_order`): Select the type of filter you need for your kitchen.
    -   Options: Standard, Premium, or Ultra. 
*   **Filter Size** (`kitchen_ventilation_size`): Please note that this field does not have options in the provided YAML schema. If you need a specific size, please describe it in the "Special Instructions" field.
*   **Filter Shape** (`kitchen_ventilation_shape`): Choose the shape of your filter from the options: Round, Square, or Rectangular.
*   **Location** (`kitchen_ventilation_location`): Select the location where you will use the filter (Downtown, Uptown, or Suburbs).
*   **Number of Filters** (`kitchen_ventilation_filter_number`): Enter the quantity of filters you need.
*   **Filter Type** (`kitchen_ventilation_filter_type`): Choose the type of filter you require (Ceramic, Metal, or Plastic).
*   **Other Filters** (`kitchen_ventilation_other_filters`): List any other filters you need, ensuring you receive all necessary equipment.
*   **Special Instructions** (`kitchen_ventilation_special_instructions`): Use this field to provide specific requests or details about your order.
*   **Phone** (`kitchen_ventilation_phone`): Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Email** (`kitchen_ventilation_email`): Provide your email address for communication.
*   **Date** (`kitchen_ventilation_date`): Enter the date you prefer to receive your filters (mm/dd/yyyy format).
