# Online Account Recharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Online Account Recharge Form" is a customer-facing form designed to collect information and payment method details from customers to facilitate the recharge process for their online accounts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your **Customer Information** (page 1) with your name, address, and other relevant details as requested.
* Choose your **Payment Method** (page 2) by selecting one of the available options (Cash, Card, or Transfer).
* Enter the **Product Name** of the item or service you are recharging (page 2).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information (Page 1)**
  * **(No Label)** (`text`, optional): Enter your name, address, and other relevant details as requested.
* **Payment Method (Page 2)**
  * **Payment Method (Select One)** (`select_one`, optional): Choose one of the available payment options (Cash, Card, or Transfer).
* **Product Name (Page 2)**
  * **Product Name (No Label)** (`text`, optional): Enter the name of the product or service you are recharging.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions and consent from your customers to collect and process their data.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact our support team at `form@gic.mx`.
