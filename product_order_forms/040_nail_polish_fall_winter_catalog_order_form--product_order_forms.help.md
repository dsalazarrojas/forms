# Nail Polish Fall Winter Catalog Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Nail Polish Fall Winter Catalog Order Form" is a form used to collect information from customers placing orders for nail polish products from the Fall Winter catalog. The form is intended to help the customer provide the necessary details to process their order accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information in the "Customer Name", "Contact Name", and "Email" fields.
2. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
3. Enter the date and time you would like to receive your order in the "Order Date" and "Order Time" fields.
4. Describe your order in the "Order Details" field.
5. Enter the product ID of the item you wish to order in the "Product Id" field.
6. Enter the name of the product you wish to order in the "Product Name" field.
7. Enter the price of the product in the "Product Price" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (text, required/optional): This field is for the customer to enter their name.
* **Contact Name** (text, required/optional): This field is for the customer to enter the name of the person they would like to be contacted by.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): This field is for the customer to enter their email address.
* **Phone** (text, required/optional): This field is for the customer to enter their phone number.
* **Order Date** (date, required/optional): This field is for the customer to select a date they would like to receive their order.
* **Order Time** (time, required/optional): This field is for the customer to select a time they would like to receive their order.
* **Order Details** (text, required/optional): This field is for the customer to provide additional details about their order.
* **Product Id** (number, required/optional): This field is for the customer to enter the product ID of the item they wish to order.
* **Product Name** (text, required/optional): This field is for the customer to enter the name of the product they wish to order.
* **Product Price** (number, required/optional): This field is for the customer to enter the price of the product they wish to order.
