# Mulch Sale Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Mulch Sale Order Form is a form used to place orders for mulch sales. It is intended for customers to provide their details and select the type of mulch they wish to purchase. The form helps to ensure that all necessary information is collected accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Customer Details** page with your name and other relevant contact information.
2. Choose the delivery date for your mulch order.
3. Select the type of mulch you would like to purchase from the options provided.
4. Enter the quantity of mulch you would like to purchase.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details**
	+ (Customer Details, text, required) Enter your name and other relevant contact information to allow us to contact you about your order.
* **Delivery Date**
	+ (delivery_date, date, required) Choose the date you would like your mulch to be delivered. This is the date we will use to schedule your delivery.
* **Delivery Time**
	+ (delivery_time, time, optional) Select a delivery time if you have a specific time frame you would like your mulch to be delivered.
* **Mulch Type**
	+ (Mulch Type, select_one, required) Choose one of the following mulch types: Oak, Pine, Cedar, Redwood, or Cypress.
* **Quantity**
	+ (Quantity, number, required) Enter the amount of mulch you would like to purchase. Please note that quantities must be a whole number.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your contact information in the **Customer Details** field to ensure we can contact you correctly.
- Be sure to select a delivery date and time that works for your schedule.
- If you have any special requests or instructions for your mulch delivery, please include them in the **Customer Details** field.
