# Turn Over Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Turn Over Form is a tool used by customers to initiate a return or exchange of a purchased product. This form collects necessary information to process your return or exchange efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the product information provided in the first page to ensure accuracy.
2. Fill out the required fields with accurate and truthful information.
3. Enter your contact information (email and phone number) to ensure timely communication.
4. Provide any additional notes or comments about the reason for return/exchange.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Name (page_2)** (`product_name`, required, text): Enter the name of the product you are returning or exchanging.
* **Reason for Return (page_2)** (`reason_for_return`, required, text): Describe the reason for returning or exchanging the product. Be as detailed as possible to help us process your return/exchange efficiently.
* **Product Price (page_2)** (`product_price`, optional, number): If you've purchased the product at a different price, enter the original price paid.
* **Product Quantity (page_2)** (`product_quantity`, required, number): Enter the quantity of the product you are returning or exchanging.
* **Date of Purchase (page_2)** (`date_of_purchase`, required, date): Enter the date you purchased the product.
* **Time of Purchase (page_2)** (`time_of_purchase`, optional, time): If available, enter the time of purchase.
* **Customer Email (page_3)** (`email`, required, email): Enter your valid email address to ensure we can contact you about your return/exchange.
* **Customer Phone (page_3)** (`phone`, optional, text): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Notes (page_3)** (`notes`, optional, note): Add any additional comments or details about your return/exchange.
* **Product Description (page_3)** (`product_description`, optional, text): If you'd like to provide more information about the product, enter a brief description.
