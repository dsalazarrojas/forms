# Factory Automation Chipset Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Factory Automation Chipset Order Form is designed to gather essential information from customers to process and fulfill orders for chipset products in the factory.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Company Details section with your company's name and address.
2. Provide your shipping address in the Shipping Address section.
3. Select the desired chipset from the Chipset Selection dropdown menu.
4. Enter the quantities of the desired chipset.
5. Add any order notes in the Order Notes field.
6. Check the Product Order Forms checkbox to indicate if you want to receive product order forms.
7. Enter your email address in the Email field.
8. Provide your phone number in the Phone field.
9. Select the category from the Category dropdown menu.
10. Add any additional description in the Description field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Details**: 
  (text, company_details, required): This field is where you provide your company's name and address. Please fill this section with your company's accurate and up-to-date information.
* **Shipping Address**: 
  (text, shipping_address, required): Fill in your company's shipping address, including the street, city, state, zip, and any other required details.
* **Chipset Selection**: 
  (select_one, chipset_selection, required): Select the desired chipset from the provided options.
* **Quantities**: 
  (text, quantities, required): Enter the quantities of the desired chipset.
* **Order Notes**: 
  (note, order_notes, required): Add any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **Product Order Forms**: 
  (select_multiple, product_order_forms, required): Check this box to indicate if you want to receive product order forms.
* **Email**: 
  (email, email, required): Enter your company's email address.
* **Phone**: 
  (text, phone, required): Provide your company's phone number.
* **Category**: 
  (select_one, category, required): Select the category of the desired chipset.
* **Description**: 
  (text, description, required): Add any additional description about your order.
