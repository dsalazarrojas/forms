# Large Format Printing Estimate Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to provide the necessary information for large format printing services. Please fill it out carefully and accurately to ensure a smooth workflow for both parties.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your contact information in the "Customer Name", "Contact Number", and "Email" fields.
3. Provide a detailed description of your project in the "Project Details" field.
4. Choose the correct "Printing Type" for your project.
5. Enter the quantity of prints required.
6. Select the size of the prints you need.
7. Choose the paper type for your prints.
8. Select the material for your prints.
9. Choose the color for your prints.
10. Enter the deadline for the project.
11. Choose the date and time for the prints to be printed.
12. If you have any additional comments or requests, enter them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Name (*)**: Enter your name and company name (if applicable).
- **Contact Number (*)**: Enter your contact number for communication purposes.
- **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes (optional).
- **Project Details (*)**: Provide a detailed description of your project, including any specific requirements or instructions.
- **Printing Type (*)**: Choose the type of printing service you require (e.g., poster, banner, etc.).
- **Quantity (*)**: Enter the number of prints required.
- **Size (*)**: Enter the size of the prints you need.
- **Paper Type (*)**: Choose the type of paper you need for your prints.
- **Material (*)**: Choose the material for your prints (e.g., glossy, matte, etc.).
- **Color (*)**: Choose the color for your prints.
- **Deadline (*)**: Enter the deadline for the project.
- **Date Printed**: The date and time the prints will be printed (system-generated).
- **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or requests you have.
- **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or requests you have (optional).
- **Special Requests**: Enter any special requests you have (optional).

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields (\*) accurately and carefully.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact our support team.
- We will review your request and provide you with an estimate as soon as possible.
