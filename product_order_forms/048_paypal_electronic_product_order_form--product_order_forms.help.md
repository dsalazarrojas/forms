# Paypal Electronic Product Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information for electronic product orders, ensuring that your order is processed correctly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the "product_order_form" field with your order details.
2. Provide your shipping details in the "shipping_details" field.
3. Enter any additional order information in the "order_details" field.
4. Enter information about the product you are ordering in the "product_info" field.
5. Finally, provide your customer information in the "customer_info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Order Form** (`product_order_form`): Enter your order details, such as the product code or description.
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`): Provide your shipping details, including your address and any special instructions for delivery.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`): Enter any additional information about your order, such as any special requests or comments.
* **Product Info** (`product_info`): Enter information about the product you are ordering, such as the product code or description.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`): Enter your contact and delivery information, including your name, email, and any other relevant details.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you enter accurate and complete information for each field to avoid any issues with processing your order.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* We appreciate your cooperation in ensuring that your order is processed efficiently and correctly.
