# Color Street Order Form - In Stock Polish Manis By Dani - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to order "In Stock Polish Manis By Dani" products. Please ensure that the products you select are available in stock before submitting the order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Select the products you wish to purchase**:
	* Browse through the options listed under "In-Stock Glitters", "In-Stock Solids", and "In-Stock Designs".
	* Choose all the products you wish to add to your order by selecting the relevant checkboxes.
2. **Enter your contact information**:
	* Type your name in the "Name" field.
	* Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. **Enter shipping information**:
	* Provide your shipping address in the "Shipping Address" field.
4. **Select your delivery options**:
	* Choose your preferred payment method from the "Preferred Payment Method" dropdown menu.
	* Select whether you would like to have your order shipped or pick it up locally.
5. **Add any additional details** (optional):
	* If you are ordering for a specific party, please provide the hostess's name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name_dani`, text, **required**): Please enter your name as it should be displayed on the order.
* **Email** (`email_dani`, email, **required**): Enter your email address where we can contact you with order updates.
* **Shipping Address** (`address_dani`, text, **required**): Enter your shipping address where you would like to receive your order.
* **In-Stock Glitters** (`instock_glitters`, select_multiple, **optional**): Choose from: Glittersweet, Fort Worth It, At the Plaza, Chelsea Ya Later.
* **In-Stock Solids** (`instock_solids`, select_multiple, **optional**): Choose from: Aspen Sky, Giza Sands, Key West Is Best, Maine-ly Mums.
* **In-Stock Designs** (`instock_designs`, select_multiple, **optional**): Choose from: Spotted in NYC, Floral Distraction, Polka Dot-com.
* **Total number of sets selected** (`sets_count_dani`, number, **required**): Enter the total number of sets you have selected.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (`dani_payment_choice`, select_one, **required**): Choose from: Venmo, PayPal, Cash (Local Pick-up Only).
* **Venmo/PayPal Username** (`dani_venmo_user`, text, **required**): Enter your Venmo or PayPal username.
* **Delivery Type** (`pickup_or_ship`, select_one, **required**): Choose from: Shipping ($4.00), Local Pick-up (Free).
* **Are you ordering for a specific party?** (`party_hostess`, text, **optional**): If so, please provide the hostess's name.
* **Message for Dani** (`dani_message`, text, **optional**): If you have any additional comments or requests, please type them here.

Note: Please ensure that you select products that are actually available in stock before submitting the order.
