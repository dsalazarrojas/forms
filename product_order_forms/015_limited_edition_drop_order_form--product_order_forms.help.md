# Limited Edition Drop Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for limited edition drop orders. The form is used to gather details about the order, including shipping and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
3. Select the shipping address details, including city, state, and zip code.
4. Choose the items you would like to order (Yes/No).
5. Select the payment method you would like to use (Yes/No).
6. Enter the shipping method you would like to use (Yes/No).
7. Select the shipping price and order total.
8. Enter the date and time you would like to make the payment.
9. Finally, submit the form when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Shipping Address 1** (`shipping_address_1`, `text`, required: false): Enter the first part of your shipping address.
* **Shipping Address 2** (`shipping_address_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter the second part of your shipping address.
* **Order Item 1** (`order_item_1`, `select_one`, required: false): Select "Yes" to order the first item or "No" if you do not want to order it.
* **Order Item 2** (`order_item_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select "Yes" to order the second item or "No" if you do not want to order it.
* **Order Price** (`order_price`, `number`, required: false): Enter the price for your order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Select "Yes" to use a payment method or "No" if you do not want to use one.
* **Payment Method Label** (`payment_method_label`, `text`, required: false): Enter a label for your payment method.
* **Payment Gateway** (`payment_gateway`, `select_one`, required: false): Select "Yes" to use a payment gateway or "No" if you do not want to use one.
* **Shipping City** (`shipping_address_3`, `text`, required: false): Enter the city for your shipping address.
* **Shipping State** (`shipping_address_4`, `text`, required: false): Enter the state for your shipping address.
* **Shipping Zip** (`shipping_address_5`, `text`, required: false): Enter the zip code for your shipping address.
* **Shipping Country** (`shipping_address_6`, `text`, required: false): Enter the country for your shipping address.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address_7`, `text`, required: false): Enter the complete shipping address.
* **Order Item 3** (`order_item_3`, `select_one`, required: false): Select "Yes" to order the third item or "No" if you do not want to order it.
* **Order Item 4** (`order_item_4`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select "Yes" to order the fourth item or "No" if you do not want to order it.
* **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Select "Yes" to use a shipping method or "No" if you do not want to use one.
* **Shipping Price** (`shipping_price`, `number`, required: false): Enter the shipping price.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total price for your order.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date for your order.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date for your payment.
* **Form Submitted** (`form_submitted`, `time`, required: false): This field is not required but is used to track when the form was submitted.

Note: The fields that are not required are optional and can be skipped. However, providing accurate and complete information will ensure that the order is processed correctly.
