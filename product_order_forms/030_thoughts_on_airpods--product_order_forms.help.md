# Thoughts On Airpods - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect your thoughts and opinions about Airpods. Your feedback is crucial in helping us improve our product and services. Please answer the questions truthfully, and we will use your input to shape the future of Airpods.

## How To Complete This Form

- Fill out the form carefully, one page at a time.
- Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your knowledge.
- If you're unsure about any question, feel free to skip it or ask for clarification.
- Once you've completed the form, submit it and review your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your first name?** (`first_name`, text, optional)
	+ This is the first name that appears on your identification documents.
* **How do you like Airpods?** (`airpods_like`, text, optional)
	+ Tell us about your experience with Airpods, e.g., what you like or dislike about them.
* **What is your last name?** (`last_name`, text, optional)
	+ This is the second name that appears on your identification documents.
* **What is your email address?** (`email`, email, optional)
	+ Enter the email address you use to contact us or receive updates about Airpods.
* **What is your phone number?** (`phone`, text, optional)
	+ This is the phone number you use to contact us or receive updates about Airpods.
* **What is your favorite feature of Airpods?** (`favorite_feature`, select_one, required)
	+ Select one feature that you like most about Airpods. You can choose from:
		+ Wireless
		+ BatteryLife
		+ Design
		+ Color
* **Which of the following features do you like most?** (`features`, select_multiple, required)
	+ Select all features that you like about Airpods. You can choose from:
		+ Wireless
		+ BatteryLife
		+ Design
		+ Color
* **How long do you plan to use Airpods for?** (`usage_duration`, time, required)
	+ Enter the amount of time you plan to use Airpods for, e.g., daily, weekly, monthly.
* **Why do you want to buy Airpods?** (`purchase_reason`, text, optional)
	+ Tell us why you want to buy Airpods, e.g., for personal use, as a gift, etc.
* **How satisfied are you with Airpods?** (`satisfaction`, select_one, required)
	+ Select one of the options below to indicate your level of satisfaction with Airpods:
		+ VerySatisfied
		+ SomewhatSatisfied
		+ NotSatisfied
* **Do you have any comments about Airpods?** (`comments`, note, optional)
	+ If you have any thoughts, suggestions, or concerns about Airpods, please share them with us.
* **What is your favorite Airpods color?** (`favorite_color`, text, optional)
	+ Enter your favorite Airpods color.
* **Do you like the design of Airpods?** (`design_pleasing`, checkbox, optional)
	+ Select "Option A" if you like the design of Airpods, or "Option B" if you don't.
* **How long do you think Airpods will last?** (`durability`, number, optional)
	+ Enter a number that indicates how long you think Airpods will last, e.g., months or years.
* **Do you have any other comments about Airpods?** (`additional_comments`, note, optional)
	+ If you have any additional thoughts or suggestions, please share them with us.
