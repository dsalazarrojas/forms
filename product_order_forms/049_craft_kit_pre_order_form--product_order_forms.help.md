# Craft Kit Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer information for craft kit pre-orders, including personal and shipping details. It is essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure smooth order processing and communication with the customer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "first name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "email" field. This will be used as your contact email for communication.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone number" field. This will help us contact you if needed.
5. Enter your shipping address in the "shipping address" field.
6. Type in your city in the "city" field.
7. Select your state from the options in the "state" field.
8. Select your country from the options in the "country" field.
9. Enter your zip code in the "zip code" field.
10. If you have any specific order requirements or requests, please provide the total amount of the order in the "order total" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name to help us address you personally.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name to help us complete your order.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you for order updates and information.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your shipping address to ensure your order is delivered correctly.
* **City** (`city`, text, required/optional): Enter the city where you would like your order shipped to.
* **State** (`state`, select_one, required/optional): Select your state from the options to help us determine the shipping location.
* **Country** (`country`, select_one, required/optional): Select your country from the options to ensure proper shipping and customs handling.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required/optional): Enter your zip code for accurate shipping and delivery.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, required/optional): If you have any specific order requirements or requests, please enter the total amount of the order.

## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy to avoid any issues with order processing.
* If you have any specific requirements or requests, please include the order total to ensure we can fulfill your needs correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us for assistance.
