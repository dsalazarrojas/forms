# Exclusive Color Phone Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers who want to pre-order a product in a specific color. The form is used to gather details such as customer contact information, order details, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Fill in your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone number" field.
5. Select the order date you would like to place the order for in the "Order date" field.
6. Select the color of the product you would like to order in the "Color" field.
7. If applicable, enter the quantity you would like to order in the "Quantity" field.
8. Select the payment method you would like to use in the "Payment method" field.
9. Enter the shipping address in the "Shipping address" field.
10. Enter the total amount of the order in the "Total amount" field.
11. Enter the payment amount in the "Payment amount" field.
12. Enter the tax amount in the "Tax amount" field.
13. Enter the shipping fee in the "Shipping fee" field.
14. If applicable, enter the discount amount in the "Discount" field.
15. If applicable, enter the store credit amount in the "Store credit" field.
16. Enter the store name in the "Store" field.
17. Enter the store location in the "Location" field.
18. If you have any additional order notes, enter them in the "Order notes" field.
19. If you have any notes for the store manager, enter them in the "Store manager notes" field.
20. If you have any notes for the sales representative, enter them in the "Sales rep notes" field.
21. Enter the customer's name in the "Customer name" field.
22. Enter the customer's email address in the "Customer email" field.
23. Enter the customer's phone number in the "Customer phone" field.
24. Enter the store owner's name in the "Store owner" field.
25. If you have any additional information, enter the store owner's phone number in the "Store owner phone" field.

* **Total amount** (`total_amount`, number, required): Enter the total amount of the order.
* **Payment amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required): Enter the payment amount.
* **Tax amount** (`tax_amount`, number, required): Enter the tax amount.
* **Shipping fee** (`shipping_fee`, number, required): Enter the shipping fee.
* **Discount** (`discount`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the discount amount.
* **Store credit** (`store_credit`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the store credit amount.
