# Laser Engraving Quote Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for a laser engraving quote request from customers. It will collect details about the project, artwork files, quantities, deadlines, quote requests, contact person, email, and phone number. The information collected will be used to generate a quote for the customer.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Project Details (page 1)**: Enter a brief description of the project you would like to have laser engraved.
2. **Artwork Files (page 1)**: If you have artwork files related to your project, please upload them here.
3. **Quantities (page 1)**: Enter the quantity of items you would like to have laser engraved.
4. **Deadlines (page 1)**: Enter the deadline for your project.
5. **Quote Request (page 1)**: Enter any additional information or requirements you have for your quote.
6. **Contact Person (page 1)**: Enter the name of the contact person for your project.
7. **Contact Email (page 1)**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
8. **Contact Phone (page 1)**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details (project_details)**: Enter a brief description of the project you would like to have laser engraved.
* **Artwork Files (artwork_files)**: Upload any artwork files related to your project, such as images or designs.
* **Quantities (quantities)**: Enter the quantity of items you would like to have laser engraved. Please note that this is a numerical field, so you can only enter numbers.
* **Deadlines (deadlines)**: Enter the deadline for your project in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`. This can be the date you need the project done by, or when you would like to receive the quote by.
* **Quote Request (quote_request)**: Enter any additional information or requirements you have for your quote, such as any specific materials you would like to use.
* **Contact Person (contact_person)**: Enter the name of the contact person for your project.
* **Contact Email (contact_email)**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone (contact_phone)**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.

Note: All fields are optional, and you can leave any field blank if not applicable.
