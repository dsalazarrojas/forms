# Jewelry Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a new product order for a jewelry product. It helps to collect relevant information from the customer, ensuring accurate and efficient order processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer's name.
2. Enter the customer's email address.
3. Provide the order date and time.
4. Enter the product name and description.
5. Add any additional notes about the order.
6. Enter the customer's phone number.
7. Provide the customer's address.
8. Enter the product price.
9. Specify the product quantity.
10. Upload or link to the product image (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the customer's name as it appears on their identification or documentation.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address where they can be contacted regarding their order.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Select the date on which the order is to be fulfilled.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, optional): Select the time on which the order is to be fulfilled.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the product being ordered.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the product being ordered.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the order, such as special requests or preferences.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number where they can be contacted.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, optional): Enter the customer's address for shipping and delivery purposes.
* **Product Price** (`product_price`, number, optional): Enter the price of the product being ordered.
* **Product Quantity** (`product_quantity`, number, optional): Specify the quantity of the product being ordered.
* **Product Image** (`product_image`, text, optional): Upload or link to the product image for easy reference.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate order processing.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the product description or customer notes for clarification.
* Double-check all entered information for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* If you need to update or cancel an existing order, contact our customer support team.
