# promotional_floor_decal_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The promotional floor decal order form is used to collect contact and shipping information from customers who wish to order promotional floor decals. This form is required for all orders, as it helps us understand the customer's needs and ensures accurate delivery of their decal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the **Contact Information** section with your name, email address, and phone number. This is the first step in the ordering process and is necessary for us to communicate with you.
2.  In the **Shipping Information** section, provide your shipping address to ensure that your decal is delivered to the correct location.
3.  If you have artwork or images you'd like to use for your decal, upload it in the **Artwork Upload** section. This will help us create your decal more accurately.
4.  Select your decal from the **Decal Selection** section. You can choose from the available options, and this selection will determine the design and material of your decal.
5.  In the **Confirm Decal Details** section, double-check the decal size and material you've selected. Ensure that everything is correct before finalizing your order.
6.  In the **Confirm Decal Details** section, select the decal size and material you've chosen from the options.
7.  Finally, select the **Submit** option to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Contact Information (required)** (`contact_info`, `text`, required): Enter your name, email address, and phone number to enable us to contact you regarding your order.
*   **Shipping Information (required)** (`shipping_info`, `text`, required): Provide your shipping address so we can deliver your decal to the correct location.
*   **Artwork Upload (optional)** (`artwork_upload`, `text`, optional): If you have artwork or images to upload, use this section to provide it. This will help us create your decal more accurately.
*   **Decal Selection (required)** (`decal_selection`, `select_one`, required): Choose from the available decal options to determine the design and material of your decal.
*   **Confirm Decal Details (required)** (`confirm_decal_details`, `select_multiple`, required): Double-check the decal size and material you've selected to ensure accuracy.
*   **Submit (optional)** (`submit`, `select_one`, optional): Select this option to complete your order.

Note: The form is designed to be used for promotional floor decal orders, and the fields are structured accordingly. If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to contact us.
