Local Produce Vendor Order Form - Help Guide
=====================================

### Purpose

This form is designed to facilitate the process of placing orders with local produce vendors for the farm. It captures essential information about the farm location, order details, and customer contact information. By using this form, customers can efficiently submit their orders and ensure that the necessary data is collected for processing.

### How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the farm location from the provided options or choose "Other" if the location is not listed.
2.  Enter the date you would like to place the order for.
3.  Select the time you would like to place the order.
4.  Enter the name of the product you would like to order.
5.  Specify the quantity of the product.
6.  Enter the price of the product.
7.  Calculate or enter the total price of the order.
8.  Enter the customer's email address.
9.  Enter the customer's phone number.
10.  Enter the customer's name.
11.  Enter the vendor's name (the person placing the order on behalf of the customer).
12.  Provide any additional order notes or instructions.
13.  Select the payment status (Pending, Paid, or Unpaid).
14.  Choose the payment method (Cash, Bank Transfer, or Credit Card).

### Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Farm Location**: 
    *   Select the farm location from the given options or type "Other" if the location is not listed.
*   **Order Date**: 
    *   Enter the date you would like to place the order for.
*   **Order Time**: 
    *   Select the time you would like to place the order.
*   **Product Name**: 
    *   Enter the name of the product you would like to order.
*   **Quantity**: 
    *   Specify the quantity of the product.
*   **Price**: 
    *   Enter the price of the product.
*   **Total Price**: 
    *   Calculate or enter the total price of the order.
*   **Customer Email**: 
    *   Enter the customer's email address.
*   **Customer Phone**: 
    *   Enter the customer's phone number.
*   **Customer Name**: 
    *   Enter the customer's name.
*   **Vendor Name**: 
    *   Enter the vendor's name (the person placing the order on behalf of the customer).
*   **Order Notes**: 
    *   Enter any additional information or instructions regarding the order.
*   **Payment Status**: 
    *   Select the payment status (Pending, Paid, or Unpaid).
*   **Payment Method**: 
    *   Choose the payment method (Cash, Bank Transfer, or Credit Card).

### Tips

*   Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure smooth order processing.
*   Double-check that you have entered the correct farm location and customer contact information.
*   If you select "Other" as the farm location, please be prepared to provide more details in the "Order Notes" field.
