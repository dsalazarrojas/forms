# item_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The item_order_form is used to collect information from customers about their order requests. This form helps the business understand the customer's needs, ensuring a smooth and efficient order process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with accurate and relevant information. Start by providing the title of the form (step 1), followed by the details of the buyer (step 2), the buyer's email (step 3), the date of the order (step 4), and any additional notes (step 5). If applicable, select the category for the item (step 6). Finally, enter the product name and any other necessary details in step 7.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Step 1: get_the_details_of_the_buyer** (`item_order_form_first_page`, text, Optional): This is the first page of the form where you can enter a brief description or any other details about your order request.
* **the_buyer_details** (`item_order_form_buyer_details`, text, Optional): Enter any additional details about the buyer that might be relevant to your order.
* **buyer_email** (`buyer_email`, email, Optional): Enter the buyer's email address for contact purposes.
* **when_did_you_want_to_get_the_item** (`item_order_form_order_date`, date, Optional): Select the date you'd like to receive the item.
* **note** (`item_order_form_note`, note, Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **category** (`item_order_form_category`, select_one, Optional): Select "Yes" if the item is related to a specific category.
* **product_name** (`item_order_form_product_name`, text, Optional): Enter the name of the product being ordered.

## Tips
- Please fill out all the required fields (marked as required: true) for accurate order processing.
- Double-check your email address for correct formatting.
- If you have trouble with any of the fields, feel free to reach out to us for assistance.
- The fields that have "Yes" or "No" options should only be filled out if the answer is either "Yes" or "No".
