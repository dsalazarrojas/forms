# 1:1 Diet Product Order Form Lou & Nick - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to place orders for the 1:1 Diet products. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure efficient processing and delivery of your order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the customer information section, which includes:
	* Full name: Enter your first and last name
	* Email address: Enter the email address you'd like to receive receipt and order updates
	* Phone number: Enter your phone number, including the country code (optional)
2. Select your preferred contact method: Email, Phone, or SMS
3. Provide order details, including:
	* Order date: Select the date you'd like to receive your order
	* Product SKU or code: Enter the SKU or code of the product you're ordering (if available)
	* Item or product name: Enter the exact name of the product you're ordering (e.g., 1:1 Diet - 28 Day Pack)
	* Quantity: Enter the number of units you'd like to order
	* Unit price (USD): Enter the price per unit
	* Total price (USD): You can either enter the total price or leave this field blank for staff to calculate
4. Choose your preferred delivery method: Standard shipping, Express shipping, Pickup, or Local delivery
5. If you're choosing a delivery method that requires an address, enter it in the delivery address field
6. If you'd like to request a specific delivery date, enter it in the requested delivery date field
7. Add any special instructions or customization notes in the special instructions field
8. Choose your payment method: Credit card, Cash, Bank transfer, PayPal, or Invoice
9. If you're paying by credit card, enter the last 4 digits of the card number
10. Finally, select the order status and enter any additional comments or notes
11. Don't forget to indicate whether you'd like to subscribe to Lou & Nick updates for product news, offers, and tips!

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name**: Your first and last name.
* **Email address**: The email address you'd like to receive receipt and order updates.
* **Phone number**: Your phone number, including the country code (optional).
* **Preferred contact method**: Select how you'd like to be contacted: Email, Phone, or SMS.
* **Order date**: The date you'd like to receive your order.
* **Product SKU or code**: The product's SKU or code (if available).
* **Item or product name**: The exact name of the product you're ordering (e.g., 1:1 Diet - 28 Day Pack).
* **Quantity**: The number of units you'd like to order.
* **Unit price (USD)**: The price per unit.
* **Total price (USD)**: You can either enter the total price or leave this field blank for staff to calculate.
* **Include gift wrap**: Select whether you'd like to include gift wrap.
* **Preferred delivery method**: Choose your preferred delivery method: Standard shipping, Express shipping, Pickup, or Local delivery.
* **Delivery address**: The address where you'd like to receive your order. (Leave blank if choosing Pickup).
* **Requested delivery date**: If you'd like to request a specific delivery date, enter it here.
* **Special instructions**: Any additional information relevant to this order.
* **Payment method**: Choose how you'd like to pay: Credit card, Cash, Bank transfer, PayPal, or Invoice.
* **Card last 4 digits**: The last 4 digits of your credit card number (optional).
* **Subscribe to Lou & Nick updates**: Select whether you'd like to receive product news, offers, and tips.
* **Order status**: Select the order status: Pending, Confirmed, Processing, Shipped, Completed, Cancelled. (For staff use only).
* **Additional comments**: Any other information relevant to this order.
