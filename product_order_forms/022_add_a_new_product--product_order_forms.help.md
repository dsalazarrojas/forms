# Add A New Product - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to add new products to your inventory. To use this form, ensure you have the necessary information about the product and its details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Ensure you have all the required information about the product, including the product name, category, price, description, and initial stock quantity.
2. Fill in the product name and category in their respective fields.
3. Enter the retail price and unit cost of the product.
4. Add a short marketing description of the product.
5. Specify the initial inventory quantity and the weight of the product in kilograms.
6. Provide the product's dimensions, if applicable.
7. Enter the contact details of the supplier, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product name** (`product_name`, text, required): Enter the official title of the product.
* **Category** (`category`, text, required): Select the product's category or department.
* **Price USD** (`price_usd`, number, required): Enter the retail price of the product in USD.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Add a short marketing description of the product.
* **Stock quantity** (`stock_quantity`, number, required): Enter the initial inventory quantity.
* **Weight kg** (`weight_kg`, number, optional): Enter the weight of the product in kilograms, used for shipping estimates.
* **Dimensions** (`dimensions`, text, optional): Enter the length, width, and height of the product in centimeters, if applicable.
* **Supplier contact** (`supplier_contact`, text, optional): Enter the contact details of the supplier, if applicable.
* **SKU** (`sku`, text, optional): Enter the stock keeping unit, if available.
* **Cost** (`cost`, number, optional): Enter the unit cost for inventory valuation, if applicable.
