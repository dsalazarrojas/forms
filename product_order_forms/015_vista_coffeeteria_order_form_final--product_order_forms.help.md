# vista_coffeeteria_order_form_final - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a clear and concise way for customers to place an order at the Vista Coffeeeteria.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Ensure you have filled in all required fields, marked with an asterisk (*)
- Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
- Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
- Enter your email address in the "Email" field in the correct format.
- Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
- Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field using the MM/DD/YYYY format.
- Enter the time you would like to pick up your order in the "Order Time" field.
- If you have any special notes or instructions for your order, enter them in the "Order Note" field.
- Enter your address in the "Address" field.
- If you have a preference for an option (Option 1), select it from the dropdown list.
- If you have multiple options (Option 2), select all that apply.
- If you would like to specify a price, enter it in the "Price" field.
- If you want to order in quantity, enter the number in the "Quantity" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: <input type="text" name="first_name"> (* required): Enter your first name as it appears on your ID or driver's license.
- **Last Name**: <input type="text" name="last_name"> (* required): Enter your last name as it appears on your ID or driver's license.
- **Email**: <input type="email" name="email"> (* required): Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., example@example.com).
- **Phone**: <input type="text" name="phone"> (* required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Date of Birth**: <input type="date" name="date_of_birth"> (* required): Enter your date of birth using MM/DD/YYYY format.
- **Order Time**: <input type="time" name="order_time"> (* required): Enter the time you would like to pick up your order in 24-hour format.
- **Order Note**: <input type="text" name="order_note" optional): Enter any special instructions or notes for your order.
- **Address**: <input type="text" name="address"> (* required): Enter your address for delivery or pick-up purposes.
- **Option 1**: <select name="option_1" multiple></select> (optional): Select one option for your order.
- **Option 2**: <select name="option_2" multiple></select> (optional): Select all applicable options for your order.
- **Price**: <input type="number" name="price" optional></input>: Enter the price of your order, if applicable.
- **Quantity**: <input type="number" name="quantity" optional></input>: Enter the quantity of your order, if applicable.
