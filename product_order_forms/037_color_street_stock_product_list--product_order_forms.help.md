# Color Street Stock Product List - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Color Street Stock Product List form is used to collect and manage product information for Color Street's stocked products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure that you have permission to access this form.
2. Click on the "Start" button to access the form.
3. Review the header page, which is a summary of the form's purpose.
4. Fill out the required fields on the main content page:
	* **Product Code**: Enter the unique identifier for the product (SKU or identifier).
	* **Product Name**: Enter the name and brief description of the product.
5. Scroll down to the next fields:
	* **Category**: Select or type in the product category.
	* **Unit Price**: Enter the decimal currency value of the product.
	* **Available Stock**: Enter the integer units of the product available.
	* **Reorder Point**: Enter the threshold to reorder the product.
	* **Supplier Name**: Enter the primary vendor for the product.
	* **Lead Time in Days**: Enter the supplier's lead time in days.
6. Add any **Notes** about the product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Code**: A unique identifier (SKU or identifier) for the product. This is a required field.
* **Product Name**: Enter the name and brief description of the product. This is a required field.
* **Category**: Select or type in the product category. This field is not required.
* **Unit Price**: Enter the decimal currency value of the product. This field is not required.
* **Available Stock**: Enter the integer units of the product available. This is a required field.
* **Reorder Point**: Enter the threshold to reorder the product. This field is not required.
* **Supplier Name**: Enter the primary vendor for the product. This field is not required.
* **Lead Time in Days**: Enter the supplier's lead time in days. This field is not required.
* **Notes**: Add any additional details about the product. This field is not required.

## Tips
* Ensure that each field is filled out accurately and consistently.
* Use the provided hints for help with filling out each field.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* Do not attempt to duplicate or reuse existing products.
