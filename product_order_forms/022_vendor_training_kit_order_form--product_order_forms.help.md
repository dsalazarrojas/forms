# Vendor Training Kit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Vendor Training Kit Order Form is designed to collect information from vendors who want to purchase a training kit. This form will help us process their order correctly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information. If you're unsure about any field, please refer to the field-by-field explanations below.
2. Submit the form to place your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Vendor Info

* **Vendor Info** (`vendor_info`, text, required: false): This field is where you can enter your company name, contact person, or any other relevant information about your company.
    - Please enter the name of your company.
    - This will help us process your order efficiently.

### 2. Select Kits

* **Select Kits** (`training_materials`, select multiple, required: false): Choose the training kits you'd like to purchase. 
    - Select one or more kits from the provided options.
    - You can choose from Kit 1, Kit 2, or Kit 3.

### 3. Shipping Details

* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, text, required: false): Enter the shipping details for your order.
    - Provide any additional shipping details, such as your shipping address or special requests.
    - This will help us process your order efficiently.

### 4. Order Notes

* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about your order.
    - If you have any special requests or comments, please enter them here.
    - This will help us understand your requirements better.

### 5. Email

* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
    - This is the email address we'll use to contact you about your order.
    - Please ensure it's accurate to receive updates about your order.

### 6. Phone

* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
    - This is the number we'll use to contact you about your order.
    - Please ensure it's accurate to receive updates about your order.

### 7. Date

* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you'd like to receive your order by.
    - Select a date that suits you for delivery.
    - This will help us plan the delivery of your order.

Tips:

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
* We'll do our best to process your order efficiently and effectively.
