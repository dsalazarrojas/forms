# Club Event Souvenir Production Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help event organizers place orders for souvenirs for their events. It collects essential information to ensure accurate production and delivery of the ordered items.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*), as they are necessary for processing your order.
2. For each field, make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure correct production and delivery of your order.
3. Review your order carefully before submitting it to ensure you have filled in all necessary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Name** (*required*): Enter the name of the person or organization placing the order.
* **Club Name** (*required*): Enter the name of the club or organization hosting the event.
* **Event Name** (*required*): Enter the name of the event these souvenirs are for.
* **Email Address** (*required*): Enter the email address of the contact person or organization.
* **Phone Number** (*required*): Enter the phone number of the contact person or organization.
* **Event Date** (*required*): Enter the date of the event these souvenirs are needed for.
* **Required Delivery Date** (*required*): Enter the date these souvenirs must be delivered.
* **Souvenir Item Type** (*required*): Choose the type of souvenir item you need (e.g., T-shirt, mug, hat).
* **Item Description** (*required*): Enter a detailed description of the souvenir item, including its style and material.
* **Design or Logo Description** (*required*): Describe what will be printed or embroidered on the souvenir item.
* **Item Color** (*required*): Enter the color of the souvenir item.
* **Total Quantity Needed** (*required*): Enter the total number of souvenirs needed.
* **Unit Price - USD** (*required*): Enter the cost per souvenir.
* **Personalization Required** (*required*): Choose whether personalization is required for the souvenir item.
* **Personalization Details** (optional): If personalization is required, enter the text or design to be printed or embroidered on each item.
* **Printing or Embroidery Method** (*required*): Choose the method of printing or embroidery for the souvenir item (e.g., screen print, embroidered, heat transfer).
* **Size Range Available** (optional): Enter the size range available for the souvenir item (e.g., S, M, L, XL).
* **Size Breakdown** (optional): If the souvenir item is available in multiple sizes, enter the breakdown of each size.
* **Special Requests or Notes** (optional): Enter any additional information or special requests for the souvenir order.
