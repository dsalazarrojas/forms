# perfume_purchase_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The perfume_purchase_order_form is a digital form designed to collect information from customers for their perfume order. It is a general guide for customers to input their information voluntarily.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Review the form and make sure you understand what each field is asking for.
2. Fill in your details in the "Customer Info" page.
3. Proceed to the "order_details" page to input your order information.
4. Move on to the "payment_info" page to add your payment details.
5. Finally, input your shipping address on the "shipping_address" page.
6. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Customer Info (Customer Info)
*   **Customer Info** (`Customer Info`, `text`, required/optional): This is the first page where you will input your basic contact information, including your name, email address, and other relevant details. Please ensure that your information is accurate and up-to-date.

### 2. Order Details (Order Details)
*   **Order Details** (`Order Details`, `text`, required/optional): On this page, you will input more details about your order, including the product you are interested in, your preferred fragrance, and any other relevant information. Please be as detailed as possible to help us process your order efficiently.

### 3. Payment Info (Payment Info)
*   **Payment Info** (`Payment Info`, `text`, required/optional): Here, you will provide your payment information, including your method of payment, card number, and any other relevant details. Make sure to double-check your information to avoid any discrepancies.

### 4. Shipping Address (Shipping Address)
*   **Shipping Address** (`Shipping Address`, `text`, required/optional): In this final page, you will input your shipping address, including your name, street address, city, state, zip code, and any other relevant details. Ensure that your address is accurate and up-to-date to avoid any delays in processing your order.
