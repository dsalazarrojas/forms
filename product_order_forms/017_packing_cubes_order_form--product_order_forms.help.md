# Packing Cubes Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Packing Cubes Order Form is a user-facing form designed to collect customer information and order details for the efficient processing and management of packing cube orders. This form serves as a communication channel for customers to share their order requirements and preferences with the business or organization handling the orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your name in the **customer name** field.
* Provide your email address in the **email** field.
* Select the date of your order in the **order date** field.
* Enter your shipping address in the **shipping address** field.
* Enter your city in the **city** field.
* Select your state or region in the **state** field.
* Enter your postal/zip code in the **zip/postal code** field.
* Describe your order details in the **order details** field.
* Include any relevant customer notes in the **customer note** field.
* Enter your phone number in the **phone** field.
* Select your country of origin in the **country** field.
* Indicate the number of products you are ordering in the **number of products** field.
* Choose the type of product you are ordering from the **product type** field.
* Re-enter your email address in the **email** field (this is a duplicate field, likely for validation or consistency purposes).
* Enter any relevant details about who submitted the form in the **submitted by** field.
* Enter your customer ID or reference number in the **customer ID** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your identification or as you prefer it to be spelled.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address where you can be reached for updates or inquiries.
* **Order date** (`order_date`, date, required: false): Select the date you wish to have your order fulfilled or shipped.
* **Shipping address** (`shipping_address`, text, required: false): Enter your complete address where you would like to receive your order.
* **City** (`shipping_city`, text, required: false): Enter the city where you would like to receive your order.
* **State** (`shipping_state`, text, required: false): Enter your state or region where you would like to receive your order.
* **Zip/Postal code** (`shipping_postal_code`, text, required: false): Enter your postal/zip code where you would like to receive your order.
* **Order details** (`order_details`, text, required: false): Describe the details of your order, including any specific requirements or preferences.
* **Customer note** (`customer_note`, text, required: false): Enter any notes or comments you would like to share with the business or organization handling your order.
* **Phone** (`shipping_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for updates or inquiries.
* **Country** (`shipping_country`, text, required: false): Enter your country of origin.
* **Number of products** (`product_count`, number, required: false): Indicate the number of products you are ordering.
* **Product type** (`product_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of product you are ordering from the provided options (Cuboid, Cubo, Cubelet, Cube, Packed Cuboid, Packed Cubo, Packed Cube, Packed Cubelet).
* **Email** (`customer_email`, email, required: false): Re-enter your email address for validation or consistency purposes.
* **Submitted by** (`form_submitted_by`, text, required: false): Enter details about who submitted the form.
* **Customer ID** (`customer_id`, text, required: false): Enter your customer ID or reference number.

## Tips
- Ensure that you enter accurate and complete information to avoid any delays or issues with your order.
- If you have any questions or concerns about your order, feel free to contact the business or organization handling your order using the provided email address.
- This form is for your convenience and efficiency, so please take a few minutes to complete it accurately and thoroughly.
