# Protein Bar Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Protein Bar Order Form is used to collect order details and customer information for protein bar orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Protein Bar Order section with the type of protein bar you would like to order.
2. In the Delivery Details section, provide the date and time you would like to receive your order.
3. In the Payment Information section, enter the total weight of the order (in kilograms).
4. In the Customer Information section, enter your name, email, and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Protein Bar Order (1)**: Select the type of protein bar you would like to order.
* **Delivery Details (2)**: Enter the date (mm/dd/yy) and time (hh:MM AM/PM) you would like to receive your order.
* **Payment Details (3)**: Enter the total weight of the order in kilograms.
* **Customer Name (8)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Customer Email (9)**: Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone (10)**: Enter your phone number for delivery confirmation.
* **Order Total (11)**: Enter the total weight of the order, which will be calculated based on the selected protein bar options.

## Tips
* Please make sure to select the correct type of protein bar you would like to order.
* Double-check the delivery date and time to ensure it aligns with your schedule.
* Ensure the entered email address and phone number are accurate to receive confirmation and updates on your order.
* Verify the total weight of the order to avoid any discrepancies.
