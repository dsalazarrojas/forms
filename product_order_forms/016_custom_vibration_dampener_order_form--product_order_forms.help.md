# Custom Vibration Dampener Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom Vibration Dampener Order Form is designed to gather information from customers for the completion of custom vibration dampener orders. This form helps to collect contact details, product information, shipping details, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the **customer info** section with your name, contact details, and any other relevant information.
2. In the **product info** section, provide details about the custom vibration dampener you are interested in purchasing, including product name or description.
3. In the **shipping info** section, enter the shipping address and any additional shipping instructions.
4. In the **payment info** section, enter the payment method and details.
5. Review the **order summary** section to ensure all information is complete and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, `text`, Required: No): This section is where you can provide your name, contact details, and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Product Info** (`product_info`, `text`, Required: No): Please enter details about the custom vibration dampener you are interested in purchasing, including product name or description.
* **Shipping Info** (`shipping_info`, `text`, Required: No): Enter the shipping address and any additional shipping instructions.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, `text`, Required: No): Enter the payment method and details.
* **Order Summary** (`order_summary`, `text`, Required: No): Review and confirm the order details before submission.
