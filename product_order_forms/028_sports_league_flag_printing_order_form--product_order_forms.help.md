# sports_league_flag_printing_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "sports_league_flag_printing_order_form" is designed to gather information from customers for their flag printing order. It is intended to be a comprehensive form that includes all necessary details to fulfill the customer's request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required information in each field.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* Double-check the "Confirmation" field for any errors or inaccuracies.
* Submit the form once you have reviewed and confirmed all the details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info**: Enter your contact information, such as name, email, and phone number, so we can get in touch with you regarding your order.
* **Flag Info**: Provide details about the flag you want to be printed, including any specific requirements or specifications.
* **Artwork**: Attach or describe the artwork or design you want to be printed on the flag.
* **Order Info**: Enter any additional information about your order, such as quantity, size, or material preferences.
* **Review and Sign**: Review your order carefully, making sure all details are correct. Sign and confirm your order to proceed with the printing process.
* **Payment Info**: Enter your payment details to complete the order.
* **Confirmation**: Review the form carefully before submitting it, ensuring all fields have been filled and are accurate.
