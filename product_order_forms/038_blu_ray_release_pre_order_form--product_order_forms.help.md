# Blu Ray Release Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for customers to pre-order Blu-ray releases before they are available on the market.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the form carefully and fill in all required fields.
2. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Enter your shipping address in the "Shipping Address" field, including your city and state/province.
6. Enter your ZIP/postal code in the "ZIP/Postal Code" field.
7. Select your country of residence in the "Country" field.
8. Select the title of the Blu-ray release you wish to pre-order in the "Blu-ray Title" field.
9. Select the release date of the Blu-ray release you wish to pre-order in the "Release Date" field.
10. Choose the format type of the Blu-ray release you wish to pre-order in the "Format Type" field.
11. Select the number of copies you wish to pre-order in the "Number of Copies" field.
12. If you wish to include bonus features, select "Yes" in the "Include Bonus Features?" field.
13. Choose your preferred payment method in the "Payment Method" field.
14. Select your notification preference for when the item ships in the "Notification Preference" field.
15. Finally, select the date you wish to pre-order the Blu-ray release in the "Pre-Order Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`customer_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required): Enter your shipping address, including your city and state/province.
* **City** (`shipping_city`, text, required): Enter the city you would like the Blu-ray to be shipped to.
* **State/Province** (`shipping_state`, text, required): Enter the state or province you would like the Blu-ray to be shipped to.
* **ZIP/Postal Code** (`shipping_zip`, text, required): Enter your ZIP or postal code.
* **Country** (`shipping_country`, text, required): Select your country of residence.
* **Blu-ray Title** (`title_name`, text, required): Select the title of the Blu-ray release you wish to pre-order.
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, date, required): Select the release date of the Blu-ray release you wish to pre-order.
* **Format Type** (`format_type`, select_one, required): Select the format type of the Blu-ray release you wish to pre-order (e.g. Standard Blu-ray, 4K Ultra HD Blu-ray, 3D Blu-ray, etc.).
* **Number of Copies** (`quantity`, number, required): Select the number of copies you wish to pre-order.
* **Price Per Unit** (`unit_price`, number, required): Enter the price per unit (Note: only required if you are reserving multiple copies).
* **Total Pre-Order Amount** (`total_price`, number, required): This field will be automatically calculated based on the number of copies and price per unit entered).
* **Special Edition or Collector's Item** (`special_edition`, select_one, required): Select whether the Blu-ray release is a special edition or collector's item.
* **Include Bonus Features?** (`bonus_features`, select_one, required): Select whether you wish to include bonus features.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select your preferred payment method (e.g. Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, etc.).
* **Notification Preference** (`notification_preference`, select_one, required): Select your preferred notification preference for when the item ships (e.g. Email, SMS, Phone call, etc.).
* **Pre-Order Date** (`preorder_date`, date, required): Select the date you wish to pre-order the Blu-ray release.
