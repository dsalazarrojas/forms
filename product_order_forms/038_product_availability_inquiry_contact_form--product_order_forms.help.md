# product_availability_inquiry_contact_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The product availability inquiry contact form is a tool used to gather information from potential customers or partners about their product availability inquires. This form helps our sales team to quickly reach out to the customer and discuss further details about the product and their availability.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form with your product name, contact name, email, and contact phone number.
2. Enter a brief message describing your availability inquires.
3. Choose how you would like to be contacted (email or phone) for further discussions.
4. Optionally, provide your company name, job title, and job function (if applicable).
5. If you are a sales representative or a marketing representative, confirm your role.
6. Indicate your customer type (consumer or business).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Name (product_name)**: A required field to input the name of the product you are inquiring about.
* **Contact Name (contact_name)**: A required field for you to input your name.
* **Email (email)**: A required field to input your email address for further discussions.
* **Contact Phone (phone)**: A required field to input your contact phone number.
* **Product Availability Inquiry (availability_inquiry_message)**: A required field to briefly describe your product availability inquires.
* **How Would You Like to Be Contacted (preferred_contact_method)**: A required select field to choose how you would like to be contacted for further discussions (email or phone).
* **Additional Comments (additional_comments)**: An optional field for you to provide any additional comments or information.
* **Company (company)**: An optional field for you to input your company name.
* **Job Title (job_title)**: An optional field for you to input your job title.
* **Job Function (job_function)**: An optional field for you to select your job function.
* **Sales Reps (sales_reps)**: An optional select field to confirm if you are a sales representative.
* **Marketing Reps (marketing_reps)**: An optional select field to confirm if you are a marketing representative.
* **Customer Type (customer_type)**: An optional select field to indicate your customer type (consumer or business).
