# Shelf Accessory Hardware Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shelf Accessory Hardware Order Form is a crucial tool for customer service representatives to collect and process customer orders for shelf accessory hardware with accuracy and efficiency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of product being ordered from the "Product Type" dropdown menu.
2.  Enter the quantity of the product needed in the "Quantity" field.
3.  Enter the unit price of the product in the "Unit Price" field.
4.  Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
5.  Enter the date of the order in the "Order Date" field.
6.  Enter the time of the order in the "Order Time" field.
7.  Enter the customer's email address in the "Customer Email" field.
8.  Enter the customer's phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
9.  Enter any additional comments or instructions in the "Order Notes" field.
10.  Select the tool assigned to the order from the "Assigned Tool" dropdown menu.
11.  Enter the delivery address in the "Delivery Address" field.
12.  Enter any delivery instructions in the "Delivery Instructions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Type (`product_type`, select_one, required/optional: false)**: Select the type of product being ordered from the dropdown menu. This will help determine what type of accessories will be included in the order.
* **Quantity (`quantity`, number, required: false)**: Enter the number of units of the product needed. If you need more than 1 unit, enter the correct number. If no quantity is required, leave this field blank.
* **Unit Price (`unit_price`, number, required: false)**: Enter the price per unit of the product. If the product's price is not known, leave this field blank.
* **Customer Name (`customer_name`, text, required: false)**: Enter the name of the customer who is ordering the product.
* **Order Date (`order_date`, date, required: false)**: Enter the date on which the order was received.
* **Order Time (`order_time`, time, required: false)**: Enter the time the order was received.
* **Customer Email (`customer_email`, email, required: false)**: Enter the email address of the customer who is placing the order.
* **Customer Phone (`customer_phone`, text, required: false)**: Enter the phone number of the customer who is placing the order.
* **Order Notes (`order_notes`, note, required: false)**: Enter any additional comments or instructions for the order.
* **Assigned Tool (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required: false)**: Select the tool that will be used to fulfill the order from the dropdown menu.
* **Delivery Address (`delivery_address`, text, required: false)**: Enter the address where the order will be delivered.
* **Delivery Instructions (`delivery_instructions`, text, required: false)**: Enter any special delivery instructions.

## Tips
When filling out this form, please ensure that all fields are accurate and complete to avoid any issues with processing the order. If you're unsure about any information, please contact the customer or your supervisor for clarification.
