# Tumbler Order Form - Help Guide

We are implementing a new form for customers to place orders for Tumblers. This form will help us better understand customer preferences and improve our customer service experience. Please use this guide to ensure you complete the form accurately and efficiently.

The form is designed to be simple and easy to use, with clear instructions and hints to guide you through the process. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select a Tumbler product from the options provided.
2. Enter the quantity of the product you wish to order.
3. Enter your shipping address.
4. Enter your email address (so we can contact you).
5. Enter your phone number (optional, but recommended for us to contact you).
6. Select a payment method (if applicable).
7. Enter the total amount you wish to pay.
8. Add any additional notes about your order (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select A Product** (`tumbler_product_select`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of Tumbler product you wish to order from the options provided. Please choose carefully, as this will affect the shipping and pricing of your order.
* **Order Quantity** (`order_quantity`, `number`, required: false): Enter the quantity of the selected product you wish to order. Please note that this field is not required, but it will help us better understand your needs.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your shipping address so we can ensure prompt and accurate delivery of your order.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your order and any updates or issues that may arise.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed. This is optional, but highly recommended for our communication.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Select a payment method (if applicable) for your order. This will affect the shipping and pricing of your order.
* **Total Amount** (`total_amount`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total amount you wish to pay for your order. Please note that this field is not required, but it will help us process your payment accurately.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes about your order, if applicable. This is an optional field, but it will help us better understand your specific needs.

## Tips

* Please make sure to select a product carefully, as this will affect the shipping and pricing of your order.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
