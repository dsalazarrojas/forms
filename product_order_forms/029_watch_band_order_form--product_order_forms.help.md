# Watch Band Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect customer information and order details to place a successful order for a watch band product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your customer name on the first page.
2.  Enter your email address on the next page.
3.  Confirm whether you are placing an order on the following page.
4.  Select the product you want to order on the next page.
5.  Enter your order size on the next page.
6.  Provide your shipping address on the following page.
7.  Select your payment method on the next page.
8.  Finally, confirm your order on the last page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your customer name?** (`customer_info`, `text`, required): This is where you enter your full name.
* **What is your email address?** (`customer_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address you have on file with your email service provider.
* **Are you placing an order?** (`order_info`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' if you have a watch band order, 'No' otherwise.
* **Which product do you want to order?** (`order_product`, `select_one`, required): Select the watch band product you want to order.
* **What is your order size?** (`order_size`, `number`, required): Enter the size of your order, e.g., 1, 2, 3, etc.
* **Please enter your shipping address** (`shipping_address`, `text`, required): Enter your shipping address for delivery of the watch band.
* **What is your payment method?** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred payment method, e.g., Credit Card, PayPal, or Other.
* **Do you want to confirm your order?** (`confirm_order`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' to confirm your order, 'No' otherwise.
