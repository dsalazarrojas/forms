# Torque Feedback Unit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Torque Feedback Unit Order Form is used to collect detailed information about customer purchases and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps listed above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Product Name** (`product_name`): Enter the name of the product you purchased.
*   **Customer Name** (`customer_name`): Enter your name as the customer who made the purchase.
*   **Order Details** (`order_details`): Provide a detailed description of your order, including any relevant details or circumstances.
*   **Delivery Preferences** (`delivery_preferences`): Specify your delivery preferences, such as delivery date or location.
*   **Customer Email** (`customer_email`): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
*   **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
*   **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`): Enter the date you made the purchase.
*   **Total Cost** (`total_cost`): Enter the total cost of the purchase.
*   **Payment Details** (`payment_details`): Provide any payment details or documentation.
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Specify how you made the payment (e.g. credit card, PayPal).
*   **Payment Status** (`payment_status`): Indicate the status of your payment (e.g. Active, Inactive).
*   **Comments** (`comments`): Provide any comments or feedback about your experience.
*   **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`): Enter any notes or comments from the customer.
*   **Purchase Type** (`purchase_type`): Specify if the purchase was for 'Yes' or 'No' (i.e. personal or business use).
*   **Purchase Reason** (`purchase_reason`): Enter the reason for the purchase.
