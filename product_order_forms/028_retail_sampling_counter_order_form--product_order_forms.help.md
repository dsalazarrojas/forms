# retail_sampling_counter_order_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The retail sampling counter order form is a crucial tool used by retail businesses to collect customer information and order details for sampling purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as the customer, ensuring it is accurate and complete.
2. Provide your email address, making sure it is a valid email address that you regularly check.
3. Enter your phone number, including any necessary country or area codes.
4. Enter product information, such as product type, size, or other relevant details. (This field is optional.)
5. Select the date of the order using the date selector provided.
6. Enter the counter name where you intend to pick up your sample or product.
7. Fill in your address, including house number and street name.
8. Enter the city where the sampling will take place.
9. Enter the state where the sampling will occur.
10. Enter your zip code, ensuring it is accurate and up-to-date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the person completing the form.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **required**): Enter a valid email address that you regularly check.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, **required**): Enter your phone number, including any necessary country or area codes.
* **Product Info** (`product_info`, text, **optional**): Enter product details, such as type, size, or color.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, **required**): Select the date of the order using the date selector provided.
* **Counter Name** (`counter_name`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the counter where you intend to pick up your sample or product.
* **Address** (`address`, text, **required**): Enter your house number and street name.
* **City** (`city`, text, **required**): Enter the city where the sampling will take place.
* **State** (`state`, text, **required**): Enter the state where the sampling will occur.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, **required**): Enter your accurate and up-to-date zip code.

## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure you have all the necessary details required by the form before submission.
