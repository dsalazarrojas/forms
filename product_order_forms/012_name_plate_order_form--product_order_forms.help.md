# Name Plate Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a custom name plate order. You will need to provide your first name, last name, email, phone number, and company name (if applicable) to complete the form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **FirstName**, which is a required field.
2. Enter your **lastName**, which is a required field.
3. Enter your **Email** address. Please note that this should be a valid email address.
4. Enter your **Phone** number.
5. Enter your **Company** name if you are a representative of a company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **FirstName**:
	+ A required text field to input your first name.
* **lastName**:
	+ A required text field to input your last name.
* **Email**:
	+ An optional email field to input your email address.
* **Phone**:
	+ An optional text field to input your phone number.
* **Company**:
	+ An optional text field to input your company name.

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure proper completion of your name plate order.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to contact us.
* This form is intended for personal or business use; please do not use it for any other purpose.
* If you need to order multiple name plates, please fill out this form multiple times.
* If you need assistance with any part of this process, please contact us for help.
