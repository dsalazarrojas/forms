# Shelf Talker Frame Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for orders of custom-designed shelf talker frames.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the required fields for each product, including product name, size, quantity, design, order date, order time, customer email, and category.
2.  If you are a customer, please ensure that you have entered your correct email address in the customer email field.
3.  If you are a staff member, please assign each product to a staff member and select whether to use a tool or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Product Name** (`product_name`): This field is used to enter the name of the product.
*  **Product Size** (`product_size`): This field is used to select the size of the product.
*  **Product Quantity** (`product_quantity`): This field is used to enter the quantity of the product.
*  **Product Design** (`product_design`): This field is used to select the design of the product.
*  **Order Date** (`order_date`): This field is used to enter the date of the order.
*  **Order Time** (`order_time`): This field is used to enter the time of the order.
*  **Customer Email** (`customer_email`): This field is used to enter the customer's email address.
*  **Category** (`category`): This field is used to enter the category of the product.
*  **Description** (`description`): This field is used to enter a description of the product.
*  **Product Name 2** (`product_name_2`): This field is used to enter the name of the second product.
*  **Product Size 2** (`product_size_2`): This field is used to select the size of the second product.
*  **Product Quantity 2** (`product_quantity_2`): This field is used to enter the quantity of the second product.
*  **Product Design 2** (`product_design_2`): This field is used to select the design of the second product.
*  **Order Date 2** (`order_date_2`): This field is used to enter the date of the second order.
*  **Order Time 2** (`order_time_2`): This field is used to enter the time of the second order.
*  **Customer Email 2** (`customer_email_2`): This field is used to enter the customer's email address for the second product.
*  **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`): This field is used to select the staff member assigned to the product.
*  **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): This field is used to select whether to use a tool or not.

## Tips
Please review each field carefully and fill in the required information. If you need help or have any questions, feel free to reach out to our support team.
