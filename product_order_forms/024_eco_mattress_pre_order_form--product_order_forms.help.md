# Eco Mattress Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Eco Mattress Pre Order Form is used to collect information from customers who want to pre-order an Eco Mattress. This form helps us to gather all the necessary details for order fulfillment and communication with the customer during the pre-ordering process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your name and email address to create a contactable account.
3. Select the type of product you are interested in pre-ordering.
4. Choose the quantity of the product you want to pre-order.
5. Provide any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
6. Choose a delivery date and time that works best for you.
7. Enter any additional information you would like to share with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, **Required**): Enter your full name as it should be displayed on the order confirmation.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **Required**): Enter your email address to be contacted for updates and confirmations.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, **Optional**): Enter your phone number for additional contact.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, **Optional**): Enter the address where you would like the order to be delivered.
* **City** (`delivery_city`, text, **Optional**): Enter the city you would like the order to be delivered to.
* **Zip** (`delivery_zip`, text, **Required**): Enter the zip code of the delivery address.
* **Product** (`product_selection`, select_one, **Required**): Select the type of Eco Mattress you are interested in pre-ordering.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, **Required**): Enter the quantity of the Eco Mattress you would like to pre-order (max 10).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, **Optional**): Enter any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, **Optional**): Choose a delivery date that works best for you.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, **Optional**): Choose a delivery time that works best for you.
* **Customer Update** (`customer_update`, text, **Optional**): Enter any information you would like to share with us.
* **Customer Update Email** (`customer_update_email`, email, **Optional**): Enter your email address to be contacted for updates.
* **Customer Update Phone** (`customer_update_phone`, text, **Optional**): Enter your phone number for additional contact.
* **Customer Update Address** (`customer_update_address`, text, **Optional**): Enter the address where you would like to be contacted.
* **Customer Update City** (`customer_update_city`, text, **Optional**): Enter the city you would like to be contacted in.
* **Customer Update Zip** (`customer_update_zip`, text, **Optional**): Enter the zip code of the contact address.
* **Customer Update Delivery Date** (`customer_update_delivery_date`, date, **Optional**): Choose a date for updates.
* **Customer Update Delivery Time** (`customer_update_delivery_time`, time, **Optional**): Choose a time for updates.
* **Customer Update Product** (`customer_update_product`, select_one, **Optional**): Select whether you would like to be updated on the product.
* **Customer Update Quantity** (`customer_update_quantity`, select_one, **Optional**): Select whether you would like to be updated on the quantity.
* **Customer Update Notes** (`customer_update_notes`, note, **Optional**): Enter any additional notes or comments about the order.
* **Customer Update Other** (`customer_update_other`, text, **Optional**): Enter any additional information you would like to share with us.
* **Email Updates** (`customer_update_email_updates`, text, **Optional**): Enter your email address to be contacted for updates.
* **Update Frequency** (`customer_update_update_frequency`, select_one, **Optional**): Select the frequency of updates you would like to receive.
