# Eco Delivery Box Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Eco Delivery Box Order Form is designed to collect details about your delivery preferences for our eco-friendly delivery boxes. This information will be used to fulfill and process your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each field's purpose.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
3. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, text, required: false)
Enter a brief description of your delivery preferences, including any specific details you'd like us to know.

### **Frequency** (`delivery_frequency`, select_one, required: false)
Choose how often you'd like to receive your deliveries: Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.

### **Order Management** (`order_management`, text, required: false)
Please provide any relevant information about your order management, such as any special requests or notes.

### **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, required: false)
Enter any payment details or instructions you'd like us to know about your order.

### **Order Confirmation** (`order_confirmation`, text, required: false)
Enter any additional comments or details you'd like to confirm about your order.

### Tips
* Please fill out each field accurately and completely.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
