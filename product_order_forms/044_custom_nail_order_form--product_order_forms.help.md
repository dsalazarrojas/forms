## Purpose
The custom_nail_order_form is a form designed to collect specific information about a customer's nail order, including product category, size, color, finish, art style, quantity, price, customer contact information, and order details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Start by selecting the Product Category** from the available options or enter a custom category not listed.
2. **Enter the desired Nail Size**, such as the length or width of the nail.
3. **Choose the preferred Nail Color** from the available options or enter a custom color.
4. **Select the desired Nail Finish**, such as matte, glossy, or textured.
5. **Add a unique touch with Nail Art**, such as a design or style.
6. **Specify the Quantity** of nails you would like to order.
7. **Enter the Price**, which will be used to confirm your order.
8. **Provide your Customer Email**, which will help us contact you about your order.
9. **Enter your Customer Phone**, which will allow us to call or text you with any updates.
10. **Enter your Customer Name** to confirm your identity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Category**: Enter the category of the nail product you are interested in ordering (e.g. Acrylic, Gel, etc.). If unsure, leave blank or select "Custom".
* **Nail Size**: Enter the length or width of the nail you would like to order.
* **Nail Color**: Select the color you would like your nail to be. You can also enter a custom color if it's not listed.
* **Nail Finish**: Choose the finish you prefer (e.g. Matte, Glossy, Textured).
* **Nail Art**: Add a unique design or style to your nail.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of nails you would like to order.
* **Price**: Enter the price for your order, or leave blank to be contacted by our team.
* **Customer Email**: Enter the email address we can use to contact you about your order.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number, which we can use to call or text you with updates.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name to confirm your identity and ensure we can tailor our service to your needs.
