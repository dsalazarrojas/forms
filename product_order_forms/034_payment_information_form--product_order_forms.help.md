# Payment Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect payment information from customers during the product order process. It is essential to complete this form accurately to ensure smooth payment processing and effective customer communication.

## How To Complete This Form

- **To complete this form, follow these steps:**

 1. Select your preferred payment method from the list of options provided in the "Select payment method" field.
 2. Enter your customer name in the "customer_name" field.
 3. Fill in your shipping address in the "shipping_address" field.
 4. Provide your mobile number in the "customer_mobile" field.
  5. If applicable, provide your Facebook URL in the "customer_facebook_url" field.
  6. Select whether you desire to make payments "Yes" or "No" in the "desired_payment_mode" field.
  7. Provide the customer's date in the "customer_date" field (format is automatically generated based on the type).
  8. Indicate if you want to schedule payments "Yes", "No" or select multiple options in the "schedule_payments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select payment method**: Choose the payment method that suits your needs.
* **Customer name**: Enter your name as the customer.
* **Shipping address**: Enter your shipping address.
* **Customer mobile**: Enter your mobile number.
* **Customer Facebook URL**: If applicable, enter your Facebook URL for identification or other purposes.
* **Desired payment mode**: Select "Yes" if you want to make payments, "No" otherwise.
* **Customer date**: This field will be automatically formatted as a date. You can enter the date in YYYY/MM/DD format.
* **Schedule payments**: If "Yes", you allow scheduled payments; "No", otherwise; select multiple options for more flexibility.

## Tips

- Please ensure you fill in all required information to avoid any issues with payment processing.
- Double-check your selections and entries for accuracy to avoid any confusion or errors.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact our support team.
- Ensure your Facebook URL, if provided, is accurate to maintain a secure connection.
- Enter your date correctly to avoid format discrepancies.
