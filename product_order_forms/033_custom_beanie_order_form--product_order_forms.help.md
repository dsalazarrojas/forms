# Custom Beanie Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from customers for custom beanie orders.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting, please make sure you have all the necessary information at hand. Take your time to fill out each field carefully, as this will help us process your order accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details** (`custom_beanie_order_form`, text, optional): Please enter a brief description of your custom beanie order.
* **Color** (`order_details`, select_one, optional): Select the color you'd like your custom beanie to be. You can choose from Beige, Blue, or Red.
* **Size** (`order_details_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more sizes for your custom beanie. You can choose from Medium, Large, or X-Large.
* **Customer Info** (`customer`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the customer.
* **First Name** (`customer_first_name`, text, optional): Enter the customer's first name.
* **Last Name** (`customer_last_name`, text, optional): Enter the customer's last name.
* **Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address so we can get in touch with them.
* **Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number so we can get in touch with them if needed.
* **Shipping Address 1** (`shipping_address_1`, text, optional): Enter the shipping address line 1.
* **Shipping Address 2** (`shipping_address_2`, text, optional): Enter the shipping address line 2.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select how you'd like to pay for your custom beanie. You can choose from Credit Card or PayPal.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, optional): Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Notes** (`order_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the order.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the customer.

## Tips
* Please double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, you can always contact us for clarification.
* If you need to make any changes to your order, please let us know as soon as possible so we can update the details accordingly.
