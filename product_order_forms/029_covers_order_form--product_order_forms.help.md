# Covers Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers about their orders, including order details, contact information, payment method, and shipping information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your order information in the "Order Information" page.
2. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" page.
3. Indicate your payment method in the "Payment Method" page (select 'Yes' or 'No').
4. Enter your shipping information in the "Shipping Information" page (this is a text area for notes).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Information (order_info)**: Enter a brief summary of your order.
* **Contact Information (contact_info)**: Provide your name and any other relevant contact details.
* **Payment Method (payment_method)**: Choose 'Yes' if you're paying online or by bank transfer, and 'No' if you'll pay in person.
* **Shipping Information (shipping_details)**: Write a note about your shipping requirements, such as address or special instructions.
