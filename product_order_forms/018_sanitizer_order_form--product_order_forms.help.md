# Sanitizer Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a product order, specifically for sanitizing products such as hand sanitizer, hand soap, and hand wipes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the product title in the "Product Order Form" field.
2. Enter the "Product Info" details in the "Product Info" field.
3. Specify the quantity of the product required in the "Quantity" field.
4. Enter the price of the product in the "Price" field.
5. Select the payment method from the options provided in the "Payment Method" field.
6. Choose the shipping method from the options provided in the "Shipping Method" field.
7. Select the product type from the options provided in the "Product Type" field.
8. Enter any additional notes in the "Notes" field.
9. Enter the order ID in the "Order ID" field.
10. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
11. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
12. Enter your date of birth in the "Date Of Birth" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Order Form** (`product_order_form`, text, required): Enter the product title in this field.
* **Product Info** (`product_info`, text, required): Enter any additional product information in this field.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the quantity of the product required.
* **Price** (`price`, number, required): Enter the price of the product.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the payment method you prefer, such as Visa, Mastercard, or Amex.
* **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, select_one, required): Choose the shipping method, such as Standard, Express, or Expedited.
* **Product Type** (`product_type`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more product types, such as Hand Sanitizer, Hand Soap, or Hand Wipes.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Enter any additional notes about the order.
* **Order ID** (`order_id`, text, required): Enter the order ID.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth.

Tips:
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Double-check the product type selection to ensure it matches the product being ordered.
* If you have any issues or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us for assistance.
