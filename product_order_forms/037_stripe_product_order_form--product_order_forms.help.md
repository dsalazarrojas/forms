# Stripe Product Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information about products ordered through the Stripe payment platform. It is intended for use by businesses or individuals who need to gather product-related data for their customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the product details section (page 2) with the relevant information about the product being ordered, such as its characteristics and features.
2. Enter the shipping details (page 3) to specify how the product should be shipped, including delivery address and any special requests.
3. Provide your customer's contact information in the customer info section (page 4), including name, email, and other relevant details.
4. Leave a review or comment in the review section (page 5) if you wish to give feedback or suggestions about the product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Stripe Product Form** (page 1):
	+ This field is a text input field where you can enter the product's name or description.
* **Product Details** (page 2):
	+ This field is also a text input field where you can enter the product's characteristics, features, or any other relevant details.
* **Shipping Details** (page 3):
	+ This field is for specifying how the product should be shipped, including delivery address and any special requests.
* **Customer Info** (page 4):
	+ This field is for entering your customer's contact information, including name, email, and other relevant details.
* **Review** (page 5):
	+ This field is for leaving a review or comment about the product, which can be feedback, suggestions, or any other type of comment.
