# Payjunction Candles Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to be used for customers to place orders for candles with Payjunction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the contact information:
	* First name: Enter your first name.
	* Last name: Enter your last name.
	* Email: Enter your email address.
	* Phone: Enter your phone number.
2. Specify the order details:
	* Order date: Enter the date of the order.
	* Order time: Enter the time of the order.
	* Order note: Add any additional notes about the order.
3. Select options:
	* Select one: Choose one option from the dropdown list.
	* Select multiple: Choose multiple options from the dropdown list.
4. Provide more details:
	* Date: Enter the date.
	* Time: Enter the time.
	* Address: Enter the address.
	* City: Enter the city.
	* State: Enter the state.
	* Country: Enter the country.
	* Order quantity: Enter the quantity of the order.
	* Order amount: Enter the amount of the order.
	* Assigned tool: Enter the tool assigned to the order.
5. Select payment method and status:
	* Payment method: Choose how you will pay for the order (e.g. credit card, PayPal).
	* Payment status: Choose the status of the payment (e.g. paid, pending).
6. Finalize the order:
	* Comments: Add any additional comments about the order.
	* Total: Enter the total amount of the order.
	* Discount: Enter the discount amount.
	* Notes: Add any additional notes about the order.
