# Breast Pump Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers to place an order for a breast pump. Please follow the instructions below to ensure you complete the form accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2.  Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field. This will be used for order confirmation.
3.  Provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field. This will help us contact you for any further information.
4.  Enter your delivery address in the "Delivery Address" field. This includes the complete address where you would like to receive your order.
5.  Enter your city in the "City" field.
6.  Select your state or province in the "State or Province" field.
7.  Enter your postal code in the "Postal Code" field.
8.  Select your country of delivery in the "Country" field.
9.  Choose your preferred type of breast pump from the "Type of Breast Pump" field.
10. If you prefer a specific pump model, enter it in the "Specific Pump Model" field.
11. If you have a brand preference, select it from the "Preferred Pump Brand" field.
12. If you would like to order any accessories, select them from the "Accessories Needed" field.
13. If you ordered flanges, choose the sizes you need from the "Flange Sizes Needed" field.
14. If you ordered storage bags, select the quantity in the "Storage Bags Quantity" field.
15. Select your desired delivery timeframe from the "Desired Delivery Timeframe" field.
16. If you are a new mother, select "Yes, first time" if this is your first time pumping.
17. Select your expected pumping frequency from the "Expected Pumping Frequency" field.
18. If your pump will be covered by insurance, select "Yes, fully covered" from the "Insurance Coverage" field.
19. Choose your payment method from the "Payment Method" field.
20. If you have any special requests or notes, enter them in the "Special Requests or Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1
* **Full Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for order confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for further contact.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, `text`, required): Enter the complete address where you would like to receive your order.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter your city.
* **State or Province** (`state_province`, `text`, required): Select your state or province.
* **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, `text`, required): Enter your postal code.
* **Country** (`country`, `text`, required): Select your country of delivery.

### Page 2
* **Type of Breast Pump** (`pump_type`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred type of breast pump.
* **Specific Pump Model** (`pump_model`, `text`, required): Enter the specific pump model name or number, if preferred.
* **Preferred Pump Brand** (`pump_brand_preference`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred pump brand, if you have one.

### Page 3
* **Accessories Needed** (`accessories_wanted`, `select_multiple`, required): Select any accessories you need.
* **Flange Sizes Needed** (`flange_sizes`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the sizes of flanges you need.
* **Storage Bags Quantity** (`storage_bags`, `number`, required): Enter the quantity of storage bags you need.

### Page 4
* **Desired Delivery Timeframe** (`delivery_timeframe`, `select_one`, required): Select your desired delivery timeframe.
* **Are you a New Mother** (`new_mother`, `select_one`, required): If this is your first time pumping, select "Yes, first time".
* **Expected Pumping Frequency** (`pumping_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select how often you will pump.
* **Insurance Coverage** (`insurance_coverage`, `select_one`, required): If your pump will be covered by insurance, select "Yes, fully covered".
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select your payment method.
* **Special Requests or Notes** (`special_requests`, `text`, required): Enter any special requests or notes.

### Page 5
* **No Field** (No field available)
