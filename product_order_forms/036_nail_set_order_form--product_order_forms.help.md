## Nail Set Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Nail Set Order Form is a customer-facing form used to capture information and payment details for customers ordering nail sets.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer information:
	* In the **customer_name** field, enter your full name as it should be recorded for the order.
	* In the **phone** field, enter your phone number to facilitate communication.
	* In the **customer_email** field, enter your email address for any necessary contact or confirmation.
	* In the **address** field, enter your address for shipping and processing purposes.
2. Choose your nail set:
	* In the **nail_set** field, enter the name of the nail set you are interested in purchasing.
3. Provide additional details:
	* In the **nail_details** field, enter any additional details or instructions regarding your order, such as size or color preference.
4. Specify your payment method:
	* In the **payment_method** field, select the method you would like to use for payment (options may vary depending on your business policies).
5. Add any comments:
	* In the **customer_comments** field, enter any additional comments or requests you have regarding your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: A text field for entering your full name as it should be recorded for the order. This field is required for order processing.
* **Phone**: A text field for entering your phone number, helping us contact you efficiently.
* **Customer Email**: A text field for entering your email address, which we may use for confirmation or contact.
* **Address**: A text field for entering your address for shipping and processing purposes.
* **Nail Set**: A text field for selecting the name of the nail set you are ordering.
* **Nail Details**: A text field for entering any additional details or instructions regarding your order, such as size or color preference.
* **Nail Price**: A numeric field for specifying the price of the nail set, which we will use for order processing and payment.
* **Payment Method**: A dropdown field with options to select the method you would like to use for payment (options may vary depending on your business policies).
* **Customer Comments**: A text field for entering any additional comments or requests you have regarding your order.
