# heat_recovery_equipment_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The heat recovery equipment order form is a tool used to collect information about the delivery of heat recovery equipment. This form is used to ensure that all necessary details are captured accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the delivery details in the corresponding field.
2. Select the equipment required from the options provided.
3. Add any project notes if necessary.
4. Specify the expected delivery date and time.
5. Enter the contact email and phone number.
6. Provide the delivery address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter the delivery address or location where the heat recovery equipment will be delivered.
* **Equipment Selections** (`equipment_selections`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more equipment options that are needed. These are:
	+ Equipment option 1
	+ Equipment option 2
	+ Equipment option 3
* **Project Notes** (`project_notes`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional project notes or information that may be helpful for the delivery.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the expected delivery date for the heat recovery equipment.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the expected delivery time for the heat recovery equipment.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the person to contact for delivery details.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the person to contact for delivery details.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address where the heat recovery equipment will be delivered.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately to avoid any delivery issues.
- If you have any additional questions or concerns, contact the support team.
