# wholesale_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to enter details about their wholesale orders, including personal information, contact details, and order specifics. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer details, including name, contact information and other relevant details.
2. Add any additional contact details for the customer.
3. Select and add the products you wish to order.
4. Confirm if you wish to include a product image.
5. Enter any additional product details as required.
6. Specify the order quantity for the products.
7. Add any notes or comments about the order.
8. Enter the customer's email address.
9. Add any additional contact information for the customer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **customer_details** (text): Enter your company name and other relevant customer details.
- **contact_details** (text): Add any additional contact information for the customer, such as phone numbers or addresses.
- **order_products** (text): List the specific products you wish to order.
- **select_products** (select multiple): Confirm that you wish to include a product image with your order.
- **product_image** (note): Add any product image information, if selected.
- **product_details** (text): Enter any additional details about the products you are ordering.
- **order_quantity** (number): Specify the quantity of each product ordered.
- **order_notes** (note): Add any additional notes or comments about the order.
- **customer_email** (email): Enter the customer's email address.
- **customer_phone** (text): Add any additional contact information for the customer, such as phone numbers.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are completed.
- If you have any issues or questions while filling this form, contact our sales team.
- Please review your order carefully before submitting for accuracy.
