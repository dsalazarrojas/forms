# Glove Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Glove Order Form is used to gather information from customers to process their glove orders. This form is typically used by authorized personnel to collect and review customer data before sending it to the relevant teams for processing and shipping.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Enter your name and contact information in the `First Name` and `Last Name` fields.
2. Type your `Email` address and `Phone` numbers in the designated fields.
3. Select a relevant date for the order from the calendar in the `Order Date` field.
4. Choose a suitable time for the order from the clock in the `Order Time` field.
5. Select one of the provided options in the `Product` field to indicate if the order is for a specific product.
6. Enter the quantity of gloves ordered in the `Quantity` field.
7. Enter the total cost of the order in the `Total` field.
8. Select which payment terms apply to the order from the `Payment Terms` field.
9. Choose which shipping terms apply to the order from the `Shipping Terms` field.
10. Select the current payment status of the order from the `Payment Status` field.
11. Select the current shipping status of the order from the `Shipping Status` field.
12. Select the payment method used for the order from the `Payment Method` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification or business cards.
* **Last Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification or business cards.
* **Company** (Text, Optional): Enter your company name, if applicable.
* **Email** (Email, Required for processing): Enter a valid email address for contact and communication purposes.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for easy communication.
* **Order Date** (Date, Optional): Select a date that you would like to have the order delivered.
* **Order Time** (Time, Optional): Choose a time slot for the order delivery.
* **Product** (Select One, Optional): Indicate if the order is for a specific product.
* **Quantity** (Number, Optional): Enter the number of gloves ordered.
* **Total** (Number, Optional): Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Payment Terms** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select which payment terms apply to the order.
* **Shipping Terms** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select which shipping terms apply to the order.
* **Payment Status** (Select One, Optional): Select the current payment status of the order.
* **Shipping Status** (Select One, Optional): Select the current shipping status of the order.
* **Payment Method** (Select One, Optional): Select the payment method used for the order.
