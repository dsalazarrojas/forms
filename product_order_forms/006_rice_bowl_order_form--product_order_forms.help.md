# Rice Bowl Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rice Bowl Order Form is designed to collect contact and order information from customers for the purpose of placing an order for a rice bowl product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Rice Bowl Order" field with the specific details of your order.
2. Enter your "Name" in the text field.
3. Provide your "Email" address in the correct format (e.g., [name]@[domain]).
4. Enter your "Phone" number in the format of digits and country code (e.g., +1-123-456-7890).
5. Select your preferred "Payment" method from the options provided:
	* Cash
	* Jotform
	* Other (Please specify in the "Note" field)
6. Choose your preferred "Pickup" method from the options provided:
	* Pickup
	* Delivery
7. If you have any additional comments or notes, please provide them in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Rice Bowl Order** (`Rice Bowl Order`): A text field where you can describe your order in detail.
* **Name** (`Name`): A text field to enter your name.
* **Email** (`Email`): A field to enter your email address in the format of a domain name and a local part (e.g., [name]@[domain]).
* **Phone** (`Phone`): A field to enter your phone number in the format of country code and digits.
* **Payment** (`Payment`): A dropdown menu with three options:
	+ Cash: Pay with cash upon delivery or pickup.
	+ Jotform: Use Jotform for payment (please specify in the "Note" field).
	+ Other: Choose this option if you have a different payment method, and please specify in the "Note" field.
* **Pickup** (`Pickup`): A dropdown menu with two options:
	+ Pickup: Pick up your order in person.
	+ Delivery: Have your order delivered to you.
* **Note** (`Note`): A field to enter any additional comments or notes you have.

## Tips
- Please fill out all required fields to ensure accurate processing of your order.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact us.
- We will do our best to accommodate your request based on the provided information.
