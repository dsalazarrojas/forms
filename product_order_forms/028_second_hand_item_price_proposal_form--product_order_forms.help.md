# Second Hand Item Price Proposal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Second Hand Item Price Proposal Form is used to create a proposal for a second-hand item's price. This form is intended for sellers to gather information from potential buyers about their interest in purchasing a specific item.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the item's title in the "Item Title" field.
2. Describe the item's condition in the "Item Condition" field.
3. Enter the price you are offering for the item in the "Item Price" field.
4. Enter the buyer's name in the "Buyer Name" field.
5. Enter the buyer's phone number in the "Buyer Phone" field.
6. Enter the buyer's email address in the "Buyer Email" field.
7. Enter any additional message to the buyer in the "Buyer Message" field.
8. If the item has a photo, enter the URL or link to the photo in the "Item Photo" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Item Title** (`first_page`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the item you want to sell.
* **Item Description** (`first_page`, `text`, required): Describe the item in detail, including its condition, size, color, etc.
* **Item Price** (`item_price`, `number`, required): Enter the price you are offering for the item.
* **Item Condition** (`item_condition`, `select_one`, required): Select the condition of the item, which is its overall quality and state of repair.
* **Buyer Name** (`buyer_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the buyer interested in purchasing the item.
* **Buyer Phone** (`buyer_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the buyer's phone number for easy contact.
* **Buyer Email** (`buyer_email`, `text`, required): Enter the buyer's email address for communication.
* **Buyer Message** (`buyer_message`, `text`, required): Enter any additional message for the buyer, if desired.
* **Item Photo** (`item_photo`, `text`, optional): If the item has a photo, enter the URL or link to the photo for reference.
