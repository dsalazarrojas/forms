# Prop Replica Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and process information for product replica orders. It is intended for customers to provide their details and for the company to track and fulfill orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer information section with your name, address, and other relevant details.
2. Provide order details, including any specific requests or instructions.
3. Enter your shipping address and contact information for the order to be delivered.
4. Choose your preferred payment method for the order.
5. Add any comments or instructions for the order in the order comments section.
6. Select the order status to indicate the order's current state.
7. Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information (Customer Information)**: This field is for collecting your personal details, including your name and address. Enter your name and address as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Order Details (Order Details)**: Provide any specific requests or instructions for your order in this field. This will help us understand your needs and ensure accurate fulfillment.
* **Shipping Address (Shipping Address)**: Enter your shipping address where you would like the order to be delivered.
* **Shipping Contact (Shipping Contact)**: Enter your shipping contact information, including phone number and any other relevant details.
* **Payment Method (Payment Method)**: Select your preferred payment method from the options provided.
* **Order Comments (Order Comments)**: Add any comments or instructions for your order in this field. This will help us understand your specific needs.
* **Order Status (Order Status)**: Select the current status of your order from the options provided. This will help us track and update your order's progress.
* **Customer Email (Customer Email)**: Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications about your order.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your order details and shipping address are accurate to avoid any delays or issues with delivery.
* If you have any questions or concerns about your order, please contact us through the provided email address.
* We will keep you updated on the status of your order and will notify you when it is shipped or completed.
