# Live Plant Delivery Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to place orders for live plants. It gathers information about the customer, their order, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your information.
2. Review the options available for each field carefully and select the correct ones for your order.
3. Make sure to provide accurate information to ensure smooth processing of your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **first_name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your first name to identify yourself.
- **email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your email address associated with your order.
- **delivery_date** (Delivery Date, date, optional): Select the desired delivery date for your order.
- **delivery_time** (Delivery Time, time, optional): Select the desired delivery time for your order.
- **plant_id** (Plant ID, text, optional): Enter the ID of the plant you're ordering.
- **quantity** (Quantity, number, optional): Enter the number of plants you'd like to order.
- **payment_method** (Payment Method, select_one, optional): Select your preferred payment method.
- **payment_method_other** (Other Payment Method, text, optional): If you have a different payment method, enter it here.
- **comments** (Comments, text, optional): Add any comments or special instructions for your order.
- **shipping_address** (Shipping Address, text, optional): Enter your shipping address.
- **order_notes** (Order Notes, text, optional): Add any additional notes for your order.
- **payment_status** (Payment Status, select_one, optional): Select the status of your payment.
- **order_status** (Order Status, select_one, optional): Select the status of your order.
- **payment_date** (Payment Date, date, optional): Select the payment date.
- **payment_time** (Payment Time, time, optional): Select the payment time.
- **shipping_cost** (Shipping Cost, number, optional): Enter the shipping cost.
- **shipping_cost_tax** (Shipping Cost (Tax), number, optional): Enter the shipping cost tax.
- **tax** (Tax, number, optional): Enter the tax rate.
- **tax_rate** (Tax Rate, number, optional): Enter the tax rate.
- **sub_total** (Sub Total, number, optional): Enter the subtotal.
- **total** (Total, number, optional): Enter the total amount.
- **payment_method_select_multiple** (Payment Method Select Multiple, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple payment methods.
- **payment_method_select_multiple_other** (Other Payment Method Select Multiple, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple other payment methods.
- **comments** (Comments, text, optional): Add comments for your order.
- **assigned_tool** (Assigned Tool, text, optional): Enter the assigned tool.
- **payment_method_select_multiple** (Payment Method Select Multiple, select_multiple, optional): This field is duplicated; please use the other payment method field instead.
- **payment_method_select_multiple_other** (Other Payment Method Select Multiple, select_multiple, optional): This field is duplicated; please use the other payment method field instead.

Note: The following fields were not used: payment_method_select_multiple and payment_method_select_multiple. They are likely duplicates of other fields and have been removed for clarity.
