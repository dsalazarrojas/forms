# Phone Case Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Phone Case Pre Order Form is designed to collect pre order information from customers. This form is used to gather necessary details for processing and delivering phone cases efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your contact information (customer info) including your name and address.
2.  Enter details of the product you are pre-ordering.
3.  Provide the order total, which is the price of the product.
4.  If applicable, enter any custom design information for your product.
5.  Select your preferred payment gateway (e.g., Stripe, PayPal, or Other).
6.  Choose your payment method (e.g., Card, Bank Transfer, or Other).
7.  Enter the date and time you would like to receive your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Phone Case Pre Order**: This field is a text box where you can enter your phone case pre order information.
	+ Enter a brief description of your order in this field.
* **Customer Info**: This field is a text box for customer information.
	+ Enter your name and address in this field.
* **Product Info**: This field is a text box for product information.
	+ Enter details of the product you are pre-ordering in this field.
* **Shipping Address**: This field is a text box for shipping address.
	+ Enter your shipping address in this field.
* **Order Total**: This field is a number field for order total.
	+ Enter the price of the product in this field.
* **Custom Design Info**: This field is a text box for custom design information.
	+ If you have any custom design requests for your product, enter them in this field.
* **Payment Gateway**: This field is a select box for payment gateway.
	+ Select your preferred payment gateway (e.g., Stripe, PayPal, or Other).
* **Payment Method**: This field is a select multiple box for payment method.
	+ Choose your payment method (e.g., Card, Bank Transfer, or Other).
* **Order Date**: This field is a date field for order date.
	+ Select the date you would like to receive your order.
* **Order Time**: This field is a time field for order time.
	+ Select the time you would like to receive your order.

## Tips
* Please fill out each field accurately and thoroughly.
* Ensure all fields have been filled out before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, please contact our support team.
