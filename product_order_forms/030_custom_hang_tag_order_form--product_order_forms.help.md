# custom_hang_tag_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users place orders for custom hang tags related to art or design projects. Users can enter their artwork details, shipping information, payment options, and customer information to complete their order successfully.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your artwork details in the **Artwork** field.
2. Add your shipping details in the **Shipping Details** field.
3. Select your preferred payment options from the **Payment** field.
4. Add any additional notes or comments about your order in the **Notes** field.
5. Enter your email address in the **Email** field for communication or confirmation purposes.
6. Enter your phone number in the **Phone Number** field for communication or confirmation purposes.
7. Review and verify the total cost of your order in the **Total** field.
8. Enter your shipping details in the **Shipping Details 2-6** fields as required.
9. Enter any additional customer information in the **Customer Details** field.
10. Enter your customer information in the **Customer Info** field, if required.
11. Finally, select the desired workflow from the **Workflow** field to proceed with your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artwork**: Enter the name or description of the artwork you want to order a custom hang tag for. 
* **Shipping Details**: Enter your shipping address, delivery date, or any other relevant shipping information.
* **Payment**: Select your preferred payment options, such as payment methods or currencies.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication or confirmation purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for communication or confirmation purposes.
* **Total**: Review and verify the total cost of your order.
* **Shipping Details 2-6**: Enter your shipping details, which might be different from the first Shipping Details field or have a different focus/format.
* **Customer Details**: Enter any additional information about yourself, such as name, address, or contact information.
* **Customer Info**: Enter your customer information, such as order details or preferences.
* **Workflow**: Select the desired workflow to proceed with your order.

## Tips

* Make sure to review and verify all fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your artwork details for accuracy.
* Choose the correct shipping and payment options for your order.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact our support team for assistance.
