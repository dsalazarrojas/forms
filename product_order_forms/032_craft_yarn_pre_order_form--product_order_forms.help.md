# Craft Yarn Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Craft Yarn Pre Order Form is used to gather information from customers for their pre-orders of craft yarn. This includes their contact and delivery details and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer details, including name, address, city, state, and zip code.
2. Enter your delivery details, such as delivery status and method.
3. Enter your payment details, including the method and date of payment.
4. Enter any additional order comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, `text`, required): Enter your address.
* **Customer City** (`customer_city`, `text`, required): Enter the city of your address.
* **Customer State** (`customer_state`, `text`, required): Enter the state of your address.
* **Customer Zip** (`customer_zip`, `text`, required): Enter the zip code of your address.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `text`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, `text`, optional): Enter any delivery details, such as delivery address or instructions.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select your payment method, either Stripe, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of your payment, either Paid or Unpaid.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of payment.
* **Delivery Status** (`delivery_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of your delivery, either In Progress, Completed, or Canceled.
* **Delivery Note** (`delivery_note`, `note`, optional): Enter any notes about your delivery.
* **Order Items** (`order_items`, `text`, required): Enter the details of your order.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes about your order.
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, `text`, optional): Enter any shipping details or instructions.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, `number`, required): Enter the total amount of your order.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of payment.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes about your customer.
* **Comments** (`order_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments or notes about your order.
* **Comments 2** (`order_comments_2`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Comments 3** (`order_comments_2`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your order.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* This form is for pre-orders only; if you need to cancel or make changes to your order, please contact us directly.
