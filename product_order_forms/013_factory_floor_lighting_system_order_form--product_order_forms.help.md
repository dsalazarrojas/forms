# Factory Floor Lighting System Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Factory Floor Lighting System Order Form is used to collect information about a factory floor lighting system order, including delivery and installation details, contact person, and order total.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields with the necessary information for your order.
2.  Ensure that all fields with a red asterisk (\*) are filled in as they are required for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the order, including any specific requirements or notes.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, optional): Enter the delivery address where the lighting system will be delivered to.
* **Installation Location** (`installation_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the lighting system will be installed.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the lighting system should be delivered.
* **Installation Date** (`installation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the lighting system should be installed.
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, select_one, optional): Select how the lighting system will be delivered:
	+ Delivery
	+ Pickup
	+ On-site installation
* **Installation Details** (`installation_details`, text, optional): Enter any specific details about the installation, such as number of lights or fixtures.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, optional): Enter the total amount of the order.
* **Delivery Notes** (`delivery_notes`, text, optional): Enter any special delivery instructions or requests.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for handling the order.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If unsure about any field, please contact the relevant staff member for clarification.
* If there are any issues with your order, please contact our customer service department for assistance.
