# Flawless Nails Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to place orders for various nail services with our salon. Please fill out the form with accurate information to ensure a smooth and efficient order process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the nail services you would like to order.
2. Enter the price for each service, in USD.
3. Choose a tax rate.
4. Provide your customer details (name, email, phone number, and address).
5. Enter your artist's details (name, email, phone number, and address).
6. Select the order date and time.
7. Choose your payment method (Cash or Credit).
8. Enter any additional notes about your order.
9. Confirm your payment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nail Services** (Select One): Choose the nail service you would like to order (e.g., Nail Polish, Nail Acrylic, etc.). This field is required.
* **Nail Services Details** (Select Multiple): Choose multiple nail services you would like to order. This field is required.
* **Nail Service Price** (Number): Enter the price for each selected nail service. This field is not required.
* **Nail Service Price USD** (Number): Enter the price in USD for each selected nail service. This field is not required.
* **Nail Service Tax** (Select One): Choose a tax rate for your order (8% or 10%). This field is required.
* **Customer Details** (Text): Enter a brief description of your customer's details. This field is not required.
* **Customer Name** (Text): Enter your customer's name. This field is required.
* **Customer Email** (Email): Enter your customer's email address. This field is required.
* **Customer Phone** (Text): Enter your customer's phone number. This field is required.
* **Customer Address** (Text): Enter your customer's address. This field is required.
* **Order Notes** (Text): Enter any additional notes about your order. This field is not required.
* **Artist Details** (Text): Enter a brief description of your artist's details. This field is not required.
* **Artist Name** (Text): Enter your artist's name. This field is required.
* **Artist Email** (Email): Enter your artist's email address. This field is required.
* **Artist Phone** (Text): Enter your artist's phone number. This field is required.
* **Artist Address** (Text): Enter your artist's address. This field is required.
* **Artist Hours** (Text): Enter your artist's working hours. This field is required.
* **Order Date** (Date): Choose the date for your order. This field is required.
* **Order Time** (Time): Choose the time for your order. This field is required.
* **Order Notes** (Text): Enter any additional notes about your order. This field is not required.
* **Total Price** (Number): Enter the total price of your order. This field is not required.
* **Payment Method** (Select One): Choose how you will be paying (Cash or Credit). This field is required.
* **Payment Details** (Text): Enter any additional payment details. This field is required.
* **Payment Confirmation** (Text): Enter a confirmation of your payment. This field is not required.
