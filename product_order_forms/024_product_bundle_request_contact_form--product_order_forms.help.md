# Product Bundle Request Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from customers interested in requesting a product bundle. It will help us evaluate their suitability for a product bundle and guide our response and follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Provide your contact details in the "Contact Details" field. This is where you'll share your name, email, and phone number so we can get in touch with you.
2. Describe the "Use Cases" for the product bundle you are interested in. Share specific scenarios where you'd like to use the product.
3. Select the "Lead Qualifier" that best describes your level of interest in the product bundle. Choose from High, Low, or Unqualified lead.
4. Pick the "Resources" you are interested in. Our team will assess which resources are relevant to your needs.
5. Choose your "Lead Status" from New or Other. This helps us determine your place in the sales process.
6. In the "Follow Up" field, provide any additional comments or questions you have. This is an opportunity to share more about your needs and preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details** (Contact Details, text, required/optional): Enter your name, email, and phone number to ensure we can get in touch with you.
* **Use Cases** (Use Cases, text, required/optional): Describe specific scenarios where you'd like to use the product bundle.
* **Lead Qualifier** (Lead Qualifier, select_one, required): Choose High, Low, or Unqualified lead to express your level of interest.
* **Resources** (Resources, select_one, required): Pick the resources relevant to your needs.
* **Lead Status** (Lead Status, select_one, required): Choose New or Other to indicate your place in the sales process.
* **Follow Up** (Follow Up, note, required): Share any additional comments or questions you have, and we'll respond accordingly. 

## Tips

* Please ensure you are not submitting duplicate requests. If you have already submitted a request, do not fill out this form again.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us via email or phone number provided in the "Contact Details" field.
* Our team will review your submission and get back to you with more information or next steps.
