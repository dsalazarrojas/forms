# Gift Delivery Address Collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gift Delivery Address Collection form is used to gather information about the recipient and the gift, including delivery address, contact details, and any special messages or notes. This form can be used by customers or gift-givers to ensure that gifts are delivered successfully and the recipient has all the information they need to receive their gift.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the recipient's details, including their name, address, and contact information.
2. Select a delivery date for the gift.
3. Provide any additional notes or messages that the recipient should be aware of.
4. Enter the sender's details, including their contact information.
5. Add any additional information about the gift, such as a gift message or card message.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Recipient Details** (`recipient_details`, text, required: false): Enter the recipient's name and any other relevant details.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required: false): Select a date for the gift delivery.
* **Sender Details** (`sender_details`, text, required: false): Enter any information about the gift sender, such as their name and contact details.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, required: false): Enter the delivery address where the gift will be received.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, required: false): Select a time for the gift delivery.
* **Delivery Notes** (`delivery_notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes or messages for the recipient.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter the recipient's phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the recipient's email address.
* **Recipient Notes** (`recipient_notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes or messages for the recipient.
* **Sender Notes** (`sender_notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes or messages from the sender.
* **Gift Message** (`gift_message`, text, required: false): Enter a message to be included with the gift.
* **Gift Card Message** (`gift_card_message`, text, required: false): Enter a message to be included with the gift card.
* **Sender Email** (`sender_email`, email, required: false): Enter the sender's email address.
* **Sender Phone** (`sender_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the sender's phone number.
* **Order Info** (`order_info`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information about the gift order.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and complete information about the gift delivery.
* Double-check the delivery date and time to ensure the gift is delivered on the correct day and at the correct time.
* Use the delivery notes field to include any important messages or instructions for the recipient.
* Keep the sender notes field for any information that the sender wants to pass on to the recipient.
* Use the gift message field to include a personalized message with the gift.
