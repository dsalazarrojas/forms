# Nails by Christine On Hand Stock - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to track the on-hand stock of products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the On Hand Stock Form (page 1) with all the necessary details.
2. Enter the inventory count of the product on hand.
3. Select a date for the update (if necessary).
4. Enter a time for the update (if necessary).
5. Enter any email address (if applicable).
6. Enter the phone number (if applicable).
7. Add a note (if applicable).
8. Click the Submit button to save your changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **On Hand Stock Form** (`on_hand_stock_form`, `text`, Optional): Enter a short description or details about the stock update.
* **Inventory Count** (`inventory_count`, `number`, Optional): Enter the quantity of the product currently in stock.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, Optional): Select the date of the stock update (if necessary).
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, Optional): Select the time of the stock update (if necessary).
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, Optional): Enter a valid email address (if applicable).
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, Optional): Enter a valid phone number (if applicable).
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, Optional): Enter any additional information or comments about the stock update.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, Optional): Click to save your changes and submit the form.
