# Fall Pre Order - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fall Pre Order form is used to collect pre-order information for the Fall season. This form will be used to gather customer information and pre-order products that will be available in the Fall, but not yet available for immediate purchase.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **Customer Name** field.
2. Enter your email address in the **Email** field. Please ensure this is a valid email address.
3. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
4. Select the **Pre-order Product** you'd like to purchase from the options provided.
5. Enter the quantity of the product you'd like to pre-order in the **Quantity** field.
6. If you have a shipping address different from the billing address, please enter it in the **Shipping Address** field.
7. If you have any additional information you'd like to provide, please enter it in the **Additional Information** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address. This will be used to contact you regarding your pre-order.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for any further communication.
- **Pre-order Product** (`product_type`, select_one, required): Select the product you'd like to pre-order from the options provided.
- **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the number of products you'd like to pre-order.
- **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, optional): Enter your shipping address if different from your billing address.
- **Additional Information** (`note`, note, optional): If you have any additional information you'd like to provide, enter it here.
- **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): This field is not currently applicable, please do not fill in this field.
