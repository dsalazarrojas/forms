# Quick Release Connector Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quick Release Connector Order Form is used to capture customer details, delivery preferences, and order information for a product or service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Customer Name
	* Company
	* Email
	* Payment Method
2. Optionally, you can provide additional information:
	* Customer Details (optional)
	* Delivery Preferences (optional)
	* Order Confirmation Status (optional)
	* Customer Note (optional)
3. Select the relevant options for:
	* Delivery Status
	* Order Status
	* Product ID
	* Payment Method
4. Enter the following information:
	* Order Date
	* Order Time
	* Delivery Time
	* Quantity
	* Price
	* Order ID
5. Review your order and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, required): Enter the customer's name.
* **Company** (`company`, required): Enter the company name associated with the order.
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, required): Select the payment method used for the order (Cash, Credit Card, or Bank Transfer).
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, optional): Provide any additional information about the customer (e.g., order details).
* **Delivery Preferences** (`delivery_preferences`, optional): Select the preferred delivery options (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Order Confirmation Status** (`order_confirmation`, optional): Indicate the status of the order confirmation (Confirmed, Not Confirmed, or Pending).
* **Customer Note** (`customer_note`, optional): Leave a note for the customer.
* **Order ID** (`order_id`, required): Enter the order ID for the product or service.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, required): Select the status of the order (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, required): Enter the quantity of the product or service ordered.
* **Price** (`price`, required): Enter the price of the product or service.
* **Product ID** (`product_id`, required): Select the product ID for the ordered product or service.
* **Delivery Status** (`delivery_status`, required): Select the status of the delivery (In Transit, Delivered, or Cancelled).
* **Supplier Name** (`supplier_name`, optional): Enter the supplier name for the product or service.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, optional): Enter the time of the order.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, optional): Enter the delivery time.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, optional): Enter the date of the order.
* **Order Confirmation Status** (`order_confirmation_status`, optional): Indicate the status of the order confirmation (Confirmed, Not Confirmed, or Pending).
