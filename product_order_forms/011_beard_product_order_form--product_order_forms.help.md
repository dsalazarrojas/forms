# Beard Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by businesses to place orders for beard care products. Please fill out the form with your business information, product selection, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your business information, including Business Name, Contact Person Name, Email Address, and Phone Number.
2. Select the Business Type that best describes your company.
3. Provide your billing address, including Street Address, City, State/Province, and ZIP Code.
4. Choose whether to use the same address for shipping or provide a separate shipping address.
5. Fill in the shipping address if different from the billing address.
6. Select the quantity of products you need, including Beard Oil, Beard Conditioner, Beard Balm, and Beard Wash.
7. If you need additional items, provide their details in the Additional Items Needed field.
8. Choose your preferred payment method (Credit Card, Check, Wire Transfer, or PayPal).
9. Provide your Business Tax ID (if applicable).
10. Add any special instructions or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`): Enter the name of your business.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_person`): Enter the name of the person responsible for the order.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`): Choose the type of business that best describes your company.
* **Street Address** (`billing_address`): Enter the street address of your billing address.
* **City** (`billing_city`): Enter the city of your billing address.
* **State/Province** (`billing_state`): Enter the state or province of your billing address.
* **ZIP Code** (`billing_zip`): Enter the ZIP code of your billing address.
* **Same as Billing Address** (`same_as_billing`): If your shipping address is the same as your billing address, select "Yes" here.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`): Enter the street address of your shipping address (if different from billing).
* **Shipping City** (`shipping_city`): Enter the city of your shipping address (if different from billing).
* **Shipping State** (`shipping_state`): Enter the state or province of your shipping address (if different from billing).
* **Shipping Zip** (`shipping_zip`): Enter the ZIP code of your shipping address (if different from billing).
* **Beard Oil Quantity** (`beard_oil_quantity`): Enter the quantity of beard oil you need.
* **Beard Conditioner Quantity** (`beard_conditioner_quantity`): Enter the quantity of beard conditioner you need.
* **Beard Balm Quantity** (`beard_balm_quantity`): Enter the quantity of beard balm you need.
* **Beard Wash Quantity** (`beard_wash_quantity`): Enter the quantity of beard wash you need.
* **Additional Items Needed** (`additional_items`): If you need any additional items, describe them here.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Choose how you'd like to pay (Credit Card, Check, Wire Transfer, or PayPal).
* **Business Tax ID** (`tax_id`): Enter your business tax ID (if applicable).
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`): Add any special instructions or notes for the order.
