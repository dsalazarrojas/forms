# Afterpay Electronic Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Afterpay Electronic Product Order Form is designed for customers to provide their details and product specifics to facilitate electronic orders for high-quality electronics using the Afterpay payment gateway.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, fill out your Customer Name, which is a required field. This ensures that your order is associated with your correct identity.
2.  Provide your Business or Personal Email in the contact_email field. This helps Afterpay contact you for any order-related queries or updates.
3.  Enter your Shipping Address, including Street, City, and ZIP Code, in the shipping_address field. This is necessary for accurate delivery of your order.
4.  In the Hardware Details section, enter the Product Model Name in the product_model field. This helps the order processor identify the exact product being ordered.
5.  If you're ordering a specific SKU or part number, enter it in the sku_number field.
6.  Specify the quantity you wish to order in the quantity field. This informs the order process of the exact number of items being ordered.
7.  In the Condition field, select the correct condition for your order from the options provided. This ensures the order is processed according to the product's condition.
8.  Choose the Extended Warranty Options that fit your needs. This protects your purchase and provides additional support.
9.  Select the Payment Gateway used for your order. Afterpay, Stripe (Credit Card), or PayPal are the options available.
10. Calculate the subtotal before tax and shipping in the subtotal field. This is the total cost before any additional fees are added.
11. Enter the Shipping and Handling Cost in the shipping_cost field.
12. If you have any special delivery instructions, provide them in the delivery_instructions field. This helps the courier deliver your order efficiently.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Business or Personal Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email for order-related communications.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, `text`, required): Enter your shipping address for accurate delivery.
* **Product Model Name** (`product_model`, `text`, required): Enter the product model name for accurate order processing.
* **SKU or Part Number** (`sku_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the specific SKU or part number for the product.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, required): Enter the quantity of the product ordered.
* **Condition** (`product_condition`, `select_one`, required): Select the condition of the product (Brand New, Certified Refurbished, Open Box).
* **Extended Warranty Options** (`warranty_options`, `select_one`, required): Choose from Standard 1 Year, Extended 2 Years, Premium 3 Years (Accidental Damage), or No Warranty.
* **Payment Gateway** (`payment_gateway`, `select_one`, required): Select between Afterpay, Stripe (Credit Card), or PayPal for payment processing.
* **Subtotal** (`subtotal`, `number`, required): Enter the price before tax and shipping.
* **Shipping and Handling Cost** (`shipping_cost`, `number`, required): Enter the shipping and handling fee.
* **Special delivery instructions** (`delivery_instructions`, `text`, optional): Provide any special instructions for the delivery, e.g., gate code.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled accurately and completely to avoid any issues with order processing.
* Review the form before submitting to confirm all details are correct.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact Afterpay support for assistance.
