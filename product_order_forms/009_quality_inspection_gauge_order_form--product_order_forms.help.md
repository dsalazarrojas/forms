# Quality Inspection Gauge Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Quality Inspection Gauge Order Form is designed to capture information related to quality inspection gauges orders. This form is used to gather details about the order, such as shipping and customer notes, to ensure accurate and efficient processing.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the gauge orders from the "Gauge Orders" dropdown menu. Please choose all applicable options.
2.  Enter the shipping details, including any special instructions. You can add additional special instructions if necessary.
3.  Add customer notes for the order, including any additional information relevant to the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Gauge Orders (Select Multiple)** (`gauge_orders`, `select_multiple`, **Required: No**):
    This field allows you to select multiple gauge orders. Please choose all the relevant orders for this shipment.
*   **Shipping Details (Text)** (`shipping_details`, `text`, **Required: No**):
    Enter the shipping details for this order.
*   **Special Instructions (Text)** (`special_instructions`, `text`, **Required: No**):
    Add any special instructions for this shipment.
*   **Special Instructions 2 (Text)** (`special_instructions_2`, `text`, **Required: No**):
    Enter any additional special instructions if necessary.
*   **Customer Notes (Text)** (`customer_notes`, `text`, **Required: No**):
    Add any notes or comments for the customer.
*   **Customer Notes 2 (Text)** (`customer_notes_2`, `text`, **Required: No**):
    Enter any additional customer notes if necessary.
*   **Shipping Contact (Select One)** (`shipping_contact`, `select_one`, **Required: No**):
    Choose whether shipping contact is required or not.
*   **Shipping Contact 2 (Select One)** (`shipping_contact_2`, `select_one`, **Required: No**):
    Select whether shipping contact 2 is required or not. This field is a duplicate, but you may need to provide additional contact information.
*   **Shipping Address (Text)** (`shipping_address`, `text`, **Required: No**):
    Enter the shipping address for this order.
*   **Shipping Address 2 (Text)** (`shipping_address_2`, `text`, **Required: No**):
    Enter the alternative shipping address if necessary.
*   **Shipping Phone (Text)** (`shipping_phone`, `text`, **Required: No**):
    Enter the shipping phone number.
*   **Shipping Phone 2 (Text)** (`shipping_phone_2`, `text`, **Required: No**):
    Enter the alternative shipping phone number.
*   **Shipping Email (Email)** (`shipping_email`, `email`, **Required: No**):
    Enter the shipping email.
*   **Shipping Email 2 (Email)** (`shipping_email_2`, `email`, **Required: No**):
    Enter the alternative shipping email.
*   **Shipping Notes (Text)** (`shipping_notes`, `text`, **Required: No**):
    Add any additional shipping notes.

## Tips

*   Please review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
*   If you are unsure about any field, consult the relevant department or contact the customer for clarification.
*   Use this form for orders that require shipping details and customer notes.
*   If you have any special instructions or shipping notes, please include them in the relevant fields.
