# Team Branded Accessory Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form helps the team collect team branded accessory orders and ensures accurate processing and tracking. Fill out this form accurately to guarantee correct order fulfillment and satisfaction of your team member's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your team name as it's displayed in the Team Branded Accessory Order Form.
2. Provide your first name.
3. Provide your last name.
4. Enter your email address for order follow-up and confirmation.
5. Specify the delivery details in the text field.
6. Choose the quantity you'd like to order.
7. Enter the price of the order (if you have a promo code, contact your account manager).
8. Select your preferred payment type: Cash or Credit/Debit.
9. If you have a preferred payment method for the order amount, enter that here. (Note: This field is not required.)
10. Add any additional notes about your order in the text box.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name** (Team Name, text, required): Enter your team's name as it's displayed.
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your team member's email address for order updates and confirmation.
* **Delivery Details** (Delivery Details, text, optional): Specify the delivery details, e.g., date, time, location.
* **Quantity** (Quantity, number, required): Enter the number of accessories ordered.
* **Price** (Price, number, required): Enter the order price. If you have a promo code, please contact your account manager.
* **Payment Type** (Payment Type, select one, required): Choose between Cash and Credit/Debit payment types.
* **Payment Amount** (Payment Amount, number, optional): Enter the payment amount (only if you select Credit/Debit as your payment type).
* **Notes** (Notes, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your order.

## Tips
- Please ensure all required fields are filled accurately to guarantee correct order processing.
- Use the notes field to add any specific delivery requests or instructions.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your account manager for assistance.
