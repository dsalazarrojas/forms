# smart_assistant_speaker_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Assistant Speaker Order Form is used to collect information from customers for ordering a smart assistant speaker.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the customer's personal information, such as their name, email, phone number, and title.
2. Provide the customer's shipping address.
3. Select the payment method (Stripe or PayPal).
4. Enter the product price, quantity, and color of the speaker.
5. Choose the shipping status (Pending, Shipped, or Delivered).
6. Add any customer notes or messages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information**: Enter the customer's name, email, and phone number.
* **Shipping Information**: Enter the customer's shipping address.
* **Payment Information**: Select the payment method (Stripe or PayPal).
* **Order Details**: Enter the product price, quantity, and color of the speaker.
* **Delivery Information**: Choose the shipping status (Pending, Shipped, or Delivered).
* **Customer Message**: Add any notes or messages from the customer.
* **Customer Company**: Enter the customer's company name (if applicable).
* **Delivery Date**: Enter the delivery date (optional).
* **Delivery Time**: Enter the delivery time (optional).
* **Order Total**: Enter the total cost of the order (calculated automatically).
* **Customer Title**: Enter the customer's title (if applicable).
* **Customer Department**: Enter the customer's department (if applicable).
* **Customer Job Title**: Enter the customer's job title (if applicable).
* **Customer Note**: Add any additional notes from the customer.
