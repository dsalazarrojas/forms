# Precision Mold Production Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Precision Mold Production Order Form is used to collect information from customers for the production of molds and other precision parts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the product name in the "Product Name" field. This is the name of the product you would like to order.
2. Provide any additional production specifications in the "Production Specifications" field. This can include any specific details about the product, such as material requirements or design notes.
3. If applicable, upload any relevant files in the "Files" field. These files will be used for reference during the production process.
4. Enter the quantities of the product you would like to order in the "Quantities" field.
5. Select the delivery needs from the options in the "Delivery Needs" field. This will help us understand how quickly you need the product delivered.
6. Enter the customer's contact information in the "Customer Contact" field. This is for internal use and will help us keep in touch with you during the production process.
7. Select the production location from the options in the "Production Location" field. This will help us determine where the product will be produced.
8. Enter the production date and time in the "Production Date" and "Production Time" fields respectively.
9. Enter the delivery date and time in the "Delivery Date" and "Delivery Time" fields respectively.
10. Enter the customer's email address in the "Customer Email" field.
11. Enter the customer's phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Name (Product Name)**: Enter the name of the product you would like to order.
* **Production Specifications (Production Specifications)**: Provide any additional production specifications for the product, such as material requirements or design notes.
* **Files (Files)**: Upload any relevant files for reference during the production process.
* **Quantities (Quantities)**: Enter the quantities of the product you would like to order.
* **Delivery Needs (Delivery Needs)**: Select the delivery needs from the options provided, such as rush order or regular order.
* **Customer Contact (Customer Contact)**: Enter the customer's contact information for internal use.
* **Production Location (Production Location)**: Select the production location from the options provided, such as domestic or international.
* **Production Date (Production Date)**: Enter the date when the product will be produced.
* **Production Time (Production Time)**: Enter the time when the product will be produced.
* **Delivery Date (Delivery Date)**: Enter the date when the product will be delivered.
* **Delivery Time (Delivery Time)**: Enter the time when the product will be delivered.
* **Customer Email (Customer Email)**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone (Customer Phone)**: Enter the customer's phone number.
