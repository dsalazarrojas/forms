# Road Sign Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow customers to order custom road signs by providing all necessary details for production, delivery, and payment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your sign request in the `sign_request` field.
2. Describe your artwork requirements in the `artwork` field.
3. Provide delivery details in the `delivery_details` field.
4. Select your delivery location in the `delivery_location` field.
5. Enter your payment details in the `payment_details` field.
6. Specify your contact information in the `customer` field.
7. Choose your customer type in the `customer_type` field.
8. Enter any customer notes in the `customer_note` field.
9. Select the total number of signs ordered in the `total_signs_ordered` field.
10. Choose the sign size in the `sign_size` field.
11. Choose the sign color in the `sign_color` field.
12. Select the sign shape in the `sign_shape` field.
13. If required, choose whether to request artwork in the `artwork_request` field.
14. Enter the delivery date in the `delivery_date` field.
15. Choose the delivery time in the `delivery_time` field.
16. Select the delivery method in the `delivery_method` field.
17. Choose the payment method in the `payment_method` field.
18. Enter your payment terms in the `payment_terms` field.
19. Enter the total payment amount in the `payment_total` field.
20. Enter your phone number in the `customer_phone` field.
21. Enter your email address in the `customer_email` field.
22. Enter your delivery address in the `delivery_address` field.
23. Add any notes in the `notes` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sign Request**: Enter a brief description of the sign you want to order. (text, required: false)
* **Artwork**: Describe the artwork requirements for your sign. (text, required: false)
* **Delivery Details**: Provide any specific delivery instructions. (text, required: true)
* **Delivery Location**: Enter your delivery location. (text, required: false)
* **Payment Details**: Enter payment details if applicable. (text, required: false)
* **Contact Information**: Enter your name and any other contact details. (text, required: false)
* **Customer Type**: Choose whether you are a Customer, Organization, or Other. (select_one, required: true)
* **Customer Note**: Add any additional customer notes. (text, required: false)
* **Total Signs Ordered**: Enter the total number of signs you want to order. (number, required: true)
* **Sign Size**: Choose the size of the sign. (select_one, required: true)
* **Sign Color**: Choose the color of the sign. (select_one, required: true)
* **Sign Shape**: Choose the shape of the sign. (select_one, required: true)
* **Artwork Request**: If required, choose whether to request artwork. (select_multiple, required: false)
* **Delivery Date**: Enter the delivery date. (date, required: false)
* **Delivery Time**: Choose the delivery time. (time, required: false)
* **Delivery Method**: Choose the delivery method. (select_one, required: false)
* **Payment Method**: Choose the payment method. (select_one, required: true)
* **Payment Terms**: Enter any payment terms. (text, required: true)
* **Payment Total**: Enter the total payment amount. (number, required: true)
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number. (text, required: false)
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address. (email, required: false)
* **Delivery Address**: Enter your delivery address. (text, required: false)
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes. (text, required: false)
