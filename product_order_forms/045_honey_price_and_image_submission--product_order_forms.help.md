# honey_price_and_image_submission - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for submitting general information and a relevant image about honey pricing. It is intended to gather data for a honey market research project. Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions and authority to submit the information. If you are unsure about any field, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Scroll through the form and fill in the required fields.
2. Upload a clear and relevant image related to the honey price.
3. Double-check your submissions for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (text)** (`General Information`, required: false, label: General Information): This field is for entering any general information related to the honey price, such as its origin or production process.
* **Honey Image (text)** (`Honey Image`, required: false, label: Honey Image): Please upload a clear and relevant image of the honey price.
* **Submission Information (text)** (`Submission Information`, required: false, label: Submission Information): This field is for providing any additional submission information related to the honey price, such as its price range or market trend.

## Tips

* Ensure that the image you upload is of good quality and relevant to the honey price.
* Double-check your submissions for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, contact us at form@gic.mx.
