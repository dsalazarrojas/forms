# Powder Release Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Powder Release Product Order Form is designed to collect information from customers or users for orders related to industrial or commercial powder handling processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer information in the "customer_info" fields.
2. Enter your contact details in the "contact_details" fields.
3. Specify the product order in the "product_order" fields.
4. Provide your shipping address in the "shipping_address" fields.
5. Add any additional order notes in the "order_notes" fields.
6. Enter payment information in the "order_payment" fields.
7. Sign the form with your digital signature in the "customer_signature" fields.
8. Repeat steps 1-7 for any additional orders or transactions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer_info** (`customer_info`, `text`, optional): Enter your name, company, or other relevant customer information.
* **contact_details** (`contact_details`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact phone number, email, or other relevant contact information.
* **product_order** (`product_order`, `text`, optional): Specify the product(s) you want to order, including quantity and any special instructions.
* **shipping_address** (`shipping_address`, `text`, optional): Enter your shipping address, including street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **order_notes** (`order_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes or instructions for your order.
* **order_payment** (`order_payment`, `text`, optional): Enter payment information, including method and amount.
