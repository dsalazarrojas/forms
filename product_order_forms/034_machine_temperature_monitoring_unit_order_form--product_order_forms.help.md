# Machine Temperature Monitoring Unit Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to place an order for a machine temperature monitoring unit. Fill out the form to provide necessary information for the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter shipping details in the "Shipping Details" page.
2. Enter product details in the "Product Details" page.
3. Enter order summary, payment details, and other relevant information in the "Order Summary" page.
4. Provide shipping address, contact information, and other relevant details in the "Shipping Address" page.
5. Enter contact information for the customer in the "Contact Information" page.
6. Enter payment method and payment date in the "Payment Details" page.
7. Enter order notes, product type, category, and other relevant details in the "Order Notes" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Shipping Details** (Text, Required): Enter the shipping details of the order, such as shipping date and shipping time.
- **Product Details** (Text, Required): Enter the name and quantity of the product being ordered.
- **Payment Details** (Text, Required): Enter the payment method and payment date.
- **Order Summary** (Text, Required): Enter a brief summary of the order.
- **Shipping Address** (Text, Required): Enter the shipping address of the order.
- **Contact Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the contact person.
- **Contact Phone** (Text, Required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
- **Contact Email** (Email, Required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
- **Product Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the product being ordered. 
  Note: This field is optional and may not be required for all orders.
- **Product Quantity** (Number, Optional): Enter the quantity of the product being ordered. 
  Note: This field is only required if product name is entered.
- **Unit Price** (Number, Optional): Enter the unit price of the product. 
  Note: This field is only required if product name and quantity are entered.
- **Customer Notes** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional notes about the customer.
- **Customer Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the customer.
- **Customer Address** (Text, Optional): Enter the address of the customer.
- **Customer Email** (Email, Optional): Enter the email address of the customer.
- **Customer Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the customer.
- **Unit Number** (Number, Optional): Enter a unique unit number for the order.
- **Order Notes** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional notes about the order.

Please fill out the form carefully and ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting it.
