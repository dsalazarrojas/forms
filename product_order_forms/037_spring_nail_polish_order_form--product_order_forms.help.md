# Spring Nail Polish Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to place an order for spring nail polish. It collects information about the customer's preferences for the polish, including color, size, and quantity. Please review the form carefully to ensure accuracy and avoid duplicate orders.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form and select the fields as required or optional.
2. Provide your name, email, and phone number in the customer information field.
3. Choose your preferred spring nail polish color from the options provided.
4. Select the size of the polish you would like to order.
5. Choose the shape of the nail polish you desire.
6. Select the type of finish you would like for your polish.
7. Enter the quantity of polish you would like to order.
8. Enter the date for your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information (Customer Info)** (`text`, required): This field is for you to enter your name, email, and phone number.
* **Nail Polish (Nail Polish)** (`select_multiple`, optional): Choose one or more of the available spring nail polish colors.
	+ Red
	+ Pink
	+ Blue
	+ Yellow
	+ Green
	+ Purple
	+ Orange
	+ Brown
* **Nail Size (Nail Size)** (`select_one`, optional): Select the size of the polish you would like to order (14ml, 17ml, 20ml, 22ml).
* **Nail Shape (Nail Shape)** (`select_one`, optional): Choose the shape of the nail polish (Oval, Round, Square).
* **Finish (Finish)** (`select_one`, optional): Select the finish for your polish (Clear, Gloss, Matte).
* **Polish Quantity (Polish Quantity)** (`number`, optional): Enter the quantity of polish you would like to order.
* **Polish Quantity 2 (Polish Quantity 2)** (`number`, optional): Enter the quantity of polish you would like to order for the second color (if applicable).
* **Order Date (Order Date)** (`date`, optional): Enter the date for your order.

## Tips
* Please select the required fields (customer information).
* Make sure to choose the correct polish color, size, shape, and finish for your order.
* Enter the correct quantity of polish you would like to order.
* If you would like to order more than one color, please repeat the polish quantity field.
* Make sure to enter the correct date for your order.
