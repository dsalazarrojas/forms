# Retail Data Sync Device Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Data Sync Device Order Form is designed to collect data from retail customers to synchronize device orders with internal systems.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out page 1: Retail Data Sync Device Order Form
	* Enter the required information for your order
2. Fill out page 2: Shipping Details
	* Enter your shipping information
3. Fill out page 3: Internal Hardware Order
	* Enter the details for the internal hardware order
4. Fill out page 4: Shipping
	* Enter your shipping information

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Retail Data Sync Device Order Form** (`page_1`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your order.
* **Shipping Details** (`page_2`, text, required): Enter your shipping address, including your name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Internal Hardware Order** (`page_3`, text, required): Enter the specifications for the internal hardware order, including any specific requirements or details.
* **Shipping** (`page_4`, text, required): Enter your shipping address, including any special instructions or requests.
