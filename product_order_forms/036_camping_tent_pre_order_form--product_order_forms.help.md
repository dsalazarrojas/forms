# Camping Tent Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camping Tent Pre Order Form is designed to collect customer information and order details for camping tents. It's essential to fill out the form accurately to ensure that your order is processed correctly and that you receive your order on time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Customer Information section with your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide your delivery address, including the street address, city, state, and postal code.
3. Select the tent model, color, and number of tents you want to order.
4. Choose your preferred shipping method and delivery date.
5. Select any optional add-ons, such as ground sheet or carrying bag upgrades.
6. Review your order carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Company Name (optional)**: If you are a retail or resale partner, enter your company name.
* **Street Address**: Enter your delivery address, including the street number and name.
* **City**: Enter the city where you want the order to be delivered.
* **State or Province**: Enter the state or province where you want the order to be delivered.
* **Postal Code**: Enter the postal code where you want the order to be delivered.
* **Country**: Enter the country where you want the order to be delivered.
* **Tent Model**: Select the type of tent you want to order.
* **Tent Color**: Select the color of the tent.
* **Number of Tents**: Enter the quantity of tents you want to order.
* **Ground Sheet**: Choose whether you want to include a ground sheet with your order.
* **Tent Footprint**: Choose whether you want to include a tent footprint with your order.
* **Repair Kit**: Choose whether you want to include a repair kit with your order.
* **Premium Carrying Bag Upgrade**: Choose whether you want to upgrade to a premium carrying bag for your order.
* **Expected Delivery Date**: Enter the estimated delivery date for your order.
* **Shipping Method**: Choose the shipping method for your order.
* **Shipping Notification Preference**: Choose how you want to be notified of your order status.
