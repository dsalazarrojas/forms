# retail_sensor_battery_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The retail sensor battery order form is a form designed to guide users in ordering sensor batteries for their specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the "shipping_details" field with the shipping address and any additional delivery instructions.
2. Select the type of sensor batteries you need from the "sensor_batteries" field.
3. Specify the quantity of sensor batteries you require.
4. Enter any additional information about your order in the "order_details" field.
5. Choose whether your order is for "Retail" or "Operations" in the "order_type" field.
6. Provide your customer information in the "customer_info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **shipping_details** (`Shipping details`, Text, Required): Enter your shipping address and any additional delivery instructions to ensure accurate and timely delivery of your order.
* **sensor_batteries** (`Sensor batteries`, Select One, Optional): Select the type of sensor battery you need. This will ensure you get the right batteries for your specific requirements.
* **order_details** (`Order details`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional information about your order, such as special requests or notes.
* **order_type** (`Order type`, Select One, Required): Choose whether your order is for "Retail" or "Operations" to ensure accurate processing of your order.
* **customer_info** (`Customer info`, Text, Required): Provide your customer information for identification purposes.
