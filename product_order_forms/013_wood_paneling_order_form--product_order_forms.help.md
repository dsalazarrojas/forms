# Wood Paneling Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wood Paneling Order Form is a simple form designed to help customers place an order for wood paneling for their homes or offices. This form gathers information about the customer, wood type, quantity, color, wood finish, and installation type required for the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your customer information, including your name and email address.
2.  Select the type of wood you are interested in (Oak, Maple, or Pine).
3.  Enter the quantity of wood paneling you need.
4.  Choose the color of the wood (White, Black, or Brown).
5.  Select the wood finish you prefer (Stain, Paint, or Polish).
6.  Indicate the type of installation you require (Full, Half, or Partial).
7.  Add any additional notes about your order.
8.  Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
9.  Click the "Submit" button to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, required: false): This field is where you can enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself as the customer.
* **Wood Type** (`wood_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of wood you are interested in. Choose from Oak, Maple, or Pine.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required: false): Enter the quantity of wood paneling you need.
* **Color** (`color`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the color of the wood you prefer. Select from White, Black, or Brown.
* **Wood Finish** (`wood_finish`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the wood finish you prefer. Choose from Stain, Paint, or Polish.
* **Installation Type** (`installation_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate the type of installation you require. Choose from Full, Half, or Partial.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about your order.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required: false): Click this button to complete your order.

## Tips

* Please ensure you fill in all required fields to complete the form.
* If you have any questions or issues, feel free to contact us with the information provided.
* This form is for wood paneling orders only.
* Please do not use this form for other purposes.
