### Spring Plant Pre Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect pre-orders for spring plants, and the information gathered will be used to prepare for the delivery of the plants.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Plant Type**: Select the type of plant you are interested in ordering. You can choose multiple options if necessary.
2. Enter the **Plant Name**: This is the name of the specific plant you are ordering, if you are interested in buying a specific variety.
3. Enter the **Plant Quantity**: Type the number of plants you would like to order.
4. Select the **Delivery Date**: Choose the date you would like to receive your plants. Keep in mind that we will contact you to confirm availability.
5. Select the **Delivery Time**: Choose a time slot you would like to receive your plants.
6. If you would like to **Pickup Option**, choose from the following options: Yes, No.
7. If **Pickup Option** is set to Yes, choose a **Pickup Location**: Enter the location for pickup.
8. Enter your **Order Notes**: Any additional comments or requests you have for your order.
9. Enter your **Email**: Your contact email address.
10. Enter your **Phone**: Your contact phone number.
11. Enter your **Name**: Your name as it should appear on the order.
12. Enter your **Address**: Your shipping address.
13. Enter your **City**: Your city of residence.
14. Enter your **State**: Your state of residence.
15. Enter your **Zip**: Your zip code.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Plant Type** (`spring_plant_pre_order`): Select the type of spring plant you would like to order.
* **Plant Name** (`plant_name`): Enter the specific name of the plant you are interested in.
* **Plant Quantity** (`plant_quantity`): Type the number of plants you would like to order.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`): Choose the date you would like to receive your plants.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`): Choose a time slot you would like to receive your plants.
* **Pickup Option** (`pickup_option`): Choose from Yes or No if you would like to pick up your plants in store or at home.
* **Pickup Location** (`pickup_location`): If Pickup Option is set to Yes, enter the location for pickup.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`): Enter any additional comments or requests for your order.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Name** (`name`): Enter your name as it should appear on the order.
* **Address** (`address`): Enter your shipping address.
* **City** (`city`): Enter your city of residence.
* **State** (`state`): Enter your state of residence.
* **Zip** (`zip`): Enter your zip code.
