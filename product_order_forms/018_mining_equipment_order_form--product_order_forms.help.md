# Mining Equipment Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mining Equipment Order Form is a standard template used to capture critical information about mining equipment orders. It is used to ensure accurate and efficient processing of orders, helping to reduce errors and improve communication between the supplier and the customer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Enter the supplier's name and contact number.
3. Select the order date.
4. Choose the type of equipment required (e.g., Drill, Pump, Generator, or Other).
5. Enter the quantity of equipment needed.
6. Provide the total cost of the order (if applicable).
7. Select the payment terms (e.g., Cash, Credit Card, Wire Transfer).
8. Specify the order status (e.g., Pending, Approved, Rejected).
9. Add any additional notes or comments about the order.
10. Assign the order to a specific person or department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Supplier Name
* **Supplier Name** (`supplier_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the supplier providing the mining equipment.

### Contact Number
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required/true): Enter the contact number of the supplier, which can be a phone number or email address.

### Order Date
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, required/true): Select the date the order was placed.

### Equipment Type
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the type of mining equipment needed (e.g., Drill, Pump, Generator, or Other).

### Equipment Quantity
* **Equipment Quantity** (`equipment_quantity`, `number`, required/true): Enter the number of equipment units required.

### Total Cost
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, `number`, required/optional): Provide the total cost of the order, if applicable.

### Payment Terms
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the payment terms for the order (e.g., Cash, Credit Card, Wire Transfer).

### Order Status
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, `select_one`, required/true): Specify the order status (e.g., Pending, Approved, Rejected).

### Notes
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the order.

### Assigned To
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `text`, required/optional): Assign the order to a specific person or department.

By following this guide, you should be able to accurately and efficiently complete the Mining Equipment Order Form. Remember to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
