# supermarket_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "supermarket_order_form" is a form used to collect information from customers for in-store orders. This form helps store staff gather the necessary details to process the order efficiently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Provide your **Customer Info** on the first page by filling in the required fields such as name, contact info, and any other relevant details.
2.  On the second page, enter your **Order Details** and payment information to complete the transaction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, Required): This field is used to capture the customer's contact information such as name, phone number, email, and any other relevant details.
    *   Please provide your name and any other identifying information required to complete the order.
*   **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, Required): This field is used to capture the details of the order such as the order date, order type, and any special instructions.
    *   Enter the date and time of the order, along with any specific details required for the order.
*   **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, Required): This field is used to capture the payment information for the order.
    *   Please enter the payment method, amount, and any other payment-specific details.
*   **Customer Contact Info** (`customer_info_2`, text, Required): This field is used to capture the customer's contact information such as phone number, email, and any other relevant details.
    *   Please provide your phone number and email address for contact and follow-up.
*   **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, Required): This field is used to capture the total cost of the order.
    *   Enter the total cost of the order, including any applicable taxes or fees.
*   **Store Info** (`store_info`, text, Required): This field is used to capture the store's information such as address, phone number, and email.
    *   Provide the store's address, phone number, and email for contact and follow-up.
*   **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, Required): This field is used to capture any payment terms or conditions.
    *   Please enter any payment terms or conditions that apply to the order.
*   **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, note, Required): This field is used to capture any notes or comments about the customer.
    *   Enter any relevant notes or comments about the customer.
*   **Store Hours** (`store_hours`, time, Required): This field is used to capture the store's operating hours.
