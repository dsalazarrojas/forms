## Purpose
The winter skin care wish list form is used to gather information about your skin type, preferences, and concerns. This information will be used to provide you with personalized product recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your skin type from the options provided.
2. Choose the products you are interested in (Cleanser, Eye Cream, Toner, Serum, Moisturizer, Sunscreen, Primer, Exfoliant).
3. Indicate how frequently you exfoliate (Daily, Weekly, Rarely).
4. Choose how often you use sunscreen (Always, Sometimes, Never).
5. Select any additional skin concerns or conditions you're experiencing.
6. Provide a description of your skin tone.
7. Enter how frequently you exfoliate (optional).
8. Enter any additional notes or comments you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Skin Type (form_2)** (`select_one`, required): Select the type of skin you have from the options: Skin Type, Toner, Serum, Moisturizer, Sunscreen, Primer.
* **Product Preferences (form_3)** (`select_multiple`, required): Choose the products you are interested in using. Select all that apply from the options: Cleanser, Eye Cream, Toner, Serum, Moisturizer, Sunscreen, Primer, Exfoliant.
* **Skin Tone (form_4)** (`select_one`, required): Select your skin tone from the options: Fair, Medium, Tan, Dark.
* **Skin Tone Description (form_5)** (`text`, required): Provide a description of your skin tone.
* **Skin Condition (form_6)** (`select_one`, required): Select any skin conditions you are experiencing from the options: Acne, Aging, Hyper-pigmentation, Oily, Dry, Sensitive.
* **Skin Concerns (form_7)** (`select_multiple`, required): Select any skin concerns you are experiencing from the options: Acne, Aging, Hyper-pigmentation, Oily, Dry, Sensitive.
* **Exfoliant Frequency (form_8)** (`text`, required): Enter the frequency of exfoliant use.
* **Exfoliant Method (form_10)** (`select_one`, required): Select the method of exfoliant use from the options: Daily, Weekly, Rarely.
* **Sunscreen Use (form_11)** (`select_one`, required): Select how often you use sunscreen from the options: Always, Sometimes, Never.
