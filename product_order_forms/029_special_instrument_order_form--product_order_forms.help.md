# Special Instrument Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Special Instrument Order Form is designed to gather information to process customer orders accurately and efficiently. Please use this form for ordering a special instrument.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the customer information section with your name and delivery address.
2.  Choose the type of instrument you want to order from the list.
3.  If the instrument type you want to order is not on the list, enter the instrument name in the "Instrument Name" field.
4.  Provide any additional delivery details in the "Delivery Details" field.
5.  Enter your delivery address in the "Delivery Address" field.
6.  Select your payment method from the list.
7.  If required, upload any additional files related to the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Instrument Type** (`Instrument Type`, select_one): Choose the type of instrument you want to order (Guitar, Bass, or Ukulele).
* **Instrument Name** (`Instrument Name`, text): Enter the name of the instrument if it's not listed.
* **Delivery Details** (`Delivery Details`, text): Provide any additional details about the delivery.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery address`, text): Enter your delivery address.
* **Shipping Details** (`Shipping Details`, text): Provide any additional information about the shipping method.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, select_multiple): Choose between Credit Card and PayPal as payment options.
* **Upload Files** (`Upload files`, text): Upload any additional files related to the order.
* **Other Files** (`other files`, text): Upload any other files related to the order.
* **File Uploads** (`File Uploads`, text): Upload any other files related to the order.
