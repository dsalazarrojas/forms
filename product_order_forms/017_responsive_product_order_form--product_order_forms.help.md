# Responsive Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Responsive Product Order Form" is a comprehensive form designed to gather essential information for order processing and customer details. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer details, including name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide billing and shipping addresses, including the address type.
3. List the order items, including quantity and price.
4. Select the payment method (e.g., Credit Card, PayPal, or Stripe).
5. Choose the order status (e.g., Pending, Processing, or Completed).
6. Enter the date and time of sale.
7. Enter the customer's email and phone number.
8. Provide the customer's company name (if applicable).
9. Enter the customer's address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Details
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Company** (`customer_company`, text, required): Enter the customer's company name (if applicable).

### Billing Details
* **Billing Phone** (`billing_phone`, text, optional): Enter the billing contact's phone number.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, text, required): Enter the billing address.
* **Billing Company** (`billing_company`, text, optional): Enter the billing company name (if applicable).

### Shipping Details
* **Shipping Phone** (`shipping_phone`, text, optional): Enter the shipping contact's phone number.
* **Shipping Company** (`shipping_company`, text, optional): Enter the shipping company name (if applicable).
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, optional): Enter the shipping address.

### Payment and Order Information
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the payment method used (e.g., Credit Card, PayPal, or Stripe).
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_one, required): Choose the order status (e.g., Pending, Processing, or Completed).
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, required): Enter the total order amount.
* **Order Items** (`order_items`, text, required): List the order items, including quantity and price.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, required): Enter payment details (if applicable).
* **Date Of Sale** (`date_of_sale`, date, required): Enter the date of sale.
* **Time Of Sale** (`time_of_sale`, time, required): Enter the time of sale.

### Additional Information
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, text, required): Enter any additional customer notes or comments.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, required): Enter the customer's address.
* **Billing Phone** (`billing_phone`, text, required): Enter the billing contact's phone number.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, text, required): Enter the billing address.
* **Shipping Company** (`shipping_company`, text, required): Enter the shipping company name (if applicable).
* **Shipping Phone** (`shipping_phone`, text, required): Enter the shipping contact's phone number.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required): Enter the shipping address.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure you have filled in all required fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer back to this help guide for clarification.
* If you need assistance, contact our support team for help.
