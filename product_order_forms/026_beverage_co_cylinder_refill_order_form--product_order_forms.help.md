# Beverage CO₂ Cylinder Refill Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beverage CO₂ Cylinder Refill Order Form is designed to collect information for the CO₂ cylinder refilling process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the customer name, email, and phone number fields with your full name and contact information.
2. If you are a business, enter your company name.
3. Provide your delivery address, city, state, and ZIP code for the cylinder delivery.
4. Select the cylinder size and quantity you need refilled.
5. Choose the refill type and delivery method that suits your needs.
6. Select a preferred delivery date and time (if applicable).
7. Specify any special instructions for delivery access.
8. Choose a payment method.
9. Indicate if this is a rush order.
10. Describe the current condition of your cylinder.
11. Enter any special requests or additional comments about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address** (`customer_email`, email, required): We will send order confirmation here.
* **Phone Number** (`customer_phone`, text, required): For delivery coordination.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Name of your business if applicable.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, required): Full address for cylinder delivery.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): City for delivery.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): State for delivery.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (`postal_code`, text, required): Postal code for delivery.
* **Cylinder Size** (`cylinder_size`, select_one, required): What size cylinder do you need refilled.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): How many cylinders need refilling.
* **Refill Type** (`refill_type`, select_one, required): What type of service do you need.
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, select_one, required): How would you like to receive your cylinder.
* **Preferred Delivery Date** (`preferred_delivery_date`, date, required): When would you like delivery.
* **Preferred Delivery Time** (`preferred_delivery_time`, time, optional): What time works best for you.
* **Access Instructions** (`access_instructions`, text, optional): Any special instructions for delivery access.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): How would you like to pay.
* **Is This a Rush Order** (`rush_order`, select_one, required): Do you need expedited service.
* **Current Cylinder Condition** (`cylinder_condition`, select_one, optional): What is the condition of your cylinder.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Any additional requirements or notes.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Any other information about your order.
