# Template Of 2023 Girl Scout LBB Initial Cookie Order - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information related to Girl Scout cookies, such as orders, rewards, contact details, and other relevant data. It is primarily meant for the Girl Scouts to gather information and feedback from their customers, likely for internal use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Parent Information (page 1)**: This section is optional, but if you want to provide additional information about the parent, fill in the relevant details.
2. **Cookie Orders (page 2)**: Enter your cookie orders here, including the type of cookies, quantity, and any specific requests or comments.
3. **Contact Info (page 3)**: Provide your contact details, including your name, email, and phone number, so that the Girl Scouts can get in touch with you.
4. **Rewards (page 4)**: If you've earned any rewards or have any feedback, share it here.
5. **Review (page 5)**: Share your overall experience and any suggestions for improvement.
6. **Payment Info (page 6)**: Enter any relevant payment information, such as order total or payment method.
7. **Integrations (page 7)**: This section is optional, but if you have any integration requests or preferences, mention them here.
8. **Submit (page 8)**: Double-check your information and submit the form.
9. **Review 2 (page 9)**: Similar to the "Review" section, share your additional thoughts or concerns.
10. **Cookie Orders 2 (page 10)**: Enter any additional cookie orders or changes.
11. **Parent Info 2 (page 11)**: Enter any additional information about the parent.
