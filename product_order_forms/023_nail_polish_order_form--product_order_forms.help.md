# Nail Polish Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Nail Polish Order Form" is designed to collect information for placing an order for nail polish. This form is used to gather the required details for processing the order. To ensure accurate and efficient processing, please use this guide to complete the form correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the desired polish color from the available options.
2. Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
3. Provide the customer's email address in the "Customer Email" field.
4. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Order Notes" field.
5. Review the form to ensure all required fields are filled in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Polish Color** (`polish_color`, `select_one`, required): Select the color of the desired nail polish from the options provided.

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the customer.

* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the customer.

* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments for the order, such as special requests or instructions.

## Tips

* If you need to place an order without selecting a polish color, please contact the support team for assistance.
* Please ensure the email address provided is accurate and valid.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, please contact the support team for help.
