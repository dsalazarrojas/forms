# Seasonal Gift Wrapping Set Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seasonal Gift Wrapping Set Order Form is designed to collect information for orders of gift wrapping sets. This form is used to process customer orders, including details about the order, customer, and shipping information. It is essential to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that the order is processed correctly and delivered on time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Order Details** field with the specific details of the order.
2. Enter the **Product Name** of the gift item being wrapped.
3. Select the **Wrapping Kit** that will be used for the gift.
4. Enter the **Gift Wraper** that will be used for the gift.
5. Choose the **Quantity** of gift sets to be ordered.
6. Select the color of the gift wrapping **Color**.
7. Add any additional **Notes** about the order.
8. Select the **Order Type**, such as Gift Wrapping Set, Gift Wrapping Kit, or Holiday Wrapping Set.
9. Enter the **Date And Time** for the delivery of the order.
10. Choose the **Delivery Date** and time for the order.
11. Enter the **Shipping Cost** for the order.
12. Enter the **Shipping Address** of the customer.
13. Enter the **Customer Email** to contact the customer.
14. Enter the **Customer Phone** number to contact the customer.
15. Select the **Shipping Method**, such as Standard or Express.
16. Select the **Payment Method**, such as Credit Card or PayPal.
17. Enter the **Order Status**, such as New Order or Completed.
18. Add any additional **Comments** about the order.
19. Enter the **Customer Address**, **Customer City**, **Customer Country**, **Customer State**, and **Customer Zip**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details** (1) (Required): Enter the specific details of the order, such as the gift item and any special instructions.
* **Product Name** (2) (Optional): Enter the name of the product being wrapped.
* **Wrapping Kit** (3) (Required): Select the wrapping kit to be used for the gift.
* **Gift Wraper** (4) (Required): Select the gift wraper to be used for the gift.
* **Quantity** (5) (Required): Enter the number of gift sets to be ordered.
* **Color** (6) (Optional): Select the color of the gift wrapping.
* **Notes** (7) (Optional): Add any additional notes about the order.
* **Order Type** (8) (Optional): Select the type of order, such as Gift Wrapping Set, Gift Wrapping Kit, or Holiday Wrapping Set.
* **Date And Time** (9) (Required): Enter the date and time for the delivery of the order.
* **Delivery Date** (10) (Required): Enter the date for the delivery of the order.
* **Delivery Time** (11) (Required): Enter the time for the delivery of the order.
* **Shipping Cost** (12) (Required): Enter the shipping cost for the order.
* **Shipping Address** (13) (Required): Enter the shipping address of the customer.
* **Customer Email** (14) (Required): Enter the customer email to contact the customer.
* **Customer Phone** (15) (Required): Enter the customer phone number to contact the customer.
* **Shipping Method** (16) (Optional): Select the shipping method, such as Standard or Express.
* **Payment Method** (17) (Optional): Select the payment method, such as Credit Card or PayPal.
* **Order Status** (18) (Optional): Enter the order status, such as New Order or Completed.
* **Comments** (19) (Optional): Add any additional comments about the order.
* **Customer Address** (20) (Optional): Enter the customer address.
* **Customer City** (21) (Optional): Enter the customer city.
* **Customer Country** (22) (Optional): Enter the customer country.
* **Customer State** (23) (Optional): Enter the customer state.
* **Customer Zip** (24) (Optional): Enter the customer zip code.
* **Notes 2** (25) (Optional): Add any additional notes about the order.
