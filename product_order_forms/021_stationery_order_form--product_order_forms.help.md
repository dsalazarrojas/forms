# Stationery Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Stationery Order Form is used to place orders for stationery products. Please fill out this form with your order details to ensure timely delivery of your products.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your order is processed correctly.
2. If you have any questions or need assistance, do not hesitate to contact the sales department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for order updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for order updates.
* **Stationery Product** (`stationery_product`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of stationery product you want to order. Options are 'Yes' and 'No'. Please select 'Yes' if you want to order stationery products.
* **Stationery Product Quantity** (`stationery_product_quantity`, number, required: false): Enter the quantity of stationery products you want to order.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when you would like to receive your order.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes or instructions for your order.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields for accurate order processing.
* For stationery product quantity, please ensure you enter a valid number.
* For delivery date, please enter a date in the correct format (MM/DD/YY).
