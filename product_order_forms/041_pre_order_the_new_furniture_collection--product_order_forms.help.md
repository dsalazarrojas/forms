# Pre Order The New Furniture Collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Order The New Furniture Collection form is designed to collect customer information and order details for the new furniture collection. This form will help customers express their interest in purchasing items from the collection before they are released to the public.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Add any additional order details in the "Order Details" field.
5. Specify the product name you are interested in purchasing in the "Product Name" field.
6. Enter the product price you would like to purchase it for in the "Product Price" field.
7. Choose the quantity of the product you would like to order in the "Quantity" field.
8. Calculate the total price by multiplying the product price by the quantity.
9. Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" options.
10. Choose the status of your payment in the "Payment Status" field (if applicable).
11. Specify the status of your delivery in the "Delivery Status" field.
12. Add any additional notes about your order in the "Notes" field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Submit**: Submit this form to send your pre-order request.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Order Details**: Add any additional information about your order, such as special requests or questions.
* **Product Name**: Select the product you are interested in pre-ordering.
* **Product Price**: Enter the price you are willing to pay for the product.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of products you would like to purchase.
* **Total Price**: The total price is calculated by multiplying the product price by the quantity. This field is automatically populated based on your input.
* **Payment Method**: Select the method you would like to use for payment (Stripe, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
* **Payment Status**: Choose the status of your payment (Paid, Pending, or Refunded).
* **Delivery Status**: Choose the status of your delivery (Invoiced, Pending, Shipped, or Cancelled).
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
