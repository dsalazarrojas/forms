# Electronics Product Filter Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to filter electronic products based on your preferences. Fill out the form with your desired criteria, and the form will provide you with relevant results.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the category of the electronic product you are looking for (e.g., Computer, Phone, etc.).
2. Choose the price range of the product (e.g., '0', '500', etc.).
3. Select the operating system of the product (e.g., Windows, macOS, etc.).
4. Choose the brand of the product (e.g., Apple, Samsung, etc.).
5. Select the color of the product (e.g., Black, White, etc.).
6. Choose the size of the product (e.g., Small, Medium, etc.).
7. Select the resolution of the product (e.g., 4K, Full HD, etc.).
8. Choose the weight of the product (e.g., Lightweight, Heavyweight, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Filter By Category**: Select one or multiple categories of electronic products you are interested in (e.g., Computer, Phone, etc.).
* **Price Range**: Choose the price range of the product you are looking for (e.g., '0', '500', etc.). 
* **Operating System**: Select the operating system of the product you are interested in (e.g., Windows, macOS, etc.).
* **Brand**: Choose the brand of the product you are looking for (e.g., Apple, Samsung, etc.).
* **Color**: Select the color of the product you are interested in (e.g., Black, White, etc.).
* **Size**: Choose the size of the product you are looking for (e.g., Small, Medium, etc.).
* **Resolution**: Select the resolution of the product you are interested in (e.g., 4K, Full HD, etc.).
* **Weight**: Choose the weight of the product you are looking for (e.g., Lightweight, Heavyweight, etc.).

Note: The price range is a required field, which means you must select at least one option to submit the form. Other fields are optional, but you can choose as many or as few as you like.
