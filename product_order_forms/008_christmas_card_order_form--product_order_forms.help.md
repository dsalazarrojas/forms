# Christmas Card Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you create a customized Christmas card order. It will guide you through the necessary information to ensure that your order is placed correctly and on time.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the **Order Information** section with your name, email, and phone number. This information is required to process your order.
2. Choose your **Order Date** by selecting the date you would like to receive your order.
3. In the **Card Selection** section, choose the design style that suits your preference from the available options.
4. Select the **Card Size** that fits your needs.
5. Choose any **Personalization Options** you would like to include on your card.
6. Enter the **Quantity** of cards you would like to order.
7. Select the **Paper Quality** of the card, which affects the overall look and feel.
8. Choose whether to include **Envelopes** with your order.
9. Enter a **Custom Message or Greeting** if you would like to include a personal touch.
10. Fill out the **Shipping Address** section with your shipping information.
11. Select your preferred **Shipping Method** to ensure timely delivery.
12. Set a **Needed by Date** for your order, which is the date you need to receive the order by.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Order Date**: Select the date you would like to receive your order by.
  * This is a required field to ensure that your order is processed on time.
  * Enter the date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

- **First Name**: Enter your first name.
  * This is a required field for the order to be processed correctly.
  * Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification or documents.

- **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
  * This is a required field for the order to be processed correctly.
  * Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification or documents.

- **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
  * This is a required field for communication and order updates.
  * Please use the format: `username@domain.com`.

- **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
  * This is a required field for order updates and delivery notifications.
  * Please use the format: `123-456-7890`.

- **Company Name**: Enter your company or organization name (optional).
  * This is an optional field for internal use.
  * Please enter the name of your company or organization.

- **Card Selection**: Choose the design style of your Christmas card.
  * This is a required field to ensure that you receive the correct design.
  * Choose from the available options: Classic Red and Green, Modern Minimalist, Religious Theme, Corporate Elegance, Festive Family, Rustic Charm, or Winter Wonderland.

- **Card Size**: Select the size of your Christmas card.
  * This is a required field to ensure that you receive the correct size.
  * Choose from the available options: 4x6 inches, 5x7 inches, Folded 5x7, Square 6x6, or Custom.

- **Personalization Options**: Choose any personalization options you would like to include on your card.
  * This is an optional field for customization.
  * Select all that apply: Custom names, Custom message, Family photo, Company logo, or Custom colors.

- **Quantity to Order**: Enter the number of cards you would like to order.
  * This is a required field to process your order correctly.
  * Please enter the correct number of cards.

- **Paper Quality**: Select the quality of the paper used for your card.
  * This is a required field to ensure that you receive the correct quality.
  * Choose from the available options: Standard, Premium cardstock, Matte finish, Glossy finish, or Recycled paper.

- **Include Envelopes**: Choose whether to include envelopes with your order.
  * This is a required field to decide on the packaging.
  * Choose from the available options: Yes, standard white, Yes, kraft paper, Yes, colored envelopes, or No envelopes.

- **Custom Message or Greeting**: Enter a custom message or greeting for your card (optional).
  * This is an optional field for personalization.
  * Please keep the message under 100 characters.

- **Shipping Address**: Fill out the shipping address for your order.
  * This is a required field for order delivery.
  * Please enter your address in the format: `Street Address, City, State, ZIP`.

- **Shipping Method**: Select your preferred shipping method.
  * This is a required field to ensure timely delivery.
  * Choose from the available options: Standard (5-7 business days), Express (2-3 business days), Overnight, or Local pickup.

- **Needed by Date**: Set a date for your order delivery.
  * This is a required field to ensure timely delivery.
  * Enter the date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields correctly to ensure that your order is processed correctly.
* Double-check your order details before submission to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our customer support team.
