# Robotics Vision Lens Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect customer information and lens selection for a custom robotic vision lens order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer details, including your name, email, phone number, and any other relevant information.
2. Provide your shipping details, including street, city, state, zip, and country.
3. Select the lens type(s) you are interested in purchasing.
4. Add any comments or notes about your order.
5. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details:**
  * (`customer_details`, text, not required): Enter your name, title, company, and other relevant information.
* **Shipping Details:**
  * (`shipping_details`, text, not required): Enter your shipping address, including street, city, state, zip, and country.
* **Lens Selection:**
  * (`lens_selection`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more lenses to order.
* **Accept Terms:**
  * (`terms_and_conditions`, note, not required): Please review and accept the terms and conditions of your order.
* **Email:**
  * (`customer_email`, email, not required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone:**
  * (`customer_phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **Street:**
  * (`shipping_street`, text, not required): Enter your shipping street address.
* **City:**
  * (`shipping_city`, text, not required): Enter your shipping city.
* **Zip:**
  * (`shipping_zip`, text, not required): Enter your shipping zip code.
* **State:**
  * (`shipping_state`, text, not required): Enter your shipping state.
* **Country:**
  * (`shipping_country`, text, not required): Enter your shipping country.
* **Comments:**
  * (`customer_comments`, text, not required): Add any comments or notes about your order.
* **Submit:**
  * (`form_submit`, text, not required): Review and submit your form.

Note: Some fields are not required, but it's recommended to fill them in for accurate order fulfillment and communication.
