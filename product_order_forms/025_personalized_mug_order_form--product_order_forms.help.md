# Personalized-Mug-Order-Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for ordering personalized mugs. Please fill out the form with accurate details to ensure smooth processing of your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name in the "customer-name" field.
2. Describe any special features you would like on your mug in the "product-features" field.
3. Choose your preferred shipping method ("Yes" for shipping or "No" for pick-up) in the "shipping-method" field.
4. Select the date and time of your order in the "order-date" and "order-time" fields, respectively.
5. Choose your preferred payment method ("Yes" for payment or "No" for payment on delivery) in the "payment-method" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "customer-email" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "customer-phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer-Name** (Text, Optional): This field is used to collect your name for the order.
* **Product-Features** (Text, Optional): Describe any special features you would like on your mug, such as a name, initials, or message.
* **Shipping-Method** (Select One, Optional): Choose "Yes" to have your order shipped to you, or "No" to pick up your order in-store.
* **Order-Date** (Date, Optional): Select the date you would like to receive your order.
* **Order-Time** (Time, Optional): Select the time you would like to receive your order.
* **Payment-Method** (Select One, Optional): Choose "Yes" to pay on delivery, or "No" to pay now.
* **Customer-Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you about your order.
* **Customer-Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you about your order.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate processing of your order.
* If you have any special requests or questions, please contact our customer service team.
* Please review your order carefully before submission to avoid any errors.
