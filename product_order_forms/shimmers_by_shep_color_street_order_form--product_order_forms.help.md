# Shimmers By Shep Color Street Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect relevant information from customers to facilitate successful order placement and fulfillment of their product requests.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Customer Information

*   **Full name**: Enter your legal name.
*   **Email address**: Enter the email address where we'll send a confirmation.
*   **Phone number**: Optional, but recommended for easy communication.
*   **Delivery address**: Enter your address for order shipping (Street, city, state, ZIP).
### Step 2: Order Details

*   **Order date**: Date you place the order.
*   **Item or product name**: Enter the exact name of the item you're ordering.
*   **Product variant**: Choose the variant you want.
*   **Quantity**: Enter number of items.
*   **Price per item**: Enter the price per item in USD.
*   **Preferred delivery method**: Choose your shipping option.
### Step 3: Payment and Special Instructions

*   **Gift wrap**: Choose if you want gift wrapping.
*   **Discount code**: If you have a promotional code, enter it here.
*   **Order priority**: Select urgency level.
*   **Payment method**: Choose how you will pay.
*   **Terms and Conditions**: Agree to the terms before submitting.
*   **Additional comments**: Any other information you'd like to share.
