# Gaming System Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect order-related information from customers for new orders.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with your information.
2. Select your preferred payment method and game system.
3. Optionally, provide any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`prophets_email`, `email`, optional): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional details about your order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, optional): Select your preferred payment method (Cash or Card).
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, `text`, optional): If you have any specific payment instructions, enter them here.
* **Game System** (`game_system`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the game system you are interested in (PS4, Xbox, Switch).
* **Date of Purchase** (`date_of_purchase`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you would like to purchase the game system.
* **Time of Purchase** (`time_of_purchase`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you would like to purchase the game system.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, optional): Click to submit your order.
* **First Name Check** (`first_name_check`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check if your first name is correct.
* **Last Name Check** (`last_name_check`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check if your last name is correct.
* **Email Check** (`email_check`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check if your email is correct.
* **Phone Check** (`phone_check`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check if your phone number is correct.
* **Order Details Check** (`order_details_check`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check if your order details are correct.
* **Payment Method Check** (`payment_method_check`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check if your payment method is correct.
* **Game System Check** (`game_system_check`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check if your game system selection is correct.
* **Date of Purchase Check** (`date_of_purchase_check`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check if your date of purchase is correct.
* **Time of Purchase Check** (`time_of_purchase_check`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check if your time of purchase is correct.
* **Note Check** (`note_check`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check if your note is correct.
* **Submit Check** (`submit_check`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check if your submit is correct.
