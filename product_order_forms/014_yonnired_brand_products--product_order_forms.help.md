# yonni_red_brand_products - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for product orders and payments for the Yonni Red brand.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Choose the correct payment method and status.
3. Ensure all other fields are filled in according to the product order requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Order Form** (`product_order_form`, text, required/optional): Enter the product details here.
* **Total Price** (`total_price`, number, required/optional): Enter the total price of the order.
* **Send Total** (`send_total`, select_one, required/optional): Choose "Yes" to send the total price to the customer or "No" to not send.
* **Payment Type** (`payment_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose how the customer will pay (Cash App, Other, etc.).
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the customer's email address for order updates and notifications.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the customer's phone number for communication.
* **Payment Complete** (`payment_complete`, date, required/optional): Set the date when the payment was completed.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required/optional): Choose how the customer paid (Cash App, Bank Transfer, Other).
