# Christmas Gifts Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Christmas Gifts Order Form is designed to collect information for Christmas gifts orders, ensuring accurate and timely gift delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the customer contact information section with your details.
2. Select the gift category and item name or description.
3. Choose your preferred delivery method and address (if not selecting In-Store Pickup).
4. Optionally, add a gift message for the recipient.
5. Provide a requested delivery date.
6. Add any special delivery instructions.
7. Review and submit your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 2. Full Name
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed on the gift.

### 3. Phone Number
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for order confirmation and any follow-up communications.

### 4. Email Address
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for order confirmation and any follow-up communications.

### 6. Gift Category
* **Gift Category**: Select the category of the gift you are ordering, such as Toys, Electronics, Home Decor, Clothing, or Gourmet Food.

### 7. Item Name or Description
* **Item Name or Description**: Enter the name or description of the gift you are ordering.

### 8. Quantity
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of gifts you are ordering.

### 9. Would you like gift wrapping?
* **Would you like gift wrapping?**: Select the gift wrapping option you prefer, including Classic Red, Winter Blue, or Gold Star. Additional $5.00 charge applies.

### 10. Personalized Gift Message
* **Personalized Gift Message**: Enter a personalized message for the recipient (max 150 characters).

### 12. Preferred Delivery Method
* **Preferred Delivery Method**: Select your preferred delivery method, such as Standard Shipping, Express Delivery, or In-Store Pickup.

### 13. Delivery Address
* **Delivery Address**: Enter the delivery address for the gift (leave blank if selecting In-Store Pickup).

### 14. Requested Delivery Date
* **Requested Delivery Date**: Enter the date you would like the gift to be delivered by (orders must be placed by Dec 15th for Xmas delivery).

### 15. Special Instructions for Delivery
* **Special Instructions for Delivery**: Enter any special instructions for delivery, such as "Leave on porch" or "Call before delivery".

This form is designed to collect relevant information for Christmas gifts orders, ensuring accurate and timely gift delivery.
