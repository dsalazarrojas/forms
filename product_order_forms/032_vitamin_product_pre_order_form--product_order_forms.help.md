# Vitamin Product Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vitamin Product Pre Order Form is designed to allow customers to express interest in pre-ordering products. This form is for customers who want to be among the first to receive products once they are available.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the products you are interested in pre-ordering by clicking on the corresponding "Yes" option in the "Product Details" section.
2. Choose your delivery preferences in the "Delivery Preferences" section.
3. Provide your customer details in the "Customer Details" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Early Order
* **Early Order**: Check "Yes" if you want to pre-order a product, and "No" if you do not.
### Delivery Preferences
* **Delivery Preferences**: Select how you would like to receive your pre-ordered product, but note that this field is not required.
### Customer Details
* **Customer Details**: Enter your name, email address, and other relevant details to help us contact you about your pre-order.
### Product Details
* **Product Details**: Select the specific product(s) you are interested in pre-ordering.
