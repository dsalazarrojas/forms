# home-accent-pre-order-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "home-accent-pre-order-form" is a form used to pre-order a product from the Home Accent product line. It is designed to collect customer information and order details to ensure accurate processing and shipping of the ordered items.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
2. Provide your email address to facilitate communication with our team.
3. Specify the quantity of the product you would like to order.
4. Choose your preferred option for the product.
5. Enter your phone number for any follow-up communication.
6. Select the shipping date for your order.
7. Choose a shipping time that suits you best.
8. Add any additional shipping notes or instructions.
9. Enter the total cost of your order.
10. Enter your address for shipping purposes.
11. Enter the city where you reside.
12. Enter the state where you reside.
13. Enter your zip code for shipping purposes.
14. Enter any additional message you would like to include with your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name (Customer Name)** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Customer Email (Customer Email)** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address to facilitate communication with our team.
* **Quantity (product_quantity**, number, required): Select the quantity of the product you would like to order.
* **Preference (product_preference, select_one, required): Choose your preferred option for the product.
* **Customer Phone (Customer Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number for any follow-up communication.
* **Shipping Date (shipping_date, date, required): Select the shipping date for your order.
* **Shipping Time (shipping_time, time, required): Choose a shipping time that suits you best.
* **Shipping Notes (shipping_notes, note, required): Add any additional shipping notes or instructions.
* **Order Total (order_total, number, required): Enter the total cost of your order.
* **Customer Address (customer_address, text, required): Enter your address for shipping purposes.
* **Customer City (customer_city, text, required): Enter the city where you reside.
* **Customer State (customer_state, text, required): Enter the state where you reside.
* **Customer Zip (customer_zip, text, required): Enter your zip code for shipping purposes.
* **Customer Message (customer_message, text, required): Enter any additional message you would like to include with your order.**
