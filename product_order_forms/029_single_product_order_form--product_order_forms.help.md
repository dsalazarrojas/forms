# Single Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to guide users through the process of placing an order for a single product. The form is part of a larger system that facilitates order management and processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your user information in the **User Information** section.
2. If applicable, provide any relevant information about your products in the **My Products** section (note: this field is not always required).
3. Select a payment method in the **Payment Info** section:
	* Choose from Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or PayPal.
4. Enter any additional general information in the **General Information** section (note: this field is not always required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **User Information (User Information, text, optional)**
* Enter your name and contact details to help us verify your order.

### **My Products (My Products, note, optional)**
* If you have specific products related to this order, provide a brief description or reference number so we can quickly identify your items.

### **Payment Info (Payment Info, select_one, optional)**
* Select your preferred payment method to complete your order.

### **General Information (General Information, text, optional)**
* Add any additional details or comments that are relevant to your order.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out for your order to be processed correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us directly.
