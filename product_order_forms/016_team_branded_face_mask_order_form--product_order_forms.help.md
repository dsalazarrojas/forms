# Team Branded Face Mask Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect contact and order information for branded face masks ordered by team members or affiliates. It's intended for internal use within the organization and its affiliates.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate contact and order details.
2. Make sure to complete all mandatory fields, marked as "required".
3. Test the "Branded Mask" field to see how many selections are allowed.
4. Enter a clear description of the payment method and any relevant transaction details in the "Payment Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details**: Enter your full name, company name, email address, and any other relevant contact information. This field is not mandatory.
* **Branded Mask**: Select one or multiple branded masks from the provided options. Please note that the system may have a limit on the number of selections allowed. This field is not mandatory, but please ensure it's accurate.
* **Size**: Choose the size of the mask you require. Please select one size only as it is a mandatory field.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of masks you need to order. This field is required to place a valid order.
* **Payment Details**: Enter a brief description of the payment method and any relevant transaction details. Please note that this field is not mandatory, but it's recommended to provide as much information as possible for efficient order processing.

## Tips
* Please ensure all mandatory fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you need assistance with the form or have questions, please reach out to the relevant contact person.
