# Magazine Model Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect necessary permissions from models to use their images in a magazine publication. It ensures that both the model and the client have clear agreements and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the form title, making sure it's accurate and descriptive.
2. Choose the correct category from the options provided.
3. Provide a brief description of the image(s) featuring the model.
4. Enter the client's name and contact information for easy communication.
5. Specify the date and time the image was captured.
6. Mention the location where the image was taken.
7. Add the model's name and signature.
8. Record the model's agreement and date.
9. Enter the model's address.
10. Provide the contact person's name and phone number.
11. Add the contact person's email address.
12. Include the client's agreement.
13. Specify the release version and date.
14. Add any additional notes about the image or publication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Magazine Model Release Form** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the publication where the image will be used.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Choose the correct category from Model Release, Photo Release, or Billboard Advertisement.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Briefly describe the image(s) featuring the model.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Enter the client's name.
* **Date of Capture** (`date_of_capture`, date, optional): Specify the date the image was taken.
* **Time of Capture** (`time_of_capture`, time, optional): Enter the time of capture.
* **Location of Capture** (`location_of_capture`, text, optional): Mention the location where the image was taken.
* **Client Contact** (`client_contact`, text, optional): Enter the client's contact information.
* **Model Name** (`model_name`, text, optional): Enter the model's name.
* **Model Agreement** (`model_agreement`, text, optional): Record the model's agreement.
* **Model Signature** (`model_signature`, text, optional): Add the model's signature.
* **Model Date** (`model_date`, date, optional): Record the model's date.
* **Model Address** (`model_address`, text, optional): Enter the model's address.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's name and phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Add the contact person's email address.
* **Client Agreement** (`client_agreement`, text, optional): Record the client's agreement.
* **Release Version** (`release_version`, text, optional): Specify the release version.
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, date, optional): Record the release date.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about the image or publication.
