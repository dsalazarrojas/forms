# Corporate Social Responsibility News South Africa Interview Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to record and share information about corporate social responsibility news interviews in South Africa.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the title of the corporate social responsibility news interview in the "Corporate Social Responsibility News South Africa Interview Release Form" field.
2.  Provide a brief description of the interview in the "Description" field.
3.  Select the relevant category for the interview from the "Category" dropdown menu.
4.  Enter the assigned to person or team in the "Assigned To" field.
5.  Optionally, you can provide additional information in the "Description 2" field.
6.  Fill in the "Form Id" field with a unique number.
7.  Provide a file name for the output in the "Output File" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Corporate Social Responsibility News South Africa Interview Release Form** (`title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the corporate social responsibility news interview.
*   **Description** (`description`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of the interview.
*   **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, optional): Select the relevant category for the interview from the dropdown menu.
*   **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `text`, optional): Enter the person or team assigned to the interview.
*   **Description 2** (`description_2`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information about the interview.
*   **Form Id** (`form_id`, `number`, optional): Enter a unique number for the form.
*   **Output File** (`output_file`, `text`, optional): Enter a file name for the output.
*   **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `text`, optional): Re-enter the person or team assigned to the interview.
