# Photo Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for capturing consent from individuals to use their photos for certain purposes. It gathers information about the individual's demographics, contact details, and consent status for photo usage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the questions and requirements.
2. Enter your information accurately and truthfully in each field.
3. If you are a guardian or parent of the individual, please provide your own details in the relevant fields.
4. Read and agree to the photo usage agreement carefully.
5. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Prophets** (`prophets`, Number, Optional): Please enter the number of prophets, if any, associated with the photo being taken.
* **date** (`date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date of the photo capture.
* **time** (`time`, Time, Optional): Enter the time of the photo capture.
* **location** (`location`, Text, Optional): Enter the location where the photo was taken.
* **photographer** (`photographer`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the person taking the photo.
* **guardian** (`guardian`, Text, Optional): Enter any details about the guardian or parent of the individual, if applicable.
* **signature** (`signature`, Note, Optional): Please do not enter any signature information here.
* **date of birth** (`date of birth`, Date, Required): Enter the date of birth of the individual.
* **photo release** (`photo release`, Select Multiple, Required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Yes: Grant permission for the photo to be used for the agreed-upon purposes.
	+ No: Do not grant permission for the photo to be used for the agreed-upon purposes.
* **photo usage** (`photo usage`, Select Multiple, Required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Yes: Allow the photo to be used for the specified purposes, as agreed upon.
	+ No: Do not allow the photo to be used for the specified purposes.
* **contact number** (`contact number`, Text, Optional): Enter any contact number for the individual or guardian.
* **email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter any email address for the individual or guardian.
* **guardian signature** (`guardian signature`, Note, Optional): Please do not enter any signature information here.
* **guardian name** (`guardian name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the guardian or parent.
* **guardian email** (`guardian email`, Email, Required): Enter the email address of the guardian or parent.
* **guardian phone** (`guardian phone`, Text, Required): Enter the phone number of the guardian or parent.
* **guardian dob** (`guardian dob`, Date, Required): Enter the date of birth of the guardian or parent.
* **guardian address** (`guardian address`, Text, Optional): Enter any address of the guardian or parent.
* **date of guardian signature** (`date of guardian signature`, Date, Required): Enter the date on which the guardian or parent signed this form.
* **photo usage agreement** (`photo usage agreement`, Select Multiple, Required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Yes: Grant permission for the photo to be used for the specified purposes.
	+ No: Do not grant permission for the photo to be used for the specified purposes.
* **date of photo usage goals** (`date of photo usage goals`, Date, Required): Enter the date on which the photo will be used for the specified purposes.

Please note that some fields are optional, but others are required for the form to be considered complete. Ensure you understand the purpose and requirements of each field before submitting the form.
