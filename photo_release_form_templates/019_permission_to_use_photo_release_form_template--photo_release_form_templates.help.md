# Permission To Use Photo Release Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain permission from the copyright owner (Releasee) to use their photo. It is essential to ensure that you have the necessary permissions and agreements in place before using the photo.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the photo in the `Photo Title` field.
2. Enter a brief description of the photo in the `Photo description` field.
3. Enter the name of the photographer in the `Photo credit` field.
4. Enter the name of the client or company using the photo in the `Client name` field.
5. Enter the date the photo was taken in the `Date shot taken` field.
6. Enter the name of the company or person using the photo in the `Releasee title` field.
7. Enter the email address of the client or company in the `Email address` field.
8. Enter the contact phone number of the client or company in the `Phone number` field.
9. Enter the address of the client or company in the `Address` field.
10. Select either `Yes` or `No` in the `Releasee agreed` field to indicate if the Releasee has agreed to the use of their photo.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Permission to use photo release form**: This is the title of the form and is required to be filled out.
* **Photo Title**: Enter a brief title of the photo. This will help to identify the photo. (Required)
* **Photo description**: Enter a brief description of the photo. This will help to provide context to the photo. (Required)
* **Photo credit**: Enter the name of the photographer who took the photo. This is essential to give credit to the photographer. (Required)
* **Client name**: Enter the name of the client or company using the photo. This will help to identify the owner of the photo. (Required)
* **Date shot taken**: Enter the date the photo was taken. This will help to verify the photo's originality. (Required)
* **Releasee title**: Enter the name of the company or person using the photo. This is essential to identify the owner of the photo. (Required)
* **Email address**: Enter the email address of the client or company. This will help to contact them if needed. (Required)
* **Phone number**: Enter the contact phone number of the client or company. This will help to contact them if needed. (Required)
* **Address**: Enter the address of the client or company. This will help to provide contact information. (Required)
* **Releasee agreed**: Select either `Yes` or `No` to indicate if the Releasee has agreed to the use of their photo. This is essential to ensure that you have the necessary permissions. (Required)

## Tips
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and agreements in place with the Releasee before filling out this form.
* Review the form carefully to ensure that the information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Use this form to obtain permission from the Releasee to use their photo.
* Keep a record of the permission granted to the Releasee for future reference.
