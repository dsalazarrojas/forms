# Accessibility Project Photography Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Accessibility Project Photography Consent Form is a document that seeks consent from individuals for photography related to accessibility features and improvements. It is essential for collecting necessary permissions and details for a successful project.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
2.  Review the form carefully before submission.
3.  Ensure that you understand each section and its implications.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your legal name as it appears on official documents.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Provide your date of birth for minor's information. If you're an adult, it's not necessary.
*   **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Your current address to facilitate communication.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your preferred email address for follow-up communications.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Your phone number for follow-up calls or discussions.
*   **Relationship to Subject** (`relationship_to_subject`, select_one, required): Specify your relation to the person in the photographs, such as being the subject themselves, parent/guardian, or other relationships.
*   **Photography Purpose** (`photography_purpose`, select_multiple, required): Describe the purpose of the photography, including documentation, promotional materials, educational content, and other purposes.
*   **Photography Location** (`photography_location`, text, required): Specify where the photography will take place.
*   **Photography Dates** (`photography_dates`, text, required): Provide the dates of the photography session.
*   **Photography Types** (`photography_types`, select_multiple, required): Describe the types of photography you're allowing, such as individual portraits, group photos, or other types.
*   **Consent Granted** (`consent_granted`, select_one, required): Choose 'Yes, I grant full consent' if you agree to the terms without restrictions or 'Yes, with restrictions' if you need further clarification or modifications.
*   **Consent Restrictions** (`consent_restrictions`, text, optional): If you chose 'Yes, with restrictions,' describe your requirements for the consent.
*   **Usage Rights Granted** (`usage_rights_granted`, select_multiple, required): Specify the rights you're granting for the use of the photographs, such as unlimited usage or educational purposes.
*   **Usage Duration** (`usage_duration`, select_one, required): Select how long you want the organization to use the photographs, including perpetual, 1 or 3 years, or other specified timeframes.
*   **Compensation Acknowledgment** (`compensation_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Choose 'No compensation expected' if you don't anticipate any payment, 'Compensation agreed separately' if you're expecting something, or other alternatives.
*   **Withdrawal Rights** (`withdrawal_rights`, select_one, required): Confirm whether you're granting or refusing the right to withdraw consent at a later date, including future usage or not.
*   **Special Considerations** (`special_considerations`, text, optional): Describe any specific needs or accommodations you require for the individual's accessibility during the photography.
*   **Credit Preferences** (`credit_preferences`, text, optional): Specify how you'd like the subject to be credited in any publications or materials.
*   **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Electronically sign this form to acknowledge your consent.
*   **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Date and time of the electronic signature.

By reading this help guide, you'll understand each section and ensure a smooth and stress-free submission process.
