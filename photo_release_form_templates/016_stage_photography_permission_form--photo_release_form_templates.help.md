# Stage Photography Permission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to capture the necessary information to obtain permission from participants to photograph stage performances, events, or activities. It includes fields for participant information, photographer details, event details, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant's name.
2. Enter the participant's email address.
3. Provide the participant's phone number.
4. Check the "Parent Permission Given" field and select "True" if the participant's parent/guardian has given permission.
5. Enter your name and contact information (phone number and email address) as the photographer.
6. Add any additional details about the event, such as the event title, date, and time.
7. Include the location where the photography will take place.
8. Provide any additional notes or instructions for the photographer.
9. Sign and date the form to indicate that you have reviewed and agreed to the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information**: Enter the participant's name and other identifying information.
* **Participant Name**: Enter the participant's name as it appears on their ID or registration form.
* **Email**: Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Parent Permission Given**: Check "True" if the participant's parent/guardian has given permission for the photography. If not, check "False" and provide additional information.
* **Photographer Information**: Enter your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Photographer Name**: Enter your name as the photographer.
* **Photographer Phone**: Enter your phone number as the photographer.
* **Photographer Email**: Enter your email address as the photographer.
* **Participant Signature**: Sign below this line to indicate that you have reviewed and agreed to the terms.
* **Photographer Signature**: Sign below this line to indicate that you have reviewed and agreed to the terms.
* **Date**: Date the form was completed.
* **Event Details**: Provide any additional details about the event, such as the event title, date, and time.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event.
* **Location**: Enter the location where the photography will take place.
* **School/Theater/Studio**: Enter the name of the school, theater, or studio where the photography will take place.
* **Event Title**: Enter the title of the event.
* **Stage Director**: Enter the name of the stage director.
* **Stage Manager**: Enter the name of the stage manager.
* **Stage Technician**: Enter the name of the stage technician.
* **Photographer Direction**: Enter any instructions or guidance for the photographer.
* **Photographer Notes**: Enter any additional notes or instructions for the photographer.
* **Participant Notes**: Enter any additional notes or instructions for the participant.
* **Event Title**: Enter the title of the event.
* **Photographer Signature**: Sign below this line to indicate that you have reviewed and agreed to the terms.
* **School/Theater/Studio**: Enter the name of the school, theater, or studio where the photography will take place.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event.
* **Photographer Location**: Enter the location where the photography will take place.
