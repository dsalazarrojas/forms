# Social Media Photo Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Media Photo Release Form is used to obtain permission from a photographer or model to publish their photos on social media. This form ensures that the rights of the photographer or model are respected and that they are comfortable with how their photos are being used.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and honestly.
2. Enter the name and contact information of the photographer or model.
3. Select the type of release you are requesting.
4. Enter the location where the photo was taken.
5. Choose the duration of the release period.
6. Enter the IDs or URLs of the photos to be released.
7. Enter the dates when the photos are to be published.
8. Enter the media where the photos will be published.
9. Sign and date the form to confirm that you have obtained the necessary permissions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photographer** (`name`, `text`, not required): Please enter the name of the photographer or person taking the photo.
* **Model** (`name`, `text`, not required): Please enter the name of the model or person in the photo.
* **Type of Release** (`select_one`, options: `Option 1`, `Option 2`, `Option 3`): Select the type of release you are requesting. This field is used to determine the terms of use for the published photo.
* **Location** (`name`, `text`, not required): Please enter the location where the photo was taken.
* **Date of Release** (`name`, `date`, not required): Please enter the date when the photo release is valid.
* **Duration of Release** (`name`, `text`, not required): Please enter the duration of the release period.
* **Photo IDs** (`name`, `text`, not required): Enter the IDs or reference numbers of the photos to be released.
* **Photo URLs** (`name`, `text`, not required): Enter the URLs of the photos to be released.
* **Publish Dates** (`name`, `text`, not required): Enter the dates when the photos are to be published.
* **Published Media** (`name`, `text`, not required): Enter the media where the photos will be published.
* **Model Signature** (`name`, `text`, not required): This is where the model signs the photo release, indicating their consent.
* **Photographer Signature** (`name`, `text`, not required): This is where the photographer signs the photo release, indicating their consent.
