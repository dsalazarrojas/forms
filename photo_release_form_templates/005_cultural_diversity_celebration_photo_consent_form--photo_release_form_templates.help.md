# cultural_diversity_celebration_photo_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The cultural_diversity_celebration_photo_consent_form is a document used to collect consent from individuals for the use of their photo images in a cultural diversity celebration. It's essential to obtain this consent before publishing or using any photos taken during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct option for the photo release in the "photo_release" field.
2. Fill in your name in the "name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Provide your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Enter your address in the "address" field.
6. Select the date of the photo taking in the "date" field.
7. Enter the time of the photo taking in the "time" field.
8. Choose your permission level in the "permission" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **photo_release** (1, select_one, required/optional): Select one of the three options to indicate the type of photo release you are granting. Please choose one of the options carefully, as it will determine how your image will be used.
* **name** (2, text, required): Enter your full name as you would like it to appear with your image.
* **email** (3, email, required): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you regarding your consent.
* **phone** (4, text, required): Enter your phone number where we can reach you if needed.
* **address** (5, text, required): Enter your full address for our records.
* **date** (6, date, required): Select the date of the photo taking.
* **time** (7, time, required): Select the time of the photo taking.
* **permission** (8, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose whether you grant permission for your image to be used in various ways, such as for educational, promotional, or commercial purposes.
