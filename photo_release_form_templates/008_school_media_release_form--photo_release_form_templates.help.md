# School Media Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The School Media Release Form is a document used to obtain permission from parents for their child's media usage and image representation in various school-related contexts. This form ensures that parents are aware of and agree to their child's media usage, including photos, videos, and other forms of media.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand each section.
2. Provide accurate information for each field.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Parent Information** (`parent_info`, text, required: false): This field is for you to provide your name and contact information.
  - Please enter your name and contact details accurately.
  - This is the starting point for the form, and it's essential to have your contact information so we can reach out to you if needed.

- **Media Usage** (`media_usage`, text, required: false): Describe the specific media usage you are granting permission for (e.g., photo release, video release, or other).
  - Please specify the type of media you are granting permission for.
  - Be as detailed as possible to ensure we understand your wishes correctly.

- **Photo Release** (`photo_release`, note, required: false): Indicate whether you are granting permission for photo release.
  - Check 'Yes' if you are granting permission for photo release.
  - If not, you can choose 'No', and we will respect your wishes.

- **School Media** (`school_media`, select_one, required: false): Choose the specific school context where your child's media will be used (e.g., school projects, events, websites, or social media accounts).
  - Select one or more options that apply to your child's media usage.

- **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: false): Enter your child's name as it appears in our records.
  - Please enter the correct spelling of your child's name.

- **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, number, required: false): Specify your child's grade level (if applicable).
  - If your child is not in a grade level, you can leave this field blank.

- **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, note, required: false): Sign and date this field to confirm your consent.
  - Please sign and date this field to indicate your agreement.

- **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required: false): Enter the date you signed this form.
  - Please enter the date you signed this form for record-keeping purposes.

- **Media Release ID** (`media_release_id`, text, required: false): Provide a unique identifier for this media release (if required).
  - If you have been given a Media Release ID, enter it here. Otherwise, leave it blank.

- **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose who will be handling your child's media (e.g., teachers, staff members, or other individuals).
  - Select one or more options that apply to your child's media usage.

- **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
  - Please enter your email address so we can reach out to you if needed.

- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
  - Please enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.

- **School Media Release Form** (`school_media_release_form`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate the specific school context where media will be used (e.g., school projects, events, websites, or social media accounts).
  - Select one or more options that apply to your child's media usage.

- **Other Media Usage** (`other_media_usage`, select_multiple, required: false): Check 'Yes' if you grant permission for other types of media usage not listed (e.g., audio recordings).
  - Check 'Yes' if you want to grant permission for other types of media that are not listed here.

- **Student Image Release** (`student_image_release`, select_multiple, required: false): Check 'Yes' if you grant permission for your child's image to be used.
  - Check 'Yes' if you grant permission for your child's image to be used.

- **Student Other Media** (`student_other_media`, select_multiple, required: false): Check 'Yes' if you grant permission for other types of media that are not listed (e.g., audio recordings).
  - Check 'Yes' if you grant permission for other types of media that are not listed.

- **Student Media Usage** (`student_media_usage`, select_multiple, required: false): Check 'Yes' if you grant permission for media usage related to your child.
  - Check 'Yes' if you grant permission for media usage related to your child.

- **School Other Media Usage** (`school_other_media_usage`, select_multiple, required: false): Check 'Yes' if you grant permission for other types of media that are not listed.
  - Check 'Yes' if you grant permission for other types of media that are not listed.

- **Other Media Usage 2** (`other_media_usage_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Check 'Yes' if you grant permission for other types of media that are not listed.
  - Check 'Yes' if you grant permission for other types of media that are not listed.

## Tips
- Be accurate and honest when filling out this form.
- Review each section carefully before signing.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
- This form is intended to obtain your consent for your child's media representation and usage.
