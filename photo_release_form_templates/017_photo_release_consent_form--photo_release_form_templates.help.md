# Photo Release Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photo Release Consent Form is used to obtain consent from photo owners for the release of their photos. The form is designed to capture the necessary information for a photo shoot, including the photo owner's details, the purpose of the shoot, and the release conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Photographer Information" field with the name and contact details of the photographer.
2. Select the "Photo Owner" field to specify the relationship between the photo owner and the individual submitting the form (Self, Parent or Guardian, or Other).
3. If you select "Other", fill in the "Other" field with the name and contact details of the photo owner.
4. Choose the "Release Type" field to select the release conditions for the photos (Print, Internet, or Both).
5. Fill in the "Release Location" field with the location of the photo shoot.
6. Fill in the "Date" field with the date of the photo shoot.
7. Sign and date the "Photographer Signature" field.
8. Sign and date the "Photo Owner Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photo Release Form**: This is the main section of the form where you can fill in the required information.
* **Photographer Information**: Fill in this field with the name and contact details of the photographer.
* **Photo Owner**: Select the relationship between the photo owner and the individual submitting the form. You can choose from Self, Parent or Guardian, or Other.
* **Other**: If you select "Other", fill in this field with the name and contact details of the photo owner.
* **Purpose of Shoot**: Fill in this field with a brief description of the purpose of the photo shoot.
* **Release Type**: Choose the release conditions for the photos. You can select Print, Internet, or Both.
* **Release Location**: Fill in this field with the location of the photo shoot.
* **Photographer Signature**: Sign and date this field to confirm that the information is accurate.
* **Photo Owner Signature**: Sign and date this field to confirm that the photo owner has given consent for the release of their photos.
* **Date**: Fill in this field with the date of the photo shoot.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that the form is complete and accurate.
* Double-check the information before submitting the form to ensure that it is correct.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask a supervisor or a photographer for clarification.
