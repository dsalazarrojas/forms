# Talent Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Talent Release Form is a simple form used to collect essential information about talent releases. It's designed to help users quickly and accurately provide the necessary details for talent releases.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all necessary information before starting.
2. Fill out the required fields:
	* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person to be released.
	* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person to be released.
	* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the person to be released.
3. Optionally, you can add any additional **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter any other relevant contact information about the person to be released.
4. Fill out the **Project Info** (`project_info`, text, optional): Describe the project or context related to the talent release.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, text): Enter the name of the person to be released. This will help us identify the individual accurately.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter the email address of the person to be released. This will enable us to contact the person efficiently.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text): Enter the phone number of the person to be released. This will help us contact the person directly.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter any other relevant contact information about the person to be released, such as address or office phone number.
* **Project Info** (`project_info`, text, optional): Describe the project or context related to the talent release. This will help us understand the scope and purpose of the release.

## Tips

* Double-check your inputs for accuracy and completeness.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the fields.
* If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to contact us.
