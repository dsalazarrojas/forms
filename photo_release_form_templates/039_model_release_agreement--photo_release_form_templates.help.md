# Model Release Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Model Release Agreement form is used to capture information related to a model's release of their likeness, image, or representation for commercial use. This form is used to obtain permission from the model to use their likeness, image, or representation for a specific purpose.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the `model_name` field with the name of the model.
2. Enter the `client_name` field with the name of the client or organization using the model's likeness.
3. In the `date` field, select the date the model release agreement was signed.
4. In the `signature` field, enter the model's signature.
5. Enter the `business_purpose` field with a brief description of the intended use of the model's likeness.
6. Enter the `model_purpose` field with a brief description of the model's purpose in the intended use of their likeness.
7. Select the `model_agree` field to indicate the model's agreement to the terms of the release.
8. Select the `client_agree` field to indicate the client's agreement to the terms of the release.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **model_name**: Enter the name of the model who is granting permission for their likeness to be used.
* **client_name**: Enter the name of the client or organization using the model's likeness.
* **date**: Enter the date the model release agreement was signed.
* **signature**: Enter the model's signature, either physically or digitally.
* **business_purpose**: Enter a brief description of the intended use of the model's likeness, such as advertising or promotional materials.
* **model_purpose**: Enter a brief description of the model's purpose in the intended use of their likeness.
* **model_agree**: Select "1" to indicate the model agrees to the terms of the release.
* **client_agree**: Select "1" to indicate the client agrees to the terms of the release.

## Tips
* Ensure that the model's signature is accurate and complete before submitting this form.
* Ensure that the model's release agreement is obtained before completing this form.
* If the model is a minor or otherwise vulnerable, this form should not be used.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the relevant authority for clarification.
