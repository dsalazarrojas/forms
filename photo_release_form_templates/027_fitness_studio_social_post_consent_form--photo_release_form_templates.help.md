# Fitness Studio Social Post Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Studio Social Post Consent Form is used to collect client-specific social post consent information. This form is essential for ensuring that clients understand and agree to the terms of our social media posts, which helps us maintain a positive online presence.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Client Name**: Enter the client's name as it appears in our records.
2. **Photo Consent**: Check "True" if you consent to the use of your photo in social media posts. If you don't want to be photographed, check "False".
3. **Email**: Enter your email address.
4. **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
5. **Social Media**: Select the social media platforms where you agree to be posted (e.g., Facebook, Instagram, Twitter).
6. **Date**: Enter the date of your workout or visit.
7. **Time**: Enter the time of your workout or visit (optional).
8. **Note**: Add any additional information you think is relevant to your social media post.
9. **Additional Info**: Enter any additional information you want to share with our staff.
10. **Submission Date**: Enter the date you're submitting this form.
11. **Submission Time**: Enter the time you're submitting this form.
12. **Submitted By**: Select who submitted this form (Admin or Staff).
13. **Submission IP**: Enter the IP address of the device used to submit this form (optional).
14. **Submission Browser**: Enter the browser used to submit this form (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the client's name as it appears in our records.
* **Photo Consent** (`photo_consent`, select_one, optional): Check "True" to indicate you consent to the use of your photo in social media posts, or "False" if you do not.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Social Media** (`social_media`, select_multiple, optional): Select the social media platforms where you agree to be posted.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of your workout or visit.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your workout or visit (optional).
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional information you think is relevant to your social media post.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information you want to share with our staff.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date you're submitting this form.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required): Enter the time you're submitting this form.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, optional): Select who submitted this form (Admin or Staff).
* **Submission IP** (`submission_ip`, text, optional): Enter the IP address of the device used to submit this form (optional).
* **Submission Browser** (`submission_browser`, text, optional): Enter the browser used to submit this form (optional).

## Tips
- Ensure you fill out all required fields (Client Name, Email, Phone, Date, Submission Date, and Submission Time).
- If you're unsure about any field, contact our staff for guidance.
- This form is for your convenience, and our staff will review and verify the information before posting on social media.
