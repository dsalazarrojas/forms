# disaster_relief_beneficiary_photo_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect photo consent from disaster relief beneficiaries, ensuring that the organization or entity providing relief can use the photos taken during the process for their intended purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Read the form carefully** and ensure you understand the purpose and requirements before proceeding.
2. **Fill out the form fields** by entering your information in the designated sections:
	* Beneficiary Information: Enter your name and any other relevant identification information.
	* Contact Information: Provide your contact details for follow-up communication.
	* Relationship: Describe your relationship to the beneficiary (e.g., family member, friend, caregiver).
	* Permission Information: Confirm your permission for the photo release.
	* Photo Release: Confirm the specific photo permissions you grant.
	* Contact Details: Provide a contact number and email for communication.
	* Date: Enter the date you're signing this consent.
	* Photo Details: Describe the photo(s) you're releasing (e.g., name of photo, date taken).
	* License: Select the photo license you're granting (e.g., public, private, commercial).
	* Signature: Sign your name to confirm your consent.
	* Date Signed: Enter the date you sign this consent.
