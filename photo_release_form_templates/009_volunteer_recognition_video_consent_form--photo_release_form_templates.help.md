# volunteer_recognition_video_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from volunteers who will be featured in a video recognition program. The form collects contact and demographic information from the volunteers, and their permission to be featured in a video. It also asks for their consent to release their photo and any additional media. The purpose of this form is to ensure that the volunteers are comfortable with their image and likeness being used in the video, and to collect any necessary information for the video production team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first and last name in the "first_name" and "last_name" fields, respectively.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
3. Select your preferred contact method from the options listed in the "contact_preference" field.
4. Indicate whether you are comfortable with your likeness being published in a video by selecting "True" or "False" in the "permission_to_publish_video" field.
5. If you have agreed to be published, you will be asked if you are also comfortable releasing your photo and any additional media. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "photo_release" field.
6. If you have agreed to release your photo, select "Yes" or "No" in the "additional_media_release" field to indicate if you would like to release any additional media.
7. Sign the "consent" field by entering your name.
8. If you are a representative of an organization, enter the name of the organization in the "organization" field.
9. Indicate your role in the organization by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "role" field.
10. Enter your department or title in the "department" field.
11. Indicate if you are a title holder by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "title" field.
12. Enter your job title in the "job_title" field.
13. Enter your location in the "location" field.
14. Enter your date of birth in the "date_of_birth" field.
15. Enter the date the video was captured in the "date_of_video_capture" field.
16. Enter the date you are releasing your photo in the "date_of_release" field.
17. Enter the time the video was captured in the "time_of_video_capture" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **contact_preference**: Select your preferred contact method for communication with you, either Volunteer, Phone, Email, Social Media, or Mail.
* **permission_to_publish_video**: Indicate whether you are comfortable with your likeness being published in a video by selecting True or False.
* **photo_release**: If you have agreed to be published, indicate if you are also comfortable releasing your photo by selecting Yes or No.
* **additional_media_release**: If you have agreed to release your photo, select Yes or No to indicate if you would like to release any additional media.
* **consent**: Sign your name in this field to confirm your consent.
* **organization**: Enter the name of your organization, if applicable.
* **role**: Indicate your role in the organization by selecting Yes or No.
* **department**: Enter your department or title.
* **title**: Indicate if you are a title holder by selecting Yes or No.
* **job_title**: Enter your job title.
* **location**: Enter your location.
* **date_of_birth**: Enter your date of birth.
* **date_of_video_capture**: Enter the date the video was captured.
* **date_of_release**: Enter the date you are releasing your photo.
* **time_of_video_capture**: Enter the time the video was captured.
