# Photo Release Form For Minors - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photo Release Form for Minors is a document that requires the parent or guardian of a minor to provide consent for the use of their child's photo. This form ensures that the minor's identity and image are protected while allowing for the proper management of photo releases.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Provide your contact information as a parent or guardian.
2.  Enter the minor's information, including their name and birthdate.
3.  Describe the photo, including its type and date taken.
4.  Indicate whether you grant consent for the photo release.
5.  Choose how you want to license and share the photo.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Information** (1): Please fill in your name and contact information as the parent or guardian.
* **Minor Information** (2): Enter the minor's name, birthdate, and any other required information.
* **Photo Details** (3): Describe the photo, including its type and date taken.
* **Consent Date** (4): Enter the date you are granting consent for the photo release.
* **Photo Type** (5): Choose the type of photo (e.g., portrait, landscape, etc.).
* **Photo Number** (6): Enter the photo's unique identifier if applicable.
* **Photo Description** (7): Describe the photo briefly.
* **Photo Location** (8): Enter the location where the photo was taken.
* **Date Taken** (9): Enter the date the photo was taken.
* **Minor Consent** (10): Indicate whether you grant consent for the photo release.
* **Photo License** (11): Choose how to license the photo (Public or Non-Public).
* **Photo Retouching** (12): Decide whether you want us to retouch the photo.
* **Print or Publish** (13): Choose how to print or publish the photo (Print or Publish).
* **Photo Sharing** (14): Select how to share the photo on social media (Facebook, Twitter, Instagram).

## Tips
* Be sure to fill out all required fields to avoid submission issues.
* Double-check your selections for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact our support team.
