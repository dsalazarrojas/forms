# image_and_word_usage_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent and release permissions for the use of images or words.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of content to be released (photo, video, or written content).
2. Choose the consent period for the releasee and assignee (1 year, 2 years, or 5 years).
3. Fill in the releasee's details, such as their name, email, and contact information.
4. Fill in the assignee's details, such as their name, email, and contact information.
5. Choose the assignee's consent period and date.
6. Add any additional notes for the releasee and assignee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Photo Release** (`photo_release`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of photo to be released.
* **Consent Period** (`consent_period`, select_one, required): Choose the consent period for the releasee (1 year, 2 years, or 5 years).
* **Content Type** (`content_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of content to be released (photo, video, or written content).
* **Releasee Consent** (`releaseee_consent`, select_one, required): Check if the releasee has given their consent.
* **Releasee Name** (`releasee_name`, text, required): Fill in the releasee's name.
* **Releasee Email** (`releasee_email`, email, required): Fill in the releasee's email address.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Fill in the releasee's organization name.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Fill in the releasee's school name.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Fill in the releasee's contact person's name.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Fill in the releasee's contact phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Fill in the date of the release.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Fill in the time of the release.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Add any additional notes for the releasee.
* **Assignee Consent** (`assignee_consent`, select_one, required): Check if the assignee has given their consent.
* **Assignee Name** (`assignee_name`, text, required): Fill in the assignee's name.
* **Assignee Email** (`assignee_email`, email, required): Fill in the assignee's email address.
* **Assignee Organization** (`assignee_organization`, text, required): Fill in the assignee's organization name.
* **Assignee Position** (`assignee_position`, text, required): Fill in the assignee's position.
* **Assignee Contact Person** (`assignee_contact_person`, text, required): Fill in the assignee's contact person's name.
* **Assignee Contact Phone** (`assignee_contact_phone`, text, required): Fill in the assignee's contact phone number.
* **Assignee Date** (`assignee_date`, date, required): Fill in the assignee's consent date.
* **Assignee Time** (`assignee_time`, time, required): Fill in the assignee's consent time.
* **Assignee Notes** (`assignee_notes`, note, required): Add any additional notes for the assignee.
* **Assignee Consent Period** (`assignee_consent_period`, select_one, required): Choose the consent period for the assignee (1 year, 2 years, or 5 years).
* **Assignee Consent Date** (`assignee_consent_date`, date, required): Fill in the assignee's consent date.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to avoid any miscommunication or legal issues.
* If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to the relevant personnel for clarification.
* Double-check the details of both the releasee and assignee before submitting the form.
