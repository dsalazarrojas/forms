# Parent Guardian Publicity Authorization And Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Parent Guardian Publicity Authorization And Release Form is used to obtain consent from a parent or guardian for the use of a child's image, name, or likeness for publicity purposes. It is essential to fill out this form carefully and accurately to ensure that your child's rights are protected.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your name as the parent or guardian.
2. Provide your address.
3. Choose whether to authorize or deny the use of your child's image, name, or likeness for publicity purposes.
4. Select the desired termination date of authorization.
5. Enter the project title and name of the contact method and contact name.
6. Provide the contact phone number and email address.
7. Optional: Enter the date and time of the publicity release or image release expiration, and select the desired date format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Guardian Name** (Required): Fill in your name as the parent or guardian.
* **Parent Guardian Address** (Required): Provide your address where you can be contacted.
* **Publicity Authorization** (Required): Choose whether to authorize or deny the use of your child's image, name, or likeness for publicity purposes.
* **Release Termination** (Required): Select the desired termination date of authorization. Options include Immediately, After Project Completion, or Upon Request of Child's Guardian.
* **Project Title** (Required): Enter the title of the project.
* **Contact Method** (Required): Select the method of contact (Phone, Email, etc.).
* **Contact Name** (Required): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (Required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (Required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Date** (Optional): Enter the date of the publicity release or image release expiration.
* **Time** (Optional): Enter the time of the publicity release or image release expiration.
* **Notes** (Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments related to the publicity release or image release.
* **Publicity Release Termination** (Required): Select the desired termination date of authorization. Options include Immediately, Upon Notification of Publication, or Upon Request of Child's Guardian.
* **Publicity Renewal** (Optional): Enter the desired date of publicity renewal.
* **Renewal Date** (Optional): Enter the date of publicity renewal.
* **Publicity Expiration** (Optional): Enter the date of publicity expiration.
* **Publicity Expiration Date Format** (Optional): Select the format of the publicity expiration date (YYYY-MM-DD or MM/DD/YY).
* **Image Release Expiration** (Optional): Enter the date of image release expiration.
* **Image Expiration Date Format** (Optional): Select the format of the image expiration date (YYYY-MM-DD or MM/DD/YY).

Please note that this form is designed to be completed by parents or guardians, but it may also be used by project administrators or team members to gather necessary information from parents or guardians. Ensure that you fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your child's image, name, or likeness is used properly and according to your wishes.
