# senior_testimonial_story_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Senior Testimonial Story Consent Form is used to gather stories and photos from seniors, typically for professional or educational purposes. It helps to obtain their consent for sharing their stories and photos.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the testimonial story where you share your story.
2. Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
3. Provide your email address to ensure we can contact you in case of any questions or concerns.
4. If you're under 18, select 'Parent/Guardian' or 'Parent/Guardian or Other (with written permission)' for photo release.
5. Select your consent for sharing your testimonial story and photo.
6. Enter the date and time of your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Testimonial** (`testimonial_story`, text, optional): Share your story with us.
* **Name** (`name_on_story`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Consent** (`testimonial_consent`, select_multiple, optional): If you're under 18, select 'Parent/Guardian' or 'Parent/Guardian or Other (with written permission)'. If you're above 18, select your own consent.
* **Photo Release** (`photo_release`, select_one, optional): Choose your preference for photo release.
* **Testimonial Story Consent Form** (`testimonial_story_consent_form`, text, optional): Enter a statement of consent for sharing your testimonial story.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you're submitting your form.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you're submitting your form.
