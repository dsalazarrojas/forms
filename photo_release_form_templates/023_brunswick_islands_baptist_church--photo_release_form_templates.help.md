# Brunswick Islands Baptist Church - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to grant permission for photos taken at church events or programs. It is designed to be used for Sunday School, Bible School, and other church activities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Select the options for how you wish your photos to be used.
3. Read and agree to the terms of photo use.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`person_name`, `text`, required): This field is where you enter your full name as the participant.
* **Date of Birth** (`person_date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): If you are a minor (under 18 years old), please enter your date of birth to confirm your age.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, optional): If you are a minor, this field is for your parent or guardian to enter their name.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): This is your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, `text`, required): This is your email address where you can be reached.
* **Event or Location of Photos** (`photo_location`, `text`, required): Please enter the event or location where photos will be taken.
* **Date of Event** (`photo_date`, `date`, required): Please enter the date when photos will be taken.
* **Church Ministry or Program** (`church_ministry`, `text`, required): Please enter the specific church ministry or program related to the photos.
* **Allow photos on church website** (`website_use`, `select_one`, required): Check the box to indicate if you allow your photos to be published on the church website.
* **Allow photos on church Facebook** (`facebook_use`, `select_one`, required): Check the box to indicate if you allow your photos to be published on the church Facebook.
* **Allow photos for print materials** (`print_use`, `select_one`, required): Check the box to indicate if you allow your photos to be published in print materials.
* **Allow identification by name** (`name_identification`, `select_one`, required): Check the box to indicate if you allow your name to be identified with your photos.
* **Allow use for commercial purposes** (`commercial_use`, `select_one`, required): Check the box to indicate if you allow your photos to be used for commercial purposes.
* **Duration of Photo Use Permission** (`duration_of_use`, `select_one`, required): Please select how long you want your photos to be used.
* **Any Additional Restrictions** (`additional_restrictions`, `text`, optional): If you have any special requests or concerns, please enter them here.
* **I grant permission for photo release** (`release_consent`, `select_one`, required): Check the box to indicate that you grant permission for your photos to be published.
* **Date of Agreement** (`signature_date`, `date`, required): Please enter the date when you grant permission for photo use.

**Tips**

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Read each field explanation carefully before filling out the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can always ask for help or clarification.
