# Legal Photography Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect legal information and agreements from clients for a photography session. It is essential to provide accurate and thorough details to ensure a smooth and stress-free experience for both the client and the photographer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the session details, including date and time.
2. Fill in the client's details, including name, email address, and phone number.
3. Provide the client's address and location.
4. Confirm the client's permission for the photo shoot.
5. Review and agree to the agreement term.
6. Read and agree to the terms and conditions.
7. Sign the client agreement to confirm the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Details**: Enter the details of the photography session, including date and time.
* **Client Details**: Enter the client's name, email address, and phone number.
* **Address**: Enter the client's address.
* **Session Date**: Enter the date of the photography session.
* **Session Time**: Enter the time of the photography session.
* **Location**: Enter the location of the photography session.
* **Client Name**: Enter the name of the person being photographed.
* **Client Permission**: Confirm that you have the client's permission to take their photographs.
* **Agreement Term**: Review and agree to the terms of the agreement.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the photography session.
* **Client Agreement**: Sign to confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions.
* **Email**: Enter the client's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the client's phone number.
* **Photographer**: Enter the name of the photographer.
* **Client Agreement**: This field is not applicable, as it is the same as the "Agreement Term" field.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
* Review the terms and conditions carefully before signing the client agreement.
* Keep a copy of the signed agreement for future reference.
* Make sure to discuss any questions or concerns with the photographer before signing the agreement.
