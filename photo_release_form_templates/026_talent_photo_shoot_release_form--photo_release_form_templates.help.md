This form is designed for talent (individuals or entities participating in a photo shoot) to release their image rights after a photo shoot. The purpose of this form is to ensure that the talent has given their consent to the use of their images by the production company. 

It's essential to understand that this form should only be filled out once per shoot and by the correct talent, as each shoot and talent may have unique requirements. If you're unsure about filling out this form, please contact the production company.

The form is a one-page document with 11 fields. Each field is designed to capture specific information related to the photo shoot, including the release date, talent name, parental information, photo release options, project number, agency name, production company, contact information, signature, date signed, and signed by.

Here is the user-facing help guide:

# Talent Photo Shoot Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Talent Photo Shoot Release Form is a one-page document used to capture the consent of a talent to the use of their images after a photo shoot. This form ensures that the talent has given their explicit consent to the production company.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the release date, if applicable.
2. Enter your name, as the talent.
3. Provide the name and contact information of your parent or guardian, if applicable.
4. Choose whether you wish to grant a full or no release of your likeness.
5. Enter the project number assigned to this shoot.
6. Provide the name of the agency or production company responsible for the shoot.
7. Enter your contact information, which will be used for communication and coordination.
8. Sign the form to confirm your consent.
9. Date the form with the date you sign it.
10. Provide your signature to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Release Date**: Enter the date of the photo shoot if it has not already passed.
* **Talent Name**: Enter your name as the talent participating in the photo shoot.
* **Parent/Guardian**: Enter the name and contact information of your parent or guardian if you are a minor or under 18 years old.
* **Photo Release**: Choose whether you wish to grant a Full Release or No Release of your likeness. 
    + Full Release: You grant permission for the production company to use your image in any way they see fit.
    + No Release: You do not grant permission for the production company to use your image.
* **Project Number**: Enter the project number assigned to this photo shoot.
* **Agency Name**: Enter the name of the agency or production company responsible for the shoot.
* **Production Company**: Enter the name of the production company.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your contact information, which will be used for communication and coordination.
* **Signature**: Sign the form to confirm that you have read, understand, and consent to the terms.
* **Date Signed**: Date the form with the date you sign it.
* **Signed By**: Enter your signature to complete the form.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have read and understand all the terms before signing the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the production company for clarification.
* This form should only be filled out once per shoot and by the correct talent.
