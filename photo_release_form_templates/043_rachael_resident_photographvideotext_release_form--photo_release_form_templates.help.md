# rachael_resident_photographvideotext_release_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal information from residents to verify their identity and nationality. It is used for the purposes of issuing a passport and other identification documents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and any other required fields as prompted.
2. Enter your current Address and City.
3. Enter your State and Postal Code.
4. Select your Country.
5. Enter your Email and Phone Number.
6. Enter your Date of Birth.
7. Enter your Birthplace and Nationality.
8. Enter your ID Number.
9. Upload an ID Document, Birth Certificate, and other required documents as prompted.
10. Review and submit the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: (`first_name`, text, required) Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Last Name**: (`last_name`, text, required) Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Address**: (`address`, text, required) Enter your current physical address.
* **City**: (`city`, text, required) Enter the city where you currently reside.
* **State**: (`state`, text, required) Enter the state where you currently reside.
* **Postal Code**: (`postal_code`, text, required) Enter the postal code or zip code of your current location.
* **Email**: (`email`, email, required) Enter a valid email address that you use frequently.
* **Phone Number**: (`phone_number`, text, required) Enter your phone number as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: (`date_of_birth`, date, required) Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Birthplace**: (`birthplace`, text, required) Enter the city or country where you were born.
* **Nationality**: (`nationality`, text, required) Enter your nationality as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **ID Number**: (`id_number`, text, required) Enter your ID number as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Nationality Verification**: (`nationality_verification`, text, required) Enter any additional information required for nationality verification.
* **Passport Number**: (`passport_number`, text, optional) Enter your passport number if you have one.
* **Passport Expiration**: (`passport_expiration`, date, optional) Enter the expiration date of your passport if you have one.
* **Visa Expiration**: (`visa_expiration`, date, optional) Enter the expiration date of your visa if you have one.
* **ID Document Upload**: (`id_document_upload`, text, optional) Upload a copy of your ID document.
* **Birth Certificate Upload**: (`birth_certificate_upload`, text, optional) Upload a copy of your birth certificate.
* **ID Photo Upload**: (`id_photo`, text, optional) Upload a photo of your ID document.
* **Nationality Verification Document Upload**: (`nationality_verification_document_upload`, text, optional) Upload a copy of your nationality verification document.
* **Visa Document Upload**: (`visa_document_upload`, text, optional) Upload a copy of your visa document.
* **Passport Photo Upload**: (`passport_photo_upload`, text, optional) Upload a photo of your passport.
* **Document Upload**: (`document_upload`, text, optional) Upload any other required documents as prompted.

Note: This form is intended for residents to verify their identity and nationality. The fields marked as required are necessary for the verification process.
