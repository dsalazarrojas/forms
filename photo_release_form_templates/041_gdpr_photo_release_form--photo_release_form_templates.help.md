# gdpr_photo_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a photo release, which is a legal document that grants permission to use a person's likeness or image.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the first and last name of the person whose likeness is captured in the photo.
2. Enter the email address of the person whose likeness is captured in the photo.
3. Provide the date the photo was taken.
4. Enter the location where the photo was taken.
5. Enter the name of the photographer who took the photo.
6. Confirm that the photo is a model release.
7. Provide the contact information for the photographer.
8. Enter the first and last name of the subject of the photo.
9. Enter the email address of the subject.
10. Enter the phone number of the subject.
11. Enter the address of the subject.
12. Sign with a handwritten signature as the photographer.
13. Sign with a handwritten signature as the subject.
14. Enter the date the photo release was signed.
15. Confirm that the photo release was authorized by the photographer.
16. Enter the date of birth of the subject.
17. Enter the nationality of the subject.
18. Select the intended use of the photo (e.g. internet, social media, print, etc.).
19. Enter the version of the model release.
20. Select who retains the rights to the photo (photographer, subject, or both).
21. Enter the acknowledgement from the subject.
22. Enter the acknowledgement from the photographer.
23. If the photo is to be used for other purposes, enter the intended use.
24. If the photo is to be used for other purposes, enter a detailed description of the intended use.
25. Enter a detailed description of the intended use of the photo.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First and Last Name** (`first_and_last_name`, text, required): Enter the first and last name of the person whose likeness is captured in the photo.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person whose likeness is captured in the photo.
* **Date of Capture** (`date_of_capture`, date, required): Provide the date the photo was taken.
* **Location of Capture** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location where the photo was taken.
* **Photographer** (`photographer`, text, required): Enter the name of the photographer who took the photo.
* **Model Release** (`model_release`, text, required): Confirm that the photo is a model release.
* **Photographer Contact** (`photographer_contact`, text, required): Provide the contact information for the photographer.
* **Subject First and Last Name** (`subject_first_and_last_name`, text, required): Enter the first and last name of the subject of the photo.
* **Subject Email** (`subject_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the subject.
* **Subject Phone** (`subject_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the subject.
* **Subject Address** (`subject_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the subject.
* **Photographer Signature** (`photographer_signature`, text, required): Sign with a handwritten signature as the photographer.
* **Subject Signature** (`subject_signature`, text, required): Sign with a handwritten signature as the subject.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required): Enter the date the photo release was signed.
* **Release Authorized by** (`release_authorized_by`, text, required): Confirm that the photo release was authorized by the photographer.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the date of birth of the subject.
* **Nationality** (`nationality`, text, required): Enter the nationality of the subject.
* **Photo Use** (`photo_use`, select_multiple, required): Select the intended use of the photo (e.g. internet, social media, print, etc.).
* **Model Release Version** (`model_release_version`, number, required): Enter the version of the model release.
* **Photo Rights** (`photo_rights`, select_one, required): Select who retains the rights to the photo (photographer, subject, or both).
* **Subject Acknowledgement** (`subject_acknowledgement`, text, required): Enter the acknowledgement from the subject.
* **Photographer Acknowledgement** (`photographer_acknowledgement`, text, required): Enter the acknowledgement from the photographer.
* **Other Use** (`photo_use_other`, text, required): If the photo is to be used for other purposes, enter the intended use.
* **Other Use Details** (`other_use_details`, text, required): If the photo is to be used for other purposes, enter a detailed description of the intended use.
* **Other Use Details** (`other_use_details`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of the intended use of the photo.
