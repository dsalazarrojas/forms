# texas_state_rifle_association_tsra_amp_texas_state_rifle_association_foundation_tsraf - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Texas State Rifle Association and Texas State Rifle Association Foundation photo release form is designed to obtain permission from individuals to use their photos and likenesses. This form is typically used for events, activities, or publications associated with the organizations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a title for the photo release.
2. Fill out the waiver, if applicable.
3. Fill out the release of liability, if applicable.
4. Attach a photo release (the template provided will be a good starting point).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **photo_release_form_templates** (`photo_release_form_templates`, `text`, optional): This is a template for your photo release. You can modify it as needed.
* **waiver** (`waiver`, `text`, optional): This is a waiver, used to grant permission to use your likeness and image.
* **release_of_liability** (`release_of_liability`, `text`, optional): This is a release of liability, used to hold harmless those who may use your photo.
* **photo_release** (`photo_release`, `text`, optional): This is the actual photo release, where you grant permission to use your photo.

## Tips
* Be sure to fill out all fields completely and accurately.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the waiver and release of liability sections.
* Attach the template provided in the photo release section as a starting point, but modify as needed to suit your needs.
* Keep a copy of the signed photo release for your records.
