# Artifact Display Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture information about the artifact, its lender, and display details for a specific exhibition or display. It helps ensure that all necessary permissions, insurance, and other requirements are in place for a smooth and successful display.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with the required information.
2. Review each section carefully to ensure you have included all necessary details.
3. Sign the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lender Name** (`lender_name`, required, text): Enter the name of the artifact's owner or lender.
* **Email Address** (`lender_contact_email`, required, email): Provide the primary contact email for the lender.
* **Phone Number** (`lender_phone`, required, text): Enter the lender's contact number.
* **Address** (`lender_address`, required, text): Enter the lender's mailing address.
* **Organization or Institution** (`lender_organization`, optional, text): If applicable, enter the organization or institution affiliated with the lender.
* **Artifact Name or Title** (`artifact_name`, required, text): Enter the name or title of the artifact.
* **Artifact Description** (`artifact_description`, required, text): Provide a detailed description of the artifact.
* **Type of Artifact** (`artifact_type`, required, select_one): Select the category or classification of the artifact from the provided options.
* **Dimensions or Size** (`artifact_dimensions`, optional, text): Enter the height, width, depth, or weight of the artifact as relevant.
* **Current Condition** (`artifact_condition`, required, select_one): Select the current condition of the artifact from the provided options.
* **Display Location** (`display_location`, required, text): Enter the specific location where the artifact will be displayed (e.g., museum, gallery, school, or event venue).
* **Display Start Date** (`display_period_start`, required, date): Enter the date when the artifact will begin being displayed.
* **Display End Date** (`display_period_end`, required, date): Enter the date when the artifact will end being displayed.
* **Photography of Artifact Permitted** (`photography_permitted`, required, select_one): Select if photography is allowed for display and promotion.
* **Use of Images for Promotion** (`promotional_use`, required, select_one): Select if images can be used for promotional materials.
* **Lender Will Provide Insurance** (`insurance_coverage`, required, select_one): Select if the lender will provide insurance for the artifact during display.
* **Estimated Value of Artifact** (`estimated_value`, optional, number): Enter an approximate monetary value of the artifact.
* **I Consent to the Display of this Artifact** (`lender_consent`, required, select_one): Confirm your consent to display the artifact.
* **Date of Signature** (`signature_date`, required, date): Enter the date this release is executed.
