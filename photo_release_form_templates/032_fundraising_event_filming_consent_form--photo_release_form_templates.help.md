# Fundraising event filming consent form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from participants in a fundraising event for filming purposes. It is essential to ensure that participants are aware of and agree to being filmed, and that their images and videos will be used for the intended purpose. By filling out this form, participants are giving their consent to be filmed and have their images and videos used for the purpose of the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with the relevant information. Please provide accurate and truthful answers to the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Date (1)**: This field is used to enter the date of the fundraising event for which you are participating. If you are unsure of the date, please contact the event organizers.
* **Participant Name (2)**: Please enter your name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Participant Email (3)**: Enter your email address to ensure that we can contact you with any further information or instructions.
* **Filming Location (4)**: If the filming will take place at a specific location, enter the address or details of that location.
* **Photo Release (5)**: Please select 'Yes' if you are comfortable with being photographed or filmed.
* **Video Release (6)**: Please select 'Yes' if you are comfortable with being filmed or interviewed. 
* **Filming Consent (7)**: Please select 'Yes' if you are comfortable with being filmed, interviewed, or have your voice recorded.
* **Participant Phone (8)**: Please enter a phone number where we can contact you with any last-minute information or instructions.
* **Participant Note (9)**: This field is for any additional comments or information you may want to provide.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the event organizers for further guidance.
* Please note that your participation in this event is voluntary, and you can withdraw your consent at any time.
