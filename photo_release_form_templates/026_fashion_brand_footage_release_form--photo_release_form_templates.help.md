# fashion_brand_footage_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The fashion brand footage release form is used to obtain consent for the usage of recorded footage from participants in photo shoots, fashion shows, and other events. This form ensures that individuals understand how their footage will be used and gives the fashion brand the necessary permissions to use it.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your correct name, email, and phone number.
2. Enter the date you gave your consent.
3. Answer "yes" to the question about consenting to use your recorded footage.
4. Select all the ways you consent to use your footage (e.g., for campaigns, social content, brand promotions).
5. Read and understand the additional terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address as it appears on your identification.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number as it appears on your identification.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are giving consent.
* **Consent to use recorded footage** (`consent_to_use_recorded_footage`, `text`, required): Answer "yes" to confirm you consent to use your recorded footage.
* **For campaigns** (`consent_to_use_footage_for_campaigns`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the ways you consent to use your footage (e.g., for campaigns, social content, brand promotions).
* **For social content** (`consent_to_use_footage_for_social_content`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the ways you consent to use your footage (e.g., on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Twitter, YouTube, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Reddit).
* **For brand promotions** (`consent_to_use_footage_for_brand_promotions`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the ways you consent to use your footage (e.g., for product launches, event promotions, fashion show promotions, magazine promotions).
* **Additional terms and conditions** (`additional_terms_and_conditions`, `note`, optional): Read and understand the additional terms and conditions.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `note`, optional): This field is for your signature, which serves as proof of your consent.

Note: The fields marked as optional are not required but are meant to provide further clarification or additional information. Always ensure you read and understand the content of the optional fields before proceeding.
