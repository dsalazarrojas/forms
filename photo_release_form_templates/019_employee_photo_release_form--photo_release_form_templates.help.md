# Employee Photo Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Photo Release Form is a document that employees must sign to authorize the release of their photos for official purposes. This includes marketing materials, office use, or social media.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the form completely and accurately, including your name and date of birth.
2. Select the photo release permission option that applies to you, such as Social Media, Marketing Materials, or Office Use Only.
3. Sign the form to confirm your permission for the photo release.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or other official documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Photo Release Permission**: Choose the option that best describes the intended use of your photo. This will ensure that your photo is used in the correct context.
* **Photo Usage**: Select the specific use of your photo from the provided options.
* **Employee Signature**: Sign your name to confirm that you are granting permission for the photo release.
* **Date**: Enter the current date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Photo Release Version**: This field is for internal tracking purposes only and does not require a response.
## Tips
* Ensure that you have read and understood the purpose and requirements of this form before completing it.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with your supervisor or HR representative.
* Remember to sign the form to confirm your permission for the photo release.
