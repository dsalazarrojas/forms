# Entertainment PR Campaign Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for participants in an entertainment PR campaign to give their consent for the use of their likeness, image, and contact information in promotional activities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the participant information (id: 1)
	* This section is for the participant to provide their contact information. Please enter your name in the text field.
2. Provide contact details (id: 2)
	* This section is for the participant to provide their phone number and any other relevant contact details. Please enter your phone number in the format "XXX-XXX-XXXX".
3. Choose publicity use (id: 3)
	* Select all the ways you consent to be publicized in the entertainment PR campaign:
		+ Option 1: Social media
		+ Option 2: Press releases
		+ Option 3: Other (please specify in the "Note" section)
4. Select publicity method (id: 4)
	* Choose how you would like your likeness and image to be used in the campaign:
		+ Option 1: Photo release
		+ Option 2: Video release
		+ Option 3: Both photo and video release
5. Participant permission (id: 5)
	* This is a note section for any additional comments or concerns the participant has.
6. Submission date (id: 6)
	* This field is for recording the date when the participant signed the form.
7. Signed by (id: 7)
	* This section is for the participant or their representative to sign their name.
8. Participant phone number (id: 8)
	* This field is for the participant to enter their phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_info** (`participant_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your name.
* **contact_details** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact details.
* **publicity_use** (`publicity_use`, select multiple, required/optional): 
	+ Select all the ways you consent to be publicized:
		- Option 1: Social media
		- Option 2: Press releases
		- Option 3: Other (please specify in the "Note" section)
* **publicity_method** (`publicity_method`, select one, required): Choose how you would like your likeness and image to be used:
	+ Option 1: Photo release
	+ Option 2: Video release
	+ Option 3: Both photo and video release
* **participant_permission** (`participant_permission`, note, required/optional): 
	+ This is a note section for any additional comments or concerns.
* **submission_date** (`submission_date`, date, required/optional): Record the date when you signed this form.
* **signed_by** (`signed_by`, text, required/optional): Sign your name.
* **participant_phone_number** (`participant_phone_number`, number, required/optional): Enter your phone number in the format "XXX-XXX-XXXX".

## Tips

* Make sure to read and understand the terms of use before signing the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the campaign team.
* This form is only valid for the duration of the campaign. If you wish to withdraw your consent, please contact the campaign team.
