# Permission To Use Photo Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain permission from individuals or organizations to use their photos for specific purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the following information:

* Fill in the form title and description fields to provide a brief summary of the photo and its intended use.
* Enter your name and email address to provide contact information.
* Provide your phone number for additional contact options.
* Enter the date the photo was taken, if applicable.
* Specify the name of the organization or company associated with the photo.
* Describe the location where the photo was taken.
* Provide a brief description of the photo.
* Specify the photo credit or credit line, if applicable.
* Select the purpose for which you want to use the photo (e.g., marketing, education, social media, or fundraising).
* Select the type of consent you're seeking (e.g., Model/Agency Release, Property Release, Exclusive Use, Non-Exclusive Use, or Rights Management).
* Sign your name to confirm your permission.
* Date the form with the date you signed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): A brief title of the photo and its intended use.
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, optional): A brief description of the photo and its intended use.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Your name as the individual or representative of the organization providing permission.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Your phone number for additional contact options.
* **Form Date** (`form_date`, date, optional): The date the photo was taken, if applicable.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, optional): The name of the organization or company associated with the photo.
* **Photo Location** (`photo_location`, text, optional): The location where the photo was taken.
* **Photo Description** (`photo_description`, text, optional): A brief description of the photo.
* **Photo Credit** (`photo_credit`, text, optional): The photo credit or credit line, if applicable.
* **Use Purpose** (`use_purpose`, select_one, optional): The specific purpose for which you want to use the photo (e.g., marketing, education, social media, fundraising).
* **Consent Type** (`consent_type`, select_one, optional): The type of consent you're seeking (e.g., Model/Agency Release, Property Release, Exclusive Use, Non-Exclusive Use, Rights Management).
* **Signed** (`signed`, text, optional): Your signature to confirm your permission.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, optional): The date you signed the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately to ensure that the photo usage aligns with your preferences.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
* Once completed, this form serves as a permanent record of the permission granted.
