# Trade For Print Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a trade for print agreement between a model and a photographer, outlining the terms and conditions of the usage of the photographs. It ensures both parties have a clear understanding of the agreement, reducing the risk of miscommunication and potential disputes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Photographer Name and Address fields, providing the correct information.
2. Enter the Model Name and Address fields with the corresponding details.
3. Select the Usage option that best describes the intended purpose of the photographs.
4. Enter the Print Sizes, Print Copies, and Print Quantity fields with the agreed-upon values.
5. Fill in the Model Signature, Photographer Signature, Date Submitted, Date Completed, and Date Printed fields with the required dates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photographer Name** (text): Enter the name of the photographer.
* **Photographer Address** (text): Enter the address of the photographer.
* **Model Name** (text): Enter the name of the model.
* **Model Address** (text): Enter the address of the model.
* **Photographer** (text): Enter the name of the photographer (note: this field is a duplicate of the photographer_name field).
* **Agreement Date** (date): Enter the date of the agreement.
* **Usage** (select_one): Select the option that best describes the intended purpose of the photographs. Options include:
	+ Usage for Print Ads
	+ Usage for Social Media
	+ Usage for Editorial
	+ Usage for Other
* **Print Sizes** (number): Enter the agreed-upon print sizes.
* **Print Copies** (text): Enter the agreed-upon print quantity.
* **Print Quantity** (text): Enter the agreed-upon print quantity (note: this field may be redundant with the Print Copies field).
* **Model Signature** (text): Enter the model's signature.
* **Photographer Signature** (text): Enter the photographer's signature.
* **Date Submitted** (date): Enter the date the agreement was submitted.
* **Date Completed** (date): Enter the date the agreement was completed.
* **Date Printed** (date): Enter the date the photographs were printed.

## Tips

* Make sure to review the agreement carefully before signing.
* Both the model and photographer must sign the agreement.
* This form is for trade for print agreements only, not for other types of agreements.
* If you're unsure about any part of the form, consult with the model or photographer before proceeding.
