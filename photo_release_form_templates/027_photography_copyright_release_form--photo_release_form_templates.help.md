# photography_copyright_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The photography copyright release form is a legal document that transfers the rights of a photographer's work to a client. It's used to formalize the agreement between the photographer and the client, ensuring both parties understand the terms of use and ownership of the photographs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and contact information as the client.
2. Fill out your information as the photographer, including your name and contact details.
3. If applicable, describe the usage terms you wish to grant the client.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **photographer_information** (`photographer_info`, text, **required**): This field is for the photographer to enter their name and contact information. Please enter your full name and contact details accurately.
* **client_information** (`client_info`, text, **required**): This field is for the client to enter their name and contact information. Please enter your full name and contact details accurately.
* **usage_terms** (`usage_terms`, text, **optional**): If you're granting the client any specific usage terms, such as usage limits or geographic restrictions, please describe them in this field.
* **signature** (`signature_field`, text, **optional**): This field is for both the photographer and the client to sign and date the agreement. Please sign your name, and date the form to confirm your agreement.

## Tips

* Make sure to read and understand the usage terms carefully before signing the form.
* This form is a legal document, and it's essential to have a clear understanding of the terms before granting or accepting the usage rights.
* If you're unsure about any part of the form or the agreement, please ask your lawyer or legal representative for assistance.
