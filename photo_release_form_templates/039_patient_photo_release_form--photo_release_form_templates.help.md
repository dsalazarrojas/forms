# patient_photo_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The patient photo release form is a medical consent form used to obtain a patient's consent for the release of their photos to third-party organizations. This form ensures that the patient is aware of and agrees to the use of their photos for legitimate purposes, such as medical research or insurance claims.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the **Consent Date** (Field 2) - This is the date on which the patient has given their consent for the photo release.
2.  Select the **Photo Release Consent** (Field 3) - Choose 'Yes' to indicate that the patient has given their consent for the photo release or 'No' if not.
3.  Enter the **Consent Signed By** (Field 4) - This is the name of the person who has obtained the patient's consent.
4.  Enter the **Consent Signed Date** (Field 5) - This is the date on which the patient's consent was signed.
5.  Sign the **Photo Release Signature** (Field 6) - This is a mandatory field for the patient or their authorized representative to sign, indicating that they understand and agree to the photo release.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Consent Date** (Field 2, date, required): The date on which the patient has given their consent for the photo release.
* **Photo Release Consent** (Field 3, select_multiple, required): Choose 'Yes' to indicate that the patient has given their consent for the photo release or 'No' if not.
* **Consent Signed By** (Field 4, text, required): The name of the person who has obtained the patient's consent.
* **Consent Signed Date** (Field 5, date, required): The date on which the patient's consent was signed.
* **Photo Release Signature** (Field 6, text, required): A mandatory field for the patient or their authorized representative to sign, indicating that they understand and agree to the photo release.
