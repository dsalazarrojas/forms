# Art Residency Publicity Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Residency Publicity Consent Form is a document that outlines the terms and conditions under which your image, artwork, and other materials will be used by the residency program in promotional and publicity materials.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read and understand the terms and conditions outlined in the form.
2. Answer each question honestly and accurately.
3. If you need help or clarification on any question, do not hesitate to ask the residency program.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consent Date** (1): Date of consent form. Please enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Artist Name** (2): Full name of artist.
* **Artist Email** (3): Email address.
* **Artist Phone** (4): Contact phone number (optional).
* **Residency Program** (5): Name of residency program.
* **Residency Dates** (6): Start and end dates of residency.
* **Artwork Title** (7): Title of artwork (if applicable).
* **Artwork Description** (8): Description of artwork or project.
* **Photo Consent** (9): Select "Yes - I Consent" to allow the use of your photographs.
* **Photo Usage** (10): Select allowed uses of your image.
* **Artwork Photo Consent** (11): Select "Yes - I Consent" to allow the use of photographs of your artwork.
* **Artwork Usage** (12): Select allowed uses of your artwork images.
* **Video Consent** (13): Select "Yes - I Consent" to allow the use of video recordings.
* **Video Usage** (14): Select allowed uses of video.
* **Interview Consent** (15): Select "Yes - I Consent" to allow the use of interview content.
* **Interview Usage** (16): Select allowed uses of interview content.
* **Name Biography Consent** (17): Select "Yes - I Consent" to allow the use of your name and biography.
* **Statement Consent** (18): Select "Yes - I Consent" to allow the use of artist statements.
* **Geographic Scope** (19): Select where materials can be used (worldwide, North America, United States, Europe, or Other).
* **Time Limit** (20): Select the duration of use for materials (perpetual, 5 years, 3 years, 1 year, or until end of residency).
* **Exclusivity** (21): Select if there are any exclusivity restrictions.
* **Exclusivity Details** (22): Describe any exclusivity terms.
* **Credit Required** (23): Select if credit attribution is required.
* **Credit Format** (24): Specify preferred credit format.
* **Approval Required** (25): Select if approval is required before use.
* **Approval Details** (26): Specify which materials need approval.
* **Withdrawal Rights** (27): Select if you can withdraw your consent later.
* **Additional Terms** (28): Specify any additional terms or conditions.
* **Artist Signature** (29): Sign with full name.
* **Signature Date** (30): Date of signature.
* **Witness Name** (31): Name of witness.
* **Witness Signature** (32): Witness signature.

Note: This help guide is a general guide and may not cover all possible scenarios. If you are unsure about any question or have specific concerns, please contact the residency program directly.
