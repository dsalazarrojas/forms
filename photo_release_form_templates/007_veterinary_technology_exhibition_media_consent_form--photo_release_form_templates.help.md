# Veterinary Technology Exhibition Media Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about an individual's consent for media usage related to their pet's images or videos.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the options that apply to you from the "Templates" field.
2. Choose whether you consent to the photo release or not from the "Photo Release" field.
3. Provide your name in the "Name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Select the correct date from the "Date" field.
6. Choose the correct time from the "Time" field.
7. Provide any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
8. Enter the photo count in the "Photo Count" field.
9. Describe the photo location in the "Photo Location" field.
10. Provide a description of the photo in the "Photo Description" field.
11. Choose whether the photos can be used for various purposes from the "Photo Usage" field.
12. Select whether you consent to the photo release from the "Photo Release" field.
13. Provide your signature in the "Signature" field.
14. Confirm your agreement to the terms of use from the "Agreement" field.
15. Select the correct date from the "Photo Release Form Agreement Date" field.
16. Choose the correct time from the "Photo Release Form Agreement Time" field.
17. Provide any additional notes or comments in the "Photo Release Form Agreement Note" field.
18. Enter the photo count in the "Photo Release Form Agreement Photo Count" field.
19. Describe the photo location in the "Photo Release Form Agreement Photo Location" field.
20. Provide a description of the photo in the "Photo Release Form Agreement Photo Description" field.
21. Choose whether the photos can be used for various purposes from the "Photo Release Form Agreement Photo Usage" field.
22. Select whether you consent to the photo release from the "Photo Release Form Agreement Photo Release" field.
23. Provide your signature in the "Photo Release Form Agreement Signature" field.
24. Confirm your agreement to the terms of use from the "Photo Release Form Agreement Agreement" field.
