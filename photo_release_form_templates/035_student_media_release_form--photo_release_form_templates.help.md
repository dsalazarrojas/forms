## Student Media Release Form - Help Guide
### Purpose
The Student Media Release Form is used to obtain the consent of a parent or guardian for a student's media usage. This form is essential for ensuring that the student's image and name are not used without their parent or guardian's permission.

### How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the student's name in the **Student Name** field.
2. Enter the student's date of birth in the **Date of Birth** field in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
3. Select **True** or **False** to indicate whether the parent or guardian has given their consent in the **Parent or Guardian Consent** field.
4. Select the types of media where the student's image will be used (**Social Media**, **Print Materials**, or **Websites**).
5. Enter any additional notes in the **Additional Notes** field.
6. Enter the email of the parent or guardian in the **Email** field.
7. Enter the phone of the parent or guardian in the **Phone** field.
8. Enter the date and time of the parent's or guardian's consent in the **Consent Date** and **Consent Time** fields respectively.
9. Select **True** or **False** to indicate whether the school administrator has given their consent in the **School Administrator Consent** field.
10. Select **True** or **False** to indicate whether the educator has given their consent in the **Educator Consent** field.
11. Enter the name of the parent or guardian in the **Parent or Guardian Name** field.
12. Enter the contact details of the parent or guardian in the **Parent or Guardian Contact** field.
13. Select **True** or **False** to indicate whether the parent or guardian has given their consent again in the **Student Media Release Form Parent Consent 2** field.
14. Enter the signature of the student and the parent or guardian in the **Educator Signature** and **Parent or Guardian Signature** fields respectively.
15. Select the status of the consent (**Pending**, **Approved**, or **Rejected**).
16. Enter any additional notes in the **Student Media Release Form Consent Notes** field.
17. Enter the date and time of the second parent's or guardian's consent in the **Consent Date 2** and **Consent Time 2** fields respectively.
18. Enter any additional notes in the **Student Media Release Form Educator Notes** field.
19. Enter any additional notes in the **Parent or Guardian Notes** field.
20. Enter any additional notes in the **School Administrator Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name to be used for media purposes.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the student's date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Parent or Guardian Consent**: Select **True** to indicate that the parent or guardian has given their consent, or select **False** if not.
* **Image Use**: Select the types of media where the student's image will be used. Options include **Social Media**, **Print Materials**, and **Websites**.
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the student's media usage.
* **Email**: Enter the email of the parent or guardian.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone of the parent or guardian.
* **Consent Date**: Enter the date of the parent's or guardian's consent in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Consent Time**: Enter the time of the parent's or guardian's consent.
* **School Administrator Consent**: Select **True** to indicate that the school administrator has given their consent, or select **False** if not.
* **Educator Consent**: Select **True** to indicate that the educator has given their consent, or select **False** if not.
* **Parent or Guardian Name**: Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Contact**: Enter the contact details of the parent or guardian.
* **Student Media Release Form Parent Consent 2**: Select **True** to indicate that the parent or guardian has given their consent again, or select **False** if not.
* **Educator Signature**: Enter the signature of the student.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature**: Enter the signature of the parent or guardian.
* **Status**: Select the status of the consent. Options include **Pending**, **Approved**, and **Rejected**.
* **Student Media Release Form Consent Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the consent.
* **Consent Date 2**: Enter the date of the second parent's or guardian's consent.
* **Consent Time 2**: Enter the time of the second parent's or guardian's consent.
* **Student Media Release Form Educator Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the student's media usage.
* **Parent or Guardian Notes**: Enter any additional notes from the parent or guardian.
* **School Administrator Notes**: Enter any additional notes from the school administrator.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
* Double-check the information entered in the fields before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact the school administrator for clarification.
