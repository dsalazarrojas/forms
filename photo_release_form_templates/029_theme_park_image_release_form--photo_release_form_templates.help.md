# Theme Park Image Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain guests' consent for the use of their images taken during events held in the theme park. The form asks for basic guest information, event details, and photo release options to ensure that guests are aware of how their images will be used.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your guest's name in the "Guest's Name" field.
2. Enter your guest's email address in the "Guest's Email" field.
3. Enter your guest's phone number in the "Guest's Phone" field.
4. Select one or more of the photo release options in the "Photo Release" field.
5. Choose the event your guest participated in from the "Event" field.
6. Select any additional photos you would like to release in the "Additional Photos" field.
7. Choose any guest services you would like to be included in the photo release in the "Guest Services" field.
8. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
9. Enter the time of the event in the "Event Time" field.
10. Select the photo release option for the images from the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Guest's Name** (1): Enter the name of the guest who is giving consent for their image to be used.
- **Guest's Email** (2): Enter the email address of the guest for contact purposes.
- **Guest's Phone** (3): Enter the phone number of the guest for any follow-up communication.
- **Photo Release** (4): Select how you want your image to be used by the event organizers, such as for marketing, promotional, or commercial use.
- **Event** (5): Choose the event where the image was taken.
- **Additional Photos** (6): Select any other photos you would like to release in addition to the ones chosen in the "Photo Release" field.
- **Guest Services** (7): Choose any guest services that you would like to be included in the photo release.
- **Date of Birth** (8): Enter your date of birth to help the event organizers verify your identity.
- **Event Time** (9): Enter the time of the event where the images were taken.
- **Photo Release** (10): Select the photo release option for the images from the event.
