# Photo Print Release Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Photo Print Release Form Template" is a document used to provide permissions and information for photo prints. It's essential for both photographers and their clients to understand the usage rights and permissions for the printed photos.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the "Print Released Form" field (Field 1) with the specific details of the print.
2. Select the type of release you wish to provide (Field 2) - Client, Model, Both, or any other option that may be available in the future. This will determine who will be releasing the photo.
3. Enter the date of the release (Field 3) if required.
4. Provide the name of the photographer (Field 4) who took the photo.
5. Enter the contact number (Field 5) of the photographer or the studio where the photo was taken.
6. Enter the email address (Field 6) of the photographer or the studio where the photo was taken.
7. Indicate if the photographer is willing to be contacted (Field 7) - Yes or No. This will determine how the photographer can be reached.
8. Enter any additional notes or conditions (Field 8) for the photo release.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Print Released Form** (Field 1, required):
	+ This field is for entering the specific details of the print, such as the image title, photographer, or shoot date.
* **Photo Released** (Field 2, required):
	+ This field is for selecting the type of release you wish to provide. The options are:
		+ Client: The client is releasing the photo.
		+ Model: The model is releasing the photo.
		+ Both: Both the client and the model are releasing the photo.
* **Photo Release Date** (Field 3, optional):
	+ This field is for entering the date of the release, if required.
* **Photographer Name** (Field 4, optional):
	+ This field is for entering the name of the photographer who took the photo.
* **Contact Number** (Field 5, optional):
	+ This field is for entering the contact number of the photographer or the studio where the photo was taken.
* **Photographer Email** (Field 6, optional):
	+ This field is for entering the email address of the photographer or the studio where the photo was taken.
* **Photographer Contact** (Field 7, optional):
	+ This field is for indicating if the photographer is willing to be contacted - Yes or No.
* **Photo Release Agreement** (Field 8, optional):
	+ This field is for entering any additional notes or conditions for the photo release.
