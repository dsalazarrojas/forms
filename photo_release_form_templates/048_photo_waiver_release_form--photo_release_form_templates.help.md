# photo_waiver_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The photo_waiver_release_form is used to obtain a photo waiver for a photo release event. It captures the necessary information for a photo release agreement between the subject and the person taking the photo, and ensures that both parties have a clear understanding of the event's purpose, date, time, and location.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the **first_name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **last_name** field.
3. Enter your email address in the **email** field (if you have an email address).
4. Select the date and time of the photo release in the **photo_release_date** and **photo_release_time** fields, respectively.
5. Enter the location of the photo release in the **location** field.
6. Select the purpose of the photo release in the **release_purpose** field.
7. Choose the date and time of the photo release in the **release_date** and **release_time** fields, respectively.
8. Sign your name in the **signature** field.
9. Select the type of photo release in the **photo_release_type** field.
10. Choose the method of photo capture (e.g., camera, phone, etc.) in the **photo_capture_device** field.
11. Select the method of photo capture (e.g., camera, phone, etc.) in the **photo_capture_method** field.
12. Enter a brief explanation of the photo release in the **location_2** field.
13. Add any additional comments about the photo release in the **additional_comments** field.
14. Enter your contact phone number in the **contact_number** field.
15. Enter the name of the person to contact in the case of an emergency in the **contact_name** field.
16. Select the date and time you signed in the **signature_date** and **signature_time** fields.
17. Enter the date and time you signed the form in the **date_signed** and **time_signed** fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **last_name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **photo_release_date** (`photo_release_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the photo release (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **photo_release_time** (`photo_release_time`, time, optional): Select the time of the photo release (12:00 AM).
* **location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location of the photo release.
* **release_purpose** (`release_purpose`, select_one, optional): Select the purpose of the photo release.
* **release_date** (`release_date`, date, required): Choose the date of the photo release.
* **release_time** (`release_time`, time, required): Select the time of the photo release.
* **signature** (`signature`, text, required): Sign your name.
* **photo_release_type** (`photo_release_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of photo release.
* **photo_capture_device** (`photo_capture_device`, select_multiple, optional): Select the device used to capture the photo.
* **photo_capture_method** (`photo_capture_method`, select_multiple, optional): Select the method of photo capture.
* **location_2** (`location_2`, text, optional): Enter a brief explanation of the photo release.
* **additional_comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments about the photo release.
* **contact_number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **contact_name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **signature_date** (`signature_date`, date, optional): Select the date you signed the form.
* **signature_time** (`signature_time`, time, optional): Select the time you signed the form.
* **date_signed** (`date_signed`, date, required): Enter the date you signed the form.
* **time_signed** (`time_signed`, time, required): Select the time you signed the form.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check the accuracy of your information.
* Sign the form in the **signature** field with your actual signature.
* Fill out the **date_signed** and **time_signed** fields with the actual date and time you signed the form.
* Keep a copy of the form for your records.
