# Photography Payment Plan Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photography Payment Plan Form is used to collect information from customers about their photography payment plans. This information will help us understand their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the customer name field.
2. Enter your email address in the customer email field.
3. Enter your payment plan ID in the customer payment plan ID field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **photography_payment_plan_form** (`payment_plan`, text, optional): This is the main text field for describing your photography payment plan. Please enter a brief description of your payment plan, including any relevant details about the services or products you are interested in purchasing.
* **customer_name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it is typically spelled and formatted.
* **customer_email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you about your photography payment plan.
* **payment_plan_id** (`payment_plan_id`, number, optional): Enter your payment plan ID, which is a unique identifier for your payment plan. This will help us track your payment plan and ensure that we have all the necessary information to fulfill your needs.

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information in each field to ensure that we can process your request correctly.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask our staff for assistance.
* We will respond to your submission as soon as possible after receiving it.
