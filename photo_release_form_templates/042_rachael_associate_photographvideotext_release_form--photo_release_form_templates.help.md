# rachel_associate_photographvideotext_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from a photographer for the release of photos, videos, or text. It's an essential step in the publishing process to obtain the necessary permissions and agreements from photographers to use their work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields marked as "required".
2. Select the type of media you are releasing for (Photo, Video, or Text).
3. Fill out any additional information, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photographer Name** (`photographer_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the photographer who took the photo or created the text.
* **Photographer Email** (`photographer_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the photographer.
* **Photographer Address** (`photographer_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the address of the photographer.
* **Photographer Phone Number** (`photographer_phone_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the photographer.
* **Release for Use** (`release_for_use`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of media you are releasing (Photo, Video, or Text).
* **Additional Release** (`additional_release`, `note`, required/optional): This is a note field, use it to provide any additional information about the photographer or the media being released.
