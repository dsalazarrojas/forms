# League Photo Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The League Photo Order Form is designed to collect information from individuals who want to place a photo order. This form helps you gather the necessary details for a smooth and efficient photo ordering process.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the required information on the "Order Details" page.
2. Select your appointment date and start time on the second page.
3. Review your information for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details** (`Order Details`, text, required/optional: optional): This field is for you to provide any additional information about your photo order. Be as detailed as possible to ensure that we can process your order correctly.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, required/optional: optional): Please select the date of your appointment when you want to take your photo.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required/optional: optional): Select the start time of your appointment. This will help us schedule your photo shoot accordingly.

## Tips
Please note that the fields marked as "optional" can be left blank if you don't have the required information. However, providing the required information for each field will ensure that we can process your photo order accurately and promptly.
