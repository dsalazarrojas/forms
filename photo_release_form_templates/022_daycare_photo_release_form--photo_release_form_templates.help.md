# Daycare Photo Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daycare Photo Release Form is a document that allows parents or guardians to grant permission for their child's photos to be taken and shared by the daycare.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to sign the form to indicate your approval of the photo release.
3. Review the daycare's photo taking and sharing policies to understand how your child's photos will be used.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name** (1): Please enter your name as the parent or guardian of the child.
* **Email** (2): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached in case we need to contact you.
* **Phone** (3): Enter your phone number where you can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Date of Capture** (4): Enter the date when the photo was taken.
* **Photo Description** (5): Describe the photo in a brief sentence or two.
* **Release Type** (6): Choose the type of release you're granting: General, Event, or Event & General. This determines how and when the photo can be used.
* **Release Term** (7): Enter the date when the photo release expires. 
* **Release Date** (8): Enter the date when the photo release will take effect.
* **Signature** (9): Sign below to acknowledge that you've read and understood the photo release terms.
* **Photo Release** (10): Please provide any additional comments or concerns regarding the photo. 

## Tips
- Make sure to review and understand the daycare's photo taking and sharing policies before completing this form.
- If you have any concerns or questions, don't hesitate to reach out to the daycare staff.
- The daycare will ensure that your child's photos are handled with care and respect.
