# School Website Image Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture the necessary information and consent for images to be published on the school's website. It includes details about the student, the image, and the parent or guardian's consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the parent's or guardian's information in the "Parent Information" field.
2. Provide a description of the image in the "Photo Information" field.
3. Specify any restrictions or guidelines for using the image in the "Restrictions" field.
4. Sign the form digitally in the "E-Signature" field.
5. If necessary, enter the date of submission.
6. Select the type of release for the photo (Parent, School, Both).
7. Enter the student's name.
8. Sign the form digitally as the parent or guardian in the "Parent Signature" field.
9. Enter the student's ID number.
10. Enter the name of the school.
11. Enter the URL of the school's website.
12. Enter the URL of the photo.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Information**: Enter the parent's or guardian's name and contact information.
* **Photo Information**: A brief description of the photo, including the student's name, date, and event.
* **Restrictions**: Any guidelines or restrictions for using the image, such as publication dates or locations.
* **E-Signature**: Sign the form digitally to confirm that you have reviewed and agree to the terms of the form.
* **Submission Date**: If this form is being submitted for an event or publication with a specific date.
* **Photo Release**: Select which entities (Parent, School, Both) should be granted permission for the photo release.
* **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student featured in the photo.
* **Parent Signature**: Sign the form digitally as the parent or guardian.
* **Student ID**: Enter the student's ID number, if applicable.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the school where the student attends.
* **School Website URL**: Enter the URL of the school's website where the image will be published.
* **Photo URL**: Enter the URL where the image is located.
