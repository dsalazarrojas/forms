# Community Photography Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for obtaining informed consent from individuals or their guardians for photography and media usage within the community. It's essential to complete this form to ensure that participants understand how their images will be used and shared.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Review the Form

Before starting, please review the form carefully to understand the purpose and the consent process.

### Step 2: Complete the Form

1.  Enter the name of the participant or subject.
2.  Answer if the participant is a minor (under 18).
3.  Enter the contact email address and phone number.
4.  Provide the date of the event or shoot.
5.  Enter the location of the event.
6.  Choose your permission for still photography, video recording, and audio recording.
7.  Choose your consent for website and social media usage, and print media and promotional use.
8.  Read and understand the revocation policy.
9.  Sign the form with your digital signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of Participant or Subject**: Enter the name of the participant or subject.
* **Is the participant a minor - under 18?**: Check 'True' if the participant is under 18, 'False' otherwise.
* **Contact Email Address**: Enter a valid email address to contact you or the participant's guardian.
* **Contact Phone Number**: Enter a valid phone number to contact you or the participant's guardian.
* **Date of Event or Shoot**: Enter the date of the event or shoot.
* **Location of the Event**: Describe the location where the event or shoot occurred.
* **Permission for Still Photography**: Choose 'I grant full permission' to allow still photography.
* **Permission for Video Recording**: Choose 'I grant full permission' to allow video recording.
* **Permission for Audio Recording**: Choose 'I grant full permission' to allow audio recording.
* **Consent for Website and Social Media Use**: Choose 'True' to allow website and social media usage.
* **Consent for Print Media and Promotional Use**: Choose 'True' to allow print media and promotional use.
* **Revocation Policy**: Read and understand that consent may be revoked at any time by contacting us in writing.
* **Digital Signature**: Type your full name to confirm your consent.

## Tips

* Always ensure you have obtained the consent before taking any photographs or recordings.
* Keep in mind that consent can be revoked at any time.
* If you're unsure about any part of the form, please contact us for clarification.
