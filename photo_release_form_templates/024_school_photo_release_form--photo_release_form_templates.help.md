# School Photo Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The School Photo Release Form is used to obtain permission from parents to take and use photos of their child for various school purposes, such as publishing on the school's website, social media, or other school materials. This form helps ensure that parents are aware of and agree to the intended use of their child's photos.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Parent Information** (`school_photo_release_form_parent_info`): Enter the parent/guardian's name and any additional information as needed.
2. **Parent Name** (`school_photo_release_form_parent_info_2`): Enter the name of the parent/guardian giving permission.
3. **Photo Release** (`school_photo_release_form_photo_release`): Select 'yes' to grant permission for the school to take and use photos of your child, or 'no' to decline.
4. **Additional Comments** (`school_photo_release_form_additional_comments`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the photo release, such as specific restrictions or concerns.
5. **School Email** (`school_photo_release_form_school_email`): Enter the school's email address.
6. **School Phone** (`school_photo_release_form_school_phone`): Enter the school's phone number.
7-13 are **Additional Comments** (fields 5-8 are repeated): Enter any additional comments or notes as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Parent Information** (`school_photo_release_form_parent_info`): Enter the parent/guardian's name and any additional information as needed.
- **Parent Name** (`school_photo_release_form_parent_info_2`): Enter the name of the parent/guardian giving permission.
- **Photo Release** (`school_photo_release_form_photo_release`): Select 'yes' to grant permission for the school to take and use photos of your child, or 'no' to decline. Please note that this permission covers all types of photos and materials, so select accordingly.
- **Additional Comments** (`school_photo_release_form_additional_comments`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the photo release, such as specific restrictions or concerns.
- **School Email** (`school_photo_release_form_school_email`): Enter the school's email address.
- **School Phone** (`school_photo_release_form_school_phone`): Enter the school's phone number.
 
Note: Fields 7-13 are additional comment fields that can be used for any other information or notes that you may want to include.
