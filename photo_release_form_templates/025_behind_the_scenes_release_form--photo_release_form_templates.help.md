# Behind The Scenes Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Behind The Scenes Release Form is used to collect information from participants in a behind-the-scenes project, such as a film, television, or commercial production, to obtain necessary permissions and details for the project's use of their likeness or voice.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the required information accurately and completely.
2. Select the correct options for each field.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth, if applicable.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates and information about this project.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if you want us to contact you directly.
* **Mailing Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your mailing address, if you want us to mail you any materials related to this project.
* **Project/Production Name** (`project_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the project or production you are participating in.
* **Type of Project** (`project_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of project you are participating in (e.g. Film, Television, etc.).
* **Filming/Production Location** (`filming_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the project was filmed or produced.
* **Date of Filming** (`filming_date`, date, required): Enter the date the project was filmed or produced.
* **Permission to Use Behind-the-Scenes Photos** (`permit_photos`, select_one, required): Select whether or not we have permission to use behind-the-scenes photos of you.
* **Permission to Use Behind-the-Scenes Video** (`permit_video`, select_one, required): Select whether or not we have permission to use behind-the-scenes video of you.
* **Permission to Use Audio/Voice Recording** (`permit_audio`, select_one, optional): Select whether or not we have permission to use audio/voice recordings of you.
* **Permitted Use of Media** (`media_usage`, select_multiple, required): Select the permitted use of media for this project (e.g. Promotional Materials, Social Media, etc.).
* **Face May Be Visible in Behind-the-Scenes Content** (`face_recognition`, select_one, required): Select whether or not your face may be visible in behind-the-scenes content.
* **Name May Be Used with Behind-the-Scenes Content** (`name_usage`, select_one, optional): Select whether or not your name may be used with behind-the-scenes content.
* **Content Restrictions** (`content_restrictions`, text, optional): Enter any specific restrictions on how the content can be used.
* **Compensation for Participation** (`compensation_received`, select_one, optional): Select whether or not you received compensation for your participation in this project.
* **Compensation Amount** (`compensation_amount`, text, optional): Enter the amount of compensation you received, if applicable.
* **I Acknowledge Understanding of This Release** (`consent_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Select whether or not you understand and agree to the terms of this release.
* **I Consent to the Terms of This Release** (`consent_final`, select_one, required): Select whether or not you agree to the terms of this release.
* **Date of Release** (`release_date`, date, required): Enter the date of release.
