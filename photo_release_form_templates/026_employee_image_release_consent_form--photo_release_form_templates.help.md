# Employee Image Release Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain an employee's consent to use their photo and image in company materials and platforms.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill in your details:** Enter your employee name and ID number in the respective fields.
2. **Choose the use of your photo:** Select the specific areas where you are willing to have your photo used.
3. **Sign and date the form:** Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as per your employment records.
* **Employee ID Number** (`employee_id_number`, text, required): Type your official employee identification number.
* **Date of Release** (`date_of_release`, date, optional): Specify the date when you want your photo to be used, or leave it blank if you do not have a specific date in mind.
* **Employee Photo** (`employee_photo`, text, optional): If you want to attach a physical photo, please do so here.
* **We can use this information for** (`company_uses`, select_one, optional): Select the options where you want your photo to be used, or choose "None of the above" if you do not want your photo to be used in any of these platforms.
* **I give permission** (`employee_photo_release_consent`, select_one, optional): Choose "to use my name, photo, and image" to give your consent, or select "neither the company nor I" if you do not consent.
* **If yes, the photo will be used until** (`release_date`, date, optional): If you choose "to use my name, photo, and image," specify the date when you want your photo to be removed from use.
* **Signature of Employee** (`signature`, text, optional): Sign and date your consent to confirm your agreement.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Date your signature to confirm the form completion date.
