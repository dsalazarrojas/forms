# Consent And Release Form For Interviewing Photographing And Videotaping (United States) - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to obtain consent and release from participants when interviewing, photographing, or videotaping. This form ensures that participants understand their rights and liabilities, including media usage and release of liability.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in your personal information, including your full name, address, and phone number.
2. Select all the media types you consent to, such as being interviewed, photographed, or videotaped.
3. Choose the specific usage rights you grant, such as educational, commercial, or social media.
4. Confirm that you release the organization from any liability arising from the use of your image or voice.
5. Acknowledge that you will not receive financial compensation for this release.
6. Confirm your age status. If you are under 18 years old, please enter your parent or guardian's name.
7. Sign this form electronically, providing your full name as confirmation of your consent and release.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name of Participant** (`participant_full_name`, `text`, required): Please fill in your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Street Address** (`participant_address`, `text`, required): Please provide your street address.
* **City, State, and Zip Code** (`participant_city_state_zip`, `text`, required): Enter your city, state, and zip code.
* **Phone Number** (`participant_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Name of Project or Production** (`project_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the project or production you are participating in.
* **Consent to Media Types** (`media_types_consented`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all media types you consent to, such as being interviewed, photographed, or videotaped.
* **Usage Rights Grant** (`usage_rights_grant`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the specific usage rights you grant, such as educational, commercial, or social media.
* **Release of Liability** (`release_of_liability`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you release the organization from any liability arising from the use of your image or voice.
* **Compensation Acknowledgment** (`compensation_acknowledgment`, `select_one`, required): Acknowledge that you will not receive financial compensation for this release.
* **Participant Age Status** (`age_verification`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your age status. If you are under 18 years old, please enter your parent or guardian's name.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your parent or guardian if you are under 18 years old.
* **Electronic Signature** (`signature_confirmation`, `text`, required): Sign this form electronically, providing your full name as confirmation of your consent and release.
* **Date of Signature** (`date_signed`, `date`, required): Date of your electronic signature.
