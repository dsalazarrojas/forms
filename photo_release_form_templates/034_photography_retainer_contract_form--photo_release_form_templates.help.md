# photography_retainer_contract_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The <strong>Photography Retainer Contract Form</strong> is a document that outlines the terms and conditions of a contract between a photo studio and a client. It covers the details of the services offered, payment terms, and any other essential agreements.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in the client information on the first field.
2.  Select the services offered by your photo studio in the services_offered field.
3.  Specify the retainer terms and conditions in the retainer_terms field.
4.  If applicable, specify any additional retainer terms in the retainer_terms_2 field.
5.  Sign and date the contract on your part in the <strong>client_signature</strong> and <strong>photo_studio_signature</strong> fields.
6.  Fill in the time and date of the contract agreement on the date and time fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Info** (`client_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter the client's details, such as name, contact information, and any other relevant information.
* **Photo Studio Info** (`photo_studio_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter the photo studio's details, such as name, contact information, and any other relevant information.
* **Services Offered** (`services_offered`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ **Studio Services**: Services offered by the photo studio.
	+ **Photo Shoot Services**: Services offered by the photo shoot.
	+ **Editing**: Editing services.
	+ **Archiving**: Services for archiving photos.
	+ **Retouching**: Retouching services.
	+ **Printing**: Printing services.
* **Retainer Terms** (`retainer_terms`, `text`, required: false): Specify the retainer terms and conditions.
* **Additional Retainer Terms** (`retainer_terms_2`, `text`, required: false): If any additional retainer terms apply, specify them here.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, `text`, required: false): Sign and date the contract as the client.
* **Photo Studio Signature** (`photo_studio_signature`, `text`, required: false): Sign and date the contract as the photo studio.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Fill in the date of the contract agreement.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Fill in the time of the contract agreement.
