# Tree Planting Event Photo Release Consent - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain photo and media release from participants to use their likeness and photos taken during a tree planting event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please provide your name and the name of the parent or guardian (if applicable).
2. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
3. Enter your phone number for easy contact.
4. Check the "Photo Release" box if you consent to the use of your photos.
5. Check the "Media Release" box if you consent to the use of your media (audio/video).
6. Provide the date of the event in MM/DD/YY format.
7. Provide the event time in 12:00 AM format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tree Planting Event Info**: This is the description of the event where the photos were taken. Please provide a brief description of the event and its purpose.
* **Name**: Please enter your name (if you are an adult) or your name and the name of the parent or guardian (if you are a minor).
* **Parent/Guardian Name**: Please enter the name of your parent or guardian (if you are a minor).
* **Email**: Please enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Please enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Photo Release**: Check this box to indicate that you consent to the use of your photos. This will allow us to use your images in our promotional materials.
* **Media Release**: Check this box to indicate that you consent to the use of your media (audio/video). This will allow us to use your recordings in our promotional materials.
* **Event Date**: Please enter the date of the event in MM/DD/YY format.
* **Event Time**: Please enter the event time in 12:00 AM format.

## Tips
- Please note that this form must be completed by an adult or a minor accompanied by their parent or guardian.
- If you are a minor, please have your parent or guardian fill out this form on your behalf.
- Make sure to check the "Photo Release" and "Media Release" boxes if you consent to the use of your photos and media.
- Please ensure that the "Event Date" and "Event Time" are correct to avoid any confusion.
