Privacy Policy

GIC Forms is a public template library. The site may collect basic analytics and standard web server logs to understand aggregate traffic, page usage, and deployment health.

What This Site Does Not Do

  • It does not sell template downloads as user accounts.
  • It does not require visitors to submit personal information to browse the public library.
  • It does not claim that template fields are appropriate for every jurisdiction or workflow.

Third-Party Services

The site may use analytics, CDN, and hosting providers to deliver pages and measure aggregate performance. Templates themselves may link to external raw files hosted on GitHub.

Your Responsibility
