Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 25, 2026.

GIC Forms is a public template library and browser-based form editor operated from forms.gic.mx. This notice describes what information is processed when you browse the site, preview templates, save forms locally, or deploy a form to a third-party platform.

Who Controls This Site

The controller for the website content and browser application is the GIC Forms project. For privacy questions or rights requests, use the contact paths listed on Contact or open an issue in the public repository if your request relates to site behavior.

Information Processed

  • Server and CDN logs: hosting, CDN, and network providers may process IP address, user agent, request URL, referrer, and timestamp information needed to deliver and secure the site.
  • Optional analytics: Google Analytics 4 is loaded only after you opt in through the site's consent banner. When enabled, analytics may process pseudonymous usage information such as page views, device/browser metadata, approximate geography, and interaction events.
  • Browser storage: the site uses localStorage for non-sensitive preferences and locally saved forms, and sessionStorage for deployment tokens and similar session-only secrets. Saved forms remain in your browser until you delete them. Session-stored tokens are cleared when the browser session ends.
  • Form content you save locally: if you use "My Forms," the YAML content, title, category, and save timestamps are stored only in your browser on your device unless you separately export or deploy them.
  • Third-party deployment data: when you choose to deploy to Cloudflare Workers, Google Forms, Google Sheets, Tally, Formbricks, ONA, or KoboToolbox, the credentials and form payload you provide are sent directly to the selected platform or to the configured bridge worker needed to complete that action.
  • GitHub-hosted content: preview pages fetch YAML and help-guide files from the public GitHub repository and GitHub raw-content infrastructure.
  • Subscription data: if you purchase a Pro or Business subscription, payment is processed by Stripe. GIC Forms receives a subscription status token (JWT) stored in localStorage. No payment card details are stored by this site.

Cookies And Similar Technologies

The site uses browser storage technologies rather than an account system. Analytics cookies or equivalent analytics storage are not activated unless you consent. Functional storage may still be used for theme preference, saved local forms, and session-only deployment state.

Purposes Of Processing

  • To deliver pages, templates, and preview functionality.
  • To remember your browser-side preferences and locally saved work.
  • To execute a deployment or export action that you explicitly request.
  • To measure site usage only when analytics consent has been granted.
  • To secure, debug, and maintain the service.

Legal Bases

Where GDPR-style rules apply, GIC Forms relies on consent for optional analytics and legitimate interests for basic site delivery, security logging, debugging, and local browser functionality requested by the user. If Mexican privacy law applies, this notice also serves as an Aviso de Privacidad for the website's own processing activities.

Sharing And Processors

Depending on the feature you use, data may be processed by infrastructure and service providers such as GitHub, CDN/hosting providers, Google (for Analytics or Google Workspace deployment), Cloudflare, Stripe, Tally, Formbricks, ONA, or KoboToolbox. Those providers act under their own terms and privacy notices for the services they operate.

Advertising Networks

Display advertisements may appear on this site in the future, served by the following advertising networks. These networks may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to serve ads relevant to your interests.

  • Google AdSense (Publisher ID: pub-1384946079798757) — Google Privacy Policy
  • Facebook Audience Network (App ID: 1934771063562486) — Meta Privacy Policy

You may opt out of interest-based advertising through your browser settings, the Digital Advertising Alliance, or your device's ad preferences.

International Transfers

The site and its service providers may process data outside your country of residence, including in the United States and other jurisdictions where the relevant providers operate.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or port personal data, and to withdraw consent for analytics at any time. Under Mexican law, you may also exercise ARCO rights: access, rectification, cancellation, and opposition. California residents may request disclosure of categories processed and request deletion, subject to applicable exceptions. GIC Forms does not sell personal information in exchange for money.

How To Exercise Rights

For browser-stored data under your control, you can clear local site data directly in your browser. For project-level requests, contact the project through the channels listed on Contact and include enough detail to identify the relevant page, action, or data element.

Data Retention

Analytics data retention is governed by Google Analytics settings once consent is granted. Browser-stored YAML, preferences, and saved forms remain on your device until deleted by you. Session tokens are intended to expire at the end of the browser session.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and the project does not intentionally collect personal information from children through the public template library.

Your Responsibility When You Deploy A Template

If you download or deploy a template, you are responsible for your own privacy notices, consent language, data retention, processor contracts, and regulatory compliance for the actual form you publish.

Changes To This Notice

This page may be updated when the site's data flows, analytics behavior, or deployment features change. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above.
