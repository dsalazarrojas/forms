# Voice Search Optimization Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help website owners and developers assess and improve the voice search optimization of their websites.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all the requested information.
2. Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
3. If you have any doubts or questions about any field, please don't hesitate to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Website Details (1)**: This field is where you should provide information about your website, such as the website URL and any other relevant details.
* **Goals (2)**: Select all the goals you are currently trying to achieve with your website, such as Website Optimization, Voice Search Optimization, or SEO Audits.
* **Website Readiness (3)**: Indicate whether your website is ready for voice search optimization by selecting True or False.
* **Current Technology (4)**: Select any currently implemented technologies, such as Google Search Console or Bing Webmaster Tools.
* **Current Goals (5)**: Provide a brief description of your current goals for your website.
* **Target Audience (6)**: Select all the target audiences you want to reach with your website, such as Users or Businesses.
* **Target Language (7)**: Choose the language your target audience uses when interacting with your website.
* **Additional Notes (8)**: Provide any additional information that might be helpful for us to know about your website and voice search needs.
* **Contact Information (9)**: Enter your contact information, including email address.
* **Phone Number (10)**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Email Address (11)**: Enter your email address.
* **Company Name (12)**: Enter your company name.
* **Location (13)**: Enter your business location.
* **Website (14)**: Enter your website URL.
* **Current Search Engine (15)**: Select your current search engine, such as Google or Bing.
* **Current Audits (16)**: Select any current audits you are performing on your website.
* **Additional Comments (17)**: Provide any additional comments or feedback you might have about your voice search needs.
* **Internal Search Console (18)**: Select your internal search console, such as Google Search Console or Bing Webmaster Tools.
* **Sitemap (19)**: Enter your website's sitemap URL (if applicable).
* **Internal Search API (20)**: Select your internal search API, such as Google Search API or Bing Webmaster Tools.
* **Internal Search Engine API (21)**: Select your internal search engine API, such as Google Search API or Bing Webmaster Tools.
* **Search Console (22)**: Select your search console, such as Google or Bing.
* **Analytics (23)**: Select your analytics tool, such as Google Analytics or Bing Web Analytics.
* **Internal Search Report (24)**: Indicate whether you have a search report, by selecting True or False.
* **Submit (25)**: Click this button to submit your form and receive personalized assistance with your voice search optimization efforts.
