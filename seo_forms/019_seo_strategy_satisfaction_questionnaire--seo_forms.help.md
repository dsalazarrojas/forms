# SEO Strategy Satisfaction Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from clients on their experiences with our SEO services. Your input will help us refine our services and improve your overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the options that best describe your experiences with our agency. Answer each field according to your true feelings and satisfaction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (1): Please enter your name, as it appears on your contract or communication with our agency.
* **Agency** (2): Type in the name of the agency you are working with.
* **Email** (3): Enter your email address to enable us to follow up on your issue, if necessary. This is optional, but it will help us to contact you directly.
* **Performance** (4): Choose one option that best describes your experience with our agency's performance:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Poor
* **Service Quality** (5): Select all that apply to describe your experience with our agency's service quality:
	+ On time
	+ On budget
	+ Communication
	+ Support
	+ Professionalism
* **Time Management** (6): If applicable, please provide a specific date and time when you started working with our agency. This will help us understand your overall experience.
* **Follow up after submission** (7): If you would like to receive a follow-up on your issue, please enter a brief message here. This will enable us to contact you back if needed.
* **Submission Date** (8): If you know the exact date of submission of your issue, please enter it here. If you don't, you can leave this field empty, and we'll work with the date of submission as shown on your initial message.
Note that you can provide feedback as much as you like without worrying about formatting or style; we value your input more than your writing abilities. Thank you for taking the time to provide feedback; it truly matters to us!
