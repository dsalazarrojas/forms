# Link Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Link Submission Form is designed for users to submit links to be reviewed or approved by our team. It's essential to fill out the form accurately to ensure that your link is reviewed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Link Submission Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the link you're submitting.
2. Enter the URL of the link you're submitting.
3. Provide a brief description of the link.
4. Select the customer details relevant to the submission.
5. Enter the customer's email address.
6. Enter the customer's phone number (if available).
7. Choose a submission date and time.
8. Fill out any other required or optional fields as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Link Submission Form** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the title of the link you're submitting.
* **Link URL** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the URL of the link you're submitting.
* **Description** (`3`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the link.
* **Customer Details** (`4`, `select_one`, required): Select the customer details relevant to the submission.
* **Customer Email** (`5`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`6`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the customer's phone number (if available).
* **Submission Date** (`7`, `date`, required/optional): Choose a submission date.
* **Submission Time** (`8`, `time`, required/optional): Choose a submission time.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter the correct URL and customer details to ensure accurate submission.
* Double-check the form before submission to prevent errors.
* If you have any doubts or questions, contact our support team for assistance.
