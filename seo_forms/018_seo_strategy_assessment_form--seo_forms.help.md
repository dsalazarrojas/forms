# seo_strategy_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect data on the SEO strategy assessment of a website, including keyword research, technical audits, content audits, and other factors that impact SEO.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and select the options that best describe your website's situation.
2. Answer each question thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Submit the form when you have completed all the fields.
