# e-commerce_listing_optimization_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The e-commerce_listing_optimization_quiz form is designed to assess a seller's knowledge and skills in optimizing their e-commerce listings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each of the 25 questions to the best of your ability. You can select one or multiple answers for each question, depending on the question type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Seller Knowledge
* **seller_knowledge** (`seller_knowledge`, text, optional): This field asks for the seller's knowledge about e-commerce listing optimization.

### Seller Training Needs
* **training_needs** (`training_needs`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the training needs that apply to you as a seller.
	+ Yes

### Product Listing Performance
* **product_listing_performance** (`product_listing_performance`, number, optional): Rate the performance of your product listings.

### Product Title
* **product_title** (`product_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of your product.

### Product Image
* **product_image** (`product_image`, text, optional): Enter the URL of your product image.

### Product Description
* **product_description** (`product_description`, text, optional): Enter a description of your product.

### Product Price
* **product_price** (`product_price`, number, optional): Enter the price of your product.

### Product Category
* **product_category** (`product_category`, text, optional): Enter the category of your product.

### Product Tags
* **product_tags** (`product_tags`, text, optional): Enter the tags associated with your product.

### Product Brand
* **product_brand** (`product_brand`, text, optional): Enter the brand of your product.

### Product Rating
* **product_rating** (`product_rating`, text, optional): Enter the rating of your product.

### Product URL
* **product_url** (`product_url`, text, optional): Enter the URL of your product.

### Product Keywords
* **product_keywords** (`product_keywords`, text, optional): Enter the keywords associated with your product.

### Product Images
* **product_images** (`product_images`, text, optional): Enter the URLs of your product images.

### Product Video
* **product_video** (`product_video`, text, optional): Enter the URL of your product video.

### Product Reviews

### Product Rating System
* **product_rating_system** (`product_rating_system`, text, optional): Enter the rating system used for your product.

### Product Return Policy

	+ Yes

### Product Payment Method

	+ Yes

### Product Shipping Method

	+ Yes

### Product Discount

### Seller Information
* **seller_information** (`seller_information`, text, optional): Enter your seller information.

### Seller Experience

### Seller Rating

### Seller Reviews
