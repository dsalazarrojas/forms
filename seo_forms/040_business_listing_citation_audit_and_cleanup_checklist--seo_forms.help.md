# Business Listing Citation Audit And Cleanup Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help businesses identify and address inaccuracies and inconsistencies in their online listings across various platforms. It will guide you through a checklist of key areas to review and correct.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the business name as it is used in the listings.
2. List the platforms you have reviewed during the audit.
3. If you have found any duplicate listings, enter the count.
4. Describe any inconsistencies in information across listings.
5. Suggest any corrections or changes needed to fix the issues.
6. Identify the top priority items that need to be addressed first.
7. Provide any follow-up actions that need to be taken to complete the cleanup.
8. Enter the name of the person who completed the audit.
9. Finally, enter the date the audit was completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business name** (Business name, text, required): Enter the name of your business as it is used in the listings.
* **Listing platforms reviewed** (Listing platforms reviewed, text, optional): List the platforms you have checked for this audit (e.g., Google My Business, Yelp, Facebook).
* **Duplicate listings found** (Duplicate listings found, number, optional): If you have found any duplicate listings, enter the count.
* **Inconsistent information found** (Inconsistent information found, text, optional): Describe any inconsistencies you have found across listings.
* **Suggested corrections** (Suggested corrections, text, optional): Provide recommended changes to fix the issues.
* **Priority items** (Priority items, select multiple, optional): Choose the items that need the most attention first:
	+ Name
	+ Address
	+ Phone
	+ Website
* **Follow up actions** (Follow up actions, text, optional): List any additional tasks to complete the cleanup process.
* **Auditor name** (Auditor name, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who completed the audit.
* **Audit date** (Audit date, date, optional): Enter the date the audit was completed.
