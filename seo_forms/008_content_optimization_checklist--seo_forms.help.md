# Content Optimization Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Content Optimization Checklist is a tool used to evaluate the quality of a content page and suggest areas for improvement in terms of SEO and overall user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the URL of the page being optimized into the "Content URL" field.
2. Add the primary keyword related to the page in the "Primary Focus Keyword" field.
3. Add any secondary keywords related to the page in the "Secondary Keywords" field (optional).
4. Check off each item in the "On-Page SEO Elements" section as they apply to the page being optimized.
5. Answer questions about meta tags (meta title, meta description, and character count), image optimization, and internal linking.
6. Evaluate the page's readability with the Flesch-Kincaid Readability Score.
7. Confirm the inclusion of a clear Call-to-Action (CTA) and ensure the content displays correctly on mobile devices.
8. Set the completion status of the optimization process (Draft, Ready for Review, or Fully Optimized).
9. Optionally, date the review and update of the optimization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Content URL**: This is the URL of the page being optimized. Please enter the link to the page in question.
* **Primary Focus Keyword**: This is the main keyword that the page should rank for in search engines.
* **Secondary Keywords**: These are related terms to include in the content for better keyword diversity.
* **Meta Title contains primary keyword**: Answer "True" if the meta title includes the primary keyword and has a character count of 50-60, otherwise answer "False".
* **Meta Description is compelling and has CTA**: Answer "True" if the meta description is descriptive and encourages users to take action, otherwise answer "False" and ensure it has a character count of 150-160.
* **H1 and H2 tags correctly structured**: Confirm that the page's structure uses H1 and H2 tags correctly for a clear and organized reading flow.
* **Number of Internal Links Added**: Count and report the number of internal links on the page to ensure easy navigation.
* **All images have descriptive Alt Text**: Confirm that all images on the page have descriptive alt text for improved accessibility and SEO.
* **Flesch-Kincaid Readability Score**: Evaluate the page's readability score to ensure it meets the goal of 60 or higher.
* **Clear Call-to-Action (CTA) included**: Confirm the inclusion of a clear CTA on the page to guide users.
* **Content displays correctly on mobile**: Ensure the content is easily accessible and viewable on mobile devices.
* **Overall Optimization Status**: Select the completion status of the page's optimization process (Draft, Ready for Review, or Fully Optimized).
* **Review Date**: Optionally, date the review and update of the optimization process.
