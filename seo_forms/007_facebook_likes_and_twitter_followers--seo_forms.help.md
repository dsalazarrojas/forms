# Facebook Likes And Twitter Followers - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and report Facebook likes and Twitter followers for a product. It gathers information on the number of likes and followers for Facebook and Twitter, as well as the date and time of actions taken on these social media platforms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the number of Facebook likes for the product.
2. Enter the number of Twitter followers for the product.
3. Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the product has liked this on Facebook.
4. Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the product has liked this on Twitter.
5. Enter the date of the action.
6. Enter the time of the action.
7. Enter any additional notes for Facebook.
8. Enter the email of the person who reported this action.
9. Enter the phone number of the person who reported this action.
10. Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the product has liked this on Facebook (second instance).
11. Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the product has liked this on Twitter (second instance).
12. Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the product has liked this on Facebook.
13. Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the product has liked this on Twitter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Number of Facebook likes**: Enter the number of likes the product has received on Facebook.
- **Number of Twitter followers**: Enter the number of followers the product has on Twitter.
- **Has Facebook liked this?**: Answer "Yes" if the product has liked the user's Facebook post, "No" otherwise.
- **Has Twitter liked this?**: Answer "Yes" if the product has liked the user's Twitter post, "No" otherwise.
- **Date of action**: Enter the date when the action was taken.
- **Time of action**: Enter the time when the action was taken.
- **Note for Facebook**: Enter any additional notes for Facebook.
- **Email**: Enter the email of the person who reported this action.
- **Phone number**: Enter the phone number of the person who reported this action.
- **Select Facebook**: Answer "Yes" if the product has liked this on Facebook, "No" otherwise.
- **Select Twitter**: Answer "Yes" if the product has liked this on Twitter, "No" otherwise.
- **Select Facebook 2**: Answer "Yes" if the product has liked this on Facebook, "No" otherwise.
- **Select Twitter Followers 3**: Answer "Yes" if the product has liked this on Twitter, "No" otherwise.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate information for each field.
- Double-check the information before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or team lead for guidance.
