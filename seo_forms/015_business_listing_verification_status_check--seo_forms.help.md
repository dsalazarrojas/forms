# Business Listing Verification Status Check - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help business owners verify their online presence across various directories. It's a crucial step in maintaining a strong online reputation and ensuring accurate representation of your business. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form with your business details, such as business name, address, phone number, and website.
2. Specify your contact information, including name, email, and role in the business.
3. Select the directories where you'd like to check your business listing status.
4. Provide details about your current verification status, any issues experienced, and the priority level for this verification check.
5. Select your primary business category and specify whether you need multi-location management.
6. Indicate if you've attempted verification before and have any documents available for verification.
7. Choose any additional services you might need to enhance your online presence.
8. Add any additional notes you'd like to share about your business.
9. Review and submit your form to initiate the verification process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter the exact name of your business as registered.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, required): Type in your full street address.
* **City** (`business_city`, text, required): Select your city location.
* **State or Province** (`business_state`, text, required): Choose the state or province where your business is located.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (`business_zip`, text, required): Enter your business's ZIP or postal code.
* **Business Phone Number** (`business_phone`, text, required): Provide your primary phone number.
* **Business Website** (`business_website`, text, optional): Type in your full website URL, if applicable.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person requesting verification.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Type in the email address for verification results.
* **Contact Role** (`contact_role`, select_one, required): Select your role in the business (Business owner, Marketing manager, SEO specialist, External agency, Authorized representative, or Other).
* **Directories to Verify** (`directories_to_check`, select_multiple, required): Choose all directories where you'd like to check your business listing status.
* **Current Verification Knowledge** (`verification_status_known`, select_one, required): Select whether you know your current verification status (Verified on all platforms, Verified on some platforms, Not verified anywhere, or Not sure).
* **Issues Experienced** (`issues_experienced`, select_multiple, required): Select any issues you've encountered with your listings.
* **Priority Level** (`priority_level`, select_one, required): Choose the urgency of this verification check (Critical - listing down, High - major issues, Medium - routine check, or Low - informational).
* **Business Category** (`business_category`, select_one, required): Select your primary business category (Retail, Restaurant, Professional services, Healthcare, Home services, Automotive, Entertainment, or Other).
* **Number of Business Locations** (`number_of_locations`, number, required): Enter the total number of business locations needing verification.
* **Multi-Location Management** (`multi_location_management`, select_one, required): Choose whether you need multi-location management (Yes - need bulk management, Yes - need guidance, or No - single location only).
* **Previous Verification Attempts** (`previous_verification_attempts`, select_one, required): Indicate if you've attempted verification before (Yes - successful, Yes - failed, Yes - pending, or No - first attempt).
* **Documentation Available** (`documentation_available`, select_multiple, required): Select all documents available for verification (Business license, Tax documents, Utility bills, Bank statements, Photo ID, Business registration, or None).
* **Additional Services Needed** (`additional_services`, select_multiple, optional): Choose any additional services you require (Listing optimization, Review management, Citation building, Ongoing monitoring, or None).
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Provide any other information about your business.
