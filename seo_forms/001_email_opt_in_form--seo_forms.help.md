# eMail Opt In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect email addresses from users who wish to opt-in to a service or newsletter.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the `first_name` field.
2. Enter your last name in the `last_name` field.
3. Enter your email address in the `email` field.
4. Enter your confirmation email address in the `confirm_email` field to prevent spamming.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **first_name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your personal documents.
### **last_name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your personal documents.
### **email** (`email`, text, required): Enter a valid email address that you use to receive emails.
### **confirm_email** (`confirm_email`, text, required): Enter the same email address you entered in the `email` field to confirm it is correct and prevent spamming.
