# Backlink Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request backlinks from a website to another website. It helps us understand the context and purpose of your request, ensuring that it is a genuine and valid request.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill in your information as a requester, including your full name, email address, and phone number (if available).
- Provide the name and URL of the website you are requesting backlinks from (requester's website).
- Specify the URL and name of the target website you are requesting backlinks for.
- Explain why this backlink is relevant (relevance_explanation).
- Select the type of link you are requesting (link_type).
- Describe the connection between your website and the target website (unique_value).
- Provide any additional information or links that may be helpful for the review process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (requester_name, text, required): Your real name as the person submitting the request.
* **Email Address** (requester_email, email, required): Your email address for communication and verification purposes.
* **Phone Number** (requester_phone, phone, optional): Your phone number for additional contact.
* **Company/Website Name** (company_name, text, required): The name of your company or website.
* **Company Website URL** (company_website, text, required): The URL of your company or website.
* **Target Website URL** (target_website, text, required): The URL of the target website you are requesting backlinks from.
* **Target Website Name** (target_website_name, text, optional): The name of the target website (optional but recommended).
* **Contact Person Name** (target_contact_name, text, optional): The name of the contact person on the target website (if known).
* **Contact Person Email** (target_contact_email, email, optional): The email address of the contact person on the target website (if known).
* **Requested Anchor Text** (anchor_text, text, required): The text to be displayed as the backlink on the target website.
* **Landing Page URL** (landing_page, text, required): The URL of the page on the target website where the backlink will point to.
* **Suggested Page** (suggested_page, text, optional): A suggested page on the target website where the backlink could fit (optional).
* **Type of Link Requested** (link_type, select_one, required): Select the type of link you are requesting (e.g., Content Link, Resource Page Link, etc.).
* **Why This Backlink is Relevant** (relevance_explanation, text, required): Explain the connection between your website and the target website.
* **What We Offer in Return** (what_we_offer, text, optional): Describe what you offer in return for the backlink (mutual benefit, content exchange, etc.).
* **Unique Value of Our Website** (unique_value, text, required): Explain why your audience would benefit from the backlink.
* **Our Domain Authority** (our_domain_authority, integer, optional): If known, provide your website's domain authority (e.g., Moz's Ahrefs).
* **Target Website Domain Authority** (target_da, integer, optional): If known, provide the target website's domain authority (e.g., Moz's Ahrefs).
* **Additional Information or Links** (additional_info, text, optional): Provide any additional information or links that may be helpful for the review process.
* **I Confirm This Information is Accurate** (submission_confirmation, select_one, required): Confirm that the information you provided is accurate.
