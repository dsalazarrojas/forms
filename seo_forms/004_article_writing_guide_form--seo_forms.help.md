# Article Writing Guide Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to guide writers in creating well-researched, engaging, and informative articles that meet the content team's requirements. Please fill out this form carefully to provide all necessary information for the article.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter the Article Title**: Provide a clear and concise title for the article.
2. **Identify the Target Audience**: Specify who the article is intended for.
3. **Choose the Content Type**: Select the type of article you are writing (Blog Post, News Article, Guide, Tutorial, Case Study, Opinion Piece, or Research Paper).
4. **Enter the Primary Keyword or Topic**: Input the main focus of the article.
5. **Add Related Keywords (Optional)**: Provide a list of relevant keywords related to the article.
6. **Specify the Target Word Count (Optional)**: If relevant, indicate the word count target for the article.
7. **Enter the Author Name and Role**: Input the author's name and role (if applicable).
8. **Provide Content Direction and Goals**: Describe the article's purpose and what it should accomplish.
9. **Choose the Tone and Style**: Select the tone and style of the article (Formal, Conversational, Technical, Inspirational, Educational, or Humorous).
10. **Specify Formatting Requirements**: Select the article's formatting requirements (Headings and Subheadings, Bullet Points, Code Blocks, Tables, Images, Links, or Pull Quotes).
11. **Set the Publication Deadline (Optional)**: If applicable, provide the publication deadline for the article.
12. **List Internal Links (Optional)**: If relevant, provide internal links to include in the article.
13. **Add External Resources or References (Optional)**: If applicable, list external resources or references for the article.
14. **Provide Special Revision Notes (Optional)**: If necessary, add any special notes for revisions.
15. **Enter the Contact Email for Feedback (Required)**: Input the email address where feedback can be sent.
