# seo_client_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the SEO Client Questionnaire form, which is designed to gather information from clients about their website and SEO needs.

## How To Complete This Form

### Introduction

To get started, navigate to the "SEO Client Questionnaire" form. From there, you will see a brief introduction to guide you through the process.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

#### 1. **Welcome to SEO Client Questionnaire** (`title`: note, `id`: 1)
* A brief introduction to the form and its purpose.

#### 2. **Enter Your Contact Info** (`title`: text, `id`: 2)
* Enter your name and contact information in this field.

#### 3. **Enter Your Company Name** (`title`: text, `id`: 3)
* Enter your company name here.

#### 4. **Enter Your Company URL** (`title`: text, `id`: 4)
* Enter your company URL here.

#### 5. **Which Services Do You Need?** (`title`: select multiple, `id`: 5)
* Select all the services you are interested in using from the list.

#### 6. **What Services Do You Need?** (`title`: select multiple, `id`: 6)
* This field is a duplicate of the previous one. Please select all the services you are interested in using from the list.

#### 7. **Enter Your Email Address** (`title`: email, `id`: 7)
* Enter your email address here. Please use the format `example@example.com`.

#### 8. **Enter Your Phone Number** (`title`: text, `id`: 8)
* Enter your phone number here.

#### 9. **What Is Your Budget** (`title`: number, `id`: 9)
* Enter your budget here.

#### 10. **Expected Launch Date** (`title`: date, `id`: 10)
* Select the expected launch date for your project.

#### 11. **Describe Your Website** (`title`: text, `id`: 11)
* Enter a brief description of your website here.

#### 12. **Who Is Your Target Audience** (`title`: text, `id`: 12)
* Enter who your target audience is here.

#### 13. **List Your Website Features** (`title`: text, `id`: 13)
* Enter a list of features of your website here.

#### 14. **What Technology Stack Do You Use?** (`title`: text, `id`: 14)
* Enter the technology stack you use here.

#### 15. **What Website Platform Do You Use?** (`title`: select one, `id`: 15)
* Select the platform you use for your website from the list.

#### 16. **What Is Your Website Traffic?** (`title`: select one, `id`: 16)
* Select your website traffic from the list.

#### 17. **What Language Is Your Website In?** (`title`: select one, `id`: 17)
* Select the language of your website from the list.

#### 18. **What Size Is Your Website?** (`title`: select one, `id`: 18)
* Select the size of your website from the list.

#### 19. **How Old Is Your Website?** (`title`: select one, `id`: 19)
* Select the age of your website from the list.

#### 20. **What Social Media Do You Use?** (`title`: select multiple, `id`: 20)
* Select all the social media platforms you use here.

#### 21. **How Do You Handle Website Security?** (`title`: select one, `id`: 21)
* Select how you handle website security from the list.

#### 22. **What Is Your Backup Strategy?** (`title`: select multiple, `id`: 22)
* Select your backup strategy from the list.

#### 23. **Do You Use A Database?** (`title`: select one, `id`: 23)
* Select if you use a database for your website.

#### 24. **What Type Of Website Content?** (`title`: select multiple, `id`: 24)
* Select the type of content on your website from the list.

#### 25. **Additional Information** (`title`: text, `id`: 25)
* Enter any additional information here.

## Tips

* Please make sure to answer all the fields accurately to ensure we can better serve you.
* If you have any questions or need help, feel free to contact us.
* The information collected here will only be used to better serve you and provide you with personalized services.
