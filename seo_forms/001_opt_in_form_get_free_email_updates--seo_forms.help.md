# opt_in_form_get_free_email_updates - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow users to opt-in to receive free email updates from specific companies and choose the frequency and type of updates they are interested in receiving.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select whether you want to subscribe to receive email updates by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the Subscribe field.
2.  Enter your email address in the Email field. Please note that your email address will only be used for email updates and will not be shared with any third party.
3.  Choose the type of updates you are interested in by selecting one of the options in the Mailing List field (e.g., newsletter, mailing list).
4.  Select the specific company you are interested in by choosing one of the options in the Company field (e.g., Google, Amazon).
5.  If you have an organization, select the option from the Organization field.
6.  Choose the frequency of updates you prefer in the Newsletter field.
7.  Finally, select the type of mailing lists you are interested in by choosing one of the options in the Mailing Lists field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Subscribe** (optional): Select "Yes" if you want to receive email updates.
*   **Email** (optional, email): Enter your email address to receive updates. Please note that your email address will only be used for email updates and will not be shared with any third party.
*   **Mailing List** (optional, select_one): Choose the type of updates you are interested in (e.g., newsletter, mailing list).
*   **Company** (optional, select_one): Select the specific company you are interested in (e.g., Google, Amazon).
*   **Organization** (optional, select_one): If you have an organization, select your option.
*   **Newsletter** (optional, select_one): Choose the frequency of updates you prefer (e.g., weekly, daily, monthly).
*   **Mailing Lists** (optional, select_one): Choose the type of mailing lists you are interested in (e.g., weekly, daily, monthly).

## Tips
*   Please make sure to check the box in the Subscribe field to confirm your opt-in.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for assistance.
*   Your email address will only be used for email updates and will not be shared with any third party.
*   You can always opt-out from receiving email updates at any time by contacting us.
