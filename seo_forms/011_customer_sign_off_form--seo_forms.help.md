# Customer Sign Off Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Customer Sign Off Form is used to confirm that a customer has received and understood the terms of a service provided by a service provider. This form is essential for ensuring that both the customer and service provider have agreed on the terms of the service and have signed off on it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Customer Information**: Enter the customer's details in this field. This field is used to gather information about the customer.
2. **Service Provider**: Enter the name of the service provider who is providing the service to the customer.
3. **Date Signed**: Select the date on which the service was signed off by the customer. This date should match the date of the service agreement.
4. **Customer Signed**: Select 'True' if the customer has signed off the service agreement. If the customer has not signed, select 'False'. This field confirms that the customer has acknowledged and agreed to the service terms.
5. **Service Provider Signed**: Select 'True' if the service provider has signed off the service agreement. If the service provider has not signed, select 'False'. This field confirms that the service provider has acknowledged and agreed to the service terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information (Customer Information)**: A field to enter customer details.
* **Service Provider (Service Provider)**: A field to enter the name of the service provider.
* **Date Signed (Date Signed)**: A field to select the date on which the service was signed off.
* **Customer Signed (Customer Signed)**: A field to confirm if the customer has signed off the service agreement.
* **Service Provider Signed (Service Provider Signed)**: A field to confirm if the service provider has signed off the service agreement.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
* Ensure that both the customer and service provider have signed off the service agreement before proceeding.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or support team for assistance.
