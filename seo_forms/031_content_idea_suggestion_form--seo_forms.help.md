# Content Idea Suggestion Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect suggestions for content ideas from users. The form will help the content team evaluate and decide which ideas to pursue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide your name (optional) and email address (optional) so that we can contact you for follow-up questions.
2. Choose the topic category that best fits your suggestion.
3. Provide a brief title for your suggested topic.
4. Describe your topic idea in detail.
5. Explain why this topic should be written about.
6. Specify the target audience for this content.
7. Choose the type of content that you think would be suitable for this topic.
8. Provide any additional resources or references that can be included.
9. Check if you'd like to contribute to writing this content.
10. Indicate your level of expertise on this topic.
11. Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
12. Finally, indicate how timely this topic is.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`suggester_name`, text, optional): This is your full name that we'll use for follow-up questions.
* **Your Email** (`suggester_email`, email, optional): This is your email address that we can use to contact you for follow-up questions.
* **Suggestion Date** (`suggestion_date`, date, required): This is the date of your suggestion.
* **Topic Title** (`topic_title`, text, required): A brief title for your suggested topic.
* **Topic Category** (`topic_category`, select_one, required): Choose the topic category that best fits your suggestion.
* **Topic Description** (`topic_description`, text, required): Describe your topic idea in detail.
* **Why This Topic?** (`why_this_topic`, text, required): Explain why we should write about this topic.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, text, required): Specify the target audience for this content.
* **Suggested Content Type** (`content_type_suggestion`, select_one, required): Choose the type of content that you think would be suitable for this topic.
* **Related Keywords** (`related_keywords`, text, optional): Provide any relevant keywords that can be used for this topic.
* **Search Intent** (`search_intent`, select_one, optional): Specify the search intent for this content.
* **Competitor Content** (`competitor_content`, text, optional): Provide any information about similar content that already exists.
* **Questions to Answer** (`questions_to_answer`, text, optional): Identify any questions that this content should address.
* **Urgency** (`urgency`, select_one, required): Indicate how timely this topic is.
* **Additional Resources** (`additional_resources`, text, optional): Provide any links or references that can be included in this content.
* **Would You Like to Contribute?** (`would_like_contribution`, select_one, optional): Check if you'd like to contribute to writing this content.
* **Expertise Level** (`expertise_level`, select_one, optional): Indicate your level of expertise on this topic.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any other thoughts or suggestions.
