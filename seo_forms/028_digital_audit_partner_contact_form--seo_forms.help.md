# Digital Audit Partner Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Digital Audit Partner Contact Form is a tool used to collect contact information from digital audits partners. This information is used to establish a channel of communication and collaboration between our teams. It will also help us in scheduling future audits and ensuring that we have the necessary details on hand.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Fill out the required fields as prompted, including your First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone Number, and Business Name.
* Make sure to enter your information accurately and completely.
* Review your form for any errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: The name you wish to be known by within our team.
* **Last Name**: The name you were born with, or the name you go by professionally.
* **Email**: The email address we'll use to contact you for collaboration and communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: The number we'll use to contact you for scheduling future audits and other partnership matters.
* **Business Name**: The name of your company or business that we are partnering with.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you've already filled out this form in the past, please do not fill it out again.
