# seo_service_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your SEO service needs and preferences. Please fill it out accurately to ensure a smooth service booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Go to the first page (service_details) and fill in the required fields.
2. Once you have completed the first page, click "Next" or proceed to the next page (client_details) and fill in the required fields.
3. Finally, review and submit your responses on the last page (seo_preferences).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Details**: This is the first page where you will provide the details of your SEO service needs. Please fill in the form as accurately as possible.

  * **Service Type**: The type of SEO service you require (e.g., Website Optimization, Link Building, etc.).
  * **Service Description**: A brief description of your SEO service needs.
  * **Additional Information**: Any additional details or notes you would like to provide about your SEO service needs.

* **Client Details**: This page is used to collect client information. Please fill in the required fields accurately.

  * **Client Name**: The name of the client.
  * **Client Contact Information**: The contact information of the client (e.g., email, phone number).
  * **Project Details**: A brief description of the project.

* **SEO Preferences**: This is the last page where you will provide your SEO preferences.

  * **Language Preference**: The language you prefer to communicate in the service process (e.g., English, Spanish, etc.).
  * **Service Frequency**: How often would you like to receive updates about your SEO services (e.g., daily, weekly, etc.).
  * **Additional Comments**: Any additional comments or preferences you would like to share.

## Tips
* Please fill in the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth service booking process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
