# Subscribe To My List - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Subscribe To My List form is used to collect your personal information for our mailing list or newsletter. You can use this form to provide us with your contact information and description about yourself, which will help us better understand your interest and tailor our communication to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
- **Fill out the form completely**. This includes filling out all fields that are required and providing accurate and up-to-date information.
- **Use a valid email address**. This is the address where we will send our updates or promotions.
- **Use a valid phone number**. This is used to confirm your phone number.
- **Describe yourself**. This field is optional but will help us better understand your interests and tailor our communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name**: This is a field to provide your first name. If you want to be listed on our mailing list, please fill this out.
* **email**: This is the email address where we will send our updates or promotions. Please make sure it is a valid email address that you control.
* **company**: If you are associated with a company, please fill this out to let us know about your affiliation.
* **website**: If you have a website, please fill this out so we can learn more about your business or work.
* **phone**: This is an optional field to provide your phone number for confirmation purposes.
* **description**: This field is optional and is used to provide more information about yourself and why you want to be on our mailing list.
* **confirm_phone**: This field is used to confirm your phone number. Please make sure it matches the phone number you provided in the previous field.
* **confirm_email**: This field is used to confirm your email address. Please make sure it matches the email address you provided in the previous field.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
- If you have any issues or questions, please contact us using the contact information provided.
