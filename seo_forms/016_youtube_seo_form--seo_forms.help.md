# YouTube SEO Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data for optimizing YouTube video titles, descriptions, and categories to improve search engine rankings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2.  Enter the video title in the "Video Title" field.
3.  Select the video category from the provided options or enter a custom category.
4.  Add a brief description of the video in the "Video Description" field.
5.  Enter the video URL in the "Video URL" field.
6.  Provide a comma-separated list of image URLs in the "Image URLs (comma separated)" field.
7.  Select the music license type from the provided options.
8.  Select the social media platforms where you want to promote the video.
9.  Enter any additional blog links related to the video.
10. Optionally, enter your Airtable ID or Google Sheets ID for tracking purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Video Title (Video Title, text, required)**: Enter a concise and descriptive title for your video that captures its essence.
* **Video Category (Video Category, text, optional)**: Choose a relevant category for your video or enter a custom category.
* **Video Description (Video Description, text, required)**: Write a brief description of your video (less than 2000 characters) that includes key terms or phrases.
* **Video URL (Video URL, text, required)**: Enter the direct URL of your YouTube video.
* **Image URLs (comma separated) (Image URLs (comma separated), text, optional)**: Provide a list of image URLs related to your video.
* **Music Licenses (Music Licenses, select_multiple, optional)**: Select the type of music license used for your video (Public Domain, Royalty Free, Paid License, Custom).
* **Social Media (Social Media, select_multiple, optional)**: Choose the social media platforms you want to promote your video on (Twitter, Facebook, Instagram, Pinterest).
* **Blog Links (Blog Links, text, optional)**: Enter any additional blog links related to your video.
* **Airtable ID (Airtable ID, text, optional)**: Enter your Airtable ID for tracking purposes (if applicable).
* **Google Sheets ID (Google Sheets ID, text, optional)**: Enter your Google Sheets ID for tracking purposes (if applicable).
* **Zaps (Zaps, text, optional)**: Enter any additional Zaps related to your video.
