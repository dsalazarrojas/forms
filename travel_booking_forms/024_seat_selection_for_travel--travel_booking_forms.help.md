# Seat Selection For Travel - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for selecting seats for travelers on a trip. It gathers information about the traveler, trip details, and preferences. 

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Enter Traveler Information
Provide your name and contact details in the `traveler_info` field. This is required information that will help us to communicate with you efficiently.

### Step 2: Add Traveler Details
Enter any additional details about the traveler in the `traveler_details` field.

### Step 3: Enter Trip Information
Provide information about the trip in the `trip_details` field.

### Step 4: Select Seats
Choose your preferred seat(s) from the `select_a_seat` field.

### Step 5: Enter Travel Date and Time
Enter the travel date and time in the `travel_date` and `travel_time` fields respectively.

### Step 6: Choose Trip Duration
Enter the duration of the trip in the `trip_duration` field.

### Step 7: Add Travel Notes
Add any additional notes about the trip in the `notes` field.

### Step 8: Choose Travel Agency
Select the travel agency from the `travel_agency` field.

### Step 9: Enter Contact Information
Enter your email and phone number in the `email` and `phone` fields respectively.

### Step 10: Confirm Email
Confirm your email address in the `confirm_email` field.

### Step 11: Add Additional Notes
Add any additional notes about the trip in the `notes2` field.

### Step 12: Enter Trip ID
Enter the trip ID in the `trip_id` field.

### Step 13: Enter Travel Agency ID
Enter the travel agency ID in the `travel_agency_id` field.

### Step 14: Enter Travel Agency ID 2
Enter the travel agency ID 2 in the `travel_agency_id2` field.

### Step 15: Enter Traveler ID
Enter the traveler ID in the `traveler_id` field.

### Step 16: Enter Seat ID
Enter the seat ID in the `seat_id` field.

### Step 17: Enter User ID
Enter the user ID in the `user_id` field.

### Step 18: Enter Email 2
Enter your email address in the `email_2` field.

### Step 19: Enter Phone 2
Enter your phone number in the `phone_2` field.

### Step 20: Confirm Email 3
Confirm your email address in the `confirm_email_3` field.

### Step 21: Enter Additional Notes 3
Add any additional notes about the trip in the `notes_3` field.

### Step 22: Traveler Notes 3
Add any additional notes about the traveler in the `traveler_notes3` field.

### Step 23: Choose Travel Agency 3
Select the travel agency from the `travel_agency3` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **traveler_info** (`traveler_info`, text, optional): Enter your name and contact information to help us contact you efficiently.
* **traveler_details** (`traveler_details`, text, optional): Add any additional details about the traveler.
* **trip_details** (`trip_details`, text, optional): Enter any information about the trip.
* **select_a_seat** (`select_a_seat`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your preferred seat(s) for the trip.
* **travel_date** (`travel_date`, date, optional): Enter the travel date.
* **travel_time** (`travel_time`, time, optional): Enter the travel time.
* **trip_duration** (`trip_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the trip.
* **notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the trip.
* **travel_agency** (`travel_agency`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the travel agency.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **confirm_email** (`confirm_email`, select_one, optional): Confirm your email address.
* **notes2** (`notes2`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the trip.
* **trip_id** (`trip_id`, number, optional): Enter the trip ID.
* **travel_agency_id** (`travel_agency_id`, number, optional): Enter the travel agency ID.
* **travel_agency_id2** (`travel_agency_id2`, number, optional): Enter the travel agency ID 2.
* **traveler_id** (`traveler_id`, number, optional): Enter the traveler ID.
* **seat_id** (`seat_id`, number, optional): Enter the seat ID.
* **user_id** (`user_id`, number, optional): Enter the user ID.
* **email_2** (`email_2`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **phone_2** (`phone_2`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **confirm_email_3** (`confirm_email_3`, select_one, optional): Confirm your email address.
* **notes_3** (`notes_3`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the trip.
* **traveler_notes3** (`traveler_notes3`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the traveler.
* **travel_agency3** (`travel_agency3`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the travel agency.

## Tips
* Please fill out all relevant fields carefully as this information will be used to complete your seat selection.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact our support team for clarification.
* You can choose more than one seat option if required.
* Please ensure that your email and phone number are accurate.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
