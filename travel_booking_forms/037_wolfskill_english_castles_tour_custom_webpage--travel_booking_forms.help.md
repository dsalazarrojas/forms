# Wolfskill English Castles Tour Custom Webpage - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to book a tour of English castles. It allows users to select a tour date and their travel dates in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "English Castles Tour" field with the desired tour name or leave it blank.
2. Select the desired tour date in the "Tour Dates" field using the MM/DD/YYYY format.
3. Choose your travel dates for the tour in the "Arrival And Departure" field using the same MM/DD/YYYY format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **English Castles Tour** (`english_castles_tour`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the tour you would like to book or leave it blank to proceed without selection.
* **Tour Dates** (`tour_dates`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date you would like to take the tour in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Arrival And Departure** (`arrival_and_departure`, `date`, required/optional): Enter your travel dates for the tour in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
