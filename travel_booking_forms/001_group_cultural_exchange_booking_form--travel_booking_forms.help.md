# Group Cultural Exchange Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants in a cultural exchange program. It gathers details about the trip, participant preferences, and other relevant information to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the trip details, such as travel dates and duration.
2. Choose how you would like to be addressed during the trip.
3. Select your preferred participant preferences from the provided options.
4. Select your preferred participant details, such as preferred time of day.
5. Review and submit your form.
