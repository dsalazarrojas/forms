# Tourist ID Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tourist ID Registration Form is designed for tourists to register their identity, travel plans, and contact details accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your personal details, including your first and last name in the corresponding text fields.
* Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
* Select your nationality and whether you have a national ID document.
* Choose your travel date and time for check-in, check-out, and flight arrival and departure.
* Provide your ID number and passport number for security and travel purposes.
* Select the type of accommodation you will be staying in and whether it is an official ID.
* Enter the duration of your stay and your flight number.
* Choose whether you will arrive or depart on the same day.
* Enter your contact number and contact email for follow-up purposes.
* Add any additional notes about your travel plans.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your ID.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your ID.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Nationality**: Select whether you have a national ID.
* **ID Number**: Enter your ID number.
* **Travel Date**: Select the date you will be traveling.
* **Checkin Time**: Select the time you will check in.
* **Checkin Date**: Select the date you will check in.
* **Checkin Location**: Enter the location where you will be checking in.
* **Accommodation Type**: Select the type of accommodation you will be staying in.
* **Stay Duration**: Enter the duration of your stay.
* **Flight Number**: Enter your flight number.
* **Flight Date**: Select the date you will be flying.
* **Flight Time**: Select the time you will arrive or depart.
* **Arrival**: Select whether you will arrive or depart on the same day.
* **Departure**: Select whether you will arrive or depart on the same day.
* **Passport Number**: Enter your passport number.
* **Passport Expiration**: Enter the expiration date of your passport.
* **Nationality Document**: Select whether you have a national ID document.
* **Accommodation ID**: Enter your accommodation ID.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your contact email.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about your travel plans.
