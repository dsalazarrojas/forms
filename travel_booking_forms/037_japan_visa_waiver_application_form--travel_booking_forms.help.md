# Japan Visa Waiver Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Japan Visa Waiver Application Form is designed to gather information from individuals seeking a Japan visa waiver for a short-term stay. This form is for applicants who qualify for a visa waiver.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the sections in order from top to bottom.
2. Provide accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Make sure to sign and date the Declaration section, as it's a required field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Traveler Details (#1: Traveler Details)
* **Traveler Details** (`traveler_details`, text, optional): This section is for you to provide any additional information about your travel plans or requirements.

### Travel Details (#2: Travel Details)
* **Travel Details** (`travel_details`, text, optional): This section is for you to provide any additional information about your travel plans or requirements.

### Declaration (#3: Declaration)
* **Declaration** (`declaration`, text, required): Please sign and date this section to confirm that the information provided is accurate and true. This is a required field.

### Organization Details (#4: Organization Details)
* **Organization Details** (`organization_details`, text, optional): This section is not required for individual travelers. If you're representing an organization, you can provide more information about your organization here.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and complete information for each field.
- If you have any supporting documents, such as a business card or letter, you can attach them to this form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
