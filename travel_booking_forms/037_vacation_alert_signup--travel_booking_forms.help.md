# vacation_alert_signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user information for a vacation alert system. It gathers details from users about their travel preferences and subscription preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Scroll to the "User Information" page and fill out the required fields.
2. Go to the "Travel Preferences" page and provide any relevant details about your travel preferences.
3. Finally, go to the "Subscription Consent" page and fill out the form if you wish to subscribe to the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (User Information, text, optional)**: This field is where you can provide any additional information about yourself. It's not required, so you can leave it blank if you don't have any details to share.

* **Travel Preferences (Travel Preferences, text, optional)**: This field is where you can specify any special travel preferences you may have. It's not required, so again, you can leave it blank if you don't have any preferences to mention.

* **Subscription Consent (Subscription Consent, text, optional)**: This field is where you can indicate your consent to subscribe to the service. If you wish to receive notifications and updates about travel alerts, fill out this field with a clear "yes" or "no". If you do not wish to subscribe, you can leave it blank.

- Please provide accurate and honest information to help us better serve you.
