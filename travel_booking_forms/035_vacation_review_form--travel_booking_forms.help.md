# Vacation Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vacation Review Form is designed to help travel agents gather feedback from clients about their recent travel experiences. This form will assist agents in understanding their clients' needs and preferences, enabling them to provide better services and improve client satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name as the travel agent in the "Travel Agent" field.
2.  Type your client's name in the "Client Name" field.
3.  Select the date of your review from the calendar in the "Review Date" field.
4.  Describe the travel destination you visited in the "Travel Destination" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Travel Agent** (`travel_agent`, text, optional): Enter your name as the travel agent who helped the client with their trip.
*   **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Type the client's name as mentioned in your review.
*   **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, optional): Select the date you are submitting your review.
*   **Travel Destination** (`travel_destination`, text, optional): Describe the travel destination visited by the client, including the location and any notable experiences.
