# Service Fee Agreement Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Fee Agreement Template is a crucial document for travel agencies to outline the terms and conditions of their services.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter the **Client Name** (1) in the first field. This is the name of the client you are dealing with.
*   Enter the **Agency Address** (4) in the next field. This is the address of your agency where the client can contact you.
*   Enter the **Agency Contact** (5) in the following field. This is the name and contact information of the person the client can contact at your agency.
*   Enter the **Service Fee** (6) in the next field. This is the total service fee for the service provided.
*   Enter the **Service Fee Amount** (7) in the following field. This is the specific amount of the service fee.
*   Enter the **Due Date** (8) in the next field. This is the date by which the service fee is due.
*   Select the **Payment Terms** (9) in the following field. Choose the payment option that best fits the client's needs.
*   Choose the **Payment Method** (10) in the next field. Select the payment method you wish to use (e.g., bank transfer, check, etc.).
*   Choose the **Payment Frequency** (11) in the following field. Decide on how often payments will be made (e.g., monthly, weekly, etc.).
*   Select the **Cancellation Policy** (12) in the next field. Choose your agency's cancellation policy.
*   Enter the **Cancellation Fees** (13) in the following field. This is any fees associated with cancellation.
*   Enter the **Payment Terms** (14) in the next field. This is the specific payment terms for the service.
*   Enter the **Payment Terms Label** (15) in the following field. This is a label for the payment terms.
*   Enter the **Payment Terms Text** (16) in the next field. This is the text describing the payment terms.
*   Enter the **Payment Terms Frequency** (17) in the following field. This is the frequency of the payment terms.
*   Enter the **Payment Terms Amount** (18) in the following field. This is the specific payment term amount.
*   Enter the **Client Signature** (19) in the next field. This is the client's signature (can be optional).
*   Enter the **Agent Signature** (20) in the following field. This is the agent's signature (can be optional).
*   Enter the **Date** (21) in the following field. This is the date the agreement is signed.
*   Choose the **Service Fee Agreement** (22) in the next field. Select the type of service fee agreement (e.g., one-time, recurring, etc.).
*   Enter the **Payment Terms Notes** (23) in the following field. This is any additional notes about the payment terms.
*   Enter the **Additional Notes** (24) in the last field. This is any additional notes about the service.
