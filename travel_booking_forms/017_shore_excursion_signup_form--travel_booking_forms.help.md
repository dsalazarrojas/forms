# Shore Excursion Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The shore excursion signup form is designed to collect information from users who want to sign up for a guided tour or activity on a cruise ship. This form helps us understand who is booking the excursion and ensures that they are correctly signed up for the chosen activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Information, including your name and any other relevant details.
2. Provide your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
3. Enter the excursion information, including the name of the excursion you are booking.
4. Complete the payment information, including your payment method and amount.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Information
* **User Information** (text, optional): This section is where you can provide any additional information about yourself that is relevant to the excursion.
* **Name** (text, optional): If you would like to provide your name for the excursion, you can fill in this field.
* **Other Information** (text, optional): If there is any other information you would like to provide about yourself, you can fill in this field.

### Contact
* **Contact** (text, optional): This section is where you can provide your contact information, including your email address and phone number.

### Excursion Info
* **Excursion Info** (text, optional): This section is where you can enter the name of the excursion you are booking.

### Payment Information
* **Payment Information** (text, optional): This section is where you can enter your payment information, including your payment method and amount.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all relevant fields to ensure that your excursion booking is processed correctly.
* If you have any questions or issues with the form, please contact us at [support email].
