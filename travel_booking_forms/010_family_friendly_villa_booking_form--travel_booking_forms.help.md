# Family Friendly Villa Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Family Friendly Villa Booking Form" is designed to collect information from customers who are booking a villa that is suitable for families. This form helps the villa staff understand the guest's preferences and needs, ensuring they can provide a smooth and enjoyable stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) symbol.
2. Enter your Guest Information, including your name and other relevant details.
3. Provide your Contact Information, including your address and phone number.
4. Complete the Booking Details section, including your arrival and departure dates.
5. Enter your Family Details, including the number of adults and children.
6. Choose your Accommodation Options, such as the type of accommodation and any specific requests.
7. In the Special Requests section, you can add any additional comments or requests you may have.
8. Finally, confirm that you are submitting the form by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the Form Submission field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Guest Information
* **Guest Information**: This field is a text input where you can enter your name and other relevant details.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information**: This field is a text input where you can enter your contact information, including your address and phone number.

### Booking Details
* **Booking Details**: This field is a text input where you can enter details about your booking, including your arrival and departure dates.

### Family Details
* **Family Details**: This field is a text input where you can enter details about your family, including the number of adults and children.

### Accommodation Options
* **Accommodation Options**: This field is a text input where you can select your preferred accommodation options.

### Special Requests
* **Special Requests**: This field is a text input where you can add any additional comments or requests you may have.

### Form Submission
* **Form Submission**: This field is a radio button where you need to confirm that you are submitting the form. Select "Yes" to confirm.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields (\*) to ensure a smooth and enjoyable stay.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid mistakes.
* If you have any special requests or requests, please enter them in the Special Requests field so we can attend to them promptly.
