# Disneyland Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you request a quote for your travel plans to Disneyland. Please provide the necessary information, and our team will assist you in getting the best quote for your trip.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your travel details in the first field, including the type of trip you're interested in (Disneyland or Disney California Adventure Park, etc.).
2. Enter your guest details, including the number of people traveling and their ages.
3. Select the type of quote you're requesting (Single, Couple, or Family).
4. Choose the travel date for your trip.
5. Specify the price quote for your travel package.
6. Choose the currency of your quote (USD or CAD).
7. Add any additional notes or comments about your trip.
8. Enter your phone number for easy contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### disneyland_trip_details
* **disneyland_trip_details** (`disneyland_trip_details`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your travel plans to Disneyland. This could include the type of park you're interested in, the duration of your stay, and any specific preferences (e.g., special events, character dining, etc.).

### guest_details
* **guest_details** (`disneyland_guest_details`, text, required): Enter the number of guests traveling and their ages. Please provide the ages of all family members.

### quote_type
* **quote_type** (`disneyland_quote_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of quote you're interested in (Single, Couple, or Family).

### travel_date
* **travel_date** (`disneyland_travel_date`, date, required): Choose the travel date for your trip. Make sure to choose a date that aligns with our available travel dates.

### quote_price
* **quote_price** (`disneyland_quote_price`, number, required): Specify the price quote for your travel package.

### quote_currency
* **quote_currency** (`disneyland_quote_currency`, select_one, required): Choose the currency of your quote (USD or CAD).

### note
* **note** (`disneyland_note`, note, required): Add any additional notes or comments about your trip. This could include special requests, medical conditions, or other relevant information.

### phone
* **phone** (`disneyland_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for easy contact. We'll use this to call you with any questions or updates.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure we can process your quote request efficiently.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please mention them in the additional notes field.
* We'll do our best to get back to you within 24-48 hours with your quote.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to call us at your provided phone number.
