# Group Travel Arrangement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect general information about group travel arrangements. It's intended to gather details about the group's travel plans, preferences, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Provide details about the general information, travel details, contact information, special requirements, payment information, travel dates, and number of travelers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`general_info`, `text`, not required): Enter a brief description of the group's travel purpose or any additional comments.
* **Travel Details** (`travel_details`, `text`, not required): Provide any specific travel requirements, such as transportation or accommodation requests.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, not required): Enter the contact person's name, phone number, and email address.
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, `text`, not required): Specify any special requirements or requests for the group.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, `text`, not required): Provide payment details, such as payment method or estimated costs.
* **Travel Dates** (`travel_dates`, `text`, not required): Enter the travel dates or duration.
* **Travelers** (`travelers`, `text`, not required): Specify the number of travelers in the group.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and clear information to ensure that your travel arrangements are handled correctly.
- If you have any questions or need help, contact the form administrator for assistance.
