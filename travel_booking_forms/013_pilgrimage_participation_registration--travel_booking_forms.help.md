# Pilgrimage Participation Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for a pilgrimage event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your name and email address to ensure we can contact you about your travel plans.
2.  Enter the name of your travel group.
3.  Choose the dates you will be attending the pilgrimage.
4.  Select the time of day you will be arriving.
5.  Enter the date of your arrival and departure.
6.  Choose the type of travel arrangement (Single, Group, Family).
7.  Enter a brief description of why you are traveling.
8.  Enter any additional information or requests.
9.  Enter your contact number.
10.  Enter the ID number of your assigned user.
11.  Enter the name of your assigned tool.
12.  Choose the status of your travel booking (Not Started, In Progress, Completed).
13.  Enter any accommodation requests.
14.  Enter the accommodation arrangement.
15.  Enter any required travel documents.
16.  Enter any special requests.
17.  Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
