# travel_registration_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The travel registration form is designed to collect personal and travel-related information from users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Personal Information section with your details.
2. Provide your travel information, including your travel dates and destination.
3. Share your travel preferences, such as any special requests or requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Personal Information

* **Personal Information** (`personal_information`, text, required): Fill in your name, email address, and any other relevant personal details.
* **Personal Information** (`personal_information`, text, required): Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.

### Travel Information

* **Travel Information** (`travel_information`, text, required): Enter your travel dates, including the start and end dates of your trip.
* **Travel Information** (`travel_information`, text, required): Make sure to provide your travel destination, including the city or location you plan to visit.

### Travel Preferences

* **Travel Preferences** (`travel_preferences`, text, required): Describe your travel preferences, including any special requests or requirements, such as dietary restrictions or mobility needs.
* **Travel Preferences** (`travel_preferences`, text, required): Be as specific as possible about your preferences to ensure a comfortable and enjoyable travel experience.
