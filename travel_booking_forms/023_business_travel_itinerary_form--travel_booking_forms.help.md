# Business Travel Itinerary Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Travel Itinerary Form is a crucial tool used by the company to collect necessary information about business travels. This form helps the travel department to plan and prepare for your trip efficiently, ensuring that you and your colleagues have a successful and productive experience.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in all required fields to ensure that your travel information is accurate and up-to-date.
- Be as specific as possible when filling in your personal details and travel plans.
- If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to the travel department for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name (1)**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID.
* **Email Address (2)**: Enter your work email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number (3)**: Provide your contact phone number in case we need to reach you.
* **Department (4)**: Mention your department or division for better coordination.
* **Manager or Supervisor Name (5)**: Enter the name of your direct manager for approval and follow-up.
* **Purpose of Travel (6)**: Select the primary reason for your business travel from the options provided.
* **Departure Date (7)**: Enter the date you leave for your business trip.
* **Return Date (8)**: Enter the date you return from your business trip.
* **Departure City (9)**: State the city you are traveling from.
* **Destination City (10)**: Mention the city you are traveling to.
* **Destination Country (11)**: Specify the country you are visiting.
* **Primary Transportation (12)**: Select your preferred mode of transportation for the trip.
* **Accommodation Needed (13)**: Indicate your accommodation needs for the trip.
* **Hotel Preference or Booking (14)**: Mention your preferred hotel chain or booking if applicable.
* **Estimated Travel Budget (15)**: Enter the estimated budget for your trip in USD or local currency.
* **Others Traveling With You (16)**: If others are traveling with you, mention their names.
* **Special Requirements or Needs (17)**: Share any dietary, accessibility, or other special needs you may have.
* **Passport or Visa Required (18)**: Select whether you need a visa or passport for your trip.
* **Pre-Trip Briefing or Preparation (19)**: Indicate if you need a briefing or materials before your trip.
* **Additional Notes or Requests (20)**: Share any additional information or requests you may have.

Please take the time to complete this form carefully, and if you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact the travel department.
