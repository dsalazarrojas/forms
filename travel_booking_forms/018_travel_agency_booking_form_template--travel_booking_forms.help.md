# Travel Agency Booking Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from customers for a smooth and efficient travel agency booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Start by filling out the first page, "Booking Details," with the required information about the booking. This includes any relevant travel dates, destination, and booking type.**
2. **Move on to "Customer Information" and fill out the necessary details about the customer, such as name, contact details, and any special requirements or preferences.**
3. **Next, enter "Payment Details" to specify payment information for the booking.**
4. **In "Additional Information," you may include any extra details that are relevant to the booking, such as travel insurance, travel companion, or any additional requirements.**
5. **Finally, review all the filled-in fields and submit the form to complete the booking process.**

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Booking Details (Page 1)**
*   **Booking Number**: This is a unique identifier for the booking.
*   **Travel Dates**: Enter the start and end dates of the travel.
*   **Destination**: Enter the destination city, state, or country.
*   **Booking Type**: Choose the type of booking (e.g., flight, hotel, package).

### **Customer Information (Page 2)**
*   **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's full name.
*   **Contact Information**: Enter the customer's email address, phone number, and any other relevant contact details.
*   **Special Requirements**: If the customer has any specific requirements or needs, enter them here.

### **Payment Details (Page 3)**
*   **Payment Method**: Choose the payment method (e.g., credit card, bank transfer, cash).
*   **Payment Amount**: Enter the total payment amount.
*   **Card Information**: Enter the payment card number, expiry date, and any other relevant details (if applicable).

### **Additional Information (Page 4)**
*   **Travel Insurance**: If the customer requires travel insurance, enter the relevant details.
*   **Travel Companion**: If the customer is traveling with someone, enter their name and contact information.
*   **Other Requirements**: Enter any other relevant information not covered elsewhere.

### **Submit (Page 5)**
*   **Submit Button**: This is where you finalize the booking by clicking the submit button. Review all the filled-in fields before submitting to ensure accuracy.
