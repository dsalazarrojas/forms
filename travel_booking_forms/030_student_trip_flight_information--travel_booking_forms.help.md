# Student Trip Flight Information - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect flight information from students for a trip, typically for school or academic purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter the student's flight details in the "Student Flight Details" section.
* Provide emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contact Information" section.
* If needed, update trip information in the "Update Trip Information" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Flight Details** (`student_trip_flight_details`, text, optional): Enter the student's flight details, such as flight number, departure and arrival times, and destination.
* **Emergency Contact Information** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact person's name, relationship to the student, and contact details (phone number and address).
* **Update Trip Information** (`update_trip_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional updates or changes to the trip information, such as new flight numbers or changes in travel dates.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for the student's flight details.
* If the student has an emergency contact, provide their name, relationship, and contact details.
* Use the "Update Trip Information" section to make any necessary changes to the trip itinerary.
* This form is for student trip flight information, so please ensure that the entered information is relevant to the student's travel.
