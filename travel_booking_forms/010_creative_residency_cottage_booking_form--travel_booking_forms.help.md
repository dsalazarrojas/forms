# Creative Residency Cottage Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help artists and creatives book a stay at a creative residency cottage. The form collects information about the booking request, including dates, project details, and guest information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your booking request in the "Booking Request" field.
2. Enter your stay dates in the "Stay Dates" field. Please use the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
3. In the "Project Description" field, describe your project in a few sentences.
4. In the "Artist Residency Details" field, provide a brief description of your project, including the type of art form you will be working on and any specific requirements.
5. Enter your name, email, and phone number in the "Guest Name", "Guest Email", and "Guest Phone" fields, respectively.
6. Select the type of project you are booking for from the "Project Type" dropdown menu.
7. Enter your project start and end dates in the "Project Start Date" and "Project End Date" fields, respectively.
8. Choose the type of accommodation you require from the "Accommodation Type" dropdown menu.
9. Enter the total number of guests in the "Total Guest Count" field.
10. Enter the number of additional guests in the "Additional Guests" field.
11. If you have any additional requests, enter them in the "Guest Additional Request" field.
12. Enter the contact email of the artist residency in the "Artist Residency Contact" field.
13. Finally, click the "Submit" button to complete your booking request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Booking Request** (`booking_request`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your booking request.
* **Stay Dates** (`stay_dates`, date, optional): Enter your stay dates in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, optional): Describe your project in a few sentences.
* **Artist Residency Details** (`artist_residency_details`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your project, including the type of art form you will be working on and any specific requirements.
* **Guest Details** (`guest_details`, note, optional): You can add any additional information about your guests here.
* **Project Type** (`project_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of project you are booking for from the dropdown menu.
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, date, required): Enter your project start date.
* **Project End Date** (`project_end_date`, date, required): Enter your project end date.
* **Accommodation Type** (`accommodation_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of accommodation you require from the dropdown menu.
* **Total Guest Count** (`total_guest_count`, number, required): Enter the total number of guests.
* **Additional Guests** (`additional_guests`, number, required): Enter the number of additional guests.
* **Guest Additional Request** (`guest_additional_request`, text, optional): If you have any additional requests, enter them here.
* **Artist Residency Contact** (`artist_residency_contact`, email, required): Enter the contact email of the artist residency.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, required): Click this button to complete your booking request.
