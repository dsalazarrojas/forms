# flight_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The flight reservation form is used to collect information about flights and reservations for travel agencies or travel companies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Provide your Email address.
4. Enter your Phone number.
5. Enter your Date of Birth.
6. Choose a Travel Date.
7. Select a Travel Time.
8. Enter the date and time of your travel, if different from the travel date and time.
9. Select the Airline for your flight.
10. Enter the Flight Number.
11. Enter the Seat Number.
12. Choose whether or not you are traveling in a specific travel class (First Class or Economy Class).
13. If you have other travel class, enter your preferred class.
14. Choose whether or not payment has been made.
15. If payment has not been made, enter your preferred payment method.
16. Confirm your reservation details by checking or unchecking the Confirm Reservation field.
17. If your reservation has been cancelled, check or uncheck the Cancel Reservation field.
18. Select the status of your reservation (Active or Inactive).
19. If you have another travel agency, enter your agency's name.
20. Enter any other travel agency details.
21. Enter the status of your reservation (Confirmed or Pending).
22. If your reservation has a different status, enter your preferred status.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`5`, `date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth.
* **Travel Date** (`6`, `date`, required: false): Choose the date of your travel.
* **Travel Time** (`7`, `time`, required: false): Select the time of your travel.
* **Travel Date/Time** (`8`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date and time of your travel, if different from the travel date and time.
* **Airline** (`9`, `text`, required: false): Select the airline for your flight.
* **Flight Number** (`10`, `number`, required: false): Enter the flight number.
* **Seat Number** (`11`, `number`, required: false): Enter the seat number.
* **Travel Class** (`12`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose whether you are traveling in First Class or Economy Class.
* **First Class - Economy** (`13`, `text`, required: false): If you are traveling in other than First Class or Economy Class, enter your preferred travel class.
* **Travel Class Other** (`14`, `text`, required: false): Other travel class.
* **Payment Method** (`15`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your preferred payment method.
* **Credit Card - Bank Transfer** (`16`, `text`, required: false): If your preferred payment method is not credit card or bank transfer, enter your preferred method.
* **Payment Method Other** (`17`, `text`, required: false): Other payment method.
* **Confirm Reservation** (`18`, `note`, required: false): Confirm your reservation details.
* **Cancel Reservation** (`19`, `select_one`, required: false): If your reservation has been cancelled, select yes or no.
* **Yes - No** (`20`, `text`, required: false): If your reservation has been cancelled, select yes or no.
* **Travel Agency** (`21`, `text`, required: false): Enter your travel agency's name.
* **Travel Agency Other** (`22`, `text`, required: false): If you have other travel agency, enter their name.
* **Reservation Status** (`23`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the status of your reservation (Active or Inactive).
* **Confirmed - Pending** (`24`, `text`, required: false): If your reservation has another status, enter your preferred status.
* **Reservation Status Other** (`25`, `text`, required: false): Other reservation status.

## Tips
* Please ensure you enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
* If unsure about any of the fields, please ask your travel agency representative for assistance.
* If you need to make any changes to your reservation, please contact your travel agency directly.
