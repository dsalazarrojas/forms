# Cable Car Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cable Car Reservation Form is designed to facilitate smooth travel planning and preparation for your cable car service. This form collects essential information from customers to ensure that their reservations are processed efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting your cable car travel, please complete this form carefully with accurate and truthful information. You can access the form through the [insert link or platform here].

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Reserver name
* Label: **Reserver name**
* Type: Text
* This field is required.
* Please enter your full name as you would like to be listed on your travel documents.

### 2. Contact phone
* Label: **Contact phone**
* Type: Text
* This field is required.
* Enter your contact phone number for easy communication with our staff.

### 3. Reservation date
* Label: **Reservation date**
* Type: Date
* This field is required.
* Select your preferred travel date from the provided calendar or enter the date manually.

### 4. Time slot
* Label: **Time slot**
* Type: Time
* This field is required.
* Choose your preferred departure time from the available slots.

### 5. Number in party
* Label: **Number in party**
* Type: Number
* This field is required.
* Enter the exact number of people traveling with you.

### 6. Accessibility requirements
* Label: **Accessibility requirements**
* Type: Text
* This field is optional.
* If you require special assistance, such as wheelchair access, please specify here.

### 7. Special requests
* Label: **Special requests**
* Type: Text
* This field is optional.
* If you need any special arrangements made for your travel, please let us know here.

### 8. Payment method
* Label: **Payment method**
* Type: Select One
* This field is required.
* Choose between 'Pay on site' and 'Prepay online' to secure your booking.

## Tips
* Please double-check your entered information before submitting the form.
* If you encounter any issues during the booking process, contact our support staff for assistance.
* Ensure that your contact phone number is accurate to facilitate easy communication with our team.
