# Frequent Flyer Program Friend Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for our members to refer their friends to our Frequent Flyer Program. By filling out this form, you (the referrer) can help your friend join and get access to exclusive benefits.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Enter your Email address in the **Email** field.
4. In the **Who Do You Want To Refer** field, enter your friend's name.
5. In the **Referee Email** field, enter your friend's email address.
6. In the **Friends Name**, **Friend Phone**, and **Friends Email** fields, enter your friend's name, phone number, and email address, respectively.
7. In the **Friend Company**, **Job Title**, and **Department** fields, enter your friend's company, job title, and department (if applicable).
8. In the **Job Description** field, provide a brief description of your friend's job.
9. In the **Loyalty Program** field, select the loyalty program that applies to your friend.
10. In the **Program Type** field, select the type of program (Business or Personal) that applies to your friend.
11. In the **Referrer Name**, **Referrers Phone**, and **Referrer Email** fields, enter your name, phone number, and email address, respectively.
12. In the **Referrer Company**, **Referrer Jobtitle**, and **Referrer Department** fields, enter your company, job title, and department (if applicable).
13. In the **Referrer Jobdescription** field, provide a brief description of your job.
14. In the **Referrer Loyalty Program** field, select the loyalty program that applies to you.
15. In the **Referrer Program Type** field, select the type of program (Business or Personal) that applies to you.
16. Finally, click the **Submit** button to submit your referral.
