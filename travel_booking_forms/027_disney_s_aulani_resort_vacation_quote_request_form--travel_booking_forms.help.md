# Disney's Aulani Resort Vacation Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required information, marked with an asterisk (\*), for each field.
2. Enter your First Name.
3. Enter your Email.
4. Select a valid Arrival Date.
5. Select a valid Departure Date.
6. Enter the Number of Guests traveling with you.
7. Choose your preferred Room Type(s).
8. Enter your name of your Travel Agent, if you have one.
9. Enter any Special Requests you may have.
10. Enter your Phone Number.
11. Enter a Message, if you have any additional information to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, Optional): Enter your full first name to help us identify you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Optional): Enter your valid email address to contact you with our quote.
* **Arrival Date** (`arrival_date`, date, Optional): Select the date you plan to arrive at the resort. Please note that arrival dates should be in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Departure Date** (`departure_date`, date, Optional): Select the date you plan to depart from the resort. Please note that departure dates should be in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, number, Optional): Enter the total number of guests traveling with you, including children.
* **Room Type** (`room_type`, select_multiple, Optional): Choose the type of room that suits your needs. Options include:
	+ Deluxe
	+ Standard
	+ Suite
* **Travel Agent** (`travel_agent`, text, Optional): If you are working with a travel agent, please enter their name.
* **Special Request** (`special_request`, text, Optional): If you have any special requests or requirements, please specify them here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Message** (`message`, text, Optional): If you have any additional information or questions, please share them in this field.

## Tips

* Please ensure you enter accurate dates and numbers to help us prepare a precise quote for you.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* You can choose multiple room types by holding down the shift key while selecting the options.
