# Travel Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Registration Form is designed to gather information from users about their travel preferences, which will be used to provide a personalized travel experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Travel Registration Form, follow these steps:

* Start by indicating your travel preferences on the first page.
* On the second page, select your preferences from the options provided.
* On the third page, enter any additional preferences or information you may have.
* Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Preferences** (`travel_preferences`, `number`, required: false): This field asks you to indicate your travel preferences on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is "Not a fan" and 10 is "Love it!"
* **Additional Travel Preferences** (`travel_preferences_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose one or more of the options provided to indicate your additional travel preferences.
* **Client Preferences** (`client_preferences`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional information you may have about your travel preferences or preferences for your clients.

## Tips
* You can skip any page or question that you are not comfortable answering.
* You can choose to complete the form on multiple pages, and then return to it later.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
