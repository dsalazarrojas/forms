# Airport Transfer Booking Form Disney Transfer - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to book an airport transfer service for your travel needs. You will be required to provide information about yourself and your trip to ensure a smooth and efficient transfer process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your first name, last name, email address, and phone number.
2. Enter your hotel name, arrival date, and arrival time to ensure our drivers know when to pick you up.
3. Provide your flight number and number of passengers to help us prepare for your transfer.
4. Select your preferred vehicle type and airport to use for your transfer.
5. Choose your preferred meeting location with the driver and your payment method.
6. If applicable, provide any special requests or requirements, such as car seats or mobility assistance.
7. Confirm your return transfer needs and departure details, if applicable.
8. Agree to our terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name. This will help us address you correctly for your booking confirmation.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name. This is also required for confirmation purposes.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address. This is where you will receive your booking confirmation and any important updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number. This is in case we need to contact you with any last-minute changes or updates.
* **Hotel Name**: Enter the name of your Disney hotel. This will help us know where to pick you up from.
* **Hotel Confirmation Number**: If you have a confirmation number from your hotel, please enter it here. This is optional but helps us confirm your booking.
* **Arrival Date**: Enter the date you will be arriving at the airport. This is crucial for our drivers to know when to pick you up.
* **Arrival Time**: Enter your estimated arrival time at the airport. This will help us schedule your transfer accordingly.
* **Flight Number**: Enter your arriving flight number. This will help us track your flight and prepare for your transfer.
* **Number of Passengers**: Enter the number of people who need a transfer. This will help us prepare for your group.
* **Special Requests or Requirements**: If you have any special requests, such as car seats or mobility assistance, please let us know here.
* **Return Transfer Needed**: If you need a return transfer, select 'Yes' and provide your departure date and time.
* **Departure Date**: Enter the date you will be departing the airport, if you need a return transfer.
* **Departure Time**: If you need a return transfer, enter your preferred departure time.
* **Departure Flight Number**: If you need a return transfer, enter your departing flight number.
* **Vehicle Type Preferred**: Select the type of vehicle you would like to use for your transfer. We offer standard sedans, SUVs, luxury vehicles, and vans for larger groups.
* **Parking Location at Airport**: Select the airport where you will be using your transfer service.
* **Preferred Driver Meeting Location**: Choose where you would like to meet your driver, such as ground level or baggage claim.
* **Payment Method**: Select how you would like to pay for your transfer service.
* **Special Occasion or Celebration**: If you are celebrating a special occasion, such as a honeymoon or birthday, let us know here.
* **Agree to Terms and Conditions**: Please read and agree to our terms and conditions to ensure a smooth and efficient transfer process.

Note: Some fields, such as Hotel Confirmation Number, Departure Date, and Departure Flight Number, are optional but will help us confirm your booking.
