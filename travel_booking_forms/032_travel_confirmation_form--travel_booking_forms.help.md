# travel_confirmation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The travel_confirmation_form is a travel-related form designed to collect information from passengers for travel bookings. This form is used by travel agencies or companies to gather necessary details from passengers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with accurate information about your travel details. You can add any additional comments or information that you think is relevant to your booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Confirmation Form** (`travel_confirmation_form`, text, required): This field is the main title of the form. Please fill in your name as it appears on your travel documentation.
* **Passenger Info** (`passenger_info`, text, optional): This field is for any additional information about the passenger, such as any health concerns or special requests.
* **Travel Details** (`travel_details`, text, optional): This field is for any additional travel-related information, such as flight numbers or accommodation requests.
* **Travel Confirmation** (`travel_confirmation`, text, optional): This field is for any additional confirmation or verification of your travel details.
* **Flight Info** (`flight_info`, text, optional): This field is for any information about your flight, such as flight numbers or arrival/departure times.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, optional): This field is for any payment-related information, such as credit card numbers or bank details.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): This field is for any additional comments or information that you think is relevant to your travel booking.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you have any additional information or comments, please include them in the "Additional Comments" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
