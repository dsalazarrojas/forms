# travel_payment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for handling travel-related payments, such as reimbursements or expense reports. It helps travel agencies or companies process payments for various travel expenses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Travel Date** field with the date of the travel or expense.
2. Select the **group_travelers** field by choosing the relevant group type (e.g., Manager, Team, or Customer).
3. Choose a **payment_method** (e.g., Credit Card or Bank Account).
4. Enter the **amount** of the payment or expense.
5. Add any additional **note** about the payment or expense.
6. Enter your **phone** number for contact purposes.
7. Enter your **email** address for contact purposes.
8. Select the person or department responsible for handling the payment or expense (Assigned to: Manager, Assistant, or Customer).
9. Update the **payment_status** to reflect the status of the payment (Pending, Paid, or Rejected).
10. Choose the type of **payment_type** (Travel Expenses or Other).
11. Select the **expense_item** (e.g., Flight, Hotel, Food, or Transportation) associated with the payment or expense.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Date** (1): Enter the date of the travel or expense.
* **group_travelers** (2): Select the group type associated with the travel or expense.
* **payment_method** (3): Choose the payment method for the transaction.
* **amount** (4): Enter the amount of the payment or expense.
* **note** (5): Add any additional information about the payment or expense.
* **phone** (6): Enter your contact phone number.
* **email** (7): Enter your contact email address.
* **Assigned to** (8): Select the person or department responsible for handling the payment or expense.
* **Payment Status** (9): Update the payment status to reflect its current state (Pending, Paid, or Rejected).
* **Payment Type** (10): Choose the type of payment (Travel Expenses or Other).
* **Expense Item** (11): Select the associated expense item (e.g., Flight, Hotel, Food, or Transportation).
