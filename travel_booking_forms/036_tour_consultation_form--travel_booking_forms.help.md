# Tour Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tour Consultation Form is a travel planning tool designed to gather information from users about their travel plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information:
	* Provide your travel agency name in the "Travel Agency" field.
2. Enter your name in the "Traveler Name" field.
3. Enter your travel dates in the "Travel Dates" field in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
4. Select the type(s) of travel you are planning in the "Type of Travel" field.
5. Enter your travel destination in the "Travel Destination" field.
6. Enter your travel agent's name in the "Travel Agent" field.
7. Enter your contact number in the international format (e.g., +1-123-456-7890).
8. Enter any additional message in the "Message" field.
9. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Travel Agency** (`travel_agency`, text, optional): Provide the name of the travel agency you are using.
- **Traveler Name** (`traveler_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your passport.
- **Travel Dates** (`travel_dates`, date, optional): Enter your travel dates in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
- **Type of Travel** (`travel_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more types of travel you are planning (e.g., adventure, leisure, business).
- **Travel Destination** (`travel_destination`, text, optional): Enter the destination of your trip.
- **Travel Agent** (`travel_agent`, text, optional): Enter your travel agent's name.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number in the international format (e.g., +1-123-456-7890).
- **Message** (`message`, note, optional): Enter any additional information you would like to share with us.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- Use the international format for contact numbers and email addresses.
