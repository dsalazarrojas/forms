The form purpose is to allow pre-flight passengers to provide essential information about themselves for airline check-in and boarding processes. This form aims to gather necessary details such as passenger identity, travel documentation, health status, and special requests, which will be used for efficient and safe handling of their flight.

The form serves several key purposes:

*   Ensures accurate passenger information and documentation are up-to-date and valid.
*   Identifies any special requirements or restrictions for the passenger.
*   Facilitates smooth communication between the airline and the passenger.
*   Helps prevent delays or complications during the check-in and boarding process.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **Passenger Full Name** in the first field. This must match the name on your passport or government ID.
2.  Provide your **Booking Reference (PNR)**, which is usually provided by the airline or travel agent. This helps the airline identify your booking details.
3.  Enter your **Email Address** so the airline can contact you in case of any issues or updates.
4.  Enter your **Mobile Phone Number** for quick contact during the flight.
5.  Enter the **Flight Number** for which you are traveling.
6.  Select the **Date of Travel**, which is the date of your flight.
7.  For the **Passport and Documentation** section, ensure you have a valid passport. If you do not have a passport, you must have alternative documentation that the airline accepts.
8.  Enter your **Passport Number**.
9.  Select your **Passport Expiry Date**.
10.  Answer whether you have a **Valid Visa for your Destination**. This includes options for passengers who do not need a visa, are in the process of obtaining one, or have already been granted a visa.
11.  In the **Baggage and Health** section, select the number of **Bags to Check-in**.
12.  Confirm that you are not carrying any **Prohibited Dangerous Goods**.
13.  Declare if you are experiencing any symptoms of **Infectious Disease**.
14.  Select whether you require **Airport Assistance** for this flight.
15.  Finally, confirm that all the information you have provided is accurate.

# Airline Pre Flight Passenger Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Airline Pre-Flight Passenger Check In Form is designed to collect essential information from passengers prior to their flight. This includes personal and travel-related details to facilitate smooth processing and boarding procedures.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your personal details and travel information as requested in the form.
2.  Ensure the accuracy and completeness of the information provided.
3.  Review each section carefully to ensure all required fields are filled.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Passenger Full Name** (`Passenger Full Name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your passport or government ID.
*   **Booking Reference (PNR)** (`booking_reference`, `text`, required): Enter the booking reference number provided by the airline or travel agent.
*   **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
*   **Mobile Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your mobile phone number for quick contact.
*   **Flight Number** (`flight_number`, `text`, required): Enter the flight number for which you are traveling.
*   **Date of Travel** (`travel_date`, `date`, required): Select the date of your flight.
*   **Passport and Documentation** (`section_documentation`, `note`, not required): Ensure you have a valid passport or acceptable alternative documentation.
*   **Passport Number** (`passport_number`, `text`, required): Enter your passport number.
*   **Passport Expiry Date** (`passport_expiry`, `date`, required): Enter your passport expiry date.
*   **Do you have a valid visa for your destination?** (`visa_required`, `select_one`, required): Select your visa status.
*   **Number of Bags to Check-in** (`checked_bags_count`, `number`, required): Select the number of bags you are checking.
*   **I confirm I am not carrying any prohibited dangerous goods** (`dangerous_goods_declaration`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you are not carrying prohibited items.
*   **Are you currently experiencing any symptoms of infectious disease?** (`health_declaration`, `select_one`, required): Declare any health symptoms.
*   **Do you require airport assistance for this flight?** (`special_assistance_reminder`, `select_one`, required): Select your airport assistance requirements.
*   **All information provided is correct to the best of my knowledge** (`confirmation_statement`, `select_one`, required): Confirm the accuracy of your provided information.

Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information to help us process your flight smoothly. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the airline for assistance.
