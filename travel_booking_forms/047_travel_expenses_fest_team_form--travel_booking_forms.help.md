# Travel Expenses Fest Team Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect travel expenses information from team members for reimbursement purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully to ensure you understand all the required fields.
2.  Fill out all the fields as accurately as possible.
3.  If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Member's Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official identification. This is a required field.
* **Travel Expense Amount**: Enter the total amount of your travel expenses. This is a required field.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method used for your expenses. This can be Cash, Credit card, or Debit card. This field is optional.
* **Date of Arrival**: Enter the date you arrived at your destination. This field is optional.
* **Date of Departure**: Enter the date you departed from your destination. This field is optional.
* **Travel Destination**: Select the city you traveled to. You can choose from New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, Miami, Austin, or Boston. This field is optional.
* **Number of People Traveling**: Enter the number of people traveling with you. This field is optional.
* **Travel Purpose**: Enter the purpose of your travel. This field is optional.
* **Travel Mode of Transportation**: Select all modes of transportation used for your travel, such as Car, Train, Plane, Bus, or Other. This is a required field.
* **Additional Comments or Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your travel. This field is optional.
* **Travel Expenses Details**: Enter any additional details about your travel expenses, such as accommodation and food. This field is optional.
* **Review and Submit**: Please review your submission carefully and click on the "I agree to the terms and conditions" radio button to submit your form. If you do not agree to the terms and conditions, please do not submit this form. This is a required field.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
* Please review your form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
