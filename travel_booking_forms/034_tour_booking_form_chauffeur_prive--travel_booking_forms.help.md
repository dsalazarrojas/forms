# Tour Booking Form Chauffeur Prive - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a tour with Chauffeur Prive, a luxury travel service. It helps us gather the necessary information to provide a customized experience for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields with the requested information and submit it. You can skip fields if they are not required or not applicable to your tour.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tour Details (tour_booking_form_chauffeur_prive)**: Enter a brief description of the tour you're interested in booking.
* **Date (tour_booking_form_chauffeur_prive_date)**: Select the date of the tour you're interested in booking.
* **Time (tour_booking_form_chauffeur_prive_time)**: Select the time of the tour you're interested in booking.
* **Activity (tour_booking_form_chauffeur_prive_activity)**: Choose whether you want to participate in any activities during the tour.
	+ Select 'Yes' if you want to participate in activities.
	+ Select 'No' if you don't want to participate in activities.
* **Duration (tour_booking_form_chauffeur_prive_duration)**: Enter the duration of the tour in hours.
* **Distance (tour_booking_form_chauffeur_prive_distance)**: Enter the distance of the tour in kilometers or miles.
* **Price (tour_booking_form_chauffeur_prive_price)**: Enter the price of the tour in the currency specified.
* **Description (tour_booking_form_chauffeur_prive_description)**: Enter any additional details or notes about the tour.
* **Email (tour_booking_form_chauffeur_prive_email)**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone (tour_booking_form_chauffeur_prive_phone)**: Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have all the necessary information ready before filling out this form.
* Double-check the information you enter to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
