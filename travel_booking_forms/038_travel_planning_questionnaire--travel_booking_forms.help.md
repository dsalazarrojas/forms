# travel_planning_questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information for travel planning purposes. It is designed to collect basic details about the destination and travel dates. Please fill out this form to provide essential information for travel planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "destination" field with the name of your desired destination.
2. Next, enter your travel dates in the "travel_dates" field.
3. Finally, specify the duration of your trip in the "travel_duration" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Destination** (`destination`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the city, country, or region you would like to visit.
* **Travel Dates** (`travel_dates`, date, required/optional): Select the dates for your trip.
* **Travel Duration** (`travel_duration`, number, required/optional): Specify the number of days or weeks for your trip.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you enter a valid date for the "travel_dates" field.
* The "travel_duration" field requires a numerical value.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask for assistance.
