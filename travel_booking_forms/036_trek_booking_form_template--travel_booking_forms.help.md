# trek_booking_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The trek_booking_form_template is a form designed to collect information from customers interested in booking a trek or tour. It is intended to gather details about the customers and their trek preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer details section with your name, contact information, and any other relevant details you'd like to share with the tour operator.
2. In the trek details section, provide the name of the trek you are interested in, the dates you'd like to go, and any specific requests or preferences you have.
3. In the payment details section, enter any payment information you'd like to provide, such as credit card details or other payment methods.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details (customer_details)** (`name`, `text`, optional): This section is where you can provide your name and contact information, such as email and phone number. Please fill in any relevant details to help the tour operator contact and communicate with you.
* **Trek Details (trek_details)** (`name`, `text`, optional): In this section, enter the name of the trek you are interested in, and provide the dates you'd like to go on the trek. You can also include any specific requests or preferences you have, such as difficulty level or accommodation type.
* **Payment Details (payment_details)** (`name`, `text`, optional): This section is where you can enter any payment information, such as credit card details or other payment methods. Please ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date payment information to facilitate a smooth booking process.
