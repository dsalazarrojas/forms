# Travel Cancellations And Rebookings Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for travel cancellations and rebookings to collect necessary information for efficient communication between travel agencies and customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "reason for cancellation" field with the reason why the travel booking was cancelled.
2. Enter the "rebooking date" and "rebooking time" fields with the date and time when the booking was rebooked.
3. Enter your "customer email" address so that we can contact you for further information.
4. Select the "travel agency" and "travel agency manager" fields to provide their contact details.
5. Choose the "rebooked status" option to indicate whether the rebooking was confirmed or not.
6. Select the "travel agency approval" option to enter the agency's approval status and reason for approval or rejection.
7. Enter the "travel agency approval reason" field to provide any additional information for the agency's approval.
8. Enter the "rebooked by status" field to indicate the rebooking status and reason for rebooking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reason for Cancellation**: Enter the reason why the travel booking was cancelled.
* **Rebooking Date**: Enter the date when the booking was rebooked.
* **Rebooking Time**: Enter the time when the booking was rebooked.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you for further information.
* **Travel Agency**: Select your travel agency.
* **Travel Agency Manager**: Enter the name of the travel agency manager.
* **Rebooked Status**: Choose whether the rebooking was confirmed or not.
* **Travel Agency Approval**: Select the agency's approval status.
* **Travel Agency Approval Reason**: Enter any additional information for the agency's approval.
* **Rebooked By Status**: Enter the reason for rebooking.
* **Travel Agency Rebooked Date**: Enter the date when the booking was rebooked by the agency.
* **Travel Agency Rebooked Time**: Enter the time when the booking was rebooked by the agency.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate submission.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for further assistance.
* Please note that this form is for travel cancellations and rebookings only, and it is essential to fill it out correctly to avoid any confusion.
