# medical-declaration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your medical conditions, travel plans, and other relevant details to ensure that you are fit for travel.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each field carefully and fill in the required information accurately.
- Make sure to fill in all required fields, marked as "required".
- Use the date picker for date fields.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact the travel agency or operator for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Declaration Form** (`medical_declaration_form`, text, not required): This field is for any additional medical declarations or comments.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, not required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, not required): Please enter your last name.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, not required): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Address** (`address`, text, not required): Enter your current address.
* **Country** (`country`, text, not required): Enter the country you are traveling from.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, not required): Provide contact information for your emergency contact, including their name and phone number.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, not required): List any known medical conditions that may affect your travel.
* **Travel Purpose** (`travel_purpose`, text, not required): Enter the purpose of your travel.
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of your travel.
* **Return Date** (`return_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of your return.
* **Medical Form Status** (`medical_form_status`, select_one, not required): Choose the status of your medical declaration form (Completed, In Progress, Not Started).
* **Medical Form Status Hint** (`medical_form_status_hint`, text, not required): Provide any additional information about the status of your medical declaration form.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, not required): Add any additional comments about your medical declaration form.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, not required): Enter the relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Travel Document Number** (`travel_document_number`, text, not required): Enter any relevant travel document numbers.
* **Travel Document Expiration** (`travel_document_expiration`, date, not required): Enter the expiration date of your travel document.
* **Travel Document Issuance Date** (`travel_document_issuance_date`, date, not required): Enter the date your travel document was issued.
* **Flight Number** (`flight_number`, text, not required): Enter the flight number.
* **Flight Departure Date** (`flight_departure_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of your flight departure.
* **Flight Arrival Date** (`flight_arrival_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of your flight arrival.

Note: Some fields may not be relevant to your travel plans or medical conditions. If you are unsure, consult with the travel agency or operator before completing the form.
