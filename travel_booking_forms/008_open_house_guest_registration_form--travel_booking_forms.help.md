# Open House Guest Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from guests attending events at our premises. Please fill it out accurately to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in your Guest Name, which should be your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
2. Enter your Email address, which will be used to send you updates and instructions about the event.
3. Provide your Phone number, which will help us contact you in case of any last-minute updates or changes.
4. Select the date and time you plan to attend the event.
5. Specify the event location where you'll be attending.
6. If you've made travel arrangements through an agent, please fill in their name, phone number, and email address for our reference.
7. Finally, add any additional comments or requests you may have in the "Message" field, which will help us tailor our services to your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email**: A valid email address where you can be reached for updates and instructions.
* **Phone**: Your phone number for quick contact and updates.
* **Event Date**: The date you plan to attend the event. Please ensure accuracy to avoid any confusion.
* **Event Time**: The time you plan to arrive at the event.
* **Event Location**: The specific location where the event will take place.
* **Agent Name**: If you've booked travel arrangements through an agency, enter their name here.
* **Agent Phone**: The phone number of your travel agent for quick contact.
* **Agent Email**: The email address of your travel agent for updates and instructions.
* **Message**: Any additional comments or requests you have for us to know about your visit.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your contact information for accuracy to avoid any delays or miscommunications.
* If you have any special requests or needs, please let us know in the "Message" field so we can tailor our services to your requirements.
* If you've made travel arrangements through an agent, please ensure they provide us with your updated contact information to guarantee smooth communication.
