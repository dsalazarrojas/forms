## Sustainable Travel Cottage Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Sustainable Travel Cottage Booking Form is designed to collect information from customers about their travel plans, sustainability preferences, and contact details. This form will help us understand their needs and provide them with a customized booking experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, start by providing your Guest Details in the first section.
2. Next, select the dates for your travel in the Travel Dates section.
3. Then, choose your sustainability preferences in the Sustainability Preferences section. Select all options that apply to ensure a more sustainable travel experience.
4. Choose how you would like to be contacted after submitting the form: by email, phone, or not at all. In the Contact Method section, select one of the available options.
5. Finally, provide your email address and phone number (if you want to be contacted) in the Email and Phone sections.
6. Add any additional notes about your booking in the Booking Notes section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Details (Guest Details)** (`text`, required: false): Please provide your name, surname, and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Travel Dates (Travel Dates)** (`text`, required: false): Enter the dates of your travel, including start and end dates.
* **Sustainability Preferences (Sustainability Preferences)** (`select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ **Sustainable**: Select this option if you prioritize environmentally friendly practices and activities.
	+ **Non-Sustainable**: Select this option if you prefer non-sustainable practices and activities.
* **Contact Method (Contact Method)** (`select_one`, required: false):
	+ **email**: We will contact you via email.
	+ **phone**: We will contact you via phone.
	+ **None**: Do not contact you after submitting the form.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, required: false): Enter your email address to receive further instructions or information about your booking.
* **Phone (Phone)** (`text`, required: false): Enter your phone number to be contacted about your booking.
* **Booking Notes (Booking Notes)** (`note`, required: false): Add any additional information or requests you may have for your booking.

## Tips
* Please be accurate when filling out the form to ensure a smooth and efficient booking process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We will contact you according to your chosen contact method after receiving your form submission.
