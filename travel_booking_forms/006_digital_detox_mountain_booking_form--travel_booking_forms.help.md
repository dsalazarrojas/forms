# Digital Detox Mountain Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Detox Mountain Booking Form is designed to collect information from guests for their stay at the Digital Detox Mountain retreat. This form helps us to ensure that we have all the necessary details to provide an exceptional experience for our guests. Please complete this form accurately and thoroughly to help us understand your preferences and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Carefully read each field and its explanation to understand what information is required.
* Complete each field accurately and thoroughly.
* Submit the form once you have filled out all the required fields.
* If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name (text, required: false)**: Please enter your full name as you would like to be addressed during your stay.
* **Email (email, required: false)**: Enter your email address so we can communicate with you about your booking and any updates or changes.
* **Checkin Date (date, required: false)**: Select the date you would like to arrive at the retreat.
* **Checkout Date (date, required: false)**: Select the date you would like to depart from the retreat.
* **Guest Phone (text, required: false)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you for any important updates or changes.
* **Room Choice (select_multiple, required: false)**: Choose the rooms or accommodations that suit your preferences. You can select multiple options.
*   Option 1: Mountain View Room
*   Option 2: Valley View Room
*   Option 3: Suite Room
*   Option 4: Shared Room
*   Option 5: Other (please specify in the Special Requests field)
* **Room Type (select_one, required: false)**: Choose the type of room that best suits your needs.
	+ Option 1: Single Occupancy
	+ Option 2: Double Occupancy
	+ Option 3: Family Room
* **Booking Method (select_one, required: false)**: Choose how you would like to book your room.
	+ Option 1: Online Booking
	+ Option 2: Phone Booking
	+ Option 3: Email Booking
* **Special Requests (text, required: false)**: If you have any special requests or requirements, please specify them here.
* **Guest Notes (note, required: false)**: Share any additional information about your stay that might help us provide better service.
* **Travel Agent (text, required: false)**: If you have a travel agent or representative, please enter their name or contact information.
* **Guest Country (text, required: false)**: Enter your country of origin so we can provide relevant travel information.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields (if any) are filled out accurately.
* If you're unsure about any field, don't hesitate to contact us for clarification.
* We appreciate your cooperation in providing detailed and truthful information to help us provide the best possible experience for you.
