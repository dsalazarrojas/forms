# Dreamy Memory Makers - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dreamy Memory Makers form is designed to gather travel booking information from users. This form is intended for travel agency employees, travel agents, or anyone responsible for booking travel arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Select the relevant travel agency and accommodations options that fit your needs.
3. Enter any special requests, if applicable.
4. Double-check your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Agency**: Select the travel agency that will handle your booking.
	+ Choose from three options: Agency A, Agency B, or Agency C.
* **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address.
	+ This is a required field and should match the email address associated with your booking.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
	+ This is a required field and helps us contact you for any questions or updates.
* **Travel Dates**: Select your travel dates.
	+ Enter your travel start and end dates in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Arrival Time**: Select your arrival time.
	+ This is a required field and helps us arrange your transportation.
* **Total People**: Enter the number of people in your travel group.
	+ This is a required field and ensures we arrange enough space for everyone.
* **Accommodations**: Choose your preferred type of accommodations.
	+ Select from three options: Hotel, Hostel, or Airbnb.
* **Special Requests**: Enter any additional requests or instructions you'd like us to consider.
	+ This is an optional field, so you may skip it unless you have specific needs.
* **Departure Time**: Select your departure time.
	+ This is a required field and helps us arrange your transportation back home.
* **Trip Duration**: Enter the duration of your trip in days.
	+ This is a required field and helps us plan your itinerary accordingly.
* **Group Size**: Enter the number of people in your travel group.
	+ This is a required field and ensures we arrange enough space for everyone.

## Tips
- Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure that you select the correct travel agency and accommodations for your needs.
- If you have any special requests, include them in the "Special Requests" field.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us through your email address or phone number.
