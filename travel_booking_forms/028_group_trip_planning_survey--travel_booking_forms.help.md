# Group Trip Planning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your group's preferences and requirements for a trip. Please take a few minutes to complete this form to help us tailor your trip to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
2. If you are unsure about any question, ask for clarification.
3. Review your answers before submission to ensure they are accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Group Travel Preferences**:
  - *Group Travel Preferences* (`group_trip_planning_survey_page_1`, text, required/optional): This is a text field where you can write any additional preferences or requirements you have for the trip. 
- **Trip Start Date**:
  - *Trip Start Date* (`trip_start_date`, date, required/optional): This is where you will enter the date you would like the trip to start. 
- **Trip End Date**:
  - *Trip End Date* (`trip_end_date`, date, required/optional): This is where you will enter the date you would like the trip to end. 
- **Budget Allocation**:
  - *Budget Allocation* (`budget_allocation`, number, required/optional): This is where you will enter the percentage of your budget you would like to allocate to accommodation, food, etc.
  - *Budget Amount* (`budget_amount`, number, required/optional): This is where you will enter the total budget amount for the trip. 
- **Travel Destination**:
  - *Travel Destination* (`travel_destination`, text, required/optional): This is where you will enter the destination you would like to travel to. 
- **Trip Priority**:
  - *Trip Priority* (`trip_priority`, select_one, required/optional): This is where you will select your priority level for the trip (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4, Option 5). 
- **Group Members**:
  - *Group Members* (`group_members`, select_multiple, required): This is where you will select all members of your group. 
- **Contact Info**:
  - *Contact Info* (`contact_info`, email, required/optional): This is where you will enter your email address. 
- **Phone Number**:
  - *Phone Number* (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): This is where you will enter your phone number. 
- **Email Address**:
  - *Email Address* (`email_address`, email, required/optional): This is where you will enter your email address. 
- **Travel Mode**:
  - *Travel Mode* (`travel_mode`, select_one, required/optional): This is where you will select your travel mode (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4, Option 5). 
- **Trip Description**:
  - *Trip Description* (`trip_description`, text, required/optional): This is where you will enter a detailed description of your trip.
