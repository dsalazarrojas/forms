# travel_planning_questionnaire_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect travel planning information from users, including their travel preferences, dates, destinations, budget, and other relevant details. It is essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure that your travel needs are met.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by reading through each page of the form carefully.
2.  Answer all required fields truthfully, as they are essential for effective travel planning.
3.  Fill out the form completely and submit it to the travel planner.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*  **Travel Preferences** (`travel_preferences`, text, required): Enter your travel preferences, such as whether you prefer relaxation or adventure.
*  **Travel Dates** (`travel_dates`, date, required): Select your travel dates in the format MM/DD/YY.
*  **Travel Duration** (`travel_duration`, number, optional): Enter the length of your trip in days.
*  **Destination** (`destination`, select_one, required): Choose your destination among the options provided.
*  **Travel Budget** (`travel_bud`, select_multiple, optional): Select your travel budget from the provided options.
*  **Accommodation** (`accommodation`, text, optional): Enter any specific accommodation requirements or preferences.
*  **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, email, required): Enter your email address or other contact information for the travel planner.
*  **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Describe any special requests or requirements for your trip.
*  **Confirm Travel Details** (`confirm_travel_details`, select_one, required): Verify that you have reviewed and confirm your travel details.
*  **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, note, optional): Enter any additional information you think is necessary for the travel planner.
*  **Review** (`review`, select_multiple, required): Rate your experience with the travel planner.
*  **Confirm Submission** (`confirm_submission`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have reviewed and submitted your form truthfully.

## Tips
*  Be honest and accurate when filling out the form to ensure that your travel needs are met.
*  Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
*  If you have any questions or concerns, contact the travel planner for assistance.
