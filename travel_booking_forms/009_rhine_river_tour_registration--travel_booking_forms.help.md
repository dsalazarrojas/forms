# Rhine River Tour Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a tour with the Rhine River tour operators. It is essential to provide accurate information to ensure that we can tailor the tour experience to meet your expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your **Tour Operator** details in the text field.
2. Enter the **Tour Dates** you are interested in.
3. Indicate if you are traveling with a **Party Size** by selecting "Yes" or "No".
4. Provide your **Email** address to enable us to contact you with tour updates.
5. Enter your **Phone** number for us to contact you in case of any changes.
6. Add any **Notes** about your tour preferences, requirements, or concerns.
7. If necessary, select a **Confirmation Date** for your tour reservation.
8. Enter your **Departure Date** for your tour.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tour Operator**: Provide the name and contact information of the tour operator or agency you are working with.
* **Tour Dates**: Enter the dates you are interested in for your tour, in the format of MM/DD/YYYY. For example, 06/12/2025.
* **Party Size**: Select "Yes" if you are traveling with a group of people, or "No" if you are traveling solo.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you with tour updates.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you in case of any changes.
* **Notes**: Add any additional information about your tour preferences, requirements, or concerns.
* **Confirmation Date**: If you have already confirmed your tour dates, select the date you confirmed the tour.
* **Departure Date**: Enter your expected departure date for your tour, in the format of MM/DD/YYYY. For example, 06/12/2025.

## Tips

* Please make sure to provide accurate information to ensure we can tailor the tour experience to meet your expectations.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [insert contact info].
