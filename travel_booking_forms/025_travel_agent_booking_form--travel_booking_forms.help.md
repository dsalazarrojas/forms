# travel_agent_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by travel agents to book travel arrangements for customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the booking details.
2. Enter the customer's information.
3. Provide travel details.
4. Enter payment details.
5. Confirm your booking with the customer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Booking Details (`booking_details`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter a brief description of the travel arrangement, including the travel dates, destinations, and any special requests.
* **Customer Info (`customer_info`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the customer's name, email, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Travel Details (`travel_details`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter information about the travel, such as flight numbers, hotel reservations, and rental car details.
* **Payment Details (`payment_details`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the payment method and any additional payment information.
* **Confirm Booking (`confirm_booking`, text, required: false):
	+ Confirm that you have spoken with the customer about their travel plans.
* **Customer Notes (`customer_notes`, note, required: false):
	+ Enter any notes or comments about the customer.
* **Email (`email`, email, required: false):
	+ Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone (`phone`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Confirm Phone (`confirm_phone`, text, required: false):
	+ Confirm that you have spoken with the customer about their phone number.
* **Confirm Email (`confirm_email`, email, required: false):
	+ Confirm that you have spoken with the customer about their email address.
* **Confirm Phone 2 (`confirm_phone_2`, text, required: false):
	+ Confirm that you have spoken with the customer about their phone number (again).
* **Booking Status (`booking_status`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Select the current status of the booking (booked, cancelled, or pending).
* **Travel Date (`travel_date`, date, required: false):
	+ Enter the date of the travel.
* **Departure Time (`departure_time`, time, required: false):
	+ Enter the departure time of the travel.
* **Arrival Time (`arrival_time`, time, required: false):
	+ Enter the arrival time of the travel.
* **Departure Time 2 (`departure_time_2`, time, required: false):
	+ Enter the departure time of the travel (again).
* **Travel Date 2 (`travel_date_2`, date, required: false):
	+ Enter the date of the travel (again).
* **Travel Duration (`travel_duration`, number, required: false):
	+ Enter the duration of the travel.
* **Arrival Date (`arrival_date`, date, required: false):
	+ Enter the arrival date of the travel.
* **Travel Date 3 (`travel_date_3`, date, required: false):
	+ Enter the date of the travel (again).
* **Travel Duration 2 (`travel_duration_2`, number, required: false):
	+ Enter the duration of the travel (again).
* **Travel Duration 3 (`travel_duration_3`, number, required: false):
	+ Enter the duration of the travel (again).
* **Departure Date (`departure_date`, date, required: false):
	+ Enter the date of the departure.
* **Travel Duration 4 (`travel_duration_4`, number, required: false):
	+ Enter the duration of the travel.
* **Confirm Departure (`confirm_departure`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Confirm the departure date and time of the travel.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and relevant information about the travel arrangement.
* Double-check your information with the customer to ensure that everything is correct.
* Be clear and concise in your communication with the customer about their travel plans.
