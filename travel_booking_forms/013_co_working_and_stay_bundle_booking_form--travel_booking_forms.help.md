# Co Working And Stay Bundle Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for travelers to book a co-working and stay bundle that meets their specific needs and preferences. The form gathers information about the traveler's accommodation type, workspace setup, and other requirements to ensure a comfortable and productive stay.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately, including all required fields.
2. Select the preferred bundle type from the list of options.
3. Choose the type of lodging and workspace setup that suits your needs.
4. Provide your arrival and departure dates.
5. Enter your contact information, including email address and phone number.
6. Select your meal plan preference and any dietary restrictions.
7. Indicate your purpose of travel and any special requests.
8. Confirm your understanding of the cancellation policy and agree to the terms and conditions.
9. Choose your preferred payment method and provide any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Your legal name, which we will use to address you and communicate with you. (Required)
* **Email Address**: Your primary contact email, which we will use for communication. (Required)
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number, which we will use for coordination. (Required)
* **Company Name**: The name of your company or organization, if applicable. (Optional)
* **Job Title**: Your professional title, if you wish to share. (Optional)
* **Bundle Type**: Select the co-working and stay bundle that suits your needs. (Required)
* **Accommodation Type**: Choose the type of lodging that meets your preferences. (Required)
* **Workspace Type**: Select the workspace setup that suits your needs. (Required)
* **Arrival Date**: Enter the date of your arrival. (Required)
* **Departure Date**: Enter the date of your departure. (Required)
* **Number of Guests**: Enter the total number of people in your party. (Required)
* **Length of Stay**: Select the duration category that suits your stay. (Required)
* **Required Amenities**: Select the must-have amenities for your stay. (Optional)
* **Meal Plan Preference**: Choose your meal plan option. (Optional)
* **Dietary Restrictions**: Share any dietary restrictions or allergies. (Optional)
* **Purpose of Travel**: Indicate the reason for your travel. (Required)
* **Special Requests**: Share any special requests or preferences. (Optional)
* **Need Airport Transportation**: Indicate if you need airport pick-up or drop-off. (Optional)
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: Share how you found our services. (Optional)
* **Are You a Previous Guest**: Indicate if you have stayed with us before. (Required)
* **Budget Range**: Select your estimated budget for the stay. (Optional)
* **Payment Method**: Choose the payment method. (Required)
* **Cancellation Policy Understood**: Confirm your understanding of the cancellation policy. (Required)
* **Terms and Conditions Accepted**: Agree to the terms and conditions. (Required)
* **Additional Comments**: Share any additional comments or information. (Optional)
