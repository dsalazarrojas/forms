# Travel Booking Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request travel booking information. Please fill out the form to provide essential details about your travel plans, and our team will assist you in processing your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address to receive important updates about your travel booking.
3. Provide your Phone Number so we can contact you if needed.
4. Select a Date for your travel.
5. Choose a Time for your travel.
6. Indicate your Departure Airport selection from the dropdown menu.
7. Select your Destination Airport from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, **required**): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, **required**): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **optional**): Enter your email address to receive updates about your travel booking.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, **optional**): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Date** (`date`, date, **optional**): Select the date of your travel.
* **Time** (`time`, time, **optional**): Choose the time of your travel.
* **Departure Airport** (`departure_airport`, select multiple, **optional**): Select your departure airport.
* **Destination Airport** (`destination_airport`, select multiple, **optional**): Select your destination airport.

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill out all required fields (First Name and Last Name).
* If you have any special requests or preferences, please mention them in the comments section.
* Our team will review and process your travel request within a timely manner.
