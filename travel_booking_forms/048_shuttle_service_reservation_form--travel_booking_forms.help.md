# Shuttle Service Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect passenger details and shuttle service preferences for booking a shuttle service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Please select "Passenger Details" and provide a brief description of yourself and other passengers.
* Choose the pickup location from the available options (Airport, Bus Station, Train Station, or Other).
* Select the dropoff location from the available options (Airport, Bus Station, Train Station, or Other).
* Enter the date you would like to book the shuttle service.
* Choose the time you would like to book the shuttle service.
* Select the type of shuttle service you prefer (Bus, Van, Limo, or Other).
* Enter the number of passengers traveling with you.
* Provide your phone number and email address for communication purposes.
* Add any additional message or special requests for the shuttle service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Passenger Details**: Please provide a brief description of yourself and other passengers.
* **Pickup Location**: Choose the location where you would like to be picked up from.
* **Dropoff Location**: Choose the location where you would like to be dropped off at.
* **Date**: Enter the date you would like to book the shuttle service.
* **Time**: Enter the time you would like to book the shuttle service.
* **Shuttle Type**: Select the type of shuttle service you prefer.
* **Number of Passengers**: Enter the number of passengers traveling with you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Message**: Add any additional message or special requests for the shuttle service.
