# Tourist Card Recharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help tourists provide necessary information for their nationality card recharge. Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your accurate and up-to-date information.
2. If you have a nationality that is not listed in the select options, please type it in the "Nationality Other" field.
3. Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy.
4. Ensure the ID issue date and expiry date are correct.
5. Fill out the "ID Number Issue Place" field accurately.
6. Select the correct ID type and issue authority from the options.
7. If you have any other nationality ID information, please select "Other" in the "Nationality" field and provide details in the "Nationality Other" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your passport or ID.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your passport or ID.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Nationality**: Select your nationality from the list or type "Other" if not listed.
* **Nationality Other**: If you selected "Other" in the "Nationality" field, provide your nationality here.
* **Email**: Enter your email address accurately.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number accurately.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number accurately.
* **Address**: Enter your current address accurately.
* **Country**: Select your country of residence from the list.
* **National ID Number**: Enter your ID number accurately.
* **Passport Number**: Enter your passport number accurately.
* **ID Type**: Select the type of your ID from the list.
* **ID Expiry Date**: Enter your ID expiry date accurately.
* **National ID Issue Authority**: Select the authority that issued your ID from the list.
* **ID Issue Place**: Enter the place where your ID was issued accurately.
* **ID Issue Country**: Select the country where your ID was issued from the list.
* **ID Expiry Authority**: Select the authority that verifies your ID expiry from the list.
* **ID Number Issue Place**: Enter the place where your ID number was issued accurately.
* **ID Number Issue Country**: Select the country where your ID number was issued from the list.
* **ID Number Issue Authority**: Select the authority that issued your ID number from the list.
* **ID Renewal Date**: Enter the date your ID was renewed accurately.
* **National ID Renewal Authority**: Select the authority that renewed your ID from the list.
