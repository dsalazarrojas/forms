# travel_itinerary - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect travel-related information from users. It is intended to be filled out by travelers who need to submit their itinerary.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your destination.
2. Enter your travel date.
3. If you're returning from your trip, enter your return date.
4. Select the type of travel you are undertaking (outbound, inbound, or round trip).
5. Select the type of travel document you require (Passport or ID).
6. Enter your email address.
7. Enter your phone number.
8. Select your address type (street, apartment, or house).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Destination**: Enter the location you are traveling to.
* **Travel Date**: Enter the date you plan to travel.
* **Return Date**: (Optional) Enter the date you plan to return from your trip.
* **Travel Type**: Select the type of travel you are undertaking (outbound, inbound, or round trip).
* **Travel Type** (second occurrence): Select the type of travel you are undertaking (business, leisure, or holiday).
*   **Travel Type** (third occurrence): This field is not necessary as it's a repeat of the second one, but it seems like it was intended to be another type selection.
*   **Travel Document**: Select the type of travel document you require (Passport or ID).
*   **Email**: Enter your email address.
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
*   **Address**: Select the type of address you have (street, apartment, or house).
