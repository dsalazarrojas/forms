# Trip Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trip Survey form is designed to collect information about travelers, their destinations, and travel-related activities. This form will help travel agencies provide better services to their customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name
2. Enter your Last Name
3. Enter your Email
4. Enter your Phone Number
5. Select the Date of Travel
6. Select the Time of Travel
7. Select the Travel Destination
8. Select the Travel Dates
9. Enter the Duration of Travel
10. Enter your Travel Budget
11. Select your Travel Frequency
12. Enter the Reason for Travel
13. Select the Type of Travel (if applicable)
14. Select the Currency of Travel
15. Enter the Tool Assigned to you
16. Enter the User Assigned to you
17. Enter the Travel Agency
18. Enter the Travel Agency Contact
19. Enter the Travel Agency Phone Number
20. Enter Travel Notes
21. Enter Travel Comments
22. Enter the Form ID
23. Enter the Form Category
24. Enter the Output File
25. Enter a Description of your trip

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name
* **Email** (3, email, required): Enter your email address
* **Phone** (4, text, required): Enter your phone number
* **Date of Travel** (5, date, required): Select the date you plan to travel
* **Time of Travel** (6, time, required): Select the time you plan to travel
* **Travel Destination** (7, select_one, required): Select your travel destination
* **Travel Dates** (8, date, required): Select the dates you plan to travel
* **Travel Duration** (9, number, required): Enter the duration of your travel
* **Travel Budget** (10, number, required): Enter your travel budget
* **Travel Frequency** (11, select_one, required): Select how often you plan to travel
* **Travel Reason** (12, text, required): Enter the reason for your travel
* **Travel Type** (13, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of travel (if applicable)
* **Travel Currency** (14, select_one, required): Select the currency of your travel
* **Assigned Tool** (15, text, optional): Enter the tool assigned to you
* **Assigned User** (16, text, optional): Enter the user assigned to you
* **Travel Agency** (17, text, optional): Enter the travel agency
* **Travel Agency Contact** (18, text, optional): Enter the travel agency contact
* **Travel Agency Phone** (19, text, optional): Enter the travel agency phone number
* **Travel Notes** (20, note, optional): Enter travel notes
* **Travel Comments** (21, text, optional): Enter travel comments
* **Form ID** (22, text, optional): Enter the form ID
* **Form Category** (23, text, optional): Enter the form category
* **Output File** (24, text, optional): Enter the output file
* **Description** (25, text, optional): Enter a description of your trip
