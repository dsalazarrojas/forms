# travel_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect travel-related information for corporate or business travel purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each section carefully and provide information as needed.
2. Enter travel dates in the format MM/DD/YYYY (month/day/year).
3. For fields with select options, choose the relevant option for each question.
4. For text fields, enter a brief description or information.
5. For number fields, enter the corresponding numeric value.
6. For date fields, enter dates in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
7. Submit the form once you have completed all required and desired fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Dates** (`travel_dates`): Enter the travel dates in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Destination City** (`destination_city`): Enter the city of your travel destination.
* **Destination Country** (`destination_country`): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have a destination country.
* **Travel Mode** (`travel_mode`): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have a travel mode.
* **Travel Reason** (`travel_reason`): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have a travel reason.
* **Travel Group** (`travel_group`): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' for each travel group you are with.
* **Travel Arrival Time** (`travel_arrival_time`): Enter the travel arrival time in 12-hour format (e.g., 01:45 PM).
* **Travel Duration** (`travel_duration`): Enter the duration of your travel.
* **Travel Currency** (`travel_currency`): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have a travel currency.
* **Hotel Reservation** (`hotel_reservation`): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have a hotel reservation.
* **Meeting Details** (`meeting_details`): Enter a brief description of your meeting details.
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`): Enter the location of your meeting.
* **Meeting Duration** (`meeting_duration`): Enter the duration of your meeting.
* **Meal Preferences** (`meal_preferences`): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' for each meal preference.
* **Meeting Attendees** (`meeting_attendees`): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' for each meeting attendee.
* **Meeting Agenda** (`meeting_agenda`): Enter a brief description of your meeting agenda.
* **Meeting Notes** (`meeting_notes`): Enter any additional meeting notes.
* **Return Date** (`return_date`): Enter the return date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Return Time** (`return_time`): Enter the return time in 12-hour format (e.g., 01:45 PM).
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`): Enter any additional information about your trip.
* **Language** (`language`): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have a specific language requirement.
* **Currency** (`currency`): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have a currency requirement.
* **Currency Amount** (`currency_amount`): Enter the currency amount.
* **Meeting Start** (`meeting_start`): Enter the meeting start date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Meeting End** (`meeting_end`): Enter the meeting end date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Tips
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Use the provided format for date and time entries.
* Choose the correct options for each field as necessary.
* Provide all required and desired information to ensure accurate travel coordination.
