# Medical Information For Air Travel - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather medical information for air travel to ensure that you receive the necessary care and services during your flight.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the medical conditions section with any existing medical conditions you may have, such as allergies or chronic illnesses.
2. Provide details about any current medications you are taking.
3. List any necessary medical equipment you will be traveling with, such as oxygen tanks or wheelchairs.
4. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the "Special Support" question if you need any extra assistance during the flight.
5. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the "Contact Details" question if you have contact information you would like us to have in case of an emergency.
6. Select your travel dates from the calendar.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Medical Condition**: Describe any medical conditions you have that may affect your travel, such as allergies or chronic illnesses.
* **Medication Details**: List any medications you are currently taking, including the name, dosage, and any specific instructions.
* **Medical Equipment**: List any necessary medical equipment you will be traveling with, such as oxygen tanks or wheelchairs.
* **Special Support**: Answer "Yes" if you need extra assistance during the flight, such as wheelchair services or special meal arrangements.
* **Contact Details**: Answer "Yes" if you have contact information you would like us to have in case of an emergency, such as a next of kin or medical emergency contact.
* **Travel Dates**: Select your travel dates from the calendar. Be sure to enter both departure and return dates.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any information, please ask our staff for assistance.
* This form is a requirement for all passengers with medical conditions or special needs.
