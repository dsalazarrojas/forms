# Dealer Meeting Travel Plan - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Dealer Meeting Travel Plan" form is designed to gather information about travel arrangements for dealers, including meeting dates, travel methods, accommodation options, and traveler details. This form is intended to be completed by the dealer or travel administrator.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and fields to understand what information is required.
2. Enter your travel preferences, including meeting dates, travel methods, and accommodation options.
3. Provide details about your travel arrangements, such as flight information and meal preferences.
4. Ensure you complete all required fields and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Dealer Meeting Travel Plan** (`dealer_meeting_travel_plan_travel_preferences`): This is the main title section of the form.
* **Travel Details** (`dealer_meeting_travel_plan_travel_details`): Enter a brief description of your travel plans.
* **Meeting Dates** (`dealer_meeting_travel_plan_meeting_dates`): Select the dates of the meeting you wish to travel for.
* **Arrival Date** (`dealer_meeting_travel_plan_arrival_date`): Enter the arrival date of your travel.
* **Departure Date** (`dealer_meeting_travel_plan_departure_date`): Enter the departure date of your travel.
* **Travelers** (`dealer_meeting_travel_plan_travelers`): List the names of all travelers accompanying you.
* **Flight Details** (`dealer_meeting_travel_plan_flight_details`): Enter any relevant flight information, such as flight numbers or flight carriers.
* **Travel Method** (`dealer_meeting_travel_plan_travel_method`): Select the method of travel you will be using (e.g., flight, car, bus).
* **Accommodation** (`dealer_meeting_travel_plan_accommodation`): Select the accommodation options you require (e.g., hotel, hostel, Airbnb).
* **Hotel** (`dealer_meeting_travel_plan_hotel`): Enter the name of your hotel accommodation.
* **Room** (`dealer_meeting_travel_plan_room`): Enter the room number or type of accommodation you have booked.
* **Meal Plan** (`dealer_meeting_travel_plan_meal_plan`): Select your meal preferences for the travel period.
* **Meal Preferences** (`dealer_meeting_travel_plan_meal_preferences`): Enter any specific meal preferences or requirements.
* **Travel Cost** (`dealer_meeting_travel_plan_travel_cost`): Enter an estimate of the total travel cost.
* **Travel Budget** (`dealer_meeting_travel_plan_travel_budget`): Enter the allocated travel budget for the trip.
* **Currency** (`dealer_meeting_travel_plan_currency`): Select the currency you will be using for the trip (e.g., USD, EUR, CNY).
* **Currency Symbol** (`dealer_meeting_travel_plan_currency_symbol`): Enter the symbol for the selected currency.
* **Travelers Email** (`dealer_meeting_travel_plan_travelers_email`): Enter the email address of each traveler.
* **Travelers Phone** (`dealer_meeting_travel_plan_travelers_phone`): Enter the phone number of each traveler.
* **Comments** (`dealer_meeting_travel_plan_comments`): Enter any additional comments or notes about your travel arrangements.
* **Attachments** (`dealer_meeting_travel_plan_attachments`): Attach any relevant travel documents, such as receipts or itineraries.
* **Notes** (`dealer_meeting_travel_plan_notes`): Enter any additional notes about your travel plans.
* **Form Completed By** (`dealer_meeting_travel_plan_form_completed`): This field is not editable and displays the name of the person who completed the form.
* **Form Submitted** (`dealer_meeting_travel_plan_date_submitted`): This field is not editable and displays the date the form was submitted.
* **Form Completed** (`dealer_meeting_travel_plan_date_completed`): This field is not editable and displays the date the form was completed.
