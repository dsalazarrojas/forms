# Tour Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tour Booking Form is designed to collect information for booking a tour. This form will guide you through the necessary steps to provide all the required details for a successful tour booking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Review each field for any errors or incomplete fields.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Tour Details**
* **Tour Details** (`tour_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the tour you are interested in booking.

### 2. **Tour Date**
* **Tour Date** (`tour_date`, `date`, optional): Select the date you would like to take the tour. Please use the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### 3. **Start Time**
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, `text`, optional): Enter the start time of the tour (e.g., 09:00).

### 4. **End Time**
* **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the end time of the tour (e.g., 17:00).

### 5. **Guide**
* **Guide** (`guide`, `text`, optional): If you have a preferred guide, please enter their name or ID.

### 6. **Guests**
* **Guests** (`guests`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of guests attending the tour.

### 7. **Phone**
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for any updates.

### 8. **Email**
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you for any updates.

### 9. **Note**
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or requests you may have for your tour.

### 10. **Form Submit**
* **Form Submit** (`form_submit`, `text`, optional): This field is not used for data collection, but please click submit to proceed with your tour booking once you have completed all the required fields.
