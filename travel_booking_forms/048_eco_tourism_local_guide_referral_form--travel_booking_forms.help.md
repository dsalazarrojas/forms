# eco_tourism_local_guide_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The eco_tourism_local_guide_referral_form is used to refer a local guide for eco-tourism travel experiences. This form helps gather information from users who would like to refer a local guide for their travel plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the necessary information for each field. The form is designed to be easy to use and requires minimal information to get started. You can refer a local guide at your convenience by filling out this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details** (User Details, text, optional)
  This field is where you can provide some brief information about yourself. If you're referring a guide for a family member or friend, you can provide their details instead.
* **Contact Details** (Contact Details, text, optional)
  In this field, you can provide your contact information so that the local guide can reach out to you with more information or questions about your referral.
* **Travel Booking** (Travel Booking, text, optional)
  Here, you can provide any travel booking information you might have, such as travel dates or accommodation details.
* **Travel Preferences** (Travel Preferences, text, optional)
  This field is for you to describe any specific travel preferences you might have, such as dietary requirements or mobility needs.
* **Travel Booking Form** (Travel Booking Form, text, optional)
  This field is meant to be filled in by the local guide, not you. It's for them to use for any travel booking needs they might have.
* **Submit** (Submit, text, optional)
  Once you've completed all the fields, simply click "Submit" to refer a local guide. They will be in touch with you to discuss further details.
