# Sales Form Chirag - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect sales information and travel-related data for the Chirag sales team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the sales_form_chirag field with the relevant information.
2. Enter the travel_booking_form field with the travel booking information.
3. Select the travel_details options that apply to your travel booking.
4. Enter the travel_date field with the relevant date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### *Sales Form Chirag* (`sales_form_chirag`, `text`, not required)
This is the main sales form field. Enter the required sales information here.

### *Travel Booking Form* (`travel_booking_form`, `text`, not required)
Enter the travel booking information here.

### *Travel Details* (`travel_details`, `select_multiple`, not required)
Select all the details that apply to your travel booking:

* Yes
* No

### *Travel Date* (`travel_date`, `date`, not required)
Enter the travel date.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all fields for a complete submission.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact the Chirag sales team for clarification.
