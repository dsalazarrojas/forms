# Trip Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential details about employee travel requests, enabling the company to efficiently track and approve travel plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your employee ID in the "Employee ID" field.
2. Fill in the "Travel Date" field with the dates of your trip.
3. Enter the duration of your trip in the "Travel Duration" field.
4. Provide the destination of your trip in the "Destination" field.
5. State the reason for your travel in the "Reason for Travel" field.
6. If you have flight information, enter it in the "Flight Info" field.
7. Finally, fill in any other relevant details about your travel request in the "Travel Request Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required): This field is for your employee ID number. Please enter the ID number issued to you by the company.
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, date, required): This field is for the dates of your trip. Please enter the start and end dates of your travel period.
* **Travel Time** (`travel_time`, time, required): This field is for the travel duration in hours. Please enter the total number of hours you will be traveling.
* **Travel Duration** (`travel_duration`, text, required): This field is for any additional details about the duration of your trip. Please enter any special circumstances that may affect your travel.
* **Destination** (`destination`, text, required): This field is for the location or address of your destination. Please enter the city, state, or country of your trip.
* **Reason for Travel** (`reason_for_travel`, text, required): This field is for the purpose of your trip. Please enter a brief description of your travel purpose.
* **Flight Info** (`flight_info`, text, required): This field is for any additional flight information. Please enter the flight number, airline, or any other relevant flight details.
* **Travel Request Details** (`travel_request_details`, text, optional): This field is for any additional information about your travel request. Please enter any specific requirements or requests you may have for your trip.

## Tips
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you have filled in all required fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the travel department for assistance.
