# Sustainable Travel Hub Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sustainable Travel Hub Booking Form is a simple and user-friendly form designed to collect essential details from users for booking a stay at a sustainable travel destination. This form is meant to accommodate both individual and group bookings for leisure or business purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your **Form Header**: Leave this field blank as it is not required.
2. Provide **Guest Details**: Enter the name(s) of the guest(s) in this field.
3. Choose **Stay Dates**: Enter your arrival and departure dates using the date format (dd/mm/yyyy).
4. Select **Stay Duration**: Enter the total number of days you plan to stay.
5. Select **Accommodation Type**: Choose the type of accommodation that suits your needs:
	* Single occupancy: Ideal for solo travelers.
	* Double Occupancy: Suitable for two people.
	* Family Occupancy: Best for families or groups of three or more.
6. Choose **Sustainability Preferences**: Select any of the sustainability preferences that apply to you:
	* Eco-friendly: Environmentally conscious travelers.
	* Low Carbon Footprint: Travelers who want to minimize their carbon footprint.
	* No preferences: No specific sustainability preference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Details (Guest Details)** (`Guest Details`, `text`, required): Enter the name(s) of the guest(s).
* **Stay Dates (Stay Dates)** (`date`, required): Enter your arrival and departure dates using the date format (dd/mm/yyyy).
* **Stay Duration (Stay Duration)** (`number`, required): Enter the total number of days you plan to stay.
* **Accommodation Type (Accommodation Type)** (`select_multiple`, required): Choose the type of accommodation that suits your needs (Single occupancy, Double Occupancy, Family Occupancy).
* **Sustainability Preferences (Sustainability Preferences)** (`select_multiple`, required): Select any of the sustainability preferences that apply to you (Eco-friendly, Low Carbon Footprint, No preferences).
