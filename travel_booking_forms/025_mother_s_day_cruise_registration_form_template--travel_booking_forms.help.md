# Mother's Day Cruise Registration Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering for the Mother's Day Cruise event. It's essential to fill it out accurately to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions thoroughly, ensuring that the provided information is up-to-date and accurate.
2. If you're unsure about any of the fields, refer to the explanations provided below for further guidance.
3. Once completed, review your submission for correctness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`name`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: `false`): Please enter your email address. This will be used to communicate with you about the cruise event.
* **Confirm Email** (`email_confirmation`, `email`, required: `false`): Confirm your email address to ensure accuracy.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Birthdate** (`birthdate`, `date`, required: `false`): Select your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required: `true`): Select your gender to help us better understand your needs and preferences.
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, `date`, required: `false`): Select the date you plan on traveling, if applicable.
* **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, `time`, required: `false`): Enter your expected arrival time.
* **Departure Date** (`departure_date`, `date`, required: `false`): Select the date you plan on departing, if applicable.
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the number of people traveling with you.
