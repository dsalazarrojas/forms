# Hotel Booking Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for customers to book a hotel stay, providing essential details for a smooth and efficient booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Travel Details" section with your travel dates and any other relevant information.
2. In the "Customer Info" section, provide your personal details such as name, address, and contact information.
3. In the "Travel Dates" section, enter your arrival and departure dates.
4. Choose your preferred "Room Type" from the options provided (e.g. Hotel, Inn, Motel, Resort).
5. Enter your "Room Number" (if applicable).
6. In the "Guest Info" section, provide any additional guest information if applicable.
7. Select your preferred "Payment Method" from the options provided (e.g. Credit Card, Bank Transfer, Check).
8. Enter your "Payment Info" for the selected payment method.
9. Add any "Customer Note" for the hotel staff if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Details** (`travel_details`, `text`, not required): This section is for providing travel dates and other travel-related details.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, `text`, not required): This section is for providing personal details such as name, address, and contact information.
* **Travel Dates** (`travel_dates`, `text`, not required): This section is for entering your arrival and departure dates.
* **Room Type** (`room_type`, `select_multiple`, not required): This is a selection field for choosing your preferred room type (e.g. Hotel, Inn, Motel, Resort).
* **Room Number** (`room_number`, `number`, not required): This field is for entering your room number (if applicable).
* **Guest Info** (`guest_info`, `text`, not required): This section is for providing any additional guest information (if applicable).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, not required): Select your preferred payment method (e.g. Credit Card, Bank Transfer, Check).
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, `text`, not required): This field is for entering your payment information (if applicable).
* **Customer Note** (`customer_note`, `note`, not required): This field is for leaving any notes or comments for the hotel staff.
