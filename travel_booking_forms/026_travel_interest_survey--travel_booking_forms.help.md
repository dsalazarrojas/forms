# Travel Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your travel interests and preferences. Please answer the questions honestly to help us provide you with better travel recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each question carefully and answer according to your travel habits and preferences.
- Select the options that best fit your travel interests and habits.
- Make sure to fill out all the required fields (indicated by *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How often do you travel**:
	+ How often do you travel? (Required)
	+ Choose a frequency that best describes your travel habits.
* **What destination do you like to travel to**:
	+ Select all destinations that you have traveled to or would like to travel to. (Required)
	+ Examples: Beaches, cities, mountains, etc.
* **What type of travel do you enjoy most**:
	+ Choose the type of travel that best describes your preferences.
	+ Examples: Adventure, relaxation, culture, history, etc.
* **How much travel experience do you have**:
	+ If you have traveled before, select the number of years you have been traveling.
	+ If you are a beginner, select 0.
* **How much are you willing to spend on a trip**:
	+ If you have a budget for your trip, enter the maximum amount you are willing to spend.
* **What style of travel do you prefer**:
	+ Select all travel styles that you enjoy most. (Required)
	+ Examples: Solo, group, family, luxury, budget, etc.
* **How long are you willing to travel**:
	+ Select all travel lengths that you are comfortable with. (Optional)
	+ Examples: Short (less than 5 days), medium (5-10 days), long (more than 10 days)
* **What type of trip do you like**:
	+ Select all types of trips that you enjoy. (Optional)
	+ Examples: Beach vacation, city break, adventure tour, etc.
* **What style of trip do you like**:
	+ Select all travel styles that you prefer. (Optional)
	+ Examples: Active, cultural, foodie, etc.
* **What length of trip do you like**:
	+ Select all travel lengths that you enjoy. (Optional)
	+ Examples: Short (less than 5 days), medium (5-10 days), long (more than 10 days)
