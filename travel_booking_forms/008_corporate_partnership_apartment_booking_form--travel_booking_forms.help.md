# Corporate Partnership Apartment Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a corporate partnership apartment. It is intended to gather necessary information from the employee to facilitate the booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read this guide carefully before starting to fill out the form.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately. If a field is marked as required, it's essential to fill it out.
- For each section, fill in the information as accurately and thoroughly as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Corporate Partnership Apartment Booking Form**: This is the title of the form, and it's not a field that needs to be filled out. It's just a heading to guide you through the form.
- **First Name**: This field is for your first name. Please fill it in with your first name.
- **Last Name**: This field is for your last name. Please fill it in with your last name.
- **Email**: This field is for your email address. Please fill it in with the email address that you have on record. It's used to communicate with you about your booking.
- **Phone**: This field is for your phone number. Please fill it in with your phone number.
- **Company**: This field is for the name of your company. Please fill it in with the name of your company.
- **Arrival Date**: This field is for the date you plan to arrive at the apartment. Please select the date from the calendar provided.
- **Departure Date**: This field is for the date you plan to leave the apartment. Please select the date from the calendar provided.
- **Purpose of Visit**: This field is to select the purpose of your visit. Please select one of the options provided: Corporate Event/Meeting, Team Building/Training, or Personal/Family Visit.
- **Number of Guests**: This field is for how many guests you are bringing with you. Please fill it in with the number of guests.
- **Special Requests**: This field is for any special requests you might have, such as a specific type of accommodation or amenities. Please fill it in with any relevant details.

## Tips
- Double-check all the fields to ensure they are accurate and complete before submission.
- If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the relevant department for assistance.
