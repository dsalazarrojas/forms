# travel_questionnaire_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The travel questionnaire form is used to collect travel-related information from users, such as travel dates, destinations, agencies, and contact details. This form is likely used for business or corporate travel purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the travel details section, which includes the departure date, flight type, and travel agency.
2. Select your travel agency from the options provided.
3. Provide the flight number and any additional travel documents required.
4. Enter the contact person's name and number.
5. Enter the contact person's email.
6. Select the contact number and email.
7. Repeat steps 3-6 for any additional travel agencies or contact persons.
8. Finally, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Details** (`travel_details`): Enter the date of your trip, including the departure date.
* **Trip Type** (`trip_type`): Select the type of trip you are taking (outbound, inbound, return, or others).
* **Departure Date** (`departure_date`): Enter the departure date of your trip.
* **Travel Agency** (`travel_agency`): Select your travel agency from the provided options.
* **Flight Number** (`flight_number`): Enter your flight number.
* **Travel Document** (`travel_document`): Enter any additional travel documents required.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): Enter the contact number of the contact person.
* **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`): Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Travel Agency 2** (`travel_agency_2`): Select any additional travel agencies from the provided options.
* **Travel Details 2** (`travel_details_2`): Enter any additional travel details.
* **Contact Person 2** (`contact_person_2`): Enter the name of the second contact person.
* **Contact Number 2** (`contact_number_2`): Enter the contact number of the second contact person.
* **Contact E-mail 2** (`contact_email_2`): Enter the email of the second contact person.
* **Travel Agency 3** (`travel_agency_3`): Select any additional travel agencies from the provided options.
* **Travel Details 3** (`travel_details_3`): Enter any additional travel details.
* **Contact Person 3** (`contact_person_3`): Enter the name of the third contact person.
* **Contact E-mail 3** (`contact_email_3`): Enter the email of the third contact person.
* **Contact Number 3** (`contact_number_3`): Enter the contact number of the third contact person.
* **Contact Person 4** (`contact_person_4`): Enter the name of the fourth contact person.
* **Contact Person 5** (`contact_person_5`): Enter the name of the fifth contact person.
* **Contact Number 4** (`contact_number_4`): Enter the contact number of the fourth contact person.
* **Contact E-mail 3** (`contact_email_3`): Enter the email of the third contact person.

Note: Some fields seem to be redundant, please be aware that only one contact person and number should be entered per agency or travel.
