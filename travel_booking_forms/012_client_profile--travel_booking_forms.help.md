# Client Profile - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help us better understand your travel preferences and requirements for planning your perfect trip. It's essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure that we can tailor our services to your needs and provide you with a personalized travel experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please go through the form carefully and fill out each section accurately.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for your preferences and requirements.
3. If you have any additional preferences or special requests, use the "Additional Preferences or Special Requests" field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your passport or government-issued ID.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your passport or government-issued ID.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Please enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Please enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Home Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Please enter your home address including street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Passport Number** (`passport_number`, `text`, optional): If you have a passport, please enter the number for international travel purposes.
* **Passport Expiry Date** (`passport_expiry`, `date`, optional): If you have a passport, please enter the expiry date.
* **Frequent Flyer Numbers** (`frequent_flyer_numbers`, `text`, optional): If you have frequent flyer numbers, please enter them for airline and membership details.
* **Preferred Airlines** (`preferred_airlines`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select all your preferred airlines for travel purposes.
* **Seat Preference** (`seat_preference`, `select_one`, optional): Please select your preferred seat type for flights.
* **Cabin Class Preference** (`cabin_preference`, `select_one`, required): Please select your preferred cabin class for flights.
* **Hotel Loyalty Program Memberships** (`hotel_loyalty_programs`, `text`, optional): If you have hotel loyalty program memberships, please enter them for hotel details.
* **Preferred Hotel Chains** (`preferred_hotel_chains`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select all your preferred hotel chains for stays.
* **Room Type Preference** (`room_preference`, `select_one`, optional): Please select your preferred room type for hotel stays.
* **Do You Plan to Rent Cars?** (`rental_car_license`, `select_one`, optional): Please select whether you plan to rent cars on your trip.
* **Rental Car Size Preference** (`rental_car_preference`, `select_one`, optional): Please select your preferred rental car size.
* **Have You Been on a Cruise Before?** (`cruise_experience`, `select_one`, optional): Please select whether you have been on a cruise before.
* **Preferred Cruise Lines** (`preferred_cruise_lines`, `text`, optional): If you have preferences for cruise lines, please enter them.
* **Preferred Cruise Destinations** (`cruise_destination_preference`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select all your preferred cruise destinations.
* **Who Do You Typically Travel With?** (`travel_companions`, `select_one`, required): Please select who you typically travel with.
* **Travel Style** (`travel_style`, `select_one`, required): Please select your travel style for this trip.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Preferences** (`dietary_restrictions`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select all your dietary restrictions or preferences.
* **Accessibility Requirements** (`accessibility_needs`, `text`, optional): If you have any accessibility requirements, please enter them.
* **Do You Want Travel Insurance?** (`travel_insurance`, `select_one`, optional): Please select whether you want travel insurance for your trip.
* **Typical Budget Per Trip** (`budget_per_trip`, `select_one`, required): Please select your typical budget per trip excluding flights.
* **Dream Vacation Destination** (`dream_destination`, `text`, optional): If you have a dream vacation destination, please enter it.
* **Travel Goals for This Year** (`travel_goals`, `text`, optional): If you have travel goals for this year, please enter them.
* **Additional Preferences or Special Requests** (`additional_preferences`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional preferences or special requests, please enter them.

## Tips

* Please make sure to double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us for assistance.
* We appreciate your honesty and accuracy in filling out this form to ensure the best possible travel experience for you.
