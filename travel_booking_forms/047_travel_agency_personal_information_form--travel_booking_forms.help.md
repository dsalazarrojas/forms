# Travel Agency Personal Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather essential information from clients of a travel agency to ensure smooth and efficient operations. It collects personal and agency-specific details that the agency needs for various purposes, including but not limited to, travel planning, documentation, and compliance requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your **Client Name** in the `Client Name` field. This is the primary contact name for travel agency communication.
2. Enter your **Email Address** in the `Client Email` field. This should be a valid email address where you can be reached for travel-related inquiries and updates.
3. Provide your **Phone Number** in the `Client Phone` field. This will help the travel agency contact you for any last-minute travel information or updates.
4. Enter your **Birth Date** in the `Birthdate` field. This is necessary for age-related travel requirements and documentation.
5. Input your **Passport Number** in the `Passport Number` field. This is a required document for international travel.
6. Select your **Nationality** from the dropdown menu under `Nationality`. This helps the travel agency tailor travel requirements and documentation.
7. Choose your **Gender** from the dropdown menu under `Gender`. This is used for travel requirements and documentation.
8. Select the **Country of Residence** from the dropdown menu under `Country of Residence`. This helps the travel agency assess your residency requirements for international travel.
9. Input your **Travel Agency Name**, if you are submitting on behalf of a travel agency, in the `Travel Agency Name` field.
10. Enter your travel agency's **Phone Number** in the `Travel Agency Phone` field.
11. Provide your travel agency's **Email Address** in the `Travel Agency Email` field.
12. Input your travel agency's **License Number** in the `Travel Agency License` field.
13. Enter your travel agency's **Physical Location** in the `Travel Agency Physical Location` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Name** (text): Enter your personal name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
*   **Client Email** (email): Enter a valid email address where you'd like to receive travel updates and information.
*   **Client Phone** (text): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted for travel-related updates.
*   **Birthdate** (date): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
*   **Passport Number** (text): Enter your passport number as issued by the relevant authorities.
*   **Nationality** (select_one): Select your country of origin from the provided list.
*   **Gender** (select_one): Choose your gender from the options provided.
*   **Country of Residence** (select_one): Select the country where you currently reside from the provided list.
*   **Travel Agency Name** (text): Enter your travel agency name if you are submitting on behalf of an agency.
*   **Travel Agency Phone** (text): Enter your travel agency's phone number for communication.
*   **Travel Agency Email** (email): Enter your travel agency's email address for updates and information.
*   **Travel Agency License** (text): Enter your travel agency's license number as issued by the relevant authorities.
*   **Travel Agency Physical Location** (text): Enter your travel agency's physical location.

## Tips

*   Please fill the form accurately and truthfully to avoid any processing issues or errors.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please contact your travel agency representative for assistance.
*   This form is for internal use and should not be submitted for any other purpose outside the agency's operations.
*   Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and authority to provide this information on behalf of a travel agency.
