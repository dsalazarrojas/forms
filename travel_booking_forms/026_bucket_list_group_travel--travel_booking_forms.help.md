# Bucket List Group Travel - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect crucial details from group travelers for a successful and stress-free group trip.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the **Trip or Group Name** field with the name of your group trip.
2. Enter the **Trip Start Date** and **Trip End Date** for the travel period.
3. Provide your **Traveler Full Name**, **Email Address**, and **Phone Number** to ensure we can contact you effectively.
4. If you are traveling internationally, enter your **Passport Number** and **Passport Expiry Date**.
5. Enter your **Date of Birth** for identity verification purposes.
6. Select your **Nationality** for travel documentation requirements.
7. If applicable, choose your **Preferred Airline** and **Seat Preference** for travel comfort.
8. Mention any **Dietary Requirements** or **Medical Conditions** for special needs consideration.
9. Specify your **Amount Due per Person** for payment processing.
10. Confirm your **Payment Status** to avoid any payment delays.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trip or Group Name (text, required)**: Enter the name of your group trip for identification purposes.
* **Trip Start Date (date, required)**: Enter the start date of your trip for itinerary planning.
* **Trip End Date (date, required)**: Enter the end date of your trip for accurate travel duration.
* **Traveler Full Name (text, required)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your passport.
* **Email Address (email, required)**: Enter a valid email address for communication and updates.
* **Phone Number (text, required)**: Enter your contact phone number for immediate contact.
* **Passport Number (text, optional)**: Enter your passport number if you are traveling internationally.
* **Passport Expiry Date (date, optional)**: Enter your passport expiry date if you are traveling internationally.
* **Date of Birth (date, required)**: Enter your date of birth for identity verification purposes.
* **Nationality (text, optional)**: Select your nationality for travel documentation requirements.
* **Preferred Airline (text, optional)**: Choose your preferred airline for travel comfort.
* **Seat Preference (select_one, optional)**: Choose your seat preference (Window, Middle, Aisle, or No Preference).
* **Meal Preference (text, optional)**: Mention any dietary requirements or allergies.
* **Special Requests (text, optional)**: Specify any special requests (wheelchair, extra legroom, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Name (text, required)**: Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (text, required)**: Enter the contact phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Travel Insurance Purchased (select_one, required)**: Confirm whether you have travel insurance purchased.
* **Any Medical Conditions or Medications (text, optional)**: Mention any medical conditions or medications.
* **Amount Due per Person (number, required)**: Enter the amount due per person for payment processing.
* **Payment Status (select_one, required)**: Confirm your payment status (Deposit Paid, Fully Paid, Payment Plan, or Not Yet Paid).

## Tips

- Please fill in all required fields to avoid any potential issues with your group travel booking.
- Ensure that you enter accurate and correct information to guarantee smooth travel execution.
- If you have any doubts or questions, contact your group travel organizer for assistance.
- Review the form carefully before submission to avoid any errors.
