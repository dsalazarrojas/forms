# Cruise Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from passengers after a cruise, enabling the travel agency or cruise line to gather feedback, comments, or contact information for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information, which includes:
	* Travel Agency
	* Travel Dates
	* Passenger Name
	* Contact Email
	* Contact Phone
2. You can leave the optional fields blank or fill them out if relevant:
	* Passenger Contact Information
	* Ship Name
	* Cabin Number
	* Travel Type

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Agency** (1. Travel Agency, text, required/optional): Please enter the name of the travel agency or cruise line that organized your cruise.
* **Travel Dates** (2. Travel Dates, date, required/optional): Enter the dates of your travel in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Passenger Name** (3. Passenger Name, text, required/optional): Please enter your full name as it appears on your passport or government-issued ID.
* **Passenger Contact Information** (4. Passenger Contact Information, text, required/optional): If you have any special contact information, such as a hotel or address, please enter it here.
* **Ship Name** (5. Ship Name, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the ship you traveled on.
* **Cabin Number** (6. Cabin Number, text, required/optional): If you have a cabin number, please enter it here.
* **Travel Type** (7. Travel Type, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of travel you experienced:
	+ Domestic
	+ International
	+ Option 3
	+ Option 4
* **Comments** (8. Comments, text, required/optional): Share any comments or feedback you have about your experience.
* **Contact Email** (9. Contact Email, email, required): Enter your contact email address so we can follow up with you.
* **Contact Phone** (10. Contact Phone, text, required): Enter your contact phone number so we can contact you if needed.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure accurate submission.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the travel agency or cruise line directly.
* If you have any feedback or comments, please be as detailed as possible to help us improve our services.
