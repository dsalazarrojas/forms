# Walt Disney World Vacation Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form gathers essential information from users to assist travel agents in planning a magical vacation experience at Walt Disney World.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your arrival date and preferred hotel.
2. Enter the number of people in your travel party.
3. Select the type of travel experience you are looking for (e.g., Family, Couple, Solo, Group).
4. Enter your arrival time.
5. Provide details on the length of your stay.
6. Select your preferred hotel from the list.
7. Enter any special requests you may have.
8. Provide your email address and phone number.
9. Confirm your email and phone number.
10. Enter any additional information you would like to share with the travel agent.
11. Select your travel agency.
12. Enter the name of your travel agent.
13. Confirm your travel agency.
14. Select your travel currency.
15. Enter any additional special requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Arrival Date** (Page 1): The date of your arrival to Walt Disney World.
* **Travel Party** (Page 1): The number of people in your travel party (e.g., Family, Couple, Solo, Group).
* **Travel Purpose** (Page 2): The type of travel experience you are looking for (e.g., Family, Couple, Solo, Group).
* **Arrival Time** (Page 2): The time of your arrival at Walt Disney World.
* **Travel Length** (Page 2): The length of your stay at Walt Disney World.
* **Preferred Hotel** (Page 3): The hotel you would prefer to stay in during your visit.
* **Special Request** (Page 3): Any special requests you may have for your stay.
* **Email** (Page 4): Your email address for communication with the travel agent.
* **Phone** (Page 4): Your phone number for communication with the travel agent.
* **Confirm Email** (Page 4): Confirm your email address.
* **Confirm Phone** (Page 4): Confirm your phone number.
* **Additional Information** (Page 5): Any additional details you would like to share with the travel agent.
* **Travel Agent** (Page 5): The travel agency you are planning with.
* **Travel Agent Name** (Page 5): The name of your travel agent.
* **Confirm Travel Agent** (Page 5): Confirm the travel agency you are planning with.
* **Travel Currency** (Page 6): The currency of your travel (e.g., USD, Euro, Other).
* **Special Request 2** (Page 6): Any additional special requests you may have.
* **Special Request 3** (Page 7): Any additional special requests you may have.
* **Travel Type** (Page 7): The type of travel experience you are looking for (e.g., Domestic, International, National).
* **Travel Destination** (Page 7): The destination of your travel (e.g., Florida, California, Other).
* **Additional Travelers** (Page 8): The number of additional travelers in your party.
* **Arrival Airport** (Page 8): The airport of your arrival.
* **Travel Currency 2** (Page 8): The currency of your travel (e.g., USD, Euro, Other).
* **Travel Agency** (Page 9): The travel agency you are planning with.
* **Travel Agent Name** (Page 9): The name of your travel agent.
* **Confirm Travel Agent** (Page 9): Confirm the travel agency you are planning with.
* **Special Request 4** (Page 10): Any additional special requests you may have.
* **Special Request 5** (Page 10): Any additional special requests you may have.

Note: The fields marked as "confirm" are meant to confirm the accuracy of the previous field. 

Please ensure that you confirm the accuracy of your contact information to avoid any miscommunication with the travel agent.
