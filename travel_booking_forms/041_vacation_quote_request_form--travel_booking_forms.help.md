# Vacation Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information for a customized vacation package quote.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with your personal details and preferences. You can choose from the pre-defined options for vacation type, destination, and transportation requests. If you have any special requests, such as room preferences or additional services, please provide them in the "Special Requests" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer First Name** (`customer_first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Customer Last Name** (`customer_last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be contacted with updates and quotes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted with updates and quotes.
* **Vacation Type** (`vacation_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of vacation you are interested in (Beach, Mountain, City, or Other).
* **Destination** (`destination`, text, optional): Enter the name of the destination(s) you are interested in.
* **Departure Date** (`departure_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to depart on (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Return Date** (`return_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to return on (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, number, optional): Enter the number of people in your travel party.
* **Room Preferences** (`room_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the type of room you prefer (Single Room, Double Room, Triple Room).
* **Transportation Requests** (`transportation_requests`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to travel (Car Rental, Taxi, Train, Other).
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, note, optional): Enter any additional requests or services you require (e.g. wheelchair accessibility, extra bedding, etc.).
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, optional): Enter your current address for billing and communication purposes.
* **Customer City** (`customer_city`, text, optional): Enter the name of the city where you reside.
* **Customer State** (`customer_state`, text, optional): Enter the name of the state where you reside.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out to ensure accurate and complete quote.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us directly.
* We will be in touch with you within 24-48 hours to provide you with a customized quote.
