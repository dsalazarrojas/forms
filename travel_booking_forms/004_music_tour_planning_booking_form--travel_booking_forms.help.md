# Music Tour Planning Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music Tour Planning Booking Form is designed to help travel agencies or event planners collect information from potential clients who want to book a music tour. This form gathers essential details about the tour, including dates, locations, budget, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the **Single/Multiple** option from the **Dates** field to indicate whether the tour is for one performance or multiple performances.
2. Select **USA, Europe, or Asia** from the **Locations** field to choose the tour locations.
3. For the **Budget Amount** field, enter the total budget for the tour.
4. Choose the **USD or EUR** option from the **Budget Currency** field to select the currency of the budget amount.
5. Enter your **Contact Information**, such as your name and contact number, in the **Contact Info** field.
6. Enter your **Email** address and **Phone** number in the designated fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dates** (Select One): 
  - Choose whether the tour is for one performance (Single) or multiple performances (Multiple).
* **Locations** (Select Multiple): 
  - Select the locations for the tour, such as **USA, Europe, or Asia**.
* **Requirements** (Number): 
  - Enter any specific requirements for the tour, such as equipment or personnel needs.
* **Budget Amount** (Number): 
  - Enter the total budget for the tour.
* **Budget Currency** (Select One): 
  - Choose the currency for the budget amount, such as **USD** or **EUR**.
* **Contact Info** (Text): 
  - Enter your name, organization, and contact number for communication purposes.
* **Email** (Email): 
  - Enter your email address for further communication.
* **Phone** (Text): 
  - Enter your phone number for easy contact and coordination.
