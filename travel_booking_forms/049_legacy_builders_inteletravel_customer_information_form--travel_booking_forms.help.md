# Legacy Builders InteleTravel Customer Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legacy Builders InteleTravel Customer Information Form is designed to collect customer information for travel booking purposes. This form will be used to gather relevant details about the customer, which will help in providing a tailored travel experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your customer name in the **Customer Name** field.
2. Enter your contact phone number in the **Phone Number** field.
3. Enter your email address in the **Email Address** field.
4. Select your **Travel Destination** from the provided options.
5. Enter your travel dates in the **Travel Dates** field.
6. Select your **Special Requests** from the provided options.
7. Enter the duration of your travel in the **Travel Duration** field.
8. Select your preferred **Return Trip Date** from the provided options (if applicable).
9. Enter your customer comment or message in the **Customer Comment** field (if applicable).
10. Sign the form with your signature in the **Customer Signature** field.
11. Select your **Travel Type** from the provided options (if applicable).
12. Select your travel **Activity** from the provided options (if applicable).
13. Select your **Travel Duration Type** from the provided options.
14. Select the units for your travel duration in the **Travel Duration Units** field.
15. Enter the value of your travel duration in the **Travel Duration Value** field.
16. Select your **Customer Relationship** from the provided options (if applicable).
17. Select your **Customer Role** from the provided options.
18. Select your **Customer Title** from the provided options.
19. Enter your customer name in the **Customer Name 2** field (if applicable).
20. Enter your email address in the **Email Address 2** field (if applicable).
21. Enter your phone number in the **Phone Number 2** field (if applicable).
22. Select your **Travel Destination 2** from the provided options (if applicable).
23. Enter the duration of your travel in the **Travel Duration 2** field (if applicable).
24. Select your preferred **Return Trip Date 2** from the provided options (if applicable).
25. Enter your customer comment or message in the **Customer Comment 2** field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (text, required): Enter your customer name as it appears on official documents.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your contact phone number for communication purposes.
* **Email Address** (email, optional): Enter your email address for receiving travel updates.
* **Travel Destination** (select_one, optional): Select the destination of your travel.
* **Travel Dates** (date, required): Enter your travel dates in the format `DD/MM/YYYY`.
* **Special Requests** (select_multiple, optional): Select any special requests for your travel.
* **Travel Duration** (number, required): Enter the duration of your travel in days.
* **Return Trip Date** (date, optional): Select your preferred return trip date.
* **Customer Comment** (note, optional): Enter a customer comment or message for travel purposes.
* **Customer Signature** (select_one, required): Sign the form with your signature.
* **Travel Type** (select_one, optional): Select the type of travel.
* **Travel Activity** (select_multiple, optional): Select any travel activities.
* **Travel Duration Type** (select_one, required): Select the type of travel duration.
* **Travel Duration Units** (select_one, required): Select the units for your travel duration.
* **Travel Duration Value** (number, required): Enter the value of your travel duration.
* **Customer Relationship** (select_one, optional): Select your customer relationship.
* **Customer Role** (select_one, required): Select your customer role.
* **Customer Title** (select_multiple, required): Select your customer title.
