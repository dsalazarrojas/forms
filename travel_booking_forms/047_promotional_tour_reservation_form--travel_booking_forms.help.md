# promotional_tour_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for promotional tour reservations, enabling efficient organization and coordination of travel plans.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your **First Name** in the text field provided.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the text field.
3. Provide your **Email** address in the format of `email@domain.com`.
4. Enter your **Phone** number for contact purposes.
5. Select a **Promotional Code** from the dropdown menu. Options include `abc123`, `def456`, and `ghi789`.
6. Choose a specific **Travel Dates** by entering the date in the format of `YYYY-MM-DD`.
7. Select the **Travel Type** from the dropdown menu. Options include `Single Traveler` and `Group Traveler`.
8. Enter the **Travel Date** in the format of `YYYY-MM-DD`.
9. Choose the **Travel Time** by entering the time of day.
10. Enter the **Number of Guests**.
11. Provide any **Special Requests** in the text field.
12. Select **Terms of Service** from the dropdown menu. Options include `Yes` and `No`.
13. Enter the **Assigned Tool**.
14. Enter the **Assigned User**.
15. Enter the **Category**.
16. Enter the **Output File**.
17. Enter the **Form ID**.
18. Enter the **Form Title**.
19. Enter the **Category 2**.
20. Enter the **Assigned Tool 2**.
21. Enter the **Assigned User 2**.
22. Enter the **Form Id 2**.
23. Enter the **Form Title 2**.
24. Enter the **Category 3**.
25. Enter the **Assigned Tool 3**.
