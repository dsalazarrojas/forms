# hajj_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from Hajj pilgrims for travel booking purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Enroller Information page with your details.
2. Move on to the Contact Information page and provide your contact details.
3. Finally, fill out the Attendance Details page with any additional information required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Enroller Information
* **Enroller Information** (`enroller_information`, `text`, not required): Please fill out this field with your name or title.
* **Enroller Information** (`enroller_information`, `text`, not required): Please fill out this field with any additional information about yourself.
### Contact Information
* **contact_information** (`contact_information`, `text`, not required): Please fill out this field with your contact details.
### Attendance Details
* **attendance_details** (`attendance_details`, `text`, not required): Please provide any additional details about your attendance or travel plans.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately and completely.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure you have all the necessary information.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask the administrator or customer support for clarification.
* The form is designed to be user-friendly and easy to navigate.
