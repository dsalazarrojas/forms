# e_cigarette_carry_on_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from travelers regarding their e-cigarette possession and usage during their trip. This data is likely used by airlines, transportation services, or travel agencies to ensure compliance with relevant laws and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, please follow these steps:
	* Fill out your traveler information on page 1.
	* Provide your departure date and time on page 2.
	* Enter any relevant information about your e-cigarette in page 3.
	* Sign and date the form on page 5.
	* Optionally, provide a declaration on page 6 if necessary.
	* Optionally, list your travel company or airline on page 7 if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Traveler Information (Page 1, <name>, <type>: text)**: Provide your name and other identifying information as required.
* **Departure Date (Page 2, <name>: travel_date, <type>: date)**: Enter the date of your departure.
* **Departure Time (Page 2, <name>: travel_time, <type>: time)**: Enter the time of your departure.
* **E Cigarette Information (Page 3, <name>: e_cigarette_info, <type>: note)**: Describe any e-cigarettes you plan to bring on your trip, including brand, model, and any other relevant details.
* **Signature (Page 5, <name>: signature_1, <type>: text)**: Sign your name, date, and provide a contact method to verify your declaration.
* **Declaration (Page 6, <name>: declaration, <type>: text)**: Provide a declaration of your e-cigarette possession and usage if required by your travel provider.
* **Travel Company (Page 7, <name>: travel_company, <type>: text)**: List your travel company or airline if required.

## Tips
- Please ensure you understand the laws and regulations regarding e-cigarette possession and usage in your destination.
- If you're unsure about any information, consult your travel provider or contact your airline directly.
- This form is for travel purposes only, and its completion is mandatory for travel clearance.
