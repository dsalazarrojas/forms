## Purpose
The travel request form is used to collect information about travel requests, including dates, times, travel types, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by selecting the travel date and departure time.
2. Choose the travel type (Yes/No).
3. Select the travel agent (Yes/No).
4. Enter the client's name, email, and travel agency details.
5. Provide the client's phone number and address.
6. Choose the reason for travel (Yes/No).
7. Enter travel details and requests.
8. Add any additional notes or comments.
9. Select the status of the travel request (Active/Inactive).
10. Optionally, enter the assigned user and any additional details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of your travel request.
* **Departure Time** (`departure_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of your departure.
* **Return Time** (`return_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of your return.
* **Travel Type** (`travel_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select either 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your travel type.
* **Travel Type Label** (`travel_type_label`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of your travel type.
* **Travel Agent** (`travel_agent`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have a travel agent.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the client's name.
* **Email** (`client_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the client's email address.
* **Travel Agency** (`travel_agency`, `text`, required: false): Enter the travel agency's details.
* **Phone** (`travel_agent_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the travel agent's phone number.
* **Reason for Travel** (`reason_for_travel`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select either 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the reason for travel.
* **Travel Details** (`travel_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional travel details.
* **Travel Request** (`travel_request`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional travel requests.
* **Client Address** (`client_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the client's address.
* **Travel Agent Contact** (`travel_agent_contact`, `text`, required: false): Enter the travel agent's contact details.
* **Notes** (`travel_note`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Status** (`travel_request_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select between 'Active' and 'Inactive' to indicate the status of the travel request.
* **Status Label** (`travel_request_status_label`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the status.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, `text`, required: false): Enter the assigned user's details.
* **Date** (`travel_request_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the travel request.
* **Time** (`travel_request_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the travel request.
* **Type** (`travel_request_type`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the type of travel request.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Use the travel type label to provide additional context for your travel type.
* If you have a travel agent, select 'Yes' to indicate that you have one.
* If you have additional notes or comments, add them in the 'Notes' field.
* If you have an assigned user, enter their details in the 'Assigned User' field.
* Review your form before submitting to ensure all fields are complete and accurate.
