# travel_itinerary_template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about travel itineraries, including travel agency, travel type, dates, duration, destination, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the travel agency from the options provided.
2. Choose the type of travel (Hotel, Resort, AirBNB, etc.).
3. Enter the travel dates in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
4. Enter the duration of the trip in days.
5. Select the destination (Paris, Rome, Tokyo, Sydney, etc.).
6. Enter a brief description of the trip.
7. Enter any additional trip details.
8. If applicable, enter the dates and duration of any subsequent travel segments.
9. Select the destination and enter a brief description of each segment.
10. Enter any additional segment details.
11. Enter the cost of the trip.
12. Enter contact details (phone number and email).
13. Enter any additional agency information (if applicable).
14. Enter a description of any event or activity (if applicable).
15. Enter the dates and start time of the event.
16. Enter the duration of the event in hours.
17. Enter the location of the event.
18. Enter the name and contact information of the event organizer.
19. Enter any additional event details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Agency** (`1`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the travel agency you are booking with.
* **Travel Type** (`2`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of travel (Hotel, Resort, AirBNB, etc.).
* **Travel Dates** (`3`, `date`, optional): Enter the travel dates in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Travel Duration** (`4`, `number`, optional): Enter the duration of the trip in days.
* **Destination** (`5`, `select_one`, optional): Select the destination (Paris, Rome, Tokyo, Sydney, etc.).
* **Description** (`6`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the trip.
* **Trip Details** (`7`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional trip details.
* **Travel Dates 2** (`8`, `date`, optional): If applicable, enter the dates of any subsequent travel segments.
* **Travel Duration 2** (`9`, `number`, optional): If applicable, enter the duration of any subsequent travel segments.
* **Destination 2** (`10`, `select_one`, optional): If applicable, select the destination of any subsequent travel segments.
* **Description 2** (`11`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter a brief description of any subsequent travel segments.
* **Trip Details 2** (`12`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter any additional trip details.
* **Travel Cost** (`13`, `number`, optional): Enter the cost of the trip.
* **Contact Details** (`14`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Phone** (`15`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact phone number again (use if different from the previous field).
* **Contact Email** (`16`, `email`, optional): Enter your contact email.
* **Travel Agency** (`17`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the travel agency.
* **Event Description** (`18`, `text`, optional): Enter a description of any event or activity (if applicable).
* **Event Dates** (`19`, `date`, optional): Enter the dates of the event.
* **Event Start Time** (`20`, `time`, optional): Enter the start time of the event.
* **Event Duration** (`21`, `number`, optional): Enter the duration of the event in hours.
* **Event Location** (`22`, `text`, optional): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Organizer** (`23`, `text`, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the event organizer.
* **Event Details** (`24`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional event details.
* **Travel Agent** (`25`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional agency information (if applicable).
