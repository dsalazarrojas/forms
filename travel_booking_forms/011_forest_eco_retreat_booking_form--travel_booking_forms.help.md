# forest_eco_retreat_booking_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information for booking and managing forest eco retreats. It covers details such as booking dates, guest information, payment methods, and other relevant aspects of the booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide booking details such as date, start and end time, and duration.
2. Enter guest information, including name, phone number, and email.
3. Choose a payment method and status.
4. Specify the number of guests attending.
5. Make a payment confirmation.
6. Confirm the booking status.
7. Provide any special requests or reasons for cancellation.
8. Review and agree to terms and conditions.
