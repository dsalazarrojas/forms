# Medical Fitness For Air Travel Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains the Medical Fitness For Air Travel Assessment form, designed to help travelers with medical conditions or other health concerns prepare for air travel. The form is meant to assess a person's medical fitness for air travel, taking into account various factors such as travel destinations, medical conditions, and travel dates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of travel you are planning: Domestic or International.
2.  Enter your physician's name, as recommended by your doctor.
3.  If you have a medical condition that may impact your travel, describe it in this field.
4.  If you have received a medical clearance for travel, provide the clearance details here.
5.  Enter the date of your planned travel, in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Booking Form** (`travel_booking_form_1`, `select_multiple`, required: **false**): Please select the type of travel you are planning (Domestic or International).
* **Physician's Name** (`physician_name`, `text`, required: **false**): Enter the name of your attending physician or doctor who is treating your medical condition.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `text`, required: **false**): If you have a medical condition that may impact your travel, please describe it in detail here.
* **Medical Clearance** (`medical_clearance`, `text`, required: **false**): If you have received medical clearance for travel, provide the clearance details here.
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, `date`, required: **false**): Enter the date of your planned travel, in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Physician Signature** (`physician_signature`, `text`, required: **false**): This field is not currently used in this form.
 
 Note that while some fields are marked as "required", this form does not actually enforce these requirements.
