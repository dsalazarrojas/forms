# Trip Planning Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a trip planning quote, which will be used to generate a quote for the client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information about the trip.
2. Select the correct travel dates from the options provided.
3. Choose the departure and arrival points for the trip.
4. Enter the number of travelers and their preferred currency.
5. Enter the price of the trip.
6. Select the travel agency and travel agent responsible for the trip.
7. Provide any additional information about the trip.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`1`)
	+ This field is for general information about the trip.
	+ Enter a brief description of the trip, such as the destination and type of travel.
* **Client Details** (`2`)
	+ This field is for client information, such as name and contact details.
	+ Enter the client's name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Travel Dates** (`3`)
	+ This field is for selecting the travel dates for the trip.
	+ Select a travel date from the options provided.
* **Departure** (`4`)
	+ This field is for selecting the departure point for the trip.
	+ Choose the departure point from the options provided.
* **Arrival** (`5`)
	+ This field is for selecting the arrival point for the trip.
	+ Choose the arrival point from the options provided.
* **Duration** (`6`)
	+ This field is for entering the duration of the trip.
	+ Enter the number of days or nights for the trip.
* **Number of Travelers** (`7`)
	+ This field is for selecting the number of travelers.
	+ Choose the number of travelers from the options provided.
* **Currency** (`8`)
	+ This field is for selecting the preferred currency for the trip.
	+ Choose the currency from the options provided.
* **Price** (`9`)
	+ This field is for entering the price of the trip.
	+ Enter the price of the trip in the selected currency.
* **Travel Agency** (`10`)
	+ This field is for selecting the travel agency.
	+ Choose the travel agency from the options provided.
* **Travel Agency Phone** (`11`)
	+ This field is for entering the phone number of the travel agency.
	+ Enter the phone number of the travel agency.
* **Travel Agency Email** (`12`)
	+ This field is for entering the email of the travel agency.
	+ Enter the email of the travel agency.
* **Additional Information** (`13`)
	+ This field is for providing any additional information about the trip.
	+ Enter any relevant details about the trip.
* **Travel Agent** (`14`)
	+ This field is for selecting the travel agent.
	+ Choose the travel agent from the options provided.
