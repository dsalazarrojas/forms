# cruise_inquiry_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The cruise_inquiry_form is used to collect information for a customer's cruise inquiry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First name" field. This field is required for identification purposes.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field. This field is optional, but it helps to personalize the inquiry.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field. This field is used to communicate with you regarding your inquiry.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field. This field is optional, but it allows us to contact you more efficiently.
5. Select the departure date for your cruise in the "Departure date" field. This field is used to determine the available itineraries.
6. Select the return date for your cruise in the "Return date" field. This field is used to determine the available itineraries.
7. Choose the destination for your cruise in the "Destination" field. This field helps us to provide you with more specific options.
8. Indicate the duration of your cruise in the "Cruise duration" field. This field is used to determine the price and availability of the cruise.
9. If you're working with a travel agent, enter their name in the "Travel agent" field. This field is optional.
10. If you'd like to add any additional comments or information, type it in the "Comments" field. This field is optional.
11. If you'd like us to send any further communication, enter your email in the "Email sent" field. This field is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name (First name)** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last name (Last name)** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address where you can be reached regarding your inquiry.
* **Phone (Phone)** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number to enable efficient communication.
* **Departure date (Departure date)** (`departure_date`, date, required): Select the date you plan to depart for your cruise.
* **Return date (Return date)** (`return_date`, date, required): Select the date you plan to return from your cruise.
* **Destination (Destination)** (`destination`, text, required): Select the destination for your cruise.
* **Cruise duration (Cruise duration)** (`cruise_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of your cruise in number of days.
* **Travel agent (Travel agent)** (`travel_agent`, text, optional): If you're working with a travel agent, enter their name.
* **Comments (Comments)** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or information about your inquiry.
* **Email sent (Email sent)** (`email_sent`, text, optional): If you'd like us to send any further communication, enter your email.
