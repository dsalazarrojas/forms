# Luxury Cruise Packages Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather details from users regarding their inquiry about luxury cruise packages. It helps to understand their preferences and requirements for a customized cruise experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of inquiry you are making (Luxury Cruise, Private Yacht Charter, or Other).
2. Enter your name, as you would like to be contacted.
3. Provide your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Type your message to inquire about the cruise package.
6. Choose your preferred method of contact (Email, Phone, Text).
7. Specify the best time to contact you in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
8. Select your preferred language for communication (English, Spanish, French).
9. If desired, choose whether to receive follow-up communication or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Which type of inquiry are you making?** (`inquiry_type`, `select_one`): This field allows you to specify the type of luxury cruise package you are interested in. You can select from Luxury Cruise, Private Yacht Charter, or Other.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`): Please enter your name as you would like to be contacted.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`): Provide your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Message** (`inquiry_message`, `note`): Type a brief description of your inquiry about the luxury cruise package.
* **How would you like to be contacted?** (`preferred_contact_method`, `select_one`): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, Text).
* **Best time to contact?** (`best_time_to_contact`, `time`): Enter the best time to contact you in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Preferred Language** (`preferred_language`, `select_one`): Select your preferred language for communication (English, Spanish, French).
* **Follow up** (`follow_up`, `select_multiple`): Choose whether to receive follow-up communication or not, or select "Not Now" if you are not ready for follow-up.
