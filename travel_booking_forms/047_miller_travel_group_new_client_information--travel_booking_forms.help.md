# miller_travel_group_new_client_information - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "miller_travel_group_new_client_information" form is designed to collect essential travel information from clients, allowing us to better understand their needs and preferences. This data will be used to tailor our travel services to meet their expectations and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's information in the "Client Information" section (Page 1).
2. Select the type of travel the client is booking (Flight, Train, Car, Bus, or Other) in the "Travel Type" section (Page 2).
3. Enter the start and end dates of the trip in the "Travel Dates" section (Page 3).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Client Information (Page 1)
* **Client Name** (`title`, text, required): Enter the client's name.
* **Client Phone Number** (`client_phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, optional): Enter the client's email address.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Enter any special requests or requirements for the trip.

### 2. Travel Type (Page 2)
* **Travel Type** (`travel_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of travel the client is booking. Choose from Flight, Train, Car, Bus, or Other.

### 3. Travel Dates (Page 3)
* **Travel Start Date** (`travel_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the trip.
* **Travel End Date** (`travel_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the trip.

### 4. Travel Duration (Page 4)
* **Travel Duration** (`travel_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of the trip. This is the number of days the client will be traveling.

### 5. Travel Destination (Page 5)
* **Travel Destination** (`travel_destination`, text, optional): Enter the destination or location of the trip.

### 6. Arrival Time (Page 6)
* **Travel Arrival Time** (`travel_arrival_time`, time, optional): Enter the estimated arrival time of the client.

### 7. Travel Notes (Page 7)
* **Travel Notes** (`travel_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or information about the trip.

## Tips
* Ensure you fill out all required fields to avoid incomplete submissions.
* Use valid email addresses for the client's email.
* Double-check dates and duration to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact [support contact] for assistance.
