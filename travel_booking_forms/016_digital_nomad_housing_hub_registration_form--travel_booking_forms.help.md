# Digital Nomad Housing Hub Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about travelers and remote workers' accommodation requirements for a specific time period.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form with accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth registration process.
2.  Please provide all required information marked as "Required" to ensure that your needs are met.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name** (1, text, Required): Enter your full name.
*   **Contact Email** (2, email, Required): Enter your contact email address.
*   **Contact Phone** (3, text, Required): Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Start Date** (4, date, Required): Enter the start date of your stay.
*   **End Date** (5, date, Required): Enter the end date of your stay.
*   **Max Length of Stay** (6, number, Required): Enter the maximum number of days you plan to stay.
*   **Max Number of Guests** (7, number, Required): Enter the maximum number of guests you are expecting.
*   **Property Type** (8, select\_multiple, Required): Select the type of accommodation you are looking for (e.g., studio, 1-bedroom, 2-bedroom, etc.).
*   **Amenities** (9, select\_multiple, Required): Select the amenities you require (e.g., pool, gym, Wi-Fi, etc.).
*   **Location** (10, select\_multiple, Required): Select the preferred location for your accommodation (e.g., urban, suburban, rural, etc.).
*   **Budget** (11, select\_multiple, Required): Select your expected budget for the accommodation.
*   **House Rules** (12-15, text, Optional): Describe any additional rules or conditions you have for the accommodation.

## Tips

*   Be accurate and complete when filling out the form to ensure that your needs are met.
*   Ensure you have all required information available before submitting the form.
*   If you have any doubts or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
