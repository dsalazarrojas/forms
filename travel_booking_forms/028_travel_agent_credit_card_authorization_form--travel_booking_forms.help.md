# travel_agent_credit_card_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The travel_agent_credit_card_authorization_form is used to obtain travel agent's credit card authorization for client payments.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, ensure you have all necessary information for your client's payment.
2. Fill out the form with the following information:
	* **Client Information** (`client_info`): Enter your name and/or company name as the travel agent.
3. Enter your **Billing Information** (`billing_info`): This could be your company name or agency name, depending on your business setup.
4. Select **Payment Method** (`payment_method`):
	* Choose from Card Number, Card Type, Expiration Date, or Security Code.
5. Enter **Travel Information** (`travel_info`): Provide relevant details about the travel services or products you're authorizing for your client.
6. Enter the **Authorization Amount** (`authorization_amount`): The amount to be charged to your client's credit card.
7. Select the **Authorization Expires** (`auth_expires`): Choose the date when this authorization expires.
8. Add any **Authorization Notes** (`auth_notes`): Provide any additional notes about this payment authorization.
9. Enter the **Authorization Reason** (`auth_reason`): Provide a brief reason for the payment authorization.
10. Enter your **Client Phone** (`client_phone`): The client's phone number.
11. Enter your **Client Email** (`client_email`): The client's email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information**:
	* A text field to enter your name and/or company name as the travel agent.
* **Billing Information**:
	* A text field for your company name or agency name.
* **Payment Method**:
	* Select one of the options to determine how you want to process the payment:
		+ Card Number: The credit card number to be used for payment.
		+ Card Type: The type of credit card (e.g., Visa, Mastercard, Amex).
		+ Expiration Date: The date when the credit card expires.
		+ Security Code: The security code or CVV required for secure payment processing.
* **Travel Information**:
	* Enter relevant details about the travel services or products you're authorizing for your client.
* **Authorization Amount**:
	* Enter the amount to be charged to your client's credit card.
* **Authorization Expires**:
	* Select the date when this authorization expires.
* **Authorization Notes**:
	* Provide any additional notes about this payment authorization.
* **Authorization Reason**:
	* Enter a brief reason for the payment authorization.
* **Client Phone**:
	* Enter the client's phone number.
* **Client Email**:
	* Enter the client's email address.

## Tips

* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely before submitting the form.
* Verify your client's information is correct to avoid any payment disputes.
* Keep the authorization reason brief but descriptive for clarity. 

Note: The form fields may vary based on the options selected in the "payment_method" field.
