# Travel Package Booking Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Package Booking Information Form is designed to collect details necessary for the booking of travel packages. This form will be reviewed internally by travel agents to understand customer preferences and requirements for an efficient booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the travel package details.
2. Select the travel agent responsible for your booking.
3. Choose the type of package you are interested in.
4. Provide the start and end dates of your trip.
5. Enter the email and phone number of your travel agent.
6. Enter the customer details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **travel_package_details**: This is the description of the travel package you are interested in. Enter a brief description of the package.
* **travel_agent**: Enter the name of the travel agent responsible for your booking.
* **package_type**: Choose the type of package you are looking for from the available options:
	+ Package Type A
	+ Package Type B
* **start_date**: Select the start date of your trip.
* **end_date**: Select the end date of your trip.
* **travel_agent_email**: Enter the email of your travel agent.
* **travel_agent_phone**: Enter the phone number of your travel agent.
* **customer_details**: Enter a brief description of the customer's requirements or preferences.
* **customer_name**: Enter the name of the customer.
* **customer_email**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **customer_phone**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **customer_address**: Enter the customer's address.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled out correctly to avoid any issues with the booking process.
- Double-check the start and end dates of your trip.
- Enter accurate contact information for your travel agent.
