# Travel Request Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Request Booking Form is designed to collect essential information from users to help travel agents book their travel requests efficiently. Please fill out this form to provide accurate and relevant details about your travel request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully and enter the requested information accurately.
2. Make sure to choose the correct options for each field, as they will help the travel agent understand your preferences.
3. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Request Form**: This form is where you'll provide general information about your travel request.
* **Preferences**: Please select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your travel preferences.
* **Dates**: Choose the correct dates for your travel request.
* **Booking Details**: Enter additional details about your travel request.
* **Travel Agencies**: Select the travel agency that represents your travel request.
* **Other Agency**: If you have a preferred travel agency not listed, enter the agency's name here.
* **Other Agency Hint**: A hint for entering the other agency's name.
* **Travel Agency**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have a preferred travel agency.
* **Travel Agency Hint**: A hint for selecting your preferred travel agency.
* **Travel Agency Hint 2**: Another hint for your travel agency selection.
* **Destination**: Enter the destination of your travel request.
* **Destination Hint**: A hint for entering the destination.
* **Destination Hint 2**: Another hint for the destination.
* **Number of Guests**: Enter the number of guests traveling with you.
* **Number of Guests Hint**: A hint for entering the number of guests.
* **Number of Guests Hint 2**: Another hint for the number of guests.
* **Travel Type**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the type of travel.
* **Travel Type Hint**: A hint for selecting the travel type.
* **Travel Type Hint 2**: Another hint for the travel type.
* **Currency**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the currency.
* **Currency Hint**: A hint for selecting the currency.
* **Currency Hint 2**: Another hint for the currency.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information related to your travel request.
* **Additional Comments Hint**: A hint for entering additional comments.

Please fill out the form accurately, and don't hesitate to ask if you have any questions or need further assistance.
