# Sales Email Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture sales inquiries from customers, allowing the sales team to reach out to customers and provide them with travel options based on their interests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely.
2. Make sure to provide your name and email to allow the sales team to contact you.
3. Write a message describing your travel requirements.
4. Provide your phone number (if available) for the sales team to reach out to you.
5. Specify the date on which you plan to travel.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, Optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Customer Email** (`email`, email, Optional): Enter a valid email address that you have access to, as this will be the primary way the sales team will contact you.
* **Message** (`message`, text, Optional): Describe your travel requirements, such as destination, travel dates, and any specific needs you may have.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, Optional): Provide your phone number if you would like the sales team to contact you directly.
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, date, Optional): Enter the date on which you are planning to travel. The date should be in YYYY-MM-DD format.
