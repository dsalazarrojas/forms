# Rural Regeneration Residency Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a stay at the Rural Regeneration Residency, which is a unique opportunity to experience art, nature, and community connections in a rural setting. Please fill out the form with the required information to ensure a smooth and enjoyable stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the start date of your stay.
2. Enter the start time of your arrival.
3. Enter the end time of your departure.
4. Enter the end date of your stay.
5. Enter the name of your project or activity during your stay.
6. Select your preferred dates for the stay.
7. Provide a brief description of your project or activity details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to begin your stay at the Rural Regeneration Residency.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your arrival at the residency. This will help us prepare for your arrival.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you plan to depart from the residency. This will help us ensure a smooth checkout process.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to end your stay at the residency.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, optional): If you have a specific project or activity in mind, please provide its name. This will help us tailor our support to your needs.
* **Preferred Dates** (`preferred_dates`, select multiple, required): Choose the dates you have availability to stay at the residency. Please select all applicable dates.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your project or activity during your stay. This will help us better understand your needs and provide necessary support.
