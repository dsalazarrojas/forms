# Eco Apartment Stay Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Eco Apartment Stay Booking Form is used to collect information from guests about their upcoming stay at our eco-friendly apartments. This information is used to ensure that guests understand and agree to our policies and terms before confirming their booking.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name fields with your personal details.
2. Enter your Email address so we can contact you regarding your booking.
3. Enter your Phone number so we can contact you in case of any last-minute changes.
4. Select the Travel Date you wish to visit our apartments.
5. Select the Arrival Date and Departure Date of your stay.
6. Enter the length of your stay in days.
7. Enter the number of rooms you require for your stay.
8. Select the number of Adults and Children who will be accompanying you.
9. Agree to our Policy Agreements, which include terms such as payment terms, cancellation policies, and other rules related to your stay.
10. Provide any additional information you wish to share about your stay.
11. Finally, confirm that you have read and agree to our Terms and Conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you regarding your booking.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you in case of any last-minute changes.
* **Travel Date** (date, required): Select the date you wish to travel to our apartments.
* **Arrival Date** (date, required): Select the date you arrive at our apartments.
* **Departure Date** (date, required): Select the date you depart from our apartments.
* **Stay Length** (number, required): Enter the number of days you plan to stay with us.
* **Room Count** (number, required): Enter the number of rooms you require for your stay.
* **Adult Count** (number, required): Enter the number of adults who will be staying with you.
* **Child Count** (number, required): Enter the number of children who will be accompanying you.
* **Policy Agreements** (select_one, required): Select all policies that apply to your stay, including payment terms, cancellation policies, and other rules related to your stay.
* **Additional Info** (note, optional): Provide any additional information you wish to share about your stay, such as special requests or preferences.
* **Terms and Conditions** (select_multiple, required): Select all terms and conditions that apply to your stay, including Term 1, Term 2, and Term 3.

## Tips

* Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth booking process.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, such as extra beds or amenities, please mention them in the Additional Info field.
* Remember to review and agree to our Terms and Conditions before submitting the form.
