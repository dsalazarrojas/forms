# AT Disney Vacation Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential Disney visitors to provide personalized services and experiences during their visit. The gathered data will be used to tailor the accommodations and activities to fit their preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the "Section Header" tabs to navigate through the form's sections.
2. Fill out the "Guest Information" section with your full name, email address, and phone number (if applicable).
3. Choose your travel type from the options provided.
4. Select the "Vacation Details" section to provide the dates of your visit and the number of guests accompanying you.
5. Choose your preferred Disney park and park pass type.
6. Select your accommodation type and provide the hotel or resort name (if applicable).
7. Specify if you need transportation assistance and provide any necessary details.
8. Make any special requests, such as dietary restrictions or accessibility needs.
9. In the "Payment & Contact Preferences" section, choose your preferred payment method and provide any necessary payment information (if applicable).
10. Select your preferred language for communication.
11. Let us know how you heard about us.
12. Finally, add any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name**: Enter your legal name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email address**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for updates.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number for urgent updates (optional).
* **Traveler type**: Select your primary category as an adult, child, or infant.
* **Vacation start date**: Enter the first day of your trip using MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Vacation end date**: Enter the last day of your trip using MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Number of guests**: Count all travelers, including children and infants.
* **Preferred park**: Select the Disney park you'd like to visit most.
* **Park pass type**: Choose the pass that fits your itinerary (Single Park, Multi-Park, or Unlimited).
* **Accommodation type**: Select where you'd like to stay during your visit (Hotel, Resort, Vacation Rental, or None).
* **Hotel name**: Enter the name of the hotel or resort (if applicable).
* **Transportation needed**: Specify if you need transportation assistance.
* **Transportation details**: Provide any necessary details for transportation (optional).
* **Special requests**: Mention any dietary restrictions, accessibility needs, or other requests.
* **Payment method**: Select how you'd like to pay for your reservation.
* **Card type**: If paying by credit card, select the type of card (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, or Discover).
* **Card number**: Enter the 16-digit card number (only if paying by credit card).
* **Card expiry**: Enter the card expiry in MM/YY format (only if paying by credit card).
* **Billing address**: Enter the address associated with the payment method (if applicable).
* **Preferred contact time**: Select the best time to reach you for updates.
* **Preferred language**: Choose the language you prefer for communication.
* **How did you hear about us?**: Select all applicable sources where you heard about Disney (Friends, Social Media, Travel Agency, Website, Advertisement, or Other).
* **Additional comments**: Share any other information or feedback you'd like to provide.

## Tips

* Ensure that the dates entered for the vacation start and end dates are in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* If paying by credit card, provide the 16-digit card number and card expiry.
* Select a valid email address for updates.
* If you need transportation assistance, provide any necessary details for the transportation team to assist you.
* Be specific when making special requests, such as dietary restrictions or accessibility needs.
* If paying by credit card, ensure that the card type and card expiry are filled in correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
