# Cultural Performance Travel Support Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cultural Performance Travel Support Request Form is a tool used to request support for travel related to cultural performance activities. This form is used to gather necessary information to determine the feasibility and appropriateness of support for your travel request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide the name of the event you are requesting support for.
2. Select the type of event (Concert, Festival, Tour, or Other).
3. Enter the dates of your travel.
4. Specify the type of travel (Domestic or International).
5. Enter the location of your travel.
6. Provide a detailed breakdown of your budget.
7. Enter the contact information for the person responsible for your travel (Contact E-mail and Contact Phone).
8. If applicable, specify the organization supporting your travel.
9. Specify the type of travel funding available.
10. Enter the dates of your performance.
11. Select the length of your travel.
12. Enter any additional travel information (travel group, travel frequency, travel reason).
13. If applicable, provide the name, email, and phone number of the funder.
14. Enter any additional information regarding the funder (Funder Note).
15. Enter the location of the event.
16. Provide a brief description of the event.
17. Describe your travel arrangement.
18. Enter any travel safety concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event you are requesting support for.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of event (Concert, Festival, Tour, or Other).
* **Travel Dates** (`travel_dates`, date, optional): Enter the dates of your travel.
* **Travel Type** (`travel_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of travel (Domestic or International).
* **Travel Location** (`travel_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of your travel.
* **Travel Details** (`travel_details`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of your travel.
* **Budget** (`budget`, number, optional): Enter a detailed breakdown of your budget.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person responsible for your travel.
* **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the person responsible for your travel.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for your travel.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the organization supporting your travel.
* **Travel Funding** (`travel_funding`, text, optional): Specify the type of travel funding you are requesting.
* **Performance Dates** (`performance_dates`, date, optional): Enter the dates of your performance.
* **Travel Length** (`travel_length`, select_one, optional): Select the length of your travel (Less than 1 day, 1-3 days, 4-7 days, More than 7 days).
* **Travel Group** (`travel_group`, text, optional): Enter any additional travel information (travel group).
* **Travel Frequency** (`travel_frequency`, text, optional): Enter any additional travel information (travel frequency).
* **Travel Reason** (`travel_reason`, text, optional): Enter any additional travel information (travel reason).
* **Funder Name** (`funder_name`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the funder.
* **Funder Email** (`funder_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the funder.
* **Funder Phone** (`funder_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the funder.
* **Funder Note** (`funder_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional information regarding the funder.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the event.
* **Travel Arrangement** (`travel_arrangement`, text, optional): Describe your travel arrangement.
* **Travel Safety** (`travel_safety`, text, optional): Enter any travel safety concerns.
