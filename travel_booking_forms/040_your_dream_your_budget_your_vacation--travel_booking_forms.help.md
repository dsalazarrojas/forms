> # Your Dream. Your Budget. Your Vacation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about users' travel plans, including their dream destination, travel dates, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the destination city where you want to travel to.
2. Select the checkin and checkout dates for your trip.
3. Choose the flight class you prefer (economy, business, or first class).
4. If you have a travel agent, enter their name or contact information.
5. Enter your client email address for communication purposes.
6. Optional: Enter your client phone number for added contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dream Budget** (`dream_budget_vacation`): This field is optional and should not be confused with the **Destination City** field. If you have a specific budget in mind for your trip, please enter it here.
* **Destination City** (`destination_city`): Enter the city you want to travel to. For example, "New York City" or "Paris".
* **Checkin Date** (`checkin_date`): Enter the date you want to arrive at your destination. Please use the format "YYYY-MM-DD" (e.g., "2024-02-20").
* **Checkout Date** (`checkout_date`): Enter the date you want to leave your destination. Please use the format "YYYY-MM-DD" (e.g., "2024-02-27").
* **Flight Class** (`flight_class`): Select the class of service you prefer for your flight. Choose from "economy", "business", or "first".
* **Travel Agent** (`travel_agent`): If you have a travel agent assisting you, enter their name or contact information here.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`): Optional: Enter your phone number for added contact information.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter the correct dates for your trip, as this will help us provide you with accurate travel information.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, feel free to reach out to us for assistance.
