# disney_world_planning_and_booking_cheatsheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Disney World Planning and Booking Cheatsheet form is designed to collect information from clients to plan and book their trips to Disney World. This form helps agents and travel planners to gather all necessary details in one place, making it easier to create personalized travel plans and ensure a smooth booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form with the required and optional information.
2. Provide all relevant details for the client's planning and booking process.
3. Ensure that all fields are filled in correctly and according to the provided hints.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: planning_details
* **planning_details** (`planning_details`, `text`, required: false): This field is for collecting any additional details that the client would like to share about their trip, such as specific interests or preferences.

### Page 2: client_details
* **client_details** (`client_details`, `text`, required: false): This field is for collecting information about the client, such as their name, contact information, and any special requests.

### Page 3: travel_details
* **travel_details** (`travel_details`, `text`, required: false): This field is for gathering information about the client's travel dates, duration, and any other relevant travel details.

### Page 4: disney_preferences
* **disney_preferences** (`disney_preferences`, `text`, required: false): This field is for collecting the client's preferences for their Disney trip, such as their favorite Disney theme park or resort.

### Page 5: special_requests
* **special_requests** (`special_requests`, `text`, required: false): This field is for any additional requests or special needs that the client would like to share with the travel agent.

### Page 6: additional_questions
* **additional_questions** (`additional_questions`, `text`, required: false): This field is for any other questions or information that the client would like to share with the travel agent.

### Page 7: disney_travel_agent
* **disney_travel_agent** (`disney_travel_agent`, `email`, required: false): This field is for the travel agent's contact information, such as their email address.

### Page 8: disney_phone_number
* **disney_phone_number** (`disney_phone_number`, `text`, required: false): This field is for the travel agent's phone number.

### Page 9: disney_email
* **disney_email** (`disney_email`, `email`, required: false): This field is for the travel agent's email address.

### Page 10: disney_notes
* **disney_notes** (`disney_notes`, `note`, required: false): This field is for any notes or comments that the travel agent would like to add to the client's trip planning process.

## Tips
* Please fill in all fields accurately and completely to ensure a smooth and successful trip planning process.
* If you have any questions or need help with the form, please reach out to the travel agent for assistance.
