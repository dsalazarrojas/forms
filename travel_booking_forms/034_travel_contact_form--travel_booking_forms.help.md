# Travel Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Travel Contact Form is used to gather information from travelers about their travel preferences and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in your name to identify yourself.
2. Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
3. Provide your phone number to ensure we can contact you for any updates or questions.
4. Select your country of origin.
5. Choose the city you are traveling from.
6. Select your state or province.
7. Choose your preferred travel arrangements (Single, Double, or Suite).
8. Specify your flight preferences (Non-Stop or With Stop).
9. Select your hotel preferences (Non-Smoking, Pet-Friendly, or Breakfast Included).
10. If applicable, choose your date of departure and time of departure.
11. If applicable, choose your date of return and time of return.
12. Enter your street address for us to contact you.
13. Select your state or province for our records.
14. Enter your zip code for our records.
15. If you are working with a travel agency, please enter the agency's name.
16. Enter the name of the travel agent you are working with.
17. Enter the email address of the travel agent.
18. Enter the phone number of the travel agent.
19. Add any additional notes or comments about your travel.
