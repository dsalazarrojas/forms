# Luggage Size And Dimensions Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Luggage Size And Dimensions Inquiry Form is used to collect detailed specifications about the luggage, including its size and dimensions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form-filling process.
2. Fill out the form fields with accurate and precise information about your luggage.
3. Provide as much detail as possible to ensure efficient processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`1`, `text`, required: false): Enter your general contact information and any additional comments about the item being inquired.
* **Luggae Size** (`2`, `number`, required: false): Enter the size of the luggage in the given unit (likely inches or centimeters).
* **Dimensions** (`3`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional dimensions or specifications about the item, if necessary.
* **Base** (`4`, `number`, required: false): Enter the base dimension of the item, which could be the width or depth.
* **Height** (`5`, `number`, required: false): Enter the height of the item.
* **Width** (`6`, `number`, required: false): Enter the width of the item.
* **Length** (`7`, `number`, required: false): Enter the length of the item.
* **Weight** (`8`, `number`, required: false): Enter the weight of the item in pounds or kilograms.
* **Volume** (`9`, `number`, required: false): Enter the volume of the item.
* **Comments** (`10`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments about the item.
* **Email** (`11`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`12`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Category** (`13`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the category of the item (Air, Ship, or Other).
* **Subcategory** (`14`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the subcategory of the item (Air, Ship, or Other).
* **Tags** (`15`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose relevant tags for the item (Air, Ship, or Other).
* **Comments 2** (`16`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments about the item.
* **Submit** (`17`, `text`, required: false): Click the submit button to complete the form and proceed with the processing.
* **Form ID** (`18`, `text`, required: false): Enter a unique ID for the form submission.
* **Category ID** (`19`, `text`, required: false): Enter a unique ID for the category of the item.
* **Created at** (`20`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the form was created.
* **Updated at** (`21`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the form was last updated.
* **Created by** (`22`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the creator of the form.
* **Updated by** (`23`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose who last updated the form.
* **Updatedatdate** (`24`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the date the form was last updated.
* **Updatedatdate2** (`25`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose another date the form was updated.

Please ensure you fill out all required and optional fields accurately and thoroughly to provide the best possible experience for our team processing the form.
