# Orlando Holiday Vacation Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from travelers who are interested in a holiday vacation to Orlando. The information collected will be used to plan and tailor their trip accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the fields below with your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Trip Details (`trip_details`, text, optional)**: A field for you to describe your trip plans, such as the type of trip you are looking for (beach vacation, city exploration, etc.).
- **Travelers (`traveler_count`, number, optional)**: Enter the number of travelers accompanying you on your trip.
- **Travel Dates (`travel_dates`, date, optional)**: Enter your desired travel dates in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Arrival Date (`arrival_date`, date, optional)**: Enter the date you plan to arrive in Orlando.
- **Departure Date (`departure_date`, date, optional)**: Enter the date you plan to depart from Orlando.
- **Special Needs (`special_needs`, text, optional)**: If you have any special needs or requirements, please describe them here.
- **Accommodation Preferences (`accommodation_preferences`, select_multiple, optional)**: Select the type of accommodation you prefer (Hotel, Resort, Motel).
- **Room Type (`room_type`, select_one, optional)**: Select the type of room you would like to book (Suite, Room, Apartment).
- **Adult Count (`adult_count`, number, optional)**: Enter the number of adults traveling with you.
- **Child Count (`child_count`, number, optional)**: Enter the number of children traveling with you.
- **Travel Agent (`travel_agent`, select_multiple, optional)**: Select the travel agent who will assist you (Jimmy, Bob, Joe).
- **Special Needs Description (`special_needs_description`, text, optional)**: If you have any special needs or requirements, please describe them here.
- **Accommodation ID (`accommodation_id`, number, optional)**: Enter the ID of the accommodation you have chosen.
- **Contact Number (`contact_number`, text, optional)**: Enter your contact phone number.
- **Email (`email`, email, optional)**: Enter your email address.
- **Address (`address`, text, optional)**: Enter your physical address.

Please note that this form is for inquiry purposes only and will be reviewed by our travel agents to provide you with a customized offer.
