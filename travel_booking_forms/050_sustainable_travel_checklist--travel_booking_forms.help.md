# Sustainable Travel Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your travel habits and preferences related to sustainable travel practices. Your responses will help us tailor our services to cater to your needs and values.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each question carefully and answer based on your real-life experiences.
- Fill out the form at your own pace, taking as much time as you need.
- If you have any questions or doubts, refer to this guide for clarification.

* **What is your primary mode of transportation for your next trip?** (`user_input_2`): This question helps us understand your preferred mode of transportation (e.g., car, bus, bike, walk).
