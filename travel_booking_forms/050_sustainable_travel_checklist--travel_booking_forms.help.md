# Sustainable Travel Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your travel habits and preferences related to sustainable travel practices. Your responses will help us tailor our services to cater to your needs and values.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each question carefully and answer based on your real-life experiences.
- Fill out the form at your own pace, taking as much time as you need.
- If you have any questions or doubts, refer to this guide for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **To encourage sustainable travel, we want to get to know a little about you.** (`user_input_1`): This is an open-ended text field where you can share a little about yourself, such as your interests or motivations for sustainable travel.
* **What is your primary mode of transportation for your next trip?** (`user_input_2`): This question helps us understand your preferred mode of transportation (e.g., car, bus, bike, walk).
* **How do you usually book your travel accommodations?** (`user_input_3`): Choose how you typically book your accommodations, such as hotels, Airbnb, or a travel agency.
* **Do you consider sustainable travel practices as important when making your travel decisions?** (`user_input_4`): This question assesses how much you value sustainable travel practices when planning your trip.
* **Have you participated in any eco-tourism activities?** (`user_input_5`): Check if you've engaged in any eco-tourism activities, such as wildlife conservation, cultural immersion, or environmentally focused tours.
* **Which of the following actions do you take to reduce your environmental impact?** (`user_input_6`): Select multiple actions you take to reduce your environmental impact, such as reducing energy usage or recycling.
* **How often do you offset flights?** (`user_input_7`): This question gauges how frequently you offset flights to compensate for carbon emissions.
* **Do you prefer sustainable accommodations?** (`user_input_8`): This question explores your preference for eco-friendly accommodations.
* **How likely are you to choose accommodations with eco-friendly practices?** (`user_input_9`): This question assesses your likelihood of choosing accommodations that support sustainability.
* **Do you usually take public transport to get to your destinations?** (`user_input_10`): Check if you use public transport for your travels.
* **How often do you choose local tour operators for your activities?** (`user_input_11`): This question looks at how frequently you choose local tour operators for your activities.
* **Do you use a refillable water bottle during your trip?** (`user_input_12`): Check if you use a refillable water bottle during your travels.
