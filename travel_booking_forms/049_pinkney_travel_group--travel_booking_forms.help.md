# pinkney_travel_group - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pinkney_travel_group form collects information from users about their travel plans to create a comprehensive travel profile.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the details required in each section. You can skip any sections that are not relevant to your travel plans.
2. Enter a brief description of your dream vacation in the "Dream Vacation" field.
3. Provide the contact person's name and any other relevant contact information.
4. Select the travel dates for your trip.
5. Choose the type of travel (air, land, or sea) that best describes your trip.
6. Select the destination(s) you plan to visit.
7. Enter the size of your travel group.
8. Enter your email address.
9. Enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Dream Vacation** (`dream_vacation`, text, required): A brief description of your dream vacation.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): The person who will be contacted about your travel plans.
* **Select travel dates** (`travel_dates`, date, required): The dates you plan to travel.
* **Select travel type** (`travel_type`, select_one, required): The type of travel (air, land, or sea) that best describes your trip.
* **Select destination** (`travel_destination`, select_multiple, required): The destination(s) you plan to visit.
* **Size of your travel group** (`travel_group_size`, number, required): The number of people in your travel group.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Your phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Be as specific as possible when describing your dream vacation and selecting destinations.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please let the travel representative know in the "Dream Vacation" field.
* If you need help with any field, please do not hesitate to contact the contact person listed above.
