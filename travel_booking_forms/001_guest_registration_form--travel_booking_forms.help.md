# Guest Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect registration information from guests for travel bookings. Please fill it out carefully and accurately to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for the guest and the travel operator.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form in a new browser tab or window, as it is a multi-page form.
- Complete each page carefully, as they are designed to guide you through the registration process.
- Review and verify your input on each page before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (<code>first_name</code>):** 
	+ A text field to enter your first name.
* **Last Name (<code>last_name</code>):**
	+ A text field to enter your last name.
* **Email (<code>email</code>):**
	+ A field to enter your email address.
* **Phone (<code>phone</code>):**
	+ A text field to enter your phone number.
* **Address (<code>address</code>):** (optional)
	+ A text field to enter your address, which is not mandatory.
* **Date of Visit (<code>date_of_visit</code>):**
	+ A date field to select the date of your visit.
* **Time of Visit (<code>time_of_visit</code>):**
	+ A time field to select the time of your visit.
* **Note (<code>note</code>):** (optional)
	+ A text field for any additional information or comments.
* **How Many Guests (<code>select_one</code>):**
	+ A dropdown menu to select the number of guests accompanying you.
* **Languages Spoken (<code>select_multiple</code>):** (optional)
	+ A dropdown menu to select the languages you speak. 
* **Note 2 (<code>note_2</code>):** (optional)
	+ A text field for any additional information or comments.

## Tips
- Ensure you fill the form accurately and honestly.
- Double-check your input on each page before proceeding.
- Review and submit the form once you have completed it.
