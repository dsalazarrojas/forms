# Usa Visa - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book travel arrangements for USA visa travel. Please fill it out carefully to ensure your travel plans are processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the travel mode you'd like to book (Airline, Train, Bus, Taxi, or Car Rental).
2. Enter the traveler's name.
3. Enter the traveler's email address.
4. Enter the traveler's phone number.
5. Choose a date for your travel.
6. Select a time for your travel.
7. (Optional) Select a travel agency for your travel arrangement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What travel mode would you like to book?** (`travel_mode`, select_one, **required**): This field allows you to choose between Airline, Train, Bus, Taxi, or Car Rental for your USA visa travel.
* **What is the traveler's name?** (`traveler_name`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the person traveling.
* **What is the traveler's email?** (`traveler_email`, email, **required**): Enter the email address of the person traveling.
* **What is the traveler's phone number?** (`traveler_phone`, text, **required**): Enter the phone number of the person traveling.
* **On what date would you like to travel?** (`travel_date`, date, **required**): Choose a date for your travel.
* **At what time would you like to travel?** (`travel_time`, time, **required**): Choose a time for your travel.
* **Select Travel Agency** (`travel_agency`, select_one, **optional**): Choose a travel agency for your travel arrangement. If you don't select a travel agency, your travel arrangement will be processed without assistance.

## Tips
* Double-check your travel mode selection to ensure it aligns with your travel plans.
* Enter the traveler's contact information accurately to ensure they can be reached for travel updates.
* Be mindful of travel date and time selections to avoid scheduling conflicts.
