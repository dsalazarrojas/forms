# Arrival And Departure Information - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information from guests about their arrival and departure details. This includes their personal and contact details, arrival and departure times, room preferences, and any special requests or needs they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide your expected arrival and departure dates and times.
3. Select your preferred method of arrival and departure (e.g., car, flight, train, or bus).
4. If you have any special requests or needs, such as a requested room type or accessibility requirements, please indicate them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Guest Name** (text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
- **Email Address** (email, required): Enter your valid email address.
- **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
- **Total Number of Guests** (number, required): Indicate the number of guests traveling with you.
- **Expected Arrival Date** (date, required): Enter your expected arrival date in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
- **Expected Arrival Time** (time, required): Enter your expected arrival time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
- **Method of Arrival** (select_one, required): Choose your mode of transportation from the available options.
- **Transportation Details** (text, optional): Provide any additional information about your transportation method (e.g., flight number, car rental).
- **Special Arrival Requests or Instructions** (text, optional): If you have any special requests or instructions, such as early check-in or luggage assistance, please specify them here.
- **Room Type Requested** (select_one, required): Choose the type of room you prefer (e.g., single, double, suite).
- **Special Accommodation Needs** (text, optional): If you have any special accommodation needs, such as a wheelchair or quiet room, please describe them here.
- **Bed Preference** (select_one, optional): Choose your preferred bed type.
- **Expected Departure Date** (date, required): Enter your expected departure date in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
- **Expected Departure Time** (time, required): Enter your expected departure time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
- **Checkout Time Preference** (select_one, optional): Choose your preferred checkout time.
- **Departure Method** (select_one, optional): Choose your method of departure.
- **Special Departure Requests** (text, optional): If you have any special requests or needs for your departure, such as late checkout or luggage storage, please specify them here.
- **Dietary Restrictions** (text, optional): If you have any dietary restrictions or preferences, please describe them here.
- **Accessibility or Mobility Needs** (text, optional): If you have any special accessibility or mobility needs, please describe them here.
- **Purpose of Stay** (select_one, optional): Choose your purpose of stay (e.g., business, leisure, event).
- **Additional Comments or Requests** (text, optional): If you have any additional comments or requests, please enter them here.
