# Support Customer Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience with our customer support team. It will help us identify areas of improvement and ensure that our support team provides the best possible service to our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select your answers to each question and provide any additional comments or feedback you may have. Make sure to answer all the required questions, as they are marked with an asterisk (*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How would you rate your overall satisfaction with our customer support?** (`rating`, `select_one`, *): Please select one of the following options:
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Very dissatisfied
* **What was the reason for your last interaction with our support team?** (`reason`, `text`, *): Briefly describe why you contacted our support team. 
* **What was the issue you had with our support team?** (`issue`, `select_multiple`, *): Please select all that apply:
	+ Not familiar with the product
	+ Not helpful
	+ Not responsive
	+ Other (please specify)
* **How would you rate the effectiveness of our support team in resolving your issue?** (`effectiveness`, `select_one`, *): Please select one of the following options:
	+ Very effective
	+ Neutral
	+ Very ineffective
* **How likely are you to recommend our company to a friend or colleague?** (`likelihood_to_recommend`, `select_one`, *): Please select one of the following options:
	+ Very likely
	+ Somewhat likely
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat unlikely
	+ Very unlikely
* **What was the resolution or outcome of your issue?** (`resolution`, `select_one`, *): Please select one of the following options:
	+ Issue was resolved
	+ Issue was not resolved
	+ I was not sure
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Would you like to provide additional comments or feedback about your experience?** (`additional_comments`, `checkbox`, _): This is an optional field. If you have any comments or feedback, please provide them here.
* **What was the issue with our support team that you experienced?** (`support_team`, `select_multiple`, _): This is an optional field. If you select any of the options, please provide a brief description.
* **How would you rate the communication from our support team?** (`communication`, `select_one`, *): Please select one of the following options:
	+ Very good
	+ Somewhat good
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat poor
	+ Very poor
* **What improvements would you like to see us make to improve customer support?** (`suggestions`, `text`, _): This is an optional field. If you have any suggestions, please provide them here.
* **Any other comments or suggestions?** (`other_comments`, `text`, _): This is an optional field. If you have any other comments or suggestions, please provide them here.
* **Additional comments or feedback?** (`open_ended`, `note`, _): This is an optional field. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please provide them here.
