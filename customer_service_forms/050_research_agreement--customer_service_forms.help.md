# Research Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Research Agreement form is used to gather information from parents or guardians for research purposes. It is intended to be completed by parents or guardians authorizing their child to participate in research projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the child's first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter the child's last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter the child's email address in the "Email" field.
4. Select "True" or "False" for "Parent Permission".
5. Enter the child's contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
6. Answer the questions in the "Question 1", "Question 2", "Question 3", "Question 4", "Question 5", and "Question 6" fields.
7. Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (text): Enter the child's first name.
* **Last name** (text): Enter the child's last name.
* **Email** (email): Enter the child's email address.
* **Parent Permission** (select_one): Select "True" if you are authorizing your child to participate, "False" otherwise.
