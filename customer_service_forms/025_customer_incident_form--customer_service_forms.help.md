# Customer Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture information about a customer's incident, allowing our team to understand the issue, gather relevant details, and provide a resolution.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the required fields: "Customer Name", "Customer Email", "Incident Type", and "Incident Outcome".
2. Fill in the contact information fields ("Customer Name", "Customer Email", "Customer Phone", "Employee Name", "Employee Email", "Employee Phone", "Manager Name", "Manager Email", "Manager Phone") with the relevant details.
3. Describe the incident in the "Incident Description" field.
4. Provide any additional incident details in the "Incident Details" field.
5. Select the "Incident Type" from the dropdown menu, which categorizes the type of incident (e.g., "Product Quality", "Vehicle Damage", or "Other").
6. Specify the "Incident Date" and "Incident Time" of the incident.
7. Attach any relevant images or files to the "Incident Image" field.
8. Add any comments from the customer and employee in the "Customer Comments" and "Employee Comments" fields.
9. Sign and date the form by filling in the "Customer Signature", "Employee Signature", and "Manager Signature" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`):
	+ Enter the customer's contact information to ensure that we can reach out to them regarding the incident.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`):
	+ Describe the incident in detail to help our team understand the issue.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`):
	+ Enter the date of the incident.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`):
	+ Enter the time of the incident.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`):
	+ Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`):
	+ Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`):
	+ Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`):
	+ Enter the name of the product related to the incident.
* **Product Version** (`product_version`):
	+ Enter the version of the product related to the incident.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`):
	+ Select the type of incident (e.g., "Product Quality", "Vehicle Damage", or "Other").
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`):
	+ Provide any additional details about the incident.
* **Customer Comments** (`customer_comments`):
	+ Add any comments from the customer.
* **Incident Outcome** (`incident_outcome`):
	+ Select the outcome of the incident (e.g., "Resolved", "Open", or "Other").
* **Incident Image** (`incident_image`):
	+ Attach any relevant images or files related to the incident.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`):
	+ Enter the employee's name involved in the incident.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`):
	+ Enter the employee's email address involved in the incident.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`):
	+ Enter the employee's phone number involved in the incident.
* **Employee Comments** (`employee_comments`):
	+ Add any comments from the employee.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`):
	+ Enter the manager's name involved in the incident.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`):
	+ Enter the manager's email address involved in the incident.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`):
	+ Enter the manager's phone number involved in the incident.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`):
	+ Add any comments from the manager.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`):
	+ Sign and date the form by the customer.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`):
	+ Sign and date the form by the employee.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`):
	+ Sign and date the form by the manager.
