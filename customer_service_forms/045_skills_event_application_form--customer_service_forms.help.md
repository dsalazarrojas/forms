# skills_event_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from applicants for skills events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **Name** field.
2. Enter your job title and any relevant job titles in the **Job Title** field.
3. Enter your email address in the **Email** field. Make sure to use a valid email address.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
5. Attach your resume in the **Resume** field.
6. Select your relevant skills in the **Skills** field. You can select multiple skills.
7. Enter your experience in the **Experience** field.
8. Enter your education in the **Education** field.
9. (Optional) Enter any reference information in the **Reference** field.
10. Select "Yes" or "No" in the **Submit** field to confirm that your application is complete and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Job Title**: Enter your current or previous job titles.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Resume**: Attach your resume.
* **Skills**: Select the skills you have, separated by commas.
* **Experience**: Enter a brief description of your relevant experience.
* **Education**: Enter your educational background.
* **Reference**: (Optional) Enter any reference information if required.
* **Submit**: Confirm that your application is complete and accurate by selecting "Yes" or "No".
