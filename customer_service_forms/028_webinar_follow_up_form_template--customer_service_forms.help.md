# Webinar Follow Up Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to gather feedback and suggestions from attendees of webinars, allowing us to understand their experience and gather input for future improvements. By completing this form, you will be able to provide us with valuable insights to help us better serve our customers.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have all the information you need to provide a thorough response.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
3. Use the "feedback" field to share any comments or thoughts you have about your experience.
4. Rate your satisfaction with the webinar using the "rating" field.
5. If you have any suggestions for improvement, use the "suggest_improvements" field to share them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **feedback** (Webinar Follow Up Form Template / text / optional):
	+ Use this field to share any comments or thoughts about your experience with the webinar.
* **rating** (Webinar Follow Up Form Template / number / optional):
	+ Answer on a scale from 1 to 5 to indicate your level of satisfaction with the webinar.
* **suggest_improvements** (Webinar Follow Up Form Template / text / optional):
	+ Share your suggestions for improvement for future webinars.

## Tips

* Please be as detailed as possible when providing feedback and suggestions.
* Keep your responses clear and concise.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
