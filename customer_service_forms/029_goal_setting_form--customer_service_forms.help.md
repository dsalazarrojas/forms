# Goal Setting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to set and track goals, especially those with a specific start and end date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the yearly goals section to share your goals with others.
2. Select the goals you have achieved.
3. Enter any comments about your goals.
4. Set the target start and end dates for your goal.
5. Set the target duration for your goal.
6. Set the actual start and end dates for your goal.
7. Set the actual duration for your goal.
8. Select the status of your goal.
9. Enter any comments about the goal's progress.
10. Repeat steps 1-9 for each of your goals.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Share yearly goals** (`yearly_goals`, text, optional): Share your yearly goals with others. This field allows you to enter your goals for the year.
* **Goals achieved** (`goals_achieved`, select_multiple, required): Select the goals you have achieved. This field is required because it's essential to track progress.
* **Goal comments** (`goal_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments about your goal. This field is useful for adding additional information.
* **Target start date** (`target_start_date`, date, optional): Set the target start date for your goal. This field helps you plan and schedule your goal.
* **Target end date** (`target_end_date`, date, optional): Set the target end date for your goal. This field helps you plan and schedule your goal.
* **Target duration** (`target_duration`, time, optional): Set the target duration for your goal. This field helps you estimate how long it will take to complete your goal.
* **Actual start date** (`actual_start_date`, date, optional): Set the actual start date for your goal. This field helps you track when you began working on your goal.
* **Actual end date** (`actual_end_date`, date, optional): Set the actual end date for your goal. This field helps you track when you completed your goal.
* **Actual duration** (`actual_duration`, time, optional): Set the actual duration for your goal. This field helps you estimate how long it took to complete your goal.
* **Goal status** (`goal_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of your goal. This field is required because it helps you track progress.
