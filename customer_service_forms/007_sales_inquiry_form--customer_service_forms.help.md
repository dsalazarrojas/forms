# Sales Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information from customers who are interested in inquiring about sales-related issues or requests. It helps our customer service team understand their needs and provide the most suitable solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Please fill in the form with the required and optional information as prompted.
2. Ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **customer_service_forms_customer_info** (`text`, optional): This field is for customers to provide any relevant information about themselves, such as name, contact details, or any other relevant context about their inquiry.
* **customer_service_forms_sales_inquiry** (`text`, optional): In this field, customers can describe their sales inquiry or request in as much detail as possible.
* **customer_service_forms_contact_preferences** (`text`, optional): Here, customers can specify their preferred contact method (e.g., email, phone, or in-person meeting) and any other contact details they are willing to share.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form as accurately and completely as possible.
- If you have any additional information or attachments that you'd like to share, you can attach them to the form or provide them separately.
- Our customer service team will review your submission and respond to you as soon as possible.
