# Safety Recall Information Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Safety Recall Information Request form is used to gather information from customers who are inquiring about a safety recall related to a product they own. Please fill out this form to initiate the recall process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the product details, including a brief description of the product and any relevant information related to the recall.
2. Describe your recall inquiry in the text field provided.
3. Enter your product ID, if known.
4. Select the contact method you prefer to receive follow-up information about the recall.
5. Enter your email address, if you would like to receive updates about the recall via email.
6. Enter your phone number, if you would like to be contacted via phone.
7. Add any additional comments or information that may be helpful to the recall process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Product Details (product_details)**: Enter a brief description of your product, including its model number and any relevant features. This information helps us understand your product's specifications and identify the issue.
- **Recall Inquiry (recall_inquiry)**: Describe the issue you're experiencing with your product and any symptoms you're observing. Be as detailed as possible so we can better understand your concern.
- **Product ID (product_id)**: If you know your product ID, please enter it here. This helps us expedite the recall process.
- **Contact Details (contact_details)**: Select how you would like to be contacted about the recall process, such as Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
- **Email (email)**: If you would like to receive updates about the recall, enter your email address here.
- **Phone Number (phone_number)**: If you would like to be contacted via phone, enter your phone number here.
- **Additional Comments (additional_comments)**: Add any additional information that may be relevant to the recall process, such as your order ID or purchase date.

## Tips
- Please be as detailed as possible when filling out this form.
- If you're unsure about any information, contact our customer service team for guidance.
- We will respond to your inquiry as soon as possible, and we appreciate your patience and cooperation during the recall process.
