# Insurance Policy Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Insurance Policy Complaint Form is designed to gather information from customers about their insurance policy complaints. This form helps our customer service team to understand the nature of the issue, identify the problem, and take appropriate action to resolve it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select whether you have a service form by choosing "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu.
2. Provide a brief description of your issue in the text field.
3. Choose the type of complaint from the dropdown menu (General, Medical, or Policy).
4. Enter your policy number in the text field.
5. Select all applicable services from the dropdown menu (e.g., "Yes" if you need assistance with multiple services).
6. Enter your name and phone number in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Service Forms**: Choose "Yes" if you have a service form, or "No" if you don't. This helps us determine whether you need assistance with a specific service.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of your issue in this text field. This will help us understand the nature of your problem.
* **Complaint Type**: Select the type of complaint you are experiencing (General, Medical, or Policy). This helps us direct your issue to the correct department.
* **Policy Number**: Enter your policy number, which is required for our customer service team to look up your account information.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name so we can contact you back.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you to discuss your issue.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when describing your issue to help us quickly understand and resolve your problem.
* Make sure to enter your policy number correctly to avoid delays in processing your complaint.
* If you have questions or need assistance with anything else, don't hesitate to contact us after submitting this form.
