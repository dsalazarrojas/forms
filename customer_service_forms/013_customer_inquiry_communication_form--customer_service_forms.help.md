# Customer Inquiry Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Inquiry Communication Form is designed for customers to submit their inquiries, feedback, or questions to the company. This form allows customers to provide their details and a description of their inquiry, which will be reviewed and addressed by the company's support team.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Provide your Email.
4. Enter your Phone number.
5. If you are representing a company, provide the Company name.
6. Select the type(s) of Inquiry Type (e.g., Yes, No).
7. Enter a detailed Description of your inquiry.
8. If you have a specific Question, provide it in this field.
9. If applicable, assign a specific Tool related to your inquiry.
10. Choose a date for the Assigned Date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your first name for reference purposes.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Enter your last name for reference purposes.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Company** (`Company`, text, optional): If you represent a company, enter the name of the company.
* **Inquiry Type** (`Inquiry Type`, select_multiple, required): Choose the type(s) of inquiry you have (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').
* **Description** (`Description`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of your inquiry or question.
* **Question** (`Question`, text, optional): If you have a specific question, provide it here.
* **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`, text, optional): If applicable, assign a specific tool related to your inquiry.
* **Assigned Date** (`Assigned Date`, date, required): Choose a date for the Assigned Date.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely for our support team to assist you efficiently.
* Use a valid email address and phone number to ensure we can contact you.
* Be as descriptive as possible when filling out the Description field.
* Use the Inquiry Type field to help our support team understand the type of inquiry you have.
* If you have a specific question, provide it in the Question field.
