# Doula Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Doula Billing Form is a tool used to collect billing information from clients for services rendered by a doula. This form is intended to help doulas accurately track their services and charges.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Client Name in the text field.
2. Enter the Client Email address.
3. Enter the Service Date, using the date format mm/dd/yyyy.
4. Enter the Service Time, using the 12-hour clock format.
5. Enter the Total Hours spent on the services.
6. Enter the Hourly Rate.
7. Select the Services Rendered from the dropdown menu. This can be one of the following options:
	* Prenatal Visits
	* Birth Planning Sessions
	* Postpartum Support
8. If additional services were rendered, select them from the dropdown menu as well.
9. Enter the Total Charges, which will be calculated based on the Total Hours and Hourly Rate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (client_name): Enter the client's full name.
* **Client Email** (client_email): Enter the client's email address.
* **Service Date** (service_date): Enter the date of service using the mm/dd/yyyy format (e.g., 01/15/2024).
* **Service Time** (service_time): Enter the time of service using the 12-hour clock format (e.g., 10:00 AM).
* **Total Hours** (total_hours): Enter the total hours spent on the services.
* **Hourly Rate** (hourly_rate): Enter the hourly rate of the services.
* **Services Rendered** (services_rendered): Select one or multiple services rendered from the dropdown menu.
* **Other Services** (services_rendered_other): Select additional services rendered, if any.
* **Total Charges** (total_charges): The total charges will be calculated based on the Total Hours and Hourly Rate.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter the correct client information to ensure accurate billing.
* If additional services were rendered, ensure to select them in the "Other Services" field.
* If there are any issues with the form, contact the doulas support team for assistance.
