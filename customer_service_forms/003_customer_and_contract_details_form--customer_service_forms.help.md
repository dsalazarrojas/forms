# Customer And Contract Details Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Customer And Contract Details Form is used to collect customer information and contract details for general business purposes. This form is designed to gather essential information about a customer, including their contact details, contract specifics, and services they may require.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the customer's First Name in the `customer_first_name` field.
2. Enter the customer's Last Name in the `customer_last_name` field.
3. Enter the customer's Title in the `customer_title` field.
4. Enter the customer's Company in the `customer_company` field.
5. Enter the customer's Department in the `customer_department` field.
6. Enter the customer's Job Title in the `customer_job_title` field.
7. Enter the customer's Address Line 1 in the `customer_address_line_1` field.
8. Enter the customer's Address Line 2 in the `customer_address_line_2` field.
9. Enter the customer's City in the `customer_city` field.
10. Enter the customer's State in the `customer_state` field.
11. Enter the customer's Postal Code in the `customer_postal_code` field.
12. Enter the customer's Country in the `customer_country` field.
13. Enter the contract number in the `contract_number` field.
14. Enter the contract date in the `contract_date` field.
15. Select the contract start date in the `contract_start_date` field.
16. Select the contract end date in the `contract_end_date` field.
17. Select the type of contract in the `contract_type` field.
18. Select the employee handling this contract in the `employee` field.
19. Enter the customer's Email in the `customer_email` field.
20. Enter the customer’s Phone in the `customer_phone` field.
21. Enter the customer’s Fax in the `customer_fax` field.
22. Enter the customer's Cell Phone in the `customer_cellphone` field.
23. Enter the customer's Language in the `customer_language` field.
24. Select the customer's language preference in the `customer_language_preference` field.
25. Enter any additional notes about the customer in the `customer_note` field.
26. Enter any additional notes about the contract in the `customer_contract_note` field.
27. Select multiple services required by the customer in the `customer_service` field.
28-35. Select multiple services required by the customer (repeated)
