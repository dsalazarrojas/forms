# Divorce Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Divorce Survey" form is designed to collect information about your divorce-related experiences and circumstances.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Begin by filling in the introductory section, if available.
2. Provide your name and your spouse's name in the respective fields.
3. Answer the questions regarding the reason for divorce, childcare arrangements, and property division.
4. Indicate how spousal and financial support are handled, including the duration and amount of support.
5. Report any assets, debts, liabilities, and income.
6. Answer the question regarding asset division, if applicable.
7. Enter the amount of spousal and financial support received, as well as other expenses incurred.
8. Enter the amount of assets you own, if applicable.
9. Finally, review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intro (Text)**: This field is the introduction to the survey, providing an opportunity to describe your current situation.
* **Husband's Name (Text)**: Enter the name of your spouse.
* **Wife's Name (Text)**: Enter your own name.
* **Divorce Reason (Text)**: Describe the reason for your divorce.
* **Children Care (Text)**: Indicate how you care for your children, including custody arrangements.
* **Property Division (Text)**: Describe how property is divided between you and your spouse.
* **Custody Arrangements (Text)**: Describe the custody arrangements for children.
* **Asset Division (Text)**: Describe how assets are divided between you and your spouse.
* **Spousal Support (Text)**: Indicate how spousal support is handled.
* **Financial Support (Text)**: Indicate how financial support is handled.
* **Other Expenses (Text)**: Report any other expenses you have incurred as a result of the divorce.
* **Spousal Support Duration (Text)**: Indicate how long spousal support is received.
* **Financial Support Duration (Text)**: Indicate how long financial support is received.
* **Other Expenses Duration (Text)**: Report how long you have been incurring other expenses.
* **Assets (Text)**: Describe any assets you own.
* **Debts (Text)**: List any debts you have.
* **Liabilities (Text)**: Describe any liabilities.
* **Income (Text)**: Report your current income.
* **Asset Division (Text)**: Describe how assets are divided between you and your spouse.
* **Financial Situation (Text)**: Describe your current financial situation.
* **Spousal Support Amount (Text)**: Enter the amount of spousal support you receive.
* **Other Support Amount (Text)**: Enter the amount of other support you receive.
* **Financial Support Amount (Text)**: Enter the amount of financial support you receive.
* **Other Expenses Amount (Text)**: Enter the total amount of other expenses you have incurred.
* **Assets Amount (Text)**: Enter the value of assets you own.
* **Financial Situation (Text)**: Describe your current financial situation.

Note: This help guide is a general reference and may not cover every possible scenario. If you have specific questions or concerns, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for further assistance.
