# strength_empiresconsult_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer information and feedback for customer service representatives to assist with inquiries and issues.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Describe your goal or request in the "Goal" field.
6. Provide a detailed description of your issue or inquiry in the "Description" field.
7. Select a date for your appointment or issue in the "Date" field.
8. Choose a time for your appointment or issue in the "Time" field.
9. Select one option from the "Select One" field.
10. Select multiple options from the "Select Multiple" field (optional).
11. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field (optional).
12. Confirm your email address in the "Email Confirmed" field (optional).
13. Confirm your phone number in the "Phone Confirmed" field (optional).
14. Select one option from the "Select One Confirmed" field (optional).
15. Select multiple options from the "Select Multiple Confirmed" field (optional).
16. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Note Confirmed" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required):
	+ Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (text, required):
	+ Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email** (email, required):
	+ Enter the email address you use to contact you.
* **Phone** (text, required):
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Goal** (text, required):
	+ Describe the purpose or goal of your interaction with us.
* **Description** (text, optional):
	+ Provide a detailed description of your issue or inquiry.
* **Date** (date, required):
	+ Select the date for your appointment or issue.
* **Time** (time, required):
	+ Choose the time for your appointment or issue.
* **Select One** (select_one, required):
	+ Select one option that applies to your situation.
* **Select Multiple** (select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select multiple options that apply to your situation.
* **Note** (note, optional):
	+ Enter any additional notes or comments about your issue or inquiry.
* **Email Confirmed** (email, optional):
	+ Confirm the accuracy of your email address.
* **Phone Confirmed** (text, optional):
	+ Confirm the accuracy of your phone number.
* **Select One Confirmed** (select_one, optional):
	+ Confirm the accuracy of your selected option.
* **Select Multiple Confirmed** (select_multiple, optional):
	+ Confirm the accuracy of your selected options.
* **Note Confirmed** (note, optional):
	+ Enter any additional notes or comments about your confirmed issue or inquiry.
