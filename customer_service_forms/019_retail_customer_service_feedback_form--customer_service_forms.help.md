# Retail Customer Service Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from retail customers to understand their experiences and perceptions of our customer service quality. Your input will help us identify areas of improvement and provide better service to our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions according to your experience with our customer service. For each question, select the option that best describes your experience. You can skip any questions that are not relevant to your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **How was your shopping experience** (`customer_experience`, text, optional)
	* Tell us about your experience with our retail store.

### 2. **How helpful were the staff** (`staff_helpfulness`, select_one, optional)
	* Select the option that best describes how helpful the staff were during your visit.

### 3. **How would you rate the staff attitude** (`staff_attitude`, select_multiple, optional)
	* Select all that apply to the staff attitude during your visit.

### 4. **How would you rate the product quality** (`product_quality`, select_multiple, optional)
	* Select all that apply to the product quality during your visit.

### 5. **How would you rate the service quality** (`service_quality`, select_one, optional)
	* Select the option that best describes the service quality during your visit.

### 6. **How effective was the communication** (`communication_effectiveness`, select_multiple, optional)
	* Select all that apply to the communication effectiveness during your visit.

### 7. **How was the resolution of your issue** (`resolution_of_issue`, select_one, optional)
	* Select the option that best describes the resolution of your issue.

### 8. **How satisfied are you overall** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required)
	* Select the option that best describes your overall satisfaction with our customer service.

### 9. **What is your email address** (`email_address`, email, optional)
	* Enter your email address so we can contact you for further feedback or clarification.
