# Structured Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Structured Inquiry Form is a tool used to gather information from customers after they've been in contact with your company's customer service team. This form is meant to help your company's team track and respond to customer issues in a structured and organized way.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Fill in the required fields: `Issue Description`, `Priority`, `Customer Name`, `Customer Email`, `Contact Number`, and `Issue Type`.
*   Choose the correct options for `Priority` and `Issue Type` by selecting from the dropdown menus.
*   Provide as much detail as possible in the `Issue Description` and `Customer Request` fields.
*   Enter the `Customer Name` and `Contact Number` exactly as they appear on your account.
*   Enter a valid email address for `Customer Email`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Issue Description** (Required, Text): Please describe the issue you are experiencing. This is an open field where you can provide as much detail as possible.
*   **Priority** (Required, Select One): Indicate the level of priority for your issue. Select 'Yes' if it is urgent, and 'No' otherwise.
*   **Customer Name** (Required, Text): Enter your full name exactly as it appears on your account.
*   **Customer Email** (Required, Email): Enter the email address you use to contact us.
*   **Contact Number** (Required, Text): Enter your contact number exactly as it appears on your account.
*   **Issue Type** (Required, Select One): Indicate the type of issue you are experiencing. Select 'Yes' if it is related to a specific type of issue, and 'No' otherwise.
*   **Customer Request** (Required, Text): Please describe what you would like us to do to resolve your issue.
*   **Internal Notes** (Optional, Text): Provide any additional information that may help us to better understand your issue.
*   **Follow-up Date** (Optional, Date): If you expect to hear back from us on a specific date, please enter it here.
*   **Follow-up Date** is optional because it is only used when the internal team needs a reminder for follow-up purposes.
