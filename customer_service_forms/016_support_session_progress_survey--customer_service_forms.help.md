# Support Session Progress Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on customer satisfaction and experience after a support session. It helps us identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance our support process and services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Choose your level of satisfaction with the support session from the dropdown menu.
2.  Provide a brief description of your primary concern during the support session.
3.  Describe the resolution to your concern.
4.  Rate your satisfaction with the communication from the support agent.
5.  Select how likely you are to recommend our service to others.
6.  If you have any additional comments or suggestions, you can include them here.
7.  Provide your email address to receive future updates and offers.
8.  Enter your phone number for further communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **How satisfied are you with the support session?** (`Very Satisfied`, `Somewhat Satisfied`, `Neutral`, `Somewhat Dissatisfied`, `Very Dissatisfied`): This question asks about your overall satisfaction with the support session. Please select the option that best represents your feelings.
*   **What was your primary concern during the support session?**: This question asks you to describe the main issue you were trying to resolve during the support session. Please provide a brief description in this field.
*   **What was the resolution to your concern?**: This question asks you to describe how your issue was resolved. Please provide a brief description of the outcome.
*   **How did you rate the communication with your support agent?** (`Very Good`, `Good`, `Fair`, `Poor`, `Very Poor`): This question asks about your experience with the communication from the support agent. Please select the option that best represents your feelings.
*   **How likely are you to recommend our service to others?** (`Very Likely`, `Somewhat Likely`, `Not Likely`, `Not at All Likely`): This question asks about your likelihood of recommending our service to others. Please select all the options that apply.
*   **Additional comments or suggestions**: This is an open field where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions you might have about the support session.
*   **Email address**: This field is for your email address. Please enter the email address where you would like to receive future updates and offers.
*   **Phone number**: This field is for your phone number. Please enter your phone number for further communication.
