# Product Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Registration form is a crucial tool for customers to provide us with the necessary information about their products. This form helps us keep accurate records of our customers' products, enabling us to offer the best possible support and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the product details, including the product serial number.
2. Provide your contact information, so we can get in touch with you about your product.
3. Enter your order information, including the order date.
4. You can optionally provide your product image, but it's not required.
5. Fill in the product price, if you're providing it.
6. Enter the product name.
7. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product details** (`product_details`, text, required): Enter the basic details of your product.
* **Customer info** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Provide your name, email, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Contact info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your shipping address or any other contact details.
* **Order info** (`order_info`, text, required): Enter the order number or any other order-related details.
* **Shipping info** (`shipping_info`, text, optional): Enter shipping address or any other shipping-related details.
* **Order date** (`order_date`, date, required): Enter the date you received the order.
* **Product image** (`product_image`, text, optional): If you'd like to include a product image, upload it here.
* **Product serial number** (`product_serial_number`, text, required): Enter the product's serial number.
* **Product warranty** (`product_warranty`, text, optional): Enter the product's warranty information.
* **Product price** (`product_price`, number, optional): Enter the product's price, if known.
* **Product name** (`product_name`, text, required): Enter the product's name.

## Tips
* Please fill out this form with accurate information.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask our support team for help.
* This form is not intended for editing or updating existing registrations.
