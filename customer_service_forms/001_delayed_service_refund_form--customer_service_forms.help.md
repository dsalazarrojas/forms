# Delayed Service Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Delayed Service Refund Form is used to request a refund for a delayed service. This form helps customers to provide relevant information about their issue, allowing our team to efficiently process their refund request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form fields with the required information.
2. Provide a clear explanation of the issue and the reason for the refund request in the "Refund Reason" field.
3. Enter the refund amount in the "Refund Amount" field.
4. Provide the relevant service dates and duration in the "Service Start Date", "Service End Date", and "Service Duration" fields.
5. Enter any customer notes or additional information in the "Customer Notes" field.
6. Enter a brief response from the business in the "Business Response" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_service_form_1`, Text, Optional): Enter your name and contact information to help us get in touch with you about your refund request.
* **Service Details** (`service_details`, Text, Optional): Describe the service that was delayed and the issue you experienced.
* **Refund Reason** (`refund_reason`, Text, Optional): Explain the reason for your refund request. Please provide a clear and concise explanation of the situation.
* **Refund Amount** (`refund_amount`, Number, Optional): Enter the amount you are requesting as a refund.
* **Service Start Date** (`service_start_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the start date of the delayed service.
* **Service End Date** (`service_end_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the end date of the delayed service.
* **Service Duration** (`service_duration`, Number, Optional): Enter the duration of the delayed service.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, Text, Optional): Add any additional information or comments about your refund request.
* **Business Response** (`business_response`, Text, Optional): Enter a brief response from the business regarding your refund request.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the issue is not resolved through other means before submitting this form.
* Please provide as much detail as possible about the issue and the reason for the refund request.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your support team for assistance.
