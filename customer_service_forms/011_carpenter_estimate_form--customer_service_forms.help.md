# Carpenter Estimate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Carpenter Estimate Form is a tool designed to gather information from customers about their carpentry project. This form helps the carpenter to provide an accurate estimate for the project by collecting essential details about the project, customer contact information, and project preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, including "Customer Name", "Email Address", "Phone Number", "Property Address", "Type of Carpentry Project", "Is Removal of Existing Items Required", "Project Timeline", "Preferred Start Date", "Estimated Budget Range", and "Are Permits Required".
2. Provide additional information in the optional fields, such as "Project Details", "Project Description", "Wood Type Preference", "Finish Preference", "Site Access Information", "Access Details", "Permits Required", and "Additional Comments or Questions".
3. Select the appropriate options for each field, such as "Yes", "No", or "Unsure" for questions that require a choice.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Estimate Number**: This field is not required but provides a reference number for the estimate.
### 2. **Estimate Date**: This field is required and represents the date of the estimate request.
### 3. **Customer Information**: This field is required and allows you to provide your contact details, including your legal name, email address, and phone number.
### 4. **Project Details**: This field is optional and allows you to provide additional information about your project.
### 5. **Project Description**: This field is required and describes the work needed to be done for your project.
### 6. **Room or Location**: This field is optional and indicates where the work will be performed.
### 7. **Approximate Dimensions**: This field is optional and provides measurements if known.
### 8. **Wood Type Preference**: This field is optional and allows you to specify your preferred wood type.
### 9. **Finish Preference**: This field is optional and allows you to specify your preferred finish.
### 10. **Is Removal of Existing Items Required**: This field is required and indicates if demolition work is needed.
### 11. **Project Timeline**: This field is required and specifies when you want the work to be done.
### 12. **Preferred Start Date**: This field is optional and provides an ideal start date for the project.
### 13. **Estimated Budget Range**: This field is optional and indicates your budget expectations.
### 14. **Site Access Information**: This field is required and provides information about site access.
### 15. **Access Details**: This field is optional and describes any site access challenges.
### 16. **Are Permits Required**: This field is required and indicates if building permits are needed.
### 17. **Do You Have Photos of the Project Area**: This field is required and indicates if you have visual reference materials.
### 18. **How Did You Hear About Us**: This field is optional and provides referral source information.
### 19. **Additional Comments or Questions**: This field is optional and allows you to provide any additional information or questions.
### 20. **Consultation Preference**: This field is required and indicates how you prefer to proceed with the estimate.

By following this guide, customers can complete the Carpenter Estimate Form accurately and efficiently, providing the carpenter with the necessary information to provide a proper estimate for the project.
