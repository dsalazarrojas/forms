# Loyalty Integration Query Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user feedback and queries related to loyalty integration. It is intended to gather information from customers who have questions or concerns about the loyalty integration process with our platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title that describes your query or concern (optional).
2. Provide your email address so we can contact you back.
3. Type in your name for reference.
4. Describe your message or question about the loyalty integration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User input** (`user_input`, text, optional): This field is for entering any additional information you'd like to provide about your query or concern.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you back with a response or further questions.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Type in your name for reference in our records.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): This field is for entering a detailed description of your query or concern about the loyalty integration.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when entering your query or concern.
* If you've already sent a similar query, you can also include a link to the previous message, or mention it in your new message.
* If you'd like to follow up on a previous query, make sure to include the query ID in your new message.
