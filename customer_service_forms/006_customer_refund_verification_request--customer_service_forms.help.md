# Customer Refund Verification Request - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to submit a formal request for a refund for a customer's purchase. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out this form completely, if possible. The information you provide will help our customer service team to verify and process your refund request quickly and accurately. 
2. Make sure to select the correct reason for your refund request from the options provided. 
3. Double check the order item details to ensure the correct item(s) and date of purchase are included. 
4. Enter the correct date of purchase and the refund amount you are requesting. 
5. Provide any additional notes or comments about your purchase in the customer note field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Details** (`customer_refund_request`, text, optional): This is the main description of your refund request. Please explain the reason why you are requesting a refund in this field. 
* **Order Item Details** (`order_item_details`, select_one, optional): Select the correct item from the list that you are requesting a refund for. This will help our customer service team to quickly identify the item and verify the refund. 
* **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you made the purchase. This will help our customer service team to verify the date of purchase and ensure that the refund is processed correctly. 
* **Purchase Amount** (`purchase_amount`, number, optional): Enter the total amount you paid for the purchase. This will help our customer service team to calculate the correct refund amount. 
* **Refund Reason** (`refund_reason`, text, optional): Please select a valid reason for your refund request from the options provided. This will help our customer service team to quickly identify the reason for your refund request. 
* **Refund Requested Date** (`refund_requested_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are requesting the refund for. This will help our customer service team to track the status of your refund request. 
* **Customer Note** (`customer_note`, note, optional): You can provide any additional notes or comments about your purchase or refund request in this field. This will help our customer service team to better understand your situation. 
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so that we can contact you with updates about your refund request. This will help us to get in touch with you if we need more information about your request. 
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you if we need more information about your refund request. This will help us to get in touch with you quickly and efficiently. 

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out this form completely and accurately. 
* Double check the information you enter in this form to ensure that it is correct. 
* If you are unsure about anything, please contact our customer service team for assistance.
