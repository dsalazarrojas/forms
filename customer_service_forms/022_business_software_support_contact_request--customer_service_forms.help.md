# Business Software Support Contact Request - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used for customers and end-users to submit support requests for our business software products. It helps us understand the issue or problem you're facing and provides us with the necessary information to assist you effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your full name, email address, and phone number (if available).
2. Enter the name of your organization or company.
3. Indicate the type of issue you're experiencing (e.g., Technical Issue, Feature Request, etc.).
4. Specify the software product and version you're using.
5. Describe the issue or problem you're encountering.
6. If applicable, include any error messages or codes you've received.
7. Explain how to reproduce the issue (if possible).
8. Choose your preferred contact method (email, phone, or no contact needed).
9. If available, specify when you are available for follow-up discussions.
10. Finally, date and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name** (`requester_name`): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`requester_email`): Enter your email address for support contact.
* **Phone Number** (`requester_phone`): Enter your contact phone number (if available).
* **Company Name** (`company_name`): Enter the name of your organization or company.
* **Account or Customer Number** (`account_number`): If available, enter your account ID.
* **Type of Support Issue** (`support_issue_type`): Select the type of issue you're experiencing (e.g., Technical Issue, Feature Request, etc.).
* **Software Product** (`software_product`): Enter the name of the software product you're using.
* **Software Version** (`software_version`): Enter the version of the software you're running.
* **Issue Priority** (`issue_priority`): Indicate the urgency of the issue (Critical, High, Medium, Low).
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`): Describe the issue or problem you're encountering.
* **Error Message or Code** (`error_message`): If applicable, include any error messages or codes you've received.
* **Steps to Reproduce Issue** (`steps_to_reproduce`): Explain how to reproduce the issue (if possible).
* **Operating System** (`operating_system`): If web-based, indicate the web browser you're using (e.g., Chrome, Firefox, etc.).
* **Browser Type** (`browser_type`): Select the type of browser you're using (e.g., Chrome, Firefox, etc.).
* **Attachments or Screenshots** (`attachments`): Attach files to help explain the issue (if applicable).
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`support_contact_preference`): Choose your preferred contact method (email, phone, or no contact needed).
* **Available for Follow-up** (`available_for_followup`): If available, specify when you are available for follow-up discussions.
* **Support Request Date** (`request_submission_date`): Date of this support request.

Please note that some fields are optional and only required if applicable.
