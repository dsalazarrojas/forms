# New Customer Details Form Oliana Foods - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Customer Details Form is used to collect essential information from new customers. This form is designed for internal use and helps our customer service team to efficiently manage customer relationships and provide accurate information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First name** in the "First name" field. This is a required field.
2. Enter your **Last name** in the "Last name" field. This is a required field.
3. Enter your **Email address** in the "Email" field. This is a required field and must be in the correct email format (e.g., `username@domain.com`).
4. Enter your **Phone number** in the "Phone" field. This is a required field.
5. Enter the date of your **Order** in the "Order date" field. This is a required field.
6. Enter the time of your **Order** in the "Order time" field. This is a required field.
7. Enter the date for your **Delivery** in the "Delivery date" field. This is a required field.
8. Enter the time for your **Delivery** in the "Delivery time" field. This is a required field.
9. Enter your **Address** in the "Address" field. This is a required field.
10. If you have any additional comments or notes, you can enter them in the "Note" field. This field is optional.
