# Customer Account Merge Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to initiate a merge of a customer's accounts in the system. Fill out this form to provide your contact information and select the type of merge you're looking for.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form with your contact information, which includes:
	+ **Customer Information**: Enter your customer information in the first field.
	+ **Company**: Enter your company's name in the second field.
	+ **Email**: Enter your email address in the third field.
	+ **Phone**: Enter your phone number in the fourth field.
* Select the **Merge Type** in the fifth field:
	+ **Merge Accounts**: Select this option to merge your customer accounts in the system.
	+ **Merge Company**: Select this option to merge your company's accounts in the system.
* If necessary, provide additional **Reason** for the merge in the sixth field.
* If you have any comments or notes about the merge, you can provide them in the seventh field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, required): Enter your customer information.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter your company's name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Merge Type** (`merge_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of merge you're looking for:
	+ **Merge Accounts**: Merge your customer accounts in the system.
	+ **Merge Company**: Merge your company's accounts in the system.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, optional): If you have a specific reason for the merge, you can enter it here.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): If you have any comments or notes about the merge, you can enter them here.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter your company's name.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): If you have any additional contact information, you can enter it here.
