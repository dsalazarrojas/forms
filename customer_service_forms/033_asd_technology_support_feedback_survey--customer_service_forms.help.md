# ASD Technology Support Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from customers who have interacted with our technology support team. It's designed to help us understand how we can improve our support services and better serve our customers in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and email address (if you wish to be contacted for follow-up).
2. Answer the questions regarding your support request, such as when you requested support and what type of support you needed.
3. Rate your experience with our support team, including response time, technician knowledge, and solution effectiveness.
4. Provide additional feedback or suggestions for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`respondent_name`, text, optional): Enter your name (if you wish to provide it).
* **Email** (`respondent_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address (if you wish to be contacted for follow-up).
* **Support Request Date** (`support_request_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you requested support.
* **Support Issue Type** (`support_issue_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of technology support you requested, such as "Software Problem" or "Hardware Issue".
* **Support Channel Used** (`support_channel_used`, select_one, required): Select how you reached out for support, like "Phone" or "Email".
* **Response Time Satisfaction** (`response_time_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate how satisfied you were with the response time.
* **Technician Knowledge** (`technician_knowledge`, select_one, required): Rate the knowledge of the technician who helped you.
* **Solution Effectiveness** (`solution_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Rate how effective the solution provided was.
* **Communication Clarity** (`communication_clarity`, select_one, required): Rate how clear the communication was during support.
* **Overall Support Satisfaction** (`overall_support_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the support experience.
* **Support Staff Professionalism** (`support_staff_professionalism`, select_one, required): Rate the professionalism of the support staff.
* **Follow-up Necessary** (`follow_up_necessary`, select_one, required): Indicate if follow-up support was necessary.
* **Would Recommend Support** (`would_recommend_support`, select_one, required): Indicate if you would recommend our technology support to others.
* **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about the support experience.

## Tips
* Please answer each question as honestly as possible.
* You can skip any fields that you don't feel like filling out.
* If you have any issues or concerns that you'd like to discuss further, feel free to contact us directly.
