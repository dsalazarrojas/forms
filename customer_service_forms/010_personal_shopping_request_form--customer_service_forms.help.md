# Personal Shopping Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Shopping Request Form is a tool to allow customers to submit their personal shopping requests to the service team. This form is designed to be user-friendly and easy to complete.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the reason for your request in the "How may we assist you today?" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. If you have a phone number, you can enter it in the "Phone" field.
4. Write your shopping request or message in the "Customer Message" field.
5. Add any additional comments or feedback in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shopping Request** (`shopping_request`, `text`, optional): A brief description of your shopping request.
* **How may we assist you today?** (`customer_request`, `select_one`, required): Select the reason for your request from the options provided.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted by phone.
* **Customer Message** (`customer_message`, `text`, optional): Write your shopping request or message here.
* **Additional Comments** (`feedback`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share with us.
