# Sugaring Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sugaring Consultation Form is used to book and schedule sugaring treatments. This form is intended for new clients who are interested in booking a treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your First Name and Last Name.
2. Enter your Phone Number and Email.
3. Specify your Expected Length of Sugaring Treatment in centimeters.
4. Select the Areas to Focus On (e.g., Chest, Back, Shoulders, etc.).
5. Choose how often you want to have sugaring treatments (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
6. Select your Preferred Sugaring Therapist.
7. Choose your Preferred Payment Method (e.g., Credit Card, Cash).
8. Add any Additional Comments or Additional Information about your treatment.
9. If you have any Areas of Concern, select them.
10. Add any Additional Notes about your treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your last name.
* **Phone Number** (Number, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Expected Length of Sugaring Treatment** (Number, Optional): Specify the length of your sugaring treatment in centimeters.
* **Areas to Focus On** (Select Multiple, Required): Select multiple areas where you would like the treatment to focus on.
* **How often do you want to have sugaring treatments?** (Select One, Required): Choose how often you want to have sugaring treatments (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **Expected Length of Treatment** (Number, Optional): Specify the length of your treatment in minutes.
* **Additional Comments** (Note, Optional): Add any additional comments about your treatment.
* **Preferred Sugaring Therapist** (Select One, Required): Choose if you have a preferred sugaring therapist.
* **Areas of Concern** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select any areas of concern you have about your treatment.
* **Additional Information** (Text, Optional): Add any additional information about your treatment.
* **Treatment Preferences** (Note, Optional): Add any treatment preferences.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (Select One, Required): Choose your preferred payment method (e.g., Credit Card, Cash).
* **Additional Notes** (Note, Optional): Add any additional notes about your treatment.

Note: All fields marked as Required are mandatory and must be completed to submit the form.
