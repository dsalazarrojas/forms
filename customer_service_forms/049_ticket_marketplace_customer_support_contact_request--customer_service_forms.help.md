# Ticket Marketplace Customer Support Contact Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow customers to submit a support request to the Ticket Marketplace platform. It captures essential information about the customer's issue, priority, and department requirements, ensuring that their support request is handled efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Customer Name** in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Provide your **Email Address** in the "Customer Email" field.
3. Specify your **Phone Number** in the "Customer Phone" field (optional).
4. Describe the **Issue Description** in the "Issue Description" field.
5. Select your **Priority Level** (High, Medium, or Low) in the "Customer Priority" field.
6. Choose the relevant **Department** for your issue (Marketing, Technical, Sales, Finance, or Other) in the "Customer Department" field.
7. Select a **Support Agent** for your issue (John Doe, Jane Doe, or John Smith) in the "Customer Support Agent" field.
8. Optionally, provide additional **Comments** in the "Customer Comment" field.
9. Select a **Follow-up Date** for your issue (if required).
10. Finally, provide a detailed **Support Request** in the "Customer Support Request" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name (Customer Name)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Customer Email (Customer Email)**: Enter your valid email address.
* **Customer Phone (Customer Phone)**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Issue Description (Issue Description)**: Describe your issue in detail, including any relevant information or images.
* **Customer Priority (Customer Priority)**: Select the priority level for your issue (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Customer Department (Customer Department)**: Choose the department that best handles your issue (Marketing, Technical, Sales, Finance, or Other).
* **Customer Support Request (Customer Support Request)**: Write a detailed description of your support request.
* **Customer Follow-up (Customer Follow-up)**: If you want to schedule a follow-up, select a date.
* **Customer Support Agent (Customer Support Agent)**: Select a support agent to handle your issue (John Doe, Jane Doe, or John Smith).
* **Customer Comment (Customer Comment)**: Provide any additional comments or details about your issue.
* **Customer Support Response (Customer Support Response)**: Not applicable for customer-facing forms.
 
Please note that some fields are required, and you need to fill them out before submitting the form. If you have any doubts or issues, please contact our support team. 

Tips: Please ensure that you fill out all required fields to ensure that your support request is handled effectively. Also, be as detailed as possible when providing your issue description and support request. This will help our support agents to resolve your issue efficiently. 

Note: This form is designed to guide you through a structured process. Please follow the instructions carefully to avoid any issues with your support request.
