# customer_service_satisfaction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The customer_service_satisfaction_survey form is designed to collect customer feedback and opinions about the service provided by our company. It is essential for us to understand your experience and identify areas that need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2.  Fill out the form with your contact information (email and phone number).
3.  Select the assigned agent who assisted you.
4.  Provide your comments and feedback on the service provided.
5.  Rate your overall satisfaction with the service.
6.  Indicate your satisfaction with the support provided by the assigned agent.
7.  Select the time and date of your interaction with the service.
8.  Finally, submit your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **customer_service_satisfaction_survey_1** (Text): This field is used to provide a description of your service experience.
*   **overall_experience** (Number): Rate your overall experience with the service, with 5 being the highest satisfaction level.
*   **satisfaction_with_service** (Select One): Rate your satisfaction with the service, with 5 being the highest satisfaction level.
*   **service_provided** (Text): Describe the service provided.
*   **email** (Email): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
*   **phone** (Text): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
*   **assigned_agent** (Select One): Select the agent who assisted you.
*   **satisfaction_with_support** (Select Multiple): Rate your satisfaction with the support provided by the assigned agent.
*   **time** (Time): Select the time of your interaction with the service.
*   **date** (Date): Select the date of your interaction with the service.
*   **submit** (Text): This field is automatically generated and not editable. You can only submit your response.
*   **overall_satisfaction** (Text): This field is used to provide a brief description of your overall satisfaction with the service.
*   **satisfaction_score** (Number): Enter a number to represent your overall satisfaction with the service.

## Tips
*   Please fill out the form only once to avoid duplication of responses.
*   Be honest and detailed in your answers.
*   Your feedback is valuable to us and will help us improve our services.
