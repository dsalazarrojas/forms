# Customer Service Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Customer Service Contact Form is designed for customers to contact the company's customer service team with their questions, comments, or concerns. This form is intended to be used for general inquiries, billing issues, or product-related questions. You can use this form to get in touch with our customer service team and receive assistance with your concerns.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please ensure you have a clear and concise message describing your question, comment, or concern.
2. Complete each field according to the instructions below.
3. Click on the "Submit" button to send your message to our customer service team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name, if you wish to provide it.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name, if you wish to provide it.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so that our customer service team can respond to your message.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): Describe your question, comment, or concern in as much detail as possible.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you prefer to be contacted over the phone, enter your phone number here.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Select the category that best describes your concern. Choose from:
	+ General
	+ Billing
	+ Product
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, optional): Choose "Yes" to submit your message and "No" to cancel your submission.
