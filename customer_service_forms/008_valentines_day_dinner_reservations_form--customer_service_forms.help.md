# valentines_day_dinner_reservations_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to make Valentine's Day dinner reservations. It will help us gather information about your preferences and requests to provide a memorable and enjoyable experience for you and your loved one.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer information section with your name, email, and phone number.
2. In the reservation details section, provide us with the number of guests and any special occasions or events you'd like to celebrate.
3. If you have any special requests, such as flowers or candles, let us know in the special requests section.
4. Confirm your reservation by selecting "True" in the confirm reservation section.
5. Finally, if you have any additional comments or requests, use the note sections to provide more information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, required): Please enter your name, email address, and phone number.
* **Reservation Details** (`reservation_details`, text, required): Provide the number of guests and any special occasions or events you'd like to celebrate.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, note, optional): Let us know if you have any special requests, such as flowers or candles.
* **Confirm Reservation** (`confirm_reservation`, select_one, required): Select "True" to confirm your reservation.
* **Customer Service** (`customer_service`, select_one, optional): Select "True" to indicate that you'd like to receive customer service.
* **Note** (note, optional): Use this section to provide any additional comments or requests.
