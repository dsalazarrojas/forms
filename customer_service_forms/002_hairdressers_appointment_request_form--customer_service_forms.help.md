# Hairdressers Appointment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers for hairdressers' appointment requests. It helps hairdressers manage appointments more efficiently and provide better customer service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Customer Information** page with your details, including your name, and any other relevant information.
2. Select the services you would like to book an appointment for (if any).
3. Choose your preferred hairdresser from the list.
4. If desired, schedule a specific date and time for your appointment.
5. Add any additional message or comments you'd like to share with the hairdresser.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information** (`Customer Information`, text, required):
	+ This page is where you will enter your personal details, such as your name and any other relevant information.
* **Services** (`Services`, select multiple, not required):
	+ Select the services you would like to book an appointment for, such as Haircut, Coloring, or Styling.
* **Stylist** (`Stylist`, select one, not required):
	+ Choose your preferred hairdresser from the list.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, not required):
	+ If you have a specific date in mind for your appointment, enter it here.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, not required):
	+ If you have a preferred time for your appointment, enter it here.
* **Message** (`Message`, note, not required):
	+ Add any additional information or comments you'd like to share with the hairdresser.
