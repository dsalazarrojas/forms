# Annual Business Planning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about your business plans and strategies for the upcoming year. It helps us understand your company's goals, target market, customer retention strategies, and budget allocation. The insights gathered from this form will aid us in tailoring our marketing and product development efforts to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To get the most out of this form, please ensure you have all necessary information and answers ready before starting. Here's a step-by-step guide:

1. Start with your personal details: Please fill in your name and email address accurately.
2. Provide your company's information: Fill in your company's name and any relevant details that might help us understand your business.
3. Outline your key goals and objectives for the next year. This will help us tailor our support and services to fit your needs.
4. Share your revenue and growth expectations for the coming year.
5. Discuss any expansion or diversification plans you might have.
6. Describe your target market and the ideal customer base you're aiming for.
7. Identify key market trends and competitive advantages.
8. Discuss current and anticipated challenges.
9. Outline how you plan to address these challenges.
10. Provide an overview of your marketing strategy, product development, and customer retention strategies for the next year.
11. Finally, share your budget allocation plans for the upcoming year.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Customer Information
- **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Please fill in your name accurately.
- **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter your business's name and any relevant details.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): This is how we will contact you with updates and questions.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Your contact number in case we need to reach you via phone.

### Business Goals
- **Top 3 Goals for Next Year** (`primary_goals`, `text`, required): Describe your key objectives for the next year.
- **Target Revenue for Next Year** (`revenue_target`, `number`, optional): This is your expected revenue goal for the next year. If this is unknown, please describe how you plan to reach it.
- **Expected Growth Rate** (`growth_expectations`, `number`, optional): The percentage growth you anticipate for the next year.

### Market and Customer
- **Target Market or Customer Base** (`target_market`, `text`, required): Who do you aim to serve or sell to?
- **Key Market Trends** (`market_trends`, `text`, optional): Important trends in your industry or market.
- **Your Competitive Advantages** (`competitive_advantages`, `text`, optional): What sets you apart from others?

### Challenges
- **Current Business Challenges** (`current_challenges`, `text`, optional): Challenges you're currently facing.
- **Anticipated Obstacles Next Year** (`anticipated_obstacles`, `text`, optional): Challenges you foresee in the next year.
- **Risk Mitigation Strategies** (`risk_mitigation`, `text`, optional): How you plan to address these challenges.

### Financial
- **Planned Budget Allocation** (`budget_allocation`, `text`, optional): How you plan to distribute your resources.

### Strategic Planning
- **Marketing Strategy for Next Year** (`marketing_strategy`, `text`, optional): How you plan to reach and serve your target market.
- **Product or Service Development Plans** (`product_development`, `text`, optional): New or existing services you plan to develop.
- **Customer Retention Strategies** (`customer_retention`, `text`, optional): How you plan to keep your customers.

## Tips

- Be as detailed and honest as possible with your answers.
- If you're unsure about any field, don't hesitate to ask for clarification.
- Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure we understand your needs correctly.
