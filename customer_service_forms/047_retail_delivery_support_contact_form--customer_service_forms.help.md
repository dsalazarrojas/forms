# Retail Delivery Support Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to report issues or concerns related to their retail delivery. Use this form to provide detailed descriptions of the problems you are experiencing and any relevant information that may help us resolve the issue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your contact information, including your e-mail address and phone number, so we can get in touch with you to discuss your issue.
2. Describe the issue you are experiencing with your retail delivery in the "Issue Description" field.
3. If applicable, select the "Delivery Issue" field to specify the nature of the problem.
4. If you have evidence (e.g., photos, videos) that may help us resolve the issue, you can upload it using the "Evidence Upload" field.
5. Select the "Issue Date Range" field to specify when the issue occurred.
6. If you have any additional comments or information about the issue, please use the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Service Contact**: This is a text field where you can enter your name or any other contact information you'd like to provide.
* **Delivery Issue**: This field is used to specify the nature of the issue you're experiencing. If you're unsure, you can leave it blank and describe the issue in the "Issue Description" field.
* **Order Reference**: This field is where you can enter your order number or any other reference number associated with your delivery.
* **Evidence Upload**: This field allows you to upload files such as photos or videos that may help us resolve the issue. You can select multiple files for upload.
	+ Yes: Upload evidence
	+ No: Do not upload evidence
* **Issue Description**: This is a text field where you can describe the issue you are experiencing with your delivery in detail.
* **E-mail**: This field is used to enter your email address so we can get in touch with you to discuss your issue.
* **Phone**: This field is where you can enter your phone number so we can get in touch with you to discuss your issue.
* **Retail Location**: This field is where you can enter the location where you picked up your delivery.
* **Issue Date**: This field is not used in this form. Please disregard.
* **Issue Time**: This field is not used in this form. Please disregard.
* **Issue Date Range**: This field is used to select when the issue occurred. Please choose one of the following options:
	+ Before 1 week
	+ Between 1-2 weeks
	+ After 2 weeks
* **Evidence URL**: This field is not used for uploading files, but rather for entering a URL related to the evidence you are uploading.
* **Additional Comments**: This field is where you can enter any additional information that may be helpful in resolving your issue.
