# Restaurant's Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer feedback about a specific restaurant, allowing the restaurant management to understand customer satisfaction and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the restaurant you are providing feedback about.
2. Rate your experience with the restaurant (1-10).
3. Type your feedback in the text area below.
4. Enter your email address so that we can contact you if needed.
5. If you want to be contacted for further feedback or questions, enter your phone number.
6. (Optional) Select one of the options to provide additional context about your visit (e.g., lunch, dinner, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **restaurant_id**: Enter the restaurant ID number (if known).
* **rating**: Rate your experience with the restaurant (1-10).
* **feedback**: Type your honest and clear feedback about your experience.
* **email**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you if needed.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number to be contacted for further feedback or questions (optional).
* **time**: (Not applicable in this form)
* **date**: (Not applicable in this form)
* **select_one**: Select one of the options to provide additional context about your visit (e.g., lunch, dinner, etc.).
