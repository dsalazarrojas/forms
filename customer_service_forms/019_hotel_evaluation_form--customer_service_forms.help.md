# Hotel Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hotel evaluation form is designed to help us understand your stay experience at our hotel. Please take a few minutes to complete this form and provide your honest feedback about our facilities and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the dates of your stay
2. Choose your room type
3. Rate the quality of the room and other hotel facilities
4. Evaluate the cleanliness and amenities
5. Rate the customer service
6. Consider the value for money
7. Indicate whether you will return to the hotel
8. Leave any additional comments or suggestions

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Stay Date** (`id: 1`, `type: date`, `required: false`): Enter the dates of your stay.
* **Check Out** (`id: 2`, `type: date`, `required: false`): Enter the date you checked out of the hotel.
* **Stay Duration** (`id: 3`, `type: number`, `required: false`): Enter the number of days you stayed at the hotel.
* **Room Type** (`id: 4`, `type: select_one`, `required: false`): Select the type of room you stayed in.
* **Room Number** (`id: 5`, `type: select_multiple`, `required: false`): Select the room number or numbers you stayed in.
* **Room Quality** (`id: 6`, `type: select_one`, `required: false`): Rate the quality of the room.
* **Cleanliness** (`id: 7`, `type: select_one`, `required: false`): Evaluate the cleanliness of the room.
* **Amenities** (`id: 8`, `type: select_multiple`, `required: false`): Select the amenities you used during your stay.
* **Customer Service** (`id: 9`, `type: select_one`, `required: false`): Rate the helpfulness of the hotel staff.
* **Value for Money** (`id: 10`, `type: select_one`, `required: false`): Evaluate the value for money you got from your stay.
* **Will Return** (`id: 11`, `type: select_one`, `required: false`): Indicate whether you will return to the hotel.
* **Recommendation** (`id: 12`, `type: note`, `required: false`): Leave any recommendations or suggestions for the hotel management.
* **Comments** (`id: 13`, `type: note`, `required: false`): Leave any comments about your stay.
* **Phone** (`id: 14`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email** (`id: 15`, `type: email`, `required: false`): Enter your email address.
* **Website** (`id: 16`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the website of your company or organization.
* **Name** (`id: 17`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter your name.
* **Company** (`id: 18`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Address** (`id: 19`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter your business address.
* **City** (`id: 20`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the city of your business.
* **State** (`id: 21`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the state of your business.
* **Zip** (`id: 22`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the zip of your business.
* **Country** (`id: 23`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the country of your business.
* **Submit** (`id: 24`, `type: select_one`, `required: false`): Click the submit button to send your feedback.
* **Note** (`id: 25`, `type: note`, `required: false`): Leave a note about your stay.
