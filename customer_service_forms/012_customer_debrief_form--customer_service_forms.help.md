# Customer Debrief Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for debriefing customers after interactions to gather important details about their experience, concerns, or feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form questions and ensure you have a clear understanding of what is being asked.
2. Provide thorough and honest answers to each question.
3. Submit the form to complete the debriefing process.
