# BEST Seint Color Match - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "BEST Seint Color Match" form is designed to collect information about your skin tone, undertone, eye color, hair color, sun reaction, foundation history, coverage preference, skin type, skin concerns, and preferred finish. This information will help our team create a personalized Seint color match tailored to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out each section according to the instructions provided. Make sure to be as accurate as possible when answering each question to ensure the best possible color match.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name for our records.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where we can send your color match results.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number for potential follow-up questions or concerns.
* **Skin Tone** (`skin_tone`, select_one, required): Select your natural skin tone from the options provided.
* **Skin Undertone** (`skin_undertone`, select_one, required): Choose your undertone based on the options provided.
* **Eye Color** (`eye_color`, select_one, required): Select your natural eye color from the options provided.
* **Hair Color** (`hair_color`, select_one, required): Choose your current hair color from the options provided.
* **How Does Your Skin React to Sun** (`sun_reaction`, select_one, required): Answer how your skin reacts to sun exposure.
* **Foundation Shades Previously Used** (`foundation_history`, text, optional): List any foundation shades that worked well for you in the past.
* **Coverage Preference** (`coverage_preference`, select_one, required): Select how much coverage you prefer for your foundation.
* **Skin Type** (`skin_type`, select_one, required): Choose your skin type from the options provided.
* **Skin Concerns** (`skin_concerns`, select_multiple, required): Select any skin concerns you have from the options provided.
* **Preferred Finish** (`preferred_finish`, select_one, required): Choose the finish you prefer for your foundation.
* **Upload Clear Face Photo** (`photo_upload`, text, optional): Upload a clear face photo in natural lighting with no makeup for better color matching.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Share any additional details that can help us create a better color match for you.

**Tips:** Please answer each question as accurately as possible to help us create the best possible color match for you. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
