# ness_new_guest_amp_covid_19_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from new guests about their COVID-19 status and vaccination history. It helps to identify potential risks and provide necessary precautions to protect our community.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the following required information:
	* **New Guest** (1)
	* **Email** (3)
	* **Phone** (4)
	* **Date** (5)
	* **Time** (6)
2. If applicable, select the following:
	* **Covid Consent** (2): Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have been vaccinated or not.
	* **Have you had Covid-19?** (8): Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have been previously infected.
	* **Vaccination Status** (9): Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' to indicate your current vaccination status.
3. Provide any additional information in the **Note** field (7) if you have any relevant comments or questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Guest** (1): A text field to enter your name.
* **Covid Consent** (2): A dropdown to indicate if you have been vaccinated or not.
* **Email** (3): A field to input your email address.
* **Phone** (4): A field to input your phone number.
* **Date** (5): A date field to select your date of birth or arrival.
* **Time** (6): A time field to select your time of arrival.
* **Note** (7): A text area to provide any additional comments or questions.
* **Have you had Covid-19?** (8): A dropdown to indicate if you have been previously infected.
* **Vaccination Status** (9): A dropdown to select your current vaccination status.
* **Previous Covid Diagnosis** (10): A dropdown to indicate if you have been previously diagnosed with COVID-19.
