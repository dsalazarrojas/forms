# Customer Service Experience Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Service Experience Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience with our customer service team. This form is an essential tool to help us identify areas of improvement and provide a more personalized experience for our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. For questions with multiple select options, choose the one that best describes your experience.
3. Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the "Customer Service Experience Comments" field.
4. Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up or if you'd like to receive a response to your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Customer Service Experience Feedback Form

* **Customer Service Experience Feedback Form** (`page_1`, text, required/optional): This is the first page of the form where you can provide your feedback about our customer service experience. Please write your thoughts and suggestions in this field.
* **Customer Service Satisfaction** (`page_2`, select_one, required/optional): This question asks how satisfied you are with our customer service. Choose one of the options below:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Not Satisfied
* **Customer Service Quality** (`page_3`, select_multiple, required/optional): This question asks about the quality of our customer service. You can select multiple options:
	+ Overall Quality
	+ Response Time
	+ Communication Skills
	+ Product Knowledge
* **Customer Service Experience** (`page_4`, select_one, required/optional): This question asks about your experience with our customer service. Choose one of the options below:
	+ Very Good
	+ Somewhat Good
	+ Not Good
* **Customer Service Improvement** (`page_5`, select_multiple, required/optional): This question asks if there's anything we can improve about our customer service. You can select multiple options:
	+ Improve Response Time
	+ Improve Communication
	+ Improve Product Knowledge
* **Customer Service Experience Comments** (`page_6`, note, required/optional): This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you'd like to make about our customer service experience. Please use this field to provide any specific details or feedback.
* **Customer Service Email** (`page_7`, email, required/optional): This field is for your email address. If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up or if you'd like to receive a response to your feedback, please enter your email address here.
* **Customer Service Phone** (`page_8`, text, required/optional): This field is for your phone number. If you'd like to be contacted by phone or if you'd like to provide a phone number for follow-up, please enter your phone number here.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Customer Service Experience Comments" field.
* If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up or if you'd like to receive a response to your feedback, please enter your email address in the "Customer Service Email" field.
