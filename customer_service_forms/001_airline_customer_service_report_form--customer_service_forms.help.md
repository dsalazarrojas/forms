## Airline Customer Service Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide the airline's customer service team with the necessary information to address and resolve customer incidents or issues that occurred during travel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Provide your personal information, including your full legal name as it appears on your booking and your contact email and phone number.
2.  Enter the flight number that you took, as it appears on your boarding pass or travel documents.
3.  Specify the date of your travel, as it appears on your boarding pass or travel documents.
4.  Select the category of the issue that occurred during your travel.
5.  Describe the incident or issue that occurred, including any relevant details and times.
6.  Specify the severity of the issue as perceived by you.
7.  Provide a clear and concise statement of the desired resolution for the issue.
8.  If applicable, provide the names or descriptions of staff members involved in the incident.
9.  Select whether follow-up is required for your case.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Passenger name** (`passenger_name`, `text`, **required**): Please enter your full legal name as it appears on your booking.
*   **Contact email** (`contact_email`, `email`, **required**): Provide a valid email address for follow-up communication.
*   **Contact phone number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, **optional**): Include your country code if you are outside of your local region.
*   **Flight number** (`flight_number`, `text`, **required**): Enter the flight number that you took as it appears on your boarding pass or travel documents.
*   **Date of travel** (`travel_date`, `date`, **required**): Enter the date of your travel as it appears on your boarding pass or travel documents.
*   **Issue category** (`issue_category`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the primary category that best describes the issue that occurred during your travel.
*   **Incident location** (`incident_location`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the area where the issue occurred, such as gate, boarding, onboard, or other.
*   **Incident description** (`incident_description`, `text`, **required**): Please provide a clear summary of what occurred during your travel, including any relevant details and times.
*   **Severity of issue** (`severity`, `select_one`, **required**): Select how serious the issue was perceived by you.
