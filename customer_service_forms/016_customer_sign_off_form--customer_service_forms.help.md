# Customer Sign Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect basic details from customers, such as terms and conditions acceptance and a signature. It is meant to be filled out by customers when they sign off with your company or for internal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer details in the "customer_details" field.
2. Read and agree to the terms and conditions in the "terms_and_conditions" field.
3. Sign your name in the "signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **customer_details** (`customer_details`, `text`, false): Enter your name and any other relevant details as required by your representative.
* **terms_and_conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, `text`, false): Read and agree to the terms and conditions provided. This is a non-binding agreement that outlines the rules and expectations for our business relationship.
* **signature** (`signature`, `note`, false): Sign your name in the space provided to confirm your acceptance of the terms and conditions and agreement to our business relationship.

## Tips
* Make sure to read the terms and conditions carefully before signing off.
* If you're unsure about any of the terms, ask your representative for clarification.
* Remember to sign your name clearly in the "signature" field.
