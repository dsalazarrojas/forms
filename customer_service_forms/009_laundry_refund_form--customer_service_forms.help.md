# Laundry Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The laundry refund form is used to report any issues with our laundry service and request a refund.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant service issue from the form.
2. Enter your name and contact number so we can reach out to you for further information.
3. Choose a reason for the refund request.
4. Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Service Issue**: <br>
	* This field is where you can describe the issue with our laundry service. Please provide as much detail as possible about the problem you experienced.
- **Customer Name**: <br>
	* This is your name as it appears on your account or the name of the person requesting the refund.
- **Contact Number**: <br>
	* This is your contact phone number where you can be reached to discuss your refund request.
- **Refund Reason**: <br>
	* Please select the reason for your refund request from the available options (e.g., dirty or damaged clothes, missing items, etc.).
- **Email**: <br>
	* This is your email address where we can contact you regarding your refund request.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form as completely and accurately as possible to help us process your refund request efficiently.
* If you have any supporting documents or evidence of the issue, please attach them to your form or provide them to us separately.
* We will review and respond to your form submission as soon as possible after receiving it.
