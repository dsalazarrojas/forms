# credit_card_statement_communication_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to communicate their credit card statement information and any disputes or issues related to their transactions. It is designed to help our support team to efficiently address your inquiry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the form fields with your credit card statement information.
2. Enter your contact details so we can get in touch with you.
3. Provide a detailed description of the issue or dispute you are experiencing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Credit Card Statement Communication Form** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title for your credit card statement communication.
* **Category** (`form_category`, text, optional): Select the category related to your credit card statement communication.
* **Description** (`form_description`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of your credit card statement communication.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your credit card statement.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for communication.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can get in touch with you.
* **Transaction Date** (`transaction_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the transaction related to your credit card statement communication.
* **Transaction Amount** (`transaction_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of the transaction.
* **Transaction Details** (`transaction_details`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of the transaction.
* **Dispute Type** (`dispute_type`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Dispute Type: Select all that apply.
	+ Dispute Reason: Enter the reason for the dispute.
	+ Dispute Description: Enter a detailed description of the dispute.
* **Dispute Description** (`dispute_description`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of the dispute.

## Tips
* Please fill in all the required fields to ensure our team can assist you efficiently.
* Be as detailed as possible in your description to help us understand your issue.
* If you have any supporting documents, please attach them to your submission.
* Our team will contact you at the email address you provided to discuss further about your credit card statement communication.
