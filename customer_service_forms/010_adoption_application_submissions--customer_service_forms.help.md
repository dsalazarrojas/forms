# Adoption Application Submissions - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Adoption Application Submissions form is used to gather relevant information from potential adopters to help our organization make informed decisions about their adoption application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the submission date of your application.
2. Provide your full name and email address.
3. Enter your phone number.
4. Complete your home address.
5. Specify the type of pet you are interested in adopting.
6. If you are renting, provide your landlord's approval status.
7. Indicate your previous pet ownership experience and any references you would like to provide.
8. Confirm your ability to afford veterinary care for the pet.
9. State your reasons for wanting to adopt a pet.
10. Commit to caring for the pet for a certain amount of time.
11. Select the status of your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submission Date**: Enter the date you submitted your application.
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it is currently used.
* **Email Address**: Enter your current email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your current phone number.
* **Home Address**: Enter your current home address.
* **Pet You Are Interested In**: Choose the type of pet you are interested in adopting.
* **Household Composition**: Enter the number of adults and children in your household.
* **Ages of Children in Household**: Enter the ages of any children in the household (optional).
* **Other Pets in Household**: Describe any current pets you have (optional).
* **Housing Type**: Choose the type of housing you have.
* **Landlord Approval (if renting)**: If you are a renter, select your landlord's approval status.
* **Previous Pet Ownership Experience**: Describe your previous pet ownership experience.
* **Veterinarian Name and Phone**: For reference purposes, enter the name and phone number of your veterinarian.
* **Personal Reference Name and Phone**: For reference purposes, enter the name and phone number of a personal reference.
* **Can you afford veterinary care?**: Confirm that you can afford veterinary care for the pet.
* **Why do you want to adopt a pet?**: Provide a clear and concise reason for wanting to adopt a pet.
* **How long do you commit to caring for this pet?**: Choose how long you commit to caring for the pet.
* **Application Status**: Select the current status of your application.

Note: All fields marked as "required" are necessary for the adoption process.
