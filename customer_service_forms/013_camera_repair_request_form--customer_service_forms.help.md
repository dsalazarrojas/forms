# Camera Repair Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camera Repair Request Form is a submission form for customers seeking camera repair services. It allows you to provide detailed information about the issue with your camera, enabling our repair team to quickly and accurately diagnose and repair the problem.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name as the person requesting repair in the "Customer Name" field.
2.  Provide your contact phone number and email address in the "Contact Phone" and "Contact Email" fields respectively for us to contact you regarding the repair status.
3.  Specify the model of your camera and its serial number if available.
4.  Describe the problem or damage in the "Problem Description" field.
5.  Indicate your urgency level (Routine, Urgent, or Emergency) in the "Urgency Level" field.
6.  If you have a preferred date to bring the camera in for repair, enter it in the "Preferred Drop Off Date" field. This is optional but helps us manage our schedule.
7.  Confirm that you consent to perform diagnostics and provide a cost estimate by selecting "True" in the "Consent to Perform Diagnostic and Provide Estimate" field.
8.  If you have any additional notes or information that might aid the repair process, enter them in the "Additional Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the person submitting this request. This will help us address any communication or correspondence.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number so we can call or text you about your repair status and any other relevant updates.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address to receive repair status updates and any other necessary information from us.
* **Camera Model**: Please specify the model of your camera, including brand and type. This helps our technicians quickly identify the correct spare parts and repair methods.
* **Serial Number**: If you have the serial number of your camera, please provide it. This can help us expedite the repair process, especially if your camera is under warranty or requires special handling.
* **Problem Description**: Describe the issue with your camera, including any symptoms you've observed, such as distorted images, malfunctioning parts, or other problems. This will guide the repair team to identify the correct repair approach.
* **Urgency Level**: Select one of the three urgency levels: Routine, Urgent, or Emergency. This helps us prioritize repairs and manage our workload.
* **Preferred Drop Off Date**: If you have a preferred date to bring in your camera for repair, enter it here. This helps us schedule repairs more efficiently.
* **Consent to Perform Diagnostic and Provide Estimate**: Select "True" to allow our team to perform a diagnostic and provide a cost estimate for the repairs. This is a requirement for proceeding with the repair.
* **Additional Notes**: If there's anything else you'd like to share with us to help with the repair, such as recent software updates or other relevant details, please enter it here. This can aid our team in diagnosing and addressing the issue effectively.
