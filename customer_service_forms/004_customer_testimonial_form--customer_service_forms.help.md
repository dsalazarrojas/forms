# Customer Testimonial Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and testimonials from customers about their overall experience with the business.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the question "What is your overall experience with our business?" in the text field. You can describe your positive or negative experience, and any suggestions or comments about our company.
2. Write a testimonial about our company in the text field below. This will help us understand your thoughts and feelings about our business.
3. Rate your experience with our company on the scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
4. Decide whether you would recommend our business to others. Select 'True' if you would recommend us, and 'False' if you would not.
5. Enter your email address in the text field. This will help us contact you if we need to follow up on your testimonial.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your overall experience with our business?** (testimonial_form, text, optional):
	+ This field is a text box where you can describe your overall experience with our business.
* **Please write testimonial about our company** (testimonial, text, optional):
	+ This field is a text box where you can express your thoughts and feelings about our company.
* **Rate your experience with our company** (testimonial_score, number, optional):
	+ This field is a scale where you can rate your experience with our company from 1 to 5.
* **Would you recommend our business?** (recommended, select_one, optional):
	+ Select 'True' if you would recommend our business, and 'False' if you would not.
* **Your Email** (email_address, email, optional):
	+ This field is where you enter your email address, which we may use to contact you about your testimonial.
