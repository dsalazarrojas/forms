# CMS - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer information and issue details to provide efficient support and resolution.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Please enter your full name as the customer.
2.  Select the preferred contact method (Phone, Email, or SMS) for the support team to reach you.
3.  Choose the category of the issue (Billing, Product, Service, Technical, or Other).
4.  Provide a detailed description of the problem you are experiencing.
5.  If relevant, enter the order number related to the issue.
6.  Indicate the urgency level of the issue (Low, Medium, or High).
7.  Enter any additional notes for internal use by the support team.
8.  Choose the current status of the resolution (Open, In progress, Resolved, or Closed).
9.  If applicable, select the date the issue was closed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, Required): Enter your full name as the customer.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, Optional): Select how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or SMS).
* **Issue Type** (`issue_type`, select_one, Required): Category of the customer issue (Billing, Product, Service, Technical, or Other).
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, text, Required): Describe the problem in detail.
* **Order Number** (`order_number`, text, Optional): If related to a purchase, include the order number.
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency`, select_one, Optional): Select the urgency level of the issue (Low, Medium, or High).
* **Assigned Agent** (`assigned_agent`, text, Optional): Note who is handling the case internally.
* **Resolution Notes** (`resolution_notes`, text, Optional): Enter any additional notes for the support team.
* **Resolution Status** (`resolution_status`, select_one, Required): Current status of the ticket (Open, In progress, Resolved, or Closed).
* **Closed Date** (`closed_date`, date, Optional): Date the issue was closed if applicable.
