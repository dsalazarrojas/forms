# Complaint Response Time Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and measure the time it takes to respond to customer complaints.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant fields to fill out based on your experience with the complaint.
2. Fill out each field with accurate and relevant information.
3. Make sure to answer all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tracking Date** (`tracking_date`): Date of tracking entry, which is the date and time you filled out this form.
* **Tracker Name** (`tracker_name`): Name of the person logging this complaint.
* **Department** (`tracker_department`): Department that handled the complaint.
* **Complaint ID** (`complaint_id`): Reference number for the complaint.
* **Complaint Received Date** (`complaint_received_date`): Date the complaint was first received.
* **Complaint Received Time** (`complaint_received_time`): Time the complaint was first received.
* **First Response Date** (`first_response_date`): Date of the first response to the complaint.
* **First Response Time** (`first_response_time`): Time of the first response.
* **Response Time in Hours** (`response_time_hours`): Number of hours taken to respond.
* **SLA Target** (`sla_target`): Target time to respond as per SLA.
* **SLA Status** (`sla_status`): Status of the response time in relation to the SLA target.
* **Reason for SLA Miss** (`miss_reason`): Reason for missing the SLA target (if applicable).
* **Complaint Priority** (`complaint_priority`): Priority assigned to the complaint.
* **Complaint Category** (`complaint_category`): Category of the complaint.
* **Response Method** (`response_method`): Method used to respond to the complaint.
* **Responder Name** (`responder_name`): Name of the person responding to the complaint.
* **Responder Role** (`responder_role`): Role of the person responding to the complaint.
* **Resolution Date** (`resolution_date`): Date of resolution of the complaint.
* **Total Resolution Time** (`resolution_time_hours`): Number of hours taken to resolve the complaint.
* **Resolution SLA Status** (`resolution_sla_status`): Status of the resolution time in relation to the SLA target.
* **Customer Satisfaction with Wait** (`customer_wait_satisfaction`): Customer satisfaction with the wait time.
* **Delays Encountered** (`delays_encountered`): Number of delays encountered while responding.
* **Delay Reasons** (`delay_reasons`): Reasons for delays (if applicable).
* **Number of Escalations** (`escalations`): Number of times the complaint was escalated.
* **Number of Handoffs** (`handoffs`): Number of handoffs made while responding.
* **Business Hours Calculation** (`business_hours_only`): Whether the response time is calculated in business hours only.
* **Weekend or Holiday Impact** (`weekend_holiday_impact`): Impact of weekends or holidays on the response time.
* **Improvement Suggestions** (`improvement_suggestions`): Suggestions for improvement.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`): Any additional notes on the complaint.

Note: The fields marked as required must be filled out in order to complete the form.
