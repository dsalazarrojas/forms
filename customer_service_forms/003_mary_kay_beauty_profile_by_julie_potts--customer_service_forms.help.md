# mary_kay_beauty_profile_by_julie_potts - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for customers who want to create a profile with Mary Kay Beauty by Julie Potts. We collect your information to provide personalized services and offers.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill in the required information below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mary Kay Beauty Profile** (`mary_kay_beauty_profile`, text, required): Please provide a brief description of your experience with Mary Kay Beauty.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in our system.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address. This will be used to send you personalized offers and updates.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for additional contact options.
* **Customer Experience** (`customer_experience`, text, optional): Share your experience with our products or services for our review and reference.
* **Consultant Name** (`consultant_name`, text, optional): If you are working with a Mary Kay Beauty consultant, enter their name here.
* **Additional comments** (not listed in inputSchema): If you have any additional information or requests, please enter them in the space below.
