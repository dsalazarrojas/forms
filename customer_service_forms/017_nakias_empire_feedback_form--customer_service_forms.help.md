# Nakias Empire Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nakias Empire Feedback Form is designed to be used by customers to provide feedback on the services provided by the company. This form will help the team at Nakias Empire to understand customer needs, concerns, and preferences, which can be used to make improvements and enhance the overall customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of feedback you want to provide.
2. Fill in your contact information (name, email, and phone number, if applicable).
3. Provide a detailed description of your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Type** (`feedback_type`, select_one, required: false): Please select the type of feedback you want to give us. This helps us understand the nature of your feedback.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Fill in your name so we know who you are.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Provide your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): If you'd like to be contacted, please fill in your phone number.
* **Message** (`message_form`, note, required: false): Provide a detailed description of your feedback, including any suggestions or concerns you may have.

**Tips**

* Be honest and clear in your feedback.
* Use specific examples or details to help us understand your point.
* Don't worry if the form has any issues, we're here to help!
* If you have any technical issues or bugs, please use the designated bug reporting process.
