# photo model release - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photo Model Release form is used to obtain consent from customers or models for the usage of their images in various photo shoots, events, or situations where images are captured.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer's name.
2. Provide the date of the photo shoot or event.
3. Enter the customer's email address.
4. Provide their phone number.
5. Describe the photo release terms.
6. Sign and date the photo release.
7. Sign as a witness (if required).
8. Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional)
Enter the name of the customer or model whose image is being captured.

### **Date** (`date`, date, optional)
Provide the date of the photo shoot or event.

### **Email** (`email`, email, optional)
Enter the customer's email address.

### **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional)
Provide the customer's phone number.

### **Photo Release** (`photo_release`, text, optional)
Describe the terms of the photo release, including the intended use of the images and any specific conditions or restrictions.

### **Release Date** (`photo_release_date`, date, optional)
Enter the date of the photo release.

### **Model Details** (`model_details`, text, optional)
Provide any additional details about the model, such as their contact information or any specific requirements.

### **Agreement** (`agreement`, note, optional)
This field is reserved for any additional comments, terms, or agreements between the model and the photographer or event organizer.

### **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, text, optional)
Sign the form to indicate consent to the photo release terms.

### **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`, text, optional)
If required, sign as a witness to the photo release terms.

## Tips
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure accurate and complete information.
* Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and truthfully.
* Understand the implications of the photo release terms before signing.
* Obtain the necessary signatures to validate the photo release.
* Keep a copy of the completed form for your records.
