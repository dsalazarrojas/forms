# Sound Quality Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sound Quality Complaint Form is used to report sound quality issues to the customer support team. If you are experiencing a problem with sound quality, please fill out this form to provide us with the necessary information to investigate and resolve the issue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the "Customer Name" and "Customer Email" fields, respectively.
2. Describe the sound quality issue you are experiencing in the "Complaint Description" field.
3. Provide the date and time you encountered the issue in the "Issue Date" and "Issue Time" fields, respectively.
4. Specify your preferred sound quality attributes:
	* "Sound Quality Issue" field: Describe the issue you are experiencing (e.g., low volume, static noise).
	* "Sound Frequency" field: If relevant, enter the frequency of the sound issue.
	* "Sound Intensity" field: If relevant, enter the intensity of the sound issue.
	* "Sound Duration" field: If relevant, enter how long the sound issue lasted.
5. Attach any relevant files or recordings to the form by pasting the URL or uploading a file to the "Attachment" field.
6. Click the "Submit" button to send the form to the customer support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Name
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Customer Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for further questions or updates.

*   **Complaint Description**: Provide a detailed description of the sound quality issue you are experiencing. For example, "My music playback is too quiet" or "I hear static in the background."

*   **Issue Date**: Enter the date you first encountered the sound quality issue.

*   **Issue Time**: Enter the time you first encountered the sound quality issue.

*   **Sound Quality Issue**: Describe the specific sound quality issue you are experiencing, such as "low volume" or "static noise".

*   **Sound Frequency**: If relevant, enter the frequency of the sound issue (e.g., 100 Hz).

*   **Sound Intensity**: If relevant, enter the intensity of the sound issue (e.g., 3 out of 10).

*   **Sound Duration**: If relevant, enter how long the sound issue lasted (e.g., 10 seconds).

*   **Attachment**: If you have a file or recording related to the sound quality issue, attach it here by pasting the URL or uploading a file.

## Tips
*   If you have any additional information or clarification, please provide it in the "Complaint Description" field.
*   Please ensure your contact information is accurate so we can reach out to you for further questions or updates.
*   If you have a file or recording related to the sound quality issue, attach it to the form.
