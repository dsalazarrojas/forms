# notice_of_cancellation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "notice_of_cancellation_form" is used to collect information from customers about their intention to cancel an existing agreement or service. This form helps gather specific details about the cancellation, making it easier to process and manage cancellations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Cancellation Details** (`cancellation_details`, `text`, required): This field is for providing a brief description of the cancellation.
2. **Reason For Cancellation** (`reason_for_cancellation`, `text`, required): Please select a clear and concise reason for the cancellation.
3. **Effective Date** (`effective_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date on which the cancellation will take effect.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Cancellation Details**: Enter a brief description of the cancellation, such as "Service not needed anymore" or "Other reason not specified".
* **Reason For Cancellation**: Choose a reason that best describes the cancellation, such as "Insufficient support" or "Unsatisfied with service".
* **Effective Date**: Enter the date on which the cancellation will take effect, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
