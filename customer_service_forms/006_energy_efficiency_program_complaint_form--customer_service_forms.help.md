# Energy Efficiency Program Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Energy Efficiency Program Complaint Form is used to collect and document customer complaints, ensuring that they are resolved efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "Customer Complaint" field with a clear and concise description of the issue you are experiencing.
2. Select the relevant "Energy Type" to indicate the type of energy you are using (e.g., Electric, Gas, or Other).
3. If applicable, choose the "Program Services" that are related to your complaint (e.g., Energy Savings, Rebates, or Energy Audits).
4. Enter your "Customer Name" and "Contact Number" so that we can get back to you.
5. Provide a brief description of your complaint in the "Complaint Description" field.
6. If you have any additional comments or information, enter them in the "Additional Comments" field.
7. Finally, indicate the status of your complaint by selecting "Is Escalated" and "Is Resolved" accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Complaint**: A brief description of your issue with the Energy Efficiency Program.
* **Energy Type**: The type of energy you are using (e.g., Electric, Gas, or Other).
* **Program Services**: Select the services that are related to your complaint (e.g., Energy Savings, Rebates, or Energy Audits).
* **Customer Name**: Your name, so we can get back to you.
* **Contact Number**: Your contact number, so we can reach you.
* **Complaint Description**: A detailed description of your issue.
* **Additional Comments**: Any additional information or comments you have.
* **Is Escalated**: Indicate if your complaint has been escalated to a higher level of support.
* **Is Resolved**: Indicate if your complaint has been resolved.
* **Customer Service Email**: Not a field for customer entry, but for customer service use only.
* **Customer Service Phone**: Not a field for customer entry, but for customer service use only.
* **Date Committed To**: A date field for customer service use only, not for customer entry.
* **Customer Service Hours**: A time field for customer service use only, not for customer entry.
* **Customer Service Notes**: A field for customer service use only, not for customer entry.

Note: The fields marked as "for customer service use only" are not visible to customers and are used internally by the customer service team to manage and track complaints.
