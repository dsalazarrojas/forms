# heroes_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Heroes Survey form is designed to collect basic information about a person. This form is likely used in customer-facing contexts, such as customer service, onboarding, or sales.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Heroes Survey form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Last name** in the second field.
3. Enter your **Email** in the format of a standard email address (e.g., `john.doe@example.com`).
4. Enter your **Contact Number** (e.g., phone or phone extension).
5. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the standard date format (e.g., `MM/DD/YYYY`).
6. Enter your **Time of Birth** in the 24-hour format (e.g., `HH:MM`).
7. Provide any additional **Notes** about yourself.
8. Enter your **Phone** number (e.g., a phone number or phone extension).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a standard email address (e.g., `john.doe@example.com`).
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, number, optional): Enter your phone number or phone extension.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the date of your birth in the standard date format (e.g., `MM/DD/YYYY`).
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, optional): Enter the time of your birth in the 24-hour format (e.g., `HH:MM`).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or information about yourself.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number or phone extension.

## Tips
* Be sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure the best experience.
* If you have any issues or need assistance, feel free to contact us.
