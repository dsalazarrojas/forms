# Customer Visit Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document customer visits and gather feedback on the service provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Visit Details" page.
2. Enter the visit date in the "Visit Date" field.
3. Select the visit time in the format "HH:MM:SS".
4. Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
5. Enter the customer's contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
6. Enter the customer's email address in the "Email" field.
7. Select the service provided from the drop-down menu (Service A, Service B, or Service C).
8. Enter any additional notes about the service provided in the "Service Notes" field.
9. Rate the customer's overall experience from the options provided (Very Good, Good, Average, or Very Poor).
10. Enter any additional comments or feedback in the "Additional Comments" field.
11. Attach any relevant files or documents to the form using the "Attachments" field.
12. Select the type of attachment (Attachment 1, Attachment 2, Attachment 3, or Attachment 4).
13. Select the overall experience from the options provided (Very Good, Good, Average, or Very Poor).
14. Indicate if you would recommend the service to the customer (True or False).
15. Enter a brief explanation for your recommendation in the "Would Recommend Why" field.
16. Enter a summary of the visit in the "Visit Summary" field.
17. Enter any additional information about the visit in the "Additional Information" field.
18. Enter any comments or feedback about the visit in the "Comments" field.
19. Indicate if follow-up is required (True or False).
20. Enter the date for follow-up in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Visit Details**: Enter a brief description of the visit.
* **Visit Date**: Enter the date of the visit in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Visit Time**: Enter the time of the visit in HH:MM:SS format.
* **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's name.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the customer's contact number.
* **Email**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **Service Provided**: Select the type of service provided from the drop-down menu.
* **Service Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the service provided.
* **Visit Rating**: Rate the customer's overall experience from the options provided.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about the visit.
* **Attachments**: Attach any relevant files or documents to the form.
* **Attachment 1-4**: Attach specific files or documents related to the visit.
* **Overall Experience**: Rate the customer's overall experience from the options provided.
* **Would Recommend**: Indicate if you would recommend the service to the customer.
* **Would Recommend Why**: Enter a brief explanation for your recommendation.
* **Visit Summary**: Enter a summary of the visit.
* **Additional Information**: Enter any additional information about the visit.
* **Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback about the visit.
* **Follow Up Required**: Indicate if follow-up is required.
* **Follow Up Date**: Enter the date for follow-up in YYYY-MM-DD format.
