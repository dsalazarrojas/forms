# Fall/Winter 2020 Color Street Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to process orders for the Fall/Winter 2020 Color Street campaign. Please fill out the form with your customer information and preferences to place an order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information ready.
2. Fill out the fields with your customer information and preferences.
3. Make sure to enter accurate and complete information, as incorrect or incomplete information may cause delays in processing your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, Text, Not Required): Enter the customer's name, as listed on their card.
* **Color Streeets Line** (`color_streets_line`, Select One, Not Required): Check 'Yes' if you would like to include the Color Streeets Line product in your order.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, Text, Not Required): Enter the customer's shipping address.
* **Credit Card Information** (`credit_card`, Text, Not Required): Enter the customer's credit card number.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, Email, Not Required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Text, Not Required): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Color Streeets Code** (`color_streets_code`, Text, Not Required): Enter the Color Streeets Code found in the Color Streeets catalog.

## Tips
* Please double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you need assistance, don't hesitate to contact our customer service team.
