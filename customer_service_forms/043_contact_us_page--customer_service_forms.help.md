# Contact Us Page - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers about their contact reasons, preferences, and details regarding their inquiry or issue. By filling out this form, customers can get in touch with us and receive assistance with their concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form in a new tab or window.
2.  Enter your customer name and email address in the respective fields.
3.  Choose the reason for your contact from the options provided.
4.  Select the product or service related to your inquiry.
5.  Describe your issue or question in brief.
6.  Provide detailed information about your issue.
7.  Indicate if you have contacted us before or not.
8.  Optionally provide a reference ID if you have one.
9.  Choose your preferred method of communication.
10. Specify your availability for contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (2, Customer Name, required): Please enter your full name here.
* **Email Address** (3, Email Address, required): Enter your email address here.
* **Phone Number** (4, Phone Number, optional): If you prefer to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number here.
* **Account ID** (5, Account ID, optional): If you are a registered customer, enter your account ID here.
* **Reason for Contact** (6, Reason for Contact, required): Select the category that best describes your reason for contacting us.
* **Product or Service** (7, Product or Service, optional): Choose the product or service related to your inquiry.
* **Issue Summary** (8, Issue Summary, required): Briefly describe your issue or question.
* **Detailed Description** (9, Detailed Description, required): Provide a more detailed description of your issue.
* **Have You Contacted Us Before** (10, Have You Contacted Us Before, optional): Indicate if you have contacted us before. If yes, you can leave your previous contact's reference ID if applicable.
* **Previous Reference ID** (11, Previous Reference ID, optional): If you have a previous reference ID, enter it here.
* **Preferred Response** (12, Preferred Response, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted (email, phone call, or text message).
* **Best Time to Reach You** (13, Best Time to Reach You, optional): Indicate your availability for contact.

## Tips
- Please be as brief and clear as possible when describing your issue.
- If you have a reference ID from a previous contact, it will help us quickly look up your request.
- Choose your preferred response method for faster communication.
- If you are available during specific hours, please indicate so we can plan accordingly.
