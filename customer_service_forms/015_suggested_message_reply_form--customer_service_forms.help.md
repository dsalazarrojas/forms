# Suggested Message Reply Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Suggested Message Reply Form is used to create standardized messages that can be sent to customers in response to their inquiries.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the team who will be sending the reply: Choose from Team, Team Members, or Managers.
2. Set the submission type: Choose from Email, Message, or Social.
3. Choose how consistently you reply to similar inquiries: Choose from Always, Sometimes, or Never.
4. Select the social media platform to reply on: Choose from Facebook, Twitter, or Instagram.
5. Set the submission status: Choose from In Progress, Sent, or Draft.
6. Set the priority of the reply: Choose from High, Low, or Medium.
7. Enter any additional notes about the reply.
8. Enter your name, email, and department (if applicable) to attribute the reply.
9. Enter the date and time of submission.
10. Finally, select the team or individual who submitted the reply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team** (`Teams`, `Select One`, **required**: false)
	+ Select the team who will be sending the reply.
* **Submission Type** (`submission_type`, `Select One`, **required**: false)
	+ Set the type of submission: Email, Message, or Social.
* **Consistent Replies** (`consistent_replies`, `Select One`, **required**: false)
	+ Choose how consistently you reply to similar inquiries: Always, Sometimes, or Never.
* **Social Inquiries** (`social_inquiries`, `Select Multiple`, **required**: false)
	+ Select the social media platforms to reply on: Customer Service, Support, Help and Support.
* **Replies** (`consistent_replies`, `Select One`, **required**: false)
	+ Set the consistency of replies to similar inquiries: Always, Sometimes, or Never.
* **Reply to Email** (`email_preferences`, `Text`, **required**: false)
	+ Enter any additional notes about the reply.
* **Reply to Social** (`social_media`, `Select One`, **required**: false)
	+ Enter a note about the reply.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, `Select One`, **required**: false)
	+ Set the submission status: In Progress, Sent, or Draft.
* **Priority** (`priority`, `Select One`, **required**: false)
	+ Set the priority of the reply: High, Low, or Medium.
* **Form Submitter** (`form_submitter`, `Text`, **required**: false)
	+ Enter your name to attribute the reply.
* **Form Submitter Email** (`form_submitter_email`, `Email`, **required**: false)
	+ Enter your email to attribute the reply.
* **Form Submitter Phone** (`form_submitter_phone`, `Text`, **required**: false)
	+ Enter your phone number to attribute the reply.
* **Form Submitter Department** (`form_submitter_department`, `Select One`, **required**: false)
	+ Select the department who submitted the reply: Sales, Marketing, Customer Support.
* **Submission Datetime** (`submission_datetime`, `Date`, **required**: false)
	+ Enter the date and time of submission.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, `Time`, **required**: false)
	+ Enter the time of submission.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `Note`, **required**: false)
	+ Enter any additional notes about the reply.
* **Draft Reply** (`draft`, `Text`, **required**: false)
	+ Enter a draft reply.
* **Submission Type** (`team`, `Select One`, **required**: false)
	+ Select the team or individual who submitted the reply.
