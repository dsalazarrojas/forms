# Suggested Message Reply Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Suggested Message Reply Form is used to create standardized messages that can be sent to customers in response to their inquiries.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the team who will be sending the reply: Choose from Team, Team Members, or Managers.
2. Set the submission type: Choose from Email, Message, or Social.
3. Choose how consistently you reply to similar inquiries: Choose from Always, Sometimes, or Never.
4. Select the social media platform to reply on: Choose from Facebook, Twitter, or Instagram.
5. Set the submission status: Choose from In Progress, Sent, or Draft.
6. Set the priority of the reply: Choose from High, Low, or Medium.
7. Enter any additional notes about the reply.
8. Enter your name, email, and department (if applicable) to attribute the reply.
9. Enter the date and time of submission.
10. Finally, select the team or individual who submitted the reply.
