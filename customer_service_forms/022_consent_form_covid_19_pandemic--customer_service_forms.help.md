# Consent Form COVID 19 Pandemic - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain a patient's consent for treatment or services during the COVID-19 pandemic. It gathers information about the patient's health status, travel history, and ability to follow safety protocols, as well as their understanding and agreement to receive services during the pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the patient's information (Section 1).
2. Answer the screening questions (Section 2).
3. Select your response to the screening questions (Section 3-5).
4. Agree to the terms of service (Section 6).
5. Provide your electronic signature and date (Sections 7-8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Full Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Fill in the patient's full name as it appears on their identification.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`patient_phone`, text, required): Enter the patient's phone number where they can be reached.
* **Are you currently experiencing fever, cough, or shortness of breath?** (`current_symptoms`, select_one, required): Select 'True' if you are experiencing any of these symptoms, 'False' otherwise.
* **Have you traveled internationally in the last 14 days?** (`travel_history`, select_one, required): Select 'True' if you have traveled internationally in the last 14 days, 'False' otherwise.
* **Have you been in close contact with anyone diagnosed with COVID-19?** (`contact_history`, select_one, required): Select 'True' if you have been in close contact with anyone diagnosed with COVID-19, 'False' otherwise.
* **I understand the risks of receiving services during the pandemic** (`service_risk_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Select 'I understand and acknowledge' if you are aware of the risks, 'Other' otherwise.
* **I agree to follow all office safety protocols (masks, distancing)** (`safety_protocol_agreement`, select_one, required): Select 'I agree' if you agree to follow the safety protocols, 'Other' otherwise.
* **I am aware that telehealth may be an alternative to in-person visits** (`telehealth_option_awareness`, select_one, required): Select 'I am aware' if you are aware of telehealth as an alternative, 'Other' otherwise.
* **Do you consent to proceed with the treatment/service?** (`final_consent_check`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes, I consent' if you agree to proceed with the treatment or service, 'No, I do not consent' otherwise.
* **Electronic Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Provide your electronic signature.
* **Date** (`date_signed`, date, required): Enter the date you signed the form.

## Tips
Please make sure to answer truthfully and accurately. If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
