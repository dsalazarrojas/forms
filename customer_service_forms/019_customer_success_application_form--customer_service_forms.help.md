# customer_success_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your business for our customer success programs and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your company name.
2. Provide the name of the contact person.
3. Enter the contact person's email address.
4. Enter the contact person's phone number.
5. Select whether your business is of type "Yes" or "No".
6. Enter your business address.
7. Enter your city.
8. Enter your state.
9. Enter your zip code.
10. Enter your job title.
11. Enter a brief description of your business.
12. Select whether your business has social media presence on various platforms.
13. Select the year your business was founded.
14. Enter your business annual revenue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company name.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Person Email** (`contact_person_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Contact Person Phone** (`contact_person_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether your business is "Yes" or "No".
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your business address.
* **Business City** (`business_city`, `text`, required: false): Enter your city.
* **Business State** (`business_state`, `text`, required: false): Enter your state.
* **Business Zip** (`business_zip`, `text`, required: false): Enter your zip code.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter your job title.
* **Business Description** (`business_description`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of your business.
* **Business Industry** (`business_industry`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether your business is "Yes" or "No".
* **Business Website** (`business_website`, `text`, required: false): Enter your business website.
* **Facebook** (`business_facebook`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether your business has a Facebook presence.
* **Twitter** (`business_twitter`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether your business has a Twitter presence.
* **LinkedIn** (`business_linkedin`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether your business has a LinkedIn presence.
* **Instagram** (`business_instagram`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether your business has an Instagram presence.
* **YouTube** (`business_youtube`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether your business has a YouTube presence.
* **Tumblr** (`business_tumblr`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether your business has a Tumblr presence.
* **Pinterest** (`business_pinterest`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether your business has a Pinterest presence.
* **Flickr** (`business_flickr`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether your business has a Flickr presence.
* **Twitch** (`business_twitch`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether your business has a Twitch presence.
* **Angel Listed** (`business_angel_listed`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select whether your business is "Yes" or "No".
* **Founded Date** (`business_founded_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the year your business was founded.
* **Annual Revenue** (`business_annual_revenue`, `number`, required: false): Enter your business annual revenue.
