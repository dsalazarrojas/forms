# Tombstone Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Tombstone Order Form is a tool to help customers provide the necessary information to place an order for a headstone. This form will collect customer details such as name, address, contact information, and other relevant information to process their order.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information to complete the form.
2. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required") with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Use the options provided for fields with select options (marked as "select_one") to choose the correct response.
4. Ensure that all fields are filled in before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Service Forms** (id: 1): This field is a note field where customers can provide any additional comments or information related to their order.
* **tombstone order form** (id: 2): This is the main field where customers can provide the details of their order, such as the type of headstone they are interested in.
* **name** (id: 3): Please fill in your full name as it should appear on the headstone.
* **email** (id: 4): Please provide your email address so we can contact you about your order.
* **phone** (id: 5): Please provide your phone number so we can contact you about your order.
* **address** (id: 6): Please fill in your address as it should appear on the headstone.
* **city** (id: 7): Please provide the city where you would like the headstone delivered.
* **state** (id: 8): Please choose whether you would like to include state on the headstone. Options are: "Yes" or "No".
* **zip** (id: 9): Please provide your zip code as it should appear on the headstone.
* **date of death** (id: 10): Please provide the date of death as it should appear on the headstone.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact our customer service team.
* Please review your order carefully before submitting it to ensure it is correct.
