# data_entry_form_2019_ventered_limited - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect customer information and service requests for the 2019 Ventered Limited product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email** in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your **Phone Number**.
3. Select a **Customer Service Type** from the dropdown list.
4. Enter your **Date of Birth** and **Birth Time**.
5. Select your **Assigned Tool** from the dropdown list.
6. Enter the **Date** and **Time** of your **Customer Service Request** if applicable.
7. Add any **Notes** if needed.
8. Select the **Data Type** and **Data Source** of the request.
9. Enter the **Customer Service Request** description.
10. Add any additional **Customer Service Request Notes** if needed.
11. Enter any **Other Notes** if needed.
11. Enter your **Submitted By Name**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Birth Time** (`birth_time`, time, optional): Enter your birth time in 12-hour format (e.g., 09:00 AM).
* **Customer Service Type** (`customer_service_type`, select_one, optional): Select one of the provided options for the type of customer service required.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your request.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select the tool assigned for this request.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, date, optional): The date and time this form was submitted.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter your name as the person submitting this request.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the provided options for notes about the customer.
* **Data Type** (`data_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the provided options for the type of data.
* **Data Source** (`data_source`, select_one, optional): Select one of the provided options for the source of the data.
* **Customer Service Request** (`customer_service_request`, text, optional): Enter a description of the customer service request.
* **Customer Service Request Date** (`customer_service_request_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the customer service request.
* **Customer Service Request Time** (`customer_service_request_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the customer service request.
* **Customer Service Request Notes** (`customer_service_request_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the customer service request.
* **Other Notes** (`other_notes`, note, optional): Enter any other notes related to the request.
* **Customer Service Request** (`customer_service_request`, text, optional): Enter a description of the customer service request.
