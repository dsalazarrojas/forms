# IGo4less Customer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IGo4less Customer Registration Form is used to collect personal and contact information from customers. This information will be used to create or update a customer's profile, enabling us to better serve and communicate with them.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide the following information as requested in the form fields.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled in, as indicated by the "required" label.
3. You can leave optional fields blank if not relevant.
4. Once you have completed all the fields, click the submit button to send your registration information to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Form** (`customer_form`, text, **required**):
    This field is the unique identifier for the customer. Please provide a brief and descriptive title that we will use to identify your account.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, **required**):
    Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents, e.g., passport or ID card.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, **optional**):
    Provide your email address that we will use to communicate with you about your account, updates, and promotions. Please format your email as `name@domain.com` or `domain.com`.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, **optional**):
    Enter your phone number for us to contact you, e.g., for account updates or issues.
* **Customer Note** (`customer_note`, note, **optional**):
    This field is for any additional information you want to provide about yourself, e.g., your company name or any specific requirements.
* **Customer Category** (`customer_category`, select_multiple, **optional**):
    Select one or more options that best describe your company or business type.
* **Category** (`customer_category`, select_one, **optional**):
    Choose one category that best describes you or your business. You can select from the available options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, or Option 4.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, **optional**):
    Enter your physical address where we can send any important documents or items. Please format as `street_address, city, country` for clarity.
