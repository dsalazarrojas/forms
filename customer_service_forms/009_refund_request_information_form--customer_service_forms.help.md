# refund_request_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers regarding refund requests. It will be used to process and address their inquiries. Please ensure that you are requesting a refund for a valid reason and that your details are accurate to avoid delays in processing your refund.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Customer Name** in the "Customer Name" field. This is the name of the person requesting the refund.
2. Provide your **Contact Number** in the "Contact Number" field. This is the phone number where you can be reached for further questions or clarification.
3. Enter the **Date Of Refund Request** field to specify the date when you initially requested the refund. This will help us to track the timeline of your refund request.
4. Type a brief **Description Of Request** in the space provided. Please describe the reason for your refund request in detail.
5. In the **Reason For Refund Request** field, explain the reason why you are requesting a refund. Be as clear and concise as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents or as you prefer to be known.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Provide your phone number or any other contact method we can use to reach you.
* **Date Of Refund Request** (`date_of_refund_request`, date, optional): Enter the date when you originally requested the refund, if known.
* **Description Of Request** (`description_of_request`, text, optional): Describe your refund request in a brief and clear manner.
* **Reason For Refund Request** (`reason_for_refund_request`, text, optional): Explain the reason why you are requesting a refund.
