# monthly_report_2021 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a monthly report specifically for 2021. The goal is to gather data from customers about their experiences and any issues they may have encountered during the month.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read through the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and fields included.
2. Fill in the required information for each field as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Monthly Report 2021** (`monthly_report_2021`, `text`, required/optional): This is the title of the form, please do not modify it.
* **customer_service_fields** (`customer_service_fields`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all the customer service fields that apply to your experience. These fields are:
	+ customer service field 1
	+ customer service field 2
	+ customer service field 3
* **Date** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the date of your experience or interaction with our customer service team.
* **Time** (`time`, required/optional): Enter the time of your experience or interaction with our customer service team.
* **Report Notes** (`report_notes`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your experience.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your customer name, if you are a customer service representative or agent, please enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the customer's email address, if you are a customer service representative or agent, please enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the customer's phone number, if you are a customer service representative or agent, please enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Service Field 4** (`customer_service_field_4`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all the customer service fields that apply to your experience. These fields are:
	+ customer service field 4 option 1
	+ customer service field 4 option 2
	+ customer service field 4 option 3

## Tips
* Please fill in all the required fields as prompted.
* Ensure you are providing accurate and detailed information to help us improve our customer service.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
