## Restaurant Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Restaurant Feedback Form" is a tool designed to collect feedback from customers about their dining experience at a restaurant. This information will help restaurants identify areas for improvement and provide better customer experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

- Read each page carefully and make sure you're providing accurate and honest feedback.
- Fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) unless you're not providing any feedback for that specific page.
- Use the options provided for each field to select your response. For fields with multiple select options, choose all that apply.
- If a field is optional, you can skip it if you're not comfortable leaving feedback on that particular aspect.
- Review your form before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Feedback About The Restaurant (Page 1)
- **Feedback About The Restaurant** (`restaurant_feedback`, note, required): This field is a free-text area where you can describe your overall experience at the restaurant. Please be as specific as possible, mentioning details about what you liked or disliked.

### 2. Food Quality (Page 1)
- **Food Quality** (`food_quality`, select_one, optional): Rate the quality of the food you had at the restaurant. Choose from the options: Very Good, Neutral, or Poor.

### 3. Service Attitude (Page 1)
- **Service Attitude** (`service_attitude`, select_one, optional): Rate the attitude of the service staff. Choose from the options: Very Good, Neutral, or Poor.

### 4. Cleanliness (Page 1)
