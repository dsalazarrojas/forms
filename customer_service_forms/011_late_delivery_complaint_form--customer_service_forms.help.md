# late_delivery_complaint_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to handle customer complaints about late deliveries. It will help the customer service team to identify and address the issues promptly.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Name (Customer Name)** (`required`): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Delivery Date (delivery_date)** (`required`): Enter the date the delivery was late.
*   **Order Number (order_number)** (`not required`): If you have an order number, please enter it here for reference.
*   **Complaint Reason (complaint_reason)** (`not required`): Describe the reason for your complaint (e.g., delayed by x hours or days).
*   **Customer Phone (customer_phone)** (`not required`): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted.
*   **Email Address (email_address)** (`not required`): Enter your email address for further communication.
*   **Customer Address (customer_address)** (`not required`): Enter your address for further communication.
*   **Delivery Person (delivery_person)** (`not required`): If you know the name of the person who made the delivery, enter it here.
*   **Delivery Time (delivery_date_time)** (`not required`): Enter the time the delivery was late.
*   **Customer Comment (customer_comment)** (`not required`): Add any additional comments or details about your complaint.
*   **Complaint Type (complaint_type)** (`not required`): Select the reason for your complaint (Delayed Delivery, Damaged Goods, Missing Item, or Other).
*   **Customer Service Agent (customer_service_agent)** (`not required`): Select the customer service agent that handled your case (Team or Agent).
*   **Additional Details (additional_details)** (`not required`): Provide any additional information about your complaint.

## Tips

*   Be as specific as possible when entering your complaint reason and delivery date/time.
*   If you have any additional details, please provide them in the additional details field.
*   If you have any further questions or need assistance, contact our customer service team.
