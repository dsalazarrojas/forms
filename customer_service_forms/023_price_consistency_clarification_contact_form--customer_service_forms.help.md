# price-consistency-clarification-contact-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers to clarify price discrepancies.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your account or the name you use for communication.
2. **Customer Email**: Enter your email address as it appears on your account or the one you use for communication.
3. **Description of Price Discrepancy**: Briefly describe the price issue you have encountered.
4. **Best Number to Contact**: Provide your best contact number for the team to reach out to you.
5. **Best Time to Contact**: Enter the time of day you are most available to be contacted. (Note: This field is optional)
6. **Customer Preferred Language**: Select your preferred language of communication.
7. **Customer Preferred Currency**: Choose your preferred currency for price comparison.
8. **Customer Comments**: If you have any additional comments or concerns, please provide them here. (Note: This field is optional)
9. **Internal Representation**: Enter any internal representation or reference number for your case. (Note: This field is optional)
10. **Customer Name Alias**: Enter any alias or nickname you use for your account. (Note: This field is optional)
11. **Customer Email Alias**: Enter any email alias or alternative email address you use. (Note: This field is optional)
12. **Customer Phone Alias**: Enter any phone number alias or alternative phone number you use. (Note: This field is optional)
13. **Customer Date of Last Contact**: Enter the date of your last contact with our team. (Note: This field is optional)
14. **Customer Time of Last Contact**: Enter the time of your last contact with our team. (Note: This field is optional)
15. **Customer Last Contact Channel**: Select the channel through which you last contacted our team.
16. **Customer Last Contact Method**: Select the method through which you last contacted our team.
17. **Customer Preferred Contact Channel**: Select the channel you prefer to contact our team.
18. **Customer Preferred Contact Method**: Select the method you prefer to contact our team.
19. **Relevant Details**: Provide any additional details that might help our team to resolve the price discrepancy.
20. **Price**: Enter the price that you believe to be incorrect.
21. **Price Alias**: Enter the price alias or reference number for your case. (Note: This field is optional)
22. **Customer Preferred Currency Alias**: Enter the currency alias or alternative currency you prefer. (Note: This field is optional)
23. **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product or service related to the price discrepancy. (Note: This field is optional)
24. **Item Name**: Enter the name of the item or component related to the price discrepancy. (Note: This field is optional)
25. **Reason for Contact**: Enter the reason for contacting us. (Note: This field is optional)
