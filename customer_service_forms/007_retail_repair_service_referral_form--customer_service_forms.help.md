# Retail Repair Service Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Repair Service Referral Form is designed to collect information from customers who need repair services. This form helps to efficiently process and manage repair requests, ensuring that the customer receives the necessary support in a timely manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's details, including name, address, and city.
2. Select the contact method (phone, email, or other).
3. Enter the contact information (phone number or email) based on the selected contact method.
4. Provide a repair description to help the customer service team understand the issue.
5. Select the repair status (in progress, completed, cancelled, scheduled, or pending).
6. Enter any customer service notes if necessary.
7. Select the store where the repair service was performed.
8. Enter the date the form was submitted.
9. Enter the submitted by name.
10. Select the customer service representative who handled the request.
11. Enter the customer service representative's name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details** (id: 10): Enter the customer's details, including name, address, and city.
	+ **Customer Name** (id: 13): Enter the customer's name.
	+ **Customer Address** (id: 11): Enter the customer's address.
	+ **City** (id: 12): Enter the customer's city.
* **Customer Info** (id: 2): Enter any additional information about the customer (optional).
* **Repair Service Details** (id: 3): Enter a brief description of the repair service needed.
* **Contact Method** (id: 4): Select how the customer prefers to be contacted (phone, email, or other).
* **Phone** (id: 5): Enter the customer's phone number if they selected phone as their contact method.
* **Email** (id: 6): Enter the customer's email address if they selected email as their contact method.
* **Other** (id: 7): Enter any other contact method if it was selected (optional).
* **Customer Message** (id: 8): Enter a message or comment for the customer service team.
* **Store Name** (id: 9): Enter the store where the repair service was performed.
* **Repair Status** (id: 17): Select the status of the repair service (in progress, completed, cancelled, scheduled, or pending).
* **Customer Service Notes** (id: 18): Enter any additional notes for the customer service team (optional).
* **Store Service Notes** (id: 19): Enter any additional notes for the store (optional).
* **Submitted By** (id: 20): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted Date** (id: 21): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Store Name** (id: 22): Enter the store where the repair service was performed.
* **Submitted By Name** (id: 23): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Customer Service Rep** (id: 24): Select the customer service representative who handled the request.
* **Customer Service Rep Name** (id: 25): Enter the customer service representative's name.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check your contact information to ensure that it is correct.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the customer service team for assistance.
* Be as detailed as possible when describing the repair service needed.
