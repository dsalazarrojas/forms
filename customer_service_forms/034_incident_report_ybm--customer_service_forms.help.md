# Incident Report YBM - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Incident Report YBM form is designed to collect relevant information about incidents that have occurred in the YBM, facilitating efficient incident reporting and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the relevant incident type from the provided options.
2. Enter the date and time the incident occurred in the `incident_date` and `incident_time` fields, respectively.
3. Choose the time zone where the incident took place from the `incident_time_zone` options.
4. Select the location where the incident occurred from the `incident_location` options.
5. Provide a detailed description of the incident in the `incident_description` field.
6. Enter your name and role as the reporter in the `incident_reporter_name` and `incident_reporter_role` fields, respectively.
7. Add your contact email and phone number in the `incident_reporter_email` and `incident_reporter_phone` fields, respectively.
8. Choose the incident status from the `incident_status` options.
9. Add any additional comments about the incident in the `incident_comment` field.
10. Select the incident category and priority from the `incident_category` and `incident_priority` options, respectively.
11. Choose the assignee and escalation path from the `incident_assignee` and `incident_escalate_to` options, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **incident_report_ybm**: This field is used to title and identify the incident report. (Required: False)
* **incident_type**: Select the type of incident that occurred. (Required: False)
* **incident_date**: Enter the date the incident occurred. (Required: False)
* **incident_time**: Enter the time the incident occurred. (Required: False)
* **incident_time_zone**: Choose the time zone where the incident took place. (Required: False)
* **incident_location**: Select the location where the incident occurred. (Required: False)
* **incident_description**: Provide a detailed description of the incident. (Required: False)
* **incident_reporter_name**: Enter your name as the reporter. (Required: False)
* **incident_reporter_role**: Select your role as the reporter. (Required: False)
* **incident_reporter_email**: Enter your email as the reporter. (Required: False)
* **incident_reporter_phone**: Enter your phone number as the reporter. (Required: False)
* **incident_status**: Choose the current status of the incident. (Required: False)
* **incident_comment**: Add any additional comments about the incident. (Required: False)
* **incident_category**: Select the incident category. (Required: False)
* **incident_priority**: Choose the incident priority. (Required: False)
* **incident_assignee**: Select the assignee of the incident. (Required: False)
* **incident_escalate_to**: Choose the escalation path for the incident. (Required: False)

Please note that all fields are optional unless marked as required. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure efficient incident resolution.
