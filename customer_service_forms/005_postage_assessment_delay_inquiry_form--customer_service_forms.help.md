This form will help in identifying the causes of delays and improving the overall shipping process.

The form is intended to be used by customers to report any issues they have experienced with their shipments. The form will help in tracking and resolving these issues efficiently.

4.  Provide the address of the shipper (if known).
5.  Specify the reason for the delay (Communication, Delivery, Pickup, or Other).

7.  Enter your customer name and any relevant customer information.
8.  Provide any contact information for follow-up purposes.
