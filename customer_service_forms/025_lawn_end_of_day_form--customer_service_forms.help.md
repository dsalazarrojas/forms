# Lawn End Of Day Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Lawn End Of Day Form is used to report the customer's lawn maintenance activities and any issues encountered on the day. This form helps the customer service team to gather important information about the lawn's condition and the customer's experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter lawn activities**: In the "Lawn Completed Activities" field, describe the activities performed on the lawn, such as mowing, edging, or trimming.
2. **Assess lawn condition**: In the "Lawn Condition" field, select the condition of the lawn from the options provided: "Lawn is in good condition," "Lawn is in fair condition," or "Lawn is in poor condition."
3. **List issues encountered**: In the "Issues Encountered" field, describe any issues or problems that arose during the lawn maintenance work.
4. **Equipment used**: In the "Equipment Used" field, select the equipment used during the maintenance work, such as string trimmers, lawn mowers, or aerators.
5. **Customer signature**: Sign the "Customer Signature" field to confirm that the form is complete and accurate.
6. **Technician signature**: Sign the "Technician Signature" field to confirm that the work is complete and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lawn Completed Activities** (`note`, `required`): Describe the activities performed on the lawn.
* **Lawn Condition** (`select_one`, `required`): Assess the lawn's condition from the options: "Lawn is in good condition," "Lawn is in fair condition," or "Lawn is in poor condition."
* **Issues Encountered** (`note`, `required`): Describe any issues or problems that arose during the lawn maintenance work.
* **Equipment Used** (`select_multiple`, `optional`): Select the equipment used during the maintenance work, such as string trimmers, lawn mowers, or aerators.
* **Customer Signature** (`text`, `required`): Sign to confirm that the form is complete and accurate.
* **Technician Signature** (`text`, `required`): Sign to confirm that the work is complete and accurate.
