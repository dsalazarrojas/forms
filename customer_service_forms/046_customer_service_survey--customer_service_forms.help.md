# Customer Service Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a tool for customers to provide feedback and suggestions after visiting our service center or office. By completing this form, you will help us understand your experience and identify areas where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email** to provide us with your contact information.
- Choose your **Satisfaction Level** by selecting one of the three options: **High**, **Medium**, or **Low**.
- Select all the **Areas of Improvement** that you think we should focus on, such as **Communication**, **Resolution**, **Responsiveness**, or **Timeliness**.
- If you have visited us before, please enter the **Date of Visit**. If you're unsure, you can leave this field blank.
- If you visited us during a specific time, please enter the **Time of Visit**. If you're unsure, you can leave this field blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Please enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
- **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how satisfied you are with your visit by selecting one of the three options: **High**, **Medium**, or **Low**.
- **Areas of Improvement** (`areas_of_improvement`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select all the areas that you think we should improve on, such as **Communication**, **Resolution**, **Responsiveness**, or **Timeliness**.
- **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, `date`, required: false): If you're able to recall the date of your visit, please enter it. If not, you can leave this field blank.
- **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, `time`, required: false): If you can remember the time of your visit, please enter it. If not, you can leave this field blank.

## Tips
- Please be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
- If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to add them to the **areas_of_improvement** field.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, you can always contact us for assistance.
