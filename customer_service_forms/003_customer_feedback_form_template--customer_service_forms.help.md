# Customer Feedback Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers to help us understand their needs and preferences. Please complete this form to provide your suggestions and opinions on how we can improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "How can we improve?" field and choose one or more options that you think would help us improve our services.
2. In the "Feedback" field, describe your thoughts on how we can improve our services.
3. Rate your experience by selecting a number from 1 to 5.
4. If you have a contact email address, please enter it so we can follow up with you.
5. Once you have completed all the above steps, submit the form to send your feedback.

* Please be as specific as possible in your feedback and suggestions.
