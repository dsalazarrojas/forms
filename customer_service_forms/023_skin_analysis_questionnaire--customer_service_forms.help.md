# Skin Analysis Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skin Analysis Questionnaire is a form designed to collect information from customers about their skin-related concerns and preferences. This form helps skincare professionals provide personalized advice and guidance on skin care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Select the correct answer for each question, if applicable. If a question does not apply to you, please answer "No" or leave it blank.
- Fill out each field with your thoughts, experiences, or answers as requested.
- Make sure to review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Skin Assessment**: This field is where you can describe your skin concerns or issues. Please provide as much detail as possible.
* **Skin Type**: If you select "Yes", please specify which skin type you identify with: e.g., sensitive, oily, combination, etc.
* **Skin Sensitivities**: If you select "Yes", specify which areas of your skin are sensitive, e.g., face, hands, feet, etc.
* **Skin Habits**: Describe your current skin care habits, including any products or routines you use.
* **Skin Goals**: What do you hope to achieve with your skin care? For example, "Reduce acne," "Improve skin tone," etc.
* **Skin History**: Provide a brief history of your skin, including any past treatments, medications, or allergies.
* **Additional Skin Concerns**: If you have any other skin-related concerns that are not listed above, please describe them here.
* **Skin Treatment**: Please describe any current skin treatments you are undergoing or have undergone.
* **Recommended Treatment Plan**: If you have already received a treatment plan from a skincare professional, please describe it here.
* **Follow-up Appointment**: If you have already scheduled a follow-up appointment, please enter the date and time.
* **Follow-up Appointment Date**: If you have already scheduled a follow-up appointment, please enter the date of the appointment.

Note: Some fields are optional and do not require an answer. Please only answer what is relevant to you.
