# Runner Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Runner Tracking Form is used to collect information from runners participating in events or activities. This information is used to record and verify the runner's participation and performance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Name**, as you would like it to be recorded in our systems.
2. Provide your **Email** so we can contact you if needed.
3. Enter your **Phone Number** so we can reach you.
4. Enter the **Start Date** and **Start Time** of your event or activity.
5. Enter the **Finish Date** and **Finish Time**, if applicable.
6. Enter the **Distance Run** covered during the event or activity.
7. Enter any additional **Notes** about your experience.
8. Optionally, provide **Runner Comments** if you have any thoughts or feedback about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Runner Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be recorded.
* **Runner Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Runner Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can reach you.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date of your event or activity.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of your event or activity.
* **Finish Date**: Enter the date of your event or activity, if applicable.
* **Finish Time**: Enter the finish time of your event or activity, if applicable.
* **Distance Run**: Enter the distance you covered during your event or activity.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional information about your experience.
* **Runner Comments**: Enter any thoughts or feedback about your experience.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
* Double-check your entries for accuracy to avoid errors.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact us and we'll be happy to help.
