# Hotel Room Service Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hotel Room Service Order Form is designed to collect information from guests who wish to order room service from our hotel. This form will help us gather the necessary details to provide the best possible service experience for our guests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your guest's first name in the "Guest First Name" field.
2. Enter your guest's last name in the "Guest Last Name" field.
3. Enter your guest's phone number in the "Guest Phone Number" field.
4. Enter your guest's email address in the "Guest Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your guest's first name as it appears on their ID or passport.
* **Guest Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your guest's last name as it appears on their ID or passport.
* **Guest Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your guest's phone number for any additional instructions or updates.
* **Guest Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your guest's email address so we can contact them with their order details and updates.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any special requests or dietary requirements, please let us know in the "Guest Phone Number" field.
* You can also use this form to request additional services or amenities not listed on the form.
