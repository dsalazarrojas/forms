# Customer Service Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Service Complaint Form is a channel for customers to report their complaints or concerns about our service. Filling out this form helps us to understand your experience and identify areas where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of complaint you wish to file by choosing from the list of options under "Complaint Type".
2. Enter your name and provide a description of your complaint in the "Complaint Description" field.
3. If you have a personal email address, please enter it in the "Email" field. This will help us to contact you for further questions or updates.
4. If you have a phone number, please enter it in the "Phone" field. This will help us to contact you if we need more information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Complaint Type
* **Complaint Type** (`Complaint Type`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose from the list of options to specify the type of complaint you are filing.
### Name
* **Name** (`Name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name to help us identify you and respond to your complaint.
### Complaint Description
* **Complaint Description** (`Complaint Description`, text, required/optional): Please provide a detailed description of your complaint to help us understand the issue you're facing.
### Email
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you for further questions or updates.
### Phone
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required/optional): If you have a phone number, enter it to enable us to contact you if we need more information.

## Tips
* Please be as clear and detailed as possible in your complaint description.
* If you have any supporting documentation or evidence, please attach it to the form or provide a link to it.
* If you wish to remain anonymous, you can fill out the form without providing your contact information. However, please note that this may limit our ability to follow up with you about your complaint.
* If you have any further questions or need help with the form, do not hesitate to reach out to our customer service team.
