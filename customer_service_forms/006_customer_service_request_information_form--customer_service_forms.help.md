# customer_service_request_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "customer_service_request_information_form" is a form designed to collect information from customers regarding their service requests. This form helps the customer service team to understand the customer's needs and provide the best possible solution.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*). 
2.  Enter your customer name in the "customer_name" field.
3.  Provide your email address in the "email" field.
4.  Enter your phone number in the "phone_number" field.
5.  Describe your issue in the "description" field.
6.  Indicate your department preference in the "department" field.
7.  Select your priority level in the "priority" field.
8.  If applicable, select the person or team you would like the issue to be assigned to in the "assigned_to" field.
9.  Provide any additional comments or information in the "comments" field.
10.  Finally, confirm your status in the "status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Id** (Form Id, number, required: false): This field is used to uniquely identify the form.
* **customer_name** (customer_name, text, required: false): Enter your name so that we can address you properly.
* **email** (email, email, required: false): Provide your email address so that we can contact you for further clarification or updates.
* **phone_number** (phone_number, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you via phone for further assistance.
* **description** (description, text, required: false): Describe the issue you are facing to help us understand and solve it efficiently.
* **department** (department, select_one, required: false): Choose the department you would like to deal with your issue, and we will direct it to the relevant team.
* **priority** (priority, select_one, required: false): Select the urgency level of your issue. This will help us to prioritize it accordingly.
* **assigned_to** (assigned_to, select_multiple, required: false): If you would like the issue to be assigned to a specific person or team, please select them from the list.
* **comments** (comments, text, required: false): Add any additional information that you think might be helpful for us to resolve your issue.
* **status** (status, select_one, required: false): Please confirm your status regarding the issue, whether active or inactive.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by customers, and it's essential to ensure that all required fields are filled in to avoid delays in processing your request.
