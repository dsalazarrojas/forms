# Water Service Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers about their water service application. It is used to understand the customer's needs and provide them with the necessary services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "First Name", "Last Name", and "Address" fields with your relevant contact information.
2. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select your "Water Usage" level from the provided options (e.g., Low, Medium, High).
5. Choose the "Service Requests" option that best applies to your needs (e.g., Residential, Commercial, Agricultural).
6. Enter any additional service requests in the "Service Requests Other" field.
7. If necessary, add any customer notes or comments in the "Customer Notes" field.
8. Select one or more agents that you would like to be assigned to your service request from the "Assigned Agent" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
*   First Name: (`First Name`, `text`, required/optional): This is the customer's first name.

### Last Name
*   Last Name: (`Last Name`, `text`, required/optional): This is the customer's last name.

### Address
*   Address: (`Address`, `text`, required/optional): This is the customer's address.

### Email
*   Email: (`Email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.

### Phone
*   Phone: (`Phone`, `text`, required/optional): This is your phone number.

### Water Usage
*   Water Usage: (`Water Usage`, `number`, required/optional): Select your water usage level (e.g., Low, Medium, High).

### Start Date
*   Start Date: (`Start Date`, `date`, required/optional): Choose the date when you would like to start the service.

### Service Requests
*   Service Requests: (`Service Requests`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the option that best describes your needs (e.g., Residential, Commercial, Agricultural).

### Service Requests Other
*   Service Requests Other: (`Service Requests Other`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional service requests.

### Customer Notes
*   Customer Notes: (`Customer Notes`, `note`, required/optional): Add any customer notes or comments.

### Assigned Agent
*   Assigned Agent: (`Assigned Agent`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select one or more agents that you would like to be assigned to your service request.

### Tips
*   Please fill in all required fields.
*   If you select a specific service request, you can add more details in the "Service Requests Other" field.
*   Be as specific as possible when entering your contact information and service requests. This will help our team provide you with the best possible service.
