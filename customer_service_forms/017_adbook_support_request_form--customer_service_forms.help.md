**AdBook Support Request Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The AdBook Support Request Form is a digital tool for users to report and describe issues they're experiencing with AdBook. By providing accurate and detailed information, users can help the support team quickly identify and resolve the problem.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name and email address.
2. Provide a brief description of the problem you're experiencing with AdBook.
3. Select the severity level of the issue (Low, Medium, High, Critical).
4. Choose your preferred contact method (Email, Phone, Slack).
5. If necessary, provide additional information that can aid in resolving the issue.
6. Attach any relevant files or screenshots if required.
7. Review and submit the form.
