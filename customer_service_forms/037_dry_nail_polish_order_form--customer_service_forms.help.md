# dry_nail_polish_order_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "dry_nail_polish_order_form" is a customer service form designed to collect information from customers about their order preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your level of service required using the "customer service" field on the first page.
2. Enter your name and phone number in the corresponding fields.
3. Select your nail polish preferences:
	* **Nail Polish Color**: Choose the color of the nail polish you are interested in.
	* **Nail Polish Size**: Select the size of the nail polish you are looking for.
	* **Nail Polish Type**: Choose the type of nail polish you prefer.
	* **Nail Polish Brand**: Select the brand of nail polish you are interested in.
	* **Nail Polish Price**: Enter the price you are willing to pay for the nail polish.
4. Select the type of customer service you require:
	* **Customer Service**: Choose the level of customer service you need.
5. Finally, enter the date of your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Service** (Yes/No): 
	+ This field is used to indicate the level of service required.
* **Customer Name**: 
	+ Enter your name to associate with your order.
* **Customer Email**: 
	+ Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you about your order.
* **Customer Phone**: 
	+ Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you about your order.
* **Nail Polish Color** (Yes/No): 
	+ Choose the color of the nail polish you are interested in.
* **Nail Polish Size** (Yes/No): 
	+ Select the size of the nail polish you are looking for.
* **Nail Polish Type** (Yes/No): 
	+ Choose the type of nail polish you prefer.
* **Nail Polish Brand** (Yes/No): 
	+ Select the brand of nail polish you are interested in.
* **Nail Polish Price** (number): 
	+ Enter the price you are willing to pay for the nail polish.
* **Customer Service** (Yes/No): 
	+ Choose the level of customer service you need.
* **Order Date** (date): 
	+ Enter the date of your order.

## Tips
Please ensure that you fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that we can process your order correctly.
If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
