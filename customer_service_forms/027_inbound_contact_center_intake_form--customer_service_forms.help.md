# Inbound Contact Center Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inbound Contact Center Intake Form is designed to collect information from customers, helping us better understand their inquiries and provide timely and effective support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Customer Inquiries Form (Page 1): Provide a brief description of your inquiry.
2. Enter your contact details: Fill in your name, email address, phone number, and other relevant information.
3. Select the Category, Type, and Priority of your inquiry: Choose the most relevant category, type, and priority for your issue.
4. Enter your work schedule: Provide your work location, shift, hours, and days of work.
5. Add any additional comments or notes: If you have any further information to provide, you can add it in the Note field.
6. Enter the date and time of your inquiry: If possible, provide the exact date and time of when you submitted your inquiry.
7. Confirm your details: Double-check that all the information you entered is accurate and up-to-date.
