# Store Visit Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Store Visit Report form is used to document and report on a customer's visit to a store, allowing store managers and staff to track customer interactions, identify potential issues, and improve store operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the date and time of the customer's visit in the "store visit date" and "store visit time" fields.
2. Enter the store number and customer ID in their respective fields.
3. If applicable, enter any notes or comments about the customer's visit in the "notes" field.
4. If applicable, enter any issues or concerns raised by the customer in the "customer issues" field.
5. If applicable, enter any store-related issues or concerns in the "store issues" field.
6. If applicable, enter the resolution or outcome of the customer's visit in the "resolution" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **store visit date** (`store_visit_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the customer's visit in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **store visit time** (`store_visit_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the customer's visit in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **store number** (`store_number`, `number`, required: false): Enter the store number where the customer visited.
* **customer id** (`customer_id`, `number`, required: false): Enter the customer's ID.
* **store manager id** (`store_manager_id`, `number`, required: false): Enter the store manager's ID (if applicable).
* **notes** (`notes`, `text`, required: false): Enter any notes or comments about the customer's visit.
* **customer issues** (`customer_issues`, `text`, required: false): Enter any issues or concerns raised by the customer.
* **store issues** (`store_issues`, `text`, required: false): Enter any store-related issues or concerns.
* **resolution** (`resolution`, `text`, required: false): Enter the resolution or outcome of the customer's visit.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have accurate and clear information about the customer's visit before filling out the form.
* Use the "notes" field for any additional comments or information that is not captured in the other fields.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with your store manager or supervisor for guidance.
