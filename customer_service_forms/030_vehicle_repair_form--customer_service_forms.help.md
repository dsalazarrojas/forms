# vehicle_repair_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is for customers to report a vehicle repair issue to the relevant service department.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form with the following information:
	+ Vehicle Problem: Describe the problem with your vehicle in as much detail as possible.
	+ Description: Provide a detailed description of the issue, including any error messages or symptoms.
	+ Vehicle Type: Identify the type of vehicle (e.g. car, truck, motorcycle).
	+ Contact Info: Enter your contact information, including name and phone number.
	+ Name: Enter your name.
	+ Email: Enter your email address.
	+ Phone: Enter your phone number.
	+ Year: Enter the year of your vehicle.
	+ Make: Enter the make of your vehicle.
	+ Model: Enter the model of your vehicle.
	+ Color: Identify the color of your vehicle.
	+ License Plate: Enter your vehicle's license plate number.
	+ Mileage: Enter the mileage of your vehicle.
	+ Problem Location: Describe the location of the problem.
	+ Repair Services: Identify any repair services that have already been performed.
	+ Additional Notes: Provide any additional information about the issue.
	+ Submitter Name: Enter the name of the submitter.
	+ Submitter Email: Enter the email of the submitter.
	+ Submitter Phone: Enter the phone number of the submitter.
	+ Submitter Date: Enter the date the form was submitted.
	+ Review Status: Identify the current status of the review.
	+ Reviewer Name: Enter the name of the reviewer.
	+ Reviewer Phone: Enter the phone number of the reviewer.
	+ Reviewer Date: Enter the date of the review.
