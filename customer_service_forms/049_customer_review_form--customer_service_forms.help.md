## Purpose
The customer review form is designed to collect feedback, ratings, and recommendations from customers about their experience with our company.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read the welcome message at the top of the page to understand the context and purpose of the form.
2.  Start by filling in your name and email address.
3.  Rate your overall experience with our company. Please select a number between 1 and 4 to indicate your satisfaction level.
4.  Share your thoughts and feedback about your experience in the text box labeled "Review".
5.  Let us know if you would recommend our company to others.
6.  Provide your phone number so we can get in touch with you for any follow-up questions or comments.
7.  If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please share them in the text box labeled "Comments".
8.  Select the correct options for "Submitted by" to indicate who submitted the form.
9.  Finally, submit your review by clicking the "Submit" button.
