# Billing Cycle And Payment Due Date Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers about their billing cycle and payment due dates. Please fill it out to help our customer service team understand your needs and respond accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Customer Information" tab and fill out the required fields with your account details.
2. In the "Inquiry Information" section, select the type of inquiry you are making.
3. Fill out the relevant fields according to your inquiry type.
4. In the "Contact Information" section, choose how you would like to be contacted.
5. Confirm that you are authorized to inquire about your account.
6. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_first_name`, `customer_last_name`, `customer_email`, `customer_phone`, `account_number`)
  - Enter your first name, last name, email address, phone number, and account number in the required fields.
* **Inquiry Type** (`inquiry_type`)
  - Select the type of inquiry you are making (e.g., billing cycle question, payment due date question).
* **Category of Inquiry** (`inquiry_category`)
  - Choose the category of your inquiry (e.g., billing cycle question, payment due date question).
* **Billing Cycle Question** (`billing_cycle_question`)
  - Select the specific question you have about your billing cycle (e.g., when does my billing cycle start).
* **Due Date Question** (`due_date_question`)
  - Select the specific question you have about your payment due date (e.g., when is my next payment due).
* **Current Billing Cycle** (`current_billing_cycle`)
  - Choose your current billing cycle frequency (e.g., monthly, bi-monthly).
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`)
  - Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g., email, phone call).
* **Preferred Contact Time** (`preferred_contact_time`)
  - Choose a preferred time to be contacted (e.g., morning, afternoon).
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency_level`)
  - Select the level of urgency for your inquiry (e.g., high, medium).
* **Specific Question** (`specific_question`)
  - Describe your billing or payment question in detail.
* **Invoice Number** (`invoice_number`)
  - Enter the reference invoice number if applicable.
* **Invoice Date** (`invoice_date`)
  - Enter the date on the invoice in question.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`)
  - Enter the payment amount related to your inquiry.
* **Previous Contact** (`previous_contact`)
  - Select whether you have previously contacted us about this issue.
* **Previous Reference Number** (`previous_reference`)
  - Enter the case or ticket number if previously contacted.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`)
  - Enter any additional information that may help our customer service team understand your needs.
* **Notification Preference** (`notification_preference`)
  - Choose how you would like to be notified of updates (e.g., email updates).
* **Authorization** (`authorization`)
  - Confirm that you are authorized to inquire about your account.
