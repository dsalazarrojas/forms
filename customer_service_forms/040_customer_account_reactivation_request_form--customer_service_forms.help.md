# Customer Account Reactivation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Customer Account Reactivation Request Form" is a tool used to request the reactivation of a customer account that has been inactive or suspended. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the status of your account by choosing from the options 'Yes' or 'No' in the "Account Type" field.
2. Enter your name in the "customer_name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field in the format of `customer@example.com`.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field without the dash (-) or parentheses ().
5. Enter the reason for reactivation in the "reason_for_reactivation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Account Type** (`account_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose 'Yes' if your account has been inactive or suspended for no more than 6 months, with the exception of accounts which have been suspended due to non-payment. If not, choose 'No'.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your account.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address in the format of `customer@example.com`.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number without the dash (-) or parentheses ().
* **Reason for Reactivation** (`reason_for_reactivation`, text, required/optional): Enter a short explanation for why your account should be reactivated.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the account meets the criteria for reactivation before submitting this form.
* If you are unsure about the status of your account, please contact us to verify.
* It is recommended to fill out the form completely and correctly for a quicker reactivation process.
