# free_color_street_sample_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to request free street samples from your company's customer service team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "name" field.
2. Provide your phone number in the "phone" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Specify the color you are requesting in the "color_request" field.
5. Choose a date and time for when you would like to receive your sample in the "date" and "time" fields.
6. Enter any additional details about your request in the "customer_service_form_request_details" field (optional).
7. Provide any comments or notes about your request in the "comments" field (optional).
8. Select the contact person who should be responsible for fulfilling your request in the "contact_person" field.
9. Select the tool that should be assigned to fulfill your request in the "assigned_tool" field.
10. Select the person who will be assigned to fulfill your request in the "assigned_to" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Service Form Request** (`customer_service_form_request`, `text`, required): This field is where you can enter any additional information about your request.
* **name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Color Request** (`color_request`, `text`, required): Specify the color you are requesting.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Choose a date for when you would like to receive your sample.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Choose a time for when you would like to receive your sample.
* **Customer Service Form Request Details** (`customer_service_form_request_details`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional details about your request.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any comments or notes about your request.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required): Select the contact person who will be responsible for fulfilling your request.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required): Select the tool that will be used to fulfill your request.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `text`, required): Select the person who will be assigned to fulfill your request.
