# Delivery & Install Wizard - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Delivery & Install Wizard" form is used to schedule delivery and installation of equipment or goods to customers. It collects customer information and preferences for delivery and installation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form by providing the required customer information.
2. Select the preferred installation type and customer contact method.
3. Provide any additional information as required or desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's name as it appears on their identification or records.
* **Request Date**: Select the date the customer requested the delivery and installation.
* **Delivery Date**: Select the date the customer would like to receive the delivery and installation.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number for communication purposes.
* **Email**: Enter the customer's email address for communication purposes.
* **Delivery Location**: Enter the address or location where the delivery will be made.
* **Installation Type**: Select either "In-home Installation" or "Off-site Installation" to indicate the type of installation required.
* **Installation Date**: Select the date the installation is scheduled to take place.
* **Delivery Time**: Select the time of day for delivery.
* **Customer Contact Method**: Select how the customer would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, Text Message, or In-app).
* **Customer Signature**: This field is for the customer to sign to confirm their order.
* **Staff Signature**: This field is for the staff member to sign to confirm their acceptance of the order.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check the customer's information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If the customer has any specific requirements or requests, make sure to include them in the additional comments section.
* This form is designed to be completed by customer service representatives, so make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
