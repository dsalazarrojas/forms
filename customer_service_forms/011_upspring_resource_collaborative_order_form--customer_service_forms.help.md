# upspring_resource_collaborative_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "upspring_resource_collaborative_order_form" is designed to help the organization collect data on households in need of resources and allocate them effectively. This form will help us understand the needs of your household and provide you with the necessary information to place orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Start by filling out the "family_members" field, which is a text field where you can enter the number of people living in your household.
2. Next, select the type of family you are part of by choosing one of the options in the "family_type" field.
3. Then, indicate the status of your family by selecting one or more options in the "family_status" field.
4. Provide your household's income by filling out the "household_income" field.
5. Enter the size of your household by filling out the "household_size" field.
6. Describe the items your household needs by filling out the "requested_items" field.
7. Indicate the type of item you are requesting by selecting one or more options in the "item_type" field.
8. Describe the condition of the items you are requesting by selecting one of the options in the "item_condition" field.
9. Choose the location where you would like to pick up your items by selecting one of the options in the "pickup_location" field.
10. Select the method of pickup you prefer, either "Delivery" or "Pickup", in the "pickup_method" field.
11. Enter the contact name and contact number of the person responsible for receiving the resources by filling out the "contact_name" and "contact_number" fields, respectively.
12. Enter the contact email of the person responsible for receiving the resources by filling out the "contact_email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
• **family_members** (text, required/optional): Enter the number of people living in your household.
• **family_type** (select_one, required/optional): Select the type of family you are part of.
• **family_status** (select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate the status of your household.
• **household_income** (number, required/optional): Enter your household's income.
• **household_size** (number, required/optional): Enter the size of your household.
• **requested_items** (text, required/optional): Describe the items your household needs.
• **item_type** (select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate the type of item you are requesting.
• **item_condition** (select_one, required/optional): Describe the condition of the items you are requesting.
• **pickup_location** (select_one, required/optional): Choose the location where you would like to pick up your items.
• **pickup_method** (select_one, required/optional): Select the method of pickup you prefer.
• **contact_name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person responsible for receiving the resources.
• **contact_number** (text, required/optional): Enter the contact number of the person responsible for receiving the resources.
• **contact_email** (email, required/optional): Enter the contact email of the person responsible for receiving the resources.

## Tips

* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will review and process your order as soon as possible.
