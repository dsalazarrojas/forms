# Cosmetics Formula Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cosmetics Formula Record Form is designed to collect preferences regarding lipsticks from customers. This data can be used for marketing, product development, or customer segmentation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your favorite lipstick color from the options provided.
2. Choose the lipstick brand you like.
3. Select your preferred finish for lipsticks (Matte, Gloss, Satin, or Metallic).
4. Indicate how important packaging is to you (Very important, Somewhat important, Not very important, or Not at all important).
5. Select the shade(s) of lipstick that you prefer (Light, Medium, or Dark).
6. Choose your price range for lipsticks (Less than $5, Between $5-$10, Between $10-$20, or More than $20).
7. Do you like flavored lipsticks? (Yes or No).
8. Select your preferred lipstick size (Small, Medium, or Large).
9. Choose the shape of your preferred lipsticks (Round, Square, or Oval).
10. Select the texture of your preferred lipsticks (Creamy, Gel, Liquid, or Powder).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your favorite lipstick color** (`lipstick_color`, text, Optional): This field allows you to select your favorite color for lipsticks.
* **Which lipstick brand do you like** (`brand`, select_one, Optional): Choose the brand of lipstick you prefer.
* **What finish do you prefer** (`finish`, select_one, Optional): Select the finish of the lipstick you prefer (Matte, Gloss, Satin, or Metallic).
* **How important is packaging for you** (`packaging`, select_multiple, Optional): Indicate how important packaging is for you.
* **What shade do you prefer** (`shade`, select_multiple, Optional): Select the shade(s) of lipstick that you prefer.
* **What is your price range for lipsticks** (`price_range`, select_one, Optional): Choose your price range for lipsticks.
* **Do you like flavored lipsticks** (`favorite_lipstick_flavor`, select_one, Optional): Do you like flavored lipsticks?.
* **What size lipstick do you prefer** (`lipstick_size`, select_one, Optional): Select the size of the lipstick that you prefer.
* **What shape do you prefer for lipsticks** (`lipstick_shape`, select_one, Optional): Choose the shape of the lipstick that you prefer.
* **What texture do you prefer for lipsticks** (`lipstick_texture`, select_one, Optional): Select the texture of the lipstick that you prefer (Creamy, Gel, Liquid, or Powder).
