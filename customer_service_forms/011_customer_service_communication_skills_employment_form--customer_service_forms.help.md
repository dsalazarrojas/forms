# Customer Service Communication Skills Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a candidate's customer service communication skills for employment purposes. It is essential to fill out the form accurately and completely to help us assess their suitability for the job.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone Number in the relevant fields.
2. Select your Employment Status from the options provided.
3. If applicable, enter your Job Title, Department, Years of Experience, Languages Spoken, Work Schedule, Job Type, Notice Period (if required), Notice Period Unit, Notice Period Frequency, and Job Description.
4. Choose your Availability and Work Location from the options provided.
5. If necessary, specify your Job Industry, Job Category, Job Level, Job Role, Job Employment Type, and Job Employment Status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address that you regularly use for communication.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your primary phone number that we may contact you on.

### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth as it appears on your identification documents.

### Employment Status
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, required): Choose from the options:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
	+ Retired
	+ Terminated

### Job Title
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): If you are currently or previously employed, enter your job title as it appears on your identification documents or in your professional resume.

### Department
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): If you are currently or previously employed, enter your department as it appears on your identification documents or in your professional resume.

### Years of Experience
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, number, optional): If you have relevant experience in customer service, enter the number of years you have worked in this field.

### Languages Spoken
* **Languages Spoken** (`languages_spoken`, select_multiple, optional): Check the languages you can communicate in fluently, separated by commas:
	+ English
	+ Spanish
	+ French
	+ Other

### Work Schedule
* **Work Schedule** (`work_schedule`, select_one, optional): Choose from the options:
	+ Full-time
	+ Part-time
	+ Remote
	+ Flexible

### Job Type
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, optional): Choose from the options:
	+ Full-time
	+ Part-time
	+ Contract
	+ Freelance

### Notice Period
* **Notice Period** (`notice_period`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the number of days you need for notice period.

### Notice Period Unit
* **Notice Period Unit** (`notice_period_unit`, select_one, optional): Choose from the options:
	+ Days
	+ Months
	+ Weeks

### Notice Period Frequency
* **Notice Period Frequency** (`notice_period_frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose from the options:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly

### Availability
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, required): Choose from the options:
	+ Weekdays
	+ Weekends
	+ Flexible
	+ Variable

### Work Location
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, select_multiple, optional): Check the locations where you can work from:
	+ Onsite
	+ Offsite
	+ Remote
	+ Hybrid

### Job Description
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the job you are applying for or have previously held.

### Job Category
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, text, optional): Enter a job category relevant to your work experience or skills.

### Job Industry
* **Job Industry** (`job_industry`, text, optional): Enter a job industry relevant to your work experience or skills.

### Job Level
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, text, optional): Enter a job level relevant to your work experience or skills.

### Job Role
* **Job Role** (`job_role`, text, optional): Enter a job role relevant to your work experience or skills.

### Job Employment Type
* **Job Employment Type** (`job_employment_type`, text, optional): Enter a job employment type relevant to your work experience or skills.

### Job Employment Status
* **Job Employment Status** (`job_employment_status`, select_one, optional): Choose from the options:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
	+ Retired
	+ Terminated

### Job Employment Type Other
* **Job Employment Type Other** (`job_employment_type_other`, text, optional): Enter any other job employment type not listed above.
