# Cleaning Customer Data Sheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about the customer, including personal details, service preferences, and billing information. The data collected will help us provide the best possible service to the customer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the **Personal Information** section, which includes providing your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email Address**.
2. Next, enter your **Phone Number** and **Alternate Contact Number** (if applicable).
3. Then, specify the **Date Registered** when you first started using our services.
4. In the **Service Location** section, provide your **Service Address**, including the **City** and **State** where the cleaning is performed.
5. Choose your preferred **Property Type**, such as Residential or Commercial.
6. Specify the **Access Code or Key Instructions** for the property (if applicable).
7. In the **Cleaning Preferences** section, choose your preferred **Service Frequency**, **Preferred Day of Week**, and **Preferred Time Slot** for cleaning.
8. Indicate your **Cleaning Products Preference**, such as Standard, Eco-Friendly, or Hypoallergenic.
9. List any areas requiring **Extra Attention**, such as kitchens or bathrooms.
10. Identify any areas to **Avoid or Exclude**, like a specific room.
11. In the **Household Information** section, specify the **Number of Household Members** and whether there are **Children in the Home**.
12. Indicate whether you have **Pets in the Home**, and if so, specify the types of pets.
13. Provide any **Pet Care Instructions**, if necessary.
14. In the **Health and Safety** section, mention any **Allergies or Chemical Sensitivities** and **Mobility Issues or Accessibility Needs**.
15. Specify the type of **Security System** and any instructions to disarm it.
16. In the **Billing Information** section, choose your preferred **Payment Method** and provide your **Billing Address** (if different from the service address).
17. Specify your **Invoice Email Address**, where invoices will be sent.
18. In the **Communication Preferences** section, choose your preferred **Contact Method** and indicate whether you want to receive **Marketing Communications**.
19. Finally, specify how you **Hear About Us** (e.g., through a friend, social media, or online advertising).
20. Add any **Additional Notes or Comments** about your services or account.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your given name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname.
* **Email Address**: Provide your primary email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your primary phone number.
* **Alternate Contact Number**: Enter a secondary phone number, if applicable.
* **Date Registered**: Specify when you first started using our services.
* **Service Address**: Enter the address where cleaning is performed.
* **City**: Enter the city or town where the service address is located.
* **State**: Enter the state or province where the service address is located.
* **ZIP or Postal Code**: Enter the postal code or ZIP for the service address.
* **Property Type**: Choose from options like Residential or Commercial.
* **Access Code or Key Instructions**: Specify how cleaners can access the property.
* **Service Frequency**: Choose from options like Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or One-Time.
* **Preferred Day of Week**: Choose from options like Monday, Tuesday, or Flexible.
* **Preferred Time Slot**: Choose from options like Early Morning or Late Afternoon.
* **Cleaning Products Preference**: Choose from options like Standard, Eco-Friendly, or Hypoallergenic.
* **Areas Requiring Extra Attention**: List areas that need special care, like kitchens or bathrooms.
* **Areas to Avoid or Exclude**: Specify areas that cleaners should not enter.
* **Number of Household Members**: Choose from options like 1 person or 7 or more people.
* **Children in the Home**: Choose from options like No Children or Yes - Infants or Toddlers.
* **Pets in the Home**: Choose from options like No Pets or Birds.
* **Pet Care Instructions**: Provide any care instructions for pets.
* **Allergies or Chemical Sensitivities**: Specify any allergies or sensitivities in the household.
* **Mobility Issues or Accessibility Needs**: Specify any mobility issues or needs.
* **Security System Information**: Specify the type of security system and any instructions to disarm it.
* **Preferred Payment Method**: Choose from options like Credit Card or Cash.
* **Billing Address**: Enter your billing address (if different from the service address).
* **Invoice Email Address**: Enter the email address where invoices will be sent.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Choose from options like Phone Call or Email.
* **Marketing Communications Consent**: Choose from options like Yes or No.
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: Choose from options like Friend or Online Search.
* **Additional Notes or Comments**: Add any other information about your services or account.

## Tips

* Double-check all fields for accuracy and completeness.
* Ensure you provide all required information.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact our support team for assistance.
