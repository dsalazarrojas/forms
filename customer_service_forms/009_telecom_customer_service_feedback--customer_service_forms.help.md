# Telecom Customer Service Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers on their interactions with our telecom services. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form to help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select how you interacted with our services:
	* Choose from the options provided.
2. Rate your satisfaction:
	* Rate from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Not Satisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
3. Share your comments:
	* Type any comments or thoughts you have about your interaction.
4. Choose your preferred channel:
	* Select which channel you would prefer to use for future interactions.
5. Provide contact details:
	* Enter any relevant contact information.
6. Enter your email address:
	* Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted.
7. Share how we can improve our service:
	* Type any suggestions or ideas you have for improving our services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Service Feedback** (`customer_service_feedback`):
	+ This is the main feedback field where you can share any comments or thoughts about your interaction with our services.
* **How did you interact with us** (`customer_service_feedback_channels`):
	+ Select how you interacted with our services, such as Phone, Email, Web, or Chat.
* **On a scale of 1-5, how would you rate your satisfaction** (`satisfaction_rating`):
	+ Rate your satisfaction from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Not Satisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
* **Which channel would you prefer to use?** (`telecom_channel_preferences`):
	+ Select which channel you would prefer to use for future interactions, such as Phone, Email, Web, or Chat.
* **Contact details** (`contact_details`):
	+ Enter any relevant contact information, such as your name and phone number.
* **Email** (`email`):
	+ Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted.
* **How can we improve our service?** (`telecom_service_improvement`):
	+ Type any suggestions or ideas you have for improving our services.
