# Scentsy Holiday Collection Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for placing orders for the Scentsy Holiday Collection. It collects customer and order information to ensure accurate and efficient processing of orders.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the customer information section, including your name, email, phone number, company, job title, and representative information.
- Select the order type, date, and time of the order.
- Add a holiday message or gift message if desired.
- Provide any additional order notes.
- Choose a payment method and enter the total cost of the order.
- Confirm whether the order has been successfully placed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Holiday Collection Order Form** (`form1`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your holiday order.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Enter your name, email, phone number, company, job title, and representative information.
* **Shipping Info** (`shipping_info`, text, optional): Enter your shipping details.
* **Order Info** (`order_info`, text, optional): Enter order specifics such as order type, order date and time, and order notes.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, optional): Enter your shipping address.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Customer Company** (`customer_company`, text, optional): Enter your company name.
* **Customer Job Title** (`customer_job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Representative** (`customer_rep`, select_one, optional): Select a representative from the list provided.
* **Representative Email** (`customer_rep_email`, text, optional): Enter the representative's email address.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the order.
* **Holiday Message** (`holiday_message`, text, optional): Add a holiday message if desired.
* **Gift Message** (`gift_message`, text, optional): Add a gift message if desired.
* **Order Type** (`order_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of order (holiday or gift).
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional order details.
* **Customer Message** (`customer_message`, text, optional): Add any customer message.
* **Shipping Cost** (`shipping_cost`, number, optional): Enter the shipping cost of the order.
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, number, optional): Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select the payment method (cash, credit card, or other).
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional order notes.
* **Order Confirmation** (`order_confirmation`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm whether the order has been successfully placed.
* **Form Comments** (`form_comments`, text, optional): Add any comments about the form.
