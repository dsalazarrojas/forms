# Digital Wallet Subscription Cancellation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to cancel a digital wallet subscription. If you are a customer and want to cancel your subscription, please follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill in all the required fields and follow the instructions for each field. If you are unsure about any field, please contact our customer service team for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, `text`, required: false): Please fill in your customer information, including your name and any relevant contact details.
* **Subscription Details** (`subscription_details`, `text`, required: false): Please provide any relevant subscription details, such as your subscription ID or account information.
* **Cancellation Reason** (`cancellation_reason`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose a reason for cancelling your subscription from the list:
	+ Choice 1
	+ Choice 2
	+ Choice 3
* **Signature** (`signature`, `note`, required: false): Please sign this form to confirm that you are cancelling your subscription. This will be used as a record of your cancellation.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your phone number.
* **Date of Cancellation** (`date_of_cancellation`, `date`, required: false): Please select the date of cancellation.
* **Time of Cancellation** (`time_of_cancellation`, `time`, required: false): Please select the time of cancellation.
* **Confirm Cancellation** (`confirmation`, `select_one`, required: false): Please confirm that you want to cancel your subscription:
	+ 'True'
	+ 'False'
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Please add any additional notes or comments about your subscription.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, `note`, required: false): Please sign this form to confirm your cancellation.
* **Representative Signature** (`representative_signature`, `note`, required: false): Please sign this form to confirm your cancellation.
