# Service Order Status Check Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Service Order Status Check Form is used to check the status of a service order. This form gathers information about a service order, including its current status, the date and time it was requested, and the contact information of the service requester.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, simply fill in the required information as prompted. All required fields are marked with an *.
2. Select the current status of the service order from the "Service Order Status" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the service order ID and date/time it was requested.
4. Enter any additional comments about the service order.
5. Optionally, enter the service requester's email and phone number for further communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Order Status** (`service_order_status_check_form_page_1_service_order_status`): Select the current status of the service order from the dropdown menu. This can be either "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Service Order ID** (`service_order_status_check_form_page_1_service_order_id`): Enter the ID number of the service order.
* **Service Order Date** (`service_order_status_check_form_page_1_service_order_date`): Enter the date and time the service order was requested.
* **Service Order Time** (`service_order_status_check_form_page_1_service_order_time`): Enter the time the service order was requested.
* **Service Requester** (`service_order_status_check_form_page_1_service_requester`): Enter the name of the person or organization requesting the service.
* **Service Requester Email** (`service_order_status_check_form_page_1_service_requester_email`): Enter the email address of the service requester (optional).
* **Service Requester Phone** (`service_order_status_check_form_page_1_service_requester_phone`): Enter the phone number of the service requester (optional).
* **Additional Comments** (`service_order_status_check_form_page_1_additional_comments`): Enter any additional comments about the service order (optional).
