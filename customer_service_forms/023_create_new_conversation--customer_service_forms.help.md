# Create New Conversation - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to create a new conversation for customer support purposes, guiding users through a process of assigning, subjecting, and prioritizing a conversation with a customer.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required and optional fields, as indicated by the label next to each field.
2. Ensure that the "Assign To" field is selected from the provided options.
3. Provide a clear and concise subject for the conversation.
4. Enter any relevant details about the conversation.
5. Select the preferred customer service channel for the conversation.
6. Choose the priority level of the conversation (if applicable).
7. Select the type of conversation (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Create New Conversation**: Enter the title of the new conversation (not required).
* **Assign To**: Select the person or team to assign the conversation to. Options: John, Jane, Jim.
* **Conversation Subject**: Enter a short description of the conversation subject (optional).
* **Conversation Details**: Enter any additional details about the conversation (optional).
* **Customer Service Channel**: Select the customer service channel to use for the conversation. Options: Phone, Email, Chat.
* **Priority Level**: Select the priority level of the conversation if applicable. Options: High, Medium, Low.
* **Conversation Type**: Select the type of conversation if applicable. Options: Support, Project, Vendor.
