# Complaint Resolution Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a crucial part of our customer service process, as it provides a structured way to document and close complaints in a way that ensures customer satisfaction. It helps us to track the progress of complaint resolution, provide feedback, and continuously improve our service.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the acknowledgment date field with the date of this acknowledgment.
2. Enter the complaint ID number provided to you.
3. Fill out the original complaint date field with the date the complaint was filed.
4. Enter your full name as the customer.
5. Enter your contact email address.
6. Enter your contact phone number.
7. Select the category of the complaint that you experienced.
8. Provide a brief description of the complaint summary.
9. Fill out the resolution date field with the date the complaint was resolved.
10. Select the method of resolution used.
11. Provide a detailed explanation of the resolution description.
12. Indicate if any compensation was offered and select the type of compensation.
13. If applicable, enter the compensation amount.
14. Select whether you accept the resolution.
15. Provide your satisfaction rating with the resolution.
16. Select the quality of handling of your complaint.
17. Select the quality of communication with your representative.
18. Select the speed of resolution.
19. Select whether you would recommend our service to others.
20. Select whether you will continue to do business with us.
21. If you have any additional feedback, enter it in the "Additional feedback" field.
22. Indicate whether we may contact you for further follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Acknowledgment Date** (1) (`acknowledgment_date`, `date`, `required`): This field should be filled out with the date that this acknowledgment form is being completed.
* **Complaint ID** (2) (`complaint_id`, `text`, `required`): Enter the ID number of the complaint that was filed.
* **Original Complaint Date** (3) (`complaint_date`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date that the complaint was originally filed.
* **Customer Name** (4) (`customer_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your full name as the customer.
* **Customer Email** (5) (`customer_email`, `email`, `required`): Enter your contact email address.
* **Customer Phone** (6) (`customer_phone`, `text`, `required`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Complaint Category** (7) (`complaint_category`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the category of the complaint that you experienced.
* **Complaint Summary** (8) (`complaint_summary`, `text`, `required`): Provide a brief description of the complaint.
* **Resolution Date** (9) (`resolution_date`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date of the resolution.
* **Resolution Method** (10) (`resolution_method`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the method used to resolve your complaint.
* **Resolution Description** (11) (`resolution_description`, `text`, `required`): Provide a detailed explanation of the resolution.
* **Compensation Offered** (12) (`compensation_offered`, `select_one`, `required`): Indicate if any compensation was offered and select the type of compensation.
* **Compensation Amount** (13) (`compensation_amount`, `number`, `required`: If applicable, enter the compensation amount.
* **Customer Accepts Resolution** (14) (`customer_acceptance`, `select_one`, `required`): Select whether you accept the resolution.
* **Satisfaction with Resolution** (15) (`satisfaction_rating`, `select_one`, `required`): Select your satisfaction rating with the resolution.
* **Satisfaction with Handling** (16) (`handling_rating`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the quality of handling of your complaint.
* **Communication Rating** (17) (`communication_rating`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the quality of communication with your representative.
* **Timeliness Rating** (18) (`timeliness_rating`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the speed of resolution.
* **Would Recommend** (19) (`would_recommend`, `select_one`, `required`): Select whether you would recommend our service to others.
* **Will Continue Business** (20) (`will_continue_business`, `select_one`, `required`): Select whether you will continue to do business with us.
* **Additional Feedback** (21) (`additional_feedback`, `text`, `required`: If you have any additional comments, enter them here.
* **May We Contact You** (22) (`follow_up_contact`, `select_one`, `required`): Indicate whether we may contact you for further follow-up.
* **Customer Signature** (23) (`customer_signature`, `text`, `required`): Type your full name as a signature.
* **Signature Date** (24) (`signature_date`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date that you sign this form.
* **Company Representative** (25) (`representative_name`, `text`, `required`: Enter the name of the representative who handled your complaint.
* **Representative Signature** (26) (`representative_signature`, `text`, `required`): Type the name of the representative as a signature.

## Tips

* Please fill out this form carefully and honestly.
* Make sure to enter accurate information to the best of your ability.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* This form is a crucial part of our customer service process, and your feedback is invaluable to us.
