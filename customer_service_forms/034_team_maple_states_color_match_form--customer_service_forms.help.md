# Team Maple States Color Match Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for matching your team with a color.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your team's new business name in the "new_business" field.
2. Enter the name of the artist associated with your team in the "artist" field.
3. Enter the name of the Chelsea player on your team in the "chelsea" field.
4. Enter the name of the Jessica player on your team in the "jessica" field.
5. Enter any other information about your team that you would like to provide in the "anything_else" field.
6. Select the first color you would like to match with your team from the "color_1" drop-down menu.
7. Select the second color you would like to match with your team from the "color_2" drop-down menu.
8. Enter the date you would like to start matching with your team in the "date" field.
9. Enter the time you would like to start matching with your team in the "time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **new_business** (text, required: no): Enter the name of your team's new business.
* **artist** (text, required: no): Enter the name of the artist associated with your team.
* **chelsea** (text, required: no): Enter the name of the Chelsea player on your team.
* **jessica** (text, required: no): Enter the name of the Jessica player on your team.
* **anything_else** (text, required: no): Enter any other information about your team.
* **color_1** (Select One, required: no): Select the first color you would like to match with your team.
* **color_2** (Select One, required: no): Select the second color you would like to match with your team.
* **date** (date, required: no): Enter the date you would like to start matching with your team.
* **time** (time, required: no): Enter the time you would like to start matching with your team.

Note: The fields marked as "Select One" (color_1 and color_2) require you to select one of the provided options. The fields marked as "date" and "time" require you to enter a date and time in the format "YYYY-MM-DD" and "HH:MM" respectively. 

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all the fields that are relevant to your team.
* If you're not sure which colors to select, try to choose the ones that best represent your team's style or brand.
* If you're not sure what date and time to enter, try to choose the first date and time of your team's match schedule.

This form is a simple color matching form that aims to help Team Maple States match their teams with colors. The fields are designed to be easy to understand and fill out. If you have any questions, feel free to reach out to us for further assistance.
