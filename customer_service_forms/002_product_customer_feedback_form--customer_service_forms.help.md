# Product Customer Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to collect feedback from customers about our products. Your input will help us understand your experience with our product and improve it for future customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide feedback, follow these steps:

1.  Provide a brief description of your experience with our product in the "Product Customer Feedback" field.
2.  Rate your overall satisfaction with our product by selecting a number (1-5) in the "Overall Rating" field.
3.  Share your thoughts about the product in the "Product Review" field.
4.  Enter the name of the product you are providing feedback about in the "Product Name" field.
5.  If you would like to follow up on your feedback, enter your name in the "Contact Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Customer Feedback**:
	+ This is a text field where you can provide any comments or suggestions you have about our product.
	+ Please be as detailed as possible to help us understand your experience.
* **Overall Rating**:
	+ Rate your overall satisfaction with our product by selecting a number from 1 to 5.
	+ 1-2: Dissatisfied, 3: Neutral, 4-5: Satisfied.
* **Product Review**:
	+ Share your thoughts about the product, what you liked or disliked, and what we can improve.
* **Product Name**:
	+ Enter the name of the product you are providing feedback about.
* **Contact Name**:
	+ If you would like to follow up on your feedback, please enter your name so we can contact you.

## Tips

* Please be honest and provide as much detail as possible in the "Product Customer Feedback" field.
* You can select a rating of 1-5 in the "Overall Rating" field to indicate your satisfaction.
* If you have any follow-up questions or concerns, please enter your name in the "Contact Name" field.
* We value your feedback and appreciate your time in filling out this form.
