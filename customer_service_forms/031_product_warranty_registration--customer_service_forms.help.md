# Product Warranty Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Warranty Registration form is used to collect essential information from customers about their products, including the start and end dates of the warranty, product details, and customer contact information. This form helps us to track and manage customer warranties, ensuring that we can provide the best possible support and service to our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the product name and serial number for the product you are registering.
2. Select the start and end dates of the warranty period.
3. Enter your name, email address, phone number, and other relevant customer information.
4. Provide your address details, including city and state.
5. Select your state from the list of available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the product you are registering.
- **Warranty Start Date** (`warranty_start_date`, date, optional): Select the start date of the warranty period. This date is not required but recommended for accurate tracking.
- **Warranty End Date** (`warranty_end_date`, date, optional): Select the end date of the warranty period. This date is not required but recommended for accurate tracking.
- **Serial Number** (`serial_number`, text, optional): Enter the serial number of the product.
- **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Client Address Line 1** (`client_address_line_1`, text, optional): Enter the first line of your address.
- **Client Address Line 2** (`client_address_line_2`, text, optional): Enter the second line of your address.
- **Client City** (`client_city`, text, optional): Enter the city of your address.
- **Client State** (`client_state`, select_one, optional): Select your state from the list of available options.
- **Client Postal Code** (`client_postal_code`, text, optional): Enter your postal code (zip code).

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure smooth warranty registration and tracking.
* Double-check the product details and customer information to avoid any errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
