# Customer Retention Initiative Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow customers to provide feedback, suggestions, or concerns related to our customer retention initiative. This information will help us understand how to better serve our customers and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Contact Us" page.
2. Fill out the form with your information and message.
3. Select the "Consent to Follow-up" option to indicate your preference.
4. Add any additional comments or concerns in the "Additional Comments" field.
5. Submit the form to send us your message.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Us** (`contact_form`, text, optional): This is the main form field where you can type in your message or feedback.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Please enter your name so we can address you personally.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you directly.
* **Reason for Visit** (`reason_for_visit`, text, optional): Let us know why you are reaching out to us.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number you'd like to provide, please enter it here.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): This is where you can type in any additional comments or concerns.
* **Consent to Follow-up** (`consent_to_follow_up`, select_one, required): Please select one of the options to indicate if you consent to be followed up on. Options are:
	+ Option 1: Yes
	+ Option 2: No
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): This is an optional field for any extra information you'd like to share.
