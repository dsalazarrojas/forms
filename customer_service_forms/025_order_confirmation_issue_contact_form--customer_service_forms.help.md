# Order Confirmation Issue Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to help us understand and resolve order confirmation issues. Please provide as much information as possible to ensure we can assist you quickly and efficiently.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the requested information and submit. If you're unsure about any field, you can contact us and we'll be happy to assist.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Confirmation Issue Contact Form**: Enter a brief description of the issue with your order.
* **order_id**: Enter the ID of the affected order to help us locate the issue.
* **customer_order_number**: Enter the customer's order number as it appears on their order confirmation.
* **order_date**: Enter the date of the order, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **order_time**: Enter the time of the order, in the format 12:00 PM or 13:00.
* **customer_order_number**: This field is a duplicate of **customer_order_number**. You can enter the customer's order number again, or simply ignore this field.
* **order_details**: Enter any additional information about the order that may help us understand the issue.
* **customer_name**: Enter the customer's name associated with the affected order.
