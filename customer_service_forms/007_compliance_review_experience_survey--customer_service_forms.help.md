# Compliance Review Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Compliance Review Experience Survey is a form designed to collect feedback on the customer service experience, allowing the organization to identify areas of improvement and enhance its review process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the instructions below:

1. Read the survey introduction at the top to understand the purpose and context of the form.
2. Provide your name if you choose to do so (optional).
3. Enter the date of the review interaction (service date).
4. Rate the clarity of the information provided (Very clear, Somewhat clear, Not clear).
5. Rate the professionalism of the staff involved (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
6. Rate the timeliness of the review process (Very timely, Adequate, Delayed).
7. Rate the communication quality (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
8. Rate your overall satisfaction with the review experience (5, 4, 3, 2, 1).
9. If you have suggestions for improvement, provide them in the text field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### -- Survey Introduction --
* **Survey Introduction** (`section_intro`, note, required: false): This section provides an introduction to the survey, explaining its purpose and context. Please read and understand the introduction to ensure you provide accurate and relevant feedback.

### Respondent name
* **Respondent name** (`respondent_name`, text, required: false): If you would like to provide your name for feedback, please enter it here. This information is optional and will not affect your feedback.

### Service date
* **Service date** (`service_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the review interaction. This helps us track the timing of your experience.

### Clarity of information provided
* **Clarity of information provided** (`clarity_of_information`, select_one, required: true): Rate how clear the review findings were. Choose from the options: Very clear, Somewhat clear, Not clear.

### Professionalism of staff
* **Professionalism of staff** (`professionalism`, select_one, required: true): Rate the professionalism of the staff involved. Choose from the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.

### Timeliness of the review
* **Timeliness of the review** (`timeliness`, select_one, required: true): Rate the timeliness of the review process. Choose from the options: Very timely, Adequate, Delayed.

### Communication quality
* **Communication quality** (`communication_quality`, select_one, required: true): Rate the communication quality. Choose from the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.

### Overall satisfaction
* **Overall satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required: true): Rate your overall satisfaction with the review experience. Choose from the options: 5, 4, 3, 2, 1.

### Suggestions for improvement
* **Suggestions for improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, text, required: false): Provide any suggestions for improvement you may have. This is an optional field, and your feedback is valuable regardless of whether you choose to use it.
