# Internet Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Internet Service Request Form is used to collect information from customers about their internet service requests. This form helps our technicians to identify the type of service needed, the location of the service, and the status of the service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of internet service you are requesting (DSL, Fiber, or Cable).
2. Choose the location where you would like to have the service installed or repaired (Home, Business, or Mobile).
3. Enter the date and time you would like to schedule the service request.
4. Choose the current status of your service (Open or Closed).
5. If you have any additional comments or concerns, you can enter them in the space provided.
6. If you know the name of the technician who will be assigned to your request, you can enter their name in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info** (customer_info, text, not required): This is where you can enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Service Type** (service_type, select_one, required): Choose the type of internet service you need (DSL, Fiber, or Cable).
* **Service Location** (service_location, select_multiple, required): Select the location where you would like to have the service installed or repaired (Home, Business, or Mobile).
* **Request Date** (request_date, date, not required): If you know the date you would like to schedule the service request, enter it here.
* **Request Time** (request_time, time, not required): If you know the time you would like to schedule the service request, enter it here.
* **Service Status** (service_status, select_one, required): Choose the current status of your service (Open or Closed).
* **Customer Comment** (customer_comment, text, not required): If you have any additional comments or concerns, you can enter them here.
* **Assigned Technician** (assigned_technician, text, not required): If you know the name of the technician who will be assigned to your request, enter their name here.
* **Technician Comment** (technician_comment, text, not required): This field is for the technician's internal notes, not intended for customer input. Do not fill this field unless directed by a technician.

## Tips

* Please ensure that your contact information is accurate so that our technicians can reach you with updates.
* If you are unsure about any of the options, select the default value or choose "Unknown" and our technicians will assist you.
