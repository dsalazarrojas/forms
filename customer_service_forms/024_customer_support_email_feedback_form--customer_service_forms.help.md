# Customer Support Email Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their interactions with our customer support team, particularly regarding their email correspondence. Your input will help us improve our services and provide better support in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields and provide accurate and concise information for each field.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
3. If you have a suggestion or feedback, please provide it in the `Issue Description` field.
4. Select the appropriate `Customer Service Interaction` option that best describes your experience.
5. Enter your `Email` address in the format `yourname@domain.com`.
6. If you have a `Phone Number`, enter it in the format `123-456-7890` or `123.456.7890`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Support Email Feedback Form** (`customer_support_email_feedback_form`, text, required/optional): This is the title of the form that you're currently filling out.
- **Customer Service Interaction** (`customer_service_interaction`, select_one, required/optional): Please select the option that best describes your experience with our customer support team, such as:
  • Option 1: Responded promptly to my inquiry
  • Option 2: Took some time to respond but was helpful
  • Option 3: Unable to resolve my issue
- **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, text, required/optional): Describe the issue you experienced with our customer support team, including any relevant details or context.
- **Email** (`email_address`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address in the format `yourname@domain.com`.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number in the format `123-456-7890` or `123.456.7890`.
