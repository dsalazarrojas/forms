# Customer Support Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Support Feedback Survey is designed to collect feedback from customers who have engaged with our customer support team. The information collected through this form will help us identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance our support services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Customer Survey**: Please provide a brief description of your experience with our customer support team. 
2. **Support Services**: Select the services you received from us during your interaction. 
3. **Rating**: Rate your overall experience with us (Good, Fair, or Bad). 
4. **Email**: If you'd like to be contacted for future reference or follow-up, please enter your email address. 
5. **Phone Number**: If you'd like to be contacted by phone for further discussion, please enter your phone number. 
6. **Comments**: Share any additional comments or feedback about your experience. 
7. **Category**: Select the category that best describes your experience (e.g., Product, Service, or General). 
8. **Subcategory**: Select all subcategories that apply to your experience (e.g., Feature, Support, or Billing). 
9. **Submit**: Choose to submit your feedback or cancel without saving changes. 
10. **Customer Service Level**: Rate your experience with our customer service level (e.g., Good, Fair, or Bad). 
11. **Suggestions**: If you have any suggestions for improvement, please provide them in this field. 
12. **Contact**: If you'd like to be contacted for further discussion, please provide your contact information. 
13. **Email (Secondary)**: If you'd like to be contacted for future reference or follow-up, please enter your secondary email address. 
14. **Category (Secondary)**: Select the category that best describes your experience (e.g., Product, Service, or General). 
15. **Subcategory (Secondary)**: Select all subcategories that apply to your experience (e.g., Feature, Support, or Billing).
