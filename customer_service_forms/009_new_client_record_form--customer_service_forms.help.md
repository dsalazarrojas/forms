# New Client Record Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The New Client Record Form is designed to create new client records for customer service or support teams. This form collects general information about the client, such as name, email, and contact details, as well as more specific information like date and time of birth, and notes for the support team to review.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the New Client Record Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the First Name field with the client's first name.
2. Fill in the Last Name field with the client's last name.
3. Enter the client's Email address.
4. Enter the client's Phone Number.
5. Enter the client's Date of Birth.
6. Enter the client's Time of Birth.
7. Enter the client's Address.
8. Add any relevant Client Notes for the support team to review.
9. Select the Assigned Tool(s) relevant to the client's needs.
10. Review and submit the form to create the new client record.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter the client's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter the client's last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the client's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the client's date of birth.
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, optional): Enter the client's time of birth.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the client's address.
* **Client Notes** (`client_notes`, text, optional): Add any relevant notes for the support team to review.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select the tool(s) relevant to the client's needs. Note: This field allows for single selection.
* **Another Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select the tool(s) relevant to the client's needs. Note: This field allows for multiple selections.
* **Another Field** (`another_field`, number, optional): Enter a numerical value for this field.
* **Another Integer Field** (`another_integer_field`, number, optional): Enter an integer value for this field.
* **Yet Another Field** (`yet_another_field`, number, optional): Enter a numerical value for this field.
* **Yet Another Integer Field** (`yet_another_integer_field`, number, optional): Enter an integer value for this field.
* **Yet Another Decimal Field** (`yet_another_integer_field_2`, number, optional): Enter a decimal value for this field.
* **Yet Another Integer Field 2** (`yet_another_integer_field_3`, number, optional): Enter an integer value for this field.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields (First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone Number).
* Review the form for any additional information that might be needed before submitting.
* Double-check the selections in the Assigned Tool and Another Assigned Tool fields to ensure they are accurate.
