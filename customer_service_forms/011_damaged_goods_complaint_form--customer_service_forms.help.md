# Damaged Goods Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to report damage or defects to items received from our company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Please fill out the form with the requested information, including product information and order number.
2. Describe the issue you are experiencing with the damaged goods.
3. You may upload an image of the damaged product if applicable.
4. Submit the form to help us investigate and resolve the issue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Name**: Please enter the name of the product that was damaged or defective. This will help us identify the item and investigate the issue.
* **Product Description**: Describe the product in detail, including any relevant information that may help us understand the issue. This can include the product's features, functionality, or packaging.
* **Product Image**: If possible, upload an image of the damaged product to help us better understand the issue. Please ensure the image is clear and well-lit.
* **Order Number**: Enter the order number for the damaged goods. This will help us locate the order in our system and expedite the investigation process.
* **Product Information**: Please provide any additional information about the product that may be relevant to the issue. This can include the product's serial number, packaging, or other distinguishing features.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form clearly and accurately to ensure that we can investigate the issue efficiently.
* If you have previously submitted this form, please do not resubmit the same form. Instead, contact customer service to discuss your issue.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, please contact customer service for assistance.
