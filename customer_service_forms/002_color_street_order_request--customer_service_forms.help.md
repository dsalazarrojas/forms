# Color Street Order Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Color Street Order Request form is a tool used to collect information from customers for order fulfillment and customer service purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
2. Enter your valid email address for order confirmation and future contact.
3. Enter your contact phone number for any order inquiries.
4. Select the date of the order request.
5. Select your preferred Color Street collection from the provided options.
6. List your favorite Color Street shades.
7. Specify the total quantity of nail strip sets you wish to order.
8. If desired, select your top priority shade, backup shade, and third choice shade from the provided options.
9. Enter your shipping address where the order will be delivered.
10. Choose your desired shipping speed (Standard, Express, or Overnight).
11. If you wish to include a gift message, enter it in the provided field.
12. Enter any additional comments or requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for order confirmation and future contact.
* **Phone Number** (`customer_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number for any order inquiries.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required): Select the date of the order request.
* **Preferred Collection** (`preferred_collection`, select_one, required): Choose the Color Street collection that interests you most from the provided options.
* **Favorite Shades** (`favorite_shades`, text, required): List your favorite Color Street shades.
* **Total Quantity Requested** (`quantity_requested`, number, required): Specify the total quantity of nail strip sets desired.
* **First Choice Shade** (`specific_shade_1`, text, optional): If desired, select your top priority shade from the provided options.
* **Second Choice Shade** (`specific_shade_2`, text, optional): If desired, select your backup shade preference from the provided options.
* **Third Choice Shade** (`specific_shade_3`, text, optional): If desired, select your third choice shade from the provided options.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required): Enter your complete mailing address where the order will be delivered.
* **Shipping Preference** (`shipping_preference`, select_one, required): Choose your desired shipping speed from the provided options.
* **Gift Message** (`gift_message`, text, optional): If you wish to include a gift message, enter it in this field.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or requests.
