# Bus Service Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to renew your existing bus service contract. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure a smooth renewal process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the customer information section with your name, customer account number, and email address.
2. Provide your current service details, including service type and route number.
3. Choose the type and duration of the renewal.
4. Select your preferred payment method.
5. Optionally, provide any special requirements or additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents. (<name>, <type>: text, required)
* **Customer ID**: Enter your customer account number. (<name>, <type>: text, required)
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address. (<name>, <type>: email, required)
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number. (<name>, <type>: text, required)
* **Address**: Enter your mailing address. (<name>, <type>: text, required)
* **Current Service Information**: Note any important details about your current service. (<name>, <type>: note, optional)
* **Current Route Number**: Enter the route number you currently use. (<name>, <type>: text, required)
* **Service Type**: Select the type of service you receive. (<name>, <type>: select_one, required)
* **Current Contract Start Date**: Enter the start date of your current service contract. (<name>, <type>: date, required)
* **Current Contract End Date**: Enter the end date of your current service contract. (<name>, <type>: date, required)
* **Renewal Information**: Note any details about your renewal request. (<name>, <type>: note, optional)
* **Renewal Type**: Select the type of renewal you are requesting. (<name>, <type>: select_one, required)
* **Renewal Period**: Select the duration of the renewal. (<name>, <type>: select_one, required)
* **Preferred Renewal Start Date**: Enter the date you would like the renewed service to begin. (<name>, <type>: date, required)
* **Requested Service Changes**: If you have any changes to your current service, enter them here. (<name>, <type>: text, optional)
* **Expected Passenger Count**: Enter the number of passengers using the service. (<name>, <type>: number, required)
* **Special Requirements**: If you have any special needs, enter them here. (<name>, <type>: text, optional)
* **Current Service Satisfaction**: Rate your current service satisfaction level. (<name>, <type>: select_one, required)
* **Preferred Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method. (<name>, <type>: select_one, required)
* **Promotional Code**: If you have a promotional code, enter it here. (<name>, <type>: text, optional)
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any other information or comments about your renewal request. (<name>, <type>: text, optional)

By following these instructions, you should be able to complete the form accurately and efficiently. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our customer service team.
