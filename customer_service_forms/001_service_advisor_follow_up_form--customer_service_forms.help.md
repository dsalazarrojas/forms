# Service Advisor Follow Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback and suggestions from customers after a service appointment has been completed. Please use this form to follow up with customers and help us improve the service experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the "Service Advisor Follow Up Form" option in your application. Fill out the form as completely or as partially as you're comfortable sharing, but make sure to answer all the required fields (though most are optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_header**: This field is a title placeholder. Please ignore it.
* **customer_name**: Enter the customer's name as provided during the service appointment.
* **service_advisor_name**: Enter the service advisor's name as provided during the service appointment.
* **service_advisor_email**: Enter the service advisor's email as provided during the service appointment.
* **service_advisor_phone**: Enter the service advisor's phone number as provided during the service appointment.
* **customer_feedback**: Please provide any customer feedback, concerns or comments you'd like to share with the service department.
* **follow_up_date**: Enter the date you follow up with the customer.
* **service_advisor_notes**: Add any notes about the customer's service appointment or feedback that would be helpful to know for future interactions.
* **service_feedback**: Enter any feedback you'd like to share about the service advisor's performance.
* **customer_feedback_2**: This is the customer's feedback about your service. Please share with them and use it as a reference point for future interactions.
* **customer_suggestions**: Enter any suggestions you'd like to share with the service department based on the customer's feedback.
* **form_footer**: This field is a title placeholder. Please ignore it.

## Tips
* If you're unsure about a customer's information, please reach out to your supervisor for clarification.
* Please provide as much information as you're comfortable sharing.
* Filling out this form helps us to better understand your experience and concerns, and improves the service experience for our customers.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact your supervisor.
