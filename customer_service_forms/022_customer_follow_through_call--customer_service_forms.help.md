# Customer Follow Through Call - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Follow Through Call form is used to document and track information about follow-through calls with customers, which is an essential part of providing excellent customer service.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, fill in the necessary information for each field, following the instructions for each field below.
2. Make sure to answer all the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*) asterisk.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Initial Contact** (page 1): Enter a brief description of the initial contact made with the customer.
* **Follow Through** (page 1): Enter the details of the follow-through action taken, such as a call, email, or message.
* **Call Outcome** (page 2): Select the outcome of the follow-through call, whether it was successful or not.
* **Date of Follow Through Call** (page 2): Enter the date of the follow-through call.
* **Time of Follow Through Call** (page 3): Enter the time of the follow-through call.
* **Follow Through Type** (page 3): Select the type of follow-through action taken, such as a follow-up call, email, or phone call.
* **Next Steps** (page 4): Enter a brief description of the next steps planned or taken for the customer.
* **Customer Response** (page 4): Enter any notable customer responses or comments regarding the follow-through call. (Optional)
* **Follow Through Time** (page 5): Enter the estimated time it took to complete the follow-through call or action.
* **Follow Up Plan** (page 5): Select whether or not to create a follow-up plan for the customer.
