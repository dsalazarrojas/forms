# Cheesecake Preferences Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Cheesecake Preferences Questionnaire" is designed to gather information about your cheesecake preferences, including your personal details, eating habits, and preferences for flavors, toppings, and more. This data will help us understand your cheesecake needs and provide you with a better experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your details:
	* `customer_first_name`: Enter your first name as it is spelled on your identification documents.
	* `customer_last_name`: Enter your last name as it is spelled on your identification documents.
	* `email_address`: Enter your valid email address.
	* `phone_number`: Enter your phone number (optional).
2. Choose your preferences:
	* `age_group`: Select the age group that best represents you:
		+ 18-25
		+ 26-35
		+ 36-45
		+ 46-55
		+ 56-65
		+ 65+
	* `crust_preference`: Choose your preferred cheesecake crust:
		+ Graham Cracker
		+ Chocolate Cookie
		+ Vanilla Wafer
		+ Oreo
		+ No Crust
		+ No Preference
	* `texture_preference`: Select your preferred cheesecake texture:
		+ Dense and rich
		+ Light and fluffy
		+ Creamy and smooth
		+ Slightly grainy
		+ No Preference
	* `topping_preference`: Choose your favorite cheesecake toppings:
		+ Fruit sauce
		+ Chocolate sauce
		+ Caramel sauce
		+ Fresh berries
		+ Nuts
		+ Whipped cream
		+ Graham cracker crumbs
		+ Chocolate chips
		+ Plain no toppings
3. Select your preferred cheesecake serving options:
	* `serving_temperature`: Choose how you prefer your cheesecake to be served:
		+ Chilled from refrigerator
		+ Room temperature
		+ Slightly warmed
		+ Frozen
		+ No Preference
	* `new_flavor_interest`: Indicate your interest in trying new cheesecake flavors:
		+ Very interested
		+ Somewhat interested
		+ Not particularly interested
		+ Not interested
4. Specify your preferred packaging and price range:
	* `packaging_preference`: Choose your preferred cheesecake packaging:
		+ Individual slice
		+ Whole cake
		+ Sampler pack
		+ No Preference
	* `price_range`: Select the price range you are willing to pay per slice:
		+ Under 5 dollars
		+ 5-8 dollars
		+ 8-12 dollars
		+ 12-15 dollars
		+ Over 15 dollars
