# Investigator Service Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Investigator Service Form is used to request investigative services from our organization. Customers can submit a request using this form, specifying the service they need, their contact information, and their preferred contact method. This form helps our investigators understand the customer's requirements, ensuring that we provide the most effective assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the required fields:
    *   Contact Number: Enter your contact number where you can be reached.
    *   Investigator Service Request: Provide a detailed description of the service you need from our investigator.
    *   Location: Specify the location where the service will be performed.
*   Select your preferred contact method:
    *   Preferred Contact Method: Choose how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or Text (SMS)).
*   Select the services you require:
    *   Investigator Service Type: Choose all the services you need (Background Check, Surveillance, Investigation, Background Check and Surveillance, Investigation and Surveillance, or Other).
*   If you select "Other," provide a detailed description of the service in the space provided.
*   Optional fields:
    *   Email: Enter your email address where you can be contacted (optional).
    *   Phone: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number where you can be reached.
*   **Investigator Service Request** (`investigator_service_request`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the service you need from our investigator.
*   **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Specify the location where the service will be performed.
*   **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or Text (SMS)).
*   **Investigator Service Type** (`investigator_service_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the services you need (Background Check, Surveillance, Investigation, Background Check and Surveillance, Investigation and Surveillance, or Other).
*   **Investigator Service Other** (`investigator_service_other`, text, optional): If you selected "Other," provide a detailed description of the service in this space.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be contacted (optional).
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted (optional).

## Tips
*   Be as specific as possible when describing the service you need.
*   Check your contact information for accuracy before submitting the form.
