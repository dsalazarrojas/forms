# customer_loyalty_merge_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The customer loyalty merge request form is used to merge multiple customer accounts into a single account, allowing for easier management and tracking of customer interactions. This form is designed to collect relevant information from customers to facilitate a successful merge process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your customer ID.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Provide your full name.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Specify your reason for merging the accounts.
6. Enter the customer ID of the second account you would like to merge.
7. Select the type of account you are managing (loyalty, rewards, or account).
8. Select the status of the account you are managing (active or inactive).
9. Enter your account manager's name.
10. Enter the customer's email address.
11. Enter the customer's phone number.
12. Enter the date and time of the request.
13. Select the status of the merge request (pending, approved, or declined).
14. Enter any additional notes regarding the merge request.
15. Select the status of the email sent to the customer regarding the merge request (sent, not sent, or error).
16. Enter any merge notes.
17. Enter any further notes regarding the merge.
18. Enter the customer's loyalty status.
19. Enter the account number.
20. Enter any additional notes.
21. Enter the customer's email address.
22. Enter the customer's email address again.
23. Enter any merge notes for this merge request.
24. Enter any final merge notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer ID** (`customer_id`, `number`, required): Enter the customer ID associated with the first account you would like to merge.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address associated with the first account you would like to merge.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the customer.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Reason** (`reason`, `text`, required): Specify the reason for merging the accounts.
* **Customer ID 2** (`customer_id_2`, `text`, optional): Enter the customer ID of the second account you would like to merge (if applicable).
* **Account Type** (`account_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of account you are managing (loyalty, rewards, or account).
* **Account Status** (`account_status`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the status of the account you are managing (active or inactive).
* **Account Manager** (`account_manager`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your account manager.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date and time of the merge request.
* **Merge Status** (`merge_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the status of the merge request (pending, approved, or declined).
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required): Enter any additional notes regarding the merge request.
* **Email Status** (`email_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the status of the email sent to the customer regarding the merge request (sent, not sent, or error).
* **Merge Notes** (`merge_notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any merge notes.
* **Request Date Time** (`request_date_time`, `time`, required): Enter the date and time of the request.
* **Customer Loyalty** (`customer_loyalty`, `text`, optional): Enter the customer's loyalty status.
* **Account Number** (`account_number`, `text`, required): Enter the account number.
* **Notes 1** (`notes_1`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes.
* **Notes 2** (`notes_2`, `text`, required): Enter any further notes regarding the merge.
* **Email 1** (`email_1`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Email 2** (`email_2`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's email address again.
* **Merge Notes 1** (`merge_notes_1`, `text`, required): Enter any merge notes.
* **Merge Notes 2** (`merge_notes_2`, `text`, required): Enter any final merge notes.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate information to avoid errors in the merge process.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact your account manager for assistance.
- The form should only be used for customers who have not previously been merged or do not have an existing account.
