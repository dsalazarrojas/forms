# store_operations_inquiry_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The store_operations_inquiry_contact_form is a form used to gather information from customers about store operations inquiries. This form helps the store staff to identify the issue, provide a resolution, and improve customer satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Fill in the required information in the corresponding fields.
2. Ensure that you submit the form after providing all the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Store Operations Inquiry
* **Store Operations Inquiry** (`store_operations_inquiry`, text, not required): This field is for the customer to type their store operations inquiry.
* **Name** (`name`, text, not required): This field is for the customer to enter their name.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, select multiple, not required): Select one or more options for the customer's contact information (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): This field is for the customer to enter their email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): This field is for the customer to enter their phone number.
* **Inquiry Details** (`inquiry_details`, text, not required): This field is for the customer to provide additional details about their store operations inquiry.
* **Store Location** (`store_location`, text, not required): This field is for the customer to enter their store location.
* **Store Operations Submission** (`store_operations_inquiry_submission`, text, not required): This field is for the customer to enter their submission.
* **Store Operations Files** (`store_operations_inquiry_files`, text, not required): This field is for the customer to enter their store operations files.
* **Store Operations Attachments** (`store_operations_inquiry_attachments`, text, not required): This field is for the customer to enter their store operations attachments.
* **Store Operations Comments** (`store_operations_inquiry_comments`, text, not required): This field is for the customer to add comments to their store operations inquiry.
* **Store Operations Status** (`store_operations_inquiry_status`, select one, not required): Select one of the options (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3) to indicate the status of the store operations inquiry.
* **Store Operations Type** (`store_operations_inquiry_type`, select one, not required): Select one of the options (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3) to indicate the type of store operations inquiry.
* **Store Operations Form Submit** (`store_operations_inquiry_form_submit`, text, not required): This field is for the customer to confirm the submission of their store operations inquiry.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide all the required information to facilitate the store staff in addressing your store operations inquiry.
* If you have any attachments or files to support your store operations inquiry, please attach them to the corresponding field.
