# damaged_goods_refund_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to process damaged goods refunds. Please fill it out thoroughly and accurately to ensure a smooth refund process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Customer Service Form** (`Yes' or 'No'): Check the relevant box to indicate whether you have a damaged goods issue.
2. **Product Details**: Type in a detailed description of the product.
3. **Customer Details**: Enter your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
4. **Reason For Refund**: Provide a clear explanation of the reason for the refund.
5. **Product Image URL**: If available, paste the URL of an image of the damaged product.
6. **Product Description**: Describe the product in detail, including its original condition and any defects or issues.
7. **Order ID**: Select the relevant order from the options provided.
8. **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your order invoice.
9. **Customer Email**: Enter your email address to ensure that we can contact you about your refund status.
10. **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number for further contact.
11. **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your refund.
12. **Upload File**: If you have further supporting documents, you can upload them here.
13. **Accept**: Select 'Submit' to send your refund request, or 'Decline' to cancel it.
14. **Declined Reason**: If your refund request is declined, please provide a brief explanation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Service Form**: This checkbox indicates that you have a damaged goods issue and want to proceed with a refund.
- **Product Details**: Provide a detailed description of the product to facilitate the return process.
- **Customer Details**: Fill in your contact information to ensure that we can contact you about your refund status.
- **Reason For Refund**: Describe the reason for the refund accurately and thoroughly.
- **Product Image URL**: Upload an image of the product, if available, to support your refund request.
- **Product Description**: Describe the product's condition and any defects or issues you've encountered.
- **Order ID**: Select the correct order from the options provided to associate with your refund request.
- **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your order invoice.
- **Customer Email**: Enter your email address to receive updates about your refund status.
- **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number for further contact.
- **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your refund request.
- **Upload File**: Upload any supporting documents for your refund request.
- **Accept**: Choose to submit or decline your refund request.
- **Declined Reason**: If your refund request is declined, provide a brief explanation.
