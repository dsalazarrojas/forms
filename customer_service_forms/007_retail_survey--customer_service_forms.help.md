# retail_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "retail_survey" form is a tool used to gather feedback and evaluate customers' experiences with the retail store. It helps identify areas of improvement and inform business decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the primary reason for your visit from the options provided on page 1.
2.  Choose how you found us from the options on page 2.
3.  Enter your age in the number field on page 3.
4.  Rate your experience as satisfying or not on page 4.
5.  Indicate if you have had a previous experience with us on page 5.
6.  Select how often you visit our store on page 6.
7.  Answer whether you visited our store today on page 7.
8.  Choose your mode of transportation to our store on page 8.
9.  If you'd like, enter your phone number on page 9. (Note: This field is optional.)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the primary reason for your visit?** (`page_1`, `select_one`, required):
    This field is designed to understand why customers visit the store. Select one of the provided options: social media, word of mouth, ad, or other.
* **How did you find us?** (`page_2`, `select_one`, required):
    This question is to understand customers' sources of information about the store. Choose one of the options: social media, word of mouth, ad, or other.
* **What is your age?** (`page_3`, `number`, required):
    Please enter your age to provide demographic information.
* **How satisfying is your experience?** (`page_4`, `select_one`, required):
    Rate your experience with the store on a scale of very satisfying, somewhat satisfying, neither satisfying nor unsatisfying, very unsatisfying, or not applicable.
* **Do you have a previous experience with us?** (`page_5`, `select_one`, required):
    This question is to gauge whether customers have visited before. Choose yes or no.
* **How often do you visit our store?** (`page_6`, `select_multiple`, required):
    Select how often you visit our store from the options: very often, often, occasionally, rarely, or never.
* **Have you been to our store today?** (`page_7`, `select_one`, required):
    Indicate whether you visited the store today or not.
* **How did you get to our store?** (`page_8`, `select_multiple`, required):
    Choose your mode of transportation to our store from the options: car, public transportation, on foot, bicycle, or other.
* **Do you have a phone number?** (`page_9`, `text`, optional):
    If you'd like, you can enter your phone number for follow-up or further communication.
