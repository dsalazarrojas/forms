# landscape_customer_satisfaction_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and information from customers about their overall experience with a particular service or product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Overall Experience** (`Overall Experience`, select_one, **not required**): Select one of the options (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor) that best describes your experience with the service.
  - This field helps us understand the level of satisfaction you have with the service.
  - Please choose the option that you feel best represents your experience.

- **Customer Information** (`Customer Information`, text, **not required**): Enter any relevant information about yourself as a customer (e.g., your name, email, or order number).
  - This field is optional, but it helps us understand who you are and provide personalized feedback.
  - If you have an order number or reference number, please enter it here so we can associate your feedback with your account.

- **Feedback on Service** (`Feedback on Service`, text, **not required**): Share any thoughts or comments you have about the service you received.
  - This field is optional, but it gives us valuable insights into how we can improve the service.
  - Please be as specific as possible and provide any relevant details.

- **Additional Feedback** (`Additional Feedback`, text, **not required**): If there's anything else you'd like to share or discuss, please enter it here.
  - This field is optional, but it provides an opportunity to share any additional thoughts or comments.
  - If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to enter them here.

## Tips
- Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your abilities.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask.
