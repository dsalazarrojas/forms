## food_service_satisfaction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "food_service_satisfaction_survey" form is used to collect feedback from customers on their experience with the food service. This form is part of the quality control process to ensure that the food service meets customer expectations and standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the relevant options that reflect your experience with the food service.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) as they are necessary to ensure that your feedback is processed correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Food Service Survey** (`food_service_survey`, text, optional): This field is a general question where you can provide any comments or feedback about your experience with the food service.
* **Food Quality** (`food_quality`, select_one, optional): Select an option that best describes the quality of the food you received. The options are:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Food Appearance** (`food_appearance`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more options that best describe the appearance of the food you received. The options are:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, select_one, required\*): Select an option that best describes the quality of the service you received. The options are:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Food Temperature** (`food_temperature`, number, optional): This field is for you to input the temperature of the food you received. Please note that you should enter a numerical value.
* **Food Price** (`food_price`, number, optional): This field is for you to input the price of the food you received. Please note that you should enter a numerical value.
* **Service Attitude** (`service_attitude`, select_one, required\*): Select an option that best describes the attitude of the service staff you interacted with. The options are:
	+ Positive
	+ Neutral
	+ Negative
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required\*): Select an option that best describes your overall satisfaction with the food service. The options are:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor

## Tips

* Make sure to answer all required fields as they are necessary for processing your feedback correctly.
* You can provide additional comments or feedback in the "Food Service Survey" field.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact the food service staff.
