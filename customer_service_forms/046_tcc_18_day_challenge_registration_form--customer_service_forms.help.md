# tcc_18_day_challenge_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The tcc_18_day_challenge_registration_form is a user-facing form designed to gather information from customers for the 18-day challenge. It allows users to provide their registration details and settings preferences, as well as other relevant information such as enquiry email addresses and notification options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the registration form title in the text field provided.
2. Choose your settings preferences by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' from the dropdown options.
3. Enter your enquiry email address in the text field provided.
4. Enter a notification email address in the text field, which will be used to send notifications about the 18-day challenge.
5. In the second notification field, enter any additional information you'd like to provide.
6. Finally, choose whether to publish the form or not by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' from the dropdown options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form** (`form_title`, text, optional): This field allows you to enter the title of your registration form. 
* **Settings** (`form_settings`, select_multiple, optional): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to select your settings preferences.
* **Enquiry Email** (`form_enquiry_email`, text, optional): Enter your enquiry email address in this field.
* **Notification 1** (`form_enquiry_notification_1`, email, optional): Enter a notification email address to be used for the 18-day challenge.
* **Notification 2** (`form_enquiry_notification_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional information you'd like to provide for the notification.
* **Publish** (`form_publish`, select_one, optional): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to select whether to publish the form or not.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
- If you have any issues or concerns, please contact our customer support team.
- Please note that the form settings and notifications are optional, but if you choose to publish the form, it will be available for other users to see.
