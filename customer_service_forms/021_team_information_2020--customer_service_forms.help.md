# Team Information 2020 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Information 2020 form is designed to collect essential details about your team, including their name, leader, contact information, and any additional notes. This form will help us better understand your team's structure and communication preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: Team Name, Team Leader, email, and Phone.
2. If you're unsure about the Team Leader, select "Other" from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter your team's email address in the email field.
4. Enter your team's phone number in the Phone field.
5. Add any additional notes about your team in the Notes field.
6. Enter your team's address in the Address field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Name** (Text, required): Enter the name of your team, which will be displayed on our internal records.
* **Team Leader** (Select One, required): Choose the leader of your team from the dropdown menu. If you need to select "Other," please provide more information about the team leader in the Notes field.
* **email** (Email, required): Enter your team's email address for internal communication.
* **Phone** (Text, required): Enter your team's phone number for easy contact.
* **Notes** (Note, optional): Add any additional information or comments about your team.
* **Address** (Text, optional): Enter your team's address for our records.
