# Community Service Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to report concerns or complaints about our community services. Please provide details about the issue you have experienced so that our staff can investigate and address it.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your details, including your name and contact information.
2. Select the service area or department related to your complaint.
3. Give a brief description of the issue.
4. Provide any additional information you think would be helpful, such as the date and location of the incident.
5. If you would like to remain anonymous, please select "Yes" under the "Submit Anonymously" section.
6. Finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Complainant Full Name** (`complainant_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name.
* **Contact Email or Phone** (`complainant_contact`, text, required): Enter your contact email or phone number for follow-up and resolution.
* **Service Area or Department** (`service_area`, select_one, required): Choose the service area or department related to your complaint (e.g., Sanitation, Parks, Community Programs, Support Services, or Other).
* **Complaint Summary** (`complaint_summary`, text, required): Provide a clear and concise description of the issue.
* **Date of Incident** (`incident_date`, date, optional): If you know the date of the incident, please enter it. If not, just leave this field blank.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): If the issue is related to a specific location, enter the address or facility name.
* **Desired Resolution** (`desired_resolution`, text, optional): If you have a specific outcome in mind, please describe it.
* **Submit Anonymously** (`anonymous_submission`, select_one, optional): Choose "Yes" if you do not want to be contacted about this complaint.

**Tips**
* Please be clear and concise in your complaint description to help us understand the issue better.
* If you have any supporting documentation or evidence, please attach it to the form or contact our office directly.
* We will make sure to review and address your complaint in a timely and thorough manner.
