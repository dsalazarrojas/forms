# Cafe Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cafe Feedback Form is a tool used to collect customer feedback on their experiences at our cafe. The data collected helps us understand what we do well and what we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name if you'd like to be associated with your feedback.
2. Select the date of your visit to the cafe.
3. If you have an order number, enter it to help us track your feedback.
4. Rate your experience with our service and products using the provided scales.
5. Indicate how likely you are to return to the cafe based on your experience.
6. Provide any suggestions for improvement you have for our service or menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name if you'd like to be associated with your feedback. This is not required, but it will help us know who you are.
* **Visit date** (`visit_date`, date, optional): Select the date of your visit to the cafe. This will help us track your experience over time.
* **Order number** (`order_number`, text, optional): If you have an order number, enter it to help us identify your visit. This is not required if you didn't order anything.
* **Service rating** (`service_rating`, number, required): Rate our service on a scale of 1 (low) to 5 (high). This will help us know how we did in terms of service.
* **Product quality rating** (`product_quality`, number, required): Rate the quality of our products on a scale of 1 (low) to 5 (high). This will help us know how we did in terms of product quality.
* **Cleanliness rating** (`cleanliness`, number, required): Rate the cleanliness of our cafe on a scale of 1 (low) to 5 (high). This will help us know how we did in terms of cleanliness.
* **Likely to return** (`likely_to_return`, select_one, required): Indicate how likely you are to return to the cafe based on your experience. Choose from Very likely, Likely, Neutral, or Unlikely.
* **Suggestions for improvement** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Provide any ideas you have for how we can improve our service or menu. This is your chance to help us make our cafe better!
