# Airport Services Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form allows you to report any issues or incidents you experienced while traveling through our airport. Please use this form to provide us with a detailed description of what happened, the category of the service that was affected, and any other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) in the form, including:
	* Full Name
	* Email Address
	* Phone Number
	* Date of Incident
	* Airport Terminal
	* Category of Service Complaint
	* Specific Location in Airport
	* Desired Resolution
	* Whether you'd like to be contacted about the complaint
2. Provide as much detail as possible in the "Detailed Description of the Issue" field.
3. If applicable, enter the staff member's name or ID who you interacted with.
4. Make sure to include any relevant information, such as your ticket or booking number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (1, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your passport or government-issued ID.
* **Email Address** (2, email, required): Enter your email address so that we can contact you for further information or updates about your complaint.
* **Phone Number** (3, text, required): Enter your phone number for potential follow-up communication.
* **Date of Incident** (4, date, required): Enter the date of the incident you are reporting.
* **Airport Terminal** (5, select_one, required): Select the terminal where the incident occurred.
* **Category of Service Complaint** (7, select_one, required): Choose the category that best describes the issue you experienced.
* **Specific Location in Airport** (8, text, required): Provide the specific location where the incident occurred, such as "Near Gate 15" or "Food Court Level 2".
* **Staff Member Name or ID** (9, text, optional): If you interacted with a staff member, enter their name or ID for further investigation.
* **Detailed Description of the Issue** (10, text, required): Explain what happened in as much detail as possible, providing any relevant context or circumstances.
* **Impact on Your Travel** (11, text, optional): Describe how the incident affected your travel plans or experiences.
* **Desired Resolution** (13, text, required): Specify what you would like us to do to resolve the issue.
* **Follow-up Preference** (14, select_one, required): Choose whether you'd like to be contacted about the complaint.
* **Ticket or Booking Number** (15, text, optional): If relevant, enter your ticket or booking number for further reference.
* **Would you like to be contacted regarding this complaint?** (14, select_one, required): Choose whether you'd like to be contacted about the complaint, and if so, provide an address or contact information.
* **Ticket or Booking Number** (15, text, optional): If relevant, enter your ticket or booking number for further reference.
