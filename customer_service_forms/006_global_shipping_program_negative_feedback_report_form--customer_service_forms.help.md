# Global Shipping Program Negative Feedback Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Shipping Program Negative Feedback Report Form is used to collect information about negative experiences or issues that customers have had with the shipping program. This form helps identify problems and areas for improvement in the shipping process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Ensure you have all the necessary information to provide a clear and accurate report.
*   Enter your base information, including any relevant details about your order and customer information.
*   Describe the issue you experienced, including any images or evidence that may help us better understand the problem.
*   Specify the type of issue you encountered (e.g., Negative, Positive).
*   Select the shipping method used and the speed of shipment (e.g., Standard, Expedited, Priority).
*   Provide any additional feedback or comments about the issue.
*   If applicable, enter your e-mail address and phone number for us to contact you for follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Base Info**:
    Base Info is the first section of the form where you enter your base information, which should include any relevant details about your order.
    Enter any relevant information about your order here.
*   **Customer Info**:
    Customer Info is the section where you can enter your customer information.
    Enter your customer information here.
*   **Order Info**:
    Order Info is the section where you enter information about your order.
    Enter any relevant order information here.
*   **Shipping Info**:
    Shipping Info is the section where you enter information about the shipping method and speed.
    Select the shipping method used (e.g., Ground, Air, Other) and the speed of shipment (e.g., Standard, Expedited, Priority).
*   **Issue Description**:
    Issue Description is the section where you describe the issue you experienced.
    Provide a clear and detailed description of the issue, including any images or evidence that may help us better understand the problem.
*   **Image**:
    Image is the section where you can attach an image of the issue.
    Attach an image or screenshot that illustrates the problem.
*   **Issue Type**:
    Issue Type is the section where you specify the type of issue you encountered.
    Select the type of issue (e.g., Negative, Positive).
*   **Shipping Method**:
    Shipping Method is the section where you select the shipping method used.
    Select the method used (e.g., Ground, Air, Other).
*   **Shipping Speed**:
    Shipping Speed is the section where you select the speed of shipment.
    Select the speed of shipment (e.g., Standard, Expedited, Priority).
*   **Feedback**:
    Feedback is the section where you provide any additional comments or feedback about the issue.
    Provide any additional feedback or comments about the issue.
*   **E-Mail**:
    E-Mail is the section where you enter your e-mail address.
    Enter your e-mail address so we can contact you for follow-up.
*   **Phone**:
    Phone is the section where you enter your phone number.
    Enter your phone number so we can contact you for follow-up.
*   **Date**:
    Date is the section where you enter the date of the issue.
    Enter the date of the issue.
*   **Time**:
    Time is the section where you enter the time of the issue.
    Enter the time of the issue.
*   **Notes**:
    Notes is the section where you can add any additional notes about the submission.
    Enter any additional notes or comments about this submission.
*   **Follow-up**:
    Follow-up is the section where you select the follow-up option.
    Select the follow-up option (e.g., 1 day, 2 days, etc.).
