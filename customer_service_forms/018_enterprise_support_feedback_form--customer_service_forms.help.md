# Enterprise Support Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Enterprise Support Feedback Form is used to capture and track feedback from customers after interacting with our support team.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
2.  Provide as much detail as possible in the issue description.
3.  Select the appropriate options for each field as accurately as possible.
4.  Click the "Submit" button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Enterprise Support Feedback Form Title** (`form_1_title`, `text`, required): This is the title of the feedback form. Please fill out a short and descriptive title for your feedback.
*   **Customer Service** (`customer_service_2`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the services that your issue is related to.
*   **Issue Title** (`issue_title_3`, `text`, required): A brief title of the issue you are providing feedback on.
*   **Issue Description** (`issue_description_4`, `text`, required): A detailed description of the issue you are providing feedback on.
*   **Resolution Status** (`resolution_status_5`, `select_one`, required): Select the current resolution status of the issue.
*   **Resolution Comment** (`resolution_comment_6`, `text`, required): Any comments or explanations about the resolution status.
*   **Feedback Comment** (`feedback_comment_7`, `text`, required): Your comments and suggestions on how the issue could be improved.
*   **Feedback Rating** (`feedback_rating_8`, `number`, required): Rate your satisfaction with the support service (1-5).
*   **Feedback Satisfaction** (`feedback_satisfaction_9`, `select_multiple`, required): Select how satisfied you are with the support service.
*   **Submit Date** (`submit_date_10`, `date`, required): The date you submit your feedback.
*   **Submit Time** (`submit_time_11`, `time`, required): The time you submit your feedback.
*   **Submit Year** (`submit_year_12`, `number`, required): The year you submit your feedback.
*   **Submit Month** (`submit_month_13`, `select_one`, required): The month you submit your feedback.
*   **Submit Day of the Week** (`submit_weekday_15`, `select_one`, required): The day of the week you submit your feedback.
*   **Submit Hour** (`submit_hour_16`, `select_one`, required): The hour you submit your feedback.
*   **Issue Date** (`issue_date_17`, `date`, required): The date the issue occurred.
*   **Issue Year** (`issue_year_18`, `number`, required): The year the issue occurred.
*   **Issue Month** (`issue_month_19`, `select_one`, required): The month the issue occurred.
*   **Issue Day of the Week** (`issue_weekday_20`, `select_one`, required): The day of the week the issue occurred.
*   **Issue Time** (`issue_time_21`, `time`, required): The time the issue occurred.
*   **Issue Location** (`issue_location_22`, `text`, required): The location where the issue occurred.
*   **Issue Subject** (`issue_subject_23`, `text`, required): A brief subject of the issue.
*   **Issue Description** (`issue_description_24`, `text`, required): A detailed description of the issue.
*   **Submitted By** (`submitted_by_25`, `text`, required): Your name or identifier.

Please note that the required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*) and must be filled out.
