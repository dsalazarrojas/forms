### Red Banner Contact Form - Help Guide

**Purpose**
This form is designed to provide a convenient channel for customers to contact our team with their inquiries, feedback, or concerns. We strive to be responsive and attentive to all messages, ensuring that your issues are handled promptly and effectively.

### How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button, which will send us an email with the provided information.
2. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field (required).
3. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field (optional).
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field (required).
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).
6. Enter your message in the "Message" field (required).
7. Select the date of your visit or concern (optional).
8. Select the time of your visit or concern (optional).

### Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents or as you prefer to be addressed.
  - This field is required for us to address you properly and ensure that your concerns are handled effectively.
  - Enter your first name, e.g., John.

- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents or as you prefer to be addressed.
  - If you don't want to enter your last name, you can leave this field blank.
  - Enter your last name, e.g., Smith.

- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter a valid email address associated with your account or a personal email address.
  - This field is required for us to contact you back and ensure that your message is delivered to the right person.
  - Enter a valid email address, e.g., [john.smith@email.com](mailto:john.smith@email.com).

- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you prefer to be contacted via phone calls.
  - If you don't want to enter your phone number, you can leave this field blank.
  - Enter your 10-digit phone number, e.g., 1234567890.

- **Message** (`message`, text, required): Please enter a clear and concise description of your inquiry, feedback, or concern.
  - This field is required for us to understand your issue and provide an effective response.
  - Enter a clear and concise message, e.g., "I would like to inquire about your services."

- **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the date of your visit or concern.
  - If you don't know the exact date or prefer not to enter it, you can leave this field blank.
  - Select a date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

- **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select the time of your visit or concern.
  - If you don't know the exact time or prefer not to enter it, you can leave this field blank.
  - Select a time in the format HH:MM:SS.

### Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate information to ensure that we can contact you effectively.
* If you have any concerns or attachments, please include them in your message.
* You can always contact us directly if you have any pressing issues or need immediate assistance.
