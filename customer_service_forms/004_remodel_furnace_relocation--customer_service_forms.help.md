# Remodel Furnace Relocation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from customers who want to schedule a furnace relocation service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Upload any supporting documents or files related to the furnace relocation.
2. Provide your name and email address.
3. Enter your phone number.
4. Schedule the renovation date and time.
5. Specify the start and end dates of the project.
6. Describe the area where the furnace will be relocated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Upload File** (Upload File, text, Optional): This is a text field where you can upload any supporting documents or files related to the furnace relocation.
* **Name** (Name, text, Optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your records.
* **Email** (Email, email, Optional): Enter a valid email address so we can contact you about your furnace relocation.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, Optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with updates and questions.
* **Schedule Renovation** (Schedule Renovation, date, Optional): Select a date and time for the furnace relocation.
* **Start Date** (Start Date, date, Optional): Enter the start date of the project.
* **End Date** (End Date, date, Optional): Enter the end date of the project.
* **Area** (Area, text, Optional): Describe the location where the furnace will be relocated, including any specific details or instructions.

## Tips
* Make sure to attach any relevant documents or files related to the furnace relocation.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* Please note that the start and end dates are not necessarily the dates of the furnace relocation, but rather the dates for the project's duration.
