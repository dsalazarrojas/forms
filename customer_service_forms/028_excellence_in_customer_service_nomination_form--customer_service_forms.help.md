# excellence_in_customer_service_nomination_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect customer service excellence nominations for recognition or rewards within your organization. This form gathers information from users about a deserving employee or team member who provides outstanding customer service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, start by filling in the User Input section with a brief description of the nominee's exceptional service.
2. Next, enter the User's Name who is submitting the nomination.
3. Provide the Nominee's Email address.
4. Select the Department where the nominee works from the list of available options.
5. Choose the type of recognition or award the nominee deserves (Recognition, Award, or Acknowledgment).
6. If applicable, choose all types of recognition the nominee deserves (All of the Above).
7. Enter the Nominee's Name.
8. Provide the Nominee's Job Title.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Input** (`User Input`, `text`, optional): A brief description of the nominee's exceptional service. Please keep it concise and descriptive.
- **User Name** (`User Name`, `text`, optional): The name of the person submitting the nomination.
- **Email** (`Email`, `email`, optional): The email address of the person submitting the nomination.
- **Department** (`Department`, `select_one`, required): Select the department where the nominee works from the available options.
- **Nomination Type** (`Nomination Type`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the type of recognition the nominee deserves. You can select one or more from the options.
- **Nominee's Name** (`Nominee's Name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person being nominated.
- **Nominee's Job Title** (`Nominee's Job Title`, `text`, optional): Enter the nominee's job title or position.

## Tips
- Make sure to keep your nomination concise and clear.
- Ensure that you're submitting a new nomination and not a duplicate.
- Double-check that all required fields are filled out correctly.
- If you need further assistance, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
