# customer_service_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The customer_service_form is a tool for customers to submit their queries or feedback to the customer service team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select one of the contact methods: Phone, Email, or Chat.
2. Enter your contact details, if applicable.
3. Type in your message to describe the issue or concern you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Method** (Phone, Email, Chat): Choose how you want to be contacted by the customer service team.
* **Contact Details** (text, optional): If you selected Phone as your contact method, fill in your phone number in this field. If you selected Email, fill in your email address.
* **Message** (text, optional): Type in the message you want to send to the customer service team.
* **Customer Name** (text, optional): If you prefer to be known by a name while communicating with us, fill it in here.
* **Customer Email** (email, optional): If you selected Email as your contact method, fill in your email address here. If not, this field will be blank.
* **Customer Phone** (text, optional): If you selected Phone as your contact method, fill in your phone number here.
* **Customer Subject** (text, optional): Briefly describe the subject or issue you are contacting us about.
* **Message** (text, optional): Type in a detailed message describing the issue or concern you are experiencing.
