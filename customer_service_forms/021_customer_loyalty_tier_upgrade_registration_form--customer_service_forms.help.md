# customer_loyalty_tier_upgrade_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The customer loyalty tier upgrade registration form is a crucial tool for customers to request a loyalty tier upgrade. By filling out this form, customers can inform the service team about their current loyalty level, desired level, and reasons for the upgrade, allowing the team to review and process their request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **Customer Name**, a required field that will help the service team identify you.
2.  Provide your **Email**, a required field that will allow the service team to contact you about your upgrade.
3.  Enter your **Phone**, a required field that will enable the service team to contact you if needed.
4.  Choose your **Current Loyalty Level**, either High or Low, depending on your current status.
5.  Choose your **Desired Loyalty Level**, either High or Low, depending on your desired status.
6.  Enter the **Upgrade Reason**, a required field where you can explain why you want to upgrade your loyalty tier.
7.  Enter the **When Did You Join?**, a required field that will help the service team track your account history.
8.  Enter the **Last Upgrade**, a required field that will help the service team track your upgrade history.
9.  Optionally, you can provide additional **Notes**, if you want to add any extra information about your request.
10. Check if you want to be **Assigned To A User**, either Yes or No, depending on your preference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (Customer Name, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter the email address we have on file for you.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Current Loyalty Level** (Current Loyalty Level, select_one, required): Choose your current loyalty level, either High or Low.
* **Desired Loyalty Level** (Desired Loyalty Level, select_one, required): Choose your desired loyalty level, either High or Low.
* **Upgrade Reason** (Upgrade Reason, text, required): Explain why you want to upgrade your loyalty tier.
* **When Did You Join?** (When Did You Join?, date, required): Enter the date you joined the service.
* **Last Upgrade** (Last Upgrade, date, required): Enter the date of your last upgrade.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, optional): Enter any additional information about your request.
* **Assigned To A User** (Assigned To A User, select_one, optional): Check Yes if you want to be assigned to a specific user for handling your request.
