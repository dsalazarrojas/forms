# Beauty Service Client Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beauty Service Client Application Form is designed to gather essential information about customers who wish to book an appointment at the salon.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form by following the instructions on each page.
2. Enter your contact information, including your name, email address, phone number, and home address.
3. Select your preferred appointment time and services.
4. List any medical conditions or allergies that may affect your service.
5. Answer the questions regarding your skin and hair type.
6. Confirm your agreement to terms and conditions, privacy policy, and consent to receive services.
7. Indicate your interest in receiving marketing communications.
8. Submit the form to complete your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, `email`, required): Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the best phone number to reach you at.
* **Home Address** (`client_address`, `text`, required): Enter your full street address, including city and zip code.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Services Interested In** (`services_interested`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the services that interest you (Hair Styling, Hair Coloring, Hair Cutting, etc.).
* **Preferred Appointment Time** (`preferred_appointment_time`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred appointment time (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, etc.).
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `select_multiple`, required): Select any medical conditions that may affect your service (Pregnancy, Diabetes, etc.).
* **Skin Type** (`skin_type`, `select_one`, not required): Select your skin type (Normal, Dry, Oily, etc.).
* **Hair Type** (`hair_type`, `select_one`, not required): Select your hair type (Straight, Wavy, Curly, etc.).
* **Product Allergies** (`product_allergies`, `text`, not required): List any allergies to beauty products.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, `text`, not required): List any medications that may affect your service.
* **Previous Salon or Service Provider** (`previous_salon`, `text`, not required): If applicable, enter the name of your previous salon or service provider.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_heard_about_us`, `select_one`, required): Select how you found out about our services (Google Search, Facebook, etc.).
* **Referral Name** (`referral_source`, `text`, not required): If applicable, enter the name of the person who referred you to our salon.
* **Special Requests or Accommodations** (`special_requests`, `text`, not required): Enter any special requests or accommodations you may need.
* **Terms and Conditions Acceptance** (`terms_acceptance`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Privacy Policy Agreement** (`privacy_policy`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you agree to our privacy policy.
* **Consent to Receive Services** (`consent_to_service`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you consent to receive beauty services.
* **Receive Marketing Communications** (`marketing_communications`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you would like to receive marketing communications.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, `date`, required): The date you submitted this application.

## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
* Take your time to fill out the form carefully.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact the salon.
