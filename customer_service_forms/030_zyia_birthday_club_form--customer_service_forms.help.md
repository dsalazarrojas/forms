# zyia_birthday_club_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The zyia_birthday_club_form is used to collect information from customers interested in joining the Zyia Birthday Club. This form helps our team understand who's joining the club and tailor our marketing efforts and customer service accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the `Customer Name` field.
2. Provide your email address in the `Email` field.
3. Select a valid date for your birthdate in the `Birthdate` field.
4. Choose whether you belong to a VIP group or not in the `VIP Group` field. You can select one or more options.
5. (Optional) If you'd like to share a special message with us, you can type it in the `Special Message` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, Required/Optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Required/Optional): Enter your valid email address where you'd like us to contact you.
* **Birthdate** (`birthdate`, date, Required/Optional): Enter a valid date in the format `mm/dd/yyyy` to indicate your birthdate.
* **VIP Group** (`vip_group`, select_multiple, Required/Optional): Select one or more options to indicate if you belong to a VIP group.
* **Special Message** (`special_message`, text, Required/Optional): (Optional) Share a special message or note with us, if you'd like.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your email address for errors before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any of the options or fields, feel free to ask our team for assistance.
