# Covid 19 Customer Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Covid 19 Customer Check In Form is designed to collect essential information from customers about their recent contact with our medical office. This form is intended to help our staff better understand your needs and provide more efficient support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to ensure you're not duplicating any information you may have already provided.
2. Fill out the required fields, which are marked as such.
3. Enter your information accurately and completely.
4. Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Check In Information
* **Check In Information** (`customer_check_in_form`, text, optional): This field is not currently being used, but you can enter any additional information you feel is relevant to your interaction with our medical office.

### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, text, optional): Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address. This will help us contact you regarding your visit or any other concerns.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number in the format XXX-XXX-XXXX or XXX.XXX.XXXX.

### Medical Office
* **Medical Office** (`medical_office`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you are visiting a medical office for your appointment. Please select 'Yes' if you are visiting, and 'No' otherwise.

### Vaccine Type
* **Vaccine Type** (`vaccine_type`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you have received a vaccine or not. Please select 'Yes' if you have, and 'No' otherwise.

### Vaccination Date
* **Vaccination Date** (`vaccination_date`, date, optional): If you have received a vaccine, enter the date of vaccination in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Vaccination Time
* **Vaccination Time** (`vaccination_time`, time, optional): If you have received a vaccine, enter the time of vaccination in the format HH:MM AM/PM.

### Notes
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional information or comments about your visit or vaccination.
