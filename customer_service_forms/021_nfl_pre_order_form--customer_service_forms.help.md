# Nfl Pre Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to submit their pre-orders for Nfl-related products or services. It is intended to gather necessary information from the customer, including their contact details, order preferences, and any additional notes they may want to include.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Select the date and time of your purchase from the "Date of Purchase" and "Time of Purchase" fields, respectively.
5. Choose your preferred team from the "Team Preferred" field.
6. Select the type of ticket you would like to purchase from the "Ticket Type" field (multiple selections allowed).
7. Enter the number of tickets you would like to purchase in the "Ticket Count" field.
8. Choose your preferred merchandise from the "Merchandise" field.
9. Finally, include any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name in this field.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Provide your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Purchase** (`date_of_purchase`, date, required: false): Select the date of your purchase.
* **Time of Purchase** (`time_of_purchase`, time, required: false): Select the time of your purchase.
* **Team Preferred** (`team_preferred`, select_one, required: false): Choose your preferred team.
* **Ticket Type** (`ticket_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the type of ticket you would like to purchase (multiple selections allowed).
* **Ticket Count** (`ticket_count`, number, required: false): Enter the number of tickets you would like to purchase.
* **Merchandise** (`merchandise_preferred`, select_one, required: false): Choose your preferred merchandise.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Include any additional notes or comments you would like to add to your order.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate information to ensure smooth order processing.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our customer service team.
* Please note that the availability of certain options may be subject to change.
* Please review our terms and conditions before submitting your order.
