# Copywriter Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Copywriter Intake Form is a tool for copywriters and their clients to collect and share information about companies that require content creation services. This form helps the copywriter to get a better understanding of the company's needs, target audience, and services offered.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information about the company, including its name, website, and contact details.
2. Select the services offered by the company from the options provided.
3. Choose the target audience of the company.
4. Provide information about the company's social media presence.
5. Fill in any additional details about the company, such as its size, type, status, and revenue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Data** (`form_data`, text, optional): This field is a description of the company's needs and requirements for the project.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): The name of the company that needs content creation services.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, optional): The website of the company that needs content creation services.
* **Business Description** (`business_description`, text, optional): A brief description of the company's business.
* **Services Offered** (`services_offered`, select_multiple, optional): Check the services offered by the company (e.g., Yes, No).
* **Products Offered** (`products_offered`, select_multiple, optional): Check the products offered by the company (e.g., Yes, No).
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, select_one, optional): Choose the target audience of the company (e.g., Yes, No).
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): A person in charge of the company.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): The email of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): The phone number of the contact person.
* **Social Media** (`social_media`, select_multiple, optional): Check the social media presence of the company (e.g., Yes, No).
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, optional): The address of the company.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, optional): The zip code of the company.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): The city of the company.
* **Country** (`country`, text, optional): The country of the company.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): The state of the company.
* **Company Description** (`company_description`, text, optional): A description of the company.
* **Industry** (`company_industry`, select_one, optional): Choose the industry of the company (e.g., Yes, No).
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, optional): Choose the size of the company (e.g., Yes, No).
* **Company Type** (`company_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of the company (e.g., Yes, No).
* **Company Status** (`company_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the status of the company (e.g., Active, Inactive).
* **Company Founded** (`company_founded`, date, optional): The date the company was founded.
* **Company Revenue** (`company_revenue`, number, optional): The revenue of the company.
* **Company Facebook** (`company_facebook`, select_one, optional): Check if the company has a Facebook presence (e.g., Yes, No).
* **Company Twitter** (`company_twitter`, select_one, optional): Check if the company has a Twitter presence (e.g., Yes, No).
