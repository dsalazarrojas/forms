The purpose of this form is to collect information about an image advertisement, including its category, description, image URL, and other relevant details. This form is required for customers to provide their image advertisements.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out each field with the required information.

3.  Enter a brief description of your image advertisement.
4.  Add the URL of the image you want to advertise.
