# Instagram Support Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Instagram Support Feedback Form is a tool designed to collect feedback from users on their experience with our support services. By filling out this form, you can share your thoughts and suggestions on how we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your email address in the "User Email" field.
2. Type or describe your issue or feedback in the "User Message" field.
3. Select the relevant option that best describes your product issue type.
4. Describe your issue in more detail in the "Product Issue Description" field.
5. If applicable, choose the category that best fits your product issue.
6. Rate your overall satisfaction with the support provided in the "User Satisfaction" field.
7. Choose whether your feedback is of the type "Yes" or "No" in the "Feedback Type" field.
8. Add any additional comments or details in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Email (user_email)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you for further assistance, if needed.
* **User Message (user_message)**: Type a detailed description of your issue or feedback. Be as descriptive as possible.
* **Product Issue Type (product_issue_type)**: Choose "Yes" if you had an issue with the product, "No" otherwise.
* **Product Issue Description (product_issue_description)**: Describe your issue in more detail.
* **Product Issue Category (product_issue_category)**: Choose "Yes" if your issue is related to a specific category.
* **Support Request Description (support_request_description)**: If you have a specific request for support, type it here.
* **User Satisfaction (user_satisfaction)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the support provided.
* **Feedback Type (feedback_type)**: Choose "Yes" if this feedback is intended as feedback, "No" otherwise.
* **User Phone (user_phone)**: Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for further assistance.
* **Additional Comments (additional_comments)**: Share any additional thoughts or suggestions you have on how we can improve our support services.
