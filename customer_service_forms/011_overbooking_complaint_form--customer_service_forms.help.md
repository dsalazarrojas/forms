# overbooking_complaint_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "overbooking_complaint_form" is used to report an overbooking issue, allowing our customer service team to investigate and resolve the issue effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide a detailed description of the overbooking issue in the Complaint Description field.
2. Select the category of the issue from the Complaint Category options.
3. Choose how you would like to be contacted from the Contact Method options.
4. Enter your contact information in the Contact Info field.
5. Optionally, select a date if the issue occurred on a specific date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Complaint Description (text)**: A field to provide a detailed description of the overbooking issue you are experiencing. Please include any relevant dates, times, and location details.
* **Complaint Category (select_multiple)**: Choose the category of the issue from the options provided. This helps the customer service team to better understand the issue and prioritize their response.
* **Contact Method (select_one)**: Select how you would like to be contacted by the customer service team, such as by email, phone, or in-app message.
* **Contact Info (text)**: Enter your contact information, including your name, email address, or phone number, so that the customer service team can contact you efficiently.
* **Date (date)**: If the issue occurred on a specific date, please select it to help the customer service team investigate the issue further.
