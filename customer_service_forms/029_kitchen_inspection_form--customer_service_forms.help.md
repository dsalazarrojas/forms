# Kitchen Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kitchen Inspection Form is used to assess the condition of a kitchen and identify areas for improvement. It helps kitchen staff or management to inspect and report on various aspects of the kitchen, including food storage, temperature control, personal hygiene, facilities, equipment, supervision, and potential hazards.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully to understand what is being asked.
2. Answer each question honestly and accurately based on your observation or knowledge of the kitchen.
3. Use the options provided for select questions, and type your response in the text fields.
4. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please use the "Additional Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Food Storage** (`food_storage`, select_multiple): This question asks about the state of food storage in the kitchen, including how food is stored, labeled, and handled. Select all that apply from the options:
	+ Bread
	+ Meat
	+ Poultry
* **Temperature Control** (`temperature_control`, text): This question asks about the condition of temperature control systems, such as refrigerators and freezers. Check the current temperature settings and ensure they are within the safe range.
* **Personal Hygiene** (`personal_hygiene`, text): This question asks about the overall cleanliness and maintenance of facilities related to personal hygiene, including sinks, toilets, and showers.
* **Facilities** (`facilities`, text): This question asks about the condition of kitchen facilities, including floors, walls, and equipment. Check for cleanliness and maintenance.
* **Equipment** (`equipment`, text): This question asks about the condition and functionality of kitchen equipment, including stoves, dishwashers, and utensils.
* **Supervision** (`supervision`, text): This question asks about the level of supervision or monitoring in the kitchen, including the head chef or manager's presence.
* **Hazards** (`hazards`, select_one): Identify any potential hazards, such as spills, leaks, or broken equipment. Select "Yes" if you find any hazards, or "No" if there are none.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note): This field is for any additional comments or concerns you may have about the kitchen's condition.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, note): This field is for any further comments or suggestions on how to improve the kitchen's condition.
* **Date** (`date`, date): Enter the date you completed this form.
* **Time** (`time`, time): Enter the time you completed this form.
