# Mystic Client Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mystic Client Waiver Form is a general waiver form used to collect information about the client, their contact details, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your client's name.
2. Enter the date and time of your visit.
3. Provide your address, city, state, and zip code.
4. Enter your phone number and email address.
5. If applicable, provide emergency contact information, including the name, relationship, and phone number of the emergency contact.
6. Sign and date your section.
7. If applicable, sign and date the emergency contact section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your client's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of your visit.
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of your visit.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your address for contact purposes.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the city where you live.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your state of residence.
* **Zip** (`zip`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Alternate** (`emergency_contact_phone_alternate`, `text`, required/optional): Enter an alternate phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, `note`, required/optional): Sign and date your section.
* **Client Signature Date** (`client_signature_date`, `date`, required/optional): Date your signature.
* **Client Signature Time** (`client_signature_time`, `time`, required/optional): Time your signature.
* **Emergency Contact Signature** (`emergency_contact_signature`, `note`, required/optional): Sign and date your emergency contact's section.
* **Emergency Contact Signature Date** (`emergency_contact_signature_date`, `date`, required/optional): Date your emergency contact's signature.
* **Emergency Contact Signature Time** (`emergency_contact_signature_time`, `time`, required/optional): Time your emergency contact's signature.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled accurately and completely.
* Make sure to sign and date your section and your emergency contact's section.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your representative.
