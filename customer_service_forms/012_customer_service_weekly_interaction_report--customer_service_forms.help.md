# Customer Service Weekly Interaction Report - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Customer Service Weekly Interaction Report is used to record and document customer interactions that take place during a week. This form helps customer service teams to track and analyze customer interactions, improving their quality of service, identifying patterns and trends, and making data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by ensuring that you have reviewed all relevant customer service records and interactions from the past week.
2. Fill out the form fields as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (customer name): Enter the name of the customer that you interacted with.
* **Date** (date): Select the date of the interaction from the calendar picker.
* **Time Spent** (time spent, optional): Enter the approximate time you spent interacting with the customer.
* **Issue Description** (issue description): Describe the customer's issue or concern.
* **Resolution Method** (resolution method, select multiple):
	+ Select all methods that applied to resolve the issue.
* **Outcome** (outcome, select one):
	+ Select the outcome of the interaction:
		- Resolved: The issue was resolved to the customer's satisfaction.
		- Unresolved: The issue remains unresolved.
		- Pending: The issue is still pending further action or resolution.
		- Other: Please select this option if the outcome does not fit any of the above categories.
* **Customer Feedback** (customer feedback, optional): Record any customer feedback or comments about the interaction.

## Tips

- Be as detailed as possible when filling out the issue description and customer feedback fields.
- If you are unsure about any information, please clarify or skip this form.
- This form is for reporting weekly interactions, so if you need to report an incident or issue that occurred outside of this timeframe, use the relevant incident or issue reporting form instead.
