# Premier COVID Testing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Premier COVID Testing Form is designed to streamline the testing process and improve the accuracy of test results.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's information (Patient Name, Patient Email, and Patient Phone).
2. Select the correct test type (Yes or No).
3. Provide the sample collection date and time.
4. Fill in any additional information, such as Test Results, Sample Type, Sample Location, Sample Condition, and Test Result Note.
5. Sign and date the form with your name and title (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information**: This section is where you will fill in the patient's details.
	+ **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name.
	+ **Patient Email**: Enter the patient's email address.
	+ **Patient Phone**: Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Test Type**: Select 'Yes' if the patient requires testing, 'No' otherwise.
* **Sample Collection Date**: Enter the date when the sample was collected.
* **Sample Collection Time**: Enter the time when the sample was collected.
* **Test Results**: Enter the result of the test (if available).
* **Sample Type**: Enter the type of sample collected (e.g., blood, saliva, etc.).
* **Sample Location**: Enter the location where the sample was collected.
* **Sample Condition**: Enter any relevant conditions or details about the sample.
* **Test Result Note**: Enter any additional notes about the test results.
* **Patient Age**: Enter the patient's age.
* **Patient Gender**: Enter the patient's gender.
* **Patient Weight**: Enter the patient's weight.
* **Sample Container**: Enter the container used for the sample (e.g., tube, vial, etc.).
* **Test Date**: Enter the date the test was conducted.
* **Test Time**: Enter the time the test was conducted.
* **Test Result**: Enter the final result of the test (e.g., positive, negative, etc.).
* **Doctor Signature**: Sign and date with your name and title (if applicable).
* **Doctor Name**: Enter your name and title.
* **Doctor Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Doctor Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Patient Signature**: Sign and date with your name.
* **Sample ID**: Enter the ID of the sample.

Please fill in all required fields accurately to ensure smooth and efficient processing of the test.
