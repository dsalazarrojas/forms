# customer_service_agent_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about potential customers interested in becoming a customer service agent. The form is used to determine the suitability of the individual for the position and to schedule an interview or assessment if they pass the initial evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, please fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Phone Number if you have one.
3. Select one option from the Select One field.
4. Select one or multiple options from the Select Multiple field.
5. If applicable, enter a date and time when you are available for an interview.
6. Enter notes about your qualifications or experience as a customer service agent.
7. Enter any additional notes about yourself as a customer service agent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted for further instructions.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number if you have one.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one option from the list to demonstrate your preferences or interests.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or multiple options from the list to demonstrate your skills or qualifications.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Enter a date when you are available for an interview.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Enter a time when you are available for an interview.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required: false): Enter any notes about your qualifications or experience as a customer service agent.
* **Note 1** (`note_1`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes about yourself as a customer service agent.
