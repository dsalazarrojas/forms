# Adoption Application Submissions - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide detailed information about yourself and your ability to provide a suitable home for a pet. It helps our team understand your preferences, experience, and lifestyle, which will enable us to match you with the right pet for you.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form carefully and truthfully, providing as much detail as possible.
- Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
- If you need help or have questions, feel free to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First Name** (`applicant_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification or driver's license.
  - Example: John

- **Last Name** (`applicant_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification or driver's license.
  - Example: Doe

- **Email Address** (`applicant_email`, email, required): Enter your primary email address that we can use to contact you.
  - Format: `example@example.com`

- **Phone Number** (`applicant_phone`, text, required): Enter your best phone number that we can use to contact you.
  - Example: 123-456-7890

- **Street Address** (`applicant_address`, text, required): Enter your current street address.
  - Example: 123 Main Street

- **City** (`applicant_city`, text, required): Enter the city of your residence.
  - Example: Anytown

- **State** (`applicant_state`, text, required): Enter the state or province of your residence.
  - Example: CA

- **Zip Code** (`applicant_zip`, text, required): Enter your postal code.
  - Example: 12345

- **Pet You Are Interested In** (`interested_pet_name`, text, required): Enter the name or ID of the pet you're interested in adopting.
  - Example: P123

- **Why Are You Interested in This Pet** (`pet_preference_reason`, text, required): Explain why you're interested in this pet, e.g., because you like its age or its breed.
  - Example: I want to provide a loving home for a young and playful companion.

- **Previous Pet Experience** (`previous_pet_experience`, text, required): Share your previous experience with pets, including any successes and failures.
  - Example: I had a cat for 10 years and it was a great companion.

- **Why Are You Adopting Now** (`why_adopting_now`, text, required): Explain why you're adopting a pet now, e.g., because you've recently moved to a new home or because you've lost a previous pet.
  - Example: I recently moved to a new home and I'm ready for a new furry friend.

- **Describe Your Home Environment** (`home_environment`, text, required): Provide details about your living space, including the number of people living with you and any pets you already have.
  - Example: I live in a small apartment with one person and a cat.

- **Daily Care Plan for Pet** (`daily_care_plan`, text, required): Describe how you plan to care for the pet daily, including feeding, exercise, and grooming.
  - Example: I will feed the pet twice a day and provide regular exercise and grooming.

- **Veterinary Care Commitment** (`veterinary_care`, select_one, required): Choose your level of commitment to regular veterinary care:
  - Example: Yes, definitely

- **Your Lifestyle and Activity Level** (`lifestyle_activity`, text, optional): Describe your daily activities and how they impact your ability to care for a pet.
  - Example: I work from home and have a flexible schedule, so I can provide plenty of attention and care.

- **Any Pet Behavior Concerns or Special Needs** (`pet_behavior_concerns`, text, optional): List any concerns or special needs you might have regarding pet behavior.
  - Example: My previous cat had anxiety and required a lot of attention.

- **References Available** (`references_available`, select_one, required): Choose whether you have references available:
  - Example: Yes

- **Agree to Follow-up Contact** (`agree_follow_up`, select_one, required): Choose whether you agree to follow-up contact:
  - Example: Yes

- **Agreement Acceptance** (`signature_agreement`, select_one, required): Choose whether you accept our adoption agreement:
  - Example: Yes

Please note that all required fields have a green checkmark indicating that they must be completed before submitting the form.
