# Initial Client Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect information from clients regarding their initial interview details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Fill in your Address.
4. Enter your Email address.
5. Provide your Phone number.
6. Enter your Date of Birth in the correct format (dd/mm/yyyy).
7. Enter your Job Title.
8. Enter your Industry.
9. Enter your Company.
10. Select your Role.
11. Enter your Position.
12. Enter your Department.
13. Enter a brief description of your current Job Description.
14. Enter your Work Experience.
15. Enter your Education Background.
16. List your relevant Skills.
17. Provide information about your References.
18. Enter your Job Status.
19. Enter the type of Job you are applying for (Job Type).
20. Enter your current Job Level.
21. Enter the Job Category you are applying for.
22. Enter your Job Role.
23. Enter the location of the Job.
24. Enter a brief description of the Job.
25. Add any additional Comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): This field requires the first name of the client.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): This field requires the last name of the client.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): This field requires the client's address.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): This field requires the client's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): This field requires the client's phone number.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): This field requires the client's date of birth in the correct format (dd/mm/yyyy).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): This field requires the client's job title.
* **Industry** (`industry`, text, required): This field requires the industry the client is in.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): This field requires the client's company.
* **Role** (`role`, text, required): This field requires the role the client is applying for.
* **Position** (`position`, text, required): This field requires the client's position.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): This field requires the client's department.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required): This field requires a brief description of the client's current job.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, required): This field requires the client's work experience.
* **Education Background** (`education_background`, text, required): This field requires the client's education background.
* **Skills** (`skills`, text, required): This field requires the client's relevant skills.
* **References** (`references`, text, required): This field requires information about the client's references.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, text, required): This field requires the client's job status.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, text, required): This field requires the type of Job the client is applying for.
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, text, required): This field requires the client's current job level.
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, text, required): This field requires the job category the client is applying for.
* **Job Role** (`job_role`, text, required): This field requires the client's job role.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, text, required): This field requires the location of the job.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required): This field requires a brief description of the job.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): This field requires any additional comments from the client.

Note: All fields are required, except for those marked as optional.
