# Marketing Service Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to capture information from customers about their marketing service requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer service order form with a brief description of the services you are requesting.
2. Provide your email address in the E-mail field.
3. If you have a phone number, enter it in the Phone field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Service Order Form** (Required/Optional): This field is where you can provide a brief description of the services you are requesting. This can include any specific details or requirements you have for the services.
* **E-mail** (Required/Optional): Please enter your email address so we can contact you about your service request.
* **Phone** (Required/Optional): If you have a phone number, enter it so we can contact you about your service request.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide clear and concise information in the customer service order form.
* Double-check your email address for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
