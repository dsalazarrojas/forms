# Customer Support Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help us understand your experience with our customer support services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Answer each question carefully and thoroughly.
2. Make sure to complete all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*) next to the label.
3. Click the "Submit" button at the end of the form to submit your feedback.
4. If you want to request support, please select "True" in the "Do you want to request support?" field.
5. You can submit your feedback on the same day or at a later time, it's up to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Support Evaluation Form** (`customer_support_evaluation_form_page_1`, text, not required): This is the title of the form, please don't worry about it, just type your name here.
* **Contact Information** (`customer_support_evaluation_form_page_2`, text, not required): Please enter your name, email, and phone number so we can get in touch with you if needed.
* **Evaluation Form** (`evaluation_form_support_services_page_3`, text, not required): This is where you can write your feedback about our customer support services.
* **Customer Name** (`contact_name`, text, not required): Enter your name, this will help us know who is submitting the feedback.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, not required): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you if needed.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Notes** (`contact_notes`, note, not required): This is where you can add any additional comments or notes about your experience.
* **Rating** (`evaluation_rating`, number, not required): Please select a rating from 1 to 5 to indicate how satisfied you are with our support services.
* **Comments** (`evaluation_comments`, text, not required): This is where you can add any comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Do you want to request support?** (`contact_request`, select_one, not required): If you need help, please select "True" to let us know.
* **Submit** (`evaluation_form_submit`, select_multiple, not required): Please select "Submit" to submit your feedback, or "Cancel" to discard your feedback, or "Save and Continue" to save your feedback and continue later.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_form_date`, date, not required): This is where we will record the date and time when you submit your feedback.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_form_time`, time, not required): This is where we will record the time when you submit your feedback.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
* If you have any issues or problems that we can solve, select "True" in the "Do you want to request support?" field.
* If you need help with anything, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* Your feedback is valuable to us, thank you for taking the time to provide it.
* If you want to save your feedback and continue later, select "Save and Continue".
