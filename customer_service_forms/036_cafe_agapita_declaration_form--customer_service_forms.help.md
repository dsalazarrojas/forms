# Cafe Agapita Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cafe Agapita Declaration Form is used to collect information from visitors about their health status and recent activities, helping the cafe maintain a safe environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the "Visitor name" field with your name as it appears on your official identification.
2. Enter the "Visit date" field with the date you are visiting the cafe.
3. Answer the "I declare I have no COVID symptoms" question truthfully, indicating whether you have any COVID symptoms or not.
4. Answer the "Recent travel history" question, indicating whether you have traveled outside the local area in the past 14 days.
5. Answer the "Contact with confirmed cases" question, indicating whether you have had close contact with anyone with confirmed COVID cases in the past 14 days.
6. If you wish, you can enter your temperature reading in the "Temperature reading" field.
7. Enter your signature or typed name as it appears on your official identification.
8. Provide any additional health comments in the "Comments" field if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Visitor name** (text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Visit date** (date, required): Enter the date of your visit to the cafe.
* **I declare I have no COVID symptoms** (select_one, required): Answer truthfully whether you have any COVID symptoms or not.
	+ True: I do not have any COVID symptoms.
	+ False: I have COVID symptoms.
* **Recent travel history** (select_one, required): Answer whether you have traveled outside the local area in the past 14 days.
	+ True: I have traveled outside the local area in the past 14 days.
	+ False: I have not traveled outside the local area in the past 14 days.
* **Contact with confirmed cases** (select_one, required): Answer whether you have had close contact with anyone with confirmed COVID cases in the past 14 days.
	+ True: I have had close contact with confirmed cases.
	+ False: I have not had close contact with confirmed cases.
* **Temperature reading** (number, optional): If you have taken your temperature, enter it here.
* **Signature reference** (text, required): Enter your typed name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Comments** (text, optional): Provide any additional health comments or concerns.

## Tips
- Please answer the questions truthfully to ensure the safety of other visitors and staff at the cafe.
- If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask a staff member for assistance.
- If you have any concerns or questions, do not hesitate to contact the cafe management.
