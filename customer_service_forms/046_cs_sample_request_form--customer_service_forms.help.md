# CS Sample Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers who are requesting samples or products from the company.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, providing your personal and business contact information.
2. Choose the type of sample or product you are interested in.
3. Specify the number of samples you would like to request.
4. Describe how you intend to use the sample.
5. Choose your preferred timeline for decision-making.
6. Indicate your level of business interest in the product or service.
7. If you have an estimated budget for this category, please enter it.
8. If you are not the decision-maker, please indicate who is.
9. Provide your shipping address and city, state, and ZIP code.
10. Specify any special requirements or instructions for delivery.
11. Choose your preferred contact method.
12. Indicate whether you would like the company to follow up with you about your sample.
13. Add any additional comments or questions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (requestor_name, text, required): Please enter your full name.
* **Your Email Address** (requestor_email, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Your Phone Number** (requestor_phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Company Name** (company_name, text, optional): Enter your company or organization name.
* **Date of Request** (request_date, date, required): Choose the date you are requesting the sample.
* **Type of Sample Requested** (sample_type, select_one, required): Choose the type of sample or product you are interested in (e.g., Product sample, Material sample, etc.).
* **Specific Product or Service Interested In** (product_interest, text, required): Specify the specific product or service you are interested in.
* **Number of Samples Requested** (sample_quantity, number, required): Enter the number of samples you would like to request.
* **Intended Use of Sample** (intended_use, text, required): Describe how you plan to use the sample.
* **Expected Timeline for Decision** (expected_timeline, select_one, optional): Choose your expected timeline for making a decision.
* **Level of Business Interest** (business_interest, select_one, required): Indicate your level of business interest in the product or service.
* **Estimated Annual Budget** (annual_budget, select_one, optional): Enter your estimated annual budget for this category (if known).
* **Are You the Decision Maker** (decision_maker, select_one, required): If you are not the decision-maker, please indicate who is.
* **Shipping Address** (shipping_address, text, required): Enter your shipping address.
* **City, State, ZIP Code** (shipping_city_state_zip, text, required): Enter your city, state, and ZIP code.
* **Special Requirements or Instructions** (special_requirements, text, optional): Specify any special requirements or instructions for delivery.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (contact_preference, select_one, required): Choose your preferred contact method (e.g., Email, Phone call, etc.).
* **Permission to Follow Up** (follow_up_permission, select_one, required): Indicate whether you would like the company to follow up with you about your sample.
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (additional_notes, text, optional): Add any additional comments or questions you may have.
