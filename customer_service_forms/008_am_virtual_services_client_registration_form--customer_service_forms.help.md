# AM Virtual Services Client Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential clients interested in hiring virtual services from AM Virtual Services. The form aims to gather essential details about the client's business needs, preferences, and requirements to tailor services accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your complete legal name.
2. Provide your professional email address for service communications.
3. Enter your contact phone number for service coordination.
4. If applicable, provide your business name and select the type of business you operate in.
5. If applicable, indicate the number of employees in your organization.
6. Choose the virtual services you are interested in (e.g., Virtual Assistant, Data Entry, etc.).
7. Select the desired service frequency (e.g., one-time, weekly, etc.).
8. Estimate the approximate number of hours you need per week.
9. Indicate your expected monthly budget for virtual services.
10. Select your preferred time zone.
11. If necessary, mention any specific qualifications or requirements for your virtual assistant.
12. Finally, specify your previous experience with virtual services (if any).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`client_full_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your complete legal name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, `email`, **required**): Provide your professional email address for service communications and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, `text`, **required**): Enter your contact phone number for service coordination and communication.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, **optional**): Enter the name of your company or organization (if applicable).
* **Type of Business** (`business_type`, `select_one`, **optional**): Select the industry or sector of your business (e.g., Retail, Service, Technology, etc.).
* **Business Size** (`business_size`, `select_one`, **optional**): Indicate the approximate number of employees in your organization (e.g., 1-10, 11-50, etc.).
* **Services Interested In** (`interested_services`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Choose the virtual services you are interested in (e.g., Virtual Assistant, Data Entry, etc.).
* **Desired Service Frequency** (`service_frequency`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the frequency of services you require (e.g., one-time, weekly, etc.).
* **Estimated Hours Per Week** (`estimated_hours_per_week`, `number`, **optional**): Enter the approximate number of hours needed per week for the selected services.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, `select_one`, **optional**): Indicate your estimated monthly budget for the selected services (e.g., Under $500, etc.).
* **Desired Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, `date`, **optional**): Enter the date when you would like to begin services.
* **Time Zone Preference** (`timezone_preference`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose your time zone (e.g., EST, CST, etc.).
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, `text`, **optional**): Mention any specific qualifications or requirements for your virtual assistant.
* **Previous Experience with Virtual Services** (`previous_experience`, `select_one`, **optional**): Specify your previous experience with virtual services (e.g., No Experience, Limited, etc.).
