# Is The Milk You Are Having Safe!!! - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help you determine the safety and quality of the milk you are drinking. It is a simple tool to aid in the detection of potential adulterations in milk, such as water, starch, and detergent adulterations. The form will guide you through a series of questions to help you make an informed decision about whether the milk is safe to consume.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each question carefully and choose the correct answer for each field.
2. Answer all questions, but you can skip any fields that are not relevant to your situation.
3. If you are unsure about any question, try to provide the best possible answer based on your observations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Detect water adulteration** (`water_deterioration_detection`, select_one, required: false):
	+ This question helps you determine if the milk may have been adulterated with water. Choose "Water adulteration" if you suspect that the milk has an unusual amount of water in it.
* **Check the milk** (`milk_quality_check`, text, required: false):
	+ Look for any visible signs of unusual color, smell, or texture in the milk. If you notice anything unusual, write a brief description in this field.
* **Detect starch adulteration** (`starch_deterioration`, select_one, required: false):
	+ This question helps you determine if the milk may have been adulterated with starch. Choose "Starch adulteration" if you suspect that the milk has an unusual amount of starch in it.
* **Detect detergent adulteration** (`detergent_deterioration`, select_one, required: false):
	+ This question helps you determine if the milk may have been adulterated with detergent. Choose "Detergent adulteration" if you suspect that the milk has an unusual amount of detergent in it.

## Tips

* Always inspect the milk carefully before answering these questions.
* If you are unsure about any question, try to provide the best possible answer based on your observations.
* This form is not a substitute for professional testing or laboratory analysis. If you are concerned about the safety or quality of your milk, consult a dairy expert or a medical professional.
