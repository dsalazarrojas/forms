# Pre Complaint Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Pre Complaint Intake Form is designed to collect information about incidents or complaints from customers. This form is used to gather details about the incident, customer contact information, and other relevant details that will help the customer service team to efficiently handle the complaint.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in a clear and concise manner.
2. Provide as much detail as possible about the incident.
3. Select the correct customer service staff member that handled your issue.
4. Provide your contact details so that we can get in touch with you.
5. Select the correct customer service agency that handled your issue.
6. Select the correct customer service team that handled your issue.
7. Route the form to the correct team based on your concerns.
8. Select the correct incident nature.
9. Select the correct incident category.
10. Provide a detailed description of the incident.
11. Provide your email address so that we can get in touch with you.
12. Provide your phone number so that we can get in touch with you.
13. Provide any additional notes that you would like to share.
14. Provide any additional notes that the customer service staff would like to share.
15. Select the status of the incident.
16. Select the type of incident.
17. Select the category of incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Details (Page 1)**: This field is used to gather details about the incident. Please provide as much detail as possible about the incident.
* **Customer Service Staff (Page 1)**: Please select the customer service staff member that handled your issue.
* **Contact Details (Page 1)**: Please provide your contact details so that we can get in touch with you.
* **Customer Service Agency (Page 1)**: Please select the customer service agency that handled your issue.
* **Customer Service Team (Page 1)**: Please select the customer service team that handled your issue.
* **Route Concerns (Page 1)**: Please route this form to the correct team based on your concerns.
* **Incident Date (Page 2)**: Please provide the date of the incident.
* **Incident Time (Page 2)**: Please provide the time of the incident.
* **Customer Details (Page 2)**: Please provide your details as a customer.
* **Customer Service Agency (Page 2)**: Please select the customer service agency that handled your issue.
* **Customer Service Team (Page 2)**: Please select the customer service team that handled your issue.
* **Incident Nature (Page 3)**: Please select the nature of the incident.
* **Incident Category (Page 3)**: Please select the category of the incident.
* **Incident Description (Page 3)**: Please provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **Customer Email (Page 4)**: Please provide your email address so that we can get in touch with you.
* **Customer Phone (Page 4)**: Please provide your phone number so that we can get in touch with you.
* **Customer Note (Page 4)**: Please provide any additional notes that you would like to share.
* **Staff Note (Page 4)**: Please provide any additional notes that the customer service staff would like to share.
* **Incident Status (Page 5)**: Please select the status of the incident.
* **Incident Type (Page 5)**: Please select the type of incident.
* **Category (Page 5)**: Please select the category of incident.
