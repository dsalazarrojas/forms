# sales_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sales referral form is used to record the details of a customer referral, including the customer's contact information, referral type, and sales person information. This form is designed to be completed by the sales team to submit a referral for processing.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the customer's name, email, and phone number in the respective fields.
2.  Select the type of referral ("Yes" or "No") in the "Referral Type" field.
3.  Enter the sales person's name, email, and phone number in the respective fields.
4.  Add any additional message or comments about the referral.
5.  Confirm that the referral is submitted correctly by selecting "Yes" in the "Confirm Referral" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sales Referral Form Page 1** (`text`, required/optional): This is the first page of the form where you can enter the customer information.
* **Referral Type** (`select_one`, required/optional): Select "Yes" if the customer has referred someone, "No" otherwise.
* **Customer Name** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Email** (`email`, required/optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Email Confirmed** (`note`, required/optional): This field is not used for data entry.
* **Referring Salesperson** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the sales person's name.
* **Customer Message** (`text`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or message about the referral.
* **Confirm Referral** (`select_one`, required/optional): Confirm that the referral is submitted correctly by selecting "Yes".
* **Referral Code** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the referral code (if available).
* **Sales Person Name** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the sales person's name.
* **Sales Person Email** (`email`, required/optional): Enter the sales person's email address.
* **Sales Person Phone** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the sales person's phone number.
* **Sales Person Message** (`text`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or message about the sales person.
* **Sales Manager Name** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the sales manager's name.
* **Sales Manager Email** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the sales manager's email address.
* **Sales Manager Phone** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the sales manager's phone number.
* **Manager Message** (`text`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or message about the sales manager.
* **Sales Manager Note** (`note`, required/optional): This field is not used for data entry.
* **Sales Referral** (`text`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or message about the referral.
* **Customer Referral Code** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the customer referral code (if available).
* **Form Submitter** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your name and title.
* **Form Submitter Email** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Form Submitter Phone** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date Submitted** (`date`, required/optional): This field is automatically populated with the current date and time.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure the form is submitted correctly.
* Use the "Referral Type" field to indicate whether the customer has referred someone.
* If you are not sure about any field, leave it blank and move on to the next field.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
