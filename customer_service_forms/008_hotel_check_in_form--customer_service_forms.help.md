# Hotel Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information from hotel guests during the check-in process, allowing hotel staff to efficiently process their arrival and provide a smooth and personalized experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the date and time of your check-in.
2. Provide your name and contact email address.
3. Enter any comments or special requests you may have.
4. Enter the date and time of your check-out.
5. Select your preferred room type (Hotel Room, Suite, or Apartment).
6. If you have a client ID, please enter it. (Note: This is optional.)
7. Enter the room number where you will be staying.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Check In Date** (`checkin_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to check in to the hotel.
- **Check In Time** (`checkin_time`, time, required): Enter the time you plan to check in.
- **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
- **Guest Email** (`guest_email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
- **Check In Comments** (`checkin_comment`, note, required): Enter any comments or special requests you may have.
- **Check Out Date** (`checkout_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to check out of the hotel.
- **Check Out Time** (`checkout_time`, time, required): Enter the time you plan to check out.
- **Client ID** (`client_id`, text, optional): If you have a client ID, please enter it for our records.
- **Room Number** (`room_number`, text, required): Enter the room number you will be staying in.
- **Room Type** (`room_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of room you prefer (Hotel Room, Suite, or Apartment).
- **Special Requests** (`special_request`, text, optional): If you have any special requests, please enter them here.
