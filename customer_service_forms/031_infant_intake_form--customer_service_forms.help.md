# Infant Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an infant's birth and family details. It is meant to be completed by a primary caregiver, typically the mother or father, and is used to gather essential data for medical records and care planning.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form for each page, using the following steps:

   a. Enter the infant's first name
   b. Enter the infant's last name
   c. Enter the date of birth
   d. Enter the infant's weight and height at birth
   e. Enter the infant's age (if the infant is not a newborn)
   f. Enter the contact information of the primary caregiver
   g. Choose the primary caregiver
   h. Specify the language(s) the primary caregiver can understand or speak
   i. Indicate whether the infant is pregnant or not
   j. Enter the number of days or weeks pregnant (if applicable)
   k. Enter the expected due date
   l. Specify the pregnancy status (singleton, twins, or other)
   m. Specify the first language(s) learned by the infant
   n. Enter the last name used during pregnancy
   o. Enter phone numbers and email addresses of the primary caregiver
   p. Enter any additional notes or comments
   q. Specify the preferred language(s) to be used for communication

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (first_name, text, required): Enter the infant's first name.
* **Last Name** (last_name, text, required): Enter the infant's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth, date, required): Enter the infant's date of birth.
* **Weight** (weight, number, required): Enter the infant's weight at birth.
* **Height** (height, number, required): Enter the infant's height at birth.
* **Age** (age, number, required): Enter the infant's age (if not a newborn).
* **Contact Information** (contact_information, text, required): Enter the contact information of the primary caregiver.
* **Primary Caregiver** (primary_caregiver, select_one, required): Choose the primary caregiver (Infant Mom, Infant Dad, or Other).
* **Language** (language, select_one, required): Specify the language(s) the primary caregiver can understand or speak.
* **Pregnant?** (is_pregnant, select_one, required): Indicate whether the infant is pregnant or not.
* **Pregnant Days/Weeks** (pregnant_days, number, required): Enter the number of days or weeks pregnant.
* **Due Date** (due_date, date, required): Enter the expected due date.
* **Pregnancy Status** (pregnancy_status, select_one, required): Specify the pregnancy status (singleton, twins, or other).
* **First Language Learned** (first_language_learned, select_multiple, required): Specify the first language(s) learned by the infant.
* **Last Name of Pregnancy** (last_name_of_pregnancy, text, required): Enter the last name used during pregnancy.
* **Phone Number** (phone_number, text, optional): Enter any phone number for the primary caregiver.
* **Address** (address, text, optional): Enter the address of the primary caregiver.
* **Additional Notes** (notes, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Language Preference** (language_preference, select_multiple, required): Specify the preferred language(s) for communication.
* **Additional Notes** (additional_notes, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Additional Phone Numbers** (additional_phone_numbers, text, optional): Enter any additional phone numbers.
* **Additional Emails** (additional_emails, email, optional): Enter any additional email addresses.
* **Additional URLs** (additional_urls, text, optional): Enter any additional URLs or web addresses.

Note: Please provide any relevant documentation or medical information in the **Notes** field.
