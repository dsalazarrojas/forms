# Mattress Warranty Claim Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Mattress Warranty Claim Form is designed to help customers report issues with their mattress and initiate the warranty claim process. Please fill out this form to provide the necessary information to assess and address your issue.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your User Name and Email to help us identify you and contact you regarding your claim.
2. Enter your Order Number, which can be found on your receipt or order confirmation email.
3. Select the date you purchased your mattress to help us track the purchase date.
4. Describe the issue you are experiencing with your mattress in the Issue Description field. Please provide as much detail as possible to help us understand the problem.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): This field is for you to enter your name, which will help us identify you and contact you regarding your claim.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your claim.
* **Order Number** (`order_number`, text, optional): Please enter the order number associated with your purchase. This can be found on your receipt or order confirmation email.
* **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, date, optional): Select the date you purchased your mattress to help us track the purchase date.
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, text, optional): Describe the problem you are experiencing with your mattress. Be as detailed as possible so we can understand the issue.

## Tips

* Please provide as much information as possible to help us assess and address your issue promptly.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can contact us for clarification.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure we can process your claim efficiently.
