# Electric Toothbrush Warranty Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Electric Toothbrush Warranty Claim Form is used to process customer warranty claims for electric toothbrushes. This form is typically used when a customer has experienced an issue with their product and needs to submit a claim to receive warranty service or repair.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal contact information (First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone).
2. Describe in detail the problem you are experiencing with your electric toothbrush (Problem Description).
3. Attach any relevant documents or pictures of the issue.
4. Select the category that best describes the issue (Electric Toothbrush, Other, Unknown).
5. Select the status of your submission (New, Approved, Rejected).
6. Add any additional notes or comments.
7. Attach any additional documents or pictures as needed.
