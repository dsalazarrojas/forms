**shipping_label_issue_contact_form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The shipping label issue contact form is designed to help customers report any issues or concerns they may have regarding their shipping labels. This form is used to gather information about the problem, the customer's contact details, and the follow-up status of the issue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information: Please fill in your contact person, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide shipping order information: Enter the shipping order ID to help us identify the issue.
3. Describe the problem: Briefly describe the issue you are experiencing with your shipping label.
4. Attach any relevant documents: If you have any supporting documents or images related to the issue, please attach them.
5. Choose your follow-up status: Select the status of the follow-up on the issue.
6. Select the issue priority: Indicate the urgency of the issue, whether it's Low, Medium, or High.
7. Add any additional information: If you have any other relevant information about the issue, please provide it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Person**: Enter your name and title/position.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Shipping Order ID**: Enter the ID of the shipping order related to the issue.
* **Issue Description**: Describe the problem you are experiencing with your shipping label.
* **Attachments**: If you have any relevant documents or images, attach them to help us understand the issue.
* **Attachments Files**: List the file names of the attached documents or images.
* **Issue Priority**: Select the urgency of the issue, whether it's Low, Medium, or High.
* **Follow Up Status**: Indicate the status of the follow-up on the issue, whether it's Not Followed Up, Followed Up, Followed Up - Scheduled, or Followed Up - Completed.
* **Follow Up Date**: Enter the date when the follow-up was made.
* **Follow Up Time**: Enter the time when the follow-up was made.
* **Customer Service Team**: Enter the name of the customer service team member responsible for the issue.
* **Customer Service Agent**: Enter the name of the customer service agent handling the issue.

Note: The fields marked as "text" can be filled with any relevant information, while "email" and "date" fields require the corresponding type of input. The "select_one" fields have predefined options.
