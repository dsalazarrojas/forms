# Out Of Stock Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Out Of Stock Order Form is used to report and track out-of-stock orders from customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name in the "customer name" field.
2. Enter the product id for the out of stock product in the "product id" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "customer email" field (if you have one).
4. Enter your phone number in the "customer phone" field (if you have one).
5. Select the reason for the order being out of stock from the dropdown menu in the "reason for ordering" field.
6. Select an alternative product id from the dropdown menu in the "alternative product id" field (if available).
7. Enter any additional message you have for the customer service team in the "message" field.
8. Select the person the customer would like to be assigned to from the dropdown menu in the "assigned to" field.
9. Finally, enter any additional customer message or notes in the "customer message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer name`, `text`, required: false): This is where you can enter your name so that our customer service team can follow up with you.
* **Product ID** (`product id`, `number`, required: false): Enter the product id of the product that is out of stock.
* **Customer Email** (`customer email`, `email`, required: true): This is an optional field where you can enter your email address, so that our team can get in touch with you.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer phone`, `text`, required: true): Enter your phone number in this field, so our team can contact you to discuss your order.
* **Reason for Ordering** (`reason for ordering`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the reason for your order from the dropdown menu. This will help us to provide you with a clear understanding of why your order is out of stock.
* **Alternative Product ID** (`alternative product id`, `select_one`, required: false): If you would like to consider an alternative product, select it from the dropdown menu.
* **Message** (`message`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional message or notes for our customer service team.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned to`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the person you would like to be assigned to, so we can follow up with you promptly.
* **Customer Message** (`customer message`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional information about your order or preferences.
