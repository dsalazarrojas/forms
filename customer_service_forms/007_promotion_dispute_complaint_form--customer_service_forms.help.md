# promotion_dispute_complaint_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to report any issues or disputes they have with promotions they have received.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Review the form fields carefully and ensure you have filled out all required fields.
*   Fill out the form with as much detail as possible to help the customer service team better understand your issue.
*   If you have any attachments or supporting documents, please attach them to the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Customer Dispute** (customer_dispute, text, required): Please describe the issue or problem you have with the promotion.
*   **Promotion Details** (promotion_details, text, required): Provide as much detail as possible about the promotion you received, including any relevant dates and amounts.
*   **Complaint** (complaint, text, required): Explain the issue or problem you are experiencing with the promotion.
*   **Contact Info** (contact_info, text, required): Please provide your contact information, including name and email address, so we can get back to you.
*   **Business Info** (business_info, text, required): If you are contacting us on behalf of a business, please include their name and address.
*   **Review Details** (review_details, text, required): Explain what led you to dispute or complain about the promotion, including any relevant dates and events.
*   **Resolution Status** (resolution_status, select one, optional): If applicable, please select the status of your issue: Active, Inactive, or select another status that is relevant.
*   **Dispute Status** (dispute_status, select one, optional): If applicable, please select the status of your dispute: Open, Resolved, or Closed.
*   **Resolution Outcome** (resolution_outcome, select multiple, optional): If applicable, please select the outcome of your issue: Approved, Denied, or In Progress.
*   **Comments** (comments, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information that may be helpful for us to resolve your issue.

## Tips
*   Please be as detailed as possible when filling out the form.
*   If you have any attachments or supporting documents, please attach them to the form.
*   A prompt response will be sent to you once we receive your form submission.
*   If you have any follow-up questions or need further assistance, please don't hesitate to contact us.
