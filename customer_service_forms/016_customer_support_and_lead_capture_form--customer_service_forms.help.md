# Customer Support And Lead Capture Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers who are seeking support or who may be interested in capturing leads. It helps the customer support team to understand the customer's needs and provide the necessary support or lead follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Customer Support and Lead Capture**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you are seeking support or interested in capturing leads.
2. **Customer Service Request**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have any existing support requests.
3. **Capture Lead**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you are interested in capturing a lead.
4. **Customer Inquiry**: If you have a question or concern, please enter it in the text field.
5. **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
6. **Customer Email**: Enter your email address.
7. **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number.
8. **Customer Message**: If you have a message or comment, please enter it in the text field.
9. **Customer Lead Capture**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you are interested in capturing a lead.
10. **Lead Status**: Select "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the status of your lead.
11. **Lead Source**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate the source of your lead.
12. **Lead Priority**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate the priority of your lead.
13. **Customer Support Request**: If you have a request for support, please enter it in the text field.
14. **Customer Follow-up Date**: If you would like to schedule a follow-up, please select a date.
15. **Customer Support Request Date**: If you have a request for support, please select a date.
16. **Customer Support Request Time**: If you have a request for support, please select a time.
17. **Customer Support Request Note**: If you have any additional comments or notes, please enter them in the text field.
18. **Customer Support Request Email**: If you have an email address associated with your request for support, please enter it.
19. **Customer Support Request Phone**: If you have a phone number associated with your request for support, please enter it.
20. **Customer Support Request Message**: If you have a message or comment, please enter it in the text field.
21. **Customer Support Request Attachments**: If you have any attachments associated with your request for support, please select "Yes" or "No".
