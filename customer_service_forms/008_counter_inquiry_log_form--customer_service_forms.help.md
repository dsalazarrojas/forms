# counter_inquiry_log_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "counter_inquiry_log_form" form is designed to log and track customer service inquiries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the page "customer_service_inquiry" and enter the customer name and details of the inquiry.
2. Enter the "inquiry_date" of the customer service interaction.
3. Enter the "counter_number" if applicable.
4. Select the "customer_service_agent" who handled the inquiry.
5. Enter the "customer_service_agent_id" and "customer_service_agent_name" if applicable.
6. Add any additional "notes" about the inquiry.
7. Select the "submitted_by" field to indicate who submitted the inquiry.
8. Enter the "submitted_by_user", "submitted_by_department", and "submitted_by_location" if applicable.
9. Enter the "submitted_by_date" and "submitted_by_time" if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **customer_service_inquiry** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the customer name and details of the inquiry.
* **customer_name** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the customer name.
* **inquiry_date** (`3`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the customer service interaction.
* **counter_number** (`4`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the counter number if applicable.
* **customer_service_agent** (`5`, `text`, required/optional): Select the customer service agent who handled the inquiry.
* **customer_service_agent_id** (`6`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the customer service agent id if applicable.
* **customer_service_agent_name** (`7`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the customer service agent name if applicable.
* **notes** (`8`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes about the inquiry.
* **submitted_by** (`9`, `time`, required/optional): Select the field to indicate who submitted the inquiry.
* **submitted_by_user** (`10`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the user who submitted the inquiry if applicable.
* **submitted_by_department** (`11`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the department that submitted the inquiry if applicable.
* **submitted_by_location** (`12`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the location where the inquiry was submitted from if applicable.
* **submitted_by_date** (`13`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date the inquiry was submitted.
* **submitted_by_time** (`14`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time the inquiry was submitted.
* **submitted_by_user_id** (`15`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the id of the user who submitted the inquiry if applicable.

## Tips
* Ensure each field is filled out correctly to avoid errors.
* Only fill out the necessary fields to avoid unnecessary duplication of work.
* Review and submit the form once all required fields are filled out.
* If unsure about any field, please contact your customer service team for assistance.
