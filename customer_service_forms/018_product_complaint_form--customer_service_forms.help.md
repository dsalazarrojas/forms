# Product Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Complaint Form is designed to collect customer complaints about products sold or offered by our company. If you have encountered an issue with a product, please fill out this form to help us understand the problem and resolve it as quickly as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as follows:

* Follow the on-screen instructions to add or remove pages as needed.
* Fill out each field with the requested information, using the following guidelines:
	+ Product Id: Enter the product's unique identifier number.
	+ Description: Provide a brief description of the issue you are experiencing.
	+ Issue: Describe the specific problem you are encountering with the product.
	+ Report Date: Enter the date the issue was reported.
	+ Report Time: Enter the time the issue was reported.
	+ Email: Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding the issue.
	+ Phone: Enter your phone number so we can contact you by phone.
	+ Product Image: If you have an image of the product that may help us diagnose the issue, you can upload it here.
	+ Additional Comments: If you have any additional information or comments about the issue, enter them here.
* Click the "Submit" button to send the form to our support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Id** (`product_id`, number, required): Enter the product's unique identifier number.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the issue you are experiencing.
* **Issue** (`issue`, text, optional): Describe the specific problem you are encountering with the product.
* **Report Date** (`report_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the issue was reported.
* **Report Time** (`report_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the issue was reported.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding the issue.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you by phone.
* **Product Image** (`product_image`, text, optional): If you have an image of the product that may help us diagnose the issue, you can upload it here.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional information or comments about the issue, enter them here.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to send the form to our support team.
* **Cancel** (`cancel`, text, optional): Click this button to discard your form and start over. 

## Tips
* Please provide as much information as possible to help our support team resolve the issue quickly and efficiently.
* If you have attachments or images related to the issue, you can attach them to the form or upload them using the 'Product Image' field.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact us directly and we will be happy to assist you.
