# Freight Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Freight Complaint Form is designed for customers to report issues with their freight shipments. This form helps our team identify and address potential problems with freight shipments in a timely and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. For fields with options, select only one or more relevant options as needed.
4. Use the "Submit" button to send the completed form to our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shipping Issue** (`shipping_issue`, text, required): Describe the issue you are experiencing with your freight shipment.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the issue you are experiencing.
* **Date Delivered** (`date_delivered`, date, optional): If you are unsure of the date your shipment was delivered, you can leave this field blank.
* **Time Delivered** (`time_delivered`, time, optional): If you are unsure of the time your shipment was delivered, you can leave this field blank.
* **Date Received** (`date_received`, date, optional): If you are unsure of the date your shipment was received, you can leave this field blank.
* **Time Received** (`time_received`, time, optional): If you are unsure of the time your shipment was received, you can leave this field blank.
* **Product Condition** (`product_condition`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if the product condition is not as expected.
* **Contact First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Contact Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Company** (`contact_company`, text, optional): Enter your company name.
* **Customer Feedback** (`customer_feedback`, text, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience.
* **Shipping Service** (`shipping_service`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if you were using our shipping service.
* **Issue Type** (`issue_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if this issue is related to the type of shipping service used.
* **Issue Details** (`issue_details`, note, optional): Provide any additional details about the issue you are experiencing.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if a customer signature is required for this issue.
* **Company Signature** (`company_signature`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if a company signature is required for this issue.

## Tips

* Please be as detailed as possible when describing the issue you are experiencing.
* If you are unsure of any information, it's okay to leave it blank.
* Our team will review and respond to your complaint in a timely manner.
