# detailed_real_estate_customer_service_forms - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information about customer service interactions in the real estate sector. It is intended to gather essential details from customers, realtors, and company representatives for follow-up purposes, contact preferences, and note-taking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by filling out the initial contact section with your details (Initial Contact).
2. Provide your client details (Client Details) if required.
3. Input your realtor details (Realtor Details) if necessary.
4. Specify your contact preferences (Contact Preferences) by selecting one or more of the options (Phone, Email, or Text).
5. Schedule a follow-up date and time (Follow-up) for future interactions.
6. Add any additional notes (Note 1-18) as needed for future reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Initial Contact** (Initial Contact, text, required): Enter your name and contact information in this section.
* **Client Details** (Client Details, number, optional): If you are interacting with a client, provide their details here (e.g., name, phone number, email).
* **Realtor Details** (Realtor Details, text, optional): If you are a realtor, provide your details here (e.g., name, contact information).
* **Company Details** (Company Details, text, optional): If you are representing a company, provide your company's details here (e.g., name, address, contact information).
* **Contact Preferences** (Contact Preferences, select one or more, required): Specify how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or Text).
* **Follow-up** (Follow-up, date, required): Schedule a follow-up date for future interactions.
* **Follow-up Time** (Follow-up Time, time, optional): Specify a follow-up time if desired.
* **Note 1-18** (Note 1-18, note, optional): Add any additional notes for future reference.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields (Initial Contact, Contact Preferences, and Follow-up).
* If you are interacting with a client, provide their details in the Client Details section.
* If you are a realtor, provide your details in the Realtor Details section.
* If you are representing a company, provide your company's details in the Company Details section.
* Use the Contact Preferences section to specify how you would like to be contacted.
* Use the Follow-up section to schedule future interactions.
* Use the Note sections for any additional comments or notes.
