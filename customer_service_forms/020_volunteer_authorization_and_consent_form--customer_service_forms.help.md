# Volunteer Authorization And Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather volunteer authorization and consent from individuals who wish to participate in activities with our organization. It's essential to have this information to ensure we can properly coordinate volunteer efforts and provide necessary support.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review the form carefully and ensure you are aware of the information required.
2.  Select the correct category from the "Customer Service Forms" dropdown menu.
3.  Enter your name, and any other relevant details as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Service Forms** (`customer_service_forms`): Select the type of customer service form you wish to use (Volunteer, Non-Profit, or Both).
* **Volunteer Name** (`volunteer_name`): Enter the name of the volunteer.
* **Organization Awareness** (`organization_awareness`): Briefly describe your awareness of our organization's mission, goals, and values.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`): Provide any contact information you feel is relevant for our organization.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`): Enter the name of the person to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`): Enter the email of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`): Enter the address of the emergency contact.
* **Do you wish to volunteer for this organization?** (`consent_1`): Select 'Yes' if you wish to volunteer, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Terms & Conditions** (`terms_conditions`): Read and agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Date** (`date_signed`): Enter the date you signed this form.
* **Time** (`time_signed`): Enter the time you signed this form.
* **Signed by** (`signed_by`): Enter your name as the person signing this form.
* **Volunteer Status** (`volunteer_status`): Select your current volunteer status (Active or Inactive).
