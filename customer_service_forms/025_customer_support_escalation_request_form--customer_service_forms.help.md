# customer_support_escalation_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers escalate their support requests to the support team. Please use this form to report any issues or problems you are experiencing with our service or product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields with the required information and submit it to the support team. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please refer to the field-by-field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Support Escalation Request** (`customer_support_escalation_request`): This is the title of the form that you are filling out. It is not required, but it helps us keep track of the issue.
* **Customer Issue** (`customer_issue`): Describe the problem or issue you are experiencing with our service or product. This could be a technical difficulty, a feature request, or any other type of issue.
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`): Provide a detailed description of the issue, including any error messages, screenshots, or other relevant information.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`): Please provide your name as the customer requesting support.
* **Issue Priority** (`issue_priority`): Choose the priority level of your issue, which helps us determine the urgency of the support request. Select from the following options:
	+ Critical: High priority issue that requires immediate attention.
	+ High: Issue that requires attention but can be delayed for a short time.
	+ Medium: Issue that requires attention, but can be delayed for some time.
	+ Low: Issue that is minor and can be addressed at a later time.
* **Customer Contact E-mail** (`customer_contact_email`): Please provide your email address where we can contact you for further information or updates.
* **Customer Contact Phone** (`customer_contact_phone`): If you prefer to be contacted over the phone, please provide your phone number.
* **Customer Support E-mail** (`customer_support_email`): This is the email address of the support team that will handle your issue. You can leave it blank if you are unsure.
