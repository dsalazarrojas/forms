# Pre Plan Funeral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Plan Funeral Form is designed for customers who want to pre-plan their funeral arrangements to ensure their wishes are met and their family members are prepared for the funeral. This form helps customers collect vital information about their preferences, contact information, and other important details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you have included all the necessary information.
2. Enter your preferences for the burial site.
3. Provide the date and time of the funeral ceremony.
4. Specify the location of the burial site.
5. Enter the contact information for the person who should be contacted in case of an emergency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Family Preferences** (`family_preferences`, note, optional): This field is where you can note any specific preferences or requests you have for your funeral ceremony, such as any favorite songs, flowers, or other personal touches.
* **Burial Site** (`burial_site`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to indicate that you want to be buried, or "No" to indicate you prefer cremation or other alternative arrangements.
* **Ceremony Type** (`ceremony_type`, text, optional): If you have a specific type of ceremony in mind (e.g., traditional, graveside, or eco-friendly), enter it here.
* **Date and Time** (`date_time`, date, required): Enter the date and time you would like the funeral ceremony to take place.
* **Time of Day** (`time_of_day`, time, required): Enter the specific time of day for the funeral ceremony.
* **Burial Location** (`burial_location`, text, required): Specify the location where you would like to be buried or cremated.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of the person who should be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person, if applicable.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person, if applicable.
* **Contact Notes** (`contact_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or instructions for the contact person.
