# Hydromassage Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a liability waiver for customers who will be undergoing a hydromassage treatment. It helps to ensure that customers are aware of and agree to the potential risks associated with the treatment, and provides necessary contact and medical information for emergency purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out all required fields (indicated by a red asterisk *): 
* Enter your first and last name in the respective fields.
* Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* Enter your email address.
* Enter your contact number.
* Enter any relevant medical history, such as previous injuries or medical conditions.
* Enter any previous medical procedures you have undergone.
* Enter the name and relationship of the emergency contact person.
* Enter the name and relationship of your medical insurance provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, *): 
	+ Please enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, *): 
	+ Please enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, *): 
	+ Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email** (`email`, email, *): 
	+ Please enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, *): 
	+ Please enter a contact number where you can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, *): 
	+ Please list any medical history or conditions that may impact your treatment.
* **Medical Procedures** (`medical_procedures`, text, *): 
	+ Please list any previous medical procedures you have undergone.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, *): 
	+ Please enter the name of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, *): 
	+ Please enter your relationship with the emergency contact person (e.g. spouse, parent, etc.).
* **Medical Insurance Provider** (`medical_insurance_provider`, text, *): 
	+ Please enter the name of your medical insurance provider.
* **Medical Insurance Provider Relationship** (`medical_insurance_provider_relationship`, text, *): 
	+ Please enter your relationship with the medical insurance provider (e.g. employee, spouse, etc.).
