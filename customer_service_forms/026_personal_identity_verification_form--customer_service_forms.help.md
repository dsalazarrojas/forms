# Personal Identity Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Identity Verification Form is used to confirm your personal details for various purposes, such as customer onboarding, background checks, or government services.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in your full name as it appears on your government-issued identification documents.
- Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
- Enter your phone number.
- Enter your full address.
- Provide your date of birth in the mm/dd/yyyy format.
- Enter your Social Security Number.
- Select the type of government-issued identification you possess.
- Enter any additional notes about your government-issued identification, if needed.
- Enter any information on the front of your government-issued identification, if needed.
- Enter any information on the back of your government-issued identification, if needed.
- Enter any information on the other side of your government-issued identification, if needed.
- If you have a driver's license, enter the information on the front of the license.
- If you have a driver's license, enter the information on the back of the license.
- If you have a driver's license, enter any additional information on the other side of the license, if needed.
- If you have an ID Card, enter the information on the front of the ID Card.
- If you have an ID Card, enter the information on the back of the ID Card.
- If you have an ID Card, enter any additional information on the other side of the ID Card, if needed.
- If you have a Passport, enter the information on the front of the Passport.
- If you have a Passport, enter the information on the back of the Passport.
- If you have a Passport, enter any additional information on the other side of the Passport, if needed.
- Enter any notes about your Social Security Number, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (Text, Optional): Please enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued identification documents.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (Text, Optional): Enter your full address.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Required): Enter your date of birth in the mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Social Security Number** (Text, Required): Enter your Social Security Number.
* **Government Identification Number** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the type of government-issued identification you possess (ID Card, Driver License, Passport, State ID).
* **Government ID Notes** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional notes about your government-issued identification, if needed.
* **Government ID Front** (Text, Optional): Enter any information on the front of your government-issued identification, if needed.
* **Government ID Back** (Text, Optional): Enter any information on the back of your government-issued identification, if needed.
* **Government ID Other Side** (Text, Optional): Enter any information on the other side of your government-issued identification, if needed.
* **Driver License Front** (Text, Optional): If you have a driver's license, enter the information on the front of the license.
* **Driver License Back** (Text, Optional): If you have a driver's license, enter the information on the back of the license.
* **Driver License Other Side** (Text, Optional): If you have a driver's license, enter any additional information on the other side of the license, if needed.
* **ID Card Front** (Text, Optional): If you have an ID Card, enter the information on the front of the ID Card.
* **ID Card Back** (Text, Optional): If you have an ID Card, enter the information on the back of the ID Card.
* **ID Card Other Side** (Text, Optional): If you have an ID Card, enter any additional information on the other side of the ID Card, if needed.
* **Passport Front** (Text, Optional): If you have a Passport, enter the information on the front of the Passport.
* **Passport Back** (Text, Optional): If you have a Passport, enter the information on the back of the Passport.
* **Passport Other Side** (Text, Optional): If you have a Passport, enter any additional information on the other side of the Passport, if needed.
* **Social Security Number Notes** (Note, Optional): Enter any notes about your Social Security Number, if needed.
