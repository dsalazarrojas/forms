# Contact Support Team - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide walks you through the "Contact Support Team" form, designed to help our support team understand your issue quickly and efficiently. Please fill out the form below to provide essential information about your question or problem.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your **[Your Name]** in the "Your Name" field. This will help us identify you and personalize our response.
2.  Provide your **Contact Email Address** in the "Email Address" field. This will help us contact you regarding your issue.
3.  If you have a **Phone Number**, enter it in the "Phone Number" field. This will help us reach out to you via phone.
4.  If your issue is related to a specific **Account Number**, please enter it in the "Account Number" field. This is optional but helpful for our team's reference.
5.  Select the **Issue Category** that best describes your problem from the provided options. This will help us direct your issue to the correct team member.
6.  Choose the **Product or Service** related to your issue from the options provided. This will ensure we address the right product.
7.  Briefly describe your **Issue Summary** in the provided field. This should give us an idea of what's wrong.
8.  For more detailed information, write a **Detailed Description** of your issue in the provided field.
9.  Specify when your issue **Occurs** using one of the frequency options provided. This helps us understand your problem's timing.
10. If applicable, enter your **Browser or Device** you are using to access our services.
11. If you have an **Error Code or Message**, enter it in the provided field. This might be helpful for our team to troubleshoot.
12. Describe the **Steps to Reproduce** the issue in a step-by-step manner. This will help us replicate your problem and resolve it faster.
13. If you have any **Attachments** (e.g., screenshots), you can attach them here. These are optional but may be helpful for further analysis.
14. Set the **Priority Level** of your issue using one of the provided options. This will help us understand how urgent your issue is.
15. Select your **Preferred Contact Method** from the options provided. This will determine how we respond to your issue.
16. Finally, indicate your **Best Time to Contact** when you are available. This helps us plan our response accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your full name for personalized communication.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for further contact.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted via phone, enter your number here.
* **Account Number** (`account_number`, text, optional): If your issue is related to a specific account, enter the account number here.
* **Issue Category** (`issue_category`, select_one, required): Choose the category that best describes your issue from the provided options.
* **Product or Service** (`product`, select_one, required): Select the product or service related to your issue from the options provided.
* **Issue Summary** (`issue_summary`, text, required): Briefly describe your issue here to give us an idea of what's wrong.
* **Detailed Description** (`issue_details`, text, required): Provide a more detailed description of your issue for accurate resolution.
* **Frequency** (`occurrence`, select_one, required): Choose when your issue occurs using one of the provided options.
* **Browser or Device** (`browser_device`, text, optional): If you're using a specific browser or device, enter it here for our reference.
* **Error Code or Message** (`error_code`, text, optional): If you have an error code or message, enter it here for further analysis.
* **Steps to Reproduce** (`steps_to_reproduce`, text, optional): Describe the steps to reproduce your issue to help us resolve it faster.
* **Attachments** (`attachments`, text, optional): Attach any relevant files or screenshots here, if applicable.
* **Priority Level** (`priority`, select_one, required): Set the priority level of your issue using one of the provided options.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact`, select_one, required): Choose how you'd like to be contacted in response to your issue.
* **Best Time to Contact** (`availability`, select_one, optional): Indicate a good time for us to contact you, if possible.
