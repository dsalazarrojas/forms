# Telecom Customer Service Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us better understand your telecom service needs and preferences for a smooth and efficient service onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your name and address in the **Customer Details** section.
2. In the **Service Preferences** section, select your primary service type (Home, Business, or Mobile).
3. In the **Installation Requests** section, provide any specific installation requests or instructions you have.
4. Enter your contact information in the **Contact Information** section, including your phone numbers and email addresses.
5. If you have a preferred installation date and time, enter it in the **Installation Date** and **Installation Time** sections.
6. Add any additional comments or information you think we should know in the **Notes** section.
7. Indicate if you have any assigned technicians or tools in the **Assigned Technician** and **Assigned Tool** sections.
8. Confirm your service type, billing cycle, and contract terms in the respective sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, required): Enter your name and address to help us identify you quickly and efficiently.
* **Service Preferences** (`service_preferences`, select_multiple, required): Select your primary service type (Home, Business, or Mobile).
* **Installation Requests** (`installation_requests`, text, optional): Provide any specific installation requests or instructions you have.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter your phone numbers and email addresses to ensure we can contact you effectively.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, optional): Enter your street address to help us tailor our services to your needs.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your primary phone number.
* **Alternate Phone Number (optional)** (`phone_number2`, text, optional): Enter your alternate phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your primary email address.
* **Alternate Email** (`email2`, email, optional): Enter your alternate email address.
* **Installation Date** (`installation_date`, date, optional): Enter your preferred installation date if you have one.
* **Installation Time** (`installation_time`, time, optional): Enter your preferred installation time if you have one.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or information you think we should know.
* **Assigned Technician** (`assigned_technician`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you have any assigned technicians.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you have any assigned tools.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, optional): Select if you need specific services.
* **Billing Cycle** (`billing_cycle`, select_one, optional): Choose how often you want to be billed for your services.
* **Contract Terms** (`contract_terms`, select_one, optional): Confirm if you need to discuss contract terms or have any questions.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your customer service representative for assistance.
* Please note that some fields have specific formats or requirements for accuracy.
