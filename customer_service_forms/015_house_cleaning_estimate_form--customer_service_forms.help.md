# House Cleaning Estimate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The House Cleaning Estimate Form is a tool used to collect information from customers for the purpose of providing a quote for house cleaning services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of house you are requesting a quote for.
2. Enter the square footage of your house.
3. Estimate the labor cost and total cost of the cleaning services needed.
4. Choose any additional services you would like to include in the quote (e.g. Carpet Cleaning, Upholstery Cleaning, Tile Cleaning, Window Cleaning).
5. Provide any additional comments or information you would like to share with the cleaning service provider.
6. Enter your contact information so the cleaning service provider can get back to you with your quote.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **House** (`house_cleaning_estimate_form_2`): Select the type of house you are requesting a quote for. Please choose from the following options: House, Office, Bedroom, or Bathroom.
* **Square Footage** (`house_cleaning_estimate_form_3`): Enter the square footage of your house.
* **Estimated Labor Cost** (`house_cleaning_estimate_form_4`): Estimate the labor cost of the cleaning services needed. This is an estimate and will be reviewed by the cleaning service provider.
* **Estimated Total Cost** (`house_cleaning_estimate_form_5`): Enter your estimated total cost for the cleaning services.
* **Additional Services** (`house_cleaning_estimate_form_6`): Select any additional services you would like to include in the quote. You can select multiple services by holding down the Ctrl key and clicking on each service. The options are:
	+ Carpet Cleaning
	+ Upholstery Cleaning
	+ Tile Cleaning
	+ Window Cleaning
* **Additional Comments** (`house_cleaning_estimate_form_8`): If you have any additional comments or information you would like to share with the cleaning service provider, please enter them here.
* **Contact Information** (`house_cleaning_estimate_form_9`): Enter your contact information so the cleaning service provider can get back to you with your quote. Please enter your email address in the format of name@domain.com. 

## Tips
* Be as accurate as possible when estimating the labor and total cost of the cleaning services.
* Make sure to select all additional services that apply to get a complete quote.
* Enter any relevant comments or information that may affect the quote.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy before submitting the form.
