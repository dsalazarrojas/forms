# Airline Disability Accommodation Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect details about your experience with the airline's disability accommodation during your travel. It will help us understand your concerns and address them efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please provide your full name as the person submitting this complaint.
2.  Enter your email address to receive updates regarding this complaint.
3.  Optionally, provide your phone number for follow-up communication.
4.  Enter the flight number associated with your travel.
5.  Specify the date you traveled.
6.  Choose the category that best describes your disability type.
7.  Describe the accommodation you requested from the airline.
8.  Indicate whether the requested accommodation was provided.
9.  Provide a detailed description of the issue, including any interactions with airline staff.
10.  Choose one or more desired outcomes for your complaint.
11.  If desired, select that you would like the customer service team to follow up with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Complainant name** (`Complainant name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as the person submitting this complaint.
*   **Contact email** (`Contact email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for updates regarding this complaint.
*   **Contact phone number** (`Contact phone number`, `text`, optional): If you'd like, provide your phone number for follow-up communication.
*   **Flight number** (`Flight number`, `text`, required): Enter the flight number associated with your travel.
*   **Date of travel** (`Date of travel`, `date`, required): Specify the date when the accommodation issue occurred.
*   **Disability type** (`Disability type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the category that best applies to your situation.
*   **Accommodation requested** (`Accommodation requested`, `text`, required): Describe the accommodation you requested from the airline.
*   **Accommodation provided** (`Accommodation provided`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether the accommodation you requested was provided.
*   **Incident description** (`Incident description`, `text`, required): Provide a detailed account of the issue, including any interactions with airline staff.
*   **Outcome sought** (`Outcome sought`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose one or more desired resolutions for your complaint, such as a refund or rebooking.
*   **Follow up required** (`Follow up required`, `select_one`, required): If you'd like, select that you would like the customer service team to follow up with you.
