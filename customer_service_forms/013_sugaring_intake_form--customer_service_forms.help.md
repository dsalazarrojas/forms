# Sugaring Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sugaring Intake Form is designed to collect information from clients to determine their sugar waxing preferences and schedule appointments with our skilled technicians. This form is used to book appointments for sugaring services such as hair removal and hair treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide your contact information**: Enter your full name, email, and phone number in the relevant fields.
2. **Select your hair type**: Choose how your hair is currently growing (Balding, Hirsute, or Other).
3. **Choose hair locations**: Select which areas on your body are in need of sugaring services.
4. **Select your hair color**: Choose the color of your hair.
5. **Determine your current hair length**: Enter your current hair length.
6. **Determine your desired hair length**: Enter the desired length for your hair.
7. **Indicate your skin type**: Choose whether you have sensitive, normal, or allergic skin.
8. **Choose your treatment location**: Decide whether you would like to receive your treatment at home or in our office.
9. **Select your available days**: Choose which days you are available for your appointment.
10. **Schedule your appointment time**: Select the start and end times for your appointment.
11. **Choose your technician**: Select which technician you would like to work with.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (1, text, required): This is the name of the client who is scheduling the appointment.
* **Email** (2, email, required): Enter the client's email address.
* **Phone** (3, text, required): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Description** (4, text, optional): This is an optional field for any additional comments or information the client would like to provide.
* **Hair Type** (5, select_one, required): Choose how your hair is currently growing (Balding, Hirsute, or Other).
* **Hair Location** (6, select_multiple, required): Select which areas on your body are in need of sugaring services (Back, Stomach, Arms, Legs, or Other).
* **Hair Color** (7, select_one, required): Choose the color of your hair.
* **Hair Length** (8, number, required): Enter your current hair length.
* **Desired Length** (9, number, required): Enter the desired length for your hair.
* **Skin Type** (10, select_one, required): Choose whether you have sensitive, normal, or allergic skin.
* **Treatment Location** (11, select_one, required): Decide whether you would like to receive your treatment at home or in our office.
* **Available Days** (12, select_one, required): Choose which days you are available for your appointment.
* **Start Time** (13, time, required): Select the start time for your appointment.
* **End Time** (14, time, required): Select the end time for your appointment.
* **Technician** (15, select_one, required): Select which technician you would like to work with.

## Tips
* Please make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure we can best serve you.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* Our technicians are trained to handle any skin type and hair condition.
