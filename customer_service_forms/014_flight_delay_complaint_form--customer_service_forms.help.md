# Flight Delay Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers who experienced flight delays. The goal is to gather details about the delay and allow customers to provide feedback to the airline.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit Complaint" field to begin the form.
2. Enter the date of your flight delay in the "Date of Delay" field. This should be in the format of MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 02/12/2022).
3. Enter the time of your flight delay in the "Time of Delay" field. This should be in the format of HH:MM (e.g., 14:30).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submit Complaint** (`flight_delay_complaint_form`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your flight delay experience.
* **Date of Delay** (`flight_delay_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your flight delay in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 02/12/2022).
* **Time of Delay** (`flight_delay_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your flight delay in the format HH:MM (e.g., 14:30).

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when describing your delay experience.
- If you experienced any issues with check-in, baggage handling, or in-flight services, please mention them specifically.
- If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Submit Complaint" field.
