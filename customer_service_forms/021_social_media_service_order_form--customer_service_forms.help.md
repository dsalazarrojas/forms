# social_media_service_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to order social media services, such as social media setup and management. Please fill out this form to provide your contact information and order details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the introduction page with your contact information.
2. Select your preferred payment method (Credit Card or Bank Transfer).
3. Choose the social media services you'd like to order (Social Media Setup and Management).
4. Select a date and time for the service (if desired).
5. Describe the services you'd like to order in the "services" field.
6. Provide any additional comments or instructions in the "comments" field.
7. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Page 1: Introduction**
	* This is the first page of the form, where you can enter your contact information.
- **payment_method**
	* Select your preferred payment method (Credit Card or Bank Transfer).
- **service**
	* Choose the social media services you'd like to order (Social Media Setup and Management).
- **appearance**
	* Select a date for the service (if you have a specific date in mind).
- **start_time**
	* Select a start time for the service (if you have a specific start time in mind).
- **end_time**
	* Select an end time for the service (if you have a specific end time in mind).
- **services**
	* Describe the services you'd like to order in the text field.
- **comments**
	* Provide any additional comments or instructions about your order.
- **email**
	* Enter your email address for contact purposes.
- **phone**
	* Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
