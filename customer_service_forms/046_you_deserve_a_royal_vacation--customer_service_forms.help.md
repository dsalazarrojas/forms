# you_deserve_a_royal_vacation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about employee vacation details, including start and end dates, reasons for the vacation, location, employee name, manager's name, department, and return date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Employee Name
	* Manager Name
	* Department
2. Provide optional fields:
	* Vacation Start Date
	* Vacation End Date
	* Reason for Vacations
	* Vacation Location
	* Return Date

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vacation Start Date**: Enter the date when your vacation starts.
* **Vacation End Date**: Enter the date when your vacation ends.
* **Reason for Vacations**: Briefly describe the reason for your vacation (optional).
* **Vacation Location**: Enter the location where you'll be during your vacation.
* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as an employee.
* **Manager Name**: Enter your manager's name.
* **Department**: Enter your department.
* **Return Date**: Enter the date when you're expected to return from your vacation.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all the required fields.
* If you're unsure about a field's format, refer to the field's label or contact your HR representative for help.
* If you're providing a reason for your vacation, be brief but clear.
