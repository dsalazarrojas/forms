# start_with_more_information - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "start_with_more_information" form is designed to collect additional information from customers to better understand their needs and preferences. This form is used to gather details that will help us provide more personalized support and services to them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "contact" field to indicate whether you would like to be contacted by us regarding your query.
2. Fill in your name as it appears on your identification.
3. Enter your phone number if you would like to be reached via phone.
4. Provide your email address if you have one.
5. Enter a valid address where you can be reached.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **contact (select one)**:
	+ Select "Yes" if you would like to be contacted by us via phone, email, or mail. Select "No" otherwise.
* **name**:
	+ Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **phone**:
	+ Enter your phone number if you would like to be reached via phone. This should be a valid 10-digit number in the format (123) 456-7890.
* **email**:
	+ Enter your valid email address to receive updates and communications from us.
* **address**:
	+ Enter a valid address where you can be reached. This should be a physical address, not a PO box.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that we can provide you with the best possible service.
* If you have any issues or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* Please note that the information provided will be kept confidential and used only for the purpose of providing support services.
