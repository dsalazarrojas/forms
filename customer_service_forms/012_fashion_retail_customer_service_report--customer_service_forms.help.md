# Fashion Retail Customer Service Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report and document customer service incidents in a retail setting. It helps to collect information about issues that customers have with products, orders, or in-store experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the incident details, such as date, time, and location.
2. Select the type of incident that occurred.
3. Describe the incident in detail.
4. Provide information about the customer service officer handling the incident.
5. Enter the customer's contact information.
6. Provide any additional comments or feedback from the customer.
7. Select the outcome of the incident.
8. Add any relevant images or videos related to the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`): Briefly describe the incident that occurred.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`): Provide a detailed description of what happened.
* **Customer Service Officer** (`customer_service_officer`): Enter the name of the customer service officer who is handling this incident.
* **Customer Service Officer ID** (`customer_service_officer_id`): Enter the ID number of the customer service officer.
* **Customer ID** (`customer_id`): Enter the ID number of the customer.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`): Enter the name of the customer.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`): Enter the email address of the customer.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`): Enter the phone number of the customer.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`): Enter the address of the customer.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`): Select the type of incident that occurred (e.g., Order Issue, Product Return, Customer Complaint).
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`): Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`): Select the status of the incident (e.g., Resolved, In Progress, Awaiting Response).
* **Incident Outcome** (`incident_outcome`): Select the outcome of the incident (e.g., Successful Resolution, Customer Satisfied, Improvement).
* **Customer Feedback** (`customer_feedback`): Enter any comments or feedback from the customer.
* **Customer Service Officer Comment** (`customer_service_officer_comment`): Enter any comments or feedback from the customer service officer.
* **Store ID** (`store_id`): Enter the ID number of the store.
* **Store Name** (`store_name`): Enter the name of the store.
* **Store Address** (`store_address`): Enter the address of the store.
* **Incident Image** (`incident_image`): Upload any relevant images related to the incident.
* **Incident Video** (`incident_video`): Upload any relevant videos related to the incident.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`): Enter the location where the incident occurred (this field is not to be confused with the previous `incident_location` field).
* **Sales Associate** (`sales_associate`): Select the sales associate involved in the incident (e.g., Sales Associate, Customer Support, Store Manager, Regional Manager).

Note: Some fields might not be relevant to every incident, and it's up to the user to determine what information is necessary for each incident.
