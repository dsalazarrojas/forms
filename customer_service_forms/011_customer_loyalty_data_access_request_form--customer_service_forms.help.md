# Customer Loyalty Data Access Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request access to customer loyalty data for internal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with your request and submit. If you're requesting access for a particular reason, please provide a brief explanation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Loyal** (`customer_loyalty_data_access_request`, text, optional): Enter a descriptive title for your request, if you want to (e.g., "Request for Loyalty Data for Marketing Analysis").
- **Customer ID** (`customer_id`, number, required): Enter the customer's ID number.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the customer's email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number (if available).
- **Customer Data Access Request** (`customer_data_access_request`, text, required): Describe the specific data you are requesting access to.
- **Date Access** (`date_access`, date, required): Enter the date you would like to access the customer data (if available).
- **Time Access** (`time_access`, time, required): Enter the time you would like to access the customer data (if available).
- **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`, text, required): Provide a brief explanation of why you are requesting access to the customer data.
- **Submit** (`customer_data_access_request_form_submit`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' to submit the form, 'No' to cancel the request.

## Tips
- Please be specific when requesting access to customer data.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact our customer service team for assistance.
