# Toy Product Missing Parts Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers about missing parts for a toy product they have purchased from our company. It helps us to identify and resolve issues related to missing parts in a timely and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Make sure you are submitting this form for a product you have purchased from our company.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
3. Select the correct options for each field, such as "Yes" or "No" for missing parts, and choose a reason for the request from the dropdown menu.
4. Provide any additional information you think will help us understand the issue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product ID** (`product_id`, number, required): Enter the ID of the product you are reporting missing parts for. If you are unsure, you can contact our customer support team for assistance.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your order receipt or invoice.
* **Order Number** (`order_number`, number, optional): Enter the number of your order or the order ID if you have it.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the product for which you are reporting missing parts.
* **Part Description** (`part_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the part that is missing.
* **Missing Parts** (`missing_parts`, select multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if any parts are missing from your product.
* **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`, select one, optional): Choose a reason for the missing parts from the dropdown menu.
	+ Missing part is not working as intended
	+ Part is damaged beyond repair
	+ Other
* **Requested Date** (`requested_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are submitting this report.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like us to contact you regarding this issue.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you would like us to email you regarding this issue.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, note, optional): Provide any additional information you think will help us understand the issue and resolve it efficiently.
