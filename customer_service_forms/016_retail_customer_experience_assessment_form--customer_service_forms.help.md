# Retail Customer Experience Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Retail Customer Experience Assessment Form is designed to gather feedback and opinions from customers about their experiences with our retail service. The form is used to identify areas of improvement and ensure that our customers receive the best service possible. This form is not mandatory, and the information collected will be used to improve our retail services and customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand what each question is asking.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Make sure to submit the form once you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer_experience** (`customer_experience`, text, optional)
	+ This is a text field where you can describe your overall experience with our retail service.
* **customer_frustration** (`customer_frustration`, number, optional)
	+ This question asks you to rate your level of frustration with your experience, with higher numbers indicating greater frustration.
* **retail_services** (`retail_services`, select multiple, optional)
	+ This question allows you to choose one or more of the following options:
		- Option 1
		- Option 2
		- Option 3
	+ Please choose the options that best describe your experience with our retail services.
* **customer_satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction`, number, optional)
	+ This question asks you to rate your level of satisfaction with your experience, with higher numbers indicating greater satisfaction.
* **customer_service** (`customer_service`, text, optional)
	+ This is a text field where you can describe your experience with our customer service.
* **retail_competitor** (`retail_competitor`, select one, optional)
	+ This question asks you to choose one of the following options:
		- Option 1
		- Option 2
		- Option 3
	+ Please choose the option that best describes our retail service compared to a competitor.
* **customer_feedback** (`customer_feedback`, note, optional)
	+ This is a free-text field where you can provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional)
	+ If you would like to be contacted about your feedback, please provide your email address.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers.
* If you have any concerns or issues that were not addressed in the form, please provide them in the **customer_feedback** field.
* If you would like to be contacted about your feedback, please make sure to provide your email address in the **email** field.
