# Escape Room Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Escape Room Waiver form is a mandatory document that customers must complete before participating in an escape room experience. It serves as a legal agreement between the customer and the game operator, outlining the rules and guidelines for the game.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Customer Information**: Enter your name and any other contact information required.
2. Fill out the **participant_details** section with your relevant details.
3. Review and sign the **waiver** section, acknowledging that you have read and understood the rules and guidelines.
4. Enter your signature and current date in the **signature** section.
5. Provide the date and time you started and ended the game in the **waiver_date** and **waiver_time** sections.
6. Enter the number of participants in the **participant_count** field.
7. Enter the room number and game length in the **room_number** and **game_length** fields.
8. Enter the start and end time of the game in the **game_start_time** and **game_end_time** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer**: Please enter your name and any other contact information required.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your contact details, such as your name, email, and phone number.
* **Participant Details**: Please enter the details of all participants, including their name, contact information, and any special instructions.
* **Waiver**: This is a mandatory section where you must acknowledge that you have read and understood the rules and guidelines of the game. Please review the waiver carefully before signing.
* **Signature**: Please sign this section to confirm that you have read and understood the rules and guidelines.
* **Waiver Date**: Please enter the current date.
* **Waiver Time**: Please enter the current time.
* **Customer Service Representative**: This is an optional field for you to provide contact information for your customer service representative.
* **Game Master's Name**: Please enter the name of the game master who will be running the game.
* **Escape Room Staff**: This is an optional field for you to provide contact information for the escape room staff.
* **Game Master's Email**: Please enter the email address of the game master.
* **Escape Room Staff Email**: This is an optional field for you to provide the email address of the escape room staff.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Please enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Info**: This is an optional field for you to provide the contact information of the emergency contact person.
* **Participant Count**: Please enter the number of participants in the game.
* **Room Number**: Please enter the room number where the game is being played.
* **Game Length**: Please enter the length of the game in minutes.
* **Game Start Time**: Please enter the start time of the game.
* **Game End Time**: Please enter the end time of the game.

Note: It is essential to review each field carefully and fill out the form accurately to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience.
