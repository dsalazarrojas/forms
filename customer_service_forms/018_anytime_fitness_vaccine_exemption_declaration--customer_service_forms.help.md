# Anytime Fitness Vaccine Exemption Declaration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect exemption requests from gym members who wish to declare that they do not need to be vaccinated against COVID-19 or other diseases.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your member information:
	* **Member full name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your membership card.
	* **Membership ID number**: If you have a membership ID number, please enter it here. If not, please leave this field blank.
	* **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
	* **Email address**: Enter your email address.
	* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number.
	* **Facility location**: Enter the name of the facility where you are requesting the exemption.
2. Decline vaccination:
	* **Type of exemption requested**: Select the type of exemption you are requesting: "Medical exemption", "Religious exemption", or "Personal/Philosophical exemption".
	* **If medical - describe medical condition**: If you selected "Medical exemption", please describe your medical condition.
	* **Medical provider documentation provided?**: Indicate if you have attached medical provider documentation or if you will provide it.
	* **If religious - describe your religious beliefs**: If you selected "Religious exemption", please describe your religious beliefs.
	* **Religious organization or community**: If you selected "Religious exemption", enter the name of your religious organization or community.
	* **If philosophical - describe your beliefs**: If you selected "Philosophical exemption", please describe your philosophical beliefs.
3. Acknowledge risks:
	* **I acknowledge the health risks of not being vaccinated**: Select "I acknowledge" to confirm that you understand the health risks of not being vaccinated.
	* **I acknowledge I may spread disease to others**: Select "I acknowledge" to confirm that you understand that you may spread disease to others.
	* **I understand and accept the facility policy**: Select "I understand and accept" to confirm that you understand and accept the facility policy.
	* **I am willing to undergo regular health screening**: Select "Yes, willing" to confirm that you are willing to undergo regular health screening.
	* **I understand facility may require additional measures**: Select "Yes, I understand" to confirm that you understand that the facility may require additional measures.
4. Declaration:
	* **I declare the information provided is true and accurate**: Select "Yes, true and accurate" to confirm that the information provided is true and accurate.
