# full_width_contact_page_responsive - Help Guide
## Purpose
The full_width_contact_page_responsive form is a contact form designed for customers to reach out to us with their inquiries and requests. It is used to collect contact information and embed an iframe into the response page.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your contact information, including contact name, contact email, and contact message.
2. If you have a contact phone number, please fill it in for easy communication.
3. If you have a contact address, please fill it in for easy location identification.
4. If you have an iframe URL, please fill it in to embed it into the response page.
5. Customize the iframe settings, including width, height, alignment, margin, and padding, to fit your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Name** (contact_name, text, required): Enter your name in this field.
* **Contact Email** (contact_email, email, required): Enter your email address in this format: `example@example.com`.
* **Contact Phone** (contact_phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number in this format: `123-456-7890`.
* **Contact Message** (contact_message, text, required): Enter a brief message with your inquiry.
* **Contact Address** (contact_address, text, optional): Enter your address in this format: `Street Address, City, State, ZIP`.
* **Embed Iframe URL** (embedded_iframe_url, text, required): Enter the URL of the iframe you want to embed.
* **Embed Iframe Width** (embedded_iframe_width, number, optional): Enter the width of the iframe in pixels.
* **Embed Iframe Height** (embedded_iframe_height, number, optional): Enter the height of the iframe in pixels.
* **Embed Iframe Alignment** (embedded_iframe_alignment, select_one, optional): Select the alignment of the iframe: `none`, `center`, `left`, or `right`.
* **Embed Iframe Margin** (embedded_iframe_margin, number, optional): Enter the margin of the iframe in pixels.
* **Embed Iframe Padding** (embedded_iframe_padding, number, optional): Enter the padding of the iframe in pixels.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure a successful submission.
- If you are not sure about the iframe settings, you can leave them as default.
- If you have any issues or questions, please contact us.
