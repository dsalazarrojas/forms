# Satellite TV Local Channel Waiver Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Satellite TV Local Channel Waiver Request Form is used to request a waiver for a specific local channel on your satellite TV service. This form is reviewed and approved by the local channel manager.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Local Channel Waiver Request** (`satellite_tv_local_channel_waiver_request`): This is the main field for the request. Please provide a clear and concise reason for requesting a waiver for the local channel.
2. **Customer Name** (`customer_name`): Enter your name as it appears on your account or billing information.
3. **Customer Address** (`customer_address`): Enter your address as it appears on your account or billing information.
4. **Channel ID** (`channel_id`): If you have a channel ID, please enter it here.
5. **Channel Number** (`channel_number`): If you have a channel number, please enter it here.
6. **Local Channel Waiver Reason** (`local_channel_waiver_reason`): This is an optional note field for you to provide additional information about your waiver request. Use this field to explain your reason for requesting a waiver in more detail.
7. **Local Channel Waiver Date** (`local_channel_waiver_date`): If you have a specific date for the waiver, please enter it here. This will help the local channel manager to schedule the review process accordingly.
8. **Satellite TV Service Address** (`satellite_tv_service_address`): Enter the address where you receive your satellite TV service.
9. **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`): Please sign this form to confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete.
10. **Local Channel Manager Name** (`local_channel_local_channel_manager_name`): This field is not required for customers. It's for internal use only.
11. **Local Channel Manager Title** (`local_channel_local_channel_manager_title`): This field is not required for customers. It's for internal use only.
12. **Local Channel Manager Phone** (`local_channel_local_channel_manager_phone`): This field is not required for customers. It's for internal use only.

## Tips

* Please make sure to review all fields before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your local channel manager or customer service representative.
