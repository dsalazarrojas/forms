# Little Acres Learning Academy Emergency Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Little Acres Learning Academy Emergency Form is used to collect information from parents or guardians about their child's medical history, allergies, and emergency contact information. This form helps us ensure that we have the necessary information to provide the best possible care for your child in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your child's First Name and Last Name.
2. Enter your child's Date of Birth.
3. Select the relationship between the emergency contact and your child.
4. Provide contact information for the emergency contact (e.g., phone number, address).
5. List any allergies or medical conditions your child has.
6. List any known medical diseases or conditions that your child has.
7. Provide any relevant medical history information.
8. If applicable, select and enter the details of any emergency medical certification.
9. Sign and date the form to confirm that the information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your child's first name. This will help us to identify your child correctly.
* **Last Name**: Enter your child's last name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your child's date of birth.
* **Parent Permission**: This field is only required if you are filling this form as a parent or guardian. Select the relationship between the emergency contact and your child (e.g., Parent, Grandparent, Legal Custodian).
* **Contact Info**: Enter the phone number or contact information of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Info**: Enter any additional contact information for the emergency contact (e.g., address, email).
* **Allergy Info**: List any allergies or medical conditions your child has.
* **Medical Condition**: List any medical diseases or conditions that your child has.
* **Medical History**: Provide any relevant medical history information.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Select the relationship between the emergency contact and your child.
* **Medical Treatment**: Indicate if your child has received medical treatment for the listed conditions.
* **Medical Treatment Notes**: If applicable, provide any additional notes about your child's medical treatment.
* **Emergency Medical Certification**: Indicate if your child has an emergency medical certification.
* **Medical Certification Number**: If applicable, enter the number of the medical certification.
* **Medical Certification Date**: If applicable, enter the date of the medical certification.
* **Medical Certification Provider**: If applicable, enter the name and address of the medical provider.
* **Medical Certification Provider Phone**: If applicable, enter the phone number of the medical provider.
* **Medical Certification Provider Fax**: If applicable, enter the fax number of the medical provider.
* **Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm that the information is accurate.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check the accuracy of all the information provided.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
