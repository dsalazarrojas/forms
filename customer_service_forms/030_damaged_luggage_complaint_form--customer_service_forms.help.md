# damaged_luggage_complaint_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers who have experienced issues with their luggage during travel. It will help our customer service team to quickly and efficiently address the problem and provide a response to the customer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Review and submit the form once you have filled in all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Report Issue
* **Report Issue** (`report_issue`, `text`, optional): Here, you can describe the issue with your luggage, such as a damage or loss during your recent travel. Please provide a clear and concise explanation of what happened.

### Report Details
* **Report Details** (`report_details`, `text`, optional): In this section, you can provide more details about your issue. This could include the travel dates, flight numbers, and any other relevant information that might help us better understand the situation.

### Resolution Request
* **Resolution Request** (`resolution_request`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more options from the following:
	+ Airline
	+ Travel Company
Please choose the option that best describes the resolution you would like to see, or select both if you would like to see both.

### Attachment
* **Attachment** (`attachment`, `text`, optional): If you have any supporting documents or photos related to your issue, you can attach them here. This could include receipts, photos of the damaged luggage, or any other relevant evidence.

## Tips
* Be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out the form.
* If you have any supporting documentation, please attach it to the form to help us better understand your issue.
* Our team will review your form and respond to you as soon as possible.
