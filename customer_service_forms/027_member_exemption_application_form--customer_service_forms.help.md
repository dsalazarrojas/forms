# member_exemption_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The member exemption application form is used to apply for a member exemption, a benefit that can be granted by the organization. This form collects information about the customer's exemption type, reason, frequency, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the exemption type that best describes your situation.
2. Choose multiple exemption frequencies if required.
3. Enter a valid exemption reason.
4. Enter a valid exemption date.
5. Enter any additional comments or information you'd like to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member Exemption Form Type** (member_exemption_form_type): Select the exemption type that best describes your situation.
* **Member Exemption Form Subtype** (member_exemption_form_subtype): Choose one or more subtypes that match your exemption type.
* **Member Exemption Form Reason** (member_exemption_form_reason): Enter a valid reason for your exemption.
* **Member Exemption Form Date** (member_exemption_form_date): Enter a future date for your exemption to start.
* **Member Exemption Form Time** (member_exemption_form_time): Enter a time for your exemption to start.
* **Member Exemption Exemption Amount** (member_exemption_exemption_amount): Enter the amount of the exemption.
* **Member Exemption Exemption Frequency** (member_exemption_exemption_frequency): Choose one or more frequencies that your exemption will occur.
* **Member Exemption Application Comments** (member_exemption_application_comments): Enter any additional comments or information you'd like to provide.
* **Member Exemption Form Email** (member_exemption_form_email): Enter your email address.
* **Member Exemption Form Phone** (member_exemption_form_phone): Enter your phone number.
* **Member Exemption Form Organization** (member_exemption_form_organization): Enter your organization's name.
* **Member Exemption Form Address** (member_exemption_form_address): Enter your address.
* **Member Exemption Form City** (member_exemption_form_city): Enter your city.
* **Member Exemption Form State** (member_exemption_form_state): Enter your state.
* **Member Exemption Form Zip** (member_exemption_form_zip): Enter your zip code.
* **Member Exemption Form Country** (member_exemption_form_country): Enter your country.
* **Member Exemption Form Language** (member_exemption_form_language): Enter your language.
* **Member Exemption Form Additional Comments** (member_exemption_form_additional_comments): Enter any additional comments or information you'd like to provide.
* **Member Exemption Form Exemption Date** (member_exemption_form_exemption_date): Enter a future date for your exemption to start.
* **Member Exemption Form Exemption End Date** (member_exemption_form_exemption_end_date): Enter a future date for your exemption to end.
* **Member Exemption Exemption Type** (member_exemption_exemption_type): Enter the type of exemption.
* **Member Exemption Exemption Status** (member_exemption_exemption_status): Enter the status of your exemption.
* **Member Exemption Exemption Notes** (member_exemption_exemption_notes): Enter any additional notes or information you'd like to provide.
* **Member Exemption Form Submit** (member_exemption_form_submit): Select how you'd like to submit your application.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the exemption type selected matches the reason provided.
* Please enter a valid exemption date.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact our customer service team for assistance.
