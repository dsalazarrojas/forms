# Furniture Removals Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Furniture Removals Quote Form" is a tool used to collect information from customers requesting a quote for furniture removal services. This form helps the customer service team to gather necessary details about the customer's move, including their contact information, move details, and any additional services required.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide your address and city.
3. Select your state from the list.
4. Enter the date of your move.
5. Enter the time of your move.
6. Indicate whether you are moving long distance or locally.
7. Describe the items you are moving.
8. Select any additional services you require (e.g. packing, cleaning, etc.).
9. Enter your contact information for the customer service team.
10. Enter any notes or comments about your move.
11. Indicate whether the quote has been accepted or declined.
12. Enter the reason for declining the quote (if applicable).
13. Enter the quote ID (if applicable).
14. Enter any internal notes about the customer's account.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information (1)**: Enter your name, email, and phone number.
* **Name (2)**: Enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email (3)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (4)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address (5)**: Enter your address.
* **City (6)**: Enter your city.
* **State (7)**: Select your state from the list.
* **Date of Move (8)**: Enter the date you are moving.
* **Time of Move (9)**: Enter the time you are moving.
* **Type of Move (10)**: Indicate whether you are moving locally or long distance.
* **Move Distance (11)**: Describe the distance of your move.
* **Move Items (12)**: Describe the items you are moving.
* **Extra Services (13)**: Select any additional services you require (e.g. packing, cleaning, etc.).
* **Contact Info (14)**: Enter your contact information for the customer service team.
* **Quote (15)**: Enter the quote amount.
* **Notes (16)**: Enter any notes or comments about your move.
* **Accepted (17)**: Indicate whether the quote has been accepted or not.
* **Declined (18)**: Indicate whether the quote has been declined.
* **Reason Declined (19)**: Enter the reason for declining the quote (if applicable).
* **Quote ID (20)**: Enter the quote ID (if applicable).
* **Customer Service Note (21)**: Enter any comments or notes about the customer's account.
* **Internal Notes (22)**: Enter any internal notes about the customer's account.
* **Removal Quote (23)**: Enter the removal quote details.
* **Quote Status (24)**: Indicate the status of the quote (e.g. pending, issued, completed, declined).
* **Updated (25)**: The date and time the form was updated.
