# Healthy India Wealthy India - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help gather information about health-related issues in nearby areas and gather feedback on the effectiveness of different treatments. The form is meant to be completed by individuals who have experienced or know someone who has experienced these issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the disease that is more effective in your nearby areas from the list of options on page 1.
2. If you have experienced discrimination, please describe it in the text field on page 2.
3. Choose the diseases that are problems for you from the list of options on page 3.
4. You may also provide additional information about your financial situation in the text field on page 4.
5. Finally, describe our motive for creating this form in the text field on page 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Disease in nearby areas
* **Disease in nearby areas** (`select_one`, required: false): Select the disease that is more effective in your nearby areas. Please choose one of the options:
	+ Disease A
	+ Disease B
	+ Disease C
### Page 2: Person discriminated
* **Person discriminated** (`text`, required: false): Describe the experience of discrimination you or someone else has faced. This is an optional field.
### Page 3: Disease problems
* **Disease problems** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the diseases that are problems for you or someone else.
	+ Disease 1
	+ Disease 2
	+ Disease 3
### Page 4: Financially manner
* **Financially manner** (`note`, required: false): Please note any information about your financial situation that you feel is relevant to your response. This field is optional and is used to provide context about your situation.
### Page 5: Our motive
* **Our motive** (`text`, required: false): Describe our motive for creating this form. Please note that our motive is to help individuals with financially manner-related issues, but we also want to hear about our form and how it can be improved.
