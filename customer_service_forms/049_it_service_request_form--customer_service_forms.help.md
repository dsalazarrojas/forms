# it_service_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "it_service_request_form" is a form used to submit service requests to the internal IT team for assistance with various IT-related issues. This form allows customers to provide necessary contact details, describe the issue, and specify the requested service type.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Choose the relevant "Service Request Type" that best suits your request.
2.  Enter your contact details, including your email and phone number.
3.  Select the required services you need assistance with.
4.  Describe the issue you are experiencing (optional).
5.  Specify the date and time you would like the service to be provided.
6.  Select the priority level of your request.
7.  Choose the person you would like your request to be assigned to.
8.  Choose the status of your request.
9.  Add any additional comments about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Service Request Type** (`service_request_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of service you are requesting. This field will help the IT team understand the nature of your request.
*   **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter your contact information so that the IT team can reach out to you.
*   **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the specific services you require assistance with. This field allows you to choose multiple services at once.
*   **Description** (`description`, note, optional): Provide any additional context or details about your issue.
*   **Service Request Date** (`service_request_date`, date, optional): Specify the date when you would like the service to be provided.
*   **Service Request Time** (`service_request_time`, time, optional): Choose a specific time for the service to be provided.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so that the IT team can communicate with you.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so that the IT team can contact you.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information about your request.
*   **Priority** (`priority`, select_one, optional): Choose the priority level of your request. This will help the IT team understand the urgency of your request.
*   **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, optional): Choose the person you would like your request to be assigned to.
*   **Status** (`status`, select_one, optional): Choose the status of your request. This will help the IT team track the progress of your request.

Note: The fields marked as "optional" can be skipped if not applicable to your request.
