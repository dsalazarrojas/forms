# NCSL Reimbursement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The NCSL Reimbursement Form is used to request reimbursement for expenses related to the National Capital Stockholders Limited (NCSL) company. This form collects information about the individual submitting the request, including their name, contact details, and expense-related information such as date, time, and amount.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the required fields.
2. Enter your Email address to ensure we can contact you about your reimbursement request.
3. Provide the Date of the expense in the Date field.
4. Enter the Time of the expense in the Time field (optional).
5. Specify the Amount of the expense.
6. Enter a brief Description of the reason for the expense.
7. If you were using an Assigned Tool during the expense, enter its name in the Assigned Tool field.
8. If you would like to be informed about the status of your reimbursement request, enter your Phone number.
9. If you have already submitted the form, enter your Form ID to facilitate tracking of your request.
10. Finally, enter the Date and Time of when you submitted the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your official email address where you can be reached for communication about your reimbursement request.
* **Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the expense in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time** (time, optional): Enter the time of the expense (HH:MM format).
* **Amount** (number, required): Enter the amount of the expense.
* **Description** (text, required): Enter a brief description of the reason for the expense.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number to be informed about the status of your reimbursement request.
* **Assigned Tool** (text, optional): Enter the name of the tool assigned to you during the expense.
* **Submitted By** (text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Form ID** (text, optional): Enter the ID of the previous submitted form to track your reimbursement request.
* **Submitted Date** (date, optional): Enter the date when you submitted the form in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Submitted Time** (time, optional): Enter the time when you submitted the form in HH:MM format.
* **Submitted Date YMD** (date, optional): This field is a duplicate of the **Submitted Date** field and will be removed in a future update.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact our customer support team.
* This form is used to request reimbursement for official expenses only.
* Ensure that you have all necessary documentation supporting your expense before submitting the form.
