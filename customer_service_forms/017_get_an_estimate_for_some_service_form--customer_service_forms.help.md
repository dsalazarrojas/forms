# get_an_estimate_for_some_service_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers to generate an estimate for a specific service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the service you are interested in by choosing from the options provided.
2. Provide your customer information, including name and email address.
3. Specify the date and time you would like to receive the estimate for.
4. Select the tool you would like to be assigned to you.
5. Enter any additional notes about your service needs.
6. Provide your phone number for contact.
7. Enter your message to the customer service representative.
8. Enter any additional notes about the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Details** (`service_details`): Enter a brief description of the service you require.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`): Select the customer you are representing (Customer 1, Customer 2, or Customer 3).
* **Estimate Value** (`get_an_estimate`): Enter a value for the estimate (a numerical value).
* **Service Hours** (`service_hours`): Select the date and time you would like to receive the estimate for (Today, Tomorrow, or Next 7 Days).
* **Get Estimate For** (`get_estimate_for`): Select up to three dates and times you would like to receive the estimate for.
* **Service Address** (`service_address`): Enter any additional address information (e.g., building number, floor).
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`): Enter your email address so we can contact you about the estimate.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about the estimate.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): Select the tool you would like to be assigned to you (Chat Jimmy or Other).
* **Customer Message** (`customer_message`): Enter any additional information or questions you have for the customer service representative.
* **Service Notes** (`service_notes`): Enter any additional notes about the service.
