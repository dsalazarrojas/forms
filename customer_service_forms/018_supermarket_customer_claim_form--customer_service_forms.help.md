# Supermarket Customer Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supermarket Customer Claim Form is a tool used to collect information from customers about their experiences, concerns, or complaints about the supermarket. This form helps the supermarket management to identify and address customer issues efficiently and improve their services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your details in the "Customer Form" field.
2.  Describe the product that was involved in the issue in the "Product Details" field.
3.  Provide a detailed description of the problem in the "Issue Description" field.
4.  Enter the date and time when the issue occurred in the "Date and Time" field.
5.  Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field for follow-up purposes.
6.  Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
7.  If relevant, attach any files or documents that support your complaint in the "Issue Attachment" field.
8.  Indicate whether the issue was resolved internally in the "Issue Route" field.
9.  Indicate whether a team was assigned to handle the issue in the "Assigned Team" field.
10. Provide any further comments or messages in the "Customer Message" field.
11. Choose a follow-up time frame in the "Follow Up" field.
12. Enter the name of the supermarket where the issue occurred in the "Supermarket Name" field.
13. Enter your name in the "Customer Name" field.
14. Enter your email address in the "Customer Email" field.
15. Enter your mobile number in the "Customer Mobile" field.
16. Enter your address in the "Customer Address" field.
