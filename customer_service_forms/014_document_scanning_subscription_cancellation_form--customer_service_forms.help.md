# Document Scanning Subscription Cancellation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to cancel a customer's subscription. Please fill out this form to initiate the cancellation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the options for "cancel_subscription" and select "Yes" if you wish to cancel your subscription.
2. Provide a reason for cancellation in the "reason_for_cancellation" field. This will help us understand the cause of the cancellation.
3. Add any additional comments or notes in the "customer_notes" field.
4. Confirm your cancellation decision in the "confirm_cancellation" field by selecting "Yes".
5. Enter your email address in the "email" field to allow us to contact you.
6. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "phone" field.
7. In the "follow_up_details" field, provide any additional details we might need to follow up on your cancellation.
8. Sign and date the "customer_signature" field to confirm your identity.
9. If your provider is available, ask them to sign and timestamp the "provider_signature" field.
10. Finally, select a cancellation date in the "cancellation_date" field to confirm the cancellation date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Cancel Subscription** (cancel_subscription, select_multiple, required: false)
  Select "Yes" to cancel your subscription.
* **Reason for Cancellation** (reason_for_cancellation, text, required: false)
  Enter a brief description of why you are cancelling your subscription.
* **Customer Notes** (customer_notes, note, required: false)
  Add any additional comments or notes you'd like to share with us.
* **Confirm Cancellation** (confirm_cancellation, select_one, required: false)
  Select "Yes" to confirm your cancellation decision.
* **Email** (email, email, required: false)
  Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (phone, text, required: false)
  If you have a phone number, enter it here.
* **Follow Up Details** (follow_up_details, note, required: false)
  Provide any additional details we might need to follow up on your cancellation.
* **Customer Signature** (customer_signature, date, required: false)
  Sign and date this field to confirm your identity.
* **Provider Signature** (provider_signature, time, required: false)
  If your provider is available, ask them to sign and timestamp this field.
* **Cancellation Date** (cancellation_date, date, required: false)
  Select the date you wish to cancel your subscription.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your cancellation is processed correctly.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
