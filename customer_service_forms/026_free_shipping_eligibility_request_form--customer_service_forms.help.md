# Free Shipping Eligibility Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers request free shipping eligibility on their orders. Please fill out the form with your order information and shipping details to help us determine if you are eligible for free shipping.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Make sure to complete all required fields.
3. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, optional):
  Please enter your customer name and contact information.
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, text, optional):
  Please provide any relevant shipping information, such as your shipping address or special instructions.
* **Free Shipping Eligibility** (`shipping_method`, select_one, optional):
  Choose "Yes" if you are requesting free shipping.
* **Free Shipping Type** (`shipping_type`, select_multiple, optional):
  Select all types of free shipping you are requesting (e.g. "Yes", "Yes", etc.).
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, optional):
  Enter your shipping address for the relevant order.
* **Shipping Cost** (`shipping_cost`, number, optional):
  Enter the shipping cost for the order.
* **Shipping Speed** (`shipping_speed`, select_one, optional):
  Choose "Yes" if you would like to prioritize fast shipping for this order.
* **Shipping Weight** (`shipping_weight`, number, optional):
  Enter the weight of the item(s) being shipped.
* **Shipping Status** (`shipping_status`, select_multiple, optional):
  Select the shipping status for the order (e.g. "Active", "Inactive"). 

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you have any special shipping requests or instructions, please include them in the "Shipping Details" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our customer service team.
