# Flight Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to input their flight reservation details, which will be used to process their flight reservation requests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field according to your flight details:
	* **First Name** (`first_name`): Type your first name.
	* **Last Name** (`last_name`): Type your last name.
	* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
	* **Phone** (`phone`): Type your phone number.
	* **Flight Number** (`flight_number`): Select your flight number from the options provided.
	* **Departure Date** (`departure_date`): Select the departure date of your flight.
	* **Return Date** (`return_date`): Select the return date of your flight, if applicable.
	* **Departure Time** (`departure_time`): Select the departure time of your flight.
	* **Travel Class** (`travel_class`): Select the travel class of your flight (Economy, Business, or First Class).
	* **Flight Status** (`flight_status`): Select the flight status (On Time, Delayed, or Boarding Closed).
	* **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`): Select the arrival time of your flight.
	* **Travel Agent** (`travel_agent`): Type your travel agent's name.
	* **Customer Name** (`customer`): Type your name (first and last).
	* **Passenger Type** (`passenger_type`): Type the type of passenger (e.g., adult, child, senior).
	* **Customer Type** (`customer_type`): Type the type of customer (e.g., individual, business, etc.).
	* **Customer ID** (`customer_id`): Type your customer ID number.
	* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`): Enter your email address.
	* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`): Type your phone number.
2. Add any **Special Requests** you may have in the "Flight Details" field.
3. Review your input carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Flight Number**: Select the flight number that corresponds with your flight reservation.
* **Departure Date**: Select the date of departure for your flight.
* **Return Date**: If you are booking a return flight, select the date of return.
* **Departure Time**: Select the time of departure for your flight.
* **Travel Class**: Select the travel class of your flight (Economy, Business, or First Class).
* **Flight Status**: Select the status of your flight (On Time, Delayed, or Boarding Closed).
* **Arrival Time**: Select the time of arrival for your flight.
* **Travel Agent**: Enter the name of the travel agent assisting you with your flight reservation.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Passenger Type**: Select the type of passenger (e.g., adult, child, senior).
* **Customer Type**: Enter the type of customer (e.g., individual, business, etc.).
* **Customer ID**: Enter your customer ID number.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Flight Details**: Enter any special requests or comments regarding your flight.
* **Special Requests**: Enter any additional special requests you may have.
