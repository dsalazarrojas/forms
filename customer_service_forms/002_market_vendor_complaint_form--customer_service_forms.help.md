# Market Vendor Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Market Vendor Complaint Form is designed to collect and track customer complaints about market vendors, allowing customers to voice their concerns and market administrators to address them efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the Customer Name field.
2. Select all vendors involved in the complaint.
3. Describe the complaint in the Complaint field.
4. Enter your Market ID, the ID assigned to your market account.
5. Choose the date of the incident or when you realized the issue.
6. Enter your contact information, including your name and email address (or phone number), so that we can reach out to you for further clarification or updates.
7. Enter your Complainant ID, the ID associated with your account.
8. Enter your Vendor ID, the ID assigned to the vendor involved in the complaint.
9. Choose how you would like us to handle the complaint, between Report to Market, Report to Vendors, or Report to Both.
10. Enter a detailed description of the issue in the Description field.
11. Indicate the current status of the complaint, whether Open or Closed.
12. Choose whether we should follow up on the issue, by selecting True or False.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your account with us.
* **Vendors** (select multiple, required): Select all vendors involved in the complaint.
* **Complaint** (text, required): Describe the issue you are reporting, providing as much detail as possible.
* **Market Id** (number, required): Enter your Market ID, which can be found in your account settings or in our customer service communication.
* **Date** (date, required): Enter the date of the incident or when you first realized the issue.
* **Contact** (text, required): Enter your contact information, including your name and email address or phone number, so we can reach out to you for further clarification or updates.
* **Complainant ID** (number, required): Enter your Complainant ID, which can be found in your account settings or in our customer service communication.
* **Vendor ID** (number, required): Enter the Vendor ID of the vendor involved in the complaint.
* **Action** (select one, required): Choose how you would like us to handle the complaint, between Report to Market, Report to Vendors, or Report to Both.
* **Description** (text, required): Provide a detailed description of the issue.
* **Status** (select one, required): Indicate the current status of the complaint, whether Open or Closed.
* **Follow up** (select one, required): Choose whether we should follow up on the issue, by selecting True or False.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
- Make sure to include your Market ID and Vendor ID for accurate tracking.
- If you are unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to contact our customer service team for assistance.
