# Pre-Sales-Questionnaire-Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pre-Sales-Questionnaire-Form is used to gather information about a customer's pre-sales inquiries. This form helps to identify the potential sales lead, the decision-making process, and other essential details to determine the best course of action for sales and customer support teams.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your title, if applicable.
2.  Provide your name.
3.  Enter your company name.
4.  Select your job position and answer the questions about sales leads and decision makers.
5.  Enter your email and phone number.
6.  Enter your company's website and address.
7.  Select the sales channels for your company.
8.  Enter the name and contact information of the sales representative assigned to you.
9.  Finally, provide any additional comments or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (Text, Required): If you have a title, please enter it here.
* **Customer Name** (Text, Required): Please enter your name.
* **Company Name** (Text, Required): Enter your company's name.
* **Job Position** (Select One, Optional): Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have a specific job position within the company.
* **Department** (Text, Optional): Enter your department name.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your company email address.
* **Phone** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number.
* **Sales Lead** (Select One, Required): Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you are a sales lead.
* **Decision Maker(s)** (Select Multiple, Required): Answer 'Yes' or 'No' for each decision maker in your company.
* **Company URL** (Text, Required): Enter your company's website.
* **Sales Representative Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the sales representative assigned to you.
* **Sales Representative Phone** (Text, Required): Enter the phone number of the sales representative.
* **Sales Representative Email** (Email, Required): Enter the email address of the sales representative.
* **Sales Representative Title** (Text, Required): Enter the title of the sales representative.
* **Company Address** (Text, Required): Enter your company's address.
* **Company City** (Text, Required): Enter your city.
* **Company State** (Text, Required): Enter your state.
* **Postal Code** (Text, Required): Enter your post office code.
* **Company Country** (Text, Required): Enter your country.
* **Sales Channel(s)** (Text, Required): Select all sales channels that apply to your company.
* **Additional Channels** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional sales channels that are not listed.
