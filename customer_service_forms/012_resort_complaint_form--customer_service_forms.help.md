# resort_complaint_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The resort_complaint_form is designed to gather information from customers who have experienced an issue during their stay at the resort. This form will help us to understand what happened and how we can improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the type of complaint you are filing (e.g. Food, Room, Service, or Other).
2. Provide a detailed description of the issue you experienced.
3. Enter your phone number so we can contact you to discuss the matter further.
4. Enter your email address so we can reply to your complaint and keep you updated on our actions.
5. Enter your name, which will be used to identify you in our system.
6. If you experienced any issues during your stay, please provide the dates for your stay, check-in and check-out dates, and the date of the incident.
7. Provide any additional feedback you would like to share about your experience.
8. If you have any attachment related to your complaint (e.g. a photo of the issue), you can upload it here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Complaint Type** (Select One)
	+ Please select the category of your complaint (Food, Room, Service, or Other).
* **Description** 
	+ Type in a detailed description of the issue you experienced during your stay.
* **Phone**
	+ Enter your phone number so we can contact you to discuss the matter further.
* **Email**
	+ Enter your email address so we can reply to your complaint and keep you updated on our actions.
* **Name**
	+ Enter your name, which will be used to identify you in our system.
* **Stay Date**, **Checkin Date**, and **Checkout Date**
	+ If you experienced any issues during your stay, please provide the dates for your stay, check-in and check-out dates, and the date of the incident.
* **Feedback**
	+ Provide any additional feedback you would like to share about your experience.
* **Attachment**
	+ If you have any attachment related to your complaint (e.g. a photo of the issue), you can upload it here.

## Tips

* Please make sure to provide as much detail as possible when describing your complaint.
* If you have any supporting documentation (e.g. photos, videos), please attach them to the form.
* Our team will review your complaint and respond to you within 3 business days.
