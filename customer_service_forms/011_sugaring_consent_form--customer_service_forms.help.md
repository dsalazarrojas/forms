# Sugaring Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Sugaring Consent Form is a document that requires the patient's consent for various services provided by the business. This form ensures that the patient is aware of and agrees to the terms and conditions of the services they are receiving.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the services and terms and conditions outlined.
2. Fill in your consent for the service(s) you are receiving, choosing from the options provided.
3. Enter your name to confirm your identity.
4. Provide a valid email address for communication purposes.
5. Optionally, provide your date of birth and service date if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Date of Consent
* **Date of Consent** (`date`, optional): Date on which you are providing consent for the service.

### Service Type
* **Service Type** (`select_one`, optional): Choose the type of service you are receiving. Options: Sugar, Chemical Peel, Microblading

### Signature
* **Signature** (`text`, optional): A handwritten signature to confirm your consent.

### Consent
* **Consent** (`select_multiple`, required): Select from the options to confirm your understanding of the consent form. Options: I have read and understand the consent form, Other

### Patient Name
* **Patient Name** (`text`, optional): Your full name.

### Patient Email
* **Patient Email** (`email`, optional): A valid email address for communication purposes.

### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth** (`date`, optional): Your date of birth.

### Service Date
* **Service Date** (`date`, optional): The date on which you will be receiving the service.

### Service Provider
* **Service Provider** (`text`, optional): The name of the service provider.

## Tips

* Please take the time to carefully review the form and understand the services and terms and conditions outlined.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to the service provider.
* This form is a one-time consent form, please ensure you review and understand the terms and conditions before signing.
