# Opening Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a general purpose checklist for customer interactions, likely used for administrative or quality assurance tasks. It is intended to gather relevant customer information and details about their visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name (Customer) in the first field.
2. Add your details (if any) in the Customer Details field.
3. Select a valid date for your visit using the calendar or manual input.
4. Choose a time of your visit from the time selection options.
5. In the Reason for Visit field, select one or more reasons that apply to you.
6. Select your name and ID number (if required) from the list of options.
7. Leave a signature to confirm your visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Opening Checklist** 
	* (opening_checklist, text, optional)
This field is the title of the form. You do not need to fill this field as it is a header.

### 2. **Customer**
	* (customer, text, required)
This field is for your name. Please enter your name accurately and legibly so that we can identify you properly.

### 3. **Customer Details**
	* (customer_details, text, optional)
This field is for adding any additional details relevant to your visit. You can add anything you feel is important about your visit.

### 4. **Date of Visit**
	* (date_of_visit, date, optional)
Please select a valid date for your visit using the calendar. If you know the date manually, you can input it manually.

### 5. **Time of Visit**
	* (time_of_visit, time, optional)
Select a valid time for your visit from the given options.

### 6. **Reason for Visit**
	* (reason_for_visit, select_multiple, optional)
Select one or more reasons that apply to your visit. These options are Medical Condition, Other, Referral, Test, and Walk-in.

### 7. **Employee Name**
	* (employee_name, select_one, optional)
Select the name of the employee who assisted you during your visit.

### 8. **Employee ID Number**
	* (employee_id_number, text, optional)
This field is for the employee's ID number. If required, please enter the correct ID.

### 9. **Customer ID Number**
	* (customer_id_number, text, optional)
This field is for your ID number. If required, please enter the correct ID.

### 10. **Customer Signature**
	* (customer_signature, note, optional)
This field is for you to leave a signature to confirm your visit.

### 11. **Employee Signature**
	* (employee_signature, note, optional)
This field is for the employee to sign and confirm your visit.

# Tips
- Be as accurate and clear as possible when filling the form.
- Make sure to select correct and relevant reasons for your visit.
- If required, verify your ID numbers are correct.
- A signature is a way to confirm your visit, please make it legible and clear.
