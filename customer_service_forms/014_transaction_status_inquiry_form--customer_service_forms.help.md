# Transaction Status Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Transaction Status Inquiry Form is used to gather information about the status of a transaction, allowing customer service representatives to quickly and efficiently assist customers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the type of transaction you are inquiring about.
2. Enter the date of the transaction.
3. Enter the time of the transaction.
4. Enter the customer's name.
5. Enter the transaction's ID number.
6. Enter the transaction amount.
7. Enter any relevant status notes or comments about the transaction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Transaction Type (transaction_type)**: Select the type of transaction you are inquiring about. 
* **Transaction Date (transaction_date)**: Enter the date of the transaction in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Transaction Time (transaction_time)**: Enter the time of the transaction in HH:MM AM/PM format.
* **Customer Name (customer_name)**: Enter the name of the customer associated with the transaction.
* **Transaction ID (transaction_id)**: Enter the unique ID number assigned to the transaction.
* **Transaction Amount (transaction_amount)**: Enter the total amount of the transaction.
* **Status Note (status_note)**: Enter any relevant status updates or comments about the transaction.
