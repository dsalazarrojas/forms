# Customer Loyalty Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Loyalty Survey is a form used to gather feedback from customers about their experiences with the store. Their responses will help us better understand their needs and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please answer the questions as honestly as possible. The form is divided into several sections, each with its own set of questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Loyalty** (`1`, `customer_loyalty_survey`, optional): Please rate your sense of loyalty to our store. 
* **Satisfaction Level** (`2`, `customer_satisfaction_level`, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with our store. 
* **Are you enrolled in our loyalty program?** (`3`, `loyalty_program_enrollment`, optional): Tell us if you're part of our loyalty program.
* **How often do you visit our store?** (`4`, `frequency_of_visits`, optional): Choose how often you visit our store. 
* **Overall Experience** (`5`, `overall_experience`, optional): Please provide the date of your last visit to the store.
* **Store Hours** (`6`, `store_hours`, optional): Unfortunately, this field is not used in the form and should be removed.
* **How well do employees know their products?** (`7`, `employee_knowledge`, optional): Rate how well store employees know their products.
* **How friendly are store employees?** (`8`, `employee_friendliness`, optional): Rate how friendly store employees are.
* **Customer Service** (`9`, `customer_service`, optional): Choose your level of satisfaction with our customer service. 
* **Email** (`10`, `email_address`, optional): Enter your email address for any further communication.
* **Phone** (`11`, `phone_number`, optional): Enter your phone number for any further communication.
* **Store Feedback** (`12`, `store_feedback`, optional): Write any additional comments or concerns about the store.
* **Additional Comments** (`13`, `additional_comments`, optional): Share any other feedback or comments you may have.
* **Customer ID** (`14`, `customer_id`, optional): Enter your customer ID.
* **Employee ID** (`15`, `employee_id`, optional): Enter the employee ID of the staff member who served you.
* **Store ID** (`16`, `store_id`, optional): Enter the store ID.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions truthfully and honestly.
* If you have any additional comments or concerns, please write them in the "Store Feedback" field.
* If you'd like to be contacted for further communication, please enter your email address and phone number.
