# (INFINITY LEAD GEN) Independent Contractor Service Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the (INFINITY LEAD GEN) Independent Contractor Service Agreement form. This form is a legally binding contract between you, the independent contractor, and your client, outlining the terms and conditions of your services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your full legal name, email address, and phone number.
2. Enter your business entity type (e.g., Sole Proprietorship, Single-member LLC, etc.).
3. Provide your tax ID number (SSN or EIN).
4. Select your services you will offer (e.g., Lead Qualification & Screening, Customer Support / Help Desk, etc.).
5. Enter your years of experience in the industry.
6. Choose your weekly availability level (e.g., Full-time, Part-time, etc.).
7. Enter your proposed rate of pay (in USD).
8. Confirm your equipment and setup status.
9. Select your preferred payment terms (e.g., Net 15, Net 30, etc.).
10. Agree to the terms and conditions of the contract, including confidentiality, non-disclosure, and tax responsibilities.
11. Authorize a background check (if applicable).
12. Choose a desired start date for your services.
13. (Optional) Enter an emergency contact name and phone number.
14. Sign the form digitally with your full legal name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Legal Name** (`full_legal_name`, text, required): This is your full legal name as it appears on your tax documents.
* **Business Name (DBA)** (`business_name_dba`, text, optional): If you are operating under a business name different from your personal name, enter it here. Otherwise, leave blank.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): This is your primary contact email address for invoices and communications.
* **Mobile Phone Number** (`phone_number`, phone, required): Enter your mobile phone number for urgent scheduling and verification calls.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): This is required for background checks and tax verification purposes.
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, text, required): Enter your service address and mailing location.
* **City, State, ZIP Code** (`city_state_zip`, text, required): Enter your city, state, and ZIP code for your service address.
* **Business Entity Type** (`business_structure`, select_one, required): Choose how your business is organized for tax purposes (e.g., Sole Proprietorship, Single-member LLC, etc.).
* **Tax ID (SSN or EIN)** (`tax_id_number`, text, required): Enter your SSN if you are a sole proprietor or EIN if you are an incorporated entity.
* **Services You Will Provide** (`services_offered`, select_multiple, required): Choose the services you will offer to your client (e.g., Lead Qualification & Screening, Customer Support / Help Desk, etc.).
* **Years of Experience** (`years_experience`, integer, required): Enter the number of years you have experience in the industry.
* **Weekly Availability** (`availability_level`, select_one, required): Choose your typical weekly availability level (e.g., Full-time, Part-time, etc.).
* **Proposed Rate (USD)** (`compensation_rate`, decimal, required): Enter your proposed rate of pay in USD (e.g., 25.00 or 5.50).
* **Equipment & Setup Status** (`equipment_status`, select_multiple, required): Confirm your equipment and setup status (e.g., Reliable high-speed internet, Quiet workspace / home office, etc.).
* **Preferred Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred payment terms (e.g., Net 15, Net 30, etc.).
* **Legal Acknowledgments** (`legal_acknowledgments`, select_multiple, required): Select all terms you agree to, including confidentiality, non-disclosure, and tax responsibilities.
* **Background Check Authorization** (`background_check_consent`, select_one, required): Authorize a background check (if applicable).
* **Desired Start Date** (`agreement_effective_date`, date, required): Choose your desired start date for your services.
* **How did you hear about this opportunity?** (`referral_source`, text, optional): Enter how you found out about this opportunity (e.g., Social Media, Referral, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter an emergency contact name and phone number (if applicable).
* **Digital Signature** (`digital_signature`, text, required): Sign the form digitally with your full legal name, acknowledging you agree to the terms and conditions.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have reviewed and understood all the terms and conditions before signing the form.
* Double-check your personal and business details for accuracy.
* Choose your services and availability carefully to ensure you can provide quality services to your client.
* Confirm your proposed rate of pay and payment terms with your client.
* Authorize a background check (if applicable) to ensure a smooth onboarding process.
