# Tech Support Client Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from clients after a tech support session has ended, ensuring that their concerns have been addressed and to gather feedback on the service experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, fill in the required information for each field, which includes:
* Client Name: Enter the client's name as it appears in the support ticket or your own records.
* Discharge Comments: Briefly describe the outcome of the support session, including any action taken or resolution reached.
* Client Satisfaction: Rate the client's overall satisfaction with the support experience, using the provided numbers (e.g., 1-5).
* Client Confirmation: Indicate whether the client has confirmed that their issue has been resolved (Yes/No/Maybe).
* Additional Feedback: Share any extra comments or feedback about the support experience.
* Service Provider Notes: Add any internal notes for the service provider, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): Enter the client's name as it appears in the support ticket or your own records.
* **Discharge Comments** (`discharge_comments`, `text`, required): Briefly describe the outcome of the support session, including any action taken or resolution reached.
* **Client Satisfaction** (`client_satisfaction`, `number`, required): Rate the client's overall satisfaction with the support experience, using the provided numbers (e.g., 1-5).
* **Client Confirmation** (`client_confirmation`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate whether the client has confirmed that their issue has been resolved (Yes/No/Maybe).
* **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, `text`, optional): Share any extra comments or feedback about the support experience.
* **Service Provider Notes** (`service_provider_notes`, `note`, optional): Add any internal notes for the service provider, if necessary.
