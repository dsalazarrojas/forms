# my_voice_profile - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "my_voice_profile" form is designed to collect information about your singing style, experience, and goals. This information will help us understand your unique needs and preferences, enabling us to provide you with personalized services and recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required information in the text fields. These fields are marked as "required".
2. For the select_one fields, choose one of the provided options that best matches your singing style, experience, or goals.
3. For the checkbox field, select "Yes" if you sing in front of an audience, and "No" otherwise.
4. You can add any additional information you think would be helpful for us to know in the "Additional Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **My Voice Profile** (`My Voice Profile`, text, required): This field is for you to describe your current singing style.
* **Singing Lessons** (`Singing Lessons`, select_one, optional): If you have singing lessons, select one of the following options:
	+ Vocal Technique
	+ Voice Type
* **Singing Lessons Frequency** (`Singing Lessons Frequency`, select_one, optional): If you have singing lessons, select how often you attend them:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
* **Singing Style** (`Singing Style`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options to describe your singing style:
	+ Musical Theatre
	+ Classical
	+ Pop
* **Other Singing Style** (`Other Singing Style`, text, optional): If none of the above options match your singing style, type in your own description (e.g., jazz, rock).
* **Vocal Range** (`Vocal Range`, text, optional): Describe your current vocal range.
* **Singing Experience** (`Singing Experience`, select_one, optional): If you have singing experience, select one of the following options:
	+ Beginner
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced
* **Singing in Front of an Audience** (`Singing in Front of an Audience`, checkbox, optional): Select "Yes" if you sing in front of an audience, and "No" otherwise.
* **Singing Goals** (`Singing Goals`, text, optional): What are your singing goals? This could be to improve your technique, perform in front of a large audience, or another goal specific to you.
* **Additional Information** (`Additional Information`, note, optional): If you have any other information that you think would be helpful for us to know, add it here.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer all the questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
- If you're unsure about any of the options, feel free to ask for clarification or guidance.
- This form is designed to be completed in a few minutes, so take your time and don't rush through it.
