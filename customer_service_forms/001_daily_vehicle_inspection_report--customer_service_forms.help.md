# Daily Vehicle Inspection Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the daily inspection of a vehicle, providing a record of its condition and status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Vehicle ID of the vehicle being inspected.
2. Select the type of vehicle being inspected (Company, Private, or Company-Owned).
3. Enter the inspection date.
4. Enter the vehicle model.
5. Enter the vehicle year.
6. Enter the vehicle mileage.
7. For each inspection item, briefly describe any issues or problems found.
8. Rate the vehicle's condition as Good, Fair, or Poor.
9. Enter any additional notes about the vehicle's condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle ID**: Enter the unique identifier assigned to the vehicle. (1)
* **Vehicle Type**: Select the category of the vehicle being inspected (Company, Private, or Company-Owned). (2)
* **Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the vehicle inspection. (3)
* **Vehicle Model**: Enter the make and model of the vehicle. (4)
* **Vehicle Year**: Enter the year of the vehicle. (5)
* **Mileage**: Enter the current mileage of the vehicle. (6)
* **Inspection Item 1**: Briefly describe any issues or problems found with the vehicle's first inspection item. (7)
* **Inspection Item 2**: Briefly describe any issues or problems found with the vehicle's second inspection item. (8)
* **Inspection Item 3**: Briefly describe any issues or problems found with the vehicle's third inspection item. (9)
* **Vehicle Condition**: Rate the overall condition of the vehicle as Good, Fair, or Poor. (10)
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional comments or observations about the vehicle's condition. (11)
