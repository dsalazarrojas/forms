# customer_experience_dashboard_access_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from customers about their experience on our platform. It helps us understand their expectations and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Provide your Email in the Email field.
3. Select one option from the Select One field.
4. Choose multiple options from the Select Multiple field.
5. Input a Date in the Date field.
6. Choose a Time in the Time field.
7. Write a note in the Note field.
8. (Continue filling out the Note fields as needed, noting that each additional Note field is optional.)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: no): Enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: no): Enter your email address that you use for our services.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: no): This field is not currently required, but it's useful to have your contact number in case we need to reach out to you.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, `select`, required: no): Choose one option from the list that best describes your experience.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, `select`, required: no): Choose multiple options from the list that best describe your experience.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: no): Input a date for any upcoming events or appointments.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: no): Input a time for any upcoming events or appointments.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required: no): Write any additional comments or notes you'd like to share with us.
* **Note 2-18** (`note_2`, `note`, required: no): Write any additional comments or notes you'd like to share with us. (Continue with Note 3-18)

## Tips
- Be as accurate as possible when filling out the fields.
- Ensure that you're using the correct format for each field.
- If you're unsure, please ask for assistance.
- This form is used to improve our services and provide better experiences for our customers.
