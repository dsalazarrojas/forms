# Sb19 Supplier Hired In Equipment Insurance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sb19 Supplier Hired In Equipment Insurance Form is used to record information about a supplier who has hired equipment from the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the supplier's name in the **Customer Name** field.
2. Enter the email address of the supplier in the **Email** field.
3. Fill out the **Date In Hire** and **Time In Hire** fields with the date and time the equipment was hired.
4. Enter the **Date Out** and **Time Out** fields with the expected date and time the equipment will be returned.
5. Select the option from the **Hired By** field that best describes the person who hired the equipment.
6. Add any additional notes about the equipment hire or return in the **Note** and **Note 2** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supplier Hired In Equipment Insurance Form**: This is the title of the form.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): The name of the supplier who hired the equipment.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): The email address of the supplier.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): The phone number of the supplier.
* **Date In Hire** (`date_in_hire`, date, required: false): The date the equipment was hired.
* **Time In Hire** (`time_in_hire`, time, required: false): The time the equipment was hired.
* **Date Out** (`date_out`, date, required: false): The date the equipment is expected to be returned.
* **Time Out** (`time_out`, time, required: false): The time the equipment is expected to be returned.
* **Hired By** (`hired_by`, select_one, required: false): The person who hired the equipment. Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Any notes about the equipment hire or return.
* **Note 2** (`note_2`, note, required: false): Any additional notes about the equipment hire or return.
