# Feedback Follow Up Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Feedback Follow Up Request Form is used to request feedback follow up from a customer. It provides a structured way for customers to communicate their feedback and preferences, allowing customer service teams to better understand their needs and tailor their support.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter your User Input, providing any additional information about your feedback.
*   Provide your Contact Information so we can get in touch with you.
*   Indicate if you have previously provided feedback on this topic.
*   Specify the Follow Up Request, describing what you would like to see happen next.
*   Describe the Requested Resolution, providing clear details on what you hope to achieve.
*   Indicate the Best Contact Method, choosing how you would like to be contacted (e.g., email, phone, etc.).
*   Provide the Next Follow Up Date, specifying when you would like to be followed up on again.
*   Select the Follow Up Status, indicating if this issue is currently Active or Inactive.
*   Add any additional Follow Up Notes, providing extra details for our team to reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your feedback.
*   **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Provide your name, email, phone number, or any other contact details so we can get in touch with you.
*   **Previous Feedback** (`previous_feedback`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you have provided feedback on this topic before.
*   **Follow Up Request** (`follow_up_request`, text, optional): Describe what you would like to see happen next.
*   **Requested Resolution** (`requested_resolution`, text, optional): Provide clear details on what you hope to achieve.
*   **Best Contact Method** (`best_contact_method`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g., email, phone, etc.).
*   **Next Follow Up Date** (`next_follow_up_date`, date, optional): Specify when you would like to be followed up on again.
*   **Follow Up Status** (`follow_up_status`, select_one, optional): Indicate if this issue is currently Active or Inactive.
*   **Follow Up Notes** (`follow_up_notes`, note, optional): Add any extra details for our team to reference.
