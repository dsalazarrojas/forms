# Customer Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from customers on their experiences with our company. It helps us understand what we are doing well and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully before filling it out.
2. Fill out the form with your honest feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Feedback**: This field is for you to provide your honest feedback about your experience with our company.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
* **Customer Name**: Please enter your full name for identification purposes.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if we need more information.
	+ Type: email
	+ Required: false
* **Customer Phone**: If you have any phone-related feedback, please enter your phone number or a message with your contact number.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
* **Date of Incident**: Please enter the date you experienced this issue or had a positive experience.
	+ Type: date
	+ Required: false
* **Select Multiple**: Please select all that apply. This field is for multiple choices.
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Required: false
* **Time**: Please enter the time you visited or experienced this issue. We are open from [insert hours of operation].
	+ Type: time
	+ Required: false
* **Note**: Any additional comments or information about your experience. Please be as detailed as possible.
	+ Type: note
	+ Required: false
* **Select One**: Please select one option that best describes your experience.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: false
* **Customer Service Rating**: Rate your experience from 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: false
