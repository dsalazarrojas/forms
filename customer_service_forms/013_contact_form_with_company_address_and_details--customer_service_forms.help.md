# Contact Form With Company Address And Details - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to contact the company with various types of inquiries and requests. It includes fields for company address and contact details, as well as a message field for customers to provide additional information.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your company name and contact person's name.
2. Provide your email address for correspondence.
3. Select the nature of your message (General inquiry, Customer support, Partnership, Media request, or Other).
4. Enter your phone number or other contact information (optional).
5. Enter your company address (street, city, state, postal code).
6. Select your preferred contact method (Email or Phone).
7. Use the map to verify the company location (optional).
8. Enter any additional information to help us respond to your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter your company or organization name.
* **Contact person name** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the person at your company.
* **Email address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for correspondence.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your company phone number or personal contact number.
* **Company address** (`company_address`, text, optional): Enter your company's street address, city, state, and postal code.
* **Inquiry type** (`inquiry_type`, select_one, required): Select the nature of your message (General inquiry, Customer support, Partnership, Media request, or Other).
* **Message** (`message`, text, required): Enter the details of your inquiry or message.
* **Preferred contact method** (`preferred_contact`, select_one, optional): Select how you would like to be contacted (Email or Phone).
* **Map note** (`map_note`, note, optional): Use this field to verify your company's location on the map.
* **Additional information** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Enter any other details that might help us respond to your inquiry.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (Contact person name, Email address, Inquiry type, and Message).
* Use the map to ensure that your company's location is accurate.
* Enter any additional information to help us provide a more accurate response.
* Select your preferred contact method to receive a response from us.
