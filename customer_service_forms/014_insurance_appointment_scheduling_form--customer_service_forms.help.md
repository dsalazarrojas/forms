# Insurance Appointment Scheduling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to schedule insurance appointments with customers. It is used to gather necessary information from customers to ensure that appointments are scheduled correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the insurance status from the options provided.
2. Choose the customer information required for the appointment (e.g., phone number, email).
3. Provide the appointment details, such as date and time.
4. Enter the appointment duration.
5. Enter the customer's phone number and email.
6. Add any additional customer notes.
7. Choose the form submission status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Insurance** (`insurance_appointment_scheduling_form`): Select the insurance status for the appointment.
* **Customer** (`customer_service_form`): Choose the customer information required for the appointment.
* **Scheduling** (`scheduling_form`): Enter the appointment details, including date and time.
* **Appointment Details** (`appointment_details`): Select additional appointment details, such as appointment type or purpose.
* **Date of Appointment** (`date_of_appointment`): Enter the date of the appointment.
* **Time of Appointment** (`time_of_appointment`): Enter the time of the appointment.
* **Appointment Duration** (`appointment_duration`): Enter the duration of the appointment in minutes.
* **Customer Phone Number** (`customer_phone_number`): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer E-mail** (`customer_email`): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`): Enter any additional customer notes or comments.
* **Form Submission** (`form_submission`): Choose the form submission status, indicating whether the form has been submitted.

Note: This form is not intended for submission and is used to gather information only.
