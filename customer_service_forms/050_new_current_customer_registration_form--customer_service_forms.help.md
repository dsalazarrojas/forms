# New Current Customer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register new customers, capturing essential details about them. It serves as an essential tool for our customer service team to gather information about new customers and provide them with a seamless onboarding experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information about the new customer.
2. Fill in the required fields: First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone (if applicable).
3. Provide a valid Email address for the customer.
4. Choose a Category and Sub Category (if applicable) that best describes the customer's needs.
5. Enter any additional details in the Note field (if necessary).
6. Review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter the customer's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter the customer's last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `text`, **required**): Enter a valid email address for the customer.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, **required**): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, **required**): Enter the customer's date of visit.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, **required**): Enter the customer's time of visit.
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose the primary category for the customer (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Sub Category** (`sub_category`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Select one or more sub-categories for the customer (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, **optional**): Enter any additional details or notes about the customer.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your customer service representative for assistance.
* The form is not designed for customers to fill out themselves. It is intended for our internal use only.
