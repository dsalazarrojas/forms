# Laundry Service Online Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is a form for customers to place orders for a laundry service, specifying their preferred pickup and delivery details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions for each page below. Each page has its own set of fields, which are explained in the field-by-field explanations section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1 - Pickup and Delivery
* **Pickup and Delivery** (`pickup_and_delivery`, select_one, optional): Please select your preferred pickup and delivery option. Choose from two available options.
*   *Page 2 - Pickup*
*   * **Pickup** (`pickup`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to pick up your laundry.
*   * **Pickup Time** (`pickup_time`, time, optional): Select the time you would like to pick up your laundry.
*   * **Pickup Time** (`pickup_time`, time, optional): Please select the time you would like to pick up your laundry. Note: This field has the same label as the "Pickup Time" field on Page 3.
*   *Page 3 - Delivery Address*
*   * **Pickup Time** (`pickup_time`, time, optional): Select the time you would like to pick up your laundry. Note: This field has the same label as the "Pickup Time" field on Page 2.
*   * **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, optional): Enter the address where you would like your laundry to be delivered.
