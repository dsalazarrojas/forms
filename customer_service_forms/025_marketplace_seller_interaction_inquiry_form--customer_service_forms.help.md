## Marketplace Seller Interaction Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document and track interactions between the marketplace sellers and customers, and to record the follow-up actions taken by sellers in response to customer inquiries or issues.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Buyer Information**: Please provide a brief description of the customer's inquiry or issue. This field is required.
2. **Seller Information**: Provide a brief description of the interaction or communication with the customer. This field is not required.
3. **Interaction Type**: Choose the type of interaction that occurred (Phone Call, Email, Live Chat, etc.). This field is required.
4. **Date of Interaction**: Enter the date on which the interaction occurred. This field is required and should be in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. **Time of Interaction**: Enter the time of the interaction in 24-hour format (HH:MM AM/PM). This field is required.
6. **Follow Up Status**: Check the box indicating whether the interaction has been followed up on, is scheduled for follow up, or has not been followed up on yet. This field is required.
7. **Additional Information**: Provide any additional comments or details about the interaction. This field is not required.
8. **Assigned Agent**: Enter the name of the agent who will be following up with the customer. This field is required.

* **Buyer Inquiry** (`buyer_inquiry`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the customer's inquiry or issue.
* **Buyer Info** (`buyer_info`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the customer's information.

* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or details about the interaction.
* **Assigned Agent** (`assigned_agent`, text, required): Enter the name of the agent who will be following up with the customer.
