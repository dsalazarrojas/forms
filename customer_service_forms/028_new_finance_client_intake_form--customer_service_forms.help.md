The New Finance Client Intake Form is used to collect information from new financial clients, including their personal details, employment status, financial institution, income range, and any additional notes. This form is designed to be completed by the client to provide essential information for our team to understand their needs and provide personalized services. To ensure the form is completed accurately, please review the following instructions and guidelines carefully.

# New Finance Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Finance Client Intake Form is used to collect essential information from new financial clients, enabling our team to provide tailored services.
## How To Complete This Form
Before starting to fill out the form, please ensure you have the necessary information and documents ready. The form is divided into several sections, each with its corresponding field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`new_finance_client_intake_form_page_1`, text, required: false): Please provide your first name, which will be used to address you in our correspondence.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: true): This is a required field, please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: true): Enter a valid email address that we can use to communicate with you. Please ensure it matches the email address you use for official purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): If you have a preferred contact method, please provide your phone number. This will help us reach you for any important updates.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): If you wish to share your date of birth, please enter it here. This information will be used to tailor our services to your needs.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): If you have a preferred address or location, please provide it here. This will help us serve you better in case of any visits or communications.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, required: true): Please select your current employment status from the options provided:
	+ Employed
	+ Self-Employed
	+ Retired
	+ Unemployed
* **Financial Institution** (`financial_institution`, select_multiple, required: true): Please select all financial institutions that apply to you:
	+ Bank
	+ Credit Union
	+ Investment Company
	+ Other
* **Income Range** (`income_range`, select_multiple, required: true): Please select your income range from the options provided:
	+ Low
	+ Medium
	+ High
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): If you have any additional information you'd like to share with our team, please enter it here. This is a free text field for any comments or questions.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure we can provide you with the right services.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, please don't hesitate to ask our team for clarification.
- Please ensure your email address is valid and accessible to our team.
- For employment status, select the option that best describes your current situation.
- For financial institution, select all options that apply to your financial situation.
- For income range, select the option that best reflects your current income level.
