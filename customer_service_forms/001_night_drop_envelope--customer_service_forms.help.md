# Night Drop Envelope - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Night Drop Envelope form is designed to collect information from customers who have dropped off a vehicle for repair or service during non-business hours. This form helps our customer service team understand the customer's needs and provide timely and efficient service.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the required fields as best as you can.
2.  Provide as much detail as possible in each field.
3.  Submit the form after completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Service Form**: This field is for entering any additional comments or requests from the customer.
*   **Contact Info**: Enter the customer's contact information, including name, phone number, and email address.
*   **Vehicle Info**: Enter the vehicle's information, such as make and model year.
*   **Vehicle Make**: Select 'Yes' if the vehicle is a specific make, such as Toyota or Ford.
*   **Vehicle Model Year**: Enter the year of the vehicle.
*   **Service Requests**: Enter any specific service requests or comments from the customer.
*   **Additional Info**: Enter any additional comments or information from the customer.
*   **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's name.
*   **Customer Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number.
*   **Customer Email**: Enter the customer's email address.
*   **Customer Zip**: Enter the customer's zip code.
*   **Service Type**: Select 'Yes' if the service is for a specific service type, such as oil change or tire rotation. Select 'No' if it's not applicable.
*   **Repair Location**: Select the location where the repair will take place.
*   **Service Date**: Enter the date of the service.
*   **Service Time**: Enter the time of the service.
*   **Customer Notes**: Enter any notes or comments from the customer.
*   **Customer Signature**: The customer's signature.
*   **Technician Signature**: The technician's signature.
*   **Service Provider**: Select 'Yes' if the service is provided by our service provider. Select 'No' if it's not applicable.
*   **Customer ID**: Enter the customer's ID number.
*   **Service Type 2**: Select 'Yes' if the service is for a specific service type 2, such as alignment or brake service. Select 'No' if it's not applicable.
*   **Technician ID**: Enter the technician's ID number.
*   **Submitted By**: Enter the employee's name or ID who submitted the form.
*   **Customer Zip 2**: Enter the customer's zip code 2.
*   **Service Type 3**: Select 'Yes' if the service is for a specific service type 3, such as tune-up or oil flush. Select 'No' if it's not applicable.

## Tips

*   Please provide as much information as possible to help our customer service team provide better service.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our customer service team.
*   This form is for non-business hours drop off, if you need immediate service during business hours, please visit us in person or call us at [phone number].
