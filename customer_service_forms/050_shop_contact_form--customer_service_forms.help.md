# Shop Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shop Contact Form is designed to allow customers to submit their contact information and any questions or comments they may have about the shop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Shop Name in the "Shop Name" field. This will help us identify you and your inquiry.
2. Enter your Shop Email address in the "Shop Email" field. This will allow us to respond to your inquiry via email.
3. Enter your Shop Phone number in the "Shop Phone" field.
4. Provide a brief description of the issue or query in the "form_description" field.
5. Answer "Yes" or "No" in the "shop_subject" field to indicate whether you want to be contacted via email.
6. If you have any additional comments or message, you can enter it in the "shop_message" field.
7. Finally, enter your name in the "Shop Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shop Name (Shop Name, text, required)**: Enter your name so we can identify you and your inquiry.
* **Shop Email (Shop Email, email, required)**: Enter your email address so we can respond to your inquiry.
* **Shop Phone (Shop Phone, text, required)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Brief Description (form_description, text, required)**: Provide a brief description of the issue or query.
* **Want to be contacted via email (shop_subject, select_one, required)**: Answer "Yes" if you want to be contacted via email, "No" otherwise.
* **Optional Message (shop_message, note, required)**: If you have any additional comments or message, enter it here.
* **Send Email (shop_send_email, text, required)**: Not applicable to this form.
