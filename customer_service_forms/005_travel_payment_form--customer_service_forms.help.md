# travel_payment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The travel payment form is used to collect payment information from customers for their travel bookings.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the travel payment form, follow the steps below:

1. Make sure you have all the necessary information ready before proceeding.
2. Enter your **Customer Name** into the corresponding field.
3. Enter your **Customer Email** in the format of `example@company.com`.
4. Enter your **Customer Phone**, in the format of `123-456-7890`.
5. Select the **Travel Date** you want to travel.
6. Enter your **Payment Amount**, in the format of `12.34`.
7. Choose your preferred **Payment Method** from the options provided.
8. In the **Description** field, provide a brief reason for your payment.
9. Finally, click on the **Submit** button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form ID**: A unique identifier for the form, not required but recommended for reference.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Customer Email**: A valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Customer Phone**: Your phone number for any follow-up inquiries.
* **Travel Date**: Select the date of your travel.
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the exact amount you want to pay.
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you want to make the payment (Cash, Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or PayPal).
* **Description**: Briefly describe the reason for your payment.
* **Submit**: Click this button to complete the form and submit your payment information.
* **Customer Name Held**: This field is not required for payment, but it is used internally for record-keeping.
