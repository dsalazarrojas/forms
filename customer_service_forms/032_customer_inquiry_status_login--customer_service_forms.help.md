# customer_inquiry_status_login - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer inquiry status information from customers. It is used internally to keep track of customer interactions and ensure efficient resolution of their inquiries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the "customer_inquiry_status_login_form" text field and enter your desired status update.
2. From the dropdown options, select the most relevant category for your status update (e.g., "Customer Service Representative", "Customer Service Team Leader", or "Manager").
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field for contact purposes.
4. Optionally, if you want to add any additional notes, type them in the "phone" text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer_inquiry_status_login_form** (text, required/optional: no): Enter a brief and accurate status update about the customer inquiry. This could be a resolution, an update, or any relevant information that will help the customer service team understand the status.
* **select_one** (select_one, required/optional: no): Choose the relevant category for your status update from the options provided (Customer Service Representative, Customer Service Team Leader, or Manager). This will help the internal team understand who is responsible for the inquiry's resolution.
* **email** (email, required/optional: no): Enter a valid email address so that the customer service team can contact you with any questions or updates.
* **phone** (text, required/optional: no): This field is optional and can be used for any additional notes or information that you want to share with the internal team.

## Tips
* Please ensure the information provided is accurate and relevant to the customer inquiry.
* If you're unsure about what to enter in any field, please contact the customer service team for guidance.
* Keep your status updates concise and clear for the customer service team's ease of use.
