# 5K Run Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for participants to register for the 5K run event. It captures essential information about the participant, including personal details, medical conditions, and event preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Please review the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose of each section.
2.  Fill out the form accurately and truthfully to ensure a smooth and safe experience for all participants.
3.  If you have any issues or concerns, don't hesitate to contact the event organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant full name** (`participant_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, optional): Select your gender from the options provided (Female, Male, Non-binary, Prefer not to say).
* **Email address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for registration confirmations and event updates.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, `phone`, required): Enter your phone number for day-of-event contact.
* **Emergency contact name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency contact phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `phone`, required): Enter the best phone number to reach your emergency contact.
* **T-shirt size** (`shirt_size`, `select_one`, required): Choose your t-shirt size from the options provided (XS, S, M, L, XL, XXL).
* **Race category** (`race_category`, `select_one`, required): Choose the category that best fits your participation (Competitive (chip-timed), Recreational (untimed), Walker).
* **Medical conditions or allergies** (`medical_conditions`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the medical conditions or allergies that apply to you.
* **Registration fee paid** (`registration_fee_amount`, `decimal`, required): Enter the numeric amount of the registration fee paid in the local currency.
* **I agree to the event waiver and release of liability** (`agree_waiver`, `select_one`, required): Select "True" to agree to the event waiver and release of liability.
* **Additional notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Provide any special requests or notes about your participation, such as accessibility needs.

## Tips
* Please ensure you fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any issues or concerns, don't hesitate to contact the event organizers.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid mistakes.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask the event organizers for clarification.
