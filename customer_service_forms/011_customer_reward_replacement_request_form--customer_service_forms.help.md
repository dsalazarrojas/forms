# Customer Reward Replacement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers who need to replace a reward item due to it being lost, damaged, or stolen. The purpose of this form is to gather details about the reward item and the customer's contact information so that our customer service team can assist with the replacement process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your Customer Name, which is required to identify you as the customer.
2. Enter a brief description of the Reward Item Description to help our customer service team understand the item's details.
3. Select the appropriate Reward Item Status (Lost, Damaged, or Stolen) to inform us of the item's condition.
4. Enter your Customer Email and Customer Phone Number so that we can contact you for further assistance.
5. Enter your Customer Order Number, which is required for our records.
6. Enter the Reason for Replacement, a brief explanation of why the item needs to be replaced.
7. Enter your Customer Reward ID, which is the unique identifier of the reward item.
8. Enter the Customer Reward Value, which is the monetary value of the reward item.
9. If you are being assisted by a Customer Service Representative, please enter their name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your account or identification.
* **Reward Item Description**: A brief description of the reward item, including its type, size, color, and any other identifying features.
* **Reward Item Status**: Select the status of the reward item:
	+ Lost: The item is misplaced or missing.
	+ Damaged: The item is in a damaged or broken state.
	+ Stolen: The item was taken without permission.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your valid email address so that we can contact you for further assistance.
* **Customer Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Customer Order Number**: Enter the order number associated with the reward item.
* **Reason for Replacement**: A brief explanation of why the reward item needs to be replaced.
* **Customer Reward ID**: Enter the unique identifier of the reward item.
* **Customer Reward Value**: Enter the monetary value of the reward item.
* **Customer Service Representative**: If you are being assisted by a customer service representative, please enter their name.
* **Customer Contact Email**: Enter a valid email address for communication.
* **Customer Phone Number**: Enter a valid phone number for communication.
