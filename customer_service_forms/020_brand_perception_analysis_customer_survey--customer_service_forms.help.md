This form was designed to collect data on customer perception of a brand by asking questions about their familiarity, satisfaction, and experience. The form is intended to help businesses gauge the effectiveness of their marketing strategies and identify areas for improvement.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

## Purpose
The Brand Perception Analysis Customer Survey is a tool to gather information about customers' feelings and opinions towards a specific brand. This survey helps brands to assess their public image, identify strengths and weaknesses, and adjust their strategies accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the correct age range from the options provided (Under 18, 18 to 34, 35 to 54, or 55 and over).
2. Choose how familiar you are with the brand, with options ranging from Very familiar to Not familiar.
3. Indicate your overall satisfaction with the brand, with options from Very satisfied to Dissatisfied.
4. Select how you perceive the value you get for the price you pay, with options ranging from High value to Low value.
5. Provide any words that describe the brand's attributes in the text field.
6. Choose how likely you are to return or interact with the brand again, with options ranging from Very likely to Unlikely.
7. If you have any suggestions to improve the brand experience, type them in the text field.
8. Provide any additional comments or feedback about the brand in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent age group** (Under 18, 18 to 34, 35 to 54, 55 and over): This question helps to determine the target audience of the brand.
* **Familiarity with brand** (Very familiar, Somewhat familiar, Not familiar): This question measures how well you know the brand.
* **Overall satisfaction** (Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied): This question assesses your overall feeling towards the brand.
* **Value perception** (High value, Fair value, Low value): This question examines your perception of the value you get for the price you pay.
* **Brand attributes** (text field): This question allows you to describe the brand's attributes or qualities that stand out to you.
* **Likelihood to return** (Very likely, Likely, Unlikely): This question measures how likely you are to return or interact with the brand again.
* **Improvement suggestions** (text field): This question gives you an opportunity to suggest ways to improve the brand experience.
* **Additional comments** (text field): This question allows you to provide any other feedback or thoughts about the brand.

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and based on your actual experience.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.

Note: The form is intended to be completed by individuals who have some familiarity with the brand, but not necessarily customers who have purchased from the brand recently.
