# helpline_status_check_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to check the status of a customer's helpline support issue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the customer's support case and gather any necessary information before filling out the form.
2. Enter the customer's support agent's name in the "Customer Support Agent" field.
3. Select the customer's current status ("Yes", "No", "Other") in the "Status" field.
4. Enter the customer's ID number in the "Customer ID" field.
5. Enter the date of the customer's last contact in the "Date Last Contacted" field.
6. Enter the date of the customer's next contact in the "Date Next Contact" field.
7. Enter the time of the customer's last contact in the "Time Last Contacted" field.
8. Enter the time of the customer's next contact in the "Time Next Contact" field.
9. Enter any additional notes about the customer's support case in the "Note" field.
10. Enter the customer's email address in the "Email" field.
11. Enter the customer's phone number in the "Phone" field.
12. Enter the customer service manager's name in the "Customer Service Manager" field.
13. Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
14. Enter any additional status notes in the "Status Notes" field.
15. Enter the customer's status in the "Status" field.
16. Enter the customer's ID number (if applicable) in the "Customer ID" field.
17. Enter the date of the customer's last contact (if applicable) in the "Date Last Contacted" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Support Agent** (`customer_support_agent`, text, optional): Enter the name of the customer support agent who is handling the customer's support case.
* **Status** (`status`, select multiple, optional): Select the customer's current status ("Yes", "No", "Other").
* **Customer ID** (`customer_id`, number, optional): Enter the customer's ID number.
* **Date Last Contacted** (`date_last_contacted`, date, optional): Enter the date of the customer's last contact.
* **Date Next Contact** (`date_next_contact`, date, optional): Enter the date of the customer's next contact.
* **Time Last Contacted** (`time_last_contacted`, time, optional): Enter the time of the customer's last contact.
* **Time Next Contact** (`time_next_contact`, time, optional): Enter the time of the customer's next contact.
* **Note** (`note`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the customer's support case.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Service Manager** (`customer_service_manager`, text, optional): Enter the name of the customer service manager.
* **Customer Name** (`customer`, text, optional): Enter the customer's name.
* **Status Notes** (`status_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional status notes.
* **Status** (`status_2`, text, optional): Enter the customer's status.
* **Customer ID 2** (`customer_id_2`, number, optional): Enter the customer's ID number (if applicable).
* **Date Last Contacted 2** (`date_last_contacted_2`, date, optional): Enter the date of the customer's last contact (if applicable).
