# Costume Measurement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect measurements of a customer's body for the purpose of generating a custom-made clothing item.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the form accurately:

* Fill in the "Actor Name" field with your name as it appears on your identification card or the name of the character you are representing (for costume purposes).
* Select the "Measurement Taken By" field to specify who took the measurements, so we can verify the accuracy of the measurements.
* Measure and enter your Bust, Waist, Hip Circumference, Sleeve Length, Pant Length, and Shoe Size using your actual measurements in inches. Note that these measurements are required for the generation of your custom-made clothing item.
* If applicable, select your Body Type (Petite, Average, Tall, or Athletic) to help us better understand your body proportions.
* If you are unsure of your body measurements, please enter the sizes of clothing you wear for reference. Note that this will not affect the generation of your custom-made item but will help us better fit your style.
* You can choose multiple clothing sizes to accommodate different body parts or types of clothing. Select the relevant sizes from the list and enter the corresponding numbers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Actor Name** (`actor_name`, required): This is the name you would like to be displayed on your custom-made clothing item.
* **Measurement Taken By** (`measurement_taken_by`, optional): Please specify who took the measurements, so we can verify the accuracy of the measurements.
* **Bust** (`bust`, optional): Enter your bust measurement in inches.
* **Waist** (`waist`, optional): Enter your waist measurement in inches.
* **Hip Circumference** (`hip_circumference`, optional): Enter your hip circumference measurement in inches.
* **Sleeve Length** (`sleeve_length`, optional): Enter your sleeve length measurement in inches.
* **Pant Length** (`pant_length`, optional): Enter your pant length measurement in inches.
* **Shoe Size** (`shoe_size`, optional): Enter your shoe size measurement in inches.
* **Height** (`height`, optional): Enter your height measurement in inches.
* **Weight** (`weight`, optional): Enter your weight measurement in inches. **Please note:** We will not use this information for generating your custom-made clothing item.
* **Body Type** (`body_type`, optional): Select your body type from the dropdown menu to help us better understand your body proportions. This will not affect the generation of your custom-made clothing item.
* **Clothing Size** (`clothing_size`, optional): Select the sizes of clothing you wear for reference. This is not required for the generation of your custom-made item but will help us better fit your style. 

Note: Please ensure that you fill in all the fields accurately to ensure a well-fitting custom-made clothing item. If you are unsure of any measurement, please refer to a reliable source, such as your driver's license or a medical professional.
