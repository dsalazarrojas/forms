# Customer Loyalty Reward Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers to tailor loyalty rewards that best fit their preferences and needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form correctly:

1.  Ensure all required fields are filled. The fields marked as "required" are essential in processing your request.
2.  Choose your loyalty preferences, including your preferred reward and contact method.
3.  Select your reward frequency to indicate how often you want to receive rewards.
4.  Enter your customer information correctly, including date of birth, email, and phone number.
5.  Optionally, you can add notes if you need to provide additional context or information.
6.  Select your preferred contact method to receive notifications.
7.  Finally, review your selections to ensure everything is accurate before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information (customer_information)**: This section is where you'll input your basic customer information, including your date of birth.
* **Loyalty Preferences (loyalty_preferences)**: This is where you can select the types of rewards you are interested in receiving. Please choose all that apply.
* **Preferred Reward (preferred_reward)**: Select your preferred type of reward from the available options.
* **Date of Birth (date_of_birth)**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Birth (time_of_birth)**: Enter your time of birth in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Customer Email (customer_email)**: Enter your correct email address where you can be reached for updates and rewards information.
* **Customer Phone (customer_phone)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for updates and rewards information.
* **Notes (notes)**: This is an optional field where you can provide additional comments or context to help us understand your request better.
* **Email Notification (email_notification)**: If you prefer email as a contact method, check this box to receive updates and rewards information via email.
* **Phone Notification (phone_notification)**: If you prefer phone calls, check this box to receive updates and rewards information over the phone.
* **Preferred Contact Method (preferred_contact_method)**: Choose how you'd like to be contacted for updates and rewards information.
* **Reward Frequency (reward_frequency)**: Select how often you'd like to receive rewards.
