# customer_enquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers who have a specific inquiry or question for the service. It is intended to be used by customers to provide their details, reason for enquiring, and contact information in order to receive support or assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the form with your customer name in the "Customer Enquiry Form" field.
2.  Provide a brief description of yourself in the "Customer Details" field.
3.  Select one or more reasons from the "Enquiry Reason" field as to why you are enquiring.
4.  Enter your email address in the "Customer Contact" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Customer Enquiry Form** (`Customer Enquiry Form`, text, required/optional): A text field where you can enter any information you'd like to provide about your customer enquiry.
*   **Customer Details** (`Customer Details`, text, required/optional): A text field where you can enter a brief description about yourself.
*   **Enquiry Reason** (`Enquiry Reason`, multiple-select, required/optional): Select one or more reasons why you are enquiring.
	+ Option 1: Option 1
	+ Option 2: Option 2
	+ Option 3: Option 3
*   **Customer Contact** (`Customer Contact`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so that we can contact you regarding your enquiry.
