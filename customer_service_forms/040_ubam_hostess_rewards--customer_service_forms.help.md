# UBAM Hostess Rewards - Help Guide
## Purpose
The UBAM Hostess Rewards form is used to allow hostesses to claim rewards for their work. This form is used for selections, updates, and comments related to rewards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of reward you have earned, such as a Gift Card or Other.
2. If you have a current catalog link, please update it.
3. Type in your UBAM E-Commerce Page information if needed.
4. Provide any comments you would like to make about your reward.
5. Choose whether you are claiming the reward for yourself, your spouse, or both.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Hostess Rewards Selections** (`hostess_rewards_form`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the type of reward you have earned, such as Gift Card or Other.
* **Catalog Link** (`hostess_rewards_catalog_link`, text, optional): If you have a current catalog link, please update it to ensure you are receiving the correct rewards.
* **UBAM E-Commerce Page** (`ubam_ecommerce_page`, text, optional): Type in your UBAM E-Commerce Page information if required.
* **Comments** (`form_comments`, note, optional): Provide any comments you would like to make about your reward.
* **Hostess Rewards Recipient** (`hostess_rewardees`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you are claiming the reward for yourself, your spouse, or both.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct type of reward you have earned.
* If you have any comments, please make sure they are clear and concise.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can ask for help from your supervisor or manager.
* Make sure to review your submission before submitting to ensure all fields are filled out correctly.
