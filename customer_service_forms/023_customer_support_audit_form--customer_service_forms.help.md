# Customer Support Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide feedback on a specific customer support interaction. It helps us understand how well our team is doing and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form fields with information about your recent customer support interaction.
2. Rate the quality of the interaction using the "Form Rating" field.
3. Provide a description of the interaction in the "Customer Support Form Description" field.
4. Add any additional comments about the interaction in the "Customer Support Form Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Support Form ID** (`customer_support_form_id`, number, optional): Enter the unique ID of the customer support interaction you are providing feedback on.
* **Form Rating** (`form_rating`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following ratings to describe the quality of the interaction:
	+ Very Poor
	+ Poor
	+ Good
	+ Excellent
* **Form Rating Comment** (`rating_comment`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of why you chose the selected rating.
* **Form Name** (`customer_support_form_name`, text, optional): Enter a name for the form, this will help us to identify the interaction.
* **Service Interaction Date** (`service_interaction_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the customer support interaction.
* **Time Spent** (`time_spent`, number, optional): Enter the amount of time spent on the customer support interaction.
* **Form Description** (`customer_support_form_description`, text, optional): Describe the purpose of the customer support interaction.
* **Form Tags** (`customer_support_form_tags`, text, optional): Enter any relevant tags or keywords that describe the interaction.
* **Form Comments** (`customer_support_form_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the interaction.
