# some_of_my_favorite_things - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your favorite things. It has three fields that you can fill out: a title for your favorite things, a description of your favorite things, and a field to specify what your favorite thing is.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "some of my favorite things" field with a title that describes your favorite things.
2. In the "description" field, provide a brief description of your favorite things.
3. In the "gift" field, specify what your favorite thing is.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **some of my favorite things**: This is the title field where you can give a name to your favorite things. This field is optional and can be blank if you don't have a specific name for them.
* **description**: This field allows you to provide a brief description of your favorite things. It is also an optional field, and you can skip it if you don't have a description in mind.
* **gift**: This field is to specify what your favorite thing is. It is optional and can be blank if you don't have a favorite thing in mind.

## Tips
* Remember to fill out only the fields that are relevant to your answers.
* If you're unsure about the purpose of a field, you can always refer back to this help guide for clarification.
* If you have trouble completing any field, try to ask someone for assistance.
* You can always review your answers before submitting the form to ensure everything is correct and complete.
