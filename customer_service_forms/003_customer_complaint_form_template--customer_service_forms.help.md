# Customer Complaint Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to help customers report and describe their complaints or concerns about our products or services. It is designed to gather essential information from customers to ensure we can assist them promptly and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the **Customer Complaint Form Template** field with your name and/or contact information (if you are not already logged in).
2.  Select all relevant issues you are experiencing from the **Customer Complaint Details** dropdown menu. You can choose multiple options by holding down the Ctrl key (Windows) or Command key (Mac).
3.  In the **Customer Complaint Description** field, type a detailed description of the issue you are experiencing.
4.  In the **Customer Service Representative's Name** field, you can optionally enter the name of your assigned customer service representative if you have already been assigned one. Otherwise, this field can be left blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Complaint Form Template** (`customer_complaint_form_template`, text, required): Enter your name and/or contact information if you are not already logged in.
- **Customer Complaint Details** (`customer_complaint_details`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more of the following issues: 
    * option 1000: Issue 1 - Description to be filled in by the developer
    * option 1001: Issue 2 - Description to be filled in by the developer
  This field is required, as it helps us identify the specific issue you are experiencing.
- **Customer Complaint Description** (`customer_complaint_description`, note, required): Type a detailed description of the issue you are experiencing. Please provide as much information as possible.
- **Customer Service Representative's Name** (`customer_service_representative_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your assigned customer service representative, if you have already been assigned one. If not, you can leave this field blank.

## Tips
- Make sure to select all relevant issue options from the **Customer Complaint Details** dropdown menu to ensure we have a clear understanding of your concerns.
- In the **Customer Complaint Description** field, be as detailed as possible to help us provide the best assistance.
- If you are having difficulty with this form or need further assistance, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
