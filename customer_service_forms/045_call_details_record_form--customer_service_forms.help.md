# Call Details Record Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Call Details Record Form is used to document customer interactions, ensuring that customer service teams have accurate and complete information to analyze and improve their services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date of the call from the calendar.
2. Choose the time of the call.
3. Enter the Call ID or reference number (if applicable).
4. Fill in the Agent Name and Agent ID.
5. Provide the Customer Name and Customer Phone Number.
6. Enter the Customer Email (if applicable).
7. Select the Call Type (Inbound, Outbound, Callback, etc.).
8. Choose the Reason for the Call (Product inquiry, Customer service, etc.).
9. Enter the Call Duration (in minutes).
10. Choose the Call Status (Completed, Partial resolution, Not resolved, etc.).
11. Provide a Call Summary, describing the interaction.
12. If necessary, specify if Follow-up is needed and enter the follow-up Action.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Call Date** (`2`): Date of the call.
* **Call Time** (`3`): Time of the call.
* **Call ID** (`4`): A unique ID for the call or a reference number. 
* **Agent Name** (`6`): Name of the agent who handled the call.
* **Agent ID** (`7`): ID number of the agent who handled the call.
* **Team** (`8`): The team that handles the call.
* **Customer Name** (`10`): Name of the customer.
* **Customer Phone Number** (`11`): Phone number of the customer.
* **Customer Email** (`12`): Email of the customer.
* **Customer ID** (`13`): Unique customer ID.
* **Account Number** (`14`): Account number associated with the customer.
* **Call Type** (`16`): Type of call (Inbound, Outbound, etc.).
* **Reason for Call** (`17`): Reason for the call (Product inquiry, Customer service, etc.).
* **Call Duration (minutes)** (`18`): Duration of the call in minutes.
* **Call Status** (`19`): Status of the call (Completed, Partial resolution, etc.).
* **Call Summary** (`21`): A description of the interaction.
* **Resolution Provided** (`23`): A description of the resolution provided to the customer.
* **Follow-up Needed?** (`24`): If follow-up is required after the call.
* **Follow-up Action** (`25`): Action required for follow-up.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and complete information.
- Consult with your supervisor or team lead if unsure about any field.
- Review your submission for completeness and accuracy before submitting.
