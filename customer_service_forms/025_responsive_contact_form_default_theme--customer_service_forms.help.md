# Responsive Contact Form Default Theme - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for feedback, questions, or comments. It's a generic contact form that can be used by any department or team within the company.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate page to fill out, depending on the purpose of your communication.
2. Fill out the required fields (if any) and provide additional information in the optional fields.
3. Select your preferred options from the dropdown menus (if present).
4. Submit the form to send your message to the company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **contact_form** (`contact_form`, page 1, text, required: false): This is the main contact form section. Enter your message or feedback in this field.
* **name** (`input1`, text, required: false): Enter your name so we can address you personally.
* **email** (`input2`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you back.
* **message** (`input3`, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of your feedback or question.
* **contact_form2** (`form2`, text, required: false): This is another contact form section. Enter your message or feedback here.
* **name2** (`input6`, text, required: false): Enter your name for this second form section.
* **message2** (`input7`, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of your feedback or question for this second form section.
* **contact_form3** (`form3`, text, required: false): Another contact form section.
* **name3** (`input9`, text, required: false): Enter your name for this third form section.
* **select1** (`input10`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the options to provide an answer or preference.
* **select2** (`input11`, select_multiple, required: false): Select multiple options to provide a detailed answer or preference.

## Tips

- Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure your message is clear and effective.
- Use the dropdown menus to select your options carefully.
- If you're unsure about any field, it's okay to ask for clarification or assistance.
