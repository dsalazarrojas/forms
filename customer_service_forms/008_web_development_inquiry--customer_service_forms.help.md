# web_development_inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your web development inquiry, helping our customer support team to better understand your needs and provide efficient support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields, and provide as much information as possible to ensure our team can assist you effectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the purpose of your web development inquiry?** (`purpose_of_inquiry`, `text`, required: false): Please describe the reason for your web development inquiry. This will help our team understand your needs and provide relevant support.
* **What is your current website's URL?** (`current_website_url`, `text`, required: false): If you have an existing website, please provide the URL so our team can review it.
* **What specific web development service do you need?** (`specific_services_needed`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the specific service you require, such as "Yes", "No", or "Other" (if you have a different service in mind).
* **What is the expected launch date for your project?** (`expected_launch_date`, `date`, required: false): If you have a specific deadline for your project, please provide the date.
* **Are you looking to develop a new website or enhance your existing one?** (`new_or_existing`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose between "new" and "existing" to let us know whether you're starting from scratch or upgrading your existing website.
* **What is your website's primary function?** (`primary_function`, `text`, required: false): Describe the main purpose of your website.
* **What is your company's name?** (`company_name`, `text`, required: false): If you're representing a company, please provide your company's name.
* **What is your email?** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **What is your phone number?** (`phone_number`, `number`, required: false): Provide your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Is this a one-time or ongoing service?** (`one_time_or_ongoing`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose between "one-time" and "ongoing" to indicate the nature of the service.
* **Are there any specific technologies you have in mind for your website?** (`specific_technologies`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any specific technologies you've thought of for your website.
* **What is your preferred method of contact?** (`preferred_contact_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how you'd like to be contacted, whether it's by phone, email, or in-person.
* **Do you have any specific design requirements or expectations?** (`design_requirements`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any specific design requirements or expectations you have for your website.
* **Are there any specific features you need for your website?** (`specific_features`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any specific features you require for your website.
* **Do you need assistance with website testing and maintenance?** (`testing_maintenance`, `checkbox`, required: false): Check "Option A" or "Option B" if you need help with testing and maintenance.
* **What is the estimated budget for your project?** (`estimated_budget`, `number`, required: false): Provide your estimated budget for the project.
* **Are there any specific deadlines or milestones for your project?** (`deadlines_milestones`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any specific deadlines or milestones you have for your project.
* **Are there any other services you'd like to inquire about?** (`other_services`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any other services you'd like to inquire about.
