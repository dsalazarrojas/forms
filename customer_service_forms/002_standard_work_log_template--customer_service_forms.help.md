# Standard Work Log Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Standard Work Log Template" form is used to record and track work completed by employees or agents. It provides information about who completed the work, when it was done, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your user input (id: 1) in the designated field.
2. Select the date (id: 2) when the work was completed.
3. Enter the time (id: 3) the work was completed.
4. Add any additional notes (id: 4) about the work completed.
5. Enter the email (id: 5) of the employee or agent who completed the work.
6. Enter the phone number (id: 6) of the employee or agent who completed the work.
7. Specify what work was actually done (id: 7).
8. Select whether the customer information is "Yes" or "No" (id: 8).
9. Enter any comments (id: 9) about the work completed.
10. Enter the date and time (id: 10, id: 11) the form was submitted.
11. Enter your name (id: 12).
12. Enter your title (id: 13).
13. Enter where the form was submitted to (id: 14).
14. Enter your phone number (id: 15).
15. Enter your email address (id: 16).
16. Select whether you have customer information (id: 17).
17. Enter any additional titles (id: 18).
18. Enter your title (id: 19).
19. Enter where the form was submitted to (id: 20).
20. Enter any additional phone numbers (id: 21).
21. Enter the email address of where the form was submitted to (id: 22).
22. Select whether you have additional customer information (id: 24).
23. Enter your email address (id: 25).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input (id: 1)**: Enter any relevant information about the work completed.
* **Date (id: 2)**: Select the date when the work was completed.
* **Time (id: 3)**: Enter the time the work was completed.
* **Notes (id: 4)**: Add any additional notes about the work completed.
* **Email (id: 5)**: Enter the email of the employee or agent who completed the work.
* **Phone (id: 6)**: Enter the phone number of the employee or agent who completed the work.
* **Work Done (id: 7)**: Specify what work was actually done.
* **Customer Info (id: 8)**: Select whether you have customer information.
* **Comments (id: 9)**: Enter any comments about the work completed.
* **Submit Date (id: 10)**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Submit Time (id: 11)**: Enter the time the form was submitted.
* **Submitted By (id: 12)**: Enter your name.
* **Submitter (id: 13)**: Enter your title.
* **Submitted To (id: 14)**: Enter where the form was submitted to.
* **Submitter Phone (id: 15)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Email (id: 16)**: Enter your email address.
* **Work Log (id: 17)**: Select whether you have customer information.
* **Submitted By Title (id: 18)**: Enter any additional titles.
* **Submitter Title (id: 19)**: Enter your title.
* **Submitted To 2 (id: 20)**: Enter where the form was submitted to.
* **Submitted To Phone (id: 21)**: Enter any additional phone numbers.
* **Submitted To Email (id: 22)**: Enter the email address of where the form was submitted to.
* **Customer Info 2 (id: 24)**: Select whether you have additional customer information.
* **Submitted By Email (id: 25)**: Enter your email address.

**Tips**

* Be clear and concise when filling out the form.
* Use the correct format for dates and times.
* Ensure that you have all necessary information before submitting the form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or manager.
