# Home Safety Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Safety Assessment Form is used to gather information about a customer's home safety features. This form is typically used by a customer support or service team to understand the safety status of a customer's home.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Home Safety Form** (Page 1):
	* Fill in the form with relevant information about your home's safety features.
2. **Home Safety Assessor** (Page 2):
	* Enter your name and any relevant details about your safety assessment.
3. **Home Safety Date** (Page 3):
	* Choose the date of the safety assessment.
4. **Home Safety Time** (Page 4):
	* Choose the time of the safety assessment.
5. **Home Safety Notes** (Page 5):
	* Enter any additional notes or comments about your safety assessment.
6. **Home Safety Email** (Page 6):
	* Enter your email address for follow-up communication.
7. **Home Safety Phone** (Page 7):
	* Enter your phone number for follow-up communication.
8. **Home Safety Address** (Page 8):
	* Enter your home's address for reference.
9. **Home Safety City** (Page 9):
	* Enter the city of your home's location.
10. **Home Safety State** (Page 10):
	* Enter the state of your home's location.
11. **Home Safety Zip** (Page 11):
	* Enter the zip code of your home's location.
12. **Home Safety Assessment Status** (Page 12):
	* Choose the assessment status (Passed or Failed).
13. **Assigned User** (Page 13):
	* Enter the name of the user assigned to the assessment.
14. **Assigned Date** (Page 14):
	* Choose the date the assessment was assigned.
