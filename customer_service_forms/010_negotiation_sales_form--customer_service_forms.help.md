# negotiation_sales_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The negotiation sales form is designed to capture sales information from customers, allowing your team to track and document sales negotiations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the product field with the name of the product being sold.
2. Enter the terms of the sale, including any relevant details.
3. Select the sale date from the calendar.
4. Enter the customer's name in the customer_name field.
5. Enter the customer's email address in the customer_email field.
6. Enter the customer's phone number in the customer_phone field.
7. Enter any additional customer message in the customer_message field.
8. Provide a detailed description of the negotiation details.
