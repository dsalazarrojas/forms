# Virtual Assistant Exit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Assistant Exit Form is designed to collect information from businesses that are exiting or discontinuing their virtual assistant service. This form helps us understand the reasons behind the exit and ensures a smooth transition of services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields with your business details and contact information.
2.  Review and ensure the accuracy of the information you provide.
3.  Submit the form to notify us of your business's exit and receive further instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Virtual Assistant Exit Form** (`title`, `text`, **not required**): This is the main title of the form that will be used to identify your business's exit. You can enter a brief description of your decision to exit the virtual assistant service.
*   **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, **not required**): Enter the name of your business.
*   **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `note`, **not required**): Provide any additional contact information or details that you wish to share with us, such as your phone number or street address.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, **not required**): Enter your business email address so we can get in touch with you if needed.

## Tips

*   Ensure you enter accurate and up-to-date information to facilitate a smooth transition.
*   Review your submission before submitting to avoid errors.
*   If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us using the provided contact email.
