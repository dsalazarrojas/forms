# Product Development Request (PDR) - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Development Request (PDR) form is designed to gather essential information from stakeholders for product development purposes. It is a crucial tool for capturing and prioritizing product ideas, ensuring that resources are allocated effectively and projects are completed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter a descriptive name for the product you are requesting.** In the "Product Name" field, provide a clear and concise name for the product you want to develop.
2. **Select the category of the product.** Choose the category that best suits your product in the "Category" field. This will help in organizing and filtering requests in our system.
3. **Describe the product.** In the "Description" field, provide a brief yet detailed description of the product you are requesting.
4. **Specify the type of product.** In the "Product Type" field, specify the type of product you are requesting (e.g., software, hardware, etc.).
5. **Set the priority level.** Choose the priority level for your product request in the "Priority" field. This will help us understand the urgency and importance of your request.
6. **Select the current status.** In the "Status" field, select the current status of your product request (e.g., Approved, In Progress, Not Started).
7. **Specify the assigned tool.** In the "Assigned Tool" field, specify the tool that is currently being used or will be used for this product development project.
8. **Provide the creation date.** In the "Created At" field, provide the date when you created this product request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter a clear and concise name for the product you are requesting.
* **Category** (`category_id`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the category that best suits your product in our system.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief yet detailed description of the product you are requesting.
* **Product Type** (`product_type`, `text`, required: false): Specify the type of product you are requesting (e.g., software, hardware, etc.).
* **Priority** (`priority`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the priority level for your product request in our system.
* **Status** (`status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the current status of your product request in our system.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required: false): Specify the tool that is currently being used or will be used for this product development project.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, `date`, required: false): Provide the date when you created this product request.

## Tips
* Ensure that you provide accurate and descriptive information to help our team make informed decisions about your product development request.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to your product development team for clarification.
* This form is for internal use only and any information provided will be reviewed and prioritized by our team.
