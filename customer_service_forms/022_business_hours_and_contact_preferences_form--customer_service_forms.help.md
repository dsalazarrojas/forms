# Business Hours And Contact Preferences Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect business identity and contact preferences for a given business or organization. It includes operating hours, contact methods, and emergency contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the business's name and title.
2. Select the primary operating time zone and business hours for Monday to Friday.
3. Set the operating hours for Saturday and Sunday.
4. Enter the primary contact person and their preferred contact method.
5. Choose a secondary contact method (optional).
6. Enter the emergency contact phone number (optional).
7. Decide whether to subscribe to the operational updates newsletter.
8. Select the effective date for these preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the business or organization.
* **Primary Operating Time Zone** (text, required): Enter the time zone where the business operates, e.g. EST, GMT+1, PST.
* **Monday - Friday Hours** (text, required): Enter the hours of operation for Monday to Friday in HH:MM AM/PM format.
* **Saturday Hours** (text, required): Enter the hours of operation for Saturday, or "Closed" if not operating.
* **Sunday Hours** (text, required): Enter the hours of operation for Sunday, or "Closed" if not operating.
* **Holiday Schedule Notes** (text, optional): Enter a general policy for handling holidays.
* **Primary Contact Person** (text, required): Enter the name of the primary contact person.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (select_one, required): Choose how you prefer to be contacted, e.g. Email, Phone Call, SMS / Text Message, WhatsApp, or Physical Mail.
* **Secondary Contact Method** (select_one, optional): Choose a secondary contact method in case the primary method is unavailable.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number for emergency contact.
* **Subscribe to Operational Updates Newsletter** (select_one, required): Choose whether to subscribe to the operational updates newsletter.
* **Effective Date** (date, required): Select the date when these preferences become effective.

Tips:
- Use the correct time zone format (e.g. EST, GMT+1, PST).
- Ensure that Monday to Friday hours are complete and accurate.
- If not operating on Saturday or Sunday, enter "Closed".
- Choose a primary and secondary contact method that makes sense for your business.
- If you prefer not to subscribe to the newsletter, select "No, thank you".
- Enter the correct effective date for these preferences.
