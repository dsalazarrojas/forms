# Kaycies_Fragrance_Flower_Party - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kaycies Fragrance Flower Party form is designed to collect information from customers for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the required information:
	* Name
	* Email
	* Phone
2. Select one of the options:
	* Select One
3. (Optional) Fill in the following fields:
	* Note
	* Customer Service Note
	* Customer Service Phone

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (1): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (3): Enter your email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone** (4): Enter your phone number for follow-up communication.
* **Select One** (5): Choose one of the two options provided.
* **Select Multiple** (6): Choose one or more of the two options provided.
* **Date** (7): Enter your preferred date for the event.
* **Time** (8): Enter your preferred time for the event.
* **Note** (9): Enter any additional information or comments about the event.
* **Customer Service Note** (10): Enter any notes or comments for the customer service team.
* **Customer Service Phone** (11): Enter any additional phone number for the customer service team to contact you.

## Tips
* Ensure you fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues, feel free to contact the customer service team.
