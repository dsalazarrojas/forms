# Land Shipping Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Land Shipping Assessment Form is designed to collect information from customers regarding their shipping needs and preferences. This form helps us to assess the customer's shipping requirements and provide a more accurate and efficient shipping experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the customer's information in the **Customer Information** section.
2.  Provide shipping information in the **Shipping Information** section.
3.  Provide assessment information in the **Assessment Information** section.
4.  Add any additional information in the **Additional Information** section.
5.  Choose the preferred delivery option in the **Delivery Information** section.
6.  Select the preferred shipping method in the **Shipping Method** section.
7.  Select the payment terms in the **Payment Terms** section.
8.  Choose the contact preference in the **Contact Preference** section.
9.  Choose the preferred customer service level in the **Customer Service** section.
10.  Enter the estimated and actual delivery dates in the **Estimated Delivery** and **Actual Delivery** sections.
11.  Select the payment status in the **Payment Status** section.
12.  Enter the total cost in the **Total Cost** section.
13.  Enter any additional notes in the **Notes** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Information** (Customer Information, text, required: false): This field is for the customer to provide their basic information such as name and contact details.
*   **Shipping Information** (Customer Shipping, text, required: false): This field is for the customer to provide their shipping information.
*   **Assessment Information** (Assessment Information, text, required: false): This field is for the customer to provide any additional information that may help with their assessment.
*   **Additional Information** (Additional Information, note, required: false): This field is for the customer to add any additional information that may be relevant to their shipment.
*   **Delivery Information** (Delivery Information, select_one, required: false): This field is for the customer to choose their preferred delivery option.
*   **Shipping Method** (Shipping Method, select_multiple, required: false): This field is for the customer to select their preferred shipping method.
*   **Payment Terms** (Payment Terms, select_multiple, required: false): This field is for the customer to select their preferred payment terms.
*   **Contact Preference** (Contact Preference, select_multiple, required: false): This field is for the customer to choose their preferred contact method.
*   **Customer Service** (Customer Service, select_multiple, required: false): This field is for the customer to choose their preferred customer service level.
*   **Estimated Delivery** (Estimated Delivery, date, required: false): This field is for the customer to enter their estimated delivery date.
*   **Actual Delivery** (Actual Delivery, date, required: false): This field is for the customer to enter their actual delivery date.
*   **Payment Status** (Payment Status, select_one, required: false): This field is for the customer to select their payment status.
*   **Total Cost** (Total Cost, number, required: false): This field is for the customer to enter their total cost.
*   **Notes** (Notes, note, required: false): This field is for the customer to add any additional notes.

## Tips
Make sure to fill in all the required information, but be aware that not all fields are marked as required, so you have some flexibility in how you complete the form. However, try to provide as much information as possible to ensure accurate assessment.
