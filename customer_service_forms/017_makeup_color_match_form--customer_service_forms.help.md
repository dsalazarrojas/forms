# Makeup Color Match Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help match a customer's skin and hair color to a makeup color palette that suits their needs. It will guide them through a series of questions to determine their skin type, color preference, hair color, complexion type, face shape, and skin condition to provide personalized recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select your skin type from the list of options on the "Skin Type" field.
2. Choose your preferred color tone from the list of options on the "Color Preference" field.
3. Enter your hair color in the "Hair Color" field.
4. Enter your eye color in the "Eye Color" field.
5. Select your complexion type from the list of options on the "Complexion Type" field.
6. Enter your pore size in the "Pore Size" field.
7. Select your face shape from the list of options on the "Face Shape" field.
8. Check any skin conditions you may have, such as dry, oily, sensitive, or acne-prone skin, on the "Skin Condition" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Skin Type** (`select_multiple`): 
Choose your skin type from the list of options:
* Fair: Light skin with a fair skin tone.
* Light: Light skin with a warm undertone.
* Medium: Medium skin with a balanced skin tone.
* Dark: Dark skin with a cool undertone.
* Olive: Olive skin with a warm undertone.
* Cool: Cool skin with a pink undertone.
* Neutral: Neutral skin with a balanced undertone.

### **Color Preference** (`select_multiple`): 
Choose your preferred color tone:
* Warm: Warm colors such as peach, pink, or red.
* Cool: Cool colors such as blue, purple, or green.
* Neutral: Neutral colors such as beige, brown, or gray.

### **Hair Color** (`text`): 
Enter your natural hair color, such as "brown", "blonde", or "black".

### **Eye Color** (`text`): 
Enter your natural eye color, such as "blue", "green", or "hazel".

### **Complexion Type** (`select_one`): 
Choose your complexion type:
* Fair: Fair complexion.
* Medium: Medium complexion.
* Oval: Oval complexion.

### **Pore Size** (`text`): 
Enter the size of your pores, such as "small", "medium", or "large".

### **Face Shape** (`select_one`): 
Choose your face shape:
* Heart-Shaped: Heart-shaped face.
* Oval: Oval face.
* Square: Square face.

### **Skin Condition** (`select_multiple`): 
Check any skin conditions you may have:
* Dry: Dry skin.
* Oily: Oily skin.
* Sensitive: Sensitive skin.
* Acne-Prone: Acne-prone skin.
* Other: Other skin conditions.
