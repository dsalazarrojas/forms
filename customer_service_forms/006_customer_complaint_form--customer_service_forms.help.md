# Customer Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for customers to submit complaints about issues they are experiencing. Please use this form to provide as much detail as possible about the issue, so our support team can help you effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
- Click on the "Submit" button to send the form to our support team.
- Fill out each field as much as possible, providing as much detail as you can.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_info`, `text`, required: false): This field is for you to provide any general information about your complaint.
* **Complaint Description** (`complaint_description`, `text`, required: false): Please provide a detailed description of the issue you are experiencing.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required: false): If you have any contact details (e.g. phone number, email address) that our support team may need to contact you, please provide them here.
* **Complaint Type** (`complaint_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select all applicable complaint types (e.g. Technical Issue, Non-Technical Issue, Other) that apply to your complaint.
* **Category** (`complaint_category`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the category related to your complaint (e.g. Customer Support, Sales, Other).
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required: false): Please provide a more detailed description of your complaint, if you have any additional information.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required: false): If you have a preferred contact person for your complaint, please provide their name here.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): If you have an email address for the contact person, please provide it here.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): If you have a phone number for the contact person, please provide it here.
* **Customer Feedback** (`customer_feedback`, `text`, required: false): Please let us know if you would like to provide any feedback about your experience with us.
* **Issue Status** (`issue_status`, `text`, required: false): This field is not used for customer complaints, please ignore it.
