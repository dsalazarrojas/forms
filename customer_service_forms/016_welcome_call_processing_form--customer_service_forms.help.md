# Welcome Call Processing Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers during a welcome call, which is an introductory call made to new customers to introduce them to our services. The purpose of this form is to document the details of the call, including the customer's contact information, special requests, and preferences. This form will help us to efficiently process the welcome calls and ensure that our customers receive the best possible support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the first name field.
2. Enter your last name in the last name field.
3. Enter your email address in the email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the phone field.
5. Enter your room number (if applicable).
6. Provide any special requests in the special requests field.
7. Select your preferred language from the language field (you can select multiple languages).
8. Enter the date of your arrival in the date of arrival field.
9. Enter the time of your arrival in the time of arrival field.
10. Provide any special needs or requirements in the special needs field.
11. Add any additional notes about your welcome call in the notes field.
12. If you would like to sync this information with our CRM, select the sync to CRM option.
13. Choose which CRM system you would like to sync with in the crm system field.
14. If you would like to provide a URL for the CRM sync, enter it in the crm sync url field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number here.
* **Room Number** (`room_number`, text, optional): Enter your room number here (if applicable).
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Describe any special requests or requirements you may have.
* **Language** (`language`, select multiple, optional): Select your preferred language(s). You can select multiple languages.
	+ **Spanish**: Select Spanish as your preferred language.
	+ **French**: Select French as your preferred language.
	+ **German**: Select German as your preferred language.
	+ **Italian**: Select Italian as your preferred language.
	+ **Portuguese**: Select Portuguese as your preferred language.
	+ **Russian**: Select Russian as your preferred language.
	+ **Chinese**: Select Chinese as your preferred language.
	+ **Japanese**: Select Japanese as your preferred language.
* **Date of Arrival** (`date_of_arrival`, date, optional): Enter the date of your arrival.
* **Time of Arrival** (`time_of_arrival`, time, optional): Enter the time of your arrival.
* **Special Needs** (`special_needs`, text, optional): Describe any special needs or requirements you may have.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about your welcome call.
* **Sync to CRM** (`crm_sync`, select one, optional): Select if you would like to sync this information with our CRM.
* **Sync to CRM System** (`crm_system`, select one, optional): Choose which CRM system you would like to sync with.
* **CRM Sync URL** (`crm_sync_url`, text, optional): If you would like to provide a URL for the CRM sync, enter it here.

Note: The form fields are optional, but filling out this form will help us to better serve you.
