# Tattoo Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Tattoo Release Form" is a document used to record the agreement between a client and an artist for the release of a tattoo design. This form is used to collect information about the client and the tattoo, ensuring a clear understanding of the release agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Tattoo Release Form**: Enter a brief title for the release form.
2. **Description**: Describe the tattoo you have received.
3. **Client Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
4. **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
5. **Client Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth.
6. **Client Address**: Enter your residential or mailing address.
7. **Artist Tattoo Name**: Enter the name of the artist who performed the tattoo.
8. **Tattoo Description**: Describe the tattoo design you have received.
9. **Consent Date**: Enter the date you agree to the terms and conditions of the release.
10. **Tattoo Design File**: Upload or link to the digital file of the tattoo design.
11. **Client Signature**: Sign this form to confirm you agree to the terms and conditions of the release.
12. **Artist Signature**: Sign this form to confirm you, as the artist, agree to the terms and conditions of the release.
