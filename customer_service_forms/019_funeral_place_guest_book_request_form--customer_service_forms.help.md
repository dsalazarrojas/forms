# Funeral Place Guest Book Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from guests visiting a funeral or memorial service to express their condolences and create a digital guest book for the family of the deceased.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your contact information:
	* **Guest Book Name**: Enter a name of the funeral place or memorial service you visited.
	* **Guest Book Request**: Provide a brief description or explanation of your visit.
2. Enter your personal details:
	* **Email**: Enter your email address so that the family can contact you if needed.
	* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so that the family can contact you if needed.
3. Leave a message:
	* **Message**: Share a message or condolences with the family.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Book Name**: A name of the funeral place or memorial service you visited. This field is not required.
* **Guest Book Request**: A brief description or explanation of your visit. This field is not required.
* **Email**: Your email address so that the family can contact you if needed. This field is not required.
* **Phone**: Your phone number so that the family can contact you if needed. This field is not required.
* **Message**: A message or condolences to share with the family. This field is not required.
* **Name**: Your name. This field is not required.
