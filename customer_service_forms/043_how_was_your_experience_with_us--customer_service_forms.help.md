# How Was Your Experience With Us? - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers on their experience with our organization. It allows customers to rate their satisfaction and provide comments, contact details, and any additional feedback they might have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your rating of our service by choosing one or more options from the "Rate Your Experience" section.
2. In the "What was your rating" section, select one option that best represents your experience.
3. Provide any comments or feedback you might have in the "What are your comments" section.
4. If you want to be contacted, please provide your contact details in the "Do you want to be contacted" and "Email" sections.
5. You can also provide your name and phone number in the "Name" and "Phone" sections, respectively.
6. Finally, if you have any additional feedback, please type it in the "Any Additional Feedback" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Rate Your Experience** (rate_experience, select_multiple, required: false): This section allows you to rate your experience with our organization. Please select all the options that apply to you.
* **What was your rating** (rating, select_one, required: false): Choose one option that best represents your experience.
* **What are your comments** (comments, note, required: false): Please provide any comments or feedback you might have about your experience.
* **Do you want to be contacted** (contact_details, text, required: false): If you want to be contacted, please provide your contact details, such as your address, phone number, and email.
* **Your Email** (email, email, required: false): Please provide your email address if you want us to contact you.
* **Any Additional Feedback** (additional_feedback, text, required: false): This is an optional field where you can provide any additional feedback you might have.
* **Name** (name, text, required: false): Please provide your name for us to associate it with your feedback.
* **Name (Held)** (name_held, text, required: false): This field is not used for public contact, but rather for our internal use.
* **Phone** (phone, text, required: false): Please provide your phone number if you want us to contact you.
* **Email (Held)** (email_held, email, required: false): This field is not used for public contact, but rather for our internal use.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as "required" in the form. You can choose to fill in as many or as few fields as you like, depending on your preferences.
