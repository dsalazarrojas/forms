# Marketplace Billing Support Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketplace Billing Support Contact Form is a tool for customers to submit their billing-related inquiries, issues, or general questions to the marketplace's support team. This form is designed to gather essential information to provide timely and effective assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and detailed information.
2. Select the most relevant contact method (Email, Phone, or Other) and provide your contact details.
3. Choose the type of issue you are facing (General Inquiry, Billing Issue, or Other).
4. Select the priority level of your issue (Low, Medium, or High).
5. Specify the status of your issue (Open, Closed, or In Progress).
6. Add any additional information that might help the support team understand your concern.
7. Click the "Submit" button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, required): This is the first section of the form where you can enter your contact information.
* **Billing Inquiries** (Billing Inquiries, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of your billing-related issue or inquiry.
* **Marketplace Information** (Marketplace Information, number, optional): If available, enter the relevant marketplace information related to your issue.
* **Marketplace ID** (Marketplace ID, number, optional): If available, enter your marketplace ID related to your issue.
* **Contact Method** (Contact Method, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, or Other).
* **Contact Details** (Contact Details, text, optional): Provide your contact details, such as email or phone number.
* **Issue Description** (Issue Description, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of issue you are facing (General Inquiry, Billing Issue, or Other).
* **Issue Category** (Issue Category, select_one, optional): Choose the category of your issue (Billing, General, or Other).
* **Issue Priority** (Issue Priority, select_one, optional): Select the priority level of your issue (Low, Medium, or High).
* **Issue Status** (Issue Status, select_one, optional): Specify the status of your issue (Open, Closed, or In Progress).
* **Additional Info** (Additional Info, text, optional): Add any additional information that might help the support team understand your concern.
* **Submit** (Submit, text, required): Click this button to send the form to the support team.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure that the support team can assist you efficiently.
* Be as detailed as possible in the "Billing Inquiries" field to help the support team understand your issue.
* If you need assistance with a specific marketplace, enter the relevant marketplace information.
* If you prefer to be contacted through a method other than Email or Phone, select "Other" in the "Contact Method" field.
* Select the most relevant issue category and priority level to help the support team understand the urgency of your issue.
