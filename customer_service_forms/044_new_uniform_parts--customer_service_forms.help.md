# New Uniform Parts - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about new uniform parts from customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the following information:

- **New Uniform Parts**: This is the main subject of the form, where you can describe the new uniform parts being reported.
- **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's name who is reporting the new uniform parts.
- **Contact Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number for further contact.
- **Contact Email**: Enter the customer's email address for further communication.
- **Contact Date**: Enter the date you contacted the customer regarding the new uniform parts.
- **Part Type**: Select 'Yes' if the part is new or 'No' if it's not.
- **Serial Number**: Indicate whether the part has a serial number ('Yes' or 'No').
- **Part Number**: Enter the part's number.
- **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the part.
- **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the new uniform parts.
