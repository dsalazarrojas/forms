# Homeschooled Customer Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Homeschooled Customer Feedback Form is designed to collect information from parents or guardians about their experience with our homeschooling program. This form will help us understand how we can improve our services to better support you and your family.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the necessary information.
2. Review each field carefully and answer accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Homeschooled child name
* **Homeschooled child name**: Please enter the name of the child for whom you are providing feedback. This will help us understand who is involved in the homeschooling process.

### Homeschooled child grade level
* **Homeschooled child grade level**: Please select the current grade level of the child. This will help us understand their current educational level.

### Homeschooled program start date
* **Homeschooled program start date**: Please enter the date when your child started the homeschooling program. This is helpful in tracking the program's progression.

### Homeschooled program end date
* **Homeschooled program end date**: Please enter the date when your child's homeschooling program ended or is expected to end. This is helpful in tracking the program's duration.

### Homeschooled parent name
* **Homeschooled parent name**: Please enter your name or the name of the parent/guardian providing the feedback. This is required for communication purposes.

### Homeschooled program name
* **Homeschooled program name**: Please enter the name of the homeschooling program or service you are using. This helps us understand the specific program being evaluated.

### Contact method
* **Contact method**: Please select how you would like to be contacted regarding this feedback. Options include:
	+ Contact email
	+ Contact phone
	+ Contact mail

### Contact phone type
* **Contact phone type**: Please select the type of phone number you would like to be contacted on. Options include:
	+ Landline
	+ Mobile
	+ Other (please specify)

### Contact email
* **Contact email**: Please enter the email address where you would like to be contacted. This is only required if you selected "Contact email" in the previous field.

### Contact notes
* **Contact notes**: Please add any additional notes or comments you would like to share with us regarding contact preferences or other feedback.

### Homeschooled parent signature
* **Homeschooled parent signature**: Please enter your signature, as this confirms that the feedback is from the parent/guardian.

### Homeschooled district signature
* **Homeschooled district signature**: Please enter your district or school signature (if applicable). This is optional but helps us understand the district's perspective.

This guide should help you complete the Homeschooled Customer Feedback Form accurately and efficiently. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
