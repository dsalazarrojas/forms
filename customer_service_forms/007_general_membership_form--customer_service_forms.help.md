# General Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The General Membership Form is used to collect membership details from customers, including payment information and membership status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and fill out the required fields.
2. Provide accurate and detailed information about your membership status and payments.
3. Ensure that you fill out all relevant fields, but do not worry about optional fields unless you have specific information to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Membership Details (1)**: This section asks for a brief overview of your membership status.
* **Payments (2)**: Select the type of payment method you use for your membership (Yes/No).
* **Name (3)**: Enter your name as it appears on official documents.
* **Membership Type (4)**: Choose the type of membership you hold (Member/Sponsor).
* **Contact Email (5)**: Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone (6)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Notes (7)**: Provide any additional information about your contact preferences.
* **Address Line 1 (8)**: Enter your street address.
* **Address Line 2 (9)**: Enter your apartment or suite number (if applicable).
* **City (10)**: Enter your city or town.
* **State (11)**: Enter your state or province.
* **Zip (12)**: Enter your postal code.
* **Payment Method (13)**: Select the payment method you use (Credit Card/PayPal/Check).
* **Payment Frequency (14)**: Choose how often you make payments (One-time/Monthly).
* **Payment Amount (15)**: Enter the amount you pay for your membership.
* **Member Since (16)**: Enter the date you became a member.
* **Expiration Date (17)**: Enter the date your membership expires.
* **Membership Status (18)**: Choose your current membership status (Active/Inactive/Pending).
* **Renewal Date (19)**: Enter the date your membership is set to renew.
* **Payment Terms (20)**: Enter any payment terms or conditions.
* **Payment Terms Agreement (21)**: Enter your agreement or signature to the payment terms.
* **Payment Terms Date (22)**: Enter the date you agreed to the payment terms.
* **Other (Membership Type) (23)**: If your membership type is not listed, enter a description of your type.
* **Membership Notes (24)**: Provide any additional information about your membership status.
* **Other (Membership Status) (25)**: If your membership status is not listed, enter a description of your status.
