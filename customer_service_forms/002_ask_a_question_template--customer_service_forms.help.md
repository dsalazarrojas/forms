# Ask A Question Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers who have a question or inquiry about our products, services, or general information.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email Address** to identify yourself as the customer submitting the question.
2. Choose the **Subject of Your Question** to provide a brief context for your inquiry.
3. Select the **Type of Inquiry** (e.g., General Information, Product Support, etc.) to help our team understand your request.
4. Indicate the **Priority Level** of your question (Low, Medium, High, or Urgent) to expedite our response.
5. Provide a **Detailed Description of Your Question** to ensure our team understands your issue accurately.
6. If applicable, enter your **Customer Account Number** and **Order or Reference Number** for our team to reference.
7. Choose your **Preferred Contact Method** (Email, Phone, Text Message, or No Preference) to determine the best way for us to respond to you.
8. Select a **Best Time to Contact You** (if applicable) to ensure our team reaches you at the most convenient time.
9. If necessary, indicate if you need to **Attach a File** to support your question.
10. Choose the **Product or Service Category** (if relevant) to help our team understand the context of your inquiry.
11. Identify yourself as a **Customer Type** (New, Existing, or Prospective) to tailor our response to your situation.
12. Consent to our **Contact Regarding This Inquiry** (Yes or No) to allow us to communicate with you about this issue.
13. Optionally, choose to receive our **Newsletter** to stay updated on news and offers.
14. Finally, confirm the **Date of Submission** to record the date of your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`customer_first_name`, Text, Required): Enter your first name for identification.
* **Last Name (`customer_last_name`, Text, Required): Enter your last name for identification.
* **Email Address (email_address, Email, Required): Enter the email address we will use to respond to you.
* **Phone Number (phone_number, Text, Optional): If you'd like to provide an optional contact number.
* **Subject of Your Question (subject, Text, Required): Briefly describe the subject of your question.
* **Type of Inquiry (inquiry_type, Select One, Required): Choose the type of your inquiry (e.g., General Information, Product Support, etc.).
* **Priority Level (priority_level, Select One, Required): Select the urgency level of your question (Low, Medium, High, or Urgent).
* **Detailed Description of Your Question (question_details, Text, Required): Provide a detailed explanation of your question for our team to understand.
* **Customer Account Number (customer_account_number, Text, Optional): If you have a customer account, provide the number for our reference.
* **Order or Reference Number (order_reference, Text, Optional): If relevant to your question, enter the order or reference number.
* **Preferred Contact Method (preferred_contact_method, Select One, Required): Choose how you'd like to be contacted (Email, Phone, Text Message, or No Preference).
* **Best Time to Contact You (best_contact_time, Select One, Optional): Select a time convenient for you to be contacted.
* **Do you need to attach a file? (attachments_needed, Select One, Optional): Indicate if you need to attach a file to support your question.
* **Product or Service Category (product_category, Select One, Optional): Select the category of the product or service related to your inquiry.
* **Customer Type (customer_type, Select One, Required): Identify yourself as a new, existing, or prospective customer.
* **May we contact you regarding this inquiry? (consent_communication, Select One, Required): Consent to our communication about this inquiry.
* **Would you like to receive our newsletter? (newsletter_signup, Select One, Optional): Choose to receive our newsletter for updates and promotions.
* **Date of Submission (question_submission_date, Date, Required): Confirm the date of submission with the current date.
