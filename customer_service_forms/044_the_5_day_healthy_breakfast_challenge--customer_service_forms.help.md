# the_5_day_healthy_breakfast_challenge - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 5-day healthy breakfast challenge is a guide to help you set and achieve your breakfast goals. This form will aid you in setting a realistic and achievable breakfast plan for the next 5 days, providing you with a clear understanding of what to expect and how to track your progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions and prompts on each page to set your breakfast goals, track your progress, and provide information on your morning motivation and habits.
2. Fill out all the required fields, indicated by a red asterisk (*).
3. Use the options provided for the "breakfast_goals" field to select the motivation behind your healthy breakfast challenge.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Day** (1, number, required/optional): Please enter a number indicating the day of your challenge.
* **Second Day** (2, text, required/optional): Enter your breakfast preferences or goals for the second day.
* **Third Day** (3, text, required/optional): Describe your breakfast goals and preferences for the third day.
* **Fourth Day** (4, number, required/optional): Enter the day number of your challenge.
* **Fifth Day** (5, text, required/optional): Enter your breakfast goals and preferences for the fifth day.
* **Breakfast Goals** (6, select_one, required): Select the motivation behind your healthy breakfast challenge from the provided options.
	+ Breakfast Goals: Choose this option if your goal is to simply improve your breakfast habits.
	+ Healthy Eating: Choose this option if you want to develop a healthier diet.
	+ Weight Loss: Choose this option if your aim is to lose weight.
	+ Muscle Gain: Choose this option if you wish to gain muscle mass.
	+ Improved Sleep: Choose this option if better sleep is your primary concern.
* **Morning Motivation** (7, time, required/optional): Enter your morning motivation in 24-hour format (e.g., 06:00, 07:00, 08:00, etc.).
* **Breakfast Time** (8, time, required/optional): Enter the time when you usually have breakfast in 24-hour format (e.g., 06:00, 07:00, 08:00, etc.).
* **Morning Routine** (9, text, required/optional): Describe your morning routine or daily activities.
* **Morning Habits** (10, text, required/optional): List your healthy morning habits, such as exercise, meditation, or reading.

## Tips
- Please answer truthfully and accurately to get the most out of this challenge.
- You can return to this form after completing the 5-day challenge to track your progress and make further adjustments.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask for help.
