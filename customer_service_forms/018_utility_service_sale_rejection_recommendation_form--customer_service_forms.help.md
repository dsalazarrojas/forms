# Utility Service Sale Rejection Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and formalize the rejection of a sale, providing a clear and concise explanation of the reason for rejection and next steps. The form is designed to facilitate communication between Sales and Quality teams, ensuring that all relevant information is documented and shared.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the Sales Leader involved in the sale.
2. Provide a clear and concise explanation of the reason for rejection, using one of the provided options or specifying "Other (please specify)".
3. Outline the next steps to be taken, including any further actions or follow-ups required.
4. Enter the email address and phone number of the Sales Person responsible for the sale.
5. Enter the date and time of the sale.
6. Select the type of service being sold and its current status.
7. Enter the current sales status.
8. Enter the name of the Quality Person responsible for the sale.
9. Enter the date and time of the quality check.
10. Enter the current quality status.
11. Enter any additional notes about the sale.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Sales Leader** (`sales_leader`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the Sales Leader involved in the sale.
- **Reason for Rejection** (`reason_for_rejection`, text, required): Provide a clear and concise explanation of the reason for rejection, using one of the provided options or specifying "Other (please specify)".
- **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, required): Outline the next steps to be taken, including any further actions or follow-ups required.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the Sales Person responsible for the sale.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the Sales Person responsible for the sale.
- **Sales Person** (`sales_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the Sales Person responsible for the sale.
- **Sales Date** (`sales_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the sale.
- **Sales Time** (`sales_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the sale.
- **Service Type** (`service_type`, text, required): Select the type of service being sold.
- **Service Status** (`service_status`, text, required): Select the current status of the service.
- **Sales Status** (`sales_status`, text, required): Select the current sales status.
- **Quality Person** (`quality_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the Quality Person responsible for the sale.
- **Quality Date** (`quality_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the quality check.
- **Quality Time** (`quality_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the quality check.
- **Quality Status** (`quality_status`, text, required): Select the current quality status.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* If the reason for rejection is "Other (please specify)", please provide a clear and concise explanation.
* If there are any additional notes about the sale, please include them in the "Sales Notes" or "Quality Notes" fields.
