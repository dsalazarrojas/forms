# Client Consultation Skin By Janet Lynne - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Consultation Skin By Janet Lynne form is designed to gather information about your skin profile, concerns, and history to provide personalized recommendations for your skin health.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with your information carefully and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please ask a representative for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): This field is used to collect your full name for reference during the consultation.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): This field is used to collect your date of birth, which may be used to identify any age-related skin concerns.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): This field is used to collect your email address, which may be used to contact you with further information or recommendations.
* **Skin Profile** (`section_skin_profile`, note, optional): This field is a note section where you can describe any skin profile-related information or concerns.
* **How would you describe your skin type?** (`skin_type`, select_one, required): This field is used to collect information about your skin type, which is used to determine your skin's natural characteristics.
	+ Dry: Your skin is characterized by a lack of natural oils, making it prone to dryness and flakiness.
	+ Oily: Your skin is characterized by an overproduction of natural oils, making it prone to acne and greasiness.
	+ Combination: Your skin is a mix of dry and oily characteristics.
	+ Sensitive: Your skin is sensitive to products or environmental factors.
	+ Normal: Your skin is average in terms of natural oils and moisture.
* **What is your primary skin concern?** (`skin_concern`, select_one, required): This field is used to collect information about your primary skin concern, which will guide the consultation's recommendations.
	+ Acne: Acne-related issues such as breakouts, blackheads, or whiteheads.
	+ Aging: Fine lines, wrinkles, or age-related skin concerns.
	+ Pigmentation: Skin discoloration, dark spots, or uneven skin tone.
	+ Rosacea: A skin condition characterized by redness, acne, or sensitivity.
	+ Texture: Skin texture issues such as roughness, smoothness, or firmness.
	+ Dehydration: Dryness, flakiness, or a lack of natural moisture.
* **Describe your current skincare routine** (`current_routine`, text, optional): This field is used to collect information about your current skincare routine, which will help the consultant understand your current skin care habits.
* **List any known allergies** (`known_allergies`, text, required): This field is used to collect information about any known allergies, such as nuts, citrus, or shellfish.
* **Are you currently using any prescription skin medications?** (`skin_medications`, text, optional): This field is used to collect information about any prescription skin medications you may be taking.
* **How frequent is your sun exposure?** (`sun_exposure`, select_one, optional):
	+ Low: You spend minimal time outdoors or use sunscreen regularly.
	+ Moderate: You spend some time outdoors, but use sunscreen occasionally.
	+ High: You spend most of your time outdoors, without using sunscreen regularly.
* **-- Treatment History --** (`section_history`, note, optional): This field is a note section where you can describe any previous skin treatments, such as facials, chemical peels, or microdermabrasion.
* **Date of last facial treatment** (`last_facial_date`, date, optional): This field is used to collect the date of your last facial treatment.
* **Have you ever had a chemical peel or microdermabrasion?** (`previous_treatments`, select_one, optional):
	+ 'True': You have had a chemical peel or microdermabrasion before.
	+ 'False': You have not had a chemical peel or microdermabrasion before.
* **I consent to the recommended skin treatment** (`treatment_consent`, select_one, required):
	+ 'True': You consent to the recommended skin treatment.
	+ I consent: You consent to the recommended skin treatment.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
- If you are unsure about any question, please ask a representative for assistance.
- The information collected will be used to provide personalized recommendations for your skin health.
