# Customer Gift Assistance Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Gift Assistance Contact Form is a tool for customers to inquire about gift options and provide information about their gift preferences. It is designed to help the customer service team understand their gift needs and preferences to provide better assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. On the first page, fill out your contact information:
	* Enter your name in the "Gift Inquirer Name" field.
	* Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
	* If you would like to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number in the "Telephone" field.
2. On the second page, select your gift preferences:
	* Choose the type of gift you are interested in (Gift Card, Product, or Service).
	* Select the type of gift card you are interested in (Visa, Mastercard, Discover, Amex).
	* Choose the merchant where you would like to purchase the gift from (Amazon, eBay, or Other).
3. Add any additional notes or comments about your gift preferences in the "Note" field.
4. Finally, submit the form by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Gift Inquirer Name** (`customer_gift_inquiry_name`, text, optional): Your name as the person inquiring about the gift.
	* Please enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
- **Email** (`gift_inquiry_email`, email, optional): Your email address for contact.
	* Please enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
- **Telephone** (`gift_inquiry_telephone`, text, optional): Your phone number for contact.
	* If you would like to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number here.
- **Gift Type** (`gift_inquiry_type`, select_one, required): Type of gift you are interested in (Gift Card, Product, or Service).
	* Please select the type of gift you are interested in.
- **Gift Card Type** (`gift_inquiry_card_type`, select_multiple, required): Type of gift card you are interested in (Visa, Mastercard, Discover, Amex).
	* Please select all types of gift cards you are interested in.
- **Merchant** (`gift_inquiry_merchant`, select_one, required): Merchant where you would like to purchase the gift from (Amazon, eBay, or Other).
	* Please select the merchant where you would like to purchase the gift from.
- **Note** (`gift_inquiry_note`, note, optional): Any additional notes or comments about your gift preferences.
	* Please use this field to add any additional information that may help us assist you better.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate processing of your inquiry.
- If you have any questions or need further assistance, please contact our customer service team.
