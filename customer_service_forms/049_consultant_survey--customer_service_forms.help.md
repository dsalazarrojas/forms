# Consultant Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Consultant Survey is designed to gather essential information from clients or potential clients about their consultation needs and preferences. This form helps the consultancy team to better understand your requirements and prepare for a productive consultation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name, email, and phone number to identify yourself as a client or potential client.
2.  Select the primary topic of interest for the consultation.
3.  Provide a brief summary of the primary challenge or problem you are facing.
4.  Indicate your single most important goal for this consultation.
5.  Choose your preferred meeting format (e.g., video call, phone call, in-person).
6.  Select a preferred date and time for the consultation.
7.  Estimate the project budget range and urgency.
8.  Finally, provide any additional information or details that you would like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Full Name** (`customer_full_name_ident`, text, required): Please enter your full name as a client or potential client.
*   **Customer Contact Email** (`customer_email_ident`, email, required): Enter your contact email address where you can be reached.
*   **Customer Phone Number** (`customer_phone_ident`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
*   **Organization Name (If applicable)** (`organization_name_ident`, text, optional): If you are a representative of an organization, please enter the organization name.
*   **Primary Topic of Interest for Consultation** (`primary_consultation_topic`, select_one, required): Select the most relevant category for your consultation needs.
*   **Brief Summary of the Primary Challenge or Problem** (`primary_challenge_desc`, text, required): Provide a brief summary of the primary challenge or problem you are facing.
*   **What is your single most important goal for this consultation?** (`desired_consultation_outcome`, text, required): Clearly state your single most important goal for this consultation.
*   **Preferred Meeting Format** (`meeting_format_pref`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred meeting format (e.g., video call, phone call, in-person).
*   **Preferred Date for Consultation** (`preferred_consultation_date`, date, required): Select a preferred date for the consultation.
*   **Preferred Time Slot** (`preferred_consultation_time`, time, required): Choose a preferred time slot for the consultation.
*   **Estimated Project Budget Range ($)** (`project_budget_range_ident`, select_one, required): Estimate the project budget range.
*   **How soon do you plan to start?** (`project_urgency_ident`, select_one, required): Indicate how soon you plan to start the project.
*   **How did you hear about our consultancy?** (`referral_source_ident`, select_one, optional): If you can, provide information about how you heard about our consultancy.
*   **Any additional details you'd like to share** (`additional_comments_desc`, text, optional): Provide any additional information or details that you would like to share.
*   **Date of Consultation Request** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are submitting this consultation request.
*   **Authorized Representative Name** (`authorized_representative_name_label`, text, required): Please enter the authorized representative's name.
*   **Authorized Representative Title** (`authorized_representative_title_label`, text, required): Enter the authorized representative's title.
