# Customer Experience Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Experience Report Form is a general feedback or report form designed to collect information about your experience with our company, product, or service. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form to help us understand your concerns, suggestions, or simply share your experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the **general_info** field (page 1) with a brief description of your experience. This field is not required, so feel free to provide as much or as little information as you'd like.
2. On the same page, select your rating for your experience by choosing one of the options: 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5.
3. If you'd like to provide more details about your experience, use the **experience_details** field (page 1).
4. For additional contact information, fill out the **contact_info**, **phone**, and **email** fields (pages 1-2).
5. If you have any comments or additional feedback, use the **comments** field (page 2).
6. Enter your **date** of the experience (page 2).
7. Choose a time for the experience using the **time** field (page 2).
8. Finally, click the **submit** button to send us your report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **general_info**: A brief description of your experience with our company, product, or service.
* **experience_details**: More information about your experience.
* **rating**: Select one of the following options: 1 (very dissatisfied), 2, 3 (neutral), 4 (very satisfied), or 5 (extremely satisfied).
* **comments**: Any additional thoughts, suggestions, or feedback you'd like to share.
* **contact_info**: Your name, address, or any other contact information you'd like to provide.
* **phone**: Your phone number for follow-up or additional contact.
* **email**: Your email address for follow-up or additional contact.
* **date**: The date of your experience.
* **time**: The time of your experience.
* **submit**: Click to send us your report.
* **back**: Click to return to the previous page. 

Please note that some fields are not required, so feel free to fill out as many or as few fields as you'd like. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
