# Christmas Lighting Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers to request Christmas lighting services, including their contact information, property details, design preferences, and scheduling requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form in the following order:
   1. Enter your contact information (Name, Email Address, Phone Number)
   2. Provide your property information (Service Address, City, Postal Code)
   3. Select your property type and approximate building height
   4. Choose your desired light types and color scheme
   5. Select your preferred style and budget range
   6. Choose your desired installation and removal dates
   7. Specify your maintenance requirements
   8. Share any previous experience with our service
   9. Provide any inspiration photos or special requests
   10. Let us know how you heard about us
   10. Choose your consultation preference

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your first and last name to enable us to contact you.
* **Company Name** (optional): If you are a business representative, enter your company name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for quote and communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for consultation scheduling.
* **Service Address**: Enter the address where lights will be installed.
* **City**: Enter the city where the service address is located.
* **Postal Code**: Enter the postal code where the service address is located.
* **Property Type**: Choose the type of property where lights will be installed.
* **Building Height**: Choose the approximate building height.
* **Desired Light Types**: Select the types of lights you want installed.
* **Color Scheme Preference**: Choose your preferred color scheme.
* **Style Preference**: Choose the overall aesthetic style you prefer.
* **Budget Range**: Choose your estimated budget for the installation.
* **Desired Installation Start**: Enter the date when you would like us to start installation.
* **Desired Removal Date**: Enter the date when you would like us to remove the lights.
* **Maintenance Needed**: Specify your maintenance requirements.
* **Previous Professional Experience**: Let us know if you have used our service before.
* **Inspiration Photos**: Share any reference images you have.
* **Special Requests**: Specify any specific requirements you have.
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: Let us know how you heard about us.
* **Consultation Preference**: Choose how you would like to consult with us.

## Tips

* Be as accurate as possible when filling out the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* This form is designed to provide us with the necessary information to provide you with the best possible service.
* Please ensure that you fill out this form for the right purpose and not for any other reason.
* If you have already filled out this form previously, please disregard this form and do not fill it out again.
