# Customer Complaint Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to report their complaints and provide feedback to the service provider. It helps the customer service team to identify and address issues efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the fields as required (if any). The form has optional fields that allow you to provide more information, but you can leave them blank if you don't have the relevant details.
2. Choose the category that best describes your complaint (Customer Service, Product, Order, or Other).
3. Enter the date and time of the incident (or leave blank if not applicable).
4. Provide a detailed description of your complaint.
5. Enter your email address (if you want to be contacted for further clarification or updates).
6. Enter your phone number (if you want to be contacted by phone).
7. If you have attachments (e.g., photos, documents) that support your complaint, select 'Yes' to attach them.
8. Click the 'Submit' button to send your complaint report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **complaint_date** (Optional): Enter the date of the incident, if known.
* **complaint_time** (Optional): Enter the time of the incident, if known.
* **complaint_category** (Required): Select one of the following categories:
	+ Customer Service: Issues with customer service interactions.
	+ Product: Problems with products or services provided.
	+ Order: Issues with order fulfillment or processing.
	+ Other: For anything else not listed above.
* **complaint_description** (Required): Describe the issue you are reporting.
* **complaint_email** (Optional): Enter your email address to be contacted for further clarification or updates.
* **complaint_phone** (Optional): Enter your phone number to be contacted by phone.
* **complaint_note** (Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **complaint_attachments** (Optional): Select 'Yes' if you have attachments to provide (e.g., photos, documents).
* **submit** (Not a field for user input, this is the button to submit the form): Click to send your complaint report.
