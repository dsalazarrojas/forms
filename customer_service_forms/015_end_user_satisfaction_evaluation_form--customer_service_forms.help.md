# End User Satisfaction Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your experience with our company's services. Your feedback will help us improve our performance and ensure that you get the best possible service in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your contact email address to ensure we can get back to you regarding your feedback.
3. Rate your overall satisfaction with our services using the scale provided.
4. Provide a date and time when you experienced our services.
5. Share your comments about your experience with our company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name as you would like it to be displayed in our records.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name as you would like it to be displayed in our records.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your feedback.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Choose a rating from 1 to 5 to express your overall satisfaction with our services.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, optional): Please provide a rating for the overall quality of our services.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you experienced our services.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time when you experienced our services.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Share your thoughts and suggestions for improvement.
