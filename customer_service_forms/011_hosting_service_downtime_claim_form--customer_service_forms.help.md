# Hosting Service Downtime Claim Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to report and claim compensation for service downtime. Please use this form to report any issues that have caused service disruption or unavailability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your User Name to help us identify you.
2. Enter the name of the affected Service.
3. Specify the start and end times of the downtime.
4. Describe the type of downtime you experienced.
5. Explain the impact of the downtime on your use of the service.
6. If applicable, enter the compensation amount you are requesting.
7. Add any comments or additional information about the incident.
8. Select the user who should be assigned to handle your claim (though this is optional).
9. Choose the category for your claim (e.g., customer service forms).
10. Select the status of your claim (e.g., new, pending, closed).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (1, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your profile.
* **Service Name** (2, text, required): Enter the name of the service that was affected by the downtime.
* **Start Time** (3, date, required): Enter the date and time when the downtime started.
* **End Time** (4, date, required): Enter the date and time when the downtime ended.
* **Downtime Type** (5, text, required): Choose one of the following:
	+ "Unplanned Maintenance"
	+ "Technical Issues"
	+ "Network Errors"
* **Impact** (6, text, required): Describe the impact of the downtime on your use of the service.
* **Compensation Amount** (7, number, optional): Enter the amount of compensation you are requesting, if applicable.
* **Comments** (8, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information about the incident.
* **Assigned User** (9, select_one, optional): Select the user who should be assigned to handle your claim.
* **Category** (10, select_multiple, optional): Choose the category for your claim (e.g., customer service forms, Other).
* **Status** (11, select_one, optional): Select the status of your claim (e.g., new, pending, closed).
