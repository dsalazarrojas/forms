# Employee Peer Review Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Peer Review Template is a form used to conduct peer reviews of employees within an organization. It helps evaluate an employee's performance, teamwork, leadership, and other essential skills and qualities. The template provides a structured format for reviewers to provide feedback and suggestions for growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. **Review the employee's performance**: Carefully review the employee's work and behavior over a specific period.
2. **Fill out the form**: Go through each section and answer the questions honestly and thoughtfully.
3. **Provide specific examples**: Support your answers with specific examples or anecdotes about the employee's performance.
4. **Be constructive and objective**: Focus on providing constructive feedback that is fair and objective.
5. **Submit the form**: Once complete, submit the form to ensure it is recorded in the employee's file.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee being reviewed.
* **Peer Name**: Enter your name as the reviewer.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the review.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the review.
* **Department**: Choose the department the employee works in.
* **Job Title**: Enter the employee's job title.
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter the employee's supervisor's name.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the employee's manager's name.
* **Rating Scale**: Select the rating scale to use for the review (e.g., Good, Fair).
* **Overall Evaluation**: Evaluate the employee's overall performance using the selected rating scale.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions for the employee.
* **Employee ID**: Enter the employee's ID number (if applicable).
* **Rating**: Enter your rating of the employee's performance.
* **Evaluating Status**: Choose the status of the employee's evaluation (e.g., Active, Inactive).
* **Initiative Taking**: Check the boxes for the employee's initiative-taking abilities.
* **Adaptability**: Rate the employee's adaptability.
* **Teamwork**: Rate the employee's teamwork skills.
* **Communication**: Evaluate the employee's communication skills.
* **Leadership**: Rate the employee's leadership skills.
* **Time Management**: Rate the employee's time management skills.
* **Work Ethics**: Evaluate the employee's work ethics.
* **Performance**: Enter a free-text description of the employee's performance.
* **Peer Review Comments**: Enter any comments or suggestions for the employee.
* **Peer Review Status**: Choose the status of the peer review (e.g., In Progress, Completed).
