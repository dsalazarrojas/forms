# Travel Agency Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on a customer's recent travel experience to help us improve our services and tailor them to our customers' needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief description of your travel experience.
2. Enter your contact information (name, email, and phone number).
3. Rate your travel agency's services.
4. Provide any additional feedback or comments.
5. Select the travel agency that you used for your recent travel experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Agency Feedback** (`travel_agency_feedback_form`): Enter a brief description of your travel experience.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`): Enter your name, email, and phone number so we can contact you for further clarification or follow-up.
* **Your Name** (`name`): Enter your full name.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address. Please use a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Feedback Form** (`feedback_form`): Provide any additional feedback or comments about your travel experience.
* **Rating** (`rating`): Rate your overall experience with the travel agency. Choose from 1 to 5 stars.
* **Rating Score** (`rating_score`): Enter a rating score out of 5. This will help us measure your satisfaction level.
* **Travel Agency** (`travel_agency`): Indicate whether you used the travel agency for your recent travel experience.
* **Other** (`travel_agency_other`): If you answered "Other" for the travel agency, please provide a brief description of which travel agency you used.
* **Travel Agency 3** (`travel_agency_3`): Select all travel agencies that you used for your recent travel experience.
* **Date of Travel** (`date_of_travel`): Enter the date of your recent travel experience in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
