# rc_perfomance_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **rc_perfomance_evaluation_form** is a form designed to collect performance evaluation data from users. This form helps individuals provide feedback on the performance of a service they have received.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form fields with the requested information.
2.  Ensure you answer all required fields as indicated by the "Required" flag.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*  **Performance Evaluation** (`performance_evaluation`, text, Optional): This field is where you can provide your overall evaluation of the service. Please enter your comments or feedback in this section.
*  **Performance Scale** (`performance_scale`, select_one, Optional): Choose one of the following options to evaluate the performance of the service:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
*  **Date of Evaluation** (`date_of_evaluation`, date, Optional): Enter the date when you received the service or evaluation.
*  **Time of Evaluation** (`time_of_evaluation`, time, Optional): Enter the time when you received the service or evaluation.
*  **Note** (`note`, note, Optional): If you have any additional comments or feedback, you can enter them here.
*  **Email** (`email`, email, Optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you if necessary.
*  **Phone** (`phone`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if necessary.

## Tips
*  Please ensure you answer all required fields accurately and completely.
*  If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Note" field.
*  Your email and phone fields are optional but recommended so we can contact you if necessary.
