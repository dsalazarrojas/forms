# Streaming Channel Subscription Management Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for customers to update their streaming channel subscription preferences and notify the customer service team of any changes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the available options for each field and select the ones that apply to you.
2. Fill out the form with the required information.
3. Click submit to send the form to the customer service team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Stream Channel Subscription Preferences (1)**: This field allows you to select your preferred streaming channel subscription settings. 
* **Plan Change (2)**: Select "Yes" if you want to change your plan, "No" otherwise. 
* **Notification Choices (3)**: Select one or more options to receive notifications about your subscription.
* **Contact Customer Service Team (4)**: Select the reason you want to contact the customer service team.
* **Subscription Update Notification (5)**: Select "Yes" if you want to receive notifications about subscription updates.
* **Reason for Plan Change (6)**: If you selected "Yes" for plan change, provide a brief reason for the change.
* **Notification Frequency (7)**: Select "Yes" if you want to receive notifications about your subscription at a specific frequency.
* **Streaming Channel Subscription Management Notes (8)**: Add any additional notes about your subscription management.
* **Email (9)**: Enter your email address so the customer service team can contact you.
* **Phone (10)**: Enter your phone number so the customer service team can contact you.
* **Additional Info (11)**: Add any additional information about your subscription management.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the available options for each field carefully before submitting the form.
* Enter accurate and complete information in each field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the customer service team for assistance.
