# Customer Support Portal Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a support channel for customers to access support for a product or service they are using. Please fill out the form with your information to help our support team assist you with your issue or question.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill in your details in the corresponding fields. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`customer_support_portal_access_form_first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name to help the support team identify you. 
* **Last Name** (`customer_support_portal_access_form_last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name to help the support team identify you. 
* **Email** (`customer_support_portal_access_form_email`, email, required): Enter your email address to allow our support team to contact you. This is a required field for our team to verify your identity and get back to you.
* **Organization** (`customer_support_portal_access_form_organization`, text, optional): If you are accessing the support portal for your business or organization, please enter your company name here. This will help our support team better understand your needs. 
* **Phone** (`customer_support_portal_access_form_phone`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted by phone for support, please enter your phone number here. 
* **Note** (`customer_support_portal_access_form_note`, note, optional): If you have any additional information about your issue or question, please enter it in the note field. This will help our support team better understand your problem and provide more effective support. 
* **Date** (`customer_support_portal_access_form_date`, date, optional): Please select the date you are referring to for your issue or question. This will help our support team understand the context of your problem. 

## Tips
Please make sure to enter your information accurately and thoroughly to ensure our support team can assist you effectively. If you have any questions or concerns while filling out this form, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
