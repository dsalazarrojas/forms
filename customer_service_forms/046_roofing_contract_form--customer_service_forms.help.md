# Roofing Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers related to a roofing project. It will gather details about the project, the customer's contact information, and any contract terms.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, click on each field and input the required information. The form is divided into multiple sections, each with its own label and description.
2. Start by filling out the project details section.
3. Move on to the customer info section and enter your name, contact information, and any additional comments.
4. If necessary, provide contract terms and conditions.
5. Finally, add your signature to the customer signature field.
6. Review and submit the form once you have filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details** (project_details):
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: False
	+ Explanation: This section is for providing details about the roofing project. Enter a brief description of the project, including any relevant information such as project scope, location, and expected timeline.
* **Customer Info** (customer_info):
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: False
	+ Explanation: This section is for providing your name and contact information. Enter your name, email address, phone number, and any other relevant details.
* **Contract Terms** (contract_terms):
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: False
	+ Explanation: If there are any special terms or conditions associated with the project, enter them in this section.
* **Customer Signature** (customer_signature):
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: False
	+ Explanation: Add your signature here to confirm that you have read, understood, and agreed to the contract terms.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have filled out all the required fields before submitting the form.
* Be as specific and detailed as possible when filling out each section.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
* This form is for non-commercial purposes only.
* The form will be reviewed and processed in a timely manner once submitted.
