## Home Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Home Assessment Form is designed to gather information about a customer's residence, including contact details, home type, and other relevant characteristics. This form is used to capture data for various purposes, such as insurance claims, property assessments, or other business-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer's name in the **customer_name** field.
2. Enter your customer's contact number in the **contact_number** field.
3. Enter your customer's email address in the **email** field (if applicable).
4. Select the type of residence from the **home_type** dropdown menu (e.g., Apartment, House).
5. Choose the size of the residence by selecting one or more options from the **home_size** dropdown menu (e.g., 1, 2, 3).
6. Enter any additional information or comments in the **comments** field (optional).
7. If applicable, upload photos of the residence in the **photos** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer_name** (text, required): Enter the customer's name as it appears on their official identification.
* **contact_number** (text, required): Enter the customer's contact number.
* **email** (email, optional): If the customer has a valid email address, enter it here (optional).
* **date** (date, required): Enter the date of the assessment (MM/DD/YY).
* **address** (text, required): Enter the customer's address, including street, suite, or apartment number.
* **notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional information or comments about the assessment (optional).
* **home_type** (select_one, required): Select the type of residence (e.g., Apartment, House).
* **home_size** (select_multiple, required): Select the size of the residence (e.g., 1, 2, 3).
* **condition** (text, optional): Enter any notes about the condition of the residence (optional).
* **comments** (text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the assessment (optional).
* **photos** (date, optional): Upload photos of the residence (optional).
