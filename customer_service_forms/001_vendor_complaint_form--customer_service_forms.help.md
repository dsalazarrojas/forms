# Vendor Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Complaint Form is designed to collect complaints from customers about their experiences with vendors. This form helps vendors gather valuable feedback to improve their services and respond to customer concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Please fill out the required fields with as much detail as possible.
2. Select the options that best describe the complaint.
3. Be honest and clear in your description of the complaint.
4. Make sure to submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Main Page** (1)
* **Main Page (label)**: This is the main page of the form where you will enter the main details of your complaint.
* **Type: text** (`required: false`): Enter your complaint here in the space provided.

### 2. **Complaint Details** (2)
* **Complaint Details (label)**: This section is where you will provide a detailed description of your complaint.
* **Type: text** (`required: false`): Enter as much detail as possible about your complaint.

### 3. **Vendor Name** (3)
* **Vendor Name (label)**: This field is where you will enter the name of the vendor you are complaining about.
* **Type: text** (`required: false`): Enter the name of the vendor.

### 4. **Product/Services** (4)
* **Product/Services (label)**: If your complaint is related to a product or service, enter the name of the product or service.
* **Type: text** (`required: false`): Enter the name of the product or service.

### 5. **Complaint Description** (5)
* **Complaint Description (label)**: This is where you will enter a detailed description of your complaint.
* **Type: text** (`required: false`): Enter as much detail as possible about your complaint.

### 6. **Vendor ID** (6)
* **Vendor ID (label)**: This field is where you will enter the ID of the vendor you are complaining about.
* **Type: text** (`required: false`): Enter the ID of the vendor.

### 7. **Customer ID** (7)
* **Customer ID (label)**: This field is where you will enter your customer ID.
* **Type: text** (`required: false`): Enter your customer ID.

### 8. **Date** (8)
* **Date (label)**: This field is where you will enter the date of your complaint.
* **Type: date** (`required: false`): Enter the date of your complaint.

### 9. **Time** (9)
* **Time (label)**: This field is where you will enter the time of your complaint.
* **Type: time** (`required: false`): Enter the time of your complaint.

### 10. **Complaint Type** (10)
* **Complaint Type (label)**: This field is where you will select the type of your complaint.
* **Type: select_one** (`required: false`): Select one of the options provided.

### 11. **Vendor Complaint** (11)
* **Vendor Complaint (label)**: This field is where you will select multiple complaints related to the vendor.
* **Type: select_multiple** (`required: false`): Select multiple options provided.

### 12. **Notes** (12)
* **Notes (label)**: This is where you will enter any additional information you feel is necessary.
* **Type: text** (`required: false`): Enter any additional information.

### 13. **Submitted By** (13)
* **Submitted By (label)**: This field is where you will enter who submitted this complaint.
* **Type: text** (`required: false`): Enter who submitted the complaint.

## Tips
* Please be honest and clear in your description of the complaint.
* If you need to attach any supporting documents or evidence, please make sure to do so in the space provided.
* If you have any further questions or need assistance, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* You can submit the form once you have completed all the fields.
