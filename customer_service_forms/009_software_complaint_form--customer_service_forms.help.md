# Software Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Software Complaint Form is a tool designed for customers to submit their complaints and feedback related to our software product. It is a simple and straightforward form that will guide you through the process of submitting your complaint.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the relevant option for "Software Complaint" from the dropdown menu.
2.  Enter the name of the software application that you are experiencing issues with.
3.  Provide a detailed description of the issue you are experiencing.
4.  Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you for further clarification or updates.
5.  Enter your phone number to enable us to contact you if needed.
6.  Select the date you encountered the issue (if known).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Software Complaint** (`software_complaint_form`, select_multiple, required): Please select "Yes" if you are experiencing a complaint, and "No" otherwise. This will help us understand whether you are submitting a complaint or feedback.
* **Application Name** (`application_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the software application that you are experiencing issues with. This will help us to identify the specific application you are referring to.
* **Description** (`description`, note, optional): Provide a detailed description of the issue you are experiencing. Please be as specific as possible to help us resolve the issue efficiently.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you for further clarification or updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number to enable us to contact you if needed.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the date you encountered the issue (if known). This will help us to track the history of your complaint and respond accordingly.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you complete all required fields accurately.
* Be as specific as possible when providing the description of the issue.
* If you have any attachments or screenshots that can support your complaint, please feel free to attach them to your submission.
* You can submit multiple complaints or feedback at once.
* If you are unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to contact us for assistance.
