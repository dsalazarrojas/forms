# Identity Creative Agency Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Identity Creative Agency Form is designed to gather information from users about their identities and roles. This form is used by the agency to verify user information and provide relevant services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your role from the "Role" field dropdown menu.
6. Select your industry from the "Industry" field dropdown menu.
7. Enter your company name in the "Company" field.
8. Enter the location of your office or place of work in the "Location" field.
9. Provide a brief description of your work experience and background in the "Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name, as you would like to be addressed.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name, as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Role** (`role`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one of the following roles:
	+ Marketing
	+ Public Relations
	+ Online Marketing
* **Industry** (`industry`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more of the following industries:
	+ Marketing Agency
	+ Public Relations
	+ Online Marketing
	+ PR Firm
	+ Advertising Agency
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company's name.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location of your office or place of work.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of your work experience and background.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us directly.
* If you are experiencing technical difficulties, try clearing your browser cache or contacting our support team.
