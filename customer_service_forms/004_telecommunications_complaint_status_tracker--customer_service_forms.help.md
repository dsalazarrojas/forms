# Telecommunications Complaint Status Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track the status of customer complaints in the telecommunication industry. It helps telecom companies to manage their complaints and updates from customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with the necessary information.
2. Select the correct options for Assigned Technician, Resolution Status, and Telecom Company.
3. Enter the customer update and comment fields if necessary.
4. Review the form before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Complaint**: Enter a brief description of the customer's complaint.
* **Assigned Technician**: Select the technician assigned to handle the complaint.
* **Assigned Date**: Enter the date when the complaint was assigned to the technician.
* **Resolution Status**: Select the current status of the resolution (Open, Closed, or On Hold).
* **Customer Update**: Enter any updates from the customer regarding their complaint.
* **Resolution Comment**: Enter any comments from the technician or manager regarding the resolution.
* **Customer Complaint Details**: Enter a detailed description of the customer's complaint.
* **Customer Complaint Status**: Select the current status of the customer's complaint (New, In Progress, or Resolved).
* **Customer Update Comment**: Enter any additional comments from the customer.
* **Telecom Company**: Select the telecom company related to the complaint.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Only submit the form once for each unique complaint.
* Review the form regularly to track the progress of complaints and ensure timely resolution.
