# Complaint Log Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Complaint Log Tracker is a form used to record and track customer complaints. It helps us to manage and resolve issues efficiently, ensuring that customers receive timely and effective support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Complaint ID** field with a unique identifier for the complaint.
2. Enter the **Log Date** of when the complaint was logged.
3. Enter the **Complaint Date** of when the complaint occurred.
4. Enter the **Complaint Time** of when it happened (optional, but recommended).
5. Fill out the **Complainant Name**, **Complainant Email**, and **Complainant Phone** fields with the relevant information.
6. Select the **Complainant Type** based on their relationship to the organization (e.g., Customer, Client, Employee, etc.).
7. Choose the **Complaint Category** based on the type of complaint (e.g., Product Quality, Service Quality, etc.).
8. Fill out the **Complaint Subject** field with a brief summary of the issue.
9. Enter the **Complaint Description** field with a detailed description of the problem.
10. Select the **Priority Level** based on the urgency of the complaint (e.g., Critical, High, Medium, etc.).
11. Choose the **Severity Level** based on the impact of the complaint (e.g., Severe, High, Moderate, etc.).
12. Enter the **Assigned To** field with the person responsible for handling the complaint.
13. Select the **Assigned Department** based on the department responsible for resolving the complaint (e.g., Customer Service, Sales, etc.).
14. Choose the **Status** of the complaint (e.g., New, In Progress, Pending, etc.).
15. Fill out the **Expected Resolution Date** and **Actual Resolution Date** fields if applicable (if not known, leave blank).
16. Enter the **Resolution Description** field with details of how the issue was resolved.
17. Select the **Customer Satisfaction** level based on the customer's satisfaction with the resolution.
18. Check the **Follow-up Required** field based on whether a follow-up is necessary.
19. Enter the **Follow-up Date** if a follow-up is required.
20. Enter the **Root Cause** field with an explanation of the issue's cause.
21. Enter the **Preventive Action** field with suggestions for preventing similar issues.
22. Enter the **Related Complaints** field with any linked complaints.
23. Check the **Escalation History** field based on whether the issue has been escalated.
24. Enter the **Internal Notes** field with any additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Complaint ID**: Unique identifier for the complaint.
* **Log Date**: Date the complaint was logged.
* **Complaint Date**: When the complaint occurred.
* **Complaint Time**: When the complaint happened (optional).
* **Complainant Name**: Person making the complaint.
* **Complainant Email**: Contact email.
* **Complainant Phone**: Contact phone.
* **Complainant Type**: Relationship to organization.
* **Complaint Category**: Type of complaint.
* **Complaint Subject**: Brief summary of the issue.
* **Complaint Description**: Detailed description of the problem.
* **Priority Level**: Urgency of the complaint.
* **Severity Level**: Impact of the complaint.
* **Assigned To**: Person responsible for handling the complaint.
* **Assigned Department**: Department responsible for resolving the complaint.
* **Status**: Complaint status.
* **Expected Resolution Date**: Date the issue is expected to be resolved.
* **Actual Resolution Date**: Date the issue was resolved.
* **Resolution Description**: Details of how the issue was resolved.
* **Customer Satisfaction**: Customer's satisfaction with the resolution.
* **Follow-up Required**: Whether a follow-up is necessary.
* **Follow-up Date**: Date to follow up (if necessary).
* **Root Cause**: Explanation of the issue's cause.
* **Preventive Action**: Suggestions for preventing similar issues.
* **Related Complaints**: Any linked complaints.
* **Escalation History**: Whether the issue has been escalated.
* **Internal Notes**: Additional information.

## Tips
* Be specific and clear in your responses to ensure accurate tracking and resolution of complaints.
* Fill out all required fields.
* Use the provided options for select fields to choose the most accurate and relevant option.
* If unsure about a field, leave it blank or ask for clarification.
* Save your progress and come back to the form if necessary.
* Review and update the form regularly to reflect changes in the complaint status.
