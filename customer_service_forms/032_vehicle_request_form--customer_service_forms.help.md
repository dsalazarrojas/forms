# Vehicle Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about vehicle requests from customers. It helps us understand the specific requirements for vehicle type, event location, event date and time, and other details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form title to describe your vehicle request.
2. Enter the date and time of the event where the vehicle will be used.
3. Enter the duration of the event.
4. Enter the location of the event.
5. Choose the type of vehicle you require (School Bus, Tour Bus, or Limousine).
6. Enter the number of vehicles you need (if applicable).
7. Select the color(s) of the vehicle you need (if applicable).
8. Enter the year of the vehicle (if applicable).
9. Enter the name of the driver who will be operating the vehicle.
10. Enter the driver's license number.
11. Select the make of the vehicle (if applicable).
12. Enter the model of the vehicle (if applicable).
13. Select the color of the vehicle (if applicable).
14. Enter the year of the vehicle (if applicable).
15. Enter the license plate number of the vehicle (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **vehicle_request_form** (Title, Text, Optional): A brief description of your vehicle request.
* **event_date** (Date, Required): Enter the date of the event where the vehicle will be used.
* **event_time** (Time, Required): Enter the time of the event where the vehicle will be used.
* **event_duration** (Number, Optional): Enter the duration of the event.
* **event_location** (Text, Required): Enter the location of the event.
* **vehicle_type** (Select One, Required): Choose the type of vehicle you require (School Bus, Tour Bus, or Limousine).
* **vehicle_quantity** (Number, Optional): Enter the number of vehicles you need (if applicable).
* **vehicle_color** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the color of the vehicle you need (Red, Blue, or Black).
* **vehicle_year** (Number, Optional): Enter the year of the vehicle (if applicable).
* **driver_name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the driver who will be operating the vehicle.
* **driver_license** (Text, Required): Enter the driver's license number.
* **vehicle_make** (Select One, Optional): Select the make of the vehicle (Toyota, Ford, or Honda).
* **vehicle_model** (Text, Optional): Enter the model of the vehicle.
* **vehicle_year** (Number, Optional): Enter the year of the vehicle (if applicable).
* **vehicle_license** (Text, Optional): Enter the license plate number of the vehicle (if applicable).
* **Submit** (Text, Required): Click Submit to send your vehicle request.
