# customer_service_record_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and document customer service interactions, providing a structured way to capture essential information from customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your details in the `customer_details` field.
2. Provide a description of the issue you are facing in the `issue_description` field.
3. Share your message with the customer service representative in the `customer_message` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### customer_details (Text)
* **customer_details** (`customer_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name, email, and any other relevant contact information.

### issue_description (Text)
* **issue_description** (`issue_description`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the problem or issue you are facing.

### customer_message (Text)
* **customer_message** (`customer_message`, `text`, required/optional): Share any additional comments or messages with the customer service representative.

## Tips
* Please provide clear and concise information in each field to ensure that the customer service representative can efficiently resolve your issue.
* If you have any attachments or documents to support your issue, you can attach them to your message.
* If you need assistance with anything else, don't hesitate to reach out to the customer service team.
