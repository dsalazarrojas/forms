# Holiday Cake Order Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Holiday Cake Order Form Template is used to collect information for holiday cake orders. This form is used to capture all the necessary details for processing the order, including the order details, customer information, payment information, and order status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of order: Choose between Single or Multiple to indicate the type of order.
2. Enter cake design: Provide a description of the cake design.
3. Enter customer information: Enter the customer's name and any other relevant details.
4. Enter payment information: Enter the payment details, including any applicable discounts.
5. Enter order date and time: Enter the date and time for the order.
6. Enter cake description: Briefly describe the cake being ordered.
7. Enter order total: Enter the total cost of the order.
8. Enter customer price: Enter the price that the customer has agreed to pay.
9. Select order status: Select the current status of the order, which can be Completed, Pending, or Cancelled.
10. Add any notes: Enter any additional notes about the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Order Details
* **Order Type** (`1`, `select_one`, **Required**): Choose between Single or Multiple to indicate the type of order.

### Cake Design
* **Cake Design** (`2`, `text`, **Optional**): Provide a description of the cake design.

### Customer Information
* **Customer Information** (`3`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter the customer's name and any other relevant details.

### Payment Information
* **Payment Information** (`4`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter the payment details, including any applicable discounts.

### Order Date and Time
* **Order Date and Time** (`5`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter the date and time for the order.

### Cake Description
* **Cake Description** (`6`, `text`, **Required**): Briefly describe the cake being ordered.

### Order Total
* **Order Total** (`7`, `number`, **Required**): Enter the total cost of the order.

### Customer Price
* **Customer Price** (`8`, `number`, **Required**): Enter the price that the customer has agreed to pay.

### Discount
* **Discount** (`9`, `number`, **Optional**): Enter any applicable discounts.

### Tax
* **Tax** (`10`, `number`, **Optional**): Enter any applicable tax.

### Order Status
* **Order Status** (`11`, `select_multiple`, **Required**): Select the current status of the order, which can be Completed, Pending, or Cancelled.

### Notes
* **Notes** (`12`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter any additional notes about the order.
