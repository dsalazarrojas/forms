# pop_up_feedback_form_by_julie_potts - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers after an event. Your input will help us improve future events and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the "event_type" that best describes the event you attended or interacted with.
2. Provide your "feedback" in the text field.
3. If you would like to provide your "email" or "phone_number", you can do so in the respective fields.
4. If you have any additional "note" about your experience, you can provide it here.
5. If you would like to specify who "created_by" (Julie Potts or John), you can select from the options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Prophets Prophets** (`event_prophets_prophets`, text, required/optional): This field is where you can describe the event you attended or interacted with.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required/optional): Provide your thoughts, comments, or suggestions about the event.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of event you attended or interacted with.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): If you would like to provide your email address, you can do so here.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): If you would like to provide your phone number, you can do so here.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional notes about your experience.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, select_one, required/optional): Select who created the event you attended or interacted with. Options are Julie Potts and John.
* **Created By Other** (`created_by_other`, select_one, required/optional): If none of the above options apply, select "other".
