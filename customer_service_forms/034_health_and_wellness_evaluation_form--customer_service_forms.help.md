# health_and_wellness_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The health_and_wellness_evaluation_form is designed to collect client data and evaluate their health and wellness status. This form aims to provide a comprehensive assessment of a client's health and wellness, helping healthcare professionals make informed decisions and provide personalized care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "Client Data" page.
2.  Enter the client's **Client Name** in the text field next to "Client Name."
3.  Enter the client's **Email** in the email field next to "Email."
4.  Enter the client's **Phone** number in the text field next to "Phone."
5.  Enter the client's **Date Of Birth** in the date field next to "Date Of Birth."
6.  Enter the client's **Medical History** in the text field next to "Medical History."
7.  Enter the client's **Medications** in the text field next to "Medications."
8.  Enter the client's **Allergies** in the text field next to "Allergies."
9.  Enter the client's **Health Goal** in the text field next to "Health Goal."
10. Enter the client's **Current Health Status** in the text field next to "Current Health Status."
11. Enter the client's **Wellness Routine** in the text field next to "Wellness Routine."

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Client Name** (client_data, text, required): This field is where you should enter the client's full name.
*   **Email** (email, email, required): Enter the client's email address here. Ensure it is a valid email format (e.g., username@example.com).
*   **Phone** (phone, text, required): Enter the client's phone number here. This can be in the format of 123-456-7890 or +1 123 456 7890.
*   **Date Of Birth** (date, date, required): Enter the client's date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Medical History** (medical_history, text, required): Describe the client's medical history, including any past illnesses, surgeries, or allergies.
*   **Medications** (medications, text, required): List the client's current medications, including dosage and frequency.
*   **Allergies** (allergies, text, required): List the client's allergies, including any known food or environmental allergies.
*   **Health Goal** (health_goal, text, required): Describe the client's health and wellness goals, such as weight loss or improved blood pressure.
*   **Current Health Status** (current_health_status, text, required): Describe the client's current health status, including any health issues or concerns.
*   **Wellness Routine** (wellness_routine, text, required): Describe the client's daily wellness routine, including exercise, diet, or stress management.
