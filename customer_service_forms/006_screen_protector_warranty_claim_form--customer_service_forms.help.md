# screen_protector_warranty_claim_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to submit a warranty claim for a screen protector product. This form is used to report any issue or defect with your screen protector and to obtain a replacement or repair.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Gather all necessary information and documents before starting to fill out the form.
2. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
3. Review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Customer Details** (text)
• Customer Details: This field is used to capture your contact information. Please enter your name and any other relevant details as prompted.

### 2. **Customer Name** (text)
• Customer Name: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.

### 3. **Customer Email** (email)
• Customer Email: Enter your email address where you can be reached for updates and follow-up.

### 4. **Customer Phone** (text)
• Customer Phone: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further communication.

### 5. **Product Details** (text)
• Product Details: Describe the product you're submitting the claim for, including any relevant model numbers or serial numbers.

### 6. **Product Name** (text)
• Product Name: Enter the name of the product you're submitting the claim for.

### 7. **Purchase Date** (date)
• Purchase Date: Enter the date of purchase for the product. This is the date the product was purchased.

### 8. **Serial Number** (text)
• Serial Number: Enter the serial number of the product, if available.

### 9. **Issue Description** (text)
• Issue Description: Describe the issue or defect you're experiencing with the product. Be as specific as possible.

### 10. **Supporting Documents** (note)
• Supporting Documents: Attach any relevant documents or images that support your claim, such as receipts, photos, or videos.

### 11. **Retailer Signature** (note)
• Retailer Signature: This field is not required for customers. It's for retailers to confirm they've reviewed the claim.

### 12. **Customer Signature** (note)
• Customer Signature: Please sign and date this field to confirm that you're submitting this claim in good faith.

## Tips
- Please ensure that your submission is accurate and complete to avoid delays in processing your claim.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, you can contact our customer service team for help.
- We'll review and respond to your submission as soon as possible.
