# Submit Your Questions Or Requests - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to provide a channel for customers to submit their questions or requests to the customer support team. It allows customers to get help and support on various topics, including Support, General Inquiry, and Other categories.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit Your Questions Or Requests" form.
2. Type your question or request in the "What's your question or request?" text field.
3. Fill in your name and email address if you want to be contacted for follow-up.
4. Enter your phone number if you need to be contacted over the phone.
5. Provide the date and time you prefer to be followed up on.
6. Select a category that best describes your question or request.
7. Provide a detailed description of your question or request.
8. Choose whether you would like to be followed up on and, if so, when you would like to be contacted.
9. Finally, click the "Submit" button to send your question or request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. What's your question or request? (Required: No)
• **What's your question or request?**: A text field where you can type in your inquiry.

### 2. Name (Required: No)
• **Name**: A text field where you can enter your name.

### 3. Email (Required: No)
• **Email**: An email field where you can enter your email address.

### 4. Phone number (Required: No)
• **Phone number**: A text field where you can enter your phone number.

### 5. Date (Required: No)
• **Date**: A date field where you can select a date for follow-up.

### 6. Time (Required: No)
• **Time**: A time field where you can select a time for follow-up.

### 7. Category (Required: No)
• **Category**: A select multiple field where you can choose one or more categories that best describe your question or request.

### 8. Description (Required: No)
• **Description**: A text field where you can provide a detailed description of your question or request.

### 9. How would you like to be followed up on? (Required: Yes)
• **How would you like to be followed up on?**: A select one field where you can choose whether you would like to be followed up on and, if so, when you would like to be contacted.

### 10. Follow-up time (Required: No)
• **Follow-up time**: A time field where you can select a time for follow-up, if you have chosen to be followed up on.
