## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Address Line 1
* **Address Line 1** (`address_line_1`, Text, Required/Optional): The first line of your residential address. Please enter your address in the format "123 Main St".

### Address Line 2
* **Address Line 2** (`address_line_2`, Text, Required/Optional): The second line of your residential address. This can be the apartment number, suite number, or any additional address information. Please enter your address in the format " Apt 101".

### City
* **City** (`city`, Text, Required/Optional): The city where you reside. Please enter the name of the city where you live.

### State
* **State** (`state`, Text, Required/Optional): The state or province where you reside. Please enter the abbreviated state name (e.g., "CA" for California).

### Postal Code
* **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, Text, Required/Optional): The postal code or zip code for your area. Please enter the 5-digit or 9-digit zip code for your city.

### Country
* **Country** (`country`, Text, Required/Optional): The country where you reside. Please enter the name of your country.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Required/Optional): Your phone number. Please enter your phone number in the format "123-456-7890".

### Signature
* **Signature** (`signature`, Text, Required/Optional): A handwritten or digital signature of the customer. Please enter your signature as it appears on your identification documents.

## Tips
