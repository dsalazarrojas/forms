# Updated Nail Strip Order Form With Quantity - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect information from customers to place an order for nail strips with specific details such as quantity, type, size, color, and price.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please enter the quantity of nail strips you would like to order.
2. Select the type of nail strips you want to order from the options provided.
3. Choose the size of the nail strips you would like to order.
4. Select the color of the nail strips you would like to order from the options provided.
5. Enter the price you would like to pay for the nail strips.
6. Enter the price for the specific size of the nail strips.
7. Provide your shipping address where you would like the nail strips to be delivered.
8. Add any additional order notes if necessary.
9. Enter your email address to contact you in case of any issues with your order.
10. Enter your phone number to contact you in case of any urgent matters related to your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Quantity (1)**: Enter the number of nail strips you would like to order.
* **Nail Strips Type (3)**: Select the type of nail strips you want to order from the options provided. This could be strip type 1, strip type 2, strip type 3, strip type 4, strip type 5, strip type 6, strip type 7, strip type 8.
* **Nail Strips Size (4)**: Choose the size of the nail strips you would like to order. Select from the options provided, such as size 1, size 2, size 3, etc.
* **Nail Strips Color (5)**: Select the color of the nail strips you would like to order from the extensive list of colors provided.
* **Price (6)**: Enter the price you would like to pay for the nail strips. This could be different from the price listed for the specific size.
* **Size Price (7)**: Enter the price for the specific size of the nail strips you are ordering.
* **Shipping Address (8)**: Provide your shipping address where you would like the nail strips to be delivered.
* **Order Notes (9)**: Add any additional notes or comments about your order if necessary.
* **Email (10)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you in case of any issues with your order.
* **Phone (11)**: Enter your phone number to contact you in case of any urgent matters related to your order.
