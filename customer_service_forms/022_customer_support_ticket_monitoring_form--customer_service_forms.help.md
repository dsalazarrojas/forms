# Customer Support Ticket Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Support Ticket Monitoring Form is designed to help our team monitor and resolve technical issues reported by our customers. To ensure that we can effectively assist you, please complete this form with as much detail as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select whether your issue is a "Yes" or "No" to indicate that you are reporting a technical issue.
2. Enter your name in the "Customer Name" field. This will help us identify you and address your issue.
3. Enter your email address in the "Customer Email" field. This will enable us to contact you regarding your issue.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Customer Phone" field. This will allow us to contact you directly if needed.
5. In the "Issue Description" field, describe your problem in as much detail as possible. This will help us understand the issue and provide a prompt resolution.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Support Ticket (1)**: Select "Yes" if you are reporting a technical issue, "No" otherwise.
* **Customer Name (2)**: Enter your full name as you would like us to address you.
* **Customer Email (3)**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Customer Phone (4)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you directly if needed.
* **Issue Description (5)**: Describe your technical issue in as much detail as possible. This could include steps you've taken to try and resolve it, error messages, or any other relevant information.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when filling out the "Issue Description" field. This will help us resolve your issue more efficiently.
* If you have any supporting files or screenshots, please feel free to attach them to your form to provide more context.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us directly.
