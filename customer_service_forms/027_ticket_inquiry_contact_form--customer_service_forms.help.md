# Ticket Inquiry Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers to inquire about a specific ticket or issue they are experiencing with our service. This form helps our customer service team to quickly address and resolve the issue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name and email address as a contact information.
2. Enter the ticket or reference number related to your inquiry.
3. Clearly describe the question or issue you are experiencing.
4. Select the type of event related to your inquiry.
5. Select the date and time you are traveling.
6. If you want to add any additional comments or notes, please use the "Note" field.
7. If you want to follow up on this issue, select the follow up date.
8. Lastly, you don't need to fill in the customer service contact details if you are filling out this form as a customer. However, if you are filling this out as a customer service representative, please enter your contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (customer_name): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Customer Email** (customer_email): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Ticket Number** (ticket_number): Enter the reference number of the ticket you are inquiring about.
* **What's the Issue?** (what_is_the_question): Briefly describe the question or issue you are experiencing.
* **Event Type** (event_type): Select the type of event related to your issue.
* **Travel Date** (travel_date): Enter the date you are traveling.
* **Travel Time** (travel_time): Enter the time you are traveling.
* **Note** (note): Add any additional comments or notes related to your issue.
* **Customer Service Phone** (customer_service_phone): Enter your contact phone number if you are filling out this form as a customer. (Optional)
* **Customer Service Email** (customer_service_email): Enter your customer service email address. (Optional)
* **Priority** (priority): Select the priority level of your issue (High, Medium, Low).
* **Follow Up** (follow_up): Select a follow-up date if you want us to follow up on this issue.
* **Event(s)** (event): Select any additional events related to your issue. (Optional)
* **Customer Service** (customer_service): Select the customer service representative that will handle your issue. (Optional)

Note: Please fill out this form as accurately and clearly as possible. If you have any questions or need assistance, contact us at your preferred contact method.
