# Dave's Personal Training Consultation Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your personal training goals, background, and preferences to better understand how a personal training consultation from Dave's Personal Training Consultation can help you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your Last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Select your Date of birth in the "Date of birth" field.
5. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Provide your Address in the "Address" field.
7. Select your Marital status in the "Marital status" field.
8. Select the reasons for your training goals in the "Training goals" field.
9. Select your background in Martial arts in the "Martial arts background" field.
10. Select your current Fitness level in the "Fitness level" field.
11. Indicate whether you have a Nutrition plan in place in the "Nutrition plan" field.
12. Select the days of the week you are available to train in the "Available days" field.
13. Select the preferred times of the day for your training sessions in the "Preferred times" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name (Text)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name (Text)**: Enter your last name.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Date of birth (Date)**: Select your date of birth.
* **Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address (Note)**: Enter your address.
* **Marital status (Select One)**: Select your marital status.
	+ Single
	+ Married
	+ Divorced
	+ In a relationship
* **Training goals (Select Multiple)**: Select the reasons for your training goals.
	+ Fitness
	+ Nutrition
	+ Martial arts
* **Martial arts background (Select Multiple)**: Select your background in martial arts.
	+ Karate
	+ Judo
	+ Taekwondo
* **Fitness level (Select One)**: Select your current fitness level.
	+ Beginner
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced
* **Nutrition plan (Select One)**: Indicate whether you have a Nutrition plan in place.
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Available days (Select Multiple)**: Select the days of the week you are available to train.
	+ Monday
	+ Tuesday
	+ Wednesday
	+ Thursday
	+ Friday
* **Preferred times (Select Multiple)**: Select the preferred times of the day for your training sessions.
	+ Morning
	+ Afternoon
	+ Evening
	+ Anytime

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact our representative for assistance.
