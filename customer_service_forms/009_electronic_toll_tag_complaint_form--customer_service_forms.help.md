# Electronic Toll Tag Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Electronic Toll Tag Complaint Form is a user-friendly tool designed to assist customers in reporting any incidents or issues they may be experiencing with their electronic toll tags. This form aims to collect relevant information from customers to help us identify and resolve the issue promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
- To start, select "Electronic Toll Tag Complaint Form" from the list of available forms.
- Click on the "Submit" button to begin filling out the form.
- Fill out the form fields as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Information (<user_information>)**: This field is for your personal details such as name and contact information. Please provide your name and a valid email address to ensure we can contact you regarding your complaint.
- **Incident Details (<incident_details>)**: In this section, describe the issue you're experiencing with your electronic toll tag. Provide a detailed account of what happened, including dates and times if applicable.
- **Images (<images>)**: If you'd like to attach any images or screenshots related to your issue, please upload them here.
- **Supporting Documents (<supporting_documents>)**: If you'd like to attach any supporting documents, such as receipts or invoices, please upload them here.
- **Comments (<comments>)**: If you have any additional comments or remarks regarding your complaint, feel free to add them here.
- **Assignee (<assignee>)**: Please indicate whether you would like the issue to be assigned to a specific agent.
- **Assigned Agent (<assigned_agent>)**: If you have a preferred agent assigned to handle your issue, please select "Yes" here.
- **Priority (<priority>)**: Please select "Yes" if you consider your issue to be high-priority, which will expedite the resolution process.
- **Status (<status>)**: Select the status of your issue among "Active", "Inactive" options.
 
## Tips
- Ensure all fields are accurately and thoroughly filled out to help us better understand your concern.
- If you're unsure about any field, please refer to this guide or contact our support team for assistance.
- Duplicate complaints will not be entertained, so please ensure you haven't already submitted a complaint about the same incident.
- Please be patient and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.
