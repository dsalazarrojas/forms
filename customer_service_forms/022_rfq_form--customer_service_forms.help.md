# rfq_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a quote for a project and gather essential details about the project and the contact person.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the business name and contact person's first and last name.
2. Provide the contact person's phone number and email address.
3. Enter a brief description of the project.
4. Select the expected project date(s).
5. Enter the project budget.
6. Choose the project location.
7. Add any additional notes about the project.
8. Enter the contact person's title.
9. Enter the contact person's company name.
10. Enter the contact person's department.
11. Enter the contact person's external phone number and email address.
12. Select the expected delivery date.
13. Enter the project status.
14. Enter the expected completion date.
15. Enter the project manager's name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the business or company.
* **Contact First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text, required: false): Enter the first name of the contact person.
* **Contact Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, text, required: false): Enter the last name of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the project.
* **Expected Project Date** (`expected_project_date`, date, required: false): Select the expected project date.
* **Project Budget** (`project_budget`, text, required: false): Enter the budget for the project.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the project.
* **Project Notes** (`project_notes`, text, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the project.
* **Contact Title** (`contact_title`, text, required: false): Enter the contact person's title.
* **Contact Company** (`contact_company`, text, required: false): Enter the contact person's company name.
* **Contact Department** (`contact_department`, text, required: false): Enter the contact person's department.
* **Contact External Phone** (`contact_ext_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the contact person's external phone number.
* **Contact External Email** (`contact_ext_email`, email, required: false): Enter the contact person's external email address.
* **Expected Delivery Date** (`expected_delivery_date`, date, required: false): Select the expected delivery date.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, text, required: false): Enter the project status.
* **Expected Completion Date** (`expected_completion_date`, date, required: false): Select the expected completion date.
* **Project Manager** (`project_manager`, text, required: false): Enter the project manager's name.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields, even if they are not marked as required.
* Double-check the expected dates and budget to ensure accuracy.
* Enter any additional notes about the project to provide context.
* Ensure the contact person's email and phone number are accurate.
* Review the project status and expected dates carefully to ensure they are up-to-date.
