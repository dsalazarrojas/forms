# Product Authenticity Verification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to verify the authenticity of a product. To complete this form, please provide the requested information to help us confirm the product's legitimacy.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form fields with the correct information about the product you are inquiring about.
2. Be sure to provide accurate and detailed information in each field to ensure a smooth verification process.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please ask for help or clarification from our customer service team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Information**: Provide a brief description of the product you are verifying.
* **Customer Details**: Enter your name and other relevant customer information.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter your shipping address, including zip code, state, and country.
* **Product Authenticity**: Select "Verified" if the product is authentic, "Suspect" if you suspect it may not be authentic.
* **Reason for Suspension**: If you suspect the product is not authentic, provide a brief explanation.
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional information or comments about the product, enter them here.
* **Date of Purchase**: Enter the date you purchased the product.
* **Time of Purchase**: Enter the time you purchased the product (optional).
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Shipping Zip**: Enter the shipping zip code of the product.
* **Shipping City**: Enter the city where the product was shipped from.
* **Shipping State**: Select the state where the product was shipped from (if not California, New York, or Other).
* **Shipping Country**: Select the country where the product was shipped from (if not United States, Canada, or Mexico).
* **Shipping Region**: Select the region where the product was shipped from (if not North America, South America, or Other).
* **Shipping Province**: Enter any additional province information (if applicable).
* **Product Description**: Provide a detailed description of the product.
* **Product Image**: Provide an image of the product (if possible).
* **Certificate of Authenticity**: Provide a certificate of authenticity from the product manufacturer or supplier.
* **Proof of Purchaser Identity**: Provide proof of your identity as the purchaser.
* **Product Barcode**: Enter the product's barcode (if available).
* **Product SKU**: Enter the product's stock keeping unit (if available).
* **Purchase Date**: Enter the date you purchased the product.
* **Purchase Time**: Enter the time you purchased the product (if available).
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information about the product.
