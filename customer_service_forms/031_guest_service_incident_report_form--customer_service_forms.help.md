# Guest Service Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guest Service Incident Report Form is designed to collect information about incidents or issues reported by guests. This form aims to gather details about the incident, including the date and time it occurred and the specific circumstances surrounding it, to ensure that guest complaints are addressed and resolved efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, guests can follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of the incident in the "Guest Service Incident Report" field.
2. Provide the date and time of the incident in the "Date and Time" field.
3. Enter the time of the incident in the "Time" field. If the time is not known, you can leave this field blank.
4. Describe the incident in detail in the "Incident Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Service Incident Report** (`guest_service_incident_report`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the incident, including what happened and any relevant details.
* **Date and Time** (`incident_date_time`, date, optional): Enter the date and time of the incident. This field is optional, but providing this information helps staff understand the timing and context of the incident.
* **Time** (`incident_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the incident. If the time is not known, you can leave this field blank.
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, text, optional): Describe the incident in detail, including any relevant context or circumstances surrounding it. Provide as much information as possible to help staff understand and address the issue.
