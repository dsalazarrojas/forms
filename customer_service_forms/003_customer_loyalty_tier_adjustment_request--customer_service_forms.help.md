# Customer Loyalty Tier Adjustment Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request a change to your loyalty tier. This form is used to notify our team of your request to move up or down a tier, based on changes in your purchase history or other relevant factors.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with the required information.
2. Select the reason for the adjustment you are requesting.
3. Indicate the desired new loyalty tier.
4. Choose a date and time for when the change should take effect.
5. Add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Loyalty Tier Adjustment Request**: This is the title of the form, which should be self-explanatory.
* **Loyalty Tier ID**: This is a unique identifier for the customer's current loyalty tier. Please note that this field is not editable.
* **Loyalty Tier Name**: This field shows the name of the customer's current loyalty tier. Please note that this field is not editable.
* **Reason for Adjustment**: Select the reason for requesting a change to your loyalty tier. Choose the corresponding tier number to indicate the reason for the request.
	+ 1: Tier 1
	+ 2: Tier 2
	+ 3: Tier 3
	+ 4: Tier 4
	+ 5: Tier 5
* **Adjustment Amount**: This field is not applicable for this form.
* **Current Loyalty Tier**: This field shows the customer's current loyalty tier. Please note that this field is not editable.
* **New Loyalty Tier**: Select the new loyalty tier you would like to be assigned.
	+ 1: Tier 1
	+ 2: Tier 2
	+ 3: Tier 3
	+ 4: Tier 4
	+ 5: Tier 5
* **Date of Change**: Choose the date and time when the change should take effect.
* **Time of Change**: Choose the time of day when the change should take effect.
* **Note**: Add any additional comments or notes about the request.
