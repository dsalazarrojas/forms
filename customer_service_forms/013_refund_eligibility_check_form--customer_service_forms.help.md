# refund_eligibility_check_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to check the eligibility for a refund for a customer's order. It gathers information from the customer about the reason for the refund, the order id, refund type, and other details to help customer support staff process the refund.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all the required fields with accurate information.
2. Select the correct refund type (Full Refund, Partial Refund, or No Refund) and provide the reason for the refund in the text field below.
3. Enter the order id of the order you are requesting a refund for.
4. Enter the customer's contact information, including name, email, phone number, address, postal code, country, city, state, zip, and time zone.
5. Enter the date and time of the refund.
6. Add any additional notes or comments about the refund request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer_service_form** (Text, Optional): This field allows you to enter a brief description of the customer's issue or concern.
* **refund_type** (Select One, Optional): Select the type of refund you are requesting (Full Refund, Partial Refund, or No Refund).
* **reason_for_refund** (Text, Optional): Enter a description of the reason for the refund.
* **order_id** (Number, Optional): Enter the id of the order you are requesting a refund for.
* **refund_amount** (Number, Optional): Enter the amount of the refund you are requesting.
* **customer_service_team** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the customer service team handling the refund request.
* **date_of_refund** (Date, Optional): Enter the date when the refund will be processed.
* **time_of_refund** (Time, Optional): Enter the time when the refund will be processed.
* **note** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the refund request.
* **email** (Email, Optional): Enter the email address of the customer.
* **phone_number** (Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the customer.
* **customer_name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the customer.
* **customer_contact_number** (Text, Optional): Enter the contact number of the customer.
* **customer_email** (Email, Optional): Enter the email address of the customer.
* **customer_phone_number** (Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the customer.
* **customer_address** (Text, Optional): Enter the address of the customer.
* **customer_postal_code** (Text, Optional): Enter the postal code of the customer.
* **customer_country** (Text, Optional): Enter the country of the customer.
* **customer_city** (Text, Optional): Enter the city of the customer.
* **customer_state** (Text, Optional): Enter the state of the customer.
* **customer_zip** (Text, Optional): Enter the zip code of the customer.
* **customer_country_code** (Text, Optional): Enter the country code of the customer.
* **customer_time_zone** (Time, Optional): Enter the time zone of the customer.
* **submitted_by** (Text, Optional): Enter the person submitting the refund request.

Note: The form is designed to collect data for customer support staff to process refunds. It's essential to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that the refund request is processed correctly.
