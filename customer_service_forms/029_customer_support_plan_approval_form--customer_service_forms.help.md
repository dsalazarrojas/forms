## customer_support_plan_approval_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The customer support plan approval form is used to document and track the approval process of customer support plans. This form is designed to be completed by support teams to provide visibility and updates on the status of customer support plans within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Complete the form in a new tab or window for each customer support plan you need to approve. This will ensure that each plan has its own separate form and updates can be easily tracked.
2.  Fill out the "Customer Support Plan" field with a brief description of the plan, including the support team's name, the dates of the plan's creation and completion, and any other relevant details.
3.  In the "Client Request" field, describe the client's request that the support plan is intended to address.
4.  Select the "Support Plan" options accordingly:
   *   If the plan is approved, select 'Yes'.
   *   If the plan is not approved, select 'No'.
5.  If the plan is approved, enter the date and time of approval in the "Approval Date" and "Approval Time" fields.
6.  Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the customer support plan is active or inactive in the "Customer Support Plan Status" field.
7.  Use the "Customer Support Plan Note" and "Client Request Note" fields to capture any additional information or comments regarding the support plan and client's request.
8.  Enter any comments or justifications for the approval or rejection of the plan in the "Approval Comment" field.
9.  Finally, click "Submit" to save your completion of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Customer Support Plan
*   <label>Customer Support Plan</label> (`Customer Support Plan`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to describe the customer support plan, including details about the support team, dates, and any other relevant information.

### 2. Client Request
*   <label>Client Request</label> (`Client Request`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to describe the client's request that the support plan is intended to address.

### 3. Support Plan
*   <label>Support Plan</label> (`Support Plan`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field is used to select whether the plan is approved or not. If the plan is approved, select 'Yes'; if not, select 'No'.

*   <label>Approval Date</label> (`Approval Date`, `date`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the date of approval of the customer support plan.

*   <label>Approval Time</label> (`Approval Time`, `time`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the time of approval of the customer support plan.

### 9. Client Request Note

### 10. Approval Comment

### 11. Customer Support Plan

### 12. Customer Support Plan Status
*   <label>Customer Support Plan Status</label> (`Customer Support Plan Status`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field is used to select the status of the customer support plan. Select 'Active' if the plan is active; 'Inactive' if not.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill out each field as accurately and concisely as possible.
*   Use the "Customer Support Plan Note" and "Client Request Note" fields to capture any additional information or comments that may not fit in the other fields.
*   Ensure that the "Customer Support Plan Status" field accurately reflects the current status of the plan.
*   Use the "Approval Comment" field to enter any comments or justifications for the approval or rejection of the plan.
