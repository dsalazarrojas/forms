# orthopaedic_hospital_patient_experience_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from patients regarding their experience at our orthopaedic hospital. The information collected will help us understand how we can improve our services to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply answer the questions honestly and thoroughly. You can type your answers in the text fields, select from the options for select fields, or check multiple options for select multiple fields. If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to ask.

* **Were you admitted to the hospital?** (`question_1`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select all the ways you found out about this hospital that apply.
	+ `yes`: The hospital's website, social media, word of mouth, or other sources.
	+ `no`: No, I did not find out about this hospital.
	+ `unknown`: Other (please specify in question 9).
* **How did you find out about the hospital?** (`how_did_you_find_out_about_the_hospital`, `select_one`, required): Please select one of the following options to answer this question.

	+ `No`: You did not find out about the hospital.
* **What was the purpose of your visit?** (`question_2`, `select_one`, required): Please select one of the following options to answer this question.
	+ `Yes`: I was admitted to the hospital for orthopaedic procedures.
	+ `No`: Not for orthopaedic procedures.
