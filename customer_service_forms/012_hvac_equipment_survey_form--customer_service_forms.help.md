# HVAC Equipment Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HVAC Equipment Survey Form is designed to collect information from customers about their HVAC equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields with the relevant information and submit it for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Heating Type**: Select the type of heating equipment you are using (Gas, Electric, or Other).
* **Installation Date**: Enter the date the equipment was installed.
* **Last Service Date**: Enter the last date the equipment was serviced.
* **Equipment Status**: Select the status of the equipment (New, Old, or Obsolete).
* **Condition**: Select the current condition of the equipment (Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Additional Notes**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the equipment.
* **Email**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name as the contact person.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address as the contact person.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact us at your email address provided.
