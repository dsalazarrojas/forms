# Ministry Volunteer Recruitment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals who are interested in volunteering for the ministry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. If you have a message or any additional information, please enter it in the "message" field. (This field is optional.)
6. Select your volunteer type by choosing from the options provided in the "volunteer_type" field.
7. Indicate your availability for shifts by selecting the days and times you are available from the "volunteer_shifts", "available_days", and "available_times" fields.
8. Confirm your availability by selecting "Yes" from the "available_duration" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (text)**: Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name (text)**: Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email (email)**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Message (text)**: If you have any additional information, please enter it here. (Optional)
* **Volunteer Type (select one)**: Indicate your volunteer type by selecting from the options provided.
* **Volunteer Shifts (select multiple)**: Select the shifts you are available to volunteer for.
* **Available Days (select multiple)**: Select the days you are available to volunteer on.
* **Available Times (select multiple)**: Select the times you are available to volunteer on.
* **Available Duration (select multiple)**: Confirm your availability duration.
