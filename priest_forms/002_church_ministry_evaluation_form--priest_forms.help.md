# Church Ministry Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Church Ministry Evaluation Form is designed to provide a framework for assessing the effectiveness of various ministry roles within a church. This form allows users to evaluate leadership, communication, and teamwork skills, as well as provide comments and recommendations for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
### General Guidelines

- Ensure you are evaluating the correct period of time (month and year or range) for the evaluation.
- Enter your name as the evaluator.
- Select the specific ministry role being evaluated.
- Answer all required fields accurately and honestly.
- Provide specific examples or reasons for your ratings and comments.
- Use the "Today's date" as the submission date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Evaluation period** (`Evaluation period`, text, required): This field is where you enter the month and year or range of the period being evaluated.
* **Evaluator name** (`Evaluator name`, text, required): Please enter your name as the person completing this form.
* **Ministry role being evaluated** (`Ministry role`, select_one, required): This is the specific ministry role you are evaluating, such as Pastor or Youth Leader.
* **Leadership effectiveness rating** (`Leadership effectiveness rating`, number, required): Rate the leadership effectiveness of the evaluated role using a scale of 1 (low) to 5 (high).
* **Communication quality rating** (`Communication quality rating`, number, required): Rate the communication quality of the evaluated role using a scale of 1 (low) to 5 (high).
* **Teamwork and collaboration feedback** (`Teamwork and collaboration feedback`, text, optional): Provide specific feedback on strengths and areas for improvement in teamwork and collaboration.
* **Program impact assessment** (`Program impact assessment`, select_one, required): Rate the impact of the evaluated role on the congregation using High, Moderate, or Low.
* **Recommended training or support** (`Recommended training or support`, text, optional): Suggest any additional training or support the evaluated role may need.
* **Overall comments and recommendations** (`Overall comments and recommendations`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or recommendations for the evaluated role.
* **Submission date** (`Submission date`, date, required): Use the current date as the submission date for the evaluation.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate in your evaluations and ratings.
- Provide specific examples or reasons for your ratings and comments.
- Review each field carefully to ensure you are evaluating the correct period and providing sufficient information.
- Ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
