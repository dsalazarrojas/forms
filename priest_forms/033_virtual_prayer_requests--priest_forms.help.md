# Virtual Prayer Requests - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Prayer Requests form is a tool for users to submit prayer requests to a church or place of worship. This form allows users to input their requests and relevant contact information, which will be recorded and potentially shared with priests or prayer teams.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Email Address** in the required field.
2. Write a brief **Message** describing your prayer request.
3. Provide the **Church** where you attend or would like to submit a prayer request.
4. If desired, write a **Message for Priest** to include additional details about your request.
5. If applicable, write a **Message for Visitors** to include a welcome message or additional information for visitors.
6. Enter the **Name of Visitors** if you are hosting an event or gathering.
7. Enter the **Name of Priest** who will be serving or praying for you.
8. Enter the **Contact Number** of the church or individual.
9. Enter the **Contact Email** of the church or individual.
10. Enter the **Contact Address** of the church or individual.
11. Enter the **Website** of the church or individual.
12. Enter the **Social Media** channels for the church or individual.
13. If desired, select the **Date of Visit** and **Time of Visit** for an event or gathering.
14. Enter the **Name of Guest** if you are visiting as a guest.
15. Enter the **Name of Host** if you are hosting an event or gathering.
16. Enter a **Message from Host** to include a welcome message or additional information for hosts.
17. Finally, submit your completed form to record your prayer request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Email Address**: Enter your email address to allow the church or place of worship to follow up with you about your request.
- **Email Address** (required): A unique identifier for each user to prevent duplicate requests.
- **Church**: Enter the name of the church or place of worship where you attend or would like to submit a prayer request.
- **Message for Priest**: Write a brief message to include additional details about your request.
- **Message for Visitors**: Write a welcome message or additional information for visitors, if applicable.
- **Name of Visitors**: If you are hosting an event or gathering, enter the name of the visitors.
- **Name of Priest**: Enter the name of the priest or individual serving or praying for you.
- **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of the church or individual for follow-up.
- **Contact Email**: Enter the contact email of the church or individual for follow-up.
- **Contact Address**: Enter the contact address of the church or individual.
- **Website**: Enter the website of the church or individual.
- **Social Media**: Enter the social media channels for the church or individual.
- **Date of Visit**: If desired, select the date of your visit for event or gathering.
- **Time of Visit**: If desired, select the time of visit for event or gathering.
- **Name of Guest**: If you are visiting as a guest, enter the name of the guest.
- **Name of Host**: If you are hosting an event or gathering, enter the name of the host.
- **Message from Host**: Enter a welcome message or additional information from the host.
- **Name of Guest** (optional): Enter the name of the guest if you are visiting as a guest.
- **Name of Host** (optional): Enter the name of the host if you are hosting an event or gathering.
- **Message from Host** (optional): Enter a welcome message or additional information from the host.
- **Contact Number** (optional): Enter the contact number of the church or individual.
- **Contact Email** (optional): Enter the contact email of the church or individual.
- **Contact Address** (optional): Enter the contact address of the church or individual.
- **Website** (optional): Enter the website of the church or individual.
- **Social Media** (optional): Enter the social media channels for the church or individual.
- **Date of Visit** (optional): If desired, select the date of your visit for event or gathering.
- **Time of Visit** (optional): If desired, select the time of visit for event or gathering.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and relevant information to help the church or place of worship serve you better.
- Use a clear and concise message to ensure that your prayer request is understood correctly.
- If you are hosting an event or gathering, make sure to include necessary contact information for visitors.
- If you are a guest, include your name and any other necessary information.
- This form is intended for prayer requests, so be respectful and considerate in your message.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the church or place of worship directly.
