# Baby Dedication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Baby Dedication Form is a document designed to capture essential information about your baby's details, family, and dedication ceremony preferences. This form helps us understand your commitment and promises as a parent/guardian and ensures that your baby's spiritual journey is well-planned and meaningful.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review each section carefully and fill out the required fields (marked with *) with accurate information.
2.  Enter the baby's name, date of birth, and parent/guardian details.
3.  Select the desired dedication date and type of ceremony (if applicable).
4.  Provide your commitment statement and specify important spiritual values you want to instill in your child.
5.  Choose godparents or sponsors (if applicable).
6.  Indicate whether you plan to hold a reception after the dedication ceremony.
7.  Confirm your agreement to participate in the dedication process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Baby's Name (2)** (text, *, Full name): Enter your baby's full name as it appears on their birth certificate or ID.
* **Baby's Date of Birth (3)** (date, *, format: YYYY-MM-DD): Enter your baby's date of birth.
* **Baby's Gender (4)** (select_one, not *, options: Male, Female): Select your baby's gender.
* **Parent/Guardian 1 Name (5)** (text, *, Full name): Enter your parent/guardian 1's full name.
* **Parent/Guardian 1 Email (6)** (email, *, format: example@example.com): Enter your parent/guardian 1's email address.
* **Parent/Guardian 1 Phone (7)** (text, not *, format: 123-456-7890): Enter your parent/guardian 1's phone number.
* **Parent/Guardian 2 Name (8)** (text, not *, Full name): Enter your parent/guardian 2's full name (if applicable).
* **Parent/Guardian 2 Email (9)** (email, not *, format: example@example.com): Enter your parent/guardian 2's email address (if applicable).
* **Church Name (11)** (text, *, Full name): Enter the name of the church where the dedication will take place.
* **Pastor or Minister Name (12)** (text, *, Full name): Enter the name of the pastor or minister leading the dedication ceremony.
* **Desired Dedication Date (13)** (date, *, format: YYYY-MM-DD): Select the date you desire for the dedication ceremony.
* **Your Commitment Statement (15)** (text, *, How will you raise your child in faith?): Write a brief statement expressing your commitment to raising your child in faith.
* **Important Spiritual Values to Teach (16)** (text, *, What do you want to instill?): Describe the spiritual values you want to pass on to your child.
* **Godparents or Sponsors Chosen? (17)** (select_one, not *, options: True, False, Unsure): Indicate whether you have chosen godparents or sponsors for your child.
* **Godparent or Sponsor 1 Name (18)** (text, not *, Full name): Enter the name of your first chosen godparent or sponsor (if applicable).
* **Godparent or Sponsor 2 Name (19)** (text, not *, Full name): Enter the name of your second chosen godparent or sponsor (if applicable).
* **Type of Dedication Ceremony (21)** (select_one, not *, options: Full dedication service, Special blessing, Infant baptism, Other): Choose the type of dedication ceremony you prefer.
* **Expected Number of Attendees (22)** (number, not *, format: 1-100): Estimate the number of people you expect to attend the ceremony.
* **Reception Planned? (23)** (select_one, not *, options: True, False, Unsure): Indicate whether you plan to hold a reception after the dedication ceremony.
* **Special Requests or Considerations (24)** (text, not *, Music, readings, traditions, etc.): Specify any special requests or considerations for the ceremony (e.g., music, readings, or traditions).
* **I understand and agree to participate in this dedication (25)** (select_one, *, options: Yes, I agree, No, I do not agree): Confirm that you understand and agree to participate in the dedication ceremony.

Note: Asterisks (*) indicate required fields, while other fields are optional.
