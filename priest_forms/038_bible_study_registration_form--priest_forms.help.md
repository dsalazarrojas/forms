# Bible Study Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to register individuals for Bible study courses or programs. It helps us understand your personal and educational background, course preferences, and learning needs. The information collected will be used to tailor the course content and format to best meet your requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer all the required questions (marked as "required") and provide as much detail as possible.
2. Select your preferred options for the course format, level, and duration.
3. Choose your motivation for taking the course and what you hope to achieve.
4. Specify your availability and preferred learning environment.
5. If applicable, mention any special needs or requirements.
6. Finally, provide any additional comments or questions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for course communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact number for communication.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth in Month - Day - Year format (e.g., 02/12/1990).
* **Parish or Church Affiliation** (`parish_affiliation`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your church or parish community.
* **Highest Level of Education** (`education_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose your highest level of education completed (e.g., High school, Some college, College degree, Advanced degree, Other).
* **Current Bible Literacy Level** (`bible_literacy`, `select_one`, required): Choose how well you know the Bible (e.g., Very familiar, Somewhat familiar, Limited familiarity, Very little, No knowledge).
* **Prior Bible Study Experience** (`prior_study`, `select_one`, required): Choose your prior experience with Bible study (e.g., Extensive study, Regular participation, Some experience, Little experience, No prior study).
* **Course or Class Name** (`course_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the course or class you are registering for.
* **Course Level** (`course_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose the level of the course (e.g., Introductory, Intermediate, Advanced, Professional certification, Other).
* **Preferred Course Format** (`course_format`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred course format (e.g., In-person classroom, Online/distance learning, Hybrid (mix of both), Self-paced, No preference).
* **Course Duration Preference** (`course_duration`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred course duration (e.g., Short-term (4-6 weeks), One semester, Full year, Multi-year program, No preference).
* **What Motivates Your Interest in This Course** (`study_motivation`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all the reasons that motivate your interest in this course (e.g., Personal spiritual growth, Deepen faith understanding, Better know Scripture, Prepare for ministry, Fulfill educational requirement, Community engagement, Intellectual interest).
* **Your Learning Objectives** (`learning_objectives`, `text`, required): Describe what you hope to achieve through this course.
* **Specific Topics of Interest** (`specific_topics`, `text`, optional): Mention any specific topics you would like to focus on.
* **Hours Per Week You Can Dedicate** (`time_commitment`, `select_one`, required): Choose how many hours per week you can dedicate to studying (e.g., Less than 3 hours, 3-5 hours, 5-10 hours, 10-15 hours, More than 15 hours, Flexible).
* **Expected Attendance Rate** (`attendance_expectation`, `select_one`, required): Choose how often you can attend classes (e.g., Every session, Most sessions, Regular attendance, Occasional attendance, Undecided).
* **Preferred Learning Environment** (`learning_environment`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you prefer to learn (e.g., Quiet individual study, Group discussion, Lecture and notes, Interactive activities, Mix of methods).
* **Location Preference (if applicable)** (`location_preference`, `text`, optional): Enter the preferred location for your study (if applicable).
* **Accessibility or Accommodation Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, `text`, optional): Mention any special requirements or needs.
* **Technology Access Available** (`technology_access`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your technology access (e.g., Full access, Limited access, No access, Will arrange).
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or questions you may have.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all required questions.
* Be as specific as possible in your answers.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask us.
* We will review your registration and be in touch with you soon.
