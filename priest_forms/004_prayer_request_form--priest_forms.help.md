# Prayer Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow users to submit a prayer request to be addressed by the form owners.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Go to the "Request Details" page and provide a clear and concise description of your prayer request.
2. On the "Full Name" page, enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
3. On the "Email" page, enter your email address where you would like to receive a response to your prayer request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Request Details** (`request_details`, note, required: false)
Enter a clear and concise description of your prayer request in this field. Please provide as much detail as possible to help the form owners understand your request.

### **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required: false)
Enter your full name as it appears on your identification. This will help the form owners to address your request correctly.

### **Email** (`email`, email, required: false)
Enter the email address where you would like to receive a response to your prayer request. This will ensure that you receive updates and responses to your request in a timely manner.

## Tips
- Please be as clear and concise as possible when submitting your prayer request.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at `form@gic.mx`.
- You can also check our <a href="https://www.example.com/check_requests">request status page</a> to see if your request has already been made.
