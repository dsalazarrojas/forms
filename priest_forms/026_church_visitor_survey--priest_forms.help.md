# Church Visitor Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Church Visitor Survey is designed to gather information about our church visitors' experiences, preferences, and opinions. This will help us understand what we do well and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions as honestly as you can. Please select the options that best fit your experience. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to our staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Your Name <label> (1. visitor_name, text, optional)
* Your name is for identification purposes only. If you prefer to remain anonymous, you may leave this field blank.

### Email Address <label> (2. email, email, optional)
* We will use your email address to follow-up with you, if needed. You can leave this field blank if you don't want to be contacted.

### Date of Visit <label> (3. visit_date, date, required)
* Please select the date of your most recent visit.

### Which Service Did You Attend <label> (4. service_type, select_one, required)
* Select the service you attended, such as Sunday Morning Traditional, Sunday Morning Contemporary, etc.

### Is This Your First Visit <label> (5. first_visit, select_one, required)
* Check if this is your first visit to our church.

### How Often Have You Visited <label> (6. visit_frequency, select_one, optional)
* If you have visited before, select the frequency of your visits.

### How Did You Hear About Our Church <label> (7. how_heard, select_one, required)
* Select how you heard about our church, such as friend or family, website, etc.

### How Far Did You Travel <label> (8. distance_traveled, select_one, required)
* Select the distance you traveled to attend our church.

### How Well Were You Welcomed <label> (9. welcome_rating, select_one, required)
* Rate the welcome you received from our staff and volunteers.

### How Would You Rate the Service <label> (10. service_rating, select_one, required)
* Rate the quality of our church service.

### How Would You Rate the Sermon <label> (11. sermon_rating, select_one, required)
* Rate the quality of our church sermon.

### How Would You Rate the Music <label> (12. music_rating, select_one, required)
* Rate the quality of our church music.

### How Would You Rate Our Facilities <label> (13. facility_rating, select_one, required)
* Rate the cleanliness and comfort of our facilities.

### How Was Your Children's Church Experience <label> (14. children_experience, select_one, optional)
* If you have children, rate their experience in our children's church programs.

### How Likely Are You to Return <label> (15. likely_return, select_one, required)
* Rate how likely you are to return to our church.

### Would You Recommend Our Church <label> (16. recommend_church, select_one, required)
* Let us know if you would recommend our church to friends or family.

### What Did You Like Best <label> (17. best_part, text, required)
* Share what you enjoyed most about your visit.

### What Could We Improve <label> (18. improvement_areas, text, optional)
* Share any areas we could improve.

### What Are You Interested In <label> (19. interested_in, select_multiple, optional)
* Select any areas of interest, such as membership information, Bible study, etc.

### Do You Have a Prayer Request <label> (20. prayer_request, text, optional)
* If you have a prayer request, share it with us.

### Any Additional Comments <label> (21. additional_comments, text, optional)
* Share any additional thoughts or comments you have.
