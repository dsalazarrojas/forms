# Christmas Mass Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Christmas Mass Survey form is designed to gather feedback from attendees of the Christmas mass event. This form helps us understand your overall experience, satisfaction, and suggestions for improvement. Your input is invaluable in helping us plan and organize future events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by providing your contact information**: Please fill in your full name and email address (if you'd like us to follow up with you).
2. **Choose your relationship to the parish**: Select your current status with our parish to help us understand your connection to our community.
3. **Select the mass you attended**: Choose all the masses you attended to help us understand your experience.
4. **Rate your overall satisfaction**: Choose how satisfied you were with the mass event.
5. **Evaluate the mass experience**: Rate the music quality, homily, liturgy, atmosphere, and decorations to help us understand the overall experience.
6. **Assess the logistics**: Rate the seating comfort, temperature, parking experience, and usher hospitality to help us understand the practical aspects of the event.
7. **Share your thoughts**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions for improvement in the text fields.
8. **Submit your feedback**: Once you've completed the form, click the submit button to send us your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (1)**: Provide your full name, which we'll use to contact you if needed.
* **Email Address (2)**: Enter your email address for follow-up communication (optional).
* **Parish Status (3)**: Select your relationship to our parish to help us understand your connection to our community.
* **Age Group (4)**: Select your age range to help us understand your demographic.
* **Mass Attended (6)**: Choose all the masses you attended to provide a comprehensive understanding of your experience.
* **Overall Satisfaction (7)**: Rate how satisfied you were with the mass event.
* **Music Quality (8)**: Evaluate the music and choir performance.
* **Homily Rating (10)**: Rate the quality of the homily.
* **Liturgy Rating (11)**: Rate the overall liturgy experience.
* **Atmosphere Rating (12)**: Rate the atmosphere and ambiance of the event.
* **Decorations Rating (13)**: Rate the quality of the Christmas decorations.
* **Seating Comfort (15)**: Evaluate the comfort of the seating arrangement.
* **Temperature Comfort (16)**: Rate the temperature of the event space.
* **Parking Experience (17)**: Rate the parking availability and experience.
* **Usher Hospitality (19)**: Rate the helpfulness of the ushers.
* **Will You Return (22)**: Indicate if you'll attend future masses.
* **Recommend to Others (23)**: Indicate if you'd recommend our mass event to others.
* **Best Part of Mass (24)**: Describe what you enjoyed most about the event.
* **Suggestions for Improvement (25)**: Provide any suggestions for enhancing the mass experience.
* **Additional Comments (26)**: Share any other feedback or thoughts about the event.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your responses, as your feedback is essential to helping us improve future mass events.
* If a field is optional, feel free to skip it or provide additional information if you'd like.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
