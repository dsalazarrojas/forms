# liturgy_attendance_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track attendance at liturgy services held on Sunday or Saturday. It helps to record the number of attendees and visitors present, and any additional information or comments about the service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form for each liturgy service held on Sunday or Saturday.
2. Answer each field according to the actual attendance:
   * For attendees, choose "Yes" for those present and "No" for those not present.
   * For visitors, choose "Yes" for those present and "No" for those not present.
   * Record the total number of attendees or visitors present in the "Total" fields.
3. Enter the date and time of the service in the respective fields.
4. Use the "Note" field to record any additional information or comments about the service.

* **Saturday Service (ID 2)** (text, text, not required): Record the title of the Saturday service.
* **Weekly Meeting (ID 3)** (text, text, not required): Record the title of the weekly meeting (if applicable).
* **Attendees 1-5 (ID 4)** (select multiple, select multiple, not required): Choose "Yes" for attendees present and "No" for attendees not present.
