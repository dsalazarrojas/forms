# Pastors Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for supervisors or administrators to evaluate a pastor's performance, providing feedback on their abilities, effectiveness, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Form Title** field with a brief description of the form's purpose and content.
2. In the **Description** field, provide a more detailed explanation of the form's content and what it aims to achieve.
3. Select the **Category** field to indicate the area or type of performance being evaluated (e.g., pastoral care, leadership, or administrative tasks).
4. Rate the pastor's performance using the **Form Rating** field (required). This field is used to gauge the overall effectiveness of the pastor's work.
5. Provide comments on the form's content in the **Form Comments** field (optional).
6. Rate the pastor's qualities or characteristics using the **Pastor Rating** field (optional). This field is used to assess the pastor's strengths and weaknesses.
7. Offer specific feedback on the pastor's performance in the **Pastor Comments** field (optional).
8. Enter the date and time the form was submitted in the **Date Submitted** and **Time Submitted** fields (optional).
9. Select the person who submitted the form in the **Submitted By** field (optional). If you are the submitter, select "Assigned".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (form_title, text, required/optional): A brief description of the form's purpose and content.
* **Description** (form_description, text, required/optional): A more detailed explanation of the form's content and what it aims to achieve.
* **Category** (category, text, optional): The area or type of performance being evaluated (e.g., pastoral care, leadership, or administrative tasks).
* **Form Rating** (form_rating, number, required): A score out of 10 indicating the overall effectiveness of the pastor's work.
* **Form Comments** (form_comments, text, optional): Comments or feedback on the form's content.
* **Pastor Rating** (pastor_rating, number, optional): A score out of 10 evaluating the pastor's qualities or characteristics.
* **Pastor Comments** (pastor_comments, text, optional): Specific feedback on the pastor's performance.
* **Date Submitted** (date_submitted, date, required/optional): The date and time the form was submitted.
* **Time Submitted** (time_submitted, time, required/optional): The date and time the form was submitted.
* **Submitted By** (submitted_by, select_one, optional): The person who submitted the form.
