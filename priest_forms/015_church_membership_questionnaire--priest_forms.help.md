# Church Membership Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from church members to help us understand their demographics, level of involvement, and opinions on church programs and services. Your input will help us improve our services and provide better support to our community.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. If you are unsure about any of the questions, please don't hesitate to ask for help or clarification.
4. You can save your progress and come back to it later if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Your Full Name** (`member_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official records.
- **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your church-provided email address or the one you use most frequently.
- **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Provide your phone number that you would like us to use for communication.
- **How Long Have You Been a Member** (`membership_duration`, select_one, required): Choose how long you have been a member of our church.
- **How Often Do You Attend Services** (`service_attendance`, select_one, required): Select how often you attend our services.
- **Are You Part of a Small Group** (`small_group`, select_one, required): Choose whether you are part of a small group or not.
- **Do You Currently Volunteer** (`volunteer_service`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you currently volunteer at the church.
- **Which Areas Do You Serve In** (`volunteer_areas`, select_multiple, optional): Select all areas where you serve or have served at the church.
- **How Would You Rate Your Spiritual Growth** (`spiritual_growth`, select_one, required): Rate your spiritual growth over the past year.
- **How Involved Do You Feel in the Church** (`church_involvement`, select_one, required): Indicate how involved you feel in the church community.
- **Have You Received Pastoral Care When Needed** (`pastoral_care`, select_one, required): Choose whether you have received pastoral care when needed.
- **How Would You Rate Our Sermons** (`sermon_feedback`, select_one, required): Rate our sermons.
- **Do You Feel Part of Our Church Community** (`community_feeling`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you feel part of our church community.
- **How Would You Rate Church Communication** (`church_communication`, select_one, required): Rate our church communication.
- **What Is Your Favorite Aspect of Our Church** (`favorite_aspect`, text, required): Write about your favorite aspect of our church.
- **What Areas Need Improvement** (`improvement_areas`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions or concerns about areas that need improvement.
- **What New Programs Would You Like** (`new_programs`, text, optional): Share your ideas for new programs you would like to see at the church.
- **Would You Recommend Our Church** (`recommend_church`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you would recommend our church to others.
- **Do You Have Any Prayer Requests** (`prayer_requests`, text, optional): Share any prayer requests you have, if comfortable doing so.
- **Any Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Share anything else you would like to communicate.
