# Church Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather feedback from church members and attendees to help the church understand their satisfaction with various aspects of the church's services and facilities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your name and email address if you wish to be contacted for follow-up.
2. Select your current membership status from the options provided.
3. Choose how long you have attended the church from the options.
4. Answer questions about your satisfaction with various aspects of the church, such as worship services, sermons, music, children's ministry, youth ministry, pastoral care, community feeling, welcome experience, communication, facilities, volunteer opportunities, small groups, church impact, and recommendation.
5. If you have suggestions for new ministries or prayer requests, feel free to share them in the text boxes.
6. Finally, add any additional comments or suggestions you might have.
