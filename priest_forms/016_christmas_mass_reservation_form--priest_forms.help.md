# Christmas Mass Reservation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Christmas Mass Reservation Form is designed to help you reserve a spot at our parish's Christmas Mass. This form helps us gather the necessary information to ensure we can provide you and your family with a comfortable and meaningful Christmas celebration. Please fill out this form to let us know your preferences and any special requests you may have.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fully read and review the form before filling it out**: Take a moment to understand each field's purpose and requirements.
2. **Fill out each section**: Complete each section carefully, following the instructions provided for each field.
3. **Submit the form**: Once you've filled out the form, click the submit button to reserve your spot.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parishioner Name** (`parishioner_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or driver's license.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address for reservation confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for urgent notifications.
* **Family Name** (`family_name`, text, required): Enter the name for your family reservation listing.
* **Preferred Mass Date** (`mass_date`, date, required): Select the date you prefer for your mass reservation.
* **Preferred Mass Time** (`mass_time`, select_one, required): Choose the mass time that suits you best.
* **Number Attending** (`number_attending`, select_one, required): Indicate the total number of people in your party.
* **Number of Children** (`children_count`, select_one, optional): If you have children under 12, please indicate the number of children.
* **Seating Preference** (`seating_preference`, select_one, required): Let us know where you would like to sit.
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, select_one, required): If you have any special accessibility needs, please let us know.
* **Mass Intention Request** (`intention_request`, select_one, required): If you would like to request a special mass intention, please choose an option.
* **Intention Name** (`intention_name`, text, optional): If you requested a special mass intention, enter the name of the intention.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Add any additional information or requests you may have.
* **Communion Preference** (`communion_preference`, select_one, optional): If you would like to receive communion, please choose an option.
* **Joining Choir** (`choir_interest`, select_one, optional): If you would like to sing during mass, let us know.
* **Volunteer Interest** (`volunteer_interest`, select_one, optional): If you would like to help during mass, please choose an option.
* **First Time Visitor** (`first_time_visitor`, select_one, required): If this is your first time visiting our parish, let us know.
* **Parish Registration Status** (`parish_registration`, select_one, optional): If you are a registered parishioner, please indicate.
* **Parking Needed** (`parking_needed`, select_one, required): If you need parking information, please let us know.
* **Special Notes** (`special_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional information or requests you may have.
* **Prayer Requests** (`prayer_requests`, text, optional): If you have any specific prayer intentions, please add them here.
