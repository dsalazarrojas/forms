# Parental Consent And Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parental Consent And Release Form is used to gather parental consent for a minor's participation in a program or activity. This form is required to ensure that parents or guardians provide permission for their child's involvement and to release the organization from liability in case of any issues that may arise.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review and understand the purpose of the form.
2. Check the "Parental Consent" field to indicate if you are granting or withholding consent.
3. Select your response to the "Release" question.
4. Enter your name in the "Guardian Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parental Consent** (`text`, required/optional): Check this box to indicate that you are granting or withholding consent for your child's participation in the program or activity.
* **Release** (`select_one`, required/optional): Select "Yes" to release the organization from liability, or "No" to indicate you are not releasing liability.
* **Guardian Name** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your name as a parent or guardian, so we can contact you in case of any issues related to your child's participation.
