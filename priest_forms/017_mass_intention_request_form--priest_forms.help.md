# Mass Intention Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mass Intention Request Form is a tool used to request the offering of masses for various intentions, such as deceased, living, dying, sick, or general intentions. This form is used to ensure that the information submitted is accurate and respectful.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Start by providing your first name and last name.
*   Next, enter your address.
*   Then, enter your city and state.
*   After that, enter your zip code.
*   Choose the type of mass you are requesting (Deceased, Living, Dying, Sick, or General).
*   Enter the date and time you would like the mass to be said.
*   Provide the location where you would like the mass to be said.
*   If desired, enter a dedication message for the mass.
*   Enter any donation you would like to make in support of the mass.
*   Finally, enter a note or any additional information regarding the mass.
*   Please enter the name and contact information of the person requesting the mass.
*   You can submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (text, Required: False): This field is for your first name.
*   **Last Name** (text, Required: False): This field is for your last name.
*   **Address** (text, Required: False): This field is for your address.
*   **City** (text, Required: False): This field is for your city.
*   **State** (text, Required: False): This field is for your state.
*   **Zip** (text, Required: False): This field is for your zip code.
*   **Intention Mass Type** (select_one, Required: False): This field is for selecting the type of mass you are requesting (Deceased, Living, Dying, Sick, or General).
*   **Intention Mass Date** (date, Required: False): This field is for the date you would like the mass to be said.
*   **Intention Mass Time** (time, Required: False): This field is for the time you would like the mass to be said.
*   **Intention Mass Location** (text, Required: False): This field is for the location where you would like the mass to be said.
*   **Intention Mass Dedication** (text, Required: False): This field is for entering a dedication message for the mass.
*   **Intention Mass Donation** (text, Required: False): This field is for entering any donation you would like to make in support of the mass.
*   **Intention Mass Note** (text, Required: False): This field is for any additional information or note regarding the mass.
*   **Name of the Requestor** (text, Required: False): This field is for the name of the person submitting this request.
*   **Phone** (text, Required: False): This field is for the phone number of the person submitting this request.
*   **Email** (email, Required: False): This field is for the email of the person submitting this request.

Note: The fields marked as "Required: False" are optional and can be left blank if not necessary.
