# Church Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from church attendees on their experience with the church's services, programs, and overall atmosphere. Your input will help us understand your needs and preferences, enabling us to make informed decisions to improve our services.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety, answering all questions to the best of your ability.
2. For optional questions, you may choose to provide any information you feel comfortable sharing.
3. Select the options that best match your experience and feelings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Respondent name** (`respondent_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name for the purpose of tracking your feedback, but it is not required.
* **Attendance frequency** (`attendance_frequency`, select_one, required): Select how often you attend church services:
	+ Weekly: You regularly attend services every week.
	+ Biweekly: You attend services every other week.
	+ Monthly: You attend services once a month.
	+ Occasionally: You occasionally attend services, but not regularly.
	+ First time visitor: This is your first time visiting the church.
* **Overall satisfaction** (`satisfaction_overall`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the church on a scale from 1 (low) to 5 (high).
* **Worship experience** (`worship_experience`, select_one, required): Select how satisfied you are with the worship and music:
	+ Very dissatisfied: You are extremely unhappy with the worship and music.
	+ Dissatisfied: You are somewhat unhappy with the worship and music.
	+ Neutral: You are neither satisfied nor dissatisfied.
	+ Satisfied: You are somewhat satisfied with the worship and music.
	+ Very satisfied: You are extremely satisfied with the worship and music.
* **Teaching quality** (`teaching_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of messages and sermons:
	+ Very poor: You found the messages and sermons to be of very poor quality.
	+ Poor: You found the messages and sermons to be of poor quality.
	+ Average: You found the messages and sermons to be average.
	+ Good: You found the messages and sermons to be of good quality.
	+ Excellent: You found the messages and sermons to be of excellent quality.
* **Community engagement** (`community_engagement`, select_one, required): Select how well the church supports your community needs:
	+ Not at all: The church does not support your community needs at all.
	+ Poorly: The church only partially supports your community needs.
	+ Adequately: The church somewhat supports your community needs.
	+ Well: The church supports your community needs fairly well.
	+ Exceptionally: The church exceptionally well supports your community needs.
* **Volunteer interest** (`volunteer_interest`, select_one, required): Would you consider volunteering for church activities?
	+ True: Yes, you are interested.
	+ False: No, you are not interested.
	+ Maybe: Maybe you might consider volunteering in the future.
* **Suggestions for improvement** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Provide any ideas or feedback for the church leadership.
* **Optional demographic information** (`demographic_optional`, text, optional): If you wish, provide any demographic information (age range, etc.) that you feel is relevant.
* **Consent to follow up** (`consent_contact`, select_one, required): Do you consent to follow up with you about your feedback?
	+ True: Yes, you consent.
	+ False: No, you do not consent.
