# Church Affiliation Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals seeking to affiliate with our church. It guides applicants through providing personal information, faith background, and background check consent.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the personal questions (fields 2-6) with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Choose your faith tradition and provide years of faith practice (fields 13-14).
3. Select the type of affiliation you are seeking (field 17).
4. Indicate your areas of ministry interest (field 18).
5. Provide a reason for seeking affiliation with our church (field 19).
6. If applicable, provide background information for references (fields 22-23).
7. Consent to a background check (field 21).
8. Confirm agreement with our church doctrine and values (fields 25-26).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (field 2): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (field 3): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (field 4): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Email Address** (field 5): Enter a valid email address for communication.
* **Phone Number** (field 6): Enter a contact phone number.
* **Street Address** (field 8): Enter your street address or mailing address.
* **City** (field 9): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State or Province** (field 10): Select your state or province of residence.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (field 11): Enter your ZIP or postal code.
* **Religious Tradition** (field 13): Select your faith tradition (e.g., Catholic, Protestant, etc.).
* **Years of Faith Practice** (field 14): Indicate how many years you have been practicing your faith.
* **Type of Affiliation Sought** (field 17): Select the type of affiliation you are seeking (e.g., Full membership, Associate membership, etc.).
* **Areas of Ministry Interest** (field 18): Select all areas that interest you for ministry (e.g., Teaching or education, Music or choir, etc.).
* **Why Do You Seek Affiliation with Our Church** (field 19): Provide a brief reason for seeking affiliation with our church.
* **Reference Name (1)** (field 22): Enter the name of a personal or professional reference.
* **Reference Contact Information (1)** (field 23): Enter your reference's contact information (email or phone).
* **Consent to Background Check** (field 21): Consent to a background check as part of the application process.
* **I Agree with the Church Doctrine and Beliefs** (field 25): Confirm that you agree with our church's doctrine and values.
* **I Agree to Conduct Myself in Accordance with Church Values** (field 26): Confirm that you agree to conduct yourself in accordance with our church values.
