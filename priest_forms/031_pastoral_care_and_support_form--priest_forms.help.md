# Pastoral Care And Support Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pastoral Care And Support Form is designed to help individuals seeking pastoral care and support from our institution. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form, and we'll do our best to provide the care and support you need.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information on pages 1 and 2.
2. Choose the type of pastoral care and support you need (e.g., Prayer, Guidance, Spiritual Counseling).
3. Provide your contact details, such as name, phone number, and email address.
4. Select a suitable date and time for the follow-up meeting.
5. Choose a location for the follow-up meeting.
6. Provide additional information about your preferences for follow-up meetings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Info (Page 1)
* **Name**: This is your name, which will help us address you correctly.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further discussions.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can communicate with you.
* **Additional Comments**: This is an optional field for any other information you'd like to share with us.

### Pastoral Care Needed (Page 2)
* **What Kind Of Pastoral Care Do You Need**: Choose the type of care and support you need (e.g., Prayer, Guidance, Spiritual Counseling).
* **Other (please specify)**: If you need something else that's not listed, type it in here.

### Follow-up (Page 3)
* **Would You Like To Schedule A Follow-up**: If you'd like to schedule a follow-up meeting, please select 'True'.
* **How Often Do You Think You Would Like To Meet With A Pastor**: Choose how often you'd like to meet with a pastor (e.g., Every Sunday, Once A Week Ongoing).
* **Additional Comments**: This is an optional field for any further information you'd like to share about your preferences.

### Additional Information (Page 4)
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: This is an optional field for any information about how you heard about our institution.
* **How Often Do You Attend Church**: This is an optional field for any information about your church attendance.
* **Additional Comments**: This is an optional field for any further information you'd like to share.
