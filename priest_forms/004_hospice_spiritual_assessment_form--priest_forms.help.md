# hospice_spiritual_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on a patient's spiritual well-being in a hospice care setting.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability, being as thorough as possible.
2. If you are unsure about any question, please leave it blank or provide a brief explanation for why it is not applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information**: This section is for providing basic patient demographic information, such as name and date of birth.
* **Family Information**: This section is for providing information about the patient's family members or support network.
* **Spiritual History**: This section is for describing the patient's spiritual history, including any significant events or experiences that have shaped their spiritual beliefs or practices.
* **Spiritual Supports**: This section is for listing any spiritual supports or resources that the patient finds helpful or comforting.
* **Religious Belief**: This section is for describing the patient's religious or spiritual beliefs.
* **Spiritual Practices**: This section is for listing any spiritual practices or activities that are important to the patient.
* **Spiritual Needs**: This section is for identifying any spiritual needs or concerns that the patient has.
* **Care Team**: This section is for listing any members of the care team who have been involved in the patient's care.
* **Hospice ID**: This section is for recording the patient's hospice ID number.
* **Assessment Date**: This section is for recording the date and time of the spiritual assessment.
* **Time of Day**: This section is for recording the time of day of the spiritual assessment.
* **Spiritual State**: This section is for describing the patient's current spiritual state, including any spiritual concerns or distress.

## Tips

* Please be as thorough and honest as possible when completing this form.
* If you are unsure about any question, please discuss it with the patient or their family member.
* This form is a tool to help guide the care team in understanding the patient's spiritual needs and preferences.
