# religious_ceremony_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to register a religious ceremony. It captures the essential details of the ceremony, including title, date, time, venue, officiant, ceremony type, and a brief description.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Review your responses for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Ceremony Title** (`title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title of the ceremony, such as "Baptism" or "Wedding".
* **Ceremony Date** (`ceremony_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the ceremony using the calendar picker or enter it manually in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Ceremony Time** (`ceremony_time`, time, optional): Select the time of the ceremony using the time picker or enter it manually in the format `HH:MM`.
* **Venue** (`venue`, text, optional): Enter the location where the ceremony will take place, such as "St. Mary's Church" or "Community Center".
* **Officiant** (`officiant`, select_one, optional): Choose the person authorized to perform the ceremony from the dropdown menu, which lists available options.
* **Ceremony Type** (`ceremony_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of ceremony from the dropdown menu, which lists available options.
* **Ceremony Description** (`ceremony_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the ceremony, including any special details or notes.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide any relevant contact information, such as a phone number or email address, for future reference.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to ensure accurate records.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the form's help documentation or contact the relevant authority for guidance.
* This form is for internal use only, so please keep sensitive or confidential information to a minimum.
