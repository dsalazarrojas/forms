# Church Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Church Incident Report Form is designed to collect information about incidents that occur within the church community. This data will be used to analyze and identify potential issues, improve incident response procedures, and provide support to those affected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information (fields 1-3).
2. Select the date and time of the incident (fields 4-5).
3. Choose the location where the incident occurred (field 7).
4. Select the type of incident (field 8).
5. List the individuals involved in the incident (field 9).
6. Indicate if there were any witnesses (field 10).
7. Provide a detailed description of the incident (field 12).
8. Report any injuries sustained (field 13).
9. Describe any injuries (field 14).
10. Indicate if medical response was provided (field 15).
11. Report any property damage (field 16).
12. Describe any property damage (field 17).
13. Describe the immediate response to the incident (field 18).
14. Indicate if authorities were notified (field 19).
15. Indicate if follow-up action is needed (field 20).
16. Provide any additional comments or suggestions (field 23).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Your Full Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification or membership card.
- **Your Email Address**: Enter your email address.
- **Your Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
- **Your Role in the Church**: Select the role you hold in the church (e.g. Staff, Volunteer, Member, etc.).
- **Date of Incident**: Enter the date of the incident (DD/MM/YYYY).
- **Time of Incident**: Enter the time of the incident (HH:MM AM/PM).
- **Location of Incident**: Choose where the incident occurred (e.g. Main Sanctuary, Fellowship Hall, etc.).
- **Type of Incident**: Select the category of the incident (e.g. Injury/Accident, Property Damage, etc.).
- **Names of Persons Involved**: List all individuals involved in the incident.
- **Were There Any Witnesses**: Indicate if there were any witnesses (Yes/No).
- **Witness Names and Contact Information**: If there were witnesses, list their names and contact information.
- **Detailed Description of Incident**: Provide a detailed description of what happened.
- **Were Any Injuries Sustained**: Indicate if any injuries were sustained (e.g. No injuries, Minor Injuries, etc.).
- **Describe Any Injuries**: Describe any injuries sustained.
- **What Medical Response Was Provided**: Describe the medical response taken (e.g. First aid, 911 called, etc.).
- **Was There Property Damage**: Indicate if there was any property damage (Yes/No).
- **Describe Property Damage**: Describe any property damage.
- **What Immediate Actions Were Taken**: Describe the immediate response to the incident.
- **Were Authorities Notified**: Indicate if authorities were notified (e.g. Police, Fire, etc.).
- **Is Follow-Up Action Needed**: Indicate if follow-up action is needed (Yes/No).
- **Suggested Follow-Up Actions**: Provide any additional suggestions or recommendations.
- **Do You Have Photos of the Incident**: Indicate if you have photos or videos of the incident (Yes/No).
- **Any Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
