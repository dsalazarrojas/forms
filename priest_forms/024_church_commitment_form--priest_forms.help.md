# Church Commitment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Church Commitment Form is designed to capture member commitments, including financial pledges and volunteer service commitments. This form helps the church understand and plan for the needs and expectations of its members.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form to ensure you understand what each field requests.
2. Fill out each required field as completely as possible.
3. If you're unsure about any field, contact the church administration.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (full_name): Enter your full name as it should appear on our records.
* **Email Address** (contact_email): Enter your email address for communication from the church.
* **Mailing Address** (mailing_address): Enter the address where you would like to receive mailings and documents from the church.
* **Financial Commitment Period** (commitment_period): Commit to a specific financial commitment period, such as calendar year or mid-year campaign.
* **Total Annual Pledge Amount (**) (total_annual_pledge): Enter your total annual pledge amount in US dollars.
* **Intended Giving Frequency** (giving_frequency): Select how frequently you intend to give, such as weekly, monthly, quarterly, or one-time.
* **Preferred Method of Giving** (payment_method_preference): Choose your preferred method of giving, such as online or personal checks.
* **Commit to Serve In Area(s) (volunteer_commitment): Commit to serve in one or more areas, such as greeters, children's ministry, or audiovisual tech.
* **Spiritual Growth Pledge** (spiritual_growth_pledge): Choose your commitment to regular attendance and spiritual growth, such as committing to participate in worship and community.
* **Prayer Commitment** (prayer_support): Choose your commitment to pray for the church leadership and mission.
* **Additional Goals** (additional_goals): Enter any personal spiritual goals for the year.
* **Envelopes** (request_giving_envelopes): Let us know if you'd like a set of giving envelopes mailed to you.
* **Date of Commitment** (date_of_commitment): Enter the date you are making this commitment.
Note: Some fields are optional, but filling them out will help us better understand your commitments and provide more personalized service.
