# Church Reopening Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Church Reopening Survey is designed to gather feedback from church members, visitors, staff, and volunteers on their comfort levels and preferences regarding the reopening of the church after the pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the form:

1. Respond to each question honestly, and provide as much detail as possible in the open-ended questions.
2. Choose your preferred service option (in person only, virtual only, or hybrid) and select all the safety measures that matter to you.
3. Provide your full name and affiliation with the church (member, visitor, staff, or volunteer).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Respondent name** (1): Enter your full name.
* **Affiliation with church** (2): Choose your relationship with the church: 
  * Member: I am a member of the church.
  * Visitor: I am a visitor or attendee of the church.
  * Staff: I am an employee or staff of the church.
  * Volunteer: I am a volunteer for the church.
* **Comfort level attending in person** (3): Rate your comfort level from 1 (not comfortable) to 5 (very comfortable).
* **Preferred service option** (4): Choose one option:
  * In person only: I prefer in-person services.
  * Virtual only: I prefer virtual services.
  * Hybrid: I prefer a mix of in-person and virtual services.
* **Importance of safety measures** (5): Select all the safety measures that matter to you.
  * Mask requirement: Wearing a mask is important to me.
  * Social distancing: Maintaining social distancing is important to me.
  * Hand sanitizing stations: Having hand sanitizing stations is important to me.
  * Limited capacity: Limiting the capacity of the church is important to me.
* **Suggestions for reopening** (6): Provide any suggestions you have for ensuring a safe and comfortable environment for everyone.
* **Will to volunteer** (7): Indicate your willingness to volunteer for reopening efforts (yes or no).
* **Contact information for follow up** (8): Provide your contact information (email or phone) so we can follow up with you.
* **Submission date** (9): Please ensure this form is submitted on the same day you complete it.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide as much detail as possible.
* If you have any suggestions or concerns, feel free to provide them in the open-ended question.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, please don't hesitate to contact us.
