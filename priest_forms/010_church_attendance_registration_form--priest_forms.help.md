# Church Attendance Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Church Attendance Registration Form is designed to collect information from visitors attending a church service, allowing us to better plan and prepare for their visit. Please ensure that the provided information is accurate to help us make the most out of your visit.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Attendee name**: Enter your full name.
2. **Email address** (optional): Provide your email address for any follow-up communication.
3. **Phone number** (optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
4. **Service date**: Select the date of the service you plan to attend.
5. **Service time**: Choose the service time you plan to attend. Please select between "Morning service", "Afternoon service", and "Evening service".
6. **Number in party**: Include everyone in your party, including yourself.
7. **Seating request** (optional): If you require any special seating arrangements, such as accessible seating, please specify.
8. **Purpose of visit** (optional): If you are visiting for a particular reason, such as the volunteer program or an event, please provide a brief description.
9. **First time visitor** (optional): Indicate if this is your first time visiting the church.
10. **Consent to use data** (optional): Grant us permission to use the collected data for registration and planning purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee name**: This is where you enter your full name.
* **Email address** (optional): If you want us to be able to contact you by email, please enter your email address. This is optional, but it helps with follow-ups and updates.
* **Phone number** (optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone for any reason.
* **Service date**: This is where you choose the date of the service you plan to attend. Make sure to select the correct date so we can prepare accordingly.
* **Service time**: Select the time you plan to attend the service, either "Morning service", "Afternoon service", or "Evening service".
* **Number in party**: Count everyone in your group, including children and infants, when filling this field.
* **Seating request**: If you need any special seating arrangements, let us know so we can accommodate your needs.
* **Purpose of visit** (optional): Describe why you are visiting the church, like for the volunteer program or an event.
* **First time visitor** (optional): Indicate if this is your first time visiting the church.
* **Consent to use data** (optional): Grant permission for us to use your data for registration and planning. This helps us to better prepare for your visit.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in the required fields (service date, attendee name, etc.) for a successful registration.
* If a field is marked as optional, feel free to leave it out if you do not want to provide it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask.
