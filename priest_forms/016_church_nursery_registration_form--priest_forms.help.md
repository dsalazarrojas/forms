# Church Nursery Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information about your child's details, medical conditions, and nursery-related preferences to ensure the church nursery can provide the best possible care for your child.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. If you are unsure about any information, do not hesitate to ask the nursery staff for clarification.
3. Please answer all the questions honestly to ensure the nursery staff can provide the best possible care for your child.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Child's Full Name** (`child_name`, Text, Required): Fill in your child's full name, first and last name.
* **Child's Nickname** (`child_nickname`, Text, Optional): Provide a nickname your child is often called.
* **Child's Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, Required): Enter your child's date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Gender** (`gender`, Select One, Required): Choose the correct gender of your child.
* **Parent/Guardian Full Name** (`parent_name`, Text, Required): Fill in your full name as the parent or guardian of your child.
* **Parent/Guardian Email** (`parent_email`, Email, Required): Enter your email address so the nursery staff can contact you if needed.
* **Parent/Guardian Phone Number** (`parent_phone`, Text, Required): Enter your best contact number for the nursery staff to reach you during service hours.
* **Home Address** (`home_address`, Text, Required): Enter your home address, including street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Emergency Contact Name and Phone** (`emergency_contact`, Text, Required): Provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact person.
* **Authorized Pick-up** (`authorized_pickup`, Text, Required): List all individuals authorized to pick up your child.
* **Does Your Child Have Any Allergies** (`allergies`, Select One, Required): Check if your child has any allergies.
* **Please Describe Allergies** (`allergies_detail`, Text, Optional): Describe your child's allergies if yes is selected above.
* **Does Your Child Have Any Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, Select One, Required): Check if your child has any medical conditions.
* **Please Describe Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions_detail`, Text, Optional): Describe your child's medical conditions if yes is selected above.
* **Is Your Child Taking Any Medications** (`medications`, Select One, Required): Check if your child is taking any medications.
* **Please List Medications and Dosages** (`medications_detail`, Text, Optional): List your child's medications and dosages if yes is selected above.
* **Special Instructions for Caregivers** (`special_instructions`, Text, Optional): Provide any special instructions for the nursery staff to follow for your child's care.
* **Does Your Child Need Diaper Changes** (`diaper_change`, Select One, Required): Check if your child needs diaper changes.
* **Will Your Child Need Feeding** (`feeding_needs`, Select One, Required): Check if your child needs feeding.
* **Does Your Child Nap Regularly** (`nap_time`, Select One, Required): Check if your child naps regularly.
* **Does Your Child Need Comfort Items** (`comfort_items`, Text, Optional): Provide any comfort items your child needs, such as a pacifier or a blanket.
* **Which Services Will Your Child Attend Nursery?** (`nursery_schedule`, Select Multiple, Required): Select all services your child will attend.
* **Illness Policy Acknowledgment** (`illness_policy`, Select One, Required): Check if you understand and agree to the nursery's illness policy.
* **Photo Release for Nursery Activities** (`photo_release`, Select One, Required): Choose if you grant permission for photos to be taken for nursery activities.
* **Parent/Guardian Signature** (`parent_signature`, Text, Required): Sign your name as the parent or guardian.
* **Date of Signature** (`signature_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date of your signature.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer all questions accurately and honestly.
* If you need help or clarification on any question, do not hesitate to ask the nursery staff.
* Please note that this form is a requirement for nursery service, and failure to submit it may delay your child's attendance in the nursery.
* If you have any questions or concerns after reviewing this help guide, please contact the nursery staff.
* Once you have completed this form, it will be kept confidential and only used to provide the best care for your child.
