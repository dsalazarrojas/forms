# Church Donation Receipt Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create and print a receipt for donations received by the church. It captures essential information about the donation, including receipt details, church and donor information, donation specifics, and acknowledgment and signature. By using this form, you can ensure accurate and standardized recording of donations for tax purposes and other administrative needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form fields carefully**: Each field is labeled with its purpose and is required or optional as indicated.
2. **Enter receipt information**: In the "Receipt Number", "Receipt Date", and "Receipt Time" fields, enter the corresponding details for the donation receipt.
3. **Provide church information**: Enter the name, address, and phone number of the church receiving the donation.
4. **Enter donor information**: Enter the donor's name, email, phone number, and mailing address.
5. **Specify donation details**: Choose the type, amount, currency, and category of the donation.
6. **Acknowledge the donation**: Select the appropriate acknowledgment of receipt option and sign the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Receipt Number**: A unique identifier for the donation receipt. (Required)
* **Receipt Date**: The date the donation was received. (Required)
* **Receipt Time**: The time the donation was received. (Optional)
* **Church Name**: The name of the church receiving the donation. (Required)
* **Church Address**: The address of the church receiving the donation. (Required)
* **Church Phone Number**: The phone number of the church. (Optional)
* **Church Tax ID or EIN**: The tax ID or EIN of the church. (Optional)
* **Donor Name**: The name of the person or entity making the donation. (Required)
* **Donor Email**: The email address of the donor. (Required)
* **Donor Phone Number**: The phone number of the donor. (Optional)
* **Donor Mailing Address**: The mailing address of the donor. (Optional)
* **Donation Amount**: The amount of the donation. (Required)
* **Currency**: The currency of the donation. (Optional)
* **Type of Donation**: The type of donation, such as monetary or in-kind. (Required)
* **Donation Category**: The category or purpose of the donation. (Required)
* **Donation Description**: A brief description of the donation if needed. (Optional)
* **Donation is Tax-Deductible**: A statement confirming the donation is tax-deductible. (Required)
* **Tax Receipt Statement**: A statement indicating the donation receipt is issued. (Optional)
* **Receipt Issued By (Name)**: The name of the person issuing the receipt. (Required)
* **Title of Authorized Person**: The title of the person issuing the receipt. (Optional)
* **Acknowledgment of Receipt**: A statement acknowledging the receipt of the donation. (Required)

Note: Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely to ensure the form is valid and useful for tax purposes and other administrative needs.
