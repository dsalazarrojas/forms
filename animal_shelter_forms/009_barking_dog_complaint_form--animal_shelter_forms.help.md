# Barking Dog Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals who are experiencing issues with barking dogs in their neighborhood or community. Please complete this form to help us understand the problem and work towards finding a resolution.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, please fill out all required fields (marked as "true" in the form schema).
*   For multiple-choice questions, select one option that best describes your experience with the barking dog.
*   For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as possible about your experience with the barking dog.
*   If you have any additional information that may be helpful in resolving the issue, please provide it in the "Additional Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Your Full Name** (`complainant_name`, text, **required**): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Your Address** (`complainant_address`, text, **required**): Enter your complete street address where you reside.
*   **Your Phone Number** (`complainant_phone`, text, **required**): Enter your best contact number for follow-up communication.
*   **Your Email Address** (`complainant_email`, email, **required**): Enter your email address for follow-up communication.
*   **Dog Owner Name if Known** (`dog_owner_name`, text, **optional**): If you are aware of the dog's owner, please enter their name.
*   **Dog Owner Address** (`dog_owner_address`, text, **required**): Enter the address where the dog is located.
*   **Your Relationship to the Property** (`relationship_to_property`, select_one, **required**): Select how you are affected by the barking dog, such as neighbor, adjacent property, or passerby.
*   **Description of Dog** (`dog_description`, text, **optional**): Provide any details about the dog, such as breed, color, or size.
*   **Number of Dogs** (`number_of_dogs`, select_one, **required**): Select the number of dogs that are barking.
*   **How Often Does Barking Occur** (`barking_frequency`, select_one, **required**): Select how frequently the barking occurs.
*   **How Long Does Barking Last** (`barking_duration`, select_one, **required**): Select the duration of each barking episode.
*   **Time of Day When Barking Occurs** (`time_of_day`, select_multiple, **required**): Select all the times of day when barking occurs.
*   **When Did the Problem Start** (`start_date`, date, **required**): Enter the approximate date when the barking problem started.
*   **Known Triggers for Barking** (`triggers_identified`, select_multiple, **required**): Select any triggers that you believe cause the barking.
*   **How Does This Affect You** (`impact_description`, text, **required**): Describe how the barking affects your daily life.
*   **Is Your Sleep Disturbed** (`sleep_disturbed`, select_one, **required**): Select if you are frequently or occasionally woken up by the barking.
*   **Do You Work From Home** (`work_from_home`, select_one, **required**): Select if the barking interferes with your work from home.
*   **Have You Contacted the Owner** (`previous_contact`, select_one, **required**): Select if you have spoken to the dog owner.
*   **Owner Response if Any** (`owner_response`, text, **optional**): If the dog owner has responded, describe their response.
*   **Do You Have Evidence** (`evidence_available`, select_one, **required**): Select if you have audio or video recordings, written logs, or witness statements that support your complaint.
*   **Desired Resolution** (`desired_resolution`, select_one, **required**): Select the desired outcome for resolving the issue.
*   **Request Confidentiality** (`confidentiality_request`, select_one, **required**): Select if you request that your identity be kept confidential.
*   **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, **optional**): Provide any other relevant details that may be helpful in resolving the issue.
