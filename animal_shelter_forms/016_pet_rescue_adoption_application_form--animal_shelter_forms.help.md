# Pet Rescue Adoption Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Rescue Adoption Application Form is designed to gather information from potential adopters and foster families about the pets they are interested in adopting or fostering. This form helps our team match the right pet with the right owner, ensuring a smooth and successful adoption process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Select the correct pet type from the options provided.
3. Enter your contact information, including your name and email address.
4. Provide your phone numbers (home and cell) for easy communication.
5. Fill out your address, including street address, city, and state.
6. Enter your zip code to ensure we can contact you efficiently.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Name** (`pet_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the pet you are interested in adopting or fostering.
* **Pet Type** (`pet_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of pet you are interested in adopting or fostering. Options include:
	+ Pet Cat
	+ Pet Dog
	+ Pet Bird
	+ Pet Fish
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the contact person for the application.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Provide your email address so we can contact you about your application.
* **Home Phone** (`home_phone`, text, optional): Enter your home phone number for easy communication.
* **Cell Phone** (`cell_phone`, text, optional): Enter your cell phone number for easy communication.
* **Street** (`street`, text, optional): Enter your street address to help us find you.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter your city to help us find you.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter your state to help us find you.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter your zip code to ensure we can contact you efficiently.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form completely and truthfully.
* If you are filling out the form for a foster application, please make sure to specify your foster status and provide any relevant information about your foster home.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to us. We are here to help.
