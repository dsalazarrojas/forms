# Animal Welfare Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help animal shelters, rescues, and caregivers evaluate the welfare of an animal in their care. It assesses various aspects of the animal's living conditions, including access to clean drinking water, nutrition, cleanliness of the living area, space for movement, veterinary care records, physical health, behavior, and overall welfare score.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Inspection Reference ID, which is a unique identifier for the inspection.
2. Enter the Animal Name or ID, which can be the animal's name or its identification number.
3. Enter the name of the shelter or facility where the animal is housed.
4. Answer the questions regarding the availability of clean drinking water, nutritional adequacy of food, cleanliness of the living area, space for movement, and availability of veterinary care records.
5. Describe any physical injuries or diseases observed and provide your behavioral observations.
6. Enter an overall welfare score out of 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
7. Provide any recommendations for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspection Reference ID**: A unique identifier for the inspection. This is a required field.
* **Animal Name or ID**: Enter the animal's name or identification number. This is a required field.
* **Shelter or Facility Name**: Enter the name of the shelter or facility where the animal is housed. This is a required field.
* **Access to Clean Drinking Water**: Answer whether the animal has access to clean drinking water. Options are:
	+ Yes - Readily available
	+ No - Not available
	+ Needs Improvement - Water is dirty or not available
* **Nutritional Adequacy of Food**: Answer whether the animal's food is adequate and nutritious. Options are:
	+ Yes - Appropriate diet
	+ No - Inadequate or spoiled
	+ Needs Improvement - Low quality food
* **Cleanliness of Living Area**: Answer the cleanliness of the animal's living area. Options are:
	+ Clean - Exceptionally well kept
	+ Satisfactory - Acceptable
	+ Dirty - Needs immediate cleaning
	+ Dangerous - Hazardous conditions
* **Space for Natural Movement**: Answer whether the animal has sufficient space for movement. Options are:
	+ Sufficient Space
	+ Restricted Space
	+ Insufficient Space
* **Veterinary Care Records Current**: Answer whether the animal's veterinary care records are up to date. Options are:
	+ Yes - All records up to date
	+ No - Records are missing
	+ Partial - Some records available
* **Observed Signs of Physical Injury or Disease**: Describe any visible health issues or physical injuries observed. This field is optional.
* **Behavioral Observation**: Answer whether the animal is calm and social, anxious or fearful, aggressive or reactive, depressed or lethargic. Options are:
	+ Calm and Social
	+ Anxious or Fearful
	+ Aggressive or Reactive
	+ Depressed or Lethargic
* **Overall Welfare Score**: Enter a score out of 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* **Recommendations for Improvement**: Provide any specific actions required to meet the standards of care. This field is optional.
