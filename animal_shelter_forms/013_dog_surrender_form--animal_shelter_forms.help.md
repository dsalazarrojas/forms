# Dog Surrender Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for dog owners to provide necessary information for a smooth surrender process. It includes contact details, dog's breed, reason for surrender, and other relevant information to ensure the dog's well-being and care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information for contact details, dog breed, reason for surrender, foster application, owner signature, veterinary signature, signature date, and signature time.
2. If applicable, provide additional information about the dog's medical condition, microchip information, previous adoption experience, adoption agreement, veterinary contact, owner information, shelter information, veterinary information, owner phone, veterinarian phone, owner address, and veterinarian address.
3. Review and submit the form to complete the surrender process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (Required): Please enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
* **Dog Breed** (Required): Select the breed of the dog you are surrendering.
* **Reason for Surrender** (Required): Explain the reason for surrendering your dog.
* **Foster Application** (Required): Indicate if you are interested in the foster application process.
* **Medical Condition**: If the dog has any medical conditions, please list them here.
* **Microchip Information**: Provide any microchip information, if applicable.
* **Previous Adoption Experience**: If you have adopted or fostered a dog before, please select the relevant option.
* **Adoption Agreement**: If you are interested in the adoption agreement, please provide details.
* **Veterinary Contact**: Provide the contact information for your veterinarian.
* **Owner Information**: Share any additional information about yourself as the owner.
* **Shelter Information**: Provide any relevant information about the shelter.
* **Veterinary Information**: Share any information about the dog's veterinary care.
* **Owner Signature** (Required): Sign the form to confirm your surrender of the dog.
* **Veterinary Signature** (Required): Sign the form to confirm the veterinarian's involvement.
* **Signature Type**: Select how you signed the form (wet or dry).
* **Signature Date** (Required): Enter the date you signed the form.
* **Signature Time** (Required): Enter the time you signed the form.
* **Owner Email** (Optional): Provide your email address.
* **Veterinarian Email** (Optional): Provide your veterinarian's email address.
* **Other Information** (Optional): Share any additional information about the dog or surrender process.
* **Owner Phone** (Optional): Provide your phone number.
* **Veterinarian Phone** (Optional): Provide your veterinarian's phone number.
* **Owner Address** (Optional): Share your address.
* **Veterinarian Address** (Optional): Share your veterinarian's address.
* **Notes** (Optional): Add any additional comments or information about the dog.
