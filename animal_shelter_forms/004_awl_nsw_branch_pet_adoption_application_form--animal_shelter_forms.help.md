# AWL NSW Branch Pet Adoption Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help the AWL NSW Branch understand your household and preferences for adopting a pet. It is a crucial step in the adoption process and will help our team to find the best possible match between you and a suitable pet.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read each section of the form and ensure that you provide accurate and honest information.
- Complete all required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact our team for guidance.
- Once you have completed the form, submit it to us and we will review your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Your Full Name** (`applicant_full_name`, text, `*`): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
- **Email Address** (`applicant_email`, email, `*`): Enter your valid email address that we can contact you with.
- **Phone Number** (`applicant_phone`, text, `*`): Enter your phone number that we can contact you with.
- **Residential Address** (`applicant_address`, text, `*`): Enter your street address, suburb, state, and postcode.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, `*`): Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
- **Type of Dwelling** (`dwelling_type`, select_one, `*`): Choose from House with yard, Townhouse, Apartment or unit, Rural property, or Other.
- **Do you own or rent your home?** (`own_or_rent`, select_one, `*`): Choose from Own, Rent, or Living with family.
- **Landlord permission** (`landlord_permission`, select_one, `*`): Choose from Yes, No, or Not applicable.
- **Is your yard fully fenced?** (`fenced_yard`, select_one, `*`): Choose from Yes - fully fenced, Partially fenced, Not fenced, or No yard.
- **Number of adults in the household** (`number_of_adults`, number, `*`): Enter the number of adults living in your household.
- **Number of children in the household** (`number_of_children`, number, `*`): Enter the number of children living in your household.
- **Ages of children** (`children_ages`, text, `**`): If you have children, enter their ages.
- **Pet type interest** (`pet_type_interest`, select_one, `*`): Choose from Dog, Cat, Rabbit, or Other small animal.
- **Specific animal name** (`specific_animal_name`, text, `**`): If you have a particular animal in mind, enter its name or ID.
- **Do you currently have any pets?** (`current_pets`, select_one, `*`): Choose from Yes, or No.
- **Current pet details** (`current_pets_details`, text, `**`): If you have current pets, describe them.
- **Previous pet experience** (`previous_pet_experience`, text, `**`): Describe your previous experience with pets.
- **Hours pet alone** (`hours_pet_alone`, select_one, `*`): Choose from Less than 4 hours, 4 to 6 hours, 6 to 8 hours, or More than 8 hours.
- **Exercise and enrichment plan** (`exercise_plan`, text, `*`): Describe how you plan to exercise and enrich the pet.
- **Preferred veterinarian** (`veterinarian_name`, text, `**`): If you have a preferred veterinarian, enter their name or clinic.
- **Agreement acknowledgement** (`agreement_acknowledgement`, select_one, `*`): Choose from Yes, I understand and agree, or Other.
- **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, `**`): If you have any additional comments or information to share, enter them here.
