# Puppy Adoption Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Puppy Adoption Form is designed to gather information about you and the puppy you wish to adopt. This includes your contact details, address, and information about the puppy's characteristics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields marked as "required".
2. Provide accurate and up-to-date information about yourself and the puppy you wish to adopt.
3. If you are not sure about a particular field, ask for help from the adoption team.
4. Double-check your form before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Home Address** (`home_address`, text, optional): Enter your current home address. This will help us contact you in case of emergencies or to schedule visits.
- **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your full name for adoption records.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where we can contact you for updates and adoption instructions.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for further communication and emergencies.
- **Puppy Name (if known)** (`puppy_name`, text, optional): If you have the puppy's name, please enter it here. If not, leave this field blank.
- **Puppy Age** (`puppy_age`, number, optional): Enter the age of the puppy you wish to adopt. This will help us match you with the right age group.
- **Puppy Breed** (`puppy_breed`, text, optional): Describe the breed of the puppy you are interested in adopting.
- **Puppy Weight** (`puppy_weight`, number, optional): Enter the weight of the puppy you wish to adopt. This will help us match you with the right size range.
- **Puppy Color** (`puppy_color`, text, optional): Describe the color of the puppy you are interested in adopting.
- **City** (`owner_address_city`, text, optional): Enter the city where you live.
- **State** (`owner_address_state`, text, optional): Enter your state or province for local regulations and matching purposes.

## Tips
* Double-check your form for any typos or incorrect information before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the adoption team for assistance.
* This form is for adoption purposes only and will not be shared with third parties.
