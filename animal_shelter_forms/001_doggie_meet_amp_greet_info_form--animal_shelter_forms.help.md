# Doggie Meet & Greet Info Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from pet owners and their pets to facilitate meet-and-greet sessions. It helps our team understand the pet's details and owner's preferences to provide a smooth and enjoyable experience for both.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer the questions based on your pet's information and your own preferences for the meet-and-greet session.
2.  Select the options that best apply to your pet and situation.
3.  Provide any additional notes or comments in the "Pet Notes" or "Owner Notes" fields if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **pet_info** (Select): 
    - Please indicate whether your pet is a yes or no, indicating that you're willing to share your pet's information.
* **pet_name** (Text): 
    Enter your pet's name.
* **pet_breed** (Select): 
    - Choose your pet's breed from the available options.
* **pet_age** (Number): 
    Enter your pet's age.
* **owner_name** (Text): 
    Enter your name.
* **owner_phone** (Text): 
    Enter your phone number.
* **meet_time** (Time): 
    Select the time you're available to meet.
* **sitter_info** (Select): 
    - Indicate whether you're willing to share information with our pet sitter.
* **pet_notes** (Note): 
    Add any additional notes about your pet that might be helpful for the sitter.
* **owner_notes** (Note): 
    Add any additional notes about yourself and your preferences for the meet-and-greet session.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact our team.
* This form is for information purposes only, and your input will help us provide a better experience for you and your pet.
