# rescue_animal_adoption_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "rescue_animal_adoption_application_form" is designed to gather information from individuals interested in adopting an animal from a rescue organization. This form helps the organization assess the applicant's suitability for adoption and ensures the best possible match between the applicant and the animal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your contact information, including your contact type, phone number, and address.
2.  Enter your email address, which will be used for communication.
3.  Optionally, provide your name (if you'd like to be known by a name other than your username).
4.  If you have a pet already, enter its name and type (e.g., dog, cat, etc.).
5.  Enter your pet's age.
6.  Add a message to express your interest in adopting an animal or provide additional information about your lifestyle and experience with pets.
7.  Click "Submit" to send your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information (contact_info)** (text, required): Enter your name and/or your preferred contact type (e.g., email or phone).
* **Contact Type (contact_type)** (select one, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Contact Number (contact_number)** (number, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your application.
* **Address (address)** (text, required): Enter your current address for our records.
* **Email (email)** (email, required): Enter a valid email address we can use to communicate with you about your application.
* **Name (name)** (text, optional): If you'd like to be known by a name other than your username, enter it here.
* **Pet Name (pet_name)** (text, optional): If you already have a pet, enter its name.
* **Pet Type (pet_type)** (select one, required): Select the type of animal you're interested in adopting (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Pet Age (pet_age)** (number, required): Enter the age of your pet.
* **Message (message)** (note, optional): Add any additional information about your interest in adopting an animal or your experience with pets.
