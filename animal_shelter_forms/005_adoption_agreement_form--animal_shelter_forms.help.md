This is a guide to help users understand the adoption agreement form. The purpose of this form is to collect information from adopters (individuals or families) about their adoption of an animal from an animal shelter. The form is used to ensure that both the adopter and the shelter are on the same page regarding the care and responsibilities of the adopted animal.

The form is used to collect essential information about the adopter, such as their name, contact method, and signature. The form also includes details about the animal being adopted, such as its name and date of adoption.

The fields included in this form are:

*   **Adopter Full Name** (`adopter_name`, text, required): The full name of the individual or family adopting the animal.
*   **Contact Phone or Email** (`adopter_contact`, text, required): The best contact method for the adopter.
*   **Pet Name** (`pet_name`, text, required): The name of the animal being adopted.
*   **Adoption Date** (`adoption_date`, date, required): The date of the adoption transfer.
*   **Agreement Terms Acknowledged** (`terms_and_conditions`, select_one, required): A confirmation that the adopter understands the care responsibilities.
*   **Return Policy Understanding** (`return_policy_understanding`, select_one, required): A confirmation that the adopter understands the shelter's return policy.
*   **Vaccination Record Received** (`vaccination_record_received`, select_one, optional): An acknowledgment of whether the shelter provided vaccination records for the adopted animal.
*   **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): The name and phone number of an emergency contact person.
*   **Adopter Signature Name** (`signature`, text, required): The name as it appears on the adopter's signature.
*   **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): The date of the adoption agreement.

Please note that the **Vaccination Record Received** field is optional and is used to record if the shelter provided vaccination records for the adopted animal.

# Adoption Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect information about the adopter and the animal being adopted, as well as to ensure that both the adopter and the shelter are on the same page regarding the care and responsibilities of the adopted animal.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields as follows:

*   Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
*   Provide your best contact method (phone or email).
*   Enter the name of the animal being adopted.
*   Enter the date of the adoption transfer.
*   Confirm that you understand the care responsibilities of the adopted animal.
*   Confirm that you understand the shelter's return policy.
*   If applicable, acknowledge if the shelter provided vaccination records for the adopted animal.
*   Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact person, if applicable.
*   Sign your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
*   Enter the date of the adoption agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Adopter Full Name**: The full name of the individual or family adopting the animal.
*   **Contact Phone or Email**: Enter your best contact method (phone or email).
*   **Pet Name**: The name of the animal being adopted.
*   **Adoption Date**: The date of the adoption transfer.
*   **Agreement Terms Acknowledged**: Confirm that you understand the care responsibilities of the adopted animal.
*   **Return Policy Understanding**: Confirm that you understand the shelter's return policy.
*   **Vaccination Record Received**: Acknowledge if the shelter provided vaccination records for the adopted animal.
*   **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact person, if applicable.
*   **Adopter Signature Name**: Sign your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
*   **Signature Date**: Enter the date of the adoption agreement.

## Tips

*   Ensure that you understand the care responsibilities of the adopted animal.
*   Review the shelter's return policy to be aware of the process.
*   If the shelter provided vaccination records for the adopted animal, acknowledge it.
*   If you have an emergency contact person, ensure that you provide their name and phone number.
*   Sign your name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
*   Enter the correct date for the adoption agreement.
*   Review and confirm the accuracy of all the information provided.
