# Rabbit Adoption Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rabbit Adoption Application Form is designed to be completed by individuals interested in adopting a rabbit from our animal shelter. This form helps us to understand your preferences and match you with a suitable rabbit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address in the respective fields.
2. Choose the type of rabbit you are interested in adopting (Bunny, Hopper, or Cute).
3. Select your preferred age range for the rabbit.
4. If applicable, choose any health conditions you would like the rabbit to be in (Healthy, Injured, or Sick).
5. Choose a date for when you would like to adopt the rabbit.
6. Select a length of time you would like to be able to adopt the rabbit for.
7. Enter a reason for why you are interested in adopting a rabbit from us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, **required**): Please enter your full first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, **required**): Please enter your full last name.
- **Address** (`address`, text, **optional**): Please enter your address so we can contact you for further information.
- **Email** (`email`, email, **optional**): Please enter a valid email address so we can contact you.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, **optional**): Please enter your phone number so we can contact you.
- **Type** (`rabbit_type`, select_one, **optional**): Select the type of rabbit you are interested in adopting (Bunny, Hopper, or Cute).
- **Age** (`rabbit_age`, number, **optional**): Please select the age range you are interested in for the rabbit.
  - 1-2 years: Young rabbits that are still in their juvenile phase.
  - 2-5 years: Adult rabbits that are fully grown but still active.
  - 5+ years: Older rabbits that are settled and calm.
- **Health** (`rabbit_health`, select_multiple, **optional**): Please select any health conditions you would like the rabbit to be in (Healthy, Injured, or Sick).
  - Healthy: A healthy rabbit with no known medical issues.
  - Injured: A rabbit that has been injured but is recovering.
  - Sick: A rabbit that requires medical attention.
- **Date** (`adoption_date`, date, **optional**): Choose the date for when you would like to adopt the rabbit.
- **Length** (`adoption_length`, time, **optional**): Please select the length of time you would like to be able to adopt the rabbit for.
  - 1-2 weeks: A short-term adoption.
  - 2-6 weeks: A medium-term adoption.
  - 6+ weeks: A long-term adoption.
- **Reason** (`adoption_reason`, text, **optional**): Please enter a reason why you would like to adopt a rabbit from us.

## Tips
- Please answer all fields to ensure we can find the best match for you.
- Be as specific as possible when selecting the type and age of rabbit you are interested in.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
