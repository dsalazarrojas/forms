# Dog Ownership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from potential dog owners to assess their eligibility and preparedness for dog ownership. It will help our team determine if you are a suitable candidate for dog adoption.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through each page and fill in the required information. You can select from multiple options in select fields and provide a brief description in text fields. Make sure to answer all questions truthfully and accurately to help us make the best decision for both you and the dog.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Info** (`pet_info`): Describe your pet, including its age, breed, and any relevant health information.
* **Pet Owner Info** (`pet_owner_info`): Provide some information about your pet, including its name, age, and breed.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): Enter your contact information, including your name, address, and phone number.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): Enter your phone number for follow-up contact.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address for follow-up communication.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`): Provide any additional information that you think will help us understand you better.
* **Pet** (`pet`): Choose whether you have a pet or not.
* **Owner Experience** (`owner_experience`): Indicate your experience with pet ownership, with options ranging from 0 to 20 years.
* **Owner Education** (`owner_education`): Select your level of education, from High School to PhD.
* **Owner Income** (`owner_income`): Enter your monthly income, if applicable.
* **Owner Living Status** (`owner_living_status`): Choose your living status, including Single, Married, Divorced, or Widowed.
* **Pet Living Status** (`pet_living_status`): Choose the living status of your pet, including Indoor or Outdoor.
* **Pet Age** (`pet_age`): Enter your pet's age.
* **Pet Breed** (`pet_breed`): Enter your pet's breed.
* **Owner Vehicles** (`owner_vehicles`): Indicate if you have a vehicle or not.
* **Income Frequency** (`owner_income_frequency`): Choose the frequency of your income, including Monthly, Yearly, or Quarterly.
* **Owner Housing Status** (`owner_housing_status`): Choose your current housing status, including Active or Inactive.
* **Owner Rental Status** (`owner_rental_status`): Choose your rental status, including Rented, Owned, or Mortgage.
* **Owner Income Source** (`owner_income_source`): Select the source of your income, including Self, Spouse, Partner, Parental Support, or Other.
* **Pet Health Insurance** (`pet_health_insurance`): Indicate if you have pet health insurance or not.
* **Owner Vaccination Status** (`owner_vaccination_status`): Indicate if you are up-to-date on vaccinations or not.
* **Owner Vaccination History** (`owner_vaccination_history`): Indicate if you have a vaccination history or not.
* **Pet License Status** (`pet_license_status`): Indicate if your pet has a license or not.
* **Pet Microchip Status** (`pet_microchip_status`): Indicate if your pet is microchipped or not.
* **Signature** (`signature`): Date and sign the form to acknowledge you have read and agreed to the terms.

**Tips**

* Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure a smooth process.
* If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask our team for help.
* We will review your form and get back to you with any further questions or concerns.
* Thank you for your interest in adopting a dog, and we look forward to hearing from you soon!
