# Pet Food Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about pet food requests to fulfill and track the needs of animals in our shelter. Please provide the necessary details to ensure that your pet receives the correct food.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required information on each field.
2. Choose the correct options from the select menus.
3. Enter the correct quantity and unit price of the food needed.
4. Indicate the contact method for follow-up communication.
5. Provide any additional comments about the request.
6. Finally, submit the form to send in your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Pet Food Request (`pet_food_request`)
* **Pet Food Request** (text): Please provide a brief description of the food your pet needs.
*   Required.

### 2. Pet Name (`pet_name`)
* **Pet Name** (text): Enter the name of the pet for which you are making a request.
*   Required.

### 3. Pet Type (`pet_type`)
* **Pet Type** (select): Choose the type of pet you are requesting food for. Options: cat, dog, bird, fish, other.
*   This will help us ensure we have the correct food available for your pet.

### 4. Food Type (`food_type`)
* **Food Type** (select): Select the type of food your pet needs. Options: dry, wet, grain-free, other.
*   This will help us choose the correct food for your pet.

### 5. Quantity (`quantity`)
* **Quantity** (number): Enter the amount of food your pet needs.
*   Please ensure you provide accurate information to avoid errors.

### 6. Unit Price (`unit_price`)
* **Unit Price** (number): Enter the price per unit of food you are requesting.
*   This will help us ensure we have enough funds for the requested food.

### 7. Total Price (`total_price`)
* **Total Price** (number): This field is automatically calculated based on the quantity and unit price you entered.
*   Do not enter a value as it will be filled in automatically.

### 8. Contact Method (`contact_method`)
* **Contact Method** (select): Choose how you would like to be contacted for follow-up communication. Options: email, phone, fax, other.
*   Please select a contact method to ensure we can follow up on your request.

### 9. Comments (`comments`)
* **Comments** (text): Provide any additional information about your request.
*   This will help us better understand your needs and provide the best service possible.

### 10. Submit (`submit`)
* **Submit** (select): Choose how you would like to submit your form. Options: submit form, Other.
*   Please select "submit form" to send in your request.
