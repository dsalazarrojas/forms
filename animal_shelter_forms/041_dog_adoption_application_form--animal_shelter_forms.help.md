# Dog Adoption Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals interested in adopting a dog from an animal shelter.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the "Applicant Information" field with your personal details.
2.  Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" field.
3.  In the "Adoption Information" field, mention any specific adoption requirements or preferences you have.
4.  In the "Pet Information" field, provide specific details about the pet you are interested in adopting.
5.  If you have any additional comments or requests, use the "Additional Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Applicant Information (id: 1)**: This field collects your personal details, such as your name, address, and any other relevant information.
*   **Contact Information (id: 2)**: This field is where you can provide your contact details, including your phone number and email address.
*   **Adoption Information (id: 3)**: This field is where you can mention any specific adoption requirements or preferences you have, such as the type of dog you are interested in or any allergies you may have.
*   **Pet Information (id: 4)**: This field is where you can provide specific details about the pet you are interested in adopting, such as their age, size, and breed.
*   **Additional Notes (id: 5)**: This field is optional, and you can use it to make any additional comments or requests you might have.

## Tips

*   Make sure to provide accurate and clear information in each field.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact the animal shelter directly.
*   The animal shelter will review and process your application as soon as possible.
