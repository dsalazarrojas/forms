# Dog Kennel Cleaning Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help kennel staff record and manage information about dog kennel cleaning tasks. This includes details about the kennel, the frequency of cleaning, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of animal being kept in the kennel (Cat, Dog, or Other).
2. Choose the size of the kennel (Large, Medium, or Small).
3. Select the frequency of cleaning (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely, or Never).
4. Choose the cleaning supplies used (Mop, Broom, Disinfectant).
5. Enter the date and time of the cleaning task.
6. Report the time spent on cleaning.
7. Enter any additional notes or comments about the task.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **kennel_details**: Enter a brief description of the kennel being cleaned.
* **animal_type**: Select the type of animal being kept in the kennel (Cat, Dog, or Other).
* **kennel_size**: Choose the size of the kennel (Large, Medium, or Small).
* **cleaning_frequency**: Select the frequency of cleaning (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely, or Never).
* **cleaning_supplies_used**: Choose the cleaning supplies used (Mop, Broom, Disinfectant).
* **date_and_time**: Enter the date and time of the cleaning task.
* **time_spent_cleaning**: Report the time spent on cleaning the kennel.
* **note**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the cleaning task.
* **assigned_tool**: Enter any assigned tools or equipment used for the cleaning task.
* All other fields are notes for additional comments or details, feel free to use as needed.
