# Dog Shot Record Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This template is used to record information about a dog's shot record, including details about the dog's health, the shot administered, and follow-up information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's information, including their name, owner's name, and any medical conditions they may have.
2. Record the date and time the shot was administered.
3. Specify the type of shot given.
4. If necessary, record any medical conditions or symptoms.
5. Note any medication administered.
6. Schedule a follow-up date and time.
7. Record the follow-up result and details.
8. Specify if the dog is an animal type that requires special handling.
9. Identify the type of shot given.
10. Identify the medical staff member who administered the shot.
11. Record any notes about the shot and follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Dog Shot Record**: This is the title of the form.
* **Patient Info**: Enter the dog's name and any relevant medical information.
* **Owner**: Enter the dog's owner's name.
* **Shot Date**: Enter the date the shot was administered.
* **Shot Time**: Enter the time the shot was administered.
* **Vaccine Administered**: Describe the type of vaccine or shot given.
* **Medical Condition**: If the dog has a medical condition, enter it here.
* **Symptoms**: If the dog is experiencing any symptoms, describe them here.
* **Medication Administered**: If any medication was given, list it here.
* **Follow Up**: Schedule a follow-up date and time.
* **Follow Up Date**: Enter the date of the follow-up.
* **Follow Up Time**: Enter the time of the follow-up.
* **Follow Up Result**: Record the result of the follow-up.
* **Follow Up Details**: Provide any additional details about the follow-up.
* **Animal Type**: Specify if the dog is an animal type that requires special handling.
* **Shot Type**: Identify the type of shot given.
* **Medical Staff**: Identify the medical staff member who administered the shot.
* **Shot Given By**: Enter the name of the person who administered the shot.
* **Notes**: Record any additional comments or notes about the shot and follow-up.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the contact phone number of the owner or veterinarian.
* **Email Address**: Enter the contact email address of the owner or veterinarian.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of the contact person.
* **Medical Notes**: Record any medical notes or comments.
* **Follow Up Instructions**: Provide any follow-up instructions for the owner.
