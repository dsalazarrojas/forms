# pet_owner_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from pet owners about their pets for an animal shelter.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the form in its entirety, ensuring you have answered all required fields (though some fields are not required, it's still beneficial to provide as much information as possible).
2. Select the correct pet type from the options provided.
3. Enter your pet's age in the designated field.
4. Provide your pet's breed (if known).
5. Describe your pet's color.
6. Enter your pet's weight.
7. Enter your owner's name.
8. Enter your owner's email address.
9. Enter your owner's phone number.
10. Provide a brief description of your pet's habit or behavior (be as specific as possible, e.g., playful, lazy, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Type** (`Pet pet_type`, select_one): Choose the type of your pet from the options provided (e.g., Pet dog, Pet cat, Pet bird, Pet fish, Other).
* **Pet Age** (`pet_age`, number): Enter your pet's age.
* **Pet Breed** (`pet_breed`, text): Provide the breed of your pet (if known).
* **Pet Color** (`pet_color`, text): Describe your pet's color.
* **Pet Weight** (`pet_weight`, number): Enter your pet's weight.
* **Owner's Name** (`owner_name`, text): Enter your name as the pet's owner.
* **Owner's Email** (`owner_email`, email): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Owner's Phone** (`owner_phone`, text): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Pet Habit** (`pet_habit`, text): Briefly describe your pet's habit or behavior (be as specific as possible).
