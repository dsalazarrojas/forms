# Pet Health Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Health Record Form is designed to collect and store essential information about the health status and medical history of pets in an animal shelter. This form helps staff keep accurate and up-to-date records, ensuring proper care and handling for the pets.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Pet Info** (`pet_info`, text, Required)
	* Please provide details about the pet, including its name, age, breed, and other relevant information.
2. **Medications** (`medications`, select_multiple, Required)
	* List the medications that the pet is taking, if any.
3. **Food Intake** (`food_intake`, number, Required)
	* Record the amount of food the pet should intake daily or weekly.
4. **Vaccination Info** (`vaccination_info`, time, Required)
	* Note the date and details of the pet's vaccination history.
5. **Behavior** (`behavior`, note, Required)
	* Provide any relevant behavioral notes about the pet.
6. **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_one, Required)
	* Identify any allergies the pet has.
7. **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, text, Required)
	* Record any relevant medical notes or observations.
8. **Vet Info** (`vet_info`, email, Required)
	* Enter the contact information of the vet who treated or diagnosed the pet.
9. **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, Required)
	* Record the contact information of the pet's owner or caregiver.
10. **Owner Info** (`owner_info`, select_multiple, Required)
	* Choose the owner or caregiver who is responsible for the pet.
11. **Pet Photo** (`pet_photo`, select_one, Required)
	* Choose the photo associated with the pet.
12. **Pet Description** (`pet_description`, text, Required)
	* Provide a description of the pet's appearance, breed, or any distinctive features.
13. **Medical History** (`medical_history`, select_one, Required)
	* Identify the medical history of the pet.
14. **Medical Status** (`medical_status`, select_one, Required)
	* Record the pet's current medical status.
15. **Weight** (`weight`, number, Required)
	* Record the pet's current weight.
16. **Medical Status Description** (`medical_status_description`, text, Required)
	* Provide a description of the pet's current medical status.
17. **Medical Notes Additional** (`medical_notes_additional`, text, Required)
	* Enter any additional medical notes or observations.
18. **Medical Status Update** (`medical_status_update`, date, Required)
	* Record the date of the last medical status update.
19. **Medical Status Last Update** (`medical_status_last_update`, date, Required)
	* Record the date of the last medical status update.
20. **Medical Status Last Update Time** (`medical_status_last_update_time`, time, Required)
	* Record the time of the last medical status update.
21. **Medical Status Last Update User** (`medical_status_last_update_user`, select_one, Required)
	* Choose the user who performed the last medical status update.
22. **Medication Schedule** (`medication_schedule`, select_multiple, Required)
	* List the medications scheduled for the pet.
23. **Medical History Update** (`medical_history_update`, text, Required)
	* Record any updates to the pet's medical history.
24. **Medical Status Last Update Reason** (`medical_status_last_update_reason`, select_one, Required)
	* Identify the reason for the last medical status update.
25. **Vet Name** (`vet_name`, select_one, Required)
	* Choose the vet who performed the last medical status update.

## Tips

* Always fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed.
* Review and update the form regularly to ensure the pet's medical status is up-to-date.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
* If unsure about any field, consult with a veterinarian or animal shelter staff member.
* Keep the form confidential and secure, as it contains sensitive information about the pet.
