# Pet Adoption Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Adoption Application Form is a preliminary step to get in touch with potential adopters and gauge their interest in adopting a pet from the animal shelter. It helps us understand your preferences and demographics to better assist you in finding your new furry friend.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your information and preferences. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as you would like it to be used by our staff.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number where we can reach you.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your current address so we can get in touch with you if needed.
* **How did you hear about us?** (`contact_method`, select_one, required: false): Tell us how you heard about the animal shelter.
	+ Select 'Yes' if you have already been in touch with us before, or 'No' if this is your first time.
* **Which type of pet are you interested in?** (`pet_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Let us know what type of pet you are interested in adopting. Select all that apply.
	+ Cats
	+ Dogs
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Desired Adoption Date** (`adoption_date`, date, required: false): Choose a date range when you would like to adopt a pet.
* **How do you plan to adopt the pet?** (`adoption_method`, select_one, required: false): Tell us how you plan to adopt the pet.
	+ Adoption from the shelter
	+ Foster care
	+ Private adoption
* **What type of pet are you interested in adopting?** (`adoption_type`, select_one, required: false): Let us know what type of pet you are interested in adopting.
	+ Cat
	+ Dog
* **Additional comments or questions?** (`contact_note`, note, required: false): If you have any additional comments or questions, feel free to let us know.
