# hedgehog_adoption_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help potential adopters provide information about themselves and their desired pet's details to facilitate the adoption process for hedgehogs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the User Info section with your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
2. Select your preferred contact method(s) in the Contact Preference section.
3. Choose the type of pet you are interested in adopting in the Pet Type section.
4. Enter the name of the pet you are interested in adopting in the Pet Name section.
5. If applicable, enter the age of the pet you are interested in adopting in the Age section.
6. Enter the date you would like to adopt the pet in the Adoption Date section.
7. Provide any additional notes or comments in the Notes section.
8. Enter your email address in the Email section.
9. Enter your phone number in the Phone section.
10. Enter your address in the Address section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info**: Please enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Contact**: Select one or more contact methods that you prefer. This may include phone or email.
* **Contact Preference**: Choose how you would like to be contacted by the shelter staff. You can choose to be contacted by phone, email, or both.
* **Pet Type**: Select the type of pet you are interested in adopting. Currently, the options are Hedgehog, Cat, Dog, or None.
* **Pet Name**: If you know the name of the pet you are interested in adopting, enter it here.
* **Age**: If you know the age of the pet you are interested in adopting, enter it here.
* **Adoption Date**: If you know the date you would like to adopt the pet, enter it here.
* **Notes**: If you have any additional comments or questions, enter them here.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your address.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your contact preference and contact information for accuracy.
* Be specific about the type and name of the pet you are interested in adopting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the shelter staff using the preferred contact method.
