# Animal Adoption Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animal Adoption Appointment Form is designed to help you schedule an appointment to meet our available animals for adoption. By filling out this form, you'll help us better understand your lifestyle, preferences, and needs, so we can match you with the perfect animal companion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal contact information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Choose a preferred appointment date and time that works for you.
3. Select the type(s) of animal you're interested in adopting.
4. Share information about your household, including the number of people living with you, whether you have children, and your current pets.
5. Describe your housing situation and any special requests or questions you may have.
6. Answer a few more questions about your lifestyle, experience, and preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`applicant_name`, required): Please enter your full name, including first and last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, required): Enter your email address so we can confirm your appointment and keep you updated on adoption progress.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, required): Provide your phone number in case we need to contact you directly.
* **Home Address** (`address`, required): Enter your street address, city, state, and zip code for our records.
* **Schedule Your Adoption Appointment** (`appointment_intro`, optional): This is a brief introduction to help you understand the adoption process and what to expect.
* **Preferred Appointment Date** (`preferred_date`, required): Choose a date that works best for you to meet our animals.
* **Preferred Appointment Time** (`preferred_time`, required): Select a time that suits you best, considering our available appointment slots.
* **Alternative Date** (`alternative_date`, optional): If your first-choice date is not available, please provide an alternative date.
* **What type of animal are you interested in adopting** (`animal_interest`, required): Select all types of animals you're interested in adopting. You can choose from Dog, Cat, Rabbit, Bird, Small animal (hamster, guinea pig), and Reptile.
* **Are you interested in a specific animal?** (`specific_animal`, optional): If you've seen an animal online or in person that you're interested in, please describe it here.
* **How many people live in your household?** (`household_members`, required): Enter the total number of people living in your household.
* **Do you have children under 18?** (`children_ages`, required): Indicate whether you have children under 18 living in your household.
* **Do you currently have pets?** (`current_pets`, required): Tell us about your current pets, including their type, breed, age, and sex.
* **Please describe your current pets** (`pet_details`, optional): Provide more details about your current pets, if applicable.
* **What type of housing do you live in?** (`housing_type`, required): Select your current housing situation, such as House with yard, House without yard, Apartment, etc.
* **Do you own or rent your home?** (`ownership_status`, required): Indicate whether you own or rent your home.
* **If renting, do you have landlord approval for pets?** (`landlord_approval`, optional): If you rent, please indicate whether you have landlord approval for pets.
* **Do you have a fenced yard?** (`yard_info`, optional): If you live in a house with a yard, please indicate whether it's fully fenced or partially fenced.
* **Have you adopted from a shelter before?** (`adoption_experience`, required): Share your experience with adoption, whether it's multiple times or once.
* **How would you describe your activity level?** (`lifestyle`, required): Choose a description that fits your activity level, such as Very active - outdoor enthusiast or Low activity - prefer indoor time.
* **How many hours per day are you away from home?** (`work_schedule`, required): Indicate how many hours you're away from home per day.
* **Do you have a veterinarian?** (`veterinarian_name`, optional): If you have a veterinarian, please provide their name.
* **Any special requests or questions?** (`special_requests`, optional): Share any special requests or questions you have about the adoption process.
* **How did you hear about us?** (`referral_source`, optional): Indicate how you found out about our shelter.
