# Puppy Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to gather information from an animal's owner regarding the adoption process. It collects details such as owner and pet information, adoption reason, and contact details for the shelter. This form is a crucial part of the adoption process, as it ensures that the shelter has all necessary information to facilitate the adoption process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure that all fields are filled in correctly according to their respective labels.
3. Review and edit your responses as needed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Puppy Owner Info

* **Puppy Owner Info** (`puppy_owner_info`, text, required: false): This field is where the owner provides information about themselves, such as their name and contact details.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the owner of the puppy.
* **Owner Phone** (`owner_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the owner.
* **Owner Email** (`owner_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the owner.
* **Puppy Info** (`puppy_info`, text, required: false): Provide any relevant information about the puppy, such as its breed, age, or medical conditions.
* **Puppy Name** (`puppy_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the puppy.
* **Puppy Age** (`puppy_age`, number, required: false): Enter the age of the puppy.
* **Adoption Reason** (`adoption_reason`, text, required: false): Explain the reason for adoption, if applicable.

### Page 2: Contact Information

* **Veterinary Contact** (`veterinary_contact`, text, required: false): Enter the contact information of the puppy's veterinarian.
* **Shelter Contact** (`shelter_contact`, text, required: false): Enter the contact information of the shelter staff.
* **Signature** (`signature`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of signature required, such as Shelter, Foster Home, or Rescue.
* **Date Of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the visit.
* **Time Of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the visit.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Provide any additional notes or comments.

### Page 3: Adoption Information

* **Animal ID** (`animal_id`, text, required: false): Enter the ID number of the animal.
* **User ID** (`user_id`, text, required: false): Enter the ID number of the user.
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the user.
* **User Phone** (`user_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the user.
* **User Email** (`user_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the user.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of application.
* **Application Time** (`application_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of application.
* **Shelter Name** (`shelter_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the shelter.
* **Shelter Phone** (`shelter_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the shelter.
* **Shelter Email** (`shelter_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the shelter.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of signature.

### Tips

- Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the shelter staff for assistance.
