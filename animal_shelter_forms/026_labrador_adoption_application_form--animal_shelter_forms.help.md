# labrador_adoption_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help users complete the Labrador Adoption Application Form correctly and efficiently. The form is intended to collect contact information, details about the pet, and adoption information from users who are interested in adopting a labrador.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Scroll through each page, starting with "Contact Info".
2. Fill out the required and optional fields with your information.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed before proceeding.
4. Review and submit the form once you have provided all necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Info** (Contact Info, text, required): Provide your name, email address, and phone number so that we can contact you.
* **Pet Info** (Pet Info, text, optional): Share any relevant details about your current pet, if applicable.
* **Adoption Details** (Adoption Details, text, optional): If you have any questions or concerns about the adoption process, please let us know here.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Make sure to proofread your answers before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
