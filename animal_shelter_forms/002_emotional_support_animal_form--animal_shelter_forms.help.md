# Emotional Support Animal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to document information about your emotional support animal, which is a living creature that provides emotional support and alleviates symptoms of a disability. You will need to provide details about your animal, such as its type and name, and your relationship with it as the owner. Your landlord or property manager will also be asked to confirm that they are aware of your emotional support animal and provide their consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of animal that provides you with emotional support.
2. Enter the name of your animal.
3. Enter your name and phone number.
4. Date of your disability diagnosis.
5. The name and contact information of your doctor who diagnosed you with the disability.
6. Confirm that you have your landlord's consent to keep the animal in your rental or living space.
7. If necessary, provide additional information about your emotional support animal.
8. Confirm that your landlord has agreed to keep the animal in your space.
9. Finally, sign and submit the form to confirm that the information provided is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Is this a cat** (`animal_type`): Select the type of animal that provides you with emotional support. This could be a cat, dog, or other animal.
* **Pet name** (`animal_name`): Enter the name of your emotional support animal.
* **Owner name** (`owner_name`): Enter your name as the owner of the emotional support animal.
* **Owner phone** (`owner_phone`): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you if needed.
* **Disability diagnosis date** (`emotional_disability_diagnosis_date`): Enter the date of your disability diagnosis.
* **Doctor's name** (`emotional_disability_diagnosis_provider`): Enter the name of the doctor who diagnosed you with the disability.
* **Landlord consent** (`landlord_consent`): Confirm that your landlord is aware of your emotional support animal and has agreed to allow it in your space.
* **Additional information** (`additional_info`): If necessary, provide any additional details about your emotional support animal that we should know.
* **Owner signature** (`owner_signature`): Confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Landlord signature** (`landlord_signature`): Confirm that your landlord is aware of and agrees with the information provided.

## Tips

* Make sure to complete this form accurately and thoroughly.
* If you need to provide additional information, be as detailed as possible.
* This form is designed to help us understand your needs and accommodate you better.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
