# # Dog Adoption Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to help us understand your household and pet preferences so we can find the perfect match for you and your family. It's a crucial step in the dog adoption process, and your cooperation is greatly appreciated!

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information, including your contact number and email address.
2. Share your household details, such as the number of members in your household and any pets you already have.
3. Give us an idea of your experience with pets, including any veterinary information.
4. Specify your pet preferences, including type, age, breed, size, color, and health.
5. Add any additional notes about your ideal pet.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Home Address** (1, text, optional): This field is where you provide the address where you live with your family.
* **Household Members** (2, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the members of your household.
* **Contact Info** (3, text, optional): This field is for you to provide your contact information, such as your phone number and email address.
* **Vet Info** (4, text, optional): If you have a veterinarian, please share their contact information so we can follow up with them.
* **Pet Experience** (5, text, optional): Share your experience with pets, including any relevant medical history or allergies.
* **Contract Terms** (6, note, optional): This field is for any additional comments or questions you have about the adoption contract.
* **Contact Number** (7, text, optional): Provide your contact phone number.
* **Contact E-mail** (8, email, optional): Share your email address.
* **Home Phone** (9, text, optional): If you have a home phone, provide the number.
* **Home E-mail** (10, email, optional): If you have a home email address, provide it here.
* **Mobile Number** (11, text, optional): Share your mobile phone number.
* **Mobile E-mail** (12, email, optional): If you have a mobile email address, provide it here.
* **Address** (13, text, optional): Provide your home address.
* **City** (14, text, optional): Share the city where you live.
* **State** (15, text, optional): Share the state where you live.
* **Zip** (16, text, optional): Provide your zip code.
* **Country** (17, text, optional): Share the country where you live.
* **Pet Info** (18, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the pet you're interested in adopting.
* **Pet Type** (19, select_one, optional): Select the type of pet you're interested in adopting (Dog, Cat, or Other).
* **Pet Age** (20, text, optional): Share the age of the pet you're interested in adopting.
* **Pet Breed** (21, text, optional): Specify the breed of the pet you're interested in adopting.
* **Pet Size** (22, text, optional): Share the size of the pet you're interested in adopting.
* **Pet Color** (23, text, optional): Share the color of the pet you're interested in adopting.
* **Pet Health** (24, text, optional): Share any health concerns or requirements for the pet.
* **Pet Notes** (25, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the pet you're interested in adopting.
