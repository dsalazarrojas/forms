# Dog Temperament Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dog Temperament Questionnaire Form is designed to collect information about a dog's behavior, socialization, energy level, health status, and other relevant characteristics.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Start by filling in the form with information about your dog.
*   Make sure to fill in all required fields (marked as 'required' in the form).
*   For fields that allow multiple selections (e.g. 'socializationStatus', 'behavior_issues', 'rescue_organization'), select all relevant options.
*   Be accurate and honest when providing information about your dog's characteristics and behaviors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **userInput**: This field is for any additional information about your dog that isn't captured elsewhere in the form.
    *   Enter up to 255 characters in this field.
*   **dogBreed**: This field asks about your dog's breed.
    *   Select 'Yes' if your dog is a pure-bred breed from a specific breed group (e.g. Labradors are a pure-bred breed).
    *   Select 'No' if your dog is not a pure-bred breed or doesn't fit into a specific breed group.
*   **socializationStatus**: This field assesses your dog's level of socialization.
    *   Select 'Active' if your dog is social and interacts well with strangers.
    *   Select 'Inactive' if your dog is less social or has difficulty interacting with others.
*   **energyLevel**: This field estimates your dog's energy level.
    *   Please provide any relevant information about your dog's energy level (e.g. 'low', 'medium', 'high').
*   **healthStatus**: This field is for any health issues or conditions that affect your dog.
    *   Provide any medical conditions or health issues that may impact your dog's care or behavior.
*   **behaviorIssues**: This field assesses your dog's behavioral issues.
    *   Select 'Yes' for any behavioral issues that may impact their adoption or care (e.g. 'barking', 'whining', etc.).
*   **rescueOrganization**: This field indicates if your dog was rescued from an organization.
    *   Select 'Yes' if your dog was rescued from a local animal shelter or rescue organization.
    *   Select 'No' otherwise.
*   **ownerName**: This field is for your name and contact information.
    *   Enter your name and any relevant contact information (e.g. phone number, email).
*   **contactInfo**: This field is for any additional contact information or notes.
    *   Enter any additional information that would be helpful for us to contact you.
*   **dogLicense**: This field is for your dog's current license status.
    *   Select 'Yes' if your dog is licensed, 'No' otherwise. 

## Tips

*   This form should be filled out for each dog separately.
*   Make sure to be accurate and honest when filling this form.
*   If you're unsure about a field, it's okay to ask for clarification.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
