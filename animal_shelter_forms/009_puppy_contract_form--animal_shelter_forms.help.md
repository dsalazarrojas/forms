# Puppy Contract Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Puppy Contract Form is used to document the terms and conditions of a puppy adoption contract, ensuring a clear understanding between the buyer and seller of the agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the buyer's first and last name.
2. Provide the buyer's email address.
3. Enter the buyer's phone number.
4. Enter the buyer's address.
5. Enter the puppy's date of birth.
6. Enter the date of the contract.
7. Review and sign the contract terms.
8. Choose the payment method (Visa, Mastercard, Amex).
9. Select the type of contract (Sales Contract, Adoption Contract).
10. Enter any contract notes or comments.
11. Enter the contract end date (the date the contract will end).
12. Determine the contract status (Active, Expired, Cancelled).
13. Choose the contract type (Puppy Sale, Adoption).
14. Enter the payment status.
15. Sign and confirm the buyer's agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, required): Enter the first name of the buyer.
* **Last Name** (Text, required): Enter the last name of the buyer.
* **Email** (Email, required): Enter the buyer's email address.
* **Phone** (Text, required): Enter the buyer's phone number.
* **Address** (Text, required): Enter the buyer's address.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, required): Enter the date of birth of the puppy.
* **Date of Contract** (Date, required): Enter the date the contract was agreed upon.
* **Contract Terms** (Text, required): Review and sign the contract terms.
* **Payment Method** (Select one, required): Choose the payment method (Visa, Mastercard, Amex).
* **Contract Type** (Select one, required): Choose the type of contract (Sales Contract, Adoption Contract).
* **Puppy Name** (Text, required): Enter the name of the puppy.
* **Puppy Type** (Select one, required): Choose the type of puppy (Bulldog, Poodle, Collie).
* **Breeder Info** (Text, optional): Enter any information about the breeder.
* **Seller Info** (Text, required): Enter information about the seller.
* **Buyer Info** (Text, required): Enter information about the buyer.
* **Contract Notes** (Text, required): Enter any contract notes or comments.
* **Contract Date** (Date, required): Enter the date the contract was agreed upon.
* **Contract End Date** (Date, required): Enter the date the contract will end.
* **Contract Status** (Select one, required): Choose the contract status (Active, Expired, Cancelled).
* **Payment Status** (Text, required): Enter the payment status.
* **Buyer Agreement** (Note, required): Sign and confirm the buyer's agreement.
* **Seller Agreement** (Note, required): Sign and confirm the seller's agreement.
* **Payment Terms** (Text, required): Enter the payment terms.
* **Buyer Signature** (Note, required): Sign the buyer's signature.
* **Seller Signature** (Note, required): Sign the seller's signature.
