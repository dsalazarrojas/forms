# Emotional Support Animal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Emotional Support Animal Form is designed to gather information about your emotional support animal to determine eligibility for assistance or accommodation. This form is used to collect details about your animal, your contact information, and any relevant medical documentation or notes from your healthcare provider.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category for your request (e.g., "Animal Shelter Forms" or "Other").
2. Enter a brief description of your pet.
3. Enter your name.
4. Enter your email address.
5. Enter your phone number.
6. Select the type of pet you have (e.g., "Dog", "Cat", or "Other").
7. Enter your pet's name.
8. Enter your pet's breed (if applicable).
9. Enter your pet's age.
10. If you have a mental disability, provide a brief description.
11. If you have a doctor's note or recommendation, enter your doctor's name and provide the note.
