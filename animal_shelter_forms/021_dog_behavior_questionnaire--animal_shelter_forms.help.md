# Dog Behavior Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dog Behavior Questionnaire is a simple form designed to collect information about a dog's behavior and characteristics. The form is intended to help animal shelters and rescue organizations better understand and care for the dogs in their facilities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Introduction**: This section is a note field for any additional comments or information you'd like to share about the dog.
2. **Dog Breed**: Select the breed of the dog from the options provided.
3. **Dog Age**: Enter the age of the dog in years.
4. **Dog Sex**: Select the sex of the dog from the options provided.
5. **Behavior Score**: Select one or more of the options to indicate the dog's behavior, such as:
	* 0 - The dog is well-behaved and easy-going.
	* 1 - The dog has some behavioral issues, but is still trainable.
	* 2 - The dog has significant behavioral issues and may require special attention.
6. **Training History**: Select one or more of the options to indicate the dog's training history, such as:
	* Basic Obedience - The dog has basic obedience training.
	* Advanced Obedience - The dog has advanced obedience training.
	* No Formal Training - The dog has not received formal training.
7. **Veterinary Visits**: Select one or more of the options to indicate the frequency of veterinary visits, such as:
	* More than 3 times a year - The dog visits the vet more than 3 times per year.
	* Every 3 months - The dog visits the vet every 3 months.
	* Every 6 months - The dog visits the vet every 6 months.
	* Less than once a year - The dog has not visited the vet in over a year.
8. **Owner Info**: This section is a note field for any additional comments or information you'd like to share about the owner or the dog's history.
9. **Dog Info**: Select one or more of the options to indicate any characteristics or issues with the dog, such as:
	* Bullying - The dog bullies other dogs or people.
	* Fearful - The dog is fearful or anxious.
	* Independent - The dog is independent or aloof.
	* Aggressive - The dog has aggressive behavior.
	* Submissive - The dog is submissive or timid.

## Tips
* Please select only the options that are relevant to the dog's behavior and history.
* If you're unsure about any of the options, please select "Other" and provide additional comments or context.
* If you're concerned about a specific issue or characteristic of the dog, please provide additional comments or context in the "owner info" or "dog info" fields.
