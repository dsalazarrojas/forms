# Puppy Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Puppy Questionnaire is designed to gather information about a newly adopted puppy, including details about the puppy's age, breed, weight, and medical history.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, fill in the required fields, and we will guide you through each section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Puppy Form (<type:text>):** Enter a brief description of your new puppy.
* **Owner Info (<type:text>):** Enter your contact information so we can stay in touch with you.
* **Name (<type:text>):** Enter your puppy's name.
* **Age (<type:number>):** Enter your puppy's age.
* **Breed (<type:text>):** Enter your puppy's breed.
* **Weight (<type:number>):** Enter your puppy's weight.
* **Shelter Info (<type:text>):** Enter any relevant information about your puppy's shelter history.
* **Adoption Date (<type:date>):** Enter the date of your adoption.
* **Contact Info (<type:text>):** Enter your contact information for follow-up.
* **Medical Conditions (<type:text>):** Enter any medical conditions or allergies your puppy may have.
* **Veterinarian Notes (<type:text>):** Enter any notes from your veterinarian about your puppy's health.
* **Vaccination History (<type:text>):** Enter your puppy's vaccination history.
* **Training History (<type:text>):** Enter any relevant training history about your puppy.
* **Microchip Serial Number (<type:text>):** Enter your puppy's microchip serial number.
* **Microchip Registration (<type:text>):** Enter your puppy's microchip registration.
* **Microchip Company (<type:text>):** Enter the microchip company that registered your puppy.
* **Microchip Type (<type:text>):** Enter the type of microchip your puppy has.
* **Microchip Number (<type:text>):** Enter your puppy's microchip number.
* **Microchip Serial Number Copy (<type:text>):** Enter a copy of your puppy's microchip serial number.
* **Microchip Registration Copy (<type:text>):** Enter a copy of your puppy's microchip registration.
* **Microchip Type Copy (<type:text>):** Enter a copy of your puppy's microchip type.
* **Notes (<type:text>):** Enter any additional notes about your puppy.
* **Adoption Process (<type:text>):** Enter a brief description of your adoption process.
* **Adoption Process Description (<type:text>):** Enter a detailed description of your adoption process.
