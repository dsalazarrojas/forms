# Cat Adoption Contract Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide will walk you through the process of completing the cat adoption contract form. The form is designed to collect essential information about the adopter, the cat, and the terms of the adoption.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2.  Ensure that you have permission from the adopter to access their information.
3.  Review each field carefully and provide the requested information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Adopter Full Name**: Enter the legal name of the adopter.
*   **Email Address**: Enter the adopter's email address.
*   **Phone Number**: Enter the adopter's phone number.
*   **Full Address**: Enter the complete residential address of the adopter.
*   **Cat Name**: Enter the name of the cat being adopted.
*   **Cat ID Number**: Enter the shelter's identification number for the cat.
*   **Cat Breed**: Enter the breed or mix of the cat.
*   **Cat Color**: Enter the primary color and any notable markings on the cat.
*   **Cat Age**: Enter the age of the cat in years or months.
*   **Cat Gender**: Select the gender of the cat.
*   **Adoption Fee Paid**: Enter the amount paid for the adoption.
*   **Payment Date**: Enter the date of payment.
*   **Payment Method**: Select the method of payment.
*   **Microchip Number**: Enter the microchip number if applicable.
*   **Vaccination Status**: Select the current vaccination status of the cat.
*   **Spayed or Neutered**: Select whether the cat has been spayed or neutered.
*   **Known Medical Conditions**: Enter any known medical conditions of the cat.
*   **Agreement to Provide Proper Care**: Select "Yes - I Agree" to confirm that you will provide proper care for the cat.
*   **Agreement to Veterinary Care**: Select "Yes - I Agree" to confirm that you will provide veterinary care for the cat.
*   **Agreement to Return if Needed**: Select "Yes - I Agree" to confirm that you will return the cat if unable to keep it.
*   **Shelter Representative Name**: Enter the name of the staff member who handled the adoption.
*   **Contract Date**: Enter the date of contract signing.
*   **Adopter Signature Date**: Enter the date of the adopter's signature.

Note: The fields marked as "required" are mandatory and must be completed before proceeding with the adoption.
