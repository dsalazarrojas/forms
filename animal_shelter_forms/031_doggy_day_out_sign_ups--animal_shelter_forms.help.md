# Doggy Day Out Sign Ups - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for dog owners to sign up for the "Doggy Day Out" event. It collects necessary information for event organization and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information, including your name and email address.
2. Choose a date for the event you'd like to sign up for.
3. Select the start and end times of the event.
4. Provide details about your dog, including its name and any necessary information.
5. Enter your information as the dog's owner.
6. Choose a time to sign up for the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false):** Enter your name and email address to help us get in touch with you about the event.
* **Sign Up Date (`sign_up_date`, `date`, required: false):** Choose the date you'd like to sign up for the event. Please select a date from the available options.
* **Start Time (`start_time`, `time`, required: false):** Select the start time of the event. This is the time when the event will begin.
* **End Time (`end_time`, `time`, required: false):** Choose the end time of the event. This is the time when the event will conclude.
* **Dog Information (`dog_info`, `text`, required: false):** Enter details about your dog, including its name and any relevant information.
* **Owner Information (`owner_info`, `text`, required: false):** Enter details about you as the dog's owner, including your name and any necessary information.
* **Sign Up Time (`sign_up_time`, `date`, required: false):** Choose a time to sign up for the event. This is when you'll be attending the event with your dog.
