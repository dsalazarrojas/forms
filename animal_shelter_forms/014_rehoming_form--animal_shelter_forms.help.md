# Rehoming Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about an animal being surrendered to an animal shelter. It helps the shelter understand the reasons for surrendering the animal and ensures a smooth rehoming process.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting the form, please make sure you have all the necessary information and documents ready. Follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) as you would in any typical form.
2. Be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling in the fields, especially when describing the animal and its condition.
3. If you're unsure about any field, you can leave a note in the "Additional Notes" field for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (*required*): Enter the first name of the owner.
* **Last Name** (*required*): Enter the last name of the owner.
* **Contact Email** (*required*): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Animal Type** (*required*): Select the type of animal being surrendered (Cat, Dog, or Other).
* **Reason for Rehoming** (*required*): Explain the reason for surrendering the animal.
* **Pet Name** (*required*): Enter the name of the animal being surrendered.
* **Pet Age** (*required*): Enter the age of the animal.
* **Pet Description** (*required*): Describe the animal's breed, sex, size, and any distinctive features.
* **Owner Signature** (*required*): Sign the form to confirm the information provided.
* **Vet Certificate** (optional): If the animal has a vet certificate, attach it or provide a copy.
* **Photo Permission** (*required*): Indicate whether you grant permission for the shelter to use photos of the animal.
* **Adoption Agreement** (*required*): Check the box if you are willing to adopt out the animal.
* **Surrender Form Signed** (*required*): Check the box if you have signed the surrender form.
* **Date of Adoption** (*required*): Enter the date the animal was adopted.
* **Date of Surrender** (*required*): Enter the date the animal is being surrendered.
* **Signature of Owner** (*required*): Sign the form as the owner of the animal.
* **Signature of Surrenderer** (*required*): Sign the form as the person surrendering the animal.
* **Photo of Pet** (*required*): Attach a photo of the animal.
* **Notes** (optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the animal or surrender process.
* **Additional Notes** (optional): Add any other information that may be helpful for the shelter.
* **Photo of Owner** (optional): Attach a photo of the owner.
* **Photo of Surrenderer** (optional): Attach a photo of the person surrendering the animal.
* **Photo of Animal** (optional): Attach a photo of the animal.
* **Photo of Surrender Location** (optional): Attach a photo of the location where the animal is being surrendered.
* **Photo of Surrender Vehicle** (optional): Attach a photo of the vehicle being used for the surrender process.

Remember to review the form carefully before submission and ensure you have provided all the necessary information and documents.
