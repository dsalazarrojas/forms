# SPCA Adoption Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SPCA Adoption Contract is a form used by the shelter to gather information about the adopter and contract details related to the adoption process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked as "required" in the form. This will include:
	* Contract Details (field 4)
2. Fill in any additional fields as required by the shelter or if you feel necessary.
3. Sign and date the Adopter Signature 2 field (field 5) to indicate your agreement to the adoption contract.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contract Details (Field 4)**: This field is required and must be filled in with the details of the adoption contract, including any specific terms and conditions agreed upon between the adopter and the shelter.
* **Adopter Info (Field 1)**: Provide your name, address, and contact information.
* **Adopter Info 2 (Field 2)**: Provide any additional information about yourself that the shelter may need to know.
* **Animal Info (Field 3)**: Provide information about the animal being adopted, such as its species, breed, age, and any medical conditions.
* **Adopter Signature 2 (Field 5)**: Sign and date this field to indicate your agreement to the adoption contract.
