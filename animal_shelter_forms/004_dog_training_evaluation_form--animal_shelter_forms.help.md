# Dog Training Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dog Training Evaluation Form is designed to help assess the behavior and training needs of dogs in our animal shelter. This form is used to gather information from our animal shelter staff and foster parents about the dog's personality, behavior, and training level. The information collected will help us provide the best possible care and training for our furry friends.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the appropriate training level for the dog from the options provided in the "training_needs" field.
2.  Provide a brief written assessment of the dog's behavior in the "behavior_assessment" field.
3.  Rate the dog's behavior from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Needs Improvement" and 5 is "Excellent".
4.  Save your changes and submit the form to complete the evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dog Training Form** (`dog_training_form`, `number`, required: false): This field is where you enter a unique identifier number for the dog training form. This helps us track the progress and results of the training program.
* **Training Needs** (`training_needs`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the training level that best fits the dog's current training needs. Select one of the options provided: Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **Behavior Assessment** (`behavior_assessment`, `note`, required: false): Write a brief and concise description of the dog's behavior, including any notable traits or concerns.
* **Behavior Rating** (`behavior_rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate the dog's behavior from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Needs Improvement" and 5 is "Excellent". This rating will help us understand the dog's progress and adjust the training program accordingly.
