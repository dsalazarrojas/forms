# Simple Pet Adoption Application Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for individuals who wish to adopt a pet from an animal shelter. By completing this form, you will help us understand your preferences and needs, so we can provide you with the best match for your new pet.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with as much detail as possible, but don't worry too much about grammar or formatting.
2. Please enter your information in the space provided for each field.
3. Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
4. If you have any additional comments or questions, use the space provided at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, not required): 
	+ This is where you can provide some background information about yourself, such as your name, contact information, and any other details you think would be helpful for us to know.
* **Pet Info** (`pet_info`, text, not required): 
	+ Please describe the type of pet you are interested in adopting, such as a dog, cat, or other species. You can also provide any specific breed or characteristics you are looking for.
* **Household Info** (`household_info`, text, not required): 
	+ Describe your household, including the number of people living in your home, and any other pets you already have.
* **Pet Requirements** (`pet_requirements`, text, not required): 
	+ Share any specific requirements or needs you have for the pet you are interested in adopting, such as energy level, grooming needs, or training experience.
* **Contact Preference** (`contact_preference`, text, not required): 
	+ Let us know how you would like to be contacted, such as phone, email, or in-person.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, not required): 
	+ If you have any additional comments or questions, please use this space to provide them.
* **User Signature** (`user_signature`, text, not required): 
	+ Please sign this form to indicate that you have read and agreed to our adoption process.
* **User Agreement** (`user_agreement`, text, not required): 
	+ This is a required field where you will be asked to agree to our adoption policies and procedures.

## Tips
- Make sure to proofread your answers for accuracy and completeness.
- Please be patient, as we will review and process your application carefully.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
