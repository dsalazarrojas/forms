# Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect application information from individuals interested in volunteering at an animal rescue organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Be honest when providing information, especially in regards to your medical history and availability.
3. If you have any volunteer experience or education relevant to animal care, please provide details.
4. Provide at least one emergency contact name and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Application** (`volunteer_application_form`, text, optional): A brief introduction to your application, including your interest in volunteering with the animal rescue organization.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Provide your full name, address, phone number, and email address so that we can contact you regarding your application.
* **Volunteer Preferences** (`volunteer_preferences`, text, optional): Describe your interests and preferences when volunteering, such as specific animal species or types of activities you would be interested in.
* **Animal Preferences** (`animal_preferences`, text, optional): List any animals you are willing to care for or work with, or any specific animal species you would like to avoid.
* **Availability** (`availability`, text, optional): Indicate your availability to volunteer, including any days of the week or times of the day when you are available.
* **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, text, optional): Provide the name and contact information of at least one emergency contact person in case we need to reach out to them.
* **Volunteer Experience** (`volunteer_experience`, text, optional): Share any relevant volunteer experience you have, including the organizations you volunteered with and your role.
* **Education and Certifications** (`education_and_certifications`, text, optional): List any relevant education or certifications you have that may be beneficial to your volunteer work.
* **References** (`references`, text, optional): Provide names and contact information of at least two professional references.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions or allergies that may impact your ability to volunteer, please disclose them in this section.
