# Cat Adoption Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cat Adoption Application form is designed to collect information from potential adopters to ensure that they are a good fit for our feline friends. This form will help us understand your lifestyle, preferences, and experience with cats.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your name, email address, phone number, and home address.
2. Provide information about your housing situation, including your type of residence, landlord approval, and other pets in the household.
3. Share your experience with cats, including any previous ownership and veterinary references.
4. Specify the type of environment you can provide for the cat (indoor or outdoor).
5. Share your adoption motivation and any additional information about your lifestyle.
6. Agree to our adoption terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Full Name (1)** (`text`, required): Please enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address (2)** (`email`, required): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number (3)** (`text`, required): Your contact phone number.
* **Home Address (4)** (`text`, required): Your current home address.
* **City (5)** (`text`, required): Enter your city name.
* **State (6)** (`text`, required): Enter your state or province name.
* **ZIP or Postal Code (7)** (`text`, required): Enter your ZIP or postal code.
* **Housing Type (8)** (`select_one`, required): Choose your type of residence (House - Owned, House - Rented, Apartment - Owned, Apartment - Rented, Condo, or Other).
* **Landlord Approval for Pets (9)** (`select_one`, required): If you rent your home, indicate if you have your landlord's approval for pets.
* **Do You Have Other Pets (10)** (`select_one`, required): Indicate if you have other pets in your household.
* **Pet Details (11)** (`text`, optional): List your current pets, including type and age.
* **Children in Home (12)** (`select_one`, required): Indicate if there are children under 12 in your home.
* **Children Ages (13)** (`text`, optional): If applicable, provide ages of your children.
* **Cat You Are Interested In (14)** (`text`, required): Enter the name or ID of the cat you are interested in.
* **Have You Owned Cats Before (15)** (`select_one`, required): Specify your experience with cats (Yes - Currently Own, Yes - Previously Owned, No - First Time).
* **Veterinarian Name (16)** (`text`, optional): If applicable, enter your veterinarian's name.
* **Veterinarian Phone (17)** (`text`, optional): If applicable, enter your veterinarian's phone number.
* **Will Cat Be Indoor or Outdoor (18)** (`select_one`, required): Specify where the cat will live (Indoor Only, Indoor with Supervised Outdoor, Indoor and Outdoor).
* **Hours Away from Home (19)** (`text`, optional): Enter how many hours you are away from home daily.
* **Why Do You Want to Adopt (20)** (`text`, required): Explain why you want to adopt a cat.
* **Personal References (21)** (`text`, optional): Provide name and phone number of a personal reference.
* **Agreement to Terms (22)** (`select_one`, required): Agree to our adoption terms.
* **Application Date (23)** (`date`, required): Date of application.
