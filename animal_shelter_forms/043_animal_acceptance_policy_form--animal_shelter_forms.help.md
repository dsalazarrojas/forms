# Animal Acceptance Policy Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animal Acceptance Policy Form is designed to help ensure that you, as an animal adopter, understand and agree to the terms and conditions of our animal acceptance policy. This form is a crucial step in the adoption process and helps us to assess your suitability as an adopter.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read through the policy carefully and make sure you understand the terms and conditions.
2. Answer the questions honestly, and select the correct options for each field.
3. Sign your name in the designated space to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Property type**: Select the type of residence where the animal will be kept, such as a house or apartment.
* **Do you have other pets**: If you currently have other pets, please select 'Yes' and provide information about them in the space provided.
* **I agree to the animal acceptance policy**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions of our animal acceptance policy.
* **Signature**: Print your name to confirm your agreement to the policy.
* **Date signed**: Enter the date you are signing the agreement.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all required fields truthfully to ensure a smooth adoption process.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the policy, please don't hesitate to ask our staff for clarification.
* Remember to sign the form carefully, as this is a legal agreement.
