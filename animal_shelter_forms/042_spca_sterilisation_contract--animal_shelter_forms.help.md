# spca_sterilisation_contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the agreement and details of animal sterilization procedures in animal shelters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the future_date field with the planned sterilization date.
2. Provide the owner's details in the owner_details field.
3. Enter the veterinarian's details in the vet_details field.
4. Describe the sterilization procedure and details in the sterilization_details field.
5. Fill in the future_sterilisation_date field with the expected completion date of the sterilization.
6. Fill in the signed_date field with the date and time the form is signed.
7. Obtain the owner's signature in the owner_signature field.
8. Obtain the veterinarian's signature in the vet_signature field.
9. Provide a brief reason for the sterilization in the reason field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Future Date** (`future_date`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the animal will be sterilized.
* **Owner Details** (`owner_details`, text, optional): This field is intended to capture the owner's name and contact information, or any other relevant details.
* **Veterinarian Details** (`vet_details`, text, optional): Enter the veterinarian's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Sterilisation Details** (`sterilisation_details`, text, optional): Describe the sterilization procedure and any specific requirements or precautions to be taken.
* **Future Sterilisation Date** (`future_sterilisation_date`, date, optional): Enter the expected completion date of the sterilization procedure.
* **Signed Date** (`signed_date`, date, optional): The date and time the form is signed by all parties involved.
* **Owner Signature** (`owner_signature`, text, optional): Obtain the owner's signature, confirming their agreement to the sterilization procedure.
* **Veterinarian Signature** (`vet_signature`, text, optional): Obtain the veterinarian's signature, confirming their agreement and completion of the sterilization procedure.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, optional): Briefly state the reason for the sterilization.
