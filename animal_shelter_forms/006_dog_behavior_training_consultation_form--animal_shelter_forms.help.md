# Dog Behavior Training Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for dog owners or caregivers to gather information about a dog's behavior and training history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Dog Owners Info** section with your name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide detailed information about your dog, including its name, breed, age, and any relevant health issues.
3. In the **Observation** section, describe any notable behaviors or observations you have made about your dog's behavior.
4. In the **Behavior Observation** section, take notes on any specific behaviors or issues you have observed with your dog.
5. In the **Training Methods** section, list any training methods you have used to address the identified behaviors or issues.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Dog Owners Info (Required)**: This section is for you to provide your contact information and any relevant details about yourself and your dog.
	+ **Name**: Your name, please.
	+ **Email**: Your email address, so we can get in touch with you.
	+ **Phone Number**: Your phone number, in case we need to call you.
* **Dog Info (Required)**: This is where you provide details about your dog, including:
	+ **Name**: Your dog's name.
	+ **Breed**: Your dog's breed.
	+ **Age**: Your dog's age.
	+ **Health Issues**: Any relevant health issues your dog may have.
* **Observation (Optional)**: Use this space to describe any notable behaviors or observations you have made about your dog's behavior. This could include things like:
	+ Specific incidents or events.
	+ Patterns or habits.
	+ Any changes in behavior.
* **Behavior Observation (Optional)**: In this section, take notes on any specific behaviors or issues you have observed with your dog. This could include:
	+ Details about what you've seen or heard.
	+ How often or how severe the behavior is.
	+ Any attempts you've made to address the behavior.
* **Training Methods (Optional)**: If you have used any training methods to address the identified behaviors or issues, list them here, including:
	+ What methods you've used.
	+ How they've been implemented.
	+ Results or outcomes you've seen.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when providing information about your dog's behavior and history.
- If you have any supporting documents or records, such as veterinary reports or training logs, please attach them to this form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
