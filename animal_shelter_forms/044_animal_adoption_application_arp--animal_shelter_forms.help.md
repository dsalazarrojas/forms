# Animal Adoption Application ARP - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animal Adoption Application ARP is designed to be a comprehensive and accurate form for users applying for animal adoption. This guide explains each section and field in detail to ensure a smooth and successful application process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by selecting the "Applicant Information" page**. This section is the foundation for the application and is required to be filled out completely.
2. **Provide your contact information**. Fill in your full name and email address in the designated fields.
3. **Add your contact information**. Enter your phone number for further communication regarding your application.
4. **Describe your household situation**. Select the correct residence type from the options provided.
5. **Specify your pet preference**. This could be the species, age, or breed of the pet you are interested in adopting.
6. **List your household members**. Include the number and ages of all individuals living in your household.
7. **Add veterinary references**. If applicable, provide the name and contact information of a veterinarian who can vouch for your pet care history.
8. **Confirm your agreement**. Review and agree to the adoption terms and responsibilities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact email**: Enter a valid email address for communication and updates.
* **Phone number**: Enter your best contact number for us to reach you.
* **Residence type**: Select "Own" if you own your home or "Rent" if you rent your home.
* **Pet preference**: Describe the species, age, or breed of pet you are interested in adopting.
* **Household members**: Enter the number and ages of all individuals living in your household.
* **Veterinary reference**: If applicable, enter the name and contact information of a veterinarian who can vouch for your pet care history.
* **I agree to adoption terms and responsibilities**: Select "True" to confirm your understanding and agreement to the adoption terms and responsibilities.
