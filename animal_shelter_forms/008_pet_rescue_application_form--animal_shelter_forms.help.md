# Pet Rescue Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Rescue Application Form is designed to help potential adopters apply to take a pet into their care. The form gathers information about the user's goals and preferences for adopting a pet, as well as details about the pet they are interested in adopting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "Why are you applying for a pet" section, where you can provide a brief explanation of your reasons for applying.
2. Provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
3. Answer questions about the pet you are interested in adopting, including its age, breed, and size.
4. Choose the adoption process that you prefer, from Application, Interview, Home Visit, Meet and Greet, or Adoption.
5. Provide any relevant medical history for yourself or your family members.
6. Add any additional medical notes or information about your pet.
7. Confirm whether you are willing to be a pet sitter or not.
8. Sign the form to confirm that you have read and understood the terms and conditions.
9. Finally, provide information about your pet, including its name, breed, and type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Why are you applying for a pet (User Goals)**: A brief explanation of your reason for applying for a pet. Please provide a detailed explanation of why you are interested in adopting a pet.
* **Contact Information**: This section asks for your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number. Please ensure that you provide the most up-to-date and accurate information.
* **Pet Details**: Please provide information about the pet you are interested in adopting, including its age, breed, and size.
* **Adoption Process**: Choose the adoption process that you prefer from the options provided. This will help us understand how you would like to proceed with the adoption process.
* **Medical History**: If you or a family member has any medical history, please provide it in this section.
* **Medical Notes**: If you have any additional medical information that is relevant to the adoption process, please provide it in this section.
* **Pet Sitter Information**: Indicate whether you are willing to be a pet sitter or not. Please note that this is an optional section.
* **Signature**: This is a confirmation that you have read and understood the terms and conditions of the adoption process.
* **Pet Name**: Please provide the name of the pet you are interested in adopting.
* **Pet Type**: Indicate the type of pet you are interested in adopting.
* **Pet Type 2**: If you are interested in adopting more than one pet, please indicate the type of the second pet.
* **Pet Breed**: Please provide the breed of the pet you are interested in adopting.
* **Pet Breed 2**: If you are interested in adopting more than one pet, please provide the breed of the second pet.
* **User Goals 2**: If you are interested in adopting more than one pet, please provide a brief explanation of your reason for applying for the second pet.
* **User Information**: Please provide any additional information about yourself that is relevant to the adoption process.
* **Pet Size**: Indicate the size of the pet you are interested in adopting.
* **Pet Age**: Please provide the age of the pet you are interested in adopting.
* **Pet Type 2**: If you are interested in adopting more than one pet, please indicate the type of the second pet.
