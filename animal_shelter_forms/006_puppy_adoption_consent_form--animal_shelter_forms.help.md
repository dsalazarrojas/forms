# Puppy Adoption Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Puppy Adoption Consent Form is a critical document that helps the animal shelter understand the adopter's commitment to caring for a new furry friend.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by reading the form carefully and filling in all the required information.
- For each field, make sure to double-check the provided options for correct information.
- Fill in the adopter's name in the "Adopter Name" field.
- Provide the correct address in the "Adopter Address" field.
- Enter the adopter's phone number in the "Phone" field.
- Enter the adopter's email in the "Email" field.
- Select the correct dog ID from the options provided in the "Dog" field.
- Enter the adoption date in the "Adoption Date" field.
- Choose the adoption term (6 months, 1 year, or 2 years) in the "Adoption Term" field.
- Indicate the adoption status (Adopted or Pending) in the "Adoption Status" field.
- In the "Adoption Terms" field, provide any additional information or details about the adoption.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Adopter Name**: Enter the name of the person adopting the puppy.

  (`<name>`, `text`, required: false)

**Tips**
- Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Make sure to review the adoption agreement carefully before signing.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the animal shelter staff.
- The adoption process involves a series of steps, and this form is a critical component of that process.
