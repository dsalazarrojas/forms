# Foster Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The foster application form is used to gather information from individuals or families interested in becoming foster parents to animals in need at our shelter. The form helps us understand their interests, preferences, and availability to provide a suitable match for the animals and ensure a smooth foster experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the foster application form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Provide your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Choose the species of animal you are interested in fostering (Cat, Dog, or Other) in the "species" field.
6. Select the type(s) of animals you are interested in fostering (Mammal, Bird, or Reptile) in the "animal_type" field. You can select multiple options by holding down the Ctrl key (or Command key on a Mac).
7. Enter the number of animals you are willing to foster in the "foster_animals" field.
8. Choose a date you are available to start fostering in the "available_start_date" field.
9. If applicable, choose a date you would like to end the fostering arrangement in the "end_date" field.
10. Enter your address in the "address" field.
11. Enter your zip code in the "zip" field.
12. Provide an alternative phone number for emergency contact in the "phone2" field.
13. Enter the name of the person you would like to have as an emergency contact in the "emergency_contact_name" field.
14. Enter the phone number of the emergency contact in the "emergency_contact_phone" field.
15. If you have any additional comments or information about your foster experience, please enter them in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name (Text)**: Enter your first name for reference.
* **last_name (Text)**: Enter your last name for reference.
* **email (Email)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your foster application.
* **phone (Text)**: Enter a phone number where we can reach you.
* **species (Select One)**: Choose the species of animal you are interested in fostering (Cat, Dog, or Other).
* **animal_type (Select Multiple)**: Choose the type(s) of animals you are interested in fostering (Mammal, Bird, or Reptile). You can select multiple options by holding down the Ctrl key (or Command key on a Mac).
* **foster_animals (Text)**: Enter the number of animals you are willing to foster.
* **available_start_date (Date)**: Choose a date you are available to start fostering.
* **end_date (Date)**: Choose a date you would like to end the fostering arrangement (if applicable).
* **address (Text)**: Enter your address for location information.
* **zip (Text)**: Enter your zip code for location information.
* **phone2 (Text)**: Enter an alternative phone number for emergency contact.
* **emergency_contact_name (Text)**: Enter the name of the person you would like to have as an emergency contact.
* **emergency_contact_phone (Text)**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **notes (Note)**: Enter any additional comments or information about your foster experience.
