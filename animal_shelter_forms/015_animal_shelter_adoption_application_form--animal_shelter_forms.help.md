# Animal Shelter Adoption Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us get to know you and your family better so we can find the perfect match for you and your new furry friend. Please answer all questions honestly, and don't worry, we'll take care of the rest.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the requested information. If a field is not applicable to you, please select "Not Applicable" or "False" as needed. Don't worry if you're not sure about something, just let us know and we'll discuss it with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (1, text, required): Enter your full name, including any middle or last names.
* **Email Address** (2, email, required): Enter the email address you'd like us to contact you with.
* **Phone Number** (3, text, required): Enter your primary contact phone number.
* **Street Address** (4, text, required): Enter your current street address.
* **City** (5, text, required): Enter your city name.
* **State or Province** (6, text, required): Enter your state or province name.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (7, text, required): Enter your zip or postal code.
* **Type of Housing** (8, select_one, required): Select the type of housing you live in:
	+ House - Owned
	+ House - Rented
	+ Apartment or Condo - Owned
	+ Apartment or Condo - Rented
	+ Townhouse - Owned
	+ Townhouse - Rented
	+ Mobile Home
	+ Other
* **If Renting Do You Have Landlord Approval for Pets** (9, select_one, required): Select how you've obtained approval from your landlord:
	+ Yes - Written Approval Available
	+ Yes - Verbal Approval
	+ False
	+ Not Applicable - I Own
* **Do You Have a Yard** (10, select_one, required): Select the status of your yard:
	+ Yes - Fenced
	+ Yes - Unfenced
	+ False
	+ Not Applicable
* **Fence Height** (11, text, required): If you selected "Yes" for "Do You Have a Yard", enter the height of your fence in feet.
* **Number of People in Household** (12, number, required): Enter the number of people living in your household, including yourself.
* **Ages of Children in Home** (13, text, required): If you have children, list their ages.
* **Do You Currently Have Other Pets** (14, select_one, required): Select whether you currently have other pets:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Describe Your Current Pets** (15, text, required): If you selected "True" for "Do You Currently Have Other Pets", describe your current pets, including species, breed, age, and temperament.
* **Have You Ever Owned Pets Before** (16, select_one, required): Select whether you've ever owned pets before:
	+ Yes - Multiple Pets
	+ Yes - One Pet
	+ No - First Time Owner
* **What Happened to Your Previous Pets** (17, text, required): If you selected "Yes" for "Have You Ever Owned Pets Before", describe what happened to your previous pets.
* **Which Animal Are You Interested in Adopting** (18, text, required): Enter the animal you're interested in adopting, or provide the animal's ID if you know it.
* **Type of Animal** (19, select_one, required): Select the type of animal you're interested in adopting:
	+ Dog
	+ Cat
	+ Rabbit
	+ Bird
	+ Small Mammal
	+ Reptile
	+ Other
* **Preferred Age Range** (20, select_one, required): Select the age range of the animal you're interested in:
	+ Puppy or Kitten (Under 6 months)
	+ Young (6 months - 2 years)
	+ Adult (2-7 years)
	+ Senior (7+ years)
	+ No Preference
* **Preferred Size** (21, select_one, required): Select the size of the animal you're interested in:
	+ Small (Under 20 lbs)
	+ Medium (20-50 lbs)
	+ Large (50-80 lbs)
	+ Extra Large (80+ lbs)
	+ Not Applicable
	+ No Preference
* **Desired Activity Level** (22, select_one, required): Select the activity level of the animal you're interested in:
	+ Low - Couch Potato
	+ Moderate - Some Daily Exercise
	+ High - Very Active
	+ Very High - Working or Athletic Dog
	+ No Preference
* **Why Do You Want to Adopt** (23, text, required): Share your motivation for adopting an animal.
* **Describe Your Daily Care Plan** (24, text, required): Describe how you'll care for the animal, including exercise, feeding, and attention.
* **What Is Your Work Schedule** (25, text, required): Enter your typical work schedule, including hours away from home.
* **What Are Your Plans for Pet Care During Vacations** (26, text, required): Describe how you'll care for the animal during vacations or extended absences.
* **What Are Your Plans for Training** (27, text, required): Describe any plans you have for training the animal.
* **Veterinarian Name and Contact** (28, text, optional): If you have a current or previous veterinarian, enter their name and contact information.
* **Personal Reference Name and Phone** (29, text, required): Enter the name and phone number of a personal reference who knows you well.
* **I Understand There Is an Adoption Fee** (30, select_one, required): Select whether you understand that there may be an adoption fee:
	+ Yes I Understand
	+ Other
* **I Agree to Return the Animal to the Shelter If I Cannot Keep Them** (31, select_one, required): Select whether you agree to return the animal to the shelter if you cannot keep them:
	+ Yes I Agree
	+ No I Do Not Agree
* **I Agree to Provide Proper Veterinary Care Including Vaccinations** (32, select_one, required): Select whether you agree to provide proper veterinary care, including vaccinations:
	+ Yes I Agree
	+ No I Do Not Agree
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (33, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you may have.

Please submit this form carefully and honestly, and we'll be in touch with you soon to discuss your application. Thank you for considering adoption from our shelter!
