# Dog Bio Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and update information about a dog's bio, which is a crucial aspect of the animal shelter's database.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the General Information section with the dog's details, such as its age, breed, and description.
2. In the Behavior section, describe the dog's behavior, including any training or training needs.
3. In the Personality section, share your observations about the dog's personality, such as its temperament and socialization level.
4. In the Bio Update section, provide any new or updated information about the dog.
5. Upload a photo of the dog and add a caption to describe the photo and its relevance to the dog's characteristics.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (Text, Optional): Enter the dog's age, breed, and brief description to help potential adopters understand the dog's background.
* **Behavior** (Text, Optional): Describe the dog's behavior, including any training or training needs, to help potential adopters understand the dog's needs and characteristics.
* **Personality** (Text, Optional): Share your observations about the dog's personality, such as its temperament and socialization level, to help potential adopters understand the dog's personality.
* **Bio Update** (Text, Optional): Provide any new or updated information about the dog, such as changes in its behavior or training needs.
* **Photo Upload** (Text, Optional): Upload a photo of the dog that represents its appearance or personality.
* **Photo Caption** (Text, Optional): Add a brief description of the photo, including its relevance to the dog's characteristics.

## Tips
* Be accurate and truthful when providing information about the dog.
* Use clear and concise language to help potential adopters understand the dog's needs and characteristics.
* Make sure to upload a clear and relevant photo of the dog.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask the shelter staff for guidance.
