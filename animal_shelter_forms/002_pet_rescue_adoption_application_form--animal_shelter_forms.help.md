# Pet Rescue Adoption Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Rescue Adoption Application Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in adopting a pet from the rescue organization. This form helps the organization assess whether the pet is a good match for the applicant and ensures a smooth adoption process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Provide the required contact information (email, phone number, and address).
3. Answer each field's question to the best of your ability.
4. Review your submission before submitting it to the organization for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Name (1)**: Enter the name of the pet you are interested in adopting.
* **Contact Email (2)**: Provide your email address so the organization can contact you about the adoption process.
* **Pet Type (3)**: Select the type of pet you are interested in adopting (Cat, Dog, or Other).
* **Pet Breed (4)**: If you are interested in a specific breed, type it here.
* **Pet Age (5)**: Enter the age of the pet you are interested in adopting.
* **Contact Phone (6)**: Enter your phone number so the organization can contact you about the adoption process.
* **Food and Treats (7)**: Describe the dietary needs and preferences of the pet.
* **Contact Note (8)**: Add any additional information about your contact that you would like to share with the organization.
* **Health (9)**: Enter any health concerns or special needs of the pet you are interested in adopting.
* **Weight (10)**: Enter the weight of the pet you are interested in adopting.
* **Contact Address (11)**: Enter your address so the organization can mail any necessary documents or information.
* **Owner Name (12)**: Enter the name of the pet's owner or your name.
* **City (13)**: Enter the city where you reside.
* **State (14)**: Enter the state where you reside.
* **Owner Phone (15)**: Enter your phone number or the owner's phone number.
* **Owner Address (16)**: Enter your address or the pet's owner's address.
* **Owner Note (17)**: Add any additional information about you or the pet's owner that you would like to share with the organization.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date contact information.
- Be as specific as possible when describing the pet's needs and preferences.
- If you are interested in adopting a pet with specific health needs or concerns, please let us know.
- The organization will review your submission and contact you before proceeding with the adoption process.
