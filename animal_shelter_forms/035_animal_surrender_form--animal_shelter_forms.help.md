# Animal Surrender Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for animal owners to surrender their pets to the animal shelter. It gathers essential information about the pet and its owner to facilitate a smooth and safe surrender process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields (Owner Information, Animal Information, Surrender Details, etc.) and ensure you provide accurate and complete information.
2. Review the options available for each field and choose the relevant ones for your situation.
3. If any information is unclear or incomplete, please provide additional comments or explanations in the "Additional Comments" field.
4. Sign the form with your electronic signature and provide the date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Owner Full Name** (`owner_name`): Your legal name as per the identification provided.
* **Email Address** (`owner_email`): Your contact email address, which will be used for communication.
* **Phone Number** (`owner_phone`): Your contact phone number, which will be used for communication.
* **Street Address** (`owner_address`): Your current address, which will be used for communication and potential visits.
* **City** (`owner_city`): The city you are currently residing in.
* **State or Province** (`owner_state`): The state or province you are currently residing in.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (`owner_zip`): The postal code of your current address.
* **Requested Surrender Date** (`surrender_date`): The date you would like to surrender your pet.
* **Animal Name** (`animal_name`): The name of the pet being surrendered.
* **Species** (`animal_species`): Select the species of your pet (Dog, Cat, Rabbit, Bird, Small Mammal, Reptile, or Other).
* **Breed** (`animal_breed`): If known, provide the breed of your pet.
* **Age** (`animal_age`): Provide the age of your pet in years or months.
* **Sex** (`animal_sex`): Select the sex of your pet (Male or Female).
* **Color and Markings** (`animal_color`): Describe your pet's physical characteristics.
* **Weight** (`animal_weight`): Provide the weight of your pet in pounds.
* **Is Your Pet Microchipped** (`microchip_status`): Indicate if your pet is microchipped.
* **Microchip Number** (`microchip_number`): If known, provide the microchip number.
* **Is Your Pet Spayed or Neutered** (`spay_neuter_status`): Indicate if your pet is spayed or neutered.
* **Reason for Surrender** (`surrender_reason`): Select the primary reason for surrendering your pet.
* **Please Explain Your Situation** (`surrender_details`): Provide additional context or information about your situation.
* **Are There Any Behavior Issues** (`behavior_issues`): Indicate if your pet has any known behavior issues.
* **Behavior Details** (`behavior_details`): Describe any behavior issues if applicable.
* **Are There Any Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`): Indicate if your pet has any known medical conditions.
* **Medical Details** (`medical_details`): Describe any medical conditions and treatments if applicable.
* **Veterinarian Name** (`veterinarian_name`): Name of the current or previous veterinarian for your pet.
* **Veterinary Clinic** (`vet_clinic`): Name of the current or previous veterinary clinic.
* **Veterinarian Phone** (`vet_phone`): Contact phone number of the current or previous veterinarian.
* **Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`): Select the vaccination status of your pet (Up to Date, Partially Vaccinated, Not Vaccinated, or Unknown).
* **Daily Routine** (`daily_routine`): Describe your pet's daily feeding and exercise schedule.
* **Diet Information** (`diet_info`): Provide information about your pet's diet and feeding schedule.
* **What Does Your Pet Love or Dislike** (`likes_dislikes`): Describe your pet's preferences and quirks.
* **Your Pet Is Good With** (`good_with`): Select all that apply for your pet's social interactions.
* **How Long Have You Owned This Pet** (`ownership_duration`): Provide the length of time you have owned your pet in years or months.
* **Has This Pet Been Surrendered Before** (`previous_surrenders`): Indicate if this pet has been surrendered previously.
* **Have You Attempted to Rehome** (`rehome_attempted`): Indicate if you have attempted to rehome your pet.
* **Surrender Fee** (`surrender_fee`): If applicable, provide the surrender fee.
* **I Transfer Full Ownership to the Shelter** (`ownership_transfer`): Confirm that you understand you cannot reclaim your pet.
* **I Certify All Information Is Accurate** (`information_accuracy`): Confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Provide any additional information that may be helpful.
* **Owner Electronic Signature** (`owner_signature`): Your electronic signature as per your full name.
* **Date Signed** (`surrender_date_signed`): The date and time you signed this form.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are accurate and complete to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure or have questions, please contact the shelter staff for guidance.
* The surrender process may take some time to complete; please be patient and understanding.
* Your cooperation and honesty are greatly appreciated to ensure a smooth and safe surrender process for your pet.
