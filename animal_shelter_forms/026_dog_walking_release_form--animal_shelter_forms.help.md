# dog_walking_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain information from dog owners for dog walking or pet sitting services, providing essential details about the dog's owner, the dog, and relevant medical or walking information to ensure the dog's safety and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name, address, and contact information in the "Owner Information" section (Page 1).
2. Fill in your dog's information, including name, breed, and age in the "Dog Information" section (Page 1).
3. Provide information about the walk or pet sitting service you are requesting, including dates, times, and duration in the "Walk Information" section (Page 1).
4. If applicable, provide any relevant medical information about your dog in the "Medical Information" section (Page 1).
5. Sign the "Signature" section (Page 1) to confirm that you have read and understood the form.
6. Select whether you understand and agree to the terms of this form in the "Agreement" section (Page 1).
7. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" section (Page 1).
8. Provide emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contact" section (Page 1), if applicable.
9. Enter information about your dog's veterinarian or vet's name, phone number, and address in the "Veterinarian Information" section (Page 1), if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <strong>Owner Information (Page 1)</strong>
• **Owner Information**: This field is used to collect the owner's name, address, and contact information. Please fill in your name, address, and contact details.

### <strong>Dog Information (Page 1)</strong>
• **Dog Name, Breed, Age**: Enter your dog's name, breed, and age to provide essential information about your dog.

### <strong>Walk Information (Page 1)</strong>
• **Walk Information**: Provide the dates, times, and duration of the walk or pet sitting service you are requesting.

### <strong>Medical Information (Page 1)</strong>
• **Medical Information**: If your dog has any medical conditions or allergies, please provide relevant information to ensure their safety during the service.

### <strong>Signature (Page 1)</strong>
• **Signature**: Sign your name to confirm that you have read and understood the form.

### <strong>Agreement (Page 1)</strong>
• **Agreement**: Select whether you understand and agree to the terms of this form. If not, select "I do not understand or agree to the terms of this form".

### <strong>Notes (Page 1)</strong>
• **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your dog or the service.

### <strong>Emergency Contact (Page 1)</strong>
• **Emergency Contact**: If applicable, provide the name, phone number, and address of an emergency contact person.

### <strong>Veterinarian Information (Page 1)</strong>
• **Veterinarian Information**: If applicable, enter the vet's name, phone number, and address.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure the smooth execution of the dog walking or pet sitting service.
* If you are unsure about any information or have questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* This form is a critical part of our service, and we appreciate your cooperation in providing accurate and complete information.
