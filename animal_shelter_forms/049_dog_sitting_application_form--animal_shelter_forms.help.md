# Dog Sitting Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dog Sitting Application Form is designed to collect information from dog owners seeking to board their dogs in the shelter. This form helps shelter staff understand the dog's needs and preferences, ensuring a smooth and safe experience for both the dog and its owner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the dog's breed from the provided options.
2. Enter the dog's name and age in the respective fields.
3. Provide a valid email address for communication purposes.
4. Optionally, specify any special needs the dog may have, such as medical conditions or behavioral issues.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about the dog's behavior or preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Dog Breed** (`dog_breed`, `select_one`, required): Choose the breed of your dog from the provided options.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your dog's name.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name and contact information for communication purposes.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact number for any updates or concerns.
* **Dog Age** (`dog_age`, `number`, optional): Enter your dog's age.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, optional): Select the start date of the boarding period.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, optional): Select the end date of the boarding period.
* **Special Needs** (`special_needs`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check any special needs your dog may have, such as medical conditions or behavioral issues.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your dog's behavior or preferences.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for any updates or concerns.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter your address for any delivery or pick-up purposes.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled accurately and truthfully to avoid any issues during the boarding process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the shelter staff for assistance.
* The shelter staff will review and process your application thoroughly to ensure a smooth experience for both you and your dog.
