# Lost And Found Pet Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Lost And Found Pet Form is used to report lost or found animals to help facilitate their safe return. Please use this form to provide detailed information about your pet, including its species, size, and contact information, to increase the chances of it being reunited with its owner.

</thinking>

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Answer the questions truthfully and accurately to provide the most helpful information.
2.  Fill in the form with your details.
3.  Upload any relevant pictures or descriptions to help identify the pet.
4.  Select the options that best fit your pet's characteristics.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Reporting Reason** (`Reporting Reason`, `text`, required): Please enter a brief explanation of why your pet is lost.
*   **Date Lost** (`Date Lost`, `date`, optional): If possible, provide the date when your pet was lost.
*   **Type of Pet** (`Type of Pet`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of pet (e.g., dog, cat, bird, etc.).
*   **Breed of Pet** (`Breed of Pet`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the breed of your pet.
*   **City** (`City`, `text`, optional): Enter the city where your pet was last seen.
*   **Street** (`Street`, `text`, optional): Enter the street where your pet was last seen.
*   **Pet's Name** (`Pet's Name`, `text`, optional): Enter your pet's name.
*   **Gender** (`Gender`, `select_one`, optional): Select your pet's gender (e.g., male, female, unknown, etc.).
*   **Color** (`Color`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your pet's colors (e.g., black, white, gray, etc.).
*   **Age of Pet** (`Age of Pet`, `number`, optional): If applicable, enter your pet's age.
*   **Size of Pet** (`Size of Pet`, `text`, optional): Enter your pet's size (e.g., small, medium, large, etc.).
*   **Contact Name** (`Contact Name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name.
*   **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, `email`, optional): Enter your contact email.
*   **Upload Pictures** (`Upload Pictures`, `note`, optional): If possible, upload pictures or videos of your pet.
*   **Pictures** (`Pictures`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe the pictures or videos uploaded.
*   **Pet Type** (`Pet Type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select additional types of your pet (e.g., dog, cat, bird, etc.).
*   **Pet Breed** (`Pet Breed`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your pet's breeds (e.g., Labrador, Poodle, etc.).
*   **Pet Color** (`Pet Color`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select additional colors of your pet (e.g., black, white, gray, etc.).
*   **Pet Size** (`Pet Size`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your pet's size (e.g., small, medium, large, etc.).
*   **Pet Gender** (`Pet Gender`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your pet's genders (e.g., male, female, unknown, etc.).
*   **Pet Age** (`Pet Age`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your pet's age range (e.g., 1-5, 6-10, etc.).
*   **Pet Status** (`Pet Status`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your pet's status (e.g., Active, Inactive, etc.).
*   **Date Found** (`Date Found`, `date`, optional): If found, enter the date when your pet was found.
*   **Description** (`Description`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe your pet's characteristics, habits, or any distinctive features.

Note: All fields are optional, but the more information you provide, the better the chances of reuniting with your pet.
