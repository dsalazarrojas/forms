# Dog Temperament Questionnaire Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Dog Temperament Questionnaire Form" is a comprehensive questionnaire designed to gather essential information about a dog's personality, behavior, and needs. This form will help pet owners, shelter staff, and potential adopters make informed decisions when finding the perfect forever home for a dog.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question thoroughly and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Be as specific as you can when describing a dog's behavior, personality, and characteristics.
3. Use the provided fields to capture all relevant information, and don't hesitate to add any additional details in the "Additional Information" field.

* **General Information** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This field is for providing basic information about the dog, such as its name, breed, age, and sex.
* **Health Information** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Here, you can mention any health issues or concerns the dog may have. Be as detailed as possible, including any medical conditions, allergies, or injuries.
* **Socialization Information** (`3`, `text`, required: false): Describe how well the dog socializes with people, other animals, and in new environments. This will help assess its adaptability and potential for a smooth transition to a new home.
* **Behavior Information** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Outline any behavioral traits or quirks the dog may exhibit, such as barking, digging, or destructive behavior. This will help identify potential needs or training requirements.
* **Additional Information** (`5`, `text`, required: false): If there's anything else you think is essential to share about the dog, such as its favorite toys, activities, or training habits, please include it here.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when describing the dog's characteristics and behavior.
* Use specific examples or anecdotes to help illustrate a dog's traits.
* Don't hesitate to ask for help or guidance if you're unsure about how to complete a particular section.
* Remember, the more information you provide, the better we can match this dog with the perfect forever home.
