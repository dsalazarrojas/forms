# Animal Abuse Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about animal abuse cases, including location, date, description of the animal, and any evidence that may be available.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the form to understand the sections and fields.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked as "true" or required in the form.
3. Be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling in each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **-- Report Information --** (`section_report_information`, `note`, required: false): This section provides a brief introduction to the form and its purpose.
- **Reporter name** (`reporter_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person reporting the incident, or choose to remain anonymous if desired.
- **Contact email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Provide an email address for follow-up contact, if desired.
- **Incident location** (`incident_location`, `text`, required: true): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
- **Incident date** (`incident_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the incident, if known.
- **Animal description** (`animal_description`, `text`, required: true): Describe the animal involved, including breed, color, and size.
- **Evidence available** (`evidence_available`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate if evidence such as photos or witness contact information is available.
- **Additional details** (`additional_details`, `text`, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the incident, including any relevant details.

## Tips
* Be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling out the form.
* If you are reporting an incident, please provide as much information as possible.
* If you are not able to provide all the required information, please provide what you can and submit the form.
