# Pet Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Survey form is used to collect information about a pet and its owner. This information is used to better understand the relationship between the owner and their pet and to provide valuable insights for animal shelter organizations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field with the requested information about your pet and its owner.
2. Answer each question carefully and accurately.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the animal shelter staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **pet_name** (Text, Required: False): Enter the name of your pet.
* **owner_name** (Text, Required: False): Enter your name as the pet's owner.
* **pet_type** (Select One, Required: False): Select the type of pet you own (Cat, Dog, Bird, Fish, Other).
* **owner_age** (Number, Required: False): Enter your age.
* **pet_age** (Number, Required: False): Enter your pet's age.
* **email** (Email, Required: False): Enter your email address.
* **phone_number** (Text, Required: False): Enter your phone number.
* **pet_weight** (Number, Required: False): Enter your pet's weight (in kg or lbs).
* **owner_weight** (Number, Required: False): Enter your weight (in kg or lbs).
* **owner_height** (Number, Required: False): Enter your height (in cm or feet and inches).
* **address1** (Text, Required: False): Enter your address (street, city, state, zip).
* **address2** (Text, Required: False): Enter additional address information (suite, floor, etc.).
* **pet_description** (Text, Required: False): Enter any additional information about your pet.
* **owner_notes** (Note, Required: False): Enter any notes or comments about your ownership of the pet.
* **pet_breed** (Text, Required: False): Enter the breed of your pet.
* **pet_color** (Text, Required: False): Enter the color of your pet.
* **owner_gender** (Select One, Required: False): Select your gender (Male, Female, Other).
* **pet_gender** (Select One, Required: False): Select your pet's gender (Male, Female, Other).
* **owner_marital_status** (Select One, Required: False): Select your marital status (Married, Single, Divorced, Other).
* **pet_diet** (Text, Required: False): Enter your pet's diet (e.g. food, treats, etc.).
* **pet_medication** (Text, Required: False): Enter any medication your pet is taking.
* **owner_education** (Select One, Required: False): Select your education level (Some High School, High School, College, University, Other).
* **owner_income** (Text, Required: False): Enter your income level (optional).
* **pet_vaccinations** (Select Multiple, Required: False): Select any vaccinations your pet has received (Rabies, Distemper, Heartworm, Other).
* **notes** (Note, Required: False): Enter any additional notes or comments about your pet.
