# Dog Foster Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from potential dog foster families, allowing us to understand their background, household, pet experience, and contact details. This information helps us match the best-suited foster family with a dog in need.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information, including your contact details and any relevant background information.
2. Provide details about your household, such as the number of people living there and the size of your home.
3. Share information about your pet experience, including any previous experience with dogs.
4. Submit your application, and a member of our team will review it to determine if we have a suitable dog for you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Information** (`personal_information`): Please provide your name, address, and contact details.
* **Household Details** (`household_details`): Describe your household, including the number of people living there and the size of your home.
* **Pet Details** (`pet_details`): Share any previous experience you have with dogs, including their breed, size, and age.
* **Application** (`application`): Write a brief introduction of yourself and your family, explaining why you are interested in fostering a dog and what qualities you think you can bring to a new canine companion.
* **Contact** (`contact`): Provide a way for us to reach out to you, such as a phone number or email address.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* Be concise and clear in your answers.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us directly.
* We will review your application and contact you within [timeframe] to discuss further.
