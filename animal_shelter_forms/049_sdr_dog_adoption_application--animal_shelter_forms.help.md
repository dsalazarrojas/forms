# sdr-dog-adoption-application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for potential adopters to provide information about themselves and the dog they are interested in adopting. The form gathers personal details such as name, contact information, and the dog's breed, age, and adoption reason. This information will be used to process and facilitate the adoption process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the corresponding fields. These fields are used to identify you as the potential adopter.
2. Enter your **Email** and **Phone** numbers in the corresponding fields. These fields are used to contact you for further information and follow-up.
3. Provide your **Address** and **City** in the corresponding fields. This information will help us to contact you and arrange for the adoption process.
4. Select your **State** from the drop-down menu. This field is used to determine the relevant local regulations and guidelines for adoption.
5. Enter your **Zip** code in the corresponding field. This field is used to further verify your address.
6. Select the **Dog Breed** you are interested in adopting from the drop-down menu. This field is used to match you with the right dog for your needs.
7. Choose the **Dog Age** from the drop-down menu. This field is used to understand the dog's needs and requirements.
8. Enter the **Adoption Date** of the dog you are interested in adopting. This field is used to determine the dog's availability.
9. Provide a brief explanation for the reason you are interested in adopting the dog. This can include any medical or behavioral concerns you have about the dog.
10. If applicable, select any **Special Needs** the dog has from the drop-down menu. This field is used to ensure you are aware of and prepared to meet the dog's needs.
11. Choose the **Adoption Agreement** that applies to your situation. This field is used to determine your commitment to the adoption process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address we can use to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your street address or postal address.
* **City**: Enter the city where you live.
* **State**: Choose your state of residence.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **Dog Breed**: Select the breed of dog you are interested in adopting.
* **Dog Age**: Choose the age of the dog you are interested in adopting.
* **Adoption Date**: Enter the date you are interested in adopting the dog.
* **Adoption Reason**: Provide a brief explanation for why you want to adopt the dog.
* **Special Needs**: If the dog has any special needs, select them from the list.
* **Adoption Agreement**: Choose the agreement that applies to your situation.

## Tips
* Please fill in all required fields to ensure a complete and accurate application.
* Make sure to select the correct dog breed and age from the menus.
* Be honest about any special needs the dog may have.
* Carefully review and agree to the adoption agreement.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
