# Rescue Dog Adoption Application - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from potential adopters to help the animal shelter assess their suitability as a home for a rescue dog. The form is divided into several sections, each focusing on a specific aspect of the applicant's information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  **Provide Applicant Information**: Fill out the first page with your details, including your name, address, and contact information.
2.  **Describe Your Household**: On the second page, provide information about your household, including the number of people living with you and any pets you already have.
3.  **Share Your Pet Experience**: In the third section, share your experience with pets, including any relevant training or experience with dogs.
4.  **Choose Your Desired Pet**: Select the type of pet you are interested in adopting, which is essential for the shelter to match you with a suitable dog.
5.  **Share Household Details**: In the next section, provide information about your home and yard, including any fencing or relevant features.
6.  **Check Your Conditions**: On the sixth page, select any conditions that you can meet to care for a dog, such as spaying/neutering or microchipping.
7.  **Add Any Additional Comments**: In the final section, include any additional comments or questions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Info** (`applicant_info`, text, required: false): This field is used to collect your name, address, and contact information. Please enter your name, address, and phone number to ensure we can contact you.
* **Household Info** (`household_info`, text, required: false): Describe your household, including the number of people living with you and any pets you already have.
* **Pet Experience** (`pet_experience`, text, required: false): Share your experience with pets, including any relevant training or experience with dogs.
* **Desired Pet** (`desired_pet`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of pet you are interested in adopting. This will help the shelter match you with a suitable dog.
* **Household Details** (`household_details`, text, required: false): Provide information about your home, including any features that may affect a dog's safety and well-being.
* **Conditions** (`conditions`, select_multiple, required: false): Check any conditions that you can meet to care for a dog, such as spaying/neutering or microchipping. This will help the shelter understand your ability to meet the dog's needs.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Use this field to add any additional comments or questions you may have.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required: false): Click "Submit" to complete and submit your application.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form, as it will help the shelter understand your ability to care for a rescue dog.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* Once you have submitted your application, we will review it and follow up with you to discuss further.
* We appreciate your time and effort in completing this form, and thank you for considering adopting a rescue dog!
