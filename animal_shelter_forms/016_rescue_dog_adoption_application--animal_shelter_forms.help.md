# rescue_dog_adoption_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The rescue_dog_adoption_application form is designed to gather information from potential adopters about themselves and their household to determine if they are a suitable match for a rescue dog.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the following fields with your information and answer the questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible. Make sure to review your submission before confirming it is complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Details** (Personal Details, text, optional): Please provide your name, address, and any other relevant personal details you feel comfortable sharing.
* **Household Details** (Household Details, text, optional): Describe your household, including the number of people living there, pets, and any other relevant information.
* **Pet Experience** (Pet Experience, text, optional): If you have experience with pets, please describe your experience, including any relevant training or certifications.
* **Desired Pet** (Desired Pet, text, optional): Describe the type of dog you are interested in adopting, including breed, size, and any specific needs or requirements.
* **Conditions** (Conditions, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions or allergies that may impact your ability to care for a dog, please list them here.
* **Contact** (Contact, text, optional): Provide a way for us to contact you to discuss your application.
* **Submission** (Submission, text, optional): This field is intended for you to confirm that you are submitting this form. Please type "I confirm" to proceed.
* **Confirm Submission** (Confirm Submission, text, optional): Double-check that your submission is complete and accurate before proceeding.
* **Confirm Details** (Confirm Details, text, optional): Review your submission to ensure everything is correct and complete.
* **Confirm Submission 2** (Confirm Submission 2, text, optional): Another chance to confirm that your submission is complete and accurate.

## Tips
* Please be as thorough and accurate as possible when filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us directly.
* We appreciate your honesty when disclosing any conditions or allergies that may impact your ability to care for a dog.
* We will contact you to discuss your application and answer any further questions you may have.
