# Tutoring Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from students who are interested in receiving tutoring services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On the first page, enter your student details in the text field provided.
2. On the second page, enter your name and email address.
3. On the third page, enter your phone number.
4. On the fourth page, describe your tutoring needs.
5. On the fifth page, schedule a session by clicking on the "Schedule Session" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Details**:
	+ **Student Details** (`tutoring_registration_form`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about yourself that you think are relevant to the tutoring service.
* **Student Name**:
	+ **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name.
* **Student Email**:
	+ **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Student Phone**:
	+ **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Tutoring Needs**:
	+ **Tutoring Needs** (`tutoring_needs`, text, optional): Describe the specific areas where you need help or guidance.
* **Schedule Session**:
	+ **Schedule Session** (`schedule_session`, text, optional): Select a time slot for your session.
