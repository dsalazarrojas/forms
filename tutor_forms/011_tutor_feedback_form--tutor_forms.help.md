# Tutor Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tutor Feedback Form is a tool used to gather feedback from students about their experience with their tutor and the class they are taking. This feedback is crucial in helping us to identify areas of improvement and make necessary changes to better serve our students.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each page carefully to ensure you have all the information needed.
2. Select the correct options for each field where applicable.
3. Provide detailed answers to the text fields.
4. Make sure to submit your feedback before leaving the page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Class Taught
* **Class Taught** (`class_taught`, `select_one`, required: false): Check if you are taking a class or not. Select "Yes" if you are taking a class, "No" if you are not.

### Student Performance
* **Student Performance** (`student_performance`, `select_one`, required: true): Check how you think you have performed in the class. Select "Yes" if you feel you have performed well, "No" if you feel you need improvement.

### Communication Skills
* **Communication Skills** (`communication_skills`, `text`, required: true): Share your thoughts on how well the tutor has communicated with you. Please provide a detailed answer in a few sentences.

### Feedback On Class
* **Feedback On Class** (`feedback_on_class`, `text`, required: true): Share your thoughts on the class itself. Is there anything you particularly liked or disliked about the class? Please provide a detailed answer in a few sentences.

### Feedback On Tutor
* **Feedback On Tutor** (`feedback_on_tutor`, `text`, required: true): Share your thoughts on the tutor. How did the tutor perform? Were there any areas of improvement for the tutor? Please provide a detailed answer in a few sentences.

### Overall Experience
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, `select_one`, required: true): Check how overall you feel about your experience with the tutor and class. Select "Yes" if you are satisfied, "No" if not.

### Suggestions for Improvement
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, `text`, required: false): If you have any suggestions on how we can improve our services, please share them here. This will help us to improve your experience and the experience of other students.

### Feedback On Materials
* **Feedback On Materials** (`feedback_on_materials`, `text`, required: false): Share any thoughts on the materials provided for the class. Did you find them helpful? Were there any issues with them?

### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): If you have any additional comments or thoughts, please share them here.

### Submitted By
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `text`, required: true): Please enter your name so we can contact you if needed.

## Tips
* Make sure to be honest and accurate in your feedback.
* Be specific when providing feedback, especially when answering text fields.
* If you have any concerns or issues, please raise them in the relevant field.
* If you need help, don't hesitate to ask!
