# Math Lesson Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Math Lesson Inquiry Form is designed to gather essential details from students seeking math lesson help. This form helps in scheduling and providing timely assistance to students. 

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Ensure you have filled in all the required fields.
2.  Select your math level from the options provided.
3.  Provide your lesson plan if you have one.
4.  Schedule a suitable date and time for the tutor's assistance.
5.  Specify the time of day the tutor is available.
6.  Complete the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, Required): Enter your name for communication and reference purposes. 
* **Last Name** (text, Required): Enter your surname.
* **Email** (email, Required): Input your email address for contact and communication.
* **Math Level** (Select One, Optional): 
  * 1 - Basic
  * 2 - Intermediate
  * 3 - High School
  * 4 - University/ College
  * 5 - Master's/Advanced
* **Lesson Plan** (text, Optional): If you have specific lesson plans, provide the details here. 
* **Schedule** (date, Optional): Select the date you are available for the lesson. 
* **Tutor Availability** (time, Optional): Specify the time of day you are available for the lesson. 

## Tips
* Be sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* Check your email for any responses from the form administrator.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the form administrator for clarification.
