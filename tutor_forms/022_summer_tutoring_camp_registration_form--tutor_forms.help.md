# Summer Tutoring Camp Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Summer Tutoring Camp Registration Form is used to register students for the Summer Tutoring Camp program. Fill out this form to provide your name, contact information, and birthdate.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form by providing the required information as prompted.
2. Submit your completed form to proceed with the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional)
	+ Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional)
	+ Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional)
	+ Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional)
	+ Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Birthdate** (`birthdate`, date, optional)
	+ Enter your date of birth in the standard `MM/DD/YYYY` format.
