# Tutor Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for booking a tutor appointment for students.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Name** in the "Name" field.
2. Provide your **Email** address in the "Email" field.
3. Choose a **Date** for your appointment from the calendar.
4. Select your preferred **Start Time** and **End Time** for the appointment.
5. Choose the **Location** where you would like to have the appointment.
6. (Optional) Add any additional **Notes** about the appointment.
7. Select the **Assigned Tutor** from the list.
8. Choose the **Available Tutors** from the list (optional).
9. Click the **Submit** button to confirm your appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name**: Enter your name to identify yourself.
- **Email**: Enter your email address to help us contact you.
- **Date**: Choose a date for your appointment using the calendar.
- **Start Time**: Select your preferred start time for the appointment.
- **End Time**: Select your preferred end time for the appointment.
- **Location**: Choose the location for the appointment.
- **Notes**: Provide any additional information about the appointment (optional).
- **Assigned Tutor**: Select the tutor assigned to you.
- **Available Tutors**: Choose any additional tutors you're interested in meeting with (optional).
- **Submit**: Confirm your appointment and submit the form.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately to avoid any issues with your appointment.
- Be sure to choose your available tutors carefully.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact form@gic.mx.
