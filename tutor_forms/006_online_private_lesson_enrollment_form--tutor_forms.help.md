# online-private-lesson-enrollment-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for the private lesson enrollment process. It gathers student and instructor information, lesson duration, payment details, and other relevant details necessary for the successful execution of the private lesson.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information for the student, including their name.
2. Fill out the instructor's information.
3. Provide the start and end dates for the lesson.
4. Enter the duration of the lesson.
5. Choose the payment method.
6. Enter the payment details.
7. Check the payment status.
8. Acknowledge the instructor's and student's agreements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Private Lesson Enrollment Form (page 1)**: Fill out this form to provide general information about the private lesson.
* **Student Name (student_name)**: Enter the student's name.
* **Instructor Name (instructor_name)**: Enter the instructor's name.
* **Start Date (start_date)**: Enter the date the lesson will start.
* **End Date (end_date)**: Enter the date the lesson will end.
* **Lesson Duration (lesson_duration)**: Enter the duration of the lesson.
* **Lesson Fees (lesson_fees)**: Enter the lesson fees.
* **Payment Method (payment_method)**: Choose the payment method from the options provided.
* **Payment Details (payment_details)**: Provide additional payment details if necessary.
* **Payment Status (payment_status)**: Choose the current payment status from the options provided.
* **Instructor Agreement (instructor_agreement)**: Read and acknowledge the instructor's agreement to provide the lesson.
* **Student Agreement (student_agreement)**: Read and acknowledge the student's agreement to receive the lesson.
* **Payment Method and Status (payment_method and payment_status)**: Ensure that the payment method and status match the provided payment details.
* **Additional Tips**:
	+ Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any issues with the lesson execution.
	+ Review the agreements carefully before submitting the form to ensure you understand the terms and conditions.
	+ Keep the payment details confidential and secure.
