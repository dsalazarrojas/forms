# writing_center_appointment_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for students to request a writing center appointment to receive guidance on their writing assignments. It is crucial to provide accurate and complete information to ensure the most beneficial experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your Student ID or name, if available.
3. Describe the appointment request, providing as much detail as possible about the assignment and the help you need.
4. If necessary, provide additional schedule information, such as any conflicts or time constraints.
5. Include any additional student information that is not already provided in the first field.
6. Enter any additional schedule information that is not already provided in the first field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Info** (student_info): Your name or student ID, to help staff members identify you.
* **Appointment Request** (appointment_request): Describe the assignment you're working on and what kind of help you need with writing.
* **Schedule Info** (schedule_info): List any scheduling conflicts or limitations, to help us better coordinate your appointment.
* **Additional Student Info** (student_info_2): If you have any other relevant information about your schedule or assignment that's not already mentioned, include it here.
* **Additional Schedule Info** (schedule_info_2): If you need to provide further schedule information, enter it here.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when describing your appointment request.
* If you're unsure about what to enter in any field, ask your instructor or the writing center staff for guidance.
* If you need to cancel or reschedule an appointment, contact the writing center staff as soon as possible.
