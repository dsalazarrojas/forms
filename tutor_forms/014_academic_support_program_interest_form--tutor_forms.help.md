# Academic Support Program Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from students seeking academic support for their studies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name, email address, and phone number in the relevant fields.
2. Select all the subjects you need support in.
3. Choose your current academic level.
4. If you're taking a specific course, enter the course code.
5. Select the type of support you prefer.
6. Choose the days you're available for sessions.
7. Select the time blocks you're available for sessions.
8. Briefly describe your current academic challenges, if any.
9. Indicate if you've used our support services before.
10. Choose your preferred method of contact for follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name (inquirer_name)** (`Student Name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your full name, as it will appear on the support schedule and communication.
* **Email Address (inquirer_email)** (`Email Address`, `email`, `required`): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for updates and communication.
* **Phone Number (inquirer_phone)** (`Phone Number`, `text`, `required`): Enter a valid phone number where you can be reached for updates and communication.
* **Subjects of Interest (subjects_of_interest)** (`Subjects of Interest`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select all the subjects you need support in, such as Mathematics, Sciences, Writing or Composition, History or Social Studies, or Languages.
* **Current Level (level_of_study)** (`Current Level`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose your current academic level, whether it's High School, Undergraduate, or Graduate.
* **Course Code (specific_course_code)** (`Course Code`, `text`, `optional`): If you're taking a specific course, enter the course code, such as MATH 101 or ENG 202.
* **Preferred Type of Support (type_of_support)** (`Preferred Type of Support`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose the support method that suits you best, including Individual Tutoring, Group Study Sessions, Writing Lab, or Peer Mentoring.
* **Available Days (preferred_days)** (`Available Days`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select all the days you're available for sessions, including Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday.
* **Preferred Time Blocks (time_blocks)** (`Preferred Time Blocks`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Choose multiple time blocks you're available for sessions, such as Morning (8am-12pm), Afternoon (12pm-4pm), or Evening (4pm-8pm).
* **Briefly Describe Your Current Academic Challenges (academic_challenges)** (`Briefly Describe Your Current Academic Challenges`, `text`, `optional`): If you have any academic challenges, briefly describe them in this field.
* **Used Our Support Services Before (previous_support)** (`Used Our Support Services Before`, `select_one`, `required`): Indicate if you've used our support services before, either true or false.
* **Preferred Method for Follow-up (contact_method_preference)** (`Preferred Method for Follow-up`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose your preferred method for follow-up, including Email, Phone Call, or Text Message.
