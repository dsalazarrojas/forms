# Student Private Tuition Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Private Tuition Registration Form is designed to collect essential details from students and their parents/teachers for private tuition purposes. This form ensures that all relevant information is captured correctly, and no duplicate registrations occur for the same student.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's name.
2. Enter the student's contact number.
3. Provide the parent's name.
4. Enter the address of the student's school.
5. Enter the teacher's name.
6. Enter the address of the teacher's school.
7. Enter the student's email.
8. Enter the student's phone number.
9. Enter the teacher's email.
10. Enter the teacher's phone number.
11. Enter the student's date of birth (DOB).
12. Enter the student's DOB time.
13. Enter the teacher's DOB.
14. Enter the teacher's DOB time.
15. Enter the start date of the tuition session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's full name.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the student's contact number for further communication.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Enter the parent's name, if applicable.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the student's school address.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the student's email.
* **Message** (`message`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes for the tuition session.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter the school's name.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, optional): Enter the school's address.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Enter the teacher's name.
* **Teacher Address** (`teacher_address`, text, optional): Enter the teacher's address.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter the student's email.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Teacher Phone** (`teacher_phone`, text, optional): Enter the teacher's phone number.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, email, optional): Enter the teacher's email.
* **Student DOB** (`student_dob`, date, optional): Enter the student's date of birth.
* **Student DOB Time** (`student_dob_time`, time, optional): Enter the student's time of birth.
* **Teacher DOB** (`teacher_dob`, date, optional): Enter the teacher's date of birth.
* **Teacher DOB Time** (`teacher_dob_time`, time, optional): Enter the teacher's time of birth.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the tuition session.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly to avoid any issues during the tuition process.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authority for clarification.
* Double-check all entered information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
