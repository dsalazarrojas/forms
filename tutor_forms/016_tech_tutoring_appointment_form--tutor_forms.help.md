# Tech Tutoring Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tech Tutoring Appointment Form is used to collect information from students who are requesting a tech tutoring session. This form helps the tutoring service to assign a tutor and schedule the session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Select a date for your appointment in the "Date" field.
6. Select a time for your appointment in the "Time" field.
7. Choose the length of your appointment in the "Length of Appointment" field.
8. If you have a preferred tutor, select them from the list in the "Preferred Tutor" field.
9. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
10. Click the "Submit" button to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address in this field.
* **Phone Number** (text): Enter your phone number in this field.
* **Date** (date): Select a date for your appointment in this field.
* **Time** (time): Select a time for your appointment in this field.
* **Length of Appointment** (number): Choose the length of your appointment (e.g., 1 hour, 2 hours, etc.).
* **Preferred Tutor** (select multiple): If you have a preferred tutor, select them from the list in this field.
* **Notes** (note): Add any additional notes or comments about your appointment in this field.
* **Submit** (text): Click this button to submit your form and schedule your appointment.
