# Virtual Hardware Tutor Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users on their experience with our virtual hardware tutorials. Your input will help us identify areas for improvement and create more effective and user-friendly tutorials.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply select the relevant options that apply to your experience with our virtual hardware tutorials. You can select one or multiple answers for each question. Once you're finished, submit your form to share your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Virtual Hardware Tutor Survey** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This is the title of our survey. Please enter your thoughts about your experience with our virtual hardware tutorials.
* **Satisfaction** (`2`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with our virtual hardware tutorials, on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Satisfaction Reasons** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check the reasons that contributed to your satisfaction score. You can select one or multiple reasons:
	+ Technical issues
	+ Content quality
	+ Interface clarity
* **Technical Issues** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check the technical issues you encountered during your experience with our virtual hardware tutorials. You can select one or multiple issues:
	+ Audio issues
	+ Video issues
	+ Connection issues
* **Learning Impact** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Please share your thoughts about how our virtual hardware tutorials have impacted your learning.
* **Suggestions** (`6`, `text`, required: false): Do you have any suggestions for improving our virtual hardware tutorials? Please share them with us.
