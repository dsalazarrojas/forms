# Tutoring Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and store information about clients and their tutoring contracts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter client information in the "Client Information" section.
2. Select a regular schedule in the "Tutoring Schedule" section.
3. Choose the contract terms in the "Contract Terms" section.
4. Select the tutoring frequency in the "Tutoring Frequency" section.
5. Enter the rate in the "Rate" section.
6. Choose the currency for the contract in the "Currency" section.
7. Add any additional notes in the "Notes" section.
8. Select the start date of the contract in the "Contract Start Date" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information** (`client_info`, text, optional): Enter the client's name, address, or other relevant details.
* **Tutoring Schedule** (`tutoring_schedule`, time, optional): Enter a regular schedule for the client's tutoring sessions.
* **Contract Terms** (`contract_terms`, select_one, optional): Select the term of the contract, e.g., Term 1, Term 2, or Term 3.
* **Tutoring Frequency** (`tutoring_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how often the client will receive tutoring, e.g., Term 1, Term 2, or Term 3.
* **Rate** (`rate`, number, optional): Enter the rate for the contract in the chosen currency.
* **Currency** (`currency`, select_one, optional): Select the currency for the contract, e.g., USD, EUR, or Other.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional information or comments about the contract.
* **Contract Start Date** (`contract_start_date`, date, optional): Choose the start date of the contract.
