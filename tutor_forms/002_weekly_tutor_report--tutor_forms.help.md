# Weekly Tutor Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to report the progress of a student after a lesson. It should be filled out by the tutor who conducted the lesson and submitted on the same day it was conducted. The form provides fields for essential details such as student name, student ID, lesson date and time, lesson topic, student's reaction, next steps, and submission details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure that you only fill out one form per student per week.
2. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
3. Enter the student's ID number in the "Student ID" field.
4. Select the date and time of the lesson in the "Lesson Date" and "Lesson Time" fields, respectively.
5. Enter the topic of the lesson in the "Lesson Topic" field.
6. Describe how the student reacted to the lesson in the "Lesson Outcome" field.
7. Enter any next steps planned for the student in the "Next Steps" field.
8. Enter the date and time you submitted the form in the "Submission Date" and "Submission Time" fields, respectively.
9. Enter your name in the "Submitted By" field.
10. Enter your email address in the "Submitted By Email" field (if applicable).
11. Enter any additional comments in the "Comments" field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name.
* **Student ID**: Enter the student's ID number.
* **Lesson Date**: Enter the date of the lesson.
* **Lesson Time**: Enter the time of the lesson.
* **Lesson Topic**: Enter the topic of the lesson.
* **Lesson Outcome**: Describe how the student reacted to the lesson.
* **Next Steps**: Enter any next steps planned for the student.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date you submitted the form.
* **Submission Time**: Enter the time you submitted the form.
* **Submitted By**: Enter your name.
* **Submitted By Email**: Enter your email address (if you're submitting on behalf of your team).
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments (if applicable).

## Tips

* Ensure that you submit the form on the same day as the lesson was conducted.
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to ensure accurate reporting.
* If you're submitting on behalf of your team, ensure that you enter your email address in the "Submitted By Email" field.
