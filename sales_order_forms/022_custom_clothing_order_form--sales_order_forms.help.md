# Custom Clothing Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a custom clothing order.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields marked as "Required".
2. For fields with options, select the correct option that best represents your choice.
3. Double-check your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Information** (customer_info, text, Optional): Enter your name, address, and other relevant details as desired.
- **Order Details** (order_details, text, Optional): Describe your custom clothing order in as much detail as needed.
- **Shipping Address** (shipping_address, text, Optional): Enter your shipping address where you would like to receive your order.
- **Order Date** (order_date, date, Optional): Enter the date you would like to place your order by.
- **Customer Email** (customer_email, email, Optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.
- **Customer Phone** (customer_phone, text, Optional): Enter your phone number for any additional information or questions.
- **Order Comments** (order_comments, note, Optional): Add any additional comments or messages you would like to include with your order.
- **Shipping Method** (shipping_method, select_one, Required): Choose how you would like to receive your order (Shipping by Mail, Shipping by Hand Delivery, or Local Pickup).
- **Shipping Cost** (shipping_cost, number, Optional): Enter an estimate of the shipping cost (not required but can help with order fulfillment).
- **Customer Notes** (customer_notes, note, Optional): Add any additional notes or messages you would like to include with your order.
- **Order Status** (order_status, select_one, Required): Choose the current status of your order (Pending, Processing, Delivered, Cancelled).
- **Customer Gender** (customer_gender, select_one, Required): Choose your gender.
- **Customer Age** (customer_age, number, Optional): Enter your age.
- **Customer Height** (customer_height, number, Optional): Enter your height.
- **Customer Phone** (customer_phone, text, Optional): Enter your phone number for any additional information or questions.
- **Order Comments** (order_comments, note, Optional): Add any additional comments or messages you would like to include with your order.
