# Fall/Winter 2020 Color Street Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect customer information for a sale order related to the Fall/Winter 2020 line. Please fill out this form to provide the necessary details for your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Fall/Winter 2020 Line" field with the specific product line you are interested in purchasing.
2. Enter your customer name in the "Customer Name" field.
3. Enter your address in the "Address 1" and "Address 2" fields.
4. Enter your city in the "City" field.
5. Select your state from the "State" options, either "Yes" or "No".
6. Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Fall/Winter 2020 Line**: Enter the specific Fall/Winter 2020 product line you are interested in purchasing.
  - This field is not required, but it helps us understand your specific product selection.
- **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
  - This field is not required, but it's used to associate your order with your identity.
- **Address 1**: Enter your address line 1.
  - This field is not required, but it's used to complete your address.
- **Address 2**: Enter your address line 2.
  - This field is not required, but it's used to complete your address.
- **City**: Enter the city where you reside.
  - This field is not required, but it's used to complete your address.
- **State**: Select "Yes" if you have a specific state preference, "No" otherwise.
  - This field is not required, but it helps us understand your state preference.
- **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
  - This field is not required, but it's used to complete your address.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
  - This field is not required, but it's used to contact you regarding your order.
