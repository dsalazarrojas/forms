# stephanies_color_street_on_hand_stock_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "stephanies_color_street_on_hand_stock_form" form is designed for sales orders, particularly for stock items. The form collects relevant information related to sales orders, such as stock item status, color, quantity, date, and other details.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined in the "How To Complete" section.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Sales Order Form
* **Sales Order Form**: A brief summary of the sales order.
### Stock Item
* **Stock Item**: Indicate if the stock item is available or not.
* Select "Yes" if the stock item is available for sale, or "No" otherwise.
### Color
* **Color**: Select one or multiple colors for the stock item.
* Use this field to specify colors that match the stock item's color.
### Quantity
* **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the stock item.
* Ensure to enter a valid numeric value.
### Date
* **Date**: Schedule a specific date for the sales order.
* Enter a valid date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
### Time
* **Time**: Schedule a specific time for the sales order.
* Enter a valid time in the format HH:MM.
### Nail Polish
* **Nail Polish**: Any additional comments or notes about the stock item.
* Use this field to provide any extra information about the stock item.
### Email
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* Ensure to enter a valid email address.
### Phone
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* Use this field to provide any contact information.
### Additional Info
* **Additional Info**: Any further comments or notes about the sales order.
* Use this field to provide any extra information about the sales order.
