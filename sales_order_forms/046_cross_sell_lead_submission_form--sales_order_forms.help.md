# Cross Sell Lead Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cross Sell Lead Submission Form is used to submit leads for cross-selling opportunities. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill in the required fields with the relevant information. The form has several sections and fields that need to be completed. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the company that the customer belongs to.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person at the company.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Company Title** (`company_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the contact person in the company.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department of the contact person in the company.
* **Priority** (`priority`, select_one, required): Select the priority of the opportunity. Options are 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, required): Enter the date for follow-up.
* **Lead Status** (`lead_status`, select_one, required): Select the lead status. Options are 'Active' and 'Inactive'.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, select_one, required): Select the assigned user.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the lead.
* **Opportunity Type** (`opportunity_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of opportunity. Options are 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **Customer Type** (`customer_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of customer. Options are 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the customer.
* **Lead Type** (`lead_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of lead.
* **Account Name** (`account_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the account.
* **Account Type** (`account_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of account. Options are 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, optional): Enter the website of the company.
* **Industry** (`company_industry`, select_multiple, optional): Select the industry of the company. Options are 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **Follow-up User** (`follow_up_user`, select_one, optional): Select the user for follow-up. Options are 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): Enter the date for follow-up.
* **Lead ID** (`lead_id`, text, optional): Enter the ID of the lead.
* **Opportunity** (`opportunity`, text, optional): Enter the opportunity.
* **Description** (`description`, note, optional): Enter the description of the lead.
* **Sales Person** (`sales_person`, text, optional): Enter the sales person.

**Tips**

* Please ensure that the company and customer names match existing records before submitting a new lead.
* This form should only be used for leads where the customer has already been contacted or engaged by the sales team.
* The form is used to gather information for cross-selling opportunities, so please only submit leads that have a high potential for cross-selling.
