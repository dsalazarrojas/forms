# nails-n-lattes-with-tabitha-wishlist-and-shopping-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture sales order information for nails and lattes with Tabitha's wishlist and shopping form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Fill in your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select a date in the "Date" field.
6. Select a time in the "Time" field.
7. Add any additional notes in the "Note" field.
8. Choose whether or not you have a wishlist in the "Wishlist" field.
9. Select the items you would like to purchase in the "Items" field.
10. Choose the category for your order in the "Category" field.
11. Confirm your category selection in the "Confirm Category" field.
12. Enter the ID of the form in the "Form ID" field.
13. Provide a description of your order in the "Description" field.
14. Select the tool assigned to your order in the "Assigned Tool" field.
15. Enter your user ID in the "User ID" field.
16. Enter the ID of the order in the "Order ID" field.
17. Enter the version of the form in the "Form Version" field.
18. Enter the version of the user in the "User Version" field.
19. Enter the status of the form in the "Form Status" field.
20. Enter the status of the order in the "Order Status" field.
21. Add any comments about the form in the "Form Comments" field.
22. Enter the timestamp of your submission in the "Timestamp" field.
23. Enter any additional information in the "Custom Field 1" field.
