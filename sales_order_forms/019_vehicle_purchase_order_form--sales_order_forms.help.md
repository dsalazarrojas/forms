# Vehicle Purchase Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture essential information for a vehicle purchase transaction, ensuring accurate and complete data collection.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

*   Enter your **Customer Name** (`Customer Name`) - this field requires your name for our records.
*   Enter your **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`) - provide your phone number where we can reach you.
*   Enter your **Email Address** (`Email Address`) - we need your email address for secure communication.
*   Choose the **Vehicle Year** (`Vehicle Year`) - select the year of the vehicle you're interested in purchasing.
*   Choose the **Vehicle Make** (`Vehicle Make`) - select the make of the vehicle from the options provided (e.g., Toyota, Ford, Honda, Tesla, BMW).
*   Enter the **Vehicle Model** (`Vehicle Model`) - type in the model of the vehicle you're interested in.
*   Choose the **Vehicle Color** (`Vehicle Color`) - select the color of the vehicle from the available options (e.g., Blue, Red, Green, Yellow, Black, White).
*   Enter the **Purchase Price** (`Purchase Price`) - type in the price you're willing to pay for the vehicle.
*   Enter the **Sale Price** (`Sale Price`) - type in the sale price of the vehicle.
*   Enter the **Discount** (`Discount`) - enter the discount amount if applicable.
*   Calculate the **Total Price** (`Total Price`) - automatically generated based on the purchase and sale price.
*   Choose the **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`) - select the payment method from the options provided (e.g., Cash, Credit Card, Bank Transfer).
*   Enter the **Delivery Date** (`Delivery Date`) - choose the date you'd like to receive your vehicle.
*   Choose the **Delivery Time** (`Delivery Time`) - select the time you'd like to receive your vehicle.
*   Choose the **Vehicle Status** (`Vehicle Status`) - select the status of the vehicle from the options provided (e.g., Active, Inactive, Pending).
*   Enter the **Vehicle Type** (`Vehicle Type`) - select the type of vehicle (e.g., Car, Truck, Motorcycle).
*   Choose the **Vehicle Category** (`Vehicle Category`) - select the category of the vehicle from the options provided (e.g., New, Used, Certified Pre-Owned).
