# Factory Automation Chipset Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Factory Automation Chipset Order Form is a tool for factory automation customers to order chipsets for their operations. This form is used to collect important information about the company, shipping address, chipset selection, quantities, and any additional order notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Company Details**: Enter your company's details, including any relevant information that will help us better understand your needs.
2. **Shipping Address**: Provide your shipping address, including any relevant contact information.
3. **Chipset Selection**: Select the chipsets you would like to order, choosing one or more of the options provided.
4. **Quantities**: Enter the quantities of each selected chipset you would like to order.
5. **Order Notes**: Add any additional information or comments about your order, such as special instructions or preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Company Details** (`company_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your company's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
- **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your shipping address, including street address, city, state, and zip code.
- **Chipset Selection** (`chipset_selection`, select_multiple, required/true): Select one or more of the chipsets you would like to order from the provided options.
- **Quantities** (`quantities`, text, required/true): Enter the quantities of each selected chipset you would like to order.
- **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, text, required/optional): Add any additional information or comments about your order, such as special instructions or preferences.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- Be as specific as possible when entering quantities to ensure accurate ordering.
- If you have any special requests or instructions, include them in the Order Notes field.
