# Robotics End Arm Tooling Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Robotics End Arm Tooling Order Form is a sales order form designed to gather necessary information for ordering robotics tooling tools.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Please fill in the application info section with any relevant details about your order.
2. Select the tooling products you are interested in ordering (e.g. Robot Arm, End Effector, Suction Cup).
3. Choose how you would like to submit your order (Online Order or Phone Order).
4. Provide detailed application information if needed.
5. Enter your contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Info (text)**: This field is for entering any additional information about your order. Please provide any relevant details you think the sales team might need to know.
* **Tooling Products (Select Multiple)**: Here, you can choose from the following options:
  • Robot Arm
  • End Effector
  • Suction Cup
* **Submit Order (Select One)**: Choose how you would like to submit your order. The options are:
  • Online Order
  • Phone Order
* **Application Info Details (text)**: If you have any additional details about your application, please enter them here.
* **Contact Info (text)**: Please enter your contact information so we can get in touch with you.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate processing of your order.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
- Our sales team will review and process your order promptly.
