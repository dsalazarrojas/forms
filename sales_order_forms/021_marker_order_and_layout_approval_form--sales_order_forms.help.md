# Marker Order And Layout Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Marker Order And Layout Approval Form is used to approve or reject marker orders and layouts. It's a crucial step in the sales process, ensuring that the order details are accurate and the marker layout is approved before it's sent to the customer.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill in the required fields**: Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately, including:
	* `Contact Person`
	* `Payment Method`
	* `Marker Layout`
	* `Approval Status`
2. **Choose correct payment method**: Select the correct payment method from the list of options.
3. **Choose correct marker layout**: Select the correct marker layout from the list of options.
4. **Select approval status`: Select the correct approval status from the list of options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Marker Order Details**: A text field for entering the details of the marker order.
* **Customer Contact**: A text field for entering the customer's contact information.
* **Contact Person`: A dropdown menu for selecting the contact person responsible for the order.
* **Order Status**: A dropdown menu for selecting the current status of the order (New, In Progress, Completed).
* **Order Date`: A date field for entering the date the order was placed.
* **Delivery Date`: A date field for entering the expected delivery date.
* **Order Total`: A numeric field for entering the total cost of the order.
* **Payment Method`: A dropdown menu for selecting the payment method (Cash, Credit Card, PayPal).
* **Marker Layout`: A dropdown menu for selecting the marker layout (Landscape, Portrait, Square).
* **Approval Status`: A dropdown menu for selecting the approval status (Approved, Rejected, Pending).
* **Approval Notes`: A text field for entering any notes or comments about the approval process.
* **Comments`: A text field for entering any comments or feedback about the order.
* **Marker Notes`: A text field for entering any notes or comments about the marker.
* **Customer Notes`: A text field for entering any notes or comments from the customer.
* **Custom Fields`: A text field for entering any custom fields or information.
* **Custom Fields` is not a field for entering actual custom fields, it's actually a field for entering any additional information or notes. 

Note: Some fields may not be visible or editable for certain users, depending on their role or access permissions. If you're unsure about a field or its purpose, please reach out to your administrator for clarification.
