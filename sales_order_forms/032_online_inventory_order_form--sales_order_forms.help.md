# Online Inventory Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Inventory Order Form is used by sales teams to record orders from customers and manage inventory effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the **Customer Details** page. Enter the customer's name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
2. On the next page, select the **Item Ordered** field and enter the quantity of the item ordered.
3. Enter the **Total** value of the order in the next field.
4. Fill out the **Date and Time** fields to accurately record the order date and time.
5. Add any **Notes** about the order in the next field.
6. Enter the **Contact Information** for the customer, including their email and phone number.
7. Finally, select the **Assigned Tool** and **Submitted By** options to specify the person responsible for the order and any relevant options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details (customer_details)**: Enter the customer's name and contact information to ensure accurate records.
* **Item Ordered (item_ordered)**: Enter the quantity of the item ordered by the customer.
* **Total (total)**: Enter the value of the order in the format $X.XX.
* **Date Ordered (date_ordered)**: Enter the date the order was placed in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time Ordered (time_ordered)**: Enter the time the order was placed in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Notes (notes)**: Add any additional comments or information about the order.
* **Contact Information (contact_info)**: Enter the customer's email and phone number for follow-up.
* **Email (email)**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Assigned Tool (assigned_tool)**: Select the tool or person responsible for the order.
* **Submitted By (submitted_by)**: Select the person or team submitting the order.
