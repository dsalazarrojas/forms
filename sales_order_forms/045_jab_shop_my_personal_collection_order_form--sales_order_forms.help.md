# JAB Shop My Personal Collection Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The JAB Shop My Personal Collection Order Form is a tool for customers to place orders for items from your personal collection. This form allows you to gather information about the customer, their shipping address, and the products they are interested in purchasing. By using this form, you will be able to create a detailed record of the customer's order and track its progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer's information, including their name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide the customer's shipping address, including zip code, city, state, and country.
3. Select the products you are interested in purchasing and enter the quantity for each.
4. Add any additional notes about the order in the "Notes" field.
5. Review the order summary to ensure all information is accurate.
6. Choose whether to continue shopping or confirm your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's name and contact information.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's shipping address.
* **Zip** (`shipping_info_4`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's zip code.
* **City** (`shipping_info_2`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's city.
* **State** (`shipping_info_3`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's state.
* **Country** (`shipping_info_7`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's country.
* **Products** (`product_info_1`, `text`, required): Select the products you are interested in purchasing.
* **Product Name** (`product_info_1_1`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the product you are interested in purchasing.
* **Quantity** (`product_info_1_2`, `number`, required): Enter the quantity of the product you are interested in purchasing.
* **Order Summary** (`order_summary`, `text`, required): Review and confirm the order details.
* **Order Number** (`order_summary_1`, `text`, required): Enter the order number (will be automatically generated).
* **Order Date** (`order_summary_2`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the order.
* **Total Price** (`order_summary_5`, `number`, required): Enter the total price of the order.
* **Notes** (`order_summary_4`, `note`, required): Add any additional notes about the order.
* **Email** (`order_summary_7`, `email`, required): Enter the customer's email.
* **Phone** (`order_summary_8`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Confirm** (`order_summary_8`, `select_multiple`, optional): Confirm or cancel the order (select multiple for multiple options).
* **Continue shopping** (`order_summary_9`, `select_one`, optional): Choose to continue shopping or confirm the order.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes about the order (optional).
* **Note 1** (`notes_1`, `note`, optional): Add a note about the order (optional).
* **Note 2** (`notes_2`, `note`, optional): Add another note about the order (optional).
* **Note 3** (`notes_3`, `note`, optional): Add yet another note about the order (optional).

## Tips
* Make sure to review the order summary carefully before confirming the order.
* If you need to make changes to the order, you can do so by contacting the customer service team.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, please ask a team member for assistance.
