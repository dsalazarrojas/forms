# Food Franchise POS Terminal Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a digital sales order form used in a food franchise point-of-sale terminal. It is designed to be completed by the terminal operator to collect information about sales, including terminal details, customer information, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the terminal information on page 1.
2. Move on to filling out the order information, including the order type and any special instructions.
3. Next, enter the customer information, including their name and contact details.
4. Provide shipping information, including the shipping address and any special instructions.
5. Enter the payment information, including the payment method and any relevant details.
6. Add any additional order notes or comments as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sales Order Form** (`title`, `text`, optional): This field is the main title of the form and serves as a placeholder for the form title.
* **terminal_info** (`terminal_info`, `text`, optional): This field is for the terminal number or other terminal-specific information.
* **order_info** (`order_info`, `text`, optional): This field is for entering order information, such as the order type or any special instructions.
* **customer_info** (`customer_info`, `text`, optional): This field is for entering customer information, including their name and contact details.
* **shipping_info** (`shipping_info`, `text`, optional): This field is for entering shipping information, including the shipping address and any special instructions.
* **payment_info** (`payment_info`, `text`, optional): This field is for entering payment information, including the payment method and any relevant details.
* **order_notes** (`order_notes`, `text`, optional): This field is for adding any additional order notes or comments.
* **submission** (`submission`, `text`, optional): This field is for submitting the form, which will be sent to the relevant systems for processing.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all relevant fields to ensure accurate sales data collection.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to your sales manager or customer support for assistance.
