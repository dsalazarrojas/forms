# Mobile Device Sales Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Mobile Device Sales Submission" form is designed to collect sales information about mobile devices. It is intended to be used by mobile device sellers to submit their sales information for processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the Seller Details section, which includes information about the seller such as their name, email, phone number, and address.
2. Provide details about the mobile device being sold, including its specs, model, year, color, and condition.
3. Add up to 4 photos of the device.
4. Select the seller from the dropdown list.
5. Enter the price of the mobile device.
6. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Seller Details** (`seller_details`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the seller's name and other identifying information.
* **Device Specs** (`device_specs`, text, optional):
	+ Provide details about the mobile device being sold, such as its storage, processor, and other specs.
* **Photos** (`photos`, text, optional):
	+ Add up to 4 photos of the mobile device.
* **Price** (`price`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the price of the mobile device.
* **Seller** (`seller_select`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the seller from the dropdown list.
* **Seller Name** (`seller_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the seller's name.
* **Seller Email** (`seller_email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter the seller's email address.
* **Seller Phone** (`seller_phone`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the seller's phone number.
* **Device Model** (`device_model`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the mobile device's model number.
* **Year** (`device_year`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the year of the mobile device.
* **Color** (`device_color`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the color of the mobile device.
* **Price** (`device_price`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the price of the mobile device.
* **Condition** (`device_condition`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the condition of the mobile device (New, Used, or Refurbished).
* **Device Photos** (`device_photo1`, `device_photo2`, `device_photo3`, `device_photo4`, text, optional):
	+ Add up to 4 photos of the mobile device.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
* Double-check the seller's information to ensure it is correct.
* Use clear and high-quality photos to showcase the mobile device.
* Enter the correct price and condition of the mobile device.
