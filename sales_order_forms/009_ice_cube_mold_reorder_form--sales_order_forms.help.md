# Ice Cube Mold Reorder Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers reorder ice cube molds in an efficient and accurate manner.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Customer Information**: Please fill in the customer's details, including name, contact number, and email.
2. Choose the **products** you would like to reorder by selecting the relevant options.
3. Enter the **quantity** of each product you would like to reorder.
4. Click on the **Submit** button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer**: This field is used to collect the customer's name. Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact**: This field is used to collect the customer's contact number. Please enter your contact number in the format XXX-XXX-XXXX.
* **Email**: This field is used to collect the customer's email address. Please enter a valid email address.
* **Products**: This field is used to select the products you would like to reorder. Please choose from the available options.
* **Quantity**: This field is used to enter the quantity of each product you would like to reorder. Please enter a whole number.
* **Submit**: This button is used to submit your order. Please review your order carefully before clicking this button.
