# Sales Process Template For Electricians - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture information for sales orders from electricians. It helps to ensure that all parties involved in the sales process have a clear understanding of the order details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
2.  Review each field carefully to ensure you've provided the necessary information.
3.  Make sure to sign and date the form when required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your contact information for easy communication and follow-up.
- **Job Request** (`job_request`, `text`, required: false): Describe the job or task you're requesting.
- **Customer Requirements** (`customer_requirements`, `text`, required: false): List any specific requirements the customer has for the job.
- **Sales Rep** (`sales_rep`, `select_one`, required: false): Check 'Yes' if you're available to take on the job; otherwise, check 'No'.
- **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Check how you'd like to be contacted (e.g., call, email, text).
- **Contact Method Details** (`contact_method_details`, `text`, required: false): Provide additional details on your preferred contact method (e.g., phone number, email).
- **Job Details** (`job_details`, `text`, required: false): Describe the job details, including location, scope of work, and any other relevant information.
- **Job Requirements** (`job_requirements`, `text`, required: false): List any special requirements for the job, such as equipment or material needed.
- **Sales Process Steps** (`sales_process_steps`, `text`, required: false): Outline the steps you'd take to complete the job, from initial contact to final completion.
- **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, `text`, required: false): Sign and date this section to confirm your agreement to the order details.
- **Sales Rep Signature** (`sales_rep_signature`, `text`, required: false): Sign and date this section to confirm you've reviewed and agreed upon the order details.

## Tips

- Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately to avoid misunderstandings.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all information is complete and correct.
- Make sure to sign and date all required fields.
- Use this form as a guide; if you have specific needs or concerns not covered, discuss them with your sales representative or customer directly.
