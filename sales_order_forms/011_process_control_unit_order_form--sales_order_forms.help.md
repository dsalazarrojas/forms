# Process Control Unit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Process Control Unit Order Form is used to capture information regarding the selection of units for a sales order. It guides the user through the selection of units, delivery details, and payment information to ensure accurate and complete sales order processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. On the first page, select the desired unit selections from the list of options provided.
2. Proceed to the second page to provide delivery details.
3. On the third page, choose the payment method for the order.
4. On the subsequent pages, select any additional unit selections as needed.
5. On the final page, specify any payment terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Unit Selections (Page 1)
* **Unit Selections**: Select all the units you want to purchase for this sales order. You can choose 'Yes' for each available unit.
### Delivery Details (Page 2)
* **Delivery Details**: Enter the delivery details for this sales order.
### Payments (Page 3)
* **Payments**: Select the payment method for this sales order. Options include Online Payment, Offline Payment, and Bank Transfer.
### Unit Selections 1 (Page 4)
* **Unit Selections 1**: Select any additional unit selections as needed.
### Unit Selections 2 (Page 5)
* **Unit Selections 2**: Choose one unit from the list of options provided.
### Payment Terms (Page 6)
* **Payment Terms**: Specify any payment terms for this sales order.

## Tips
* Make sure to select all relevant unit selections to ensure accurate sales order processing.
* Choose the correct payment method to avoid any potential issues.
* Ensure that payment terms are clearly specified to avoid any confusion.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are completed.
