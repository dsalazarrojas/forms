# Machine Vision Camera Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Machine Vision Camera Order Form is a form used to collect information related to camera orders, including camera type, product type, application details, and various other sales and order related details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the camera type you need for your application.
2. Select all applicable product types.
3. Provide any additional application details.
4. Enter your shipping details, including shipping address.
5. Select any other relevant options as needed.
6. Enter any additional order comments or notes.
7. Enter your customer information, including customer name, customer company, and customer email.
8. Select a sales person or sales team members involved in the sale.
9. Enter any sales notes.
10. Enter the delivery date and time.
11. Enter the product ID, product name, product quantity, and product price.
12. Finally, review all the information you have entered to ensure it is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Camera Type** (`Camera Type`, select_one, required): Select the type of camera you need for your application.
- **Product Type** (`Product Type`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable product types.
- **Application Details** (`Application Details`, text, optional): Provide any additional details about the application of the camera.
- **Shipping Details** (`Shipping Details`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you need this information for shipping details.
- **Sales Details** (`Sales Details`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable sales details.
- **Customer Information** (`Customer Information`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you want to provide customer information.
- **Order Comments** (`Order Comments`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable order comments.
- **Shipping Address** (`Shipping Address`, text, optional): Enter the shipping address of the customer.
- **Application Note** (`Application Note`, text, optional): Enter a note for application details.
- **Customer Phone** (`Customer Phone`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you want to provide customer phone number.
- **Shipping Email** (`Shipping Email`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you want to provide shipping email.
- **Customer Email** (`Customer Email`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you want to provide customer email.
- **Sales Person** (`Sales Person`, select_multiple, optional): Select the sales person involved in the sale.
- **Sales Team** (`Sales Team`, select_multiple, optional): Select the sales team members involved in the sale.
- **Order Notes** (`Order Notes`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable order notes.
- **Delivery Date** (`Delivery Date`, date, optional): Enter the delivery date of the order.
- **Delivery Time** (`Delivery Time`, time, optional): Enter the delivery time of the order.
- **Product ID** (`Product ID`, text, optional): Enter the product ID.
- **Product Name** (`Product Name`, text, optional): Enter the product name.
- **Customer Name** (`Customer Name`, text, optional): Enter the customer name.
- **Customer Company** (`Customer Company`, text, optional): Enter the customer company.
- **Product Quantity** (`Product Quantity`, number, optional): Enter the product quantity.
- **Product Price** (`Product Price`, number, optional): Enter the product price.
- **Customer Address** (`Customer Address`, text, optional): Enter the customer address.
- **Sales Notes** (`Sales Notes`, text, optional): Enter sales notes for the order.
