# Machine Vision Camera Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Machine Vision Camera Order Form is a form used to collect information related to camera orders, including camera type, product type, application details, and various other sales and order related details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the camera type you need for your application.
2. Select all applicable product types.
3. Provide any additional application details.
4. Enter your shipping details, including shipping address.
5. Select any other relevant options as needed.
6. Enter any additional order comments or notes.
7. Enter your customer information, including customer name, customer company, and customer email.
8. Select a sales person or sales team members involved in the sale.
9. Enter any sales notes.
10. Enter the delivery date and time.
11. Enter the product ID, product name, product quantity, and product price.
12. Finally, review all the information you have entered to ensure it is accurate and complete.
