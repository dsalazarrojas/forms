# Merchandising Mannequin Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Merchandising Mannequin Order Form is designed to collect information about custom-made mannequins for sales orders. This form gathers details about the base product, mannequin, custom requests, shipping, payment, customer information, and sales information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Base Information section with your name, contact information, and other essential details.
2. Provide information about the mannequin, including its type, size, and any specific features or requests.
3. Share any custom requests or special requirements for the mannequin.
4. Enter your shipping details, including the shipping address and any specific instructions.
5. Provide payment information, including the payment method and amount.
6. List the mannequin orders, including the quantity and any specific orders.
7. Share your customer information, including your name and contact details.
8. Enter your shipping address to ensure accurate delivery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Base Information** (`base_info`): This field is for your basic information, where you can enter your name, contact details, and any other relevant information about yourself or your company.
* **Mannequin Information** (`mannequin_info`): This field is for providing specific details about the mannequin, such as its type, size, and any custom features or requests.
* **Custom Requests** (`custom_requests`): This field is for sharing any custom requests or special requirements for the mannequin.
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`): This field is for entering your shipping address and any specific shipping instructions.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`): This field is for providing payment information, including the payment method and amount.
* **Mannequin Orders** (`mannequin_orders`): This field is for listing the mannequin orders, including the quantity and any specific orders.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`): This field is for sharing your customer information, including your name and contact details.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`): This field is for entering your shipping address to ensure accurate delivery.
* **Sales Info** (`sales_info`): This field is for providing sales information, which is not explicitly defined in the form fields but might be used for internal purposes.

## Tips
Make sure to double-check all fields before submitting the form to avoid errors in processing. Ensure that all required fields are filled out, and if a field is not required, it's up to you to decide whether or not to fill it in.
