# International Sales Action Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to guide international sales teams through a checklist of key actions and considerations for successful sales operations. The form ensures that all necessary steps are taken and all relevant documentation is reviewed before proceeding with international sales activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the "export_documentation" field on page 1, ensuring that all required export documents are complete and accurate.
2. On page 2, review the "review_documentation" field to ensure that all documentation is complete and meets company standards.
3. On page 3, enter the "compliance_steps" field to document any compliance-related actions taken.
4. On page 4, enter the "logistics_steps" field to document any logistics-related actions taken.
5. On page 5, review the "review_compliance" field to ensure that all compliance steps are complete and accurate.
6. On page 6, review the "review_logistics" field to ensure that all logistics steps are complete and accurate.
7. On page 7, review the "review_all_steps" field to ensure that all steps are complete and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **export_documentation** (Page 1, text, required): Enter a detailed description of all relevant export documents, such as commercial invoices, customs documents, and shipping documents.
- **review_documentation** (Page 2, text, required): Enter a detailed review of all documentation, including any discrepancies or issues found.
- **compliance_steps** (Page 3, text, required): Describe the compliance-related actions taken, such as obtaining necessary licenses or certifications.
- **logistics_steps** (Page 4, text, required): Describe the logistics-related actions taken, such as arranging transportation or storage.
- **review_compliance** (Page 5, text, required): Review and describe the compliance steps taken, ensuring they align with company policies.
- **review_logistics** (Page 6, text, required): Review and describe the logistics steps taken, ensuring they align with company policies.
- **review_all_steps** (Page 7, text, required): Review and describe all steps taken, ensuring they align with company policies and procedures.
## Tips
- Double-check all fields for accuracy and completeness before proceeding.
- Ensure that all compliance and logistics steps are taken in accordance with company policies and procedures.
- Keep a record of all steps taken and actions completed to ensure accountability and transparency.
