# Shelf Talker Printing Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shelf Talker Printing Order Form is designed to collect sales order information from customers. This form gathers details such as customer contact information, order specifics, delivery information, and payment terms to enable efficient processing and fulfillment of orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer's contact information, including their name, email, phone number, and company title.
2. Select the desired products and artwork files related to the order.
3. Provide delivery details, including the delivery date and time.
4. Specify the payment terms and method.
5. Enter any additional order notes or notes for the customer.
6. Finally, review and submit the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **artwork_files**: Choose one or more artwork files that you would like to include with your order. Selecting multiple files will allow you to upload multiple artworks.
* **delivery_details**: Provide the delivery date and time in this field. Please ensure that this information is accurate to avoid any delays or miscommunication.
* **payment_info**: Enter the payment terms and method in this field. This will help us process the payment efficiently.
