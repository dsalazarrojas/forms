# Order Management Workflow Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Order Management Workflow Form is used to collect and manage information about sales orders. This form helps ensure that all relevant details are recorded and tracked, allowing for efficient order processing and customer communication. By filling out this form, you will be able to view and update customer details, shipping information, order status, comments, customer notes, and salesperson notes.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To begin, select the "sales_order_details" page.
*   Enter the sales order details in the corresponding text field.
*   Click on the "Next" button to proceed to the next page.
*   On the "customer_details" page, enter the customer's details in the text field.
*   Click on the "Next" button to proceed to the next page.
*   On the "shipping_details" page, enter the shipping details in the text field.
*   Click on the "Next" button to proceed to the next page.
*   On the "order_status" page, select the current status of the order from the dropdown menu.
*   Click on the "Next" button to proceed to the next page.
*   On the "order_comments" page, enter any comments about the order in the text field.
*   Click on the "Next" button to proceed to the next page.
*   On the "customer_notes" page, enter any customer notes in the text field.
*   Click on the "Next" button to proceed to the next page.
*   On the "salesperson_notes" page, enter any salesperson notes in the text field.
*   Click on the "Submit" button to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **sales_order_details**:
    *   (`title`: Order Management Workflow Form, `type`: text, required/optional: no)
    This field is used to enter the sales order details. Enter any relevant information about the order, such as order number, customer name, or any other details that are essential for processing the order.
*   **customer_details**:
    *   (`title`: customer_details, `type`: text, required/optional: no)
    Enter the customer's details in this text field. This includes their name, address, contact number, or any other relevant information.
*   **shipping_details**:
    *   (`title`: shipping_details, `type`: text, required/optional: no)
    Enter the shipping details, such as address, shipping date, or any other relevant information related to the order's shipping.
*   **order_status**:
    *   (`title`: order_status, `type`: select_one, required/optional: no)

*   **order_comments**:
    *   (`title`: order_comments, `type`: text, required/optional: no)
    Enter any comments about the order in this text field. This can include special requests, instructions, or any other relevant information for the salesperson or customer.
*   **customer_notes**:
    *   (`title`: customer_notes, `type`: text, required/optional: no)

    *   (`title`: salesperson_notes, `type`: text, required/optional: no)
