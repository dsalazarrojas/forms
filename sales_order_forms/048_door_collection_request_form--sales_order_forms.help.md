# Door Collection Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request the collection of a door from a customer. The form is designed to gather information about the customer, including their name, email, phone number, address, and pickup details. This information will be used to process the door collection request efficiently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Customer Name" field. This is a required field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This is a required field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field. This is a required field.
4. (Optional) Enter your address in the "Address" field.
5. Select a "Pickup Date" from the calendar to schedule the pickup.
6. Choose a "Pickup Time" from the available options.
7. Provide any additional comments about the pickup in the "Pickup Comments" field.
8. Select the "Door Type" from the available options (Front, Rear, or Other).
9. Select the size of the door you want to collect from the available options (Small, Medium, or Large).
10. Select the material of the door you want to collect from the available options (Wood, Metal, or Other).
11. Add any additional comments about your request in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your name to help us identify you and process your request efficiently.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your request.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you about your request.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Provide your address if you want to schedule the pickup at your location.
* **Pickup Date** (`pickup_date`, `date`, required): Select a date for the pickup from the calendar.
* **Pickup Time** (`pickup_time`, `time`, required): Choose a time slot for the pickup.
* **Pickup Comments** (`pickup_comments`, `note`, optional): Add any comments about the pickup.
* **Door Type** (`door_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of door you want to collect (Front, Rear, or Other).
* **Door Size** (`door_size`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the size of the door you want to collect (Small, Medium, or Large).
* **Door Material** (`door_material`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the material of the door you want to collect (Wood, Metal, or Other).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments about your request.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required): Click this button to submit your request.
* **Cancel** (`cancel`, `text`, optional): Click this button to cancel your request.
* **Invalid** (`invalid`, `text`, optional): This button does not appear to have any effect.
* **No Answer** (`no_answer`, `text`, optional): This field does not appear to have any purpose.
* **No Option** (`no_option`, `text`, optional): This field does not appear to have any effect.
* **Error** (`error`, `text`, optional): This button does not appear to have any effect.
* **No Value** (`no_value`, `text`, optional): This field does not appear to have any purpose.
* **No Answer Label** (`no_answer_label`, `text`, optional): This field does not appear to have any purpose.
* **No Answer Value** (`no_answer_value`, `text`, optional): This field does not appear to have any purpose.
* **No Answer Text** (`no_answer_text`, `text`, optional): This field does not appear to have any purpose.
* **No Answer Comment** (`no_answer_comment`, `text`, optional): This field does not appear to have any purpose.
* **No Answer Hints** (`no_answer_hints`, `text`, optional): This field does not appear to have any purpose.

Note: The "Invalid", "No Answer", "No Option", "Error", "No Value", "No Answer Label", "No Answer Value", "No Answer Text", "No Answer Comment", and "No Answer Hints" fields are not currently used in this form and can be ignored.
