# Cross Promotion Flyer Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cross Promotion Flyer Order Form is a sales order form designed to collect customer information and order details from potential clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal and contact information in the **User Info** section.
2. Provide details about your order in the **Order Info** section.
3. Enter your payment information in the **Payment Info** section.
4. Enter your shipping information in the **Shipping Info** section.
5. Optionally, provide any additional comments or feedback in the **Review** section.
6. Enter your email address in the **Email** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, required: false): Enter your name, contact number, and any other relevant personal details.
* **Order Info** (`order_info`, text, required: false): Provide details about the order, such as the product or service you are interested in.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, required: false): Enter your payment method, such as credit card number, or any other payment details.
* **Shipping Info** (`shipping_info`, text, required: false): Enter your shipping address and any other delivery information.
* **Review** (`review_info`, note, required: false): Leave any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for correspondence.
