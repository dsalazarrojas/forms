# Zyia Styling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Zyia Styling Form is designed to collect customer information for styling services, including name, size preferences, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Customer Information** section with your name and any other details you'd like to share with us.
2. In the **Size and Shape** section, please specify your preferred size and shape for the styling service.
3. In the **Contact Information** section, provide your contact details so we can get back to you with updates and further instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, required: false): This section is for you to provide your name and any other relevant customer details.
* **Size and Shape** (`size_and_shape`, text, required: false): Specify your preferred size and shape for the styling service. This will help us understand your requirements better.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Provide your contact details, including phone number and email, so we can get back to you with updates and further instructions.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields, although they are not marked with an asterisk (*). This ensures we have enough information to process your request.
* If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
