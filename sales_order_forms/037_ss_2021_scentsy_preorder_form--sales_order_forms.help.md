# SS 2021 Scentsy Preorder Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SS 2021 Scentsy Preorder Form is used to allow customers to select one or multiple products from the SS 2021 Scentsy collections and submit their pre-order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the customer information fields:
	* First Name: Enter your first name.
	* Last Name: Enter your last name.
	* Phone Number: Enter your phone number.
	* Email: Enter your email address.
	* Address: Enter your address.
2. Select your product(s) from the list:
	* Product: Choose one or multiple products from the SS 2021 Collections list.
3. Confirm your selections:
	* Confirm Product: Confirm the product(s) you selected.
4. Fill out the submission fields:
	* Submit: Confirm that you want to submit your pre-order.
	* Confirm Submit: Confirm that you want to submit your pre-order.
5. Review and confirm your information:
	* Confirm Name: Confirm your name.
	* Confirm Phone Number: Confirm your phone number.
	* Confirm Email: Confirm your email address.
	* Confirm Address: Confirm your address.
	* Confirm Order Comments: Confirm any additional comments about your order.
6. Submit your pre-order:
	* Click the "Submit" button to finalize your pre-order.
