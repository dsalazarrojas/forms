# Fruit Sale Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fruit Sale Form Template is designed to capture information from customers during sales interactions, such as sale details or customer feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "sale_form" field with any relevant information you'd like to share with the sales team.
2. Provide information about the customer in the "customer_info" field.
3. Add any additional sale details in the "sale_info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **sale_form** (`sale_form`, text, not required): Enter any notes or comments about the sale.
* **customer_info** (`customer_info`, text, not required): Provide information about the customer, such as their name, contact details, or purchase history.
* **sale_info** (`sale_info`, text, not required): Add any other relevant sale details, such as product information or sale date.

## Tips
If you're unsure about what to enter in each field, feel free to ask the sales team for guidance. They'll be happy to help.
You can also use this form to capture feedback from customers about their experience with your sales team.
Don't hesitate to ask for clarification if you're unsure about any information you need to enter.
