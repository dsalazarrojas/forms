# kitchen_milk_delivery_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by sales or customer service teams to gather information from customers about their milk delivery preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`): Enter the customer's details, including their name, address, and other relevant information.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`): Enter the customer's name.
* **Address** (`address`): Enter the customer's address.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`): Select the date for milk delivery.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`): Select the time for milk delivery.
* **Milk Type** (`milk_type`): Select the type of milk to be delivered (e.g., regular milk, almond milk, etc.).
* **Milk Quantity** (`milk_quantity`): Enter the quantity of milk to be delivered.
* **Milk Unit** (`milk_unit`): Specify the unit of measurement for milk (e.g., gallons, liters, etc.).
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`): Enter the address where milk will be delivered.
* **Delivery Instructions** (`delivery_instructions`): Enter any special instructions for delivery.
* **Delivery Status** (`delivery_status`): Specify the status of the delivery (e.g., active or inactive).
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter the contact person's email.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`): Optionally, enter any additional instructions or notes.
* **Billing Info** (`billing_info`): Optionally, enter billing information, if applicable.
* **Billing Contact** (`billing_contact`): Optionally, enter the billing contact person's name.
* **Billing Email** (`billing_email`): Optionally, enter the billing contact person's email.
* **Billing Phone** (`billing_phone`): Optionally, enter the billing contact person's phone number.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`): Optionally, enter any notes or comments from the customer.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`): Optionally, enter any payment terms or information.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`): Optionally, specify the payment status (e.g., active or inactive).
* **Payment Status Label** (`payment_status_label`): Optionally, enter a label for the payment status.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`): Optionally, enter any additional comments or notes from the customer.
