# Factory Fire Alarm System Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Factory Fire Alarm System Order Form is designed to collect information for the purpose of ordering a factory fire alarm system. This form is intended for sales and technical personnel to ensure accurate and complete information is gathered from customers before creating a quote or placing an order. Completing this form will help streamline the process and ensure all necessary details are collected.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out each field with the requested information.
2.  Ensure all required fields are filled out as indicated by the asterisk (*) symbol next to the field label.
3.  Review your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Person (1)**: Enter the name of the contact person at the site.
* **Site Address (2)**: Enter the address of the site where the fire alarm system will be installed.
* **Site Contact Number (3)**: Enter the contact number of the site.
* **Fire Alarm Requirements (4)**: Enter any specific requirements or needs for the fire alarm system.
* **System Type (5)**: Select the type of fire alarm system needed. This will help the sales and technical team determine the best fit for your site.
* **Quantity (6)**: Enter the number of systems needed for the site.
* **Installation (7)**: Select the type of installation desired. This could be a full installation or just the hardware and software components.
* **Site Details (8)**: Enter any additional details about the site.
* **Notes (9)**: Enter any additional notes or information about the site.
* **Site Email (10)**: Enter the email address of the site.
* **Site Phone (11)**: Enter the contact phone number of the site.
* **Site Fax (12)**: Enter the fax number of the site.
* **Site Website (13)**: Enter the website of the site.
* **Site City (14)**: Enter the city where the site is located.
* **Site State (15)**: Enter the state where the site is located.
* **Site Zip (16)**: Enter the zip code of the site.
* **Site Country (17)**: Enter the country where the site is located.
* **Site Time Zone (18)**: Enter the time zone of the site.
* **Site Notes (19)**: Enter any additional notes about the site.
* **Site Manager (20)**: Enter the name of the site manager.
* **Site Manager Phone (21)**: Enter the contact phone number of the site manager.
* **Site Manager Email (22)**: Enter the email address of the site manager.
* **Site Manager Notes (23)**: Enter any additional notes about the site manager.
* **Site Manager Title (24)**: Enter the title of the site manager.
* **Site Manager Designee (25)**: Enter the site manager's designee.
* **Sales Person (26)**: Enter the name of the sales person.
* **Sales Person Phone (27)**: Enter the contact phone number of the sales person.
* **Sales Person Email (28)**: Enter the email address of the sales person.
* **Sales Person Notes (29)**: Enter any additional notes about the sales person.
* **Sales Person Title (30)**: Enter the title of the sales person.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Review the form for any errors or missing information before submitting.
* If unsure about any field or information, contact the sales and technical teams for assistance.
