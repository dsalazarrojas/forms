# Spring Collection 2021 Pre Order Now - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for the Spring Collection 2021 Pre Order Now sales order form. The purpose of this form is to gather details from users about their order preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the **Category** of your order.
2. Provide a brief **Description** of your order.
3. Choose the **Assigned Tool** associated with your order.
4. Enter any **Form IDs** if applicable.
5. Specify the **Output File** name and path for your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Select the category related to your order, such as Sales Order Forms, Other, or Unknown.
* **Description** (`description`, note, optional): Briefly describe the order you wish to place.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Choose the tool assigned to your order from chatjimmy, other, or unknown.
* **Form IDs** (`form_ids`, text, optional): Enter any applicable form IDs related to your order.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, optional): Specify the output file name and path for your order.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields, if any.
* Use clear and concise language when filling in the description.
* Double-check your category and assigned tool selections for accuracy.
* Enter valid and relevant form IDs and output file information.
