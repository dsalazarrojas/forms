# november_gh_pre_order - Help Guide
## Purpose
The November GH Pre-Order form is designed for customers to provide pre-order information for a sale that will take place in November. This form collects essential details that will be used to process the order correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your **Customer Information** (Field 1) including any relevant details.
2. Enter your **Shipping Address** (Field 2) where you would like the order to be delivered.
3. Add **Shipping Details** (Field 3) if necessary.
4. Enter **Order Details** (Field 4) to ensure your order is processed correctly.
5. Select your preferred **Shipping Method** (Field 5) from the options provided.
6. Provide **Payment Information** (Field 6) to complete your order.
7. Add any additional **Customer Note** (Field 7) if necessary.
8. Choose your preferred **Payment Method** (Field 8) from the options provided.
9. Enter your **Date of Birth** (Field 9) to complete your order.

### 1. **Customer Information (Customer Information)**
*   **Customer Information** (Customer Information, text, optional)
    *   Enter your name and any other relevant customer information.

    *   Enter the address where you would like the order to be delivered.

*   **Shipping Method** (Shipping Method, select one, optional)
    *   Choose from Standard Shipping, Express Shipping, or Local Pickup.
