# Gym Custom Wall Mirror Installation Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers order a custom wall mirror for their gym or fitness center. By filling out this form, customers can provide the necessary information to customize their mirror installation according to their preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields (marked as "true" in the form).
2. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
5. Specify the size of the mirror you need in the "mirror_size" field.
6. Select the frame type for the mirror from the options provided in the "frame_type" field.
7. Indicate the type of wall you have in mind for the mirror installation in the "wall_type" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Your name, which will be used to contact you regarding your order.
* **Last Name**: Your last name, which will be used to contact you regarding your order.
* **Email**: The email address that will be used to contact you about your order, as well as to send you updates and notifications.
* **Mirror Size**: Specify the size of the mirror you need, e.g., 24" x 36", 48" x 60", etc.
* **Frame Type**: Choose the type of frame that suits your preference:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
* **Wall Type**: Describe the type of wall you have for the mirror installation. This will help us determine the best approach for mounting the mirror.
