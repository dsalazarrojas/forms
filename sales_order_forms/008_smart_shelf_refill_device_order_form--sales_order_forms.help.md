# Smart Shelf Refill Device Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Smart Shelf Refill Device Order Form is a simplified order form designed for customers to place new orders or refill their existing inventory of smart shelf devices. This form helps you gather essential details about the order to ensure accurate processing and timely delivery.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On the "order_details" page, provide a detailed description of the order you wish to place.
2. On the "contact_details" page, enter your contact information so that our team can get in touch with you regarding the order.
3. On the "shipping_details" page, specify the shipping details for your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required: false): Please provide a clear and concise description of the order you wish to place. This could include the type of device you need, the quantity, or any special requests.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): Enter your name, email address, and phone number so that our team can contact you regarding the order.
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, text, required: false): Specify the shipping address where you would like the order delivered, including any special instructions or notes.
