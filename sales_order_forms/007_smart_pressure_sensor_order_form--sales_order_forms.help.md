# Smart Pressure Sensor Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Pressure Sensor Order Form is a sales order form designed to collect information from customers for the purchase of a smart pressure sensor product. This form serves as a tool for processing customer orders and shipping details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. **Review and enter shipping details on the first page** (`shipping_details` page)
2. **Enter order specifics on the second page** (`order_details` page)
3. **Submit the form** (click on `submit_payment`)

* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`):
	+ This page is for entering shipping information such as address, contact details, and any other shipping-related information.
	+ Please enter your shipping details accurately to ensure prompt and correct delivery of your order.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`):
	+ Enter the specifics of your order, including any additional instructions or requests.
