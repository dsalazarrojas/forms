# Full Color Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Full Color Order Form is a sales order form used to collect customer information and order details for full color products or services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your customer information and order details.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you have provided all necessary information.
3. Once you have finished, click the submit button to send the form to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Color Order** (`full_color_order_form_page_1`, text, not required): Please provide a clear and concise description of the full color product or service you are ordering. This will help us understand your request better.
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, not required): Please provide your name, email, and any other relevant information about yourself as our customer.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, not required): Please provide any additional information about your order, such as quantity, special requests, or instructions.
* **Color Preferences** (`color_preferences`, text, not required): Please indicate any specific color preferences you have for your order.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
