# Factory Signal Light Tower Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential details from customers for ordering signal light towers. Please provide accurate information to ensure a smooth sales process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the **Order Details** section with the required information.
2. Provide your **Customer Info**, including your name, email, and phone number.
3. Select the **Tower Type** that suits your needs.
4. Choose the **Location** where you would like the tower to be installed.
5. Enter your **Address** where the tower will be installed.
6. If necessary, add **Special Instructions** for the sales team or delivery personnel.
7. Enter the **Height** of the tower you require.
8. Enter the **Quantity** of towers you would like to order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Order Details
* **Order Details** (Page 1, id: 1): A text field where you can enter a brief description of your order.
### Customer Info
* **Customer Info** (Page 2, id: 2): Enter your name, email address, and phone number to allow us to contact you efficiently.
### Delivery Details
* **Delivery Details** (Page 3, id: 3): Enter any special delivery instructions or requests.
### Special Instructions
* **Special Instructions** (Page 4, id: 4): If you have any specific requirements or requests for the tower installation, enter them here.
### Tower Type
* **Tower Type** (Page 5, id: 5): Select the type of tower that suits your needs from the available options: Signal, Light, or Tower.
### Address
* **Address** (Page 6, id: 6): Enter the physical location where the tower will be installed.
### Location
* **Location** (Page 7, id: 7): Choose from the available options where you would like the tower to be installed (New York, Los Angeles, or Chicago).
### Email
* **Email** (Page 8, id: 8): Enter your email address to allow us to communicate with you efficiently.
### Phone
* **Phone** (Page 9, id: 9): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
### Height
* **Height** (Page 10, id: 10): Enter the height of the tower you require.
### Quantity
* **Quantity** (Page 11, id: 11): Enter the number of towers you would like to order.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately to avoid any potential issues during the sales process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We recommend reviewing your order carefully before submitting it to ensure it's correct.
