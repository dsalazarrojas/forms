# Color Street Stock On Hand Order Form - Gorgeous Nails With Ginny - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for a stock on hand order with Ginny, for a business or personal order fulfillment service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the "Name" and "Email Address" fields, respectively.
2. Enter your shipping address in the "Shipping Address" field.
3. List the sets you want from Ginny's current stock in the "List the sets you want from my current stock" field.
4. Enter the total number of sets you want to order in the "Total Number of Sets" field.
5. If you have any questions or would like to use the B3G1 deal, select the correct option in the "B3G1 Deal applied?" field.
6. Choose your payment method and enter your payment username in the "How are you paying?" and "Payment Username" fields, respectively.
7. Select your delivery type (shipping or pick-up) in the "Delivery Type" field.
8. If you have any additional comments for Ginny, enter them in the "Message for Ginny" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`ginny_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name to help us process your order.
* **Email Address** (`ginny_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you with any questions or updates about your order.
* **Shipping Address** (`ginny_address`, `text`, required): Enter your shipping address to ensure accurate delivery of your order.
* **Cell Phone** (`ginny_phone`, `text`, optional): If you have any questions or concerns about your order, please enter your cell phone number so we can contact you.
* **List the sets you want from my current stock** (`ginny_sets_list`, `text`, required): This is where you list the sets you want to order from Ginny's current stock. Be as specific as possible so we can fulfill your order accurately.
* **Total Number of Sets** (`ginny_total_sets`, `number`, required): Enter the total number of sets you want to order.
* **B3G1 Deal applied?** (`ginny_b3g1_promo`, `select_one`, required): This field indicates whether you would like to use the B3G1 deal or not.
* **How are you paying?** (`ginny_payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you would like to pay for your order.
* **Payment Username** (`ginny_payment_id`, `text`, required): Enter your payment username associated with your chosen payment method.
* **Delivery Type** (`ginny_delivery_choice`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you would like to receive your order (shipping or pick-up).
* **Hostess Name** (`ginny_host_name`, `text`, optional): If you are ordering on behalf of someone else, enter their name.
* **Message for Ginny** (`ginny_message`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or requests for your order.
