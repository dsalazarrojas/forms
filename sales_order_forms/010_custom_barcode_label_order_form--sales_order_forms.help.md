# Custom Barcode Label Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom Barcode Label Order Form is designed to gather all the necessary information for creating a custom barcode label for the customer. This form is used to create a label with a specific category, specifications, artwork, and quantity. It also includes space for description, payment details, shipping details, order notes, email, phone, file uploads, and terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the custom barcode label from the list provided.
2. Enter the specifications for the barcode label, such as size and material.
3. Choose the artwork for the label, either front, back, or both.
4. Enter the quantity of the custom barcode label needed.
5. Add a description of the custom barcode label.
6. Enter the payment details, including the payment method and amount.
7. Provide shipping details, including the shipping address and any special instructions.
8. Add any additional order notes or comments.
9. Enter the customer's email address.
10. Enter the customer's phone number.
11. Upload any required files for the custom barcode label.
12. Agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Custom Barcode Label Order Form** (`custom_barcode_label_order_form`): Enter the name of the custom barcode label in this text field.
* **Category** (`category`): Select the category of the custom barcode label from the list provided. This will help us understand the type of label being created.
* **Barcode Label Specifications** (`specifications`): Enter any specifications for the barcode label, such as size and material.
* **Artwork** (`artwork`): Choose the artwork for the label, either front, back, or both.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`): Enter the quantity of the custom barcode label needed.
* **Description** (`description`): Add a description of the custom barcode label, this will help us understand what the label is for.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`): Enter the payment method and amount for the custom barcode label.
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`): Enter the shipping address and any special instructions.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`): Add any additional order notes or comments.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **File Uploads** (`file_uploads`): Upload any required files for the custom barcode label.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`): Read and agree to the terms and conditions for the custom barcode label.
* **Custom Barcode Label Artwork Image** (`custom_barcode_label_artwork_image`): Upload a custom image for the label, if applicable.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`): Read and agree to the terms and conditions for the custom barcode label.
