# Arbonne - Pre Order Form 2020 - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Arbonne - Pre Order Form 2020 is designed to collect customer information and details related to their pre-order. This form helps customers submit their pre-order requests and provides an easy way for them to specify their product preferences and payment information. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in its entirety. 
2. Enter your customer information in the relevant fields. 
3. Select your preferred product category and specific products from the list provided. 
4. Specify the quantity of each product you are pre-ordering. 
5. Review and confirm your subtotal amount before proceeding. 
6. Select your preferred payment method. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Full Name** (Customer Full Name, text, required): Enter your full name for identification purposes. 
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required): Enter your active email address for communication. 
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes. 
* **Shipping Address** (Shipping Address, text, required): Enter your full shipping address for delivery purposes. 
* **City** (City, text, required): Enter your city name. 
* **State or Province** (State or Province, text, required): Enter your state or province name. 
* **Zip or Postal Code** (Zip or Postal Code, text, required): Enter your zip or postal code for delivery purposes. 
* **Product Category** (Product Category, select_one, required): Select the product category you are pre-ordering, such as Skincare, Wellness, Hair Care, Makeup, or Bundles. 
* **Specific Products Ordered** (Specific Products Ordered, text, required): List the specific products you are pre-ordering or their SKUs. 
* **Total Quantity** (Total Quantity, number, required): Enter the number of units of each product you are pre-ordering. 
* **Subtotal Amount** (Subtotal Amount, number, required): Review and confirm the subtotal amount before proceeding. 
* **Tax Amount** (Tax Amount, number, optional): If applicable, enter the tax amount. 
* **Shipping Cost** (Shipping Cost, number, optional): If applicable, enter the shipping cost. 
* **Total Amount Due** (Total Amount Due, number, required): Final total including all charges. 
* **Preferred Delivery Date** (Preferred Delivery Date, date, optional): Enter your preferred delivery date, if applicable. 
* **Special Delivery Instructions** (Special Delivery Instructions, text, optional): If needed, provide any special delivery instructions. 
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select_one, required): Select your preferred payment method, such as Credit card, Debit card, Bank transfer, Check, or Other.
