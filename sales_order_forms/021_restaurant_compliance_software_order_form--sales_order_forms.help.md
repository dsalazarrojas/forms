# Restaurant Compliance Software Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restaurant Compliance Software Order Form is a tool used to capture and record information about a restaurant's compliance software order. This form helps ensure that all necessary details are collected accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer's information, including their name, address, city, state, zip, phone number, and email.
2. Choose the software package(s) you wish to order, from the available options.
3. Select your preferred payment method.
4. Enter the date and time of the order.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Enter your customer's name and other relevant details.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter your contact information, including phone number and email.
* **Software Packages** (`software_packages`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the software packages you wish to order from the available options.
* **Requirements** (`requirements`, text, optional): Describe any specific requirements or specifications for the software ordered.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the order.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the order.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your customer's name.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, optional): Enter your customer's address.
* **Customer City** (`customer_city`, text, optional): Enter your customer's city.
* **Customer State** (`customer_state`, text, optional): Enter your customer's state.
* **Customer Zip** (`customer_zip`, text, optional): Enter your customer's zip code.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your customer's phone number.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your customer's email address.
* **Software Vendor** (`software_vendor`, text, optional): Enter the software vendor's name.
* **Software Name** (`software_name`, text, optional): Enter the software name.
* **Software Version** (`software_version`, text, optional): Enter the software version.
* **Software Quantity** (`software_quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of software ordered.
* **Software Price** (`software_price`, number, optional): Enter the price of the software.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred payment method.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, optional): Enter the payment date.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the customer.
* **Vendor Notes** (`vendor_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the vendor.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred payment method, which includes Bank Transfer, Check, or Credit Card.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, optional): Enter the payment date for the order.

Note: Some fields are marked as "optional," which means they do not need to be filled out, but can be included if additional information is needed.
