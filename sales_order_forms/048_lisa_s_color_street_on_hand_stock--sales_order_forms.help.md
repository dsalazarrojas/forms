# LisasColorStreetOnHandStock - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect sales order data for a specific stock color request. It is intended to be used by sales teams to capture the customer's information and preferences for the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the form data in the first field.
2. Fill in the customer's information, including their name and any additional details in the text fields.
3. Enter the customer's email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter the customer's phone number in the "phone_number" field.
5. Provide details about the stock information in the "stock_info" field.
6. Select the desired color for the order from the "color_request" dropdown menu.
7. Indicate if you want to host a party for the customer (yes/no) in the "host_party" field.
8. If you have any additional information to share, enter it in the "more_info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data**: (`form_data`, text, required/optional) This field is not currently used in the form. You can enter any information you like here.
* **Customer Info**: (`customer_info`, text, required/optional) Enter the customer's basic details, such as any relevant background information.
* **Customer Name**: (`customer_name`, text, required/optional) Enter the customer's name.
* **Email**: (`email`, text, required/optional) Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone Number**: (`phone_number`, text, required/optional) Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Stock Info**: (`stock_info`, text, required/optional) Provide details about the stock being ordered.
* **Color Request**: (`color_request`, select_one, required/optional) Select the desired color for the order from the options: Red, Blue, Green, or Yellow.
* **Host Party**: (`host_party`, select_multiple, required/optional) Indicate if you want to host a party for the customer by selecting "True", "False", or "maybe".
* **More Info**: (`more_info`, text, required/optional) Enter any additional information you think is relevant to the order.
