# shabbos_chef_menu_4 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The shabbos_chef_menu_4 form is used to collect information for sales orders, specifically for the Shabbos Chef menu 4.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant menu items from the list.
2. Choose whether the customer has provided "Yes" or "No" to some information.
3. Enter the date and time of the order.
4. Provide the customer's name, email, phone number, and address.
5. Add any additional notes about the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Menu Items** (`menu_items`, `text`, required/optional): List the items from the Shabbos Chef menu 4 that the customer has ordered.
* **customer-info** (`customer_info`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Indicate whether the customer has provided their information such as phone number, address, and email.
* **order-date** (`order_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the order.
* **order-time** (`order_time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of the order.
* **customer-name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the customer's name.
* **customer-email** (`customer_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **customer-phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **customer-address** (`customer_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the customer's address.
* **order-notes** (`order_notes`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes about the order.

## Tips
- Make sure to list all relevant menu items to ensure accurate order processing.
- Ensure the customer information is up-to-date and accurate.
- Double-check the order date and time to ensure correct delivery or pickup.
- Enter all necessary customer contact information for follow-up purposes.
