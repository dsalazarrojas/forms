# Color Street Inventory Order Form - Sassy Sparkling Nails With Sabrina - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for placing a sales order for Sassy Sparkling Nails With Sabrina. Please fill it out carefully to ensure accurate and complete order details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your contact information:
	* Customer Name: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
	* Email Address: Enter your email address for order tracking and receipt purposes.
	* Shipping Address: Enter your full shipping address, including street, city, state, and ZIP code.
2. Select your Sassy Styles:
	* Choose all the glitter sets you want from the Glitter Sets Selection dropdown list.
	* Choose all the solid color sets you want from the Solid Color Sets Selection dropdown list.
	* Choose all the art and design sets you want from the Art and Design Sets Selection dropdown list.
3. Apply any special promotions:
	* If you are using a B3G1 (Buy 3 Get 1 Free) Promotion, select the corresponding option.
4. Checkout and payment:
	* Select how you would like to be invoiced: PayPal, Venmo, or Cash App.
	* Enter your payment handle or username for the selected payment method.
	* If you are applying host credit from a party, select the corresponding option.
5. Add any personal note:
	* You can add a personal note for Sabrina if needed.

* **Customer Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
