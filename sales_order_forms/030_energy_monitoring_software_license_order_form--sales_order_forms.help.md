# Energy Monitoring Software License Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers interested in purchasing energy monitoring software. This form will gather all the necessary details for processing and fulfilling the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer information section with your name and contact details.
2. Enter the software details you are interested in purchasing.
3. Provide your payment details for the order.
4. Include any additional agreement details if required.
5. Enter your delivery details for the software to be shipped.
6. Enter your billing address if different from the delivery address.
7. Add any order comments or notes.
8. Review and sign the license agreement.
9. Sign the form with your signature.
10. Enter the date of signing.
11. Enter your company name and website.
12. Enter your company phone number and email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information** (`customer_information`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other relevant customer information.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Enter your contact name, title, phone number, and email.
* **Software Details** (`software_details`, text, optional): Describe the software you are interested in purchasing.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, optional): Enter your payment method and any other payment information.
* **Agreement Details** (`agreement_details`, text, optional): Enter any specific agreement terms or conditions.
* **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, text, optional): Enter your delivery address and any specific delivery instructions.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, text, optional): Enter your billing address if different from the delivery address.
* **Company Details** (`company_details`, text, optional): Enter your company name and website.
* **Order Comments** (`order_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the order.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, text, optional): Add any other order notes or instructions.
* **License Agreement** (`license_agreement`, text, optional): Review and agree to the license terms.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Sign the form with your signature.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, text, optional): Sign the form with your customer's signature.
* **Date** (`date`, text, optional): Enter the date of signing.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter your company name.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, optional): Enter your company website.
* **Company Phone** (`company_phone`, text, optional): Enter your company phone number.
* **Company Email** (`company_email`, text, optional): Enter your company email.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the contact name.
* **Contact Title** (`contact_title`, text, optional): Enter the contact title.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, text, optional): Enter the contact email.
* **Address Line 1** (`address_line1`, text, optional): Enter the address line 1.
