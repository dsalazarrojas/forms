# industrial_pressure_valve_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for placing orders for industrial pressure valves. The purpose of this form is to collect necessary information from the buyer to process and fulfill the order accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the contact details section with your name, phone number, and email.
2. Enter your shipping details, including the destination address.
3. Select the desired products from the product selection section.
4. Enter any relevant contact information.
5. Enter the shipping address for the order.
6. Provide any additional order details.
7. Specify the delivery terms.
8. Specify the payment terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, phone number, and email to ensure we can contact you regarding your order.
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, text, required/optional): Enter the address where you would like your order shipped.
* **Product Selection** (`product_selection`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select one or more of the available options for the industrial pressure valve.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional contact information that may be relevant to your order.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the shipping address where you would like your order shipped.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information regarding your order.
* **Delivery Terms** (`delivery_terms`, text, required/optional): Specify any special delivery instructions.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, required/optional): Specify any payment terms that need to be considered.

## Tips
Please make sure to double-check all fields before submitting the form to avoid any mistakes.
