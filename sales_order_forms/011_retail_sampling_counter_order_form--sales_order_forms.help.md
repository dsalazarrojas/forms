# Retail Sampling Counter Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Sampling Counter Order Form is used to capture details about retail sampling counter orders, including customer information, product selection, and order requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. Select the products you are interested in sampling.
3. Provide any additional comments or details about your order in the "counter_request_note" field.
4. Submit the form when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Retail Sampling Counter Order Form** (`retail_sampling_counter_form`, text, optional): This is the title of the form.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Please enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Product Selection** (`product_selection`, select multiple, optional): Choose the products you are interested in sampling.
* **Product Info** (`product_info`, text, optional): If there are specific products you are interested in, please enter the details here.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, optional): Enter your shipping address for order delivery.
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, text, optional): Enter any special shipping instructions or requests.
* **Counter Order Details** (`counter_order_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about your order.
* **Counter Info** (`counter_info`, text, optional): If you have any specific counter instructions, please enter them here.
* **Counter Request Date** (`counter_request_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to receive your order.
* **Counter Request Time** (`counter_request_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to receive your order.
* **Counter Request Note** (`counter_request_note`, note, optional): Leave any additional comments or requests about your order.
* **Counter Request Email** (`counter_request_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Counter Request Phone** (`counter_request_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Form Submit** (`form_submit`, text, optional): Please submit the form to complete your order.

Note: Please review each field carefully and ensure accuracy before submitting.
