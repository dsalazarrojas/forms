# Equipment Vibration Detector Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from users to facilitate the ordering process of equipment vibration detectors. It will help us understand your needs and ensure accurate delivery and payment processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide your general information, which includes any relevant details we need to know about you.
2. Enter your address, where the ordered equipment will be delivered.
3. Provide your payment details to complete the order.
4. Enter your shipping details, which will help us process your order accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Information (General Information, text, required: false)**: This field is for capturing any general information about yourself that we need to know to process your order. Please provide any relevant details, such as your name, contact number, or other identifying information.
- **Address (Address, text, required: false)**: This field is for providing your delivery address. Please ensure you enter it accurately, as this will help us deliver your equipment vibration detector to the correct location.
- **Payment Details (Payment Details, text, required: false)**: This field is for payment information. You can enter your credit card details, payment method, or other payment information as per your preference.
- **Shipping Details (Shipping Details, text, required: false)**: This field is for additional shipping information. You can enter package tracking numbers, delivery instructions, or any other relevant details to help us process your order accurately.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill in all the required fields to ensure prompt processing of your order.
- Double-check your entries for accuracy, especially your address and payment details.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
