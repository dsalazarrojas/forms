# Shelf Security Rail Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for capturing order details related to shelf security rails, including shipping and payment information for your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title and details of the shelf security rail you are ordering on the **Order Details** page.
2. Add your shipping address on the **Shipping Details** page.
3. Provide payment information on the **Payment Information** page.
4. Review your order summary on the **Review** page.
5. Finally, submit your contact information and order details on the **Customer Information** and **Contact** pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required):
	+ Enter the title and specific details of the shelf security rail you are ordering.
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, text, required):
	+ Provide your shipping address, including street, city, state, zip, and any additional comments.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_details`, text, required):
	+ Enter your payment method and any relevant payment terms.
* **Review** (`review`, text, required):
	+ Double-check your order details and payment information before submission.
* **Shipping** (`shipping`, text, optional):
	+ Add any shipping instructions or special requirements.
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, required):
	+ Enter your name, email, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Contact** (`contact`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any additional contact information or notes about your order.
* **Shelf Security Rail Order Form** (`title`, text, required):
	+ Enter a title for your order summary (e.g., "Shelf Security Rail Order for John Doe"). 

**Tips:**
* Please ensure all fields are filled accurately and completely.
* You can add any additional comments or attachments on the **Contact** page.
* Review your order before submission to avoid any errors or issues.
