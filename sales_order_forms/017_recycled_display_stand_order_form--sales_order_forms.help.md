# Recycled Display Stand Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recycled Display Stand Order Form is used to collect information from customers for a recycled display stand order. This form helps sales teams and customer service representatives gather essential data for processing orders efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:

1. Provide your customer information in the "Customer Information" field.
2. Enter your order specifics in the "Order Information" field.
3. Enter your shipping information in the "Shipping Information" field.
4. Specify your payment method and details in the "Payment Information" field.
5. Choose your delivery preferences in the "Delivery Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Enter your name, address, phone number, and email to ensure we can contact you about your order.
* **Order Information** (`order_info`, text, optional): Share details about the item(s) you would like to purchase, such as product code, quantity, and any special instructions or comments.
* **Shipping Information** (`shipping_info`, text, optional): Enter your shipping address and any additional shipping notes, like preferred shipping date or method.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, optional): Choose your payment method and provide necessary payment details to complete the transaction.
* **Delivery Information** (`delivery_info`, text, optional): Specify your delivery preferences, including pickup or delivery options, to ensure timely and convenient delivery of your order.

## Tips
- Please fill in all relevant fields to ensure smooth order processing.
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact our customer service team for assistance.
