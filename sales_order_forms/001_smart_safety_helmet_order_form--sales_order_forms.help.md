# Smart Safety Helmet Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect order details from customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the product options you want to order.
2. Choose your preferred payment method.
3. Fill in your shipping details and address.
4. Provide order date and time.
5. Enter any additional information as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Options** (`product_options`, select_multiple, required): Select the Smart Helmet you want to order. You can choose one or multiple helmets from the available options (Smart Helmet 1, Smart Helmet 2, Smart Helmet 3).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you want to pay for your order. You can select either Stripe or PayPal.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the customer.
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, text, optional): Enter your shipping details here.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, optional): Enter your shipping address here.
* **Delivery Preferences** (`delivery_preferences`, text, optional): Enter any special delivery preferences you may have.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional): Enter any specific details about your order.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Select the date you want to receive your order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, optional): Select the time you want to receive your order.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, optional): Enter the payment amount if you're paying in cash or a different payment method.

## Tips
* Make sure to select all required fields to ensure your order is processed correctly.
* Use clear and accurate information to fill out the form to avoid any issues with delivery or order fulfillment.
* If you have any questions or issues, contact your sales representative for assistance.
