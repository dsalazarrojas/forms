# e_commerce_platform_integration_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for integration with an e-commerce platform to record and process orders. It collects various information about the order, customer, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, ensure you have all the necessary information about the order, customer, and payment details ready.
2. Fill in the customer information, including customer name, email, and phone number.
3. Provide details about the order, including product price, order total, and order date.
4. Choose the payment method and order status.
5. Enter shipping details, including address, city, state, and zip code.
6. Enter any additional notes or comments about the order.
7. Review and submit the form to record and process the order.
