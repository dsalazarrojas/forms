# event_sales_tracker_closing_event_7_12_14 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track sales events for closing events 7, 12, and 14.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the required information and permissions to access the form.
2. Fill in the form accurately and honestly, taking your time to ensure everything is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details**
  * Label: Client Details
  * Type: text
  * Required: Optional
  * Explanation: Enter the client's details, such as their name and contact information. This will help us understand who the sale is related to.
* **Package Information**
  * Label: Package Information
  * Type: text
  * Required: Optional
  * Explanation: Provide a brief description of the package or product being sold. This will help us track the specifics of the sale.
* **Payment Type**
  * Label: Payment Type
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: Optional
  * Explanation: Indicate whether payment has been made or not, with options 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Payment Amount**
  * Label: Payment Amount
  * Type: number
  * Required: Optional
  * Explanation: Enter the amount paid for the sale, if applicable.
* **Notes**
  * Label: Notes
  * Type: note
  * Required: Optional
  * Explanation: Add any additional comments or notes related to the sale. This can be used for future reference or to clarify any details.

## Tips

* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
