# robotics_vision_lens_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "robotics_vision_lens_order_form" is designed to capture customer and order information for robotic vision lenses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form correctly, follow these steps:

1.  Complete the "Customer Details" page by filling in the required information for the customer.
2.  Move on to the "Shipping Details" page and fill in the necessary shipping information.
3.  Next, move to the "Order Details" page and enter the details about the order.
4.  On the "Lens Selection" page, select the appropriate lens type from the dropdown menu.
5.  If applicable, acknowledge the "Terms and Conditions" by signing on the designated area.
6.  Finally, add any customer notes or comments on the "Customer Notes" page.
7.  Once all the pages are completed, review your submission to ensure it is complete and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Details (customer_details)** (`1`, `text`, required:false)
    Enter the customer's name, email address, and other contact information as required.
*   **Shipping Details (shipping_details)** (`2`, `text`, required:false)
    Fill in the shipping address, contact number, and any other relevant shipping details.
*   **Order Details (order_details)** (`3`, `text`, required:false)
    Provide a detailed description or notes about the order, including any special instructions.
*   **Lens Selection (lens_selection)** (`4`, `select_one`, required:false)
    Choose the type of lens that suits the order by selecting one of the provided options.
*   **Terms and Conditions (terms_and_conditions)** (`5`, `note`, required:false)
    Sign this area to acknowledge that you have read and will comply with the terms and conditions.
*   **Customer Signature (customer_signature)** (`6`, `note`, required:false)
    Sign here to confirm your acceptance of the order.
*   **Customer Notes (customer_notes)** (`8`, `note`, required:false)
    Add any additional notes, comments, or questions about the order or customer interaction.

## Tips

*   Double-check your submissions for accuracy and completeness before finalizing.
*   Ensure all required fields are filled out.
*   Keep the form clear and organized to facilitate easy reference and review.
*   If you're unsure about any field or process, consult with a sales representative or customer support team.
*   Review the terms and conditions carefully before signing.
