# restaurant_safety_certification_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The restaurant safety certification order form is designed to collect contact information and payment details for the purpose of ordering a restaurant safety certification. This form helps restaurant owners or managers to provide their contact details and payment information for the certification process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your restaurant's safety certification order information.
2. Provide your contact details.
3. Enter payment details if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **restaurant_safety_certification_order_form** (`restaurant_safety_certification_order_form`, text, required/optional: false):
	+ This is the main title of the certification order form.
	+ Enter your restaurant's safety certification order information here.
- **contact_details** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional: false):
	+ Enter your name, email address, and phone number to enable us to contact you regarding your certification order.
	+ This field is optional, but it's recommended to provide your contact details for smooth communication.
- **payment_details** (`payment_details`, text, required/optional: false):
	+ If you want to order the certification, enter your payment information here.
	+ This field is optional, but it's necessary to provide payment details for processing your order.
 
## Tips
- Please make sure to enter accurate contact and payment information to ensure a smooth certification process.
- If you have any questions or concerns regarding the certification process, feel free to contact us.
 
Note: The form is divided into three fields for a simple and straightforward completion process.
