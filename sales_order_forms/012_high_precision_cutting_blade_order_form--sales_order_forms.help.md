# high_precision_cutting_blade_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to order high precision cutting blades. It will help us understand their requirements and preferences for the delivery of the blades.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:
1. Fill out all the required fields.
2. Choose your preferred delivery method.
3. If you have any special instructions or comments, include them in the "Special Instructions" field.
4. If you need to indicate your response to any additional question, please select the relevant answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, `text`, required: false)
Enter your name and other relevant contact information in this field. This is where you will be contacted if we need more information from you.

### 2. **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, `text`, required: false)
Enter your delivery address where you would like us to deliver the blades. Please ensure that this address is accurate and complete.

### 3. **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, `note`, required: false)
If you have any special instructions or comments regarding the delivery or the blades, please include them here.

### 4. **Additional Comments** (`special_instructions_2`, `note`, required: false)
If you have any additional comments or information you would like to provide, please include them here.

### 5. **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, `text`, required: false)
Please sign this form to confirm that you are providing the information above.

### 6. **Customer Details 2** (`customer_details_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
Please select "Yes" if this form is for you, or "No" if it is not.

### 7. **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, `select_one`, required: false)
Choose your preferred delivery method: Ground Delivery, Express Delivery, or Pickup.

### Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and completely.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please contact us for clarification.
* Please review your form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all fields are filled out correctly.
