# Gym Branded Keychain Bulk Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gym Branded Keychain Bulk Order Form is used to gather detailed information from customers who are placing a bulk order for branded keychains.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information:
	* The bulk details should include the specific requirements for your order.
	* Select the keychain color from the dropdown menu.
	* Enter the quantity ordered and unit price.
	* Enter the total price, which is the product of the quantity ordered and unit price.
	* Provide the shipping details, including any specific instructions or requirements.
2. Enter your contact information, including:
	* Customer Name
	* Customer Email
	* Customer Phone
	* Customer Address
	* Customer City
	* Customer Postal (Zip Code)
	* Customer Country
	* Customer State
	* Customer Zip
	* Customer Notes
3. If applicable, select the shipping details, including:
	* Shipping Company
	* Shipping Address
	* Shipping City
	* Shipping Postal (Zip Code)
	* Shipping Country
	* Shipping State
4. Choose your payment method from the dropdown menu.
5. Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
6. Enter the order date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Bulk Details**: Briefly describe the specific requirements for your bulk order.
* **Keychain Color**: Select the color of the keychain you are ordering.
* **Quantity Ordered**: Enter the number of keychains you are ordering.
* **Unit Price**: Enter the price per keychain.
* **Total Price**: This field is automatically populated with the product of the quantity ordered and unit price.
* **Shipping Details**: Provide any specific shipping instructions or requirements.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Customer Address**: Enter your address.
* **Customer City**: Enter the city where you are located.
* **Customer Postal (Zip Code)**: Enter your postal/zip code.
* **Customer Country**: Enter the country where you are located.
* **Customer State**: Enter your state or province of residence.
* **Customer Zip**: Enter your postal/zip code.
* **Customer Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Shipping Company**: If shipping to a different company, enter the name of the company.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter the shipping address.
* **Shipping City**: Enter the city of the shipping address.
* **Shipping Postal (Zip Code)**: Enter the postal/zip code of the shipping address.
* **Shipping Country**: Enter the country of the shipping address.
* **Shipping State**: Enter the state or province of the shipping address.
* **Payment Method**: Select the method by which you will be paying for your order.
* **Order Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date of your order.
