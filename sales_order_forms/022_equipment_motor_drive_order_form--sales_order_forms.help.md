# Equipment Motor Drive Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equipment Motor Drive Order Form is used by the sales team to collect information from customers for sales orders related to motor drives. This form helps ensure that all necessary details are captured and that orders are processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's information in the `customer_info` field.
2. Provide the customer's contact number in the `contact_number` field.
3. Enter the equipment information in the `equipment_info` field.
4. Select the equipment type from the options provided in the `equipment_type` field.
5. Enter the quantity of equipment ordered in the `quantity` field.
6. Choose a delivery date in the `delivery_date` field.
7. Select a delivery time in the `delivery_time` field.
8. Enter any additional notes or comments in the `order_notes` field.
9. Enter the customer's email address in the `customer_email` field.
10. Enter any additional notes or comments in the `equipment_notes` field.
11. Specify the payment terms in the `payment_terms` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Motor Drive Order Form**: This is the main form title that you will be filling out.
* **Customer Info**: Enter the customer's name, company, or any other relevant information.
* **Contact Number**: Provide the customer's contact number so we can get back to them.
* **Equipment Info**: Enter any additional details about the equipment being ordered.
* **Equipment Type**: Select whether the equipment ordered is a motor drive or not.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of units of equipment being ordered.
* **Delivery Date**: Choose a date for the equipment to be delivered.
* **Delivery Time**: Select a time for the equipment to be delivered.
* **Order Notes**: Add any additional comments or instructions for the order.
* **Customer Email**: Enter the customer's email address so we can get back to them.
* **Equipment Notes**: Add any additional comments or instructions for the equipment being ordered.
* **Payment Terms**: Specify the payment terms and conditions.
