# Tips Of The Trade By Darcie Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tips Of The Trade By Darcie Order Form is designed to collect information from customers interested in purchasing a product or service related to the "Tips Of The Trade" by Darcie.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer information, including your name, contact details, and any other relevant information.
2. Provide your shipping address and any additional shipping details.
3. Select your preferred payment method (Credit, Debit, or EFT).
4. Enter the date and time you would like to receive your order.
5. Enter any product information or specifications you would like to include in your order.
6. Add any additional notes or comments about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, optional): This field is for your name, contact details, and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, optional): Enter your shipping address, including your street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional shipping details, such as specific delivery times or instructions.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select multiple, optional): Choose your preferred payment method (Credit, Debit, or EFT).
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to receive your order.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to receive your order.
* **Product Information** (`product_info`, text, optional): Enter any product information or specifications you would like to include in your order.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your order.
