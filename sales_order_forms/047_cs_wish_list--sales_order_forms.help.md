# CS Wish List - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CS Wish List form is a tool to help customers communicate their wishes and preferences with sales representatives or team members. It's a simple and structured form that guides customers through the process of creating a wish list, including details about themselves, the occasion, and their desired items.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, email address, and phone number (if available) in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter a brief title for your wish list, which will be used to identify it.
3. Select the occasion or purpose of your wish list from the options provided.
4. Provide the desired date for receiving the items on your wish list.
5. List the items you desire by entering the product name, SKU or code, and quantity for each item. Be sure to include approximate prices for each item.
6. Specify which items are most important or must-have items by listing them under the "Priority or Must-Have Items" field.
7. Choose who you want to share your wish list with by selecting from the options provided.
8. Add any special notes or instructions for your sales representatives or team members.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Your Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name.
- **Email Address** (`customer_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to receive notifications.
- **Phone Number** (`customer_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number (if you have one).
- **Customer Account Number** (`account_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your customer account number (if applicable).
- **Wish List Name** (`wishlist_name`, `text`, required): Enter a title for your wish list.
- **Occasion or Purpose** (`wishlist_occasion`, `select_one`, required): Select the occasion or purpose for your wish list.
- **Desired Date for Items** (`wishlist_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date by which you would like to receive the items on your wish list.
- **Item 1 - Product Name** (`item_1_name`, `text`, required): Enter the product name for the first item.
- **Item 1 - Product SKU or Code** (`item_1_sku`, `text`, optional): Enter the SKU or code for the first item.
- **Item 1 - Quantity Desired** (`item_1_quantity`, `number`, required): Enter the quantity desired for the first item.
- **Item 1 - Approximate Price** (`item_1_price`, `number`, optional): Enter the approximate price for the first item.
- **Item 2 - Product Name** (`item_2_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the product name for the second item.
- **Item 2 - Product SKU or Code** (`item_2_sku`, `text`, optional): Enter the SKU or code for the second item.
- **Item 2 - Quantity Desired** (`item_2_quantity`, `number`, optional): Enter the quantity desired for the second item.
- **Item 2 - Approximate Price** (`item_2_price`, `number`, optional): Enter the approximate price for the second item.
- **Item 3 - Product Name** (`item_3_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the product name for the third item.
- **Item 3 - Quantity Desired** (`item_3_quantity`, `number`, optional): Enter the quantity desired for the third item.
- **Priority or Must-Have Items** (`priority_items`, `text`, optional): List the items that are most important or must-have items.
- **Wish List Sharing Preference** (`sharing_preference`, `select_one`, optional): Choose who you want to share your wish list with.
- **Special Notes or Instructions** (`special_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any special notes or instructions for sales representatives or team members.
