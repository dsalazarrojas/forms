# Factory Power Backup Battery Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for ordering power backup batteries from the factory. It collects contact information, delivery details, and payment information to facilitate the production and delivery of the ordered batteries.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Step 1: Gather Required Information**
Fill out all the required fields and provide accurate information where applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter your name, company, address, phone number, and email address.
### Delivery Details
* **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the delivery address, including any specific instructions or requirements.
### Delivery Date
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required): Select a date for delivery.
### Delivery Time
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, required): Select a time for delivery.
### Preferred Date
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, date, optional): Select an alternative date for delivery if the preferred date is not available.
### Preferred Time
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, time, optional): Select an alternative time for delivery if the preferred time is not available.
### Order Details
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, note, required): Enter any special requests or instructions for the order.
### Quantity
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the number of batteries ordered.
### Unit Price
* **Unit Price** (`unit_price`, number, required): Enter the unit price of the ordered batteries.
### Total Price
* **Total Price** (`total_price`, number, required): The total price of the ordered batteries will be calculated based on the quantity and unit price.
### Payment Method
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select a payment method, including Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
### Payment Information
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, required): Enter your payment information for the selected payment method.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, include them in the Order Details field.
* Contact our sales team if you have any questions or concerns.
* We will contact you with any further instructions or information regarding your order.
