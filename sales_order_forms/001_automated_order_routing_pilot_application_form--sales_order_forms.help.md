# Automated Order Routing Pilot Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for the Automated Order Routing Pilot Application. The purpose is to collect information from companies participating in the pilot, ensuring that their integration needs and expectations are captured accurately. This data will help us understand and cater to their specific requirements during the pilot.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
2. Provide a valid business email address where you can be reached for follow-up communication.
3. Enter your organization's name that will be participating in the pilot.
4. Choose your company's approximate size.
5. Describe, in a few sentences, the primary use case for the pilot, focusing on how automated order routing will benefit your organization.
6. If applicable, list the major systems you plan to integrate with as part of the pilot.
7. Give a numeric estimate of the monthly order volume your organization expects during the pilot.
8. Describe measurable outcomes you expect from the pilot and how they will be achieved.
9. Enter the name of the person responsible for integration and technical issues during the pilot.
10. Provide a direct phone number with the country code for this contact person.
11. If desired, choose an approximate date for the pilot start.
12. Add any other relevant information or constraints that might affect the pilot.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Applicant Full Name
* **Applicant Full Name** (`applicant_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.

### Applicant Email
* **Applicant Email** (`applicant_email`, `email`, required): Provide a valid business email address where we can reach you for follow-up.

### Company Name
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter your organization's name that will be participating in the pilot.

### Company Size
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, `select_one`, required): Select your company's approximate size:
	+ 1-10
	+ 11-50
	+ 51-200
	+ 201-1000
	+ 1000+

### Primary Use Case
* **Primary Use Case** (`primary_use_case`, `text`, required): Briefly describe how automatic order routing will be used in your organization.

### Integration Capabilities
* **Integration Capabilities** (`integration_capabilities`, `text`, optional): List major systems to integrate with, for example, ERP or WMS, if applicable.

### Expected Monthly Order Volume
* **Expected Monthly Order Volume** (`expected_monthly_order_volume`, `number`, required): Give a numeric estimate.

### Pilot Success Criteria
* **Pilot Success Criteria** (`success_criteria`, `text`, required): Describe measurable outcomes you expect from the pilot.

### Technical Contact Name
* **Technical Contact Name** (`technical_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person responsible for integration and technical issues during the pilot.

### Technical Contact Phone
* **Technical Contact Phone** (`technical_contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Provide a direct phone number with the country code for this contact person.

### Pilot Start Date
* **Preferred Pilot Start Date** (`pilot_start_date`, `date`, optional): Choose an approximate date you would like to begin the pilot.

### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Any other information or constraints that might affect the pilot.

Tips:
* Ensure the email address provided is valid and active.
* Be as specific as possible with your answers to help us understand your needs accurately.
* If integrating with existing systems, mention them for our team's guidance on possible solutions.
