# Piano Sales Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a sales agreement for buying and selling pianos. It captures the necessary information for a legally binding transaction between the buyer and seller, ensuring a smooth and transparent sales process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the buyer's name and email address on pages 1-2.
2. Specify the piano model and condition on pages 3-4.
3. Choose a payment method and date on pages 5-6.
4. Sign and date the form on pages 7-8 for both the buyer and seller.
5. Check the agreement and acceptance on pages 9-10, and sign and date the form on page 11 if agreed upon.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the buyer.
* **Email** (`2`, `email`, required): Enter the buyer's email address.
* **Piano Model** (`3`, `text`, required): Specify the model of the piano being sold.
* **Piano Condition** (`4`, `select_one`, required): Choose the condition of the piano, which can be Excellent, Fair, or Poor.
* **Payment Method** (`5`, `text`, required): Choose the payment method for the purchase (e.g., cash, credit card, etc.).
* **Payment Date** (`6`, `date`, required): Enter the date of payment.
* **Buyer Signature** (`7`, `text`, required): Sign your name as the buyer, confirming your acceptance of the sales terms.
* **Seller Signature** (`8`, `text`, required): Sign your name as the seller, confirming the sale and acceptance of the sales terms.
* **Agreement** (`9`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional agreement terms or conditions.
* **Buyer Acceptance** (`10`, `select_one`, optional): Check if the buyer has accepted the agreement. If yes, proceed to the next step.
* **Agreement Accepted** (`11`, `date`, optional): Date and sign the form if the buyer has accepted the agreement.

## Tips

* Ensure you carefully read and understand the terms of the sales agreement before signing.
* Make sure to include any additional terms or conditions in the "Agreement" field.
* If the buyer accepts the agreement, date and sign the "Agreement Accepted" field.
