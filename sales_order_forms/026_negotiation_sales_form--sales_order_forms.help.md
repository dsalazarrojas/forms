# Negotiation Sales Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to aid sales professionals in documenting and finalizing sales negotiations, ensuring accurate and comprehensive data collection for future reference and follow-up actions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Select a Product**: Choose the product that is the focus of the current sales negotiation. This selection will serve as a reference point for the rest of the form.
2. **Finalize Terms**: Provide a brief description or notes about the agreement reached with the customer regarding the sale.
3. **Transaction Date**: Enter the date of the sales transaction.
4. **Transaction Time**: Enter the time of the sales transaction. Ensure this matches the date entered in the Transaction Date field.
5. **Transaction Time Period**: Select a time period (e.g., morning, afternoon, evening) of the sales transaction. This helps in understanding the timing of the deal.
6. **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the sale.
7. **Email**: Enter the email address of the customer or relevant contact person.
8. **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the customer or relevant contact person.
9. **Terms**: Enter any specific terms agreed upon during the sale.
10. **Sale Amount**: Enter the total sale amount in numerical format.
11. **Customer Type**: Select whether the customer is a 'Yes' or 'No' for your organization's records.
12. **Deals**: Select all applicable deals associated with this sale.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Choose A Product**: Choose the product being sold, as this is the core of the sales negotiation.
  - *type*: Select a single product from the given options.
* **Finalize Terms**: Add a brief description or notes about the agreement reached with the customer.
  - *type*: A text field allowing users to enter any details or terms agreed upon.
* **Transaction Date**: Enter the date on which the sale took place.
  - *type*: Date field for entering the transaction date.
* **Transaction Time**: Enter the time on which the sale happened.
  - *type*: Time field for entering the transaction time.
* **Transaction Time Period**: Select a time period for further context on the sale time.
  - *type*: Select multiple option.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or observations about the sale.
  - *type*: A text field for entering personal notes.
* **Email**: Enter the customer or contact person's email.
  - *type*: Email field for contact information.
* **Phone**: Enter the customer or contact person's phone number.
  - *type*: Text field for contact information.
* **Terms**: Enter specific terms agreed upon during the sale.
  - *type*: Text field for detailed terms.
* **Sale**: Enter the total sale amount in numerical format.
  - *type*: Number field for entering sale amount.
* **Customer**: Indicate whether the customer is relevant for your organization's records.
  - *type*: Select one option.
* **Deals**: Select all applicable deals associated with this sale.
  - *type*: Select multiple options.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are filled in accurately and completely for accurate sales records.
- If any field is not relevant, skip it as it is optional.
- This form is designed to aid in efficient and organized documentation of sales negotiations.
