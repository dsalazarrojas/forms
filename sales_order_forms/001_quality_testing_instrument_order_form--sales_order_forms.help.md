# Quality Testing Instrument Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quality Testing Instrument Order Form is used to place an order for quality testing instruments. It captures details such as shipping and payment information. This form is used by users to ensure that orders are correctly placed and efficiently fulfilled.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each section completely and accurately, ensuring that all required fields have input.
2. Select the correct instrument type (instrument, service, or other) from the available options.
3. Provide details about the instrument price, quantity, and discount.
4. Select a payment method (credit card, bank transfer, or other) and provide bank account details if necessary.
5. Review and submit the form once all fields have been filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Instrument Order
**Instrument Order (1)**: 
The Instrument Order field is the first field in the form. It is used to select the type of instrument being ordered (instrument, service, or other). Select the correct option from the available choices to ensure that the form is correctly filled out.

### 2. Shipping Details
**Shipping Details (2)**: 
The Shipping Details field is used to select the shipping option (instrument, service, or other). This is the same as the instrument order field, as both are used for different aspects of shipping. Select the correct option to ensure that the form is correctly filled out.

### 3. Payment
**Payment (3)**: 
The Payment field is used to select the payment option (credit card, bank transfer, or other). Select the correct option and provide bank account details if necessary.

### 4. Order Date
**Order Date (4)**: 
The Order Date field is the date on which the order is to be fulfilled. Enter a date in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY) to ensure that the order is correctly scheduled.

### 5. Ship Date
**Ship Date (5)**: 
The Ship Date field is the date on which the order is to be shipped. Enter a date in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY) to ensure that the order is correctly shipped.

### 6. Contact Name
**Contact Name (6)**: 
The Contact Name field is the name of the person filling out the form. This information is used for contact purposes.

### 7. Contact Email
**Contact Email (7)**: 
The Contact Email field is the email address of the person filling out the form. This is the contact email for the person filling out the form.

### 8. Contact Phone
**Contact Phone (8)**: 
The Contact Phone field is the phone number of the person filling out the form. This is the contact phone number for the person filling out the form.

### 9. Order Notes
**Order Notes (9)**: 
The Order Notes field is a free text field where any additional information about the order can be entered.

### 10. Shipping Address
**Shipping Address (10)**: 
The Shipping Address field is the address to which the order is to be shipped. Enter the address in the correct format to ensure that the order is correctly shipped.

### 11. Instrument Type
**Instrument Type (11)**: 
The Instrument Type field is the type of instrument being ordered (instrument, service, or other). Select the correct option from the available choices.

### 12. Instrument Description
**Instrument Description (12)**: 
The Instrument Description field is a free text field where a description of the instrument being ordered can be entered.

### 13. Quantity
**Quantity (13)**: 
The Quantity field is the number of instruments being ordered. Enter the correct quantity to ensure that the order is correctly fulfilled.

### 14. Total Cost
**Total Cost (14)**: 
The Total Cost field is the total cost of the instruments being ordered. This field is not required, but it is recommended to be filled out for accurate calculations.

### 15. Instrument Price
**Instrument Price (15)**: 
The Instrument Price field is the price of each instrument being ordered. Enter the correct price to ensure that the total cost is correctly calculated.

### 16. Discount
**Discount (16)**: 
The Discount field is the discount to be applied to the order. Enter the correct discount to ensure that the total cost is correctly calculated.

### 17. Subtotal
**Subtotal (17)**: 
The Subtotal field is the subtotal of the order. This field is calculated automatically based on the total cost and discount.

### 18. Tax
**Tax (18)**: 
The Tax field is the tax to be applied to the order. Enter the correct tax rate to ensure that the total cost is correctly calculated.

### 19. Shipping Cost
**Shipping Cost (19)**: 
The Shipping Cost field is the cost of shipping the order. Enter the correct shipping cost to ensure that the total cost is correctly calculated.

### 20. Total Price
**Total Price (20)**: 
The Total Price field is the total cost of the order. This field is calculated automatically based on the total cost, discount, tax, and shipping cost.

### 21. Payment Method
**Payment Method (21)**: 
The Payment Method field is the method of payment (credit card, bank transfer, or other). Select the correct option to ensure that the form is correctly filled out.

### 22. Bank Name
**Bank Name (22)**: 
The Bank Name field is the name of the bank account to be used for payment. This field is only required if the payment method is bank transfer.

### 23. Bank Account Number
**Bank Account Number (23)**: 
The Bank Account Number field is the account number of the bank account to be used for payment. This field is only required if the payment method is bank transfer.

### 24. Payment Status
**Payment Status (24)**: 
The Payment Status field is the status of the payment (paid, pending, or declined). This field is automatically updated based on the payment method and payment details.

### 25. Order Status
**Order Status (25)**: 
The Order Status field is the status of the order (in process, complete, or cancelled). This field is automatically updated based on the order details and payment status.
