# In Store Music Licensing Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from music store employees and owners to help us better understand their music licensing needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1.  Fill in the form with your store details.
2.  Select the type of store you have.
3.  Enter your store's address.
4.  Provide any additional usage information about the music you are interested in.
5.  Enter the number of employees you have.
6.  Read and agree to the store license agreement (note: this is a one-time agreement and not a payment method).
7.  Select the options that apply to your store for the consent section.
8.  Enter any payment information if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Store Details** (`store_details`, text, required: false): Enter your store's name, address, and other relevant details. This will help us get a better understanding of your store.
*   **Store Type** (`store_type`, select one, required: false): Select the type of store you have from the options provided (Retail, Wholesale, Food and Beverage, Grocery).
*   **Store Address** (`store_address`, text, required: false): Enter your store's address. This will help us get in touch with you in case of any queries.
*   **Usage Info** (`usage_info`, text, required: false): Provide any additional usage information about the music you are interested in.
*   **Store Employees** (`store_employees`, number, required: false): Enter the number of employees you have.
*   **Store License Agreement** (`store_license_agreement`, note, required: false): Read and agree to this one-time store license agreement.
*   **Store Consent** (`store_consent`, select multiple, required: false): Select any options that apply to your store in this section (I agree to the Terms and Conditions, Other).
*   **Store Payment Info** (`store_payment_info`, text, required: false): Enter any payment information if required.
