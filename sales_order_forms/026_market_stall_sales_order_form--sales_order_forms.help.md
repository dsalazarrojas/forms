# Market Stall Sales Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect sales order information from customers purchasing at market stalls, trade shows, or fairs. It helps you keep a record of customer contact details, order history, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form with the customer's **form data** (e.g., name, date, order number).
2. Enter the customer's **customer details**, including their name and contact information.
3. Provide the **order details**, such as product/service description, quantity, and price.
4. Enter the **payment details**, including the payment method and amount.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data** (`form_data`, `text`, optional): Enter any relevant information about the order, such as the date, order number, or other relevant details.
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, `text`, optional): Fill in the customer's name and contact information, including their phone number and email address.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, `text`, optional): Describe the products or services sold to the customer, including the quantity and price.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, `text`, optional): Record the payment method and amount paid.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, optional): Click this button to complete and submit the form.

## Tips
- Make sure to complete all required fields (none are marked as required in this form, so use your best judgment).
- Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
- You can modify the form to fit your specific needs, but it's recommended to keep it simple and easy to fill out.
