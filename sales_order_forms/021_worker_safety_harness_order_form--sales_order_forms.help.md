# Worker Safety Harness Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Worker Safety Harness Order Form is designed to collect essential information for ordering worker safety harnesses. Please complete this form accurately to ensure timely and correct delivery of the necessary equipment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked as `required` in the form.
2. Ensure that you have selected the correct options for the "Job Type", "Safety Harness Type", "Assigned To", and "Order Status" fields.
3. Enter your contact information (phone number and email address) to enable efficient communication.
4. Submit the form to initiate the ordering process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Worker Name** (`worker_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the worker who requires the safety harness.
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, text, optional): Provide the location where the worker will be performing the job.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of job the worker will be performing (e.g., construction, maintenance, etc.).
* **Safety Harness Type** (`safety_harness_type`, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of safety harnesses required for the job (e.g., full-body, fall arrest, etc.).
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the number of safety harnesses required.
* **Date Ordered** (`date_ordered`, date, optional): Enter the date the safety harnesses were ordered.
* **Comments** (`order_comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or instructions for the order.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter the email address for contact purposes.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_multiple, required): Select the person or team responsible for delivering the safety harnesses.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of the order (e.g., pending, in progress, completed).
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.

Note: This guide is a general explanation of the form fields. Please refer to your organization's specific guidelines and procedures for any additional requirements or instructions.
