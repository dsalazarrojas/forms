# (Slick Client's) L'Oréal's Professional Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help L'Oréal's professional consultants and stylists collect client information and provide personalized recommendations for hair and scalp treatments.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Client Information**: Please fill out this section with your personal details, including your full name and email address. This information will be used to contact you for follow-up discussions.
2. **Hair & Scalp Consultation**: Describe your current hair type, texture, and scalp condition. This will help our consultants understand your needs and preferences.
3. **Current Hair Concerns**: Select all the issues you are experiencing with your hair, such as dryness, oiliness, or breakage.
4. **Previous Chemical Treatments**: If you have had any chemical treatments in the past, please select all that apply.
5. **Last Professional Service Date**: If you have visited a salon recently, please indicate the date of your last service.
6. **Desired Hair Goals**: Describe your ideal hair look, feel, or health goals.
7. **Allergies or Sensitivities**: List any known allergies or sensitivities to hair products or ingredients.
8. **Stylist Recommendations**: Our consultants will use this section to provide personalized recommendations for you.
9. **Consultation Date**: Please provide the date and time of your consultation.
10. **Consultant/Stylist Name**: This field will be filled in by our consultant/stylist.
11. **Recommended Service Plan**: Our consultant/stylist will describe the services to be performed during your visit.
12. **Recommended L'Oréal Products**: Our consultant/stylist will suggest products for your at-home hair care regimen.
13. **Estimated Total Cost**: Please indicate the approximate cost for services and recommended products.
14. **Stylist's Internal Notes**: Our consultant/stylist will add any further observations or instructions for future reference.
15. **Client Acknowledgment & Consent**: Please indicate your agreement to proceed with the recommended services and understand the estimated cost.
16. **Electronic Signature**: Type your full name to acknowledge your consent to this consultation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client's Full Name**: Your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address**: Your email address for follow-up discussions.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number for communication (optional).
* **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth for age-specific recommendations (optional).
* **Preferred Contact Method**: How would you like us to contact you for follow-up discussions (e.g., email, phone call, text message).
* **Hair Type**: Select the type of hair you have (Fine, Medium, Thick).
* **Hair Texture**: Select the texture of your hair (Straight, Wavy, Curly, Coily).
* **Scalp Condition**: Select your current scalp condition (Normal, Dry, Oily, Sensitive, Dandruff, Other).
* **Current Hair Concerns**: Select all the issues you are experiencing with your hair (e.g., Dryness, Oiliness, Breakage, Frizz, Hair loss/Thinning, Dandruff/Flakiness, Sensitivity, Dullness/Lack of Shine, Color Fade).
* **Previous Chemical Treatments**: Select all the chemical treatments you have had in the past (e.g., Coloring, Highlights/Balayage, Perm, Relaxer, Keratin/Smoothing Treatment, Bleaching, None).
* **Last Professional Service Date**: The date of your last salon visit (optional).
* **Desired Hair Goals**: Describe your ideal hair look, feel, or health goals.
* **Allergies or Sensitivities**: List any known allergies or sensitivities to hair products or ingredients.
* **Consultation Date**: The date of your consultation with our consultant/stylist.
* **Consultant/Stylist Name**: This field will be filled in by our consultant/stylist.
* **Recommended Service Plan**: Our consultant/stylist will describe the services to be performed during your visit.
* **Recommended L'Oréal Products**: Our consultant/stylist will suggest products for your at-home hair care regimen.
* **Estimated Total Cost**: Please indicate the approximate cost for services and recommended products.
* **Stylist's Internal Notes**: Our consultant/stylist will add any further observations or instructions for future reference.
* **Client Agreement**: Please indicate your agreement to proceed with the recommended services and understand the estimated cost.
* **Electronic Signature**: Type your full name to acknowledge your consent to this consultation.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure our consultants can provide the best service for you.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your consultant/stylist for clarification.
* This form is a tool to help us provide personalized recommendations; our consultants/stylists will use it as a starting point for your consultation.
