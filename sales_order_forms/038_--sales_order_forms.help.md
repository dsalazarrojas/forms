# استمارة تدريبات الميديا - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Order Form is used to document and record sales orders, capturing essential details for accurate and efficient sales management.

## How To Complete This Form

To ensure accuracy and efficiency in filling out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields, denoted by the asterisk (*) symbol.
2. Enter unique records for each sale to avoid duplication and unnecessary data.
3. Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sales Order Form 1** (`sales_order_form_1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the sales order or any additional comments if necessary.
* **Sales Order Number** (`sales_order_number`, `text`, required): Enter a unique number for this sales order.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the client or customer.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the order was made or received.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time the order was made or received.
* **Sale Amount** (`sale_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the total amount of the sale.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose the payment method used for this sale (e.g., Cash, Credit Card, etc.).
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, `text`, required): Enter any additional payment information or instructions.
* **Sale Status** (`sale_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose the status of the sale (e.g., Completed, Pending, etc.).
* **Sale Note** (`sale_note`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments for the sale.
* **Sale Date 2** (`sale_date_2`, `date`, optional): Enter an optional date related to the sale.
* **Sale Time 2** (`sale_time_2`, `time`, optional): Enter an optional time related to the sale.
* **Sale Amount 2** (`sale_amount_2`, `number`, optional): Enter an optional amount related to the sale.
* **Payment Method 2** (`payment_method_2`, `select_one`, optional): Choose an optional payment method for this sale.
* **Payment Details 2** (`payment_details_2`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional payment information or instructions.
* **Sale Status 2** (`sale_status_2`, `select_one`, optional): Choose an optional status update for this sale.
* **Sale Note 2** (`sale_note_2`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments for this sale.
* **Sale Date 3** (`sale_date_3`, `date`, optional): Enter an optional date related to the sale.
* **Sale Time 3** (`sale_time_3`, `time`, optional): Enter an optional time related to the sale.
* **Sale Amount 3** (`sale_amount_3`, `number`, optional): Enter an optional amount related to the sale.
* **Payment Method 3** (`payment_method_3`, `select_one`, optional): Choose an optional payment method for this sale.
* **Payment Details 3** (`payment_details_3`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional payment information or instructions.
* **Sale Status 3** (`sale_status_3`, `select_one`, optional): Choose an optional status update for this sale.
* **Sale Note 3** (`sale_note_3`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments for this sale.

## Tips
- Double-check all entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
- Use unique records for each sale to avoid duplication and potential errors.
- Enter relevant and detailed information in the payment details fields for accurate tracking and follow-up.
- Update status fields as necessary to reflect changes in the sale's progress.
