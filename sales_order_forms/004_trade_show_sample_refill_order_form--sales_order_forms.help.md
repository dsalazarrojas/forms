# Trade Show Sample Refill Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trade Show Sample Refill Order Form is designed for trade show participants to submit their refill order information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by accessing the "Customer Info" page, where you will be asked to either select your customer name from the provided options or enter your phone number.
   If you are a returning customer, please select your customer name for easier order tracking.
2. Proceed to the "Shipping Method" page to choose between two shipping options.
3. On the "Order Details" page, provide any additional information about your order.
   Please be detailed and clear in your description to help us process your order efficiently.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info**: Choose your customer name from the provided options, or type your phone number for identification purposes.
* **Shipping Method**: Select one of the two available shipping options to ensure timely and convenient delivery.
* **Order Details**: Type any additional information related to your order, such as specific products, quantities, or any special requests.
