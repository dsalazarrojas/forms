# valentines_day_2021 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for placing orders for Valentine's Day 2021 sales. Please fill out the form carefully and completely to ensure accurate processing of your order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the **Order Info** page with your order details.
2. Proceed to the **Shipping Info** page and provide your shipping address and contact information.
3. Move on to the **Order Details** page and enter any additional information about your order.
4. On the **Payment Info** page, select your payment method and enter the amount you wish to pay.
5. On the **Shipping Address** page, enter your shipping address.
6. On the **Phone** page, enter your phone number.
7. On the **Email** page, enter your email address.
8. On the **Shipping Address** page, enter your shipping city.
9. On the **State** page, enter your state or region.
10. On the **Zip** page, enter your zip or postal code.
11. On the **Payment Method** page, select your preferred payment method (Card, PayPal, or Apple Pay).
12. On the **Amount** page, enter the amount you wish to pay.
13. On the **Customer Info** page, enter your customer information (required).
14. On the **Name** page, enter your name.
15. On the **Email** page, enter your email address.
16. On the **Phone** page, enter your phone number.
17. On the **Note** page, enter any additional notes about your order.
18. On the **Date** page, select the date of the order.
19. On the **Time** page, select the time of the order.
20. On the **Status** page, select the status of your order (Pending, Approved, or Cancelled).
21. On the **Status Note** page, enter any additional notes about the status of your order.
22. On the **Total** page, enter the total amount of the order.
