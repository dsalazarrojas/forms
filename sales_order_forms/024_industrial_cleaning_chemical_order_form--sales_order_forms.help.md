# Industrial Cleaning Chemical Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place orders for industrial cleaning chemicals. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the chemicals you wish to order from the list of available options.
2. Enter your delivery details, including delivery location, city, post code, state, and country.
3. Enter any additional notes or comments about the order.
4. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.
5. Enter any additional delivery notes or comments.
6. Enter the chemical name, quantity, unit price, and total price.
7. Enter the delivery date and time.
8. Select the payment term and enter any additional payment terms.
9. Enter any order notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Chemicals**: Select the chemicals you wish to order from the list of available options.
* **Delivery Details**: Enter the delivery location, city, post code, state, and country for the order.
* **Terms Acknowledgment**: Acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms and conditions of the order.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Delivery Location**: Enter the location where the order will be delivered.
* **Delivery City**: Enter the city where the order will be delivered.
* **Delivery Postal Code**: Enter the postal code where the order will be delivered.
* **Delivery State**: Enter the state where the order will be delivered.
* **Delivery Country**: Enter the country where the order will be delivered.
* **Chemical Name**: Enter the name of the chemical being ordered.
* **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the chemical being ordered.
* **Units**: Enter the units of the chemical being ordered.
* **Unit Price**: Enter the price per unit of the chemical being ordered.
* **Total Price**: Enter the total price of the chemical being ordered.
* **Delivery Date**: Enter the date when the chemical will be delivered.
* **Delivery Time**: Enter the time when the chemical will be delivered.
* **Payment Term**: Select the payment term for the order.
* **Payment Terms**: Enter any additional payment terms.
* **Order Notes**: Enter any notes or comments about the order.
* **Order Notes 2**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the order.
* **Chemical ID**: Enter the ID of the chemical being ordered.
* **Delivery Notes**: Enter any notes or comments about the delivery.
* **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the customer.
* **Customer Email**: Enter the email address of the customer.

Note: This form has been designed to be user-friendly and easy to use. If you have any questions or need further assistance, please don't hesitate to contact us.
