This help guide aims to explain the Floor Direction Sticker Order Form, which is designed to collect essential information for placing floor direction stickers. The form is likely used in a warehouse or store context to track the order of products on the floor. The purpose of the form is to gather details about the business, delivery information, product selection, delivery date, delivery time, notes, email, and phone number. This form will be used internally, so only relevant fields are required and optional fields are included for additional context.

# Floor Direction Sticker Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Floor Direction Sticker Order Form is designed to collect information for placing floor direction stickers in a warehouse or store setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Business Info section with your company details.
2. Provide delivery information, including the date and time of delivery.
3. Select the products you would like to have stickers for.
4. Add any additional notes about the order.
5. Enter your email address for contact.
6. Optionally, provide your phone number for additional contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Info (Business Info)** (`business_info`, `text`, not required): Enter your company name and any other relevant business details.
* **Delivery Info (Delivery Info)** (`delivery_info`, `text`, not required): Provide the delivery details, including the date and time of delivery.
* **Product Selection (Product Selection)** (`product_selection`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select the products you would like to have stickers for. The options are 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **Delivery Date (Delivery Date)** (`delivery_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the desired delivery date for the order.
* **Delivery Time (Delivery Time)** (`delivery_time`, `time`, not required): Enter the desired delivery time for the order.
* **Notes (Notes)** (`notes`, `note`, not required): Add any additional notes about the order.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, `email`, not required): Enter your email address for contact.
* **Phone (Phone)** (`phone`, `text`, not required): Optionally, provide your phone number for additional contact.
