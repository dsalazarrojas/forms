# Custom Packaging Tape Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers for custom packaging tape orders. Please fill out this form with your relevant details so we can process your order accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Customer Information**: Fill in your name and any other relevant customer details.
2. **Artwork**: Provide a detailed description of your custom packaging tape artwork requirements.
3. **Shipping**: Select whether you want to include shipping or not. If you select "Yes", please provide additional shipping details.
4. **Payment**: Enter the payment amount for your custom packaging tape order.
5. **Notes**: Add any additional notes or instructions for your order.
6. **Email**: If you'd like to receive updates or confirmation of your order, enter your email address here.
7. **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you for any further details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer**: This field is for your name and any other relevant customer details.
* **Customer Information**: This section is for general customer information, such as name, address, etc. It's essential to fill this out accurately.
* **Artwork**: Describe your custom packaging tape artwork requirements in detail. This includes any specific design, color, or text you'd like to feature on your tape.
* **Shipping**: Select "Yes" if you'd like to have shipping included with your order. If you select "Yes", please provide additional shipping details, such as shipping address, etc.
* **Payment**: Enter the total payment amount for your custom packaging tape order.
* **Notes**: Add any extra notes or instructions for your order that the team should be aware of.
* **Email**: Enter your email address if you'd like to receive updates or confirmations of your order.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you for any additional details or questions.
