# Washing Machine Purchase Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to record the customer's agreement with the purchase details for a washing machine purchase, including contact information, purchase date, machine type, purchase price, delivery address, payment method, payment due date, and payment total.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields: Contact Name, Email, Phone, Purchase Date, Machine Type, Payment Method, and Payment Due Date.
2. Fill in the optional fields: Contact Address, Payment Total, and signature.
3. Make sure to select the correct Machine Type from the dropdown options.
4. Enter the Purchase Price as a numerical value.
5. Enter the Payment Total as a numerical value.
6. Sign the form with your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the customer's name as they will be contacted.
- **Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the customer's email address.
- **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the customer's phone number.
- **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the purchase.
- **Machine Type** (`machine_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of washing machine purchased (e.g. Washing Machine, Dryer, Other).
- **Machine Type Options**: The options are Washing Machine, Dryer, and Other.
- **Purchase Price** (`purchase_price`, number, required): Enter the price of the machine.
- **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, optional): Enter the delivery address if required.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the method of payment (e.g. Cash, Credit Card, PayPal).
- **Payment Method Options**: The options are Cash, Credit Card, and PayPal.
- **Payment Due Date** (`payment_due_date`, date, required): Enter the date payment is due.
- **Payment Total** (`payment_total`, number, optional): Enter the total payment amount if required.
- **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Sign the form with your signature.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
- Double-check the payment information to avoid errors.
- If you need to make any changes to the purchase agreement, contact your sales representative.
