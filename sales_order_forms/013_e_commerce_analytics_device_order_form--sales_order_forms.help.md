# E Commerce Analytics Device Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect E-commerce analytics data for tracking sales orders. The collected data includes customer information, order details, shipping methods, and payment methods.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer's information (page 1).
2. Complete the order details section (page 2).
3. Provide shipping details for the order (page 3).
4. Enter payment information (page 4).
5. Add any additional customer address information (page 5).
6. List the items ordered (page 6).
7. Select the shipping method (page 7).
8. Indicate the order status (page 8).
9. Choose the payment method (page 9).
10. Enter the order date and ship date (pages 10 and 11).
11. Add any notes about the order (page 12).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Customer Information (page 1)
* **Customer Information**: This field is used to capture the customer's name, contact details, and any other relevant information.
* **Customer Address (page 5)**: This field is used to capture the customer's address, including street, state, and zip code.

### 2. Order Details (page 2)
* **Order Details**: This field is used to capture the details of the order, including any relevant product information.

### 3. Shipping Details (page 3)
* **Shipping Details**: This field is used to capture the shipping method and any relevant shipping details.

### 4. Payment Info (page 4)
* **Payment Info**: This field is used to capture the payment method and any relevant payment details.

### 5. Customer Address (page 5)
* **Customer Address**: This field is used to capture the customer's address, including street, state, and zip code.

### 6. Order Items (page 6)
* **Order Items**: This field is used to list the items ordered by the customer.

### 7. Shipping Method (page 7)
* **Shipping Method**: This field is used to select the shipping method (Yes/No).

### 8. Order Status (page 8)
* **Order Status**: This field is used to select the order status (Active/Inactive).

### 9. Payment Method (page 9)
* **Payment Method**: This field is used to select the payment method (Yes/No).

### 10. Order Date (page 10)
* **Order Date**: This field is used to enter the date of the order.

### 11. Ship Date (page 11)
* **Ship Date**: This field is used to enter the shipping date.

### 12. Notes (page 12)
* **Notes**: This field is used to add any additional notes about the order.
