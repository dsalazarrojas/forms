# Kitchen Flooring Safety Mat Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kitchen Flooring Safety Mat Order Form is a form used to collect information from customers for ordering safety mats for their kitchen flooring.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Details" field.
2. Provide your delivery address in the "Delivery Address" field.
3. Select the products you want to order in the "Product Selections" field.
4. Enter your payment details in the "Payment Details" field.
5. Write a brief summary of your order in the "Order Summary" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, email, and phone number to contact you for any questions or concerns.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, required/optional): Provide your delivery address where you would like to receive your order.
* **Product Selections** (`product_selections`, text, required/optional): Choose the specific safety mat products you would like to order. Be sure to specify quantity and any customization options.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your payment method and any relevant payment information (e.g. credit card info).
* **Order Summary** (`order_summary`, text, required/optional): Write a brief summary of your order, including any special requests or instructions.
