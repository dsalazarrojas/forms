# custom-vibration-dampener-order-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer information and order details for a custom vibration dampener product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Customer Information" section below with your contact details and order preferences.
2. Provide your order details in the "Order Details" section.
3. Confirm your order in the "Confirm Order" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Information** (1, text, **required**):
This is where you'll provide your basic contact information and any additional details we need to know about you.
- **Order Details** (2, text, **required**):
Here, enter the specifics about your custom vibration dampener order, including any special requests or modifications.
- **Confirm Order** (3, text, **required**):
Double-check your order details to ensure everything is correct before submitting.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as **required**).
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact our support team.
* We'll review and process your order as soon as possible after submission.
