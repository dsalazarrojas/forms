# Maintenance Spray Lubricant Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Maintenance Spray Lubricant Order Form" is a tool used to collect details for maintenance requests related to spray lubricants. Fill out this form to provide necessary information for the fulfillment of these requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form:

1. Start by providing customer details on **page 1, "Customer Details"**.
2. Select the **Spray Lubricant** that is required from the available options.
3. Choose the **Spray Concentration** needed for the request.
4. Specify the **Delivery Date** and **Delivery Time** for the order.
5. Provide the **Quantity** of spray lubricant needed.
6. Add any **Notes** about this order.
7. Include your **Email** address and **Phone** number for easy contact.
8. Select the **Assigned** representative or service from the options provided.
9. Set the **Status** of the order as "In Progress" or "Completed".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Details**: Enter your name and any other contact information you'd like us to use.
- **Spray Lubricant**: Choose the specific type of spray lubricant you need. (Optional)
- **Spray Concentration**: Select the suitable concentration of the spray lubricant. For example, 1%, 2%, or 5%.
- **Delivery Date**: Enter the date when you'd like the order delivered.
- **Delivery Time**: Indicate the time of day when you'd like the order delivered.
- **Quantity**: Specify the amount of spray lubricant you need.
- **Notes**: Add any specific comments or instructions about this order.
- **Email**: Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you easily.
- **Phone**: Provide your phone number in case we need to call you about this order.
- **Assigned**: Choose who you'd like to be assigned to fulfill this order, or select "Other" for your choice.
- **Status**: Set the status of the order as "In Progress" or "Completed" to track its progress.
