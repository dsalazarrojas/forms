# Serum Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Serum Order Form is designed to collect information from customers or sales representatives for sales orders related to serums.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please follow these steps:
	* Fill out the form page by page, starting from the first page.
	* Answer each question to the best of your abilities.
	* Review and confirm your order on the final page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Main Page (Page 1)
* **Serum Order Form** (`Main Page`, `text`, required/optional): The title of this form is displayed here.

### 2. Customer Info (Page 2)
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info_page`, `text`, required/optional): This section is meant to collect general customer information. Please fill in this page according to your knowledge.

### 3. Order Detail (Page 3)
* **Order Detail** (`order_detail_page`, `text`, required/optional): This is where you will fill in details about the order.

### 4. Payment Info (Page 4)
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info_page`, `text`, required/optional): This section is meant for payment information, if applicable.

### 5. Order Review (Page 5)
* **Order Review** (`order_review_page`, `text`, required/optional): Review and confirm your order here.

### 6. Confirmation (Page 6)
* **Confirmation** (`confirmation_page`, `text`, required/optional): Confirm your order and review the details here.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all necessary fields to avoid missing information.
* Double-check your order for any errors or inaccuracies.
