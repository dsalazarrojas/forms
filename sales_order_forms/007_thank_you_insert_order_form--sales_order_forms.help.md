# thank_you_insert_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and information from customers after an order has been placed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the `first_name` field. This field is optional, but it's nice to have for our records.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the `last_name` field. This field is also optional.
3. Enter your **Order ID** in the `order_id` field. This will help us locate your order in our system.
4. Enter the date you received your order in the `order_date` field. This will help us track order fulfillment.
5. Enter the time you received your order in the `order_time` field. This is optional, but it can help us better understand your order experience.
6. Enter the total amount you spent on your order in the `order_total` field. This is optional.
7. Provide feedback in the `feedback_question` field. This is an open-ended question for you to share your thoughts about your experience.
8. If you'd like to share a photo of your order, use the `photo_question` field.
9. Share any comments about your order in the `order_comments` field. This is optional, but it helps us understand your experience.
10. Enter your customer **Email** in the `customer_email` field. We'll use this to contact you if needed.
11. Enter your customer **Phone Number** in the `customer_phone` field. This is optional, but it's helpful for our team to contact you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Order ID** (`order_id`, text, optional): Enter the ID of your order.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you received your order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you received your order.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, optional): Enter the total amount you spent on your order.
* **Feedback Question** (`feedback_question`, text, optional): Provide any feedback or thoughts about your experience.
* **Photo Question** (`photo_question`, text, optional): If you have a photo related to your order, share it here.
* **Order Comments** (`order_comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments about your order.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for our team to contact you.

## Tips

* Please be sure to enter accurate information, especially for fields like Order Date and Order Total.
* If you're unsure about any field, you can always ask our support team for help.
* We appreciate your feedback and look forward to hearing about your experience!
