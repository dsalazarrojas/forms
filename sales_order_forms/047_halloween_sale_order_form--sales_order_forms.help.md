# halloween_sale_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for a Halloween sale or order. It gathers details about the sale, shipping, and customer information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info**: Enter the customer's name. 
* **Sale Items**: List the items being sold.
* **Sale Quantity**: Enter the quantity of each item.
* **Sale Price**: Enter the sale price.
* **Shipping Method**: Choose the shipping method.
* **Customer Message**: Enter any additional message to the customer.
