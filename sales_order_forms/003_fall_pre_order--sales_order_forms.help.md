# Fall Pre Order - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fall Pre Order form is a sales order form used to collect customer information, product selection, and shipping details for orders that are due to be shipped during the Fall season. This form is meant for customers to accurately and completely provide their order details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Fall Pre Order form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your **Order Information** (`Order Information`, `text`, `false`), providing a clear and accurate description of your order.
2.  Enter your **Customer Information** (`Customer Information`, `text`, `false`), including any relevant details about yourself and your order.
3.  Select the desired **Product** (`Product`, `select_one`, `false`) from the options provided.
4.  Fill in your **Shipping Information** (`Shipping Information`, `text`, `false`), including any relevant shipping details.
5.  Provide a **Review** (`Review`, `note`, `false`) of your order to help us ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Order Information** (`Order Information`, `text`, `false`): This field is used to enter a description of your order. Please provide a clear and concise description of your order.
*   **Customer Information** (`Customer Information`, `text`, `false`): Enter any relevant details about yourself and your order.
*   **Product** (`Product`, `select_one`, `false`): Select the desired product for your order.
*   **Shipping Information** (`Shipping Information`, `text`, `false`): Fill in your shipping details, including any relevant information.
*   **Review** (`Review`, `note`, `false`): Provide a review of your order to help us ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Tips
*   Double-check your order details to ensure accuracy.
*   Use clear and concise descriptions for all fields.
*   Select the correct product from the options provided.
*   Provide any additional shipping details if necessary.
