# Wholesale Sample Memo - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Wholesale Sample Memo** form is a tool used to provide detailed information about wholesale samples, which includes customer and product data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **customer_info** field by selecting the relevant option (Yes/No) to indicate the customer's status.
2. In the **product_info** field, choose the correct option (Yes/No) to specify the product's status.
3. Type any additional comments or notes in the **wholesale_sample_memo_form** field, if required or necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **wholesale_sample_memo_form** (text, optional): Fill in any additional comments or notes related to the customer's order status and product information.
* **customer_info** (select multiple, optional): Choose 'Yes' if the customer has requested the product, or 'No' if they have not.
* **product_info** (select one, optional): Select 'Yes' if the product is available, or 'No' if it is not.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure correct order fulfillment and customer communication.
* Review the form before submitting to avoid any duplicate entries or errors.
