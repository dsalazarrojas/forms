# Robotics Calibration Stand Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for customers to request a robotics calibration stand for their specific needs. Please fill out the form below to provide us with the necessary information to fulfill your request.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form completely and accurately to provide us with the necessary information.
- Make sure to select the correct options for each field.
- If you have any special requests or notes, please include them in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Info**: This is a brief description of the order, please provide a short statement about the order.
- **Name**: Your name, as it will appear on the order receipt and delivery documents.
- **Customer Email**: Your email address, where we will contact you with updates and delivery information.
- **Customer Phone**: Your phone number, in case we need to reach you directly.
- **Calibration Stand Details**: A detailed description of the calibration stand you are requesting.
- **Quantity**: The number of calibration stands you need.
- **Date of Delivery**: The date you would like to receive the calibration stand.
- **Time of Delivery**: The time of day you would like to receive the calibration stand.
- **Calibration Stand Type**: Please select the type of calibration stand you need from the list below:
  - Stand Type 1
  - Stand Type 2
  - Stand Type 3
- **Calibration Stand Type Other**: If the options above do not fit your needs, please describe your specific requirements.
- **Calibration Stand Size**: A brief description of the size of the calibration stand you need.
- **Delivery Address**: The address where you would like to receive the calibration stand.
- **Delivery Notes**: Any special delivery instructions or notes for the delivery team.
- **Assigned Agent**: The agent who will be handling your order. Please select from the list below:
  - chatjimmy
  - Agent 2
  - Agent 3
- **Notes**: Any additional comments or requests you have about the order.
- **Form Comments**: Any additional information you would like to provide about the order.

## Tips
- Please double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us directly.
