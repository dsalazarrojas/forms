# Industrial Cable Tray Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Industrial Cable Tray Order Form is designed to collect relevant information from customers to process their cable tray orders efficiently. This form ensures that all necessary details are captured to facilitate accurate processing and timely delivery of the ordered cable trays.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review each section carefully and fill in the required information accurately.
* Fill in the general information, including your name and company name (if applicable).
* Provide details about the cable tray you wish to order, including type and quantity.
* Indicate whether you have a contact person at your organization (Yes/No).
* Enter delivery and payment information.
* Specify shipping method (Yes/No).
* Add any special instructions or notes for the order.
* Provide contact information for the sales representative handling the order (if desired).
* Add order notes, if applicable.
* Enter customer contact information, including email and phone number.
* Enter additional customer information, such as company name and address.
* Confirm the customer's city, state, and postal code.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (`General Information`, `text`, required/optional):**
	+ This section is used to capture the customer's name and company name (if applicable).
* **Cable Tray Details (`Cable Tray Details`, `text`, required/optional):**
	+ Describe the type and quantity of the cable tray you wish to order.
* **Customer Contact (`Customer Contact`, `select_multiple`, required/optional):**
	+ If yes, provide a contact person's name and number to reach them.
* **Delivery Information (`Delivery Information`, `text`, required/optional):**
	+ Enter the delivery date and time you prefer.
* **Payment Information (`Payment Information`, `text`, required/optional):**
	+ Indicate your payment method (e.g., credit card, check, etc.).
* **Shipping Information (`Shipping Information`, `text`, required/optional):**
	+ Specify your preferred shipping method (e.g., ground, air).
* **Special Requests (`Special Requests`, `text`, required/optional):**
	+ Add any special instructions or notes for the order.
* **Order Date (`Order Date`, `date`, required/optional):**
	+ Enter the date on which you want to place the order.
* **Order Time (`Order Time`, `time`, required/optional):**
	+ Enter the time on which you want to place the order.
* **Customer Information (`Customer Information`, `text`, required/optional):**
	+ Provide the customer's name, company name, and title.
* **Delivery Address (`Delivery Address`, `text`, required/optional):**
	+ Enter the customer's shipping address, including city, state, and postal code.
* **Payment Method (`Payment Method`, `select_multiple`, required/optional):**
	+ Indicate your preferred payment method (e.g., credit card, check, etc.).
* **Shipping Method (`Shipping Method`, `select_one`, required/optional):**
	+ Specify your preferred shipping method (e.g., ground, air).
* **Special Instructions (`Special Instructions`, `text`, required/optional):**
	+ Add any special requests or notes for the order.
* **Sales Representative (`Sales Rep`, `text`, required/optional):**
	+ Provide the sales representative's name and contact information.
* **Order Notes (`Order Notes`, `text`, required/optional):**
	+ Add any additional notes about the order.
* **Customer Email (`Customer Email`, `email`, required/optional):**
	+ Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone (`Customer Phone`, `text`, required/optional):**
	+ Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Company (`Customer Company`, `text`, required/optional):**
	+ Enter the customer's company name.
* **Customer Address (`Customer Address`, `text`, required/optional):**
	+ Enter the customer's address.
* **Customer City (`Customer City`, `text`, required/optional):**
	+ Enter the customer's city.
* **Customer State (`Customer State`, `text`, required/optional):**
	+ Enter the customer's state.
* **Customer Postal Code (`Customer Postal Code`, `text`, required/optional):**
	+ Enter the customer's postal code.

Note: This help guide explains each field with its corresponding label.
