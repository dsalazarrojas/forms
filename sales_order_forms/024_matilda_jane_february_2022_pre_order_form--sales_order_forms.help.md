# Matilda Jane February 2022 Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to pre-order the Matilda Jane collection. Please fill out the form with your required information.

## How To Complete This Form
- Ensure you have all the required fields filled out as indicated by the asterisk (\*).
- Please provide your First Name in the field labeled "First Name".
- Please provide your Last Name in the field labeled "Last Name".
- Enter your Email address in the field labeled "Email".
- Enter your Phone number in the field labeled "Phone".
- Select the desired Order Date for your pre-order.
- Choose the Product you wish to pre-order.
- Enter the quantity of the product you wish to pre-order.
- Enter any additional Comments or Notes about your pre-order.
- If you have a Shipping Date in mind, enter it in the field labeled "Shipping Date".
- Select the Shipping Method for your pre-order (Overnight, Standard, or Express).
- Select the Payment Method for your pre-order (Cash, Credit, or PayPal).
- Enter the Total Cost of your pre-order.
- Enter any applicable Discount for your pre-order.
- Enter the Tax applicable to your pre-order.
- Enter the Grand Total of your pre-order.
- If you have a Customer ID, please enter it in the field labeled "Customer ID".
- If you have a Customer Name, please enter it in the field labeled "Customer Name".
- If you have a Customer Email, please enter it in the field labeled "Customer Email".
- Enter any additional Order Notes.
- Enter your Shipping Address.
- Enter your Shipping City.
- Enter your Shipping State.
- Enter your Shipping Postal Code.
- Select the Payment Status for your pre-order (Paid or Unpaid).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address here.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number here.
* **Order Date** (`Order Date`, `date`, required): Select the date of your order.
* **Product** (`Product`, `text`, required): Choose the product you wish to order.
* **Quantity** (`Quantity`, `number`, required): Enter the quantity of the product you wish to order.
* **Comments/Notes** (`Comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Shipping Date** (`Shipping Date`, `date`, optional): Select the shipping date if you have one in mind.
* **Shipping Method** (`Shipping Method`, `select_one`, required): Select the shipping method for your pre-order (Overnight, Standard, or Express).
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the payment method for your pre-order (Cash, Credit, or PayPal).
* **Total Cost** (`Total Cost`, `number`, required): Enter the total cost of your pre-order.
* **Discount** (`Discount`, `number`, optional): Enter any applicable discount for your pre-order.
* **Tax** (`Tax`, `number`, required): Enter the tax applicable to your pre-order.
* **Grand Total** (`Grand Total`, `number`, required): Enter the grand total of your pre-order.
* **Customer ID** (`Customer ID`, `text`, optional): Enter your customer ID if applicable.
* **Customer Name** (`Customer Name`, `text`, optional): Enter your customer name if applicable.
* **Customer Email** (`Customer Email`, `email`, optional): Enter your customer email if applicable.
* **Order Notes** (`Order Notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional order notes.
* **Shipping Address** (`Shipping Address`, `text`, optional): Enter your shipping address.
* **Shipping City** (`Shipping City`, `text`, optional): Enter your shipping city.
* **Shipping State** (`Shipping State`, `text`, optional): Enter your shipping state.
* **Shipping Postal Code** (`Shipping Postal Code`, `text`, required): Enter your shipping postal code.
* **Payment Status** (`Payment Status`, `select_one`, required): Select the payment status for your pre-order (Paid or Unpaid).
