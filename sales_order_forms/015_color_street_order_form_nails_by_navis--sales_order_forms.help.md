The form collects contact information (name, email, address, phone number) and order details (polish selection, set names, total sets, sample request). # Color Street Order Form - Nails By Navis - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Color Street Order Form is used for customers to order Color Street products with Nails By Navis. It captures contact and order information for both pre-orders and stock orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your **Full Name** in the `Full Name` field (required).
2. Enter your **Email Address** in the `Email Address` field (required).
3. Provide your **Mailing Address** in the `Mailing Address` field (required).
4. If you have a **Phone Number**, enter it in the `Phone Number` field (optional).
5. Select the **Name of Sets you want to pre-order or buy** in the `Name of Sets you want to pre-order or buy` field (required).
6. Enter the **Total Number of Sets** you are ordering in the `Total Number of Sets` field (required).
7. Check the **Do you need a sample (twosie) sent?** dropdown to indicate if you need a sample sent (required).
8. Choose your **Payment Method** from the available options (required).
9. Enter your **Payment Username/ID** in the `Payment Username/ID` field (required).
10. Select your **Delivery Method** from the available options (required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (navis_customer_name, text, required): Please enter your full name as it should appear on your order confirmation and any communications from us.
* **Email Address** (navis_email, email, required): Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **Mailing Address** (navis_address, text, required): Enter your mailing address for us to send your order and any samples or communications.
* **Phone Number** (navis_phone, text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it to allow for easy contact.
* **Name of Sets you want to pre-order or buy** (navis_set_names, text, required): Select the name(s) of the set(s) you want to order.
* **Total Number of Sets** (navis_total_sets, number, required): Enter how many sets you are ordering.
* **Do you need a sample (twosie) sent?** (navis_sample_request, select_one, required): Check if you need a sample sent to you.
* **Payment Method** (navis_payment_type, select_one, required): Choose how you will be paying for your order.
* **Payment Username/ID** (navis_payment_username, text, required): Enter your payment username or ID.
* **Delivery Method** (navis_delivery_method, select_one, required): Select how you want your order delivered.
* **If this is for a party, who is the hostess?** (navis_hostess_name, text, optional): If this is an order for a party, enter the hostess's name.
* **Anything else for Navis?** (navis_notes, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or requests, enter them here.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to complete your order.
- Double-check your selections and payment information to ensure accuracy.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us directly.
