# customer_quote_page_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to gather customer information for the sales quote page.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. Navigate through the pages using the "Next" and "Back" buttons.
3. Use the hints provided for guidance on each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (Text): Enter your home or office address.
* **Vehicle Make** (Text): Enter the make of the vehicle you're interested in.
* **Vehicle Model** (Text): Enter the model of the vehicle you're interested in.
* **Desired Delivery Date** (Date): Select the date when you would like to receive the vehicle.
* 
The form is designed to be user-friendly, and the hints provided will guide you through the process. If you have any issues or questions, don't hesitate to reach out to the sales team for assistance.
