# Team Media Kit Reprint Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Media Kit Reprint Order Form is used to request a reprint of a Team Media Kit. This form will ask for your information and specific details about your order, and will guide you through the process of submitting your request.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out your **First Name** and **Last Name** to provide your contact information.
- You can choose to provide your **Department** (optional) if it is relevant to your order.
- You can also choose to provide your **Email** and **Phone** if you would like to be contacted about your order.
- If you select **Yes** for **Department**, we will ask for your **Department** again. Please select **Yes** if your department is one of the options below.
- You can select multiple **Team Members** you would like to include in your order.
- Please provide the **Date** and **Time** for the reprint if it is different from the default date and time.
- You can also select the **Order Type** if it is a rush order (optional).
- You can choose the **Quantity** of reprints you need.
- Finally, you can add any additional **Message** you have regarding your order.
