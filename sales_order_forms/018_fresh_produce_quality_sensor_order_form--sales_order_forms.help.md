# Fresh Produce Quality Sensor Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fresh Produce Quality Sensor Order Form is designed to collect information about the order, sensor specifications, and the actual order placed for quality monitoring in the fresh produce industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Start by entering the order details** in the "order_details" field on Page 1.
2. **Provide sensor details** on Page 2, specifying any necessary information about the sensor being ordered.
3. **Enter the sensor order** on Page 3, ensuring all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **order_details** (Page 1)
* **order_details**: This field is where you can enter any relevant details about the order, such as the date, customer name, or order number. 

### **sensor_details** (Page 2)
* **sensor_details**: Here, you can provide any specific details about the sensor being ordered, including its model number, brand, or any special requests.

### **sensor_order** (Page 3)
* **sensor_order**: This field is for entering the actual order placed, ensuring all necessary information is provided, such as the quantity, shipping date, and expected delivery date.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are filled out accurately to avoid delays or issues with order processing.
- If you're unsure about any field or process, consult with the sales team or procurement team for clarification.
- Keep a record of your form completion for reference or audit purposes.
