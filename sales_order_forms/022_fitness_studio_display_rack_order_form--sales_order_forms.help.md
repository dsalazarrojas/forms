# Fitness Studio Display Rack Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Studio Display Rack Order Form is a tool for fitness studios to submit information about their products for display in a rack system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of your gym in the **Gym Name** field.
2. Provide details about the delivery, such as any specific instructions or notes, in the **Delivery Details** field.
3. Select a date for the preferred display in the **Preferred Date** field.
4. Enter any additional order details, such as quantity or product specifics, in the **Order Details** field.
5. Specify the delivery location for the products in the **Delivery Location** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Gym Name (text)**: Enter the name of your fitness studio.
* **Delivery Details (text)**: Provide any notes or instructions about the delivery.
* **Preferred Date (date)**: Select a date for when you would like the products to be displayed.
* **Order Details (text)**: Enter any additional details about the order.
* **Delivery Location (text)**: Specify where the products should be delivered, such as a specific room or area in the studio.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and relevant information in each field to ensure efficient and accurate display of products.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact the support team at form@gic.mx.
