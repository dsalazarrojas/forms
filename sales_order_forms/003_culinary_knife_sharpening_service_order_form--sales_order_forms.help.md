# Culinary Knife Sharpening Service Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place an order for a culinary knife sharpening service. It collects information from customers about their knife sharpening needs, allowing the business to provide the service efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Customer Name and Email Address in the relevant fields.
2. Specify the number of knives you need sharpened using the Knife Quantity field.
3. Select the type of service you require using the Sharpening Service field.
4. Choose how frequently you want your knives sharpened using the Sharpening Frequency field.
5. Enter the date you are able to drop off your knives using the Drop Off Date field.
6. Select the date you want to pick up your sharpened knives using the Pick Up Date field.
7. Choose your preferred payment method using the Payment Method field.
8. Enter any additional information you would like to provide in the Customer Address field, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, not required): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed for communication and service purposes.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, not required): Enter your email address to enable communication about your order and any updates.
* **Knife Quantity** (`knife_quantity`, number, not required): Enter the number of knives you need sharpened. This will help us determine the total number of knives to sharpen.
* **Sharpening Service** (`sharpening_service`, select_one, not required): Choose the type of service you need:
	+ **Basic**: Suitable for occasional use.
	+ **Deluxe**: Suitable for frequent use.
	+ **Premium**: Suitable for high-frequency use.
* **Sharpening Frequency** (`sharpening_frequency`, select_multiple, not required): Choose how frequently you need your knives sharpened:
	+ **Daily**: For daily use.
	+ **Weekly**: For weekly use.
	+ **Monthly**: For monthly use.
* **Drop Off Date** (`drop_off_date`, date, not required): Enter the date when you are able to drop off your knives for sharpening.
* **Pick Up Date** (`pick_up_date`, date, not required): Enter the date when you would like to pick up your sharpened knives.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, not required): Choose your preferred payment method:
	+ **Credit Card**: Payment will be processed at the time of pickup.
	+ **Bank Transfer**: Payment will be processed prior to pickup.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, not required): Enter any additional information you would like to provide, such as your address or any specific instructions.

Note: All fields are not required, but providing accurate information in all fields will help the business provide the best possible service.
