# Used Car Sales Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for used car sales transactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the vehicle make, model, year, mileage, and price of the vehicle sold.
2. Enter the sale date and time of the vehicle.
3. Select the sale type (New or Used).
4. Choose the payment method (Cash, Credit, or Other).
5. Enter the customer's information, including their name, contact number, email, and address.
6. Select the customer's country, state, and city.
7. Enter the customer's postal code.
8. Note any sale notes or comments.
9. Enter any customer notes or comments.
10. Specify the payment terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, `text`, required): Enter the manufacturer of the vehicle sold.
- **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, `text`, required): Enter the model of the vehicle sold.
- **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, `number`, required): Enter the year of the vehicle sold.
- **Vehicle Mileage** (`vehicle_mileage`, `number`, required): Enter the mileage of the vehicle sold.
- **Sale Price** (`sale_price`, `number`, required): Enter the price at which the vehicle was sold.
- **Sale Date** (`sale_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the sale.
- **Sales Person** (`sales_person`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the sales representative who sold the vehicle.
- **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's name.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's contact number.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the customer's email.
- **VIN** (`vin`, `text`, required): Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) of the vehicle sold.
- **Sale Type** (`sale_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of sale (New or Used).
- **Vehicle Condition** (`vehicle_condition`, `select_one`, required): Select the condition of the vehicle (New or Used).
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select the payment method (Cash, Credit, or Other).
- **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's address.
- **Sale Location** (`sale_location`, `text`, required): Enter the sale location.
- **Customer City** (`customer_city`, `text`, required): Enter the city where the customer resides.
- **Customer State** (`customer_state`, `text`, required): Enter the state where the customer resides.
- **Customer Postal Code** (`customer_postal_code`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's postal code.
- **Customer Country** (`customer_country`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's country.
- **Sale Notes** (`sale_notes`, `note`, required): Enter any notes or comments about the sale.
- **Customer Note** (`customer_note`, `note`, required): Enter any notes or comments from the customer.
- **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `text`, required): Specify the payment terms.
- **Sale Date Time** (`sale_date_time`, `time`, required): Enter the date and time of the sale.
- **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of vehicle sold (Car, Truck, Motorcycle, Boat, or RV).

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check the accuracy of the entered information to avoid any discrepancies.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your sales representative for assistance.
