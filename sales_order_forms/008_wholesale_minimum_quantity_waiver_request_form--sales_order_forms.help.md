# Wholesale Minimum Quantity Waiver Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Wholesale Minimum Quantity Waiver Request Form is used to request a waiver for the minimum quantity requirement for a product. This form is typically used when a business needs to order a lower quantity of a product, and they need to provide a valid reason for the waiver.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the Product SKU of the product for which you are requesting the waiver.
2. Enter the quantity you would like to order.
3. Provide a brief reason for why you need the minimum quantity waiver.
4. Select the type of waiver you are requesting from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product SKU (Text)**: Enter the unique identifier for the product you are requesting the waiver for.
* **Quantity (Number)**: Enter the quantity you would like to order.
* **Reason (Text)**: Provide a brief explanation for why you need the minimum quantity waiver. This will help us understand your request.
* **Waiver Type (Select One)**: Choose one of the waiver types from the options provided. This will determine the type of waiver you are requesting.
