# Heat Recovery Equipment Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for sales orders of heat recovery equipment. It's essential to complete this form accurately to ensure the correct equipment is ordered and delivered to the customer's site.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling in the **Delivery Details** page (page 1), which includes general information about the order.
- Move on to the **delivery_info** page (page 2), where you can add any specific details related to the delivery of the equipment.
- The **product_selections** page (page 3) is not applicable for this form.
- The **project_notes** page (page 4) allows you to add any notes or comments related to the project.
- On the **delivery_method** page (page 5), choose between 'Yes' or 'No' regarding whether delivery method needs to be selected.
- Fill in the **delivery_date** page (page 6) with a specific date for the delivery.
- Enter the specific time for the delivery on **delivery_time** page (page 7).
- The **delivery_location** page (page 8) is used to capture the location of the delivery.
- On **Contact Info** page (page 9), you can enter the contact information related to the project.
- Fill in the **Contact Name** field (page 10) with the name of the contact person.
- Enter the **Contact Email** (page 11) of the contact person.
- Enter the contact **phone** (page 12) number.
- Enter the **Project Manager** (page 13) who will be managing the project.
- Finally, enter the **Project Address** (page 14) where the project is located.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Delivery Details** (page 1): A brief summary of the order.
* **delivery_info** (page 2): Additional details related to the delivery of the equipment.
* **product_selections** (page 3): Not applicable for this form.
* **project_notes** (page 4): Any notes or comments related to the project.
* **delivery_method** (page 5): Whether delivery method needs to be selected.
* **delivery_date** (page 6): A specific date for the delivery.
* **delivery_time** (page 7): A specific time for the delivery.
* **delivery_location** (page 8): The location of the delivery.
* **Contact Info** (page 9): Contact information related to the project.
* **Contact Name** (page 10): Name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (page 11): Email of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (page 12): Phone number of the contact person.
* **Project Manager** (page 13): Manager of the project.
* **Project Address** (page 14): Address of the project location.
