# HOA Fee Reduction Support Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from homeowners about their needs and circumstances related to HOA fees, with the goal of identifying areas where fee reductions can be applied to better support them.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Select "HOA Dues"**: Choose one or more of the options that apply to your situation:
	* Yes (if applicable)
	* No (if not applicable)
2. **Indicate "Service Value"**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you believe the services provided by the HOA are valuable to you:
	* Yes (if you believe the services are valuable)
	* No (if you don't believe the services are valuable)
3. **Explain "Fee Reduction Needs"**: Provide a brief description of your specific needs and circumstances related to fee reduction:
	* A short text field where you can describe your needs and circumstances
4. **Specify "Fee Reduction Amount"**: Enter a numeric value for the amount of fee reduction you are seeking:
	* A numerical field where you can enter the amount of reduction you need
5. **Indicate "Fee Reduction Frequency"**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you would like to apply for a fee reduction on a regular basis:
	* Yes (if you would like to apply regularly)
	* No (if you would like to apply one-time only)
6. **List "HOA Fees"**: Enter a list of specific fees you are currently paying:
	* A text field where you can list your fees
7. **List "HOA Services"**: Enter a list of services you are currently receiving:
	* A text field where you can list your services
8. **Respond to "HOA Board Response"**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you would like the HOA board to contact you:
	* Yes (if you would like to be contacted)
	* No (if you don't want to be contacted)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **HOA Dues**: This section asks you to select one or more options that apply to your situation regarding HOA dues. Please choose the options that are relevant to your circumstances.
* **Service Value**: This section asks you to select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you believe the services provided by the HOA are valuable to you.
* **Fee Reduction Needs**: This section is a text field where you can explain your specific needs and circumstances related to fee reduction.
* **Fee Reduction Amount**: This section asks you to enter a numeric value for the amount of fee reduction you are seeking.
* **Fee Reduction Frequency**: This section asks you to select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you would like to apply for a fee reduction on a regular basis.
* **HOA Fees**: This section is a text field where you can list the specific fees you are currently paying.
* **HOA Services**: This section is a text field where you can list the services you are currently receiving.
* **HOA Board Response**: This section asks you to select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you would like the HOA board to contact you.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have a clear understanding of your needs and circumstances before filling out this form.
* If you have already submitted this form, please do not resubmit it unless your circumstances have changed.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the HOA administration for assistance.
