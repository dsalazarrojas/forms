# residential_building_management_feedback_poll_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and ratings from residents on various aspects of their residential building management experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the pages you want to complete by checking the boxes next to each page title.
2. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (*) and provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
3. For select fields, choose the relevant option that best answers your question.
4. Ensure that you have answered all required fields before submitting the form.
