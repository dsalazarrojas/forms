# HOA Records Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The HOA Records Request Form is a document used to request various records from the Homeowners Association (HOA). This form is designed to gather the necessary information to efficiently process your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form with the required information.
2.  Select the records you are requesting.
3.  Sign and date the form, as applicable.
4.  Submit the form to the HOA.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Homeowner Request** (`homeowmer_request`): This field is a text field where you can enter your request.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`): Enter the date you are requesting the records.
* **Request Time** (`request_time`): Enter the time you are requesting the records.
* **Request Note** (`request_note`): This field is a text area where you can provide additional information about your request.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`): Enter your address.
* **City** (`city`): Enter the city where you are located.
* **State** (`state`): Select 'Yes' if you are requesting records from the HOA.
* **Zip** (`zip`): Enter your zip code.
* **Records Requested** (`records_requested`): Select the type of records you are requesting (e.g. HOA meeting minutes, financial records, bylaws, etc.).
* **Other Records** (`other_records`): If you selected 'Other' in the previous field, enter the type of records you are requesting.
* **Requester** (`requester`): Enter your name.
* **Requester Type** (`requester_type`): Select the type of person requesting the records (e.g. Homeowner, Real estate agent, Lender, etc.).
* **Agreement** (`agreement`): Select 'True' if you agree to sign an agreement.
* **Signature** (`signature`): Select 'Signature' if you agree to sign an agreement.
* **Agreement Signature Date** (`agreement_signature_date`): Enter the date you sign the agreement.
* **Agreement Signature** (`agreement_signature`): Enter your signature.
* **Requester Signature** (`requester_signature`): Enter your signature.
* **Requester Signature Date** (`requester_signature_date`): Enter the date you sign the agreement.
* **Requester Note** (`requester_note`): This field is a text area where you can provide additional information about your request.
* **Requester Agreement** (`requester_agreement`): Select 'True' if you agree to sign an agreement.
* **Requester Agreement Signature** (`requester_agreement_signature`): Select 'Signature' if you agree to sign an agreement.
* **Requester Agreement Signature Date** (`requester_agreement_signature_date`): Enter the date you sign the agreement.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you are requesting records for a valid purpose.
* If you are requesting records for a friend or family member, ensure they have given you authority to request on their behalf.
* If you are requesting records for a third party (e.g. a business), provide the necessary documentation to support your request.
