# HOA Community Improvement Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HOA Community Improvement Voting Form is a way for homeowners to provide suggestions and feedback on potential community improvements. This form helps the HOA Management identify areas of need and make informed decisions about which projects to undertake.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter a unique project ID (if you already know it).
2. Enter the project name in the Project Name field.
3. Provide a brief description of the project in the Project Description field.
4. Enter your name as it appears on your resident card or a commonly used name by neighbors.
5. Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates.
6. Enter your phone number, but this is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Id** (`project_id`, number, required: false): Enter a unique project ID, if you already have one. If not, you can leave this field blank.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the project you'd like to suggest.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, note, required: false): Provide a brief description of the project, explaining why it's beneficial for the community.
* **Resident Name** (`resident_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your resident card or a commonly used name by neighbors.
* **Resident Email** (`resident_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates and progress on the project.
* **Resident Phone** (`resident_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number, but this is optional.

## Tips
* Please fill out this form only once per project.
* Make sure to provide a clear and concise project description.
* If you have any further questions or need clarification, please contact your HOA Management team.
