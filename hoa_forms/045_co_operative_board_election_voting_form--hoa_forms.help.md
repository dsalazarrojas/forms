# Co-Operative Board Election Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Co-Operative Board Election Voting Form is a secure and confidential ballot system designed to allow co-operative members to cast their votes for the Board of Directors and weigh in on special resolutions. This guide will walk you through each section of the form to ensure you complete it accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Member Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your membership documents. This is a required field to ensure your identity is verified.
2. **Membership ID Number**: Enter your membership ID number as it appears on your membership documents. This is a required field to ensure your identity is verified.
3. **Property Address or Unit Number**: Enter your property address or unit number as it appears on your records with the co-operative. This is a required field to ensure your eligibility to vote.
4. **-- Voter Verification --**: This section is for verification purposes only and is not editable. Please do not fill out any fields here.
5. **I certify that I am a member in good standing of the co-operative**: Select one of the two options:
	* 'True': Confirm your membership status.
	* I certify: Provide additional comments or context if necessary.
6. **-- Board Election Ballot --**: This section is for informational purposes only and is not editable. Please do not fill out any fields here.
7. **Select your candidates for Board of Directors - Choose up to 3**:
	* Select up to three candidates from the list provided. You may choose more than one candidate but must choose at most three.
8. **-- Special Resolutions --**: This section is for informational purposes only and is not editable. Please do not fill out any fields here.
9. **Resolution 1 - Annual Operating Budget Approval**: Select one of the three options:
	* Approve
	* Reject
	* Abstain
10. **Resolution 2 - Proposed Amendment to Co-operative Bylaws**: Select one of the three options:
	* Approve
	* Reject
	* Abstain
11. **-- Feedback --**: This section is optional and allows you to provide comments on board candidates and current leadership.
12. **Any comments for the board candidates or current leadership?**: Enter any comments or feedback you have for the board candidates or current leadership.
13. **-- Authentication --**: This section is for informational purposes only and is not editable. Please do not fill out any fields here.
14. **Digital Signature for ballot verification**: Enter your digital signature for ballot verification. This is a required field to ensure the integrity of the voting process.
15. **Date of Vote**: Select the date you are casting your vote. This is a required field to ensure accurate tracking of votes.

**Tips**

* Make sure to fill out each required field accurately and completely.
* Select up to three candidates for the Board of Directors.
* Choose one of the three options for each resolution.
* Use this opportunity to provide feedback on board candidates and current leadership.
* Enter your digital signature and date of vote to ensure your ballot is verified.
