# Residential Community Rules Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about an individual's emergency contact information in a residential community setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and click submit. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information about your emergency contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): This field is for your first name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): This field is for your email address.
* **Property Owner** (`property_owner`, text, required): This field is for the owner of the property.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, text, required): This field is for the type of property you own.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required): This field is for your property address.
* **Unit Number** (`unit_number`, text, required): This field is for the unit number of your property.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact_person`, text, required): This field is for the emergency contact person's name.
* **Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): This field is for the emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, required): This field is for the emergency contact person's email address.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, required): This field is for the relationship between you and your emergency contact person. Please select one of the provided options (Emergency Contact Person, Family Member, Friend, Neighbor, Other).
* **Alternate Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_alternate_phone`, text, required): This field is for the alternate phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Alternate Email** (`emergency_contact_alternate_email`, email, required): This field is for the alternate email address of your emergency contact person.
* **Alternate Relationship** (`emergency_contact_alternate_relationship`, select_one, required): This field is for the alternate relationship between you and your emergency contact person. Please select one of the provided options (Emergency Contact Person, Family Member, Friend, Neighbor, Other).
* **Alternate Relationship (specify)** (`emergency_contact_alternate_relationship_other`, text, required): This field is for specifying the alternate relationship if "Other" is selected.
* **Message** (`emergency_contact_message`, text, required): This field is for leaving a message for your emergency contact person.
* **To** (`emergency_contact_message_to`, text, required): This field is for specifying who to send the message to.
* **From** (`emergency_contact_message_from`, text, required): This field is for specifying who the message is from.
