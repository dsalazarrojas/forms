# Bike Shed Planning Permission Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Bike Shed Planning Permission Application form is designed to gather essential information for a bike shed planning permission. This form collects applicant details, bike shed specifics, and supporting documentation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information ready.
2. Select the correct answers for each field.
3. Review your submissions for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant name** (`applicant_name`, `text`, required):
    Enter your full name as the applicant or the company applying for the bike shed planning permission.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required):
    Enter the email address you'd like to use for communication related to this application.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional):
    If you prefer to be contacted over the phone, enter your phone number here.
* **Property address** (`property_address`, `text`, required):
    Enter the address where the bike shed will be located.
* **Proposed dimensions** (`proposed_dimensions`, `text`, required):
    Enter the length, width, and height of the bike shed in meters.
* **Proposed materials** (`proposed_materials`, `text`, required):
    Describe the primary materials planned for the bike shed construction.
* **Intended use** (`intended_use`, `select_one`, required):
    Choose from the options: 'Private storage', 'Communal storage', 'Repair station', or 'Commercial use'.
* **Neighbour notifications** (`neighbour_notifications`, `select_one`, optional):
    Indicate whether neighbours have been informed about the bike shed.
* **Supporting documents list** (`supporting_documents`, `text`, optional):
    List any attached plans or photos that support your application.
* **Declaration name** (`declaration_signature`, `text`, required):
    Type your full name to confirm your submission.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in to avoid submission errors.
* Double-check your submissions for accuracy.
* Attach any necessary supporting documents to support your application.
* If you need help with a specific field or have questions, refer to the system's general help documentation.
