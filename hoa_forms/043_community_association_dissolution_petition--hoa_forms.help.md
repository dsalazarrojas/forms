# Community Association Dissolution Petition - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from a community association's board of directors or a member of the association when requesting dissolution of the association.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the appropriate category of association you are filing for.
2.  Enter the details about the association you are filing for, including its name, address, and incorporation date.
3.  Provide information about the primary petitioner, including their name, address, email, and phone number.
4.  Specify your ownership status within the association.
5.  Indicate how long you have been a member of the association.
6.  State the primary reason for dissolution and provide a detailed explanation for the reasons.
7.  Indicate whether you have supporting documentation for the dissolution request.
8.  If applicable, provide details about member support for the dissolution request.
9.  Indicate the total number of members and estimated percentage of members supporting the petition.
10. Specify how association assets should be handled in case of dissolution.
11. Confirm whether there are outstanding debts or obligations.
12. Confirm that the legal requirements for dissolution have been reviewed.
13. Provide a proposed timeline for the dissolution process.
14. Certify that the information provided is accurate and authorized.
15. Sign and date the form to confirm.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Petition Information**: Enter a brief description of the petition, including its purpose and context.
* **Petition Date**: Enter the date of the petition in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Petition ID**: Enter the unique reference number for the petition.
* **Association Information**: Enter a brief description of the association, including its purpose and goals.
* **Association Name**: Enter the full legal name of the association.
* **Association Address**: Enter the primary address of the association.
* **Association Type**: Select the type of association this is (e.g. Homeowners Association (HOA), Condominium Association, etc.).
* **Incorporation Date**: Enter the date the association was incorporated.
* **Petitioner Information**: Enter a brief description of the primary petitioner, including their role in the association.
* **Petitioner Name**: Enter the full name of the primary petitioner.
* **Petitioner Email**: Enter the contact email address of the primary petitioner.
* **Petitioner Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the primary petitioner.
* **Petitioner Address**: Enter the address of the primary petitioner.
* **Ownership Status**: Select your ownership status in the association (e.g. Homeowner, Condo Owner, Property Owner, Authorized Representative).
* **Years as Member**: Select how long you have been a member of the association.
* **Dissolution Reasons**: Enter a brief description of the reasons for dissolution.
* **Primary Reason**: Select the primary reason for dissolution (e.g. Financial Issues, Lack of Participation, etc.).
* **Detailed Reasons**: Enter a detailed explanation for the reasons for dissolution.
* **Supporting Documentation**: Indicate whether you have supporting documentation for the dissolution request.
* **Member Support**: Enter information about member support for the dissolution request.
* **Total Members**: Enter an approximate total number of members.
* **Supporting Signatures**: Enter the number of members supporting the petition.
* **Percentage Support**: Select the estimated percentage of members supporting the petition.
* **Asset Distribution**: Enter how association assets should be handled in case of dissolution.
* **Debt Obligations**: Indicate whether there are outstanding debts or obligations.
* **Legal Compliance**: Indicate whether the legal requirements for dissolution have been reviewed.
* **Proposed Timeline**: Enter a proposed timeline for the dissolution process.
* **Petitioner Signature**: Certify that the information provided is accurate and authorized.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date of the signature.

## Tips

*   This form should only be completed by authorized individuals.
*   Ensure you have all necessary information before completing this form.
*   Review the form carefully to ensure accuracy.
*   Consult with a legal expert before proceeding with the dissolution process.
*   Keep supporting documentation and other relevant records in case of future reference.
