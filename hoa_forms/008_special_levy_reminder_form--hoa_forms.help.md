# Special Levy Reminder Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Special Levy Reminder Form is designed to collect information from individuals or businesses regarding a special levy reminder. This form helps accounting or legal professionals to track outstanding payments, update the status of levies, and maintain accurate records.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information, which includes:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Address
	* Phone
	* Email
	* Acknowledgement Date (the date the levy was acknowledged)
	* Acknowledgement Time (the time the levy was acknowledged)
	* Status (e.g., Paid, Partially Paid, Unpaid)
	* Notes (any additional comments or information)
	* Levy Notice Date (the date the levy notice was sent)
2. Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and honestly, as the form will be used for official records and communication.
3. Submit the form once all information is filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (First Name)** (`name: first_name`, `type: text`, required: false): This field is used to collect the individual's or business' first name.
* **Last Name (Last Name)** (`name: last_name`, `type: text`, required: false): This field is used to collect the individual's or business' last name.
* **Address (Address)** (`name: address`, `type: text`, required: false): This field is used to collect the individual's or business' address.
* **Phone (Phone)** (`name: phone`, `type: text`, required: false): This field is used to collect the individual's or business' phone number.
* **Email (Email)** (`name: email`, `type: text`, required: false): This field is used to collect the individual's or business' email address.
* **Acknowledgement Date (Acknowledgement Date)** (`name: acknowledgement_date`, `type: date`, required: false): This field is used to collect the date the levy was acknowledged.
* **Acknowledgement Time (Acknowledgement Time)** (`name: acknowledgement_time`, `type: time`, required: false): This field is used to collect the time the levy was acknowledged.
* **Status (Status)** (`name: status`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false): This field is used to select the status of the levy (e.g., Paid, Partially Paid, Unpaid).
* **Notes (Notes)** (`name: notes`, `type: text`, required: false): This field is used to collect any additional comments or information related to the levy.
* **Levy Notice Date (Levy Notice Date)** (`name: levy_notice_date`, `type: date`, required: false): This field is used to collect the date the levy notice was sent.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and honestly.
* Use this form for official reminders and records only.
* If a field is not relevant to your situation, it's okay to leave it blank.
* You can modify the form as needed to suit your specific use case.
