# Bylaw Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bylaw Complaint Form is designed to report alleged violations of the Homeowners Association (HOA) regulations. It is crucial to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to enable the HOA management to take swift and appropriate action.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Bylaw Complaint Form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your information as the complainant.
2.  Enter your full name and address as the contact person.
3.  Ensure you have a working phone number for follow-up contact.
4.  Describe the incident, including the category of violation, the date and time of occurrence (if applicable), and provide a detailed description of the issue.
5.  Indicate whether you have evidence (photos or videos) supporting your complaint and whether you have previously contacted the resident or property owner.
6.  Select your preferred method for further follow-up communication from the HOA.
7.  Skip the "Official Use Only" section, as it is not intended for completion by the public.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Your Full Name (`reporter_name`)** (`text`, required): Your full name as the contact person for communication and follow-up.
*   **Your Property Address (`reporter_address`)** (`text`, required): Your property address to be associated with the complaint, helping the HOA locate the issue.
*   **Contact Phone Number (`reporter_phone`)** (`text`, required): A phone number where you can be reached for further communication or follow-up.
*   **Address of the Alleged Violation (`violation_address`)** (`text`, required): The address where the violation occurred, which is necessary for the HOA to locate and address the issue effectively.
*   **Category of Violation (`category_of_violation`)** (`select_one`, required): Select the type of violation you are reporting, which helps the HOA direct their investigation and resolution efforts.
*   **Date of Occurrence (`date_of_occurrence`)** (`date`, required): Provide the date when the violation was first observed.
*   **Time of Occurrence (`time_of_occurrence`)** (`time`, optional): If applicable, provide the time of the incident for more accurate reporting.
*   **Is this an ongoing issue (`is_ongoing`)** (`select_one`, required): Indicate whether the issue you are reporting is still ongoing.
*   **Detailed Description (`detailed_description`)** (`text`, required): Provide a detailed description of the issue, including what you observed and why you believe it constitutes a violation.
*   **Evidence Availability (`evidence_available`)** (`select_one`, required): Indicate whether you have photographic or video evidence supporting your complaint.
*   **Previous Contact (`previous_contact`)** (`select_one`, required): If you have contacted the resident or property owner, select "True" and provide details on the outcome of that contact, if you have the opportunity to do so.
*   **Outcome of Previous Contact (`contact_outcome`)** (`text`, optional): If you have previously contacted the resident or property owner, describe the outcome of that interaction.
*   **Preferred Follow-up Method (`follow_up_preference`)** (`select_one`, required): Select your preferred method for the HOA's follow-up communication regarding your complaint.
*   **Official Use Only** (`note`, not intended to be completed by the public): This section is strictly for official use and should not be filled by the public.
