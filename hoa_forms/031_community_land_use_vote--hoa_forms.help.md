# Community Land Use Vote - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for collecting votes from community members on land use decisions. It is designed to gather information from eligible residents or property owners.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Voter name** in the first field. This is your full name for verification purposes.
2. Enter the address of the property you are voting on in the **Property address** field. This ensures that you are eligible to vote on this land use decision.
3. If you wish to receive confirmation of your vote, you can enter your email address in the **Email address** field.
4. Select your **Vote choice** from the options: Approve, Reject, or Abstain.
5. If you wish to provide a reason for your vote, you can enter it in the **Reason for vote** field.
6. Confirm your **Eligibility to vote** by selecting Confirm.
7. If you are voting by proxy, select **True** in the **Voting by proxy** field and enter the name of the proxy voter in the **Proxy name** field.
8. Optionally, you can provide additional comments in the **Additional comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Voter name** (text, required): Enter your full name for verification purposes.
* **Property address** (text, required): Enter the address of the property you are voting on to confirm your eligibility.
* **Email address** (email, optional): Enter your email address if you wish to receive confirmation of your vote.
* **Vote choice** (select_one, required): Select Approve, Reject, or Abstain to indicate your position on the land use decision.
* **Reason for vote** (text, optional): Provide a brief explanation of your vote.
* **Eligibility to vote** (select_one, required): Confirm your eligibility to vote by selecting Confirm.
* **Voting by proxy** (select_one, optional): Select True if you are voting by proxy, False otherwise.
* **Proxy name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the proxy voter if you are voting by proxy.
* **Additional comments** (text, optional): Provide any additional notes or comments for the council.
