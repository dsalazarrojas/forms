# Community Condition Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Condition Report form is used to collect and track reports of community conditions that require attention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the required fields: Reporter name, Report date, Location of issue, Condition type, Severity level, and Description of condition.
2. Optionally, you can upload a photo and provide a recommended action.
3. Make sure to provide as much detail as possible in the "Description of condition" field to help community managers understand the issue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reporter name** (`reporter_name`, text, Optional): This is the name of the person submitting the report. You can leave this field blank if you wish to submit the report anonymously.
* **Report date** (`report_date`, date, Required): The date you observed the issue.
* **Location of issue** (`location`, text, Required): Provide the address or a detailed description of the location of the issue.
* **Condition type** (`condition_type`, select_one, Required): Choose the type of issue from the list:
	+ Trash or debris
	+ Vandalism
	+ Road or sidewalk damage
	+ Lighting issues
	+ Landscaping neglect
* **Severity level** (`severity`, select_one, Required): Estimate the urgency of the issue using the following scale:
	+ Low
	+ Medium
	+ High
* **Description of condition** (`description`, text, Required): Provide a detailed description of the issue, including any supporting evidence you have.
* **Photo attached** (`photo_attached`, select_one, Optional): Check this box if you have attached a photo of the issue.
* **Recommended action** (`recommended_action`, text, Optional): Suggest specific steps that can be taken to resolve the issue.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible in the "Description of condition" field to help community managers understand the issue.
* If the issue has already been reported, please do not resubmit. Instead, contact community management directly.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to community management for assistance.
