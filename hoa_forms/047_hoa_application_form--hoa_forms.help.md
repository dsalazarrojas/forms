# HOA Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HOA Application Form is designed to collect information about the applicant and their contact details for HOA processing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your contact details, including email and phone number.
3. Review the information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Information (1)** (`applicant_info`, text, optional): Enter your name, address, and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Contact Details (2)** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter your email address and phone number to help the HOA team contact you.
* **HOA Info (3)** (`hoa_info`, text, optional): Enter any relevant information about the HOA you are applying to.
* **Goals and Objectives (4)** (`goals_and_objectives`, text, optional): Describe your goals and objectives for applying to the HOA.
* **Payment Info (5)** (`payment_info`, text, optional): Enter any information about payment methods or requirements.
* **Additional Documents (6)** (`additional_documents`, text, optional): Upload any additional documents required by the HOA.
* **Payment Method (7)** (`payment_method`, text, optional): Specify the payment method you will use.
* **Review (8)** (`review`, text, optional): Review your information before submitting.
* **Submit (9)** (`submit`, text, optional): Submit the form to complete the application process.
* **Email (10)** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to receive communications from the HOA.
* **Phone (11)** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for the HOA to contact you.

Note: Fields with "text" type can be free-formatted.
