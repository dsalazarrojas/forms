# Resident Rules Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Resident Rules Acknowledgment Form" is a document used to verify that a resident has acknowledged and understood the rules and regulations of their property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and understand the rules and regulations.
2. Enter your name and property address in the relevant fields.
3. Select the type of property and agreement term from the available options.
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the comments field.
5. Sign the form with your signature and date.
6. Have the manager sign the form with their signature and date.
7. Review and submit the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Introduction
* This is an introduction to the form and provides the resident with an overview of the purpose and importance of completing this form.

### Acknowledgment Date
* This field is used to record the date the resident acknowledged and understood the rules and regulations.
* Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
