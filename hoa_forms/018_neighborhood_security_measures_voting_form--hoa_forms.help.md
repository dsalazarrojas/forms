# Neighborhood Security Measures Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on the security and safety measures present in a neighborhood. The data collected will be used to identify areas that require improvement and provide valuable insights for decision-making purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the options provided in each field carefully and select the relevant information for your neighborhood.
2. For the "Security Measures" and "Safety Measures" fields, choose multiple options that apply to your neighborhood.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Provide any additional comments or observations in the "Comments" field.
6. Select the date of your submission in the "Date" field.
7. Choose the time of your submission in the "Time" field.
8. Select the address of the neighborhood you are reporting on from the dropdown list.
9. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
10. Click "Submit" to submit your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Security Measures** (`security_measures`, select_multiple, required): This field allows you to select multiple security measures that are currently present in your neighborhood, such as motion lights, security cameras, and alarm systems. Please choose all that apply.
* **Safety Measures** (`safety_measures`, select_multiple, required): This field allows you to select multiple safety measures that are currently present in your neighborhood, such as streetlights, emergency response systems, and safety signs. Please choose all that apply.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address to be contacted for further information or clarification.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number to be contacted for further information or clarification.
* **Comments** (`comment`, note, required): Provide any additional comments or observations about your neighborhood's security and safety measures. This is a free-text field, so feel free to write as much or as little as you want.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select the date of your submission. This will be used to track when the information was collected.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Choose the time of your submission. This will be used to track when the information was collected.
* **Address** (`address`, select_one, required): Select the address of the neighborhood you are reporting on from the dropdown list. Please choose the correct address from the options provided.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click "Submit" to submit your response.
