# hoadomain_proxy_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The `hoadomain_proxy_form` is designed to capture information about an event or scenario. It serves as a proxy for other systems or stakeholders to act upon.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: **Event**, **name**, **address**, **phone**. These fields are marked as "required" and are essential for providing a complete description of the event.
2. Provide a brief description of the event in the **Event** field.
3. Enter your name in the **name** field.
4. Type in your address in the **address** field.
5. Enter a short reason or motivation for the form submission in the **why** field.
6. If you have a phone number, enter it in the **phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Event** (Event, text, required)
Provide a brief description of the event or scenario you want to report or inquire about.

### 2. **name** (name, text, required)
Enter your name to be associated with this form submission.

### 3. **address** (address, text, required)
Type in your address to help with any follow-up communication or action related to this form submission.

### 4. **why** (why, text, optional)
Give a short reason or motivation for this form submission.

### 5. **phone** (phone, text, optional)
If you have a phone number, enter it in case any follow-up communication or action requires direct contact.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and sufficient information in each field to ensure that the form is processed correctly.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or support.
* This form is designed to be a proxy for other systems or stakeholders, so please be concise and clear in your input.
