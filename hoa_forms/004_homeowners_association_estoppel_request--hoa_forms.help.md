# Homeowners Association Estoppel Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Homeowners Association Estoppel Request form is used to obtain information from homeowners regarding their estoppel type, name, address, phone number, email, authorization dates, delivery preferences, and additional notes. This form is likely used for property management or legal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the correct Estoppel Type from the dropdown menu.
2.  Enter your name.
3.  Enter your address.
4.  Enter your phone number.
5.  Enter your email address.
6.  Select your preferred delivery methods (Mail, Email, Fax, In-Person).
7.  Add any additional notes you'd like to include.
8.  Enter the date you signed the authorization.
9.  Enter the time you signed the authorization.
10.  If applicable, enter the date the buyer signed the authorization and the date the property manager signed the authorization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Estoppel Type** (hoas_estoppel_type, select_one, required: false): Select the type of estoppel request you are submitting (Homeowners Association Estoppel Request, Homeowners Association Estoppel Response, or Homeowners Association Estoppel Certificate).
* **Name** (hoas_name, text, required: false): Enter your name, which will be associated with this estoppel request.
* **Address** (hoas_address, text, required: false): Enter your address, which will be associated with this estoppel request.
* **Phone Number** (hoas_phone, text, required: false): Enter your phone number, which will be used for contact purposes.
* **Email** (hoas_email, email, required: false): Enter your email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
* **Signed Authorization Date** (hoas_signed_auth_date, date, required: false): Enter the date you signed the authorization.
* **Signed Authorization Time** (hoas_signed_auth_time, time, required: false): Enter the time you signed the authorization.
* **Delivery Preferences** (hoas_delivery_preferences, select_multiple, required: false): Select how you would like to receive the response (Mail, Email, Fax, In-Person).
* **Notes** (hoas_notes, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about your estoppel request.
* **Buyer Signed Authorization Date** (hoas_buyer_signed_auth_date, date, required: false): If applicable, enter the date the buyer signed the authorization.
* **Property Manager Signed Authorization Date** (hoas_property_manager_signed_auth_date, date, required: false): If applicable, enter the date the property manager signed the authorization.

## Tips
Please ensure that all information provided is accurate and up-to-date. If you need to submit a duplicate request, please do not complete this form again. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities or your property manager.
