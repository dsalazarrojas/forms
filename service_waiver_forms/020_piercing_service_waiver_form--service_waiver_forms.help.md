# Piercing Service Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Piercing Service Waiver Form is used to obtain a client's consent for piercing services, acknowledge the risks involved, and provide necessary contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Client Consent**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your consent for piercing services. If you select 'No', please explain why you are not consenting.
2. **Risk Acknowledgement**: Please carefully read and acknowledge the risks involved with piercing services.
3. **Studio Name**: Enter the name of the studio where you wish to receive piercing services.
4. **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for any follow-up or emergency contact.
5. **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted for any follow-up or communication.
6. **Emergency Contact** (optional): If you have an emergency contact, please enter their name and contact information.
7. **Medical Notes** (optional): If you have any medical concerns or conditions that we should be aware of, please enter them here.
8. **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/1990).
9. **Consent Date**: Enter the date you are signing this form, in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/2022).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Consent**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your consent for piercing services. If you select 'No', please explain why you are not consenting.
* **Risk Acknowledgement**: Acknowledge the risks involved with piercing services. This is a required field and should be carefully read and understood.
* **Studio Name**: Enter the name of the studio where you wish to receive piercing services. This is the name of the studio where you will be receiving the service.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for any follow-up or emergency contact. This is the number we can use to contact you if needed.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted for any follow-up or communication. This is the email address we can use to contact you.
* **Emergency Contact** (optional): If you have an emergency contact, please enter their name and contact information. This is the person we can contact in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Notes** (optional): If you have any medical concerns or conditions that we should be aware of, please enter them here. This is an optional field, but please provide any necessary information about your medical history.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/1990). This is required information and is used to verify your identity.
* **Consent Date**: Enter the date you are signing this form, in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/2022). This is the date you are giving consent for piercing services.
