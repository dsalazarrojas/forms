# Furniture Moving Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Furniture Moving Liability Waiver" form is a document that clients must fill out before a furniture moving or storage service is provided. The form is used to ensure that clients are aware of potential risks and have agreed to the terms of service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you understand the questions and options.
2. Fill out any required fields with the requested information.
3. In the "Waiver" section, select one or more of the options that best represent your understanding of the risks and terms of service.
4. Sign and date the "Client Signature" field to confirm your agreement.
5. If you have any comments or questions, please fill out the "Client Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Move Details** (move_details, text, optional): Please enter a brief description of your move, including details such as the move location and any specific requirements or requests.
* **Move Date** (move_date, date, optional): Enter the date of your move.
* **Move Time** (move_time, time, optional): Enter the time of your move.
* **Client Name** (client_name, text, optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Phone Number** (phone_number, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Address** (address, text, optional): Enter your current address (or address of the move location).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (emergency_contact_name, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (emergency_contact_relationship, text, optional): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person (e.g., family member, neighbor, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Number** (emergency_contact_number, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact** fields are used to provide contact information for someone who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Signature** (signature, text, optional): Please sign and date this field to confirm that you have read and understand the terms of service.
* **Client Signature** (client_signature, text, optional): Please sign and date this field to confirm that you have read and understand the terms of service.
* **Waiver** (waiver, select_multiple, required): In this section, select one or more of the options that represent your understanding of the risks and terms of service:
	+ "I am aware that accidents can occur while moving or storing household goods."
	+ "I understand that accidents can happen while moving or storing household goods."
	+ "I have read, understand and agreed to the above terms."
	+ "I acknowledge that I have read, understand and agreed to the above terms."
* **Client Comments** (client_comments, text, optional): If you have any comments or questions, please use this field to provide additional information.
* **Staff Comments** (staff_comments, text, optional): Please use this field for staff or team members to provide additional information or notes about the client's request.
* **Staff Signature** (staff_signature, text, optional): This field is intended for staff or team members to sign and date to confirm that they have reviewed and agreed to the terms of service.
