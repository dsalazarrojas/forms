# Liability Waiver For Services - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect contact information, emergency contacts, consent, dates, and other relevant details from individuals or entities that wish to engage in services that require liability waivers. This information helps us manage risks and ensure compliance with relevant laws and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by providing your contact information on page 1, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. On page 2, provide the emergency contact information, including the name and phone number of an authorized emergency contact.
3. On page 3, indicate your consent to participate in the services. Please note that this consent is a mandatory step.
4. On page 4, enter the dates of the services you wish to engage in.
5. On page 5, sign the waiver to confirm your agreement.
6. On page 6, provide any medical certification or related information.
7. On page 7, report any medical history or conditions that may impact your participation in the services.
8. On page 8, provide the name and contact information of an authorized emergency medical authority.
9. On page 9, enter the name and contact number of an emergency contact.
10. On page 10, sign and date the waiver.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details**: (`contact_details`, `text`, required: `false`): Please provide your name, email address, and phone number to enable us to contact you regarding the services.
* **Emergency Contacts**: (`emergency_contacts`, `text`, required: `false`): Provide the name and phone number of an authorized emergency contact.
* **Consent**: (`consent`, `text`, required: `false`): Indicate your consent to participate in the services.
* **Dates**: (`dates`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the dates of the services you wish to engage in.
* **Signatures**: (`signatures`, `text`, required: `false`): Sign the waiver to confirm your agreement.
* **Medical Certification**: (`medical_certification`, `text`, required: `false`): Provide any medical certification or related information.
* **Medical History**: (`medical_history`, `text`, required: `false`): Report any medical history or conditions that may impact your participation in the services.
* **Emergency Medical Authority**: (`emergency_medical_authority`, `text`, required: `false`): Provide the name and contact information of an authorized emergency medical authority.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the name of an authorized emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Number**: (`emergency_contact_number`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the phone number of an authorized emergency contact.
* **Signature Date**: (`signature_date`, `date`, required: `false`): Sign and date the waiver to confirm your agreement.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
* If you need to fill out the form for multiple individuals or entities, repeat the process for each one, using unique contact information.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
