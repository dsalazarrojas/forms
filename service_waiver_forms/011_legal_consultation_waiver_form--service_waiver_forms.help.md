# Legal Consultation Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information for a legal consultation waiver, which is typically used to document the terms and conditions of a legal consultation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name in the **Client Name** field.
2. Enter the attorney's name or legal professional's name in the **Attorney Name** field.
3. Enter the client's address in the **Client Address** field.
4. Select the type of consultation from the **Consultation Type** dropdown menu.
5. Enter the legal professional's name in the **Legal Professional Name** field.
6. Enter the legal professional's contact information in the **Legal Professional Contact** field.
7. Enter the legal professional's address in the **Legal Professional Address** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`Client Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the individual or organization requesting the legal consultation.
* **Client Address** (`Client Address`, text, optional): Enter the address associated with the client, which may be their physical or mailing address.
* **Attorney Name** (`Attorney Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the attorney or legal professional representing the client.
* **Consultation Type** (`Consultation Type`, select one, optional): Select the type of consultation from the available options (consultation, review, appeal).
* **Legal Professional Name** (`Legal Professional Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the attorney or legal professional providing the consultation.
* **Legal Professional Contact** (`Legal Professional Contact`, text, optional): Enter the contact information for the legal professional, which may include phone number, email, or office address.
* **Legal Professional Address** (`Legal Professional Address`, text, optional): Enter the address associated with the legal professional, which may be their office or physical address.
