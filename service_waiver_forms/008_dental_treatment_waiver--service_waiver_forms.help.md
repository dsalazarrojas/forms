# dental_treatment_waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the waiver of dental treatment for patients who cannot receive treatment due to certain health conditions or medical emergencies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient information section with the patient's details.
2. Provide the medical practice information, including the practice name, phone number, email, address, and license number.
3. Sign and date the form as the doctor.
4. Ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **contact_info** (contact_info, text, optional): This field is used to capture any contact information about the patient.
* **medical_history** (medical_history, text, required): This field is used to document the patient's medical history, including any relevant medical conditions or allergies.
* **dental_history** (dental_history, text, optional): This field is used to document the patient's dental history.
* **treatment_details** (treatment_details, text, required): This field is used to document the details of the treatment that is being waived.
* **signature** (signature, text, optional): This field is used to capture the patient's signature.
* **patient_signature** (patient_signature, text, optional): This field is used to capture the doctor's signature.
* **doctor_signature** (doctor_signature, text, required): This field is used to capture the doctor's signature.
* **date** (date, date, optional): This field is used to capture the date of the waiver.
* **time** (time, time, optional): This field is used to capture the time of the waiver.
