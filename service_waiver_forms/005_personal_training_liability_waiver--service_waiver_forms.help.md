**Purpose:** This form is used to obtain a liability waiver from clients prior to participating in personal training services. The form helps protect the trainer and training facility from potential lawsuits by ensuring that clients understand and agree to the risks associated with physical activity and training. The waiver is typically signed on the first day of training and is a requirement for participation.

To ensure that you complete the form correctly, please only fill out the required fields (marked as "true" in this guide).

**How To Complete This Form:**

1.  Fill out all fields marked as "true" (required).
2.  Make sure to enter the correct contact information, including date of birth, in the required format.
3.  Describe your relationship to the client for emergency contact purposes.

**Field-by-Field Explanation:**

*   **Personal Training Liability Waiver** (`personal-training-liability-waiver`): A text box where you can enter any notes or comments related to the waiver.
*   **Client Name** (`client-name`): Enter the full name of the client.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact-email`): Enter the client's email address (if provided).
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact-phone`): Enter the client's phone number (if provided).
*   **Date of Birth** (`date-of-birth`): Enter the client's date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YY or MM/DD/YY).
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency-contact-name`): Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
*   **Relationship** (`relationship`): Describe your relationship to the client.
*   **Signed Waiver** (`signed-waiver`): Sign this waiver to acknowledge your understanding and agreement to the terms.
*   **Trainer Employed** (`employed-trainer`): Enter the name of your employer.
*   **Years of Experience** (`experience-years`): Enter the number of years you have been training.
*   **Trainer License Number** (`license-number`): Enter your license number.
*   **Trainer Certification** (`certification`): Enter your certification.
*   **Medical Clearance** (`medical-clearance`): Enter your medical clearance information.
*   **Emergency Medical Info** (`emergency-medical-info`): Enter any emergency medical information.
*   **Medical Procedure** (`medical-procedure`): Enter any medical procedures you may have.
*   **Medical Condition** (`medical-condition`): Enter any medical conditions you may have.
*   **Medical Insurance** (`medical-insurance`): Enter your medical insurance information.
*   **Emergency Contact Relationship Description** (`relationship-description`): Describe your relationship to the emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency-contact-address`): Enter the address of the emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact City** (`emergency-contact-city`): Enter the city of the emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact State** (`emergency-contact-state`): Enter the state of the emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Zip** (`emergency-contact-zip`): Enter the zip code of the emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Notes** (`emergency-notes`): Enter any additional notes about the emergency contact.
*   **Date** (`date`): Enter the date of the waiver.
