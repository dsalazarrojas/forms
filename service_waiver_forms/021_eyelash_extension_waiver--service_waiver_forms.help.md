# Eyelash Extension Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a service waiver for eyelash extensions. It's designed to gather information and collect a waiver of liability from clients or patients who have received eyelash extension services. The form is likely used to protect the service provider from potential legal issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the "Service Waiver" section with a clear and concise statement of your understanding of the service provided.
2.  Enter the name of the service provider (individual or entity) in the "Service Provider" field.
3.  Enter the start date of the service in the "Date Services Began" field.
4.  Enter the end date of the service in the "Date Services Ended" field.
5.  Provide a brief description of the service in the "Service Details" field.
6.  Read and acknowledge the "Liability Release" section carefully, making sure you understand the implications of waiving your rights.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Waiver** (`Service Waiver`, `text`, required/optional): A clear and concise statement of your understanding of the service provided.
* **Service Provider** (`Service Provider`, `text`, required/optional): The name of the individual or entity providing the service.
* **Date Services Began** (`Date Services Began`, `date`, required/optional): The start date of the service.
* **Date Services Ended** (`Date Services Ended`, `date`, required/optional): The end date of the service.
* **Service Details** (`Service Details`, `text`, required/optional): A brief description of the service performed.
* **Liability Release** (`Liability Release`, `text`, required/optional): A legal statement where you are waiving your rights to sue the service provider. Please read this section carefully before signing.
