# Camper Medical Administration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camper Medical Administration Form is used to collect medical information about each camper, including their personal details, medical history, and any necessary authorization and consent for emergency medical treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately, using the guidelines provided below for each section.
2. If a camper has no known medical conditions or allergies, you can leave the corresponding fields blank.
3. Make sure to sign and date the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter the camper's full name as it appears on their birth certificate or passport.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the camper's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Age**: Enter the camper's age as of the start date of the camp season.
* **Camp ID Number**: Enter the camper's ID number, if applicable.
* **Known Allergies**: List any known allergies, including medication and environmental allergies.
* **Allergy Severity Level**: Indicate the severity level of the allergy (Mild, Moderate, Severe, or Life-threatening).
* **Chronic Medical Conditions**: List any chronic medical conditions, such as asthma or diabetes.
* **Previous Surgeries or Hospitalizations**: List any previous surgeries or hospitalizations.
* **Current Medications**: List any current medications, including the name, dosage, and frequency.
* **Authorization to Administer Medications**: Select 'Yes, I authorize' to grant permission for camp staff to administer medications.
* **Primary Physician Name**: Enter the name of the camper's primary physician.
* **Physician Phone Number**: Enter the primary physician's phone number.
* **Physician Address**: Enter the primary physician's address.
* **Emergency Medical Contact**: Enter the name and contact information of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Health Insurance Provider**: Enter the name of the camper's health insurance provider.
* **Policy Number**: Enter the policy number for the camper's health insurance.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Special Food Needs**: List any dietary restrictions or special food needs.
* **Mobility Limitations or Physical Restrictions**: List any mobility limitations or physical restrictions.
* **Mental Health Conditions**: List any mental health conditions.
* **Consent to Emergency Medical Treatment**: Select 'Yes, I authorize' to grant permission for emergency medical treatment.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by the camper or their parent/guardian. If you are unsure about any of the information, please contact the camper's primary physician for guidance.
