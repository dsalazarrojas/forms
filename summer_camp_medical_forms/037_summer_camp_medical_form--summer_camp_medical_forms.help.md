# Summer Camp Medical Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Camp Medical Form is designed to gather important medical information about summer campers to ensure their health and safety during their stay at the camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and answer questions truthfully.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
3. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, **optional**): This field is for providing any previous medical conditions, surgeries, or allergies that may be relevant to your health and safety during the camp stay.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, **optional**): Select all the foods you are allergic to, such as Peanut, Shellfish, Beef, or Pork. This information will help us to take necessary precautions during meal times.
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, **optional**): List any medications you are currently taking, including dosage and frequency.
* **Vaccinations** (`vaccinations`, text, **optional**): Provide any information about vaccinations you have received, including dates and types of vaccinations.
 
## Tips
- If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
- If you have any medical conditions not listed here, please describe them in the "Medical History" field.
- Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy before submitting the form. 
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
