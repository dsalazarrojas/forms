# summer_camp_health_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect health information from campers to ensure their safety and well-being during the summer camp experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide accurate and honest information about the camper's medical conditions, medications, allergies, medical history, and other relevant health details.
2. Fill out the form for each camper individually, ensuring that the information is complete and up-to-date.
3. Review the form carefully to ensure that all fields are completed accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Camper Information** (`camper_info`, text, required: false): Provide the camper's name, contact information, and any other relevant details that may be helpful for the camp staff.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, required: false): List any pre-existing medical conditions that the camper may have, such as allergies, diabetes, or asthma.
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, required: false): List any medications that the camper is currently taking, including dosage and frequency.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required: false): List any known allergies or sensitivities that the camper has, including foods, environmental factors, or substances.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): Provide a brief medical history, including any previous injuries, illnesses, or surgeries.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false): Provide the contact information for the camper's emergency contact, including name, phone number, and relationship.
* **Insurance** (`insurance`, text, required: false): Provide insurance information, including policy number, provider name, and contact details.
* **Doctor Info** (`doctor_info`, text, required: false): Provide the name and contact information of the camper's doctor or healthcare provider.

## Tips

* Ensure that the form is completed accurately and completely for each camper.
* Keep the form in a secure and private location.
* Review the form regularly to ensure that the information is up-to-date and accurate.
* Use this form in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.
