# Camper Confidential Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Camper Confidential Information Form is used to collect essential information about a camper that is relevant to their safety and well-being during their stay at the summer camp. This information will be reviewed by our staff to make informed decisions about the camper's care and to communicate effectively with their parents or guardians.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Please fill in the personal details of the camper, including their first and last name, nickname (if applicable), date of birth, age, and gender.
2. Provide the contact information of the camper's parents or guardians, including their full name, relationship, phone number, and email.
3. If you have a second parent or guardian, please provide their information as well.
4. Fill in the medical information, including the camper's current medications, allergies, medical conditions, and immunization status.
5. Please select the camper's dietary restrictions and behavioral concerns.
6. If applicable, provide information about the camper's special needs or accommodations.
7. Answer the questions about bedwetting, homesickness concerns, and other caregiver information.
8. Finally, please sign and date the form to confirm the information provided.
