# Medical Employment Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect medical employment information from employees, providing essential data to facilitate smooth medical care and emergency planning. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the form section by section, ensuring you provide accurate and complete information.
2. Begin with the "Employee Contact" section, providing your personal details.
3. Move on to the "Emergency Contact" section, listing the person(s) to be contacted in case of an emergency.
4. Next, enter your medical insurance information, including your provider and any relevant details.
5. Then, provide your medical history, including any allergies or conditions that may impact your care.
6. List your current medications and the medical insurance provider's information.
7. Fill out the "Insurance Group Plan" section with the relevant details, including the group ID and plan type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Contact** (`title`, `text`, required): Enter your personal contact information, including your name, address, phone number, and any other relevant details.
* **Emergency Contact** (`title`, `text`, required): List the person(s) you would like us to contact in case of an emergency, including their name, relationship, phone number, and address.
* **Medical Insurance** (`title`, `text`, required): Enter your current medical insurance information, including your provider's name, policy number, and any other relevant details.
* **Medical History** (`title`, `text`, required): Describe any medical conditions, allergies, or past experiences that may impact your care, such as surgeries, hospitalizations, or previous illnesses.
* **Allergies** (`title`, `text`, required): List any allergies or sensitivities you have, including the substance and severity.
* **Medications** (`title`, `text`, required): Enter a list of your current medications, including the name, dosage, and frequency.
* **Medical Insurance Provider** (`title`, `text`, required): Enter your medical insurance provider's name and contact information.
* **Insurance Group Plan** (`title`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your insurance group plan, including the group ID and plan type.
* **Insurance Group ID** (`title`, `text`, required): Enter your insurance group ID, if applicable.
* **Insurance Group Plan Type** (`title`, `text`, required): Enter the type of your insurance group plan.

## Tips
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
* Ensure the correct contact information is provided in the "Emergency Contact" section.
* If you have any medical insurance, please ensure it is listed accurately in the "Medical Insurance" and "Medical Insurance Provider" sections.
* If you have a medical condition or allergy, please provide detailed information in the "Medical History" and "Allergies" sections.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your healthcare provider or contact us for assistance.
