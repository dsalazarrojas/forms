# Camper Special Dietary Needs Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camper Special Dietary Needs Form is used to gather essential information about your child's special dietary needs to ensure their safe and healthy participation in the summer camp program. This form helps us to understand their requirements, allergies, and medical conditions to provide suitable accommodations and support.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form with accurate and detailed information about your child.
2.  Ensure you answer all required fields (marked as `required` in the form).
3.  If you're unsure about any question or need clarification, please contact us for assistance.
4.  Once completed, please review the form carefully before submitting it to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Camper First Name** (`1`, `text`, `required`): Enter your child's first name.
* **Camper Last Name** (`2`, `text`, `required`): Enter your child's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`3`, `date`, `required`): Enter your child's birth date in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Age** (`4`, `number`, `required`): Enter your child's current age.
* **Parent or Guardian Full Name** (`5`, `text`, `required`): Enter your full name.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone** (`6`, `text`, `required`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (`7`, `email`, `required`): Enter your email address.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`8`, `text`, `required`): Enter the full name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`9`, `text`, `required`): Enter the emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Type of Dietary Restriction** (`10`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select all applicable dietary restrictions that apply to your child, such as Food allergies, Medical condition, and so on.
* **Food Allergies** (`11`, `text`, `optional`): List any food allergies your child has (if not already selected in the "Type of Dietary Restriction" field).
* **Allergy Severity** (`12`, `select_one`, `optional`): Describe the severity of your child's allergy reaction (if applicable).
* **Specific Foods to Avoid** (`13`, `text`, `required`): List all specific foods your child must avoid.
* **Is Cross-Contamination a Concern?** (`14`, `select_one`, `required`): Select whether cross-contamination is a concern for your child.
* **Medical Condition Diet** (`15`, `text`, `optional`): Describe any medical condition requiring a special diet (if not already selected in the "Type of Dietary Restriction" field).
* **Religious Dietary Requirements** (`16`, `text`, `optional`): Describe any religious dietary requirements (if not already selected in the "Type of Dietary Restriction" field).
* **Is This for Health or Ethical Reasons?** (`17`, `select_one`, `optional`): Select the reason for your child's dietary choices.
* **Acceptable Protein Sources** (`18`, `text`, `optional`): List acceptable protein sources for your child.
* **Acceptable Grains** (`19`, `text`, `optional`): List acceptable grain sources for your child.
* **Dairy Alternatives** (`20`, `text`, `optional`): List dairy alternatives used by your child.
* **Camper Favorite Foods** (`21`, `text`, `optional`): List your child's favorite foods.
* **Foods Camper Dislikes** (`22`, `text`, `optional`): List foods your child will not eat.
* **Typical Breakfast** (`23`, `text`, `optional`): Describe your child's typical breakfast.
* **Typical Lunch** (`24`, `text`, `optional`): Describe your child's typical lunch.
* **Typical Dinner** (`25`, `text`, `optional`): Describe your child's typical dinner.
* **Typical Snacks** (`26`, `text`, `optional`): List safe snack options for your child.
* **EpiPen Required** (`27`, `select_one`, `required`): Select whether your child carries EpiPen or not.
* **How Many EpiPens Will Be Provided?** (`28`, `number`, `optional`): If applicable, list the number of EpiPens to be provided.
* **Other Medications for Allergic Reactions** (`29`, `text`, `optional`): List any other medications for allergic reactions.
* **Has Camper Attended Camp Before with Dietary Needs?** (`30`, `select_one`, `required`): Select whether your child has attended camp before with dietary needs.
* **What Challenges Occurred Previously?** (`31`, `text`, `optional`): Describe any previous challenges or issues.
* **Would You Like to Speak with Kitchen Staff?** (`32`, `select_one`, `optional`): Select whether you'd like to speak with the kitchen staff.
* **Additional Dietary Notes** (`33`, `text`, `optional`): Add any additional dietary notes.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature** (`34`, `text`, `required`): Type your full name.
* **Date Signed** (`35`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date you signed the form.

Please review the form carefully and ensure all required fields are completed before submitting it to us. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
