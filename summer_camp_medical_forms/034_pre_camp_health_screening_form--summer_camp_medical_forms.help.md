# Pre Camp Health Screening Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pre Camp Health Screening Form is designed to collect health screening information from campers before they arrive at the camp. The form helps ensure that each camper's health status is assessed, and any necessary medical clearance is obtained.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Camper Information**: Enter the camper's name, contact information (phone number and email), and any other relevant details.
2. **Contact Information**: Provide the contact information for the parent or guardian of the camper.
3. **Health Checklist**: Answer all questions thoroughly, indicating any health-related concerns or allergies.
4. **Camper Information Confirmed**: Review and confirm the camper's information entered in the previous steps.
5. **Additional Information**: Enter any additional health information that is relevant to the camper.
6. **Medical Authorization**: Sign and date the medical authorization section, if necessary.
7. **Camper Signoff**: Sign the form to confirm that the camper has read and understands the health screening form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Camper Information**:
  * **Camper's Name**: Enter the camper's name, as it should appear on their identification.
  * **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the camper's parent/guardian.
  * **Email**: Enter the email address of the camper's parent/guardian.
* **Contact Information**:
  * **Parent/Guardian's Name**: Enter the name of the camper's parent or guardian.
  * **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the camper's parent or guardian.
  * **Email**: Enter the email address of the camper's parent or guardian.
* **Health Checklist**:
  * **Please answer all questions thoroughly**: This section is designed to gather information about the camper's health status.
  * **Allergies**: Indicate any allergies or health-related concerns the camper may have.
  * **Medications**: List any medications the camper is currently taking.
  * **Health Conditions**: Indicate any health conditions that may affect the camper's well-being.
* **Camper Information Confirmed**:
  * **Review and confirm**: Review the camper's information entered in the previous steps.
* **Additional Information**:
  * **Enter any additional health information**: Provide any additional health information that is relevant to the camper.
* **Medical Authorization**:
  * **Medical Authorization Section**: This section is designed for camps to obtain medical clearance, if necessary.
  * **Signature**: Sign and date the medical authorization section, if applicable.
* **Camper Signoff**:
  * **Signature**: Sign the form to confirm that the camper has read and understands the health screening form.

## Tips

* Please review the form carefully and ensure that all information is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about a question or need customization, please review your camp's policies and procedures.
* Ensure that you have obtained medical clearance, if necessary, before the camper arrives at the camp.
