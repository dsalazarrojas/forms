# Camper Physical Examination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Camper Physical Examination Form is designed to collect essential health information from campers to ensure their well-being and safety during the summer camp season.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and answer all questions accurately and completely.
2. Enter the camper's full name in the "Full Name" field.
3. Enter the camper's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Enter the camper's age in the "Age" field.
5. Select the camper's gender from the drop-down menu.
6. Enter the camper's height in inches in the "Height (inches)" field.
7. Enter the camper's weight in pounds in the "Weight (lbs)" field.
8. Enter the camper's blood pressure as a two-part ratio (e.g., 120/80).
9. Enter the camper's pulse rate in beats per minute.
10. Enter the camper's body temperature in Fahrenheit.
11. Answer questions regarding recent illnesses, chronic conditions, allergies, and current medications.
12. Indicate whether the camper is up-to-date with vaccinations.
13. Enter the date of the camper's last tetanus shot (if applicable).
14. Indicate the camper's COVID-19 vaccination status.
15. Assess the camper's general health status and select the appropriate option.
16. Provide clearance or restrictions for camp activities.
17. Enter any activity restrictions or special notes (if applicable).
18. Sign and date the form with your physician's signature and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter the camper's full name as it appears on their government-issued ID.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the camper's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Age**: Enter the camper's age in years.
* **Gender**: Select the camper's gender from the drop-down menu.
* **Height (inches)**: Enter the camper's height in inches.
* **Weight (lbs)**: Enter the camper's weight in pounds.
* **Blood Pressure**: Enter the camper's blood pressure as a two-part ratio (e.g., 120/80).
* **Pulse Rate (beats per minute)**: Enter the camper's pulse rate.
* **Body Temperature (Fahrenheit)**: Enter the camper's body temperature.
* **Recent Illness or Infection**: Describe any recent illnesses or infections the camper has experienced in the past four weeks.
* **Chronic Medical Conditions**: List any chronic medical conditions the camper has, such as asthma or diabetes.
* **Allergies**: List any known allergies the camper has, including medication and food allergies.
* **Current Medications**: List any current medications the camper is taking, including name, dosage, and frequency.
* **Up to Date with Vaccinations**: Indicate whether the camper is up-to-date with all required vaccinations.
* **Last Tetanus**: Enter the date of the camper's last tetanus shot (if applicable).
* **COVID-19 Vaccination Status**: Indicate the camper's COVID-19 vaccination status.
* **General Health Status**: Assess the camper's general health status by selecting an option from the drop-down menu.
* **Physician Clearance for Camp Activities**: Provide clearance or restrictions for the camper to participate in camp activities.
* **Activity Restrictions or Special Notes**: Enter any activity restrictions or special notes for the camper.
* **Physician Signature and Date**: Sign and date the form with your physician's signature and date.
