# Day Camp Camper Health Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential information about day campers to ensure their health, safety, and well-being during the camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please fill out all the fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* Enter your camper's name and address in the respective fields.
* Provide your contact information (phone number and email) so we can reach you if needed.
* Select your camper's health status from the options provided.
* If applicable, provide any medical information, allergies, or immunizations your camper may have.
* Sign and date the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **camper_health (Page 1)**: Enter a brief description of your camper's health status.
* **camper_name**: Enter your camper's full name.
* **camper_address**: Enter your camper's complete address.
* **camper_contact_number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **camper_email**: Enter your contact email.
* **camper_dob (Date of Birth)**: Enter your camper's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **camper_health_status**: Select your camper's health status from the options provided (High, Medium, or Low).
* **camper_medical_info**: If your camper has any medical information, allergies, or conditions, please provide details.
* **camper_allergies**: If your camper has any allergies, list them here.
* **camper_immunizations**: If your camper has any immunizations, list them here.
* **camper_medical_conditions**: If your camper has any medical conditions, list them here.
* **camper_medical_history**: If your camper has any medical history, provide details.
* **camper_family_info**: Provide any relevant family medical information.
* **camper_emergency_contact**: If you want to designate someone to contact in case of an emergency, provide their name and contact information.
* **camper_medical_insurance**: If your camper has medical insurance, provide the policy number and details.
* **camper_health_screening_complete**: Indicate whether the health screening is complete or not.
* **camper_signature**: Sign and date the form when complete.
* **camper_date**: Enter the date you completed the form.
* **camper_time**: Enter the time you completed the form.
* **camper_location**: Enter the location where you completed the form.
* **camper_form_completed_by**: Enter the person who completed the form.
* **camper_form_completed_on**: Enter the date the form was completed.
* **camper_form_submitted_by**: Enter the person who submitted the form.
* **camper_form_submitted_on**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **camper_form_version**: Enter the version of the form used.
