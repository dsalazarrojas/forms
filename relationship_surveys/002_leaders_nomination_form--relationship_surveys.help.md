# Leaders Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to nominate leaders who have made significant contributions to the organization or team for recognition or awards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to access the form.
2. Fill in the required and optional fields as needed.
3. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter the first name of the nominee.
- **Last Name**: Enter the last name of the nominee.
- **Date Of Birth**: Enter the date of birth of the nominee (in YYYY-MM-DD format, e.g., 1990-06-01).
- **Email**: Enter the email address of the nominee (in the format `example@example.com`).
- **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the nominee (e.g., `123-456-7890`).

## Tips
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask your supervisor or HR representative for assistance.
- Double-check your submission for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
- This form will be reviewed by the leadership team before further action is taken.
