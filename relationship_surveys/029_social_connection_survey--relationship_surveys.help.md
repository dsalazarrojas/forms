# Social Connection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data about students' perceptions and feelings towards their school environment, teachers, and peers. It helps identify areas of strength and weakness in the social connections within the school, enabling better decision-making and improvement of relationships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully to understand what is being asked.
2. Answer each question honestly, using the provided rating scale or options.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) next to the label.
4. Submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Participation** (`school_participation`, number, optional): This question asks you to rate how often you participate in school activities, such as clubs, sports, or events. Please rate your participation level on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Rarely" and 5 is "Frequently".
* **How friendly are people in your school?** (`friendliness`, number, optional): This question measures how welcoming and friendly people are in your school. Rate your experience on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Not at all friendly" and 5 is "Very friendly".
* **How much do teachers support you?** (`teacher_support`, number, optional): This question gauges your perception of how supportive your teachers are. Rate your experience on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Not at all supportive" and 5 is "Very supportive".
* **How much do parents support you?** (`parent_support`, number, optional): This question asks you to rate how supportive your parents are. Rate your experience on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Not at all supportive" and 5 is "Very supportive".
* **School Climate** (`school_climate`, select_multiple, optional): This question has five options: "Very Good", "Good", "Fair", "Bad", and "Very Bad". Choose all that apply to describe your school's overall atmosphere and culture.
* **How connected are you to teachers?** (`teacher_connection`, number, optional): This question measures how connected you feel with your teachers. Rate your experience on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Not at all connected" and 5 is "Very connected".
* **How connected are students in your school?** (`student_connections`, number, optional): This question asks you to rate how connected you feel with your peers. Rate your experience on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Not at all connected" and 5 is "Very connected".
* **How connected are you with your peers?** (`peer_connection`, number, optional): This question measures how connected you feel with your classmates. Rate your experience on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Not at all connected" and 5 is "Very connected".
* **How good is your school environment?** (`school_environment`, select_multiple, optional): This question has five options: "Very Good", "Good", "Fair", "Bad", and "Very Bad". Choose all that apply to describe your school environment.
