# Peer To Peer Relationship Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Peer To Peer Relationship Survey is designed to collect self-reported information about interactions with others, helping to understand the nature and frequency of these relationships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill out the `survey_name` field with your name or identifier.
- Select the type of relationship you have with the person you're reporting on (e.g., Friend, Family, Coworker).
- Choose how often you interact with this person (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Never).
- Provide additional comments about your relationship, if applicable.
- Select a date for the survey (note: this field is likely for tracking purposes).
- Choose a start time for your interaction (e.g., morning, afternoon, evening).
- Choose an end time for your interaction (e.g., hour, minute).
- Enter the duration of your interaction.
- Rate your relationship on a scale (likely out of 5 or 10, but exact scale not specified).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Survey Name** (`survey_name`, text, optional): Enter your name or identifier for this relationship.
- **Relationship Type** (`relationship_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose a type of relationship (e.g., Friend, Family, Coworker) that best describes you and the person you're reporting on.
- **Frequency** (`frequency`, select_one, optional): Select how often you interact with this person (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Never).
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide more information about your relationship (e.g., work, school, personal).
- **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, optional): Select a date for this survey (likely for tracking purposes).
- **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Choose a start time for your interaction (e.g., morning, afternoon, evening).
- **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Choose an end time for your interaction (e.g., hour, minute).
- **Duration** (`duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of your interaction.
- **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your relationship on a scale (likely out of 5 or 10, but exact scale not specified).

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
- If you're unsure about a field, try to provide the best answer you can.
- If you have any questions or need clarification, contact the survey administrators.
