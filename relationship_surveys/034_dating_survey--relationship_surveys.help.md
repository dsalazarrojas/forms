# Dating Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dating Survey is designed to collect personal information from users to help us better understand their preferences, behaviors, and experiences in the context of relationships. This form is used to gather data for internal analysis and improvement purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please ensure you have a stable internet connection to complete the form smoothly.
2. Fill out the form at your own pace, and do not hesitate to reach out if you encounter any difficulties.
3. For each field, provide accurate and relevant information to ensure the quality of the data collected.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, optional): Enter your first name as it is written on your official identification documents.
* **Last Name** (2, text, optional): Enter your last name as it is written on your official identification documents.
* **Email** (3, email, optional): Enter a valid email address.
* **Date of Birth** (4, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 1999-01-01).
* **Phone** (5, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Relationship Status** (6, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the following options:
	+ Married
	+ In a relationship
	+ Single
* **Preferred Communication Method** (7, select_one, optional): Select your preferred method of communication:
	+ SMS
	+ Email
	+ Call
	+ Video call
* **Time of Day Preferred Communication** (8, time, optional): Enter your preferred time of day for communication (e.g., 08:00).
* **How Did You Hear About This Survey?** (9, select_one, optional): Select how you learned about this survey:
	+ Facebook
	+ Instagram
	+ Twitter
	+ Email
	+ Other
* **Why Did You Fill Out This Survey?** (10, text, optional): Explain your reason for filling out this survey.
* **Age** (11, number, optional): Enter your age.
* **How Were You Feeling?** (12, select_one, optional): Select how you were feeling:
	+ Happy
	+ Neutral
	+ Sad
* **What Do You Like?** (13, text, optional): Describe something you enjoy doing or like.
* **What Do You Dislike?** (14, text, optional): Describe something you dislike or dislike doing.
* **What Is Your Favorite Food?** (15, select_one, optional): Select your favorite food:
	+ Pizza
	+ Sushi
	+ Tacos
	+ Burgers
* **What Is Your Favorite Color?** (16, text, optional): Enter your favorite color.
* **How Did You Hear About Us?** (17, select_one, optional): Select how you learned about our organization:
	+ Facebook
	+ Instagram
	+ Twitter
	+ Email
	+ Other
* **What Do You Like to Do?** (18, text, optional): Describe something you enjoy doing.
* **What Do You Dislike to Do?** (19, text, optional): Describe something you dislike doing.
* **How Did You Get Here?** (20, text, optional): Explain how you found out about our organization.
* **When Did You Get Here?** (21, text, optional): Enter the date and time you first visited our organization.
* **What Is Your Occupation?** (22, text, optional): Enter your occupation or profession.
* **How Did You Join?** (23, text, optional): Describe how you joined our organization.
* **What Is Your Education?** (24, text, optional): Enter your educational background.
* **What Do You Do?** (25, text, optional): Describe your job or profession.
