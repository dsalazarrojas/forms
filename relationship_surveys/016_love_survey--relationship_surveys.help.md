# Love Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Love Survey form is designed to collect information about relationships or romance. Please answer the questions to help us better understand your experiences and feelings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name to help us get to know you better.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name to help us understand your identity.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address to help us contact you if needed.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and accurately.
* You can skip any question if you prefer not to answer.
* If you have any questions or issues, contact us at form@gic.mx.
