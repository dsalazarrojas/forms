# Relationship Status Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess an individual's relationship status and dynamics. The questions cover various aspects of a person's relationship, including their partner's characteristics, goals, and the quality of their relationship.

## How to Complete This Form

1. The form has 25 questions divided across multiple pages.
2. Please answer each question to the best of your ability, being honest about your relationship status and dynamics.
3. If you're unsure about a question or need more information, you can always contact the researcher or study administrator for guidance.
4. The form is designed to take approximately [estimated time] minutes to complete, so please take your time and answer carefully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How would you describe your current relationship status?** (`relationship_status`, text, optional): Describe your current relationship status, e.g., married, dating, engaged, etc.
* **How long have you been with your partner?** (`length_of_time`, number, optional): Answer in years or months.
* **How many cuddles do you share with your partner?** (`frequency_of_cuddles`, number, optional): Estimate the number of times you physically touch or cuddle with your partner per day.
* **What's your favorite type of cuddle?** (`favorite_cuddle`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the options provided, e.g., Cuddle on the couch, Cuddle in the kitchen, etc.
* **What is your partner's name?** (`partner_name`, text, optional): Provide your partner's name.
* **What is your partner's age?** (`partner_age`, number, optional): Answer in years.
* **What is your partner's annual income range?** (`partner_income_range`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the provided income ranges, e.g., Under 20k, 20-30k, etc.
* **Do you have kids?** (`own_children`, select_one, optional): Answer yes or no.
* **What's your family structure?** (`family_structure`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the provided options, e.g., Married, Divorced, etc.
* **What is your partner's education level?** (`partner_education`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the provided education levels, e.g., Elementary, High school, etc.
* **How satisfied are you with your relationship?** (`relationship_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the provided satisfaction levels, e.g., Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, etc.
* **What kind of relationship do you have with your partner?** (`relationship_quality`, select_one, optional): Select one of the provided options, e.g., Romantic, Friendship, etc.
* **What's your preferred communication style with your partner?** (`communication_style`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the provided communication styles, e.g., Direct, Indirect, etc.
* **What's your level of trust with your partner?** (`trust_level`, number, optional): Answer on a scale, e.g., 1-10.
* **How do you usually resolve conflicts with your partner?** (`conflict_resolution`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the provided options, e.g., Together, Alone, etc.
* **How frequent are conflicts in your relationship?** (`conflict_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select one of the provided options, e.g., Often, Rarely, etc.
* **What is your love language?** (`love_language`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the provided love languages, e.g., Words of Affirmation, Quality time, etc.
* **What are your relationship goals?** (`relationship_goals`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the provided options, e.g., Short term, Long term, etc.
* **What are your partner's relationship goals?** (`partner_goals`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the provided options, e.g., Short term, Long term, etc.
* **What type of relationship do you have with your partner?** (`relationship_type`, select_one, optional): Select one of the provided options, e.g., Romantic partnership, Friendship, etc.
* **What causes conflicts in your relationship?** (`conflict_cause`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the provided options, e.g., Communication, Intimacy, etc.
* **What stage is your relationship in?** (`relationship_stage`, select_one, optional): Select one of the provided options, e.g., Just started, Dating, etc.
* **What kind of work does your partner do?** (`partner_work`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the provided options, e.g., Office job, Entrepreneur, etc.
* **What are your partner's interests?** (`partner_interest`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the provided options, e.g., Hiking, Reading, etc.
* **What is your partner's education level?** (`partner_education`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the provided options, e.g., Elementary, High school, etc.
