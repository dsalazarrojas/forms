# Government Relationship Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Government Relationship Survey aims to understand resident satisfaction with government services, the accessibility and transparency of these services, and areas where services can be improved. This survey will help identify areas of strength and weakness in government services and inform decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select an option for each field, filling in the form accordingly.
2. Answer the text fields (e.g., "What do you think?") in the text box provided.
3. Choose a numerical rating for fields with a scale (e.g., "On a scale of 1-10, how would you rate your satisfaction with government services?").
4. If there are multiple options available for a field, choose all that apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you think?** (government_relation_ship_form, text, required): Answer this question in the text box provided. This field gauges your overall impression of government services.
* **Government Services Satisfaction** (government_services_satisfaction, number, required): Rate your satisfaction with government services on a scale of 1-10.
* **Government Services Accessibility** (government_services_accessibility, number, required): Rate the accessibility of government services on a scale of 1-10.
* **Government Transparency** (government_transparency, text, required): Enter your thoughts on government transparency.
* **Service Interaction** (service_interaction, select_one, required): Choose how you typically interact with government services: 
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Resident Satisfaction** (resident_satisfaction, select_multiple, required): Choose all that apply to rate your satisfaction with the government:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **How often do you interact with government services?** (government_services_frequency, time, required): Choose a time interval for how often you interact with government services.
* **Service Improvement** (service_improvement, text, required): Suggest improvements for government services.
* **Resident Feedback** (resident_feedback, text, required): Share your feedback about government services.
* **Service Feedback** (service_feedback, text, required): Share your feedback about government services.
* **Government Services Satisfaction 2** (government_services_satisfaction_2, number, required): Rate your satisfaction with government services on a scale of 1-10.
* **Resident Satisfaction 2** (resident_satisfaction_2, number, required): Rate your satisfaction with the government on a scale of 1-10.

## Tips
Please fill in each field honestly and thoroughly, as your feedback will contribute to improving government services and enhancing the relationship with the community.
