# Transgender Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Transgender Survey is a tool designed to collect demographic data on transgender individuals to better understand their experiences and provide support services. This form is intended to be completed by transgender individuals to help them share their information and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Choose the correct options for each multiple-choice field.
3. Enter any other relevant details that you feel are important to share.
4. Review your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required/optional): Please fill in your legal name as it appears on official documents.
* **Gender Identity** (`gender_identity`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose all that apply from the options provided.
	+ Male: You identify as male.
	+ Female: You identify as female.
	+ Non-Binary: You identify as non-binary or genderqueer.
	+ Genderqueer: You identify as genderqueer.
* **Preferred Name** (`preferred_name`, text, required/optional): If you prefer to be known by a different name, please enter it here.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address, for example, example@example.com.
* **Preferred Pronouns** (`preferred_pronouns`, text, required/optional): If you prefer to be addressed with specific pronouns, please enter them here.
* **Relationship History** (`relationship_history`, text, required/optional): Share any relevant information about your relationship history, if you feel comfortable doing so.
* **Family Member Name** (`family_member_name`, text, required/optional): If applicable, enter the name of a family member.
* **Family Member Relationship** (`family_member_relationship`, select_one, required/optional): Choose how you are related to the family member.
	+ Parent: You are a parent.
	+ Partner: You are a partner or spouse.
	+ Sibling: You are a sibling.
	+ Other: Specify the relationship in the "Family Member Relationship" field.
* **Family Member Contact Info** (`family_member_contact_info`, text, required/optional): If you feel comfortable sharing, enter the contact information of your family member.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required/optional): If you feel comfortable sharing, enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, required/optional): If you feel comfortable sharing, enter the contact information of your emergency contact.

## Tips
* Please fill in any fields that you feel are relevant and comfortable sharing.
* You can skip any fields that you do not feel comfortable sharing or are not applicable to you.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact form@gic.mx.
