# Intimacy Expectations Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about individuals' expectations and preferences regarding intimacy in relationships. The survey aims to understand respondents' comfort levels with different types of intimacy, such as emotional, physical, and sexual intimacy, as well as their preferred communication channels and conflict resolution methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the options that best match your expectations and preferences for intimacy in relationships. You can choose multiple options for each section, and you can select "Other" if none of the provided options fit your expectations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Relationship Expectations**: Select all the types of intimacy you expect or desire in a relationship. This can include Intimacy, Other (please specify in the "Other" field).
* **Communication Channels**: Choose the channels you would use to communicate with your partner for intimacy. This can include Email, Phone, Social Media, Text, Video, or Other (please specify).
* **Conflict Resolution**: Select how you would prefer to resolve conflicts in a relationship. This can be Direct, Indirect, Mediation, or Other (please specify).
* **Intimacy Expectations**: Note down any other expectations or preferences you have for intimacy that are not listed.
* **Emotional Intimacy**: Rate your expected level of emotional intimacy on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Sexual Intimacy**: Rate your expected level of sexual intimacy on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Intimacy Expectations Scale**: Select all the levels of intimacy you expect or desire in a relationship. This can include High, Medium, Low, Very Low, or None.
* **Communication Channels Scale**: Select all the communication channels you would use for intimacy. This can include High, Medium, Low, Very Low, or None.
* **Intimacy Expectations Scale**: Select all the levels of intimacy you expect or desire in a relationship. This can include High, Medium, Low, Very Low, or None.
* **Relationship Expectations**: Select all the types of intimacy you expect or desire in a relationship. This can include High, Medium, Low, Very Low, or None.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to ask your relationship manager for clarification.
* The more honest and open you are, the more valuable the survey results will be.
* You can always skip any questions that do not apply to you or your relationship.
