# parent_stress_and_burnout_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The parent_stress_and_burnout_survey form is designed to collect data on parent's stress and burnout levels, coping mechanisms, and relationships with their child and spouse. This information will help understand the stress and burnout experiences of parents and inform interventions and support strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions to the best of your abilities.
2. For select-type questions, choose the option that best describes your situation.
3. If you are unsure or have no preference, you can select "No answer" or "Unknown".
4. Take your time to answer each question, and don't hesitate to ask for help if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Stress Level** (`parent_stress_level`, number, required: false)
	+ This question asks about your current level of stress. Please enter a number between 1 and 10, where 1 is "Very Low" and 10 is "Very High".
* **Parent Coping Strategies** (`parent_coping_strategies`, text, required: false)
	+ Please describe the strategies you use to cope with stress, such as exercise, talking to a friend, or relaxation techniques.
* **Parent Support Network** (`parent_support_network`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Please choose the people you have a support network with, such as family members, friends, or a therapist. You can select multiple options.
* **Child Emotional Wellbeing** (`child_emotional_wellbeing`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This question asks about your child's emotional wellbeing, and you can choose the option that best describes their situation.
* **Parent Physical Health** (`parent_physical_health`, select_one, required: false)
	+ Please choose the option that best describes your current physical health.
* **Parent Mental Health** (`parent_mental_health`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This question asks about your current mental health, and you can choose the option that best describes your situation.
* **Parent Socioeconomic Status** (`parent_socioeconomic_status`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Please choose the socioeconomic status that best describes your current situation, such as Active or Inactive.
* **Parent Relationship With Child** (`parent_relationship_with_child`, date, required: false)
	+ Please enter the date of your last interaction or activity with your child.
* **Parent Relationship With Spouse** (`parent_relationship_with_spouse`, time, required: false)
	+ Please enter the amount of time you spend with your spouse on a daily basis.
* **Parent Coping Strategies** (`parent_coping_strategies`, text, required: false)
	+ Please describe the strategies you use to cope with stress, such as exercise, talking to a friend, or relaxation techniques.
* **Child Coping Strategies** (`child_coping_strategies`, text, required: false)
	+ Please describe the strategies your child uses to cope with stress, such as drawing, talking to a friend, or playing.
* **Child Physical Health** (`child_physical_health`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This question asks about your child's physical health, and you can choose the option that best describes their situation.
* **Child Mental Health** (`child_mental_health`, select_one, required: false)
	+ Please choose the option that best describes your child's mental health.
* **Child Physical Health** (`child_physical_health`, select_one, required: false)
	+ Please choose the option that best describes your child's mental health.

## Tips
- Take your time to answer each question carefully.
- If you are unsure or have no preference, you can select "No answer" or "Unknown".
- This form is for your information and support only, and your answers will be kept confidential.
- If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to ask.
