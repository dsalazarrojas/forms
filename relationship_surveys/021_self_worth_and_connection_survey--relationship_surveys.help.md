# Self Worth And Connection Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to assess various aspects of self-worth and social connections, allowing users to reflect on their relationships with themselves and others. It consists of multiple sections, each focusing on distinct areas of self-worth and social connections.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by providing answers to the questions in the Self Worth Section and Social Connection Section.
2. Answer the questions in the Relationships Section, which may help you reflect on your relationships with others.
3. For the self-worth and social connection scales, select the frequency or intensity of your feelings from the given options:
	* Not at all, Somewhat, Moderately, Highly, or (for frequency questions) Never, Rarely, Sometimes, Often, Almost Always.

## Tips

This form is designed to help you reflect on your self-worth and social connections. Be honest with your answers, as they will help you better understand your relationships with yourself and others. Take your time to answer each question thoroughly, and remember that this survey is a personal reflection tool, not a competition or evaluation.
