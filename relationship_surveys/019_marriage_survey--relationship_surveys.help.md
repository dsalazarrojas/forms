# Marriage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Marriage Survey is designed to collect information about the respondent's marital status and relationship satisfaction. It will help identify areas where couples can work on improving their relationship.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select all answers that apply to you.
2. Please provide honest and accurate answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Getting Married** (`getting_married`, select_one, required): Answer "Yes" if you are currently married or in a long-term relationship.
* **Married To** (`married_to`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the names of your spouse(s).
* **How Long Married** (`how_long_married`, select_one, optional): How long have you been married? Select the corresponding duration of your marriage.
* **Have Children** (`have_children`, select_one, optional): Answer "Yes" if you have children.
* **Number Of Children** (`number_of_children`, number, optional): If you have children, select the number of children you have.
* **Married Aged** (`married_ages`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the ages at which you've had children.
* **How Much Do We Do** (`how_much_do`, select_one, optional): Answer "Yes" if you and your partner frequently do activities together.
* **How Much Don't We Do** (`how_much_dont_do`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the activities you and your partner don't frequently do together.
* **Married Life Satisfaction** (`married_life_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Answer "Satisfied" if you are satisfied with your relationship.
* **Married Financial Satisfaction** (`married_financial_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Answer "Satisfied" if you are satisfied with your finances.
* **Married Relationship Satisfaction** (`married_relationship_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Answer "Satisfied" if you are satisfied with your relationship.
* **Married Leisure Satisfaction** (`married_leisure_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Answer "Satisfied" if you are satisfied with your leisure activities.

## Tips

* Answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* Make sure to select all answers that apply to you.
* If you've filled out this survey in the past, please let us know, so we can avoid duplicate surveys.
