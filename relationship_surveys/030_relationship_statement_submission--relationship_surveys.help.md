# relationship_statement_submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for individuals to provide a detailed account of their relationships, including relationship history, supporting documents, and contact information. The form is used to gather information for internal purposes, such as tracking and analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Click on the "Start" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Read each section carefully and fill out the required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
3. Provide a clear and concise answer to each question.
4. If you have supporting documents, select them from the provided options.
5. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is this form for?** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false): This section is a brief introduction to the form, explaining its purpose and what to expect. Please read this section carefully before proceeding.
* **Relationship Details** (`couple_details`, `text`, required: false): This section asks for a general description of the relationship. Please provide a clear and concise answer.
* **Relationship History** (`relationship_history`, `text`, required: false): This section requests a detailed account of the relationship history. Please provide a clear and concise narrative.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select up to three supporting documents from the options provided. These documents will be reviewed as part of the relationship evaluation process.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `date`, required: false): This section requests the date of the last relationship update. Please provide the date in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Signature 2** (`signature2`, `date`, required: false): This section requests the date of the next relationship update. Please provide the date in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Organization Details** (`organization_details`, `text`, required: false): This section asks for a detailed description of the organization involved in the relationship. Please provide a clear and concise answer.
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, required: false): This section asks for the name of the organization involved in the relationship. Please provide the correct name.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required: false): This section requests a detailed account of the contact information. Please provide a clear and concise answer.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): This section asks for the email address of the contact person. Please provide the correct email address.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities.
* Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
