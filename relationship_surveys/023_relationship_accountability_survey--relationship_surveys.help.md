# Relationship Accountability Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Relationship Accountability Survey is designed to collect information about your current relationship status and dynamics. This survey will help us understand how you relate to others and identify potential areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read through each section carefully to ensure you understand the questions and their relevance to you.
- Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
- If a question does not apply to you, please select "N/A" or "Not Applicable" as an option.
- Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false): A brief overview of the purpose and context of the survey.
* **Relationship Status** (`relationship_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the current relationship status from the options provided:
	+ Single
	+ In a relationship
	+ Married
	+ Engaged
	+ In a civil partnership
	+ Widowed
* **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, `text`, required: false): If applicable, provide the name of your partner.
* **Partner Email** (`partner_email`, `email`, required: false): If applicable, provide the email address of your partner.
* **Partner Phone** (`partner_phone`, `text`, required: false): If applicable, provide the phone number of your partner.
* **My Name** (`my_name`, `text`, required: false): Provide your name.
* **My Email** (`my_email`, `email`, required: false): Provide your email address.
* **My Phone** (`my_phone`, `text`, required: false): Provide your phone number.
