# relationship_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this relationship questionnaire is to gather information about your relationship with your partner. This form will help us understand your dynamics, goals, and overall satisfaction with the relationship.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2.  Provide detailed responses where possible.
3.  Select the most accurate options for each question.
4.  If a question does not apply, select the "None" option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. What is your relationship status?** (`relationship_status`, `select_one`, required)
    This question asks about your current relationship status, such as being single, in a relationship, or something else.
* **2. How old is your partner?** (`partner_s_age`, `number`, optional)
    This question asks for the age of your partner.
* **3. What is your partner's gender?** (`partner_s_gender`, `select_one`, required)
    Please select the option that best describes your partner's gender.
* **4. What is your partner's income?** (`partner_s_income`, `number`, optional)
    This question asks for your partner's income.
* **5. What is your partner's education level?** (`partner_s_education`, `select_multiple`, optional)
    Select all that apply to your partner's education level.
* **6. What is your partner's occupation?** (`partner_s_occupation`, `text`, optional)
    Please enter your partner's occupation.
* **7. How long have you been in a relationship with your partner?** (`partner_s_relationship_length`, `time`, optional)
    This question asks for the length of your relationship with your partner.
* **8. What is your communication style?** (`communication`, `note`, optional)
    Please enter a brief description of your communication style.
* **9. How would you describe your partner's mood?** (`partner_s_mood`, `note`, optional)
    Describe your partner's mood.
* **10. How would you describe your own mood?** (`self_mood`, `note`, optional)
    Describe your own mood.
* **11. What are your goals for this relationship?** (`relationship_goals`, `note`, optional)
    Please enter your goals for this relationship.
* **12. What are your partner's goals for this relationship?** (`partner_s_goals`, `note`, optional)
    Please enter your partner's goals for this relationship.
* **13. How do you usually resolve conflicts?** (`conflict_resolution`, `note`, optional)
    Describe your process for resolving conflicts.
* **14. What strategies do you use to resolve conflicts?** (`conflict_resolution_strategies`, `note`, optional)
    Please enter strategies you use to resolve conflicts.
* **15. What is your communication style?** (`communication_style`, `note`, optional)
    Please enter a brief description of your communication style.
* **16. How often do conflicts arise in your relationship?** (`conflict_frequency`, `note`, optional)
    Describe the frequency of conflicts in your relationship.
* **17. What topics do conflicts usually revolve around?** (`conflict_topics`, `note`, optional)
    Please enter a brief description of the common conflict topics.
* **18. How satisfied are you with conflict resolution in your relationship?** (`conflict_resolution_satisfaction`, `note`, optional)
    Describe your level of satisfaction with conflict resolution.
* **19. How satisfied are you with your relationship?** (`relationship_satisfaction`, `note`, optional)
    Rate your overall satisfaction with your relationship.
* **20. Is there anything else you would like to mention or comment about your relationship?** (`comments`, `note`, optional)
    Enter any additional comments or observations you have about your relationship.
