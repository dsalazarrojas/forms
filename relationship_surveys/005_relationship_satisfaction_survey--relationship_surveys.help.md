# Relationship Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Relationship Satisfaction Survey is designed to assess the quality and dynamics of relationships between patients and their healthcare providers or therapists. The survey gathers information on several key areas, including patient-therapist expectations, communication skills, conflict resolution, emotional intelligence, and relationship satisfaction. This information can help healthcare providers improve their relationship-building skills and ultimately enhance patient outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Start by reviewing the instructions and ensuring you have all necessary information.
* Answer the questions and select the corresponding response options for each field.
* For fields with multiple options (Select Multiple, Select One), choose the options that best fit your answer.
* Provide notes or comments where required or needed.
* Once finished, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Survey** (Patient Survey, Text, Required): This section is meant to gather specific information about the patient's experiences and feelings related to their relationship with the healthcare provider.
* **Relationship Expectations** (Relationship Expectations, Select Multiple, Required): Please select all options that apply to the patient's expectations of the healthcare provider's role in their care.
* **Patient Relationship** (Patient Relationship, Select One, Optional): Check 'Yes' if you feel you have a good relationship with your healthcare provider.
* **Communication** (Communication, Select Multiple, Optional): Indicate how often you have effective communication with your healthcare provider, such as 'Yes', 'No' or 'Rarely'.
* **Conflict Resolution** (Conflict Resolution, Select Multiple, Required): Check all options that describe how you resolve conflicts with your healthcare provider.
* **Emotional Intelligence** (Emotional Intelligence, Select Multiple, Optional): Select options that reflect the level of emotional intelligence you have observed or experienced with your healthcare provider.
* **Conflict Tactics** (Conflict Tactics, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose options describing the tactics you have used or would use to resolve conflicts with your healthcare provider.
* **Relationship Quality** (Relationship Quality, Select Multiple, Required): Select options that describe the quality of your relationship with your healthcare provider (Good, Poor).
* **Relationship Stability** (Relationship Stability, Select Multiple, Optional): Check options that describe the stability of your relationship with your healthcare provider.
* **Relationship Satisfaction** (Relationship Satisfaction, Select Multiple, Required): Choose options that describe your satisfaction level with your relationship (Satisfied, Dissatisfied).
* **Therapist Survey** (Therapist Survey, Select Multiple, Optional): Check 'Yes' if you have had a satisfactory interaction with your therapist.
* **Survey Comments** (Survey Comments, Note, Optional): Provide comments or observations about your experiences or feelings related to this survey.
* **Communication Skills** (Communication Skills, Select Multiple, Optional): Select options that describe the communication skills you have observed or experienced with your healthcare provider.
* **Conflict Resolution Tactics** (Conflict Resolution Tactics, Select One, Optional): Check 'Yes' if you have used conflict resolution tactics with your healthcare provider.
* **Relationship Expectations Tactics** (Relationship Expectations Tactics, Text, Optional): Provide a brief description of your expectations for the relationship with your healthcare provider.
* **Communication Skills Used** (Communication Skills Used, Select Multiple, Optional): Select options that describe the communication skills you have used with your healthcare provider.
* **Conflict Tactics Used** (Conflict Tactics Used, Select Multiple, Optional): Check options that describe the conflict tactics you have used with your healthcare provider.
* **Relationship Quality Used** (Relationship Quality Used, Select Multiple, Optional): Select options that describe the quality of your relationship with your healthcare provider.
* **Relationship Stability Used** (Relationship Stability Used, Select Multiple, Optional): Check options that describe the stability of your relationship with your healthcare provider.
* **Relationship Satisfaction Used** (Relationship Satisfaction Used, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose options that describe your satisfaction level with your relationship with your healthcare provider.
* **Therapist Survey Used** (Therapist Survey Used, Select Multiple, Optional): Check 'Yes' if you have used a survey with your therapist.
* **Survey Comments Used** (Survey Comments Used, Note, Optional): Provide comments or observations about your experiences or feelings related to this survey.
* **Therapist Comments** (Therapist Comments, Note, Optional): Provide comments or observations from your healthcare provider or therapist.
* **Relationship Comments** (Relationship Comments, Note, Optional): Provide comments or observations about your relationship with your healthcare provider.
* **Relationship Comments Used** (Relationship Comments Used, Note, Optional): Provide comments or observations about your relationship with your healthcare provider.
