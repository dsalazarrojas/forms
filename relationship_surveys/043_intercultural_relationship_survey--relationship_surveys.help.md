# Intercultural Relationship Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Intercultural Relationship Survey is designed to collect data on relationships between individuals from different cultural backgrounds. This form aims to gather information on demographics, relationship type, duration, status, cultural and socioeconomic characteristics, and self-assessment of partnership qualities, conflicts, and satisfaction levels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the survey questions carefully to understand the type of information being collected.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled in (marked as "required" in the form).
3. Answer the questions according to your personal experiences and knowledge.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name Of Partner 1** (Text, required): Enter the name of your partner.
* **Ethnicity Of Partner 1** (Select One, required): Select the ethnicity of your partner from the options provided.
* **Name Of Partner 2** (Text, required): Enter the name of your partner.
* **Ethnicity Of Partner 2** (Select One, required): Select the ethnicity of your partner from the options provided.
* **Partner Relationship Type** (Select One, required): Choose the type of relationship you have with your partner (e.g., Romantic Relationship, Family Relationship, Friend/Peer).
* **Partner Relationship Duration** (Select Multiple, required): Choose the duration of your relationship (e.g., Less than 1 month, 1-3 months, etc.).
* **Partner Relationship Status** (Select One, required): Select the current status of your relationship (e.g., Together, Separated/Divorced, Engaged, etc.).
* **Partner Culture** (Select Multiple, optional): Select the cultural background of your partner (e.g., Individualist, Collectivist, Indigenist, etc.). If unsure, leave blank.
* **Partner Socioeconomic Status** (Select Multiple, optional): Select the socioeconomic status of your partner (e.g., Low, Medium, High). If unsure, leave blank.
* **Self Cultural Identity Label** (Text, required): Enter your self-identified cultural identity label.
* **Self Cultural Values Label** (Text, required): Enter your self-identified cultural values label.
* **Partner Socioeconomic Status 2** (Select Multiple, optional): Select the socioeconomic status of your partner (e.g., Low, Medium, High). If unsure, leave blank.
* **Partner Cultural Identity Label** (Text, optional): Enter your partner's self-identified cultural identity label. If unsure, leave blank.
* **Partner Cultural Values Label** (Text, optional): Enter your partner's self-identified cultural values label. If unsure, leave blank.
* **Self Partnership Quality** (Select One, required): Assess your partnership quality as High, Medium, or Low.
* **Partner Partnership Quality** (Select One, required): Assess your partner's partnership quality as High, Medium, or Low.
* **Self Satisfaction Level** (Select One, required): Assess your satisfaction level with your partnership as High, Medium, or Low.
* **Partner Satisfaction Level** (Select One, required): Assess your partner's satisfaction level with your partnership as High, Medium, or Low.
* **Self Confidence Level** (Select One, required): Assess your confidence level with your partnership as High, Medium, or Low.
* **Partner Confidence Level** (Select One, required): Assess your partner's confidence level with your partnership as High, Medium, or Low.
* **Self Conflict Level** (Select One, required): Assess your conflict level with your partnership as High, Medium, or Low.
* **Partner Conflict Level** (Select One, required): Assess your partner's conflict level with your partnership as High, Medium, or Low.
* **Communication Level** (Select One, required): Assess the communication level with your partner as High, Medium, or Low.
* **Overall Quality Of Relationship** (Select One, required): Assess the overall quality of your partnership as High, Medium, or Low.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* If unsure about any question, leave it blank and move on.
* Ensure that all questions are answered before submitting the form.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
