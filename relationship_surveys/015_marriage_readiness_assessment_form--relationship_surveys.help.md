# Marriage Readiness Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Marriage Readiness Assessment Form is designed to help individuals assess their readiness for marriage by gathering information on their current relationship status, expectations, challenges, and goals.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Be as specific as possible when providing your answers.
3. Consider your current relationship status and the challenges you've faced.
4. Think about what makes your relationship stronger and what would make it weaker.
