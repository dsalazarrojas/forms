# Boyfriend Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Boyfriend Application Form is a tool used to collect essential information from individuals interested in forming a relationship with the company. This data will help us assess compatibility and suitability for potential partnerships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Applicant name" field.
2. If applicable, provide your current age.
3. Share your hobbies and interests in the "Interests" field.
4. Describe your relationship goals in the "Relationship goals" field.
5. If relevant, mention your current occupation or student status in the "Occupation" field.
6. Briefly describe your core values in the "Values alignment" field.
7. Indicate your typical availability for dates or calls in the "Availability" field.
8. (Optional) Share any references or social links that you'd like to share with us in the "References" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant name**: Enter your full name as you'd like it to be known in the company.
* **Age**: If you're comfortable sharing your age, please enter it in years.
* **Interests**: Share your hobbies, passions, or areas you enjoy spending time on. This can be anything from playing sports to cooking or traveling.
* **Relationship goals**: Describe what you're looking for in a relationship. This can be anything from casual friendship to long-term partnership.
* **Occupation**: If you're currently working or studying, please share your profession or academic program.
* **Values alignment**: Briefly describe your core values that guide your decisions and actions. This can be anything from environmentalism to social justice.
* **Availability**: Indicate your typical availability for dates or calls. Please be honest, as this will help us plan and communicate with you effectively.
* **References**: (Optional) Share any social media links, LinkedIn profiles, or other relevant references that you'd like to share with us. This can be anything from a personal blog to a work portfolio.
