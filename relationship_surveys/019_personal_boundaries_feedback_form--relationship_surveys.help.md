# Personal Boundaries Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Boundaries Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from users about their personal boundaries experiences with the organization. This information will help us understand how we can better support you and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on this page with your honest and thoughtful feedback.
2. Please fill in the blanks with your comments and experiences.
3. Make sure to select the correct options for each question.
4. Review your answers to ensure you haven't missed any questions.
5. Click the submit button to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Submission** (`form_submission`, note, required): Please provide your personal feedback and comments about your recent interaction or experience with the organization. Be as detailed as possible.
* **How did you hear** (`how_did_you_hear`, text, required): Please tell us how you became aware of this form. For example, you saw a post on social media, a friend told you, or you saw a poster in the office.
* **Why are you submitting this form** (`why_submitting_form`, text, required): Please explain why you are submitting this form. Are you looking for more information, reporting a concern, or simply providing feedback?
* **Have you received any information about this form** (`have_you_received_information`, select_multiple, required): How did you first learn about this form? 
	+ Information from a friend
	+ Information from another website
	+ Information from another employee
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Do you feel safe** (`do_you_feel_safe`, select_one, required): On a scale of 1-3, how safe do you feel when interacting with our services?
	+ True (I feel very safe)
	+ False (I do not feel safe)
* **Do you feel respected** (`do_you_feel_respected`, select_one, required): On a scale of 1-3, how respected do you feel when interacting with our services?
	+ True (I feel very respected)
	+ False (I do not feel respected)
* **Would you like to receive more information** (`would_like_to_receive_more_information`, select_one, required): Would you like to learn more about our services or boundaries through additional resources or communications?
	+ True (Yes, I would like to learn more)
	+ False (No, I have enough information)
* **Would you like support** (`would_like_support`, select_one, required): Would you like to have someone from our organization reach out to you for support or guidance?
	+ True (Yes, I would like support)
	+ False (No, I am fine on my own)

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your responses. Your feedback is valuable to us.
- If you have any concerns or issues, please mention them in the "Form Submission" field.
- If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, we'd love to hear them.
- We appreciate your time and effort in providing this feedback. It will help us serve you better.
