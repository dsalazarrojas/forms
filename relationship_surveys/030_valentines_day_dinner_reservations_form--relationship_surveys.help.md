# Valentines Day Dinner Reservations Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to allow users to make reservations for a Valentine's Day dinner event.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting, make sure to have the following information ready:
- A valid email address
- Your full name
- Your phone number
- The preferred date and time of the event
- The size of your party
- The time slot for the reservation

To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
4. Select the preferred date for the reservation in the "Date" field.
5. Select the preferred time slot for the reservation in the "Time Slot" field.
6. Enter the size of your party in the "party size" field.
7. Fill in the "Reservation Date" field with the date of the event.
8. Enter the "Reservation Time" field with the time of the event.
9. If you have any special requests, fill in the "Special Requests" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Fill in your full name.
* **email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select the preferred date for the reservation.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Select the preferred time for the reservation.
* **party size** (`party_size`, select_multiple, required): Select the size of your party (2, 3, 4, or 5).
* **Time Slot** (`time_slot`, select_one, required): Select the time slot for the reservation (18:00-20:00, 20:00-22:00, 22:00-00:00).
* **Reservation Date** (`reservation_date`, date, required): Fill in the date of the event.
* **Reservation Time** (`reservation_time`, time, required): Fill in the time of the event.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): If you have any special requests, fill this field in.
