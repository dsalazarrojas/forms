# Youth Climate Policy Understanding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Climate Policy Understanding Survey aims to gather data on the opinions and concerns of young people regarding climate change policy. This survey will help policymakers understand the priorities and needs of the youth demographic, enabling them to make informed decisions that address climate change-related issues.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the instructions carefully and ensure you have a clear understanding of the survey's purpose.
2. Provide honest and accurate information for each field.
3. Complete each field carefully, ensuring that you fill in the correct information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Survey Participant Information**
* **Label:** Survey Participant Information
* **Type:** Note
* **Purpose:** This section is intended to provide general information about yourself, such as age and education level.

### 2. **Age**
* **Label:** Age
* **Type:** Number
* **Purpose:** Please enter your age.

### 3. **Education Level**
* **Label:** Education Level
* **Type:** Select One
* **Purpose:** Choose the level of education you have completed (0-5, with 0 being "no formal education" and 5 being "graduate degree or higher").

### 4. **Gender**
* **Label:** Gender
* **Type:** Select One
* **Purpose:** Choose your gender identity.

### 5. **Climate Change Concerns**
* **Label:** Climate Change Concerns
* **Type:** Select Multiple
* **Purpose:** Choose the most important issue that you think the world should be focusing on right now.

### 6. **Policy Priority**
* **Label:** Policy Priority
* **Type:** Select Multiple
* **Purpose:** Choose one or multiple policy priorities that you think should be implemented.

### 7. **Government Response**
* **Label:** Government Response
* **Type:** Select Multiple
* **Purpose:** Choose the government response that you think should be taken on climate change.

### 8. **Policy Recommendation**
* **Label:** Policy Recommendation
* **Type:** Text
* **Purpose:** Provide a brief explanation on what you think would be the best way to address climate change.

### 9. **Conclusion**
* **Label:** Conclusion
* **Type:** Note
* **Purpose:** Summarize your key points in this section.

### 10. **Contact Information**
* **Label:** Contact Information
* **Type:** Note
* **Purpose:** Provide your contact information for follow-up and verification purposes.
