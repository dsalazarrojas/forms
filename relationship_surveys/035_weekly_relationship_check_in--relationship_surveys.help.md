# Weekly Relationship Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you reflect on your relationship progress, partner support, communication, conflict resolution, emotional intimacy, and relationship satisfaction over the past week. It's a self-assessment tool meant to promote healthy relationships and provide insights into areas for growth and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Review and answer each question carefully**. Take a moment to reflect on your experiences and feelings from the past week.
2. **Choose the best response** for each question, selecting an option that best describes your situation.
3. **Submit your answers** to record your progress and track changes over time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How would you describe your progress this week?** (`relationship_progress`, text, **optional**): Please describe any significant events, changes, or developments that have happened in your relationship over the past week. Be as concise or detailed as you like, but try to keep it brief.
* **How supportive was your partner this week?** (`partner_support`, select_one, **optional**: Yes/No): Check the box that best reflects how much support you felt from your partner this week.
* **How would you describe your communication this week?** (`communication`, text, **optional**): Describe how you've been communicating with your partner, including any notable successes or challenges.
* **How would you describe your resolution of conflicts this week?** (`conflict_resolution`, text, **optional**): Think about any conflicts or disagreements you've had with your partner and describe how you resolved them.
* **How would you describe your emotional intimacy this week?** (`emotional_intimacy`, text, **optional**): Reflect on how connected you've felt with your partner on an emotional level, including any moments of closeness or distance.
* **How would you describe your relationship satisfaction this week?** (`relationship_satisfaction`, text, **optional**): Rate your overall satisfaction with your relationship, thinking about what's working well and what's not.
