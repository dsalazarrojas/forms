# Parent Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about parents' engagement with their child's education. Your participation will help us understand how parents interact with their children and identify areas where we can improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. For each question, choose the answer that best describes your typical behavior or feelings.
2. You may select multiple options for questions that ask for multiple choices.
3. Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
4. You may choose "No Response" if you do not have a preference or do not perform an activity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **survey_title** (text, optional): This is the title of the survey. Please enter a brief title that describes the purpose of your answers.
* **survey_sub_title** (text, optional): This is a sub-title that provides more context to the survey title.
* **How involved do parents feel in their child's education?** (select_multiple, optional):
	+ **Very Involved**: You are very engaged in your child's education and actively participate in their learning process.
	+ **Somewhat Involved**: You are somewhat engaged, but not as actively as you could be.
	+ **Not Very Involved**: You are not very engaged, but you still try to be involved.
	+ **Not at All Involved**: You do not feel involved in your child's education.
	+ **No Response**: You do not feel involved in your child's education.
* **How many hours do you spend per week on educational activities with your child?** (number, optional): This is the number of hours you typically spend per week on educational activities with your child.
* **What is your preferred method of communication with teachers?** (select_one, optional):
	+ **Email**: You prefer to communicate with teachers via email.
	+ **Phone**: You prefer to communicate with teachers via phone.
	+ **Text Message**: You prefer to communicate with teachers via text message.
	+ **In Person**: You prefer to communicate with teachers in person.
	+ **No Response**: You do not have a preferred method of communication.
* **How often do you read to your child?** (select_one, optional):
	+ **Daily**: You read to your child every day.
	+ **Several times a Week**: You read to your child a few times a week.
	+ **Sometimes**: You read to your child occasionally.
	+ **Rarely**: You rarely read to your child.
	+ **No Response**: You do not read to your child.
* **When do you usually read to your child?** (time, optional): This is the time of day when you usually read to your child. Please enter a time in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00 for 8am).
* **How do you usually read to your child?** (select_one, optional):
	+ **In the car**: You usually read to your child in the car.
	+ **At the dinner table**: You usually read to your child at the dinner table.
	+ **In bed**: You usually read to your child in bed.
	+ **During a story time**: You usually read to your child during a designated story time.
	+ **Other**: You read to your child in a different location.
* **Where do you usually read to your child?** (select_one, optional):
	+ **Home**: You usually read to your child at home.
	+ **Library**: You usually read to your child at the library.
	+ **Bookstore**: You usually read to your child at a bookstore.
	+ **Park**: You usually read to your child at a park.
	+ **Other**: You read to your child in a different location.
* **How do you usually listen to music with your child?** (select_one, optional):
	+ **At home**: You usually listen to music with your child at home.
	+ **In the car**: You usually listen to music with your child in the car.
	+ **At school**: You usually listen to music with your child at school.
	+ **Other**: You listen to music with your child in a different location.
* **How do you usually watch TV with your child?** (select_one, optional):
	+ **Together**: You usually watch TV with your child together.
	+ **Separately**: You usually watch TV separately from your child.
	+ **Other**: You watch TV with your child in a different way.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* Thank you for participating in this survey!
