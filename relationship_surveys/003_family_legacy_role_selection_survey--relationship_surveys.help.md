# Family Legacy Role Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Legacy Role Selection Survey is a tool used to identify and document the roles and responsibilities associated with preserving family legacies. It helps individuals understand their relationships with one another and their roles in maintaining family legacy.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
3. Be as detailed as possible in your answers, but avoid unnecessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member Identification** (`member-identification`, text, optional): This field asks for the identification of the family member(s) participating in this survey.
* **Role Identification** (`role-identification`, text, optional): Enter the roles or responsibilities associated with the family legacy.
* **Skill Identification** (`skill-identification`, text, optional): Describe the skills or areas of expertise relevant to preserving family legacies.
* **Role Preferences** (`role-preferences`, select_one, optional):
	+ Member of Family: You have a close relationship with family members and are willing to take on family roles.
	+ Family Historian: You are interested in documenting and preserving family history.
	+ Family Heir: You are part of a family succession plan and have a role in maintaining family legacy.
	+ Family Trustee: You are responsible for managing family assets and making decisions about their distribution.
	+ Family Archivist: You are skilled in preserving family documents and artifacts.
	+ Family Curator: You are responsible for maintaining family artifacts and heirlooms.
	+ Family Steward: You are in charge of overseeing family properties and resources.
	+ Family Protector: You are concerned about the safety and security of family members and assets.
	+ Family Legacy Champion: You are a strong advocate for family legacy and its preservation.
	+ Family Ambassador: You represent the family to the public or media.
	+ Family Leader: You are a leader within the family and make important decisions.
	+ Family Member: You are a general family member with no specific role.
* **Interested Members** (`interested-members`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Grandparent: You have a close relationship with grandparents.
	+ Parent: You have a close relationship with parents.
	+ Sibling: You have a close relationship with siblings.
	+ Spouse: You are married or in a committed relationship with a family member.
	+ Partner: You have a close relationship with a non-spousal partner.
	+ Child: You have a close relationship with children.
	+ Grandchild: You have a close relationship with grandchildren.
	+ Extended Relative: You have a close relationship with extended family members (e.g., aunts, uncles, cousins).
	+ Family Friend: You have a close relationship with a family friend.
	+ Other (please specify): You have a close relationship with someone else in the family.
* **Other Member Specify** (`other-member-specify`, text, optional): If you selected "Other (please specify)", enter the name of the family member.
* **Legacy Preservation Plan** (`legacy-preservation-plan`, text, optional): Describe your plan for preserving family legacy.
* **Preservation Plan** (`preservation-plan`, text, optional): Enter your preservation plan.
* **Preservation Plan Description** (`preservation-plan-description`, text, optional): Describe the preservation plan in detail.
* **Preservation Plan Actions** (`preservation-plan-actions`, text, optional): List the actions you plan to take to preserve family legacy.
* **Preservation Plan Goals** (`preservation-plan-goals`, text, optional): Identify the goals of your preservation plan.
* **Preservation Plan Motivation** (`preservation-plan-motivation`, text, optional): Explain your motivation for preserving family legacy.
* **Preservation Plan Challenges** (`preservation-plan-challenges`, text, optional): Describe the challenges you face when preserving family legacy.
* **Preservation Plan Successes** (`preservation-plan-successes`, text, optional): List the successes you've achieved in preserving family legacy.
* **Preservation Plan Impact** (`preservation-plan-impact`, text, optional): Explain the impact of your preservation plan on the family.
* **Preservation Plan Timeline** (`preservation-plan-actions-timeline`, text, optional): Describe the timeline for implementing your preservation plan.
* **Preservation Plan Budget** (`preservation-plan-budget`, number, optional): Enter a budget for your preservation plan.
* **Preservation Plan Schedule** (`preservation-plan-schedule`, text, optional): Create a schedule for implementing your preservation plan.
* **Preservation Plan Communication** (`preservation-plan-communication`, text, optional): Plan for communicating with family members about the preservation plan.
* **Preservation Plan Memoization** (`preservation-plan-memoization`, text, optional): Plan for the future management of your preservation plan.
* **Preservation Plan Ownership** (`preservation-plan-ownership`, text, optional): Identify the ownership of family legacy.
* **Preservation Plan Continuity** (`preservation-plan-continuity`, text, optional): Describe the continuity of family legacy.
* **Preservation Plan Continuity Plan** (`preservation-plan-continuity-plan`, text, optional): Create a plan for ensuring the continuity of family legacy.
* **Preservation Plan Continuity Goals** (`preservation-plan-continuity-plan-goals`, text, optional): Identify the goals of your continuity plan.
* **Preservation Plan Continuity Motivation** (`preservation-plan-continuity-plan-motivation`, text, optional): Explain your motivation for the continuity plan.
