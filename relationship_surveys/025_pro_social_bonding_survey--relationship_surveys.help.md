# Pro Social Bonding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect information about your relationship with your social group. It aims to understand your attachment, participation, leadership, and overall well-being within the group.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question honestly, thinking about your relationship with your social group.
2. For numerical questions, choose the option that best represents your answer.
3. For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply.
4. For open-ended questions, provide a brief explanation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
