# couples_wedding_vision_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The couples_wedding_vision_survey form is designed to collect essential information about a couple's wedding vision, including their preferences and ideas for their special day. This form helps wedding planners and vendors understand their needs, ensuring that they can provide the best services and accommodations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide your couple's details, including your names and contact information.
2. Share your wedding budget.
3. Choose your preferred date for the wedding.
4. Select your preferred venue type (indoor or outdoor).
5. Specify the number of guests you plan to invite (50, 100, or 150).
6. Share any additional contact details that we need to know.
7. Choose the vendors you would like to work with (planners, venues, photographers, caterers, etc.).
8. Provide any additional comments or notes about your wedding vision.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Couple Details** (`Couple Details`, text, required/optional): Please enter your names and any other relevant contact information.
* **Budget** (`budget`, number, required/optional): Enter your estimated wedding budget.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, required/optional): Select the date of your wedding.
* **Venue Preferences** (`Venue Preferences`, select_one, required/optional): Choose between an indoor or outdoor venue.
* **Guest Preferences** (`Guest Preferences`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the number of guests you plan to invite (50, 100, or 150).
* **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional contact information we need to know.
* **Vendors** (`Vendors`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the vendors you would like to work with (planners, venues, photographers, caterers, etc.).
* **Comments** (`Comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your wedding vision.
