# Client Relationship Strength Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Relationship Strength Survey is designed to measure the strength of your relationship with our organization. The survey consists of several questions that cover different aspects of our partnership, from trust and communication to commitment and future prospects.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete each question carefully and honestly, taking into account your overall experience with our organization.
2. For each question, choose the option that best reflects your perception of our relationship.
3. You can add any additional comments or suggestions in the "Additional Comments" field.
4. Once you've completed the survey, click "Submit" to send it to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name or Organization** (`client_name`, `text`, **required**): Please enter your name or the name of your organization.
* **How Long Have You Been Our Client?** (`relationship_duration`, `select_one`, **required**): This question helps us understand how long you've been our client. Choose the option that best reflects your time with us.
	+ Less than 6 months: You've been with us for less than 6 months.
	+ 6-12 months: You've been with us for 6 to 12 months.
	+ 1-2 years: You've been with us for 1-2 years.
	+ 2-5 years: You've been with us for 2-5 years.
	+ 5+ years: You've been with us for 5 years or more.
* **Level of Trust in Our Organization** (`trust_level`, `select_one`, **required**): This question measures how much trust you have in our organization. Choose the option that best reflects your perception.
	+ Very high trust: You have a very high level of trust in our organization.
	+ High trust: You have a high level of trust in our organization.
	+ Moderate trust: You have a moderate level of trust in our organization.
	+ Low trust: You have a low level of trust in our organization.
	+ Very low trust: You have a very low level of trust in our organization.
* **Our Reliability and Consistency** (`reliability`, `select_one`, **required**): This question assesses our reliability and consistency. Choose the option that best reflects your experience.
	+ Excellent: We are extremely reliable and consistent.
	+ Good: We are generally reliable and consistent.
	+ Satisfactory: We meet your expectations, but not always.
	+ Fair: We sometimes struggle to meet your expectations.
	+ Poor: We frequently struggle to meet your expectations.
* **Openness to Communication** (`communication_openness`, `select_one`, **required**): This question measures how open and communicative we are as an organization. Choose the option that best reflects your experience.
	+ Very open: We are very open and communicative.
	+ Open: We are generally open and communicative.
	+ Neutral: We are not always open and communicative.
	+ Somewhat guarded: We are somewhat closed off or guarded in communication.
	+ Very guarded: We are very closed off or guarded in communication.
* **Value and Goal Alignment** (`value_alignment`, `select_one`, **required**): This question assesses whether our values and goals align with yours. Choose the option that best reflects your experience.
	+ Strongly aligned: Our values and goals are very similar to yours.
	+ Well aligned: Our values and goals are generally aligned with yours.
	+ Somewhat aligned: Our values and goals are somewhat aligned with yours.
	+ Poorly aligned: Our values and goals are not well aligned with yours.
	+ Not aligned: Our values and goals are not aligned with yours.
* **History of Conflicts or Issues** (`conflict_history`, `select_one`, **required**): This question measures the frequency and severity of conflicts or issues in our relationship. Choose the option that best reflects your experience.
	+ None: There have been no conflicts or issues.
	+ Minimal: There have been a few minor conflicts or issues.
	+ Some: There have been several conflicts or issues.
	+ Several: There have been many conflicts or issues.
	+ Significant: There have been a large number of conflicts or issues.
* **How We Handle Problems** (`problem_resolution`, `select_one`, **required**): This question assesses how we handle problems or issues that arise. Choose the option that best reflects your experience.
	+ Excellent: We handle problems very effectively.
	+ Good: We generally handle problems effectively.
	+ Adequate: We sometimes struggle to handle problems.
	+ Poor: We frequently struggle to handle problems.
	+ Very poor: We very frequently struggle to handle problems.
* **Relationship is Mutually Beneficial** (`mutual_benefit`, `select_one`, **required**): This question assesses whether our relationship is beneficial for both parties. Choose the option that best reflects your experience.
	+ Strongly agree: We strongly believe our relationship is mutually beneficial.
	+ Agree: We generally believe our relationship is mutually beneficial.
	+ Neutral: We're not sure if our relationship is mutually beneficial.
	+ Disagree: We don't believe our relationship is mutually beneficial.
	+ Strongly disagree: We strongly believe our relationship is not mutually beneficial.
* **Our Commitment to Your Success** (`commitment_level`, `select_one`, **required**): This question assesses our commitment to your success. Choose the option that best reflects your experience.
	+ Extremely committed: We are very committed to your success.
	+ Very committed: We are generally committed to your success.
	+ Committed: We are somewhat committed to your success.
	+ Somewhat committed: We are not very committed to your success.
	+ Not committed: We are not committed to your success.
* **Outlook for Future of Relationship** (`future_prospects`, `select_one`, **required**): This question assesses your outlook for the future of our relationship. Choose the option that best reflects your expectations.
	+ Very positive: We expect a very positive future for our relationship.
	+ Positive: We expect a positive future for our relationship.
	+ Neutral: We're not sure about the future of our relationship.
	+ Uncertain: We are uncertain about the future of our relationship.
	+ Negative: We expect a negative future for our relationship.
* **Strongest Aspects of Our Relationship** (`strengths_of_relationship`, `text`, **optional**): This question allows you to specify the strongest aspects of our relationship. Please provide any additional comments or feedback that might be helpful to us.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`improvement_areas`, `text`, **optional**): This question allows you to specify areas that could be improved in our relationship. Please provide any additional comments or feedback that might be helpful to us.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, **optional**): This question allows you to provide any additional comments or feedback about our relationship. Please feel free to add any relevant comments that might be helpful to us.

## Tips
* Be honest and genuine in your responses, as they will help us better understand your relationship with us.
* Take your time to complete the survey carefully.
* If you have any suggestions or comments, feel free to add them in the "Additional Comments" field.
* We appreciate your participation and feedback!
