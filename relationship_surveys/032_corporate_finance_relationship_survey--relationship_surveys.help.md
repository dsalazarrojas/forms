# Corporate Finance Relationship Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Corporate Finance Relationship Survey is a form designed to collect feedback from corporate finance customers to help us improve our service quality and collaboration. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Start by reading the introduction section, which explains the purpose of the survey.
2. Fill in the contact information section with your details.
3. Answer the service quality questions by selecting a rating from 1 to 5.
4. Provide feedback on what we can do better in the feedback section.
5. In the collaboration section, select the area where we need to improve and provide a suggestion.
6. In the follow-up section, choose how often you would like to be contacted for feedback and the channel you would like to use for feedback.
7. Finally, provide your submission details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false): This section is an introduction to the survey and explains its purpose. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **What is the main reason for this survey?** (`introduction_question`, `text`, required: true): Please provide a brief explanation for taking this survey.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required: true): Enter your name.
* **Job Title** (`contact_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter your job title (if applicable).
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: true): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: true): Enter your phone number.
* **How satisfied with your service quality?** (`rating`, `number`, required: true): Select a rating from 1 to 5 to indicate your satisfaction level with our service quality.
* **What can we do better?** (`feedback`, `text`, required: true): Provide feedback on what we can do better.
* **How effective is our collaboration?** (`collaboration`, `number`, required: true): Select a rating from 1 to 5 to indicate the effectiveness of our collaboration.
* **Which area do we need to improve?** (`collaboration_question`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the area where we need to improve.
* **What collaboration suggestion do you have?** (`collaboration_suggestion`, `text`, required: false): Provide a suggestion on how we can improve our collaboration.
* **Follow-up contact information** (`follow_up`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose how often you would like to be contacted for feedback and the channel you would like to use for feedback.
* **How often would you like to be contacted for feedback?** (`follow_up_frequency`, `select_one`, required: false): Select how often you would like to be contacted for feedback.
* **What channel would you like to use for feedback?** (`follow_up_channel`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the channel you would like to use for feedback.
* **Follow-up message** (`follow_up_message`, `text`, required: false): Provide a follow-up message if you have any.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required: true): Enter the date of your submission.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, `time`, required: true): Enter the time of your submission.
* **Submission Location** (`submission_location`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the location of your submission.
* **Submission Comments** (`submission_comment`, `text`, required: false): Provide any comments about your submission.
* **Submission IP Address** (`submission_ip`, `text`, required: false): Enter your IP address.
* **Submission User Agent** (`submission_useragent`, `text`, required: false): Enter your user agent.
* **Submission OS** (`submission_os`, `text`, required: false): Enter your operating system.
* **Submission Device** (`submission_device`, `text`, required: false): Enter your device.
* **Submission Browser** (`submission_browser`, `text`, required: false): Enter your browser.
* **Submission Country** (`submission_country`, `text`, required: false): Enter your country.
