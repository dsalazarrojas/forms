# Kissing Experience Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Kissing Experience Survey is a questionnaire designed to gather information about your experiences and preferences related to kissing. This survey aims to collect feedback on your comfort level, frequency, and preference of different types of kissing. Your responses will help us better understand your thoughts and feelings about kissing.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Introduction**: This is a note field that provides a brief introduction to the survey. You can read it before proceeding to the next section.
2. To complete the survey, simply select your answers from the provided options for each question. You can choose one or multiple options depending on the question type.
3. **Save and submit**: Once you have completed all the questions, click the "Save and Submit" button to submit your responses.
