# online_dating_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online_dating_survey form is designed to collect information about users' online dating habits and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select a response for each question.
2. Answer every question that applies to you.
3. If unsure or do not find a matching option, you may select "No" or "Other" and provide details in the "Other" field.
