# Wedding Music Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect music selection preferences for the wedding ceremony and reception.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions on each page of the form, one page at a time.
2. For each question, select the most suitable option or enter your preferred text answer.
3. Make sure to complete all questions, but do not feel required to answer all questions if they do not apply to your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Wedding Music Selection Survey (Page 1)
* **Wedding Music Selection Survey** (`text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the music or theme you have in mind for your wedding.

### 2. Wedding Music Selection (Page 2)
* **Wedding Music Selection** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select up to two options from the following:
	+ First Dance
	+ Father Daughter
	+ Father Son
	+ Grand Entrance
	+ Mother Son
	+ Mother Daughter
	+ Special Dance
	+ Wedding Party

### 3. Wedding Party (Page 3)
* **Wedding Party** (`select_one`, optional): Select one performer for the wedding party:
	+ Beyoncé
	+ Bruno Mars
	+ Elvis
	+ John
	+ Justin
	+ Maroon 5
	+ Michael Jackson
	+ One Direction
	+ The Beatles
	+ The Wedding Singer

### 4. Special Dance (Page 4)
* **Special Dance** (`text`, optional): If you have a special dance in mind that is not listed, enter a brief description of it.

### 5. First Dance (Page 5)
* **First Dance** (`select_one`, optional): Select one song for the first dance:
	+ I'm a Little Too Late
	+ I Choose You
	+ Love on Top
	+ Love Shack
	+ Something Like
	+ That's What Love
	+ The Wedding
	+ You're in Love

## Tips
* Please be as specific and detailed as possible when answering the questions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your wedding planner or coordinator.
* Remember, this form is part of the wedding planning process, and your answers will help create a memorable and enjoyable wedding experience.
