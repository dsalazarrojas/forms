# Couples Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Couples Survey is designed to collect information about the dynamics of a relationship, including communication style, conflict resolution, emotional and physical intimacy, and relationship satisfaction. This data can be used for research, analysis, or personal reflection purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
* Choose the answer that best describes your relationship.
* For questions with a scale (e.g., "emotional intimacy_scale"), select a number that reflects your level of emotional intimacy.
* For questions with multiple options (e.g., "physical_touch"), select all that apply.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate analysis.
