# Romance Reading Habits Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your romance reading habits and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred method of reading romance novels (e.g. books, audiobooks, e-books).
2. Choose your favorite romance subgenre (e.g. contemporary, historical, fantasy, paranormal).
3. Indicate how often you read romance novels.
4. Select your age group.
5. Choose your education level.
6. Select your income level.
7. Indicate your occupation.
8. Choose your employment status.
9. Select your household size.
10. Choose your income source.
11. Indicate how often you receive income.
12. Select your employment type.
13. Select your employment status.
14. (Optional) Enter the number of hours you work per week.
15. Select your education level.
16. Enter your household income.
17. Select your marital status.
18. Select your family status.
19. (Optional) Enter the number of dependents in your household.
20. Finally, let us know how you found this survey (e.g. easy to use, difficult to use, not applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your preferred method of reading romance?** (`romance_reading_habits_survey`, `select_multiple`): This question asks about your preferred method of reading romance novels, such as books, audiobooks, or e-books.
* **Favorite romance subgenre** (`favorite_romance_subgenre`, `select_one`): This question asks about your favorite subgenre of romance novels, such as contemporary, historical, fantasy, or paranormal.
* **How often do you read romance novels?** (`reading_frequency`, `select_one`): This question asks about how often you read romance novels, such as rarely, occasionally, or frequently.
* **Preferred reading format** (`preferred_reading_format`, `select_multiple`): This question asks about your preferred format for reading romance novels, such as book, audiobook, e-book, graphic novel, or comic book.
* **Additional comments** (`romance_reading_habits_survey_note`, `note`): This question provides an opportunity to add any additional comments or thoughts about your romance reading habits.
* **Age group** (`age_group`, `select_one`): This question asks about your age group, ranging from 18-24 to 65 or older.
* **Education level** (`education_level`, `select_one`): This question asks about your education level, ranging from elementary to doctoral.
* **Income level** (`income_level`, `select_one`): This question asks about your household income, ranging from less than $25,000 to $100,000 or more.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, `text`): This question asks about your occupation.
* **Employment status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`): This question asks about your employment status, such as employed, unemployed, retired, or student.
* **Employment sector** (`employment_sector`, `select_one`): This question asks about your employment sector, such as private company, public agency, self-employed, or healthcare.
* **Household size** (`household_size`, `select_one`): This question asks about the size of your household, ranging from 1 to 7 or more.
* **Income source** (`income_source`, `select_multiple`): This question asks about your income source, such as salary, investment, business, or social security.
* **How often do you receive income?** (`income_frequency`, `select_one`): This question asks about how often you receive income, such as rarely, occasionally, or frequently.
* **Employment type** (`employment_type`, `select_one`): This question asks about your employment type, such as full-time, part-time, self-employed, or student.
* **Employment status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`): This question repeats the previous question about employment status.
* **How many hours do you work per week?** (`work_hours`, `number`): This question asks about the number of hours you work per week, but this is optional.
* **Education level** (`education_level`, `select_one`): This question repeats the previous question about education level.
* **Household income** (`household_income`, `number`): This question asks about your household income.
* **Marital status** (`marital_status`, `select_one`): This question asks about your marital status, such as single, in a relationship, married, divorced, or widowed.
* **Family status** (`family_status`, `select_one`): This question asks about your family status, such as single, married with children, married without children, or widowed with children.
* **Number of dependents** (`number_of_dependents`, `select_one`): This question asks about the number of dependents in your household.
* **How did you find this survey?** (`survey_completed`, `select_one`): This question asks about your experience with this survey, such as easy to use, difficult to use, or not applicable.
