# Best Friend Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your best friend, including personal details, contact information, and preferences. The data collected will help us understand the dynamics of relationships and provide insights for research or analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your full name, including your legal title.
2. Enter a valid email address for your best friend, which will be used for contact purposes.
3. Provide your best friend's phone number, including the country code if they are outside your local region.
4. Select the relevant date that best applies to your relationship.
5. Choose the option that best describes your relationship in terms of excellence, goodness, fairness, or poor quality.
6. If applicable, provide a whole number to indicate the quantity or rating of your relationship.
7. Add any additional details about your friend that you feel are relevant.
8. Select all that apply from the options to confirm your agreement with the terms of this survey.
9. If necessary, provide an estimated value for your relationship in decimal format.
10. Enter a preferred time for any scheduled interactions or activities with your friend.
11. Finally, provide any closing comments or notes about your relationship.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (text, required): Enter your legal full name.
* **Email address** (email, required): Enter a valid email address for your best friend.
* **Phone number** (text, required): Include the country code if they are outside your local region.
* **Date** (date, required): Select the relevant date that best applies to your relationship.
* **Primary selection** (select_one, required): Choose the option that best describes your relationship in terms of excellence, goodness, fairness, or poor quality.
* **Quantity or rating** (number, optional): Provide a whole number to indicate the quantity or rating of your relationship.
* **Additional details** (text, optional): Provide any additional details about your friend that you feel are relevant.
* **Agreement** (select_multiple, required): Select all that apply to confirm your agreement with the terms of this survey.
* **Estimated value** (number, optional): Use decimal format when providing an estimated value for your relationship.
* **Preferred time** (time, optional): Enter a time if scheduling is required.
* **Final comments or notes** (text, optional): Provide any closing comments or notes about your relationship.
