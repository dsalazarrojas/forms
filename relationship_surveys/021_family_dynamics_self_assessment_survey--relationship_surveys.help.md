# Family Dynamics Self Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Dynamics Self Assessment Survey is designed to help individuals understand their family dynamics and communication style within their relationships. This survey is intended for use within families to assess how they communicate, handle conflicts, and make decisions. Its purpose is to aid in identifying areas of improvement, fostering healthy relationships, and promoting effective communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Please select the options that best describe your current relationship dynamics.
2. Choose one or more options for each section.
3. Answer honestly and accurately to ensure a comprehensive assessment.
4. Review your answers for completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Communication Style** (`communication`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select the options that describe your current communication style within your relationships.
	+ Options: 
		- Communication Style: How often you communicate with your family members.
		- Conflict Resolution: How you handle disagreements with your family.
		- Problem Solving: How you approach solving problems within your family.
		- Decision Making: How you make decisions as a family.
* **Relationships** (`relationship`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select the relationships you have with other family members.
	+ Options: 
		- Siblings: Brothers and sisters.
		- Spouses: Partners or spouses.
		- Extended Family: Grandparents, aunts, uncles, or other extended family members.
		- Other: Specify other relationships (e.g., step-siblings, adopted family members).
* **Self-Assessment** (`self_assessment`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Evaluate your current self-assessment of your communication style within your relationships.
	+ Options: 
		- Excellent: You rarely or never have communication problems in your relationships.
		- Good: You have some communication problems, but they are manageable.
		- Fair: You have some communication problems that affect your relationships.
		- Poor: You have significant communication problems in your relationships.
