# Romantic Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Romantic Survey is a tool to help individuals reflect on their romantic relationships. It's a series of questions designed to gather information about different aspects of a relationship, including communication, intimacy, trust, and conflict resolution. By answering these questions, you'll get a better understanding of your relationship dynamics.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your honest answers to the questions.
2. Select the most accurate answer for each question.
3. Review your answers to understand your relationship strengths and weaknesses.
