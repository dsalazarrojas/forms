# Dating Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Dating Questionnaire is a form designed to collect information about individuals and their relationships. It gathers data on personal details such as user information, relationship status, and compatibility levels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your User ID**: Fill in your unique identifier in the User ID field.
2. **Provide Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the Date of Birth field.
3. **Enter First Name and Last Name**: Fill in your first and last names in the First Name and Last Name fields, respectively.
4. **Select Gender**: Choose your gender from the options provided in the Gender field.
5. **Enter Email and Phone**: If applicable, provide your email and phone number in the Email and Phone fields, respectively.
6. **Enter Meeting Details**: Fill in the Date Of Meeting field if you'd like to report the date you last met your partner.
7. **Rate Compatibility**: Rate your level of compatibility with your partner from the options provided in the Level Of Compatibility field.
8. **Enter Notes**: Provide any additional comments or thoughts about the relationship in the Notes field.
9. **Enter Partner Information (Optional)**: If you're filling out the form for a partner, fill in their email and phone number in the Partner Email and Partner Phone fields, respectively.
10. **Rate Partner Compatibility**: Rate your partner's level of compatibility from the options provided in the Partner Level Of Compatibility field.
11. **Select Relationship Status**: Choose your current relationship status from the options provided in the Relationship Status field.
12. **Enter Last Contact Date (Optional)**: Fill in the date of your last contact with your partner in the Date Of Last Contact field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User ID** (`user_id`, `number`, required/optional): A unique identifier for the user.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required/optional): The date of birth of the individual.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): The first name of the individual.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): The last name of the individual.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required/optional): The gender of the individual.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): The email address of the individual or their partner.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): The phone number of the individual or their partner.
* **Date Of Meeting** (`date_of_meeting`, `date`, required/optional): The date of the meeting between the individual and their partner.
* **Level Of Compatibility** (`level_of_compatibility`, `select_one`, required/optional): A rating of the level of compatibility between the individual and their partner.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required/optional): Any additional comments or thoughts about the relationship.
* **Partner Email** (`partner_email`, `email`, required/optional): The email address of the partner.
* **Partner Phone** (`partner_phone`, `text`, required/optional): The phone number of the partner.
* **Relationship Status** (`relationship_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): The current relationship status of the individual.
* **Date Of Last Contact** (`date_of_last_contact`, `date`, required/optional): The date of the last contact with the partner.
* **Partner Level Of Compatibility** (`partner_level_of_compatibility`, `select_one`, required/optional): A rating of the level of compatibility of the partner.
