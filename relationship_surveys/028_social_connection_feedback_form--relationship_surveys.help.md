# Social Connection Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from users about their feelings of connection within our community. Your input will help us identify areas of strength and weakness, and guide our future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
* Read each question carefully and answer the ones that apply to you.
* You can select multiple answers for questions that allow it.
* Be honest in your responses.
* Use the open-ended questions to provide additional comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to our community!** (Introduction, text):
	+ This is a text field where you can say hello and introduce yourself to our community.
* **How do you feel connected** (Select Multiple):
	+ This question has four options: "I feel very well connected," "I feel somewhat connected," "I feel not very connected," and "I feel not at all connected." Choose the one that best describes your current state of mind.
* **What helps you feel connected** (Select Multiple):
	+ This question has four options: "Family," "Friends," "Community," and "Work." Select all the things that contribute to your sense of connection.
* **What hinders you from feeling connected** (Select Multiple):
	+ This question has four options: "Lack of trust," "Communication barriers," "Cultural or language differences," and "Physical or mental health issues." Choose all the things that make it harder for you to feel connected.
* **What would improve connection** (Select Multiple):
	+ This question has four options: "Better communication," "Increased community engagement," and "More social activities." Select all the things that would help you feel more connected.
* **How often do you participate** (Time):
	+ This question is about how often you engage with our community. Please use the format "X weeks/months/years" or "X times per week/month/year."
* **How satisfied are you with our community** (Select One):
	+ This question has five options: "Very satisfied," "Somewhat satisfied," "Neutral," "Somewhat dissatisfied," and "Very dissatisfied."
* **What do you recommend we improve** (Text):
	+ This is a text field where you can suggest any changes or improvements you think we should make to the community.
* **Is there anything else you would like to share with us?** (Note):
	+ This is an open-ended question where you can share any additional thoughts or comments.
* **Is there anything else you would like to share with us?** (Note):
	+ This is another open-ended question for you to share more thoughts or comments.
* **Would you like to be contacted?** (Select One):
	+ This question is about whether you'd like to be contacted in the future. Please select "True" or "False."
* **Email** (Email):
	+ This is a field for your email address, in case we need to contact you.
* **Phone** (Text):
	+ This is a field for your phone number, in case we need to contact you.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your responses.
* Use the open-ended questions to provide additional context and suggestions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.

Please take a few minutes to complete this form, and thank you for your participation!
