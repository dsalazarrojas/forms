# Manifestation Journey Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect information about your journey with a coach or coaching program. It's meant to gather insights on your experiences, progress, and outcomes from coaching sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and make sure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Fill in the required fields (none are marked as required in this form, so you can fill out any field you feel is relevant).
3. Provide as much detail as possible in each field, as it will help us better understand your journey.
4. When selecting options, choose the ones that best describe your experiences or progress.
5. If you're unsure about anything, feel free to ask for clarification.
