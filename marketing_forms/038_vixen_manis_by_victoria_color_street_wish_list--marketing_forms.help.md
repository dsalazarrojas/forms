# Vixen_Monis_by_victoria_color_street_wish_list - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from customers about their wishes for Vixen Monis by Victoria, a street art project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose your option for the wish list from the dropdown list.
2. Enter your name in the customer name field.
3. Enter your email address in the email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the phone field.
5. Enter your address in the address field.
6. Enter a specific item you wish to see featured on the street in the wish item field.
7. Choose a category for your wish in the category field.
8. Select a date and time for the artwork to be installed in the date and time fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **wish_list** (Yes/No, select_multiple, optional): Check all that apply to express your wish for the street art project.
* **customer_name** (text, required): Enter your name.
* **email** (email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **address** (text, optional): Enter your address.
* **wish_item** (text, optional): Enter a specific item you wish to see featured on the street.
* **wish_category** (text, optional): Choose a category for your wish.
* **date** (date, optional): Select a date for the artwork to be installed.
* **time** (time, optional): Select a time for the artwork to be installed.
