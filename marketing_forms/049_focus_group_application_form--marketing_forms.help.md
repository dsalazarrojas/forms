# Focus Group Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect demographic and interest information from individuals interested in participating in a focus group.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read each field explanation below to ensure you understand what information is required.
- Fill out the form accurately and completely.
- If you need help with any field, feel free to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address that you use to receive communications from us.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number (including area code) that we may use to contact you.
* **Birth Date** (`Birth Date`, date, optional): Please enter your birthdate in MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Location** (`Location`, text, optional): Please enter your current location or city you reside in.
* **Occupation** (`Occupation`, text, optional): Please enter your current occupation or job title.
* **Company** (`Company`, text, optional): Please enter your current or previous company name (if applicable).
* **Role** (`Role`, select_one, optional): Please select the role that best describes your job function: Sales, Marketing, HR, or Other.
* **Department** (`Department`, select_one, optional): Please select the department that best describes your work area: Sales, Marketing, HR, Other, or Finance.
* **Interests** (`Interests`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all that apply: Sports, Hobbies, Music, Reading, Traveling, Movies, Food, or Other.
* **How Discovered** (`How Discovered`, select_one, optional): Please select how you discovered this focus group opportunity: Social Media, Friends, Family, Online Ads, or Other.
* **What Brings** (`What Brings`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all that apply: Sports, Hobbies, Music, Reading, Traveling, Movies, Food, or Other.
* **Time Invested** (`Time Invested`, time, optional): Please enter the number of hours per week you are willing to invest in the focus group.
* **Favorite Food** (`Favorite Food`, text, optional): Please enter your favorite food item.
* **Favorite Music** (`Favorite Music`, text, optional): Please enter your favorite music genre or artist.
* **Favorite Movie** (`Favorite Movie`, text, optional): Please enter your favorite movie title.
* **Favorite Book** (`Favorite Book`, text, optional): Please enter your favorite book title.
