# Brand Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Brand Registration Form is used to create a comprehensive brand profile, which will be used to populate a brand directory for easier customer discovery and connection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Brand Registration Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the official name of your brand in the **Brand Name** field.
2. Optionally, provide a tagline or slogan in the **Brand Tagline** field.
3. Enter the registered legal name of your company in the **Legal Company Name** field.
4. Provide your business registration number or tax ID in the **Business Registration Number** field.
5. Select the type of business entity you represent (e.g., Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, LLC, Corporation, Nonprofit, or Other) in the **Business Type** field.
6. Choose the primary industry category of your brand from the list provided in the **Industry Category** field.
7. Enter the name of the founder or owner in the **Founder Name** field.
8. Provide the contact email of the founder or owner in the **Founder Email** field.
9. Enter the contact phone number of the founder or owner in the **Founder Phone** field.
10. Type the full business address in the **Business Address** field.
11. If applicable, provide the current website URL of your brand in the **Business Website** field.
12. List your social media accounts in the **Social Media Handles** field.
13. Enter the date the brand launched or will launch in the **Brand Launch Date** field.
14. Indicate the trademark status of your brand (Trademark Registered, Trademark Pending, Not Trademarked, or Planning to Trademark) in the **Trademark Status** field.
15. Choose whether you have a logo ready or not in the **Brand Logo** field.
16. Describe your target customers in the **Target Market** field.
17. Provide a brief description of your brand story in the **Brand Story** field.
18. Select the reason for registering your brand (New Brand Launch, Brand Acquisition, Rebrand, Legal Requirement, or Directory Listing) in the **Purpose of Registration** field.
