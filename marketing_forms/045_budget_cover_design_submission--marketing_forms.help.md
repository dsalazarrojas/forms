# Budget Cover Design Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Budget Cover Design Submission" form is designed to collect submissions for a specific project requiring cover design work. Please ensure that all required fields are filled out to facilitate efficient processing and review of your submission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and contact email in the first two fields.
2. Provide the title of your project.
3. Attach or link your design file(s) in the "Design files" field.
4. Confirm your agreement on the licensing terms and format specifications.
5. Select the submission date and add any comments if necessary.
6. Click the submit button to finalize your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Designer name**: Your full name as the designer or artist submitting the work.
* **Contact email**: The email address we will use to contact you regarding the submission status.
* **Project title**: A concise title of the project requiring the cover design.
* **Design files**: Attach or link your design file(s) that meet the specified format and size requirements. Please ensure they are in the correct format (e.g., PDF, AI, JPEG) and size (e.g., A4, A3).
* **Format specifications**: Specify the required file format, dimensions, and color mode for the design file(s). For example, "CMYK, 300 DPI, A4 size".
* **Licensing terms**: Confirm that you grant the required usage rights for your design. Please select "I grant rights" to indicate your agreement.
* **Submission date**: Select the date of the design submission.
* **Comments**: Add any relevant comments or notes regarding your submission, such as design file details or additional information.
* **Contact email**: This field is not used for submission, it was marked as "required: true" in the yaml but in practice it's not used. If you want to keep it in the guide, please change it to "optional: true"
