# Beauty Blogger Collaboration Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from beauty bloggers and influencers who are interested in collaborating with our brand. This information will help us understand your social media presence, content focus, and collaboration interests, allowing us to determine the best collaboration opportunities for you.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:
1. Fill in all required fields. These are marked with an asterisk (*) and cannot be left blank.
2. For select fields, choose the relevant options that apply to you.
3. Enter information in the correct format for numerical fields (e.g., 25-35 for age range).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Blogger/Influencer Name** (`blogger_name`, text, *): Your name as it appears on your social media profiles or your actual name.
* **Real Name** (`real_name`, text, *): Your real name as it appears on your identification documents or your passport.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, *): Your email address associated with your social media profiles or a valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, *): Your phone number as it appears on your social media profiles or a valid phone number.
* **Location/Time Zone** (`location`, text, *): Your location where you reside or your time zone.
* **Primary Platform** (`primary_platform`, select_one, *): Choose the primary social media platform where you have the most followers. This can be Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Blog, Facebook, LinkedIn, or Twitter.
* **Instagram Handle** (`instagram_handle`, text, optional): Your Instagram username if you have an Instagram presence.
* **Instagram Follower Count** (`instagram_followers`, number, optional): The number of followers you have on Instagram.
* **YouTube Channel Name** (`youtube_channel`, text, optional): Your YouTube channel name if you have a YouTube presence.
* **YouTube Subscriber Count** (`youtube_subscribers`, number, optional): The number of subscribers you have on YouTube.
* **TikTok Handle** (`tiktok_handle`, text, optional): Your TikTok username if you have a TikTok presence.
* **TikTok Follower Count** (`tiktok_followers`, number, optional): The number of followers you have on TikTok.
* **Blog URL** (`blog_url`, text, optional): Your blog URL if you have a blog presence.
* **Primary Audience Age Group** (`audience_age`, select_one, *): Choose your primary audience age group. This can be 13-18, 18-25, 25-35, 35-45, or 45+.
* **Primary Audience Gender** (`audience_gender`, select_one, *): Choose your primary audience gender. This can be Female, Male, or Mixed.
* **Primary Audience Location** (`audience_location`, text, *): Choose your primary audience location.
* **Content Specialty** (`content_specialty`, select_one, *): Choose your content focus area. This can be Makeup, Skincare, Hair Care, Nail Art, General Beauty, Lifestyle, or Fashion.
* **Average Engagement Rate** (`engagement_rate`, number, *): Enter the average engagement rate for your social media content.
* **Types of Collaboration Interested In** (`collaboration_types`, select_multiple, *): Choose the types of collaboration opportunities you are interested in. This can be Sponsored Posts, Product Reviews, Unboxing Videos, Takeovers, Tutorials, or Affiliate Marketing.
* **Rate per Post** (`rate_per_post`, number, *): Enter the rate per post that you are expecting.
* **Maximum Posting Frequency per Month** (`posting_frequency`, number, *): Enter the maximum posting frequency per month.
* **Previous Beauty Brand Collaborations** (`previous_collaborations`, text, optional): If applicable, describe any previous collaborations with beauty brands.
* **Media Kit Attached** (`media_kit`, select_one, *): Choose if you have a media kit attached.
* **Portfolio or Website Link** (`portfolio_link`, text, optional): Enter a link to your portfolio or website if applicable.
* **I Agree to the Collaboration Terms** (`terms_accepted`, select_one, *): Choose 'Yes' to agree to the collaboration terms.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately. For numerical fields, ensure the format is correct (e.g., 25-35 for age range). If you're unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to ask. This form will help us get to know you better and determine the best collaboration opportunities for you.
