# Name Our New Equipment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a new piece of equipment, allowing users to suggest a name and provide context for the equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a name for the equipment in the "Name" field.
2. Add a nickname or a brief description of the equipment in the "Nickname" field (optional).
3. Provide a reason why this equipment is necessary or relevant in the "Reason" field (optional).
4. Specify the context in which this equipment will be used in the "Context User" field (optional).
5. Provide a short description of the reasoning behind your equipment choice in the "Reasoning" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`form_name`, text, required): Enter a unique and descriptive name for the equipment.
* **Nickname** (`form_nickname`, text, optional): Add a nickname or a brief description of the equipment to help identify it.
* **Reason** (`form_reason`, text, optional): Provide a brief explanation of why this equipment is necessary or relevant.
* **Context User** (`form_context_user`, text, optional): Specify the context in which the equipment will be used, such as a specific department or team.
* **Reasoning** (`form_reasoning`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the reasoning behind your equipment choice.
