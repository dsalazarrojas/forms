# Subscription Cancellation Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather information about why customers are cancelling their subscriptions. This data will help our team understand customer needs and preferences, making it possible to improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field and make sure you understand its purpose.
2. Fill out all required fields, which are marked with a red asterisk (*) in the form.
3. Choose at least one reason for cancellation from the 'Reason for Cancellation' dropdown menu.
4. Be as detailed as possible in your responses, especially in the 'Customer Cancelled', 'Customer Recommendation', and 'Cancellation Details' fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Cancelled**: Please type in your cancellation reason as a short text description. This helps us understand what led to your cancellation decision.
* **Reason for Cancellation**: Select one of the provided options that best describes why you are cancelling your subscription.
	+ Monthly Subscription: You are cancelling due to changes in your monthly plan or billing.
	+ Yearly Subscription: You are cancelling due to changes in your yearly plan or billing.
	+ Bi-annual Subscription: You are cancelling due to changes in your bi-annual plan or billing.
* **Service Improvement Area**: Select all the ways you would like to see our service improved. You can choose more than one option.
	+ Improvement Ideas: We would like to hear your suggestions or ideas to improve our service.
	+ Customer Support: You would like to see our support team more responsive or helpful.
	+ Service Quality: You would like to see improvements in our service quality, such as faster response times or more features.
* **Customer Recommendation**: Please provide any suggestions or ideas you have for how we can improve our service.
* **Cancellation Details**: Please provide any additional comments or details about your cancellation.
* **Satisfaction Level**: Rate your overall satisfaction with our service as a way to help us understand your experience.
	+ Very Satisfied: You are extremely satisfied with our service.
	+ Satisfied: You are satisfied with our service.
	+ Neutral: You are neutral, neither satisfied nor dissatisfied.
	+ Not Satisfied: You are not satisfied with our service.
	+ Very Dissatisfied: You are extremely dissatisfied with our service.
* **Cancellation Date**: Please enter the date you are cancelling your subscription.
* **Cancellation Time**: Please enter the time you are cancelling your subscription.

## Tips
* Please be honest in your responses, as this information will help us improve our service.
* If you have already submitted feedback, you can disregard this form.
* If you are currently in the process of renewing or reactivating your subscription, please do not complete this form.
