# Color Street Current Inventory - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Color Street Current Inventory form is used to display the current stock status of a product. It provides an overview of the product's details, including the owner name, internal identifier (SKU or product code), product name, quantity on hand, reorder threshold, storage location, last restock date, supplier, and notes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Log in to your account to access the Color Street Current Inventory form.
2. Click on the form to start filling it out.
3. Fill out each field with the required information.
4. Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner name** (`owner_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the account or stylist who owns the product.
* **SKU or product code** (`sku`, text, required): Enter the internal identifier for the product.
* **Product name** (`product_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the product.
* **Quantity on hand** (`quantity_on_hand`, number, required): Enter the current quantity of the product in stock.
* **Reorder threshold** (`reorder_threshold`, number, optional): Enter the minimum desired stock level for the product. (Leave blank if not applicable)
* **Storage location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the warehouse or shelf location where the product is stored. (Leave blank if not applicable)
* **Last restock date** (`last_restock_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the last restocking event. (Leave blank if not applicable)
* **Supplier** (`supplier`, text, optional): Enter the name of the supplier or vendor. (Leave blank if not applicable)
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the product's inventory status. (Leave blank if not applicable)

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information entered to avoid errors.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your account manager or stylist for assistance.
