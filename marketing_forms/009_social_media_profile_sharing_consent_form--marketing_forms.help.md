# social_media_profile_sharing_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a user's consent for sharing their social media profile information. It is an optional form and is used to collect user input on their social media profiles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are willing to share your social media profile information.
2. Choose which social media profiles you are willing to share your information on. Select from the options:
	* Facebook
	* Twitter
	* Instagram
	* LinkedIn
	* Other (please specify)
3. Enter your email address if you are willing to share it.
4. Enter your phone number if you are willing to share it.
5. If you are willing to share your date of birth, please enter it in the format "mm/dd/yyyy" (e.g., 01/01/1990).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **user_input** (Select): This is where you indicate whether you are willing to share your social media profile information. Select "Yes" to allow us to share your information, "No" to decline.
* **profile_type** (Select): This is where you choose which social media profiles you are willing to share your information on. Select from the options:
	+ Facebook
	+ Twitter
	+ Instagram
	+ LinkedIn
	+ Other (please specify)
* **email** (Email): If you are willing to share your email address, enter it here. This will only be shared with the selected social media profiles.
* **phone** (Text): If you are willing to share your phone number, enter it here. This will only be shared with the selected social media profiles.
* **date_of_birth** (Date): If you are willing to share your date of birth, enter it in the format "mm/dd/yyyy" (e.g., 01/01/1990).

## Tips
* Please be aware that sharing your social media profile information will allow us to share your information with the selected social media profiles.
* If you have any questions or concerns about this form, feel free to contact us at [insert contact info].
* Remember to review our privacy policy to understand how we handle your personal data.
