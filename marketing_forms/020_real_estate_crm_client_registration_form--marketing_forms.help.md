# real_estate_crm_client_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect client registration information for real estate CRM system. It's intended to onboard new clients in the real estate industry, including property investors, agents, or agencies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field, following the format `example@email.com`.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your Business Name in the "Business Name" field (for agents or agencies).
6. Enter your Address in the "Address" field.
7. Enter the Location of your business in the "Location" field.
8. Select your Agency Type in the "Agency Type" field (Residential, Commercial, or Other).
9. Enter your Agency's Address in the "Agency Address" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text): A required field for your personal first name.
* **Last Name** (Text): A required field for your personal last name.
* **Email** (Email): A required field for your professional email address.
* **Phone** (Text): A required field for your contact phone number.
* **Business Name** (Text): A required field for agents or agencies, indicating your business name.
* **Address** (Text): An optional field for your business or office address.
* **Location** (Text): An optional field for the location of your business or office.
* **Agency Name** (Text): A required field for agents or agencies, indicating your agency name.
* **Agency Type** (Select One): A required field to select your agency type (Residential, Commercial, or Other).
* **Agency Address** (Text): An optional field for your agency's address.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
- Use a valid email address and phone number for professional communication.
- If you're an agent or agency, ensure that your business name and agency type are accurate and up-to-date.
- If you're unsure about any field, please ask your project lead or relevant team for clarification.
