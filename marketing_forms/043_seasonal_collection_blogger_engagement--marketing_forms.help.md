# seasonal-collection-blogger-engagement - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is for bloggers to express their interest in collaborating with our seasonal collection. It will help us understand their engagement level with our content and identify potential partners for future collaborations. Please ensure you have reviewed the form carefully before filling it out, as this will save time for both you and our team.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form completely**, including all required fields.
2. **Ensure that you have the correct information** for each field:
	* Blog name
	* Blog URL
	* Blog username
	* Blog email
	* Phone number
	* Social media accounts (select the platforms you are active on)
	* Blog platform (select the platform your blog is hosted on)
	* Whether you prefer outreach or not (for future collaboration opportunities)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **seasonal collection_blogger_engagement**:
	+ A descriptive title for your blog.
* **blog_name**:
	+ The name of your blog, as it appears on the internet.
* **blog-url**:
	+ The URL or website address of your blog.
* **blog_username**:
	+ The username or handle you use on your blog.
* **blog-email**:
	+ The email address associated with your blog or account.
* **blog_phone**:
	+ A phone number to contact you, if you prefer it.
* **social_media**:
	+ Select the social media platforms you are active on, such as Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, or Pinterest. This will help us understand your reach and potential audience.
* **blog_description**:
	+ A brief note about your blog and what you do.
* **blog_website**:
	+ An optional field to provide a website for your blog or social media presence.
* **blog_platform**:
	+ Select the platform where your blog is hosted, such as YouTube, TikTok, Twitch, Vimeo, or Instagram.
* **outreach_preferences**:
	+ Indicate whether you are interested in future outreach and collaboration opportunities from us.
	+ If you are not interested, please explain why in the **blog-description** field.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately to avoid any delays in our review process.
* Be as detailed as possible with your blog and social media information.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to reach out to us for guidance.
* If you are not interested in future collaborations, please let us know so we can prioritize our outreach efforts.
