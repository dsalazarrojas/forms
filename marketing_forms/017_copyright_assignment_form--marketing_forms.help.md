# Copyright Assignment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Copyright Assignment Form is a document used to assign ownership of a work to a specific individual or entity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the field labeled "name".
2. Enter your Email Address in the field labeled "Email Address".
3. Provide a brief description of the work in the field labeled "Work Description".
4. Indicate whether the work is a copyright assignment by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the field labeled "Copyright Holder".
5. Enter the date the work was completed in the field labeled "Work Date" and the time it was completed in the field labeled "Work Time".
6. Select "Yes" or "No" for the field labeled "Work License" to indicate whether the work is licensed or not.
7. Choose the country where the work was created in the field labeled "Work Country".
8. If necessary, provide any additional terms or agreements related to the work in the field labeled "Agreement Terms".
9. If you have submitted this form, enter the date in the field labeled "Form Submitted".
10. If you have signed an agreement, enter the date in the field labeled "Agreement Signed".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name** (`name`, text, optional): Fill in your name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your Email Address.
* **Work Description** (`work_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the work.
* **Copyright Holder** (`copyright_holder`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether the work is a copyright assignment by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Work Date** (`work_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the work was completed.
* **Work Time** (`work_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the work was completed.
* **Work License** (`work_license`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if the work is licensed, "No" otherwise.
* **Work Country** (`work_country`, select_one, optional): Choose the country where the work was created.
* **Agreement Terms** (`agreement_terms`, note, optional): Provide any additional terms or agreements related to the work.
* **Form Submitted** (`form_submitted`, date, optional): If you have submitted this form, enter the date.
* **Agreement Signed** (`agreement_signed`, date, optional): If you have signed an agreement, enter the date.
