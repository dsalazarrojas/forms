## reputation-management-client-order-form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The reputation-management-client-order-form is a tool used to collect information from clients for reputation management purposes. This form helps gather essential details about the client and their contact and order information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Client Info** (`client_info`): Enter the client's name, company name, or other identifying information.
2. **Contact Details** (`contact_details`): Fill in the client's contact person's name, email address, and phone number.
3. **Order Details** (`order_details`): Provide any additional order-specific information that is not already covered in the first two fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Info** (`client_info`):
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Description: This field is used to enter the client's name, company name, or other identifying information that will help us understand who the client is.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`):
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Description: Enter the name, email address, and phone number of the client's contact person.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`):
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Description: Provide any additional details about the order that are not already covered in the first two fields, such as order date or special instructions.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate data collection.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you need to add or modify any fields, please let us know so we can update the form accordingly.
