# promotion_partnership_application_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The promotion partnership application form is designed to collect information from potential partners for promotions and collaborations. This form helps us understand your interests and needs, ensuring we can work together effectively and efficiently. Please only submit one application per partner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the form title, which should be the name of your promotion or partnership initiative.
2. Enter your name and email address so we can get in touch with you.
3. Provide a phone number where we can reach you.
4. Describe your promotion or partnership idea, including its goals and objectives.
5. Specify the date and time you would like to discuss your proposal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Promotion Partnership Application Form**: This field is where you enter the title of your promotion or partnership initiative.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: Not required, but recommended to clearly identify your application.
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your business cards or official documents.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: Yes
* **Email**: Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
  * Type: Email
  * Required: Yes
* **Phone**: Provide a phone number where we can reach you.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: Yes
* **Description**: Explain your promotion or partnership idea, including its goals and objectives.
  * Type: Note
  * Required: Yes
* **Date**: Specify the date you would like to discuss your proposal.
  * Type: Date
  * Required: Yes
* **Time**: Specify the time you would like to discuss your proposal.
  * Type: Time
  * Required: Yes

## Tips

* Make sure to provide a clear and concise description of your proposal.
* Be specific about the goals and objectives of your promotion or partnership.
* Ensure your contact information is up-to-date and accurate.
