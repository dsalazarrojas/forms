# monthly_market_update_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The monthly_market_update_request form is designed to help the marketing team gather user preferences and interests for market updates. It's meant to be filled out by users who are interested in learning more about market trends, product updates, promotions, and other related topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Enter your Email address.
3. Select your preferred delivery frequency (Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly).
4. Choose the topics you'd like to learn more about (Market Trends, Product Updates, Promotions, or any other topic).
5. Select how you'd like to stay subscribed to our market insights (Newsletter, Email, or SMS).
6. Choose how you'd like to receive market insights (Email, SMS, or Phone call).
7. If you'd like to provide your phone number (optional).
8. Add any additional notes or comments (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`user_input`, Optional): Your name as you'd like to be referred to in our marketing communications.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, Optional): Enter your email address to receive marketing updates and insights.
* **Delivery frequency** (`delivery_frequency`, Optional): Select how often you'd like to receive market updates (Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Topic of interest** (`topic_interest`, Optional): Choose the topics you'd like to learn more about (e.g., Market Trends, Product Updates, Promotions).
* **Subscription options** (`subscribe_to`, Optional): Select how you'd like to stay subscribed to our market insights (Newsletter, Email, or SMS).
* **Delivery method** (`delivery_medium`, Optional): Choose how you'd like to receive market insights (Email, SMS, or Phone call).
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, Optional): If you'd like to receive updates via phone, enter your phone number.
* **Additional note** (`note`, Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes you'd like to share with us.
