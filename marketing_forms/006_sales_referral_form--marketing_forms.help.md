# Sales Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Referral Form is used to collect information for sales referrals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **First Name**: Enter your first name. This is a required field for your personal identification.
2. **Last Name**: Enter your last name. This is a required field for your personal identification.
3. **Email**: Enter your email address. This is a field for your contact information and will be used for future reference and follow-up. Please enter a valid email address.
4. **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number. This is a field for your contact information and will be used for follow-up communication.
5. **Sales Person**: Select the sales person who referred you to us. This field is optional, but it helps us keep track of internal referrals. Choose one of the listed sales people.
6. **Referrer**: Select the person or entity who referred you to us. This field is optional, but it helps us keep track of internal referrals. Choose one of the listed referrer options.
7. **Referral Date**: Enter the date you were referred to us. This is the date you were approached for sales purposes.
8. **Referral Time**: Enter the time you were referred to us. This is the time of the referral.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (sales_referral_form_first_name)**: Text box for entering your first name.
* **Last Name (sales_referral_form_last_name)**: Text box for entering your last name.
* **Email (sales_referral_form_email)**: Email address field for entering your contact email.
* **Phone Number (sales_referral_form_phone_number)**: Text box for entering your phone number.
* **Sales Person (sales_referral_form_sales_person)**: Select box for choosing the sales person who referred you.
* **Referrer (sales_referral_form_referrer)**: Select box for choosing the person or entity who referred you.
* **Referral Date (sales_referral_form_referral_date)**: Date field for entering the referral date.
* **Referral Time (sales_referral_form_referral_time)**: Time field for entering the referral time.
