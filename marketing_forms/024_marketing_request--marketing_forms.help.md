# marketing_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to capture marketing requests from users, allowing you to track and respond to these requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the form with your marketing request details**, including date and time you'd like assistance with.
2. **Choose the agent you'd like to be assigned to** your request from the drop-down menu.
3. **Select the type of request you're making** from the available options.
4. **Provide a brief description of your request**, so the assigned agent can better understand your needs.
5. **Choose the status of your request**, whether it's Active, Inactive, or Pending.
6. **Indicate the priority of your request** as High, Medium, or Low.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Marketing Request**: This field is where you enter a brief description of your marketing request. 
- **Date**: Enter the date you need assistance with your marketing request.
- **Time**: Enter the time you need assistance with your marketing request.
- **Assigned Agent**: Choose the agent you'd like to be assigned to your request from the drop-down menu. The available options are: Agent 1, Agent 2, and Agent 3.
- **Request Type**: Select the type of request you're making from the available options: Option 1, Option 2, and Option 3.
- **Description**: Provide a brief description of your request, so the assigned agent can better understand your needs.
- **Status**: Choose the status of your request from the drop-down menu. The available options are: Active, Inactive, and Pending.
- **Priority**: Indicate the priority of your request as High, Medium, or Low.
