# Lead Qualification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lead Qualification Form is used to gather essential information about potential leads, enabling the sales team to effectively evaluate and manage their progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the title of the lead in the "Lead Title" field.
2.  Select the lead's status from the "Lead Status" dropdown menu.
3.  Choose the lead's category from the "Lead Category" dropdown menu.
4.  Indicate the lead's priority by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the "Lead Priority" dropdown menu.
5.  Provide a brief description of the lead in the "Lead Description" field.
6.  Enter the contact person's name in the "Lead Contact Person" field.
7.  Enter the lead's company name in the "Lead Company" field.
8.  Enter the lead's email address in the "Lead Email" field.
9.  Enter the lead's phone number in the "Lead Phone" field.
10. Enter the lead's address in the "Lead Address" field.
11. Enter the date and time of the lead creation in the "Lead Date Of Lead" and "Lead Time Of Lead" fields, respectively.
12. If the priority date is different from the lead creation date, enter it in the "Lead Priority Date" field.
13. Enter the next action or task in the "Lead Next Action" field.
14. Enter any additional comments or notes about the lead in the "Lead Comments" field.
15. Select the sales person assigned to the lead from the "Lead Sales Person" field.
16. Enter any status updates about the lead in the "Lead Status Update" field.
17. Enter the lead's assigned tool in the "Lead Assigned Tool" field.
18. Enter the lead's assigned user in the "Lead Assigned User" field.
19. Enter the lead's ID in the "Lead ID" field.
20. Indicate if the lead was sourced from an external source by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the "Lead Lead Source" dropdown menu, and if so, describe the source in the "Lead Lead Source Other" field.
21. Enter any additional comments or notes about the lead in the "Lead Comments" field.
22. Finally, click "Submit" to save the lead information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lead Title**: This field is where you enter the title of the lead. It's a required field.
* **Lead Status**: Choose the current status of the lead (Active or Inactive).
* **Lead Category**: Select the category of the lead (Yes or No).
* **Lead Priority**: Indicate the priority level of the lead by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Lead Description**: Enter a brief description of the lead.
* **Lead Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person in charge of the lead.
* **Lead Company**: Enter the name of the lead's company.
* **Lead Email**: Enter the lead's email address.
* **Lead Phone**: Enter the lead's phone number.
* **Lead Address**: Enter the lead's address.
* **Lead Date Of Lead**: Enter the date the lead was created.
* **Lead Time Of Lead**: Enter the time the lead was created.
* **Lead Priority Date**: If different from the lead creation date, enter the priority date.
* **Lead Next Action**: Enter the next action or task to be performed on the lead.
* **Lead Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the lead.
* **Lead Sales Person**: Select the sales person assigned to the lead.
* **Lead Status Update**: Enter any status updates about the lead.
* **Lead Assigned Tool**: Enter the tool assigned to the lead.
* **Lead Assigned User**: Enter the user assigned to the lead.
* **Lead ID**: Enter the lead's ID.
* **Lead Lead Source**: If the lead was sourced from an external source, indicate by selecting "Yes" or "No".
*   If "Yes", enter the source in the **Lead Lead Source Other** field.
*   Provide any additional comments or notes about the lead in the **Lead Comments** field.

**Tips**

* Be thorough when filling out the form to ensure accurate information.
* Use the fields to capture all relevant details about the lead.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask your supervisor or team lead for clarification.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed.
* This form is an essential tool for effective lead management, so use it consistently and accurately.
