# Market Research Tool Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Market Research Tool Requisition Form is used to collect market research tool requisitions from various departments within the company. It helps to centralize the process of requesting and tracking market research tools.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Make sure you have all the necessary information before filling out the form.
2. Select the tool you are requesting from the 'Tool Name' field.
3. Provide a detailed description of the tool you are requesting in the 'Description' field.
4. If you have a preferred vendor or provider for the tool, select it from the 'Assigned Tool' field.
5. Choose the category for the tool from the 'Category' field.
6. If you have any comments or additional information, please include them in the 'Comments' field.
7. Finally, make sure to fill out the 'Submitted By' field with your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_id** (`form_id`, `number`, not required): This field is an identifier for the form.
* **tool_name** (`tool_name`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the market research tool you are requesting.
* **description** (`description`, `text`, not required): Provide a detailed description of the tool you are requesting.
* **assigned_tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, not required): If you have a preferred vendor or provider for the tool, select it from the dropdown list.
* **category** (`category`, `select_multiple`, not required): Choose the category for the tool from the dropdown list.
* **submitted_at** (`submitted_at`, `date`, not required): Enter the date you submitted the form (this will be populated automatically).
* **status** (`status`, `text`, not required): Enter the status of the tool request (this will be populated automatically).
* **submitted_by** (`submitted_by`, `email`, not required): Enter your email address for tracking purposes.
* **phone_number** (`phone_number`, `text`, not required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **comments** (`comments`, `text`, not required): Add any additional comments or information about the tool request.
* **submitted_date** (`submitted_date`, `date`, not required): This field will be populated automatically.
* **due_date** (`due_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the due date for the tool request.
* **priority** (`priority`, `number`, not required): Enter the priority level of the tool request (this will be populated automatically).
