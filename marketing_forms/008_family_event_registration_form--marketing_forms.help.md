# Family Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Event Registration Form is used to collect information about an upcoming family event, including details such as event date, time, location, and ticket information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information about the event.
2. Make sure to include all necessary details to ensure that the event organizers can properly plan and prepare for the event.
3. Double-check your input before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details**: Enter a brief description of the event, including details such as event type, description, and any additional information that would be helpful for event organizers.
* **Ticket Information**: Enter information about event tickets, including ticket type, price, and availability.
* **Registrant Information**: Enter registrant name and contact information, including phone number and email.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your own contact information, including phone number and email, to ensure that event organizers can reach you with any further questions or updates.
* **Event Date**: Select the date of the event from the calendar.
* **Event Time**: Select the time of the event from the available time slots.
* **Event Organizer's Contact Information (optional)**: Enter your name and contact information if you are the event organizer or have a role in the event planning process.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure that event organizers can properly plan and prepare for the event.
* Double-check your input before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any information, do not hesitate to contact event organizers for clarification.
