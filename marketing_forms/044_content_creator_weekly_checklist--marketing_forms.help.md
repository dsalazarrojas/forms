# Content Creator Weekly Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help content creators keep track of their weekly content planning and creation activities. It's a tool to ensure that all necessary information is up-to-date and accurate, and to facilitate collaboration among team members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields: `creator_name`, `week_start_date`, `content_item_1_title`, `content_item_1_status`, `content_item_1_publish_date`, `content_item_1_platform`, `checklist_completed`, `signature_confirmation`, and `privacy_agreement`.
2. Provide optional information for `content_item_2_title` if applicable.
3. Confirm that the information provided is accurate by checking `checklist_completed` and `signature_confirmation`.
4. Date and time stamp the `checklist_submission_date` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Creator Name** (`creator_name`, `text`, required): Please fill in your full name as the content creator.
* **Week Start Date** (`week_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the start date of the week for which you are providing this checklist.
* **Content Item 1 Title** (`content_item_1_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the first content item for this week.
* **Content Item 1 Status** (`content_item_1_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of the first content item from the options: Planned, In Progress, Completed, Published, or Delayed.
* **Content Item 1 Publish Date** (`content_item_1_publish_date`, `date`, required): Enter the publish date of the first content item.
* **Content Item 1 Platform** (`content_item_1_platform`, `select_one`, required): Select the platform for which the first content item is intended (e.g. Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Twitter, LinkedIn, Facebook, or Other).
* **Collaborators and Confirmation** (`section_collaborators_confirmation`, `note`, required): Provide details for any collaborators involved in the content creation for this week.
* **Names of Collaborators for the Week** (`collaborator_names`, `text`, optional): List the names of collaborators involved in this week's content creation.
* **Content Item 2 Title (if applicable)** (`content_item_2_title`, `text`, optional): Provide the title of any additional content item created this week (if applicable).
* **Confirmation** (`section_confirmation`, `note`, required): Confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete.
* **Checklist Completed?** (`checklist_completed`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that the checklist is complete and accurate.
* **I confirm that the information provided is accurate** (`signature_confirmation`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that the information provided is accurate and true.
* **I have read and agree to the privacy policy regarding my data** (`privacy_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you have read and agree to the privacy policy regarding your data.
* **Date of Checklist Submission** (`checklist_submission_date`, `date`, required): Date and time stamp the date and time of submission.

Please complete the form carefully and accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, contact your supervisor or the content team.
