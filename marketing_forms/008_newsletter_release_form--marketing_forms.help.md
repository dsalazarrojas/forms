# Newsletter Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Newsletter Release Form" is a tool used to release newsletters and track their publication status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have a valid reason for completing this form, as it is not a required form for every user. Review your tasks or discussions for relevant information on the form's purpose.
2. Check that you are not duplicating your work or effort by searching the existing forms or database.
3. Complete the following fields:

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Publish Date** (<type> date, <required> false):
This field is used to indicate the date on which the newsletter was published. Choose a date from the calendar to select the publish date.
- **Approval Date** (<type> date, <required> false):
This field is used to indicate the date on which the newsletter was approved. Choose a date from the calendar to select the approval date.
- **Published Channel** (<type> select_multiple, <required> false):
Select the channels where the newsletter was published, such as email, website, or social media. Choose from the list of options provided.
- **Approval Level** (<type> select_multiple, <required> false):
Select the approval level for the newsletter, such as High or Low. Choose from the list of options provided.
- **Notes** (<type> note, <required> false):
Enter any additional comments or notes about the newsletter.
- **Issue Number** (<type> number, <required> false):
Enter the specific issue number of the newsletter, if applicable.
- **Issue Title** (<type> text, <required> false):
Enter the title of the specific issue of the newsletter, if applicable.

## Tips
- Make sure to accurately select the correct publish and approval dates.
- Clearly select all applicable channels and approval levels.
- Use the 'Notes' field to add any necessary context or details.
- If you are unsure, consult with your team or team leader for guidance.
- Remember that not all forms may require completion, only those specifically relevant to the task or discussion.
- Review existing forms or database to check for duplication of work or effort.
