# Content Distribution Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Content Distribution Authorization Form is a document that grants authorization for content distribution, allowing businesses and grantors to share their content through various channels. This form is designed to provide a structured process for requesting and obtaining authorization for content distribution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form correctly, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your business full name and identification number accurately.
2.  Provide the full name of the grantor (if different from the business owner).
3.  Fill out the description of the content to be distributed.
4.  Select all authorized distribution channels for the content.
5.  Provide the date when the content distribution authorization starts and ends.
6.  Confirm whether the distribution authorization is granted.
7.  Sign the privacy agreement to confirm that you have read and agreed to the data privacy terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Full Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter your full business name for identification purposes.
* **Business Identification Number** (`business_id`, text, required): Enter your business identification number as per your business registration details.
* **Grantor Full Name** (`grantor_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the person granting authorization, if different from the business owner.
* **Description of the Content to be Distributed** (`content_description`, text, required): Briefly describe the content to be distributed.
* **Authorized Distribution Channels** (`authorized_distribution_channels`, select_multiple): Select all the channels through which you want to distribute your content. This could include Social Media, Email, Website, SEO, Paid Search, Referral, or Other.
* **Authorization Start Date** (`distribution_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date from which the content distribution authorization starts.
* **Authorization End Date** (`distribution_end_date`, date, required): Enter the date until which the content distribution authorization is valid.
* **Distribution Authorization Granted?** (`authorization_granted`, select_one): Confirm whether the content distribution authorization is granted or not.
* **I confirm that the information provided is accurate** (`signature_confirmation`, select_one): Sign to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **I have read and agree to the privacy policy regarding my data** (`privacy_agreement`, select_one): Confirm that you have read and agree to the privacy policy regarding your data.
* **Date of Authorization Agreement** (`date_of_authorization`, date, required): Enter the date you sign this agreement, confirming your understanding of the terms and conditions.
