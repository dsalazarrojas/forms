# Find A Local Dealer - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Find A Local Dealer" form is designed to help you locate a local dealer who can provide you with the products or services you need. This form is used for customers to input their specific requirements, such as dealer name, location, and product interests, to find a suitable dealer nearby.

## How To Complete This Form
To find a local dealer, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the name of the dealer you are looking for in the "Dealer Name" field.
2.  Type in your location in the "Location" field.
3.  Select your product interests from the dropdown list in the "Product Interests" field. This will help the system narrow down the search results based on your preferences.
4.  Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field, if you want to be contacted by a dealer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Dealer Name (text)**: This field is used to input the name of the dealer you are looking for. It can be any name you know or a brand name. 
*   **Location (text)**: Enter your current location in this field so that the system can suggest dealers near you.
*   **Product Interests (select multiple)**: This field gives you options to select how you are looking for a dealer, such as "Dealers in my Area", "Dealers near me", "Dealers online", or "Other".
*   **Contact Number (text)**: Enter your contact number so that dealers can contact you directly.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all the necessary fields to get the most relevant search results.
- If you're not sure what your product interests are, you can simply select "Other" and add a brief description in the "Dealer Name" field.
- Use a valid contact number to ensure dealers can contact you effectively.
