# Beauty Marketing Collaboration Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for beauty industry professionals to apply for collaboration opportunities with your company. The form is designed to gather information about your business, marketing goals, and collaboration preferences. By filling out this form, you will be able to showcase your brand or company and connect with potential partners for marketing collaborations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Choose the correct application type (Individual, Brand, Agency, or Other)
2. Fill in your full name or brand name
3. Provide your email address
4. Enter your phone number
5. Enter your website or portfolio URL
6. Select your company type (Beauty Brand, Marketing Agency, PR Firm, Influencer Network, or Other)
7. Enter your company's founding year
8. Choose your company size (Solo, 2-5 employees, 5-20 employees, 20-50 employees, or 50+ employees)
9. Describe your primary focus areas
10. Select your primary audience demographics (age, gender, interests, etc.)
11. Enter your current audience or client base size
12. List the social media platforms you have a presence on and your follower count
13. Enter the size of your email list or newsletter subscribers
14. Estimate your monthly reach or impressions
15. Choose the types of collaboration you are interested in (Product Promotion, Brand Partnerships, Content Creation, Event Marketing, Affiliate, Custom Campaigns)
16. Describe your previous campaign experience
17. Describe your ideal partnership or client profile
18. Select your rate structure preference (Fixed Project Fee, Per-Post Rate, Performance-Based, Combination, Flexible)
19. Enter your minimum budget requirement
20. Outline your preferred contract terms (duration, deliverables, etc.)
21. Select a date for your availability to start
22. Agree to the collaboration terms

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Are You Applying As A** (`entity_type`): Choose the correct application type. This will help us understand your background and what you are looking for in a collaboration.
* **Full Name or Brand Name** (`full_name`): This is your company or brand name that will be displayed on the application.
* **Email Address** (`email`): Enter your email address where we can contact you about the collaboration.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`): Enter your phone number where we can contact you for more information.
* **Website or Portfolio** (`organization_website`): Provide your website or portfolio URL so we can review your work.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`): Enter your company name if you are applying as a brand or agency.
* **Company Type** (`company_type`): Choose the type of company you are applying as (Beauty Brand, Marketing Agency, PR Firm, Influencer Network, or Other).
* **Company Type (continued)** (`company_type`): This will help us understand your company's specialization.
* **Year Founded** (`year_founded`): Enter the year your company was founded.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`): Choose the size of your company (Solo, 2-5 employees, 5-20 employees, 20-50 employees, or 50+ employees).
* **Primary Focus Areas** (`company_focus`): Describe the main areas you focus on in your marketing efforts.
* **Primary Audience Demographics** (`primary_audience`): Select the demographics that make up your primary audience.
* **Current Audience or Client Base Size** (`audience_size`): Enter the size of your current audience or client base.
* **Social Media Presence** (`social_media_presence`): List the social media platforms you have a presence on and your follower count.
* **Email List Size or Newsletter Subscribers** (`email_list_size`): Enter the size of your email list or newsletter subscribers.
* **Estimated Monthly Reach or Impressions** (`monthly_reach`): Enter your estimated monthly reach or impressions.
* **Types of Collaboration Interested In** (`collaboration_types`): Choose the types of collaborations you are interested in (Product Promotion, Brand Partnerships, Content Creation, Event Marketing, Affiliate, Custom Campaigns).
* **Previous Beauty Campaign Experience** (`campaign_experience`): Describe your previous campaign experience.
* **Ideal Partnership or Client Profile** (`ideal_partnership`): Describe your ideal partnership or client profile.
* **Rate Structure Preference** (`rate_structure`): Choose your rate structure preference (Fixed Project Fee, Per-Post Rate, Performance-Based, Combination, Flexible).
* **Minimum Budget Requirement** (`minimum_budget`): Enter your minimum budget requirement.
* **Preferred Contract Terms** (`contract_terms`): Outline your preferred contract terms (duration, deliverables, etc.).
* **Availability to Start** (`availability_start`): Select a date for your availability to start.
* **I Agree to the Collaboration Terms** (`terms_agreement`): Select "Yes" to agree to the collaboration terms.
