# marketing_campaign_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The marketing campaign release form is a tool used to collect and document essential details about marketing campaigns. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and comprehensively to ensure that marketing campaigns are well-planned, executed, and recorded for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter a title for your marketing campaign in the **Marketing Campaign Release Form** field.
2.  Select the **Marketing Forms** category that best describes your campaign.
3.  Write a brief description of your campaign in the **Collect essential details for your marketing campaigns** field.
4.  Choose the objectives that align with your campaign goals by selecting from the **Align Objectives** dropdown list.
5.  Identify the target audiences for your campaign by selecting from the **Target Audiences** dropdown list.
6.  Select the budget for your campaign by choosing from the **Budgets** dropdown list.
7.  Optionally enter a form ID, which will be generated automatically.
8.  Assign a tool to your campaign by selecting from the **Assigned Tool** dropdown list.
9.  Finally, choose the output file format by selecting from the **Output File** dropdown list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Marketing Campaign Release Form** (`form_title`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief title for your marketing campaign.
*   **Marketing Forms** (`category`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of marketing form this campaign belongs to (e.g., Marketing, Sales, Product).
*   **Collect essential details for your marketing campaigns** (`description`, note, required/optional): Write a concise description of your campaign's objectives and goals.
*   **Align Objectives** (`align_objectives`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the objectives that align with your campaign goals (e.g., Align Objectives 1, Align Objectives 2).
*   **Target Audiences** (`target_audiences`, select_multiple, required/optional): Identify the target audiences for your campaign (e.g., Target Audience 1, Target Audience 2).
*   **Budgets** (`budgets`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the budget for your campaign (e.g., Budget 1, Budget 2).
*   **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, required/optional): Enter a unique ID for the form (optional).
*   **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the tool assigned to your campaign (e.g., chatjimmy, Other).
*   **Output File** (`output_file`, select_one, required/optional): Select the output file format for your campaign (e.g., file 1, file 2).

## Tips
When filling out this form, please ensure that you provide accurate and comprehensive information about your marketing campaign. Review each field carefully and ensure that you select the correct options to represent your campaign goals and objectives accurately. If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, please don't hesitate to reach out for support.
