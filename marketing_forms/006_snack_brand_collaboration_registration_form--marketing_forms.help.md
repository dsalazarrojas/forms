# Snack Brand Collaboration Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Snack Brand Collaboration Registration Form is designed for snack brands to provide information and preferences for collaboration with a particular platform or program. This form is intended for review and management by platform administrators.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your snack brand's information and preferences.
2. Answer the questions regarding collaboration requests and submission status.
3. Provide additional details about your submission as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Inputs** (`user_inputs`, text, optional): This field is for entering general information about the snack brand. You can fill out any additional details you'd like to provide about your company.
* **Snack Brand Inputs** (`snack_brand_inputs`, text, optional): This field is for entering specific information about your snack brand. Please provide any relevant details about your brand.
* **Collaboration Requests** (`collaboration_requests`, select multiple, optional): Please select all collaboration requests your brand is interested in. Options include `Yes`, `No`, and `Other`. If you have any other requests, please select `Other` and provide a brief description.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select one, optional): Please select the submission status you expect. Options include `Yes`, `No`, and `Other`. If you have any additional information, please provide it in the next field.
* **Submission Info** (`submission_info`, text, optional): Please provide any additional details about your submission.
* **More Submission Info** (`submission_info_2`, text, optional): This field is for providing additional information about your submission.
* **Follow up question** (`submission_status_2`, text, optional): This field is for answering a follow-up question about your submission.
* **Another follow up question** (`submission_status_3`, text, optional): This field is for answering another follow-up question about your submission.
* **More details about the submission** (`submission_info_3`, text, optional): Please provide any additional details about your submission.
* **Submission Status 3 2** (`submission_status_3_2`, text, optional): This field is for providing the status of your submission.
* **Even more details about the submission** (`submission_info_4`, text, optional): This field is for providing additional information about your submission.
* **Last follow up question** (`submission_status_4`, text, optional): This field is for answering the last follow-up question about your submission.
* **Additional information about the submission** (`submission_info_5`, text, optional): This field is for providing any additional information about your submission.
* **Final follow up question** (`submission_status_5`, text, optional): This field is for answering the final follow-up question about your submission.
* **Last additional comments or information** (`submission_info_6`, text, optional): This field is for providing any final comments or information about your submission.
* **Final submission status** (`submission_status_6`, text, optional): This field is for providing the final status of your submission.

Note: All fields are optional and you can fill them out as needed. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please contact the platform administrators.
