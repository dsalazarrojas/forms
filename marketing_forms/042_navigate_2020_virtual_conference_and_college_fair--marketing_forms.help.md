# navigate_2020_virtual_conference_and_college_fair - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for registering and submitting session proposals for the 2020 Virtual Conference and College Fair. It will be used to gather information about potential speakers and their proposals.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with your registration information. 
2. Enter your name, email address, and phone number.
3. Provide a title for your session proposal.
4. Write your session proposal in the provided text area.
5. Submit the form once you have filled out all required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter your full name as you would like it to appear in the conference materials.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional: false): Enter your email address where you can be reached for communication about your session proposal.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for communication about your session proposal.
* **Session Proposal** (`session_proposal`, `text`, required/optional: false): Provide a brief description of your session, including its title, abstract, and any relevant details.
* **Title** (`proposal_title`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter the title of your session proposal.

## Tips

* Please make sure to provide a clear and concise title and description of your session proposal.
* If you have any questions or need help with your submission, contact the conference organizers.
* Please review your proposal carefully before submitting it to ensure that it is accurate and complete.
