# nexus_device_performance_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The nexus_device_performance_report_form is a form used to report on the performance of a specific device. This form is designed to collect relevant information about the device's performance, including device selection, start and end dates, comments, and other details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the device you wish to report on using the "Device Selection" field.
2. Choose the start and end dates of the performance period you are reporting on.
3. Enter any comments or observations about the device's performance.
4. Enter your email and phone number for contact purposes.
5. Select the tool assigned to the device from the "Assigned Tool" field.
6. Enter your name and role for verification purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Device Selection**: Select the device for which you are reporting on its performance.
* **Start Date**: Choose the start date of the performance period you are reporting on.
* **End Date**: Choose the end date of the performance period you are reporting on.
* **Comments**: Enter any comments or observations about the device's performance.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool assigned to the device from the list of options.
* **User Name**: Enter your name for verification purposes.
* **User Role**: Enter your role for verification purposes.
* **Device Name**: Enter the name of the device (optional).

## Tips

* Ensure that you select the correct device and tool for accurate reporting.
* Enter your contact information for easy communication.
* Provide clear and concise comments about the device's performance.
