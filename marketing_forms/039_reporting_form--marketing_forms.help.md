# Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reporting Form is a tool used to gather data from different departments or entities for reporting and tracking purposes. It helps to collect specific information related to marketing, doctor, and reporting fields, enabling the organization to stay organized and informed.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form correctly:

1. Review the form's pages and sections to understand what information is being asked.
2. Ensure that you fill out all the required fields (marked as required).
3. Provide accurate and relevant information for each field.
4. Double-check your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reporting Form** (`reporting_form`, text, optional): This field is the main section for reporting.
* **Marketing Form** (`marketing_form`, text, optional): This field is used for marketing purposes.
* **Doctor Form** (`doctor_form`, text, optional): This field is intended for doctor-related reporting.
* **Doctor Reporting** (`doctor_reporting_form`, text, optional): Similar to the doctor form, this field is used for doctor reporting.
* **Reporting Form Fields** (`reporting_form_fields`, text, optional): This field is for reporting form fields information.
* **Marketing Forms** (`marketing_forms`, text, optional): This section is used for marketing forms.
* **Doctor Forms** (`doctor_forms`, text, optional): This field is for doctor forms.
* **Reporting Form Fields 1** (`reporting_form_fields_1`, text, optional): Another section for reporting form fields.
* **Reporting Form Fields 2** (`reporting_form_fields_2`, text, optional): Another section for reporting form fields.
* **Marketing Form 1** (`marketing_form_1`, text, optional): This field is for marketing form 1.
* **Marketing Form 2** (`marketing_form_2`, text, optional): This field is for marketing form 2.

## Tips
* Be concise and clear in your entries.
* Use the provided field hints and labels as a guide.
* If you're unsure about a field, contact the form administrators for assistance.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
