# CM Personal Shopper - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CM Personal Shopper form is used to gather information from clients about their preferences, style, and needs to provide the best possible shopping experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name in the **Client name** field.
2. Enter the client's contact phone number in the **Contact phone** field.
3. Enter the client's email address in the **Email address** field (this is optional but recommended for appointment confirmations).
4. Select the client's style preferences from the **Style preferences** field (you can choose multiple options).
5. Enter the client's clothing sizes or measurements in the **Clothing sizes** field.
6. If applicable, enter the client's estimated spending range for the session in the **Budget range** field.
7. Select a preferred **Appointment date** for the shopping session.
8. Choose the client's delivery preference from the **Delivery preferences** field.
9. If there's anything else you'd like to mention about your visit or session, enter it in the **Additional notes** field.
10. Agree to store the client's information for future sessions by checking the box for **Consent to store client information**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client name**: Enter the full name of the client.
* **Contact phone**: Enter the best number to reach the client.
* **Email address**: Enter the client's email address for appointment confirmations (optional).
* **Style preferences**: Select the style that best describes the client's taste, such as Casual, Business, or Formal.
* **Clothing sizes**: Enter the client's clothing sizes or measurements.
* **Budget range**: Enter the estimated spending range for the session (optional).
* **Appointment date**: Select a preferred date for the shopping session.
* **Delivery preferences**: Choose how the client would like to receive their items, such as In store pickup, Home delivery, or Virtual appointment.
* **Additional notes**: Enter any special requests or restrictions.
* **Consent to store client information**: Check the box to agree to store the client's information for future sessions.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Be as specific as possible when answering questions.
* If you're unsure about any field, leave it blank and let the client's stylist know.
* The form will be reviewed and used to tailor the shopping experience for the client.
