# Seasonal Offer Clarification Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to clarify details about a seasonal promotion and gather information about their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields with accurate and truthful information.
2. Provide detailed information about your seasonal promotion of interest.
3. Choose the correct status of your seasonal promotion.
4. Add any additional notes or comments about your seasonal promotion.
5. Submit the form to send us your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Seasonal Offer Clarification** (`seasonal_offer_clarification_contact_form`, text, required): This field is the main title of the form and should be filled out with the name of the seasonal promotion you are clarifying.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Please fill out your name accurately and truthfully.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address where we can contact you about your seasonal promotion.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Fill out your phone number where we can contact you about your seasonal promotion.
* **Question** (`question`, text, required): Describe your question or inquiry about the seasonal promotion.
* **Seasonal Promotion** (`seasonal_promotion`, text, required): Provide detailed information about the seasonal promotion you are clarifying.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Specify the location where the seasonal promotion will take place.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Choose the date when the seasonal promotion will start.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Choose the time when the seasonal promotion will start.
* **Seasonal Promotion Clarification Contact Form Comments** (`seasonal_promotion_clarification_contact_form_comments`, text, required): Enter any additional comments or notes about your seasonal promotion.
* **Seasonal Promotion Description** (`seasonal_promotion_description`, text, required): Describe the seasonal promotion in detail.
* **Seasonal Promotion Status** (`seasonal_promotion_status`, select_one, required): Choose the status of your seasonal promotion.
* **Seasonal Promotion Notes** (`seasonal_promotion_notes`, text, required): Add any additional notes or comments about your seasonal promotion.
* **Seasonal Promotion Image** (`seasonal_promotion_image`, text, required): Upload an image about your seasonal promotion.
* **Seasonal Promotion Video** (`seasonal_promotion_video`, text, required): Upload a video about your seasonal promotion.
* **Seasonal Promotion Description Image** (`seasonal_promotion_description_image`, text, required): Upload an image about your seasonal promotion description.

* **Seasonal Promotion Description Image URL** (`seasonal_promotion_description_image_url`, text, required): Enter the URL of your seasonal promotion description image.
