# Course Lead Offer Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture and manage course lead offer agreements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter relevant details for each field based on your current course lead situation.
3. Review your entries before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Agreement Status (\*)**: Select the status of the agreement, which can be Active, Pending, Rejected, or Cancelled.
* **Lead Offer Agreement ID (\*):** A unique identifier for the lead offer agreement.
* **Agreement Date:** The date on which the agreement is made.
* **Agreement Expires:** The date by which the agreement expires.
* **Terms:** Any additional terms or conditions of the agreement.
* **Sign:** The signature of the person making the agreement.
* **Notes:** Any additional notes or comments about the agreement.
* **Contact Information:** The contact information of the person making the agreement.
* **Lead Information:** The information about the lead, such as name, email, or phone number.
* **Course Lead:** The course being offered as part of the lead.
* **Course Lead Agreement Date:** The date on which the course lead agreement is made.
* **Course Lead Agreement Expires:** The date by which the course lead agreement expires.
* **Course Lead Agreement Terms:** Any additional terms or conditions of the course lead agreement.
* **Course Lead Agreement Signature:** The signature of the person making the course lead agreement.
* **Course Lead Agreement Notes:** Any additional notes or comments about the course lead agreement.
* **Status 2:** The status of the course lead agreement.
* **Course Lead Agreement Expires 2:** The date by which the course lead agreement 2 expires.
* **Course Lead Agreement Terms 2:** Any additional terms or conditions of the course lead agreement 2.
* **Course Lead Agreement Signature 2:** The signature of the person making the course lead agreement 2.
* **Course Lead Agreement Notes 2:** Any additional notes or comments about the course lead agreement 2.

Note: The fields marked with an asterisk (\*) are required fields. Please ensure that these fields are completed accurately to avoid any issues with the form submission.
