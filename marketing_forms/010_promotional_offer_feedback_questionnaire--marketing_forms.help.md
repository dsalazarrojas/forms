# promotional_offer_feedback_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The promotional offer feedback questionnaire is used to collect feedback from customers after an offer. This form allows customers to provide details about the promotional offer they received, including any customer details, their feedback, and other relevant information such as email address and phone number.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Scroll down to the "Offer Details" page and provide a detailed description of the promotional offer.
3. On the "Customer Details" page, select all that apply from the options provided.
4. On the "Feedback" page, provide any additional comments or thoughts about the promotional offer.
5. On the "Email" page, enter your email address (if you have one).
6. If you have a phone number, enter it on the "Phone Number" page.
7. On the "Date" page, select the date you received the promotional offer.
8. On the "Time" page, select the time you received the promotional offer.
9. If you have any additional comments or notes, enter them on the "Notes" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Offer Details** (`offer_details`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the promotional offer you received.
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply from the options provided to provide customer details.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or thoughts about the promotional offer.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you have one.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you have one.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the date you received the promotional offer.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select the time you received the promotional offer.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about the promotional offer.

## Tips

* Make sure to select all that apply from the customer details options for a more thorough customer feedback.
* If you have any additional comments or thoughts about the promotional offer, make sure to include them on the "Feedback" and "Notes" pages.
* Double-check your contact information (email and phone number) for accuracy.
* Select the correct date and time you received the promotional offer for accurate feedback.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, contact us directly.
