# Offer Building Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Offer Building Form is designed to help marketing teams create and manage offers, such as discounts, promotions, or other types of offers, within the company's internal system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the **Offer Name** field with a descriptive title for your offer.
2.  In the **Offer Description** field, provide a brief overview of the offer's details.
3.  Choose the desired **Offer Tags** for categorization and filtering.
4.  Select the **Assignee** to assign the offer to a specific person or team.
5.  Choose the **Status** to track the offer's life cycle (Draft, Published, or Archived).
6.  Select the **Visibility** setting to control who can see the offer (Public or Private).
7.  Optionally, add a **Visibility Note** to provide additional context for the visibility setting.
8.  For offers set to **Public** visibility, choose the **Publish Date** and **Publish Time** to schedule the offer for public release.
9.  Finally, add any additional **Offer Notes** and **Publish Notice** for internal reference and record-keeping.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Offer Building Form**: A text field to provide a name for your offer.
* **Offer Name**: A text field to input a descriptive title for your offer. This should be unique and clearly identify the offer.
* **Offer Description**: A text field to provide a short overview of the offer's details. 
*   *Offer Tags*: A multiple-select field to tag your offer with relevant keywords for categorization and filtering.
*   *Offer Date*: A date field to input the date of the offer.
*   *Offer Time*: A time field to input the time of the offer.
*   *Offer Notes*: A note field to add internal comments or references for the offer.
*   *Assignee*: A text field to assign the offer to a specific person or team.
*   **Status**: A select-one field to track the offer's life cycle (Draft, Published, or Archived).
*   **Visibility**: A select-one field to control who can see the offer (Public or Private).
*   **Visibility Note**: A text field to provide additional context for the visibility setting.
*   **Publish Date**: A date field to schedule the offer's public release.
*   **Publish Time**: A time field to schedule the offer's public release.
*   **Publish Notice**: A note field to add a comment for the public release.
*   **Public Notice**: A field not present in the original inputSchema, but a common field for internal notes, not to be considered here. 

## Tips
*   Use the **Offer Tags** field to ensure your offer can be easily filtered and searched within the company's systems.
*   Be cautious when assigning **Visibility**, as this will affect how many people can see and interact with the offer.
*   Use the **Publish Date** and **Publish Time** fields to schedule releases according to company policies and guidelines.
