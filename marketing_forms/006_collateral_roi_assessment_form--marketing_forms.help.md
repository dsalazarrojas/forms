# Collateral ROI Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Collateral ROI Assessment Form is designed to help evaluate the effectiveness of marketing campaigns or projects. It provides a way to measure the return on investment (ROI) of marketing collateral, such as brochures, flyers, business cards, and digital ads, by tracking various metrics like impressions, engagements, conversions, and revenue generated.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select the assessment date**: Choose the date on which you want to evaluate the ROI of the campaign.
2. **Enter campaign details**: Enter the name of the campaign or project, the name of the manager responsible for it, and their email address.
3. **Choose collateral types**: Select all the types of marketing collateral used in the campaign, such as brochures, flyers, or digital ads.
4. **Select distribution channels**: Choose how the collateral was distributed, such as email, social media, or in-person events.
5. **Enter campaign metrics**: Enter the total production cost, distribution cost, campaign duration, and other metrics like impressions, engagements, conversions, and revenue generated.
6. **Assess quality**: Evaluate the quality of the collateral and the target audience feedback.
7. **Make recommendations**: Provide suggestions for future campaigns based on your assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`): The date on which you want to evaluate the ROI of the campaign.
* **Campaign or Project Name** (`campaign_name`): The name of the campaign or project you want to evaluate.
* **Campaign Manager** (`campaign_manager`): The name of the person responsible for the campaign.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`): The email address of the campaign manager.
* **Types of Marketing Collateral Used** (`collateral_types`): Select all the types of marketing collateral used in the campaign. Examples include:
	+ Brochures
	+ Flyers
	+ Business Cards
	+ Posters
	+ Digital Ads
* **Distribution Channels** (`distribution_channels`): Choose how the collateral was distributed. Examples include:
	+ Email
	+ Social Media
	+ Direct Mail
	+ In-person Events
	+ Online Advertising
	+ Partner Distribution
* **Campaign Duration** (`campaign_duration`): The duration of the campaign in days.
* **Total Production Cost** (`total_production_cost`): The total cost of producing the collateral.
* **Distribution Cost** (`distribution_cost`): The cost of distributing the collateral.
* **Total Investment** (`total_investment`): The sum of all campaign expenses.
* **Total Impressions** (`impressions`): The number of people who saw the collateral.
* **Total Engagements** (`engagements`): The number of clicks, opens, or interactions with the collateral.
* **Leads Generated** (`leads_generated`): The number of qualified leads from the campaign.
* **Conversions** (`conversions`): The number of sales or completed actions from the campaign.
* **Revenue Generated** (`conversion_value`): The total revenue generated from the campaign.
* **Engagement Rate** (`engagement_rate`): The percentage of engagements divided by impressions.
* **Conversion Rate** (`conversion_rate`): The percentage of conversions divided by leads.
* **Cost Per Lead** (`cost_per_lead`): The total investment divided by the number of leads.
* **Return on Investment** (`roi_percentage`): The net profit divided by the investment.
* **Target Audience Feedback** (`target_audience_feedback`): Evaluate the feedback from the target audience. Select one:
	+ Very Positive
	+ Positive
	+ Neutral
	+ Negative
	+ Not Measured
* **Design and Message Quality** (`quality_assessment`): Evaluate the quality of the collateral. Select one:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Satisfactory
	+ Needs Improvement
* **Recommendations for Future Campaigns** (`future_recommendations`): Provide suggestions for future campaigns based on your assessment.
