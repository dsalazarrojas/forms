# Marketing Collateral Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request marketing collateral for various purposes. It collects information about the requester, their marketing goals, and requirements for the marketing materials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your company name in the "company" field.
2. Provide your name and email address in the "name" and "email" fields.
3. Describe the marketing collateral you need in the "description" field.
4. Specify the marketing budget for the project.
5. Select the marketing goal you are trying to achieve from the "marketing_goal" dropdown.
6. Choose the marketing channels you want to use from the "marketing_channels" dropdown.
7. Select the frequency of the marketing campaign from the "marketing_frequency" dropdown.
8. Indicate the target audience for the marketing materials from the "target_audience" dropdown.
9. Select any additional marketing channels you want to use from the "marketing_materials" dropdown.
10. (Optional) Add any additional comments or notes about the request in the "comments" field.
11. (Optional) Enter any custom information in the "custom_field" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company** (`company`, `text`, required): Enter your company's name.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief description of the marketing collateral you need.
* **Marketing Budget** (`marketing_budget`, `number`, optional): Enter the marketing budget for the project.
* **Marketing Goal** (`marketing_goal`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more marketing goals you want to achieve.
* **Marketing Channels** (`marketing_channels`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the marketing channels you want to use.
* **Marketing Frequency** (`marketing_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select the frequency of the marketing campaign.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, `select_one`, required): Select the target audience for the marketing materials.
* **Marketing Materials** (`marketing_materials`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the marketing materials you need.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person who created the request.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the request was created.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the request was last updated.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the user assigned to the project.
* **Status** (`status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the status of the project.
* **Priority** (`priority`, `select_one`, required): Select the priority of the project.
* **Notes** (`note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the request.
* **Custom Field** (`custom_field`, `text`, optional): Enter any custom information about the project.
