# debt_freedom_grant_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from applicants seeking debt freedom grants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Form Title** in the first field.
2. Fill in your **Applicant Name** in the second field.
3. Enter your **Applicant Email** in the third field.
4. Select the **Debt Type** you are seeking a grant for from the dropdown menu.
5. Enter the **Loan Amount** in the fourth field.
6. Enter your **Phone Number** in the fifth field.
7. Enter any additional **Note** about your application in the sixth field.
8. Select the **Form Category** for your application from the dropdown menu.
9. Enter the name of the **Agent** who is handling your application.
10. Enter the **Submission Date** in the date format (yyyy-mm-dd).
11. Enter the **Submission Time** in the 24-hour time format (HH:MM).
12. If you want to, enter a **Form ID** to identify the form uniquely.
13. If you want to, enter an **Output File** name to generate a file with the form data.
14. If you want to, enter the **Assigned Tool** used to generate the form.
15. If you want to, enter a **Description** of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Title** (1, text, required): Enter a title for the form that represents your application.
- **Applicant Name** (2, text, required): Enter your full name.
- **Applicant Email** (3, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
- **Debt Type** (4, select_one, required): Select the type of debt you are seeking a grant for (Credit Card Debt, Medical Debt, or Personal Loan).
- **Loan Amount** (5, number, required): Enter the amount of the loan.
- **Phone Number** (6, text, required): Enter your phone number.
- **Note** (7, note, optional): Enter any additional note about your application.
- **Form Category** (8, select_one, required): Select the category of the form (Marketing Forms or Lead Generation).
- **Agent** (9, text, required): Enter the name of the agent handling your application.
- **Submission Date** (10, date, required): Enter the date you submitted the form in the yyyy-mm-dd format.
- **Submission Time** (11, time, required): Enter the time you submitted the form in the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
- **Form ID** (12, text, optional): Enter a unique identifier for the form.
- **Output File** (13, text, optional): Enter a name for the output file.
- **Assigned Tool** (14, text, optional): Enter the tool used to generate the form.
- **Description** (15, text, optional): Enter a description of the form.
