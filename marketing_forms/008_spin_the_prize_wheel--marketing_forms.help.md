# Spin The Prize Wheel - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spin The Prize Wheel form is used to capture and store information about prize winners, including their name, contact details, and other relevant data. This form is typically used in marketing campaigns where prizes are awarded to customers or participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Spin The Prize Wheel form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
3. Provide your Phone Number if required.
4. Enter your Company Name if applicable.
5. Enter your Job Title if required.
6. Select your Department from the options provided (Marketing, Sales, Customer Service, IT, Finance, or Operations).
7. Choose your Role from the available options (Manager, Team Member, Leader, or Other).
8. Enter the date you are entering the data (if required).
9. Select the time you entered the data (if required).
10. Describe the prize you have won (Additional Info).
11. Agree to the Terms and Conditions.
12. Upload a Prize Image (if required).
13. Add the winner's information to the Winner List (if required).
14. Enter the winner's Email address (if required).
15. Enter the winner's Phone number (if required).
16. Enter the winner's Address (if required).
17. Enter the winner's City (if required).
18. Enter the winner's State (if required).
19. Enter the winner's Zip (if required).
20. Enter the winner's Country (if required).
21. Enter the assigned tool to the winner (if required).
22. Enter any other relevant information in the Form ID field (if required).
