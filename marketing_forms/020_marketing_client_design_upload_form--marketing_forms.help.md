# Marketing Client Design Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for marketing clients to submit project details, client information, and uploaded designs for review and processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Project Details" tab to provide project-related information.
2. Click on the "Client Info" tab to enter client-related details.
3. Click on the "Upload Designs" tab to upload the required design files.
4. Enter the "Email" address to contact you for any follow-up communication.
5. Click the "Submit" button to finalize the form submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Project Details (<project_details>)**: Enter a brief description of the marketing project.
  - This field is used to collect project-related information, such as project name, goals, and objectives.
  - It is a text field, so you can type in a maximum of 1000 characters.
  - It is not required, but it's recommended to provide as much detail as possible.
- **Client Info (<client_info>)**: Enter the client's name and contact information.
  - This field is used to collect client-related information, such as name, contact person, and company.
  - It is a text field, so you can type in a maximum of 1000 characters.
  - It is not required, but it's recommended to provide accurate contact information.
- **Upload Designs (<upload_designs>)**: Upload the required design files.
  - This field is used to collect uploaded design files, such as PDFs, images, or other supported file formats.
  - You can upload a maximum of 5 files at a time.
  - It is not required, but it's recommended to upload relevant design files.
- **Email (<email>)**: Enter the email address where you want to receive follow-up communication.
  - This field is used to collect the email address where you want to receive communication about your form submission.
  - It is a valid email address format.
  - It is not required, but it's recommended to provide a valid email address.
- **Submit**: Click this button to finalize the form submission.
  - This button is used to submit the form once all fields are filled out.
  - It is the final step to complete the form submission.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Use a valid email address for follow-up communication.
* Upload only relevant design files to avoid confusion.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact form@gic.mx.
