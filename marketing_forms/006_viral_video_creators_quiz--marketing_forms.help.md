# viral-video-creators-quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help viral video creators identify their target audience and plan how to use viral video content effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions and answer each question to the best of your ability. You may skip questions that are not relevant to your situation or if you're unsure about any of the questions, please try to answer based on your best understanding of the situation.

* **What is Viral Video Content: 2** (`page_2`, select_one, required): This question asks you to select the type of content you're interested in creating. Please choose between "short-videos" and "long-videos".
* **How Do You Plan To Use Viral Video Content: 3** (`page_3`, select_multiple, required): This question asks you to select the platforms where you plan to use the content you create. Please choose one or more of the options: "youtube", "tik-tok", "instagram", "facebook".
