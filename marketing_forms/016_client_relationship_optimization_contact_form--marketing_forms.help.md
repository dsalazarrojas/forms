# Client Relationship Optimization Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help your team understand and improve the client relationship. It's meant to be completed by clients, prospects, and partners to express their needs, concerns, or inquiries related to their business relationship with your company.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. For each field, provide as much detail as possible to help your team better understand your needs and concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your company's name (if applicable).
* **Type of Business Relationship** (`relationship_type`, select_one, required): Choose how your business relates to our company (e.g., Current Client, Former Client, Prospect, etc.).
* **Length of Relationship** (`relationship_length`, select_one, optional): Choose how long you've been working with our company (e.g., Less than 6 months, 6-12 months, etc.).
* **Primary Need or Inquiry** (`primary_need`, select_one, required): Choose the main reason for this contact (e.g., Service inquiry, Issue resolution, etc.).
* **Message or Details** (`message_content`, text, required): Provide as much detail as possible about your inquiry or concern.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you'd like to be contacted (e.g., Email, Phone, Video call, etc.).
* **Best Time to Contact You** (`availability`, text, optional): Provide a good time for us to contact you.
* **Current Relationship Strength** (`relationship_strength`, select_one, optional): Choose how you perceive your current relationship with our company (e.g., Very strong, Strong, etc.).
* **Service Satisfaction Level** (`service_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Choose how satisfied you are with our service (e.g., Very satisfied, Satisfied, etc.).
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): Provide any feedback on how we can improve our service.
* **Follow-up Requested** (`follow_up_requested`, select_one, optional): Choose if you'd like a follow-up interaction.

Note: Fields marked as "required" must be completed for the form to be valid.
