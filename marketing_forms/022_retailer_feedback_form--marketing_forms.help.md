# Retailer Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retailer Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with our retail partners.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information (if provided).
2. Provide a rating for your overall experience (on a scale of 1-5).
3. Type in your feedback and comments about your experience with the retail partner.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer (1)**: Enter your name and contact information so we can get in touch with you if we need to follow up.
* **Feedback (2)**: Please provide a clear and concise description of your experience, including what worked well and what didn't.
* **Rating (3)**: Select a number between 1-5 to rate your overall experience. A rating of 1 indicates a poor experience, while 5 indicates an excellent experience.
* **Comment (4)**: This is an optional field where you can provide additional comments or suggestions about your experience.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out this form for each retail partner you are providing feedback on.
* Be honest and clear in your feedback, as it will help us make informed decisions about our retail partners.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us directly so we can follow up and resolve them.
