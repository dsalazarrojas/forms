# Link Tracking Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Link Tracking Request Form is used to track links for marketing and analytics purposes. To ensure accurate tracking, please fill out the form completely and correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the "request_type" field** with the type of link you're trying to track (text, image, email, or phone).
2. **Enter the "link_url" field** with the actual link you want to track.
3. **Add a brief description of the link in the "link_description" field**.
4. **Select the "stakeholder" field** with all personnel who should have access to the link.
5. **Choose the "reporting_frequency" field** based on how often you'd like to receive tracking reports (once, daily, weekly, or monthly).
6. **Select the "stakeholder_access" field** with the level of access each stakeholder should have (read, edit).
7. **Choose whether "jotform" is enabled** for the link.
8. **Enter your "notes" field** with any additional information about the link.
9. **Enter your contact information** in the "email," "phone," "user_name," "user_email," "user_phone," "user_company," "user_position," "user_department," "user_job_title," and "user_address" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_title**: Enter a descriptive title for the link.
* **request_type**: Choose the type of link you're trying to track (text, image, email, or phone).
* **link_url**: Enter the actual link you want to track.
* **link_description**: Add a brief description of the link.
* **schedule**: Select when you'd like to receive tracking reports (once, daily, weekly, or monthly).
* **stakeholder**: Select all personnel who should have access to the link.
* **stakeholder_access**: Choose the level of access each stakeholder should have (read, edit).
* **jotform**: Choose whether to enable Jotform for the link.
* **notes**: Enter any additional information about the link.
* **email**: Enter your email address.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **user_name**: Enter your name.
* **user_email**: Enter your work email address.
* **user_phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **user_company**: Enter your company name.
* **user_position**: Enter your position title.
* **user_department**: Enter the department you work in.
* **user_job_title**: Enter your job title.
* **user_address**: Enter your physical address.
* **company**: Enter the name of your company.
* **zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **city**: Enter the city you work in.
* **state**: Enter the state you work in.
* **country**: Enter the country you work in.
