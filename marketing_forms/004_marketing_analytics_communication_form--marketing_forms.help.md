# marketing_analytics_communication_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The marketing analytics communication form is used to provide feedback and insights on marketing analytics data to marketing teams for analysis and improvement of marketing campaigns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **campaign_id** field with the ID of the relevant marketing campaign.
2. Enter **form_data** with any additional information you want to share about the campaign, such as specific questions or comments.
3. If you have any feedback, comments, or suggestions, enter **feedback**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Campaign_id** (`campaign_id`, number, required): Enter the ID of the marketing campaign you are providing feedback on.
* **Form Data** (`form_data`, text, optional): Add any additional information you want to share about the campaign, such as specific questions or comments.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Provide any feedback, comments, or suggestions you have about the campaign.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate campaign IDs to ensure the correct campaign is being analyzed.
* Be concise and clear in your form data and feedback for easy analysis.
* If you're unsure about how to complete this form, contact your marketing team or analytics team for guidance.
