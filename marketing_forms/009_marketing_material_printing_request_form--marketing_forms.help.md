# Marketing Material Printing Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect requests for marketing material printing. Fill out this form to provide us with the necessary details for printing your marketing materials.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the **Marketing Material Printing Request Form** field with the title of your printing request.
2. In the **Print Specs** field, describe the print specs for the marketing material.
3. Enter the **Quantities** of the marketing material needed.
4. Enter the **Deadline** for the printing request.
5. Choose the **Material**, **Color**, **Finish**, **Size**, **Quantity**, **Unit**, and **Shape** of the printed material from the respective select fields.
6. Enter any **Notes** about the printing request.
7. Enter your **Email** and **Phone** for contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Marketing Material Printing Request Form**: Enter the title of your printing request.
* **Print Specs**: Describe the print specs for the marketing material.
* **Quantities**: Enter the quantity of the marketing material needed.
* **Deadline**: Enter the deadline for the printing request.
* **Artwork Upload**: This field is not used in this form and may be considered for removal.
* **Select Material**: Choose the material to be printed.
	+ Select one or more from: Material A, Material B, Material C
* **Select Color**: Choose the color for the printed material.
	+ Select one or more from: Color A, Color B, Color C
* **Select Finish**: Choose the finish type for the printed material.
	+ Select one or more from: Finish A, Finish B
* **Select Size**: Choose the size of the printed material.
	+ Select one or more from: Size A, Size B
* **Select Quantity**: Choose the quantity of the printed material.
	+ Select one or more from: Quantity A, Quantity B
* **Select Unit**: Choose the unit for the printed material.
	+ Select one or more from: Unit A, Unit B
* **Select Shape**: Choose the shape of the printed material.
	+ Select one or more from: Shape A, Shape B
* **Select Material Type**: Choose the material type for the printed material.
	+ Select one or more from: Material Type A, Material Type B
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the printing request.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact information.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact information.
