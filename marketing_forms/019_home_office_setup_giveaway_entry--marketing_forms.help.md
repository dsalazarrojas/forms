> **Home Office Setup Giveaway Entry - Help Guide**
 
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your needs and preferences for setting up your home office for work purposes. Please answer the following questions to help us create a better work-from-home experience for you.
 
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information and click the "Submit" button. Make sure to provide accurate and honest information, as this will help us tailor our solutions to your needs.
 
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (1): Please enter your full name.
* **Email** (3): Enter your work email address.
* **Work From Home Needs** (4): Describe your needs while working from home (e.g., quiet space, good internet connection).
* **Preferred Work Location** (5): Choose where you prefer to work from: Home, Office, or Other (if Other, please specify).
* **How Often Do You Work From Home** (6): Select how often you work from home: Always, Occasionally, Rarely.
* **Company** (7): Enter your company name (if applicable).
* **Department** (8): Enter your department name (if applicable).
* **Phone** (9): Enter your work phone number (if applicable).
* **Job Title** (10): Enter your job title.
* **Job Function** (11): Enter your job function.
* **Date You Started Working From Home** (12): Enter the date you started working from home (if applicable).
* **Work From Home Frequency (Other)** (13): If your work-from-home frequency is not listed, describe it here.
* **How Long Do You Plan to Work From Home** (14): Select how long you plan to work from home: Less than 1 month, 1-3 months, 1-6 months, 6-12 months, More than 1 year.
* **Current Work Location** (15): Enter your current work location (if applicable).
* **Other Work Location** (16): If your current work location is not listed, describe it here.
* **How Long Do You Want to Work From Home** (17): Select how long you want to work from home: Less than 1 month, 1-3 months, 1-6 months, 6-12 months, More than 1 year.
* **Where Do You Want to Work From Home** (18): Choose where you want to work from: Home, Office, Other (if Other, please specify).
* **Preferred Commute Time** (19): Choose your preferred commute time.
* **Preferred Commute Distance** (20): Enter your preferred commute distance (if applicable).
* **Other Work From Home Needs** (21): Describe any other needs you have while working from home.
* **Why Don't You Currently Work From Home** (22): Describe why you don't currently work from home.
* **Current Work Location (2)** (23): Enter your current work location (if applicable).
* **Work From Home Needs List** (24): Select which of the following you need while working from home: Yes, No.
* **Job Title List** (25): Select which of the following you have: Yes, No.
 
Note: The fields with numbers in parentheses are the corresponding field IDs from the YAML.
