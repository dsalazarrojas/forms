# Outreach Guest Post Submission - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Outreach Guest Post Submission" form is designed for submitting guest post ideas or pitches to be featured on our blog. The form is used by our team to review and evaluate the submitted ideas.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the title of your guest post pitch.
2. Enter a brief draft of your guest post content.
3. Provide your author bio, including your name, email, and phone number.
4. Select a category for your guest post from the options provided.
5. Choose the channels where you would like your guest post to be shared.
6. Enter the date and time you would like your guest post to be published.
7. Add any additional notes or comments about your guest post.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Post Pitch**: This is the title of your guest post. It should be a clear and concise title that accurately represents the content of your post.
* **Draft**: This is a brief summary of your guest post content. Please keep it concise and to the point.
* **Author Bio**: This is a brief description of yourself, including your name, email, and phone number. This will help us know who you are and how to contact you.
* **Email**: This is your email address. Please make sure to enter a valid email address so we can contact you if we need more information.
* **Phone**: This is your phone number. If you have any phone number associated with your account, please enter it here. (Note: This field is not currently being used)
* **Category**: This is the category for your guest post. Please select one from the options provided to help us understand the topic of your post.
* **Channels**: This is where you want your guest post to be shared. Please select one or more from the options provided.
* **Date**: This is the date you would like your guest post to be published. Please enter a date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Time**: This is the time you would like your guest post to be published. Please enter a time in the format `HH:MM:SS`.
* **Note**: This is any additional information or comments you have about your guest post.
* **Select One**: This is an optional field where you can select one option from the provided options.
* **Select Multiple**: This is an optional field where you can select multiple options from the provided options.

**Note**: If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to us and we will be happy to clarify.
