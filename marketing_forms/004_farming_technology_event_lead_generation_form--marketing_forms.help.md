# Farming Technology Event Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Farming Technology Event Lead Generation Form is designed to capture event lead generation data from individuals involved in farming technology.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the source of the lead from the options provided.
2. Enter the name of the individual or company.
3. Enter the email address of the individual or company.
4. Select the job title and job function(s) relevant to the event.
5. Enter the name of the company.
6. Select whether or not to attend the event.
7. Enter the date of the event.
8. Enter the time of the event.
9. Enter the event location.
10. Add any additional event notes.
11. Select the method for follow-up contact (phone, email, chat, or other).
12. Enter the follow-up contact method other (if selected).
13. Select the frequency of follow-up (optional).
14. Select the next steps for follow-up (optional).
15. Enter the next steps other (if selected).
16. Select the method for last follow-up contact (phone, email, chat, or other).
17. Enter the last follow-up contact method other (if selected).
18. Select the next steps for last follow-up contact (optional).
19. Enter the date of last follow-up contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lead Source** (`lead_source`, `select_multiple`): Select the source of the lead from the options provided.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`): Enter the name of the individual or company.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`): Enter the email address of the individual or company.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `select_one`): Select the job title relevant to the event.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, `select_multiple`): Select the job function(s) relevant to the event.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`): Enter the name of the company.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, `select_one`): Select whether or not to attend the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`): Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, `text`): Enter the event location.
* **Event Notes** (`event_notes`, `note`): Add any additional event notes.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, `select_multiple`): Select the method for follow-up contact.
* **Follow Up Other** (`follow_up_other`, `text`): Enter the follow-up contact method other (if selected).
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, `select_multiple`): Select the next steps for follow-up (optional).
* **Next Steps Other** (`next_steps_other`, `text`): Enter the next steps other (if selected).
* **Follow-up Method** (`follow_up_method`, `select_one`): Select the method for last follow-up contact.
* **Follow Up Method Other** (`follow_up_method_other`, `text`): Enter the follow-up contact method other (if selected).
* **Follow-up Frequency** (`follow_up_frequency`, `number`): Select the frequency of follow-up (optional).
* **Follow-up Next Steps** (`follow_up_next_steps`, `select_multiple`): Select the next steps for last follow-up contact (optional).
* **Follow-up Last Contact** (`follow_up_last_contact`, `date`): Enter the date of last follow-up contact.
* **Follow-up Last Contact Method** (`follow_up_last_contact_method`, `select_one`): Select the method for last follow-up contact.
* **Follow-up Last Contact Other** (`follow_up_last_contact_other`, `text`): Enter the last follow-up contact method other (if selected).
* **Follow-up Last Contact Next Steps** (`follow_up_last_contact_next_steps`, `select_multiple`): Select the next steps for last follow-up contact (optional).
