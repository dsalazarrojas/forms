# November Warmer of the Month Pre-Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The November Warmer of the Month Pre-Order Form is used to collect contact information and preferences from customers who are interested in pre-ordering a specific product, expected to be released in the near future.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you are comfortable with the information you are providing.
2. Fill out any fields that are relevant to your needs.
3. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pre-Order Form** (`pre_order_form`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is where you can enter any additional information about your pre-order.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter your name to help us identify you as a customer.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false)
	+ Enter your email address to allow us to contact you about your pre-order.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter your address if you would like to receive updates or confirmations about your pre-order.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter your phone number if you would like to receive updates or confirmations about your pre-order.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, required: false)
	+ Select one option from the dropdown menu to indicate your preference.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Select multiple options from the dropdown menu to indicate your preferences.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false)
	+ Enter a date to indicate when you would like to pre-order the product.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false)
	+ Enter a time to indicate when you would like to pre-order the product.
* **Confirm** (no visible field in the YAML, but added as a general tip)
	+ Make sure to review and confirm your information before submitting the form.

## Tips
* Please double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us.
* We will follow up with you about your pre-order in due time.
