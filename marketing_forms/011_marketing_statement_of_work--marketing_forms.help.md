# Marketing Statement Of Work - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and track marketing projects, providing a centralized platform for capturing essential information about project scope, budget, timeline, team members, and client details.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To start, ensure you have all necessary project information ready before filling out the form.
*   Review each field carefully and provide accurate details as required.
*   If a field is not applicable to your project, you can leave it blank or mark it as "No" (for select options).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Project Scope** (`project_scope`, `text`, `Optional`): Briefly describe the project's objective and goals.
*   **Deliverables** (`deliverables`, `select_multiple`, `Optional`): List the expected outcomes or results of the project.
*   **Project Timelime** (`project_timeline`, `date`, `Optional`): Enter the project's start and end dates.
*   **Budget** (`budget`, `number`, `Optional`): Enter the project's total budget.
*   **Project Budget Breakdown** (`project_budget_breakdown`, `select_one`, `Optional`): Choose how the budget will be broken down (Client, Agency, Project).
*   **Client** (`client`, `select_one`, `Optional`): Select the client type (Client, Agency, Project).
*   **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, `Optional`): Enter the client's name.
*   **Agency** (`agency`, `text`, `Optional`): Enter the agency's name.
*   **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, `date`, `Optional`): Enter the project's start date.
*   **Project End Date** (`project_end_date`, `date`, `Optional`): Enter the project's end date.
*   **Project Notes** (`project_notes`, `note`, `Optional`): Add any additional project notes.
*   **Project Status** (`project_status`, `select_one`, `Optional`): Select the project's status (Active, Inactive, On Hold).
*   **Project Manager** (`project_manager`, `text`, `Optional`): Enter the project manager's name.
*   **Team Members** (`team_members`, `select_multiple`, `Optional`): Indicate if team members are involved in the project.
*   **Project Team Lead** (`project_team_lead`, `text`, `Optional`): Enter the project team lead's name.
*   **Project Manager Phone** (`project_manager_phone`, `text`, `Optional`): Enter the project manager's phone number.
*   **Project Manager Email** (`project_manager_email`, `text`, `Optional`): Enter the project manager's email address.
*   **Project Manager Contact** (`project_manager_contact`, `text`, `Optional`): Enter the project manager's contact details.
*   **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, `text`, `Optional`): Enter the client's phone number.
*   **Client Email** (`client_email`, `text`, `Optional`): Enter the client's email address.
*   **Client Contact** (`client_contact`, `text`, `Optional`): Enter the client's contact details.

Note: This form is not meant to be completed for every project, only for internal documentation and tracking purposes.
