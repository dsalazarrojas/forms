# what_would_you_like_to_know - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users on what they would like to know or achieve in a specific context.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your answer or feedback in the designated text boxes as needed.
2. Select the appropriate category options according to your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **what_would_you_like_to_know**: A text field for users to provide their answer or feedback.
* **category**: Select from the options provided to indicate the context or category of your response.
* **description**: This field is not explicitly described, so it's likely a catch-all for users to provide additional comments or explanations. (Required: false)
* **description_1** to **description_20**: These fields are similar to **description**, providing additional text boxes for users to provide further comments or explanations. (Required: false)
* **assigned_tool**: A select field to indicate if a tool is assigned to this form or category. (Required: false)
* **form_ids**: A text field to input specific form IDs or codes. (Required: false)
* **output_file**: A text field for outputting specific file names or IDs. (Required: false)
* **description_2** to **description_20**: More additional text fields for users to provide comments or explanations. (Required: false)

## Tips
* Make sure to understand the context and purpose of this form before filling it out.
* If you have any doubts or confusion, please contact the form creators or the relevant department.
* Be mindful of providing accurate and relevant information to help with data collection and analysis.
