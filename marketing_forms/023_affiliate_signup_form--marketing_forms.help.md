# Affiliate Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for affiliates to create an account and access our affiliate program. It collects necessary information for your account setup, marketing activities, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Personal Information**: Provide your full name, email address, and phone number to create your account.
2. **Business Information**: Enter your business name and website URL to help us understand your marketing activities.
3. **Marketing Channels**: Select your primary marketing channel and any additional channels you use.
4. **Traffic and Demographics**: Estimate your website traffic and describe your target audience.
5. **Marketing Experience**: Indicate your years of marketing experience and previous affiliate experience.
6. **Performance Metrics**: Enter your average conversion rate, commission preference, and payment expectations.
7. **Content Creation Ability**: Confirm your ability to create marketing content.
8. **Terms and Conditions**: Agree to our terms and conditions, disclosure compliance, and brand guidelines.
9. **Payment Information**: Choose your preferred payment method and provide account details.
10. **Electronic Signature**: Sign to confirm your affiliate signup.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (2, text, required): Enter your complete legal name.
* **Email Address** (3, email, required): This will be your primary email for account access and communications.
* **Phone Number** (4, text, optional): Enter your phone number for account verification.
* **Business Name** (5, text, optional): Enter your business name or company name (if applicable).
* **Website URL** (6, text, required): Enter the URL of your website or primary marketing platform.
* **Primary Marketing Channel** (8, select_one, required): Select the channel you primarily use for marketing (e.g., Social Media, Blog/Content Marketing).
* **Social Media Platforms** (9, select_multiple, required): Select any additional social media platforms you use for marketing.
* **Monthly Traffic** (10, select_one, required): Estimate your website traffic.
* **Audience Demographics** (11, text, required): Describe your target audience (age, interests, location, etc.).
* **Marketing Experience** (12, select_one, required): Indicate your years of marketing experience.
* **Previous Affiliate Experience** (13, select_one, required): Confirm your previous affiliate experience.
* **Previous Affiliate Programs** (14, text, optional): If yes, list the programs you've participated in.
* **Average Conversion Rate** (16, text, optional): Enter your typical conversion rate.
* **Typical Commission Preference** (17, select_one, required): Select your commission percentage preference.
* **Payment Expectations** (18, select_one, required): Choose how often you'd like to receive payments.
* **Marketing Approach** (19, text, required): Describe how you plan to promote our products/services.
* **Content Creation Ability** (20, select_one, required): Confirm your ability to create marketing content.
* **Terms and Conditions** (22, select_one, required): Agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Disclosure Compliance** (23, select_one, required): Agree to properly disclose affiliate relationships as required by law.
* **Brand Guidelines Compliance** (24, select_one, required): Agree to follow brand guidelines when promoting products.
* **Prohibited Practices Acknowledgment** (25, select_one, required): Understand and agree not to use spam, misleading advertising, or other prohibited practices.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (27, select_one, required): Choose your preferred payment method (e.g., Direct Deposit, PayPal).
* **Electronic Signature** (30, text, required): Sign to confirm your affiliate signup.

## Tips
* Please ensure you are eligible to use our affiliate program before submitting this form.
* Double-check your information for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact our support team.
