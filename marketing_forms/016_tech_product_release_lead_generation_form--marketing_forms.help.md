# Tech Product Release Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to generate leads for tech product releases, allowing us to better understand your interest and preferences for new products and updates.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  On the first page, select "Product Release Interest Form" and express your interest in our tech product releases.
2.  On the second page, select one or multiple audiences that match your interests.
3.  On the third page, enter a brief description of your lead details.
4.  On the fourth page, enter your email address.
5.  On the fifth page, enter your phone number.
6.  On the sixth page, enter your company name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Release Interest Form** (Page 1, tech_product_release_lead_generation_form_page_1): Select "Yes" to express your interest in our tech product releases.
* **Segmented Audiences** (Page 2, tech_product_release_lead_generation_form_page_2): Select one or multiple audiences that match your interests. For example, if you're interested in gaming products, select "Gaming" from the list.
* **Lead Details** (Page 3, tech_product_release_lead_generation_form_page_3): Briefly describe why you're interested in our tech product releases. This will help us understand your needs and preferences.
* **Email** (Page 4, tech_product_release_lead_generation_form_page_4): Enter your email address to receive updates about our latest products and releases.
* **Phone** (Page 5, tech_product_release_lead_generation_form_page_5): Enter your phone number, if you'd like to receive call reminders or follow-up updates.
* **Company Name** (Page 6, tech_product_release_lead_generation_form_page_6): Enter your company name, if applicable. This will help us better understand your organizational needs and interests. 

## Tips

* Be honest and sincere with your answers, as this will help us better serve your needs and interests.
* If you've already submitted a lead on a similar product or topic, we may contact you to confirm or gather additional information.
* We'll only contact users who have expressed genuine interest and are eligible to receive updates.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [support email address].
