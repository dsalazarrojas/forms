# Mobile Experience Waitlist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Mobile Experience Waitlist Form is designed to collect your interests and preferences regarding mobile experiences. By filling out this form, you'll help our team understand what you're looking for in a mobile experience, allowing us to better tailor our approach to meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of mobile device you're interested in.
2. Choose the operating system you currently use on your mobile device.
3. Indicate when you'd like to experience the mobile experience.
4. Provide your email address for updates and further information.
5. Enter your mobile number for further contact.
6. Select who referred you to this form (if applicable).
7. Add any additional comments about your mobile experience interest (optional).
8. Choose the version of your operating system you're currently using.
9. Select the brand of your mobile device.
10. Specify when you'd like to receive your mobile experience.
11. On a scale of 0-10, indicate how much you're interested in getting a mobile experience.
12. Provide any other information you'd like to share with us (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select the type of mobile device you're interested in**
  • `apple`/`google`: Select the type of mobile device you're interested in, whether it's an Apple or Google device.
* **Which operating system do you currently use on your mobile device?**
  • `iOS`/`android`/`windows`: Choose the operating system you're currently using on your mobile device.
* **When are you interested in experiencing mobile on your device?**
  • `date`: Indicate when you'd like to experience the mobile experience.
* **Provide your email address for updates and further information**
  • `email`: Enter your email address for us to contact you about your mobile experience interest.
* **Provide your mobile number for further contact**
  • `text`: Enter your mobile number for further contact from our team.
* **Who referred you to this form?**
  • `referral1`/`referral2`/`referral3`: Select who referred you to this form, if applicable.
* **Additional comments about your mobile experience interest**
  • `note`: Share any additional comments or thoughts about your mobile experience interest (optional).
* **Which version of your operating system are you currently using?**
  • `iOS 14`/`iOS 15`/`Android 10`/`Android 11`/`Windows 10`/`Windows 11`: Choose the version of your operating system you're currently using.
* **What brand of mobile device do you have?**
  • `Apple`/`Google`: Select the brand of your mobile device.
* **When would you like to receive your mobile experience?**
  • `date`: Indicate when you'd like to receive your mobile experience.
* **On a scale of 0-10, how much are you interested in getting a mobile experience?**
  • `number`: Rate your interest level on a scale of 0-10.
* **Any other information you'd like to share with us?**
  • `note`: Provide any other information you'd like to share with us (optional).
