# Advertising Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for collecting information from potential advertisers for marketing campaigns. It helps our team understand your goals, target audience, and campaign strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately with your contact information and required fields.
- Please select the primary objective for the campaign from the provided options.
- Enter the estimated budget for the campaign.
- If applicable, describe the target audience and preferred ad placement channels.
- If necessary, provide additional notes or instructions for your campaign.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Name**: Please enter the full name of the primary contact.
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the organization or business.
* **Email Address**: Enter the business email address for correspondence.
* **Phone Number**: If applicable, enter the direct contact phone number.
* **Advertising Goal**: Select the primary objective for the campaign (Brand Awareness, Lead Generation, Sales, or Event Promotion).
* **Budget Estimate**: Enter the estimated budget for the campaign in USD.
* **Target Audience Description**: Describe the target demographic and geography.
* **Ad Placement Preference**: Select the preferred channels or platforms for advertising (Social Media, Search Engines, Display Banners, Email Newsletter, or Podcast).
* **Campaign Start Date**: Enter the planned launch date for the campaign.
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional instructions or files to share about the campaign.

## Tips
- Ensure that you have provided all required fields.
- Double-check your information before submission.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our team.
