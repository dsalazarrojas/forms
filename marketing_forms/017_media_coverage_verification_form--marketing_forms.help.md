# Media Coverage Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify media coverage for publications. It ensures that all relevant details of a publication are accurately recorded.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you are the assigned user for the publication and that it is not a duplicate.
2. Select the correct organization or individual responsible for the publication.
3. Enter the correct date of publication.
4. Provide the URL of the publication.
5. Upload or link the publication image.
6. Choose the type of publication (e.g., Print, Online, Social Media, Television, or Other).
7. Enter the name of the publication.
8. Specify the location where the publication was distributed (e.g., local, national, international).
9. Enter the number of citations the publication received.
10. Provide any additional citation sources (optional).
11. Evaluate the impact of the publication (on a scale of 1-10).
12. Enter the frequency of the publication (on a scale of 1-10).
13. Add any notes about the publication (optional).
14. Select the user assigned to the publication from the dropdown options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Organization Name
• **Organization Name** (`Organization Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the organization responsible for the publication.

### 2. Date of Publication
• **Date of Publication** (`date_publication`, date, required): Enter the date the publication was released.

### 3. Publication URL
• **Publication URL** (`url`, text, required): Provide the URL where the publication can be accessed.

### 4. Publication Image
• **Publication Image** (`publication_image`, text, required): Upload or link the publication image.

### 5. Type of Publication
• **Type of Publication** (`publication_type`, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of publication from the options:
  • Print
  • Online
  • Social Media
  • Television
  • Other

### 6. Name of Publication
• **Name of Publication** (`publication_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the publication.

### 7. Location of Publication
• **Location of Publication** (`publication_location`, text, required): Specify the location where the publication was distributed.

### 8. Number of Citations
• **Number of Citations** (`publication_citations`, number, required): Enter the number of citations the publication received.

### 9. Citation Sources
• **Citation Sources** (`publication_citation_sources`, text, optional): Provide any additional sources for the citations.

### 10. Impact of Publication
• **Impact of Publication** (`publication_impact`, number, optional): Evaluate the impact of the publication on a scale of 1-10.

### 11. Frequency of Publication
• **Frequency of Publication** (`publication_frequency`, number, optional): Evaluate the frequency of the publication on a scale of 1-10.

### 12. Notes
• **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about the publication.

### 13. Assigned to
• **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required): Select the user assigned to the publication from the dropdown options.
