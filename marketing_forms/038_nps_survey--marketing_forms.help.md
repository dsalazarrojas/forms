# NPS Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The NPS Survey is designed to gauge customer satisfaction and gather feedback on your experience with our products or services. Your responses will help us understand what we're doing well and where we need to improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Read each page carefully and answer the questions according to your experience.
2. On the first page, you'll be asked to rate how likely you are to recommend our products or services on a scale of 0-10.
3. On the second page, you'll have the opportunity to provide more detailed feedback about your experience.
4. Answer the questions honestly, and don't worry about grammar or spelling.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **On a scale of 0-10, how likely are you to recommend our products or services?** (`question_1`, number, required: false)
	+ This question asks about your willingness to recommend our products or services to others.
* **What do you like about our products or services?** (`question_2`, text, required: false)
	+ Share your positive experiences and things you enjoy about our products or services.
* **What do you dislike about our products or services?** (`question_3`, text, required: false)
	+ Share your concerns or issues you've faced with our products or services.
* **How likely are you to recommend our competitors?** (`question_4`, number, required: false)
	+ Compare your experience with our competitors to help us understand the market landscape.
* **What would make you more likely to use our products or services?** (`question_5`, text, required: false)
	+ Suggest ways we could improve our products or services to make them more appealing to you.
* **What would make you less likely to use our products or services?** (`question_6`, text, required: false)
	+ Share any concerns or issues that might influence your decision to use our products or services.
* **Any other comments?** (`question_7`, text, required: false)
	+ Add any additional feedback or insights you'd like to share.

## Tips
- Be honest and open in your responses.
- Answer the questions based on your genuine experience.
- If you have any additional feedback, feel free to share it in the "Any other comments?" field.

Note that this form is optional, and you're not required to complete it to participate in the survey. Your input is valuable to us, and we appreciate your time and feedback.
