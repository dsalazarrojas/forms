# Media Feedback Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from users about media content.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the media content you'd like to provide feedback on.
* Fill out the form fields with your feedback.
* Submit the form to send your feedback to our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Media Title** (`media_title_label`, text, optional): Enter the title of the media content you're providing feedback on. This will help us reference the specific content you're talking about.
* **Media Rating** (`media_rating_label`, number, optional): Select a rating between 1 and 10 to indicate your level of satisfaction or engagement with the media content.
* **Media Description** (`media_description_label`, text, optional): Provide a more in-depth description of your experience with the media content, explaining your reasons for the rating you've given it.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when providing feedback, so we can effectively use your input to improve our content.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas for future content, feel free to include them in your feedback.
* Your participation in providing feedback is greatly appreciated, and we're committed to using it to make our content better for everyone.
