# Subscription Giveaway Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users interested in participating in a subscription giveaway. The purpose of this form is to gather contact and subscription details from potential participants, ensuring their entry meets the required criteria to be eligible for the giveaway.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your entry name in the "Entry Name" field.
2. Enter your valid email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select your subscription plan (Monthly, Yearly, or Lifelong) in the "Plan" field.
5. Choose the social media channel(s) you would like to use to interact with us (Facebook, TikTok, or Twitter) in the "Channel" field.
6. Enter the date and time you would like to submit your entry in the "Entry Date" and "Entry Time" fields.
7. If you have any additional comments or information about your entry, you can type it in the "Entry Note" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Entry Name (entry_name)**: Please enter the name you would like to be known by during this giveaway.
* **Email (entry_email)**: Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone (entry_phone)**: Please provide a phone number where we can reach you for further clarification or communication.
* **Plan (entry_plan)**: Select one of the subscription options: Monthly, Yearly, or Lifelong. This will determine the length of your subscription.
* **Channel (entry_channel)**: Choose the social media channel(s) you would like to use to interact with us (e.g., Facebook, TikTok, or Twitter).
* **Entry Date (entry_date)**: Enter the date you would like to submit your entry in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Entry Time (entry_time)**: Enter the time you would like to submit your entry in the format 12:HH:MM:SS.
* **Entry Note (entry_note)**: If you have any additional comments or information about your entry, please type it in this field (optional).
