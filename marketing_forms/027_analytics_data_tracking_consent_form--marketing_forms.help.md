# Analytics Data Tracking Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect visitor data for analytics and marketing purposes, such as website usage, behavior tracking, and targeted advertising. It is essential to understand that this form is for visitors who have given their explicit consent to be tracked.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and understand its purpose.
2. Select all the types of tracking you are comfortable with (e.g., Cookies, Analytics, Advertising, Behavior tracking).
3. Choose a data retention period that suits your preferences (30 days, 1 year, or indefinite).
4. Confirm that you consent to tracking by selecting "True".
5. Optionally, provide your email address for any questions or concerns.
6. Add any comments or additional information (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **-- Consent for Data Tracking --** (section_consent): This section serves as a general introduction to the form and explains the purpose of collecting visitor data.
- **Visitor name** (visitor_name): This field is optional and allows visitors to input their name for consent records, providing a more personalized touch.
- **Email address** (email): This field is also optional and is used for consent records or future contact regarding privacy inquiries.
- **Types of tracking** (tracking_types): Select all the types of tracking you are comfortable with, such as Cookies, Analytics, Advertising, or Behavior tracking.
- **Data retention period** (retention_period): Choose a retention period that suits your preferences: 30 days, 1 year, or indefinite.
- **Consent given** (consent_given): Confirm your consent to tracking by selecting "True".
- **Contact for questions** (contact_for_questions): This field is optional and provides an email address for any questions or concerns related to privacy.
- **Comments** (comments): Add any additional information or comments, if desired.
