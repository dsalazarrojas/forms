# Woo Worx Marketing Services - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a comprehensive marketing services form designed to collect detailed information from businesses seeking marketing services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form carefully and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Ensure you have all the necessary details about your business before completing this form.
3. Follow the sections and questions to gather all the required information about your business.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of your business.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, Text, Required): Enter the address of your business.
* **Business Description** (`business_description`, Text, Required): Enter a brief description of your business.
* **Business Website** (`business_website`, Text, Required): Enter your business website URL.
* **Business Telephone** (`business_telephone`, Text, Required): Enter your business telephone number.
* **Business Email** (`business_email`, Email, Required): Enter your business email address.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, Text, Required): Identify your target audience for the marketing campaign.
* **Marketing Strategy** (`marketing_strategy`, Text, Required): Describe your marketing strategy for the campaign.
* **Marketing Budget** (`marketing_budget`, Number, Required): Enter your allocated marketing budget for the campaign.
* **Marketing Objectives** (`marketing_objectives`, Text, Required): Define your marketing objectives for the campaign.
* **Marketing Channels** (`marketing_channels`, Select Multiple, Required): Select the marketing channels you plan to use for the campaign (e.g. Social Media, Email, Print Ads).
* **Marketing Media** (`marketing_media`, Select Multiple, Required): Select the marketing media you plan to use for the campaign (e.g. Facebook, Twitter, Google Ads).
* **Marketing Message** (`marketing_message`, Text, Required): Enter a clear and concise marketing message for the campaign.
* **Marketing Plan** (`marketing_plan`, Text, Required): Describe your marketing plan for the campaign.
* **Date Start** (`date_start`, Date, Required): Enter the start date of your marketing campaign.
* **Date End** (`date_end`, Date, Required): Enter the end date of your marketing campaign.
* **Time Start** (`time_start`, Time, Required): Enter the start time of your marketing campaign.
* **Time End** (`time_end`, Time, Required): Enter the end time of your marketing campaign.
* **Location** (`location`, Text, Required): Enter the location where the campaign will take place.
* **Business History** (`business_history`, Text, Optional): Enter any relevant business history or background information.
* **Business Products** (`business_products`, Text, Optional): List any products or services your business offers.
* **Business Services** (`business_services`, Text, Optional): List any services your business offers.
* **Business Equipment** (`business_equipment`, Text, Optional): List any equipment your business uses.
* **Target Demographics** (`target_demographics`, Text, Optional): Identify any target demographics for your business.
* **Marketing Tools** (`marketing_tools`, Text, Optional): List any marketing tools you are currently using or plan to use.

Note: All fields marked as "Required" are mandatory and must be filled out for the form to be considered complete.
