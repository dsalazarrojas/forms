# influencer_demographic_validation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The influencer_demographic_validation_form is designed to collect and validate demographic information from influencers for marketing campaigns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Header**: Enter a brief header for the form, which will serve as a title. (Text, Optional)
* **Demographic Information Section**: Select one or more options from the dropdown menu that best describe your demographic profile. This will help us understand your audience. (Select One/Many, Optional)
* **Age Range**: Choose your age range from the provided options. For example, 18-24, 25-34, 35-44, etc. (Select One, Optional)
* **Location**: Enter your location, which will help us understand your geographical reach. (Text, Optional)
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number, which will be used for further communication. (Text, Optional)
* **Email**: Enter your email address, which will be used for further communication. (Email, Optional)
* **Timezone**: Select your timezone from the available options. This will help us schedule content collaborations more effectively. (Time, Optional)

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate demographic information, as this will help us match you with relevant opportunities.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* You can also submit multiple demographic profiles for our team to review.
