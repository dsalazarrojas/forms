# real_estate_marketing_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The real_estate_marketing_intake_form is designed to collect marketing information for real estate businesses, helping them to get guidance on marketing strategies and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the following sections as accurately as possible to ensure that we can provide you with the best marketing support for your real estate business.
2. Please answer all questions in the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Real Estate Marketing Intake Form 1**: A brief description of your real estate business to help us better understand your marketing needs.
 
- **Real Estate Marketing Intake Form 2**: An additional description of your real estate business (optional, but recommended for more detailed information).
 
- **First Name**: Please enter your first name, as this will help us personalize our marketing advice.
 
- **Last Name**: Enter your last name, so we can address you correctly and keep your contact information organized.
 
- **Email**: This is your email address where we will send our responses and marketing advice.
 
- **Contact Number**: Provide a contact number where we can reach you for follow-up questions or further information.
 
- **Real Estate Type**: Select the type of real estate business you have (residential, commercial, industrial, etc.).
 
- **Marketing Budget**: Enter your current marketing budget for your real estate business.
 
- **Real Estate Marketing Goal**: Select the primary goal for your marketing efforts (lead generation, brand-awareness, website traffic, etc.).
 
- **Real Estate Marketing Strategy**: Choose the marketing strategies you are interested in (social media advertising, email marketing, search engine optimization, etc.).
 
- **Lead Capture Form**: A field for any additional information or notes about your lead capture processes (optional).

Please note that some fields are optional, but filling out all sections will help us provide you with better and more accurate marketing advice.

## Tips
Remember to provide accurate and detailed information about your real estate business and marketing goals to ensure you get the most out of this form. If you have any further questions or need clarification on any field, please feel free to contact us.
