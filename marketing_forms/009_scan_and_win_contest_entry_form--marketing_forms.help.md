# Scan And Win Contest Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is intended for individuals to enter a contest. To ensure accuracy and consistency in the information provided, please review the following guide to understand how to complete it correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow the instructions below. Please note that all fields are optional, unless marked as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`user_first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`user_last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address that you can use to receive notifications about the contest.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you if needed.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, number, optional): Enter your zip code or postal code for geographical identification.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth, following the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, optional): Enter your time of birth in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Which of the following do you have** (`select_one`, select_one, optional): Select one of the provided options: A, B, or C.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple options from X, Y, or Z.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional information or comments about your entry.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, email, optional): Verify the email address you entered earlier.
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, text, optional): Enter again the phone number you provided earlier.
* **Submit** (`submit`, note, optional): This is a submit button to finalize your entry.
