# Marketing Portfolio Submission - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals submit their marketing portfolio to our team. This form is intended for marketing professionals, students, and enthusiasts to showcase their work, skills, and achievements in the field of marketing. Please use this form to share your marketing portfolio, which should include project highlights, contact information, and any other relevant details that demonstrate your expertise.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Click on the "marketing_portfolio_submission" text box (page 1) to enter your title in a text format.
* Enter a brief description of your projects, skills, or qualifications in the "project_highlights" text box (page 2). This is an optional field, and you can submit any relevant information.
* Fill in your contact information, including your name, email, and any other relevant contact details, in the "contact_info" text box (page 3).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Title** (`marketing_portfolio_submission`, text, optional): Enter a brief title that describes your portfolio or submission.
### **Project Highlights** (`project_highlights`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your projects, skills, or qualifications in this field. This is an optional field, so you can skip it if you don't have anything to share.
### **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your name, email, and any other relevant contact details in this field. This is an optional field, so you can skip it if you don't want to provide your contact information.
### **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to submit your form. This is a final step, but it's not a required field.
