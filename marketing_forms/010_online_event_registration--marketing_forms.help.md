# online_event_registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about an online event. It gathers details about the event, such as its title and description, and attendee information, like their name and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the "Event Details" section, where you can provide a brief description of the event.
2. In the "Event Info" section, add any additional information about the event that is relevant to your attendance.
3. Finally, in the "Attendee Info" section, enter your name and any other contact details you'd like to share with the event organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Details (text)**: This is where you can provide a brief description of the event.
  - **Event Info (text)**: Add any additional information about the event that is relevant to your attendance.
  - **Attendee Info (text)**: Enter your name and any other contact details you'd like to share with the event organizers.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill in all required fields, although some fields are not marked as required.
- Be as detailed as possible in the "Event Info" and "Attendee Info" sections to ensure the event organizers have all the necessary information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
