# Join The Waitlist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form allows users to join a waitlist to receive marketing updates from the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Join The Waitlist" page.
2. Fill out the form with the required information.
3. Select any additional contact methods and update preferences.
4. Click the "Submit" button to join the waitlist.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Join The Waitlist** (`waitlist_for_updates`, note, optional): This is a title page that explains the form's purpose.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to receive updates from us.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number to receive calls or SMS updates from us.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, optional): Check if you consent to receive marketing updates from us.
* **Contact** (`contact`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more ways you'd like to be contacted (Email, Phone, Mail).
* **Updates** (`updates`, select_multiple, optional): Select how frequently you'd like to receive updates (Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly).
* **Preferences** (`preferences`, text, optional): Enter any specific preferences you have for the updates (e.g., language, format).
* **Minimalist** (`minimalist`, note, optional): This field is currently not used. 
* **None** (`none`, note, optional): This field is also not used and should be ignored.
* 

Note: All fields are optional unless specified as required.
