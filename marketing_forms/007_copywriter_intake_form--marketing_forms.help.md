# Copywriter Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Copywriter Intake Form is a tool for businesses and individuals to provide information about their copywriting needs. It helps marketing teams and agencies understand client requirements and tailor their services accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your business name and email address.
2.  Provide your phone number (optional).
3.  Select your business type from the options provided.
4.  Describe the services you need help with.
5.  List your products or services.
6.  Identify your target audience.
7.  Write a brief description of your business.
8.  Explain why you would choose to work with us.
9.  Provide your business website, if applicable.
10. Enter your business address, if applicable.
11. Share your business's Facebook and Twitter handles, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Business Name** (text, required): This field is for entering the name of your business.
*   **Email** (email, required): This field is for entering your business contact email address.
*   **Phone Number** (text, optional): This field is for entering your business phone number, if applicable.
*   **Business Type** (select multiple, optional): This field allows you to select one or more options for your business type from the provided options.
*   **Services** (text, optional): This field is for describing the services you need help with.
*   **Products** (text, optional): This field is for listing your business products or services.
*   **Target Audience** (text, optional): This field is for identifying your target audience.
*   **Business Description** (text, optional): This field is for providing a brief description of your business.
*   **Why Choose Us** (text, optional): This field is for explaining why you would choose to work with us.
*   **Business Website** (text, optional): This field is for providing your business website, if applicable.
*   **Business Address** (text, optional): This field is for entering your business address, if applicable.
*   **Business Facebook** (text, optional): This field is for sharing your business's Facebook handle, if applicable.
*   **Business Twitter** (text, optional): This field is for sharing your business's Twitter handle, if applicable.

## Tips
*   Please be as clear and concise as possible in your responses.
*   If you have any specific requirements or preferences for your copywriting project, include them in the form.
*   You can provide any additional information you think would be helpful for the marketing team to understand your needs.
