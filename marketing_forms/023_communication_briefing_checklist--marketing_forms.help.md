# Communication Briefing Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to ensure that all relevant details about a communication briefing are captured and recorded. It guides teams in preparing and executing effective communication strategies by outlining the key aspects of a campaign, including objectives, target audience, key messages, and timelines.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the briefing information and ensure it's comprehensive and accurate.
* Enter the briefing title and reference number.
* Select the campaign name and project manager.
* Define the main objective of the campaign.
* Identify the target audience and their demographics.
* Enter the key messages and primary message.
* Choose the desired tone and style of communication.
* Select the communication channels to be used.
* Set a launch and end date for the campaign.
* Define the budget and its allocation.
* Identify stakeholders who have been consulted.
* Confirm stakeholder approval.
* Assess potential risks or challenges.
* Outline the metrics for success.
* Check off completed tasks from the checklist.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Briefing Information**: Enter a brief description of the communication briefing.
* **Briefing Date**: Enter the date of the briefing.
* **Briefing ID**: Enter a unique reference number for the briefing.
* **Campaign Name**: Enter the name of the campaign or project.
* **Project Manager**: Enter the name of the project manager.
* **Briefing Objectives**: Enter a brief description of the main objectives of the briefing.
* **Primary Objective**: Enter the main goal of the communication campaign.
* **Target Audience**: Select the intended audience for the campaign.
* **Audience Demographics**: Enter key demographic information about the target audience.
* **Key Messages**: Enter a brief description of the key messages to be communicated.
* **Primary Message**: Enter the main message to be conveyed.
* **Supporting Messages**: Enter any additional supporting points.
* **Tone and Style**: Choose the desired tone and style of communication.
* **Communication Channels**: Select the channels to be used for communication.
* **Launch Date**: Enter the date the campaign will launch.
* **End Date**: Enter the date the campaign will end.
* **Key Milestones**: Enter important dates and milestones.
* **Budget Information**: Enter a brief description of the campaign budget.
* **Total Budget**: Enter the total budget for the campaign in USD.
* **Budget Allocation**: Enter how the budget is allocated.
* **Stakeholder Alignment**: Enter a brief description of stakeholder approval status.
* **Stakeholders Consulted**: Select the stakeholders who have been consulted.
* **Stakeholder Approval**: Confirm if all stakeholders have approved.
* **Risk Assessment**: Enter potential risks or challenges.
* **Success Metrics**: Enter how success will be measured.
* **Checklist Items**: Select the completed tasks for the briefing.
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional information or comments.
