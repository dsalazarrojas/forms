# Sponsorship Collaboration Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow companies or organizations to inquire about potential sponsorship opportunities with the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your company name in the field labeled "Company Name".
2. Provide your address in the field labeled "Address".
3. Enter your email address in the field labeled "Email".
4. Provide your phone number in the field labeled "Phone".
5. In the optional field "Description", briefly describe the partnership or sponsorship opportunity you are interested in.
6. Select a category for your partnership inquiry from the list of available options.
7. Optionally, add relevant tags related to your partnership or sponsorship opportunity in the field "Tags".
8. In the optional field "Comments", provide any additional information you feel would be helpful for the company to understand your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (Company Name, text, required)
	+ This field is used to collect the name of your company or organization.
* **Address** (Address, text, required)
	+ This field is used to collect your company's or organization's address.
* **Email** (Email, email, required)
	+ This field is used to collect your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required)
	+ This field is used to collect your company's or organization's phone number.
* **Description** (Description, text, optional)
	+ This field is used to provide a brief description of the partnership or sponsorship opportunity you are interested in.
* **Category** (Category, select_one, required)
	+ This field is used to select a category for your partnership inquiry. Please choose from the available options: Marketing Forms or Other.
* **Tags** (Tags, note, optional)
	+ This field is used to add relevant tags related to your partnership or sponsorship opportunity. This can help the company better understand your needs and provide more relevant information.
* **Comments** (Comments, text, optional)
	+ This field is used to provide any additional information you feel would be helpful for the company to understand your inquiry.
