# Flooring Promotion Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers interested in learning more about our flooring promotions. We will use the data collected to provide relevant offers and services tailored to their preferences. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone number" field.
5. Select a date you are available to meet with our sales representative in the "Date" field.
6. Choose a time that works best for you in the "Time" field.
7. Select the type of flooring you are looking for from the "What type of flooring are you looking for?" field.
8. Choose multiple flooring types you are interested in from the "What types of flooring are you interested in?" field.
9. If you have any additional comments or questions, please enter them in the "Additional comments or questions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address to receive communications from us.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Choose a date you are available to meet with our sales representative.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Choose a time that works best for you.
* **What type of flooring are you looking for?** (`select_one`, select, optional): Select the type of flooring you are interested in.
* **What types of flooring are you interested in?** (`select_multiple`, select, optional): Select multiple flooring types that you are interested in.
* **Additional comments or questions** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you have.

## Tips

* Please make sure to enter accurate information for each field to ensure we can serve you best.
* You can select multiple flooring types in the "What types of flooring are you interested in?" field by holding down the control key and selecting multiple options.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* We will follow up with you to confirm your registration and provide a quote.
