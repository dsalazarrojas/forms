The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers who participate in the contest to win a getaway.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Phone Number, Company Name, and Job Title (if applicable).
3. Choose how often you visit our store and how long you have been coming to our store.
4. Choose how you usually spend your free time and what you love to do in your free time.

7. Confirm that you agree to the entry terms and conditions.
