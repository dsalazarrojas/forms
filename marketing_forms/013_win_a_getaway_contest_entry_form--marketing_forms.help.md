# Win A Getaway Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers who participate in the contest to win a getaway.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Phone Number, Company Name, and Job Title (if applicable).
3. Choose how often you visit our store and how long you have been coming to our store.
4. Choose how you usually spend your free time and what you love to do in your free time.
5. Explain why you want to win a getaway.
6. Confirm your email address to verify your entry.
7. Confirm that you agree to the entry terms and conditions.
8. Provide any additional entry details or comments you think are relevant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Confirm Email** (`email_confirm`, email, required: false): Confirm your email address to verify your entry.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required: false): Enter your company name (if applicable).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Enter your job title (if applicable).
* **Entry Details** (`entry_details`, note, required: false): Provide any additional entry details or comments you think are relevant.
* **How did you hear about this contest?** (`how_did_you_hear`, select_one, required: false): Choose how you heard about this contest.
* **What would you say to us?** (`what_would_you_say`, note, required: false): Explain why you would like to win a getaway.
* **What do you want to win?** (`what_do_you_want`, note, required: false): State what you would like to win.
* **How long have you been coming to our store?** (`how_long_has_been_coming`, select_one, required: true): Choose how long you have been coming to our store.
* **How often do you visit our store?** (`how_often_visit`, select_one, required: true): Choose how often you visit our store.
* **How do you spend most of your free time?** (`how_do_you_spend`, note, required: false): Explain how you usually spend your free time.
* **What do you love to do in your free time?** (`what_do_you_love`, note, required: false): Explain what you love to do in your free time.
* **Why do you want to win this getaway?** (`win_reason`, note, required: false): Explain why you want to win this getaway.
