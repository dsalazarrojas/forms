# Marketing Consultation Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketing Consultation Application Form is used to gather information from clients about their marketing needs and preferences for an effective consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before filling it out.
2. Ensure you are filling out the form on your own behalf or as a representative of the client.
3. Fill in the required fields (if any).
4. Choose your preferred communication channels and contact time for the consultation.
5. Write a clear and concise message describing your goals and the consultation details.
6. Specify any next steps you would like to take after the consultation.
7. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details** (Client Details, text, required:false): Please enter your name and contact information to get in touch with you for the consultation.
* **Goals** (goals, select_multiple, required:false): Select your short-term, medium-term, or long-term marketing goals.
* **Budget** (Budget, number, required:false): Enter your budget for the marketing consultation.
* **Communication Channels** (Communication Channels, select_multiple, required:false): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, Text, or Video call).
* **Preferred Contact Time** (Preferred Contact Time, time, required:false): Select your preferred time for the consultation (e.g., morning, afternoon).
* **Consultation Details** (Consultation Details, text, required:false): Describe the specific details you would like to discuss during the consultation.
* **Next Steps** (Next Steps, text, required:false): Specify any next steps you would like to take after the consultation.
