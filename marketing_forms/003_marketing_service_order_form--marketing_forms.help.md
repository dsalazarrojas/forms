# Marketing Service Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketing Service Order Form is used for project managers to submit requests for marketing services to the marketing team. This form gathers essential information about the project, which is then used to prioritize and manage tasks efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "What is your name?" field.
2. Describe the project in the "What is the project about?" field.
3. Enter the project start date in the "When is the project start date?" field.
4. Enter the project deadline in the "When is the project deadline?" field.
5. Enter the project lead's name in the "Project Lead Name" field.
6. Enter the project lead's email address in the "What is your email?" field.
7. Enter the project lead's phone number in the "What is your phone?" field.
8. Select the project status in the "What is your project status?" field.
9. Enter any comments or additional information in the "Comments" field.
10. Enter any notes or comments about the project in the "Notes" field.
11. Enter a review or evaluation of the project in the "Review" field.
12. Summarize the project in the "Summary" field.
13. (Optional) Enter additional project lead information for leads 2 and 3 in the "Lead 2" and "Lead 3" fields.
14. (Optional) Enter email addresses and phone numbers for leads 2 and 3 in the "Email 2" and "Phone 2" fields for lead 2, and "Email 3" and "Phone 3" fields for lead 3.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name? (customer_name, text, required)**: Enter your name to identify yourself and your project.
* **What is the project about? (project_details, text, required)**: Describe the project in a few sentences to help the marketing team understand the context.
* **When is the project start date? (project_start_date, date, required)**: Enter the date when the project is expected to start.
* **When is the project deadline? (project_deadline, date, required)**: Enter the date when the project is expected to end.
* **Project Lead Name (project_lead_name, text, required)**: Enter the name of the project lead.
* **What is your email? (project_lead_email, email, required)**: Enter the email address of the project lead.
* **What is your phone? (project_lead_phone, text, required)**: Enter the phone number of the project lead.
* **What is your project status? (project_status, select_one, required)**: Select the current status of the project.
* **Comments (comments, text, required)**: Enter any additional comments or information about the project.
* **Notes (notes, text, required)**: Enter any notes or comments about the project.
* **Review (review, text, required)**: Enter a review or evaluation of the project.
* **Summary (summary, text, required)**: Enter a summary of the project.
* **Lead 2 (project_lead_2, text, optional)**: (Optional) Enter additional project lead information for lead 2.
* **Email 2 (project_lead_2_email, email, optional)**: (Optional) Enter the email address of lead 2.
* **Phone 2 (project_lead_2_phone, text, optional)**: (Optional) Enter the phone number of lead 2.
* **Lead 3 (project_lead_3, text, optional)**: (Optional) Enter additional project lead information for lead 3.
* **Email 3 (project_lead_3_email, email, optional)**: (Optional) Enter the email address of lead 3.
* **Phone 3 (project_lead_3_phone, text, optional)**: (Optional) Enter the phone number of lead 3.
