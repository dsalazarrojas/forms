# Marketing Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketing Quote Form is a tool used to collect essential information from clients about their marketing needs. This information helps our team to understand their requirements, provide an accurate quote, and deliver exceptional service.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your client's name and contact information in the **Client Information** section.
2. Select the services you require in the **Services Selected** section. Our team will use this information to tailor our approach to your needs.
3. If applicable, please provide a brief description of your project in the **Project Description** section.
4. Enter your budget for the project in the **Project Budget** field. This will help us to determine the scope and resources required.
5. Estimate the duration of your project in the **Estimated Project Duration** field.
6. Enter the contact person's name, email, and phone number in the **Contact Person** fields.
7. If you're ready to proceed, select **Yes** from the **Terms and Agreement** dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (Client Information)** (`1`, `text`, **required**): Enter your name and contact details.
* **Services Selected (Select One)** (`2`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose the services you need for your project.
	+ Select **Social Media Marketing** for social media management and advertising services.
	+ Select **Content Creation** for content development, such as blog posts, articles, and videos.
	+ Select **Branding** for logo design, brand identity, and visual identity.
	+ Select **SEO** for search engine optimization and website analysis.
	+ Select **Advertising** for paid advertising services.
* **Project Description (Project Description)** (`3`, `text`, **optional**): Briefly describe your project in 200 words or less.
* **Project Budget (Project Budget)** (`4`, `number`, **required**): Enter your budget for the project in numbers (e.g., 1000 for $1000).
* **Estimated Project Duration (Estimated Project Duration)** (`5`, `number`, **required**): Estimate the duration of your project in days or weeks.
* **Contact Person (Contact Person)** (`6`, `text`, **optional**): Enter the contact person's name.
* **Contact Email (Contact Email)** (`7`, `email`, **optional**): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Contact Phone (Contact Phone)** (`8`, `text`, **optional**): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Terms and Agreement (Terms and Agreement)** (`9`, `text`, **optional**): Select **Yes** to indicate that you've read and agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Submit (Submit)** (`10`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Select **Yes** to submit your form and start the project. Select **No** if you need more time or want to cancel.
