# Market Researcher Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to collect contact and company details of market researchers for marketing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your contact details, including your name, email, and phone number.
* Provide information about your job title and company type.
* Select the size and type of your company.
* Optionally, provide additional details about your company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details**: This section is used to collect your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
* **Researcher Information**: In this section, please provide any additional details about yourself and your role as a market researcher.
* **Company Information**: This section is used to collect information about your company, including its size and type.
* **Job Title**: Select your job title from the list to help us understand your role within your company.
* **Company Size**: Select the size of your company from the list to help us understand its scale.
* **Company Type**: Select the type of your company from the list to help us understand its nature.
* **Email**: Enter your company email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your company phone number.

**Tips**

* Make sure to fill in all required fields (none of the fields on this form are required, so you can fill in as much or as little information as you like).
* Double-check that you've entered accurate information to ensure that we can reach you and your company effectively.
