# SAS Observation And Coaching Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SAS Observation And Coaching Form is designed to collect data on sales performance and coaching. It helps sales managers track and evaluate the progress of their sales team and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Answer all required fields (indicated by *). 
3. Fill out the Sales Performance field with a numerical value representing the sales performance of the sales representative.
4. Select the Coaching Areas that you would like to coach the sales representative on. Choose multiple areas if necessary.
5. Enter any Observations you have on the sales representative's performance.
6. Add Coaching Notes to provide additional guidance and feedback to the sales representative.
7. Enter the Sales Manager Name who is completing the form.
8. Select the Coaching Frequency, which represents when the coaching took place.
9. Choose the Coaching Status, indicating whether the sales representative is currently active, inactive, or unknown.
10. Enter any Sales Coaching Form Comments.
11. Choose the Sales Coaching Outcome, which represents the impact of the coaching on the sales performance.
12. Enter a brief description of the Sales Coaching Outcome Reason.
13. Enter a comment on the Sales Coaching Outcome.
14. Finally, enter the Coaching Outcome Date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sales Performance** (`sales_performance`, number): Enter a numerical value representing the sales performance of the sales representative.
* **Coaching Areas** (`coaching_areas`, multiple select): Choose one or multiple areas to coach the sales representative on. 
* **Observations** (`observations`, text): Enter any observations you have on the sales representative's performance.
* **Coaching Notes** (`coaching_notes`, text): Add Coaching Notes to provide additional guidance and feedback to the sales representative.
* **Sales Manager Name** (`sales_manager_name`, text): Enter the name of the sales manager completing the form.
* **Coaching Frequency** (`coaching_frequency`, date): Select the date when the coaching took place.
* **Coaching Status** (`coaching_status`, select one): Choose whether the sales representative is currently Active, Inactive, or Unknown.
* **Sales Coaching Form Comments** (`sales_coaching_form_comments`, text): Enter any comments on the sales coaching form.
* **Sales Coaching Outcome** (`sales_coaching_outcome`, select one): Choose the impact of the coaching on the sales performance, either Improved Performance, No Change, or Decreased Performance.
* **Sales Coaching Outcome Reason** (`sales_coaching_outcome_reason`, text): Enter a brief description of the Sales Coaching Outcome Reason.
* **Sales Coaching Outcome Comment** (`sales_coaching_outcome_comment`, text): Enter a comment on the Sales Coaching Outcome.
* **Coaching Outcome Date** (`coaching_outcome_date`, date): Enter the date of the Coaching Outcome.
