# Homework Template LR - Help Guide
## Purpose
This template is designed to collect information about homework submissions, providing a standardized format for users to report their progress.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the second field.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the third field.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the fourth field.
5. If applicable, select the status of the **Completed Homework** in the fifth field.
6. Enter the **Completed Homework Date** in the sixth field.
7. Enter the **Completed Homework Time** in the seventh field.
8. Enter any **Completed Homework Note** about the task in the eighth field.
9. Enter the location where the **Completed Homework** was completed in the ninth field.
10. Select the group to which you were assigned the **Completed Homework** from the options in the tenth field.
11. If applicable, enter any other notes about the **Other Homework Completed** in the eleventh field.
12. Enter the name of the **Assigned Tool** used for the task in the twelfth field.
13. Add any **Other Notes** about the task in the thirteenth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **Completed Homework** (select_one): Select the status of the completed homework (e.g., "Completed Homework" or "Not Completed").
* **Completed Homework Date** (date): Enter the date the homework was completed.
* **Completed Homework Time** (time): Enter the time the homework was completed.
* **Completed Homework Note** (note): Enter any notes about the completed homework.
* **Completed Homework Location** (text): Enter the location where the homework was completed.
* **Assigned To** (select_multiple): Select the group to which you were assigned the homework (e.g., "Research Group 1" or "Research Group 2").
* **Assigned Tool** (text): Enter the name of the tool assigned for the task.
* **Other Homework Completed** (select_one): Select whether you completed the homework ("Completed Homework") or not ("Not Completed" or "No Response").
* **Other Notes** (note): Enter any other notes about the task.

This form is designed to provide a comprehensive and structured way to report on homework submissions. Please fill it out carefully and accurately to ensure consistency and accuracy in your reporting.
