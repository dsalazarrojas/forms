# Quiz Form For Kartra - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users to improve our marketing strategies and better tailor our content to our audience's needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select your preferred outcome from the options provided in the "Select 1" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select 1** (Select One, optional): This field allows you to choose one of the provided outcomes. Select the option that best describes your preference.
* **Email** (Email, optional): Enter your email address to receive updates and information from us. This is a one-time entry, so please make sure to enter it correctly.

## Tips
Please make sure to complete the form only once, as duplicate submissions will not be accepted. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us for assistance.
