# Audit Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data for the audit report, which is a document that provides an objective and unbiased assessment of a business's performance and market position.

## How To Complete This Form

* Ensure you have all necessary information and documents ready before starting the form.
* Review each field carefully and answer accurately to the best of your knowledge.
* If you're unsure about a particular field, please don't hesitate to contact the auditor for clarification.
* Fill out all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Date** (1, date, required):
	+ The date the audit was conducted.
* **Business Name** (2, text, required):
	+ The name of the business being audited.
* **Industry Type** (3, select_one, required):
	+ The industry type that best describes the business (e.g., Retail, Technology, etc.).
* **Market Segment** (4, text, required):
	+ The primary market segment served by the business.
* **Geographic Coverage** (5, select_multiple, required):
	+ The areas where the business operates (e.g., Local, Regional, National, International).
* **Current Market Share Percentage** (6, number, optional):
	+ Estimated market share percentage.
* **Key Competitors Identified** (7, number, optional):
	+ Number of direct competitors.
* **Market Growth Rate** (8, number, optional):
	+ Estimated annual market growth rate.
* **Customer Satisfaction Level** (9, select_one, required):
	+ Rate of current customer satisfaction (e.g., Very Unsatisfied, Satisfied, etc.).
* **Brand Recognition Score** (10, select_one, required):
	+ Rate of brand awareness in the market.
* **Current Pricing Strategy** (11, select_one, optional):
	+ The current pricing approach used by the business.
* **Active Marketing Channels** (12, select_multiple, required):
	+ Select all marketing channels used (e.g., Digital Advertising, Social Media, etc.).
* **Social Media Presence** (13, select_one, optional):
	+ If the business is active on social media.
* **Monthly Digital Marketing Spend** (14, number, optional):
	+ Approximate budget in dollars.
* **Customer Acquisition Cost** (15, number, optional):
	+ Average cost to acquire one customer.
* **Sales Trend** (16, select_one, required):
	+ The current sales direction (e.g., Increasing, Stable, Declining).
* **Main Challenges** (17, text, optional):
	+ Describe primary business challenges.
* **Growth Opportunities** (18, text, optional):
	+ Potential areas for market expansion.
* **Auditor Name** (19, text, required):
	+ The name of the person conducting the audit.
* **Auditor Email** (20, email, required):
	+ Email for contact and follow-up.

Please fill out the form carefully, and if you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the auditor for clarification.
