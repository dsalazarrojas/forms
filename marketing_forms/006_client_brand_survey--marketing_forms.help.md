# Client Brand Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This guide is to help you complete the Client Brand Survey, a tool designed to gather insights about your brand's image, perception, and performance. The survey collects information on various aspects, including awareness, perception, communication, quality, and areas for improvement. By providing your honest opinions, you'll contribute to the development of our brand and help us understand where we stand in the market.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email Address (if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up).
2. Select your Industry from the given options.
3. Rate your awareness of our brand and choose words that best describe it.
4. Compare our brand to competitors.
5. Rate the overall quality and value of our brand.
6. Indicate how much you trust and would recommend our brand.
7. Evaluate the effectiveness of our brand's communication channels.
8. Select where you've seen our brand (select all that apply).
9. Identify areas that need improvement.
10. Specify our brand strengths.
11. Mention any weaknesses.
12. Provide additional comments about our brand.
13. Finally, choose the date you completed the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`respondent_first_name`, `text`, Optional): For follow-up contact purposes, please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`respondent_last_name`, `text`, Optional): For follow-up contact purposes, please enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, `email`, Required): Enter your email address for survey results and follow-up.
* **Industry** (`industry`, `select_one`, Required): Choose your industry from the given options (Technology, Healthcare, Finance, Retail, Manufacturing, Education, Hospitality, Professional Services, or Other).
* **How Would You Rate Your Awareness of Our Brand?** (`brand_awareness_rating`, `select_one`, Required): Select how aware you are of our brand (Very Aware, Somewhat Aware, Neutral, Somewhat Unaware, or Very Unaware).
* **Which Words Best Describe Our Brand?** (`brand_perception`, `select_multiple`, Required): Choose up to 5 words that best describe our brand (Innovative, Trustworthy, Professional, Friendly, Expensive, Affordable, High Quality, Reliable, Modern, Traditional, Outdated, Confusing).
* **How Do We Compare to Competitors?** (`brand_comparison`, `select_one`, Required): Indicate how you compare our brand to competitors (Much Better, Somewhat Better, About the Same, Somewhat Worse, Much Worse, or Not Sure).
* **Rate the Quality of Our Brand** (`brand_quality_rating`, `select_one`, Required): Select our overall brand quality (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Rate the Value for Money** (`brand_value_rating`, `select_one`, Required): Choose how our price compares to quality (Excellent Value, Good Value, Fair Value, Poor Value, Very Poor Value).
* **How Much Do You Trust Our Brand?** (`brand_trust_level`, `select_one`, Required): Indicate how much you trust our brand (Completely Trust, Mostly Trust, Somewhat Trust, Slightly Trust, Do Not Trust).
* **How Likely Are You to Recommend Our Brand?** (`likelihood_to_recommend`, `select_one`, Required): Select how likely you are to recommend our brand (Very Likely, Likely, Neutral, Unlikely, Very Unlikely).
* **How Effective Is Our Brand Communication?** (`brand_communication_effectiveness`, `select_one`, Required): Evaluate the effectiveness of our brand's communication channels (Very Effective, Effective, Neutral, Ineffective, Very Ineffective).
* **Where Have You Seen Our Brand?** (`brand_channels`, `select_multiple`, Required): Select all the channels where you've seen our brand (Social Media, Website, Email Marketing, Print Advertising, TV or Radio, Events or Trade Shows, Word of Mouth, Never Seen Our Brand).
* **What Areas Need Improvement?** (`brand_improvement_areas`, `select_multiple`, Required): Identify areas that need improvement for our brand (Product Quality, Customer Service, Pricing, Website Experience, Communication, Brand Image, Delivery Speed, Innovation).
* **What Are Our Brand Strengths?** (`brand_strengths`, `text`, Optional): Mention what our brand does well.
* **What Are Our Brand Weaknesses?** (`brand_weaknesses`, `text`, Optional): Indicate areas where we can improve.
* **Additional Comments About Our Brand** (`additional_feedback`, `text`, Optional): Share any other thoughts or comments about our brand.
* **Survey Completion Date** (`survey_completion_date`, `date`, Required): Select the date you completed the survey.

## Tips

* Be honest and provide your genuine opinions.
* Select only options that apply to your experience with our brand.
* For multiple-choice questions, choose one or more options that best fit your opinion.
* For open-ended questions, provide detailed and specific answers.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
