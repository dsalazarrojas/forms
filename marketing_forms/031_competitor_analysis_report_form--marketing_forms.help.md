# Competitor Analysis Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather and document analysis on competitors in the market, providing insights for strategic decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the **Report Date** (page 1) with the date of the analysis report.
2. Provide your **Analyst Name** (page 2) and **Contact Email** (page 3) for identification.
3. Choose your **Department** (page 3) from the provided options to indicate which team is preparing the report.
4. Select the **Report Period** (page 3) that this analysis covers, such as weekly, monthly, quarterly, or annual.
5. Enter the **Industry Focus** (page 4) you are analyzing.
6. Identify the **Primary Competitor** (page 4) and its **Headquarters** (page 5) location.
7. Provide details on the **Competitor Employee Count** (page 5) and **Pricing Strategy** (page 6).
8. List the **Target Market** (page 6) and **Distribution Channels** (page 7) the competitor uses.
9. Outline the **Marketing Channels** (page 7) used by the competitor.
10. Mention **Recent Initiatives** (page 8) or new moves/launches by the competitor.
11. Evaluate the **Financial Health** (page 8) of the competitor.
12. Outline the **Technology Capabilities** (page 9) and **Strategic Partnerships** (page 9) the competitor has.
13. Identify the **Leadership Team** (page 10) and **Competitive Threats** (page 10) posed by the competitor.
14. Note any **Opportunities for Us** (page 10) to exploit gaps in the competitor's offerings or actions.
15. Provide **Recommended Actions** (page 10) to be taken by your team.
16. Determine the **Priority Level** (page 11) of the response required.
17. Select who should **Receive this Report** (page 12) to ensure relevant parties are informed.
18. Add any **Additional Comments** (page 12) or observations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Date**: The date on which this analysis report was created.
* **Analyst Name**: The person preparing this report for identification.
* **Contact Email**: The email address of the person preparing this report.
* **Department**: The team that is responsible for preparing this report.
* **Report Period**: The time frame covered by this report.
* **Industry Focus**: The specific industry being analyzed.
* **Primary Competitor**: The main competitor being analyzed.
* **Headquarters**: The location of the competitor's main offices.
* **Competitor Employee Count**: The approximate number of employees of the competitor.
* **Pricing Strategy**: How the competitor prices its products or services.
* **Target Market**: Who the competitor serves.
* **Distribution Channels**: How the competitor reaches its customers.
* **Marketing Channels**: How the competitor markets its products or services.
* **Recent Initiatives**: New moves or launches by the competitor.
* **Financial Health**: The competitor's financial stability.
* **Technology Capabilities**: The competitor's technical strengths.
* **Strategic Partnerships**: The competitor's strategic alliances.
* **Leadership Team**: Key executives of the competitor.
* **Competitive Threats**: Threats posed by the competitor to our company.
* **Opportunities for Us**: Gaps we can exploit in the competitor's offerings or actions.
* **Recommended Actions**: What we should do in response to the competitor's actions.
* **Priority Level**: The level of urgency required for the response.
* **Report Distribution**: Who should receive this report to be informed.
* **Additional Comments**: Any other observations or notes.
