# lead_scoring_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess and score leads based on their source, priority, engagement, score, and status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the lead source where the lead originated from (e.g., Email, Website, Referral, etc.).
2. Choose the lead priority, from Low, Medium, or High, indicating its level of urgency.
3. Enter a numerical value for the lead engagement, representing the level of interest or activity.
4. Enter a numerical value for the lead score, representing the lead's value or potential.
5. Select the lead status, from Open, Closed, Converted, or Lost, indicating its current state.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Lead Source (Page 1)
* **Lead Source**: This field determines where the lead came from (e.g., Email, Website, Referral, etc.). Select one or multiple options that apply.

### Lead Priority (Page 2)
* **Lead Priority**: This field indicates the lead's level of urgency or importance. Choose from Low, Medium, or High to prioritize the lead.

### Lead Engagement (Page 3)
* **Lead Engagement**: This field represents the level of interest or activity from the lead. Enter a numerical value to indicate the lead's level of engagement.

### Lead Score (Page 4)
* **Lead Score**: This field calculates the lead's value or potential. Enter a numerical value to represent the lead's score.

### Lead Status (Page 5)
* **Lead Status**: This field indicates the current state of the lead. Select from Open, Closed, Converted, or Lost to update the lead's status.

## Tips
- Ensure that you select the correct lead source to accurately represent its origin.
- Choose the correct priority level to prioritize the lead accordingly.
- Enter a numerical value for engagement that reflects the lead's interest or activity level.
- Enter a meaningful numerical value for the lead score that represents its value or potential.
- Update the lead status to reflect its current state accurately.
