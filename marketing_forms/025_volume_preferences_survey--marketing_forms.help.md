# Volume Preferences Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your volume preferences and provide more value to our customers. Please answer this form carefully and honestly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and select the relevant options for each field. Make sure to read each field's hint for additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What type of customer volume data do you have?** (`volume_preferences_survey`, `select_one`, required)
  - Select the type of customer volume data you have. This could be sales data, order data, or other data that you work with.
* **How often do you have customer volume data?** (`customer_volume`, `number`, required)
  - Enter the frequency of when you update your customer volume data. This could be a daily, weekly, or monthly frequency.
* **What volume frequency average daily?** (`volume_averagedaily`, `time`, required)
  - Enter the average time of day when you update your customer volume data.
* **What volume frequency type?** (`volume_frequency_type`, `select_one`, optional)
  - Select the type of volume frequency you have, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Which customer segment do you work with?** (`customer_segment`, `select_multiple`, optional)
  - Select the customer segment you work with, such as enterprise, small business, or other.
* **Where do you source your volume data?** (`volume_data_source`, `text`, optional)
  - Enter the source of your volume data, such as a CRM, ERP, or database.
* **What type of volume data source?** (`volume_data_source_type`, `select_one`, optional)
  - Select the type of volume data source, such as CRM, ERP, or database.
* **When was the last time you updated your customer volume data?** (`volume_data_last_updated`, `date`, optional)
  - Enter the date of the last time you updated your customer volume data.
* **What time of day was the last time you updated your customer volume data?** (`volume_data_last_updated_time`, `time`, optional)
  - Enter the time of day of the last time you updated your customer volume data.
* **Who updated your customer volume data last?** (`volume_data_last_updated_by`, `select_one`, optional)
  - Select who updated your customer volume data last, such as automated or manually.
* **Who updated your customer volume data last (by whom)?** (`volume_data_last_updated_by_who`, `text`, optional)
  - Enter the name of the person who updated your customer volume data last.
* **Who updated your customer volume data last (by whom, by whom)?** (`volume_data_last_updated_who_who_who`, `select_multiple`, optional)
  - Select the names of people who updated your customer volume data last.
* **Did you update your customer volume data last (by whom, by whom, by whom)?** (`volume_data_last_updated_who_who_who_who`, `text`, optional)
  - Enter 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you updated your customer volume data last, and who did.
* **Did you update your customer volume data last (by whom, by whom, by whom, by whom)?** (`volume_data_last_updated_who_who_who_who_who`, `select_multiple`, optional)
  - Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you updated your customer volume data last, and who did.
* **Did you update your customer volume data last (by whom, by whom, by whom, by whom, by whom)?** (`volume_data_last_updated_who_who_who_who_who_who`, `select_one`, optional)
  - Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you updated your customer volume data last, and who did.
* **Did you update your customer volume data last (by whom, by whom, by whom, by whom, by whom, by whom)?** (`volume_data_last_updated_who_who_who_who_who_who_who`, `text`, optional)
  - Enter 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you updated your customer volume data last, and who did.
* **Did you update your customer volume data last (by whom, by whom, by whom, by whom, by whom, by whom, by whom)?** (`volume_data_last_updated_who_who_who_who_who_who_who_who`, `select_multiple`, optional)
  - Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you updated your customer volume data last, and who did.
* **Other (if you have a different update frequency or update source)?** (`volume_data_last_updated_who_who_who_who_who_who_who_who_who`, `text`, optional)
  - If your update frequency or source is different from what's listed, enter the details here.

## Tips

* If you have any questions or need further clarification, please let us know.
