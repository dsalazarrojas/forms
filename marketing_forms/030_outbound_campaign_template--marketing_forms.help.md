# Outbound Campaign Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Outbound Campaign Template form is designed to collect contact and campaign information for marketing and sales purposes. This form helps in organizing and tracking campaign data for future reference and analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Outbound Campaign Template" form.
2. Fill in the required and optional fields as needed.
3. Ensure all provided information is accurate and up-to-date.
4. Review your completed form before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person or organization.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person or organization.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter the first name of the contact person.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter the last name of the contact person.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person or organization.
* **Campaign Name** (`campaign_name`, text, optional): Enter a name for the campaign, if applicable.
* **Campaign Date** (`campaign_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the campaign, if applicable.
* **Contact Notes** (`contact_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the contact or campaign.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter any additional phone numbers associated with the contact or campaign.

Note: The phone number field is repeated in the form to accommodate multiple phone numbers. Ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information in all fields.
