# Marketing Release Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to obtain marketing release authorization from individuals, allowing them to opt-in or opt-out of marketing communications from [Company Name]. The form is used to collect necessary information for marketing purposes, including name, contact details, and consent preferences.

Before completing this form, please ensure you are not already authorized for marketing communications with [Company Name] and that this form is not a duplicate submission. If you have any doubt, please contact [Support Contact] for clarification.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name**.
2. Enter your **Email** address accurately.
3. Provide your **Phone Number** for contact purposes.
4. Select your **Consent** preference for being contacted by [Company Name].
5. Sign the **Signature** field to acknowledge your consent.
6. Choose your **Time Zone** for your location.
7. Select your preferred **Time** for marketing communications.
8. Choose the types of marketing communications you consent to, such as **Marketing Permission**, **Promotional Permission**, or **Event Permission**.
9. If applicable, provide any **Additional Info** for our reference.
10. If not required, you can skip the **Consent Expires** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Required): Enter your first name accurately.
* **Last Name** (Required): Enter your last name accurately.
* **Email** (Required): Enter your email address accurately.
* **Phone** (Required): Enter your phone number accurately.
* **Consent** (Required): Choose to consent or not to be contacted by [Company Name].
* **Signature** (Required): Acknowledge your consent by signing this form.
* **Time Zone** (Required): Choose your time zone for your location.
* **Time** (Required): Select your preferred time for marketing communications.
* **Consent Type** (Required): Choose the types of marketing communications you consent to, such as **Marketing Permission**, **Promotional Permission**, or **Event Permission**.
* **Additional Info** (Optional): Provide any additional information for our reference.
* **Consent Expires** (Optional): If not required, you can skip these fields.
*   * **Consent Expires In** (Optional): If required, enter the number of days your consent expires.
*   * **Consent Expires Unit** (Optional): Choose the unit of time, either **days** or **weeks**.
*   * **Consent Expiration Date** (Optional): If required, enter the date when your consent expires.
*   * **Consent Expiration Time** (Optional): If required, enter the time when your consent expires.
*   * **Consent Expiration In Days** (Optional): If required, enter the number of days your consent expires.
*   * **Consent Expiration** (Optional): If required, describe your consent expiration in a free text field.
*   * **Consent Expiration Date** (Optional): If required, enter the date when your consent expires.
*   * **Consent Expiration Time** (Optional): If required, enter the time when your consent expires.
*   * **Consent Expiration In Days** (Optional): If required, enter the number of days your consent expires.
*   * **Consent Expiration Unit** (Optional): If required, choose the unit of time, either **days** or **weeks**.
* **Company Name** (Optional): Enter the name of the company you represent.
* **Contact Title** (Optional): Enter your contact title.
* **Consent Given** (Optional): If not required, enter the time when you gave your consent.
*   * **Consent Given Unit** (Optional): If required, choose the unit of time, either **seconds**, **minutes**, or **hours**.
*   * **Consent Given In** (Optional): If required, enter the time when you gave your consent.
*   * **Consent Expiration** (Optional): If required, describe your consent expiration in a free text field.
*   * **Consent Expiration Date** (Optional): If required, enter the date when your consent expires.
*   * **Consent Expiration Time** (Optional): If required, enter the time when your consent expires.
*   * **Consent Expiration In Days** (Optional): If required, enter the number of days your consent expires.
*   * **Consent Expiration Unit** (Optional): If required, choose the unit of time, either **days** or **weeks**.

Note that some fields are marked as "Optional", which can be skipped if not required. Please review the fields carefully and provide accurate information for the required fields.
