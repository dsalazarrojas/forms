# Jewelry Collection Release Influencer Collaboration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help the jewelry collection release team collaborate with social media influencers. It collects necessary information to facilitate a successful collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for each field.
2. Be sure to check the "Agreement" box to acknowledge you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
3. Submit the form to initiate the collaboration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information**: Please enter your basic contact information (name, email, and website) to help us get in touch with you.
* **Collaboration Details**: Describe the collaboration you are interested in and any specific requirements or expectations you may have.
* **Product Media**: If applicable, provide links or URLs to any relevant product media (images, videos, or other multimedia content).
* **Social Media**: Share your social media handles (e.g., Instagram, TikTok, Twitter, or Facebook) so we can find you easily.
* **Submission**: Provide a brief description of the submission you are willing to share with us.
* **Agreement**: Check this box to indicate that you have read and agree to our terms and conditions.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth collaboration process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
