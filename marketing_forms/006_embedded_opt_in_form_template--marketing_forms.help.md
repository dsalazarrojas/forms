# Embedded Opt In Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us better understand our customers' preferences and demographics to tailor our marketing efforts to their needs. Your responses will help us create more relevant and targeted promotional materials.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your **Form Name** (`form_name`): This is a text input field where you can provide a name for the form, such as "Summer Promotion Form" or "Product Launch Form".
2. Enter your **Email**: This is a required field where you should provide your email address.
3. Enter your **Name**: This is a text input field where you can provide your name.
4. Enter your **Phone Number**: This is a text input field where you can provide your phone number.
5. Choose your **Email Opt In** preference:
	* Select 'Yes' to opt-in for promotional emails.
	* Select 'No' if you do not want to receive promotional emails.
6. Choose your **Email Frequency**:
	* Select 'Daily' for daily email updates.
	* Select 'Weekly' for weekly email updates.
	* Select 'Monthly' for monthly email updates.
7. Enter your **Notes**: This is an optional text area where you can provide any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Name**: (`form_name`) - A text input field for providing a name to the form.
* **Email** (`email`) - A required email input field where you should provide your email address.
* **Name** (`name`) - A text input field for your name.
* **Phone** (`phone`) - A text input field for your phone number.
* **Email Opt In** (`email_opt_in`) - A select multiple field where you choose whether you want to opt-in for promotional emails.
* **Email Frequency** (`email_frequency`) - A select one field where you choose how often you want to receive promotional emails.
* **Communication** (`communication`) - A text input field for any additional comments or notes.
* **Unsubscribe** (`unsubscribe`) - A select one field where you choose whether you want to unsubscribe from promotional emails.
* **Email Type** (`email_type`) - A select one field where you choose the type of emails you want to receive.
* **Promotion Type** (`promotion_type`) - A select one field where you choose the type of promotions you want to receive.
* **Time Zone** (`timezone`) - A time input field for your time zone.
* **Date** (`date`) - A date input field for a specific date.
* **Notes** (`notes`) - A text area for any additional comments or notes.
* **Custom 1** (`custom_1`) - A select multiple field for any additional custom options.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have the authority to collect and utilize the information gathered by this form.
* Do not fill out this form if you've already provided the same information in the past.
* This form is meant for marketing and promotional purposes only.
