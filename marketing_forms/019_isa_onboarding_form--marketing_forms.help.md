# ISA Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ISA Onboarding Form is used to capture employee information and preferences for the onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First name, Last name, and Email in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Phone number for contact purposes.
3. Provide your Company information.
4. Fill in your Job title, Department, and Job type.
5. Select your Location.
6. If applicable, enter your Date of birth, Date of hire, and Work experience in years.
7. Enter your Salary requirement.
8. Specify your Available hours per week.
9. Choose your Work type (e.g., Full time, Part time, Remote).
10. Add any Onboarding notes if needed.
11. Select your Job status (e.g., Active, Inactive, Onboarding).
12. Indicate your Job satisfaction level (e.g., Satisfied, Neutral, Unsatisfied).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your work email address.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your work phone number.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter your current company's name.
* **Job title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title in the company.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter your department in the company.
* **Job type** (`job_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of job you hold (e.g., Software Engineer, Marketing Manager, Sales).
* **Location** (`location`, select_one, required): Select your location (e.g., New York, Los Angeles, Chicago).
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): If applicable, enter your Date of birth.
* **Date of hire** (`date_of_hire`, date, optional): If applicable, enter your Date of hire.
* **Work experience** (`work_experience_years`, number, optional): If applicable, enter your work experience in years.
* **Salary requirement** (`salary_requirement`, number, optional): Enter your expected salary.
* **Available hours** (`available_hours`, number, optional): Specify your available hours per week.
* **Work type** (`work_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of work you're comfortable with (e.g., Full time, Part time, Remote).
* **Onboarding notes** (`onboarding_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes for onboarding purposes.
* **Job status** (`job_status`, select_one, optional): Select your job status (e.g., Active, Inactive, Onboarding).
* **Job satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate your job satisfaction level (e.g., Satisfied, Neutral, Unsatisfied).

## Tips
* Double-check your entered information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure you have all necessary information ready before filling out this form to avoid duplication of efforts.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your HR representative.
