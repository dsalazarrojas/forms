# Global Market Insights Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic and market insights information from users. The purpose of this form is to gather data on respondents' demographic characteristics, such as age, income, occupation, education level, and other relevant factors that can provide valuable insights into market trends and consumer behavior.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
2. For select_one and select_multiple type fields, select only one or multiple options that best apply to you.
3. For text type fields, provide a clear and concise answer in the provided text box.
4. If a field is marked as required, please make sure to complete it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Form (1)** (`survey_form`, text, required: false): This is a text box where you can provide any additional comments or information related to your answers.
* **Demographic Questions (2)** (`demographic_questions`, select_multiple, required: false): This is a multi-select field where you can choose one or multiple options that best describe your demographics:
	+ Age Group 18-24
	+ Age Group 25-34
	+ Age Group 35-44
	+ Age Group 45-54
	+ Age Group 55-64
	+ Age Group 65 or above
* **Income Range (3)** (`income_range`, select_one, required: false): Choose one option that best describes your annual household income:
	+ Less than $25,000
	+ $25,000-$49,999
	+ $50,000-$74,999
	+ $75,000-$99,999
	+ $100,000 or more
* **Occupation (4)** (`occupation`, text, required: false): Please provide your current occupation or job title.
* **Education Level (5)** (`education_level`, select_one, required: false): Choose the highest level of education you have completed:
	+ Less than High School
	+ High School
	+ Some College
	+ College
	+ Postgraduate
* **Gender (6)** (`gender`, select_one, required: false): Select your gender:
	+ Female
	+ Male
	+ Other
* **Marital Status (7)** (`marital_status`, select_one, required: false): Choose your current marital status:
	+ Single
	+ Married
	+ Divorced
	+ Widowed
	+ In a domestic partnership
* **Family Size (8)** (`family_size`, select_one, required: false): Choose the number of people living in your household:
	+ 1
	+ 2
	+ 3
	+ 4
	+ 5 or more
* **Children (9)** (`children`, select_one, required: false): Choose the number of children you have:
	+ 0
	+ 1
	+ 2
	+ 3
	+ 4
	+ 5 or more
* **Country (10)** (`country`, text, required: false): Please provide the name of your country of residence.
* **Language (11)** (`language`, select_one, required: false): Choose the language you prefer to communicate:
	+ English
	+ Spanish
	+ French
	+ Chinese
	+ Japanese
	+ Arabic
	+ Hindi
* **City (12)** (`city`, text, required: false): Please provide the name of your city or town.
* **State (13)** (`state`, select_one, required: false): Choose your state or region:
	+ Yes
	+ No

Note that some fields may be optional and you are not required to complete them. If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to contact us.

Tips:

* Please answer questions honestly and thoroughly to ensure the accuracy of the data.
* If you select multiple options in the Demographic Questions field, you can use comma-separated values.
* If you are unsure of your answers, please choose the option that best fits your situation.
* You can always contact us if you have any questions or concerns.
