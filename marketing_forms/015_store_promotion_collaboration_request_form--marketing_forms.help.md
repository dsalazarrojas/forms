# store_promotion_collaboration_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed for store owners to request collaboration with other businesses for promotions and marketing activities. The form helps to gather information about the promotion, including the store name, partner name, proposal details, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the store name and partner name fields with the relevant information.
2. Provide a brief description of the proposal in the proposal details field.
3. Select the proposal type from the available options.
4. Enter the title of the proposal.
5. Fill in the proposal description field with a detailed description of the promotion.
6. Enter the campaign details and select the campaign type from the available options.
7. Enter the campaign name.
8. If applicable, fill in the promotion date and promotion time fields.
9. Describe the collaboration requirements in the collaboration requirements field.
10. Enter the expected outcome of the collaboration in the collaboration outcome field.
11. Fill in the agency details, agency name, agency email, and agency phone fields with the relevant information.
12. Fill in the retailer details, retailer name, retailer email, and retailer phone fields with the relevant information.
13. Fill in the brand details, brand name, brand email fields with the relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Store Name** (`store_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your store.
* **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the partner you are requesting collaboration with.
* **Proposal Details** (`proposal_details`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the proposal.
* **Proposal Type** (`proposal_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of proposal from the available options.
* **Proposal Title** (`proposal_title`, text, required): Enter a title for the proposal.
* **Proposal Description** (`proposal_description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the promotion.
* **Campaign Details** (`campaign_details`, text, required): Enter the details of the campaign.
* **Campaign Type** (`campaign_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of campaign from the available options.
* **Campaign Name** (`campaign_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the campaign.
* **Promotion Date** (`promotion_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the promotion.
* **Promotion Time** (`promotion_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the promotion.
* **Collaboration Requirements** (`collaboration_requirements`, text, required): Describe the requirements for the collaboration.
* **Collaboration Outcome** (`collaboration_outcome`, text, required): Enter the expected outcome of the collaboration.
* **Agency Details** (`agency_details`, text, required): Enter the details of the agency.
* **Agency Name** (`agency_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the agency.
* **Agency Email** (`agency_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the agency.
* **Agency Phone** (`agency_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the agency.
* **Retailer Details** (`retailer_details`, text, required): Enter the details of the retailer.
* **Retailer Name** (`retailer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the retailer.
* **Retailer Email** (`retailer_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the retailer.
* **Retailer Phone** (`retailer_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the retailer.
* **Brand Details** (`brand_details`, text, required): Enter the details of the brand.
* **Brand Name** (`brand_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the brand.
* **Brand Email** (`brand_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the brand.

Note: All fields marked as required must be filled in for the form to be submitted successfully.
