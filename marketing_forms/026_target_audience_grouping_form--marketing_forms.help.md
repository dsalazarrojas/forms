> # Target Audience Grouping Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you create targeted audience groups for your marketing efforts. It's intended to be used by marketing teams to categorize and group customers or prospects based on demographic information.

> ## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter a brief name for your audience group in the "Grouping Name" field.
2. Provide a description of your audience group in the "Grouping Description" field.
3. Add any additional notes or comments about your audience group in the "Grouping Notes" field.
4. Finally, enter any relevant demographic information about your audience group in the "Demographic Value" field.

> ## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Demographic Value** (`demographic_value`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the demographic information you're grouping by (e.g. "Age", "Location", "Interest").
* **Grouping Name** (`grouping_name`, text, optional): Enter a brief name for your audience group (e.g. "Millennials", "Urban Dwellers", "Tech Enthusiasts").
* **Grouping Description** (`grouping_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of your audience group (e.g. "Demographics of people born between 1980 and 1995", "People living in urban areas", "Individuals interested in tech and innovation").
* **Grouping Notes** (`grouping_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your audience group (e.g. "This group is focused on buying high-end electronics", "Targeting local businesses in the area", "This group is primarily interested in sustainable products").
