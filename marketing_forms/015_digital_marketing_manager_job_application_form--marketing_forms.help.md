# Digital Marketing Manager Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect your application for the Digital Marketing Manager position. Please provide accurate and truthful information about your qualifications and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Read each section carefully and make sure you have a clear understanding of what is required.
2.  Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3.  Make sure you have the necessary permissions to provide any references or share your portfolio.
4.  Review each question and make sure you are providing the correct type of information (e.g., text, selection, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your current role and job responsibilities?** (`job_application_form_1`, `text`, required)
	+ Please describe your current role and job responsibilities, including your job title, department, and a brief description of your duties.
* **What motivates you to apply for this position?** (`job_application_form_2`, `text`, required)
	+ Please explain why you are interested in the Digital Marketing Manager position and what motivates you to apply.
* **Can you tell us about your relevant experience in digital marketing?** (`job_application_form_3`, `text`, required)
	+ Describe your experience in digital marketing, including any relevant projects, achievements, and skills you've developed.
* **What skills do you have that make you a strong candidate for this role?** (`job_application_form_4`, `text`, required)
	+ List any relevant skills you possess, such as social media management, content creation, or data analysis.
* **What type of employment status do you have?** (`job_application_form_5`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Choose your current employment status: Full-time, Part-time, or Freelance.
* **What level of education have you completed?** (`job_application_form_6`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Select your highest level of education completed: Bachelor's degree, Master's degree, or Ph.D.
* **Can you provide examples of your portfolio?** (`job_application_form_7`, `text`, required)
	+ Please share links or descriptions of your relevant work or projects that demonstrate your skills.
* **Do you have any references we can contact?** (`job_application_form_8`, `checkbox`, optional)

* **What is your expected salary range for this role?** (`job_application_form_9`, `number`, required)
	+ Provide your expected salary range for the position.
* **Can you provide your current contact information?** (`job_application_form_10`, `text`, required)
	+ Please provide your current email address and phone number.
* **What is your availability for the job?** (`job_application_form_11`, `text`, optional)
	+ If you have any constraints on your availability, such as a specific start date, please describe them.
* **Can you tell us about your achievements in digital marketing?** (`job_application_form_12`, `text`, required)
	+ Describe any notable achievements or accomplishments you've had in digital marketing.
