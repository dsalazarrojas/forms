# Retail Digital Marketing Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Digital Marketing Audit Form is designed to help us evaluate and improve our digital marketing strategy, customer base, and performance metrics. This form collects information about your marketing strategy, customer base, and performance metrics to help us identify areas for improvement and provide recommendations for growth.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your marketing strategy from the options provided (Good, Fair).
2. List your current channels used for marketing (e.g., social media, email, etc.).
3. Specify your marketing goals (e.g., increase sales, improve customer engagement, etc.).
4. Indicate if you have performance metrics in place (Yes or No).
5. Select your target audience (Yes or No).
6. Provide your marketing budget.
7. List your marketing channels used (e.g., social media, email, etc.).
8. Specify your marketing objectives (e.g., increase sales, improve customer engagement, etc.).
9. Indicate the current performance of each channel (Yes or No).
10. Select your target performance (Yes or No).
11. Specify your marketing milestones and progress towards those milestones.
12. Indicate your customer service and support capabilities (Yes or No).
13. Indicate the current customer satisfaction level (using a number value).
14. Provide an estimate of customer base growth (e.g., number, percentage, etc.).
15. Indicate your customer base segmentation strategy and goals (e.g., demographic, behavior, etc.).
16. Specify your customer base segmentation milestones and progress towards those milestones.
17. Provide an estimate of customer base segmentation progress (e.g., number, percentage, etc.).
18. Indicate if you have a customer base segmentation strategy in place (Yes or No).
19. Specify your customer base segmentation goals (e.g., demographic, behavior, etc.).
