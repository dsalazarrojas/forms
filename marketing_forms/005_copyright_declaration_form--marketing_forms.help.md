# Copyright Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Copyright Declaration Form" is designed to collect information about the copyright holder's identity, contact details, and agreement to specific terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you have reviewed and understood the purpose and instructions of this form.
3. Double-check your entries for errors before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Copyright Holder**: A field to record the name of the copyright holder.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
	+ Purpose: To record the name of the individual or organization holding the copyright.
* **Email**: A field to record the email address of the copyright holder.
	+ Type: email
	+ Required: false
	+ Purpose: To record the email address of the copyright holder for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: A field to record the phone number of the copyright holder.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
	+ Purpose: To record the phone number of the copyright holder for contact purposes.
* **Copyright Date**: A field to record the date the copyright was granted.
	+ Type: date
	+ Required: false
	+ Purpose: To record the date the copyright was granted, ensuring accuracy and consistency.
* **Address**: A field to record the address of the copyright holder.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
	+ Purpose: To record the physical address of the copyright holder for contact purposes.
* **Copyrighted Works**: A field to record a description of the copyrighted work(s).
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
	+ Purpose: To record a brief description of the copyrighted work(s) for reference.
* **Copyright Notice**: A field to record any additional copyright information or notes.
	+ Type: note
	+ Required: false
	+ Purpose: To record any specific copyright information or notes that require attention.
* **Agreement**: A field to confirm agreement to terms and conditions.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: false
	+ Purpose: To confirm agreement to the terms and conditions.
* **Terms of Use**: A field to record terms of use for the copyrighted work.
	+ Type: text
