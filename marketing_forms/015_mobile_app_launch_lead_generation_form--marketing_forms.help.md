# Mobile App Launch Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your interests and preferences regarding the mobile app launch. Please answer the questions as accurately as possible to help us better understand our audience.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the options that best fit your answers for each question.
2. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to write them down in the "Additional comments or feedback" field.
3. Once you have completed all the fields, click the "Submit" button to send your information to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What interests you about the mobile app?** (`mobile_app_launch_lead_generation_form_1`, text, optional): This is a text field where you can describe what aspects of the mobile app interest you the most. Please be as specific as possible.
* **How did you hear about the mobile app?** (`mobile_app_launch_lead_generation_form_2`, select_one, optional): This question is asking about the source of your knowledge about the mobile app. You can select one of the options:
	+ Social media
	+ Word of mouth
	+ Online advertisement
	+ Other (please specify)
* **What is your preferred way of getting updates on the mobile app?** (`mobile_app_launch_lead_generation_form_3`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the ways you would like to receive updates about the mobile app:
	+ Email
	+ Phone call
	+ Message in app
	+ Other (please specify)
* **When will you be interested in trying the mobile app?** (`mobile_app_launch_lead_generation_form_4`, date, optional): This is a date field where you can select the date you would be interested in trying the mobile app.
* **How likely are you to try the mobile app in the next 2 weeks?** (`mobile_app_launch_lead_generation_form_5`, number, optional): This question is about your willingness to try the mobile app. Please select a number between 1 (not likely) and 10 (very likely).
* **How would you like to be contacted for further information?** (`mobile_app_launch_lead_generation_form_6`, select_one, optional): If you would like to be contacted for further information, choose one of the options:
