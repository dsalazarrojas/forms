# Outreach Brochure Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Outreach Brochure Request Form is designed to collect essential information about outreach brochures requested by users. This form helps gather details that are needed for efficient brochure production and delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your User Name in the first field.
2. Provide your Email address in the second field.
3. Enter your Phone number in the third field.
4. If applicable, select the quantity of brochures you need.
5. Choose a delivery date for the brochure.
6. Optionally, select a delivery time if needed.
7. Provide any additional notes regarding your request.
8. Confirm if you have an organization or school affiliation.
9. Enter your School Name, School Address, School Phone, and School Email if applicable.
10. Optionally, enter your School Fax number and School Website.
11. Enter any additional information about your request.
12. Provide comments or any other information about your request.
13. Confirm how you want the brochures to be delivered, including delivery status and method.
14. Select if you want the brochures to be delivered frequently.
15. Finally, enter the delivery address for the brochures.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (`user_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it is known to you.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your official email address associated with your request.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, optional): If you want more than one brochure, enter the quantity here.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, required): Choose the date you would like to receive the brochure.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, `time`, optional): If you need the brochure delivered at a specific time, select it here.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional information about your request.
* **Organization** (`organization`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if you have an organization or school affiliation.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your school or organization name if applicable.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, `text`, optional): Enter your school address if applicable.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your school phone number if applicable.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, `text`, optional): Enter your school email if applicable.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, optional): Enter the department name if applicable.
* **School Fax** (`school_fax`, `text`, optional): Enter your school fax number if applicable.
* **School Website** (`school_website`, `text`, optional): Enter your school website if applicable.
* **Request Details** (`request_details`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information about your request.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments or information about your request.
* **Delivery Instructions** (`delivery_instructions`, `text`, optional): Enter any specific instructions regarding the delivery of your brochure.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional information about your request.
* **Request Type** (`request_type`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if this is a general or a specific request.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you submitted your request.
* **Delivery Status** (`delivery_status`, `select_one`, required): Confirm the status of your brochure delivery.
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, `select_one`, required): Confirm the delivery method of your brochure.
* **Delivery Frequency** (`delivery_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Confirm how frequently you would like to receive the brochure.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address where you would like to receive the brochure.
