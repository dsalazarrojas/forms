# Facebook Ad Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facebook Ad Intake Form is used to gather information about a Facebook ad campaign. This form is essential for our team to track and manage ad campaigns effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Page Title** in the "Page Title" field. This is the name of the Facebook page associated with the ad campaign.
2. Enter the **Page Link** in the "Page Link" field. This is the link to the Facebook page where the ad will be published.
3. Enter a brief **Ad Description** in the "Ad Description" field. This is a short summary of the ad campaign.
4. Enter the **Client Name** in the "Client Name" field. This is the name of the client associated with the ad campaign.
5. Enter the **Client E-mail** in the "Client E-mail" field. This is the email of the client associated with the ad campaign.
6. Enter the **Ad Image URL** in the "Ad Image URL" field. This is the link to the image used in the ad campaign.
7. Select the **Ad Target Audience** from the options provided in the "Ad Target Audience" field. This determines who will see the ad.
8. Enter the **Ad Budget** in the "Ad Budget" field. This is the total budget for the ad campaign.
9. Enter the **Ad Spend** in the "Ad Spend" field. This is the amount spent on the ad campaign.
10. If applicable, enter the **Ad Start Date** and **Ad End Date** fields. These determine the start and end dates of the ad campaign.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Page Title** (text, required): Enter the name of the Facebook page associated with the ad campaign.
* **Page Link** (text, required): Enter the link to the Facebook page where the ad will be published.
* **Ad Description** (text, optional): Enter a short summary of the ad campaign.
* **Client Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the client associated with the ad campaign.
* **Client E-mail** (email, required): Enter the email of the client associated with the ad campaign.
* **Ad Image URL** (text, optional): Enter the link to the image used in the ad campaign.
* **Ad Target Audience** (select multiple, optional): Select from the options provided to determine who will see the ad.
* **Ad Budget** (number, required): Enter the total budget for the ad campaign.
* **Ad Spend** (number, required): Enter the amount spent on the ad campaign.
* **Ad Start Date** (date, optional): If applicable, enter the start date of the ad campaign.
* **Ad End Date** (date, optional): If applicable, enter the end date of the ad campaign.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Double-check the fields for errors before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the relevant stakeholders for clarification.
* If you are duplicating a process, please check with the relevant team members before filling out the form.
