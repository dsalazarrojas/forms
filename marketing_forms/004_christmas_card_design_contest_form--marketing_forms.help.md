# Christmas Card Design Contest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for artists to submit their entries for the Christmas Card Design Contest. It's a quick and easy way for artists to provide their details, and for the organizers to verify and review their entries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Artist name**: Enter your full name as the submitter.
2. **Contact email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for contest updates.
3. **Design title**: Enter a short descriptive title for your entry.
4. **Medium used**: Select the medium used in your design (e.g., Digital, Watercolor, etc.).
5. **Upload link or image URL**: Provide a public link to your entry.
6. **Age group**: Select your age group (Youth under 18, Adult 18-35, or Adult 36 and above).
7. **Agreement to contest rules**: Confirm that you agree to the contest rules.
8. **Optional artist statement**: Enter a brief description of your design.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Artist Information
* **Artist name** (`artist_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the submitter.

### Artist Contact Information
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for contest updates.

### Design Details
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): This is an optional field for quicker contact.
* **Design title** (`design_title`, text, required): Enter a short descriptive title for your entry.
* **Medium used** (`medium_used`, select_one, required): Select the medium used in your design (e.g., Digital, Watercolor, etc.).

### Entry Submission
* **Upload link or image URL** (`upload_link`, text, required): Provide a public link to your entry.

### Age and Category Information
* **Age group** (`age_group`, select_one, required): Select your age group (Youth under 18, Adult 18-35, or Adult 36 and above).

### Contest Agreement
* **Agreement to contest rules** (`agreement_terms`, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to the contest rules.

### Optional Description
* **Artist statement** (`artist_statement`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your design if you choose to do so.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide a valid email address and upload link to your entry.
* Be honest about the medium used in your design.
* Confirm your agreement to the contest rules before submitting.
* Keep your artist statement brief if you choose to provide one.
