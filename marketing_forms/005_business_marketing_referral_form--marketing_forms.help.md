# Business Marketing Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Marketing Referral Form is designed to help us identify potential business contacts who might benefit from our services. By filling out this form, you can refer a contact and provide valuable information about them, which will help us tailor our approach to their specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name, email address, and phone number to identify yourself as the referrer.
2. Provide the name, email address, and phone number of the contact being referred.
3. Choose how you know the referred contact (e.g., Business Associate, Client, Colleague, Friend, Family Member, or Other).
4. Select the areas of interest for our services that might apply to the referred contact (e.g., Marketing Strategy, Digital Marketing, Brand Development, Content Creation, Social Media, SEO/SEM, Email Marketing, or Analytics).
5. Explain why you are making this referral and why you think our services might be a good match for the referred contact.
6. Indicate if the referred contact is open to being contacted by us.
7. (Optional) If you know the referred contact's company size, select it from the provided options.
8. (Optional) If you know the referred contact's industry or business type, select it from the provided options.
9. (Optional) Select the estimated budget range of the referred contact.
10. (Optional) Select when the referred contact might be ready to start working with us.
11. (Optional) Select how you would like to be rewarded for your referral.
12. Provide any additional comments or information about the referred contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name** (`referrer_name`, text, Required): Enter your full name to identify yourself as the referrer.
* **Your Email Address** (`referrer_email`, email, Required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about this referral.
* **Your Phone Number** (`referrer_phone`, text, Required): Provide your phone number for us to contact you about this referral.
* **Your Company Name** (`referrer_company`, text, Optional): If you represent a company, enter your company name here. This is not required.
* **How do you know the referred contact?** (`referral_relationship`, select_one, Required): Choose how you know the referred contact (e.g., Business Associate, Client, Colleague, Friend, Family Member, or Other).
* **Referred Contact Name** (`referred_contact_name`, text, Required): Enter the full name of the person being referred.
* **Referred Contact Email** (`referred_contact_email`, email, Required): Enter the email address of the referred contact.
* **Referred Contact Phone** (`referred_contact_phone`, text, Required): Enter the phone number of the referred contact.
* **Referred Person Company** (`referred_company_name`, text, Optional): If the referred contact has a company, enter its name here. This is not required.
* **Industry or Business Type** (`referred_company_industry`, select_one, Required): Select the industry or business type of the referred contact (e.g., Technology, Healthcare, Finance, Manufacturing, Retail, Services, Non-profit, or Other).
* **Company Size** (`referred_company_size`, select_one, Optional): Select the estimated number of employees of the referred contact's company (e.g., Startup, Small, Medium, Large, Enterprise).
* **Areas of Interest for Services** (`interest_areas`, select_multiple, Required): Select the areas of interest for our services that might apply to the referred contact (e.g., Marketing Strategy, Digital Marketing, Brand Development, Content Creation, Social Media, SEO/SEM, Email Marketing, or Analytics).
* **Why Are You Making This Referral?** (`referral_reason`, text, Required): Explain why you are making this referral and why you think our services might be a good match for the referred contact.
* **Are they open to being contacted?** (`contact_receptivity`, select_one, Required): Indicate if the referred contact is open to being contacted by us.
* **Estimated Budget Range** (`referral_budget_range`, select_one, Optional): If you know the referred contact's estimated budget, select it from the provided options (e.g., Under $10,000, $10,000 - $50,000, $50,000 - $100,000, $100,000 - $250,000, Over $250,000, or Unknown).
* **Timeframe for Implementation** (`referral_timeline`, select_one, Optional): If you know when the referred contact might be ready to start working with us, select it from the provided options (e.g., Immediate, Next 3 months, Next 6 months, Next 12 months, or Unknown).
* **Preferred Reward Option** (`referral_bonus_preference`, select_one, Optional): If you would like to be rewarded for your referral, select how you would like to be rewarded (e.g., Monetary commission, Discount on services, Credit toward future services, or Other).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_notes`, text, Optional): Provide any additional comments or information about the referred contact.
