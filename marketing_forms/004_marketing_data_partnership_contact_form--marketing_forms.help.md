# Marketing Data Partnership Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for potential marketing data partners to provide contact information and indicate their partnership interest. It is not for general inquiries or sales pitches.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your business email address in the email field.
3. Provide your phone number in the phone field.
4. Enter your company name in the company field (if applicable).
5. Type in a brief message in the message field (if you wish to provide additional information).
6. Select your partnership interest from the dropdown menu.
7. Choose your preferred contact method from the dropdown menu.
8. Choose your preferred contact time from the dropdown menu (if you wish).
9. If you have any further information, enter it in the message 2 field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name here.
* **Email**: Enter your professional email address. This will be used for communication.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number. We will use this to contact you.
* **Company**: If you represent a company, enter it here. If not, leave blank.
* **Partnership Interest**: Select one of the options below:
	+ Partnership: We are interested in forming a partnership.
	+ Collaboration: We are interested in collaborating on a project.
	+ Sales: We are interested in purchasing your services.
	+ Other: Other (please specify in the message field).
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Select one of the options below:
	+ Phone Call: We prefer to be contacted by phone.
	+ Email: We prefer to be contacted by email.
	+ Chat: We prefer to be contacted by chat.
	+ Other: Other (please specify in the message field).
* **Preferred Contact Time**: Select one of the options below:
	+ Morning: We are available to be contacted in the morning.
	+ Afternoon: We are available to be contacted in the afternoon.
	+ Evenings: We are available to be contacted in the evenings.
	+ Weekends: We are available to be contacted on weekends.
* **Message 2**: If you have any additional information, enter it here.
* **Message**: Type in a brief message if you wish to provide additional information.
