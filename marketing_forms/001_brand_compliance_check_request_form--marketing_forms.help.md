# Brand Compliance Check Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Brand Compliance Check Request Form is a critical tool for ensuring that all marketing materials align with the brand identity and comply with relevant laws and regulations. It helps to identify and address potential issues before they go live, preventing costly mistakes and reputational damage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full legal name in the "Requestor Full Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Requestor Email Address" field. This will be used for updates and notifications.
3. Select your department from the "Department" field, as it is essential to track internal stakeholders' access to the review process.
4. Enter the name of the project requiring compliance review in the "Project Name" field.
5. Choose the type of compliance check required in the "Type of Compliance Check" field.
6. Describe the materials requiring review in the "Materials Description" field.
7. Select the target audience for the materials in the "Target Audience" field.
8. Choose the distribution channels where the materials will be used in the "Distribution Channels" field. (Select all applicable options.)
9. Specify the review deadline in the "Review Deadline" field, which is the date when the review should be completed.
10. Indicate the priority level of the review in the "Priority Level" field, as it will help in scheduling the review process.
11. Confirm if brand guidelines are attached in the "Have you attached brand guidelines" field.
12. Provide any additional comments or information that may aid the reviewer in the "Additional Comments" field.
13. Sign the form electronically with your full name in the "Electronic Signature" field.
14. Record the date of the request in the "Submission Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requestor Full Name** (`requestor_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your full legal name.
* **Requestor Email Address** (`requestor_email`, `email`, **required**): Provide your email address where updates and notifications will be sent.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, **required**): Select your department to ensure proper tracking and access control.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter the name of the project requiring compliance review.
* **Type of Compliance Check** (`compliance_type`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose the type of compliance check needed for the project.
* **Materials Description** (`materials_description`, `text`, **required**): Describe the materials requiring review in detail.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, `select_one`, **optional**): Indicate who the materials are intended for.
* **Distribution Channels** (`distribution_channels`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Specify where the materials will be distributed or displayed. (Select all applicable options.)
* **Review Deadline** (`deadline_date`, `date`, **required**): Set the review deadline to ensure timely completion.
* **Priority Level** (`priority_level`, `select_one`, **required**): Indicate the urgency level of the review, which affects scheduling.
* **Have you attached brand guidelines** (`brand_guidelines_attached`, `select_one`, **required**): Confirm if brand guidelines have been attached for review.
* **Previous Review Reference** (`previous_review_reference`, `text`, **optional**): If the project has been reviewed before, provide a reference number.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, **optional**): Add any extra information that may aid the reviewer.
* **Electronic Signature** (`signature`, `text`, **required**): Sign the form electronically with your full name.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, **required**): Record the date the request is made.
