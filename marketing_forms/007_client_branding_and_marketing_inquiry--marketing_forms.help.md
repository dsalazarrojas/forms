# Client Branding And Marketing Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your branding and marketing requirements so we can provide you with tailored services and solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields with accurate and detailed information.
2. For fields with multiple options, select all relevant choices to help us understand your needs.
3. If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text, required): Your legal first name.
*   **Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, text, required): Your legal last name.
*   **Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Your primary contact email address.
*   **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Your best contact number.
*   **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Your company's legal name.
*   **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, optional): Your current company website URL.
*   **Industry** (`company_industry`, select one, required): Your business sector.
*   **Company Size** (`company_size`, select one, required): Number of employees in your company.
*   **Project Type** (`project_type`, select multiple, required): Services you need (e.g., Brand Strategy, Logo Design, etc.).
*   **Primary Project Goals** (`project_goals`, select multiple, required): What you want to achieve (e.g., Increase Brand Awareness, Generate More Leads, etc.).
*   **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, text, required): Describe your ideal customer.
*   **Target Demographics** (`target_demographics`, text, optional): Age, gender, location, etc.
*   **Current Branding Status** (`current_branding_status`, select one, required): Your current branding status (e.g., Have Complete Brand Guidelines, Have Logo Only, etc.).
*   **Main Competitors** (`competitors`, text, optional): List 2-3 main competitors.
*   **Project Timeline** (`project_timeline`, select one, required): When you need this project completed.
*   **Project Budget Range** (`project_budget`, select one, required): Estimated budget for this project.
*   **Marketing Channels of Interest** (`marketing_channels`, select multiple, optional): Where you want to market (e.g., Social Media, Search Engines, etc.).
*   **Brand Personality Traits** (`brand_personality`, select multiple, required): How your brand should feel (e.g., Professional, Friendly, Innovative, etc.).
*   **Brands You Admire** (`inspiration_brands`, text, optional): Examples of brands you like.
*   **Additional Notes or Requirements** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Anything else we should know.
*   **How Did You Hear About Us?** (`how_did_you_hear`, select one, optional): Referral source.
*   **Inquiry Date** (`inquiry_date`, date, required): Date of your inquiry.
