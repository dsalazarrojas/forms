# Voice Search Optimization Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a website's readiness and optimization status for voice search engines.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the site details, including its name and any other relevant information.
2. Specify the site's goals, such as increasing sales or improving user engagement.
3. Assess the site's readiness for voice search, including its current status and any challenges it faces.
4. Select the relevant keywords that the site targets.
5. Identify the target audience for the site.
6. Enter the site's URL.
7. Choose the type of site (static or dynamic).
8. Select the traffic sources for the site (organic or paid).
9. Identify the voice assistance platforms used (Amazon Alexa or Google Assistant).
10. Choose the voice search engine used (Google or Bing).
11. Determine the site's optimization status (optimized or not optimized).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Site Details**: Enter a brief description of the website, including its name and any other relevant information.
* **Goals**: Specify the main goals of the website, such as increasing sales or improving user engagement.
* **Readiness**: Assess the site's current readiness for voice search, including any challenges or obstacles it faces.
* **Keywords**: Select the relevant keywords that the site targets. For example, if the site sells apples, select "apple".
* **Target Audience**: Identify the target audience for the site, such as men or women.
* **Site URL**: Enter the URL of the website.
* **Site Type**: Choose the type of site (static or dynamic).
* **Traffic**: Select the traffic sources for the site, such as organic or paid.
* **Voice Assistance**: Identify the voice assistance platforms used, such as Amazon Alexa or Google Assistant.
* **Voice Search Engine**: Choose the voice search engine used, such as Google or Bing.
* **Site Optimization Status**: Determine the site's optimization status, such as optimized or not optimized.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information about the website.
- Select the relevant options for each field based on the site's actual status.
- If you are unsure about any field, you can leave it blank or ask someone else for clarification.
- This form is a self-assessment tool, and the answers provided will be used to identify areas of improvement and suggest recommendations for voice search optimization.
