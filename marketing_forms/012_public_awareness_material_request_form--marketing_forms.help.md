# public_awareness_material_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request public awareness materials such as brochures, posters, and digital assets. It is designed to help internal staff or external stakeholders provide detailed information about their requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields for each section below.
2. Ensure that all required fields have been completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Brochure Request** (1, text, required): Please provide the title of the brochure or asset you are requesting. Make sure to include any relevant details such as size or edition.
* **Poster Request** (2, text, required): Enter the title or description of the poster you are requesting.
* **Digital Asset Request** (3, text, required): Enter the title or description of the digital asset you are requesting.
* **Contact Request** (4, text, required): Enter a contact method for the person requesting the material, such as a phone number or email address.
* **Request Details** (5, text, required): Provide any additional comments or details about your request.
* **Brochure Format** (6, select_one, required): Select the format of the brochure or asset you are requesting (Poster, Brochure, Digital).
* **Poster Size** (7, number, required): Enter the size of the poster you are requesting (e.g. 18 x 24 inches).
* **Request Priority** (8, select_one, required): Select the priority of your request (High, Medium, Low).
* **Asset Type** (9, select_multiple, required): Select the type of asset you are requesting (Brochure, Poster, Digital).
* **Request Prophet** (10, select_multiple, required): Select the person or entity requesting the asset (John, Mary, David).
* **Request Date** (11, date, required): Enter the date when you need the request to be fulfilled.
* **Request Time** (12, time, required): Enter the time when you need the request to be fulfilled.
* **Email** (13, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (14, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (15, text, required): Enter your physical address.
* **Comment** (16, text, required): Provide any additional comments or details about your request.
* **Note** (17, note, required): This is an optional field for any additional comments or notes.
* **Assigned User** (18, select_multiple, required): Select whether the request is assigned to a specific user or not.
* **Assigned Tool** (19, text, required): Enter the tool or software used to fulfill the request.
* **Created By** (20, text, required): Enter the name of the person creating the request.
* **Created Date** (21, date, required): Enter the date the request was created.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your information for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant department or team.
