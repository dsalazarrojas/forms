# Customer Loyalty Gamification Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers to tailor our customer loyalty program to their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information about yourself.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all required fields are filled.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Details
* **Customer ID**: (`id`, `text`, required): Enter your unique ID or identifier as per your company's protocol.
* **Customer Name**: (`name`, `text`, required): Fill in your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Contact Information**: (`email`, `text`, optional): Enter your email address or other contact details if you'd like to be reached out.
### Program Details
* **Program Selection**: (`program_name`, `text`, required): Choose the specific loyalty program you're interested in.
* **Program Level**: (`program_level`, `text`, required): Select the level of involvement you'd like to have in the program.
* **Preferred Communication Method**: (`communication_method`, `text`, required): Choose how you'd like to receive updates and information about the program.
### Agreement
* **Agreement**: (`agreement`, `text`, required): Confirm that you agree to participate in the program and abide by its rules.
## Tips
* Please fill in all required fields to ensure that your application is complete and processed promptly.
* If you have any doubts or queries, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for assistance.
* This form is for the purpose of customer data collection only and will not be used for any other purpose.
