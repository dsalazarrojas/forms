# promotional_giveaway_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The promotional giveaway order form is used to place orders for promotional giveaways, contests, or promotions. Fill out this form to place your order and provide necessary information for fulfillment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your name in the **Name** field.
* Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
* Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
* Select the type of promotion you are interested in from the **Promotion Type** dropdown menu.
* Enter the name of the promotion in the **Promotion Name** field.
* Choose the type of participant (individual or team) in the **Participant Type** field.
* Enter the quantity of items you wish to order in the **Quantity** field.
* Enter the date and time you would like the giveaway to take place in the **Giveaway Date** and **Giveaway Time** fields, respectively.
* Enter the date you would like the promotion to end in the **End Date** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (text, required): Enter your name to be associated with this order.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address to receive communication about your order.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter a phone number for contact and communication purposes.
* **Promotion Type** (select_one, optional): Choose the type of promotion (giveaway, contest, or promotion) you are interested in.
* **Promotion Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the promotion you are interested in (if applicable).
* **Participant Type** (select_one, optional): Choose the type of participant (individual or team) for this order.
* **Quantity** (number, optional): Enter the quantity of items you wish to order.
* **Giveaway Date** (date, optional): Enter the date you would like the giveaway to take place.
* **Giveaway Time** (time, optional): Enter the time you would like the giveaway to take place.
* **End Date** (date, optional): Enter the date you would like the promotion to end.
* **Form Footer** (note, not required): This field is not used for data entry, but is intended to display a message to the user, such as a thank you note or important instructions.
