# market_feasibility_research_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to submit a request for market feasibility research. The purpose of this form is to gather information about your research topic, objectives, and requirements in order to assess the feasibility of your project. Fill out this form to initiate the research process and an assigned team member will review and follow up with you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Click on the "Submit" button to send your research request.
* Fill in the "What is your research topic" field with a brief description of your research topic.
* Provide a "Research brief" that outlines the scope of your research project.
* Select the "Target audience" for your research from the available options (Business, Consumer, or Other).
* Choose the primary "Research objective" from the available options (Cost reduction, Revenue growth, or Competitive advantage).
* Select the research "Methodologies" you would like to use for your project (Surveys, Interviews, or Case studies).
* If required, upload any "Research brief attachments" related to your project.
* Indicate how you would like to receive the "submission details" (Form submission, Email, or Phone).
* Provide your "Email" address and/or "Phone" number.
* Add any "submission notes" you would like to include with your request.
* If necessary, upload "Research brief attachments files".
* Select the "Submission status" from the available options (Draft, Approved, or Completed).
* If necessary, provide the "Form submission URL" and "Form submission date" and time".
* Select the "Assigned user" who will be responsible for your project (Jimmy, Other, or no selection).
* If necessary, provide their "Assigned user email" and/or "Assigned user phone number".
* Add any "Assigned user notes" you would like to include.
* Select the "Created by" who initiated the project (Admin, Other, or no selection).
* If necessary, provide their "Created by email" and/or "Created by phone number".
* Add any "Created by notes" you would like to include.
* Finally, click on the "Submit" button to complete the form and initiate the research process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your research topic**: Describe your research topic in a few sentences.
* **Research brief**: Write a brief outline of your research project, including its scope and requirements.
* **Target audience**: Select the group you would like your research to target (Business, Consumer, or Other).
* **Research objectives**: Select the main objective of your research project (Cost reduction, Revenue growth, or Competitive advantage).
* **Research methodologies**: Choose the research methods you would like to use (Surveys, Interviews, or Case studies).
* **Research brief attachments**: Attach any relevant files or documents related to your research brief.
* **Submission details**: Indicate how you would like to receive the project submission (Form submission, Email, or Phone).
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Submission notes**: Add any additional information you would like to include with your submission.
* **Research brief attachments files**: Attach any additional files or documents related to your research brief.
* **Submission status**: Select the current status of your submission (Draft, Approved, or Completed).
* **Form submission URL**: If your submission was completed through this form, enter the submission URL.
* **Form submission date**: Enter the date and time of your submission.
* **Assigned user**: Select the user who will be responsible for your project (Jimmy, Other, or no selection).
* **Assigned user email**: Enter the email address of the assigned user.
* **Assigned user phone**: Enter the phone number of the assigned user.
* **Assigned user notes**: Add any additional information for the assigned user.
* **Created by**: Select the user who initiated the research project (Admin, Other, or no selection).
* **Created by email**: Enter the email address of the creator.
* **Created by phone**: Enter the phone number of the creator.
* **Created by notes**: Add any additional information for the creator.
* **Form submission URL**: If your submission was completed through this form, enter the submission URL.
* **Form submission date**: Enter the date and time of your submission.
* **Form submission time**: Enter the time of your submission.
* **Created by**: Select the user who initiated the project (Admin, Other, or no selection).
* **Created by email**: Enter the email address of the creator.
* **Created by phone**: Enter the phone number of the creator.
* **Created by notes**: Add any additional information for the creator.

Note: This form is for internal use within the organization, and all fields are optional except where specified as required.
