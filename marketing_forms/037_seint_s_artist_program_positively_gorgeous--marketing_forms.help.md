# Seints-Artist-Program-Positively-Gorgeous - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals who are interested in Seint's Artist Program. It is used to collect contact information and gather information on how you heard about the program and what interests you within the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your Full Name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your Email in the "Email" field. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
3. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. If you have a question about the program, select 'Yes' or 'No' in the "Question" field.
5. If you are interested in Seint's Artist Program, select 'Yes' or 'No' in the "Submit" field.
6. Select how you heard about Seint's Artist Program from the list in the "How did you hear about Seint's Artist Program?" field. If you selected 'Other', please provide the source in the "Additional Comments" field.
7. If you have any additional comments or questions, please use the "Additional Comments" field.
8. Finally, select your interests in Seint's Artist Program from the list in the "What interests you about Seint's Artist Program?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (contact_name, text, required): Please fill in your full name for us to contact you.
* **Email** (contact_email, email, required): Please enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone** (contact_phone, text, required): Please enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Question** (contact_question, select_one, required): This field is used to gauge your interest in the program. Select 'Yes' if you have a question about the program.
* **Submit** (contact_submit, select_multiple, required): This field is used to indicate your interest in Seint's Artist Program. Select 'Yes' if you are interested.
* **How did you hear about Seint's Artist Program?** (artist_program_interesting, select_one, required): Please select from the list how you heard about Seint's Artist Program.
* **Additional Comments** (additional_comments, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, please use this field.
* **What interests you about Seint's Artist Program?** (contact_artist_program_interesting, select_one, optional): Select from the list what interests you about Seint's Artist Program.
* **What interests you about Seint's Artist Program? 2** (contact_artist_program_interesting2, select_multiple, optional): Select from the list your additional interests in Seint's Artist Program.
