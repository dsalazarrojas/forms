# Affiliate Dashboard Access Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting access to the affiliate dashboard, which provides valuable information and tools to manage and optimize your affiliate marketing activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Affiliate Information**
	* Enter your complete legal name in the **Full Name** field.
2. **Affiliate Details**
	* Enter your preferred username for the dashboard in the **Preferred Username** field.
	* Enter your primary email address in the **Email Address** field, which will be used for account access and notifications.
3. **Contact Information**
	* Enter your phone number in the **Phone Number** field (optional).
	* If applicable, enter your company name in the **Company Name** field.
4. **Program Information**
	* Enter the name of the affiliate program you're associated with in the **Affiliate Program Name** field.
	* Enter your unique affiliate ID in the **Affiliate ID** field.
	* Enter the date you joined the affiliate program in the **Join Date** field.
5. **Traffic Sources**
	* Select your primary sources of traffic from the list in the **Traffic Sources** field.
6. **Traffic Volume**
	* Choose the approximate number of visitors you generate monthly in the **Average Monthly Traffic** field.
7. **Access Request**
	* Select the level of access you're requesting in the **Requested Access Level** field.
	* Enter the reason for your access request in the **Reason for Requesting Access** field.
8. **Intended Use**
	* Describe how you plan to use the dashboard information in the **Intended Use of Dashboard** field.
9. **Reports and Data**
	* Select which reports or data points you need access to in the **Specific Reports or Data Needed** field.
10. **Security and Compliance**
	* Acknowledge that you'll follow all security protocols and protect sensitive data in the **Security Measures Acknowledged** field.
	* Agree to use the data solely for affiliate marketing purposes in the **Data Usage Agreement** field.
	* Acknowledge that you've read and agree to the privacy policy in the **Privacy Policy Acknowledged** field.
	* Agree to the terms of service for dashboard access in the **Terms of Service Agreement** field.
11. **Verification**
	* Confirm that you're prepared to provide identity verification documents in the **Identity Verification Documents** field.
	* Confirm that you're prepared to provide business verification documents (if applicable) in the **Business Verification Documents** field.
	* Indicate whether you're authorized to request dashboard access on behalf of your organization in the **Authorization Representative** field.
12. **Emergency Contact**
	* Enter the contact person's name and contact information in the **Emergency Contact** field (optional).
13. **Authorization**
	* Provide an electronic signature to authorize this request in the **Signature Authorization** field.
14. **Final Check**
	* Review your form carefully to ensure all required fields are completed accurately.

## Tips
* Be sure to fill in all required fields (*).
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.

By following this guide, you'll be able to complete the Affiliate Dashboard Access Request form accurately and efficiently, ensuring a smooth and successful request process.
