# Email Capture Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Email Capture Form is used to collect your contact information, including your email address and phone number, so we can get in touch with you for marketing, promotions, or other relevant communications.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field. This is the primary address we will use to contact you.
2. Select the option that best matches the way you want to confirm your email address: "email" to receive an automated confirmation email or "confirm email" to receive a phone call or SMS to confirm your address.
3. If you prefer to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Email Address (Email Address)** (<required/optional>): Enter your email address. Please use the format `username@domain.com`. If you are unsure, just enter the address that you use most frequently.
* **Confirm Email Address (select_one)** (<required/optional>): Select how you want to confirm your email address: "email" to receive an automated email or "confirm email" to receive a phone call or SMS.
* **Phone (Phone)** (<required/optional>): Enter your phone number if you prefer to be contacted by phone. Please use the format `+1234567890` or `123-456-7890`.

## Tips
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask for clarification.
* Double-check that your email address and phone number are accurate before submitting the form.
* If you are not comfortable sharing your email address or phone number, do not complete this form.
