# Sportswear Line Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to gather information for the launch of a new sportswear line. It is designed to capture key details about the launch, including the launch date, collection deadline, product images, release date, launch location, marketing assets, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the launch date from the calendar picker.
2. Choose the collection deadline.
3. Upload product images for the new product.
4. Enter the release date.
5. Select the launch location.
6. Enter marketing assets and collection images.
7. Write a short launch story.
8. Enter product description.
9. Identify the target audience.
10. Enter the launch budget.
11. Add any launch notes.
12. Enter the launch assets link.
13. Write a collection story.
14. Enter the collection budget.
15. Add launch location notes.
16. Select the launch location.
17. Enter the collection link.
18. Add collection notes.
19. Enter the launch team.
20. Enter the marketing strategy.
21. Enter the collection strategy.
22. Enter launch assets.
23. Enter collection assets.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Launch Date** (1, Launch Date, required/optional): Enter the date when the new sportswear line will be launched.
* **Collection Deadline** (2, Collection Deadline, required/optional): Choose the deadline for the collection.
* **Product Images** (3, Product Images, required/optional): Upload one or more reported images for the new product.
* **Release Date** (4, Release Date, required/optional): Enter the date when the new sportswear line will be released.
* **Launch Location** (5, Launch Location, required/optional): Choose where the launch will take place.
* **Marketing Assets** (6, Marketing Assets, required/optional): Enter marketing assets related to the launch.
* **Collection Images** (7, Collection Images, required/optional): Upload one or more reported images for the collection.
* **Launch Video** (8, Launch Video, required/optional): Enter a short video for the launch event.
* **Launch Story** (9, Launch Story, required/optional): Write a short story about the launch event.
* **Product Description** (10, Product Description, required/optional): Enter a description of the new sportswear line.
* **Target Audience** (11, Target Audience, required/optional): Identify the target audience for this launch.
* **Launch Budget** (12, Launch Budget, required/optional): Enter the allocated budget for the launch.
* **Launch Notes** (13, Launch Notes, required/optional): Add any notes about the launch.
* **Launch Assets Link** (14, Launch Assets Link, required/optional): Enter the link to launch assets.
* **Collection Story** (15, Collection Story, required/optional): Write a story about the collection.
* **Collection Budget** (16, Collection Budget, required/optional): Enter the allocated budget for the collection.
* **Launch Location Notes** (17, Launch Location Notes, required/optional): Add any notes about the launch location.
* **Launch Location** (18, Launch Location, required/optional): Choose where the launch will take place.
* **Collection Link** (19, Collection Link, required/optional): Enter the link to the collection.
* **Collection Notes** (20, Collection Notes, required/optional): Add any notes about the collection.
* **Launch Team** (21, Launch Team, required/optional): Enter the team involved in the launch.
* **Marketing Strategy** (22, Marketing Strategy, required/optional): Enter the marketing strategy for the launch.
* **Collection Strategy** (23, Collection Strategy, required/optional): Enter the strategy for the collection.
* **Launch Assets** (24, Launch Assets, required/optional): Enter launch assets.
* **Collection Assets** (25, Collection Assets, required/optional): Enter collection assets.

Note: The fields with 'required/optional' label are either required or optional depending on the specific use case.
