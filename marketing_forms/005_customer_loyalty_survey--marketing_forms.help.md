# Customer Loyalty Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information from customers about their satisfaction level, contact information, and purchase history. The data collected will be used to improve customer loyalty programs and overall customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the customer satisfaction level question by selecting one of the options below the field.
2. Enter your email address and phone number in the corresponding fields.
3. Write any feedback or comments about your recent purchases or experiences.
4. Select any applicable customer loyalty programs you are or are not part of.
5. Enter the date and time of your last purchase.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Satisfaction Level** (`customer_satisfaction_level`, select_one, **Required**): This question asks about your current satisfaction level with our company. Please select one of the options: very satisfied, satisfied, not satisfied, or very unhappy.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **Required**): Enter your email address. This will be used to contact you for any further questions or to follow up on your feedback.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, **Required**): Enter your phone number. This will be used to contact you for any further questions or to follow up on your feedback.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, **Optional**): Write any comments or feedback about your recent purchases or experiences with our company. This is your chance to tell us about any positive or negative feedback you may have.
* **Customer Loyalty Program** (`customer_loyalty_program`, select_multiple, **Optional**): Select any applicable customer loyalty programs you are or are not part of.
* **Date of Last Purchase** (`date_of_last_purchase`, date, **Required**): Enter the date of your last purchase. This will help us track your purchase history.
* **Time of Last Purchase** (`time_of_last_purchase`, time, **Required**): Enter the time of your last purchase. This will help us track your purchase history and schedule.
