# identity_creative_agency_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from creative agencies interested in creating an identity for their brand, product, or service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all the fields you feel are necessary for your identity project.
2. Ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date contact information.
3. Use the form tags to indicate which type of agency you are (e.g. branding, web design, digital marketing).
4. If you have any social media presence, fill it out to make it easier for us to contact you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title**: A short title that describes the identity project (e.g. branding, web design, product identity).
* **Contact Name**: Your full name or the name of the person responsible for the identity project.
* **Contact Email**: Your email address for communication and feedback.
* **Contact Phone**: Your phone number for quicker communication.
* **Contact Address**: Your agency's address for postal communication.
* **Contact Website**: Your agency's website for more information.
* **Contact Facebook**: Your agency's Facebook page for social media communication.
* **Contact Twitter**: Your agency's Twitter handle for quick updates.
* **Contact Instagram**: Your agency's Instagram handle for visual representation.
* **Contact LinkedIn**: Your agency's LinkedIn profile for professional networking.
* **Contact YouTube**: Your agency's YouTube channel for video content.
* **Contact Pinterest**: Your agency's Pinterest page for creative inspiration.
* **Contact Tumblr**: Your agency's Tumblr blog for short posts.
* **Contact Snapchat**: Your agency's Snapchat account for quick updates.
* **Contact Email List**: Your agency's email list for newsletters and updates.
* **Contact Other**: Other contact information not listed above (e.g. Slack, Discord, etc.).
* **Form Description**: A brief description of the identity project.
* **Agency Type**: A short description of your agency type (e.g. branding, web design, digital marketing).
* **Marketing Channel**: The platform(s) where your agency will be marketing (e.g. social media, email, advertising).
* **Target Audience**: A brief description of your target audience.
* **Marketing Objectives**: A brief description of your marketing objectives.
* **Key Messages**: A few key messages you want to convey.
* **Form Tags**: A list of tags describing your agency type (e.g. branding, web design, digital marketing).
* **Submit**: Submit the form to send your information to us.

## Tips
* Please ensure you fill out all required fields.
* Use clear and concise language in all fields.
* If you have any questions or need help, feel free to ask us at form@gic.mx.
