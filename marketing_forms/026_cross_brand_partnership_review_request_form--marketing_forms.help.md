## cross_brand_partnership_review_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **cross-brand partnership review request form** is used for partners to request a review of their cross-brand partnership. This form is meant to be completed by the partner themselves, and it will be used by our team to evaluate and review their partnership status. This form is not meant to be used for other purposes, such as requesting access to new features or reporting technical issues. If you are experiencing any issues with our platform, please use the [report issue] [link to issue report form] instead.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow the instructions on each page. Each page is divided into sections with clear labels and explanations for each field. Make sure to provide as much detail as possible in each field to help our review team evaluate your partnership effectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Details** (Note): This is a free text field where you can provide any additional information about your request.
* **Partner Name**: Enter the name of your company or brand.
* **Partner Email**: Enter your partner email address. This will be used to contact you if we need more information.
* **Partner Phone**: Enter your partner phone number. This will be used to contact you if we need more information.
* **Partner Address**: Enter your partner address. This will help us understand your location.
* **Business Name**: Enter the name of your business.
* **Business Type**: Select the type of business you operate (Sole Proprietor, Partnership, LLC, Corporation).
* **Business Description**: Enter a brief description of your business.
* **Business Website**: Enter the URL of your business website.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person we can contact within your company.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Partner Since**: Enter the date you started your partnership with us.
* **Partner Until**: Enter the date your partnership will end.
* **Business Since**: Enter the date your business was established.
* **Business Until**: Enter the date your business will cease operations.
* **Last Interaction**: Enter the date of your last interaction with us.

Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please contact us at [support email].
