# 2020 2021 Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 2020 2021 Registration Form is designed for individuals to register for dance classes, courses, or other events. This form collects information about the registrant's personal details, dance experience, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and ensure you understand what information is being requested.
2. Enter your information in the designated fields.
3. Select your experience level and preferred dance style.
4. Provide additional information if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your legal first and last name.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter the email address where you want to receive updates and receipts.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, `phone`, optional): Enter your phone number, including country code if outside the U.S.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Age** (`age`, `integer`, optional): Enter your age.
* **Mailing address** (`mailing_address`, `text`, optional): Enter your street address, city, state, and ZIP.
* **Emergency contact name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the full name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency contact phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `phone`, optional): Enter the phone number of the person to contact in case of an emergency, including country code if outside the U.S.
* **Experience level** (`experience_level`, `select_one`, required): Select your current skill level: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Professional.
* **Preferred dance style** (`preferred_style`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the dance style you are most interested in: Ballet, Hip-Hop, Contemporary, Jazz, Tap, Ballroom, or other (type in).
* **Number of dance classes attended** (`previous_classes`, `integer`, optional): Enter the total number of dance classes attended in the past 12 months.
* **How did you hear about us?** (`how_heard`, `select_one`, optional): Select how you heard about the dance class or event.
* **Which social media platforms do you follow us on?** (`social_media_follow`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check all social media platforms you follow us on.
* **Any special requirements or accommodations?** (`special_needs`, `text`, optional): Enter any special requirements or accommodations you may need.
* **Additional comments or notes** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any other information you'd like to share with us.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are filled out.
* Enter accurate and complete information.
* If you need assistance or have questions, contact the dance studio or organization hosting the event.
