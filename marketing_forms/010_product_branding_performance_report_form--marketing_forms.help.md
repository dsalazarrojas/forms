# Product Branding Performance Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a product's branding performance. It's meant to help analyze and evaluate the product's brand identity, customer satisfaction, and performance metrics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the **Product Brand** from the provided options.
2. Fill in the **Product Name** and **Product Image** fields to provide a clear description of the product.
3. Enter the **Product Description**, **Product Tagline**, and **Product Price** to help understand the product's features and value.
4. Rate the product's performance with numbers in the **Performance Score** and **Improvement Score** fields.
5. Select the **Marketing Channel** used for the product's promotion.
6. Fill in the **Product Category**, **Target Audience**, and **Customer Satisfaction** fields to understand the product's market position and customer feedback.
7. Enter the **Product Launch Date** and **Brand Launch Date** to track the product's release timeline.
8. Provide a brief description of the product's **Brand Value Proposition**, **Brand Identity**, **Brand Positioning**, and **Brand Consistency** to understand the product's brand essence.
9. Finally, fill in any other required fields and review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Brand**: Select the brand name from the provided options.
* **Strengths**: List the product's key strengths.
* **Weaknesses**: List the product's key weaknesses.
* **Improvement Ideas**: Suggest ideas to improve the product.
* **Product Name**: Enter the product's name.
* **Product Image**: Provide a descriptive image of the product.
* **Performance Score**: Rate the product's performance on a scale of 1-5.
* **Improvement Score**: Rate the product's improvement potential on a scale of 1-5.
* **Product Category**: Select the product's category.
* **Target Audience**: Identify the target audience for the product.
* **Marketing Channel**: Select the marketing channel used for the product's promotion.
* **Product Tagline**: Enter a short description of the product.
* **Product Description**: Enter a detailed description of the product.
* **Product Price**: Enter the product's price.
* **Product Rating**: Rate the product's rating on a scale of 1-5.
* **Brand Equity**: Rate the brand's equity on a scale of 1-5.
* **Product Reputation**: Rate the product's reputation on a scale of 1-5.
* **Customer Satisfaction**: Rate customer satisfaction on a scale of 1-5.
* **Product Launch Date**: Enter the product's launch date.
* **Brand Launch Date**: Enter the brand's launch date.
* **Brand Value Proposition**: Describe the brand's value proposition.
* **Brand Identity**: Describe the brand's identity.
* **Brand Positioning**: Describe the brand's positioning.
* **Brand Consistency**: Rate the brand's consistency on a scale of 1-5.

Please fill out the form carefully, and if you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
