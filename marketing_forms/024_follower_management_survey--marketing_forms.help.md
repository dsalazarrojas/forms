# Follower Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Follower Management Survey is designed to collect information about followers, including demographics, engagement level, and preferences. This data will help the marketing team understand the followers and tailor their marketing strategies accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Select the correct options from the dropdown menus.
3. Enter any required fields, such as follower engagement level.
4. Add any additional comments or notes about the follower, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Follower Demographics**: This section collects demographic information about the follower.
* **Follower Age Group**: Select the age group that best fits the follower's age.
* **Follower Education Level**: Select all applicable education levels that apply to the follower.
* **Follower Occupation**: Enter the follower's occupation.
* **Follower Average Time Spent**: Enter the follower's average time spent.
* **Follower Awareness About Followers**: Enter a number to indicate how aware the follower is about the followers.
* **Follower Engagement Level**: Select the follower's engagement level.
* **Follower Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the follower.
* **Follower Important Dates**: Enter any important dates related to the follower.
* **Follower Phone Number**: Enter the follower's phone number.
* **Follower Email Address**: Enter the follower's email address.
* **Follower Comments**: Add any additional comments about the follower.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form completely and honestly to ensure accurate data collection.
* If a field is not applicable, leave it blank.
* The form should only be filled out once for each follower.
* If you need help, contact the marketing team for assistance.
