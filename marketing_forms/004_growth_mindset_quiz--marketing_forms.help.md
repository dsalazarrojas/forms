**Growth Mindset Quiz - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The Growth Mindset Quiz is a set of questions designed to assess your attitudes and habits related to personal growth, learning, and self-improvement. By completing this quiz, you'll gain insight into your current mindset and identify areas where you can grow and improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions on each page. Each page has a single question, and you can respond in as much detail as you'd like to each one. Don't worry about word limits, just answer honestly and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is a growth mindset?**: This question is an introduction to the quiz, and it helps us understand what a growth mindset means to you. Please describe what you think a growth mindset is.
* **How do you approach challenges?**: When faced with a difficult task or obstacle, how do you react? Do you get frustrated, or do you try to find a way to overcome it? Describe your approach to challenges.
* **How do you respond to setbacks?**: We all experience setbacks or failures at some point in our lives. How do you handle these situations? Do you get discouraged, or do you learn from them?
* **How do you learn from feedback?**: Feedback is an opportunity to learn and improve. How do you typically respond to feedback, whether positive or negative?
* **How do you handle mistakes?**: Everyone makes mistakes, but how do you deal with them when they happen? Do you get defensive, or do you see them as a chance to learn and grow?
* **How do you stay motivated?**: What keeps you going when faced with a tough task or when you feel like giving up? Describe your motivation strategies.
* **How do you build on successes?**: When you achieve a goal or complete a challenging task, how do you celebrate and build on that success? Do you tend to focus on what went wrong, or do you bask in the achievement?
* **What are your goals?**: Finally, what are your short-term and long-term goals? Are you clear about what you want to achieve, and how do you plan to get there?
