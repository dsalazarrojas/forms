# Feedback Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Feedback Survey Form is designed to collect customer feedback on how to improve the product or service offered. It is a brief and non-intrusive way to gather user's opinions, thoughts, and suggestions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the following steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and in detail.
2. Select the correct options for your answers.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, provide them in the "Additional comments" field.
4. Enter your email address and phone number if you would like to be contacted about your feedback.
5. Provide your rating and explanation for your rating in the "Overall rating" and "Why do you think this is your rating?" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What can we improve?** (`feedback_questions`, `note`, required: false): This is a text field where you can provide any feedback or suggestions you have on how to improve the product or service.
* **Rate your experience** (`customer_feedback`, `select_one`, required: false): This is a rating system where you can select how satisfied you are with our product or service. The options are Very Dissatisfied, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Satisfied, and Very Satisfied.
* **How can we contact you?** (`contact_details`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This is a field where you can select how you would like to be contacted in case we need to follow up on your feedback. The options are Phone, Email, Text Message, and Social Media.
* **Additional comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: false): This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions you have.
* **What is your email?** (`email`, `email`, required: false): This is the field where you can enter your email address if you would like to be contacted about your feedback.
* **What is your phone number?** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): This is the field where you can enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Today's date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): This is the field where you can select the current date.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): This is the field where you can select the current time.
* **Overall rating** (`rating_score`, `number`, required: false): This is a field where you can rate your overall experience with our product or service.
* **Why do you think this is your rating?** (`rating_comment`, `note`, required: false): This is a text field where you can explain your rating and why you chose that particular rating.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions honestly and accurately.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them in the "Additional comments" field.
* If you would like to be contacted about your feedback, please enter your email address and phone number.
* Your feedback is valuable to us, so please don't hesitate to share your thoughts and suggestions.
