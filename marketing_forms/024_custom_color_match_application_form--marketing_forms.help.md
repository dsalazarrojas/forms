# custom_color_match_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers or users regarding their preferences and details. The goal is to gather accurate and relevant data to match colors or provide customized services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields as instructed below.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and relevant information to ensure the best possible outcome.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info** (`Customer Info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name and any other identifying information as prompted.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your contact details such as email and phone number to allow us to get in touch with you.
* **Skin Info** (`Skin Info`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to gather more information about your skin, which might include type, color, or other related details.
* **Tan Info** (`Tan Info`, `text`, required/optional): Provide more information about your tan, which might include duration, intensity, or other related details.
* **Custom Color Preferences** (`Custom Color Preferences`, `text`, required/optional): Share your preferred custom color options. Be as specific as possible to ensure we can accurately match your requirements.
