# Book Launch Crew Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Book Launch Crew Registration form is a tool for authors and publishers to collaborate with book enthusiasts, like book bloggers and reviewers, to promote books and increase their online presence. By filling out this form, you'll be able to express your interest in joining the Book Launch Crew and participating in the book launch process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Book Launch Crew Registration form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out your personal details, including your full name, email address, and phone number (if you have one).
2.  Choose your primary social media platform and approximate follower count.
3.  Select the types of content you're interested in creating (e.g., book reviews, blog posts, etc.).
4.  Enter the title, author, and launch date of the book you're interested in supporting.
5.  Choose how you'd like to help (e.g., social media posts, book reviews, etc.) and when you're available to post (e.g., pre-launch teasers, launch day, etc.).
6.  If you need an advance review copy (ARC), select the type of format you prefer.
7.  If you need a physical ARC, enter your shipping address.
8.  Choose your preferred genres to work with.
9.  If you've worked with this author before, select 'True'.
10.  If you have any special skills or resources, mention them in the 'Special Skills or Resources' field.
11.  If you have any scheduling constraints, enter them in the 'Availability Notes' field.
12.  Finally, choose how you heard about this opportunity and agree to the launch crew guidelines.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name (Crew Member Name)** (`crew_member_name`): Enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`): We will send updates to this email address. Please ensure it is correct to receive important information about book launches.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Provide your phone number for urgent communications (if you choose to).
* **Primary Social Media Handle** (`social_media_handle`): Your main social media username.
* **Approximate Follower Count** (`follower_count`): Enter your total number of followers across all your social media platforms.
* **Primary Platform** (`platform_preference`): Select the platform you use most often for sharing content.
* **Approximate Follower Count (Follower Count)** (`follower_count`): This is an estimate of your total followers across all your social media platforms.
* **Content Type** (`content_type`): Select the types of content you're interested in creating (e.g., book reviews, author interviews, etc.).
* **Book Title for Launch** (`book_title`): Enter the title of the book you're interested in supporting.
* **Book Author** (`book_author`): Enter the author's name.
* **Book Launch Date** (`launch_date`): Enter the date of the book's launch.
* **How Would You Like to Help?** (`participation_type`): Select how you'd like to help (e.g., social media posts, book reviews, etc.).
* **Where Will You Post Reviews?** (`review_platforms`): Select the platforms where you'll be posting reviews.
* **When Can You Post?** (`content_schedule`): Choose when you're available to post content (e.g., pre-launch teasers, launch day, etc.).
* **Do You Need an Advance Review Copy?** (`arc_needed`): Select if you need an advance review copy (ARC).
* **Advance Review Copy Format Preference** (`arc_format_preference`): Choose the format you prefer for the ARC.
* **Shipping Address for ARC** (`shipping_address`): Enter your shipping address if you need a physical ARC.
* **Your Preferred Genres** (`genre_preferences`): Select the genres you're interested in working with.
* **Have You Worked With This Author Before?** (`previous_collaboration`): Select 'True' if you've worked with this author before.
* **Previous Collaboration Notes** (`collaboration_notes`): Provide any details about previous collaborations with this author.
* **Promotional Materials Needed** (`promotional_materials`): Select the promotional materials you need.
* **Special Skills or Resources** (`special_skills`): Describe any special skills or resources you offer.
* **Availability Notes** (`availability_notes`): Enter any scheduling constraints or availability notes.
* **How Did You Hear About This Opportunity?** (`how_did_you_hear`): Choose how you heard about this opportunity.
* **I Agree to Launch Crew Guidelines** (`terms_agreement`): Select 'Yes I agree' to confirm your participation.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`): Date of registration.
