# thirty_one_gifts_springsummer_2021_catalog - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spring Summer 2021 catalog request form is designed to allow customers to request access to our Spring Summer 2021 catalog. This form is intended for informational and promotional purposes, and all fields are optional.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required and optional fields as needed. You can access the form through the provided link or by following the instructions of your contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Spring Summer 2021 Catalog Request** (`springsummer_2021_catalog_request`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of why you would like to receive the Spring Summer 2021 catalog.
* **Party Name** (`party_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or company requesting the catalog.
* **Party Contact** (`party_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name and title of the person who will be receiving the catalog. This can be a person or a contact person at a company.
* **VIP Code** (`vip_code`, number, optional): If you have a VIP code, please enter it here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your catalog request.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you regarding your catalog request.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter a date for when you would like to receive the catalog.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter a time for when you would like to receive the catalog.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional information or comments regarding your catalog request.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure we can tailor our marketing efforts to meet your specific needs.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us directly.
