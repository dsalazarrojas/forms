# Marketing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect marketing-related information from users, such as contact details, marketing preferences, and consent for marketing materials.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your relevant information, ensuring that you provide accurate and up-to-date details.
2. Select your preferred marketing channels for receiving marketing materials and updates.
3. Provide your email address and phone number (if applicable) for marketing communications.
4. Specify your marketing materials preferences.
5. Review and confirm your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (contact_info)**: Enter your email address to receive marketing updates and information.
* **Marketing Preferences (marketing_preference)**: Choose your preferred marketing channels by selecting one or more from Text Message, Email, and Phone.
* **Call Information (call_info)**: Specify whether you would like to receive calls from us. You can select "Yes, allow calls" or "False".
* **Email Information (email_info)**: Enter your email address to receive marketing-related updates, new products, special deals, and news.
* **Phone Information (phone_info)**: Enter your phone number to receive marketing-related calls and updates (if selected in the "Call Information" field).
* **Address Information (address_info)**: Provide your address for marketing-related activities and events (if applicable).
* **Marketing Materials (marketing_materials)**: Check all the marketing materials you would like to receive, such as brochures, emails, and phone calls.
* **Save Information (save_info)**: This is an optional note field where you can store any additional comments or information about your marketing preferences.

## Tips

* Be accurate and up-to-date with your contact information to ensure you receive marketing updates and materials.
* Select your preferred marketing channels to tailor your experience and receive relevant marketing materials.
* Review your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact our marketing team for assistance.
