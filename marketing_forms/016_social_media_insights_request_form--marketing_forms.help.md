# social_media_insights_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request insights on social media advertising performance and audience engagement from our team. Please fill out the form to provide us with your company information and specific insights you are looking for.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form on each page with the required information.
2. Ensure that you have filled out all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
3. Select all applicable options for "insights_frequency" and "assigned_tool" fields.
4. Click the "Submit" button to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **company** (1, text, required/optional): Enter your company name.
* **product** (2, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the product or service you are using.
* **company_url** (3, text, required/optional): Enter your company website URL.
* **email** (4, email, required): Enter your valid company email address.
* **phone** (5, text, required/optional): Enter your company phone number.
* **insights_requested** (6, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of insights you are looking for:
	+ Insights on social media advertising performance
	+ Insights on social media audience engagement
* **insights_frequency** (7, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the frequency of insights you are looking for:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Biweekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ Bi-annually
	+ Annually
* **notes** (8, note, required/optional): Add any additional information you would like to provide about your insights request.
* **assigned_tool** (9, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the tools you are using:
	+ Chatjimmy
	+ Another tool
	+ Another tool2
