# Newsletter Details Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect details from users about their interest in a newsletter. This includes their name, email, preferred contact method, interest in the newsletter, and subscription frequency.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  To start, fill out the "First name" and "Last name" fields with your full name.
2.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3.  Confirm your email address in the "Confirm email" field.
4.  Choose your interest in the newsletter in the "Interest in the newsletter" field. You can select more than one option.
5.  Select your preferred contact method from the "Preferred contact" field.
6.  Confirm your preferred contact method in the "Confirm contact method" field.
7.  Choose your preferred email frequency in the "Email frequency" field.
8.  Confirm your email frequency in the "Confirm email frequency" field.
9.  Confirm your interest in the newsletter in the "Confirm interest in newsletter" field.
10. Finally, click "Submit" to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (1, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (2, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (3, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Confirm email** (4, email, required/optional): Confirm your email address.
* **Interest in the newsletter** (6, select multiple, required/optional): Choose your interest in the newsletter.
* **Interest in the newsletter** (23, select multiple, required/optional): Confirm your interest in the newsletter.
* **Preferred contact** (7, select one, required/optional): Choose your preferred contact method.
* **Contact Method** (22, select one, required/optional): Confirm your preferred contact method.
* **Subscribe** (9, select one, required/optional): Confirm your subscription.
* **Confirm contact method** (22, select one, required/optional): Confirm your contact method.
* **Email frequency** (19, select one, required/optional): Choose your email frequency.
* **Confirm email frequency** (20, select one, required/optional): Confirm your email frequency.
* **Notes** (12, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes.
* **Confirm** (13, select one, required/optional): Confirm your details.
* **Agree Terms Text** (14, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments.
* **Accept terms** (16, select one, required/optional): Confirm your acceptance of terms.
* **Confirm accept** (18, select one, required/optional): Confirm your acceptance of terms.
* **Submit** (25, select one, required/optional): Submit your form.
