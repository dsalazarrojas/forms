# Utm Generator Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Utm Generator Form is used to generate UTM values for marketing campaigns. It helps in creating and tracking campaign details such as name, type, source, budget, lead generation, sales generation, traffic, start and end dates, start and end times, cost, and other relevant information. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter a unique and descriptive name for your campaign in the "Utm Generator Form".
2. Select the type of campaign, such as "Marketing Lead Gen", "Marketing Sales Gen", or "Website Traffic".
3. Choose the campaign source from the available options, including "Facebook", "Google", "Instagram", "Twitter", "YouTube", and "Website".
4. Enter the budget for your campaign.
5. Enter the number of leads generated by your campaign.
6. Enter the number of sales generated by your campaign.
7. Enter the traffic generated by your campaign.
8. Select the status of your UTM, either "Active" or "Inactive".
9. Enter the start date for your UTM.
10. Enter the end date for your UTM.
11. Enter the start time for your UTM.
12. Enter the end time for your UTM.
13. Enter the number of leads generated by your UTM.
14. Enter the number of sales generated by your UTM.
15. Enter the traffic generated by your UTM.
16. Enter the cost of your UTM.
17. Enter any additional notes about your UTM.
18. Enter any tags or keywords related to your UTM.
19. Enter a link related to your UTM.
20. Enter a file related to your UTM.
21. Enter an image related to your UTM.
22. Enter an email address for your UTM.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Utm Generator Form** (form_title, text, optional): Enter a descriptive title for your campaign, e.g., "Summer Sale" or "Winter Campaign".
* **Campaign name** (campaign_name, text, optional): Enter a unique and descriptive name for your campaign.
* **Campaign type** (campaign_type, select_one, optional): Select the type of campaign, such as "Marketing Lead Gen" or "Website Traffic".
* **Campaign source** (campaign_source, select_one, optional): Select the source of your campaign, such as "Facebook" or "Google".
* **Campaign budget** (campaign_budget, text, optional): Enter the budget for your campaign, e.g., "500".
* **Campaign lead gen** (campaign_lead_gen, number, optional): Enter the number of leads generated by your campaign.
* **Campaign sales gen** (campaign_sales_gen, number, optional): Enter the number of sales generated by your campaign.
* **Campaign traffic** (campaign_traffic, number, optional): Enter the traffic generated by your campaign.
* **Utm status** (utm_status, select_one, optional): Select the status of your UTM, either "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Utm start date** (utm_start_date, date, optional): Enter the start date for your UTM.
* **Utm end date** (utm_end_date, date, optional): Enter the end date for your UTM.
* **Utm start time** (utm_start_time, time, optional): Enter the start time for your UTM.
* **Utm end time** (utm_end_time, time, optional): Enter the end time for your UTM.
* **Utm lead gen** (utm_lead_gen, number, optional): Enter the number of leads generated by your UTM.
* **Utm sales gen** (utm_sales_gen, number, optional): Enter the number of sales generated by your UTM.
* **Utm traffic** (utm_traffic, number, optional): Enter the traffic generated by your UTM.
* **Utm cost** (utm_cost, text, optional): Enter the cost of your UTM.
* **Utm notes** (utm_notes, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about your UTM.
* **Utm tags** (utm_tags, text, optional): Enter any tags or keywords related to your UTM.
* **Utm link** (utm_link, text, optional): Enter a link related to your UTM.
* **Utm file** (utm_file, text, optional): Enter a file related to your UTM.
* **Utm image** (utm_image, text, optional): Enter an image related to your UTM.
* **Utm email** (utm_email, email, optional): Enter an email address for your UTM.
