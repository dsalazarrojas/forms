# Eco Product Collaboration Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for users interested in collaborating on an eco product project. It's intended for those who want to share their ideas, skills, or interests with our team.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form title, your name, company name, and email address.
2. Select your role(s) in the project.
3. Enter your phone number (optional).
4. Write a brief message describing your collaboration interests and expectations.
5. Select your company's size, industry, and interests.
6. Enter your job title, job description, company website, and other company details (if applicable).
7. Choose areas of collaboration you're interested in.
8. Add any additional job function or job description details (if applicable).
9. Upload a company logo (optional).
10. Provide an email address for submission feedback or communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter a descriptive title for your form submission.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Please enter your name, as it will be used for internal collaboration.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter your company name.
* **Role** (`role`, select_multiple, required): Select the roles you'd like to play in the project, e.g., "Project Manager" or "Marketing Specialist".
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you'd like to be reached by phone, enter your phone number.
* **Message** (`message`, note, required): Briefly describe your collaboration interests and expectations.
* **Collaboration Interests** (`collaboration_interests`, select_one, optional): Select specific areas where you'd like to collaborate, e.g., "Product Development" or "Content Creation".
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, optional): Choose your company's size, e.g., "Small", "Medium", or "Large".
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title within the company.
* **Job Description** (`job_desc`, text, optional): Add a brief description of your job responsibilities.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, optional): Enter your company website (if applicable).
* **Collaboration Areas** (`collaboration_areas`, select_multiple, optional): Select additional areas where you'd like to collaborate, e.g., "Research and Development" or "Marketing and Sales".
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, text, optional): Add any additional job function details (if applicable).
* **Company Description** (`company_desc`, text, optional): Briefly describe your company and its mission.
* **Logo** (`company_logo`, text, optional): Upload your company logo (if applicable).
* **Submission Email** (`submission_email`, email, optional): Provide an email address for submission feedback or communication.

Note: Optional fields are not required but are useful for providing more context and information to the collaboration team.
