# cts_cb - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "cts_cb" form is used to capture information related to callbacks, call-to-sets, notes, contact details, and tool assignments. This form is likely used for sales or marketing purposes, such as capturing follow-up information after a meeting or interaction with a customer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options from the "Call Backs" field, indicating whether callbacks are necessary or not.
2. Choose the relevant call-to-set from the "Call To Sets" field.
3. Provide any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Select the relevant date in the "Date" field.
7. Select the relevant time in the "Time" field.
8. Choose the tool assigned to you from the "Assigned Tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Call Backs (Call Backs, Select Multiple, required: false)**: Select "Yes" if callbacks are necessary, "No" otherwise.
* **Call To Sets (Call To Sets, Select One, required: false)**: Choose the relevant call-to-set from the options provided.
* **Notes (Notes, Note, required: false)**: Enter any additional comments or notes related to the interaction.
* **Email (Email, Email, required: false)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (Phone, Text, required: false)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date (Date, Date, required: false)**: Select the relevant date from the calendar picker.
* **Time (Time, Time, required: false)**: Select the relevant time from the time picker.
* **Assigned Tool (Assigned Tool, Select One, required: false)**: Choose the tool assigned to you from the options provided.
