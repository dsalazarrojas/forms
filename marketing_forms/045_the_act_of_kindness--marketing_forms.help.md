# The Act Of Kindness - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect stories and insights about acts of kindness that people have experienced or would like to experience. It aims to gather diverse perspectives on how kindness can positively impact mental health and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:
1. Answer each question in detail, providing as much information as you feel is relevant.
2. Be honest and sincere in your responses.
3. Ensure that your answers are original and not duplicate any existing submissions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Act Of Kindness**: A space to describe your personal experience of an act of kindness you've performed or witnessed.
* **Start With One Small Act**: Share a story about a small act of kindness that sparked a chain reaction of positivity.
* **Bring Joy Something**: Share an experience where you've brought joy to someone, and how it impacted you.
* **Bring Surprise Something**: Share a time when someone surprised you with an act of kindness, and how it made you feel.
* **Bring Help Something**: Describe a situation where you helped someone in need, and how it affected you.
* **Help Person Positive Outlook**: Share an experience where helping someone had a positive impact on their outlook on life.
* **Kindness Cost Little**: Share a story about an act of kindness that required minimal effort, but had a significant impact.
* **Many Cost Nothing**: Share an experience where you found that acts of kindness didn't cost anything to perform.
* **Price Tag Happiness**: Describe a situation where performing an act of kindness brought you happiness and satisfaction.
* **Make A Difference**: Share an experience where you made a difference in someone's life through an act of kindness.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when sharing your stories.
- Use your own words to describe your experiences.
- If you have any questions or need clarification, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
