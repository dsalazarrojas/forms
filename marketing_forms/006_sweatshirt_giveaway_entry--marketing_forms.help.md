## Sweatshirt Giveaway Entry - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for users to enter a free gift card contest for a sweatshirt. Please provide the necessary information to be considered for the contest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the User Information form.
2.  Enter your Contact Details.
3.  Select the size of the sweatshirt you prefer.
4.  Write a brief message for the contest entry, if desired.
5.  Provide your entry date and time, if applicable.
6.  Add any additional comments, if necessary.
7.  Enter your email address for contact purposes.
8.  Optionally, provide a phone number for further communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information**: Please provide your basic contact details such as name, etc. This field helps us get to know you better.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your address, postal code, and other relevant contact information so we can reach you if you win.
* **Sweatshirt Size**: Select the size of the sweatshirt you're interested in - Small, Medium, or Large.
* **Contest Entry**: Provide a brief message explaining why you deserve the free gift card. This is optional.
* **Entry Date**: If you are entering for a specific contest, please enter the date you want to be considered for.
* **Entry Time**: If applicable, enter the time you want to be considered for the contest entry.
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any special requests or messages you'd like to share with us, this is the place to do so.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address for us to contact you if you win or for any other contest-related communication.
* **Phone Number**: This is an optional entry for your phone number. Enter it if you want us to contact you via call for further details.

Note: Please be aware that entering the same information multiple times may lead to your entry being considered duplicate, making it ineligible. If you have any concerns, please contact us for assistance.
