# Website Traffic Quality Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Website Traffic Quality Assessment Form is used to collect data about users' demographics, behavior on the website, and conversion rates to assess the website's quality and effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer all the questions truthfully and thoroughly.
2. Provide accurate information about yourself, your behavior on the website, and your conversion experiences.
3. Use the email field to submit your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Demographics** (`user_demographics`, text, optional): 
This field is for you to provide basic information about yourself, such as your name, location, or any other identifying details. This will help us understand your background and demographics.
* **User Behavior** (`user_behavior`, text, optional): 
Describe your behavior on the website, such as how you interacted with its content, features, or tools. Be specific about what you did, how you felt, and why you made certain choices.
* **Conversion Rate** (`conversion_rate`, text, optional): 
Share your experiences with converting on the website, including successful or unsuccessful attempts. Explain why you did or did not complete the desired action, and what factors might have influenced your decision.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): 
Enter your valid email address to submit your response and receive any follow-up information about your assessment. This will help us contact you and provide personalized feedback.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and thoroughly to provide valuable insights.
* If you have trouble filling out any of the fields, feel free to ask for clarification.
* Your feedback is greatly appreciated and will help us improve the website.
