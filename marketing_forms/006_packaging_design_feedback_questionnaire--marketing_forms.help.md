# Packaging Design Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the overall design of our product packaging. It will help us understand how customers perceive our packaging's visual aspects, such as its overall impression, color scheme, and logo design.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions as honestly as possible. You may choose one or multiple answers for select options, and provide any additional feedback you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Design Impressions**: This question is designed to gauge your overall impression of the design. Please select one of the options that best describes your impression.
	+ Design is eye-catching
	+ Design is functional
	+ Other
* **What do you like about the design?**: This question is an open-ended question for you to provide any specific aspects of the design you liked.
* **Color Scheme**: Please select all the colors that you think are used in the design.
	+ Blue
	+ Red
	+ Green
	+ Other
* **Color Intensity**: Please select the level of intensity for the colors used in the design.
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **Font Style**: This question is designed to gauge your overall impression of the font used.
	+ Simple
	+ Fancy
	+ Other
* **Logo Design**: This question is designed to gauge your opinion on the logo's design.
	+ Good
	+ Bad
	+ Neutral
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the design.
* **Additional Feedback**: Please provide any additional comments or suggestions on the design.
