# Housekeeping Quick Estimate Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect basic information from clients for a quick estimate of a housekeeping project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Client Name**, the person in charge of the project.
2. Select the **Service Type** that best describes the project's scope (e.g., One-time cleaning, Regular cleaning, etc.).
3. Provide the **Square Footage** of the area to be cleaned.
4. Specify the **Number of Stories** in the building or area.
5. Check if a **Site Visit Request** is necessary (e.g., to inspect the area before the project).
6. Enter the **Estimate Price** (if available or known).
7. Add any additional **Notes** about the project or client requirements.
8. Enter the client's **Email** address (if available).
9. Provide the client's **Phone Number** (if available).
10. Enter the estimated project completion time, including the date (if available).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Enter the name of the person in charge of the project.
* **Service Type**: Select the type of service required (e.g., one-time cleaning, regular cleaning, etc.).
* **Square Footage**: Enter the total square footage of the area to be cleaned.
* **Number of Stories**: Enter the number of stories in the building or area.
* **Site Visit Request**: Check this option if you need a site visit before starting the project.
* **Estimate Price**: Enter the estimated price of the project (if available).
* **Notes**: Add any additional information about the project or client requirements.
* **Email**: Enter the client's email address (if available).
* **Phone Number**: Enter the client's phone number (if available).
* **Estimated Completion Time**: Enter the estimated time to complete the project, including the date.
