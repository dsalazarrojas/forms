# Travel Habits Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather information about your travel habits, preferences, and restrictions. This will help us better understand your travel behaviors and improve our travel-related services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and choose the option that best describes your travel habits.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select one or more options that apply to you.
3. Fill out all the questions, but do not worry if you're unsure about some answers.
4. Submit the form when you're finished.
