# Custom Proof - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom Proof form is designed to collect relevant details for a custom proof to be used in marketing materials. This form is intended for internal use and should only be completed when a custom proof is required.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Determine if a custom artwork proof is required by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the Artwork Proof field.
2. Enter the company's slogan in the Company Slogan field.
3. Enter the company's mission statement in the Company Mission Statement field.
4. Specify the size of the proof in the Size of Proof field. This field is typically used for measuring the size of a printed proof.
5. Enter the company's values in the Company Values field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Artwork Proof** (`Artwork Proof`, select multiple, optional):
	+ This field is used to confirm if a custom artwork proof is required.
* **Company Slogan** (`Company Slogan`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the company's slogan.
* **Company Mission Statement** (`Company Mission Statement`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the company's mission statement.
* **Size of Proof** (`Size of Proof`, number, optional):
	+ Specify the size of the proof in inches or another unit of measurement.
* **Company Values** (`Company Values`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the company's values.

## Tips
- Only complete this form if a custom proof is required.
- All fields are optional, so fill out only the relevant fields.
- Enter all required information accurately and completely for best results.
