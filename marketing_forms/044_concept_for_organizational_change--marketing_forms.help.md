# Concept For Organizational Change - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Concept for Organizational Change" form is used to document and propose new ideas or changes within the organization. It helps to gather essential information about the proposed change, including project details, expected outcomes, and key stakeholders involved. This form is used to facilitate the formal approval process and ensure that all necessary information is captured and considered before proceeding with the change.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a short and descriptive title for the project.
2. Enter the name and title of the executive sponsor or owner of the project.
3. Summarize the proposed change and its objectives in a brief description.
4. Describe the expected benefits and outcomes of the change.
5. Identify the key stakeholders involved in the change process.
6. Estimate the high-level milestones and timeline for the project.
7. List the necessary resources required for the project (budget, personnel, and technology needs).
8. Indicate whether you are requesting formal approval for the proposed change.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project name** (Project name, text, true): This field is used to enter a short and descriptive title for the project.
* **Sponsor name** (Sponsor name, text, true): This field is used to enter the name and title of the executive sponsor or owner of the project.
* **Summary description** (Summary description, text, true): This field is used to provide a brief description of the proposed change and its objectives.
* **Expected benefits** (Expected benefits, text, true): This field is used to describe the expected outcomes and value for the organization.
* **Key stakeholders** (Key stakeholders, text, false): This field is used to identify the groups or roles impacted by the change.
* **Estimated timeline** (Estimated timeline, text, false): This field is used to estimate the high-level milestones and timeline for the project.
* **Resource requirements** (Resource requirements, text, false): This field is used to list the necessary resources required for the project (budget, personnel, and technology needs).
* **Approval requested** (Approval requested, select one, true): This field is used to indicate whether you are requesting formal approval for the proposed change.
 
 ## Tips
 Be sure to provide as much detail as necessary to support your proposal, but keep the form concise and focused on the essential information.
