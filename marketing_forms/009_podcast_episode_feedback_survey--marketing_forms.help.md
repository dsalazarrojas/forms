# Podcast Episode Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about a podcast episode. Please take a few minutes to provide your honest feedback to help us improve future episodes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Answer the questions that are required (if any) and provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
2. For the rating field, select a number between 1 and 5 to rate your overall experience with the episode.
3. In the 'Select one', 'Select multiple', and 'Select all' fields, choose one or multiple options that best describe your opinion or preference.
4. If you have any additional feedback or comments, type them in the 'Additional feedback' field.
5. Finally, submit your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. What do you think about this episode? (intro_text)
* **What do you think about this episode**: Please share your thoughts about the episode, such as what you liked or didn't like, or anything that stood out to you.

### 2. Rate this episode 1-5 (rating)
* **Rate this episode 1-5**: Select a number between 1 and 5 to rate your overall experience with the episode.

### 3. What did you like most about this episode (feedback)
* **What did you like most about this episode**: Please tell us what you enjoyed or appreciated most about the episode.

### 4. What did you like least about this episode (what_didnt_like)
* **What did you like least about this episode**: If there was something that didn't quite stand out or was missing for you, please let us know here.

### 5. Do you have any suggestions for improvement (suggestions)
* **Do you have any suggestions for improvement**: If you have any thoughts on how we could improve future episodes, please type them here.

### 6. Email (email)
* **Email**: Please provide your email address so we can contact you for any follow-up.

### 7. Phone (phone)
* **Phone**: Please note that we will not be contacting you on this phone number for anything. We just want to gather some demographic information.

### 8. When did you listen to this episode (date_time)
* **When did you listen to this episode**: Please select the date and time you listened to the episode.

### 9. Time (time)
* **Time**: This field is not currently being used, so please just type something if you feel it's necessary.

### 10. Additional feedback (note)
* **Additional feedback**: If you have any other comments or thoughts, please type them here.

### 11. Select one (select_one)
* **Select one**: Please choose one of the options that best describes your opinion or preference. This field is meant to help us understand your opinion on [insert description here].

### 12. Select multiple (select_multiple)
* **Select multiple**: Choose one or multiple options from the list that best describe your opinion or preference.

### 13. Select all (select_all)
* **Select all**: Choose one of the options that best describe your opinion or preference. (This field is meant to gauge your overall preference)

### 14. Select one2 (select_one2)
* **Select one2**: Please choose one of the options that best describe your opinion or preference. (This field is meant to gauge your opinion on [insert description here]).

### 15. Select multiple2 (select_multiple2)
* **Select multiple2**: Choose one or multiple options from the list that best describe your opinion or preference.

### 16. Date and time (date_time2)
* **Date and time**: This field is not currently being used, so please just type something if you feel it's necessary.

### 17. Time2 (time2)
* **Time2**: This field is not currently being used, so please just type something if you feel it's necessary.

Please note that some field labels are a bit unclear, so I've tried to add descriptions to help guide the users through the form.
