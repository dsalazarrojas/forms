# promotional_email_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect email addresses and preferences from customers for marketing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your email address in the "Email address" field.
2. Select your email preferences:
	* If you select "Marketing", you will receive marketing emails from us.
	* If you select "Non-marketing", you will receive non-marketing emails from us.
3. Choose a contact method:
	* If you select "Email" as your contact method, you will receive emails from us.
	* If you select "Phone" or "Mail" as your contact method, you will receive emails or phone calls/mail from us.
4. Select your consent:
	* If you select "True", you agree to receive emails from us.
	* If you select "False", you do not agree to receive emails from us.
5. If you selected a contact method, select a frequency for how often you would like to receive emails:
	* If you select "Daily", you will receive emails from us daily.
	* If you select "Weekly", you will receive emails from us weekly.
6. If you selected a contact method, enter your preferred contact method option:
	* If you selected "Email" as your contact method, enter your preferred email address.
	* If you selected "Phone" as your contact method, enter your phone number.
	* If you selected "Mail" as your contact method, enter your mail address.
7. Enter any additional notes you would like to include.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Email address** (1, text, required):
	+ Enter your email address that you would like to use for communication.
* **Email preferences** (2, select_multiple, required):
	+ Select whether you would like to receive marketing or non-marketing emails.
* **Contact me** (3, select_one, required):
	+ Choose how you would like to be contacted: email, phone, or mail.
* **Email frequency** (4, select_one, required):
	+ If you selected a contact method, select how often you would like to receive emails: daily or weekly.
* **Contact method** (7, select_multiple, required):
	+ If you selected a contact method, select the specific contact method you would like to use.
* **Consent** (11, select_one, required):
	+ Select whether you agree or disagree to receive emails from us.
* **Consent (True/False)** (12, text, required):
	+ Enter "True" to agree or "False" to disagree.
* **Notes** (14, note, optional):
	+ Enter any additional information or comments you would like to include.
* **Marketing/Non-marketing** (15/16, text, not required):
	+ If you selected "Marketing", enter a specific purpose for marketing emails.
	+ If you selected "Non-marketing", enter a specific purpose for non-marketing emails.
