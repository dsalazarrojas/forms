# Dynamic Partner Leads Collection - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Dynamic Partner Leads Collection form is used to collect and store partner information from various sources. This form helps in maintaining a centralized database of partner details, which can be used for various marketing and business purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure that you have all the required information before filling out the form.
2.  Fill out the form with the requested information for each partner.
3.  Select the partner type from the dropdown menu.
4.  Enter the submit date if applicable.
5.  Add any additional partner information in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Partner Information (partner_leads_collection_1)**: Enter any additional details about the partner in this text field. This can include any relevant notes or comments.
* **Contact Email (contact_email)**: Enter the contact email of the partner. This will be used to contact the partner if needed.
* **Contact Phone (contact_phone)**: Enter the contact phone number of the partner.
* **Company Name (company_name)**: Enter the name of the partner's company.
* **Job Title (job_title)**: Enter the job title of the partner.
* **Partner Type (partner_type)**: Select the type of partner from the dropdown menu. This will help in categorizing and grouping partners for easy reference.
* **Submit Date (submit_date)**: Enter the date when the partner information was collected. This is optional and only required if the information is time-sensitive.
* **Additional Partner Information (partner_leads_collection_2)**: Enter any additional details about the partner in this text field. This can include any relevant notes or comments.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that you are entering accurate and up-to-date information for the partner.
*   If you are updating partner information, make sure to review the existing information to avoid duplication.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the partner or contact the relevant team member for clarification.
*   This form is for collection and storage of partner information, please do not use it for sending direct messages or emails to the partner.
