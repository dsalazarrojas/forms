# product_demo_and_freebie_sample_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to request a product demo or freebie sample from our company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields with the requested information. Make sure to provide accurate and detailed answers to help our sales team assist you better.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Form** (`product_demo_and_freebie_sample_request_form`, text, optional): This is the main section where you can describe your request in detail. Please provide a clear and concise description of the product demo or freebie sample you are interested in.
* **Contact** (`product_demo_contact`, text, optional): Enter your contact information so that we can get in touch with you to discuss your request further.
* **Demo Type** (`demo_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of demo or sample you are interested in. Please select either "Product Demo" or "Freebie Sample".
* **Demo Date** (`demo_date`, date, optional): If you have a specific date in mind for the demo, please enter it here. This will help us plan and schedule the demo accordingly.
* **Demo Time** (`demo_time`, time, optional): If you have a specific time in mind for the demo, please enter it here. This will help us plan and schedule the demo accordingly.
* **Sample Amount** (`sample_amount`, number, optional): If you are interested in a freebie sample, please specify the quantity you would like to receive.
* **Shipping Amount** (`shipping_amount`, number, optional): Please enter the shipping cost for the sample you are interested in. This will help us plan and budget for the shipping accordingly.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): If you are requesting the demo or sample on behalf of a company, please enter the name of the company here.

Note: All fields are optional, but providing detailed and accurate information will ensure that we can better assist you with your request.
