# Win Your Dream Week Contest Entry - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about their dream week. The form is used to identify potential winners of the "Win Your Dream Week" contest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form entry page with your details.
2. On the second page, provide your dream week details.
3. Submit the form to enter the contest.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Enter your details** (`form_entry`, text, optional)
Enter your name, which will be used to identify you.

### **What's your dream week?** (`dream_week`, text, optional)
Describe your ideal week in as much detail as possible.

### **Name** (`name`, text, optional)
Enter your name for registration purposes.

### **Email** (`email`, email, optional)
Enter your email address to receive updates and news from us.

### **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional)
Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

### **Country** (`country`, select_one, optional)
Select your country of origin.

### **State** (`state`, select_one, optional)
Select your state or region of origin.

### **City** (`city`, text, optional)
Enter the city where you live or visit.

### **Age** (`age`, number, optional)
Enter your age.

### **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional)
Enter your date of birth.

### **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, optional)
Enter your time of birth.

### **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, optional)
Select your gender.

### **Agree to our terms and conditions** (`agree_terms`, note, optional)
Read and agree to our terms and conditions by typing yes.

### **Agreed** (`agreed`, text, optional)
Type yes to confirm your agreement to our terms and conditions.

### **Accept email** (`accept_email`, text, optional)
Type yes to accept emails from us.

### **Accept SMS** (`accept_sms`, text, optional)
Type yes to accept SMS from us.

### **Accept Call** (`accept_call`, text, optional)
Type yes to accept calls from us.

### **Accept Mail** (`accept_mail`, text, optional)
Type yes to accept mail from us.

### **Accept Social Media** (`accept_social_media`, text, optional)
Type yes to accept social media from us.

### **Facebook** (`facebook`, select_one, optional)
Select if you are on Facebook.

### **Twitter** (`twitter`, select_one, optional)
Select if you are on Twitter.

### **Instagram** (`instagram`, select_one, optional)
Select if you are on Instagram.

### **LinkedIn** (`linkedin`, select_one, optional)
Select if you are on LinkedIn.

### **Pintrest** (`pintrest`, select_one, optional)
Select if you are on Pintrest.

Note: This form is not meant to be a survey, but rather a contest entry form. Please answer honestly and accurately to help us understand your dream week better.
