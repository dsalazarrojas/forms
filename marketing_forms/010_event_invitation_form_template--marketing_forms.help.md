# Event Invitation Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Invitation Form Template is a form used to gather information from event attendees, including their RSVP status, dietary preferences, and contact details. This form is essential for event organizers to plan and prepare for the event according to attendees' needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1.  Enter your name in the **Event Invitation Form Template** field.
2.  Select your RSVP status by choosing one or more of the options in the **RSVP** field.
3.  For the **Dietary Preference** field, select one or more of the options that apply to you (Vegetarian, Non-vegetarian, or Omnivore).
4.  Enter the date of your attendance in the **Date** field, if applicable.
5.  Enter the time of your attendance in the **Time** field, if applicable.
6.  Add any notes or comments in the **Note** field, if necessary.
7.  Provide your email address in the **Email** field.
8.  Finally, enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Invitation Form Template (1)**: This is the form title. Please enter your name here.
* **RSVP (2)**: Choose your RSVP status by selecting one or more of the options (RSVP Yes, RSVP No, RSVP Maybe). This is a required field.
* **Dietary Preference (3)**: Select one or more dietary preferences from the options (Vegetarian, Non-vegetarian, Omnivore). This is a required field.
* **Date (4)**: Enter the date of your attendance if you have one.
* **Time (5)**: Enter the time of your attendance if you have one.
* **Note (6)**: Add any additional notes or comments about your attendance.
* **Email (7)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone (8)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
