# Fundraiser for Elementary School PTA - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help elementary school PTAs organize and promote fundraising activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. Fill out each field with the requested information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Information** (`general_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your school's general information, such as the school name and email address.
* **Fundraiser Type** (`fundraiser_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of fundraiser, such as Classroom Project, School Event, or Sports Team.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your school's name.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the fundraiser.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the start date of the fundraiser.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the end date of the fundraiser.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your school's email address.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your school's phone number.
* **Target Amount** (`target_amount`, `number`, required: false): Set the target amount for the fundraiser.
* **Share Via** (`share_via`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose how you want to share the fundraiser, such as email, text, or social media.
* **Share With** (`share_with`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select who you want to share the fundraiser with, such as parents, teachers, or school staff.
* **Share Note** (`share_note`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional share note.
* **Fundraiser Goal** (`fundraiser_goal`, `number`, required: false): Set the fundraiser goal.
* **Share URL** (`share_url`, `text`, required: false): Enter the URL to share the fundraiser.
* **Share Message** (`share_message`, `text`, required: false): Enter a share message for the fundraiser.
* **Share Message Subject** (`share_message_subject`, `text`, required: false): Enter the subject for the share message.
* **Share Message Body** (`share_message_body`, `text`, required: false): Enter the body of the share message.
* **Share Message Email** (`share_message_email`, `text`, required: false): Enter the email for the share message.
* **Share Message Phone** (`share_message_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number for the share message.
* **Share Message Social** (`share_message_social`, `text`, required: false): Enter the social media message.
* **Share Message Text** (`share_message_text`, `text`, required: false): Enter the text message for sharing.
* **Share Message Twitter** (`share_message_twitter`, `text`, required: false): Enter the Twitter message.
* **Share Message Instagram** (`share_message_instagram`, `text`, required: false): Enter the Instagram message.
* **Share Message Facebook** (`share_message_facebook`, `text`, required: false): Enter the Facebook message.
* **Share Message LinkedIn** (`share_message_linkedin`, `text`, required: false): Enter the LinkedIn message.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out each field accurately to create a comprehensive plan for your fundraiser.
* Double-check the fields before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact your school's PTA/PTO administrators.
