# Claim Your Free Item - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Claim Your Free Item" form is designed to help customers claim free items from our company. This form collects necessary information to verify eligibility, shipping details, and preferences for the free item. Please follow the instructions below to complete the form accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first and last name in the First Name and Last Name fields.
2. Provide a valid email address and phone number for contact and delivery purposes.
3. If you have a promotional code, enter it in the Promotional Code field.
4. Select how you heard about this offer from the options provided.
5. Choose the free item you would like to claim from the Select Your Free Item options.
6. If applicable, select the quantity and color preferences for your chosen item.
7. Enter your shipping address details, including address line 1, city, state, and ZIP code.
8. Select your preferred shipping country.
9. Provide any special delivery instructions.
10. Confirm your eligibility and accept the terms and conditions.
11. Decide if you would like to join our newsletter and receive SMS notifications.
12. If you were referred, provide the name of the friend or family member who referred you.
13. Add any additional comments or questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as you prefer it to be spelled.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as you prefer it to be spelled.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address for contact and update purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter a valid phone number for delivery coordination.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth to verify eligibility.
* **Promotional Code** (`promotional_code`, `text`, optional): If you have a promotional code, enter it here.
* **How Did You Hear About This Offer** (`how_heard`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the ways you heard about this offer.
* **Select Your Free Item** (`item_selection`, `select_one`, required): Choose one free item from the options provided.
* **Quantity** (`item_quantity`, `select_one`, required): Select the quantity of the free item you would like to claim.
* **Color Preference** (`color_preference`, `select_one`, optional): If applicable, select your color preference for your chosen item.
* **Shipping Address Line 1** (`shipping_address_line1`, `text`, required): Enter your street address or PO box.
* **Shipping Address Line 2** (`shipping_address_line2`, `text`, optional): Enter your apartment, suite, or unit number.
* **City** (`shipping_city`, `text`, required): Enter the city of your shipping address.
* **State or Province** (`shipping_state`, `text`, required): Enter the state or province of your shipping address.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (`shipping_zip`, `text`, required): Enter the ZIP code of your shipping address.
* **Country** (`shipping_country`, `select_one`, required): Select your shipping country.
* **Special Delivery Instructions** (`delivery_instructions`, `text`, optional): Enter any special delivery instructions.
* **Do You Confirm You Are Eligible** (`eligibility_confirmation`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you are eligible to claim the free item.
* **Do You Accept the Terms and Conditions** (`terms_acceptance`, `select_one`, required): Accept the terms and conditions to complete the form.
* **Would You Like to Join Our Newsletter** (`newsletter_signup`, `select_one`, required): Decide if you want to join our newsletter for updates and offers.
* **Would You Like SMS Notifications** (`sms_notifications`, `select_one`, required): Decide if you want to receive SMS notifications about your order.
* **Who Referred You** (`referral_source`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the friend or family member who referred you.
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you have.
