# Branding Wall Placement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for a branding wall placement request. It will help us understand your needs and preferences for a successful installation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out each section carefully. You will need to provide the following information:
* Your name and contact details (requestor_name, requestor_email, and requestor_phone)
* Your department or team (department)
* Your company name (company_name) and office location (office_location)
* Details about the wall location (wall_location_description) and its size (wall_dimensions)
* The type of wall (wall_type)
* The type of branding you want to display (branding_type)
* The content to be displayed on the wall (branding_content)
* Your preferred colors for the branding (brand_colors)
* Your design preference (design_preference)
* Whether you have brand assets ready (existing_brand_assets)
* Your estimated budget (budget_range)
* If you have approval from management (approval_status)
* Any special requirements or considerations (special_requirements)
* Any additional notes or information (additional_notes)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Requestor Full Name
*   **Requestor Full Name**: Your full legal name

### 2. Email Address
*   **Email Address**: Contact email

### 3. Phone Number
*   **Phone Number**: Contact phone number

### 4. Department
*   **Department**: Your department or team

### 5. Company Name
*   **Company Name**: Name of your organization

### 6. Office Location
*   **Office Location**: Which office location

### 7. Wall Location Description
*   **Wall Location Description**: Describe where the wall is located

### 8. Wall Dimensions
*   **Wall Dimensions**: Approximate size of the wall

### 9. Wall Type
*   **Wall Type**: What type of wall is it

### 10. Branding Type
*   **Brand Type**: What type of branding do you want

### 11. Branding Content
*   **Brand Content**: What should the branding display

### 12. Brand Colors
*   **Brand Colors**: Specify brand colors to use

### 13. Design Preference
*   **Design Preference**: Describe your design preference

### 14. Do You Have Brand Assets
*   **Do You Have Brand Assets**: Logo and brand files ready

### 15. Installation Timeline
*   **Installation Timeline**: When do you want installation

### 16. Budget Range
*   **Budget Range**: Estimated budget

### 17. Management Approval
*   **Management Approval**: Do you have approval from management

### 18. Special Requirements
*   **Special Requirements**: Any special requirements or considerations

### 19. Additional Notes
*   **Additional Notes**: Any other information to share
