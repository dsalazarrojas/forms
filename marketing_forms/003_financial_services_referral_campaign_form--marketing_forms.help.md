# Financial Services Referral Campaign Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from the people you are referring to our financial services, so we can provide them with relevant information and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the necessary fields and provide as much relevant information as you can. You can refer multiple services by selecting them in the "Referral Type" field. You can also add a brief message to let us know how we can assist the person being referred.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Referral Question** (`referral_question`, text, required: false): This is the question you are referring to our financial services to answer. Please write it as concisely and clearly as possible.
  - Example: "John Doe, our accountant has recommended your company for financial services."
  - Please provide a clear and descriptive question that we can use to identify the referral's needs.
- **Referral Type** (`referral_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the services we can offer that apply to the person being referred:
  - Financial Services: For people looking for investment advice, financial planning, or accounting services.
  - Insurance: For people seeking insurance quotes, claims, or policy information.
  - Banking: For people looking for banking services such as loans, credit cards, or checking accounts.
- **Referral Name** (`referral_name`, text, required: false): The name of the person being referred. Please use their full name for our records.
- **Referral Email** (`referral_email`, email, required: false): The email address of the person being referred. This will help us stay in touch with them directly.
- **Referral Phone** (`referral_phone`, text, required: false): The phone number of the person being referred. Please provide their contact number for follow-up.
- **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required: false): The name of the business the person being referred represents.
- **Business Email** (`business_email`, email, required: false): The email address of the business being referred. This will help us stay in touch with the business directly.
- **Business Phone** (`business_phone`, text, required: false): The phone number of the business being referred. Please provide their contact number for follow-up.
- **Message** (`message`, text, required: false): Provide a brief message about the person being referred and any additional information that can help us understand their needs.

## Tips
- Please fill out only the required fields and provide relevant information.
- You can refer multiple services by selecting them in the "Referral Type" field.
- If you're unsure about any field, please let us know and we'll get back to you.
