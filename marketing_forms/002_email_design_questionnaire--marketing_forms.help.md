# Email Design Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about customers' preferences and details for email marketing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required and optional fields as follows:

* Enter your email address in the "Customer Email" field.
* Enter your name in the "Customer Name" field.
* Enter your phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
* Enter your company name in the "Customer Company" field.
* Enter the content of the email you would like to receive.
* Select your preferred email design options from the dropdown menu.
* Provide any additional personal details you would like to share.
* Indicate your visual preferences, if any.
* Specify your material preferences, if any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Email (1)
* **Customer Email** (Customer Email, email, required): Enter your email address that you would like to use for email marketing purposes.

### Customer Name (2)
* **Customer Name** (Customer Name, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like to be addressed in email communications.

### Customer Phone (3)
* **Customer Phone** (Customer Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to share it with us for further communication.

### Customer Company (4)
* **Customer Company** (Customer Company, text, optional): Enter your company name if you have one.

### Email Content (5)
* **Email Content** (Email Content, text, optional): Enter the content of the email you would like to receive, e.g., product information, promotions, etc.

### Design Preferences (6)
* **Design Preferences** (Design Preferences, select multiple, optional):
	+ Select one or multiple options from the dropdown menu:
		- Plain Text Only
		- Plain Text and Images
		- HTML Only
		- HTML and Images

### Personal Details (7)
* **Personal Details** (Personal Details, text, optional): Enter any personal details you would like to share with us.

### Visual Preferences (8)
* **Visual Preferences** (Visual Preferences, number, optional): Indicate your preferred level of visual content, if any, e.g., 1-10.

### Material Preferences (9)
* **Material Preferences** (Material Preferences, text, optional): Enter your material preferences, if any.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
