# Public Relations Package Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather information from users who are interested in the public relations package. It is a non-mandatory form, and submitting it will help us gauge interest and potentially provide additional services to the users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your name in the "Your Name" field.
2.  Enter your organization's name in the "Organization" field.
3.  Select your role from the options provided in the "Role" field.
4.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please note that this field requires a valid email address.
5.  Fill in your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6.  Enter the date you are interested in using the public relations package in the "Date" field.
7.  Fill in the time you are interested in using the public relations package in the "Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Public Relations Package Registration Form**: This is the form title, and it is for information purposes only.
* **Your Name**: Please fill in your name as it appears on your business card or identification. This field is not required.
* **Organization**: Enter your organization's name as it appears on your business card or identification. This field is not required.
* **Role**: Select the best description of your role in the organization from the options provided. This field is not required.
* **Email**: Please enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you. This field is not required.
* **Phone**: Fill in your phone number as it appears on your business card or identification. This field is not required.
* **Date**: Enter the date you are interested in using the public relations package in the format MM/DD/YYYY. This field is not required.
* **Time**: Enter the time you are interested in using the public relations package in the format HH:MM AM/PM. This field is not required.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all the fields accurately and truthfully to ensure that we can provide the best possible service to you.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* If you have already registered, you will not be able to submit this form again.
