# Frequent Flyer Program Friend Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Frequent Flyer Program Friend Referral Form is used to capture information from customers who are referring friends to our program. This information is used to track referrals and reward customers for bringing in new members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Enter your friend's first name in the "friend's first name" field.
6. Enter your friend's last name in the "friend's last name" field.
7. Enter your friend's email address in the "friend's email" field.
8. Enter the date of referral in the "date of referral" field.
9. If your friend is employed, select "Yes" or "No" in the "friend's company" field.
10. If your friend's company is not listed, select "other" in the "friend's company" field.
11. Enter the date of your friend's joining in the "friend's date of joining" field.
12. Enter your friend's date of birth in the "friend's date of birth" field.
13. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "friend's referent company" field.
14. If your friend's company is not listed, select "other" in the "friend's referent company" field.
15. Enter your friend's phone number in the "friend's phone" field.
16. Enter the date of referral end in the "date of referral end" field.
17. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "referent company" field.
18. If your friend's company is not listed, select "other" in the "referent company" field.
19. Enter your friend's date of referral in the "referent date of referral" field.
20. Enter the date of referral end in the "referent date of referral end" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name. (Required)
* **Email**: Enter your email address. (Required)
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Friend's first name**: Enter your friend's first name.
* **Friend's last name**: Enter your friend's last name.
* **Friend's email**: Enter your friend's email address. (Required)
* **Referral code**: Enter a referral code (optional).
* **Referent first name**: Enter the first name of the person you are referring.
* **Referent last name**: Enter the last name of the person you are referring.
* **Referent email**: Enter the email address of the person you are referring. (Required)
* **Company**: Select "Yes" or "No" if the friend's company is listed.
* **Other**: Select "Yes" or "No" if the friend's company is not listed.
* **Referent company**: Select "Yes" or "No" if the friend's company is listed.
* **Other**: Select "Yes" or "No" if the friend's company is not listed.
* **Referent phone**: Enter the phone number of the person you are referring.
* **Friend's phone**: Enter your friend's phone number.
* **Date of birth**: Enter the date of birth of the person you are referring.
* **Date of joining**: Enter the date of joining of the person you are referring.
* **Date of referral**: Enter the date of referral.
* **Date of referral end**: Enter the date of referral end.
* **Referent date of referral**: Enter the date of referral of the person you are referring.
* **Referent date of referral end**: Enter the date of referral end of the person you are referring.
