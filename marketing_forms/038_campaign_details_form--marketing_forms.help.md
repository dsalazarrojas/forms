# Campaign Details Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Campaign Details Form is used to plan and track campaign details. This information helps the marketing team to prepare for the campaign launch and measure its success.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields: Campaign Name, Campaign Manager Name, Start Date, Target Audience, Primary Channels, Key Messages, and Success Metrics.
2. Optionally, add the End Date, Budget Estimate, and Additional Notes fields if they are relevant to your campaign.
3. Select the channels you plan to use for your campaign in the Primary Channels field.
4. Ensure you have filled out all the required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Campaign Name** (`campaign_name`, text, required):
  Enter the official campaign or project name.
* **Campaign Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required):
  Enter the primary contact person responsible for the campaign.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required):
  Enter the planned launch date of the campaign.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional):
  Enter the planned end date of the campaign, or leave it blank if not applicable.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, text, required):
  Briefly describe the target demographic for the campaign.
* **Budget Estimate** (`budget_estimate`, number, optional):
  Estimate the campaign's budget, if applicable.
* **Primary Channels** (`primary_channels`, select multiple, required):
  Select the channels you plan to use for the campaign:
  • Email
  • Social Media
  • Print
  • Events
  • Broadcast
* **Key Messages** (`key_messages`, text, required):
  Enter the core messages or calls to action for the campaign.
* **Success Metrics** (`success_metrics`, text, required):
  Describe how you will measure the campaign's success.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional):
  Enter any other planning details that may be useful for the campaign.
