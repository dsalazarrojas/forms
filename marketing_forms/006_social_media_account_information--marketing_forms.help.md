# Social Media Account Information - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Media Account Information form is used to collect essential details about your social media presence, helping us to better understand your online identity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of social media platform you use (e.g., Twitter, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok).
2. Enter your social media URL (the URL of your social media profile).
3. Enter your social media username (the handle or ID used to login to your account).
4. If applicable, provide any additional social media account information, such as the password or phone number associated with your account.
5. Choose whether you want to opt-in for email, phone, or notes communication from us.
6. Submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Social Media Type** (social_media_type): Select the type of social media platform you use (e.g., Twitter, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok).
* **Social Media URL** (social_media_url): Enter the URL of your social media profile.
* **Social Media Username** (social_media_username): Enter your social media username or handle.
* **Social Media Password** (social_media_password): Enter your social media account password (not recommended for security reasons, only fill in if strictly necessary).
* **Social Media Email** (social_media_email): Enter your social media account's email address.
* **Social Media Phone** (social_media_phone): Enter your social media account's phone number.
* **Social Media Notes** (social_media_notes): Provide any additional notes about your social media account.
* **Social Media Email Opt-in** (social_media_email_opt_in): Choose whether you want to opt-in for email communication from us.
* **Social Media Phone Opt-in** (social_media_phone_opt_in): Choose whether you want to opt-in for phone communication from us.
* **Social Media Notes Opt-in** (social_media_notes_opt_in): Choose whether you want to opt-in for notes communication from us.
