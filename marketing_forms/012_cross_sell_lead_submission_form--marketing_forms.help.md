# cross_sell_lead_submission_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users for cross-selling purposes. It is intended to capture essential details about the lead, cross-selling information, contact information, and sales information to facilitate effective sales and business development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you provide accurate and complete information.
2. Enter your responses in the designated fields, and make sure to fill out all required fields.
3. Click the "Submit" button to send the form to the designated recipient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Lead Information** (`lead_info`, text, optional):
	* This field is for providing general details about the lead. Please enter any relevant information that will help us understand the lead's background.
- **Cross Sell Information** (`cross_sell_info`, text, optional):
	* This field is for sharing any cross-selling information that might be relevant to the sales process. Please provide any additional information that will help us make the sale.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional):
	* This field is for sharing contact information to facilitate communication with the lead. Please ensure you enter accurate and up-to-date contact details.
- **Sales Information** (`sales_info`, text, optional):
	* This field is for providing any additional sales information that will help drive the sales process forward. Please keep this field brief and focused on essential details.

## Tips
* Double-check your responses for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* Ensure all required fields are filled out to ensure the form is properly submitted.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to the form administrator for clarification.
