# Market Sizing Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Market Sizing Template is a tool for companies to assess their market potential, growth rate, and competitive position. It's designed to help you understand your target market and identify areas for growth and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your company information, including your name and contact details.
2. Select your title and market segment.
3. Estimate your market size, market share, and revenue potential.
4. Assess your market entry barriers, competitive position, and growth rate.
5. Identify your revenue streams and market entry barriers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your company's name.
* **First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your company email address.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your company phone number.
* **Title** (`contact_title`, select_one, optional): Select your title from the list.
* **Market Size** (`market_size`, number, optional): Estimate your market size.
* **Market Share** (`market_share`, number, optional): Estimate your market share.
* **Revenue Potential** (`revenue_potential`, number, optional): Estimate your revenue potential.
* **Market Entry Barriers** (`market_entry_barrier`, select_multiple, optional): Select the level of market entry barriers you face.
* **Market Segment** (`market_segment`, select_one, optional): Select your market segment.
* **Time In Market** (`time_in_market`, time, optional): Estimate the time you've been in the market.
* **Market Potential** (`market_potential`, select_one, optional): Select whether your market potential is high or low.
* **Market Growth Rate** (`market_growth_rate`, number, optional): Estimate your market growth rate.
* **Competitive Position** (`competitive_position`, select_multiple, optional): Select your competitive position.
* **Revenue Streams** (`revenue_streams`, select_multiple, optional): Select your revenue streams.
* **Market Entry Barriers 2** (`market_entry_barrier_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select the level of market entry barriers you face.
* **Market Segment 2** (`market_segment_2`, select_one, optional): Select your market segment.
* **Time In Market 2** (`time_in_market_2`, time, optional): Estimate the time you've been in the market.
* **Revenue Streams 2** (`revenue_streams_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select your revenue streams.
* **Market Entry Barriers 2** (`market_entry_barrier_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select the level of market entry barriers you face.
* **Competitive Position 3** (`competitive_position_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select your competitive position.
* **Market Growth Rate 2** (`market_growth_rate_2`, number, optional): Estimate your market growth rate.
* **Revenue Streams 3** (`revenue_streams_3`, select_multiple, optional): Select your revenue streams.
