# Customer Interest Profile - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect customer information and categorize their interests for marketing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Please fill out the form completely and honestly. This will help us better understand your interests and provide more relevant content and offers to you.
*   Select the correct category that best describes your interests from the "Category" dropdown menu.
*   If you have any additional comments or notes, please use the "Notes" field to provide more information.
*   Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field if you'd like to be contacted for further discussions.
*   Provide your email address in the "Email" field if you'd like to receive updates or further communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Customer** (`customer_interest_profile_form_customer`, select_one, required/optional): Select the correct customer option that best describes the customer you are representing.
*   **Interest** (`customer_interest_profile_form_interest`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the customer's interest.
*   **Notes** (`customer_interest_profile_form_notes`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about the customer's interest.
*   **Phone** (`customer_interest_profile_form_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the customer's phone number, if available.
*   **Email** (`customer_interest_profile_form_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the customer's email address, if available.
*   **Category** (`customer_interest_profile_form_category`, select_one, required/optional): Select the category that best describes the customer's interest.
*   **Description** (`customer_interest_profile_form_description`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the customer's interest.
*   **Category** (repeated, note that this is the second instance of "Category" field): Select the category that best describes the customer's interest.
*   **Option 1**, **Option 2**, **Option 3** (repeated, note that these are options for the "Customer" field): These are the available options for the "Customer" field. Select the correct option that best describes the customer you are representing.
