# Trade Show Booth Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your trade show booth's specific requirements and needs. It will help us better understand your needs and ensure that we can provide the best possible experience for you and your team.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form and understand what each field is asking for.
2. Fill in the required fields (marked as `required: true`) with accurate information.
3. Select the correct options for each field that apply to your booth.
4. Save your progress as you go, and feel free to ask if you have any questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Booth Address** (`booth_address`, text, optional): Enter the address where your booth will be located.
* **Booth Number** (`booth_number`, number, optional): Enter your booth number assigned by the trade show organizers.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for your booth.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Booth Equipment** (`booth_equipment`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select 'Yes' if your booth requires any equipment such as tables, chairs, etc.
* **Electrical Services** (`electrical_services`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you require any electrical services such as power outlets, lighting, etc.
* **Water Services** (`water_services`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you require any water services such as water coolers, etc.
* **Waste Disposal** (`waste_disposal`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you require any waste disposal services such as trash cans, recycling bins, etc.
* **Parking Lot** (`parking_lot`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you require parking for your team or visitors.
* **Internet Access** (`internet_access`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you require internet access for your team or visitors.
* **Electrical Outlets** (`electrical_outlets`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you require any electrical outlets.
* **Furniture and Decor** (`furniture_and_decor`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you require any furniture or decorations for your booth.
* **Flooring** (`flooring`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you require any flooring for your booth.
* **Lighting** (`lighting`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you require any lighting for your booth.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask.
* This form is for your convenience, and we appreciate your feedback.
