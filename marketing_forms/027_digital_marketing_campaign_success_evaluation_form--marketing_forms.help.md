# Digital Marketing Campaign Success Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help evaluate the success of digital marketing campaigns. It captures key information about the campaign, including its name, objectives, budget, duration, results, and channels used.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the campaign details and ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill out the campaign name, objectives, and budget fields.
3. Select the campaign duration.
4. Choose the results of the campaign (Success, Partial Success, or Failed).
5. Select the marketing channels used (Social Media, Email Marketing, Influencer Marketing, or Content Marketing).
6. Enter the target audience size (number).
7. Select any measurable metrics that were tracked (Website Visits, Social Media Engagement, or Conversion Rate).
8. Select any unmeasurable metrics that were tracked (Website User Experience or Customer Satisfaction).
9. Review and update the form as needed.
10. Submit the form for evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Campaign Name
* **Campaign Name**: The name of the campaign, e.g., "Summer Sale 2023".
* **Objectives**: A brief description of the campaign's goals, e.g., "Increase website visits by 10% and conversion rate by 5%".

### Budget
* **Budget**: The total budget allocated for the campaign.
* **Note**: This field is required as it helps in understanding the financial resources spent on the campaign.

### Duration
* **Duration**: The length of the campaign, e.g., "30 days".

### Results
* **Results**: The outcome of the campaign (Success, Partial Success, or Failed).

### Marketing Channels
* **Marketing Channels**: Select all the channels used for the campaign, e.g., Social Media, Email Marketing.

### Target Audience
* **Target Audience**: The demographic characteristics of the audience, e.g., "Age 18-35, Location New York".

### Target Audience Size
* **Target Audience Size**: The estimated number of people in the target audience.

### Measurable Metrics
* **Measurable Metrics**: Select all the metrics that were tracked, e.g., Website Visits, Social Media Engagement.

### Unmeasurable Metrics
* **Unmeasurable Metrics**: Select all the metrics that were not tracked but are relevant to the campaign, e.g., Customer Satisfaction.

### Notes
* **Notes**: Additional comments or insights about the campaign.
