# Holiday Payback Contest Entry - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users about a holiday payback contest entry.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information:
	* User ID (optional)
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Email
	* Story (optional)
	* Company (optional)
	* Location (optional)
	* Photo (optional)
	* Phone (optional)
	* Business Website (optional)
	* Entry Reason (optional)
	* Social Media (optional)
	* Preferred Contact Method (optional)
	* Holiday (date of the holiday)
	* Time (time of the holiday)
	* Year (year of the holiday)
	* Form Submitted By (who submitted the form)
	* Form Submitted (date the form was submitted)
2. Select your preferred contact method from the options:
	* Phone
	* Email
	* Social Media
	* Postal Mail

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User ID** (`user_id`, number, optional): A unique identifier for the user, if you have one.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Your email address for follow-up communication.
* **Story** (`story`, text, optional): A brief story about your holiday experience.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): The company you represent.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Your location during the holiday.
* **Photo** (`photo`, note, optional): An optional photo related to your holiday story.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Your phone number for follow-up communication.
* **Business Website** (`business_website`, text, optional): The website of your business.
* **Entry Reason** (`entry_reason`, text, optional): Why you're entering the contest.
* **Social Media** (`social_media`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ Facebook
	+ Instagram
	+ Twitter
	+ LinkedIn
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the following:
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ Social Media
	+ Postal Mail
* **Holiday** (`holiday`, date, optional): The date of the holiday.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): The time of the holiday.
* **Year** (`year`, number, optional): The year of the holiday.
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following:
	+ ChatJIMMY
	+ User
	+ Other
	+ Automated
* **Form Submitted** (`form_submitted`, date, optional): The date the form was submitted.
* **User IP** (`user_ip`, text, optional): The IP address of the user.
* **User Agent** (`user_agent`, text, optional): The user agent of the browser.
* **User Browser** (`user_browser`, text, optional): The browser used to submit the form.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your entry is complete and valid.
* If you're not sure what any of the fields mean, feel free to ask for clarification.
