# Candy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Candy Survey is designed to gather information about your preferences, consumption habits, and purchasing behavior related to candy. This form aims to collect data on your demographics, candy preferences, consumption habits, and purchasing habits to better understand the candy market.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the appropriate answers for each question based on your personal experiences and preferences.
2. You will be asked about your age group, gender, location, and other demographic information.
3. Then, you will be asked about your candy consumption habits, such as how often you consume candy, what type of candy you prefer, and when you typically eat candy.
4. You will also be asked about your purchasing behavior, including where you buy candy and how price-sensitive you are.
5. Finally, you will have the opportunity to provide any additional comments or suggestions about your candy shopping experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your age group?**: This question asks about your age range. Select one of the provided options: Under 13, 13-18, 19-25, 26-35, 36-45, 46-55, 56-65, or 65 plus.
* **Gender**: This question asks about your gender identity. Select one of the provided options: Male, Female, Non-binary, Prefer not to say, or Other.
* **What is your location?**: This question asks about your city or region. Please type in the name of your location.
* **How often do you consume candy?**: This question asks about how frequently you consume candy. Select one of the provided options: Daily, Several times per week, Weekly, A few times per month, Monthly, or Never.
* **On average, how much candy do you consume per week?**: This question asks about your estimated weekly candy consumption. Please type in your answer in ounces or servings.
* **When do you typically eat candy?**: This question asks about when you usually eat candy. Select all that apply from the provided options: After meals, As a snack, Before bed, At social events, Stress relief, Movie/entertainment, Holiday seasons, or Never.
* **What is your favorite type of candy?**: This question asks about your preferred type of candy. Select one of the provided options: Chocolate, Gummies, Hard Candies, Lollipops, Licorice, Taffy, Caramels, Mints, or Other.
* **Which of these candy characteristics appeal to you?**: This question asks about your preferences for candy characteristics. Select all that apply from the provided options: Sweet taste, Fruity flavor, Chocolate flavor, Sour taste, Chewy texture, Crunchy texture, Long-lasting flavor, Natural ingredients, Low sugar, Novelty/Fun packaging.
* **Do you have a favorite candy brand?**: This question asks about your favorite candy brand. Select one of the provided options: Yes, No, or Multiple favorites.
* If yes, which brand(s) do you prefer?: This question is optional and allows you to list your favorite candy brands if you selected "Yes" in the previous question.
* **Do you have any dietary restrictions affecting candy consumption?**: This question asks about any dietary restrictions you may have. Select all that apply from the provided options: Allergies, Vegetarian/Vegan, Low sugar diet, Dairy-free, Gluten-free, Kosher, Halal, or No restrictions.
* **How important is sugar content when choosing candy?**: This question asks about your priority for sugar content in candy. Select one of the provided options: Very Important, Important, Somewhat Important, Not Important, or Not Considered.
* **Are you interested in healthier candy alternatives?**: This question asks about your interest in healthier candy options. Select one of the provided options: Very Interested, Interested, Somewhat Interested, Not Interested.
* **Where do you typically purchase candy?**: This question asks about where you buy candy. Select all that apply from the provided options: Grocery stores, Convenience stores, Candy shops, Online retailers, Movie theaters, Gas stations, or Other.
* **How price-sensitive are you when buying candy?**: This question asks about your price sensitivity when purchasing candy. Select one of the provided options: Very sensitive, Sensitive, Neutral, Not sensitive, Buy premium regardless of price.
* **What would improve your candy shopping experience?**: This question is optional and allows you to provide suggestions for improving your candy shopping experience.
* **Any other comments about candy?**: This question is optional and allows you to share any other comments or thoughts about candy.
