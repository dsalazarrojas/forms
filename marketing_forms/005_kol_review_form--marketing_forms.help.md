# KOL Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The KOL Review Form is used to collect feedback and evaluation on the performance and communication style of Key Opinion Leaders (KOLs) in the business.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each section completely and accurately, providing feedback that aligns with the specific purpose of each field.
2. Use the provided options or select "None" if none of the options apply.
3. Do not repeat or duplicate effort in each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### KOL Rating (1)
* **KOL Rating** (`kol_rating`, number, required/optional): Rate the KOL's performance on their business opportunity, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.

### KOL Performance (2)
* **KOL Performance** (`kol_performance`, number, required/optional): Evaluate the KOL's performance on their business opportunity, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.

### Engagement Level (3)
* **Engagement Level** (`engagement_level`, number, required/optional): Rate the KOL's engagement level, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.

### Communication Style (4)
* **Communication Style** (`communication_style`, select_one, required/optional): Evaluate the KOL's communication style, selecting Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.

### Overall Rating (5)
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, required/optional): Rate the KOL's overall performance, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.

### Performance Score (6)
* **Performance Score** (`performance_score`, number, required/optional): Evaluate the KOL's performance, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.

### Engagement Score (7)
* **Engagement Score** (`engagement_score`, number, required/optional): Evaluate the KOL's engagement level, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.

### Key Opinion Leaders (8)
* **Key Opinion Leaders** (`kols`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the strengths and weaknesses of the KOL, selecting Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.

### Review (9)
* **Review** (`review`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback on the KOL's performance.

### Additional Comments (10)
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback on the KOL's performance.

### KOLs Notes (11)
* **KOLs Notes** (`kols_notes`, note, required/optional): Provide any notes on the KOL's performance.

### KOLs Comments (12)
* **KOLs Comments** (`kols_comments`, note, required/optional): Provide any comments on the KOL's performance.

### Business Owner (13)
* **Business Owner** (`business_owner`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the business owner.

### Contact Person (14)
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact person's name.

### Email (15)
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the contact person's email.

### Phone (16)
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact person's phone number.

### Review Date (17)
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date the review was completed.

### Review Time (18)
* **Review Time** (`review_time`, time, required/optional): Select the time the review was completed.

### Review Frequency (19)
* **Review Frequency** (`review_frequency`, text, required/optional): Enter the review frequency.

### Review Notes (20)
* **Review Notes** (`review_notes`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback on the review.

### Business Owner Notes (21)
* **Business Owner Notes** (`business_owner_notes`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback on the business owner's performance.

### Business Owner Comments (22)
* **Business Owner Comments** (`business_owner_comments`, note, required/optional): Provide any comments on the business owner's performance.

### Follow-up Actions (23)
* **Follow-up Actions** (`follow_up_actions`, text, required/optional): Enter any follow-up actions.

### Assigned User (24)
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, text, required/optional): Enter the assigned user's name.

### Assigned Tool (25)
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required/optional): Enter the assigned tool's name.

Note: The fields marked as "required/optional" can be filled out or left blank depending on the user's preference. However, it is recommended to fill out all fields to ensure a comprehensive review.
