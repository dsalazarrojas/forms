# designer_entry_form_fashion_show - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from fashion designers and teams who are interested in participating in a fashion show.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and resources ready before starting.
2. Select your team member status.
3. Fill in your organization and contact email.
4. Provide your collection name, number of looks, and collection segment.
5. Upload your sketches and images.
6. Enter your personal and team contact information (name, address, phone number, email, Facebook, Instagram, and website links).
7. Add a brief biography, portfolio link, and designer contact information.
8. Enter your team's name, description, Facebook, Instagram, and website links.
9. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Team member** (`select_one`): Select 'Yes' if you are a team member or 'No' if you are not.
- **organization** (`text`): Enter your organization's name (e.g., "ABC Fashion Designers").
- **Organization website** (`text`): Enter your organization's website (e.g., "https://example.com").
- **Contact email** (`email`): Enter your contact email address (e.g., "designer@example.com").
- **Contact phone** (`text`): Enter your contact phone number (e.g., "123-456-7890").
- **Contact details** (`text`): Enter any additional contact details (e.g., "123 Main St, Anytown, USA").
- **Availability** (`text`): Enter your availability for the fashion show (e.g., "Available on Friday").
- **Collection name** (`text`): Enter your collection's name (e.g., "Spring/Summer Collection 2023").
- **Number of looks** (`number`): Enter the number of looks in your collection (e.g., "10").
- **Collection segment** (`text`): Enter your collection's segment (e.g., "Women's clothing").
- **Sketches and images** (`text`): Upload your collection's sketches and images.
- **Designer name** (`text`): Enter your personal name (e.g., "John Doe").
- **Biography** (`text`): Enter a brief description of yourself (e.g., "Fashion designer with 10 years of experience").
- **Portfolio link** (`text`): Enter your portfolio link (e.g., "https://yourportfolio.com").
- **Designer contact email** (`email`): Enter your personal contact email address (e.g., "johndoe@example.com").
- **Designer contact phone** (`text`): Enter your personal contact phone number (e.g., "123-456-7890").
- **Designer address** (`text`): Enter your personal address (e.g., "123 Main St, Anytown, USA").
- **Designer Facebook** (`text`): Enter your personal Facebook link (e.g., "https://facebook.com/johndoe").
- **Designer Instagram** (`text`): Enter your personal Instagram link (e.g., "https://instagram.com/johndoe").
- **Designer website** (`text`): Enter your personal website link (e.g., "https://yourwebsite.com").
- **Team description** (`text`): Enter a brief description of your team (e.g., "Team of 5 fashion designers").
- **Team name** (`text`): Enter your team's name (e.g., "ABC Design Team").
- **Team Facebook** (`text`): Enter your team's Facebook link (e.g., "https://facebook.com/teamabc").
- **Team Instagram** (`text`): Enter your team's Instagram link (e.g., "https://instagram.com/teamabc").
- **Team website** (`text`): Enter your team's website link (e.g., "https://teamwebsite.com").
