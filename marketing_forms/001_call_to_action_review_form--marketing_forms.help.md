# Call To Action Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Call To Action Review Form" is a crucial tool for our team to evaluate and improve the effectiveness of our calls to action on our website. Your feedback will help us better understand what works and what doesn't, allowing us to make data-driven decisions to enhance user experience and conversion rates.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by entering your name or team name under "Reviewer Name" if you're representing an organization.
2.  Type your company or organization's name in the "Company or Organization" field.
3.  Provide your contact email address under "Email Address" so we can reach out if needed.
4.  Select where you initially saw the call to action from the "Where did you see this call to action" dropdown (e.g., homepage, landing page, email newsletter, etc.).
5.  Choose the type of action the call to action was asking you to take from the "Type of Call to Action" dropdown (e.g., purchase, sign up, download, etc.).
6.  Rate the clarity of the call to action on a scale from 1 (very unclear) to 5 (very clear).
7.  Assess the visibility of the call to action on a scale from 1 (hard to find) to 5 (very prominent).
8.  Indicate your level of motivation to click the call to action on a scale from 1 (not motivated at all) to 5 (extremely motivated).
9.  Rate the effectiveness of the call to action's color on a scale from 1 (very ineffective) to 5 (very effective).
10.  If you think the call to action's text or copy was effective, please type your thoughts under "What did you think about the CTA text or copy".
11.  If you experienced any barriers to clicking the call to action, select them from the list provided.
12.  If you have any suggestions for improvement, type them under "Improvement suggestions".
13.  Finally, rate the overall effectiveness of the call to action on a scale from 1 (very ineffective) to 5 (very effective).
14.  Let us know if you would recommend this CTA approach to others, and
15.  If you have any additional comments, type them under "Additional Comments".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, text, required): Your full name or team name that you're representing. This will help us know who provided the feedback.
*   **Company or Organization** (`company_organization`, text, required): Name of your business or organization.
*   **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Your contact email address for follow-up or further questions.
*   **Where did you see this call to action** (`cta_location`, select_one, required): Select the platform or location where you initially saw the call to action.
    +   Website homepage
    +   Landing page
    +   Email newsletter
    +   Social media post
    +   Paid advertisement
    +   Blog article
    +   Mobile app
    +   Other
*   **Type of Call to Action** (`cta_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of action the call to action was asking you to take.
    +   Purchase now
    +   Sign up or register
    +   Download resource
    +   Subscribe to newsletter
    +   Request demo
    +   Contact sales
    +   Learn more
    +   Share content
*   **How clear was the call to action** (`cta_clarity_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the clarity of the call to action on a scale from 1 (very unclear) to 5 (very clear).
    +   1 - Very unclear
    +   2 - Somewhat unclear
    +   3 - Neutral
    +   4 - Somewhat clear
    +   5 - Very clear
*   **How visible was the CTA button or link** (`cta_visibility_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the visibility of the call to action on a scale from 1 (hard to find) to 5 (very prominent).
    +   1 - Hard to find
    +   2 - Somewhat hidden
    +   3 - Neutral
    +   4 - Easy to see
    +   5 - Very prominent
*   **How motivated were you to click the CTA** (`cta_motivation_level`, select_one, required): Indicate your level of motivation on a scale from 1 (not motivated at all) to 5 (extremely motivated).
    +   Not motivated at all
    +   Slightly motivated
    +   Moderately motivated
    +   Very motivated
    +   Extremely motivated
*   **Did the CTA color stand out** (`cta_color_effectiveness`, select_one, optional): Select your response on the effectiveness of the call to action's color.
    +   Yes - very effective
    +   Somewhat effective
    +   Neutral
    +   Not very effective
    +   No - blended in completely
*   **What did you think about the CTA text or copy** (`cta_copy_feedback`, text, optional): Type your thoughts on the wording used in the call to action.
*   **What prevented you from clicking the CTA if anything** (`barriers_to_action`, select_multiple, optional): Select all barriers that prevented you from clicking the call to action.
    +   Nothing - I clicked it
    +   Unclear value proposition
    +   Not interested in the offer
    +   Too many form fields expected
    +   Distrust of the website
    +   Poor timing
    +   Technical issues
    +   Other
*   **What improvements would make you more likely to click** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): Type your suggestions for improvement.
*   **Overall how effective is this call to action** (`overall_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Rate the overall effectiveness of the call to action on a scale from 1 (very ineffective) to 5 (very effective).
    +   Very ineffective
    +   Somewhat ineffective
    +   Neutral
    +   Somewhat effective
    +   Very effective
*   **Would you recommend this CTA approach to others** (`would_recommend`, select_one, optional): Choose yes or no, or maybe.
    +   'True'
    +   'False'
    +   Maybe
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Any other feedback you'd like to share.

Please take a few minutes to provide your honest feedback and suggestions. Thank you for your time and contribution. This form's purpose is to help us improve user experience and conversion rates, and your input is vital to this process.
