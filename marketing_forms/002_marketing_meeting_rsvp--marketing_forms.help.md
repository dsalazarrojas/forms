# Marketing Meeting RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a simple RSVP (response to invitation to attend) for a marketing meeting. It helps attendees provide their details, such as date, time, and name, and confirm their attendance.

## How To Complete This Form

### To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the correct meeting date from the dropdown list.
2. Choose the correct meeting time from the dropdown list.
3. Enter your name as it is spelled correctly.
4. Enter your email address to ensure you receive any further communications.
5. If you have a phone number, enter it for easy contact.
6. Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to confirm your attendance.
7. Add any additional comments or questions.
8. If applicable, select the assigned tool or resource.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Date**: Select the date of the marketing meeting you plan to attend.
* **Meeting Time**: Choose the time of the marketing meeting you plan to attend.
* **Participant Name**: Enter your full name as it is spelled correctly.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address to receive any further communication regarding the meeting.
* **Participant Phone**: If you have a phone number, enter it for easy contact.
* **Attendance Confirmation**: Select 'Yes' to confirm your attendance or 'No' if you cannot attend.
* **Additional Comments**: Write any additional comments or questions you have regarding the meeting.
* **Assigned Tool**: If applicable, select the assigned tool or resource. 

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information, especially your email address.
* Double-check the meeting date and time to ensure you attend at the correct time. 
* If you cannot attend, please select 'No' for attendance confirmation.
* Any additional comments will be reviewed by the meeting organizers before the meeting.
* If you have any questions, feel free to add them in the 'Additional Comments' field. 
* If you have any issues with the assigned tool, let us know in the 'Additional Comments' field.
