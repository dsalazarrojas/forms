# Market Research Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to capture essential information about market research projects, facilitating collaboration and decision-making among project team members and managers.


## How To Complete This Form

1. **Provide a brief title for the project** (`form_name`, text, optional): Enter a concise title that summarizes the project's main objective.
2. **Enter the research objective** (`research_objective`, text, required): Describe the main objective of the project, outlining what the research aims to achieve.
3. **Enter the contact person's name** (`contact_person`, text, required): Provide the name of the person responsible for the project.
4. **Enter the contact person's email address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person for communication and collaboration.
5. **Enter the contact person's phone number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Provide the contact person's phone number for further communication.
6. **Describe the research questions** (`research_questions`, text, required): Outline the specific questions that the research aims to answer.
7. **Specify the research methods** (`research_methods`, text, optional): Describe the methods and tools used to collect and analyze data.
8. **Identify the target audience** (`target_audience`, text, optional): Describe the specific group or demographic the research is intended for.
9. **Choose data collection tools** (`data_collection_tools`, select multiple, optional): Select the tools used for data collection (e.g., Online surveys, Face-to-face, Phone, Mail).
10. **Choose data analysis techniques** (`data_analysis_techniques`, select multiple, optional): Select the techniques used for data analysis (e.g., Descriptive Statistics, Inferential Statistics, Data Mining).
11. **Set a date and time for the project** (`timeframes`, date, required): Choose a specific date and time for the project's completion or start.
12. **Enter the research scope** (`research_scope`, text, optional): Describe the project's scope, focusing on its specific objectives and boundaries.
13. **Allocate the budget** (`budget_allocation`, number, required): Enter the allocated budget for the project.
14. **Enter the total budget** (`total_budget`, number, required): Enter the total budget for the project.
15. **Choose a location for the research** (`research_location`, text, required): Specify the location where the research will take place.
16. **Select the research status** (`research_status`, select one, optional): Choose the current status of the research (e.g., Active, Inactive, Completed, Cancelled).
17. **Add any additional notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or observations about the project.
