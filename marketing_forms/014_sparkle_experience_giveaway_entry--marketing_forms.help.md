# Sparkle Experience Giveaway Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who want to participate in the Sparkle Experience Giveaway. Please fill out the form accurately to be considered for the giveaway.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the first name field with your name as it appears on your official identification.
2. Fill out the last name field with your last name as it appears on your official identification.
3. Enter your email address in the email field. Make sure to enter the correct one to receive updates and communications about the giveaway.
4. For the confirm email field, select "I want to join" to confirm your email address.
5. Choose the "yes" option in the confirm email field to confirm your email address.
6. Fill out the phone number field with your phone number if you have one.
7. For the confirm phone field, select "yes" to confirm your phone number.
8. Choose the correct date field to select the day you would like to join the giveaway. If no specific date is selected, it will be treated as "Any" date.
9. Fill out the time field with your preferred time of day.
10. You can add any additional notes or comments about yourself in the note field.
11. Fill out any numbers required for the entry_12 field.
12. Fill out any other fields as required or desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **first_name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
- **last_name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
- **email**: Enter your email address to receive updates and communications about the giveaway.
- **confirm_email**: Select "I want to join" to confirm your email address.
- **phone**: If you have a phone number, enter it here.
- **confirm_phone**: Select "yes" to confirm your phone number.
- **date**: Select the date you would like to join the giveaway.
- **time**: Enter your preferred time of day.
- **note**: Add any additional notes or comments about yourself.
- **entry_12**: Fill out any number required for this field.
- **entry_23**: Fill out any other fields as required or desired.
- **entry_24**: Select the correct option to confirm your answer.
- **entry_25**: Select multiple options as desired.
- **entry_17**: Select the correct option to confirm your answer.
