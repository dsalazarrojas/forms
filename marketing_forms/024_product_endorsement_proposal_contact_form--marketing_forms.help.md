# Product Endorsement Proposal Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Product Endorsement Proposal Contact Form" is a tool used to collect contact information and proposals from potential customers or clients interested in collaborating with the company. It helps facilitate communication and collaboration by providing a clear and structured approach to sharing ideas and proposals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter a brief title to describe your proposal in the "Form Title" field.
2. Provide a clear and concise description of your proposal in the "Form Description" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field, as this is a required field for us to contact you.
4. Select one of the options in the "Select One" field, as this will help us understand your preferences.
5. Select multiple relevant options in the "Select Multiple" field, as this will help us understand your needs and preferences.
6. (Optional) Enter a date and/or time if relevant to your proposal.
7. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
8. Finally, click the "Form Submit" button to send your proposal to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title that describes your proposal.
- **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, optional): Provide a clear and concise description of your proposal.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, number, optional): Enter your phone number if you prefer to be contacted via phone.
- **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the options to help us understand your preferences.
  * Example: If you select "Option 1", we will understand that you prefer this option.
- **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required): Choose multiple relevant options to help us understand your needs and preferences.
  * Example: If you select "Option 1" and "Option 3", we will understand that you prefer these options.
- **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter a date if relevant to your proposal.
- **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter a time if relevant to your proposal.
- **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your proposal.
  * Example: If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to include them here.
- **Form Submit** (`form_submit`, text, required): Finally, click this button to send your proposal to us.
