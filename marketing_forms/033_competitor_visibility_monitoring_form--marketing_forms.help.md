# Competitor Visibility Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help the team monitor and respond to competitor marketing activities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

### Step 1: Fill in the required fields
* Ensure you have completed all the required fields (marked with an asterisk *).

### Step 2: Answer each question accurately
* Be honest and objective when describing what you observed.
* Use the options provided for select_one and select_multiple fields.
* If the answer is not listed, choose "Other" and provide an explanation.

### Step 3: Add any additional information
* If you have any additional comments or observations, use the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Monitoring Date** (`monitoring_date`, date, required): Date of observation.
* **Observer Name** (`observer_name`, text, required): Person making the observation.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Your department.
* **Competitor Name** (`competitor_name`, text, required): Name of the competitor observed.
* **Type of Observation** (`observation_type`, select_one, required): What you observed (e.g., marketing campaign, product launch, etc.).
* **Channel or Platform** (`observation_channel`, select_one, required): Where you observed the activity (e.g., company website, social media, etc.).
* **Date of Activity** (`observation_date`, date, required): Date of the activity.
* **Visibility Level** (`visibility_rating`, select_one, required): How prominent was the observation (e.g., very high, high, low, etc.).
* **Message Clarity** (`message_clarity`, select_one, required): How clear was the message (e.g., very clear, clear, etc.).
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, select_one, required): Who was the target audience (e.g., enterprise customers, consumers, etc.).
* **Geographic Focus** (`geographic_focus`, select_one, required): Which geographic area was targeted.
* **Competitive Impact** (`competitive_impact`, select_one, required): How significant is the impact on us.
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency_level`, select_one, required): How quickly should we respond.
* **Response Needed** (`our_response_needed`, select_one, required): Do we need to respond?
* **Follow-up Required** (`follow_up_required`, select_one, required): Do we need to monitor this continuously?
* **Stakeholders to Notify** (`stakeholders_to_notify`, select_multiple, optional): Who should know about this observation.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Any other relevant information.
* **Evidence URL or Link** (`evidence_url`, text, optional): Link to supporting evidence.
* **Evidence Attachment** (`evidence_attachment`, text, optional): Screenshot or document supporting the observation.
* **Related Products or Services** (`related_products`, text, optional): Relevant products or services affected.
* **Estimated Investment** (`estimated_investment`, select_one, optional): Estimated investment made by the competitor.
* **Expected Duration** (`expected_duration`, select_one, optional): Estimated duration of the activity.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): Date to check again.
