# Customer Survey Implementation Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about customer surveys that will be implemented in the business. It is used for approval purposes only and is not related to the submission of the survey itself.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the title of the customer survey in the first field.
2. Choose the category of the survey from the list provided.
3. Provide a brief description of the survey.
4. Select the tool(s) assigned to the survey from the list provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Survey Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the customer survey.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Choose the category of the survey from the options provided. Options include: Marketing Forms, Sales Forms, and Other Forms.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the survey.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more tools assigned to the survey from the list provided. Options include chatjimmy, chatjane, and chatjohn.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out this form correctly to ensure accurate and efficient processing of the customer survey implementation approval request.
* If you are unsure about any part of this form, please contact form@gic.mx for more information.
