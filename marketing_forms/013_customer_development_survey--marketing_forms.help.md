# Customer Development Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and information from customers about our services or products. Your responses will help us understand your needs and preferences, enabling us to improve our offerings and better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant answer for each question from the provided options.
2. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Form** (Select Multiple): Choose all the options that apply to you.
	+ Yes: This is the correct response if you are interested in our services or products.
	+ No: This is the correct response if you are not interested.
* **Customer Info** (Text): Enter your company name or personal name.
* **Feedback** (Text): Provide any comments or suggestions you have about our services or products.
* **Service or Product Info** (Text): Describe the service or product you are interested in or have used.
* **Improvement Areas** (Text): Share areas where you think we can improve our services or products.
* **Comments** (Text): Add any additional comments or feedback.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your valid email address so we can contact you for follow-up questions or offers.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted.
* **Service or Product Features** (Text): Describe the features of the service or product you are interested in or have used.
* **Company Name** (Text): Enter your company name if you represent a business.
* **Company Info** (Text): Provide additional information about your company.
* **Contact Person** (Text): Enter the name of the person to contact for further information.
* **Contact Person Title** (Text): Enter the title of the contact person.
* **Contact Person Title** (Text): Enter the title of the contact person.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed and honest as possible when providing feedback and information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [insert contact email or phone number].
* We value your feedback and will use it to improve our services and products.
* Your email address will be used for follow-up questions or offers only if you choose to be contacted.
