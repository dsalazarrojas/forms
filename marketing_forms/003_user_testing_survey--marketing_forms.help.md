# User Testing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The User Testing Survey form is designed to collect your honest feedback and ratings on our products and services. Your responses will help us understand what we do well and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read through the form carefully and ensure you understand each question and field.
2. Choose your answers based on your genuine experience and opinions.
3. Fill out the form completely, but feel free to skip any questions that do not apply to you.
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Testing Survey** (`user_testing_survey`, text, not required): This is the main field where you'll provide your thoughts and comments on your experience with our product. Please be as detailed as possible in describing your experience, suggestions, or concerns.
* **User Rating** (`user_rating`, number, not required): Rate your overall experience with our product or service on a scale of 1-5.
* **Product Improvement** (`product_improvement`, text, not required): Share with us any ideas you have for improving our product or service. This could be a feature request, an interface suggestion, or any other idea you think would benefit our users.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, not required): Use this field to provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience with our product or service. This can include suggestions, concerns, or just your overall thoughts.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted regarding your feedback or for follow-up questions. This is optional, but it ensures we can get in touch with you if needed.

## Tips
- Please be honest and thorough in your answers.
- If you have any attachments (screenshots, images, or files) that would support your feedback, feel free to attach them.
- We value your feedback and appreciate your time in taking this survey!
