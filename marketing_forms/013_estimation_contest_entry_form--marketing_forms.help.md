# Estimation Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for participants to submit their estimates or guesses for a contest or competition. It is a way for you to provide your predictions and participate in the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details such as your name, email, and phone number.
2. Choose your estimates for each participant by selecting the number of guesses.
3. Select the entry date and time.
4. Submit your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How many do you think?** (`participant_guesses`): Select the number of guesses you think a participant will have.
	+ Under 10: You think the participant will have less than 10 guesses.
	+ 10-20: You think the participant will have between 10 and 20 guesses.
	+ Over 20: You think the participant will have more than 20 guesses.
* **First Name** (`first_name`): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`): Enter the email address of the participant.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`): Enter the phone number of the participant.
* **Your Guess** (`guess`): Enter the number of guesses you think the participant will have.
* **Entry Date** (`entry_date`): Select the date you are submitting your entry.
* **Entry Time** (`entry_time`): Select the time you are submitting your entry.
* **Entry Time** (`entry_time_hh_mm_ss`): Select the time you are submitting your entry.
* **Participant Guesses 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8** (`participant_guesses_1` to `participant_guesses_8`): Select the number of guesses for each participant.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`): Enter the name of the organization you represent.
* **Organization Phone** (`organization_phone`): Enter the phone number of the organization.
* **Entry Date** (`entry_date`): Select the date you are submitting your entry.
* **Entry Time** (`entry_time`): Select the time you are submitting your entry.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`): Enter the email address of the participant.

Note: All fields are optional, but please ensure you fill in all required fields to complete your entry.
