# Copyright Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Copyright Registration Form is used to collect information about the registration of a copyright for a specific work. This form is intended for use by individuals or organizations that need to register their work for copyright purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Determine the purpose of your registration: If you are registering your work for copyright purposes, select "Yes" from the "copyright_registration_form" field.
2. Provide your copyright name: Enter your copyright name in the "copyright_name" field.
3. Enter your name: Enter your name in the "author_name" field.
4. Enter the copyright date: Enter the date of the copyright in the "copyright_date" field.
5. Provide the title of the work: Enter the title of the work in the "work_title" field.
6. Enter a brief description of the work: Enter a brief description of the work in the "work_description" field.
7. Check the work type: Select "Yes" or "No" from the "work_type" field, depending on whether the work is eligible for copyright registration.
8. Select the countries where the work will be used: Select all applicable countries from the "country" field.
9. Provide contact information: Enter any additional contact information in the "contact_information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Copyright Registration Form** (`copyright_registration_form`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select "Yes" if you are registering your work for copyright purposes.
* **Copyright Name** (`copyright_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your copyright name.
* **Author Name** (`author_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name.
* **Copyright Date** (`copyright_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the copyright.
* **Work Title** (`work_title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the title of the work.
* **Work Description** (`work_description`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the work.
* **Work Type** (`work_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select "Yes" if the work is eligible for copyright registration.
* **Country** (`country`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all applicable countries where the work will be used.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional contact information.
