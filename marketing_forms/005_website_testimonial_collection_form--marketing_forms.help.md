# Website Testimonial Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Website Testimonial Collection Form" is a crucial tool for gathering user feedback and testimonials, which are used to inform product development and improvement decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of testimonial you want to submit: Testimonial, Review, or Suggestion.
2. Enter your testimonial content in the text area.
3. Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5.
4. If you have an account with us, enter your email address to allow us to contact you for follow-up questions or to share your testimonial.
5. Provide your phone number for us to contact you if needed.
6. Select the people you want to be associated with the testimonial (e.g., chatbot, human).
7. Choose the person who submitted the testimonial (e.g., you, a friend, family member).
8. Enter the date and time you submitted the testimonial.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Website Testimonial Collection Form**: This field is not a field to fill in; it is the title of the form.
* **testimonial_type**: Select the type of testimonial you want to submit (Testimonial, Review, Suggestion).
	+ This will help us understand the nature of your feedback.
* **testimonial_content**: Enter your testimonial or review in this text area.
	+ Be as detailed as possible, including any relevant information or examples.
* **testimonial_rating**: Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5.
	+ This will help us understand your satisfaction level with our website.
* **testimonial_url**: Enter the URL of your testimonial or review (if applicable).
	+ This can be a link to your blog post, social media post, or any other online presence.
* **email**: Enter your email address if you have an account with us.
	+ This allows us to contact you for follow-up questions or to share your testimonial.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you if needed.
	+ This is optional but helps us reach out to you if we need more information.
* **assigned_to**: Select the people you want to be associated with the testimonial (e.g., chatbot, human).
	+ This helps us understand the context of your feedback.
* **submitted_by**: Choose the person who submitted the testimonial (e.g., you, a friend, family member).
	+ This helps us understand who provided the feedback.
* **submitted_on**: Enter the date and time you submitted the testimonial (YYYY-MM-DD).
	+ This helps us track the timing of your feedback.
* **submitted_at**: Enter the time you submitted the testimonial (HH:MM).
	+ This helps us track the exact timing of your submission.

## Tips
* Be as detailed and specific as possible when providing your testimonial or review.
* Make sure to rate your experience honestly and accurately.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please feel free to contact us.
* If you have any questions or need help, ask us!
