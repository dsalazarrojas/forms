# Jotform Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from users through a series of questions and gather their feedback and comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Name" field with your full name. This is a required field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This is a required field.
3. In the "Quiz" section, select all options that apply to you. You can choose one or multiple options.
4. Select your desired action for submitting the quiz. You can either "Submit" or "Retake" the quiz.
5. If you want to provide a score, enter your score in the corresponding field.
6. Optionally, add any comments you would like to share with us.
7. Finally, provide any feedback about your experience with the quiz.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`<text>`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`<email>`, required): Enter your email address to allow us to get in touch with you.
* **Quiz** (`<select_multiple>`, required): Choose all options that apply to you from the provided list.
* **Submit** (`<select_one>`, required): Select "Submit" to finish the quiz and proceed, or "Retake" to start over.
* **Score** (`<number>`, required): Enter the score you believe you achieved in the quiz.
* **Comments** (`<note>`, optional): Provide any comments or additional information you'd like to share.
* **Feedback** (`<text>`, optional): Share your feedback about your experience with this quiz.
