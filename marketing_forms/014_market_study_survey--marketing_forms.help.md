# Market Study Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Market Study Survey is a form used to collect information from customers about their preferences and demographics. The data collected will be used to better serve you and improve our marketing efforts.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information (1)**: Please enter your full name.
* **Customer Opinions (2)**: Describe your thoughts and opinions about our products or services. This is an optional field.
* **Buying Habits (3)**: Share with us your usual purchase behavior.
* **Customer Preferences (4)**: Tell us about your personal preferences.
* **Customer Goals (5)**: Share your short-term and long-term goals.
* **Customer Values (6)**: Tell us about your personal values.
* **Customer Background (7)**: Please provide some background information about yourself.
* **Email Address (8)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number (9)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth (10)**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Time of Birth (11)**: Enter your time of birth.
* **Customer Interest (12)**: Share with us what you are interested in.
* **Customer Opinion (13)**: Share your overall opinion about our products or services.
* **Tips**: Please ensure that your email address and phone number are accurate and up to date.
