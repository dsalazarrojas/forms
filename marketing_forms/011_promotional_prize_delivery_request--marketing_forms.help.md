# promotional_prize_delivery_request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and demographic information from users who wish to receive a promotional prize. Please fill out this form accurately and honestly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin the form.
2. Fill out the "First Name" field with your first name, as per your identification documents.
3. Fill out the "Last Name" field with your last name, as per your identification documents.
4. Enter your "Address" in the space provided, including your street address or apartment number.
5. Enter your "Phone" number, including your area code and the phone number itself.
6. Enter your "Email" address, which will be used to contact you if you are chosen as a winner.
7. Select your "Date of Birth" in the calendar provided, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as per your identification documents. This is a required field.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as per your identification documents. This is a required field.
* **Address**: Enter your street address or apartment number. This is a required field.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, including your area code and the phone number itself. This is a required field.
* **Email**: Enter your email address, which will be used to contact you if you are chosen as a winner. This is a required field.
* **Date of Birth**: Select your date of birth in the calendar provided, in the format MM/DD/YYYY. This is a required field.

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill out the form accurately and honestly.
