# Sign Up For Seasonal Collection Updates - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Sign Up For Seasonal Collection Updates" form allows you to provide your information to receive personalized updates on our seasonal collections. This includes updates on new products, promotions, and events related to your preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field. This will be used to send you updates on our seasonal collections.
4. Optionally, enter your phone number in the Phone field to receive SMS updates.
5. Select your preferred seasons for updates in the Preferences field. Choose one or multiple seasons that interest you the most (Summer, Winter, Spring, or Fall).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`text`, optional): Enter your first name as you'd like to be addressed.
* **Last Name** (`text`, optional): Enter your last name as you'd like to be addressed.
* **Email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address to receive updates on our seasonal collections.
* **Phone** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number to receive SMS updates on our seasonal collections.
* **Preferences** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select the seasons for which you'd like to receive updates. Choose one or multiple options: Summer, Winter, Spring, or Fall.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter your email address correctly to receive updates.
* If you're concerned about spam, consider not entering your phone number.
* You can select multiple seasons for more tailored updates.
* If you're interested in updates for only one season, select that season only.
* You can always unsubscribe from future updates if you no longer want to receive them.
