# sports_media_content_partnership_application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Sports Media Content Partnership Application form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please carefully read through each section and enter the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partner Application** (`partner_application`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of your proposed partnership or collaboration. This is an optional field, but providing a concise and clear overview of your idea will help us understand your proposal better.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): Please provide your contact information, including your email address and phone number. This will help us to get in touch with you regarding your application.
* **Collaboration Proposal** (`collaboration_proposal`, text, required: false): This is an open field for you to elaborate on your partnership proposal in more detail. Please use this space to provide as much information as possible about your idea.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you would like your application to be reviewed by our team.
* **Review Time** (`review_time`, time, required: false): Choose a specific time for your application to be reviewed. Please note that this is optional and we will review your application at the earliest opportunity.
* **Evaluation Score** (`evaluation_score`, number, required: false): Provide a numerical evaluation of your proposal, on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Evaluation Comments** (`evaluation_comments`, text, required: false): Enter any comments or feedback you may have regarding your evaluation.
* **Partnership Terms** (`partnership_terms`, text, required: false): Describe the terms and conditions of your proposed partnership, including any relevant contracts or agreements.
* **Collaboration Outcomes** (`collaboration_outcomes`, text, required: false): Outline the expected outcomes and results of your partnership, including any measurable goals and objectives.
* **Partner Intake** (`partner_intake`, select_one, required: true): Choose one of the provided options to indicate your partner's willingness to collaborate with us.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you regarding your application.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number for us to contact you in case of any further questions or clarifications.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, note, required: false): This is a free-form text field for any agreement or contract-related comments.
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, select_multiple, required: true): Select one or more of the provided options to indicate the current status of your application.
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`, select_multiple, required: false): Enter any comments or feedback you may have regarding the review of your application.
* **Agreement Status** (`agreement_status`, select_multiple, required: true): Select one or more of the provided options to indicate the current status of your partnership agreement.
* **Agreement Comments** (`agreement_comments`, select_multiple, required: false): Enter any comments or feedback you may have regarding your partnership agreement.
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you would like your partnership agreement to be finalized.
* **Agreement Time** (`agreement_time`, time, required: false): Choose a specific time for your partnership agreement to be finalized.
* **Agreement Notes** (`agreement_notes`, text, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments regarding your partnership agreement.

## Tips

* Please ensure that your application is thorough and well-structured to increase your chances of approval.
* Be clear and concise in your writing to avoid any misunderstandings.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, do not hesitate to contact us.
