# Panel Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Panel Application Form is a tool used to collect and track applications for a panel, allowing users to provide their personal and payment information. This form helps to streamline the application process and ensures that all necessary details are captured efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information, including your application details, contact information, payment method, and other relevant fields.
2. Submit the form to initiate the application process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application Info (ID 1)**: Enter a brief description of your application.
* **Applicant (ID 2)**: Enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Contact Info (ID 3)**: Enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
* **Payment Info (ID 4)**: Enter your payment method and amount (if applicable).
* **Submission Date (ID 12)**: Enter the date you submitted the application.
* **Submission Time (ID 13)**: Enter the time you submitted the application.
* **Payment Date (ID 14)**: Enter the date you made the payment (if applicable).
* **Payment Time (ID 15)**: Enter the time you made the payment (if applicable).
* **Payment Method (ID 16)**: Select your payment method (Cash, Check, or Other).
* **Other Payment Method (ID 17)**: Enter any additional information about your payment method (if not using Cash or Check).
* **Payment Amount (ID 18)**: Enter the payment amount (if applicable).
* **Payment Status (ID 19)**: Select the status of your payment (Approved, Denied, or Pending).

Note: The following fields are not required for you to fill in, but are included for your information and reference:

* **Application Status (ID 6)**: This field is automatically populated based on the application process.
* **Application Form ID (ID 7)**: This field is automatically generated and not editable.
* **Notes (ID 9)**: This field is for any additional comments or information about your application.
* **Email (ID 10)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (ID 11)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Submission Date (ID 12)**: Enter the date you submitted the application.
* **Submission Time (ID 13)**: Enter the time you submitted the application.
* **Application Status Label (ID 8)**: This field is automatically populated based on the application process.
