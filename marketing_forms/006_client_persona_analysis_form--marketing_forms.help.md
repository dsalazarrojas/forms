# Client Persona Analysis Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help marketers and business owners develop a comprehensive understanding of their client personas, which are fictional representations of a specific group of people who share similar characteristics, behaviors, and needs. By filling out this form, you will be able to gather and analyze data about your target audience, which will help you tailor your marketing strategies to effectively communicate with and serve their needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly to ensure accurate results.
2. Be specific and provide as much detail as possible for each question.
3. Select the options that best fit your target audience's characteristics, behaviors, and needs.
4. You can skip questions that do not apply to your case or that you cannot find information on.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name**: Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Primary Product or Service**: Describe the main product or service you offer.
* **Describe Your Target Market**: Give a broad description of who you serve.
* **Persona Name**: Give this persona a name, e.g., Marketing Mary.

	+ High School

* **Additional Insights**: Enter anything else that defines this persona.

## Tips

* Be honest and specific when answering each question.
* Provide as much detail as possible for each question.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting.
* Use this form as a starting point for further research and refinement.
* Keep in mind that the more complete and accurate your responses, the better your client personas will be.
