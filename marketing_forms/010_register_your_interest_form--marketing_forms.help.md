# Register Your Interest Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is meant to collect contact information and preferences from individuals interested in learning more about our company and its services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email** in the respective fields.
2. Fill out your **Role** by selecting one of the options or type "Other" if none of the provided options match your position.
3. If you're associated with a company, provide your **Company** name in the text field.
4. If applicable, enter your **Phone** number so we can easily contact you.
5. In the **Comments** field, provide any additional information you'd like us to know.
6. Once you've filled out all the required fields, click submit to send us your interest.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your surname for accurate contact records.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Provide your business email address so we can follow up with you about our services.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): If you're associated with a company, enter the name here.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Choose your position within the company, or type "Other" if it doesn't match the options.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the name of the department you're associated with, if applicable.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional information or context that may be helpful for us to know.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill out the required fields correctly to ensure that our team can follow up with you effectively. If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to contact us.
