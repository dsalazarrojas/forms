# Carnival Ticket Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Carnival Ticket Form is designed to collect information from users who are interested in purchasing tickets for an event. This form helps the user to provide accurate and required details for a smooth ticket purchasing experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
2. Ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information for all fields.
3. Review all sections carefully, especially the payment and confirmation sections.
4. Click the "Submit" button to complete your ticket order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`Full Name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, `email`, required): This is where your digital ticket will be sent. 
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `text`, required): Provide your contact phone number for any follow-up communication. 
* **Event Date Selection** (`Event Date`, `date`, required): Choose the date you will attend the event.
* **Preferred Time Slot** (`Event Location`, `time`, optional): If you have a preferred time slot, please select it to help us manage capacity.
* **Event Location** (`Event Location`, `text`, required): Enter the location of the event.
* **Ticket Quantity** (`Ticket Quantity`, `number`, required): Enter the total number of tickets you need.
* **Ticket Type** (`Ticket Type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the best option for your group (General Admission, VIP Pass, Kids Pass, or Family Bundle).
* **Pre-purchase Ride Vouchers** (`Pre-purchase Ride Vouchers`, `select_one`, required): Select the number of ride vouchers you need (None, 5 Pack, 10 Pack, or Unlimited Day Pass).
* **Pre-purchase Food Vouchers** (`Pre-purchase Food Vouchers`, `select_one`, required): Choose the food vouchers you want (None, 20 dollar Voucher, 50 dollar Voucher).
* **Ticket Number (Unique ID)** (`Ticket Number (Unique ID`, `number`, optional): This is an automatically generated unique ID for your ticket.
* **Billing Address** (`Billing Address`, `text`, optional): Enter your billing address for payment processing.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, Venmo, or Cash at Gate).
* **Promotional Code** (`Promotional Code`, `text`, optional): If you have a code, enter it here.
* **I acknowledge that tickets are non-refundable** (`I acknowledge that tickets are non-refundable`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you understand tickets are non-refundable.
* **I agree to follow all event safety guidelines** (`I agree to follow all event safety guidelines`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you will follow all event rules and park conduct.
* **Subscribe to future event notifications** (`Subscribe to future event notifications`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether you want to stay informed about next year's carnival.
* **Special requests or accommodations** (`Special requests or accommodations`, `text`, optional): If you have any special requests, enter them here.
* **I confirm my ticket order details** (`I confirm my ticket order details`, `text`, required): Type your full name to finalize your ticket order details.

Note: Some fields are optional but may be required depending on the user's preference or the event's requirements. Always review each section carefully before submitting the form.
