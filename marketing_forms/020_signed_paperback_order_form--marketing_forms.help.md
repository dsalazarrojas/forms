# signed_paperback_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The `signed_paperback_order_form` form is used to process orders for signed paperbacks. It captures basic information about the submitter and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields as prompted.
2. Ensure the email address entered is valid to help with order tracking.
3. Enter payment details correctly to ensure smooth order processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submitter Details** (Submitter Details, text, optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the order.
* **name** (name, text, optional): Enter the name of the individual or organization placing the order.
* **phone** (phone, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number for the order.
* **email** (email, email, optional): Enter a valid email address for order tracking and communication.
* **payment_details** (payment_details, text, optional): Enter any additional information related to the payment method, such as payment terms or conditions.

## Tips

* Ensure all fields are filled in accurately and correctly to prevent delays in order processing.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact our support team.
* This form is designed for signed paperbacks; if you're looking to order other products or services, please use the relevant forms.
