# Marketing Plan Evaluation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the effectiveness of a marketing plan by assessing various aspects of the strategy. It's a helpful tool for marketing professionals to identify strengths and weaknesses of their marketing plan and make data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information about your marketing plan.
2. Select the corresponding options for each field based on your marketing strategy.
3. Provide a detailed answer for the optional fields where necessary.
4. Review and submit the form to get a comprehensive evaluation of your marketing plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Marketing Plan Name** (`marketing_plan_name`, text, required)
* This is the title of your marketing plan, which should be a concise and descriptive name that reflects the plan's goals and objectives.

### 2. **Goal/Objective** (`goal_objective`, text, required)
* Describe the main goal and objective of your marketing plan, including what you hope to achieve and why.

### 3. **Target Market** (`target_market`, text, required)
* Identify the specific market or audience your marketing plan is targeting, including demographics, preferences, or interests.

### 4. **Marketing Strategy** (`marketing_strategy`, text, optional)
* Provide a brief description of your marketing strategy, including the tactics and approaches you plan to use to achieve your objectives.

### 5. **Competition Analysis** (`competition_analysis`, text, optional)
* Research and describe the competition in your target market, including their strengths and weaknesses.

### 6. **Market Trends** (`market_trends`, text, optional)
* Identify the current market trends and how they may impact your marketing plan, including changes in consumer behavior or preferences.

### 7. **Marketing Milestones** (`marketing_milestones`, text, optional)
* List the key milestones and deadlines for your marketing plan, including specific targets and timelines.

### 8. **Strategy Quality** (`strategy_quality`, select_multiple, required)
* Evaluate the quality of your marketing strategy by selecting from the following options:
	+ High: Well-planned and well-executed strategy with clear objectives and tactics.
	+ Medium: Good strategy, but with some room for improvement.
	+ Low: Poor strategy with unclear objectives and ineffective tactics.

### 9. **Marketing Objectives** (`marketing_objectives`, select_one, required)
* Choose the main objective of your marketing plan from the following options:
	+ Increase brand awareness
	+ Increase sales
	+ Increase customer engagement

### 10. **Marketing Strategy Score** (`marketing_strategy_score`, select_one, required)
* Evaluate the overall score of your marketing strategy by selecting from the following options:
	+ 1-2: Poor strategy with major flaws.
	+ 3-4: Fair strategy with some room for improvement.
	+ 5-6: Good strategy with some minor flaws.

### 11. **Customer Satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction`, select_one, required)
* Assess the level of customer satisfaction with your marketing efforts by selecting from the following options:
	+ Satisfied
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Neutral

### 12. **Data Quality** (`data_quality`, select_one, required)
* Evaluate the quality of the data used in your marketing plan by selecting from the following options:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor

### 13. **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required)
* Provide any additional feedback or comments about your marketing plan, including areas for improvement or suggestions.

### 14. **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, optional)
* Describe any next steps or future plans you have for your marketing strategy, including new initiatives or adjustments.
