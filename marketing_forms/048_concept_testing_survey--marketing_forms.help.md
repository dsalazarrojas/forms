# Concept Testing Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and data from potential users to test and validate a new concept. This helps us understand how appealing the concept is to different types of users, their willingness to pay for it, and any potential barriers to adoption. By analyzing this data, we can refine and improve the concept to make it more appealing and accessible to a wider audience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on "Submit" to start the form.
2. Please answer all the required fields (marked as "required: true").
3. Be honest and provide your genuine feedback.
4. Click on "Submit" again to send your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Segment** (`respondent_segment`, text, required/optional): This field helps us understand which group you belong to. Please enter your demographic or user segment.
* **Appeal Rating** (`appeal_rating`, select_one, required/true): How appealing is the concept to you? Please select one of the options: Very appealing, Somewhat appealing, Neutral, or Not appealing.
* **Willingness to Pay** (`willingness_to_pay`, select_one, required/true): Would you pay for this product or service? Please select: True, Maybe, or False.
* **Main Use Case** (`main_use_case`, text, required/optional): Please describe how you would use this concept.
* **Barriers to Adoption** (`barriers_to_adoption`, text, required/optional): If there are any reasons you would not adopt this solution, please list them.
* **Feature Priorities** (`feature_priorities`, text, required/optional): Which features matter most to you? Please list them.

## Tips

* Please be honest and provide your genuine feedback.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Barriers to Adoption" or "Main Use Case" fields.
* If you have any technical issues while completing the form, please contact our support team.
