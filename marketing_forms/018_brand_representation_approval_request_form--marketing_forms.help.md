# Brand Representation Approval Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request brand representation, such as logo usage, brand messaging, product representation, spokesperson roles, social media representation, press or media appearances, and partnership announcements. It's designed to provide all necessary information for the brand approval process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill in all required fields**, as indicated by the asterisk (\*) symbol in the field label.
2. **Submit the form** once all required fields are filled in.
3. **Attach any supporting materials** mentioned in the "Supporting Documents" field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requestor Full Name (\*):** Your full legal name, as listed on your government ID or passport.
* **Contact Email (\*):** Your email address that can be used for communication about your request.
* **Contact Phone:** Your phone number that can be reached at for further questions.
* **Department:** Please select the department you belong to, such as Marketing, Sales, Communications, Product, Human Resources, External Agency, or Partner Organization.
* **Company Name (\*):** The name of your organization, which should match your government ID or business registration.
* **Type of Representation (\*):** Choose the type of brand representation you are requesting, such as Logo Usage, Brand Messaging, Product Representation, Spokesperson Role, Social Media Representation, Press or Media Appearance, Event Representation, or Partnership Announcement.
* **Context of Representation (\*):** Select where this representation will occur, such as Public Event, Media Interview, Social Media, Corporate Website, Print Publication, Video Content, Internal Communication, or External Communication.
* **Date of Representation (\*):** The date when you expect this representation to occur.
* **Description of Representation (\*):** A brief description of how you plan to represent the brand.
* **Brand Elements to Be Used (\*):** Select all brand elements that will be used, such as Company Logo, Brand Colors, Brand Fonts, Tagline, Brand Voice, Product Images, Marketing Materials, or None.
* **Target Audience:** Describe the audience for this representation, such as internal staff, external stakeholders, or the general public.
* **Expected Reach:** Estimate the number of people who will see this representation, such as internal only, small group (under 100), medium audience (100-1000), large audience (1000-10,000), or mass media (10,000+).
* **Key Messages:** Write a brief description of the key messages that will be communicated through this representation.
* **Potential Risks to Brand Reputation:** Identify any potential risks to the brand reputation, such as Low Risk, Medium Risk, or High Risk.
* **Previous Approval:** Indicate if similar representation has been approved before, such as True or False.
* **Supporting Documents:** Check if you have any supporting materials to submit with your request, such as Yes, Will Attach, or No Documents.
* **Approval Timeline Needed (\*):** Select the date by which you need approval for this representation.
* **Additional Comments:** Provide any other relevant information or comments about your request.

By following this guide, you should be able to complete the "Brand Representation Approval Request Form" accurately and effectively. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the relevant department.
