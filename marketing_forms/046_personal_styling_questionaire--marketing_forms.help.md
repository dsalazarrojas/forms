# Personal Styling Questionaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Styling Questionaire is designed to collect information about an event or party's style, theme, size, date, time, location, and other relevant attributes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the style of the party from the options provided (Style 1, Style 2, or Style 3).
2. Choose the theme of the party from the options provided (Theme goals or Other).
3. Select the size of the party from the options provided (Small, Medium, or Large).
4. Enter the date of the party in the required format (mm/dd/yyyy).
5. Enter the time of the party in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
6. Choose the location of the party from the options provided (Location 1, Location 2, or Location 3).
7. Select whether party details should be included (Yes or No).
8. If Yes, choose the time for the party from the options provided (Time 1 or Time 2).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **party_style**: Select the overall style of the party from the options provided.
	+ This field will help us understand the overall aesthetic and atmosphere of the party.
* **party_theme**: Choose the theme of the party from the options provided.
	+ This field will help us understand the underlying idea or concept behind the party.
* **party_size**: Select the expected number of guests for the party.
	+ This field will help us plan for the party's space and resource needs.
* **party_date**: Enter the date of the party in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
	+ This field will help us schedule the party and ensure it doesn't conflict with other events.
* **party_time**: Enter the time of the party in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
	+ This field will help us schedule the party and ensure it doesn't conflict with other events.
* **party_location**: Choose the location of the party from the options provided.
	+ This field will help us determine the party's venue and logistical requirements.
* **party_details2**: Select whether you want to include party details in the invitation.
	+ This field will help us decide whether to include additional information in the invitation.
* **party_time2**: If party details are included, choose the time for the party from the options provided.
	+ This field will help us provide accurate time information in the invitation.

## Tips
* Please ensure the information provided is accurate and complete to ensure the party's details are correctly conveyed to guests.
* If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to the party organizer or event planner for clarification.
* Please review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
