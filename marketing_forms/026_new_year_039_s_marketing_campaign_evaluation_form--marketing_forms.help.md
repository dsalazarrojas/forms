# New Year's Marketing Campaign Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The primary purpose of this form is to assess the performance of a marketing campaign, gathering data on its objective, target audience, budget, results, and evaluation criteria.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Answer each question carefully**: Provide detailed and honest responses to each field.
2. **Use the correct units**: When filling in numerical fields, ensure the units match the expected format (e.g., days for campaign duration).
3. **Select relevant options**: Choose the most relevant options for each field, such as target audience or marketing channels used.
4. **Provide clear and concise answers**: Avoid unnecessary descriptions or explanations; keep your answers brief and to the point.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What was the primary objective of the marketing campaign?** (`primary_objective`, text, required): This field asks for a brief description of the main goal of the campaign.
* **Target audience** (`target_audience`, select_one, required): Select the group of people the campaign was designed to target (e.g., Young Adults, Middle Age, Seniors).
* **Which marketing channels were used during the campaign?** (`marketing_channels`, select_multiple, required): Choose all applicable channels used to promote the campaign (e.g., Social Media, Email Marketing, TV Advertising).
* **Campaign budget** (`campaign_budget`, number, required): Enter the total budget allocated for the campaign, using the correct currency units (e.g., dollars, euros, pounds).
* **Campaign results** (`campaign_results`, text, required): Describe the key outcomes of the campaign, including successes and challenges faced.
* **Evaluation criteria** (`evaluation_criteria`, select_multiple, required): Select up to three criteria to evaluate the campaign's performance (e.g., Effectiveness, Efficiency, Credibility).
* **Evaluation scale** (`evaluation_scale`, select_one, required): Choose a scale to evaluate the campaign's performance, such as 1-10 or 1-5.
* **Start date** (`date_start`, date, required): Enter the start date of the campaign, in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **End date** (`date_end`, date, required): Enter the end date of the campaign, in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Campaign duration** (`campaign_duration`, number, required): Enter the length of the campaign, in units matching the expected format (e.g., days).
* **Evaluation score** (`evaluation_score`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the campaign's evaluation score, based on the selected evaluation criteria.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or insights about the campaign's performance.
