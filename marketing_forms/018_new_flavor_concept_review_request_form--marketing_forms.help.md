# New Flavor Concept Review Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit new flavor concepts for review and approval. Please fill out this form completely and accurately to ensure that your submission meets the necessary criteria.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on each field to select or enter your information.
2. For fields with options, choose the correct answer from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter your name, phone number, and email address in the corresponding fields.
4. Review and submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Flavor Name**: Enter the name of the new flavor concept you are submitting for review.
* **Description**: Enter a brief description of the new flavor concept, including the type and category of the flavor.
* **Idea Source**: Explain where the idea for the new flavor concept came from (e.g. customer feedback, market research, etc.).
* **Status**: Choose the status of the new flavor concept (e.g. Active, Inactive, etc.).
* **Review Team Members**: Choose which team members will review the new flavor concept (e.g. 'Yes', 'No').
* **Review Date**: Enter the date you would like to have the new flavor concept reviewed by the review team.
* **Review Time**: Enter the time of day you would like to have the new flavor concept reviewed by the review team.
* **Review Notes**: Enter any additional comments or information about the new flavor concept.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for follow-up communications.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for follow-up communications.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date you are submitting the new flavor concept for review.
* **Review Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information about the new flavor concept.
* **Next Steps**: Enter any next steps or follow-up actions for the review team.
* **Submission Status**: Choose the status of the new flavor concept (e.g. Active, Inactive, etc.).
* **User Signature**: Sign your name to confirm that the submission is complete and accurate.
* **Submitter Name**: Enter your name as the submitter of the new flavor concept.
* **Form ID**: Enter a unique ID for the new flavor concept (optional).
