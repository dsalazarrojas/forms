# Marketing Professional Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for marketing professionals to apply for opportunities within the organization. Please provide accurate and detailed information to help us assess your qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out all required fields**, marked with an *asterisk* (*), carefully and accurately.
2. **Double-check your email address**, as this will be used for communication.
3. **Select all relevant skills** from the options provided.
4. **Attach your resume and portfolio**, if applicable.
5. **Submit your application**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. First Name (*First Name*)
    *First Name*: A personal name that will be used for communication and record-keeping.
### 2. Last Name (*Last Name*)
    *Last Name*: A personal name that will be used for communication and record-keeping.
### 3. Email (*Email*)
    *Email*: A unique address used for communication and will be used for future contacts.
### 4. Job Title (*Job Title*)
    *Job Title*: A brief description of your current or previous job titles.
### 5. Company (*Company*)
    *Company*: A name of the company you are currently or previously working with.
### 6. Skills (*Skills*)
    *Select any of the following skills that you possess:*
        * Marketing
        * Graphic Design
        * Video Editing
        * Writing
        * Data Analysis*
### 7. LinkedIn (*LinkedIn*)
    *LinkedIn*: A link to your public LinkedIn profile or a username associated with your profile.
### 8. Resume (*Resume*)
    *A copy of your current resume or a link to it, if it is publicly available.*
### 9. Portfolio (*Portfolio*)
    *A link or a copy of your portfolio, if it is publicly available.*
### 10. Contact Number (*Contact Number*)
    *A contact number where we can reach out to you.*
### 11. Address (*Address*)
    *A physical address where we can send you information or send you a physical copy of any documents.*
### 12. Zip Code (*Zip Code*)
    *Zip Code*: A zip code associated with the address provided.
### 13. Date of Birth (*Date of Birth*)
    *Date of birth is not required, but it will be used to determine eligibility.*
### 14. Availability (*Availability*)
    *A brief description of your current availability.*
### 15. Education (*Education*)
    *A brief description of your education background.*
### 16. Experience (*Experience*)
    *A brief description of your work experience.*
### 17. Certifications (*Certifications*)
    *Select any of the following certifications that you possess:*

        * Google Ads*
### 18. Language (*Language*)
    *Select one language from the options provided:*
        * English
        * Spanish
        * French
        * German*
### 19. Resume File (*Resume File*)
    *A link or a copy of your resume, if it is publicly available.*
### 20. Portfolio File (*Portfolio File*)
    *A link or a copy of your portfolio, if it is publicly available.*
### 21. Portfolio URL (*Portfolio URL*)
    *A link to your portfolio, if it is publicly available.*
### 22. Social Media (*Social Media*)
    *Select any of the following social media that you use:*
        * LinkedIn
        * Facebook

        * Twitter*
### 23. Other Skills (*Other Skills*)
    *Any other skills that you have, which are not listed above.*
### 24. Why Me (*Why Me*)
    *A brief statement of why you are the best fit for this opportunity.*
