# Skincare Launch Content Creators Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us find the best candidate for the Skincare Launch Content Creators job position. Please fill out the form thoroughly and accurately, and we will be in touch with you soon.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*). These fields are essential for us to assess your application.
2. Be as detailed as possible with your answers, especially in the resume and message fields.
3. Make sure to choose the correct options from the select options fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name.
* **email** (`email`, `email`, required): Please enter your email address.
* **phone** (`phone`, `text`, required=false): This field is optional, but please enter your phone number if you want us to be able to contact you.
* **resume** (`resume`, `note`, required=false): Please upload or paste a link to your resume or portfolio.
* **work_experience** (`work_experience`, `select_multiple`, required): Are you available to work on weekdays?
	+ 'Yes' if you can work during weekdays (Monday to Friday)
	+ 'No' otherwise
* **portfolio** (`portfolio`, `select_one`, required): Do you have a portfolio that showcases your work?
	+ 'Yes' if you have a portfolio online or on a personal website
	+ 'No' otherwise
* **available_time** (`available_time`, `time`, required): How many hours per week are you available to work?
* **why_skincare** (`why_skincare`, `select_one`, required): Why do you want to work in the skincare industry?
	+ 'Yes' if you have a genuine interest in the industry
	+ 'No' otherwise
* **skincare_launch_team** (`skincare_launch_team`, `select_one`, required): Are you willing to work with our skincare launch team?
	+ 'Yes' if you can work collaboratively with the team
	+ 'No' otherwise
* **message** (`message`, `note`, required=false): Please add any additional information that will help us get to know you better.

## Tips
- Please make sure to proofread your application before submitting it.
- If you have any issues or concerns, feel free to contact us and we will be happy to help.
