# Dealer Spotlight Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dealer Spotlight Submission Form is used to gather information about dealers and their expertise for marketing campaigns and events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information about the dealer.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for Category and Form Type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Dealer Name** (`dealer_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the dealer.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the dealer's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter the dealer's phone number.
* **Website** (`website`, text, required: false): Enter the dealer's website.
* **LinkedIn** (`social_linkedin`, text, required: false): Enter the dealer's LinkedIn link.
* **Facebook** (`social_facebook`, text, required: false): Enter the dealer's Facebook link.
* **Twitter** (`social_twitter`, text, required: false): Enter the dealer's Twitter link.
* **Instagram** (`social_instagram`, text, required: false): Enter the dealer's Instagram link.
* **Logo URL** (`logo_url`, text, required: false): Upload or enter the URL of the dealer's logo.
* **Logo Alt Text** (`logo_alt_text`, text, required: false): Enter a short text to describe the dealer's logo.
* **Spotlight Image** (`spotlight_image`, text, required: false): Upload or enter the URL of an image of the dealer.
* **Bio** (`bio`, text, required: false): Enter a short bio about the dealer.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Enter the location where the dealer operates.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the available options for the category.
* **Subcategory** (`subcategory`, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the available options for the subcategory.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the company the dealer represents.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter the dealer's address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required: false): Enter the city where the dealer operates.
* **State** (`state`, text, required: false): Enter the state where the dealer operates.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required: false): Enter the dealer's zip code.
* **Country** (`country`, text, required: false): Enter the country where the dealer operates.
* **Form Type** (`form_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the available options for the form type.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information about the dealer.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for assistance.
- If you have already been featured in a Dealer Spotlight in the past year, please do not submit this form again.
