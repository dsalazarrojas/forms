# Agency Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agency Request Form is designed to help our team understand your business needs and preferences for agency support. This form will help us tailor our support to your specific requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out each page of the form, answering the questions and selecting options as needed.
2. Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
3. If you have any additional comments or questions, feel free to add them to the "Additional Requirements" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name (1)**: Enter the name of your business. This will help us understand who you are and what you do.
* **Contact Person Name (2)**: Enter the name of the primary contact person for your business. This will allow us to direct communication and questions to the right person.
* **Contact Person Title (3)**: Enter the title of the primary contact person. This helps us understand their role in the business.
* **Contact Email (4)**: Enter the email address for the primary contact person. This is the best way for us to reach out to you with questions or updates.
* **Contact Phone (5)**: Enter the phone number for the primary contact person. This is the best way for us to reach out to you with urgent questions or updates.
* **Business Address (6)**: Enter the complete business address. This helps us understand your business location.
* **Business Industry (7)**: Select the industry your business operates in. This helps us understand your business's market and competition.
* **Business Description (8)**: Provide a brief description of your business. This helps us understand your business's products or services.
* **Marketing Goals (9)**: This is a header for the next section, which is about your marketing goals.
* **Marketing Objectives (10)**: Select all the marketing objectives that apply to your business, such as increasing brand awareness or driving sales.
* **Target Audience (11)**: Describe your target audience, including demographics and interests.
* **Current Marketing Channels (12)**: List any marketing channels you are currently using.
* **Marketing Budget Range (13)**: Select your approximate monthly marketing budget.
* **Preferred Services (14)**: Select the services you are most interested in, such as social media management or content creation.
* **Timeline Expectations (15)**: Select when you would like to start working with an agency.
* **Previous Agency Experience (16)**: If you have worked with a marketing agency before, select yes or no.
* **Previous Agency Details (17)**: If you have worked with a marketing agency before, provide details about your experience.
* **Success Metrics (18)**: Describe how you measure marketing success.
* **Additional Requirements (19)**: Enter any additional requirements or information for the agency.
