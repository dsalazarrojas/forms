# Ad Relevance Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to provide feedback on the relevance of advertisements you have seen. Your input will help us improve our ad campaigns and provide more relevant ads to you.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Ad Relevance Feedback Form**
   - This form is split into several sections, each labeled as a header.
2. **Advertisement Details**
   - Select the Ad Reference / ID option from the provided dropdown menu.
   - You can enter a brief description of the product or brand if you know it.
3. **Where Did You See This Ad?**
   - Select where you saw the ad from the options provided.
4. **Your Feedback**
   - Rate the relevance of the ad to your interests from 1 to 5.
   - Select your primary impression of the ad from the options provided.
   - Check the box to indicate if you took any action after seeing the ad.
5. **Improvement Suggestions**
   - If you have any ideas on how we can improve our ad experience for you, please enter them here.
6. **Optional Demographic Info**
   - Select your age range from the options provided.
   - Choose any topics that interest you from the list provided.
7. **Consent and Submission**
   - Check the box to agree to allow this feedback to be used to personalize your ad experience.
8. **Submission**
   - Click the submit button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Ad Reference / ID** (`ad_identification`, Text, Required): This is a text field where you can enter the ID or name of the ad, if you know it.
* **Where Did You See This Ad?** (`platform_seen`, Select One, Required): This field allows you to select where you saw the ad.
* **How Relevant Was This Ad?** (`relevance_rating`, Number, Required): Rate the ad from 1 to 5 based on its relevance to your interests.
* **What Was Your Primary Impression?** (`ad_perception`, Select One, Required): Select the most accurate option that describes your primary impression of the ad.
* **Did You Take Any Action?** (`action_taken`, Select One, Required): Check the box that best describes what you did after seeing the ad.
* **How Can We Improve?** (`improvement_suggestion`, Text, Optional): If you have any ideas, enter them here.
* **Your Age Range** (`age_range`, Select One, Optional): Select your age range from the options provided.
* **Select Topics You Are Interested In** (`interests_selection`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose any topics that interest you.
* **Consent** (`feedback_consent`, Select One, Required): Check the box to agree to allow this feedback to be used to personalize your ad experience.
* **Date** (`submission_date`, Date, Required): This is a required date field to record the submission date.

## Tips

* Please fill out as much information as possible to help us better understand your feedback.
* If you are unsure about a field, please do not hesitate to ask.
