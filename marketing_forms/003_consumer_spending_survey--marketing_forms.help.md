# Consumer Spending Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Consumer Spending Survey is designed to gather insights on consumer spending habits and preferences. This survey aims to understand how often consumers purchase products or services, their average spend per purchase, and their preferred purchase channels. The survey will also help us understand factors that influence purchase decisions and gather feedback on our current offerings.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question carefully and truthfully based on your personal experiences.
2. For demographic questions, select the age range that best describes you.
3. Select only one preferred purchase channel for each question.
4. Choose up to three factors that influence your purchasing decisions.
5. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the 'Additional comments' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Age range (id: 2)**: Select your age range from the options provided.
* **Gender (id: 3)**: Select the option that best describes your gender.
* **Household income range (id: 4)**: Select your household income range from the options provided.
* **Purchase frequency for category (id: 5)**: Select how often you purchase products or services in this category.
* **Average spend per purchase (id: 6)**: Enter the typical amount you spend on each purchase in your local currency.
* **Preferred purchase channel (id: 7)**: Select where you usually buy the product or service.
* **Brand loyalty (id: 8)**: Select how loyal you are to a specific brand.
* **Factors influencing purchase (id: 9)**: Select up to three key factors that influence your purchasing decisions.
* **Additional comments (id: 10)**: Share any additional feedback or comments about your purchasing habits.
* **Permission to contact (id: 11)**: If you agree to be contacted for follow-up, select your preferred contact method.
