The goal of this guide is to walk a non-technical user through the Client Registration form, explaining each field and its purpose. This guide will cover the form's purpose, how to complete it, and provide explanations for each field.

# Client Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects personal and business information from clients for our marketing purposes. We will use this information to tailor our marketing efforts to your business needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. Enter your personal information (First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and Date of Birth).
3. Enter your business information (Business Name, Type of Business, Industry, and Billing Address).
4. If applicable, enter your shipping address (Street Address, City, State/Province, ZIP/Postal Code, and Country).
5. If you have special requirements or preferences, please enter them in the field provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First Name** (id: 2, name: first_name, type: text, required: true): Enter your first name as it appears on your personal identification documents.
- **Last Name** (id: 3, name: last_name, type: text, required: true): Enter your last name as it appears on your personal identification documents.
- **Email Address** (id: 4, name: email, type: email, required: true): Enter your business or personal email address. We will never share your email with anyone.
- **Phone Number** (id: 5, name: phone, type: text, required: true): Enter your phone number including your country code.
- **Date of Birth** (id: 6, name: date_of_birth, type: date, required: false): If applicable, enter your date of birth. (Optional)
- **Business Name** (id: 8, name: business_name, type: text, required: true): Enter your business name as it appears on your business registration documents.
- **Type of Business** (id: 9, name: business_type, type: select_one, required: true): Select your type of business from the options provided.
- **Industry** (id: 10, name: industry, type: text, required: true): Enter your industry or field of business.
- **Business Website** (id: 11, name: website, type: text, required: false): If your business has a website, enter the URL.
- **Street Address** (id: 13, name: billing_street, type: text, required: true): Enter the street address of your business.
- **City** (id: 14, name: billing_city, type: text, required: true): Enter the city of your business.
- **State/Province** (id: 15, name: billing_state, type: text, required: true): Enter the state or province of your business.
- **ZIP/Postal Code** (id: 16, name: billing_zip, type: text, required: true): Enter the zip or postal code of your business.
- **Country** (id: 17, name: billing_country, type: text, required: true): Enter the country of your business.
- **Same as Billing** (id: 19, name: same_as_billing, type: select_one, required: false): If your shipping address is the same as your billing address, select 'Yes'.
- **Shipping Street Address** (id: 20, name: shipping_street, type: text, required: false): If your shipping address is different from your billing address, enter the shipping street address.
- **Shipping City** (id: 21, name: shipping_city, type: text, required: false): If your shipping address is different from your billing address, enter the shipping city.
- **Any special requirements or preferences?** (id: 22, name: special_requirements, type: text, required: false): Enter any special requirements or preferences you may have.

This guide has been created to help non-technical users understand the purpose and requirements of the Client Registration form. It is intended to be concise and practical, with each field explained clearly and simply.
