# Rewards Redemption Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rewards Redemption Log Form is used to record and track rewards redemptions for customers, providing valuable feedback and insight to improve the reward program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the reward details in the "reward" field.
2. Select the date of the reward redemption in the "date" field using the calendar picker.
3. Enter the amount of the reward in the "amount" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "email" field, if you want to be contacted about your entry.
5. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "phone" field.
6. Choose whether you reviewed the reward in the "review" field by selecting "Yes" or "No".
7. Select the status of your reward redemption in the "status" field by choosing from the options provided.
8. Indicate whether you received the correct reward in the "reason" field by selecting "Yes" or "No".
9. Provide any additional comments about your experience with the reward in the "comments" field.
10. If you have an attachment or evidence to provide, enter it in the "attachment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reward** (Required): Enter a detailed description of the reward received.
* **Date** (Required): Select the date you received the reward using the calendar picker.
* **Amount** (Required): Enter the value of the reward.
* **Email** (Optional): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted about your entry.
* **Phone** (Optional): Enter your phone number if you have one.
* **Review** (Required): Choose whether you reviewed the reward by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Status** (Required): Select the status of your reward redemption from the options provided.
* **Reason** (Required): Indicate whether you received the correct reward.
* **Comments** (Optional): Provide any comments or feedback about your experience with the reward.
* **Attachment** (Optional): Attach any relevant files or evidence to support your entry.
