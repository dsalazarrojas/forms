The "Publishing Book Fair Lead Generation" form is used to collect leads and generate new leads from various sources (e.g., web, social media, events). # Publishing Book Fair Lead Generation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and manage leads from various sources, categorize them, and set up follow-up actions for the publishing team.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the category of interest for the lead.
2.  Fill in the name and email of the contact person.
3.  Provide the time and date for the event or follow-up action.
4.  Choose the person to be assigned to handle the lead.
5.  Select the lead source.
6.  Choose the status of the lead.
7.  Select the category for the lead.
8.  Choose the time for the event.
9.  Select the location and type of event.
10.  Write a follow-up message or note, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Publishing Book Fair Lead Generation (1)** (`number`, **required**): This is a required field that serves as a unique identifier for the lead.
*   **Publishing Book Fair Lead Generation Interests (2)** (`select_multiple`, **required**): Select one or more categories for the lead from the options: Children's Literature, Non-Fiction, Fiction, Romance, Mystery, Thriller, Horror, Fantasy, Science Fiction, Historical Fiction, Poetry, Biography, and Autobiography.
*   **Name - (3)** (`text`, **required**): Enter the name of the contact person.
*   **Email (4)** (`email`, **required**): Enter the email address of the contact person.
*   **Phone (5)** (`text`, **optional**): Enter the phone number of the contact person (if available).
*   **Time (6)** (`time`, **required**): Enter the time for the event or follow-up action in the format HH:MM.
*   **Follow-up Time (7)** (`time`, **required**): Enter the follow-up time in the format HH:MM.
*   **Assigned To (8)** (`select_one`, **required**): Choose the person to be assigned to handle the lead from the options: John, Jane, or Jim.
*   **Follow-up Note (9)** (`note`, **optional**): Write a follow-up note for the lead.
*   **Lead Source (10)** (`select_one`, **required**): Choose the lead source from the options: Web, Social Media, or Event.
*   **Lead Status (11)** (`select_one`, **required**): Choose the status of the lead from the options: New, Existing, or Qualified.
*   **Lead Category (12)** (`select_one`, **required**): Choose the category for the lead from the options: Literary, Marketing, or Sales.
*   **Event Time (13)** (`select_one`, **required**): Choose the time of the event from the options: AM, PM, or All Day.
*   **Event Date (14)** (`date`, **required**): Enter the date of the event in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
*   **Event Location (15)** (`text`, **required**): Enter the location of the event.
*   **Event Type (16)** (`select_multiple`, **required**): Choose the type of event from the options: Conference, Seminar, Workshop, or Book Fair.
*   **Follow-up Message (17)** (`note`, **optional**): Write a follow-up message for the lead.
*   **Lead Qualifier (18)** (`select_one`, **required**): Choose the lead qualifier from the options: Publishing Team, Author, Editor, or Designer.
*   **Follow-up Method (19)** (`select_one`, **required**): Choose the follow-up method from the options: Phone Call, Email, Text Message, or In Person.
*   **Event Size (20)** (`select_one`, **optional**): Choose the size of the event from the options: Small, Medium, or Large.
*   **Lead Source Info (21)** (`text`, **required**): Enter any additional information about the lead source.
*   **Lead Notes (22)** (`note`, **optional**): Write any additional notes about the lead.
*   **Follow-up Date (23)** (`date`, **optional**): Enter the follow-up date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
*   **Follow-up Status (24)** (`select_one`, **optional**): Choose the follow-up status from the options: New, Followed-up, or No Follow-up.
*   **Lead Qualifier Info (25)** (`text`, **required**): Enter any additional information about the lead qualifier.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill in all required fields.
*   Choose the correct lead category and source.
*   Enter accurate and up-to-date information about the lead.
*   Use the follow-up note to add any additional comments or details about the lead.
*   Review the form thoroughly before submitting it.
