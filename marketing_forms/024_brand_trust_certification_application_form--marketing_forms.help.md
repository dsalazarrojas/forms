# Brand Trust Certification Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Brand Trust Certification Application Form is used by organizations to apply for a trust certification. This certification is a way for organizations to demonstrate their commitment to trustworthiness and good business practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Organization Name** (Page 1): Provide the full and legal name of the organization applying for the certification.
2. Enter the **Contact Email** (Page 2): Provide the email address of the person or team responsible for the application and correspondence related to the certification.
3. Describe the **Trust Practices Summary** (Page 3): In the space provided, describe the policies and practices that demonstrate your organization's commitment to trustworthiness.
4. (Optional) List **Supporting Documents** (Page 4): If applicable, list the documents you are providing with the application.
5. Select the **Certification Level Requested** (Page 5): Choose the level of certification you seek: Bronze, Silver, or Gold.
6. Provide the **Primary Contact** (Page 6): Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for the application.
7. Make a **Declaration** (Page 7): Confirm that the information provided is accurate by selecting "I confirm".
8. Add any **Additional Comments** (Page 8): If you have any additional information or context you'd like to provide to the certifying body, enter it here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Organization Name
* **Organization Name** (text, required): Enter the full and legal name of the organization applying for the certification.

### 2. Contact Email
* **Contact Email** (email, required): Enter the email address of the person or team responsible for the application and correspondence related to the certification.

### 3. Trust Practices Summary
* **Trust Practices Summary** (text, required): Describe the policies and practices that build trust. This may include things like commitment to transparency, accountability, and customer satisfaction.

### 4. Supporting Documents
* **Supporting Documents** (note, optional): If you have any supporting documents related to the application, list them here. This may include copies of policies, licenses, or certifications from other reputable organizations.

### 5. Certification Level Requested
* **Certification Level Requested** (select_one, required): Choose the level of certification you seek: Bronze, Silver, or Gold. Each level typically requires varying degrees of documentation and review.

### 6. Primary Contact
* **Primary Contact** (text, required): Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for the application.

### 7. Declaration
* **Declaration** (select_one, required): Confirm that the information provided is accurate. You will either select "I confirm" or "I do not confirm" here.

### 8. Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (text, optional): If you have any additional information or context you'd like to provide to the certifying body, enter it here.
