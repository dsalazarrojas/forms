## Wine And Meal News - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Wine And Meal News" form is designed to collect feedback from clients about their recent wine and meal pairing experience. This information helps our staff to better understand client preferences and make more informed recommendations in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1.  Select one of the options for "Select A Meal" and one of the options for "Select A Wine". These questions will help us understand your preferences.
2.  In the "Wine And Meal Pairing" field, provide any notes you have about your experience, such as what you enjoyed or what didn't go well.
3.  If you had two or more wines during your visit, please fill in the corresponding "First Wine", "Second Wine", and "Third Wine" fields. The "Price" field is where you enter the cost of the wines you had.
4.  In the "Meal Type" field, select the types of meals you had during your visit.
5.  Finally, add any additional comments about your experience in the "Client Comments" and "Staff Comments" fields. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select A Meal** (select_one, required): Choose one of the options provided to select the type of meal you had during your visit.
* **Select A Wine** (select_one, required): Choose one of the options provided to select the type of wine you had during your visit.
* **Wine And Meal Pairing** (note, required): Provide notes about your experience with the wine and meal pairing. This will help our staff understand what worked and what didn't.
* **Date Of Visit** (date, optional): Enter the date of your visit if you'd like to provide more context.
* **Notes** (text, optional): Add any additional notes about your experience.
* **Wine And Meal Info** (text, optional): Add any additional information about the wine and meal you had.
* **First Wine** (text, optional): Enter the name of the first wine you had.
* **First Wine Price** (number, optional): Enter the price of the first wine.
* **Second Wine** (text, optional): Enter the name of the second wine (if any).
* **Second Wine Price** (number, optional): Enter the price of the second wine (if any).
* **Third Wine** (text, optional): Enter the name of the third wine (if any).
* **Third Wine Price** (number, optional): Enter the price of the third wine (if any).
* **Meal Type** (select_multiple, required): Select the types of meals you had during your visit. 
* **Client Comments** (text, optional): Add any additional comments about your experience.
* **Staff Comments** (text, optional): Add any additional comments about your experience. 

Note that some of the fields are marked as required or optional. Please make sure to fill in all the required fields to ensure that your feedback is complete. 

Tips:

- Make sure to be as detailed as possible in your answers to provide the best possible feedback.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
- We appreciate your feedback and look forward to using it to improve our services. 

This guide is designed to be user-friendly and easy to follow. If you need any further clarification, please let us know.
