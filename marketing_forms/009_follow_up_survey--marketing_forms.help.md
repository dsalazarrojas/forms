# Follow Up Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your experience with our product or service. Your input will help us improve our services and ensure we meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Fill out each section carefully and thoroughly.
3. Review your answers before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Feedback** (`customer_feedback`, text, required/optional): Please describe your experience with our service.
* **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, number, required/optional): Rate your experience on a scale of 1 to 10.
* **Rating Scale Comment** (`rating_scale_comment`, text, required/optional): Explain your rating choice.
* **Customer Experience** (`customer_experience`, text, required/optional): Share your thoughts on how we've done in the past.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers.
* Take your time to ensure your input is accurate and complete.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
