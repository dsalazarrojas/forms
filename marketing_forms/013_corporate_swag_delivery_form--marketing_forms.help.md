# corporate_swag_delivery_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers or clients for corporate swag delivery purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the request items you are interested in by choosing from the options in the "Request Items" field.
2.  Choose whether you would like the items to be shipped or not by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Shipping Details" field.
3.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4.  Enter your name in the "Name" field.
5.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6.  Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
7.  Enter your time in the "Time" field.
8.  Enter any additional notes about your order in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Request Items** (`request_items`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field allows you to select multiple request items for corporate swag delivery.
*   **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, select_one, required/optional): This field is used to choose whether the items will be shipped or not.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): This field is used to collect your email address.
*   **Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect your name.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect your phone number.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): This field is used to collect your date of birth.
*   **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): This field is used to collect your time.
*   **Note** (`note`, note, required/optional): This field is used to collect any additional notes about your order.
