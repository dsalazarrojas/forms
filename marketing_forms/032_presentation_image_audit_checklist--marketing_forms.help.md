# <string> - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help evaluate and score slides based on various criteria, such as image and content attributes. The goal is to provide a structured assessment of each slide's quality and effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the provided information about the slide.
2. Complete each field according to your assessment of the slide's attributes.
3. Enter a numerical value for "Image Text Balance" (id: 7) as it is a numerical field.
4. Enter your overall assessment in the "Overall Score" field (id: 15).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Slide Title** (`slide_title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the title of the slide, a brief and descriptive name that captures its essence.
- **Slide Subject** (`slide_subject`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the slide's content or subject matter.
- **Image URL** (`image_url`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the URL of the slide's image, if applicable.
- **Image Type** (`image_type`, `text`, required/optional): Select the type of image used on the slide (e.g., photograph, illustration, diagram).
- **Image Size** (`image_size`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the size of the slide's image in terms of dimensions or proportions.
- **Image Color Balance** (`image_color_balance`, `text`, required/optional): Assess the color balance of the image, considering factors like contrast and saturation.
- **Image Text Balance** (`image_text_presentation`, `number`, required/optional): Evaluate the balance between text and image on the slide, using a numerical value.
- **Image Composition** (`image_composition`, `text`, required/optional): Assess the composition of the image, considering factors like symmetry and harmony.
- **Image Audio** (`image_audio`, `text`, required/optional): Select the type of audio or sound used on the slide, if applicable.
- **Image Text Legibility** (`image_text_legibility`, `text`, required/optional): Evaluate the legibility of text on the slide, considering factors like font size and color.
- **Slide Content** (`slide_content`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the slide's content or text.
- **Slide Visual Appeal** (`slide_visual_appeal`, `text`, required/optional): Assess the visual appeal of the slide, considering factors like color palette and overall aesthetic.
- **Slide Clarity** (`slide_clarity`, `text`, required/optional): Evaluate the clarity of the slide's message or content.
- **Slide Uniqueness** (`slide_uniqueness`, `text`, required/optional): Assess the uniqueness of the slide's content or design.
- **Overall Score** (`overall_score`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a numerical value to score the overall quality and effectiveness of the slide.

## Tips
- Be as specific and detailed as possible when completing each field.
- Use numerical values for fields that require quantifiable data.
- Ensure consistency in your assessment across all fields for a fair and accurate evaluation.
