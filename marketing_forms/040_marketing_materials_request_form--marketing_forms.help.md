# Marketing Materials Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect marketing materials requests from customers or potential customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your user information if you are a new customer or prospect.
2. Indicate if you are interested in marketing materials (e.g. 'Yes' or 'No').
3. Enter the date you are interested in receiving the materials.
4. Specify the time you prefer to receive the materials.
5. Enter your contact information (name of company, job position, email, and phone number).
6. If desired, include a message with any additional information or requests.
7. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (User Information)** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Marketing Materials (Marketing Materials)** (`marketing_materials`, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' if you are interested in marketing materials, or 'No' if you are not.
* **Date (Date)** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to receive the marketing materials.
* **Time Requested (Time Requested)** (`time_requested`, time, optional): Enter the time you prefer to receive the marketing materials.
* **Contact Information (Contact Information)** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your company name, job position, email, and phone number.
* **Message (Message)** (`message`, note, optional): Enter any additional information or requests you have.
* **Company (Company)** (`company`, text, optional): Enter the name of your company.
* **Job Position (Job Position)** (`job_position`, text, optional): Enter your job position or title.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone (Phone)** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
