# Product Launch Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Launch Feedback Questionnaire is designed to collect valuable insights and feedback from users about their experience with the product launch. This information helps us understand how users perceive the product, identify areas of improvement, and make data-driven decisions for future developments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your User ID (if provided by the company) to help us identify you as a user.
2. Provide the name of the product you are submitting feedback about.
3. Enter the launch date of the product to help us keep track of the timeline.
4. Share your overall feedback about the product in a brief message.
5. If you have any specific areas where you think the product could be improved, select all that apply.
6. If you have any additional comments or questions, please use the open-ended question field to share your thoughts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User ID (1)**: Enter your User ID, if you have one.
* **Product Name (2)**: Type the name of the product you are submitting feedback about.
* **Launch Date (3)**: Enter the date the product was launched.
* **Feedback (4)**: Share your overall feedback about the product in a few sentences.
* **Improvement areas (5)**: If you have any suggestions for improvement, select all that apply.
* **Open-ended question (6)**: If you have any additional comments or questions, please use this field to share your thoughts.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback, especially when answering the open-ended question.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to reach out to us for clarification.
* We appreciate your honest and constructive feedback, as it helps us create better products for you and our community.
