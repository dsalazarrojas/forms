# vsl_questionnaire_template - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form, "vsl_questionnaire_template", is designed to collect feedback on an offer, product, or service. It includes fields for personal details, demographics, and feedback to help us understand your thoughts and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* First name
	* Last name
	* How do you like our offer? (Select One)
	* How do you like our product? (Select One)
	* Select Multiple (1) and Select Multiple (2)
	* Select One (2) and Select One (4)
2. Provide your email address (optional)
3. Choose your preferred date and time (optional)
4. Leave notes or comments (optional)
5. Select any additional options that apply to you (Select Multiple)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (Text): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last name** (Text): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address. This is optional, but we'd love to hear from you.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number. This is optional.
* **Date** (Date): Select your date of birth or a relevant date.
* **Time** (Time): Select your time of day.
* **Note** (Note): Provide any additional comments or remarks.
* **How do you like our offer?** (Select One): Rate your experience with our offer. Choose from Very good, Good, or Bad.
* **Which of the following apply?** (Select Multiple): Select all that apply.
* **How do you feel about our offer?** (Select One): Rate your experience with our offer. Choose from Very good, Good, or Bad.
* **Select Multiple2** (Select Multiple): Select all that apply.
* **How do you like our product?** (Select One): Rate your experience with our product. Choose from Very good, Good, or Bad.
* **Select Multiple3** (Select Multiple): Select all that apply.
* **Date (2)** (Date): Select a relevant date.
* **Time (2)** (Time): Select your time of day.
* **Note (2)** (Note): Provide any additional comments or remarks.
* **Select One4** (Select One): Rate your experience with our offer. Choose from Very good, Good, or Bad.
* **Select Multiple4** (Select Multiple): Select all that apply.
* **Date (4)** (Date): Select a relevant date.
* **Time (4)** (Time): Select your time of day.
* **Note (4)** (Note): Provide any additional comments or remarks.
* **Email (2)** (Email): Enter your email address. This is optional.
