# Website Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Website Referral Form is designed to collect information from users who wish to refer others to a website. This form allows users to input their referral code, invitation code, name, and email address for follow-up communication.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the Referral Code field.
2. Enter your referral code, if provided.
3. Click on the Invitation Code field, if applicable.
4. Enter the invitation code, if provided.
5. Click on the Name field.
6. Enter your name as it appears on your identification or as you prefer to be addressed.
7. Click on the Email field.
8. Enter your email address in the format of "name@example.com".
9. Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Referral Code** (`Referral Code`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the referral code you were provided with.
* **Invitation Code** (`Invitation Code`, `text`, required/optional): If you were given an invitation code, enter it here.
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification or as you prefer to be addressed.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address in the format "name@example.com".

## Tips
* Make sure to enter the correct referral or invitation code, if applicable.
* Use your actual name, as it appears on your identification or as you prefer to be addressed.
* Double-check your email address for accuracy before submitting the form.
