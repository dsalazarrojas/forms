# Business Marketing New Member Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about your business and help us understand your marketing goals, challenges, and interests. The information collected will aid us in providing tailored support and services to help you grow your business.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your name, job title, and company details, including name, address, phone number, and website (if applicable).
2. Select your company's industry from the provided options.
3. Specify how many years your company has been in operation.
4. Choose the type of membership you are applying for based on your company's size.
5. Select your primary marketing goals and objectives.
6. Choose all the marketing channels you currently use.
7. Determine your approximate monthly marketing budget.
8. Identify your marketing challenges and areas of interest.
9. Indicate how you heard about us, if referred, and by whom.
10. Express your interest in networking and volunteering.
11. Select the committees you would like to join.
12. Provide any additional information about your business that you think would be helpful for us to know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name**: Your full name as the primary contact person.
	+ This field is required to ensure we address your communication correctly.
* **Job Title**: Your position in the company.
	+ This field helps us understand your role and responsibilities.
* **Company Name**: Your business's legal name.
	+ This field is required to verify your business identity.
* **Company Address**: Your business's full address.
	+ This field is required for our records and communication.
* **Company Phone**: Your business phone number.
	+ This field is required for contact and communication.
* **Company Website**: Your business's full website URL (if applicable).
	+ This field is optional, but it helps us understand your online presence.
* **Industry**: Select the category that best describes your business.
	+ This field helps us understand your business type and provide relevant support.
* **Years in Business**: How long your company has been in operation.
	+ This field helps us assess your business's maturity and experience.
* **Number of Employees**: Your company's total number of employees.
	+ This field helps us understand your business size and scale.
* **Membership Type**: Choose the type of membership that suits your business size.
	+ This field is required to determine your membership benefits and services.
* **Marketing Goals**: Select your primary marketing objectives.
	+ This field helps us understand your marketing strategy and goals.
* **Current Marketing Channels**: Select all the marketing channels you currently use.
	+ This field helps us understand your marketing efforts and provide relevant support.
* **Monthly Marketing Budget**: Approximate your monthly marketing spend.
	+ This field is optional, but it helps us understand your marketing resources.
* **Marketing Challenges**: Identify your marketing challenges and areas of interest.
	+ This field helps us understand your needs and provide tailored support.
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: Specify the source of your referral.
	+ This field helps us understand how you learned about us.
* **Referrer Name**: If you were referred, provide the referrer's name.
	+ This field is optional, but it helps us understand the referral context.
* **Networking Interest**: Indicate your interest in networking opportunities.
	+ This field helps us understand your networking preferences.
* **Volunteer Interest**: Express your interest in volunteering for the organization.
	+ This field is optional, but it helps us understand your commitment.
* **Committee Preferences**: Select committees you would like to join.
	+ This field helps us understand your interests and skills.
* **Additional Information**: Provide any other information you think would be helpful for us to know.
	+ This field is optional, but it helps us understand your business context.
