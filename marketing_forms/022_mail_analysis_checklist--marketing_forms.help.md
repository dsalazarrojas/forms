# Mail Analysis Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mail Analysis Checklist is a form designed to help users analyze and evaluate the effectiveness of mail campaigns or marketing efforts. It collects data on various aspects of the mail campaign, including mailing strategy, frequency, results, and target audience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the mailing process and strategy used in your mail campaign.
2. Answer questions about the mailing frequency and mail channel used.
3. Provide details about the mail list size and content.
4. Evaluate the mail results, including open rate, click rate, and conversion rate.
5. Check if any of the recipients opted out or unsubscribed from the mail campaign.
6. Assess the impact of the mail campaign on the target audience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Mailing Process (1) 
* Mailing Process (mailing_process, text, required): This field is used to briefly describe the mailing process used in the campaign.

### Mailing Strategy (2)
* Mailing Strategy (mailing_strategy, select_multiple, required): Select all the mailing strategies used in the campaign (e.g., 'Good' and 'Fair').

### Mail Frequency (3)
* Mail Frequency (mail_frequency, select_one, required): Choose whether the mail campaign was sent with a high frequency ('Yes') or not ('No').

### Mail Channel (4)
* Mail Channel (mail_channel, select_one, required): Select the channel through which the mail was sent ('Yes' or 'No').

### Mail List (5)
* Mail List (mail_list, text, required): Provide details about the size of the mail list.

### Mail Content (6)
* Mail Content (mail_content, text, required): Describe the content of the mail campaign.

### Mail Timing (7)
* Mail Timing (mail_timing, date, required): Enter the date and time when the mail was sent.

### Mail Results (8)
* Mail Results (mail_results, select_one, required): Evaluate the results of the mail campaign, whether 'Yes' or 'No'.

### Mail Budget (9)
* Mail Budget (mail_budget, text, required): Enter the budget allocated for the mail campaign.

### Mail Strategy Impact (10)
* Mail Strategy Impact (mail_strategy_impact, select_multiple, required): Select the impact of the mail strategy on the campaign ('Good' and 'Fair').

### Mail List Size (11)
* Mail List Size (mail_list_size, text, required): Provide details about the size of the mail list.

### Mail Subscribed (12)
* Mail Subscribed (mail_subscribed, select_one, required): Choose whether the mail campaign resulted in any recipient subscribing ('Yes' or 'No').

### Mail Open Rate (13)
* Mail Open Rate (mail_open_rate, select_one, required): Evaluate the open rate of the mail campaign, whether 'Good', 'Fair', or 'Unknown'.

### Mail Click Rate (14)
* Mail Click Rate (mail_click_rate, select_one, required): Evaluate the click rate of the mail campaign, whether 'Good', 'Fair', or 'Unknown'.

### Mail Conversion Rate (15)
* Mail Conversion Rate (mail_conversion_rate, select_one, required): Evaluate the conversion rate of the mail campaign, whether 'Good', 'Fair', or 'Unknown'.

### Mail Unsubscribe (16)
* Mail Unsubscribe (mail_unsubscribe, select_one, required): Choose whether any recipient unsubscribed from the mail campaign ('Yes' or 'No').

### Mail Unsubscribe Reason (17)
* Mail Unsubscribe Reason (mail_unsubscribe_reason, text, required): Provide details about the reason for unsubscribing from the mail campaign.

### Mail Opt-out (18)
* Mail Opt-out (mail_opt_out, select_one, required): Choose whether any recipient opted out of the mail campaign ('Yes' or 'No').

### Mail Opt-out Reason (19)
* Mail Opt-out Reason (mail_opt_out_reason, text, required): Provide details about the reason for opting out of the mail campaign.

### Mail Bounce (20)
* Mail Bounce (mail_bounce, select_one, required): Choose whether any mail bounced back ('Yes' or 'No').

### Mail Goals (21)
* Mail Goals (mail_goals, select_multiple, required): Select the goals of the mail campaign ('Yes' and 'No').

### Mail Target (22)
* Mail Target (mail_target, select_one, required): Choose whether the mail campaign targeted a specific audience ('Yes' or 'No').

### Mail Target Age (23)
* Mail Target Age (mail_target_age, select_one, required): Choose whether the mail campaign targeted recipients of different ages ('Yes' or 'No').

### Mail Target Gender (24)
* Mail Target Gender (mail_target_gender, select_one, required): Choose whether the mail campaign targeted recipients of different genders ('Yes' or 'No').

### Mail Target Location (25)
* Mail Target Location (mail_target_location, select_one, required): Choose whether the mail campaign targeted recipients from different locations ('Yes' or 'No').

### Mail Open Rate Impact (26)
* Mail Open Rate Impact (mail_open_rate_impact, select_one, required): Evaluate the impact of the mail campaign on the open rate ('Good' or 'Fair').
