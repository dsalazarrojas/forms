# Social Media Profile Enhancement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Social Media Profile Enhancement" form is designed to collect information about social media profiles for clients or agencies. This form is meant to ensure accurate and consistent representation of the client's online presence across different platforms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the "Client Details" section with the relevant information about the client.
2. In the "Goals" section, describe the objectives and objectives of the social media campaign.
3. In the "Branding Preferences" section, specify the branding guidelines and preferences for the social media profile.
4. In the "Contact Information" section, provide your contact information, including phone number and email.
5. For the "Social Media Links" section, enter the links to the client's or agency's social media profiles on Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, and YouTube.
6. In the "Agency" section, indicate whether the profile is managed by an agency, and if so, provide the agency's name, website, phone number, and email.
7. In the "Additional Info" section, provide any additional information that you think is relevant to the social media profile.
