# Tap Fundamentals Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect registration information from students or customers for the Tap Fundamentals course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in your First Name
* Fill in your Last Name
* Enter your Email
* Enter your Phone number
* Select the Start Date of your course
* Choose the type of course you are registered for (Tap Fundamentals, Tap Fundamentals 2, or Tap Fundamentals 3)
* Enter the Number of Students you are registering
* Enter the Number of Sessions you are attending
* Select the length of your course (1, 2, 3, 4, or 5 sessions)
* If your course length is not one of the options above, please enter the number of sessions in the "Other (number of sessions)" field
* Add any additional Notes about your registration
* Add any Comments about your registration
* Choose who you are registered to (chatjimmy, chatjoe, or chatjim)
* Optionally, enter a unique Form ID (not required but recommended for tracking purposes)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Your last name.
* **Email** (text, required): A valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (text, required): Your phone number.
* **Start Date** (date, required): The date you plan to start the course.
* **Course Type** (select_one, required): The type of course you are registering for (Tap Fundamentals, Tap Fundamentals 2, or Tap Fundamentals 3).
* **Number of Students** (number, required): The number of students you are registering.
* **Number of Sessions** (number, required): The number of sessions you are attending.
* **Course Length** (select_multiple, required): The length of your course (1, 2, 3, 4, or 5 sessions).
* **Other (number of sessions)** (text, optional): If your course length is not one of the options above, enter the number of sessions here.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Additional comments or notes about your registration.
* **Comments** (note, optional): Additional comments about your registration.
* **Assigned To** (select_one, required): Who you are registered to (chatjimmy, chatjoe, or chatjim).
* **Form ID** (text, optional): A unique ID for your registration (not required but recommended for tracking purposes).
