# Customer Retention Post Campaign Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on the effectiveness of marketing campaigns in retaining customers. Sales teams will fill out this form for each customer to provide feedback on their retention status and the reasons behind it.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Fill out the form for each customer who participated in the campaign.
*   Answer all applicable questions, providing as much detail as possible.
*   Make sure to save the completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name (1)
*   **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required: false): Enter the customer's first name.

### Last Name (2)
*   **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Enter the customer's last name.

### Email (3)
*   **Email** (`3`, `email`, required: false): Enter the customer's email address.

### How was the customer retained? (4)
*   **How was the customer retained?** (`4`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose whether the customer was retained (`Yes`) or not retained (`No`).

### Why were they retained or not? (5)
*   **Why were they retained or not?** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief explanation for why the customer was retained or not.

### Date of outcome (6)
*   **Date of outcome** (`6`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the customer's outcome.

### Time of outcome (7)
*   **Time of outcome** (`7`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the customer's outcome.

### Note about outcome (8)
*   **Note about outcome** (`8`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about the customer's outcome.

### Campaign Outcome 1 (9)
*   **Campaign Outcome 1** (`9`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose whether Campaign Outcome 1 was True (`True`), False (`False`), or Undecided.

### Campaign Outcome 2 (10)
*   **Campaign Outcome 2** (`10`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose whether Campaign Outcome 2 was True (`True`), False (`False`), or Undecided.

### Campaign Outcome 3 (11)
*   **Campaign Outcome 3** (`11`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all applicable outcomes from Campaign Outcome 3 (e.g., True (`True`), False (`False`), Undecided).

### How many periods do you compare? (12)

### Second Period to Compare (13)
*   **Second Period to Compare** (`13`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose Yes (`Yes`) or No (`No`).

### Performance Metric (14)
*   **Performance Metric** (`14`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose Yes (`Yes`) or No (`No`).

### Second Period Metric (15)
*   **Second Period Metric** (`15`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose Yes (`Yes`) or No (`No`).

### Value of second period (16)
*   **Value of second period** (`16`, `number`, required: false): Enter the value of the second period.

### Value of comparison period (17)
*   **Value of comparison period** (`17`, `number`, required: false): Enter the value of the comparison period.

### Change in Performance (18)
*   **Change in Performance** (`18`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose Yes (`Yes`) or No (`No`).

### Value of Change in Performance (19)
*   **Value of Change in Performance** (`19`, `number`, required: false): Enter the value of the change in performance.

### Share with Stakeholders? (20)
*   **Share with Stakeholders?** (`20`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose Yes (`Yes`) or No (`No`).

### Why or why not? (21)

### Time to Share (23)
