# Reel Details Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reel Details Submission Form is designed to collect information related to marketing campaigns, forms, or other relevant marketing-related topics. This form will help us better understand the context and details of the marketing efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Title** field with a brief description of the form or campaign.
2. Enter a detailed **Description** of the form or campaign.
3. Select the **Category** of the form, which is usually "Marketing Forms" for marketing-related topics.
4. Add relevant **Tags** to help us quickly identify the form's purpose.
5. Specify the **Sub Category** of the form, if applicable.
6. Identify the **Target Audience** for the form.
7. Upload any relevant **Form File** (e.g., image, document).
8. Provide your **Submitter** information (name, email, etc.).
9. Finally, specify when you **Submitted** the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reel Details (1)**: Enter a brief description of the form or campaign.
* **Title (2)**: Enter a short title that briefly describes the form or campaign.
* **Description (3)**: Enter a detailed description of the form or campaign.
* **Category (4)**: Select "Marketing Forms" as the category for marketing-related topics.
* **Tags (5)**: Add relevant keywords or tags to quickly identify the form's purpose.
* **Sub Category (6)**: Specify a sub category if applicable (e.g., marketing campaign, survey, etc.).
* **Target Audience (7)**: Identify the target audience for the form.
* **Form File (8)**: Upload an image, document, or any other relevant file related to the form.
* **Submitter (9)**: Provide your name and contact information.
* **Submit Time (10)**: Specify when you submitted the form.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately to provide valuable insights.
* If a field is not applicable, leave it blank.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form to ensure that the information is relevant and correct.
