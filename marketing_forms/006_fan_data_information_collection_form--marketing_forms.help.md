# Fan Data Information Collection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from fans about their personal details, interests, and preferences. The form is used to gather insights and data that will help us better understand our audience and improve our marketing efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address in the first three fields.
2. Select your Age group, and if applicable, choose "Other" and enter your actual age.
3. If you have an event or concert ticket, enter the Event Name in the next field.
4. Enter your Date of Birth and Time of Birth in the next two fields.
5. Answer "Select One" by choosing "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu.
6. Choose multiple options for "Select Multiple" by selecting the relevant answers from the dropdown menu.
7. Fill in your Address, Note, and Fan Interest in the next three fields.
8. Enter your Favorite Artist's name in the next field.
9. Describe how you found us in the "How Did You Find Us" field.
10. Enter any additional Follow Up Information if you'd like to be contacted.
11. If you have a Favorite Band, enter its name.
12. Enter your Social Media handle and URL (if applicable).
13. Select the type of Phone you have.
14. Enter your Email type (e.g., Gmail, Yahoo, etc.).
15. Enter the type of Website you have (e.g., WordPress, Wix, etc.).
16. Select the type of Social Media you have (e.g., Facebook, Instagram, etc.).
17. Enter the type of Social Media URL you have (e.g., http, https, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address that you use to receive communications.
* **Age**: Select your age group or enter your actual age if "Other" is selected.
* **Event Name**: If you have an event or concert ticket, enter the name of the event or concert.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Birth**: Enter your time of birth in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Select One**: Answer "Yes" or "No" to the question.
* **Select Multiple**: Choose multiple options from the dropdown menu.
* **Address**: Enter your home or office address.
* **Note**: Enter any additional information about yourself.
* **Fan Interest**: Enter your interest or hobby related to music or the music industry.
* **Favorite Artist**: Enter the name of your favorite artist or band.
* **How Did You Find Us**: Describe how you discovered our website, social media, or event.
* **Follow Up Information**: Enter any additional information for follow-up purposes.
* **Favorite Band**: Enter the name of your favorite band or artist.
* **Social Media**: Enter your social media handle (e.g., @username).
* **Social Media URL**: Enter the URL of your social media profile (e.g., http://example.com).
* **Phone Type**: Select the type of phone you have (e.g., iPhone, Android, etc.).
* **Email Type**: Enter the type of email service you use (e.g., Gmail, Yahoo, etc.).
* **Website Type**: Enter the type of website you have (e.g., WordPress, Wix, etc.).
* **Social Media Type**: Select the type of social media you use (e.g., Facebook, Instagram, etc.).
* **Social Media Url Type**: Select the type of Social Media URL you have (e.g., http, https, etc.).
