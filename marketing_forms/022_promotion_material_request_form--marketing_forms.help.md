# Promotion Material Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for promotion material requests from customers. It helps to ensure that all relevant details are captured for the request, including customer information, event details, and specific requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the required fields (marked with *) with accurate information.
2.  Select the relevant options for the type of material requested and the purpose of the event.
3.  Upload any example designs that may be helpful for the design process.
4.  Add any additional information that may be relevant for the design team.
5.  Fill in the assigned fields if you are the internal contact assigned to this request or have been given access to the assigned tool/project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (1, text, *): Please enter the customer's name.
* **Email Address** (2, email, *): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone Number** (3, text, *): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Type of Material Requested** (4, select_one): Select the type of material requested from the options.
*   • Brochures, Flyers, and Handouts
   • Posters and Banners
   • Other (please specify)
* **Event Purpose** (5, select_one): Select the purpose of the event from the options.
*   • Promotional Event
   • Sales Event
   • Product Launch
   • Other (please specify)
* **Event Date** (6, date, *): Enter the date of the event.
* **Example Designs (if any)** (7, text): If you have any relevant designs, please attach them here.
* **Additional Information** (8, text, *): Add any relevant information about the event or material requirements.
* **Internal contact assigned to this request** (9, text): If you are the internal contact assigned to this request, enter your name here.
* **Output File** (10, text): If you need a specific output file for this form, enter it here.
* **Internal contact tool/project** (11, text): If you are given access to a specific tool/project, enter it here.
* **Internal contact project** (12, text): If you are assigned to a specific project, enter it here.

## Tips
Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked with *) to ensure that the design team has all the necessary information to create the promotion material. If you are not sure about any field, please reach out to your internal contact for clarification.
