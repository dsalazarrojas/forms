# Phone Case Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Phone Case Survey Form is designed to gather information from users about their preferences and habits related to phone cases. This form will help us understand how important phone cases are to users, what types of cases they prefer, and what features they would like to see in a custom phone case.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the options that best describe your preferences and habits related to phone cases. You will be asked questions about your current use of phone cases, the features you prefer, and your willingness to pay for a customized phone case.
