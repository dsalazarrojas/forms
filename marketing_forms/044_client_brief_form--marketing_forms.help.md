# Client Brief Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Brief Form is a crucial document that helps us define the scope and vision for your next project. Please fill in this form to provide us with essential information about your project, and we will use it to guide the project's development.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please ensure that you have all the necessary information before filling out the form.
2. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as such in the form.
3. Use the provided hints as a guide to fill in the form accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company/Organization Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required):
	+ This field is where you enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Primary Project Contact** (`project_contact`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter the name and title of the person responsible for the project.
* **Contact Email** (`email_address`, `email`, required):
	+ Enter your email address where we can reach you for further inquiries.
* **Contact Phone** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Proposed Project Name** (`project_name`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter a suggested name for your project.
* **Brief Description of the Project** (`project_description`, `text`, required):
	+ Describe the project, its goals, and objectives.
* **Primary Target Audience** (`target_audience`, `text`, required):
	+ Identify the primary target audience for your project.
* **Category of Work** (`project_type`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Select from the available categories to determine the scope of the project.
* **Success Criteria (KPIs)** (`goals_and_kpis`, `text`, required):
	+ Define the key performance indicators that will measure the project's success.
* **Hard Deadline (if any)** (`project_deadline`, `date`, optional):
	+ If your project has a strict deadline, enter the date here.
* **Allocated Budget ($)** (`estimated_budget`, `number`, required):
	+ Enter the budget allocated for your project.
* **Inspiration or Reference Links** (`reference_links`, `text`, optional):
	+ Share any links that inspired your project or serve as references.
* **Any specific technical or legal requirements?** (`additional_requirements`, `text`, optional):
	+ Mention any special requirements, such as ADA compliance or specific CMS, that we should be aware of.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, optional):
	+ If you'd like to track when you submitted the form, enter the date here.

Note: The form is organized into sections for easier navigation and clarity. Please review each section carefully and fill in the required fields accurately to ensure a smooth project execution.
