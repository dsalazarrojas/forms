# Customer Advocacy Program Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Customer Advocacy Program Application Form is designed to gather information from customers about their advocacy program application. This form will help us to identify potential advocates and tailor our program accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, email, and choose your current company from the select options.
2. Provide your job title in the text field.
3. Select how long you have been at your company from the select options.
4. Select your current industry from the select options.
5. Choose your current role from the select options.
6. Select your top priorities from the select options.
7. (Optional) Choose how you heard about us from the select options.
8. Submit your application for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name?** (`customer_advocacy_program_application_form_1`, text, required/optional): This is a text field where you can enter your name. It is not required, but it is helpful for us to know your name.
* **Email** (`customer_advocacy_program_application_form_2`, email, required/true): This is a field where you can enter your email address. This field is required, as we will use it to contact you.
* **Phone** (`customer_advocacy_program_application_form_3`, text, required/false): This is a text field where you can enter your phone number. It is not required, but it is useful for us to contact you.
* **What is your company?** (`customer_advocacy_program_application_form_4`, select_one, required/true): Select your current company from the available options.
* **What is your job title?** (`customer_advocacy_program_application_form_5`, text, required/false): This is a text field where you can enter your job title.
* **How long have you been at your company?** (`customer_advocacy_program_application_form_6`, select_multiple, required/true): Select how long you have been at your company from the available options.
* **What industry do you work in?** (`customer_advocacy_program_application_form_7`, select_one, required/true): Select your current industry from the available options.
* **What is your current role?** (`customer_advocacy_program_application_form_8`, select_multiple, required/true): Select your current role from the available options.
* **What are your biggest priorities?** (`customer_advocacy_program_application_form_9`, select_multiple, required/true): Select your top priorities from the available options.
* **How did you hear about us?** (`customer_advocacy_program_application_form_10`, select_multiple, required/false): (Optional) This is a field where you can choose how you heard about us.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
- We will review your application and contact you for further discussion.
