# # Business Profile Attributes Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on your client's business profile, including their contact information, industry type, size, revenue, target market, key products or services, unique selling proposition, primary sales channels, digital presence, social media activity, marketing budget, main challenges, growth plans, preferred contact method, and the date the survey was completed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Use the provided options to select the most suitable answers for your client's business profile.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of your client or business contact.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the client's company.
* **Type of Business** (`business_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of business your client operates in (e.g., Retail, Services, Manufacturing, Technology, Healthcare, Finance, Education, Other).
* **Industry Sector** (`industry_sector`, `text`, optional): Enter the industry sector your client operates in.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, `select_one`, required): Select the number of employees in your client's company.
* **Annual Revenue Range** (`annual_revenue`, `select_one`, optional): Select the approximate annual revenue of your client's business.
* **Years in Business** (`years_in_business`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of years your client's business has been operating.
* **Primary Target Market** (`primary_target_market`, `text`, required): Enter the main customers your client caters to.
* **Geographic Presence** (`geographic_presence`, `text`, optional): Enter the regions or territories where your client operates.
* **Key Products or Services** (`key_products_services`, `text`, required): Enter the main offerings provided by your client's business.
* **Unique Selling Proposition** (`unique_selling_proposition`, `text`, optional): Enter what makes your client's business stand out.
* **Primary Sales Channels** (`primary_channels`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the primary channels through which your client sells their products or services (e.g., Direct Sales, Retail Stores, Online E-commerce, Wholesale, Partnerships, Other).
* **Digital Presence** (`digital_presence`, `select_one`, optional): Select the level of your client's digital presence (e.g., Strong, Moderate, Minimal, None).
* **Social Media Active Platforms** (`social_media_active`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the social media platforms your client uses (e.g., Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter, TikTok, YouTube, Other).
* **Marketing Budget** (`marketing_budget`, `select_one`, optional): Select the approximate annual marketing spend of your client's business.
* **Main Challenges** (`main_challenges`, `text`, optional): Enter the primary challenges your client faces.
* **Growth Plans** (`growth_plans`, `text`, optional): Enter your client's growth objectives.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`contact_preference`, `select_one`, optional): Select the preferred contact method for your client (e.g., Email, Phone, In-person, Video Call, No Contact).
* **Survey Completion Date** (`survey_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date this survey was completed.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
- Use the provided options to select the most suitable answers for your client's business profile.
- If a field is not applicable to your client's business, you can leave it blank, but make sure to note this in the corresponding field.
- If you're unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
