# Claim Your Holiday Coupon - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture information from customers to offer a holiday coupon or promotion tailored to their preferences and behavior.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Are You a New or Returning Customer, Select Your Holiday Coupon, Shopping Frequency, Do You Accept the Terms and Conditions, Yes, I accept, Join Our Newsletter for More Deals, Yes, sign me up, and Receive Text Message Alerts, Yes, opt me in.
2. Provide optional fields: Phone Number, Preferred Product Category, Mailing Address, City, State, ZIP Code, Is This a Gift Purchase, and Special Requests or Notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name for us to address you.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name for us to address you.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter the email address where we will send your coupon.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for order confirmations (if you'd like).
* **Are You a New or Returning Customer** (`customer_type`, select_one, required): Select whether you're a new customer or have shopped with us before.
* **Select Your Holiday Coupon** (`coupon_preference`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred holiday coupon offer from the options listed.
* **Preferred Product Category** (`preferred_category`, select_one, optional): Select the category of products you usually shop for.
* **Shopping Frequency** (`shopping_frequency`, select_one, required): Indicate how often you shop with us.
* **How Did You Hear About This Offer** (`referral_source`, select_multiple, optional): Select all ways you heard about this offer (if applicable).
* **What Is Your Birthday Month** (`birthday_month`, select_one, optional): Enter your birthday month for special birthday offers (if you'd like).
* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, text, optional): Provide your mailing address if you require a physical coupon.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter your city.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter your state.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, text, optional): Enter your postal code.
* **Do You Accept the Terms and Conditions** (`terms_acceptance`, select_one, required): Agree to the coupon terms and conditions.
* **Join Our Newsletter for More Deals** (`newsletter_signup`, select_one, required): Sign up for our newsletter for more exclusive offers.
* **Receive Text Message Alerts** (`sms_opt_in`, select_one, required): Opt-in for text message alerts.
* **Is This a Gift Purchase** (`gift_option`, select_one, optional): Confirm if this is a gift purchase for someone else.
* **Special Requests or Notes** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Enter any special requests or notes.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your email address for accuracy.
* Consider joining our newsletter for more exclusive offers.
