# rebranding_poster_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for rebranding posters, including user input, poster specifications, and payment details. It's intended for marketing or design teams involved in the rebranding process.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "user_input" field with your relevant details.
2. Provide "poster_specs" to ensure that the poster meets your requirements.
3. Enter your "branding_assets" information, such as logos or other visual materials.
4. Enter the "payment_amount" in the desired currency.
5. Select your preferred "payment_method" from the provided options.
6. Enter your "billing_address" for payment processing purposes.
7. Select any applicable "payment_terms" from the provided options.
8. Enter any additional "notes" or comments.
9. Enter your "email" for contact purposes.
10. Provide your "phone" number for contact purposes.
11. Upload any relevant "file_1" to "file_10" as needed.
12. Enter any "user_message" you'd like to include.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **user_input** (text, required): Enter your relevant details for the rebranding poster.
- **poster_specs** (text, required): Provide specifications for the poster, including size, material, and design requirements.
- **branding_assets** (text, required): Enter your branding assets, such as logos or other visual materials.
- **payment_amount** (number, required): Enter the payment amount in the desired currency.
- **payment_method** (select_one, required): Select your preferred payment method from the options provided.
- **billing_address** (text, required): Enter your billing address for payment processing purposes.
- **payment_terms** (select_multiple, required): Select any applicable payment terms from the options provided.
- **notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
- **email** (email, required): Enter your email for contact purposes.
- **phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **file_1** (text, optional): Upload any relevant file 1.
- **file_2** (text, optional): Upload any relevant file 2.
- **file_3** (text, optional): Upload any relevant file 3.
- **file_4** (text, optional): Upload any relevant file 4.
- **file_5** (text, optional): Upload any relevant file 5.
- **file_6** (text, optional): Upload any relevant file 6.
- **file_7** (text, optional): Upload any relevant file 7.
- **file_8** (text, optional): Upload any relevant file 8.

- **file_10** (text, optional): Upload any relevant file 10.
- **user_message** (text, optional): Enter any additional message you'd like to include.
