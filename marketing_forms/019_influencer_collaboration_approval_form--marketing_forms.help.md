# Influencer Collaboration Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Influencer Collaboration Approval Form is used to collect and store information about potential collaborators for marketing purposes. This form is filled out by team members who review and approve collaboration requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Provide your Email in the Email field.
4. Enter your Phone Number in the Phone Number field.
5. Select your Role from the options provided in the Role field.
6. Choose the Category of collaboration from the options in the Category field.
7. Enter your years of experience in the Years in Business field.
8. Select the type of collaboration you are requesting in the Collaboration Type field.
9. Choose the type of content you will be creating in the Content Type field.
10. If applicable, upload a file to support your collaboration request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it is used on your business card or social media profile.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it is used on your business card or social media profile.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your professional email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Select the type of collaboration you are seeking:
	+ Content Creator
	+ Brand Ambassador
	+ Influencer
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, required): Choose the category of collaboration:
	+ Marketing
	+ Social Media
	+ Other
* **Experience** (`experience`, number, optional): Enter the number of years you have been in business.
* **Years in Business** (`years_in_business`, number, optional): Enter the number of years your company has been in business.
* **Collaboration Type** (`collaboration_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of collaboration you are requesting:
	+ Sponsored Content
	+ Product Placement
	+ Event Partnership
* **Content Type** (`content_type`, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of content you will be creating:
	+ Video
	+ Image/Photo
	+ Live Stream
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, date, optional): Date the collaboration request was approved.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the collaboration.
* **File** (`file_uploader`, text, optional): Upload any supporting files or documents for your collaboration request.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure you have the necessary permissions to upload files.
- If you are unsure about the category or collaboration type, please consult with a team member before submitting the form.
