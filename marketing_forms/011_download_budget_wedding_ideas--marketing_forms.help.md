# Download Budget Wedding Ideas - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from potential wedding clients to help vendors or event planners understand their budget and planning preferences for a special event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the form title, which should be a brief description of the event.
2. Select the budget category that best describes the type of event you are planning (e.g., Marketing Forms, Wedding Forms).
3. Enter a detailed description of the event in the Event Description field.
4. Enter your contact email and phone number so that we can reach out to you with any further questions or proposals.
5. Indicate whether you would like to be followed up on after we have received your form submission.
6. If you have any special requests or requirements for the event, enter them in the Special Requests field.
7. Choose a venue type that best suits your event (e.g., Church, Outdoor, Restaurant).
8. Estimate your budget for the event.
9. Enter the number of guests you expect to attend.
10. Choose a dress code for your event (e.g., Formal, Casual, No Dress Code).
11. Estimate the number of guests you expect to attend.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of your event. This will help us understand the context of your planning.
* **Budget Category** (`form_budget_category`, select, required: false): Choose a category that best describes the type of event you are planning. This will help us narrow down the options for your event.
* **Event Description** (`form_description`, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of your event, including any special requirements or requests.
* **Contact Email** (`form_contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so that we can reach out to you with any questions or proposals.
* **Contact Phone** (`form_contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you with any questions or proposals.
* **Planning Info - Decorations** (`form_planning_info_decorations`, text, required: false): If you have any specific decoration requirements for your event, enter them here.
* **Planning Info - Music** (`form_planning_info_music`, text, required: false): If you have any specific music requirements for your event, enter them here.
* **Planning Info - Food & Beverages** (`form_planning_info_food_beverages`, text, required: false): If you have any specific food and beverage requirements for your event, enter them here.
* **Follow-up** (`form_follow_up`, select, required: false): Indicate whether you would like to be followed up on after we have received your form submission.
* **Special Requests** (`form_special_requests`, text, required: false): If you have any special requests or requirements for your event, enter them here.
* **Venue** (`form_venue`, select, required: false): Choose a venue type that best suits your event (e.g., Church, Outdoor, Restaurant).
* **Budget Estimate** (`form_budget_estimate`, number, required: false): Estimate your budget for the event.
* **Guests** (`form_guests`, number, required: false): Enter the number of guests you expect to attend.
* **Dress Code** (`form_dress_code`, select, required: false): Choose a dress code for your event (e.g., Formal, Casual, No Dress Code).
* **Estimated Guest Count** (`form_guest_count`, number, required: false): Re-enter the number of guests you expect to attend.

Note: This guide is based on the form's internal structure and field labels. The explanations and labels were kept to the best effort to provide clarity while avoiding unsupported business logic.
