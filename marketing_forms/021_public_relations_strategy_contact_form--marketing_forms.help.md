# public_relations_strategy_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and details about the company's public relations strategy. The information collected will help us to provide you with the most relevant and up-to-date information about our public relations services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the 'Company' field with the name of your company.
2. Enter the 'Campaign Name' if you are interested in a specific campaign.
3. Select the campaign duration from the options provided.
4. Choose the type of campaign you are interested in from the options provided.
5. In the 'Message' field, provide a brief description of your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company**: Enter the name of your company.
* **Campaign Name**: Enter the name of the campaign you are interested in.
* **Campaign Duration**: Select the duration of the campaign you are interested in from the options provided.
	+ Less than 1 week
	+ 1-2 weeks
	+ 2-4 weeks
	+ More than 4 weeks
* **Campaign Type**: Choose the type of campaign you are interested in from the options provided.
	+ PR
	+ Event
	+ Product
	+ Partnership
	+ Other
* **Campaign Goals**: Enter a brief description of your campaign goals.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Message**: Provide a brief description of your inquiry.

## Tips
* Be accurate and up-to-date with the information you provide.
* Make sure to provide clear and concise information in the 'Message' field.
* Our team will review your inquiry and respond with the most relevant information.
