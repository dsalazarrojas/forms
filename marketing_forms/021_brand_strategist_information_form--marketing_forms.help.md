# Brand Strategist Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential clients who are looking to work with a brand strategist to develop their brand's identity and strategy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide your company's name and website (if applicable).
3. Select your industry and company size.
4. Describe your main business challenge and what you hope to achieve with branding.
5. Outline your target audience and main competitors.
6. List your unique differentiators and core brand values.
7. Choose the brand personality traits that best describe your brand.
8. Indicate if you have previous experience with brand strategists.
9. Select your desired project timeline and budget range.
10. Finally, provide any additional notes or comments about your project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Full Name (1)**: Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address (2)**: Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number (3)**: Enter your best contact phone number.
* **Company Name (4)**: Enter your company's name.
* **Company Website (5)**: Enter your company's website URL (if applicable).
* **Industry (6)**: Select the industry your company operates in.
* **Company Size (7)**: Choose the number of employees in your company.
* **Annual Revenue Range (8)**: Select your approximate annual revenue range.
* **Current Branding Status (9)**: Indicate your current branding status.
* **Main Business Challenge (10)**: Describe your biggest challenge right now.
* **Brand Goals (11)**: Describe what you hope to achieve with branding.
* **Target Audience (12)**: Describe your ideal customer.
* **Main Competitors (13)**: List your top 3 competitors.
* **Unique Differentiator (14)**: Describe what makes you different from competitors.
* **Core Brand Values (15)**: List 3-5 core values of your brand.
* **Desired Brand Personality (16)**: Choose the personality traits that best describe your brand.
* **Previous Brand Work (17)**: Indicate if you have worked with a brand strategist before.
* **Project Timeline (18)**: Select when you'd like to start the project.
* **Budget Range (19)**: Choose your estimated budget for branding.
* **How Did You Hear About Us (20)**: Indicate where you found us.
* **Additional Notes (21)**: Provide any additional comments or information about your project.
