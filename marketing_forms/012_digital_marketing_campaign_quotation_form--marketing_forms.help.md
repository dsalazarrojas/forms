# Digital Marketing Campaign Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from clients for a digital marketing campaign quotation. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with the required and optional information as specified.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a precise quotation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Data**: A text area for any additional information you would like to share about the project.
  * This field is for general information and can be left blank if no additional details are needed.
- **Client Name**: The name of the client company or individual.
  * Please enter the name of the client as it appears on their business card or website.
- **Email**: The email address of the client.
  * Please ensure that this is a valid email address where we can contact the client.
- **Project Manager**: The name and title of the project manager responsible for the project.
  * If the project has a designated project manager, please enter their name and title.
- **Phone**: The phone number of the client.
  * Please enter the phone number where the client can be contacted.
- **Services**: Select all the services you would like to have included in the quotation.
  * Choose all the services that apply to your project needs.
- **Budget**: The budget allocated for the project.
  * Please enter the exact budget for the project, or provide a range if applicable.
- **Submission Date**: The date you would like to submit the project for quotation.
  * Choose the date you would like the quotation to be completed and returned.
- **Submission Time**: The time you would like the quotation to be submitted.
  * Choose the time of day you would like the quotation to be submitted.
- **File Uploads**: This is an optional field to upload any attachments or files related to the project.
  * You can upload any relevant files or documents to support your project needs.
- **Additional Comments**: A text area for any additional comments or instructions you would like to provide.
  * This field is for any additional information or instructions that may be relevant to the project.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
- We will contact you with any further questions or clarification needed for the quotation.
