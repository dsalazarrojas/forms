# website_marketing_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a website's marketing efforts, which will be used for analysis and comparison with other websites.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the provided text fields.
2. Select the relevant options for the questions that have a multiple-choice response (e.g. target_audience, conversion_rates, website_performance, website_marketing_activities).
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by a asterisk \*), as they are necessary for the form's data analysis.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### What are your website marketing objectives
* **What are your website marketing objectives** (text, required): Describe your website's marketing objectives, such as increasing traffic, generating leads, or improving brand awareness.

### Target Audience
* **Target Audience** (select multiple, required): Identify the groups you are targeting with your marketing efforts (e.g. 'Yes', 'No').

### Website Traffic
* **Website Traffic** (number, required): Enter the current volume of traffic on your website.

### Conversion Rates
* **Conversion Rates** (select one, optional): Rate your conversion rates as Good or Fair.

### Website Content
* **Website Content** (text, optional): Describe the content you plan to create or are currently using for marketing (e.g. blog posts, videos, etc.).

### Marketing Channels
* **Marketing Channels** (text, optional): Describe the marketing channels you use (e.g. social media, email marketing).

### Website Performance
* **Website Performance** (number, required): Enter a number to rate the performance of your website.

### Marketing Budget
* **Marketing Budget** (text, optional): Report the budget allocated for marketing efforts.

### Marketing Strategy
* **Marketing Strategy** (text, optional): Describe your current marketing strategy.

### Website Marketing Activities
* **Website Marketing Activities** (select multiple, optional): Identify the activities you are using for marketing (e.g. 'Yes', 'No').

## Tips
- Make sure to be as specific as possible when answering each question.
- If unsure about any of the questions, consult with your marketing team or department.
- The more detailed information you provide, the better the analysis will be.
