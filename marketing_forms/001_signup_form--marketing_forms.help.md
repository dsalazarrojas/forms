# Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to sign up for a service. To use this form, you will be asked to provide your email address, name, and agree to terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a valid email address in the Email field.
2. Enter your name in the Name field.
3. Enter a password in the Password field.
4. Select Agree from the dropdown menu, acknowledging that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter a valid email address that you use to receive communication from us.
* **Name** (`text`, required): Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed on our website.
* **Password** (`text`, required): Enter a strong password to secure your account. Please make sure it is at least 8 characters long.
* **Agree** (`select_one`, required): Select Yes to acknowledge that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions of this service.
