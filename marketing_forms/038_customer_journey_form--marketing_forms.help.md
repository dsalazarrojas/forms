# customer_journey_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The customer journey form is designed to collect feedback about your experience with our business. This form helps us understand how you found us, navigated our process, and felt about your experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all that apply for "How did you find us" to answer how you initially found out about our business.
2. Choose all that apply for "How did you navigate the process" to answer how you moved through our business process.
3. Select one that best describes how you felt about your experience with our business.
4. Choose one for "Where did you find our business" to answer where you first discovered our business.
5. Type in your response for "How did you navigate our online business" to answer how you interacted with our online presence.
6. Select all that apply for "How did you navigate our physical business location" to answer how you interacted with our physical presence.
7. Select "Yes" or "No" for "How did we get in contact with you" to answer how you initially got in touch with our business.
8. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Have you visited us before" to answer if you have visited our business location before.
9. Select all that apply for "What type of customer are you" to answer your categorization as a customer.
10. Enter your age in the corresponding field to answer your age.
11. Select all that apply for "How did you contact us" to answer how you interacted with our business for support.
12. Rate your customer journey on a scale of 0-10 to give us a numerical score.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How did you find us** (Select multiple options):
	+ This field is asking how you initially found out about our business.
* **How did you navigate the process** (Select multiple options):
	+ This field is asking how you moved through our business process.
* **How did you feel about the experience** (Select one option):
	+ This field is asking about your feelings and emotions towards your experience with our business.
* **Where did you find our business** (Select one option):
	+ This field is asking where you first discovered our business.
* **How did you navigate our online business** (Text field):
	+ This field is asking about your interactions with our online presence, type in your response and we'll get back to you.
* **How did you navigate our physical business location** (Select multiple options):
	+ This field is asking how you interacted with our physical presence.
* **How did we get in contact with you** (Select one option):
	+ This field is asking how you initially got in touch with our business.
* **Have you visited us before** (Select one option):
	+ This field is asking if you have visited our business location before.
* **What type of customer are you** (Select multiple options):
	+ This field is asking about your categorization as a customer.
* **What is your age** (Number field):
	+ This field is asking for your age.
* **How did you contact us** (Select multiple options):
	+ This field is asking about how you interacted with our business for support.
* **How would you rate your customer journey** (Number field):
	+ This field is asking for a numerical score of your experience.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly to provide valuable feedback.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please don't hesitate to type them in the "How did you navigate our online business" field.
* Make sure to rate your customer journey on a scale of 0-10, where 0 is the worst and 10 is the best.
* If you are unsure about any question, please don't worry and answer with your best effort.
