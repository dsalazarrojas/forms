# Website Design Gift Card Claim - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Website Design Gift Card Claim form is used to allow clients to claim a gift card related to a website design project. Please ensure you are eligible to claim the gift card before filling out this form.

<thinking>

To prevent duplicate claims and ensure accuracy, we check for internal database records to confirm that the code provided is valid. If the code has been already claimed or is not valid, the form will not be processed.

If you are a client who has been assigned a website design gift card, please complete the form below with the required information to successfully claim your gift card.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Client Info` section with your company's details.
2. Enter a valid `Gift Code` from your email.
3. Provide a brief description of your project details.
4. Select a `Project Goal` that aligns with the project's objectives.
5. Enter your `Email` and `Phone` contact information.
6. In the `Verify Gift Code` section, select the corresponding gift code from the list.
7. Finally, submit your claim by selecting the `Submit` button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Info** (`client_info`, text, optional): Enter your company's name and contact information.
* **Gift Code** (`gift_code`, text, optional): Enter a valid code from your email.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your project's objectives and scope.
* **Project Goal** (`goal`, text, optional): Select a project goal that aligns with the project's objectives.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Enter a short description of your project.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your company's phone number.
* **Verify Gift Code** (`gift_code_confirmation`, select_multiple, optional): Select the corresponding gift code from the list.
* **Submit** (no label, select_one, optional): Select "Submit" to complete your gift card claim.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the gift code you enter is valid and has not been already claimed by someone else.
* If you need help with your claim, contact our support team.
