# cyber_promotion_registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The cyber_promotion_registration form is used to collect information from users for a specific event or promotion. This form is designed to gather essential details such as names, contact information, and event-specific data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Name** in the text field provided.
2. Enter your **Email** in the format of `username@example.com` in the email field.
3. Provide your **Phone** number in the text field.
4. Select a **Date** for the event from the date picker.
5. Choose a **Time** for the event from the time picker.
6. Enter any **Notes** you'd like to include about the event or promotion.
7. If applicable, specify the **Name of Event** to associate with this registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Name (1)
* **Name**: A text field where you can enter your name as you'd like to be addressed.

### Email (2)
* **Email**: Enter your email address in the format `username@example.com`.

### Phone (3)
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in the format `(123) 456-7890`.

### Date (4)
* **Date**: Choose a date using the calendar picker for the event.

### Time (5)
* **Time**: Select a time using the time picker for the event.

### Notes (6)
* **Notes**: Provide any additional information or comments about the event or promotion.

### Name of Event (7)
* **Name of Event**: If applicable, enter the name or identifier of the event you're registering for.

Note: Ensure you fill out all fields correctly to ensure a smooth and accurate registration process.
