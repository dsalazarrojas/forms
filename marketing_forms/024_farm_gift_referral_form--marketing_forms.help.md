# Farm Gift Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you refer a friend or family member to our farm and potentially receive a reward in return. Please fill out this form accurately and honestly, as it will be used to verify the authenticity of your referral.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and contact information.
2. Enter the name and phone number of the person you are referring.
3. Choose a reward type for the referral (optional).
4. Enter the reward type you have chosen.
5. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (First_name)** (`first_name`, text, **required**): Enter your first name as it appears on your ID.
* **Last Name (Last_name)** (`last_name`, text, **required**): Enter your last name as it appears on your ID.
* **Phone (Contact_number)** (`contact_number`, text, **required**): Enter your contact information, including your phone number.
* **Referred Name (Ref_name)** (`ref_name`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the person you are referring.
* **Referred Phone (Ref_phone)** (`ref_phone`, text, **required**): Enter the phone number of the person you are referring.
* **Get Reward (Get_reward)** (`get_reward`, text, **required**): Choose the reward you would like to receive in return for your referral.
* **Reward Type (Reward_type)** (`reward_type`, text, **required**): Enter the type of reward you have chosen for your referral.
* **Reference Reward (Ref_reward)** (`ref_reward`, number, **required**): Enter a number to indicate the reward you have chosen.

Please note that if you are unsure about any of the fields, please refer to your farm manager or contact the farm administration for assistance.
