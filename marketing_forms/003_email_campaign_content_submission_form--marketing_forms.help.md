# email_campaign_content_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about email campaigns, enabling the marketing team to create and send targeted email communications to subscribers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter the email campaign subject**: In the "Email Campaign Subject" field, enter a brief description of the email campaign. This will serve as the title for the campaign and should be concise and attention-grabbing.
2. **Provide sender information**: In the "Sender Information" field, enter the name and contact details of the person sending the email. This could be a single individual or a team's name.
3. **Enter email details**: In the "Email Details" field, describe the content and purpose of the email campaign. This could include a brief summary of the message, the target audience, and any specific action required from the subscriber.
4. **Set up campaign settings**: In the "Campaign Settings" field, choose the appropriate settings for the email campaign. This could include the sender's email address, any attachments or images to be included, and other campaign-specific requirements.
5. **Add any additional information**: In the "Additional Information" field, enter any other relevant details about the email campaign. This could include any specific requirements or requests from the sender, such as a specific reply email address or any other unique circumstances surrounding the campaign.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Email Campaign Subject**: Enter a brief and descriptive title for the email campaign.
* **Sender Information**: Enter the name and contact details of the person sending the email, to help identify the sender and facilitate communication.
* **Email Details**: Provide a clear and concise description of the email's content and purpose.
* **Campaign Settings**: Choose settings such as sender email, attachments, and other requirements specific to the campaign.
* **Additional Information**: Enter any other relevant details about the email campaign, such as specific requests or requirements from the sender.
 
## Tips
- Double-check your information before submitting.
- Only submit one form per email campaign.
 
Note: If any field is not filled out and is marked as required, it will be highlighted with a red "Required Field" label. This ensures that the user understands which fields are essential to complete the form and proceed with the submission.
