# Market Analysis Consultation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Market Analysis Consultation Request Form is used by Marketing teams to request a consultation with potential customers to discuss their business needs and provide personalized marketing solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the contact information section with your details.
2. Provide your name and email address so we can get in touch with you.
3. Enter your phone number if you prefer to be contacted by phone.
4. Enter your company name and position to help us understand your role in the organization.
5. Specify the consultation subject to help us understand your business needs.
6. Select the available days and times for your consultation.
7. If applicable, provide a required document or any other relevant documents.
8. Specify how you would like to receive marketing communications (e.g., social media, email, in-app message).
9. Select the sources from which you learned about our marketing services (e.g., Google, Facebook, email marketing).
10. If applicable, select any additional customer service channels you would like to use.
11. Provide any additional comments or information about your consultation needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (contact_information): Enter your details so we can get in touch with you.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Contact** (contact_name): Enter your name.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Phone Number** (phone_number): Enter your phone number if you prefer to be contacted by phone.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Email Address** (email_address): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
	+ Type: email
	+ Required: no
* **Company** (company): Enter your company name to help us understand your role in the organization.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Position** (position): Enter your position in the company.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Consultation Subject** (consultation_subject): Enter a brief description of your consultation subject.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Available Days** (available_days): Select the days you are available for consultation.
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: no
* **Start Time** (start_time): Select the start time of your consultation.
	+ Type: time
	+ Required: no
* **End Time** (end_time): Select the end time of your consultation.
	+ Type: time
	+ Required: no
* **Required Document** (required_document): If applicable, attach any required document.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Other Document** (other_document): If applicable, attach any other relevant document.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Consultation Request** (consultation_request): Enter any additional comments about your consultation needs.
	+ Type: note
	+ Required: no
* **Marketing Channel** (marketing_channel): Select how you would like to receive marketing communications.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: no
* **Marketing Source** (marketing_source): Select the sources from which you learned about our marketing services.
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Required: no
* **Customer Service** (customer_service): Select any additional customer service channels you would like to use.
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Required: no
* **Additional Comments** (additional_comments): Enter any additional comments or information about your consultation needs.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Submission Date** (submission_date): The date and time the form was submitted.
	+ Type: date
	+ Required: no

Please note that all fields are optional, but providing accurate and relevant information will help our team better understand your needs and provide more efficient service.
