# Send Free Sample Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers to send them free samples or products.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, fill out the required and optional fields with the requested information.
- Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure that you receive relevant free samples or products.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
- **Sample Type** (`sample_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of free sample you would like to receive. Options include:
	+ Free Sample
	+ Free Product
	+ Discount Coupon
	+ Other
- **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of free samples or products you would like to receive.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
- **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the free sample or product you would like to receive.
- **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the category that best describes your interest in receiving a free sample or product. Options include:
	+ Marketing
	+ Sales
	+ Customer Service
- **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the product you are interested in.
- **Price Range** (`price_range`, number, optional): Enter the price range of the product you are interested in (if applicable).
