# elevator_pitch_generator_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you create a clear and concise elevator pitch for your project or pitch.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the Pitch Details section, which includes a text field where you can describe your project or pitch.
2. In the Team Members section, select the team members and their roles involved in your project or pitch.
3. Choose the pitch length and tone that best suits your project or pitch in the Pitch Length and Pitch Tone sections.
4. Write a brief and memorable elevator pitch in the Elevator Pitch section.
5. Outline the follow-up steps after the meeting in the Follow-up Steps section.
6. Provide meeting details, including date, location, and attendees, in the Meeting Details section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pitch Details** (`pitch_details`, `text`, not required): Briefly describe your project or pitch in this text field.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, `select_multiple`, not required): List team members and their roles involved in your project or pitch. Select multiple options to include all team members.
* **Pitch Length** (`pitch_length`, `select_one`, not required): Choose the pitch length that best suits your project or pitch: Brief (1-2 minutes), 3-5 minutes, 5-7 minutes, or More than 7 minutes.
* **Value Proposition** (`value_proposition`, `text`, not required): Describe your value proposition in this text field.
* **Pitch Tone** (`pitch_tone`, `select_one`, required): Select the tone of your pitch: Inspirational, Solution-oriented, Problem-focused, or Neutral.
* **Elevator Pitch** (`elevator_pitch`, `text`, not required): Write a quick and memorable summary of your project or pitch in this text field.
* **Follow-up Steps** (`followup_steps`, `text`, not required): Describe follow-up steps after meeting in this text field.
* **Meeting Details** (`meeting_details`, `text`, not required): Include meeting date, location, and attendees in this text field.

## Tips

* Be as concise as possible when filling out the form to ensure your pitch is clear and memorable.
* Use the provided options for team members and pitch length to ensure you have a clear and comprehensive pitch.
* Ensure your pitch tone is consistent with your project or pitch.
* Use the provided space to describe follow-up steps after meeting to ensure you have a clear plan.
