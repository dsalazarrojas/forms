# Tech Market Interest Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tech Market Interest Inquiry Form is designed to help us understand your company's interest in our technology offerings. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form and help us provide more relevant information and resources tailored to your company's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
- Click on each page icon at the top right of the form to navigate between the pages.
- Fill out the form by entering your information into the corresponding fields.
- Make sure to provide accurate information about your company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Intro
* **Intro** (`Intro`, `note`, required: false): This page provides a brief introduction to the form. Please read through the instructions and proceed to the next page.

### Page 2: Company Information
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your company name as it is currently known in the market.
* **Company Role** (`company_role`, text, required: false): Describe your company's role or department that is interested in our technology.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Enter your current job title.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, text, required: false): Briefly describe your company size (e.g., Small, Medium, Large).
* **Company Budget** (`company_budget`, number, required: false): Enter your company's budget for technology investment.

### Page 3: Technology Interest
* **Technology Areas of Interest** (`technology_areas`, select_multiple, required: false): Check all areas of interest that apply to your company.
* **Technology Funding** (`technology_funding`, text, required: false): Briefly describe your company's technology funding plans or budget.
* **Technology Interest** (`technology_interest`, text, required: false): Explain what technology areas your company is interested in.
* **Decision Maker** (`decision_maker`, text, required: false): Identify the person who makes decisions about technology investments in your company.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required: false): Briefly describe the department that is interested in our technology.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, text, required: false): Enter your current job function.
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, text, required: false): Briefly describe your job level or position.
* **Technology Familiarity** (`technology_familiarity`, text, required: false): Describe your current level of familiarity with our technology.
* **Technology Adoption** (`technology_adoption`, select_multiple, required: false): Check all areas where you have adopted our technology.
* **Technology Priorities** (`technology_priorities`, text, required: false): Describe your company's technology priorities.
* **Technology Benefits** (`technology_benefits`, text, required: false): Describe the benefits your company has experienced from using our technology.
* **Technology Challenges** (`technology_challenges`, text, required: false): Explain the challenges your company faced while implementing our technology.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out each field accurately and truthfully.
- If you are unsure about a field, please leave it blank or ask for clarification.
- Provide detailed information to help us better understand your company's needs.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
- Thank you for taking the time to complete this form and help us better understand your company's interest in our technology.
