# Client Campaign Content Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect and manage client campaign details. It includes information about the client's name, contact information, and campaign specifics such as name, type, objective, start and end dates, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your details as prompted by the labels and hints provided for each field.
3. Ensure that you have the necessary permissions to upload the content and confirm this on the final page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (\*):** <small>Your legal first name.</small>
* **Last Name (\*):** <small>Your legal last name.</small>
* **Email Address (\*):** <small>Primary contact email.</small>
* **Phone Number (\*):** <small>Best contact number.</small>
* **Company Name (\*):** <small>Largest business name.</small>
* **Campaign Name (\*):** <small>Campaign official name.</small>
* **Campaign Type (\*):** <small>Select the type of campaign (e.g., Email Marketing, Social Media Campaign, etc.).</small>
* **Marketing Channels (\*):** <small>Select all applicable marketing channels (e.g., Facebook, Instagram, etc.).</small>
* **Campaign Objective (\*):** <small>Primary goal of the campaign (e.g., Increase Brand Awareness, Generate Leads, etc.).</small>
* **Target Audience (\*):** <small>Who is the campaign for (e.g., customers, employees, etc.).</small>
* **Campaign Start Date (\*):** <small>When the campaign begins.</small>
* **Campaign End Date (\*):** <small>When the campaign ends.</small>
* **Content Type Being Uploaded (\*):** <small>Select the category of campaign assets (e.g., Ad Copy, Images, etc.).</small>
* **Number of Assets (\*):** <small>Approximate number of files being uploaded.</small>
* **Campaign Budget Range (\*):** <small>Total budget for the campaign.</small>
* **Key Messages (\*):** <small>Main points to communicate to the audience.</small>
* **Call to Action (\*):** <small>What the users are expected to do.</small>
* **Brand Guidelines Followed (\*):** <small>If brand rules are strictly followed, mostly followed, some deviations, or no guidelines are available.</small>
* **Content Approval Status (\*):** <small>Current status of the assets (e.g., final approved, pending review, etc.).</small>
* **Legal Review (\*):** <small>Confirmation of whether content has been legally reviewed.</small>
* **Special Requirements (\*):** <small>Any specific needs or constraints for the campaign.</small>
* **Success Metrics (\*):** <small>Metrics to measure campaign success (e.g., Click-Through Rate, Conversion Rate, etc.).</small>
* **Additional Notes:** <small>Any other relevant information about the campaign.</small>
* **I Confirm I Have Rights to All Uploaded Content (\*):** <small>A confirmation of ownership rights for all content uploaded.</small>
* **Submission Date:** <small>Date the form was submitted.</small>
