# Mobile Web & Marketing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to manage marketing forms for mobile web and marketing purposes. It is used to collect and store information about marketing forms, including category, description, and assigned tool. The form is used to create, edit, or delete marketing forms.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the category of your marketing form from the "Category" dropdown list.
2. Enter a brief description of your marketing form in the "Description" field.
3. Decide whether the form will have a file output (e.g., CSV, Excel) and select "Yes" or "No" accordingly.
4. Assign the tool used for the form, if applicable.
5. Optionally, you can enter a title for the form, but it's not required.
6. Confirm that the form has a unique form ID.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category (2)** (`Category (2)`, select_one, required): Choose a category for your marketing form. This will help in organizing and searching for forms in the system. The options available are "Marketing Forms" and "Other".
* **Description (2)** (`description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your marketing form. This will help in understanding the purpose and content of the form.
* **Title** (`title`, text, optional): Enter a title for your marketing form, if you want to give it a name. However, note that this field is not required.
* **Form IDs** (`form_ids`, number, required): Enter a unique ID for your marketing form. This will help in identifying the form in the system.
* **Assigned Tool 2** (`assigned_tool_2`, text, optional): Assign a tool used for the form, if applicable.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Assign a tool used for the form. Select from the available options: "chatjimmy" and "Other".
* **Output File** (`output_file`, select_one, required): Determine if the form will have a file output (e.g., CSV, Excel). Select "Yes" if you want to generate a file, "No" otherwise.
* **Here's a great way to create a form for use with advertising and marketing of mobile websites.** (`here_s_a_great_way_to_create_a_form`, note, optional): This field is not required and seems to be just a note to the user.
* **Description** (`marketing_forms_description`, text, required): Enter a description of your marketing form, but it's not used in the form fields, it's just a duplicate field.
* **Marketing Forms Category** (`marketing_forms_category`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more categories for your marketing form. Select from the available options: "Marketing Forms" and "Other Form Type".
