# Marketing Permit Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Marketing Permit Request Form is used to collect information from users who are requesting marketing permits from the company.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
3. Enter your Street Address in the Street Address field.
4. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
5. Select a valid Date of Birth in the Date of Birth field.
6. Select all applicable Marketing Permits from the Marketing Permits field.
7. Choose an Assigned Tool from the Assigned Tool field.
8. Enter any additional information in the Output File field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (*): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (*): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Street Address** (*): Enter the address where you reside or work.
* **Email** (*): Enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Marketing Permits** (*): Select all applicable marketing permits you are requesting (Radio Permit, TV Permit, Digital Permit).
* **Assigned Tool**: Choose the tool that will be used to process your request (Chatjimmy, Chatbot).
* **Output File** (optional): Enter any additional information that you would like to include in the output file.

## Tips

* Please double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant department for clarification.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you are requesting multiple marketing permits, please select all applicable options in the Marketing Permits field.
