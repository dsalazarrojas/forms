# Brand Influencer Launch RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from brand influencers for the launch event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your information and preferences.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and honest answers to each question.
3. Review your submission before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Your full legal name.
* **Stage Name or Handle**: Your public influencer name or handle.
* **Email Address**: Your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number**: Contact number for the event day.
* **Primary Social Platforms**: Select all the social media platforms where you create content.
	+ Instagram
	+ TikTok
	+ YouTube
	+ Twitter or X
	+ Facebook
	+ LinkedIn
	+ Pinterest
	+ Snapchat
* **Total Follower Count**: Approximate total followers across platforms.
* **Content Niche**: Select the type of content you create.
	+ Lifestyle
	+ Beauty and Fashion
	+ Fitness and Health
	+ Technology
	+ Food and Cooking
	+ Travel
	+ Gaming
	+ Business and Finance
	+ Parenting
	+ Other
* **Attendance Status**: Select whether you will be attending the event.
	+ Yes, I Will Attend
	+ No, Cannot Attend
	+ Maybe, Will Confirm Later
* **Event Date**: Date of the launch event.
* **Bringing a Plus One**: Select whether you will bring a guest.
	+ No, Attending Alone
	+ Yes, One Guest
* **Guest Name**: If bringing a guest, enter their full name.
* **Dietary Requirements**: Select any dietary restrictions you may have.
	+ None
	+ Vegetarian
	+ Vegan
	+ Gluten-Free
	+ Dairy-Free
	+ Nut Allergy
	+ Kosher
	+ Halal
* **Content Creation at Event**: Select whether you plan to create content at the event.
	+ Yes, Will Create Content
	+ Maybe
	+ No, Just Attending
* **Brand Collaboration Interest**: Select your interest in future collaborations.
	+ Very Interested
	+ Somewhat Interested
	+ Not Interested
* **Media Consent**: Select whether we can use photos of you for marketing purposes.
	+ Yes, I Consent
	+ No, I Do Not Consent
* **T-Shirt Size**: Select your preferred T-shirt size.
	+ XS
	+ S
	+ M
	+ L
	+ XL
	+ XXL
* **Transportation Needs**: Select your transportation method and needs.
	+ Driving, Need Parking
	+ Using Public Transit
	+ Need Ride Share
	+ Walking
* **Additional Notes**: Add any special requests or questions you may have.

Please fill out the form honestly and accurately, and we'll handle the rest. Thank you for your interest in our launch event!
