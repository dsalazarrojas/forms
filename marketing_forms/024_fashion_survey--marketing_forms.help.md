# fashion_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers on their fashion preferences and feedback. It is intended for marketing or customer service use cases.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the "Customer" page with your basic information, such as your name and contact details.
2. On the "Contact" page, provide any additional contact information you'd like to share with us.
3. On the "Fashion Preferences" page, describe your fashion preferences, such as favorite styles, brands, or designers.
4. Finally, use the "Feedback" page to share any feedback, suggestions, or comments you might have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer (Customer)**: Enter your name and any other details you'd like to share with us.
* **Contact (Contact)**: Provide your phone number, email, or other contact information.
* **Fashion Preferences (Fashion Preferences)**: Describe your favorite fashion styles, brands, or designers.
* **Feedback (Feedback)**: Share any comments, suggestions, or feedback about our company or products.
