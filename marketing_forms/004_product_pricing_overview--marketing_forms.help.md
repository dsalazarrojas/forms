# Product Pricing Overview - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Product Pricing Overview" form is a tool to gather essential information about a product, enabling informed marketing, product development, and sales decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing a brief description of the product in the "Product Description" field.
2. Enter the product's title in the "Product Title" field.
3. Enter the product's price in the "Price" field.
4. If applicable, enter the current discount offered in the "Discount" field.
5. Select the product's currency using the "Currency" dropdown menu.
6. If applicable, indicate if the product is currently on promo, and select the relevant option in the "Promo" field.
7. Enter the product's competitors or similar products in the "Competitors" field.
8. Choose the target audience for the product from the "Target Audience" dropdown menu.
9. Provide a brief description of the product's competitive positioning in the "Competitive Positioning" field.
10. If necessary, enter the date of the price update in the "Price Updates" field.
11. Finally, enter the last time the product information was updated in the "Last Updated" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the product, highlighting its key features and benefits.
* **Product Title** (`product_title`, text, optional): Enter the product's title, which should accurately represent the product and its purpose.
* **Price** (`price`, number, optional): Enter the product's price, taking into account any applicable discounts.
* **Discount** (`discount`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the current discount offered for the product.
* **Currency** (`currency`, select_one, optional): Select the product's currency from the provided options (USD, EUR, JPY).
* **Promo** (`promo`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if the product is currently on promo, choosing from "True", "False", or "In Progress".
* **Competitors** (`competitors`, text, optional): Enter the product's competitors or similar products in the market.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, select_one, optional): Choose the target audience for the product from the provided options (Ecommerce, Wholesale, Product Teams).
* **Competitive Positioning** (`competitive_positioning`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the product's competitive positioning in the market.
* **Price Updates** (`price_updates`, date, optional): If necessary, enter the date of the price update.
* **Last Updated** (`last_updated`, time, optional): Enter the last time the product information was updated.
