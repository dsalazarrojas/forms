# Solar Facebook Ads Strategy Call Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users for a solar Facebook ads strategy call request. It's meant for sales or business development activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address, if you have one. Note: This field is not required.
3. Provide your Phone number.
4. Select a Date for the call.
5. Select a Time for the call.
6. Enter any additional information about your Company and Location (if you have one). Note: These fields are optional.
7. Provide any Comments about the request, if needed.
8. Confirm your Email, Phone number, Date, and Time by filling in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name here. This is the first part of your name that identifies you uniquely.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name here. This is the second part of your name that identifies you uniquely.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you have a professional or personal email address, enter it here. This is where we'll use to contact you.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number here. This is where you can be reached during business hours.
- **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select a date for the call using the calendar. This is when we'll schedule the call.
- **Time** (`time`, time, required): Select a time for the call from the available options. This is when we'll hold the call.
- **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter your company name here (if you have one). This is to help us understand your business context.
- **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter your location or address here (if you have one). This is to help us understand where you're located.
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional information about your request here. This is to provide more context.
- **Confirm Email** (`email_confirmed`, email, required): Confirm your email address. This is to ensure accuracy.
- **Confirm Phone** (`phone_confirmed`, text, required): Confirm your phone number. This is to ensure accuracy.
- **Confirm Date** (`date_confirmed`, date, required): Confirm the call date you selected. This is to ensure accuracy.
- **Confirm Time** (`time_confirmed`, time, required): Confirm the call time you selected. This is to ensure accuracy.

## Tips
- Please double-check your email and phone number for accuracy when confirming.
- Use a specific date and time that suits your schedule.
- If you have any questions or need clarification, please let us know in the Comments field.
