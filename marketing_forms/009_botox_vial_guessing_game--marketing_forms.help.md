# Botox Vial Guessing Game - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Botox Vial Guessing Game form is a contest where participants try to guess the number of vials of Botox in a display. This guide will walk you through the required steps to complete the form correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your first name, last name, email address, phone number, date of birth, and age verification.
2. Indicate your current patient status and your interest in Botox treatment.
3. Enter your guess for the number of vials of Botox in the display.
4. Provide an optional explanation for your guess.
5. Choose how you would like to be contacted for contest winner announcement and marketing purposes.
6. Select whether you would like a complimentary consultation.
7. Sign up for our newsletter if you are interested.
8. Provide your social media handle if you have one.
9. Agree to the contest rules and terms.
10. Consent to the use of your contact information for contest and marketing purposes.
11. Agree to receive marketing communications from our clinic.
