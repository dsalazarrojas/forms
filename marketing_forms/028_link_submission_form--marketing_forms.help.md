# Link Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Link Submission Form is a tool for users to share links with the organization. It collects personal information and details about the submitted links, enabling the organization to review and process them efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your user information in the "User Information" page, including your name, phone number, and email (if applicable).
2. Provide the link URL and submission date/time in the respective fields.
3. Add any comments or details about the submission in the "User Comments" field.
4. Select the "Submission Type" to categorize the submission.
5. Provide any additional submission details if necessary.
6. Enter the name of the contact person responsible for handling the submission.
7. Select the submission status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (Page 1)**
  • **User Name** (`user_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as you want it to be displayed.
  • **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
  • **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Link Information (Page 2)**
  • **Link URL** (`link_url`, `text`, required): Enter the URL of the link you want to share.
  • **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you submitted the link.
  • **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you submitted the link.
* **Comments (Page 3)**
  • **User Comments** (`user_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any comments or details about the submission.
* **Submission Details (Page 4)**
  • **Submission Type** (`submission_type`, `text`, required): Select the category of the submission.
  • **Submission Details** (`submission_details`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional submission details if necessary.
* **Submission Information (Page 5)**
  • **Assigned Contact** (`assigned_contact`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for handling the submission.
  • **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, `text`, required): Select the status of the submission.
  • **Submission URL** (`submission_url`, `text`, optional): Enter the URL of the submission (if applicable).
