# Client Marketing Package Selection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Marketing Package Selection form is used to gather information about your marketing needs and preferences. This information will help us tailor the best-suited marketing package for your business. Please take a few minutes to complete this form accurately, and we'll be in touch soon.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have a clear understanding of your marketing goals and preferences.
2. Fill in your Client Name and Business Name in the respective fields.
3. Select a Marketing Package that suits your needs from the options provided.
4. Choose your Desired Start Date for the marketing campaign.
5. Specify the Minimum Contract Length for your marketing services (if applicable).
6. Define your top 3 Marketing Goals for our services.
7. Indicate the channels you're most interested in (e.g., SEO, PPC, Social Media, Content Marketing, Email Marketing).
8. Identify your Primary Competitor.
9. (Optional) Select your Preferred Payment Method.
10. Agree to the terms and conditions of the selected package.
11. Provide your Digital Signature (Full Name).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Please enter your Company or Client Name as it appears on your business card or website.
* **Business Name**: Enter your business's official name.
* **Package Selection**:
	+ Basic: Suitable for small businesses with limited marketing budgets.
	+ Standard: Ideal for businesses looking for a well-rounded marketing package.
	+ Premium: Recommended for businesses requiring advanced marketing solutions.
	+ Enterprise: Customized for large-scale businesses with unique needs.
* **Desired Start Date**: Choose the start date for your marketing campaign.
* **Minimum Contract Length**: Select the duration of your marketing contract (3, 6, or 12 months).
* **What are your top 3 marketing goals?**: Describe your top 3 marketing goals, e.g., increase sales, improve website traffic, or enhance brand awareness.
* **Which channels are you most interested in?**: Select all channels relevant to your business, e.g., SEO, PPC, Social Media, Content Marketing, or Email Marketing.
* **Who is your primary competitor?**: Identify your main competitor in the market.
* **Preferred Payment Method**: Choose your preferred payment method (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or PayPal).
* **I agree to the terms and conditions of the selected package**: Confirm that you have reviewed and agree to the terms and conditions of your chosen package.
* **Signature - Type Full Name**: Please enter your full name for digital signature purposes.
