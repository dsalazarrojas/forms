# Expert Freedom Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for experts to apply for freedom or access to a specific resource, opportunity, or program. To be eligible, you must provide accurate and complete information in the following fields.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter a valid Email address that you use and check regularly.
3. Enter your Phone number so we can contact you if needed.
4. If applicable, enter your Company name (this is optional).
5. Select either "Yes" or "No" for the Role field, depending on your status.
6. Enter a valid Date in the Date field (mm/dd/yyyy format).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address that you use and check regularly. We will use this to communicate with you.
- **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
- **Company** (text, optional): If you are affiliated with a company, enter its name.
- **Role** (select_one, required): Select "Yes" if you have the required role or status, and "No" otherwise.
- **Date** (date, required): Enter a valid Date in the mm/dd/yyyy format.
