# Media Buy Planning Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to plan media buys for TV, Radio, Print, and Online channels by scheduling appointments with clients and media buyers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the client name, email, and phone number to contact the client.
2. Enter the client's media spend.
3. Select the media buyer assigned to the client.
4. Choose the media channels to be used (TV, Radio, Print, or Online).
5. Schedule the appointment date and time.
6. Enter the appointment location.
7. Provide any additional appointment details.
8. Enter any client notes.
9. Select the user responsible for the appointment.
10. Select the tool used for the appointment.
11. Enter the form ID.
12. Enter the person who created the form.
13. Enter a description of the form.
14. Select the category of the form (e.g., Marketing Forms).
15. Enter the date the form was created.
16. Enter the date the form was last updated.
17. Enter the person who last updated the form.
18. Enter the date the form was deleted (if applicable).
