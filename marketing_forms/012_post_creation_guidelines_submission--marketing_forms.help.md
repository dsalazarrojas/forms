# Post Creation Guidelines Submission - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Post Creation Guidelines Submission form is used to collect information about a post creation guidelines submission. This form is essential for ensuring that all relevant information is gathered and that the submission process is completed accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the **Contributor Information** section with your name and other relevant details.
2.  In the **Draft Post** section, provide a brief description of the post you are submitting.
3.  In the **Attached Files** section, select whether or not there are any files attached with your submission.
4.  In the **Confirm Guidelines** section, select whether or not you have confirmed the guidelines.
5.  In the **Confirm Guidelines Justification** section, provide a justification for confirming or not confirming the guidelines.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contributor Information** (<name> text, required: false):
    *   This field is used to enter your name and other relevant details.
*   **Draft Post** (<name> text, required: false):
    *   This field is for a brief description of the post you are submitting.
*   **Attached Files** (<name> select_multiple, required: false):
    *   Select 'Yes' if you have any files attached with your submission, 'No' otherwise.
*   **Confirm Guidelines** (<name> select_one, required: false):
    *   Select 'Yes' if you have confirmed the guidelines, 'No' otherwise.
*   **Confirm Guidelines Justification** (<name> text, required: false):
    *   If you have confirmed or not confirmed the guidelines, explain the reason why in this field.
