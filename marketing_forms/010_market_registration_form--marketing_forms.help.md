# Market Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Market Registration Form is a collection of fields that gather information about an individual's or business's market presence and contact details. This form is designed to capture necessary data for marketing purposes, such as understanding market demographics and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to understand what each section is asking for.
2. Begin with the "personal_details" section.
3. Enter your personal details, such as your name and any other relevant information.
4. Proceed to the "business_details" section and enter your business information.
5. In the "contact_info" section, provide your contact details.
6. Review the "terms and conditions" section and accept or decline the terms.
7. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
8. Finally, enter your phone number in the "phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Personal Details
* **Personal Details** (`personal_details`, text, required/optional): Enter any personal details you would like to share, such as your name, title, or other relevant information. This field is optional but can be useful for building a personal connection with your audience.

### Business Details
* **Business Details** (`business_details`, text, required/optional): Enter information about your business, including its name, address, or industry. This field is optional but helps us understand your business presence.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): Provide your contact details, including your name, email, or phone number. This field is optional but enables us to get in touch with you.

### Terms and Conditions
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms and conditions`, text, required/optional): Review and accept or decline our terms and conditions. Please note that this field is optional but required for us to proceed with your market registration.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for us to contact you. This field is optional but recommended for communication purposes.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for us to reach you. This field is optional but useful for scheduling calls or meetings.
