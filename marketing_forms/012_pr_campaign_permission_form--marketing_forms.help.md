# PR Campaign Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information and consent for a PR campaign. It includes fields for basic contact details and a checkbox for consent to participate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Campaign Name** in the designated field. This is the name of the PR campaign you are interested in participating in.
2. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Company** fields with your contact information.
3. Enter your **Job Title**, **Department**, and **Email** address where required.
4. If applicable, enter your **Phone** number.
5. Check the box next to **I agree to participate** to confirm your consent to be part of the PR campaign. 
6. If you have any additional comments, feel free to type them in the **Additional comments** field.
7. Finally, click the submit button to send your information to the campaign team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Campaign Name**: Enter the name of the PR campaign you are participating in.
* **First Name**: Type your first name for contact purposes.
* **Last Name**: Type your last name for contact purposes.
* **Company**: Enter your company name or organization for reference.
* **Job Title**: If you have a job title, enter it here. If not, you can leave this field blank.
* **Department**: If you have a department name, enter it here. If not, you can leave this field blank.
* **Email**: Enter your professional email address for communication.
* **Phone**: If you have a phone number, enter it here. If not, you can leave this field blank.
* **I agree to participate**: Check this box to confirm your consent to participate in the PR campaign.
* **Additional comments**: If you have any comments or information you'd like to share with the campaign team, type them in this field.
