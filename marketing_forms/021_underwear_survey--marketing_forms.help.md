# Underwear Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Underwear Survey is designed to gather your preferences and habits related to underwear. Your participation will help us better understand our customers' needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Select only one option for select_one fields (e.g. gender, income).
3. Select multiple options for select_multiple fields (e.g. type, size, color).
4. Use the options provided in each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **gender** (select_one): Select your gender (male or female).
* **age** (number): Enter your age.
* **income** (select_one): Select your income level (high, low, or medium).
* **type** (select_multiple): Select the types of underwear you prefer (brief, boxer, boxer brief).
* **size** (select_multiple): Select your preferred underwear sizes (large, small, or medium).
* **color** (select_multiple): Select the colors you like in your underwear (red, blue, black).
* **material** (select_multiple): Select the materials you prefer in your underwear (cotton, poly, microfiber).

## Tips
- Please answer all questions, but do not feel compelled to answer questions if you are unsure or uncomfortable.
- Select only what applies to you.
- If you have any difficulties or concerns, contact our support team.
