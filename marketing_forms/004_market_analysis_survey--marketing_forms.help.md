# Market Analysis Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Market Analysis Survey is designed to collect information about customers for market analysis purposes. This data will be used to inform sales, marketing, and product teams' strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all required fields and fill out as much as you can in optional fields.
2. Be truthful and accurate in your responses.
3. Use real names and contact information (e.g., email or phone number) for demographic info, contact info, and customer service feedback.
4. Select all relevant market trends that apply to you.
5. Describe your favorite hobbies in the "customer_preferences" field.
6. Answer questions regarding purchase history, if applicable.
7. Provide any additional information that might be helpful in the "additional_info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer_demographics** (`demographic_info`, text, optional): This field is for collecting general information about you, such as your name, address, and other demographic details.
* **customer_contact** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact information, like your email or phone number, for follow-up or future communication.
* **customer_preferences** (`preferences`, text, optional): Describe your favorite hobbies or interests that might be relevant to our products or services.
* **market_trends** (`market_trends`, select multiple, optional): Select all the market trends that apply to you, such as Market Trend 1, Market Trend 2, or Market Trend 3.
* **products_and_services** (`product_interest`, text, optional): Tell us about the products or services that interest you or your company uses.
* **customer_experience** (`customer_experience`, text, optional): Share your experiences with our company or products, including any feedback or suggestions.
* **purchase_history** (`purchase_history`, number, optional): If you've made purchases with us, please indicate how many times you've purchased, including any repeat purchases.
* **customer_service** (`customer_service`, text, optional): Share any feedback about our customer service, including successes or areas for improvement.
* **additional_info** (`additional_info`, note, optional): Provide any additional information that might be helpful in understanding your needs or preferences.
