# Customer Acquisition Strategy Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Acquisition Strategy Feedback Form is designed to collect your feedback and suggestions on our current customer acquisition strategy. Your input will help us improve our approach and better serve you and our other customers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your opinion on the current customer acquisition strategy.
2. Provide any suggestions for improvements or changes you would like to see.
3. Let us know if you would like to be contacted for further feedback or updates.
4. Rate how likely you are to recommend our company based on the current customer acquisition strategy.
5. Share any features or services offered by our competitors that you would like to see us offer.
6. If you have any other comments or questions, please feel free to include them.
7. Suggest any additional features or information you would like to see in future updates.
8. Confirm if you would like us to contact you for discussion or updates on your feedback.
9. Let us know if you would like to see any additional features or information in future updates.
10. Confirm if you would like us to contact you for updates on your feedback.
11. Finally, let us know if you would like to receive updates on your feedback.
