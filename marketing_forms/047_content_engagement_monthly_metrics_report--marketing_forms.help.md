# Content Engagement Monthly Metrics Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and report on content engagement metrics for a specific month and year. It provides a comprehensive overview of the performance of various content channels, including social media, email, website, and SEO.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the month and year of the report from the dropdown menus.
2. Enter the total website traffic for the month.
3. Enter the total social media engagement (likes, shares, comments) for the month.
4. Enter the average email open rate and click-through rate for the month.
5. Choose the primary performing channel for the month.
6. Provide key insights and lessons learned for the month.
7. Confirm that the report is completed accurately.
8. Agree to the privacy policy regarding your data.
9. Enter the date of report submission.
10. Enter your full name as the analyst.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Month of the Report** (id: 2): Select the month of the report from the dropdown menu.
* **Year of the Report** (id: 3): Select the year of the report from the dropdown menu.
* **Total Website Traffic for the Month** (id: 5): Enter the total number of visits to the website for the month.
* **Total Social Media Engagement (Likes, Shares, Comments)** (id: 6): Enter the total social media engagement for the month.
* **Average Email Open Rate (%)** (id: 7): Enter the average email open rate for the month.
* **Average Click-Through Rate (%)** (id: 8): Enter the average click-through rate for the month.
* **Primary Performing Channel for the Month** (id: 10): Choose the channel that performed the best for the month.
* **Key Insights and Lessons Learned for the Month** (id: 11): Provide a brief description of the key insights and lessons learned for the month.
* **Metrics Report Completed?** (id: 13): Confirm that the report is completed accurately.
* **I confirm that the information provided is accurate** (id: 14): Agree that the information provided is accurate.
* **I have read and agree to the privacy policy regarding my data** (id: 15): Agree to the privacy policy regarding your data.
* **Date of Report Submission** (id: 16): Enter the date of report submission.
* **Analyst Full Name** (id: 17): Enter your full name as the analyst.
