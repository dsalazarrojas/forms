# Newsletter Article Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Newsletter Article Submission Form is a tool for users to submit articles for consideration to be featured in a publication or newsletter.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of the article you are submitting.
2. Provide your name as the author of the article.
3. Enter your email address (if you would like to receive a response or update on the submission status).
4. Enter the name of the company or organization you represent (if applicable).
5. Enter a title for the article you are submitting.
6. Write a brief summary of the article in 100 words or less.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, optional): This field allows you to provide a brief description of the article you are submitting. This description will help the editors understand the context and topic of the article.
* **Author** (`author`, text, optional): Enter your name as the author of the article. This will be attributed to the article if it is accepted for publication.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you would like to receive a response or update on the submission status, please enter your email address here. This will help us contact you regarding your submission.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the company or organization you represent. This is helpful for our records and may be used for attribution.
* **Article Title** (`article_name`, text, optional): Enter a title for the article you are submitting. This will be the title displayed on the published article.
* **Article Summary** (`article_body`, text, optional): Write a brief summary of the article in 100 words or less. This will help the editors evaluate the article's relevance and interest to our readers.

## Tips
* Be concise and clear in your submission. A well-structured and well-written article will increase its chances of being accepted.
* Ensure that the article you submit has not been previously published or submitted through this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns regarding your submission, please contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
