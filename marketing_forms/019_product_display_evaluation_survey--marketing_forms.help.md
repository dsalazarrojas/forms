# Product Display Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather your feedback and opinions on the product display in our store. Your input will help us to improve and refine our product display to better meet your needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the rating that best reflects your opinion for each question.
2. Choose the options that best apply to your situation.
3. Answer any open-ended questions in as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How would you rate the overall appearance of the product**: This question asks for your overall opinion on the appearance of the product display. Choose from 1 (Not At All) to 5 (Strongly) based on how visually appealing you found it.
* **Was this product well-matched to its surroundings**: This question asks if you felt the product display was well-integrated with the store's surroundings. Choose from 1 (Not At All) to 5 (Strongly) based on how well you think it matched the store's aesthetic.
* **How much do you feel the product display was eye-catching**: This question asks for your opinion on how eye-catching you found the product display. Choose from 1 (Not At All) to 5 (Strongly) based on how attention-grabbing you found it.
* **How much did you find the product display helpful**: This question asks for your opinion on how helpful you found the product display. Choose from 1 (Not At All) to 5 (Strongly) based on how useful you found it in making a purchasing decision.
* **What would you rate your overall experience with the product display**: This question asks for your overall opinion on your experience with the product display. Choose from 1 (Not At All) to 5 (Strongly) based on how positive or negative your experience was.
* **How would you rate the effectiveness of the product display**: This question asks for your opinion on how effective you think the product display was in communicating product information. Choose from 1 (Not At All) to 5 (Strongly) based on how well you think it communicated product details.
* **Do you have any additional comments regarding the product display**: This question is an open-ended question where you can provide any other comments or suggestions you have regarding the product display.
* **What was the primary reason you visited this store**: This question asks for your reason for visiting the store. Choose from 1 (To purchase a product) to 5 (Other) based on why you visited the store.
* **How many times have you visited this store in the last 30 days**: This question asks for the number of times you have visited the store in the past month.
