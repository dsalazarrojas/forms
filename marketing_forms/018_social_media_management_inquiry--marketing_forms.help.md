# Social Media Management Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your social media management needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information in the "Contact Information" field.
2. Provide details about the social media platforms you're currently using in the "Social Media Info" field.
3. List the social media channels you'd like to manage in the "Social Media Channels" field.
4. Write a brief message explaining your social media management requirements and preferences in the "Message" field.
5. If you have any attachments (e.g., screenshots, files) related to your inquiry, you can upload them in the "Attachments" field.
6. Finally, confirm your submission by typing "Confirm" in the "Confirm" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, required): This field is where you'll provide us with your name, email address, and other relevant contact details.
* **Social Media Info** (Social Media Info, text, required): Here, you'll describe the social media platforms you're currently using (e.g., Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn).
* **Social Media Channels** (Social Media Channels, text, required): List the social media channels you'd like to manage (e.g., Instagram, YouTube, TikTok).
* **Message** (Message, text, required): Write a brief message explaining your social media management requirements and preferences.
* **Attachments** (Attachments, text, optional): You can upload any attachments related to your inquiry, such as screenshots or files.
* **Confirm** (Confirm, text, required): Type "Confirm" in this field to confirm your submission.
