# Marketing Campaign Audit Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Marketing Campaign Audit Form is a tool to evaluate and gather information on past marketing campaigns. It helps to identify areas of improvement and informs future marketing strategies by reviewing the performance of previous campaigns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the marketing campaign you wish to audit.
2. Select the relevant information from the options provided for each field.
3. Enter any additional information or comments in the text fields.
4. Submit the form to complete the audit.

* **Audited By** (`audited_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the person who audited this campaign.

### 5. Marketing Objective
* **Marketing Objective** (`marketing_objective`, select_one, required): Please select one of the following objectives that best describes the campaign's goal:
	+ Yes

### 6. Marketing Tactics
* **Marketing Tactics** (`marketing_tactics`, text, required): Provide any notable marketing tactics used during this campaign.

### 7. Results
* **Results** (`results`, text, required): Please provide any notable results or outcomes from this campaign.
