# sales_and_marketing_strategy_plan - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "sales_and_marketing_strategy_plan" form is designed to help organizations develop and document their sales and marketing strategies. It ensures that all necessary information is collected to create a comprehensive plan that aligns with the organization's overall goals and objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read through each section carefully and understand the information required.
* Fill in the organization's name in the "organization_name" field.
* Specify the sales and marketing objectives for the organization in the "sales_objectives" and "marketing_objectives" fields, respectively.
* Identify the target audience for the strategy plan in the "target_audience" field.
* Choose the sales channels to be used (if any) in the "sales_channels" field.
* Describe the sales and marketing tactics to be employed in the "sales_tactics" and "market_tactics" fields, respectively.
* Allocate a budget for the strategy plan in the "budget_allocation" field.
* Indicate whether performance metrics will be tracked for sales and marketing in the "sales_performance_metrics" and "marketing_performance_metrics" fields.
* Set a timeline for the strategy plan in the "timeline" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This is a placeholder field for you to enter any additional information or comments about the form.
* **organization_name** (`2`, `text`, required: true): Enter the name of the organization.
* **sales_objectives** (`3`, `text`, required: true): Describe the sales objectives of the organization, such as increase sales, expand customer base, etc.
* **marketing_objectives** (`4`, `text`, required: true): Describe the marketing objectives of the organization, such as increase brand awareness, enhance customer engagement, etc.
* **target_audience** (`5`, `text`, required: true): Identify the target audience for the sales and marketing strategy plan.
* **sales_channels** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all applicable sales channels (e.g., online, offline, phone, email).
* **sales_tactics** (`7`, `text`, required: true): Describe the sales tactics to be employed, such as cold calling, trade shows, etc.
* **market_tactics** (`8`, `text`, required: true): Describe the marketing tactics to be employed, such as social media, content marketing, etc.
* **budget_allocation** (`9`, `number`, required: true): Allocate a budget for the strategy plan.
* **sales_performance_metrics** (`10`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate whether performance metrics will be tracked for sales, such as sales revenue, customer base, etc.
* **marketing_performance_metrics** (`11`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate whether performance metrics will be tracked for marketing, such as engagement rates, conversion rates, etc.
* **timeline** (`12`, `date`, required: true): Set a timeline for the strategy plan.
