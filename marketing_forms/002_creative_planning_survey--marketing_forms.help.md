# Creative Planning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Creative Planning Survey" is a form designed to gather information about your team's habits, preferences, and methods when it comes to planning, communication, and collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow these steps:

* Answer each question based on your actual experiences and current practices.
* For select_multiple, select_one, and select_one fields, choose the options that best describe your team's habits.
* For text fields (planning_method, creative_process_improvement, feedback_frequency, feedback_method, communication_channel, communication_frequency, project_budget, collaboration, and other_planning_tools), provide a brief description of your team's current practices.
