# youtube_video_feedback_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from viewers on YouTube videos. The form is designed to capture various information about the video, including the viewer's name, email, ratings, and comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name in the "What's your name?" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Rate the video by selecting a number from 1 to 10 in the "How would you rate this video?" field.
4. Specify the video title in the "What's the video title?" field.
5. Choose the category that best describes the video content from the dropdown menu in the "What category does this video belong to?" field.
6. Enter the length of the video in minutes in the "How long is the video?" field.
7. Assess the quality of the video by selecting all that apply from the "What's the quality of the video?" field.
8. Report how long you watched the video in the "How long did you watch the video for?" field.
9. Specify the date and time you watched the video in the "What date and time did you watch the video?" field.
10. Enter any comments or feedback about the video in the "Comments" field (optional).
11. Provide any suggestions for the video in the "Suggestions" field (optional).
12. Select the video ID from the dropdown menu in the "Video ID" field.
13. Enter the tool assigned for this video in the "Which tool was assigned for this video?" field.
14. Enter the video ID for this form in the "Video ID for this form" field (optional).
15. Enter a title for this form in the "Title" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What's your name? (1)**: Enter your name to identify yourself as the respondent.
* **Email (2)**: Enter your email address to contact you for follow-up questions or to provide more information.
* **How would you rate this video? (3)**: Rate the video from 1 to 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* **What's the video title? (4)**: Enter the title of the video as you know it.
* **What category does this video belong to? (5)**: Choose from video, image, audio, or other to describe the video content.
* **How long is the video? (6)**: Enter the length of the video in minutes.
* **What's the quality of the video? (7)**: Select all that apply from Good and Poor.
* **How long did you watch the video for? (8)**: Report how long you watched the video.
* **What date and time did you watch the video? (9)**: Enter the date and time you watched the video.
* **Comments (10)**: Enter any comments or feedback about the video (optional).
* **Suggestions (11)**: Provide any suggestions for the video (optional).
* **Video ID (12)**: Select the video ID from the dropdown menu.
* **Which tool was assigned for this video? (13)**: Enter the tool assigned for this video.
* **Video ID for this form (14)**: Enter the video ID for this form (optional).
* **Title (15)**: Enter a title for this form (optional).
