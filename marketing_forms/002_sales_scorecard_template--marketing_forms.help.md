# Sales Scorecard Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Scorecard Template is designed to provide a unified view of sales performance across various aspects, including sales score, target, status, and sales channels.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant sales channels and fill out the corresponding fields accordingly.
2. For sales score and target, enter the actual sales data or the target amount/percentage.
3. Choose the correct status from the dropdown list, indicating the current stage of your sales progress.
4. Enter the last updated date when you last updated the sales scorecard.
5. Fill out the sales frequency field with the time interval between updates.
6. Enter any additional notes about the sales status.
7. Enter your email and phone number for contact purposes.
8. Fill out any additional sales channels as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Info**: This field is where you can enter any general information about your sales performance.
* **Sales Scorecard**: Enter the scorecard number to track your sales progress.
* **Sales Goal Target**: Choose between "Goal" and "Target" to indicate whether you are aiming to meet a goal or have already met a target.
* **Product Category**: Enter the category of the product you are selling.
* **Product Subcategory**: Enter the subcategory of the product you are selling.
* **Sales Channel**: Enter the channel through which you sold or will sell the product.
* **Sales Score**: Enter the sales amount or score.
* **Sales Target**: Enter the target sales amount or percentage.
* **Sales Goal Status**: Choose from the options to indicate the status of your sales goal (Not Started, In Progress, Completed).
* **Last Updated**: Enter the date when you last updated the sales scorecard.
* **Sales Frequency**: Enter the time interval between updates.
* **Status**: Enter any additional notes about the sales status.
* **Email Sales**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Sales**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Sales Channel 2/Sales Channel 3**: Enter any additional sales channels used.

Note: This guide is a general explanation and might need to be adapted according to your specific sales process and internal policies.
