# Product Packaging Design Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Product Packaging Design Assessment Form is a tool designed to help designers, manufacturers, and packaging experts assess and evaluate the various aspects of product packaging design. This form provides a comprehensive overview of the product packaging design, allowing users to make informed decisions and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and fields carefully to understand what information is required.
2. Answer each field according to your specific product packaging design needs and goals.
3. Ensure that you answer all required fields.
4. Review your answers for accuracy and completeness.
5. Submit the form to view the results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Name** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the product being assessed.
* **Packaging Description** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the product packaging.
* **Product Type** (`3`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of product from the options provided (Bottled, Canned, or Other).
* **Packaging Design** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the design features of the product packaging (Simple, Colorful, or Eye Catching).
* **Packaging Color** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the color(s) of the product packaging (Red, Green, Blue, or Yellow).
* **Size and Weight** (`6`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the size and weight of the product packaging.
* **Size and Weight Unit** (`7`, `text`, required/optional): Select the unit of measurement for the size and weight (e.g., inches, grams, etc.).
* **Shape and Materials** (`8`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the shape and materials used in the product packaging.
* **Size of Packaging** (`9`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the size of the product packaging from the options provided (Small, Medium, or Large).
* **Weight of Packaging** (`10`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the weight of the product packaging from the options provided (Lightweight, Heavy).
* **Packaging Materials** (`11`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the materials used in the product packaging (Plastic, Paper, Metal, Glass).
* **Shape of Packaging** (`12`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the shape of the product packaging from the options provided (Cube, Cylinder, Rectangular, Sphere, Irregular).
* **Color of Packaging** (`13`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the color(s) of the product packaging (Red, Green, Blue, Yellow).
* **Finish of Packaging** (`14`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the finish of the product packaging from the options provided (Glossy, Matte, Textured, Embossed).
* **Packaging Designer** (`15`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the person or team responsible for designing the product packaging.
* **Packaging Type** (`16`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the type of product packaging (e.g., box, bag, bottle, etc.).
* **Material of Packaging** (`17`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the material(s) used in the product packaging.
* **Packaging Technology** (`18`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the technology used in the product packaging from the options provided (Automatic, Manual, Both).
* **Sustainability** (`19`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the level of sustainability of the product packaging from the options provided (High, Medium, Low).
* **Product Packaging Design** (`20`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the design features of the product packaging (Simple, Colorful, Eye Catching).

* **Eco-Friendly Materials** (`22`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select whether the product packaging uses eco-friendly materials (True or False).
* **Product Packaging Size** (`23`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the size of the product packaging.
* **Packaging Cost** (`24`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the cost of the product packaging from the options provided (Low, Medium, High).
