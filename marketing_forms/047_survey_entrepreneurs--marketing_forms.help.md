# Survey Entrepreneurs - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Survey Entrepreneurs form is designed to collect information from entrepreneurs and business owners for marketing purposes. This guide will help you complete the form accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the main page, which includes:
	* `contact_info`: Enter your name, email address, and phone number.
	* `company_name`: Enter your company's name.
	* `job_title`: Enter your job title.
2. On the second page, provide more in-depth information about your business:
	* `ceo_email` and `ceo_phone`: Enter the email and phone number of your CEO or business owner.
	* `ceo_job_title`: Enter the job title of your CEO or business owner.
	* `company_type` and `revenue` and `employees`: Choose the type of business you have, enter your revenue, and number of employees.
	* `industry`: Select the industry your business operates in.
3. Finally, provide optional information:
	* `website`, `address`, `description`, `business_name`, `location`, `business_type`, `year_established`, `ceo_facebook`, `ceo_twitter`, `ceo_linkedin`: These fields are optional and can be left blank if not applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **contact_info (Text)**: Enter your name, email address, and phone number.
* **company_name (Text)**: Enter your company's name.
* **job_title (Text)**: Enter your job title.
* **contact_info (Text)**: Enter your name, email address, and phone number (same as the top field).
* **company_name (Text)**: Enter your company's name (same as the top field).
* **job_title (Text)**: Enter your job title (same as the top field).
* **ceo_email (Text)**: Enter the email address of your CEO or business owner.
* **ceo_phone (Text)**: Enter the phone number of your CEO or business owner.
* **ceo_job_title (Text)**: Enter the job title of your CEO or business owner.
* **company_type (Select One)**: Choose the type of business you have (small business, big business, startup, or other).
* **revenue (Number)**: Enter your company's revenue.
* **employees (Text)**: Enter the number of employees in your company.
* **industry (Select Multiple)**: Select the industry your business operates in.
* **company_size (Text)**: Enter the size of your company (optional).
* **location (Text)**: Enter your company's location (optional).
* **business_type (Select One)**: Choose the type of business you have (optional).
* **year_established (Text)**: Enter the year your company was established (optional).
* **ceo (Text)**: Enter the name of your CEO or business owner (optional).
* **ceo_facebook (Text)**: Enter the Facebook profile of your CEO or business owner (optional).
* **ceo_twitter (Text)**: Enter the Twitter profile of your CEO or business owner (optional).
* **ceo_linkedin (Text)**: Enter the LinkedIn profile of your CEO or business owner (optional).

**Tips**

* Double-check your input for accuracy and completeness.
* Avoid using placeholder text or default values.
* Take note of any required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
