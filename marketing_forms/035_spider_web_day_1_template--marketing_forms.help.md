# spider_web_day_1_template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form, "spider_web_day_1_template", is used to collect user information for web development purposes. It gathers data about the user's name, contact details, and schedule preferences for the Spider Web Day 1 event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "First name" field with your name as it appears on your identification.
2. Enter your valid email address in the "Email" field. This will be used for communication purposes.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field, if you have one.
4. Select the date of your availability for the event.
5. Choose a time slot that suits you best.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name (First name, text, optional)**: Enter your first name.
* **Email (Email, email, optional)**: Enter your email address. This is where you can be reached for event communications.
* **Phone (Phone, text, optional)**: If you have a phone number, enter it here. If not, you can leave this blank.
* **Date (Date, date, optional)**: Select the date you are available for the event.
* **Time (Time, time, optional)**: Choose a time slot that suits your schedule.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure we can contact you properly.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask us.

Note: The form is designed to be simple and easy to use. If you have any trouble completing the form, contact us for assistance.
