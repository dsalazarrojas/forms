# press_release_writer_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to refer potential candidates for job openings at companies in the marketing and press release writing fields.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, company, and email address to help us get in touch with you.
2. Choose the type of company you represent (public, private, or other).
3. Select the industry of the company.
4. Choose the size of the company.
5. Choose the location of the company.
6. Select the job title of the position.
7. Provide a brief job description.
8. Confirm if the job opening currently exists.
9. Provide a job opening description, if applicable.
10. Choose the status of the job opening.
11. Choose the type of job (full-time, part-time, or contract).
12. Provide the city of the company location.
13. Fill in any other required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (1, text, required): Please fill in your name.
* **Company** (2, text, required): This is the name of the company you represent.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Please provide your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone** (4, text, required): This is the phone number of the person referring this job opening.
* **Description** (5, note, optional): Please provide a brief description of the company and job opening.
* **Company Website** (6, text, optional): If the company has a website, please provide the URL.
* **Company Type** (7, select_one, required): This is the type of company you represent (public, private, or other).
* **Industry** (8, select_multiple, required): Select all the industries that apply to the company.
* **Company Size** (9, select_multiple, required): Select the size of the company (large, medium, small, or other).
* **Location** (10, select_multiple, required): Select the location of the company (north america, europe, asia, africa, or other).
* **Job Title** (11, select_one, required): This is the title of the job opening.
* **Job Description** (12, text, required): Please provide a brief description of the job opening.
* **Contact Person** (13, text, required): This is the name of the person who can be contacted about the job opening.
* **Job Opening** (14, select_one, required): Confirm if the job opening currently exists.
* **Job Opening Description** (15, text, optional): If the job opening exists, please provide a brief description of the job.
* **Job Status** (16, select_one, required): This is the status of the job opening (open, closed).
* **Job Start Date** (17, date, optional): If the job opening is open, provide the expected start date.
* **Job End Date** (18, date, optional): If the job opening is closed, provide the end date.
* **Job Type** (19, select_multiple, required): This is the type of job (full-time, part-time, or contract).
* **Job Hours** (20, text, optional): If applicable, provide the number of hours worked.
* **Job Description** (21, text, optional): This is an additional description of the job.
* **Company Address** (22, text, optional): If applicable, provide the company address.
* **Country** (23, text, optional): If applicable, provide the country of the company.
* **City** (24, text, required): This is the city of the company location.
* **State** (25, text, optional): If applicable, provide the state of the company location.
