# Marketing Lead Generation Specialist Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about your qualifications, experience, and availability to help us assess your fit for the Marketing Lead Generation Specialist role. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to provide a thorough understanding of your background and qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to provide a valid email address and phone number for contact purposes.
3. Attach a resume or provide a link to your professional online presence (e.g., LinkedIn profile).
4. Describe your job title and experience, including the level of experience you have.
5. Specify your preferred work schedule and availability.
6. Provide information about your expected annual salary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name
**First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name.

### Last Name
**Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name.

### Email
**Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter a valid email address we can use to contact you.

### Job Title
**Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Please enter your current or previous job title.

### Job Title Experience
**Job Title Experience** (`job_title_experience`, text, optional): Describe your experience in your current or previous job title.

### Job Title Level
**Job Title Level** (`job_title_level`, text, optional): Please enter the level of experience you have in your job title (e.g., junior, senior, expert).

### Resume
**Resume** (`resume`, text, optional): Please attach your resume or provide a link to your online resume.

### LinkedIn
**LinkedIn** (`linkedin`, text, optional): Please enter your LinkedIn profile URL.

### LinkedIn URL
**LinkedIn URL** (`linkedin_url`, text, optional): Please enter your LinkedIn profile URL if different from your main LinkedIn profile.

### Phone
**Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please enter a valid phone number we can use to contact you.

### Availability
**Availability** (`availability`, text, optional): Describe your availability for work.

### Location
**Location** (`location`, text, optional): Please enter your preferred location.

### Preferred Work Schedule
**Preferred Work Schedule** (`preferred_work_schedule`, text, optional): Describe your preferred work schedule.

### Availability Work Type
**Availability Work Type** (`availability_work_type`, text, optional): Please describe your availability work type (e.g., full-time, part-time, freelance).

### Expected Annual Salary
**Expected Annual Salary** (`expected_annual_salary`, text, optional): Please enter your expected annual salary.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
- Attach a resume or provide a link to your online resume.
- Use a valid email address and phone number for contact purposes.
- Describe your job title and experience clearly.
- Specify your preferred work schedule and availability.
