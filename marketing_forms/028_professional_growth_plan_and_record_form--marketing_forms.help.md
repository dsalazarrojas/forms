# Professional Growth Plan And Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to record and track professional growth plans for marketing employees, ensuring alignment with company objectives and a structured approach to development and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Select the category**: Choose "Marketing Forms" to categorize your growth plan and record.
2. Fill in the **A Professional Growth Plan and Record Form**: Describe your growth plan and record in this field.
3. **Select the output file**: If you want to save the completed form as a file, select an option here. (Note: This field is optional, but recommended for future reference.)
4. **Choose the assigned tool**: Identify the tool used to support your growth plan and record. (Note: This field is optional, but recommended for tracking tool associations.)
5. **Enter the form IDs**: Enter the IDs assigned to your growth plan and record for tracking and referencing purposes. (Note: This field is required for accurate tracking and record-keeping.)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Marketing Forms (1)** (`category`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to categorize your growth plan and record under the "Marketing Forms" category.
* **A Professional Growth Plan and Record Form (3)** (`description`, `text`, required/true): Describe your growth plan and record in this field. This is a required field to ensure accurate and detailed information.
* **Output File (4)** (`output_file`, `text`, required/optional): Select an option to save the completed form as a file for future reference and tracking.
* **Assigned Tool (5)** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required/optional): Choose the tool used to support your growth plan and record. (Optional)
* **FORM IDS (6)** (`form_ids`, `text`, required/true): Enter the IDs assigned to your growth plan and record for tracking and referencing purposes. This is a required field for accurate tracking and record-keeping.
