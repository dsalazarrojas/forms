# Graphic Design Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Graphic Design Request form is a tool to collect and process design requests from clients or customers. It helps to gather essential information about the project, including the title, description, deadline, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter a title for your design request.
2. Provide your name.
3. Enter your contact email address.
4. Add a detailed description of your design request.
5. If applicable, specify a deadline for the project completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Title** (`title`, `text`, not required): Enter a brief and descriptive title for your design request.
- **Name** (`name`, `text`, not required): Please enter your name or the name of the person requesting the design service.
- **Contact E-Mail** (`contact_email`, `email`, not required): Enter your email address so that we can get in touch with you regarding your design request.
- **Description** (`description`, `note`, not required): Provide a detailed description of the design you require, including specifics about the project's scope, format, and expectations.
- **Deadline** (`deadline`, `date`, not required): If you have a specific deadline for the project completion, please enter the date. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
