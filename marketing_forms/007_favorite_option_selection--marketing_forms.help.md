# Favorite Option Selection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about their favorite options or preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your favorite options from the list provided. You can select multiple options if the field allows it.
2. Fill in any text fields with additional information as needed.
3. Make sure to submit your selection to confirm your answers.
