# Brand Integrity Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Brand Integrity Audit Form is designed to evaluate the consistency and compliance of a brand's visual and messaging elements. It helps auditors assess the brand's overall consistency and identify potential issues that require attention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date of the audit.
2. Enter the name of the person performing the audit.
3. Describe the brand elements reviewed (e.g., logo, messaging, packaging).
4. Rate the overall consistency of the brand elements (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
5. Provide a description of any compliance issues found.
6. Suggest remediation actions to address the issues.
7. Choose the priority level of the actions (High, Medium, or Low).
8. Enter the date for reassessment or follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Audit date**: The date on which the audit was performed.
* **Auditor name**: The name of the person performing the audit.
* **Brand elements reviewed**: A description of the brand elements reviewed during the audit (e.g., logo, messaging, packaging).
* **Consistency rating**: The overall consistency of the brand elements on a scale of Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Compliance issues found**: A description of any issues found during the audit.
* **Recommended actions**: Suggestions for remediation steps to address the issues found.
* **Priority level**: The urgency of the remediation actions, with High, Medium, or Low options.
* **Follow up date**: The date for reassessment or follow-up.

## Tips
* Make sure to rate the consistency based on the provided options (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* Provide specific examples or descriptions for compliance issues and recommended actions.
* Use the priority level to indicate the urgency of addressing the issues.
* The follow-up date is optional but recommended for future reassessments or follow-ups.
