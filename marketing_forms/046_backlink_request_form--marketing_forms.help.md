# Backlink Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a backlink from us for promoting your website, content, or business. It is designed to help us understand your needs and preferences for the backlink.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields (denoted by *) with accurate information.
2. If you are requesting a reciprocal link, please answer the questions accordingly.
3. If you are interested in guest posting, please answer the questions about guest post topic ideas.
4. Fill out any additional fields that you feel are relevant to your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Full Name** (`requester_name`, text, required): Fill in your first and last name.
* **2. Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your business or organization name.
* **3. Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your business email address.
* **4. Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact number.
* **5. Website URL** (`website_url`, text, required): Enter the full URL of your website.
* **6. Industry or Niche** (`industry`, select_one, required): Choose the industry that best describes your website or business.
* **7. Type of Content for Backlink** (`content_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of content that you are requesting a backlink for.
* **8. Content URL** (`content_url`, text, required): Enter the URL of the content you are requesting a backlink for.
* **9. Target Page for Backlink** (`target_page`, text, required): Select the page on your website where you would like the backlink to be placed.
* **10. Preferred Anchor Text** (`anchor_text`, text, required): Enter the text that you prefer to be linked.
* **11. Backlink Type Requested** (`backlink_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of backlink you are requesting.
* **12. Will You Provide Reciprocal Link?** (`reciprocal_link`, select_one, required): Answer truthfully if you can provide a reciprocal link.
* **13. Interested in Guest Posting?** (`guest_post_offer`, select_one, optional): Answer if you are interested in guest posting.
* **14. Guest Post Topic Ideas** (`guest_post_topics`, text, optional): Enter ideas for guest post topics.
* **15. Social Media Following** (`social_media_following`, select_one, optional): Select your approximate social media following.
* **16. Domain Authority** (`domain_authority`, text, optional): Enter your domain authority if known.
* **17. Previous Link Building Collaborations** (`previous_collaborations`, text, optional): List any notable sites you have collaborated with.
* **18. Value Proposition** (`value_proposition`, text, required): Explain why we should link to your content.
* **19. Expected Timeline** (`timeline`, select_one, required): Select when you would like the backlink to be placed.
* **20. Budget for Link Placement** (`budget`, select_one, optional): Select a budget range if applicable.
* **21. Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Enter any other information that you think is relevant.
* **22. Request Date** (`request_date`, date, required): Enter the date of this request.

Note: This form is designed to gather information about your backlink request. Please be honest and accurate when filling out the form. We will review your request and get back to you as soon as possible. Thank you for your request!
