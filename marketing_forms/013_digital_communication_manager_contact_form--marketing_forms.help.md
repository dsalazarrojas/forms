# Digital Communication Manager Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and details from customers or prospects interested in our digital communication services. It helps us understand their preferences and provide a better experience for them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all the required fields (indicated by an asterisk *) accurately.
2. Choose your preferred contact method and provide your phone number for us to get in touch with you.
3. Briefly describe your message or question in the provided text area.
4. Select your preferred channel (e.g., Social media, Referral, Other) for how you found us, if applicable.
5. Choose all the relevant channels (e.g., Social media, Referral, Other) through which you heard about us, if applicable.
6. Fill in the date and time you would like to be contacted.
7. Add any additional notes you'd like to share with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Request** (*): This is the main subject of your contact request. Be as specific as possible so we can understand your query or question.
* **Name** (*): Please provide your name so we can address you properly.
* **Email** (*): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone Number** (*): Add your phone number so we can reach you by phone.
* **Message** (*): Briefly describe your message or question in this text area.
* **How did you find us** (*): Select from Social media, Referral, or Other to help us understand how you got to know us.
* **How did you hear about us** (Optional): Choose all relevant channels through which you heard about us.
* **Date** (*): Select the date you would like us to contact you on.
* **Time** (*): Choose a time you would prefer for us to contact you.
* **Note** (Optional): Add any additional notes or information you'd like to share with us.

Please take a few minutes to review the fields and fill them in accurately. If you have any questions or need assistance, don't hesitate to contact us.
