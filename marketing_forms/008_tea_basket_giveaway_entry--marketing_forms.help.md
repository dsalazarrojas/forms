# tea_basket_giveaway_entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "tea_basket_giveaway_entry" form is for participants in a tea giveaway event to provide their information and preferences for the gift card. This form is used to collect necessary details for the gift card and any other relevant information for the participant. The form is designed to be completed by external participants, such as customers or potential customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details:
	* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
	* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
	* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
	* **Address**: Enter your home or office address.
2. Provide delivery information:
	* **Delivery Details**: Enter any specific instructions or requests for delivery.
3. Answer the gift card questions:
	* **Agree**: Check if you agree to receive the gift card.
	* **Tea Brand**: Check if you'd like to receive a gift card from this tea brand.
	* **Gift Card Option**: Choose how you'd like to receive the gift card (Gift Card, Cash, or Tea Subscription).
	* **Gift Card Value**: Enter the value of the gift card if you've chosen a monetary value.
	* **Gift Card Email**: Enter an email address where the gift card will be sent.
4. Add any additional information:
	* **Participant Details**: Enter any additional details about yourself or your participation.
	* **Participant Info**: Enter any other information you'd like to share.
	* **Participant Messages**: Enter any messages or comments you'd like to share with the giveaway organizers.
