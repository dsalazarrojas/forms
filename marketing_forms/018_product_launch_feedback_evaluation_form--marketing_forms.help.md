# Product Launch Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users on products and services after their launch. This feedback will help our team understand what worked well and what didn't during the launch process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your **User Email** (a required field) to help us identify your feedback.
2. Provide the **Product Name** (optional) if you are commenting on a specific product.
3. Rate your **Feedback Rating** (optional) on the product launch:
	* Excellent
	* Good
	* Fair
	* Poor
4. Share your **Feedback Comment** (optional) about the product launch.
5. Enter the **Product Launch Date** and **Product Launch Time** (optional) if you have information on it.
6. Provide your **Contact Name** (required) and **Contact Email** (required) so we can get back to you about your feedback.
7. Enter your **Contact Phone Number** (required) so we can reach out to you for follow-up questions.
8. Add any additional **Review Comment** (optional) about the product or service.
9. Rate your experience with the product or service with **Review Rating** (optional) on the following:
	* Excellent
	* Good
	* Fair
	* Poor
10. Select the **Product Category** (required) that the product or service falls under:
	* Electronics
	* Software
	* Books
	* Clothing
	* Others

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Email** (Email, Required): Please enter your email address where we can reach out to you about your feedback.
* **Product Name** (Text, Optional): If you're commenting on a specific product, enter its name.
* **Feedback Rating** (Select One, Optional): Rate your experience with the product launch:
	+ Excellent: The product was great!
	+ Good: The product was okay, but had some minor issues.
	+ Fair: The product had some major issues.
	+ Poor: The product was not what I expected.
* **Feedback Comment** (Text, Optional): Share your thoughts and suggestions about the product launch.
* **Product Launch Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date when the product was launched.
* **Product Launch Time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time when the product was launched.
* **Contact Name** (Text, Required): Enter your name so we can get back to you.
* **Contact Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address so we can reach out to you.
* **Contact Phone** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number so we can get in touch with you.
* **Review Comment** (Text, Optional): Share your thoughts about the product or service.
* **Review Rating** (Select Multiple, Optional): Rate your experience with the product or service:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Product Category** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose the category that the product or service falls under.
	+ Electronics
	+ Software
	+ Books
	+ Clothing
	+ Others
