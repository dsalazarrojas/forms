# Printed Materials Development Brief - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Printed Materials Development Brief form is designed to collect project information for Printed Materials Development (PMD) projects. This form is used to create a standardized and comprehensive record of each project, which helps project managers and stakeholders track and communicate project details accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
### Project Description
Please provide a brief description of your project, including its objectives, scope, and key features. This section is crucial for understanding the project's goals and requirements. 

### Project Title
Enter a concise title of your project, summarizing its main objective or purpose.

### Project Status
Select the current status of your project from the options provided:
*   In Production: The project is currently being developed or created.
*   In Review: The project is undergoing review or evaluation.
*   On Hold: The project has been delayed or put on hold.
*   Completed: The project has been finished and delivered.

### Material Type
Select the type(s) of printed materials your project will involve:
*   Paper: Printed materials such as paper-based documents or reports.
*   Digital: Electronic or digital materials such as e-books or online reports.
*   Hybrid: Combination of paper and digital materials.

### Required Resources
Choose the resources required for your project:
*   3D Printer: A 3D printer is necessary for project development.
*   Design Software: Design software is required for project creation.

### Budget Allocated
Enter the budget allocated for your project.

### Estimated Cost
Estimate the cost of your project.

### Project Manager
Enter the name of the project manager responsible for your project.

### Project Owner
Enter the name of the project owner.

### Project Manager Email
Enter the email address of the project manager.

### Project Owner Email
Enter the email address of the project owner.

### Project Start Date
Enter the start date of your project.

### Project End Date
Enter the expected completion or end date of your project.

### Project Tags
Select any relevant tags or keywords associated with your project:
*   Tag 1
*   Tag 2
*   Tag 3

### Project Notes
Provide a brief summary or any additional information related to your project.

### Review Date
Enter the date when you would like the project to be reviewed.

### Submission Date
Enter the date when you would like to submit the project.

### Phone
Enter the contact phone number of the project owner or manager.

### Address
Enter the address of the project owner or manager.

### Website
Enter the website associated with the project owner or manager.

### Contact
Enter the contact information for the project owner or manager.

### Project Owner Name
Enter the name of the project owner.

## Tips
*   Always fill out the form in your own words to ensure accurate project information.
*   Be thorough and detailed in your project description and notes.
*   Use relevant tags or keywords to help identify your project.
*   Double-check the project status and dates for accuracy.
*   Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please do not hesitate to ask for clarification or guidance.
