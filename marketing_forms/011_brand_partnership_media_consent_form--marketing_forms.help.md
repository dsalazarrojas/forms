# Brand Partnership Media Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Brand Partnership Media Consent Form is designed to obtain permission from potential partners to use their brand assets and share their story with our audience. This form is crucial for our marketing and partnership development team to ensure we respect their boundaries and comply with their requirements while promoting their brand.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the following information:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information. This includes your partner's name, contact details, partnership name, and consent preferences.
2. Select the media types you are comfortable with our team sharing about your brand, such as social media posts, email marketing, or website features.
3. Choose how you would like your brand assets to be used, including the scope of the usage and duration of the consent.
4. Decide if you need to approve content before it's posted, and if so, how soon do you want to be notified.
5. Specify any brand restrictions or usage limitations you have.
6. Grant or deny permission for our team to use your logo, testimonials, or image likeness.
7. Disclose if this partnership is paid or unpaid, and if so, what type of compensation is involved.
8. Indicate when you can withdraw your consent in case you change your mind or the partnership ends.
9. Sign the form with your name and date to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partner Full Name (Required):** Enter your legal name or your organization's name.
* **Partner Type (Required):** Choose how you want to be classified as a partner (Individual Influencer, Brand/Company, Media Organization, Content Creator, Agency, or Other).
* **Contact Email (Required):** Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Contact Phone (Required):** Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Partnership or Campaign Name (Required):** Enter the name of the partnership or campaign you're participating in.
* **Media Types You Consent To (Required):** Select all the media types you're comfortable with us sharing about your brand (Social Media Posts, Website Features, Email Marketing, Print Advertising, Video Content, Podcast Mentions, Press Releases, Case Studies).
* **Brand Usage Scope (Required):** Choose how you want your brand assets to be used (Global Usage, Regional Usage Only, Platform Specific, Campaign Specific).
* **Consent Duration (Required):** Select the duration of the consent (6 Months, 1 Year, 2 Years, 3 Years, Perpetual).
* **Content Approval Required (Required):** Decide if you need to approve content before it's posted, and if so, when you want to be notified (Yes, Approval Required, No, Pre-Approved, Review Within 24 Hours).
* **Brand Restrictions (Optional):** Add any restrictions or limitations you have on the usage of your brand assets.
* **Logo Usage Permission (Required):** Grant or deny permission for our team to use your logo (Yes, Full Usage Rights, Limited Usage, No Logo Usage).
* **Testimonial Consent (Required):** Decide if you're comfortable with us using your testimonials or quotes (Yes, May Use Testimonials, No Testimonials, Approval Required Per Use).
* **Image and Likeness Consent (Required):** Grant or deny permission for our team to use your photo or likeness (Yes, Full Consent, Limited Consent, No Consent).
* **Social Media Handles (Optional):** List your social media accounts for our team to connect with you.
* **Compensation Disclosure (Required):** Disclose if this partnership is paid or unpaid, and if so, what type of compensation is involved (Yes, Paid Partnership, No, Unpaid Collaboration, Product Exchange Only).
* **Termination Clause (Required):** Indicate when you can withdraw your consent in case you change your mind or the partnership ends (Can Withdraw with 30 Days Notice, Can Withdraw Anytime, Cannot Withdraw During Campaign).
* **Authorized Signatory (Required):** Enter the name of the person signing on behalf of your brand.
* **Signature Date (Required):** Date of signing.
* **Additional Terms (Optional):** Add any additional terms or agreements that need to be included.
* **Logo and Likeness Consent (Required):** Grant or deny permission for our team to use your logo and likeness.
