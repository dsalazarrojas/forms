# Physician nexus usage survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a survey designed to collect information from physicians about their usage of our services. It helps us understand how often they visit, what services they receive, and how they rate our services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by providing your **First name**, **Last name**, **Email**, and **Phone**. These fields are all optional, but they help us personalize our interactions with you.
2. Please select the **Date of birth** of the physician.
3. Choose the **Time of visit** for the most recent visit.
4. You may add any **Notes** about your visits.
5. Select how many times you have visited our organization with **How many visits do you have with us?**
6. Choose the services you received during your visit with **Which services do you receive during your visit?**
7. Add any **Additional notes** about your visits.
8. For the most recent visit, enter the **Email confirmation** if you have one.
9. Select the **Date and time of visit** for the most recent visit.
10. Choose the **Time of visit 2** for the most recent visit.
11. Enter the **Phone 2** number for the most recent visit.
12. Select how often you visit with **How often do you visit us?**
13. Choose the days you visit with **Which days do you visit us?**
14. Add any **Additional notes 2** about your visits.
15. Enter the **Email 2** for the most recent visit.
16. Select the **Date of visit 2** for the most recent visit.
17. Choose how you rate our services with **How do you rate our service?**
18. Select the type of appointment you have with **What type of appointment do you have?**
19. Choose whether you'd like to schedule an appointment with **Would you like to schedule an appointment?**
20. Select the type of appointment you prefer with **What type of appointment do you prefer?**
21. Add any **Additional note 3** about your visits.
