# Content Idea Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Content Idea Submission Form is a tool for contributors to submit their content ideas. It helps our team review, evaluate, and potentially create a content piece based on the submitted idea.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your information as a contributor, including your name and email.
2.  Provide a proposed title for the content idea.
3.  Select the category of content this idea is best suited for (Industry News, How-to Guide, Case Study, Opinion Piece, Product Update, or Interview).
4.  Briefly describe the content idea and its value.
5.  Specify the target persona this content is for (Small Business Owners, Enterprise IT Managers, Marketing Directors, Freelance Designers, or General Consumers).
6.  Indicate the suggested visuals needed (images, videos, or charts) for the content.
7.  Choose the priority level of the content idea (Low - Seasonal or evergreen, Medium - Timely but not urgent, or High - Urgent news or product launch).
8.  Estimate the time it will take to create the content in hours.
9.  Add any additional resources or data needed to create the content.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <h2 id="page:contributor_info">Contributor Information</h2>
*   **Contributor Name** (`contributor_name`, `text`, required): Provide your name as the contributor.
*   **Contributor Email** (`contributor_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
*   **Department or Agency** (`department_or_agency`, `text`, optional): If applicable, specify your department or agency.

### <h2 id="page:content_pitch">Content Pitch Details</h2>
*   **Proposed Title or Headline** (`pitch_title`, `text`, required): Enter a catchy and relevant title for the content idea.
*   **Content Category** (`content_category`, `select_one`, required): Select the category of content this idea is best suited for (Industry News, How-to Guide, Case Study, Opinion Piece, Product Update, or Interview).
*   **Pitch Summary** (`pitch_summary`, `text`, required): Briefly describe the content idea and its value in 2-3 sentences.
*   **Target Persona** (`target_persona`, `select_one`, required): Specify the target persona this content is for (Small Business Owners, Enterprise IT Managers, Marketing Directors, Freelance Designers, or General Consumers).
*   **Suggested Visuals** (`suggested_visuals`, `text`, optional): If applicable, specify the images, videos, or charts needed for the content.
*   **Priority Level** (`priority_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose the priority level of the content idea (Low - Seasonal or evergreen, Medium - Timely but not urgent, or High - Urgent news or product launch).

### <h2 id="page:additional_info">Additional Information</h2>
*   **Estimated Time to Create (Hours)** (`estimated_completion_time`, `number`, optional): Estimate the total production time in hours.
*   **Required Resources or Data** (`additional_resources`, `text`, optional): If applicable, specify any additional resources or data needed for the content.
