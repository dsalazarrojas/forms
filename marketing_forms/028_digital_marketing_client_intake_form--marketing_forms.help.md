# Digital Marketing Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from potential clients about their digital marketing needs and preferences, which will help us determine how to best serve them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the job title and name of the company or organization you are representing.
2. Provide your contact email address.
3. Enter your contact phone number.
4. If applicable, select the current status of your job.
5. Choose the type of job you need help with from the options provided (Social Media Management, Content Creation, SEO, etc.).
6. If necessary, enter your estimated budget for the job.
7. Enter any relevant notes about your project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Job Title and Name**: Enter the title and name of your company or organization. This will help us understand who you are and what you represent.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address so we can reach out to you with any questions or updates.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Job Start Date**: Enter the start date of your job, if it has already begun.
* **Job Deadline**: Enter the deadline date for your job, if it has a specific end date.
* **Job Status**: If your job is currently active, pending, or cancelled, select the corresponding status.
* **Client Location**: Enter the location of your company or organization.
* **Job Type**: Select one of the options to indicate the type of job you need help with.
* **Notes**: Enter any relevant notes about your project that will help us better understand your needs.
* **Client Budget**: If you have an estimated budget for the job, enter the amount.

# Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure we have enough information to serve you best.
- If you need help with something that is not listed as an option, please let us know and we will do our best to accommodate your needs.
- We will use the information you provide to determine the best course of action to help you with your digital marketing needs.
