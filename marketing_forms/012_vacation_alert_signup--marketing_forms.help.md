# vacation-alert-signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users for the purpose of setting up a vacation alert system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the travel agency questions: Select your preferred travel agency from the list.
2. Choose your subscription preferences: Select the options that apply to you.
3. Enter your contact information: Provide your email address, first name, last name, and phone number.
4. Consent to notifications: Select whether you want to receive notifications.
5. Choose notification preferences: Select the frequency and channel of notifications you prefer.
6. Review and submit: Review all your answers and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Agency** (`travel_agency`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred travel agency from the list.
* **Subscriber Preference** (`subscriber_preference`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the options that apply to you.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `text`, required): Enter your email address in the format XXXX-XXXXX@XXXXX.com (e.g., 555-1234-5678@abcc.de).
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number in the format XXX-XXX-XXXX (e.g., 555-123-4567).
* **Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether you want to receive notifications.
* **Category Preference** (`category_preference`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the options that apply to you.
* **Language Preference** (`language_preference`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the options that apply to you.
* **Timezone Preference** (`timezone_preference`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the options that apply to you.
* **Notification Preference** (`notification_preference`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether you want to receive notifications.
* **Notification Frequency** (`notification_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Select the frequency of notifications you prefer.
* **Notification Channel** (`notification_channel`, `select_one`, optional): Select the channel for notifications you prefer.
