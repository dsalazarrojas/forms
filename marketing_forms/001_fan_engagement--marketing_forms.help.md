## Purpose
The Fan Engagement form is designed to collect information about a customer or fan's engagement with a brand or product. This form helps to gather relevant details such as their interest in the product or service, their availability for communication, and any additional comments or notes they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Fan Engagement Form with your relevant details.
2. Choose whether you are a fan of the product or service (Fan Type) from the options provided.
3. Enter your name, email address, and phone number in the respective fields.
4. If required, select a date and time for any scheduled appointment or event.
5. Add any additional comments or notes about your engagement with the product or service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Fan Type (`fan_type`, select_one, required/optional)**: Choose whether you are a fan of the product or service by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' from the options.
- **Name (`name`, text, required/optional)**: Enter your name to associate with your engagement.
- **Email (`email`, email, required/optional)**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
- **Phone Number (`phone_number`, text, required/optional)**: Enter your phone number for additional contact details.
- **Date (`date`, date, required/optional)**: If required, select a date for any scheduled appointment or event.
- **Time (`time`, time, required/optional)**: If required, select a time for any scheduled appointment or event.
- **Note (`note`, note, required/optional)**: Add any additional comments or notes about your engagement with the product or service.

## Tips
- Make sure to complete the form accurately and honestly to ensure we can best assist you.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for further assistance.
- This form is for internal use only, so please do not share it with anyone else.
- The information collected will be used to improve our services and products.
