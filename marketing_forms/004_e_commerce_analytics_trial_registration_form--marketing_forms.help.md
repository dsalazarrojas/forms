# E Commerce Analytics Trial Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The E Commerce Analytics Trial Registration Form is used to collect information about trials that are currently taking place or are scheduled to take place. This form helps us understand the context and goals of these trials and enable us to better serve our customers and partners.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Be as detailed as possible when filling out the descriptive fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **1. Company Name** (Company Name, text, required): This is the name of the company that is conducting the trial.
- **2. Contact First Name** (Contact First Name, text, optional): This is the first name of the contact person for the trial. 
- **3. Contact Last Name** (Contact Last Name, text, optional): This is the last name of the contact person for the trial. 
- **4. Email** (Email, email, optional): This is the email address of the contact person for the trial.
- **5. Company URL** (Company URL, text, optional): This is the URL of the company's website. 
- **6. Phone** (Phone, text, optional): This is the phone number of the contact person for the trial. 
- **7. Contact Position** (Contact Position, text, optional): This is the position of the contact person for the trial. 
- **8. Company Location** (Company Location, text, optional): This is the location of the company that is conducting the trial. 
- **9. Trial Start Date** (Trial Start Date, date, optional): This is the start date of the trial. 
- **10. Trial End Date** (Trial End Date, date, optional): This is the end date of the trial. 
- **11. Company Description** (Company Description, note, optional): This is a description of the company that is conducting the trial. 
- **12. Trial Description** (Trial Description, note, optional): This is a description of the trial. 
- **13. Company Sector** (Company Sector, select_multiple, optional): This is the sector of the company that is conducting the trial. 
  - Software
  - Technology
  - E-commerce
  - Finance
  - Health
  - Other
- **14. Industry** (Industry, select_multiple, optional): This is the industry of the company that is conducting the trial. 
  - Software
  - Technology
  - Retail
  - Finance
  - Health
  - Other
- **15. Trial Type** (Trial Type, select_one, optional): This is the type of trial. 
  - Beta Trial
  - Pilot Trial
  - Focus Group
  - Other
- **16. Trial Status** (Trial Status, select_one, optional): This is the status of the trial. 
  - Active
  - Scheduled
  - Completed
  - Cancelled
- **17. Trial Goal** (Trial Goal, text, optional): This is the goal of the trial. 
- **18. Trial Budget** (Trial Budget, number, optional): This is the budget of the trial. 
- **19. Company Size** (Company Size, select_multiple, optional): This is the size of the company that is conducting the trial. 
  - Less than 10
  - 10-50
  - 50-100
  - 100-500
  - More than 500
- **20. Trial Duration** (Trial Duration, number, optional): This is the duration of the trial. 
- **21. Company Address** (Company Address, text, optional): This is the address of the company that is conducting the trial. 
- **22. Trial Participants** (Trial Participants, number, optional): This is the number of participants in the trial. 
- **23. Trial Outcomes** (Trial Outcomes, note, optional): This is a description of the outcomes of the trial. 
- **24. Assigned User** (Assigned User, text, optional): This is the user who is assigned to this form. 

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the descriptive fields.
* Only fill out this form if you are the contact person for the trial.
* If you are not sure about any of the fields, please do not fill out the form and contact us directly.
* We will get back to you with any questions or concerns we have about your submission.
