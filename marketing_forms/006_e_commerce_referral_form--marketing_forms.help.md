# e-commerce referral form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The e-commerce referral form is a tool for businesses or individuals to track and manage referrals and reward their customers or partners for referring others.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the first two fields.
2. Provide your Email address to receive any rewards or notifications.
3. Select the Referral Source from the options provided (e.g., Referral, Online Ad, Social Media, or Offline).
4. Enter the Referred User's information, including their Name, Email address, and Phone number (if applicable).
5. Choose the Reward you would like to offer (e.g., Free Product, Discount, or Cash).
6. If you have any additional information about the reward, please provide it in the Reward Details field.
7. Confirm if the Referred User's Email address is confirmed.
8. If the Referred User's Email address is confirmed, provide the email address.
9. Confirm if you have accepted the Terms of Service.
10. If yes, provide details about the Terms of Service you accepted.
11. Finally, submit the form and confirm that you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name here.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name here.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address here.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if you want to be contacted).
- **Referral Source** (`referral_source`, select_one, required): Select how you referred the customer.
- **Referred User** (`referred_user`, text, required): Enter the referred customer's name.
- **Reward** (`reward`, select_multiple, required): Choose the reward you want to offer.
- **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the referral.
- **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the referral.
- **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the referral.
- **Email Confirmed** (`email_confirmed`, select_one, required): Confirm if the Referred User's Email address is confirmed.
- **Referred User Phone** (`referred_phone`, text, optional): Enter the referred customer's phone number.
- **Reward Details** (`reward_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about the reward.
- **Terms Accepted** (`terms_accepted`, select_one, optional): Confirm if you have accepted the Terms of Service.
- **Terms Confirmed** (`terms_confirmed`, text, optional): Enter any details about the Terms of Service you accepted.
- **Reward Details Terms** (`reward_details_terms`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the reward terms.
- **Terms Accepted Hint** (`terms_accepted_hint`, text, optional): Enter any details about the Terms of Service you accepted.
- **Reward Details Terms 2** (`reward_details_terms_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the reward terms.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, text, optional): Enter the Referred User’s email address.
- **Referred User Phone Country** (`referred_phone_country`, text, optional): Enter the Referred User’s phone country.
- **Type of reward** (`reward_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of reward.
- **If other, please specify** (`reward_other`, text, optional): Enter any other type of reward.
- **Referral Program** (`referral_program`, text, optional): Enter any referral program details.
- **Submitted** (`submitted`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have submitted the form.

## Tips
- Please fill out this form only once per referred customer.
- Ensure that you have the necessary information and consent to submit this form.
- Avoid submitting duplicate referrals.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the form owner.
