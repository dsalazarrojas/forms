# Promotional Giveaway Design Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Promotional Giveaway Design Order Form is designed to collect information from businesses for promotional giveaway design services. This form is used to gather essential details such as business name, design ID, product name, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your Form ID to identify the specific giveaway design order.
2. Enter your Business Name to help us understand who you are and what your business is about.
3. Provide the Design ID to specify the unique design order.
4. Enter the Product Name and Product Type to give us a clear understanding of the product being designed.
5. Specify the Quantity required for the giveaway.
6. Choose the Size and Color options that fit your design requirements.
7. Select the Font and Font Size for the design.
8. Describe the Design Description in detail.
9. Set a Deadline for when you need the design to be completed.
10. Choose the Prophets (if applicable) to ensure we have a clear understanding of your requirements.
11. Add any Notes to provide additional context or instructions.
12. Enter your Email and Phone number to ensure we can contact you efficiently.
13. Optionally, choose Minimalist options to specify your design preferences.
14. Enter the Design Image URL, Image URL, Product URL, Product URL 2, and Product URL 3 to provide supporting images and URLs for the design.
15. Finally, review and submit the form to initiate the giveaway design process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form ID (1)**: A unique identifier for the design order.
* **Business Name (2)**: Your business name and a brief description.
* **Design ID (3)**: A unique identifier for the design order.
* **Product Name (4)**: The name of the product being designed.
* **Product Type (5)**: The type of product being designed.
* **Quantity (6)**: The number of products required.
* **Size (7)**: The size of the product.
* **Color (8)**: The color of the product.
* **Font (9)**: The font to be used in the design.
* **Font Size (10)**: The font size for the design.
* **Design Description (11)**: A detailed description of the design requirements.
* **Deadline (12)**: The date and time when you need the design to be completed.
* **Prophets (13)**: A list of prophets or experts who should be involved in the design process.
* **Notes (14)**: Additional context or instructions for the design team.
* **Email (15)**: Your email address for communication.
* **Phone (16)**: Your phone number for communication.
* **Minimalist (17)**: Optional design preference for a minimalist style.
* **Minimalist 2 (18)**: Optional design preference for another minimalist style.
* **Design Image URL (19)**: A URL of the design image.
* **Image URL (20)**: A URL of the image to be used in the design.
* **Product URL (21)**: A URL of the product to be designed.
* **Product URL 2 (22)**: A URL of the product to be designed.
* **Product URL 3 (23)**: A URL of the product to be designed.
* **Product URL 4 (24)**: A URL of the product to be designed.
