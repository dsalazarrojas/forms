# Client Intake Form For Video Advertising - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your video advertising campaign, including its objective, target audience, and technical requirements. Please complete this form to ensure that we have a clear understanding of your needs and can create a high-quality video that meets your expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

* Read each section carefully and provide accurate information about your video advertising campaign.
* Enter your information in the relevant fields, and make sure to select the correct options for each field.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask your marketing team or project manager for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required): This is your company name or the name of the organization you represent.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_person`, `text`, required): This is the name of the person responsible for this campaign, or the person to contact for further information.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): This is the email address of the contact person or the email address where you can be reached.
* **-- Campaign Goals --** (`section_goals`, `note`, optional): Briefly describe the main objectives of your video campaign. This will help us understand the purpose and direction of your video.
* **What is the primary objective of this video?** (`video_objective`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the following options: 
	+ Brand Awareness
	+ Lead Generation
	+ Product Launch
	+ Educational
	+ Event Promotion
* **Who is the target audience for this campaign?** (`target_audience`, `text`, required): Describe the demographic or profile of your target audience.
* **Core Message - What is the one thing the audience should remember?** (`core_message`, `text`, required): This is the key message or takeaway that you want your audience to remember after watching the video.
* **-- Technical Requirements --** (`section_technical`, `note`, optional): This section is for you to describe any specific technical requirements for your video, such as video length or format.
* **Preferred Video Length** (`video_length`, `select_one`, optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ 15 seconds
	+ 30 seconds
	+ 60 seconds
	+ 2-3 minutes
	+ 5+ minutes
* **Preferred Video Formats** (`video_formats`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable formats for your video:
	+ 16-9 Widescreen
	+ 9-16 Vertical
	+ 1-1 Square
* **Desired Delivery Deadline** (`delivery_deadline`, `date`, required): Enter the date by which you would like to receive the video.
* **Estimated Budget for Video Production** (`production_budget`, `number`, optional): Enter the estimated budget for the video production in USD.
* **-- Creative Direction --** (`section_creative`, `note`, optional): This section is for you to describe any specific creative direction or tone you'd like the video to have.
* **Desired Tone for the Video** (`video_tone`, `select_one`, optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ Professional
	+ Humorous
	+ Inspirational
	+ Energetic
	+ Minimalist
* **Existing Assets - e.g. logos, brand guides, raw footage** (`existing_assets`, `text`, optional): If you have any existing assets, such as logos or brand guides, please describe them here.
