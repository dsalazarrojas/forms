# Sponsorship Benefit Clarification Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sponsorship Benefit Clarification Request Form is designed to help sponsors obtain clarification on their sponsorship benefits. This form is intended to be used by sponsors who need more information on the benefits they are entitled to.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, please make sure to provide accurate and clear information. The following steps will guide you through the process:

1. Fill out the general information section, providing a brief description of the sponsorship.
2. In the Contact Person section, provide the name and contact email of the person responsible for the sponsorship.
3. Fill out the Sponsor Details section, providing any additional information about the sponsorship.
4. In the Sponsor 1 Sponsorship Type section, specify the sponsorship type for the first sponsor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information (General Information, text, optional)**: A brief description of the sponsorship, including any relevant details or context.
* **Contact Person (Contact Person, text, required)**: The name of the person responsible for the sponsorship.
* **Contact Email (Contact Email, email, required)**: The contact email of the person responsible for the sponsorship.
* **Sponsor Details (Sponsor Details, text, optional)**: Any additional information about the sponsorship.
* **Sponsor 1 Sponsorship Type (Sponsor 1 Sponsorship Type, text, optional)**: The type of sponsorship for the first sponsor.
