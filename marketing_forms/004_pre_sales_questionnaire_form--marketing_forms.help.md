# pre_sales_questionnaire_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pre-sales questionnaire form is designed to collect contact information and company details from potential customers or partners for marketing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with your information. You can fill out any or all of the fields as needed. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **Company** (text): Enter your company name.
* **Position** (text): Enter your position or role within the company.
* **Job Title** (text): Enter your job title within the company.
* **Department** (text): Enter your department within the company.
* **Company Website** (text): Enter your company website URL.
* **Company Size** (text): Enter your company's size or number of employees.
* **Revenue** (number): Enter your company's revenue.

## Tips
* Ensure that your email address is valid and up-to-date.
* Double-check your company website URL for accuracy.
* If you're unsure about your company size, you can provide an estimate or a range (e.g., 10-50 employees).
