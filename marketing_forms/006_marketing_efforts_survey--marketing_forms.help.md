# Marketing Efforts Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your marketing efforts, including your role in the company, marketing strategies, and preferences for social media, email marketing, and paid advertising. The goal is to help us better understand your needs and provide you with more effective marketing support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and select your preferences for each question. You can select multiple options for some questions, so feel free to pick the ones that apply to you.
