# Cooler Bag Competition Entry - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information for a competition related to a cooler bag. The primary purpose of this form is to gather essential details from participants, including their consent to terms of service, delivery information, and personal contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields, which are marked as required in the form.
2. In the State field, select only one state from the provided options.
3. Ensure you have filled out all fields correctly before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details**: 
  * **Name** (`name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name.
  * **Email** (`email`, `text`, optional): Enter your valid email address.
  * **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact number.
  * **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter your address.
  * **City** (`city`, `text`, optional): Enter your city.
* **Delivery Information**:
  * **Delivery Information** (`delivery_info`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional delivery information.
* **Consent**:
  * **Do you agree with the terms of the service?** (`participant_consent`, `select_one`, optional): Select "I understand and agree" if you agree with the terms.
* **Contact Information**:
  * **State** (`state`, `select_one`, required): Select your state from the provided options (New York, California, Florida, Other).
  * **Zip** (`zip`, `text`, optional): Enter your zip code.

## Tips
- Double-check that all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
- Ensure you have provided valid contact information in the correct format.
