# Logo Creation Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Logo Creation Onboarding Form is a comprehensive form designed to help you create a professional logo for your company. This form guides you through the process of specifying the essential elements of your logo, including the company name, tagline, primary and secondary branding colors, font, font color, and logo placement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the company name in the "Company Name" field.
2. Provide the correct spelling of the company name in the "Company Spelling" field.
3. Briefly describe the company in the "Tagline" field.
4. Specify the tagline placement in the "Tagline Placement" field.
5. Select the primary and secondary branding colors from the "Primary Branding Color" and "Secondary Branding Color" dropdown menus.
6. Choose a free online color selection tool in the "Color Selection Tool" field.
7. Upload a color image in the "Upload Color Image" field.
8. Enter the primary branding color hex code in the "Primary Branding Color Hex Code" field.
9. Enter the secondary branding color hex code in the "Secondary Branding Color Hex Code" field.
10. Select the font from the "Font" dropdown menu.
11. Choose the font color from the "Font Color" dropdown menu.
12. Select a free online font selection tool in the "Font Color Selection Tool" field.
13. Upload a font image in the "Upload Font Image" field.
14. Enter the font name in the "Font Name" field.
15. Specify the font size in the "Font Size" field.
16. Choose the font style from the "Font Style" dropdown menu.
17. Select the logo type from the "Logo Type" dropdown menu.
18. Choose the logo placement from the "Logo Placement" dropdown menu.
19. Specify the logo width in the "Logo Width" field.
20. Specify the logo height in the "Logo Height" field.
21. Specify the logo placement position in the "Logo Placement Position" field.
22. Choose the tagline placement position from the "Tagline Placement Position" dropdown menu.
23. Enter the tagline text in the "Tagline Text" field.
24. (Optional) Enter the tagline text in the "Tagline" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Company Spelling** (`company_spelling`, text, required): Enter the correct spelling of your company name.
* **Tagline** (`tagline`, text, required): Briefly describe your company in this field.
* **Tagline Placement** (`tagline_placement`, text, required): Specify where you want the tagline to be placed.
* **Primary Branding Color** (`primary_branding_color`, select_multiple, required): Choose a primary branding color for your logo.
* **Secondary Branding Color** (`secondary_branding_color`, select_multiple, required): Choose a secondary branding color for your logo.
* **Color Selection Tool** (`color_selection`, text, required): Select a free online color selection tool.
* **Upload Color Image** (`upload_color_image`, text, required): Upload a color image.
* **Primary Branding Color Hex Code** (`primary_branding_color_hex`, text, required): Enter the primary branding color hex code.
* **Secondary Branding Color Hex Code** (`secondary_branding_color_hex`, text, required): Enter the secondary branding color hex code.
* **Font** (`font`, select_one, required): Choose a font for your logo.
* **Font Color** (`font_color`, select_multiple, required): Choose a font color for your logo.
* **Font Color Selection Tool** (`font_color_selection_tool`, text, required): Select a free online font color selection tool.
* **Upload Font Image** (`upload_font_image`, text, required): Upload a font image.
* **Font Name** (`font_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your font.
* **Font Size** (`font_size`, number, required): Specify the font size for your logo.
* **Font Style** (`font_style`, select_one, required): Choose the font style for your logo.
* **Logo Type** (`logo_type`, select_one, required): Choose the logo type for your logo.
* **Logo Placement** (`logo_placement`, select_multiple, required): Choose the placement of your logo.
* **Logo Width** (`logo_width`, number, required): Specify the width of your logo.
* **Logo Height** (`logo_height`, number, required): Specify the height of your logo.
* **Logo Placement Position** (`logo_placement_position`, text, required): Specify the placement position of your logo.
* **Tagline Placement Position** (`tagline_placement_position`, select_one, required): Choose the placement position of your tagline.
* **Tagline Text** (`tagline_text`, text, required): Enter the text of your tagline.
* **Tagline** (`tagline`, text, optional): This field is optional.
