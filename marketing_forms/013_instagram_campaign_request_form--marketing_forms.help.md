# Instagram Campaign Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps us assess your business's eligibility for an Instagram campaign. Please fill out the form accurately and completely with your business information, campaign requirements, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **account_information** field on the first page with your business name and address.
2. On the second page, enter your **business_details** in the **business_details** field.
3. On the third page, describe your **campaign_requests** in the **campaign_requests** field.
4. Enter your **contact_information** in the **contact_info** field on the fourth page.
5. Fill out your **date** field with a valid date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
6. Provide a valid **email** address for us to contact you.
7. If you have a **phone**, please enter it in the **phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **title** (account_information, text, required): Please enter your business name.
* **account_info** (account_information, text, optional): Please enter your business address (if different from your name).
* **business_info** (business_details, text, optional): Please describe your business, including your industry, products/services, and target audience.
* **campaign_request** (campaign_requests, text, optional): Describe the campaign you're requesting, including the desired content, duration, and any specific requirements.
* **contact_info** (contact_information, text, optional): Enter your name, job title, and a valid phone number or email address for us to contact you.
* **date** (date, date, optional): Enter a valid date for your campaign start and end dates (mm/dd/yyyy format).
* **email** (email, email, optional): Please enter a valid email address for us to contact you.
* **phone** (phone, text, optional): If you have a phone number, please enter it so we can contact you.

## Tips
- Please ensure that your form is accurate and complete to increase your chances of getting approved.
- If you have any issues or questions, contact us at <contact email or phone number>.
