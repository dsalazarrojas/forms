# Mailing List - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mailing List form is used to collect information from users who want to join your mailing list. This includes their email address and other relevant details such as their preferred email marketing software.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please fill in the required fields as follows:
	* Join Our Mailing List: Enter your email address to join our mailing list.
	* Mailing List: If you have a preferred mailing list, please let us know by providing its name.
	* Description: Provide a brief description of your mailing list preferences.
	* Integration: If you have an email marketing software integration set up, please indicate if it's 'Yes' or 'No'.
	* Email Marketing Software: Select your preferred email marketing software from the options provided.
	* Mailchimp Constant Contact: If you have Mailchimp Constant Contact integration set up, indicate if it's 'Yes' or 'No'.
	* Other Integration: If you have any other email marketing software integration set up, provide its name and details.
	* Other Email Marketing Software: Select your other email marketing software from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Join Our Mailing List** (`join_email`, email, required): Enter your email address to join our mailing list.
* **Mailing List** (`mailing_list`, text, optional): If you have a preferred mailing list, provide its name.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your mailing list preferences.
* **Integration** (`integration`, text, optional): If you have an email marketing software integration set up, indicate if it's 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Email Marketing Software** (`email_marketing_software`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred email marketing software from the options provided.
* **Mailchimp Constant Contact** (`mailchimp_constant_contact`, select_multiple, optional): If you have Mailchimp Constant Contact integration set up, indicate if it's 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Other Integration** (`other_integration`, text, optional): If you have any other email marketing software integration set up, provide its name and details.
* **Other Email Marketing Software** (`other_email_marketing_software`, select_multiple, optional): Select your other email marketing software from the options provided.

## Tips
- Please ensure your email address is correct to avoid any issues with subscription confirmation.
- If you have any other email marketing software integration set up, please provide its name and details in the 'Other Integration' field.
