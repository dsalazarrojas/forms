# real_estate_investor_onboarding_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from real estate investors for onboarding purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the contact information section with your company's details.
	* Enter your company name in the "Company Name" field.
	* Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
	* Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
2. Provide your company's contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
	* Enter your company's email address in the "Company Email" field.
	* Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
3. Provide your company's physical address in the "Address" section.
	* Enter the first line of your company's address in the "Address 1" field.
	* Enter the second line of your company's address in the "Address 2" field.
	* Enter the city name in the "City" field.
4. Choose your state of operation from the dropdown menu in the "State" field.
5. Enter your zip code in the "Zip Code" field.
6. Select the type of investment you're interested in from the dropdown menu in the "Investment Type" field.
7. Select the type of investor you are (Private, Institutional, Individual, or Other) from the dropdown menu in the "Investor Type" field.
8. Enter the amount of money you're willing to invest in the "Investment Amount" field.
9. Choose the investment term you're interested in (Short-term, Medium-term, or Long-term) from the dropdown menu in the "Investment Term" field.
10. Select how often you'd like to invest (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually) from the dropdown menu in the "Investment Frequency" field.
11. Choose your current investment status (Active, Inactive, or Closed) from the dropdown menu in the "Investment Status" field.
12. If you have any additional information about your investment, you can enter it in the "Investment Note" field.
13. Enter a brief description of your company in the "Company Description" field.
14. If you have a website, enter the URL in the "Company Website" field.
15. If you have a Facebook page, enter the URL in the "Company Facebook" field.
16. If you have a Twitter page, enter the URL in the "Company Twitter" field.
17. If you have a LinkedIn page, enter the URL in the "Company LinkedIn" field.
18. If you have an Instagram page, enter the URL in the "Company Instagram" field.
19. Enter your email address in the "Investor Email" field.
20. Enter your phone number in the "Investor Phone" field.
