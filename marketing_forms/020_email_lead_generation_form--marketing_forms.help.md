# Email Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information from individuals interested in learning more about our services, with the goal of generating leads for potential clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Lead Name** in the first field. This is where you will enter your name, which will be used to identify you as a potential client.
2. Enter your **Email** address in the second field. This is the email address that we will use to follow up with you and provide more information about our services.
3. Enter your **Company** name in the third field. If you are representing a company, please enter the company name here.
4. Enter your **Position** in the fourth field. This is your job title or role within the company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lead Name**: A required field where you enter your name, which will be used to identify you as a potential client.
* **Email**: An optional field where you enter your email address. Please ensure it is accurate to receive follow-up communications from us.
* **Company**: An optional field where you enter the name of the company you represent, if applicable.
* **Position**: An optional field where you enter your job title or role within the company.
