# Custom Candle Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom Candle Quote Request Form is a structured way for customers to submit their custom candle quote requests. It ensures that all necessary information is gathered accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately, including Customer Email, Product ID, and Quote Status.
2.  Enter Customer Name, Quote Date, and Customer Phone if necessary.
3.  Upload any relevant files or messages as needed.
4.  Enter the Quote Total, if required.
5.  Review and submit the form carefully to ensure that all information is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   *   **Customer Email**: Enter the customer's email address. This is a required field and will be used to send quotes and updates to the customer.
*   *   **Customer Name**: Enter your name or the name of the person submitting the request. This field is optional, but it helps us to personalize communication.
*   *   **Product ID**: Enter the product ID number corresponding to the custom candle product you are requesting. This is a required field and is used to identify the product.
*   *   **Quote Date**: Enter the date you would like the quote to be generated for. This field is optional, but it helps us to prioritize quotes accordingly.
*   *   **Custom Candle Quote Request Form Uploads**: Attach any relevant files or images to support your request.
*   *   **Custom Candle Quote Request Form Messages**: Add any additional information or comments you'd like to share with us.
*   *   **Quote Total**: Enter the total quote amount if required.
*   *   **Quote Status**: Select the status of the quote from the dropdown list. This will determine the next steps in the quote process.
*   *   **Customer Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number, if you need to reach out to them for any further information.
*   *   **Custom Candle Quote Request Form Tags**: Add any relevant tags or keywords to help us to categorize and prioritize your request.
*   *   **Custom Candle Quote Request Form Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about your request, if needed.
