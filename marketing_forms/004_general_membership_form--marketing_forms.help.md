# General Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The General Membership Form is a simple form designed to collect basic information from individuals who want to join or express interest in a particular group or community. This form is typically used for marketing purposes to gather contact details and other relevant data from potential or existing members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the General Membership Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Provide accurate and complete information in each field.
3. Review your details carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Membership Details** (`general_membership_form`, text, required: false): This is the first field on the form where you can briefly describe your interest in joining or your current membership status.
* **Member Details** (`member_details`, text, required: false): This field allows you to provide more information about yourself as a potential or existing member.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your physical address for contact and communication purposes.
* **City** (`city`, text, required: false): Provide the city where you live or work.
* **State** (`state`, text, required: false): Enter the state where you reside or work.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required: false): Enter your postal code or zip code for mail and delivery purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address that you use for communication.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number for communication and follow-up purposes.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, required: false): This field is for any relevant payment information you'd like to share.
