# Brand Image Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge the brand image of a company, gathering feedback on its perceived value, trust level, and overall reputation. It's meant to help the brand improve its perception among various groups, including customers, employees, partners, and stakeholders.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, follow these steps:
1. Select your Respondent role (e.g., Customer, Employee, Partner, Other).
2. Rate your Familiarity with the brand (e.g., Very Familiar, Somewhat Familiar, Not Familiar).
3. Enter the Top association that comes to mind about the brand.
4. Rate your Trust level with the brand (e.g., High, Moderate, Low).
5. Rate your Likelihood to recommend the brand (e.g., Very Likely, Likely, Unlikely, Very Unlikely).
6. Choose the Perceived value for the money (e.g., High, Fair, Low).
7. Provide Improvement suggestions for the brand.
8. Add any Additional comments or feedback about the brand.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Respondent role
* **Respondent role (Customer, Employee, Partner, Other)**: This field asks about your relationship with the brand. Choose the option that best describes your connection to the brand.
### Familiarity with brand
* **Familiarity with brand (Very familiar, Somewhat familiar, Not familiar)**: Rate how well you know the brand, from very familiar to not familiar at all.
### Top association
* **Top association (text)**: Write a one-word answer that best represents your perception of the brand.
### Trust level
* **Trust level (High, Moderate, Low)**: Rate your level of trust with the brand, from high to low.
### Likelihood to recommend
* **Likelihood to recommend (Very likely, Likely, Unlikely, Very unlikely)**: Rate how likely you are to recommend the brand, from very likely to very unlikely.
### Perceived value
* **Perceived value (High, Fair, Low)**: Rate the value you perceive the brand offers for the money.
### Improvement suggestions
* **Improvement suggestions (text)**: Suggest ways the brand can improve its perception.
### Additional comments
* **Additional comments (text)**: Add any other feedback or thoughts about the brand.
## Tips
* Please be honest and provide your true feedback. This survey is meant to help the brand improve, so your input is valuable.
* You can skip fields that don't apply to you or don't make sense in your response.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
