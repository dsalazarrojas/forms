# Face Wash Product Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect comprehensive data about face wash products. It is intended for genuine submissions from face wash product manufacturers or representatives.

## How to Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and detailed information about the face wash product.
2. Ensure that you select all relevant ingredients for the product.
3. Provide a detailed description of the product, including its usage and characteristics.
4. Enter the pricing information for the product.
5. Upload any relevant images for the product.
6. Optionally, provide videos or other files to support the product submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Data** (product_data): This field is required and should include the basic product information, such as name, description, and category.
* **Ingredients** (ingredients): Select all relevant ingredients used in the face wash product from the provided options (Sulfur, Benzoin, Glycerin, Fragrance). This field is required.
* **Product Description** (product_description): Provide a detailed description of the face wash product, including its characteristics and usage.
* **Pricing** (pricing): Enter the pricing information for the face wash product.
* **Images** (images): Upload any relevant images for the face wash product.
* **Videos** (videos): Optionally, upload any videos that demonstrate the product's features or usage.
* **Files** (files): Optionally, upload any additional files that support the face wash product submission.
* **Assets** (assets): This field is required and should include any relevant assets or materials associated with the face wash product.
* **Packaging** (packaging): This field is optional, but providing packaging information can help with product logistics and distribution. 
* **Instructions** (instructions): This field is optional, but providing usage instructions can help customers understand the product's benefits and usage.
